# Revelation source checks and editorial decisions

All 405 numbered verse units were compared with the German Bible Society’s displayed NA28 running text. The English was reviewed in chapter context, with attention to clause order, spoken cadence, imagery, and repeated terms. The critical apparatus, UBS6/ECM comparison, independent Greek review, and human read-aloud review remain pending.

## Chapters 1–7

- **Chapter 1 (20 units).** “Reads aloud” in 1:3 follows the singular reader contrasted with plural listeners. Preserve “who is and who was and who is coming,” firstborn “of the dead,” seven Churches, the voice John turns to see, and the dual keys of death and Hades. Retain NA28’s “freed” (λύσαντι) in 1:5 and include the displayed single-bracketed “of the ages” at 1:6. No additional title is inserted at 1:11 and no Amen is inserted at 1:18. The active English construction in 1:20 resolves the opening fragment without adding content to the mystery. Review “burnished bronze” as a provisional gloss for the uncertain metal term. [NA28 Revelation 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.1)
- **Chapter 2 (29 units).** Preserve the recurring messages “Thus says,” “Let the one who has an ear hear,” “the one who conquers,” and the named audiences, with ordinary you/your throughout. At 2:10, “some of you” marks the shift from singular addressee to plural people who will be tested. Preserve the Greek images “stumbling block,” “kidneys and hearts,” “kill … with death,” “shepherd them with an iron rod,” and the white stone. Review the bodily idiom and the possible sense of pestilence in the death phrase, and the scope of “as they say” at 2:24. No wife, sickbed, or alternative deeds are inferred. [NA28 Revelation 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.2)
- **Chapter 3 (22 units).** Preserve name/alive/dead and names/garments, the kept/keep repetition at 3:10, and the distinct contrasts in the hot/cold and lukewarm passages. “Sanctuary” represents ναός. Keep “beginning” at 3:14, leaving the term’s possible senses for a note. Keep the metaphor “word of My endurance,” and the ordinary-address “you.” The displayed single-bracketed connecting word at 3:20 requires no independent English word in the conditional construction. [NA28 Revelation 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.3)
- **Chapter 4 (11 units).** Preserve the sequence of twenty-four thrones, seven torches (distinct from the lampstands in 1:12), the sea of glass, and four living creatures. Keep the threefold holy and the distinct order “who was and who is and who is coming.” At 4:11 retain divine Thou/Thy and both verbs “existed and were created.” The association of voices with the throne is preserved without deciding whether they signify speech or another sound. [NA28 Revelation 4](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.4)
- **Chapter 5 (14 units).** Preserve scroll, seven seals, the Lion, Root, and standing slaughtered Lamb. The seven eyes are the antecedent in 5:6, and the second “seven” is represented despite NA28’s brackets. The perfect at 5:7 is idiomatically carried as the completed taking in the narrative. At 5:9 the object of purchase is implicit in the displayed Greek and expressed as “people,” with no first-person pronoun supplied. At 5:10 preserve “them” and “they will reign.” Maintain song repetitions and Thou/Thy for address to the Lamb. [NA28 Revelation 5](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.5)
- **Chapter 6 (17 units).** Preserve four calls of “Come,” colors, repeated sword/slaughter language, choenix and denarius without conversion, pale green horse, Death and Hades, and the plural “Their wrath.” “Master” represents δεσπότης. At 6:8 retain the literal “with death,” leaving the possible Septuagintal sense of pestilence in the review queue. At 6:11 “in number” expresses the inherited interpretation of completion and has no separate Greek noun. Review whether the context means completion of the group or completion of its suffering. [NA28 Revelation 6](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.6)
- **Chapter 7 (17 units).** Preserve the sequence, names, and counts of all twelve tribes, including the bracket of “sealed” at the start and end of the list. Ordinary “you” at 7:14 addresses an elder. Retain the active coming from the great tribulation, literal tent image, the Lamb who shepherds, and the closely connected imagery of the waters of life. Preserve blessing, glory, wisdom, thanksgiving, honor, power, and strength in source order. [NA28 Revelation 7](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.7)

## Chapters 8–14

- **Chapter 8 (13 units).** Keep the seventh seal's half-hour of silence, the seven trumpets, the angel’s censer, the prayers and incense, and the threefold burning refrain. The great star burns like a torch (lampas). Preserve the repeated thirds, the one eagle, and its threefold woe. Keep 'voices' for the sounds at the altar, and use 'trumpet blasts' where the voices are explicitly those of the trumpet. Dative 'with the prayers' in 8:3–4 and the exact relation of the thirds of day/night in 8:12 remain open to closer review. [NA28 Revelation 8](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.8)
- **Chapter 9 (21 units).** Preserve the shaft and abyss, the ascending smoke, the granted authority, five months, the emphatic negation, and the shift to present 'death flees.' The locusts have crowns only by resemblance and the two names stay distinct. Include 'four horns' in 9:13, where the edition prints 'four' in single brackets. The mounted armies number twice ten thousand times ten thousand. Keep the repeated fire, smoke and sulfur and the concrete 'sorcerous potions.' Review the apparatus behind 9:13 'four' and the precise color terms in 9:17. In 9:21 the pharmacological term in context may be understood of sorcery more broadly. [NA28 Revelation 9](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.9)
- **Chapter 10 (11 units).** Distinguish the little scroll in 10:2,9–10 from the scroll in 10:8. Keep the seven thunders, the threefold creation formula, the age formula, and the plural 'they say.' The angel addresses John with ordinary 'your,' and 'no more delay' conveys the contextual sense of 'time will be no longer.' Review the proleptic aorist for the completion of the mystery at 10:7 and the force of 'concerning' in 10:11. [NA28 Revelation 10](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.10)
- **Chapter 11 (19 units).** Preserve the sanctuary distinction, the two time measures, the singular 'dead body' and later plural 'dead bodies,' and the alternation of present and future in 11:10. In 11:3, the existing supplied 'authority' completes Greek 'I will give to My two witnesses' in English. 'Seven thousand people' renders the idiom literally involving 'names of people' in 11:13. Direct thanks to God retains Thee, Thou hast and Thy. The same stem is reflected in wrathful/wrath at 11:18 and destroy/destroy at the close. Review the supplied object at 11:3, 'spirit of life' versus 'breath of life' at 11:11, and the ingressive 'begun to reign' at 11:17. The bracketed connective at 11:2 and article at 11:16 have no separate English effect. [NA28 Revelation 11](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.11)
- **Chapter 12 (18 units).** Keep the present/past shift in 12:4, shepherd rather than a generic ruling verb in 12:5, the three castings in 12:9, the paired 'accuser'/'accuses,' and the repeated 'because of' in 12:11. Keep 'life' singular, the three time expressions and the face of the serpent, and the river and swallowing images. The publisher has a third-person singular verb in 12:18, with the dragon as the natural antecedent. Retain the existing verse number and 'he stood.' Review the subject of 'cast' at 12:4, the compressed syntax in 12:7, the force of 'even unto death,' and the witnesses behind 12:18. The bracketed article in 12:12 has no separate English effect. [NA28 Revelation 12](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.12)
- **Chapter 13 (18 units).** In 13:3 supply 'I saw' from 13:1 to govern the continued accusative. 'Followed the beast in wonder' conveys both the wonder and the following expressed by the Greek. Retain the relation of God's tent to those dwelling in heaven, the passive sword clause and captivity/sword repetitions, breath given to the image, and 666. The close of 13:8 follows source order, with 'from the foundation of the world' in its final position. In 13:8, the final phrase can be taken with the name's inscription or the Lamb's slaughter; compare 17:8. Review the singular/plural ending at 13:1, the optional connective at 13:5, the bracketed conjunction at 13:15 and the possible senses of 'human number' at 13:18. [NA28 Revelation 13](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.13)
- **Chapter 14 (20 units).** Retain 'something like' for the single-bracketed comparative in 14:3, 'seated' in 14:6, repeated 'fallen,' and the distinction between God's fury and wrath in 14:10. Keep 'the faith of Jesus,' the rest and works, and the distinct dry grain harvest and ripe grape harvest. The 14:15 address to the Son of Man figure uses Thy; the 14:18 angel-to-angel address uses 'your.' Retain the optional 'came out' in 14:18. The winepress distance remains in stadia. Review the genitive 'faith of Jesus' (faith in Jesus or Jesus’ faith/faithfulness), possible force of Babylon’s 'fury' in 14:8, and the punctuation of 'from now on' at 14:13. The second bracket in 14:18 is an article with no independent English effect. [NA28 Revelation 14](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.14)

## Chapters 15–22

- **Chapter 15.** Retain King of the nations and the three reasons in the song. Use sea of glass, sanctuary, and fury in continuity with the earlier visions. Preserve the identifying phrase about the plagues with the displayed uncertainty over the article recorded in the stage note. Review the force of standing upon or by the sea in 15:2. [NA28 Revelation 15](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.15)
- **Chapter 16.** Keep the repeated bowl formula, the parallel pouring of blood, the sun and throne, and the distinction between fury and wrath. In 16:16 the singular verb may have the neuter plural spirits as its subject. The inherited plural English reading remains provisional. Use “It is done” at 16:17 and leave the talent unconverted. [NA28 Revelation 16](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.16)
- **Chapter 17.** Use you in the angel’s address to John and retain the beast’s threefold was/is not/will be present formula, the seven and ten groups, and the repeated purpose vocabulary. Harlot and fornication carry the traditional register and preserve the sustained image. Review the Greek agreement in 17:3–4 and retain the attachment of “from the foundation of the world” to the writing in 17:8. [NA28 Revelation 17](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.17)
- **Chapter 18.** Keep fallen twice, drunk in 18:3, the repeated repayments and mixing, the three groups of mourners, and the repeated no-more clauses. Include the displayed bracketed prison clause in 18:2. Thyine names the wood rather than reducing it to a generic fragrance. “Slaves—human lives” retains the movement from bodies to human lives in 18:13. The traditional transliteration amomum remains provisional. [NA28 Revelation 18](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.18)
- **Chapter 19.** Retain the displayed bracketed “our” in 19:6 and “like” in 19:12. The aorist at 19:6 is rendered as taking up the reign. Keep righteous acts, dipped in blood, and the emphatic He Himself in both clauses of 19:15. Shepherd retains the image shared with 2:27 and 12:5. Preserve the repeated flesh in the birds’ summons and distinguish fury from wrath. [NA28 Revelation 19](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.19)
- **Chapter 20.** Render the repeated aorist of living as “came to life” in 20:4–5. Retain the possible distinction between those beheaded and those who did not worship by keeping both clauses. Keep the thousand years, the second death, Death and Hades, and the book of life without adding a chronology or explanation. [NA28 Revelation 20](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.20)
- **Chapter 21.** Keep the plural peoples, the bracketed “their God” and “for,” and the distinct absence of death, mourning, crying, and pain. The plural “They have come to pass” at 21:6 reflects the displayed Greek. Keep all twelve names, gates, foundations, and the ancient units. The gem identifications and exact dimension measured in 21:17 warrant review. “Sanctuary” retains the same rendering of naos across the book. [NA28 Revelation 21](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.21)
- **Chapter 22.** Keep the street and river, the two sides and twelve crops, and the repeated words of prophecy. The inherited attachment of the street phrase to the river in 22:2 remains provisional. Keep the paired adding and taking away, the washing of robes, tree of life, and the closing “with all.” The printed NA28 reading has no second Amen after 22:21. [NA28 Revelation 22](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/REV.22)

The German Bible Society reports that UBS6 incorporates ECM changes to Revelation, Mark and Acts. Those changes require a separate collation with the provisional text. [German Bible Society on UBS6](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/the-sixth-edition-of-the-greek-new-testament-ubs6)
