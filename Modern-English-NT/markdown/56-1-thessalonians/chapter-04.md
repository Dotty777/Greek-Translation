## Text

**4:1** Finally then, brothers, we ask and urge you in the Lord Jesus that, just as you received from us how you must walk and please God—just as you also are walking—you would abound even more.

**4:2** For you know what commands we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

**4:3** For this is God's will, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality,

**4:4** that each one of you know how to acquire his own vessel in sanctification and honor,

**4:5** not in the passion of desire, even as the Gentiles who do not know God,

**4:6** and not to overstep and take advantage of his brother in this matter, because the Lord is an avenger concerning all these things, just as we also told you beforehand and solemnly testified.

**4:7** For God did not call us to impurity, but in sanctification.

**4:8** Consequently, the one who rejects this does not reject man, but God, who also gives His Holy Spirit to you.

**4:9** Now regarding brotherly love, you have no need for anyone to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another;

**4:10** for indeed you are doing this toward all the brothers who are throughout all Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers, to abound even more,

**4:11** and to aspire to live quietly, to attend to your own affairs, and to work with your own hands, just as we commanded you,

**4:12** so that you may walk properly toward those outside and have need of nothing.

**4:13** Now we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, concerning those who are asleep, so that you may not grieve as the rest also do who have no hope.

**4:14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, in the same way God will also bring with Him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus.

**4:15** For this we tell you by a word of the Lord, that we who are living, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will by no means precede those who have fallen asleep.

**4:16** For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a commanding shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first;

**4:17** then we who are living, who remain, will be caught up together with them in clouds to meet the Lord in the air, and thus we will always be with the Lord.

**4:18** So then, encourage one another with these words.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — Greek skeuos ("vessel") is widely debated as referring either to one's own body (with ktasthai meaning "possess" or "control") or to one's own wife (with ktasthai meaning "acquire" in marriage).
- **4:14** — The prepositional phrase "through Jesus" (dia tou Iesou) may modify "those who have fallen asleep" (dying in union with Jesus) or the verb "will bring" (God will bring them through Jesus' agency).
- **4:17** — "To meet" translates Greek eis apantesin, a term commonly used in the Greco-Roman world for citizens going out to welcome an approaching dignitary and escort him back into the city.
