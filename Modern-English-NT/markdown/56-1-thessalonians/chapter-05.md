## Text

**5:1** Now regarding the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no need for anything to be written to you,

**5:2** for you yourselves know thoroughly that the day of the Lord comes just like a thief in the night.

**5:3** Whenever they say, "Peace and security," then sudden destruction comes upon them just like birth pains upon a pregnant woman, and they will by no means escape.

**5:4** But you, brothers, are not in darkness, so that the day should overtake you like a thief;

**5:5** for all of you are sons of light and sons of day; we are neither of night nor of darkness.

**5:6** So then, let us not sleep as the rest do, but let us stay awake and be sober.

**5:7** For those who sleep, sleep by night, and those who get drunk are drunk by night.

**5:8** But since we belong to the day, let us be sober, putting on a breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.

**5:9** For God did not appoint us to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

**5:10** who died on our behalf, so that whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with Him.

**5:11** Therefore, encourage one another and build each other up, just as indeed you are doing.

**5:12** Now we ask you, brothers, to acknowledge those who labor among you and preside over you in the Lord and admonish you,

**5:13** and to regard them most highly in love on account of their work. Live at peace among yourselves.

**5:14** And we urge you, brothers: admonish the disorderly, comfort the fainthearted, support the weak, be patient toward all.

**5:15** See to it that no one returns evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good, both toward one another and toward all.

**5:16** Always rejoice,

**5:17** pray unceasingly,

**5:18** in everything give thanks; for this is God's will in Christ Jesus for you.

**5:19** Do not extinguish the Spirit,

**5:20** do not treat prophecies with contempt;

**5:21** but test all things; hold fast to what is good,

**5:22** abstain from every kind of evil.

**5:23** Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you thoroughly, and may your spirit and soul and body be preserved whole and blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**5:24** Faithful is the One who calls you, who will also do it.

**5:25** Brothers, pray on our behalf.

**5:26** Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.

**5:27** I put you under oath by the Lord that this letter be read to all the brothers.

**5:28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

## Translator Notes

- **5:4** — The Greek text reads the nominative kleptes ("like a thief"), matching v. 2; some manuscripts read the accusative plural kleptas ("like thieves").
- **5:10** — Here "awake" and "asleep" shift from their moral sense in vv. 6-7 to serve as metaphors for being alive or dead at Christ's return.
- **5:14** — Greek ataktous ("the disorderly") originally denoted soldiers out of rank and here describes members disrupting the community, particularly those refusing to work (cf. 4:11; 2 Thess. 3:6-11).
- **5:22** — Greek eidos ("kind" or "form") may denote either a category/species ("every kind of evil") or a visible appearance ("every appearance of evil").
