## Text

**17:1** Now He said to His disciples, "It is impossible for stumbling blocks not to come, yet woe to the one through whom they come!

**17:2** It would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck and he were cast into the sea than for him to cause one of these little ones to stumble.

**17:3** Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him.

**17:4** And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times turns back to you saying, 'I repent,' you shall forgive him."

**17:5** And the apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith."

**17:6** And the Lord said, "If you have faith like a mustard seed, you would say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and be planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.

**17:7** Now which of you, having a slave plowing or tending sheep, will say to him when he comes in from the field, 'Come forward at once and recline at table,'

**17:8** and will not instead say to him, 'Prepare something for me to eat for supper, and fasten your belt and serve me while I eat and drink, and after that you yourself will eat and drink'?

**17:9** He does not thank the slave because he did the things that were commanded, does he?

**17:10** So you also, when you have done all the things commanded you, say, 'We are unworthy slaves; we have done what we were obligated to do.'"

**17:11** And it happened while He was traveling to Jerusalem that He was passing between Samaria and Galilee.

**17:12** And as He was entering a certain village, ten leprous men met Him, who stood at a distance,

**17:13** and they lifted up their voices, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"

**17:14** And when He saw them, He said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." And it happened that as they were going, they were cleansed.

**17:15** Now one of them, when he saw that he had been healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice,

**17:16** and he fell on his face at His feet, giving thanks to Him. And he was a Samaritan.

**17:17** Then Jesus answered and said, "Were not the ten cleansed? Yet where are the nine?

**17:18** Were none found who turned back to give glory to God except this foreigner?"

**17:19** And He said to him, "Get up and go; your faith has saved you."

**17:20** Now having been questioned by the Pharisees as to when the kingdom of God is coming, He answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation,

**17:21** nor will they say, 'Look, here!' or, 'There!' For look, the kingdom of God is in your midst."

**17:22** And He said to the disciples, "Days will come when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

**17:23** And they will say to you, 'Look, there!' or, 'Look, here!' Do not go off, nor pursue them.

**17:24** For just as the lightning, flashing from one side under heaven, shines to the other under heaven, so will the Son of Man be in His day.

**17:25** But first He must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

**17:26** And just as it happened in the days of Noah, so it will also be in the days of the Son of Man:

**17:27** they were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were being given in marriage, until the day Noah entered into the ark, and the flood came and destroyed all of them.

**17:28** Likewise, just as it happened in the days of Lot: they were eating, they were drinking, they were buying, they were selling, they were planting, they were building;

**17:29** but on the day Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from heaven and destroyed all of them.

**17:30** In the very same way it will be on the day the Son of Man is revealed.

**17:31** On that day, let the one who is on the housetop, with his belongings in the house, not come down to take them away; and likewise let the one in the field not turn back to what is behind.

**17:32** Remember Lot's wife.

**17:33** Whoever seeks to preserve his life will lose it, and whoever loses it will keep it alive.

**17:34** I tell you, on that night there will be two in one bed; one will be taken and the other will be left.

**17:35** There will be two women grinding together; one will be taken, and the other will be left."

**17:37** And answering, they said to Him, "Where, Lord?" And He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures will also be gathered together."

## Translator Notes

- **17:10** — "Unworthy" translates Greek achreioi, which literally means "useless" or "unprofitable," referring here to slaves who have earned no special merit beyond their required duty.
- **17:11** — The Greek phrase dia meson Samareias kai Galilaias can be understood either as "between Samaria and Galilee" (along their border) or "through the middle of Samaria and Galilee."
- **17:21** — The Greek phrase entos hymon is debated and can mean either "in your midst" (among you) or "within you."
- **17:35** — Verse 36 ("Two men will be in the field; one will be taken and the other will be left") is absent from the earliest and best Greek manuscripts and is omitted in the critical text.
- **17:37** — The Greek word aetoi can mean either "vultures" or "eagles"; "vultures" fits the proverb's imagery of carrion birds gathering around a body (soma).
