## Text

**22:1** Now the Feast of Unleavened Bread, which is called the Passover, was drawing near.

**22:2** And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might do away with Him, for they feared the people.

**22:3** Now Satan entered into Judas, the one called Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve.

**22:4** And he went away and conferred with the chief priests and captains on how he might betray Him to them.

**22:5** And they rejoiced and agreed to give him silver.

**22:6** And he consented, and was seeking an opportunity to betray Him to them apart from a crowd.

**22:7** Now the day of Unleavened Bread came, on which the Passover had to be sacrificed.

**22:8** And He sent Peter and John, saying, "Go and prepare the Passover for us, so that we may eat."

**22:9** Then they said to Him, "Where do You want us to prepare it?"

**22:10** And He said to them, "Look, when you have entered the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him into the house that he enters.

**22:11** And you will say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest room where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?"'

**22:12** And he will show you a large upper room that has been furnished; prepare it there."

**22:13** So they departed and found it just as He had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

**22:14** And when the hour arrived, He reclined at table, and the apostles with Him.

**22:15** And He said to them, "With longing I have longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer;

**22:16** for I say to you that I will certainly not eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

**22:17** And having received a cup and given thanks, He said, "Take this and divide it among yourselves;

**22:18** for I say to you, from now on I will certainly not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

**22:19** And having taken bread and given thanks, He broke it and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, which is being given for you; do this in remembrance of Me."

**22:20** And likewise the cup after they had dined, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is being poured out for you.

**22:21** Yet look, the hand of the one betraying Me is with Me on the table.

**22:22** For the Son of Man indeed goes according to what has been determined, yet woe to that man through whom He is betrayed."

**22:23** And they began to discuss among themselves which of them it might be who was about to do this.

**22:24** Now a dispute also arose among them as to which of them was considered to be the greatest.

**22:25** And He said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them, and those who hold authority over them are called Benefactors.

**22:26** But not so with you; rather, let the greatest among you become like the youngest, and the one who leads like the one who serves.

**22:27** For who is greater, the one who reclines at table or the one who serves? Is it not the one who reclines at table? Yet I am in your midst as the one who serves.

**22:28** Now you are the ones who have remained with Me in My trials;

**22:29** and I confer on you, just as My Father conferred a kingdom on Me,

**22:30** so that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

**22:31** Simon, Simon, look, Satan has demanded to have you, to sift you like wheat;

**22:32** but I have pleaded for you that your faith may not fail; and you, once you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."

**22:33** And he said to Him, "Lord, with You I am prepared to go both into prison and to death."

**22:34** And He said, "I tell you, Peter, a rooster will not crow today until you have three times denied knowing Me."

**22:35** And He said to them, "When I sent you out without a moneybag or knapsack or sandals, did you lack anything?" And they said, "Nothing."

**22:36** Then He said to them, "But now, let the one who has a moneybag take it, and likewise a knapsack; and let the one who has none sell his cloak and buy a sword.

**22:37** For I say to you that this which is written must be fulfilled in Me: 'And He was counted with the lawless.' For indeed what concerns Me has its fulfillment."

**22:38** And they said, "Lord, see, here are two swords." And He said to them, "That is enough."

**22:39** And going out, He proceeded according to custom to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples also followed Him.

**22:40** And having arrived at the place, He said to them, "Pray not to enter into temptation."

**22:41** And He Himself withdrew from them about a stone's throw, and kneeling down, He was praying,

**22:42** saying, "Father, if You are willing, take this cup away from Me; yet let not My will, but Yours, be done."

**22:43** Then an angel from heaven appeared to Him, strengthening Him.

**22:44** And coming into agony, He was praying more fervently, and His sweat became like thick drops of blood falling onto the ground.

**22:45** And rising from prayer, He came to the disciples and found them sleeping from sorrow,

**22:46** and He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Rise up and pray, so that you will not enter into temptation."

**22:47** While He was still speaking, look, there was a crowd, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was going ahead of them; and he drew near to Jesus to kiss Him.

**22:48** Then Jesus said to him, "Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**22:49** And when those around Him saw what was about to happen, they said, "Lord, should we strike with a sword?"

**22:50** And a certain one of them struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his right ear.

**22:51** But Jesus answered and said, "Leave it at this!" And touching the ear, He healed him.

**22:52** Then Jesus said to the chief priests and captains of the temple and elders who had come against Him, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs?

**22:53** While I was with you day by day in the temple, you did not stretch out your hands against Me. But this is your hour, and the authority of darkness."

**22:54** Now having seized Him, they led Him away and brought Him into the house of the high priest; and Peter was following at a distance.

**22:55** And when they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter was sitting among them.

**22:56** And a certain servant girl, seeing him sitting by the light and gazing intently at him, said, "This man also was with Him."

**22:57** But he denied it, saying, "I do not know Him, woman."

**22:58** And after a little while, another man saw him and said, "You also are one of them." But Peter said, "Man, I am not."

**22:59** And after an interval of about an hour, another man kept insisting, saying, "In truth, this man also was with Him, for he indeed is a Galilean."

**22:60** But Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

**22:61** And turning, the Lord looked straight at Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how He had said to him, "Before a rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times."

**22:62** And going outside, he wept bitterly.

**22:63** Now the men who were holding Him were mocking Him and beating Him,

**22:64** and having blindfolded Him, they kept questioning Him, saying, "Prophesy! Who is the one who struck You?"

**22:65** And blaspheming, they were saying many other things against Him.

**22:66** And when day came, the assembly of elders of the people was gathered together, both chief priests and scribes, and they led Him away into their Sanhedrin,

**22:67** saying, "If You are the Christ, tell us." But He said to them, "If I tell you, you will certainly not believe;

**22:68** and if I ask a question, you will certainly not answer.

**22:69** Yet from now on, the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of God."

**22:70** Then they all said, "Are You then the Son of God?" And He said to them, "You yourselves say that I am."

**22:71** And they said, "Why do we still have need of testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from His mouth."

## Translator Notes

- **22:15** — "With longing I have longed" literally reflects the Greek cognate-dative idiom (epithymia epethymesa), a Semitic construction expressing intense desire.
- **22:19** — The words from "which is being given for you" through the end of verse 20 are omitted in Codex Bezae and some Old Latin witnesses, but are strongly supported by the earliest Greek manuscripts, including P75, Sinaiticus, and Vaticanus.
- **22:31** — In Greek, "you" in verse 31 (hymas) is plural, encompassing all the disciples, whereas "you" in verse 32 (sou, sy) shifts to the singular, addressing Simon Peter individually.
- **22:36** — "The one who has none" (ho me echon) has no stated object in Greek; it may refer either to lacking a moneybag and knapsack or, anticipating the end of the clause, to lacking a sword.
- **22:43** — Verses 43–44 are omitted by several early manuscripts (including P75, Vaticanus, and Alexandrinus), while attested by Sinaiticus, Codex Bezae, early patristic writers, and the majority of later witnesses.
- **22:51** — "Leave it at this!" (eate heos toutou) is variously understood either as stopping the disciples ("No more of this!") or as addressing the arresting group ("Permit even this," so He might heal the slave's ear).
- **22:70** — "You yourselves say that I am" (hymeis legete hoti ego eimi) is a reserved yet affirmative idiom in Greek, acknowledging the claim while leaving the phrasing to the questioners.
