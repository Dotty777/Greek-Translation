## Text

**1:1** Since many have undertaken to draw up a narrative concerning the events that have been fulfilled among us,

**1:2** just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and attendants of the word handed them down to us,

**1:3** it seemed good to me also, having traced everything accurately from the beginning, to write to you in sequence, most excellent Theophilus,

**1:4** so that you may know the certainty regarding the accounts about which you were instructed.

**1:5** In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a certain priest named Zechariah, from the division of Abijah; and he had a wife from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.

**1:6** Now both of them were righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord.

**1:7** And they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both of them were advanced in their days.

**1:8** Now it happened, while he was serving as priest in the order of his division before God,

**1:9** according to the custom of the priesthood, it fell to him by lot to burn incense upon entering the sanctuary of the Lord,

**1:10** and the entire multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of incense.

**1:11** Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.

**1:12** And Zechariah was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him.

**1:13** But the angel said to him, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your petition has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear a son for you, and you will call his name John.

**1:14** And there will be joy and gladness for you, and many will rejoice at his birth.

**1:15** For he will be great before the Lord, and he will by no means drink wine or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother's womb,

**1:16** and he will turn many of the sons of Israel back to the Lord their God.

**1:17** And he himself will go before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to children and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to prepare for the Lord a people made ready."

**1:18** And Zechariah said to the angel, "How will I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her days."

**1:19** And the angel answered and said to him, "I am Gabriel, who stands before God, and I was sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.

**1:20** And look, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their proper time."

**1:21** And the people were waiting for Zechariah, and they were marveling at his lingering in the sanctuary.

**1:22** But when he came out, he was unable to speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary; and he kept making signs to them, and remained mute.

**1:23** And it happened, when the days of his service were completed, that he departed to his house.

**1:24** Now after these days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she kept herself hidden for five months, saying,

**1:25** "In this way the Lord has acted for me in the days when He looked with favor to take away my disgrace among people."

**1:26** Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,

**1:27** to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary.

**1:28** And having come in to her, the angel said, "Greetings, you who have been shown favor; the Lord is with you."

**1:29** But she was greatly troubled at this statement and kept pondering what kind of greeting this might be.

**1:30** And the angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

**1:31** And look, you will conceive in your womb and give birth to a son, and you will call His name Jesus.

**1:32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High, and the Lord God will give to Him the throne of His father David,

**1:33** and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and there will be no end to His kingdom."

**1:34** Then Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I do not know a man?"

**1:35** And the angel answered and said to her, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; therefore also the one to be born will be called holy, the Son of God.

**1:36** And look, your relative Elizabeth has herself also conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren.

**1:37** For no word from God will be impossible."

**1:38** And Mary said, "Here is the servant of the Lord; may it happen to me according to your word." And the angel departed from her.

**1:39** Now in these days Mary arose and went with haste into the hill country, to a city of Judah,

**1:40** and she entered the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.

**1:41** And it happened, when Elizabeth heard the greeting of Mary, that the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit,

**1:42** and she cried out with a loud voice and said, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb.

**1:43** And why has this happened to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

**1:44** For look, when the sound of your greeting came into my ears, the baby in my womb leaped with gladness.

**1:45** And blessed is she who believed that there will be a fulfillment of the things spoken to her from the Lord."

**1:46** And Mary said, "My soul extols the Lord,

**1:47** and my spirit has exulted in God my Savior,

**1:48** because He has looked upon the humble condition of His servant; for look, from now on all generations will call me blessed.

**1:49** For the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is His name,

**1:50** and His mercy is to generations and generations for those who fear Him.

**1:51** He has exercised might with His arm; He has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their heart.

**1:52** He has brought down rulers from thrones and lifted up the humble;

**1:53** He has filled the hungry with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty.

**1:54** He has come to the aid of His servant Israel, in remembrance of mercy,

**1:55** just as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his offspring forever."

**1:56** And Mary stayed with her about three months and returned to her house.

**1:57** Now the time was fulfilled for Elizabeth to give birth, and she bore a son.

**1:58** And her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy toward her, and they were rejoicing with her.

**1:59** And it happened on the eighth day that they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to call him Zechariah, after the name of his father.

**1:60** And his mother answered and said, "No; rather, he will be called John."

**1:61** And they said to her, "There is no one from your relatives who is called by this name."

**1:62** Then they were making signs to his father as to what he would want him to be called.

**1:63** And asking for a writing tablet, he wrote, saying, "John is his name." And they all marveled.

**1:64** And immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue freed, and he began speaking, blessing God.

**1:65** And fear came upon all those living around them, and throughout the entire hill country of Judea all these matters were being talked about,

**1:66** and all who heard stored them in their hearts, saying, "What then will this child be?" For indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.

**1:67** And his father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,

**1:68** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, because He has visited and accomplished redemption for His people,

**1:69** and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of His servant David,

**1:70** just as He spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets from ancient times:

**1:71** salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

**1:72** to show mercy toward our fathers and to remember His holy covenant,

**1:73** the oath that He swore to our father Abraham, to grant to us,

**1:74** having been rescued from the hand of enemies, to serve Him without fear

**1:75** in holiness and righteousness before Him all our days.

**1:76** And you also, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High; for you will go before the Lord to prepare His ways,

**1:77** to give knowledge of salvation to His people in the forgiveness of their sins,

**1:78** because of the tender mercy of our God, by which the dawn from on high will visit us,

**1:79** to shine upon those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace."

**1:80** Now the child kept growing and becoming strong in spirit, and he was in the deserts until the day of his public appearance to Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **1:28** — Greek κεχαριτωμένη is a perfect passive participle meaning "one who has been shown favor" or "graced," emphasizing God's initiative in bestowing grace.
- **1:35** — Following the punctuation of the Greek text, ἅγιον ("holy") serves as the predicate of κληθήσεται ("will be called") with "the Son of God" in apposition; alternatively, τὸ γεννώμενον ἅγιον may be taken together as "the holy child to be born will be called the Son of God."
- **1:37** — Greek ῥῆμα means "word" or "statement" (echoed in Mary's "your word" in v. 38), though in Septuagint idiom it can also mean "thing" or "matter"; παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ literally denotes what comes "from God."
- **1:45** — Greek ὅτι may introduce the content of Mary's belief ("that there will be a fulfillment") or give the reason for her blessedness ("for there will be a fulfillment").
- **1:74** — Following the Greek text (ἐκ χειρὸς ἐχθρῶν), no possessive "our" appears before "enemies," whereas some manuscripts add ἡμῶν ("our").
- **1:78** — Greek ἀνατολή denotes "dawn" or "sunrise" and also renders the messianic title "Branch" in the Septuagint (Zech 3:8; 6:12); the future tense ἐπισκέψεται ("will visit") is translated here rather than the aorist variant ἐπεσκέψατο ("has visited").
