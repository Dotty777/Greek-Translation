## Text

**8:1** And it happened afterward that He was journeying through each city and village, proclaiming and announcing the good news of the kingdom of God, and the twelve were with Him,

**8:2** along with certain women who had been healed from evil spirits and illnesses: Mary, who was called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out,

**8:3** and Joanna, wife of Chuza, Herod's manager, and Susanna, and many other women, who were ministering to them out of their own possessions.

**8:4** Now when a large crowd was gathering together and people from city after city were coming to Him, He spoke by way of a parable:

**8:5** "The sower went out to sow his seed. And as he was sowing, some fell beside the road, and it was trampled underfoot, and the birds of the sky devoured it.

**8:6** And other seed fell down onto the rock, and when it sprouted, it dried up because it had no moisture.

**8:7** And other seed fell in the midst of the thorns, and the thorns grew up together with it and choked it.

**8:8** And other seed fell into the good soil, and when it sprouted, it produced fruit a hundredfold." As He said these things, He called out, "The one who has ears to hear, let him hear."

**8:9** Now His disciples were questioning Him as to what this parable might be.

**8:10** And He said, "To you it has been granted to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to the rest it is in parables, so that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.

**8:11** Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God.

**8:12** And those beside the road are the ones who have heard; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, so that they may not believe and be saved.

**8:13** And those on the rock are the ones who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; and these have no root; they believe for a time, and in a time of testing they fall away.

**8:14** And that which fell into the thorns, these are the ones who have heard, and as they go along they are choked by anxieties and wealth and pleasures of life, and they do not bring fruit to maturity.

**8:15** And that in the good soil, these are the ones who, having heard the word with a noble and good heart, hold it fast and bear fruit with endurance.

**8:16** Now no one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel or places it underneath a bed, but places it on a lampstand, so that those who enter may see the light.

**8:17** For nothing is hidden that will not become manifest, nor anything concealed that will not be made known and come into the open.

**8:18** Watch, therefore, how you hear; for whoever has, to him it will be given, and whoever does not have, even what he thinks he has will be taken away from him."

**8:19** Now His mother and His brothers came to Him, and they were unable to get to Him because of the crowd.

**8:20** And it was reported to Him, "Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, wishing to see You."

**8:21** And He answered and said to them, "My mother and My brothers are these who hear the word of God and do it."

**8:22** Now it happened on one of those days that He stepped into a boat along with His disciples, and He said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side of the lake." And they launched out.

**8:23** And while they were sailing, He fell asleep. And a squall of wind came down onto the lake, and they were filling with water and were in danger.

**8:24** And they came to Him and woke Him up, saying, "Master, Master, we are perishing!" And having woken up, He rebuked the wind and the surging waves of the water, and they ceased, and there came a calm.

**8:25** And He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And struck with fear, they marveled, saying to one another, "Who then is this, that He commands even the winds and the water, and they obey Him?"

**8:26** And they sailed to the region of the Gerasenes, which is directly opposite Galilee.

**8:27** And as He stepped out onto the land, a certain man from the city who had demons met Him; and for a long time he had not worn clothing, and he was not staying in a house, but in the tombs.

**8:28** And when he saw Jesus, he cried out, fell down before Him, and said with a loud voice, "What is there between me and You, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg You, do not torment me!"

**8:29** For He had commanded the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For many times it had seized him, and though he was kept under guard, bound with chains and shackles, he would break the bonds apart and be driven by the demon into the deserted places.

**8:30** And Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" And he answered, "Legion," because many demons had entered into him.

**8:31** And they were imploring Him not to command them to go away into the abyss.

**8:32** Now there was a large herd of pigs feeding there on the mountain; and they implored Him to permit them to enter into them, and He permitted them.

**8:33** And the demons came out from the man and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and was drowned.

**8:34** And when the herdsmen saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the city and in the countryside.

**8:35** And people went out to see what had happened, and they came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed and in his right mind; and they became afraid.

**8:36** And those who had witnessed it reported to them how the man who had been demon-possessed had been made well.

**8:37** And the entire multitude of the surrounding region of the Gerasenes asked Him to depart from them, because they were gripped with great fear; so He got into a boat and returned.

**8:38** Now the man from whom the demons had gone out was begging Him that he might be with Him, but He sent him away, saying,

**8:39** "Return to your house, and recount all that God has done for you." And he went away, proclaiming throughout the entire city all that Jesus had done for him.

**8:40** Now as Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed Him, for they were all waiting expectantly for Him.

**8:41** And look, a man came whose name was Jairus, and he was a ruler of the synagogue; and falling at the feet of Jesus, he implored Him to come into his house,

**8:42** because he had an only daughter, about twelve years old, and she was dying. Now as He was going, the crowds were crushing in around Him.

**8:43** And a woman who had a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent her entire livelihood on physicians and could not be healed by anyone,

**8:44** approached from behind and touched the fringe of His garment, and immediately her flow of blood stopped.

**8:45** And Jesus said, "Who is the one who touched Me?" And as all were denying it, Peter said, "Master, the crowds are hemming You in and pressing against You."

**8:46** But Jesus said, "Someone touched Me, for I perceived that power has gone out from Me."

**8:47** And when the woman saw that she had not gone unnoticed, she came trembling and fell down before Him, and declared in the presence of all the people the reason why she had touched Him and how she had been healed immediately.

**8:48** And He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has made you well; go in peace."

**8:49** While He was still speaking, someone came from the synagogue ruler's house, saying, "Your daughter has died; do not trouble the Teacher any longer."

**8:50** But Jesus, hearing this, answered him, "Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be made well."

**8:51** And when He came to the house, He did not allow anyone to enter with Him except Peter and John and James, and the father and the mother of the child.

**8:52** Now everyone was weeping and mourning for her, but He said, "Stop weeping, for she has not died, but is sleeping."

**8:53** And they were laughing at Him, knowing that she had died.

**8:54** But He took hold of her hand and called out, saying, "Child, rise."

**8:55** And her spirit returned, and she got up immediately; and He directed that something be given to her to eat.

**8:56** And her parents were amazed, but He charged them to tell no one what had happened.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — The critical text reads "them" (referring to Jesus and the twelve), while some manuscripts read "Him."
- **8:26** — Manuscripts vary here and in 8:37 among "Gerasenes," "Gadarenes," and "Gergesenes"; the critical text reads "Gerasenes."
- **8:28** — Literally, "What to me and to You?", a Greek and Semitic idiom expressing resistance or asking what business one party has with another.
- **8:43** — The clause "who had spent her entire livelihood on physicians" is present in the critical text, though omitted by a few early manuscripts.
- **8:48** — Here, as in 8:36 and 8:50, the Greek verb sozo ("to save," cf. 8:12) is rendered "made well," reflecting its dual sense of physical healing and deliverance.
