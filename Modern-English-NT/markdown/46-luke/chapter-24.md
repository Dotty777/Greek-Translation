## Text

**24:1** Now on the first day of the week, at very early dawn, they came to the tomb, bringing the spices that they had prepared.

**24:2** And they found that the stone had been rolled away from the tomb,

**24:3** and when they went in, they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.

**24:4** And it happened, while they were perplexed about this, look, two men stood beside them in dazzling clothing.

**24:5** And as they became terrified and bowed their faces to the ground, they said to them, "Why are you seeking the Living One among the dead?

**24:6** He is not here, but has been raised. Remember how He spoke to you while He was still in Galilee,

**24:7** saying that the Son of Man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and on the third day rise."

**24:8** And they remembered His words,

**24:9** and having returned from the tomb, they reported all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.

**24:10** Now they were Mary Magdalene and Joanna and Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them; they were telling these things to the apostles.

**24:11** And these words appeared before them like nonsense, and they disbelieved them.

**24:12** But Peter rose and ran to the tomb; and stooping down to look in, he saw the linen cloths alone; and he went away to his home, marveling at what had happened.

**24:13** And look, two of them on that very day were traveling to a village sixty stadia distant from Jerusalem, whose name was Emmaus,

**24:14** and they were conversing with one another about all these things that had taken place.

**24:15** And it happened, while they were conversing and discussing, that Jesus Himself drew near and was walking along with them,

**24:16** but their eyes were restrained from recognizing Him.

**24:17** And He said to them, "What are these words that you are exchanging with one another as you walk?" And they stood still, looking downcast.

**24:18** And one named Cleopas answered and said to Him, "Are You the only visitor staying in Jerusalem who has not known the things that happened in it in these days?"

**24:19** And He said to them, "What things?" And they said to Him, "The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who was a man, a prophet, mighty in deed and word before God and all the people,

**24:20** and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered Him to a sentence of death and crucified Him.

**24:21** But we were hoping that He was the One about to redeem Israel; yet indeed, besides all these things, this is the third day since these things happened.

**24:22** Moreover, certain women from among us astonished us; having been at the tomb early in the morning

**24:23** and not finding His body, they came saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that He is alive.

**24:24** And some of those with us went to the tomb and found it just as the women also had said, but Him they did not see."

**24:25** And He said to them, "O foolish ones and slow in heart to believe in all that the prophets spoke!

**24:26** Did not the Christ have to suffer these things and enter into His glory?"

**24:27** And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, He interpreted for them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself.

**24:28** And they drew near to the village where they were going, and He acted as though He were going farther.

**24:29** And they strongly urged Him, saying, "Stay with us, because it is toward evening and the day has already declined." And He went in to stay with them.

**24:30** And it happened, while He reclined with them, that taking the bread He said a blessing, and having broken it, He began giving it to them;

**24:31** and their eyes were opened, and they recognized Him; and He vanished from them.

**24:32** And they said to one another, "Was not our heart burning within us while He was speaking to us on the road, while He was opening the Scriptures to us?"

**24:33** And rising up that very hour, they returned to Jerusalem and found the eleven and those with them gathered together,

**24:34** saying, "Truly the Lord has been raised and has appeared to Simon."

**24:35** And they recounted the events on the road, and how He was made known to them in the breaking of the bread.

**24:36** Now while they were speaking these things, He Himself stood in their midst and said to them, "Peace to you."

**24:37** But startled and becoming terrified, they thought they were seeing a spirit.

**24:38** And He said to them, "Why are you troubled, and why do questionings arise in your heart?

**24:39** See My hands and My feet, that it is I Myself; touch Me and see, because a spirit does not have flesh and bones just as you see Me having."

**24:40** And having said this, He showed them His hands and His feet.

**24:41** And while they were still disbelieving from joy and marveling, He said to them, "Do you have anything to eat here?"

**24:42** And they gave Him a piece of broiled fish;

**24:43** and taking it, He ate before them.

**24:44** Now He said to them, "These are My words that I spoke to you while I was still with you, that all things written in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms concerning Me must be fulfilled."

**24:45** Then He opened their mind to understand the Scriptures, and He said to them,

**24:46** "Thus it is written, that the Christ would suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,

**24:47** and that repentance for forgiveness of sins would be proclaimed in His name to all the nations—beginning from Jerusalem;

**24:48** you are witnesses of these things.

**24:49** And look, I am sending the promise of My Father upon you; but you remain in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."

**24:50** And He led them out as far as Bethany, and lifting up His hands, He blessed them.

**24:51** And it happened, while He was blessing them, that He parted from them and was carried up into heaven.

**24:52** And after worshiping Him, they returned to Jerusalem with great joy,

**24:53** and they were continually in the temple, blessing God.

## Translator Notes

- **24:12** — This verse is absent from Codex Bezae and some Old Latin witnesses (a Western non-interpolation), though strongly attested in P75, Sinaiticus, and Vaticanus. Following the Greek punctuation, the idiom 'πρὸς ἑαυτόν' modifies 'went away' ('to his home'), though it can also be construed with 'marveling' ('marveling to himself').
- **24:13** — Sixty stadia is approximately seven miles (about eleven kilometers); a few later witnesses read 'one hundred sixty stadia'.
- **24:47** — The participle 'beginning' (ἀρξάμενοι) is nominative masculine plural, linking grammatically with 'you' in verse 48 rather than with the accusative 'repentance'.
- **24:51** — The clause 'and was carried up into heaven' (along with 'after worshiping Him' in verse 52) is absent from a few early witnesses such as original Sinaiticus and Codex Bezae, but is widely supported by P75, Vaticanus, and the majority of manuscripts.
