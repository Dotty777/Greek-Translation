## Text

**23:1** And the entire multitude of them rose up and brought Him before Pilate.

**23:2** And they began to accuse Him, saying, "We found this man subverting our nation and forbidding the paying of taxes to Caesar, and saying that He Himself is Christ, a king."

**23:3** Now Pilate questioned Him, saying, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And He answered him and said, "You say so."

**23:4** Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no basis for a charge in this man."

**23:5** But they grew more insistent, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning even from Galilee as far as here."

**23:6** When Pilate heard this, he asked whether the man was a Galilean,

**23:7** and when he learned that He was under Herod's jurisdiction, he sent Him up to Herod, who himself was also in Jerusalem in those days.

**23:8** Now when Herod saw Jesus, he was exceedingly glad, for he had been desiring for a long time to see Him because of hearing about Him, and he was hoping to see some sign done by Him.

**23:9** And he kept questioning Him with many words, yet He answered him nothing.

**23:10** And the chief priests and the scribes were standing there, vehemently accusing Him.

**23:11** And Herod as well, along with his soldiers, treated Him with contempt and mocked Him, and having dressed Him in a splendid garment, he sent Him back to Pilate.

**23:12** And both Herod and Pilate became friends with one another on that very day, for previously they had been at enmity with each other.

**23:13** Then Pilate, having called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,

**23:14** said to them, "You brought this man to me as one leading the people astray, and look, having examined Him before you, I found no basis for a charge in this man regarding the things of which you accuse Him.

**23:15** Nor did Herod either, for he sent Him back to us; and look, nothing deserving of death has been done by Him.

**23:16** Therefore I will punish Him and release Him."

**23:18** But they all cried out together, saying, "Away with this man, and release Barabbas to us!"

**23:19** He was one who had been thrown into prison because of a certain insurrection that had occurred in the city, and for murder.

**23:20** Again Pilate addressed them, wanting to release Jesus.

**23:21** But they kept shouting out, saying, "Crucify, crucify Him!"

**23:22** And a third time he said to them, "Why, what evil has this man done? I found no basis for death in Him. Therefore I will punish Him and release Him."

**23:23** But they kept pressing with loud voices, demanding that He be crucified, and their voices prevailed.

**23:24** And Pilate ruled that their demand be granted.

**23:25** And he released the one who had been thrown into prison for insurrection and murder, whom they were demanding, while Jesus he handed over to their will.

**23:26** And as they led Him away, taking hold of a certain Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from the countryside, they laid the cross on him to carry behind Jesus.

**23:27** And a large multitude of the people was following Him, and of women who were mourning and lamenting for Him.

**23:28** But turning to them, Jesus said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me; rather, weep for yourselves and for your children,

**23:29** for look, days are coming in which they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that did not give birth, and the breasts that did not nurse.'

**23:30** Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall on us,' and to the hills, 'Cover us,'

**23:31** for if they do these things with the green wood, what will happen with the dry?"

**23:32** And two others also, who were criminals, were being led out with Him to be put to death.

**23:33** And when they arrived at the place called Skull, there they crucified Him and the criminals, one on the right and one on the left.

**23:34** And Jesus was saying, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." And dividing His garments among themselves, they cast lots.

**23:35** And the people stood watching. And the rulers also were sneering, saying, "He saved others; let Him save Himself if this is the Christ of God, the Chosen One."

**23:36** And the soldiers also mocked Him, approaching and offering Him sour wine,

**23:37** and saying, "If You are the King of the Jews, save Yourself!"

**23:38** Now there was also an inscription over Him: "This is the King of the Jews."

**23:39** And one of the criminals who had been hanged was reviling Him, saying, "Are You not the Christ? Save Yourself and us!"

**23:40** But answering, the other rebuked him and said, "Do you not even fear God, since you are under the same condemnation?

**23:41** And we indeed justly, for we are receiving what we deserve for what we did; but this man has done nothing wrong."

**23:42** And he was saying, "Jesus, remember me when You come in Your kingdom."

**23:43** And He said to him, "Truly I say to you, today you will be with Me in Paradise."

**23:44** Now it was already about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour,

**23:45** as the sun failed, and the curtain of the sanctuary was torn down the middle.

**23:46** And crying out with a loud voice, Jesus said, "Father, into Your hands I entrust My spirit." And having said this, He breathed His last.

**23:47** Now when the centurion saw what had happened, he was glorifying God, saying, "Certainly, this man was righteous."

**23:48** And all the crowds who had gathered together for this spectacle, having observed the things that happened, were turning back, beating their breasts.

**23:49** And all His acquaintances stood at a distance, as well as the women who had followed with Him from Galilee, watching these things.

**23:50** And now there was a man named Joseph, who was a council member, a good and righteous man—

**23:51** this man had not consented to their plan and action—from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who was waiting for the kingdom of God;

**23:52** this man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus,

**23:53** and having taken it down, he wrapped it in a linen cloth and laid Him in a rock-hewn tomb where no one had yet been laid.

**23:54** And it was the day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was dawning.

**23:55** Now the women who had come with Him from Galilee followed closely behind, and they observed the tomb and how His body was laid.

**23:56** Then returning, they prepared spices and perfumes. And on the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.

## Translator Notes

- **23:18** — Verse 17 (mentioning an obligation to release one prisoner at the festival) is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts; the Saints Greek Critical Text prints it within double brackets ⟦ ⟧.
- **23:34** — Jesus' prayer for forgiveness ("Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing") is absent from several early manuscripts (including P75, B, and D*) but is well attested in other early witnesses and patristic citations, and stands in this Greek text.
- **23:43** — The Greek text has no punctuation around "today" (semeron); placing the comma before "today" matches Luke's regular idiom after "Truly I say to you" rather than attaching "today" to "I say."
- **23:45** — The phrase tou heliou eklipontos literally means "as the sun failed" (a verb often used of an eclipse, though a Passover full moon rules out a natural solar eclipse); many later manuscripts read "and the sun was darkened."
- **23:54** — The verb epephosken literally means "was dawning" or "was growing light," used here for the approach of the Jewish Sabbath day beginning at sunset.
- **23:17 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
