## Text

**21:1** And looking up, He saw the rich putting their gifts into the treasury.

**21:2** And He saw a certain needy widow putting two lepta there,

**21:3** and He said, "Truly I tell you that this poor widow put in more than all of them;

**21:4** for all of these put into the gifts out of their surplus, but she out of her lack put in all the livelihood that she had."

**21:5** And as some were speaking about the temple, that it was adorned with fine stones and dedicated offerings, He said,

**21:6** "As for these things that you are observing, days will come in which a stone will not be left upon a stone that will not be torn down."

**21:7** And they questioned Him, saying, "Teacher, when therefore will these things be, and what is the sign when these things are about to take place?"

**21:8** And He said, "Watch out that you are not misled; for many will come in My name, saying, 'I am,' and, 'The time has drawn near.' Do not go after them.

**21:9** And when you hear of wars and disturbances, do not be terrified; for these things must take place first, but the end is not immediately."

**21:10** Then He was saying to them, "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom,

**21:11** and there will be great earthquakes, and in various places famines and plagues, and there will be terrors and great signs from heaven.

**21:12** But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and persecute you, handing you over to the synagogues and prisons, as you are brought before kings and governors on account of My name.

**21:13** It will turn out for you as a testimony.

**21:14** Settle it therefore in your hearts not to prepare beforehand to make a defense,

**21:15** for I will give you a mouth and wisdom that all those opposing you will not be able to resist or contradict.

**21:16** Yet you will be handed over even by parents and brothers and relatives and friends, and they will put some of you to death,

**21:17** and you will be hated by all because of My name.

**21:18** And not a hair from your head will by any means perish.

**21:19** By your endurance, gain your souls.

**21:20** And when you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has drawn near.

**21:21** Then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains, and let those who are in the midst of it depart, and let those who are in the countryside not enter into it,

**21:22** because these are days of vengeance, so that all things that have been written may be fulfilled.

**21:23** Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days; for there will be great distress upon the land and wrath against this people,

**21:24** and they will fall by the edge of the sword and will be taken captive into all the nations, and Jerusalem will be trampled by Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.

**21:25** And there will be signs in the sun and moon and stars, and on the earth anguish of nations in perplexity at the roaring of the sea and its surging,

**21:26** with people fainting from fear and expectation of the things coming upon the inhabited world, for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.

**21:27** And then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.

**21:28** Now when these things begin to take place, straighten up and lift up your heads, because your redemption is drawing near."

**21:29** And He spoke a parable to them: "Look at the fig tree and all the trees;

**21:30** when they already sprout, seeing it you know for yourselves that summer is already near.

**21:31** So also you, when you see these things taking place, know that the kingdom of God is near.

**21:32** Truly I say to you that this generation will by no means pass away until all things take place.

**21:33** Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will by no means pass away.

**21:34** But watch yourselves, so that your hearts may not be weighed down with carousing and drunkenness and the anxieties of life, and that day come upon you suddenly

**21:35** like a trap; for it will come upon all those who dwell on the face of all the earth.

**21:36** But stay awake at all times, praying that you may have strength to escape all these things that are about to take place, and to stand before the Son of Man."

**21:37** Now during the days He was teaching in the temple, but at night He would go out and spend the night on the mount called Olivet;

**21:38** and all the people would come early in the morning to Him in the temple to listen to Him.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — A lepton (plural lepta) was the smallest bronze coin in circulation in first-century Judea, worth a tiny fraction of a day's wage.
- **21:19** — The critical text reads the aorist imperative ("gain"), whereas some manuscripts read the future indicative ("you will gain"). Greek psychas can denote either "souls" or "lives."
- **21:25** — The critical text reads the genitive noun "roaring" (echous), whereas later manuscripts read the participle "roaring" (echouses) modifying "sea."
- **21:36** — The critical text reads "have strength" (katischysete), whereas some later manuscripts read "be counted worthy" (kataxiothete).
