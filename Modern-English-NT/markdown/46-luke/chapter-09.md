## Text

**9:1** Now having called the twelve together, He gave them power and authority over all the demons and to heal diseases.

**9:2** And He sent them out to proclaim the kingdom of God and to cure the sick.

**9:3** And He said to them, "Take nothing for the journey, neither a staff, nor a bag, nor bread, nor money; nor are you to have two tunics.

**9:4** And whatever house you enter, remain there and depart from there.

**9:5** And as for all who do not receive you, when you depart from that city, shake off the dust from your feet as a testimony against them."

**9:6** So going out, they were traveling through the villages, announcing the good news and healing everywhere.

**9:7** Now Herod the tetrarch heard of all the things that were happening, and he was thoroughly perplexed because it was being said by some that John had been raised from the dead,

**9:8** and by some that Elijah had appeared, and by others that one of the ancient prophets had risen.

**9:9** And Herod said, "I myself beheaded John, but who is this man about whom I hear such things?" And he kept seeking to see Him.

**9:10** When the apostles returned, they recounted to Him all that they had done. And taking them along, He withdrew privately to a city called Bethsaida.

**9:11** But when the crowds found out, they followed Him. And welcoming them, He was speaking to them about the kingdom of God and curing those who had need of healing.

**9:12** Now the day began to decline, and the twelve came up and said to Him, "Send the crowd away, so that going into the surrounding villages and countryside they may lodge and find provisions, because here we are in a desolate place."

**9:13** But He said to them, "You yourselves give them something to eat." And they said, "We have no more than five loaves and two fish, unless perhaps we ourselves go and buy food for all these people."

**9:14** For there were about five thousand men. And He said to His disciples, "Have them recline in groups of about fifty each."

**9:15** And they did so, and had them all recline.

**9:16** Then taking the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up toward heaven, He blessed them and broke them, and kept giving them to the disciples to set before the crowd.

**9:17** And they all ate and were satisfied, and what was left over by them was picked up, twelve baskets of broken pieces.

**9:18** And it happened that while He was praying alone, the disciples were with Him, and He questioned them, saying, "Who do the crowds say that I am?"

**9:19** And answering, they said, "John the Baptist, and others say Elijah, and others that one of the ancient prophets has risen."

**9:20** And He said to them, "But who do you say that I am?" And answering, Peter said, "The Christ of God."

**9:21** But strictly warning them, He instructed them to tell this to no one,

**9:22** saying, "The Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised on the third day."

**9:23** And He was saying to them all, "If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross daily, and follow Me.

**9:24** For whoever desires to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life on account of Me, that one will save it.

**9:25** For what is a person profited if he gains the whole world, yet loses or forfeits himself?

**9:26** For whoever is ashamed of Me and My words, of that one the Son of Man will be ashamed when He comes in His glory and the glory of the Father and of the holy angels.

**9:27** But I say to you truly, there are some of those standing here who will certainly not taste death until they see the kingdom of God."

**9:28** Now it happened about eight days after these words that, taking Peter and John and James along, He went up onto the mountain to pray.

**9:29** And it happened while He was praying that the appearance of His face became different, and His clothing became gleaming white.

**9:30** And look, two men were talking with Him, who were Moses and Elijah,

**9:31** who, having appeared in glory, were speaking of His departure, which He was about to fulfill in Jerusalem.

**9:32** Now Peter and those with him were weighed down with sleep; but becoming fully awake, they saw His glory and the two men standing with Him.

**9:33** And it happened as they were parting from Him that Peter said to Jesus, "Master, it is good for us to be here; and let us make three tents, one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah"—not knowing what he was saying.

**9:34** While he was saying these things, a cloud appeared and was overshadowing them; and they became afraid as they entered into the cloud.

**9:35** Then a voice came out of the cloud, saying, "This is My Son, the Chosen One; listen to Him!"

**9:36** And when the voice had sounded, Jesus was found alone. And they kept silent, and in those days reported to no one anything of the things they had seen.

**9:37** Now it happened on the next day, when they came down from the mountain, that a large crowd met Him.

**9:38** And look, a man from the crowd shouted, saying, "Teacher, I beg You to look upon my son, because he is my only child,

**9:39** and look, a spirit seizes him, and suddenly he cries out, and it convulses him with foaming, and scarcely withdraws from him, mauling him;

**9:40** and I begged Your disciples to cast it out, and they were not able."

**9:41** And answering, Jesus said, "O unbelieving and twisted generation, how long will I be with you and put up with you? Bring your son here."

**9:42** Even while he was approaching, the demon threw him down and violently convulsed him. But Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and cured the boy and gave him back to his father.

**9:43** And all were astonished at the majesty of God. Now while everyone was marveling at all the things He was doing, He said to His disciples,

**9:44** "As for you, put these words into your ears, for the Son of Man is about to be handed over into the hands of men."

**9:45** But they did not understand this saying, and it was concealed from them so that they might not perceive it; and they were afraid to ask Him about this saying.

**9:46** Now an argument arose among them as to which of them might be the greatest.

**9:47** But Jesus, knowing the reasoning of their heart, took hold of a little child and set him beside Himself,

**9:48** and said to them, "Whoever receives this little child in My name receives Me, and whoever receives Me receives the One who sent Me; for the one who is least among all of you, this one is great."

**9:49** And answering, John said, "Master, we saw someone casting out demons in Your name, and we tried to stop him because he does not follow along with us."

**9:50** But Jesus said to him, "Do not stop him, for whoever is not against you is for you."

**9:51** Now it happened, as the days for His being taken up were being fulfilled, that He Himself set His face firmly to go to Jerusalem,

**9:52** and He sent messengers ahead of Him. And going on, they entered a village of the Samaritans to make preparations for Him;

**9:53** yet they did not receive Him, because His face was set on going to Jerusalem.

**9:54** And seeing this, the disciples James and John said, "Lord, do You want us to command fire to come down from heaven and consume them?"

**9:55** But turning, He rebuked them.

**9:56** And they went on to another village.

**9:57** And as they were going along the road, someone said to Him, "I will follow You wherever You go."

**9:58** And Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens and the birds of the sky have nests, yet the Son of Man has nowhere to rest His head."

**9:59** And He said to another, "Follow Me." But he said, "Lord, permit me first to go and bury my father."

**9:60** But He said to him, "Leave the dead to bury their own dead; as for you, go and proclaim the kingdom of God."

**9:61** And another also said, "I will follow You, Lord; but first permit me to say farewell to those in my house."

**9:62** But Jesus said, "No one who has put his hand to a plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — Some early manuscripts (notably Codex Vaticanus) omit "the sick," reading simply "and to cure."
- **9:28** — Luke's "about eight days" reflects inclusive reckoning of the interval that Matthew 17:1 and Mark 9:2 express as "six days."
- **9:31** — The Greek word exodos literally means "departure" or "exodus," echoing Israel's Exodus while referring to Jesus' impending death, resurrection, and ascension.
- **9:35** — The earliest manuscripts read "the Chosen One," whereas later manuscripts harmonize with Matthew 17:5 and Mark 9:7 to read "the Beloved."
- **9:51** — The Greek term analempsis ("taking up" or "ascension") opens Luke's travel narrative to Jerusalem and looks ahead to Jesus' passion, resurrection, and ascension.
- **9:55** — Many later manuscripts add to this verse, "and said, 'You do not know what kind of spirit you are of; for the Son of Man did not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them,'" which is absent from the earliest manuscripts.
