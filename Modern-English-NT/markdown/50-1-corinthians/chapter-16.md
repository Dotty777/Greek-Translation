## Text

**16:1** Now concerning the collection for the saints, just as I directed the churches of Galatia, you also are to do so.

**16:2** On the first day of every week, let each one of you set something aside on his own, storing it up as he may prosper, so that no collections will be made when I come.

**16:3** And when I arrive, whomever you approve by letters, these I will send to carry your gift to Jerusalem;

**16:4** and if it is worthwhile for me to go as well, they will go with me.

**16:5** Now I will come to you once I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia,

**16:6** and with you perhaps I will stay, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I may go.

**16:7** For I do not wish to see you now in passing, for I hope to remain with you for some time, if the Lord permits.

**16:8** Yet I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost;

**16:9** for a wide and effective door stands open for me, and there are many opponents.

**16:10** Now if Timothy comes, see to it that he is without fear among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I am.

**16:11** Therefore let no one treat him with contempt. Rather, send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.

**16:12** Now concerning Apollos the brother, I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers; yet it was not at all the will that he come now, but he will come when he has an opportunity.

**16:13** Keep watch, stand firm in the faith, be courageous, be strong.

**16:14** Let everything of yours be done in love.

**16:15** Now I urge you, brothers (you know the household of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have devoted themselves to service for the saints),

**16:16** that you also be subject to such people, and to everyone who works together and labors.

**16:17** And I rejoice at the arrival of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these men have supplied what was lacking from you,

**16:18** for they refreshed both my spirit and yours. Therefore recognize such people.

**16:19** The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, along with the church in their house.

**16:20** All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**16:21** The greeting is by my own hand—Paul's.

**16:22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. Marana tha.

**16:23** May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

**16:24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The phrase "by letters" (di' epistolon) may modify either "approve" (following the punctuation here) or "send" ("these I will send with letters"). "Gift" translates charis (lit. "grace"), used here for the charitable contribution.
- **16:12** — The Greek literally reads "it was not at all a will" (ouk en thelema) without a possessive pronoun, leaving open whether Paul refers to Apollos's own desire or to God's will.
- **16:13** — "Be courageous" translates andrizesthe (lit. "act like men"), an established Greek and Septuagint idiom for displaying courage and resolve.
- **16:22** — "Marana tha" is an Aramaic invocation transliterated in Greek meaning "Our Lord, come!" (divided differently as Maran atha, it can mean "Our Lord has come").
