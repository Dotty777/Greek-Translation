## Text

**10:1** For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud and all passed through the sea,

**10:2** and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,

**10:3** and all ate the same spiritual food,

**10:4** and all drank the same spiritual drink; for they were drinking from a spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.

**10:5** Yet with most of them God was not well pleased, for they were laid low in the wilderness.

**10:6** Now these things became examples for us, so that we would not crave evil things, just as they also craved them.

**10:7** And do not become idolaters, as some of them did; just as it is written, "The people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to play."

**10:8** Nor let us practice sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day.

**10:9** Nor let us put the Lord to the test, as some of them tested Him and were destroyed by the serpents.

**10:10** And do not grumble, just as some of them grumbled and perished by the destroyer.

**10:11** Now these things happened to them as examples, and they were written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages have arrived.

**10:12** So then, let the one who thinks he is standing watch out that he does not fall.

**10:13** No temptation has taken hold of you except what is common to humanity; and God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but along with the temptation will also make the way out, so that you may be able to bear up under it.

**10:14** Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

**10:15** I speak as to discerning people; judge for yourselves what I say.

**10:16** The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a participation in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a participation in the body of Christ?

**10:17** Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake from the one bread.

**10:18** Look at Israel according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices participants in the altar?

**10:19** What then am I saying? That food sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?

**10:20** No, but that the things the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God; and I do not want you to become participants with demons.

**10:21** You cannot drink the Lord's cup and the cup of demons; you cannot share in the Lord's table and the table of demons.

**10:22** Or are we provoking the Lord to jealousy? We are not stronger than He is, are we?

**10:23** All things are permissible, yet not all things are beneficial. All things are permissible, yet not all things build up.

**10:24** Let no one seek his own good, but that of the other.

**10:25** Eat everything sold in the meat market without raising any question for the sake of conscience,

**10:26** for the earth belongs to the Lord, and its fullness.

**10:27** If one of the unbelievers invites you and you want to go, eat everything set before you without raising any question for the sake of conscience.

**10:28** But if anyone says to you, "This was offered in sacrifice," do not eat it, for the sake of the one who pointed it out and for the sake of conscience—

**10:29** I mean not your own conscience, but that of the other person. For why is my freedom judged by another's conscience?

**10:30** If I partake with thankfulness, why am I spoken evil of concerning that for which I give thanks?

**10:31** Therefore, whether you eat or drink or do anything else, do everything to the glory of God.

**10:32** Give no cause for stumbling either to Jews or to Greeks or to the church of God,

**10:33** just as I also please everyone in everything, not seeking my own benefit, but that of the many, so that they may be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Paul draws on a Jewish interpretive tradition that the water-giving rock of Exodus 17 and Numbers 20 accompanied Israel in the wilderness, identifying its spiritual reality with Christ.
- **10:8** — Numbers 25:9 gives the number of those who died in the plague as 24,000.
- **10:9** — The Greek source text reads 'the Lord' (ton Kyrion), while many ancient manuscripts read 'Christ' (ton Christon) and a few read 'God' (ton theon).
- **10:28** — The speaker uses the neutral term hierothyton ('offered in sacrifice') rather than Paul's term eidōlothyton ('sacrificed to idols', v. 19). Later manuscripts repeat v. 26 at the end of this verse, which is omitted in the earliest witnesses.
