## Text

**3:1** And I, brothers, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual people, but as to people of flesh, as to infants in Christ.

**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able. In fact, even now you are still not able,

**3:3** for you are still fleshly. For where there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not fleshly and walking according to human ways?

**3:4** For whenever someone says, "I am of Paul," and another, "I of Apollos," are you not merely human?

**3:5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, even as the Lord gave to each one.

**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God kept causing the growth.

**3:7** Consequently, neither the one planting nor the one watering is anything, but God who causes the growth.

**3:8** Now the one who plants and the one who waters are one, yet each will receive his own reward according to his own labor.

**3:9** For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's field, God's building.

**3:10** According to the grace of God that was given to me, like a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another is building upon it. But let each one watch how he builds upon it.

**3:11** For no one can lay another foundation besides the one that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

**3:12** Now if anyone builds upon the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,

**3:13** each one's work will become evident, for the Day will make it clear, because it is revealed with fire, and the fire itself will test what sort each one's work is.

**3:14** If the work that anyone has built upon it remains, he will receive a reward.

**3:15** If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; yet he himself will be saved, but so as through fire.

**3:16** Do you not know that you are God's temple, and that God's Spirit dwells in you?

**3:17** If anyone destroys the temple of God, God will destroy him; for the temple of God is holy, and that is what you are.

**3:18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks himself to be wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may become wise.

**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness before God. For it is written, "He catches the wise in their craftiness,"

**3:20** and again, "The Lord knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are futile."

**3:21** So then let no one boast in people. For all things are yours,

**3:22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world or life or death, or things present or things to come, all things are yours,

**3:23** yet you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Paul uses sarkinos ("of flesh") here before shifting to the related adjective sarkikos ("fleshly") in verse 3.
- **3:4** — The critical Greek text reads "merely human" (literally "human beings") rather than "fleshly," which appears in some later manuscripts under the influence of verse 3.
- **3:9** — "God's fellow workers" can mean either coworkers who belong to God or workers who labor in partnership with God.
- **3:16** — "Temple" translates naos, the inner sanctuary where God's presence dwells, and "you" is plural throughout verses 16-17, referring to the church collectively.
