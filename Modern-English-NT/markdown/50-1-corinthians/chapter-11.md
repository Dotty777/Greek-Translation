## Text

**11:1** Become imitators of me, just as I also am of Christ.

**11:2** Now I commend you because you remember me in all things and hold firmly to the traditions just as I handed them down to you.

**11:3** Yet I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of a woman is the man, and the head of Christ is God.

**11:4** Every man who prays or prophesies with something over his head disgraces his head.

**11:5** But every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered disgraces her head, for it is one and the same thing as a woman whose head is shaved.

**11:6** For if a woman is not covered, let her also have her hair cut short; but if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut short or to be shaved, let her be covered.

**11:7** For a man ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory of God; but the woman is the glory of man.

**11:8** For man is not from woman, but woman from man;

**11:9** for indeed man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man.

**11:10** Therefore the woman ought to have authority on her head because of the angels.

**11:11** Nevertheless, neither is woman apart from man nor man apart from woman in the Lord;

**11:12** for just as the woman is from the man, so also the man is through the woman; yet all things are from God.

**11:13** Judge among yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God uncovered?

**11:14** Does not even nature itself teach you that if a man wears long hair, it is a dishonor to him,

**11:15** but if a woman wears long hair, it is a glory to her? For her long hair has been given to her in place of a covering.

**11:16** Now if anyone seems inclined to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of God.

**11:17** Now in giving this instruction, I do not commend you, because you come together not for the better, but for the worse.

**11:18** For in the first place, when you come together as a church, I hear that divisions exist among you, and in part I believe it.

**11:19** For there must also be factions among you, so that those who are approved may also become evident among you.

**11:20** Therefore, when you come together in one place, it is not to eat the Lord's supper,

**11:21** for in eating, each one goes ahead with his own supper, and one goes hungry while another gets drunk.

**11:22** Do you not indeed have houses for eating and drinking? Or do you despise the church of God and disgrace those who have nothing? What shall I say to you? Shall I commend you? In this I do not commend you.

**11:23** For I received from the Lord what I also handed down to you, that the Lord Jesus, on the night in which He was betrayed, took bread,

**11:24** and after giving thanks, He broke it and said, "This is My body, which is for you; do this in remembrance of Me."

**11:25** In the same way He also took the cup after supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood; do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of Me."

**11:26** For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the death of the Lord until He comes.

**11:27** Therefore, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner will be guilty concerning the body and the blood of the Lord.

**11:28** But let a person examine himself, and in that way let him eat from the bread and drink from the cup;

**11:29** for the one who eats and drinks eats and drinks judgment upon himself, not discerning the body.

**11:30** Because of this, many among you are weak and sick, and a considerable number are asleep.

**11:31** But if we carefully judged ourselves, we would not be judged;

**11:32** yet when we are judged by the Lord, we are being disciplined, so that we will not be condemned along with the world.

**11:33** Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.

**11:34** If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that you will not come together for judgment. And the remaining matters I will arrange whenever I come.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — Greek aner and gyne can mean "man/woman" or "husband/wife," and the metaphorical sense of kephale ("head") is debated between authority and source.
- **11:4** — Greek kata kephales echon is literally "having [something] down from his head," referring to a head covering.
- **11:10** — Greek exousian echein epi tes kephales is literally "to have authority on her head," variously understood as a sign of authority over her or her own authority to pray and prophesy.
- **11:24** — The critical Greek text reads simply "which is for you," omitting the later manuscript addition "broken" (klomenon).
- **11:29** — The critical Greek text omits "in an unworthy manner" and "of the Lord" found in later manuscripts; "the body" may refer to Christ's eucharistic body, the gathered church, or both.
