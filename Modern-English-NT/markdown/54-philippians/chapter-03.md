## Text

**3:1** Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. Writing the same things to you is not tiresome for me, and for you it is a safeguard.

**3:2** Watch out for the dogs, watch out for the evil workers, watch out for the mutilation.

**3:3** For we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of God and boast in Christ Jesus and place no confidence in the flesh,

**3:4** although I myself also have confidence in the flesh. If any other person thinks he may place confidence in the flesh, I even more:

**3:5** circumcised on the eighth day, from the lineage of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; with respect to the law, a Pharisee;

**3:6** with respect to zeal, persecuting the church; with respect to the righteousness that is in the law, having become blameless.

**3:7** But whatever things were gains to me, these I have counted as loss because of Christ.

**3:8** Even more than that, I also count all things to be loss because of the surpassing excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, on account of whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and I count them as refuse so that I may gain Christ

**3:9** and be found in Him, not having a righteousness of my own from the law, but the one through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God based on faith,

**3:10** in order to know Him and the power of His resurrection and fellowship in His sufferings, being conformed to His death,

**3:11** if somehow I may arrive at the resurrection from the dead.

**3:12** Not that I have already obtained it or have already been perfected, but I pursue it, if indeed I may take hold of that for which I was also taken hold of by Christ Jesus.

**3:13** Brothers, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of it; yet one thing I do: forgetting the things behind and stretching forward to the things ahead,

**3:14** I pursue toward the goal for the prize of the upward calling of God in Christ Jesus.

**3:15** Therefore, as many of us as are mature, let us think this way; and if in anything you think differently, God will reveal this to you as well.

**3:16** Only, to the point we have attained, let us keep in step along that same line.

**3:17** Become fellow imitators of me, brothers, and observe closely those who walk in this way, just as you have us as a pattern.

**3:18** For many walk, about whom I often told you and now also say while weeping, that they are the enemies of the cross of Christ,

**3:19** whose end is destruction, whose god is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame, who have their minds set on earthly things.

**3:20** For our citizenship is in the heavens, from where we also eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,

**3:21** who will transform the body of our lowliness to be conformed to the body of His glory, according to the working by which He is able even to subject all things to Himself.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Greek katatome ("mutilation," literally "a cutting down") forms a sharp wordplay with peritome ("circumcision") in v. 3.
- **3:8** — Greek skybala ("refuse") is a strong term for discarded waste, scraps, or excrement, emphasizing the stark contrast with knowing Christ.
- **3:9** — The Greek phrase pisteos Christou is debated and may be rendered either as an objective genitive ("faith in Christ") or a subjective genitive ("the faithfulness of Christ").
- **3:20** — Greek politeuma ("citizenship") can also denote a "commonwealth" or "colony," language with particular resonance for believers living in the Roman colony of Philippi.
