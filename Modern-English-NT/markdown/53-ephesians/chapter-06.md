## Text

**6:1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is right.

**6:2** "Honor your father and mother," which is the first commandment with a promise,

**6:3** "so that it may go well with you and you will be long-lived on the earth."

**6:4** And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but raise them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

**6:5** Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in sincerity of your heart, as to Christ,

**6:6** not with eye-service as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God, from the soul

**6:7** serving with goodwill, as to the Lord and not to people,

**6:8** knowing that each one, whatever good he may do, will receive this back from the Lord, whether slave or free.

**6:9** And masters, do the same things toward them, ceasing from threatening, knowing that the Lord of both them and you is in the heavens, and there is no partiality with Him.

**6:10** Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of His strength.

**6:11** Put on the complete armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.

**6:12** For our struggle is not against blood and flesh, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.

**6:13** For this reason, take up the complete armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having accomplished everything, to stand.

**6:14** Stand therefore, having belted your waist with truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,

**6:15** and having fitted your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace,

**6:16** in all circumstances taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to extinguish all the fiery arrows of the evil one.

**6:17** And receive the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,

**6:18** with all prayer and petition, praying on every occasion in the Spirit, and to this end staying alert with all perseverance and petition for all the saints,

**6:19** and on my behalf, that a message may be given to me in the opening of my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,

**6:20** on behalf of which I am an ambassador in a chain, so that in it I may speak boldly, as I must speak.

**6:21** Now so that you also may know the things concerning me, how I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make everything known to you,

**6:22** whom I sent to you for this very purpose, so that you may know the things concerning us and he may encourage your hearts.

**6:23** Peace to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**6:24** Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruptibility.

## Translator Notes

- **6:6** — The phrase "from the soul" (ek psyches) can be construed either with "doing the will of God" in 6:6 or, following the punctuation of the Greek text, with "serving with goodwill" in 6:7.
- **6:15** — The Greek noun hetoimasia ("readiness") can denote either preparedness to share the gospel or a firm footing supplied by it.
- **6:16** — The critical text reads en pasin ("in all circumstances"), whereas other manuscripts read epi pasin ("in addition to all" or "above all").
- **6:20** — The Greek noun halysei ("in a chain") is singular, likely referring to the single coupling chain binding Paul to a guard.
- **6:24** — The phrase en aphtharsia literally means "in incorruptibility" (or "imperishability"), conveying an undying, uncorrupted love for Christ; the earliest manuscripts omit "Amen."
