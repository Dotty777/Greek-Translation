## Text

**2:1** And you, being dead in your trespasses and sins,

**2:2** in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit now working in the sons of disobedience;

**2:3** among whom we all also once conducted ourselves in the desires of our flesh, carrying out the wishes of the flesh and of the thoughts, and we were by nature children of wrath, just like the rest.

**2:4** Yet God, rich in mercy, on account of His great love with which He loved us,

**2:5** even while we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—

**2:6** and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,

**2:7** so that in the coming ages He might demonstrate the surpassing riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

**2:8** For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves; it is God's gift,

**2:9** not from works, so that no one may boast.

**2:10** For we are His handiwork, having been created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we should walk in them.

**2:11** Therefore remember that formerly you, the Gentiles in the flesh, who are called "uncircumcision" by the so-called "circumcision" in the flesh made by hands,

**2:12** that at that time you were apart from Christ, alienated from the citizenship of Israel and foreigners to the covenants of the promise, not having hope and without God in the world.

**2:13** But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far away were brought near by the blood of Christ.

**2:14** For He Himself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the dividing wall of the barrier, the hostility, in His flesh,

**2:15** having nullified the law of commandments in ordinances, so that in Himself He might create the two into one new man, making peace,

**2:16** and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, having put the hostility to death by it.

**2:17** And having come, He proclaimed good news of peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near;

**2:18** because through Him both of us have access in one Spirit to the Father.

**2:19** So then, you are no longer foreigners and resident aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God,

**2:20** having been built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus Himself being the cornerstone,

**2:21** in whom the whole structure, being fitted together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord,

**2:22** in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek leaves "you, being dead..." grammatically suspended without a main verb until God's action is stated in vv. 4-5.
- **2:8** — The pronoun "this" (touto) is neuter, while "grace" and "faith" are feminine; it may refer to the whole preceding clause.
- **2:16** — "By it" (en autō) refers to the cross, though it may also be understood as "in Himself" (Christ).
- **2:21** — Without the Greek article, pasa oikodomē ("the whole structure") can also be translated "every building" or "every structure."
