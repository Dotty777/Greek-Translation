## Text

**5:1** Therefore become imitators of God, as beloved children,

**5:2** and walk in love, just as Christ also loved you and gave Himself over for us as an offering and a sacrifice to God for a fragrant aroma.

**5:3** But sexual immorality and all impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints,

**5:4** nor obscenity and foolish talk or coarse jesting, which are not proper, but rather thanksgiving.

**5:5** For you know this for certain, that no sexually immoral or impure or greedy person—that is, an idolater—has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.

**5:6** Do not let anyone deceive you with empty words, for on account of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

**5:7** Therefore do not become partakers with them;

**5:8** for you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord; walk as children of light,

**5:9** (for the fruit of the light is in all goodness and righteousness and truth),

**5:10** discerning what is pleasing to the Lord.

**5:11** And do not participate in the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather even expose them;

**5:12** for it is shameful even to speak of the things done by them in secret.

**5:13** Yet all things, being exposed by the light, are made visible, for everything that is made visible is light.

**5:14** Therefore it says, "Wake up, you who sleep, and rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."

**5:15** Watch carefully, then, how you walk, not as unwise, but as wise,

**5:16** redeeming the opportunity, because the days are evil.

**5:17** For this reason do not become foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

**5:18** And do not become drunk with wine, in which is dissipation, but be filled with the Spirit,

**5:19** speaking to one another in psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing and making music in your heart to the Lord,

**5:20** giving thanks always for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God the Father,

**5:21** submitting to one another in the fear of Christ;

**5:22** wives, to your own husbands, as to the Lord,

**5:23** because a husband is head of the wife, just as Christ also is head of the church, He Himself being Savior of the body.

**5:24** Yet as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

**5:25** Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself over for her,

**5:26** in order that He might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of the water in the word,

**5:27** so that He Himself might present the church to Himself glorious, not having a spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but so that she may be holy and blameless.

**5:28** In this way husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies; the one who loves his own wife loves himself,

**5:29** for no one at any time hated his own flesh, but nurtures and cherishes it, just as Christ also does the church,

**5:30** since we are members of His body.

**5:31** For this reason a man will leave father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.

**5:32** This mystery is great, but I am speaking concerning Christ and concerning the church.

**5:33** Nevertheless, let each one of you individually also love his own wife just as himself, and the wife must respect her husband.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Greek text shifts from the second person ("loved you") to the first person ("for us"), a reading well attested in early manuscripts.
- **5:5** — "Of Christ and God" places a single Greek article before both nouns, which many grammarians understand as identifying Christ as God ("of Him who is Christ and God").
- **5:14** — An unidentified quotation—likely an early Christian hymn echoing Isaiah 26:19 and 60:1—introduced with the standard scriptural citation formula "it says."
- **5:22** — The earliest Greek text has no verb in this verse, carrying over "submitting" directly from verse 21.
- **5:33** — "Respect" translates the Greek verb meaning "fear" or "revere," echoing "the fear of Christ" in verse 21.
