## Text

**3:1** For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles—

**3:2** if indeed you heard of the administration of the grace of God given to me for you;

**3:3** by revelation the mystery was made known to me, just as I wrote earlier in brief,

**3:4** in light of which, as you read, you are able to understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,

**3:5** which was not made known to the sons of men in other generations, as it was now revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:

**3:6** that the Gentiles are joint heirs, and members of the same body, and sharers together of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,

**3:7** of which I became a servant according to the gift of the grace of God given to me according to the working of His power—

**3:8** to me, less than the least of all saints, this grace was given—to proclaim to the Gentiles the good news of the unsearchable riches of Christ,

**3:9** and to enlighten all concerning what is the administration of the mystery hidden from the ages in God, who created all things,

**3:10** so that now the multifaceted wisdom of God might be made known through the church to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly places,

**3:11** according to the purpose of the ages that He accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord,

**3:12** in whom we have boldness and access in confidence through faith in Him.

**3:13** Therefore I ask you not to lose heart over my afflictions on your behalf, which is your glory.

**3:14** For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,

**3:15** from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,

**3:16** that according to the riches of His glory He may grant you to be made strong with power through His Spirit in the inner man,

**3:17** that Christ may dwell through faith in your hearts in love; having been rooted and grounded,

**3:18** so that you may have full strength to grasp with all the saints what is the width and length and height and depth,

**3:19** and to know the love of Christ that goes beyond knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.

**3:20** Now to Him who is able to accomplish exceedingly beyond all that we ask or imagine, according to the power working in us,

**3:21** to Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations of the age of the ages. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — "Less than the least" renders elachistoterō, an emphatic Greek comparative built upon a superlative adjective.
- **3:12** — "Faith in Him" translates the Greek genitive tēs pisteōs autou, which some take as a subjective genitive meaning "His faithfulness."
- **3:15** — Greek uses a wordplay linking "Father" (patēr, v. 14) with "family" or "lineage" (patria).
- **3:17** — Following the Greek punctuation here, "in love" modifies "dwell," though many link it with "having been rooted and grounded."
- **3:21** — "Of the age of the ages" (tou aiōnos tōn aiōnōn) is a literal rendering of an emphatic Greek idiom meaning "forever and ever."
