## Text

**3:1** Now in those days John the Baptist arrived, preaching in the wilderness of Judea,

**3:2** saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near."

**3:3** For this is the one spoken of through Isaiah the prophet, saying, "The voice of one calling out in the wilderness: 'Make ready the way of the Lord; make His paths straight.'"

**3:4** Now John himself had clothing made from camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was locusts and wild honey.

**3:5** Then Jerusalem was going out to him, and all Judea and all the surrounding region of the Jordan,

**3:6** and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River as they confessed their sins.

**3:7** But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, "Offspring of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

**3:8** Therefore produce fruit worthy of repentance,

**3:9** and do not presume to say within yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father,' for I tell you that God is able from these stones to raise up children for Abraham.

**3:10** Already indeed the ax lies at the root of the trees; every tree, therefore, that does not produce good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire.

**3:11** I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, yet He who comes after me is stronger than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry; He Himself will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.

**3:12** His winnowing fork is in His hand, and He will clean out His threshing floor thoroughly and gather His wheat into the granary, while He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire."

**3:13** Then Jesus arrived from Galilee at the Jordan to John in order to be baptized by him.

**3:14** But John was trying to hinder Him, saying, "I have need to be baptized by You, and You are coming to me?"

**3:15** But answering, Jesus said to him, "Allow it for now, for in this way it is proper for us to fulfill all righteousness." Then he allowed Him.

**3:16** And having been baptized, Jesus immediately came up out of the water; and look, the heavens were opened, and He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, coming upon Him.

**3:17** And look, a voice came from the heavens, saying, "This is My Son, the Beloved, in whom I am well pleased."

## Translator Notes

- **3:11** — The preposition "in" ("in water," "in the Holy Spirit and fire") can also be rendered instrumentally as "with"; "fire" is variously taken to signify purifying refinement or eschatological judgment (compare 3:10, 12).
- **3:15** — "Fulfill all righteousness" refers to carrying out God's saving will and covenant plan in full, here through Jesus' solidarity with John's baptism.
- **3:16** — The critical Greek text reads simply "the heavens were opened," without the addition "to Him" found in some manuscripts.
