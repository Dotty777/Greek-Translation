## Text

**15:1** Then Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem came to Jesus, saying,

**15:2** "Why do Your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread."

**15:3** And He answered and said to them, "Why do you also transgress the commandment of God because of your tradition?

**15:4** For God commanded, saying, 'Honor father and mother,' and, 'Let the one who speaks evil of father or mother surely die.'

**15:5** But you say, 'Whoever says to his father or mother, "Whatever you might gain from me is a gift,"

**15:6** he is not to honor his father.' And you have nullified the word of God because of your tradition.

**15:7** Hypocrites! Rightly did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying,

**15:8** 'This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart keeps far from Me;

**15:9** yet in vain they worship Me, teaching as teachings the commands of men.'"

**15:10** And calling the crowd to Himself, He said to them, "Hear and understand:

**15:11** It is not what goes into the mouth that defiles a person, but what comes out of the mouth—this defiles a person."

**15:12** Then the disciples came and said to Him, "Do You know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard the saying?"

**15:13** And He answered and said, "Every plant that My heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.

**15:14** Leave them alone; they are blind guides. And if a blind man guides a blind man, both will fall into a pit."

**15:15** Then Peter answered and said to Him, "Explain the parable to us."

**15:16** And He said, "Are you also still without understanding?

**15:17** Do you not yet perceive that everything entering into the mouth passes into the stomach and is expelled into the latrine?

**15:18** But the things that come out of the mouth come forth from the heart, and those defile a person.

**15:19** For out of the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual immoralities, thefts, false testimonies, slanders.

**15:20** These are the things that defile a person; but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a person."

**15:21** And going out from there, Jesus withdrew into the districts of Tyre and Sidon.

**15:22** And look, a Canaanite woman came out from that region and was crying out, saying, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David; my daughter is severely demon-possessed."

**15:23** But He did not answer her a word. And His disciples came and were urging Him, saying, "Send her away, because she is crying out behind us."

**15:24** And He answered and said, "I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel."

**15:25** But she came and bowed down before Him, saying, "Lord, help me."

**15:26** And He answered and said, "It is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the little dogs."

**15:27** And she said, "Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs eat from the crumbs that fall from their masters' table."

**15:28** Then Jesus answered and said to her, "Woman, great is your faith! Let it be done for you as you wish." And her daughter was healed from that hour.

**15:29** And moving on from there, Jesus came along the Sea of Galilee, and going up onto the mountain, He sat there.

**15:30** And large crowds came to Him, having with them the mute, the blind, the lame, the crippled, and many others, and they laid them at His feet, and He healed them;

**15:31** so that the crowd marveled as they saw the mute speaking, the crippled made whole, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.

**15:32** Now Jesus, calling His disciples to Himself, said, "I have compassion on the crowd, because they have remained with Me three days now and do not have anything to eat; and I do not wish to send them away hungry, otherwise they might faint on the way."

**15:33** And the disciples said to Him, "Where would we get so many loaves in a desolate place as to satisfy so large a crowd?"

**15:34** And Jesus said to them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

**15:35** And directing the crowd to recline on the ground,

**15:36** He took the seven loaves and the fish, and having given thanks, He broke them and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

**15:37** And they all ate and were satisfied; and they picked up what was left over of the broken pieces, seven large baskets full.

**15:38** And those who ate were four thousand men, besides women and children.

**15:39** And after sending the crowds away, He got into the boat and came to the region of Magadan.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — Greek Doron ("gift") refers to a vowed sacred offering dedicated to God (called "Corban" in Mark 7:11); the following clause in v. 6 indicates that this vow was treated as absolving a son from the duty of supporting his father.
- **15:14** — The Greek text here reads simply "blind guides," whereas many later manuscripts add "of the blind."
- **15:26** — Greek kynaria is a diminutive ("little dogs" or household pets), distinct from wild street dogs, contrasting the children at the table with pets in the house.
- **15:37** — Greek spyris denotes a large woven hamper or provision basket (also in 16:10), distinct from the smaller wicker basket (kophinos) mentioned in 16:9.
