## Text

**27:1** Now when morning had come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus so as to put Him to death;

**27:2** and having bound Him, they led Him away and handed Him over to Pilate the governor.

**27:3** Then Judas, who had betrayed Him, seeing that He had been condemned, was filled with remorse and returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and elders,

**27:4** saying, "I have sinned in betraying innocent blood." But they said, "What is that to us? You see to it."

**27:5** And throwing the silver coins into the sanctuary, he withdrew; and going away, he hanged himself.

**27:6** But the chief priests, taking the silver coins, said, "It is not lawful to put them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood."

**27:7** And after taking counsel, they bought with them the Potter's Field for the burial of strangers.

**27:8** For that reason, that field has been called Field of Blood until today.

**27:9** Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying, "And they took the thirty silver coins, the price of the One who had been priced, whom some of the sons of Israel priced,

**27:10** and they gave them for the potter's field, just as the Lord instructed me."

**27:11** Now Jesus stood before the governor; and the governor questioned Him, saying, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And Jesus said to him, "You say so."

**27:12** And while He was being accused by the chief priests and elders, He answered nothing.

**27:13** Then Pilate said to Him, "Do You not hear how many things they are testifying against You?"

**27:14** And He did not answer him regarding even a single word, so that the governor marveled greatly.

**27:15** Now at each feast, the governor was accustomed to release one prisoner to the crowd, whichever one they wanted.

**27:16** And at that time they had a notorious prisoner called Barabbas.

**27:17** Therefore, when they had gathered together, Pilate said to them, "Whom do you want me to release to you, Barabbas or Jesus who is called Christ?"

**27:18** For he knew that they had handed Him over out of envy.

**27:19** Now while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, "Have nothing to do with that righteous man, for today I have suffered many things in a dream because of Him."

**27:20** But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas and destroy Jesus.

**27:21** And the governor answered and said to them, "Which of the two do you want me to release to you?" And they said, "Barabbas."

**27:22** Pilate said to them, "What then should I do with Jesus who is called Christ?" All of them said, "Let Him be crucified!"

**27:23** But he said, "Why, what evil did He do?" Yet they kept crying out all the more, saying, "Let Him be crucified!"

**27:24** Now when Pilate saw that he was achieving nothing, but rather an uproar was starting, he took water and washed his hands in front of the crowd, saying, "I am innocent of this blood; you see to it yourselves."

**27:25** And all the people answered and said, "His blood be upon us and upon our children!"

**27:26** Then he released Barabbas to them; but after flogging Jesus, he handed Him over to be crucified.

**27:27** Then the governor's soldiers, taking Jesus into the Praetorium, gathered the entire cohort around Him.

**27:28** And having stripped Him, they put a scarlet cloak around Him,

**27:29** and weaving a crown of thorns, they set it on His head, and a reed in His right hand; and kneeling down before Him, they mocked Him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**27:30** And spitting on Him, they took the reed and kept striking Him on the head.

**27:31** And when they had mocked Him, they stripped Him of the cloak, put His garments on Him, and led Him away to crucify Him.

**27:32** Now as they were going out, they found a man from Cyrene, Simon by name; this man they pressed into service to carry His cross.

**27:33** And arriving at a place called Golgotha, which means Place of a Skull,

**27:34** they gave Him wine mixed with gall to drink; and having tasted it, He would not drink.

**27:35** And after crucifying Him, they divided His garments among themselves, casting lots,

**27:36** and sitting down, they were keeping watch over Him there.

**27:37** And they placed above His head the written charge against Him: "This is Jesus, the King of the Jews."

**27:38** Then two bandits were crucified with Him, one on the right and one on the left.

**27:39** And those passing by were reviling Him, shaking their heads

**27:40** and saying, "You who destroy the sanctuary and build it in three days, save Yourself! If You are the Son of God, come down from the cross."

**27:41** Likewise, the chief priests also, mocking Him together with the scribes and elders, were saying,

**27:42** "He saved others; He is unable to save Himself. He is King of Israel; let Him come down now from the cross, and we will believe in Him.

**27:43** He has trusted in God; let Him deliver Him now, if He delights in Him; for He said, 'I am the Son of God.'"

**27:44** In the same way, the bandits who had been crucified with Him were also reproaching Him.

**27:45** Now from the sixth hour, darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour.

**27:46** And around the ninth hour, Jesus cried out in a loud voice, saying, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" that is, "My God, My God, why have You abandoned Me?"

**27:47** And some of those standing there, hearing this, were saying, "This man is calling Elijah."

**27:48** And immediately one of them ran, took a sponge, filled it with sour wine, placed it on a reed, and offered Him a drink.

**27:49** But the rest were saying, "Leave Him alone; let us see whether Elijah is coming to save Him."

**27:50** And Jesus, crying out again in a loud voice, gave up His spirit.

**27:51** And look, the curtain of the sanctuary was torn in two from top to bottom; and the earth quaked, and the rocks were split,

**27:52** and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who were asleep were raised,

**27:53** and having come out of the tombs after His resurrection, they entered the holy city and appeared to many.

**27:54** Now the centurion and those with him who were keeping watch over Jesus, seeing the earthquake and the things that took place, were greatly terrified, saying, "Truly this man was the Son of God!"

**27:55** And many women were there watching from a distance, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving Him.

**27:56** Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee's sons.

**27:57** Now when evening had come, a rich man from Arimathea arrived, named Joseph, who himself had also become a disciple of Jesus.

**27:58** This man approached Pilate and requested the body of Jesus. Then Pilate ordered it to be given over.

**27:59** And taking the body, Joseph wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,

**27:60** and placed it in his own new tomb, which he had cut out in the rock; and having rolled a large stone against the doorway of the tomb, he departed.

**27:61** Now Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the tomb.

**27:62** Now on the following day, which is after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered together before Pilate,

**27:63** saying, "Sir, we remembered how that deceiver said while still alive, 'After three days I will be raised.'

**27:64** Therefore, order the tomb to be secured until the third day, so that His disciples do not come and steal Him and say to the people, 'He has been raised from the dead,' and the last deception will be worse than the first."

**27:65** And Pilate said to them, "You have a guard; go and secure it as you know how."

**27:66** So they went and secured the tomb, sealing the stone along with the guard.

## Translator Notes

- **27:6** — The Greek term korbanas transliterates the Aramaic word for the sacred temple treasury, related to the term for a dedicated offering (compare Mark 7:11).
- **27:9** — Although Matthew names Jeremiah, the wording draws primarily on Zechariah 11:12-13 blended with potter and field imagery from Jeremiah (Jeremiah 18:1-12; 19:1-13; 32:6-15), attributing the composite quotation to the major prophet.
- **27:17** — Some manuscripts read "Jesus Barabbas" here and in verse 16, heightening the contrast with "Jesus who is called Christ," but the earliest and broadest witnesses read simply "Barabbas."
- **27:24** — Greek "of this blood" can also be construed as "of this man's blood"; many later manuscripts add "righteous" ("of the blood of this righteous man"), echoing verse 19.
- **27:46** — The Greek text here reads the Aramaic form "Eloi, Eloi" (as in Sinaiticus and Vaticanus), whereas other manuscripts read the Hebrew form "Eli, Eli," which more directly explains the bystanders' confusion with "Elijah" in verse 47.
- **27:49** — Several early Alexandrian manuscripts insert a sentence here stating that another took a spear and pierced His side, and water and blood came out (compare John 19:34), which is absent from our Greek text and most witnesses.
- **27:54** — Because "Son of God" lacks the definite article in Greek, the phrase can be translated either "the Son of God" or "a son of God"; within Matthew's narrative it directly answers the taunts in verses 40 and 43.
- **27:65** — The Greek verb echete is ambiguous in form and can be read either as an indicative ("You have a guard") or as an imperative ("Take a guard").
