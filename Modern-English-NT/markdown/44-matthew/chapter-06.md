## Text

**6:1** "Now take care not to practice your righteousness before people in order to be seen by them; otherwise you have no reward with your Father who is in the heavens.

**6:2** Therefore, when you do an act of charity, do not sound a trumpet before you, just as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, so that they may be honored by people. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.

**6:3** But when you do an act of charity, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,

**6:4** so that your act of charity may be in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.

**6:5** And when you pray, you must not be like the hypocrites; for they love to stand and pray in the synagogues and on the corners of the wide streets, so that they may be seen by people. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.

**6:6** But you, when you pray, go into your inner room, shut your door, and pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.

**6:7** And when you pray, do not babble repetitively just as the Gentiles do, for they suppose that they will be heard because of their many words.

**6:8** Therefore, do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask Him.

**6:9** You, then, pray in this way: 'Our Father who is in the heavens, may Your name be held holy,

**6:10** may Your kingdom come, may Your will be done, as in heaven, so also on earth.

**6:11** Give us today our daily bread;

**6:12** and forgive us our debts, just as we also have forgiven our debtors.

**6:13** And do not bring us into temptation, but rescue us from the evil one.'

**6:14** For if you forgive people their transgressions, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.

**6:15** But if you do not forgive people, neither will your Father forgive your transgressions.

**6:16** Now when you fast, do not become gloomy like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces so that they may be seen by people to be fasting. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.

**6:17** But you, when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,

**6:18** so that you may not be seen by people to be fasting, but by your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.

**6:19** Do not store up treasures for yourselves on the earth, where moth and corrosion ruin them, and where thieves break in and steal;

**6:20** but store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where neither moth nor corrosion ruins them, and where thieves do not break in or steal.

**6:21** For where your treasure is, there also your heart will be.

**6:22** The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore, if your eye is sound, your whole body will be full of light;

**6:23** but if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If, therefore, the light that is in you is darkness, how great is that darkness!

**6:24** No one is able to serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will cling to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and wealth.

**6:25** For this reason I tell you: do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing?

**6:26** Look closely at the birds of the sky: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth much more than they are?

**6:27** And which of you by being anxious is able to add a single cubit to his lifespan?

**6:28** And why are you anxious about clothing? Observe closely how the lilies of the field grow: they neither toil nor spin;

**6:29** yet I tell you that not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of these.

**6:30** Now if God clothes the grass of the field in this way, which exists today and tomorrow is thrown into an oven, will He not much more clothe you, you of little faith?

**6:31** Therefore, do not be anxious, saying, 'What will we eat?' or 'What will we drink?' or 'What will we wear?'

**6:32** For the Gentiles eagerly seek all these things; for your heavenly Father knows that you need all of them.

**6:33** But seek first the kingdom and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.

**6:34** Therefore, do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Enough for the day is its own trouble."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The earliest manuscripts read δικαιοσύνην ("righteousness"), whereas later manuscripts harmonize with 6:2 by reading ἐλεημοσύνην ("charitable giving").
- **6:11** — The rare Greek adjective ἐπιούσιον ("daily") is debated in meaning; scholars also propose "necessary for existence" or "for the coming day."
- **6:13** — Greek τοῦ πονηροῦ may be taken as masculine ("the evil one") or neuter ("evil"). The liturgical doxology ("For Yours is the kingdom...") is absent from the earliest manuscripts and from this Greek text.
- **6:22** — In Jewish idiom, a "sound" or "single" eye (ἁπλοῦς) signifies generosity and undivided devotion, contrasting with a "bad" or "evil" eye (πονηρός, 6:23), which signifies greed or envy.
- **6:27** — Greek ἡλικίαν can mean either "lifespan" (with "cubit" used metaphorically for a brief measure of time) or physical "stature."
