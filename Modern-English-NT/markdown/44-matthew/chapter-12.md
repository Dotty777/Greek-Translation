## Text

**12:1** At that time Jesus walked through the grainfields on the Sabbath; His disciples became hungry and began to pluck heads of grain and to eat.

**12:2** But the Pharisees saw this and said to Him, "Look, Your disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath."

**12:3** He replied to them, "Have you not read what David did when he became hungry, along with those who were with him,

**12:4** how he entered the house of God and they ate the loaves of presentation, which was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those with him, except for the priests alone?

**12:5** Or have you not read in the Law that on the Sabbaths the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath and are guiltless?

**12:6** Now I say to you that something greater than the temple is here.

**12:7** But if you had known what it means, 'I want mercy and not sacrifice,' you would not have condemned the guiltless.

**12:8** For the Son of Man is the Lord of the Sabbath."

**12:9** Moving on from there, He went into their synagogue.

**12:10** And look, a man was there who had a withered hand. And they questioned Him, saying, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?" so that they might accuse Him.

**12:11** He said to them, "What man among you who has one sheep, if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not take hold of it and lift it out?

**12:12** How much more valuable, then, is a person than a sheep! Therefore, it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath."

**12:13** Then He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and it was restored sound, like the other.

**12:14** But the Pharisees went out and took counsel against Him, as to how they might destroy Him.

**12:15** Jesus, knowing this, withdrew from there. Many followed Him, and He healed them all,

**12:16** and He warned them strictly not to make Him known,

**12:17** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

**12:18** "Look, My Servant whom I have chosen, My Beloved in whom My soul delights; I will place My Spirit upon Him, and He will announce justice to the nations.

**12:19** He will not quarrel nor shout aloud, nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets.

**12:20** A bruised reed He will not break, and a smoldering wick He will not extinguish, until He brings justice forth to victory.

**12:21** And in His name nations will hope."

**12:22** Then a demon-possessed man who was blind and mute was brought to Him, and He healed him, so that the mute man spoke and saw.

**12:23** All the crowds were astounded and were saying, "Could this man be the Son of David?"

**12:24** But when the Pharisees heard it, they said, "This man does not cast out demons except by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

**12:25** Knowing their thoughts, He said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand.

**12:26** And if Satan casts out Satan, he has become divided against himself; how then will his kingdom stand?

**12:27** And if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons cast them out? For this reason, they themselves will be your judges.

**12:28** But if by the Spirit of God I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has arrived upon you.

**12:29** Or how can anyone enter the house of the strong man and carry off his goods, unless he first binds the strong man? And then he will plunder his house.

**12:30** The one who is not with Me is against Me, and the one who does not gather with Me scatters.

**12:31** For this reason I say to you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven.

**12:32** And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; yet whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, neither in this age nor in the one to come.

**12:33** Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree rotten and its fruit rotten; for from the fruit the tree is known.

**12:34** Offspring of vipers, how can you speak good things while being evil? For out of the overflow of the heart the mouth speaks.

**12:35** The good person brings out good things from the good treasure, and the evil person brings out evil things from the evil treasure.

**12:36** Now I say to you that for every idle word people will speak, they will render an account concerning it on the day of judgment;

**12:37** for from your words you will be justified, and from your words you will be condemned."

**12:38** Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered Him, saying, "Teacher, we want to see a sign from You."

**12:39** He answered and said to them, "An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, yet no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.

**12:40** For just as Jonah was in the belly of the sea monster for three days and three nights, so will the Son of Man be in the heart of the earth for three days and three nights.

**12:41** Men of Nineveh will stand up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at the proclamation of Jonah—and look, something greater than Jonah is here.

**12:42** The queen of the South will rise up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to listen to the wisdom of Solomon—and look, something greater than Solomon is here.

**12:43** Now when the unclean spirit goes out from a person, it passes through waterless places seeking rest, and finds none.

**12:44** Then it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came out.' And upon arriving, it finds it vacant, swept, and put in order.

**12:45** Then it goes and takes along with itself seven other spirits more evil than itself, and entering in, they settle there; and the last state of that person becomes worse than the first. Thus it will also be with this evil generation."

**12:46** While He was still speaking to the crowds, look, His mother and brothers were standing outside, seeking to speak to Him.

**12:47** Someone said to Him, "Look, Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, seeking to speak to You."

**12:48** But answering, He said to the one who told Him, "Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?"

**12:49** And stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, "Here are My mother and My brothers!

**12:50** For whoever does the will of My Father who is in heaven, that one is My brother and sister and mother."

## Translator Notes

- **12:4** — The Greek verb ephagon ("they ate") is plural in the earliest manuscripts, including David's companions, whereas later manuscripts harmonize it to the singular "he ate." "Loaves of presentation" literally renders tous artous tes protheseos, the consecrated bread set out in the sanctuary (1 Sam 21:1-6).
- **12:6** — The Greek adjective meizon ("something greater") is neuter rather than masculine, pointing broadly to the greater reality of Jesus's messianic ministry and kingdom; the same neuter construction recurs with pleion in 12:41-42.
- **12:20** — Matthew's quotation of Isaiah 42:3 renders the closing phrase as "to victory" (eis nikos), differing from the standard Hebrew ("in truth") and Septuagint wording to highlight the triumphant outcome of the Servant's justice.
- **12:40** — Greek ketos refers to a huge sea creature or sea monster, echoing the Septuagint rendering of the "great fish" in Jonah 2:1.
- **12:47** — This verse is omitted by several important early manuscripts (such as original Sinaiticus and Vaticanus), likely through an accidental scribal skip between the identical endings lalesai ("to speak") in verses 46 and 47.
