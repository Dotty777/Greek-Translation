## Text

**1:1** The book of the lineage of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.

**1:2** Abraham fathered Isaac, and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers,

**1:3** and Judah fathered Perez and Zerah by Tamar, and Perez fathered Hezron, and Hezron fathered Ram,

**1:4** and Ram fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon,

**1:5** and Salmon fathered Boaz by Rahab, and Boaz fathered Obed by Ruth, and Obed fathered Jesse,

**1:6** and Jesse fathered David the king. And David fathered Solomon by the wife of Uriah,

**1:7** and Solomon fathered Rehoboam, and Rehoboam fathered Abijah, and Abijah fathered Asaph,

**1:8** and Asaph fathered Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat fathered Joram, and Joram fathered Uzziah,

**1:9** and Uzziah fathered Jotham, and Jotham fathered Ahaz, and Ahaz fathered Hezekiah,

**1:10** and Hezekiah fathered Manasseh, and Manasseh fathered Amos, and Amos fathered Josiah,

**1:11** and Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers at the time of the deportation to Babylon.

**1:12** And after the deportation to Babylon, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel, and Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel,

**1:13** and Zerubbabel fathered Abiud, and Abiud fathered Eliakim, and Eliakim fathered Azor,

**1:14** and Azor fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Achim, and Achim fathered Eliud,

**1:15** and Eliud fathered Eleazar, and Eleazar fathered Matthan, and Matthan fathered Jacob,

**1:16** and Jacob fathered Joseph the husband of Mary, from whom Jesus was born, who is called Christ.

**1:17** Therefore all the generations from Abraham until David are fourteen generations, and from David until the deportation to Babylon, fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon until the Christ, fourteen generations.

**1:18** Now the birth of Jesus Christ came about in this way: after His mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together, she was found to be with child by the Holy Spirit.

**1:19** Now Joseph her husband, being a righteous man and not wanting to expose her publicly, decided to divorce her secretly.

**1:20** But after he had pondered these things, look, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, "Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for what has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.

**1:21** And she will give birth to a son, and you will call His name Jesus, for He Himself will save His people from their sins."

**1:22** Now all of this has happened so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet would be fulfilled, saying,

**1:23** "Look, the virgin will be with child and will give birth to a son, and they will call His name Immanuel," which translated means, "God with us."

**1:24** And when Joseph woke from sleep, he did just as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and took his wife,

**1:25** and he did not know her until she gave birth to a son; and he called His name Jesus.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek "biblos geneseos" ("book of origin/lineage") is the phrase used in the Septuagint of Genesis 2:4 and 5:1.
- **1:7** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "Asaph" here (and in 1:8) rather than King Asa, and similarly "Amos" in 1:10 rather than King Amon; later manuscripts assimilate both names to the Old Testament kings.
- **1:21** — The name Jesus (Hebrew Yeshua) means "the Lord saves," grounding the angel's declaration that He will save His people from their sins.
- **1:25** — "Did not know her" preserves the biblical idiom for marital relations (in the imperfect tense); the critical Greek text reads simply "a son" without "her firstborn" found in later manuscripts.
