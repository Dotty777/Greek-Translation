## Text

**9:1** And getting into a boat, He crossed over and came to His own city.

**9:2** And look, they were bringing to Him a paralyzed man lying on a bed. And Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralyzed man, "Take courage, child; your sins are forgiven."

**9:3** And look, some of the scribes said within themselves, "This man is blaspheming."

**9:4** And Jesus, seeing their thoughts, said, "Why are you thinking evil things in your hearts?

**9:5** For which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Get up and walk'?

**9:6** But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins"—then He said to the paralyzed man, "Get up, pick up your bed, and go to your house."

**9:7** And he got up and went away to his house.

**9:8** And when the crowds saw this, they were afraid, and they glorified God, who had given such authority to men.

**9:9** And as Jesus was passing on from there, He saw a man sitting at the tax booth, called Matthew, and He said to him, "Follow Me." And he rose and followed Him.

**9:10** And it happened that as He was reclining at table in the house, look, many tax collectors and sinners came and were reclining with Jesus and His disciples.

**9:11** And when the Pharisees saw this, they were saying to His disciples, "Why does your Teacher eat with the tax collectors and sinners?"

**9:12** But when He heard this, He said, "Those who are healthy do not have need of a physician, but those who are ill.

**9:13** Now go and learn what this means: 'I desire mercy and not sacrifice.' For I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners."

**9:14** Then the disciples of John came to Him, saying, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast, while Your disciples do not fast?"

**9:15** And Jesus said to them, "Can the sons of the bridal chamber mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? Yet the days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast.

**9:16** And no one puts a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; for its patch pulls away from the garment, and a worse tear occurs.

**9:17** Nor do people put new wine into old wineskins; otherwise, the wineskins burst, and the wine pours out and the wineskins are ruined. Instead, they put new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved."

**9:18** While He was speaking these things to them, look, a ruler approached and bowed down before Him, saying, "My daughter has just died; yet come and lay Your hand on her, and she will live."

**9:19** And Jesus got up and followed him, as did His disciples.

**9:20** And look, a woman who had been suffering from a flow of blood for twelve years approached from behind and touched the fringe of His garment,

**9:21** for she kept saying within herself, "If I only touch His garment, I will be made well."

**9:22** But Jesus, turning and seeing her, said, "Take courage, daughter; your faith has made you well." And the woman was made well from that hour.

**9:23** And when Jesus came into the house of the ruler and saw the flute players and the crowd making a commotion,

**9:24** He said, "Leave, for the girl has not died, but is sleeping." And they were laughing at Him.

**9:25** But when the crowd had been put outside, He entered and took hold of her hand, and the girl got up.

**9:26** And this report went out into all that land.

**9:27** And as Jesus passed on from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out and saying, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!"

**9:28** And when He entered the house, the blind men approached Him, and Jesus said to them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this?" They said to Him, "Yes, Lord."

**9:29** Then He touched their eyes, saying, "According to your faith let it be done for you."

**9:30** And their eyes were opened. And Jesus sternly warned them, saying, "See that no one knows about it."

**9:31** But they went out and spread the news about Him throughout all that land.

**9:32** Now as they were going out, look, they brought to Him a mute man who was demon-possessed.

**9:33** And when the demon had been cast out, the mute man spoke; and the crowds marveled, saying, "Never has anything like this been seen in Israel."

**9:34** But the Pharisees were saying, "By the ruler of the demons He casts out the demons."

**9:35** And Jesus was going around through all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom and healing every disease and every sickness.

**9:36** And seeing the crowds, He felt compassion for them, because they were troubled and cast down, like sheep having no shepherd.

**9:37** Then He said to His disciples, "The harvest indeed is plentiful, yet the workers are few.

**9:38** Therefore plead with the Lord of the harvest that He may send out workers into His harvest."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The critical text reads idon ('seeing') rather than the variant eidos ('knowing'), vividly depicting Jesus perceiving their thoughts.
- **9:15** — 'Sons of the bridal chamber' is a Semitic idiom referring to the wedding guests or attendants of the bridegroom.
- **9:21** — Greek sozo ('save') is used here and in v. 22 in the sense of physical healing and restoration ('be made well').
- **9:34** — This verse is omitted in Codex Bezae (D) and some Old Latin witnesses, though it is strongly attested in the primary Alexandrian and Byzantine manuscripts.
- **9:36** — Greek eskylmenoi kai errimmenoi literally means 'troubled/harassed and cast down,' vividly portraying a neglected and vulnerable crowd.
