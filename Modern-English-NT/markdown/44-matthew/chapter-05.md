## Text

**5:1** Now upon seeing the crowds, He went up onto the mountain; and after He sat down, His disciples came up to Him.

**5:2** And opening His mouth, He began teaching them, saying,

**5:3** "Blessed are those who are poor in spirit, because the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.

**5:4** Blessed are those who grieve, because they themselves will be comforted.

**5:5** Blessed are the gentle, because they themselves will inherit the earth.

**5:6** Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, because they themselves will be satisfied.

**5:7** Blessed are the merciful, because they themselves will be shown mercy.

**5:8** Blessed are those who are pure in heart, because they themselves will see God.

**5:9** Blessed are the peacemakers, because they themselves will be called sons of God.

**5:10** Blessed are those who have been persecuted for the sake of righteousness, because the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.

**5:11** Blessed are you whenever people insult you and persecute you and falsely speak every kind of evil against you on My account.

**5:12** Rejoice and be overjoyed, because your reward is great in heaven; for in the same way they persecuted the prophets who came before you.

**5:13** You yourselves are the salt of the earth; yet if the salt becomes tasteless, with what will it be made salty? It is no longer good for anything except to be thrown outside and trampled underfoot by people.

**5:14** You yourselves are the light of the world. A city situated on top of a mountain cannot be hidden;

**5:15** neither do people light a lamp and place it beneath a measuring basket, but rather on the lampstand, and it shines for everyone who is in the house.

**5:16** In the same way, let your light shine before people, so that they may see your good deeds and give glory to your Father who is in heaven.

**5:17** Do not suppose that I came to annul the Law or the Prophets; I did not come to annul, but to fulfill.

**5:18** For truly I say to you, until heaven and earth pass away, not a single iota nor a single stroke of a letter will ever pass away from the Law until everything comes to pass.

**5:19** Therefore, whoever sets aside one of the least of these commandments and teaches people accordingly will be called least in the kingdom of heaven; yet whoever practices and teaches them, this person will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

**5:20** For I tell you that unless your righteousness exceeds that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will certainly not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

**5:21** You have heard that it was told to those of ancient times, 'You must not murder, and whoever murders will be liable to judgment.'

**5:22** Yet I tell you that everyone who is angry with his brother will be liable to judgment; and whoever says to his brother, 'Raca,' will be liable to the Sanhedrin; and whoever says, 'You fool,' will be liable to the Gehenna of fire.

**5:23** So if you are presenting your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you,

**5:24** leave your gift there in front of the altar and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come back and present your gift.

**5:25** Come to friendly terms with your legal opponent quickly while you are on the road with him, so that the opponent does not hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you get thrown into prison.

**5:26** Truly I say to you, you will certainly not come out from there until you have paid back the last quadrans.

**5:27** You have heard that it was said, 'You must not commit adultery.'

**5:28** Yet I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman in order to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

**5:29** Now if your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your body parts be lost than for your entire body to be thrown into Gehenna.

**5:30** And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your body parts be lost than for your entire body to go off into Gehenna.

**5:31** Furthermore, it was said, 'Whoever divorces his wife must give her a certificate of divorce.'

**5:32** Yet I tell you that everyone who divorces his wife, except on the ground of sexual immorality, causes her to become an adulteress, and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.

**5:33** Again, you have heard that it was told to those of ancient times, 'You must not swear falsely, but you must fulfill your oaths to the Lord.'

**5:34** Yet I tell you not to swear an oath at all: neither by heaven, because it is God's throne;

**5:35** nor by the earth, because it is the footstool for His feet; nor by Jerusalem, because it is the city of the Great King;

**5:36** nor should you swear by your head, because you cannot make a single hair white or black.

**5:37** Instead, let your statement be 'Yes, yes,' or 'No, no'; anything beyond these comes from the evil one.

**5:38** You have heard that it was said, 'Eye for eye and tooth for tooth.'

**5:39** Yet I tell you not to resist an evil person; rather, whoever slaps you on the right cheek, turn the other one to him as well;

**5:40** and to the one who wants to take you to court and obtain your tunic, let him have your cloak as well;

**5:41** and whoever compels you into service for one mile, go along with him for two.

**5:42** Give to the one who asks from you, and do not turn away from the one who wants to borrow from you.

**5:43** You have heard that it was said, 'You must love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'

**5:44** Yet I tell you: love your enemies and pray on behalf of those who persecute you,

**5:45** so that you may become sons of your Father who is in heaven; because He causes His sun to rise on the evil and the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

**5:46** For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same thing?

**5:47** And if you greet only your brothers, what extraordinary thing are you doing? Do not even the Gentiles do the same thing?

**5:48** Therefore, you yourselves must be complete, just as your heavenly Father is complete."

## Translator Notes

- **5:22** — Raca is an Aramaic term of contempt meaning roughly 'empty-headed.' The critical Greek text omits 'without cause' after 'angry with his brother,' and Gehenna refers to the Valley of Hinnom as an image of final judgment.
- **5:26** — A quadrans was a small Roman bronze coin equal to one-fourth of an assarion, representing a trivial sum.
- **5:32** — The Greek noun porneia covers unlawful sexual conduct broadly, and the passive infinitive moicheuthenai literally conveys causing her to be made an adulteress (or subjected to adultery).
- **5:37** — The Greek phrase tou ponerou may be read as masculine ('the evil one') or neuter ('evil').
- **5:48** — The Greek adjective teleioi ('complete' or 'perfect') carries the sense of moral wholeness, maturity, and undivided devotion.
