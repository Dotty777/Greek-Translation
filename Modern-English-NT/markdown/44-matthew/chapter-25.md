## Text

**25:1** "Then the kingdom of heaven will be likened to ten virgins who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom.

**25:2** Now five of them were foolish, and five were wise.

**25:3** For when the foolish ones took their lamps, they took no oil with them;

**25:4** but the wise ones took oil in flasks along with their own lamps.

**25:5** Now while the bridegroom was delayed, they all grew drowsy and were sleeping.

**25:6** But at midnight a cry rang out: 'Look, the bridegroom! Come out to meet him!'

**25:7** Then all those virgins got up and trimmed their lamps.

**25:8** And the foolish ones said to the wise ones, 'Give us some of your oil, because our lamps are going out.'

**25:9** But the wise ones answered, saying, 'No, there will certainly not be enough for us and for you; go rather to those who sell and buy for yourselves.'

**25:10** And while they were going away to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready entered with him into the wedding banquet, and the door was shut.

**25:11** Afterward the other virgins also came, saying, 'Lord, lord, open to us!'

**25:12** But he answered and said, 'Truly I say to you, I do not know you.'

**25:13** Stay awake, therefore, because you know neither the day nor the hour.

**25:14** For it is just like a man going on a journey, who called his own slaves and entrusted his possessions to them.

**25:15** To one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one, to each according to his own ability, and he went on his journey. Immediately

**25:16** the one who had received the five talents went and traded with them, and gained five more talents.

**25:17** In the same way, the one with the two also gained two more.

**25:18** But the one who had received the one went away, dug in the ground, and hid his master's money.

**25:19** Now after a long time the master of those slaves came and settled accounts with them.

**25:20** And the one who had received the five talents came forward and brought five more talents, saying, 'Master, you entrusted five talents to me; look, I have gained five more talents.'

**25:21** His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful slave! You were faithful over a few things; I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your master.'

**25:22** The one with the two talents also came forward and said, 'Master, you entrusted two talents to me; look, I have gained two more talents.'

**25:23** His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful slave! You were faithful over a few things; I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your master.'

**25:24** Then the one who had received the one talent also came forward and said, 'Master, I knew you, that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow and gathering from where you did not scatter;

**25:25** and being afraid, I went away and hid your talent in the ground. Look, you have what is yours.'

**25:26** But his master answered and said to him, 'Wicked and lazy slave! You knew that I reap where I did not sow and gather from where I did not scatter?

**25:27** Therefore you ought to have deposited my money with the bankers, and upon returning I would have received back what is mine with interest.

**25:28** Therefore take the talent away from him and give it to the one who has the ten talents.

**25:29** For to everyone who has, more will be given and he will abound; yet from the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.

**25:30** And throw the worthless slave out into the outer darkness; in that place there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.'

**25:31** Now when the Son of Man comes in His glory, and all the angels with Him, then He will sit on the throne of His glory.

**25:32** And all the nations will be gathered before Him, and He will separate them one from another, just as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats;

**25:33** and He will place the sheep on His right, but the goats on the left.

**25:34** Then the King will say to those on His right, 'Come, you blessed ones of My Father, inherit the kingdom that has been prepared for you from the foundation of the world.

**25:35** For I was hungry and you gave Me food to eat; I was thirsty and you gave Me a drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in;

**25:36** naked, and you clothed Me; I was sick and you visited Me; I was in prison and you came to Me.'

**25:37** Then the righteous will answer Him, saying, 'Lord, when did we see You hungry and feed You, or thirsty and give You a drink?

**25:38** And when did we see You a stranger and take You in, or naked and clothe You?

**25:39** And when did we see You sick or in prison and come to You?'

**25:40** And answering, the King will say to them, 'Truly I say to you, to the degree that you did it to one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did it to Me.'

**25:41** Then He will also say to those on the left, 'Depart from Me, accursed ones, into the eternal fire that has been prepared for the devil and his angels.

**25:42** For I was hungry and you did not give Me food to eat; I was thirsty and you did not give Me a drink;

**25:43** I was a stranger and you did not take Me in; naked, and you did not clothe Me; sick and in prison, and you did not visit Me.'

**25:44** Then they themselves will also answer, saying, 'Lord, when did we see You hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and did not serve You?'

**25:45** Then He will answer them, saying, 'Truly I say to you, to the degree that you did not do it to one of the least of these, neither did you do it to Me.'

**25:46** And these will depart into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life."

## Translator Notes

- **25:6** — The Greek reads literally 'come out for a meeting' (without 'him'), using a term commonly applied to going out to welcome an arriving dignitary; 'him' is implied by context.
- **25:13** — Some later manuscripts add 'in which the Son of Man is coming,' which is absent from the earliest Greek witnesses and the Saints Greek Critical Text.
- **25:15** — A talent was a large weight of silver equal to roughly 6,000 denarii, or about twenty years of wages for a day laborer. The adverb 'Immediately' stands at the end of verse 15 in the Greek text to introduce the sentence continuing in verse 16.
- **25:40** — The phrase 'these brothers of Mine' (Greek adelphoi, which can include brothers and sisters) is variously understood as referring specifically to Christ's followers or more broadly to all people in need.
