## Text

**2:1** Now after Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, look, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem,

**2:2** saying, "Where is He who was born king of the Jews? For we saw His star in the east, and we came to worship Him."

**2:3** And when King Herod heard it, he was disturbed, and all of Jerusalem with him.

**2:4** And having assembled all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he was inquiring from them where the Christ was to be born.

**2:5** And they said to him, "In Bethlehem of Judea, for so it has been written through the prophet:

**2:6** 'And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are in no way least among the rulers of Judah; for from you a ruler will come out who will shepherd My people Israel.'"

**2:7** Then Herod, secretly summoning the magi, ascertained precisely from them the time of the star's appearance.

**2:8** And sending them to Bethlehem, he said, "Go and search carefully concerning the child; and as soon as you find Him, report back to me, so that I too may come and worship Him."

**2:9** And after listening to the king, they went on their way; and look, the star that they had seen in the east was going ahead of them, until it came and stood over where the child was.

**2:10** And on seeing the star, they rejoiced with an exceedingly great joy.

**2:11** And coming into the house, they saw the child with Mary His mother; and falling down, they worshiped Him. And opening their treasures, they offered Him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

**2:12** And having been warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they withdrew to their own country by another route.

**2:13** Now after they had withdrawn, look, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, "Rise, take the child and His mother, flee into Egypt, and stay there until I tell you, for Herod is about to seek the child in order to destroy Him."

**2:14** So he rose, took the child and His mother by night, and withdrew into Egypt,

**2:15** and he was there until the death of Herod, so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet would be fulfilled, saying, "Out of Egypt I called My Son."

**2:16** Then Herod, seeing that he had been tricked by the magi, became exceedingly angry, and he sent and put to death all the boys in Bethlehem and in all its surrounding districts, two years old and under, according to the time that he had ascertained precisely from the magi.

**2:17** Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying,

**2:18** "A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and much lamentation; Rachel weeping for her children, and she was unwilling to be comforted, because they are no more."

**2:19** Now when Herod died, look, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,

**2:20** saying, "Rise, take the child and His mother, and go into the land of Israel, for those who were seeking the child's life have died."

**2:21** So he rose, took the child and His mother, and entered the land of Israel.

**2:22** But when he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there; and having been warned in a dream, he withdrew into the districts of Galilee,

**2:23** and arriving there, he settled in a city called Nazareth, so that what was spoken through the prophets would be fulfilled, that He will be called a Nazarene.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek phrase "en te anatole" ("in the east") can also be understood astronomically as "at its rising."
- **2:15** — Matthew quotes Hosea 11:1, originally spoken of Israel's exodus from Egypt, applying it typologically to Jesus as God's Son.
- **2:23** — No single Old Testament passage contains these exact words; citing "the prophets" collectively, Matthew likely draws on wordplay with Hebrew "netser" ("branch," Isa 11:1) and/or prophetic themes of the Messiah's humble origins.
