## Text

**24:1** And leaving the temple, Jesus was going on His way, and His disciples approached to show Him the buildings of the temple.

**24:2** But He answered and said to them, "Do you not see all these things? Truly I tell you, by no means will a stone be left here upon a stone that will not be torn down."

**24:3** Now while He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, the disciples approached Him privately, saying, "Tell us, when will these things be, and what will be the sign of Your coming and of the close of the age?"

**24:4** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Watch out that no one leads you astray.

**24:5** For many will come in My name, saying, 'I am the Christ,' and they will lead many astray.

**24:6** And you are going to hear of wars and reports of wars. See that you are not alarmed, for it must take place, but the end is not yet.

**24:7** For nation will rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines and earthquakes in various places.

**24:8** But all these things are the beginning of labor pains.

**24:9** Then they will hand you over to affliction and kill you, and you will be hated by all the nations because of My name.

**24:10** And then many will fall away, and they will hand one another over and hate one another.

**24:11** And many false prophets will rise up and lead many astray.

**24:12** And because lawlessness will multiply, the love of the many will grow cold.

**24:13** Yet the one who perseveres to the end, that one will be saved.

**24:14** And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole inhabited world as a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.

**24:15** Therefore, when you see the abomination of desolation that was spoken of through Daniel the prophet standing in the holy place (let the reader understand),

**24:16** then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains;

**24:17** let the one on the housetop not go down to take the things out of his house,

**24:18** and let the one in the field not turn back to take his cloak.

**24:19** And woe to the women who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days!

**24:20** And pray that your flight will not take place in winter or on a Sabbath.

**24:21** For then there will be great affliction, such as has not occurred from the beginning of the world until now, nor will by any means occur.

**24:22** And if those days had not been cut short, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the chosen ones, those days will be cut short.

**24:23** Then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'Here!' do not believe it.

**24:24** For false christs and false prophets will rise up and perform great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen ones.

**24:25** See, I have told you beforehand.

**24:26** Therefore, if they say to you, 'Look, He is in the wilderness,' do not go out; 'Look, He is in the inner rooms,' do not believe it.

**24:27** For just as lightning comes out from the east and shines all the way to the west, so the coming of the Son of Man will be.

**24:28** Wherever the carcass is, there the vultures will be gathered together.

**24:29** And immediately after the affliction of those days, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its radiance, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.

**24:30** And then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.

**24:31** And He will send out His angels with a loud trumpet, and they will gather together His chosen ones from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other.

**24:32** Now learn the parable from the fig tree: whenever its branch has already become tender and sprouts leaves, you know that summer is near.

**24:33** In the same way, you also, when you see all these things, know that He is near, at the doors.

**24:34** Truly I tell you, this generation will by no means pass away until all these things take place.

**24:35** Heaven and earth will pass away, yet My words will by no means pass away.

**24:36** But concerning that day and hour no one knows, neither the angels of the heavens nor the Son, except the Father alone.

**24:37** For just as the days of Noah were, so the coming of the Son of Man will be.

**24:38** For just as in the days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, up to the day Noah entered the ark,

**24:39** and they did not know until the flood came and swept them all away—so also the coming of the Son of Man will be.

**24:40** Then two men will be in the field; one is taken and one is left.

**24:41** Two women will be grinding at the mill; one is taken and one is left.

**24:42** Stay awake, therefore, because you do not know on what day your Lord is coming.

**24:43** But know this: if the householder had known during which watch the thief was coming, he would have stayed awake and would not have allowed his house to be broken into.

**24:44** For this reason you also must be ready, because the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect.

**24:45** Who then is the faithful and sensible slave whom the master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time?

**24:46** Blessed is that slave whom his master, when he arrives, will find doing so.

**24:47** Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions.

**24:48** But if that wicked slave says in his heart, 'My master is taking a long time,'

**24:49** and begins to beat his fellow slaves, and eats and drinks with drunkards,

**24:50** the master of that slave will come on a day he does not expect and at an hour he does not know,

**24:51** and will cut him in two and assign his portion with the hypocrites. In that place there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth."

## Translator Notes

- **24:3** — Greek parousia means "coming" or "presence" (often used for a royal arrival), and synteleia denotes the "completion" or "close" of the age.
- **24:28** — Greek aetoi literally means "eagles," though in antiquity the term also included large carrion birds such as vultures, which fits the mention of a carcass.
- **24:33** — The clause has no explicit subject in Greek and can be translated either "He is near" (the Son of Man) or "it is near" (His coming or the end).
- **24:36** — Some later manuscripts omit "nor the Son," which the earliest witnesses and the Saints Greek Critical Text include.
- **24:51** — Greek dichotomesei literally means "will cut in two," denoting either literal execution or, figuratively, severe punishment and expulsion.
