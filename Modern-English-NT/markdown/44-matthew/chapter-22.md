## Text

**22:1** And answering, Jesus again spoke to them in parables, saying,

**22:2** "The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a man, a king, who prepared a wedding banquet for his son.

**22:3** And he sent out his slaves to summon those who had been invited to the wedding banquet, and they were unwilling to come.

**22:4** Again he sent out other slaves, saying, 'Tell those who have been invited: Look, I have prepared my feast; my bulls and the fattened livestock have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding banquet.'

**22:5** But paying no attention, they went away, one to his own field and another to his business;

**22:6** and the rest seized his slaves, mistreated them, and killed them.

**22:7** Then the king became angry, and sending his armies, he destroyed those murderers and set their city on fire.

**22:8** Then he said to his slaves, 'The wedding banquet is ready, yet those who were invited were not worthy.

**22:9** Go therefore to the main intersections of the roads, and invite to the wedding banquet as many as you find.'

**22:10** And going out into the roads, those slaves gathered together everyone whom they found, both evil and good; and the wedding banquet was filled with reclining guests.

**22:11** But when the king came in to view the reclining guests, he saw a man there who was not wearing a wedding garment.

**22:12** And he said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here not having a wedding garment?' And he was struck silent.

**22:13** Then the king said to the attendants, 'Bind his feet and hands and cast him out into the outer darkness; in that place there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.'

**22:14** For many are called, yet few are chosen."

**22:15** Then the Pharisees went away and took counsel on how they might trap Him in speech.

**22:16** And they sent their disciples to Him along with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know that You are truthful and teach the way of God in truth, and You are not concerned about anyone, for You do not look at the face of people.

**22:17** Tell us therefore what You think: Is it lawful to pay a poll tax to Caesar, or not?"

**22:18** But Jesus, knowing their malice, said, "Why are you testing Me, hypocrites?

**22:19** Show Me the coin of the poll tax." And they brought Him a denarius.

**22:20** And He said to them, "Whose is this image and inscription?"

**22:21** They said, "Caesar's." Then He said to them, "Pay back therefore the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God."

**22:22** And when they heard this, they marveled; and leaving Him, they went away.

**22:23** On that day Sadducees came to Him, saying that there is no resurrection, and they questioned Him,

**22:24** saying, "Teacher, Moses said, 'If someone dies having no children, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.'

**22:25** Now there were seven brothers among us; and the first married and died, and having no offspring, he left his wife to his brother.

**22:26** Likewise also the second and the third, through all seven.

**22:27** And after all of them, the woman died.

**22:28** In the resurrection, therefore, of which of the seven will she be the wife? For they all had her."

**22:29** But answering, Jesus said to them, "You are in error, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God.

**22:30** For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.

**22:31** Now concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, saying,

**22:32** 'I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living."

**22:33** And when the crowds heard this, they were astonished at His teaching.

**22:34** Now when the Pharisees heard that He had silenced the Sadducees, they gathered together in one place.

**22:35** And one of them, an expert in the law, questioned Him, testing Him:

**22:36** "Teacher, which commandment is greatest in the law?"

**22:37** And He said to him, "You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with your whole mind.

**22:38** This is the great and first commandment.

**22:39** And a second is similar to it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself.

**22:40** On these two commandments hang the whole law and the prophets."

**22:41** Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus questioned them,

**22:42** saying, "What do you think concerning the Christ? Whose son is He?" They said to Him, "David's."

**22:43** He said to them, "How then does David in the Spirit call Him 'Lord,' saying,

**22:44** 'The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at My right hand until I place Your enemies beneath Your feet'?

**22:45** If David then calls Him 'Lord,' how is He his son?"

**22:46** And no one was able to answer Him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day on to question Him any longer.

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — The phrase 'main intersections of the roads' (Greek diexodous ton hodon) literally refers to the outlets or thoroughfares where city streets open onto country roads.
- **22:16** — 'Look at the face of people' is a literal rendering of a Semitic idiom meaning to show partiality or be swayed by someone's outward status.
- **22:30** — Some manuscripts add 'of God' after 'angels,' whereas the critical text reads simply 'like angels in heaven.'
- **22:44** — Some later manuscripts read 'a footstool for Your feet' in conformity with the Septuagint of Psalm 110:1, while the critical text reads 'beneath Your feet' (hypokato).
