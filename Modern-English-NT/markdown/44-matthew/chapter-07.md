## Text

**7:1** "Do not judge, so that you will not be judged;

**7:2** for with whatever judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with whatever measure you measure, it will be measured to you.

**7:3** And why do you look at the splinter that is in your brother's eye, yet do not notice the beam in your own eye?

**7:4** Or how will you say to your brother, 'Let me take the splinter out of your eye,' when look, the beam is in your own eye?

**7:5** Hypocrite, first take the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take the splinter out of your brother's eye.

**7:6** Do not give what is holy to dogs, and do not throw your pearls before pigs, so that they do not trample them under their feet and turn and tear you to pieces.

**7:7** Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened for you.

**7:8** For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.

**7:9** Or what person is there among you whose son will ask him for bread—will he hand him a stone?

**7:10** Or if he also asks for a fish—will he hand him a snake?

**7:11** If you, then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in the heavens give good things to those who ask Him!

**7:12** Therefore, everything that you want people to do for you, do in the same way for them also; for this is the Law and the Prophets.

**7:13** Enter through the narrow gate; for wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many are those who enter through it.

**7:14** For narrow is the gate and constricted is the road that leads to life, and few are those who find it.

**7:15** Watch out for false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing, yet inwardly are ravenous wolves.

**7:16** By their fruits you will recognize them. Do people gather grapes from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?

**7:17** In this way, every good tree produces fine fruit, but the diseased tree produces bad fruit.

**7:18** A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor can a diseased tree produce fine fruit.

**7:19** Every tree that does not produce fine fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.

**7:20** So then, by their fruits you will recognize them.

**7:21** Not everyone who says to Me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter the kingdom of the heavens, but the one who does the will of My Father who is in the heavens.

**7:22** Many will say to Me on that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in Your name, and in Your name drive out demons, and in Your name perform many mighty works?'

**7:23** And then I will declare to them, 'I never knew you; depart from Me, you who practice lawlessness.'

**7:24** Therefore, everyone who hears these words of Mine and does them will be likened to a wise man who built his house on the rock.

**7:25** And the rain came down, and the torrents came, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, for it had been founded on the rock.

**7:26** And everyone who hears these words of Mine and does not do them will be likened to a foolish man who built his house on the sand.

**7:27** And the rain came down, and the torrents came, and the winds blew and struck against that house; and it fell, and its collapse was great."

**7:28** And it happened when Jesus finished these words, the crowds were astonished at His teaching;

**7:29** for He was teaching them as one having authority, and not as their scribes.

## Translator Notes

- **7:6** — "What is holy" (τὸ ἅγιον) alludes to sacrificial meat set apart for God and is used metaphorically for sacred truth or teaching.
- **7:14** — The Greek text reads ὅτι ("for"), while some manuscripts read τί ("how narrow is the gate!"). Greek τεθλιμμένη ("constricted") literally means pressed or hemmed in.
- **7:17** — Greek σαπρὸν ("diseased") literally denotes what is rotten, decayed, or unfit, contrasting with ἀγαθὸν ("good").
