## Text

**10:1** And calling His twelve disciples to Himself, He gave them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out and to heal every disease and every affliction.

**10:2** Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; and James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother;

**10:3** Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;

**10:4** Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, the one who also betrayed Him.

**10:5** These twelve Jesus sent out, instructing them and saying, "Do not go onto a road of the Gentiles, and do not enter a city of the Samaritans;

**10:6** but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

**10:7** And as you go, proclaim, saying, 'The kingdom of heaven has come near.'

**10:8** Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. Without cost you received; without cost give.

**10:9** Do not acquire gold, nor silver, nor copper for your belts,

**10:10** no bag for the road, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.

**10:11** And into whatever city or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and remain there until you leave.

**10:12** And as you enter the house, greet it.

**10:13** And if indeed the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.

**10:14** And whoever does not receive you nor listen to your words, as you go outside that house or that city, shake off the dust of your feet.

**10:15** Truly I say to you, it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that city.

**10:16** Look, I am sending you out as sheep in the midst of wolves; therefore be prudent as serpents and guileless as doves.

**10:17** But be on guard against people, for they will hand you over to councils, and in their synagogues they will flog you;

**10:18** and you will even be brought before governors and kings on account of Me, as a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.

**10:19** But when they hand you over, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak, for in that hour what you should speak will be given to you;

**10:20** for it is not you who are speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking in you.

**10:21** And brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child; and children will rise up against parents and have them put to death.

**10:22** And you will be hated by all because of My name; yet the one who endures to the end, that one will be saved.

**10:23** And whenever they persecute you in this city, flee to the next; for truly I say to you, you will by no means finish going through the cities of Israel until the Son of Man comes.

**10:24** A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a slave above his master.

**10:25** It is sufficient for the disciple that he become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more the members of his household!

**10:26** Therefore do not fear them, for nothing is covered that will not be uncovered, and hidden that will not be made known.

**10:27** What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim upon the housetops.

**10:28** And do not be afraid of those who kill the body yet are unable to kill the soul; rather, fear the One who is able to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.

**10:29** Are not two sparrows sold for a copper coin? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground apart from your Father.

**10:30** And as for you, even the hairs of your head have all been numbered.

**10:31** Therefore do not fear; you are of greater value than many sparrows.

**10:32** Everyone, therefore, who will acknowledge Me before people, I also will acknowledge him before My Father who is in heaven;

**10:33** but whoever denies Me before people, I also will deny him before My Father who is in heaven.

**10:34** Do not suppose that I came to bring peace on the earth; I came not to bring peace, but a sword.

**10:35** For I came to divide a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

**10:36** and a man's enemies will be the members of his own household.

**10:37** The one who loves father or mother above Me is not worthy of Me; and the one who loves son or daughter above Me is not worthy of Me;

**10:38** and whoever does not take up his cross and follow after Me is not worthy of Me.

**10:39** The one who has found his life will lose it, and the one who has lost his life on account of Me will find it.

**10:40** The one who receives you receives Me, and the one who receives Me receives the One who sent Me.

**10:41** The one who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet's reward, and the one who receives a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will receive a righteous person's reward.

**10:42** And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, truly I say to you, he will by no means lose his reward."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — "Cananaean" transliterates Greek Καναναῖος, derived from an Aramaic term meaning "zealous one" or "Zealot" (cf. Luke 6:15), not a resident of Cana or Canaan.
- **10:23** — "Finish going through" renders Greek τελέσητε ("complete"), referring to completing the mission circuit through Israel's towns; interpreters debate whether the coming of the Son of Man refers to His resurrection vindication, the judgment on Jerusalem in AD 70, or His final return.
- **10:28** — "Gehenna" transliterates Greek γέεννα (from the Hebrew Valley of Hinnom south of Jerusalem), used in Jewish and early Christian usage as a term for final divine judgment and hell.
- **10:29** — "Copper coin" translates Greek ἀσσάριον (assarion), a small Roman bronze coin worth one-sixteenth of a denarius; "apart from your Father" literally renders ἄνευ τοῦ πατρὸς ὑμῶν, conveying without His knowledge or will.
