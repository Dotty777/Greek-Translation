## Text

**4:1** Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit to be tempted by the devil.

**4:2** And having fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward He became hungry.

**4:3** And the tempter approached and said to Him, "If You are the Son of God, command that these stones become loaves of bread."

**4:4** But answering, He said, "It is written, 'Not on bread alone will a person live, but on every word going forth through the mouth of God.'"

**4:5** Then the devil took Him along into the holy city and placed Him on the pinnacle of the temple,

**4:6** and said to Him, "If You are the Son of God, throw Yourself down, for it is written, 'To His angels He will give orders concerning You, and on their hands they will lift You up, so that You do not strike Your foot against a stone.'"

**4:7** Jesus said to him, "Again, it is written, 'You shall not test the Lord your God.'"

**4:8** Again, the devil took Him along to an exceedingly high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory,

**4:9** and said to Him, "I will give all these things to You if You fall down and worship me."

**4:10** Then Jesus said to him, "Go away, Satan! For it is written, 'The Lord your God you shall worship, and Him alone you shall serve.'"

**4:11** Then the devil left Him, and look, angels approached and were serving Him.

**4:12** Now when He heard that John had been handed over, He withdrew into Galilee.

**4:13** And leaving Nazareth, He came and settled in Capernaum by the sea, in the district of Zebulun and Naphtali,

**4:14** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying:

**4:15** "Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations—

**4:16** the people sitting in darkness saw a great light, and for those sitting in the region and shadow of death, a light dawned on them."

**4:17** From that time Jesus began to proclaim and to say, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near."

**4:18** Now while walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon who is called Peter and Andrew his brother, throwing a casting net into the sea, for they were fishermen.

**4:19** And He said to them, "Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of people."

**4:20** And immediately leaving the nets, they followed Him.

**4:21** And going farther from there, He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, repairing their nets, and He called them.

**4:22** And immediately leaving the boat and their father, they followed Him.

**4:23** And He was going around throughout all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.

**4:24** And the report about Him went out into all Syria, and they brought to Him all who were unwell, those gripped by various diseases and severe pains, the demon-possessed and epileptics and paralytics, and He healed them.

**4:25** And great crowds followed Him from Galilee and the Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judea and beyond the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Greek pterygion literally means "little wing" and here refers to a high parapet, wing, or turret of the temple complex, traditionally rendered "pinnacle."
- **4:12** — Greek paredothe literally means "was handed over" (i.e., arrested or delivered into custody), the same verb Matthew uses later for Jesus being handed over.
- **4:15** — Greek hodon thalasses ("the way of the sea") reflects a Hebrew phrase meaning "toward the sea" or referring to the major trade route; Greek ton ethnon can be translated either "of the nations" or "of the Gentiles."
- **4:24** — Greek seleniazomenous literally means "moonstruck," an ancient term for people experiencing epilepsy or seizures.
