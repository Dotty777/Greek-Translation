## Text

**28:1** Now after the Sabbath, as it was dawning toward the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb.

**28:2** And look, a great earthquake occurred, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, came near, rolled away the stone, and was sitting on top of it.

**28:3** And his appearance was like lightning, and his garment white as snow.

**28:4** And from fear of him the guards were shaken and became like dead men.

**28:5** And answering, the angel said to the women, "As for you, do not be afraid, for I know that you are seeking Jesus, the One who was crucified.

**28:6** He is not here, for He has been raised, just as He said. Come here and see the place where He was lying.

**28:7** And go quickly and tell His disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead, and look, He is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see Him.' Look, I have told you."

**28:8** And departing quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, they ran to report to His disciples.

**28:9** And look, Jesus met them, saying, "Greetings!" And coming near, they grasped His feet and worshiped Him.

**28:10** Then Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go and report to My brothers to depart into Galilee, and there they will see Me."

**28:11** Now while they were on their way, look, some of the guard came into the city and reported to the chief priests all the things that had happened.

**28:12** And when they had assembled with the elders and taken counsel, they gave a large amount of silver to the soldiers,

**28:13** saying, "Say, 'His disciples came by night and stole Him while we were sleeping.'

**28:14** And if this is heard before the governor, we will win him over and make you free from concern."

**28:15** And taking the silver, they did as they were instructed. And this report was spread widely among the Jews to this very day.

**28:16** Now the eleven disciples journeyed into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had directed them,

**28:17** and seeing Him, they worshiped, yet some doubted.

**28:18** And Jesus came near and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and upon the earth.

**28:19** Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

**28:20** teaching them to keep everything that I commanded you. And look, I am with you all the days, until the completion of the age."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Greek phrase opse sabbaton can mean "late on the Sabbath," but in Hellenistic Greek opse with the genitive regularly means "after," matching the following clause describing the dawn of the first day of the week.
- **28:6** — Later Greek manuscripts add "the Lord" ("where the Lord was lying"), whereas the earliest witnesses omit the title and read simply "where He was lying."
- **28:17** — The Greek verb distazo ("doubted" or "hesitated") and the construction hoi de ("yet some") are debated as to whether a portion of the eleven wavered or whether the disciples as a whole felt initial hesitation alongside worship.
- **28:19** — The Greek preposition eis is literally "into" ("into the name").
