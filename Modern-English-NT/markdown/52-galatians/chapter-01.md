## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle, not from people nor through a person, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him from the dead,

**1:2** and all the brothers who are with me, to the churches of Galatia:

**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,

**1:4** who gave Himself for our sins so that He might rescue us out of the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,

**1:5** to whom belongs the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**1:6** I am astonished that you are so quickly turning away from the One who called you in the grace of Christ to a different gospel,

**1:7** which is not another; only there are some who are unsettling you and wanting to distort the gospel of Christ.

**1:8** But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim to you a gospel contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.

**1:9** As we have said beforehand, now I say again: if anyone is proclaiming to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.

**1:10** For am I now trying to win over people, or God? Or am I seeking to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be a slave of Christ.

**1:11** For I make known to you, brothers, that the gospel proclaimed by me is not human in origin;

**1:12** for neither did I receive it from a human, nor was I taught it, but through a revelation of Jesus Christ.

**1:13** For you heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how beyond measure I was persecuting the church of God and ravaging it,

**1:14** and I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my contemporaries among my own people, being far more zealous for my ancestral traditions.

**1:15** But when the One who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me through His grace was pleased

**1:16** to reveal His Son in me, so that I might proclaim Him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult with flesh and blood,

**1:17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away to Arabia and returned again to Damascus.

**1:18** Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas, and I stayed with him fifteen days.

**1:19** But I did not see any other of the apostles, except James, the Lord's brother.

**1:20** Now regarding what I am writing to you, look, before God, I am not lying.

**1:21** Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

**1:22** And I was still unknown by face to the churches of Judea that are in Christ;

**1:23** they only kept hearing, "The one who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the faith that he formerly ravaged,"

**1:24** and they kept glorifying God on account of me.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Greek text places 'our' with 'Lord Jesus Christ' rather than 'God the Father' (other manuscripts read 'God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ').
- **1:6** — Paul pairs two Greek words for 'another' across verses 6-7 (heteros and allos), highlighting that this rival message is a different kind of message rather than another legitimate version of the gospel.
- **1:12** — The phrase 'a revelation of Jesus Christ' can denote either a revelation given by Jesus Christ (subjective genitive) or a revelation disclosing Jesus Christ (objective genitive; compare 1:16).
- **1:15** — This Greek text omits 'God' before 'the One who set me apart,' leaving the divine subject implied (other manuscripts explicitly supply 'God').
