## Text

**5:1** For freedom Christ set us free; therefore stand firm, and do not again be held in a yoke of slavery.

**5:2** Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you let yourselves be circumcised, Christ will not benefit you at all.

**5:3** And I testify again to every man who lets himself be circumcised that he is a debtor to do the whole law.

**5:4** You have been cut off from Christ, you who are trying to be justified by law; you have fallen away from grace.

**5:5** For by the Spirit, from faith, we eagerly await the hope of righteousness.

**5:6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any force, but faith active through love.

**5:7** You were running well; who obstructed you from obeying the truth?

**5:8** This persuasion is not from the One calling you.

**5:9** A small amount of leaven leavens the entire lump of dough.

**5:10** I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will think no other way; but the one troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever he may be.

**5:11** But I, brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been nullified.

**5:12** I wish that those upsetting you would even castrate themselves.

**5:13** For you were called to freedom, brothers; only do not use that freedom as an occasion for the flesh, but through love be slaves to one another.

**5:14** For the entire law has been fulfilled in a single saying, in this: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**5:15** But if you are biting and devouring one another, watch that you are not consumed by one another.

**5:16** Now I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will certainly not fulfill the desire of the flesh.

**5:17** For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these stand in opposition to one another, so that you do not do the things you wish.

**5:18** But if you are being led by the Spirit, you are not under law.

**5:19** Now the works of the flesh are plain, which are sexual immorality, impurity, debauchery,

**5:20** idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousies, outbursts of rage, selfish ambitions, dissensions, factions,

**5:21** envies, drunken bouts, carousings, and things similar to these, about which I forewarn you, just as I told you beforehand, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

**5:22** Now the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,

**5:23** gentleness, self-control; there is no law against such things.

**5:24** Now those belonging to Christ Jesus crucified the flesh together with its passions and desires.

**5:25** If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk in line with the Spirit.

**5:26** Let us not become conceited, challenging one another, envying one another.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The participle energoumene may be read as middle ("active/working through love") or passive ("made effective through love").
- **5:12** — The verb apokopsontai literally means "cut themselves off," a pointed play on circumcision referring to castration or emasculation.
- **5:20** — The Greek text here reads the plural zeloi ("jealousies"), matching the surrounding plural vices, whereas some manuscripts read the singular zelos ("jealousy").
- **5:22** — The Greek noun pistis can mean either "faith" or "faithfulness"; in this list of moral virtues, "faithfulness" (trustworthiness) fits the context.
