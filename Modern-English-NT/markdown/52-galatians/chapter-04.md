## Text

**4:1** Now I say that as long as the heir is a child, he does not differ at all from a slave, though he is master of everything;

**4:2** rather, he is under guardians and stewards until the time appointed by the father.

**4:3** In the same way we also, when we were children, were enslaved under the elemental things of the world.

**4:4** But when the fullness of the time arrived, God sent out His Son, born from a woman, born under the law,

**4:5** so that He might redeem those who were under the law, in order that we might receive the adoption as sons.

**4:6** And because you are sons, God sent out the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying out, "Abba, Father!"

**4:7** So then, you are no longer a slave but a son; and if a son, also an heir through God.

**4:8** However, at that time, not knowing God, you were enslaved to those that by nature are not gods.

**4:9** But now, having come to know God—or rather, having been known by God—how are you turning back again to the weak and poor elemental things, to which you desire to be enslaved all over again?

**4:10** You are scrupulously observing days and months and seasons and years.

**4:11** I am afraid for you, that perhaps I have labored for you in vain.

**4:12** Become as I am, for I also became as you are, brothers, I plead with you. You did me no wrong;

**4:13** and you know that it was because of a weakness of the flesh that I preached the good news to you the first time,

**4:14** and your trial in my flesh you did not despise or spurn; instead, you welcomed me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.

**4:15** Where then is that blessedness of yours? For I testify to you that, if it had been possible, you would have gouged out your eyes and given them to me.

**4:16** Therefore, have I become an enemy of yours by speaking the truth to you?

**4:17** They eagerly seek you, not in a good way, but they want to shut you out, so that you will eagerly seek them.

**4:18** Yet it is good to be eagerly sought for a good purpose at all times, and not only when I am present with you,

**4:19** my children, with whom I am again in labor until Christ is formed in you.

**4:20** And I wish I could be present with you right now and change my tone, because I am at a loss concerning you.

**4:21** Tell me, you who want to be under the law: do you not hear the law?

**4:22** For it is written that Abraham had two sons: one from the slave woman and one from the free woman.

**4:23** Yet the one from the slave woman was born according to the flesh, while the one from the free woman was born through promise.

**4:24** Now these things are spoken allegorically: for these women are two covenants—one from Mount Sinai, bearing children into slavery, which is Hagar.

**4:25** Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and she corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery together with her children.

**4:26** But the Jerusalem above is free; she is our mother.

**4:27** For it is written, "Be glad, barren woman who does not give birth; burst forth and shout, you who have no labor pains; because the children of the desolate woman are many more than those of the woman who has a husband."

**4:28** Now as for you, brothers, just like Isaac, you are children of promise.

**4:29** Yet just as at that time the one born according to the flesh kept persecuting the one born according to the Spirit, so it is now as well.

**4:30** But what does the Scripture say? "Drive out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman will certainly not inherit along with the son of the free woman."

**4:31** For this reason, brothers, we are not children of a slave woman, but of the free woman.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The phrase ta stoicheia tou kosmou ('the elemental things of the world') is debated and may refer to rudimentary religious principles, basic elements of the universe, or cosmic spiritual powers.
- **4:14** — The verb exeptysate literally means 'spat out,' an ancient idiom expressing strong aversion or a gesture to ward off ill omen.
- **4:25** — The Greek places the neuter article to before Hagar, treating the name itself as a term linked to Mount Sinai in Arabia; some ancient manuscripts omit 'Hagar' here.
- **4:28** — The critical text reads 'you...are' (hymeis...este), addressing the Galatians directly, whereas some later manuscripts read 'we...are.'
