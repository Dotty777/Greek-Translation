## Text

**6:1** Brothers, even if a person is overtaken in some trespass, you who are spiritual, restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness, keeping watch on yourself, so that you also may not be tempted.

**6:2** Bear one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.

**6:3** For if someone thinks himself to be something while being nothing, he deceives his own mind.

**6:4** But let each person test his own work, and then he will have his ground for boasting in regard to himself alone and not in regard to the other.

**6:5** For each person will bear his own load.

**6:6** Now let the one who is instructed in the word share in all good things with the one who instructs.

**6:7** Do not be misled: God is not mocked; for whatever a person sows, that he will also reap.

**6:8** Because the one sowing to his own flesh will reap decay from the flesh, but the one sowing to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.

**6:9** And let us not lose heart in doing what is good, for at the proper time we will reap if we do not grow faint.

**6:10** So then, as we have opportunity, let us do what is good toward all people, and especially toward those belonging to the household of the faith.

**6:11** See with what large letters I have written to you with my own hand.

**6:12** As many as want to make a fine impression in the flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, only so that they will not be persecuted because of the cross of Christ.

**6:13** For not even those who have been circumcised keep the law themselves, yet they want you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.

**6:14** As for me, may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

**6:15** For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is anything, but a new creation.

**6:16** And as many as will walk in line with this rule, peace be upon them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.

**6:17** From this point on, let no one give me trouble, for I bear the brand marks of Jesus in my body.

**6:18** May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Paul distinguishes "burdens" (bare, heavy weights) here from "load" (phortion, an individual pack or responsibility) in v. 5; the verb anaplerosete is future indicative ("you will fulfill"), though some manuscripts read an imperative.
- **6:11** — "Large letters" likely reflects Paul taking the pen for emphasis (or possibly poor eyesight), and egrapsa may be read as a simple past ("I have written") or an epistolary aorist ("I am writing").
- **6:14** — The masculine relative pronoun di hou may refer either to the cross ("through which") or to Christ ("through Whom").
- **6:16** — The conjunction kai ("and") before "upon the Israel of God" is debated: it may either equate the "Israel of God" with all who follow this rule ("even upon...") or designate believing Jews alongside Gentile believers ("and upon...").
