## Text

**11:1** And when they were approaching Jerusalem, at Bethphage and Bethany near the Mount of Olives, He sent two of His disciples

**11:2** and said to them, "Go into the village opposite you, and immediately as you enter it, you will find a colt tied on which no person has yet sat; untie it and bring it.

**11:3** And if anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord has need of it, and immediately He will send it back here.'"

**11:4** And they went away and found a colt tied by the door outside on the street, and they untied it.

**11:5** And some of those standing there were saying to them, "What are you doing, untying the colt?"

**11:6** And they spoke to them just as Jesus had said, and they let them go.

**11:7** And they brought the colt to Jesus and placed their cloaks on it, and He sat on it.

**11:8** And many spread their cloaks on the road, and others leafy branches that they had cut from the fields.

**11:9** And those going ahead and those following were crying out, "Hosanna! Blessed is the One who comes in the name of the Lord!

**11:10** Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David! Hosanna in the highest!"

**11:11** And He entered Jerusalem, into the temple; and after looking around at everything, since the hour was already late, He went out to Bethany with the Twelve.

**11:12** And on the next day, when they had come out from Bethany, He became hungry.

**11:13** And seeing from a distance a fig tree that had leaves, He went to see if perhaps He would find anything on it; and when He came to it, He found nothing except leaves, for it was not the season for figs.

**11:14** And answering, He said to it, "May no one ever eat fruit from you again!" And His disciples were listening.

**11:15** And they came to Jerusalem. And entering the temple, He began to drive out those who were selling and those who were buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of those who were selling the doves,

**11:16** and He would not allow anyone to carry any object through the temple.

**11:17** And He was teaching and saying to them, "Is it not written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations'? But you have made it a cave of robbers."

**11:18** And the chief priests and the scribes heard it, and they were seeking how they might destroy Him; for they feared Him, because the entire crowd was astonished at His teaching.

**11:19** And whenever evening came, they would go outside the city.

**11:20** And as they were passing by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots.

**11:21** And remembering, Peter said to Him, "Rabbi, look! The fig tree that You cursed has withered!"

**11:22** And answering, Jesus said to them, "Have faith in God.

**11:23** Truly I say to you that whoever says to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that what he speaks will come about, it will be done for him.

**11:24** For this reason I say to you, all things that you pray and ask for, believe that you have received them, and they will be yours.

**11:25** And whenever you stand praying, forgive if you have anything against anyone, so that your Father who is in heaven may also forgive you your transgressions."

**11:27** And they came again to Jerusalem. And as He was walking in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders came to Him,

**11:28** and they were saying to Him, "By what authority are You doing these things? Or who gave You this authority to do these things?"

**11:29** And Jesus said to them, "I will also ask you one question; answer Me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things:

**11:30** The baptism of John—was it from heaven or from people? Answer Me."

**11:31** And they were reasoning among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will say, 'Why then did you not believe him?'

**11:32** But should we say, 'From people'?"—they were afraid of the crowd, for everyone held that John truly was a prophet.

**11:33** And answering Jesus, they said, "We do not know." And Jesus said to them, "Neither am I telling you by what authority I do these things."

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — The closing clause ("and immediately He will send it back here") can be punctuated either as part of the disciples' promise that the Lord will promptly return the colt, or as Jesus' assurance that the owner will immediately send it to Him.
- **11:22** — Literally "Have faith of God"; the genitive is usually taken objectively as "faith in God," though some read it as "faith from God" or "God's faithfulness."
- **11:24** — The verb elabete is aorist, literally 'you received.'
- **11:25** — The verse numbered 11:26 in later manuscripts (a parallel to Matthew 6:15) is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts; this text prints it in double brackets.
- **11:32** — The leaders' question breaks off mid-thought (aposiopesis) as the narrator interjects the reason for their hesitation.
- **11:26 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
