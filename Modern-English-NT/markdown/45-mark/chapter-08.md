## Text

**8:1** In those days, when there was again a large crowd and they had nothing to eat, calling His disciples to Himself, He said to them,

**8:2** "I feel compassion for the crowd, because they have remained with Me for three days already and have nothing to eat.

**8:3** And if I send them away hungry to their homes, they will faint on the way; and some of them have come from far away."

**8:4** And His disciples answered Him, "Where will anyone be able to satisfy these people with bread here in a desolate place?"

**8:5** And He asked them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven."

**8:6** And He instructed the crowd to recline on the ground; and taking the seven loaves, after giving thanks, He broke them and kept giving them to His disciples to serve, and they served them to the crowd.

**8:7** They also had a few small fish; and after blessing them, He told them to serve these as well.

**8:8** And they ate and were satisfied; and they picked up seven large baskets of leftover broken pieces.

**8:9** Now there were about four thousand people. And He sent them away.

**8:10** And immediately getting into the boat with His disciples, He came to the region of Dalmanutha.

**8:11** And the Pharisees came out and began to debate with Him, seeking from Him a sign from heaven, testing Him.

**8:12** And sighing deeply in His spirit, He said, "Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I say to you, no sign will be given to this generation."

**8:13** And leaving them, embarking again, He departed to the other side.

**8:14** And they had forgotten to bring bread, and except for one loaf, they had none with them in the boat.

**8:15** And He was cautioning them, saying, "Watch out! Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod."

**8:16** And they were reasoning with one another because they had no bread.

**8:17** And aware of this, Jesus said to them, "Why are you reasoning because you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or understand? Do you have your heart hardened?

**8:18** Having eyes, do you not see, and having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember,

**8:19** when I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces you picked up?" They said to Him, "Twelve."

**8:20** "And when I broke the seven for the four thousand, how many large basketfuls of broken pieces did you pick up?" And they said, "Seven."

**8:21** And He said to them, "How do you not yet understand?"

**8:22** And they came to Bethsaida. And they brought a blind man to Him and begged Him to touch him.

**8:23** And taking hold of the blind man's hand, He led him outside the village; and after spitting on his eyes and laying His hands on him, He asked him, "Do you see anything?"

**8:24** And looking up, he said, "I see people, for I see them like trees, walking around."

**8:25** Then again He laid His hands on his eyes; and he looked intently and was restored, and began to see everything clearly.

**8:26** And He sent him to his home, saying, "Do not even enter the village."

**8:27** And Jesus and His disciples went out to the villages of Caesarea Philippi; and on the way He asked His disciples, saying to them, "Who do people say that I am?"

**8:28** And they told Him, saying, "John the Baptist; and others say Elijah; and others, one of the prophets."

**8:29** And He asked them, "But who do you say that I am?" Peter answered and said to Him, "You are the Christ."

**8:30** And He sternly warned them to tell no one about Him.

**8:31** And He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and the chief priests and the scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise.

**8:32** And He was speaking the word plainly. And taking Him aside, Peter began to rebuke Him.

**8:33** But turning around and seeing His disciples, He rebuked Peter and said, "Get behind Me, Satan! For you are not setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of human beings."

**8:34** And calling the crowd to Himself along with His disciples, He said to them, "If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow Me.

**8:35** For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life on account of Me and the gospel will save it.

**8:36** For what does it benefit a person to gain the entire world and forfeit his life?

**8:37** For what could a person give in exchange for his life?

**8:38** For whoever is ashamed of Me and My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels."

## Translator Notes

- **8:12** — Literally "if a sign will be given to this generation," a Semitic elliptical oath idiom expressing an emphatic negation.
- **8:20** — Mark distinguishes two kinds of baskets in 8:19 and 8:20: kophinos (v. 19) is a smaller hand-basket, whereas spyris (vv. 8, 20) is a large woven hamper.
- **8:26** — Some later manuscripts expand Jesus' command with "nor tell anyone in the village," whereas the earliest witnesses preserve the shorter text translated here.
- **8:35** — The Greek word psyche (also in vv. 36-37) spans both physical "life" and one's inner "soul" or "self."
