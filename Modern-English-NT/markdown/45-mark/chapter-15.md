## Text

**15:1** And immediately, early in the morning, the chief priests with the elders and scribes and the entire Sanhedrin held a consultation; and having bound Jesus, they led Him away and handed Him over to Pilate.

**15:2** And Pilate questioned Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And answering him, He said, "You say so."

**15:3** And the chief priests were accusing Him of many things.

**15:4** Then Pilate questioned Him again, saying, "Do You answer nothing? Look how many things they are accusing You of."

**15:5** Yet Jesus answered nothing further, so that Pilate was amazed.

**15:6** Now at the festival he used to release to them one prisoner, whomever they requested.

**15:7** And the man called Barabbas had been bound with the insurrectionists who had committed murder in the insurrection.

**15:8** And going up, the crowd began to ask him to do just as he used to do for them.

**15:9** And Pilate answered them, saying, "Do you want me to release for you the King of the Jews?"

**15:10** For he knew that it was because of envy that the chief priests had handed Him over.

**15:11** But the chief priests stirred up the crowd so that he would instead release Barabbas for them.

**15:12** And answering again, Pilate said to them, "Then what should I do with the One you call the King of the Jews?"

**15:13** And they cried out again, "Crucify Him!"

**15:14** But Pilate said to them, "Why, what evil has He done?" Yet they cried out all the more, "Crucify Him!"

**15:15** So Pilate, wanting to satisfy the crowd, released Barabbas for them, and after having Jesus flogged, he handed Him over so that He might be crucified.

**15:16** Then the soldiers led Him away inside the courtyard, which is the Praetorium, and they called together the entire cohort.

**15:17** And they dressed Him in purple, and after weaving a crown of thorns, they placed it on Him.

**15:18** And they began to salute Him, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**15:19** And they kept striking His head with a reed and spitting on Him, and bending their knees, they were bowing in homage to Him.

**15:20** And when they had mocked Him, they stripped the purple off Him and put His own garments on Him. And they led Him out to crucify Him.

**15:21** And they pressed into service a passerby, Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the countryside, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry His cross.

**15:22** And they brought Him to the place Golgotha, which is translated, "Place of a Skull."

**15:23** And they tried to give Him wine mixed with myrrh, but He did not take it.

**15:24** And they crucified Him and divided His garments among themselves, casting lots for them as to who should take what.

**15:25** Now it was the third hour, and they crucified Him.

**15:26** And the inscription of the charge against Him had been written: "The King of the Jews."

**15:27** And with Him they crucified two robbers, one on His right and one on His left.

**15:29** And those passing by kept reviling Him, shaking their heads and saying, "Aha! You who destroy the temple and build it in three days,

**15:30** save Yourself by coming down from the cross!"

**15:31** In the same way, the chief priests also, mocking Him among themselves together with the scribes, were saying, "He saved others; He cannot save Himself.

**15:32** Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, so that we may see and believe!" And those who were crucified with Him were reproaching Him.

**15:33** And when the sixth hour came, darkness fell over the whole land until the ninth hour.

**15:34** And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" which is translated, "My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?"

**15:35** And some of those standing nearby, hearing this, were saying, "Look, He is calling Elijah."

**15:36** Then someone ran and filled a sponge with sour wine, put it on a reed, and offered it to Him to drink, saying, "Wait, let us see if Elijah is coming to take Him down."

**15:37** And Jesus let out a loud cry and breathed His last.

**15:38** And the curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.

**15:39** Now when the centurion, who was standing directly opposite Him, saw that He breathed His last in this way, he said, "Truly this man was the Son of God!"

**15:40** There were also women looking on from a distance, among whom were both Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome,

**15:41** who, when He was in Galilee, used to follow Him and serve Him, as well as many other women who had come up with Him to Jerusalem.

**15:42** And when evening had already arrived, since it was Preparation Day, that is, the day before the Sabbath,

**15:43** Joseph of Arimathea, a respected member of the council who himself was also waiting for the kingdom of God, came, and gathering his courage, went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.

**15:44** And Pilate was surprised that He was already dead, and summoning the centurion, he questioned him as to whether He had died some time ago.

**15:45** And when he learned this from the centurion, he granted the corpse to Joseph.

**15:46** And after buying a linen cloth, he took Him down, wrapped Him in the linen cloth, and laid Him in a tomb that had been cut out of rock; and he rolled a stone against the entrance of the tomb.

**15:47** Now Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses were watching where He had been laid.

## Translator Notes

- **15:25** — "The third hour" corresponds to roughly 9:00 a.m. in ancient daylight reckoning; likewise, the sixth and ninth hours in 15:33-34 correspond to about noon and 3:00 p.m.
- **15:27** — The earliest Greek manuscripts lack verse 28 (printed in double brackets in this text), which probably entered later manuscripts from Luke 22:37.
- **15:34** — Jesus quotes Psalm 22:1 in Aramaic ("Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?"), whose opening sounds resemble the name Elijah, explaining the bystanders' misunderstanding in verse 35.
- **15:39** — The predicate huios theou has no article and stands before the verb, so it can be translated either 'the Son of God' or 'a son of God.'
- **15:28 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
