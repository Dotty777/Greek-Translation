## Text

**9:1** And He was saying to them, "Truly I tell you that there are some of those standing here who will by no means taste death until they see the kingdom of God having come in power."

**9:2** And after six days, Jesus took Peter and James and John with Him and led them up onto a high mountain privately by themselves; and He was transfigured before them,

**9:3** and His garments became gleaming, intensely white, so white that no launderer on the earth is able to whiten them like that.

**9:4** And Elijah appeared to them with Moses, and they were conversing with Jesus.

**9:5** And Peter responded and said to Jesus, "Rabbi, it is good for us to be here; and let us make three tents, one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

**9:6** For he did not know what to answer, because they had become terrified.

**9:7** And a cloud formed, overshadowing them, and a voice came out of the cloud: "This is My beloved Son; listen to Him."

**9:8** And suddenly, looking around, they no longer saw anyone except Jesus alone with them.

**9:9** And as they were coming down from the mountain, He instructed them to tell no one the things they had seen, except after the Son of Man has risen from the dead.

**9:10** And they kept this saying to themselves, discussing what rising from the dead meant.

**9:11** And they were questioning Him, saying, "Why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?"

**9:12** And He said to them, "Elijah indeed comes first and restores all things; and how is it written concerning the Son of Man that He should suffer many things and be treated with contempt?

**9:13** But I tell you that Elijah has indeed come, and they did to him whatever they wished, just as it is written concerning him."

**9:14** And when they came to the disciples, they saw a large crowd around them and scribes debating with them.

**9:15** And immediately the entire crowd, upon seeing Him, were greatly amazed, and running up, they were greeting Him.

**9:16** And He questioned them, "What are you debating among yourselves?"

**9:17** And one from the crowd answered Him, "Teacher, I brought my son to You, who has a mute spirit;

**9:18** and wherever it seizes him, it slams him down, and he foams at the mouth and grinds his teeth and becomes rigid; and I spoke to Your disciples to cast it out, and they were not able."

**9:19** And answering them, He said, "O unbelieving generation, how long will I be with you? How long will I put up with you? Bring him to Me."

**9:20** And they brought him to Him. And when the spirit saw Him, immediately it convulsed him violently, and falling upon the ground, he was rolling about, foaming at the mouth.

**9:21** And He questioned his father, "How long a time has it been since this happened to him?" And he said, "From childhood;

**9:22** and often it threw him both into fire and into waters in order to destroy him. But if You can do anything, have compassion on us and help us."

**9:23** And Jesus said to him, "'If You can'! All things are possible for the one who believes."

**9:24** Immediately the father of the child cried out and was saying, "I believe; help my unbelief!"

**9:25** And when Jesus saw that a crowd was running together, He rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, "You mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him and never enter into him again."

**9:26** And crying out and convulsing him greatly, it came out; and he became like one dead, so that most of them were saying that he had died.

**9:27** But Jesus, taking hold of his hand, raised him up, and he stood up.

**9:28** And when He had entered a house, His disciples were questioning Him privately, "Why were we not able to cast it out?"

**9:29** And He said to them, "This kind cannot come out by anything except by prayer."

**9:30** And going out from there, they were passing through Galilee, and He did not want anyone to know;

**9:31** for He was teaching His disciples and saying to them, "The Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of men, and they will kill Him; and when He has been killed, after three days He will rise."

**9:32** But they did not understand the statement, and they were afraid to question Him.

**9:33** And they came to Capernaum. And when He was in the house, He questioned them, "What were you discussing on the way?"

**9:34** But they kept silent, for on the way they had argued with one another about who was the greatest.

**9:35** And sitting down, He called the twelve and said to them, "If anyone wishes to be first, he shall be last of all and servant of all."

**9:36** And taking a child, He set him in their midst; and taking him in His arms, He said to them,

**9:37** "Whoever receives one of such children in My name receives Me; and whoever receives Me does not receive Me, but the One who sent Me."

**9:38** John said to Him, "Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in Your name, and we were trying to stop him, because he was not following us."

**9:39** But Jesus said, "Do not stop him, for there is no one who will perform a mighty work in My name and be able soon afterward to speak evil of Me.

**9:40** For anyone who is not against us is for us.

**9:41** For whoever gives you a cup of water to drink on account of the name, because you belong to Christ, truly I say to you that he will by no means lose his reward.

**9:42** And whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it would be better for him if a donkey-turned millstone were hung around his neck and he were thrown into the sea.

**9:43** And if your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life maimed than, having two hands, to go away into Gehenna, into the unquenchable fire.

**9:45** And if your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life lame than, having two feet, to be thrown into Gehenna.

**9:47** And if your eye causes you to stumble, throw it out; it is better for you to enter into the kingdom of God with one eye than, having two eyes, to be thrown into Gehenna,

**9:48** where their worm does not perish, and the fire is not quenched.

**9:49** For everyone will be salted with fire.

**9:50** Salt is good; but if the salt becomes unsalty, with what will you season it? Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another."

## Translator Notes

- **9:16** — The Greek text reads the reflexive pronoun 'αὑτούς' ('among yourselves' / 'with one another'); other editions read 'αὐτούς' ('with them', referring to the disciples).
- **9:23** — The Greek places the definite article 'Τό' before 'Εἰ δύνῃ' ('The "If You can"!'), quoting and challenging the father's hesitant condition from verse 22.
- **9:29** — Several early manuscripts (including Codex Vaticanus) end the verse with 'by prayer'; most others add 'and fasting'.
- **9:43** — 'Gehenna' transliterates the Greek name for the Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem, used for the place of final fiery judgment. Verses 44 and 46, which repeat verse 48 in later manuscripts, are absent from the earliest witnesses.
- **9:49** — Later manuscripts add 'and every sacrifice will be salted with salt' (compare Leviticus 2:13).
- **9:44 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts (compare 9:48).
- **9:46 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts (compare 9:48).
