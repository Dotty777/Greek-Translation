## Text

**1:1** The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, Son of God.

**1:2** Just as it has been written in Isaiah the prophet: "Look, I am sending My messenger before Your face, who will prepare Your way;

**1:3** a voice of one crying out in the wilderness: 'Make ready the way of the Lord; make His paths straight,'"

**1:4** John appeared, the one baptizing in the wilderness and proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.

**1:5** And all the Judean countryside and all the people of Jerusalem were going out to him, and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.

**1:6** And John was clothed with camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist, and he was eating locusts and wild honey.

**1:7** And he was proclaiming, saying, "After me comes the One stronger than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.

**1:8** I baptized you with water, but He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit."

**1:9** And it happened in those days that Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized in the Jordan by John.

**1:10** And immediately, coming up out of the water, He saw the heavens being torn open and the Spirit descending like a dove upon Him;

**1:11** and a voice came out of the heavens: "You are My Son, the Beloved; in You I delight."

**1:12** And immediately the Spirit drove Him out into the wilderness.

**1:13** And He was in the wilderness forty days, being tempted by Satan, and He was with the wild animals, and the angels were serving Him.

**1:14** And after John was handed over, Jesus came into Galilee, proclaiming the gospel of God

**1:15** and saying, "The appointed time has been fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near; repent and believe in the gospel."

**1:16** And as He was passing along beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and Andrew, the brother of Simon, casting a net in the sea, for they were fishermen.

**1:17** And Jesus said to them, "Come after Me, and I will make you become fishers of people."

**1:18** And immediately, leaving the nets, they followed Him.

**1:19** And going on a little farther, He saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, and them also in the boat mending the nets,

**1:20** and immediately He called them. And leaving their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men, they went away after Him.

**1:21** And they entered into Capernaum. And immediately on the Sabbath, having gone into the synagogue, He was teaching.

**1:22** And they were astonished at His teaching, for He was teaching them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

**1:23** And immediately there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,

**1:24** saying, "What is there between us and You, Jesus the Nazarene? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!"

**1:25** And Jesus rebuked it, saying, "Be silenced and come out of him!"

**1:26** And the unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying out with a loud voice, came out of him.

**1:27** And they were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, "What is this? A new teaching! With authority He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him."

**1:28** And immediately the report about Him went out everywhere into the whole surrounding region of Galilee.

**1:29** And immediately, having come out of the synagogue, they went into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

**1:30** Now Simon's mother-in-law was lying down with a fever, and immediately they told Him about her.

**1:31** And approaching her, He raised her up, taking hold of her hand; and the fever left her, and she began serving them.

**1:32** Now when evening came, when the sun set, they were bringing to Him all those who were ill and those who were demon-possessed;

**1:33** and the whole city was gathered together at the door.

**1:34** And He healed many who were ill with various diseases, and He drove out many demons; and He would not permit the demons to speak, because they knew Him.

**1:35** And very early in the morning, while it was still night, rising up, He went out and departed to a desolate place, and there He was praying.

**1:36** And Simon and those with him searched for Him,

**1:37** and they found Him and said to Him, "Everyone is looking for You."

**1:38** And He said to them, "Let us go elsewhere into the neighboring towns, so that I may proclaim there also, for this is why I came out."

**1:39** And He went proclaiming in their synagogues throughout all Galilee and driving out the demons.

**1:40** And a leper came to Him, imploring Him and kneeling down, saying to Him, "If You are willing, You are able to cleanse me."

**1:41** And moved with compassion, stretching out His hand, He touched him and said to him, "I am willing; be cleansed."

**1:42** And immediately the leprosy departed from him, and he was cleansed.

**1:43** And sternly warning him, He immediately sent him away,

**1:44** and said to him, "See that you say nothing to anyone, but go, show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, as a testimony to them."

**1:45** But having gone out, he began to proclaim widely and to spread the word, so that He was no longer able to enter openly into a city, but remained outside in desolate places; and they were coming to Him from everywhere.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The title "Son of God" is included in the Saints Greek Critical Text and most manuscripts (such as B, D, and W), though it is omitted in a few early witnesses, including the original hand of Codex Sinaiticus.
- **1:2** — Mark introduces a composite quotation blending Exodus 23:20, Malachi 3:1, and Isaiah 40:3 under the name of Isaiah, the major prophet whose passage forms the climax in verse 3.
- **1:27** — The phrase "with authority" may either qualify "a new teaching" or introduce "He commands even the unclean spirits"; this translation follows the punctuation of the Greek text, connecting it with the verb "commands."
- **1:41** — While nearly all Greek manuscripts read "moved with compassion" (splanchnistheis), Codex Bezae (D) and several Old Latin witnesses read "moved with anger" (orgistheis).
- **1:43** — Both verbs here are notably forceful: "sternly warning" (embrimesamenos) conveys urgent intensity, and "sent him away" (exebalen) is the same Greek verb used of driving out demons in verses 34 and 39.
