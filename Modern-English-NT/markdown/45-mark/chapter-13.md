## Text

**13:1** And as He was going out of the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, "Teacher, look! What remarkable stones and what remarkable buildings!"

**13:2** And Jesus said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? Not a stone will be left here upon a stone that will not be torn down."

**13:3** And as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew began questioning Him privately:

**13:4** "Tell us when these things will happen, and what the sign will be when all these things are about to be brought to completion."

**13:5** And Jesus began to say to them, "Watch out that no one misleads you.

**13:6** Many will come in My name, saying, 'I am,' and they will mislead many.

**13:7** Now when you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not be alarmed; this must take place, but the end is not yet.

**13:8** For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there will be earthquakes in various places; there will be famines. These are the beginning of birth pains.

**13:9** But you, watch out for yourselves; they will hand you over to councils, and in synagogues you will be beaten, and you will stand before governors and kings on account of Me, as a testimony to them.

**13:10** And the gospel must first be proclaimed to all the nations.

**13:11** And when they lead you away and hand you over, do not be anxious beforehand about what you should speak, but whatever is given to you in that hour, speak that; for it is not you who are speaking, but the Holy Spirit.

**13:12** And brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child, and children will rise up against parents and put them to death.

**13:13** And you will be hated by all because of My name. But the one who endures to the end, that one will be saved.

**13:14** Now when you see the abomination of desolation standing where it ought not to be (let the reader understand), then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains;

**13:15** and let the one who is on the housetop not go down, nor enter to take anything out of his house;

**13:16** and let the one who is in the field not turn back to take his cloak.

**13:17** And woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days.

**13:18** But pray that it may not happen in winter.

**13:19** For those days will be an affliction such as has not occurred from the beginning of the creation that God created until now, and will by no means ever occur.

**13:20** And if the Lord had not cut short the days, no flesh would have been saved. But for the sake of the chosen ones whom He chose, He cut short the days.

**13:21** And then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' 'Look, there!' do not believe it.

**13:22** For false christs and false prophets will arise and will produce signs and wonders in order to lead astray, if possible, the chosen ones.

**13:23** But you, watch out; see, I have told you everything beforehand.

**13:24** But in those days, after that affliction, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its radiance,

**13:25** and the stars will be falling from heaven, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.

**13:26** And then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.

**13:27** And then He will send out the angels and will gather together His chosen ones from the four winds, from the end of earth to the end of heaven.

**13:28** Now learn the parable from the fig tree: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near.

**13:29** So you also, when you see these things taking place, know that it is near, at the doors.

**13:30** Truly I say to you, this generation will by no means pass away until all these things take place.

**13:31** Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will by no means pass away.

**13:32** But concerning that day or the hour, no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, except the Father.

**13:33** Watch out, stay alert, and pray, for you do not know when the time is.

**13:34** It is like a man away on a journey, who left his house and gave his slaves the authority, to each his work, and commanded the doorkeeper to stay awake.

**13:35** Therefore stay awake, for you do not know when the master of the house is coming, whether in the evening, or at midnight, or at the crowing of the rooster, or in the morning,

**13:36** so that he may not come suddenly and find you sleeping.

**13:37** And what I say to you, I say to all: stay awake!"

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — The Greek phrase "I am" (ego eimi) has no expressed predicate; it may mean "I am he" (the Christ) or echo the divine self-designation.
- **13:14** — The Greek participle for "standing" (hestekota) is masculine even though "abomination" (bdelygma) is neuter, pointing to a personal figure or image.
- **13:29** — Greek expresses no subject for "is near"; it may refer either to "it" (the fulfillment of these events) or to "He" (the Son of Man).
- **13:33** — The words "and pray" (kai proseuchesthe) appear in this Greek text (following many manuscripts), though they are absent from several early Alexandrian witnesses.
