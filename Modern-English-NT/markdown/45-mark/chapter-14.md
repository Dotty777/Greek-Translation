## Text

**14:1** Now it was two days until the Passover and the Unleavened Bread. And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might seize Him by deceit and kill Him.

**14:2** For they were saying, "Not during the feast, or else there will be an uproar of the people."

**14:3** And while He was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, as He was reclining at table, a woman came with an alabaster jar of very costly perfume of genuine nard. After breaking the alabaster jar, she poured it over His head.

**14:4** But some were indignant among themselves: "Why has this waste of the perfume taken place?

**14:5** For this perfume could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and given to the poor." And they were scolding her.

**14:6** But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why are you causing her trouble? She has performed a good work for Me.

**14:7** For you always have the poor with you, and whenever you wish you are able to do good to them, but you do not always have Me.

**14:8** What she was able to do, she did; she anointed My body in advance for burial.

**14:9** And truly I say to you, wherever the gospel is proclaimed throughout the whole world, what this woman did will also be spoken of as a memorial of her."

**14:10** And Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went off to the chief priests so that he might hand Him over to them.

**14:11** And when they heard this, they rejoiced and promised to give him money. And he was seeking how he might hand Him over at an opportune time.

**14:12** And on the first day of Unleavened Bread, when they would sacrifice the Passover, His disciples said to Him, "Where do You wish us to go and prepare so that You may eat the Passover?"

**14:13** And He sent two of His disciples and said to them, "Go into the city, and a man carrying a pitcher of water will meet you. Follow him,

**14:14** and wherever he enters, tell the owner of the house that the Teacher says, 'Where is My guest room where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?'

**14:15** And he himself will show you a large upper room, furnished and ready; and prepare for us there."

**14:16** And the disciples went out and came into the city, and they found things just as He had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

**14:17** And when evening came, He arrived with the twelve.

**14:18** And while they were reclining and eating, Jesus said, "Truly I say to you that one of you will hand Me over, one who is eating with Me."

**14:19** They began to be grieved and to say to Him one by one, "Surely not I?"

**14:20** And He said to them, "It is one of the twelve, the one dipping with Me into the bowl.

**14:21** For the Son of Man goes just as it has been written concerning Him, yet woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is handed over! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born."

**14:22** And while they were eating, He took bread, and after blessing, He broke it and gave it to them, and said, "Take; this is My body."

**14:23** And taking a cup, after giving thanks, He gave it to them, and they all drank from it.

**14:24** And He said to them, "This is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out on behalf of many.

**14:25** Truly I say to you that I will by no means drink again of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God."

**14:26** And having sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**14:27** And Jesus said to them, "You will all stumble, because it has been written, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.'

**14:28** Yet after I have been raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee."

**14:29** But Peter said to Him, "Even if all will stumble, yet not I."

**14:30** And Jesus said to him, "Truly I say to you that today, this night, before a rooster crows twice, you yourself will deny Me three times."

**14:31** But he kept speaking emphatically, "If I must die with You, I will by no means deny You." And all of them were saying the same thing as well.

**14:32** And they came to a place whose name was Gethsemane, and He said to His disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

**14:33** And He took Peter and James and John along with Him, and began to be deeply dismayed and in anguish.

**14:34** And He said to them, "My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, to the point of death; stay here and keep awake."

**14:35** And going a little farther, He fell on the ground and was praying that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from Him.

**14:36** And He was saying, "Abba, Father, all things are possible for You; take this cup away from Me; yet not what I wish, but what You wish."

**14:37** And He came and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, "Simon, are you sleeping? Were you not able to keep awake for one hour?

**14:38** Keep awake and pray, so that you may not come into temptation; the spirit indeed is eager, yet the flesh is weak."

**14:39** And going away again, He prayed, speaking the same words.

**14:40** And coming again, He found them sleeping, for their eyes were weighed down, and they did not know what to answer Him.

**14:41** And He came the third time and said to them, "Are you still sleeping and taking your rest? It is enough! The hour has come; look, the Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of sinners.

**14:42** Get up, let us go; look, the one handing Me over has drawn near."

**14:43** And immediately, while He was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, arrived, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.

**14:44** Now the one handing Him over had given them an agreed signal, saying, "The one whom I kiss, He is the one; seize Him and lead Him away securely."

**14:45** And when he arrived, he immediately stepped up to Him and said, "Rabbi," and kissed Him.

**14:46** And they laid their hands on Him and seized Him.

**14:47** But a certain one of those standing by drew his sword, struck the slave of the high priest, and cut off his ear.

**14:48** And answering, Jesus said to them, "Have you come out as against a robber with swords and clubs to arrest Me?

**14:49** Day by day I was with you in the temple teaching, and you did not seize Me; yet this is so that the Scriptures may be fulfilled."

**14:50** And leaving Him, they all fled.

**14:51** And a certain young man was following along with Him, wearing a linen cloth over his naked body, and they seized him.

**14:52** But leaving the linen cloth behind, he fled naked.

**14:53** And they led Jesus away to the high priest, and all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes gathered together.

**14:54** And Peter followed Him from a distance, right inside into the courtyard of the high priest, and he was sitting with the officers and warming himself by the firelight.

**14:55** Now the chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were seeking testimony against Jesus in order to put Him to death, and they were not finding any.

**14:56** For many were giving false testimony against Him, and the testimonies were not consistent.

**14:57** And some stood up and were giving false testimony against Him, saying,

**14:58** "We heard Him say, 'I will destroy this temple made with hands, and in three days I will build another not made with hands.'"

**14:59** And not even on this point was their testimony consistent.

**14:60** And standing up in the center, the high priest questioned Jesus, saying, "Do You answer nothing? What is it that these men are testifying against You?"

**14:61** But He kept silent and answered nothing. Again the high priest was questioning Him and said to Him, "Are You the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?"

**14:62** And Jesus said, "I am; and you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Power and coming with the clouds of heaven."

**14:63** Then the high priest, tearing his garments, said, "What further need do we have of witnesses?

**14:64** You have heard the blasphemy. How does it seem to you?" And they all condemned Him to be deserving of death.

**14:65** And some began to spit on Him and to cover His face and to strike Him with their fists and to say to Him, "Prophesy!" And the officers received Him with slaps.

**14:66** And while Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the servant girls of the high priest came,

**14:67** and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked intently at him and said, "You also were with the Nazarene, Jesus."

**14:68** But he denied it, saying, "I neither know nor understand what you are saying." And he went outside into the forecourt.

**14:69** And when the servant girl saw him, she began again to say to those standing by, "This man is one of them."

**14:70** But he was denying it again. And after a little while, those standing by were again saying to Peter, "Truly you are one of them, for you are a Galilean as well."

**14:71** But he began to invoke curses and to swear, "I do not know this man whom you are speaking of."

**14:72** And immediately a rooster crowed a second time. And Peter remembered the word, how Jesus had said to him, "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times." And breaking down, he wept.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — The Greek adjective pistikes describing nard is uncertain in meaning; it is most commonly taken as "genuine" or "pure," though some interpret it as "liquid" or as a loanword denoting a specific variety of nard.
- **14:10** — The Greek verb paradidomi literally means "hand over" or "deliver up" and is used consistently across verses 10, 11, 18, and 21, carrying the sense of betrayal in Judas's action.
- **14:24** — The critical Greek text reads "My blood of the covenant" without the word "new," which was added in later manuscripts in assimilation to Luke 22:20 and 1 Corinthians 11:25.
- **14:36** — "Abba" is the Aramaic word for "Father," which Mark retains and immediately translates into Greek as ho pater.
- **14:41** — The Greek verb apechei ("It is enough") is unusual here and is sometimes understood in a commercial sense ("It is settled" or "The account is paid"); the preceding verbs ("sleeping and taking your rest") can be read either as a question or as an ironic imperative.
- **14:62** — "The Power" (tes dynameos) is a reverential Jewish circumlocution for God, matching "the Blessed One" in verse 61.
- **14:68** — The critical Greek text ends this verse at "forecourt"; many later manuscripts add "and a rooster crowed" to align with Jesus's prediction in verse 30 and the second crowing in verse 72.
- **14:72** — The Greek participle epibalon is famously difficult and debated; interpreters have variously understood it as "breaking down," "thinking about it," "covering his head," or "rushing out."
