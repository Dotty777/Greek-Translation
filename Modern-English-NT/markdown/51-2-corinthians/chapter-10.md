## Text

**10:1** Now I myself, Paul, appeal to you by the gentleness and forbearance of Christ—I who am humble face to face among you, yet bold toward you when absent;

**10:2** and I ask that when present I may not need to be bold with the confidence with which I count on acting daringly against certain ones who count us as walking according to the flesh.

**10:3** For though we walk in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh—

**10:4** for the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful before God for tearing down strongholds—tearing down reasonings

**10:5** and every lofty thing raised up against the knowledge of God, and capturing every thought into the obedience of Christ,

**10:6** and standing ready to punish every disobedience, whenever your obedience has been fulfilled.

**10:7** You are looking at what is on the surface. If anyone is confident in himself that he belongs to Christ, let him consider this again within himself, that just as he belongs to Christ, so also do we.

**10:8** For even if I should boast somewhat more about our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for tearing you down, I will not be put to shame,

**10:9** so that I may not appear as though trying to frighten you through the letters;

**10:10** for "The letters," one says, "are weighty and forceful, yet the presence of the body is weak and the speech is despised."

**10:11** Let such a person consider this, that what we are in word through letters when absent, such we are also when present in deed.

**10:12** For we do not dare to classify or compare ourselves with some of those who commend themselves; instead, measuring themselves by themselves and comparing themselves with themselves, they lack understanding.

**10:13** We, however, will not boast beyond measure, but according to the measure of the sphere which God apportioned to us as a measure, to reach even as far as you—

**10:14** for we are not overextending ourselves as though we did not reach to you, since we arrived even as far as you in the gospel of Christ—

**10:15** not boasting beyond measure in the labors of others, but having hope that as your faith increases, we will be enlarged among you according to our sphere to overflowing,

**10:16** to proclaim the gospel in the regions beyond you, not to boast in another's sphere over things already made ready.

**10:17** But let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord;

**10:18** for it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Greek dynata to theo ("powerful before God") uses a dative that can mean "powerful in God's sight," "powerful for God," or (as an intensive idiom) "divinely powerful."
- **10:7** — The verb blepete can be construed either as an indicative ("You are looking at what is on the surface") or as an imperative ("Look at what is right before your face").
- **10:13** — Greek kanon ("sphere") refers to a measuring rod or boundary line defining the ministerial territory apportioned by God.
