## Text

**6:1** And working together, we also appeal to you not to receive the grace of God in vain;

**6:2** for He says, "At an acceptable time I listened to you, and on a day of salvation I helped you." Look, now is the acceptable time; look, now is the day of salvation—

**6:3** giving no cause for offense in anything, so that the ministry will not be found fault with,

**6:4** but in everything commending ourselves as servants of God: in great endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in dire straits,

**6:5** in beatings, in imprisonments, in disturbances, in toils, in sleepless nights, in fastings,

**6:6** in purity, in knowledge, in forbearance, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without hypocrisy,

**6:7** in the word of truth, in the power of God; through the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and the left,

**6:8** through honor and dishonor, through bad report and good report; as impostors, yet truthful;

**6:9** as unknown, yet well known; as dying, and look, we live; as disciplined, yet not put to death;

**6:10** as grieving, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet enriching many; as having nothing, and yet possessing everything.

**6:11** Our mouth stands open to you, Corinthians; our heart has opened wide.

**6:12** You are not hemmed in by us, rather you are hemmed in within your own affections;

**6:13** now in equal return—I speak as to children—open wide yourselves also.

**6:14** Do not become unequally yoked with unbelievers; for what partnership do righteousness and lawlessness have, or what fellowship does light have with darkness?

**6:15** And what harmony does Christ have with Beliar, or what portion does a believer have with an unbeliever?

**6:16** And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, just as God said, "I will dwell in them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be My people."

**6:17** "Therefore, come out from their midst and be separate," says the Lord, "and do not touch anything unclean; and I will welcome you,

**6:18** and I will be a Father to you, and you will be sons and daughters to Me," says the Lord Almighty.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The participle "working together" (synergountes) lacks an explicit object in Greek, leaving open whether Paul means working together with God (cf. 1 Cor 3:9) or alongside his fellow ministers.
- **6:12** — Greek "splagchna" literally denotes the inward parts as the seat of deep emotion and is translated "affections."
- **6:15** — Beliar is a Greek spelling of Belial (meaning "worthlessness" or "wickedness"), used in Jewish writings of the Second Temple period as a name for Satan.
