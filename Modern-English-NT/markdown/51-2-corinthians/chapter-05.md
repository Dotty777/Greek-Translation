## Text

**5:1** For we know that if our earthly house, the tent, is dismantled, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens.

**5:2** For indeed in this we groan, longing to be clothed over with our dwelling from heaven,

**5:3** if indeed, having been clothed, we will not be found naked.

**5:4** For indeed, we who are in the tent groan, being burdened, because we do not want to be unclothed, but to be clothed over, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.

**5:5** Now the One who prepared us for this very purpose is God, who gave us the down payment of the Spirit.

**5:6** Therefore, being always confident and knowing that while we are at home in the body we are away from the Lord—

**5:7** for we walk by faith, not by appearance—

**5:8** yet we are confident, and we prefer rather to be away from the body and to be at home with the Lord.

**5:9** Therefore we also make it our aim, whether at home or away, to be well-pleasing to Him.

**5:10** For all of us must be made manifest before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive back the deeds done through the body according to what he practiced, whether good or evil.

**5:11** Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people, yet to God we have been made manifest; and I hope that we have been made manifest in your consciences as well.

**5:12** We are not commending ourselves to you again, but are giving you an occasion for boasting on our behalf, so that you may have an answer for those who boast in appearance and not in heart.

**5:13** For if we were out of our minds, it was for God; if we are of sound mind, it is for you.

**5:14** For the love of Christ compels us, having concluded this, that One died on behalf of all; therefore all died;

**5:15** and He died on behalf of all, so that those who live would no longer live for themselves, but for the One who died and was raised on their behalf.

**5:16** Consequently, from now on we know no one according to the flesh; even though we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we no longer know Him that way.

**5:17** Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation: the old things have passed away; look, new things have come into being!

**5:18** Now all these things are from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation,

**5:19** namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and having committed to us the word of reconciliation.

**5:20** Therefore we serve as ambassadors on behalf of Christ, as though God were appealing through us; we implore you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God.

**5:21** He made the One who did not know sin to be sin on our behalf, so that we might become God's righteousness in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — Some manuscripts read "having been unclothed" (ekdysamenoi) rather than "having been clothed" (endysamenoi); the conjunction "if indeed" (ei ge) can also carry a confident sense ("since indeed").
- **5:7** — Greek "eidos" denotes visible appearance or outward form, here contrasted with faith.
- **5:10** — Some manuscripts read "worthless" (phaulon) instead of "evil" (kakon).
- **5:17** — The verbless Greek clause "kaine ktisis" may be understood either cosmically ("there is a new creation") or individually ("that person is a new creature"); later manuscripts add "all things" (ta panta) to the final clause.
- **5:21** — Greek "hamartia" ("sin") here may carry the sense of "sin offering" (as often in the Septuagint).
