## Text

**8:1** Now we make known to you, brothers, the grace of God that has been given among the churches of Macedonia,

**8:2** that in a severe test of affliction, the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded into the wealth of their generosity.

**8:3** For according to their ability, I testify, and beyond their ability, of their own accord,

**8:4** begging us with much urging for the grace and the sharing in the ministry to the saints—

**8:5** and not as we had hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and to us through the will of God,

**8:6** so that we urged Titus that, just as he had previously begun, so he would also complete among you this grace as well.

**8:7** But just as you abound in everything—in faith and speech and knowledge and all earnestness and in the love from us in you—see that you abound in this grace also.

**8:8** I am not speaking by way of command, but proving through the earnestness of others the genuineness of your love as well.

**8:9** For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that on your account He became poor, even though He was rich, so that by His poverty you might become rich.

**8:10** And in this matter I give my opinion, for this is beneficial for you, who previously began from last year not only to do it, but also to desire it.

**8:11** But now complete the doing of it as well, so that just as there was the eagerness to desire it, so also there may be the completion of it out of what you have.

**8:12** For if the eagerness is present, it is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have.

**8:13** For this is not so that there may be relief for others and affliction for you, but as a matter of equality:

**8:14** at the present time your abundance is for their need, so that their abundance may also be for your need, in order that there may be equality;

**8:15** just as it is written: "The one with much did not have too much, and the one with little did not have too little."

**8:16** Now thanks be to God, who puts the same earnestness on your behalf into the heart of Titus,

**8:17** because he indeed welcomed our appeal, yet being even more earnest, he has gone out to you of his own accord.

**8:18** And we have sent with him the brother whose praise in the gospel is throughout all the churches—

**8:19** and not only that, but he was also appointed by the churches as our travel companion in this grace that is being administered by us for the glory of the Lord Himself and our own eagerness—

**8:20** guarding against this, so that no one may find fault with us in this abundant gift that is being administered by us;

**8:21** for we aim at what is honorable not only before the Lord, but also before people.

**8:22** And we have sent with them our brother whom we have often tested in many things and found earnest, and now much more earnest because of his great confidence in you.

**8:23** As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker for you; as for our brothers, they are messengers of the churches, the glory of Christ.

**8:24** Therefore, display to them the proof of your love and of our boasting on your behalf, before the face of the churches.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Greek haplotēs literally means "singleness" or "sincerity" and in the context of the collection (also 9:11, 13) denotes wholehearted generosity.
- **8:4** — Verses 3-4 contain an ellipsis in Greek with no finite verb for giving; Paul breaks off the sentence and resumes in 8:5 with "they gave themselves."
- **8:7** — Some manuscripts read "your love for us," whereas the critical text followed here reads "the love from us in you."
- **8:20** — The noun hadrotēs ("abundant gift") occurs only here in the New Testament and refers to the large monetary collection administered by Paul and his coworkers.
- **8:23** — "Messengers" translates apostoloi, used here of commissioned delegates sent by the churches rather than the Twelve.
