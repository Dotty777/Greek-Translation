## Text

**3:1** Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some do, letters of commendation to you or from you?

**3:2** You yourselves are our letter, inscribed in our hearts, known and read by all people,

**3:3** being manifested that you are a letter of Christ, ministered by us, inscribed not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on tablets that are hearts of flesh.

**3:4** And such confidence we have through Christ toward God.

**3:5** Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to consider anything as coming from ourselves, but our sufficiency is from God,

**3:6** who also made us sufficient as ministers of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit; for the letter kills, while the Spirit makes alive.

**3:7** Now if the ministry of death, engraved in letters on stones, came in glory, so that the sons of Israel could not gaze steadily at the face of Moses because of the glory of his face, which was being done away with,

**3:8** how will the ministry of the Spirit not be even more in glory?

**3:9** For if the ministry of condemnation had glory, much more does the ministry of righteousness abound in glory.

**3:10** For indeed, what was glorified has not been glorified in this respect, on account of the surpassing glory;

**3:11** for if what was being done away with came through glory, much more is what remains in glory.

**3:12** Having such a hope, therefore, we use great boldness,

**3:13** and are not like Moses, who used to put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel would not gaze steadily at the end of what was being done away with.

**3:14** But their minds were hardened. For until this very day the same veil remains unlifted at the reading of the old covenant, because in Christ it is done away with;

**3:15** but to this day, whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their heart.

**3:16** Yet whenever one turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.

**3:17** And the Lord is the Spirit; and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

**3:18** And we all, with unveiled face, seeing as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, just as from the Lord, the Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The critical text reads "in our hearts" (ἡμῶν); a few manuscripts read "in your hearts" (ὑμῶν).
- **3:7** — The verb καταργέω (also in vv. 11, 13, 14) means "render inoperative," "bring to an end," or "do away with."
- **3:14** — The conjunction ὅτι may be taken causally ("because in Christ it is done away with") or as content ("it not being unveiled that in Christ it is done away with").
- **3:18** — The middle participle κατοπτριζόμενοι is debated between "seeing as in a mirror" and "reflecting as a mirror"; the closing phrase ἀπὸ κυρίου πνεύματος may be rendered "from the Lord, the Spirit," "from the Spirit of the Lord," or "from the Lord who is the Spirit."
