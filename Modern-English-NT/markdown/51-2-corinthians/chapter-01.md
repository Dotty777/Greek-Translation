## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Timothy the brother, to the church of God that is in Corinth, with all the saints who are in all of Achaia:

**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:3** Blessed is the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassions and God of all comfort,

**1:4** who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those in any affliction through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

**1:5** For just as the sufferings of Christ abound toward us, so through Christ our comfort also abounds.

**1:6** Now if we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; or if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which operates in the endurance of the same sufferings that we also suffer,

**1:7** and our hope for you is firm, knowing that just as you are sharers in the sufferings, so also you are in the comfort.

**1:8** For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, concerning our affliction that occurred in Asia, that we were weighed down excessively, beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of living.

**1:9** Indeed, we ourselves had the sentence of death within ourselves, so that we would not rely on ourselves, but on God who raises the dead;

**1:10** who rescued us from so great a death and will rescue us; on Him we have set our hope that He will rescue us yet again,

**1:11** as you also join in helping on our behalf through prayer, so that for the gracious gift granted to us from many persons, thanks may be given through many on our behalf.

**1:12** For our boasting is this, the testimony of our conscience: that in holiness and sincerity of God, not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, we conducted ourselves in the world, and even more abundantly toward you.

**1:13** For we write nothing else to you except what you read or also understand; and I hope that you will understand until the end,

**1:14** just as you also have understood us in part, that we are your reason for boasting, even as you also are ours, in the day of our Lord Jesus.

**1:15** And in this confidence I was intending to come to you first, so that you might have a second grace,

**1:16** and by way of you to pass through into Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and by you to be sent on my way into Judea.

**1:17** Therefore, when I was intending this, did I really act with fickleness? Or the things that I plan, do I plan according to the flesh, so that with me there is "Yes, yes" and "No, no"?

**1:18** But God is faithful, that our word to you is not "Yes" and "No."

**1:19** For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was proclaimed among you through us—through me and Silvanus and Timothy—was not "Yes" and "No," but in Him it has become "Yes."

**1:20** For however many promises of God there are, in Him is the "Yes"; therefore also through Him is the "Amen" to God for glory through us.

**1:21** Now the One who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,

**1:22** who also sealed us and gave the down payment of the Spirit in our hearts.

**1:23** But I call upon God as witness against my own soul, that it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth.

**1:24** Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers for your joy; for by faith you stand firm.

## Translator Notes

- **1:10** — The critical text reads the future "will rescue" twice (ῥύσεται ... ῥύσεται); other manuscripts read the present "rescues" (ῥύεται) or omit the first clause.
- **1:11** — The Greek phrases "from many persons" (lit. "faces") and "through many" can be construed either as prayer offered by many leading to the gift or as thanksgiving rising from many for the gift.
- **1:12** — The critical text reads "holiness" (ἁγιότητι); many later manuscripts read "simplicity" or "sincerity" (ἁπλότητι).
- **1:22** — "Down payment" (ἀρραβών) denotes a first installment that guarantees full payment to follow.
