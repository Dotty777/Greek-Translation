## Text

**4:1** For this reason, having this ministry as we were shown mercy, we do not lose heart,

**4:2** but we have renounced the hidden things of shame, not walking in craftiness nor falsifying the word of God, but by the manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every human conscience before God.

**4:3** Yet even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing,

**4:4** among whom the god of this age blinded the minds of the unbelievers, so that the illumination of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, would not shine forth.

**4:5** For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for the sake of Jesus.

**4:6** For God, who said, "Out of darkness light will shine," is the One who shone in our hearts for the illumination of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

**4:7** Now we have this treasure in clay vessels, so that the extraordinary greatness of the power may be God's and not from us;

**4:8** afflicted in every way, yet not hemmed in; perplexed, yet not in despair;

**4:9** persecuted, yet not abandoned; thrown down, yet not destroyed;

**4:10** always carrying about in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be manifested in our body.

**4:11** For we who live are constantly being handed over to death for the sake of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be manifested in our mortal flesh.

**4:12** So then, death is at work in us, but life in you.

**4:13** Now having the same spirit of faith, according to what has been written, "I believed; therefore I spoke," we also believe, and therefore we also speak,

**4:14** knowing that the One who raised Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and will present us with you.

**4:15** For all things are for your sake, so that grace, having increased through more people, may cause thanksgiving to overflow to the glory of God.

**4:16** Therefore we do not lose heart; but even though our outer person is decaying, still our inner person is being renewed day by day.

**4:17** For the momentary lightness of our affliction is producing for us far beyond all measure an eternal weight of glory,

**4:18** since we do not look at the things that are seen, but at the things that are unseen; for the things that are seen are temporary, whereas the things that are unseen are eternal.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek infinitive "shine forth" (augasai) can be taken intransitively with "the illumination" as its subject (as translated) or transitively ("so that they might not see the illumination").
- **4:14** — Some manuscripts read "the Lord Jesus" instead of "Jesus," and "through Jesus" instead of "with Jesus."
- **4:15** — The phrase "through more people" (dia ton pleionon) can modify either "having increased" or "may cause thanksgiving to overflow," and forms a wordplay with "increased" (pleonasasa).
