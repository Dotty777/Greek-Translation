## Text

**2:1** For I decided this for myself, not to come to you again in sorrow.

**2:2** For if I cause you sorrow, then who is the one making me glad except the one who is made sorrowful by me?

**2:3** And I wrote this very thing so that when I came I would not have sorrow from those over whom I ought to rejoice, having confidence in all of you that my joy is that of you all.

**2:4** For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not so that you might be made sorrowful, but so that you might know the love that I have more abundantly toward you.

**2:5** Now if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused sorrow to me, but in part—so as not to be too severe—to all of you.

**2:6** Sufficient for such a person is this punishment inflicted by the majority,

**2:7** so that on the contrary you should rather forgive and comfort him, otherwise such a person might be swallowed up by excessive sorrow.

**2:8** Therefore I urge you to reaffirm love toward him.

**2:9** For to this end I also wrote, so that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things.

**2:10** Now whomever you forgive anything, I also do; for indeed what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, I did it for your sake in the presence of Christ,

**2:11** so that we might not be taken advantage of by Satan; for we are not unaware of his designs.

**2:12** Now when I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ, and a door had been opened for me in the Lord,

**2:13** I had no relief in my spirit because I did not find Titus my brother; instead, taking leave of them, I went out into Macedonia.

**2:14** But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumphal procession in Christ and reveals through us the fragrance of the knowledge of Him in every place.

**2:15** For we are a sweet aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing;

**2:16** to the one a fragrance from death to death, to the other a fragrance from life to life. And who is sufficient for these things?

**2:17** For we are not like the many, peddling the word of God; rather, as from sincerity, and as from God, in the sight of God we speak in Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — Paul inserts the parenthetical phrase "in part—so as not to be too severe" to soften his statement that the offender caused sorrow to the entire congregation rather than to Paul alone.
- **2:14** — The verb θριαμβεύω ("lead in triumphal procession") evokes a Roman victory parade, though scholars debate whether Paul pictures apostles as conquered captives displayed by God or as participants in Christ's victory.
- **2:17** — "Peddling" (καπηλεύω) refers to huckstering or adulterating wares (such as wine) for personal profit.
