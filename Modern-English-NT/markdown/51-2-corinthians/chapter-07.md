## Text

**7:1** Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of flesh and spirit, bringing holiness to completion in the fear of God.

**7:2** Make room for us; we wronged no one, we corrupted no one, we took advantage of no one.

**7:3** I do not say this for condemnation, for I have said before that you are in our hearts to die together and to live together.

**7:4** Great is my boldness toward you; great is my boasting on your behalf. I am filled with comfort; I overflow with joy in all our affliction.

**7:5** For even when we came into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we were afflicted in every way—conflicts outside, fears inside—

**7:6** yet God, who comforts the downcast, comforted us by the coming of Titus;

**7:7** and not only by his coming, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted over you, as he reported to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced even more.

**7:8** For even if I grieved you with the letter, I do not regret it; even if I did regret it (I see that that letter grieved you, even if for a while),

**7:9** now I rejoice, not because you were grieved, but because you were grieved into repentance; for you were grieved according to God, so that in nothing you might suffer loss from us.

**7:10** For grief according to God produces repentance leading to salvation without regret, whereas the grief of the world produces death.

**7:11** For look at how much earnestness this very thing, being grieved according to God, produced in you—indeed what defense, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what punishment! In everything you proved yourselves to be innocent in the matter.

**7:12** So even though I wrote to you, it was not on account of the one who did wrong, nor on account of the one who was wronged, but in order that your earnestness on our behalf might be revealed to you before God.

**7:13** Because of this we have been comforted. And in addition to our comfort, we rejoiced even more abundantly at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by all of you.

**7:14** For if I have boasted to him at all on your behalf, I was not put to shame; but just as we spoke all things to you in truth, so also our boasting before Titus proved to be truth.

**7:15** And his affection is even more abundant toward you as he remembers the obedience of all of you, how you received him with fear and trembling.

**7:16** I rejoice that in everything I have confidence in you.

## Translator Notes

- **7:9** — The phrase "according to God" (also in 7:10-11) literally denotes grief aligned with God's will and purpose, contrasted with "the grief of the world" in 7:10.
- **7:10** — The Greek adjective ametameleton ("without regret") can modify either "repentance" or "salvation"; the translated word order preserves that link.
- **7:12** — Some manuscripts reverse the pronouns to read "our earnestness on your behalf," though the critical text followed here reads "your earnestness on our behalf."
