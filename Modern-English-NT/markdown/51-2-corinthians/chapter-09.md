## Text

**9:1** For concerning the ministry to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you,

**9:2** for I know your eagerness, about which I boast on your behalf to the Macedonians, that Achaia has been prepared since last year; and your zeal has stirred up the majority of them.

**9:3** Yet I have sent the brothers so that our boast on your behalf might not prove empty in this respect, in order that, just as I was saying, you may be prepared,

**9:4** otherwise, if any Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we—not to say you—would be put to shame in this confidence.

**9:5** Therefore I considered it necessary to urge the brothers to go ahead to you and arrange beforehand your previously promised blessing, so that it may be ready as a blessing and not as greed.

**9:6** Now consider this: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows with blessings will also reap with blessings.

**9:7** Let each one give just as he has resolved in his heart, not out of grief or out of necessity, for God loves a joyful giver.

**9:8** And God is able to make every grace abound toward you, so that in everything, at all times, having all sufficiency, you may abound for every good work

**9:9** (just as it is written: "He scattered abroad, he gave to the poor; his righteousness remains forever";

**9:10** and the One who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed and increase the yield of your righteousness),

**9:11** being enriched in everything for all generosity, which produces through us thanksgiving to God—

**9:12** for the ministry of this service is not only fully supplying the needs of the saints, but also abounding through many thanksgivings to God—

**9:13** through the proof of this ministry, they glorify God for the submission of your confession to the gospel of Christ and for the generosity of your sharing toward them and toward all,

**9:14** and in their prayer on your behalf, longing for you because of the surpassing grace of God upon you.

**9:15** Thanks be to God for His gift beyond description.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The noun hypostasis ("confidence") denotes Paul's confident assurance or undertaking in boasting about the Corinthians; some later manuscripts add "of boasting."
- **9:5** — Paul plays on eulogia ("blessing," referring to a generous gift, continued in 9:6) contrasted with pleonexia ("greed," i.e., a sum extracted grudgingly).
- **9:9** — Quoting Psalm 112:9 (LXX 111:9), where the subject ("he") is the righteous, generous person who fears the Lord.
- **9:15** — The adjective anekdiēgētos ("beyond description") appears only here in the New Testament, meaning impossible to recount in full.
