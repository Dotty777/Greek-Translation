## Text

**6:1** Let all who are slaves under a yoke regard their own masters as worthy of all honor, so that the name of God and the teaching may not be maligned.

**6:2** And those who have believing masters must not look down on them because they are brothers; rather, let them serve all the more, because those who partake of the benefit are believers and beloved. Teach and exhort these things.

**6:3** If anyone teaches a different doctrine and does not consent to sound words—those of our Lord Jesus Christ—and to the teaching that accords with godliness,

**6:4** he is puffed up with conceit, understanding nothing, yet having an unhealthy craving for controversies and battles over words, out of which arise envy, strife, slanders, wicked suspicions,

**6:5** and constant friction among people corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, who suppose that godliness is a source of gain.

**6:6** Yet godliness with contentment is a great source of gain.

**6:7** For we have brought nothing into the world, so that we cannot carry anything out either;

**6:8** and having food and covering, with these we will be content.

**6:9** But those who desire to become rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many senseless and harmful desires that plunge people into ruin and destruction.

**6:10** For the love of money is a root of all evils; by craving it, some have strayed away from the faith and pierced themselves through with many pangs.

**6:11** But as for you, O man of God, flee from these things; pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, and gentleness.

**6:12** Contend in the good contest of the faith; take hold of eternal life, to which you were called, and you made the good confession before many witnesses.

**6:13** I command you before God, who gives life to all things, and before Christ Jesus, who bore witness to the good confession before Pontius Pilate,

**6:14** that you keep the commandment spotless and above reproach until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**6:15** which He will display at the proper times—the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of those who reign and Lord of those who rule,

**6:16** who alone possesses immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no human being has seen nor can see. To Him be honor and eternal dominion. Amen.

**6:17** Command those who are rich in the present age not to be arrogant nor to fix their hope on the uncertainty of wealth, but on God, who richly provides us with all things for enjoyment;

**6:18** to do good, to be rich in good works, to be generous, ready to share,

**6:19** storing up for themselves a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of what is truly life.

**6:20** O Timothy, guard the deposit, turning away from profane empty chatter and the opposing arguments of what is falsely called knowledge,

**6:21** which some have professed and so missed the mark regarding the faith. Grace be with you.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — "Those who partake of the benefit" most likely refers to the believing masters who benefit from the faithful service of their Christian slaves.
- **6:7** — The critical text has the conjunction ὅτι ("so that" or "because") directly linking the clauses; some later manuscripts add "it is clear" (δῆλον) to smooth the syntax.
- **6:10** — Greek has no definite article before "root" (ῥίζα), so the phrase may be read as "a root" or "the root" of all evils.
- **6:20** — "The deposit" (τὴν παραθήκην) refers to the apostolic gospel and sound teaching entrusted to Timothy's care.
- **6:21** — The pronoun "you" (ὑμῶν) is plural in the critical text, extending the closing blessing beyond Timothy to the entire congregation.
