## Text

**5:1** Do not sharply rebuke an older man, but appeal to him as a father, to younger men as brothers,

**5:2** to older women as mothers, to younger women as sisters, in all purity.

**5:3** Honor widows who are truly widows.

**5:4** But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to practice godliness toward their own household and to make repayment to their parents, for this is acceptable before God.

**5:5** Now she who is truly a widow and has been left alone has fixed her hope on God and continues in petitions and prayers night and day,

**5:6** whereas she who lives in self-indulgence is dead even while she lives.

**5:7** Command these things as well, so that they may be above reproach.

**5:8** Yet if anyone does not care for his own, and especially for members of his household, he has renounced the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

**5:9** Let a widow be enrolled if she is not less than sixty years old, the wife of one husband,

**5:10** well attested for good works: if she has raised children, if she has shown hospitality, if she has washed the saints' feet, if she has assisted the afflicted, if she has devoted herself to every good work.

**5:11** But refuse younger widows; for whenever their passions draw them away from Christ, they want to marry,

**5:12** thus incurring judgment because they have set aside their first faith.

**5:13** At the same time they also learn to be idle, going around from house to house; and not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, talking about things they ought not to.

**5:14** Therefore I desire younger women to marry, to bear children, to manage their households, and to give the adversary no opportunity for slander;

**5:15** for some have already turned aside after Satan.

**5:16** If any believing woman has widows, let her assist them, and let the church not be burdened, so that it may assist those who are truly widows.

**5:17** Let the elders who lead well be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in word and teaching.

**5:18** For the Scripture says, "You shall not muzzle an ox while it is threshing," and, "The worker is worthy of his wages."

**5:19** Do not accept an accusation against an elder except on the basis of two or three witnesses.

**5:20** Rebuke those who persist in sin before all, so that the rest may also have fear.

**5:21** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels to keep these things without prejudgment, doing nothing out of partiality.

**5:22** Do not lay hands hastily on anyone, nor share in the sins of others; keep yourself pure.

**5:23** No longer drink only water, but use a little wine on account of your stomach and your frequent ailments.

**5:24** The sins of some people are conspicuous, going ahead to judgment, while for others they follow behind as well;

**5:25** likewise, good works are also conspicuous, and those that are otherwise cannot be hidden.

## Translator Notes

- **5:4** — "Parents" translates Greek προγόνοις ("forebears"), which in this context includes both living parents and grandparents.
- **5:12** — "First faith" (τὴν πρώτην πίστιν) here likely refers to an initial pledge or commitment of single-minded devotion to Christ.
- **5:16** — The critical text reads "any believing woman" (πιστή), while some later manuscripts expand the phrase to "any believing man or woman."
- **5:18** — The first quotation is from Deuteronomy 25:4, and the second matches Jesus' saying in Luke 10:7, both introduced under "the Scripture says."
