## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and of the Lord Jesus Christ, our hope,

**1:2** to Timothy, a genuine child in faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** Just as I urged you to remain in Ephesus while I was going to Macedonia, so that you might charge certain people not to teach a different doctrine,

**1:4** nor to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which produce speculations rather than the stewardship of God that is in faith—

**1:5** Now the goal of this charge is love from a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith,

**1:6** from which some, missing the mark, turned aside into empty talk,

**1:7** wanting to be teachers of the law, yet understanding neither what they are saying nor the matters about which they make confident assertions.

**1:8** Now we know that the law is good, if one uses it lawfully,

**1:9** knowing this, that law is not laid down for a righteous person, but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for those who kill their fathers and those who kill their mothers, for murderers,

**1:10** for the sexually immoral, for men who lie with men, for slave traders, for liars, for perjurers, and for anything else that stands contrary to sound teaching,

**1:11** in accordance with the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.

**1:12** I give thanks to the One who strengthened me, Christ Jesus our Lord, because He considered me faithful, appointing me to service,

**1:13** even though previously I was a blasphemer and a persecutor and a violent aggressor; yet I was shown mercy because I acted ignorantly in unbelief,

**1:14** and the grace of our Lord overflowed exceedingly, along with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**1:15** Trustworthy is this saying and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am the first.

**1:16** Yet for this reason I was shown mercy, so that in me as the first, Jesus Christ might display complete patience as a pattern for those who were going to believe in Him for eternal life.

**1:17** Now to the King of the ages—incorruptible, invisible, the only God—be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.

**1:18** This charge I entrust to you, child Timothy, in accordance with the prophecies previously spoken about you, so that through them you may wage the good warfare,

**1:19** holding onto faith and a good conscience, which some have pushed away and have suffered shipwreck regarding the faith.

**1:20** Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I handed over to Satan so that they might be disciplined not to blaspheme.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Textual note: the Saints Greek Critical Text reads "the Lord Jesus Christ" (Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ) rather than "Christ Jesus" alone.
- **1:4** — Verses 3–4 form an incomplete sentence (anacoluthon) in Greek, breaking off before a main clause. The noun οἰκονομίαν ("stewardship") can also denote God's household administration or saving plan.
- **1:10** — The rare compound ἀρσενοκοίταις (literally "men who lie with males") echoes the Greek wording of Leviticus 18:22 and 20:13 in the Septuagint.
- **1:16** — "First" (πρώτῳ) echoes v. 15 and can signify either foremost in degree ("worst") or first in a prototypical line of recipients of Christ's mercy.
