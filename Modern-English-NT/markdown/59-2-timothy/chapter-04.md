## Text

**4:1** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is about to judge the living and the dead, and by His appearing and His kingdom:

**4:2** proclaim the word; stand ready in season and out of season; convict, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.

**4:3** For a time will come when they will not tolerate sound teaching, but in accordance with their own desires they will pile up teachers for themselves, having ears that itch,

**4:4** and they will turn their ears away from the truth, and will turn aside to myths.

**4:5** But you, be clear-minded in everything, bear hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fully carry out your ministry.

**4:6** For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure has arrived.

**4:7** I have contended in the good contest, I have completed the race, I have kept the faith.

**4:8** From now on, the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give to me on that day—and not only to me, but also to all those who have loved His appearing.

**4:9** Be diligent to come to me quickly.

**4:10** For Demas deserted me, having loved the present age, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

**4:11** Luke alone is with me. Take Mark along and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for ministry.

**4:12** But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

**4:13** When you come, bring the cloak that I left in Troas with Carpus, as well as the scrolls, especially the parchments.

**4:14** Alexander the metalworker did much evil to me—the Lord will repay him according to his works—

**4:15** against whom you also should be on guard, for he strongly opposed our words.

**4:16** At my first defense no one appeared beside me, but all deserted me—may it not be counted against them—

**4:17** but the Lord stood beside me and empowered me, so that through me the proclamation might be fully carried out and all the Gentiles might hear; and I was rescued from the mouth of a lion.

**4:18** The Lord will rescue me from every evil work and will save me into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**4:19** Greet Prisca and Aquila and Onesiphorus's household.

**4:20** Erastus stayed in Corinth, but I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.

**4:21** Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers.

**4:22** May the Lord be with your spirit. May grace be with you.

## Translator Notes

- **4:7** — The Greek terms ἀγών ("contest" or "fight") and δρόμος ("race") draw on athletic imagery that continues into the victor's crown (στέφανος, wreath) in verse 8.
- **4:10** — The Greek critical text reads Γαλατίαν ("Galatia"), though several early manuscripts read Γαλλίαν ("Gaul").
- **4:14** — The Greek critical text reads the future indicative ἀποδώσει ("will repay"), expressing a statement of certainty rather than the optative wish ("may he repay") found in later manuscripts.
- **4:22** — The Greek pronoun shifts from singular σου ("your spirit") to plural ὑμῶν ("with you"), extending the final blessing to the wider congregation; later manuscripts also add "Jesus Christ" after "the Lord" and "Amen" at the end.
