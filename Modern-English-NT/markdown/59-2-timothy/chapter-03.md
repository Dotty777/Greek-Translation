## Text

**3:1** But know this, that in the last days harsh times will set in.

**3:2** For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,

**3:3** heartless, irreconcilable, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, not loving what is good,

**3:4** treacherous, reckless, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

**3:5** holding an outward form of godliness, yet having denied its power; turn away from these people also.

**3:6** For among them are those who creep into households and take captive weak women weighed down with sins, led along by various desires,

**3:7** always learning and never able to come to a knowledge of the truth.

**3:8** Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the truth—people corrupted in mind, disqualified regarding the faith.

**3:9** Yet they will not advance any further, for their folly will be completely plain to all, just as theirs also became.

**3:10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance,

**3:11** my persecutions, and my sufferings, such as happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium, and at Lystra—what persecutions I endured! And out of them all the Lord rescued me.

**3:12** And indeed, all who desire to live in a godly manner in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.

**3:13** But evil people and impostors will advance to what is worse, deceiving and being deceived.

**3:14** You, however, remain in the things you have learned and become convinced of, knowing from whom you learned them,

**3:15** and that from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

**3:16** All Scripture is God-breathed and beneficial for teaching, for rebuke, for correction, and for discipline in righteousness,

**3:17** so that the man of God may be proficient, thoroughly equipped for every good work.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — Jannes and Jambres are the traditional Jewish names for the Egyptian magicians who opposed Moses in Exodus 7:11, 22.
- **3:14** — The pronoun "whom" (tinon) is plural in Greek, encompassing Timothy's mother and grandmother (1:5) as well as Paul.
- **3:16** — The clause lacks an explicit verb "is"; while most interpreters take both adjectives as predicates ("All Scripture is God-breathed and beneficial"), it can also be construed attributively ("Every God-breathed scripture is also beneficial").
