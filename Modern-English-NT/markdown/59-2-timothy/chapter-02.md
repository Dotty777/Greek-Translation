## Text

**2:1** You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.

**2:2** And the things that you heard from me in the presence of many witnesses, entrust these to faithful people who will be competent to teach others also.

**2:3** Share in suffering hardship as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

**2:4** No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of everyday life, so that he may please the one who enlisted him.

**2:5** And also, if anyone competes as an athlete, he is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.

**2:6** The hardworking farmer must be the first to share in the crops.

**2:7** Consider what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.

**2:8** Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, of the offspring of David, according to my gospel,

**2:9** in which I suffer hardship to the point of chains as a criminal; yet the word of God is not chained.

**2:10** For this reason I endure all things for the sake of the elect, so that they also may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.

**2:11** The saying is trustworthy: For if we died with Him, we will also live with Him;

**2:12** if we endure, we will also reign with Him; if we deny Him, He will also deny us;

**2:13** if we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself.

**2:14** Remind them of these things, solemnly charging them before God not to dispute about words, which is useful for nothing, to the ruin of the hearers.

**2:15** Make every effort to present yourself to God as approved, an unashamed worker, properly handling the word of truth.

**2:16** But avoid irreverent empty chatter, for they will advance further into ungodliness,

**2:17** and their talk will spread like gangrene; among whom are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

**2:18** who have missed the mark regarding the truth, saying that the resurrection has already taken place, and they are overturning the faith of some.

**2:19** Nevertheless, the firm foundation of God stands, bearing this seal: "The Lord knows those who are His," and, "Let everyone who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness."

**2:20** Now in a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and earthenware, and some are for honor and others for dishonor.

**2:21** Therefore, if anyone cleanses himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.

**2:22** Flee youthful desires, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.

**2:23** But refuse foolish and ignorant controversies, knowing that they breed quarrels.

**2:24** And a servant of the Lord must not quarrel, but be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient when wronged,

**2:25** with gentleness instructing those who stand in opposition, so that perhaps God may grant them repentance leading to a knowledge of the truth,

**2:26** and they may come to their senses out of the snare of the devil, having been captured alive by him to do his will.

## Translator Notes

- **2:14** — The Greek text here reads "before God" (tou Theou); some manuscripts read "before the Lord" (tou kyriou).
- **2:15** — "Properly handling" translates orthotomounta, literally "cutting straight," used metaphorically for accurately expounding and applying the word of truth.
- **2:19** — The first quotation echoes Numbers 16:5 in the Septuagint; the second draws on Old Testament language such as Numbers 16:26 and Isaiah 26:13.
- **2:26** — The pronouns "by him" (autou) and "his will" (ekeinou) are grammatically ambiguous: both may refer to the devil, or "him" may refer to the devil (or to the Lord's servant) while "his" refers to God ("to do His will").
