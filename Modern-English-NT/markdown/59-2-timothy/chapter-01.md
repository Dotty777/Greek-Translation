## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, according to the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus,

**1:2** to Timothy, beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** I give thanks to God, whom I serve from my ancestors with a pure conscience, as I continually keep remembrance of you in my petitions night and day,

**1:4** longing to see you, remembering your tears, so that I may be filled with joy,

**1:5** recalling to mind the sincere faith within you, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that it is in you also.

**1:6** For this reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands;

**1:7** for God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love, and self-discipline.

**1:8** Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me His prisoner, but share in suffering hardship for the gospel according to the power of God,

**1:9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal times,

**1:10** yet has now been made manifest through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who rendered death powerless and brought life and incorruptibility to light through the gospel,

**1:11** for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher.

**1:12** For this reason I also suffer these things, yet I am not ashamed; for I know whom I have trusted, and I am persuaded that He is able to guard my deposit until that day.

**1:13** Hold the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus;

**1:14** guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

**1:15** You know this, that all those in Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**1:16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain;

**1:17** instead, when he was in Rome, he searched diligently for me and found me—

**1:18** may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day—and you know very well all the ways he served in Ephesus.

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — Greek pneuma can refer either to a human disposition ("a spirit") or to the Holy Spirit; sophronismos denotes self-discipline, moderation, or sound judgment.
- **1:12** — "My deposit" (ten paratheken mou) may mean either what Paul has entrusted to God (such as his soul or life) or what God has entrusted to Paul (the gospel ministry; compare 1:14).
- **1:18** — Paul's wish that Onesiphorus find mercy "on that day" (the day of judgment) and his greeting only to Onesiphorus's household (1:16; 4:19) have led many interpreters to infer that Onesiphorus had died, though others view him simply as absent.
