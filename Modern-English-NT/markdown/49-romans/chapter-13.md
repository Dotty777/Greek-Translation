## Text

**13:1** Let every person submit to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except by God, and the ones that exist have been appointed by God.

**13:2** Therefore, whoever resists authority has opposed the ordinance of God, and those who stand in opposition will receive judgment upon themselves.

**13:3** For rulers are not a source of fear to a good deed, but to an evil one. Do you want not to fear authority? Do what is good, and you will have praise from it;

**13:4** for it is God's servant to you for good. But if you do what is evil, be afraid; for it does not carry the sword for nothing. For it is God's servant, an avenger for wrath against the one who practices evil.

**13:5** Therefore it is necessary to submit, not only because of wrath, but also because of conscience.

**13:6** For because of this you also pay taxes, since they are ministers of God, continually devoting themselves to this very thing.

**13:7** Pay to all what is owed: tax to whom tax is due, toll to whom toll is due, fear to whom fear is due, honor to whom honor is due.

**13:8** Owe nothing to anyone except to love one another; for the one who loves another has fulfilled the law.

**13:9** For the commands, "You shall not commit adultery, you shall not murder, you shall not steal, you shall not covet," and any other commandment there may be, are summed up in this saying: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**13:10** Love works no evil against a neighbor; therefore, love is the fulfillment of the law.

**13:11** And do this, knowing the time, that it is already the hour for you to awaken from sleep; for salvation is nearer to us now than when we first believed.

**13:12** The night has far advanced, and the day has drawn near. Let us therefore lay aside the deeds of darkness, and let us put on the armor of light.

**13:13** Let us walk properly as in the daytime, not in revelry and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy.

**13:14** Rather, put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and do not make provision for the flesh concerning its desires.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — Verses 1-2 feature a Greek wordplay on the root tasso ("order/arrange"): "submit" (hypotasso), "appointed" (tasso), "resists" (antitasso), and "ordinance" (diatage).
- **13:9** — Several later manuscripts add "You shall not bear false witness" to the list of commandments, but it is absent from the earliest witnesses.
- **13:11** — "The time" (kairos) denotes the decisive eschatological era rather than ordinary clock time, and the aorist "believed" points to when the readers first came to faith.
