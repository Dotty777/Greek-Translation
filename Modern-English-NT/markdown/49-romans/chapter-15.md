## Text

**15:1** Now we who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of those without strength, and not to please ourselves.

**15:2** Let each one of us please his neighbor for what is good, toward building up;

**15:3** for Christ also did not please Himself, but just as it is written, "The insults of those who insult You fell upon Me."

**15:4** For whatever was written beforehand was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and through the encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.

**15:5** Now may the God of endurance and encouragement grant you to have the same mindset with one another according to Christ Jesus,

**15:6** so that with one accord, with one voice, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15:7** Therefore welcome one another, just as Christ also welcomed you, to the glory of God.

**15:8** For I say that Christ has become a servant of the circumcision on behalf of the truth of God, in order to confirm the promises to the fathers,

**15:9** and for the Gentiles to glorify God for His mercy; just as it is written, "For this reason I will give praise to You among the Gentiles, and to Your name I will sing praise."

**15:10** And again it says, "Rejoice, Gentiles, with His people."

**15:11** And again, "Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples extol Him."

**15:12** And again Isaiah says, "There will be the root of Jesse, and the One who rises to rule over the Gentiles; in Him the Gentiles will hope."

**15:13** Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.

**15:14** Now I myself also am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able also to admonish one another.

**15:15** Yet I have written to you rather boldly on some points, as reminding you again, because of the grace given to me by God

**15:16** to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, performing priestly service with the gospel of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.

**15:17** Therefore I have my boast in Christ Jesus in the things pertaining to God;

**15:18** for I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,

**15:19** in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit of God, so that from Jerusalem and all around as far as Illyricum I have fully proclaimed the gospel of Christ,

**15:20** and thus making it my ambition to proclaim the gospel not where Christ had been named, so that I might not build upon another's foundation,

**15:21** but just as it is written, "Those who were not told about Him will see, and those who have not heard will understand."

**15:22** For this reason also I have many times been hindered from coming to you.

**15:23** But now, having no longer any place in these regions, and having had a longing for many years to come to you,

**15:24** whenever I travel to Spain—for I hope while passing through to see you and to be sent on my way there by you, once I have first been satisfied in part with your company—

**15:25** but right now I am going to Jerusalem, serving the saints.

**15:26** For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the saints who are in Jerusalem.

**15:27** Indeed they were pleased to do so, and they are debtors to them; for if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are also obligated to serve them in material things.

**15:28** Therefore, when I have completed this and have sealed this fruit to them, I will set out by way of you for Spain.

**15:29** And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.

**15:30** Now I urge you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in prayers to God on my behalf,

**15:31** that I may be rescued from those who are disobedient in Judea, and that my service for Jerusalem may prove acceptable to the saints,

**15:32** so that I may come to you in joy through the will of God and be refreshed together with you.

**15:33** Now may the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **15:9** — Greek ethne means both "nations" and "Gentiles"; Paul links Old Testament passages about the nations (vv. 9-12) directly to his call to welcome Gentile believers.
- **15:19** — "Fully proclaimed" translates Greek peplerokenai (literally "fulfilled" or "completed"), referring to the completion of Paul's pioneer proclamation across the region.
- **15:24** — The Greek sentence breaks off here without completing its main clause (an anacoluthon) as Paul pivots in v. 25 to explain his immediate journey to Jerusalem.
- **15:26** — "Contribution" translates Greek koinonia (literally "fellowship" or "sharing"), portraying the relief collection for Jerusalem as an act of tangible partnership, echoed by the related verb "shared" (ekoinonesan) in v. 27.
