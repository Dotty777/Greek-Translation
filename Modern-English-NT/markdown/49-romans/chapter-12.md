## Text

**12:1** Therefore I urge you, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy, well-pleasing to God, which is your rational service of worship.

**12:2** And do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of the mind, so that you may test and approve what the will of God is—what is good and well-pleasing and complete.

**12:3** For through the grace given to me I say to every person who is among you not to think more highly than one ought to think, but to think with a view to sound judgment, just as God has apportioned to each a measure of faith.

**12:4** For just as in one body we have many members, and all the members do not have the same function,

**12:5** so we, the many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.

**12:6** Now having gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: whether prophecy, according to the proportion of faith;

**12:7** or service, in serving; or the one who teaches, in teaching;

**12:8** or the one who encourages, in encouragement; the one who shares, with simplicity; the one who leads, with diligence; the one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.

**12:9** Let love be without hypocrisy. Detest what is evil; cling to what is good.

**12:10** In brotherly love be tenderly devoted to one another; in honor outdo one another;

**12:11** in diligence not lagging behind, in spirit boiling with zeal, serving the Lord;

**12:12** rejoicing in hope, enduring in affliction, persevering in prayer;

**12:13** sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.

**12:14** Bless those who persecute; bless and do not curse.

**12:15** Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.

**12:16** Be of the same mind toward one another; do not set your mind on high things, but associate with the lowly. Do not become wise in your own estimation.

**12:17** Repay no one evil for evil; take thought for what is honorable in the sight of all people.

**12:18** If possible, so far as it depends on you, live at peace with all people.

**12:19** Do not avenge yourselves, beloved, but give place to wrath; for it is written, "Vengeance belongs to Me; I will repay," says the Lord.

**12:20** Instead, "if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him a drink; for by doing this you will heap coals of fire upon his head."

**12:21** Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil with good.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — λογικὴν λατρείαν literally means "rational/reasonable cultic service" (also understood as "spiritual worship"); "rational service of worship" preserves both the philosophical sense of λογικός (engaging the mind, picked up in 12:2) and the sacrificial sense of λατρεία.
- **12:2** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads τοῦ νοός ("the mind") without the possessive pronoun "your." δοκιμάζειν means to test and discern with approval; τέλειον means "complete" or "perfect."
- **12:3** — Paul uses a fourfold play on the root φρον- (ὑπερφρονεῖν, φρονεῖν, φρονεῖν, σωφρονεῖν), reflected by repeating "think" and closing with "sound judgment."
- **12:8** — ἁπλότης means "singleness of mind, simplicity," which in contexts of giving carries the nuance of ungrudging generosity.
- **12:10** — τῇ τιμῇ ἀλλήλους προηγούμενοι literally means "going ahead of one another in honor," rendered "in honor outdo one another."
- **12:11** — Greek τῷ πνεύματι can refer either to the human spirit ("in spirit") or to the Holy Spirit ("by/in the Spirit").
- **12:14** — The Saints Greek Critical Text has τοὺς διώκοντας without "you" (ὑμᾶς), so it is rendered "those who persecute."
- **12:16** — τοῖς ταπεινοῖς can be masculine ("the lowly," i.e. humble people, rendered here) or neuter ("humble tasks/things"), contrasting with the neuter τὰ ὑψηλά ("high things").
- **12:19** — τῇ ὀργῇ ("to wrath") lacks a possessive pronoun in Greek; the context and the following Scripture quotation show it refers to God's wrath.
