## Text

**10:1** Brothers, the desire of my heart and prayer to God on their behalf is for salvation.

**10:2** For I bear witness concerning them that they have zeal for God, yet not according to knowledge;

**10:3** for, not knowing the righteousness of God and seeking to establish their own, they did not submit to the righteousness of God.

**10:4** For Christ is the end of the law, resulting in righteousness for everyone who believes.

**10:5** For Moses writes concerning the righteousness from the law, "The person who does these things will live by it."

**10:6** But the righteousness from faith speaks this way: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will ascend into heaven?'" (that is, to bring Christ down),

**10:7** or, "Who will descend into the abyss?" (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).

**10:8** Rather, what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart"--that is, the word of faith that we proclaim:

**10:9** that if with your mouth you confess Jesus as Lord, and in your heart you believe that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved;

**10:10** for with the heart one believes for righteousness, and with the mouth one confesses for salvation.

**10:11** For the Scripture says, "Anyone who believes in Him will not be put to shame."

**10:12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, since the same One is Lord of all, rich toward all who call upon Him;

**10:13** for "Whoever calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved."

**10:14** How then are they to call upon Him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in Him whom they have not heard? And how are they to hear without someone proclaiming?

**10:15** And how are they to proclaim unless they are sent? Just as it is written: "How beautiful are the feet of those who announce good news of good things!"

**10:16** Yet not all obeyed the gospel; for Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed our report?"

**10:17** Therefore faith is from hearing, and hearing is through the word of Christ.

**10:18** But I say, did they not hear? Indeed they did: "Their voice went out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the inhabited world."

**10:19** But I say, did Israel not know? First Moses says, "I will provoke you to jealousy by what is not a nation; by a nation without understanding I will stir you to anger."

**10:20** And Isaiah is bold and says, "I was found by those who were not seeking Me; I became manifest to those who were not inquiring of Me."

**10:21** But concerning Israel He says, "All day long I stretched out My hands to a disobedient and contrary people."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Greek telos can mean either "end" (termination) or "goal" (culmination/purpose), and interpreters debate whether Paul intends one or both senses here.
- **10:5** — The critical Greek text reads en aute ("by it," referring to the righteousness from the law), whereas some manuscripts read en autois ("by them," referring to the things done).
- **10:14** — Literally "whom they have not heard" (taking the genitive relative pronoun as the direct object of hearing), indicating that Christ Himself is heard in the proclamation of the gospel.
- **10:17** — Greek akoe links 10:16 and 10:17 with a dual sense of "report/message" (10:16) and "hearing" (10:17); some later manuscripts read "the word of God" rather than "the word of Christ."
