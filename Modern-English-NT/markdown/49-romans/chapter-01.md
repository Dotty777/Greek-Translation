## Text

**1:1** Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus, a called apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,

**1:2** which He promised beforehand through His prophets in holy Scriptures,

**1:3** concerning His Son, who came from the seed of David according to the flesh,

**1:4** who was designated Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,

**1:5** through whom we received grace and apostleship for the obedience of faith among all the nations for the sake of His name,

**1:6** among whom you also are those called of Jesus Christ;

**1:7** to all who are in Rome, beloved of God, called saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:8** First, I give thanks to my God through Jesus Christ concerning all of you, because your faith is being proclaimed in the whole world.

**1:9** For God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of His Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you

**1:10** always in my prayers, requesting that somehow now at last I may succeed in the will of God to come to you.

**1:11** For I long to see you, so that I may impart some spiritual gift to you in order that you may be established,

**1:12** that is, to be mutually encouraged among you through each other's faith, both yours and mine.

**1:13** Now I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that many times I planned to come to you, and have been prevented until now, so that I might have some fruit among you also, just as among the rest of the Gentiles.

**1:14** Both to Greeks and to barbarians, both to the wise and to the foolish, I am a debtor;

**1:15** so, as for my part, I am eager to proclaim the gospel to you also who are in Rome.

**1:16** For I am not ashamed of the gospel, since it is God's power for salvation to everyone who believes, both to the Jew first and to the Greek.

**1:17** For the righteousness of God is revealed in it from faith to faith, just as it is written, "But the righteous one will live by faith."

**1:18** For the wrath of God is being revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of people who suppress the truth in unrighteousness,

**1:19** because what can be known about God is plain within them, for God made it plain to them.

**1:20** For His invisible qualities have been clearly perceived since the creation of the world, being understood by the things made—both His eternal power and divine nature—with the result that they are without excuse,

**1:21** because, although knowing God, they neither glorified Him as God nor gave thanks, but became futile in their reasonings, and their senseless heart was darkened.

**1:22** Claiming to be wise, they became fools,

**1:23** and exchanged the glory of the imperishable God for a likeness of an image of a perishable human being, and of birds, four-footed animals, and reptiles.

**1:24** Therefore God handed them over in the desires of their hearts to impurity, so that their bodies would be dishonored among themselves,

**1:25** since they exchanged the truth of God for the lie, and revered and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.

**1:26** Because of this, God handed them over to passions of dishonor; for their females exchanged the natural function for that which is contrary to nature,

**1:27** and likewise also the males, having abandoned the natural function of the female, burned in their craving toward one another, males with males working out indecency and receiving in themselves the due penalty of their error.

**1:28** And just as they did not see fit to keep God in their knowledge, God handed them over to a depraved mind, to do things that are not fitting,

**1:29** having been filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, greed, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, evil-mindedness; whisperers,

**1:30** slanderers, God-haters, insolent, haughty, braggarts, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

**1:31** senseless, untrustworthy, without natural affection, merciless;

**1:32** who, although fully knowing the righteous decree of God, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do them, but also give approval to those who practice them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Greek horisthentos can mean 'designated,' 'appointed,' or 'declared.' The phrase 'Spirit of holiness' reflects a Semitic idiom, and 'by the resurrection of the dead' literally translates ex anastaseos nekron.
- **1:17** — The phrase 'from faith to faith' (ek pisteos eis pistin) is variously understood as 'by faith from start to finish,' 'from God's faithfulness to human faith,' or 'from one believer's faith to another.' In the Habakkuk 2:4 quotation, 'by faith' can modify either 'the righteous one' or 'will live.'
- **1:28** — Paul uses a Greek wordplay between edokimasan ('see fit' / 'approve') and adokimon ('depraved' / 'unfit' / 'disqualified').
- **1:30** — Greek theostygeis can be read actively ('God-haters') or passively ('hated by God').
