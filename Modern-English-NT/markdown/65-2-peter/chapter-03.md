## Text

**3:1** Beloved, this is already the second letter I am writing to you, in both of which I am stirring up your sincere mind by way of reminder,

**3:2** that you should remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Savior given through your apostles,

**3:3** knowing this first of all, that in the last days mockers will come with mocking, walking according to their own desires

**3:4** and saying, "Where is the promise of His coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things remain just as they were from the beginning of creation."

**3:5** For they deliberately overlook this fact, that heavens existed from long ago, and an earth formed out of water and through water by the word of God,

**3:6** through which the world of that time, being flooded with water, perished.

**3:7** But by the same word the present heavens and earth have been stored up for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly people.

**3:8** But do not let this one thing escape your notice, beloved, that with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.

**3:9** The Lord does not delay regarding the promise, as some regard delay, but is patient toward you, not wanting any to perish, but all to come to repentance.

**3:10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a roar, and the elements will be dissolved with intense heat, and the earth and the works in it will be found.

**3:11** Since all these things are thus to be dissolved, what sort of people ought you to be in holy conduct and acts of godliness,

**3:12** awaiting and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be set on fire and dissolved, and the elements will melt with intense heat!

**3:13** But according to His promise we are awaiting new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.

**3:14** Therefore, beloved, since you are awaiting these things, be diligent to be found by Him spotless and blameless, in peace.

**3:15** And regard the patience of our Lord as salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you according to the wisdom given to him,

**3:16** as he also does in all his letters when speaking in them about these things. In them there are some things hard to understand, which the untaught and unstable distort, as they also do the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction.

**3:17** You therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, be on your guard so that you are not carried away by the error of lawless people and fall from your own stability;

**3:18** but grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:10** — The Greek text reads "heurethesetai" ("will be found," often understood as "exposed" or "laid bare" for judgment), whereas other manuscripts read "katakaesetai" ("will be burned up") or "ouch heurethesetai" ("will not be found"). Greek "stoicheia" ("elements") may refer either to the basic physical components of the cosmos or to heavenly bodies.
- **3:12** — Greek "speudontas" can mean either "hastening" (speeding the arrival of God's day through faithful living) or "earnestly desiring."
- **3:16** — Greek "en hais" ("in them") is feminine plural and refers to Paul's letters ("epistolais"), though some manuscripts read neuter "en hois" ("in these matters").
