## Text

**4:1** After these things I looked, and see, an open door in heaven, and the first voice that I heard, like a trumpet speaking with me, was saying, "Come up here, and I will show you the things that must take place." After these things,

**4:2** immediately I was in the Spirit; and see, a throne was set in heaven, and on the throne One was sitting,

**4:3** and the One sitting was like a jasper stone and a carnelian in appearance, and a rainbow around the throne was like an emerald in appearance.

**4:4** And around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and on the thrones were twenty-four elders sitting, clothed in white garments, and on their heads were golden crowns.

**4:5** And from the throne proceed flashes of lightning and sounds and peals of thunder; and seven torches of fire are burning before the throne, which are the seven spirits of God,

**4:6** and before the throne is something like a sea of glass, like crystal. And in the center of the throne and around the throne are four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind;

**4:7** and the first living creature is like a lion, and the second living creature is like a calf, and the third living creature has a face like that of a human being, and the fourth living creature is like a flying eagle;

**4:8** and the four living creatures, each one of them having six wings, are full of eyes all around and inside; and they have no rest day and night, saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God, the Almighty, the One who was and the One who is and the One who is coming."

**4:9** And whenever the living creatures give glory and honor and thanksgiving to the One sitting on the throne, to the One living forever and ever,

**4:10** the twenty-four elders will fall down before the One sitting on the throne, and will worship the One living forever and ever, and will cast their crowns before the throne, saying,

**4:11** "Worthy are You, our Lord and our God, to receive the glory and the honor and the power, because You created all things, and on account of Your will they existed and were created."

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Greek source punctuates a period before "After these things" (μετὰ ταῦτα), beginning the sentence that continues in 4:2; many editions instead attach the phrase to the end of the voice's speech ("what must take place after these things").
- **4:2** — Greek ἐν πνεύματι lacks the article (literally "in spirit") and refers either to being moved by the Holy Spirit or to entering a visionary spiritual state (cf. 1:10).
- **4:6** — The phrase "in the center of the throne and around the throne" (ἐν μέσῳ τοῦ θρόνου καὶ κύκλῳ τοῦ θρόνου) is spatially difficult, likely describing the four creatures positioned around the middle of each side of the throne.
- **4:9** — In 4:9-10 the Greek pairs ὅταν ("whenever") with future indicative verbs ("will fall down... will worship... will cast"), a characteristic idiom in Revelation expressing repeated, ongoing action.
- **4:11** — Greek ἦσαν καὶ ἐκτίσθησαν ("they existed and were created") has a notable textual history; some manuscripts read "they are" or "they did not exist."
