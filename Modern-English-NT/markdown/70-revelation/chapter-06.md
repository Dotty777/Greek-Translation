## Text

**6:1** And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying, as if with the voice of thunder, "Come!"

**6:2** And I saw, and look, a white horse, and the one sitting on it had a bow; and a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering and in order to conquer.

**6:3** And when He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, "Come!"

**6:4** And another horse, fiery red, went out; and to the one sitting on it, it was given to take peace from the earth and so that they would slaughter one another, and a great sword was given to him.

**6:5** And when He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, "Come!" And I saw, and look, a black horse, and the one sitting on it had a pair of scales in his hand.

**6:6** And I heard something like a voice in the middle of the four living creatures saying, "One quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius; and do not harm the oil and the wine."

**6:7** And when He opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, "Come!"

**6:8** And I saw, and look, a pale green horse; and the name of the one sitting on it was Death, and Hades was following with him. And authority was given to them over a fourth of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with pestilence, and by the wild beasts of the earth.

**6:9** And when He opened the fifth seal, I saw underneath the altar the souls of those who had been slaughtered because of the word of God and because of the testimony they held.

**6:10** And they cried out with a loud voice, saying, "How long, Master, holy and true, will You not judge and avenge our blood against those who dwell on the earth?"

**6:11** And a white robe was given to each of them, and it was told to them that they should rest a little while longer, until both their fellow slaves and their brothers, who were about to be killed just as they also were, should be completed.

**6:12** And I saw when He opened the sixth seal, and a great earthquake occurred; and the sun became black like sackcloth made of hair, and the whole moon became like blood,

**6:13** and the stars of the sky fell to the earth, as a fig tree drops its unripe figs when shaken by a great wind,

**6:14** and the sky parted like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved out of its place.

**6:15** And the kings of the earth and the nobles and the commanders and the rich and the mighty, and every slave and free person, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains.

**6:16** And they say to the mountains and to the rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the face of the One sitting on the throne and from the wrath of the Lamb,

**6:17** because the great day of Their wrath has arrived, and who is able to stand?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The summons "Come!" (Greek erchou) here and in verses 3, 5, and 7 is addressed to the emerging horsemen rather than to John; later manuscripts add "and see," which is absent from the earliest witnesses.
- **6:6** — "Quart" translates Greek choinix (about one liter, a single day's grain ration), and a denarius was a typical day's wage for a laborer, depicting extreme famine prices.
- **6:8** — "Pale green" (Greek chloros) describes a sickly greenish-yellow pallor. "Pestilence" translates Greek thanatos (literally "death"), reflecting the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew deber ("plague" or "pestilence," as in Ezekiel 14:21).
- **6:11** — "Should be completed" renders the Greek passive verb plerothosin, referring to the full number of fellow martyrs being reached without an explicit noun for "number" in the text.
- **6:17** — The critical text reads the plural "Their wrath" (auton), referring jointly to the One sitting on the throne and the Lamb, whereas some manuscripts read the singular "His wrath."
