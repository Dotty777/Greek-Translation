## Text

**22:1** And he showed me a river of the water of life, bright as crystal, flowing out from the throne of God and of the Lamb

**22:2** in the middle of its street. And on this side and on that side of the river was the tree of life, producing twelve fruits, yielding its fruit each month; and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.

**22:3** And there will be no curse any longer. And the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and His servants will serve Him,

**22:4** and they will see His face, and His name will be upon their foreheads.

**22:5** And there will be no night any longer, and they have no need of the light of a lamp or of the light of the sun, because the Lord God will shine upon them; and they will reign forever and ever.

**22:6** And he said to me, "These words are faithful and true, and the Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets, sent His angel to show His servants the things that must happen soon;

**22:7** and look, I am coming quickly. Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book."

**22:8** And I, John, am the one hearing and seeing these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who was showing me these things.

**22:9** And he said to me, "Do not do that! I am a fellow servant of yours and of your brothers the prophets and of those who keep the words of this book. Worship God."

**22:10** And he said to me, "Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book, because the time is near.

**22:11** Let the one who acts unjustly still act unjustly, and let the filthy one still be defiled, and let the righteous one still do righteousness, and let the holy one still be made holy."

**22:12** "Look, I am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, to repay each one according to what his work is.

**22:13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.

**22:14** Blessed are those who wash their robes, so that they will have the right to the tree of life and may enter through the gates into the city.

**22:15** Outside are the dogs and the sorcerers and the sexually immoral and the murderers and the idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices a lie.

**22:16** I, Jesus, sent My angel to testify these things to you for the churches. I am the Root and the Offspring of David, the bright morning star."

**22:17** And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come." And let the one who hears say, "Come." And let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life freely.

**22:18** I myself testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to these things, God will add to him the plagues written in this book;

**22:19** and if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away his share from the tree of life and out of the holy city, the things written in this book.

**22:20** The One who testifies to these things says, "Yes, I am coming quickly." Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.

**22:21** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The phrase "in the middle of its street" can be punctuated with the river in v. 1 (as here, following the Greek punctuation) or with the tree of life in v. 2. The singular noun "tree" is used collectively to depict the tree of life lining both banks of the river.
- **22:3** — Greek katathema (a New Testament hapax) can mean either "curse" or "accursed thing" (cf. Zech 14:11). The singular pronouns "His" and "Him" refer jointly to God and the Lamb sharing one throne.
- **22:7** — The quotation shifts without a new introductory formula into the first-person voice of Christ ("I am coming quickly"), either spoken directly by Christ or relayed on His behalf by the angel speaking in v. 6.
- **22:14** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "those who wash their robes," whereas later manuscripts and the Textus Receptus read "those who do His commandments," a variation aided by the close visual and phonetic similarity in Greek.
- **22:19** — Greek manuscripts read "the tree of life"; the reading "the book of life" in the Textus Receptus entered via a Latin copying confusion between ligno ("tree") and libro ("book").
- **22:21** — The earliest Greek text ends concisely with "with all"; later manuscripts expand the closing benediction to "with all the saints" or "with you all" and add "Amen."
