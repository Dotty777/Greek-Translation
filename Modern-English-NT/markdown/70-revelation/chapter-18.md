## Text

**18:1** After these things I saw another angel descending out of heaven, having great authority, and the earth was illuminated by his glory.

**18:2** And he cried out with a mighty voice, saying, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! And she has become a dwelling place of demons, a prison for every unclean spirit, and a prison for every unclean and hated bird,

**18:3** because from the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality all the nations have fallen, and the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality with her, and the merchants of the earth grew rich from the power of her luxury."

**18:4** And I heard another voice out of heaven, saying, "Come out of her, My people, so that you do not participate in her sins and so that you do not receive any of her plagues;

**18:5** for her sins have piled up all the way to heaven, and God has remembered her wrongdoings.

**18:6** Pay her back just as she herself paid, and pay back double according to her deeds; in the cup that she mixed, mix a double portion for her.

**18:7** As much as she glorified herself and lived in luxury, give her that same measure of torment and mourning. For in her heart she says, 'I sit as a queen, and I am not a widow, and I will never see mourning.'

**18:8** Therefore in one day her plagues will come: death and mourning and famine; and she will be burned up with fire, for mighty is the Lord God who has judged her.

**18:9** And the kings of the earth, who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her, will weep and wail over her when they see the smoke of her burning,

**18:10** standing at a distance because of the fear of her torment, saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city, Babylon, the mighty city! For in one hour your judgment has come.'

**18:11** And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo anymore,

**18:12** cargo of gold and silver and precious stones and pearls and fine linen and purple and silk and scarlet, and every kind of citron wood and every article of ivory and every article made of most costly wood and bronze and iron and marble,

**18:13** and cinnamon and spice and incense and perfume and frankincense and wine and olive oil and fine flour and wheat and cattle and sheep, and horses and carriages and slaves, and human souls.

**18:14** And the ripe fruit of your soul's desire has departed from you, and all the sumptuous and splendid things have perished from you, and they will never find them again.

**18:15** The merchants of these things, who grew rich from her, will stand at a distance because of the fear of her torment, weeping and mourning,

**18:16** saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city, she who was clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet, and adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls,

**18:17** for in one hour such great wealth has been laid waste!' And every shipmaster and everyone who sails to any place, and sailors and all who work the sea, stood at a distance

**18:18** and were crying out as they saw the smoke of her burning, saying, 'What city is like the great city?'

**18:19** And they threw dust on their heads and were crying out, weeping and mourning, saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city, in which all who had ships on the sea grew rich from her wealth, for in one hour she has been laid waste!'

**18:20** Rejoice over her, heaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, for God has rendered judgment for you against her."

**18:21** And a mighty angel lifted up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, "In this way, with violence, Babylon, the great city, will be thrown down, and will never be found again.

**18:22** And the sound of harpists and musicians and flute players and trumpeters will never be heard in you again, and no craftsman of any craft will ever be found in you again, and the sound of a mill will never be heard in you again,

**18:23** and the light of a lamp will never shine in you again, and the voice of bridegroom and bride will never be heard in you again; for your merchants were the magnates of the earth, because by your sorcery all the nations were deceived,

**18:24** and in her was found the blood of prophets and saints, and of all who have been slaughtered on the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — Greek phylake ("prison" or "haunt") can denote either a place of confinement for unclean spirits and birds or a desolate lair; some later manuscripts add a third clause mentioning "every unclean and hated beast."
- **18:3** — The critical Greek text reads peptokan ("have fallen"), supported by the earliest manuscripts, whereas some later manuscripts read pepokan ("have drunk"); Greek thymou can mean either "passion" or "wrath."
- **18:13** — Greek somaton literally means "bodies," a standard commercial term for slaves, paired climactically with psychas anthropon ("human souls" or "human lives," echoing Ezekiel 27:13).
- **18:17** — Greek pas ho epi topon pleon literally reads "everyone who sails to a place," referring to seafaring passengers traveling from port to port; later manuscripts read "all the company on ships."
- **18:20** — Greek ekrinen ho theos to krima hymon ex autes literally means "God has judged your judgment from her," an idiom indicating that God has vindicated His people by executing judgment against Babylon.
