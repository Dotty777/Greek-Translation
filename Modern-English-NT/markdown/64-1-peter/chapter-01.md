## Text

**1:1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen temporary residents of the dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

**1:2** according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in the sanctification of the Spirit, for obedience and the sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.

**1:3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to His abundant mercy gave us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

**1:4** into an imperishable, undefiled, and unfading inheritance, reserved in heaven for you,

**1:5** who by the power of God are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

**1:6** In this you greatly rejoice, even though now for a little while, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials,

**1:7** so that the proving of your faith, much more precious than gold that perishes though tested through fire, may be found for praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

**1:8** Though you have not seen Him, you love Him; and though you do not see Him now, yet believing in Him, you greatly rejoice with an inexpressible and glorified joy,

**1:9** receiving the outcome of your faith, the salvation of souls.

**1:10** Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace for you inquired diligently and searched carefully,

**1:11** searching into what time or what kind of time the Spirit of Christ in them was indicating, testifying beforehand about the sufferings for Christ and the glories after these things.

**1:12** To them it was revealed that they were serving not themselves, but you, in these things, which have now been announced to you through those who proclaimed the good news to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, things into which angels long to look.

**1:13** Therefore, having girded the loins of your mind, being completely sober-minded, set your hope on the grace being brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

**1:14** As children of obedience, do not be conformed to the former desires in your ignorance,

**1:15** but like the Holy One who called you, you yourselves also become holy in all your conduct,

**1:16** because it is written, "You are to be holy, because I am holy."

**1:17** And if you call on as Father the One who judges impartially according to each person's work, conduct yourselves in fear during the time of your sojourning,

**1:18** knowing that you were redeemed not with perishable things, silver or gold, from your futile conduct handed down from your forefathers,

**1:19** but with precious blood, as of an unblemished and spotless lamb, the blood of Christ,

**1:20** who was foreknown before the foundation of the world, yet was manifested at the end of the times for your sake,

**1:21** who through Him are believers in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.

**1:22** Having purified your souls in obedience to the truth for sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from the heart,

**1:23** having been born again not from perishable seed, but from imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.

**1:24** For, "All flesh is like grass, and all its glory like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls off,

**1:25** but the word of the Lord endures forever." And this is the word that was proclaimed as good news to you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:11** — Greek eis tina e poion kairon can be taken as "what time or what kind of time" (both interrogatives modifying "time") or "what person or what kind of time."
- **1:13** — The adverb teleios ("completely") stands between "being sober-minded" and "set your hope"; following the punctuation of the Greek source text here, it modifies "being sober-minded," though many construe it with "set your hope."
- **1:23** — The genitive participles zontos kai menontos ("living and enduring") may grammatically modify either "word" (as translated) or "God" (i.e., "the word of the living and enduring God").
