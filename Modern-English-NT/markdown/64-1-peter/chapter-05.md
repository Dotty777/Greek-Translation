## Text

**5:1** Therefore, I exhort the elders among you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a sharer in the glory that is about to be revealed:

**5:2** shepherd the flock of God among you, not under compulsion, but willingly according to God, and not for shameful gain, but eagerly,

**5:3** nor as domineering over those allotted to you, but becoming examples to the flock.

**5:4** And when the Chief Shepherd is manifested, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.

**5:5** Likewise, you younger ones, submit to the elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves in humility toward one another, because "God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble."

**5:6** Therefore, humble yourselves under God's mighty hand, so that He may exalt you in due time,

**5:7** casting all your worry upon Him, because He cares about you.

**5:8** Be sober; stay watchful. Your adversary, the devil, walks about like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour.

**5:9** Resist him, steadfast in the faith, knowing that the same sufferings are being experienced by your brotherhood in the world.

**5:10** Now the God of all grace, who called you into His eternal glory in Christ, after you have suffered a little while, will Himself restore, steady, strengthen, and establish you.

**5:11** To Him be the might forever and ever. Amen.

**5:12** Through Silvanus, the faithful brother as I consider him, I have written briefly to you, exhorting and testifying that this is the true grace of God; stand firm in it.

**5:13** She who is in Babylon, chosen alongside you, greets you, as does Mark, my son.

**5:14** Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to you all who are in Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Greek critical text omits the participle "exercising oversight" (episkopountes), which appears in many manuscripts, and includes "according to God" (meaning in accordance with God's will).
- **5:3** — "Those allotted to you" literally translates "the allotments" (ton kleron), referring to the portions of God's flock entrusted to the elders' care.
- **5:5** — The rare Greek verb enkomboomai ("clothe yourselves") originally denoted tying on a garment or servant's apron, picturing active, humble service.
- **5:13** — "She who is in Babylon, chosen alongside you" uses a feminine singular form widely understood to designate the church at Rome, with "Babylon" serving as a figurative name for the imperial capital.
