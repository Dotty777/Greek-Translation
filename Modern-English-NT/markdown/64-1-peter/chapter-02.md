## Text

**2:1** Therefore, putting off all wickedness and all deceit, hypocrisies, envies, and all slanders,

**2:2** like newborn infants, long for the pure spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow to salvation,

**2:3** if you have tasted that the Lord is kind.

**2:4** Coming to Him, a living stone, rejected indeed by people yet chosen and precious in the sight of God,

**2:5** you yourselves also, as living stones, are being built up as a spiritual house for a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

**2:6** For it stands in Scripture: "Look, I am laying in Zion a cornerstone, chosen and precious, and the one who believes in Him will never be put to shame."

**2:7** Therefore the honor is for you who believe; but for those who disbelieve, "The stone that the builders rejected, this very one has become the head of the corner,"

**2:8** and, "A stone of stumbling and a rock of offense." They stumble by disobeying the word, to which they were also appointed.

**2:9** But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for His possession, so that you may declare the praiseworthy deeds of the One who called you out of darkness into His wonderful light;

**2:10** who once were not a people, yet now are God's people; who had not been shown mercy, yet now have been shown mercy.

**2:11** Beloved, I urge you as sojourners and temporary residents to abstain from fleshly desires, which wage war against the soul.

**2:12** Keep your conduct honorable among the Gentiles, so that in the matter in which they slander you as evildoers, by observing your good works they may glorify God on the day of visitation.

**2:13** Be subject to every human institution for the Lord's sake: whether to a king as supreme,

**2:14** or to governors as sent by him for the punishment of evildoers and the praise of those who do good

**2:15** (for this is the will of God, that by doing good you silence the ignorance of foolish people),

**2:16** as free people, and not holding your freedom as a covering for wickedness, but as slaves of God.

**2:17** Give honor to everyone, love the brotherhood, fear God, honor the king.

**2:18** Household servants, be subject to your masters in all fear, not only to those who are good and gentle, but also to those who are harsh.

**2:19** For this is favor, if because of conscience toward God someone endures sorrows while suffering unjustly.

**2:20** For what credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten, you endure? But if, when you do good and suffer, you endure, this is favor with God.

**2:21** For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered on your behalf, leaving you an example so that you should follow in His steps,

**2:22** who did not commit sin, nor was deceit found in His mouth;

**2:23** who, when reviled, did not revile in return; when suffering, did not threaten, but kept entrusting Himself to the One who judges righteously;

**2:24** who Himself carried our sins in His body up to the tree, so that, having died to sins, we might live to righteousness; by His wound you were healed.

**2:25** For you were straying like sheep, yet now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — Greek logikon can mean "spiritual," "rational," or "pertaining to the word," echoing the enduring word of God in 1:23-25.
- **2:7** — Greek he time ("the honor") echoes entimon ("precious") in 2:4 and 2:6, and may also be understood as "the preciousness" or "the value" of the stone.
- **2:8** — The clause eis ho kai etethesan ("to which they were also appointed") is debated as to whether it refers to stumbling as the appointed consequence of disobedience or to disobedience itself.
- **2:19** — Greek charis ("favor" or "grace") here refers to conduct that wins God's approval; syneidesin theou can mean "conscience toward God" or "mindful awareness of God."
