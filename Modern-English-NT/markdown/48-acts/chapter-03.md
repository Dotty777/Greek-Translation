## Text

**3:1** Now Peter and John were going up into the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour.

**3:2** And a certain man, lame from his mother's womb, was being carried along, whom they placed daily at the gate of the temple called Beautiful to ask for alms from those entering into the temple.

**3:3** When he saw Peter and John about to enter into the temple, he asked to receive alms.

**3:4** And Peter, fixing his gaze on him along with John, said, "Look at us."

**3:5** So he gave them his attention, expecting to receive something from them.

**3:6** And Peter said, "Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have, this I give to you: in the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, walk!"

**3:7** And taking hold of him by the right hand, he raised him up; and immediately his feet and ankles were strengthened.

**3:8** And leaping up, he stood and began to walk, and he went with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God.

**3:9** And all the people saw him walking and praising God.

**3:10** And they recognized him as the one who used to sit for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple, and they were filled with wonder and astonishment at what had happened to him.

**3:11** Now while he was clinging to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them at the portico called Solomon's, utterly amazed.

**3:12** When Peter saw this, he answered the people, "Men of Israel, why are you amazed at this, or why do you fix your gaze on us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?

**3:13** The God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified His Servant Jesus, whom you handed over and denied in the presence of Pilate, when he had decided to release Him.

**3:14** But you denied the Holy and Righteous One and asked for a murderer to be granted to you,

**3:15** and you put to death the Founder of life, whom God raised from the dead, of which we are witnesses.

**3:16** And on the basis of faith in His name, His name has strengthened this man whom you see and know; and the faith that is through Him has given him this complete health in the presence of all of you.

**3:17** And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, just as your rulers also did.

**3:18** But what God announced beforehand through the mouth of all the prophets, that His Christ would suffer, He has fulfilled in this way.

**3:19** Repent therefore and turn back, so that your sins may be wiped away,

**3:20** so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord, and that He may send the Christ appointed for you, Jesus,

**3:21** whom heaven must receive until the times of the restoration of all things about which God spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets from ancient times.

**3:22** Moses indeed said, 'The Lord our God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers; you will listen to Him in everything that He may speak to you.

**3:23** And it will be that every soul who does not listen to that prophet will be utterly destroyed from among the people.'

**3:24** And indeed all the prophets from Samuel and those who came after, as many as have spoken, have also proclaimed these days.

**3:25** You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant which God made with your fathers, saying to Abraham, 'And in your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed.'

**3:26** To you first, God, having raised up His Servant, sent Him to bless you by turning each one away from your wicked ways."

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — Many later manuscripts add "Rise up and" before "walk," whereas the earliest witnesses read simply "walk."
- **3:13** — Greek pais can mean "child" or "servant"; so also in 3:26, 4:27, and 4:30.
- **3:15** — Greek archegos denotes an originator, founder, or pioneering leader; "Founder of life" conveys the sense of the one who originates and leads into life.
- **3:22** — The critical Greek text reads "the Lord our God" (first-person plural), while some manuscripts harmonize with Deuteronomy 18:15 to read "your God."
