## Text

**2:1** And when the day of Pentecost was being fulfilled, they were all together in one place.

**2:2** And suddenly a sound came out of heaven like that of a violent rushing wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.

**2:3** And tongues as of fire appeared to them, dividing apart, and one rested upon each one of them.

**2:4** And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues, just as the Spirit was granting them to speak out.

**2:5** Now there were Jews residing in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation under heaven.

**2:6** And when this sound occurred, the multitude came together and was bewildered, because each one was hearing them speaking in his own language.

**2:7** And they were astonished and marveled, saying, "Look, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

**2:8** And how is it that we each hear in our own language in which we were born?

**2:9** Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those residing in Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,

**2:10** Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the regions of Libya around Cyrene, and visiting Romans,

**2:11** both Jews and proselytes, Cretans and Arabs—we hear them speaking in our own tongues the great deeds of God."

**2:12** And they were all astonished and thoroughly perplexed, saying to one another, "What does this mean?"

**2:13** But others, mocking, were saying, "They are full of sweet wine."

**2:14** But Peter, standing up with the eleven, raised his voice and spoke out to them: "Men of Judea and all who reside in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and give ear to my words.

**2:15** For these men are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is the third hour of the day;

**2:16** but this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:

**2:17** 'And it will be in the last days,' says God, 'I will pour out from My Spirit upon all flesh, and your sons and your daughters will prophesy, and your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams;

**2:18** and even upon My male slaves and upon My female slaves in those days I will pour out from My Spirit, and they will prophesy.

**2:19** And I will grant wonders in heaven above and signs on the earth below, blood and fire and vapor of smoke.

**2:20** The sun will be turned into darkness and the moon into blood, before the great and glorious day of the Lord comes.

**2:21** And it will be that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'

**2:22** Men of Israel, listen to these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a man attested to you by God with mighty works and wonders and signs which God performed through Him in your midst, just as you yourselves know—

**2:23** this Man, delivered over by the predetermined purpose and foreknowledge of God, you nailed up by the hand of lawless men and put to death,

**2:24** whom God raised up, having loosed the pangs of death, because it was not possible for Him to be held fast by it.

**2:25** For David says concerning Him: 'I was continually seeing the Lord before me, because He is at my right hand, so that I may not be shaken.

**2:26** Therefore my heart was glad and my tongue exulted; moreover, my flesh also will dwell in hope;

**2:27** because You will not abandon my soul to Hades, nor will You allow Your Holy One to see decay.

**2:28** You have made known to me paths of life; You will fill me with gladness in Your presence.'

**2:29** Men and brothers, it is permissible to speak to you with boldness concerning the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is among us to this day.

**2:30** Being a prophet, therefore, and knowing that God had sworn to him with an oath to seat one from the fruit of his loins upon his throne,

**2:31** having foreseen this, he spoke concerning the resurrection of the Christ, that He was neither abandoned to Hades, nor did His flesh see decay.

**2:32** This Jesus God raised up, of which we all are witnesses.

**2:33** Having been exalted, therefore, to the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He has poured out this which you see and hear.

**2:34** For David did not ascend into the heavens, yet he himself says: 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand,

**2:35** until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet."'

**2:36** Therefore let all the house of Israel know with certainty that God has made Him both Lord and Christ—this Jesus whom you crucified."

**2:37** Now when they heard this, they were pierced to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, "What should we do, men and brothers?"

**2:38** And Peter said to them, "Repent, and each one of you must be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

**2:39** For the promise is for you and for your children and for all who are far off, as many as the Lord our God calls to Himself."

**2:40** And with many other words he testified solemnly and was urging them, saying, "Be saved from this crooked generation."

**2:41** So then, those who welcomed his word were baptized, and on that day about three thousand souls were added.

**2:42** And they were continually devoting themselves to the teaching of the apostles and to the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to the prayers.

**2:43** And fear was coming upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were taking place through the apostles.

**2:44** And all who had believed were together and held all things in common,

**2:45** and they were selling their properties and possessions and distributing them to all, as anyone had need.

**2:46** And day by day, continuing steadfastly with one mind in the temple and breaking bread from house to house, they were partaking of food with exultation and simplicity of heart,

**2:47** praising God and having favor with all the people. And day by day the Lord kept adding to their gathering those who were being saved.

## Translator Notes

- **2:24** — Greek odinas literally means "birth pangs" (reflecting the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew chevel, which can mean either "cords/bonds" or "pangs"), paired with lysas ("having loosed"), evoking both the agony and the bondage of death.
- **2:30** — The critical Greek text preserves the shorter reading without "that according to the flesh He would raise up the Christ," leaving the infinitive kathisai ("to seat") directly dependent on God's oath to David.
- **2:33** — The dative te dexia may be taken either as locative ("to/at the right hand of God," echoing v. 34) or as instrumental ("by the right hand of God," referring to divine power).
- **2:47** — The critical Greek text concludes with epi to auto (literally "together" or "to the same place," used idiomatically for the assembled community as in vv. 1 and 44), whereas later manuscripts read "to the church."
