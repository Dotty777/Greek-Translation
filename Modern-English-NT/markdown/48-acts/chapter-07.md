## Text

**7:1** Then the high priest said, "Are these matters so?"

**7:2** And he said, "Men, brothers and fathers, listen! The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham while he was in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran,

**7:3** and said to him, 'Go out from your land and from your relatives, and come into the land that I will show you.'

**7:4** Then he went out from the land of the Chaldeans and lived in Haran. And from there, after his father died, He relocated him into this land in which you now live,

**7:5** and He did not give him an inheritance in it, not even the space of a foot; yet He promised to give it to him as a possession and to his offspring after him, even though he had no child.

**7:6** And God spoke in this way, that his offspring would be resident aliens in a foreign land, and they would enslave them and mistreat them for four hundred years.

**7:7** 'And the nation to which they will be enslaved I Myself will judge,' God said, 'and after these things they will come out and worship Me in this place.'

**7:8** And He gave him the covenant of circumcision; and so he fathered Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day, and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered the twelve patriarchs.

**7:9** And the patriarchs, being jealous of Joseph, sold him into Egypt; yet God was with him,

**7:10** and rescued him from all his afflictions, and gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and he appointed him ruler over Egypt and all his household.

**7:11** Now a famine came over all Egypt and Canaan, and great affliction, and our fathers could not find food.

**7:12** But when Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent out our fathers the first time.

**7:13** And on the second visit Joseph was made known to his brothers, and Joseph's family became known to Pharaoh.

**7:14** Then Joseph sent and summoned his father Jacob and all his relatives, seventy-five persons in all.

**7:15** And Jacob went down to Egypt. And he and our fathers died,

**7:16** and they were carried over to Shechem and laid in the tomb that Abraham had bought for a sum of silver from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.

**7:17** But as the time of the promise that God had pledged to Abraham was drawing near, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt,

**7:18** until another king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.

**7:19** This man dealt craftily with our race and mistreated our fathers, making them expose their infants so that they would not be kept alive.

**7:20** At that time Moses was born, and he was beautiful to God; and he was brought up for three months in his father's house.

**7:21** And when he had been exposed, Pharaoh's daughter took him up and brought him up for herself as a son.

**7:22** And Moses was instructed in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was powerful in his words and deeds.

**7:23** Now when a forty-year period was coming to completion for him, it came into his heart to visit his brothers, the sons of Israel.

**7:24** And seeing one of them being wronged, he defended him and avenged the oppressed man by striking down the Egyptian.

**7:25** And he supposed that his brothers understood that God was giving them deliverance through his hand, but they did not understand.

**7:26** And on the following day he appeared to them as they were fighting and tried to reconcile them to peace, saying, 'Men, you are brothers; why are you wronging one another?'

**7:27** But the one who was wronging his neighbor pushed him away, saying, 'Who appointed you ruler and judge over us?

**7:28** Do you want to kill me in the same way you killed the Egyptian yesterday?'

**7:29** And at this word Moses fled and became a resident alien in the land of Midian, where he fathered two sons.

**7:30** And when forty years had been completed, an angel appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, in a flame of fire in a bush.

**7:31** When Moses saw it, he marveled at the sight; and as he was approaching to look closely, the voice of the Lord came:

**7:32** 'I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob.' And trembling, Moses did not dare to look closely.

**7:33** And the Lord said to him, 'Untie the sandals from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.

**7:34** I have surely seen the mistreatment of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their groaning, and I have come down to rescue them. And now come, I will send you to Egypt.'

**7:35** This Moses, whom they disowned, saying, 'Who appointed you ruler and judge?'—this man God has sent as both ruler and redeemer by the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.

**7:36** This man led them out, performing wonders and signs in the land of Egypt and in the Red Sea and in the wilderness for forty years.

**7:37** This is the Moses who said to the sons of Israel, 'God will raise up for you a prophet from among your brothers like me.'

**7:38** This is the one who was in the assembly in the wilderness with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai, and with our fathers—the one who received living oracles to give to you,

**7:39** to whom our fathers were unwilling to be obedient, but they pushed him away and in their hearts turned back to Egypt,

**7:40** saying to Aaron, 'Make for us gods who will go before us; for this Moses, who led us out of the land of Egypt—we do not know what has happened to him.'

**7:41** And they made a calf in those days and brought a sacrifice to the idol, and were rejoicing in the works of their hands.

**7:42** But God turned away and gave them over to worship the host of heaven, just as it is written in the book of the prophets: 'Did you offer to Me slaughtered animals and sacrifices for forty years in the wilderness, house of Israel?

**7:43** And you took up the tent of Moloch and the star of the god Rompha, the images that you made to bow down to them; and I will relocate you beyond Babylon.'

**7:44** Our fathers had the tent of testimony in the wilderness, just as the One who spoke to Moses directed him to make it according to the pattern that he had seen,

**7:45** which our fathers also, having received it in turn, brought in with Joshua when taking possession of the nations that God drove out from before our fathers, until the days of David,

**7:46** who found favor before God and asked to find a dwelling place for the house of Jacob.

**7:47** But Solomon built a house for Him.

**7:48** Yet the Most High does not live in things made by hands, just as the prophet says:

**7:49** 'Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet; what kind of house will you build for Me,' says the Lord, 'or what is the place of My rest?

**7:50** Did not My hand make all these things?'

**7:51** Stiff-necked and uncircumcised in hearts and ears, you are always resisting the Holy Spirit; as your fathers did, so also do you.

**7:52** Which of the prophets did your fathers not persecute? And they killed those who announced beforehand the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become betrayers and murderers,

**7:53** you who received the law by the ordinances of angels, and did not keep it."

**7:54** Now as they heard these things, they were cut to their hearts, and they were gnashing their teeth at him.

**7:55** But being full of the Holy Spirit, he gazed intently into heaven and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God,

**7:56** and he said, "Look, I see the heavens opened up and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God."

**7:57** But crying out with a loud voice, they covered their ears and rushed upon him with one accord,

**7:58** and having driven him out of the city, they began stoning him. And the witnesses laid down their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul.

**7:59** And they were stoning Stephen as he was calling out and saying, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

**7:60** And kneeling down, he cried out with a loud voice, "Lord, do not charge this sin against them." And when he had said this, he fell asleep.

## Translator Notes

- **7:14** — Stephen follows the Septuagint count of seventy-five persons (Gen 46:27 LXX; Exod 1:5 LXX), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists seventy.
- **7:16** — Stephen compresses two Old Testament accounts: Abraham's purchase of the burial cave at Machpelah from Ephron the Hittite (Gen 23) and Jacob's purchase of land at Shechem from the sons of Hamor, where Joseph's bones were buried (Gen 33:19; Josh 24:32).
- **7:20** — The Greek phrase asteios to theo (literally 'beautiful to God') is a Semitic idiom denoting either pleasing in God's sight or extraordinarily beautiful.
- **7:30** — The earliest witnesses read simply 'an angel,' while later manuscripts expand the phrase to 'an angel of the Lord' in harmonization with Exodus 3:2.
- **7:38** — Early manuscripts (including P74, B, and C) read 'to you' (hymin), whereas later witnesses assimilate to 'to us' (hemin).
- **7:43** — The Greek text reads 'Rompha' (with manuscript variants such as Rephan), reflecting the Septuagint of Amos 5:26; Stephen also replaces Amos 5:27's 'Damascus' with 'Babylon' in view of the Babylonian exile.
- **7:45** — The Greek name Iesous here renders the Hebrew name Joshua, Moses' successor.
- **7:46** — Early witnesses (including Sinaiticus*, B, and D) read 'for the house of Jacob' (oiko), while later manuscripts conform the wording to Psalm 132:5 by reading 'for the God of Jacob' (theo).
