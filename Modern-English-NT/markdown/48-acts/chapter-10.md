## Text

**10:1** Now a certain man in Caesarea named Cornelius, a centurion of the cohort called the Italian,

**10:2** devout and fearing God with all his household, doing many acts of charity for the people and praying to God continually,

**10:3** saw clearly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming in to him and saying to him, "Cornelius."

**10:4** And fixing his eyes on him and becoming frightened, he said, "What is it, sir?" And he said to him, "Your prayers and your acts of charity have ascended for a memorial before God.

**10:5** And now send men to Joppa and send for a certain Simon who is called Peter;

**10:6** he is lodging with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea."

**10:7** Now when the angel who was speaking to him departed, calling two of the household servants and a devout soldier from those who attended him,

**10:8** and explaining everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.

**10:9** On the next day, while they were traveling and approaching the city, Peter went up onto the housetop to pray about the sixth hour.

**10:10** And he became very hungry and wanted to eat; but while they were making preparations, a trance came upon him,

**10:11** and he sees heaven opened and a certain object like a large sheet descending, being lowered by four corners onto the earth,

**10:12** in which were all the four-footed animals and crawling creatures of the earth and birds of the sky.

**10:13** And a voice came to him, "Get up, Peter; slaughter and eat."

**10:14** But Peter said, "By no means, Lord, because I have never eaten anything common and unclean."

**10:15** And a voice came to him again a second time, "What God has cleansed, you must not regard as common."

**10:16** Now this happened three times, and immediately the object was taken up into heaven.

**10:17** Now while Peter was greatly perplexed within himself about what the vision that he had seen might mean, look, the men who had been sent by Cornelius, having inquired for Simon's house, stood at the gate,

**10:18** and calling out, they were asking whether Simon, who is called Peter, was lodging there.

**10:19** While Peter was reflecting on the vision, the Spirit said to him, "Look, men are looking for you.

**10:20** But get up, go down, and travel with them without hesitating at all, because I have sent them."

**10:21** So Peter went down to the men and said, "Look, I am the one you are looking for. What is the reason why you are here?"

**10:22** And they said, "Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and fearing God, and well spoken of by the whole nation of the Jews, was divinely directed by a holy angel to send for you to his house and to hear words from you."

**10:23** So he invited them in and lodged them. On the next day he got up and went out with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa went along with him.

**10:24** And on the next day he entered Caesarea. Now Cornelius was expecting them, having called together his relatives and close friends.

**10:25** And when it happened that Peter entered, Cornelius met him, fell at his feet, and bowed down in worship.

**10:26** But Peter raised him up, saying, "Stand up; I myself am also a man."

**10:27** And conversing with him, he went inside and found many who had gathered together,

**10:28** and he said to them, "You yourselves know how unlawful it is for a Jewish man to associate with or to visit a foreigner; yet God has shown me to call no person common or unclean.

**10:29** Therefore I also came without objection when I was sent for. So I ask for what reason you have sent for me."

**10:30** And Cornelius said, "Four days ago to this very hour, I was praying at the ninth hour in my house, and look, a man stood before me in shining clothing

**10:31** and said, 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard and your acts of charity have been remembered before God.

**10:32** Therefore send to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter; he is lodging in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea.'

**10:33** So I sent for you at once, and you have done well in coming. Now then, we are all present before God to hear all the things that have been commanded to you by the Lord."

**10:34** Then Peter opened his mouth and said, "In truth I comprehend that God is not one who shows partiality,

**10:35** but in every nation the one who fears Him and practices righteousness is acceptable to Him.

**10:36** The word that He sent to the sons of Israel, proclaiming good news of peace through Jesus Christ—He is Lord of all—

**10:37** you yourselves know the event that took place throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism that John proclaimed:

**10:38** Jesus from Nazareth, how God anointed Him with the Holy Spirit and power, who went around doing good and healing all those oppressed by the devil, because God was with Him.

**10:39** And we are witnesses of all the things that He did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem. They also put Him to death by hanging Him on a tree.

**10:40** This One God raised on the third day and granted Him to become visible,

**10:41** not to all the people, but to witnesses who had been chosen beforehand by God, to us, who ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead.

**10:42** And He commanded us to proclaim to the people and to testify solemnly that He is the One who has been appointed by God as Judge of the living and the dead.

**10:43** To Him all the prophets testify that everyone believing in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name."

**10:44** While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all those who were listening to the word.

**10:45** And the believers from the circumcision, as many as had come with Peter, were amazed that the gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even on the Gentiles;

**10:46** for they were hearing them speaking in tongues and magnifying God. Then Peter responded,

**10:47** "Can anyone withhold the water so that these should not be baptized, who received the Holy Spirit just as we also did?"

**10:48** And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay for some days.

## Translator Notes

- **10:19** — The Greek text reads simply "men" without a numeral; other manuscripts read "three" or "two."
- **10:20** — The Greek phrase μηδὲν διακρινόμενος (middle voice) means "without hesitating at all"; compare the active μηδὲν διακρίναντα, "making no distinction," in 11:12.
- **10:30** — The idiomatic time expression "Four days ago to this very hour" indicates four days earlier at this same time of day (the ninth hour, about 3:00 p.m.); the critical text omits "fasting," which appears in later manuscripts.
- **10:36** — Verses 36–38 preserve an abrupt spoken Greek construction in which the opening accusative ("The word that He sent...") is interrupted by the parenthetical statement "He is Lord of all" and resumed in v. 37 by "you yourselves know the event that took place."
