## Text

**13:1** Now in Antioch, in the church that was there, there were prophets and teachers: Barnabas, and Simeon who was called Niger, and Lucius of Cyrene, and Manaen, who was raised with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.

**13:2** While they were ministering to the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, "Set apart now for Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them."

**13:3** Then, after fasting and praying and laying their hands on them, they sent them off.

**13:4** So, having been sent out by the Holy Spirit, they went down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed away to Cyprus.

**13:5** When they arrived in Salamis, they were proclaiming the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews; and they also had John as an assistant.

**13:6** When they had gone through the entire island as far as Paphos, they found a certain man, a magician and Jewish false prophet whose name was Bar-Jesus,

**13:7** who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man. This man summoned Barnabas and Saul and sought to hear the word of God.

**13:8** But Elymas the magician (for that is how his name is translated) was opposing them, seeking to turn the proconsul away from the faith.

**13:9** But Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, fixed his gaze on him

**13:10** and said, "You who are full of all deceit and all villainy, son of the devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not stop twisting the straight ways of the Lord?

**13:11** And now, look, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you will be blind, not seeing the sun for a time." Immediately a mist and darkness fell upon him, and going about, he began searching for people to lead him by the hand.

**13:12** Then the proconsul, seeing what had happened, believed, being astounded at the teaching of the Lord.

**13:13** Now setting sail from Paphos, Paul and his companions came to Perga in Pamphylia; but John departed from them and returned to Jerusalem.

**13:14** But they went on from Perga and arrived at Pisidian Antioch; and going into the synagogue on the Sabbath day, they sat down.

**13:15** After the reading of the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue rulers sent word to them, saying, "Brothers, if you have any word of encouragement for the people, speak."

**13:16** So Paul stood up, and motioning with his hand, he said, "Men of Israel and you who fear God, listen.

**13:17** The God of this people Israel chose our fathers, and He made the people great during their stay as foreigners in the land of Egypt, and with an uplifted arm He led them out of it.

**13:18** And for a period of about forty years He put up with them in the wilderness.

**13:19** And after destroying seven nations in the land of Canaan, He gave their land to them as an inheritance

**13:20** in about four hundred and fifty years. And after these things He gave judges until Samuel the prophet.

**13:21** Then they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul the son of Kish, a man from the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.

**13:22** And after removing him, He raised up David for them as king, concerning whom He also testified and said, 'I have found David the son of Jesse, a man after My heart, who will do all My will.'

**13:23** From this man's offspring God, according to promise, brought to Israel a Savior, Jesus,

**13:24** after John had proclaimed beforehand, ahead of His coming, a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.

**13:25** And as John was completing his course, he kept saying, 'What do you suppose me to be? I am not He; rather, look, One is coming after me, the sandal of whose feet I am not worthy to untie.'

**13:26** Brothers, sons of the family of Abraham, and those among you who fear God, to us the word of this salvation has been sent.

**13:27** For those living in Jerusalem and their rulers, failing to recognize Him or the voices of the prophets that are read every Sabbath, fulfilled them by condemning Him.

**13:28** And though they found no grounds for death, they asked Pilate that He be put to death.

**13:29** And when they had carried out all the things written concerning Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb.

**13:30** But God raised Him from the dead;

**13:31** and for many days He appeared to those who had gone up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now His witnesses to the people.

**13:32** And we bring you the good news about the promise made to the fathers,

**13:33** that God has completely fulfilled it for our children by raising up Jesus, just as it is also written in the second Psalm: 'You are My Son; today I have fathered You.'

**13:34** And as for the fact that He raised Him from the dead, no longer about to return to decay, He has spoken in this way: 'I will give you the holy and faithful blessings of David.'

**13:35** Therefore He also says in another place, 'You will not allow Your Holy One to see decay.'

**13:36** For David, after serving the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep and was laid with his fathers and saw decay;

**13:37** but the One whom God raised did not see decay.

**13:38** Therefore let it be known to you, brothers, that through Him forgiveness of sins is being proclaimed to you, and from everything from which you could not be justified in the law of Moses,

**13:39** in Him everyone who believes is justified.

**13:40** Watch out, then, so that what was spoken in the prophets may not come upon you:

**13:41** 'Look, you scoffers, and be amazed, and perish; for I am doing a work in your days, a work that you will never believe even if someone recounts it to you in full.'"

**13:42** As they were going out, the people were urging that these words be spoken to them on the next Sabbath.

**13:43** And when the synagogue assembly broke up, many of the Jews and of the devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking with them, were urging them to remain in the grace of God.

**13:44** On the next Sabbath nearly the whole city gathered together to hear the word of God.

**13:45** But when the Jews saw the crowds, they were filled with jealousy and began contradicting the things being spoken by Paul, speaking abusively.

**13:46** Then Paul and Barnabas spoke out boldly and said, "It was necessary for the word of God to be spoken to you first; since you push it away and judge yourselves unworthy of eternal life, look, we are turning to the Gentiles.

**13:47** For so the Lord has commanded us: 'I have placed you as a light for the Gentiles, so that you may be for salvation to the end of the earth.'"

**13:48** When the Gentiles heard this, they began rejoicing and glorifying the word of the Lord, and as many as had been appointed to eternal life believed.

**13:49** And the word of the Lord was spreading throughout the entire region.

**13:50** But the Jews incited the devout women of high standing and the leading men of the city, and stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and drove them out of their territory.

**13:51** But they shook off the dust of their feet against them and went to Iconium.

**13:52** And the disciples were continually being filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **13:18** — The Greek text reads ἐτροποφόρησεν ("put up with" or "endured their ways"); another well-attested variant reads ἐτροφοφόρησεν ("cared for" or "carried like a nurse," echoing Deut 1:31 LXX).
- **13:20** — In this textual order, "in about four hundred and fifty years" concludes the preceding summary of Israel's history through the conquest of Canaan, rather than measuring the period of the judges that follows.
- **13:33** — The Greek text reads τοῖς τέκνοις ἡμῶν ("for our children"); some manuscripts read τοῖς τέκνοις αὐτῶν ἡμῖν ("for us, their children").
- **13:48** — The perfect passive participle τεταγμένοι ("had been appointed") reflects Luke's standard usage for external appointment, though some interpreters understand it in a middle sense ("were disposed" or "aligned themselves").
