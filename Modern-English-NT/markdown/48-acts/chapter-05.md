## Text

**5:1** Now a certain man named Ananias, together with his wife Sapphira, sold a piece of property,

**5:2** and kept back some of the proceeds, with his wife also knowing about it, and bringing a certain part, he placed it at the feet of the apostles.

**5:3** But Peter said, "Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart for you to lie to the Holy Spirit and keep back some of the proceeds of the field?

**5:4** While it remained, did it not remain yours, and after it was sold, was it not under your authority? Why is it that you put this deed in your heart? You did not lie to people, but to God."

**5:5** And as Ananias heard these words, he fell down and breathed his last; and great fear came upon all who heard.

**5:6** Then the younger men rose up, wrapped him up, and carrying him out, they buried him.

**5:7** Now an interval of about three hours passed, and his wife, not knowing what had happened, came in.

**5:8** And Peter responded to her, "Tell me, did you sell the field for this much?" And she said, "Yes, for that much."

**5:9** Then Peter said to her, "Why is it that you agreed together to test the Spirit of the Lord? Look, the feet of those who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out."

**5:10** And immediately she fell at his feet and breathed her last; and when the young men came in, they found her dead, and carrying her out, they buried her beside her husband.

**5:11** And great fear came upon the whole church and upon all who heard these things.

**5:12** Now through the hands of the apostles many signs and wonders were taking place among the people; and they were all with one accord in Solomon's Portico.

**5:13** Yet none of the rest dared to join themselves to them, but the people held them in high esteem,

**5:14** and even more, believers in the Lord were being added, multitudes of both men and women;

**5:15** so that they even carried the sick out into the streets and placed them on cots and mats, in order that as Peter came by, at least his shadow might overshadow some of them.

**5:16** And the multitude from the cities around Jerusalem was also gathering together, bringing the sick and those troubled by unclean spirits, who were all being healed.

**5:17** But the high priest rose up, along with all those with him—that is, the sect of the Sadducees—and they were filled with jealousy,

**5:18** and they laid hands on the apostles and put them in public custody.

**5:19** But an angel of the Lord during the night opened the doors of the prison, and leading them out, said,

**5:20** "Go, and taking your stand in the temple, speak to the people all the words of this life."

**5:21** And when they heard this, they entered the temple at daybreak and began teaching. Now when the high priest and those with him arrived, they summoned the Sanhedrin and the entire council of elders of the sons of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.

**5:22** But the officers who arrived did not find them in the prison, and returning, they reported,

**5:23** saying, "We found the prison locked with complete security and the guards standing at the doors, but when we opened them, we found no one inside."

**5:24** Now when both the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were greatly perplexed about them as to what this might come to.

**5:25** Then someone came and reported to them, "Look, the men whom you put in the prison are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

**5:26** Then the captain went with the officers and brought them, without force, for they were afraid of the people, that they might be stoned.

**5:27** And when they had brought them, they set them before the Sanhedrin. And the high priest questioned them,

**5:28** saying, "We strictly ordered you not to teach in this name, and look, you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and you intend to bring this man's blood upon us."

**5:29** But Peter and the apostles answered and said, "We must obey God rather than people.

**5:30** The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you put to death by hanging Him on a tree.

**5:31** Him God exalted to His right hand as Leader and Savior, to grant repentance to Israel and forgiveness of sins.

**5:32** And we are witnesses of these things, and so is the Holy Spirit, whom God has given to those who obey Him."

**5:33** Now when they heard this, they were infuriated and wanted to put them to death.

**5:34** But a certain Pharisee in the Sanhedrin named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law respected by all the people, stood up and ordered the men to be put outside for a short time,

**5:35** and he said to them, "Men of Israel, take care for yourselves regarding these men, what you are about to do.

**5:36** For before these days Theudas rose up, claiming himself to be somebody, and a number of men, about four hundred, joined him; he was killed, and all who followed him were dispersed and came to nothing.

**5:37** After him Judas the Galilean rose up in the days of the census and drew away people after himself; he too perished, and all who followed him were scattered.

**5:38** And now I say to you, stay away from these men and let them alone (for if this plan or this work is from men, it will be overthrown,

**5:39** but if it is from God, you will not be able to overthrow them), or else you may even be found fighting against God." And they were persuaded by him,

**5:40** and after calling the apostles in and flogging them, they ordered them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and released them.

**5:41** So they went on their way from the presence of the Sanhedrin, rejoicing that they were considered worthy to be dishonored for the sake of the Name.

**5:42** And every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not cease teaching and proclaiming the good news of Jesus as the Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **5:14** — In Greek, "the Lord" (to kyrio) can modify either "believers" ("believers in the Lord") or "were being added" ("were being added to the Lord").
- **5:28** — The critical Greek text omits the negative particle found in later manuscripts, framing the clause as a direct accusation ("We strictly ordered you") rather than a rhetorical question.
- **5:31** — The Greek dative "te dexia autou" may be locative ("to His right hand") or instrumental ("by His right hand"); the title "archegon" carries the sense of "Leader," "Prince," or "Pioneer."
- **5:36** — Gamaliel places Theudas before Judas the Galilean (AD 6), which historians frequently discuss alongside Josephus's account of a later rebel named Theudas (c. AD 44-46).
