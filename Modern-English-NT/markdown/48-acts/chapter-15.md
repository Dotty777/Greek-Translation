## Text

**15:1** Now some men came down from Judea and were teaching the brothers, "Unless you are circumcised according to the custom of Moses, you cannot be saved."

**15:2** When Paul and Barnabas had no small dissension and debate with them, they appointed Paul and Barnabas and some others among them to go up to the apostles and elders in Jerusalem concerning this question.

**15:3** So, having been sent on their way by the church, they passed through both Phoenicia and Samaria, recounting in detail the conversion of the Gentiles, and they brought great joy to all the brothers.

**15:4** When they arrived in Jerusalem, they were welcomed by the church and the apostles and the elders, and they reported all that God had done with them.

**15:5** But some of those from the sect of the Pharisees who had believed stood up, saying, "It is necessary to circumcise them and to command them to keep the law of Moses."

**15:6** Then the apostles and the elders gathered together to look into this matter.

**15:7** After there had been much debate, Peter stood up and said to them, "Brothers, you know that in the early days God made a choice among you, that by my mouth the Gentiles should hear the word of the gospel and believe.

**15:8** And God, who knows the heart, bore witness to them by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as He also did to us;

**15:9** and He made no distinction between us and them, cleansing their hearts by faith.

**15:10** Now therefore, why are you putting God to the test by placing a yoke on the neck of the disciples that neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?

**15:11** On the contrary, we believe that through the grace of the Lord Jesus we are saved, in the same way as they also are."

**15:12** Then all the multitude fell silent, and they listened to Barnabas and Paul as they recounted all the signs and wonders that God had done among the Gentiles through them.

**15:13** After they became silent, James responded, saying, "Brothers, listen to me.

**15:14** Simeon has related how God first showed His care to take from the Gentiles a people for His name.

**15:15** And with this the words of the prophets agree, just as it is written:

**15:16** 'After these things I will return, and I will rebuild the fallen tabernacle of David; I will rebuild its ruins, and I will restore it,

**15:17** so that the rest of mankind may seek the Lord, and all the Gentiles upon whom My name has been called, says the Lord, who makes these things

**15:18** known from ages past.'

**15:19** Therefore I judge that we should not trouble those from the Gentiles who are turning to God,

**15:20** but rather write to them to abstain from the pollutions of idols, and from sexual immorality, and from what is strangled, and from blood.

**15:21** For from ancient generations Moses has had in every city those who proclaim him, since he is read in the synagogues every Sabbath."

**15:22** Then it seemed good to the apostles and the elders, together with the whole church, to choose men from among themselves to send to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas—Judas called Barsabbas, and Silas, leading men among the brothers—

**15:23** writing by their hand: "The apostles and the elders, brothers, to the brothers from the Gentiles throughout Antioch and Syria and Cilicia: Greetings.

**15:24** Since we have heard that some who went out from us have troubled you with words, unsettling your souls, though we gave them no instruction,

**15:25** it seemed good to us, having come to one accord, to choose men and send them to you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul,

**15:26** men who have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15:27** Therefore we have sent Judas and Silas, who will themselves also report the same things by word of mouth.

**15:28** For it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us to lay upon you no greater burden than these necessary things:

**15:29** that you abstain from things sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from things strangled, and from sexual immorality. If you keep yourselves free from these, you will do well. Farewell."

**15:30** So when they were sent off, they went down to Antioch, and having gathered the multitude together, they delivered the letter.

**15:31** When they read it, they rejoiced over the encouragement.

**15:32** And Judas and Silas, who were prophets themselves as well, encouraged the brothers with many words and strengthened them.

**15:33** After spending some time there, they were sent off in peace by the brothers to those who had sent them.

**15:35** But Paul and Barnabas remained in Antioch, teaching and proclaiming the good news of the word of the Lord, along with many others as well.

**15:36** Now after some days Paul said to Barnabas, "Let us return and visit the brothers in every city in which we proclaimed the word of the Lord, to see how they are doing."

**15:37** Now Barnabas wanted to take along John, called Mark, as well.

**15:38** But Paul thought it best not to take along this man who had withdrawn from them in Pamphylia and had not gone with them to the work.

**15:39** And there arose such a sharp disagreement that they separated from one another, and Barnabas took Mark with him and sailed away to Cyprus,

**15:40** while Paul chose Silas and departed, having been commended by the brothers to the grace of the Lord.

**15:41** And he went through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.

## Translator Notes

- **15:14** — "Simeon" (Symeon) is the Semitic form of Peter's name Simon.
- **15:16** — James quotes Amos 9:11-12 following the Septuagint, which reads "the rest of mankind" rather than the Masoretic Text's "the remnant of Edom."
- **15:20** — "Pollutions" (alisgema) is a rare word occurring only here in the New Testament, clarified in the council's letter (v. 29) as "things sacrificed to idols."
- **15:23** — The critical text reads "the apostles and the elders, brothers" in apposition, whereas later manuscripts insert "and the" before "brothers."
- **15:33** — The earliest manuscripts omit verse 34 (present in later manuscripts: "But it seemed good to Silas to remain there"), so verse numbering proceeds directly to 15:35.
- **15:34 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
