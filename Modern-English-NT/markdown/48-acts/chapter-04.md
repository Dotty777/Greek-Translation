## Text

**4:1** Now as they were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them,

**4:2** greatly disturbed because they were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.

**4:3** And they laid hands on them and placed them in custody until the next day, for it was already evening.

**4:4** Yet many of those who had heard the word believed, and the number of the men came to be about five thousand.

**4:5** Now it happened on the next day that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem,

**4:6** along with Annas the high priest, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of high-priestly descent.

**4:7** And having set them in the center, they began to inquire, "By what power or in what name did you do this?"

**4:8** Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, "Rulers of the people and elders,

**4:9** if we are being examined today regarding a good deed done to a sick man, as to how this man has been made well,

**4:10** let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel that in the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead—by Him this man stands before you healthy.

**4:11** This One is the stone that was scorned by you, the builders, which has become the head of the corner.

**4:12** And in no one else is there salvation, for there is not another name under heaven that has been given among people by which we must be saved."

**4:13** Now as they observed the boldness of Peter and John and realized that they were uneducated and ordinary men, they were amazed; and they recognized that they had been with Jesus.

**4:14** And seeing the man who had been healed standing with them, they had nothing to say in reply.

**4:15** So having ordered them to leave the Sanhedrin, they began conferring with one another,

**4:16** saying, "What should we do with these men? For that a notable sign has taken place through them is evident to all who live in Jerusalem, and we cannot deny it.

**4:17** Yet so that it may not spread any further among the people, let us threaten them to speak no longer in this name to anyone."

**4:18** And having called them in, they commanded them not to speak or to teach at all in the name of Jesus.

**4:19** But Peter and John answered and said to them, "Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God, you judge;

**4:20** for we cannot refrain from speaking about the things we have seen and heard."

**4:21** And after threatening them further, they released them, finding no way to punish them because of the people, since everyone was glorifying God for what had happened;

**4:22** for the man on whom this sign of healing had taken place was more than forty years old.

**4:23** When they had been released, they went to their own people and reported everything that the chief priests and the elders had said to them.

**4:24** And when they heard this, they lifted up their voice with one accord to God and said, "Sovereign Master, You are the One who made heaven and earth and the sea and all that is in them,

**4:25** who by the Holy Spirit, through the mouth of our father David Your servant, said, 'Why did the nations rage and the peoples devise empty things?

**4:26** The kings of the earth took their stand, and the rulers were gathered together as one against the Lord and against His Christ.'

**4:27** For truly in this city there were gathered together against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, along with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel,

**4:28** to do whatever Your hand and Your purpose determined beforehand to take place.

**4:29** And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and grant to Your slaves to speak Your word with all boldness,

**4:30** while You stretch out Your hand for healing, and signs and wonders take place through the name of Your holy Servant Jesus."

**4:31** And when they had prayed, the place in which they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began speaking the word of God with boldness.

**4:32** Now the multitude of those who had believed was of one heart and soul, and not a single one claimed that any of his possessions was his own, but everything was held in common among them.

**4:33** And with great power the apostles were bearing testimony to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.

**4:34** For there was not even a needy person among them, because as many as were owners of lands or houses would sell them and bring the proceeds of what was being sold

**4:35** and lay them at the feet of the apostles; and distribution was made to each one according as anyone had need.

**4:36** Now Joseph, who was called Barnabas by the apostles (which translated means Son of Encouragement), a Levite, a native of Cyprus,

**4:37** owning a field, sold it, brought the money, and laid it at the feet of the apostles.

## Translator Notes

- **4:9** — Greek sesostai (from sozo, "save" or "heal") means "has been made well" here and prepares for the wordplay with "salvation" and "be saved" in 4:12.
- **4:25** — The Greek syntax in the earliest manuscripts is highly compressed (literally "who of our father through the Holy Spirit of the mouth of David your servant said"); the translation smooths the clause while retaining all elements of the text.
- **4:29** — Greek doulois ("slaves") is distinct from pais ("Servant" or "servant") used for David in 4:25 and Jesus in 4:27 and 4:30.
