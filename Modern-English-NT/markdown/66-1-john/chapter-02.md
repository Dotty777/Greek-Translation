## Text

**2:1** My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you will not sin. And if anyone does sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ, the righteous One;

**2:2** and He Himself is the atonement for our sins, and not for ours alone, but also for the whole world.

**2:3** And by this we know that we have come to know Him: if we keep His commandments.

**2:4** The one who says, "I have come to know Him," and does not keep His commandments is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.

**2:5** But whoever keeps His word, truly in that person the love of God has been brought to perfection. By this we know that we are in Him:

**2:6** the one who says he remains in Him ought himself to walk just as He walked.

**2:7** Beloved, I am not writing a new commandment to you, but an old commandment that you had from the beginning; the old commandment is the word that you heard.

**2:8** Yet again, I am writing a new commandment to you, which is true in Him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light is already shining.

**2:9** The one who says he is in the light while hating his brother is in the darkness until now.

**2:10** The one who loves his brother remains in the light, and there is no cause for stumbling in him.

**2:11** But the one who hates his brother is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness blinded his eyes.

**2:12** I am writing to you, little children, because your sins have been forgiven on account of His name.

**2:13** I am writing to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have conquered the evil one.

**2:14** I wrote to you, children, because you have come to know the Father. I wrote to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning. I wrote to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have conquered the evil one.

**2:15** Do not love the world nor the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him,

**2:16** because everything that is in the world—the desire of the flesh and the desire of the eyes and the boastfulness of life—is not from the Father, but is from the world.

**2:17** And the world is passing away, and so is its desire, but the one who does the will of God remains forever.

**2:18** Children, it is the last hour; and just as you heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have arisen. From this we know that it is the last hour.

**2:19** They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have remained with us. Yet they went out so that it might be revealed that not all are of us.

**2:20** And you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know.

**2:21** I did not write to you because you do not know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie is from the truth.

**2:22** Who is the liar except the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This is the antichrist, the one who denies the Father and the Son.

**2:23** Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either; the one who confesses the Son has the Father as well.

**2:24** As for you, let what you heard from the beginning remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning remains in you, you too will remain in the Son and in the Father.

**2:25** And this is the promise that He Himself promised to us: eternal life.

**2:26** I wrote these things to you concerning those who are leading you astray.

**2:27** And as for you, the anointing that you received from Him remains in you, and you have no need for anyone to teach you; instead, as His anointing teaches you about all things—and is true and is no lie—even as it taught you, remain in Him.

**2:28** And now, little children, remain in Him, so that when He is revealed we may have confidence and not be put to shame before Him at His coming.

**2:29** If you know that He is righteous, you recognize that everyone who practices righteousness has also been born of Him.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek noun hilasmos ("atonement") is variously rendered "propitiation" (turning away wrath) or "expiation" (wiping away guilt).
- **2:14** — The shift from present "I am writing" in vv. 12-13 to aorist "I wrote" in v. 14 is often understood either as an epistolary aorist written from the readers' standpoint or as looking back to the preceding section.
- **2:20** — The Greek text reads "you all know" (oidate pantes), while many later manuscripts read "you know all things" (oidate panta).
- **2:27** — The verb menete is identical in form for the imperative ("remain in Him") and the indicative ("you remain in Him"); the imperative matches the clear command in v. 28.
