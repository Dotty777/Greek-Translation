## Text

**3:1** Look at what sort of love the Father has given us, that we might be called children of God—and so we are. Because of this the world does not know us, since it did not know Him.

**3:2** Beloved, now we are children of God, and it has not yet been revealed what we will be. We know that when He is revealed we will be like Him, because we will see Him just as He is.

**3:3** And everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as He is pure.

**3:4** Everyone who practices sin practices lawlessness as well, and sin is lawlessness.

**3:5** And you know that He was revealed to take away sins, and in Him there is no sin.

**3:6** Everyone who remains in Him does not sin; everyone who sins has neither seen Him nor come to know Him.

**3:7** Little children, let no one lead you astray: the one who practices righteousness is righteous, just as He is righteous.

**3:8** The one who practices sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was revealed: so that He might destroy the works of the devil.

**3:9** Everyone who has been born of God does not practice sin, because His seed remains in him; and he is not able to sin, because he has been born of God.

**3:10** By this the children of God and the children of the devil are evident: everyone who does not practice righteousness is not of God, nor is the one who does not love his brother.

**3:11** For this is the message that you heard from the beginning: that we should love one another,

**3:12** not like Cain, who was of the evil one and slaughtered his brother. And for what reason did he slaughter him? Because his own works were evil, whereas those of his brother were righteous.

**3:13** Do not be amazed, brothers, if the world hates you.

**3:14** We know that we have crossed over from death into life, because we love the brothers. The one who does not love remains in death.

**3:15** Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life remaining in him.

**3:16** By this we have come to know love: that He laid down His life on our behalf; and we ought to lay down our lives on behalf of the brothers.

**3:17** But whoever has the world's means of living and sees his brother in need, and closes his compassion against him, how does the love of God remain in him?

**3:18** Little children, let us not love in word or with the tongue, but in deed and truth.

**3:19** By this we will know that we are of the truth and will reassure our hearts before Him,

**3:20** whenever our heart condemns us, because God is greater than our heart and knows all things.

**3:21** Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence before God,

**3:22** and whatever we ask we receive from Him, because we keep His commandments and do the things that are pleasing in His sight.

**3:23** And this is His commandment: that we believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as He gave us a commandment.

**3:24** And the one who keeps His commandments remains in Him, and He in him. And by this we know that He remains in us: by the Spirit whom He gave to us.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Because the verb phanerothe ("is revealed") has no explicit subject, the clause can mean either "when He is revealed" (cf. 2:28) or "when it [what we will be] is revealed."
- **3:6** — The present-tense verbs "does not sin" and "sins" (and "to sin" in v. 9) are debated, being interpreted either as referring to habitual, ongoing sinning or as an absolute contrast between the realms of light and darkness.
- **3:9** — "His seed" (sperma autou) is variously interpreted as God's word, the Holy Spirit, or the divine life imparted in the new birth.
- **3:20** — The Greek has a syntactically difficult double hoti ("that/because"); taking the first as indefinite ho ti ean ("whenever" or "in whatever") followed by causal hoti ("because") is the most widely adopted reading.
