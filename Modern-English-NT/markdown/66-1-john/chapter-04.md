## Text

**4:1** Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but put the spirits to the test to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.

**4:2** By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh is from God,

**4:3** and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God; and this is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming, and now it is already in the world.

**4:4** You are from God, little children, and you have conquered them, because greater is the One who is in you than the one who is in the world.

**4:5** They are from the world; because of this, they speak from the world, and the world listens to them.

**4:6** We are from God. The one who knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. From this we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of deception.

**4:7** Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

**4:8** The one who does not love has not come to know God, because God is love.

**4:9** By this the love of God was revealed among us: that God has sent His only-begotten Son into the world so that we might live through Him.

**4:10** In this is love: not that we have loved God, but that He Himself loved us and sent His Son as a propitiation for our sins.

**4:11** Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.

**4:12** No one has seen God at any time; if we love one another, God remains in us, and His love is made complete in us.

**4:13** By this we know that we remain in Him and He in us, because He has given to us from His Spirit.

**4:14** And we ourselves have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son as Savior of the world.

**4:15** Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in him, and he in God.

**4:16** And we have come to know and have believed the love that God has for us. God is love, and the one who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him.

**4:17** By this, love has been made complete with us, so that we may have confidence on the day of judgment, because just as He is, so also are we in this world.

**4:18** There is no fear in love, but complete love drives out fear, because fear involves punishment, and the one who fears has not been made complete in love.

**4:19** We love, because He Himself loved us first.

**4:20** If anyone says, "I love God," and hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, is not able to love God, whom he has not seen.

**4:21** And we have this commandment from Him: that the one who loves God must also love his brother.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — Early Greek manuscripts read simply 'does not confess Jesus'; later manuscripts expand this to match v. 2, while a few Latin and patristic witnesses read 'annuls' or 'dissolves' Jesus.
- **4:9** — The Greek adjective monogenes ("only-begotten," matching John 1:14, 18; 3:16, 18) denotes a unique, one-of-a-kind son; "among us" translates en hemin (lit. "in us").
- **4:10** — On hilasmos ("propitiation"), see the note at 2:2.
- **4:19** — This text reads simply 'We love' without a direct object; other manuscripts add 'Him' or 'God'.
