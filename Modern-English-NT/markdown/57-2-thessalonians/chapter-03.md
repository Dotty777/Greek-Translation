## Text

**3:1** Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may run and be glorified, just as it also is with you,

**3:2** and that we may be rescued from perverse and evil men; for faith does not belong to all.

**3:3** But the Lord is faithful, who will establish you and guard you from the evil one.

**3:4** And we have confidence in the Lord concerning you, that what we command, you both do and will do.

**3:5** Now may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the steadfastness of Christ.

**3:6** Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you keep away from every brother who walks in a disorderly way and not according to the tradition that they received from us.

**3:7** For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not act in a disorderly way among you,

**3:8** nor did we eat bread free of charge from anyone, but with toil and hardship, working night and day so as not to be a burden to any of you;

**3:9** not because we do not have the right, but in order to give ourselves as a pattern to you, so that you might imitate us.

**3:10** For even when we were with you, we used to command you this: "If anyone is not willing to work, neither let him eat."

**3:11** For we hear that some among you are walking in a disorderly way, working at nothing, but acting as busybodies.

**3:12** Now such people we command and urge in the Lord Jesus Christ that, working with quietness, they eat their own bread.

**3:13** But as for you, brothers, do not lose heart in doing good.

**3:14** And if anyone does not obey our word through this letter, take note of that person and do not associate with him, so that he may feel ashamed.

**3:15** Yet do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

**3:16** Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you peace continually in every way. The Lord be with you all.

**3:17** The greeting is by my own hand, Paul's, which is a sign in every letter; this is how I write.

**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek phrase tou ponerou can be taken as masculine ("the evil one") or neuter ("evil").
- **3:5** — Both "the love of God" and "the steadfastness of Christ" are ambiguous genitives that may refer to God's own love and Christ's endurance or to the believers' love for God and Christ-like endurance.
- **3:6** — Greek ataktos ("in a disorderly way") denotes acting out of order or shirking duty, here tied to idleness; the critical text reads the third-person plural "they received" rather than the variant "you received."
- **3:11** — The Greek employs a pun contrasting ergazomenous ("working") with periergazomenous ("acting as busybodies" or "meddling").
