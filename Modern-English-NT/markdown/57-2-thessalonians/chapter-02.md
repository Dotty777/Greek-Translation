## Text

**2:1** Now we ask you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to Him,

**2:2** not to be quickly shaken from your reason nor to be alarmed, whether by a spirit, or by a word, or by a letter as if through us, to the effect that the day of the Lord is already present.

**2:3** Let no one deceive you in any way; for it will not come unless the rebellion comes first and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction,

**2:4** who opposes and exalts himself over every so-called god or object of worship, so that he sits in the temple of God, showing himself to be God.

**2:5** Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I used to tell you these things?

**2:6** And now you know what restrains him, so that he will be revealed in his own time.

**2:7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only the one who restrains at present does so until he is out of the way.

**2:8** And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of His mouth and bring to nothing by the appearance of His coming.

**2:9** That one's coming is according to the working of Satan, with all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,

**2:10** and with all deception of unrighteousness for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so as to be saved.

**2:11** And because of this, God sends them a working of delusion, so that they will believe the lie,

**2:12** in order that all may be judged who did not believe the truth, but took pleasure in unrighteousness.

**2:13** But we ought to give thanks to God always concerning you, brothers loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation through sanctification by the Spirit and faith in the truth,

**2:14** to which He called you through our gospel, for the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**2:15** So then, brothers, stand firm and hold fast to the traditions that you were taught, whether by word or by our letter.

**2:16** Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father, who loved us and gave eternal encouragement and good hope in grace,

**2:17** encourage your hearts and establish them in every good work and word.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Greek clause is elliptical, omitting the main verb ("it will not come") before the condition; "rebellion" translates apostasia (also rendered "apostasy").
- **2:6** — Paul refers to the unnamed restrainer with a neuter participle here ("what restrains") and a masculine participle in 2:7 ("the one who restrains"), where the verb in the main clause is also elliptical.
- **2:13** — The critical text reads ap' arches ("from the beginning"); a well-attested manuscript variant reads aparchen ("as firstfruits").
- **2:17** — The optative verbs "encourage" and "establish" are grammatically singular in Greek despite the compound subject of Christ and God the Father in 2:16, highlighting their unified action.
