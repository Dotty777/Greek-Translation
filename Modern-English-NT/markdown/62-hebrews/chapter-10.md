## Text

**10:1** For the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, not the image itself of the realities, can never, by the same sacrifices that they offer continually year by year, perfect those who draw near.

**10:2** Otherwise, would they not have ceased being offered, since the worshipers, once cleansed, would no longer have any consciousness of sins?

**10:3** Instead, in them there is a reminder of sins year by year,

**10:4** for it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to remove sins.

**10:5** Therefore, when entering the world, He says, "Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but a body You prepared for Me;

**10:6** in whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin You were not pleased.

**10:7** Then I said, 'Look, I have come (in the scroll of the book it is written about Me) to do Your will, God.'"

**10:8** Saying above, "Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin You did not desire, nor were You pleased with them" (which are offered according to the law),

**10:9** then He has said, "Look, I have come to do Your will." He removes the first so that He may establish the second.

**10:10** By that will, we have been sanctified through the offering of Jesus Christ's body once for all.

**10:11** And every priest stands day by day ministering and repeatedly offering the same sacrifices, which can never completely remove sins.

**10:12** He, however, after offering a single sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,

**10:13** waiting from that time onward until His enemies are placed as a footstool for His feet.

**10:14** For with one offering He has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.

**10:15** And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after saying,

**10:16** "This is the covenant that I will establish with them after those days," says the Lord: "I will put My laws upon their hearts, and upon their minds I will write them,"

**10:17** and, "Their sins and their lawless deeds I will never again remember."

**10:18** Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

**10:19** Therefore, brothers, having confidence for entrance into the sanctuary by the blood of Jesus,

**10:20** which He inaugurated for us as a new and living way through the curtain, that is, His flesh,

**10:21** and having a great priest over the house of God,

**10:22** let us draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.

**10:23** Let us hold firmly to the confession of our hope unwaveringly, for He who promised is faithful;

**10:24** and let us consider one another in order to spur on love and good works,

**10:25** not abandoning our own gathering together, as is the custom of some, but encouraging one another, and so much the more as you see the day approaching.

**10:26** For if we continue sinning deliberately after having received the knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins remains any longer,

**10:27** but a certain terrifying expectation of judgment and a fury of fire that is about to devour the adversaries.

**10:28** Anyone who has rejected the law of Moses dies without mercy on the basis of two or three witnesses.

**10:29** How much worse a punishment do you suppose he will be counted worthy of who has trampled underfoot the Son of God, and regarded the blood of the covenant by which he was sanctified as common, and insulted the Spirit of grace?

**10:30** For we know the One who said, "Vengeance belongs to Me; I will repay." And again, "The Lord will judge His people."

**10:31** It is terrifying to fall into the hands of the living God.

**10:32** But recall the earlier days, in which, after being enlightened, you endured a great struggle of sufferings,

**10:33** partly being made a spectacle through both reproaches and afflictions, and partly becoming companions of those who were treated this way.

**10:34** For you both sympathized with the prisoners and accepted with joy the plundering of your possessions, knowing that you yourselves have a better and abiding possession.

**10:35** Therefore, do not cast aside your confidence, which holds a great reward;

**10:36** for you need endurance, so that after doing the will of God you may receive the promise.

**10:37** For, "Yet a very little while, the One who is coming will come and will not delay;

**10:38** but My righteous one will live by faith, and if he shrinks back, My soul is not pleased with him."

**10:39** We, however, are not among those who shrink back to destruction, but among those who have faith for the preservation of the soul.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — "A body You prepared for Me" follows the Septuagint of Psalm 40:6 (LXX 39:7), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "ears You have dug [or opened] for Me."
- **10:12** — The Greek phrase "for all time" (εἰς τὸ διηνεκές) stands between "sacrifice" and "sat down" and may modify either—"offered one sacrifice for sins for all time" or "sat down for all time."
- **10:17** — The Greek sentence begun in v. 15 ("for after saying...") is elliptical, leaving the resumed verb of speaking (e.g., "He then adds") unexpressed before this clause.
- **10:34** — The critical Greek text reads "the prisoners" (τοῖς δεσμίοις) and "you yourselves" (ἑαυτούς); some later manuscripts read "my chains" and add "in heaven."
