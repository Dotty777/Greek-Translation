## Text

**13:1** Let brotherly love remain.

**13:2** Do not forget hospitality, for through this some have unknowingly hosted angels.

**13:3** Remember the prisoners, as though bound with them, and those being mistreated, since you yourselves are also in the body.

**13:4** Let marriage be honored among all, and the marriage bed be undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.

**13:5** Let your way of life be free from the love of money, being content with what you have; for He Himself has said, "I will never leave you, nor will I ever abandon you,"

**13:6** so that we say with confidence, "The Lord is my helper, and I will not be afraid. What will man do to me?"

**13:7** Remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; closely observing the outcome of their way of life, imitate their faith.

**13:8** Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today, and for eternity.

**13:9** Do not be carried away by diverse and strange teachings, for it is good for the heart to be strengthened by grace, not by foods, in which those who walk have not been benefited.

**13:10** We have an altar from which those who serve the tabernacle have no authority to eat.

**13:11** For the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought into the holy places by the high priest for sin are burned up outside the camp.

**13:12** For this reason Jesus also, so that He might sanctify the people through His own blood, suffered outside the gate.

**13:13** So then, let us go out to Him outside the camp, bearing His disgrace.

**13:14** For we do not have an enduring city here, but we are seeking the one to come.

**13:15** Through Him, therefore, let us offer up a sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of lips confessing His name.

**13:16** And do not forget doing good and sharing, for God is well pleased with such sacrifices.

**13:17** Obey your leaders and yield to them, for they keep watch over your souls as ones who will give an account, so that they may do this with joy and not with groaning, for that would be unprofitable for you.

**13:18** Pray for us, for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves honorably in all things.

**13:19** And I urge you even more to do this, so that I may be restored to you sooner.

**13:20** Now may the God of peace, who brought up from the dead the great Shepherd of the sheep by the blood of an eternal covenant, our Lord Jesus,

**13:21** equip you in every good thing for doing His will, working in us what is well-pleasing before Him through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**13:22** Now I urge you, brothers, bear with the word of exhortation, for indeed I have written to you in few words.

**13:23** Know that our brother Timothy has been released, with whom I will see you if he comes soon.

**13:24** Greet all your leaders and all the saints; those from Italy send you greetings.

**13:25** May grace be with all of you.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — The opening clauses are verbless in Greek and may be read either as an exhortation ("Let marriage be honored...") or as an indicative statement ("Marriage is honorable..."); similarly, "among all" (en pasin) can also be rendered "in every respect."
- **13:10** — The meaning of "altar" here is debated, variously understood as Christ's sacrifice on the cross, Christ Himself, or the heavenly sanctuary, contrasted with the Levitical tabernacle service.
- **13:21** — Some manuscripts read "working in you" (hymin) instead of "working in us" (hemin), and some omit "and ever" (ton aionon); the translation follows the critical text.
- **13:24** — The phrase "those from Italy" (hoi apo tes Italias) may refer either to believers in Italy sending greetings outward or to Italian believers currently with the author sending greetings back to Italy.
