## Text

**3:1** Therefore, if you were raised together with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God.

**3:2** Keep your minds on the things above, not on the things on the earth.

**3:3** For you died, and your life has been hidden with Christ in God.

**3:4** When Christ, your life, is manifested, then you also will be manifested with Him in glory.

**3:5** Therefore, put to death the members that are on the earth: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed, which is idolatry,

**3:6** on account of which the wrath of God is coming,

**3:7** in which you also once walked when you were living in these things.

**3:8** But now you also put away all these things: wrath, fury, malice, slander, and obscene speech from your mouth.

**3:9** Do not lie to one another, since you have stripped off the old man together with his practices,

**3:10** and have put on the new man, who is being renewed to full knowledge according to the image of the One who created him,

**3:11** where there is not Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, slave, free; rather, Christ is all and in all.

**3:12** Therefore, as chosen ones of God, holy and beloved, put on hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience,

**3:13** bearing with one another and forgiving each other if anyone has a complaint against another; just as the Lord also forgave you, so you also must do.

**3:14** And over all these things put on love, which is the bond of perfection.

**3:15** And let the peace of Christ govern in your hearts, to which you were also called in one body; and be grateful.

**3:16** Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs in grace, singing in your hearts to God.

**3:17** And whatever you do, in word or in deed, do all things in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through Him.

**3:18** Wives, submit yourselves to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

**3:19** Husbands, love your wives and do not become bitter toward them.

**3:20** Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing in the Lord.

**3:21** Fathers, do not provoke your children, so that they may not lose heart.

**3:22** Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh in all things, not in acts of eye-service as people-pleasers, but in singleness of heart, fearing the Lord.

**3:23** Whatever you do, work from the soul, as for the Lord and not for people,

**3:24** knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance. You serve the Lord Christ.

**3:25** For the one who does wrong will receive back the wrong he did, and there is no partiality.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — Many later manuscripts add "upon the sons of disobedience" (likely influenced by Ephesians 5:6); the earlier critical text omits the phrase.
- **3:15** — The verb rendered "govern" literally means "act as umpire" or "arbitrate."
- **3:16** — Punctuation is debated: "in all wisdom" may modify either "dwell" (as punctuated here) or "teaching and admonishing," and "in grace" may also mean "with gratitude."
- **3:24** — The Greek verb form can be read either as an indicative ("You serve the Lord Christ") or as an imperative ("Serve the Lord Christ").
