## Text

**2:1** For I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you and those in Laodicea, and all who have not seen my face in the flesh,

**2:2** so that their hearts may be encouraged, knit together in love and into all the wealth of the full assurance of understanding, into the full knowledge of the mystery of God, Christ,

**2:3** in whom all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.

**2:4** I say this so that no one may delude you with persuasive speech.

**2:5** For even though I am absent in the flesh, yet in the spirit I am with you, rejoicing and seeing your good order and the firmness of your faith in Christ.

**2:6** Therefore, as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in Him,

**2:7** having been rooted and being built up in Him and being established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.

**2:8** Watch out that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the elementary principles of the world, and not according to Christ.

**2:9** For in Him all the fullness of deity dwells bodily,

**2:10** and you have been brought to fullness in Him, who is the head of every ruler and authority,

**2:11** in whom you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands, in the stripping off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,

**2:12** having been buried with Him in baptism, in which you were also raised with Him through faith in the working of God, who raised Him from the dead.

**2:13** And you, who were dead in trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh—He made you alive together with Him, having forgiven us all our trespasses,

**2:14** having wiped out the handwritten record of debt against us with its decrees, which was hostile to us; and He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross.

**2:15** Having disarmed the rulers and the authorities, He made an open display of them, triumphing over them in Him.

**2:16** Therefore let no one judge you in food and in drink, or with regard to a festival or a new moon or Sabbaths,

**2:17** which are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ.

**2:18** Let no one disqualify you, delighting in humility and the worship of angels, entering into the things he has seen, puffed up without reason by the mind of his flesh,

**2:19** and not holding fast to the Head, from whom the whole body, being supplied and knit together through the joints and ligaments, grows with a growth from God.

**2:20** If you died with Christ to the elementary principles of the world, why, as though living in the world, do you submit to decrees,

**2:21** "Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch"

**2:22** —all of which are destined for decay through use—according to human commands and teachings?

**2:23** Such things indeed have an appearance of wisdom in self-imposed worship and humility and harsh treatment of the body, yet they are of no value against the gratification of the flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **2:8** — The Greek stoicheia ("elementary principles") can refer either to foundational teachings and religious rudiments or to elemental spiritual powers governing the cosmos (likewise in 2:20).
- **2:14** — The Greek cheirographon refers to a handwritten certificate of debt, used metaphorically for the binding record of obligation standing against humanity.
- **2:15** — The final phrase "in Him" (referring to Christ) can also be rendered "by it" (referring to the cross in v. 14).
- **2:18** — The phrase "entering into the things he has seen" (ha heoraken embateuon) is difficult and may refer to initiation into visionary experiences or delving into claimed visions; some later manuscripts insert a negative ("things he has not seen").
- **2:23** — The final clause is grammatically ambiguous and may mean either that ascetic practices have no value in restraining fleshly gratification or that they have no true worth and serve only to gratify the flesh.
