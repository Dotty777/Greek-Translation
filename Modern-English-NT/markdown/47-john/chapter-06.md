## Text

**6:1** After these things Jesus departed to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, that of Tiberias.

**6:2** And a large crowd kept following Him, because they were observing the signs that He was performing on those who were sick.

**6:3** And Jesus went up onto the mountain, and He was sitting there with His disciples.

**6:4** Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was near.

**6:5** So Jesus, lifting up His eyes and seeing that a large crowd was coming toward Him, said to Philip, "Where should we buy bread so that these people may eat?"

**6:6** Now He was saying this, testing him, for He Himself knew what He was about to do.

**6:7** Philip answered Him, "Two hundred denarii worth of bread is not enough for them, so that each one may receive a small amount."

**6:8** One of His disciples, Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, said to Him,

**6:9** "There is a young boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these among so many?"

**6:10** Jesus said, "Have the people recline." Now there was abundant grass in that place. So the men reclined, about five thousand in number.

**6:11** Jesus therefore took the loaves, and after giving thanks, He distributed them to those who were reclining; likewise also from the fish, as much as they wanted.

**6:12** And when they were filled, He said to His disciples, "Gather up the broken pieces that are left over, so that nothing may be lost."

**6:13** So they gathered them up and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves that were left over by those who had eaten.

**6:14** So when the people saw the sign that He had performed, they kept saying, "This is truly the Prophet who is coming into the world."

**6:15** Jesus, therefore, knowing that they were about to come and seize Him to make Him king, withdrew again to the mountain Himself alone.

**6:16** And when evening arrived, His disciples went down to the sea,

**6:17** and after boarding a boat, they were going across the sea to Capernaum. Darkness had already come, and Jesus had not yet come to them,

**6:18** and the sea was being stirred up as a strong wind was blowing.

**6:19** Then, when they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea and coming near to the boat, and they became afraid.

**6:20** But He told them, "It is I; do not be afraid."

**6:21** So they wanted to take Him into the boat, and immediately the boat arrived at the land to which they were going.

**6:22** On the next day, the crowd that stood on the other side of the sea saw that no other small boat was there except one, and that Jesus had not entered the boat with His disciples, but His disciples had departed alone.

**6:23** Other small boats came from Tiberias near to the place where they had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks.

**6:24** Therefore, when the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, nor were His disciples, they themselves boarded the small boats and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.

**6:25** And having found Him on the other side of the sea, they said to Him, "Rabbi, when did You arrive here?"

**6:26** Jesus answered them and said, "Truly, truly, I tell you, you are seeking Me not because you saw signs, but because you ate from the loaves and were satisfied.

**6:27** Do not work for the food that perishes, but for the food that endures to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you; for God the Father has placed His seal on Him."

**6:28** So they said to Him, "What must we do so that we may work the works of God?"

**6:29** Jesus answered and said to them, "This is the work of God: that you believe in Him whom He sent."

**6:30** Then they said to Him, "What sign, therefore, are You performing, so that we may see and believe You? What work are You doing?

**6:31** Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it stands written, 'He gave them bread from heaven to eat.'"

**6:32** Therefore Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, it is not Moses who has given you the bread from heaven, but My Father is giving you the true bread from heaven.

**6:33** For the bread of God is that which descends from heaven and gives life to the world."

**6:34** So they said to Him, "Lord, give us this bread always."

**6:35** Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life. The one who comes to Me will certainly not hunger, and the one who believes in Me will never thirst at any time.

**6:36** But I told you that you have indeed seen Me, and yet you do not believe.

**6:37** Everything that the Father gives Me will come to Me, and the one who comes to Me I will by no means cast out;

**6:38** for I have descended from heaven not to do My own will, but the will of Him who sent Me.

**6:39** And this is the will of Him who sent Me: that of everything He has given Me, I should lose nothing, but should raise it up on the last day.

**6:40** For this is the will of My Father: that everyone who sees the Son and believes in Him should have eternal life, and I Myself will raise him up on the last day."

**6:41** The Jews, therefore, were grumbling about Him because He said, "I am the bread that descended from heaven,"

**6:42** and they were saying, "Is this not Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How does He now say, 'I have descended from heaven'?"

**6:43** Jesus answered and said to them, "Stop grumbling with one another.

**6:44** No one is able to come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him, and I Myself will raise him up on the last day.

**6:45** It stands written in the Prophets, 'And all of them will be taught by God.' Everyone who has heard from the Father and learned comes to Me.

**6:46** Not that anyone has seen the Father except the One who is from God; this One has seen the Father.

**6:47** Truly, truly, I tell you, the one who believes has eternal life.

**6:48** I am the bread of life.

**6:49** Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and died.

**6:50** This is the bread descending from heaven, so that a person may eat from it and not die.

**6:51** I am the living bread that descended from heaven. If anyone eats from this bread, he will live forever; and indeed the bread that I will give is My flesh, for the life of the world."

**6:52** So the Jews were quarreling with one another, saying, "How is this man able to give us the flesh to eat?"

**6:53** Therefore Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you do not have life in yourselves.

**6:54** The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I Myself will raise him up on the last day.

**6:55** For My flesh is true food, and My blood is true drink.

**6:56** The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood remains in Me, and I in him.

**6:57** Just as the living Father sent Me, and I live because of the Father, so the one who eats Me will also live because of Me.

**6:58** This is the bread that descended from heaven—not as the fathers ate and died; the one who eats this bread will live forever."

**6:59** He said these things while teaching in a synagogue in Capernaum.

**6:60** Therefore, when many of His disciples heard this, they said, "This saying is hard; who is able to listen to it?"

**6:61** But Jesus, knowing within Himself that His disciples were grumbling about this, said to them, "Does this cause you to stumble?

**6:62** What if, then, you should see the Son of Man ascending where He was before?

**6:63** The Spirit is the One who gives life; the flesh is of no benefit at all. The words that I have spoken to you are spirit and are life.

**6:64** Yet there are some among you who do not believe." For from the beginning Jesus knew who the ones were who did not believe, and who it was that would betray Him.

**6:65** And He was saying, "For this reason I have told you that no one is able to come to Me unless it has been given to him from the Father."

**6:66** Because of this, many of His disciples went back and were no longer walking with Him.

**6:67** Therefore Jesus said to the Twelve, "You do not also want to leave, do you?"

**6:68** Simon Peter answered Him, "Lord, to whom will we go? You have words of eternal life,

**6:69** and we ourselves have believed and come to know that You are the Holy One of God."

**6:70** Jesus answered them, "Did I Myself not choose you, the Twelve? Yet one from among you is a devil."

**6:71** Now He was speaking of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, for he was about to betray Him—one of the Twelve.

## Translator Notes

- **6:19** — A stadion was a Greek unit of length of about 600 feet (185 meters), so twenty-five to thirty stadia was roughly 3 to 3.5 miles (4.5 to 5.5 kilometers).
- **6:20** — Greek ego eimi, literally 'I am,' functions idiomatically here as self-identification ('It is I').
- **6:33** — The masculine participle ho katabainon agrees grammatically with masculine artos ('bread') and may be rendered either 'that which descends' or 'He who descends,' anticipating Jesus' self-identification in verse 35.
- **6:34** — Greek Kyrie can mean either 'Lord' or respectful 'Sir.'
- **6:54** — Greek trogo (vv. 54, 56-58) literally denotes chewing or eating and functions as the present-tense counterpart to aorist phago ('eat') in vv. 49-53.
- **6:62** — The Greek is an elliptical conditional question ('What if, then...'), leaving the implied result unstated for the listeners to weigh.
