## Text

**13:1** Now before the feast of the Passover, knowing that His hour had come that He should depart from this world to the Father, Jesus, having loved His own who were in the world, loved them to the end.

**13:2** And while supper was underway, the devil having already put into the heart that Judas Iscariot, son of Simon, should betray Him,

**13:3** knowing that the Father had given all things into His hands, and that He had come out from God and was going to God,

**13:4** He got up from supper and laid aside His outer garments, and taking a towel, wrapped it around Himself.

**13:5** Then He poured water into the basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around Him.

**13:6** So He came to Simon Peter, and he said to Him, "Lord, are You washing my feet?"

**13:7** Jesus answered and said to him, "What I am doing you do not know now, but you will understand after these things."

**13:8** Peter said to Him, "You will never wash my feet!" Jesus answered him, "If I do not wash you, you have no share with Me."

**13:9** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!"

**13:10** Jesus said to him, "The one who has bathed has no need except to wash his feet, but is completely clean; and you are clean, yet not all of you."

**13:11** For He knew the one who was betraying Him; because of this He said, "Not all of you are clean."

**13:12** So when He had washed their feet and taken His outer garments and reclined at table, He said to them again, "Do you know what I have done for you?

**13:13** You call Me 'Teacher' and 'Lord,' and you speak rightly, for I am.

**13:14** If then I, the Lord and the Teacher, washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.

**13:15** For I have given you an example, so that just as I did for you, you also should do.

**13:16** Truly, truly, I say to you, a slave is not greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him.

**13:17** If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.

**13:18** I am not speaking about all of you; I know whom I have chosen—but so that the Scripture may be fulfilled: 'The one who eats bread with Me has lifted up his heel against Me.'

**13:19** From now on I am telling you before it happens, so that when it happens you may believe that I am.

**13:20** Truly, truly, I say to you, whoever receives anyone I send receives Me, and the one who receives Me receives the One who sent Me."

**13:21** Having said these things, Jesus was troubled in spirit, and He testified and said, "Truly, truly, I say to you that one of you will betray Me."

**13:22** The disciples were looking at one another, perplexed as to whom He was speaking about.

**13:23** Now one of His disciples, whom Jesus loved, was reclining close to Jesus's chest.

**13:24** So Simon Peter signaled to him to ask who it might be about whom He was speaking.

**13:25** Leaning back like that against Jesus's chest, he said to Him, "Lord, who is it?"

**13:26** Jesus answered, "It is the one to whom I will give the morsel once I dip it." So after dipping the morsel, He gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.

**13:27** And after the morsel, Satan then entered into him. So Jesus said to him, "What you are doing, do quickly."

**13:28** Now no one of those reclining at table understood why He said this to him.

**13:29** For some were supposing, since Judas kept the money box, that Jesus was saying to him, "Buy what we need for the feast," or that he should give something to the poor.

**13:30** So after taking the morsel, he went out immediately. And it was night.

**13:31** Therefore, when he had gone out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in Him;

**13:32** and God will glorify Him in Himself, and will glorify Him at once.

**13:33** Little children, I am with you a little while longer. You will seek Me, and just as I said to the Jews, 'Where I am going you cannot come,' I now tell you as well.

**13:34** I am giving you a new commandment, that you love one another; just as I loved you, that you also should love one another.

**13:35** By this everyone will know that you are My disciples, if you have love among one another."

**13:36** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, where are You going?" Jesus answered him, "Where I am going you cannot follow Me now, but you will follow later."

**13:37** Peter said to Him, "Lord, why can I not follow You right now? I will lay down my life for You."

**13:38** Jesus answered, "Will you lay down your life for Me? Truly, truly, I say to you, a rooster will not crow until you have denied Me three times."

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — Greek eis telos can mean either "to the end" (temporally, through His death) or "to the utmost," and both nuances may be intended here.
- **13:2** — Greek literally reads "having already put into the heart" without a possessive pronoun; it is usually understood as Judas's heart, though some take it as the devil's own purpose.
- **13:10** — Some early manuscripts omit "except to wash his feet" (ei me tous podas), reading simply "has no need to wash."
- **13:19** — Greek ego eimi literally reads "I am," though it can also be understood as "I am He."
- **13:32** — Some manuscripts include "If God has been glorified in Him" at the start of this verse, which is omitted in the earliest witnesses.
