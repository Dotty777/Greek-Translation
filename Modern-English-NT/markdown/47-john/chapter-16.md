## Text

**16:1** "These things I have spoken to you so that you may not be made to stumble.

**16:2** They will put you out of the synagogue; indeed, an hour is coming when everyone who kills you will think he is offering sacred service to God.

**16:3** And these things they will do because they have not known the Father nor Me.

**16:4** Yet these things I have spoken to you so that when their hour comes, you may remember them, that I told you. These things, however, I did not tell you from the beginning, because I was with you.

**16:5** Now, however, I am going to the One who sent Me, and none of you asks Me, 'Where are You going?'

**16:6** Instead, because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

**16:7** Yet I tell you the truth: it is to your advantage that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Advocate will not come to you; if, however, I depart, I will send Him to you.

**16:8** And when He comes, He will convict the world concerning sin, and concerning righteousness, and concerning judgment:

**16:9** concerning sin, because they do not believe in Me;

**16:10** concerning righteousness, because I am going to the Father and you see Me no longer;

**16:11** and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.

**16:12** I still have many things to say to you, yet you cannot bear them now.

**16:13** When He comes, however, the Spirit of truth, He will guide you into all the truth; for He will not speak on His own, rather whatever He will hear He will speak, and He will declare to you the things that are coming.

**16:14** He will glorify Me, because He will take from what is Mine and declare it to you.

**16:15** Everything that the Father has is Mine; for this reason I said that He takes from what is Mine and will declare it to you.

**16:16** A little while, and you no longer see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me."

**16:17** So some of His disciples said to one another, "What is this that He is telling us, 'A little while, and you do not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me'; and, 'Because I am going to the Father'?"

**16:18** So they were saying, "What is this 'little while' that He says? We do not know what He is speaking about."

**16:19** Jesus knew that they wanted to question Him, and He said to them, "Are you inquiring with one another about this, that I said, 'A little while, and you do not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me'?

**16:20** Truly, truly, I say to you that you will weep and lament, while the world will rejoice; you will be sorrowful, yet your sorrow will turn into joy.

**16:21** When a woman is giving birth she has sorrow, because her hour has come; when, however, she has delivered the child, she no longer remembers the distress, because of the joy that a human being has been born into the world.

**16:22** So you also have sorrow now; yet I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and no one takes your joy away from you.

**16:23** And on that day you will not question Me about anything. Truly, truly, I say to you, whatever you ask the Father, He will give to you in My name.

**16:24** Until now you have asked for nothing in My name; ask and you will receive, so that your joy may be made full.

**16:25** These things I have spoken to you in figures of speech; an hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you in figures of speech, rather I will tell you plainly about the Father.

**16:26** On that day you will ask in My name, and I am not saying to you that I will ask the Father on your behalf;

**16:27** for the Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and have believed that I came forth from God.

**16:28** I came forth from the Father and have come into the world; again, I am leaving the world and going to the Father."

**16:29** His disciples say, "Look, now You are speaking plainly and are saying no figure of speech.

**16:30** Now we know that You know all things and have no need for anyone to question You; by this we believe that You came forth from God."

**16:31** Jesus answered them, "Do you now believe?

**16:32** Look, an hour is coming, and has come, when you will be scattered, each one to his own home, and will leave Me alone; and yet I am not alone, because the Father is with Me.

**16:33** These things I have spoken to you so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you have distress, yet take courage: I have conquered the world."

## Translator Notes

- **16:8** — Greek elegxei ("will convict") carries a forensic range of meaning that includes exposing guilt, proving wrong, and convicting.
- **16:13** — The Greek text reads the future tense akousei ("will hear"), highlighting the Spirit's ongoing reception and declaration of what He receives.
- **16:23** — In this Greek text, "in My name" follows "He will give to you," connecting the phrase directly to the Father's giving rather than only to the disciples' asking.
- **16:27** — The Greek text reads "from God" rather than "from the Father," matching the disciples' confession in verse 30.
