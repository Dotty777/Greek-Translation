## Text

**8:1** ⟦But Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.⟧

**8:2** ⟦And at dawn He came again into the temple, and all the people were coming; and having sat down, He began teaching them.⟧

**8:3** ⟦Now the scribes and the Pharisees bring to Him a woman caught in adultery; and having set her in the middle,⟧

**8:4** ⟦they say to Him, testing Him, "Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of committing adultery.⟧

**8:5** ⟦Now in the law Moses commanded us to stone such women; what then do You say?"⟧

**8:6** ⟦Now they were saying this to test Him, so that they might have grounds to accuse Him. But Jesus, bending down, was writing with His finger on the ground, acting as though He did not notice.⟧

**8:7** ⟦Yet as they persisted in questioning Him, He straightened up and said to them, "Let the one among you who is without sin throw the stone at her first."⟧

**8:8** ⟦And bending down again, He was writing on the ground.⟧

**8:9** ⟦Now they, when they heard it and were convicted by their conscience, began going out one by one, starting from the elders; and Jesus alone was left, and the woman who was in the middle.⟧

**8:10** ⟦Then Jesus straightened up and, seeing no one except the woman, said to her, "Where are those accusers of yours? Did no one condemn you?"⟧

**8:11** ⟦And she said, "No one, Lord." And Jesus said, "Neither do I judge you; go, and sin no more."⟧

**8:12** So Jesus spoke to them again, saying, "I am the light of the world. The one who follows Me will never walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life."

**8:13** Therefore the Pharisees said to Him, "You testify concerning Yourself; Your testimony is not true."

**8:14** Jesus replied and said to them, "Even if I testify concerning Myself, My testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going; yet you do not know where I come from or where I am going.

**8:15** You judge according to the flesh; I do not judge anyone.

**8:16** And even if I do judge, My judgment is genuine, because I am not alone, but it is I and the Father who sent Me.

**8:17** And in your own law also it is written that the testimony of two people is true.

**8:18** I am the One testifying concerning Myself, and the Father who sent Me testifies concerning Me."

**8:19** Therefore they were saying to Him, "Where is Your Father?" Jesus replied, "You know neither Me nor My Father; if you knew Me, you would know My Father as well."

**8:20** He spoke these words in the treasury while teaching in the temple, and no one seized Him, because His hour had not yet come.

**8:21** So He said to them again, "I am going away, and you will seek Me, and you will die in your sin. Where I am going, you are not able to come."

**8:22** Therefore the Jews were saying, "He will not kill Himself, will He, since He says, 'Where I am going, you are not able to come'?"

**8:23** And He was saying to them, "You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.

**8:24** Therefore I told you that you will die in your sins; for unless you believe that I am, you will die in your sins."

**8:25** Therefore they were saying to Him, "Who are You?" Jesus said to them, "Why am I even speaking to you at all?

**8:26** I have many things to speak and to judge concerning you, yet the One who sent Me is true, and the things I heard from Him, these I speak to the world."

**8:27** They did not realize that He was telling them about the Father.

**8:28** So Jesus said to them, "When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and I do nothing on My own, but just as the Father taught Me, these things I speak.

**8:29** And the One who sent Me is with Me. He has not left Me alone, because I do the things pleasing to Him at all times."

**8:30** While He was speaking these things, many believed in Him.

**8:31** Therefore Jesus was saying to the Jews who had believed Him, "If you remain in My word, you are truly disciples of Mine,

**8:32** and you will know the truth, and the truth will free you."

**8:33** They answered Him, "We are offspring of Abraham and have never been enslaved to anyone. How do You say, 'You will become free'?"

**8:34** Jesus replied to them, "Truly, truly, I say to you that everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin.

**8:35** Now the slave does not remain in the house forever; the son remains forever.

**8:36** Therefore, if the Son frees you, you will really be free.

**8:37** I know that you are offspring of Abraham; yet you seek to kill Me, because My word has no room in you.

**8:38** I speak the things that I have seen with the Father; and so you also do the things that you heard from the father."

**8:39** They answered and said to Him, "Abraham is our father." Jesus said to them, "If you are children of Abraham, you would be doing the works of Abraham;

**8:40** but now you seek to kill Me, a man who has spoken to you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.

**8:41** You are doing the works of your father." They said to Him, "We were not born out of sexual immorality; we have one Father, God."

**8:42** Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love Me, for I came forth from God and am here; for I have not even come on My own, but He sent Me.

**8:43** Why do you not understand My speech? Because you are not able to hear My word.

**8:44** You are from your father the devil, and you want to do the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning and did not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. Whenever he speaks a lie, he speaks from what is his own, because he is a liar and the father of it.

**8:45** Yet as for Me, because I tell the truth, you do not believe Me.

**8:46** Who among you convicts Me of sin? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe Me?

**8:47** The one who is from God hears the words of God; for this reason you do not hear, because you are not from God."

**8:48** The Jews replied and said to Him, "Do we not rightly say that You are a Samaritan and have a demon?"

**8:49** Jesus replied, "I do not have a demon; rather, I honor My Father, yet you dishonor Me.

**8:50** I, however, do not seek My glory; there is One who seeks and judges.

**8:51** Truly, truly, I say to you, if anyone keeps My word, he will never see death."

**8:52** The Jews said to Him, "Now we know that You have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets as well, yet You say, 'If anyone keeps My word, he will never taste death.'

**8:53** You are not greater than our father Abraham, who died, are You? And the prophets died too. Who do You make Yourself out to be?"

**8:54** Jesus replied, "If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing; it is My Father who glorifies Me, of whom you say, 'He is our God,'

**8:55** and you have not known Him, yet I know Him. And if I say that I do not know Him, I will be a liar like you; but I do know Him, and I keep His word.

**8:56** Abraham your father exulted that he would see My day, and he saw it and was glad."

**8:57** Therefore the Jews said to Him, "You are not yet fifty years old, and You have seen Abraham?"

**8:58** Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I say to you, before Abraham came into being, I am."

**8:59** So they picked up stones to throw at Him, yet Jesus concealed Himself and went out from the temple.

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — The Saints Greek Critical Text includes the participial phrase μὴ προσποιούμενος ("not taking notice / acting as though He did not notice"), which is translated here.
- **8:7** — τὸν λίθον carries the definite article ("the stone"), referring to the first stone in a stoning execution, and πρῶτον is adverbial ("first").
- **8:9** — The Saints Greek Critical Text includes καὶ ὑπὸ τῆς συνειδήσεως ἐλεγχόμενοι ("and being convicted by their conscience"), which appears in the Byzantine textual tradition.
- **8:11** — While 8:10 uses κατέκρινεν ("condemn"), the Saints Greek Critical Text here reads the simple verb κρίνω ("judge"), preserved distinctly as "judge."
- **8:24** — The Greek phrase "egō eimi" ("I am") lacks a predicate here (as also in 8:28 and 8:58).
- **8:25** — The Greek construction "Tēn archēn ho ti kai lalō hymin;" is famously difficult; punctuated as a question in the critical text it means "Why am I even speaking to you at all?" though it is alternatively read as a statement, "Just what I have been telling you from the beginning."
- **8:38** — The critical text reads "from the father" without a possessive pronoun, leaving the identity of their father ambiguous until Jesus explicitly names the devil in 8:44.
- **8:44** — The final clause "the father of it" ("ho patēr autou") most naturally refers back to "a lie" (falsehood), though some take it to mean "the father of the liar."
- **8:58** — The aorist infinitive "genesthai" ("came into being") used of Abraham contrasts with the present "egō eimi" ("I am").
