## Text

**9:1** And passing along, He saw a man blind from birth.

**9:2** And His disciples questioned Him, saying, "Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, so that he would be born blind?"

**9:3** Jesus answered, "Neither this man nor his parents sinned, but it was so that the works of God would be revealed in him.

**9:4** It is necessary for us to work the works of the One who sent Me while it is day; night is coming when no one is able to work.

**9:5** For as long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world."

**9:6** Having said these things, He spat on the ground, made mud from the saliva, and spread the mud on his eyes,

**9:7** and said to him, "Go, wash in the pool of Siloam" (which is translated "Sent"). So he went away, washed, and returned seeing.

**9:8** Therefore the neighbors and those who had previously seen him as a beggar were saying, "Is this not the one who used to sit and beg?"

**9:9** Some were saying, "This is he." Others were saying, "No, but he is like him." He himself was saying, "I am he."

**9:10** So they were saying to him, "How were your eyes opened?"

**9:11** He answered, "The man who is called Jesus made mud and spread it on my eyes, and said to me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' So I went away and washed, and I received sight."

**9:12** And they said to him, "Where is He?" He said, "I do not know."

**9:13** They brought him to the Pharisees, the one who was formerly blind.

**9:14** Now it was a Sabbath on the day when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.

**9:15** So again the Pharisees also were questioning him as to how he received sight. And he said to them, "He put mud on my eyes, and I washed, and I see."

**9:16** Therefore some of the Pharisees were saying, "This man is not from God, because He does not keep the Sabbath." Others were saying, "How is a sinful man able to perform such signs?" And there was a division among them.

**9:17** Therefore they said again to the blind man, "What do you yourself say concerning Him, seeing that He opened your eyes?" And he replied, "He is a prophet."

**9:18** Therefore the Jews did not believe concerning him that he had been blind and had received sight, until they called the parents of him who had received sight

**9:19** and questioned them, saying, "Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?"

**9:20** His parents therefore answered and said, "We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;

**9:21** but how he now sees we do not know, or who opened his eyes we ourselves do not know. Ask him; he is of age; he himself will speak for himself."

**9:22** His parents said these things because they were afraid of the Jews; for the Jews had already agreed that if anyone confessed Him as Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.

**9:23** For this reason his parents said, "He is of age; question him."

**9:24** So a second time they called the man who had been blind and said to him, "Give glory to God; we know that this man is a sinner."

**9:25** He therefore answered, "Whether He is a sinner, I do not know; one thing I know, that although I was blind, I now see."

**9:26** So they said to him, "What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?"

**9:27** He answered them, "I already told you, and you did not listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You do not also want to become His disciples, do you?"

**9:28** And they reviled him and said, "You are a disciple of that man, but we are disciples of Moses.

**9:29** We know that God has spoken to Moses, but as for this man, we do not know where He is from."

**9:30** The man answered and said to them, "Indeed, this is the astonishing thing: you do not know where He is from, and yet He opened my eyes.

**9:31** We know that God does not listen to sinners, but if anyone is God-fearing and does His will, He listens to that one.

**9:32** From the beginning of time it has never been heard that anyone opened the eyes of a person born blind.

**9:33** If this man were not from God, He would be unable to do anything."

**9:34** They answered and said to him, "You were born completely in sins, and are you teaching us?" And they threw him out.

**9:35** Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and having found him, He said, "Do you believe in the Son of Man?"

**9:36** He answered and said, "And who is He, sir, so that I may believe in Him?"

**9:37** Jesus said to him, "You have both seen Him, and the One who is speaking with you is He."

**9:38** And he said, "I believe, Lord." And he worshiped Him.

**9:39** And Jesus said, "For judgment I Myself came into this world, so that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind."

**9:40** Those of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these things and said to Him, "We are not also blind, are we?"

**9:41** Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would not have sin; but as it is, you say, 'We see.' Your sin remains."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "We must... the One who sent Me" (translated here), whereas later manuscripts harmonize the opening pronoun to "I must."
- **9:24** — "Give glory to God" is an Old Testament formula of solemn adjuration calling on someone to speak the truth before God (cf. Joshua 7:19).
- **9:35** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "the Son of Man," while many later manuscripts read "the Son of God."
- **9:36** — The Greek vocative kyrie can mean either "sir" (as a respectful address prior to recognition) or "Lord" (as in v. 38, where the man worships Him).
