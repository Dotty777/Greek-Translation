## Text

**2:1** And on the third day a wedding took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.

**2:2** Now Jesus and His disciples were also invited to the wedding.

**2:3** And when the wine ran out, the mother of Jesus said to Him, "They have no wine."

**2:4** And Jesus said to her, "What is that to Me and to you, woman? My hour has not yet arrived."

**2:5** His mother said to the servants, "Whatever He tells you, do it."

**2:6** Now six stone water jars were set there according to the purification of the Jews, each holding two or three measures.

**2:7** Jesus said to them, "Fill the water jars with water." And they filled them to the top.

**2:8** And He said to them, "Draw some out now and bring it to the banquet steward." And they brought it.

**2:9** When the banquet steward tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it came from—though the servants who had drawn the water knew—the banquet steward called the bridegroom,

**2:10** and said to him, "Every person serves the good wine first, and when they have become drunk, the lesser wine; you have kept the good wine until now."

**2:11** Jesus performed this as the beginning of the signs in Cana of Galilee and revealed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.

**2:12** After this He went down to Capernaum, He and His mother and His brothers and His disciples; and they stayed there not many days.

**2:13** And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

**2:14** And He found in the temple those who were selling oxen and sheep and doves, and the moneychangers sitting there,

**2:15** and having made a whip of cords, He drove all of them out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen, and He poured out the coins of the moneychangers and overturned the tables.

**2:16** And to those who were selling the doves He said, "Take these things away from here; stop making My Father's house a house of trade."

**2:17** His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for Your house will consume Me."

**2:18** Therefore the Jews answered and said to Him, "What sign do You show us, seeing that You do these things?"

**2:19** Jesus answered and said to them, "Destroy this sanctuary, and in three days I will raise it up."

**2:20** Therefore the Jews said, "This sanctuary was built over forty-six years, and will You raise it up in three days?"

**2:21** But He was speaking about the sanctuary of His body.

**2:22** So when He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had been saying this; and they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.

**2:23** Now while He was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in His name, observing His signs that He was performing.

**2:24** Yet Jesus Himself was not entrusting Himself to them, because He knew all people,

**2:25** and because He had no need for anyone to testify about man, for He Himself knew what was in man.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The Greek phrase literally asks "What to Me and to you?", a Semitic idiom distancing the speaker from another's concern or request. The vocative γύναι ("woman") was a normal, polite form of address.
- **2:6** — A Greek metretes ("measure") was a liquid unit of roughly 9 to 10 gallons (about 35 to 40 liters), meaning each stone jar held approximately 20 to 30 gallons.
- **2:19** — John uses naos ("sanctuary," referring to the inner temple building) here and in vv. 20-21, distinguishing it from hieron ("temple," the entire temple precinct) in vv. 14-15.
- **2:24** — John plays on the verb pisteuo: while many "believed" in Jesus' name (v. 23), Jesus was not "entrusting" Himself to them (v. 24).
