## Text

**4:1** So when the Lord learned that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John—

**4:2** though Jesus Himself was not baptizing, but His disciples were—

**4:3** He left Judea and went away again into Galilee.

**4:4** Now it was necessary for Him to pass through Samaria.

**4:5** So He came to a city of Samaria named Sychar, near the plot of land that Jacob gave to Joseph his son;

**4:6** and Jacob's spring was there. Therefore Jesus, exhausted from the journey, was sitting just as He was beside the spring; it was about the sixth hour.

**4:7** A woman from Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give Me a drink."

**4:8** For His disciples had gone away into the city in order to buy food.

**4:9** Then the Samaritan woman said to Him, "How is it that You, being a Jew, ask me for a drink, when I am a Samaritan woman?" For Jews do not have dealings with Samaritans.

**4:10** Jesus answered and said to her, "If you knew the gift of God and who the One is who is saying to you, 'Give Me a drink,' you yourself would have asked Him, and He would have given living water to you."

**4:11** The woman said to Him, "Sir, You have no bucket, and the well is deep; where then do You get this living water?

**4:12** You are not greater than our father Jacob, are You, who gave us the well and drank from it himself, along with his sons and his livestock?"

**4:13** Jesus answered and said to her, "Everyone who drinks from this water will thirst again;

**4:14** but whoever drinks from the water that I will give him will certainly never thirst; rather, the water that I will give him will become in him a spring of water leaping up into eternal life."

**4:15** The woman said to Him, "Sir, give me this water, so that I will not be thirsty nor keep coming all the way here to draw."

**4:16** He said to her, "Go, summon your husband, and come here."

**4:17** The woman answered and said to Him, "I do not have a husband." Jesus said to her, "You have rightly said, 'A husband I do not have';

**4:18** for you have had five husbands, and the one whom you now have is not your husband; this you have spoken truthfully."

**4:19** The woman said to Him, "Sir, I see that You are a prophet.

**4:20** Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, and you say that in Jerusalem is the place where one must worship."

**4:21** Jesus said to her, "Believe Me, woman, that an hour is coming when you will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.

**4:22** You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, because salvation is from the Jews.

**4:23** Yet an hour is coming, and is here now, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth; for indeed the Father seeks such people as those who worship Him.

**4:24** God is spirit, and it is necessary for those worshiping Him to worship in spirit and truth."

**4:25** The woman said to Him, "I know that Messiah is coming, the One who is called Christ; when that One comes, He will announce all things to us."

**4:26** Jesus said to her, "I am He, the One speaking to you."

**4:27** And at this point His disciples arrived, and they were marveling that He was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, "What are You seeking?" or, "Why are You speaking with her?"

**4:28** So the woman left her water jar behind, went away into the city, and said to the people,

**4:29** "Come, see a man who told me all the things that I did! Could this possibly be the Christ?"

**4:30** They came out from the city and were coming to Him.

**4:31** In the meantime, the disciples were urging Him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

**4:32** But He said to them, "I have food to eat that you yourselves do not know."

**4:33** So the disciples were saying to one another, "No one brought Him anything to eat, did they?"

**4:34** Jesus said to them, "My food is to do the will of the One who sent Me and to bring His work to completion.

**4:35** Do you not say, 'There are still four months, and the harvest comes'? Look, I tell you, lift up your eyes and observe the fields, that they are white for harvest. Already

**4:36** the one who reaps is receiving wages and is gathering fruit for eternal life, so that the one who sows and the one who reaps may rejoice together.

**4:37** For in this the saying is true: 'One is the sower, and another is the reaper.'

**4:38** I Myself sent you to reap what you yourselves have not labored for; others have labored, and you have entered into their labor."

**4:39** Now from that city many of the Samaritans believed in Him because of the word of the woman who was testifying, "He told me all the things that I did."

**4:40** So when the Samaritans came to Him, they were asking Him to stay with them; and He stayed there two days.

**4:41** And far more believed because of His word,

**4:42** and they were saying to the woman, "No longer do we believe because of your speech; for we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this One is truly the Savior of the world."

**4:43** Now after the two days, He departed from there into Galilee;

**4:44** for Jesus Himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own homeland.

**4:45** So when He arrived in Galilee, the Galileans welcomed Him, having seen all the things that He had done in Jerusalem at the feast; for they themselves also had gone to the feast.

**4:46** Therefore He came again to Cana of Galilee, where He had made the water wine. And there was a certain royal official whose son was sick in Capernaum.

**4:47** Having heard that Jesus had arrived from Judea into Galilee, this man went to Him and kept asking that He would come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.

**4:48** Therefore Jesus said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you will by no means believe."

**4:49** The royal official said to Him, "Sir, come down before my little child dies."

**4:50** Jesus said to him, "Go on your way; your son is alive." The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him and set off on his way.

**4:51** And while he was already going down, his slaves met him, saying that his boy was alive.

**4:52** Therefore he inquired from them the hour at which he had taken a turn for the better. So they told him, "Yesterday, at the seventh hour, the fever left him."

**4:53** So the father recognized that it was at that very hour in which Jesus had said to him, "Your son is alive"; and he himself believed, along with his entire household.

**4:54** Now this again was a second sign that Jesus performed after coming out of Judea into Galilee.

## Translator Notes

- **4:9** — Greek συγχρῶνται literally means "use together with," which may refer specifically to sharing drinking vessels in addition to broader social dealings between Jews and Samaritans.
- **4:26** — Greek Ἐγώ εἰμι is literally "I am," serving both as a predicate identification ("I am He") and as an echo of the divine self-designation in the Greek Old Testament (cf. Exod 3:14; Isa 43:10).
- **4:35** — In the Greek text, ἤδη ("Already") appears after the punctuation break at the end of v. 35 to open the sentence completed in v. 36, though some punctuation traditions attach it to v. 35 ("already white for harvest").
- **4:48** — Both verbs ("see" and "believe") are second-person plural in Greek, showing that Jesus addresses not only the royal official individually but the wider Galilean audience as well.
