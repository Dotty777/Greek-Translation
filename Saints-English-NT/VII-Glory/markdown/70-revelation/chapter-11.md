## Text

**11:1** And there was given to me a reed like a rod, and it was said, "Arise and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and those who worship in it.

**11:2** Yet leave outside the court that is beyond the temple and do not measure it, for it has been given to the Gentiles, and they shall trample the holy city for forty-two months.

**11:3** And I will grant to My two witnesses that they shall prophesy for one thousand two hundred sixty days, arrayed in sackcloth."

**11:4** These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing before the Lord of the earth.

**11:5** And if anyone desires to harm them, fire goes forth out of their mouth and devours their enemies; and if anyone should desire to harm them, in this manner he must be slain.

**11:6** These have authority to shut heaven, so that no rain may fall during the days of their prophecy; and they have authority over the waters to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with every plague as often as they desire.

**11:7** And when they have finished their testimony, the beast that ascends out of the abyss shall wage war against them, overcome them, and slay them.

**11:8** And their dead body shall lie in the wide street of the great city that is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord also was crucified.

**11:9** And some from the peoples, tribes, tongues, and nations gaze upon their dead body for three and a half days, and they do not permit their dead bodies to be laid in a tomb.

**11:10** And those who dwell upon the earth rejoice over them and make merry, and they shall send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who dwell upon the earth.

**11:11** And after the three and a half days, a breath of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet, and great dread fell upon those who beheld them.

**11:12** And they heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, "Come up here." And they ascended into heaven in the cloud, and their enemies beheld them.

**11:13** And in that very hour there came a great earthquake, and a tenth of the city fell; and seven thousand persons were slain in the earthquake, and the rest were filled with terror and gave glory to the God of heaven.

**11:14** The second woe has passed away; behold, the third woe is coming swiftly.

**11:15** Then the seventh angel blew his trumpet, and loud voices sounded in heaven, saying, "The kingdom of the world has become the dominion of our Lord and of His Christ, and He shall reign forever and ever."

**11:16** And the twenty-four elders who sat upon their thrones before God fell upon their faces and worshiped God,

**11:17** saying, "We give thanks to You, O Lord God Almighty, the One who is and who was, because You have indeed taken Your great power and begun to reign.

**11:18** And the nations were filled with wrath, and Your wrath has come, and the time for the dead to be judged, and to grant the reward to Your servants the prophets, and to the saints, and to those who fear Your name, both small and great, and to destroy those who destroy the earth."

**11:19** Then the temple of God in heaven was opened, and the ark of His covenant appeared within His temple; and there were flashes of lightning, and voices, and thunders, and an earthquake, and great hail.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Greek participle "saying" appears without an explicit subject in the critical text (omitting "and the angel stood"), functioning impersonally to introduce the command.
- **11:8** — The Greek uses the collective singular "their dead body" here and in the first half of verse 9 before shifting to the plural "their dead bodies" in the second half of verse 9.
- **11:13** — Literally "seven thousand names of men," a biblical idiom denoting seven thousand individual persons (compare Acts 1:15; Revelation 3:4).
- **11:17** — The critical Greek text reads "who is and who was," omitting "and who is to come," reflecting the declaration that God's eschatological reign has now begun.
