## Text

**2:1** To the angel of the church in Ephesus, write: "Thus says He who holds fast the seven stars in His right hand, He who walks in the midst of the seven golden lampstands:

**2:2** I know your works, your toil and your steadfast endurance, and that you cannot bear with evil men; and you tested those who call themselves apostles and are not, and found them to be false;

**2:3** and you have steadfast endurance, and have borne up for the sake of My name, and have not grown weary.

**2:4** Yet I have this against you, that you have forsaken your first love.

**2:5** Remember therefore from where you have fallen, and repent and perform the first works; otherwise, I am coming to you, and I will remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

**2:6** Yet this you have, that you hate the deeds of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

**2:7** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To him who overcomes I will grant to eat from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God."

**2:8** And to the angel of the church in Smyrna, write: "Thus says the First and the Last, who became dead and came to life:

**2:9** I know your tribulation and your poverty—yet you are rich—and the slander from those who declare themselves to be Jews, and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.

**2:10** Do not fear any of the things that you are about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to cast some of you into prison, that you may be tested, and you shall have tribulation for ten days. Be faithful even to death, and I will give you the crown of life.

**2:11** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. He who overcomes shall by no means be harmed by the second death."

**2:12** And to the angel of the church in Pergamum, write: "Thus says He who holds the sharp two-edged sword:

**2:13** I know where you dwell, where the throne of Satan is; and you hold fast My name, and did not deny the faith in Me even in the days of Antipas, My witness, My faithful one, who was slain among you, where Satan dwells.

**2:14** Yet I have a few things against you, because you have there some who hold fast the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to cast a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat things sacrificed to idols and to commit sexual immorality.

**2:15** Thus you also have some who hold fast the teaching of the Nicolaitans in like manner.

**2:16** Repent therefore; otherwise, I am coming to you swiftly, and I will wage war against them with the sword of My mouth.

**2:17** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To him who overcomes I will give some of the hidden manna, and I will give him a white stone, and upon the stone a new name written, which no one knows except him who receives it."

**2:18** And to the angel of the church in Thyatira, write: "Thus says the Son of God, who has His eyes like a flame of fire, and His feet are like burnished bronze:

**2:19** I know your works, and your love and faith and service and steadfast endurance, and that your latter works are greater than the first.

**2:20** Yet I have this against you, that you tolerate the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, and she teaches and leads My servants astray to commit sexual immorality and to eat things sacrificed to idols.

**2:21** And I gave her time to repent, yet she is not willing to repent of her sexual immorality.

**2:22** Behold, I cast her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her into great tribulation, unless they repent of her deeds;

**2:23** and her children I will slay with death; and all the churches shall know that I am He who searches minds and hearts, and I will render to each one of you according to your works.

**2:24** Now to you I say, the rest of you in Thyatira, as many as do not hold this teaching, who have not known the deep things of Satan, as they call them: I lay upon you no other burden;

**2:25** only hold fast what you have until I come.

**2:26** And to him who overcomes and keeps My works until the end, I will grant authority over the nations,

**2:27** and he shall shepherd them with a rod of iron, as the vessels of clay are shattered to pieces,

**2:28** just as I also have received from My Father; and I will give him the morning star.

**2:29** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Translator Notes

- **2:13** — Greek places "Antipas, My witness, My faithful one" in an abrupt nominative apposition after "in the days," and "My faith" functions as an objective genitive meaning "the faith in Me."
- **2:22** — "Onto a bed" (Greek kline) implies a bed of sickness and judgment, forming an ironic contrast with her bed of adultery; the Greek text reads "her deeds" rather than "their deeds."
- **2:23** — "With death" (Greek en thanato) reflects a Semitic idiom used in the Septuagint to denote deadly pestilence or certain mortal judgment.
- **2:27** — "Shall shepherd" (Greek poimanei) echoes the Septuagint of Psalm 2:9 and carries the dual sense of shepherding and sovereign ruling.
