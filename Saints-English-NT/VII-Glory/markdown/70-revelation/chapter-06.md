## Text

**6:1** And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying as with a voice of thunder, "Come!"

**6:2** And I looked, and behold, a white horse; and he who sat upon it held a bow, and a crown was given to him, and he went forth conquering and to conquer.

**6:3** And when He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, "Come!"

**6:4** And another horse, fiery red, went forth; and to him who sat upon it was granted to take peace from the earth, and that men should slay one another; and there was given to him a great sword.

**6:5** And when He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, "Come!" And I looked, and behold, a black horse; and he who sat upon it held a pair of scales in his hand.

**6:6** And I heard as it were a voice in the midst of the four living creatures saying, "A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius; and do not harm the oil and the wine."

**6:7** And when He opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, "Come!"

**6:8** And I looked, and behold, a pale green horse; and the name of him who sat upon it was Death, and Hades followed along with him. And authority was given to them over a fourth of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with pestilence, and by the wild beasts of the earth.

**6:9** And when He opened the fifth seal, I saw beneath the altar the souls of those who had been slain because of the word of God and because of the testimony which they held.

**6:10** And they cried out with a loud voice, saying, "How long, O Sovereign Lord, holy and true, will You not judge and avenge our blood upon those who dwell upon the earth?"

**6:11** And a white robe was given to each one of them; and it was told to them that they should rest yet a little season, until both their fellow servants and their brethren, who were about to be slain even as they were, should be completed.

**6:12** And I looked when He opened the sixth seal, and a great earthquake occurred; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became like blood;

**6:13** and the stars of heaven fell to the earth, even as a fig tree casts its unripe figs when shaken by a mighty wind.

**6:14** And the heaven parted like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved out of its place.

**6:15** And the kings of the earth, and the great men, and the commanders, and the rich, and the mighty, and every bondservant and every free man, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains,

**6:16** and said to the mountains and to the rocks, "Fall upon us and hide us from the face of Him who sits upon the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb;

**6:17** for the great day of Their wrath has come, and who is able to stand?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:6** — A choenix (rendered "quart") was roughly a dry quart—about one day's ration of wheat—while a denarius was a common laborer's daily wage, indicating severe famine prices.
- **6:8** — Greek chloros denotes a pale, greenish-yellow, ashen hue; "pestilence" translates thanato (literally "death"), reflecting the Septuagint idiom for plague or pestilence (cf. Ezekiel 14:21).
- **6:17** — The Greek critical text reads "Their wrath," referring jointly to Him who sits upon the throne and the Lamb.
