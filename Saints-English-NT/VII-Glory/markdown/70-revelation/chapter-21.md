## Text

**21:1** And I saw a new heaven and a new earth; for the first heaven and the first earth passed away, and the sea is no more.

**21:2** And I saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God, made ready as a bride adorned for her husband.

**21:3** And I heard a great voice from the throne saying, "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and He shall dwell with them, and they shall be His peoples, and God Himself shall be with them,

**21:4** and shall wipe away every tear from their eyes; and death shall be no more; neither mourning nor outcry nor pain shall be any longer. The former things have passed away."

**21:5** And He who was seated upon the throne said, "Behold, I make all things new." And He said to me, "Write, for these words are faithful and true."

**21:6** And He said to me, "They have come to pass! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. To the one who thirsts I will give freely from the fountain of the water of life.

**21:7** The one who overcomes shall inherit these things, and I will be God to him, and he shall be a son to Me.

**21:8** But as for the cowardly, the faithless, the detestable, the murderers, the sexually immoral, the sorcerers, the idolaters, and all liars, their portion shall be in the lake burning with fire and brimstone, which is the second death."

**21:9** And there came one of the seven angels who held the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues, and he spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb."

**21:10** And he bore me away in the Spirit to a mountain great and high, and showed me the holy city, Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God,

**21:11** having the glory of God; her radiance was like a stone most precious, like a jasper stone, crystal-clear;

**21:12** she had a wall great and high, and twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names inscribed upon them, which are those of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel:

**21:13** on the east three gates, and on the north three gates, and on the south three gates, and on the west three gates.

**21:14** And the city's wall had twelve foundations, and upon them were twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.

**21:15** And the one who spoke with me held a golden reed as a measure, so that he might measure the city and her gates and her wall.

**21:16** And the city lies foursquare, and her length is as much as her breadth. And he measured the city with the reed to twelve thousand stadia; her length and her breadth and her height are equal.

**21:17** And he measured her wall, one hundred and forty-four cubits, the measure of a man, which is that of an angel.

**21:18** And the structure of her wall was jasper, and the city was pure gold, resembling pure glass.

**21:19** The foundations of the city's wall were adorned with every precious stone: the first foundation was jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,

**21:20** the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.

**21:21** And the twelve gates were twelve pearls, each single gate being of one pearl; and the street of the city was pure gold, like translucent glass.

**21:22** And I saw no temple in her, for the Lord God the Almighty is her temple, and the Lamb.

**21:23** And the city stands in no need of the sun nor of the moon to shine upon her, for the glory of God illumined her, and the Lamb is her lamp.

**21:24** And the nations shall walk by her light, and the kings of the earth bring their glory into her;

**21:25** and her gates shall by no means be closed by day, for there shall be no night there,

**21:26** and they shall bring into her the glory and the honor of the nations.

**21:27** And nothing unclean shall by any means enter into her, nor anyone who practices abomination and falsehood, except those written in the book of life of the Lamb.

## Translator Notes

- **21:3** — The critical Greek text reads the plural "His peoples" (signifying the redeemed from all nations) and omits the closing phrase "and be their God" present in some manuscripts.
- **21:6** — The critical text reads the plural "They have come to pass!" referring to all the promised realities; later manuscripts read the singular "It is done!"
- **21:16** — Twelve thousand stadia is roughly fourteen hundred miles (about two thousand two hundred kilometers), giving the city the symmetrical shape of a vast cube recalling the Most Holy Place.
- **21:24** — The critical Greek text reads simply "the nations" and "their glory," without the later expansions "of those who are saved" and "and honor."
