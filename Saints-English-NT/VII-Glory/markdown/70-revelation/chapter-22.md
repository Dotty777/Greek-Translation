## Text

**22:1** And he showed me a river of water of life, radiant as crystal, flowing forth out of the throne of God and of the Lamb

**22:2** in the midst of her street. And on either side of the river was the tree of life, bearing twelve fruits, yielding its fruit month by month; and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.

**22:3** And nothing accursed shall exist any more; and the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in her, and His servants shall serve Him,

**22:4** and they shall behold His face, and His name shall be upon their foreheads.

**22:5** And night shall be no more, and they have no need of the light of a lamp or the light of the sun, for the Lord God shall shine upon them; and they shall reign forever and ever.

**22:6** And he said to me, "These words are faithful and true; and the Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets, sent His angel to show to His servants what must shortly come to pass.

**22:7** And behold, I come swiftly! Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book."

**22:8** And I, John, am the one who hears and sees these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who was showing these things to me.

**22:9** And he says to me, "See that you do not do it! I am a fellow servant of yours and of your brethren the prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book. Worship God!"

**22:10** And he says to me, "Do not seal up the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is at hand.

**22:11** Let him who acts unjustly still act unjustly, and let the filthy still be defiled, and let the righteous still practice righteousness, and let the holy still be sanctified."

**22:12** "Behold, I am coming swiftly, and My reward is with Me, to render to each one as his work is.

**22:13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.

**22:14** Blessed are those who wash their robes, that theirs may be the right to the tree of life, and that they may enter through the gates into the city.

**22:15** Outside are the dogs and the sorcerers and the sexually immoral and the murderers and the idolaters, and every one who loves and practices a lie."

**22:16** "I, Jesus, sent My angel to bear witness to you of these things for the churches. I am the Root and the Offspring of David, the radiant Morning Star."

**22:17** And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" And let the one who hears say, "Come!" And let him who thirsts come; let him who wills take water of life freely.

**22:18** I bear witness to every one who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to these things, God shall add to him the plagues written in this book;

**22:19** and if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his portion from the tree of life and out of the holy city, which are written in this book.

**22:20** He who bears witness to these things says, "Yes, I come swiftly." Amen! Come, Lord Jesus!

**22:21** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all.

## Translator Notes

- **22:7** — Without a new introductory formula, the angel's speech shifts directly into the first-person voice of Christ, relaying His promise immediately.
- **22:14** — The earliest and best Greek manuscripts read "those who wash their robes," whereas later manuscripts read "those who do His commandments," a closely similar phrase in Greek spelling and sound.
- **22:19** — Greek manuscripts read "from the tree of life"; the reading "from the book of life" entered printed editions through back-translation from the Latin Vulgate.
- **22:21** — The earliest Greek manuscripts end concisely with "The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all," whereas later manuscripts expand the closing with "Christ," "the saints," and "Amen."
