## Text

**13:1** And I saw a beast ascending out of the sea, having ten horns and seven heads, and upon its horns ten diadems, and upon its heads a name of blasphemy.

**13:2** And the beast that I saw was like a leopard, and its feet were like those of a bear, and its mouth was like the mouth of a lion. And the dragon gave to it his power, and his throne, and great authority.

**13:3** And I saw one of its heads as though it had been slain to death, yet its mortal wound was healed. And the whole earth marveled and followed after the beast;

**13:4** and they worshiped the dragon because he had given the authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who is able to wage war against it?"

**13:5** And there was given to it a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and authority was given to it to act for forty-two months.

**13:6** And it opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme His name and His tabernacle—those who dwell in heaven.

**13:7** And it was permitted to wage war against the saints and to overcome them; and authority was given to it over every tribe, people, tongue, and nation.

**13:8** And all who dwell upon the earth shall worship it, everyone whose name has not been written in the book of life of the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world.

**13:9** If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

**13:10** If anyone is for captivity, he goes; if anyone shall slay with the sword, with the sword he must be slain. Here is the patient endurance and the faith of the saints.

**13:11** And I saw another beast ascending out of the earth, and it had two horns like a lamb, yet it spoke like a dragon.

**13:12** And it exercises all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and it causes the earth and those who dwell in it to worship the first beast, whose mortal wound was healed.

**13:13** And it performs great signs, so that it even causes fire to come down out of heaven to the earth in the sight of men.

**13:14** And it deceives those who dwell upon the earth through the signs that it was granted to perform in the presence of the beast, telling those who dwell upon the earth to make an image for the beast that has the wound of the sword and yet lived.

**13:15** And it was granted to it to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast might even speak and cause as many as would not worship the image of the beast to be slain.

**13:16** And it causes all—both the small and the great, the rich and the poor, the free and the bondservants—to be given a mark upon their right hand or upon their forehead,

**13:17** and so that no one may be able to buy or to sell except the one who has the mark—the name of the beast or the number of its name.

**13:18** Here is wisdom: let the one who has understanding reckon the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man, and its number is six hundred sixty-six.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Greek text reads the singular "a name of blasphemy" upon the beast's heads rather than the plural "names of blasphemy."
- **13:8** — The phrase "from the foundation of the world" stands at the end of the Greek clause and may modify either "the Lamb who was slain" or "has not been written" (compare 17:8).
- **13:10** — The first clause is notably terse in Greek (literally "if anyone into captivity goes"), conveying that those appointed to captivity must endure it; the second clause uses the active future "shall slay" rather than the passive reading found in some witnesses.
- **13:18** — The number six hundred sixty-six is widely understood either as a gematria (the numerical sum of the letters of a name) or as symbolic of fallen humanity falling short of the perfection represented by seven.
