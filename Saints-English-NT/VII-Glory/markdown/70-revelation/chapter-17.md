## Text

**17:1** And one of the seven angels who held the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the judgment of the great harlot who sits upon many waters,

**17:2** with whom the kings of the earth committed fornication, and those who dwell upon the earth became drunk from the wine of her fornication."

**17:3** And he carried me away into a wilderness in the Spirit, and I saw a woman seated upon a scarlet beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns.

**17:4** And the woman was arrayed in purple and scarlet, and adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls, holding in her hand a golden cup full of abominations and the unclean things of her fornication,

**17:5** and upon her forehead a name was written, a mystery: "Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots and of the Abominations of the Earth."

**17:6** And I saw the woman drunk with the blood of the saints and with the blood of the witnesses of Jesus. And when I saw her, I marveled with great astonishment.

**17:7** And the angel said to me, "Why did you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and of the beast that carries her, which has the seven heads and the ten horns.

**17:8** The beast that you saw was, and is not, and is about to ascend out of the abyss and go into destruction. And those who dwell upon the earth—whose names have not been written in the book of life from the foundation of the world—shall marvel when they behold the beast, that it was, and is not, and shall appear.

**17:9** Here is the mind that has wisdom: The seven heads are seven mountains upon which the woman sits; and they are seven kings:

**17:10** five have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come; and when he comes, he must remain a little while.

**17:11** And as for the beast that was and is not, it is itself also an eighth, and is of the seven, and goes into destruction.

**17:12** And the ten horns that you saw are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom, yet they receive authority as kings for one hour together with the beast.

**17:13** These have one mind, and they give their power and their authority to the beast.

**17:14** These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them, for He is Lord of lords and King of kings; and those who are with Him are called, and chosen, and faithful."

**17:15** And he says to me, "The waters that you saw, where the harlot sits, are peoples and multitudes and nations and tongues.

**17:16** And the ten horns that you saw, and the beast, these shall hate the harlot, and shall make her desolate and naked, and shall eat her flesh, and shall burn her up with fire;

**17:17** for God has put it into their hearts to fulfill His purpose, and to be of one mind, and to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God shall be fulfilled.

**17:18** And the woman whom you saw is that great city which holds dominion over the kings of the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **17:5** — Greek mysterion ("a mystery") may be read either as an appositive describing the symbolic character of the name or as the opening word of the inscription itself ("Mystery, Babylon the Great...").
- **17:8** — The closing verb parestai literally means "shall be present" or "shall appear," forming an intentional parody of the divine title "who is and who was and who is to come" (1:4, 8).
- **17:9** — Greek ore ("mountains") may also be rendered "hills," a double symbol associated both with Rome's seven hills and with seven kings or kingdoms.
- **17:16** — The Greek critical text reads "and the beast" (kai to therion), showing the beast joining the ten horns against the harlot, whereas later Byzantine manuscripts read "upon the beast" (epi to therion).
