## Text

**3:1** And to the angel of the church in Sardis, write: "Thus says He who has the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your works, that you have a name that you are alive, and yet you are dead.

**3:2** Be watchful, and strengthen the things that remain, which were about to die, for I have not found your works complete in the sight of My God.

**3:3** Remember therefore how you have received and heard, and keep it, and repent. Therefore, if you do not keep watch, I will come like a thief, and you shall by no means know at what hour I will come upon you.

**3:4** Yet you have a few names in Sardis who have not defiled their garments, and they shall walk with Me in white, for they are worthy.

**3:5** He who overcomes shall thus be clothed in white garments, and I will by no means blot out his name from the book of life, and I will confess his name before My Father and before His angels.

**3:6** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

**3:7** And to the angel of the church in Philadelphia, write: "Thus says the Holy One, the True One, He who holds the key of David, He who opens and no one shall shut, and shuts and no one opens:

**3:8** I know your works—behold, I have set before you an open door, which no one is able to shut—that you have little strength, yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name.

**3:9** Behold, I am giving some from the synagogue of Satan, of those who declare themselves to be Jews, and are not, but lie—behold, I will make them come and bow down before your feet, and know that I have loved you.

**3:10** Because you have kept the word of My steadfast endurance, I also will keep you from the hour of trial which is about to come upon the whole inhabited world, to test those who dwell upon the earth.

**3:11** I am coming swiftly; hold fast what you have, lest anyone take your crown.

**3:12** He who overcomes, I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God, and he shall by no means go out any longer; and I will write upon him the name of My God, and the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem, which descends out of heaven from My God, and My new name.

**3:13** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

**3:14** And to the angel of the church in Laodicea, write: "Thus says the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Beginning of the creation of God:

**3:15** I know your works, that you are neither cold nor hot. I wish that you were cold or hot!

**3:16** Thus, because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am about to spew you out of My mouth.

**3:17** Because you say, 'I am rich, and have grown wealthy, and have need of nothing,' and you do not know that you are wretched, pitiable, poor, blind, and naked,

**3:18** I counsel you to buy from Me gold refined by fire, that you may become rich, and white garments, that you may clothe yourself and the shame of your nakedness may not be exposed, and eyesalve to anoint your eyes, that you may see.

**3:19** As many as I love, I reprove and chasten; be zealous therefore, and repent.

**3:20** Behold, I stand at the door and knock; if anyone hears My voice and opens the door, then I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me.

**3:21** He who overcomes, I will grant him to sit with Me upon My throne, just as I also overcame and sat down with My Father upon His throne.

**3:22** Let him who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — "Thus" translates the Greek adverb houtos ("in this manner") in the critical text, rather than the variant pronoun houtos ("this one").
- **3:10** — "The word of My steadfast endurance" may denote either Christ's command to endure patiently or the word exemplified by His own patient endurance.
- **3:14** — "The Beginning of the creation of God" renders Greek arche, which carries the sense of origin, active source, or sovereign ruler of creation rather than a created being.
