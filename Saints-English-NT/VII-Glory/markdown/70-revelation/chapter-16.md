## Text

**16:1** And I heard a loud voice out of the temple saying to the seven angels, "Go and pour out the seven bowls of the wrath of God upon the earth."

**16:2** So the first went and poured out his bowl upon the earth, and a foul and malignant sore came upon the men who had the mark of the beast and those who worshiped his image.

**16:3** And the second poured out his bowl into the sea, and it became blood as of a dead man; and every soul of life died, everything that was in the sea.

**16:4** And the third poured out his bowl into the rivers and the springs of waters, and they became blood.

**16:5** And I heard the angel of the waters saying, "Righteous are You, the One who is and who was, the Holy One, because You have judged these things,

**16:6** for they shed the blood of saints and prophets, and You have given them blood to drink. They are worthy of it."

**16:7** And I heard the altar saying, "Yes, Lord God Almighty, true and righteous are Your judgments."

**16:8** And the fourth poured out his bowl upon the sun, and it was granted to it to scorch men with fire.

**16:9** And men were scorched with great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God who has authority over these plagues; and they did not repent so as to give Him glory.

**16:10** And the fifth poured out his bowl upon the throne of the beast, and his kingdom became darkened; and they gnawed their tongues because of the pain,

**16:11** and they blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and their sores, and they did not repent of their deeds.

**16:12** And the sixth poured out his bowl upon the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up, that the way of the kings from the rising of the sun might be prepared.

**16:13** And I saw coming out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet, three unclean spirits like frogs.

**16:14** For they are spirits of demons performing signs, which go forth to the kings of the whole inhabited world, to gather them together for the battle of the great day of God Almighty—

**16:15** "Behold, I am coming like a thief! Blessed is he who watches and keeps his garments, lest he walk naked and they see his shame."—

**16:16** and they gathered them together to the place that is called in Hebrew Armageddon.

**16:17** And the seventh poured out his bowl upon the air—and a loud voice came out of the temple from the throne, saying, "It is done!"—

**16:18** and there were lightnings and voices and thunderings; and there was a great earthquake, such as had never occurred since men have been upon the earth, so mighty an earthquake and so great.

**16:19** And the great city was divided into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell; and Babylon the great was remembered before God, to give her the cup of the wine of the fury of His wrath.

**16:20** And every island fled away, and mountains were not found.

**16:21** And great hail, about a talent in weight, came down from heaven upon men; and men blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail, because its plague is exceedingly great.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Greek reads "every soul of life" (pasa psyche zoes, a Semitic genitive of quality meaning "every living creature"; compare Gen 1:30 LXX), with the neuter plural phrase "the things in the sea" (ta en te thalasse) in apposition.
- **16:5** — Following the critical Greek text, which addresses God as "the One who is and who was, the Holy One" (ho hosios), omitting "who is to come" because the final judgment has now arrived.
- **16:7** — In the critical Greek text, the altar itself speaks directly, personifying the souls of the martyrs beneath it (compare 6:9), rather than introducing "another from the altar."
- **16:16** — Greek synegagen is singular, agreeing grammatically with the neuter plural "spirits" of 16:13-14 ("they gathered"), though some interpreters take God or the dragon as the implied subject.
- **16:21** — A Greek talent weighed roughly 75 to 100 pounds (about 34 to 45 kilograms), emphasizing the devastating magnitude of the final hailstorm.
