## Text

**4:1** Therefore, since Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves likewise with the same resolve, for the one who has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,

**4:2** so as to live the remaining time in the flesh no longer for the desires of men, but for the will of God.

**4:3** For the time already past is sufficient to have carried out the desire of the Gentiles, having walked in debauchery, lusts, drunkenness, revelries, drinking bouts, and lawless idolatries.

**4:4** In this they are astonished that you do not run together with them into the same flood of dissipation, reviling you;

**4:5** who shall render an account to Him who stands ready to judge the living and the dead.

**4:6** For to this end the gospel was proclaimed even to the dead, that they might be judged according to men in the flesh, yet live according to God in the spirit.

**4:7** Now the end of all things has drawn near. Therefore be of sound judgment and sober-minded for prayers.

**4:8** Above all, maintain earnest love among yourselves, because love covers a multitude of sins.

**4:9** Offer hospitality to one another without murmuring.

**4:10** Even as each has received a gift of grace, use it in serving one another as good stewards of the varied grace of God.

**4:11** If anyone speaks, let it be as the oracles of God; if anyone serves, let it be from the strength that God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen.

**4:12** Beloved, do not be astonished at the fiery ordeal coming upon you for your testing, as though some strange thing were befalling you,

**4:13** but insofar as you share in the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, so that at the revelation of His glory you may also rejoice with exultant gladness.

**4:14** If you are reviled in the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests upon you.

**4:15** For let none of you suffer as a murderer, or a thief, or an evildoer, or as a meddler in the affairs of others;

**4:16** yet if someone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed; rather, let him glorify God in this name.

**4:17** For the season has arrived for judgment to begin at the household of God; and if it begins first with us, what shall be the end of those who disobey the gospel of God?

**4:18** And if the righteous one is saved with difficulty, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?

**4:19** Therefore let those also who suffer according to the will of God commend their souls to a faithful Creator in doing good.

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — Commentators debate whether 'the dead' refers to believers who heard the gospel during their lifetimes and have since died, or to the spirits of the dead in the realm of the dead (compare 3:19).
- **4:14** — Later Byzantine manuscripts add 'on their part He is blasphemed, but on your part He is glorified,' which is absent from the earliest Greek witnesses.
- **4:15** — The rare Greek noun allotriepiskopos occurs only here in the New Testament and denotes an unauthorized overseer of another's affairs, meaning a meddler or busybody.
- **4:16** — The critical Greek text reads 'in this name' (en to onomati touto), referring to the name 'Christian,' whereas later manuscripts read 'in this matter' (en to merei touto).
