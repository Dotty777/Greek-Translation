## Text

**6:1** Therefore, leaving behind the elementary word concerning Christ, let us press on toward maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God,

**6:2** instruction concerning washings, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.

**6:3** And this we will do, if indeed God permits.

**6:4** For it is impossible for those who have once been enlightened, and have tasted of the heavenly gift, and have become sharers of the Holy Spirit,

**6:5** and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the coming age,

**6:6** and then have fallen away, to be renewed again to repentance, crucifying afresh for themselves the Son of God and holding Him up to public disgrace.

**6:7** For land that drinks in the rain which often falls upon it, and brings forth vegetation useful to those for whose sake it is tilled, partakes of blessing from God;

**6:8** but if it yields thorns and thistles, it is rejected and near to a curse, whose end is to be burned.

**6:9** Yet, beloved, concerning you we are convinced of better things—things that accompany salvation—even though we speak in this manner.

**6:10** For God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love which you have shown toward His name, in having ministered to the saints and in continuing to minister.

**6:11** And we earnestly desire that each one of you show the same diligence for the full assurance of hope until the end,

**6:12** so that you may not become sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

**6:13** For when God gave a promise to Abraham, since there was no one greater by whom He could swear, He swore by Himself,

**6:14** saying, "Assuredly, blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you."

**6:15** And thus, having waited patiently, he obtained the promise.

**6:16** For men swear by one who is greater, and among them an oath for confirmation puts an end to all dispute.

**6:17** In the same way, God, desiring all the more to show to the heirs of the promise the immutability of His counsel, interposed with an oath,

**6:18** so that through two immutable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled for refuge may have strong encouragement to lay hold of the hope set before us.

**6:19** This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both secure and steadfast, entering into the inner place behind the veil,

**6:20** where as a forerunner on our behalf Jesus has entered, having become a High Priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The critical Greek text has accusative didachen ("instruction") in apposition to "foundation" (v. 1), and plural baptismon ("washings"), which elsewhere in Hebrews (9:10) denotes ceremonial washings and here may encompass ritual washings and baptisms.
- **6:6** — The present participles ("crucifying ... and holding Him up") leave open whether the clause states the reason renewal is impossible ("since") or its duration ("while"); anastaurountas can denote either "crucifying afresh" or simply "crucifying."
- **6:10** — The critical Greek text reads "your work and the love," without the later textual addition "labor of" before "love."
