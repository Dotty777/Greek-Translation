## Text

**1:1** God, having spoken long ago in many portions and in many ways to the fathers by the prophets,

**1:2** has at the end of these days spoken to us by a Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom also He made the ages;

**1:3** who, being the radiance of His glory and the exact impress of His essence, and sustaining all things by the word of His power, having made purification for sins, sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,

**1:4** having become by so much superior to the angels as He has inherited a name more excellent than theirs.

**1:5** For to which of the angels did He at any time say, "You are My Son; this day I have begotten You," and again, "I shall be a Father to Him, and He shall be a Son to Me"?

**1:6** And when He again brings the firstborn into the inhabited world, He says, "And let all the angels of God worship Him."

**1:7** And concerning the angels He says, "He who makes His angels spirits and His ministers a flame of fire."

**1:8** But to the Son He says, "Your throne, O God, is forever and ever, and the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of His kingdom.

**1:9** You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You with the oil of exultation above Your companions."

**1:10** And, "You, Lord, in the beginning laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the works of Your hands.

**1:11** They shall perish, yet You remain; and they all shall grow old like a garment,

**1:12** and like a mantle You shall roll them up, like a garment they shall also be changed; but You are the same, and Your years shall not fail."

**1:13** But to which of the angels has He ever said, "Sit at My right hand until I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet"?

**1:14** Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth for service on behalf of those who are to inherit salvation?

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — The adverb "again" (palin) may modify "brings" (pointing to a second entry or return into the inhabited world) or "says" (introducing another Scripture quotation, as in 1:5).
- **1:8** — The Greek text reads "of His kingdom" (tes basileias autou) rather than "of Your kingdom" (as in Psalm 45:6 / 44:7 LXX and some manuscripts).
- **1:12** — The Greek text includes "like a garment" (hos himation) before "they shall also be changed," echoing 1:11.
