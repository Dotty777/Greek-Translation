## Text

**3:1** Therefore, holy brethren, sharers in a heavenly calling, consider carefully the Apostle and High Priest of our confession, Jesus,

**3:2** who was faithful to Him who appointed Him, just as Moses also was in His house.

**3:3** For this One has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by as much as he who built the house has greater honor than the house.

**3:4** For every house is constructed by someone, yet He who constructed all things is God.

**3:5** And Moses indeed was faithful in all His house as a servant, for a testimony of the things that were to be spoken,

**3:6** but Christ as a Son over His house; whose house we are, if we hold fast the boldness and the boasting of our hope.

**3:7** Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says, "Today, if you hear His voice,

**3:8** do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, on the day of testing in the wilderness,

**3:9** where your fathers tested Me by trial and saw My works

**3:10** for forty years. Therefore I was indignant with this generation, and I said, 'They always go astray in their heart, and they have not known My ways.'

**3:11** As I swore in My wrath, 'They shall never enter My rest.'"

**3:12** Take heed, brethren, lest there be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief in falling away from the living God;

**3:13** but exhort one another day by day, as long as it is called "Today," lest any one of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

**3:14** For we have become sharers in Christ, if indeed we hold fast our original assurance firm to the end,

**3:15** while it is said, "Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion."

**3:16** For who, having heard, rebelled? Indeed, was it not all those who came out of Egypt through Moses?

**3:17** And with whom was He indignant for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?

**3:18** And to whom did He swear that they would not enter His rest, except to those who disobeyed?

**3:19** And we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The Greek text omits "firm to the end" here (words that appear in 3:14).
- **3:9** — The Greek text reads "tested ... by trial" (without an explicit object pronoun "Me") and connects "for forty years" (3:10) with "saw My works" rather than with God's indignation.
- **3:11** — The Greek particle ei ("if") reflects the Hebrew oath idiom of Psalm 95:11, functioning as an emphatic negative ("They shall never enter").
- **3:14** — The noun hypostasis (rendered "essence" in 1:3) is used here in the sense of firm assurance or confidence.
