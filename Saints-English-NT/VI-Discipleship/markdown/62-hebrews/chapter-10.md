## Text

**10:1** For the law, having a shadow of the coming good things and not the very image of those realities, is never able, with the same sacrifices which they offer continually year after year, to make perfect those who draw near.

**10:2** For otherwise, would they not have ceased being offered, because the worshipers, having been purified once, would have no further consciousness of sins?

**10:3** Yet in them there is a remembrance of sins year by year.

**10:4** For it is impossible that the blood of bulls and of goats should remove sins.

**10:5** Therefore, as He enters into the world, He says, "Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, yet a body You prepared for Me.

**10:6** In whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin You took no delight.

**10:7** Then I said, 'Behold, I have come—in the scroll of the book it stands written concerning Me—to do Your will, O God.'"

**10:8** When He says above, "Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin You did not desire, nor did You delight in them"—which are offered according to the law—

**10:9** then He has said, "Behold, I have come to do Your will." He does away with the first in order that He may establish the second.

**10:10** By that will we stand sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

**10:11** And indeed every priest stands day after day ministering and offering time and again the same sacrifices, which are never able to take away sins.

**10:12** But this One, having offered a single sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,

**10:13** waiting from that time onward until His enemies are set as a footstool for His feet.

**10:14** For by a single offering He has brought to perfection for all time those who are being sanctified.

**10:15** And the Holy Spirit also bears witness to us; for after having said,

**10:16** "This is the covenant that I will establish with them after those days," the Lord says, "I will put My laws upon their hearts, and upon their mind I will inscribe them,

**10:17** and their sins and their iniquities I will never again remember."

**10:18** Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer any offering for sin.

**10:19** Therefore, brethren, since we have boldness to enter the sanctuary by the blood of Jesus,

**10:20** by that new and living way which He dedicated for us through the veil, that is to say, His flesh,

**10:21** and since we have a great Priest over the house of God,

**10:22** let us draw near with a genuine heart in complete assurance of faith, our hearts having been sprinkled from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.

**10:23** Let us hold firm the confession of our hope unwaveringly, for faithful is He who promised.

**10:24** And let us take careful thought for one another, to spur on love and good works,

**10:25** not abandoning our own assembling together, as is the custom of some, but exhorting one another, and all the more as you see the Day drawing near.

**10:26** For if we willfully keep on sinning after receiving the knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins remains any longer,

**10:27** but rather a certain dreadful expectation of judgment and a fury of fire that is about to devour the adversaries.

**10:28** Whoever sets aside the law of Moses dies without mercy upon the testimony of two or three witnesses.

**10:29** Of how much severer punishment, do you suppose, will he be judged worthy who has trampled underfoot the Son of God, and has regarded as profane the blood of the covenant by which he was sanctified, and has outraged the Spirit of grace?

**10:30** For we know Him who has said, "Vengeance belongs to Me; I will repay," and again, "The Lord shall judge His people."

**10:31** It is a dreadful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.

**10:32** Yet call to remembrance the earlier days, in which, after you had been enlightened, you endured a great conflict of sufferings:

**10:33** on the one hand being made a public spectacle through both reproaches and afflictions, and on the other hand becoming partners with those who were dealt with in this manner.

**10:34** For you showed sympathy to those in bonds, and with joy you accepted the plundering of your possessions, knowing that you yourselves have a better and abiding possession.

**10:35** Therefore do not cast aside your boldness, which carries a great reward.

**10:36** For you stand in need of endurance, so that, after doing the will of God, you may obtain the promise.

**10:37** For "yet a very little while, He who is coming shall come and will not tarry;

**10:38** but My righteous one shall live by faith; and if he shrinks back, My soul takes no delight in him."

**10:39** Yet we do not belong to those who shrink back to destruction, but to those who have faith for the preserving of the soul.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — Quoting Psalm 39:7 LXX (40:6 MT), the Greek text reads "a body You prepared for Me," reflecting the Septuagint rendering rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "ears You have opened for me."
- **10:12** — The Greek phrase for "for all time" (eis to dienekes) stands between the two clauses and can modify either "having offered a single sacrifice for sins" (as translated here) or "sat down at the right hand of God."
- **10:34** — The critical Greek text reads "those in bonds" (the prisoners) rather than the later variant "my chains," and does not include "in heaven" after "a better and abiding possession."
- **10:38** — Following the Septuagint of Habakkuk 2:4, the critical text includes the pronoun "My" ("My righteous one," omitted in some later manuscripts) and links "if he shrinks back" directly to "shrink back" in verse 39.
