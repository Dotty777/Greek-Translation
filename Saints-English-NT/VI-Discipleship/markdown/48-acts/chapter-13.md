## Text

**13:1** Now there were in Antioch, in the church that was there, prophets and teachers: Barnabas, Simeon who was called Niger, Lucius the Cyrenian, Manaen who had been raised alongside Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.

**13:2** As they were ministering to the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, "Set apart now for Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them."

**13:3** Then, having fasted and prayed and laid hands upon them, they sent them forth.

**13:4** So these men, being sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed away to Cyprus.

**13:5** And when they arrived at Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews; and they also had John as their attendant.

**13:6** When they had passed through the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain sorcerer, a Jewish false prophet, whose name was Bar-Jesus,

**13:7** who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, a man of understanding. This man summoned Barnabas and Saul and earnestly sought to hear the word of God.

**13:8** Yet Elymas the sorcerer—for so his name is translated—withstood them, seeking to turn the proconsul away from the faith.

**13:9** Then Saul, who is also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, fixed his eyes intently upon him

**13:10** and said, "O you who are full of all deceit and all villainy, son of the devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not cease perverting the straight paths of the Lord?

**13:11** And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you shall be blind, not seeing the sun for a season." At once a mist and darkness fell upon him, and wandering about, he sought those who would lead him by the hand.

**13:12** Then the proconsul, seeing what had taken place, believed, being astonished at the teaching of the Lord.

**13:13** Now Paul and his companions set sail from Paphos and came to Perga in Pamphylia; yet John departed from them and returned to Jerusalem.

**13:14** Going on from Perga, they came to Pisidian Antioch, and entering the synagogue on the Sabbath day, they sat down.

**13:15** After the reading of the Law and the Prophets, the rulers of the synagogue sent word to them, saying, "Men and brethren, if there is any word of exhortation among you for the people, speak."

**13:16** Then Paul rose up, and motioning with his hand, he said, "Men of Israel, and you who fear God, listen.

**13:17** The God of this people Israel chose our fathers and exalted the people during their sojourn in the land of Egypt, and with an uplifted arm He brought them out of it.

**13:18** And for a period of about forty years He bore with their ways in the wilderness.

**13:19** And when He had overthrown seven nations in the land of Canaan, He allotted their land to them as an inheritance,

**13:20** which took about four hundred and fifty years. And after these things He gave them judges until Samuel the prophet.

**13:21** And afterward they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul the son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.

**13:22** And when He had removed him, He raised up David for them as king, of whom He also testified and said, 'I have found David the son of Jesse, a man after My own heart, who will carry out all My will.'

**13:23** From the seed of this man, according to promise, God brought to Israel a Savior, Jesus,

**13:24** after John had first proclaimed, before His coming, a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.

**13:25** And as John was completing his course, he said, 'What do you suppose me to be? I am not He. Yet behold, One is coming after me, the sandal of whose feet I am not worthy to untie.'

**13:26** Men and brethren, sons of the family of Abraham, and those among you who fear God, to us the word of this salvation has been sent forth.

**13:27** For those who dwell in Jerusalem and their rulers, because they did not recognize Him nor the voices of the prophets that are read every Sabbath, fulfilled them in condemning Him.

**13:28** And though they found no cause for death, they asked Pilate that He should be put to death.

**13:29** Now when they had carried out all that was written concerning Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb.

**13:30** Yet God raised Him from the dead,

**13:31** and for many days He appeared to those who had gone up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now His witnesses to the people.

**13:32** And we bring you the good tidings of the promise made to the fathers,

**13:33** that God has fulfilled this very promise for our children by raising up Jesus, as it is also written in the second Psalm: 'You are My Son; this day I have begotten You.'

**13:34** And concerning the fact that He raised Him from the dead, no more to return to corruption, He has spoken thus: 'I will give you the faithful and holy mercies of David.'

**13:35** Therefore He also says in another Psalm: 'You will not permit Your Holy One to see corruption.'

**13:36** For David, after he had served the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep, was gathered to his fathers, and saw corruption;

**13:37** yet He whom God raised up did not see corruption.

**13:38** Let it be known to you, therefore, men and brethren, that through Him forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you, and from all things from which you could not be justified by the law of Moses,

**13:39** in Him everyone who believes is justified.

**13:40** Take heed, therefore, lest what has been spoken in the Prophets come upon you:

**13:41** 'Behold, you scoffers, marvel and perish; for I am working a deed in your days, a deed which you will by no means believe, even if someone recounts it to you.'"

**13:42** As they were going out, the people pleaded that these words be spoken to them on the next Sabbath.

**13:43** Now when the assembly of the synagogue was dismissed, many of the Jews and devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking with them, urged them to abide in the grace of God.

**13:44** On the next Sabbath almost the whole city gathered together to hear the word of God.

**13:45** Yet when the Jews saw the multitudes, they were filled with jealousy and contradicted the things spoken by Paul, blaspheming.

**13:46** Then Paul and Barnabas spoke out boldly and said, "It was necessary that the word of God should be spoken to you first; yet since you thrust it away and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, behold, we turn to the Gentiles.

**13:47** For so the Lord has commanded us: 'I have appointed you as a light for the Gentiles, that you should be for salvation to the farthest end of the earth.'"

**13:48** Now when the Gentiles heard this, they rejoiced and glorified the word of the Lord; and as many as had been appointed to eternal life believed.

**13:49** And the word of the Lord was being spread abroad throughout all that region.

**13:50** Yet the Jews incited the devout women of high standing and the leading men of the city, and stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and cast them out from their borders.

**13:51** So they shook off the dust of their feet against them and came to Iconium.

**13:52** And the disciples were continually filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **13:18** — The Greek text reads etropophoresen ("bore with their ways"), whereas some manuscripts differ by one letter and read etrophophoresen ("cared for" or "nursed," echoing Deuteronomy 1:31 LXX).
- **13:20** — In the Greek critical text, "about four hundred and fifty years" concludes the preceding period (the sojourn in Egypt, the wilderness wandering, and the inheritance of Canaan) rather than modifying the era of the judges.
- **13:26** — The Greek critical text reads "to us" (hemin), including Paul and his Jewish hearers together, whereas later Byzantine manuscripts read "to you" (hymin).
- **13:33** — The Greek critical text reads "for our children" (tois teknois hemon), whereas later manuscripts read "for us, their children."
