## Text

**7:1** Now the high priest said, "Are these things so?"

**7:2** And he said, "Men, brethren, and fathers, listen: The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham while he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Haran,

**7:3** and said to him, 'Go out from your land and from your kindred, and come into the land that I shall show you.'

**7:4** Then he departed from the land of the Chaldeans and dwelt in Haran. And from there, after the death of his father, He removed him into this land in which you now dwell,

**7:5** and He did not give him an inheritance in it, not even the breadth of a foot; yet He promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his offspring after him, though he had no child.

**7:6** And God spoke thus: that his offspring would be sojourners in a foreign land, and that they would bring them into bondage and afflict them four hundred years;

**7:7** 'and the nation to which they shall be in bondage I will judge,' God said, 'and after these things they shall come forth and worship Me in this place.'

**7:8** And He gave him the covenant of circumcision; and so he fathered Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day, and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs.

**7:9** And the patriarchs, envious of Joseph, sold him into Egypt; yet God was with him,

**7:10** and delivered him from all his afflictions, and granted him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and he appointed him ruler over Egypt and all his household.

**7:11** Now a famine came upon all Egypt and Canaan, and great affliction, and our fathers found no sustenance;

**7:12** but when Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent out our fathers the first time;

**7:13** and the second time Joseph was made known to his brothers, and the kindred of Joseph became known to Pharaoh.

**7:14** Then Joseph sent and summoned his father Jacob and all his kindred, seventy-five souls in all;

**7:15** and Jacob went down into Egypt. And he died, he and our fathers;

**7:16** and they were carried over to Shechem and laid in the tomb that Abraham had bought for a sum of silver from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.

**7:17** But as the time of the promise drew near, which God had pledged to Abraham, the people increased and multiplied in Egypt,

**7:18** until another king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.

**7:19** He dealt craftily with our race and afflicted our fathers, making them cast out their infants so that they would not be kept alive.

**7:20** At that time Moses was born, and he was fair in the sight of God; and he was nurtured three months in the house of his father.

**7:21** And when he was cast out, the daughter of Pharaoh took him up and reared him for herself as a son.

**7:22** And Moses was trained in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was mighty in his words and works.

**7:23** Now when forty years of his life were fulfilled, it entered his heart to visit his brethren, the sons of Israel.

**7:24** And seeing one of them being wronged, he defended him and avenged the one who was oppressed by striking down the Egyptian.

**7:25** For he supposed that his brethren understood that God was giving them deliverance by his hand, but they did not understand.

**7:26** And on the following day he appeared to them as they were fighting, and tried to reconcile them in peace, saying, 'Men, you are brethren; why do you wrong one another?'

**7:27** But the one who was wronging his neighbor thrust him aside, saying, 'Who appointed you a ruler and a judge over us?

**7:28** Do you intend to slay me in the same manner as you slew the Egyptian yesterday?'

**7:29** And at this saying Moses fled and became a sojourner in the land of Midian, where he fathered two sons.

**7:30** And when forty years had been fulfilled, an angel appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, in a flame of fire in a bush.

**7:31** When Moses saw it, he marveled at the sight; and as he drew near to look closely, the voice of the Lord came:

**7:32** 'I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.' And Moses trembled and did not dare to look.

**7:33** Then the Lord said to him, 'Take the sandals off your feet, for the place on which you stand is holy ground.

**7:34** I have surely seen the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their groaning, and I have come down to deliver them; and now come, I will send you to Egypt.'

**7:35** This Moses whom they rejected, saying, 'Who appointed you a ruler and a judge?'—this man God has sent as both ruler and redeemer by the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.

**7:36** This man led them out, performing wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and in the Red Sea, and in the wilderness for forty years.

**7:37** This is the Moses who said to the sons of Israel, 'God will raise up for you a Prophet like me from among your brethren.'

**7:38** This is the one who was in the congregation in the wilderness with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai and with our fathers, who received living oracles to give to you;

**7:39** whom our fathers refused to obey, but thrust him aside and in their hearts turned back to Egypt,

**7:40** saying to Aaron, 'Make for us gods who shall go before us; for as for this Moses who led us out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has happened to him.'

**7:41** And they made a calf in those days, and offered a sacrifice to the idol, and rejoiced in the works of their hands.

**7:42** Then God turned away and gave them over to worship the host of heaven, as it is written in the book of the Prophets: 'Did you bring Me slain beasts and sacrifices for forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?

**7:43** Indeed, you took up the tabernacle of Moloch and the star of the god Rompha, the images that you made to worship; and I will carry you away beyond Babylon.'

**7:44** Our fathers had the tabernacle of testimony in the wilderness, just as He who spoke to Moses directed him to make it according to the pattern that he had seen,

**7:45** which our fathers, having received it in turn, also brought in with Joshua when taking possession of the nations whom God drove out before the face of our fathers, until the days of David;

**7:46** who found favor in the sight of God and asked to find a dwelling place for the house of Jacob.

**7:47** Yet Solomon built a house for Him.

**7:48** However, the Most High does not dwell in things made by hands, as the prophet says:

**7:49** 'Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet. What kind of house will you build for Me, says the Lord, or what is the place of My rest?

**7:50** Did My hand not make all these things?'

**7:51** You stiff-necked people, uncircumcised in hearts and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit; just as your fathers did, so do you.

**7:52** Which of the prophets did your fathers not persecute? And they slew those who foretold the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become the betrayers and murderers,

**7:53** you who received the law by the direction of angels, and did not keep it."

**7:54** Now when they heard these things, they were cut to their hearts and gnashed their teeth at him.

**7:55** But he, full of the Holy Spirit, looked intently into heaven and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God,

**7:56** and said, "Behold, I see the heavens opened wide, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God."

**7:57** Then they cried out with a loud voice, covered their ears, and rushed upon him with one accord,

**7:58** and casting him out of the city, they began stoning him. And the witnesses laid down their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul.

**7:59** And they kept stoning Stephen as he called out and said, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

**7:60** Then kneeling down, he cried out with a loud voice, "Lord, do not count this sin against them." And having said this, he fell asleep.

## Translator Notes

- **7:14** — "Seventy-five souls" follows the Septuagint reading of Genesis 46:27 and Exodus 1:5, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text numbers Jacob's household at seventy.
- **7:16** — The critical Greek text reads "in Shechem" (rather than "the father of Shechem" in later manuscripts). Stephen compresses into one summary Abraham's purchase of the burial cave at Machpelah (Genesis 23) and Jacob's purchase of land from the sons of Hamor at Shechem (Genesis 33:19; Joshua 24:32).
- **7:20** — The Greek phrase "fair in the sight of God" (asteios to theo) may also function as a Semitic superlative meaning "exceedingly fair" or "extraordinarily beautiful."
- **7:30** — The critical Greek text reads simply "an angel" (angelos), whereas later Byzantine manuscripts add "of the Lord."
- **7:38** — The critical Greek text reads "to give to you" (hymin), underscoring Stephen's direct address to his hearers, whereas other manuscripts read "to give to us" (hemin).
- **7:43** — "Rompha" transliterates the critical Greek reading Rompha (cf. Septuagint Amos 5:26 Raiphan, later manuscripts Remphan, and Hebrew Kiyyun), the name of a pagan star-god associated with Saturn.
- **7:46** — The critical Greek text reads "for the house of Jacob" (to oiko Iakob), whereas later manuscripts harmonize with Psalm 132:5 by reading "for the God of Jacob" (to theo Iakob).
