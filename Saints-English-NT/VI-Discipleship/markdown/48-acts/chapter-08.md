## Text

**8:1** Now Saul was approving of his execution. And on that day a great persecution broke out against the church in Jerusalem, and all were scattered throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria, except the apostles.

**8:2** And devout men bore Stephen away for burial and made a great lamentation over him.

**8:3** But Saul was laying waste to the church; going from house to house and dragging away men and women, he delivered them over to prison.

**8:4** Therefore those who had been scattered went about proclaiming the good news of the word.

**8:5** Then Philip, going down to the city of Samaria, proclaimed the Christ to them.

**8:6** And the multitudes with one accord gave heed to the things spoken by Philip, as they heard and beheld the signs that he was performing;

**8:7** for from many of those who had unclean spirits, the spirits came forth crying with a loud voice, and many who were paralyzed and lame were healed.

**8:8** And there arose great joy in that city.

**8:9** Now a certain man by the name of Simon had previously been practicing magic in the city and amazing the nation of Samaria, declaring himself to be someone great,

**8:10** to whom all gave heed, from the least to the greatest, saying, "This man is the Power of God that is called Great."

**8:11** And they paid attention to him because for a considerable time he had amazed them with his magic arts.

**8:12** Yet when they believed Philip as he proclaimed the good news concerning the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, both men and women were baptized.

**8:13** And Simon himself also believed; and after being baptized, he stayed continually with Philip, and beholding the signs and great works of power taking place, he was amazed.

**8:14** Now when the apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them,

**8:15** who went down and prayed concerning them that they might receive the Holy Spirit;

**8:16** for He had not yet fallen upon any one of them, but they had only been baptized into the name of the Lord Jesus.

**8:17** Then they laid their hands upon them, and they received the Holy Spirit.

**8:18** Now when Simon saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the hands of the apostles, he offered them money,

**8:19** saying, "Give to me also this authority, so that anyone upon whom I lay my hands may receive the Holy Spirit."

**8:20** But Peter said to him, "May your silver perish along with you, because you supposed that you could obtain the gift of God with money.

**8:21** You have no part nor lot in this matter, for your heart is not upright in the presence of God.

**8:22** Repent therefore of this wickedness of yours, and plead with the Lord that perhaps the intent of your heart may be forgiven you;

**8:23** for I perceive that you are in the gall of bitterness and in the fetter of iniquity."

**8:24** And Simon answered and said, "Plead with the Lord on my behalf, that none of the things you have spoken may come upon me."

**8:25** So after they had solemnly testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they returned toward Jerusalem, proclaiming the good news to many villages of the Samaritans.

**8:26** Now an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, "Arise and journey toward the south to the road that descends from Jerusalem to Gaza." This is a desert road.

**8:27** And he arose and went; and behold, an Ethiopian man, a eunuch, a high official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasury, had come to Jerusalem to worship,

**8:28** and he was returning; and sitting in his chariot, he was reading the prophet Isaiah.

**8:29** Then the Spirit said to Philip, "Draw near and join yourself to this chariot."

**8:30** So Philip ran up and heard him reading Isaiah the prophet, and said, "Do you truly understand what you are reading?"

**8:31** And he said, "How indeed could I, unless someone guides me?" And he urged Philip to come up and sit with him.

**8:32** Now the passage of the Scripture that he was reading was this: "Like a sheep He was led to slaughter, and like a lamb silent before its shearer, so He does not open His mouth.

**8:33** In humiliation His justice was taken away. Who shall declare His generation? For His life is taken away from the earth."

**8:34** And the eunuch answered Philip and said, "I ask you, concerning whom does the prophet say this—concerning himself, or concerning some other man?"

**8:35** Then Philip opened his mouth, and beginning from this Scripture, he proclaimed to him the good news of Jesus.

**8:36** Now as they journeyed along the road, they came to some water; and the eunuch said, "Behold, water! What prevents me from being baptized?"

**8:38** And he commanded the chariot to halt; and both of them went down into the water, Philip and the eunuch, and he baptized him.

**8:39** Now when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught Philip away, and the eunuch did not see him anymore, for he went on his way rejoicing.

**8:40** But Philip was found at Azotus; and passing through, he proclaimed the good news to all the cities until he came to Caesarea.

## Translator Notes

- **8:5** — The Greek text reads "the city of Samaria" with the definite article, likely referring to the capital city of the region, whereas some manuscripts omit the article ("a city of Samaria").
- **8:26** — The Greek phrase rendered "toward the south" can also mean "at noon." The final remark may refer either to the desert route itself or to the uninhabited site of Old Gaza.
- **8:33** — Verses 32 and 33 follow the Septuagint text of Isaiah 53:7-8 rather than the Masoretic Hebrew wording; the critical Greek text also reads "In humiliation" without "His."
- **8:36** — The earliest Greek manuscripts move directly from verse 36 to verse 38 without the later confession of faith traditionally numbered as verse 37.
- **8:37 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
