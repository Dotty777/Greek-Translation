## Text

**4:1** Now while they were speaking to the people, the priests, the captain of the temple, and the Sadducees came upon them,

**4:2** being greatly provoked because they were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.

**4:3** And they laid hands upon them and placed them in custody until the next day, for evening had already come.

**4:4** Yet many of those who heard the word believed, and the number of the men came to be about five thousand.

**4:5** And it came to pass on the next day that their rulers, elders, and scribes were assembled in Jerusalem,

**4:6** along with Annas the high priest, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and as many as belonged to the high-priestly family.

**4:7** And having set them in the midst, they inquired, "By what power or in what name did you do this?"

**4:8** Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, spoke to them: "Rulers of the people and elders,

**4:9** if we are being examined this day concerning a good deed done to an infirm man, by what means this man has been made whole,

**4:10** let it be known to you all and to all the people of Israel that by the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead—by Him this man stands before you in sound health.

**4:11** He is the stone that was despised by you, the builders, which has become the head of the corner.

**4:12** And in no other is there salvation; for neither is there any other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

**4:13** Now as they beheld the boldness of Peter and John and perceived that they were unlettered and untrained men, they marveled; and they recognized them, that they had been with Jesus.

**4:14** And seeing the man who had been healed standing there with them, they had nothing to say in opposition.

**4:15** So having ordered them to depart outside the council, they conferred among themselves,

**4:16** saying, "What shall we do with these men? For indeed, that a notable sign has taken place through them is manifest to all who dwell in Jerusalem, and we are unable to deny it.

**4:17** Yet lest it spread any further among the people, let us threaten them to speak no longer in this name to any man."

**4:18** And calling them in, they charged them neither to speak at all nor to teach in the name of Jesus.

**4:19** But Peter and John answered and said to them, "Whether it is right before God to heed you rather than God, you yourselves judge;

**4:20** for we cannot refrain from speaking of the things that we have seen and heard."

**4:21** And after threatening them further, they released them, finding no way to punish them on account of the people, because all were glorifying God for what had come to pass.

**4:22** For the man upon whom this sign of healing had been performed was more than forty years old.

**4:23** Upon being released, they went to their own companions and reported all that the chief priests and elders had spoken to them.

**4:24** And when they heard it, they lifted up their voice to God with one accord and said, "Sovereign Lord, You who made heaven and earth and the sea and all things that are in them,

**4:25** who by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of our father David, Your servant, spoke: 'Why did the nations rage, and the peoples devise vain things?

**4:26** The kings of the earth took their stand, and the rulers assembled together against the Lord and against His Anointed.'

**4:27** For truly in this city both Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel, were assembled against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed,

**4:28** to do whatever Your hand and Your counsel had determined beforehand to come to pass.

**4:29** And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and grant to Your bondservants to speak Your word with all boldness,

**4:30** as You stretch out Your hand for healing, and as signs and wonders come to pass through the name of Your holy Servant Jesus."

**4:31** And when they had prayed, the place where they were assembled was shaken; and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and continued to speak the word of God with boldness.

**4:32** Now the multitude of those who had believed were of one heart and soul, and not even one said that any of his possessions was his own, but all things were common among them.

**4:33** And with great power the apostles rendered their testimony to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.

**4:34** For neither was there any needy person among them; for as many as owned lands or houses sold them and brought the proceeds of the things that were sold,

**4:35** and laid them at the apostles' feet; and it was distributed to each one according as any had need.

**4:36** Now Joseph, who was surnamed Barnabas by the apostles—which is translated, Son of Encouragement—a Levite, a Cypriot by birth,

**4:37** having a field of his own, sold it and brought the money and laid it at the feet of the apostles.

## Translator Notes

- **4:9** — Greek sesostai ("has been made whole" or "healed") is from the same verb (sozo) translated "be saved" in 4:12, linking the man's physical restoration to spiritual salvation.
- **4:25** — The Greek text contains a compressed, grammatically difficult phrase combining "our father," "through the Holy Spirit," and "by the mouth of David Your servant," rendered here according to its evident sense.
- **4:26** — Greek tou christou autou literally means "His Anointed" or "His Christ," echoing Psalm 2:2 (LXX) and linking directly to "whom You anointed" in 4:27.
- **4:36** — The Greek critical text reads "Joseph" rather than the later Byzantine variant "Joses."
