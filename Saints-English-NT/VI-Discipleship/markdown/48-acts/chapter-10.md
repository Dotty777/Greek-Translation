## Text

**10:1** Now a certain man in Caesarea named Cornelius, a centurion of the cohort called the Italian,

**10:2** a devout man and one who feared God with all his household, giving many alms to the people and praying to God continually,

**10:3** saw plainly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming in to him and saying to him, "Cornelius."

**10:4** And fixing his eyes on him, filled with fear, he said, "What is it, Lord?" And he said to him, "Your prayers and your alms have ascended as a memorial before God.

**10:5** And now send men to Joppa, and send for a certain Simon who is called Peter.

**10:6** He is lodging with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea."

**10:7** Now when the angel who had spoken to him departed, he summoned two of his household servants and a devout soldier from among those who waited on him continually,

**10:8** and having recounted all things to them, he sent them to Joppa.

**10:9** On the following day, as they were journeying and approaching the city, Peter went up upon the housetop to pray, about the sixth hour.

**10:10** And he became very hungry and wished to eat; yet while they were making preparation, a trance came upon him,

**10:11** and he beheld heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending like a great linen sheet, being lowered by four corners to the earth,

**10:12** in which were all kinds of four-footed animals and creeping things of the earth, and birds of heaven.

**10:13** And there came a voice to him, "Arise, Peter; slay and eat."

**10:14** But Peter replied, "By no means, Lord, for I have never eaten anything common and unclean."

**10:15** And a voice came to him again the second time, "What God has made clean, you shall not call common."

**10:16** Now this occurred three times, and immediately the vessel was taken up into heaven.

**10:17** Now while Peter was greatly perplexed within himself as to what the vision he had seen might signify, behold, the men sent by Cornelius, having inquired for Simon's house, stood at the gate,

**10:18** and calling out, they inquired whether Simon, who was called Peter, was lodging there.

**10:19** Now while Peter was pondering over the vision, the Spirit said to him, "Behold, men are seeking you.

**10:20** But arise, go down, and travel with them, doubting nothing at all, for I have sent them."

**10:21** So Peter went down to the men and said, "Behold, I am the one whom you seek. What is the cause for which you are here?"

**10:22** And they said, "Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and one who fears God, well attested by the entire nation of the Jews, was divinely directed by a holy angel to send for you to his house and to hear words from you."

**10:23** Therefore he invited them in and lodged them. On the next day he arose and set out with them, and some of the brethren from Joppa went along with him.

**10:24** And on the following day he entered Caesarea. Now Cornelius was eagerly awaiting them, having gathered together his kinsmen and close friends.

**10:25** And when it came to pass that Peter entered, Cornelius met him, fell down at his feet, and worshiped him.

**10:26** But Peter raised him up, saying, "Arise; I myself am also a man."

**10:27** And as he conversed with him, he went inside and found many gathered together.

**10:28** And he said to them, "You yourselves know how unlawful it is for a Jewish man to join himself to or approach one of another nation; yet to me God has shown that I should not call any man common or unclean.

**10:29** Therefore I also came without objection when summoned. I inquire, then, for what reason you sent for me."

**10:30** And Cornelius said, "Four days ago, to this very hour, I was keeping the ninth hour of prayer in my house; and behold, a man stood before me in radiant apparel,

**10:31** and said, 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your alms have been remembered in the sight of God.

**10:32** Send therefore to Joppa and call for Simon, who is called Peter; he is lodging in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea.'

**10:33** At once, therefore, I sent to you, and you have done well in coming. Now, then, we are all present in the sight of God to hear everything that has been commanded you by the Lord."

**10:34** Then Peter opened his mouth and said, "Truly I perceive that God is not one who shows partiality,

**10:35** but in every nation he who fears Him and practices righteousness is acceptable to Him.

**10:36** As for the word which He sent to the sons of Israel, proclaiming the good news of peace through Jesus Christ—He is Lord of all—

**10:37** you yourselves know the event that came to pass throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism which John proclaimed:

**10:38** Jesus of Nazareth—how God anointed Him with the Holy Spirit and with power, who passed through doing good and healing all those oppressed by the devil, because God was with Him.

**10:39** And we are witnesses of all the things He did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem—whom they also slew, hanging Him on a tree.

**10:40** Him God raised up on the third day and granted that He should become manifest,

**10:41** not to all the people, but to witnesses appointed beforehand by God—to us, who ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead.

**10:42** And He charged us to proclaim to the people, and to bear solemn witness that He is the One ordained by God to be Judge of the living and the dead.

**10:43** To Him all the prophets bear witness, that everyone who believes in Him shall receive remission of sins through His name."

**10:44** As Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all who were hearing the word.

**10:45** And the believers from the circumcision, as many as had accompanied Peter, were amazed because upon the Gentiles also the gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out;

**10:46** for they were hearing them speak in tongues and magnify God. Then Peter responded,

**10:47** "Can anyone withhold the water, that these should not be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit even as we ourselves have?"

**10:48** And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to remain for several days.

## Translator Notes

- **10:19** — The critical Greek text reads simply "men are seeking you," omitting the numeral "three" (or "two") that appears in later manuscripts to harmonize with verses 7–8.
- **10:30** — The critical Greek text omits "fasting" (present in later Byzantine manuscripts), and the Greek idiom literally meaning "from the fourth day until this hour" refers to the corresponding hour of the day four days earlier.
- **10:36** — Verses 36–38 display loose, emphatic Greek syntax (anacoluthon), moving from the opening accusative "the word which He sent" (v. 36) to "the event that came to pass" (v. 37) and "Jesus of Nazareth" (v. 38) as Peter summarizes the gospel message.
- **10:48** — The critical Greek text reads "in the name of Jesus Christ," whereas later Byzantine manuscripts read "in the name of the Lord."
