## Text

**5:1** Now a certain man named Ananias, together with his wife Sapphira, sold a possession,

**5:2** and he kept back a portion of the proceeds, his wife also being aware of it, and bringing a certain part, he laid it at the feet of the apostles.

**5:3** Then Peter said, "Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart, that you should lie to the Holy Spirit and keep back part of the price of the land?

**5:4** While it remained, did it not remain your own? And once it was sold, was it not within your own authority? Why have you purposed this deed in your heart? You have not lied to men, but to God."

**5:5** And as Ananias heard these words, he fell down and breathed his last; and great fear fell upon all who heard.

**5:6** Then the younger men arose, wrapped him up, and carried him out and buried him.

**5:7** Now an interval of about three hours passed, and his wife came in, not knowing what had taken place.

**5:8** And Peter answered her, "Tell me, did you sell the land for such a price?" And she answered, "Yes, for such a price."

**5:9** Then Peter said to her, "Why is it that you have agreed together to test the Spirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of those who have buried your husband are at the door, and they shall carry you out."

**5:10** And instantly she fell down at his feet and breathed her last; and when the young men came in, they found her dead, and carrying her out, they buried her beside her husband.

**5:11** And great fear came upon the whole church and upon all who heard these things.

**5:12** Now through the hands of the apostles many signs and wonders were done among the people; and they were all together with one accord in Solomon's Portico.

**5:13** Yet none of the rest dared to join them, but the people magnified them,

**5:14** and all the more were believers added to the Lord, multitudes of both men and women,

**5:15** so that they even carried the sick out into the streets and laid them on cots and mats, that as Peter passed by, at least his shadow might overshadow some of them.

**5:16** Also a multitude gathered from the cities around Jerusalem, bringing the sick and those afflicted by unclean spirits, and every one of them was healed.

**5:17** Then the high priest rose up, along with all those who were with him—that is, the sect of the Sadducees—and they were filled with jealousy,

**5:18** and they laid hands upon the apostles and placed them in the public prison.

**5:19** But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the prison, and leading them out, he said,

**5:20** "Go, take your stand in the temple, and speak to the people all the words of this life."

**5:21** And when they heard this, they entered the temple at daybreak and began to teach. Now the high priest and those who were with him arrived and convened the Council and all the senate of the sons of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.

**5:22** But when the officers arrived, they did not find them in the prison; so they returned and reported,

**5:23** saying, "We found the prison locked with all security, and the guards standing at the doors; yet when we opened them, we found no one inside."

**5:24** Now when the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were greatly perplexed about them, wondering what this would come to.

**5:25** Then someone came and reported to them, "Behold, the men whom you put in the prison are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

**5:26** Then the captain went with the officers and brought them without force, for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.

**5:27** And having brought them, they set them before the Council. And the high priest questioned them,

**5:28** saying, "We strictly charged you not to teach in this name, and behold, you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and you intend to bring the blood of this man upon us."

**5:29** But Peter and the apostles answered and said, "It is necessary to obey God rather than men.

**5:30** The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you put to death by hanging Him upon a tree.

**5:31** This One God has exalted to His right hand as Prince and Savior, to grant repentance to Israel and the forgiveness of sins.

**5:32** And we are witnesses of these things, and so is the Holy Spirit, whom God has given to those who obey Him."

**5:33** Now when they heard this, they were cut to the quick and desired to slay them.

**5:34** But a certain Pharisee in the Council named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law held in honor by all the people, stood up and commanded that the men be put outside for a little while.

**5:35** And he said to them, "Men of Israel, take heed to yourselves concerning what you are about to do with these men.

**5:36** For before these days Theudas rose up, claiming himself to be somebody, and a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves to him. He was slain, and all who obeyed him were dispersed and came to nothing.

**5:37** After him Judas the Galilean rose up in the days of the census and drew away people after himself; he also perished, and all who obeyed him were scattered.

**5:38** And now I say to you, keep away from these men and leave them alone; for if this counsel or this work is of men, it shall be overthrown,

**5:39** but if it is of God, you will not be able to overthrow them—lest you even be found fighting against God." And they were persuaded by him,

**5:40** and when they had called in the apostles and beaten them, they commanded them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.

**5:41** So they departed from the presence of the Council, rejoicing that they had been counted worthy to suffer dishonor on behalf of the Name.

**5:42** And every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not cease teaching and proclaiming the good news that Jesus is the Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **5:28** — The critical Greek text reads a direct statement here rather than a question ("Did we not strictly charge you...?" in later manuscripts), underscoring the high priest's blunt accusation.
- **5:31** — The Greek dative phrase can be rendered either locatively ("to His right hand") or instrumentally ("by His right hand"), and "Prince" (Greek archegos) carries the sense of leader, pioneer, or author.
- **5:39** — The critical Greek text reads the future "you will not be able to overthrow them" (referring to the apostles), whereas later manuscripts read "you cannot overthrow it" (referring to the work).
- **5:41** — The critical Greek text reads simply "the Name" without a possessive pronoun, using it as an absolute reverential designation for Jesus.
