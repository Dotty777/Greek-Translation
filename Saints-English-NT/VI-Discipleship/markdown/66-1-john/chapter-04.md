## Text

**4:1** Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but examine the spirits, whether they are from God, for many false prophets have gone forth into the world.

**4:2** In this way you recognize the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh is from God,

**4:3** and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God; and this is the spirit of the antichrist, of which you have heard that it is coming, and even now it is already in the world.

**4:4** You are from God, little children, and have conquered them, for greater is He who is in you than he who is in the world.

**4:5** They are from the world; for this reason they speak out of the world, and the world hears them.

**4:6** We are from God; the one who knows God hears us, while the one who is not from God does not hear us. From this we recognize the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

**4:7** Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

**4:8** The one who does not love has not come to know God, because God is love.

**4:9** By this the love of God was made manifest among us: that God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, so that we might live through Him.

**4:10** In this lies love: not that we have loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as the propitiation for our sins.

**4:11** Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we ought also to love one another.

**4:12** No one has ever beheld God. If we love one another, God abides in us, and His love is brought to perfection in us.

**4:13** By this we know that we abide in Him and He in us: because He has given to us from His Spirit.

**4:14** And we ourselves have beheld and bear witness that the Father has sent the Son to be the Savior of the world.

**4:15** Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God—God abides in him, and he in God.

**4:16** And we have known and have put our faith in the love that God has toward us. God is love; and the one who abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him.

**4:17** By this means love has been brought to perfection with us, so that we may have confidence in the day of judgment, because just as He is, so also are we in this world.

**4:18** There is no fear in love; rather, perfect love drives out fear, because fear carries punishment, and the one who fears has not been brought to perfection in love.

**4:19** We love, because He loved us first.

**4:20** If someone says, "I love God," yet hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen is unable to love God whom he has not seen.

**4:21** And we have this commandment from Him: that the one who loves God must also love his brother.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read simply "does not confess Jesus," whereas later manuscripts expand the clause to echo verse 2 ("Jesus Christ having come in the flesh").
- **4:9** — The Greek adjective monogenes, traditionally rendered "only begotten," emphasizes Christ's status as the unique, one-and-only Son of God.
- **4:19** — The Greek critical text omits an explicit object ("Him") after "We love," presenting love broadly as the believer's response to God's prior love.
