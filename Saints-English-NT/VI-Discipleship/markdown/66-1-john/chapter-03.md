## Text

**3:1** Behold what manner of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God—and so we are! For this reason the world does not know us, because it did not know Him.

**3:2** Beloved, now we are children of God, and it has not yet been manifested what we shall be. We know that when He is manifested, we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him just as He is.

**3:3** And everyone who has this hope set on Him purifies himself, even as He is pure.

**3:4** Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness; and sin is lawlessness.

**3:5** And you know that He was manifested that He might take away sins, and in Him there is no sin.

**3:6** Everyone who abides in Him does not sin; everyone who sins has neither seen Him nor come to know Him.

**3:7** Little children, let no one lead you astray. He who does righteousness is righteous, even as He is righteous.

**3:8** He who commits sin is of the devil, for from the beginning the devil has been sinning. To this end the Son of God was manifested, that He might destroy the works of the devil.

**3:9** Everyone who has been born of God does not commit sin, for His seed abides in him; and he is not able to sin, because he has been born of God.

**3:10** By this the children of God and the children of the devil are made manifest: everyone who does not do righteousness is not of God, nor is he who does not love his brother.

**3:11** For this is the message which you heard from the beginning: that we are to love one another,

**3:12** not as Cain, who was of the wicked one and slew his brother. And for what reason did he slay him? Because his own works were evil, while those of his brother were righteous.

**3:13** Do not marvel, brethren, if the world hates you.

**3:14** We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brethren. He who does not love abides in death.

**3:15** Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding within him.

**3:16** By this we have come to know love: that He laid down His life on our behalf. And we ourselves ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.

**3:17** But whoever possesses the world's goods, and beholds his brother in need, yet closes his heart against him, how does the love of God abide in him?

**3:18** Little children, let us not love in word nor with the tongue, but in deed and in truth.

**3:19** By this we shall know that we are of the truth, and we shall reassure our hearts before Him

**3:20** in whatever our heart may condemn us, for God is greater than our heart and knows all things.

**3:21** Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have boldness toward God,

**3:22** and whatever we ask we receive from Him, for we keep His commandments and do those things which are pleasing before Him.

**3:23** And this is His commandment: that we should believe in the name of His Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as He gave commandment to us.

**3:24** And he who keeps His commandments abides in Him, and He in him. And by this we know that He abides in us: by the Spirit whom He has given to us.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The critical Greek text includes the emphatic clause kai esmen ("and so we are"), affirming the present reality of believers' adoption as God's children.
- **3:2** — Greek ean phanerothe has no explicit subject and may be rendered either "when He is manifested" (matching 2:28) or "when it is manifested" (referring to what believers shall be).
- **3:6** — The Greek present-tense forms of hamartano ("sin") here and in 3:8-9 are widely understood to describe persistent, habitual sinning rather than a claim to sinless perfection (cf. 1:8-2:1).
- **3:20** — The Greek contains two occurrences of hoti; following standard critical grammar, the first is taken with ean as indefinite ("in whatever") and the second as causal ("for"), linking 3:20 directly to 3:19.
