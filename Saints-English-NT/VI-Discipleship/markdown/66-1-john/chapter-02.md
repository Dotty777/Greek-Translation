## Text

**2:1** My little children, these things I write to you, that you may not sin. And if anyone should sin, we have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.

**2:2** And He Himself is the propitiation for our sins, yet not for ours alone, but also for the whole world.

**2:3** And in this we know that we have known Him, if we keep His commandments.

**2:4** The one who says, "I have known Him," yet does not keep His commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him.

**2:5** But whoever keeps His word, truly in him the love of God has been brought to perfection. By this we know that we are in Him:

**2:6** he who says he abides in Him ought himself to walk even as He walked.

**2:7** Beloved, it is no new commandment that I write to you, but an old commandment which you had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which you heard.

**2:8** Yet again, I write to you a new commandment, which is true in Him and in you, for the darkness is passing away and the true light already shines.

**2:9** He who says he is in the light, yet hates his brother, is in the darkness even until now.

**2:10** He who loves his brother abides in the light, and in him there is no occasion for stumbling.

**2:11** But he who hates his brother is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and he does not know where he goes, for the darkness has blinded his eyes.

**2:12** I write to you, little children, because your sins have been forgiven you for the sake of His name.

**2:13** I write to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning. I write to you, young men, because you have overcome the wicked one.

**2:14** I have written to you, children, because you have come to know the Father. I have written to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God abides in you, and you have overcome the wicked one.

**2:15** Do not love the world, nor the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in him;

**2:16** for everything in the world—the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the arrogance of life—is not from the Father, but is from the world.

**2:17** And the world is passing away, along with its lust; yet he who does the will of God abides forever.

**2:18** Children, it is the last hour; and just as you heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have arisen, by which we know that it is the last hour.

**2:19** They went out from us, yet they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have remained with us; rather, they departed that they might be made manifest, that none of them are of us.

**2:20** And you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know.

**2:21** I did not write to you because you do not know the truth, but because you know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.

**2:22** Who is the liar, except he who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This one is the antichrist, he who denies the Father and the Son.

**2:23** Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either; he who confesses the Son has the Father as well.

**2:24** As for you, let that which you heard from the beginning abide in you. If what you heard from the beginning abides in you, you also shall abide in the Son and in the Father.

**2:25** And this is the promise which He Himself promised to us: eternal life.

**2:26** These things I have written to you concerning those who seek to lead you astray.

**2:27** And as for you, the anointing which you received from Him abides in you, and you have no need that anyone should teach you; rather, as His anointing teaches you concerning all things—and it is true, and is no lie—just as it has taught you, abide in Him.

**2:28** And now, little children, abide in Him, so that when He is manifested we may have boldness and not shrink in shame from Him at His coming.

**2:29** If you know that He is righteous, you know that everyone who does righteousness has also been born of Him.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Greek parakletos ("Advocate") denotes one called alongside to plead on another's behalf; in the Gospel of John the same term designates the Holy Spirit.
- **2:2** — Greek hilasmos ("propitiation") refers to an atoning sacrifice that turns away wrath and removes the guilt of sin.
- **2:14** — Following the critical Greek verse division, "I have written to you, children" begins 2:14 rather than closing 2:13, marking a shift from present-tense "I write" (grapho) to aorist "I have written" (egrapsa) and from teknia ("little children") to paidia ("children").
- **2:20** — The critical Greek text reads pantes ("you all know") rather than panta ("you know all things").
