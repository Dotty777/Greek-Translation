## Text

**5:1** Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God, and everyone who loves the One who gives birth loves also the one who has been born of Him.

**5:2** In this way we know that we love the children of God: whenever we love God and keep His commandments.

**5:3** For this is what the love of God is, that we keep His commandments; and His commandments are not burdensome,

**5:4** because all that has been born of God conquers the world. And this is the victory that has conquered the world: our faith.

**5:5** And who is the one who conquers the world, except the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

**5:6** This is the One who came by water and blood—Jesus Christ; not by the water only, but by the water and by the blood. And it is the Spirit who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth.

**5:7** For there are three who bear witness:

**5:8** the Spirit and the water and the blood, and the three agree as one.

**5:9** If we accept the testimony of men, the testimony of God is greater; for this is the testimony of God, that He has borne witness concerning His Son.

**5:10** The one who believes in the Son of God has the testimony within himself; the one who does not believe God has made Him a liar, because he has not believed in the testimony that God has borne concerning His Son.

**5:11** And the testimony is this: that God gave eternal life to us, and this life is in His Son.

**5:12** The one who has the Son has the life; the one who does not have the Son of God does not have the life.

**5:13** I have written these things to you, that you may know that you possess eternal life—to you who believe in the name of the Son of God.

**5:14** And this is the boldness that we have before Him: that if we ask anything according to His will, He listens to us.

**5:15** And if we know that He listens to us in whatever we ask, we know that we possess the requests we have asked from Him.

**5:16** If anyone sees his brother committing a sin that does not lead to death, he shall ask, and He will give him life—to those who commit sin not leading to death. There is a sin leading to death; I do not say that he should pray concerning that.

**5:17** All unrighteousness is sin, yet there is sin that does not lead to death.

**5:18** We know that everyone who has been born of God does not sin; rather, the One who was born of God keeps him, and the evil one does not touch him.

**5:19** We know that we are from God, and the entire world lies under the power of the evil one.

**5:20** And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know the True One; and we are in the True One, in His Son Jesus Christ. This One is the true God and eternal life.

**5:21** Little children, guard yourselves from idols.

## Translator Notes

- **5:7** — In the earliest Greek manuscripts, verses 7 and 8 name only the three witnesses (the Spirit, the water, and the blood); the Trinitarian expansion ("the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit," known as the Comma Johanneum) entered Greek manuscripts only in the late medieval period from Latin tradition.
- **5:16** — The identity of "a sin leading to death" is debated (often understood as hardened apostasy or final rejection of Christ), and the unstated subject of "He will give him life" is ordinarily understood to be God.
- **5:18** — The Greek critical text distinguishes the believer ("everyone who has been born of God," perfect participle) from Christ ("the One who was born of God," aorist participle) who "keeps him" (auton), rather than the later variant "keeps himself" (heauton).
- **5:20** — "This One" (houtos) most naturally refers to its nearest antecedent, "Jesus Christ," affirming His full deity, though some interpreters take it to refer back to "the True One" (God the Father).
