## Text

**3:1** Finally, my brethren, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is not wearisome to me, and for you it is a safeguard.

**3:2** Beware of the dogs, beware of the wicked workers, beware of the mutilation.

**3:3** For we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of God, and glory in Christ Jesus, and place no confidence in the flesh,

**3:4** though I myself also have reason for confidence in the flesh. If anyone else thinks he may trust in the flesh, I even more:

**3:5** circumcised on the eighth day, of the lineage of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee;

**3:6** as to zeal, persecuting the church; as to the righteousness that is in the law, proved blameless.

**3:7** Yet whatever things were gains to me, these I have reckoned as loss for the sake of Christ.

**3:8** More than that, I also reckon all things to be loss on account of the surpassing worth of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, for whose sake I have suffered the loss of all things, and I consider them as refuse, so that I may gain Christ

**3:9** and be found in Him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God resting upon faith,

**3:10** that I may know Him, and the power of His resurrection, and fellowship in His sufferings, becoming conformed to His death,

**3:11** if by any means I may reach the resurrection from among the dead.

**3:12** Not that I have already obtained it, or have already been made perfect; rather, I press forward, if indeed I may take hold of that for which I was also taken hold of by Christ Jesus.

**3:13** Brethren, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of it; but one thing I do: forgetting the things that lie behind and straining forward to the things that lie ahead,

**3:14** I press onward toward the goal for the prize of God's upward calling in Christ Jesus.

**3:15** Therefore, as many of us as are mature, let us be of this mind; and if in anything you think otherwise, God will reveal this also to you.

**3:16** Nevertheless, to the measure we have already attained, let us walk in the same way.

**3:17** Brethren, join in imitating me, and keep your eyes on those who walk in this way, just as you have us for a pattern.

**3:18** For many walk, of whom I often spoke to you, and now tell you even weeping, as the enemies of the cross of Christ:

**3:19** whose end is destruction, whose god is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame—those whose minds are fixed upon earthly things.

**3:20** For our citizenship exists in heaven, from which we also eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,

**3:21** who shall transform the body of our humiliation to be conformed to the body of His glory, according to the working by which He is able even to subject all things to Himself.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek critical text reads "by the Spirit of God" (pneumati theou), whereas later manuscripts read "worship God in spirit."
- **3:8** — The Greek term skybala denotes refuse, scraps, or dung, stressing the utter worthlessness of Paul's former credentials compared with knowing Christ.
- **3:9** — The phrase "faith in Christ" (pisteos Christou) can also be translated "the faithfulness of Christ," and interpreters debate whether Christ is the object or the subject of this faith.
- **3:16** — The Greek critical text preserves a shorter reading here without the later expansion "by the same rule, let us be of the same mind"; the verb stoichein means to keep in step or walk in line with.
- **3:20** — The Greek noun politeuma means "citizenship" or "commonwealth," a term of special resonance for believers living in the Roman colony of Philippi.
