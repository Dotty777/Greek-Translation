## Text

**3:1** And I, brethren, could not speak to you as to spiritual men, but as to men of flesh, as to infants in Christ.

**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able; indeed, even now you are still not able,

**3:3** for you are still carnal. For where jealousy and strife exist among you, are you not carnal, and walking as mere men?

**3:4** For when one says, "I am of Paul," and another, "I am of Apollos," are you not merely men?

**3:5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, even as the Lord gave to each one.

**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God was giving the growth.

**3:7** Therefore neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but only God who gives the growth.

**3:8** Now he who plants and he who waters are one, yet each shall receive his own reward according to his own labor.

**3:9** For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's cultivated field, you are God's building.

**3:10** According to the grace of God given to me, as a skilled master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds upon it. Yet let each one take heed how he builds upon it.

**3:11** For no one is able to lay any other foundation than that which is already laid, which is Jesus Christ.

**3:12** Now if anyone builds upon the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,

**3:13** each one's work shall become manifest; for the Day shall disclose it, because it is revealed in fire, and the fire itself shall test what sort each one's work is.

**3:14** If anyone's work that he has built upon it abides, he shall receive a reward.

**3:15** If anyone's work is burned up, he shall suffer loss; yet he himself shall be saved, though so as through fire.

**3:16** Do you not know that you are God's temple, and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?

**3:17** If anyone destroys the temple of God, God shall destroy him; for the temple of God is holy, and that temple you are.

**3:18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks himself to be wise in this age, let him become foolish, so that he may become wise.

**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God; for it is written, "He catches the wise in their craftiness,"

**3:20** and again, "The Lord knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are futile."

**3:21** Therefore let no one boast in men. For all things belong to you:

**3:22** whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come—all things are yours,

**3:23** and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Paul here uses sarkinois ("of flesh," pointing to immaturity), shifting in 3:3 to sarkikoi ("carnal," pointing to fleshly behavior marked by jealousy and strife).
- **3:5** — Paul deliberately employs the neuter pronoun "What" (ti) rather than "Who," emphasizing the instrumental role of apostles as servants rather than rival patrons.
- **3:9** — "God's fellow workers" (theou synergoi) can mean either fellow laborers who belong to God or co-workers who labor together with God.
- **3:13** — "The Day" refers to the final Day of the Lord, when the true quality of each builder's work will be disclosed and tested.
