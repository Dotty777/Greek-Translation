## Text

**11:1** Become imitators of me, even as I also am of Christ.

**11:2** Now I praise you because in all things you remember me and hold firmly to the traditions just as I delivered them to you.

**11:3** But I desire you to know that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of a woman is man, and the head of Christ is God.

**11:4** Every man who prays or prophesies with his head covered puts his head to shame;

**11:5** but every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered puts her head to shame, for it is one and the same as having her head shaved.

**11:6** For if a woman does not cover herself, let her also be shorn; yet if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or to be shaved, let her cover herself.

**11:7** For a man truly ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory of God; whereas the woman is the glory of man.

**11:8** For man is not from woman, but rather woman from man;

**11:9** and indeed man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man.

**11:10** For this reason the woman ought to have authority upon her head, because of the angels.

**11:11** Nevertheless, neither is woman apart from man, nor is man apart from woman, in the Lord;

**11:12** for just as the woman came from the man, so also does the man come through the woman; yet all things are from God.

**11:13** Judge among yourselves: is it fitting for a woman to pray to God uncovered?

**11:14** Does not even nature itself instruct you that if a man wears long hair, it is a dishonor to him,

**11:15** whereas if a woman wears long hair, it is a glory to her? For her long hair has been given to her in place of a covering.

**11:16** Yet if anyone is inclined to be contentious, we hold no such custom, nor do the churches of God.

**11:17** Now in giving this charge I do not commend you, because you assemble not for the better, but for the worse.

**11:18** For in the first place, when you come together as a church, I hear that divisions exist among you, and in part I believe it.

**11:19** For there must indeed be factions among you, in order that those who are approved may also become manifest among you.

**11:20** When you assemble together in one place, therefore, it is not the Lord's Supper that you eat,

**11:21** for in the meal each one takes his own supper beforehand, and one remains hungry while another is drunk.

**11:22** What! Do you not have houses in which to eat and drink? Or do you despise the church of God and put to shame those who have nothing? What shall I say to you? Shall I commend you? In this I do not commend you.

**11:23** For I myself received from the Lord what I also handed down to you, that the Lord Jesus, on the night in which He was betrayed, took bread,

**11:24** and after He had given thanks, He broke it and said, "This is My body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of Me."

**11:25** In like manner He also took the cup after supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood; do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of Me."

**11:26** For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the death of the Lord until He comes.

**11:27** Therefore, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner shall be guilty concerning the body and the blood of the Lord.

**11:28** But let a man test himself, and so let him eat from the bread and drink from the cup;

**11:29** for whoever eats and drinks, without discerning the body, eats and drinks judgment upon himself.

**11:30** For this reason many among you are weak and ill, and not a few sleep.

**11:31** But if we discerned ourselves rightly, we would not be judged;

**11:32** but when we are judged by the Lord, we are chastened, lest we be condemned together with the world.

**11:33** Therefore, my brethren, when you assemble to eat, wait for one another.

**11:34** If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, lest you assemble for judgment. As for the rest, I shall set them in order when I come.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — Greek aner and gyne can mean either "man/woman" or "husband/wife," and kephale ("head") is debated as signifying authority, source, or prominence.
- **11:10** — Greek exousian literally means "authority," often understood either as a sign of authority on the woman's head or as her own authority to pray and prophesy; the meaning of "because of the angels" is also debated.
- **11:15** — Greek anti peribolaiou can be understood either as "in place of a covering" (substitutional) or "as a covering" (functional equivalent).
- **11:24** — The critical Greek text reads simply "which is for you," without the later Byzantine additions "Take, eat" and "broken" found in some manuscripts.
- **11:29** — The critical Greek text omits "in an unworthy manner" and "of the Lord," leaving "the body" open to referring to Christ's sacrificed body, the church as His body, or both.
