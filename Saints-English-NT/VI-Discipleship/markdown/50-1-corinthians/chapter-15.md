## Text

**15:1** Now I make known to you, brethren, the gospel which I proclaimed to you, which you also received, in which you also stand,

**15:2** through which you are also being saved, if you hold fast the word that I proclaimed to you—unless indeed you believed in vain.

**15:3** For I handed down to you as of foremost importance what I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,

**15:4** and that He was buried, and that He has been raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,

**15:5** and that He appeared to Cephas, and then to the twelve;

**15:6** after that He appeared to over five hundred brethren at one time, the greater part of whom remain to this present time, though some have fallen asleep;

**15:7** afterward He appeared to James, and then to all the apostles;

**15:8** and last of all, as to an untimely birth, He appeared to me also.

**15:9** For I am the least of the apostles, unfit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.

**15:10** Yet by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace toward me did not prove in vain; on the contrary, I labored more abundantly than all of them—yet not I, but the grace of God with me.

**15:11** Whether then it is I or they, thus we preach, and thus you believed.

**15:12** Now if it is preached of Christ that He has been raised from the dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?

**15:13** Yet if there is no resurrection of the dead, neither has Christ been raised;

**15:14** and if Christ has not been raised, then empty is our preaching, and empty also is your faith;

**15:15** moreover, we are also found to be false witnesses of God, because we bore witness concerning God that He raised up Christ—whom He did not raise, if indeed the dead are not raised.

**15:16** For if the dead are not raised, neither has Christ been raised;

**15:17** and if Christ has not been raised, your faith is fruitless; you are still in your sins.

**15:18** Then indeed those who fell asleep in Christ have perished.

**15:19** If in this life alone we have placed our hope in Christ, we are more to be pitied than all men.

**15:20** Yet now Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.

**15:21** For since death came through a man, through a Man also comes the resurrection of the dead;

**15:22** for just as in Adam all die, so also in Christ shall all be made alive.

**15:23** Yet each one in his own order: Christ the firstfruits, and afterward those who belong to Christ at His coming;

**15:24** then comes the end, when He delivers up the kingdom to God the Father, when He has abolished every rule and every authority and power.

**15:25** For He must reign until He has placed all enemies beneath His feet.

**15:26** The last enemy to be abolished is death.

**15:27** For "He has subjected all things beneath His feet." Yet when it says, "All things have been subjected," it is evident that this is apart from the One who subjected all things to Him.

**15:28** And when all things have been subjected to Him, then the Son Himself shall also be subjected to the One who subjected all things to Him, that God may be all in all.

**15:29** Otherwise, what will those do who are baptized on behalf of the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why then are they baptized on their behalf?

**15:30** And why do we also stand in peril every hour?

**15:31** Daily I die—I affirm it by the boasting in you, brethren, which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**15:32** If from human motives I fought with wild beasts in Ephesus, what gain is it to me? If the dead are not raised, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."

**15:33** Do not be led astray: "Evil associations corrupt good character."

**15:34** Awake to soberness as is right, and do not go on sinning; for some have no knowledge of God. To your shame I speak this.

**15:35** Yet someone will say, "How are the dead raised, and with what sort of body do they come?"

**15:36** Foolish one! That which you yourself sow is not made alive unless it dies;

**15:37** and what you sow, you do not sow the body that shall be, but a bare kernel—perhaps of wheat, or of some other seed;

**15:38** yet God gives it a body just as He has willed, and to each of the seeds its own body.

**15:39** Not all flesh is the same flesh, but there is one kind of men, another flesh of beasts, another flesh of birds, and another of fish.

**15:40** There are both heavenly bodies and earthly bodies; yet one is the glory of the heavenly, and another is that of the earthly.

**15:41** One is the glory of the sun, and another the glory of the moon, and another the glory of the stars; for star differs from star in glory.

**15:42** Thus it is also with the resurrection of the dead: it is sown in corruption, and it is raised in incorruption;

**15:43** it is sown in dishonor, and it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, and it is raised in power;

**15:44** it is sown a natural body, and it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is a spiritual body as well.

**15:45** Thus also it stands written: "The first man, Adam, became a living soul"; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.

**15:46** Yet the spiritual is not first, but rather the natural, and afterward the spiritual.

**15:47** The first man is from the earth, made of dust; the second Man is from heaven.

**15:48** As is the one made of dust, so also are those who are made of dust; and as is the heavenly One, so also are those who are heavenly.

**15:49** And just as we have borne the image of the one made of dust, let us also bear the image of the heavenly One.

**15:50** Now this I declare, brethren, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, neither does corruption inherit incorruption.

**15:51** Behold, I speak a mystery to you: we shall not all fall asleep, yet all of us shall be changed,

**15:52** in an instant, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet; for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we ourselves shall be changed.

**15:53** For this which is corruptible must clothe itself with incorruption, and this which is mortal must clothe itself with immortality.

**15:54** And when this which is corruptible has clothed itself with incorruption, and this which is mortal has clothed itself with immortality, then shall come to pass the word that stands written: "Death has been swallowed up in victory."

**15:55** "Where, O Death, is your victory? Where, O Death, is your sting?"

**15:56** Now the sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law;

**15:57** yet thanks be to God, who grants us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15:58** Therefore, my beloved brethren, be steadfast, unshakable, abounding in the work of the Lord at all times, knowing that your toil is not in vain in the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **15:29** — The precise practice behind being baptized on behalf of the dead is widely debated among interpreters, ranging from proxy baptism for departed believers to baptism undertaken in hope of reunion with deceased Christians. In the second question, the Greek critical text reads "on their behalf" rather than repeating "for the dead."
- **15:47** — The Greek critical text reads simply "the second Man is from heaven," without the later Byzantine addition "the Lord."
- **15:49** — The Greek text reads the hortatory subjunctive ("let us also bear"), attested by the majority of early manuscripts, while other witnesses read the future indicative ("we shall also bear").
- **15:55** — Following the earliest Greek manuscripts, this verse places "victory" before "sting" and addresses "Death" in both clauses rather than "Hades" in the second.
