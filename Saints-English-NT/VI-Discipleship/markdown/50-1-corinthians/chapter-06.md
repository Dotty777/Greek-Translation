## Text

**6:1** Does any one of you, when he has a dispute against another, dare to go to law before the unrighteous, and not before the saints?

**6:2** Or do you not know that the saints shall judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you unworthy to settle the smallest cases?

**6:3** Do you not know that we shall judge angels—how much more the affairs of this life?

**6:4** If, therefore, you have cases concerning the affairs of this life, do you seat as judges those who are of no account in the church?

**6:5** I speak this to your shame. Can it be that there is not even one wise man among you who will be able to decide between his brethren,

**6:6** but instead brother goes to law against brother, and that before unbelievers?

**6:7** In fact, it is already altogether a defeat for you that you have lawsuits with one another. Why do you not rather suffer wrong? Why do you not rather let yourselves be defrauded?

**6:8** Instead, you yourselves do wrong and defraud, and that to your own brethren.

**6:9** Or do you not know that the unrighteous shall not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor the effeminate, nor men who lie with men,

**6:10** nor thieves, nor the covetous, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor extortioners shall inherit the kingdom of God.

**6:11** And such were some of you; yet you were washed clean, yet you were sanctified, yet you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and in the Spirit of our God.

**6:12** All things are lawful for me, yet not all things are profitable; all things are lawful for me, yet I will not be mastered by anything.

**6:13** Foods are for the stomach, and the stomach is for foods; yet God will bring both it and them to nothing. Now the body is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body;

**6:14** and God has both raised the Lord and shall also raise us up through His power.

**6:15** Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Shall I therefore take the members of Christ and make them into members of a harlot? May it never be!

**6:16** Or do you not know that he who unites himself to a harlot is one body with her? For He says, "The two shall become one flesh."

**6:17** Yet he who unites himself to the Lord is one spirit with Him.

**6:18** Flee from sexual immorality! Every sin that a man may commit is outside the body, yet he who practices sexual immorality sins against his own body.

**6:19** Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? And you are not your own,

**6:20** for you were purchased with a price. Therefore glorify God in your body.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Following the punctuation of the Greek text, the verb is rendered as a question ("do you seat...?"), though some interpreters read it as an ironic imperative ("appoint them!").
- **6:9** — The Greek uses two distinct terms: malakoi ("the effeminate," referring in antiquity to the passive partner in male same-sex acts) and arsenokoitai ("men who lie with men," a compound reflecting Lev 18:22 and 20:13 LXX).
- **6:12** — The repeated assertion "All things are lawful for me" (along with "Foods are for the stomach, and the stomach is for foods" in 6:13) is widely understood as echoing slogans circulating in Corinth that Paul qualifies and corrects.
- **6:20** — The Saints Greek text ends at "in your body," omitting the later manuscript addition "and in your spirit, which are God's."
