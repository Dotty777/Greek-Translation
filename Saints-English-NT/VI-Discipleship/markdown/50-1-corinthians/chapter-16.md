## Text

**16:1** Now concerning the collection for the saints, just as I instructed the churches of Galatia, so you must do also.

**16:2** On the first day of every week, let each one of you set something aside by himself, storing up according as he may prosper, lest collections be made when I come.

**16:3** And when I arrive, whomever you approve by letters, these I will send to carry your gracious gift to Jerusalem;

**16:4** and if it is fitting for me to go as well, they shall travel with me.

**16:5** Now I will come to you once I have passed through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia,

**16:6** and perhaps I shall remain with you, or even pass the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I may go.

**16:7** For I do not wish to see you now in passing, for I hope to abide with you for some time, if the Lord permits.

**16:8** But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost;

**16:9** for a wide and effective door stands open to me, and there are many adversaries.

**16:10** Now if Timothy comes, see to it that he is with you without fear, for he performs the work of the Lord, just as I do.

**16:11** Let no one therefore despise him. Rather, send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am awaiting him with the brethren.

**16:12** Now concerning Apollos our brother, I urged him greatly to come to you with the brethren; yet it was not at all his will to come now, but he will come when he has an opportunity.

**16:13** Keep watch, stand steadfast in the faith, be valiant, be strong.

**16:14** Let everything you do be done in love.

**16:15** Now I exhort you, brethren—you know the household of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have dedicated themselves to the service of the saints—

**16:16** that you also submit yourselves to such ones, and to every fellow worker and laborer.

**16:17** Now I rejoice over the arrival of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus, for these men have made up for what was lacking on your part,

**16:18** for they refreshed both my spirit and yours. Therefore recognize such men.

**16:19** The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the church in their house.

**16:20** All the brethren send you their greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**16:21** This greeting is by my own hand—Paul's.

**16:22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. Maranatha.

**16:23** May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

**16:24** My love is with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The Greek phrase "by letters" may modify either "approve" (as punctuated in the Greek text) or "send," yielding "those whom you approve I will send with letters."
- **16:12** — Literally "there was not at all a will," which is ambiguous in Greek and may refer either to Apollos's own inclination or to the will of God.
- **16:13** — The Greek verb rendered "be valiant" literally means "act like men," an idiom for displaying courage and steadfast resolve.
- **16:22** — "Maranatha" transliterates an Aramaic expression left untranslated in the Greek; divided as in this text, it is a prayer meaning "Our Lord, come!" (if divided differently, it can mean "Our Lord has come").
