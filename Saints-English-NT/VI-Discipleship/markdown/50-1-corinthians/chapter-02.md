## Text

**2:1** And I, when I came to you, brethren, did not come with loftiness of speech or of wisdom, declaring to you the testimony of God.

**2:2** For I resolved to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.

**2:3** And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling,

**2:4** and my speech and my proclamation were not in persuasive words of wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power,

**2:5** so that your faith might not rest in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.

**2:6** Yet we do speak wisdom among the mature—a wisdom, however, not of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing.

**2:7** Rather, we speak God's wisdom in a mystery, the hidden wisdom, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory,

**2:8** which none of the rulers of this age has understood; for if they had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

**2:9** But just as it is written, "Things which no eye has seen, and no ear has heard, and which have not entered the heart of man—the things God has prepared for those who love Him."

**2:10** For to us God revealed them through the Spirit; for the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God.

**2:11** For who among men knows the things of a man except the spirit of the man that is within him? In the same way also, no one has known the things of God except the Spirit of God.

**2:12** Now we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we may know the things freely bestowed upon us by God.

**2:13** These things we also speak, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Spirit, interpreting spiritual things to the spiritual.

**2:14** But the natural man does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him; and he is not able to understand them, because they are spiritually discerned.

**2:15** Yet the spiritual man examines all things, while he himself is examined by no one.

**2:16** For "who has known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct Him?" Yet we possess the mind of Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek text reads "the testimony of God" (to martyrion tou theou); an ancient manuscript variant reads "the mystery of God" (to mysterion tou theou).
- **2:13** — The Greek participial clause (pneumatikois pneumatika synkrinontes) is syntactically open and may also be rendered "combining spiritual truths with spiritual words" or "comparing spiritual things with spiritual."
- **2:14** — "The natural man" translates Greek psychikos anthropos (literally "soulish man"), denoting a person who lives solely by unrenewed human faculties without the Spirit of God.
