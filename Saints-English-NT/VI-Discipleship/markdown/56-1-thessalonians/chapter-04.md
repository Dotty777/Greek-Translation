## Text

**4:1** Finally then, brethren, we ask and exhort you in the Lord Jesus, that as you received from us how you ought to walk and to please God—just as indeed you are walking—you would abound still more.

**4:2** For you know what commands we delivered to you through the Lord Jesus.

**4:3** For this is God's will, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality,

**4:4** that every one of you know how to possess his own vessel in sanctification and honor,

**4:5** not in passionate lust, even as the Gentiles who do not know God,

**4:6** that no one transgress and defraud his brother in this matter, because the Lord is an avenger concerning all these things, just as we also told you beforehand and solemnly testified.

**4:7** For God did not call us for uncleanness, but in sanctification.

**4:8** Consequently, he who rejects this does not reject man, but God, who also gives His Holy Spirit to you.

**4:9** Now concerning brotherly love, you have no need that we write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another;

**4:10** for indeed you practice it toward all the brethren throughout all Macedonia. Yet we exhort you, brethren, to abound still more,

**4:11** and to make it your ambition to live quietly, to attend to your own affairs, and to labor with your own hands, just as we commanded you,

**4:12** so that you may walk honorably toward those who are outside, and be in need of nothing.

**4:13** Now we do not want you to be unaware, brethren, concerning those who sleep, lest you sorrow even as the rest do, who have no hope.

**4:14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so also God, through Jesus, will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep.

**4:15** For this we say to you by a word of the Lord, that we who live, who remain until the coming of the Lord, shall by no means precede those who have fallen asleep.

**4:16** For the Lord Himself, with a shout of command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God, shall descend from heaven; and the dead in Christ shall rise first.

**4:17** Then we who live, who remain, shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air; and so we shall always be with the Lord.

**4:18** So then, comfort one another with these words.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek phrase rendered "possess his own vessel" is debated, understood either as exercising self-control over one's own body or as taking and living with one's own wife in holiness.
- **4:8** — The critical Greek text reads the present participle "gives" and "to you," emphasizing God's ongoing gift of the Spirit, whereas some later manuscripts read the past tense "has given" and "to us."
- **4:14** — In Greek, "through Jesus" stands between "those who have fallen asleep" and "will bring," and may modify either God's act of bringing them or those who have fallen asleep in Jesus.
