## Text

**1:1** Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace and peace to you.

**1:2** We give thanks to God at all times for all of you, making mention of you in our prayers, unceasingly

**1:3** calling to mind your work of faith, your labor of love, and your endurance of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ in the presence of our God and Father,

**1:4** knowing, brethren beloved by God, your election,

**1:5** because our gospel did not come to you in word alone, but also in power, and in the Holy Spirit, and in abundant assurance, just as you know what manner of men we proved to be among you for your sake.

**1:6** And you yourselves became imitators of us and of the Lord, having received the word amid great affliction with the joy of the Holy Spirit,

**1:7** so that you became patterns to all who believe in Macedonia and in Achaia.

**1:8** For from you the word of the Lord has resounded, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place your faith toward God has gone forth, so that we have no need to speak anything.

**1:9** For they themselves declare concerning us what manner of entrance we had to you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve a living and true God,

**1:10** and to await His Son from the heavens, whom He raised from the dead—Jesus, who rescues us from the coming wrath.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The critical Greek text preserves the shorter greeting without the later scribal expansion 'from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.'
- **1:4** — The Greek syntax connects 'by God' directly with 'beloved' ('brethren beloved by God') rather than with 'your election.'
- **1:7** — The critical text reads the plural 'patterns' (τύπους), whereas some manuscripts read the singular 'pattern' (τύπον).
