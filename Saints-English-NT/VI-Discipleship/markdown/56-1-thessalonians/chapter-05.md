## Text

**5:1** Now concerning the times and the seasons, brethren, you have no need for anything to be written to you,

**5:2** for you yourselves know full well that the day of the Lord comes just as a thief in the night.

**5:3** When they say, "Peace and security!" then sudden destruction comes upon them, as birth pangs upon a woman with child; and they shall by no means escape.

**5:4** Yet you, brethren, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you like a thief.

**5:5** For all of you are sons of light and sons of day; we are not of the night, nor of darkness.

**5:6** So then, let us not sleep as the rest do, but let us keep watch and be sober.

**5:7** For those who sleep, sleep by night, and those who become drunk are drunk by night.

**5:8** Yet as those who are of the day, let us be sober, having put on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.

**5:9** For God did not appoint us for wrath, but for the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

**5:10** who died on our behalf, so that whether we are awake or sleep, we may live together with Him.

**5:11** Therefore comfort one another and build each other up, just as indeed you are doing.

**5:12** Now we ask you, brethren, to acknowledge those who labor among you, who preside over you in the Lord and admonish you,

**5:13** and to esteem them beyond measure in love on account of their work. Live in peace among yourselves.

**5:14** And we exhort you, brethren: admonish the disorderly, console the fainthearted, sustain the weak, be longsuffering toward all.

**5:15** See to it that no one repays evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good, both toward one another and toward all.

**5:16** Always rejoice,

**5:17** unceasingly pray,

**5:18** in everything give thanks; for this is God's will in Christ Jesus concerning you.

**5:19** Do not extinguish the Spirit;

**5:20** do not treat prophecies with contempt;

**5:21** but test all things; hold fast to what is good;

**5:22** refrain from every kind of evil.

**5:23** Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you wholly, and may your entire spirit and soul and body be preserved without blame at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**5:24** Faithful is He who calls you, who will also perform it.

**5:25** Brethren, pray on our behalf.

**5:26** Salute all the brethren with a holy kiss.

**5:27** I put you under oath by the Lord that this epistle be read to all the brethren.

**5:28** May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

## Translator Notes

- **5:10** — Here Paul adapts the imagery of verses 6-7 to echo 4:13-17, using "awake" and "sleep" to refer to whether believers are still alive or have died when Christ returns.
- **5:14** — The Greek term rendered "the disorderly" denotes those living out of step with apostolic order, in this context particularly those neglecting honest labor (cf. 4:11).
- **5:22** — The Greek noun eidos here carries the sense of "kind" or "form" (every sort of evil), though it has also been traditionally rendered "appearance."
- **5:27** — Following the critical Greek text, which reads "all the brethren"; some later manuscripts add "holy" before "brethren."
