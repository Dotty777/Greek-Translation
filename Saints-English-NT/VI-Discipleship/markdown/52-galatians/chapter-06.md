## Text

**6:1** Brethren, even if a man is overtaken in some transgression, you who are spiritual, restore such a one in a spirit of meekness, keeping watch over yourself, lest you also be tempted.

**6:2** Carry one another's burdens, and thus you shall fulfill the law of Christ.

**6:3** For if anyone supposes himself to be something while being nothing, he deludes his own mind.

**6:4** Rather, let each one scrutinize his own work, and then he shall have cause for boasting in himself alone, and not in regard to another;

**6:5** for each one shall carry his own load.

**6:6** Now let the one who is instructed in the word share in all good things with the one who instructs him.

**6:7** Do not be led astray: God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, this very thing he shall also reap;

**6:8** because he who sows to his own flesh shall reap corruption from the flesh, whereas he who sows to the Spirit shall reap eternal life from the Spirit.

**6:9** And let us not lose heart in doing what is good, for in the appointed season we shall reap, if we do not grow faint.

**6:10** So then, while we have opportunity, let us perform what is good toward all people, and above all toward those who belong to the household of faith.

**6:11** Behold with what large letters I write to you with my own hand.

**6:12** All who desire to make a fair display in the flesh, these compel you to receive circumcision, solely so that they may not be persecuted on account of the cross of Christ.

**6:13** For not even those who have been circumcised themselves keep the law; rather, they desire you to receive circumcision so that they may boast in your flesh.

**6:14** Yet may it never be that I should boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world stands crucified to me, and I to the world.

**6:15** For neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but rather a new creation.

**6:16** And for all who shall order their walk by this rule, peace be upon them and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.

**6:17** From this time forward, let no one cause me trouble, for I bear in my body the brand marks of Jesus.

**6:18** May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brethren. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Greek bare ("burdens") refers to heavy, crushing loads shared in fellowship, contrasted with phortion ("load" or personal pack) in verse 5; the critical text also reads the future indicative "you shall fulfill" rather than an imperative.
- **6:11** — Greek egrapsa ("I write") is an epistolary aorist marking the point where Paul takes the pen himself to write in large letters, whether for solemn emphasis or because of weak eyesight.
- **6:15** — The critical Greek text reads simply "neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision," without the phrase "in Christ Jesus" found in later Byzantine manuscripts.
- **6:16** — The conjunction kai ("and") before "upon the Israel of God" may be taken either additively (referring to believing Israel alongside Gentile believers) or epexegetically ("even upon the Israel of God") to identify all who walk by this rule.
