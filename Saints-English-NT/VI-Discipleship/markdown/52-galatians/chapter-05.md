## Text

**5:1** For liberty Christ has set us free; stand steadfast, therefore, and do not again be held fast by a yoke of bondage.

**5:2** Behold, I, Paul, declare to you that if you receive circumcision, Christ will be of no profit to you.

**5:3** And again I bear solemn witness to every man who receives circumcision that he is a debtor to perform the entire law.

**5:4** You have been cut off from Christ, all you who strive to be justified by law; you have fallen away from grace.

**5:5** For by the Spirit, out of faith, we earnestly await the hope of righteousness.

**5:6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is of any avail, but faith working through love.

**5:7** You were running well; who impeded you from obeying the truth?

**5:8** Such persuasion is not from Him who calls you.

**5:9** A little leaven leavens the entire batch of dough.

**5:10** I am confident concerning you in the Lord that you will adopt no other mind; yet he who unsettles you shall bear the judgment, whoever he may be.

**5:11** But as for me, brethren, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished.

**5:12** I wish that those who throw you into turmoil would even mutilate themselves!

**5:13** For you were called to liberty, brethren; only do not make this liberty an occasion for the flesh, but through love be servants to one another.

**5:14** For the entire law stands fulfilled in a single saying, in this: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**5:15** Yet if you keep biting and devouring one another, take heed lest you be consumed by one another.

**5:16** Now I say, walk by the Spirit, and you shall by no means carry out the desire of the flesh.

**5:17** For the flesh sets its desire against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these stand opposed to one another, so that you do not perform the very things you wish.

**5:18** Yet if you are being led by the Spirit, you are not under law.

**5:19** Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these: sexual immorality, uncleanness, licentiousness,

**5:20** idolatry, sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousies, outbursts of fury, selfish rivalries, dissensions, factions,

**5:21** envyings, bouts of drunkenness, revelries, and things like these; concerning which I forewarn you, even as I warned you before, that those who practice such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

**5:22** Yet the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,

**5:23** meekness, self-control; against things such as these there is no law.

**5:24** Now those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh together with its passions and cravings.

**5:25** If we live by the Spirit, by the Spirit let us also order our steps.

**5:26** Let us not become vainglorious, challenging one another, envying one another.

## Translator Notes

- **5:12** — The Greek verb apokopsontai ("mutilate themselves" or "cut themselves off") carries sharp irony in a dispute over circumcision, alluding to the self-emasculation practiced in Anatolian pagan cults.
- **5:19** — In this catalog of the works of the flesh (5:19-21), the critical Greek text omits "adultery" in verse 19 and "murders" in verse 21, both of which appear in later Byzantine manuscripts.
- **5:24** — The critical Greek text reads "Christ Jesus" here rather than "Christ" alone as in later manuscripts.
