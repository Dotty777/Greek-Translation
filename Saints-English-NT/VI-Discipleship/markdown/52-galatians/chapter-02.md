## Text

**2:1** Then after an interval of fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along as well.

**2:2** And I went up in accordance with a revelation, and I set before them the gospel that I proclaim among the Gentiles—yet privately to those of repute—lest somehow I should be running, or had run, in vain.

**2:3** But not even Titus, who was with me, though he was a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised.

**2:4** Yet it was because of the false brethren secretly brought in, who stole in to spy out our liberty which we possess in Christ Jesus, so that they might bring us into bondage—

**2:5** to whom we did not yield in subjection even for an hour, so that the truth of the gospel might abide with you.

**2:6** And from those who were reputed to be something—whatever they once were makes no difference to me; God shows partiality to no man—for those who were of repute added nothing to me;

**2:7** but on the contrary, seeing that I had been entrusted with the gospel for the uncircumcised, just as Peter was for the circumcised

**2:8** (for He who worked powerfully in Peter for an apostleship to the circumcised worked also in me for the Gentiles),

**2:9** and recognizing the grace that had been given to me, James and Cephas and John, who were esteemed to be pillars, gave to me and Barnabas the right hand of fellowship, so that we should go to the Gentiles, and they to the circumcised.

**2:10** Only they asked that we remember the poor—the very thing I also was diligent to do.

**2:11** But when Cephas came to Antioch, I withstood him to his face, because he stood condemned;

**2:12** for before certain men came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles; yet when they arrived, he began to draw back and separate himself, fearing those of the circumcision.

**2:13** And the rest of the Jews likewise acted hypocritically with him, so that even Barnabas was carried away by their hypocrisy.

**2:14** But when I saw that they were not walking uprightly in accord with the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas before them all, "If you, being a Jew, live after the manner of the Gentiles and not after the manner of the Jews, how is it that you compel the Gentiles to live as Jews?"

**2:15** We ourselves are Jews by birth, and not sinners from among the Gentiles,

**2:16** yet knowing that a man is not justified by works of the law, but only through faith in Jesus Christ, we also have believed in Christ Jesus, so that we might be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the law; for by works of the law no flesh shall be justified.

**2:17** But if, while seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves also were found to be sinners, is Christ therefore a minister of sin? May it never be!

**2:18** For if I rebuild the things which I tore down, I show myself to be a transgressor.

**2:19** For I through the law died to the law, so that I might live to God. I have been crucified with Christ;

**2:20** and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me; and the life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and delivered Himself up for me.

**2:21** I do not nullify the grace of God; for if righteousness is through the law, then Christ died in vain.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — Verses 4-5 reflect an abrupt grammatical break (anacoluthon) in the Greek text as Paul interrupts his sentence to denounce the intrusion of the false brethren.
- **2:15** — Many interpreters regard Paul's address to Cephas begun in verse 14 as continuing through verse 21, moving from the Antioch incident into a broader theological defense of justification by faith.
- **2:16** — The Greek phrase pistis Christou ("faith in Christ") is grammatically capable of being read either as an objective genitive ("faith in Christ") or a subjective genitive ("the faithfulness of Christ"); the same construction recurs in verse 20.
- **2:19** — The clause "I have been crucified with Christ" stands at the end of verse 19 in the critical Greek text, whereas many older English translations place it at the beginning of verse 20.
