## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle, not from men nor through any man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him from among the dead,

**1:2** and all the brethren with me, to the churches of Galatia:

**1:3** Grace be to you, and peace, from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,

**1:4** who gave Himself for our sins, so that He might rescue us out of the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,

**1:5** to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**1:6** I marvel that you are so swiftly turning away from Him who called you in the grace of Christ to a different gospel,

**1:7** which is not another; only there are some who trouble you and desire to distort the gospel of Christ.

**1:8** Yet even if we, or an angel from heaven, should proclaim a gospel to you contrary to that which we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.

**1:9** Just as we have said beforehand, now I also say again: if anyone proclaims a gospel to you contrary to that which you received, let him be accursed.

**1:10** For am I now striving to win over men, or God? Or do I seek to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I would not be a bondservant of Christ.

**1:11** For I make known to you, brethren, that the gospel proclaimed by me is not according to man;

**1:12** for neither did I receive it from any man, nor was I taught it, but it came by a revelation of Jesus Christ.

**1:13** For you have heard of my former manner of life in Judaism, how beyond measure I persecuted the church of God and sought to destroy it,

**1:14** and I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my contemporaries among my own people, being abundantly more zealous for the traditions of my fathers.

**1:15** But when He who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me through His grace was pleased

**1:16** to reveal His Son in me, so that I might proclaim Him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult with flesh and blood,

**1:17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me; rather, I departed into Arabia, and returned once more to Damascus.

**1:18** Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas, and I remained with him fifteen days.

**1:19** Yet I did not see any other of the apostles, except James, the brother of the Lord.

**1:20** Now in the things which I write to you, behold, before God, I do not lie.

**1:21** Then I came into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

**1:22** And I was still unknown in person to the churches of Judea which are in Christ;

**1:23** only they kept hearing, "He who formerly persecuted us now proclaims the faith which he once sought to destroy,"

**1:24** and they were glorifying God in me.

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — In verses 6-7, Paul contrasts "a different gospel" (heteron) with "another" (allo), underscoring that any distortion of the message of Christ is no true gospel at all.
- **1:15** — The Greek critical text reads literally "He who set me apart," without the explicit noun "God" found in later manuscripts, though God is clearly the implied subject.
- **1:18** — The verb historesai denotes visiting someone to become personally acquainted and inquire of them; "Cephas" is the Aramaic name of the apostle Peter.
