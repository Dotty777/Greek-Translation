## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus, according to the command of God our Savior and of the Lord Jesus Christ, our hope,

**1:2** To Timothy, a genuine child in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and from Christ Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** As I exhorted you to remain in Ephesus when I was traveling to Macedonia, that you might charge some not to teach a different doctrine,

**1:4** nor to devote attention to myths and unending genealogies, which produce disputes rather than the stewardship of God that is in faith—

**1:5** Now the goal of this charge is love issuing from a pure heart, a good conscience, and an unfeigned faith,

**1:6** from which some, having swerved, have turned away into fruitless talk,

**1:7** desiring to be teachers of the law, comprehending neither what they say nor the things they so confidently assert.

**1:8** Now we know that the law is good, provided one uses it lawfully,

**1:9** understanding this, that the law is not laid down for a righteous person, but for the lawless and unruly, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for slayers of fathers and slayers of mothers, for murderers,

**1:10** for the sexually immoral, for men who lie with men, for enslavers, for liars, for perjurers, and for anything else that stands opposed to sound doctrine,

**1:11** in accordance with the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.

**1:12** I give thanks to Christ Jesus our Lord, who has strengthened me, because He judged me faithful, appointing me to ministry,

**1:13** though formerly I was a blasphemer, a persecutor, and an insolent oppressor; yet I received mercy, because I acted ignorantly in unbelief,

**1:14** and the grace of our Lord overflowed exceedingly, together with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**1:15** Faithful is the saying and worthy of complete acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost;

**1:16** yet for this very reason I received mercy, that in me as the foremost, Jesus Christ might display His utmost longsuffering, as an example for those who are to believe in Him for eternal life.

**1:17** Now to the King of the ages, incorruptible, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.

**1:18** This charge I entrust to you, child Timothy, according to the prophecies formerly spoken concerning you, that by them you may wage the noble warfare,

**1:19** holding faith and a good conscience, which some have thrust away and so have suffered shipwreck concerning the faith;

**1:20** among whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan so that they may be disciplined not to blaspheme.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Greek oikonomian theou ("stewardship of God") refers to God's redemptive administration or plan; the em-dash marks an abrupt syntactic break (anacoluthon) in Paul's opening sentence.
- **1:10** — Greek arsenokoitais literally denotes "men who lie with men" (echoing the Septuagint of Leviticus 18:22 and 20:13), and andrapodistais refers to slave-traders or kidnappers.
- **1:17** — The Greek text reads mono theo ("the only God"); later Byzantine manuscripts add sopho ("the only wise God").
