## Text

**4:1** Now the Spirit explicitly declares that in later times some shall fall away from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons,

**4:2** through the hypocrisy of those who speak lies, whose own conscience has been seared with a branding iron,

**4:3** forbidding marriage and commanding to abstain from foods which God created to be partaken of with thanksgiving by those who believe and have come to know the truth.

**4:4** For every created thing of God is good, and nothing is to be cast away when it is received with thanksgiving,

**4:5** for it is sanctified through the word of God and prayer.

**4:6** By setting these things before the brethren, you will be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished on the words of the faith and of the good teaching which you have closely followed.

**4:7** But refuse profane and old wives' tales; rather, train yourself for godliness.

**4:8** For bodily training is profitable for a little, yet godliness is profitable for all things, holding promise for the present life and for the life to come.

**4:9** Trustworthy is the saying and worthy of full acceptance.

**4:10** For to this end we labor and strive, because we have placed our hope in the living God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of those who believe.

**4:11** Command these things and teach them.

**4:12** Let no one look down upon your youth, but become a pattern for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.

**4:13** Until I come, give close attention to the public reading, to exhortation, to teaching.

**4:14** Do not neglect the gift of grace that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the eldership.

**4:15** Take pains with these things; be wholly given to them, so that your progress may be manifest to all.

**4:16** Pay close heed to yourself and to the teaching; persevere in these things, for in doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.

## Translator Notes

- **4:10** — Following the critical text, this verse reads "we strive" (Greek agonizometha) rather than the later textual variant "we suffer reproach."
- **4:12** — The critical Greek text lists five areas of example here, omitting the phrase "in spirit" found in later manuscripts.
- **4:13** — "The public reading" (Greek anagnosis) refers to the formal reading of the Scriptures in the gathered congregation.
