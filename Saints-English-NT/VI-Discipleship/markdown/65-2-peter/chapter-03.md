## Text

**3:1** Beloved, this is now the second epistle I am writing to you, in both of which I am stirring up your sincere mind by reminder,

**3:2** that you should remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets, and the commandment of the Lord and Savior given through your apostles,

**3:3** knowing this first of all, that in the last days scoffers will come with scoffing, walking according to their own lusts,

**3:4** and saying, "Where is the promise of His coming? For ever since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue just as they have been from the beginning of creation."

**3:5** For they willfully overlook this fact, that by the word of God the heavens existed from long ago, and the earth was formed out of water and through water,

**3:6** by which the world that then existed perished, being deluged with water.

**3:7** But the heavens and the earth that now exist have been stored up by the same word for fire, being kept until the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.

**3:8** But do not let this one thing escape your notice, beloved, that with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.

**3:9** The Lord is not slow concerning the promise, as some regard slowness, but is longsuffering toward you, not desiring that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.

**3:10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a mighty roar, and the elements will be dissolved in fervent heat, and the earth and the works that are in it will be laid bare.

**3:11** Since all these things are thus to be dissolved, what manner of persons ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness,

**3:12** looking for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be set ablaze and dissolved, and the elements will melt away in fervent heat?

**3:13** Yet according to His promise we await new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.

**3:14** Therefore, beloved, since you await these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, without spot and blameless;

**3:15** and consider the longsuffering of our Lord to be salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you according to the wisdom given to him,

**3:16** as he also does in all his epistles, speaking in them concerning these things; in which there are some things hard to understand, which the untaught and unstable distort, as they also do the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction.

**3:17** You therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, be on your guard, lest being led away with the error of the lawless, you fall from your own steadfastness;

**3:18** but grow in grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Early manuscripts read "your apostles," whereas later manuscripts read "us, the apostles."
- **3:10** — Early manuscripts read "will be found" (rendered "will be laid bare," signifying exposure to God's judgment), whereas later manuscripts read "will be burned up."
- **3:12** — "Hastening" may denote either earnestly longing for the day of God or hastening its arrival through godly living and repentance.
- **3:18** — "To the day of eternity" translates a unique New Testament phrase signifying the unending eternal day.
