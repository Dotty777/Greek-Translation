## Text

**1:1** Simeon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have obtained a faith of equal preciousness with ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:

**1:2** May grace and peace be multiplied to you in the full knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** Seeing that His divine power has bestowed upon us all things pertaining to life and godliness, through the knowledge of Him who called us by His own glory and virtue,

**1:4** through which He has given to us His precious and greatest promises, that by these you may become sharers in the divine nature, having fled the corruption that is in the world through evil desire.

**1:5** And for this very reason, applying all diligence, in your faith supply virtue, and in virtue knowledge,

**1:6** and in knowledge self-control, and in self-control steadfastness, and in steadfastness godliness,

**1:7** and in godliness brotherly kindness, and in brotherly kindness love.

**1:8** For if these things are yours and are abounding, they render you neither idle nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:9** For whoever lacks these things is blind, being shortsighted, and has forgotten the cleansing of his former sins.

**1:10** Therefore, brethren, be all the more diligent to confirm your calling and election; for if you do these things, you shall never stumble;

**1:11** for in this manner the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplied to you.

**1:12** Therefore I intend always to remind you concerning these things, even though you know them and are firmly established in the present truth.

**1:13** And I consider it right, as long as I remain in this tabernacle, to stir you up by reminder,

**1:14** knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle is soon, just as our Lord Jesus Christ also showed me.

**1:15** And I will also be diligent that after my departure you may be able at all times to call these things to remembrance.

**1:16** For we did not follow artfully devised fables when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we had become eyewitnesses of His majesty.

**1:17** For He received honor and glory from God the Father when such a voice was borne to Him by the Majestic Glory, "This is My Son, My beloved, in whom I am well pleased"—

**1:18** and this voice we ourselves heard borne out of heaven while we were with Him on the holy mountain.

**1:19** And we have the prophetic word made more certain, to which you do well to give heed as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star arises in your hearts;

**1:20** knowing this first of all, that no prophecy of Scripture comes from one's own interpretation;

**1:21** for no prophecy was ever brought forth by the will of man, but men spoke from God as they were borne along by the Holy Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text uses Simeon (Symeon), the Semitic form of Simon. The phrase "our God and Savior Jesus Christ" shares a single Greek article, identifying Jesus Christ as both God and Savior.
- **1:3** — Early manuscripts read "by His own glory and virtue" (or "excellence"), whereas later manuscripts read "to glory and virtue."
- **1:12** — Early manuscripts read "I intend" (or "I will always be ready"), whereas later manuscripts read "I will not be negligent."
- **1:20** — "From one's own interpretation" is debated as referring either to a reader's private interpretation or to a prophet's own human origination and unraveling of a vision, the latter connecting directly with verse twenty-one.
