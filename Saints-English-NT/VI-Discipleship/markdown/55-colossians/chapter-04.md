## Text

**4:1** Masters, render to your bondservants what is just and equitable, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven.

**4:2** Persevere in prayer, keeping watch in it with thanksgiving,

**4:3** praying at the same time for us also, that God may open for us a door for the word, to declare the mystery of Christ, for which I am also in bonds,

**4:4** so that I may manifest it as I ought to speak.

**4:5** Walk in wisdom toward outsiders, redeeming the time.

**4:6** Let your speech always be in grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer every person.

**4:7** Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow bondservant in the Lord, will make known to you everything concerning me,

**4:8** whom I have sent to you for this very purpose, that you may know our circumstances and that he may comfort your hearts,

**4:9** together with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of your own. They will make known to you everything that is happening here.

**4:10** Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, sends greetings to you, and Mark, the cousin of Barnabas (concerning whom you received instructions: if he comes to you, receive him),

**4:11** and Jesus who is called Justus. Of those from the circumcision, these alone are fellow workers for the kingdom of God, who have become a consolation to me.

**4:12** Epaphras, who is one of your own, a bondservant of Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you, always wrestling fervently for you in his prayers, that you may stand complete and fully assured in all the will of God.

**4:13** For I testify on his behalf that he has great toil for you, and for those in Laodicea, and for those in Hierapolis.

**4:14** Luke, the beloved physician, sends greetings to you, and Demas also.

**4:15** Give greetings to the brethren in Laodicea, and to Nympha and the church in her house.

**4:16** And when this epistle has been read among you, see to it that it is also read in the church of the Laodiceans, and that you also read the one from Laodicea.

**4:17** And say to Archippus, "Attend to the ministry that you received in the Lord, that you may fulfill it."

**4:18** This greeting is by my own hand—Paul. Remember my bonds. Grace be with you.

## Translator Notes

- **4:8** — The critical Greek text reads "that you may know our circumstances" (second person plural), whereas other manuscripts read "that he may know your circumstances."
- **4:13** — The critical Greek text reads "great toil" (πόνον) rather than "great zeal" (ζῆλον), which appears in later manuscripts.
- **4:15** — The feminine pronoun "her house" (αὐτῆς) identifies Νύμφαν as a woman named Nympha; some manuscripts read the masculine pronoun "his," taking the name as Nymphas.
- **4:16** — The letter "from Laodicea" is not preserved under that title; scholars have variously suggested it was a lost Pauline epistle or a circular letter such as Ephesians.
