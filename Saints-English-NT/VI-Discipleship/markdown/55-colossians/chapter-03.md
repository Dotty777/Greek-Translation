## Text

**3:1** Therefore, if you were raised together with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God.

**3:2** Fix your minds on the things above, not on the things upon the earth.

**3:3** For you have died, and your life lies hidden with Christ in God.

**3:4** When Christ, who is your life, is manifested, then you also shall be manifested with Him in glory.

**3:5** Therefore put to death the members that are upon the earth: fornication, impurity, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.

**3:6** On account of these things the wrath of God is coming.

**3:7** In them you also once walked, when you were living in these things.

**3:8** But now you also lay aside all these things: anger, fury, malice, slander, and shameful speech from your mouth.

**3:9** Do not lie to one another, since you have stripped off the old man with his deeds,

**3:10** and have clothed yourselves with the new man, who is being renewed to true knowledge according to the image of Him who created him,

**3:11** where there is no Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, bondservant, or freeman; but Christ is all, and in all.

**3:12** Clothe yourselves therefore, as the chosen of God, holy and beloved, with a heart of compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, and longsuffering,

**3:13** bearing with one another and forgiving each other, if anyone has a grievance against another; just as the Lord also graciously forgave you, so you also must do.

**3:14** And over all these things clothe yourselves with love, which is the bond of perfection.

**3:15** And let the peace of Christ preside in your hearts, to which you were also called in one body; and be thankful.

**3:16** Let the word of Christ dwell richly in you in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs in grace, singing in your hearts to God.

**3:17** And all that you do, whether in word or in deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, offering thanks to God the Father through Him.

**3:18** Wives, submit yourselves to your husbands, as is proper in the Lord.

**3:19** Husbands, love your wives, and do not grow bitter toward them.

**3:20** Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing in the Lord.

**3:21** Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they grow disheartened.

**3:22** Bondservants, obey your masters according to the flesh in everything, not with eye-service, as pleasers of men, but with singleness of heart, fearing the Lord.

**3:23** Whatever you do, work from the soul, as to the Lord and not to men,

**3:24** knowing that from the Lord you shall receive the recompense of the inheritance; it is the Lord Christ whom you serve.

**3:25** For he who does wrong shall receive back the wrong he has committed, and there is no partiality.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The critical Greek text omits the phrase "upon the sons of disobedience," which appears in some later manuscripts (likely assimilated from Ephesians 5:6).
- **3:13** — The Greek text reads "the Lord" (κύριος) rather than "Christ" or "God," which appear as variants in other manuscripts.
- **3:16** — The Greek punctuation joins "in all wisdom" with the indwelling of Christ's word, though it can alternatively modify "teaching and admonishing." The critical text also reads "to God" rather than "to the Lord."
- **3:24** — The verb "serve" (δουλεύετε) may be understood either as an indicative ("it is the Lord Christ whom you serve") or as an imperative ("Serve the Lord Christ!").
