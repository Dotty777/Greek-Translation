## Text

**2:1** For I desire you to know how great a conflict I have on behalf of you and of those in Laodicea, and of all who have not seen my face in the flesh,

**2:2** so that their hearts may be encouraged, knit together in love and reaching all the riches of the full assurance of understanding, to the full knowledge of the mystery of God, that is, Christ,

**2:3** in whom all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge lie hidden.

**2:4** This I say lest anyone delude you with persuasive speech.

**2:5** For even though I am absent in the flesh, yet I am with you in spirit, rejoicing and beholding your good order and the firmness of your faith in Christ.

**2:6** As therefore you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in Him,

**2:7** having been rooted and being built up in Him, and established in the faith just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.

**2:8** Beware lest anyone carry you off captive through philosophy and empty deceit, according to the tradition of men, according to the elementary principles of the world, and not according to Christ.

**2:9** For in Him all the fullness of the Godhead dwells in bodily form,

**2:10** and in Him you have been made full, who is the head of every principality and authority,

**2:11** in whom you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands, in the stripping off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,

**2:12** having been buried together with Him in baptism, in which you were also raised together with Him through faith in the working of God, who raised Him from among the dead.

**2:13** And you, who were dead in your transgressions and in the uncircumcision of your flesh, He made alive together with Him, having freely forgiven us all our transgressions,

**2:14** having blotted out the written bond with its decrees that stood against us, which was opposed to us; and He has taken it out of the way, nailing it to the cross.

**2:15** Having disarmed the principalities and the authorities, He made an open display of them, triumphing over them in it.

**2:16** Therefore let no one judge you in food and in drink, or in the matter of a festival or a new moon or Sabbaths,

**2:17** which are a shadow of the things that are to come, while the substance belongs to Christ.

**2:18** Let no one rob you of your prize, delighting in self-abasement and the worship of angels, entering into the things which he has seen, puffed up without cause by the mind of his flesh,

**2:19** and not holding fast the Head, from whom the entire body, supplied and knit together through the joints and ligaments, increases with the growth that comes from God.

**2:20** If you died with Christ from the elementary principles of the world, why, as though living in the world, do you submit yourselves to decrees:

**2:21** "Do not handle, nor taste, nor touch,"

**2:22** all of which perish with use, according to the commandments and teachings of men?

**2:23** Such things indeed carry a show of wisdom in self-made worship and self-abasement and unsparing severity toward the body, yet they are of no value against the gratification of the flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The critical Greek text reads concisely "the mystery of God, Christ," placing Christ in apposition to the mystery; later manuscripts expand this to "of God, both of the Father and of Christ."
- **2:8** — The Greek phrase rendered "the elementary principles of the world" can denote either rudimentary religious teachings and cosmic elements or elemental spirits thought to rule the cosmos (compare verse 20).
- **2:15** — The closing Greek phrase may be translated either "in it" (referring back to the cross in verse 14) or "in Him" (referring to Christ).
- **2:18** — The critical Greek text reads "entering into the things which he has seen," alluding to claimed visionary experiences; later manuscripts insert a negative to read "things which he has not seen."
- **2:23** — The final Greek clause is compressed and debated, and may be read either as "of no value against the gratification of the flesh" or as "of no true honor, serving only to gratify the flesh."
