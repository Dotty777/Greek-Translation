## Text

**3:1** Finally, pray for us, brethren, that the word of the Lord may speed onward and be glorified, just as it also is among you,

**3:2** and that we may be delivered from perverse and evil men; for not all possess faith.

**3:3** Yet the Lord is faithful; He shall establish you and guard you from the evil one.

**3:4** And we are confident in the Lord regarding you, that what we command, you both do and shall do.

**3:5** And may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the endurance of Christ.

**3:6** Now we charge you, brethren, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw yourselves from every brother who walks disorderly and not according to the tradition which they received from us.

**3:7** For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not act disorderly among you,

**3:8** nor did we eat bread from anyone without paying, but in toil and hardship we labored night and day, so that we would not burden any one of you;

**3:9** not because we do not have the right, but in order to give ourselves to you as a pattern, so that you should imitate us.

**3:10** For indeed when we were with you, we kept charging you with this: "If anyone is unwilling to work, neither let him eat."

**3:11** For we hear that some are walking disorderly among you, doing no work at all, yet meddling as busybodies.

**3:12** Now we charge and exhort such persons in the Lord Jesus Christ that, working in quietness, they eat their own bread.

**3:13** But you, brethren, do not lose heart in doing what is good.

**3:14** And if anyone does not obey our word through this letter, mark that person and do not associate with him, so that he may be put to shame.

**3:15** And do not consider him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

**3:16** Now may the Lord of peace Himself grant you peace continually in every way. The Lord be with you all.

**3:17** The greeting is with my own hand—Paul's—which is a sign in every letter; thus I write.

**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek tou ponerou can be taken either as masculine ("the evil one") or as neuter ("evil").
- **3:5** — "The love of God" and "the endurance of Christ" may refer to God's love for believers and Christ's own endurance, or to believers' love for God and patient endurance for Christ.
- **3:6** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads the third-person plural "they received" (parelabosan), rather than the variants "you received" or "he received."
- **3:11** — The Greek uses a play on words between ergazomenous ("working") and periergazomenous ("acting as busybodies").
