## Text

**2:1** Now we urge you, brethren, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to Him,

**2:2** that you not be quickly shaken from your mind or alarmed, whether by spirit or by word or by letter as if from us, to the effect that the day of the Lord has already come.

**2:3** Let no one deceive you in any manner; for that day shall not come unless the falling away comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of perdition,

**2:4** who opposes and exalts himself above everything called a god or an object of worship, so that he sits in the temple of God, displaying himself to be God.

**2:5** Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I used to tell you these things?

**2:6** And now you know what restrains him, so that he may be revealed in his own season.

**2:7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only the one who restrains for the present remains until he is taken out of the way.

**2:8** And then the lawless one shall be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus shall slay with the breath of His mouth and destroy by the appearing of His coming.

**2:9** The coming of that lawless one is in accordance with the working of Satan, with all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,

**2:10** and with every deception of unrighteousness for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so that they might be saved.

**2:11** And for this cause God sends them a working of delusion, so that they may believe the lie,

**2:12** in order that all may be condemned who did not believe the truth, but delighted in unrighteousness.

**2:13** But we ought to give thanks to God always for you, brethren beloved of the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation in sanctification by the Spirit and faith in the truth,

**2:14** to which He called you through our gospel, for the gaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**2:15** So then, brethren, stand firm and hold fast to the traditions which you were taught, whether by word or by our letter.

**2:16** Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave us eternal consolation and good hope in grace,

**2:17** comfort your hearts and strengthen them in every good work and word.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads "the day of the Lord" (kyriou) rather than "the day of Christ."
- **2:3** — The Greek leaves the main clause implied after "for" (supplied here as "that day shall not come"), and reads "the man of lawlessness" (anomias) rather than "the man of sin."
- **2:6** — Paul shifts from the neuter "what restrains" (to katechon) in verse 6 to the masculine "the one who restrains" (ho katechon) in verse 7 without naming the restrainer, variously identified as civil order, an angelic force, or the Holy Spirit.
- **2:13** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads "from the beginning" (ap' arches) rather than the variant "as firstfruits" (aparchen).
