## Text

**15:1** Now we who are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

**15:2** Let each one of us please his neighbor for his good, to build him up.

**15:3** For Christ also did not please Himself, but as it is written: "The reproaches of those who cast reproach on You fell upon Me."

**15:4** For whatever was written beforehand was written for our teaching, that through steadfastness and through the consolation of the Scriptures we might have hope.

**15:5** Now may the God of steadfastness and consolation grant you to be of one mind among yourselves according to Christ Jesus,

**15:6** that with one accord and with one mouth you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15:7** Therefore receive one another, even as Christ also received you, to the glory of God.

**15:8** For I tell you that Christ has become a servant to the circumcision on behalf of the truth of God, so as to confirm the promises given to the fathers,

**15:9** and in order that the Gentiles might glorify God for His mercy; as it is written: "Therefore I will praise You among the Gentiles, and to Your name I will sing praises."

**15:10** And again it says: "Be glad, O Gentiles, with His people."

**15:11** And again: "Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples extol Him."

**15:12** And again Isaiah says: "There shall be the Root of Jesse, even He who rises to rule over the Gentiles; on Him the Gentiles shall set their hope."

**15:13** Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe, so that you may abound in hope in the power of the Holy Spirit.

**15:14** Now I myself am persuaded regarding you, my brethren, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to admonish one another as well.

**15:15** Nevertheless, on some points I have written to you rather boldly, by way of reminding you, because of the grace granted to me by God,

**15:16** to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving as a priest of the gospel of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.

**15:17** Therefore in Christ Jesus I have cause for boasting in the things that pertain to God.

**15:18** For I will not venture to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, in word and deed,

**15:19** by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit of God; so that from Jerusalem and around as far as Illyricum I have fully proclaimed the gospel of Christ,

**15:20** and thus making it my ambition to proclaim the gospel not where Christ was already named, lest I build upon another's foundation,

**15:21** but as it is written: "Those to whom no report was brought concerning Him shall see, and those who have not heard shall understand."

**15:22** Therefore I have also been hindered many times from coming to you.

**15:23** Yet now, having no longer any place in these regions, and having had a deep longing for many years to come to you,

**15:24** whenever I journey to Spain, for I hope to see you as I pass through and to be sent on my way there by you, once I have first had my fill in part of your company—

**15:25** yet now I am going to Jerusalem, ministering to the saints.

**15:26** For Macedonia and Achaia were well pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.

**15:27** Indeed they were well pleased, and they are debtors to them; for if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are obligated also to serve them in material things.

**15:28** Therefore, when I have completed this and have sealed this fruit to them, I shall set out by way of you for Spain.

**15:29** And I know that in coming to you, I shall come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.

**15:30** Now I appeal to you, brethren, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in prayers to God on my behalf,

**15:31** that I may be rescued from those who are disobedient in Judea, and that my ministry for Jerusalem may prove acceptable to the saints,

**15:32** so that in joy I may come to you through the will of God and find rest together with you.

**15:33** Now may the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **15:7** — The Greek critical text reads "received you" rather than "received us," sharpening Paul's direct appeal to the Roman congregations.
- **15:19** — The Greek text reads "the Spirit of God," where ancient manuscripts vary among "the Spirit of God," "the Holy Spirit," and simply "the Spirit."
- **15:24** — The Greek critical text omits "I will come to you" after "Spain," leaving an abrupt pause before Paul resumes his thought in verse 25.
- **15:29** — The Greek critical text reads "the blessing of Christ" without the additional words "of the gospel" found in later manuscripts.
