## Text

**8:1** Now therefore there is no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus;

**8:2** for the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has set you free from the law of sin and of death.

**8:3** For what was impossible to the law, in that it was weak through the flesh, God—having sent His own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and concerning sin—condemned sin in the flesh,

**8:4** that the righteous requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit.

**8:5** For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit.

**8:6** For the mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace;

**8:7** because the mind of the flesh is enmity toward God, for to the law of God it is not subject, nor indeed can it be;

**8:8** and those who are in the flesh cannot please God.

**8:9** Yet you are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, this one is not His.

**8:10** And if Christ is in you, the body indeed is dead because of sin, yet the spirit is life because of righteousness.

**8:11** And if the Spirit of Him who raised up Jesus from the dead dwells in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead shall give life also to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who dwells in you.

**8:12** Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh;

**8:13** for if you live according to the flesh, you are about to die, but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you shall live.

**8:14** For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God.

**8:15** For you did not receive a spirit of bondage again to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption as sons, by whom we cry, "Abba, Father!"

**8:16** The Spirit Himself bears witness together with our spirit that we are children of God;

**8:17** and if children, also heirs: heirs indeed of God, and joint heirs with Christ, if indeed we suffer together with Him, that we may also be glorified together with Him.

**8:18** For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that is about to be revealed to us.

**8:19** For the earnest expectation of the creation eagerly awaits the revealing of the sons of God;

**8:20** for the creation was subjected to vanity, not willingly, but on account of Him who subjected it, in hope

**8:21** that the creation itself also shall be set free from the bondage of corruption into the liberty of the glory of the children of God.

**8:22** For we know that the whole creation groans together and travails in birth pangs together until now;

**8:23** and not only so, but we ourselves also, having the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, eagerly awaiting adoption as sons, the redemption of our body.

**8:24** For in hope we were saved; yet hope that is seen is not hope, for what a man sees, why does he still hope for it?

**8:25** But if we hope for that which we do not see, through steadfast endurance we eagerly await it.

**8:26** And in like manner the Spirit also helps our weakness; for what we should pray as we ought we do not know, but the Spirit Himself makes intercession with groanings unutterable,

**8:27** and He who searches the hearts knows what is the mind of the Spirit, because according to God He makes intercession on behalf of the saints.

**8:28** Now we know that for those who love God, all things work in concert for good—for those who are called according to His purpose.

**8:29** For whom He foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, that He might be the firstborn among many brethren;

**8:30** and whom He predestined, these He also called; and whom He called, these He also justified; and whom He justified, these He also glorified.

**8:31** What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who is against us?

**8:32** He indeed who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up on behalf of us all, how shall He not also with Him freely grant us all things?

**8:33** Who shall bring a charge against God's elect? It is God who justifies;

**8:34** who is he who condemns? It is Christ Jesus who died, yes rather, who was raised, who also is at the right hand of God, who also makes intercession on our behalf.

**8:35** Who shall part us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or anguish, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?

**8:36** Even as it is written, "For Your sake we are put to death all the day long; we were accounted as sheep for slaughter."

**8:37** Yet in all these things we prevail abundantly through Him who loved us.

**8:38** For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers,

**8:39** nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing shall have power to part us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Saints Greek Critical Text ends verse 1 at 'in Christ Jesus,' without the later explanatory clause found in some manuscripts.
- **8:2** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads second-person singular se ('has set you free,' supported by Sinaiticus, Vaticanus, and early witnesses), whereas other manuscripts read first-person singular me ('me').
- **8:15** — Aramaic Abba ('Father') is preserved alongside the Greek ho pater, reflecting intimate filial address; huiothesia literally means 'adoption as sons.'
- **8:28** — Greek panta synergei eis agathon is rendered literally ('all things work together / in concert for good'); some manuscripts add ho theos ('God works all things together'), which is also a possible grammatical construal of the shorter text.
- **8:38** — The critical text places dynameis ('powers') after 'things present, nor things to come,' matching the earliest manuscripts.
