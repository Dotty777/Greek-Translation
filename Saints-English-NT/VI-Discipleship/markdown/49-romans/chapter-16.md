## Text

**16:1** Now I commend to you our sister Phoebe, who is also a servant of the church that is in Cenchreae,

**16:2** that you may welcome her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and assist her in whatever matter she may have need of you; for indeed she herself has been a benefactor to many, and to myself as well.

**16:3** Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

**16:4** who ventured their own necks for my life, to whom not I alone give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles;

**16:5** and greet the church that is in their house. Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Asia to Christ.

**16:6** Greet Mary, who labored greatly for you.

**16:7** Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners, who are distinguished among the apostles, who were also in Christ before me.

**16:8** Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord.

**16:9** Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved.

**16:10** Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. Greet those who belong to the household of Aristobulus.

**16:11** Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet those of the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.

**16:12** Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet the beloved Persis, who labored greatly in the Lord.

**16:13** Greet Rufus, elect in the Lord, and his mother and mine.

**16:14** Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brethren who are with them.

**16:15** Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.

**16:16** Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.

**16:17** Now I exhort you, brethren, to take note of those who create divisions and stumbling blocks contrary to the teaching that you learned, and turn away from them;

**16:18** for such persons do not serve our Lord Christ, but their own belly, and through smooth speech and flattery they deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.

**16:19** For your obedience has reached all. Therefore I rejoice over you; yet I desire you to be wise in what is good, and innocent in what is evil.

**16:20** And the God of peace will swiftly crush Satan beneath your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus be with you.

**16:21** Timothy, my fellow worker, and Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my kinsmen, greet you.

**16:22** I, Tertius, who wrote this epistle, greet you in the Lord.

**16:23** Gaius, who is host to me and to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, and our brother Quartus greet you.

**16:24** ⟦The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.⟧

**16:25** Now to Him who is able to strengthen you according to my gospel and the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery kept silent through ages eternal,

**16:26** but now made manifest, and through the prophetic Scriptures made known to all the nations, according to the command of the eternal God, for the obedience of faith—

**16:27** to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory forever. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Greek diakonos can denote either a general servant or minister or an official deacon or deaconess of the church.
- **16:7** — The Greek phrase episemoi en tois apostolois is debated and may mean either 'distinguished among the apostles' (counting Andronicus and Junia within the broader circle of apostles) or 'well known to the apostles.' The Greek name Iounian is standardly taken as the feminine name Junia.
- **16:24** — This benediction is enclosed in double brackets because it is omitted by the earliest manuscripts, though it appears in many later witnesses.
- **16:27** — The closing doxology (verses twenty-five through twenty-seven) exhibits a slight grammatical break at 'to whom,' referring back to God; in ancient manuscripts the doxology also appears after 14:23 or 15:33 in some witnesses.
