## Text

**10:1** Brethren, my heart's desire and my supplication to God on their behalf is for their salvation.

**10:2** For I testify concerning them that they have a zeal for God, yet not according to true knowledge.

**10:3** For being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own, they did not submit themselves to the righteousness of God.

**10:4** For the end of the law is Christ, for righteousness to everyone who believes.

**10:5** For Moses writes concerning the righteousness that is by the law, "The man who does these things shall live by it."

**10:6** But the righteousness that is from faith speaks thus: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who shall ascend into heaven?'"—that is, to bring Christ down—

**10:7** or, "Who shall descend into the abyss?"—that is, to bring Christ up from the dead.

**10:8** Yet what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart"—that is, the word of faith which we proclaim:

**10:9** that if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you shall be saved.

**10:10** For with the heart one believes for righteousness, and with the mouth confession is made for salvation.

**10:11** For the Scripture says, "Everyone who believes on Him shall not be put to shame."

**10:12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; for the same Lord is Lord of all, abounding in riches toward all who call upon Him.

**10:13** For "everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."

**10:14** How then shall they call upon Him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in Him whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without one who proclaims?

**10:15** And how shall they proclaim unless they are sent? Just as it is written: "How beautiful are the feet of those who bring glad tidings of good things!"

**10:16** Yet not all obeyed the gospel; for Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed our report?"

**10:17** Therefore faith comes by hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

**10:18** Yet I ask, have they not heard? Indeed they have: "Into all the earth has their voice gone forth, and to the ends of the world their words."

**10:19** Yet I ask, did Israel not know? First Moses declares: "I will stir you to jealousy by those who are not a nation; by a nation without understanding I will provoke you to anger."

**10:20** And Isaiah speaks boldly, saying: "I was found by those who did not seek Me; I showed Myself openly to those who did not inquire of Me."

**10:21** Yet concerning Israel he says: "All the day long I stretched forth My hands to a disobedient and defiant people."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Greek noun translated "end" (telos) encompasses both termination and goal or culmination, conveying that Christ both concludes the era of the Mosaic law and fulfills its intended purpose.
- **10:5** — The earliest Greek text reads the feminine singular "by it," referring back to the righteousness from the law, whereas later manuscripts read the plural "by them," referring to the commandments of Leviticus 18:5.
- **10:15** — Following the earliest Greek manuscripts, this quotation from Isaiah 52:7 omits the phrase "of those who proclaim the good news of peace" present in later textual witnesses.
- **10:17** — The critical Greek text reads "the word of Christ," whereas later manuscripts read "the word of God."
