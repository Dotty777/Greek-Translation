## Text

**13:1** Let every soul submit to the governing authorities; for no authority exists except by God, and those that exist have been ordained by God.

**13:2** Therefore whoever resists authority has opposed the ordinance of God, and those who oppose it shall receive judgment upon themselves.

**13:3** For rulers are not a terror to good conduct, but to evil conduct. Do you desire not to fear the authority? Practice what is good, and you will receive praise from it;

**13:4** for it is a servant of God for your good. But if you do evil, be afraid, for it does not bear the sword in vain; for it is a servant of God, an avenger to bring wrath upon the one who practices evil.

**13:5** Therefore it is necessary to be in subjection, not only on account of wrath, but also for the sake of conscience.

**13:6** For on this account you also pay taxes, since they are ministers of God, attending steadfastly to this very thing.

**13:7** Render to everyone what is due: taxes to whom taxes are due, revenue to whom revenue, fear to whom fear, honor to whom honor.

**13:8** Owe nothing to anyone, except to love one another; for whoever loves another has fulfilled the law.

**13:9** For these commandments—"You shall not commit adultery," "You shall not murder," "You shall not steal," "You shall not covet," and any other commandment there may be—are summed up in this word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**13:10** Love works no harm to a neighbor; thus love is the fulfillment of the law.

**13:11** And do this, discerning the time, that it is already the hour for you to awaken from sleep; for our salvation is nearer now than when we came to believe.

**13:12** The night is far advanced, and the day has drawn near. Let us therefore lay aside the works of darkness, and put on the armor of light.

**13:13** Let us walk honorably, as in the daytime, not in revelry and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy,

**13:14** but clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ, and do not make provision for the flesh to fulfill its lusts.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Greek reads "by God" (hypo theou) in both clauses, underscoring direct divine agency in ordaining civil authority.
- **13:9** — The critical Greek text cites four commandments from the Decalogue before summarizing the law with Leviticus 19:18; later manuscripts add "You shall not bear false witness."
- **13:11** — The critical Greek text reads "for you" (hymas) before shifting to "our salvation" (hemon), whereas later manuscripts harmonize the first pronoun to "for us."
