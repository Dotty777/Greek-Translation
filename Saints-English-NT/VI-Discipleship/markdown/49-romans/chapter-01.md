## Text

**1:1** Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, called as an apostle, separated for the gospel of God,

**1:2** which He promised beforehand through His prophets in the sacred Scriptures,

**1:3** concerning His Son, who came from the seed of David according to the flesh,

**1:4** and was appointed Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,

**1:5** through whom we received grace and apostleship for the obedience of faith among all the nations on behalf of His name,

**1:6** among whom you also are those who are called of Jesus Christ,

**1:7** to all who are in Rome, beloved by God, called as saints: Grace be to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:8** First, I give thanks to my God through Jesus Christ concerning all of you, because your faith is proclaimed throughout the whole world.

**1:9** For God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of His Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you,

**1:10** always in my prayers, pleading that somehow now at last I may be granted a way in the will of God to come to you.

**1:11** For I deeply long to see you, so that I may impart to you some spiritual gift in order that you may be firmly established—

**1:12** that is, to be comforted together among you through each other's faith, both yours and mine.

**1:13** Now I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, that many times I purposed to come to you—and was hindered until now—that I might obtain some fruit among you also, just as among the rest of the Gentiles.

**1:14** Both to Greeks and to barbarians, both to the wise and to the foolish, I am a debtor.

**1:15** Thus, as far as it depends on me, I am eager to proclaim the gospel also to you who are in Rome.

**1:16** For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is God's power for salvation to everyone who believes, first to the Jew and also to the Greek.

**1:17** For in it God's righteousness is revealed from faith to faith, even as it is written, "The righteous one shall live by faith."

**1:18** For the wrath of God is being revealed from heaven against all impiety and unrighteousness of men who suppress the truth in unrighteousness,

**1:19** because what may be known of God is manifest among them, for God has made it manifest to them.

**1:20** For His invisible realities, from the creation of the world, are clearly seen, being understood through the things that have been made—both His everlasting power and His divine nature—so that they are without excuse;

**1:21** because, though knowing God, they neither glorified Him as God nor gave Him thanks, but became vain in their reasonings, and their senseless heart was darkened.

**1:22** Declaring themselves to be wise, they became fools,

**1:23** and exchanged the glory of the incorruptible God for a likeness of the image of corruptible man, and of birds, and of four-footed beasts, and of creeping things.

**1:24** Therefore God delivered them over in the lusts of their hearts to uncleanness, for their bodies to be dishonored among themselves—

**1:25** those who exchanged the truth of God for the lie, and revered and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed throughout the ages. Amen.

**1:26** For this cause God delivered them over to dishonorable passions; for their females exchanged natural relations for that which is against nature,

**1:27** and likewise also the males, forsaking natural relations with the female, were inflamed in their craving for one another, males with males committing indecency, and receiving in themselves the recompense that was due for their error.

**1:28** And just as they did not approve of keeping God in their knowledge, God delivered them over to a reprobate mind, to do the things that are not fitting,

**1:29** being filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, covetousness, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, guile, malignity; whisperers,

**1:30** slanderers, haters of God, insolent, proud, boasters, devisers of evil, disobedient to parents,

**1:31** without understanding, faithless, without natural affection, unmerciful—

**1:32** who, knowing full well the righteous decree of God, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do them, but also give hearty approval to those who practice them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Greek participle horisthentos may be rendered either 'appointed' (installed in messianic office) or 'declared' (publicly demonstrated). The phrase 'Spirit of holiness' reflects a Semitic idiom referring to the Holy Spirit.
- **1:16** — The Greek critical text reads simply 'the gospel,' omitting 'of Christ,' which appears in later manuscripts.
- **1:17** — Quoting Habakkuk 2:4, the Greek phrase 'by faith' (ek pisteos) may modify either 'shall live' ('The righteous one shall live by faith') or 'the righteous one' ('The one who is righteous by faith shall live').
- **1:29** — The Greek critical text lists four vices in the opening group, omitting 'sexual immorality' (porneia), and similarly lists four adjectives in verse 31 without 'unforgiving' (aspondous), both of which appear in later manuscripts.
