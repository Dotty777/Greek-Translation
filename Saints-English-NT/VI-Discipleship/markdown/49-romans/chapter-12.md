## Text

**12:1** I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the compassions of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, well-pleasing to God, which is your reasonable worship;

**12:2** and do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of the mind, that you may prove what is the will of God, that which is good and well-pleasing and perfect.

**12:3** For through the grace given to me I say to everyone who is among you not to think more highly than he ought to think, but to think with sober-mindedness, according as God has apportioned to each a measure of faith.

**12:4** For even as in one body we have many members, yet all the members do not have the same function,

**12:5** so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members one of another.

**12:6** Now having differing gifts according to the grace given to us: whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith;

**12:7** or ministry, in our ministering; or he who teaches, in his teaching;

**12:8** or he who exhorts, in his exhortation; he who imparts, in simplicity; he who leads, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.

**12:9** Let love be without hypocrisy: abhorring that which is evil, cleaving to that which is good;

**12:10** in brotherly love tenderly affectionate one toward another; in honor outdoing one another;

**12:11** in diligence not slothful; in spirit fervent; serving the Lord;

**12:12** in hope rejoicing; in tribulation enduring; in prayer continuing steadfastly;

**12:13** sharing in the needs of the saints; pursuing hospitality.

**12:14** Bless those who persecute; bless, and do not curse.

**12:15** Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.

**12:16** Be of the same mind one toward another; not setting your mind on lofty things, but associating with the lowly. Do not become wise in your own estimation.

**12:17** Repaying no one evil for evil; taking thought for honorable things before all men;

**12:18** if it is possible, as much as depends on you, living at peace with all men;

**12:19** not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to wrath, for it is written, "To Me belongs vengeance; I will recompense, says the Lord."

**12:20** Rather, "If your enemy hungers, feed him; if he thirsts, give him a drink; for in doing this you shall heap coals of fire upon his head."

**12:21** Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil through good.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Greek logiken latreian ('reasonable worship' or 'spiritual service') denotes worship offered with the whole rational self, not merely ritual formality.
- **12:2** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads tou noos ('of the mind') without a possessive pronoun, and in v. 14 omits 'you' after 'those who persecute.'
- **12:3** — Paul weaves a fourfold Greek wordplay around phroneo ('think'): hyperphronein ('think too highly'), phronein ('think'), and sophronein ('be sober-minded').
- **12:16** — Greek tois tapeinois is ambiguous between neuter ('lowly things/tasks') and masculine ('lowly persons'); 'the lowly' preserves that breadth.
- **12:20** — Quoting Proverbs 25:21-22, the Greek shifts from second-person plural (vv. 1-19) to second-person singular here and in the imperatives of v. 21.
