## Text

**5:1** Having been justified, therefore, by faith, let us have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ,

**5:2** through whom also we have gained access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we exult in the hope of the glory of God.

**5:3** And not only so, but we also exult in tribulations, knowing that tribulation works steadfastness,

**5:4** and steadfastness, tested character; and tested character, hope.

**5:5** And hope does not put to shame, for the love of God has been poured forth in our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us.

**5:6** For even while we were still weak, at the appointed time Christ died for the ungodly.

**5:7** For scarcely will one die for a righteous man; yet perhaps for the good man someone might even dare to die.

**5:8** But God shows forth His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

**5:9** Much more, therefore, having now been justified by His blood, we shall be saved through Him from the wrath.

**5:10** For if, while being enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved by His life.

**5:11** And not only so, but we also exult in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.

**5:12** Therefore, even as through one man sin entered into the world, and death through sin, so also death passed through to all men, because all sinned—

**5:13** for until the law, sin was in the world, yet sin is not reckoned when there is no law.

**5:14** Yet death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those who did not sin in the likeness of Adam's transgression, who is a type of the One who was to come.

**5:15** But not as the trespass, so also is the gracious gift. For if by the trespass of the one the many died, much more did the grace of God and the gift in the grace of the one man Jesus Christ abound to the many.

**5:16** And not as through the one who sinned is the gift; for the judgment came from one to condemnation, but the gracious gift came from many trespasses to justification.

**5:17** For if by the trespass of the one death reigned through the one, much more shall those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the One, Jesus Christ.

**5:18** Therefore, even as through one trespass it resulted in condemnation for all men, so also through one act of righteousness it resulted in justification of life for all men.

**5:19** For even as through the disobedience of the one man the many were constituted sinners, so also through the obedience of the One the many shall be constituted righteous.

**5:20** Now the law entered alongside, that the trespass might multiply; yet where sin multiplied, grace abounded far more,

**5:21** that just as sin reigned in death, so too might grace reign through righteousness to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The earliest and strongest manuscripts read the hortatory subjunctive "let us have" (echomen), whereas later manuscripts read the indicative "we have" (echomen with omicron).
- **5:6** — The Greek critical text contains a double eti ("still/yet"), reflected here by "even while we were still weak."
- **5:12** — The phrase eph' ho is much debated, commonly understood as causal ("because"), though sometimes taken as consecutive ("with the result that") or relative ("in whom"). Paul breaks off his comparison here and resumes it in 5:18.
