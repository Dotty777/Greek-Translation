## Text

**3:1** For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles—

**3:2** if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of the grace of God given to me for you;

**3:3** by revelation the mystery was made known to me, just as I have written beforehand in brief,

**3:4** by which, as you read, you are able to perceive my insight into the mystery of Christ,

**3:5** which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:

**3:6** that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, and members of the same body, and fellow partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,

**3:7** of which I was made a minister according to the gift of the grace of God that was given to me according to the working of His power—

**3:8** to me, less than the least of all saints, this grace was given—to proclaim to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ,

**3:9** and to enlighten all as to what is the stewardship of the mystery that was hidden from the ages in God, who created all things,

**3:10** so that now the manifold wisdom of God might be made known through the church to the principalities and authorities in the heavenly places,

**3:11** according to the purpose of the ages which He accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord,

**3:12** in whom we have boldness and access in confidence through faith in Him.

**3:13** Therefore I ask that you not lose heart in my tribulations on your behalf, which is your glory.

**3:14** For this cause I bow my knees to the Father,

**3:15** from whom every family in the heavens and upon the earth is named,

**3:16** that He may grant to you, according to the riches of His glory, to be made strong with power through His Spirit in the inner man,

**3:17** so that Christ may dwell through faith in your hearts in love; being rooted and founded,

**3:18** that you may have full strength to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

**3:19** and to know the love of Christ which transcends knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.

**3:20** Now to Him who is able to do beyond all things, immeasurably more than what we ask or conceive, according to the power that is working within us,

**3:21** to Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:9** — The Greek text reads "stewardship" (oikonomia) rather than "fellowship" (koinonia) and omits "through Jesus Christ" at the end of the verse.
- **3:12** — The Greek phrase dia tes pisteos autou is traditionally rendered "through faith in Him," though it may also be understood as "through His faithfulness."
- **3:14** — The Greek text reads simply "to the Father," omitting "of our Lord Jesus Christ" found in later manuscripts. Verses 14 and 15 feature a Greek wordplay between "Father" (pater) and "family" (patria).
- **3:17** — Following the punctuation of the Greek text, "in love" is joined to Christ dwelling in believers' hearts, though it is often construed with "rooted and founded."
