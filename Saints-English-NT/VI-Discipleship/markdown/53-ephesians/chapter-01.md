## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus and the faithful in Christ Jesus:

**1:2** Grace be to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,

**1:4** even as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blemish in His sight in love,

**1:5** having predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,

**1:6** to the praise of the glory of His grace, which He graciously bestowed upon us in the Beloved,

**1:7** in whom we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of trespasses, according to the riches of His grace,

**1:8** which He caused to abound toward us in all wisdom and understanding,

**1:9** making known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure which He purposed beforehand in Him,

**1:10** for the administration of the fullness of the times, to gather up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things upon the earth—in Him,

**1:11** in whom we also were chosen as a heritage, having been predestined according to the purpose of Him who works all things according to the counsel of His will,

**1:12** that we who first set our hope in Christ should be to the praise of His glory;

**1:13** in whom you also, having heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation—in whom also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,

**1:14** who is the pledge of our inheritance, for the redemption of the acquired possession, to the praise of His glory.

**1:15** For this reason I also, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and that which is toward all the saints,

**1:16** do not cease giving thanks on your behalf, remembering you in my prayers,

**1:17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may grant to you a spirit of wisdom and of revelation in the full knowledge of Him,

**1:18** with the eyes of your heart enlightened, that you may know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the saints,

**1:19** and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of His strength,

**1:20** which He has worked in Christ, raising Him from the dead and seating Him at His right hand in the heavenly places,

**1:21** far above every principality and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in that which is to come;

**1:22** and He subjected all things beneath His feet, and gave Him as head over all things to the church,

**1:23** which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all things in all.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — In Greek, "in love" can either conclude verse 4 ("holy and without blemish in His sight in love") or introduce the participle in verse 5 ("in love having predestined us").
- **1:10** — The Greek verb rendered "to gather up" (anakephalaiosasthai) literally means "to sum up" or "to bring together under one head."
- **1:11** — The Greek passive verb eklerothemen can mean either "we were chosen as a heritage" (as God's own portion) or "we obtained an inheritance."
- **1:15** — The Greek text omits the word "love" found in later manuscripts, leaving the article to refer back to the Ephesians' faith (or faithfulness) toward all the saints.
