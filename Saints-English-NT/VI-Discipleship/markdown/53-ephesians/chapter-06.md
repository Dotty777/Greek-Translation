## Text

**6:1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is just.

**6:2** "Honor your father and your mother"—which is the first commandment with a promise—

**6:3** "that it may go well with you, and you shall be long-lived upon the earth."

**6:4** And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but nurture them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

**6:5** Bondservants, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as to Christ,

**6:6** not by way of eye-service as pleasers of men, but as bondservants of Christ doing the will of God, from the soul

**6:7** serving with goodwill, as to the Lord and not to men,

**6:8** knowing that whatever good thing each one does, he shall receive this back from the Lord, whether bondservant or free.

**6:9** And masters, do the same things toward them, laying aside threatening, knowing that the Master of both them and you is in the heavens, and there is no partiality with Him.

**6:10** Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of His strength.

**6:11** Put on the complete armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the stratagems of the devil;

**6:12** for our wrestling is not against blood and flesh, but against the principalities, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly places.

**6:13** Therefore take up the complete armor of God, that you may be able to resist in the evil day, and having accomplished all things, to stand.

**6:14** Stand therefore, having girded your loins with truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,

**6:15** and having shod your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace,

**6:16** in all circumstances taking up the shield of faith, with which you shall be able to extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one;

**6:17** and receive the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,

**6:18** through all prayer and supplication, praying at every season in the Spirit, and to this end keeping watch with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints,

**6:19** and on my behalf, that a word may be given to me in the opening of my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,

**6:20** for which I serve as an ambassador in a chain, that in it I may speak with boldness, as I must speak.

**6:21** Now that you also may know the things concerning me and how I am faring, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, will make all things known to you,

**6:22** whom I have sent to you for this very purpose, that you may know the things concerning us, and that he may encourage your hearts.

**6:23** Peace be to the brethren, and love together with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**6:24** Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption.

## Translator Notes

- **6:9** — The Greek word kyrios means both "master" and "Lord," creating a deliberate wordplay between earthly masters and the heavenly Master. The critical text reads "of both them and you," underscoring Christ's impartial authority over bondservant and master alike.
- **6:12** — The Greek text places "blood and flesh" in that order and reads "of this darkness" without the phrase "of this age" found in later manuscripts.
- **6:16** — The critical text reads en pasin ("in all circumstances" or "with all of these") rather than epi pasin ("above all").
- **6:24** — Literally "in incorruption" or "in imperishability," indicating an undying, incorruptible love for Christ. The earliest manuscripts conclude the epistle here without "Amen."
