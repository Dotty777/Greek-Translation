## Text

**4:1** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is about to judge the living and the dead, and by His appearing and His kingdom:

**4:2** Preach the word; stand ready in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, and exhort with all longsuffering and instruction.

**4:3** For a time shall come when they will not bear with sound doctrine, but according to their own desires they will heap up teachers for themselves, having itching ears,

**4:4** and they shall turn away their hearing from the truth, and be turned aside to fables.

**4:5** But you, remain sober-minded in all things, endure affliction, perform the work of an evangelist, fully accomplish your ministry.

**4:6** For I am even now being poured out as a drink offering, and the season of my departure has arrived.

**4:7** I have fought the noble fight, I have completed the course, I have preserved the faith.

**4:8** Now there is reserved for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, shall award to me on that day—and not to me alone, but also to all who have loved His appearing.

**4:9** Strive to come to me quickly;

**4:10** for Demas forsook me, having loved this present age, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

**4:11** Luke alone is with me. Take Mark along and bring him with you, for he is helpful to me in ministry.

**4:12** As for Tychicus, I sent him to Ephesus.

**4:13** When you come, bring the cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus, and the scrolls, above all the parchments.

**4:14** Alexander the coppersmith inflicted much harm upon me—the Lord shall repay him according to his works.

**4:15** You also must guard yourself against him, for he fiercely opposed our words.

**4:16** At my first defense no one came to my side, but all forsook me—may it not be reckoned against them.

**4:17** Yet the Lord stood at my side and gave me strength, so that through me the proclamation might be brought to fullness and all the Gentiles might hear; and I was delivered from the mouth of the lion.

**4:18** The Lord shall deliver me from every wicked deed and preserve me safely into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**4:19** Greet Prisca and Aquila, as well as the household of Onesiphorus.

**4:20** Erastus stayed at Corinth, and Trophimus I left sick in Miletus.

**4:21** Strive to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brethren send you greetings.

**4:22** May the Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The critical Greek text reads "Christ Jesus" and introduces "His appearing and His kingdom" with "and" as part of the solemn adjuration, whereas later Byzantine manuscripts read "the Lord Jesus Christ" and "at His appearing."
- **4:6** — The verb rendered "being poured out as a drink offering" recalls the sacrificial libation (cf. Philippians 2:17), while the Greek noun for "departure" (analysis) was commonly used for unmooring a ship or striking camp.
- **4:14** — The Greek text here uses the future indicative "shall repay" as a confident statement echoing Psalm 62:12 (LXX 61:13), whereas later manuscripts read the optative wish "may the Lord repay."
- **4:22** — The critical text reads simply "the Lord" without "Jesus Christ" and ends without "Amen." Notably, the pronoun shifts from singular "your spirit" (addressed to Timothy) to plural "you all" (extending the blessing to the congregation).
