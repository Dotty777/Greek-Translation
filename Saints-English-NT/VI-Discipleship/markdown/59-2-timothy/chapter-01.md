## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, according to the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus,

**1:2** To Timothy, beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** I give thanks to God, whom I serve from my forefathers with a clear conscience, as I unceasingly remember you in my supplications night and day,

**1:4** earnestly longing to see you, mindful of your tears, so that I may be filled with joy,

**1:5** being reminded of the unfeigned faith within you, which dwelt first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it is in you as well.

**1:6** For this cause I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.

**1:7** For God did not give us a spirit of timidity, but of power and love and self-discipline.

**1:8** Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me His prisoner; rather, share in hardship for the gospel according to the power of God,

**1:9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before times eternal,

**1:10** yet has now been made manifest through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and incorruption to light through the gospel,

**1:11** for which I was appointed a herald, and an apostle, and a teacher.

**1:12** For this cause I also suffer these things, yet I am not ashamed; for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that He is able to guard my deposit until that Day.

**1:13** Retain the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**1:14** Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

**1:15** You know this, that all who are in Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**1:16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain;

**1:17** rather, when he arrived in Rome, he diligently searched for me and found me—

**1:18** may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that Day—and you know very well how many ways he ministered in Ephesus.

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — The Greek term rendered "timidity" (deilia) denotes cowardice or faintheartedness, while "self-discipline" (sophronismos) conveys sober-mindedness and self-control.
- **1:11** — The critical Greek text reads "a herald, and an apostle, and a teacher," without the phrase "of the Gentiles" found in later manuscripts.
- **1:12** — "My deposit" (ten paratheken mou) may refer either to what Paul has entrusted to God for safekeeping or to the gospel ministry God has entrusted to Paul (compare 1:14).
- **1:18** — The solemn repetition "the Lord ... from the Lord" reflects the Greek text (ho kyrios ... para kyriou), emphasizing the mercy granted on the final Day of judgment.
