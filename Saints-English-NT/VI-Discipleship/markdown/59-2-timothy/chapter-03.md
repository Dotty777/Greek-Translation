## Text

**3:1** But understand this, that in the last days grievous times will set in:

**3:2** for men will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, revilers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,

**3:3** without natural affection, irreconcilable, slanderers, unrestrained, fierce, haters of what is good,

**3:4** treacherous, reckless, swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

**3:5** holding an outward form of godliness, yet having denied its power. Turn away from such as these.

**3:6** For among them are those who creep into households and take captive weak women weighed down with sins, swayed by various passions,

**3:7** ever learning, yet never able to attain to the knowledge of the truth.

**3:8** And just as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so these also withstand the truth—men corrupted in mind, disqualified regarding the faith.

**3:9** Yet they will advance no further, for their folly will be evident to all, just as theirs also became.

**3:10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my longsuffering, my love, my steadfastness,

**3:11** my persecutions and sufferings—such as befell me at Antioch, at Iconium, and at Lystra—what persecutions I endured! Yet out of them all the Lord delivered me.

**3:12** Indeed, all who desire to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted;

**3:13** whereas wicked men and impostors will advance from bad to worse, leading others astray and being led astray themselves.

**3:14** You, however, abide in the things you have learned and of which you have become convinced, knowing from whom you learned them,

**3:15** and that from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

**3:16** All Scripture is God-breathed and beneficial for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for discipline in righteousness,

**3:17** so that the man of God may be competent, thoroughly furnished for every good work.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — Jannes and Jambres are the names preserved in Jewish tradition for the Egyptian magicians who opposed Moses (Exodus 7:11, 22).
- **3:14** — The Greek pronoun "whom" (tinon) is plural in the critical text, encompassing not only Paul but also Timothy's mother Eunice and grandmother Lois (compare 1:5; 3:15).
- **3:16** — "God-breathed" translates theopneustos; while the clause may also be read "Every God-breathed Scripture is also beneficial," the predicate construction "All Scripture is God-breathed and beneficial" is the standard reading.
