## Text

**11:1** Oh, that you would bear with me in a little foolishness—yet indeed, do bear with me!

**11:2** For I am jealous for you with a godly jealousy, because I betrothed you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ;

**11:3** yet I fear lest somehow, just as the serpent deceived Eve by his craftiness, your minds should be corrupted from the sincerity and purity that are toward Christ.

**11:4** For if indeed the one who comes preaches another Jesus whom we did not preach, or if you receive a different spirit which you did not receive, or a different gospel which you did not accept, you bear with it readily enough!

**11:5** For I consider that I am in no way inferior to these superlative apostles.

**11:6** And even if I am unskilled in speech, yet I am not so in knowledge; rather, in every way we have made this manifest to you in all things.

**11:7** Or did I commit a sin by humbling myself so that you might be exalted, because I preached the gospel of God to you without charge?

**11:8** I plundered other churches, taking wages from them for my ministry to you,

**11:9** and when I was present with you and fell into need, I was a burden to no one; for what I lacked, the brethren who came from Macedonia fully supplied. And in everything I kept myself from being burdensome to you, and so I shall keep myself.

**11:10** As the truth of Christ is in me, this boasting of mine shall not be silenced in the regions of Achaia.

**11:11** Why? Because I do not love you? God knows.

**11:12** Yet what I am doing I shall also continue to do, so that I may cut off the opportunity of those who desire an opportunity, that in the matter of which they boast they may be found to be just as we are.

**11:13** For such men are false apostles, treacherous workmen, disguising themselves as apostles of Christ;

**11:14** and it is no marvel, for Satan himself disguises himself as an angel of light;

**11:15** therefore it is no great thing if his ministers also disguise themselves as ministers of righteousness, whose end shall be according to their works.

**11:16** Again I say, let no one suppose me to be a fool—yet if otherwise, receive me even as a fool, so that I also may boast a little.

**11:17** What I speak, I do not speak according to the Lord, but as it were in foolishness, in this assurance of boasting.

**11:18** Since many boast according to the flesh, I also shall boast.

**11:19** For you gladly bear with fools, being so wise yourselves!

**11:20** For you bear with it if someone brings you into bondage, if someone devours you, if someone takes advantage of you, if someone exalts himself, if someone strikes you in the face.

**11:21** To my shame I say it, as though we have been too weak! Yet in whatever anyone dares to boast—I speak in foolishness—I dare also.

**11:22** Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they the seed of Abraham? So am I.

**11:23** Are they ministers of Christ? I speak as one out of his mind—I am more so: in labors far more abundantly, in imprisonments far more often, in beatings beyond measure, in deaths many times;

**11:24** from the Jews five times I received forty lashes less one;

**11:25** three times I was beaten with rods, once I was stoned, three times I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I have spent in the deep;

**11:26** on frequent journeys, in perils of rivers, in perils of robbers, in perils from my own people, in perils from the Gentiles, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils on the sea, in perils among false brethren,

**11:27** in labor and hardship, in sleeplessness often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.

**11:28** Apart from the other things, there is the daily pressure upon me: my anxious care for all the churches.

**11:29** Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn?

**11:30** If I must boast, I shall boast of the things concerning my weakness.

**11:31** The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, who is blessed forever, knows that I do not lie.

**11:32** In Damascus the governor under King Aretas was guarding the city of the Damascenes in order to seize me,

**11:33** and I was lowered in a woven basket through a window in the wall, and escaped from his hands.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — The critical text includes 'and purity' (καὶ τῆς ἁγνότητος), echoing the 'pure virgin' imagery of 11:2; some later manuscripts omit this phrase.
- **11:5** — The ironic Greek phrase τῶν ὑπερλίαν ἀποστόλων (literally 'the super-extra apostles,' rendered 'these superlative apostles') is Paul's sarcastic designation for his rivals in Corinth (repeated in 12:11).
- **11:23** — The critical text follows the early manuscript order placing imprisonments before beatings ('in labors far more abundantly, in imprisonments far more often, in beatings beyond measure').
