## Text

**5:1** For we know that if our earthly tent-dwelling is torn down, we have a building from God, a house not fashioned by hands, eternal in the heavens.

**5:2** Indeed, in this we groan, yearning deeply to be clothed over with our dwelling from heaven,

**5:3** if indeed, once clothed, we shall not be found naked.

**5:4** For while we remain in this tent, we groan under our burden, because we do not wish to be unclothed, but rather to be clothed over, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.

**5:5** Now the One who has prepared us for this very purpose is God, who has given to us the pledge of the Spirit.

**5:6** Therefore we are always of good courage, knowing that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord—

**5:7** for by faith we walk, and not by sight—

**5:8** we are of good courage, indeed, and are well pleased rather to be away from the body and to make our home with the Lord.

**5:9** Therefore we also make it our ambition, whether at home or away, to be pleasing to Him.

**5:10** For all of us must appear openly before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive what is due for the deeds done through the body, according to what he has done, whether good or evil.

**5:11** Knowing therefore the fear of the Lord, we persuade men; yet to God we are clearly known, and I trust that we are clearly known in your consciences as well.

**5:12** We are not commending ourselves to you once again, but rather giving you an occasion to boast on our behalf, so that you may have a reply for those who boast in outward appearance and not in the heart.

**5:13** For if we are beside ourselves, it is for God; if we are in our right mind, it is for your sake.

**5:14** For the love of Christ constrains us, since we have reached this conviction: that One died on behalf of all, and therefore all have died;

**5:15** and He died on behalf of all, so that those who live should no longer live for themselves, but for Him who died for their sake and was raised.

**5:16** Consequently, from now on we know no one according to the flesh; even if we once knew Christ according to the flesh, yet now we know Him in that way no longer.

**5:17** Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation; the old things have passed away; behold, new things have come into being.

**5:18** Now all these things are from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ and gave to us the ministry of reconciliation,

**5:19** namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not reckoning their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the word of reconciliation.

**5:20** Therefore we serve as ambassadors on behalf of Christ, as though God were making His appeal through us; we implore you for the sake of Christ: be reconciled to God.

**5:21** The One who knew no sin, He made to be sin on our behalf, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — Following the critical Greek reading "once clothed" (endysamenoi); some manuscripts read "once unclothed" (ekdysamenoi).
- **5:14** — Following the critical Greek text, which omits "if" before "One died," presenting the statement directly rather than conditionally.
- **5:17** — The terse Greek phrase "kaine ktisis" has no expressed verb and may be rendered either "there is a new creation" or "he is a new creation." The critical Greek text also omits "all things" in the final clause, reading literally "behold, new things have come into being."
- **5:21** — Greek "hamartia" ("sin") here is often understood against the Septuagint background as bearing the sacrificial sense of a "sin offering," while Paul's wording concisely states "sin."
