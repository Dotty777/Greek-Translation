## Text

**2:1** For I resolved this for myself, not to come to you once more in sorrow.

**2:2** For if I cause you sorrow, then who is there to make me glad, except the very one who is made sorrowful by me?

**2:3** And I wrote this very thing lest, upon coming, I should suffer sorrow from those over whom I ought to rejoice; for I am confident in all of you that my joy belongs to all of you.

**2:4** For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you with many tears, not so that you might be grieved, but so that you might know the love that I bear so abundantly for you.

**2:5** Now if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused sorrow to me, but in part—lest I be too severe—to all of you.

**2:6** For such a man, this punishment by the majority is sufficient,

**2:7** so that, on the contrary, you should rather forgive and console him, lest somehow such a one be swallowed up by excessive sorrow.

**2:8** Therefore I exhort you to confirm your love for him.

**2:9** For it was also for this purpose that I wrote, so that I might know your proven character, whether you are obedient in everything.

**2:10** And to whomever you forgive anything, I forgive also; for indeed, what I have forgiven—if I have forgiven anything—I have done for your sake in the presence of Christ,

**2:11** so that Satan may not gain an advantage over us, for we are not unaware of his designs.

**2:12** Now when I arrived at Troas for the gospel of Christ, and a door stood open for me in the Lord,

**2:13** I had no rest in my spirit because I did not find my brother Titus; so taking leave of them, I departed for Macedonia.

**2:14** But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumphal procession in Christ, and makes manifest through us the fragrance of the knowledge of Him in every place.

**2:15** For we are the sweet aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing:

**2:16** to the one, a fragrance from death to death; to the other, a fragrance from life to life. And who is sufficient for such things?

**2:17** For we are not like the many, peddling the word of God; rather, as from sincerity, as from God, we speak before God in Christ.

## Translator Notes

- **2:10** — Greek en prosopo Christou literally means "in the face (or presence) of Christ," underscoring that Paul grants forgiveness under Christ's direct gaze and authority.
- **2:14** — Greek thriambeuo evokes a Roman triumphal procession in which a conqueror leads captives, portraying Paul as a conquered servant through whom the knowledge of Christ is spread abroad.
- **2:17** — The critical text reads "the many" (hoi polloi), contrasting Paul with numerous speakers who peddled religious teaching for financial gain (kapeleuontes).
