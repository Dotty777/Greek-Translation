## Text

**12:1** Boasting is necessary; though indeed it is not profitable, yet I shall move on to visions and revelations of the Lord.

**12:2** I know a man in Christ who fourteen years ago—whether in the body I do not know, or outside the body I do not know, God knows—such a man was caught up as far as the third heaven.

**12:3** And I know such a man—whether in the body or apart from the body I do not know, God knows—

**12:4** that he was snatched up into Paradise and heard unutterable sayings, which no man is permitted to speak.

**12:5** On behalf of such a man I shall boast, yet on behalf of myself I shall not boast, except in weaknesses.

**12:6** For even if I should desire to boast, I shall not be a fool, because I shall speak the truth; yet I refrain, lest anyone should credit to me more than what he sees in me or hears from me,

**12:7** even by reason of the surpassing greatness of the revelations. Therefore, lest I should exalt myself beyond measure, a thorn in the flesh was given to me, a messenger of Satan, to batter me—lest I should exalt myself beyond measure.

**12:8** Concerning this I appealed to the Lord three times that it might depart from me.

**12:9** And He has said to me, "My grace suffices for you, for power is brought to perfection in weakness." Most gladly therefore I shall rather boast in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may dwell upon me.

**12:10** Therefore I delight in weaknesses, in insults, in necessities, in persecutions and distresses, for Christ's sake; for whenever I am weak, then I am mighty.

**12:11** I have become a fool! You compelled me to it; for I ought to have been commended by you. Indeed, I was in no way inferior to these superlative apostles, even though I am nothing.

**12:12** Truly the signs of an apostle were accomplished among you with all perseverance, by signs and wonders and mighty works.

**12:13** For in what respect were you made inferior to the rest of the churches, except that I myself was not a burden to you? Forgive me this wrong!

**12:14** Behold, this third time I am ready to come to you, and I shall not be a burden; for I do not seek what is yours, but you. For children ought not to lay up treasure for their parents, but parents for their children.

**12:15** And I shall most gladly spend and be utterly spent for the sake of your souls. If I love you the more abundantly, am I to be loved the less?

**12:16** Be that as it may, I did not weigh you down; yet, being crafty, I caught you by deceit!

**12:17** Did I take advantage of you through any of those whom I have sent to you?

**12:18** I urged Titus to go, and sent the brother along with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same spirit? Did we not walk in the very same footsteps?

**12:19** Have you been supposing all along that we are making our defense to you? It is before God in Christ that we speak; and all these things, beloved, are for your building up,

**12:20** for I fear lest somehow, when I come, I may find you not as I wish, and I myself may be found by you such as you do not wish—lest there be strife, jealousy, outbursts of wrath, selfish ambitions, slanders, whisperings, conceits, disorders;

**12:21** lest when I come again my God should humble me before you, and I should mourn over many of those who sinned formerly and have not repented of the impurity, sexual immorality, and licentiousness which they practiced.

## Translator Notes

- **12:7** — Following the critical text punctuation, 'even by reason of the surpassing greatness of the revelations' completes the thought of 12:6, and Paul frames the thorn-in-the-flesh clause by repeating 'lest I should exalt myself beyond measure.'
- **12:9** — The critical text reads simply 'power' (ἡ γὰρ δύναμις) without 'My,' though Christ's power is clearly intended. The verb ἐπισκηνώσῃ literally means 'pitch a tent over' or 'tabernacle upon,' recalling God's dwelling presence.
- **12:19** — The critical text reads Πάλαι ('all along' or 'for a long time') rather than Πάλιν ('again'), which appears in later manuscripts.
