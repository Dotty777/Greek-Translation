## Text

**10:1** Now I myself, Paul, appeal to you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ—I who when face to face am lowly among you, yet when absent am bold toward you;

**10:2** and I implore you that when I am present I may not have to be bold with that confidence by which I intend to be daring against some who regard us as walking according to the flesh.

**10:3** For although we walk in the flesh, we do not conduct our warfare according to the flesh—

**10:4** for the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh, but mighty before God for the tearing down of strongholds—tearing down reasonings

**10:5** and every lofty height that raises itself against the knowledge of God, and leading every thought captive into the obedience of Christ,

**10:6** and holding ourselves ready to punish every act of disobedience, whenever your obedience is made complete.

**10:7** Look at what is before your face. If anyone is convinced in himself that he belongs to Christ, let him consider this again within himself, that just as he belongs to Christ, so also do we.

**10:8** For even if I boast somewhat more regarding our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for tearing you down, I shall not be put to shame,

**10:9** lest I should appear as if I sought to terrify you through my letters;

**10:10** for "His letters," one says, "are weighty and mighty, yet his bodily presence is weak, and his speech is despised."

**10:11** Let such a person consider this: that just as we are in word through letters when absent, such shall we also be in deed when present.

**10:12** For we do not dare to rank or compare ourselves with certain ones who commend themselves; rather, measuring themselves by themselves and comparing themselves with themselves, they are without understanding.

**10:13** We, however, shall not boast beyond measure, but according to the measure of the domain that God apportioned to us as a measure, to reach even as far as you—

**10:14** for we are not overextending ourselves as though we did not reach to you, since we came all the way even to you with the gospel of Christ—

**10:15** not boasting beyond measure in the labors of others, yet having hope that, as your faith grows, we shall be greatly enlarged among you according to our domain to overflowing,

**10:16** so as to preach the gospel in the regions beyond you, and not to boast in another's domain concerning work already prepared.

**10:17** Yet let him who boasts, boast in the Lord;

**10:18** for it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Greek dative τῷ θεῷ ('before God') may denote God's appraisal ('mighty in God's sight'), divine agency ('mighty through God'), or a Semitic-style superlative ('divinely mighty').
- **10:7** — The verb βλέπετε can be read as an imperative ('Look at what is before your face'), an indicative ('You look at outward appearances'), or an interrogative ('Do you look at outward appearances?').
- **10:12** — Some later manuscripts omit 'they are without understanding' (οὐ συνιᾶσιν) along with 'we' (ἡμεῖς) at the start of 10:13; the critical text retains both.
