## Text

**9:1** For indeed, concerning the ministry for the saints, it is unnecessary for me to write to you,

**9:2** for I know your readiness, concerning which I boast on your behalf to the Macedonians, that Achaia has been prepared since last year; and your zeal has roused the majority of them.

**9:3** Yet I have sent the brethren, lest our boast concerning you prove empty in this particular, so that, just as I was saying, you may be prepared,

**9:4** lest somehow, if Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we—not to say you—should be put to shame in this confidence.

**9:5** Therefore I considered it necessary to urge the brethren to go on ahead to you and arrange in advance your previously promised blessing, so that it may be ready in this way as a blessing and not as covetousness.

**9:6** Now know this: he who sows sparingly shall also reap sparingly, and he who sows with blessings shall also reap with blessings.

**9:7** Let each one give just as he has resolved in his heart, not out of sorrow or under compulsion; for God loves a joyful giver.

**9:8** And God has power to make every grace abound to you, so that in every respect, at all times, having all sufficiency, you may abound toward every good work;

**9:9** (just as it is written: "He has scattered abroad, he has given to the poor; his righteousness abides forever";

**9:10** now He who provides seed to the sower and bread for food shall supply and multiply your seed, and shall increase the produce of your righteousness);

**9:11** being enriched in every respect for all generosity, which through us brings about thanksgiving to God—

**9:12** for the ministry of this sacred service is not only fully supplying the wants of the saints, but is also overflowing through many thanksgivings to God—

**9:13** by the proof afforded by this ministry, they glorify God for the submission of your confession to the gospel of Christ, and for the generosity of your fellowship toward them and toward all,

**9:14** and in their supplication on your behalf, yearning for you because of the surpassing grace of God upon you.

**9:15** Thanks be to God for His gift beyond all telling!

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — "Blessing" (eulogia) here designates a generous, willing gift (cf. Gen 33:11), set in contrast to "covetousness" (pleonexia), which implies reluctant greed or a begrudged exaction.
- **9:6** — "With blessings" (ep' eulogiais) retains the lexical echo of "blessing" in verse 5, functioning idiomatically to mean bountifully or generously.
- **9:9** — Paul quotes Psalm 112:9 (LXX 111:9), where the pronouns refer to the generous righteous person who fears the Lord, though some readers also discern God's own liberality behind the words.
- **9:10** — The critical text employs future indicative verbs ("shall supply and multiply... and shall increase") as an assured promise, rather than the optative wish ("may He supply...") found in later manuscripts.
