## Text

**4:1** Therefore, having this ministry just as we have been shown mercy, we do not lose heart;

**4:2** rather, we have renounced the secret things of shame, neither walking in cunning nor adulterating the word of God, but by the open manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every human conscience in the presence of God.

**4:3** Yet even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing,

**4:4** in whose case the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelieving, lest the radiant light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine forth.

**4:5** For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for the sake of Jesus.

**4:6** For God, who spoke, "Out of darkness light shall shine," is the One who has shone in our hearts to bring the illumination of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

**4:7** Yet we hold this treasure in vessels of clay, so that the surpassing greatness of the power may belong to God and not come from us;

**4:8** pressed hard in every way, yet not crushed; perplexed, yet not driven to despair;

**4:9** persecuted, yet not abandoned; thrown down, yet not destroyed,

**4:10** continually bearing about in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be made manifest in our body.

**4:11** For we who are living are continually given over to death for the sake of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be made manifest in our mortal flesh.

**4:12** Thus death is at work in us, yet life in you.

**4:13** Yet having the same spirit of faith, in accordance with what stands written, "I believed; therefore I spoke," we also believe, and therefore we also speak,

**4:14** knowing that He who raised Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and present us together with you.

**4:15** For all things are on your behalf, so that grace, increasing through the greater number, may make thanksgiving overflow to the glory of God.

**4:16** For this reason we do not lose heart; rather, even though our outer man is wasting away, our inner man is renewed day after day.

**4:17** For this momentary lightness of our affliction is working out for us, in measure beyond all measure, an eternal weight of glory,

**4:18** as we fix our gaze not on the things that are seen, but on the things that are unseen; for the things that are seen are transient, but the things that are unseen are eternal.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The critical Greek text omits "to them" after the verb, supporting the intransitive sense "lest the radiant light ... should shine forth" (or transitively, "lest they see the radiant light").
- **4:14** — Following the critical Greek text, which reads "Jesus" (without "the Lord") and "with Jesus" rather than "through Jesus."
- **4:17** — Literally "according to excess to excess," an emphatic Greek idiom conveying surpassing, incomparable measure.
