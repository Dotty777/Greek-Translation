## Text

**6:1** Working together with Him, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain;

**6:2** for He says, "At a favorable time I listened to you, and on a day of salvation I came to your aid." Behold, now is the most favorable time; behold, now is the day of salvation.

**6:3** We put no cause of stumbling in anything, so that the ministry may not be discredited,

**6:4** rather, in everything we commend ourselves as servants of God: in great endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,

**6:5** in beatings, in imprisonments, in tumults, in toils, in sleepless nights, in fastings;

**6:6** in purity, in knowledge, in forbearance, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in unfeigned love,

**6:7** in the word of truth, in the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and the left,

**6:8** through honor and dishonor, through ill report and good report; as impostors, yet truthful;

**6:9** as unknown, yet well known; as dying, and behold, we live; as disciplined, yet not put to death;

**6:10** as grieving, yet always rejoicing; as impoverished, yet enriching many; as having nothing, yet possessing all things.

**6:11** Our mouth stands open to you, Corinthians; our heart has been opened wide.

**6:12** You are not restricted in us; rather, you are restricted in your own affections.

**6:13** Now in equal return—I speak as to children—open wide your hearts also.

**6:14** Do not become unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership is there between righteousness and lawlessness, or what fellowship does light have with darkness?

**6:15** And what harmony is there between Christ and Beliar, or what portion does a believer share with an unbeliever?

**6:16** And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, just as God has said: "I will dwell among them and walk among them; I will be their God, and they shall be My people."

**6:17** Therefore, "Come out from their midst and be separated," says the Lord, "and do not touch anything unclean; and I will welcome you,

**6:18** and I will be a Father to you, and you shall be sons and daughters to Me," says the Lord Almighty.

## Translator Notes

- **6:12** — Greek "splagchnois" literally refers to the inward parts, regarded in biblical idiom as the seat of deep affection and compassion.
- **6:15** — "Beliar" preserves the critical Greek form (Beliar), a variant of the Hebrew name Belial ("worthlessness"), used in Second Temple Jewish writings as a designation for Satan.
- **6:16** — Following the critical Greek text, which reads "we are the temple of the living God" rather than "you are."
