## Text

**2:1** But as for you, speak the things that befit sound doctrine:

**2:2** that older men be temperate, dignified, sober-minded, sound in faith, in love, and in steadfastness.

**2:3** Older women likewise are to be reverent in demeanor, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good,

**2:4** so that they may instruct the younger women to love their husbands and to love their children,

**2:5** to be sober-minded, pure, workers at home, kind, and subject to their own husbands, so that the word of God may not be reviled.

**2:6** Likewise, exhort the younger men to be sober-minded;

**2:7** in all things presenting yourself as a pattern of good works; in your teaching showing integrity, dignity,

**2:8** and sound speech beyond condemnation, so that the opponent may be put to shame, having nothing evil to say about us.

**2:9** Bondservants are to be subject to their own masters in everything, to be well-pleasing, not contradicting,

**2:10** not pilfering, but displaying all good fidelity, so that in every way they may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior.

**2:11** For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all mankind,

**2:12** instructing us that, renouncing ungodliness and worldly desires, we should live soberly, uprightly, and godly in this present age,

**2:13** awaiting the blessed hope and the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ,

**2:14** who gave Himself for us, that He might redeem us from all lawlessness and purify for Himself a people peculiarly His own, zealous for good works.

**2:15** Speak these things, exhort, and reprove with full authority; let no one disregard you.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The earliest manuscripts read oikourgous ('workers at home' or 'busy at home'), whereas some later manuscripts read oikourous ('keepers at home').
- **2:11** — Without the definite article before soterios, the phrase reads 'has appeared, bringing salvation to all mankind,' though 'to all mankind' may modify either 'bringing salvation' or 'has appeared.'
- **2:13** — A single Greek definite article governs both 'great God' and 'Savior' (tou megalou theou kai soteros hemon), directly identifying Jesus Christ as both God and Savior.
