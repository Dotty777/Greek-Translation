## Text

**1:1** Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God's elect and the full knowledge of the truth that conforms to godliness,

**1:2** in the hope of eternal life, which God, who does not lie, promised before eternal ages,

**1:3** and in His own proper times manifested His word through the proclamation with which I was entrusted, according to the command of God our Savior,

**1:4** To Titus, a true child in our common faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

**1:5** For this reason I left you behind in Crete, that you might put in order the things that remain lacking, and appoint elders in every city just as I directed you:

**1:6** if anyone is beyond reproach, the husband of one wife, having faithful children, not under a charge of dissipation nor rebellious.

**1:7** For an overseer must be beyond reproach, as a steward of God—not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not given to wine, not a brawler, not eager for shameful gain;

**1:8** but hospitable, a lover of what is good, sober-minded, upright, devout, self-controlled,

**1:9** holding steadfastly to the trustworthy word according to the teaching, so that he may be able both to exhort in sound doctrine and to refute those who contradict it.

**1:10** For there are many unruly men, empty talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision,

**1:11** whose mouths must be silenced, who overturn whole households, teaching things they ought not to teach for the sake of shameful gain.

**1:12** One from among them, a prophet of their own, said, "Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons."

**1:13** This testimony is true. For this cause reprove them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,

**1:14** not giving heed to Jewish myths and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.

**1:15** All things are pure to those who are pure; yet to the defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure, but both their mind and their conscience have been defiled.

**1:16** They profess to know God, yet by their works they deny Him, being detestable, disobedient, and disqualified for every good work.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The critical Greek text reads 'Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior,' omitting 'mercy' and 'the Lord' found in later Byzantine manuscripts.
- **1:6** — The Greek adjective translated 'faithful' (pista) can mean either 'believing' (Christian) or 'trustworthy/faithful' in conduct.
- **1:12** — Paul quotes a hexameter line attributed to the sixth-century BC Cretan poet and seer Epimenides of Knossos.
