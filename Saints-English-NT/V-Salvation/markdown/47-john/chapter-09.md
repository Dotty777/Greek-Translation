## Text

**9:1** And as He passed along, He saw a man blind from his birth.

**9:2** And His disciples questioned Him, saying, "Rabbi, who sinned—this man or his parents—that he should be born blind?"

**9:3** Jesus replied, "Neither this man sinned nor his parents, but that the works of God might be made manifest in him.

**9:4** We must perform the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; night is coming when no one is able to work.

**9:5** So long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world."

**9:6** Having spoken these things, He spat on the ground and made clay out of the saliva, and He anointed his eyes with the clay,

**9:7** and said to him, "Go and wash in the pool of Siloam" (which is interpreted, "Sent"). Therefore he went away and washed, and returned seeing.

**9:8** Therefore the neighbors and those who previously had seen him, that he was a beggar, were saying, "Is this not the one who used to sit and beg?"

**9:9** Some were saying, "This is the man." Others were saying, "No, rather he resembles him." He himself kept saying, "I am he."

**9:10** So they were saying to him, "How were your eyes opened?"

**9:11** He answered, "The man who is called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes, and said to me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' Therefore I departed and washed, and I received my sight."

**9:12** And they said to him, "Where is He?" He answered, "I do not know."

**9:13** They brought the man who had once been blind to the Pharisees.

**9:14** Now it was a Sabbath on the day that Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.

**9:15** So the Pharisees also questioned him again how he had received his sight. And he said to them, "He laid clay upon my eyes, and I washed, and I see."

**9:16** Therefore some of the Pharisees were saying, "This man is not from God, because He does not observe the Sabbath." Others were saying, "How can a sinful man perform such signs?" And there was a division among them.

**9:17** So they spoke to the blind man again: "What do you yourself say concerning Him, since He opened your eyes?" And he answered, "He is a prophet."

**9:18** Therefore the Jews did not believe concerning him that he had been blind and had gained his sight, until they summoned the parents of the man who had received his sight,

**9:19** and questioned them, saying, "Is this your son, of whom you say that he was born blind? How then does he see now?"

**9:20** So his parents answered and said, "We know that this man is our son and that he was born blind;

**9:21** but how he now sees we do not know, nor do we ourselves know who opened his eyes. Question him; he is of age; he himself shall speak for himself."

**9:22** His parents spoke these things because they feared the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed among themselves that if anyone confessed Him to be the Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.

**9:23** For this reason his parents said, "He is of age; question him."

**9:24** So they summoned a second time the man who had been blind and said to him, "Give glory to God! We ourselves know that this man is a sinner."

**9:25** Therefore he replied, "Whether He is a sinner I do not know; this one thing I know, that though I was blind, I now see."

**9:26** Therefore they said to him, "What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?"

**9:27** He replied to them, "I have told you already, and you did not listen. Why do you desire to hear it again? Do you yourselves also wish to become His disciples?"

**9:28** And they reviled him and said, "You yourself are His disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.

**9:29** We know that God has spoken to Moses; yet as for this man, we do not know where He is from."

**9:30** The man answered and said to them, "Why, in this lies the marvel, that you yourselves do not know where He is from, and yet He opened my eyes.

**9:31** We know that God does not listen to sinners; rather, if anyone is a worshiper of God and carries out His will, He listens to him.

**9:32** Never since the world began has it been heard that anyone opened the eyes of one born blind.

**9:33** If this man were not from God, He would be able to do nothing."

**9:34** They replied and said to him, "You were born wholly in sins, and are you teaching us?" And they cast him out.

**9:35** Jesus heard that they had cast him out, and when He found him, He said, "Do you yourself believe in the Son of Man?"

**9:36** He answered and said, "And who is He, Lord, so that I may believe in Him?"

**9:37** Jesus said to him, "You have both seen Him, and the One who speaks with you is He."

**9:38** And he said, "I believe, Lord!" And he worshiped Him.

**9:39** And Jesus said, "For judgment I came into this world, so that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind."

**9:40** Some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these things, and they said to Him, "Surely we are not blind also?"

**9:41** Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would not have sin; yet now you say, 'We see.' Your sin abides."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The critical Greek text reads plural "We must" together with singular "who sent Me," associating the disciples with Jesus' mission; later manuscripts harmonize to "I must."
- **9:24** — "Give glory to God!" is an established biblical oath formula (cf. Joshua 7:19) adjuring the speaker to confess the truth before God.
- **9:35** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "the Son of Man," whereas later witnesses read "the Son of God."
- **9:36** — Greek kyrie here may carry respectful address ("Sir") prior to Jesus' self-revelation, deepening into worshipful confession ("Lord") in verse 38.
