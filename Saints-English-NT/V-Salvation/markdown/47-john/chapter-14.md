## Text

**14:1** "Do not let your heart be troubled; believe in God, and believe in Me also.

**14:2** In the house of My Father there are many abodes; if it were not so, I would have told you, for I am going to prepare a place for you.

**14:3** And if I go and prepare a place for you, I am coming again and shall take you to Myself, that where I am, you also may be.

**14:4** And where I am going, you know the way."

**14:5** Thomas said to Him, "Lord, we do not know where You are going; how are we able to know the way?"

**14:6** Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life; none comes to the Father except through Me.

**14:7** If you had known Me, you would also have known My Father; from now on you know Him and have seen Him."

**14:8** Philip said to Him, "Lord, show us the Father, and it suffices us."

**14:9** Jesus said to him, "For so long a time I have been with you, and have you not come to know Me, Philip? Whoever has seen Me has seen the Father; how is it that you say, 'Show us the Father'?

**14:10** Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father is in Me? The words which I speak to you I do not speak from Myself; rather, the Father who abides in Me performs His works.

**14:11** Believe Me that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me; otherwise, believe because of the works themselves.

**14:12** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, he who believes in Me shall also do the works that I do, and greater works than these he shall do, because I am going to the Father.

**14:13** And whatever you may ask in My name, this I shall do, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.

**14:14** If you ask Me anything in My name, I Myself shall do it.

**14:15** If you love Me, you shall keep My commandments.

**14:16** And I will ask the Father, and He shall give you another Comforter, that He may be with you forever—

**14:17** the Spirit of truth, whom the world is unable to receive, because it neither beholds Him nor knows Him; you know Him, for He abides with you and shall be in you.

**14:18** I shall not leave you as orphans; I am coming to you.

**14:19** Yet a little while and the world beholds Me no more, but you behold Me; because I live, you also shall live.

**14:20** On that day you yourselves shall know that I am in My Father, and you are in Me, and I am in you.

**14:21** Whoever has My commandments and keeps them, that is the one who loves Me; and the one who loves Me shall be loved by My Father, and I shall love him and manifest Myself to him."

**14:22** Judas—not Iscariot—said to Him, "Lord, what has come to pass that You are about to manifest Yourself to us and not to the world?"

**14:23** Jesus answered and said to him, "If anyone loves Me, he shall keep My word; and My Father shall love him, and We shall come to him and make Our abode with him.

**14:24** Whoever does not love Me does not keep My words; and the word that you are hearing is not Mine, but belongs to the Father who sent Me.

**14:25** I have spoken these things to you while abiding with you.

**14:26** Yet the Comforter, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father shall send in My name—He shall teach you all things and remind you of everything that I told you.

**14:27** Peace I leave with you; My own peace I give to you; not in the way the world gives do I give to you. Do not let your heart be troubled, nor let it be fainthearted.

**14:28** You heard that I told you, 'I am going away and am coming to you.' If you loved Me, you would have rejoiced because I am going to the Father, for the Father is greater than I.

**14:29** And now I have spoken to you before it happens, that when it comes to pass, you may believe.

**14:30** I shall not speak much longer with you, for the ruler of the world is coming, and in Me he has nothing;

**14:31** rather, it is so that the world may know that I love the Father, and just as the Father has commanded Me, so I do. Arise, let us depart from here."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — The Greek form pisteuete can be read as either indicative ("you believe") or imperative ("believe") in both clauses, permitting a statement followed by a command or two parallel commands.
- **14:2** — Greek monai ("abodes" or "dwelling places") shares the same root as "abide" (meno) and "abode" (mone) in 14:23. Because the Greek includes hoti, the second clause can also be read as a question: "if it were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you?"
- **14:14** — Strong early manuscript witnesses include the pronoun "Me" (me), highlighting prayer directed to Jesus Himself, whereas some later manuscripts omit it.
- **14:16** — Greek parakletos ("Comforter") refers to one called alongside to console, strengthen, counsel, or plead as an advocate, and is variously rendered "Comforter," "Advocate," "Counselor," or "Helper."
