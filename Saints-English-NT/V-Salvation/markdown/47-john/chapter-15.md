## Text

**15:1** "I am the true vine, and My Father is the vineyard keeper.

**15:2** Every branch in Me that bears no fruit He removes, and every branch that bears fruit He prunes, so that it may bear more fruit.

**15:3** Already you are clean on account of the word that I have spoken to you.

**15:4** Abide in Me, and I in you. Just as the branch is unable to bear fruit of itself unless it abides in the vine, so neither can you unless you abide in Me.

**15:5** I am the vine; you are the branches. The one who abides in Me, and I in him, he bears much fruit; for apart from Me you are able to do nothing.

**15:6** If anyone does not abide in Me, he is cast forth like the branch and withers; and they gather them and throw them into the fire, and they are burned.

**15:7** If you abide in Me, and My words abide in you, ask for whatever you desire, and it shall be done for you.

**15:8** In this My Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit and become My disciples.

**15:9** Just as the Father has loved Me, I also have loved you; abide in My love.

**15:10** If you keep My commandments, you shall abide in My love, even as I have kept the Father's commandments and abide in His love.

**15:11** These things I have spoken to you, so that My joy may be in you, and your joy may be made full.

**15:12** This is My commandment: that you love one another, even as I have loved you.

**15:13** No one has greater love than this: that one should lay down his life for his friends.

**15:14** You are My friends if you do the things that I command you.

**15:15** I no longer call you servants, because the servant does not know what his master is doing; rather, I have called you friends, because all that I heard from My Father I have made known to you.

**15:16** It was not you who chose Me, but I who chose you and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should abide, so that whatever you ask the Father in My name He may give to you.

**15:17** These things I command you, so that you may love one another.

**15:18** If the world hates you, know that it has hated Me before you.

**15:19** If you were of the world, the world would love what is its own; yet because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, for this reason the world hates you.

**15:20** Remember the word that I spoke to you: 'A servant is not greater than his master.' If they persecuted Me, they will persecute you as well; if they kept My word, they will keep yours as well.

**15:21** Yet all these things they will do against you on account of My name, because they do not know the One who sent Me.

**15:22** If I had not come and spoken to them, they would have had no sin; yet now they have no excuse for their sin.

**15:23** He who hates Me also hates My Father.

**15:24** If I had not done among them the works that no one else has done, they would have had no sin; yet now they have both seen and hated both Me and My Father.

**15:25** Yet this is so that the word written in their law might be fulfilled: 'They hated Me without cause.'

**15:26** When the Advocate comes, whom I shall send to you from the Father—the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father—He shall bear witness concerning Me.

**15:27** And you also bear witness, because you have been with Me from the beginning."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Greek employs a play on words between airei ("removes") and kathairei ("prunes" or "cleanses"), linking directly to katharoi ("clean") in verse 3.
- **15:8** — The critical Greek text reads the subjunctive genesthe ("and become My disciples") rather than the future indicative ("you will be") found in some later manuscripts.
- **15:10** — The critical Greek text reads "the Father's commandments" (tou Patros) without the possessive pronoun "My."
- **15:26** — Greek parakletos ("Advocate") denotes one called alongside to plead, assist, counsel, or comfort, and is also translated "Helper," "Counselor," or "Comforter."
