## Text

**7:1** And after these things Jesus went about in Galilee; for He did not wish to walk in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill Him.

**7:2** Now the feast of the Jews, the Feast of Tabernacles, was at hand.

**7:3** Therefore His brothers said to Him, "Depart from this place and go into Judea, so that Your disciples also may behold Your works which You are doing;

**7:4** for no one does anything in secret and yet himself seeks to be in the open. If You are doing these things, manifest Yourself to the world."

**7:5** For not even His brothers were believing in Him.

**7:6** Jesus therefore said to them, "My time is not yet here, but your time is always ready.

**7:7** The world cannot hate you, yet it hates Me, because I bear witness concerning it that its works are evil.

**7:8** You go up to the feast; I am not yet going up to this feast, because My time has not yet been fulfilled."

**7:9** And having said these things to them, He remained in Galilee.

**7:10** But when His brothers had gone up to the feast, then He Himself also went up, not openly, but as it were in secret.

**7:11** Therefore the Jews were seeking Him at the feast and saying, "Where is He?"

**7:12** And there was much murmuring concerning Him among the multitudes: some were saying, "He is good," while others were saying, "No, rather He leads the multitude astray."

**7:13** Yet no one spoke openly concerning Him for fear of the Jews.

**7:14** Now when the feast was already half over, Jesus went up into the temple and was teaching.

**7:15** Therefore the Jews marveled, saying, "How does this Man know letters, not having studied?"

**7:16** Jesus therefore answered them and said, "My teaching is not My own, but His who sent Me.

**7:17** If anyone is willing to do His will, he shall know concerning the teaching, whether it is of God or whether I speak from Myself.

**7:18** The one who speaks on his own seeks his own glory; but He who seeks the glory of the One who sent Him, this One is true, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.

**7:19** Has not Moses given you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why do you seek to kill Me?"

**7:20** The multitude answered, "You have a demon. Who is seeking to kill You?"

**7:21** Jesus answered and said to them, "One work I did, and you all marvel.

**7:22** Because of this Moses has given you circumcision—not that it is from Moses, but from the fathers—and on the Sabbath you circumcise a man.

**7:23** If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath lest the law of Moses be broken, are you angry with Me because I made a whole man well on the Sabbath?

**7:24** Do not judge by appearance, but judge righteous judgment."

**7:25** Therefore some of the people of Jerusalem were saying, "Is this not He whom they are seeking to kill?

**7:26** And behold, He speaks openly, and they say nothing to Him! Can it be that the rulers have truly come to know that this is the Christ?

**7:27** Yet as for this Man, we know where He is from; but when the Christ comes, no one knows where He is from."

**7:28** Therefore Jesus cried out as He was teaching in the temple, saying, "Indeed you know Me, and you know where I am from; yet I have not come from Myself, but the One who sent Me is true, whom you do not know.

**7:29** I know Him, because I am from Him, and He sent Me."

**7:30** Therefore they sought to seize Him, and no one laid his hand on Him, because His hour had not yet arrived.

**7:31** Yet many from the multitude believed in Him, and were saying, "When the Christ comes, will He perform more signs than those which this Man has done?"

**7:32** The Pharisees heard the multitude murmuring these things concerning Him, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to seize Him.

**7:33** Therefore Jesus said, "For a little while longer I am with you, and then I am going to Him who sent Me.

**7:34** You will seek Me and shall not find Me; and where I am, you cannot come."

**7:35** Therefore the Jews said among themselves, "Where does this Man intend to go that we will not find Him? Is He about to go to the Dispersion among the Greeks and teach the Greeks?

**7:36** What is this saying which He spoke, 'You will seek Me and shall not find Me; and where I am, you cannot come'?"

**7:37** Now on the last day, the great day of the feast, Jesus was standing, and He cried out, saying, "If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink.

**7:38** The one who believes in Me, just as the Scripture has said, out of his innermost being shall flow rivers of living water."

**7:39** Now this He spoke concerning the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were about to receive; for the Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.

**7:40** Therefore some from the multitude, when they heard these words, were saying, "This is truly the Prophet."

**7:41** Others were saying, "This is the Christ." Yet some were saying, "Surely the Christ does not come from Galilee?

**7:42** Did not the Scripture say that the Christ comes from the seed of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?"

**7:43** Therefore a division arose among the multitude on account of Him.

**7:44** And some of them desired to seize Him, yet no one laid hands upon Him.

**7:45** The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, "Why did you not bring Him?"

**7:46** The officers answered, "Never has a man spoken like this!"

**7:47** Therefore the Pharisees answered them, "Have you also been led astray?

**7:48** Has anyone among the rulers believed in Him, or among the Pharisees?

**7:49** Yet this multitude, which does not know the law, is accursed."

**7:50** Nicodemus—the one who had come to Him previously, being one of them—said to them,

**7:51** "Does our law judge a man unless it first hears from him and learns what he is doing?"

**7:52** They answered and said to him, "Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that no prophet arises out of Galilee."

**7:53** ⟦And each man departed to his own house;⟧

## Translator Notes

- **7:8** — The Greek text reads "not yet" (oupo), whereas some early manuscripts read "not" (ouk), a variant reflecting how readers reconciled Jesus' statement here with His private ascent to the feast in verse 10.
- **7:22** — The phrase "Because of this" (dia touto) opens verse 22 in accordance with the Greek punctuation, though some interpreters attach it to the close of verse 21 ("and you all marvel because of this").
- **7:38** — The Greek word koilia literally means belly or womb and metaphorically denotes one's innermost being. While the Greek punctuation connects the rivers of living water to the believer, an early alternative punctuation joins "the one who believes in Me" to "let him drink" in verse 37, referring the rivers to Christ Himself.
- **7:39** — The Greek literally reads "for the Spirit was not yet," without the word "given"; it refers to the promised outpouring of the Spirit after Jesus' glorification rather than to the Spirit's eternal existence.
- **7:46** — The Greek text preserves the shorter reading "Never has a man spoken like this," whereas later manuscripts add "as this Man speaks."
- **7:53** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; the passage 7:53-8:11 is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
