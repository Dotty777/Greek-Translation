## Text

**8:1** ⟦but Jesus departed to the Mount of Olives.⟧

**8:2** ⟦And at dawn He came again into the temple, and all the people were coming; and having sat down, He was teaching them.⟧

**8:3** ⟦Now the scribes and the Pharisees bring to Him a woman caught in adultery; and having set her in the midst,⟧

**8:4** ⟦they say to Him, testing Him, "Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act, committing adultery.⟧

**8:5** ⟦Now in the law Moses commanded us that such women should be stoned; what therefore do You say?"⟧

**8:6** ⟦And this they were saying to test Him, that they might have cause to accuse Him. But Jesus, stooping down, wrote with His finger upon the ground, taking no heed.⟧

**8:7** ⟦Yet as they continued asking Him, He lifted Himself up and said to them, "Let him who is without sin among you cast the stone at her first."⟧

**8:8** ⟦And stooping down again, He wrote upon the ground.⟧

**8:9** ⟦And they, having heard, and being convicted by their conscience, went out one by one, beginning from the elders; and Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.⟧

**8:10** ⟦And Jesus, lifting Himself up and beholding no one save the woman, said to her, "Where are those accusers of yours? Has no one condemned you?"⟧

**8:11** ⟦And she said, "No one, Lord." And Jesus said, "Neither do I judge you; go, and sin no more."⟧

**8:12** Therefore Jesus spoke to them again, saying, "I am the light of the world. The one who follows Me shall by no means walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life."

**8:13** Therefore the Pharisees said to Him, "You bear witness concerning Yourself; Your testimony is not true."

**8:14** Jesus answered and said to them, "Even if I bear witness concerning Myself, My testimony is true, for I know where I came from and where I am going; yet you do not know where I come from or where I am going.

**8:15** You pass judgment according to the flesh; I do not judge anyone.

**8:16** Yet even if I do judge, My judgment is true, because I am not alone, but it is I and the Father who sent Me.

**8:17** Moreover, in your own Law it is written that the testimony of two men is true.

**8:18** I am the One who bears witness concerning Myself, and the Father who sent Me bears witness concerning Me."

**8:19** Therefore they said to Him, "Where is Your Father?" Jesus answered, "You know neither Me nor My Father; if you knew Me, you would know My Father as well."

**8:20** He spoke these words in the treasury as He taught in the temple; yet no one seized Him, because His hour had not yet come.

**8:21** Therefore He said to them again, "I am going away, and you shall seek Me, and in your sin you shall die. Where I am going, you cannot come."

**8:22** Therefore the Jews said, "Will He kill Himself, since He says, 'Where I am going, you cannot come'?"

**8:23** And He said to them, "You are from beneath, and I am from above; you are from this world, and I am not from this world.

**8:24** Therefore I told you that you shall die in your sins; for unless you believe that I am, you shall die in your sins."

**8:25** Therefore they said to Him, "Who are You?" Jesus said to them, "Why do I even speak to you at all?

**8:26** I have many things to speak and to judge concerning you; yet He who sent Me is true, and the things that I heard from Him, these I speak to the world."

**8:27** They did not realize that He was speaking to them concerning the Father.

**8:28** Therefore Jesus said to them, "When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you shall know that I am, and that I do nothing of Myself, but just as the Father taught Me, these things I speak.

**8:29** And He who sent Me is with Me; He has not left Me alone, because I always do the things that are pleasing to Him."

**8:30** While He was speaking these things, many believed in Him.

**8:31** Therefore Jesus said to those Jews who had believed Him, "If you abide in My word, truly you are My disciples,

**8:32** and you shall know the truth, and the truth shall set you free."

**8:33** They answered Him, "We are the seed of Abraham, and have never at any time been in bondage to anyone. How do You say, 'You shall become free'?"

**8:34** Jesus answered them, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin.

**8:35** Now the slave does not abide in the house forever; the son abides forever.

**8:36** Therefore if the Son sets you free, you shall be truly free.

**8:37** I know that you are the seed of Abraham; yet you seek to kill Me, because My word finds no place in you.

**8:38** I speak the things that I have seen with the Father; and you therefore do the things that you heard from the father."

**8:39** They answered and said to Him, "Our father is Abraham." Jesus said to them, "If you were children of Abraham, you would be doing the works of Abraham;

**8:40** but now you seek to kill Me, a man who has spoken to you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.

**8:41** You do the works of your father." They said to Him, "We were not born of fornication; we have one Father—God."

**8:42** Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love Me, for I came forth from God and am here; for neither have I come of Myself, but He sent Me.

**8:43** Why do you not understand My speech? Because you are unable to hear My word.

**8:44** You are of your father the devil, and you wish to carry out the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning, and has not stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him. Whenever he speaks falsehood, he speaks out of what is his own, for he is a liar and the father of it.

**8:45** Yet because I speak the truth, you do not believe Me.

**8:46** Which one of you convicts Me concerning sin? If I speak truth, why do you not believe Me?

**8:47** The one who is of God hears the words of God; for this reason you do not hear, because you are not of God."

**8:48** The Jews answered and said to Him, "Do we not rightly declare that You are a Samaritan and have a demon?"

**8:49** Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon; rather, I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me.

**8:50** Yet I do not seek My glory; there is One who seeks and judges.

**8:51** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, if anyone keeps My word, he shall never behold death."

**8:52** The Jews said to Him, "Now we know that You have a demon! Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet You say, 'If anyone keeps My word, he shall never taste death.'

**8:53** Surely You are not greater than our father Abraham, who died? And the prophets died. Who do You make Yourself out to be?"

**8:54** Jesus answered, "If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing. It is My Father who glorifies Me, of whom you say, 'He is our God,'

**8:55** yet you have not known Him. But I know Him; and even if I should say that I do not know Him, I shall be a liar like you. Rather, I do know Him, and I keep His word.

**8:56** Your father Abraham rejoiced greatly that he would see My day, and he saw it and was glad."

**8:57** Therefore the Jews said to Him, "You are not yet fifty years old, and You have seen Abraham?"

**8:58** Jesus said to them, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, before Abraham came into being, I am."

**8:59** Therefore they took up stones to cast at Him; but Jesus hid Himself and went out from the temple.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text throughout 8:1-11, marking a passage absent from the earliest Greek witnesses.
- **8:6** — The Saints Greek Critical Text includes the participial phrase me prospoioumenos ('taking no heed / acting as though He did not notice').
- **8:7** — Greek ton lithon carries the definite article ('the stone'), referring to the first stone in a stoning execution.
- **8:9** — The Saints Greek Critical Text includes kai hypo tes syneideseos elenchomenoi ('and being convicted by their conscience'), attested in the Byzantine textual tradition.
- **8:11** — Where verse 10 uses katekrinen ('condemned'), the Saints Greek Critical Text here reads the simple verb krino ('judge'), rendered distinctly.
- **8:25** — The Greek clause is notoriously difficult and may be construed either as an exasperated question (following the critical text's punctuation, "Why do I even speak to you at all?") or as a statement ("Just what I have been telling you from the beginning").
- **8:38** — The critical Greek text omits possessive pronouns in both clauses ("the Father" and "the father") and shifts from "seen with" to "heard from," leaving the true identity of their father unspoken until verse 44.
- **8:54** — The critical Greek text reads "our God" (quoting their own claim directly), whereas some later manuscripts change the pronoun to "your God."
- **8:58** — The Greek sharply contrasts the aorist "came into being" for Abraham with the timeless present "I am" for Jesus, echoing the divine self-designation of Exodus 3:14 and Isaiah 43:10 in the Greek Old Testament.
- **8:59** — The critical Greek text ends the chapter here, without the later manuscript expansion "going through the midst of them, and so passed by."
