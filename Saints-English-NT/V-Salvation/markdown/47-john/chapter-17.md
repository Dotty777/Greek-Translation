## Text

**17:1** These things Jesus spoke, and lifting His eyes toward heaven, He said, "Father, the hour has come; glorify Your Son, so that the Son may glorify You,

**17:2** even as You gave Him authority over all flesh, that to all whom You have given Him, He should give eternal life.

**17:3** Now this is eternal life: that they may know You, the only true God, and Him whom You sent, Jesus Christ.

**17:4** I glorified You upon the earth, having completed the work which You have given Me to do.

**17:5** And now, Father, glorify Me in Your presence with the glory which I had beside You before the world existed.

**17:6** I have made Your name manifest to the men whom You gave Me out of the world. Yours they were, and to Me You gave them, and they have kept Your word.

**17:7** Now they have come to know that all things which You have given Me are from You,

**17:8** for the words which You gave Me I have given to them; and they received them and truly knew that I came forth from You, and they believed that You sent Me.

**17:9** I pray on their behalf; I do not pray for the world, but for those whom You have given Me, because they are Yours,

**17:10** and all things that are Mine are Yours, and Yours are Mine, and I have been glorified in them.

**17:11** And I am no longer in the world, yet they are in the world, and I am coming to You. Holy Father, keep them in Your name which You have given Me, that they may be one, even as We are.

**17:12** While I was with them, I kept them in Your name which You have given Me, and I guarded them; and not one of them perished except the son of perdition, that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

**17:13** Yet now I am coming to You, and these things I speak in the world, so that they may have My joy made full within themselves.

**17:14** I have given Your word to them, and the world hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

**17:15** I do not pray that You take them away from the world, but that You keep them from the evil one.

**17:16** Of the world they are not, even as I am not of the world.

**17:17** Consecrate them in the truth; Your word is truth.

**17:18** Even as You sent Me into the world, I also sent them into the world.

**17:19** And on their behalf I consecrate Myself, so that they themselves also may be consecrated in truth.

**17:20** Yet I do not pray for these alone, but also for those who believe in Me through their word,

**17:21** that all of them may be one, even as You, Father, are in Me and I in You, that they also may be in Us, in order that the world may believe that You sent Me.

**17:22** And the glory which You have given Me I have given to them, so that they may be one, even as We are one:

**17:23** I in them, and You in Me, that they may be perfected into one, so that the world may know that You sent Me and loved them even as You loved Me.

**17:24** Father, as for those whom You have given Me, I desire that where I am, they also may be with Me, so that they may behold My glory which You have given Me, because You loved Me before the foundation of the world.

**17:25** Righteous Father, even though the world did not know You, yet I knew You, and these knew that You sent Me;

**17:26** and I have made Your name known to them, and will make it known, so that the love with which You loved Me may be in them, and I in them."

## Translator Notes

- **17:11** — The Greek text reads "in Your name which You have given Me" (with the relative pronoun referring to the Father's name rather than to the disciples, as in some later manuscripts and similarly in 17:12).
- **17:15** — The Greek phrase "ek tou ponerou" can mean either "from evil" (neuter) or "from the evil one" (masculine), with Johannine usage favoring the personal reference to Satan.
- **17:17** — The verb "hagiazo" ("consecrate" or "sanctify") carries the sense of setting apart as holy for God's service, illuminating Jesus's words in 17:19 ("I consecrate Myself"); the critical text reads "in the truth" without "Your."
- **17:24** — The Greek uses a collective neuter singular ("that which You have given Me," as also in 17:2) followed by a masculine plural ("they also"), viewing believers together as the Father's unified gift to the Son.
