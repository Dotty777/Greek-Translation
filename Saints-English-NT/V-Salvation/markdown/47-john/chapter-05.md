## Text

**5:1** After these things there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

**5:2** Now there is in Jerusalem, beside the Sheep Gate, a pool called in Hebrew Bethzatha, which has five porticoes.

**5:3** In these lay a multitude of the sick—blind, lame, and withered.

**5:5** Now a certain man was there who had been in his infirmity for thirty-eight years.

**5:6** When Jesus saw him lying there, and knew that he had already been so for a long time, He said to him, "Do you wish to be made whole?"

**5:7** The infirm man answered Him, "Sir, I have no man to put me into the pool whenever the water is troubled; but while I am coming, another goes down before me."

**5:8** Jesus said to him, "Arise, take up your mat, and walk."

**5:9** And immediately the man was made whole, and took up his mat and walked. Now that day was the Sabbath.

**5:10** Therefore the Jews said to him who had been healed, "It is the Sabbath, and it is not lawful for you to carry the mat."

**5:11** But he answered them, "He who made me whole—He said to me, 'Take up your mat and walk.'"

**5:12** They asked him, "Who is the man who said to you, 'Take up and walk'?"

**5:13** Now the man who had been healed did not know who He was, for Jesus had slipped away, since a multitude was in that place.

**5:14** After these things Jesus found him in the temple and said to him, "Behold, you have been made whole. Sin no more, lest something worse befall you."

**5:15** The man departed and reported to the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him whole.

**5:16** And for this cause the Jews were persecuting Jesus, because He was doing these things on the Sabbath.

**5:17** But He answered them, "My Father is working until now, and I also am working."

**5:18** For this reason, therefore, the Jews sought all the more to kill Him, because He was not only breaking the Sabbath, but also calling God His own Father, making Himself equal with God.

**5:19** Therefore Jesus answered and said to them, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, the Son cannot do anything of Himself, except what He sees the Father doing; for whatever He does, these things the Son also does in like manner.

**5:20** For the Father loves the Son and shows Him all that He Himself is doing; and greater works than these He will show Him, so that you may marvel.

**5:21** For just as the Father raises the dead and gives life, so also the Son gives life to whom He wills.

**5:22** For not even the Father judges anyone, but He has given all judgment to the Son,

**5:23** so that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. Whoever does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent Him.

**5:24** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, he who hears My word and believes Him who sent Me has eternal life, and does not come into judgment, but has crossed over out of death into life.

**5:25** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, an hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear shall live.

**5:26** For just as the Father has life in Himself, so He also gave to the Son to have life in Himself;

**5:27** and He gave Him authority to execute judgment, because He is the Son of Man.

**5:28** Do not marvel at this, for an hour is coming in which all who are in the tombs shall hear His voice

**5:29** and shall come forth—those who have done good to a resurrection of life, and those who have practiced evil to a resurrection of judgment.

**5:30** I am able to do nothing on My own; just as I hear, I judge, and My judgment is righteous, because I do not seek My own will, but the will of Him who sent Me.

**5:31** If I bear witness concerning Myself, My testimony is not true.

**5:32** There is Another who bears witness concerning Me, and I know that the testimony He bears concerning Me is true.

**5:33** You yourselves have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.

**5:34** Yet I do not accept testimony from man, but I speak these things so that you may be saved.

**5:35** He was the lamp that was burning and shining, and you were willing to rejoice for an hour in his light.

**5:36** But the testimony I have is greater than that of John; for the works that the Father has given Me to accomplish—these very works that I am doing—bear witness concerning Me, that the Father has sent Me.

**5:37** And the Father who sent Me, He Himself has borne witness concerning Me. Neither have you heard His voice at any time, nor have you seen His form,

**5:38** and you do not have His word abiding in you, because you do not believe the One whom He sent.

**5:39** You search the Scriptures, because you suppose that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that bear witness concerning Me;

**5:40** and yet you are unwilling to come to Me, so that you may have life.

**5:41** I do not receive glory from men,

**5:42** but I know you, that you do not have the love of God within yourselves.

**5:43** I have come in the name of My Father, and you do not receive Me; if another should come in his own name, that one you will receive.

**5:44** How are you able to believe, when you receive glory from one another, and do not seek the glory that comes from the only God?

**5:45** Do not suppose that I shall accuse you before the Father; the one who accuses you is Moses, in whom you have set your hope.

**5:46** For if you believed Moses, you would believe Me, for he wrote concerning Me.

**5:47** Yet if you do not believe his writings, how shall you believe My words?"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**5:4** *(after `5:3`)* ⟦For an angel went down at a certain season into the pool and troubled the water; whoever then stepped in first after the troubling of the water was made whole, whatever disease he was afflicted with.⟧

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Greek critical text reads Bethzatha (rather than Bethesda), and the feminine adjective for "Sheep" is traditionally understood to imply the Sheep Gate.
- **5:3** — The earliest Greek manuscripts conclude verse 3 at "withered" and omit the explanation of an angel stirring the water found in later manuscripts as the end of verse 3 and verse 4.
- **5:27** — In Greek "Son of Man" appears here without the definite article, echoing Daniel 7:13 and underscoring Christ's messianic authority to execute judgment.
- **5:39** — The Greek verb form can be taken either as an indicative ("You search," fitting the surrounding argument in verses 39-40) or as an imperative ("Search").
- **5:4 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
