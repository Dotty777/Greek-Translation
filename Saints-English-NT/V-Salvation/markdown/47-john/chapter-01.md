## Text

**1:1** In the beginning the Word was, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

**1:2** This One was in the beginning with God.

**1:3** All things through Him came into being, and apart from Him came into being not even one thing. That which has come into being

**1:4** in Him was life, and the life was the light of men;

**1:5** and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it.

**1:6** There came a man, sent forth from God, whose name was John.

**1:7** This man came for witness, that he might bear witness concerning the light, that all might believe through him.

**1:8** He was not that light, but came that he might bear witness concerning the light.

**1:9** The true light, which enlightens every man, was coming into the world.

**1:10** He was in the world, and the world came into being through Him, yet the world did not know Him.

**1:11** To His own things He came, and His own people did not receive Him.

**1:12** Yet as many as received Him, to them He gave authority to become children of God, even to those who believe upon His name,

**1:13** who were begotten, not of bloods, nor of the will of flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

**1:14** And the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us; and we beheld His glory, glory as of an only-begotten from the Father, full of grace and truth.

**1:15** (John bears witness concerning Him, and has cried out, saying, "This was He of whom I spoke, 'He who comes after me has become before me, because He was prior to me.'")

**1:16** For out of His fullness we all have received, even grace upon grace;

**1:17** because the law was given through Moses, while grace and truth came into being through Jesus Christ.

**1:18** No one has ever beheld God; the only-begotten God, who is in the bosom of the Father, He has made Him known.

**1:19** And this is the witness of John, when the Jews sent forth priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, "Who are you?"

**1:20** And he confessed and did not deny, yes, he confessed, "I am not the Christ."

**1:21** And they asked him, "What then? Are you Elijah?" And he says, "I am not." "Are you the prophet?" And he answered, "No."

**1:22** They said therefore to him, "Who are you, that we may give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say concerning yourself?"

**1:23** He said, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of the Lord,' even as Isaiah the prophet said."

**1:24** Now those who had been sent were of the Pharisees.

**1:25** And they asked him and said to him, "Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?"

**1:26** John answered them, saying, "I baptize in water; in the midst of you stands One whom you do not know,

**1:27** He who comes after me, of whom I am not worthy to loose the strap of His sandal."

**1:28** These things came to pass in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

**1:29** On the next day he sees Jesus coming to him, and says, "Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!

**1:30** This is He on whose behalf I said, 'After me comes a man who has become before me, because He was prior to me.'

**1:31** And I did not know Him; yet that He might be made manifest to Israel, for this cause I came baptizing in water."

**1:32** And John bore witness, saying, "I have beheld the Spirit descending as a dove out of heaven, and He abode upon Him.

**1:33** And I did not know Him; but He who sent me to baptize in water, the same said to me, 'Upon whomever you see the Spirit descending and abiding upon Him, this is He who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.'

**1:34** And I have seen, and have borne witness that this One is the Son of God."

**1:35** On the next day again John was standing, and two of his disciples;

**1:36** and looking upon Jesus as He walked, he says, "Behold, the Lamb of God!"

**1:37** And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.

**1:38** Now Jesus, having turned and beheld them following, says to them, "What do you seek?" And they said to Him, "Rabbi (which being interpreted is called Teacher), where do You abide?"

**1:39** He says to them, "Come, and you shall see." They came therefore and saw where He abode, and they abode with Him that day; it was about the tenth hour.

**1:40** Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard from John and followed Him.

**1:41** This man finds first his own brother Simon and says to him, "We have found the Messiah" (which being interpreted is Christ).

**1:42** He brought him to Jesus. Looking upon him, Jesus said, "You are Simon, the son of John; you shall be called Cephas" (which is interpreted Peter).

**1:43** On the next day He desired to go forth into Galilee, and He finds Philip; and Jesus says to him, "Follow Me."

**1:44** Now Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter.

**1:45** Philip finds Nathanael and says to him, "Him of whom Moses wrote in the law, and also the prophets, we have found, Jesus son of Joseph, the one from Nazareth."

**1:46** And Nathanael said to him, "Out of Nazareth can any good thing be?" Philip says to him, "Come and see."

**1:47** Jesus saw Nathanael coming to Him and says concerning him, "Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!"

**1:48** Nathanael says to Him, "From where do You know me?" Jesus answered and said to him, "Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

**1:49** Nathanael answered Him, "Rabbi, You are the Son of God; You are King of Israel!"

**1:50** Jesus answered and said to him, "Because I said to you that I saw you beneath the fig tree, do you believe? Greater things than these you shall see."

**1:51** And He says to him, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, you shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — In Greek, theos en ho logos fronts the anarthrous predicate noun theos to affirm the Word's full deity without confounding His person with the Father; pros ton theon expresses personal communion ('with God').
- **1:3** — Following the punctuation of the Saints Greek Critical Text, ho gegonen ('That which has come into being') opens verse 4; other editions place the stop after it at the end of verse 3. In v. 5 katelaben carries a double nuance of 'overcame' and 'comprehended.'
- **1:14** — Greek eskenosen literally means 'pitched His tent / tabernacled,' evoking God's dwelling presence in the tabernacle of Exodus.
- **1:18** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads monogenes theos ('the only-begotten God,' supported by P66, P75, Sinaiticus, and Vaticanus), whereas later manuscripts read 'the only-begotten Son.'
- **1:51** — Although Jesus speaks directly to Nathanael ('to him'), the Greek shifts from second-person singular (v. 50) to second-person plural ('to you, you shall see'), encompassing all the disciples.
