## Text

**10:1** "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, he who does not enter through the door into the fold of the sheep, but climbs over from another place, that man is a thief and a robber.

**10:2** Yet he who enters through the door is the shepherd of the sheep.

**10:3** For him the doorkeeper opens, and the sheep listen to his voice; and he calls his own sheep by name and leads them forth.

**10:4** When he has brought forth all his own, he walks before them, and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.

**10:5** A stranger, however, they will by no means follow, but will flee from him, because they do not know the voice of strangers."

**10:6** Jesus spoke this figure of speech to them, yet they did not understand what things He was speaking to them.

**10:7** Therefore Jesus spoke to them again: "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.

**10:8** All, as many as came before Me, are thieves and robbers; yet the sheep did not listen to them.

**10:9** I am the door; if anyone enters through Me, he shall be saved, and shall go in and go out and shall find pasture.

**10:10** The thief does not come except that he may steal and slay and destroy; I came that they may have life, and may have it in abundance.

**10:11** I am the good Shepherd; the good Shepherd lays down His life on behalf of the sheep.

**10:12** The hireling, who is not a shepherd and to whom the sheep do not belong, beholds the wolf coming and abandons the sheep and flees—and the wolf snatches them and scatters them—

**10:13** because he is a hireling and has no concern for the sheep.

**10:14** I am the good Shepherd, and I know My own, and My own know Me,

**10:15** even as the Father knows Me and I know the Father; and I lay down My life on behalf of the sheep.

**10:16** And I have other sheep that are not of this fold; those also I must bring, and they shall hear My voice, and they shall become one flock, one Shepherd.

**10:17** For this cause the Father loves Me, because I lay down My life that I may take it again.

**10:18** No one has taken it from Me; rather, I lay it down of Myself. I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it again. This commandment I received from My Father."

**10:19** A division arose once more among the Jews on account of these words.

**10:20** And many among them were saying, "He has a demon and is mad. Why are you listening to Him?"

**10:21** Others were saying, "These words are not those of one possessed by a demon. Can a demon possibly open the eyes of the blind?"

**10:22** Then the Feast of Dedication took place in Jerusalem; it was winter,

**10:23** and Jesus was walking in the temple, in the portico of Solomon.

**10:24** Therefore the Jews encircled Him and were saying to Him, "How long do You keep our souls in suspense? If You are the Christ, tell us plainly."

**10:25** Jesus answered them, "I told you, and yet you do not believe. The works that I do in the name of My Father, these testify concerning Me.

**10:26** Yet you do not believe, because you are not of My sheep.

**10:27** My sheep listen to My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me;

**10:28** and I give eternal life to them, and they shall by no means ever perish, and no one shall snatch them from My hand.

**10:29** That which My Father has given Me is greater than all, and no one is able to snatch them from the Father's hand.

**10:30** The Father and I are one."

**10:31** Again the Jews took up stones in order to stone Him.

**10:32** Jesus answered them, "I have shown you many good works from the Father; for which work among them are you stoning Me?"

**10:33** The Jews answered Him, "For a good work we are not stoning You, but for blasphemy, and because You, being a man, make Yourself God."

**10:34** Jesus answered them, "Does it not stand written in your law, 'I said, You are gods'?

**10:35** If He called those gods to whom the word of God came—and the Scripture cannot be broken—

**10:36** concerning Him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, do you say, 'You blaspheme,' because I said, 'I am the Son of God'?

**10:37** If I am not doing the works of My Father, do not believe Me;

**10:38** but if I am doing them, even if you do not believe Me, believe the works, so that you may know and continue to know that the Father is in Me, and I in the Father."

**10:39** Therefore they tried once again to seize Him, yet He escaped from their hand.

**10:40** And He departed once more beyond the Jordan to the place where John was first baptizing, and He remained there.

**10:41** And many came to Him and were saying, "John indeed performed no sign, yet all that John spoke concerning this Man was true."

**10:42** And there many believed in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **10:16** — The Greek critical text reads the plural verb (they shall become one flock, one Shepherd), emphasizing the union of sheep from different folds, whereas later manuscripts read the singular (there shall be).
- **10:18** — The Greek critical text uses the aorist tense (has taken), emphasizing that no one has ever wrested Christ's life from Him, while later manuscripts read the present tense (takes).
- **10:29** — Following the earliest Greek witnesses with neuter forms, Jesus declares that what the Father has given Him is greater than all; many later manuscripts read masculine forms (My Father, who has given them to Me, is greater than all).
- **10:38** — The Greek critical text pairs the aorist and present subjunctives of the same verb (know and continue to know), underscoring both initial realization and abiding comprehension, whereas later manuscripts read know and believe.
