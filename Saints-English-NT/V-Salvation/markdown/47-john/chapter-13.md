## Text

**13:1** Now before the Feast of the Passover, Jesus, knowing that His hour had arrived to pass out of this world to the Father, having loved His own who were in the world, loved them to the very end.

**13:2** And while supper was taking place, the devil having already put it into the heart that Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, should betray Him,

**13:3** Jesus, knowing that the Father had placed all things into His hands, and that He had come forth from God and was returning to God,

**13:4** arose from supper, laid aside His outer garments, and having taken a linen towel, girded Himself.

**13:5** Then He poured water into the basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to wipe them dry with the towel with which He was girded.

**13:6** So He came to Simon Peter, and Peter said to Him, "Lord, are You washing my feet?"

**13:7** Jesus answered and said to him, "What I am doing you do not know now, yet you shall understand after these things."

**13:8** Peter said to Him, "By no means shall You ever wash my feet!" Jesus answered him, "Unless I wash you, you have no portion with Me."

**13:9** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, not my feet alone, but also my hands and my head!"

**13:10** Jesus said to him, "The one who has bathed has no need except to wash his feet, but is wholly clean; and you are clean, yet not all of you."

**13:11** For He knew who was betraying Him; because of this He said, "Not all of you are clean."

**13:12** So when He had washed their feet, taken His outer garments, and reclined at table, He spoke to them again, "Do you understand what I have done to you?

**13:13** You address Me as 'Teacher' and 'Lord,' and you speak rightly, for so I am.

**13:14** Therefore, if I, the Lord and the Teacher, washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.

**13:15** For I have set an example for you, that just as I have done to you, you also should do.

**13:16** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, a servant is not greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him.

**13:17** If you know these things, blessed are you if you practice them.

**13:18** I do not speak concerning all of you; I know those whom I have chosen. Yet it is that the Scripture may be fulfilled, 'He who eats bread with Me has lifted up his heel against Me.'

**13:19** From now on I am telling you before it comes to pass, that when it comes to pass, you may believe that I am.

**13:20** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, whoever receives anyone I send receives Me; and whoever receives Me receives Him who sent Me."

**13:21** Having spoken these things, Jesus was troubled in spirit, and testified and said, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you that one of you shall betray Me."

**13:22** The disciples looked at one another, at a loss to know of whom He was speaking.

**13:23** Now one of His disciples, the one whom Jesus loved, was reclining in the bosom of Jesus.

**13:24** So Simon Peter motioned to him to inquire who it might be of whom He was speaking.

**13:25** Then, leaning back thus upon the breast of Jesus, he said to Him, "Lord, who is it?"

**13:26** Jesus answered, "He is the one to whom I will give the morsel once I dip it." Then, having dipped the morsel, He gave it to Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot.

**13:27** And after the morsel, Satan then entered into him. Therefore Jesus said to him, "What you are doing, do swiftly."

**13:28** Yet none of those reclining at the table knew why He spoke this to him.

**13:29** For some supposed, since Judas kept the money box, that Jesus was saying to him, "Purchase what we need for the feast," or that he should give something to the poor.

**13:30** So after receiving the morsel, he went out immediately; and it was night.

**13:31** Therefore, when he had gone out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in Him.

**13:32** And God shall glorify Him in Himself, and immediately He shall glorify Him.

**13:33** Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You shall seek Me; and just as I declared to the Jews, 'Where I go you cannot come,' so now I say to you also.

**13:34** A new commandment I give you, that you love one another: just as I loved you, that you likewise love one another.

**13:35** In this all shall know that you are My disciples, if you have love one toward another."

**13:36** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, where are You going?" Jesus answered him, "Where I go you cannot follow Me now, yet afterward you shall follow."

**13:37** Peter said to Him, "Lord, why can I not follow You at this time? I will lay down my life for You."

**13:38** Jesus answered, "Will you lay down your life for Me? Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, a rooster shall by no means crow until you have denied Me three times."

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — Greek eis telos carries the twofold meaning "to the end" (temporally, through His impending death) and "to the uttermost" (to the fullest measure of love).
- **13:10** — Greek distinguishes two verbs here: louo ("bathe" the whole body) and nipto ("wash" a part, such as the feet). A few early manuscripts omit "except to wash his feet," though the phrase is retained in the Greek text translated here.
- **13:19** — Greek ego eimi ("I am") echoes the divine self-declaration of Exodus 3:14 and Isaiah 43:10, while also conveying the contextual sense "I am He."
- **13:32** — Some later manuscripts begin this verse with the clause "If God is glorified in Him," which is absent from the earlier witnesses followed in this text.
