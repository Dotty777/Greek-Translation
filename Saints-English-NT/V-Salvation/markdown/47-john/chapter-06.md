## Text

**6:1** After these things Jesus departed across the Sea of Galilee, that is, the Sea of Tiberias.

**6:2** And a great multitude was following Him, because they beheld the signs which He was performing on those who were sick.

**6:3** Now Jesus ascended the mountain, and there He sat with His disciples.

**6:4** And the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was near.

**6:5** Therefore, lifting up His eyes and beholding that a great multitude was coming to Him, Jesus said to Philip, "Where shall we buy bread, so that these people may eat?"

**6:6** Now He was saying this to test him, for He Himself knew what He was about to do.

**6:7** Philip answered Him, "Bread worth two hundred denarii does not suffice for them, that each one may receive a little."

**6:8** One of His disciples, Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, said to Him,

**6:9** "There is a young boy here who has five barley loaves and two small fish; yet what are these among so many?"

**6:10** Jesus said, "Have the people recline." Now there was abundant grass in the place. Therefore the men reclined, about five thousand in number.

**6:11** Then Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, He distributed them to those who were reclining; and likewise of the small fish, as much as they desired.

**6:12** And when they were filled, He said to His disciples, "Gather the leftover fragments, lest anything be lost."

**6:13** So they gathered them and filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves, which remained over from those who had eaten.

**6:14** Therefore, when the people saw the sign which He had performed, they were saying, "This is truly the Prophet who is coming into the world."

**6:15** Knowing, therefore, that they were about to come and seize Him in order to make Him king, Jesus withdrew again to the mountain by Himself alone.

**6:16** And when evening fell, His disciples went down to the sea,

**6:17** and embarking in a boat, they were crossing over the sea toward Capernaum. Now darkness had already fallen, and Jesus had not yet come to them,

**6:18** and the sea was being stirred up as a great wind was blowing.

**6:19** When they had rowed, therefore, about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they beheld Jesus walking upon the sea and drawing near to the boat; and they became afraid.

**6:20** And He said to them, "It is I; do not fear."

**6:21** Therefore they were willing to receive Him into the boat, and at once the boat was at the land toward which they were going.

**6:22** On the next day, the multitude that stood on the far side of the sea saw that no other small boat had been there except one, and that Jesus had not embarked in the boat with His disciples, but His disciples had departed alone.

**6:23** Other small boats came from Tiberias near the place where they had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks.

**6:24** When the multitude therefore saw that Jesus was not there, nor His disciples, they themselves embarked in the small boats and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.

**6:25** And having found Him across the sea, they said to Him, "Rabbi, when did You arrive here?"

**6:26** Jesus answered them and said, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, you seek Me not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves and were satisfied.

**6:27** Do not work for the food that perishes, but for the food that abides to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you; for upon Him God the Father has set His seal."

**6:28** Therefore they said to Him, "What are we to do, that we may work the works of God?"

**6:29** Jesus answered and said to them, "This is the work of God: that you believe in the One whom He sent."

**6:30** Therefore they said to Him, "What sign, then, do You perform, so that we may see and believe You? What work do You do?

**6:31** Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it is written, 'Bread from heaven He gave them to eat.'"

**6:32** Jesus therefore said to them, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, it is not Moses who has given you the bread from heaven, but My Father gives you the true bread from heaven;

**6:33** for the bread of God is He who descends from heaven and gives life to the world."

**6:34** Therefore they said to Him, "Lord, give us this bread at all times."

**6:35** Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to Me shall by no means hunger, and whoever believes in Me shall never thirst.

**6:36** Yet I told you that you have indeed seen Me, and still you do not believe."

**6:37** "All that the Father gives to Me shall come to Me, and whoever comes to Me I shall by no means cast out,

**6:38** for I have descended from heaven, not to do My own will, but the will of Him who sent Me.

**6:39** Now this is the will of Him who sent Me, that of all that He has given to Me I should lose none, but should raise it up on the last day.

**6:40** For this is the will of My Father: that everyone who beholds the Son and believes in Him should have eternal life, and I Myself shall raise him up on the last day."

**6:41** Therefore the Jews were murmuring about Him because He said, "I am the bread that descended from heaven,"

**6:42** and they were saying, "Is this not Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How does He now say, 'I have descended from heaven'?"

**6:43** Jesus answered and said to them, "Do not murmur with one another.

**6:44** No one is able to come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him; and I shall raise him up on the last day.

**6:45** It is written in the Prophets, 'And all of them shall be taught by God.' Everyone who has heard from the Father and learned comes to Me.

**6:46** Not that anyone has seen the Father, except the One who is from God; this One has seen the Father.

**6:47** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, the one who believes has eternal life.

**6:48** I am the bread of life.

**6:49** Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and died.

**6:50** This is the bread that descends from heaven, so that anyone may eat of it and not die.

**6:51** I am the living bread that descended from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he shall live forever; and the bread that I will give is My flesh, for the life of the world."

**6:52** Therefore the Jews were contending with one another, saying, "How can this man give us His flesh to eat?"

**6:53** Jesus therefore said to them, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you do not have life in yourselves.

**6:54** The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I Myself shall raise him up on the last day.

**6:55** For My flesh is true food, and My blood is true drink.

**6:56** The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood abides in Me, and I in him.

**6:57** Just as the living Father sent Me, and I live because of the Father, so also the one who eats Me shall live because of Me.

**6:58** This is the bread that descended from heaven, not as the fathers ate and died; the one who eats this bread shall live forever."

**6:59** These things He spoke in a synagogue while teaching in Capernaum.

**6:60** Many of His disciples, therefore, upon hearing this, said, "This saying is hard; who is able to listen to it?"

**6:61** Now Jesus, knowing within Himself that His disciples were murmuring about this, said to them, "Does this cause you to stumble?

**6:62** What, then, if you should behold the Son of Man ascending to where He was before?

**6:63** The Spirit is the One who gives life; the flesh avails nothing. The words that I have spoken to you are spirit and are life.

**6:64** Yet there are some among you who do not believe." For Jesus knew from the beginning which ones did not believe, and who it was that would betray Him.

**6:65** And He was saying, "For this reason I have told you that no one is able to come to Me unless it has been granted to him from the Father."

**6:66** From that time many of His disciples turned back and no longer walked with Him.

**6:67** Jesus therefore said to the Twelve, "Do you also wish to depart?"

**6:68** Simon Peter answered Him, "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have words of eternal life,

**6:69** and we ourselves have believed and come to know that You are the Holy One of God."

**6:70** Jesus answered them, "Did I Myself not choose you, the Twelve? Yet one among you is a devil."

**6:71** Now He was speaking of Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot; for he was about to betray Him, one of the Twelve.

## Translator Notes

- **6:11** — The Greek critical text states directly that Jesus distributed the loaves to those reclining, without the later manuscript expansion adding 'to the disciples, and the disciples to those reclining.'
- **6:19** — Twenty-five to thirty stadia equals roughly three to three and a half miles (about five kilometers), placing the boat well out toward the middle of the Sea of Galilee.
- **6:20** — The Greek phrase 'Ego eimi' serves immediately as a word of recognition ('It is I'), while also carrying the solemn Johannine resonance of the divine self-revelation ('I am'; cf. Exodus 3:14).
- **6:33** — The Greek masculine participle 'ho katabainon' agrees grammatically with both 'bread' ('that which descends') and a person ('He who descends'), preparing for Jesus's explicit declaration in verse 35.
- **6:47** — The Greek critical text reads simply 'the one who believes has eternal life,' without the later manuscript addition 'in Me.'
- **6:51** — The Greek critical text ends with 'is My flesh, for the life of the world,' omitting the repeated clause 'which I will give' found in later manuscripts.
- **6:55** — The Greek critical text uses the adjective 'alethes' ('true food ... true drink') rather than the later adverbial variant 'alethos' ('food indeed ... drink indeed').
- **6:69** — The Greek critical text reads 'the Holy One of God,' whereas later Byzantine manuscripts expand Peter's confession toward Matthew 16:16 ('the Christ, the Son of the living God').
