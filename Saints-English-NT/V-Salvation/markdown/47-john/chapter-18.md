## Text

**18:1** Having spoken these things, Jesus went forth with His disciples across the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which He and His disciples entered.

**18:2** Now Judas also, who was betraying Him, knew the place, because Jesus had often gathered there with His disciples.

**18:3** So Judas, having taken the cohort of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and from the Pharisees, came there with lanterns, torches, and weapons.

**18:4** Jesus therefore, knowing all the things that were coming upon Him, went forth and said to them, "Whom do you seek?"

**18:5** They answered Him, "Jesus the Nazarene." He said to them, "I am He." And Judas also, who was betraying Him, was standing with them.

**18:6** Therefore, when He said to them, "I am He," they stepped back and fell to the ground.

**18:7** Again therefore He questioned them, "Whom do you seek?" And they said, "Jesus the Nazarene."

**18:8** Jesus answered, "I told you that I am He; if therefore you seek Me, let these men go,"

**18:9** so that the word might be fulfilled which He spoke: "Of those whom You have given Me, I did not lose a single one."

**18:10** Then Simon Peter, who had a sword, drew it, struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear. Now the servant's name was Malchus.

**18:11** Therefore Jesus said to Peter, "Put the sword into its sheath. The cup which the Father has given Me, shall I not drink it?"

**18:12** Then the cohort of soldiers and the commander and the officers of the Jews seized Jesus and bound Him,

**18:13** and led Him first to Annas; for he was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was high priest that year.

**18:14** Now Caiaphas was the one who had counseled the Jews that it was expedient for one man to die on behalf of the people.

**18:15** And Simon Peter was following Jesus, along with another disciple. Now that disciple was known to the high priest, and he entered with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest;

**18:16** but Peter stood outside at the door. So the other disciple, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the doorkeeper, and brought Peter inside.

**18:17** Then the servant girl who kept the door said to Peter, "Surely you also are not one of this man's disciples?" He replied, "I am not."

**18:18** Now the servants and the officers were standing there, having made a charcoal fire because it was cold, and they were warming themselves; and Peter also was standing with them, warming himself.

**18:19** Then the high priest questioned Jesus concerning His disciples and concerning His teaching.

**18:20** Jesus answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world. I always taught in synagogue and in the temple, where all the Jews gather together, and in secret I spoke nothing.

**18:21** Why do you question Me? Question those who have heard what I spoke to them; behold, these know the things which I said."

**18:22** Now when He had spoken these things, one of the officers standing by struck Jesus with his hand, saying, "Is this how You answer the high priest?"

**18:23** Jesus answered him, "If I spoke wrongly, bear witness to the wrong; yet if rightly, why do you strike Me?"

**18:24** Annas therefore sent Him bound to Caiaphas the high priest.

**18:25** Now Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. So they said to him, "Surely you also are not one of His disciples?" He denied it and said, "I am not."

**18:26** One of the high priest's servants, being a kinsman of the one whose ear Peter had cut off, said, "Did I not see you with Him in the garden?"

**18:27** Therefore Peter denied it again, and at once a rooster crowed.

**18:28** Then they brought Jesus from Caiaphas into the Praetorium; and it was early morning. Yet they themselves did not enter the Praetorium, lest they be defiled, but rather might eat the Passover.

**18:29** Therefore Pilate went outside to them and said, "What accusation do you bring against this man?"

**18:30** They answered and said to him, "If this man were not doing evil, we would not have delivered Him over to you."

**18:31** Therefore Pilate said to them, "Take Him yourselves, and according to your law judge Him." The Jews said to him, "It is not permitted for us to put anyone to death,"

**18:32** so that the word of Jesus might be fulfilled which He spoke, indicating by what kind of death He was about to die.

**18:33** Then Pilate entered the Praetorium again, summoned Jesus, and said to Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?"

**18:34** Jesus answered, "Do you say this of your own accord, or did others tell you concerning Me?"

**18:35** Pilate answered, "Am I a Jew? Your own nation and the chief priests delivered You over to me. What have You done?"

**18:36** Jesus answered, "My kingdom is not of this world. If My kingdom were of this world, My servants would be fighting, lest I be delivered over to the Jews; yet as it is, My kingdom is not from here."

**18:37** Therefore Pilate said to Him, "Then You are a king?" Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. To this end I have been born, and to this end I have come into the world, that I may bear witness to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth hears My voice."

**18:38** Pilate said to Him, "What is truth?" And having said this, he went out again to the Jews and said to them, "I find no grounds for a charge in Him.

**18:39** Yet you have a custom that I should release one man to you at the Passover; do you wish therefore that I release to you the King of the Jews?"

**18:40** Therefore they cried out again, saying, "Not this man, but Barabbas!" Now Barabbas was a robber.

## Translator Notes

- **18:5** — Jesus's reply "I am He" translates the Greek "ego eimi" (literally "I am"), echoing God's self-disclosure in the Scriptures and helping explain why the arresting party stepped back and fell to the ground in 18:6.
- **18:13** — Annas had served as high priest earlier (A.D. 6-15) and retained major influence and the courtesy title while his son-in-law Caiaphas held the official office that year.
- **18:37** — Jesus's reply "You say that I am a king" is a qualified affirmation acknowledging kingship while distancing Himself from Pilate's political sense of the title; some interpreters punctuate it as "You say so, for I am a king."
- **18:40** — The Greek term "lestes" ("robber" or "bandit") was frequently used in first-century Judea for violent rebels or insurrectionists.
