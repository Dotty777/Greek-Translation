## Text

**20:1** Now on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene came early to the tomb while darkness still remained, and saw the stone removed from the tomb.

**20:2** Therefore she ran and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple, the one whom Jesus loved, and said to them, "They have taken the Lord from the tomb, and we do not know where they have laid Him."

**20:3** Peter therefore went forth, and the other disciple, and they were going to the tomb.

**20:4** And the two were running together; and the other disciple ran ahead more swiftly than Peter and arrived at the tomb first.

**20:5** And stooping down to look inside, he saw the linen wrappings lying there; yet he did not enter.

**20:6** Therefore Simon Peter also came, following after him, and entered the tomb; and he beheld the linen wrappings lying there,

**20:7** and the face cloth that had been upon His head, not lying with the linen wrappings, but folded up apart in a place of its own.

**20:8** Then the other disciple, who had come to the tomb first, entered also, and he saw and believed.

**20:9** For they did not yet understand the Scripture, that it was necessary for Him to rise from the dead.

**20:10** Therefore the disciples departed again to their own homes.

**20:11** But Mary was standing outside at the tomb, weeping. So as she wept, she stooped down to look inside the tomb,

**20:12** and she beheld two angels in white seated there, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.

**20:13** And they said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping?" She replied to them, "They have removed my Lord, and I do not know where they have laid Him."

**20:14** When she had spoken these words, she turned around and beheld Jesus standing there, and she did not know that it was Jesus.

**20:15** Jesus said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping? Whom do you seek?" She, supposing that He was the gardener, said to Him, "Sir, if You have borne Him away, tell me where You have placed Him, and I will take Him away."

**20:16** Jesus said to her, "Mary!" Turning, she said to Him in Hebrew, "Rabboni!" (which means Teacher).

**20:17** Jesus said to her, "Do not cling to Me, for I have not yet ascended to the Father; rather, go to My brethren and tell them, 'I am ascending to My Father and your Father, and to My God and your God.'"

**20:18** Mary Magdalene came bringing word to the disciples, "I have seen the Lord," and that He had said these things to her.

**20:19** Therefore, when it was evening on that day, the first day of the week, and the doors were locked where the disciples were because of fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in their midst, and said to them, "Peace be to you."

**20:20** And when He had said this, He showed His hands and His side to them. Therefore the disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.

**20:21** Jesus therefore said to them again, "Peace be to you; just as the Father has sent Me, I also am sending you."

**20:22** And having said this, He breathed upon them and said to them, "Receive the Holy Spirit.

**20:23** If you forgive the sins of any, they stand forgiven to them; if you retain the sins of any, they stand retained."

**20:24** Now Thomas, one of the twelve, the one called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.

**20:25** Therefore the other disciples were saying to him, "We have seen the Lord." Yet he said to them, "Unless I see in His hands the imprint of the nails, and place my finger into the imprint of the nails, and place my hand into His side, I will by no means believe."

**20:26** And after eight days His disciples were inside once again, and Thomas was with them. Jesus came while the doors were locked, and stood in their midst and said, "Peace be to you."

**20:27** Next He said to Thomas, "Bring your finger here and behold My hands; and bring your hand and place it into My side; and do not become unbelieving, but believing."

**20:28** Thomas replied and said to Him, "My Lord and my God!"

**20:29** Jesus said to him, "Because you have seen Me, have you believed? Blessed are those who did not see, and yet believed."

**20:30** Indeed, Jesus also performed many other signs before His disciples, which are not written in this book;

**20:31** yet these things have been written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that through believing you may have life in His name.

## Translator Notes

- **20:16** — In the Greek text, Jesus addresses Mary with the Semitic form Mariam, and she replies in Aramaic/Hebrew with Rabboni, an elevated form of Rabbi meaning 'Teacher' or 'my Master.'
- **20:17** — The Greek present imperative 'Do not cling to Me' (literally 'Do not touch Me') implies ceasing an ongoing or attempted hold; the critical Greek text reads 'to the Father' rather than 'to My Father.'
- **20:29** — Following the critical Greek text, the vocative 'Thomas' is absent here, and the opening clause is rendered as a question ('Because you have seen Me, have you believed?').
- **20:31** — Greek manuscripts divide between the present subjunctive ('may continue to believe') and the aorist subjunctive ('may come to believe'); the present tense here encompasses both coming to faith and persevering in it.
