## Text

**3:1** Now there was a man from among the Pharisees, Nicodemus by name, a ruler of the Jews.

**3:2** This man came to Him by night and said to Him, "Rabbi, we know that You have come from God as a teacher; for no one is able to perform these signs which You do unless God is with him."

**3:3** Jesus answered and said to him, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born anew, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

**3:4** Nicodemus said to Him, "How is a man able to be born when he is old? Surely he cannot enter into his mother's womb a second time and be born?"

**3:5** Jesus answered, "Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

**3:6** What is born of the flesh is flesh, and what is born of the Spirit is spirit.

**3:7** Do not marvel that I told you, 'You must be born anew.'

**3:8** The wind blows where it wills, and you hear its sound, yet you do not know where it comes from and where it goes; thus is everyone who has been born of the Spirit."

**3:9** Nicodemus answered and said to Him, "How can these things come to pass?"

**3:10** Jesus answered and said to him, "You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not know these things?

**3:11** Assuredly, assuredly, I say to you, what We know We speak, and what We have seen We bear witness to, yet you do not receive Our testimony.

**3:12** If I have spoken to you of earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I speak to you of heavenly things?

**3:13** And no one has ascended into heaven except He who came down from heaven, the Son of Man.

**3:14** And just as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,

**3:15** that everyone who believes in Him may have eternal life.

**3:16** For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that everyone who believes in Him may not perish, but have eternal life.

**3:17** For God did not send the Son into the world that He might judge the world, but that the world might be saved through Him.

**3:18** He who believes in Him is not judged; yet he who does not believe has already been judged, because he has not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.

**3:19** And this is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light, for their works were evil.

**3:20** For everyone who practices wicked things hates the light and does not come to the light, lest his works be exposed.

**3:21** Yet he who does the truth comes to the light, that his works may be made manifest, that they have been accomplished in God."

**3:22** After these things Jesus and His disciples went into the land of Judea, and there He remained with them and was baptizing.

**3:23** Now John also was baptizing in Aenon near Salim, because abundant waters were there; and people were coming and being baptized,

**3:24** for John had not yet been cast into prison.

**3:25** Then a dispute arose on the part of John's disciples with a Jew concerning purification.

**3:26** And they came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, He who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have borne witness—behold, this One is baptizing, and all are coming to Him."

**3:27** John answered and said, "A man cannot receive even a single thing unless it has been given to him from heaven.

**3:28** You yourselves bear witness for me that I said, 'I am not the Christ,' but rather, 'I have been sent before Him.'

**3:29** He who has the bride is the bridegroom; yet the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and listens to him, rejoices with great joy because of the bridegroom's voice. Therefore this joy of mine has been made full.

**3:30** He must increase, yet I must decrease."

**3:31** He who comes from above is over all; he who is from the earth is of the earth and speaks from the earth. He who comes from heaven is over all.

**3:32** What He has seen and heard, to this He bears witness, yet no one receives His testimony.

**3:33** He who has received His testimony has set his seal that God is true.

**3:34** For He whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for He does not give the Spirit by measure.

**3:35** The Father loves the Son, and into His hand He has given all things.

**3:36** He who believes in the Son has eternal life; yet he who disobeys the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of God abides upon him.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek adverb anothen carries a deliberate double meaning—"anew/again" and "from above"—which explains Nicodemus' literal misunderstanding in verse 4 while anticipating "from above" in verse 31.
- **3:13** — The earliest Greek manuscripts end the verse with "the Son of Man"; later manuscripts add the clause "who is in heaven."
- **3:16** — Greek monogenes denotes "only begotten" or "unique, one of a kind." Because ancient Greek manuscripts lack quotation marks, interpreters differ on whether verses 16-21 continue Jesus' words to Nicodemus or provide the evangelist's reflection.
- **3:25** — The critical Greek text reads the singular "a Jew," whereas later manuscripts read the plural "the Jews."
- **3:31** — Many interpreters treat verses 31-36 as the evangelist's theological reflection following John the Baptist's climactic words in verse 30, though others view them as a continuation of John's testimony.
