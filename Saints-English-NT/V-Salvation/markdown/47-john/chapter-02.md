## Text

**2:1** And on the third day a wedding took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.

**2:2** Now Jesus and His disciples were also invited to the wedding.

**2:3** And when the wine failed, the mother of Jesus said to Him, "They have no wine."

**2:4** And Jesus said to her, "What is that to Me and to you, woman? My hour has not yet arrived."

**2:5** His mother told the servants, "Whatever He says to you, do it."

**2:6** Now six stone water jars were set there according to the purification of the Jews, each holding two or three measures.

**2:7** Jesus said to them, "Fill the water jars with water." So they filled them to the very brim.

**2:8** And He said to them, "Draw some now, and bring it to the master of the banquet." And they brought it.

**2:9** When the master of the banquet tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it came from—though the servants who had drawn the water knew—the master of the banquet called the bridegroom,

**2:10** and said to him, "Every man sets forth the good wine first, and when they have drunk freely, then the lesser; you have reserved the good wine until now."

**2:11** This beginning of the signs Jesus performed in Cana of Galilee, and revealed His glory; and His disciples believed in Him.

**2:12** After this He went down to Capernaum—He, His mother, His brothers, and His disciples—and they did not remain there many days.

**2:13** And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

**2:14** And in the temple He found those who were selling oxen, sheep, and doves, and the money changers sitting there.

**2:15** And having made a whip of cords, He drove them all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and He poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned the tables.

**2:16** And to those who sold the doves He said, "Take these things away from here; do not make My Father's house a house of commerce."

**2:17** His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for Your house will devour Me."

**2:18** Therefore the Jews answered and said to Him, "What sign do You show us, seeing that You do these things?"

**2:19** Jesus answered and said to them, "Tear down this temple, and in three days I will raise it up."

**2:20** Then the Jews said, "In forty-six years this temple was built, and will You raise it up in three days?"

**2:21** He, however, was speaking of the temple of His body.

**2:22** Therefore, when He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had said this, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus spoke.

**2:23** Now while He was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in His name, beholding His signs which He was performing.

**2:24** Yet Jesus Himself did not entrust Himself to them, because He knew all men,

**2:25** and because He had no need that anyone should bear witness concerning man, for He Himself knew what was in man.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — Literally "What is it to Me and to you, woman?"—a Semitic idiom signaling gentle distance rather than disrespect, as "woman" (Greek gynai) was a polite form of address.
- **2:6** — A "measure" (Greek metretes) was a liquid measure of roughly nine to ten gallons (about thirty-nine liters), meaning each stone jar held about twenty to thirty gallons.
- **2:17** — Quoting Psalm 69:9 (LXX 68:10); the critical Greek text reads the future tense "will devour Me," whereas later manuscripts harmonize to the past tense "has eaten Me up."
- **2:24** — The Greek uses the same verb (pisteuo) for the crowd's "believed" in verse 23 and Jesus' refusal to "entrust" Himself to them here, highlighting the contrast between superficial faith and true trust.
