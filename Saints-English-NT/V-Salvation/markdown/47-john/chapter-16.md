## Text

**16:1** "These things I have spoken to you, lest you be caused to stumble.

**16:2** They will expel you from the synagogues; indeed, an hour is coming when everyone who kills you will suppose that he is offering sacred service to God.

**16:3** And they will do these things because they have known neither the Father nor Me.

**16:4** Yet I have spoken these things to you, so that when their hour comes you may remember them, that I Myself told you. Now I did not tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you.

**16:5** But now I am going to the One who sent Me, and not one of you asks Me, 'Where are You going?'

**16:6** Rather, because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

**16:7** Yet I tell you the truth: it is for your benefit that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Advocate will not come to you; but if I depart, I shall send Him to you.

**16:8** And when He comes, He will convict the world concerning sin, and concerning righteousness, and concerning judgment:

**16:9** concerning sin, because they do not believe in Me;

**16:10** concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father and you no longer behold Me;

**16:11** and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.

**16:12** I still have many things to speak to you, yet you are not able to bear them at this time.

**16:13** Yet when He comes, the Spirit of truth, He will guide you into all the truth; for He will not speak from Himself, but whatever He shall hear He will speak, and He will declare to you the things that are to come.

**16:14** He will glorify Me, because He will receive from what is Mine and will declare it to you.

**16:15** All things that the Father has are Mine; for this reason I said that He receives from what is Mine and will declare it to you.

**16:16** A little while, and you no longer behold Me; and again a little while, and you shall see Me."

**16:17** Therefore some of His disciples said to one another, "What is this that He says to us: 'A little while, and you do not behold Me; and again a little while, and you shall see Me'; and, 'Because I go to the Father'?"

**16:18** So they were saying, "What is this that He says, 'a little while'? We do not know what He is speaking of."

**16:19** Jesus knew that they desired to question Him, and He said to them, "Are you inquiring among yourselves about this, that I said, 'A little while, and you do not behold Me; and again a little while, and you shall see Me'?

**16:20** Assuredly, I say to you that you will weep and lament, while the world will rejoice; you will be made sorrowful, yet your sorrow shall become joy.

**16:21** A woman, when she is giving birth, has sorrow because her hour has come; yet when she has delivered the child, she no longer remembers the anguish, on account of the joy that a human being has been born into the world.

**16:22** Therefore you also now have sorrow; yet I shall see you again, and your heart shall rejoice, and no one will take your joy away from you.

**16:23** And in that day you will question Me about nothing. Assuredly, I say to you, if you ask the Father for anything, He will give it to you in My name.

**16:24** Until this time you have asked for nothing in My name; ask, and you shall receive, so that your joy may be made full.

**16:25** These things I have spoken to you in figures of speech; an hour is coming when I shall no longer speak to you in figures of speech, but shall declare to you plainly concerning the Father.

**16:26** In that day you will ask in My name, and I do not say to you that I shall petition the Father on your behalf;

**16:27** for the Father Himself loves you, because you yourselves have loved Me and have believed that I came forth from God.

**16:28** I came forth from the Father and have come into the world; again, I am leaving the world and departing to the Father."

**16:29** His disciples say, "Behold, now You speak plainly, and You speak no figure of speech.

**16:30** Now we know that You know all things and have no need for anyone to question You; by this we believe that You came forth from God."

**16:31** Jesus answered them, "Do you believe now?

**16:32** Behold, an hour is coming, and indeed has come, when you will be scattered, each to his own home, and will leave Me alone; yet I am not alone, because the Father is with Me.

**16:33** These things I have spoken to you, so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you have tribulation; yet be of good courage—I have overcome the world."

## Translator Notes

- **16:16** — The critical Greek text does not include "because I go to the Father" at the end of this verse; the disciples' quotation in verse 17 echoes Jesus' words in verse 10.
- **16:23** — In the critical Greek text, "in My name" follows "He will give it to you," highlighting that the Father's giving—as well as the disciples' asking (v. 24)—is granted in Christ's name.
- **16:27** — The critical Greek text reads "from God" (para tou theou), whereas some manuscripts harmonize to "from the Father."
- **16:33** — The critical Greek text reads the present tense echete ("you have tribulation"), depicting affliction as a present reality rather than only a future one.
