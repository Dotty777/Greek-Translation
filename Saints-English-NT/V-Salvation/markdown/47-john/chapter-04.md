## Text

**4:1** Now when the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John—

**4:2** although Jesus Himself was not baptizing, but rather His disciples—

**4:3** He departed from Judea and went away again into Galilee.

**4:4** And it was necessary for Him to pass through Samaria.

**4:5** Therefore He came to a city of Samaria called Sychar, near the parcel of land which Jacob gave to Joseph his son.

**4:6** Now the well of Jacob was there. Jesus therefore, having grown weary from the journey, was sitting thus beside the well; it was about the sixth hour.

**4:7** A woman from Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give Me to drink."

**4:8** For His disciples had departed into the city, that they might buy provisions.

**4:9** Therefore the Samaritan woman said to Him, "How is it that You, being a Jew, ask to drink from me, who am a Samaritan woman?" For Jews do not associate with Samaritans.

**4:10** Jesus answered and said to her, "If you had known the gift of God, and who He is who says to you, 'Give Me to drink,' you would have asked Him, and He would have given you living water."

**4:11** The woman said to Him, "Sir, You have no vessel to draw with, and the well is deep; where then do You obtain this living water?

**4:12** Are You greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank from it himself, as well as his sons and his livestock?"

**4:13** Jesus answered and said to her, "Everyone who drinks of this water shall thirst again;

**4:14** but whoever drinks of the water which I shall give him shall never thirst; rather, the water which I shall give him shall become within him a fountain of water welling up into eternal life."

**4:15** The woman said to Him, "Sir, give me this water, that I may neither thirst nor come all the way here to draw."

**4:16** He said to her, "Go, summon your husband, and come here."

**4:17** The woman answered and said to Him, "I have no husband." Jesus said to her, "You have spoken rightly in saying, 'I have no husband';

**4:18** for you have had five husbands, and the one whom you now have is not your husband; this you have spoken truthfully."

**4:19** The woman said to Him, "Sir, I discern that You are a prophet.

**4:20** Our fathers worshiped upon this mountain, and you say that in Jerusalem is the place where one must worship."

**4:21** Jesus said to her, "Believe Me, woman, an hour is coming when neither upon this mountain nor in Jerusalem shall you worship the Father.

**4:22** You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, because salvation is from the Jews.

**4:23** Yet an hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth; for indeed the Father seeks such as these to worship Him.

**4:24** God is Spirit, and it is necessary for those who worship Him to worship in spirit and truth."

**4:25** The woman said to Him, "I know that Messiah is coming—He who is called Christ; when that One comes, He will declare all things to us."

**4:26** Jesus said to her, "I am He, the One who speaks to you."

**4:27** And upon this His disciples arrived, and they were marveling that He was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, "What do You seek?" or "Why are You speaking with her?"

**4:28** Therefore the woman left her water jar and went away into the city, and said to the people,

**4:29** "Come, see a man who told me all the things that I did! Could this possibly be the Christ?"

**4:30** They went out from the city and were coming toward Him.

**4:31** Meanwhile the disciples were urging Him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

**4:32** But He said to them, "I have food to eat which you do not know."

**4:33** Therefore the disciples were saying to one another, "Could anyone have brought Him something to eat?"

**4:34** Jesus said to them, "My food is that I should do the will of Him who sent Me, and accomplish His work.

**4:35** Do you not say, 'There are still four months, and the harvest comes'? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes and look upon the fields, for they are white for harvest. Already

**4:36** the reaper receives wages and gathers fruit for eternal life, that both the sower and the reaper may rejoice together.

**4:37** For in this the saying is true: 'One is the sower, and another is the reaper.'

**4:38** I sent you to reap that for which you have not toiled; others have toiled, and you have entered into their labor."

**4:39** Now many of the Samaritans from that city believed in Him on account of the word of the woman who testified, "He told me all things that I did."

**4:40** Therefore, when the Samaritans came to Him, they implored Him to remain with them; and He remained there two days.

**4:41** And far more believed on account of His word,

**4:42** and they were saying to the woman, "We no longer believe because of your report; for we ourselves have heard, and we know that this One is truly the Savior of the world."

**4:43** Now after the two days He went forth from there into Galilee.

**4:44** For Jesus Himself bore witness that a prophet has no honor in his own homeland.

**4:45** Therefore, when He arrived in Galilee, the Galileans welcomed Him, having beheld all the things which He had done in Jerusalem during the feast; for they themselves had also gone to the feast.

**4:46** Therefore He came again into Cana of Galilee, where He had made the water wine. And there was a certain royal official whose son was ill in Capernaum.

**4:47** This man, having heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, went away to Him and pleaded that He would come down and heal his son; for he was about to die.

**4:48** Jesus therefore said to him, "Unless you behold signs and wonders, you shall by no means believe."

**4:49** The royal official said to Him, "Sir, come down before my little child dies!"

**4:50** Jesus said to him, "Go; your son lives." The man believed the word which Jesus spoke to him, and went on his way.

**4:51** And while he was already going down, his servants met him, saying that his boy lives.

**4:52** Therefore he inquired of them the hour at which he began to mend. So they said to him, "Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him."

**4:53** The father therefore knew that it was at that very hour in which Jesus had said to him, "Your son lives"; and he himself believed, and his entire household.

**4:54** Now this again was the second sign that Jesus performed, having come from Judea into Galilee.

## Translator Notes

- **4:9** — The Greek verb συγχρῶνται can mean either 'associate with' or more specifically 'use vessels in common with,' reflecting Jewish purity customs concerning Samaritans.
- **4:26** — Literally 'I am, the One speaking to you' (Ἐγώ εἰμι); while serving as a direct self-identification as the Messiah, the wording also echoes the solemn divine self-revelation of Exodus 3:14 and Isaiah 43:10 and 52:6.
- **4:35** — In the critical Greek text, the adverb ἤδη ('Already') stands after the pause at the end of verse 35 and introduces the sentence continuing into verse 36, whereas older editions attach it to the preceding clause ('already white for harvest').
- **4:42** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read simply 'the Savior of the world,' without the additional title 'the Christ' found in later Byzantine witnesses.
- **4:46** — The Greek term βασιλικός denotes an officer in royal service, most likely an official attached to the court of Herod Antipas, tetrarch of Galilee.
