## Text

**1:1** The book of the lineage of Jesus Christ, the Son of David, the Son of Abraham.

**1:2** Abraham fathered Isaac, and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers,

**1:3** and Judah fathered Perez and Zerah by Tamar, and Perez fathered Hezron, and Hezron fathered Ram,

**1:4** and Ram fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon,

**1:5** and Salmon fathered Boaz by Rahab, and Boaz fathered Obed by Ruth, and Obed fathered Jesse,

**1:6** and Jesse fathered David the king. And David fathered Solomon by the wife of Uriah,

**1:7** and Solomon fathered Rehoboam, and Rehoboam fathered Abijah, and Abijah fathered Asaph,

**1:8** and Asaph fathered Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat fathered Joram, and Joram fathered Uzziah,

**1:9** and Uzziah fathered Jotham, and Jotham fathered Ahaz, and Ahaz fathered Hezekiah,

**1:10** and Hezekiah fathered Manasseh, and Manasseh fathered Amos, and Amos fathered Josiah,

**1:11** and Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers at the time of the exile to Babylon.

**1:12** And after the exile to Babylon, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel, and Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel,

**1:13** and Zerubbabel fathered Abiud, and Abiud fathered Eliakim, and Eliakim fathered Azor,

**1:14** and Azor fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Achim, and Achim fathered Eliud,

**1:15** and Eliud fathered Eleazar, and Eleazar fathered Matthan, and Matthan fathered Jacob,

**1:16** and Jacob fathered Joseph, the husband of Mary, from whom Jesus was born, who is called Christ.

**1:17** Therefore all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations, and from David to the exile to Babylon are fourteen generations, and from the exile to Babylon to the Christ are fourteen generations.

**1:18** Now the birth of Jesus Christ came about in this manner: When His mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together, she was found to be with child by the Holy Spirit.

**1:19** And Joseph her husband, being a righteous man and not wishing to expose her to public disgrace, resolved to divorce her secretly.

**1:20** But as he pondered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, "Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary as your wife, for that which has been conceived in her is of the Holy Spirit.

**1:21** And she shall bear a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He Himself shall save His people from their sins."

**1:22** Now all this took place that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying:

**1:23** "Behold, the virgin shall be with child and shall bear a Son, and they shall call His name Immanuel," which is translated, "God is with us."

**1:24** And Joseph, awakening from sleep, did as the angel of the Lord had commanded him, and took his wife,

**1:25** yet he did not know her until she had borne a Son; and he called His name Jesus.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek word genesis means "origin," "birth," or "lineage," echoing the Septuagint of Genesis 2:4 and 5:1.
- **1:7** — The Greek critical text reads "Asaph" (rather than "Asa," the king of Judah in 1 Kings 15:8), a spelling attested in the earliest manuscripts of Matthew.
- **1:10** — The Greek critical text reads "Amos" (rather than "Amon," the king of Judah in 2 Kings 21:18), reflecting the earliest manuscript tradition of Matthew.
- **1:25** — The Greek critical text reads simply "a Son"; later Byzantine manuscripts expand this to "her firstborn Son."
