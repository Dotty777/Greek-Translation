## Text

**28:1** Now after the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to view the sepulcher.

**28:2** And behold, a great earthquake occurred; for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven and approached, rolled away the stone, and was sitting upon it.

**28:3** Now his countenance was like lightning, and his garment was white as snow.

**28:4** And from fear of him, those keeping watch were shaken and became as dead men.

**28:5** Then the angel answered and said to the women, "As for you, do not be afraid; for I know that you are seeking Jesus, who has been crucified.

**28:6** He is not here, for He has been raised, just as He said. Come, behold the place where He lay.

**28:7** And go swiftly and tell His disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead; and behold, He is going before you into Galilee; there you shall see Him.' Behold, I have told you."

**28:8** And departing swiftly from the tomb with fear and great joy, they ran to report to His disciples.

**28:9** And behold, Jesus met them, saying, "Rejoice!" And drawing near, they clasped His feet and worshiped Him.

**28:10** Then Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go, report to My brethren that they should depart into Galilee, and there they shall see Me."

**28:11** Now while they were on their way, behold, some of the guard came into the city and reported to the chief priests all that had come to pass.

**28:12** And having assembled with the elders and taken counsel together, they gave a substantial sum of silver to the soldiers,

**28:13** saying, "Say, 'His disciples came by night and stole Him away while we were sleeping.'

**28:14** And if this should come before the governor, we ourselves will persuade him and keep you free from care."

**28:15** So they received the silver and did as they were taught. And this report has been spread abroad among the Jews until this very day.

**28:16** Now the eleven disciples departed into Galilee, to the mountain that Jesus had designated for them,

**28:17** and when they saw Him, they worshiped, yet some doubted.

**28:18** And drawing near, Jesus spoke to them, saying, "All authority in heaven and upon the earth has been given to Me.

**28:19** Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,

**28:20** teaching them to keep all things that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you all the days, even until the close of the age."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Greek phrase opse de sabbaton can mean "late on the Sabbath," but in Hellenistic Greek opse with the genitive commonly means "after," which aligns here with the dawning of the first day of the week.
- **28:6** — The Greek critical text reads simply "where He lay," omitting the clarifying title "the Lord" found in later manuscripts.
- **28:9** — Greek chairete serves both as a standard greeting ("Greetings!") and literally as a call to "Rejoice!"; the critical text also omits the later scribal expansion "And as they went to tell His disciples" at the opening of the verse.
- **28:17** — The Greek construction hoi de edistasan ("yet some doubted" or "hesitated") lacks an explicit contrasting "some" in the first clause, so interpreters debate whether only a portion of the eleven hesitated or whether all experienced a mixture of worship and uncertainty.
- **28:20** — The phrase "all the days" literally renders pasas tas hemeras (often smoothed to "always"), "the close of the age" renders synteleias tou aionos, and the critical text concludes without the later liturgical addition "Amen."
