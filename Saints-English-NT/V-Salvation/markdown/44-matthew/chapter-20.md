## Text

**20:1** "For the kingdom of heaven is like a master of a house who went out early at dawn to hire laborers for his vineyard.

**20:2** And when he had agreed with the laborers for a denarius a day, he sent them into his vineyard.

**20:3** And going out about the third hour, he saw others standing idle in the marketplace,

**20:4** and he said to those men, 'Go into the vineyard as well, and whatever is just I will give you.'

**20:5** So they went. Going out again about the sixth and the ninth hour, he did likewise.

**20:6** And about the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing, and said to them, 'Why have you stood here all the day idle?'

**20:7** They said to him, 'Because no one hired us.' He said to them, 'You also go into the vineyard.'

**20:8** Now when evening had come, the master of the vineyard said to his steward, 'Call the laborers and pay them their wages, beginning from the last to the first.'

**20:9** And when those hired about the eleventh hour came, each received a denarius.

**20:10** And when the first came, they supposed that they would receive more; yet they also each received a denarius.

**20:11** And when they had received it, they murmured against the master of the house,

**20:12** saying, 'These last worked only one hour, and you have made them equal to us who have borne the burden of the day and the scorching heat.'

**20:13** But answering one of them, he said, 'Friend, I am not wronging you. Did you not agree with me for a denarius?

**20:14** Take what belongs to you and go your way. I choose to give to this last man the same as to you.

**20:15** Or is it not lawful for me to do what I wish with what is mine? Or is your eye evil because I am good?'

**20:16** Thus the last shall be first, and the first last."

**20:17** And as Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, He took the twelve disciples aside privately, and on the road He said to them,

**20:18** "Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man shall be delivered over to the chief priests and scribes, and they shall condemn Him to death,

**20:19** and they shall deliver Him over to the Gentiles to mock and to scourge and to crucify; and on the third day He shall rise again."

**20:20** Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came to Him with her sons, kneeling down before Him and asking something from Him.

**20:21** And He said to her, "What do you desire?" She said to Him, "Command that these two sons of mine may sit, one at Your right hand and one at Your left, in Your kingdom."

**20:22** But Jesus answered and said, "You do not know what you ask. Are you able to drink the cup that I am about to drink?" They said to Him, "We are able."

**20:23** He said to them, "My cup indeed you shall drink; yet to sit at My right hand and at the left is not Mine to grant, but it belongs to those for whom it has been prepared by My Father."

**20:24** And when the ten heard it, they were indignant concerning the two brothers.

**20:25** But Jesus summoned them to Himself and said, "You know that the rulers of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them, and the great ones wield authority over them.

**20:26** It shall not be so among you; rather, whoever desires to become great among you shall be your servant,

**20:27** and whoever desires to be first among you shall be your slave—

**20:28** even as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many."

**20:29** And as they were going out from Jericho, a great multitude followed Him.

**20:30** And behold, two blind men sitting beside the road, when they heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out, saying, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!"

**20:31** And the multitude rebuked them that they should be silent; yet they cried out all the more, saying, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!"

**20:32** And Jesus stood still, called them, and said, "What do you desire Me to do for you?"

**20:33** They said to Him, "Lord, that our eyes may be opened."

**20:34** And moved with compassion, Jesus touched their eyes; and immediately they regained their sight and followed Him.

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — A denarius was a standard Roman silver coin equivalent to a day's wage for a common laborer.
- **20:15** — "Is your eye evil" renders a Semitic idiom expressing envy, jealousy, or a begrudging spirit toward another's generosity.
- **20:16** — The earliest Greek manuscripts end the verse here; later manuscripts add "for many are called, but few are chosen," harmonizing with Matthew 22:14.
- **20:22** — The earliest manuscripts do not include the reference to being baptized with Jesus's baptism in verses 22-23, which later witnesses imported from Mark 10:38-39.
