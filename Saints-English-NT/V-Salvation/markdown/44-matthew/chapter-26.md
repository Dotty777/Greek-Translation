## Text

**26:1** And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished all these words, He said to His disciples,

**26:2** "You know that after two days the Passover comes, and the Son of Man is delivered up to be crucified."

**26:3** Then the chief priests and the elders of the people gathered together in the courtyard of the high priest, who was called Caiaphas,

**26:4** and took counsel together that they might seize Jesus by deceit and put Him to death.

**26:5** But they were saying, "Not during the feast, lest an uproar arise among the people."

**26:6** Now when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,

**26:7** a woman came to Him holding an alabaster flask of very precious ointment, and she poured it upon His head as He reclined at table.

**26:8** But when the disciples saw it, they were indignant, saying, "For what purpose is this waste?

**26:9** For this could have been sold for a great sum and given to the poor."

**26:10** But Jesus, perceiving this, said to them, "Why do you trouble the woman? For she has done a noble work for Me.

**26:11** For the poor you always have with you, but Me you do not always have.

**26:12** For in pouring this ointment upon My body, she did so to prepare Me for burial.

**26:13** Assuredly, I say to you, wherever this gospel is proclaimed throughout the whole world, what she has done shall also be spoken of as a memorial for her."

**26:14** Then one of the twelve, the one called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests

**26:15** and said, "What are you willing to give me, and I will deliver Him to you?" And they weighed out to him thirty pieces of silver.

**26:16** And from that time on he kept seeking an opportunity to betray Him.

**26:17** Now on the first day of Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying, "Where do You wish us to prepare for You to eat the Passover?"

**26:18** And He said, "Go into the city to a certain man, and say to him, 'The Teacher says: My time is near; at your house I will keep the Passover with My disciples.'"

**26:19** And the disciples did just as Jesus had instructed them, and they made ready the Passover.

**26:20** Now when evening had come, He reclined at table with the twelve.

**26:21** And while they were eating, He said, "Assuredly, I say to you that one of you shall betray Me."

**26:22** And deeply grieved, each one of them began to say to Him, "Surely it is not I, Lord?"

**26:23** And He answered and said, "The one who has dipped his hand with Me in the dish, that one shall betray Me.

**26:24** The Son of Man indeed goes away just as it is written concerning Him, yet woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born."

**26:25** Then Judas, who was betraying Him, answered and said, "Surely it is not I, Rabbi?" He said to him, "You have said so."

**26:26** Now while they were eating, Jesus took bread, and after blessing it, He broke it and gave it to the disciples, saying, "Take, eat; this is My body."

**26:27** And taking a cup, and having given thanks, He gave it to them, saying, "Drink from it, all of you,

**26:28** for this is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out on behalf of many for the forgiveness of sins.

**26:29** Yet I say to you, from now on I shall by no means drink of this fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new with you in the kingdom of My Father."

**26:30** And after singing a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**26:31** Then Jesus said to them, "All of you shall stumble on account of Me this night, for it is written: 'I will strike the Shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered.'

**26:32** Yet after I have been raised, I will go before you into Galilee."

**26:33** But Peter answered and said to Him, "Though all stumble on account of You, I will never stumble."

**26:34** Jesus said to him, "Assuredly, I say to you that during this night, before a rooster crows, you shall deny Me three times."

**26:35** Peter said to Him, "Even if I must die with You, I will by no means deny You." And all the disciples spoke likewise.

**26:36** Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and He said to the disciples, "Sit here while I go over there and pray."

**26:37** And taking with Him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, He began to be grieved and in deep anguish.

**26:38** Then He said to them, "My soul is deeply grieved, even to death; remain here and keep watch with Me."

**26:39** And advancing a little farther, He fell upon His face, praying and saying, "My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass away from Me; yet not as I will, but as You will."

**26:40** And He came to the disciples and found them sleeping, and He said to Peter, "Were you not able then to keep watch with Me for one hour?

**26:41** Keep watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is eager, yet the flesh is weak."

**26:42** Again, a second time, He went away and prayed, saying, "My Father, if this cannot pass away unless I drink it, let Your will be done."

**26:43** And coming again, He found them sleeping, for their eyes were weighed down.

**26:44** And leaving them again, He went away and prayed a third time, speaking the same word once more.

**26:45** Then He came to the disciples and said to them, "Are you still sleeping and taking your rest? Behold, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is delivered up into the hands of sinners.

**26:46** Arise, let us go! Behold, the one who betrays Me has drawn near."

**26:47** And while He was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs from the chief priests and elders of the people.

**26:48** Now the one betraying Him had given them a sign, saying, "Whomever I kiss, He is the one; seize Him."

**26:49** And immediately approaching Jesus, he said, "Greetings, Rabbi!" and kissed Him.

**26:50** But Jesus said to him, "Friend, do that for which you have come." Then they came forward, laid hands upon Jesus, and seized Him.

**26:51** And behold, one of those who were with Jesus stretched out his hand, drew his sword, and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

**26:52** Then Jesus said to him, "Return your sword to its place, for all who take up the sword shall perish by the sword.

**26:53** Or do you suppose that I cannot appeal to My Father, and He will at once provide Me with more than twelve legions of angels?

**26:54** How then should the Scriptures be fulfilled, that thus it must come to pass?"

**26:55** In that hour Jesus said to the multitudes, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs, to capture Me? Day after day I sat in the temple teaching, and you did not seize Me.

**26:56** Yet all this has come to pass that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled." Then all the disciples abandoned Him and fled.

**26:57** Now those who had seized Jesus led Him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were assembled.

**26:58** But Peter followed Him from a distance as far as the courtyard of the high priest, and going inside, he sat with the officers to see the outcome.

**26:59** Now the chief priests and the whole council were seeking false testimony against Jesus so that they might put Him to death,

**26:60** yet they found none, though many false witnesses came forward. At last, however, two came forward

**26:61** and said, "This man declared, 'I am able to destroy the temple of God and to build it in three days.'"

**26:62** And the high priest arose and said to Him, "Do You make no answer? What is it that these men testify against You?"

**26:63** But Jesus remained silent. And the high priest said to Him, "I put You under oath by the living God that You tell us whether You are the Christ, the Son of God."

**26:64** Jesus said to him, "You have said so. Moreover, I say to you, from now on you shall see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of the Power, and coming upon the clouds of heaven."

**26:65** Then the high priest tore his garments, saying, "He has spoken blasphemy! What further need do we have of witnesses? Look, now you have heard the blasphemy!

**26:66** What is your judgment?" And they answered and said, "He is worthy of death."

**26:67** Then they spat in His face and struck Him with their fists, while others slapped Him,

**26:68** saying, "Prophesy to us, Christ! Who is the one who struck You?"

**26:69** Now Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. And a servant girl came up to him, saying, "You also were with Jesus the Galilean."

**26:70** But he denied it before them all, saying, "I do not know what you are saying."

**26:71** And when he had gone out to the gateway, another girl saw him and said to those who were there, "This man was with Jesus the Nazarene."

**26:72** And again he denied it with an oath, "I do not know the man!"

**26:73** And after a little while those who were standing by came up and said to Peter, "Truly you also are one of them, for indeed your speech gives you away."

**26:74** Then he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know the man!" And immediately a rooster crowed.

**26:75** And Peter remembered the word of Jesus, who had said, "Before a rooster crows, you shall deny Me three times." And going outside, he wept bitterly.

## Translator Notes

- **26:15** — The Greek verb estesan literally means "weighed out," echoing the Septuagint wording of Zechariah 11:12 and reflecting the ancient custom of weighing silver bullion or coinage.
- **26:25** — Jesus's reply "You have said so" (Greek Sy eipas) is a formal Semitic idiom of guarded affirmation that confirms the truth of Judas's question while placing its weight back upon him.
- **26:28** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads "My blood of the covenant" without "new," following the earliest manuscripts and directly echoing Exodus 24:8.
- **26:31** — Jesus cites Zechariah 13:7 with the verb rendered in the first-person singular ("I will strike"), attributing the striking of the Shepherd to God's sovereign purpose.
- **26:45** — The Greek verbs katheudete and anapauesthe have the same form in both the indicative and imperative moods, allowing either a question ("Are you still sleeping and taking your rest?") or an ironic command ("Sleep on now and take your rest").
- **26:50** — Jesus's elliptical words (Greek eph' ho parei, literally "for which you are here") are punctuated as a statement in the Saints Greek Critical Text, implying "do that for which you have come," though some read the clause as a question ("Why have you come?").
- **26:64** — "The Power" (Greek tes dynameos) is a reverent Jewish circumlocution for God; Jesus weaves together Psalm 110:1 and Daniel 7:13 to declare His messianic vindication.
