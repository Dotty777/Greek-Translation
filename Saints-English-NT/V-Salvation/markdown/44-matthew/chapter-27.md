## Text

**27:1** Now when morning had arrived, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel together against Jesus so that they might put Him to death.

**27:2** And having bound Him, they led Him away and delivered Him over to Pilate the governor.

**27:3** Then Judas, the one who had betrayed Him, seeing that He was condemned, was seized with remorse and returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,

**27:4** saying, "I have sinned in betraying innocent blood." And they said, "What is that to us? You see to it!"

**27:5** And casting down the pieces of silver into the sanctuary, he departed; and going away, he hanged himself.

**27:6** Now the chief priests, taking the pieces of silver, said, "It is not lawful to cast them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood."

**27:7** And having taken counsel together, they purchased with them the Potter's Field as a burial place for strangers.

**27:8** Therefore that field has been called the Field of Blood to this day.

**27:9** Then was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying, "And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of Him who was valued, on whom some of the sons of Israel set a price,

**27:10** and they gave them for the potter's field, just as the Lord directed me."

**27:11** Now Jesus stood before the governor; and the governor questioned Him, saying, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And Jesus said to him, "You say so."

**27:12** And as He was being accused by the chief priests and elders, He made no answer.

**27:13** Then Pilate said to Him, "Do You not hear how many things they witness against You?"

**27:14** Yet He gave him no answer, not even to a single word, so that the governor marveled exceedingly.

**27:15** Now at each feast the governor was accustomed to release to the multitude one prisoner whom they wished.

**27:16** And at that time they held a notorious prisoner named Barabbas.

**27:17** Therefore, when they had gathered together, Pilate said to them, "Whom do you wish that I release to you—Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?"

**27:18** For he knew that out of envy they had delivered Him up.

**27:19** Now while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent word to him, saying, "Have nothing to do with that righteous Man, for I have suffered many things this day in a dream on account of Him."

**27:20** But the chief priests and the elders convinced the multitudes that they should demand Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.

**27:21** Then the governor answered and said to them, "Which of the two do you wish me to release to you?" And they said, "Barabbas."

**27:22** Pilate said to them, "Then what shall I do with Jesus, the one called Christ?" All of them said, "Let Him be crucified!"

**27:23** And he replied, "Why, what evil has He done?" Yet they kept crying out all the more, saying, "Let Him be crucified!"

**27:24** Now when Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing, but rather that an uproar was rising, he took water and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, "I am innocent of this blood; you yourselves see to it."

**27:25** And in answer, all the people said, "His blood be upon us and upon our children!"

**27:26** Then he released Barabbas to them; and having scourged Jesus, he delivered Him over to be crucified.

**27:27** Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the Praetorium and assembled the whole cohort around Him.

**27:28** And having stripped Him, they put a scarlet cloak around Him,

**27:29** and having woven a crown of thorns, they placed it upon His head, and a reed in His right hand; and kneeling before Him, they mocked Him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**27:30** And spitting upon Him, they took the reed and kept striking Him upon the head.

**27:31** And when they had mocked Him, they stripped Him of the cloak, clothed Him with His own garments, and led Him away to crucify Him.

**27:32** Now as they were going out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name; him they pressed into service to bear His cross.

**27:33** And having come to a place called Golgotha, which is called Place of a Skull,

**27:34** they gave Him wine mingled with gall to drink; yet when He had tasted it, He was unwilling to drink.

**27:35** And having crucified Him, they divided His garments among themselves by casting lots,

**27:36** and sitting down, they kept guard over Him there.

**27:37** And they placed above His head the written charge against Him: "This is Jesus, the King of the Jews."

**27:38** At that time two robbers were crucified along with Him, one on the right hand and one on the left.

**27:39** And those who were passing by reviled Him, shaking their heads

**27:40** and saying, "You who tear down the sanctuary and rebuild it in three days, save Yourself! If You are the Son of God, come down from the cross."

**27:41** Likewise the chief priests also, mocking along with the scribes and elders, said,

**27:42** "He saved others, yet He cannot save Himself! He is the King of Israel; let Him now come down from the cross, and we will believe in Him.

**27:43** He has placed His trust in God; let Him rescue Him now if He delights in Him, for He said, 'I am the Son of God.'"

**27:44** And in the same manner the robbers also, who were crucified with Him, reproached Him.

**27:45** Now from the sixth hour, darkness fell upon all the land until the ninth hour.

**27:46** And about the ninth hour, Jesus cried aloud with a great voice, saying, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?"—which means, "My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?"

**27:47** Now certain ones of those who were standing there, hearing this, were saying, "This Man is calling Elijah."

**27:48** And at once one of them ran, and taking a sponge and filling it with sour wine, he placed it upon a reed and gave it to Him to drink.

**27:49** But the others said, "Wait, let us see whether Elijah comes to save Him."

**27:50** And Jesus, crying out once more with a great voice, yielded up His spirit.

**27:51** And behold, the veil of the sanctuary was torn from top to bottom in two; and the earth shook, and the rocks were split apart,

**27:52** and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised up,

**27:53** and coming forth from the tombs after His resurrection, they entered into the holy city and appeared to many.

**27:54** Now the centurion and those with him who were guarding Jesus, upon seeing the earthquake and the things that had happened, feared exceedingly, saying, "Truly this Man was the Son of God!"

**27:55** And many women were there, looking on from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering to Him;

**27:56** among whom were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.

**27:57** And when evening arrived, a wealthy man from Arimathea came, Joseph by name, who had himself also become a disciple of Jesus.

**27:58** This man approached Pilate and requested the body of Jesus. Then Pilate ordered it to be handed over.

**27:59** And taking the body, Joseph wrapped it in a clean linen shroud,

**27:60** and placed it in his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock; and having rolled a great stone to the doorway of the tomb, he departed.

**27:61** And Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, seated facing the tomb.

**27:62** Now on the next day, which is the day after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered together before Pilate,

**27:63** saying, "Sir, we have remembered how that deceiver said while He was still alive, 'After three days I will rise.'

**27:64** Therefore command that the tomb be made secure until the third day, lest His disciples come and steal Him away and say to the people, 'He has been raised from the dead,' and the last deception will be worse than the first."

**27:65** Then Pilate said to them, "You have a guard; go, make it secure as you know how."

**27:66** So they went and made the tomb secure with the guard, sealing the stone.

## Translator Notes

- **27:6** — The Greek word korbonan is an Aramaic loanword denoting the sacred temple treasury where consecrated offerings were kept.
- **27:9** — Matthew attributes this composite citation to Jeremiah, weaving language from Zechariah 11:12–13 together with potter and field imagery from Jeremiah (such as Jeremiah 18–19 and 32:6–15).
- **27:24** — The critical Greek text reads simply "of this blood," without the clarifying phrase "of this righteous Man" found in later manuscripts.
- **27:32** — The Greek verb translated "pressed into service" (angareuo) is a technical term for Roman military requisitioning of civilians to bear loads (compare Matthew 5:41).
- **27:35** — The critical Greek text ends after "by casting lots," omitting the explicit quotation of Psalm 22:18 found in later manuscripts (compare John 19:24).
- **27:46** — The critical Greek text reads the Aramaic form "Eloi" (matching Mark 15:34) rather than the Hebrew "Eli" found in other manuscripts, quoting Psalm 22:1.
- **27:54** — In Greek, the phrase "Son of God" (theou huios) lacks the definite article; while Matthew's Gospel points to the definite confession "the Son of God," the centurion's words can also be read qualitatively as "a son of God."
- **27:65** — The Greek verb echete can be read either as an indicative ("You have a guard") or as an imperative ("Take a guard").
