## Text

**19:1** And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these words, that He departed from Galilee and came to the borders of Judea beyond the Jordan.

**19:2** And large multitudes followed Him, and there He healed them.

**19:3** And Pharisees came to Him, testing Him and saying, "Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any cause?"

**19:4** And He answered and said, "Have you not read that the One who made them from the beginning made them male and female,

**19:5** and said, 'For this cause a man shall leave his father and his mother and be united to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh'?

**19:6** Thus they are no longer two, but one flesh. What God has joined together, therefore, let no man separate."

**19:7** They said to Him, "Why then did Moses command to give a certificate of divorce and to send her away?"

**19:8** He said to them, "Moses, because of the hardness of your hearts, allowed you to divorce your wives, but from the beginning it has not been so.

**19:9** Now I say to you that whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another commits adultery; and the one who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."

**19:10** The disciples said to Him, "If such is the case of a man with his wife, it is not profitable to marry."

**19:11** But He said to them, "Not all can receive this word, but only those to whom it has been given.

**19:12** For there are eunuchs who were born so from their mother's womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by men, and there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. Let the one who is able to receive this receive it."

**19:13** Then little children were brought to Him so that He might lay His hands upon them and pray, but the disciples rebuked them.

**19:14** But Jesus said, "Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them, for to such as these belongs the kingdom of heaven."

**19:15** And after laying His hands upon them, He departed from there.

**19:16** And behold, one came to Him and said, "Teacher, what good thing shall I do that I may have eternal life?"

**19:17** And He said to him, "Why do you ask Me concerning what is good? There is One who is good. Yet if you desire to enter into life, keep the commandments."

**19:18** He said to Him, "Which ones?" And Jesus said, "These: 'You shall not murder,' 'You shall not commit adultery,' 'You shall not steal,' 'You shall not bear false witness,'

**19:19** 'Honor your father and your mother,' and 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'"

**19:20** The young man said to Him, "All these things I have kept; what do I still lack?"

**19:21** Jesus said to him, "If you desire to be perfect, go, sell your possessions and give to the poor, and you shall have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me."

**19:22** But when the young man heard that word, he departed sorrowful, for he had many possessions.

**19:23** Then Jesus said to His disciples, "Assuredly, I say to you that only with difficulty shall a rich man enter the kingdom of heaven.

**19:24** And again I say to you, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God."

**19:25** When the disciples heard this, they were exceedingly astonished, saying, "Who then can be saved?"

**19:26** But Jesus looked upon them and said to them, "With men this is impossible, yet with God all things are possible."

**19:27** Then Peter answered and said to Him, "Behold, we have forsaken all things and followed You. What then shall there be for us?"

**19:28** And Jesus said to them, "Assuredly, I say to you that in the regeneration, when the Son of Man sits upon the throne of His glory, you who have followed Me shall also sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

**19:29** And everyone who has left houses, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields, for the sake of My name, shall receive a hundredfold and shall inherit eternal life.

**19:30** Yet many who are first shall be last, and the last first."

## Translator Notes

- **19:9** — The final clause ("and the one who marries a divorced woman commits adultery") is present in the Greek text translated here (supported by Codex Vaticanus, Codex Ephraemi, and the Majority Text), though omitted in some witnesses such as Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Bezae.
- **19:17** — The Greek text reads "Why do you ask Me concerning what is good? There is One who is good" (alongside "Teacher" rather than "Good Teacher" in 19:16), reflecting the earliest Matthean manuscripts; later manuscripts harmonize the wording with Mark 10:17-18 and Luke 18:18-19.
- **19:28** — "Regeneration" translates the Greek palingenesia, signifying the eschatological renewal and restoration of all creation at the coming of the Son of Man in glory.
- **19:29** — The Greek text translated here retains both "or wife" and "a hundredfold," whereas some early manuscripts omit "or wife" or read "manifold" (pollaplasiona).
