## Text

**3:1** Now in those days John the Baptist appeared, preaching in the wilderness of Judea,

**3:2** saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near!"

**3:3** For this is he who was spoken of through Isaiah the prophet, saying, "The voice of one crying out in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight His paths.'"

**3:4** Now John himself wore a garment of camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was locusts and wild honey.

**3:5** Then Jerusalem and all Judea and all the region around the Jordan were going out to him,

**3:6** and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.

**3:7** But seeing many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, "Offspring of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

**3:8** Therefore bring forth fruit worthy of repentance,

**3:9** and do not presume to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father'; for I tell you that God is able from these stones to raise up children to Abraham.

**3:10** Even now the ax is already laid at the root of the trees; therefore every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire.

**3:11** I indeed baptize you with water for repentance, yet He who comes after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry; He Himself shall baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

**3:12** His winnowing fork is in His hand, and He shall thoroughly cleanse His threshing floor and gather His wheat into the barn, but the chaff He shall burn up with unquenchable fire."

**3:13** Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.

**3:14** But he tried to prevent Him, saying, "I have need to be baptized by You, and do You come to me?"

**3:15** Yet Jesus answered and said to him, "Permit it at this time, for in this way it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness." Then he permitted Him.

**3:16** And having been baptized, Jesus immediately came up from the water; and behold, the heavens were opened, and He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, coming upon Him.

**3:17** And behold, a voice came from the heavens, saying, "This is My beloved Son, in whom I take great delight."

## Translator Notes

- **3:11** — The Greek preposition en may be rendered "with" or "in"; baptism "with the Holy Spirit and fire" denotes both purifying renewal and eschatological judgment.
- **3:15** — "Fulfill all righteousness" refers to Jesus' complete obedience to God's redemptive will and His solidarity with those He came to save.
- **3:16** — The Greek critical text reads "the heavens were opened" without the words "to Him" found in later manuscripts.
