## Text

**12:1** At that time Jesus passed through the grainfields on the Sabbath; and His disciples became hungry and began to pluck heads of grain and eat.

**12:2** When the Pharisees saw this, they said to Him, "Behold, Your disciples are doing what it is not lawful to do on the Sabbath."

**12:3** And He said to them, "Have you not read what David did when he became hungry, and those who were with him,

**12:4** how he entered into the house of God, and they ate the showbread, which it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those with him, but for the priests alone?

**12:5** Or have you not read in the Law that on the Sabbath days the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath and are guiltless?

**12:6** Yet I say to you, something greater than the temple is in this place.

**12:7** And if you had understood what it means, 'I desire mercy, and not sacrifice,' you would not have condemned the guiltless.

**12:8** For the Son of Man is the Lord of the Sabbath."

**12:9** And departing from that place, He came into their synagogue.

**12:10** And behold, a man was there who had a withered hand. And they questioned Him, saying, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?" so that they might accuse Him.

**12:11** And He said to them, "What man among you who owns one sheep, if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not take hold of it and lift it up?

**12:12** How much greater in worth, then, is a man than a sheep! Thus it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath."

**12:13** Then He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." So he stretched it out, and it was restored sound, like the other.

**12:14** But when the Pharisees went out, they took counsel against Him as to how they might destroy Him.

**12:15** Yet Jesus, knowing this, withdrew from that place; and many followed Him, and He healed them all,

**12:16** and He strictly charged them that they should not make Him known,

**12:17** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying:

**12:18** "Behold, My Servant whom I have chosen, My Beloved in whom My soul delights; I will place My Spirit upon Him, and He shall proclaim justice to the Gentiles.

**12:19** He will not strive nor cry aloud, nor shall anyone hear His voice in the streets.

**12:20** A bruised reed He shall not break, and a smoldering wick He shall not quench, until He brings justice forth to victory;

**12:21** and in His name the Gentiles shall hope."

**12:22** Then a demon-possessed man who was blind and mute was brought to Him; and He healed him, so that the mute man spoke and saw.

**12:23** And all the multitudes were astonished and were saying, "Can this be the Son of David?"

**12:24** But when the Pharisees heard this, they said, "This man does not cast out demons except by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

**12:25** And knowing their thoughts, He said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.

**12:26** And if Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself; how, then, shall his kingdom stand?

**12:27** And if I by Beelzebul cast out demons, by whom do your sons cast them out? For this reason they themselves shall be your judges.

**12:28** Yet if I cast out demons by the Spirit of God, then indeed the kingdom of God has come upon you.

**12:29** Or how can anyone enter into the house of the strong man and seize his property, unless he first binds the strong man? And then he shall plunder his house.

**12:30** The one who is not with Me is against Me, and the one who does not gather with Me scatters.

**12:31** For this reason I say to you, every sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven men, yet the blasphemy against the Spirit shall not be forgiven.

**12:32** And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it shall be forgiven him; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, either in this age or in the coming one.

**12:33** Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree bad and its fruit bad; for by its fruit the tree is known.

**12:34** Offspring of vipers! How are you able to speak good things, being evil? For from the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.

**12:35** The good man from the good treasury brings forth good things, and the evil man from the evil treasury brings forth evil things.

**12:36** Yet I say to you that for every idle word which men shall speak, they shall render an account concerning it on the day of judgment;

**12:37** for from your words you shall be justified, and from your words you shall be condemned."

**12:38** Then certain of the scribes and Pharisees answered Him, saying, "Teacher, we wish to see a sign from You."

**12:39** But answering, He said to them, "A wicked and adulterous generation demands a sign, yet no sign shall be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.

**12:40** For just as Jonah was in the belly of the great fish three days and three nights, so shall the Son of Man be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.

**12:41** The men of Nineveh shall stand up in the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and behold, something greater than Jonah is here.

**12:42** The queen of the South shall rise up in the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, something greater than Solomon is here.

**12:43** Now when the unclean spirit goes out from a man, it passes through waterless places seeking rest, and does not find any.

**12:44** Then it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came out.' And having come, it finds it unoccupied, swept, and put in order.

**12:45** Then it goes and takes along with itself seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and entering in, they dwell there; and the final state of that man becomes worse than the first. Even so shall it be with this wicked generation."

**12:46** While He was still speaking to the multitudes, behold, His mother and brothers were standing outside, seeking to speak to Him.

**12:47** And someone said to Him, "Behold, Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, seeking to speak to You."

**12:48** But answering, He said to the one who spoke to Him, "Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?"

**12:49** And stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, "Behold, My mother and My brothers!

**12:50** For whoever does the will of My Father who is in heaven, that one is My brother and sister and mother."

## Translator Notes

- **12:4** — The Greek critical text reads the plural "they ate" (referring to David and his companions), whereas later manuscripts assimilate to the singular "he ate." "The showbread" renders the Greek expression "the loaves of presentation" set before God in the sanctuary (Leviticus 24:5-9).
- **12:6** — "Something greater" translates a neuter Greek adjective, pointing to the messianic kingdom and divine presence embodied in Jesus Himself (compare the parallel neuter expressions in verses 41 and 42).
- **12:24** — "Beelzebul" follows the Greek text as the title of the ruler of the demons, rather than the Latin-influenced spelling "Beelzebub."
- **12:40** — "The great fish" renders the Greek word ketos (also used in the Septuagint of Jonah 2:1), which denotes a massive sea creature or sea monster.
- **12:47** — A few early Greek manuscripts omit this verse, likely due to a scribal eye-skip from "to speak" at the end of verse 46 to the same verb at the end of verse 47, though it is strongly attested across the manuscript tradition.
