## Text

**10:1** And having summoned His twelve disciples to Himself, He granted them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every infirmity.

**10:2** Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; and James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother;

**10:3** Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;

**10:4** Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed Him.

**10:5** These twelve Jesus sent forth, charging them and saying, "Do not depart into the way of the Gentiles, and do not enter a city of the Samaritans;

**10:6** but go instead to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

**10:7** And as you go, proclaim, saying, 'The kingdom of heaven has drawn near.'

**10:8** Heal those who are sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. Without cost you have received; without cost give.

**10:9** Acquire neither gold, nor silver, nor copper for your belts,

**10:10** nor a provision bag for the journey, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the laborer is worthy of his sustenance.

**10:11** Now whatever city or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and abide there until you depart.

**10:12** And as you enter the house, greet it.

**10:13** And if indeed the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; yet if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.

**10:14** And whoever will not receive you nor listen to your words, as you go forth out of that house or city, shake off the dust from your feet.

**10:15** Assuredly, I say to you, it shall be more bearable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah in the day of judgment than for that city.

**10:16** Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves; therefore be prudent as serpents and guileless as doves.

**10:17** Yet beware of men, for they shall deliver you up to councils and scourge you in their synagogues.

**10:18** And you shall even be brought before governors and kings for My sake, as a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.

**10:19** Yet when they deliver you up, do not be anxious about how or what you shall speak; for it shall be given to you in that hour what you shall speak.

**10:20** For it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you.

**10:21** Now brother shall deliver up brother to death, and a father his child; and children shall rise up against parents and put them to death.

**10:22** And you shall be hated by all on account of My name; yet he who endures to the end, this one shall be saved.

**10:23** Now when they persecute you in this city, flee to the next; for assuredly, I say to you, you shall by no means finish going through the cities of Israel until the Son of Man comes.

**10:24** A disciple is not above the teacher, nor is a bondservant above his master.

**10:25** It is sufficient for the disciple that he become like his teacher, and the bondservant like his master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household!

**10:26** Therefore do not fear them; for nothing is veiled that shall not be revealed, and nothing hidden that shall not be made known.

**10:27** What I speak to you in the darkness, declare in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim upon the housetops.

**10:28** And do not fear those who slay the body yet are unable to slay the soul; rather, fear Him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

**10:29** Are not two sparrows sold for a small copper coin? Yet not one of them shall fall to the ground apart from your Father.

**10:30** And as for you, even the hairs of your head are all numbered.

**10:31** Do not fear, therefore; you are of greater worth than many sparrows.

**10:32** Everyone therefore who shall confess Me before men, him I also shall confess before My Father who is in heaven;

**10:33** yet whoever denies Me before men, him I also shall deny before My Father who is in heaven.

**10:34** Do not suppose that I came to cast peace upon the earth; I did not come to cast peace, but a sword.

**10:35** For I came to divide a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

**10:36** and a man's enemies shall be those of his own household.

**10:37** The one who loves father or mother more than Me is not worthy of Me; and the one who loves son or daughter more than Me is not worthy of Me.

**10:38** And he who does not take up his cross and follow after Me is not worthy of Me.

**10:39** The one who finds his life shall lose it, and the one who loses his life for My sake shall find it.

**10:40** He who welcomes you welcomes Me, and he who welcomes Me welcomes Him who sent Me.

**10:41** He who welcomes a prophet in the name of a prophet shall receive a prophet's reward, and he who welcomes a righteous man in the name of a righteous man shall receive a righteous man's reward.

**10:42** And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, assuredly, I say to you, he shall by no means lose his reward."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — "Cananaean" transliterates Greek Kananaios (from Aramaic qan'an, meaning "Zealot"), identifying Simon's zealot affiliation rather than Canaanite ancestry.
- **10:25** — "Beelzebul" follows the Greek critical text (ending in -l rather than -b), a name applied in Jewish usage to the prince of demons.
- **10:28** — "hell" translates Greek geenna (Gehenna), referring to the Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem as the emblem of final judgment.
- **10:29** — "a small copper coin" translates Greek assarion, a Roman bronze coin valued at one-sixteenth of a denarius.
