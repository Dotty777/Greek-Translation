## Text

**15:1** Then Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem came to Jesus, saying,

**15:2** "Why do Your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands whenever they eat bread."

**15:3** And He answered and said to them, "Why do you also transgress the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition?

**15:4** For God commanded, saying, 'Honor your father and your mother,' and, 'He who reviles father or mother, let him surely die.'

**15:5** Yet you say, 'Whoever says to his father or his mother, "Whatever you might have gained from me is a gift devoted to God,"

**15:6** he need not honor his father.' So you have made void the word of God for the sake of your tradition.

**15:7** Hypocrites! Rightly did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying,

**15:8** 'This people honors Me with their lips, yet their heart is far removed from Me;

**15:9** and in vain do they worship Me, teaching as doctrines the precepts of men.'"

**15:10** And having summoned the multitude to Himself, He said to them, "Hear and understand:

**15:11** it is not what enters into the mouth that defiles a man, but what proceeds out of the mouth—this defiles a man."

**15:12** Then the disciples came to Him and said, "Do You know that the Pharisees took offense when they heard this word?"

**15:13** And He answered and said, "Every plant that My heavenly Father has not planted shall be uprooted.

**15:14** Leave them alone; they are blind guides. And if a blind man leads a blind man, both shall fall into a pit."

**15:15** Then Peter answered and said to Him, "Explain the parable to us."

**15:16** And He said, "Are you also still without understanding?

**15:17** Do you not yet perceive that whatever enters the mouth passes into the stomach and is cast out into the latrine?

**15:18** Yet the things that proceed from the mouth come forth out of the heart, and those defile a man.

**15:19** For out of the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.

**15:20** These are the things that defile a man, but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a man."

**15:21** And departing from there, Jesus withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon.

**15:22** And behold, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders and cried out, saying, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is grievously afflicted by a demon."

**15:23** Yet He did not answer her a word. And His disciples approached and urged Him, saying, "Send her away, for she keeps crying out after us."

**15:24** And He answered and said, "I was not sent to any except the lost sheep of the house of Israel."

**15:25** Yet she approached and worshiped Him, saying, "Lord, help me!"

**15:26** And He answered and said, "It is not right to take the children's bread and cast it to the little dogs."

**15:27** And she said, "Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs eat of the crumbs that fall from their masters' table."

**15:28** Then Jesus answered and said to her, "O woman, great is your faith! Let it be done for you as you desire." And her daughter was healed from that very hour.

**15:29** And moving on from there, Jesus came beside the Sea of Galilee, and going up onto the mountain, He sat down there.

**15:30** And great multitudes came to Him, bringing with them the mute, the blind, the lame, the maimed, and many others; and they laid them at His feet, and He healed them,

**15:31** so that the multitude marveled when they saw the mute speaking, the maimed made whole, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.

**15:32** Then Jesus summoned His disciples to Himself and said, "I have compassion upon the multitude, because they have remained with Me now three days and have nothing to eat; and I do not wish to send them away fasting, lest they faint along the way."

**15:33** And the disciples said to Him, "Where could we obtain enough loaves in a desolate place to satisfy so great a multitude?"

**15:34** And Jesus said to them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

**15:35** And having directed the multitude to recline upon the ground,

**15:36** He took the seven loaves and the fish, and having given thanks, He broke them and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes.

**15:37** And all ate and were satisfied; and they gathered up seven large baskets full of the fragments that remained.

**15:38** Now those who ate were four thousand men, besides women and little children.

**15:39** And having dismissed the multitudes, He embarked in the boat and came into the region of Magadan.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — Greek doron ("gift") refers to a vow dedicating property or support to God (called Corban in Mark 7:11), which Pharisaic tradition treated as releasing a person from the obligation to provide for parents.
- **15:14** — Following the Greek critical text, Jesus calls the Pharisees "blind guides" without the addition "of the blind" found in later manuscripts.
- **15:39** — The Greek critical text reads "Magadan," a locality on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee, where later manuscripts read "Magdala."
