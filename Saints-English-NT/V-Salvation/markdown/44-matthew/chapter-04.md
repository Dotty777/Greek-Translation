## Text

**4:1** Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit, to be tempted by the devil.

**4:2** And having fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward He became hungry.

**4:3** And drawing near, the tempter said to Him, "If You are the Son of God, command that these stones become loaves of bread."

**4:4** But He answered and said, "It is written, 'Not by bread alone shall man live, but by every word going forth through the mouth of God.'"

**4:5** Then the devil took Him into the holy city, and placed Him upon the pinnacle of the temple,

**4:6** and said to Him, "If You are the Son of God, cast Yourself down; for it is written, 'He shall command His angels concerning You, and upon their hands they shall lift You up, lest You strike Your foot against a stone.'"

**4:7** Jesus said to him, "Again it is written, 'You shall not test the Lord your God.'"

**4:8** Again the devil took Him to an exceedingly high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory,

**4:9** and said to Him, "I will give all these things to You, if You fall down and worship me."

**4:10** Then Jesus said to him, "Depart, Satan! For it is written, 'You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him alone you shall serve.'"

**4:11** Then the devil left Him, and behold, angels drew near and ministered to Him.

**4:12** Now when He heard that John had been delivered up, He withdrew into Galilee.

**4:13** And leaving Nazareth, He came and dwelt in Capernaum by the sea, within the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali,

**4:14** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

**4:15** "Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, along the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,

**4:16** the people sitting in darkness have seen a great light, and to those sitting in the region and shadow of death, light has dawned upon them."

**4:17** From that time Jesus began to preach and to say, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near."

**4:18** Now as He walked beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon who is called Peter and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.

**4:19** And He said to them, "Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of men."

**4:20** And immediately leaving their nets, they followed Him.

**4:21** And proceeding from there, He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with their father Zebedee, mending their nets; and He called them.

**4:22** And immediately leaving the boat and their father, they followed Him.

**4:23** And He went about throughout all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every infirmity among the people.

**4:24** And the report of Him went forth into all Syria; and they brought to Him all who were sick, afflicted with various diseases and torments, those possessed by demons, and epileptics, and paralytics; and He healed them.

**4:25** And great multitudes followed Him from Galilee and Decapolis, and Jerusalem and Judea, and beyond the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **4:10** — The critical Greek text reads simply "Depart, Satan" (Hypage, Satana); later manuscripts add "behind Me" (opiso mou), likely assimilated from Matthew 16:23.
- **4:12** — The Greek verb paredothe ("was delivered up" or "handed over") refers to John's arrest and imprisonment, using the same term Matthew later applies to the handing over of Jesus. Early Greek manuscripts also omit "Jesus" as an explicit subject here, as well as in 4:18 and 4:23.
- **4:13** — The Greek text spells the town Nazara, an early Greek form of Nazareth.
- **4:24** — The Greek participle seleniazomenous ("epileptics") literally means "moon-struck," an ancient term for epilepsy and convulsive seizures.
