## Text

**22:1** And answering, Jesus spoke to them again in parables, saying,

**22:2** "The kingdom of heaven is likened to a certain king who gave a wedding feast for his son.

**22:3** And he sent forth his servants to summon those who had been invited to the wedding feast, yet they were unwilling to come.

**22:4** Again he sent forth other servants, saying, 'Tell those who have been invited, "Behold, I have prepared my dinner; my oxen and the fattened cattle have been slain, and all things are ready. Come to the wedding feast."'

**22:5** But paying no heed, they went away, one to his own farm, and another to his trade.

**22:6** And the rest laid hold of his servants, treated them shamefully, and put them to death.

**22:7** Then the king was filled with wrath; and sending forth his armies, he destroyed those murderers and set their city on fire.

**22:8** Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding feast indeed is ready, but those who were invited were not worthy.

**22:9** Go therefore to the main thoroughfares, and as many as you may find, invite to the wedding feast.'

**22:10** So those servants went out into the roads and gathered all whom they found, both evil and good; and the wedding hall became full of guests.

**22:11** Now when the king entered to look upon the guests, he saw there a man not clothed in a wedding garment.

**22:12** And he said to him, 'Friend, how did you enter here without having a wedding garment?' And he was rendered speechless.

**22:13** Then the king said to the attendants, 'Bind his feet and hands and cast him out into the outer darkness; in that place there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.'

**22:14** For many are summoned, yet few are chosen."

**22:15** Then the Pharisees went out and took counsel together as to how they might ensnare Him in speech.

**22:16** And they sent their disciples to Him along with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know that You are truthful and teach the way of God in truth, and You defer to no one; for You do not look upon the outward appearance of men.

**22:17** Tell us, therefore, what do You think? Is it lawful to pay tribute to Caesar, or not?"

**22:18** But Jesus, knowing their malice, said, "Why are you putting Me to the test, you hypocrites?

**22:19** Show Me the coin used for the tribute." And they brought Him a denarius.

**22:20** And He said to them, "Whose is this image and inscription?"

**22:21** They said, "Caesar's." Then He said to them, "Render therefore to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God."

**22:22** And upon hearing this, they marveled; and leaving Him, they departed.

**22:23** On that day some Sadducees, who say that there is no resurrection, approached Him and questioned Him,

**22:24** saying, "Teacher, Moses declared, 'If any man dies having no children, his brother shall take his wife in marriage and raise up offspring for his brother.'

**22:25** Now there were among us seven brothers. And the first married and died, and having no offspring, he left his wife to his brother;

**22:26** in like manner also the second and the third, down to all seven.

**22:27** And last of all, the woman died.

**22:28** In the resurrection, therefore, whose wife among the seven shall she be? For all of them had her."

**22:29** But answering, Jesus said to them, "You are in error, knowing neither the Scriptures nor the power of God.

**22:30** For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, rather they are as angels in heaven.

**22:31** Yet regarding the resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,

**22:32** 'I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? He is not the God of the dead, rather of the living."

**22:33** And when the multitudes heard this, they were astounded at His teaching.

**22:34** Now when the Pharisees heard that He had silenced the Sadducees, they assembled together in one place.

**22:35** And one of them, an expert in the law, questioned Him, testing Him:

**22:36** "Teacher, which commandment is greatest in the law?"

**22:37** And He said to him, "You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with your whole mind.

**22:38** This is the greatest and foremost commandment.

**22:39** And a second is similar to it: 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'

**22:40** Upon these two commandments the whole law hangs, and the prophets."

**22:41** Now while the Pharisees were assembled together, Jesus questioned them,

**22:42** saying, "What do you think concerning the Christ? Whose son is He?" They said to Him, "David's."

**22:43** He said to them, "How then does David in the Spirit call Him Lord, saying,

**22:44** 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand, until I place Your enemies beneath Your feet"'?

**22:45** If therefore David calls Him Lord, how is He his son?"

**22:46** And no one was able to answer Him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day forward to question Him any longer.

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — The Greek phrase tas diexodous ton hodon literally denotes the outlets where city roads open out onto the main highways, rendered here as "the main thoroughfares."
- **22:13** — The Greek text reads "Bind his feet and hands and cast him out," without the later Byzantine expansion "take him away and," and uses diakonoi ("attendants") rather than douloi ("servants") as in verses 3–10.
- **22:30** — The Greek text reads "as angels in heaven," without the later addition "of God" found in some manuscripts.
- **22:44** — The Greek text reads hypokato ton podon sou ("beneath Your feet") rather than hypopodion ton podon sou ("the footstool of Your feet"), which appears in later manuscripts harmonized to Psalm 110:1 (LXX 109:1).
