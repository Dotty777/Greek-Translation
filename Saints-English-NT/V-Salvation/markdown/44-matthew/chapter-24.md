## Text

**24:1** And Jesus went out from the temple and was going on His way, and His disciples came to Him to point out to Him the buildings of the temple.

**24:2** But He answered and said to them, "Do you not see all these things? Assuredly, I say to you, not one stone shall by any means be left here upon another that shall not be cast down."

**24:3** Now as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately, saying, "Tell us, when shall these things be, and what shall be the sign of Your coming and of the consummation of the age?"

**24:4** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Take heed lest anyone lead you astray.

**24:5** For many shall come in My name, saying, 'I am the Christ,' and shall lead many astray.

**24:6** And you shall hear of wars and reports of wars; see that you are not alarmed, for this must come to pass, yet the end is not yet.

**24:7** For nation shall rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there shall be famines and earthquakes in various places.

**24:8** Now all these things are the beginning of birth pangs.

**24:9** Then they shall deliver you over to tribulation and put you to death, and you shall be hated by all the nations on account of My name.

**24:10** And then many shall fall away, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.

**24:11** And many false prophets shall arise and lead many astray.

**24:12** And because lawlessness shall be multiplied, the love of the many shall grow cold.

**24:13** Yet he who endures to the end, the same shall be saved.

**24:14** And this gospel of the kingdom shall be proclaimed throughout the whole inhabited world as a testimony to all the nations, and then the end shall come.

**24:15** Therefore, when you see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand),

**24:16** then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains;

**24:17** let him who is on the housetop not come down to take away the things out of his house;

**24:18** and let him who is in the field not turn back to take his cloak.

**24:19** And woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days!

**24:20** And pray that your flight may not be in winter nor on a Sabbath;

**24:21** for then there shall be great tribulation, such as has not come to pass from the beginning of the world until now, no, nor ever shall be.

**24:22** And if those days had not been cut short, no flesh would have been saved; yet for the sake of the elect those days shall be cut short.

**24:23** Then if anyone says to you, 'Behold, here is the Christ!' or 'Here!' do not believe it.

**24:24** For false christs and false prophets shall arise and perform great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the elect.

**24:25** Behold, I have told you beforehand.

**24:26** Therefore if they say to you, 'Behold, He is in the wilderness!' do not go forth; 'Behold, He is in the inner chambers!' do not believe it.

**24:27** For just as the lightning flashes forth from the east and shines even to the west, so shall the coming of the Son of Man be.

**24:28** Wherever the carcass is, there the vultures shall be gathered together.

**24:29** And immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give its radiance, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken.

**24:30** And then the sign of the Son of Man shall appear in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth shall mourn, and they shall behold the Son of Man coming upon the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.

**24:31** And He shall send forth His angels with a loud trumpet, and they shall gather together His elect from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other.

**24:32** Now learn the parable from the fig tree: as soon as its branch has become tender and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near.

**24:33** Even so you also, when you see all these things, know that He is near, right at the doors.

**24:34** Assuredly, I say to you, this generation shall by no means pass away until all these things have come to pass.

**24:35** Heaven and earth shall pass away, yet My words shall never pass away.

**24:36** Now concerning that day and hour no one knows, neither the angels of the heavens nor the Son, except the Father alone.

**24:37** For just as the days of Noah were, so shall the coming of the Son of Man be.

**24:38** For as they were in those days before the flood, eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ark,

**24:39** and they did not know until the flood came and swept them all away—so also shall the coming of the Son of Man be.

**24:40** Then two men shall be in the field: one is taken, and one is left;

**24:41** two women shall be grinding at the mill: one is taken, and one is left.

**24:42** Keep watch therefore, for you do not know on what day your Lord is coming.

**24:43** Now know this: if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief was coming, he would have kept watch and would not have permitted his house to be broken into.

**24:44** For this reason you also must be ready, for the Son of Man is coming at an hour that you do not expect.

**24:45** Who then is the faithful and prudent servant, whom the master has set over his household, to give them their food in due season?

**24:46** Blessed is that servant whom his master, when he comes, shall find doing so.

**24:47** Assuredly, I say to you, he shall set him over all his possessions.

**24:48** Yet if that wicked servant says in his heart, 'My master delays,'

**24:49** and begins to strike his fellow servants, and eats and drinks with the drunkards,

**24:50** the master of that servant shall come on a day when he does not expect him, and at an hour that he does not know,

**24:51** and shall cut him in two and appoint his portion with the hypocrites; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The critical Greek text reads "famines and earthquakes," without the addition "and pestilences" found in later Byzantine manuscripts (cf. Luke 21:11).
- **24:28** — The Greek word aetoi can denote either eagles or carrion-eating vultures (such as the griffon vulture); "vultures" fits the proverb's mention of a carcass.
- **24:33** — The Greek verb estin ("is") has no expressed subject and may refer either to the Son of Man ("He is near") or to His coming and the end ("it is near").
- **24:36** — The phrase "nor the Son" (oude ho huios) is strongly attested in the earliest manuscripts and preserved in the critical text, though omitted in many later manuscripts.
- **24:42** — The critical Greek text reads "what day" (poia hemera) rather than "what hour" (poia hora), which appears in later manuscripts by assimilation to verse 44.
