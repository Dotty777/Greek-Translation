## Text

**21:1** And when they drew near to Jerusalem and came to Bethphage, by the Mount of Olives, Jesus then sent forth two disciples,

**21:2** saying to them, "Go into the village across from you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied, and a colt with her; untie them and lead them to Me.

**21:3** And if anyone says anything to you, you shall say, 'The Lord has need of them,' and he will send them at once."

**21:4** Now this took place that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

**21:5** "Say to the daughter of Zion, 'Behold, your King comes to you, meek and mounted upon a donkey, and upon a colt, the foal of a beast of burden.'"

**21:6** So the disciples went and did just as Jesus had instructed them;

**21:7** they brought the donkey and the colt, placed their garments on them, and He sat upon them.

**21:8** And a very great multitude spread their own garments on the road, while others were cutting branches from the trees and spreading them in the way.

**21:9** And the multitudes going before Him and those following cried out, saying, "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!"

**21:10** And when He entered Jerusalem, the entire city was shaken, saying, "Who is this?"

**21:11** And the multitudes were saying, "This is the prophet Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee."

**21:12** And Jesus went into the temple and cast out all those selling and buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of those selling doves.

**21:13** And He said to them, "It is written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer,' yet you are making it a den of robbers."

**21:14** And the blind and the lame approached Him in the temple, and He healed them.

**21:15** But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wondrous things that He did, and the children crying out in the temple and saying, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" they were indignant,

**21:16** and they said to Him, "Do You hear what these are saying?" And Jesus said to them, "Yes. Have you never read, 'Out of the mouth of infants and nursing babes You have prepared praise'?"

**21:17** And leaving them, He went out of the city to Bethany, and lodged there.

**21:18** Now early in the morning, as He was returning to the city, He became hungry.

**21:19** And seeing a lone fig tree beside the road, He went up to it and found nothing on it except leaves only; and He said to it, "Never again shall fruit come from you forever." And the fig tree withered instantly.

**21:20** And when the disciples saw it, they marveled, saying, "How did the fig tree wither instantly?"

**21:21** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Assuredly, I say to you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and cast into the sea,' it shall be done.

**21:22** And all things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you shall receive."

**21:23** Now when He came into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people approached Him as He was teaching, saying, "By what authority are You doing these things? And who gave You this authority?"

**21:24** And Jesus answered and said to them, "I also will ask you one thing; if you tell Me that, I too will tell you by what authority I do these things:

**21:25** The baptism of John—where was it from? From heaven or from men?" And they reasoned among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will say to us, 'Why then did you not believe him?'

**21:26** But if we say, 'From men,' we fear the multitude, for all hold John to be a prophet."

**21:27** So they answered Jesus and said, "We do not know." And He Himself said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.

**21:28** But what do you think? A man had two sons; and coming to the first, he said, 'Son, go work today in the vineyard.'

**21:29** And he answered and said, 'I will not,' yet afterward he relented and went.

**21:30** Then he came to the other and spoke in the same manner; and he answered and said, 'I will, sir,' yet he did not go.

**21:31** Which of the two did the will of the father?" They said, "The first." Jesus said to them, "Assuredly, I say to you that the tax collectors and the harlots go ahead of you into the kingdom of God.

**21:32** For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, whereas the tax collectors and the harlots believed him; and when you saw it, you did not afterward relent so as to believe him.

**21:33** Hear another parable: There was a householder who planted a vineyard, and set a hedge around it, and dug a winepress in it, and built a tower; and he leased it to vinedressers and departed on a journey.

**21:34** Now when the season of the fruits drew near, he sent his servants to the vinedressers to receive his fruits.

**21:35** And the vinedressers seized his servants, and beat one, slew another, and stoned another.

**21:36** Again he sent other servants, more than the first, and they treated them in the same manner.

**21:37** At last he sent his son to them, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

**21:38** But when the vinedressers saw the son, they said among themselves, 'This is the heir. Come, let us slay him and possess his inheritance.'

**21:39** So they seized him, cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him.

**21:40** Therefore, when the master of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those vinedressers?"

**21:41** They said to Him, "He will bring those wretches to a wretched end, and will lease out the vineyard to other vinedressers who will render to him the fruits in their seasons."

**21:42** Jesus said to them, "Have you never read in the Scriptures, 'The stone that the builders rejected, this has become the head of the corner; this came from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes'?

**21:43** Therefore I say to you that the kingdom of God shall be taken from you and given to a nation bringing forth its fruits.

**21:44** And the one who falls upon this stone shall be shattered; yet on whomever it falls, it will crush him to dust."

**21:45** Now when the chief priests and the Pharisees heard His parables, they perceived that He was speaking about them.

**21:46** And although they sought to seize Him, they feared the multitudes, because they regarded Him as a prophet.

## Translator Notes

- **21:5** — Matthew combines Isaiah 62:11 with Zechariah 9:9, rendering the poetic parallelism of Zechariah literally with both a donkey and a colt, matching the two animals brought in verses 2 and 7.
- **21:16** — Jesus quotes Psalm 8:2 following the Greek Septuagint reading ("You have prepared praise") rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("You have ordained strength").
- **21:31** — Ancient manuscripts differ in the order of the two sons in verses 28–31; the Saints Greek Critical Text places the initially refusing yet obedient son first, leading to the answer "The first."
- **21:41** — The Greek uses an emphatic wordplay, kakous kakos apolesei (literally, "wicked men wickedly he will destroy"), captured here as "bring those wretches to a wretched end."
- **21:44** — Although omitted by a small number of Western manuscripts (such as Codex Bezae), this verse is strongly attested across the major textual traditions and is retained in the Saints Greek Critical Text.
