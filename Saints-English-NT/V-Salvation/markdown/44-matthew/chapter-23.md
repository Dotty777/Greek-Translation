## Text

**23:1** Then Jesus addressed the multitudes and His disciples,

**23:2** saying, "Upon the seat of Moses the scribes and the Pharisees sit.

**23:3** Therefore, all that they tell you, do and keep; yet do not act according to their works, for they speak, and do not do.

**23:4** And they bind burdens heavy and difficult to bear, and place them upon the shoulders of men; yet they themselves are unwilling to move them with their finger.

**23:5** Moreover, all their works they perform so as to be seen by men; for they widen their phylacteries and enlarge the tassels,

**23:6** and they love the place of honor at feasts and the foremost seats in the synagogues,

**23:7** and salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called 'Rabbi' by men.

**23:8** But as for you, do not be called 'Rabbi,' for One is your Instructor, and all of you are brethren.

**23:9** And do not call anyone on earth your father, for One is your heavenly Father.

**23:10** And do not let yourselves be called instructors, because your Instructor is One—the Christ.

**23:11** Yet the greatest among you shall be your servant.

**23:12** And everyone who exalts himself shall be humbled, and everyone who humbles himself shall be exalted.

**23:13** But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of heaven before men; for you do not enter yourselves, nor do you permit those who are entering to enter.

**23:15** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you traverse the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte, and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of hell as yourselves.

**23:16** Woe to you, blind guides, who say, 'Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; yet whoever swears by the gold of the temple is bound.'

**23:17** You foolish and blind! For which is greater, the gold, or the temple that has sanctified the gold?

**23:18** And, 'Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; yet whoever swears by the gift upon it is bound.'

**23:19** You foolish and blind! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift?

**23:20** Therefore, the one who swears by the altar swears by it and by all things upon it;

**23:21** and the one who swears by the temple swears by it and by Him who dwells in it;

**23:22** and the one who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by Him who sits upon it.

**23:23** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you tithe mint and dill and cumin, and have neglected the weightier matters of the law—justice and mercy and faithfulness. Yet these you ought to have practiced, without neglecting the others.

**23:24** Blind guides, who strain out the gnat, yet swallow the camel!

**23:25** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish, yet within they are full of robbery and self-indulgence.

**23:26** You blind Pharisee, first cleanse the inside of the cup and of the dish, so that the outside of them may also become clean.

**23:27** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you resemble whitewashed tombs, which outwardly indeed appear fair, yet inwardly are full of the bones of the dead and all uncleanness;

**23:28** in like manner you also outwardly appear righteous to men, yet inwardly you are filled with hypocrisy and lawlessness.

**23:29** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you construct the tombs of the prophets and adorn the memorials of the righteous,

**23:30** and say, 'If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partners with them in the blood of the prophets.'

**23:31** Thus you testify against yourselves that you are the sons of those who murdered the prophets.

**23:32** And you yourselves, fill up the measure of your fathers!

**23:33** Serpents, offspring of vipers! How shall you escape from the judgment of hell?

**23:34** For this reason, behold, I am sending to you prophets, wise men, and scribes: some of them you will slay and crucify, and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues and persecute from city to city,

**23:35** so that upon you may come all the righteous blood poured out upon the earth, from the blood of Abel the righteous to the blood of Zechariah, son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar.

**23:36** Assuredly, I tell you, all these things shall come upon this generation.

**23:37** Jerusalem, Jerusalem, she who slays the prophets and stones those who have been sent to her—how often I desired to gather your children together, even as a hen gathers her brood under her wings, and you were not willing!

**23:38** Behold, your house is left to you desolate.

**23:39** For I tell you, from this time forward you shall by no means see Me until you say, 'Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!'"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**23:14** *(after `23:13`)* ⟦Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you devour widows' houses and for a pretense make long prayers; therefore you shall receive greater condemnation.⟧

## Translator Notes

- **23:8** — The Greek text here reads kathegetes ("Instructor" or "Teacher"), matching verse 10, rather than didaskalos ("Teacher") with the later addition "the Christ."
- **23:13** — Verse 14 ("Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for you devour widows' houses...") is absent from the earliest manuscripts and from this Greek text, having entered later copies from Mark 12:40 and Luke 20:47.
- **23:15** — "Hell" translates Greek Gehenna (from the Hebrew Valley of Hinnom south of Jerusalem), used in the Gospels for the place of final divine judgment; the same word appears in verse 33.
- **23:35** — Zechariah the priest, son of Jehoiada, was stoned in the temple court in 2 Chronicles 24:20-22 (the final book of the Hebrew canon), whereas the prophet Zechariah was son of Berechiah (Zechariah 1:1); the statement spans the martyrs from the first to the last book of the Hebrew Scriptures.
- **23:14 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
