## Text

**5:1** Now when He saw the multitudes, He ascended the mountain; and after He had sat down, His disciples drew near to Him.

**5:2** And opening His mouth, He began to teach them, saying:

**5:3** "Blessed are the poor in spirit, for to them belongs the kingdom of heaven.

**5:4** Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall receive comfort.

**5:5** Blessed are the meek, for they themselves shall inherit the earth.

**5:6** Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be fully satisfied.

**5:7** Blessed are the merciful, for they shall find mercy.

**5:8** Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall behold God.

**5:9** Blessed are the peacemakers, for they themselves shall be called sons of God.

**5:10** Blessed are those who have been persecuted on account of righteousness, for to them belongs the kingdom of heaven.

**5:11** Blessed are you when men reproach you and persecute you, and falsely speak every kind of evil against you for My sake.

**5:12** Rejoice and exult, for your reward in heaven is abundant; for in the same manner they persecuted the prophets who went before you.

**5:13** You are the salt of the earth; yet if the salt loses its savor, with what shall it be salted? It is no longer good for anything except to be cast outside and trampled underfoot by men.

**5:14** You are the light of the world. A city situated upon a mountain cannot be concealed;

**5:15** neither do men light a lamp and place it beneath a measuring basket, but upon the lampstand, and it shines upon all who are in the house.

**5:16** In like manner let your light shine before men, so that they may behold your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.

**5:17** Do not suppose that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets; I did not come to abolish, but to fulfill.

**5:18** For assuredly, I say to you, until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota or one stroke of a letter shall by any means pass away from the Law, until all things come to pass.

**5:19** Therefore, whoever sets aside one of the least of these commandments and teaches men to do the same shall be called least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever practices and teaches them, this one shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

**5:20** For I say to you that unless your righteousness surpasses that of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall by no means enter into the kingdom of heaven.

**5:21** You have heard that it was spoken to those of old, 'You shall not murder, and whoever murders shall be liable to the judgment.'

**5:22** But I say to you that everyone who is angry with his brother shall be liable to the judgment; and whoever says to his brother, 'Raca,' shall be liable to the Sanhedrin; and whoever says, 'You fool,' shall be liable to the fire of hell.

**5:23** Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother holds something against you,

**5:24** leave your gift there before the altar and go; first be reconciled with your brother, and then come and present your gift.

**5:25** Come to terms quickly with your adversary while you are with him on the way, lest the adversary hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be cast into prison.

**5:26** Assuredly, I say to you, you shall by no means come forth from there until you have paid the very last copper coin.

**5:27** You have heard that it was spoken, 'You shall not commit adultery.'

**5:28** But I say to you that everyone who looks upon a woman in order to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

**5:29** Now if your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and cast it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish, and not that your whole body be cast into hell.

**5:30** And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and cast it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish, and not that your whole body depart into hell.

**5:31** It was also spoken, 'Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a decree of divorce.'

**5:32** But I say to you that everyone who divorces his wife, except on the ground of sexual immorality, causes her to commit adultery; and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.

**5:33** Again, you have heard that it was spoken to those of old, 'You shall not swear falsely, but you shall fulfill your oaths to the Lord.'

**5:34** But I say to you, do not swear an oath at all: neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God;

**5:35** nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of His feet; nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King;

**5:36** neither shall you swear by your head, for you are unable to make a single hair white or black.

**5:37** Rather, let your word be 'Yes, yes,' 'No, no'; for anything beyond these comes from the evil one.

**5:38** You have heard that it was spoken, 'An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.'

**5:39** But I say to you, do not resist one who is evil; rather, whoever strikes you on the right cheek, turn to him the other as well;

**5:40** and to him who wishes to sue you and take your tunic, let him have your cloak as well;

**5:41** and whoever presses you into service for one mile, go with him two.

**5:42** Give to the one who asks of you, and do not turn away from the one who desires to borrow from you.

**5:43** You have heard that it was spoken, 'You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'

**5:44** But I say to you, love your enemies and pray on behalf of those who persecute you,

**5:45** so that you may become sons of your Father who is in heaven; for He causes His sun to rise upon the evil and the good, and sends rain upon the righteous and the unrighteous.

**5:46** For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the very same?

**5:47** And if you greet only your brethren, what extraordinary thing do you do? Do not even the Gentiles do the very same?

**5:48** Therefore, you yourselves shall be perfect, even as your heavenly Father is perfect."

## Translator Notes

- **5:18** — Greek 'iota' denotes the smallest letter of the alphabet (corresponding to Hebrew yod), and 'stroke of a letter' (keraia) denotes a tiny pen stroke or projection distinguishing similar Hebrew letters.
- **5:22** — The critical Greek text omits 'without a cause' (eike), found in some later manuscripts. 'Raca' is an Aramaic term of contempt meaning 'empty-headed,' and 'hell' renders Greek Gehenna, the Valley of Hinnom south of Jerusalem.
- **5:37** — The Greek phrase 'from the evil one' (ek tou ponerou) may also be understood as neuter, 'from evil,' since both genders share the same genitive form.
- **5:44** — The critical Greek text preserves the shorter form of this command, without the expanded clauses ('bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you...') found in later manuscripts and paralleled in Luke 6:27-28.
