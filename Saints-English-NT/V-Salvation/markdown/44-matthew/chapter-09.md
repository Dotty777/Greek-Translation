## Text

**9:1** And stepping into a boat, He crossed over and came to His own city.

**9:2** And behold, they brought to Him a paralyzed man lying upon a bed. And seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralytic, "Take courage, child; your sins are forgiven."

**9:3** And behold, certain of the scribes said within themselves, "This man blasphemes!"

**9:4** And Jesus, seeing their thoughts, said, "Why do you ponder evil in your hearts?

**9:5** For which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Arise and walk'?

**9:6** But in order that you may know that the Son of Man has authority upon the earth to forgive sins"—then He said to the paralytic, "Rise, pick up your bed, and go to your house."

**9:7** And rising up, he departed to his house.

**9:8** Now when the multitudes saw this, they were struck with fear and glorified God, who had given such authority to men.

**9:9** And as Jesus passed on from there, He saw a man named Matthew sitting at the tax office, and He said to him, "Follow Me." And rising up, he followed Him.

**9:10** And it came to pass, as He reclined at table in the house, behold, many tax collectors and sinners came and reclined together with Jesus and His disciples.

**9:11** And upon seeing this, the Pharisees spoke to His disciples, "Why is your Teacher eating with the tax collectors and sinners?"

**9:12** Yet when He heard this, He said, "Those who are strong have no need of a physician, but those who are ill.

**9:13** Go now and learn what this means: 'I desire mercy and not sacrifice.' For I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners."

**9:14** Then the disciples of John approached Him, saying, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast, whereas Your disciples do not fast?"

**9:15** And Jesus said to them, "Can the sons of the bridal chamber mourn so long as the bridegroom is with them? Yet days shall come when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then they shall fast.

**9:16** Moreover, no one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth upon an old garment; for its patch tears away from the garment, and a worse rent is made.

**9:17** Neither do men pour new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wineskins burst, and the wine is spilled, and the wineskins are ruined; rather, they pour new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved."

**9:18** While He was speaking these things to them, behold, a certain ruler came and worshiped Him, saying, "My daughter has just now died; yet come, lay Your hand upon her, and she shall live."

**9:19** And Jesus arose and followed him, and His disciples also.

**9:20** And behold, a woman who had suffered a flow of blood for twelve years approached from behind and touched the fringe of His garment;

**9:21** for she was saying within herself, "If I only touch His garment, I shall be made well."

**9:22** Then Jesus, turning and seeing her, said, "Take courage, daughter; your faith has made you well." And the woman was made well from that very hour.

**9:23** And when Jesus came into the house of the ruler and saw the flute players and the crowd making a noisy commotion,

**9:24** He said, "Withdraw, for the girl has not died, but is sleeping." And they laughed Him to scorn.

**9:25** Yet when the crowd had been put outside, He went in and took hold of her hand, and the girl arose.

**9:26** And the report of this went forth into all that land.

**9:27** And as Jesus departed from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out and saying, "Have mercy upon us, Son of David!"

**9:28** Now when He had come into the house, the blind men approached Him; and Jesus said to them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this?" They said to Him, "Yes, Lord."

**9:29** Then He touched their eyes, saying, "According to your faith, let it be done for you."

**9:30** And their eyes were opened. And Jesus sternly charged them, saying, "See to it that no one knows of this."

**9:31** Yet having gone out, they spread His fame throughout all that land.

**9:32** Now as they were going out, behold, they brought to Him a mute man who was demon-possessed.

**9:33** And when the demon was cast out, the mute man spoke; and the multitudes marveled, saying, "Never was anything like this seen in Israel!"

**9:34** Yet the Pharisees were saying, "By the ruler of the demons He casts out the demons."

**9:35** And Jesus went about through all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every infirmity.

**9:36** And when He saw the multitudes, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed and cast down, like sheep having no shepherd.

**9:37** Then He said to His disciples, "The harvest indeed is plentiful, yet the laborers are few;

**9:38** pray therefore to the Lord of the harvest that He may send forth laborers into His harvest."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The critical text reads idon ("seeing"), highlighting Jesus' direct perception of the scribes' inward thoughts, whereas later manuscripts read eidos ("knowing").
- **9:8** — The earliest manuscripts read "were struck with fear" (ephobethesan), expressing reverent awe before divine authority; later Byzantine manuscripts read "marveled."
- **9:13** — The critical Greek text ends with "sinners"; the additional phrase "to repentance" in later manuscripts reflects harmonization with Luke 5:32.
- **9:36** — The Greek participles eskylmenoi ("harassed," literally "flayed" or "troubled") and errimmenoi ("cast down" or "helpless") depict the crowds as spiritually exhausted and neglected like untended sheep.
