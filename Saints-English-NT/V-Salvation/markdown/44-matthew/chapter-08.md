## Text

**8:1** Now when He descended from the mountain, many multitudes followed Him.

**8:2** And behold, a leper approached and worshiped Him, saying, "Lord, if You are willing, You are able to cleanse me."

**8:3** And stretching forth His hand, He touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.

**8:4** And Jesus said to him, "See that you tell no one; rather go, show yourself to the priest, and present the gift which Moses commanded, as a witness to them."

**8:5** Now when He had entered Capernaum, a centurion approached Him, imploring Him

**8:6** and saying, "Lord, my servant lies paralyzed in the house, grievously tormented."

**8:7** And He said to him, "I Myself will come and heal him."

**8:8** And in reply the centurion said, "Lord, I am not worthy that You should enter beneath my roof; rather, speak only with a word, and my servant shall be healed.

**8:9** For I also am a man set under authority, having soldiers under myself; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my bondservant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**8:10** Now when Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those who followed, "Assuredly, I say to you, with no one in Israel have I found such great faith.

**8:11** And I tell you that many shall come from east and west, and shall recline at table with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven,

**8:12** but the sons of the kingdom shall be cast out into the outer darkness; in that place there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

**8:13** Then Jesus said to the centurion, "Go; let it be done for you even as you have believed." And the servant was healed in that very hour.

**8:14** And upon entering the house of Peter, Jesus saw his mother-in-law lying sick and stricken with a fever.

**8:15** And He touched her hand, and the fever left her; and she arose and ministered to Him.

**8:16** Now when evening had come, they brought to Him many who were demon-possessed; and He cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were afflicted,

**8:17** in order that what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah might be fulfilled, saying, "He Himself took up our infirmities and bore our diseases."

**8:18** Now when Jesus saw a multitude around Him, He gave command to depart to the other side.

**8:19** And a certain scribe approached and said to Him, "Teacher, I will follow You wherever You may go."

**8:20** And Jesus said to him, "The foxes have dens and the birds of the sky have nests, yet the Son of Man has no place where He may lay His head."

**8:21** And another of the disciples said to Him, "Lord, permit me first to depart and bury my father."

**8:22** But Jesus said to him, "Follow Me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead."

**8:23** And when He stepped into a boat, His disciples followed Him.

**8:24** And behold, a fierce tempest arose upon the sea, so that the boat was being covered by the waves; yet He Himself was sleeping.

**8:25** And coming to Him, they awakened Him, saying, "Lord, save us; we are perishing!"

**8:26** And He said to them, "Why are you fearful, you of little faith?" Then rising up, He rebuked the winds and the sea, and there came a great calm.

**8:27** And the men were amazed, saying, "What sort of man is this, that both the winds and the sea obey Him?"

**8:28** Now when He had come to the other side, into the region of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men met Him as they came forth from the tombs, exceedingly fierce, so that no one was able to pass along that way.

**8:29** And behold, they cried out, saying, "What is there between us and You, Son of God? Have You come here to torment us before the time?"

**8:30** Now at a distance from them, a herd of many swine was feeding.

**8:31** So the demons implored Him, saying, "If You cast us out, send us into the herd of swine."

**8:32** And He said to them, "Go." And coming out, they departed into the swine; and behold, the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and died in the waters.

**8:33** Then those who tended them fled, and going into the city, they reported everything, including what had happened to the demon-possessed men.

**8:34** And behold, the entire city came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw Him, they urged Him to depart from their borders.

## Translator Notes

- **8:10** — The critical Greek text reads "with no one in Israel have I found such great faith," whereas later Byzantine manuscripts read "not even in Israel have I found so great a faith."
- **8:15** — The critical Greek text reads "to Him" (singular), depicting Peter's mother-in-law ministering directly to Jesus, while later manuscripts read "to them."
- **8:24** — The Greek noun seismos ("tempest") literally denotes a violent shaking or earthquake, underscoring the sudden, turbulent upheaval upon the sea.
- **8:28** — The earliest Greek manuscripts of Matthew read "Gadarenes," referring to the territory of Gadara southeast of the Sea of Galilee; later manuscripts substitute "Gergesenes" or "Gerasenes" (cf. Mark 5:1; Luke 8:26).
