## Text

**5:1** And they came to the other side of the sea, into the region of the Gerasenes.

**5:2** And as He came forth from the boat, immediately a man with an unclean spirit met Him from the tombs,

**5:3** who had his dwelling place among the tombs; and no one was able to bind him any longer, not even with a chain,

**5:4** for he had many times been bound with fetters and chains, yet the chains had been torn apart by him and the fetters shattered in pieces; and no one had the strength to tame him.

**5:5** And continually, night and day, he was among the tombs and in the mountains, crying out and gashing himself with stones.

**5:6** And seeing Jesus from afar, he ran and bowed down before Him,

**5:7** and crying out with a loud voice, he said, "What is there between me and You, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I adjure You by God, do not torment me."

**5:8** For He was saying to him, "Come forth from the man, unclean spirit!"

**5:9** And He questioned him, "What is your name?" And he said to Him, "Legion is my name, for we are many."

**5:10** And he pleaded with Him earnestly that He would not send them out of the region.

**5:11** Now there was a great herd of swine feeding there beside the mountain.

**5:12** And they implored Him, saying, "Send us into the swine, that we may enter into them."

**5:13** And He permitted them. And coming out, the unclean spirits entered into the swine; and the herd rushed headlong down the steep bank into the sea, about two thousand in number, and were drowned in the sea.

**5:14** And those who tended them fled and brought the report into the city and into the countryside; and they went out to see what it was that had come to pass.

**5:15** And they came to Jesus and beheld the demon-possessed man sitting, clothed and in his right mind—the one who had had the legion—and they were afraid.

**5:16** And those who had witnessed it related to them how it had come to pass with the demon-possessed man, and concerning the swine.

**5:17** And they began to implore Him to depart from their borders.

**5:18** And as He was entering the boat, the man who had been demon-possessed pleaded with Him that he might be with Him.

**5:19** And He did not permit him, but said to him, "Go to your house, to your own people, and report to them all that the Lord has done for you, and how He has had mercy upon you."

**5:20** And he went forth and began to proclaim in the Decapolis all the things Jesus had done for him, and everyone marveled.

**5:21** And when Jesus had crossed over again in the boat to the other side, a great multitude assembled around Him; and He was beside the sea.

**5:22** And there came one of the rulers of the synagogue, named Jairus; and seeing Him, he fell down at His feet

**5:23** and implored Him earnestly, saying, "My little daughter is at the point of death; come and lay Your hands upon her, that she may be made well and live."

**5:24** And He went with him; and a great multitude followed Him and pressed hard upon Him.

**5:25** And a woman who had been afflicted with a flow of blood for twelve years,

**5:26** and had suffered many things under many physicians, and had spent all that she possessed, and had gained no benefit but had rather grown worse,

**5:27** having heard the things concerning Jesus, came up from behind in the multitude and touched His garment.

**5:28** For she was saying, "If I may touch even His garments, I shall be made well."

**5:29** And immediately the fountain of her blood dried up, and she perceived in her body that she had been healed from the affliction.

**5:30** And immediately Jesus, recognizing within Himself that power had gone forth from Him, turned around in the multitude and said, "Who touched My garments?"

**5:31** And His disciples were saying to Him, "You see the multitude pressing hard upon You, and You say, 'Who touched Me?'"

**5:32** And He kept looking about to see the woman who had done this.

**5:33** Now the woman, afraid and trembling, aware of what had happened to her, came and fell down before Him and declared to Him all the truth.

**5:34** And He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you; depart in peace, and be whole from your affliction."

**5:35** While He was yet speaking, some came from the household of the ruler of the synagogue, saying, "Your daughter has died; why do you still trouble the Teacher?"

**5:36** But Jesus, disregarding the word being spoken, said to the ruler of the synagogue, "Do not fear; only believe."

**5:37** And He did not permit anyone to accompany Him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.

**5:38** And they came to the house of the ruler of the synagogue, and He beheld a tumult, and those weeping and wailing greatly.

**5:39** And entering in, He said to them, "Why do you raise a tumult and weep? The child has not died, but is sleeping."

**5:40** And they laughed at Him in derision. Yet He put them all out, and took along the father of the child and the mother and those with Him, and entered where the child was.

**5:41** And taking hold of the child's hand, He said to her, "Talitha koum!" which is translated, "Little girl, to you I say, arise."

**5:42** And immediately the little girl rose up and walked about, for she was twelve years old; and immediately they were astounded with great astonishment.

**5:43** And He charged them strictly that no one should come to know this, and He directed that something be given to her to eat.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The earliest Greek text reads Gerasenes; later manuscripts harmonize the geography to Gadarenes (cf. Matthew 8:28) or Gergesenes.
- **5:34** — The Greek verb sozo ("has saved you") encompasses both physical healing ("be made well," as in 5:23, 28) and spiritual deliverance.
- **5:36** — The Greek participle parakousas can mean either "disregarding/paying no heed to" or "overhearing"; some later manuscripts instead read akousas ("hearing").
- **5:41** — "Talitha koum" transcribes Aramaic meaning "Little girl, arise"; some later Greek manuscripts spell the imperative koumi.
