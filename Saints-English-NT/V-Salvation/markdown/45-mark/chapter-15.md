## Text

**15:1** And at once in the morning, the chief priests, having taken counsel with the elders and scribes and the entire council, bound Jesus, led Him away, and delivered Him over to Pilate.

**15:2** And Pilate questioned Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And answering, He said to him, "You say so."

**15:3** And the chief priests were accusing Him of many things.

**15:4** Then Pilate questioned Him again, saying, "Do You give no answer? Behold, how many accusations they bring against You."

**15:5** Yet Jesus answered nothing further, so that Pilate marveled.

**15:6** Now at the feast he was accustomed to release for them one prisoner, whom they asked for.

**15:7** And there was one called Barabbas, bound with the insurrectionists who had committed murder during the uprising.

**15:8** And the multitude, coming up, began to ask him to do just as he used to do for them.

**15:9** And Pilate answered them, saying, "Do you wish that I release for you the King of the Jews?"

**15:10** For he perceived that it was out of envy that the chief priests had delivered Him over.

**15:11** But the chief priests stirred up the multitude that he might release Barabbas for them instead.

**15:12** And Pilate answered again and said to them, "What then shall I do with Him whom you call the King of the Jews?"

**15:13** And again they cried out, "Crucify Him!"

**15:14** Then Pilate said to them, "Why? What evil has He done?" Yet they cried out all the more vehemently, "Crucify Him!"

**15:15** So Pilate, desiring to satisfy the multitude, released Barabbas to them; and having scourged Jesus, he delivered Him over to be crucified.

**15:16** Then the soldiers led Him away inside the courtyard, which is the Praetorium, and summoned together the entire cohort.

**15:17** And they dressed Him in purple, and having woven a crown of thorns, they placed it upon Him.

**15:18** And they began to greet Him, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**15:19** And they kept striking His head with a reed and spitting upon Him, and kneeling down they paid Him homage.

**15:20** And when they had mocked Him, they stripped the purple from Him and clothed Him in His own garments. Then they led Him out to crucify Him.

**15:21** And they pressed into service a certain passerby, Simon of Cyrene, coming in from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry His cross.

**15:22** And they brought Him to the place Golgotha, which, being interpreted, is Place of a Skull.

**15:23** And they offered Him wine mingled with myrrh, yet He did not take it.

**15:24** And having crucified Him, they divided His garments among themselves, casting lots over them to decide what each should take.

**15:25** And it was the third hour when they crucified Him.

**15:26** And the inscription of the charge against Him was written: "The King of the Jews."

**15:27** And with Him they crucified two robbers, one at His right hand and one at His left.

**15:29** And those who were passing by reviled Him, shaking their heads and saying, "Ha! You who tear down the temple and build it in three days,

**15:30** save Yourself by coming down from the cross!"

**15:31** In like manner the chief priests also, mocking among themselves together with the scribes, were saying, "Others He saved, yet Himself He cannot save.

**15:32** Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe!" And those who were crucified with Him also reproached Him.

**15:33** And when the sixth hour arrived, darkness came over the entire land until the ninth hour.

**15:34** And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a great voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" which, being interpreted, means, "My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?"

**15:35** And some of those standing near, upon hearing it, said, "Behold, He is calling Elijah!"

**15:36** And someone ran, filled a sponge with sour wine, put it upon a reed, and offered it to Him to drink, saying, "Wait, let us see whether Elijah is coming to take Him down."

**15:37** Then Jesus uttered a great cry and breathed His last.

**15:38** And the veil of the sanctuary was torn in two from the top to the bottom.

**15:39** Now when the centurion, who stood facing Him, saw that He breathed His last in this manner, he said, "Truly this Man was the Son of God!"

**15:40** There were also women watching from a distance, among whom were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome,

**15:41** who used to follow Him and minister to Him when He was in Galilee, along with many other women who had come up with Him to Jerusalem.

**15:42** And when evening had already come, since it was the Day of Preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath,

**15:43** Joseph of Arimathea, a respected member of the council, who was himself awaiting the kingdom of God, came, summoned courage, went in to Pilate, and requested the body of Jesus.

**15:44** And Pilate marveled that He should already be dead; and calling the centurion to himself, he inquired of him whether He had been dead for some time.

**15:45** And upon learning it from the centurion, he granted the corpse to Joseph.

**15:46** And having bought a linen shroud, he took Him down, wrapped Him in the linen, and laid Him in a tomb that had been hewn out of rock; and he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb.

**15:47** And Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses were watching where He was laid.

## Translator Notes

- **15:27** — Verse 28, which appears in later manuscripts quoting Isaiah 53:12 ('And He was numbered with the transgressors'), is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts and is omitted from this text.
- **15:34** — Jesus cries out in Aramaic, quoting Psalm 22:1; the critical Greek text preserves the Aramaic transliteration 'lema' ('why') rather than the Hebrew 'lama'.
- **15:39** — The Greek phrase 'huios theou' lacks the definite article and can be rendered either 'the Son of God' or 'a son of God'; within Mark's Gospel (cf. 1:1, 11; 9:7), it functions as the climactic Christological confession.
- **15:45** — Mark shifts from 'soma' ('body') in Joseph's request (v. 43) to 'ptoma' ('corpse') in Pilate's official grant, highlighting the Roman governor's clinical perspective.
- **15:28 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
