## Text

**11:1** And as they drew near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany, near the Mount of Olives, He sent forth two of His disciples,

**11:2** and said to them, "Go into the village opposite you, and immediately as you enter it, you will find a colt tied, on which no man has yet sat. Untie it and bring it.

**11:3** And if anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord has need of it, and He will immediately send it back here.'"

**11:4** So they departed and found a colt tied by the door outside along the street, and they untied it.

**11:5** And some of those standing there said to them, "What are you doing, untying the colt?"

**11:6** And they told them just as Jesus had said, and they let them go.

**11:7** Then they brought the colt to Jesus and cast their garments upon it, and He sat upon it.

**11:8** And many spread their garments upon the road, and others spread leafy branches cut from the fields.

**11:9** And those who went ahead and those who followed cried out, "Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!

**11:10** Blessed is the kingdom of our father David that is coming! Hosanna in the highest!"

**11:11** And He entered Jerusalem, into the temple; and having looked around at everything, since the hour was already late, He departed to Bethany with the twelve.

**11:12** And on the following day, as they departed from Bethany, He became hungry.

**11:13** And seeing from a distance a fig tree in leaf, He went to see whether He might find anything on it; and coming to it, He found nothing except leaves, for it was not the season of figs.

**11:14** And answering, He said to it, "May no one ever eat fruit from you again!" And His disciples heard it.

**11:15** Then they came to Jerusalem. And having entered the temple, He began to cast out those who were selling and those who were buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those who sold doves,

**11:16** and He would not permit anyone to carry a vessel through the temple.

**11:17** And He taught and said to them, "Is it not written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations'? Yet you have made it a den of robbers."

**11:18** And the chief priests and the scribes heard of it and sought how they might destroy Him; for they feared Him, since the whole multitude was astonished at His teaching.

**11:19** And when evening arrived, they went forth outside the city.

**11:20** And as they were passing by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots.

**11:21** And Peter, calling it to mind, said to Him, "Rabbi, behold, the fig tree which You cursed has withered!"

**11:22** And answering, Jesus said to them, "Have faith in God.

**11:23** Assuredly, I say to you, whoever says to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and be cast into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that what he speaks will come to pass, it shall be done for him.

**11:24** Therefore I say to you, everything that you pray and ask for, believe that you have received it, and it shall be yours.

**11:25** And whenever you stand praying, forgive if you hold anything against anyone, so that your Father who is in heaven may also forgive you your trespasses."

**11:27** And they came once more to Jerusalem. And while He was walking about in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders came to Him,

**11:28** and said to Him, "By what authority do You do these things? Or who gave You this authority, that You should do these things?"

**11:29** And Jesus said to them, "I too will ask you one question; answer Me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things:

**11:30** Was John's baptism from heaven or from men? Answer Me."

**11:31** And they deliberated among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will reply, 'Why then did you not believe him?'

**11:32** But shall we say, 'From men'?"—they feared the multitude, for all held that John was truly a prophet.

**11:33** And answering Jesus, they said, "We do not know." And Jesus said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**11:26** *(after `11:25`)* ⟦But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father who is in heaven forgive your trespasses.⟧

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — The clause "and He will immediately send it back here" is rendered as part of the disciples' reply, promising that the Lord will promptly return the colt; alternatively, it may be Jesus assuring the disciples that the owner will immediately send it to Him.
- **11:24** — The Greek aorist verb "have received" expresses vivid assurance that the prayer is already granted; some later manuscripts read the present tense "receive."
- **11:25** — Verse twenty-six ("But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father in heaven forgive your trespasses") is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts and is therefore omitted from the critical text.
- **11:32** — The leaders' deliberation breaks off abruptly without stating the consequence of saying "From men," as Mark intervenes to explain their fear of the multitude.
- **11:26 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
