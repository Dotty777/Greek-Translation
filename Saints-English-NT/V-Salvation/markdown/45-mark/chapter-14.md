## Text

**14:1** Now the Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread were two days away. And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might seize Him by deceit and put Him to death.

**14:2** For they were saying, "Not during the feast, lest there be a tumult of the people."

**14:3** And while He was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, as He was reclining at table, a woman came having an alabaster flask of very precious ointment of genuine nard. And breaking the flask, she poured it upon His head.

**14:4** But there were some who felt indignation among themselves: "Why has this waste of the ointment taken place?

**14:5** For this ointment could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and given to the poor." And they were reproaching her sharply.

**14:6** But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why do you cause her trouble? She has performed a noble work for Me.

**14:7** For you always have the poor with you, and whenever you wish, you are able to do good to them; but Me you do not always have.

**14:8** She has done what lay within her power; she anointed My body in advance for burial.

**14:9** And assuredly, I say to you, wherever the gospel is proclaimed throughout the whole world, what this woman has done shall also be spoken of in remembrance of her."

**14:10** Then Judas Iscariot, who was one of the twelve, went away to the chief priests in order to betray Him to them.

**14:11** And when they heard it, they rejoiced and promised to give him silver. And he kept seeking how he might betray Him at an opportune time.

**14:12** Now on the first day of Unleavened Bread, when they sacrificed the Passover lamb, His disciples said to Him, "Where do You wish us to go and make preparation, that You may eat the Passover?"

**14:13** And He sent two of His disciples and said to them, "Go into the city, and a man bearing a jar of water will meet you; follow him,

**14:14** and wherever he enters, say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says: Where is My guest room in which I may eat the Passover with My disciples?'

**14:15** And he himself will show you a large upper room, furnished and ready; and there make preparation for us."

**14:16** So the disciples went forth and entered the city, and found things just as He had told them; and they prepared the Passover.

**14:17** And when evening had come, He came with the twelve.

**14:18** And as they were reclining and eating, Jesus said, "Assuredly, I say to you, one of you will betray Me—one who is eating with Me."

**14:19** They began to be sorrowful and to say to Him one by one, "Surely it is not I?"

**14:20** And He said to them, "It is one of the twelve, the one who is dipping with Me into the dish.

**14:21** For indeed the Son of Man goes just as it is written concerning Him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born."

**14:22** And while they were eating, He took bread, and having blessed it, He broke it and gave it to them, and said, "Take; this is My body."

**14:23** And taking a cup, and having given thanks, He gave it to them, and they all drank from it.

**14:24** And He said to them, "This is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out on behalf of many.

**14:25** Assuredly, I say to you, I shall by no means drink again of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God."

**14:26** And having sung a hymn, they went forth to the Mount of Olives.

**14:27** And Jesus said to them, "You shall all be made to stumble, for it is written: 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered.'

**14:28** Yet after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee."

**14:29** But Peter said to Him, "Even though all should be made to stumble, yet not I."

**14:30** And Jesus said to him, "Assuredly, I say to you, today—this very night—before a rooster crows twice, you yourself will deny Me three times."

**14:31** But he kept speaking all the more emphatically, "Even if I must die with You, I shall by no means deny You!" And in like manner they all kept saying the same.

**14:32** And they came to a place whose name was Gethsemane; and He said to His disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

**14:33** And He took Peter, James, and John with Him, and began to be filled with dread and deep anguish.

**14:34** And He said to them, "My soul is deeply sorrowful, even to death. Abide here and keep watch."

**14:35** And going forward a little way, He fell upon the ground and was praying that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from Him.

**14:36** And He said, "Abba, Father, all things are possible to You. Remove this cup from Me; yet not what I will, but what You will."

**14:37** Then He came and found them sleeping, and He said to Peter, "Simon, are you sleeping? Were you not able to keep watch one hour?

**14:38** Keep watch and pray, lest you come into temptation. Indeed, the spirit is willing, yet the flesh is weak."

**14:39** And going away again, He prayed, speaking the same words.

**14:40** And returning again, He found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavily weighed down; and they did not know what to answer Him.

**14:41** Then He came the third time and said to them, "Are you still sleeping and taking your rest? It is enough! The hour has come; behold, the Son of Man is being betrayed into the hands of sinners.

**14:42** Arise, let us be going! Behold, the one who betrays Me has drawn near."

**14:43** And immediately, while He was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, arrived, and with him a multitude with swords and clubs from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.

**14:44** Now the one betraying Him had given them an agreed signal, saying, "Whomever I kiss, He is the One; seize Him and lead Him away securely."

**14:45** And when he arrived, he immediately stepped up to Him and said, "Rabbi!" and kissed Him.

**14:46** Then they laid their hands on Him and seized Him.

**14:47** And a certain one of those standing by drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

**14:48** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs to arrest Me?

**14:49** Day after day I was with you in the temple teaching, and you did not seize Me. Yet this is so that the Scriptures may be fulfilled."

**14:50** And forsaking Him, they all fled.

**14:51** Now a certain young man was following Him, wrapped in a linen cloth over his naked body; and they seized him,

**14:52** but leaving behind the linen cloth, he fled naked.

**14:53** And they led Jesus away to the high priest, and all the chief priests, the elders, and the scribes assembled together.

**14:54** And Peter followed Him from a distance, even inside into the courtyard of the high priest; and he was sitting with the officers and warming himself by the firelight.

**14:55** Now the chief priests and the entire council were seeking testimony against Jesus so as to put Him to death, yet they found none.

**14:56** For many were bearing false witness against Him, yet their testimonies did not agree.

**14:57** Then certain ones stood up and were bearing false witness against Him, saying,

**14:58** "We ourselves heard Him saying, 'I will tear down this temple that is made with hands, and in three days I will build another not made with hands.'"

**14:59** Yet not even on this point did their testimony agree.

**14:60** And rising up in the midst, the high priest questioned Jesus, saying, "Do You make no answer? What is it that these men testify against You?"

**14:61** But He remained silent and gave no answer. Again the high priest questioned Him and said to Him, "Are You the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?"

**14:62** And Jesus said, "I am; and you shall see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of the Power, and coming with the clouds of heaven."

**14:63** Then the high priest tore his garments and said, "What further need do we have of witnesses?

**14:64** You have heard the blasphemy! What is your judgment?" And they all condemned Him to be worthy of death.

**14:65** Then some began to spit upon Him, and to cover His face, and to strike Him with their fists, and to say to Him, "Prophesy!" And the officers received Him with blows.

**14:66** Now while Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the servant girls of the high priest came by,

**14:67** and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked closely at him and said, "You also were with the Nazarene, Jesus."

**14:68** But he denied it, saying, "I do not know, nor do I understand what you are speaking of." And he went outside into the forecourt.

**14:69** And the servant girl saw him and began again to say to those standing by, "This man is one of them."

**14:70** But he denied it again. And after a little while, those who stood by again said to Peter, "Truly you are one of them, for indeed you are a Galilean."

**14:71** Then he began to invoke curses and to swear, "I do not know this man of whom you speak!"

**14:72** And immediately a rooster crowed a second time. And Peter remembered the word, how Jesus had said to him, "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times." And as he reflected upon it, he wept.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — The rare Greek adjective pistikēs ("genuine") is debated; it is most commonly understood as "genuine" or "pure," though some interpret it as "liquid" or as a botanical loanword denoting a specific variety of nard.
- **14:22** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads simply "Take; this is My body," omitting "eat," which entered later manuscripts in assimilation to Matthew 26:26.
- **14:24** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads "My blood of the covenant" without "new," which appears in later manuscripts under the influence of Luke 22:20 and 1 Corinthians 11:25.
- **14:36** — Mark preserves the Aramaic address Abba—an intimate yet reverent term for "Father"—followed immediately by its Greek equivalent ho patēr ("Father").
- **14:41** — The rare impersonal Greek verb apechei ("It is enough!") is debated and draws from commercial usage ("paid in full"), here signifying that the waiting is over and the appointed hour has arrived.
- **14:68** — The Saints Greek Critical Text ends this verse at "into the forecourt," omitting the clause "and a rooster crowed," which appears in a number of other manuscripts.
- **14:70** — The Saints Greek Critical Text omits "and your speech is like theirs," a clause added in later manuscripts in assimilation to Matthew 26:73.
- **14:72** — The Greek participle epibalōn ("as he reflected upon it") is unusually difficult and has also been interpreted as "breaking down" (bursting into tears) or "covering his head."
