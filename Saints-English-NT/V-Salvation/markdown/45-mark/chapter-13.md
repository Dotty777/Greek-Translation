## Text

**13:1** And as He departed from the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, "Teacher, behold what manner of stones and what manner of buildings!"

**13:2** And Jesus said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone shall be left here upon another that shall not be torn down."

**13:3** And as He sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew questioned Him privately:

**13:4** "Tell us, when shall these things take place, and what will be the sign when all these things are about to be accomplished?"

**13:5** Then Jesus began to say to them, "Take heed lest anyone lead you astray.

**13:6** Many shall come in My name, saying, 'I am He,' and shall lead many astray.

**13:7** And when you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not be alarmed; these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.

**13:8** For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there shall be earthquakes in various places; there shall be famines. These are the beginning of birth pangs.

**13:9** But keep watch over yourselves: they shall deliver you over to councils, and in synagogues you shall be beaten; and you shall stand before governors and kings for My sake, as a testimony to them.

**13:10** And to all the nations the gospel must first be proclaimed.

**13:11** And when they lead you away and deliver you over, do not be anxious beforehand concerning what you shall speak; rather, whatever is given to you in that hour, speak that; for it is not you who speak, but the Holy Spirit.

**13:12** And brother shall deliver up brother to death, and a father his child; and children shall rise up against parents and put them to death.

**13:13** And you shall be hated by all on account of My name; yet he who endures to the end, that one shall be saved.

**13:14** Now when you see the abomination of desolation standing where he ought not—let the reader understand—then let those in Judea flee to the mountains;

**13:15** and let him who is upon the housetop not come down, nor enter to take anything out of his house;

**13:16** and let him who is in the field not turn back to take his cloak.

**13:17** Yet woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days!

**13:18** And pray that it may not come to pass in winter.

**13:19** For those days shall be such tribulation as has not occurred from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, and shall by no means ever be.

**13:20** And if the Lord had not cut short the days, no flesh would be saved; yet for the sake of the elect, whom He chose, He cut short the days.

**13:21** And then if anyone says to you, 'Behold, here is the Christ!' or 'Behold, there He is!' do not believe it;

**13:22** for false christs and false prophets shall arise and display signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, the elect.

**13:23** But as for you, keep watch; behold, I have foretold all things to you.

**13:24** Yet in those days, after that tribulation, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give its radiance;

**13:25** and the stars shall be falling from heaven, and the powers that are in the heavens shall be shaken.

**13:26** And then they shall behold the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.

**13:27** And then He shall send forth the angels and gather together His elect from the four winds, from the farthest end of the earth to the farthest end of heaven.

**13:28** Now from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already grown tender and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near.

**13:29** Likewise you also, when you see these things coming to pass, know that He is near, at the very doors.

**13:30** Assuredly, I say to you, this generation shall by no means pass away until all these things have come to pass.

**13:31** Heaven and earth shall pass away, yet My words shall never pass away.

**13:32** Now concerning that day or hour, no one knows—neither the angels in heaven nor the Son—except the Father.

**13:33** Take heed, keep alert, and pray, for you do not know when the time is.

**13:34** It is like a man journeying abroad, who left his house and gave his servants authority—to each one his work—and commanded the doorkeeper to keep watch.

**13:35** Keep watch, therefore, for you do not know when the master of the house comes—whether at evening, or at midnight, or at the crowing of the rooster, or at dawn—

**13:36** lest, coming suddenly, he find you sleeping.

**13:37** And what I say to you, I say to all: Keep watch!"

## Translator Notes

- **13:14** — The Greek text pairs the neuter noun 'abomination' with a masculine participle ('standing'), pointing to a personal desecrator ('where he ought not'). Unlike Matthew 24:15, the critical Greek text of Mark does not include 'spoken of by Daniel the prophet.'
- **13:29** — The Greek verb leaves its subject unexpressed and may be rendered either 'He is near' (referring to the Son of Man in verse 26) or 'it is near' (referring to the fulfillment of these events).
- **13:32** — The critical Greek text reads 'that day or hour' and explicitly includes 'nor the Son,' underscoring that knowledge of the appointed time belongs solely to the Father.
- **13:35** — Evening, midnight, the crowing of the rooster, and dawn designate the four Roman watches of the night.
