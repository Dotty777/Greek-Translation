## Text

**9:1** And He was saying to them, "Assuredly, I say to you that there are some of those standing here who shall by no means taste death until they see the kingdom of God having come in power."

**9:2** And after six days Jesus took with Him Peter and James and John, and brought them up onto a high mountain privately by themselves; and He was transfigured before them.

**9:3** And His garments became radiant, exceedingly white, such as no launderer on the earth can whiten so.

**9:4** And Elijah appeared to them together with Moses, and they were conversing with Jesus.

**9:5** And answering, Peter said to Jesus, "Rabbi, it is good for us to be here; and let us make three tabernacles—one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

**9:6** For he did not know what to answer, because they had become terrified.

**9:7** And a cloud appeared, overshadowing them, and a voice came out of the cloud: "This is My beloved Son; listen to Him."

**9:8** And suddenly, having looked around, they saw no one anymore, except Jesus alone with them.

**9:9** And as they were descending from the mountain, He charged them that they should tell no one the things which they had seen, until the Son of Man should rise from the dead.

**9:10** And they kept this saying close to themselves, discussing what rising from the dead meant.

**9:11** And they were questioning Him, saying, "Why do the scribes say that Elijah must first come?"

**9:12** And He said to them, "Elijah indeed, coming first, restores all things. And how is it written concerning the Son of Man, that He should suffer many things and be held in contempt?

**9:13** But I say to you that Elijah has also come, and they did to him whatever they desired, even as it is written concerning him."

**9:14** And when they came to the disciples, they saw a great multitude around them, and scribes disputing with them.

**9:15** And immediately the whole multitude, when they saw Him, were greatly astonished, and running up, they greeted Him.

**9:16** And He asked them, "What are you disputing among yourselves?"

**9:17** And one from the multitude answered Him, "Teacher, I have brought my son to You, who has a mute spirit;

**9:18** and wherever it seizes him, it dashes him down, and he foams at the mouth and grinds his teeth and becomes stiff. And I spoke to Your disciples that they might cast it out, and they were not able."

**9:19** And answering them, He said, "O unbelieving generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I endure you? Bring him to Me."

**9:20** And they brought him to Him. And when the spirit saw Him, it immediately threw him into violent convulsions; and falling upon the ground, he rolled about, foaming at the mouth.

**9:21** And He asked his father, "How long a time has it been since this came upon him?" And he said, "From childhood;

**9:22** and many times it has cast him both into fire and into waters in order to destroy him. Yet if You are able to do anything, have compassion on us and help us."

**9:23** And Jesus said to him, "'If You are able'! All things are possible to the one who believes."

**9:24** At once, crying out, the father of the child said, "I believe; aid my unbelief!"

**9:25** And when Jesus saw that a multitude was running together, He rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, "You mute and deaf spirit, I command you: come out of him, and never enter into him again!"

**9:26** And crying out and convulsing him violently, it came out; and he became like one dead, so that most of them said that he had died.

**9:27** But Jesus, grasping his hand, raised him up, and he arose.

**9:28** And when He had entered a house, His disciples questioned Him privately, "Why were we unable to cast it out?"

**9:29** And He said to them, "This kind cannot come forth by anything except by prayer."

**9:30** And departing from there, they were passing through Galilee, and He did not wish for anyone to know.

**9:31** For He was teaching His disciples and saying to them, "The Son of Man is being delivered into the hands of men, and they will slay Him; and having been slain, after three days He shall rise."

**9:32** Yet they did not understand this saying, and they were afraid to question Him.

**9:33** And they came into Capernaum. And when He was in the house, He questioned them, "What were you debating along the way?"

**9:34** But they remained silent, for on the way they had disputed with one another as to who was the greatest.

**9:35** And sitting down, He summoned the twelve and said to them, "If anyone wishes to be first, he shall be the last of all and the servant of all."

**9:36** And taking a little child, He set him in their midst; and taking him in His arms, He said to them,

**9:37** "Whoever welcomes one of such little children in My name welcomes Me; and whoever welcomes Me does not welcome Me, but Him who sent Me."

**9:38** John said to Him, "Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in Your name, and we were forbidding him, because he was not following us."

**9:39** But Jesus said, "Do not forbid him, for there is no one who shall perform a mighty work in My name and soon be able to speak evil of Me;

**9:40** for anyone who is not against us is for us.

**9:41** For whoever gives you a cup of water to drink on account of the name, because you belong to Christ, assuredly, I say to you that he shall by no means lose his reward.

**9:42** And whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it would be better for him if a great millstone were fastened around his neck and he were cast into the sea.

**9:43** And if your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life maimed than, having two hands, to depart into hell, into the unquenchable fire.

**9:45** And if your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life lame than, having two feet, to be cast into hell.

**9:47** And if your eye causes you to stumble, cast it out; it is better for you to enter into the kingdom of God with one eye than, having two eyes, to be cast into hell,

**9:48** where their worm does not perish, and the fire is never quenched.

**9:49** For everyone shall be salted with fire.

**9:50** Salt is good; yet if the salt loses its saltiness, with what will you season it? Have salt within yourselves, and be at peace with one another."

## Translator Notes

- **9:23** — The Greek definite article before "If You are able" functions as quotation marks echoing the father's plea in verse 22. The earliest manuscripts omit "believe" after "able."
- **9:29** — The earliest manuscripts read simply "by prayer," without the later scribal addition "and fasting."
- **9:41** — Literally "in name, because you are Christ's"; the Greek text omits "My" before "name" and uses the idiom to express belonging to the Messiah as the ground for the kindness shown.
- **9:48** — Alluding to Isaiah 66:24; the earliest manuscripts include this refrain only once here, omitting traditional verses 44 and 46, which repeat it after verses 43 and 45.
- **9:49** — The earliest manuscripts contain only this brief clause, omitting the later addition "and every sacrifice will be salted with salt" (cf. Leviticus 2:13). The imagery evokes testing or purification through trials.
- **9:44 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts (compare 9:48).
- **9:46 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts (compare 9:48).
