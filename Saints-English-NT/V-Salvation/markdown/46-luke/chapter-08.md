## Text

**8:1** Now it came to pass afterward that He journeyed through every city and village, proclaiming and bringing the glad tidings of the kingdom of God, and the twelve were with Him,

**8:2** and certain women who had been healed of evil spirits and infirmities: Mary, who was called Magdalene, out of whom seven demons had gone forth,

**8:3** and Joanna the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward, and Susanna, and many other women who ministered to them out of their own possessions.

**8:4** Now when a great multitude had gathered together, and people from every city were coming to Him, He spoke by a parable:

**8:5** "A sower went forth to sow his seed. And as he sowed, some fell beside the road, and it was trampled underfoot, and the birds of the air devoured it.

**8:6** And some fell upon the rock, and when it had sprung up, it withered away because it had no moisture.

**8:7** And some fell in the midst of the thorns, and the thorns sprang up together with it and choked it.

**8:8** And some fell into the good ground, and having sprung up, it bore fruit a hundredfold." As He spoke these things, He cried aloud, "He who has ears to hear, let him hear!"

**8:9** Then His disciples asked Him what this parable might mean.

**8:10** And He said, "To you it has been granted to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to the rest it is in parables, so that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.

**8:11** Now this is the parable: The seed is the word of God.

**8:12** And those beside the road are they who have heard; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, lest they should believe and be saved.

**8:13** And those upon the rock are they who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; yet these have no root, who believe for a season and in a time of testing fall away.

**8:14** And that which fell among the thorns—these are they who have heard, and as they go on their way they are choked by the cares and riches and pleasures of life, and they bring no fruit to maturity.

**8:15** And that in the good ground—these are they who, having heard the word with a noble and good heart, hold it fast and bear fruit with steadfast endurance.

**8:16** Now no one, when he has lighted a lamp, covers it with a vessel or sets it beneath a bed, but sets it upon a lampstand, so that those who enter may see the light.

**8:17** For there is nothing hidden that shall not become manifest, nor anything concealed that shall not be made known and come into the open.

**8:18** Take heed therefore how you hear; for whoever has, to him it shall be given, and whoever does not have, even what he thinks he has shall be taken away from him."

**8:19** Then His mother and His brethren came to Him, and they could not reach Him because of the multitude.

**8:20** And it was reported to Him, "Your mother and Your brethren are standing outside, desiring to see You."

**8:21** But He answered and said to them, "My mother and My brethren are these who hear the word of God and do it."

**8:22** Now it came to pass on one of those days that He went aboard a boat, He and His disciples, and He said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side of the lake." And they set sail.

**8:23** And as they sailed, He fell asleep. And a fierce windstorm swept down upon the lake, and they were filling with water and were in peril.

**8:24** And they came to Him and awakened Him, saying, "Master, Master, we are perishing!" Then He awoke and rebuked the wind and the surging of the water; and they ceased, and there came a calm.

**8:25** And He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And filled with fear, they marveled, saying to one another, "Who then is this, that He commands even the winds and the water, and they obey Him?"

**8:26** And they sailed to the country of the Gerasenes, which lies opposite Galilee.

**8:27** And when He stepped out onto the land, a certain man from the city met Him who had demons; and for a long time he had worn no clothing, nor did he abide in a house, but in the tombs.

**8:28** And when he saw Jesus, he cried out, fell down before Him, and with a loud voice said, "What have I to do with You, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I implore You, do not torment me!"

**8:29** For He had commanded the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For many times it had seized him, and he was kept under guard, bound with chains and shackles; yet tearing apart the bonds, he was driven by the demon into the desert places.

**8:30** And Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Legion," because many demons had entered into him.

**8:31** And they pleaded with Him not to command them to depart into the abyss.

**8:32** Now a large herd of swine was feeding there on the mountain, and they pleaded with Him to allow them to enter into them. And He gave them permission.

**8:33** Then the demons came out of the man and entered into the swine, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and was drowned.

**8:34** And when those who tended them saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the city and in the countryside.

**8:35** Then the people went out to see what had happened, and came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had departed sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.

**8:36** And those who had seen it reported to them how the man who had been demon-possessed was made well.

**8:37** Then the whole multitude of the surrounding region of the Gerasenes asked Him to depart from them, for they were seized with great fear. So He got into a boat and turned back.

**8:38** Now the man from whom the demons had gone out begged Him that he might be with Him; yet He sent him away, saying,

**8:39** "Return to your own house, and declare what great things God has done for you." And he went his way, proclaiming throughout the whole city what great things Jesus had done for him.

**8:40** Now as Jesus returned, the multitude welcomed Him, for they were all waiting for Him.

**8:41** And behold, there came a man named Jairus, and he was a ruler of the synagogue; and falling at the feet of Jesus, he pleaded with Him to come into his house,

**8:42** for he had an only daughter, about twelve years of age, and she was dying. Now as He went, the multitudes pressed closely around Him.

**8:43** And a woman who had a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her livelihood upon physicians and could not be healed by any,

**8:44** came up from behind and touched the fringe of His garment, and immediately her flow of blood ceased.

**8:45** And Jesus said, "Who is the one who touched Me?" When all denied it, Peter said, "Master, the multitudes crowd around You and press upon You."

**8:46** But Jesus said, "Someone touched Me, for I perceived power going forth from Me."

**8:47** Now when the woman saw that she had not escaped notice, she came trembling, and falling down before Him, she declared before all the people for what reason she had touched Him and how she had been healed immediately.

**8:48** And He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has made you well; go in peace."

**8:49** While He was still speaking, someone came from the house of the ruler of the synagogue, saying, "Your daughter has died; do not trouble the Teacher any longer."

**8:50** But when Jesus heard it, He answered him, "Do not fear; only believe, and she shall be made well."

**8:51** And when He came to the house, He permitted no one to go in with Him except Peter and John and James, and the father and mother of the child.

**8:52** Now all were weeping and mourning for her; but He said, "Do not weep, for she has not died, but is sleeping."

**8:53** And they laughed Him to scorn, knowing that she had died.

**8:54** But He took her by the hand and called aloud, saying, "Child, arise."

**8:55** And her spirit returned, and she arose immediately; and He directed that something be given her to eat.

**8:56** And her parents were astonished, yet He charged them to tell no one what had happened.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — The Greek text reads "to them" (referring to Jesus and the twelve together), whereas some later manuscripts read "to Him."
- **8:26** — The Greek text reads "Gerasenes" (as also in 8:37), whereas other manuscripts read "Gadarenes" or "Gergesenes."
- **8:44** — The Greek word kraspedon refers to the border or tassel (fringe) worn on the corners of a Jewish outer garment in keeping with the Law (Numbers 15:38–39).
- **8:51** — The Greek text places John before James ("Peter and John and James"), following the earliest manuscript tradition.
