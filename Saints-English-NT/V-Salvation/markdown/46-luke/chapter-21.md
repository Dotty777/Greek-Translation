## Text

**21:1** Now looking up, He beheld the rich casting their gifts into the treasury.

**21:2** And He also saw a certain needy widow casting two small copper coins there,

**21:3** and He said, "Truly I say to you, this poor widow has cast in more than all of them;

**21:4** for all these out of their abundance have cast in among the gifts, yet she out of her poverty has cast in all the livelihood that she possessed."

**21:5** And as some were speaking concerning the temple, how it was adorned with fine stones and consecrated gifts, He said,

**21:6** "As for these things which you behold, days are coming in which stone shall not be left upon stone that shall not be torn down."

**21:7** And they questioned Him, saying, "Teacher, when then shall these things be, and what shall be the sign when these things are about to take place?"

**21:8** And He said, "Take heed that you are not led astray; for many shall come in My name, saying, 'I am he,' and, 'The time has drawn near.' Do not go after them.

**21:9** And when you hear of wars and tumults, do not be terrified; for these things must take place first, yet the end does not come immediately."

**21:10** Then He said to them, "Nation shall rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom;

**21:11** and there shall be great earthquakes, and in various places famines and pestilences; and there shall be dreadful terrors and great signs from heaven.

**21:12** Yet before all these things, they shall lay their hands upon you and persecute you, delivering you over to the synagogues and prisons, as you are brought before kings and governors for the sake of My name.

**21:13** It shall turn out for you as a testimony.

**21:14** Resolve therefore in your hearts not to rehearse beforehand how to make a defense,

**21:15** for I Myself will give you a mouth and wisdom which all those who oppose you shall not be able to withstand or contradict.

**21:16** Yet you shall be betrayed even by parents and brothers and kinsmen and friends, and they shall put some of you to death;

**21:17** and you shall be hated by all on account of My name.

**21:18** Yet not a single hair from your head shall ever perish.

**21:19** By your endurance, gain possession of your souls.

**21:20** Now when you see Jerusalem encircled by armies, then know that her desolation has drawn near.

**21:21** Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains, and let those in her midst depart, and let those in the countryside not enter her,

**21:22** because these are days of vengeance, so that all things which are written may be fulfilled.

**21:23** Yet woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days; for there shall be great distress upon the land and wrath against this people.

**21:24** And they shall fall by the edge of the sword and be taken captive into all the nations; and Jerusalem shall be trodden down by Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.

**21:25** And there shall be signs in the sun and moon and stars, and upon the earth anguish of nations in perplexity at the roaring of the sea and the surging waves,

**21:26** as men faint from terror and expectation of the things coming upon the inhabited world, for the powers of the heavens shall be shaken.

**21:27** And then they shall behold the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.

**21:28** Now when these things begin to take place, stand upright and lift up your heads, because your redemption is drawing near."

**21:29** And He spoke a parable to them: "Behold the fig tree and all the trees.

**21:30** When they are already sprouting, seeing it, you know of yourselves that summer is already near.

**21:31** So you also, when you see these things taking place, know that the kingdom of God is near.

**21:32** Assuredly, I say to you that this generation shall by no means pass away until all things have come to pass.

**21:33** Heaven and earth shall pass away, yet My words shall never pass away.

**21:34** Now take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts be weighed down with dissipation and drunkenness and the anxieties of this life, and that day come upon you suddenly

**21:35** like a snare; for it shall come upon all those who dwell upon the face of all the earth.

**21:36** Yet stay awake at all times, praying that you may have strength to escape all these things that are about to take place, and to stand before the Son of Man."

**21:37** Now during the day He was teaching in the temple, yet at night He went out and lodged on the mount called Olivet.

**21:38** And all the people came early in the morning to Him in the temple to hear Him.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Greek lepta, the smallest Jewish copper coins, worth only a tiny fraction of a day's wage.
- **21:19** — The Greek text reads the aorist imperative ktesasthe ("gain possession of"), whereas several manuscripts read the future indicative ktesesthe ("you will gain"); Greek psychas can denote either "souls" or "lives."
- **21:25** — The Greek text reads the genitive noun echous ("at the roaring of the sea and the surging waves"), whereas later manuscripts read the feminine participle echouses ("the sea and the waves roaring").
- **21:36** — The Greek text reads katischysete ("may have strength" or "prevail"), whereas other manuscripts read kataxiothete ("may be counted worthy").
