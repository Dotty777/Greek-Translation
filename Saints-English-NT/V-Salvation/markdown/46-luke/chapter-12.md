## Text

**12:1** Meanwhile, when tens of thousands of the multitude had gathered together, so that they trampled upon one another, He began to say to His disciples first, "Guard yourselves against the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.

**12:2** Yet there is nothing covered over that shall not be revealed, and nothing hidden that shall not be known.

**12:3** Therefore, whatever you have said in the darkness shall be heard in the light, and what you have spoken into the ear in inner chambers shall be proclaimed upon the housetops.

**12:4** Now I say to you, My friends, do not be afraid of those who kill the body, and after these things have nothing further that they can do.

**12:5** But I will point out to you whom you should fear: fear Him who, after killing, has authority to cast into hell; yes, I say to you, fear Him!

**12:6** Are not five sparrows sold for two small copper coins? Yet not a single one of them is forgotten in the sight of God.

**12:7** Indeed, even all the hairs of your head have been numbered. Do not be afraid; you are of greater worth than many sparrows.

**12:8** Now I say to you, everyone who confesses Me before men, the Son of Man also shall confess him before the angels of God;

**12:9** but the one who denies Me in the presence of men shall be disowned in the presence of the angels of God.

**12:10** And everyone who shall speak a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; yet for the one who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven.

**12:11** Now when they bring you before the synagogues and the rulers and the authorities, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak in your defense, or what you should say;

**12:12** for the Holy Spirit shall teach you in that very hour the things you must say."

**12:13** Then someone from the multitude said to Him, "Teacher, instruct my brother to divide the inheritance with me."

**12:14** Yet He said to him, "Man, who appointed Me judge or divider over you?"

**12:15** Then He said to them, "Take heed and keep yourselves from all covetousness, for even when a person has an abundance, his life does not come from his possessions."

**12:16** And He spoke a parable to them, saying, "The land of a certain rich man yielded abundantly.

**12:17** And he reasoned within himself, saying, 'What shall I do, because I have nowhere to gather my crops?'

**12:18** And he said, 'This I will do: I will tear down my barns and build greater ones, and there I will gather all the grain and my goods.

**12:19** And I will say to my soul, "Soul, you have many good things stored up for many years; take your rest, eat, drink, and rejoice."'

**12:20** But God said to him, 'Fool! This very night your soul is demanded of you; and the things you have prepared, whose shall they be?'

**12:21** So it is with the one who stores up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God."

**12:22** And He said to His disciples, "Therefore I say to you, do not be anxious for your life, what you shall eat, nor for your body, what you shall put on.

**12:23** For life is greater than food, and the body greater than clothing.

**12:24** Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap, they have neither storeroom nor barn, yet God nourishes them. How much greater is your worth than that of the birds!

**12:25** And which of you by being anxious is able to add a cubit to his span of life?

**12:26** If then you are unable to do even the smallest thing, why are you anxious about the rest?

**12:27** Consider how the lilies grow: they neither labor nor spin; yet I say to you, not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed like one of these.

**12:28** But if God thus clothes the grass that is in the field today and tomorrow is cast into the oven, how much more shall He clothe you, you of little faith!

**12:29** And as for you, do not seek what you shall eat and what you shall drink, nor be kept in anxious suspense.

**12:30** For all these things the nations of the world eagerly seek, and your Father knows that you have need of these things.

**12:31** Rather, seek His kingdom, and these things shall be added to you.

**12:32** Do not be afraid, little flock, for your Father has been well pleased to give you the kingdom.

**12:33** Sell your possessions and give alms; make for yourselves purses that do not wear out, an unfailing treasure in the heavens, where no thief draws near and no moth destroys.

**12:34** For wherever your treasure is, there also your heart shall be.

**12:35** Keep your loins girded and your lamps burning,

**12:36** and be like men waiting for their master to return from the wedding banquet, so that when he comes and knocks, they may open to him at once.

**12:37** Blessed are those servants whom the master, upon his coming, shall find keeping watch. Assuredly, I say to you, he will gird himself and have them recline at table, and coming forward, he will serve them.

**12:38** And even if he comes in the second watch, or in the third watch, and finds them so, blessed are they!

**12:39** Now know this: if the master of the house had known at what hour the thief was coming, he would not have permitted his house to be broken into.

**12:40** You also must be ready, for at an hour you do not expect, the Son of Man is coming."

**12:41** Then Peter said, "Lord, are You speaking this parable to us, or also to all?"

**12:42** And the Lord said, "Who then is the faithful and prudent steward, whom the master shall appoint over his household, to give them their allowance of food at the proper time?

**12:43** Blessed is that servant whom his master, upon his coming, shall find doing so.

**12:44** Truly, I say to you, he shall appoint him over all his possessions.

**12:45** Yet if that servant says in his heart, 'My master delays his coming,' and begins to strike the menservants and maidservants, and to eat and drink and grow drunk,

**12:46** the master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour he does not know, and will cut him in two and assign his portion with the unfaithful.

**12:47** And that servant who knew his master's will, yet did not make ready or act according to his will, shall be beaten with many blows;

**12:48** but the one who did not know, yet did things worthy of blows, shall be beaten with few. For to everyone to whom much was given, from him much shall be required; and to the one to whom much was entrusted, of him they will demand even more.

**12:49** I have come to cast fire upon the earth, and how I long that it were already kindled!

**12:50** Yet I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how greatly I am constrained until it is brought to completion!

**12:51** Do you imagine that I came to give peace upon the earth? No, I say to you, but rather division.

**12:52** For from now on, five in one household shall be divided, three against two and two against three;

**12:53** they shall be divided, father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law."

**12:54** Then He also said to the multitudes, "Whenever you see a cloud rising in the west, at once you say, 'A rainstorm is coming,' and so it comes to pass.

**12:55** And when a south wind is blowing, you say, 'There will be scorching heat,' and so it happens.

**12:56** Hypocrites! You know how to discern the appearance of the earth and of the sky, yet how is it that you do not know how to discern this present time?

**12:57** And why do you not also judge for yourselves what is right?

**12:58** For as you go with your adversary before a magistrate, take pains along the way to be released from him, lest he drag you to the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer cast you into prison.

**12:59** I say to you, you shall by no means come out from there until you have paid even the very last mite."

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — Greek Gehenna, originally the Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem, used as the name for the place of final judgment.
- **12:20** — Literally "they demand your soul from you," an impersonal third-person plural idiom equivalent to the passive "your soul is demanded of you."
- **12:25** — Greek helikia can refer either to a person's "span of life" or to physical "stature"; pechys ("cubit," about eighteen inches) is used idiomatically for a small measure.
- **12:29** — Greek meteorizesthe, occurring only here in the New Testament, literally means to be lifted up or tossed about in midair, hence to be held in anxious suspense or troubled in mind.
- **12:46** — Greek apiston can mean either "the unfaithful" (contrasting with the faithful steward of 12:42) or "the unbelievers."
- **12:49** — Literally "and what do I wish if it were already kindled?", a Greek idiom expressing ardent longing.
- **12:59** — Greek lepton, the smallest Jewish copper coin in circulation, worth half a Roman quadrans.
