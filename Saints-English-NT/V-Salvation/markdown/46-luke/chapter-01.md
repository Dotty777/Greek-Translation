## Text

**1:1** Seeing that many have undertaken to arrange an account concerning the things that have been fully accomplished among us,

**1:2** just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us,

**1:3** it seemed fitting to me also, having closely followed all things from the very first, to write to you in orderly sequence, most noble Theophilus,

**1:4** that you may fully know the certainty concerning the words in which you were instructed.

**1:5** In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a certain priest by the name of Zechariah, of the priestly division of Abijah. He had a wife from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.

**1:6** And both of them were righteous in the sight of God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and statutes of the Lord.

**1:7** Yet they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both of them were well advanced in their days.

**1:8** Now it came to pass, while he was serving in the priestly office before God in the order of his division,

**1:9** that according to the custom of the priesthood, it fell to him by lot to burn incense upon entering the sanctuary of the Lord.

**1:10** And the entire multitude of the people was outside praying at the hour of the incense offering.

**1:11** And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing on the right of the altar of incense.

**1:12** And when Zechariah beheld him, he was troubled, and fear fell upon him.

**1:13** But the angel said to him, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your supplication has been heard; and your wife Elizabeth shall bear you a son, and you shall call his name John.

**1:14** And joy and exultation shall be yours, and many shall rejoice at his birth.

**1:15** For he shall be great before the Lord, and he shall never drink wine or strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother's womb.

**1:16** And he shall turn many of the sons of Israel back to the Lord their God.

**1:17** And he himself shall go forth before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers toward their children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a prepared people."

**1:18** And Zechariah said to the angel, "By what means shall I know this? For I am an aged man, and my wife is well advanced in her days."

**1:19** And answering, the angel said to him, "I am Gabriel, who stands before God; and I was sent to speak with you and to declare to you these good tidings.

**1:20** And behold, you shall be silent and unable to speak until the day these things come to pass, because you did not believe my words, which shall be fulfilled in their appointed season."

**1:21** And the people were waiting expectantly for Zechariah, and they marveled that he lingered so long in the sanctuary.

**1:22** Yet when he came out, he could not speak to them, and they recognized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary; and he kept making signs to them and remained speechless.

**1:23** And it came to pass, when the days of his ministry were fulfilled, that he departed to his own house.

**1:24** Now after those days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she kept herself in seclusion for five months, saying,

**1:25** "Thus the Lord has done for me in the days in which He looked upon me, to take away my reproach among men."

**1:26** And in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent forth from God to a city of Galilee whose name was Nazareth,

**1:27** to a virgin betrothed to a man named Joseph, from the house of David; and the name of the virgin was Mary.

**1:28** And the angel came in to her and said, "Rejoice, O favored one! The Lord is with you."

**1:29** But she was greatly troubled at this saying, and pondered what manner of greeting this might be.

**1:30** And the angel said to her, "Do not fear, Mary, for you have found favor before God.

**1:31** And behold, you shall conceive in your womb and give birth to a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus.

**1:32** He shall be great and shall be called the Son of the Most High; and the Lord God shall give to Him the throne of His father David,

**1:33** and He shall reign over the house of Jacob forevermore, and to His kingdom there shall be no end."

**1:34** But Mary said to the angel, "How shall this come to pass, since I do not know a man?"

**1:35** And answering, the angel said to her, "The Holy Spirit shall come upon you, and the power of the Most High shall overshadow you; therefore also the Child to be born shall be called holy, the Son of God.

**1:36** And behold, Elizabeth your kinswoman has herself also conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month with her who was called barren;

**1:37** for no word from God shall be impossible."

**1:38** And Mary said, "Behold, the handmaid of the Lord; may it be to me according to your word." And the angel departed from her.

**1:39** In those days Mary arose and journeyed with haste into the hill country, to a city of Judah,

**1:40** and she entered into the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.

**1:41** And it came to pass, as soon as Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, that the babe leaped within her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

**1:42** And she exclaimed with a loud voice and said, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!

**1:43** And how has this come about for me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

**1:44** For behold, the moment the voice of your greeting came to my ears, the babe leaped within my womb in exultation.

**1:45** And blessed is she who believed that there shall be a fulfillment of the things spoken to her from the Lord."

**1:46** And Mary said, "My soul magnifies the Lord,

**1:47** and my spirit has exulted in God my Savior;

**1:48** for He has looked upon the lowliness of His handmaid; for behold, from this time onward all generations shall call me blessed.

**1:49** For the Mighty One has done great things for me, and His name is holy;

**1:50** and His mercy extends to generations and generations upon those who fear Him.

**1:51** He has shown might with His arm; He has scattered the proud in the intent of their heart.

**1:52** He has cast down rulers from thrones and exalted the humble;

**1:53** the hungry He has filled with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty.

**1:54** He has come to the aid of Israel His servant, to remember mercy,

**1:55** just as He spoke to our forefathers, to Abraham and to his seed forevermore."

**1:56** Now Mary remained with her for about three months, and then returned to her own house.

**1:57** Now Elizabeth's time to give birth was fulfilled, and she brought forth a son.

**1:58** And her neighbors and kinsfolk heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy toward her, and they rejoiced together with her.

**1:59** And it came to pass, on the eighth day, that they came to circumcise the child; and they were about to name him Zechariah, after the name of his father.

**1:60** And his mother answered and said, "By no means; rather, he shall be called John."

**1:61** And they said to her, "Not one of your kindred is called by this name."

**1:62** Then they made signs to his father as to what he wished him to be named.

**1:63** And he asked for a writing tablet and wrote, saying, "John is his name." And all of them marveled.

**1:64** And at once his mouth was opened and his tongue was freed, and he began to speak, blessing God.

**1:65** And fear came upon all those dwelling around them; and throughout the entire hill country of Judea all these things were talked about,

**1:66** and all who heard laid them up in their hearts, saying, "What then shall this child be?" For indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.

**1:67** Then Zechariah his father was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,

**1:68** "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for He has visited and brought redemption to His people,

**1:69** and has raised up for us a horn of salvation in the house of David His servant,

**1:70** just as He spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from ages past,

**1:71** salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

**1:72** to show mercy toward our forefathers and to remember His holy covenant,

**1:73** the oath which He swore to Abraham our father, to grant to us

**1:74** that, delivered from the hand of enemies, we might serve Him without fear

**1:75** in holiness and righteousness in His presence all our days.

**1:76** And you also, little child, shall be called prophet of the Most High; for you shall go forth before the Lord to make ready His ways,

**1:77** to give the knowledge of salvation to His people in the remission of their sins,

**1:78** because of the tender mercy of our God, by which the Dawn from on high shall visit us,

**1:79** to shine upon those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, to direct our feet into the way of peace."

**1:80** And the child grew and became strong in spirit, and he was in the desert places until the day of his public appearing to Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **1:22** — The Greek adjective kophos rendered "speechless" can denote being mute, deaf, or both (compare 1:62, where signs are also made to Zechariah).
- **1:28** — The participle kecharitomene ("O favored one") literally means "one endowed with grace." Later manuscripts add "blessed are you among women" (likely assimilated from 1:42), which is absent from the earliest Greek witnesses followed here.
- **1:35** — The Greek clause may be construed either with "holy" as predicate ("the Child to be born shall be called holy, the Son of God") or as part of the subject ("that Holy One to be born shall be called the Son of God").
- **1:37** — Literally "no word from God shall be impossible" (or "void of power"), echoing Genesis 18:14 in the Septuagint; it may also be understood idiomatically as "nothing shall be impossible with God."
- **1:45** — The Greek conjunction hoti may be read either as introducing the content of Mary's faith ("believed that there shall be a fulfillment") or as causal ("believed, for there shall be a fulfillment").
- **1:51** — The past-tense (aorist) verbs in verses 51–54 reflect the biblical prophetic idiom of celebrating God's decisive saving works as already accomplished and certain.
- **1:78** — The Greek noun anatole ("Dawn" or "Dayspring") denotes the rising sun and also renders the Messianic title "Branch" in the Septuagint (e.g., Zechariah 3:8; 6:12). The earliest Greek manuscripts followed here read the future tense "shall visit," while later witnesses read "has visited."
