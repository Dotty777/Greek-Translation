## Text

**2:1** Now it came to pass in those days that a decree went forth from Caesar Augustus for all the inhabited world to be enrolled.

**2:2** (This first enrollment took place while Quirinius was governor of Syria.)

**2:3** And all were going to be enrolled, each one to his own city.

**2:4** And Joseph also ascended from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to the city of David, which is named Bethlehem, because he belonged to the house and lineage of David,

**2:5** to be enrolled with Mary, who was betrothed to him, being with child.

**2:6** Now it came to pass, while they were there, that the days were fulfilled for her to give birth.

**2:7** And she gave birth to her firstborn Son, and wrapped Him in swaddling bands, and laid Him down in a manger, because there was no room for them in the lodging place.

**2:8** Now in that same region there were shepherds abiding in the open fields, keeping the watches of the night over their flock.

**2:9** And an angel of the Lord stood beside them, and the glory of the Lord shone all around them, and they feared with a great fear.

**2:10** And the angel said to them, "Do not fear, for behold, I proclaim to you glad tidings of great joy, which shall be for all the people,

**2:11** for to you is born this day a Savior, who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David;

**2:12** and this shall be the sign for you: you shall find an infant wrapped in swaddling bands and lying in a manger."

**2:13** And suddenly there came to be with the angel a multitude of the host of heaven, praising God and saying,

**2:14** "Glory to God in the highest, and upon earth peace among men of His good pleasure."

**2:15** And it happened, when the angels had departed from them into heaven, that the shepherds were saying to one another, "Let us go across now to Bethlehem and behold this matter that has taken place, which the Lord has made known to us."

**2:16** And they came in haste and found both Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in the manger.

**2:17** And having seen, they made known concerning the word that had been spoken to them about this Child.

**2:18** And all who heard marveled concerning the things spoken to them by the shepherds,

**2:19** but Mary treasured all these words, pondering them in her heart.

**2:20** And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all that they had heard and seen, just as it had been spoken to them.

**2:21** And when eight days were fulfilled to circumcise Him, His name was called Jesus, as He had been named by the angel before He was conceived in the womb.

**2:22** And when the days of their purification were fulfilled according to the Law of Moses, they brought Him up to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord,

**2:23** just as it is written in the law of the Lord, "Every male that opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord,"

**2:24** and to offer a sacrifice according to what has been spoken in the law of the Lord: a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.

**2:25** And behold, in Jerusalem there was a man whose name was Simeon, and this man was righteous and reverent, looking for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.

**2:26** And it had been divinely revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he should see the Christ of the Lord.

**2:27** And he entered the temple in the Spirit; and as the parents brought in the Child Jesus, that they might do concerning Him according to the custom of the law,

**2:28** he himself took Him into his arms, blessed God, and said,

**2:29** "Now You dismiss Your servant, O Sovereign Lord, according to Your word, in peace;

**2:30** for my eyes have beheld Your salvation,

**2:31** which You have made ready in the presence of all the peoples:

**2:32** a light for revelation to the Gentiles, and glory for Your people Israel."

**2:33** And His father and His mother were marveling at the things being spoken concerning Him.

**2:34** And Simeon blessed them and said to Mary His mother, "Behold, this Child is set for the fall and rising up of many in Israel, and for a sign that is spoken against—

**2:35** and a sword shall pierce through your own soul also—so that the thoughts out of many hearts may be revealed."

**2:36** And there was Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher (she was well advanced in years, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity,

**2:37** and she had been a widow up to eighty-four years of age), who did not depart from the temple, worshiping with fastings and supplications night and day.

**2:38** And coming up at that very hour, she gave thanks to God and spoke concerning Him to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.

**2:39** And when they had completed everything according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city of Nazareth.

**2:40** And the Child grew and increased in strength, being filled with wisdom; and the grace of God was upon Him.

**2:41** Now His parents traveled every year to Jerusalem at the Feast of the Passover.

**2:42** And when He became twelve years old, as they went up according to the custom of the feast

**2:43** and had completed the days, as they were returning, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, and His parents did not know it.

**2:44** But supposing Him to be in the company of travelers, they went a day's journey and were searching for Him among their kinsfolk and acquaintances.

**2:45** And when they did not find Him, they turned back to Jerusalem, searching for Him.

**2:46** And it came to pass after three days that they found Him in the temple, seated in the midst of the teachers, hearing them and questioning them;

**2:47** and all who were listening to Him were astounded at His discernment and His replies.

**2:48** And when they saw Him, they were astonished; and His mother said to Him, "Child, why have You treated us like this? Behold, Your father and I have been searching for You in distress."

**2:49** And He said to them, "Why is it that you were seeking Me? Did you not know that I must be in the house of My Father?"

**2:50** Yet they did not comprehend the saying that He spoke to them.

**2:51** And He went down with them and came to Nazareth, and He continued in subjection to them. And His mother treasured up all the sayings in her heart.

**2:52** And Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and with men.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek adjective rendered "first" can also be taken comparatively as "prior," indicating an enrollment made before Quirinius was governing Syria.
- **2:14** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read the genitive "of good pleasure" (referring to those upon whom God's favor rests), whereas later manuscripts read the nominative "goodwill."
- **2:22** — The critical Greek text reads the plural "their purification," referring to Mary and the Child (or the household), while some later manuscripts read "her purification."
- **2:33** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read "His father and His mother," whereas later manuscripts substitute "Joseph" for "His father."
- **2:49** — Literally "in the things of My Father," a Greek idiom that can mean either "in the house of My Father" or "about My Father's affairs."
