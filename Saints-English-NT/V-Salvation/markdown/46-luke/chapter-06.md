## Text

**6:1** Now it came to pass on a Sabbath that He was passing through the grainfields, and His disciples were plucking the heads of grain and eating them, rubbing them with their hands.

**6:2** And certain of the Pharisees said, "Why are you doing that which is not lawful on the Sabbath?"

**6:3** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Have you not even read this, what David did when he was hungry, he and those who were with him,

**6:4** how he entered into the house of God, and taking the bread of the Presence, ate it and gave it to those with him, which it is not lawful for any to eat except the priests alone?"

**6:5** And He told them, "The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath."

**6:6** Now it came to pass on another Sabbath that He entered the synagogue and taught; and there was a man there, and his right hand was withered.

**6:7** And the scribes and the Pharisees watched Him closely, whether He would heal on the Sabbath, that they might find grounds to accuse Him.

**6:8** But He knew their thoughts, and said to the man who had the withered hand, "Arise and stand in the midst." And rising up, he stood there.

**6:9** Then Jesus said to them, "I ask you, is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save a life or to destroy it?"

**6:10** And having looked around at them all, He said to him, "Stretch out your hand." And he did so, and his hand was restored.

**6:11** But they were filled with fury, and conferred with one another concerning what they might do to Jesus.

**6:12** Now it came to pass in those days that He went out to the mountain to pray, and He continued through the night in prayer to God.

**6:13** And when day came, He called His disciples to Himself; and from them He chose twelve, whom He also named apostles:

**6:14** Simon, whom He also named Peter, and Andrew his brother, and James and John, and Philip and Bartholomew,

**6:15** and Matthew and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Simon who was called the Zealot,

**6:16** and Judas the son of James, and Judas Iscariot, who became a traitor.

**6:17** And coming down with them, He stood on a level place, together with a great crowd of His disciples and a great multitude of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and from the seacoast of Tyre and Sidon,

**6:18** who came to hear Him and to be healed of their diseases; and those who were troubled by unclean spirits were cured.

**6:19** And all the crowd sought to touch Him, for power was going forth from Him and healing them all.

**6:20** And raising His eyes toward His disciples, He said, "Blessed are you who are poor, for yours is the kingdom of God.

**6:21** Blessed are you who hunger now, for you shall be satisfied. Blessed are you who weep now, for you shall laugh.

**6:22** Blessed are you when men hate you, and when they exclude you, and reproach you, and cast out your name as evil on account of the Son of Man.

**6:23** Rejoice in that day and leap for joy, for behold, your reward is great in heaven; for in like manner their fathers treated the prophets.

**6:24** Yet woe to you who are rich, for you have received your consolation in full.

**6:25** Woe to you who are full now, for you shall hunger. Woe to you who laugh now, for you shall mourn and weep.

**6:26** Woe when all men speak well of you, for in like manner their fathers treated the false prophets.

**6:27** But I say to you who hear: Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you,

**6:28** bless those who curse you, pray for those who mistreat you.

**6:29** To the one who strikes you on the cheek, present the other also; and from the one who takes your cloak, do not keep back your tunic as well.

**6:30** Give to all who ask of you, and from the one who takes what belongs to you, do not demand it back.

**6:31** And just as you desire that men should do to you, so do to them likewise.

**6:32** And if you love those who love you, what credit is yours? For even sinners love those who love them.

**6:33** For indeed if you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is yours? Even sinners do the same.

**6:34** And if you lend to those from whom you hope to receive, what credit is yours? Even sinners lend to sinners, that they may receive back an equal amount.

**6:35** Yet love your enemies, and do good, and lend, expecting nothing in return; and your reward shall be great, and you shall be sons of the Most High, for He Himself is kind to the ungrateful and wicked.

**6:36** Be compassionate, just as your Father is compassionate.

**6:37** And do not judge, and you shall by no means be judged; and do not condemn, and you shall by no means be condemned. Pardon, and you shall be pardoned;

**6:38** give, and it shall be given to you; a good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and overflowing, they shall pour into your lap. For by the measure with which you measure, it shall be measured back to you."

**6:39** And He also spoke a parable to them: "Can a blind man guide a blind man? Will not both of them fall into a pit?

**6:40** A disciple is not above the teacher, yet everyone who is fully trained shall be like his teacher.

**6:41** And why do you look at the speck that is in your brother's eye, yet do not consider the beam that is in your own eye?

**6:42** How can you say to your brother, 'Brother, let me remove the speck that is in your eye,' when you yourself do not see the beam in your own eye? Hypocrite! First cast the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to remove the speck that is in your brother's eye.

**6:43** For no good tree produces corrupt fruit, nor again does a corrupt tree produce good fruit.

**6:44** For each tree is known by its own fruit; for men do not gather figs from thorns, nor do they harvest grapes from a bramble bush.

**6:45** The good man out of the good treasure of the heart brings forth what is good, and the evil man out of evil brings forth what is evil; for out of the overflow of the heart his mouth speaks.

**6:46** Now why do you call Me, 'Lord, Lord,' and do not do what I say?

**6:47** As for everyone who comes to Me and hears My words and does them, I will show you whom he is like:

**6:48** he is like a man building a house, who dug down deep and laid a foundation upon the rock; and when a flood arose, the river dashed against that house, and could not shake it, because it had been well built.

**6:49** Yet the one who heard and did not act is like a man who built a house upon the ground without a foundation, against which the river dashed; and immediately it collapsed, and great was the ruin of that house."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Early critical manuscripts read simply "on a Sabbath"; later manuscripts add the obscure Greek term deuteroproto ("second-first"), whose meaning remains uncertain.
- **6:16** — The Greek literally reads "Judas of James"; while traditionally rendered "son of James," some understand the genitive to mean "brother of James."
- **6:35** — The Greek phrase meden apelpizontes can be understood either as "expecting nothing in return" or as "despairing of nothing" (while a manuscript variant medena yields "causing no one to despair").
- **6:48** — The critical Greek text reads "because it had been well built," whereas later manuscripts harmonize with Matthew 7:25 by reading "for it was founded upon the rock."
