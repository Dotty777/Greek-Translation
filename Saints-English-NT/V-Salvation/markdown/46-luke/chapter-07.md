## Text

**7:1** Now when He had completed all His words in the hearing of the people, He entered into Capernaum.

**7:2** And the servant of a certain centurion was sick and about to die, who was precious to him.

**7:3** When he heard of Jesus, he sent elders of the Jews to Him, requesting that He come and restore his servant to health.

**7:4** And having come to Jesus, they urged Him earnestly, saying, "He is worthy that You should grant him this,

**7:5** for he loves our nation, and he himself built the synagogue for us."

**7:6** So Jesus went along with them. And when He was already not far distant from the house, the centurion sent friends, saying to Him, "Lord, do not trouble Yourself, for I am not worthy that You should come under my roof.

**7:7** Therefore I did not even account myself worthy to come to You; only speak a word, and let my servant be healed.

**7:8** For I also am a man appointed under authority, having soldiers under me; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and to another, 'Come,' and he comes; and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**7:9** When Jesus heard these things, He marveled at him, and turning to the multitude following Him, He said, "I say to you, not even in Israel have I found so great a faith."

**7:10** And when those who had been sent returned to the house, they found the servant well.

**7:11** And it came to pass soon afterward that He journeyed to a city called Nain, and His disciples and a great multitude went with Him.

**7:12** As He drew near to the gate of the city, behold, a man who had died was being carried out—the only son of his mother, and she was a widow; and a sizable multitude from the city was with her.

**7:13** When the Lord saw her, He was moved with compassion for her and said to her, "Do not weep."

**7:14** Then He came forward and touched the bier, and those bearing it stood still. And He said, "Young man, I say to you, rise."

**7:15** And the dead man sat up and began to speak, and He gave him back to his mother.

**7:16** Then fear seized them all, and they glorified God, saying, "A great prophet has been raised up among us," and, "God has visited His people."

**7:17** And this report concerning Him went forth throughout all Judea and all the surrounding countryside.

**7:18** Then John's disciples brought word to him concerning all these things. And summoning two of his disciples, John

**7:19** sent them to Jesus, saying, "Are You the One who is to come, or should we look for another?"

**7:20** When the men came to Him, they said, "John the Baptist has sent us to You, saying, 'Are You the One who is to come, or should we look for another?'"

**7:21** In that very hour He healed many of sicknesses, afflictions, and evil spirits, and to many who were blind He graciously granted sight.

**7:22** And He answered and said to them, "Go and report to John the things which you have seen and heard: the blind recover sight, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised, the poor have the gospel proclaimed to them.

**7:23** And blessed is the one who is not offended on account of Me."

**7:24** After the messengers of John had departed, He began to speak to the multitudes about John: "What did you go out into the wilderness to behold? A reed swayed by the wind?

**7:25** But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft garments? Behold, those who wear splendid attire and live in luxury are in royal palaces.

**7:26** But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and far more than a prophet.

**7:27** This is he concerning whom it is written: 'Behold, I send forth My messenger before Your face, who shall prepare Your way before You.'

**7:28** I say to you, among those born of women no one is greater than John; yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he."

**7:29** (And all the people when they heard, including the tax collectors, vindicated God, having been baptized with the baptism of John;

**7:30** but the Pharisees and the lawyers rejected the counsel of God for themselves, not having been baptized by him.)

**7:31** "To what then shall I compare the people of this generation, and what are they like?

**7:32** They are like children sitting in the marketplace and calling out to one another, who say, 'We played the flute for you, and you did not dance; we sang a dirge, and you did not weep.'

**7:33** For John the Baptist has come eating no bread and drinking no wine, and you say, 'He has a demon.'

**7:34** The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, 'Behold, a gluttonous man and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!'

**7:35** Yet wisdom is vindicated by all her children."

**7:36** Now one of the Pharisees invited Him to dine with him; and entering the Pharisee's house, He reclined at table.

**7:37** And behold, a woman of the city who was a sinner, upon learning that He was reclining at table in the Pharisee's house, brought an alabaster jar of fragrant ointment,

**7:38** and standing behind Him at His feet, weeping, she began to wet His feet with her tears and wiped them with the hair of her head; and she kept kissing His feet and anointing them with the ointment.

**7:39** Now when the Pharisee who had invited Him saw this, he said within himself, "If this Man were a prophet, He would know who and what sort of woman this is who touches Him, for she is a sinner."

**7:40** And Jesus answered him, "Simon, I have something to tell you." And he said, "Teacher, say it."

**7:41** "A certain moneylender had two debtors: one owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty.

**7:42** Since they had nothing with which to pay, he graciously forgave them both. Which of them, therefore, will love him more?"

**7:43** Simon answered and said, "I suppose the one to whom he forgave the greater amount." And He said to him, "You have judged rightly."

**7:44** Then turning toward the woman, He said to Simon, "Do you see this woman? I entered your house; you did not give Me water for My feet, yet she has wet My feet with her tears and wiped them with her hair.

**7:45** You did not give Me a kiss; yet she, from the moment I came in, has not ceased kissing My feet.

**7:46** You did not anoint My head with oil; yet she has anointed My feet with fragrant ointment.

**7:47** Therefore I say to you, her many sins have been forgiven, for she has loved much; but the one who is forgiven little loves little."

**7:48** Then He said to her, "Your sins have been forgiven."

**7:49** And those reclining at table with Him began to say among themselves, "Who is this, who even forgives sins?"

**7:50** And He said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you; go forth in peace."

## Translator Notes

- **7:11** — The Greek phrase literally means "soon afterward" (in the time following); some manuscripts read the feminine dative, meaning "on the next day."
- **7:19** — The Greek text here reads "to Jesus," whereas some ancient manuscripts read "to the Lord."
- **7:29** — "Vindicated God" translates Greek edikaiosan ton theon (literally "justified God"), meaning they acknowledged God's righteousness; the same verb recurs in verse 35 ("wisdom is vindicated").
- **7:47** — Greek hoti ("for") may be taken evidentially ("as shown by the fact that she has loved much," harmonizing with the parable in verses 41-43) or causally; "for" retains the Greek structure.
