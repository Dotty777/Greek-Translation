## Text

**11:1** And it came to pass, while He was praying in a certain place, that when He had finished, one of His disciples said to Him, "Lord, teach us to pray, just as John also taught his disciples."

**11:2** Then He said to them, "Whenever you pray, say, 'Father, hallowed be Your name; let Your kingdom come.

**11:3** Grant us our daily bread day by day.

**11:4** And forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone indebted to us; and do not bring us into temptation.'"

**11:5** And He said to them, "Which one of you shall have a friend, and go to him at midnight and say to him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves,

**11:6** since a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him';

**11:7** and he from within will answer and say, 'Do not cause me trouble; the door has already been shut, and my children are with me in bed; I cannot rise and give to you'?

**11:8** I say to you, even though he will not rise and give to him because he is his friend, yet because of his shameless persistence he will arise and give him as many as he needs.

**11:9** And I also say to you, ask, and it shall be given to you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you.

**11:10** For whoever asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks it shall be opened.

**11:11** Now which father among you, when his son asks for a fish, will give him a serpent instead of a fish?

**11:12** Or if he also asks for an egg, will he give him a scorpion?

**11:13** If you then, though you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more shall the Father from heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!"

**11:14** Now He was casting out a mute demon; and it came to pass, when the demon had gone out, that the mute man spoke, and the multitudes marveled.

**11:15** But some of them said, "By Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons, He casts out the demons."

**11:16** And others, testing Him, were seeking from Him a sign from heaven.

**11:17** But He, knowing their thoughts, said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is made desolate, and house falls upon house.

**11:18** And if Satan also is divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand? For you say that I cast out the demons by Beelzebul.

**11:19** Yet if I cast out the demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they themselves shall be your judges.

**11:20** But if it is by the finger of God that I cast out the demons, then the kingdom of God has indeed come upon you.

**11:21** When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own palace, his possessions are in peace.

**11:22** But when one stronger than he comes upon him and overcomes him, he takes away his full armor in which he trusted, and distributes his spoils.

**11:23** The one who is not with Me is against Me, and the one who does not gather with Me scatters.

**11:24** When the unclean spirit goes out of a man, it passes through waterless places seeking rest; and finding none, it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came out.'

**11:25** And when it comes, it finds it swept and put in order.

**11:26** Then it goes and takes along seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and they enter in and dwell there; and the last state of that man becomes worse than the first."

**11:27** And it came to pass, as He spoke these things, that a certain woman from the multitude raised her voice and said to Him, "Blessed is the womb that bore You, and the breasts at which You nursed!"

**11:28** But He said, "Yes, rather, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!"

**11:29** Now as the multitudes were crowding together, He began to say, "This generation is a wicked generation; it seeks a sign, and no sign shall be given to it except the sign of Jonah.

**11:30** For just as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so also shall the Son of Man be to this generation.

**11:31** The queen of the South shall rise up at the judgment with the men of this generation and condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, something greater than Solomon is here.

**11:32** The men of Nineveh shall stand up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and behold, something greater than Jonah is here.

**11:33** No one, when he has lit a lamp, places it in a hidden vault or under a measuring basket, but on the lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.

**11:34** Your eye is the lamp of the body. When your eye is sound, your whole body also is full of light; but when it is evil, your body also is full of darkness.

**11:35** Take heed therefore, lest the light within you be darkness.

**11:36** If then your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it shall be wholly radiant, as when the lamp illuminates you with its bright rays."

**11:37** Now as He spoke, a Pharisee asked Him to dine with him; and He went in and reclined at table.

**11:38** And when the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that He had not first washed before the meal.

**11:39** Then the Lord said to him, "Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish, yet your inward part is full of extortion and wickedness.

**11:40** Foolish ones! Did not the One who made the outside make the inside also?

**11:41** Rather, give as alms those things that are within, and behold, all things are clean to you.

**11:42** But woe to you Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every garden herb, yet pass by justice and the love of God. These you ought to have done, without neglecting the others.

**11:43** Woe to you Pharisees! For you love the foremost seat in the synagogues and greetings in the marketplaces.

**11:44** Woe to you! For you are like unmarked graves, and the men who walk over them do not know it."

**11:45** Then one of the lawyers answered and said to Him, "Teacher, in saying these things You insult us also."

**11:46** And He said, "Woe also to you lawyers! For you load men with burdens hard to bear, yet you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.

**11:47** Woe to you! For you build the tombs of the prophets, yet your fathers slew them.

**11:48** Therefore you are witnesses and consent to the deeds of your fathers; for they indeed slew them, and you build.

**11:49** For this reason also the wisdom of God said, 'I will send to them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will slay and persecute,'

**11:50** so that the blood of all the prophets, which was shed from the foundation of the world, may be required of this generation,

**11:51** from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the house. Yes, I say to you, it shall be required of this generation!

**11:52** Woe to you lawyers! For you have taken away the key of knowledge; you did not enter in yourselves, and those who were entering in you hindered."

**11:53** And when He departed from there, the scribes and the Pharisees began to assail Him fiercely and to cross-examine Him about many things,

**11:54** lying in wait for Him, to catch something out of His mouth.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The critical Greek text preserves Luke's shorter form of the Lord's Prayer, without the longer petitions harmonized from Matthew 6:9-13 in later manuscripts.
- **11:3** — The rare Greek adjective epiousios (rendered "daily") appears in the New Testament only in the Lord's Prayer and is variously understood as "daily," "for the coming day," or "necessary for subsistence."
- **11:8** — The Greek noun anaideia (rendered "shameless persistence") literally means "shamelessness" or "bold impudence," emphasizing the friend's uninhibited insistence at midnight.
- **11:17** — The concise Greek clause oikos epi oikon piptei literally reads "house falls upon house," depicting either the collapse of one house upon another in a ruined kingdom or a household divided against itself.
- **11:41** — The Greek expression ta enonta ("those things that are within") may refer either to the contents inside the cup and dish or to the inner realities of the heart.
