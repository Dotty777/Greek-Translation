## Text

**17:1** Then He said to His disciples, "It is impossible that occasions of stumbling should not come, yet woe to the one through whom they come.

**17:2** It would profit him more if a millstone were hung around his neck and he were cast into the sea, than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.

**17:3** Keep watch over yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him.

**17:4** And if seven times in the day he sins against you, and seven times returns to you, saying, 'I repent,' you shall forgive him."

**17:5** And the apostles said to the Lord, "Add to our faith."

**17:6** Then the Lord said, "If you have faith like a grain of mustard seed, you would say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.

**17:7** And which of you, having a servant plowing or tending sheep, will say to him when he comes in from the field, 'Come at once and recline at table,'

**17:8** and will not instead say to him, 'Prepare what I shall eat for supper, and gird yourself and wait on me until I have eaten and drunk; and after that you yourself shall eat and drink'?

**17:9** Does he give thanks to the servant because he did the things commanded?

**17:10** In the same way you also, when you have carried out all the things commanded you, say, 'We are unprofitable servants; we have performed what it was our duty to do.'"

**17:11** And it came to pass, as He was journeying to Jerusalem, that He passed between Samaria and Galilee.

**17:12** And as He was entering a certain village, ten leprous men met Him, who stood afar off.

**17:13** And they raised their voices, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"

**17:14** And seeing them, He said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." And it came to pass that while they were going, they were cleansed.

**17:15** Now one of them, seeing that he had been healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice,

**17:16** and he fell upon his face at His feet, giving thanks to Him; and he was a Samaritan.

**17:17** Then Jesus answered and said, "Were not the ten cleansed? Where, then, are the nine?

**17:18** Were none found who turned back to give glory to God except this foreigner?"

**17:19** Then He said to him, "Rise and go your way; your faith has made you whole."

**17:20** Now being questioned by the Pharisees as to when the kingdom of God is coming, He answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation,

**17:21** nor shall they say, 'Behold, here!' or, 'There!' For behold, the kingdom of God is within you."

**17:22** Then He said to the disciples, "Days will come when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you shall not see it.

**17:23** And they will say to you, 'Behold, there!' or, 'Behold, here!' Do not go out, nor pursue them.

**17:24** For just as the lightning flashes from one end under heaven and shines to the other end under heaven, so shall the Son of Man be in His day.

**17:25** First, however, He must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

**17:26** And just as it came to pass in the days of Noah, so shall it also be in the days of the Son of Man:

**17:27** they were eating, drinking, marrying, being given in marriage, until the day Noah entered the ark, and the flood came and destroyed all of them.

**17:28** Likewise, just as it came to pass in the days of Lot: they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building;

**17:29** yet on the day Lot departed from Sodom, it rained fire and brimstone from heaven and destroyed all of them.

**17:30** Just so shall it be on the day that the Son of Man is revealed.

**17:31** On that day, let the one who is upon the housetop, whose belongings are in the house, not come down to carry them off; and likewise let the one who is in the field not turn back to what is behind.

**17:32** Remember the wife of Lot.

**17:33** Whoever seeks to preserve his life shall lose it, and whoever loses it shall keep it alive.

**17:34** I say to you, on that night there shall be two upon one bed: one shall be taken, and the other shall be left;

**17:35** there shall be two women grinding together: one shall be taken, and the other shall be left."

**17:37** And answering, they said to Him, "Where, Lord?" And He said to them, "Where the body is, there also shall the eagles be gathered together."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**17:36** *(after `17:35`)* ⟦Two shall be in the field: the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left.⟧

## Translator Notes

- **17:11** — The Greek phrase 'dia meson' literally means 'through the midst of,' and with two regions named it indicates either the borderland between Samaria and Galilee or a route passing through both.
- **17:19** — The Greek verb 'sesoken' literally means 'has saved,' carrying the dual sense of physical healing and spiritual salvation ('made you whole').
- **17:21** — The Greek phrase 'entos hymon' is debated and may be translated either 'within you' or 'in the midst of you,' pointing to the kingdom's presence among them in Christ.
- **17:35** — The earliest Greek manuscripts omit verse 36 ('Two will be in the field; one will be taken and the other left'), which was later harmonized from Matthew 24:40.
- **17:37** — The Greek word 'aetoi' can mean either 'eagles' or 'vultures'; the proverb signifies that the coming day of judgment will be unmistakable wherever it falls.
- **17:36 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
