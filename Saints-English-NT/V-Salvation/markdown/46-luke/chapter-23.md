## Text

**23:1** Then the entire multitude of them rose up and brought Him before Pilate.

**23:2** And they began to accuse Him, saying, "We found this man subverting our nation, forbidding tribute to be paid to Caesar, and declaring that He Himself is Christ, a king."

**23:3** Then Pilate questioned Him, saying, "Are You the King of the Jews?" And answering him, He said, "You say so."

**23:4** Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the multitudes, "I find no guilt in this man."

**23:5** But they became all the more insistent, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout the whole of Judea, beginning even from Galilee to this place."

**23:6** When Pilate heard this, he inquired whether the man was a Galilean.

**23:7** And when he learned that He belonged to the jurisdiction of Herod, he sent Him away to Herod, who himself was also in Jerusalem during those days.

**23:8** Now when Herod saw Jesus, he rejoiced exceedingly, because for a long time he had desired to see Him on account of what he had heard concerning Him, and he was hoping to witness some sign performed by Him.

**23:9** And he questioned Him with many words, yet He made him no reply.

**23:10** Meanwhile, the chief priests and the scribes stood there, accusing Him vehemently.

**23:11** And Herod also, together with his soldiers, treated Him with contempt and mocked Him; and arraying Him in a splendid robe, he sent Him back to Pilate.

**23:12** And on that very day both Herod and Pilate became friends with one another, for previously they had been at enmity toward each other.

**23:13** Then Pilate summoned together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people,

**23:14** and said to them, "You have brought this man to me as one who turns the people astray; and behold, having examined Him in your presence, I have found no guilt in this man regarding any of the charges you bring against Him.

**23:15** No, nor did Herod, for he sent Him back to us; and behold, nothing deserving of death has been done by Him.

**23:16** Therefore, after chastising Him, I will release Him."

**23:18** But they cried out all together, saying, "Away with this man, and release Barabbas to us!"

**23:19** He was one who had been cast into prison on account of a certain insurrection that had taken place in the city, and for murder.

**23:20** Once more Pilate addressed them, desiring to release Jesus.

**23:21** But they kept shouting out, saying, "Crucify, crucify Him!"

**23:22** And a third time he spoke to them, "Why, what evil has this man committed? I have found no ground for death in Him; therefore, after chastising Him, I will release Him."

**23:23** Yet they pressed hard with loud voices, demanding that He be crucified; and their voices prevailed.

**23:24** So Pilate passed sentence that their demand should be granted.

**23:25** And he released the man who had been cast into prison for insurrection and murder, the one they had demanded; but Jesus he delivered up to their will.

**23:26** And as they led Him away, they seized a certain Simon of Cyrene who was coming in from the countryside, and placed the cross upon him to carry behind Jesus.

**23:27** Now a great multitude of the people was following Him, and women who were mourning and lamenting for Him.

**23:28** But turning toward them, Jesus said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me; instead, weep for yourselves and for your children,

**23:29** for behold, days are coming in which they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that did not give birth, and the breasts that did not nurse.'

**23:30** Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall upon us!' and to the hills, 'Cover us!'

**23:31** For if they do these things with the green wood, what shall happen with the dry?"

**23:32** Now two others also, who were evildoers, were being led away with Him to be put to death.

**23:33** And when they arrived at the place called The Skull, there they crucified Him, along with the evildoers, one on the right and the other on the left.

**23:34** And Jesus kept saying, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." And as they divided His garments, they cast lots.

**23:35** And the people stood watching; yet even the rulers scoffed, saying, "He saved others; let Him save Himself, if this man is the Christ of God, the Chosen One!"

**23:36** The soldiers likewise mocked Him, coming near and offering Him sour wine,

**23:37** and saying, "If You are the King of the Jews, save Yourself!"

**23:38** There was also an inscription above Him: "This is the King of the Jews."

**23:39** Then one of the evildoers who were hanged reviled Him, saying, "Are You not the Christ? Save Yourself and us!"

**23:40** But the other answered, rebuking him, and said, "Do you not even fear God, seeing that you are under the same judgment?

**23:41** And we indeed justly, for we are receiving what our deeds deserve; yet this man has done nothing amiss."

**23:42** And he said, "Jesus, remember me when You come in Your kingdom."

**23:43** And He said to him, "Assuredly, I say to you, this day you shall be with Me in Paradise."

**23:44** Now it was already about the sixth hour, and darkness fell over the whole land until the ninth hour,

**23:45** while the sun's light failed; and the veil of the temple was torn down the middle.

**23:46** And crying out with a loud voice, Jesus said, "Father, into Your hands I entrust My spirit." And when He had said this, He breathed His last.

**23:47** Now when the centurion saw what had taken place, he gave glory to God, saying, "Truly this man was righteous!"

**23:48** And all the multitudes who had gathered together for this spectacle, when they beheld the things that had taken place, turned back, beating their breasts.

**23:49** Yet all His acquaintances stood afar off, as well as the women who had accompanied Him from Galilee, looking upon these things.

**23:50** Now behold, there was a man named Joseph, who was a council member, a good and righteous man—

**23:51** he had not consented to their counsel and deed—from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who was waiting for the kingdom of God;

**23:52** this man approached Pilate and requested the body of Jesus.

**23:53** And taking it down, he wrapped it in a linen shroud and laid Him in a tomb hewn from rock, in which no one had ever yet been laid.

**23:54** It was the day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was beginning to dawn.

**23:55** And the women who had come with Him from Galilee followed closely behind, and they beheld the tomb and how His body was laid.

**23:56** Then, returning home, they prepared spices and ointments. And throughout the Sabbath they rested in accordance with the commandment.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**23:17** *(after `23:16`)* ⟦Now he was under obligation to release one to them at each feast.⟧

## Translator Notes

- **23:16** — Many later manuscripts add a verse 17 here explaining Pilate's custom of releasing a prisoner at the feast, which is absent from the earliest manuscripts and from this Greek text.
- **23:33** — Greek Κρανίον literally means "The Skull"; the traditional English name "Calvary" derives from the Latin Vulgate translation Calvaria.
- **23:34** — Jesus' opening prayer ("Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing") is absent from several early manuscripts (such as P75, Vaticanus, and the original hand of Sinaiticus), though widely attested in other ancient witnesses.
- **23:42** — Literally "in Your kingdom," denoting Jesus' coming in royal authority; a textual variant reads "into Your kingdom."
- **23:54** — Greek ἐπέφωσκεν ("was dawning") refers to the approaching onset of the Sabbath at sunset, when the new day began in Jewish reckoning.
- **23:17 *(additional)*** — Text-critical double brackets ⟦ ⟧ are preserved verbatim from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
