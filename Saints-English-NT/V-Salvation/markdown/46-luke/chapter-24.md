## Text

**24:1** Now on the first day of the week, at very early dawn, they came to the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared.

**24:2** And they found the stone rolled away from the tomb,

**24:3** but when they went inside, they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.

**24:4** And it came to pass, while they were perplexed over this, behold, two men stood beside them in dazzling apparel.

**24:5** And as they became terrified and bowed their faces to the ground, they said to them, "Why do you seek the Living One among the dead?

**24:6** He is not here, but has been raised! Remember how He spoke to you while He was still in Galilee,

**24:7** saying that the Son of Man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and on the third day rise again."

**24:8** And they recalled His words,

**24:9** and returning from the tomb, they reported all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.

**24:10** Now they were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, along with the other women who were with them; they told these things to the apostles.

**24:11** Yet these words appeared before them like idle talk, and they did not believe them.

**24:12** But Peter rose up and ran to the tomb; and stooping down, he beheld the linen cloths alone; and he departed to his own home, marveling at what had taken place.

**24:13** And behold, two of them were traveling that same day to a village sixty stadia distant from Jerusalem, named Emmaus,

**24:14** and they were conversing with one another about all these things that had happened.

**24:15** And it came to pass, while they were conversing and discussing together, that Jesus Himself drew near and journeyed with them,

**24:16** but their eyes were held fast, so that they did not recognize Him.

**24:17** And He said to them, "What are these words that you are exchanging with one another as you walk?" And they stood still, downcast in countenance.

**24:18** Then one of them, whose name was Cleopas, answered and said to Him, "Are You the only sojourner in Jerusalem who does not know the things that have happened there in these days?"

**24:19** And He said to them, "What things?" And they said to Him, "The things concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people,

**24:20** and how our chief priests and rulers delivered Him over to a sentence of death, and crucified Him.

**24:21** Yet we were hoping that He was the One about to redeem Israel. Indeed, besides all this, it is now the third day since these events took place.

**24:22** Moreover, certain women of our company astounded us, having been at the tomb early in the morning;

**24:23** and when they did not find His body, they came saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who declared that He is alive.

**24:24** And certain of those who were with us went to the tomb, and found it just as the women had said; but Him they did not see."

**24:25** Then He said to them, "O foolish ones, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!

**24:26** Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer these things and to enter into His glory?"

**24:27** And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, He interpreted to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself.

**24:28** And they drew near to the village where they were going, and He acted as though He would journey farther.

**24:29** But they strongly urged Him, saying, "Abide with us, for it is toward evening, and the day has already declined." And He went in to abide with them.

**24:30** And it came to pass, as He reclined with them, that taking the bread He blessed it, and having broken it, He gave it to them;

**24:31** and their eyes were opened, and they recognized Him; yet He vanished away from them.

**24:32** And they said to one another, "Was not our heart burning within us as He spoke to us on the road, as He opened the Scriptures to us?"

**24:33** And rising up that very hour, they returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven and those who were with them gathered together,

**24:34** saying, "Truly the Lord has been raised, and has appeared to Simon!"

**24:35** And they recounted what had occurred on the road, and how He was made known to them in the breaking of the bread.

**24:36** Now as they were speaking these things, He Himself stood in their midst and said to them, "Peace be to you."

**24:37** But startled and filled with fear, they supposed that they were beholding a spirit.

**24:38** And He said to them, "Why are you troubled, and why do doubts arise in your heart?

**24:39** Behold My hands and My feet, that it is I Myself. Touch Me and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones as you behold Me having."

**24:40** And having said this, He showed them His hands and His feet.

**24:41** Yet while they still disbelieved for joy and marveled, He said to them, "Do you have anything here to eat?"

**24:42** So they gave Him a piece of broiled fish;

**24:43** and taking it, He ate it before them.

**24:44** Then He said to them, "These are My words which I spoke to you while I was still with you, that everything written concerning Me in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms must be fulfilled."

**24:45** Then He opened their mind to understand the Scriptures, and said to them,

**24:46** "Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer and rise again from the dead on the third day,

**24:47** and that repentance for the remission of sins should be proclaimed in His name to all the nations—beginning from Jerusalem;

**24:48** you are witnesses of these things.

**24:49** And behold, I am sending the promise of My Father upon you; yet remain in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."

**24:50** And He led them out as far as Bethany, and lifting up His hands, He blessed them.

**24:51** And it came to pass, while He was blessing them, that He parted from them and was carried up into heaven.

**24:52** And they worshiped Him and returned to Jerusalem with great joy,

**24:53** and were continually in the temple, blessing God.

## Translator Notes

- **24:12** — The Greek idiom 'apelthen pros heauton' means 'departed to his own home' (compare John 20:10). This verse is preserved in the great majority of manuscripts, though omitted by Codex Bezae and several Old Latin witnesses.
- **24:13** — Sixty stadia is approximately seven miles (about eleven kilometers); a few manuscripts read one hundred sixty stadia.
- **24:42** — The earliest Greek manuscripts read simply 'a piece of broiled fish,' whereas later Byzantine manuscripts add 'and some honeycomb.'
- **24:47** — The critical text reads 'repentance for the remission of sins' (rather than 'and remission'), and the masculine nominative plural participle 'beginning from Jerusalem' links the phrase directly to the disciples' commission as witnesses in verse 48.
