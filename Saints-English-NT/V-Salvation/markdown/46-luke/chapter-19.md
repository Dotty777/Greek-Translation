## Text

**19:1** And having entered, He was passing through Jericho.

**19:2** And behold, there was a man called by name Zacchaeus, and he was a chief tax collector, and he was wealthy.

**19:3** And he was seeking to see Jesus, who He was, and could not because of the multitude, for he was small in stature.

**19:4** And running on ahead, he climbed up into a sycamore-fig tree to see Him, for He was about to pass that way.

**19:5** And when Jesus came to the place, He looked up and said to him, "Zacchaeus, hasten and come down, for today I must abide at your house."

**19:6** And he hastened and came down, and welcomed Him with joy.

**19:7** And when they saw it, they all murmured, saying, "He has gone in to lodge with a sinful man."

**19:8** Then Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, "Behold, Lord, half of my possessions I give to the poor, and if I have defrauded anyone of anything, I repay it fourfold."

**19:9** And Jesus said to him, "This day salvation has come to this house, since he too is a son of Abraham;

**19:10** for the Son of Man came to seek and to save that which is lost."

**19:11** Now as they were listening to these things, He went on to speak a parable, because He was near Jerusalem and they supposed that the kingdom of God was about to appear immediately.

**19:12** Therefore He said, "A certain man of noble birth journeyed to a distant country to receive a kingdom for himself and to return.

**19:13** And calling ten of his own servants, he gave them ten minas and said to them, 'Engage in trade until I come.'

**19:14** But his citizens hated him, and sent an embassy after him, saying, 'We do not want this man to reign over us.'

**19:15** And it came to pass, when he returned after receiving the kingdom, that he commanded these servants to whom he had given the money to be summoned to him, so that he might know what they had gained by trading.

**19:16** Then the first came before him, saying, 'Master, your mina has gained ten more minas.'

**19:17** And he said to him, 'Well done, good servant! Because you proved faithful in a very little, hold authority over ten cities.'

**19:18** And the second came, saying, 'Your mina, Master, has made five minas.'

**19:19** And to this one also he said, 'You also be over five cities.'

**19:20** And the other came, saying, 'Master, behold your mina, which I kept laid away in a handkerchief;

**19:21** for I feared you, because you are a severe man: you take up what you did not lay down, and you reap what you did not sow.'

**19:22** He says to him, 'Out of your own mouth I judge you, wicked servant! Did you know that I am a severe man, taking up what I did not lay down and reaping what I did not sow?

**19:23** Why then did you not place my money in the bank? Then at my coming I would have collected it with interest.'

**19:24** And he said to those standing by, 'Take the mina from him and give it to the one who has the ten minas'—

**19:25** and they said to him, 'Master, he has ten minas'—

**19:26** 'I say to you that to everyone who has, it shall be given; but from the one who does not have, even what he has shall be taken away.

**19:27** But as for these enemies of mine, who did not want me to reign over them, bring them here and slay them before me.'"

**19:28** And having spoken these things, He went on ahead, ascending to Jerusalem.

**19:29** And it came to pass, as He drew near to Bethphage and Bethany, toward the mount called Olivet, that He sent two of the disciples,

**19:30** saying, "Go into the village opposite you; upon entering it, you shall find a colt tied, on which no man has ever sat. Untie it and bring it.

**19:31** And if anyone asks you, 'Why are you untying it?' thus you shall say: 'The Lord has need of it.'"

**19:32** So those who had been sent departed and found it just as He had told them.

**19:33** And as they were untying the colt, its owners said to them, "Why are you untying the colt?"

**19:34** And they said, "The Lord has need of it."

**19:35** And they brought it to Jesus; and casting their own garments upon the colt, they set Jesus upon it.

**19:36** And as He went along, they spread their garments on the road.

**19:37** And as He was now approaching the descent of the Mount of Olives, the entire multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and to praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works that they had seen,

**19:38** saying, "Blessed is He who comes, the King, in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven, and glory in the highest!"

**19:39** And some of the Pharisees from the multitude said to Him, "Teacher, rebuke Your disciples."

**19:40** And answering, He said, "I say to you that if these keep silent, the stones shall cry out."

**19:41** And as He drew near and beheld the city, He wept over it,

**19:42** saying, "If only you, even you, had known in this day the things that belong to peace—but now they have been hidden from your eyes.

**19:43** For days shall come upon you when your enemies shall raise a barricade around you, and encircle you, and hem you in on every side,

**19:44** and they shall dash you to the ground, and your children within you, and they shall not leave one stone upon another within you, because you did not recognize the time of your visitation."

**19:45** And having entered into the temple, He began to cast out those who were selling,

**19:46** saying to them, "It is written, 'My house is a house of prayer,' but you have made it a den of robbers."

**19:47** And He was teaching daily in the temple; yet the chief priests and the scribes were seeking to destroy Him, along with the leading men of the people,

**19:48** and they could not find what they might do, for all the people hung upon Him, listening.

## Translator Notes

- **19:13** — A mina was a Greek unit of silver currency equal to one hundred drachmas, roughly three to four months' wages for an ordinary laborer.
- **19:25** — The bystanders' interruption breaks into the nobleman's command in verse 24, and he resumes his reply in verse 26; some ancient manuscripts omit verse 25.
- **19:38** — The Greek places anarthrous "King" (basileus) directly in apposition to the Septuagint citation of Psalm 118:26 ("He who comes in the name of the Lord").
- **19:46** — Following the Greek critical text, the quotation from Isaiah 56:7 omits the future verb "shall be," reading directly as a nominal clause ("My house is a house of prayer").
