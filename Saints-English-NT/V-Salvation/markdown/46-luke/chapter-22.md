## Text

**22:1** Now the Feast of Unleavened Bread, which is called the Passover, was drawing near.

**22:2** And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might slay Him, for they feared the people.

**22:3** Then Satan entered into Judas, who was called Iscariot, being one of the number of the twelve.

**22:4** And he departed and conferred with the chief priests and captains concerning how he might betray Him to them.

**22:5** And they rejoiced and agreed to give him silver.

**22:6** And he consented, and was seeking an opportunity to betray Him to them apart from a multitude.

**22:7** Then the Day of Unleavened Bread came, on which the Passover had to be sacrificed.

**22:8** And He sent forth Peter and John, saying, "Go and make ready the Passover for us, that we may eat."

**22:9** And they said to Him, "Where do You wish us to make ready?"

**22:10** And He said to them, "Behold, when you have entered into the city, a man bearing a jar of water will meet you; follow him into the house that he enters.

**22:11** And you shall say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest chamber in which I may eat the Passover with My disciples?"'

**22:12** And he will show you a great upper room, furnished; make ready there."

**22:13** And departing, they found it just as He had told them, and they made ready the Passover.

**22:14** And when the hour had come, He reclined at the table, and the apostles with Him.

**22:15** And He said to them, "With earnest desire I have longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer;

**22:16** for I say to you that I shall by no means eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

**22:17** And having received a cup, He gave thanks and said, "Take this and divide it among yourselves;

**22:18** for I say to you, from now on I shall by no means drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

**22:19** And taking bread, He gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, which is given on your behalf; do this in remembrance of Me."

**22:20** And in like manner the cup after supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out on your behalf.

**22:21** Yet behold, the hand of the one who betrays Me is with Me on the table.

**22:22** For indeed the Son of Man goes according to what has been determined, yet woe to that man by whom He is betrayed!"

**22:23** And they began to inquire among themselves which one of them it could be who was about to do this thing.

**22:24** Now a contention also arose among them as to which of them was regarded as the greatest.

**22:25** And He said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles rule as lords over them, and those who wield authority over them are called benefactors.

**22:26** Yet not so with you; instead, let the greatest among you become as the youngest, and the one who leads as the one who serves.

**22:27** For who is greater, the one who reclines at table or the one who serves? Is it not the one who reclines at table? Yet I am in your midst as the One who serves.

**22:28** Now you are those who have remained with Me in My trials;

**22:29** and I confer upon you, just as My Father conferred upon Me, a kingdom,

**22:30** so that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and sit upon thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

**22:31** Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has asked to have you, that he may sift you as wheat;

**22:32** but I have made supplication for you, that your faith may not fail; and you, when once you have turned back, strengthen your brethren."

**22:33** And he said to Him, "Lord, with You I am ready to go both to prison and to death."

**22:34** And He said, "I say to you, Peter, the rooster shall not crow this day until you have denied three times that you know Me."

**22:35** And He said to them, "When I sent you forth without a purse, or knapsack, or sandals, did you lack anything?" And they said, "Nothing."

**22:36** And He said to them, "But now, let the one who has a purse take it, and likewise a knapsack; and let the one who has none sell his cloak and buy a sword."

**22:37** "For I say to you that this which is written must be fulfilled in Me: 'And He was reckoned with the lawless'; for indeed what concerns Me has its fulfillment."

**22:38** And they said, "Lord, behold, there are two swords here." And He said to them, "It is enough."

**22:39** And going out, He proceeded according to custom to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples also followed Him.

**22:40** And when He arrived at the place, He said to them, "Pray that you do not enter into temptation."

**22:41** And He drew away from them about a stone's throw, and kneeling down, He prayed,

**22:42** saying, "Father, if You are willing, take away this cup from Me; yet let not My will, but Yours, be done."

**22:43** And an angel from heaven appeared to Him, strengthening Him.

**22:44** And being in agony, He prayed yet more fervently; and His sweat became like heavy drops of blood falling down upon the ground.

**22:45** And rising from prayer, He came to the disciples and found them asleep from sorrow,

**22:46** and said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Arise and pray, lest you enter into temptation."

**22:47** While He was still speaking, behold, a multitude came, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was going before them; and he approached Jesus to kiss Him.

**22:48** But Jesus said to him, "Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**22:49** And those around Him, seeing what was about to take place, said, "Lord, shall we strike with the sword?"

**22:50** And a certain one of them struck the servant of the high priest and severed his right ear.

**22:51** But Jesus answered and said, "No more of this." And touching his ear, He healed him.

**22:52** And Jesus said to the chief priests and captains of the temple and elders who had come against Him, "Have you come forth as against a robber, with swords and clubs?

**22:53** While I was with you daily in the temple, you did not stretch forth your hands against Me; yet this is your hour, and the authority of darkness."

**22:54** And having seized Him, they led Him away and brought Him into the house of the high priest; and Peter was following from afar.

**22:55** And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the courtyard and seated themselves together, Peter sat in their midst.

**22:56** And a certain maidservant, seeing him sitting by the light and gazing intently at him, said, "This man also was with Him."

**22:57** But he denied it, saying, "I do not know Him, woman."

**22:58** And a short time later, another saw him and said, "You also are one of them." But Peter replied, "Man, I am not."

**22:59** And after an interval of about one hour, another firmly insisted, saying, "In truth this man also was with Him, for indeed he is a Galilean."

**22:60** And Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And at once, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

**22:61** And turning, the Lord looked upon Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how He had spoken to him, "Before a rooster crows this day, you shall deny Me three times."

**22:62** And going outside, he wept bitterly.

**22:63** Now the men who were holding Him were mocking Him and beating Him;

**22:64** and having blindfolded Him, they were questioning Him, saying, "Prophesy! Who is the one who struck You?"

**22:65** And blaspheming, they spoke many other things against Him.

**22:66** And when day broke, the assembly of the elders of the people came together—both chief priests and scribes—and they led Him away into their council,

**22:67** saying, "If You are the Christ, tell us." And He said to them, "If I tell you, you will certainly not believe;

**22:68** and if I question you, you will certainly not answer.

**22:69** Yet from this time onward the Son of Man shall be seated at the right hand of the power of God."

**22:70** And they all said, "Are You then the Son of God?" And He said to them, "You yourselves say that I am."

**22:71** And they said, "What further need do we have of testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from His mouth."

## Translator Notes

- **22:20** — While Codex Bezae and several Old Latin manuscripts omit 22:19b-20 (resulting in a shorter text with only the cup of v. 17), the longer text is supported by the earliest papyrus (P75) and nearly all Greek manuscripts. In Greek, the participle "which is poured out" is neuter, agreeing grammatically with "cup" as metonymy for the blood within it.
- **22:29** — The verb translated "confer" (diatithemai) carries the sense of appointing or bestowing by covenant, echoing "covenant" (diatheke) in verse 20.
- **22:31** — The critical text omits "And the Lord said" (found in later manuscripts), continuing Jesus' speech directly from verse 30. Note also that "you" in verse 31 is plural (addressing all the disciples), whereas "you" in verse 32 shifts to the singular (addressing Simon Peter).
- **22:36** — The Greek phrase literally reads "the one who does not have" without stating an object; it may refer back to having no purse or money, or anticipate having no sword.
- **22:43** — Verses 43-44 are omitted in several early manuscripts (including P75, Codex Vaticanus, and a correction of Codex Sinaiticus), but are present in the original hand of Codex Sinaiticus, Codex Bezae, and early patristic citations.
- **22:51** — The Greek phrase literally means "Allow up to this"; it may be addressed either to the disciples to halt further violence ("No more of this") or to His captors to permit Him to heal the wounded servant.
- **22:68** — The critical text omits "Me or let Me go" (found in later Byzantine manuscripts), ending simply with "you will certainly not answer."
- **22:70** — The Greek reply "You yourselves say that I am" affirms the statement while placing the responsibility for the wording on the questioners themselves.
