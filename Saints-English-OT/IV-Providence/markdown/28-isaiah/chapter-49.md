## Text

**49:1** Listen to me, O islands, and give heed, O nations: "After a long time it shall stand," says the Lord. From my mother's womb He called my name,

**49:2** and He made my mouth like a sharp sword, and under the shelter of His hand He hid me; He made me like a chosen arrow, and in His quiver He hid me,

**49:3** and He said to me, "You are My servant, O Israel, and in you I shall be glorified."

**49:4** And I said, "In vain I have labored; for vanity and for nothing I have given my strength; therefore my judgment is with the Lord, and my labor is before my God."

**49:5** And now thus says the Lord, who formed me from the womb to be a servant to Himself, to gather Jacob and Israel to Him—I shall be gathered and glorified before the Lord, and God shall be my strength—

**49:6** and He said to me, "It is a great thing for you to be called My servant, to establish the tribes of Jacob and to turn back the dispersion of Israel; behold, I have given you for a covenant of a race, for a light of the nations, that you should be for salvation to the end of the earth."

**49:7** Thus says the Lord who redeems you, the God of Israel: "Sanctify him who despises his own life, him who is abhorred by the nations, the servants of rulers: kings shall see him, and rulers shall rise up and bow down to him for the sake of the Lord; for the Holy One of Israel is faithful, and I have chosen you."

**49:8** Thus says the Lord: "In an acceptable time I have heard you, and in a day of salvation I have helped you; and I have formed you and given you for a covenant of the nations, to establish the earth and to inherit desolate heritages,

**49:9** saying to those in bonds, 'Go forth,' and to those in darkness, to be revealed. Along all the ways they shall be fed, and in all the paths shall be their pasture;

**49:10** they shall neither hunger nor thirst, neither shall scorching heat nor the sun strike them; for He who has mercy on them shall comfort them, and through springs of water He shall lead them;

**49:11** and I will make every mountain a road, and every path a pasture for them.

**49:12** Behold, these shall come from afar; these from the north and the sea, and others from the land of the Persians."

**49:13** Rejoice, O heavens, and let the earth exult; let the mountains break forth into gladness; for God has had mercy on His people, and has comforted the lowly of His people.

**49:14** But Zion said, "The Lord has forsaken me, and the Lord has forgotten me."

**49:15** "Will a woman forget her child, or fail to have compassion on the offspring of her womb? Yet even if a woman should forget these, I will not forget you," said the Lord.

**49:16** Behold, upon My hands I have painted your walls, and you are continually before Me;

**49:17** and swiftly you shall be built by those by whom you were torn down, and those who laid you waste shall go forth from you.

**49:18** Lift up your eyes all around and see them all; behold, they have been gathered together and have come to you. "As I live," says the Lord, "you shall surely put them all on as an ornament, and bind them around you as an ornament, like a bride."

**49:19** For your desolate and ruined and fallen places shall now be too narrow because of the inhabitants, and those who swallow you up shall be far away from you.

**49:20** For your sons whom you have lost shall say in your ears, "The place is too narrow for me; make room for me that I may dwell."

**49:21** And you shall say in your heart, "Who has borne these for me? For I was childless and a widow; and who has brought up these for me? And I was left alone; where were these for me?"

**49:22** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, I lift up My hand to the nations, and to the islands I will raise My standard; and they shall bring your sons in their bosom, and your daughters they shall carry upon their shoulders,

**49:23** and kings shall be your foster fathers, and their princesses your nurses; upon the face of the earth they shall bow down to you, and they shall lick the dust of your feet; and you shall know that I am the Lord, and those who wait for Me shall not be put to shame."

**49:24** Shall anyone take spoils from a giant? And if someone takes captives unjustly, shall he be saved?

**49:25** Thus says the Lord: "If someone takes a giant captive, he shall take spoils; and he who takes from a mighty one shall be saved; and I will judge your cause, and I will deliver your sons;

**49:26** and those who afflicted you shall eat their own flesh, and they shall drink their own blood like new wine and become drunk; and all flesh shall perceive that I am the Lord who redeems you and upholds the strength of Jacob."

## Translator Notes

- **49:1** — The Septuagint renders the second clause "After a long time it shall stand, says the Lord," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "peoples from afar."
- **49:6** — The Septuagint reads "It is a great thing for you" (rather than "too small a thing" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and includes "for a covenant of a race" before "for a light of the nations."
- **49:7** — The Septuagint opens the oracle with the imperative "Sanctify him who despises his own life" and places "the servants of rulers" in apposition to "the nations."
- **49:12** — The Septuagint specifies "from the land of the Persians" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "from the land of Sinim."
