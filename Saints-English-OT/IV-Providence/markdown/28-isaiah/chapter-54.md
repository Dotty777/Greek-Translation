## Text

**54:1** Be glad, O barren one who does not bear; break forth and cry aloud, you who do not travail in birth; for many more are the children of the desolate woman than of her who has the husband, for the Lord has spoken.

**54:2** Enlarge the place of your tent and of your curtains; pitch it, do not spare; lengthen your cords, and strengthen your stakes,

**54:3** stretch out yet further to the right and to the left; and your seed shall inherit nations, and you shall settle desolate cities.

**54:4** Do not fear because you were put to shame, nor be abashed because you were reproached; for you shall forget everlasting shame, and the reproach of your widowhood you shall remember no more.

**54:5** For the Lord is He who makes you, the Lord of Sabaoth is His name; and He who delivered you shall Himself be called the God of Israel for all the earth.

**54:6** The Lord has not called you as a forsaken and fainthearted woman, nor as a woman hated from youth, said your God.

**54:7** For a brief time I forsook you, and with great mercy I will have mercy on you;

**54:8** in a little wrath I turned My face away from you, and with everlasting mercy I will have mercy on you, said the Lord who delivered you.

**54:9** From the water that was in the time of Noah, this is to Me: just as I swore to him in that time to the earth, that I would no longer be angry with you, nor in a threat against you should the mountains be removed,

**54:10** nor shall your hills be shaken; so neither shall the mercy from Me to you fail, nor shall the covenant of your peace ever be removed; for He said, "Gracious to you, O Lord."

**54:11** Humbled and unsettled, you were not comforted; behold, I am preparing carbuncle for your stone, and sapphire for your foundations,

**54:12** and I will make your battlements jasper, and your gates stones of crystal, and your surrounding wall choice stones,

**54:13** and all your sons taught of God, and your children in great peace.

**54:14** And in righteousness you shall be built up; keep away from injustice, and you shall not fear, and trembling shall not draw near to you.

**54:15** Behold, proselytes shall come to you through Me, and they shall sojourn with you and shall flee to you for refuge.

**54:16** Behold, I have created you, not as a coppersmith blowing upon coals and bringing forth an instrument for work; but I created you not to ruin for destruction.

**54:17** Every instrument fashioned against you I will not cause to prosper; and every voice that shall rise against you in judgment, all of them you shall defeat, and those who are guilty against you shall be in it. There is an inheritance for those who serve the Lord, and you shall be righteous to Me, says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:4** — The Greek reads "everlasting shame" (aischynen aionion) rather than the Hebrew "shame of your youth."
- **54:6** — The Greek begins with a negation ("The Lord has not called you as a forsaken... woman"), contrasting with the affirmative simile in the Hebrew text.
- **54:10** — The Greek continues the negative oath from verse 9 ("nor shall your hills be shaken") and closes with a well-known Septuagint reading using the vocative Kyrie ("O Lord") rather than the nominative Kyrios ("the Lord").
- **54:15** — The Greek reads "proselytes shall come to you through Me," presenting a promise of Gentile converts seeking refuge in Zion rather than the warning about attackers in the Hebrew.
