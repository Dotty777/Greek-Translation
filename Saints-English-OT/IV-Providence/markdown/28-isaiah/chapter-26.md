## Text

**26:1** In that day they shall sing this song in the land of Judea: "Behold, a strong city! And He shall appoint salvation as the wall and outer wall.

**26:2** Open the gates; let a people keeping righteousness and keeping truth enter,

**26:3** laying hold of truth and keeping peace; for in You with hope

**26:4** they have hoped, O Lord, forever, the great and eternal God,

**26:5** who humbled and brought down those who dwell on high; fortified cities You shall cast down and bring down to the ground,

**26:6** and the feet of the meek and lowly shall trample them.

**26:7** The way of the godly has become straight; the way of the godly is also prepared.

**26:8** For the way of the Lord is judgment; we have hoped in Your name and in the remembrance

**26:9** which our soul desires. Out of the night my spirit rises early to You, O God, because Your ordinances are light upon the earth. Learn righteousness, you who dwell upon the earth.

**26:10** For the ungodly has ceased; everyone who will not learn righteousness upon the earth shall by no means practice truth; let the ungodly be taken away, lest he see the glory of the Lord.

**26:11** O Lord, Your arm is lifted high, and they did not know it; yet when they know, they shall be put to shame; zeal shall seize an untaught people, and now fire shall devour the adversaries.

**26:12** O Lord our God, give us peace, for You have rendered all things to us.

**26:13** Possess us, O Lord; besides You we do not know another; we name Your name.

**26:14** But the dead shall by no means see life, neither shall physicians by any means raise them up; therefore You brought it upon them and destroyed them, and took away every male of theirs.

**26:15** Add evils to them, O Lord, add evils to the glorious ones of the earth.

**26:16** O Lord, in affliction I remembered You; in a little affliction Your chastening was upon us.

**26:17** And as a woman in labor draws near to give birth and cries out in her pangs, so we became to Your beloved.

**26:18** Because of the fear of You, O Lord, we conceived and were in labor and gave birth; we have accomplished the spirit of Your salvation upon the earth, yet all who dwell upon the earth shall fall.

**26:19** The dead shall rise, and those who are in the tombs shall be raised, and those who are in the earth shall rejoice; for the dew that is from You is healing to them, but the land of the ungodly shall fall."

**26:20** Go, My people, enter into your chambers, shut your door, hide yourself for a very little while, until the wrath of the Lord passes by.

**26:21** For behold, the Lord from the holy place is bringing wrath upon those who dwell upon the earth; and the earth shall uncover its blood, and shall not cover over those who were slain.

## Translator Notes

- **26:13** — The Septuagint reads the imperative ktesai hemas ("Possess us"), whereas the Masoretic Text speaks of other lords having ruled over Israel.
- **26:14** — The Septuagint reads the Hebrew consonants for "shades of the dead" as iatroi ("physicians") and "memory" as pan arsen ("every male").
- **26:17** — The Septuagint reads to agapeto sou ("to Your beloved") where the Masoretic Text reads "before You."
- **26:18** — The Septuagint reads positively ("we have accomplished the spirit of Your salvation upon the earth"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads negatively ("we have brought forth wind; we have not accomplished deliverance").
