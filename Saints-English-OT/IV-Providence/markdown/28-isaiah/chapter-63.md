## Text

**63:1** Who is this who comes from Edom, with redness of garments from Bozrah, so stately in apparel, with force and strength? "I declare righteousness and judgment of salvation."

**63:2** Why are Your garments red, and Your raiment like that from a trodden winepress?

**63:3** "I am full of the trodden winepress, and of the nations there is not a man with Me; and I trampled them in My fury, and crushed them like earth, and brought down their blood.

**63:4** For a day of recompense has come upon them, and a year of redemption is at hand.

**63:5** And I looked, and there was no helper; and I considered, and no one lent support; so My arm delivered them, and My fury stood ready.

**63:6** And I trampled them in My wrath, and brought down their blood to the earth."

**63:7** I called to mind the mercy of the Lord, the excellencies of the Lord in all that He recompenses to us. The Lord is a good judge to the house of Israel; He brings upon us according to His mercy and according to the multitude of His righteousness.

**63:8** And He said, "Are they not My people? Children, they will surely not deal falsely." And He became salvation to them

**63:9** out of all their affliction. Neither an envoy nor an angel, but He Himself saved them, because He loved them and spared them; He Himself redeemed them and took them up, and exalted them all the days of old;

**63:10** but they disobeyed and provoked His Holy Spirit; so He was turned to enmity against them, and He Himself warred against them.

**63:11** And he remembered the days of old: "Where is He who brought up from the sea the shepherd of the sheep? Where is He who placed the Holy Spirit within them,

**63:12** who led Moses by the right hand, the arm of His glory?" He prevailed over the water before him, to make for Himself an everlasting name.

**63:13** He led them through the deep like a horse through a wilderness, and they did not grow weary,

**63:14** and like cattle through a plain; the Spirit came down from the Lord and guided them; thus You led Your people, to make for Yourself a name of glory.

**63:15** Turn from heaven and look from Your holy house and Your glory; where is Your zeal and Your strength? Where is the multitude of Your mercy and Your compassions, that You have held back from us?

**63:16** For You are our Father, because Abraham did not know us, and Israel did not acknowledge us; but You, O Lord, are our Father; deliver us; from the beginning Your name is upon us.

**63:17** Why have You caused us to stray, O Lord, from Your way? Have You hardened our hearts so as not to fear You? Return for the sake of Your servants, for the sake of the tribes of Your inheritance,

**63:18** that for a little while we may inherit Your holy mountain.

**63:19** We have become as at the beginning, when You did not rule over us, nor was Your name called upon us. If You open heaven, trembling shall seize the mountains from You, and they shall melt

## Translator Notes

- **63:3** — The elliptical Greek phrase "full of the trodden winepress" reflects a different reading of the Hebrew "I have trodden the winepress alone."
- **63:9** — The Greek reads "Neither an envoy nor an angel, but He Himself saved them," stressing God's direct deliverance in contrast to the Hebrew "the angel of His presence saved them."
- **63:18** — The Greek reads "that for a little while we may inherit Your holy mountain," lacking the Hebrew clause about adversaries trampling the sanctuary.
- **63:19** — Following Septuagint versification, the closing clause corresponds to the beginning of 64:1 in Hebrew-based Bibles and runs mid-sentence into the following chapter.
