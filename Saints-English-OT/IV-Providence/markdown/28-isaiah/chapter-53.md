## Text

**53:1** Lord, who has believed our message? And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?

**53:2** We made announcement before Him as a little child, as a root in a thirsty land; He has no form nor glory. And we beheld Him, and He had no form nor beauty;

**53:3** rather, His form was without honor, failing beyond the sons of men—a man in affliction and knowing how to bear infirmity, for His countenance is turned away; He was dishonored and was not esteemed.

**53:4** This One bears our sins and suffers anguish on our behalf; yet we esteemed Him to be in travail, and in affliction, and in ill-treatment.

**53:5** But He was wounded because of our sins, and He has been made weak because of our iniquities; the chastisement of our peace was upon Him, and by His bruise we were healed.

**53:6** All of us like sheep went astray; each man wandered in his own way; and the Lord delivered Him up to our sins.

**53:7** And He, because He has been afflicted, does not open His mouth; as a sheep He was led to slaughter, and as a lamb before its shearer is silent, so He does not open His mouth.

**53:8** In His humiliation His judgment was taken away; who shall declare His generation? For His life is taken away from the earth; because of the iniquities of My people He was led to death.

**53:9** And I will give the wicked in place of His burial, and the rich in place of His death; because He wrought no iniquity, nor was deceit found in His mouth.

**53:10** And the Lord wills to cleanse Him from the affliction; if you give an offering for sin, our soul shall behold a long-lived seed.

**53:11** And the Lord wills to take away from the travail of His soul, to show Him light and to form Him with understanding, to justify a righteous One who serves many well; and their sins He Himself shall bear.

**53:12** Therefore He shall inherit many, and He shall divide the spoils of the strong, because His soul was delivered up to death, and He was reckoned among the lawless; and He Himself bore the sins of many, and because of their iniquities He was delivered up.

## Translator Notes

- **53:1** — The Septuagint opens with the vocative 'Lord' (Kyrie), echoed in John 12:38 and Romans 10:16.
- **53:2** — Greek anengeilamen ('we made announcement') is a distinctly Septuagint reading where the Hebrew has 'he grew up.' Messianic pronouns for the Servant are capitalized throughout the chapter.
- **53:5** — Greek paidia here is an orthographic variant of paideia ('chastisement/discipline'); molopi is singular ('bruise' or 'welt').
- **53:8** — This Septuagint wording ('In His humiliation His judgment was taken away') is the form quoted in Acts 8:32-33.
- **53:10** — The Saints Greek Critical Text reads second-person plural dote ('if you give') and first-person plural he psyche hemon ('our soul'), where other editions read 'your soul' and the Hebrew reads 'his soul.'
