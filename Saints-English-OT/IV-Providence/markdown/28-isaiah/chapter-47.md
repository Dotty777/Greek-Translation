## Text

**47:1** Come down, sit upon the ground, O virgin daughter of Babylon; sit on the ground, O daughter of the Chaldeans, for you shall no longer be called tender and delicate.

**47:2** Take a millstone, grind flour; uncover your veil, uncover your gray hairs, bare your legs, cross over rivers;

**47:3** your shame shall be uncovered, your reproaches shall appear; I will exact justice from you, I will no longer hand over to men.

**47:4** He who delivered you is the Lord of hosts; His name is the Holy One of Israel.

**47:5** Sit stunned, enter into the darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans; you shall no longer be called the strength of a kingdom.

**47:6** I was provoked to anger against My people; you defiled My inheritance; I gave them into your hand, yet you gave them no mercy; you made the yoke of the aged exceedingly heavy,

**47:7** and you said, "Forever I shall be a ruler." You did not consider these things in your heart, nor did you remember the last things.

**47:8** Now therefore hear these things, O delicate one, who sits at ease, who is confident, who says in her heart, "I am, and there is no other; I shall not sit as a widow, nor shall I know bereavement."

**47:9** But now it shall come suddenly upon you in your sorcery, in the exceeding strength of your enchantments,

**47:10** in the hope of your wickedness. For you said, "I am, and there is no other." Know this: the understanding of these things and your harlotry shall be shame to you; and you said in your heart, "I am, and there is no other."

**47:11** And destruction shall come upon you, and you shall by no means know it; a pit, and you shall fall into it; and misery shall come upon you, and you shall by no means be able to become clean; and destruction shall come upon you suddenly, and you shall not know it.

**47:12** Stand now with your enchantments and your abundant sorcery, which you learned from your youth, if you shall be able to profit.

**47:13** You have grown weary in your counsels; let the astrologers of heaven stand up and save you; let those who gaze at the stars declare to you what is about to come upon you.

**47:14** Behold, they shall all be burned up like brushwood on a fire, and they shall by no means deliver their soul from the flame; because you have coals of fire, sit upon them!

**47:15** These shall be your help! You have labored in trade from youth; each man has wandered away on his own, and for you there shall be no salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **47:3** — The Greek clause "I will no longer hand over to men" is elliptical (implying God's people as the object) and differs from the Hebrew text's "I will not spare any man."
- **47:6** — The Greek reads second-person "you defiled My inheritance," attributing the defilement to Babylon rather than to God as in the Hebrew text ("I profaned").
- **47:9** — The Greek omits the Hebrew clause "both of these in a single day, loss of children and widowhood," leaving bereavement from verse 8 as the implied subject of "it shall come suddenly upon you."
- **47:14** — In place of the Hebrew statement that there is no coal for warming or fire to sit before, the Greek renders an ironic command: "because you have coals of fire, sit upon them!"
