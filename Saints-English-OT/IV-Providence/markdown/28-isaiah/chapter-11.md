## Text

**11:1** And a Rod shall come forth from the root of Jesse, and a Blossom shall spring up from the root.

**11:2** And the Spirit of God shall rest upon Him, the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, the Spirit of counsel and strength, the Spirit of knowledge and godliness;

**11:3** the Spirit of the fear of God shall fill Him. He shall not judge according to outward appearance, nor shall He reprove according to report;

**11:4** but He shall render judgment for the humble, and He shall reprove the lowly of the earth; and He shall strike the earth with the word of His mouth, and with the breath through His lips He shall slay the ungodly.

**11:5** And He shall have His loins girded with righteousness, and His sides wrapped with truth.

**11:6** And the wolf shall pasture with the lamb, and the leopard shall rest with the kid, and the calf and the bull and the lion shall graze together, and a little child shall lead them.

**11:7** And the cow and the bear shall pasture together, and their young shall be together; and the lion, like the ox, shall eat straw.

**11:8** And an infant child shall lay his hand upon the holes of asps, and upon the den of the offspring of asps.

**11:9** And they shall by no means do harm, nor shall they be able to destroy anyone upon My holy mountain; for the whole earth has been filled with knowing the Lord, as abundant water covers the seas.

**11:10** And in that day there shall be the Root of Jesse, and He who rises up to rule over the nations; in Him the nations shall hope, and His rest shall be honor.

**11:11** And it shall come to pass in that day that the Lord shall yet again show His hand to be zealous for the remaining remnant of the people, that which is left by the Assyrians, and from Egypt, and from Babylonia, and Ethiopia, and from the Elamites, and from the rising of the sun, and from Arabia.

**11:12** And He shall lift up a standard to the nations, and He shall gather the lost ones of Israel, and the dispersed of Judah He shall gather from the four wings of the earth.

**11:13** And the jealousy of Ephraim shall be taken away, and the enemies of Judah shall perish; Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not afflict Ephraim.

**11:14** And they shall fly in the ships of foreigners; together they shall plunder the sea, and those from the rising of the sun, and Idumea; and upon Moab first they shall lay their hands, and the sons of Ammon shall be the first to obey.

**11:15** And the Lord shall make desolate the sea of Egypt, and He shall lay His hand upon the river with a violent wind, and He shall strike seven channels so that one may cross it in sandals;

**11:16** and there shall be a passage for My people who remain in Egypt, and it shall be for Israel like the day when he came out from the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — By translating the final quality in verse 2 as "godliness" (eusebeias) and beginning verse 3 with "the Spirit of the fear of God," the Septuagint enumerates seven distinct endowments of the Spirit.
- **11:10** — The Septuagint reads "He who rises up to rule over the nations; in Him the nations shall hope," wording quoted by Paul in Romans 15:12.
- **11:11** — The Septuagint concludes the list of lands with "from the rising of the sun, and from Arabia," where the Masoretic Text has Hamath and the islands of the sea.
- **11:14** — Greek allophylon ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines; the Greek reads "in the ships of foreigners" rather than the Masoretic Text's "upon the shoulder of the Philistines."
