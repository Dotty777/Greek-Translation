## Text

**32:1** For behold, a righteous king shall reign, and rulers shall rule with justice.

**32:2** And the man shall conceal his words, and he shall be hidden as from rushing water; and he shall appear in Zion like a glorious rushing river in a thirsty land.

**32:3** And they shall no longer be trusting in men, but they shall give their ears to hear;

**32:4** and the heart of the weak shall give heed to hear, and the stammering tongues shall quickly learn to speak peace;

**32:5** and they shall no longer tell the fool to rule, and your servants shall no longer say, "Be silent!"

**32:6** For the fool shall speak foolish things, and his heart shall devise vain things, to accomplish lawless deeds and to speak error against the Lord, to scatter hungry souls, and he shall leave the thirsty souls empty.

**32:7** For the counsel of the wicked shall devise lawless things, to destroy the humble with unjust words and to scatter the words of the humble in judgment.

**32:8** But the godly have devised prudent things, and this counsel shall abide.

**32:9** Wealthy women, rise up and hear my voice; daughters in hope, listen to my words.

**32:10** Make remembrance of a day of a year in pain with hope; the vintage has been consumed, it has ceased, and shall never come again.

**32:11** Be dismayed, be grieved, you who are confident; strip yourselves, become bare, gird your loins,

**32:12** and beat upon your breasts, because of the desirable field and the produce of the vine.

**32:13** As for the land of my people, thorns and grass shall come up, and gladness shall be taken away from every house;

**32:14** O wealthy city, forsaken houses shall abandon the wealth of the city, the desirable houses; and the villages shall be caves forever, the delight of wild donkeys, pastures of shepherds,

**32:15** until the Spirit from on high comes upon you. And Carmel shall be a wilderness, and Carmel shall be reckoned as a forest.

**32:16** And judgment shall rest in the wilderness, and righteousness shall dwell in Carmel;

**32:17** and the works of righteousness shall be peace, and righteousness shall maintain rest, and they shall be confident forever;

**32:18** and His people shall dwell in a city of peace, and shall abide in confidence, and they shall rest with wealth.

**32:19** And if the hail comes down, it shall not come upon you. And those who dwell in the forests shall be confident, as in the plain.

**32:20** Blessed are those who sow beside every water, where the ox and the donkey tread.

## Translator Notes

- **32:5** — The Septuagint reads "your servants shall no longer say, 'Be silent!'" (Siga), diverging from the Masoretic Text's saying that the scoundrel will no longer be called generous.
- **32:15** — The Greek treats the Hebrew word for "fruitful field" as a proper name, spelling it Chermel in verse 15 and Karmelo in verse 16 (both rendered "Carmel" here), and makes Carmel the subject that becomes a wilderness in verse 15.
- **32:19** — The Septuagint turns this verse into an assurance that the hail will not fall upon the people and that forest dwellers will dwell securely, whereas the Masoretic Text describes hail bringing down the forest.
