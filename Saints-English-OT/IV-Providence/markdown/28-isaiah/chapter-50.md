## Text

**50:1** Thus says the Lord: "What is the certificate of divorce of your mother, with which I sent her away? Or to which creditor have I sold you? Behold, for your sins you were sold, and for your iniquities I sent your mother away.

**50:2** Why is it that I came, and there was no man? I called, and there was no one who answered? Is My hand not strong enough to redeem? Or do I not have strength to deliver? Behold, by My rebuke I will lay waste the sea, and make rivers desolate; and their fish shall be dried up because there is no water, and they shall die in thirst.

**50:3** I will clothe the heaven with darkness, and I will make its covering like sackcloth."

**50:4** The Lord gives me a tongue of instruction, to know when it is necessary to speak a word; He appointed it for me in the morning; He added to me an ear to hear,

**50:5** and the instruction of the Sovereign Lord opens my ears, and I do not disobey, nor do I speak against it.

**50:6** I gave my back to scourges, and my cheeks to blows; and my face I did not turn away from the shame of spitting,

**50:7** and the Sovereign Lord became my helper; therefore I was not confounded, but I set my face like a solid rock, and I knew that I shall by no means be put to shame.

**50:8** For He who has justified me draws near; who is he who contends with me? Let him stand against me together; and who is he who contends with me? Let him draw near to me.

**50:9** Behold, the Lord will help me; who will harm me? Behold, all of you shall grow old like a garment, and a moth shall devour you.

**50:10** Who among you fears the Lord? Let him obey the voice of His servant; you who walk in darkness and have no light, trust in the name of the Lord and lean upon God.

**50:11** Behold, all of you kindle a fire and strengthen a flame; walk in the light of your fire and in the flame which you have kindled. Because of Me these things have come upon you; you shall lie down in sorrow.

## Translator Notes

- **50:2** — The Septuagint asks "Is My hand not strong enough to redeem?" and states that the fish "shall be dried up," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text asks whether God's hand is "too short" and says the fish "stink."
- **50:4** — The Septuagint reads "to know when it is necessary to speak a word," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("to know how to sustain the weary with a word").
- **50:6** — The Septuagint renders "to scourges" and "to blows," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to those who strike" and "to those who pluck out the beard."
