## Text

**16:1** I will send forth as creeping things upon the earth; is the mountain of the daughter of Zion a desolate rock?

**16:2** For you shall be like a nestling taken away from a bird taking flight; you shall be, O daughter of Moab. Upon these things, O Arnon, further

**16:3** take counsel; make a shelter of mourning for her continually. In midday darkness they flee; they are dismayed; do not be led away.

**16:4** The fugitives of Moab shall sojourn with you; they shall be a shelter to you from the face of the pursuer, for your alliance has been taken away, and the ruler who tramples down has perished from the earth.

**16:5** And with mercy a throne shall be set aright, and One shall sit upon it with truth in the tent of David, judging and earnestly seeking judgment and hastening righteousness.

**16:6** We have heard of the arrogance of Moab—exceedingly arrogant is he; I have taken away the pride. Not so is your divination, not so.

**16:7** Moab shall wail, for in the land of Moab all shall wail; you shall take thought for those who dwell in Deseth, and you shall not be put to shame.

**16:8** The plains of Heshbon shall mourn, and the vine of Sibmah; you who swallow up the nations, you shall trample down her vines as far as Jazer. You shall by no means reach them; wander through the wilderness; those who were sent out have been forsaken, for they crossed over the sea.

**16:9** Therefore I will weep with the weeping of Jazer for the vine of Sibmah; Heshbon and Elealeh have cast down your trees, for upon your harvest and upon your vintage I will trample, and all things shall fall.

**16:10** And gladness and exultation shall be taken away from the vineyards; and in your vineyards they shall by no means rejoice, and they shall by no means tread wine into the wine vats, for it has ceased.

**16:11** Therefore my inner being shall sound like a harp for Moab, and my inward parts like a wall you have renewed.

**16:12** And it shall come to pass that you shall be put to shame, because Moab has grown weary upon the altars; and he shall enter into her handmade shrines to pray, and it shall by no means be able to deliver him.

**16:13** This is the word which the Lord spoke concerning Moab when He spoke.

**16:14** And now I say, "Within three years, as the years of a hired servant, the glory of Moab shall be dishonored with all that great wealth, and he shall be left very small and without honor."

## Translator Notes

- **16:6** — Greek reads "your divination" (manteia) where the Hebrew text reads "his boastings."
- **16:7** — Greek preserves the distinct place-name "Deseth," corresponding to Kir-hareseth in the Hebrew text.
- **16:11** — Greek reads "like a wall you have renewed," rendering the Hebrew place-name Kir-heres as a phrase describing a rebuilt wall.
- **16:14** — Greek shifts to first-person speech ("And now I say") where the Hebrew text reads "now the Lord has spoken."
