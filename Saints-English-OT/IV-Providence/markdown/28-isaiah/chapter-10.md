## Text

**10:1** Woe to those who write wickedness; for in writing, they write wickedness,

**10:2** turning aside the justice of the poor, snatching away the judgment of the needy of My people, so that the widow becomes their plunder and the orphan their spoil.

**10:3** And what shall they do in the day of visitation? For affliction shall come upon you from afar. And to whom shall you flee to be helped? And where shall you leave your glory, lest you fall into captivity?

**10:4** For all these things His anger has not turned away, but His hand is still raised high.

**10:5** Woe to the Assyrians! The rod of My wrath and anger is in their hands.

**10:6** I will send My anger against a lawless nation, and I will command My people to take spoil and plunder, and to trample down the cities and turn them into dust.

**10:7** Yet he did not intend it thus, nor has he reasoned thus in his soul; rather, his mind shall change, even to utterly destroy nations not a few.

**10:8** And if they say to him, "You alone are ruler,"

**10:9** then he shall say, "Did I not capture the region above Babylon and Calneh, where the tower was built? And I captured Arabia, and Damascus, and Samaria.

**10:10** In the same manner as I captured these, I shall also capture all the realms. Wail, you carved images in Jerusalem and in Samaria;

**10:11** for just as I did to Samaria and her hand-made images, so I shall also do to Jerusalem and her idols."

**10:12** And it shall come to pass, when the Lord finishes doing all things on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He shall bring judgment upon the haughty mind, upon the ruler of the Assyrians, and upon the loftiness of the glory of his eyes.

**10:13** For he said, "By strength I shall act, and by the wisdom of understanding I shall remove the boundaries of nations, and their strength I shall plunder;

**10:14** and I shall shake inhabited cities, and I shall seize the whole inhabited world in my hand like a nest, and like forsaken eggs I shall take them up; and there is none who shall escape me or speak against me."

**10:15** Shall an axe be glorified without the one who chops with it? Or shall a saw be exalted without the one who pulls it? Just as when one lifts a rod or a wooden staff—and is it not so?

**10:16** Rather, the Lord of hosts shall send dishonor upon your honor, and upon your glory a burning fire shall burn;

**10:17** and the Light of Israel shall become a fire, and He shall sanctify him in a burning fire, and it shall devour the woodland like grass.

**10:18** In that day the mountains and the hills and the forests shall be extinguished, and it shall devour from soul even to flesh; and the one who flees shall be like one fleeing from a burning flame.

**10:19** And those who remain of them shall be a mere number, and a little child shall write them down.

**10:20** And it shall come to pass in that day that the remnant of Israel shall no longer be added to, and those of Jacob who have been saved shall by no means trust any longer in those who wronged them, but they shall trust in God, the Holy One of Israel, in truth.

**10:21** And the remnant of Jacob shall rest upon the mighty God.

**10:22** And though the people of Israel become like the sand of the sea, the remnant of them shall be saved;

**10:23** bringing a word to completion and cutting it short in righteousness, because the Lord shall execute a shortened word in the whole inhabited world.

**10:24** Therefore thus says the Lord of hosts: "Do not be afraid, My people, you who dwell in Zion, of the Assyrians, because he shall strike you with a rod; for I am bringing a blow upon you, to see the way of Egypt.

**10:25** For yet a little while and the anger shall cease, and My wrath shall be against their counsel."

**10:26** And God shall rouse Himself against them according to the blow of Midian in a place of affliction, and His wrath shall be on the way by the sea, toward the way to Egypt.

**10:27** And it shall come to pass in that day that his yoke shall be removed from your shoulder, and the fear of him from you, and the yoke shall be destroyed from your shoulders.

**10:28** For he shall come to the city of Aggai, and he shall pass on to Megiddo, and in Michmash he shall deposit his baggage;

**10:29** and he shall pass through a ravine and come to Aggai; fear shall seize Ramah, the city of Saul. She shall flee—

**10:30** the daughter of Gallim; she shall hear in Sa, she shall hear in Anathoth.

**10:31** And Madmenah is dismayed, and those who dwell in Gibbeir.

**10:32** Exhort today to remain on the way; with the hand beckon to the mountain, the daughter of Zion, and you hills that are in Jerusalem.

**10:33** Behold, the Sovereign, the Lord of hosts, confounds the glorious ones with might, and those who are lofty in arrogance shall be crushed, and the lofty shall be brought low,

**10:34** and the lofty shall fall by the sword, and Lebanon shall fall together with the lofty ones.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — The Septuagint reads "command My people" (to emo lao) rather than "charge him against the people of My wrath" as in the Masoretic Text.
- **10:9** — The Septuagint preserves a distinctive geographical list, naming "the region above Babylon and Calneh, where the tower was built" (alluding to Genesis 11) and "Arabia" in place of Carchemish, Arpad, and Hamath.
- **10:23** — Verses 22-23 closely match the Septuagint form quoted by the apostle Paul in Romans 9:27-28, including "a shortened word" (logon syntetmenon).
- **10:28** — Verses 28-31 preserve several Septuagint-distinct place names: Aggai (in both verses 28 and 29, for MT Aiath and Geba), Megiddo (for MT Migron), Sa (for MT Laishah), and Gibbeir (for MT Gebim).
