## Text

**44:1** "Yet now hear, Jacob My servant, and Israel whom I have chosen,"

**44:2** thus says the Lord God who made you, and who formed you from the womb: "You shall yet be helped; do not fear, My servant Jacob, and beloved Israel whom I have chosen.

**44:3** For I will give water in thirst to those who walk in a waterless land; I will put My Spirit upon your seed, and My blessings upon your children;

**44:4** and they shall spring up like grass in the midst of water, and like a willow beside flowing water.

**44:5** One shall say, 'I belong to God,' and another shall cry out in the name of Jacob; and another shall inscribe upon his hand, 'I belong to God,' and shall also cry out in the name of Israel."

**44:6** Thus says the King of Israel and He who delivers him, God of Sabaoth: "I am the first and I am after these things; besides Me there is no God.

**44:7** Who is like Me? Let him stand and call out and declare, and prepare it for Me from the time I made man forever; and let them declare to you the things that are coming before they arrive.

**44:8** Do not hide yourselves nor be led astray. Have you not given ear from the beginning, and I declared it to you? You are witnesses: is there a God besides Me?"

**44:9** And they did not hear then. Those who fashion and those who carve are all vain things, making the desires of their heart, which shall not profit them; rather, they shall be put to shame.

**44:10** Those who fashion a god and carve it are all unprofitable things,

**44:11** and all from whom they came have dried up, and they are deaf among men. Let them all be gathered and stand together, and let them be confounded and put to shame together.

**44:12** For the craftsman sharpened iron; with an adze he worked it, and with a drill he set it up, and he worked it with the arm of his strength; yet he shall hunger and grow weak, and shall by no means drink water.

**44:13** A craftsman, having chosen wood, set it up by measure, and with glue he shaped it, and made it like the form of a man and like the comeliness of a human being, to set it up in a house.

**44:14** He cut wood from the forest which the Lord planted, and the rain caused to grow tall,

**44:15** that it might be for men to burn; and taking from it, he warmed himself, and having kindled a fire, they baked loaves upon them; but the rest they fashioned into gods, and they bow down to them.

**44:16** Half of it he burned up in the fire, and upon half of it he baked loaves on the coals, and roasting meat upon it he ate and was filled; and having warmed himself, he said, "It is pleasant to me, for I have grown warm and have seen the fire!"

**44:17** But the rest he made into a carved god, and he bows down and prays to it, saying, "Deliver me, for you are my god."

**44:18** They did not know how to be discerning, for they have been darkened from seeing with their eyes and from perceiving with their heart.

**44:19** And he did not consider in his soul, nor did he know in his understanding, that he burned up half of it in the fire, and baked loaves upon its coals, and roasted meat and ate it, and made the rest of it into an abomination, and they bow down to it.

**44:20** Know that their heart is ashes, and they are led astray, and no one is able to deliver his soul; look, you will not say, "There is a lie in my right hand."

**44:21** "Remember these things, Jacob and Israel, for you are My servant; I have formed you as My servant, and you, Israel, do not forget Me.

**44:22** For behold, I have blotted out your iniquities like a cloud, and your sins like a dark mist; return to Me, and I will redeem you."

**44:23** Be glad, O heavens, for God has had mercy on Israel; sound the trumpet, you foundations of the earth; shout gladness, O mountains, you hills and all the trees that are in them; for God has redeemed Jacob, and Israel shall be glorified.

**44:24** Thus says the Lord, who redeems you and forms you from the womb: "I am the Lord who accomplishes all things; I stretched out the heaven alone, and I established the earth.

**44:25** Who else shall frustrate the signs of ventriloquists and divinations from the heart, turning wise men backward and making their counsel foolish,

**44:26** and confirming the words of His servant, and proving true the counsel of His messengers? He who says to Jerusalem, 'You shall be inhabited,' and to the cities of Idumea, 'You shall be built,' and her desolate places shall spring up;

**44:27** who says to the deep, 'You shall be laid waste, and I will dry up your rivers';

**44:28** who bids Cyrus to be wise, and says, 'He shall perform all My desires'; who says to Jerusalem, 'You shall be built, and I will lay the foundation of My holy house.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:2** — "Beloved Israel" translates the Septuagint's ho egapemenos Israel, interpreting the poetic Hebrew name "Jeshurun."
- **44:6** — "God of Sabaoth" preserves the Greek transliteration theos sabaoth (meaning "God of hosts"), and "after these things" follows the Septuagint's meta tauta rather than "the last."
- **44:21** — The Septuagint has the active imperative "do not forget Me" (me epilanthanou mou), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads passively, "you will not be forgotten by Me."
- **44:26** — "Idumea" preserves the Septuagint reading Idoumaias (Edom), where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "Judah."
- **44:28** — "Who bids Cyrus to be wise" translates the Septuagint's ho legon Kyro phronein, where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "who says of Cyrus, 'He is My shepherd.'"
