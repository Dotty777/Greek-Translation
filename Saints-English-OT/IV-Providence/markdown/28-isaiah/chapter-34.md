## Text

**34:1** Draw near, O nations, and listen, O rulers; let the earth hear and those who are in it, the inhabited world and the people who are in it.

**34:2** For the fury of the Lord is upon all the nations, and wrath is upon their number, to destroy them and deliver them up to slaughter.

**34:3** And their wounded shall be cast out, and the dead, and their stench shall rise up, and the mountains shall be drenched with their blood.

**34:4** And all the powers of the heavens shall melt away, and the heaven shall be rolled up like a scroll; and all the stars shall fall like leaves from a vine, and as leaves fall from a fig tree.

**34:5** My sword has been made drunk in heaven; behold, it shall come down upon Idumea, and upon the people of destruction with judgment.

**34:6** The sword of the Lord has been filled with blood; it has been thickened with fat, with the blood of goats and lambs, and with the fat of goats and rams. For there is a sacrifice to the Lord in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in Idumea,

**34:7** and the mighty ones shall fall together with them, both the rams and the bulls; and the land shall be made drunk with the blood, and with their fat it shall be filled.

**34:8** For it is the day of the judgment of the Lord, and a year of recompense for the judgment of Zion.

**34:9** And her ravines shall be turned into pitch, and her land into brimstone; and her land shall be like pitch burning

**34:10** night and day, and it shall not be quenched forever; and her smoke shall ascend upward; throughout her generations she shall be made desolate, and for a long time.

**34:11** Birds and hedgehogs and ibises and ravens shall dwell in her; and a measuring cord of desert shall be cast over her, and onocentaurs shall inhabit her.

**34:12** Her rulers shall be no more, for her kings and her nobles shall come to destruction.

**34:13** And thorny trees shall spring up in their cities and in her strongholds, and she shall become dwellings of sirens and a court of ostriches;

**34:14** and demons shall meet with onocentaurs, and they shall cry out one to another; there onocentaurs shall rest, having found rest for themselves.

**34:15** There the hedgehog made its nest, and the earth preserved her young in safety; there deer met together and saw one another's faces.

**34:16** By number they passed by, and not one of them perished; one did not seek another, for the Lord commanded them, and His Spirit gathered them together.

**34:17** And He Himself shall cast lots for them, and His hand apportioned it for grazing; forever you shall inherit it; for generations of generations they shall rest upon it.

## Translator Notes

- **34:5** — Here and in verse 6 the Greek uses the distinct Hellenistic regional name 'Idumea' (Idoumaia) corresponding to Hebrew Edom.
- **34:11** — Here and in verse 14 the Greek renders the desert creatures as 'onocentaurs' (donkey-centaurs), alongside 'sirens' in verse 13, reflecting ancient Greek natural-history and mythological vocabulary.
- **34:17** — The Greek shifts briefly to the second-person plural ('you shall inherit') before returning to the third-person plural ('they shall rest').
