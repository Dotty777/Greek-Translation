## Text

**41:1** Be renewed before Me, O islands, for the rulers shall renew their strength; let them draw near and speak together; then let them declare judgment.

**41:2** Who has raised up righteousness from the east, called it to His feet, and it shall go forth? He shall deliver it before the nations and dismay kings; and He shall give their swords to the earth, and their bows like driven brushwood;

**41:3** and He shall pursue them; the way of His feet shall pass through in peace.

**41:4** Who has worked and done these things? He has called it, who calls it from the generations of the beginning; I am God, the first, and for the things to come, I am.

**41:5** The nations saw and were afraid; the ends of the earth drew near and came together,

**41:6** each one deciding to help his neighbor and his brother, and he will say,

**41:7** "The craftsman has grown strong, and the coppersmith striking with the hammer, forging at the same time." At times he will say, "It is a good joining"; he has fastened them with nails; they shall set them up, and they shall not be moved.

**41:8** But you, Israel, My servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham whom I have loved,

**41:9** whom I took hold of from the ends of the earth, and from its watchtowers I called you and said to you, "You are My servant; I have chosen you and have not forsaken you."

**41:10** Do not fear, for I am with you; do not go astray, for I am your God who has strengthened you; and I have helped you, and I have made you secure with My righteous right hand.

**41:11** Behold, all who oppose you shall be ashamed and disgraced; for they shall be as nothing, and all your adversaries shall perish.

**41:12** You shall seek them and shall by no means find the men who will act insolently against you; for they shall be as nothing, and those who wage war against you shall not be.

**41:13** For I am your God, who holds your right hand, who says to you,

**41:14** "Do not fear, Jacob, tiny Israel; I have helped you," says God, who redeems Israel.

**41:15** Behold, I have made you like new saw-toothed threshing wheels of a wagon, and you shall thresh mountains and grind hills fine, and make them like chaff;

**41:16** and you shall winnow them, and the wind shall carry them away, and a tempest shall scatter them. But you shall rejoice in the holy ones of Israel,

**41:17** and the poor and the needy shall exult. For they shall seek water, and there shall be none; their tongue has been parched with thirst. I, the Lord God, the God of Israel, will hear them and will not forsake them,

**41:18** but I will open rivers upon the mountains and springs in the midst of plains; I will turn the wilderness into pools of water and the thirsty land into watercourses.

**41:19** I will set in the waterless land the cedar and the box tree, the myrtle and the cypress and the white poplar,

**41:20** that they may see and know, and consider and understand together, that the hand of the Lord has done these things, and the Holy One of Israel has shown them.

**41:21** "Your judgment draws near," says the Lord God; "your counsels have drawn near," says the King of Jacob.

**41:22** Let them draw near and declare to you what shall come to pass; or tell what the former things were, and we will apply our minds, and we shall know what the last things are; and tell us the things that are to come.

**41:23** Declare to us the things coming at the end, and we shall know that you are gods; do good and do harm, and we shall marvel and see together.

**41:24** For from where are you, and from where is your work? From the earth! They have chosen you as an abomination.

**41:25** But I have raised up the one from the north and the one from the rising of the sun; they shall be called by My name. Let the rulers come, and like potter's clay, and as a potter treads the clay, so you shall be trodden down.

**41:26** For who shall declare the things from the beginning, that we may know, and the things beforehand, and we shall say that they are true? There is no one who foretells, nor anyone who hears your words.

**41:27** I will give a beginning to Zion, and I will comfort Jerusalem.

**41:28** For from among the nations, behold, there is no one, and from among their idols there was none who declared; and if I ask them, "Where are you from?" they shall by no means answer Me.

**41:29** For they are those who make you, and in vain are those who lead you astray.

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — Greek reads "Be renewed" (ἐγκαινίζεσθε) before Me, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Keep silence."
- **41:10** — Greek reads "do not go astray" (μὴ πλανῶ), differing from the Hebrew "do not look around in dismay."
- **41:14** — Greek renders "tiny Israel" (ὀλιγοστὸς Ἰσραήλ, literally "few in number"), where the Hebrew text reads "worm Jacob, men of Israel."
- **41:27** — Greek reads "I will give a beginning to Zion, and I will comfort Jerusalem," diverging notably from the Hebrew "First to Zion, 'Behold, here they are!' and to Jerusalem I will give a herald of good news."
