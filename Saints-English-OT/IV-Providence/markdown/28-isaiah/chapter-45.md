## Text

**45:1** Thus says the Lord God to My anointed, Cyrus, whose right hand I have grasped, that nations may obey before him; and I will shatter the strength of kings, I will open doors before him, and cities shall not be shut:

**45:2** "I will go before you and level mountains; I will crush bronze doors and break iron bars in pieces;

**45:3** and I will give you dark, hidden treasures; unseen ones I will open to you, that you may know that I am the Lord God, who calls your name, the God of Israel.

**45:4** For the sake of My servant Jacob, and Israel My chosen, I will call you by My name and I will accept you; yet you did not know Me.

**45:5** For I am the Lord God, and there is no other besides Me; I strengthened you, and you did not know Me,

**45:6** that those from the rising of the sun and those from the west may know that there is none besides Me. I am the Lord God, and there is no other,

**45:7** I who fashioned light and made darkness, who makes peace and creates evils; I am the Lord God who does all these things.

**45:8** Let heaven rejoice from above, and let the clouds sprinkle righteousness; let the earth spring forth and bring forth mercy, and let it proclaim righteousness together; I am the Lord who created you."

**45:9** What better thing have I fashioned, like a potter's clay? Shall the plowman plow the earth all day long? Shall the clay say to the potter, "What are you making, because you do not work, nor do you have hands?" Shall the thing formed answer the one who formed it?

**45:10** He who says to a father, "What will you father?" and to a mother, "What are you in labor with?"

**45:11** For thus says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel, who made the things that are to come: "Ask Me concerning My sons, and concerning the works of My hands command Me.

**45:12** I made earth and man upon it; I with My hand established heaven; I commanded all the stars.

**45:13** I raised him up as king with righteousness, and all his ways are straight; he shall build My city and turn back the captivity of My people, not with ransom nor with gifts," said the Lord of hosts.

**45:14** Thus says the Lord of hosts: "Egypt has labored, and the merchandise of the Ethiopians, and the Sabeans, men of tall stature, shall cross over to you and shall be your servants; and they shall follow behind you bound in fetters, and shall cross over to you and bow down to you, and shall pray in you, because God is in you, and there is no God besides You."

**45:15** For You are God, and we did not know it, the God of Israel.

**45:16** All who oppose Him shall be put to shame and disgraced, and they shall walk in shame. "Be renewed toward Me, O islands!"

**45:17** Israel is saved by the Lord with an everlasting salvation; they shall not be put to shame nor be disgraced forever.

**45:18** Thus says the Lord who made heaven—this is the God who revealed the earth and made it; He Himself set its bounds, He did not make it in vain, but formed it to be inhabited: "I am, and there is no other.

**45:19** I have not spoken in secret, nor in a dark place of the earth; I did not say to the seed of Jacob, 'Seek what is vain.' I am, I am the Lord, who speaks righteousness and declares truth.

**45:20** Gather yourselves and come, take counsel together, you who are saved from the nations. They have no knowledge who carry their carved image of wood, and pray to gods that do not save.

**45:21** If they will declare it, let them draw near, that they may know together who made these things heard from the beginning; then it was declared to you: 'I am God, and there is no other besides Me, a righteous One and a Savior; there is none apart from Me.'

**45:22** Turn to Me and you shall be saved, you from the end of the earth; I am God, and there is no other.

**45:23** By Myself I swear—surely righteousness shall go forth from My mouth, My words shall not turn back—that to Me every knee shall bow, and every tongue shall swear by God,

**45:24** saying, 'Righteousness and glory shall come to Him, and all who separate themselves shall be put to shame';

**45:25** from the Lord they shall be justified, and in God all the seed of the sons of Israel shall be glorified."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The Greek text reads "that nations may obey before him," "the strength of kings," and "cities," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("to subdue nations," "loins of kings," and "gates").
- **45:4** — The Greek reads "by My name" and "I will accept you," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by your name" and "I have surnamed you."
- **45:9** — The Greek opens with two distinctive rhetorical questions concerning potter's clay and a plowman, where the Hebrew text pronounces a woe upon one who strives with his Maker.
- **45:13** — The Greek adds "as king" and transliterates the divine title as Kyrios Sabaoth ("the Lord of hosts"), as also in 45:14 and 47:4.
- **45:15** — The Greek reads "and we did not know it" and omits "Savior," contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "a God who hides Himself... the Savior."
