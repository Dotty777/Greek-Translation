## Text

**6:1** And it came to pass in the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated upon a high and exalted throne, and the house was full of His glory.

**6:2** And seraphim stood around Him: one had six wings, and another had six wings; with two they covered their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew.

**6:3** And one cried out to another and they said, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Sabaoth; all the earth is full of His glory."

**6:4** And the lintel was lifted up at the voice with which they cried out, and the house was filled with smoke.

**6:5** And I said, "Alas, wretched am I, for I am stunned; for being a man and having unclean lips, I dwell in the midst of a people having unclean lips, and I have seen the King, the Lord Sabaoth, with my eyes."

**6:6** And one of the seraphim was sent to me, and in his hand he held a burning coal, which he had taken with the tongs from the altar.

**6:7** And he touched my mouth and said, "Behold, this has touched your lips; it shall take away your iniquities, and thoroughly cleanse your sins."

**6:8** And I heard the voice of the Lord saying, "Whom shall I send, and who will go to this people?" And I said, "Behold, here I am; send me."

**6:9** And He said, "Go and say to this people, 'Hearing you shall hear and shall by no means understand, and seeing you shall see and shall by no means perceive.'

**6:10** For the heart of this people has grown thick, and with their ears they have heard heavily, and they have shut their eyes, lest they should ever see with their eyes and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart and turn back, and I should heal them."

**6:11** And I said, "How long, O Lord?" And He said, "Until cities are made desolate because they are not inhabited, and houses because there are no people, and the land shall be left desolate.

**6:12** And after these things God shall remove the people far away, and those who are left shall be multiplied upon the land.

**6:13** And yet a tenth part remains upon it, and again it shall be for plunder, like a terebinth and like an acorn when it falls out of its husk."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek reads "the house was full of His glory," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's description of the hem of His robe filling the temple.
- **6:3** — "Sabaoth" preserves the Greek transliteration of the Hebrew divine title meaning "of hosts" or "of armies."
- **6:10** — Rather than the Hebrew imperatives ("Make the heart of this people dull"), the Greek uses past indicatives stating that the people themselves have grown thick of heart and shut their eyes (a reading quoted in Matthew 13:15 and Acts 28:27).
- **6:12** — The Greek reads "those who are left shall be multiplied upon the land" (diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to many forsaken places), and 6:13 likewise concludes with the acorn falling from its husk without the Hebrew's final phrase about a "holy seed."
