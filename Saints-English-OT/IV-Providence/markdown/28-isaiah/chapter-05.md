## Text

**5:1** Now I will sing to the Beloved a song of my Beloved concerning my vineyard. A vineyard belonged to the Beloved on a horn, in a fertile place.

**5:2** And I placed a hedge around it and fenced it in, and I planted a Sorek vine, and I built a tower in its midst, and I dug a winepress vat in it; and I waited for it to bring forth grapes, and it brought forth thorns.

**5:3** And now, you who dwell in Jerusalem and man of Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard.

**5:4** What more shall I do for My vineyard that I have not done for it? Because I waited for it to bring forth grapes, yet it brought forth thorns.

**5:5** And now I will declare to you what I will do to My vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it shall be for plunder; and I will tear down its wall, and it shall be for trampling underfoot.

**5:6** And I will forsake My vineyard, and it shall neither be pruned nor dug, and thorns shall spring up upon it as upon dry wasteland; and I will command the clouds not to send rain upon it.

**5:7** For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the man of Judah is a beloved young plant; I waited for it to execute justice, yet it practiced lawlessness, and not righteousness, but an outcry.

**5:8** Woe to those who join house to house and bring field near to field, that they may take something away from their neighbor! Shall you dwell alone upon the earth?

**5:9** For these things have been heard in the ears of the Lord of hosts: for even if many houses come to be, they shall become a desolation—great and fair—and there shall be none dwelling in them.

**5:10** For where ten yokes of oxen labor, it shall yield one jar, and he who sows six artabas shall yield three measures.

**5:11** Woe to those who rise early in the morning and pursue strong drink, who linger late into the evening; for wine shall inflame them.

**5:12** For with lyre and harp, and timbrels and flutes, they drink wine, yet they do not regard the works of the Lord, nor do they consider the works of His hands.

**5:13** Therefore My people have become captive because they do not know the Lord, and a multitude of the dead has come to be because of famine and thirst for water.

**5:14** And Hades has enlarged its soul and opened wide its mouth without ceasing; and her glorious ones and her great ones and her rich ones and her pestilent ones shall descend.

**5:15** And mankind shall be humbled, and man shall be dishonored, and the lofty eyes shall be brought low;

**5:16** and the Lord of hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and the holy God shall be glorified in righteousness;

**5:17** and those who were plundered shall graze like bulls, and lambs shall eat the desolate places of those who were taken away.

**5:18** Woe to those who draw sins to themselves as with a long rope, and lawless deeds as with the yoke strap of a heifer,

**5:19** who say, "Let what He will do draw near with speed, that we may see, and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come, that we may know!"

**5:20** Woe to those who declare evil to be good and good to be evil, who make darkness light and light darkness, who make bitter sweet and sweet bitter.

**5:21** Woe to those who are prudent in themselves, and knowledgeable in their own sight.

**5:22** Woe to the strong ones among you who drink wine, and the mighty men who mingle strong drink,

**5:23** who justify the ungodly for the sake of bribes, and take away the right of the righteous.

**5:24** Therefore, just as stubble shall be burned by a coal of fire and consumed by an unrestrained flame, so their root shall be like chaff, and their blossom shall rise up like dust; for they did not desire the law of the Lord of hosts, but provoked the oracle of the Holy One of Israel.

**5:25** And the Lord of hosts was incensed with wrath against His people, and He laid His hand upon them and struck them; and the mountains were provoked, and their carcasses became like refuse in the midst of the road. Yet for all this His fury was not turned away, but His hand is still raised high.

**5:26** Therefore He will lift up a signal among the nations that are afar off, and He will whistle for them from the end of the earth; and behold, swiftly and lightly they come.

**5:27** They shall neither hunger nor grow weary, neither shall they slumber nor sleep; neither shall they loosen their belts from their loins, nor shall the straps of their sandals be broken;

**5:28** whose arrows are sharp, and their bows are strung. The feet of their horses are like solid rock; the wheels of their chariots have been reckoned like a tempest.

**5:29** They rage like lions, they have taken their stand like a lion's whelps; and he will seize, and cry out like a wild beast, and cast them out, and there shall be none to deliver them,

**5:30** and he will roar on their account in that day like the sound of a surging sea; and they shall look to the earth, and behold, harsh darkness in their perplexity.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — "On a horn" (en kerati) renders literally the idiom for a hillside spur; the Greek also introduces the first-person "my vineyard" here and maintains first-person verbs throughout verses 2–7.
- **5:2** — "Sorek" transliterates the Greek loanword sorek (a choice red vine), and the Septuagint reads "thorns" (akanthas) where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "wild grapes."
- **5:10** — "Six artabas" and "three measures" preserve the Septuagint's Hellenistic Egyptian dry-measure terminology and quantities rather than the Hebrew homer and ephah.
- **5:17** — The Septuagint reads "those who were plundered shall graze like bulls," a distinctive Greek rendering differing from the Masoretic Hebrew image of lambs grazing in pasture.
