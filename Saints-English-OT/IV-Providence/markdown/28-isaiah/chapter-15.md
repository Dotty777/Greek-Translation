## Text

**15:1** The word against the land of Moab. By night the land of Moab shall perish, for by night the wall of the land of Moab shall perish.

**15:2** Grieve for yourselves, for Dibon also shall perish, where your altar is; there you shall go up to weep, upon Nebo of the land of Moab. Wail! Upon every head is baldness, and all arms are gashed.

**15:3** In her broad squares gird yourselves with sackcloth, and lament upon her housetops and in her streets; all of you wail with weeping.

**15:4** For Heshbon and Elealeh have cried out; as far as Jahaz their voice has been heard; therefore the loins of the land of Moab cry out; her soul shall know it.

**15:5** The heart of the land of Moab cries out within her as far as Zoar, for she is a heifer of three years old; and by the ascent of Luhith they shall go up to you weeping by the way of Horonaim; destruction and earthquake cry out.

**15:6** The water of Nimrim shall be desolate, and her grass shall fail; for there shall be no green grass.

**15:7** Even so, shall she be saved? For I will bring Arabians upon the valley, and they shall take it.

**15:8** For the outcry has reached the border of the land of Moab, of Eglaim, and her wailing as far as the well of Elim.

**15:9** And the water of Dimon shall be filled with blood; for I will bring Arabians upon Dimon, and I will take away the seed of Moab, and Ariel, and the remnant of Admah.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — Greek reads "the land of Moab" (Moabeitis) and "the wall of the land of Moab" where the Hebrew text names Ar and Kir of Moab.
- **15:2** — Greek reads "all arms are gashed" (brachiones) rather than the Hebrew "every beard is cut off."
- **15:7** — Greek reads "Arabians" (Arabas), reflecting an alternate vocalization of the Hebrew word for "willows" (likewise in 15:9).
- **15:9** — Greek treats the Hebrew words for "lion" and "the ground" as proper names, "Ariel" and "Admah."
