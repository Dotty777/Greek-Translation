## Text

**8:1** And the Lord said to me, "Take for yourself a great new scroll, and write upon it with a man's stylus concerning swiftly making a plunder of spoils, for it is at hand;

**8:2** and make faithful men witnesses for Me: Uriah, and Zechariah the son of Berechiah."

**8:3** And I approached the prophetess, and she conceived in the womb and bore a son. And the Lord said to me, "Call his name 'Quickly Spoil, Swiftly Plunder';

**8:4** for before the child knows how to call out father or mother, he shall take the power of Damascus and the spoils of Samaria before the king of the Assyrians."

**8:5** And the Lord continued speaking to me yet again:

**8:6** "Because this people does not desire the water of Siloam that flows gently, but desires to have Rezin and the son of Remaliah as king over you,

**8:7** therefore, behold, the Lord brings up upon you the water of the river, mighty and abundant, even the king of the Assyrians and his glory; and he shall come up over every ravine of yours, and shall walk over every wall of yours,

**8:8** and he shall remove from Judea any man who is able to lift up his head or capable of accomplishing anything; and his encampment shall be so as to fill the breadth of your land." God is with us.

**8:9** Know, O nations, and be defeated; give ear, even to the farthest part of the earth; though you have grown strong, be defeated; for if you grow strong again, again you shall be defeated.

**8:10** And whatever counsel you may devise, the Lord shall scatter it; and the word which you speak shall by no means abide among you, for God is with us.

**8:11** Thus says the Lord: "With a strong hand they refuse the course of the way of this people, saying,

**8:12** 'Let them never say what is hard; for everything that this people says is hard; and you shall by no means fear its fear, nor be troubled.

**8:13** Sanctify the Lord Himself, and He shall be your fear.'

**8:14** And if you trust in Him, He shall be to you a sanctuary, and you shall not encounter Him as a stone of stumbling, nor as a rock of falling. But the houses of Jacob are in a snare, and those dwelling in Jerusalem are in a hollow pit;

**8:15** therefore many among them shall grow weak, and they shall fall and be crushed, and men living in security shall draw near and be captured."

**8:16** Then those who seal up the law so as not to learn shall be made manifest.

**8:17** And he shall say, "I will wait for God, who has turned away His face from the house of Jacob, and I will trust in Him.

**8:18** Behold, I and the children whom God has given me. And they shall be signs and wonders in the house of Israel from the Lord Sabaoth, who dwells on Mount Zion."

**8:19** And if they say to you, "Seek the mediums, and those who call out from the earth, those who speak empty words, who voice sounds out of their belly"—shall not a nation seek after its God? Why do they seek the dead on behalf of the living?

**8:20** For He gave the law for help, that they might speak not according to this word, concerning which there are no gifts to give for it.

**8:21** And a severe famine shall come upon you; and it shall come to pass, when you grow hungry, you shall be grieved and shall speak evil of the ruler and the ancestral traditions; and they shall look up to heaven above,

**8:22** and they shall gaze upon the earth below; and behold, dire perplexity and darkness, affliction and anguish and darkness so as not to see; and the one who is in anguish shall not be at a loss until a season.

**8:23** Drink this first, act swiftly, O region of Zebulun, the land of Naphtali, and the rest along the seacoast and beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Here and in 8:3 the Greek translates the symbolic Hebrew name Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz ("Quickly Spoil, Swiftly Plunder") rather than transliterating it.
- **8:8** — The Greek reads "his encampment" (rather than "his outstretched wings") and translates the name Immanuel at the close of the verse as "God is with us."
- **8:14** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and the New Testament quotations in Romans 9:33 and 1 Peter 2:8), the Greek inserts "not," promising that the one who trusts in the Lord will not encounter Him as a stone of stumbling or a rock of falling.
- **8:20** — The Greek reads "the law for help" and "no gifts to give" (reflecting a reading of the Hebrew for "dawn" as "bribe/gift"), differing sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:23** — This verse corresponds to 9:1 in most English Bibles; the Greek opens with the distinctive imperatives "Drink this first, act swiftly."
