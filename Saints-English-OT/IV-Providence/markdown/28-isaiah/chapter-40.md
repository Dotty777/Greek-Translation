## Text

**40:1** "Comfort, comfort My people," says God.

**40:2** O priests, speak to the heart of Jerusalem, comfort her, because her humiliation has been fulfilled; her sin has been remitted, for she has received from the hand of the Lord double for her sins.

**40:3** A voice of one crying out in the wilderness: "Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight the paths of our God.

**40:4** Every valley shall be filled up, and every mountain and hill shall be brought low; and all the crooked places shall become a straight path, and the rough way into level plains,

**40:5** and the glory of the Lord shall appear, and all flesh shall see the salvation of God; for the Lord has spoken."

**40:6** A voice of one saying, "Cry out!" And I said, "What shall I cry?" "All flesh is grass, and all the glory of man is like a flower of the grass.

**40:7** The grass has withered, and the flower has fallen away,

**40:8** but the word of our God abides forever."

**40:9** Go up upon a high mountain, you who bring good tidings to Zion; lift up your voice with strength, you who bring good tidings to Jerusalem; lift it up, do not be afraid; say to the cities of Judah, "Behold your God!"

**40:10** Behold, the Lord, the Lord comes with strength, and His arm with dominion; behold, His reward is with Him, and His work before Him.

**40:11** Like a shepherd He shall tend His flock, and with His arm He shall gather the lambs, and those who are with young He shall comfort.

**40:12** Who has measured the water in the hand, and heaven by a span, and all the earth by a handful? Who has weighed the mountains in a balance and the valleys on a scale?

**40:13** Who has known the mind of the Lord, and who has become His counselor, who shall instruct Him?

**40:14** Or with whom did He take counsel, that he instructed Him? Or who showed Him judgment? Or who showed Him the way of understanding?

**40:15** If all the nations have been reckoned as a drop from a bucket and as the tipping of a scale, they shall be reckoned as spittle;

**40:16** and Lebanon is not sufficient for burning, nor are all the four-footed beasts sufficient for a whole burnt offering,

**40:17** and all the nations are as nothing, and as nothingness they have been reckoned.

**40:18** To whom have you likened the Lord, and to what likeness have you likened Him?

**40:19** Did a craftsman make an image, or did a goldsmith melt gold and overlay it with gold? Did he fashion it as a likeness?

**40:20** For a craftsman chooses wood that will not rot, and skillfully he will seek how to set up an image of it so that it may not be shaken.

**40:21** Will you not know? Will you not hear? Was it not declared to you from the beginning? Have you not known the foundations of the earth?

**40:22** It is He who holds fast the circle of the earth, and those who dwell in it are like locusts; who has established heaven like a vault and stretched it out like a tent to dwell in,

**40:23** who gives rulers to rule as nothing, and has made the earth as nothing.

**40:24** For they shall by no means plant, nor shall they sow, nor shall their root take root in the earth; He has blown upon them and they withered, and a tempest shall carry them away like brushwood.

**40:25** "Now therefore, to whom have you likened Me, and I shall be exalted?" said the Holy One.

**40:26** Lift up your eyes on high and behold: who has displayed all these things? He who brings forth His ordered host by number shall call them all by name by reason of great glory, and in the might of strength nothing has escaped You.

**40:27** For do not say, O Jacob—and why did you speak, O Israel—"My way has been hidden from God, and my God has taken away judgment and departed"?

**40:28** And now, have you not known? Have you not heard? God everlasting, the God who fashioned the ends of the earth, shall neither hunger nor grow weary, nor is there any searching out of His understanding,

**40:29** giving strength to those who hunger, and sorrow to those who are not in pain.

**40:30** For younger men shall hunger, and youths shall grow weary, and chosen men shall be without strength;

**40:31** but those who wait upon God shall renew their strength; they shall sprout wings like eagles; they shall run and not grow weary; they shall walk and not hunger.

## Translator Notes

- **40:2** — The Greek explicitly addresses "O priests" and speaks of Jerusalem's "humiliation" being fulfilled.
- **40:5** — The Greek reads "the salvation of God" (the wording later cited in Luke 3:6), and in verse 7 omits the clause about the breath of the Lord blowing on the grass.
- **40:10** — The Greek doubles the divine title (kyrios Kyrios), rendered literally here as "the Lord, the Lord."
- **40:26** — The final clause literally reads "nothing has escaped You," shifting to second-person singular (often understood as an apostrophe to God, or alternatively an address to Jacob/Israel).
- **40:29** — The Greek literally states that God gives "sorrow to those who are not in pain," and throughout verses 28-31 uses "hunger" where the Hebrew has "faint."
