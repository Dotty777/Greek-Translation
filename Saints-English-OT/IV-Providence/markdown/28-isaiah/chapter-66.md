## Text

**66:1** Thus says the Lord: "Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet. What sort of house will you build for Me? And what is the place of My rest?

**66:2** For all these things My hand has made, and all these things are Mine," says the Lord; "and upon whom shall I look, except upon him who is humble and quiet, and who trembles at My words?

**66:3** But the lawless one who sacrifices a calf to Me is like one who kills a dog, and he who offers fine flour is like one who offers swine's blood, and he who gives frankincense for a memorial is like a blasphemer. And they have chosen their own ways, and their soul has desired their abominations;

**66:4** so I also will take up their mockeries, and I will repay their sins to them; because I called them and they did not obey Me, I spoke and they did not listen, and they did evil before Me and chose the things that I did not desire."

**66:5** Hear the words of the Lord, you who tremble at His word: Say, O our brethren, to those who hate you and abhor you, that the name of the Lord may be glorified and appear in their gladness, and those others shall be put to shame.

**66:6** A voice of outcry from the city, a voice from the temple, the voice of the Lord rendering recompense to the adversaries.

**66:7** Before she who was in labor gave birth, before the pain of travail came, she escaped and brought forth a male child.

**66:8** Who has heard such a thing, and who has seen it thus? Has a land been in labor in one day, or has a nation also been born all at once? For Zion has travailed and brought forth her children.

**66:9** "Yet I gave this expectation, and you did not remember Me," said the Lord. "Behold, did I not make both her who bears and her who is barren?" said your God.

**66:10** Be glad, O Jerusalem, and hold a festival in her, all you who love her; rejoice with joy together with her, all of you who mourn over her,

**66:11** that you may nurse and be satisfied from the breast of her consolation, that having nursed to the full you may delight yourselves from the entrance of her glory.

**66:12** For thus says the Lord: "Behold, I turn aside toward them like a river of peace, and like an overflowing torrent the glory of the nations; their children shall be borne upon shoulders and comforted upon knees.

**66:13** As one whom a mother comforts, so I also will comfort you, and in Jerusalem you shall be comforted.

**66:14** And you shall see, and your heart shall rejoice, and your bones shall spring up like tender grass; and the hand of the Lord shall be known to those who fear Him, and He shall threaten the disobedient."

**66:15** For behold, the Lord will come as fire, and His chariots as a tempest, to render His vengeance in wrath and His utter rejection in a flame of fire.

**66:16** For by the fire of the Lord all the earth shall be judged, and by His sword all flesh; many shall be wounded by the Lord.

**66:17** "Those who sanctify themselves and purify themselves for the gardens, and who in the doorways eat swine's flesh and the abominations and the mouse, shall be consumed together," said the Lord.

**66:18** "And I—their works and their reasoning—I am coming to gather all the nations and the tongues, and they shall come and behold My glory.

**66:19** And I will leave a sign upon them, and I will send forth those who are saved from among them to the nations—to Tarshish and Put and Lud and Meshech, and to Tubal and to Greece and the islands far off—who have neither heard My name nor seen My glory; and they shall declare My glory among the nations.

**66:20** And they shall bring your brethren out of all the nations as a gift to the Lord, with horses and chariots, in covered mule-wagons with canopies, to the holy city Jerusalem," said the Lord, "just as the sons of Israel would bring their sacrifices to Me with psalms into the house of the Lord.

**66:21** And from among them I will take priests and Levites," said the Lord.

**66:22** "For just as the new heaven and the new earth which I make remain before Me," says the Lord, "so shall your seed and your name stand.

**66:23** And it shall come to pass from month to month and from Sabbath to Sabbath that all flesh shall come to worship before Me in Jerusalem," said the Lord.

**66:24** "And they shall go forth and look upon the corpses of the men who have transgressed against Me; for their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched, and they shall be a spectacle to all flesh."

## Translator Notes

- **66:5** — Greek uses an imperative ("Say, O our brethren") addressed to the faithful rather than a report of what hostile brethren said.
- **66:9** — Greek reads "Yet I gave this expectation, and you did not remember Me," differing notably from the rhetorical question in the Hebrew text.
- **66:18** — The opening clause is elliptical in Greek ("And I—their works and their reasoning"), leaving a verb such as "know" unexpressed before "I am coming."
- **66:19** — Greek lists "Put" (Phoud) instead of "Pul," reads "Meshech" (Mosoch) where the Hebrew has "who draw the bow," and renders "Javan" as "Greece" (ten Hellada).
