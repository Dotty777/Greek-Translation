## Text

**14:1** And the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and they shall rest upon their own land; and the sojourner shall be joined to them, and shall be joined to the house of Jacob.

**14:2** And nations shall take them and bring them into their own place, and they shall inherit, and they shall be multiplied upon the land as menservants and maidservants; and those who took them captive shall become captives, and those who ruled over them shall be ruled over.

**14:3** And it shall come to pass in that day that the Lord will give you rest from your pain and your wrath, from your hard bondage in which you served them.

**14:4** And you shall take up this lamentation against the king of Babylon, and say: "How has the exactor ceased, and how has the taskmaster ceased?

**14:5** The Lord has broken the yoke of the sinners, the yoke of the rulers.

**14:6** Having struck a nation in wrath with an incurable blow, smiting a nation with a blow of wrath that did not spare, he has come to rest in confidence.

**14:7** All the earth cries aloud with gladness,

**14:8** and the trees of Lebanon have rejoiced over you, and the cedar of Lebanon: 'Since you have fallen asleep, the one who cuts us down has not come up.'

**14:9** Hades from below was embittered upon meeting you; all the giants who ruled the earth have been roused together for you, who raised up from their thrones all the kings of the nations.

**14:10** All of them shall answer and say to you, 'You also have been captured just as we were, and you have been reckoned among us!'

**14:11** Your glory has gone down into Hades, your great gladness; beneath you they shall spread decay, and your covering is the worm.

**14:12** How has the morning star, who rises at dawn, fallen from heaven? He who sent to all the nations has been shattered to the earth.

**14:13** Yet you said in your mind, 'I will ascend into heaven; above the stars of heaven I will set my throne; I will sit upon a high mountain, upon the high mountains toward the north;

**14:14** I will ascend above the clouds; I will be like the Most High.'

**14:15** Yet now you shall go down into Hades, even into the foundations of the earth.

**14:16** Those who see you shall marvel at you and say, 'This is the man who provoked the earth, who shook kings,

**14:17** who made the whole inhabited earth a desolation, and pulled down his cities, who did not release those in captivity.'

**14:18** All the kings of the nations have fallen asleep in honor, each man in his own house;

**14:19** but you shall be cast out in the mountains, like an abominable corpse, with many dead who were pierced through with swords, going down into Hades.

**14:20** Just as a garment stained with blood shall not be clean, so neither shall you be clean, because you have destroyed my land and slain my people; you shall by no means remain for all time, O wicked offspring.

**14:21** Prepare your children to be slaughtered for the sins of their father, lest they rise up and inherit the earth and fill the earth with wars."

**14:22** "And I will rise up against them," says the Lord of hosts, "and I will destroy their name and remnant and offspring." Thus says the Lord:

**14:23** "And I will make Babylonia a desolation so that hedgehogs dwell there, and it shall come to nothing; and I will make it a pit of mire for destruction."

**14:24** Thus says the Lord of hosts: "Just as I have spoken, so shall it be, and just as I have purposed, so shall it abide,

**14:25** to destroy the Assyrians upon My land and upon My mountains; and they shall be trampled underfoot, and their yoke shall be taken away from them, and their glory shall be removed from their shoulders."

**14:26** This is the counsel which the Lord has purposed against the whole inhabited earth, and this is the uplifted hand against all the nations.

**14:27** For the things which the holy God has purposed, who shall frustrate? And His uplifted hand, who shall turn back?

**14:28** In the year in which King Ahaz died, this word came:

**14:29** Do not rejoice, all you foreigners, because the yoke of him who struck you has been broken; for out of the seed of a serpent shall come forth the offspring of asps, and their offspring shall come forth as flying serpents.

**14:30** And the poor shall be fed through Him, and poor men shall rest in peace; but He shall slay your offspring with famine, and He shall slay your remnant.

**14:31** Wail, O gates of cities! Let the troubled cities cry aloud, all you foreigners! For smoke comes from the north, and there is no way to endure.

**14:32** And what shall the kings of the nations answer? "The Lord has founded Zion, and through Him the humble of the people shall be saved."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — "The sojourner" translates Greek gioras, an Aramaic loanword used in the Septuagint for a resident alien or proselyte joining Israel.
- **14:12** — The Greek uses the third person ("How has the morning star... fallen") rather than direct address, and renders the second clause "he who sent to all the nations" (ho apostellon), where the Masoretic Hebrew has "who laid low the nations."
- **14:20** — The Greek reads "my land" and "my people" (spoken within the lament), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew has "your land" and "your people."
- **14:29** — "Foreigners" (allophyloi, literally "people of another tribe") is the Septuagint's regular term for the Philistines (also in 14:31).
- **14:31** — "There is no way to endure" renders the terse Septuagint idiom ouk estin tou einai (literally "there is not of being," meaning no possibility of remaining or surviving).
