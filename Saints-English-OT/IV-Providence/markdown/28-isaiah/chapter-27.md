## Text

**27:1** In that day God shall bring the holy, great, and mighty sword upon the dragon, the fleeing serpent, upon the dragon, the crooked serpent; He shall slay the dragon.

**27:2** In that day, a fair vineyard—a desire to begin a song against her.

**27:3** I am a strong city, a besieged city; in vain I shall water her, for by night she shall be captured, and by day the wall shall fall.

**27:4** There is none that has not laid hold of her. Who will set Me to guard stubble in a field? Because of this hostility I have rejected her. Therefore, because of this, the Lord has done all that He appointed. "I have been burned up,"

**27:5** those who dwell in her shall cry out, "Let us make peace."

**27:6** Those who are coming, the children of Jacob—Israel shall sprout and blossom, and the inhabited world shall be filled with his fruit.

**27:7** As he himself struck, shall he also be struck in the same way? And as he slew, shall he thus be slain?

**27:8** Contending and reproaching, He shall send them away; were You not meditating with the harsh spirit, to slay them with a spirit of wrath?

**27:9** Therefore the iniquity of Jacob shall be taken away, and this is his blessing, when I take away his sin: when they make all the stones of the altars crushed in pieces like fine dust; and their trees shall by no means remain, and their idols shall be cut down like a forest far away.

**27:10** The settled flock shall be abandoned, like a forsaken flock; and for a long time it shall be for a pasture, and there they shall rest.

**27:11** And after a time there shall not be any green thing in her, because it has dried up. You women coming from a spectacle, come! For it is not a people having understanding; therefore He who made them shall by no means have compassion, nor shall He who formed them show mercy.

**27:12** And it shall come to pass in that day that God shall hedge in from the channel of the river as far as Rhinocorura; and you, gather the sons of Israel one by one.

**27:13** And it shall come to pass in that day that they shall sound the great trumpet, and those who were lost in the land of the Assyrians and those who were lost in Egypt shall come, and they shall worship the Lord upon the holy mountain Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — The Greek describes the sword as "holy, great, and mighty" and omits the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to the dragon being in the sea.
- **27:3** — The Septuagint reads "I am a strong city, a besieged city," diverging from the Masoretic Text ("I, the LORD, keep it") through a different reading of the Hebrew consonants.
- **27:9** — The Greek reads "his blessing" where the Masoretic Text has "the full fruit"; the clause "when I take away his sin" is echoed in Romans 11:27.
- **27:12** — "Rhinocorura" (modern El-Arish) is the Septuagint's distinct proper name for the border town marking the Brook of Egypt.
