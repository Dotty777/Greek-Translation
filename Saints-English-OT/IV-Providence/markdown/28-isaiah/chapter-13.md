## Text

**13:1** The vision which Isaiah the son of Amoz saw against Babylon.

**13:2** Upon a level mountain lift up a banner, raise the voice to them, beckon with the hand; open, O rulers!

**13:3** I command them, even I; giants are coming to fulfill My wrath, both rejoicing and acting with insolence.

**13:4** The sound of many nations upon the mountains, like that of many nations, the sound of kings and of nations gathered together! The Lord of hosts has commanded an armed warrior nation

**13:5** to come from a land far away, from the farthest foundation of heaven—the Lord and His armed warriors—to utterly destroy the whole inhabited earth.

**13:6** Wail! For the day of the Lord is near, and destruction from God shall come.

**13:7** Therefore every hand shall grow weak, and every soul of man shall lose heart;

**13:8** the ambassadors shall be thrown into turmoil, and birth pangs shall seize them like those of a woman in labor; and they shall lament one to another and be astounded, and their countenance shall change like a flame.

**13:9** For behold, the day of the Lord comes, incurable, full of wrath and anger, to make the inhabited earth a desolation and to destroy the sinners from it.

**13:10** For the stars of heaven and Orion and all the array of heaven shall not give their light; and it shall be darkened as the sun rises, and the moon shall not give her light.

**13:11** And I will command evils upon the whole inhabited earth, and upon the ungodly their sins; and I will destroy the arrogance of the lawless, and I will humble the arrogance of the proud.

**13:12** And those who remain shall be more precious than unfired gold, and a man shall be more precious than the stone that is in Souphir.

**13:13** For heaven shall be enraged, and the earth shall be shaken from its foundations, because of the fury of the wrath of the Lord of hosts, in the day when His wrath comes.

**13:14** And those who remain shall be like a fleeing gazelle and like a wandering sheep, and there shall be no one to gather them, so that each man shall turn back to his own people, and each man shall flee to his own country.

**13:15** For whoever is captured shall be overcome, and whoever are gathered together shall fall by the sword.

**13:16** And they shall dash their children down before their sight, and they shall plunder their houses, and they shall take their wives.

**13:17** Behold, I am stirring up the Medes against you, who do not regard silver, neither do they have need of gold.

**13:18** They shall shatter the arrows of the young men, and they shall by no means have mercy on your children, nor shall their eyes spare your children.

**13:19** And Babylon, which is called glorious by the king of the Chaldeans, shall be just as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah;

**13:20** it shall never be inhabited for all time, neither shall any enter it through many generations, neither shall Arabs pass through it, nor shall shepherds rest in it.

**13:21** And wild beasts shall rest there, and the houses shall be filled with noise; and sirens shall rest there, and demons shall dance there,

**13:22** and onocentaurs shall dwell there, and hedgehogs shall make their nests in their houses. It comes swiftly and shall not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — Greek gigantes ("giants") renders the Hebrew term for "mighty ones" or "warriors," a characteristic Septuagint rendering also found in Isaiah 14:9.
- **13:4** — "The Lord of hosts" translates Greek Kyrios Sabaoth, where the Septuagint of Isaiah transliterates the Hebrew word for "hosts" or "armies" (also in 13:13; 14:22, 24).
- **13:12** — Greek chrysion to apyron ("unfired gold") refers to pure native gold not refined by fire, and Souphir is the Septuagint's distinct form of Ophir, referring here to a precious stone rather than gold.
- **13:21** — The Septuagint populates Babylon's ruins with sirens (seirenes) and demons (daimonia), followed in verse 22 by onocentaurs (onokentauroi, mythical donkey-centaurs) and hedgehogs (echinoi), rendering obscure Hebrew terms for desert creatures.
