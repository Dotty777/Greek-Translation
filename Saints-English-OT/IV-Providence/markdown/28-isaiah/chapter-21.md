## Text

**21:1** The vision of the wilderness. As a tempest might pass through a wilderness, coming from a wilderness, from a land; a dreadful

**21:2** vision and a harsh one has been declared to me. He who breaks faith breaks faith; he who acts lawlessly acts lawlessly. The Elamites are upon me, and the envoys of the Persians come against me. Now I will groan and comfort myself.

**21:3** Therefore my loins have been filled with faintness, and pangs have seized me like a woman in travail; I did wrong so as not to hear; I made haste not to see.

**21:4** My heart wanders, and lawlessness overwhelms me; my soul stands in fear.

**21:5** Prepare the table; eat, drink! Arise, you princes, prepare shields,

**21:6** for thus the Lord said to me, "Go, set a watchman for yourself, and declare whatever you see."

**21:7** And I saw two mounted horsemen, and a rider of a donkey, and a rider of a camel. Listen with great attention,

**21:8** and call Uriah to the watchtower. The Lord said, "I stood continually by day, and at the encampment I stood all the night,

**21:9** and behold, he himself comes, a rider of a pair of horses." And he answered and said, "Babylon has fallen, has fallen, and all her statues and her hand-made idols have been shattered to the ground."

**21:10** Hear, you who are left behind and you who are in anguish; hear what I have heard from the Lord of hosts: the God of Israel has declared it to us.

**21:11** The vision of Idumea. One calls to me from Seir, "Guard the battlements."

**21:12** I keep watch in the morning and in the night; if you inquire, inquire, and dwell with me;

**21:13** in the forest you shall lodge at evening, in the way of Dedan.

**21:14** Bring water to meet the thirsty, you who dwell in the country of Teman; meet those who flee with bread,

**21:15** because of the multitude of the slain, and because of the multitude of those who wander, and because of the multitude of the sword, and because of the multitude of the drawn bows, and because of the multitude of those who have fallen in the war.

**21:16** For thus the Lord said to me, "Yet a year, like the year of a hired servant, the glory of the sons of Kedar shall fail,

**21:17** and the remnant of the bows of the mighty sons of Kedar shall be few." For the Lord God of Israel has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Greek reads "the envoys of the Persians come against me," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Go up, O Elam; besiege, O Media."
- **21:3** — Greek reads "I did wrong so as not to hear; I made haste not to see," diverging from the Masoretic Text ("I am bowed down so that I cannot hear; I am dismayed so that I cannot see").
- **21:8** — Greek reads "call Uriah to the watchtower" and makes "the Lord" (nominative) the speaker of "I stood continually by day," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "a lion cried out, 'My Lord, I stand continually by day.'"
- **21:11** — Greek reads "Idumea" (Edom) here and "Teman" in verse 14, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "Dumah" and "Tema."
