## Text

**60:1** Shine, shine, O Jerusalem, for your light has come, and the glory of the Lord has risen upon you.

**60:2** Behold, darkness shall cover the earth, and gloom upon the nations; but the Lord shall appear upon you, and His glory shall be seen upon you.

**60:3** And kings shall walk in your light, and nations in your brightness.

**60:4** Lift up your eyes all around and see your children gathered together; all your sons have come from afar, and your daughters shall be carried upon shoulders.

**60:5** Then you shall see, and you shall fear and be amazed in heart, because the wealth of the sea and of nations and of peoples shall turn over to you. And herds of camels shall come to you,

**60:6** and the camels of Midian and Ephah shall cover you; all from Sheba shall come bringing gold, and they shall bring frankincense, and they shall proclaim the glad tidings of the salvation of the Lord.

**60:7** And all the sheep of Kedar shall be gathered together, and the rams of Nebaioth shall come; acceptable offerings shall be brought up upon My altar, and the house of My prayer shall be glorified.

**60:8** Who are these who fly like clouds, and like doves with nestlings to Me?

**60:9** For Me the islands have waited, and the ships of Tarshish among the first, to bring your children from afar, and their silver and their gold with them, and the holy name of the Lord, and because the Holy One of Israel is glorious.

**60:10** And foreigners shall build your walls, and their kings shall attend upon you; for because of My wrath I struck you, and because of mercy I have loved you.

**60:11** And your gates shall be opened continually; day and night they shall not be shut, to bring into you the might of nations and their kings led in.

**60:12** For the nations and the kings who will not serve you shall perish, and the nations shall be made desolate with desolation.

**60:13** And the glory of Lebanon shall come to you with cypress and pine and cedar together, to glorify My holy place.

**60:14** And the sons of those who humbled you and provoked you shall come to you in fear, and you shall be called City of the Lord, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**60:15** Because you have been forsaken and hated, and there was no one to help, I will make you an everlasting rejoicing, a gladness for generations of generations.

**60:16** And you shall nurse on the milk of nations, and you shall eat the wealth of kings; and you shall know that I am the Lord who saves you, and the God of Israel who delivers you.

**60:17** Instead of bronze I will bring you gold, and instead of iron I will bring you silver, and instead of wood I will bring you bronze, and instead of stones, iron. And I will appoint your rulers in peace, and your overseers in righteousness.

**60:18** And injustice shall no longer be heard in your land, neither ruin nor misery within your borders; rather, your walls shall be called Salvation, and your gates, Engraving.

**60:19** And the sun shall no longer be for you the light of day, nor shall the rising of the moon illuminate the night for you; rather, the Lord shall be for you an everlasting light, and God your glory.

**60:20** For the sun shall not set for you, and the moon shall not fail you; for the Lord shall be for you an everlasting light, and the days of your mourning shall be fulfilled.

**60:21** And your people shall all be righteous; forever they shall inherit the land, guarding the planting, the works of His hands for glory.

**60:22** The smallest one shall become thousands, and the least one a great nation; I, the Lord, will gather them in due season.

## Translator Notes

- **60:1** — Greek opens with a doubled imperative and vocative, "Shine, shine, O Jerusalem" (Φωτίζου φωτίζου, Ἰερουσαλήμ), rather than the Hebrew "Arise, shine."
- **60:7** — Greek reads "the house of My prayer" (ὁ οἶκος τῆς προσευχῆς μου), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the house of My glory."
- **60:8** — Greek reads "with nestlings to Me" (σὺν νοσσοῖς ἐπ᾽ ἐμέ), diverging from the Hebrew "to their windows/roosts."
- **60:18** — Greek renders the name of the gates as "Engraving" (Γλύμμα, carved work), reflecting a distinct Septuagint reading where the Hebrew has "Praise."
- **60:21** — Greek reads "guarding the planting" (φυλάσσων τὸ φύτευμα), taking the Hebrew noun for "shoot/branch" from the verb meaning "to guard."
