## Text

**24:1** Behold, the Lord destroys the inhabited world, and He shall lay it waste, and uncover its face, and scatter those who dwell in it.

**24:2** And the people shall be as the priest, and the servant as the master, and the maidservant as the mistress; the buyer shall be as the seller, the lender as the borrower, and the debtor as the one to whom he owes.

**24:3** With destruction the earth shall be destroyed, and with plunder the earth shall be plundered; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

**24:4** The earth mourned, and the inhabited world was ruined; the lofty ones of the earth mourned.

**24:5** Moreover, the earth acted lawlessly because of those who inhabit it, because they transgressed the law and altered the ordinances, an everlasting covenant.

**24:6** Therefore a curse shall devour the earth, because those who inhabit it have sinned; therefore those who dwell in the earth shall be poor, and few men shall be left.

**24:7** The wine shall mourn, the vine shall mourn; all who are glad in soul shall groan.

**24:8** The gladness of timbrels has ceased, the arrogance and wealth of the ungodly have ceased, the sound of the harp has ceased.

**24:9** They were put to shame; they did not drink wine; strong drink became bitter to those who drink it.

**24:10** Every city has been made desolate; one shall shut up a house so that no one may enter.

**24:11** Wail for the wine everywhere; all gladness has ceased.

**24:12** Cities shall be left desolate, and forsaken houses shall perish.

**24:13** All these things shall be in the earth in the midst of the nations: just as when one gleans an olive tree, so shall they glean them, and when the vintage has ceased.

**24:14** These shall call out with a shout, and those who are left upon the earth shall rejoice together in the glory of the Lord; the water of the sea shall be troubled.

**24:15** Therefore the glory of the Lord shall be in the islands of the sea; the name of the Lord shall be glorious.

**24:16** O Lord God of Israel, from the wings of the earth we have heard wonders: "Hope for the godly!" And they shall say, "Woe to the treacherous!" You who set aside the law,

**24:17** terror and the pit and the snare are upon you who dwell upon the earth.

**24:18** And it shall be that he who flees from the terror shall fall into the pit, and he who climbs out of the pit shall be caught by the snare; for windows from heaven have been opened, and the foundations of the earth shall be shaken.

**24:19** With turmoil the earth shall be thrown into turmoil, and with perplexity the earth shall be perplexed.

**24:20** The earth has tottered like a drunken and reeling man, and it shall be shaken like an orchard watch-hut; for lawlessness has prevailed upon it, and it shall fall and shall by no means be able to rise.

**24:21** And God shall bring His hand upon the host of heaven and upon the kings of the earth.

**24:22** And they shall gather its assembly into a prison and shut them up in a stronghold; after many generations there shall be a visitation of them.

**24:23** And the brick shall melt away, and the wall shall fall; for the Lord shall reign from Zion and in Jerusalem, and before the elders He shall be glorified.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — Greek enomesen literally reads "acted lawlessly," reflecting the Septuagint rendering in contrast to the Masoretic Hebrew "is defiled."
- **24:6** — The Septuagint reads ptochoi esontai ("shall be poor"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "are burned."
- **24:16** — The Septuagint reads "Hope for the godly" and "you who set aside the law," differing distinctly from the Masoretic Text.
- **24:23** — The Septuagint reads the Hebrew homographs for "moon" and "sun" as "the brick" (he plinthos) and "the wall" (to teichos), yielding "the brick shall melt away, and the wall shall fall."
