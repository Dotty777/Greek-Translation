## Text

**57:1** See how the righteous man has perished, and no one takes it to heart; and righteous men are taken away, and no one considers it. For from before unrighteousness the righteous man has been taken away;

**57:2** his burial shall be in peace; he has been taken away from the midst.

**57:3** But as for you, draw near here, you lawless sons, offspring of adulterers and a harlot:

**57:4** At whom have you made sport? And against whom have you opened your mouth? And against whom have you let loose your tongue? Are you not children of destruction, a lawless offspring—

**57:5** those who invoke the idols under thick trees, slaughtering their children in the valleys in the midst of the rocks?

**57:6** That is your portion, this is your lot; and to those you poured out drink offerings, and to these you offered up sacrifices. At these things, therefore, shall I not be angry?

**57:7** Upon a high and lofty mountain, there is your bed, and there you brought up sacrifices;

**57:8** and behind the posts of your door you placed your memorials. You supposed that if you departed from Me, you would have something more; you loved those who slept with you,

**57:9** and you multiplied your harlotry with them, and you increased those who are far from you; and you sent envoys beyond your borders, and you were brought low even to Hades.

**57:10** In your many journeys you grew weary, and you did not say, "I will cease," growing strong because you did these things; therefore you did not implore Me.

**57:11** Whom did you dread and fear, that you lied to Me and did not remember, nor took Me into your mind nor into your heart? And seeing you, I overlook you, and Me you did not fear.

**57:12** And I will declare your righteousness and your evil deeds, which shall not profit you.

**57:13** When you cry aloud, let them deliver you in your affliction; for the wind shall take all of these, and a tempest shall carry them away. But those who hold fast to Me shall possess the land and shall inherit My holy mountain,

**57:14** and they shall say, "Clear the ways before him, and remove stumbling blocks from the way of My people."

**57:15** Thus says the Most High in the highest places, who inhabits eternity, Holy among the holy ones is His name, the Most High who rests among the holy ones, and gives longsuffering to the fainthearted, and gives life to those who are contrite in heart:

**57:16** "I will not take vengeance on you forever, nor will I be angry with you continually; for a spirit shall go forth from Me, and every breath I have made.

**57:17** Because of sin for a little while I grieved him, and I struck him and turned My face away from him; and he was grieved, and went downcast in his ways.

**57:18** I have seen his ways, and I healed him and comforted him, and I gave him true consolation,

**57:19** peace upon peace to those who are far off and to those who are near." And the Lord said, "I will heal them."

**57:20** But the unrighteous shall be tossed like waves and shall not be able to rest.

**57:21** "There is no rejoicing," said God, "for the ungodly."

## Translator Notes

- **57:2** — Greek reads "his burial shall be in peace; he has been taken away from the midst," differing markedly from the Masoretic image of resting upon beds.
- **57:6** — Greek asks "shall I not be angry?" (οὐκ ὀργισθήσομαι;), whereas the Masoretic Text asks whether the Lord should be appeased.
- **57:15** — Greek displays a distinctive sequence of divine epithets, including "Holy among the holy ones is His name" and "gives longsuffering to the fainthearted."
- **57:21** — Greek renders the closing proverb with χαίρειν ("rejoicing") rather than "peace," and reads simply "God" rather than "my God."
