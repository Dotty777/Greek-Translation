## Text

**22:1** The word concerning the Valley of Zion. What has happened to you, that you have all now gone up to vain housetops?

**22:2** The city is filled with those who shout; your wounded are not wounded with swords, nor are your dead the dead of war.

**22:3** All your rulers have fled, and having been captured they are harshly bound; and the strong among you have fled far away.

**22:4** Therefore I said, "Leave me alone; I will weep bitterly. Do not persist in comforting me over the ruin of the daughter of my kindred."

**22:5** For it is a day of tumult and destruction, and of trampling and wandering from the Lord of hosts; in the Valley of Zion they wander; from the least to the greatest they wander upon the mountains.

**22:6** And the Elamites took up quivers, men riding upon horses, and an assembly in battle array.

**22:7** And your choice valleys shall be filled with chariots, and the horsemen shall block up your gates,

**22:8** and they shall uncover the gates of Judah; and in that day they shall look upon the choice houses of the city,

**22:9** and they shall uncover the hidden places of the houses of the citadel of David. And they saw that they were many, and that he had turned the water of the ancient pool into the city,

**22:10** and that they had torn down the houses of Jerusalem for fortifications of the wall for the city.

**22:11** And you made for yourselves water between the two walls, inward from the ancient pool; yet you did not look to Him who made it from the beginning, nor did you see Him who created it.

**22:12** And the Lord, the Lord of hosts, called in that day for weeping and lamentation, and for shaving the head and girding with sackcloth;

**22:13** but they made gladness and exultation, slaughtering calves and sacrificing sheep, so as to eat flesh and drink wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."

**22:14** And these things have been revealed in the ears of the Lord of hosts, that this sin shall not be forgiven you until you die.

**22:15** Thus says the Lord of hosts: "Go into the treasury chamber to Shebna the treasurer, and say to him,

**22:16** 'What are you doing here, and what do you have here, that you have hewn out a tomb for yourself here, and made a tomb for yourself on high, and carved a dwelling for yourself in the rock?

**22:17** Behold now, the Lord of hosts casts out and will crush a man, and He will take away your robe and your glorious crown,

**22:18** and He will cast you into a great and measureless land, and there you shall die; and He will bring your fine chariot to dishonor, and the house of your ruler,

**22:19** and you shall be removed from your stewardship and from your station.

**22:20** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will call My servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah,

**22:21** and I will clothe him with your robe, and your crown I will give to him with power, and your stewardship I will give into his hands; and he shall be as a father to those who dwell in Jerusalem and to those who dwell in Judah.

**22:22** And I will give the glory of David to him, and he shall rule, and there shall be no one who speaks against him; and he shall shut, and there is no one who opens.

**22:23** And I will establish him as a ruler in a faithful place, and he shall become a throne of glory for his father's house.

**22:24** And every glorious one in his father's house shall trust in him, from the least to the greatest, and they shall hang upon him

**22:25** in that day.'" Thus says the Lord of hosts: "The man who is firmly fixed in a faithful place shall be moved, and he shall be taken away and fall, and the glory that is upon him shall be utterly destroyed." For the Lord has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — Greek reads "the Valley of Zion" (also in v. 5) and "vain housetops," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "the Valley of Vision" and simply "housetops."
- **22:15** — Greek transliterates the treasurer's name as Somnas (standard English Shebna), and similarly Chelkias for Hilkiah in verse 20.
- **22:22** — Greek reads "the glory of David" and "he shall rule, and there shall be no one who speaks against him; and he shall shut, and there is no one who opens," differing from the Masoretic Text's "key of the house of David" and fuller opening-and-shutting parallel.
- **22:23** — Greek renders the image personally as "a ruler" here and "the man" in verse 25, rather than the Masoretic Text's "peg."
