## Text

**52:1** Awake, awake, O Zion; put on your strength, O Zion, and put on your glory, O Jerusalem, the holy city; the uncircumcised and the unclean shall no longer pass through you.

**52:2** Shake off the dust and arise; take your seat, O Jerusalem; loose the bond from your neck, O captive daughter of Zion.

**52:3** For thus says the Lord: "You were sold for nothing, and you shall be redeemed without silver."

**52:4** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: "My people went down at the first into Egypt to sojourn there, and to the Assyrians they were led away by force;

**52:5** and now, why are you here?" Thus says the Lord: "Because My people were taken away for nothing, marvel and wail." Thus says the Lord: "Because of you, My name is continually blasphemed among the nations.

**52:6** Therefore My people shall know My name in that day, that I Myself am He who speaks; I am present, like a season upon the mountains,

**52:7** like the feet of him who brings glad tidings of a report of peace, like one bringing glad tidings of good things; for I will make your salvation heard, saying to Zion, 'Your God shall reign.'"

**52:8** For the voice of those who guard you has been lifted up, and with the voice together they shall rejoice; for eyes shall look to eyes when the Lord has mercy on Zion.

**52:9** Let the desolate places of Jerusalem break forth together into gladness, for the Lord has had mercy on her and has delivered Jerusalem.

**52:10** And the Lord shall reveal His holy arm before all the nations, and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation that comes from our God.

**52:11** Depart, depart, go out from there, and do not touch what is unclean; go out from her midst, be separated, you who bear the vessels of the Lord;

**52:12** for you shall not go out with tumult, nor shall you go by flight; for the Lord shall go forward before you, and the God of Israel who gathers you together.

**52:13** Behold, My Servant shall understand, and He shall be exalted and glorified exceedingly.

**52:14** Just as many shall be astonished at You, so shall Your appearance be without glory from men, and Your glory from mankind.

**52:15** So shall many nations marvel at Him, and kings shall keep their mouth shut; for those to whom it was not declared concerning Him shall see, and those who have not heard shall understand.

## Translator Notes

- **52:4** — The doubled Greek title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") reflects the compound Hebrew divine title Adonai YHWH.
- **52:6** — The Greek word hora ("season," also "prime" or "beauty") links the divine announcement "I am present" directly to the messenger imagery of verse 7.
- **52:14** — The Greek addresses the Servant in the second person ("You," "Your") and describes His appearance as "without glory from men."
- **52:15** — The Greek reads "marvel" (thaumasontai) rather than the Hebrew "sprinkle," and the second half of the verse is quoted closely in Romans 15:21.
