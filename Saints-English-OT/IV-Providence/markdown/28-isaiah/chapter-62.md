# Isaiah 62 — Saints Traditional English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**62:1** For the sake of Zion I will not keep silent, and for the sake of Jerusalem I will not relent, until her righteousness goes forth like light, and My salvation shall burn like a torch.

**62:2** And nations shall see your righteousness, and kings your glory; and He shall call you by the new name that the Lord shall name.

**62:3** And you shall be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a diadem of a kingdom in the hand of your God.

**62:4** And no longer shall you be called "Forsaken," and your land shall no longer be called "Desolate"; for you shall be called "My Good Pleasure," and your land "Inhabited," because the Lord has delighted in you, and your land shall be inhabited.

**62:5** And as a young man dwells with a virgin, so shall your sons dwell; and it shall come to pass that just as a bridegroom rejoices over a bride, so shall the Lord rejoice over you.

**62:6** And upon your walls, O Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen all the day and all the night, who continually shall not keep silent, remembering the Lord.

**62:7** For there is none like you, if He sets aright and makes Jerusalem a boast upon the earth.

**62:8** The Lord has sworn by His glory and by the strength of His arm: "Surely I will no longer give your grain and food to your enemies, and surely foreign sons shall no longer drink your wine for which you have labored;

**62:9** but those who gather them shall eat them and praise the Lord, and those who gather them shall drink them in My holy courts."

**62:10** Go through My gates, and make a way for My people, and cast the stones from the way; lift up a banner to the nations.

**62:11** For behold, the Lord has made it heard to the end of the earth; say to the daughter of Zion, "Behold, the Savior has come to you, having His own reward, and His work before His face."

**62:12** And He shall call them "A Holy People, Redeemed by the Lord," and you shall be called "A Sought-Out City" and "Not Forsaken."

## Translator Notes

- **62:1** — The Greek reads "My salvation," continuing the Lord's first-person speech, whereas the Hebrew reads "her salvation."
- **62:7** — The Greek reads "there is none like you," interpreting the underlying Hebrew term as "likeness" rather than "rest" or "silence."
- **62:11** — The Greek personalizes the noun as "the Savior" rather than the abstract "your salvation" found in the Hebrew.
