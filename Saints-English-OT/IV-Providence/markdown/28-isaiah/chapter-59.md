## Text

**59:1** Is the hand of the Lord not strong enough to save? Or has His ear grown heavy, so that He cannot listen?

**59:2** Rather, your sins separate between you and God, and because of your sins He has turned His face away from you, so as not to show mercy.

**59:3** For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with sins; your lips have spoken iniquity, and your tongue meditates on injustice.

**59:4** No one speaks what is righteous, nor is there true judgment; they trust in vain things and speak empty words, for they conceive trouble and bring forth iniquity.

**59:5** They have broken open the eggs of asps and weave a spider's web; he who is about to eat of their eggs, upon crushing one, finds a rotten egg, and within it is a basilisk.

**59:6** Their web shall not serve as a garment, nor shall they clothe themselves with their works; for their works are works of iniquity.

**59:7** Their feet run to wickedness, swift to shed blood, and their thoughts come from murders; ruin and misery are in their ways,

**59:8** and the way of peace they do not know, and there is no justice in their ways; for the paths which they travel are crooked, and they do not know peace.

**59:9** Therefore justice has departed from them, and righteousness shall by no means overtake them; while they waited for light, darkness came upon them; waiting for brightness, they walked in the dead of night.

**59:10** They shall grope for a wall like the blind, and as those who have no eyes they shall grope; they shall fall at noonday as at midnight; like dying men they shall groan;

**59:11** like a bear and like a dove they shall go together. We waited for justice, and there is none; salvation has withdrawn far from us.

**59:12** For our iniquity is great before You, and our sins have stood against us; for our iniquities are within us, and we have known our wrongdoings:

**59:13** we have acted impiously and lied, and turned away from following our God; we have spoken unjust things and have been disobedient; we conceived and meditated from our heart unjust words,

**59:14** and we have thrust justice back, and righteousness has stood far off; for truth has been consumed in their ways, and they could not pass through by a straight way.

**59:15** And truth has been taken away, and they turned their mind away from understanding. And the Lord saw it, and it did not please Him, because there was no justice.

**59:16** And He looked, and there was no man, and He observed, and there was no one to help; and He defended them with His arm, and with mercy He upheld Himself.

**59:17** And He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and placed a helmet of salvation upon His head; and He clothed Himself with a garment of vengeance and with His mantle,

**59:18** as One about to render recompense—reproach to the adversaries.

**59:19** And those from the west shall fear the name of the Lord, and those from the rising of the sun the glorious name; for the wrath from the Lord shall come like a violent river; it shall come with fury.

**59:20** And the Deliverer shall come for the sake of Zion, and He shall turn away impieties from Jacob.

**59:21** "And this is the covenant from Me for them," says the Lord: "My Spirit who is upon you, and the words which I have put into your mouth, shall by no means fail from your mouth and from the mouth of your seed"—for the Lord has spoken—"from this time forth and forever."

## Translator Notes

- **59:5** — Greek οὔριον literally denotes a "wind-egg" (an unfertilized or rotten egg), and βασιλίσκος ("basilisk") translates the deadly viper of the Hebrew text.
- **59:16** — Greek ἠμύνατο αὐτούς ("He defended them") and τῇ ἐλεημοσύνῃ ("with mercy") reflect the Septuagint's distinctive rendering of "His own arm brought salvation for Him" and "His righteousness."
- **59:17** — Verses 17-18 in the Septuagint condense the longer Hebrew text, omitting the cloak of zeal and the parallel clauses concerning wrath and the coastlands.
- **59:20** — Greek reads "for the sake of Zion" (ἕνεκεν Σειών) and "turn away impieties from Jacob," diverging from the Hebrew "to Zion, to those who turn from transgression in Jacob" (cf. Romans 11:26).
