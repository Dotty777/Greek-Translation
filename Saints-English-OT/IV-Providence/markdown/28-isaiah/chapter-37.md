## Text

**37:1** And it came to pass, when King Hezekiah heard it, that he tore his garments and put on sackcloth, and went up into the house of the Lord.

**37:2** And he sent Eliakim the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and the elders of the priests, clothed in sackcloth, to Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet.

**37:3** And they said to him, "Thus says Hezekiah: This day is a day of distress and reproach and rebuke and wrath; for the pang of childbirth has come upon her who is in labor, yet she has no strength to bring forth.

**37:4** May the Lord your God hear the words of Rabshakeh, which the king of the Assyrians sent to reproach the living God and to speak words of reproach which the Lord your God has heard; and you shall pray to your Lord for these who remain."

**37:5** So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

**37:6** And Isaiah said to them, "Thus you shall say to your master, 'Thus says the Lord: Do not be afraid because of the words which you have heard, with which the envoys of the king of the Assyrians have reproached Me.

**37:7** Behold, I will put a spirit into him, and when he hears a report, he shall return to his own country, and he shall fall by the sword in his own land.'"

**37:8** Then Rabshakeh returned and found the king of the Assyrians besieging Libnah, and he heard that he had departed from Lachish.

**37:9** And Tirhakah king of the Ethiopians went out to besiege him; and when he heard of it, he turned back and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,

**37:10** "Thus you shall say to Hezekiah king of Judea: Do not let your God in whom you trust deceive you, saying, 'Jerusalem shall by no means be given into the hand of the king of the Assyrians.'

**37:11** Have you not heard what the kings of the Assyrians have done to all the earth, how they destroyed it? And shall you be delivered?

**37:12** Have the gods of the nations delivered those whom my fathers destroyed—Gozan and Haran and Rapheth, which are in the land of Theemath?

**37:13** Where are the kings of Hamath, and where are those of Arpad, and of the city of Eppharuaim, Anag, and Ugaua?"

**37:14** And Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers and read it; and he went up to the house of the Lord and opened it before the Lord.

**37:15** And Hezekiah prayed to the Lord, saying,

**37:16** "O Lord of hosts, God of Israel, who sits upon the cherubim, You alone are God of every kingdom of the world; You have made heaven and earth.

**37:17** Incline Your ear, O Lord; hear, O Lord! Open Your eyes, O Lord; look, O Lord, and see the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to reproach the living God.

**37:18** Truly, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the whole world and their land,

**37:19** and have cast their idols into the fire; for they were not gods, but the works of men's hands, wood and stone, and they cast them away.

**37:20** Now therefore, O Lord our God, save us out of his hand, that every kingdom of the earth may know that You alone are God."

**37:21** Then Isaiah the son of Amoz was sent to Hezekiah and said to him, "Thus says the Lord God of Israel: I have heard what you have prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib king of the Assyrians.

**37:22** This is the word which God has spoken concerning him: The virgin daughter of Zion has despised you and mocked you; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at you.

**37:23** Whom have you reproached and provoked? And against whom have you raised your voice? And have you not lifted up your eyes on high against the Holy One of Israel?

**37:24** For through messengers you have reproached the Lord; for you have said, 'With the multitude of chariots I have gone up to the height of the mountains, and to the farthest reaches of Lebanon; and I have cut down the height of its cedar and the beauty of the cypress, and I have entered into the height of a part of the forest,

**37:25** and I have laid a bridge, and I have laid waste the waters and every gathering of water.'

**37:26** Have I not heard long ago these things which I have done? From ancient days I ordained it, and now I have shown it, to lay waste nations in strongholds and those who dwell in fortified cities.

**37:27** I weakened their hands, and they withered away, and became like dry grass on the housetops and like wild grass.

**37:28** But now I know your resting, and your going out, and your coming in.

**37:29** And your fury with which you have raged, and your bitterness, have come up to Me; and I will put a muzzle in your nose and a bridle in your lips, and I will turn you back by the way by which you came.

**37:30** And this shall be the sign to you: eat this year what you have sown, and in the second year what remains; and in the third year sow and reap, and plant vineyards, and you shall eat their fruit.

**37:31** And there shall be those who remain in Judea; they shall put forth root downward and produce seed upward.

**37:32** For out of Jerusalem shall come those who remain, and those who are saved out of Mount Zion; the zeal of the Lord of hosts shall do these things.

**37:33** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians: He shall by no means enter into this city, nor shoot an arrow against it, nor bring a shield against it, nor surround it with a siege rampart;

**37:34** but by the way by which he came, by the same he shall return, and into this city he shall by no means enter. Thus says the Lord:

**37:35** I will defend this city to save it, for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David."

**37:36** Then an angel of the Lord went out and slew one hundred and eighty-five thousand from the camp of the Assyrians; and when they rose in the morning, they found all the bodies dead.

**37:37** So Sennacherib king of the Assyrians turned back and departed, and dwelt in Nineveh.

**37:38** And while he was worshiping in the house of Nisroch his ancestral deity, Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons struck him down with swords; and they escaped into Armenia. And Esarhaddon his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **37:12** — The Greek text names Rapheth and places these cities "in the land of Theemath," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's Rezeph and "the people of Eden who were in Telassar."
- **37:13** — The Greek text preserves the distinct names Eppharuaim, Anag, and Ugaua where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah.
- **37:25** — The Greek text reads "I have laid a bridge, and I have laid waste the waters and every gathering of water," diverging markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's imagery of digging, drinking, and drying up the streams of Egypt.
- **37:26** — The Greek text reads the first-person verb "Have I not heard" (ekousa) rather than the second-person "Have you not heard" of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **37:38** — The Greek text calls Nisroch Sennacherib's "ancestral deity" (patrarchon) and locates the sons' escape in Armenia, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "his god" and "the land of Ararat."
