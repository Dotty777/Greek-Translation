## Text

**42:1** Jacob, My servant, I will uphold him; Israel, My chosen one, My soul has accepted him; I have put My Spirit upon him; he shall bring forth judgment to the nations.

**42:2** He shall not cry out nor slacken, nor shall his voice be heard outside.

**42:3** A crushed reed he shall not break, and a smoldering flax he shall not quench, but he shall bring forth judgment for truth.

**42:4** He shall shine forth and shall not be broken, until he establishes judgment upon the earth; and in his name the nations shall hope.

**42:5** Thus says the Lord God, who made heaven and fastened it, who established the earth and the things in it, and who gives breath to the people upon it and spirit to those who tread upon it:

**42:6** "I, the Lord God, have called you in righteousness, and I will take hold of your hand and strengthen you, and I have given you for a covenant of a people,

**42:7** to open the eyes of the blind, to bring out from bonds those who are bound, and from the prison house those who sit in darkness.

**42:8** I am the Lord God; this is My name; My glory I will not give to another, nor My excellencies to carved images.

**42:9** Behold, the things from the beginning have come, and new things I declare; and before they are declared, they have been made known to you."

**42:10** Sing to the Lord a new hymn; His dominion—glorify His name from the end of the earth, you who go down to the sea and sail upon it, you islands and those who dwell in them.

**42:11** Rejoice, O wilderness and its villages, the encampments and those who dwell in Kedar. Those who dwell in the rock shall rejoice; from the summit of the mountains they shall shout;

**42:12** they shall give glory to God; they shall declare His excellencies in the islands.

**42:13** The Lord God of hosts shall go forth and crush war; He shall stir up zeal and cry out against His enemies with strength.

**42:14** I have kept silent; shall I always keep silent and restrain Myself? Like a woman in childbirth I have endured; I will dismay and dry up at the same time.

**42:15** I will lay waste mountains and hills, and dry up all their grass; and I will turn rivers into islands, and dry up pools.

**42:16** And I will lead the blind in a way they have not known, and paths they did not know I will make them tread; I will turn darkness into light for them, and crooked places into a straight way. These words I will perform, and I will not forsake them.

**42:17** But they turned back. Be put to utter shame, you who trust in carved images, who say to molten images, "You are our gods."

**42:18** You deaf, hear; and you blind, look up to see.

**42:19** And who is blind except My servants, and who are deaf except those who rule over them? Even the servants of God have been blinded.

**42:20** You have seen many times, and did not take heed; the ears were opened, and you did not hear.

**42:21** The Lord God purposed that He might be justified and magnify praise.

**42:22** And I looked, and the people had become plundered and despoiled; for the snare is in the inner chambers everywhere, and in the houses as well, where they hid them; they became plunder, and there was no one rescuing the spoil, and there was none who said, "Restore!"

**42:23** Who among you will give ear to these things? Listen to the things to come,

**42:24** to which He gave Jacob for spoil, and Israel to those who plunder him. Was it not God, against whom they sinned, and they were not willing to walk in His ways nor to hear His law?

**42:25** And He brought upon them the wrath of His anger, and war prevailed over them, and those who burned them all around; and none of them knew it, nor did they lay it upon their soul.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — Greek explicitly identifies the servant as "Jacob" and the chosen one as "Israel," names absent from the Hebrew text.
- **42:4** — Greek reads "He shall shine forth" (ἀναλάμψει) and "in his name the nations shall hope" (echoed in Matthew 12:21), where the Hebrew has "he will not grow faint" and "the coastlands wait for his law."
- **42:6** — The Greek critical text ends with "for a covenant of a people" without the phrase "for a light to the nations" found in the Hebrew text and some later Greek manuscripts.
- **42:10** — Greek reads "His dominion" (ἡ ἀρχὴ αὐτοῦ), where the Hebrew text reads "His praise."
- **42:24** — Greek opens with the relative pronoun "to which" (οἷς), linking directly to "the things to come" in verse 23, where the Hebrew text begins a new question, "Who gave Jacob to the looter?"
