## Text

**23:1** The word concerning Tyre. Wail, O ships of Carthage, for she has perished, and they no longer come from the land of Kittim; she has been led away captive.

**23:2** To whom have those who dwell in the island become like, merchants of Phoenicia, crossing over the sea

**23:3** upon much water, a seed of merchants? Like a harvest being gathered in are the merchants of the nations.

**23:4** "Be ashamed, O Sidon," said the sea; and the strength of the sea said, "I have not travailed, nor have I given birth; neither have I reared young men, nor brought up virgins."

**23:5** And when it is heard by Egypt, anguish shall seize them concerning Tyre.

**23:6** Depart to Carthage; wail, you who dwell in this island.

**23:7** Was this not your arrogance from the beginning, before she was delivered up?

**23:8** Who has purposed these things against Tyre? Is she inferior, or does she lack strength? Her merchants are glorious, rulers of the earth.

**23:9** The Lord of hosts has purposed to dissolve all the arrogance of the glorious, and to bring to dishonor every glorious thing upon the earth.

**23:10** Till your land, for ships no longer come from Carthage.

**23:11** And your hand no longer prevails over the sea, the hand that provoked kings; the Lord of hosts has commanded concerning Canaan to destroy her strength.

**23:12** And they shall say, "You shall no longer continue to insult and wrong the daughter of Sidon; and even if you depart to the Kittim, not even there shall there be rest for you,

**23:13** and even to the land of the Chaldeans—this too has been laid waste by the Assyrians; its wall has fallen."

**23:14** Wail, O ships of Carthage, for your stronghold has perished.

**23:15** And it shall come to pass in that day that Tyre shall be forsaken seventy years, like the time of a king, like the time of a man; and it shall come to pass after seventy years that Tyre shall be like the song of a harlot:

**23:16** "Take a harp, wander about, O forgotten harlot city; play the harp skillfully, sing many songs, that remembrance may be made of you."

**23:17** And it shall come to pass after the seventy years that God will make a visitation upon Tyre, and she shall again be restored to her former state, and she shall be a marketplace for all the kingdoms of the inhabited world upon the face of the earth.

**23:18** And her merchandise and her hire shall be holy to the Lord; it shall not be gathered for themselves, but all her merchandise shall be for those who dwell before the Lord, to eat and drink and be filled, and for a memorial contribution before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — Throughout this chapter (vv. 1, 6, 10, 14), the Greek renders Hebrew Tarshish as "Carthage."
- **23:2** — Greek reads "To whom have those who dwell in the island become like, merchants of Phoenicia," where the Masoretic Text has "Be still, inhabitants of the coastland, merchants of Sidon."
- **23:10** — Greek reads "Till your land, for ships no longer come from Carthage," diverging markedly from the Masoretic Text ("Overflow your land like the Nile, O daughter of Tarshish; there is no more restraint").
- **23:18** — Greek concludes with "for a memorial contribution before the Lord," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "for stately clothing."
