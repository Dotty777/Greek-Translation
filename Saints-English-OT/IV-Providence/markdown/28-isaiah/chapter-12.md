## Text

**12:1** And in that day you shall say, "I bless You, O Lord, because You were angry with me, and You turned away Your wrath and had mercy on me.

**12:2** Behold, my God is my Savior; I will trust in Him and will not be afraid, for the Lord is my glory and my praise, and He has become my salvation."

**12:3** And you shall draw water with gladness from the springs of salvation.

**12:4** And in that day you shall say, "Sing hymns to the Lord, call aloud upon His name, declare His glorious deeds among the nations; remember that His name has been exalted.

**12:5** Sing hymns to the name of the Lord, for He has done lofty things; proclaim these things in all the earth.

**12:6** Exult and be glad, you who dwell in Zion, for the Holy One of Israel has been exalted in her midst."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek text reads "my God is my Savior" and "my glory and my praise is the Lord," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "God is my salvation" and "my strength and song is Yah, the LORD."
- **12:6** — The Greek concludes "in her midst" (referring to Zion in the third person), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in your midst."
