## Text

**65:1** I became manifest to those who did not inquire of Me; I was found by those who did not seek Me. I said, "Here I am," to a nation that did not call upon My name.

**65:2** I stretched out My hands all the day to a disobedient and contrary people, to those who walk in a way that is not good, but after their sins.

**65:3** This is the people that provokes Me continually before My face; they offer sacrifices in the gardens and burn incense upon the bricks to demons that do not exist,

**65:4** who lie down to sleep in the tombs and in the caves for the sake of dreams, who eat swine's flesh and the broth of sacrifices—all their vessels are defiled—

**65:5** who say, "Stay far from me, do not come near me, for I am pure." This is the smoke of My wrath; a fire burns in it all the days.

**65:6** Behold, it is written before Me: "I will not keep silent until I repay into their bosom

**65:7** their sins and the sins of their fathers," says the Lord, "who burned incense upon the mountains and reproached Me upon the hills; I will repay their works into their bosom."

**65:8** Thus says the Lord: "Just as the grape shall be found in the cluster, and they shall say, 'Do not destroy it, for a blessing is in it,' so will I do for the sake of him who serves Me; for his sake I will by no means destroy them all.

**65:9** And I will bring forth the seed out of Jacob and out of Judah, and it shall inherit My holy mountain; and My chosen ones and My servants shall inherit it, and they shall dwell there.

**65:10** And there shall be folds for flocks in the forest, and the Valley of Achor shall be a resting place for herds, for My people who have sought Me.

**65:11** But as for you who forsake Me and forget My holy mountain, who prepare a table for the demon and fill a mixed drink for Fortune,

**65:12** I will deliver you up to the sword, and all of you shall fall in slaughter; because I called you and you did not obey, I spoke and you refused to heed, and you did evil before Me and chose the things that I did not desire."

**65:13** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, those who serve Me shall eat, but you shall hunger; behold, those who serve Me shall drink, but you shall thirst; behold, those who serve Me shall be glad, but you shall be put to shame;

**65:14** behold, those who serve Me shall exult in gladness, but you shall cry out for anguish of heart, and from the crushing of your spirit you shall wail.

**65:15** For you shall leave your name for a satiety to My chosen ones, and the Lord shall slay you; but upon those who serve Me a new name shall be called,

**65:16** which shall be blessed upon the earth; for they shall bless the true God, and those who swear upon the earth shall swear by the true God. For they shall forget the former affliction, and it shall not come up into their heart.

**65:17** For the heaven shall be new and the earth shall be new, and they shall by no means remember the former things, nor shall they ever come upon their heart;

**65:18** rather, they shall find gladness and exultation in it; for behold, I make Jerusalem an exultation and My people a gladness.

**65:19** And I will exult over Jerusalem and be glad over My people; and no longer shall the voice of weeping or the voice of crying be heard in her,

**65:20** nor shall there any longer be there one who dies untimely, nor an aged man who does not fulfill his time; for the youth shall be one hundred years old, and the sinner who dies at one hundred years old shall also be accursed.

**65:21** And they shall build houses and themselves dwell in them, and they shall plant vineyards and themselves eat their produce;

**65:22** they shall by no means build and others dwell in them, nor shall they plant and others eat. For as the days of the tree of life shall be the days of My people; for they shall wear out the works of their labors.

**65:23** My chosen ones shall not toil in vain, nor bear children for a curse, because they are a seed blessed by God, and their offspring are with them.

**65:24** And it shall come to pass that before they cry out, I will heed them; while they are still speaking, I will say, 'What is it?'

**65:25** Then wolves and lambs shall pasture together, and a lion shall eat straw like an ox, and a serpent shall eat earth as bread; they shall neither harm nor destroy upon My holy mountain," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **65:10** — Greek reads "in the forest" (en to drymo) rather than the geographic name "Sharon."
- **65:11** — Greek renders the pagan deities (Gad and Meni) as "the demon" (to daimonio) and "Fortune" (te tyche).
- **65:15** — Greek reads "for a satiety" (eis plesmonen, surfeit or fullness) rather than "for an oath" or "curse."
- **65:22** — Greek explicitly reads "the tree of life" (tou xylou tes zoes) rather than simply "a tree."
