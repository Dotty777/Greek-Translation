## Text

**9:1** O people who walk in darkness, behold a great light; you who dwell in a region of the shadow of death, light shall shine upon you.

**9:2** The greater part of the people whom You brought down in Your gladness—they shall indeed rejoice before You as those who rejoice at the harvest, and in the same manner as those who divide the spoil.

**9:3** Because the yoke resting upon them has been removed, and the rod upon their neck; for He has shattered the rod of the exactors, as in the day against Midian.

**9:4** For every robe amassed by deceit, and every garment, they shall repay with restitution, and they shall wish that they had been burned with fire.

**9:5** For a Child has been born to us, a Son has been given to us, whose dominion has come upon His shoulder, and His name is called Messenger of Great Counsel; for I will bring peace upon the rulers, and health to Him.

**9:6** Great is His dominion, and of His peace there is no limit, upon the throne of David and his kingdom, to set it aright and uphold it with judgment and with righteousness, from this time forth and forevermore. The zeal of the Lord of hosts shall accomplish these things.

**9:7** The Lord sent death upon Jacob, and it came upon Israel.

**9:8** And all the people of Ephraim shall know, and those who dwell in Samaria, saying in insolence and loftiness of heart:

**9:9** "Bricks have fallen, but come, let us hew stones, and cut down sycamores and cedars, and build for ourselves a tower."

**9:10** And God shall dash upon him those who rise up against Mount Zion, and He shall scatter the enemies,

**9:11** Syria from the rising of the sun and the Greeks from the setting of the sun, who devour Israel with their whole mouth. For all these things His wrath has not turned away, but His hand is still raised high.

**9:12** And the people did not turn back until they were struck, and they did not seek the Lord.

**9:13** And the Lord took away from Israel head and tail, great and small in one day: the elder and those who respect persons, this is the leadership,

**9:14** and the prophet who teaches lawless things, he is the tail.

**9:15** And those who call this people blessed shall lead them astray, and they lead them astray in order to swallow them up.

**9:16** Therefore the Lord shall not rejoice over their young men, and He shall have no mercy on their orphans and their widows; for all are lawless and wicked, and every mouth speaks unjust things. For all these things His wrath has not turned away, but His hand is still raised high.

**9:17** And lawlessness shall burn like fire, and like dry grass it shall be devoured by fire; and it shall burn in the thickets of the forest, and consume together all that surrounds the hills.

**9:18** Because of the fury of the wrath of the Lord the whole land has been burned up, and the people shall be as though burned up by fire. A man shall not have pity on his brother,

**9:19** but he shall turn aside to the right, because he shall be hungry, and he shall eat from the left, and a man shall by no means be filled, eating the flesh of his own arm.

**9:20** For Manasseh shall eat of Ephraim, and Ephraim of Manasseh, for together they shall besiege Judah. For all these things His wrath has not turned away, but His hand is still raised high.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Septuagint reads "whom You brought down in Your gladness" with a hanging clause resumed by "they shall rejoice," differing from the Masoretic Text ("You have multiplied the nation").
- **9:5** — The Septuagint condenses the Messianic throne names into the single title "Messenger of Great Counsel" (Greek: Megales boules angelos), followed by God's promise of peace upon the rulers and health to Him.
- **9:6** — Here and throughout chapters 9-10, the Greek retains the transliteration Kyrios sabaoth ("Lord of hosts").
- **9:7** — The Septuagint reads "death" (thanaton), reflecting Hebrew consonants read as deber ("pestilence/death") rather than dabar ("word").
- **9:11** — The Septuagint distinctively names "the Greeks" (tous Hellenas) in the west where the Hebrew Masoretic Text names the Philistines.
