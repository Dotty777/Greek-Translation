## Text

**4:1** And seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, "We will eat our own bread, and we will put on our own garments; only let your name be called upon us—take away our reproach."

**4:2** And in that day God shall shine forth in counsel with glory upon the earth, to exalt and glorify the remnant of Israel;

**4:3** and it shall come to pass that what is left behind in Zion and what remains in Jerusalem—they shall be called holy, all who have been written for life in Jerusalem;

**4:4** for the Lord will wash away the filth of the sons and of the daughters of Zion, and He will cleanse the blood from their midst by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.

**4:5** And He will come, and it shall be that a cloud will overshadow every place of Mount Zion and all that is around her by day, and as of smoke and the light of a burning fire by night; and with all glory it shall be sheltered;

**4:6** and it shall be for a shade from the heat, and for a shelter and a hiding place from harshness and rain.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Septuagint reads "God shall shine forth in counsel with glory," differing from the Masoretic Hebrew reference to "the Branch of the Lord."
- **4:4** — The Greek explicitly includes "the sons and the daughters of Zion," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew mentions only the daughters.
- **4:5** — The Greek opens with "And He will come" (kai hexei), where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "and the Lord will create."
