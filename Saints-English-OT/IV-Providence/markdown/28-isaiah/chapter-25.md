## Text

**25:1** O Lord God, I will glorify You, I will sing praise to Your name, for You have done wondrous things, an ancient and true counsel; so be it.

**25:2** For You have turned cities into a mound, fortified cities so that their foundations should not fall; the city of the ungodly shall never be built.

**25:3** Therefore the poor people shall bless You, and cities of wronged men shall bless You.

**25:4** For You have been a helper to every humble city, and a shelter to those who have lost heart because of want; You shall deliver them from wicked men—a shelter for the thirsty, and the spirit of wronged men,

**25:5** like fainthearted men thirsting in Zion because of ungodly men, to whom You delivered us.

**25:6** And the Lord of hosts shall act for all the nations; upon this mountain they shall drink gladness, they shall drink wine;

**25:7** they shall anoint themselves with ointment on this mountain. Deliver all these things to the nations, for this counsel is upon all the nations.

**25:8** Death prevailed and swallowed up, and again the Lord God took away every tear from every face; the reproach of the people He took away from all the earth, for the mouth of the Lord has spoken.

**25:9** And they shall say in that day, "Behold our God, in whom we hoped, and He shall save us; this is the Lord, we waited for Him and exulted, and we shall be glad in our salvation."

**25:10** God shall give rest upon this mountain, and the land of Moab shall be trampled down just as they tread a threshing floor with wagons;

**25:11** and he shall let down his hands, just as he himself also brought low in order to destroy, and He shall humble his arrogance over the things upon which he laid his hands;

**25:12** and the height of the refuge of your wall He shall bring low, and they shall come down to the ground.

## Translator Notes

- **25:2** — The Greek text explicitly includes the negative me ("so that their foundations should not fall"), contrasting the fortified cities God upholds with the city of the ungodly that shall never be built.
- **25:6** — Greek Kyrios Sabaoth transliterates the Hebrew title rendered here as "the Lord of hosts."
- **25:8** — Unlike the Masoretic Hebrew ("He will swallow up death forever"), the Septuagint makes "death" (ho thanatos) the grammatical subject: "Death prevailed and swallowed up."
- **25:11** — The masculine pronouns shift from Moab ("he shall let down his hands... just as he himself also brought low") to God ("He shall humble his arrogance"), following the dense Septuagint syntax.
