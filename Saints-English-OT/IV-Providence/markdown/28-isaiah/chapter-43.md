## Text

**43:1** And now thus says the Lord God, who made you, Jacob, and who formed you, Israel: "Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine.

**43:2** And if you pass through water, I am with you, and rivers shall not overwhelm you; and if you go through fire, you shall by no means be burned; the flame shall not consume you.

**43:3** For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, who saves you; I have made Egypt and Ethiopia your ransom, and Syene in your place.

**43:4** Since you have become honorable before Me, you have been glorified, and I have loved you; and I will give men in your place, and rulers for your head.

**43:5** Do not fear, for I am with you; from the east I will bring your seed, and from the west I will gather you.

**43:6** I will say to the north, 'Bring,' and to the south, 'Do not hold back; bring My sons from a land afar off, and My daughters from the ends of the earth,

**43:7** all who have been called by My name.' For in My glory I have prepared him, and formed him, and made him,

**43:8** and I brought forth a blind people, and their eyes are likewise blind, and they are deaf though having ears.

**43:9** All the nations have been gathered together, and rulers shall be gathered from among them. Who will declare these things? Or who will declare to you the things from the beginning? Let them bring forth their witnesses and be justified, and let them hear and speak what is true.

**43:10** Become My witnesses, and I also am a witness," says the Lord God, "and the servant whom I have chosen, that you may know and believe and understand that I am; before Me there was no other god, and after Me there shall be none.

**43:11** I am God, and beside Me there is no one who saves.

**43:12** I have declared and saved; I reproached, and there was no stranger among us. You are My witnesses, and I am the Lord God,

**43:13** even from the beginning; and there is no one who delivers out of My hands. I will act, and who shall turn it back?"

**43:14** Thus says the Lord God who redeems you, the Holy One of Israel: "For your sake I will send to Babylon and stir up all who flee, and the Chaldeans shall be bound in ships.

**43:15** I am the Lord God, your Holy One, who appointed Israel as your king."

**43:16** Thus says the Lord, who makes a way in the sea and a path in mighty water,

**43:17** who brought forth chariots and horse and a mighty multitude—yet they shall lie down and shall not rise; they have been extinguished like an extinguished wick:

**43:18** "Do not remember the former things, and do not ponder the things of old.

**43:19** Behold, I am doing new things, which shall now spring forth, and you shall know them. And I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the waterless land.

**43:20** The wild beasts of the field shall bless Me, sirens and daughters of ostriches, because I have given water in the wilderness and rivers in the waterless land, to give drink to My chosen race,

**43:21** My people whom I made My own, to recount My praises.

**43:22** Not now have I called you, Jacob, nor have I made you grow weary, Israel.

**43:23** Not for Me are the sheep of your whole burnt offering, nor have you glorified Me with your sacrifices, nor have I burdened you with frankincense,

**43:24** nor have you bought for Me an offering with silver, nor have I desired the fat of your sacrifices; rather, in your sins you have stood before Me, and in your unrighteous deeds.

**43:25** I am, I am He who blots out your iniquities for My own sake, and your sins, and I will not remember them.

**43:26** But you, remember, and let us enter into judgment together; declare your iniquities first, that you may be justified.

**43:27** Your fathers first, and your rulers, acted lawlessly against Me,

**43:28** and the rulers defiled My holy things; therefore I gave Jacob over to destruction, and Israel to reproach."

## Translator Notes

- **43:3** — "Syene" translates the Septuagint's Soene (an Upper Egyptian border city), where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "Seba."
- **43:12** — The Greek text reads "I reproached" (oneidisa) and the first-person plural "among us" (en hemin), where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "I proclaimed" and "among you."
- **43:15** — The Septuagint reads "who appointed Israel as your king," taking Israel as the object appointed to kingship rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "the Creator of Israel, your King."
- **43:20** — "Sirens and daughters of ostriches" renders the Septuagint's seirenes kai thygateres strouthon, Greek equivalents used for desert creatures corresponding to jackals and ostriches in the Hebrew.
- **43:26** — The Septuagint explicitly supplies "your iniquities" (tas anomias sou) as what Jacob is summoned to declare first in the courtroom dispute.
