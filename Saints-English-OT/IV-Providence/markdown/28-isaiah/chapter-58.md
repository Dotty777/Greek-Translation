## Text

**58:1** Cry aloud with strength and do not spare; lift up your voice like a trumpet, and declare to My people their sins, and to the house of Jacob their iniquities.

**58:2** Day after day they seek Me, and they desire to know My ways, as a people who have practiced righteousness and have not forsaken the judgment of their God; now they ask of Me righteous judgment, and they desire to draw near to God,

**58:3** saying, "Why is it that we have fasted, and You did not see? We have humbled our souls, and You did not know?" For in the days of your fasts you find your own desires, and you goad all those who are under your hand.

**58:4** If you fast for disputes and quarrels, and strike a lowly man with fists, why do you fast for Me as today, so that your voice may be heard in outcry?

**58:5** This is not the fast that I have chosen, nor a day for a man to humble his soul; not even if you bend your neck like a ring, and spread sackcloth and ashes beneath yourself, not even so shall you call it an acceptable fast.

**58:6** I have not chosen such a fast, says the Lord; rather, loose every bond of injustice, untie the knots of oppressive contracts, send forth the broken in liberty, and tear apart every unjust written bond.

**58:7** Break your bread for the hungry, and bring the homeless poor into your house; if you see a naked man, clothe him, and you shall not disregard the kinsmen of your own seed.

**58:8** Then your light shall break forth early, and your healings shall swiftly spring up; your righteousness shall go before you, and the glory of God shall envelop you.

**58:9** Then you shall cry out, and God will listen to you; while you are still speaking, He will say, "Behold, I am here." If you remove from yourself a bond, the stretching out of the hand, and a word of murmuring,

**58:10** and give bread from your soul to the hungry, and satisfy a humbled soul, then your light shall rise in the darkness, and your darkness shall be as noonday.

**58:11** And your God shall be with you continually; you shall be filled just as your soul desires, and your bones shall be fattened, and they shall be like a well-watered garden and like a spring which water has not failed.

**58:12** And your ancient wastes shall be built up, and your foundations shall be everlasting for generations of generations; you shall be called Builder of Fences, and you shall bring your pathways in the midst to rest.

**58:13** If you turn back your foot from the Sabbaths, so as not to do your own desires on the holy day, and call the Sabbaths delightful, holy to God, and do not lift your foot for work, nor speak a word in anger from your mouth,

**58:14** then you shall trust in the Lord, and He shall bring you up upon the good things of the earth, and feed you with the inheritance of Jacob your father; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

## Translator Notes

- **58:5** — Greek reads "like a ring" (κρίκον) rather than the Hebrew "like a reed" and frames the verse as a declarative statement rather than a question.
- **58:6** — Greek reads "I have not chosen such a fast... rather..." (pointing back to verse 5) and renders the final clause as tearing apart "every unjust written bond" (συγγραφήν) rather than breaking a yoke.
- **58:11** — Greek reads "your God shall be with you continually" and "just as your soul desires," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "will guide you" and "in scorched places."
- **58:12** — Greek reads "bring your pathways in the midst to rest" (παύσεις, which can also mean "cause to cease"), where the Hebrew has "restorer of streets to dwell in."
