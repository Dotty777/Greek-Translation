## Text

**19:1** The vision of Egypt. Behold, the Lord sits upon a swift cloud and shall come into Egypt; and the handmade idols of Egypt shall be shaken at His presence, and their heart shall be overcome within them.

**19:2** And Egyptians shall rise up against Egyptians, and a man shall make war against his brother, and a man against his neighbor, city against city and district against district.

**19:3** And the spirit of the Egyptians shall be troubled within them, and I will scatter their counsel; and they shall inquire of their gods and their images, and those who speak from the earth and the mediums.

**19:4** And I will deliver Egypt into the hands of men who are harsh lords, and harsh kings shall rule over them. Thus says the Lord of hosts.

**19:5** And the Egyptians shall drink the water that is beside the sea, but the river shall fail and be dried up;

**19:6** and the rivers and the canals of the river shall fail, and every gathering of water shall be dried up, even in every marsh of reed and papyrus;

**19:7** and all the green sedge around the river, and whatever is sown along the river, shall be dried up, blasted by the wind.

**19:8** And the fishermen shall groan, and all who cast hooks into the river shall groan, and those who cast dragnets and the net-casters shall mourn.

**19:9** And shame shall seize those who work the combed flax and those who work the fine linen,

**19:10** and those who work them shall be in pain, and all who make barley beer shall be grieved and suffer pain in their souls.

**19:11** And the rulers of Tanis shall be fools; the wise counselors of the king—their counsel shall be made foolish. How shall you say to the king, "We are sons of men of understanding, sons of kings from the beginning"?

**19:12** Where now are your wise men? And let them declare to you and tell what the Lord of hosts has purposed against Egypt.

**19:13** The rulers of Tanis have failed, and the rulers of Memphis have been exalted, and they shall lead Egypt astray by tribes.

**19:14** For the Lord has mixed for them a spirit of error, and they have led Egypt astray in all their works, as a drunken man staggers while vomiting at the same time.

**19:15** And there shall be no work for the Egyptians that shall produce head and tail, and beginning and end.

**19:16** And in that day the Egyptians shall be like women, in fear and in trembling before the hand of the Lord of hosts, which He Himself shall lay upon them.

**19:17** And the land of the Jews shall become a terror to the Egyptians; whoever mentions it to them, they shall be afraid because of the counsel which the Lord has purposed against it.

**19:18** In that day there shall be five cities in Egypt speaking the Canaanite language and swearing by the name of the Lord; one city shall be called the City of Asedek.

**19:19** In that day there shall be an altar to the Lord in the land of the Egyptians, and a pillar at its border to the Lord,

**19:20** and it shall be for a sign forever to the Lord in the land of Egypt; for they shall cry out to the Lord because of those who afflict them, and He shall send them a man who shall save them; judging, he shall save them.

**19:21** And the Lord shall be known to the Egyptians; and the Egyptians shall know the Lord in that day, and they shall offer sacrifices, and they shall make vows to the Lord and fulfill them.

**19:22** And the Lord shall strike the Egyptians with a plague, and He shall heal them with healing; and they shall turn back to the Lord, and He shall hear them and heal them.

**19:23** In that day there shall be a way from Egypt to the Assyrians, and the Assyrians shall enter into Egypt, and the Egyptians shall go to the Assyrians, and the Egyptians shall serve the Assyrians.

**19:24** In that day Israel shall be third among the Egyptians and among the Assyrians, blessed in the land

**19:25** which the Lord of hosts has blessed, saying, "Blessed is My people who is in Egypt, and who is among the Assyrians, and the land, My inheritance, Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek uses the Egyptian administrative term nomos ("district" or "province") rather than the Hebrew "kingdom."
- **19:10** — The Greek specifically names zythos, Egyptian barley beer, reflecting the translators' familiarity with Egyptian life.
- **19:11** — Tanis is the Greek Septuagint name for the Egyptian city of Zoan (also in 19:13).
- **19:18** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew ha-tsedek ("righteousness") as Asedek ("City of Asedek"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "City of Destruction" (or "City of the Sun").
- **19:25** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Blessed be Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands"), the Greek addresses the Jewish diaspora: "My people who is in Egypt, and who is among the Assyrians"; likewise in 19:23 the Greek reads "the Egyptians shall serve the Assyrians."
