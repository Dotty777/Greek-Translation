## Text

**28:1** Woe to the crown of arrogance, you hirelings of Ephraim, the flower that has fallen from glory upon the summit of the rich mountain, you who are drunk without wine!

**28:2** Behold, strong and harsh is the wrath of the Lord, like descending hail that has no shelter, descending with violence; like a great multitude of water sweeping away a region, He shall make rest for the earth. With hands

**28:3** and with feet the crown of arrogance shall be trampled down, the hirelings of Ephraim.

**28:4** And the flower that has fallen from the hope of life upon the peak of the high mountain shall be like an early fig; he who sees it, before taking it into his hand, will desire to swallow it.

**28:5** In that day the Lord of hosts shall be the crown of hope woven of glory for the remnant of the people;

**28:6** they shall be left with a spirit of judgment for judgment and strength, hindering from slaying.

**28:7** For these have transgressed through wine; they have wandered because of strong drink. Priest and prophet have lost their senses because of strong drink; they have been swallowed up because of wine, they have been shaken by drunkenness, they have gone astray; this is an apparition.

**28:8** A curse shall devour this counsel, for this counsel is for the sake of covetousness.

**28:9** To whom have we declared evils, and to whom have we declared a message? Those who are weaned from milk, those who are drawn from the breast.

**28:10** Expect affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little, yet a little,

**28:11** because of contempt of lips, through another tongue; for they shall speak to this people,

**28:12** saying to them, "This is the rest for the hungry, and this is the crushing," yet they were not willing to hear.

**28:13** And the oracle of the Lord shall be to them affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little, yet a little, that they may go and fall backward, and they shall be crushed, and be in peril, and be captured.

**28:14** Therefore hear the word of the Lord, you afflicted men and rulers of this people who are in Jerusalem,

**28:15** because you have said, "We have made a covenant with Hades, and with death we have made agreements; if a rushing tempest passes through, it shall by no means come upon us; we have made falsehood our hope, and by falsehood we shall be sheltered."

**28:16** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: "Behold, I set into the foundations of Zion a costly, chosen stone, an honored cornerstone, into her foundations; and he who believes shall by no means be put to shame.

**28:17** And I will set judgment for hope, and My mercy for balances; and you who trust vainly in falsehood, that a tempest may not pass over you,

**28:18** lest it also take away your covenant of death, yet your hope toward Hades shall by no means endure; if a rushing tempest comes upon you, you shall be trampled down by it.

**28:19** Whenever it passes through, it shall seize you; morning by morning it shall pass through by day, and in the night there shall be an evil hope."

**28:20** Learn to hear, you who are hard-pressed: "We are not able to fight, and we ourselves are too weak to be gathered together."

**28:21** Like a mountain of the ungodly the Lord shall rise up, and He shall be in the valley of Gibeon; with wrath He shall perform His works, a work of bitterness; and His wrath shall deal strangely, and His decay is strange.

**28:22** And may you not rejoice, nor let your bonds grow strong; for I have heard from the Lord of hosts things completed and cut short, which He shall perform upon all the earth.

**28:23** Give ear and hear my voice; pay heed and hear my words.

**28:24** Will the plowman plow all day long? Or will he prepare seed beforehand before working the ground?

**28:25** When he has leveled its surface, does he not then sow a little black cumin and cumin, and again sow wheat, barley, and millet, and spelt within your borders?

**28:26** And you shall be instructed by the judgment of God, and you shall rejoice.

**28:27** For black cumin is not cleansed with harshness, nor does a cartwheel roll over the cumin; but black cumin is shaken with a rod, and cumin

**28:28** shall be eaten with bread. For I will not be angry with you forever, nor shall the voice of My bitterness trample you down.

**28:29** And these wonders have come forth from the Lord of hosts: take counsel, lift up vain consolation.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Septuagint reads "hirelings of Ephraim" and "drunk without wine," differing from the Masoretic Text's "drunkards of Ephraim" overcome with wine.
- **28:4** — The critical Greek text reads "the hope of life" rather than "glory" at the start of the verse.
- **28:16** — Literally "the Lord, the Lord" (double kyrios). The concluding promise ("he who believes shall by no means be put to shame") is cited in Romans 9:33, 10:11, and 1 Peter 2:6.
- **28:21** — The Greek translates Mount Perazim as "a mountain of the ungodly." The final noun literally means "decay" or "rottenness," often regarded as an early Greek variant for "bitterness."
- **28:28** — The Septuagint uniquely reads that cumin "shall be eaten with bread," followed by God's assurance that He will not be angry forever.
