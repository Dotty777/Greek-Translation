## Text

**1:1** The vision which Isaiah the son of Amoz saw, which he saw against Judea and against Jerusalem, in the reign of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judea.

**1:2** Hear, O heaven, and give ear, O earth, for the Lord has spoken: "I have brought forth sons and exalted them, yet they have rejected Me.

**1:3** An ox knows its owner, and a donkey its master's manger; yet Israel does not know Me, and the people do not understand Me."

**1:4** Woe, sinful nation, a people full of sins, a wicked seed, lawless sons! You have forsaken the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger.

**1:5** Why should you still be struck, adding lawlessness? Every head is given to pain, and every heart to grief.

**1:6** From the feet to the head, neither wound nor bruise nor festering blow—there is no poultice to apply, nor oil, nor bandages.

**1:7** Your land is desolate, your cities are burned with fire; strangers devour your country before your eyes, and it has been laid waste, overthrown by foreign peoples.

**1:8** The daughter of Zion shall be forsaken like a booth in a vineyard, and like a watchman's hut in a cucumber field, like a besieged city.

**1:9** And if the Lord of hosts had not left us a seed, we would have become like Sodom, and we would have been made like Gomorrah.

**1:10** Hear the word of the Lord, you rulers of Sodom; give heed to the law of God, you people of Gomorrah.

**1:11** "What is the multitude of your sacrifices to Me?" says the Lord. "I am full of burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of lambs and the blood of bulls and goats I do not desire,

**1:12** not even if you come to appear before Me. For who has required these things from your hands? To tread My court

**1:13** you shall not continue; if you bring fine flour, it is vain; incense is an abomination to Me. Your new moons and the Sabbaths and a great day I cannot endure; fasting and idleness

**1:14** and your new moons and your feasts My soul hates; you have become a surfeit to Me; I will no longer forgive your sins.

**1:15** Whenever you stretch forth your hands, I will turn My eyes away from you; and even if you multiply supplication, I will not listen to you, for your hands are full of blood.

**1:16** Wash yourselves, become clean, remove the wicked deeds from your souls before My eyes; cease from your wicked deeds,

**1:17** learn to do good, seek out justice, rescue the wronged, judge for the orphan, and vindicate the widow.

**1:18** And come, let us settle the dispute," says the Lord. "Even if your sins are like crimson, I will make them white as snow; and if they are like scarlet, I will make them white as wool.

**1:19** And if you are willing and listen to Me, you shall eat the good things of the land;

**1:20** but if you are not willing and do not listen to Me, a sword shall devour you," for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

**1:21** How has the faithful city Zion, full of justice, become a harlot? In her righteousness once lodged, yet now murderers!

**1:22** Your silver is debased; your tavern keepers mix the wine with water.

**1:23** Your rulers are disobedient, companions of thieves, loving bribes, pursuing recompense, not judging for orphans, and not heeding the cause of widows.

**1:24** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Sovereign of hosts: "Woe to the mighty men of Israel! For My wrath shall not cease against the adversaries, and I will execute judgment upon My enemies.

**1:25** And I will bring My hand upon you and refine you in fire to purity, and I will destroy the disobedient, and I will remove all lawless ones from you;

**1:26** and I will appoint your judges as before, and your counselors as from the beginning; and after these things you shall be called 'City of Righteousness, Faithful Mother City Zion.'"

**1:27** For with judgment her captivity shall be saved, and with mercy.

**1:28** And the lawless and the sinners shall be crushed together, and those who forsake the Lord shall be utterly consumed.

**1:29** For they shall be put to shame because of their idols which they themselves desired, and they have been put to shame over the gardens which they coveted.

**1:30** For they shall be like a terebinth that has shed its leaves, and like a garden having no water.

**1:31** And their strength shall be like a stalk of tow, and their works like sparks, and the lawless and the sinners shall be burned up together, and there shall be no one to quench them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek has "against Judea" (kata tes Ioudaias), using the territorial name Judea rather than Judah and the preposition "against," which contrasts with "concerning" (peri) in 2:1.
- **1:6** — The Greek places negative conjunctions ("neither ... nor ... nor") before the three terms for injuries, creating an abrupt construction emphasizing that no remedy—poultice, oil, or bandages—is available.
- **1:9** — "The Lord of hosts" renders Greek Kyrios sabaoth ("Lord Sabaoth"), and "seed" preserves Greek sperma, the Septuagint wording cited in Romans 9:29.
- **1:18** — The Greek uses the first-person active verb leukano ("I will make white") twice, portraying the Lord Himself actively whitening their sins.
