## Text

**35:1** Be glad, O thirsty wilderness; let the wilderness exult and blossom like a lily,

**35:2** and the waste places of the Jordan shall burst into bloom and exult. The glory of Lebanon has been given to her, and the honor of Carmel; and My people shall see the glory of the Lord and the majesty of God.

**35:3** Be strong, O drooping hands and paralyzed knees;

**35:4** Take courage, you who are fainthearted in mind; be strong, do not fear. Behold, our God renders judgment and will recompense; He Himself will come and save us.

**35:5** Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear.

**35:6** Then the lame shall leap like a deer, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be clear; for water has burst forth in the wilderness, and a ravine in a thirsty land,

**35:7** and the waterless place shall become marshes, and in the thirsty land there shall be a spring of water; there shall be gladness of birds, dwellings of reeds and marshes.

**35:8** A clean way shall be there, and it shall be called a holy way; and no unclean one shall pass through there, nor shall there be an unclean way there; but the dispersed shall walk upon it, and shall by no means go astray.

**35:9** And no lion shall be there, nor shall any of the evil beasts go up upon it, nor shall any be found there; rather, the redeemed shall walk in it

**35:10** and those gathered together on account of the Lord; and they shall return and come to Zion with gladness, and everlasting gladness shall be upon their head—praise and exultation—and gladness shall overtake them; pain and sorrow and sighing have fled away.

## Translator Notes

- **35:2** — The Greek specifies 'the waste places of the Jordan,' a distinctive Septuagint expansion not found in the Hebrew text.
- **35:6** — 'Stammerers' translates the rare Greek word mogilalos ('speaking with difficulty'), a term notably echoed in Mark 7:32.
- **35:7** — The Greek reads 'gladness of birds' rather than 'haunt of jackals,' and in verse 8 specifies 'the dispersed' walking upon the clean way.
