## Text

**55:1** You who thirst, go to the water, and all you who have no silver, go forth and buy, and eat, without silver and price, wine and fat.

**55:2** Why do you pay a price in silver, and your labor not for satisfaction? And you shall eat good things, and your soul shall delight itself in good things.

**55:3** Give heed with your ears and follow after My ways; listen to Me, and your soul shall live in good things; and I will make with you an everlasting covenant, the faithful holy things of David.

**55:4** Behold, I have given him as a testimony among the nations, a ruler and commander to the nations.

**55:5** Nations that do not know you shall call upon you, and peoples who are not acquainted with you shall flee for refuge to you, for the sake of the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you.

**55:6** Seek the Lord, and upon finding Him, call upon Him; and whenever He draws near to you,

**55:7** let the ungodly forsake his ways and a lawless man his counsels, and let him return to the Lord, and he shall receive mercy; for He will abundantly forgive your sins.

**55:8** "For My counsels are not like your counsels, nor are My ways like your ways," says the Lord;

**55:9** "but as heaven is distant from the earth, so is My way distant from your ways, and your thoughts from My mind.

**55:10** For just as rain or snow descends from heaven and shall by no means return until it drenches the earth, and makes it bring forth and sprout, and gives seed to the sower and bread for food,

**55:11** so shall My word be that goes forth from My mouth; it shall by no means return until all things that I have willed are accomplished, and I will prosper your ways and My commandments."

**55:12** For in gladness you shall go forth, and in joy you shall be taught; for the mountains and the hills shall leap up, welcoming you in joy, and all the trees of the field shall clap with their branches,

**55:13** and instead of the thornbush shall come up the cypress, and instead of the fleabane shall come up the myrtle; and the Lord shall be for a name and for an everlasting sign, and it shall not fail.

## Translator Notes

- **55:1** — Greek στέαρ reads "fat," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants (chelev, "fat") rather than the Masoretic "milk" (chalav).
- **55:3** — Greek τὰ ὅσια Δαυὶδ τὰ πιστά ("the faithful holy things of David") is the exact Septuagint phrasing later quoted in Acts 13:34.
- **55:9** — Greek uses ἀπέχει ("is distant") rather than "is higher," and sets plural "your thoughts" against singular "My mind."
- **55:11** — Greek omits "empty" and ends with a direct divine promise, "and I will prosper your ways and My commandments."
