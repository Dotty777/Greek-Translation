## Text

**17:1** The word against Damascus. Behold, Damascus shall be removed from among cities, and shall become a ruin,

**17:2** forsaken forever, for a fold of flocks and a resting place, and there shall be none to drive them away;

**17:3** and she shall no longer be a stronghold for Ephraim to flee to, and there shall no longer be a kingdom in Damascus nor the remnant of the Syrians; for you are not better than the sons of Israel and their glory. Thus says the Lord of hosts:

**17:4** "In that day there shall be a failing of the glory of Jacob, and the fatness of his glory shall be shaken.

**17:5** And it shall be as when someone gathers the standing harvest and reaps the seed of the ears of grain; and it shall be as when someone gathers an ear of grain in a hard valley,

**17:6** and stubble is left in it, or like two or three olive berries on the lofty tip, or four or five are left upon their branches," thus says the Lord God of Israel.

**17:7** In that day a man shall trust in Him who made him, and his eyes shall look upon the Holy One of Israel;

**17:8** and they shall by no means trust in the altars, nor in the works of their hands which their fingers have made, and they shall not look upon the trees nor upon their abominations.

**17:9** In that day your cities shall be forsaken, just as the Amorites and the Hivites forsook them from before the face of the sons of Israel, and they shall be desolate,

**17:10** because you have forsaken God your Savior, and have not remembered the Lord your Helper. Therefore you shall plant an unfaithful planting and an unfaithful seed;

**17:11** and on the day that you plant, you shall go astray; and if you sow in the morning, it shall blossom for a harvest on whatever day you take your allotment, and like a human father you shall allot an inheritance to your sons.

**17:12** Woe to the multitude of many nations! Like a surging sea, so you shall be troubled, and the back of many nations shall roar like water.

**17:13** Like much water are many nations, like much water rushing with violence. And He shall cast him off with contempt, and pursue him far away like the fine chaff of winnowers before the wind, and like the dust of a wheel that a tempest carries away.

**17:14** Toward evening, there shall be mourning; before morning, he shall be no more. This is the portion of those who plundered you, and the inheritance for those who inherited you.

## Translator Notes

- **17:2** — Greek reads "forsaken forever" (eis ton aiona) where the Hebrew text reads "the cities of Aroer."
- **17:5** — Greek "a hard valley" (pharangi sterea) interprets the Hebrew place-name "Valley of Rephaim."
- **17:9** — Greek specifies "the Amorites and the Hivites" fleeing before Israel, where the Hebrew text speaks of forsaken wooded heights and summits.
- **17:11** — Greek renders the difficult Hebrew harvest imagery in terms of receiving and apportioning an inheritance (klerose).
