## Text

**29:1** Woe to the city Ariel, which David fought against. Gather produce year upon year; you shall eat, for you shall eat with Moab.

**29:2** For I will distress Ariel, and her strength and wealth shall be Mine.

**29:3** And I will encircle you like David, and I will cast up a palisade around you, and I will set towers around you,

**29:4** and your words shall be brought low to the earth, and your words shall sink into the earth; and your voice shall be like those who call out from the earth, and toward the ground your voice shall grow faint.

**29:5** And the wealth of the ungodly shall be like dust from a wall, and the multitude of those who oppress you like chaff carried along; and it shall happen in an instant, suddenly,

**29:6** from the Lord of hosts; for there shall be a visitation with thunder and earthquake and a great voice, a rushing tempest and a flame of devouring fire.

**29:7** And the wealth of all the nations—all who marched against Jerusalem, and all who wage war against Jerusalem, and all who are gathered together against her and those who afflict her—shall be like one dreaming in sleep by night.

**29:8** And like those who drink and eat in sleep, and when they rise up the dream is vain; and just as a thirsty man dreams that he is drinking, and when he rises up he is still thirsty, and his soul has hoped in vain—so shall be the wealth of all the nations, all who marched against Mount Zion.

**29:9** Grow faint and be astounded; stagger in drunkenness, not from strong drink nor from wine;

**29:10** for the Lord has given you to drink of a spirit of stupor, and He shall close their eyes, and those of their prophets and of their rulers, who see hidden things.

**29:11** And all these words shall be to you like the words of the sealed book, which if they give it to a man who knows letters, saying, "Read these," he will say, "I cannot read, for it is sealed."

**29:12** And this book shall be given into the hands of a man who does not know letters, and one will say to him, "Read this," and he will say, "I do not know letters."

**29:13** And the Lord said, "This people draws near to Me with its mouth, and with their lips they honor Me, yet their heart keeps far away from Me; and in vain they revere Me, teaching precepts of men and doctrines.

**29:14** Therefore, behold, I will proceed to remove this people, and I will remove them; and I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of the discerning I will conceal."

**29:15** Woe to those who form counsel deeply, and their works shall be in darkness, and they will say, "Who has seen us? And who shall know us, or the things that we do?"

**29:16** Shall you not be reckoned as the clay of the potter? Shall the thing formed say to him who formed it, "You did not form me"? Or the thing made to him who made it, "You did not make me with understanding"?

**29:17** Not much longer, and Lebanon shall be changed like Mount Carmel, and Carmel shall be reckoned as a forest.

**29:18** And in that day the deaf shall hear the words of a book, and the eyes of the blind—those in darkness and those in mist—shall see;

**29:19** and the poor shall exult because of the Lord with gladness, and the despairing among men shall be filled with gladness.

**29:20** The lawless man has ceased, and the arrogant has perished, and those who act lawlessly for wickedness have been utterly destroyed,

**29:21** and those who cause men to sin by a word; and they shall make all who reprove in the gates a stumbling block, because they turned aside a righteous man with unjust deeds.

**29:22** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the house of Jacob, whom He set apart from Abraham: "Not now shall Jacob be put to shame, nor shall he now change his countenance;

**29:23** but when their children see My works, for My sake they shall sanctify My name, and they shall sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and they shall fear the God of Israel.

**29:24** And those who err in spirit shall know understanding, and those who murmur shall learn to obey, and the stammering tongues shall learn to speak peace."

## Translator Notes

- **29:3** — The Greek reads "like David" where the Masoretic Text has "like a circle," reflecting an ancient interchange between similar Hebrew consonants.
- **29:5** — Following the Greek critical text, this translates "from a wall" rather than the textual variant "from a wheel."
- **29:22** — The Greek reads "whom He set apart from Abraham," whereas the Masoretic Text has "who redeemed Abraham."
- **29:24** — The Septuagint concludes the chapter with the additional promise, "and the stammering tongues shall learn to speak peace."
