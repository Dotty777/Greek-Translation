## Text

**51:1** Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness and who seek the Lord: look to the solid rock which you hewed out, and to the pit of the cistern which you dug.

**51:2** Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah who travailed in birth with you; for he was one, and I called him, and blessed him, and loved him.

**51:3** And now I will comfort you, Zion, and I have comforted all her desolate places; and I will make her desolate places like a paradise, and the regions toward the west like the paradise of the Lord; gladness and exultation they shall find in her, thanksgiving and the voice of praise.

**51:4** Hear Me, hear Me, My people, and you kings, give ear to Me; for a law shall go forth from Me, and My judgment for a light of the nations.

**51:5** My righteousness draws near swiftly, and My salvation also shall go forth as light, and in My arm the nations shall hope; the islands shall wait for Me, and in My arm they shall hope.

**51:6** Lift up your eyes to heaven, and look upon the earth beneath; for heaven was made firm like smoke, and the earth shall grow old like a garment, and its inhabitants shall die just like these things; but My salvation shall be forever, and My righteousness shall never fail.

**51:7** Listen to Me, you who know judgment, you people in whose heart is My law: do not fear the reproach of men, and do not be overcome by their contempt.

**51:8** For like a garment it shall be devoured by time, and like wool it shall be devoured by a moth; but My righteousness shall be forever, and My salvation to generations of generations.

**51:9** Awake, awake, O Jerusalem, and put on the strength of your arm; awake as in the beginning of the day, as a generation of eternity.

**51:10** Are you not she who lays waste the sea, the abundance of the water of the abyss? She who made the depths of the sea a way of passage for the delivered

**51:11** and the redeemed? For by the Lord they shall be brought back, and they shall come to Zion with gladness and everlasting exultation; for praise is upon their head, and gladness shall overtake them; pain and grief and sighing have fled away.

**51:12** I, even I, am He who comforts you; know who you are, that you were afraid of a mortal man and of a son of man, who have withered like grass.

**51:13** And you forgot God who made you, who made heaven and laid the foundation of the earth; and you feared continually, all the days, the face of the wrath of him who afflicts you, because of the manner in which he purposed to take you away. And now, where is the wrath of him who afflicts you?

**51:14** For when you are saved, he shall neither stand nor tarry;

**51:15** for I am your God, who stirs up the sea and makes its waves roar; the Lord of Sabaoth is My name.

**51:16** I will put My words into your mouth, and beneath the shadow of My hand I will shelter you, with which I established heaven and laid the foundation of the earth; and He shall say to Zion, "You are My people."

**51:17** Awake, awake, arise, O Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the Lord the cup of His wrath; for the cup of downfall, the chalice of wrath, you have drunk dry and emptied out,

**51:18** and there was no one to comfort you from among all your children whom you bore, and there was no one to take hold of your hand, not even from among all your sons whom you raised up.

**51:19** These two things are set against you—who will grieve with you? Downfall and destruction, famine and sword—who will comfort you?

**51:20** Your sons are the ones in distress, who sleep at the head of every exit like a half-boiled beet, who are full of the wrath of the Lord, grown faint through the Lord God.

**51:21** Therefore hear, you who are humbled and drunk, yet not with wine.

**51:22** Thus says the Lord God, who judges His people: "Behold, I have taken out of your hand the cup of downfall, the chalice of My wrath, and you shall no longer drink it again;

**51:23** and I will put it into the hands of those who wronged you, those who humbled you, who said to your soul, 'Bow down, that we may pass over'; and you laid your inward parts level with the ground outside for those who passed by."

## Translator Notes

- **51:1** — The Greek uses active verbs ("which you hewed out... which you dug"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses passives referring to Israel being hewn from the rock.
- **51:6** — The Greek reads "was made firm like smoke" (estereothe), a notable Septuagint rendering compared with the Hebrew "will vanish like smoke."
- **51:9** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which addresses the arm of the Lord, the Greek directly addresses Jerusalem here and in verse 10.
- **51:15** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew title as Sabaoth ("of hosts"), preserved here as "the Lord of Sabaoth" (also in 54:5).
- **51:20** — The Greek vividly compares the fainting sons to "a half-boiled beet" (seutlion hemiephthon), differing markedly from the Hebrew "an antelope in a net."
