## Text

**1:1** The burden of Nineveh, the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

**1:2** The Lord is a jealous God, avenging with wrath; the Lord takes vengeance on His adversaries, and He Himself removes His enemies.

**1:3** The Lord is longsuffering, and great is His strength, and the Lord will by no means acquit; His way is in complete destruction and in violent shaking, and the clouds are the dust of His feet.

**1:4** He threatens the sea and dries it up, and makes all the rivers desolate. Bashan and Carmel are diminished, and the blossoms of Lebanon have failed.

**1:5** The mountains quaked because of Him, and the hills were shaken; and the earth shrank back from His presence—the whole world and all who dwell in it.

**1:6** Who shall endure before the face of His anger? And who shall withstand in the wrath of His fury? His fury melts dominions, and the rocks were shattered because of Him.

**1:7** The Lord is good to those who wait upon Him in the day of affliction, and He knows those who revere Him.

**1:8** And with a flood on its course He will make a complete end; darkness shall pursue those who rise up and His enemies.

**1:9** What do you devise against the Lord? He Himself will make a complete end; He will not take vengeance twice for the same thing in affliction;

**1:10** for even to its foundation it shall be laid waste, and like entangled bindweed it shall be devoured, and like stubble full of dryness.

**1:11** Out of you shall come forth a scheme against the Lord, counseling wicked things in opposition.

**1:12** Thus says the Lord who rules over many waters: "Even so they shall be separated, and your report shall not be heard anymore.

**1:13** And now I will break his rod from you, and I will tear the bonds apart."

**1:14** And the Lord shall command concerning you: "Nothing of your name shall be sown anymore; out of the house of your god I will destroy the carved images, and cast images I will make your grave." For swift—

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Greek "athoon ouk athooei" (literally, "He will not acquit an innocent one") is an emphatic Septuagint idiom rendering a Hebrew infinitive absolute, meaning the Lord will certainly not acquit anyone as guiltless.
- **1:6** — Greek "tekei archas" ("melts dominions") reflects the Septuagint text, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "is poured out like fire."
- **1:7** — Greek "tois hypomenousin auton" ("to those who wait upon Him") is a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a stronghold."
- **1:14** — The Septuagint ends 1:14 with "hoti tacheis" ("For swift—"), connecting it directly to "the feet" at the opening of 2:1, whereas the Masoretic Text concludes 1:14 with "for you are vile."
