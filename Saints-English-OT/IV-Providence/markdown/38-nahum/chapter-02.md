## Text

**2:1** Behold, upon the mountains are the feet of him who brings good tidings and proclaims peace! Keep your feasts, O Judah, pay your vows, for they shall no more continue to pass through you to decay.

**2:2** It is finished; it has been taken away. He has gone up breathing into your face, delivering from affliction. Watch the way, brace your loins, be exceedingly valiant in strength;

**2:3** for the Lord has turned back the arrogance of Jacob as the arrogance of Israel, because those who shake out have shaken them out, and their vine branches; they have destroyed

**2:4** the weapons of their might from among men, mighty men mocking in fire; the reins of their chariots are in the day of his preparation, and the horsemen shall be thrown into confusion

**2:5** in the ways, and the chariots shall be thrown into disorder and entangled together in the broad squares; their appearance is like torches of fire and like lightning darting through.

**2:6** And their nobles shall remember, and they shall flee by day and grow weak in their march, and they shall hasten to her walls and prepare their outposts.

**2:7** The gates of the cities have been opened wide, and the palaces have collapsed,

**2:8** and the foundation was uncovered, and she herself was going up, and her maidservants were led away like doves moaning in their hearts.

**2:9** And Nineveh—her waters are like a pool of water; and they, fleeing, did not stand, and there was none who looked back.

**2:10** They plundered the silver, they were plundering the gold, and there was no end to her adornment; they have been weighed down with all her desirable vessels.

**2:11** Shaking out and tossing up and boiling over, and shattering of the heart, and loosening of knees and pangs upon all loins, and the face of all is like the scorching of a pot.

**2:12** Where is the dwelling place of the lions, and the pasture that was for the cubs? Where did a lion go, that a lion's cub might enter there, and there was none to terrify?

**2:13** A lion seized enough for his cubs and strangled for his lions, and filled his lair with prey and his dwelling place with plunder.

**2:14** "Behold, I am against you," says the Lord Almighty, "and I will burn up your multitude in smoke, and the sword shall devour your lions; and I will destroy your prey from the earth, and your deeds shall no longer be heard."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Greek "eis palaiosin" ("to decay") renders the Hebrew word often translated "wickedness" or "Belial" as wearing out or growing old.
- **2:2** — Greek "emphyson" ("breathing") and "exairoumenos" ("delivering") reflect the Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "scatterer" and "guard."
- **2:7** — Greek "poleon" ("cities") is the Septuagint reading, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "rivers."
- **2:11** — Greek "proskauma chytras" ("the scorching of a pot") reflects the Septuagint understanding of the rare Hebrew phrase rendered "grow pale" in the Masoretic Text.
