## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Zephaniah, son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah, son of Amon, king of Judah.

**1:2** "Let it utterly fail from the face of the earth," says the Lord.

**1:3** "Let man and beasts fail; let the birds of heaven and the fish of the sea fail. And the ungodly shall grow weak, and I will remove the lawless from the face of the earth," says the Lord.

**1:4** "And I will stretch out My hand upon Judah, and upon all who dwell in Jerusalem; and I will remove from this place the names of Baal and the names of the priests,

**1:5** and those who worship the host of heaven upon the housetops, and those who worship and those who swear by the Lord, and those who swear by their king,

**1:6** and those who turn aside from the Lord, and those who do not seek the Lord, and those who do not hold fast to the Lord."

**1:7** Stand in awe before the face of the Lord God, for the day of the Lord is near; for the Lord has prepared His sacrifice, He has sanctified His invited guests.

**1:8** "And it shall come to pass in the day of the sacrifice of the Lord, that I will execute vengeance upon the rulers, and upon the house of the king, and upon all who are clothed in foreign garments;

**1:9** and I will openly execute vengeance upon the gateways in that day, upon those who fill the house of the Lord their God with ungodliness and deceit."

**1:10** "And it shall come to pass in that day," says the Lord, "that there shall be the sound of a cry from the gate of those who pierce, and a wailing from the second gate, and a great crashing from the hills.

**1:11** Lament, you who dwell in the cut-down place, for all the people of Canaan have become alike; all who were exalted with silver have been utterly destroyed.

**1:12** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will search Jerusalem with a lamp, and I will execute vengeance upon the men who despise their observances—those who say in their hearts, 'The Lord will by no means do good, nor will He do evil.'

**1:13** And their wealth shall become plunder, and their houses a desolation; and they shall build houses, yet they shall not dwell in them, and they shall plant vineyards, yet they shall not drink their wine."

**1:14** For the great day of the Lord is near, near and exceedingly swift; the sound of the day of the Lord is ordained bitter and harsh, mighty.

**1:15** A day of wrath is that day, a day of tribulation and distress, a day of untimely ruin and desolation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and mist,

**1:16** a day of trumpet and outcry against the fortified cities and against the high corner towers.

**1:17** "And I will bring distress upon mankind, and they shall walk like blind men, because they have sinned against the Lord." And He shall pour out their blood like dust, and their flesh like dung.

**1:18** And neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the Lord. And by the fire of His jealousy the whole earth shall be consumed, for He will make a full end, even with swiftness, upon all who dwell on the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Greek reads "the ungodly shall grow weak" and "the lawless," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "the stumbling blocks with the wicked" and "mankind."
- **1:4** — The Greek has the plural "the names of Baal" with the feminine definite article (tes Baal), reflecting the ancient practice of reading the feminine word for "shame" in place of Baal.
- **1:9** — The Greek renders this verse "openly upon the gateways" and specifies "the house of the Lord their God," differing from the Hebrew reference to leaping over the threshold and "their master's house."
- **1:10** — The Greek has "the gate of those who pierce" (corresponding to the Fish Gate in the Hebrew), and in verse 11 translates the Hebrew place name Maktesh as "the cut-down place."
