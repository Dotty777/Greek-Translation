## Text

**3:1** Alas, O illustrious and redeemed city,

**3:2** the dove! She did not heed a voice, she did not receive instruction; she did not trust in the Lord, and she did not draw near to her God.

**3:3** Her rulers within her are like roaring lions; her judges are like wolves of Arabia—they did not leave anything behind until the morning.

**3:4** Her prophets are spirit-borne, scornful men; her priests profane the holy things and act ungodly against the law.

**3:5** Yet the Lord is righteous in her midst, and He will by no means do what is unjust; morning by morning He will bring His judgment to light, and it was not hidden; and He has not known unrighteousness in exaction, nor unrighteousness to victory.

**3:6** "In destruction I have pulled down the proud; their corner towers have been laid waste. I will make their streets completely desolate, so that no one passes through; their cities have failed, so that no one exists nor dwells in them.

**3:7** I said, 'Surely fear Me and receive instruction, and you shall by no means be utterly destroyed from before her eyes, according to all that I executed in vengeance upon her.' Make ready, rise early; all their gleaning has been corrupted."

**3:8** "Therefore wait for Me," says the Lord, "until the day of My rising up for a testimony; for My judgment is upon the assemblies of the nations, to receive kings, to pour out upon them all the wrath of My fury; for by the fire of My jealousy the whole earth shall be consumed.

**3:9** For then I will turn to the peoples a tongue for her generation, that all may call upon the name of the Lord, to serve Him under one yoke.

**3:10** From the ends of the rivers of Ethiopia I will receive from among My dispersed ones; they shall bring sacrifices to Me.

**3:11** In that day you shall not be put to shame for all your practices by which you acted ungodly toward Me; for then I will take away from you the taunts of your arrogance, and you shall no longer continue to boast upon My holy mountain.

**3:12** And I will leave in your midst a meek and humble people, and they shall stand in awe of the name of the Lord,

**3:13** even the remnant of Israel; and they shall do no unrighteousness, nor speak vain things, neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth; for they shall pasture and lie down, and there shall be no one to make them afraid."

**3:14** Rejoice, O daughter of Zion! Proclaim aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem! Be glad and delight yourself with all your heart, O daughter of Jerusalem!

**3:15** The Lord has taken away your iniquities; He has redeemed you from the hand of your enemies. The King of Israel, the Lord, is in your midst; you shall see evils no more.

**3:16** In that time the Lord will say to Jerusalem, "Take courage, O Zion; do not let your hands grow weak."

**3:17** The Lord your God is in you; the Mighty One will save you. He will bring gladness upon you, and He will renew you in His love, and He will rejoice over you with delight as in a day of festival.

**3:18** "And I will gather those who have been crushed. Woe! Who has taken up a reproach against her?"

**3:19** "Behold, I am working in you for your sake in that time," says the Lord, "and I will save her who is pressed down and her who has been driven away; and I will receive them for a boast and make them renowned in all the earth.

**3:20** And they shall be put to shame in that time when I deal well with you, and in the time when I receive you; for I will make you renowned and a boast among all the peoples of the earth, when I turn back your captivity before you," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Across verses 1 and 2, the Greek addresses Jerusalem as "O illustrious and redeemed city, the dove," reading the Hebrew word for "oppressing" (hayyonah) as "the dove" (yonah).
- **3:3** — The Greek reads the proper name "wolves of Arabia," vocalizing the Hebrew consonants for "evening" as "Arabia."
- **3:7** — The Greek reads "from before her eyes" and "all their gleaning has been corrupted," reflecting a vocalization of the Hebrew consonants as "gleanings" rather than "deeds."
- **3:17** — The Greek has the notable reading "He will renew you in His love" (rather than the Hebrew "He will be quiet in His love") and places "as in a day of festival" at the close of verse 17 rather than the opening of verse 18.
