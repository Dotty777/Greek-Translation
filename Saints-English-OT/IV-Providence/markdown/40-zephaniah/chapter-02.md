## Text

**2:1** Gather yourselves together and be bound together, O undisciplined nation,

**2:2** before you become like a flower that passes away, before the wrath of the Lord comes upon you, before the day of the fury of the Lord comes upon you.

**2:3** Seek the Lord, all you humble of the earth; work justice and seek righteousness, and answer to them, that you may be sheltered in the day of the wrath of the Lord.

**2:4** For Gaza shall be plundered, and Ashkelon shall be a desolation; and Ashdod shall be cast out at noonday, and Ekron shall be uprooted.

**2:5** Woe to those who dwell along the coastal strip of the sea, you sojourners of the Cretans! The word of the Lord is against you, O Canaan, land of foreigners: "And I will destroy you from habitation;"

**2:6** and Crete shall become a pasture for flocks and a fold for sheep.

**2:7** And the coastal strip of the sea shall belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; upon them they shall pasture in the houses of Ashkelon, and in the evening they shall take lodging away from the face of the sons of Judah, for the Lord their God has visited them, and has turned back their captivity.

**2:8** "I have heard the reproaches of Moab and the buffetings of the sons of Ammon, with which they reproached My people and magnified themselves against My borders.

**2:9** Therefore, as I live," says the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, "surely Moab shall be like Sodom, and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah, and Damascus shall be left behind like a heap of the threshing floor and laid waste forever; and the remnant of My people shall plunder them, and the remnant of My nation shall inherit them."

**2:10** This they shall have in return for their arrogance, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the Lord Almighty.

**2:11** The Lord will appear against them, and He will utterly destroy all the gods of the nations of the earth; and they shall worship Him, every man from his own place, all the islands of the nations.

**2:12** "You also, O Ethiopians, are the wounded of My sword."

**2:13** And He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy the Assyrian, and He will make Nineveh a waterless desolation, like a wilderness.

**2:14** And flocks shall graze in her midst, and all the wild beasts of the earth; chameleons and hedgehogs shall lie down in her paneled ceilings, and wild beasts shall cry out in her breaches, and ravens in her gateways, for cedar is her lofty structure.

**2:15** This is the scornful city that dwells in hope, that says in her heart, "I am, and there is none after me anymore." How she has become a desolation, a pasture for wild beasts! Everyone who passes through her shall hiss and wave his hands.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek reads "before you become like a flower that passes away," differing from the Masoretic Hebrew image of the decree bringing forth and the day passing like chaff.
- **2:5** — The Greek renders the Cherethites as "Cretans" (and names "Crete" in verse 6), while translating Philistines by its standard Septuagint designation "foreigners" (allophyloi).
- **2:9** — The Greek reads the proper name "Damascus" ("left behind like a heap of the threshing floor") where the Hebrew describes a possession of nettles and a salt pit.
- **2:14** — The Greek reads "chameleons and hedgehogs," "ravens in her gateways," and "for cedar is her lofty structure," reflecting distinct Septuagint vocalizations and renderings of the Hebrew.
