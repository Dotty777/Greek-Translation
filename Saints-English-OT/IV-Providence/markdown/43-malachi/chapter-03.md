## Text

**3:1** "Behold, I am sending forth My messenger, and he shall survey the way before My face; and suddenly the Lord whom you seek shall come into His own temple, and the Messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Behold, He is coming," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:2** And who shall endure the day of His entrance? Or who shall stand firm at His appearing? For He enters like the fire of a smelting furnace, and like the lye of fullers.

**3:3** He shall sit smelting and purifying as with silver and as with gold; and He shall purify the sons of Levi and refine them just as gold and as silver; and they shall bring to the Lord a sacrifice in righteousness,

**3:4** and the sacrifice of Judah and Jerusalem shall be pleasing to the Lord, as in the days of old and as in the former years.

**3:5** "And I will draw near to you in judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorceresses, and against the adulteresses, and against those who swear falsely by My name, and against those who defraud the hired laborer of his wages, and those who oppress a widow, and those who beat orphans with the fist, and those who turn aside the judgment of a proselyte, and those who do not fear Me," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:6** "For I am the Lord your God, and I have not changed.

**3:7** And you, O sons of Jacob, do not abstain from the unrighteous deeds of your fathers; you have turned aside from My ordinances and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the Lord, the Almighty. And you said, "In what shall we return?"

**3:8** "Will a man cheat God? For you are cheating Me." Yet you shall say, "In what have we cheated You?" "Because the tithes and the firstfruits are with you;

**3:9** and looking away, you look away, and it is Me you cheat.

**3:10** The year has been completed, and you have brought all the produce into the storehouses, and his plunder shall be in his house. Examine now in this matter," says the Lord Almighty, "whether I will not open for you the floodgates of heaven, and pour out My blessing for you until there is enough.

**3:11** And I will allot for you for food, and I will by no means destroy the fruit of your ground, nor shall your vine in the field fail," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:12** "And all the nations shall call you blessed, for you yourselves shall be a desired land," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:13** "You have made your words heavy against Me," says the Lord. Yet you said, "In what have we spoken against You?"

**3:14** You said, "Vain is the one who serves God; and what profit is it that we have kept His charges, and that we have walked as suppliants before the face of the Lord Almighty?

**3:15** And now we call foreigners blessed; and all who do lawless things are built up; indeed, they have withstood God and have been delivered."

**3:16** These things those who fear the Lord spoke, each one to his neighbor; and the Lord paid heed and listened, and He wrote a book of remembrance before Him for those who fear the Lord and revere His name.

**3:17** "And they shall be Mine," says the Lord Almighty, "on the day which I make, for a treasured possession; and I will choose them in the manner in which a man chooses his own son who serves him.

**3:18** And you shall return and discern between the righteous and the lawless, and between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve.

**3:19** For behold, a day is coming, burning like a furnace, and it shall burn them; and all the foreigners and all who do lawless things shall be stubble, and the coming day shall set them ablaze," says the Lord Almighty, "and neither root nor branch shall be left of them.

**3:20** And for you who fear My name, the Sun of righteousness shall rise, and healing is in His wings; and you shall go forth and leap like calves loosed from their bonds.

**3:21** And you shall trample down the lawless, for they shall be ashes beneath your feet on the day which I make," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:22** "And behold, I am sending to you Elijah the Tishbite before the great and notable day of the Lord comes,

**3:23** who shall restore the heart of a father to a son, and the heart of a man to his neighbor, lest I come and strike the earth utterly."

**3:24** "Remember the law of Moses My servant, just as I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, statutes and ordinances."

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The Septuagint ends verse 6 after "I have not changed" and begins verse 7 with "And you, O sons of Jacob, do not abstain from the unrighteous deeds of your fathers," differing from the Masoretic verse division and reading ("you are not consumed").
- **3:8** — The Greek verb pternizo ("trip up by the heel," "supplant," here rendered "cheat") echoes the wordplay on Jacob's name in Genesis 27:36, reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb usually rendered "rob."
- **3:10** — The Septuagint opens verse 10 with "The year has been completed," uses a past-tense verb ("you have brought"), and reads "his plunder shall be in his house" rather than "that there may be food in My house."
- **3:22** — In the Septuagint, Malachi concludes within chapter 3 (verses 19-24 correspond to 4:1-6 in English Bibles), placing the promise of Elijah (3:22-23) before the command to remember the law of Moses (3:24); in 3:23 the Greek also reads "the heart of a man to his neighbor" rather than "the hearts of the children to their fathers."
