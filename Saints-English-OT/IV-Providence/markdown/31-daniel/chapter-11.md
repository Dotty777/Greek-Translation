## Text

**11:1** "And I, in the first year of Cyrus, stood for might and strength.

**11:2** And now I will declare the truth to you: behold, three more kings shall arise in Persia, and the fourth shall acquire great riches above all; and after he takes hold of his riches, he shall rise up against all the kingdoms of the Greeks.

**11:3** And a mighty king shall arise, and he shall rule with great dominion, and he shall do according to his will.

**11:4** And as soon as he has stood up, his kingdom shall be shattered and divided toward the four winds of heaven, and not to his posterity, nor according to his dominion which he ruled; for his kingdom shall be plucked up, even for others besides these.

**11:5** And the king of the south shall grow strong; and one of their princes shall grow strong above them and shall rule a great dominion.

**11:6** And after his years they shall join in alliance, and the daughter of the king of the south shall come to the king of the north to make covenants with him; yet she shall not retain the strength of an arm, and his seed shall not stand, and she shall be given up, along with those who bring her, and the young woman, and the one who strengthens her in those times.

**11:7** And one shall stand up from the blossom of her root, of his appointed place, and he shall come against the army, and shall enter into the strongholds of the king of the north, and shall deal with them and prevail.

**11:8** And also their gods with their molten images, every precious vessel of theirs, of silver and of gold, he shall carry with captivity into Egypt; and he shall stand above the king of the north.

**11:9** And he shall enter into the kingdom of the king of the south, and he shall return to his own land.

**11:10** And his sons shall gather a multitude in the midst of many; and he shall surely come and overflow, and pass through, and settle down, and contend even to his strength.

**11:11** And the king of the south shall be enraged, and shall go forth and fight with the king of the north; and he shall set forth a great multitude, and a multitude shall be given into his hand.

**11:12** And he shall take the multitude, and his heart shall be lifted up; and he shall cast down ten thousands, yet he shall not prevail.

**11:13** And the king of the north shall return and shall lead a greater multitude than the former; and at the end of the times of years he shall come advancing in great power and with much substance.

**11:14** And in those times many shall rise up against the king of the south; and the sons of the remnant of your people shall exalt themselves to establish a vision, and they shall falter.

**11:15** And the king of the north shall enter and cast up a siege mound and capture fortified cities; and the arms of the king of the south shall take their stand, and his chosen ones also shall rise up, yet there shall be no strength to stand.

**11:16** And the one who comes against him shall do according to his will, and there is no one standing before his face; and he shall stand in the land of Sabir, and it shall be brought to completion in his hand.

**11:17** And he shall set his face to enter with the strength of his whole kingdom, and he shall make all things right with him; and he shall give him a daughter of women to destroy her, yet she shall by no means remain, nor shall she be for him.

**11:18** And he shall turn his face toward the islands and capture many; and he shall put an end to rulers of their reproach, except that his own reproach shall return upon him.

**11:19** And he shall turn his face toward the strength of his own land, and he shall falter and fall, and shall not be found.

**11:20** And there shall arise from his root a plant of the kingdom in his appointed place, sending across and exacting the glory of the kingdom; and in those days he shall be shattered, yet neither face to face nor in war.

**11:21** He shall stand upon his appointed place; he was despised, and they did not bestow upon him the glory of the kingdom; yet he shall come in prosperity, and shall seize the kingdom by slippery deceits.

**11:22** And the arms of the one who overflows shall be overflowed from before his face and shall be shattered, and also the leader of the covenant.

**11:23** And from the alliances made with him he shall practice deceit, and he shall go up and prevail over them with a small nation.

**11:24** And he shall come in prosperity and into fertile regions, and shall do what neither his fathers nor his fathers' fathers have done; plunder and spoil and substance he shall scatter among them, and against Egypt he shall devise schemes, even until a time.

**11:25** And his strength and his heart shall be stirred up against the king of the south with a great army; and the king of the south shall join battle with a great and exceedingly strong army; yet they shall not stand, for they shall devise schemes against him.

**11:26** And they shall eat his provisions and shatter him; and he shall overwhelm armies, and many wounded shall fall.

**11:27** And as for both kings, their hearts shall be bent on wickedness, and at one table they shall speak lies; yet it shall not prosper, for the end is still for an appointed time.

**11:28** And he shall return to his own land with great substance, and his heart shall be against the holy covenant; and he shall act and return to his own land.

**11:29** At the appointed time he shall return and come into the south; yet the last shall not be like the first.

**11:30** And the Kittim who go forth shall come against him, and he shall be humbled; and he shall turn back and be enraged against the holy covenant, and he shall act; and he shall return and give attention to those who forsake the holy covenant.

**11:31** And offspring from him shall arise and profane the sanctuary of dominion, and they shall remove the continual sacrifice, and they shall set up a desolated abomination.

**11:32** And those who act lawlessly against the covenant shall entice with slippery deceits; but the people who know their God shall prevail and act.

**11:33** And the wise of the people shall understand in many things; yet they shall falter by sword and by flame, by captivity and by plundering for days.

**11:34** And when they falter, they shall be helped with a little help; and many shall join themselves to them with slippery deceits.

**11:35** And some of those who have understanding shall falter, to refine them by fire, and to choose, and to be revealed, until the time of the end; for it is still for an appointed time.

**11:36** And he shall do according to his will; and the king shall be exalted and magnified above every god, and shall speak boastful things, and shall prosper until the wrath is accomplished; for it is coming to completion.

**11:37** And he shall have no regard for any god of his fathers, nor for the desire of women, nor shall he have regard for any god; for he shall magnify himself above all.

**11:38** And he shall glorify the god Maozein in his place; and a god whom his fathers did not know he shall glorify with gold and silver, and with precious stone and with desirable things.

**11:39** And he shall act for the strongholds of the refuges with a foreign god; and he shall multiply glory, and he shall subject many to them, and he shall divide the land for gifts.

**11:40** And at the end of time he shall lock horns with the king of the south; and the king of the north shall be gathered against him with chariots and horsemen and with many ships; and they shall enter into the land, and he shall shatter and pass through.

**11:41** And he shall enter into the land of Sabaein, and many shall falter; yet these shall be delivered out of his hand: Edom, and Moab, and the chief of the sons of Ammon.

**11:42** And he shall stretch out his hand over the land, and the land of Egypt shall not be for deliverance.

**11:43** And he shall have dominion over the hidden treasures of gold and of silver, and over all the desirable things of Egypt, and of the Libyans and of the Ethiopians, in their strongholds.

**11:44** And reports and urgencies shall trouble him from the east and from the north; and he shall come with great wrath to destroy many.

**11:45** And he shall pitch his tent Ephadano between the seas toward the holy mountain of Sabaein; yet he shall come to his portion, and there is no one who rescues him."

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Theodotion dates this event to "the first year of Cyrus," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the first year of Darius the Mede."
- **11:14** — Theodotion reads "the sons of the remnant" (loipon) of your people, differing from the Hebrew reading "the violent ones of your people."
- **11:16** — Theodotion transliterates the Hebrew phrase for the "glorious land" as a proper name, "Sabir" here and "Sabaein" in 11:41 and 11:45.
- **11:31** — Theodotion reads "offspring" (spermata, literally "seeds") rather than "arms" or "forces," and uses a passive participle for "a desolated abomination."
- **11:38** — Theodotion transliterates the Hebrew word for "fortresses" as the name of a deity, "the god Maozein," and similarly transliterates "palatial tents" as "Ephadano" in 11:45.
