## Text

**3:1** In the eighteenth year, Nebuchadnezzar the king made a golden image, its height sixty cubits and its breadth six cubits; and he set it up on the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.

**3:2** And he sent to gather together the chief magistrates, the commanders, and the governors, the leaders, the lords, those in authority, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image which he had set up.

**3:3** And the governors, chief magistrates, commanders, leaders, great lords, those in authority, and all the rulers of the provinces were gathered together to the dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they stood before the image.

**3:4** And the herald cried aloud with might, "To you it is declared, O peoples, tribes, and tongues,

**3:5** at whatever hour you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, harp and psaltery, and every kind of music, fall down and worship the golden image which Nebuchadnezzar the king has set up;

**3:6** and whoever does not worship shall in that very hour be cast into the burning furnace of fire."

**3:7** And it came to pass, when the peoples heard the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, harp and psaltery, and every kind of music, all the peoples, tribes, and tongues fell down and worshiped the golden image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

**3:8** Then Chaldean men came forward and accused the Jews to the king,

**3:9** Nebuchadnezzar: "O king, live forever!

**3:10** You, O king, have issued a decree that every man who hears the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, harp and psaltery, and every kind of music,

**3:11** and does not fall down and worship the golden image, shall be cast into the burning furnace of fire.

**3:12** There are Jewish men whom you have appointed over the affairs of the province of Babylon—Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego—who have not obeyed your decree, O king; they do not serve your gods, nor do they worship the golden image which you have set up."

**3:13** Then Nebuchadnezzar, in wrath and fury, gave command to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; and they were brought before the king.

**3:14** And Nebuchadnezzar answered and said to them, "Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not serve my gods, nor worship the golden image which I have set up?

**3:15** Now therefore, if you are ready, so that as soon as you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, harp, psaltery, and symphony, and every kind of music, you fall down and worship the image which I have made—but if you do not worship, in that very hour you shall be cast into the burning furnace of fire. And who is the god who will deliver you out of my hand?"

**3:16** And Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered, saying to King Nebuchadnezzar, "We have no need to answer you concerning this matter.

**3:17** For there is a God whom we serve, who is able to deliver us from the burning furnace of fire; and He will rescue us out of your hands, O king.

**3:18** And if not, let it be known to you, O king, that we do not serve your gods, nor do we worship the image which you have set up."

**3:19** Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with wrath, and the expression of his countenance was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; and he commanded to heat the furnace sevenfold, until it was heated to the utmost.

**3:20** And he commanded men mighty in strength to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, and to cast them into the burning furnace of fire.

**3:21** Then those men were bound in their trousers, turbans, and leggings, and were cast into the midst of the burning furnace of fire,

**3:22** because the word of the king prevailed, and the furnace was heated exceedingly.

**3:23** And these three, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell down bound into the midst of the burning furnace.

**3:24** And they walked about in the midst of the flame, singing hymns to God and blessing the Lord.

**3:25** And Azariah stood up and prayed in this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire, he said:

**3:26** "Blessed are You, O Lord God of our fathers, and worthy of praise, and Your name is glorified forever.

**3:27** For You are righteous in all that You have done, and all Your works are true, and Your ways are upright, and all Your judgments are truth.

**3:28** And You have executed judgments of truth according to all that You have brought upon us and upon the holy city of our fathers, Jerusalem; for in truth and judgment You have brought all these things because of our sins.

**3:29** For we have sinned and acted lawlessly in departing from You, and we have grievously sinned in all things; and we have not heeded Your commandments,

**3:30** nor have we kept them, nor have we done as You commanded us, that it might go well with us.

**3:31** And all that You have brought upon us, and all that You have done to us, You have done in true judgment.

**3:32** And You have delivered us into the hands of lawless enemies and most hateful rebels, and to an unjust king, the most wicked in all the earth.

**3:33** And now we cannot open our mouth; shame and reproach have come upon Your servants and upon those who worship You.

**3:34** Do not deliver us up utterly, for Your name's sake, and do not annul Your covenant,

**3:35** and do not withdraw Your mercy from us, for the sake of Abraham who is beloved by You, and for the sake of Isaac Your servant, and Israel Your holy one,

**3:36** to whom You spoke that You would multiply their seed as the stars of heaven and as the sand along the shore of the sea.

**3:37** For, O Master, we have been made smaller than all the nations, and we are brought low in all the earth this day because of our sins.

**3:38** And there is at this time neither ruler, nor prophet, nor leader, nor burnt offering, nor sacrifice, nor oblation, nor incense, nor place to present an offering before You and find mercy.

**3:39** Yet with a contrite soul and a spirit of humility may we be accepted; as with a burnt offering of rams and bulls, and with ten thousands of fat lambs,

**3:40** so let our sacrifice be before You this day, and may we wholly follow after You; for there shall be no shame for those who trust in You.

**3:41** And now we follow You with all our heart, and we fear You and seek Your face. Do not put us to shame,

**3:42** but deal with us according to Your forbearance and according to the multitude of Your mercy.

**3:43** And deliver us according to Your wondrous works, and give glory to Your name, O Lord.

**3:44** And let all those who do evil to Your servants be confounded, and let them be put to shame from all power, and let their strength be broken;

**3:45** let them know that You are the Lord, the only God, and glorious over all the inhabited earth."

**3:46** And the king's servants who had cast them in did not cease stoking the furnace with naphtha, pitch, tow, and brushwood.

**3:47** And the flame streamed forth forty-nine cubits above the furnace,

**3:48** and it broke forth and burned up those of the Chaldeans whom it found around the furnace.

**3:49** But the angel of the Lord came down into the furnace together with Azariah and his companions, and drove the flame of the fire out of the furnace,

**3:50** and made the midst of the furnace like a whistling breeze of dew; and the fire did not touch them at all, neither did it hurt nor trouble them.

**3:51** Then the three, as out of one mouth, sang hymns and glorified and blessed God in the furnace, saying:

**3:52** "Blessed are You, O Lord God of our fathers, and worthy of praise and exalted above all forever. And blessed is the holy name of Your glory, and supremely worthy of praise and exalted above all to all ages.

**3:53** Blessed are You in the temple of Your holy glory, and supremely extolled and supremely glorious forever.

**3:54** Blessed are You upon the throne of Your kingdom, and supremely extolled and exalted above all forever.

**3:55** Blessed are You who behold the depths, sitting upon the cherubim, and worthy of praise and exalted above all forever.

**3:56** Blessed are You in the firmament of heaven, and extolled and glorified forever.

**3:57** Bless the Lord, all works of the Lord; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:58** Bless the Lord, angels of the Lord; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:59** Bless the Lord, O heavens; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:60** Bless the Lord, waters and all things above the heaven; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:61** Let every power bless the Lord; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:62** Bless the Lord, sun and moon; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:63** Bless the Lord, stars of heaven; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:64** Let every shower and dew bless the Lord; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:65** Bless the Lord, all winds; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:66** Bless the Lord, fire and heat; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:71** Bless the Lord, nights and days; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:72** Bless the Lord, light and darkness; praise and exalt Him above all forever. Bless the Lord, cold and heat; praise and exalt Him above all forever. Bless the Lord, frost and snow; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:73** Bless the Lord, lightnings and clouds; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:74** Let the earth bless the Lord; let it praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:75** Bless the Lord, mountains and hills; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:76** Bless the Lord, all things that grow upon the earth; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:77** Bless the Lord, O springs; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:78** Bless the Lord, seas and rivers; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:79** Bless the Lord, great sea creatures and all things that move in the waters; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:80** Bless the Lord, all birds of heaven; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:81** Bless the Lord, all wild beasts and cattle; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:82** Bless the Lord, O sons of men; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:83** Bless the Lord, O Israel; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:84** Bless the Lord, O priests; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:85** Bless the Lord, O servants; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:86** Bless the Lord, spirits and souls of the righteous; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:87** Bless the Lord, holy ones and humble of heart; praise and exalt Him above all forever.

**3:88** Bless the Lord, Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael; praise and exalt Him above all forever; for He has delivered us from Hades, and saved us from the hand of death, and rescued us from the midst of the furnace of burning flame, and from the midst of the fire He has rescued us.

**3:89** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy endures forever.

**3:90** Bless the God of gods, all you who worship the Lord; sing praise and give thanks, for His mercy endures forever."

**3:91** And Nebuchadnezzar heard them singing hymns and marveled, and he rose up in haste and said to his nobles, "Did we not cast three men bound into the midst of the fire?" And they said to the king, "Truly, O king."

**3:92** "Behold, I see four men unbound and walking about in the midst of the fire, and there is no harm in them, and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of God."

**3:93** Then Nebuchadnezzar drew near to the door of the burning furnace of fire and said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out and come here." And Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out from the midst of the fire.

**3:94** And the satraps, the commanders, the governors, and the king's potentates gathered together, and they beheld the men, that the fire had no power over their bodies, and the hair of their heads was not scorched, nor were their trousers altered, nor was there the smell of fire upon them.

**3:95** And Nebuchadnezzar answered and said, "Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who has sent His angel and delivered His servants, because they trusted in Him; and they set aside the word of the king and yielded up their bodies to the fire, that they might not serve nor worship any god except their own God.

**3:96** And I set forth this decree: Every people, tribe, and tongue—if any speaks blasphemy against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, they shall come to destruction, and their houses to plunder; because there is no other god who is able to rescue in this manner."

**3:97** Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in the province of Babylon, and deemed them worthy to rule over all the Jews who were in his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text (Theodotion) includes the chronological notice "In the eighteenth year," which is absent from the Aramaic Masoretic Text.
- **3:15** — The Greek term rendered "symphony" (symphonia) appears only here among the instrument lists in Theodotion (absent in 3:5, 7, 10) and may refer either to a musical ensemble or to a specific wind instrument such as a bagpipe.
- **3:24** — Verses 3:24-90 (beginning here with the Prayer of Azariah in 3:24-45) are part of the Greek text of Daniel (Theodotion) and are not found in the Aramaic Masoretic Text.
- **3:40** — The Greek phrase literally meaning "and to fulfill after You" (kai ektelesai opisthen sou) is syntactically difficult and likely reflects a Semitic idiom meaning "to wholly follow after You" (compare Numbers 14:24).
- **3:72** — In this Greek critical edition (Theodotion), the calls to "cold and heat" and "frost and snow" are grouped within 3:72 after "light and darkness," so verses 3:67-3:70 are omitted from the versification.
- **3:92** — Theodotion reads the anarthrous singular "a son of God" (huio theou), which early Christian interpreters commonly understood as a pre-incarnate appearance of the Son of God, whereas the Aramaic Masoretic Text reads plural "a son of the gods" (bar elahin).
- **3:97** — The closing clause, "and deemed them worthy to rule over all the Jews who were in his kingdom," is present in Greek Daniel (Theodotion) but absent from the Aramaic Masoretic Text.
