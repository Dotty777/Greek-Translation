## Text

**12:1** "And at that time Michael shall arise, the great prince who stands over the sons of your people; and there shall be a time of tribulation, tribulation such as has not occurred since a nation came into being on the earth until that time. At that time your people shall be saved, everyone who is written in the book.

**12:2** And many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth shall be awakened, these to eternal life, and those to reproach and to eternal shame.

**12:3** And those who have understanding shall shine like the brightness of the firmament, and some from among the many righteous like the stars forever and beyond.

**12:4** And you, Daniel, shut up the rest and seal the book until the time of the end, until many are taught and knowledge is multiplied."

**12:5** And I, Daniel, looked, and behold, two others were standing, one on this side of the bank of the river and one on that side of the bank of the river.

**12:6** And one said to the man clothed in linen garments, who was above the water of the river, "How long until the end of the wondrous things which you have spoken?"

**12:7** And I heard the man clothed in linen garments, who was above the water of the river, and he lifted up his right hand and his left hand to heaven, and swore by Him who lives forever, "For a time of times and half a time; when the scattering is completed, they shall know all these things."

**12:8** And I heard, and did not understand; and I said, "O lord, what shall be the latter end of these things?"

**12:9** And he said, "Come, Daniel, for the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end,

**12:10** when many shall be chosen and made white and refined by fire, and the lawless shall act lawlessly; and the lawless shall not understand, but the discerning shall understand.

**12:11** And from the time of the removal of the continual sacrifice—and the abomination of desolation shall be set up—there shall be one thousand two hundred and ninety days.

**12:12** Blessed is he who endures and attains to one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.

**12:13** And you, come and rest; for there are yet days until the fulfillment of the end, and you shall rise to your allotted portion at the end of days."

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Greek text (Theodotion) reads "from among the many righteous" as a partitive expression, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "those who turn many to righteousness."
- **12:4** — The Greek text reads "the rest" (tous loipous, as in Codex Vaticanus), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read "the words" (tous logous); likewise, "until many are taught" reflects the Greek verb rather than the Hebrew "many shall run to and fro."
- **12:6** — "Linen garments" renders the Greek transliteration ta baddein (from the Hebrew term for priestly linen), and the phrase "which you have spoken" is a reading distinctive to the Greek text.
- **12:7** — "A time of times" reflects the Greek genitive plural (kairon kairon), and the final clause follows the Greek ("when the scattering is completed, they shall know all these things") rather than the Hebrew ("when the shattering of the power of the holy people is finished, all these things shall be completed").
- **12:13** — The clause "for there are yet days until the fulfillment of the end" is a distinctive Greek reading not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
