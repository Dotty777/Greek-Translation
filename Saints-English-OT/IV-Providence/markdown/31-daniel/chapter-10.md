## Text

**10:1** In the third year of Cyrus king of the Persians, a word was revealed to Daniel, whose name was called Belteshazzar; and the word was true, and great power and understanding were given to him in the vision.

**10:2** In those days I, Daniel, was mourning for three weeks of days.

**10:3** I ate no choice bread, and neither meat nor wine entered my mouth, nor did I anoint myself with ointment, until the completion of three weeks of days.

**10:4** On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was beside the great river, which is Tigris Eddekel.

**10:5** And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, one man clothed in baddin, and his waist was girded with gold of Uphaz.

**10:6** And his body was like Tarshish, and his face like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes like torches of fire, and his arms and legs like the appearance of gleaming bronze, and the sound of his words like the voice of a multitude.

**10:7** And I, Daniel, alone saw the vision, and the men who were with me did not see the vision; rather, a great dread fell upon them, and they fled in fear.

**10:8** And I was left alone and saw this great vision, and no strength remained in me; and my glory was turned into corruption, and I retained no strength.

**10:9** And I heard the sound of his words; and when I heard him, I was stunned, and my face was toward the ground.

**10:10** And behold, a hand touched me and raised me upon my knees.

**10:11** And he said to me, "Daniel, greatly desired man, understand the words that I speak to you, and stand in your place, for now I have been sent to you." And while he spoke this word to me, I stood up trembling.

**10:12** And he said to me, "Do not fear, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to afflict yourself before your God, your words were heard; and I have come because of your words.

**10:13** And the prince of the kingdom of the Persians stood opposite me twenty-one days; and behold, Michael, one of the princes, came to help me, and I left him there with the prince of the kingdom of the Persians.

**10:14** And I have come to make you understand all that shall befall your people in the last of the days, for the vision is still for days."

**10:15** And while he was speaking with me according to these words, I set my face toward the ground.

**10:16** And behold, one like the likeness of a son of man touched my lips; and I opened my mouth and spoke, and said to the one standing before me, "O lord, in your vision my inward parts were turned within me, and I had no strength.

**10:17** And how shall your servant, O lord, be able to speak with this my lord? As for me, from now on no strength will remain in me, and no breath is left in me."

**10:18** Then the one like the appearance of a man touched me again and strengthened me.

**10:19** And he said to me, "Do not fear, greatly desired man; peace be to you! Be courageous and be strong." And as he spoke with me, I was strengthened, and said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

**10:20** And he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? And now I shall return to fight with the prince of the Persians; and I was going in, and the prince of the Greeks was coming.

**10:21** Nevertheless, I will declare to you what is recorded in the writing of truth; and there is not one who holds firmly with me concerning these things except Michael your prince."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Theodotion reads "great power" (dynamis megale) joined with "understanding were given to him," whereas the Hebrew text speaks of a "great conflict."
- **10:4** — Theodotion preserves a double name for the river, combining Greek "Tigris" with the transliterated Hebrew "Eddekel" (Hiddekel).
- **10:5** — Theodotion transliterates the Hebrew term for linen garments as "baddin" here and the gemstone name as "Tarshish" in 10:6 (denoting a precious stone such as beryl or chrysolite).
- **10:13** — Theodotion reads "I left him there with the prince of the kingdom of the Persians," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I was left there with the kings of Persia."
