## Text

**2:1** In the second year of the reign, Nebuchadnezzar dreamed a dream, and his spirit was deeply troubled, and his sleep departed from him.

**2:2** And the king commanded to summon the enchanters, and the magicians, and the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans, to declare to the king his dreams; and they came and stood before the king.

**2:3** And the king said to them, "I have dreamed a dream, and my spirit is distraught to know the dream."

**2:4** And the Chaldeans spoke to the king in Syriac, "O king, live forever! Tell the dream to your servants, and we will declare the interpretation."

**2:5** The king answered the Chaldeans, "The word has departed from me; if you do not make known to me the dream and the interpretation, you shall come to destruction, and your houses shall be plundered.

**2:6** But if you make known to me the dream and its interpretation, gifts and rewards and great honor you shall receive from me; only declare to me the dream and its interpretation."

**2:7** They answered a second time and said, "Let the king tell the dream to his servants, and we will declare its interpretation."

**2:8** And the king answered and said, "Of a truth I know that you are buying time, because you saw that the word has departed from me.

**2:9** If therefore you do not declare the dream to me, I know that you have conspired to speak a false and corrupt word before me until the time passes by; tell me my dream, and I shall know that you will declare to me its interpretation."

**2:10** The Chaldeans answered before the king and say, "There is not a man upon the dry land who shall be able to make known the king's matter, because no great king or ruler asks such a thing of any enchanter, magician, or Chaldean;

**2:11** for the matter which the king asks is grievous, and there is no other who shall declare it before the king except the gods, whose dwelling is not with all flesh."

**2:12** Then the king in wrath and anger commanded to destroy all the wise men of Babylon.

**2:13** And the decree went forth, and the wise men were being slain; and they sought Daniel and his companions to slay them.

**2:14** Then Daniel answered with counsel and judgment to Arioch, the chief captain of the king's guard, who had gone forth to slay the wise men of Babylon,

**2:15** "Captain of the king, why has the harsh decree gone forth from the presence of the king?" And Arioch made the matter known to Daniel.

**2:16** And Daniel petitioned the king that he would grant him time, and he would declare its interpretation to the king.

**2:17** And Daniel went into his house and made the matter known to Hananiah, and to Mishael, and to Azariah his companions;

**2:18** and they sought compassions from the God of heaven concerning this mystery, that Daniel and his companions might not perish with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.

**2:19** Then to Daniel in a vision of the night the mystery was revealed. And Daniel blessed the God of heaven,

**2:20** and said, "May the name of God be blessed from everlasting and to everlasting, for wisdom and understanding are His;

**2:21** and He changes times and seasons; He sets up kings and removes them, giving wisdom to the wise and discernment to those who know understanding;

**2:22** He reveals deep and hidden things, knowing what is in the darkness, and the light abides with Him.

**2:23** To You, O God of my fathers, I give thanks and praise, for You have given me wisdom and might, and have made known to me the things which we requested of You; yes, the vision of the king You have made known to me."

**2:24** And Daniel came to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon, and said to him, "Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon, but bring me in before the king, and I will declare the interpretation to the king."

**2:25** Then Arioch in haste brought Daniel in before the king and said to him, "I have found a man of the sons of the captivity of Judea, who will declare the interpretation to the king."

**2:26** And the king answered and said to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, "Are you able to declare to me the dream which I saw, and its interpretation?"

**2:27** And Daniel answered before the king and says, "The mystery which the king asks, it is not in the power of wise men, magicians, enchanters, or diviners to declare to the king;

**2:28** rather, there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries, and He has made known to King Nebuchadnezzar the things which must come to pass in the last days. Your dream and the visions of your head upon your bed are this:

**2:29** O king, your thoughts upon your bed arose as to what must come to pass hereafter, and He who reveals mysteries has made known to you what must come to pass.

**2:30** And to me, not by any wisdom that is in me more than all the living has this mystery been revealed, but for the sake of making known the interpretation to the king, that you may know the thoughts of your heart.

**2:31** You, O king, were beholding, and behold, one image; great was that image, and its appearance surpassing, standing before your face, and its aspect was terrible.

**2:32** An image whose head was of fine gold, its hands and breast and arms of silver, its belly and thighs of bronze,

**2:33** its legs of iron, its feet partly of iron and partly of earthenware.

**2:34** You were beholding until a stone was broken away out of a mountain without hands, and it struck the image upon its feet of iron and earthenware, and ground them to powder utterly.

**2:35** Then were ground to powder all at once the earthenware, the iron, the bronze, the silver, and the gold, and it became like dust from a summer threshing floor; and the force of the wind carried them away, and no place was found for them; and the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled all the earth.

**2:36** This is the dream; and its interpretation we will tell before the king.

**2:37** You, O king, are a king of kings, to whom the God of heaven has given a strong and mighty and honorable kingdom

**2:38** in every place where the sons of men dwell; both wild beasts of the field and birds of heaven He has given into your hand, and has set you as lord of all; you are the head of gold.

**2:39** And after you shall arise another kingdom inferior to you, and a third kingdom which is the bronze, which shall have dominion over all the earth;

**2:40** and a fourth kingdom shall be strong as the iron; in like manner as iron grinds to powder and subdues all things, so shall it grind to powder and subdue all things.

**2:41** And whereas you saw the feet and the toes partly of earthenware and partly of iron, it shall be a divided kingdom, yet of the root of iron there shall be in it, in like manner as you saw the iron mingled with the earthenware.

**2:42** And as the toes of the feet were partly of iron and partly of earthenware, a part of the kingdom shall be strong, and a part of it shall be shattered.

**2:43** Whereas you saw the iron mingled with the earthenware, they shall be mingled in the seed of men; yet they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron does not mingle with earthenware.

**2:44** And in the days of those kings shall the God of heaven raise up a kingdom which to the ages shall not be destroyed, and His kingdom shall not be left to another people; it shall grind to powder and winnow all the kingdoms, and itself shall stand to the ages,

**2:45** in like manner as you saw that out of a mountain a stone was cut without hands, and it ground to powder the earthenware, the iron, the bronze, the silver, and the gold. The great God has made known to the king what must come to pass hereafter; and true is the dream, and faithful its interpretation."

**2:46** Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell upon his face and bowed down to Daniel, and commanded to offer manna and sweet savors to him.

**2:47** And the king answered and said to Daniel, "Of a truth your God, He is God of gods and Lord of kings, and a revealer of mysteries, seeing you have been able to reveal this mystery."

**2:48** And the king magnified Daniel, and gave him great and many gifts, and set him over all the province of Babylon and as chief of satraps over all the wise men of Babylon.

**2:49** And Daniel made request of the king, and he set over the affairs of the province of Babylon Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; and Daniel remained in the court of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — Greek ho logos ap' emou apeste ('the word has departed from me') reflects Theodotion's rendering of Aramaic azda ('is firm/gone forth') as 'has escaped/departed from me.'
- **2:14** — Greek archimageiros literally means 'chief cook/butcher,' the standard Septuagint title for the royal captain of the guard (Hebrew/Aramaic rab tabbachayya).
- **2:26** — Greek transliterates Daniel's Babylonian name as Baltasar (rendered 'Belteshazzar' per standard modern biblical spelling, as with Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah in v. 17 and Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego in v. 49).
- **2:32** — Theodotion uniquely adds 'hands' (hai cheires) alongside the breast and arms of silver.
- **2:46** — Greek manna here transliterates Aramaic minchah ('grain offering/tribute'). In v. 47 the king uses plural 'your God' (hymon) for Israel's God before addressing Daniel in the singular.
