## Text

**9:1** In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, who reigned over the kingdom of the Chaldeans,

**9:2** I, Daniel, understood in the books the number of the years concerning which the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, for the fulfillment of the desolation of Jerusalem: seventy years.

**9:3** And I set my face toward the Lord God to seek prayer and supplications, in fastings and sackcloth.

**9:4** And I prayed to the Lord my God, and made confession, and said, "O Lord, the great and wondrous God, who keeps Your covenant and mercy toward those who love You and those who keep Your commandments:

**9:5** we have sinned, we have committed iniquity, we have acted lawlessly, and we have departed and turned aside from Your commandments and from Your judgments;

**9:6** and we have not heeded Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings and our rulers and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

**9:7** To You, O Lord, belongs righteousness, and to us belongs shame of face, as on this day—to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, those near and those far off in every land where You have scattered them, because of their unfaithfulness with which they have acted unfaithfully.

**9:8** In You, O Lord, is our righteousness, and to us belongs shame of face, and to our kings, and to our rulers, and to our fathers, who have sinned against You.

**9:9** To the Lord our God belong compassions and forgiveness, though we have rebelled,

**9:10** and we have not heeded the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in His laws which He set before our face by the hands of His servants the prophets.

**9:11** And all Israel have transgressed Your law and have turned aside so as not to hear Your voice; and there has come upon us the curse and the oath that is written in the law of Moses the servant of God, because we have sinned against Him.

**9:12** And He has established His words which He spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, by bringing upon us great calamities, such as have not been done under the whole heaven like what has been done in Jerusalem.

**9:13** As it is written in the law of Moses, all these calamities have come upon us; yet we have not implored the face of the Lord our God, so as to turn from our iniquities and to gain understanding in all Your truth.

**9:14** And the Lord kept watch and brought them upon us; for the Lord our God is righteous in all His doing which He has done, yet we have not heeded His voice.

**9:15** And now, O Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand and made Yourself a name as on this day—we have sinned, we have acted lawlessly.

**9:16** O Lord, in all Your mercy, let Your anger and Your wrath, I pray, be turned away from Your city Jerusalem, Your holy mountain; for we have sinned, and because of our iniquities and those of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people have become a reproach among all who are around us.

**9:17** And now hear, O Lord our God, the prayer of Your servant and his supplications, and cause Your face to shine upon Your desolate sanctuary, for Your own sake, O Lord.

**9:18** Incline Your ear, O my God, and hear; open Your eyes and behold our desolation, and that of Your city upon which Your name is called; for we do not cast our plea for mercy before You because of our own righteous deeds, but because of Your abundant compassions, O Lord.

**9:19** Hear, O Lord; forgive, O Lord; give heed, O Lord; do not delay, for Your own sake, O my God, for Your name is called upon Your city and upon Your people."

**9:20** And while I was still speaking and praying, and confessing my sins and the sins of my people Israel, and casting my plea for mercy before the Lord my God concerning the holy mountain,

**9:21** even while I was still speaking in prayer, behold, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, came flying and touched me about the time of the evening sacrifice.

**9:22** And he gave me understanding, and spoke with me and said, "Daniel, now I have come forth to instruct you in understanding.

**9:23** At the beginning of your supplication a word went forth, and I have come to declare it to you, for you are a man greatly treasured; therefore consider the word and understand the vision.

**9:24** Seventy weeks have been decreed upon your people and upon the holy city, to bring sin to an end, and to seal up sins, and to blot out iniquities, and to make atonement for iniquities, and to bring in everlasting righteousness, and to seal up vision and prophet, and to anoint a Holy of Holies.

**9:25** And you shall know and understand: from the going forth of a word to answer and to build Jerusalem until an Anointed One, a ruler, there shall be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; and it shall return and be built, both broad street and wall, and the times shall be emptied out.

**9:26** And after the sixty-two weeks, an anointing shall be utterly destroyed, and there is no judgment in it; and he shall destroy the city and the sanctuary together with the coming ruler; they shall be cut off in a flood; and until the end of the war that is cut short, desolations are by decree.

**9:27** And one week shall confirm a covenant for many; and in the half of the week My sacrifice and drink offering shall be taken away; and upon the temple shall be an abomination of desolations, and until the consummation of the time, a consummation shall be given upon the desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — Theodotion includes the opening confession "In You, O Lord, is our righteousness," which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:24** — Theodotion contains a sevenfold sequence of purposes for the seventy weeks, including the doublet "to blot out iniquities, and to make atonement for iniquities."
- **9:25** — Theodotion renders the decree as a word "to answer and to build Jerusalem" (rather than "to restore") and concludes with "and the times shall be emptied out" (rather than "in troubled times").
- **9:26** — Theodotion reads that an "anointing" (Greek chrisma) shall be utterly destroyed with "no judgment in it," and that the city and sanctuary shall be destroyed "together with the coming ruler."
- **9:27** — Theodotion includes the divine first-person pronoun "My" ("My sacrifice and drink offering shall be taken away") and reads "upon the temple" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "upon the wing."
