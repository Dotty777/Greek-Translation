## Text

**1:1** In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem and laid siege to it.

**1:2** And the Lord gave Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into his hand, along with a portion of the vessels of the house of God; and he carried them into the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and he brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.

**1:3** And the king spoke to Ashpenaz, his chief eunuch, to bring in some of the sons of the captivity of Israel, both from the royal seed and from the Phorthommin,

**1:4** young men in whom there was no blemish, handsome in appearance, having understanding in all wisdom, possessing knowledge, and discerning prudence, and who had strength in them to stand in the house of the king, and to teach them the writings and language of the Chaldeans.

**1:5** And the king appointed for them a daily portion, day by day, from the table of the king and from the wine that he drank, and to nourish them for three years, and after these things to stand before the king.

**1:6** And among them, from the sons of Judah, were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

**1:7** And the chief eunuch assigned names to them: to Daniel, Belteshazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach; and to Azariah, Abednego.

**1:8** And Daniel set it upon his heart that he would not defile himself with the table of the king or with the wine that he drank, and he petitioned the chief eunuch that he might not defile himself.

**1:9** And God granted Daniel mercy and compassion before the chief eunuch.

**1:10** And the chief eunuch said to Daniel, "I fear my lord the king, who has appointed your food and your drink, lest he see your countenances downcast compared to the youths of your own age, and you bring condemnation upon my head before the king."

**1:11** And Daniel said to Amelsad, whom the chief eunuch had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:

**1:12** "Test your servants now for ten days, and let them give us some of the seeds, and we will eat, and we will drink water;

**1:13** and let our appearances be observed before you, along with the appearances of the youths who eat from the table of the king; and according to what you see, do with your servants."

**1:14** And he listened to them, and tested them for ten days.

**1:15** And after the end of the ten days, their appearances were seen to be better, and they were stronger in flesh than the youths who ate from the table of the king.

**1:16** And Amelsad continually took away their meal and the wine of their drinking, and gave them seeds.

**1:17** And as for these youths, the four of them, God gave them understanding and insight in all learning and wisdom; and Daniel had understanding in every vision and in dreams.

**1:18** And after the end of the days when the king had said to bring them in, the chief eunuch brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.

**1:19** And the king spoke with them, and among all of them none were found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; and they stood before the king.

**1:20** And in every matter of wisdom and knowledge about which the king inquired of them, he found them ten times superior to all the enchanters and the magicians who were in all his kingdom.

**1:21** And Daniel remained until the first year of Cyrus the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Theodotion Greek adds "captivity" ("sons of the captivity of Israel") and leaves the Persian loanword for "nobles" untranslated as "Phorthommin."
- **1:7** — The Greek text renders Daniel's Babylonian name as "Baltasar," the same spelling Theodotion uses for King Belshazzar in chapter five.
- **1:11** — Theodotion renders the Hebrew title "ha-melzar" ("the steward" or "guardian") as the personal name "Amelsad" (so also in verse sixteen).
- **1:12** — Greek "spermata" literally means "seeds," representing plant-based foods such as grains, legumes, and vegetables (so also in verse sixteen).
