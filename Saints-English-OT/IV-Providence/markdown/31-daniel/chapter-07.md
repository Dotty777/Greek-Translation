## Text

**7:1** In the third year of Belshazzar king of the Chaldeans, Daniel saw a dream, and the visions of his head upon his bed; and he wrote down his dream.

**7:2** I, Daniel, was watching, and behold, the four winds of heaven were beating upon the great sea.

**7:3** And four great beasts came up from the sea, differing one from another.

**7:4** The first was like a lioness, and she had wings like those of an eagle. I watched until her wings were plucked off, and she was lifted up from the earth and made to stand upon the feet of a man, and the heart of a man was given to her.

**7:5** And behold, a second beast, like a bear; and it stood upon one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth. And thus they said to it, "Arise, devour much flesh."

**7:6** After this I watched, and behold, another beast, like a leopard; and it had four wings of a bird above it, and the beast had four heads, and authority was given to it.

**7:7** After this I watched, and behold, a fourth beast, dreadful and terrifying and exceedingly strong; and its teeth were of iron, devouring and crushing, and it trampled the remainder with its feet. And it was exceedingly different from all the beasts that were before it, and it had ten horns.

**7:8** I was considering its horns, and behold, another horn, a little one, came up in the midst of them, and three of the horns that were before it were uprooted from before it; and behold, eyes like the eyes of a man were in this horn, and a mouth speaking great things.

**7:9** I watched until thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days was seated. His garment was white as snow, and the hair of His head like pure wool; His throne was a flame of fire, and its wheels were burning fire.

**7:10** A river of fire flowed forth before Him; a thousand thousands ministered to Him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before Him. The court was seated, and books were opened.

**7:11** I watched then because of the sound of the great words which that horn was speaking, until the beast was slain and perished, and its body was given over to the burning of fire.

**7:12** And the dominion of the rest of the beasts was removed, yet length of life was given to them until a time and a time.

**7:13** I was watching in a vision of the night, and behold, One like a Son of Man was coming with the clouds of heaven; and He came up to the Ancient of Days, and was brought near before Him.

**7:14** And to Him were given the dominion and the honor and the kingdom, and all peoples, tribes, and tongues serve Him. His authority is an everlasting authority, which shall not pass away, and His kingdom shall not be destroyed.

**7:15** As for me, Daniel, my spirit shuddered within my frame, and the visions of my head troubled me.

**7:16** And I approached one of those who stood by, and sought the exact truth from him concerning all these things. And he told me the exact truth, and made known to me the interpretation of the words:

**7:17** "These four beasts are four kingdoms that shall arise upon the earth, which shall be taken away;

**7:18** and the saints of the Most High shall receive the kingdom and shall possess it forever and ever."

**7:19** And I sought exact knowledge concerning the fourth beast, because it was different from every beast, exceedingly dreadful, its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze, devouring and crushing, and it trampled the remainder with its feet;

**7:20** and concerning its ten horns that were on its head, and the other horn that came up and shook off some of the first ones, which had eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and whose appearance was greater than the rest.

**7:21** I watched, and that horn was making war against the saints and prevailed against them,

**7:22** until the Ancient of Days came and gave judgment to the saints of the Most High, and the time arrived, and the saints possessed the kingdom.

**7:23** And he said, "The fourth beast shall be a fourth kingdom on the earth, which shall surpass all the kingdoms, and shall devour the whole earth, and shall trample it down and cut it in pieces.

**7:24** And as for its ten horns, ten kings shall arise; and after them shall arise one who shall surpass in evils all those before him, and he shall humble three kings,

**7:25** and he shall speak words against the Most High, and shall wear out the saints of the Most High, and shall think to alter times and law; and it shall be given into his hand until a time and times and half a time.

**7:26** And the court was seated, and they shall remove the dominion, to consume and to destroy it to the end;

**7:27** and the kingdom and the authority and the greatness of the kings under the whole heaven were given to the saints of the Most High. And His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey Him." Thus far is the end of the account.

**7:28** As for me, Daniel, my thoughts greatly troubled me, and my countenance was changed; and I kept the matter in my heart.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Theodotion dates this vision to the "third year" of Belshazzar "king of the Chaldeans," whereas the Masoretic Text reads the "first year" of Belshazzar "king of Babylon."
- **7:13** — Theodotion reads "with the clouds of heaven" and "came up to the Ancient of Days," whereas the Old Greek reads "upon the clouds" and "came as the Ancient of Days."
- **7:17** — Theodotion interprets the four beasts as "four kingdoms" and adds "which shall be taken away," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "four kings" without the closing relative clause.
- **7:25** — The Greek verb dothesetai ("it shall be given") is singular, unlike the plural verb in the Aramaic ("they shall be given").
