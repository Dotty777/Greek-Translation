## Text

**6:1** And Darius the Mede received the kingdom, being sixty-two years old.

**6:2** And it pleased Darius, and he appointed over the kingdom one hundred and twenty satraps, that they should be throughout his whole kingdom,

**6:3** and over them three overseers, of whom Daniel was one, that the satraps might render an account to them, so that the king might not be troubled.

**6:4** And Daniel was above them, because an extraordinary spirit was in him; and the king appointed him over his whole kingdom.

**6:5** And the overseers and the satraps sought to find a pretext against Daniel; yet they found no pretext, nor offense, nor fault against him, because he was faithful.

**6:6** And the overseers said, "We shall not find a pretext against Daniel except in the ordinances of his God."

**6:7** Then the overseers and the satraps stood before the king and said to him, "O King Darius, live forever!

**6:8** All the commanders and satraps, consuls and district governors who are over your kingdom have taken counsel together to establish a royal statute and to confirm an edict, that whoever asks a petition of any god or man for thirty days, except of you, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions.

**6:9** Now therefore, O king, establish the edict and issue the writing, so that the decree of the Persians and Medes may not be altered."

**6:10** Then King Darius commanded that the decree be written.

**6:11** And Daniel, when he knew that the decree had been enacted, went into his house. And his windows were open in his upper chambers toward Jerusalem; and three times a day he knelt upon his knees, praying and giving thanks before his God, just as he had done previously.

**6:12** Then those men watched closely, and found Daniel making petition and supplication to his God.

**6:13** And approaching, they said to the king, "O king, did you not ordain an edict that every man who asks a petition of any god or man for thirty days, except of you, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions?" And the king said, "The word is true, and the decree of the Medes and Persians shall not pass away."

**6:14** Then they answered and said before the king, "Daniel, from the sons of the captivity of Judea, has not submitted to your decree, and three times a day he asks his petitions of his God."

**6:15** Then the king, when he heard the word, was greatly grieved over it, and strove earnestly on behalf of Daniel to deliver him.

**6:17** Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel and cast him into the den of lions; and the king said to Daniel, "Your God, whom you serve continually, He will deliver you."

**6:18** And they brought a stone and laid it upon the mouth of the den, and the king sealed it with his signet ring and with the signet ring of his nobles, so that the matter concerning Daniel might not be altered.

**6:19** And the king went away to his house and lay down without supper, and they brought no food to him, and sleep departed from him. And God shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not trouble Daniel.

**6:20** Then the king arose early in the morning at daylight, and came in haste to the den of lions.

**6:21** And as he drew near to the den, he cried out with a strong voice, "Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God, whom you serve continually, been able to deliver you from the mouth of the lions?"

**6:22** And Daniel said to the king, "O king, live forever!

**6:23** My God sent His angel and stopped the mouths of the lions, and they did not harm me, because uprightness was found in me before Him; and before you also, O king, I have committed no offense."

**6:24** Then the king was exceedingly glad for him, and commanded that Daniel be brought up out of the den. So Daniel was brought up out of the den, and no injury whatever was found on him, because he trusted in his God.

**6:25** And the king commanded, and they brought the men who had accused Daniel, and they were cast into the den of lions—they, their sons, and their wives; and they had not reached the bottom of the den before the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones to pieces.

**6:26** Then King Darius wrote to all peoples, tribes, and tongues dwelling in all the earth: "May peace be multiplied to you.

**6:27** From my presence this decree is set forth, that in every dominion of my kingdom men are to tremble and fear before the God of Daniel; for He is the living God, enduring forever, and His kingdom shall not be destroyed, and His dominion is to the end.

**6:28** He helps and rescues, and works signs and wonders in heaven and on earth, who has delivered Daniel from the hand of the lions."

**6:29** And Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**6:16** *(after `6:15`)* Then those men said to the king, "Know, O king, that it is the decree for Medes and Persians that no ordinance or statute which the king establishes may be altered."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Chapter 6 in the Greek text begins with what is numbered 5:31 in the Masoretic Text, shifting the subsequent verse numbering by one through verse 15.
- **6:15** — The Greek text of Theodotion ends verse 15 here and omits verse 16 (corresponding to Masoretic 6:14b-15), proceeding directly to verse 17.
- **6:19** — Greek edesmata ("food") differs from the obscure Aramaic term often rendered "music" or "diversions"; Theodotion also includes the closing sentence "And God shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not trouble Daniel," which is absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **6:21** — Theodotion reads "with a strong voice" and "from the mouth of the lions," where the Masoretic Text reads "with a sorrowful voice" and "from the lions."
