## Text

**8:1** In the third year of the reign of Belshazzar the king, a vision appeared to me—I, Daniel—after the one that had appeared to me at the first.

**8:2** And I was in Susa the citadel, which is in the region of Elam, and I was by the Ubal.

**8:3** And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, one ram was standing before the Ubal, and he had high horns; and the one was higher than the other, and the high one came up last.

**8:4** I saw the ram thrusting toward the sea, and the north, and the south; and none of the beasts could stand before him, nor was there any who could deliver from his hand; and he did according to his will and became great.

**8:5** And as I was considering, behold, a male goat came from the west over the face of all the earth, and was not touching the ground; and the goat had a horn between his eyes.

**8:6** And he came up to the ram that had the horns, whom I had seen standing before the Ubal, and he ran at him in the fury of his strength.

**8:7** And I saw him drawing close to the ram, and he was fiercely enraged against him; and he struck the ram and shattered both of his horns, and there was no strength in the ram to stand before him; and he cast him down to the ground and trampled upon him, and there was none to deliver the ram from his hand.

**8:8** And the male goat grew exceedingly great; and when he became strong, his great horn was shattered, and four horns came up beneath it toward the four winds of heaven.

**8:9** And out of one of them came forth one strong horn, and it grew exceedingly great toward the south and toward the host;

**8:10** it grew great even up to the host of heaven; and some of the host of heaven and of the stars fell to the earth, and they trampled them underfoot,

**8:11** even until the Chief Commander delivers the captivity; and because of him sacrifice was dashed down, and it prospered for him; and the sanctuary shall be made desolate.

**8:12** And sin was given against the sacrifice, and righteousness was cast down to the ground; and he acted and prospered.

**8:13** And I heard a holy one speaking; and one holy one said to Phelmouni who was speaking, "How long shall the vision stand, the sacrifice that was taken away, and the sin of desolation that was given, and the sanctuary and the host be trampled underfoot?"

**8:14** And he said to him, "Until evening and morning, two thousand three hundred days; and the sanctuary shall be cleansed."

**8:15** And it came to pass, when I, Daniel, saw the vision, that I was seeking understanding; and behold, there stood before me one like the appearance of a man.

**8:16** And I heard a man's voice from the midst of the Ubal, and it called and said, "Gabriel, make that man understand the vision."

**8:17** And he came and stood near where I was standing; and when he came, I was terrified and fell on my face; and he said to me, "Son of man, for the vision is yet for the end of the appointed time."

**8:18** And while he was speaking with me, I fell on my face to the ground; and he touched me and set me on my feet,

**8:19** and said, "Behold, I am making known to you the things that shall come to pass at the end of the wrath; for the vision is yet for the end of the appointed time.

**8:20** The ram which you saw, having the horns, is the king of the Persians and the Medes.

**8:21** And the male goat is the king of the Greeks; and the great horn that was between his eyes—he is the first king.

**8:22** And as for the one that was shattered, beneath which four horns stood up, four kings shall arise out of his nation, yet not in their own strength.

**8:23** And in the latter time of their kingdom, when their sins have reached their fullness, a king shall arise, shameless of countenance and understanding riddles.

**8:24** And mighty shall be his strength, and he shall bring astonishing destruction, and he shall prosper and act, and he shall destroy the mighty and a holy people.

**8:25** And the yoke of his collar shall prosper; deceit is in his hand, and in his heart he shall magnify himself, and by deceit he shall destroy many, and he shall stand upon the destruction of many, and like eggs he shall crush them by hand.

**8:26** And the vision of the evening and the morning that was spoken is truly so; and as for you, seal up the vision, for it is for many days."

**8:27** And I, Daniel, lay down and fell sick; and I arose and performed the king's works, and I marveled at the vision, and there was none who understood it.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Theodotion reads "Ubal" (Greek Oubal, transliterating the Hebrew word for "stream" or "canal") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text names the river "Ulai."
- **8:9** — Theodotion reads "one strong horn" growing toward the south and "toward the host," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "little horn" growing toward the south, the east, and the Glorious Land.
- **8:11** — Theodotion reads "even until the Chief Commander delivers the captivity; and because of him sacrifice was dashed down," a distinctive Greek rendering compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:13** — Theodotion leaves the Hebrew expression palmoni ("a certain one") untranslated as the Greek form "Phelmouni."
- **8:25** — Theodotion diverges markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text in this verse, reading "the yoke of his collar shall prosper," "he shall stand upon the destruction of many," and the active "like eggs he shall crush them by hand."
