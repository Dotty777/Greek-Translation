## Text

**11:1** Open wide your doors, O Lebanon, and let fire devour your cedars.

**11:2** Let the pine tree wail, because the cedar has fallen, for the mighty ones have been greatly brought to ruin. Wail, you oaks of Bashan, because the dense forest has been torn down.

**11:3** A sound of lamenting shepherds, because their majesty has been brought to ruin; a sound of roaring lions, because the pride of the Jordan has been brought to ruin.

**11:4** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Shepherd the sheep of the slaughter,

**11:5** whose buyers slaughtered them and felt no remorse, and those who sold them said, 'Blessed be the Lord, and we have become rich'; and their shepherds felt nothing at all for them.

**11:6** Therefore I will no longer spare the inhabitants of the land," says the Lord. "And behold, I am delivering men, each into the hands of his neighbor and into the hands of his king; and they shall strike down the land, and I will by no means deliver them out of their hand."

**11:7** And I will shepherd the sheep of the slaughter into Canaan; and I will take for myself two staffs—the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Measuring Line—and I will shepherd the sheep.

**11:8** And I will remove the three shepherds in one month, and my soul shall be weighed down against them, for their souls also roared against me.

**11:9** And I said, "I will not shepherd you. Let what is dying die, and let what is failing fail; and as for the rest, let each devour the flesh of his neighbor."

**11:10** And they shall take my beautiful staff, and I will cast it away, to annul my covenant which I made with all the peoples.

**11:11** And it shall be annulled in that day, and the Canaanites, the sheep that are kept for me, shall know that it is the word of the Lord.

**11:12** And I will say to them, "If it is good in your sight, give my wages, or else refuse." And they weighed out my wages, thirty pieces of silver.

**11:13** And the Lord said to me, "Drop them into the smelting furnace, and I will see whether it is genuine, just as it was tested on their behalf." And I took the thirty pieces of silver and cast them into the house of the Lord, into the smelting furnace.

**11:14** And I cast away the second staff, Measuring Line, to annul the possession between Judah and Israel.

**11:15** And the Lord said to me, "Take for yourself yet again the pastoral implements of an inexperienced shepherd.

**11:16** For behold, I am raising up a shepherd over the land: what is failing he will by no means visit, and what is scattered he will by no means seek, and what is broken he will by no means heal, and what is whole he will by no means guide aright; yet the flesh of the choice ones he shall devour, and their ankles he shall dislocate.

**11:17** Woe to those who shepherd vain things and have forsaken the sheep! A sword upon his arms and upon his right eye! His arm shall surely wither away, and his right eye shall be utterly blinded."

## Translator Notes

- **11:7** — The Greek reads "into Canaan" (and "the Canaanites" in verse 11), reflecting a different division of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text ("therefore, O afflicted of the flock"). The staff names translate Greek Kallos ("Beauty") and Schoinisma ("Measuring Line").
- **11:10** — The critical Greek text reads the third-person plural future "they shall take" (lempsontai) before shifting to the first person "I will cast it away."
- **11:13** — The Greek reads "smelting furnace" (choneuterion) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "potter," and uses the third-person passive "it was tested" (edokimasthe) in keeping with the assaying of silver.
- **11:14** — The Greek reads "the possession" (kataschesin) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the brotherhood."
