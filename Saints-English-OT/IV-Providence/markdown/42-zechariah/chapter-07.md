## Text

**7:1** And it came to pass in the fourth year of Darius the king, that the word of the Lord came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Chislev.

**7:2** And Sharezer and Arbeseer the king and his men sent to Bethel to appease the Lord,

**7:3** speaking to the priests who were in the house of the Lord Almighty and to the prophets, saying, "The sanctification has entered here in the fifth month, just as it has done already for many years."

**7:4** Then the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying,

**7:5** "Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests, saying, 'If you fast or mourn in the fifth or in the seventh months, and behold, for seventy years, have you indeed fasted a fast to Me?

**7:6** And if you eat or drink, is it not you who eat and drink?

**7:7** Are not these the words which the Lord spoke through the hands of the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and prosperous, along with her cities round about, and the hill country and the lowland were inhabited?'"

**7:8** Then the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying:

**7:9** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Judge righteous judgment, and show mercy and compassion, each one to his brother.

**7:10** Do not oppress the widow and the fatherless, the sojourner and the poor, and let none of you bear a grudge for the evil of his brother in your hearts.

**7:11** Yet they refused to give heed, and turned a senseless back, and made their ears heavy so as not to hear.

**7:12** And they set their heart to be disobedient, so as not to hear My law and the words which the Lord Almighty sent by His Spirit through the hands of the former prophets; and great wrath came from the Lord Almighty.

**7:13** And it shall come to pass that just as He spoke and they did not listen, so they shall cry out and I will by no means listen," says the Lord Almighty.

**7:14** "And I will cast them out among all the nations which they did not know, and the land shall be laid waste behind them, without anyone passing through or returning; for they have turned a choice land into desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Greek text reads "to Bethel" and names "Arbeseer the king," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-Melech").
- **7:3** — The Greek text reads "The sanctification has entered here" (referring to a consecrated offering or observance) rather than the Hebrew question about weeping and fasting.
- **7:11** — Greek "a senseless back" here and "disobedient" in verse 12 reflect distinctive Septuagint renderings where the Hebrew has "a stubborn shoulder" and a heart "like flint."
