## Text

**12:1** The burden of the word of the Lord concerning Israel. Says the Lord, who stretches out heaven, and lays the foundation of the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him:

**12:2** "Behold, I am making Jerusalem like shaking doorways to all the peoples round about, and in Judea there shall be a siege against Jerusalem.

**12:3** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will make Jerusalem a stone trampled upon by all the nations; everyone who tramples upon her shall surely mock, and all the nations of the earth shall be gathered together against her.

**12:4** In that day," says the Lord Almighty, "I will strike every horse with bewilderment and its rider with madness; but upon the house of Judah I will open wide My eyes, and all the horses of the peoples I will strike with blindness.

**12:5** And the commanders of thousands of Judah shall say in their hearts, 'We shall find for ourselves the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the Lord Almighty their God.'

**12:6** In that day I will make the commanders of thousands of Judah like a firebrand among wood, and like a torch of fire among stubble; and they shall devour on the right hand and on the left all the peoples round about, and Jerusalem shall dwell yet again by herself in Jerusalem.

**12:7** And the Lord shall save the tents of Judah as from the beginning, so that the boasting of the house of David and the arrogance of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not be magnified above Judah.

**12:8** And it shall come to pass in that day that the Lord will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and the one who is weak among them in that day shall be like David, and the house of David like the house of God, like the angel of the Lord before them.

**12:9** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will seek to remove all the nations that come against Jerusalem;

**12:10** and I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and compassion; and they shall look upon Me because they danced in mockery, and they shall mourn for Him with mourning as for a beloved one, and they shall grieve with grief as over the firstborn.

**12:11** In that day the mourning in Jerusalem shall be magnified like the mourning for a pomegranate grove being cut down in a plain;

**12:12** and the land shall mourn, tribe by tribe: the tribe of the house of David by itself, and their wives by themselves; the tribe of the house of Nathan by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**12:13** the tribe of the house of Levi by itself, and their wives by themselves; the tribe of Simeon by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**12:14** all the remaining tribes, every tribe by itself, and their wives by themselves."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek renders the image as "shaking doorways" (prothyra saleuomena), taking the underlying Hebrew word in the sense of "threshold" rather than "cup" as in the Masoretic Text.
- **12:3** — The Greek reads "a stone trampled upon" and "shall surely mock" (empaizon empaixetai), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "heavy stone" and "will surely cut themselves."
- **12:10** — The Greek reads "a spirit of grace and compassion" and "because they danced in mockery" (katorchesanto, reflecting a transposition of Hebrew consonants), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and John 19:37) reads "whom they pierced."
- **12:11** — The Greek reads "for a pomegranate grove being cut down in a plain," translating the place names Hadadrimmon and Megiddo of the Hebrew Masoretic Text as common nouns and a participle.
- **12:13** — The Greek reads "the tribe of Simeon" (tou Symeon) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the family of the Shimeites."
