## Text

**2:1** And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, four horns.

**2:2** And I said to the angel who spoke within me, "What are these, my lord?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that scattered Judah and Israel and Jerusalem."

**2:3** And the Lord showed me four craftsmen.

**2:4** And I said, "What are these coming to do?" And he said, "These are the horns that scattered Judah and shattered Israel, and none of them lifted up his head; and these have come forth to sharpen them into their hands—the four horns, the nations that lift up a horn against the land of the Lord to scatter it."

**2:5** And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, a man, and in his hand was a measuring line.

**2:6** And I said to him, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see how great her width is and how great her length."

**2:7** And behold, the angel who spoke within me stood still, and another angel was coming forth to meet him,

**2:8** and he said to him, "Run and speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem shall be fruitfully inhabited because of the multitude of men and livestock in her midst.

**2:9** And I will be to her, says the Lord, a wall of fire all around, and I will be for glory in her midst.'"

**2:10** "Ah, ah! Flee from the land of the north, says the Lord; for from the four winds of heaven I will gather you, says the Lord.

**2:11** Escape to Zion, you who dwell in the daughter of Babylon!"

**2:12** For thus says the Lord Almighty: "After glory He has sent Me against the nations that plundered you; for he who touches you is as one who touches the apple of His eye.

**2:13** For behold, I bring My hand upon them, and they shall become plunder for those who serve them; and you shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent Me."

**2:14** "Delight and rejoice, O daughter of Zion; for behold, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst, says the Lord.

**2:15** And many nations shall flee for refuge to the Lord in that day, and they shall be to Him for a people, and they shall dwell in your midst; and you shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent Me to you."

**2:16** And the Lord shall inherit Judah as His portion upon the holy land, and He shall yet choose Jerusalem.

**2:17** Let all flesh be reverent before the face of the Lord, for He has risen up from His holy clouds.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Chapter 2 in the Septuagint begins at Masoretic 1:18; verses 2:5-17 correspond to Masoretic 2:1-13.
- **2:4** — The Septuagint renders the craftsmen's purpose literally as "to sharpen them into their hands," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic "to terrify them, to cast down."
- **2:8** — The Greek adverb katakarpos ("fruitfully") renders a Hebrew term that in the Masoretic Text means "as unwalled villages."
- **2:10** — The Septuagint reads the future promise "I will gather you" from the four winds of heaven, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "I have spread you abroad."
- **2:17** — The Greek concludes with "from His holy clouds" (ek nephelon hagion autou), differing from the Masoretic Text's "from His holy dwelling."
