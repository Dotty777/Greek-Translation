## Text

**9:1** The burden of the word of the Lord in the land of Sedrach, and Damascus is His sacrifice; for the Lord watches over men and all the tribes of Israel,

**9:2** and in Hamath within her borders; Tyre and Sidon, because they were exceedingly wise.

**9:3** And Tyre built strongholds for herself, and treasured up silver like dust, and gold like the mire of the streets.

**9:4** And therefore the Lord shall take possession of them, and He shall strike her power into the sea, and she herself shall be consumed with fire.

**9:5** Ashkelon shall see and be afraid, and Gaza also, and she shall be in great anguish, and Ekron, because she has been put to shame over her transgression; and a king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall by no means be inhabited.

**9:6** And foreigners shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will pull down the arrogance of the Philistines,

**9:7** and I will take away their blood from their mouth, and their abominations from between their teeth; and these also shall be left as a remnant for our God, and they shall be like a commander of a thousand in Judah, and Ekron like the Jebusite.

**9:8** And I will set up a bulwark for My house, so that none may pass through nor return, and no one driving them out shall come upon them anymore; for now I have seen with My own eyes.

**9:9** Rejoice exceedingly, O daughter of Zion; proclaim aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem! Behold, your King comes to you; righteous and saving is He, gentle and mounted upon a beast of burden and a young colt.

**9:10** And He shall utterly destroy chariots from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war shall be utterly destroyed, and there shall be abundance and peace from the nations; and He shall rule over the waters to the sea, and over the rivers to the outlets of the earth.

**9:11** And you, by the blood of your covenant, have sent forth your prisoners out of a pit that has no water.

**9:12** You shall dwell in strongholds, O prisoners of the congregation, and in place of one day of your sojourning I will repay you double.

**9:13** For I have bent you, O Judah, as a bow for Myself; I have filled Ephraim, and I will stir up your children, O Zion, against the children of the Greeks, and I will handle you like the sword of a warrior.

**9:14** And the Lord shall be over them, and an arrow shall go forth like lightning; and the Lord Almighty shall sound with a trumpet, and shall march in the tumult of His threat.

**9:15** The Lord Almighty shall shield them; and they shall consume them, and bury them with slingstones, and drink them down like wine, and fill the bowls like an altar.

**9:16** And the Lord their God shall save them in that day, His people as sheep; for holy stones are rolled upon His land.

**9:17** For whatever good belongs to Him, and whatever beauty belongs to Him—grain for the young men, and fragrant wine for the maidens.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek preserves the distinct place name "Sedrach" (Hebrew "Hadrach") and renders "Damascus is His sacrifice," reflecting a reading of "offering" rather than the Hebrew "resting place."
- **9:9** — The Septuagint reads "proclaim aloud" (rather than "shout"), uses the active participle "saving," and describes the mount simply as "a beast of burden and a young colt."
- **9:10** — The Greek uses the third-person "He shall utterly destroy" and reads "abundance and peace from the nations," differing from the Hebrew ("I will cut off" and "he shall speak peace to the nations").
- **9:12** — The Greek reads "You shall dwell in strongholds, O prisoners of the congregation, and in place of one day of your sojourning," diverging from the Hebrew ("Return to the stronghold, O prisoners of hope; even today I declare").
- **9:16** — The Septuagint renders "holy stones are rolled upon His land," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of "jewels of a crown" gleaming over His land.
