## Text

**6:1** And I turned and lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, four chariots coming forth from the midst of two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**6:2** In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot were black horses,

**6:3** and in the third chariot were white horses, and in the fourth chariot were dappled, ash-gray horses.

**6:4** And I answered and said to the angel who spoke in me, "What are these things, lord?"

**6:5** And the angel who spoke in me answered and said, "These are the four winds of heaven; they go forth to stand before the Lord of all the earth."

**6:6** As for the one in which the black horses were, they went forth to the land of the north, and the white ones went forth behind them, and the dappled ones went forth to the land of the south,

**6:7** and the ash-gray ones went forth and looked to go and patrol the earth. And he said, "Go and patrol the earth." And they patrolled the earth.

**6:8** And they cried out, and he spoke to me, saying, "Behold, those who go forth to the land of the north have indeed quieted My wrath in the land of the north."

**6:9** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:10** "Take the things from the captivity, from the rulers and from its worthy men and from those who have known it, and you shall enter on that day into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, who has come from Babylon;

**6:11** and you shall take silver and gold and make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest;

**6:12** and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: Behold, a Man—Dayspring is His name, and from beneath Him He shall spring up, and He shall build the house of the Lord;

**6:13** and He shall receive excellence, and shall sit and rule upon His throne; and the priest shall be at His right hand, and a counsel of peace shall be between both of them.'

**6:14** And the crown shall be for those who endure, and for its worthy men, and for those who have known it, and for the favor of the son of Zephaniah, and for a psalm in the house of the Lord.

**6:15** And those who are far from them shall come and build in the house of the Lord, and you shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you. And this shall come to pass, if you diligently listen to the voice of the Lord your God."

## Translator Notes

- **6:8** — The Septuagint opens with the plural 'they cried out' and reads 'My wrath' (ton thumon mou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'My spirit.'
- **6:10** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew personal names Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah as descriptive terms ('the rulers,' 'its worthy men,' and 'those who have known it'), a pattern repeated in verse 14.
- **6:12** — The Messianic title 'Dayspring' (Greek Anatole, meaning 'Rising' or 'Shoot,' paired with the verb anatelei, 'shall spring up') translates Hebrew Tzemach ('Branch') and is echoed in Luke 1:78.
- **6:13** — The Septuagint reads 'the priest shall be at His right hand,' placing a distinct priest beside the enthroned ruler, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'he shall be a priest on his throne.'
- **6:14** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew personal name Hen as 'favor' (charita) and reads 'for a psalm' (eis psalmon) where the Masoretic Text reads 'for a memorial.'
