## Text

**10:1** Ask rain from the Lord in season, both early and late; the Lord has made manifestations, and He will give them winter rain, to each one vegetation in the field.

**10:2** For those who utter oracles spoke troubles, and the diviners, false visions; they spoke false dreams and comforted in vain. Therefore they withered away like sheep and were afflicted, because there was no healing.

**10:3** "Against the shepherds My wrath was provoked, and I will bring visitation upon the lambs; and the Lord God Almighty will visit His flock, the house of Judah, and will array them as His majestic horse in battle.

**10:4** And from him He looked, and from him He arrayed, and from him came a bow in wrath; from him shall go forth everyone who drives out together.

**10:5** And they shall be like warriors trampling mire in the streets in battle, and they shall draw up in battle array, because the Lord is with them; and the riders on horses shall be put to shame.

**10:6** And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will settle them, because I have loved them; and they shall be as though I had not turned away from them, for I am the Lord their God, and I will hear them.

**10:7** And they shall be like the warriors of Ephraim, and their heart shall rejoice as with wine; and their children shall see and be glad, and their heart shall rejoice in the Lord.

**10:8** I will signal to them and receive them, for I will redeem them; and they shall be multiplied just as they were many.

**10:9** And I will sow them among the peoples, and those who are far off shall remember Me; they shall rear their children and return.

**10:10** And I will bring them back from the land of Egypt, and from the Assyrians I will receive them; and into Gilead and into Lebanon I will bring them, and not even one of them shall be left behind.

**10:11** And they shall pass through a narrow sea, and they shall strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the rivers shall be dried up; and all the pride of the Assyrians shall be taken away, and the scepter of Egypt shall be removed.

**10:12** And I will strengthen them in the Lord their God, and in His name they shall boast," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek text reads "withered away" and "no healing," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "wandered" and "no shepherd."
- **10:3** — The Greek reads "the lambs" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the male goats" (leaders).
- **10:4** — The Greek renders the opening clauses with verbs ("from him He looked, and from him He arrayed") and "a bow in wrath," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("cornerstone," "tent peg," "battle bow").
- **10:10** — The Greek concludes "and not even one of them shall be left behind," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "until no room is found for them."
