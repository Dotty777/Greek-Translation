## Text

**8:1** And the word of the Lord Almighty came, saying:

**8:2** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "I have been jealous for Jerusalem and Zion with great jealousy, and with great wrath I have been jealous for her."

**8:3** Thus says the Lord: "I will return to Zion, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and Jerusalem shall be called the True City, and the mountain of the Lord Almighty, the Holy Mountain."

**8:4** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Elderly men and elderly women shall yet sit in the streets of Jerusalem, each one having his staff in his hand because of the multitude of days.

**8:5** And the streets of the city shall be filled with boys and girls playing in her streets."

**8:6** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "If it shall be impossible in the sight of the remnant of this people in those days, shall it also be impossible in My sight?" says the Lord Almighty.

**8:7** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Behold, I am saving My people from the land of the east and from the land of the west,

**8:8** and I will bring them in, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and they shall be to Me a people, and I will be to them God, in truth and in righteousness."

**8:9** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Let your hands be strong, you who hear in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets, from the day that the house of the Lord Almighty was founded, and the temple from the time it was built.

**8:10** For before those days the wages of men shall be of no profit, and there shall be no wages for beasts, and for him who goes out and him who comes in there shall be no peace from affliction; and I will send forth all men, each one against his neighbor.

**8:11** Yet now I am not dealing with the remnant of this people as in the former days," says the Lord Almighty,

**8:12** "but rather I will show peace: the vine shall yield its fruit, and the earth shall yield its produce, and the heaven shall give its dew; and I will give all these things as an inheritance to the remnant of this My people.

**8:13** And it shall come to pass that just as you were under a curse among the nations, O house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will deliver you thoroughly, and you shall be in blessing. Take courage, and be strong in your hands."

**8:14** For thus says the Lord Almighty: "Just as I purposed to bring harm upon you when your fathers provoked Me to anger," says the Lord Almighty, "and I did not relent,

**8:15** so I have resolved and purposed in these days to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Take courage!

**8:16** These are the things which you shall do: speak truth each one to his neighbor; judge truth and peaceful judgment in your gates;

**8:17** and let none of you devise evil against his neighbor in your hearts, and do not love a false oath; for all these things I have hated," says the Lord Almighty.

**8:18** And the word of the Lord Almighty came to me.

**8:19** The Lord Almighty says: "The fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth shall become to the house of Judah joy and gladness and good feasts; and you shall rejoice, and love truth and peace."

**8:20** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Many peoples and the inhabitants of many cities shall yet come,

**8:21** and the inhabitants of five cities shall assemble into one city, saying, 'Let us go to implore the face of the Lord and to seek the face of the Lord Almighty; I myself will go as well.'

**8:22** And many peoples and many nations shall come to seek the face of the Lord Almighty in Jerusalem, and to appease the face of the Lord."

**8:23** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "In those days, if ten men from all the languages of the nations take hold, they shall indeed take hold of the hem of a Jewish man, saying, 'We will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — The Greek verb literally means "shall it be impossible," rendering the Hebrew sense of being too difficult or wonderful in terms of impossibility.
- **8:8** — The Greek text has the first-person singular "I will dwell" (referring to the Lord), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the third-person plural "they shall dwell."
- **8:12** — The Greek text reads "but rather I will show peace," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("for the seed shall be peaceful").
- **8:21** — The Septuagint specifies "the inhabitants of five cities" assembling into "one city," whereas the Hebrew reads "the inhabitants of one city shall go to another."
