## Text

**14:1** Behold, days of the Lord are coming, and your spoils shall be divided within you.

**14:2** And I will gather together all nations against Jerusalem for war, and the city shall be taken, and the houses shall be plundered, and the women shall be defiled, and half of the city shall go forth into captivity, but the remnant of My people shall by no means be utterly destroyed from the city.

**14:3** And the Lord shall go forth and do battle against those nations, as on the day of His battle in a day of war.

**14:4** And His feet shall stand in that day upon the Mount of Olives, which is opposite Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives shall be split, half of it toward the east and the sea—an exceedingly great chasm; and half of the mountain shall lean toward the north, and half of it toward the south.

**14:5** And the valley of My mountains shall be blocked up, and the valley of the mountains shall be joined as far as Iasod, and it shall be blocked up just as it was blocked up in the days of the earthquake, in the days of Uzziah king of Judah; and the Lord my God shall come, and all the holy ones with Him.

**14:6** And it shall be in that day that there shall be no light, and there shall be both cold and frost;

**14:7** it shall be for one day, and that day is known to the Lord—neither day nor night—and toward evening there shall be light.

**14:8** And in that day living water shall go forth from Jerusalem, half of it to the first sea, and half of it to the last sea; and in summer and in spring it shall be so.

**14:9** And the Lord shall be King over all the earth; in that day the Lord shall be one, and His name one,

**14:10** encompassing all the earth and the wilderness from Geba to Rimmon to the south of Jerusalem. But Ramah shall remain in its place, from the Gate of Benjamin to the place of the First Gate, to the Gate of the Corners, and to the Tower of Hanamel, to the winepress vats of the king.

**14:11** They shall dwell in it, and there shall no longer be any curse, and Jerusalem shall dwell in confidence.

**14:12** And this shall be the calamity with which the Lord shall strike all the peoples, as many as marched against Jerusalem: their flesh shall melt away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall flow out of their sockets, and their tongue shall melt away in their mouth.

**14:13** And it shall be in that day that a great panic from the Lord shall be upon them; and each one shall seize the hand of his neighbor, and his hand shall be interlocked with the hand of his neighbor.

**14:14** And Judah also shall do battle in Jerusalem, and shall gather the wealth of all the peoples round about—gold and silver and apparel in exceedingly great abundance.

**14:15** And this shall be the calamity of the horses, and of the mules, and of the camels, and of the donkeys, and of all the beasts that are in those camps, according to this calamity.

**14:16** And it shall come to pass that as many as are left of all the nations that came against Jerusalem shall go up year by year to worship the King, the Lord Almighty, and to keep the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:17** And it shall come to pass that as many as do not go up from all the tribes of the earth to Jerusalem to worship the King, the Lord Almighty—these also shall be added to those.

**14:18** And if the tribe of Egypt does not go up nor come, upon them also shall be the calamity with which the Lord shall strike all the nations, as many as do not go up to keep the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:19** This shall be the sin of Egypt and the sin of all the nations, as many as do not go up to keep the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:20** In that day that which is upon the bridle of the horse shall be holy to the Lord Almighty; and the cauldrons in the house of the Lord shall be like bowls before the altar.

**14:21** And every cauldron in Jerusalem and in Judah shall be holy to the Lord Almighty; and all those who sacrifice shall come and take of them and boil in them; and there shall no longer be a Canaanite in the house of the Lord Almighty in that day.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The Greek reads "shall be blocked up" twice where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "flee," and preserves the distinct place name Iasod where the Hebrew reads Azel.
- **14:6** — The Septuagint reads "cold and frost" (psyche kai pagos) continuing into "for one day" (mian hemeran) in verse 7, diverging from the difficult phrasing of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **14:8** — The Greek reads "in summer and in spring" (en eari) rather than "in summer and in winter," and translates the eastern and western seas literally as "the first sea" and "the last sea."
- **14:10** — The Septuagint reads "encompassing all the earth and the wilderness," takes Ramah as a proper name rather than a verb ("shall be lifted up"), and reads Hanamel (Anameel) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Hananel.
- **14:17** — The Greek concludes with "these also shall be added to those" (that is, counted among the afflicted nations), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "upon them there shall be no rain."
