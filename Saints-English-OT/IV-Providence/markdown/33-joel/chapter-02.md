## Text

**2:1** Sound the trumpet in Zion, proclaim on My holy mountain, and let all who dwell in the land be confounded, for the day of the Lord is present, for it is near.

**2:2** A day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and thick mist! Like the dawn, a great and mighty people shall be poured out upon the mountains; none like it has existed from eternity, nor after it shall there be any again, even to the years of generations of generations.

**2:3** Before it is a consuming fire, and behind it a blazing flame; like a paradise of delight is the land before its face, and behind it a plain of desolation, and there shall be no one who escapes it.

**2:4** Like the appearance of horses is their appearance, and like horsemen, so shall they pursue.

**2:5** Like the sound of chariots they shall leap upon the tops of the mountains, and like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, and like a great and mighty people drawn up in array for battle.

**2:6** Before its face peoples shall be crushed; every face is like the scorching of a pot.

**2:7** Like warriors they shall run, and like men of war they shall climb upon the walls; each one shall march in his own way, and they shall by no means turn aside from their paths.

**2:8** Each one shall not keep away from his brother; weighed down in their armor they shall march, and upon their missiles they shall fall, yet they shall by no means be brought to an end.

**2:9** They shall seize the city, and upon the walls they shall run, and upon the houses they shall climb, and through the windows they shall enter like thieves.

**2:10** Before its face the earth shall be confounded and the heaven shall be shaken; the sun and the moon shall grow dark, and the stars shall withdraw their brightness.

**2:11** And the Lord shall utter His voice before the face of His host, for exceedingly great is His camp, for mighty are the works of His words; for great is the day of the Lord, great and exceedingly manifest, and who shall be sufficient for it?

**2:12** And now, says the Lord your God, "Turn back to Me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning."

**2:13** And rend your hearts and not your garments, and turn back to the Lord your God, for He is merciful and compassionate, longsuffering and rich in mercy, and He relents over calamities.

**2:14** Who knows whether He will turn and relent, and leave behind Him a blessing—a sacrifice and a drink offering for the Lord our God?

**2:15** Sound the trumpet in Zion, sanctify a fast, proclaim a solemn service.

**2:16** Gather the people, sanctify the assembly, select the elders, gather the infants nursing at the breasts; let the bridegroom go forth from his chamber and the bride from her bridal canopy.

**2:17** In the midst of the base of the altar the priests who minister to the Lord shall weep and say, "Spare Your people, O Lord, and do not give Your inheritance to reproach, that the nations should rule over them; lest they say among the nations, 'Where is their God?'"

**2:18** And the Lord was zealous for His land, and He spared His people.

**2:19** And the Lord answered and said to His people, "Behold, I am sending forth to you the grain and the wine and the oil, and you shall be filled with them; and I will no longer give you over to reproach among the nations.

**2:20** And the one from the north I will drive away from you, and I will thrust him out into a waterless land, and I will destroy his face into the first sea and his rear into the last sea; and his putrefaction shall ascend, and his stench shall ascend, because he has magnified his deeds."

**2:21** Take courage, O land, rejoice and be glad, for the Lord has done great things.

**2:22** Take courage, you beasts of the plain, for the plains of the wilderness have sprouted; for the tree has borne its fruit, and the fig tree and the vine have yielded their strength.

**2:23** And you children of Zion, rejoice and be glad in the Lord your God, for He has given you provisions for righteousness, and He will rain down for you the early rain and the late rain, just as before.

**2:24** And the threshing floors shall be filled with grain, and the vats shall overflow with wine and oil.

**2:25** And He will repay you for the years that the locust and the hopping locust and the blight and the caterpillar devoured—My great host, which I sent against you.

**2:26** And you shall eat to the full and be satisfied, and you shall praise the name of the Lord your God for the wondrous things He has done with you; and My people shall by no means be put to shame forever.

**2:27** And you shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and there is none else besides Me; and My people shall never again be put to shame forever.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The Greek reads "like the scorching of a pot" (πρόσκαυμα χύτρας), interpreting a rare Hebrew expression for facial pallor as pot-blackening.
- **2:17** — The Greek locates the weeping priests "in the midst of the base of the altar" (τῆς κρηπῖδος τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου) rather than "between the porch and the altar" as in Hebrew.
- **2:23** — The Septuagint reads "provisions for righteousness" (τὰ βρώματα εἰς δικαιοσύνην), where the Hebrew term is commonly rendered "early rain" or "teacher."
- **2:25** — The Greek shifts within the verse from third-person "He will repay" (ἀνταποδώσει) to first-person "My great host, which I sent."
