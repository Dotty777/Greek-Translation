# Joel 3 — Saints Traditional English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**3:1** And it shall come to pass after these things that I will pour out of My Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your old men shall dream dreams, and your young men shall see visions.

**3:2** And upon My menservants and upon the maidservants in those days I will pour out of My Spirit.

**3:3** And I will give wonders in heaven, and upon the earth blood and fire and vapor of smoke.

**3:4** The sun shall be turned into darkness and the moon into blood, before the coming of the great and manifest day of the Lord.

**3:5** And it shall come to pass that everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord shall be saved; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be one who escapes, just as the Lord has spoken, and those receiving good tidings, whom the Lord has called.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — In Septuagint versification, chapter 3 comprises five verses corresponding to 2:28–32 in Hebrew and most English Bibles; the Greek also specifies "of My Spirit" (ἀπὸ τοῦ πνεύματός μου), as quoted in Acts 2:17.
- **3:2** — The Greek reads "My menservants" with the possessive pronoun but "the maidservants" without it.
- **3:5** — The Greek renders the final clause as "those receiving good tidings" (εὐαγγελιζόμενοι), where the Hebrew reads "among the survivors."
