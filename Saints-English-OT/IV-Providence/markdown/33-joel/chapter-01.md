## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Joel, the son of Bethuel.

**1:2** Hear these things, you elders, and give ear, all you who dwell in the land. Have such things happened in our days, or in the days of your fathers?

**1:3** Tell your children concerning them, and let your children tell their children, and their children to another generation.

**1:4** What the caterpillar left, the locust devoured; and what the locust left, the hopping locust devoured; and what the hopping locust left, the blight devoured.

**1:5** Awake from their wine, you drunkards, and weep; lament, all you who drink wine to drunkenness, for gladness and joy have been taken away from your mouth.

**1:6** For a nation has come up against My land, strong and without number; its teeth are the teeth of a lion, and its jaw teeth are those of a young lion.

**1:7** It has turned My vine to desolation, and My fig trees to splintered ruin; searching, it thoroughly searched it out and cast it down; it has made its branches white.

**1:8** Lament to Me more than a bride girded with sackcloth for the husband of her virginity.

**1:9** Sacrifice and drink offering have been taken away from the house of the Lord; mourn, you priests who minister at the altar.

**1:10** For the plains are laid waste; let the land mourn, for the grain is laid waste, the wine is dried up, the oil has diminished.

**1:11** The farmers are dried up; lament, you estates, over wheat and barley, because the harvest has perished from the field.

**1:12** The vine has dried up, and the fig trees have diminished; pomegranate and palm and apple tree, and all the trees of the field, have dried up, because the sons of men have put joy to shame.

**1:13** Gird yourselves and mourn, you priests; lament, you who minister at the altar; go in, sleep in sackcloth, you who minister to God, for sacrifice and drink offering have been withheld from the house of your God.

**1:14** Sanctify a fast, proclaim a solemn service, gather the elders, all who dwell in the land, to the house of your God, and cry out earnestly to the Lord:

**1:15** "Alas, alas, alas for the day! For the day of the Lord is near, and as devastation from devastation it shall come."

**1:16** Before your eyes provisions have been utterly destroyed; from the house of your God, gladness and joy.

**1:17** Heifers have leaped at their mangers, storehouses have been laid waste, winepresses have been torn down, because the grain has dried up.

**1:18** What shall we lay up for ourselves? The herds of cattle have wept, because there was no pasture for them, and the flocks of sheep have perished.

**1:19** To You, O Lord, I will cry out, for fire has consumed the fair places of the wilderness, and flame has set ablaze all the trees of the field.

**1:20** Even the beasts of the plain have looked up to You, for the channels of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the fair places of the wilderness.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek reads "Bathuel" (Βαθουήλ), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Pethuel."
- **1:2** — The Greek reads "in our days" (first-person plural) rather than the Hebrew "in your days."
- **1:4** — The Septuagint lists four distinct plagues—caterpillar, locust, hopping locust, and plant blight—rather than four locust terms as in Hebrew.
- **1:15** — The Greek renders the Hebrew "like destruction from the Almighty" as "as devastation from devastation" (ταλαιπωρία ἐκ ταλαιπωρίας) and opens with a threefold "Alas."
- **1:17** — The Septuagint depicts heifers leaping at their mangers, differing markedly from the Hebrew image of seeds shriveling under clods.
