## Text

**1:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,

**1:2** "Arise and go to Nineveh, the great city, and preach in it, for the outcry of its wickedness has come up to Me."

**1:3** And Jonah arose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord, and he went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish, and paid his fare, and went aboard it to sail with them to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord.

**1:4** And the Lord raised up a wind upon the sea, and a great tempest arose in the sea, and the ship was in danger of being shattered.

**1:5** And the mariners were afraid, and cried out, each man to his god; and they cast the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to be lightened of them. But Jonah had gone down into the hold of the ship, and was sleeping and snoring.

**1:6** And the captain came to him and said to him, "Why are you snoring? Arise and call upon your god, so that God may rescue us and we may not perish."

**1:7** And each man said to his companion, "Come, let us cast lots and learn on whose account this calamity is among us." So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

**1:8** And they said to him, "Tell us, what is your occupation, and from where do you come, and from what country and from what people are you?"

**1:9** And he said to them, "I am a servant of the Lord, and I worship the Lord, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land."

**1:10** Then the men feared with a great fear and said to him, "What is this that you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the Lord, because he had told them.

**1:11** And they said to him, "What shall we do to you, that the sea may subside from us?" For the sea kept advancing and raising up a greater tempest.

**1:12** And Jonah said to them, "Lift me up and cast me into the sea, and the sea will subside from you; for I know that because of me this great tempest is upon you."

**1:13** Yet the men strove hard to return to the land, and they could not, for the sea kept advancing and rising up even more against them.

**1:14** Therefore they cried out to the Lord and said, "By no means, O Lord! Let us not perish on account of this man's life, and do not lay righteous blood upon us; for You, O Lord, have done as You willed."

**1:15** Then they took Jonah and cast him out into the sea, and the sea stood still from its surging.

**1:16** And the men feared the Lord with a great fear, and offered sacrifices to the Lord and vowed vows.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Greek reads "preach in it" and "the outcry of its wickedness," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "cry out against it" and omits "the outcry of."
- **1:9** — Greek reads "a servant of the Lord" (doulos Kyriou), contrasting with "a Hebrew" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:14** — Greek reads "righteous blood" (haima dikaion), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "innocent blood."
