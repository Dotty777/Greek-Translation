## Text

**7:1** When I heal Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim shall be revealed, and the wickedness of Samaria, because they have practiced falsehood; and a thief shall come in to him, a robber stripping along his road,

**7:2** so that they may sing together like those who sing in their heart. I have remembered all their wicked deeds; now their own counsels have surrounded them; they have come before My face.

**7:3** With their wicked deeds they have gladdened kings, and princes with their falsehoods.

**7:4** All of them are adulterers, like an oven burning for baking with scorching heat from the flame, from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

**7:5** In the days of your kings, the princes began to rage from wine; he stretched out his hand with pestilent men.

**7:6** For their hearts were kindled like an oven; while they were breaking down, all the night Ephraim was filled with sleep; morning came, and it was kindled like a brightness of fire.

**7:7** All of them grew hot like an oven, and they devoured their judges; all their kings have fallen; there was not one among them who called upon Me.

**7:8** Ephraim mingled himself among his peoples; Ephraim became a cake baked in the ashes, not turned.

**7:9** Strangers have devoured his strength, yet he did not know it; and gray hairs have sprouted upon him, and he did not know it.

**7:10** And the arrogance of Israel shall be humbled to his face, yet they did not return to the Lord their God, nor did they seek Him diligently in all these things.

**7:11** And Ephraim was like a senseless dove, having no heart; he called upon Egypt, and they went to the Assyrians.

**7:12** Wherever they go, I will cast My net upon them; like the birds of heaven I will bring them down; I will chasten them at the report of their affliction.

**7:13** Woe to them, because they have leaped away from Me! Wretched are they, because they have acted ungodly toward Me! Though I redeemed them, yet they spoke falsehoods against Me.

**7:14** And their hearts did not cry out to Me; rather, they wailed upon their beds; for grain and wine they slashed themselves.

**7:15** They were instructed by Me, and I strengthened their arms, yet against Me they devised evil things.

**7:16** They turned away to nothing; they became like a bent bow; their princes shall fall by the sword because of the unruliness of their tongue; this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Septuagint reads "so that they may sing together like those who sing in their heart," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("they do not consider in their hearts").
- **7:6** — The Greek reads "Ephraim was filled with sleep," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants behind the Masoretic Text's "their baker sleeps."
- **7:14** — The Greek verb "slashed themselves" reflects an underlying reading referring to self-laceration in ritual mourning, distinct from the Masoretic Text's "assemble themselves."
