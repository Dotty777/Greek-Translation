## Text

**12:1** Ephraim has surrounded Me with falsehood, and the house of Israel and Judah with ungodliness. Now God has known them, and they shall be called a holy people of God.

**12:2** But Ephraim is an evil spirit; he pursued the scorching wind all the day long. He multiplied empty and vain things, and made a covenant with the Assyrians, and carried oil in trade to Egypt.

**12:3** And the Lord has a judgment against Judah, to punish Jacob according to his ways; and according to his practices He will repay him.

**12:4** In the womb he supplanted his brother, and in his labors he prevailed with God;

**12:5** indeed, he prevailed with an angel and proved strong. They wept and pleaded with Me; in the house of On they found Me, and there it was spoken to them.

**12:6** And the Lord God Almighty shall be his memorial.

**12:7** Therefore you shall return in your God; observe mercy and judgment, and draw near to your God continually.

**12:8** As for Canaan, in his hand is a balance of unrighteousness; he has loved to oppress.

**12:9** And Ephraim said, "Indeed, I have become rich; I have found relief for myself." All his labors shall not be found for him because of the iniquities which he has sinned.

**12:10** Yet I, the Lord your God, brought you up out of the land of Egypt; I will again cause you to dwell in tents, as in the days of the feast.

**12:11** And I will speak to the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and by the hands of the prophets I was represented.

**12:12** If Gilead does not exist, then the rulers sacrificing in Gilead were false, and their altars are like tortoises upon the barren ground of a field.

**12:13** And Jacob withdrew to the plain of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept watch.

**12:14** And by a prophet the Lord brought Israel up out of the land of Egypt, and by a prophet he was preserved.

**12:15** Ephraim provoked fury and stirred up wrath; and his blood shall be poured out upon him, and the Lord will repay his reproach to him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek reads "an evil spirit," interpreting the Hebrew consonants for "feeds on wind" as "evil spirit."
- **12:5** — The Greek reads "in the house of On" (where the Hebrew has "Bethel") and shifts to third-person plural ("they wept... they found Me") with God speaking in the first person.
- **12:12** — The Greek reads "rulers sacrificing in Gilead" (vs. Hebrew "in Gilgal they sacrifice bulls") and compares their altars to "tortoises" (chelonai, vs. Hebrew "stone heaps").
