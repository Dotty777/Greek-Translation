## Text

**10:1** Israel is a vine rich in branches; its fruit is abundant. According to the multitude of its fruits he multiplied altars; according to the good things of his land he built pillars.

**10:2** They have divided their hearts; now they shall be destroyed. He Himself will tear down their altars; their pillars shall come to ruin.

**10:3** For now they shall say, "We have no king, because we did not fear the Lord. And what will the king do for us,

**10:4** speaking words of false pretexts?" He will make a covenant; judgment shall spring up like wild grass upon the barren ground of a field.

**10:5** The inhabitants of Samaria shall sojourn for the calf of the house of On, because its people mourned over it; and just as they provoked it, they shall rejoice over its glory, because it has been carried away into exile from it.

**10:6** And having bound it, they carried it away to the Assyrians as gifts to King Jarim; Ephraim shall receive it as a gift, and Israel shall be put to shame in his counsel.

**10:7** Samaria has cast off her king like a dry twig upon the face of the water.

**10:8** And the altars of On, the sins of Israel, shall be taken away; thorns and thistles shall grow up upon their altars; and they shall say to the mountains, "Cover us," and to the hills, "Fall upon us."

**10:9** From the time of the hills Israel has sinned; there they stood. War shall by no means overtake them on the hill against the children of unrighteousness

**10:10** to chasten them; and peoples shall be gathered against them when they are chastened for their two iniquities.

**10:11** Ephraim is a heifer taught to love contention, yet I will come upon the fairness of her neck; I will ride upon Ephraim; I will pass over Judah in silence; Jacob shall prevail for himself.

**10:12** Sow for yourselves in righteousness; reap for the fruit of life; light for yourselves the light of knowledge; seek the Lord until the produce of righteousness comes to us.

**10:13** Why have you passed over ungodliness in silence, reaped its iniquities, and eaten false fruit? Because you have trusted in your sins, in the multitude of your strength,

**10:14** destruction shall rise up among your people, and all your fortified places shall vanish away. Just as Prince Salaman from the house of Jeroboam, in the days of war they dashed mother upon children to the ground,

**10:15** so I will do to you, O house of Israel, because of the unrighteousness of your evil deeds.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — The Greek reads "sojourn" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "fear"), "the house of On" (for Beth-aven), and "provoked it" (where the Hebrew has "its priests").
- **10:6** — The Greek preserves the proper name Jarim (for Hebrew Jareb) and reads "in/as a gift" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "shame").
- **10:11** — The Greek renders two verbs differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reading "love contention" (vs. "love to thresh") and "pass over Judah in silence" (vs. "Judah shall plow").
- **10:14** — The Greek names "Prince Salaman from the house of Jeroboam," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel."
