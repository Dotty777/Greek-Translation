## Text

**5:1** Hear these things, O priests, and give heed, O house of Israel, and O house of the king, give ear, because the judgment is against you; for you have become a snare to the watchtower, and like a net spread out upon Tabor,

**5:2** which those who hunt the prey have set fast. Yet I am your discipliner;

**5:3** I have known Ephraim, and Israel is not absent from Me; because now Ephraim has committed harlotry, Israel has been defiled.

**5:4** They did not give their counsels to return to their God, for a spirit of harlotry is in them, and they have not known the Lord.

**5:5** And the arrogance of Israel shall be humbled to his face, and Israel and Ephraim shall grow weak in their iniquities, and Judah also shall grow weak with them.

**5:6** With sheep and calves they shall go to seek out the Lord, and they shall by no means find Him, for He has turned aside from them;

**5:7** because they have forsaken the Lord, for strange children were born to them; now the blight shall devour them and their allotments.

**5:8** Sound the trumpet upon the hills, make a loud cry upon the high places, proclaim in the house of On: Benjamin is astounded,

**5:9** Ephraim has become a desolation in the days of reproof; among the tribes of Israel I have shown faithful things.

**5:10** The rulers of Judah have become like those who shift boundaries; upon them I will pour out My fury like water.

**5:11** Ephraim has oppressed his adversary, he has trampled down justice, because he began to walk after vain things.

**5:12** And I am like turmoil to Ephraim, and like a goad to the house of Judah.

**5:13** And Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah his pain; and Ephraim went to the Assyrians and sent ambassadors to King Jareim; yet he was not able to heal you, and pain shall by no means cease from you.

**5:14** Because I am like a panther to Ephraim, and like a lion to the house of Judah; and I will seize and depart, and I will carry off, and there shall be no one who delivers.

**5:15** I will depart and return to My place, until they are brought to ruin and seek My face. In their affliction they shall rise early to Me, saying,

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek translates Hebrew Mizpah literally as "the watchtower" and uses the Greek form Itabyrion for Mount Tabor.
- **5:7** — Greek reads "the blight" (mildew or rust) rather than the Hebrew "new moon" or "month."
- **5:8** — Greek translates Gibeah and Ramah literally as "the hills" and "the high places," and concludes the verse with "Benjamin is astounded."
- **5:13** — Greek preserves the distinct name "King Jareim" (compare Hebrew Jareb or "the great king") and explicitly mentions "ambassadors."
