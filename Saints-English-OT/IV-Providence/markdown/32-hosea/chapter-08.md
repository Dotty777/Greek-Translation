## Text

**8:1** Into their bosom as earth, like an eagle against the house of the Lord, because they have transgressed My covenant and gone against My law.

**8:2** They shall cry out to Me, "O God, we have known You."

**8:3** Because Israel has turned away from good things, they have pursued an enemy.

**8:4** They reigned for themselves, and not through Me; they ruled, and did not make it known to Me; of their silver and their gold they made idols for themselves, so that they might be utterly destroyed.

**8:5** Cast off your calf, O Samaria! My wrath is provoked against them; how long shall they be unable to be cleansed

**8:6** in Israel? For a craftsman made it, and it is not God; because your calf was leading astray, O Samaria.

**8:7** For they have sown wind-blighted things, and their destruction shall receive them—a sheaf having no strength to yield meal; and even if it should yield some, strangers shall devour it.

**8:8** Israel has been swallowed up; now he has become among the nations like a useless vessel.

**8:9** For they themselves have gone up to the Assyrians; Ephraim blossomed again by himself; they loved gifts,

**8:10** therefore they shall be delivered over among the nations. Now I will take them in, and they shall cease for a little while from anointing a king and princes.

**8:11** Because Ephraim has multiplied altars, beloved altars have become to him for sins.

**8:12** I will write down a multitude for him, yet My statutes were reckoned as strange things—the beloved altars.

**8:13** For if they offer a sacrifice and eat flesh, the Lord will not accept them; now He will remember their iniquities and punish their sins; they have returned to Egypt, and among the Assyrians they shall eat unclean things.

**8:14** And Israel has forgotten Him who made him, and they have built shrines; and Judah has multiplied walled cities; and I will send forth fire into his cities, and it shall devour their foundations.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Septuagint reads "Into their bosom as earth," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Set the trumpet to your mouth") through a different reading of the Hebrew consonants.
- **8:9** — The Greek "blossomed again by himself" reflects a Hebrew root meaning "to flourish," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "a wild donkey alone by itself."
- **8:10** — The Greek reads "from anointing a king and princes," whereas the Masoretic Text refers to "the burden of the king of princes."
