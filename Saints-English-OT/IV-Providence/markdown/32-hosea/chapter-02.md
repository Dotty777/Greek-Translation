## Text

**2:1** And the number of the sons of Israel was as the sand of the sea, which shall not be measured nor numbered; and it shall come to pass that in the place where it was said to them, "You are not My people," they also shall be called sons of the living God.

**2:2** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel shall be gathered together, and they shall appoint for themselves one ruler, and they shall go up out of the land; for great is the day of Jezreel.

**2:3** Say to your brother, "My People," and to your sister, "Shown Mercy."

**2:4** Contend against your mother, contend, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband; and I will remove her harlotry from My presence, and her adultery from between her breasts,

**2:5** so that I may strip her naked and restore her as on the day of her birth; and I will make her desolate, and set her as a waterless land, and slay her with thirst.

**2:6** And I will by no means show mercy to her children, for they are children of harlotry.

**2:7** For their mother has committed harlotry; she who bore them has acted shamefully. For she said, "I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, and my garments and my linens, my oil and all things that are fitting for me."

**2:8** Therefore, behold, I hedge up her way with thorns, and I will wall up the ways, and she shall by no means find her path.

**2:9** And she shall pursue her lovers, and shall by no means overtake them; and she shall seek them, and shall by no means find them. Then she shall say, "I will go and return to my former husband, for it was better for me then than now."

**2:10** And she did not know that I gave her the grain and the wine and the oil, and multiplied silver for her; yet she made things of silver and gold for Baal.

**2:11** Therefore I will turn back and recover My grain in its season and My wine in its appointed time, and I will take away My garments and My linens, so that they may not cover her nakedness.

**2:12** And now I will uncover her uncleanness in the sight of her lovers, and no one shall by any means deliver her out of My hand.

**2:13** And I will turn away all her gladness, her feasts, and her new moons, and her Sabbaths, and all her solemn assemblies.

**2:14** And I will lay waste her vine and her fig trees, of which she said, "These are my wages that my lovers have given me." And I will make them a testimony, and the wild beasts of the field, and the birds of the heaven, and the creeping things of the earth shall devour them.

**2:15** And I will take vengeance upon her for the days of the Baalim, in which she offered sacrifice to them, and adorned herself with her earrings and her necklaces, and went after her lovers, but forgot Me, says the Lord.

**2:16** Therefore, behold, I will cause her to wander, and I will set her as a wilderness, and I will speak to her heart,

**2:17** and I will give her possessions to her from there, and the Valley of Achor to open her understanding; and she shall be humbled there according to the days of her infancy, and according to the days of her coming up out of the land of Egypt.

**2:18** And it shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord, that she shall call Me, "My Husband," and shall no longer call Me, "Baalim."

**2:19** And I will remove the names of the Baalim from her mouth, and their names shall by no means be remembered anymore.

**2:20** And I will make a covenant for them in that day with the wild beasts of the field, and with the birds of the heaven, and the creeping things of the earth; and I will shatter bow and sword and war from the earth, and I will cause you to dwell in hope.

**2:21** And I will betroth you to Myself forever; and I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and in judgment, and in mercy and in compassions,

**2:22** and I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness, and you shall know the Lord.

**2:23** And it shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord, that I will hear the heaven, and it shall hear the earth,

**2:24** and the earth shall hear the grain and the wine and the oil, and they shall hear Jezreel.

**2:25** And I will sow her for Myself upon the earth, and I will love Not Loved; and I will say to Not My People, "You are My people," and he shall say, "You are the Lord my God."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Septuagint chapter 2 begins three verses earlier than most English Bibles (covering English 1:10–2:23) and opens with the past-tense verb "was" before shifting to the future.
- **2:4** — The Greek expresses the removal of harlotry in the first person, "I will remove her harlotry from My presence," rather than as a third-person exhortation.
- **2:14** — The Greek reads "a testimony" where the Hebrew has "a forest," and expands the clause to include the birds of the heaven and the creeping things of the earth.
- **2:17** — The Greek distinctively reads "her possessions," "to open her understanding" (rather than "a door of hope"), and "she shall be humbled."
- **2:25** — Here the Greek shifts from "mercy" (1:6; 2:3) to "love," reading "I will love Not Loved" (later echoed in Romans 9:25), and expands the final reply to "You are the Lord my God."
