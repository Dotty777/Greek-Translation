## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord which came to Hosea, son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah and Jotham and Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, son of Joash, king of Israel.

**1:2** The beginning of the word of the Lord through Hosea. And the Lord said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of harlotry and children of harlotry, because the land will surely commit great harlotry, turning away from following the Lord."

**1:3** So he went and took Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

**1:4** And the Lord said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, because in yet a little while I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Judah, and I will bring to an end the kingdom of the house of Israel;

**1:5** and it shall come to pass in that day that I will shatter the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel."

**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him, "Call her name 'Not Shown Mercy,' because I will no longer continue to show mercy to the house of Israel, but rather I will surely set Myself in opposition to them.

**1:7** But I will show mercy to the sons and will save them by the Lord their God; and I will not save them by bow, nor by sword, nor by war, nor by horses, nor by horsemen."

**1:8** And she weaned Not Shown Mercy, and conceived again and bore a son.

**1:9** And He said, "Call his name 'Not My People,' because you are not My people, and I am not yours."

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Greek text reads "the house of Judah" rather than "the house of Jehu" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:6** — The Greek translates the daughter's symbolic name as "Not Shown Mercy" and concludes the verse with "I will surely set Myself in opposition to them" rather than a denial of forgiveness.
- **1:7** — The critical Greek text reads simply "the sons," omitting "of Judah" found in the Hebrew and some Greek manuscripts.
