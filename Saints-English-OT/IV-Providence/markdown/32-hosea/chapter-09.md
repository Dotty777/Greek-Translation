## Text

**9:1** Do not rejoice, O Israel, nor be glad like the peoples; for you have played the harlot away from your God, you have loved gifts upon every threshing floor of grain.

**9:2** The threshing floor and the winepress did not know them, and the wine has deceived them.

**9:3** They did not dwell in the land of the Lord; Ephraim has dwelt in Egypt, and among the Assyrians they shall eat unclean things.

**9:4** They did not pour out wine to the Lord, nor were they pleasing to Him; their sacrifices are like bread of mourning to them; all who eat them shall be defiled, because their loaves are for their own souls; they shall not enter into the house of the Lord.

**9:5** What will you do in the days of the solemn assembly, and in the day of the feast of the Lord?

**9:6** Therefore, behold, they go forth from the misery of Egypt, and Memphis shall receive them, and Machmas shall bury them; as for their silver, destruction shall inherit it; thorns shall be in the tents.

**9:7** The days of vengeance have come, the days of your recompense have come, and Israel shall be afflicted like the prophet who is beside himself, the man who is spirit-borne; by the multitude of your iniquities, your madness has been multiplied.

**9:8** Ephraim is a watchman with God; a prophet is a crooked snare upon all his ways; they have planted madness in the house of God.

**9:9** They have become corrupted as in the days of the hill; He will remember their iniquities; He will punish their sins.

**9:10** Like a cluster of grapes in the wilderness I found Israel, and like an early watchman on a fig tree I saw their fathers; they went in to Baal-peor, and became estranged in shame, and the abominable ones became like the beloved ones.

**9:11** Ephraim has flown away like a bird—their glories from births and birth pangs and conceptions;

**9:12** for even if they rear their children, they shall be made childless from among men; for indeed woe is to them; My flesh is from them.

**9:13** Ephraim, just as they presented their children for prey, so Ephraim must bring forth his children to slaughter.

**9:14** Give to them, O Lord—what will You give to them? A childless womb and dry breasts.

**9:15** All their wicked deeds are in Gilgal, for there I hated them because of the wickedness of their practices; I will drive them out of My house; I will no longer continue to love them; all their princes are disobedient.

**9:16** Ephraim is afflicted; he is dried up at his roots; he shall no longer bear fruit; for even if they bear children, I will slay the cherished ones of their womb.

**9:17** God will cast them away, because they did not listen to Him; and they shall be wanderers among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — The Septuagint preserves the proper name Machmas (Michmash) as the second burial site alongside Memphis, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "precious things" of silver.
- **9:9** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Gibeah literally as "the hill."
- **9:10** — The Greek reads "an early watchman on a fig tree" (using the same noun for "watchman" as in 9:8), where the Masoretic Text has "the first-ripe fruit on the fig tree."
- **9:12** — The Septuagint reads "My flesh is from them," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants rendered in the Masoretic Text as "when I depart from them."
- **9:13** — The Greek renders "for prey" where the Masoretic Text reads "like Tyre," reflecting a closely similar Hebrew word.
