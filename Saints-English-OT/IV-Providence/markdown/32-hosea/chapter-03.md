## Text

**3:1** And the Lord said to me, "Go again and love a woman who loves evil things and is an adulteress, even as God loves the sons of Israel, though they look to strange gods and love cakes with raisins."

**3:2** So I hired her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver, and a homer of barley, and a nebel of wine.

**3:3** And I said to her, "Many days you shall abide for me; and you shall by no means commit harlotry, nor shall you belong to any man; and so also shall I be for you."

**3:4** For many days the sons of Israel shall abide without a king, and without a prince, and without sacrifice, and without an altar, and without a priesthood, and without manifestations.

**3:5** And afterward the sons of Israel shall return and seek the Lord their God and David their king; and they shall stand in awe of the Lord and of His good things in the last days.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek describes the woman as one "who loves evil things" and reads "God" rather than the Lord in the comparison clause.
- **3:2** — A "homer" (Greek gomor) is a dry measure, and a "nebel" (Greek nebel) is a wineskin or jar measure of wine, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a half-homer of barley.
- **3:4** — Where the Hebrew lists sacred pillar, ephod, and teraphim, the Greek reads "altar," "priesthood," and "manifestations" (the Septuagint term for the priestly Urim).
