## Text

**13:1** According to the word of Ephraim, he himself received ordinances in Israel, and he assigned them to Baal and died.

**13:2** And they continued to sin, and made for themselves a molten image from their silver according to the likeness of idols, the works of craftsmen completed for them; they themselves say, "Sacrifice men, for calves have failed."

**13:3** Therefore they shall be like a morning cloud and like the early dew that departs, like chaff blown away from a threshing floor and like vapor from tears.

**13:4** Yet I am the Lord your God, who establishes the heaven and creates the earth, whose hands created all the host of heaven, and I did not show them to you to walk after them; and I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and you shall know no God besides Me, and there is no savior apart from Me.

**13:5** I shepherded you in the wilderness, in an uninhabited land,

**13:6** according to their pastures; and they were filled to satisfaction, and their hearts were lifted up; for this reason they forgot Me.

**13:7** And I will be to them like a panther, and like a leopard along the road of the Assyrians;

**13:8** I will meet them like a bear in distress, and I will tear open the enclosure of their heart, and whelps of the forest shall devour them there; wild beasts of the field shall tear them apart.

**13:9** In your destruction, O Israel, who will help?

**13:10** Where is this king of yours? And let him deliver you in all your cities; let him judge you, of whom you said, "Give me a king and a ruler."

**13:11** And I gave you a king in My anger, and I held back in My wrath.

**13:12** A bundle of iniquity is Ephraim; his sin is hidden away;

**13:13** birth pangs like those of a woman in labor shall come upon him. This is your wise son, for he shall by no means stand firm in the crushing of children.

**13:14** From the hand of Hades I will deliver them, and from death I will redeem them. Where is your penalty, O death? Where is your sting, O Hades? Comfort is hidden from My eyes,

**13:15** because this one shall cause division among brethren. The Lord shall bring a scorching wind from the wilderness upon him, and He shall dry up his veins and lay waste his springs; He Himself shall parch his land and all his desirable vessels.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Septuagint reads "Sacrifice men, for calves have failed," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of men who sacrifice kissing calves.
- **13:3** — The Septuagint literally reads "vapor from tears" (apo dakryon), a distinctive Greek reading compared with the Hebrew "smoke from a lattice."
- **13:4** — The Septuagint preserves an expanded declaration of the Lord as Creator of heaven, earth, and the heavenly host, emphasizing that Israel was not shown them in order to worship them.
- **13:14** — The Septuagint's rhetorical questions—"Where is your penalty, O death? Where is your sting, O Hades?"—closely underlie Paul's quotation in 1 Corinthians 15:55.
