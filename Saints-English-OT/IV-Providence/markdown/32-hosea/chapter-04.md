## Text

**4:1** Hear the word of the Lord, O sons of Israel, for the Lord has a judgment against those who dwell in the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God in the land;

**4:2** cursing and lying and murder and theft and adultery have been poured out upon the land, and they mingle bloodshed upon bloodshed.

**4:3** Therefore the land shall mourn, and shall be diminished together with all who dwell in it, with the wild beasts of the field, and with the creeping things of the earth, and with the birds of heaven; and the fish of the sea shall fail,

**4:4** so that no one may contend in judgment, nor anyone reprove. Yet My people are like a priest who is spoken against,

**4:5** and he shall grow weak by day, and a prophet shall grow weak with you; to the night I have likened your mother.

**4:6** My people have been likened to one who has no knowledge; because you have rejected knowledge, I also will reject you from serving as priest to Me; and you have forgotten the law of your God, and I also will forget your children.

**4:7** According to their multitude, so they sinned against Me; I will turn their glory into dishonor.

**4:8** They shall eat the sins of My people, and in their iniquities they shall take their souls.

**4:9** And it shall be: as with the people, so also with the priest; and I will avenge his ways upon him, and his counsels I will repay to him.

**4:10** And they shall eat, and shall by no means be filled; they committed harlotry, and shall by no means prosper, because they have forsaken the Lord to keep

**4:11** harlotry. And the heart of My people has received wine and strong drink;

**4:12** they inquired by omens, and by his rods they declared to him; by a spirit of harlotry they were led astray, and they committed harlotry away from their God.

**4:13** Upon the summits of the mountains they sacrificed, and upon the hills they made offerings, beneath an oak and a white poplar and an overshadowing tree, because the shelter is good. Therefore your daughters shall commit harlotry, and your brides shall commit adultery;

**4:14** and I will by no means visit punishment upon your daughters when they commit harlotry, nor upon your brides when they commit adultery; for they themselves mingled with the harlots, and offered sacrifices with the initiated ones, and the people who had understanding became entangled with a harlot.

**4:15** But as for you, O Israel, do not be ignorant; and O Judah, do not enter into Gilgal, and do not go up to the house of On, and do not swear by the living Lord.

**4:16** Because like a frenzied heifer, Israel has run wild; now the Lord shall pasture them like a lamb in a broad place.

**4:17** A partner with idols, Ephraim has set stumbling blocks for himself;

**4:18** he has chosen Canaanites; committing harlotry, they have utterly given themselves to harlotry; they have loved dishonor out of her arrogance.

**4:19** You are a whirlwind in her wings, and they shall be put to shame because of their altars.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Greek "likened" (also in 4:6) reflects reading the Hebrew root as "be like" rather than "destroy."
- **4:11** — The Greek syntax links "harlotry" to the end of 4:10 ("to keep harlotry") and reads "the heart of My people has received wine and strong drink."
- **4:14** — Greek "initiated ones" refers to devotees of pagan mystery rites, and unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek describes the people as "having understanding" yet becoming entangled with a harlot.
- **4:15** — Greek "the house of On" (also in 5:8) renders the name as On rather than the Hebrew Beth-aven.
