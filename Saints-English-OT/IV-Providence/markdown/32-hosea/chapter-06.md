## Text

**6:1** "Let us go and return to the Lord our God, for He has seized, and He will heal us; He will strike, and He will bind us up;

**6:2** He will make us whole after two days; on the third day we shall rise up,

**6:3** and we shall live before Him, and we shall know; we shall press on to know the Lord; like the ready dawn we shall find Him, and He shall come to us like rain, early and late rain for the earth."

**6:4** What shall I do to you, O Ephraim? What shall I do to you, O Judah? For your mercy is like a morning cloud, and like the early dew that passes away.

**6:5** Therefore I have mowed down your prophets; I have slain them by the word of My mouth, and My judgment shall go forth like light.

**6:6** Because I desire mercy rather than sacrifice, and the knowledge of God rather than whole burnt offerings.

**6:7** Yet they are like a man who transgresses a covenant; there Gilead despised Me, a city

**6:8** working vain things, troubling water,

**6:9** and your strength is that of a robber; priests hid the way, they murdered Shechem, because they practiced lawlessness

**6:10** in the house of Israel. I saw horrible things there, the harlotry of Ephraim; Israel and Judah were defiled. Begin to gather the vintage for yourself

**6:11** when I restore the captivity of My people,

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Greek reads "we shall rise up" rather than the Hebrew "He will raise us up," and places "and we shall live before Him" at the start of 6:3.
- **6:7** — Greek reads "like a man" rather than "like Adam" (or "at Adam"), and joins "Gilead, a city" to the verb "despised Me" before continuing into 6:8.
- **6:8** — Greek reads "troubling water" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "tracked with blood."
- **6:10** — Greek joins "and Judah" to the defilement of Israel and reads "Begin to gather the vintage for yourself," continuing directly into 6:11.
