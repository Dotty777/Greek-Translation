## Text

**5:1** And you, Bethlehem, house of Ephrathah, you are very small to be among the thousands of Judah; out of it shall come forth to Me One to be Ruler of Israel, and His goings forth are from the beginning, from the days of eternity.

**5:2** Therefore He shall give them up until the time when she who is in labor shall give birth; and the rest of their brethren shall return to the sons of Israel.

**5:3** And He shall stand and look, and the Lord shall shepherd His flock in strength, and they shall abide in the glory of the name of the Lord their God; for now He shall be magnified to the ends of the earth.

**5:4** And this shall be peace: when Asshur comes against your land, and when he treads upon your country, then seven shepherds and eight bites of men shall be raised up against him.

**5:5** And they shall shepherd Asshur with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in its trench; and He shall bring deliverance from Asshur when he comes against your land, and when he treads upon your borders.

**5:6** And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like dew falling from the Lord, and like lambs upon the grass, so that no one may be gathered together nor withstand among the sons of men.

**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts in the forest, and like a young lion among flocks of sheep, just as when he passes through, and separating them, snatches them away, and there is no one to deliver.

**5:8** Your hand shall be lifted up over those who afflict you, and all your enemies shall be utterly destroyed.

**5:9** "And it shall come to pass in that day," says the Lord, "I will utterly destroy the horses from your midst and demolish your chariots.

**5:10** And I will utterly destroy the cities of your land and remove all your strongholds.

**5:11** And I will utterly destroy your sorceries out of your hands, and there shall be no soothsayers among you.

**5:12** And I will utterly destroy your carved images and your pillars from your midst, and you shall no longer worship the works of your hands.

**5:13** And I will cut down the groves from your midst and obliterate your cities.

**5:14** And I will execute vengeance in wrath and in fury upon the nations, because they did not listen."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Greek reads "house of Ephrathah" and the relative pronoun "out of it" (ex hou), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew text read "out of you."
- **5:4** — "Eight bites of men" reflects the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew word for "princes" from a similar root meaning "to bite."
- **5:6** — The Greek reads "like lambs upon the grass," whereas the Hebrew text reads "like showers upon the grass."
