## Text

**3:1** And he shall say: "Hear now these things, O rulers of the house of Jacob and you remnant of the house of Israel: Is it not for you to know justice,

**3:2** who hate good things and seek evil things, who tear their skins from them and their flesh from their bones?

**3:3** Just as they devoured the flesh of my people, and flayed their skins from them, and crushed their bones, and cut them in pieces like flesh for a cauldron and like meat for a pot,

**3:4** so they shall cry out to the Lord, and He will not hear them; and He will turn away His face from them at that time, because they acted wickedly in their practices against them."

**3:5** Thus says the Lord concerning the prophets who lead my people astray, who bite with their teeth and proclaim peace upon him, and when nothing was put into their mouth, they raised up war against him:

**3:6** "Therefore night shall be to you without a vision, and darkness shall be to you without divination; and the sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall grow dark over them;

**3:7** and those who see dreams shall be put to shame, and the diviners shall be mocked, and all of them shall speak against them, because there shall be no one who hears them."

**3:8** Unless I fill up strength by the Spirit of the Lord, and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his impieties and to Israel his sins.

**3:9** Hear now these things, O leaders of the house of Jacob and you remnant of the house of Israel, who abhor justice and pervert all things that are upright,

**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with injustices.

**3:11** Her leaders judged for bribes, and her priests answered for hire, and her prophets divined for silver; yet they rested upon the Lord, saying, "Is not the Lord among us? Calamities shall by no means come upon us."

**3:12** Therefore because of you Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall be like a watchman's hut, and the mountain of the house shall become a grove of the forest.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek opens in the third person future ('And he shall say') and addresses 'the remnant' (hoi kataloipoi, also in 3:9) where the Hebrew refers to rulers.
- **3:8** — The Greek begins with ean me ('unless'), a literal rendering of the Hebrew adversative particle ('but truly'), contrasting the prophet's empowerment by the Spirit of the Lord with the silenced false prophets of 3:7.
- **3:12** — As in 1:6, the Greek translates the desolation of Jerusalem with oporophylakion ('a watchman's hut' in an orchard or field) rather than 'heaps of ruins.'
