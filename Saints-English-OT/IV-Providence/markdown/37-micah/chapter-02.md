## Text

**2:1** They became devisers of troubles and workers of evil upon their beds, and at the break of day they accomplished them, because they did not lift up their hands to God.

**2:2** And they coveted fields, and plundered orphans, and oppressed houses, and plundered a man and his house, even a man and his inheritance.

**2:3** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I am devising calamities against this tribe, from which you shall by no means lift up your necks, and you shall by no means walk upright suddenly, for it is an evil time."

**2:4** In that day a proverb shall be taken up against you, and a lamentation shall be lamented in song, saying, "With misery we have been made miserable; the portion of my people has been measured out with a line, and there was no one to hinder him from turning away; our fields have been divided."

**2:5** Therefore you shall have no one casting a line by lot in the assembly of the Lord.

**2:6** Do not weep with tears, nor let them shed tears over these things; for he shall not cast off reproaches,

**2:7** he who says, "The house of Jacob has provoked the Spirit of the Lord." Are these His practices? Are not His words good with him, and have they not walked uprightly?

**2:8** And formerly My people rose up in enmity; opposite his peace they flayed off his skin, so as to take away hope, the shattering of war.

**2:9** The leaders of My people shall be cast out from the houses of their luxury; because of their evil practices they have been driven out. Draw near to the everlasting mountains.

**2:10** Arise and depart, for this is not your rest because of uncleanness. You have been corrupted with corruption,

**2:11** you have been pursued when no one pursues; a spirit has established falsehood, it has dripped for you for wine and strong drink. And it shall come to pass from the drop of this people,

**2:12** Jacob shall surely be gathered with all; I will surely receive the remnant of Israel, together I will appoint his return; like sheep in affliction, like a flock in the midst of their fold, they shall leap forth from among men.

**2:13** Through the breach before their face they broke through, and passed through the gate and went out through it; and their king went out before their face, and the Lord shall lead them.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Where the Hebrew explains the wicked deeds by saying it is in the power of their hand, the Greek reads 'because they did not lift up their hands to God.'
- **2:8** — The Greek diverges notably from the Hebrew garment imagery, describing the people as flaying off skin 'opposite his peace' so as to strip away hope through 'the shattering of war.'
- **2:12** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew term for a sheepfold (Botzrah) as 'in affliction' (en thlipsei), emphasizing Israel's distress before deliverance.
