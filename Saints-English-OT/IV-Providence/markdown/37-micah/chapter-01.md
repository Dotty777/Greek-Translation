## Text

**1:1** And the word of the Lord came to Micah the son of Morasthi in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, concerning the things he saw regarding Samaria and regarding Jerusalem.

**1:2** Hear words, O peoples, and let the earth give heed, and all who are in it; and the Lord, the Lord shall be among you for a witness, the Lord from His holy house.

**1:3** For behold, the Lord comes forth from His place, and He shall descend upon the high places of the earth,

**1:4** and the mountains shall be shaken beneath Him, and the valleys shall melt like wax before the face of fire, and like water carried down a steep descent.

**1:5** All these things are because of the ungodliness of Jacob, and because of the sin of the house of Israel. What is the ungodliness of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what is the sin of the house of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

**1:6** And I will make Samaria a watchman's hut in a field and a planting of a vineyard; and I will drag down her stones into a chasm, and I will uncover her foundations.

**1:7** And all her carved images they shall cut to pieces, and all her hires they shall burn with fire, and all her idols I will give over to destruction; because from the hires of harlotry she gathered them, and from the hires of harlotry she amassed them.

**1:8** Because of this she shall beat her breast and lament; she shall walk barefoot and naked; she shall make lamentation like that of dragons and mourning like that of the daughters of sirens;

**1:9** for her wound has prevailed, because it has come even to Judah and has reached even to the gate of My people, even to Jerusalem.

**1:10** You who are in Gath, do not magnify yourselves; you who are in Akim, do not rebuild from a house a mockery; sprinkle yourselves with dust for your mockery.

**1:11** Dwelling well in her cities, she who dwells in Sennaar did not come forth to mourn the house adjoining her; she shall receive from you a stroke of grief.

**1:12** Who has begun for good toward her who dwells in sorrows? For calamities have come down from the Lord upon the gates of Jerusalem,

**1:13** a sound of chariots and horsemen. O inhabitant of Lachish, she is the leader of sin to the daughter of Zion, for in you were found the impieties of Israel.

**1:14** Therefore she shall give those who are sent away even to the inheritance of Gath, vain houses; they have come to nothing for the kings of Israel,

**1:15** until they bring the heirs, O inhabitant of Lachish; an inheritance shall come even to Adullam, the glory of the daughter of Israel.

**1:16** Shave yourself and cut off your hair for your delicate children; enlarge your widowhood like an eagle, because they have been carried away captive from you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek renders the Hebrew gentilic 'the Morashtite' as a patronymic, 'the son of Morasthi' (ton tou Morasthei).
- **1:8** — The Greek text uses the third person singular ('she shall beat her breast') referring to Samaria, and renders the mourning creatures as 'dragons' (drakonton) and 'daughters of sirens' (thygateron seirenon).
- **1:10** — The Septuagint preserves the distinct proper name Akim (where the Hebrew reads 'weep not') and interprets the wordplay on Beth-le-aphrah as rebuilding a mockery from a house.
- **1:11** — The Greek reads the place name Sennaar (corresponding to Hebrew Zaanan) and translates the Hebrew place names Shaphir and Beth-ezel descriptively as 'dwelling well' and 'the house adjoining her.'
- **1:16** — The Greek text reads 'widowhood' (cherian) rather than 'baldness' found in the Hebrew text and some later manuscripts.
