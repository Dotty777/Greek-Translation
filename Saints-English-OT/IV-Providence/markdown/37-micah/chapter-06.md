## Text

**6:1** Hear now a word. The Lord, the Lord has said, "Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice."

**6:2** Hear, O peoples, the judgment of the Lord, and you ravines, foundations of the earth; for the Lord has a controversy with His people, and He will contend with Israel.

**6:3** O My people, what have I done to you, or how have I grieved you, or how have I troubled you? Answer Me.

**6:4** For I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you from the house of bondage; and I sent before your face Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

**6:5** O My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab devised against you, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him, from the rushes even to Gilgal, so that the righteousness of the Lord might be known.

**6:6** With what shall I reach the Lord, and lay hold of my God Most High? Shall I reach Him with whole burnt offerings, with year-old calves?

**6:7** Will the Lord accept thousands of rams, or ten thousands of fat torrents? Shall I give my firstborn for ungodliness, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?

**6:8** Has it been declared to you, O man, what is good? Or what does the Lord seek from you, except to practice justice, and to love mercy, and to be ready to walk with the Lord your God?

**6:9** The voice of the Lord shall be proclaimed to the city, and He shall save those who fear His name. Hear, O tribe! And who shall adorn the city?

**6:10** Is there not fire, and the house of the lawless man storing up lawless treasures, and unrighteousness with insolence?

**6:11** Shall the lawless man be justified with a balance, and with weights of deceit in a bag?

**6:12** From these they have filled their wealth with ungodliness, and its inhabitants spoke lies; and their tongue—be exalted in their mouth.

**6:13** And I will begin to strike you; I will lay you waste in your sins.

**6:14** You shall eat, and shall by no means be satisfied; and it shall grow dark within you, and it shall turn aside, and you shall by no means be saved; and as many as are saved shall be delivered over to the sword.

**6:15** You shall sow, and shall by no means reap; you shall press the olive, and shall by no means anoint yourself with oil, and wine, and you shall by no means drink it; and the ordinances of My people shall be wiped out.

**6:16** And you have kept the statutes of Zimri and all the works of the house of Ahab, and you have walked in their ways, so that I should deliver you over to desolation and its inhabitants to hissing; and you shall bear the reproaches of the peoples.

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — "From the rushes" translates the Greek common noun schoinon, where the Septuagint rendered the Hebrew place name Shittim as a botanical term.
- **6:7** — "Fat torrents" translates the Greek cheimarron pionon, corresponding to the Hebrew "rivers of oil."
- **6:8** — The Greek begins with a passive question ("Has it been declared to you") and reads "to be ready to walk" rather than the Hebrew "to walk humbly."
- **6:12** — The Greek text contains the passive imperative "be exalted" (hypsotheti), reflecting a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew calls the tongue "deceitful."
- **6:16** — The Greek text names "Zimri" (Zambrei), a Septuagint-distinct proper name where the Hebrew text reads "Omri."
