## Text

**4:1** And it shall come to pass in the last days that the mountain of the Lord shall be manifest, made ready upon the summits of the mountains, and it shall be lifted high above the hills; and peoples shall hasten to it.

**4:2** And many nations shall go and say, "Come, let us ascend to the mountain of the Lord and to the house of the God of Jacob; and they will show us His way, and we shall walk in His paths." For from Zion a law shall go forth, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.

**4:3** And He shall judge between many peoples, and rebuke mighty nations even far away; and they shall cut down their swords into plows, and their spears into sickles; nation shall no longer lift up a sword against nation, neither shall they learn to wage war anymore.

**4:4** And each one shall rest beneath his vine, and each one beneath his fig tree, and there shall be no one to terrify them; for the mouth of the Lord Almighty has spoken these things.

**4:5** For all the peoples shall walk each in his own way, but we shall walk in the name of the Lord our God forever and beyond.

**4:6** "In that day," says the Lord, "I will gather her who has been crushed, and her who has been driven out I will receive, along with those whom I cast off.

**4:7** And I will make her who has been crushed into a remnant, and her who was cast off into a mighty nation; and the Lord shall reign over them on Mount Zion from now even forevermore."

**4:8** And you, gloomy tower of the flock, daughter of Zion, to you it shall come; even the former dominion shall enter in, a kingdom from Babylon to the daughter of Jerusalem.

**4:9** And now, why have you known evils? Was there no king for you? Or has your counsel perished, that birth pangs have seized you like a woman in labor?

**4:10** Be in travail, and take courage, and draw near, daughter of Zion, like a woman giving birth; for now you shall go out from the city, and encamp in the plain, and come even to Babylon; from there the Lord your God shall deliver you out of the hand of your enemies.

**4:11** And now many nations have been gathered against you, saying, "We shall gloat over her, and our eyes shall gaze upon Zion."

**4:12** Yet they did not know the design of the Lord, and did not understand His counsel, that He has gathered them like sheaves of the threshing floor.

**4:13** Arise and thresh them, daughter of Zion, for I will make your horns of iron, and your hooves I will make of bronze; and you shall melt down many peoples, and you shall dedicate their multitude to the Lord, and their strength to the Lord of all the earth.

**4:14** Now the daughter shall be hedged in with a hedge; he has laid a siege against you; with a rod they shall strike upon the cheek the gates of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Greek has a third-person plural verb ("they will show us"), whereas the Hebrew text reads singular ("He will teach us").
- **4:8** — The Greek describes the tower as "gloomy" (auchmodes) and uniquely adds "from Babylon," a phrase absent from the Hebrew text.
- **4:14** — The Greek text reads "the gates (pylas) of Israel," where some Greek manuscripts read "tribes" (phylas) and the Hebrew text reads "judge."
