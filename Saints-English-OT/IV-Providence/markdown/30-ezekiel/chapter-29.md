## Text

**29:1** In the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:2** "Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt,

**29:3** and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against Pharaoh, the great dragon who lies in the midst of his rivers, who says, "The rivers are mine, and I myself made them."

**29:4** And I will put snares into your jaws, and I will cause the fish of your river to cling to your fins, and I will bring you up out of the midst of your river,

**29:5** and I will cast you down swiftly, together with all the fish of your river. You shall fall upon the face of the plain, and you shall by no means be gathered nor buried; to the beasts of the earth and to the birds of heaven I have given you for food.

**29:6** Then all who dwell in Egypt shall know that I am the Lord, because you have become a staff of reed to the house of Israel.

**29:7** When they took hold of you with their hand, you were crushed; and when every hand clapped against them, and when they leaned upon you, you were shattered, and you broke all their loins.

**29:8** Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will destroy men and beasts from among you,

**29:9** and the land of Egypt shall become ruin and desolation; and they shall know that I am the Lord, because you say, "The rivers are mine, and I myself made them."

**29:10** Therefore, behold, I am against you and against all your rivers, and I will give the land of Egypt over to desolation, the sword, and ruin, from Migdol and Syene even to the borders of the Ethiopians.

**29:11** No foot of man shall pass through it, nor shall any foot of beast pass through it, and it shall not be inhabited for forty years.

**29:12** And I will make her land a ruin in the midst of a desolated land, and her cities in the midst of desolated cities shall be forty years; and I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and winnow them into the countries.

**29:13** Thus says the Lord: After forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the nations where they were dispersed,

**29:14** and I will restore the captivity of the Egyptians, and cause them to dwell in the land of Pathros, in the land from which they were taken; and it shall be a lowly dominion

**29:15** beyond all dominions, and it shall never again be exalted above the nations; and I will make them few in number, so that they shall not be more numerous among the nations.

**29:16** And they shall no longer be a hope for the house of Israel, bringing iniquity to remembrance when they follow after them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**29:17** And it came to pass in the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:18** "Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made his army serve a heavy service against Tyre; every head was made bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare; yet neither he nor his army received wages from Tyre, nor for the service which they served against her.

**29:19** Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he shall plunder her plunder and despoil her spoil, and it shall be wages for his army.

**29:20** In return for his labor which he served against Tyre, I have given him the land of Egypt. Thus says the Sovereign Lord:

**29:21** In that day a horn shall spring forth for all the house of Israel, and to you I will give an open mouth in their midst, and they shall know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Greek text dates this oracle to the first day of the tenth month (compare the twelfth day in the Masoretic Text).
- **29:4** — The Greek reads "snares" (pagidas) rather than "hooks," and "fins" literally renders pterygas ("wings" or "fins").
- **29:7** — The Greek distinctively reads "when every hand clapped against them" (compare "you tore all their shoulder" in the Masoretic Text).
- **29:19** — "The Sovereign Lord" renders the Greek double divine title kyrios Kyrios (reflecting Hebrew Adonai YHWH), which recurs in 29:20, 30:10, 13, 22, and 31:15, 18.
