## Text

**20:1** And it came to pass in the seventh year, on the fifteenth of the month, that men from the elders of the house of Israel came to inquire of the Lord, and they sat before me.

**20:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**20:3** "Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Have you come to inquire of Me? As I live, I will surely not answer you, says the Lord,

**20:4** nor will I surely avenge them.' Son of man, testify to them concerning the iniquities of their fathers,

**20:5** and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: From the day that I chose the house of Israel, and made Myself known to the seed of the house of Jacob, and became known to them in the land of Egypt, and took hold of them with My hand, saying, "I am the Lord your God,"

**20:6** in that day I took hold of them with My hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt into the land which I had prepared for them, a land flowing with milk and honey—it is a honeycomb above all the earth.

**20:7** And I said to them, "Let each one cast away the abominations of his eyes, and do not defile yourselves with the practices of Egypt; I am the Lord your God."

**20:8** Yet they turned away from Me and were not willing to listen to Me; they did not cast away the abominations of their eyes, nor did they forsake the practices of Egypt. Then I said I would pour out My fury upon them, to accomplish My wrath against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.

**20:9** Yet I acted so that My name would not be profaned at all before the nations in whose midst they were, among whom I made Myself known to them in their sight, to bring them out of the land of Egypt.

**20:10** And I led them into the wilderness,

**20:11** and I gave them My statutes and made My ordinances known to them, all which a man shall do and live by them.

**20:12** Moreover I gave them My Sabbaths, to be a sign between Me and them, that they might know that I am the Lord who sanctifies them.

**20:13** And I said to the house of Israel in the wilderness, "Walk in My statutes." Yet they did not walk in them, and they rejected My ordinances, which a man shall do and live by them, and they greatly profaned My Sabbaths. Then I said I would pour out My fury upon them in the wilderness, to consume them.

**20:14** Yet I acted so that My name would not be profaned at all before the nations, in whose sight I had brought them out.

**20:15** And I lifted up My hand against them in the wilderness, that I would not at all bring them into the land which I had given them, a land flowing with milk and honey—it is a honeycomb above all the earth—

**20:16** because they rejected My ordinances and did not walk in My statutes, and they profaned My Sabbaths and went after the devices of their heart.

**20:17** Nevertheless My eye spared them from blotting them out, and I did not make a full end of them in the wilderness.

**20:18** And I said to their children in the wilderness, "Do not walk in the laws of your fathers, nor keep their ordinances, and do not mingle with their practices nor defile yourselves.

**20:19** I am the Lord your God; walk in My statutes, and keep My ordinances and do them,

**20:20** and sanctify My Sabbaths, and let it be a sign between Me and you, that you may know that I am the Lord your God."

**20:21** Yet their children also provoked Me to bitterness; they did not walk in My statutes, nor did they keep My ordinances to do them, which a man shall do and live by them, and they profaned My Sabbaths. Then I said I would pour out My fury upon them in the wilderness, to accomplish My wrath upon them.

**20:22** Yet I acted so that My name would not be profaned at all before the nations, and I brought them out in their sight.

**20:23** And I lifted up My hand against them in the wilderness, to scatter them among the nations, to disperse them throughout the countries,

**20:24** because they did not do My ordinances, and they rejected My statutes, and they profaned My Sabbaths, and their eyes were after the devices of their fathers.

**20:25** And I also gave them statutes that were not good, and ordinances by which they shall not live.

**20:26** And I will defile them in their gifts, when I pass through all that opens the womb, so that I may destroy them.'

**20:27** Therefore speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Up to this point your fathers provoked Me to anger by their trespasses in which they trespassed against Me.

**20:28** And I brought them into the land which I had lifted up My hand to give them; and they saw every high hill and every shady tree, and they sacrificed there to their gods, and they set there a sweet aroma, and poured out there their drink offerings.

**20:29** And I said to them, "What is Abana, that you enter there?" And they called its name Abana to this very day.'

**20:30** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord: Are you defiling yourselves in the iniquities of your fathers, and do you commit harlotry after their abominations?

**20:31** And in the firstfruits of your gifts, in the dedicated offerings by which you defile yourselves in all your devices to this very day? And shall I answer you, O house of Israel? As I live, says the Lord, I will surely not answer you, nor shall this arise in your spirit.

**20:32** And it shall not be as you say, "We will be like the nations and like the tribes of the earth, to serve wood and stones."

**20:33** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, with a mighty hand, and with an uplifted arm, and with fury poured out, I will reign over you,

**20:34** and I will bring you out from the peoples and receive you from the countries where you were scattered, with a mighty hand and an uplifted arm, and with fury poured out.

**20:35** And I will lead you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.

**20:36** Just as I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will judge you, says the Lord.

**20:37** And I will cause you to pass under My rod, and I will bring you in by number,

**20:38** and I will expose from among you the ungodly and those who have revolted; for out of their sojourning I will bring them forth, yet they shall not enter into the land of Israel; and you shall know that I am the Lord, the Lord.

**20:39** And as for you, O house of Israel, thus says the Lord, the Lord: Put away, each one of you, his practices; and afterward, if you do not listen to Me, yet you shall no longer profane My holy name with your gifts and with your practices,

**20:40** for on My holy mountain, on a high mountain, says the Lord, the Lord, there all the house of Israel shall serve Me to the end. And there I will accept and there I will look upon your firstfruits and the firstfruits of your dedicated offerings, with all your consecrated things.

**20:41** With a sweet aroma I will accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples and receive you from the countries in which you were scattered; and I will be sanctified in you in the sight of the peoples.

**20:42** And you shall know that I am the Lord, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land which I lifted up My hand to give to your fathers.

**20:43** And there you shall remember your ways and your practices by which you defiled yourselves, and you shall beat your faces for all your evils.

**20:44** And you shall know that I am the Lord, when I deal thus with you so that My name may not be profaned according to your evil ways and according to your corrupt practices, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Greek text specifies the seventh year and the fifteenth of the month without naming the fifth month, whereas the Masoretic Text reads the fifth month and the tenth day.
- **20:4** — The Greek uses a first-person verb continuing the oath formula of verse 3 (literally, "if I will avenge them with vengeance"), rather than a second-person question directed to the prophet.
- **20:6** — The Greek reads "a honeycomb" (kerion) rather than "glory" or "beauty," echoing "milk and honey" (repeated in verse 15).
- **20:26** — The Greek uses the future tense ("I will defile them"), reads "when I pass through all that opens the womb," and omits the closing recognition formula found in the Masoretic Text.
- **20:29** — "Abana" is the Greek transliteration of the Hebrew term for "the high place" (ha-bamah), preserved here as a proper name in keeping with the Greek text.
