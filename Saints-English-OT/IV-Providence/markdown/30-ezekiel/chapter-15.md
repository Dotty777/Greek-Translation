## Text

**15:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**15:2** "And you, son of man, what could the wood of the vine become out of all the wood that is among the trees of the forest?

**15:3** Will they take wood from it to make for any work? Will they take a peg from it to hang any vessel upon it?

**15:4** Apart from this, it is given to fire for consumption; the fire consumes its yearly pruning from it, and it fails completely. Will it be useful for any work?

**15:5** Not even while it is still whole shall it be fit for any work; how much less, when fire also consumes it completely, will it still be fit for any work?

**15:6** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: Just as the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to fire for consumption, so have I given the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**15:7** And I will set My face against them; they shall come out from the fire, yet fire shall devour them, and they shall know that I am the Lord when I set My face against them.

**15:8** And I will give the land over to desolation, because they have committed a trespass, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **15:4** — The Greek reads "its yearly pruning" (ten kat' eniauton katharsin), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to "both of its ends."
- **15:7** — The Greek has future tense "they shall come out" and third-person plural "they shall know," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads past tense and second-person plural.
