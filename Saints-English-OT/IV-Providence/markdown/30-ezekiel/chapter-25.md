## Text

**25:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**25:2** "Son of man, set your face against the sons of Ammon, and prophesy against them,

**25:3** and you shall say to the sons of Ammon, 'Hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord: Because you rejoiced over My sanctuary that it was profaned, and over the land of Israel that it was made desolate, and over the house of Judah that they went into captivity,

**25:4** therefore, behold, I am delivering you to the sons of Kedem for an inheritance, and they shall encamp with their furnishings in you and set their dwellings in you; they shall eat your fruits, and they shall drink your fatness.

**25:5** And I will make the city of Ammon into pastures for camels, and the sons of Ammon into a pasture for sheep; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**25:6** For thus says the Lord: Because you clapped your hand and stamped with your foot, and rejoiced from your soul over the land of Israel,

**25:7** therefore I will stretch out My hand against you and give you for plunder among the nations, and I will utterly destroy you from the peoples, and I will cause you to perish from the lands with destruction; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**25:8** Thus says the Lord: "Because Moab has said, 'Behold, are not the house of Israel and Judah like all the nations?'

**25:9** therefore, behold, I am loosening the shoulder of Moab from his frontier cities, a choice land, the house of Thasimouth, of the ascent of the city by the sea,

**25:10** to the sons of Kedem, together with the sons of Ammon; I have given it to him for an inheritance, so that there may be no remembrance of the sons of Ammon.

**25:11** And upon Moab I will execute vengeance, and they shall know that I am the Lord."

**25:12** Thus says the Lord: "Because Idumea acted in taking vengeance against the house of Judah, and they bore malice and exacted vengeance,

**25:13** therefore thus says the Lord: I will also stretch out My hand against Idumea, and I will utterly destroy man and beast from it, and I will make it desolate; and those pursued from Teman shall fall by the sword.

**25:14** And I will lay My vengeance upon Idumea by the hand of My people Israel, and they shall act in Idumea according to My anger and according to My wrath; and they shall know My vengeance," says the Lord.

**25:15** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Because the foreigners acted in vengeance and rose up in vengeance, rejoicing from the soul to blot out to the very last one,

**25:16** therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I will stretch out My hand against the foreigners, and I will utterly destroy the Cretans, and I will cause the remnant who dwell on the seacoast to perish.

**25:17** And I will execute great acts of vengeance among them, and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I lay My vengeance upon them."

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — "Kedem" transliterates the Hebrew word for "east" as a proper name in the Greek, and "fatness" (pioteta) reflects a different vocalization of the Hebrew word for "milk."
- **25:9** — The Greek renders the Moabite border cities distinctively as "the house of Thasimouth, of the ascent of the city by the sea," differing from the Masoretic Text's Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim.
- **25:12** — The Greek uses the Hellenized name "Idumea" for Edom throughout verses twelve through fourteen, and in verse thirteen renders the Hebrew place name Dedan as the participle "those pursued" (diokomenoi).
- **25:15** — "The foreigners" (hoi allophyloi) is the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines; in verse sixteen, the Greek likewise identifies the Cherethites as "Cretans" (Kretas).
