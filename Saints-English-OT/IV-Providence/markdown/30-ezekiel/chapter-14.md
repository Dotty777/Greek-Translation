## Text

**14:1** And men from the elders of Israel came to me and sat before my face.

**14:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:3** "Son of man, these men have set their thoughts upon their hearts and have placed the punishment of their iniquities before their face. Shall I indeed answer them?

**14:4** Therefore speak to them, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Every man of the house of Israel who sets his thoughts upon his heart and places the punishment of his iniquity before his face, and comes to the prophet, I the Lord will answer him according to those things in which his mind is entangled,

**14:5** so that it may turn aside the house of Israel according to their hearts that are estranged from Me in their devisings.'

**14:6** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: Return and turn away from your practices and from all your impieties, and turn your faces back.

**14:7** For every man of the house of Israel and of the strangers who sojourn in Israel who estranges himself from Me and sets his devisings upon his heart and places the punishment of his iniquity before his face, and comes to the prophet to inquire of him concerning Me, I the Lord will answer him by that in which he is entangled.

**14:8** And I will set My face against that man, and I will make him a desolation and an utter ruin, and I will cut him off from the midst of My people; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**14:9** And if the prophet errs and speaks, I the Lord have led that prophet astray; and I will stretch out My hand against him and will destroy him from the midst of My people Israel.

**14:10** And they shall bear their iniquity; as is the wrongdoing of the one who inquires, so likewise shall the wrongdoing be for the prophet,

**14:11** that the house of Israel may no longer go astray from Me, and that they may no longer be defiled by all their transgressions; and they shall be to Me for a people, and I will be to them for a God, says the Lord.'"

**14:12** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:13** "Son of man, if a land sins against Me by committing a trespass, then I will stretch out My hand against it and break its staff of bread, and I will send famine upon it and cut off from it man and beasts.

**14:14** Even if these three men were in its midst—Noah, Daniel, and Job—they themselves shall be saved by their righteousness, says the Lord.

**14:15** If I also bring evil beasts upon the land and punish it, and it becomes a desolation, and there is no one passing through because of the beasts,

**14:16** even though these three men are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, neither sons nor daughters shall be saved; rather, they alone shall be saved, and the land shall be given to destruction.

**14:17** Or if I also bring a sword upon that land and say, 'Let a sword pass through the land,' and I cut off from it man and beast,

**14:18** even though these three men are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, they shall by no means deliver sons or daughters; they alone shall be saved.

**14:19** Or if I also send death upon that land, and pour out My fury upon it in blood to destroy from it man and beast,

**14:20** even though Noah, Daniel, and Job are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, neither sons nor daughters shall be left behind; they themselves shall deliver their own souls by their righteousness.

**14:21** Thus says the Lord: Even if I send My four grievous judgments—sword and famine and evil beasts and death—upon Jerusalem to destroy from it man and beast,

**14:22** yet behold, there are left behind in it those of hers who have been rescued, who bring out from it sons and daughters; behold, they are coming out to you, and you shall see their ways

**14:23** and their devisings; and you shall know that I have not done in vain all that I have done in it, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — Here and in verses 4-7, the Greek translates the Hebrew expressions for idols and stumbling blocks as "thoughts" or "devisings" (dianoemata, enthymemata) and "punishment" (kolasin).
- **14:5** — The Greek uses a third-person singular verb (plagiase, "may turn aside"), referring grammatically to "his mind" from verse 4 (or to the prophet/man), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "to seize."
- **14:19** — Greek thanatos ("death") is the Septuagint's standard rendering for deadly pestilence in this oracle (also in verse 21).
- **14:22** — The Greek has an active participle/verb ("who bring out") and omits the consolation clause found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text in verses 22-23.
