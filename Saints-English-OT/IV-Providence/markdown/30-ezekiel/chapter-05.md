## Text

**5:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a sword sharper than a barber's razor; you shall acquire it for yourself, and you shall bring it upon your head and upon your beard. And you shall take a balance of weights and divide them:

**5:2** a fourth part you shall kindle in fire in the midst of the city at the fulfillment of the days of the siege, and you shall take a fourth part and burn it up in the midst of it, and a fourth part you shall cut down with a sword all around it, and a fourth part you shall scatter to the wind, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**5:3** And you shall take from there a few in number and wrap them up in the fold of your garment.

**5:4** And from these again you shall take some and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them up in the fire; from it a fire shall go forth. And you shall say to all the house of Israel,

**5:5** 'Thus says the Lord: This is Jerusalem; in the midst of the nations I have set her, and the countries around her.

**5:6** And you shall declare My ordinances to the lawless one from among the nations, and My statutes from the countries that are around her, because they have rejected My ordinances and have not walked in My statutes.

**5:7** Therefore thus says the Lord: Because your occasion is from the nations that are around you, and you have not walked in My statutes, and you have not kept My ordinances, nor have you even acted according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you—

**5:8** therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against you, and I will execute judgment in your midst in the sight of the nations,

**5:9** and I will do in you things which I have not done, and the like of which I will never do again, according to all your abominations.

**5:10** Therefore fathers shall eat children in your midst, and children shall eat fathers; and I will execute judgments in you, and I will scatter all your remnant to every wind.

**5:11** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, surely, because you have defiled My holy things with all your abominations, I also will cast you off; My eye shall not spare, nor will I have mercy.

**5:12** A fourth part of you shall be consumed by death, and a fourth part of you shall be brought to an end by famine in your midst, and a fourth part of you I will scatter to every wind, and a fourth part of you shall fall by the sword all around you, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**5:13** And My fury and My wrath shall be fulfilled upon them, and you shall know that I, the Lord, have spoken in My zeal, when I have fulfilled My wrath upon them.

**5:14** And I will make you a desolation, and your daughters all around you, in the sight of everyone who passes by;

**5:15** and you shall be an object of groaning and wretchedness among the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments in you in the vengeance of My fury. I, the Lord, have spoken,

**5:16** even when I send forth My arrows of famine upon them, and they shall be for utter exhaustion, and I will break your staff of bread.

**5:17** And I will send forth against you famine and evil wild beasts, and I will punish you; and death and blood shall pass through upon you, and I will bring a sword upon you all around. I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Both here and in verse 12 the Septuagint divides the hair and the city's population into four fourths (burning two fourths here, corresponding to death and famine in verse 12), whereas the Masoretic Text divides them into thirds.
- **5:6** — The Greek reads "you shall declare" (ereis) to "the lawless one" (te anomo), diverging from the Masoretic Text's "she has rebelled against My ordinances with wickedness."
- **5:15** — The rare Greek pair stenakte kai delaiste ("an object of groaning and wretchedness") portrays Jerusalem as both lamented and pitiable among the surrounding nations.
