## Text

**3:1** And He said to me, "Son of man, eat up this scroll, and go and speak to the sons of Israel."

**3:2** And He opened wide my mouth, and He fed me the scroll.

**3:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, your mouth shall eat, and your belly shall be filled with this scroll that is given to you." And I ate it, and it became in my mouth like sweet honey.

**3:4** And He said to me, "Son of man, walk and go in to the house of Israel, and speak My words to them;

**3:5** for you are not sent forth to a people of deep speech—to the house of Israel—

**3:6** nor to many peoples of foreign voice or of foreign tongue, nor to those who are thick of tongue, whose words you will not understand; and if I had sent you forth to such as these, they would have listened to you.

**3:7** But the house of Israel will by no means be willing to listen to you, for they do not wish to listen to Me; because all the house of Israel are contentious and hard-hearted.

**3:8** And behold, I have made your face strong over against their faces, and I will strengthen your contention over against their contention,

**3:9** and it shall continually be mightier than rock. Do not be afraid of them, nor be dismayed before their face, for it is a provoking house."

**3:10** And He said to me, "Son of man, receive into your heart all the words that I have spoken with you, and hear with your ears;

**3:11** and walk, go in to the captivity, to the sons of your people; and you shall speak to them and say to them, 'Thus says the Lord,' whether indeed they hear or whether indeed they yield."

**3:12** And the Spirit took me up, and I heard behind me, and I heard the sound of a great earthquake: "Blessed is the glory of the Lord from His place."

**3:13** And I saw the sound of the wings of the living creatures flapping one against another, and the sound of the wheels beside them, and the sound of the earthquake.

**3:14** And the Spirit lifted me up and took me up, and I went in the impulse of my spirit; and the hand of the Lord was mighty upon me.

**3:15** And I went in to the captivity aloft, and went around among those who dwelt by the river Chebar, those who were there; and I sat there seven days, moving about in the midst of them.

**3:16** And it came to pass after the seven days that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**3:17** "Son of man, I have appointed you a watchman to the house of Israel; and you shall hear a word from My mouth, and warn them sternly from Me.

**3:18** When I say to the lawless man, 'You shall surely be put to death,' and you do not warn him, nor speak to warn the lawless man to turn away from his ways so that he may live, that lawless man shall die in his unrighteousness, but his blood I will require from your hand.

**3:19** Yet if you warn the lawless man, and he does not turn away from his lawlessness and from the way, that lawless man shall die in his unrighteousness, but you shall deliver your soul.

**3:20** And when a righteous man turns away from his righteous deeds and commits a transgression, and I set the torment before his face, he shall die; because you did not warn him, he shall also die in his sins, for his righteous deeds shall by no means be remembered; and his blood I will require from your hand.

**3:21** But if you warn the righteous man not to sin, and he does not sin, the righteous man shall surely live, because you warned him; and you shall deliver your own soul."

**3:22** And the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He said to me, "Arise and go out into the plain, and there it shall be spoken to you."

**3:23** And I arose and went out into the plain, and behold, the glory of the Lord stood there, like the vision and like the glory that I had seen by the river Chebar; and I fell upon my face.

**3:24** And the Spirit came upon me and set me upon my feet, and He spoke to me and said to me, "Go in and shut yourself up in the midst of your house.

**3:25** And you, son of man, behold, bonds have been laid upon you, and they shall bind you with them, and you shall by no means go out from the midst of them.

**3:26** And I will bind your tongue fast, and you shall become mute, and you shall not be to them a man who reproves, for it is a provoking house.

**3:27** And when I speak to you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Let him who hears, hear, and let him who disobeys, disobey,' for it is a provoking house."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Septuagint reads third-person "He opened" rather than the Hebrew MT's first-person "I opened."
- **3:7** — The Septuagint renders "contentious" here and "contention" in 3:8 where the Hebrew MT speaks of a hard "forehead."
- **3:9** — Greek dia pantos ("continually") reflects a reading of the Hebrew consonantal text as "always" rather than "adamant" or "emery."
- **3:15** — Greek meteoros ("aloft" or "suspended in midair") interprets the Hebrew expression that the MT treats as the place name Tel-abib.
