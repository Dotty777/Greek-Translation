## Text

**40:1** And it came to pass in the twenty-fifth year of our captivity, in the first month, on the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was captured, on that very day the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He brought me

**40:2** in a vision of God into the land of Israel, and He set me on an exceedingly high mountain, and upon it was like the structure of a city directly opposite.

**40:3** And He brought me in there, and behold, a man, and his appearance was like the appearance of gleaming bronze, and in his hand was a builders' cord and a measuring reed; and he was standing at the gate.

**40:4** And the man said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? See with your eyes and hear with your ears, and set upon your heart all that I show you, for you have come in here that I might show them to you; and you shall show all that you see to the house of Israel."

**40:5** And behold, there was an enclosing wall on the outside of the house all around, and in the hand of the man was a measuring reed of six cubits, each a cubit and a handbreadth; and he measured the outer wall, its width equal to the reed, and its height equal to the reed.

**40:6** And he entered the gate facing toward the east by seven steps, and he measured the vestibule of the gate as equal to the reed,

**40:7** and the side chamber equal to the reed in length and equal to the reed in width, and the vestibule between the side chambers six cubits; and the second side chamber equal to the reed in width and equal to the reed in length, and the vestibule five cubits;

**40:8** and the third side chamber equal to the reed in width and equal to the reed in length,

**40:9** and the vestibule of the gateway near the vestibule of the gate eight cubits, and the posts two cubits; and the vestibules of the gate were on the inside,

**40:10** and the side chambers of the gate were side chamber opposite side chamber, three on this side and three on that side, and one measure for the three, and one measure for the vestibules on this side and on that side.

**40:11** And he measured the width of the doorway of the gateway, ten cubits, and the breadth of the gateway, thirteen cubits.

**40:12** And a cubit drew inward in front of the side chambers on this side and on that side, and the side chamber was six cubits on this side and six cubits on that side.

**40:13** And he measured the gate from the wall of the side chamber to the wall of the side chamber, a width of twenty-five cubits; this gate faced the opposite gate.

**40:14** And the forecourt of the vestibule of the gate on the outside was twenty cubits, with side chambers of the gate all around.

**40:15** And from the forecourt of the gate on the outside to the forecourt of the vestibule of the gate on the inside was fifty cubits.

**40:16** And there were concealed windows upon the side chambers and upon the vestibules inside the gate of the court all around; and likewise for the vestibules there were windows all around on the inside, and upon the vestibule were palm trees on this side and on that side.

**40:17** And he brought me into the inner court, and behold, there were chambers and colonnades of the court all around, thirty chambers in the colonnades,

**40:18** and the porticoes were behind the gates; according to the length of the gates was the lower colonnade.

**40:19** And he measured the width of the court from the forecourt of the outer gate on the inside to the forecourt of the gate facing outward, one hundred cubits, of the gate facing toward the east. And he led me toward the north,

**40:20** and behold, a gate facing toward the north belonging to the outer court; and he measured it, both its length and its width,

**40:21** and the side chambers, three on this side and three on that side, and the posts and the vestibules and its palm trees. And it was according to the measurements of the gate facing toward the east: fifty cubits was its length, and twenty-five cubits was its breadth.

**40:22** And its windows and the vestibules and its palm trees were like the gate facing toward the east; and by seven steps they went up to it, and the vestibules were on the inside.

**40:23** And there was a gate for the inner court facing the gate of the north, in the same manner as the gate facing toward the east; and he measured the court from gate to gate, one hundred cubits.

**40:24** And he led me toward the south, and behold, a gate facing toward the south; and he measured it and the side chambers and the posts and the vestibules according to these measurements.

**40:25** And its windows and the vestibules all around were like the windows of the vestibule; fifty cubits was its length, and twenty-five cubits was its breadth.

**40:26** And it had seven steps and vestibules on the inside, and palm trees upon it, one on this side and one on that side upon the posts.

**40:27** And there was a gate opposite the gate of the inner court toward the south; and he measured the court from gate to gate, one hundred cubits in breadth toward the south.

**40:28** And he brought me into the inner court of the gate toward the south, and he measured the gate according to these measurements,

**40:29** and the side chambers and the posts and the vestibules according to these measurements, and there were windows for it and for the vestibule all around; fifty cubits was its length, and the breadth twenty-five cubits

**40:31** of the vestibule toward the outer court, and palm trees on the posts, and eight steps.

**40:32** And he brought me into the gate facing toward the east, and he measured it according to these measurements,

**40:33** and the side chambers and the posts and the vestibules according to these measurements, and there were windows for it and the vestibules all around; fifty cubits was its length, and its breadth twenty-five cubits.

**40:34** And the vestibules were toward the inner court, and palm trees were upon the posts on this side and on that side, and it had eight steps.

**40:35** And he brought me into the gate toward the north, and he measured according to these measurements,

**40:36** and the side chambers and the posts and the vestibules, and there were windows for it all around and for its vestibule; fifty cubits was its length, and its breadth twenty-five cubits;

**40:37** and the vestibules were toward the outer court, and palm trees were on the posts on this side and on that side, and it had eight steps.

**40:38** Its chambers and its doorways and its vestibules at the second gate had a drain,

**40:39** so that they might slay in it the offerings for sin and for ignorance.

**40:40** And behind the channel of the burnt offerings of the gate facing toward the north were two tables toward the east behind the second gate, and at the vestibule of the gate were two tables toward the east.

**40:41** Four on this side and four on that side behind the gate; upon them they shall slay the sacrifices, opposite the eight tables of the sacrifices.

**40:42** And four tables of the burnt offerings were of hewn stone, a cubit and a half in width, and two and a half cubits in length, and one cubit in height; upon them they shall place the instruments with which they slay the burnt offerings and the sacrifices there.

**40:43** And they shall have a hewn rim of one handbreadth all around on the inside, and over the tables above, coverings to shelter them from the rain and from the drought.

**40:44** And he brought me into the inner court, and behold, there were two halls in the inner court: one behind the gate facing toward the north, opening toward the south, and one behind the gate toward the south, facing toward the north.

**40:45** And he said to me, "This hall that faces toward the south is for the priests who keep the charge of the house;

**40:46** and the hall that faces toward the north is for the priests who keep the charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, who draw near from Levi to the Lord to minister to Him."

**40:47** And he measured the court: a length of one hundred cubits and a breadth of one hundred cubits on its four sides; and the altar was opposite the house.

**40:48** And he brought me into the vestibule of the house. And he measured the post of the vestibule, five cubits in width on this side and five cubits on that side; and the breadth of the doorway was fourteen cubits, and the shoulders of the door of the vestibule were three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side.

**40:49** And the length of the vestibule was twenty cubits, and the breadth twelve cubits; and by ten steps they went up to it, and there were pillars by the vestibule, one on this side and one on that side.

## Translator Notes

- **40:6** — Throughout chapter 40, the Greek text retains transliterated Hebrew architectural terms (ailam, thee/theim, thaielatha, aileu, ailammon, and ail), which are rendered here as vestibule(s), side chamber(s), and post(s) for clarity. Here the Greek also specifies seven steps, where the Hebrew does not give a number.
- **40:17** — The Greek reads "the inner court" here (and again in verse 34), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the outer court," and renders the surrounding structure as "colonnades" (peristyla) rather than a stone pavement.
- **40:31** — Verse 30 is absent from the Greek text, with verse 31 continuing directly from the measurements of verse 29.
- **40:38** — Verses 38–40 follow the shorter Greek text, which describes a "drain" (ekrysis) at the second gate, a "channel" (rhoax) of the burnt offerings, and sacrifices "for sin and for ignorance."
- **40:43** — The Greek describes a hewn rim of one handbreadth and "coverings" (stegas) over the tables to shelter them from rain and drought, differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text; verses 48–49 likewise preserve the Greek measurements of fourteen cubits, twelve cubits, and ten steps.
