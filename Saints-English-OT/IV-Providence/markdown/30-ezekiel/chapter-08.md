## Text

**8:1** And it came to pass in the sixth year, in the fifth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was sitting in the house, and the elders of Judah were sitting before me. And the hand of the Lord came upon me,

**8:2** and I looked, and behold, a likeness of a man: from His loins downward was fire, and from His loins upward was like the appearance of amber.

**8:3** And He stretched out the likeness of a hand and took me up by the crown of my head; and the Spirit lifted me up between earth and heaven and brought me to Jerusalem in a vision of God, to the entrance of the gate facing toward the north, where the pillar of the one who acquires stood.

**8:4** And behold, there was the glory of the Lord God of Israel, according to the vision which I saw in the plain.

**8:5** And He said to me, "Son of man, lift up your eyes toward the north." So I lifted up my eyes toward the north, and behold, from the north toward the gate of the one facing east.

**8:6** And He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen what these are doing? They are committing great lawless deeds here, so as to keep away from My sanctuary; and you shall yet see greater lawless deeds."

**8:7** And He brought me to the entrance of the court,

**8:8** and He said to me, "Son of man, dig." And I dug, and behold, a door.

**8:9** And He said to me, "Go in and see the lawless deeds which these are committing here."

**8:10** So I went in and looked, and behold, vain abominations and all the idols of the house of Israel portrayed upon it all around.

**8:11** And seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel were there; Jeconiah the son of Shaphan stood in the midst of them before them, and each man had his censer in his hand, and the vapor of the incense was ascending.

**8:12** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel are doing, each one of them in their hidden chamber? For they have said, 'The Lord does not see; the Lord has forsaken the land.'"

**8:13** And He said to me, "You shall yet see greater lawless deeds which these are committing."

**8:14** And He brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of the Lord facing toward the north, and behold, women were sitting there, lamenting for Tammuz.

**8:15** And He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen? And you shall yet see practices greater than these."

**8:16** And He brought me into the inner court of the house of the Lord; and at the entrance of the temple of the Lord, between the porches and the altar, were about twenty men, with their backs toward the temple of the Lord and their faces opposite, and these were worshiping the sun.

**8:17** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? Is it a light thing to the house of Judah to commit the lawless deeds which they have committed here? For they have filled the land with lawlessness, and behold, they are as those who mock.

**8:18** And I will deal with them in fury; My eye shall not spare, nor will I have mercy."

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek dates this vision to the "fifth month" (MT: sixth month), and in verse 2 describes "a likeness of a man" (MT: "fire").
- **8:3** — "The pillar of the one who acquires" (hē stēlē tou ktōmenou) reflects the Greek translator's rendering of the Hebrew root as "acquire" rather than "jealousy."
- **8:11** — The Greek gives the name "Jeconiah" (Iechonias), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jaazaniah."
- **8:16** — The Greek numbers the men as "about twenty" (MT: twenty-five), transliterates the architectural term for porches as plural ailam, and in verse 17 renders the closing gesture as "those who mock" (myktērizontes, literally turning up the nose).
