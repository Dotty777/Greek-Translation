## Text

**36:1** "And you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel, and say to the mountains of Israel, 'Hear the word of the Lord:

**36:2** Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Because the enemy has said against you, "Aha! Eternal wastes have become a possession for us,"'

**36:3** therefore prophesy and say, 'Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Because you were dishonored and hated by those around you, so that you became a possession for the rest of the nations, and you came up as talk upon the tongue and as a reproach to the nations;

**36:4** therefore, O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord: Thus says the Lord to the mountains and to the hills, and to the ravines and to the brooks, and to the desolate and ruined places, and to the forsaken cities, and they have become a plunder and a trampling ground to the remaining nations all around;

**36:5** therefore thus says the Sovereign Lord: Surely in the fire of My wrath I have spoken against the rest of the nations and against all Idumea, because they gave My land to themselves as a possession with gladness, dishonoring souls so as to lay it waste in plunder.'

**36:6** Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains and to the hills, and to the ravines and to the valleys, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I have spoken in My jealousy and in My wrath, because you have borne the reproaches of the nations.

**36:7** Therefore I will lift up My hand against the nations that are all around you; they themselves shall bear their dishonor.

**36:8** But as for you, O mountains of Israel, My people shall eat your grapes and your fruit, for they are hoping to come.

**36:9** For behold, I am for you, and I will look upon you, and you shall be tilled and sown;

**36:10** and I will multiply men upon you, all the house of Israel completely; and the cities shall be inhabited, and the desolate place shall be built.

**36:11** And I will multiply upon you men and beasts, and I will cause you to be inhabited as in your beginning, and I will do good to you just as in your former times; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**36:12** And I will bring forth men upon you, My people Israel, and they shall inherit you, and you shall be a possession for them; and you shall never again be bereaved of them.

**36:13** Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Because they have said to you, "You are a devourer of men, and you have been made childless by your nation,"

**36:14** therefore you shall devour men no longer, and you shall no longer bereave your nation, says the Sovereign Lord.

**36:15** And the dishonor of the nations shall no longer be heard against you, and you shall by no means bear the reproaches of peoples, says the Sovereign Lord.'"

**36:16** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**36:17** "Son of man, the house of Israel dwelt upon their own land, and they defiled it by their way, and by their idols, and by their uncleannesses; and their way before My face became like the uncleanness of a woman in her impurity.

**36:18** And I poured out My wrath upon them,

**36:19** and I scattered them among the nations, and I winnowed them into the countries; according to their way and according to their sin I judged them.

**36:20** And they went into the nations wherever they went, and they profaned My holy name, in that it was said of them, 'These are the people of the Lord, and they have gone out from His land.'

**36:21** And I spared them for the sake of My holy name, which the house of Israel profaned among the nations where they went.

**36:22** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord: I do not do this for you, O house of Israel, but rather for the sake of My holy name, which you profaned among the nations where you went.

**36:23** And I will sanctify My great name, which was profaned among the nations, which you profaned in their midst; and the nations shall know that I am the Lord, when I am sanctified in you before their eyes.

**36:24** And I will take you out of the nations, and gather you out of all the lands, and bring you into your own land.

**36:25** And I will sprinkle clean water upon you, and you shall be cleansed from all your uncleannesses and from all your idols, and I will cleanse you;

**36:26** and I will give to you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you; and I will remove the stony heart from your flesh, and I will give to you a heart of flesh.

**36:27** And I will put My Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in My ordinances, and to guard My judgments and perform them.

**36:28** And you shall dwell in the land which I gave to your fathers; and you shall be to Me a people, and I will be to you God.

**36:29** And I will save you from all your uncleannesses; and I will summon the grain and multiply it, and I will bring no famine upon you.

**36:30** And I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field, so that you may not receive the reproach of famine among the nations.

**36:31** And you shall remember your evil ways and your practices that were not good; and you shall be filled with loathing before them in your iniquities and for their abominations.

**36:32** Not for your sake am I acting, says the Sovereign Lord; it is known to you. Be ashamed and confounded because of your ways, O house of Israel.

**36:33** Thus says Adonai, the Lord: In the day when I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will also cause the cities to be inhabited, and the desolate places shall be built.

**36:34** And the desolate land shall be tilled, whereas it had become desolate before the eyes of every passerby.

**36:35** And they shall say, "That desolate land has become like a garden of delight, and the waste, desolate, and overturned cities have been fortified and inhabited."

**36:36** And the nations, as many as are left remaining around you, shall know that I, the Lord, have rebuilt the demolished places and planted the desolate places; I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it.

**36:37** Thus says Adonai, the Lord: Yet for this I will be inquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them: I will multiply them like sheep, even men,

**36:38** like holy sheep, like the sheep of Jerusalem in her feasts; so shall the desolate cities be full of flocks of men, and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — The Greek reads kyrios Kyrios (literally "lord Lord"), rendered "the Sovereign Lord" to distinguish it from simple Kyrios ("the Lord"). The Septuagint also reads "eternal wastes" (erema aionia), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "ancient heights."
- **36:8** — The Septuagint reads "your grapes" (ten staphylen) and "they are hoping to come" (elpizousin tou elthein), differing from the Hebrew "your branches" and "they are near to come."
- **36:31** — The Greek text employs third-person pronouns (auton, "their") in "before them" and "for their abominations," preserved faithfully here from the Septuagint.
- **36:33** — Here and in verse 37 the Greek transliterates Hebrew Adonai as adonai Kyrios ("Adonai, the Lord") rather than rendering it as kyrios Kyrios. In verse 35, "garden of delight" (kepos tryphes) translates the Hebrew name Eden.
