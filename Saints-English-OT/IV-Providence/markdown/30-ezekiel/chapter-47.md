## Text

**47:1** Then he brought me to the entrance of the house; and behold, water was flowing out from underneath the open court toward the east, for the front of the house faced east; and the water was coming down from the right side, from the south, to the altar.

**47:2** And he brought me out by the way of the north gate, and led me around by the outside way to the gate of the court that faces east; and behold, the water was rushing down from the right side,

**47:3** just as a man goes out opposite. And a measure was in his hand; and he measured one thousand with the measure, and he passed through in the water—water of release;

**47:4** and he measured one thousand, and he passed through in the water—water up to the thighs; and he measured one thousand, and he passed through water up to the loins.

**47:5** And he measured one thousand, and he could not pass through, for it was surging violently like a torrent that they shall not cross.

**47:6** And he said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man?" And he led me to the bank of the river

**47:7** upon my return; and behold, along the bank of the river were very many trees on this side and on that side.

**47:8** And he said to me, "This water goes out toward Galilee to the east, and went down into Arabia, and came to the sea, to the water of the outlet, and it shall heal the waters;

**47:9** and it shall be that every living soul of the creatures that swarm, wherever the river comes there, shall live; and there shall be very many fish there, because this water has come there, and it shall heal and live; everything to which the river comes there shall live.

**47:10** And fishermen shall stand there from En Gedi to En Eglaim; it shall be a drying place for dragnets, it shall be by itself; and its fish shall be like the fish of the Great Sea, a very great multitude.

**47:11** And at its outlet and at its backwater and at its overflow they shall not be healed; they are given over to salt.

**47:12** And along the river, upon its bank on this side and on that side, every tree for food shall grow up; nothing upon it shall grow old, nor shall its fruit fail; in its newness it shall bear firstfruits, because these waters of theirs flow out from the sanctuary; and their fruit shall be for food, and their growth for healing."

**47:13** Thus says the Lord God: "These are the borders of the land that you shall inherit: for the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel, an addition of a measuring line.

**47:14** And you shall inherit it, each one just like his brother, the land concerning which I lifted up My hand to give it to their fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

**47:15** And these are the borders of the land on the north side: from the Great Sea that goes down and divides around, by the entrance of Hema, Seldamma,

**47:16** Abthera, Sebram, Heliam, between the borders of Damascus and between the borders of Hamath, the court of Saunan, which are above the borders of Hauranitis.

**47:17** These are the borders from the sea: from the court of Enan, the borders of Damascus, and the regions to the north.

**47:18** And the regions to the east: between Loranitis and Damascus, and between Gileaditis and the land of Israel, the Jordan forms the border to the sea that is east of Phoenicon; these are the regions to the east.

**47:19** And the regions to the south and southwest: from Teman and Phoenicon to the water of Marimoth Kadem, extending along to the Great Sea;

**47:20** this part is south and southwest. This part the Great Sea borders, as far as opposite the entrance of Hamath, even to its entrance; these are the regions toward the sea of Hamath.

**47:21** And you shall divide this land for them, for the tribes of Israel.

**47:22** You shall cast it by lot for yourselves and for the proselytes who sojourn in your midst, who have fathered sons in your midst, and they shall be to you as native-born among the sons of Israel; with you they shall eat in an inheritance in the midst of the tribes of Israel,

**47:23** and they shall be in the tribe of the proselytes, among the proselytes who are with them; there you shall give an inheritance to them," says the Lord God.

## Translator Notes

- **47:3** — "Water of release" renders Greek hydor apheseos (which can also refer to a water channel or discharge), where the Masoretic Text reads "ankle-deep water"; in verse 4 the Greek likewise reads "thighs" rather than "knees."
- **47:8** — The Greek renders the Hebrew geographical terms gelilah ("region") and Arabah as the proper names "Galilee" and "Arabia."
- **47:12** — "Nothing upon it shall grow old" and "their growth" (anabasis) follow the Greek text closely where the Masoretic Text explicitly mentions leaves.
- **47:13** — "An addition of a measuring line" reflects the Greek etymological rendering of the Hebrew name Joseph as "addition" (prosthesis).
- **47:15** — Several place names in verses 15-19 preserve distinctive Septuagint forms (Hema, Seldamma, Abthera, Sebram, Heliam, court of Saunan, Hauranitis, Loranitis, Gileaditis, Phoenicon, Teman, and Marimoth Kadem) that differ from the Masoretic Text.
- **47:22** — The Greek reads "they shall eat" (phagontai) where the Masoretic Text has "they shall fall [by lot]," and in verse 23 reads "in the tribe of the proselytes, among the proselytes who are with them."
