## Text

**6:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:2** "Son of man, set your face steadfastly toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them,

**6:3** and you shall say, 'O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord to the mountains and to the hills, and to the ravines and to the valleys: Behold, I am bringing a sword upon you, and your high places shall be utterly destroyed,

**6:4** and your altars and your sacred precincts shall be shattered, and I will cast down your slain before your idols,

**6:5** and I will scatter your bones all around your altars.

**6:6** In all your dwelling place the cities shall be laid waste and the high places shall be made desolate, so that your altars may be utterly destroyed, and your idols shall be shattered, and your sacred precincts may be swept away,

**6:7** and the slain shall fall in your midst, and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**6:8** When there come to be among the nations some of you who escape from the sword, and in your scattering throughout the countries,

**6:9** then those of you who escape shall remember Me among the nations where they were carried captive. I have sworn against their heart that plays the harlot away from Me, and against their eyes that play the harlot after their practices, and they shall beat their faces for all their abominations;

**6:10** and they shall know that I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

**6:11** Thus says the Lord: "Clap with your hand and stamp with your foot, and say, 'Aha! Aha!' over all the abominations of the house of Israel; by the sword and by death and by famine they shall fall.

**6:12** He who is near shall fall by the sword, and he who is far off shall perish by death, and he who is besieged shall come to an end by famine; and I will fulfill My wrath upon them.

**6:13** And you shall know that I am the Lord, when your slain lie in the midst of your idols, all around your altars, upon every high hill and beneath every shady tree, where they offered a pleasing aroma to all their idols.

**6:14** And I will stretch out My hand against them, and I will make the land a desolation and a ruin, from the wilderness of Deblatha throughout all the habitation; and you shall know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Greek temene ("sacred precincts" or "shrines") translates the Hebrew term for incense altars, here and in verse 6.
- **6:9** — The Septuagint reads "I have sworn" (omomoka) against their unfaithful heart and "they shall beat their faces," diverging from the Masoretic Text's "I was crushed" and "they will loathe themselves."
- **6:11** — The Greek exclamation Euge, euge ("Aha! Aha!" or "Well done! Well done!") conveys sharp irony and derision over Israel's abominations.
- **6:14** — "Deblatha" preserves the Septuagint place-name Deblatha, corresponding to "Diblathah" (often identified with Riblah in Syria) in the Masoretic Text.
