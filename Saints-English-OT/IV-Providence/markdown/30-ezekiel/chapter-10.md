## Text

**10:1** And I looked, and behold, above the firmament that was over the head of the cherubim, like a sapphire stone, the likeness of a throne was above them.

**10:2** Then He spoke to the man clothed with the robe, "Go in among the wheels that are underneath the cherubim, fill your hands with coals of fire from the midst of the cherubim, and scatter them over the city." And he went in before my sight.

**10:3** Now the cherubim were standing on the right side of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.

**10:4** Then the glory of the Lord departed from the cherubim to the open court of the house; and the cloud filled the house, and the court was filled with the radiance of the glory of the Lord.

**10:5** And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God Shaddai speaking.

**10:6** And it came to pass, when He commanded the man clothed with the holy robe, saying, "Take fire from the midst of the wheels, from the midst of the cherubim," that he went in and stood beside the wheels.

**10:7** And he stretched forth his hand into the midst of the fire that was in the midst of the cherubim, and took some and put it into the hands of the one clothed with the holy robe; and he received it and went out.

**10:8** And I saw the cherubim: the likeness of the hands of men was underneath their wings.

**10:9** And I looked, and behold, four wheels were standing beside the cherubim, one wheel beside one cherub; and the appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of a carbuncle stone.

**10:10** And as for their appearance, all four had one likeness, just as when a wheel is in the midst of a wheel.

**10:11** When they went, they went toward their four sides; they did not turn as they went, for toward whatever place the foremost one looked, they went, and they did not turn as they went.

**10:12** And their backs, and their hands, and their wings, and the wheels were full of eyes all around on the four wheels.

**10:13** As for these wheels, they were called Gelgel in my hearing.

**10:15** And the cherubim were this living creature that I saw by the river Chebar.

**10:16** And when the cherubim went, the wheels went, and these were beside them; and when the cherubim lifted up their wings to rise up from the earth, their wheels did not turn.

**10:17** When they stood still, these stood still; and when they rose up, these rose up with them, for the spirit of life was in them.

**10:18** Then the glory of the Lord went forth from the house and mounted upon the cherubim.

**10:19** And the cherubim lifted up their wings and rose up from the earth before my sight; as they went forth, the wheels also were beside them; and they stood at the entrance of the gate of the house of the Lord facing opposite, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.

**10:20** This is the living creature that I saw beneath the God of Israel by the river Chebar, and I knew that they were cherubim.

**10:21** Each one had four faces, and each one had eight wings, and the likeness of the hands of a man was underneath their wings.

**10:22** And as for the likeness of their faces, these are the faces that I saw beneath the glory of the God of Israel by the river Chebar; and each one of them went straight forward.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Greek aithrion denotes an open court or atrium of the temple, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "threshold."
- **10:5** — Greek theou Saddai preserves the transliterated Hebrew divine title Shaddai ("the Almighty").
- **10:13** — Greek Gelgel transliterates Hebrew galgal ("whirling wheel"); verse 14 of the Hebrew text (listing the four faces) is absent from the Greek text.
- **10:21** — The Greek text reads "eight wings" for each cherub, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "four wings."
