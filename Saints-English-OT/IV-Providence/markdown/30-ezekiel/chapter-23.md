## Text

**23:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**23:2** "Son of man, there were two women, the daughters of one mother,

**23:3** and they committed harlotry in Egypt in their youth; there their breasts fell, there they were deflowered.

**23:4** And their names were Oholah the elder, and Oholibah her sister. And they became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. And as for their names: Samaria was Oholah, and Jerusalem was Oholibah.

**23:5** And Oholah committed harlotry away from Me, and she lusted after her lovers, after the Assyrians who drew near to her,

**23:6** clothed in blue, governors and commanders; young men and choice ones, all of them horsemen riding upon horses.

**23:7** And she gave her harlotry upon them, all of them choice sons of the Assyrians; and with all whom she lusted after, she defiled herself in all her devices.

**23:8** And she did not forsake her harlotry from Egypt, for they lay with her in her youth, and they deflowered her and poured out their harlotry upon her.

**23:9** Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the sons of the Assyrians, whom she lusted after.

**23:10** They uncovered her shame, they took her sons and her daughters, and her they slew with the sword; and she became a byword among women, and they executed judgments upon her among the daughters.

**23:11** And her sister Oholibah saw it, and she corrupted her lust more than she, and her harlotry more than the harlotry of her sister.

**23:12** She lusted after the sons of the Assyrians, governors and commanders who were near to her, clothed in finely bordered garments, horsemen riding upon horses, all of them choice young men.

**23:13** And I saw that she was defiled; one way belonged to both of them.

**23:14** And she added to her harlotry, and she saw men painted upon the wall, images of Chaldeans, depicted with a stylus,

**23:15** girded with embroidered bands upon their loins, and dyed cloths also upon their heads, all of them having a threefold appearance, the likeness of the sons of the Chaldeans, the land of his birth.

**23:16** And she lusted after them at the sight of her eyes, and she sent messengers to them into the land of the Chaldeans.

**23:17** And the sons of Babylon came to her into the bed of lodgers, and they defiled her in her harlotry; and she was defiled by them, and her soul turned away from them.

**23:18** And she uncovered her harlotry, and she uncovered her shame; and My soul turned away from her, just as My soul turned away from her sister.

**23:19** And you multiplied your harlotry, to call to remembrance the day of your youth, in which you committed harlotry in Egypt,

**23:20** and you lusted after the Chaldeans, whose flesh is like the flesh of donkeys, and whose private parts are the private parts of horses;

**23:21** and you revisited the iniquity of your youth, the things which you did in Egypt in your lodging place, where the breasts of your youth were.

**23:22** Therefore, Oholibah, thus says the Lord: 'Behold, I raise up your lovers against you, from whom your soul turned away, and I will bring them against you from every side:

**23:23** the sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, Phakouk and Soue and Hychoue, and all the sons of the Assyrians with them, choice young men, governors and commanders, all of them triple-ranked officers and men of renown, riding upon horses.

**23:24** And all of them shall come against you from the north, chariots and wheels with a multitude of peoples, shields and bucklers, and he shall set a watch against you all around.

**23:25** And I will set judgment before their face, and they shall execute vengeance upon you with their judgments; and I will set My jealousy against you, and they shall deal with you in the wrath of fury; they shall take away your nose and your ears, and your remnant they shall cast down with the sword. They shall take your sons and your daughters, and fire shall devour your remnant.

**23:26** And they shall strip you of your garments, and they shall take away the ornaments of your boasting.

**23:27** And I will turn away your impieties from you and your harlotry from the land of Egypt, and you shall by no means lift up your eyes toward them, and you shall remember Egypt no more.'

**23:28** For thus says the Lord, the Lord: 'Behold, I deliver you into the hands of those whom you hate, from whom your soul turned away;

**23:29** and they shall deal with you in hatred, and they shall take all your labors and your toils, and you shall be naked and ashamed, and the shame of your harlotry and your impiety shall be uncovered. And your harlotry

**23:30** has done these things to you, because you committed harlotry after the nations, and you defiled yourself with their devices.

**23:31** You have walked in the way of your sister, and I will put her cup into your hands.'

**23:32** Thus says the Lord: 'You shall drink the cup of your sister, the deep and the wide, and the overflowing one to bring drunkenness to the full,

**23:33** and you shall be filled with faintness; and the cup of desolation is the cup of your sister, and you shall drink it,

**23:34** and her feasts and her new moons I will turn away; for I have spoken,' says the Lord.

**23:35** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Because you have forgotten Me and have cast Me behind your body, you also receive your impiety and your harlotry.'"

**23:36** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, will you not judge Oholah and Oholibah? And you shall declare to them their iniquities,

**23:37** for they have committed adultery, and blood is in their hands; they committed adultery with their devices, and their children whom they bore to Me they caused to pass to them through the sacrificial fires.

**23:38** Moreover, even these things they did to Me: they defiled My sanctuary and profaned My Sabbaths;

**23:39** and when they slew their children to their idols, they also entered into My sanctuary to profane it. And because they did thus in the midst of My house,

**23:40** and because for the men who came from afar, to whom they sent messengers, as soon as they came, immediately you washed yourself, and painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with ornaments,

**23:41** and you sat upon a spread couch, with a table adorned before it, and they made merry with the incense and My oil,

**23:42** and they struck up a sound of harmony, even with men from a multitude of people coming out of the wilderness; and they put bracelets upon their hands and a crown of boasting upon their heads.

**23:43** And I said, 'Do they not commit adultery in these things?' And she herself also committed harlotry with the deeds of a harlot;

**23:44** and they went in to her; just as men go in to a harlot woman, so they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah to commit iniquity.

**23:45** And righteous men, even they shall execute vengeance upon them with the vengeance of an adulteress and with the vengeance of blood, because they are adulteresses, and blood is in their hands.

**23:46** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: 'Bring up a multitude against them, and put tumult and plunder among them,

**23:47** and stone them with the stones of multitudes, and pierce them through with their swords; their sons and their daughters they shall slay, and their houses they shall burn.

**23:48** And I will turn away impiety from the land, and all the women shall be disciplined, and they shall by no means act according to their impieties.

**23:49** And your impiety shall be put upon you, and you shall bear the sins of your devices; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **23:7** — Greek enthymemata (literally "devices" or "contrivances") is the Septuagint's characteristic rendering in Ezekiel for idols (also in vv. 30, 37, and 49).
- **23:15** — Greek opsis trisse literally means "a threefold appearance" (compare trissous, "triple-ranked officers," in v. 23), reflecting a literal Greek rendering of the Hebrew military title for officers of the third rank; the clause also ends with the singular "of his birth."
- **23:20** — The Greek text explicitly reads "the Chaldeans" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "their paramours") and uses aidoia ("private parts") rather than "emission."
- **23:23** — The Greek preserves the Septuagint proper names Phakouk, Soue, and Hychoue, corresponding to Pekod, Shoa, and Koa in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:34** — The Septuagint reads "and her feasts and her new moons I will turn away," differing markedly from the Masoretic Text's imagery of draining the cup, gnawing its sherds, and tearing the breasts.
