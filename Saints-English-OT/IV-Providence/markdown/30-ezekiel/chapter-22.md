## Text

**22:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:2** "And you, son of man, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then declare to her all her lawless deeds,

**22:3** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: O city that sheds blood in her midst so that her time may come, and makes devices against herself to defile herself!

**22:4** By their blood which you have shed you have transgressed, and by your devices which you have made you have been defiled; and you have brought your days near, and have brought on the time of your years. Therefore I have given you over as a reproach to the nations and as a mockery to all the lands that are near to you

**22:5** and to those that are far away from you; and they shall mock at you, O unclean one, renowned and abounding in lawless deeds!

**22:6** Behold, the leaders of the house of Israel, each one with his own kindred, have conspired together in you to shed blood.

**22:7** Father and mother they have reviled in you, and with the sojourner they have dealt in injustices in you; the fatherless and the widow they have oppressed,

**22:8** and My holy things they have despised, and My Sabbaths they have profaned in you.

**22:9** Men who are robbers are in you, in order to shed blood in you; and upon the mountains they have eaten in you; unholy deeds they have practiced in your midst.

**22:10** A father's shame they have uncovered in you, and her who was set apart in uncleanness they have humbled in you.

**22:11** Each one has acted lawlessly toward his neighbor's wife, and each one has defiled his daughter-in-law in ungodliness, and each one has humbled his sister, his father's daughter, in you.

**22:12** Bribes they have taken in you in order to shed blood; interest and excessive gain they have taken in you. And you have brought to completion the fullness of your wickedness in oppression, and Me you have forgotten, says the Lord.

**22:13** Now if I bring My hand upon the things you have accomplished which you have done, and upon your bloodshed that has taken place in your midst,

**22:14** shall your heart endure? Shall your hands prevail in the days in which I deal with you? I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it.

**22:15** And I will scatter you among the nations, and disperse you through the lands, and your uncleanness shall cease from you,

**22:16** and I will take an inheritance in you before the eyes of the nations; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**22:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:18** "Son of man, behold, the house of Israel has become to Me mixed together—all of them with bronze and iron and tin and lead; in the midst of silver it is mixed.

**22:19** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: Because you have become one mixture, therefore I will receive you into the midst of Jerusalem.

**22:20** Just as silver and bronze and iron and tin and lead are received into the midst of a furnace, to blow fire upon it so that it may be smelted, so I will receive you in My wrath, and I will gather and smelt you,

**22:21** and I will blow upon you in the fire of My wrath, and you shall be smelted in the midst of her.

**22:22** In the manner that silver is smelted in the midst of a furnace, so shall you be smelted in the midst of her; and you shall know that I, the Lord, have poured out My fury upon you.'"

**22:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:24** "Son of man, say to her, 'You are a land that is not watered with rain, nor has rain come upon you in the day of wrath,

**22:25** whose leaders in her midst are like roaring lions seizing prey, devouring souls by might and taking tribute; and your widows have been multiplied in your midst.'

**22:26** And her priests have rejected My law and profaned My holy things; between the holy and the profane they made no distinction, and between the unclean and the clean they made no distinction, and from My Sabbaths they veiled their eyes, and I was profaned in their midst.

**22:27** Her rulers in her midst are like wolves seizing prey to shed blood, that with greed they may gain dishonest profit.

**22:28** And her prophets who plaster them shall fall, seeing vain visions, divining lies, saying, 'Thus says the Lord,' when the Lord has not spoken—

**22:29** extorting the people of the land with injustice and plundering spoils, oppressing the poor and the needy, and not dealing with the sojourner with justice.

**22:30** And I sought from among them a man conducting himself uprightly and standing before My face wholeheartedly in the time of the land, so that I should not utterly blot her out; and I found none.

**22:31** And I have poured out My fury upon her in the fire of My wrath to consume her; their own ways I have brought upon their own heads, says the Lord, the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — Greek enthymemata ("devices" or "inventions") is the Septuagint's characteristic rendering in Ezekiel for the Hebrew term for idols; it is retained literally here and in 22:4.
- **22:16** — The Greek reads "I will take an inheritance in you" (interpreting the Hebrew consonant root as "inherit" in the first person), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "you shall be profaned in yourself."
- **22:25** — The Greek reads "whose leaders" rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "a conspiracy of her prophets."
- **22:28** — The Greek renders "who plaster them shall fall" (relating the Hebrew term for whitewash to the verb "fall"), and in 22:29 places "the people of the land" in the accusative as the victims of oppression rather than its perpetrators.
