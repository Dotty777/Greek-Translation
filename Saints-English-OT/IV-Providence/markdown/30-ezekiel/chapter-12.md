## Text

**12:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:2** "Son of man, you dwell in the midst of their iniquities, who have eyes to see and do not see, and have ears to hear and do not hear; for it is a provoking house.

**12:3** Therefore, son of man, prepare for yourself baggage for captivity by day in their sight, and you shall be led captive from your place to another place in their sight, so that they may know that it is a provoking house.

**12:4** And you shall bring out your baggage, baggage for captivity, by day before their eyes; and you yourself shall go out in the evening as a captive goes forth

**12:5** in their sight; dig for yourself into the wall, and you shall go out through it.

**12:6** In their sight you shall be lifted up upon shoulders, and you shall go out concealed; you shall cover your face and shall by no means see the land, for I have set you as a sign to the house of Israel."

**12:7** And I did so, according to all that He commanded me: I brought out baggage for captivity by day, and in the evening I dug through the wall for myself, and I went out concealed; I was lifted up upon shoulders in their sight.

**12:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:9** "Son of man, has not the house of Israel, the provoking house, said to you, 'What are you doing?'

**12:10** Say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: The ruler and the leader in Jerusalem, and to all the house of Israel who are in their midst'—

**12:11** say, 'I perform signs; just as I have done, so shall it be done to him: they shall go into deportation and into captivity.

**12:12** And the ruler in their midst shall be lifted up upon shoulders, and he shall go out concealed through the wall, and he shall dig through so that he may go out through it; he shall cover his face so that he may not be seen by any eye, and he himself shall not see the land.

**12:13** And I will spread My net over him, and he shall be caught in My snare; and I will bring him to Babylon, to the land of the Chaldeans, yet he shall not see it, and there he shall die.

**12:14** And all who are around him, his helpers, and all who uphold him, I will scatter to every wind; and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**12:15** And they shall know that I am the Lord, when I scatter them among the nations and disperse them in the countries.

**12:16** Yet I will leave of them men few in number from the sword, from famine, and from death, so that they may recount all their iniquities among the nations where they have gone; and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**12:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:18** "Son of man, you shall eat your bread with anguish, and drink water with torment and affliction.

**12:19** And you shall say to the people of the land, 'Thus says the Lord to the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They shall eat their bread with scarcity, and drink their water with desolation, so that the land may be made desolate with its fullness, for all who dwell in it are in ungodliness.

**12:20** And their inhabited cities shall be laid waste, and the land shall become a desolation; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**12:21** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:22** "Son of man, what is this proverb that you have in the land of Israel, saying, 'The days are prolonged, and vision has perished'?

**12:23** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: I will also turn away this proverb, and the house of Israel shall no longer speak this proverb'; for you shall speak to them, 'The days have drawn near, and the word of every vision.

**12:24** For there shall no longer be any false vision or one who divines flattering things in the midst of the children of Israel.

**12:25** For I, the Lord, will speak My words; I will speak and I will perform them, and I will no longer delay; for in your days, O provoking house, I will speak a word and perform it, says the Lord.'"

**12:26** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:27** "Son of man, behold, the provoking house of Israel keeps saying, 'The vision that this man sees is for many days to come, and he prophesies of times far off.'

**12:28** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: None of My words that I speak shall be delayed any longer; I will speak and I will perform it, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **12:6** — The Greek uses the passive "you shall be lifted up upon shoulders" and "concealed" (also in verses 7 and 12), where the Hebrew describes bearing baggage on the shoulder in the dark.
- **12:10** — Greek kyrios Kyrios doubles the divine title ("the Lord, the Lord"), and reads "The ruler and the leader" where the Hebrew reads "This burden [concerns] the prince."
- **12:19** — The Greek reads "in ungodliness" (asebeia) where the Hebrew reads "because of the violence."
