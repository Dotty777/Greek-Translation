## Text

**31:1** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**31:2** "Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude, 'To whom have you likened yourself in your loftiness?

**31:3** Behold, Asshur was a cypress in Lebanon, fair in his shoots and lofty in stature, and his top reached into the midst of the clouds.

**31:4** Water nourished him, the deep exalted him; she led her rivers all around his plants, and sent forth her channels to all the trees of the plain.

**31:5** Because of this, his stature was exalted above all the trees of the plain, and his boughs spread wide because of abundant water.

**31:6** In his shoots all the birds of heaven made their nests, and beneath his boughs all the beasts of the plain brought forth their young; in his shadow dwelt every multitude of nations.

**31:7** And he became fair in his loftiness because of the multitude of his boughs, for his roots reached into abundant water.

**31:8** And such cypresses were in the paradise of God; yet the pine trees were not like his shoots, and the fir trees were not like his boughs; no tree in the paradise of God was comparable to him in his beauty

**31:9** because of the multitude of his boughs; and the trees of the paradise of the delight of God envied him.

**31:10** Therefore thus says the Lord: Because you became great in stature and set your top into the midst of the clouds, and I saw when he was exalted,

**31:11** I also delivered him into the hands of a ruler of nations, and he executed his destruction;

**31:12** and pestilent strangers from the nations utterly destroyed him and cast him down upon the mountains; in all the valleys his boughs fell, and his branches were broken in every plain of the earth, and all the peoples of the nations came down from their shelter and dashed him to the ground.

**31:13** Upon his ruin all the birds of heaven rested, and upon his branches were all the beasts of the field,

**31:14** so that all the trees that are in the water might not be exalted in their stature; yet they set their top into the midst of the clouds, and they did not stand in their loftiness toward them; all who drink water—all have been given over to death, to the depth of the earth, in the midst of the sons of men, to those who go down to the pit.

**31:15** Thus says the Sovereign Lord: In the day he went down into Hades, the deep mourned for him, and I halted her rivers and restrained the abundance of water; and Lebanon grew dark over him, and all the trees of the plain fainted over him.

**31:16** At the sound of his fall the nations were shaken, when I brought him down into Hades with those who go down into the pit; and all the trees of delight and the choice trees of Lebanon, all that drink water, comforted him in the earth.

**31:17** For they also went down with him into Hades among those slain by the sword; and his seed, those who dwelt beneath his shelter, perished in the midst of their life.

**31:18** To whom have you been likened? Go down and be brought down with the trees of delight into the depth of the earth; in the midst of the uncircumcised you shall lie down with those slain by the sword. Thus shall it be with Pharaoh and the multitude of his strength, says the Sovereign Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **31:3** — Here and in verse 8, the Greek identifies the tree as a "cypress" (kyparissos) rather than a cedar, and places its top "into the midst of the clouds" (nephelon).
- **31:8** — The Greek text opens without a negation ("And such cypresses were in the paradise of God") before contrasting the pine and fir trees with Asshur's shoots and boughs.
- **31:14** — The Greek shifts into the indicative in the middle clauses ("yet they set their top... and they did not stand"), reflecting a literal rendering of difficult underlying syntax.
- **31:17** — The Greek reads "his seed" (sperma) rather than "his arm" and "perished in the midst of their life" (compare "in the midst of the nations" in the Masoretic Text).
