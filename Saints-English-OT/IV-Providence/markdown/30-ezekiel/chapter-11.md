## Text

**11:1** Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the gate of the house of the Lord that is opposite, looking toward the east; and at the entrance of the gate were about twenty-five men, and I saw in their midst Jechoniah the son of Ezer and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, the leaders of the people.

**11:2** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise vain things and give wicked counsel in this city,

**11:3** who say, 'Have not the houses been built recently? This city is the cauldron, and we are the flesh.'

**11:4** Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O son of man!"

**11:5** Then the Spirit fell upon me and said to me, "Say, 'Thus says the Lord: Thus you have spoken, O house of Israel, and I know the thoughts of your spirit.

**11:6** You have multiplied your dead in this city, and you have filled their streets with the slain.

**11:7** Therefore thus says the Lord: Your dead whom you have struck down in her midst, they are the flesh, and this city is the cauldron; but I will bring you out of her midst.

**11:8** You fear the sword, and I will bring a sword upon you, says the Lord.

**11:9** And I will bring you out of her midst and deliver you into the hands of strangers, and I will execute judgments among you.

**11:10** You shall fall by the sword; on the mountains of Israel I will judge you, and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**11:13** And it came to pass, while I was prophesying, that Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face, and cried out with a loud voice, and said, "Alas, alas, O Lord! Are You making a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"

**11:14** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**11:15** "Son of man, your brethren and the men of your captivity and all the house of Israel has come to an end, concerning whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'You are far away from the Lord; to us the land has been given for an inheritance.'

**11:16** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: I will cast them off among the nations, and I will scatter them into every land, yet I will be to them a little sanctuary in the countries wherever they go.'

**11:17** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: I will also receive them from the nations and gather them from the countries where I have scattered them, and I will give them the land of Israel.

**11:18** And they shall go there and remove all its abominations and all its iniquities from it.

**11:19** And I will give them another heart, and I will put a new spirit within them; and I will take out the stony heart from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

**11:20** that they may walk in My statutes and keep My ordinances and do them; and they shall be My people, and I will be their God.

**11:21** But as for the heart of their abominations and their iniquities, as their heart walked, I have brought their ways upon their own heads, says the Lord.'"

**11:22** Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them, above them.

**11:23** And the glory of the Lord went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that was opposite the city.

**11:24** Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the land of the Chaldeans, to the captivity, in a vision by the Spirit of God. And I went up from the vision that I had seen,

**11:25** and I spoke to the captivity all the words of the Lord that He had shown me.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Greek text names "Jechoniah the son of Ezer," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jaazaniah the son of Azzur."
- **11:3** — The Greek renders the leaders' boast as a rhetorical question ("Have not the houses been built recently?"), differing from the Hebrew ("The time is not near to build houses").
- **11:10** — The Greek reads "on the mountains of Israel" rather than "at the border of Israel" in the Hebrew text; verses 11–12 of the Hebrew text are absent from the Greek.
- **11:15** — The Greek reads "the men of your captivity" and "has come to an end," differing from the Hebrew ("your kinsmen" and "all of it").
- **11:24** — The Greek verb is first-person ("I went up from the vision"), whereas the Hebrew describes the vision ascending from the prophet.
