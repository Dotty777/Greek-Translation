## Text

**35:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**35:2** "Son of man, turn your face toward Mount Seir and prophesy against it,

**35:3** and say, 'Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I am against you, O Mount Seir, and I will stretch out My hand against you, and I will make you a desolation, and you shall be laid waste,

**35:4** and I will bring desolation upon your cities, and you shall be desolate; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**35:5** Because you became an everlasting enemy, and you lay in ambush for the house of Israel with deceit, by the hand of enemies with the sword, in the time of iniquity at the end,

**35:6** therefore, as I live, says the Sovereign Lord, surely you have sinned even to blood, and blood shall pursue you.

**35:7** And I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a waste, and I will destroy from it men and beasts,

**35:8** and I will fill the hills and your valleys with the slain, and in all your plains those slain by the sword shall fall in you.

**35:9** I will make you an everlasting desolation, and your cities shall never again be inhabited; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**35:10** Because you said, "The two nations and the two countries shall be mine, and I will inherit them," yet the Lord is there.

**35:11** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, I will deal with you according to your enmity, and I will make Myself known to you when I judge you,

**35:12** and you shall know that I am the Lord. I have heard the voice of your blasphemies, that you said, "The mountains of Israel are desolate; they have been given to us for food,"

**35:13** and you spoke boastfully against Me with your mouth; I Myself have heard.

**35:14** Thus says the Lord: In the gladness of all the earth I will make you desolate;

**35:15** you shall be desolate, O Mount Seir, and all Idumea, and it shall be utterly consumed; you shall know that I am the Lord their God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:5** — The Greek reads "you lay in ambush for the house of Israel with deceit, by the hand of enemies with the sword," where the Masoretic Hebrew speaks of giving over the sons of Israel to the power of the sword.
- **35:6** — The Greek preserves a shorter oath, "surely you have sinned even to blood, and blood shall pursue you," differing from the longer Masoretic Hebrew wording about preparing Mount Seir for blood.
- **35:7** — The Greek reads "men and beasts" (anthropous kai ktene) where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "the one who passes out and the one who returns."
- **35:15** — "Idumea" (Idoumaia) is the Greek proper name for Edom. The Greek verse is shorter than the Masoretic Hebrew and concludes with "the Lord their God."
