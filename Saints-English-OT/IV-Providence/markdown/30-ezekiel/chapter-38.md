## Text

**38:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**38:2** "Son of man, set your face against Gog and the land of Magog, the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal, and prophesy against him,

**38:3** and say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord:

**38:4** Behold, I am against the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal, and I will gather you and all your host, horses and horsemen, all of them clothed in breastplates, with a great company—shields and helmets and swords:

**38:5** Persians and Ethiopians and Libyans, all of them with helmets and shields,

**38:6** Gomer and all those around him, the house of Togarmah from the uttermost north and all those around him, and many nations with you.

**38:7** Be prepared, prepare yourself, you and all your company who are gathered with you, and you shall be a guard for Me.

**38:8** After many days he shall be prepared, and at the end of years he shall come and enter into the land brought back from the sword, of those gathered from many nations, against the land of Israel, which had become wholly desolate; and he has come forth from the nations, and they shall all dwell in peace.

**38:9** And you shall ascend like rain, and he shall come like a cloud to cover the land, and you shall be there, you and all those around you, and many nations with you.

**38:10** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: And it shall come to pass in that day that matters shall arise in your heart, and you shall devise evil thoughts,

**38:11** and you shall say, "I will go up against a cast-off land; I will come upon those who rest in quietness and dwell in peace, all of them inhabiting a land in which there is neither wall nor bars, and they have no gates,

**38:12** to take plunder and to seize their spoil, to turn my hand back against the desolate place that has been resettled, and against a nation gathered from many nations, who have acquired possessions, who dwell upon the navel of the earth."

**38:13** Sheba and Dedan, and the Carthaginian merchants and all their villages, shall say to you, "Have you come for plunder, to take plunder and to seize spoil? Have you gathered your company to take silver and gold, to carry away possessions, to seize spoil?"'

**38:14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog, 'Thus says the Lord: In that day, when My people Israel are settled in peace, shall you not be roused?

**38:15** And you shall come from your place out of the uttermost north, and many nations with you, all of them riders on horses, a great company and a mighty host,

**38:16** and you shall go up against My people Israel like a cloud to cover the land. It shall be in the last days, and I will bring you up against My land, so that all the nations may know Me, when I am sanctified in you before them.

**38:17** Thus says the Lord, the Lord to Gog: You are he of whom I spoke in former days by the hand of My servants the prophets of Israel, in those days and years, that I would bring you against them.

**38:18** And it shall come to pass in that day, in the day when Gog comes against the land of Israel, says the Lord, the Lord, that My fury shall arise,

**38:19** and My jealousy. In the fire of My wrath I have spoken: Surely in that day there shall be a great earthquake upon the land of Israel,

**38:20** and the fish of the sea, and the birds of heaven, and the beasts of the field, and all the creeping things that creep upon the earth, and all the people who are upon the face of the earth shall be shaken at the presence of the Lord; and the mountains shall be rent apart, and the ravines shall fall, and every wall shall fall to the ground.

**38:21** And I will summon against it even every terror, says the Lord; every man's sword shall be against his brother.

**38:22** And I will judge him with death and with blood, and with flooding rain and hailstones; and I will rain fire and brimstone upon him, and upon all those with him, and upon the many nations with him.

**38:23** And I shall be magnified and sanctified and glorified, and I shall be made known in the sight of many nations; and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **38:3** — The Greek reads kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), a doubled divine title characteristic of Septuagint Ezekiel, rendered literally throughout chapters 38-39.
- **38:7** — The Greek reads "a guard for Me" (moi), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a guard for them."
- **38:11** — The Greek describes the land as "a cast-off land" (gen aperrimmenen), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("a land of unwalled villages").
- **38:13** — The Greek reads "Carthaginian merchants and all their villages," reflecting the Septuagint's identification of Tarshish with Carthage and reading "villages" where the Hebrew reads "young lions."
