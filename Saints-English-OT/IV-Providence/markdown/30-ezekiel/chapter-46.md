## Text

**46:1** Thus says the Lord God: "The gate of the inner court that faces toward the east shall be shut during the six working days; on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.

**46:2** And the ruler shall enter by the way of the porch of the inner gate and shall stand at the entrance of the gate, and the priests shall offer his burnt offerings and his offerings of deliverance.

**46:3** And he shall worship at the threshold of the gate and go out, and the gate shall not be shut until evening; and the people of the land shall worship at the entrance of that gate both on the Sabbaths and at the new moons before the Lord.

**46:4** And the burnt offerings that the ruler shall bring to the Lord on the Sabbath day shall be six lambs without blemish and a ram without blemish,

**46:5** and a grain offering, a cake for the ram, and for the lambs a sacrifice as a gift of his hand, and a hin of oil for each cake.

**46:6** And on the day of the new moon, a young bull without blemish and six lambs, and there shall be a ram without blemish;

**46:7** and a cake for the ram and a cake for the young bull shall be the grain offering, and for the lambs according as his hand is able to afford, and a hin of oil for each cake.

**46:8** And when the ruler enters, he shall enter by the way of the porch of the gate, and he shall go out by the way of the gate.

**46:9** And when the people of the land enter before the Lord at the feasts, the one who enters by the way of the gate facing north to worship shall go out by the way of the gate toward the south, and the one who enters by the way of the gate toward the south shall go out by the way of the gate toward the north; he shall not return by the gate through which he has entered, but shall go out straight opposite it.

**46:10** And the ruler shall be in their midst; when they enter, he shall enter with them, and when they go out, he shall go out.

**46:11** And at the feasts and at the solemn assemblies, the grain offering shall be a cake for each young bull and a cake for each ram, and for the lambs according as his hand is able to afford, and a hin of oil for each cake.

**46:12** And if the ruler offers a freewill offering, a burnt offering of deliverance to the Lord, he shall open for himself the gate facing east, and he shall offer his burnt offering and his offerings of deliverance just as he does on the Sabbath day; and he shall go out, and he shall shut the doors after he has gone out.

**46:13** And he shall offer a lamb of the first year without blemish for a burnt offering daily to the Lord; in the morning he shall offer it.

**46:14** And he shall prepare a grain offering with it in the morning, a sixth of a measure, and a third of a hin of oil to mingle with the fine flour—a grain offering to the Lord, a perpetual ordinance.

**46:15** You shall offer the lamb, and the grain offering and the oil you shall offer in the morning, as a perpetual burnt offering."

**46:16** Thus says the Lord God: "If the ruler gives a gift to one of his sons from his inheritance, this shall belong to his sons as a possession by inheritance.

**46:17** But if he gives a gift to one of his servants, then it shall be his until the year of release, and he shall return it to the ruler; only the inheritance of his sons shall belong to them.

**46:18** And the ruler shall by no means take from the inheritance of the people to oppress them; from his own possession he shall give an inheritance to his sons, so that My people may not be scattered, each one from his possession."

**46:19** Then he brought me into the entrance behind the gate, into the chamber of the holy things of the priests facing north; and behold, there was a place set apart there.

**46:20** And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall boil the offerings for ignorance and the sin offerings, and where they shall bake the grain offering, so as not to bring them out at all into the outer court to sanctify the people."

**46:21** Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me around to the four parts of the court; and behold, there was a court at the sides of the court;

**46:22** at each side a court, a court at all four, and a small court belonging to the court, forty cubits in length and thirty cubits in width, one measure for the four.

**46:23** And there were chambers all around in them, all around the four; and kitchens were made underneath the chambers all around.

**46:24** And he said to me, "These are the houses of the cooks, where those who minister to the house shall boil the sacrifices of the people."

## Translator Notes

- **46:2** — "Porch" translates Greek ailam (also in 46:8), a transliteration of Hebrew ulam; the Greek also reads "the inner gate" rather than "from outside."
- **46:20** — "Offerings for ignorance" faithfully renders Greek agnoias, the Septuagint equivalent here for the Hebrew guilt offering.
- **46:22** — "Small court" preserves the Septuagint rendering of a difficult Hebrew architectural term often translated "unenclosed" or "vaulted."
