## Text

**42:1** And he brought me into the inner court toward the east, opposite the gate toward the north; and he brought me in, and behold, five chambers adjoining the remaining space and adjoining the partition toward the north,

**42:2** extending one hundred cubits in length toward the north, and the width fifty,

**42:3** laid out in the same manner as the gates of the inner court, and in the same manner as the colonnades of the outer court, arranged in rows facing one another as threefold galleries.

**42:4** And opposite the chambers was a walkway ten cubits in width, extending one hundred cubits in length; and their doorways were toward the north,

**42:5** and the upper walkways were likewise. For the colonnade projected out from it, from the lower colonnade, and so did the space; thus there was a colonnade and a space, and thus two galleries.

**42:6** For they were threefold, and they did not have pillars like the pillars of the outer ones; therefore they projected from the lower and the middle ones from the ground.

**42:7** And there was a light outside, in the same manner as the chambers of the outer court that look opposite the chambers toward the north, fifty cubits in length.

**42:8** For the length of the chambers looking toward the outer court was fifty cubits, and these are the ones facing those; the whole was one hundred cubits.

**42:9** And the doors of these chambers of the entrance toward the east, to enter through them from the outer court,

**42:10** were opposite the light at the beginning of the walkway; and the parts toward the south were before the face of the south, before the face of the remaining space and before the face of the partition; and the chambers

**42:11** and the walkway in front of them were according to the measurements of the chambers toward the north, both according to their length and according to their breadth, and according to all their exits and according to all their turnings, and according to their lights and according to their doorways,

**42:12** of the chambers toward the south, and according to the doorways from the beginning of the walkway as toward the light of the space of a reed, and toward the east to enter through them.

**42:13** And he said to me, "The chambers toward the north and the chambers toward the south, which are before the spaces, these are the chambers of the sanctuary, in which the priests, the sons of Zadok, who draw near to the Lord, shall eat there the most holy things; and there they shall lay the most holy things, and the sacrifice, and the sin offerings, and the offerings for ignorance, because the place is holy.

**42:14** None shall enter there except the priests; they shall not go out from the sanctuary into the outer court, so that those who draw near may continually be holy; and they shall not touch their vestments in which they minister, because they are holy; and they shall put on other garments when they touch the people."

**42:15** And the measuring of the inner house was completed. And he brought me out by the way of the gate that faces toward the east, and he measured the pattern of the house round about in order.

**42:16** And he stood behind the gate that faces toward the east, and he measured five hundred with the measuring reed.

**42:17** And he turned toward the north and measured the side facing the north, five hundred cubits with the measuring reed.

**42:18** And he turned toward the sea and measured the side facing the sea, five hundred with the measuring reed.

**42:19** And he turned toward the south and measured opposite the south, five hundred with the measuring reed,

**42:20** the four sides of the same reed; and he laid it out and their surrounding wall round about, five hundred toward the east and five hundred cubits in breadth, to make a separation between the holy places and the outer wall that was in the arrangement of the house.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — The Septuagint reads "into the inner court" (Hebrew: "outer court") and specifies "five chambers" (Hebrew: singular "the chamber").
- **42:7** — "Light" translates Greek phos, used throughout verses 7 through 12 (and 41:11) for an architectural opening or lighted space where the Hebrew reads "wall" or "front."
- **42:13** — The Septuagint explicitly identifies the ministering priests as "the sons of Zadok" and renders the guilt offerings as "the offerings for ignorance."
- **42:16** — In verses 16 through 20, the Greek measures the outer perimeter as five hundred cubits on each side (explicitly "cubits" in verses 17 and 20) rather than five hundred reeds as in the Hebrew, and in verse 20 refers to "the outer wall" rather than "the common."
