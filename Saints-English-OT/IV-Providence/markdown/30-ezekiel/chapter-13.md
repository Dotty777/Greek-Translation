## Text

**13:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**13:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel, and you shall prophesy and say to them, 'Hear the word of the Lord:

**13:3** Thus says the Lord: Woe to those who prophesy from their own heart, and do not see at all!

**13:4** Like foxes in the deserts are your prophets, O Israel!

**13:5** They did not stand in a firm place, and they gathered flocks against the house of Israel; those who say, "In the day of the Lord," did not rise up.

**13:6** Seeing falsehoods and divining vain things, they are those who say, "The Lord says," though the Lord has not sent them; and they began to establish a word.

**13:7** Have you not seen a false vision, and have you not spoken vain divinations?'

**13:8** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: Because your words are false and your divinations are vain, therefore, behold, I am against you, says the Lord,

**13:9** and I will stretch out My hand against the prophets who see falsehoods and those who utter vain things; they shall not be in the instruction of My people, nor shall they be written in the register of the house of Israel, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

**13:10** Because they led My people astray, saying, "Peace," and there was no peace; and this one builds a wall, and they plaster it—it shall fall.'

**13:11** Say to those who plaster it, 'It shall fall; and there shall be a flooding rain, and I will cast catapult stones into their joints, and they shall fall, and there shall be a sweeping wind, and it shall be torn apart.

**13:12** And behold, the wall has fallen; will they not say to you, "Where is your plaster with which you plastered it?"

**13:13** Therefore thus says the Lord: I will cause a sweeping blast to burst forth with fury, and a flooding rain shall come in My anger, and the catapult stones I will bring in fury to complete destruction,

**13:14** and I will tear down the wall which you plastered—it shall fall; and I will lay it to the ground, and its foundations shall be uncovered, and it shall fall, and you shall be consumed with reproofs; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**13:15** And I will exhaust My fury against the wall and against those who plaster it—it shall fall. And I said to you, "The wall is no more, nor are those who plaster it,

**13:16** the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem and who see peace for her, and there is no peace," says the Lord.'

**13:17** And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy from their own heart, and prophesy against them,

**13:18** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: Woe to the women who sew cushions under every elbow of the hand and make veils for every head of every stature, to pervert souls! The souls of My people were perverted, and they kept souls alive for themselves,

**13:19** and they profaned Me among My people for a handful of barley and for pieces of bread, to slay souls that ought not to die and to keep alive souls that ought not to live, by your uttering oracles to a people who listen to vain utterances.

**13:20** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: Behold, I am against your cushions, upon which you entangle souls there, and I will tear them away from your arms, and I will send out the souls—those whose souls you subvert—into dispersion,

**13:21** and I will tear apart your veils, and I will deliver My people out of your hand, and they shall no longer be in your hands for entanglement; and you shall know that I am the Lord.

**13:22** Because you perverted the heart of the righteous, though I did not pervert him, and strengthened the hands of the lawless so that he should not at all turn from his wicked way and live,

**13:23** therefore you shall by no means see falsehoods, and you shall no longer divine divinations; and I will deliver My people out of your hand, and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — The Greek Septuagint reads "in a firm place," "gathered flocks," and "those who say, 'In the day of the Lord,'" differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's imagery of repairing a breach in a wall for battle.
- **13:10** — Greek "it shall fall" (peseitai) reflects the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew word for untempered mortar or whitewash as a form of the verb "to fall," a refrain repeated in verses 11, 14, and 15.
- **13:14** — The Greek reads "with reproofs" (met' elenchon), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the midst of it."
- **13:20** — The doubled Greek divine title kyrios Kyrios is rendered literally as "the Lord, the Lord" (also in 14:6), and the Greek "into dispersion" (eis diaskorpismon) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "like birds."
