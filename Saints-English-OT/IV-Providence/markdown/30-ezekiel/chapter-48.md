## Text

**48:1** And these are the names of the tribes from the northern extremity, along the side of the descent of that which divides, to the entrance of Hamath, the court of Ailam, the border of Damascus toward the north beside the side of the court of Hamath; and theirs shall be the parts toward the east as far as the sea: Dan, one portion.

**48:2** And from the borders of Dan, the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Asher, one portion.

**48:3** And from the borders of Asher, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Naphtali, one portion.

**48:4** And from the borders of Naphtali, from the east as far as the side toward the sea: Manasseh, one portion.

**48:5** And from the borders of Manasseh, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Ephraim, one portion.

**48:6** And from the borders of Ephraim, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Reuben, one portion.

**48:7** And from the borders of Reuben, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Judah, one portion.

**48:8** And from the borders of Judah, from the side toward the east it shall remain: the firstfruits of the setting apart shall be twenty-five thousand in width, and in length like one of the portions from the side toward the east even to the side toward the sea; and the sanctuary shall be in their midst.

**48:9** The firstfruits offering which they shall set apart to the Lord shall be twenty-five thousand in length, and twenty-five thousand in width.

**48:10** Out of these shall be the firstfruits of the holy things for the priests: toward the north, twenty-five thousand; and toward the sea, ten thousand; and toward the south, twenty-five thousand; and the mountain of the holy things shall be in its midst.

**48:11** For the sanctified priests, the sons of Zadok, who keep the charges of the house, who did not go astray in the straying of the sons of Israel, just as the Levites went astray.

**48:12** And theirs shall be the firstfruits offering given from the firstfruits of the land, a most holy portion, from the borders of the Levites.

**48:13** And for the Levites, the parts adjoining the borders of the priests shall be twenty-five thousand in length, and ten thousand in width; the entire length shall be twenty-five thousand, and the width twenty thousand.

**48:14** Nothing of it shall be sold, nor shall it be measured out, nor shall the first produce of the land be taken away, for it is holy to the Lord.

**48:15** And the five thousand remaining in breadth alongside the twenty-five thousand shall be an outer rampart for the city, for dwelling and for its open space; and the city shall be in its midst.

**48:16** And these shall be its measurements: on the side toward the north, four thousand five hundred; and on the side toward the south, four thousand five hundred; and on the side toward the east, four thousand five hundred; and on the side toward the sea, four thousand five hundred.

**48:17** And the city shall have open space: toward the north, two hundred fifty; and toward the south, two hundred fifty; and toward the east, two hundred fifty; and toward the sea, two hundred fifty.

**48:18** And the remainder of the length adjoining the firstfruits of the holy things shall be ten thousand toward the east, and ten thousand toward the sea; and they shall be the firstfruits of the sanctuary, and its produce shall be for bread for those who work the city.

**48:19** And those who work the city shall work it from all the tribes of Israel.

**48:20** The entire firstfruits offering shall be twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; foursquare you shall set apart from it the first part of the sanctuary, from the possession of the city.

**48:21** And the remainder shall be for the ruler, on this side and on that side of the firstfruits of the sanctuary and toward the possession of the city, extending twenty-five thousand in length as far as the borders toward the east, and toward the sea extending twenty-five thousand as far as the borders toward the sea, adjoining the portions of the ruler; and the firstfruits of the holy things and the sanctuary of the house shall be in its midst.

**48:22** And from the Levites, from the possession of the city, it shall be in the midst of the rulers, between the borders of Judah and between the borders of Benjamin; it shall belong to the rulers.

**48:23** And as for the rest of the tribes: from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea, Benjamin, one portion.

**48:24** And from the borders of Benjamin, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Simeon, one portion.

**48:25** And from the borders of Simeon, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Issachar, one portion.

**48:26** And from the borders of Issachar, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Zebulun, one portion.

**48:27** And from the borders of Zebulun, from the side toward the east as far as the side toward the sea: Gad, one portion.

**48:28** And from the borders of Gad as far as the side toward the southwest, its borders shall be from Teman and the water of Barimoth Kadesh, of the inheritance, as far as the Great Sea.

**48:29** This is the land which you shall cast by lot for the tribes of Israel, and these are their divisions, says the Lord God.

**48:30** And these are the exits of the city on the side toward the north: four thousand five hundred by measure.

**48:31** And the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes of Israel: three gates toward the north—one gate of Reuben, and one gate of Judah, and one gate of Levi.

**48:32** And on the side toward the east, four thousand five hundred; and three gates—one gate of Joseph, and one gate of Benjamin, and one gate of Dan.

**48:33** And on the side toward the south, four thousand five hundred by measure; and three gates—one gate of Simeon, and one gate of Issachar, and one gate of Zebulun.

**48:34** And on the side toward the sea, four thousand five hundred by measure; and three gates—one gate of Gad, and one gate of Asher, and one gate of Naphtali.

**48:35** The circumference shall be eighteen thousand. And the name of the city, from whatever day it comes into being, shall be its name.

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — The Greek renders the northern landmarks as "the descent of that which divides" (where Hebrew has "the way of Hethlon") and "the court of Ailam" (where Hebrew has Hazar-enan).
- **48:9** — The Greek text gives a width of twenty-five thousand here and a total width of twenty thousand in verse 13, while verse 10 omits the eastern side and reads "the mountain of the holy things" where Hebrew has "the sanctuary of the Lord."
- **48:15** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for common land as "outer rampart" (proteichisma).
- **48:28** — The Greek reads "Teman" (for Hebrew Tamar), the distinct place name "Barimoth Kadesh" (for Meribath-kadesh), and "of the inheritance" (reading Hebrew nahalah as "inheritance" rather than "to the brook").
- **48:35** — The Greek reads "from whatever day it comes into being, shall be its name," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's "The Lord Is There."
