## Text

**2:1** This was the vision of the likeness of the glory of the Lord. And I looked, and I fell upon my face, and I heard the voice of One speaking, and He said to me, "Son of man, stand upon your feet, and I will speak to you."

**2:2** And the Spirit came upon me, and took me up, and lifted me up, and set me upon my feet; and I heard Him speaking to me,

**2:3** and He said to me, "Son of man, I send you forth to the house of Israel, those who provoke Me, who have provoked Me, they and their fathers, until this very day,

**2:4** and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord,'

**2:5** whether indeed they hear or are dismayed—for it is a provoking house—yet they shall know that you are a prophet in the midst of them.

**2:6** And you, son of man, do not fear them, nor be dismayed before their face; for they will rage furiously and rise up against you all around, and you dwell in the midst of scorpions. Do not fear their words, and do not be dismayed before their face, for it is a provoking house.

**2:7** And you shall speak My words to them, whether indeed they hear or are dismayed, because it is a provoking house.

**2:8** And you, son of man, hear the One who speaks to you; do not become provoking like the provoking house. Open wide your mouth and eat what I give to you."

**2:9** And I looked, and behold, a hand was stretched out to me, and in it was a scroll of a book.

**2:10** And He unrolled it before me, and in it was writing on the front and on the back; and there were written lamentation, and song, and woe.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Septuagint opens chapter 2 with the sentence that closes 1:28 in the Hebrew MT.
- **2:5** — Greek ptoethosin ("be dismayed") differs from the Hebrew MT ("refuse" or "forbear").
- **2:6** — Where the Hebrew MT mentions briers and thorns, the Septuagint reads verbs of fierce hostility ("rage furiously and rise up against you").
