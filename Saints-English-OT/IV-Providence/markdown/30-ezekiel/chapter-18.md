## Text

**18:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**18:2** "Son of man, what is this parable to you among the sons of Israel, saying, 'The fathers ate sour grapes, and the teeth of the children were set on edge'?

**18:3** As I live, says the Lord, this parable shall surely no longer be spoken in Israel.

**18:4** For all souls are Mine; just as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son—they are Mine; the soul that sins, it shall die.

**18:5** Now the man who shall be righteous, who practices righteousness,

**18:6** shall not eat upon the mountains, and shall by no means lift up his eyes toward the devices of the house of Israel, and shall by no means defile his neighbor's wife, and shall not draw near to a woman in her impurity,

**18:7** and shall not oppress any man, shall restore the pledge of a debtor, and shall not seize plunder, shall give his bread to the hungry and clothe the naked,

**18:8** and shall not lend his silver upon interest, nor take increase, and shall turn back his hand from unrighteousness, shall execute righteous judgment between a man and his neighbor,

**18:9** and has walked in My statutes, has kept My ordinances to do them—this man is righteous; he shall surely live, says the Lord.

**18:10** And if he fathers a pestilent son, who sheds blood and commits sin,

**18:11** he did not walk in the way of his righteous father, but even ate upon the mountains and defiled his neighbor's wife,

**18:12** and oppressed the poor and needy, and seized plunder, and did not restore a pledge, and set his eyes upon the idols, has practiced lawlessness,

**18:13** lent with interest and took increase—this man shall surely not live! He has done all these lawless deeds; he shall surely be put to death; his blood shall be upon him.

**18:14** Now if he fathers a son, and he sees all the sins of his father which he committed, and fears, and does not act according to them—

**18:15** he has not eaten upon the mountains, and has not set his eyes upon the devices of the house of Israel, and has not defiled his neighbor's wife,

**18:16** and has not oppressed any man, and has not exacted a pledge, and has not seized plunder, has given his bread to the hungry and clothed the naked,

**18:17** and has turned back his hand from unrighteousness, has taken neither interest nor increase, has practiced righteousness and walked in My statutes—he shall not die in the iniquities of his father; he shall surely live.

**18:18** As for his father, if he grievously oppresses and seizes plunder—he has done contrary things in the midst of My people—he shall die in his iniquity.

**18:19** Yet you will say, 'Why has the son not borne the iniquity of the father?' Because the son has practiced righteousness and mercy, has carefully kept all My ordinances and done them, he shall surely live.

**18:20** The soul that sins shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, nor shall the father bear the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the lawlessness of the lawless shall be upon him.

**18:21** And if the lawless man turns back from all his lawless deeds which he has committed, and keeps all My commandments, and practices righteousness and mercy, he shall surely live; he shall by no means die.

**18:22** All his transgressions which he has committed shall not be remembered; in his righteousness which he has practiced he shall live.

**18:23** Do I indeed delight in the death of the lawless, says the Lord, rather than that he should turn back from the evil way and live?

**18:24** But when a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and commits unrighteousness according to all the lawless deeds which the lawless man has committed, all his righteous deeds which he has done shall by no means be remembered; in his transgression by which he transgressed and in his sins which he sinned, in them he shall die.

**18:25** Yet you said, 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' Hear now, O house of Israel: Is My way not straight? Is it not your way that is not straight?

**18:26** When the righteous man turns back from his righteousness and commits a transgression, and dies in the transgression which he has committed, in it he shall die.

**18:27** And when a lawless man turns back from his lawlessness which he has committed and practices judgment and righteousness, this man has preserved his soul.

**18:28** And he has turned back from all his ungodly deeds which he committed; he shall surely live, he shall by no means die.

**18:29** Yet the house of Israel says, 'The way of the Lord is not upright.' Is My way not upright, O house of Israel? Is it not your way that is not upright?

**18:30** Each one according to his way I will judge you, O house of Israel, says the Lord. Return and turn away from all your ungodly deeds, and they shall not become to you a punishment of iniquity.

**18:31** Cast away from yourselves all your ungodly deeds by which you have acted impiously against Me, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; and why do you die, O house of Israel?

**18:32** For I do not desire the death of the one who is dying, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for idols with a word meaning "devices" or "imaginations" (also in verse 15), highlighting inward idolatrous schemes, whereas verse 12 uses the standard word for "idols."
- **18:11** — The Greek reads "he did not walk in the way of his righteous father," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "though he himself did none of these things."
- **18:30** — The Greek reads "a punishment of iniquity," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a stumbling block of iniquity."
- **18:32** — The Greek ends the chapter with "says the Lord," without the closing call "Therefore turn and live" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
