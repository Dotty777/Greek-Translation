## Text

**44:1** And he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary facing toward the east, and it was shut.

**44:2** And the Lord said to me, "This gate shall be shut; it shall not be opened, and no one shall pass through it, because the Lord, the God of Israel, shall enter through it, and it shall be shut.

**44:3** For the ruler—he shall sit in it to eat bread before the Lord; he shall enter by the way of the vestibule of the gate, and by its way he shall go out."

**44:4** And he brought me by the way of the gate toward the north, opposite the house; and I looked, and behold, the house of the Lord was full of glory, and I fell upon my face.

**44:5** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, set it in your heart, and see with your eyes, and hear with your ears all that I speak with you concerning all the ordinances of the house of the Lord and all its laws; and you shall set your heart upon the entrance of the house, with all its exits in all the holy places.

**44:6** And you shall say to the rebellious house, to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord God: Let it be enough for you from all your iniquities, O house of Israel,

**44:7** that you brought in foreigners, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in My sanctuary; and they profaned it when you offered bread, flesh, and blood, and you transgressed My covenant in all your iniquities,

**44:8** and you ordained the keeping of the charges in My sanctuary.

**44:9** Therefore thus says the Lord God: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter into My sanctuary, of all the foreigners who are in the midst of the house of Israel,

**44:10** except the Levites who broke away from Me when Israel went astray from Me after their own devices; and they shall bear their iniquity,

**44:11** and they shall be in My sanctuary ministering as gatekeepers at the gates of the house, and ministering to the house; they shall slay the burnt offerings and the sacrifices for the people, and they shall stand before the people to minister to them.

**44:12** Because they ministered to them before their idols, and it became to the house of Israel a punishment of iniquity, therefore I have lifted up My hand against them, says the Lord God,

**44:13** and they shall not draw near to Me to serve as priests to Me, nor approach the holy things of the sons of Israel, nor My holy of holies; and they shall bear their dishonor in the straying by which they went astray.

**44:14** And they shall bring them down to keep the charges of the house, for all its work and for all that they shall do.

**44:15** The priests, the Levites, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charges of My sanctuary when the house of Israel went astray from Me—these shall approach Me to minister to Me, and they shall stand before My face to offer to Me sacrifice, fat and blood, says the Lord God.

**44:16** These shall enter into My sanctuary, and these shall come near to My table to minister to Me, and they shall keep My charges.

**44:17** And it shall be, when they enter the gates of the inner court, they shall put on linen garments; and they shall not put on woolen garments while they minister from the gate of the inner court.

**44:18** And they shall have linen turbans upon their heads, and they shall have linen breeches upon their loins, and they shall not gird themselves tightly.

**44:19** And when they go out into the outer court to the people, they shall take off their garments in which they minister, and they shall lay them in the chambers of the sanctuary, and they shall put on other garments; and they shall not sanctify the people with their garments.

**44:20** And they shall not shave their heads, nor strip bare their hair; they shall surely cover their heads.

**44:21** And no priest shall drink wine when they enter into the inner court.

**44:22** And they shall not take a widow or a divorced woman for themselves as a wife, but only a virgin of the seed of Israel; yet if there is a widow of a priest, they may take her.

**44:23** And they shall teach My people the difference between the holy and the profane, and make known to them the difference between the clean and the unclean;

**44:24** and in a judgment of blood they shall stand to decide; they shall uphold My statutes and judge My judgments, and they shall keep My laws and My ordinances in all My feasts, and they shall sanctify My Sabbaths.

**44:25** And they shall not go in to a dead person to be defiled; except for father and mother, and for son and for daughter, and for brother, and for his sister who has had no husband, he may be defiled.

**44:26** And after he is cleansed, he shall count off seven days for himself;

**44:27** and on whatever day they enter into the inner court to minister in the holy place, they shall offer a propitiation, says the Lord God.

**44:28** And it shall be to them for an inheritance: I am their inheritance; and no possession shall be given to them among the sons of Israel, for I am their possession.

**44:29** And the sacrifices and the sin offerings and the offerings for ignorance, these they shall eat; and every devoted thing in Israel shall be theirs.

**44:30** The firstfruits of all things, and the firstborn of all things, and all the offerings set apart from all your firstfruits shall belong to the priests. And your first produce you shall give to the priest, to place your blessings upon your houses.

**44:31** And the priests shall not eat anything that died of itself or was torn by wild beasts, whether of birds or of cattle.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:2** — The Septuagint uses the future tense "shall enter through it," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the past tense "has entered."
- **44:8** — The Septuagint preserves a shorter text than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting the opening clause about failing to keep charge of the holy things.
- **44:18** — The Septuagint reads "gird themselves tightly" (literally "with force"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to girding with anything that causes sweat.
- **44:20** — Instead of prohibiting long hair and commanding trimming as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint forbids stripping the hair bare and commands that the priests "surely cover their heads."
