## Text

**37:1** And the hand of the Lord came upon me, and the Lord brought me out in the Spirit and set me down in the midst of the plain; and it was full of human bones.

**37:2** And He led me around them all about on every side; and behold, there were very many upon the face of the plain, very dry.

**37:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, shall these bones live?" And I said, "Lord, You know these things."

**37:4** And He said to me, "Prophesy over these bones, and you shall say to them, 'O dry bones, hear the word of the Lord:

**37:5** Thus says the Lord to these bones: Behold, I am bringing into you a spirit of life,

**37:6** and I will lay sinews upon you, and bring flesh upon you, and stretch skin over you, and put My Spirit into you, and you shall live; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**37:7** And I prophesied as He commanded me. And it came to pass, as I prophesied, behold, there was a shaking, and the bones drew near, each one to its joint.

**37:8** And I looked, and behold, sinews and flesh grew upon them, and skin came up over them above; yet there was no spirit in them.

**37:9** And He said to me, "Prophesy to the spirit, prophesy, son of man, and say to the spirit, 'Thus says the Lord: Come from the four winds, and breathe into these dead ones, and let them live.'"

**37:10** And I prophesied just as He commanded me; and the spirit entered into them, and they lived, and they stood upon their feet, an exceedingly great assembly.

**37:11** And the Lord spoke to me, saying, "Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel; and they say, 'Our bones have become dry, our hope has perished, we are utterly lost.'

**37:12** Therefore prophesy and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am opening your tombs, and I will bring you up out of your tombs, and I will bring you into the land of Israel;

**37:13** and you shall know that I am the Lord, when I open your graves, to bring up My people out of the graves.

**37:14** And I will put My Spirit into you, and you shall live, and I will place you upon your own land; and you shall know that I, the Lord, have spoken and will perform it, says the Lord.'"

**37:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**37:16** "Son of man, take for yourself a rod, and write upon it Judah and the sons of Israel who are joined to him; and you shall take for yourself a second rod, and write upon it, 'For Joseph, the rod of Ephraim, and all the sons of Israel who are added to him.'

**37:17** And you shall join them one to another for yourself into one rod, to bind them together, and they shall be in your hand.

**37:18** And it shall come to pass, when the sons of your people say to you, 'Will you not declare to us what these things mean to you?'

**37:19** then you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I will take the tribe of Joseph, which is by the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel joined to him, and I will place them upon the tribe of Judah, and they shall become one rod in the hand of Judah.'

**37:20** And the rods upon which you wrote shall be in your hand before them,

**37:21** and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I am taking all the house of Israel from the midst of the nations where they went, and I will gather them from all who are around them, and I will bring them into the land of Israel;

**37:22** and I will make them into a nation in My land and upon the mountains of Israel; and one ruler shall be over them, and they shall no longer be two nations, nor shall they ever again be divided into two kingdoms,

**37:23** so that they may no longer be defiled with their idols. And I will deliver them from all their iniquities by which they sinned, and I will cleanse them; and they shall be to Me a people, and I, the Lord, will be to them God.

**37:24** And My servant David shall be ruler in their midst; there shall be one shepherd of them all, for they shall walk in My statutes, and they shall keep My judgments and perform them.

**37:25** And they shall dwell upon their land which I have given to My servant Jacob, where their fathers dwelt; and they themselves shall dwell upon it. And David My servant shall be ruler forever.

**37:26** And I will make a covenant of peace with them; it shall be an everlasting covenant with them. And I will set My sanctuary in their midst forever,

**37:27** and My dwelling shall be among them; and I will be to them God, and they shall be My people.

**37:28** And the nations shall know that I am the Lord who sanctifies them, when My sanctuary is in their midst forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The Septuagint renders the setting as a "plain" (pediou) and explicitly specifies "human bones" (osteon anthropinon).
- **37:9** — Greek pneuma encompasses "spirit," "breath," and "wind" throughout verses 1-14; here the singular is rendered "the spirit" and the plural "the four winds." The Greek also reads "these dead ones" (tous nekrous toutous) rather than the Hebrew "these slain."
- **37:19** — Following the Septuagint, this verse reads "tribe" (phylen) for Joseph and Judah and "in the hand of Judah" (te cheiri Iouda), where the Hebrew reads "stick" and "in My hand."
- **37:24** — Here and in verses 22 and 25, the Septuagint uses "ruler" (archon) rather than "king," and places David "in their midst" (en meso auton).
