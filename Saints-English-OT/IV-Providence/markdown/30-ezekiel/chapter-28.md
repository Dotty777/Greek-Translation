## Text

**28:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:2** "And you, son of man, say to the prince of Tyre, 'Thus says the Lord: Because your heart was lifted up, and you said, "I am a god; I have dwelt in the habitation of God in the heart of the sea," yet you are a man and not a god, and you set your heart as the heart of a god—

**28:3** are you wiser than Daniel? Did not wise men instruct you in their knowledge?

**28:4** Was it by your knowledge or by your understanding that you made for yourself power, and gold and silver in your treasuries?

**28:5** By your great knowledge and trade you multiplied your power; your heart was lifted up in your power.

**28:6** Therefore thus says the Lord: Because you have set your heart as the heart of a god,

**28:7** for this reason, behold, I am bringing upon you strangers, pestilent men from the nations; and they shall draw their swords against you and against the beauty of your knowledge, and they shall lay your beauty low to destruction,

**28:8** and they shall bring you down, and you shall die the death of the slain in the heart of the sea.

**28:9** Will you indeed say, "I am a god," before those who slay you? Yet you are a man and not a god.

**28:10** Among a multitude of the uncircumcised you shall perish by the hands of strangers; for I have spoken, says the Lord.'"

**28:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:12** "Son of man, take up a lamentation over the prince of Tyre, and say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: You were a seal of likeness

**28:13** and a crown of beauty in the delight of the paradise of God. With every precious stone you were bound: sardius and topaz and emerald, and carbuncle and sapphire and jasper, and silver and gold, and ligure and agate and amethyst, and chrysolite and beryl and onyx; and with gold you filled your treasuries and your storehouses within you.

**28:14** From the day you were created with the cherub, I placed you on the holy mountain of God; you were in the midst of fiery stones.

**28:15** You were blameless in your days, from the day you were created until iniquities were found in you.

**28:16** By the abundance of your trade you filled your storechambers with lawlessness, and you sinned, and you were wounded from the mountain of God; and the cherub led you out from the midst of the fiery stones.

**28:17** Your heart was lifted up because of your beauty; your knowledge was corrupted along with your beauty. Because of the multitude of your sins I cast you to the ground; before kings I gave you over to be made a public example.

**28:18** Because of the multitude of your sins and the iniquities of your trade, I profaned your sanctuaries, and I will bring forth fire from the midst of you; this shall devour you, and I will turn you into ashes upon your earth before all who see you.

**28:19** And all who know you among the nations shall groan over you; you have become a ruin, and you shall exist no more forever.'"

**28:20** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:21** "Son of man, set your face against Sidon, and prophesy against her,

**28:22** and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against you, O Sidon, and I will be glorified in you; and you shall know that I am the Lord when I execute judgments in you, and I will be sanctified in you.

**28:23** Blood and death shall be in your streets, and those slain by swords shall fall within you all around you; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

**28:24** And there shall no longer be in the house of Israel a briar of bitterness and a thorn of pain from those all around them who dishonored them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**28:25** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: "I will also gather Israel from the nations where they were scattered, and I will be sanctified in them and before the peoples and the nations.

**28:26** And they shall dwell upon their own land, which I have given to My servant Jacob, and they shall dwell upon it in hope; and they shall build houses and plant vineyards, and they shall dwell in hope when I execute judgment upon all those who dishonored them among those all around them; and they shall know that I am the Lord their God, and the God of their fathers."

## Translator Notes

- **28:12** — The doubled Greek title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") reflects Hebrew Adonai YHWH; here and in verse 25 it is rendered literally to preserve the Greek wording.
- **28:13** — The Septuagint lists all twelve stones of the high priest's breastpiece (cf. Exodus 28:17-20) along with silver and gold, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists nine stones; the Greek also reads treasuries and storehouses at the end of the verse.
- **28:14** — Reading the Hebrew particle as the preposition with, the Septuagint places the prince of Tyre with the cherub rather than identifying him as an anointed cherub, and in verse 16 depicts the cherub leading him out from the fiery stones.
- **28:18** — The Septuagint has the first-person verb I profaned, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the second-person you profaned.
