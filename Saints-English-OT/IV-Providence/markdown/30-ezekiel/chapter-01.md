## Text

**1:1** And it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, as I was in the midst of the captivity by the river Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**1:2** On the fifth day of the month, this was the fifth year of the captivity of King Jehoiakim,

**1:3** and the word of the Lord came to Ezekiel the son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar. And the hand of the Lord came upon me,

**1:4** and I looked, and behold, a sweeping wind was coming from the north, and a great cloud was in it, and brightness was all around it, and flashing fire; and in the midst of it was something like the appearance of electrum in the midst of the fire, and brightness was in it.

**1:5** And in the midst was something like the likeness of four living creatures; and this was their appearance: the likeness of a man was upon them.

**1:6** And each one had four faces, and each one had four wings.

**1:7** And their legs were straight, and their feet were winged, and sparks were like flashing bronze; and their wings were light,

**1:8** and a man's hand was beneath their wings on their four sides.

**1:9** And the faces of the four of them did not turn as they walked; each one went straight ahead before their faces.

**1:10** And the likeness of their faces was this: the face of a man, and the face of a lion on the right side for the four of them, and the face of a calf on the left side for the four of them, and the face of an eagle for the four of them.

**1:11** And the wings of the four of them were stretched out above: each had two joined to one another, and two covered over their bodies.

**1:12** And each one went straight ahead before its face; wherever the spirit was going, they went, and they did not turn.

**1:13** And in the midst of the living creatures was an appearance like burning coals of fire, like the sight of torches moving to and fro among the living creatures, and brightness of the fire; and out of the fire went forth lightning.

**1:15** And I looked, and behold, one wheel was upon the earth beside the living creatures, for the four of them.

**1:16** And the appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of Tarshish, and the four of them had one likeness; and their workmanship was as if a wheel were within a wheel.

**1:17** Toward their four sides they went; they did not turn as they went,

**1:18** nor did their rims, and they had height; and I looked at them, and their rims were full of eyes all around for the four of them.

**1:19** And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.

**1:20** Wherever the cloud was, there the spirit was to go; the wheels went and were lifted up together with them, for a spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:21** When those went, they went; and when those stood still, they stood still; and when those lifted up from the earth, they were lifted up together with them, because a spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:22** And a likeness was over the heads of the living creatures, like a firmament, like the appearance of crystal, stretched out above over their wings.

**1:23** And beneath the firmament their wings were stretched out, flapping one toward another; each had two covering their bodies.

**1:24** And I heard the sound of their wings as they went, like the sound of much water; and when they stood still, their wings rested.

**1:25** And behold, there was a voice from above the firmament that was over their heads.

**1:26** Like the appearance of a sapphire stone was the likeness of a throne upon it; and upon the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the appearance of a man above.

**1:27** And I saw something like the sight of electrum from the appearance of the loins and upward, and from the appearance of the loins and downward I saw something like the appearance of fire, and its brightness was all around.

**1:28** Like the appearance of a bow when it is in the cloud in days of rain, so was the state of the brightness all around.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Greek reads Ioakeim (Jehoiakim), a form the Septuagint often uses for both Jehoiakim and Jehoiachin; the Hebrew MT reads Jehoiachin.
- **1:7** — The Septuagint uniquely describes the living creatures as having winged feet, sparks like flashing bronze, and light wings, differing markedly from the Hebrew MT.
- **1:15** — Verse 14 of the Hebrew MT is absent from the Septuagint text.
- **1:16** — Greek tharseis transliterates the Hebrew gemstone name Tarshish, traditionally identified as beryl or chrysolite.
- **1:20** — The Septuagint reads "the cloud" and "a spirit of life," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "the spirit" and "the spirit of the living creature."
