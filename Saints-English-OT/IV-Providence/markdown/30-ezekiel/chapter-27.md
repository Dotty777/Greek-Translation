## Text

**27:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**27:2** "Son of man, you also take up a lamentation for Tyre,

**27:3** and you shall say to Tyre, who dwells at the entrance of the sea, the marketplace of the peoples from many islands, 'Thus says the Lord to Tyre:

**27:4** You said, "I have clothed myself with my beauty." In the heart of the sea, for Beelim, your sons clothed you with beauty.

**27:5** Cedar from Senir was built for you; strips of cypress planks from Lebanon were taken to make for you masts of fir.

**27:6** From Bashan they made your oars; your sanctuaries they made of ivory, wooded houses from the islands of Kittim.

**27:7** Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt became your bedding, to clothe you with glory and to wrap you in blue and purple from the islands of Elishah; and they became your coverings.

**27:8** And your rulers who dwell in Sidon and the Arvadites became your oarsmen; your wise men, O Tyre, who were in you, these were your pilots.

**27:9** The elders of Byblos and their wise men were in you; these strengthened your counsel. And all the ships of the sea and their oarsmen were yours to the west of the west.

**27:10** Persians and Lydians and Libyans were in your army; your men of war hung shields and helmets in you; these bestowed your glory.

**27:11** The sons of the Arvadites and your army were upon your walls; watchmen were in your towers; they hung their quivers upon your harbors all around; these perfected your beauty.

**27:12** The Carthaginians were your merchants because of the abundance of all your strength; silver and gold and iron and tin and lead they gave for your marketplace.

**27:13** Greece, both the whole of it and the bordering regions—these traded with you in the souls of men, and vessels of bronze they gave for your merchandise.

**27:14** From the house of Togarmah, horses and horsemen supplied your marketplace.

**27:15** The sons of the Rhodians were merchants; from the islands they multiplied your merchandise with ivory tusks, and for the wares brought in you gave back your wages.

**27:16** Men were your merchandise because of the abundance of your mixed wares, oil of myrrh and embroidered work from Tarshish; and Lamoth and Chorchor supplied your marketplace.

**27:17** Judah and the children of Israel, these were your merchants in the sale of grain and ointments and cassia; and the choicest honey and oil and resin they gave for your mixed wares.

**27:18** Damascus was your merchant because of the abundance of all your wealth; wine from Helbon and wool from Miletus, and wine they gave to your marketplace.

**27:19** From Asel is wrought iron, and wheelwork is among your mixed wares.

**27:20** Dedan was your merchant with choice beasts for chariots.

**27:21** Arabia and all the princes of Kedar, these were your merchants through your hand, with camels and rams and lambs, in which they trade with you.

**27:22** The merchants of Sheba and Raamah, these were your merchants with the finest spices and precious stones; and gold they gave for your marketplace.

**27:23** Haran and Canneh, these were your merchants.

**27:24** Asshur and Charman were your merchants, bringing merchandise of blue and choice treasures bound with cords, and wares of cypress wood.

**27:25** Ships were your merchants in your abundance, in your mixed wares; and you were filled and became exceedingly heavy in the heart of the sea.

**27:26** Into much water your oarsmen brought you; the south wind shattered you in the heart of the sea.

**27:27** There were your riches, and your wages and those of your traders, and your oarsmen and your pilots and your counselors, and your traders from among your mixed wares, and all your men of war who are in you; and all your assembly in the midst of you shall fall into the heart of the sea in the day of your fall.

**27:28** At the cry of your voice your pilots shall fear with great fear,

**27:29** and all the oarsmen and the passengers shall come down from the ships, and the lookouts of the sea shall stand upon the land,

**27:30** and they shall wail over you with their voice and cry out bitterly; and they shall cast earth upon their heads and spread ashes under themselves.

**27:32** And their sons shall take up a lamentation over you, a dirge for Tyre:

**27:33** How great a wage did you find from the sea? You filled nations from your abundance, and from your mixed wares you enriched all the kings of the earth.

**27:34** Now you have been shattered in the sea; in the depth of the water are your mixed wares and all your assembly in the midst of you; all your oarsmen have fallen.

**27:35** All who dwell in the islands were appalled at you, and their kings were astounded with great astonishment, and their countenance wept.

**27:36** Merchants from among the nations hissed at you; you have become a ruin, and you shall be no more forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Throughout chapter 27 the Greek text transliterates Tyre as Sor (from Hebrew Tsor), whereas chapter 28 uses the Greek name Tyre.
- **27:4** — The Septuagint reads for Beelim (as a proper name) and your sons, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has your borders and your builders.
- **27:12** — The Greek text renders Tarshish as Carthaginians here (while transliterating Tarshish in verse 16) and likewise uses distinctive Greek geographical names in this trade list, including Byblos (v. 9), Greece (v. 13), Rhodians (v. 15), and Miletus (v. 18).
- **27:16** — The Septuagint treats Lamoth and Chorchor as proper names (corresponding to coral and ruby in the Hebrew text) and reads men where the Hebrew has Aram; similarly, Asel (v. 19) and Charman (v. 24) reflect distinctive Greek proper-name forms.
- **27:32** — Verse 31 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text is absent in the Septuagint; in verse 32 the Greek also reads their sons where the Hebrew has in their wailing.
