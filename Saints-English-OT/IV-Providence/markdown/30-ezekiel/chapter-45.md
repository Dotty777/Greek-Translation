## Text

**45:1** And when you measure out the land for an inheritance, you shall set apart a firstfruits offering to the Lord, a holy portion from the land, twenty-five thousand in length and twenty thousand in width; it shall be holy throughout all its borders all around.

**45:2** And from this there shall be a sanctuary, five hundred by five hundred, square all around, and fifty cubits for an open space around it.

**45:3** And from this measurement you shall measure a length of twenty-five thousand and a width of twenty thousand, and in it shall be the Holy of Holies.

**45:4** It shall be from the land for the priests who minister in the holy place, and it shall be for those who draw near to minister to the Lord; and it shall be a place for them for houses set apart for their sanctuary,

**45:5** twenty-five thousand in length and twenty thousand in width; and for the Levites who minister to the house, for them as a possession, cities to dwell in.

**45:6** And you shall give as the possession of the city five thousand in width and twenty-five thousand in length, corresponding to the firstfruits offering of the holy portions; they shall belong to the whole house of Israel.

**45:7** And for the ruler, on this side and on that side bordering the firstfruits offerings of the holy portions and the possession of the city, facing the firstfruits offerings of the holy portions and facing the possession of the city, on the side toward the sea and from the side toward the sea eastward; and the length shall be like one of the portions, from the borders toward the sea, and the length to the borders toward the east of the land.

**45:8** And it shall be to him for a possession in Israel, and the rulers of Israel shall no longer oppress My people; and the house of Israel shall inherit the land according to their tribes.

**45:9** Thus says the Lord God: "Let it be enough for you, O rulers of Israel! Put away injustice and misery, and execute judgment and righteousness; remove oppression from My people," says the Lord God.

**45:10** "You shall have a just balance, a just measure, and a just choenix of the measure,

**45:11** and the choenix likewise shall be one standard for receiving: the tenth of the gomor shall be a choenix, and the tenth of the gomor; its equal measure shall be according to the gomor.

**45:12** And the weights shall be twenty obols; five shekels, five, and ten shekels, and fifty shekels shall be the mina for you.

**45:13** And this is the firstfruits offering that you shall set apart: a sixth of a measure from a gomor of wheat, and a sixth of that ephah from a kor of barley.

**45:14** And the ordinance of oil: a kotyle of oil from ten kotyles, for ten kotyles are a gomor.

**45:15** And one sheep from the flock out of ten, a tribute from all the families of Israel, for sacrifices and for burnt offerings and for offerings of deliverance, to make atonement for you," says the Lord God.

**45:16** And all the people shall give this firstfruits offering to the ruler of Israel.

**45:17** And through the ruler shall be the burnt offerings and the sacrifices and the drink offerings at the feasts, and at the new moons, and on the Sabbaths, and at all the feasts of the house of Israel; he himself shall offer the sin offerings and the sacrifice and the burnt offerings and the offerings of deliverance, to make atonement for the house of Israel.

**45:18** Thus says the Lord God: "In the first month, on the first day of the month, you shall take a young bull without blemish from the herd to make atonement for the sanctuary.

**45:19** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the atonement offering and put it on the doorposts of the house, and on the four corners of the temple, and on the altar, and on the doorposts of the gate of the inner court.

**45:20** And so you shall do in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, a portion from each one, and you shall make atonement for the house.

**45:21** And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you shall have the Passover, a feast; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**45:22** And on that day the ruler shall offer for himself and for the house and for all the people of the land a young bull for a sin offering.

**45:23** And during the seven days of the feast he shall offer burnt offerings to the Lord: seven young bulls and seven rams without blemish daily for the seven days, and a kid of the goats daily for a sin offering, and a sacrifice.

**45:24** And you shall prepare a cake for each young bull and a cake for each ram, and a hin of oil for each cake.

**45:25** And in the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, at the feast, you shall do the same for seven days, according to the sin offerings, and according to the burnt offerings, and according to the grain offering, and according to the oil."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The Greek text specifies a width of twenty thousand (also in verses 3 and 5), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads ten thousand.
- **45:12** — The Greek defines the mina as fifty shekels (using Greek weights and measures such as obols, choenix, gomor, and kotyles in verses 10-14), whereas the Masoretic Text totals sixty shekels; similarly, verse 15 specifies one sheep out of ten rather than two hundred.
- **45:20** — The Greek dates this rite to the first day of the seventh month with a portion from each person, whereas the Masoretic Text specifies the seventh day of the first month for anyone who errs or is simple.
- **45:25** — "Grain offering" translates Greek manaa (here and in 46:5, 7, 11, 14, 15, 20), a transliteration of Hebrew minchah.
