## Text

**19:1** And you, take up a lamentation for the ruler of Israel,

**19:2** and you shall say, "What is your mother? A lion cub; in the midst of lions she came into being, in the midst of lions she multiplied her cubs.

**19:3** And one of her cubs sprang forth; he became a lion, and he learned to seize prey; he devoured men.

**19:4** And nations heard against him; in their destruction he was caught, and they brought him in a muzzle into the land of Egypt.

**19:5** And she saw that he had been thrust away from her, her hope had perished; and she took another of her cubs, and appointed him a lion.

**19:6** And he went about in the midst of lions; he became a lion, and he learned to seize prey; he devoured men.

**19:7** And he fed in his boldness and laid waste their cities, and he made the land and its fullness desolate at the sound of his roaring.

**19:8** Then nations from the lands round about set against him, and they spread their nets over him; in their destruction he was caught.

**19:9** And they put him in a muzzle and in a cage; he came to the king of Babylon, and he brought him into prison, so that his voice might not be heard upon the mountains of Israel.

**19:10** Your mother was like a vine and like a blossom on a pomegranate tree, planted by water; her fruit and her sprout came from abundant water.

**19:11** And she became a rod over a tribe of rulers, and she was exalted in her stature in the midst of tree trunks, and she saw her stature in the multitude of her branches.

**19:12** Yet she was broken down in wrath; to the ground she was cast down, and the scorching wind dried up her choice shoots; vengeance was taken upon them, and the rod of her strength was withered; fire consumed her.

**19:13** And now they have planted her in the wilderness, in a waterless land.

**19:14** And fire went forth from a rod of her choice shoots and devoured her, and there was in her no rod of strength; a tribe is for a parable of lamentation, and it shall be for a lamentation."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek reads "A lion cub" and "she came into being," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "A lioness" and "she lay down."
- **19:4** — The Greek renders the Hebrew word for "pit" as "destruction" (also in verse 8), reflecting the related Hebrew root meaning "to destroy."
- **19:7** — The Greek reads "he fed in his boldness," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "he knew their widows" (or "their citadels").
- **19:10** — The Greek reads "like a vine and like a blossom on a pomegranate tree," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "like a vine in your blood" (or "in your likeness").
