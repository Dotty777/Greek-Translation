## Text

**9:1** And He cried out in my ears with a loud voice, saying, "The vengeance of the city has drawn near!" And each one had the weapons of destruction in his hand.

**9:2** And behold, six men came from the way of the upper gate which faces toward the north, and each man had an axe in his hand; and one man in the midst of them was clothed in a long robe, and a belt of sapphire was upon his loins; and they went in and stood beside the bronze altar.

**9:3** And the glory of the God of Israel ascended from the cherubim, upon which it was, to the open court of the house.

**9:4** And He called to the man clothed in the long robe, who had a belt upon his loins, and said to him, "Pass through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men who groan and who grieve over all the lawless deeds that are committed in their midst."

**9:5** And to these He said in my hearing, "Go after him into the city and strike; do not spare with your eyes, and do not have mercy.

**9:6** Slay old man and young man and maiden and little children and women to utter destruction, but do not draw near to anyone upon whom is the mark; begin at My sanctuary." And they began with the elder men who were inside the house.

**9:7** And He said to them, "Defile the house, and fill the streets with the dead as you go out, and strike."

**9:8** And it came to pass, while they were striking, that I fell upon my face, and cried out and said, "Alas, O Lord! Are You utterly destroying the remnant of Israel by pouring out Your fury upon Jerusalem?"

**9:9** And He said to me, "The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah has become exceedingly, exceedingly great, for the land has been filled with many peoples, and the city has been filled with iniquity and uncleanness; for they have said, 'The Lord has forsaken the land; the Lord does not look upon it.'

**9:10** And My eye shall not spare, nor will I have mercy; I have laid their ways upon their heads."

**9:11** And behold, the man who was clothed in the long robe and girded about his loins with the belt answered and said, "I have done just as You commanded me."

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — "A belt of sapphire" (zōnē sappheirou) is a distinctive Septuagint rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a scribe's inkhorn."
- **9:3** — "The open court" (to aithrion) renders the Greek architectural term for an open atrium or courtyard, corresponding to "the threshold" in the Hebrew tradition.
- **9:9** — "Filled with many peoples" (laōn pollōn) preserves a distinctive Septuagint reading shared with 7:23, where the Hebrew tradition reads "blood."
