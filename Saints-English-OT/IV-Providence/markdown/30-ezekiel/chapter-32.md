## Text

**32:1** And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**32:2** "Son of man, take up a lamentation over Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'You have been likened to a lion of the nations, and like the dragon in the sea; you butted in your rivers, and troubled the water with your feet, and trampled your rivers.

**32:3** Thus says the Lord: And I will cast over you the nets of many peoples, and I will draw you up with My hook,

**32:4** and I will stretch you out upon the earth; the plains shall be filled with you, and I will cause all the birds to settle upon you, and I will satisfy all the beasts of all the earth;

**32:5** and I will lay your flesh upon the mountains, and I will fill them with your blood;

**32:6** and the earth shall be watered from your outpourings, from your multitude upon the mountains; I will fill the ravines from you.

**32:7** And when you are extinguished, I will cover heaven and darken its stars; the sun I will cover with a cloud, and the moon shall by no means shine its light.

**32:8** All the shining lights in heaven shall grow dark over you, and I will set darkness upon the land, says the Lord, the Lord.

**32:9** And I will provoke to anger the heart of many peoples when I lead your captivity into the nations, into a land which you did not know.

**32:10** And many nations shall be appalled over you, and their kings shall be astounded with astonishment when My sword flies before their faces, awaiting their own downfall from the day of your downfall.

**32:11** For thus says the Lord: The sword of the king of Babylon shall come upon you

**32:12** with the swords of giants, and I will cast down your strength; all of them are pestilent ones from the nations, and they shall destroy the arrogance of Egypt, and all her strength shall be crushed.

**32:13** And I will destroy all her cattle from beside abundant water, and the foot of man shall no longer trouble it, and the tread of cattle shall by no means trample it.

**32:14** So then shall their waters become still, and their rivers shall flow like oil, says the Lord.

**32:15** When I give Egypt over to destruction, and the land is made desolate together with its fullness, when I scatter all who dwell in it, then they shall know that I am the Lord.'

**32:16** It is a lamentation, and you shall lament it, and the daughters of the nations shall lament it; over Egypt and over all her strength they shall lament her, says the Lord, the Lord."

**32:17** And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**32:18** "Son of man, lament over the strength of Egypt; and the nations shall bring down her daughters dead into the depth of the earth, to those who go down into the pit.

**32:20** In the midst of those slain by the sword they shall fall with him, and all his strength shall lie down.

**32:21** And the giants shall say to you, 'Be in the depth of tumult! Whom do you surpass? Go down and lie with the uncircumcised in the midst of those slain by the sword.'

**32:22** There is Assyria and all its assembly; all the slain were placed there, and their burial is in the depth of the pit, and its assembly was placed all around its tomb, all the slain who fell by the sword,

**32:23** who caused their terror upon the land of life.

**32:24** There is Elam and all its host all around its tomb, all the slain who fell by the sword and who go down uncircumcised into the depth of the earth, who caused their terror in life; and they received their torment with those who go down into the pit

**32:25** in the midst of the slain.

**32:26** There were placed Meshech and Tubal and all their strength all around his tomb, all his slain, all uncircumcised, slain by the sword, who caused their terror in life;

**32:27** and they lay down with the giants who fell from of old, who went down into Hades in weapons of war and laid their swords beneath their heads; and their iniquities came upon their bones, because they terrified all in their life.

**32:28** And you shall lie down in the midst of the uncircumcised, with those who have been slain by the sword.

**32:29** There were placed the rulers of Assyria, who gave its strength to the wound of the sword; these lay down with the slain, with those who go down into the pit.

**32:30** There are the rulers of the north, all the commanders of Assyria, who go down slain; together with their terror and their strength they lay down uncircumcised with those slain by the sword, and they bore their torment with those who go down into the pit.

**32:31** King Pharaoh shall see them, and he shall be comforted over all their strength, says the Lord, the Lord.

**32:32** For I have set his terror in life, and he shall lie down in the midst of the uncircumcised with those slain by the sword, Pharaoh and all his multitude with him, says the Lord, the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **32:1** — The Greek text dates this oracle to the tenth month, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the twelfth month.
- **32:8** — The Greek uses the doubled divine title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), reflecting the Hebrew Adonai YHWH here and in verses 32:16, 32:31, 32:32, and 33:25.
- **32:17** — The Greek specifies the first month, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits the month; verse 32:19 does not stand as a separate verse in the Greek, as its material appears in verse 32:21.
- **32:21** — The Greek reads en bathei thorybou ("in the depth of tumult"), a distinctive textual reading where the Hebrew has "from the midst of Sheol" and some witnesses read bothrou ("pit").
- **32:29** — Here and in verse 32:30, the Greek names Assyria ("the rulers of Assyria" and "commanders of Assyria"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text names Edom in verse 32:29 and the Sidonians in verse 32:30.
