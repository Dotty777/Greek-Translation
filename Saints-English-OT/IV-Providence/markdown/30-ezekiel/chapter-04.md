## Text

**4:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a brick and set it before your face, and you shall depict upon it a city, even Jerusalem,

**4:2** and you shall lay siege against it, and build bulwarks against it, and cast up a palisade against it, and set camps against it, and station the siege engines all around.

**4:3** And you, take for yourself an iron pan, and you shall place it as an iron wall between you and the city; and you shall set your face steadily against it, and it shall be under siege, and you shall besiege it. This is a sign to the sons of Israel.

**4:4** And you shall lie upon your left side, and you shall lay the iniquities of the house of Israel upon it according to the number of the days, one hundred and fifty, that you shall lie upon it, and you shall bear their iniquities.

**4:5** And I have appointed to you their two iniquities for a number of days, one hundred and ninety days, and you shall bear the iniquities of the house of Israel.

**4:6** And you shall complete these, and you shall lie upon your right side, and you shall bear the iniquities of the house of Judah forty days; a day for a year I have appointed to you.

**4:7** And toward the siege of Jerusalem you shall set your face, and you shall make your arm firm, and you shall prophesy against it.

**4:8** And behold, I have placed bonds upon you, and you shall not turn from your one side to your other side until the days of your siege are fulfilled.

**4:9** And you, take for yourself wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet and spelt, and put them into one earthen vessel, and make them into loaves of bread for yourself; and according to the number of the days that you sleep upon your side, one hundred and ninety days, you shall eat them.

**4:10** And your food you shall eat by weight, twenty shekels a day; from time to time you shall eat them.

**4:11** And water by measure you shall drink; a sixth part of a hin from time to time you shall drink.

**4:12** And as a barley cake baked in ashes you shall eat them; in cakes of human dung you shall bake them before their eyes,

**4:13** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord God of Israel: So shall the sons of Israel eat unclean things among the nations.'"

**4:14** And I said, "By no means, O Lord God of Israel! Indeed, my soul has not been defiled in uncleanness, and what is torn by wild beasts or what died of itself I have not eaten from my birth until now, nor has any stale meat entered into my mouth."

**4:15** And He said to me, "Behold, I have given you cattle dung instead of human dung, and you shall prepare your loaves of bread upon it."

**4:16** And He said to me, "Son of man, behold, I am breaking the staff of bread in Jerusalem; and they shall eat bread by weight and in want, and water by measure and in desolation they shall drink,

**4:17** so that they may be in want of bread and water; and a man and his brother shall be brought to ruin, and they shall melt away in their iniquities."

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Septuagint specifies "one hundred and fifty" days here and "one hundred and ninety" days in verses 5 and 9, whereas the Masoretic Text gives no number in verse 4 and reads "three hundred and ninety" days in verses 5 and 9.
- **4:5** — The Greek reads "their two iniquities" (tas dyo adikias auton), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew consonantal text as "two" rather than "years of."
- **4:14** — The Greek kreas heolon ("stale meat" or "meat kept overnight") renders the Hebrew term for tainted sacrificial flesh.
