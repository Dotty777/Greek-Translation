## Text

**39:1** "And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against you, Gog, the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal;

**39:2** and I will gather you and guide you, and I will bring you up from the uttermost north, and I will lead you up upon the mountains of Israel.

**39:3** And I will destroy your bow from your left hand and your arrows from your right hand, and I will cast you down upon the mountains of Israel;

**39:4** and you shall fall, you and all those around you, and the nations that are with you shall be given to multitudes of birds; to every bird and to all the beasts of the field I have given you to be devoured.

**39:5** Upon the face of the field you shall fall, for I have spoken, says the Lord.

**39:6** And I will send fire upon Gog, and the islands shall be inhabited in peace, and they shall know that I am the Lord.

**39:7** And My holy name shall be made known in the midst of My people Israel, and My holy name shall not be profaned any more; and the nations shall know that I am the Lord, the Holy One in Israel.

**39:8** Behold, it is coming, and you shall know that it shall be, says the Lord, the Lord; this is the day of which I spoke.

**39:9** And those who dwell in the cities of Israel shall go out and make fire with the weapons—shields and pikes and bows and arrows and hand-staves and lances—and they shall burn fire with them for seven years;

**39:10** and they shall by no means take wood from the field, nor shall they cut down any from the forests, for they shall burn the weapons with fire; and they shall plunder those who plundered them, and despoil those who despoiled them, says the Lord.

**39:11** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will give Gog a renowned place, a burial place in Israel, the burial ground of those who came near the sea, and they shall wall around the mouth of the ravine;

**39:12** and there they shall bury Gog and all his multitude, and it shall be called Te, the Burial Ground of Gog. And the house of Israel shall bury them, that the land may be cleansed in seven months,

**39:13** and all the people of the land shall bury them; and it shall be a renown to them in the day that I am glorified, says the Lord.

**39:14** And they shall set apart men continually to pass through the land to bury those who remain upon the face of the earth, to cleanse it after the seven months, and they shall make search.

**39:15** And everyone who passes through the land and sees a man's bone shall build a marker beside it, until the buriers bury it in Gai, the Burial Ground of Gog;

**39:16** for indeed the name of the city is Polyandrion; and the land shall be cleansed.'

**39:17** And you, son of man, say, 'Thus says the Lord: Speak to every winged bird and to all the beasts of the field, "Gather yourselves and come; gather from all around to My sacrifice, which I have sacrificed for you, a great sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, and you shall eat flesh and drink blood.

**39:18** You shall eat the flesh of giants, and drink the blood of the princes of the earth—rams and calves and goats, and all the calves are fatted.

**39:19** And you shall eat fat to fullness, and drink blood to drunkenness, from My sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you.

**39:20** And you shall be filled at My table with horse and rider, with giant and every man of war," says the Lord.

**39:21** And I will set My glory among you, and all the nations shall see My judgment which I have executed, and My hand which I have brought upon them.

**39:22** And the house of Israel shall know that I am the Lord their God from this day and onward.

**39:23** And all the nations shall know that because of their sins the house of Israel was led captive, because they broke faith with Me; and I turned My face away from them and delivered them into the hands of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword.

**39:24** According to their uncleannesses and according to their lawless deeds I dealt with them, and I turned My face away from them.

**39:25** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: Now I will turn back the captivity in Jacob, and I will have mercy upon the house of Israel, and I will be zealous for My holy name.

**39:26** And they shall bear their dishonor and the unrighteousness which they committed, when they are settled upon their land in peace, and there shall be no one to make them afraid,

**39:27** when I bring them back from the nations and gather them out of the countries of the nations; and I shall be sanctified in them in the sight of the nations.

**39:28** And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, when I appear to them among the nations.

**39:29** And I will no longer turn My face away from them, because I have poured out My fury upon the house of Israel, says the Lord, the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **39:6** — The Greek reads "upon Gog, and the islands shall be inhabited in peace," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("upon Magog and on those who dwell securely in the coastlands").
- **39:12** — The Greek reads To te ("Te"), a textual form of To gai ("Gai," transliterating Hebrew gay, "valley"), which appears in verse 15; polyandrion denotes a common burial ground for a multitude.
- **39:16** — Polyandrion ("Burial Ground of a Multitude") is the Septuagint's Greek city name corresponding to Hebrew Hamonah.
- **39:28** — The Greek reads "when I appear to them among the nations," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to sending Israel into exile and gathering them back.
- **39:29** — The Greek reads "I have poured out My fury" (ton thymon mou) upon the house of Israel, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "My Spirit."
