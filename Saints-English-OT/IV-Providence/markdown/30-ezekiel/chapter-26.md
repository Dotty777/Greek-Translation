## Text

**26:1** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**26:2** "Son of man, because Tyre has said against Jerusalem, 'Aha! She is shattered! The nations have perished; she has turned to me; she who was full is laid waste!'

**26:3** therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against you, O Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea comes up with its waves.

**26:4** And they shall cast down the walls of Tyre, and cast down your towers; and I will scatter her dust from her, and make her a bare rock.

**26:5** She shall be a drying place for nets in the midst of the sea, for I have spoken, says the Lord; and she shall become plunder for the nations.

**26:6** And her daughters in the plain shall be slain with the sword; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

**26:7** For thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing against you, O Tyre, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the north; he is a king of kings, with horses and chariots and horsemen, and an assembly of very many nations.

**26:8** He shall slay with the sword your daughters who are in the plain; and he shall set a watchpost against you, and build a siege wall around you, and make a rampart all around against you, and an array of weapons, and he shall set his spears opposite you.

**26:9** Your walls and your towers he shall cast down with his swords.

**26:10** Because of the multitude of his horses their dust shall cover you; and at the noise of his horsemen and of the wheels of his chariots your walls shall be shaken, as he enters your gates like one entering a city from a plain.

**26:11** With the hooves of his horses they shall trample all your streets; your people he shall slay with the sword, and the foundation of your strength he shall bring down to the ground.

**26:12** And he shall plunder your wealth, and despoil your possessions; he shall cast down your walls and tear down your desirable houses; and your stones, your timber, and your dust he shall cast into the midst of your sea.

**26:13** And he shall put an end to the multitude of your music, and the sound of your harps shall be heard no more.

**26:14** And I will make you a bare rock; you shall be a drying place for nets; you shall never be built again, for I, the Lord, have spoken, says the Lord.

**26:15** For thus says the Lord, the Lord, to Tyre: Shall not the islands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when a sword is drawn in your midst?

**26:16** And all the princes from the nations of the sea shall come down from their thrones, and take off the turbans from their heads, and strip off their embroidered garments; they shall be seized with astonishment, they shall sit upon the ground and fear their own destruction, and they shall groan over you.

**26:17** And they shall take up a lamentation over you and say to you, 'How have you been destroyed from the sea, O praised city, who put her terror upon all who dwell in her?'

**26:18** And the islands shall be afraid from the day of your fall.

**26:19** For thus says the Lord, the Lord: When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that shall not be inhabited, when I bring up the deep against you, and much water covers you,

**26:20** then I will bring you down to those who go down into the pit, to the people of old, and I will make you dwell in the depths of the earth, like an everlasting desolation, with those who go down into the pit, so that you shall not be inhabited nor rise up upon the land of life.

**26:21** I will make you a destruction, and you shall exist no more forever, says the Lord, the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **26:2** — The Greek spells Tyre as "Sor" (from Hebrew Tsor), rendered here by its standard English name, and reads "the nations have perished" where the Masoretic Text has "the gates of the peoples."
- **26:15** — Here and in verses nineteen and twenty-one, the Greek reads kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), corresponding to the double divine title in Hebrew.
- **26:20** — The Greek reads "nor rise up upon the land of life," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and I will set glory in the land of the living."
