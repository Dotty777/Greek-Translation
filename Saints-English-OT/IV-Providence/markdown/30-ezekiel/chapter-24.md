## Text

**24:1** And the word of the Lord came to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, saying,

**24:2** "Son of man, write down for yourself for a day, from this day from which the king of Babylon has pressed against Jerusalem, from this very day;

**24:3** and speak a parable against the provoking house, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Set on the cauldron and pour water into it,

**24:4** and cast into it the cut pieces, every choice piece, leg and shoulder stripped from the bones,

**24:5** taken from choice beasts, and kindle the bones underneath them. It has boiled, and its bones have been cooked in its midst.

**24:6** Therefore thus says the Lord: O city of blood, a cauldron in which there is rust, and the rust has not gone out of her! Piece by piece she brought it forth; no lot has fallen upon her.

**24:7** For her blood is in her midst; upon a bare rock I have set it; I have not poured it upon the ground to cover it with earth,

**24:8** that wrath might rise up for vengeance, to be avenged. I have put her blood upon a bare rock, that it should not be covered.

**24:9** Therefore thus says the Lord: I also will magnify the people,

**24:10** and I will multiply the wood and kindle the fire, so that the flesh may melt away and the broth may be reduced,

**24:11** and that she may stand upon the coals, so that her bronze may be scorched and heated, and she may melt in the midst of her uncleanness, and her rust may cease,

**24:12** and her abundant rust shall by no means go out of her; her rust shall be put to shame,

**24:13** because you have defiled yourself. And what if you are not cleansed any more until I satisfy My wrath?

**24:14** I, the Lord, have spoken; it shall come, and I will do it. I will not hold back, nor will I have mercy. According to your ways and according to your devices I will judge you, says the Lord. Therefore I will judge you according to your bloodshed, and according to your devices I will judge you, O unclean one, renowned and abounding in provocation.'"

**24:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:16** "Son of man, behold, I am taking from you the desires of your eyes in battle array; you shall not mourn, nor shall you weep.

**24:17** You shall be a groaning of blood, of the loins, of mourning; your hair shall not be braided upon you, and your sandals shall be on your feet; you shall by no means be comforted by their lips, and the bread of men you shall by no means eat."

**24:18** And I spoke to the people in the morning in the manner He commanded me in the evening, and I did in the morning in the manner it was enjoined upon me.

**24:19** And the people said to me, "Will you not declare to us what these things are that you are doing?"

**24:20** And I said to them, "The word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:21** 'Say to the house of Israel, Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am profaning My sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the desires of your eyes, and the things that your souls spare. And your sons and your daughters whom you left behind shall fall by the sword,

**24:22** and you shall do in the manner I have done: by their mouth you shall not be comforted, and the bread of men you shall not eat;

**24:23** and your hair shall be upon your head, and your sandals on your feet; you shall neither mourn nor weep, and you shall waste away in your iniquities, and each one shall comfort his brother.

**24:24** And Ezekiel shall be a sign to you; according to all that you have done, you shall do when these things come; and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**24:25** "And you, son of man, in the day when I take their strength from them, the exaltation of their boasting, the desires of their eyes, and the uplifting of their soul, their sons and their daughters,

**24:26** in that day shall not the one who escapes come to you to declare it in your ears?

**24:27** In that day your mouth shall be opened to the one who escapes; you shall speak and shall no longer be mute. And you shall be a sign to them, and they shall know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — In the Greek text, the Lord speaks in the first person ("I have set it... I have not poured it"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads third person ("she set it... she did not pour it").
- **24:9** — The Greek text reads "magnify the people" (ton laon), a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "make the woodpile great."
- **24:17** — The Greek reads "a groaning of blood, of the loins, of mourning," reflecting an alternative reading of the Hebrew consonants compared to the Masoretic Text ("sigh silently; make no mourning for the dead").
- **24:18** — The Septuagint omits the notice of the death of Ezekiel's wife found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reporting simply that Ezekiel spoke and acted in the morning as commanded.
