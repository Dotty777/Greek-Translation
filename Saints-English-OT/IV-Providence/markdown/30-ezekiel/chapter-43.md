## Text

**43:1** And he brought me to the gate facing toward the east, and he led me out.

**43:2** And behold, the glory of the God of Israel was coming along the way toward the east, and the sound of the camp was like the sound of many redoubling their voices; and the earth shone forth like brightness from the glory round about.

**43:3** And the vision which I saw was like the vision which I saw when I went in to anoint the city, and the vision of the chariot which I saw was like the vision which I saw by the river Chebar; and I fell upon my face.

**43:4** And the glory of the Lord entered into the house by the way of the gate facing toward the east.

**43:5** And the Spirit took me up and brought me into the inner court; and behold, the house was full of the glory of the Lord.

**43:6** And I stood, and behold, a voice came out of the house, of One speaking to me, and the man was standing beside me,

**43:7** and He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man, the place of My throne and the place of the sole of My feet, in which My name shall dwell in the midst of the house of Israel forever? And the house of Israel shall no longer profane My holy name, they and their rulers, by their harlotry and by the murders of the rulers in their midst,

**43:8** when they set My doorway by their doorways and My doorposts beside their doorposts; and they made My wall as adjoining between Me and them, and they profaned My holy name by their iniquities which they committed; and I destroyed them in My wrath and in slaughter.

**43:9** And now let them cast away their harlotry and the murders of their rulers from Me, and I will dwell in their midst forever.

**43:10** And you, son of man, show the house to the house of Israel—and they shall cease from their sins—both its appearance and its arrangement,

**43:11** and they shall receive their punishment for all that they have done. And you shall draw out the house and its exits and its foundation; and all its ordinances and all its laws you shall make known to them and write down before them, and they shall keep all My statutes and all My ordinances and do them.

**43:12** And the plan of the house upon the top of the mountain—all its borders round about are holy of holies."

**43:13** And these are the measurements of the altar by the cubit of a cubit and a handbreadth: a recess of a cubit in depth, and a cubit in breadth, and a border upon its rim round about of a span. And this is the height of the altar:

**43:14** from the depth of the beginning of its hollow to this great propitiatory beneath is two cubits, and the breadth is a cubit. And from the small propitiatory to the great propitiatory is four cubits, and the breadth is a cubit.

**43:15** And the ariel is four cubits, and from the ariel and above the horns is a cubit.

**43:16** And the ariel is twelve cubits in length by twelve cubits, square upon its four sides.

**43:17** And the propitiatory is fourteen cubits in length by fourteen cubits in breadth on its four sides; and its border round about, encircling it, is half a cubit, and its circuit is a cubit round about; and its steps face toward the east.

**43:18** And He said to me, "Son of man, thus says the Lord God of Israel: These are the ordinances of the altar on the day it is made, to offer burnt offerings upon it and to pour out blood against it.

**43:19** And you shall give to the priests, the Levites, who are of the seed of Zadok, who draw near to Me, says the Lord God, to minister to Me, a young bull from the herd for a sin offering.

**43:20** And they shall take of its blood and put it upon the four horns of the altar, and upon the four corners of the propitiatory, and upon the base round about; and they shall make atonement for it.

**43:21** And they shall take the young bull of the sin offering, and it shall be burned in the set-apart place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**43:22** And on the second day they shall take two unblemished kids of the goats for a sin offering, and they shall make atonement for the altar, just as they made atonement with the young bull.

**43:23** And after you have finished the atonement, they shall offer an unblemished young bull from the herd and an unblemished ram from the flock,

**43:24** and you shall present them before the Lord; and the priests shall cast salt upon them and offer them up as burnt offerings to the Lord.

**43:25** For seven days you shall prepare a young goat for a sin offering every day; also a young bull from the herd and a ram from the flock, without blemish, they shall prepare

**43:26** for seven days; and they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and they shall fill their hands.

**43:27** And it shall be from the eighth day and onward, the priests shall offer upon the altar your burnt offerings and your offerings of salvation; and I will accept you, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **43:2** — The Septuagint reads "the sound of the camp was like the sound of many redoubling their voices," contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "like the sound of many waters."
- **43:3** — The Septuagint reads "to anoint the city" rather than "to destroy the city" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and explicitly adds "of the chariot" in describing the vision.
- **43:14** — The Septuagint renders the altar ledges as "propitiatory" (the same term used for the mercy seat in Exodus) and reads "this great propitiatory beneath" in the first clause before referring to "the small propitiatory" in the second.
- **43:15** — "Ariel" preserves the Septuagint transliteration for the altar hearth, and the Greek text reads "and above the horns is a cubit" rather than specifying four horns.
- **43:26** — "Fill their hands" translates the literal Septuagint idiom signifying priestly consecration and ordination.
