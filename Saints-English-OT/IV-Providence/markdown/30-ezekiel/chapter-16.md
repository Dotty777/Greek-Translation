## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**16:2** "Son of man, bear witness to Jerusalem of her lawless deeds,

**16:3** and you shall say, Thus says the Lord to Jerusalem: Your root and your birth are from the land of Canaan; your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.

**16:4** And as for your birth: on the day you were born, you did not bind your breasts, and you were not washed in water, nor were you salted with salt, and you were not wrapped in swaddling cloths,

**16:5** nor did My eye spare you in any of these things, to feel anything for you; and you were cast out upon the face of the field in the crookedness of your soul, on the day you were born.

**16:6** And I passed by you and saw you defiled in the blood, and I said to you, 'Out of your blood, life!'

**16:7** Be multiplied! Like the growth of the field I have made you; and you were multiplied and grew great, and you entered into cities of cities. Your breasts became firm, and your hair grew, yet you were naked and unseemly.

**16:8** And I passed by you and saw you, and behold, it was your time, even a time of those who lodge; and I spread My wings over you and covered your unseemliness, and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, says the Lord, and you became Mine.

**16:9** And I washed you in water, and rinsed away your blood from you, and anointed you with oil;

**16:10** and I clothed you in embroidered garments and shod you with hyacinth, and I girded you with fine linen and wrapped you in delicate fabric;

**16:11** and I adorned you with ornaments, and put bracelets around your hands and a necklace around your neck;

**16:12** and I put a ring on your nose, and round earrings upon your ears, and a crown of glorying upon your head;

**16:13** and you were adorned with gold and silver, and your garments were of fine linen and delicate fabric and embroidered work; you ate fine flour and oil and honey, and you became exceedingly beautiful.

**16:14** And a name went forth for you among the nations on account of your beauty, for it was made perfect in splendor, in the loveliness which I set upon you, says the Lord.

**16:15** You trusted in your beauty and played the harlot because of your name, and poured out your harlotry upon every passerby.

**16:16** And you took some of your garments, and made for yourself stitched idols and committed harlotry upon them; and you shall by no means enter in, nor shall it come to pass.

**16:17** And you took the vessels of your glorying from My gold and from My silver, from what I gave you, and made for yourself male images and committed harlotry with them,

**16:18** and you took your embroidered apparel and clothed them, and you set My oil and My incense before their face,

**16:19** and My loaves which I gave you—fine flour and oil and honey I fed you—and you set them before their face for an aroma of sweet fragrance. And it came to pass, says the Lord,

**16:20** that you took your sons and your daughters whom you bore, and sacrificed them to them to be consumed; as a small matter you committed harlotry,

**16:21** and you slew your children and gave them up in making an expiatory offering of them to them.

**16:22** This was beyond all your harlotry, and you did not remember your infancy, the day when you were naked and unseemly; defiled in your blood, you lived.

**16:23** And it came to pass after all your wicked deeds, says the Lord,

**16:24** that you built for yourself a house of harlotry, and made for yourself a public place in every open square,

**16:25** and at the head of every way you built your houses of harlotry and degraded your beauty, and parted your legs to every passerby and multiplied your harlotry,

**16:26** and you committed harlotry with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, great of flesh, and in many ways you committed harlotry, to provoke Me to anger.

**16:27** And if I stretch out My hand against you, then I will take away your ordinances and deliver you to the souls of those who hate you, the daughters of foreigners who turn you aside from your way. You acted impiously,

**16:28** and you committed harlotry with the daughters of Assyria, and not even so were you satisfied; and you committed harlotry, and you were not filled.

**16:29** And you multiplied your covenants with the land of the Chaldeans, and not even with these were you satisfied.

**16:30** How shall I deal with your daughter, says the Lord, when you do all these works of a harlot woman? And you committed harlotry threefold among your daughters;

**16:31** you built the house of harlotry at the head of every way, and you made your platform in every open square. And you became like a harlot gathering wages,

**16:32** the adulterous woman like you, receiving wages from her husband, was giving wages to all who committed harlotry with her;

**16:33** and you have given wages to all your lovers, and you loaded them down, that they might come to you from every side in your harlotry.

**16:34** And there came to be in you a perverted thing beyond other women in your harlotry, and with you they have committed harlotry in your giving wages, and wages were not given to you; and perverted things came to be in you.

**16:35** Therefore, O harlot, hear the word of the Lord:

**16:36** Thus says the Lord: Because you poured out your bronze, your shame shall also be uncovered in your harlotry toward your lovers, and toward all the devices of your lawless deeds, and in the blood of your children whom you gave to them.

**16:37** Therefore, behold, I am gathering together all your lovers with whom you mingled, and all whom you loved, together with all whom you hated; and I will gather them against you from every side and uncover your wicked deeds before them, and they shall see all your shame;

**16:38** and I will take vengeance on you with the vengeance of an adulteress, and I will set you in blood of wrath and jealousy.

**16:39** And I will deliver you into their hands, and they shall tear down your house of harlotry and pull down your platform, and they shall strip you of your garments and take the vessels of your glorying, and leave you naked and unseemly.

**16:40** And they shall bring multitudes against you, and they shall stone you with stones, and slaughter you with their swords,

**16:41** and they shall burn your houses with fire, and execute acts of vengeance upon you before many women; and I will turn you away from harlotry, and you shall by no means give wages anymore.

**16:42** And I will let loose My wrath upon you, and My jealousy shall be taken away from you; and I will rest, and I will by no means be anxious anymore.

**16:43** Because you did not remember your infancy, and you grieved Me in all these things, behold, I also have put your ways upon your head, says the Lord; and thus you have committed impiety upon all your lawless deeds.

**16:44** These are all the things which they have spoken against you in a proverb, saying, 'As is the mother,

**16:45** so is the daughter.' You are your mother's daughter, who cast off her husband and her children, and the sisters of your sisters, who cast off their husbands and their children; your mother was a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.

**16:46** Your elder sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who dwells on your left; and your sister, younger than you, who dwells on your right, is Sodom and her daughters.

**16:47** And not even so did you walk in their ways, nor did you act according to their lawless deeds; within a little you even surpassed them in all your ways.

**16:48** As I live, says the Lord, Sodom, she and her daughters, has not done as you and your daughters have done.

**16:49** Moreover, this was the iniquity of Sodom your sister: pride; in fullness of bread and in abundance she and her daughters lived in wanton luxury; this belonged to her and to her daughters; and they did not uphold the hand of the poor and needy,

**16:50** and they were haughty and committed iniquities before Me, and I took them away as I saw.

**16:51** And Samaria did not sin according to half of your sins; and you multiplied your lawless deeds beyond them, and justified your sisters by all your lawless deeds which you have done.

**16:52** You also, bear your torment by which you corrupted your sisters in your sins in which you acted lawlessly beyond them, and you justified them beyond yourself; you also, be ashamed and receive your dishonor in your justifying your sisters.

**16:53** And I will turn back their turnings—the turning of Sodom and her daughters, and I will turn back the turning of Samaria and her daughters, and I will turn back your turning in their midst,

**16:54** so that you may bear your torment and be put to dishonor because of all that you have done in provoking Me to anger.

**16:55** And your sister Sodom and her daughters shall be restored as they were from the beginning; and you and your daughters shall be restored as you were from the beginning.

**16:56** And if Sodom your sister was not a report in your mouth in the days of your pride,

**16:57** before your wicked deeds were uncovered, even as now you are a reproach to the daughters of Syria and of all those around her, the daughters of foreigners who surround you on every side,

**16:58** your impieties and your lawless deeds, you have borne them, says the Lord.

**16:59** Thus says the Lord: I will even do to you as you have done, since you dishonored these things to transgress My covenant.

**16:60** Yet I will remember My covenant with you in the days of your infancy, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant.

**16:61** And you shall remember your way, and be utterly put to dishonor when you receive your sisters, those older than you together with those younger than you; and I will give them to you for building up, and not out of your covenant.

**16:62** And I will establish My covenant with you, and you shall know that I am the Lord;

**16:63** so that you may remember and be ashamed, and never open your mouth anymore because of your dishonor, when I make atonement for you according to all that you have done, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **16:4** — The Septuagint reads "you did not bind your breasts" (οὐκ ἔδησας τοὺς μαστούς σου) rather than the Masoretic Text's "your navel cord was not cut."
- **16:7** — The Septuagint reads "cities of cities" (πόλεις πόλεων), contrasting with the Masoretic Text's "finest ornaments."
- **16:8** — The Greek phrase "a time of those who lodge" (καιρὸς καταλυόντων) diverges from the Masoretic Text's "time for love."
- **16:30** — The Septuagint reads "How shall I deal with your daughter" and "committed harlotry threefold among your daughters," diverging sharply from the Masoretic Text's "How degenerate is your heart... the deeds of a brazen harlot."
- **16:31** — Unlike the Masoretic Text ("you were not like a harlot, because you scorned payment"), the Septuagint states that Jerusalem "became like a harlot gathering wages," followed in verse 32 by taking wages from her husband and paying them to those who committed harlotry with her.
- **16:36** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew literally as "your bronze" (τὸν χαλκόν σου) and renders the term for idols as "devices" (ἐνθυμήματα).
- **16:53** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew idiom for restoring fortunes literally as "turn back their turnings" (ἀποστρέψω τὰς ἀποστροφὰς αὐτῶν).
- **16:57** — The Septuagint reads "Syria" (Συρίας, reflecting Hebrew Aram) where many Masoretic manuscripts read "Edom," and translates "Philistines" literally as "foreigners" (ἀλλοφύλων).
- **16:61** — The Septuagint reads "for building up" (εἰς οἰκοδομήν), reading the Hebrew consonants as "to build" rather than the Masoretic Text's "as daughters."
