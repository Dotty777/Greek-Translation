## Text

**41:1** And he brought me into the temple, of which he measured the vestibule: six cubits in width on this side,

**41:2** and six cubits the breadth of the vestibule on that side; and the breadth of the gateway was ten cubits, and the side walls of the gateway were five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side. And he measured its length, forty cubits, and the breadth, twenty cubits.

**41:3** And he went into the inner court and measured the jamb of the doorway, two cubits; and the doorway, six cubits; and the side walls of the doorway, seven cubits on this side and seven cubits on that side.

**41:4** And he measured the length of the doors, forty cubits, and a breadth of twenty cubits before the face of the temple. And he said, "This is the Holy of Holies."

**41:5** And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits, and the breadth of the side chamber, four cubits all around,

**41:6** and the side chambers, side chamber upon side chamber, thirty, three times, twice; and there was a space in the wall of the house among the side chambers round about, for those who take hold to see, so that they might not touch the walls of the house at all.

**41:7** And the breadth of the upper side chamber was according to the addition from the wall, toward the upper level round about the house, so that it might be widened above, and so that from the lower chambers they might ascend to the upper chambers, and from the cornices to the third-story chambers,

**41:8** and the thrael of the house was a height round about; the space of the side chambers was equal to a reed of six cubits. The spaces

**41:9** and the breadth of the wall of the side chamber on the outside were five cubits, and the remaining spaces that were between the side chambers of the house

**41:10** and between the chambers were twenty cubits in breadth, encircling the house round about.

**41:11** And the doors of the chambers opened toward the remaining space of the one door toward the north; and the one door was toward the south, and the breadth of the light of the remaining space was five cubits in width all around.

**41:12** And the open-air structure in front of the remaining space toward the sea was seventy cubits; the width of the dividing wall was five cubits in breadth all around, and its length was ninety cubits.

**41:13** And he measured opposite the house a length of one hundred cubits, and the remaining spaces and the partitions and their walls were one hundred cubits in length,

**41:14** and the breadth before the face of the house and the remaining spaces opposite were one hundred cubits.

**41:15** And he measured the length of the partition in front of the remaining space of the parts behind that house, and the remaining spaces on this side and on that side were one hundred cubits in length. And the temple, and the corners, and the outer vestibule were paneled.

**41:16** And there were latticed windows, light openings round about for the three so as to look out; and the house and the adjoining parts were paneled with wood round about, and the floor, and from the floor to the windows, and the windows folded open in three parts for looking out.

**41:17** And even to near the inner part and even to the outer part, and upon the whole wall round about, on the inside and on the outside,

**41:18** were carved cherubim; and palm trees were between cherub and cherub. Each cherub had two faces,

**41:19** the face of a man toward the palm tree on this side and on that side, and the face of a lion toward the palm tree on this side and on that side; the house was carved all around;

**41:20** from the floor to the paneling, the cherubim and the palm trees were carved.

**41:21** And the sanctuary and the temple opened out foursquare; before the face of the holy places was a vision like the appearance

**41:22** of a wooden altar, its height three cubits, and its length two cubits, and its breadth two cubits; and it had horns, and its base and its walls were of wood. And he said to me, "This is the table that is before the presence." And the temple had two doorways,

**41:23** and the sanctuary had two doorways;

**41:24** for the two swinging doorways, there were two doors for the one, and two doors for the second door.

**41:25** And there was carved work upon them, and upon the doorways of the temple were cherubim, and palm trees according to the carved work of the holy places, and solid timbers before the face of the vestibule on the outside,

**41:26** and hidden windows. And he measured on this side and on that side, to the roofings of the vestibule, and the side chambers of the house were braced together.

## Translator Notes

- **41:4** — The Greek reads "the length of the doors, forty cubits," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "its length, twenty cubits."
- **41:6** — The Greek literally reads "thirty, three times, twice" and adds "for those who take hold to see" regarding the recessed space in the wall supporting the side chambers.
- **41:8** — "Thrael" renders the untranslated Greek transliteration thrael, denoting an architectural elevation around the house where the Hebrew reads "I saw."
- **41:22** — The Greek reads literally "before the face" (here "before the presence") without "of the Lord," explicitly specifies the breadth as two cubits, and reads "horns" and "base" where the Hebrew reads "corners" and "length."
