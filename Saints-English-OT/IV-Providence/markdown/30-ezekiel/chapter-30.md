## Text

**30:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:2** "Son of man, prophesy and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Woe, woe for the day!

**30:3** For the day is near, the day of the Lord; it shall be the end of the nations.

**30:4** And a sword shall come upon the Egyptians, and there shall be turmoil in the land of Ethiopia; and the slain shall fall together in Egypt, and her foundations shall fall down together.

**30:5** Persians and Cretans and Lydians and Libyans, and all the mixed multitude, and some of the sons of My covenant shall fall by the sword in her.

**30:6** And the supports of Egypt shall fall, and the pride of her strength shall come down from Migdol to Syene; by the sword they shall fall in her, says the Lord.

**30:7** And it shall be desolated in the midst of desolated countries, and their cities shall be in the midst of desolated cities;

**30:8** and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I set fire upon Egypt and all who help her are shattered.

**30:9** In that day messengers shall go forth hastening to destroy Ethiopia, and there shall be turmoil among them in the day of Egypt; for behold, it is coming.

**30:10** Thus says the Sovereign Lord: I will also destroy the multitude of the Egyptians by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon,

**30:11** of him and his people—pestilent men from the nations sent to destroy the land; and they shall all unsheathe their swords against Egypt, and the land shall be filled with the slain.

**30:12** And I will make their rivers desolate, and I will destroy the land and its fullness by the hands of strangers; I, the Lord, have spoken.

**30:13** For thus says the Sovereign Lord: I will also destroy the nobles from Memphis and the rulers of Memphis from the land of Egypt, and they shall be no more.

**30:14** And I will destroy the land of Pathros, and set fire upon Tanis, and execute vengeance in Diospolis.

**30:15** And I will pour out My fury upon Sais, the strength of Egypt, and I will destroy the multitude of Memphis.

**30:16** And I will set fire upon Egypt, and Syene shall be thrown into utter turmoil; and in Diospolis there shall be a breach, and waters shall be poured out.

**30:17** The young men of Heliopolis and Bubastis shall fall by the sword, and the women shall go into captivity;

**30:18** and in Tahpanhes the day shall grow dark when I break there the scepters of Egypt, and the pride of her strength shall perish there; a cloud shall cover her, and her daughters shall be led away captive.

**30:19** Thus I will execute judgment in Egypt, and they shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**30:20** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:21** "Son of man, I have broken the arms of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and behold, they have not been bound up to be given healing, to have a poultice placed upon him, to be given strength to grasp a sword.

**30:22** Therefore thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I will break his arms, both the strong and the outstretched, and I will strike down his sword out of his hand;

**30:23** and I will scatter Egypt among the nations and winnow them into the countries,

**30:24** and I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put My sword into his hand, and he shall bring it upon Egypt and plunder her plunder and despoil her spoil.

**30:25** And I will fortify the arms of the king of Babylon, while the arms of Pharaoh shall fall; and they shall know that I am the Lord when I put My sword into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall stretch it out over the land of Egypt.

**30:26** And I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and winnow them into the countries; and all shall know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — The Greek reads "the end of the nations" (peras ethnon) and omits the phrase "a day of clouds" found in the Masoretic Text.
- **30:5** — The Greek names "Persians and Cretans and Lydians and Libyans" along with "the sons of My covenant" (compare Cush, Put, Lud, and "the land of the covenant" in the Masoretic Text).
- **30:14** — Here and in verses 15-17, the Greek uses Hellenistic names for Egyptian cities: Tanis (Hebrew Zoan), Diospolis (Thebes, Hebrew No), Sais (Hebrew Sin), Syene (Hebrew Sin in 30:16), Heliopolis (Hebrew Aven/On), and Bubastis (Hebrew Pibeseth).
- **30:16** — The Greek closes the verse with "and waters shall be poured out" (compare "and Noph shall have adversaries daily" in the Masoretic Text).
- **30:24** — The Greek has the king of Babylon bringing the sword upon Egypt to plunder and despoil her, where the Masoretic Text speaks of breaking Pharaoh's arms and his groaning.
