## Text

**34:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**34:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel; prophesy and say to the shepherds, 'Thus says the Sovereign Lord: O shepherds of Israel, do shepherds feed themselves? Do not shepherds feed the sheep?

**34:3** Behold, you consume the milk and clothe yourselves with the wool, and you slaughter the fatling, but you do not feed My sheep.

**34:4** You did not strengthen the weak, and you did not restore the sick to health; you did not bind up the broken, and you did not bring back the straying; you did not seek the lost, and you wore down the strong with toil.

**34:5** And My sheep were scattered because there were no shepherds, and they became food for all the wild beasts of the field.

**34:6** And My sheep were scattered on every mountain and upon every high hill; and over the face of the earth they were scattered, and there was no one seeking them out, nor anyone bringing them back.

**34:7** Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the Lord:

**34:8** As I live, says the Sovereign Lord, surely because My sheep became plunder, and My sheep became food for all the wild beasts of the plain because there were no shepherds, and the shepherds did not seek out My sheep, but the shepherds fed themselves and did not feed My sheep,

**34:9** therefore, shepherds,

**34:10** thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I am against the shepherds, and I will require My sheep from their hands, and I will turn them away from shepherding My sheep, and the shepherds shall feed them no more; and I will deliver My sheep from their mouth, and they shall no longer be food for them.

**34:11** For thus says the Lord: Behold, I Myself will seek out My sheep and watch over them.

**34:12** Just as a shepherd seeks his flock on a day when there is gloom and cloud in the midst of separated sheep, so I will seek out My sheep and bring them out from every place where they were scattered on a day of cloud and gloom.

**34:13** And I will bring them out from the nations and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them into their own land; and I will feed them upon the mountains of Israel, and in the valleys, and in every dwelling place of the land.

**34:14** In good pasture I will feed them, on the high mountain of Israel. And their sheepfolds shall be there, and they shall lie down, and there they shall rest in good abundance, and in rich pasture they shall graze upon the mountains of Israel.

**34:15** I Myself will feed My sheep, and I Myself will give them rest, and they shall know that I am the Lord. Thus says the Sovereign Lord:

**34:16** I will seek the lost, and I will bring back the straying, and I will bind up the broken, and I will strengthen the failing, and I will guard the strong, and I will feed them with judgment.

**34:17** And as for you, O sheep, thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I will judge between sheep and sheep, rams and male goats.

**34:18** And is it not enough for you that you grazed in the good pasture, and trampled down the remainder of your pasture with your feet? And that you drank the settled water, and troubled the rest with your feet?

**34:19** And did My sheep graze on what was trampled by your feet, and drink the water troubled by your feet?

**34:20** Therefore thus says the Sovereign Lord: Behold, I will judge between the strong sheep and the weak sheep.

**34:21** With your sides and your shoulders you pushed, and with your horns you butted, and every failing one you pressed out.

**34:22** And I will save My sheep, and they shall no longer be plunder; and I will judge between ram and ram.

**34:23** And I will raise up over them one shepherd, and he shall shepherd them—My servant David—and he shall be their shepherd.

**34:24** And I, the Lord, will be God to them, and David shall be prince in their midst; I, the Lord, have spoken.

**34:25** And I will make a covenant of peace with David, and I will remove evil wild beasts from the land; and they shall dwell in the wilderness and sleep in the forests.

**34:26** And I will set them around My mountain; and I will give you the rain, a rain of blessing.

**34:27** And the trees of the plain shall yield their fruit, and the earth shall yield its strength, and they shall dwell upon their land in hope of peace; and they shall know that I am the Lord when I break their yoke, and I will deliver them from the hand of those who enslaved them,

**34:28** and they shall no longer be plunder for the nations, and the wild beasts of the earth shall never again devour them; and they shall dwell in hope, and there shall be no one to terrify them.

**34:29** And I will raise up for them a plant of peace, and they shall no longer perish by famine in the land, and they shall never again bear the reproach of the nations.

**34:30** And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, and they are My people. O house of Israel, says the Lord,

**34:31** you are My sheep and the sheep of My flock, and I am the Lord your God, says the Sovereign Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The Greek doubles the divine title (kyrios Kyrios, literally "Lord, the Lord"), rendered here as "the Sovereign Lord" to distinguish it from simple Kyrios ("the Lord") and Kyrios ho theos ("the Lord God"). The Greek also opens with the address "O shepherds" and a rhetorical question rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "Woe to the shepherds... who feed themselves."
- **34:3** — The Greek reads "the milk" (to gala) rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "the fat," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants.
- **34:4** — The Greek uses the rare verb somatopoieo ("restore to bodily health") and concludes "you wore down the strong with toil," where the Masoretic Hebrew speaks of ruling the flock with force and harshness.
- **34:16** — The Greek reads "I will guard the strong" (to ischyron phylaxo), contrasting with the Masoretic Hebrew "the fat and the strong I will destroy."
- **34:25** — The Greek specifies "with David" (to Dauid) rather than "with them" as in the Masoretic Hebrew; similarly in 34:29 the Greek reads "a plant of peace" (phyton eirenes) rather than "a planting of renown."
