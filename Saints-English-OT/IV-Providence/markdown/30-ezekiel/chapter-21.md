## Text

**21:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:2** "Son of man, set your face toward Teman, and look toward Dagon, and prophesy against the chief forest of the Negeb,

**21:3** and you shall say to the forest of the Negeb, 'Hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord, the Lord: Behold, I kindle a fire in you, and it shall devour in you the green tree and every dry tree. The kindled flame shall not be quenched, and every face from the east to the north shall be burned by it.

**21:4** And all flesh shall know that I, the Lord, have kindled it; it shall not be quenched.'"

**21:5** Then I said, "By no means, O Lord, Lord! They say to me, 'Is this not a parable that is being spoken?'"

**21:6** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:7** "Therefore prophesy, son of man; set your face toward Jerusalem and look upon their holy places, and you shall prophesy against the land of Israel.

**21:8** And you shall say to the land of Israel, 'Behold, I am against you, and I will draw My sword out of its sheath, and I will destroy from you the unrighteous and the lawless.

**21:9** Because I will destroy from you the unrighteous and the lawless, therefore My sword shall go forth out of its sheath against all flesh from the east to the north,

**21:10** and all flesh shall know that I, the Lord, have drawn My sword out of its sheath; it shall not return anymore.'

**21:11** And you, son of man, groan deeply with the breaking of your loins, and in anguish you shall groan before their eyes.

**21:12** And it shall come to pass, if they say to you, 'Why are you groaning?' then you shall say, 'Because of the report, for it is coming; and every heart shall be shattered, and all hands shall grow feeble, and all flesh and every spirit shall faint, and all thighs shall be defiled with moisture. Behold, it is coming, says the Lord, the Lord.'"

**21:13** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:14** "Son of man, prophesy and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: Say, Sword, sword, be sharpened and be enraged,

**21:15** that you may slaughter victims; be sharpened that you may become gleaming, ready for destruction. Slay, despise, cast aside every tree!

**21:16** And he has given it ready for his hand to grasp; the sword has been whetted, it is ready to be given into the hand of the slayer.'

**21:17** Cry out and wail, son of man, for this has come among My people, it is among all the leaders of Israel; they shall dwell as sojourners—by the sword it has come among My people! Therefore strike upon your hand,

**21:18** for it has been justified; and shall a tribe be cast aside? It shall not be, says the Lord, the Lord.

**21:19** And you, son of man, prophesy and strike hand against hand, and double the sword; the third is a sword of the slain, the great sword of the slain. And you shall strike them with terror,

**21:20** that the heart may not be shattered, and that the weak may be multiplied at every gate. They have been delivered up to the slaughter of the sword; well is it made for slaughter, well is it made for gleaming.

**21:21** Pass through, be sharpened on the right and on the left, wherever your face is roused.

**21:22** And I also will strike My hand against My hand, and I will let loose My wrath; I, the Lord, have spoken."

**21:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:24** "And you, son of man, appoint for yourself two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to enter; both of them shall go forth from one land. And a signpost at the head of the road to a city—at the head of the road you shall appoint it—for the sword to enter against Rabbath of the sons of Ammon, and against Judea, and against Jerusalem in the midst of her,

**21:25** for a sword to enter against Rabbath of the sons of Ammon, and against Judea, and against Jerusalem in the midst of her.

**21:26** For the king of Babylon shall stand upon the ancient road, at the head of the two roads, to practice divination, to shake the rod, and to inquire of the carved images, and to inspect the liver on his right.

**21:27** The divination came against Jerusalem, to cast up a palisade, to open the mouth in a war cry, to lift up the voice with shouting, to set up a palisade against her gates, and to cast up a mound, and to build siege towers.

**21:28** And he is to them like one practicing divination before them, and he brings his iniquities to remembrance, that they may be remembered.

**21:29** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Because you have brought your iniquities to remembrance, in that your ungodly deeds have been uncovered, so that your sins are seen in all your impieties and in your practices—because you have brought them to remembrance, by these you shall be captured.

**21:30** And you, O lawless, profane leader of Israel, whose day has come in the time of the end of iniquity,

**21:31** thus says the Lord: You have removed the turban and put on the crown; it shall not remain like this for her. You have humbled what is high, and you have exalted what is low.

**21:32** Iniquity, iniquity, iniquity I will make her! Woe to her! Such shall she be until he comes to whom it belongs, and I will deliver it to him.'

**21:33** And you, son of man, prophesy and say, 'Thus says the Lord concerning the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach,' and you shall say, 'Sword, sword, drawn for slaughter and drawn for utter destruction, rise up that you may gleam!

**21:34** In your vain vision and in your divining of lies, to deliver you over upon the necks of the lawless slain, the day has come in the time of the end of iniquity.

**21:35** Turn back; do not lodge in this place where you were born. In your own land I will judge you.

**21:36** And I will pour out My wrath upon you; in the fire of My wrath I will blow upon you, and I will deliver you into the hands of barbarous men, contrivers of destruction.

**21:37** In the fire you shall be consumed; your blood shall be in the midst of your land. There shall by no means be remembrance of you, for I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Greek text reads "Dagon" (where the Masoretic Hebrew has "the south") and transliterates "Negeb" as a proper name, describing it as "the chief forest of the Negeb."
- **21:3** — The Greek uses the doubled divine title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") and reads "from the east to the north" (so also 21:9), whereas the Hebrew has "from south to north."
- **21:8** — The Greek reads "the unrighteous and the lawless" (repeated in 21:9), differing from the Masoretic Hebrew reading "the righteous and the wicked."
- **21:25** — In this Greek critical text, 21:25 repeats the final clause of 21:24 concerning the sword entering against Rabbath, Judea, and Jerusalem; both verses are translated as they stand.
- **21:32** — The Greek repeats "iniquity" three times (where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "ruin" or "overturn") and frames 21:31 with past-tense accusations rather than imperatives.
