## Text

**33:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**33:2** "Son of man, speak to the sons of your people and you shall say to them, 'A land upon which I bring a sword, and the people of the land take one man from among them and appoint him for themselves as a watchman,

**33:3** and he sees the sword coming upon the land, and blows the trumpet and gives warning to the people,

**33:4** and anyone who indeed hears the sound of the trumpet and does not take heed, and the sword comes and overtakes him, his blood shall be upon his head.

**33:5** Because having heard the sound of the trumpet he did not take heed, his blood shall be upon himself; yet this one, because he took heed, has delivered his own soul.

**33:6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not signal with the trumpet, and the people are not warned, and the sword comes and takes a soul from among them, that soul was taken because of its own iniquity, yet the blood I will require from the hand of the watchman.'

**33:7** And as for you, son of man, I have appointed you a watchman for the house of Israel; and you shall hear a word from My mouth.

**33:8** When I say to the sinner, 'You shall surely die,' and you do not speak to warn the ungodly man from his way, that lawless man shall die in his iniquity, but his blood I will require from your hand.

**33:9** Yet if you forewarn the ungodly man concerning his way, to turn back from it, and he does not turn back from his way, he shall die in his ungodliness, but you have delivered your own soul.

**33:10** And you, son of man, say to the house of Israel, 'Thus you have spoken, saying: Our errors and our iniquities are upon us, and in them we waste away; how then shall we live?'

**33:11** Say to them, 'As I live, thus says the Lord, I do not desire the death of the ungodly, as much as that the ungodly turn back from his way and live. Turn back completely from your way; and why do you die, O house of Israel?'

**33:12** Say to the sons of your people, 'The righteousness of the righteous shall by no means deliver him in the day in which he goes astray, and the iniquity of the ungodly shall by no means harm him in the day in which he turns back from his iniquity; and a righteous man shall by no means be able to be saved.

**33:13** When I say to the righteous man: This man has trusted in his righteousness—and he commits iniquity—none of his righteous deeds shall be remembered; in his unrighteousness which he has committed, in it he shall die.

**33:14** And when I say to the ungodly man: You shall surely die—and he turns back from his sin, and executes judgment and righteousness,

**33:15** and restores a pledge and repays what he seized by robbery, and walks in the statutes of life so as to commit no unrighteousness, he shall surely live and shall by no means die;

**33:16** none of his sins which he has sinned shall be remembered; because he has executed judgment and righteousness, by them he shall live.'

**33:17** Yet the sons of your people shall say, 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' Yet it is their own way that is not straight.

**33:18** When a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and commits iniquities, then he shall die in them.

**33:19** And when the sinner turns back from his iniquity and executes judgment and righteousness, by them he himself shall live.

**33:20** And this is what you have said, 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' Each one according to his ways I will judge you, O house of Israel."

**33:21** And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the fifth day of the month, of our captivity, that the one who had escaped came to me from Jerusalem, saying, "The city has been captured."

**33:22** And the hand of the Lord came upon me in the evening before he arrived, and He opened my mouth until he came to me in the morning; and once my mouth was opened, it was closed no more.

**33:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**33:24** "Son of man, those who dwell in the desolate places in the land of Israel are saying, 'Abraham was one, and he possessed the land, yet we are many; to us the land has been given for a possession.'

**33:25** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord:

**33:27** As I live, surely those in the desolate places shall fall by swords, and those on the face of the plain shall be given to the beasts of the field for food, and those in the walled places and those in the caves I will slay with death.

**33:28** And I will make the land a desolation, and the arrogance of her strength shall perish; and the mountains of Israel shall be made desolate because there is no one passing through.

**33:29** And they shall know that I am the Lord; and I will make their land a desolation, and it shall be laid waste because of all their abominations which they have committed.'

**33:30** And you, son of man—as for the sons of your people who speak about you beside the walls and in the doorways of the houses, they speak one to his brother, saying, 'Let us come together and hear what goes forth from the Lord.'

**33:31** They come to you as a people assembles, and they sit before you and hear your words, yet they will by no means do them; for falsehood is in their mouth, and their heart goes after defilements.

**33:32** And you become to them like the sound of a sweet-toned, well-tuned harp; and they shall hear your words, yet they shall by no means do them.

**33:33** And when it comes to pass, they shall say, 'Behold, it has come!' And they shall know that a prophet was in their midst."

## Translator Notes

- **33:13** — The Greek text omits the clause "that he shall surely live" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text after "When I say to the righteous man," passing directly to his trust in his own righteousness.
- **33:21** — The Greek dates the fugitive's arrival to the twelfth month, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the tenth month.
- **33:25** — The Greek text omits the remainder of verse 33:25 and all of verse 33:26 found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, passing directly from the prophetic formula to the oath in verse 33:27.
- **33:31** — The Greek reads "falsehood" (pseudos) and "defilements" (miasmaton), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of expressions of affection and dishonest gain.
