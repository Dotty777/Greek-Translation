## Text

**17:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**17:2** "Son of man, recount a tale and speak a parable to the house of Israel,

**17:3** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: The great eagle with great wings, long in span, full of talons, who has the design to enter into Lebanon—he took the choicest parts of the cedar,

**17:4** the tips of the tender shoots he plucked off, and he carried them into the land of Canaan; in a walled city he set them.

**17:5** And he took of the seed of the land and put it in the plain as a plant beside abundant water; as a thing watched over he appointed it.

**17:6** And it sprang up and became a weak and small vine, so that it might display its branches toward it, and its roots were beneath it. So it became a vine, and put forth shoots, and stretched out its trailing branches.

**17:7** And there was another great eagle, with great wings and many talons; and behold, this vine was entwined toward him, and its roots were toward him, and it sent forth its branches to him, that he might water it together with the clod of its planting.

**17:8** In a good plain beside abundant water it thrives, to produce sprouts and bear fruit, to become a great vine.'

**17:9** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: Shall it prosper? Shall not the roots of its tenderness and the fruit rot, and shall not all its early sprouts wither, and not with a mighty arm nor with many people to tear it up from its roots?

**17:10** And behold, it thrives; shall it indeed prosper? Shall it not, the moment the scorching wind touches it, be withered? Together with the clod of its sprouting it shall wither away.'"

**17:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**17:12** "Son of man, say now to the provoking house, 'Do you not know what these things were?' Say, 'When the king of Babylon comes against Jerusalem, he shall take her king and her rulers and bring them to himself to Babylon.

**17:13** And he shall take of the seed of the kingdom, and make a covenant with him, and bring him under a curse; and the leaders of the land he shall take,

**17:14** that it may become a weak kingdom, not lifting itself up at all, so as to keep his covenant and uphold it.

**17:15** Yet he shall revolt from him by sending out his own messengers to Egypt, that it may give him horses and many people. Shall he prosper? Shall the one who does contrary things be delivered? And transgressing a covenant, shall he be delivered?

**17:16** As I live, says the Lord, surely in the place of the king who made him king—he who dishonored My curse and who transgressed My covenant—with him in the midst of Babylon he shall die.

**17:17** And neither with a mighty force nor with a great throng shall Pharaoh wage war with him, in the casting up of a rampart and in the building of siege-engines to cut off souls.

**17:18** And he dishonored an oath by transgressing a covenant; and behold, I have given his hand, and all these things he has done to him; shall he be saved?'

**17:19** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: As I live, surely My oath which he dishonored, and My covenant which he transgressed—I will indeed bring it upon his own head.

**17:20** And I will spread out the net over him, and he shall be caught in his snare.

**17:21** In all his battle array they shall fall by the sword, and the remnant I will scatter to every wind; and you shall know that I, the Lord, have spoken.

**17:22** For thus says the Lord: I Myself will also take from the choice shoots of the cedar, from the top; their hearts I will pluck off, and I Myself will plant them upon a high mountain,

**17:23** and I will suspend it on the lofty mountain of Israel and plant it; and it shall bring forth a sprout and produce fruit and become a great cedar; and every bird shall rest beneath it, and every winged creature shall rest under its shadow; its branches shall be restored.

**17:24** And all the trees of the plain shall know that I am the Lord, who brings low the high tree and exalts the low tree, and dries up the green tree and makes the dry tree flourish. I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Greek reads "full of talons" and "who has the design," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "full of plumage" and "of variegated colors."
- **17:12** — The Greek frames the Babylonian king's arrival prospectively ("When the king of Babylon comes... he shall take"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks in the past tense.
- **17:18** — The Greek reads the first-person verb "I have given his hand," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the third-person "he gave his hand"; similarly, verse 16 reads "My curse" and "My covenant" in the Greek.
- **17:23** — The Greek reads "I will suspend it" (reflecting a Hebrew root meaning "to hang" rather than "lofty") and concludes with "its branches shall be restored," where the Hebrew reads "in the shadow of its branches they shall dwell."
