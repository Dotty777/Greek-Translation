## Text

**1:1** In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying, "Speak to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, of the tribe of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,

**1:2** 'Thus says the Lord Almighty, saying: This people says, "The time has not come to build the house of the Lord."'"

**1:3** And the word of the Lord came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**1:4** "Is it time for you to dwell in your paneled houses, while your house lies desolate?"

**1:5** And now thus says the Lord Almighty: "Direct your hearts now to your ways!

**1:6** You have sown much and brought in little; you have eaten, yet not to fullness; you have drunk, yet not to drunkenness; you have clothed yourselves, yet you were not warmed by them; and he who gathers wages has gathered them into a pierced pouch."

**1:7** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Set your hearts upon your ways.

**1:8** Go up to the mountain and cut timber and build the house, and I will take pleasure in it and be glorified," said the Lord.

**1:9** "You looked for much, and it became little; and it was brought into the house, and I blew it away." Because of this, thus says the Lord Almighty: "Because My house is desolate, while each of you hastens to his own house,

**1:10** therefore the heaven shall hold back from dew, and the earth shall withhold its produce.

**1:11** And I will bring a sword upon the land and upon the mountains, and upon the grain and upon the wine and upon the oil and whatever the earth brings forth, and upon men and upon livestock, and upon all the labors of their hands."

**1:12** And Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, of the tribe of Judah, and Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people heeded the voice of the Lord their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, just as the Lord their God had sent him to them; and the people feared before the presence of the Lord.

**1:13** And Haggai, the messenger of the Lord among the messengers of the Lord, said to the people, "I am with you," says the Lord.

**1:14** And the Lord stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, of the tribe of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of the remnant of all the people; and they entered and performed works in the house of the Lord Almighty, their God,

**1:15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text identifies Zerubbabel as 'of the tribe of Judah' (ek phyles Iouda), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'governor of Judah.'
- **1:4** — The Greek text reads 'your house' (ho de oikos hymon), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek manuscripts read 'this house.'
- **1:11** — The Greek text reads 'a sword' (rhomphaian), reflecting a reading of the unvocalized Hebrew consonants as cherev ('sword') rather than chorev ('drought').
- **1:13** — The Greek text reads 'among the messengers of the Lord' (en angelois Kyriou), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'in the message of the Lord.'
