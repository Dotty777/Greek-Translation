## Text

**2:1** In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the Lord spoke by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:2** "Speak now to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, of the tribe of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to all the remnant of the people, saying:

**2:3** 'Who is there among you who saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? As though it did not exist before you.

**2:4** And now be strong, Zerubbabel, says the Lord, and be strong, Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and let all the people of the land be strong, says the Lord, and work; for I am with you, says the Lord Almighty,

**2:5** and My Spirit stands in your midst; take courage.

**2:6** For the Lord Almighty says: Yet once more I will shake the heaven and the earth, and the sea and the dry land;

**2:7** and I will shake all the nations, and the choice things of all the nations shall come; and I will fill this house with glory, says the Lord Almighty.

**2:8** Mine is the silver and Mine is the gold, says the Lord Almighty.

**2:9** For the latter glory of this house shall be great, beyond the former, says the Lord Almighty; and in this place I will give peace, says the Lord Almighty, and peace of soul as a possession to everyone who builds to raise up this temple.'"

**2:10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the Lord came to Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:11** "Thus says the Lord Almighty: Ask the priests concerning the law, saying,

**2:12** 'If a man takes holy meat in the corner of his garment, and the corner of his garment touches bread, or stew, or wine, or oil, or any food, will it become holy?'" And the priests answered and said, "No."

**2:13** And Haggai said, "If one who is defiled and unclean by a dead body touches any of these, will it be defiled?" And the priests answered and said, "It will be defiled."

**2:14** And Haggai answered and said, "So is this people, and so is this nation before Me, says the Lord, and so are all the works of their hands; and whoever draws near there shall be defiled because of their early-morning gains; they shall be grieved on account of their toils, and you hated those who reprove in the gates.

**2:15** And now lay it to your hearts from this day and backward, before a stone was laid upon a stone in the temple of the Lord,

**2:16** who you were when you cast twenty measures into a barley bin, and there came to be ten measures of barley; and when you went to the wine vat to draw out fifty measures, and there came to be twenty.

**2:17** I struck you with barrenness and with blasting wind and with hail, in all the works of your hands, yet you did not turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**2:18** Apply your hearts now from this day and onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, and from the day on which the temple of the Lord was founded; lay it in your hearts,

**2:19** whether it shall be recognized upon the threshing floor, and whether still the vine and the fig tree and the pomegranate and the olive trees are not bearing fruit—from this day I will bless."

**2:20** And the word of the Lord came a second time to Haggai the prophet, on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,

**2:21** "Speak to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, of the tribe of Judah, saying: 'I am shaking the heaven and the earth, and the sea and the dry land,

**2:22** and I will overthrow the thrones of kings, and I will destroy the strength of the kings of the nations; and I will overthrow chariots and riders, and the horses and their riders shall go down, each by the sword against his brother.

**2:23** In that day, says the Lord Almighty, I will take you, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, My servant, says the Lord, and I will set you as a signet; for I have chosen you, says the Lord Almighty.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Septuagint omits the opening clause present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text concerning the covenant word made when Israel came out of Egypt.
- **2:7** — The Greek neuter plural 'the choice things of all the nations' (ta eklekta panton ton ethnon) refers to the treasured goods of the nations, rendered with a singular verb per Greek idiom.
- **2:9** — The closing clause promising 'peace of soul as a possession to everyone who builds to raise up this temple' is a distinctive Septuagint expansion not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **2:14** — The final lines concerning 'early-morning gains,' grief from toils, and hating 'those who reprove in the gates' are a Septuagint expansion not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, echoing Amos 5:10.
- **2:19** — The Greek clause 'whether it shall be recognized upon the threshing floor' differs substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('Is the seed still in the barn?'), leaving the subject of the verb unexpressed.
