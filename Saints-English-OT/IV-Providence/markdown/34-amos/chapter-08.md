## Text

**8:2** And behold, a fowler's basket. And He said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A fowler's basket." And the Lord said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will no longer continue to pass by him.

**8:3** And the paneled ceilings of the temple shall wail in that day," says the Lord, the Lord. "Many are the fallen in every place; I will cast silence."

**8:4** Hear these things now, you who crush the needy in the morning, and oppress the poor out of the land,

**8:5** saying, "When will the month pass, that we may trade, and the Sabbaths, that we may open the storehouse, to make the measure small, and to make the weight great, and to make an unjust balance,

**8:6** to buy even the poor for silver, and the humble in exchange for sandals, and we will trade from every produce?"

**8:7** The Lord swears by the pride of Jacob: "Surely all your works shall never be forgotten!

**8:8** And shall not the land be troubled because of these things, and everyone who dwells in it mourn? And destruction shall rise up like a river, and shall go down like the river of Egypt."

**8:9** "And it shall come to pass in that day," says the Lord, the Lord, "that the sun shall go down at noon, and the light shall grow dark upon the earth in the daytime.

**8:10** And I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring sackcloth upon every waist, and baldness upon every head; and I will make him like the mourning for a beloved one, and those who are with him like a day of anguish."

**8:11** "Behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "when I will send forth a famine upon the land—not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the word of the Lord.

**8:12** And the waters of the sea shall be shaken, and from the north even to the east they shall run about, seeking the word of the Lord, and they shall by no means find it.

**8:13** In that day the fair virgins and the young men shall faint in thirst,

**8:14** those who swear by the propitiation of Samaria, and those who say, 'As your god lives, O Dan!' and, 'As your god lives, O Beersheba!' And they shall fall, and shall by no means rise again."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Greek angos ixeutou ("a fowler's basket") reflects a distinct Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a basket of summer fruit."
- **8:3** — Greek phatnomata ("paneled ceilings") stands where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the songs of the temple."
- **8:12** — The Septuagint reads "the waters of the sea shall be shaken," whereas the Hebrew reads "they shall wander from sea to sea."
- **8:14** — "Propitiation of Samaria" renders Greek hilasmos (where the Hebrew has "guilt of Samaria"), and the Greek reads "your god, O Beersheba" where the Hebrew reads "the way of Beersheba."
