## Text

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing upon the altar, and He said, "Strike upon the mercy seat, and the porches shall be shaken, and cut through upon the heads of all; and the remnant of them I will slay with the sword. No fugitive among them shall escape, and no survivor among them shall be delivered.

**9:2** Though they hide themselves in Hades, from there My hand shall draw them up; and though they ascend into heaven, from there I will bring them down;

**9:3** though they conceal themselves on the summit of Carmel, from there I will search them out and take them; and though they plunge down from My eyes into the depths of the sea, there I will command the dragon, and it shall bite them;

**9:4** and though they go into captivity before the face of their enemies, there I will command the sword, and it shall slay them; and I will set My eyes upon them for evil and not for good."

**9:5** And the Lord, the Lord God Almighty—He who touches the earth and shakes it, and all who dwell in it shall mourn, and its destruction shall rise up like a river, and shall go down like the river of Egypt;

**9:6** He who builds His ascent into heaven and founds His promise upon the earth, He who summons the water of the sea and pours it out upon the face of the earth—the Lord Almighty is His name.

**9:7** "Are you not like the sons of the Ethiopians to Me, O sons of Israel?" says the Lord. "Did I not bring up Israel from the land of Egypt, and the foreigners from Cappadocia, and the Syrians from the pit?

**9:8** Behold, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the kingdom of the sinners, and I will remove it from the face of the earth; yet I will not utterly remove the house of Jacob," says the Lord.

**9:9** "For I command, and I will winnow the house of Israel among all the nations, just as winnowing is done in a winnowing basket, and no broken fragment shall fall upon the earth.

**9:10** All the sinners of My people shall die by the sword, those who say, 'The evil things shall by no means draw near nor come upon us.'

**9:11** In that day I will raise up the fallen tabernacle of David, and I will rebuild its fallen parts, and its demolished places I will raise up, and I will rebuild it as in the days of old,

**9:12** so that the remnant of mankind may earnestly seek, and all the nations upon whom My name has been called," says the Lord who performs these things.

**9:13** "Behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "when the grain harvest shall overtake the vintage, and the grape shall ripen at seedtime; and the mountains shall drip with sweet wine, and all the hills shall be thickly planted;

**9:14** and I will restore the captivity of My people Israel, and they shall build the wasted cities and dwell in them; and they shall plant a vineyard and drink their wine, and they shall plant gardens and eat their fruits;

**9:15** and I will plant them upon their own land, and they shall by no means ever again be uprooted from the land which I have given them," says the Lord God Almighty.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Greek hilasterion ("mercy seat" or "propitiatory") translates a Hebrew term usually rendered "capitals" of the temple pillars.
- **9:6** — Greek epangelian ("promise") stands where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "His vault."
- **9:7** — The Septuagint uses "the foreigners" (allophyloi, its standard term for the Philistines), "Cappadocia" (for Hebrew Caphtor), and "the pit" (bothros, for Hebrew Kir).
- **9:12** — The Septuagint reads "that the remnant of mankind may earnestly seek" (famous from its citation in Acts 15:17, whereas the Hebrew reads "that they may possess the remnant of Edom"); no direct object is stated after the verb in the Greek here.
- **9:13** — The Septuagint's harvest imagery—"the grain harvest shall overtake the vintage," "the grape shall ripen at seedtime," and "all the hills shall be thickly planted"—differs in wording from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
