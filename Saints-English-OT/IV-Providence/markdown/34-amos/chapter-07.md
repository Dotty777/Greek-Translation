## Text

**7:1** Thus the Lord God showed me: and behold, a brood of locusts coming at dawn, and behold, one creeping locust—Gog the king.

**7:2** And it shall be, if he finishes devouring the grass of the land—and I said, "O Lord, Lord, be merciful! Who shall raise up Jacob? For he is very small.

**7:3** Relent, O Lord, concerning this." "And this shall not be," says the Lord.

**7:4** Thus the Lord showed me: and behold, the Lord summoned judgment by fire, and it devoured the great deep, and devoured the portion of the Lord.

**7:5** And I said, "O Lord, cease now! Who shall raise up Jacob? For he is very small.

**7:6** Relent, O Lord, concerning this." "And this shall by no means come to pass," says the Lord.

**7:7** Thus the Lord showed me: and behold, He was standing upon a wall of adamant, and in His hand was adamant.

**7:8** And the Lord said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "Adamant." And the Lord said to me, "Behold, I am setting adamant in the midst of My people Israel; I will no longer continue to pass by him.

**7:9** And the altars of laughter shall be destroyed, and the sacred rites of Israel shall be made desolate, and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with a sword."

**7:10** Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, "Amos is forming conspiracies against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land is by no means able to bear all his words.

**7:11** For thus says Amos: 'Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall be led away captive from his land.'"

**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, "O seer, go, depart into the land of Judah, and live out your life there, and there you shall prophesy;

**7:13** but at Bethel you shall no longer continue to prophesy, for it is a sanctuary of the king, and it is a house of the kingdom."

**7:14** Then Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I was not a prophet, nor was I the son of a prophet; rather, I was a goatherd and one who pierces sycamore figs.

**7:15** And the Lord took me from the sheep, and the Lord said to me, 'Go, prophesy against My people Israel.'

**7:16** And now hear the word of the Lord: You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and you shall by no means stir up a crowd against the house of Jacob.'

**7:17** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Your wife shall play the harlot in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be measured out by a line, and you yourself shall die in an unclean land, and Israel shall be led away captive from his land.'" Thus the Lord, the Lord showed me:

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Septuagint reads "one creeping locust—Gog the king," a major textual divergence from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "after the king's mowings."
- **7:3** — The Greek uses the imperative "Relent" (literally "Repent") as a continuation of Amos's plea—and likewise in 7:6—whereas the Hebrew reports in narrative past tense that the Lord relented.
- **7:7** — Greek adamas ("adamant," an exceedingly hard stone or metal) stands here and in 7:8 where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a term commonly translated "plumb line" or "tin."
- **7:9** — "Altars of laughter" preserves the Septuagint's literal translation of the Hebrew name Isaac as gelos ("laughter").
- **7:17** — In this Greek versification, the opening clause of the fourth vision ("Thus the Lord, the Lord showed me") concludes 7:17, so chapter 8 begins at 8:2.
