## Text

**5:1** Hear this word of the Lord which I take up against you as a lamentation: "The house of Israel

**5:2** has fallen; it shall never rise again. The virgin of Israel has stumbled upon his land; there is no one to raise her up."

**5:3** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: "As for the city from which a thousand went out, a hundred shall be left; and from which a hundred went out, ten shall be left to the house of Israel."

**5:4** For thus says the Lord to the house of Israel: "Seek Me, and you shall live;

**5:5** but do not seek Bethel, and do not enter into Gilgal, and do not cross over to the Well of the Oath; for Gilgal shall surely be led into captivity, and Bethel shall be as though it did not exist."

**5:6** Seek the Lord and live, lest the house of Joseph blaze up like fire and devour it, and there be no one to quench it for the house of Israel.

**5:7** He who executes judgment on high, and has set righteousness upon the earth;

**5:8** He who makes all things and transforms them, and turns shadow into morning, and darkens day into night; who calls for the water of the sea and pours it out upon the face of the earth—the Lord is His name;

**5:9** He who apportions ruin against strength, and brings distress upon the fortress.

**5:10** They hated him who reproves in the gates, and they abhorred a holy word.

**5:11** Therefore, because they struck the poor with their fists and you accepted choice gifts from them, you have built houses of hewn stone, yet you shall by no means dwell in them; you have planted desirable vineyards, yet you shall by no means drink the wine from them.

**5:12** For I know your many ungodly deeds, and your sins are mighty, trampling down the righteous, taking bribes, and turning aside the needy in the gates.

**5:13** Therefore he who has understanding shall keep silent at that time, for it is a time of wicked men.

**5:14** Seek good and not evil, that you may live; and so the Lord God Almighty shall be with you, just as you have said:

**5:15** "We have hated evil things and loved good things." And restore judgment in the gates, so that the Lord God Almighty may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.

**5:16** Therefore thus says the Lord God Almighty: "In all the streets there shall be wailing, and in all the highways they shall say, 'Woe! Woe!' The farmer shall be called to mourning and wailing, and to those who know lamentation;

**5:17** and in all the roads there shall be wailing, for I will pass through the midst of you," said the Lord.

**5:18** Woe to those who desire the day of the Lord! To what end is this day of the Lord to you? Indeed, it is darkness and not light,

**5:19** just as if a man flees from the face of a lion, and a bear falls upon him, and he leaps into his house and leans his hands against the wall, and a serpent bites him.

**5:20** Is not the day of the Lord darkness, and not light? And is it not gloom, having no brightness?

**5:21** "I have hated, I have rejected your feast days, and I will by no means smell sacrifices in your festive assemblies.

**5:22** For though you offer to Me your burnt offerings and sacrifices, I will not accept them; neither will I look upon your peace offerings of display.

**5:23** Remove from Me the sound of your songs, and I will not listen to the melody of your instruments.

**5:24** And judgment shall roll down like water, and righteousness like an impassable torrent.

**5:25** Did you offer Me slain beasts and sacrifices in the wilderness for forty years, O house of Israel?

**5:26** Yes, you took up the tabernacle of Moloch and the star of your god Rephan, their images which you made for yourselves;

**5:27** and I will carry you away into exile beyond Damascus," says the Lord; God Almighty is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Greek doubles the divine title as kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), rendering the Hebrew compound title.
- **5:5** — The Greek translates the place name Beersheba literally as "the Well of the Oath" (to phrear tou horkou).
- **5:7** — In the Greek text, verses 7 through 9 form a continuous doxology praising the Lord ("He who executes judgment on high... who makes all things and transforms them"), differing from the Masoretic Text.
- **5:14** — The Greek connects "just as you have said" at the close of verse 14 directly to the people's claim in verse 15 ("We have hated evil things and loved good things").
- **5:26** — The Septuagint names "Moloch" and "Rephan" (Raiphan), a form echoed in Stephen's quotation of this passage in Acts 7:43.
