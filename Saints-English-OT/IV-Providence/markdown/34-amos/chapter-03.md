## Text

**3:1** Hear this word which the Lord has spoken against you, O house of Israel, and against every tribe which I brought up out of the land of Egypt, saying:

**3:2** "You only have I known from all the tribes of the earth; therefore I will avenge all your sins upon you."

**3:3** Will two walk together at all, unless they make themselves known to one another?

**3:4** Will a lion roar out of his forest when he has no prey? Will a young lion give his voice out of his den at all, unless he has seized something?

**3:5** Will a bird fall upon the earth without a fowler? Will a snare be sprung upon the earth without catching anything?

**3:6** Will a trumpet sound in a city, and a people not be terrified? Will there be calamity in a city which the Lord has not done?

**3:7** For the Lord God will by no means do anything unless He reveals instruction to His servants the prophets.

**3:8** A lion will roar, and who will not fear? The Lord God has spoken, and who will not prophesy?

**3:9** Proclaim to the regions among the Assyrians and upon the regions of Egypt, and say, "Assemble yourselves upon the mountain of Samaria, and behold many wondrous things in its midst, and the oppression that is in it."

**3:10** "And she did not know what things shall be before her," says the Lord, "those who store up unrighteousness and misery in their regions."

**3:11** Therefore thus says the Lord God: "O Tyre, your land round about shall be made desolate, and he shall bring down your strength from you, and your regions shall be plundered."

**3:12** Thus says the Lord: "Just as when the shepherd snatches out of the mouth of the lion two legs or the lobe of an ear, so shall the sons of Israel who dwell in Samaria be snatched out opposite the tribe, and in Damascus the priests."

**3:13** "Hear and testify against the house of Jacob," says the Lord God Almighty,

**3:14** "for in the day when I avenge the impieties of Israel upon him, I will also take vengeance upon the altars of Bethel, and the horns of the altar shall be demolished and shall fall to the ground.

**3:15** I will confound and strike the colonnaded house upon the summer house; and ivory houses shall perish, and many other houses shall be added," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek reads "unless they make themselves known to one another," reflecting a Hebrew reading "be known" rather than "agree" or "meet by appointment."
- **3:7** — The Greek reads paideian ("instruction" or "discipline") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "His secret counsel."
- **3:9** — The Greek reads "regions among the Assyrians" (for Hebrew "citadels in Ashdod") and "many wondrous things" (for "great tumults").
- **3:11** — The Greek reads "Tyre" (Tyros), taking the Hebrew consonantal text for "adversary" (tsar) as the name of the city Tyre.
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "opposite the tribe, and in Damascus the priests," differing sharply from the Hebrew imagery of the corner of a couch and a bed.
- **3:15** — The Greek renders the winter house as "the colonnaded house" (peripteron) and concludes with "many other houses shall be added" (reading the Hebrew verb for "come to an end" as "be added").
