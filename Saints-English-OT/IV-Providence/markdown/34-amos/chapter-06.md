## Text

**6:1** Woe to those who despise Zion, and to those who trust in the mountain of Samaria! They have harvested the firstfruits of the nations, and they themselves have entered in. O house of Israel,

**6:2** cross over, all of you, and see; and pass on from there to Hamath Rabbah, and go down from there to Gath of the foreigners, the mightiest of all these kingdoms—see whether their borders are greater than your borders.

**6:3** Those who come to an evil day, who draw near and lay hold of false sabbaths;

**6:4** those who sleep upon beds of ivory and live luxuriously upon their couches, and eat kids from the flocks and sucking calves from the midst of the herds;

**6:5** those who clap to the sound of musical instruments—they reckoned these things as abiding and not as fleeting;

**6:6** those who drink strained wine and anoint themselves with the finest ointments, and suffered nothing over the ruin of Joseph.

**6:7** Therefore now they shall be captives from the forefront of the rulers, and the neighing of horses shall be removed from Ephraim.

**6:8** For the Lord has sworn by Himself: "I abhor all the arrogance of Jacob, and I have hated his lands; and I will remove the city with all who dwell in it."

**6:9** And it shall come to pass, if ten are left in one house, that they shall die, and the survivors shall be left behind;

**6:10** and their own relatives shall take them up and strive earnestly to carry their bones out of the house; and one shall say to those who are over the house, "Is there still anyone with you?" And he shall say, "No more." And he shall say, "Keep silent, so as not to mention the name of the Lord."

**6:11** For behold, the Lord commands, and He shall strike the great house into fragments and the small house into clefts.

**6:12** Shall horses pursue among rocks? Shall they keep silent among mares? For you have turned judgment into wrath, and the fruit of righteousness into bitterness—

**6:13** you who rejoice over nothing at all, who say, "Have we not obtained horns by our own strength?"

**6:14** "For behold, I am raising up a nation against you, O house of Israel," says the Lord of hosts, "and they shall afflict you so that you do not enter into Hamath, and as far as the torrent of the west."

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Greek reads "all of you" (pantes) rather than the city name Calneh, and "Gath of the foreigners" (allophylon), the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines.
- **6:3** — The Septuagint reads "who come to an evil day" and "false sabbaths" (sabbaton pseudon), reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew text.
- **6:7** — The Greek text has the distinctive reading "the neighing of horses shall be removed from Ephraim" (chremetismos hippon ex Ephraim).
- **6:12** — The Greek asks "Shall they keep silent among mares?" (ei parasiopesontai en theleiais), reflecting a different sense of the Hebrew root meaning both "plow" and "be silent."
- **6:14** — The Greek renders the southern boundary as "the torrent of the west" (tou cheimarrou ton dysmon), translating the Hebrew term for the Arabah by way of the related word for "evening" or "west."
