## Text

**1:1** The words of Amos, which came in Accarim in Tekoa, which he saw concerning Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**1:2** And he said, "The Lord has sounded forth from Zion, and from Jerusalem He has given His voice; and the pastures of the shepherds mourned, and the summit of Carmel dried up."

**1:3** And the Lord said, "For three impieties of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn away from him, because they sawed with iron saws the women with child among those in Gilead.

**1:4** And I will send forth fire into the house of Hazael, and it shall devour the foundations of the son of Hader.

**1:5** And I will shatter the bars of Damascus, and I will utterly destroy the inhabitants from the plain of On, and I will cut down a tribe from the men of Haran; and the called people of Syria shall be taken captive," says the Lord.

**1:6** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn away from them, because they took captive the captivity of Solomon, to shut them up in Idumea.

**1:7** And I will send forth fire upon the walls of Gaza, and it shall devour its foundations.

**1:8** And I will utterly destroy the inhabitants from Ashdod, and a tribe shall be removed from Ashkelon; and I will bring My hand upon Ekron, and the remnant of the foreigners shall perish," says the Lord.

**1:9** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn away from her, because they shut up the captivity of Solomon in Idumea, and did not remember the covenant of brethren.

**1:10** And I will send forth fire upon the walls of Tyre, and it shall devour her foundations."

**1:11** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of Idumea, and for four, I will not turn away from them, because they pursued his brother with a sword, and he ravaged a mother upon the earth, and he seized his shuddering for a testimony, and he kept his onslaught for victory.

**1:12** And I will send forth fire into Teman, and it shall devour the foundations of its walls."

**1:13** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of the sons of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn away from him, because they ripped open the women with child of the Gileadites, that they might enlarge their own borders.

**1:14** And I will kindle fire upon the walls of Rabbah, and it shall devour its foundations with a cry in the day of war, and it shall be shaken in the days of its consummation;

**1:15** and its kings shall go into captivity, their priests and their rulers together," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text reads "in Accarim" (treating the Hebrew word for herdsmen as a place name) and "concerning Jerusalem" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "concerning Israel").
- **1:3** — The Greek renders the recurring refrain "I will not turn away from him/her/them" (agreeing with each nation) and reads "sawed with iron saws the women with child" rather than "threshed with iron sledges."
- **1:5** — The Greek reads "son of Hader" in verse 4 (for Ben-hadad), "a tribe from the men of Haran" (for "the scepter-holder from Beth-eden"), and "the called people of Syria" (reading Hebrew "to Kir" as "called").
- **1:6** — The Greek reads "the captivity of Solomon" (also in verse 9), reading the Hebrew word for "whole" or "complete" as the name Solomon.
- **1:11** — The Greek diverges markedly from the Hebrew here, reading "ravaged a mother upon the earth" (for "stifled his compassion") and "seized his shuddering for a testimony, and kept his onslaught for victory."
