## Text

**4:1** Hear this word, you heifers of Bashan who are on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the poor and trample the needy, who say to their masters, "Bring to us, that we may drink!"

**4:2** The Lord swears by His holy things: "Behold, days are coming upon you, and they shall take you away with weapons, and fiery pestilent men shall cast those who are with you into cauldrons heated from beneath,

**4:3** and you shall be carried out naked, one opposite another, and you shall be cast away to Mount Romman," says the Lord.

**4:4** "You entered into Bethel and acted ungodly, and at Gilgal you multiplied ungodliness; and you brought your sacrifices in the morning, and your tithes every three days.

**4:5** And they read the law outside, and proclaimed confessions. Declare that the sons of Israel have loved these things," says the Lord God.

**4:6** "And I will give you aching of teeth in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:7** "I also withheld the rain from you three months before the vintage; and I will send rain upon one city, and upon another city I will not send rain; one portion shall be rained upon, and the portion upon which I will not send rain shall wither.

**4:8** And two and three cities shall gather together to one city to drink water, and they shall by no means be satisfied; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:9** "I struck you with scorching heat and with blight; you multiplied your gardens; the caterpillar devoured your vineyards, your fig groves, and your olive groves; yet not even thus did you return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:10** "I sent forth death upon you in the way of Egypt, and I slew your young men with the sword, together with the captivity of your horses; and I brought up your camps in fire in your wrath; yet not even thus did you return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:11** "I overthrew you as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a firebrand snatched out of the fire; yet not even thus did you return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:12** "Therefore thus I will do to you, O Israel; and because I will do thus to you, prepare yourself to call upon your God, O Israel."

**4:13** For He who establishes thunder and creates spirit, and declares His Anointed to men, who makes dawn and mist, treading upon the high places of the earth—the Lord God Almighty is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Greek text reads plural "His holy things" (ton hagion autou), "with weapons" (en hoplois), and "fiery pestilent men" (empyroi loimoi), and in 4:3 names "Mount Romman" (to oros to Rhomman), diverging notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:4** — The Septuagint uses past-tense verbs ("You entered... and acted ungodly") rather than ironic imperatives, and in 4:5 renders "they read the law outside" (anegnosan exo nomon).
- **4:10** — The Greek reads "in fire" and "in your wrath" (en te orge hymon), reflecting the double sense of the underlying Hebrew word meaning both "nostril" and "wrath."
- **4:13** — The Septuagint famously reads "His Anointed" (ton christon autou, literally "His Christ") and "creates spirit" (ktizon pneuma, which can also mean "wind").
