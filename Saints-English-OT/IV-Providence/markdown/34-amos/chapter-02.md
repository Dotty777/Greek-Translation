## Text

**2:1** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of Moab, and for four, I will not turn away from him, because they burned the bones of the king of Idumea into lime.

**2:2** And I will send forth fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the foundations of its cities; and Moab shall die in weakness, with a shout and with the sound of a trumpet.

**2:3** And I will utterly destroy a judge from its midst, and all who belong to it I will slay with him," says the Lord.

**2:4** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of the sons of Judah, and for four, I will not turn away from him, because they cast away the law of the Lord and did not keep His ordinances; and their vanities which they made led them astray, after which their fathers followed.

**2:5** And I will send forth fire upon Judah, and it shall devour the foundations of Jerusalem."

**2:6** Thus says the Lord: "For three impieties of Israel, and for four, I will not turn away from him, because they sold a righteous man for silver, and a needy man for the sake of sandals,

**2:7** that tread upon the dust of the earth; and they struck the heads of the poor with fists, and turned aside the way of the humble; and a son and his father went in to the same maidservant, that they might profane the name of their God.

**2:8** And binding their garments with cords, they made curtains beside the altar, and they drank wine from extortion in the house of their God.

**2:9** Yet I removed the Amorite from before their face, whose height was like the height of a cedar, and he was strong as an oak; and I dried up his fruit from above and his roots from beneath.

**2:10** And I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you about in the wilderness forty years, to inherit the land of the Amorites.

**2:11** And I took from your sons for prophets, and from your young men for sanctification. Are these things not so, O sons of Israel?" says the Lord.

**2:12** "And you gave the sanctified ones wine to drink, and you commanded the prophets, saying, 'You shall by no means prophesy.'

**2:13** Therefore, behold, I am rolling beneath you, in the manner that a wagon full of straw rolls.

**2:14** And flight shall perish from the runner, and the mighty man shall by no means hold fast his strength, and the warrior shall by no means save his life;

**2:15** and the archer shall by no means stand his ground, and the one swift with his feet shall by no means escape safely, nor shall the horseman save his life;

**2:16** and the mighty man shall not find his heart in mighty deeds; the naked man shall flee in that day," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Greek text reads simply "all who belong to it" without an explicit noun for "rulers" or "princes."
- **2:7** — In Greek, "that tread upon the dust of the earth" is neuter plural modifying "sandals" from verse 6, followed by "struck the heads of the poor with fists" and "the name of their God."
- **2:8** — The Greek reads "binding their garments with cords, they made curtains," interpreting the Hebrew word for pledged garments as "cords."
- **2:16** — The Greek reads "shall not find his heart in mighty deeds," and uses the verb dioxetai (normally "pursue," here used intransitively in the sense of "flee" or "rush away").
