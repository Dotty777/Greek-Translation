## Text

**25:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah,

**25:2** which he spoke to all the people of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying:

**25:3** "In the thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amos, king of Judah, even to this day—twenty-three years—I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking.

**25:4** And I sent to you My servants the prophets, sending them early in the morning, yet you did not listen nor incline your ears,

**25:5** saying, 'Turn back, each one from his wicked way and from your wicked practices, and you shall dwell upon the land which I gave to you and to your fathers from everlasting to everlasting.

**25:6** Do not walk after foreign gods to serve them and to worship them, so that you do not provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands, to bring harm upon yourselves.'

**25:7** Yet you did not listen to Me.

**25:8** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Because you have not believed My words,

**25:9** behold, I am sending, and I will take a family from the north, and I will bring them against this land and against its inhabitants and against all the nations round about it; and I will lay them waste and give them over to desolation, and to hissing, and to everlasting reproach.

**25:10** And I will destroy from among them the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the fragrance of ointment and the light of the lamp.

**25:11** And all the land shall become a desolation, and they shall serve among the nations seventy years.

**25:12** And when seventy years are fulfilled, I will execute vengeance upon that nation and will make them an everlasting desolation,

**25:13** and I will bring upon that land all My words which I have spoken against it, even all that is written in this book.'"

**25:14** The things which Jeremiah prophesied against the nations; those concerning Elam: In the beginning of the reign of King Zedekiah, this word came concerning Elam.

**25:15** Thus says the Lord: "The bow of Elam is broken, the chief of their might.

**25:16** And I will bring upon Elam four winds from the four ends of heaven, and I will scatter them to all these winds; and there shall be no nation to which the outcasts of Elam shall not come.

**25:17** And I will terrify them before their enemies who seek their life, and I will bring upon them according to the wrath of My fury, and I will send My sword after them until I have utterly consumed them.

**25:18** And I will set My throne in Elam, and I will send away from there king and nobles;

**25:19** and it shall come to pass at the end of the days that I will turn back the captivity of Elam," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — The Greek text names Josiah's father as Ἀμὼς (Amos), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Amon.
- **25:9** — Throughout verses 1–12, the Septuagint lacks explicit references to Nebuchadnezzar and Babylon, referring instead to "a family from the north" and "that nation."
- **25:10** — The Greek reads ὀσμὴν μύρου ("the fragrance of ointment"), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew behind the Masoretic "sound of the millstones."
- **25:14** — In the Septuagint arrangement of Jeremiah, the oracles against the nations begin here with Elam, corresponding to Jeremiah 49:34–39 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
