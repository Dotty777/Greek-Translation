## Text

**42:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the days of Jehoiakim king of Judah, saying:

**42:2** "Go to the house of Archabein, and you shall bring them into the house of the Lord, into one of the courts, and you shall give them wine to drink."

**42:3** And I brought out Jeconiah the son of Jeremin, the son of Chabasein, and his brothers, and his sons, and all the house of Archabein,

**42:4** and I brought them into the house of the Lord, into the chamber of the sons of Jonan the son of Hananiah, the son of Gedaliah, a man of God, which is near the house of the princes who are above the house of Maaseiah the son of Shallum, the keeper of the court.

**42:5** And I set before them a jar of wine and cups, and I said, "Drink wine."

**42:6** And they said, "We will by no means drink wine, for Jonadab the son of Rechab, our father, commanded us, saying, 'You shall by no means drink wine, you and your sons forever,

**42:7** and you shall not build a house, and you shall not sow seed, and you shall not have a vineyard, for you shall dwell in tents all your days, that you may live many days upon the land where you sojourn.'

**42:8** And we have obeyed the voice of Jonadab our father, so as not to drink wine all our days, we and our wives, and our sons and our daughters,

**42:9** and not to build houses to dwell there; we have had neither vineyard nor field nor seed;

**42:10** and we have dwelt in tents, and have obeyed and done according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.

**42:11** And it came to pass when Nebuchadnezzar came up against the land, that we spoke of entering, and we entered into Jerusalem from before the army of the Chaldeans and from before the army of the Assyrians, and we have been dwelling there."

**42:12** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**42:13** "Thus says the Lord: Go and say to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, 'Will you not receive instruction to listen to My words?

**42:14** The sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have upheld the word which he commanded his children, not to drink wine, and they have not drunk; yet I spoke to you early in the morning, and I spoke, and you did not listen.

**42:15** And I sent to you My servants the prophets, saying, "Turn back, every man from his evil way, and make your deeds better, and do not go after other gods to serve them, and you shall dwell upon the land which I gave to you and to your fathers." Yet you did not incline your ears, and you did not listen.'

**42:16** And the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have upheld the commandment of their father, but this people has not listened to Me.

**42:17** Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing upon Judah and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evils that I have spoken against them.

**42:18** Therefore thus says the Lord: Because the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have obeyed the commandment of their father, to do just as their father commanded them,

**42:19** there shall never be lacking a man of the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab standing before Me all the days of the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — "Archabein" (Ἁρχαβείν) is the Septuagint's distinct Greek form for the house of the Rechabites (incorporating the Hebrew definite article), whereas "Rechab" (Ῥηχάβ) appears in verse 6 and following.
- **42:3** — The Septuagint preserves distinct proper names in verses 3–4, including "Jeconiah the son of Jeremin, the son of Chabasein" (vs. MT Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah son of Habazziniah) and "Jonan the son of Hananiah, the son of Gedaliah" (vs. MT Hanan son of Igdaliah).
- **42:11** — The Septuagint reads "the Assyrians" (Ἀσσυρίων) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Arameans" (Syrians).
- **42:18** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint omits a narrative transition introducing Jeremiah's address to the Rechabites, continues directly in the third person, and adds "of the earth" at the end of verse 19.
