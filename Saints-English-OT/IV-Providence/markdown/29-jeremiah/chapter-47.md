## Text

**47:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard from Daman had sent him away, when he had taken him in shackles in the midst of the captivity of Judah who were being led to Babylon.

**47:2** And the captain of the guard took him and said to him, "The Lord your God has spoken these evils against this place,

**47:3** and the Lord has done it, because you have sinned against Him and have not heeded His voice.

**47:4** Behold, I have loosed you from the shackles that were on your hands. If it is good in your sight to come with me to Babylon, then I will set my eyes upon you;

**47:5** but if not, depart; return to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed in the land of Judah, and dwell with him in the midst of the people in the land of Judah; to wherever is good in your eyes to go, go." And the captain of the guard gave him gifts and sent him away.

**47:6** And he came to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and dwelt in the midst of the people who were left in the land.

**47:7** And all the commanders of the army that was in the field, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah in the land, and they entrusted to him men and their wives whom he had not carried away to Babylon.

**47:8** And there came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Jophe the Netophathite, and Jezaniah the son of the Maachathite, they and their men.

**47:9** And Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, saying, "Do not be afraid of the servants of the Chaldeans; dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be better for you.

**47:10** And behold, I dwell before you at Mizpah, to stand before the Chaldeans who may come to you. But as for you, gather wine and summer fruit and oil, and put them into your vessels, and dwell in the cities that you have occupied."

**47:11** And all the Jews who were in Moab, and among the sons of Ammon, and those in Idumea, and those in all the land, heard that the king of Babylon had given a remnant to Judah, and that he had appointed over them Gedaliah the son of Ahikam.

**47:12** And they came to Gedaliah in the land of Judah, to Mizpah, and gathered wine, and summer fruit in very great abundance, and oil.

**47:13** And Johanan the son of Kareah and all the commanders of the army who were in the fields came to Gedaliah at Mizpah,

**47:14** and they said to him, "Do you indeed know that Baalis king of the sons of Ammon has sent Ishmael to you to strike down your life?" But Gedaliah did not believe them.

**47:15** And Johanan said to Gedaliah secretly in Mizpah, "Let me go now and strike down Ishmael, and let no one know, lest he strike down your life, and all Judah who are gathered to you be scattered, and the remnant of Judah perish."

**47:16** And Gedaliah said to Johanan, "Do not do this thing, for you are speaking falsely concerning Ishmael."

## Translator Notes

- **47:1** — "captain of the guard" renders Greek archimageiros (literally "chief cook"), the standard Septuagint title for the royal bodyguard commander; "Daman" preserves the Greek place name, where the Hebrew text reads "Ramah."
- **47:8** — "Jophe" preserves the distinct Greek form of the name, corresponding to "Ephai" in the Hebrew text.
- **47:9** — "the servants of the Chaldeans" follows the Septuagint reading, whereas the Hebrew text reads "to serve the Chaldeans."
- **47:11** — "Idumea" preserves the Septuagint Greek name for the land of Edom.
