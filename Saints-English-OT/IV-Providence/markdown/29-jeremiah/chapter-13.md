## Text

**13:1** Thus says the Lord: "Go and buy for yourself a linen loincloth, and put it around your waist, and it shall not pass through water."

**13:2** So I bought the loincloth according to the word of the Lord, and put it around my waist.

**13:3** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**13:4** "Take the loincloth that is around your waist, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in the cleft of the rock."

**13:5** So I went and hid it at the Euphrates, just as the Lord had commanded me.

**13:6** And it came to pass after many days that the Lord said to me, "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the loincloth which I commanded you to hide there."

**13:7** So I went to the river Euphrates and dug, and I took the loincloth from the place where I had buried it; and behold, it was ruined, so that it could not be used for anything.

**13:8** Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**13:9** "Thus says the Lord: 'In this manner I will ruin the arrogance of Judah and the arrogance of Jerusalem,

**13:10** this great arrogance—those who refuse to obey My words, and who have gone after other gods to serve them and to worship them; and they shall be like this loincloth, which shall be useful for nothing.

**13:11** For just as the loincloth clings around the waist of a man, so I caused the house of Israel and all the house of Judah to cling to Myself, to become for Me a renowned people, and a boast, and a glory; yet they did not listen to Me.'

**13:12** And you shall say to this people, 'Every wineskin shall be filled with wine.' And it shall come to pass, if they say to you, 'Do we not surely know that every wineskin shall be filled with wine?'

**13:13** then you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am filling the inhabitants of this land, and their kings—the sons of David who sit upon their throne—and the priests, and the prophets, and Judah, and all who dwell in Jerusalem, with drunkenness;

**13:14** and I will scatter them, a man and his brother, and their fathers and their sons together. I will not yearn after them, says the Lord, and I will not spare, nor will I have compassion so as not to destroy them.'"

**13:15** Hear and give ear, and do not exalt yourselves, for the Lord has spoken.

**13:16** Give glory to the Lord your God before it grows dark, and before your feet stumble upon dark mountains; and you shall wait for light, yet there shall be the shadow of death, and they shall be turned into darkness.

**13:17** But if you will not listen, your soul shall weep in secret because of arrogance, and your eyes shall bring down tears, for the flock of the Lord has been crushed.

**13:18** Say to the king and to those who rule, "Humble yourselves and sit down, for the crown of your glory has been taken down from your head."

**13:19** The cities toward the south have been shut up, and there was no one to open them; Judah has been carried into exile; they have completed an utter exile.

**13:20** Lift up your eyes, O Jerusalem, and see those who come from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, the sheep of your glory?

**13:21** What will you say when they visit you? For you yourself taught them lessons against you to hold dominion. Shall not birth pangs seize you, like a woman in labor?

**13:22** And if you say in your heart, "Why have these things come upon me?" Because of the multitude of your iniquity, your back parts have been uncovered, that your heels might be exposed to public shame.

**13:23** Can an Ethiopian change his skin, or a leopard its spots? Then you also will be able to do good, who have learned evil ways.

**13:24** And I scattered them like dry twigs driven by the wind into a wilderness.

**13:25** This is your lot and the portion of your disobedience to Me, says the Lord; since you have forgotten Me and put your hope in falsehoods,

**13:26** I also will uncover your back parts over your face, and your dishonor shall be seen,

**13:27** even your adultery, and your neighing, and the estrangement of your harlotry. Upon the hills and in the fields I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, O Jerusalem, because you have not been cleansed after Me—how much longer yet?

## Translator Notes

- **13:14** — The Septuagint reads "I will scatter them" and "I will not yearn after them," differing from the Masoretic Text's "I will dash them one against another" and "I will not pity."
- **13:17** — In the Septuagint the pronouns are second-person plural ("your soul," "your eyes") rather than first-person singular ("my soul," "my eye") as in the Masoretic Text, and the flock is "crushed" rather than "taken captive."
- **13:18** — The Septuagint renders the title as plural "those who rule" (tois dynasteuousin), whereas the Masoretic Text refers specifically to the queen mother.
