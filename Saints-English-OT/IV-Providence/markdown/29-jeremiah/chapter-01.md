## Text

**1:1** The word of God which came upon Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests, who dwelt in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,

**1:2** to whom the word of God came in the days of Josiah the son of Amos, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

**1:3** And it came in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the captivity of Jerusalem in the fifth month.

**1:4** And the word of the Lord came to him:

**1:5** "Before I fashioned you in the womb I knew you, and before you came forth I sanctified you; I have appointed you a prophet to the nations."

**1:6** And I said, "O You who are, Sovereign Lord, behold, I do not know how to speak, for I am too young."

**1:7** And the Lord said to me, "Do not say, 'I am too young,' for to all to whom I shall send you, you shall go, and according to all things that I shall command you, you shall speak.

**1:8** Do not be afraid before their face, for I am with you to deliver you," says the Lord.

**1:9** Then the Lord stretched forth His hand to me and touched my mouth; and the Lord said to me, "Behold, I have put My words into your mouth.

**1:10** Behold, I have appointed you this day over nations and kingdoms, to uproot and to tear down and to destroy, and to rebuild and to plant."

**1:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "A rod of a nut tree."

**1:12** And the Lord said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watchful over My words to perform them."

**1:13** And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "A cauldron kindled from beneath, and its face is from the face of the north."

**1:14** Then the Lord said to me, "From the face of the north evils shall be kindled upon all who dwell in the land.

**1:15** For behold, I am summoning all the kingdoms from the north of the earth," says the Lord, "and they shall come, and each one shall set his throne at the entrances of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all the walls around her, and against all the cities of Judah.

**1:16** And I will speak to them with judgment concerning all their wickedness, how they have forsaken Me and sacrificed to strange gods and worshiped the works of their own hands.

**1:17** And you, gird your loins and arise, and speak all things that I shall command you; do not be afraid before their face, nor be dismayed before them, for I am with you to deliver you," says the Lord.

**1:18** "Behold, I have set you this day as a fortified city and as a bronze wall, strong against all the kings of Judah, and against its rulers, and against the people of the land;

**1:19** and they shall fight against you, yet they shall by no means prevail against you, for I am with you to deliver you," said the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek reads "The word of God which came upon Jeremiah" and singular "who dwelt" (referring to Jeremiah), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "The words of Jeremiah" and plural "who were in Anathoth."
- **1:2** — The Greek reads "Amos" here rather than "Amon" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:6** — The Greek translates the Hebrew exclamation as "O You who are, Sovereign Lord" (Ho on despota Kyrie), echoing the divine self-revelation in Exodus 3:14.
- **1:11** — The Greek specifies a "nut tree" (karyine) rather than specifically an almond tree, and therefore does not mirror the Hebrew wordplay with "watching" in verse 12.
