## Text

**22:1** Thus says the Lord: "Go and descend to the house of the king of Judah, and you shall speak this word there,

**22:2** and you shall say, 'Hear the word of the Lord, O king of Judah who sits on the throne of David, you and your house and your people and those who enter through these gates:

**22:3** Thus says the Lord: Execute judgment and righteousness, and deliver the plundered from the hand of him who wrongs him; and do not oppress the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow, and do not act ungodly, and do not shed innocent blood in this place.

**22:4** For if you indeed do this word, then kings sitting on the throne of David and riding on chariots and horses shall enter through the gates of this house, they and their servants and their people.

**22:5** But if you do not do these words, I have sworn by Myself, says the Lord, that this house shall become a desolation.'"

**22:6** For thus says the Lord against the house of the king of Judah: "You are Gilead to Me, the head of Lebanon; surely I will make you a wilderness, cities that shall not be inhabited.

**22:7** And I will bring upon you a destroying man and his axe, and they shall cut down your choice cedars and cast them into the fire.

**22:8** And nations shall pass through this city, and each one shall say to his neighbor, 'Why has the Lord done thus to this great city?'

**22:9** And they shall say, 'Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshiped foreign gods and served them.'"

**22:10** Do not weep for the dead, nor lament for him; weep bitterly for him who goes away, for he shall return no more, nor shall he see the land of his native country.

**22:11** For thus says the Lord concerning Shallum the son of Josiah, who reigned in the place of Josiah his father, who went out from this place: "He shall not return there anymore,

**22:12** but in the place where I have exiled him, there he shall die, and he shall see this land no more."

**22:13** He who builds his house not with righteousness and his upper chambers not with justice makes his neighbor work for nothing, and will by no means pay him his wages.

**22:14** You have built for yourself a well-proportioned house, airy upper chambers fitted with windows, paneled with cedar and painted with vermilion.

**22:15** Shall you reign because you are provoked by Ahaz your father? They shall not eat and they shall not drink; it is better for you to execute judgment and righteousness.

**22:16** They did not know; they did not judge the cause of the humble nor the cause of the needy. Is this not that you do not know Me, says the Lord?

**22:17** Behold, neither your eyes nor your heart are good, but they are set on your covetousness, and on innocent blood to shed it, and on injustice and on murder to commit them.

**22:18** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah: "And concerning this man, they shall by no means mourn for him, 'Alas, brother!' nor shall they weep for him, 'Alas, lord!'

**22:19** He shall be buried with the burial of a donkey, swept off and cast out beyond the gate of Jerusalem."

**22:20** Go up to Lebanon and cry out, and raise your voice in Bashan, and shout to the region beyond the sea; for all your lovers have been crushed.

**22:21** I spoke to you in your transgression, and you said, "I will not listen." This has been your way from your youth; you have not listened to My voice.

**22:22** The wind shall shepherd all your shepherds, and your lovers shall go forth into captivity; for then you shall be ashamed and dishonored because of all who love you.

**22:23** O you who dwell in Lebanon, who make your nest in the cedars, you shall groan deeply when pangs come upon you like those of a woman in labor.

**22:24** "As I live," says the Lord, "even though Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were indeed a signet on My right hand, yet from there I would pluck you off;

**22:25** and I will deliver you into the hands of those who seek your life, whose presence you dread, into the hands of the Chaldeans.

**22:26** And I will cast you out, and your mother who bore you, into a land where you were not born, and there you shall die.

**22:27** But to the land for which they long in their souls, they shall by no means return."

**22:28** Jeconiah has been dishonored like a vessel for which there is no use, for he has been cast away and thrown out into a land that he did not know.

**22:29** O earth, earth, hear the word of the Lord:

**22:30** "Write this man down as an outcast man, for none of his seed shall prosper, sitting on the throne of David, ruling anymore in Judah."

## Translator Notes

- **22:12** — The Greek uses the first person ("where I have exiled him") rather than the third-person plural ("where they have taken him captive") of the Hebrew.
- **22:15** — The Greek reads "Ahaz your father" where the Hebrew refers to competing in "cedar" and then asks about "your father," and it continues with third-person plural future verbs ("They shall not eat and they shall not drink").
- **22:16** — Whereas the Hebrew praises Josiah's justice and asks if that is not knowing the Lord, the Greek has negative plural verbs ("They did not know; they did not judge") and a negative conclusion ("that you do not know Me").
- **22:20** — Greek "to the region beyond the sea" interprets Hebrew Abarim ("regions beyond") as a geographical phrase rather than a proper place name.
- **22:30** — Greek ekkerykton ("outcast" or "proscribed") renders Hebrew ariri ("childless"), and the Greek text does not include the Hebrew clause "a man who shall not prosper in his days."
