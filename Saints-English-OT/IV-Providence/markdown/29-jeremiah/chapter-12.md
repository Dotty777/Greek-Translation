## Text

**12:1** You are righteous, O Lord, when I make my defense before You; yet I will speak of judgments to You. Why is it that the way of the ungodly prospers? Have all those who commit treacherous deeds flourished?

**12:2** You planted them, and they took root; they bore children and brought forth fruit. You are near to their mouth, yet far from their inward parts.

**12:3** But You, O Lord, know me; You have tested my heart before You. Consecrate them for the day of their slaughter.

**12:4** How long shall the land mourn, and all the grass of the field wither, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it? The beasts and the birds have perished, because they said, "God will not see our ways."

**12:5** Your own feet run and weary you; how will you prepare yourself against horses? And in a land of peace you have no confidence; how will you do in the arrogance of the Jordan?

**12:6** For even your brethren and the house of your father—even they have dealt treacherously with you, and they themselves have cried out; from behind you they have gathered together. Do not trust in them, even though they speak fair words to you.

**12:7** I have forsaken My house; I have abandoned My inheritance; I have given My beloved soul into the hands of her enemies.

**12:8** My inheritance has become to Me like a lion in a forest; she has lifted up her voice against Me; therefore I have hated her.

**12:9** Is My inheritance to Me a hyena's cave, or a cave all around her? Go, gather all the wild beasts of the field, and let them come to devour her.

**12:10** Many shepherds have ruined My vineyard; they have defiled My portion; they have turned My pleasant portion into an untrodden wilderness.

**12:11** It has been consigned to a desolation of ruin; because of Me the whole land has been utterly laid waste, because there is no man who lays it to heart.

**12:12** Upon every pass in the wilderness destroyers have come, for the sword of the Lord shall devour from the end of the earth; there is no peace for any flesh.

**12:13** Sow wheat, and reap thorns! Their portions shall not profit them. Be ashamed of your boasting, because of the reproach before the Lord.

**12:14** For thus says the Lord concerning all the wicked neighbors who touch My inheritance which I apportioned to My people Israel: "Behold, I am tearing them away from their land, and I will cast out Judah from the midst of them.

**12:15** And it shall come to pass, after I have cast them out, that I will turn again and have mercy on them, and I will settle them, each one in his inheritance and each one in his land.

**12:16** And it shall come to pass, if they diligently learn the way of My people, to swear by My name, 'As the Lord lives,' just as they taught My people to swear by Baal, then they shall be built up in the midst of My people;

**12:17** but if they will not return, then I will remove that nation with utter removal and destruction."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Septuagint reads "they bore children" (eteknopoiesanto), reflecting a Hebrew Vorlage "they bore offspring" rather than the Masoretic Text's "they grow."
- **12:5** — The Septuagint reads "Your own feet run and weary you," understanding the Hebrew term as "feet" rather than "footmen" as in the Masoretic Text.
- **12:9** — Where the Masoretic Text speaks of a "speckled bird of prey," the Septuagint renders "a hyena's cave" (spelaion hyaines).
