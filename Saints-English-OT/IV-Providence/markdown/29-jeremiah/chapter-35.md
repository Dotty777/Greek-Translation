## Text

**35:1** And it came to pass in the fourth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah, in the fifth month, that Hananiah son of Azzur, the false prophet from Gibeon, spoke to me in the house of the Lord in the sight of the priests and all the people, saying,

**35:2** "Thus says the Lord: I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.

**35:3** Yet two years of days, and I will bring back to this place the vessels of the house of the Lord,

**35:4** and Jeconiah and the exiles of Judah; for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon."

**35:5** And Jeremiah spoke to Hananiah in the sight of all the people and in the sight of the priests who stood in the house of the Lord;

**35:6** and Jeremiah said, "Truly, may the Lord do so! May He establish your word which you prophesy, to bring back the vessels of the house of the Lord and all the exiles from Babylon to this place.

**35:7** Nevertheless, hear the word of the Lord that I speak in your ears and in the ears of all the people:

**35:8** The prophets who were before me and before you from of old prophesied against many a land and against great kingdoms concerning war;

**35:9** as for the prophet who prophesies of peace, when the word comes to pass, they shall know the prophet whom the Lord has sent to them in faithfulness."

**35:10** And Hananiah took the yoke-bars from the neck of Jeremiah in the sight of all the people, and broke them.

**35:11** And Hananiah spoke in the sight of all the people, saying, "Thus says the Lord: So will I break the yoke of the king of Babylon from the necks of all the nations." And Jeremiah went on his way.

**35:12** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah after Hananiah had broken the yoke-bars from his neck, saying,

**35:13** "Go and speak to Hananiah, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Wooden yoke-bars you have broken, and I will make in their place iron yoke-bars.

**35:14** For thus says the Lord: An iron yoke I have placed upon the neck of all the nations, to serve the king of Babylon.'"

**35:15** And Jeremiah said to Hananiah, "The Lord has not sent you, and you have caused this people to trust in what is unjust.

**35:16** Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am sending you away from the face of the earth; this year you shall die."

**35:17** And he died in the seventh month.

## Translator Notes

- **35:3** — Greek "Yet two years of days" literally renders the Hebrew idiom for two full years.
- **35:13** — Greek reads the first-person future poieso ("I will make"), attributing the iron yoke-bars directly to the Lord, whereas the Hebrew reads the second-person "you have made."
- **35:15** — Greek ep' adiko ("in what is unjust") renders the Hebrew term for falsehood with moral terminology, consistent with adika and ep' adiko in 34:14–16.
