## Text

**26:2** Concerning Egypt: against the army of Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt, who was by the river Euphrates at Charmeis, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck down in the fourth year of Jehoiakim king of Judah.

**26:3** Take up weapons and shields, and draw near to battle.

**26:4** And saddle the horses; mount up, O horsemen, and take your stand in your helmets; thrust forward the spears and put on your breastplates.

**26:5** Why is it that they are terrified and retreat backward? For their mighty men shall be cut down; in flight they fled and did not turn back, surrounded on every side, says the Lord.

**26:6** Do not let the swift flee, and do not let the mighty man escape toward the north; those beside the Euphrates have grown weak, and they have fallen.

**26:7** Who is this who shall rise up like a river, and as rivers surge with water?

**26:8** The waters of Egypt shall rise up like a river, and it said, "I will rise up and cover the earth, and I will destroy those who dwell in it."

**26:9** Mount the horses, prepare the chariots; come forth, O warriors of the Ethiopians, and Libyans fully armed with weapons, and Lydians, go up, bend the bow.

**26:10** And that day belongs to the Lord our God, a day of vengeance, to take vengeance on His enemies; and the sword of the Lord shall devour, and it shall be filled and become drunk from their blood; for there is a sacrifice for the Lord from the land of the north by the river Euphrates.

**26:11** Go up, O Gilead, and take balm for the virgin daughter of Egypt; in vain you have multiplied your remedies; there is no benefit for you.

**26:12** The nations have heard your voice, and the earth has been filled with your outcry; for warrior has weakened against warrior, and both have fallen together.

**26:13** The things which the Lord spoke by the hand of Jeremiah concerning the coming of the king of Babylon to strike the land of Egypt.

**26:14** Declare it in Migdol, and proclaim it in Memphis; say, "Take your stand and prepare, for a sword has devoured your bindweed."

**26:15** Why has Apis fled from you? Your chosen calf did not remain, because the Lord has weakened him.

**26:16** And your multitude grew weak and fell, and each one spoke to his neighbor, "Let us arise and return to our people, to our homeland, from the face of the Greek sword."

**26:17** Call the name of Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt, "Saon Esbei Emoed."

**26:18** "As I live," says the Lord God, "like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel by the sea, he shall come."

**26:19** Prepare for yourself baggage for exile, O daughter who dwells in Egypt; for Memphis shall become a desolation, and she shall be called "Woe," because there are none who dwell in her.

**26:20** Egypt is a beautified heifer; a detachment from the north has come upon her,

**26:21** and her hired soldiers in her midst are like fattened calves fed within her; for they also have turned back and fled with one accord; they did not stand, because the day of destruction has come upon them, and the time of their vengeance.

**26:22** A sound like a hissing serpent, for they march in the sand; with axes they shall come against her.

**26:23** Like men cutting timber, they shall cut down her forest, says the Lord, for it cannot be fathomed, because it multiplies beyond the locust, and they are without number.

**26:24** The daughter of Egypt has been put to shame; she has been delivered into the hands of a people from the north.

**26:25** Behold, I am taking vengeance on Ammon her son, on Pharaoh, and on those who trust in him.

**26:27** But do not fear, My servant Jacob, and do not be dismayed, O Israel; for behold, I am saving you from afar, and your seed from their captivity; and Jacob shall return and be at rest and sleep, and there shall be no one who troubles him.

**26:28** Do not fear, My servant Jacob, says the Lord, for I am with you. The fearless and delicate one has been delivered up; for I will make a complete end among all those to whom I have driven you out there, yet I will by no means cause you to come to an end; and I will chasten you in judgment, and will by no means hold you guiltless.

## Translator Notes

- **26:2** — In the Septuagint, chapter 26 corresponds to Masoretic chapter 46 and begins at verse 2; "Charmeis" is the Greek form of Carchemish.
- **26:14** — The Septuagint reads "your bindweed" (Greek smilax, a climbing plant or yew) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "your surroundings."
- **26:15** — The Septuagint uniquely names "Apis," the sacred bull-deity of Memphis, as Egypt's "chosen calf," where the Hebrew text refers more generally to Egypt's mighty ones.
- **26:16** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew phrase for "oppressing sword" as "the Greek sword" (as also in 27:16), reading the Hebrew consonants as referring to Greece (Ionia).
- **26:17** — "Saon Esbei Emoed" is the Septuagint's transliteration of the symbolic Hebrew name given to Pharaoh Necho, rather than a translation of its meaning.
