## Text

**37:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying:

**37:2** "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, saying: Write all the words which I have declared to you on a scroll.

**37:3** For behold, days are coming, declares the Lord, and I will restore the exile of My people Israel and Judah, says the Lord, and I will bring them back to the land which I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it."

**37:4** And these are the words which the Lord spoke concerning Israel and Judah.

**37:5** Thus says the Lord: "You shall hear a voice of fear; fear, and there is no peace.

**37:6** Ask and see whether a male has given birth, and concerning fear in which they shall hold fast their loins and deliverance; for I have seen every man with his hands upon his loins; faces have turned, it has become jaundice.

**37:7** For great is that day, and there is none like it; and it is a time of distress for Jacob,

**37:8** yet out of it he shall be saved. In that day, says the Lord, I will shatter the yoke from their neck and tear apart their bonds, and they themselves shall not serve strangers,

**37:9** but they shall serve the Lord their God, and I will raise up for them David their king."

**37:12** Thus says the Lord: "I have raised up a fracture; painful is your wound,

**37:13** there is no one to judge your cause; you have been treated to your pain, there is no benefit for you.

**37:14** All your friends have forgotten you; they shall by no means inquire; for with the blow of an enemy I have struck you, with stern discipline; because of all your unrighteousness, your sins have multiplied.

**37:16** Therefore all who devour you shall be devoured, and all your enemies shall eat all their own flesh; because of the multitude of your iniquities, your sins have multiplied—they have done these things to you; and those who plunder you shall become plunder, and all who prey upon you I will give for a prey.

**37:17** For I will bring up your healing; from a grievous wound I will heal you, declares the Lord; because you were called Scattered—'She is your prey, for there is no one who seeks her.'"

**37:18** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, I will restore the exile of Jacob, and I will have mercy on his captivity; and the city shall be built upon its height, and the people shall dwell according to its judgment.

**37:19** And out of them shall go forth singers, the voice of those who make merry; and I will multiply them, and they shall by no means be diminished.

**37:20** And their sons shall enter in as before, and their testimonies shall be set upright before My face; and I will punish those who afflict them,

**37:21** and those mightier than he shall be over them, and his ruler shall come forth from himself; and I will gather them, and they shall return to Me; for who is this who has set his heart to return to Me, declares the Lord?"

**37:23** For the furious wrath of the Lord has gone forth; a whirling wrath has gone forth; upon the ungodly it shall come.

**37:24** The fierce wrath of the Lord shall by no means turn back until He has executed, and until He has established the undertaking of His heart; in the latter days you shall know these things.

## Translator Notes

- **37:6** — The Greek contains the distinct clause "and concerning fear in which they shall hold fast their loins and deliverance," and uses ἴκτερος ("jaundice") for the sickly pallor of terrified faces.
- **37:12** — The Greek opens with a first-person declaration by the Lord, "I have raised up a fracture" (Ἀνέστησα σύντριμμα), where the Hebrew (30:12) reads "Your hurt is incurable"; verses 10-11, 15, and 22 of the Hebrew are absent from the Septuagint.
- **37:16** — The Greek reads "all your enemies shall eat all their own flesh" where the Hebrew (30:16) has "all your adversaries shall go into captivity," and includes the clause "because of the multitude of your iniquities..." within this verse.
- **37:17** — The Greek renders "She is your prey" (θήρευμα ὑμῶν ἐστίν) where the Hebrew (30:17) reads "It is Zion"; similarly, verse 18 reads "captivity" and "the people" for Hebrew "tents" and "palace," and verse 20 reads "testimonies" for "congregation."
