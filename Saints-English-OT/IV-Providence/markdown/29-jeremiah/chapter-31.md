## Text

**31:1** Concerning Moab. Thus says the Lord: "Woe to Nebo, for it has perished! Kiriathaim is captured; Hamath and Agath are put to shame.

**31:2** There is no longer healing for Moab, nor boasting in Heshbon; he has devised evil against her; we have cut her off from being a nation, and she shall utterly cease. Behind you a sword shall march,

**31:3** for there is the sound of those crying out from Horonaim, destruction and a great shattering.

**31:4** Moab is shattered! Proclaim it to Zogora,

**31:5** for Aloth is filled with weeping; weeping, he shall go up by the way of Horonaim; you have heard the outcry of shattering.

**31:6** Flee and save your lives, and become like a wild donkey in the wilderness.

**31:7** Because you trusted in your strongholds, you also shall be captured; and Chemosh shall go forth into captivity, his priests and his rulers together."

**31:8** And destruction shall come upon every city—it shall by no means be saved—and the valley shall perish, and the plain shall be utterly destroyed, just as the Lord has spoken.

**31:9** Give signs to Moab, for she shall surely be kindled, and all her cities shall become an untrodden waste; where shall an inhabitant come from for her?

**31:10** Cursed is the one who does the works of the Lord negligently, withholding his sword from blood.

**31:11** Moab has been at rest from his childhood, and he has been confident in his glory; he has not poured from vessel to vessel, nor has he gone into exile; therefore his taste has remained in him, and his scent has not faded.

**31:12** "Therefore, behold, his days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will send tilters to him, and they shall tilt him, and crush his vessels to pieces, and shatter his horns;

**31:13** and Moab shall be put to shame because of Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was put to shame because of Bethel, their hope, having trusted in them.

**31:14** How will you say, 'We are mighty, and a man strong for matters of war'?

**31:15** Moab—his city—has perished, and his chosen young men have gone down to the slaughter.

**31:16** The day of Moab is near to come, and his calamity is exceedingly swift.

**31:17** Mourn for him, all you around him; all of you, proclaim his name; say, 'How the glorious staff is broken, the rod of magnificence!'

**31:18** Come down from glory and sit in dampness, O you who dwell there! Dibon is obliterated, for Moab has perished; he who ravages your stronghold has come up against you.

**31:19** Stand by the way and watch, O you who dwell in Aroer; ask the one who flees and the one who escapes, and say, 'What has happened?'

**31:20** Moab is put to shame, for it is shattered! Wail and cry out; proclaim in Arnon that Moab has perished,

**31:21** and judgment comes to the land of Meisor: upon Chailon, and Rephas, and Mophas,

**31:22** and upon Dibon, and upon Nebo, and upon the house of Diblathaim,

**31:23** and upon Kiriathaim, and upon the house of Gamul, and upon the house of Maon,

**31:24** and upon Kerioth, and upon Bozrah, and upon all the cities of Moab, those far off and those near.

**31:25** The horn of Moab is broken, and his arm is shattered.

**31:26** Make him drunk, for he has magnified himself against the Lord; and Moab shall strike with his hand, and he himself also shall become an object of laughter.

**31:27** And was he not a derision to you, O Israel, and was he found among your thefts, that you waged war against him?

**31:28** The inhabitants of Moab have forsaken the cities and dwelt in the rocks; they have become like doves nesting in the rocks at the mouth of a pit.

**31:29** And I have heard of the arrogance of Moab—he has been exceedingly arrogant—his arrogance and his haughtiness, and his heart has been lifted up;

**31:30** yet I have known his deeds. Is it not enough for him? Has he not done thus?

**31:31** Therefore wail over Moab on every side; cry out over the men of Keir Hadas of drought.

**31:32** As with the weeping of Jazer I will weep for you, O vine of Oserema! Your branches crossed the sea; the cities of Jazer touched upon your summer fruit; upon your grape-gatherers destruction has fallen.

**31:33** Joy and gladness have been swept away from the land of Moab, and wine was in your winepresses; in the morning they did not tread, nor at dusk did these do so.

**31:34** From the outcry of Heshbon as far as Aitam, their cities have given forth their voice, from Zogor as far as Horonaim, and the report of Salaseia; for even the water of Nebrein shall become a burning.

**31:35** And I will destroy Moab," says the Lord, "as he goes up to the altar and burns incense to his gods.

**31:36** Therefore the heart of Moab shall moan like flutes; My heart shall moan like a flute for the shorn men; therefore the things he acquired have perished from man.

**31:37** They shall be shaved on every head in every place, and every beard shall be shaved, and all hands shall beat in mourning, and upon every loin shall be sackcloth.

**31:38** And upon all the housetops of Moab and in her streets—for I have shattered it," says the Lord, "like a vessel for which there is no use.

**31:39** How it has changed! How Moab has turned the back! He is put to shame, and Moab has become an object of laughter and resentment to all those around her."

**31:40** For thus says the Lord:

**31:41** "Ekron is captured, and the strongholds are seized,

**31:42** and Moab shall perish from being a multitude, because he has magnified himself against the Lord.

**31:43** Snare and terror and pit are upon you, O inhabitant of Moab.

**31:44** He who flees from the face of terror shall fall into the pit, and he who climbs up out of the pit shall be caught in the snare; for I will bring these things upon Moab in the year of their visitation."

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — Greek reads two proper names, Hamath and Agath, where the Masoretic Text has "the stronghold is put to shame and dismayed."
- **31:4** — Greek reads the place name Zogora (Zoar) rather than "her little ones," and in verse 5 transliterates Aloth for the ascent of Luhith; similar Septuagint-distinct place names appear in verses 21, 31, 32, and 34.
- **31:26** — Greek reads "strike with his hand" rather than the Masoretic "wallow in his vomit."
- **31:33** — Greek renders the underlying Hebrew vintage shout (hedad) with the feminine demonstrative "these" (haide); compare "these" (hoide) in 32:30.
- **31:41** — Greek reads the city Ekron (Akkaron) here rather than Kerioth, and verse 40 omits the Masoretic eagle imagery.
