## Text

**7:2** Hear the word of the Lord, all Judea.

**7:3** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: "Set right your ways and your practices, and I will make you dwell in this place.

**7:4** Do not trust in yourselves, in deceitful words, for they will not profit you at all, saying, 'The temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord it is.'

**7:5** For if you truly set right your ways and your practices, and if you truly execute justice between a man and his neighbor,

**7:6** and do not oppress the sojourner, the orphan, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, nor walk after foreign gods to your own harm,

**7:7** then I will make you dwell in this place, in the land which I gave to your fathers from everlasting even to everlasting.

**7:8** Yet if you trust in deceitful words, from which you shall not be profited,

**7:9** and murder, and commit adultery, and steal, and swear falsely, and burned incense to Baal, and walked after foreign gods whom you do not know, so that it may go ill with you,

**7:10** and came and stood before Me in the house upon which My name is called, and said, 'We have abstained from doing all these abominations'—

**7:11** is My house, upon which My name is called there in your sight, a den of robbers? And behold, I Myself have seen it," says the Lord.

**7:12** "For you went to My place which is in Shiloh, where I caused My name to dwell there before, and saw what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel.

**7:13** And now, because you have done all these works, and I spoke to you and you did not listen to Me, and I called you and you did not answer,

**7:14** therefore I will do to the house upon which My name is called, in which you trust, and to the place which I gave to you and to your fathers, just as I did to Shiloh.

**7:15** And I will cast you away from My sight, just as I cast away your brethren, all the seed of Ephraim."

**7:16** "And as for you, do not pray for this people, and do not plead for mercy to be shown to them, nor make supplication, nor approach Me on their behalf, for I will not listen.

**7:17** Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**7:18** Their sons gather wood, and their fathers kindle a fire, and their women knead dough to make cakes for the host of heaven, and they have poured out drink offerings to foreign gods that they may provoke Me to anger.

**7:19** Is it Me whom they provoke to anger?" says the Lord. "Is it not themselves, that their own faces may be put to shame?"

**7:20** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, My anger and My wrath are poured out upon this place, upon man and upon beast, and upon every tree of their field, and upon the produce of the earth; and it shall burn and shall not be quenched."

**7:21** Thus says the Lord: "Gather your burnt offerings together with your sacrifices, and eat flesh.

**7:22** For I did not speak to your fathers, nor did I command them in the day that I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, concerning burnt offerings and sacrifice.

**7:23** Rather, this word I commanded them, saying, 'Listen to My voice, and I will be to you a God, and you shall be to Me a people; and walk in all My ways which I shall command you, that it may be well with you.'

**7:24** Yet they did not listen to Me, nor did their ear attend, but they walked in the devices of their evil heart, and turned backward and not forward,

**7:25** from the day that their fathers came out of the land of Egypt even to this day. And I sent out to you all My servants the prophets by day and at dawn; indeed I sent them,

**7:26** yet they did not listen to Me, nor did their ear attend, and they stiffened their neck more than their fathers.

**7:27** And you shall speak this word to them:

**7:28** 'This is the nation that has not listened to the voice of the Lord, nor received discipline; faithfulness has failed from their mouth.'"

**7:29** Shear your head and cast it away, and take up a lamentation upon your lips, for the Lord has rejected and cast off the generation that does these things.

**7:30** "For the sons of Judah have done evil in My sight," says the Lord; "they have placed their abominations in the house upon which My name is called, to defile it.

**7:31** And they have built the altar of Topheth, which is in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I did not command them, nor did I conceive in My heart.

**7:32** Therefore, behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "when they shall no longer say, 'Altar of Topheth' and 'Valley of the son of Hinnom,' but 'The Valley of the Slain'; and they shall bury in Topheth because there is no place left,

**7:33** and the dead bodies of this people shall be food for the birds of heaven and for the wild beasts of the earth, and there shall be no one to drive them away.

**7:34** And I will bring to an end from the city of Judah and from the thoroughfares of Jerusalem the voice of those who make merry and the voice of those who rejoice, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the whole land shall become a desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Greek text omits verse 1 and the narrative introduction of verse 2 found in the Hebrew, opening the chapter directly with the prophetic summons to Judea.
- **7:4** — The Greek repeats "the temple of the Lord" twice (rather than three times as in the Hebrew) and adds "for they will not profit you at all."
- **7:10** — The Greek reads "We have abstained from doing all these abominations," expressing a false claim of innocence rather than the Hebrew "We are delivered—to do all these abominations."
- **7:12** — The Greek text reads the aorist indicative "you went" (and "saw") rather than the Hebrew imperative "go now... and see."
- **7:18** — The Greek reads "the host of heaven" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the queen of heaven."
