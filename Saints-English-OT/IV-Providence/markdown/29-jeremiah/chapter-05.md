## Text

**5:1** "Run about in the streets of Jerusalem, and look and take note and search in her broad squares, if you can find whether there is one doing justice and seeking faithfulness, and I will be merciful to them," says the Lord.

**5:2** "The Lord lives," they say; therefore do they not swear in falsehoods?

**5:3** O Lord, Your eyes are toward faithfulness; You scourged them, yet they felt no pain; You consumed them, yet they were not willing to receive discipline; they made their faces harder than rock, and they were not willing to return.

**5:4** And I said, "Perhaps they are poor, because they were unable, for they did not know the way of the Lord and the judgment of God;

**5:5** I will go to the great men and speak to them, for they have known the way of the Lord and the judgment of God." And behold, with one accord they shattered the yoke, they tore apart the bonds.

**5:6** Therefore a lion from the forest has struck them, and a wolf even to the houses has destroyed them, and a leopard has kept watch over their cities; all who go out from them shall be hunted down, because they have multiplied their ungodly deeds and have grown strong in their backslidings.

**5:7** "For which of these things shall I be merciful to you? Your sons have forsaken Me and swore by those who are not gods; and I fed them to the full, yet they committed adultery and lodged in the houses of harlots.

**5:8** They became lustful stallions; each one neighed after his neighbor's wife.

**5:9** Shall I not punish for these things?" says the Lord. "Or shall My soul not take vengeance on such a nation as this?"

**5:10** "Go up upon her ramparts and tear them down, yet do not make a complete end; leave behind her foundations, for they belong to the Lord.

**5:11** For the house of Israel has indeed dealt treacherously against Me," says the Lord, "and the house of Judah

**5:12** has lied against their own Lord, and they said, 'These things are not so; calamity shall not come upon us, and we shall not see sword or famine;

**5:13** our prophets have become wind, and the word of the Lord was not in them—so shall it happen to them.'"

**5:14** Therefore thus says the Lord Almighty: "Because you have spoken this word, behold, I have put My words into your mouth as fire, and this people as wood, and it shall devour them."

**5:15** "Behold, I am bringing upon you a nation from afar, O house of Israel," says the Lord, "a nation the sound of whose language you shall not understand.

**5:16** All of them are mighty, and they shall devour your harvest

**5:17** and your bread, and they shall devour your sons and your daughters, and they shall devour your sheep and your calves, and they shall devour your vineyards and your fig orchards and your olive groves; and they shall thresh your fortified cities, in which you have trusted, with the sword."

**5:18** "And it shall come to pass in those days," says the Lord your God, "that I will by no means bring you to a complete end."

**5:19** And it shall come to pass, when you say, "For what reason has the Lord our God done all these things to us?" then you shall say to them, "Because you served foreign gods in your land, so you shall serve strangers in a land not your own."

**5:20** Declare these things to the house of Jacob, and let it be heard in Judah:

**5:21** Hear now these things, O foolish and senseless people: they have eyes and do not see; they have ears and do not hear.

**5:22** "Will you not fear Me?" says the Lord. "Or will you not stand in awe before My presence, who have appointed the sand as a boundary for the sea, an everlasting decree, and it shall not pass over it? Though it is stirred up, yet it shall not prevail; and its waves shall roar, yet it shall not pass over it.

**5:23** But this people has come to have a disobedient and rebellious heart; they have turned aside and departed.

**5:24** And they did not say in their heart, 'Let us now fear the Lord our God, who gives us the early and the late rain at the season of the fulfillment of the appointed decree of harvest, and He has preserved it for us.'

**5:25** Your iniquities have turned these things away, and your sins have withheld good things from you.

**5:26** For ungodly men have been found among My people, and they have set snares to destroy men, and they have captured them.

**5:27** Like a snare set full of birds, so their houses are full of deceit; therefore they have become great and grown rich,

**5:28** and they have transgressed justice; they did not judge the cause of the orphan, nor did they judge the cause of the widow.

**5:29** Shall I not punish for these things?" says the Lord. "Or shall My soul not take vengeance on such a nation as this?

**5:30** Astonishment and dreadful things have come to pass upon the land:

**5:31** the prophets prophesy unrighteous things, and the priests have clapped with their hands, and My people have loved it so; and what will you do for the things that come after?"

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — Greek reads "a wolf even to the houses" and "shall be hunted down," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a wolf of the deserts" and "shall be torn in pieces."
- **5:10** — Greek commands the invaders to "leave behind her foundations, for they belong to the Lord," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("strip away her branches, for they are not the LORD's").
- **5:16** — Greek omits the opening clause "their quiver is like an open tomb" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and joins the first clause of verse 17 directly to "All of them are mighty."
- **5:31** — Greek reads "the priests have clapped with their hands" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the priests rule by their own authority."
