## Text

**45:1** And Saphanias the son of Nathan, and Gedaliah the son of Pashhur, and Jehucal the son of Shelemiah heard the words which Jeremiah was speaking to the people, saying,

**45:2** "Thus says the Lord: 'He who dwells in this city shall die by the sword and by famine, but he who goes out to the Chaldeans shall live; and his life shall be for a prize, and he shall live.'

**45:3** For thus says the Lord: 'This city shall surely be delivered into the hands of the army of the king of Babylon, and he shall capture it.'"

**45:4** And they said to the king, "Please, let that man be put to death, for he weakens the hands of the fighting men who remain in the city and the hands of all the people, by speaking to them according to these words; for this man does not prophesy peace to this people, but only evil."

**45:5** And the king said, "Behold, he is in your hands." For the king could not prevail against them.

**45:6** And they cast him into the cistern of Malchiah the king's son, which was in the court of the guard, and they lowered him into the cistern. And in the cistern there was no water, but only mire; and he was in the mire.

**45:7** And Ebed-Melech the Ethiopian, and he was in the house of the king, heard that they had put Jeremiah into the cistern; and the king was at the Gate of Benjamin.

**45:8** And he went out to him, and spoke to the king, and said,

**45:9** "You have done wickedly in what you have done, to kill this man because of the famine; for there is no longer any bread in the city."

**45:10** And the king commanded Ebed-Melech, saying, "Take into your hands thirty men from here, and bring him up out of the cistern, lest he die."

**45:11** And Ebed-Melech took the men and went into the underground house of the king, and took from there old rags and old ropes, and threw them to Jeremiah into the cistern,

**45:12** and he said, "Put these underneath the ropes." And Jeremiah did so.

**45:13** And they drew him with the ropes and brought him up out of the cistern; and Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard.

**45:14** And the king sent and called him to himself into the house of Aseleisel, which was in the house of the Lord. And the king said to him, "I will ask you a matter, and please do not hide a word from me."

**45:15** And Jeremiah said to the king, "If I declare it to you, will you not surely put me to death? And if I give you counsel, you will by no means listen to me."

**45:16** And the king swore to him, saying, "As the Lord lives, who made this soul for us, I will surely not kill you, nor will I give you into the hands of these men."

**45:17** And Jeremiah said to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'If you indeed go out to the commanders of the king of Babylon, your soul shall live, and this city shall by no means be burned with fire; and you and your house shall live.

**45:18** And if you do not go out, this city shall be delivered into the hands of the Chaldeans, and they shall burn it with fire, and you shall by no means be saved.'"

**45:19** And the king said to Jeremiah, "I am anxious about the Jews who have fled to the Chaldeans, lest they deliver me into their hands, and they mock me."

**45:20** And Jeremiah said, "They shall by no means hand you over. Hear the word of the Lord which I speak to you, and it shall be better for you, and your soul shall live.

**45:21** And if you refuse to go out, this is the word which the Lord has shown me:

**45:22** And behold, all the women who were left in the house of the king of Judah were being led out to the princes of the king of Babylon, and they were saying, 'Your men of peace have deceived you and shall prevail against you; and they shall bring down your feet in slippery places; they have turned away from you.'

**45:23** And they shall bring out your wives and your children to the Chaldeans, and you shall by no means be saved, for you shall be captured by the hand of the king of Babylon, and this city shall be burned down."

**45:24** And the king said to him, "Let no man know of these words, and you shall by no means die.

**45:25** And if the princes hear that I have spoken with you, and they come to you and say to you, 'Declare to us, what did the king speak to you? Do not hide it from us, and we will by no means put you to death. And what did the king speak to you?'

**45:26** then you shall say to them, 'I am casting my plea for mercy before the eyes of the king, that he would not send me back to the house of Jonathan to die there.'"

**45:27** And all the princes came to Jeremiah and questioned him; and he declared to them according to all these words which the king had commanded him. And they kept silent, because the word of the Lord was not heard.

**45:28** And Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard until the time when Jerusalem was captured.

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — "Saphanias the son of Nathan" follows the Greek text directly, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Shephatiah the son of Mattan" and also includes "Pashhur the son of Malchijah."
- **45:4** — "prophesy" translates the rare Greek verb chresimologeo, meaning to speak beneficially or prophesy good.
- **45:9** — The Greek addresses the king directly ("You have done wickedly in what you have done"), whereas the Hebrew text blames the officials who cast Jeremiah into the cistern.
- **45:14** — "Aseleisel" is the Septuagint transliteration of a Hebrew phrase meaning "the third [entrance]."
- **45:27** — "because the word of the Lord was not heard" preserves the Septuagint reading; the Hebrew text reads "for the matter was not overheard."
