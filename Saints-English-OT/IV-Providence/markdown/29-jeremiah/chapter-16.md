## Text

**16:1** "And you, do not take a wife," says the Lord God of Israel,

**16:2** "and neither son nor daughter shall be born to you in this place."

**16:3** For thus says the Lord concerning the sons and concerning the daughters who are born in this place, and concerning their mothers who have borne them, and concerning their fathers who have fathered them in this land:

**16:4** "By a death of sickness they shall die; they shall not be lamented, nor shall they be buried. They shall be for an example upon the face of the earth, and they shall be for the beasts of the earth and for the birds of heaven; by the sword they shall fall, and by famine they shall be consumed."

**16:5** Thus says the Lord: "Do not enter into their company of revelry, and do not go to lament, nor mourn for them, for I have withdrawn My peace from this people.

**16:6** They shall by no means lament for them, nor shall they make incisions, and they shall not shave themselves;

**16:7** and bread shall by no means be broken in their mourning for comfort over the dead; they shall not give him a cup to drink for comfort over his father and his mother.

**16:8** You shall not enter into a house of drinking to sit down with them to eat and to drink."

**16:9** For thus says the Lord God of Israel: "Behold, I am causing to cease from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

**16:10** And it shall come to pass, when you declare to this people all these words, and they say to you, 'Why has the Lord spoken all these evils against us? What is our unrighteousness? And what is our sin which we have sinned before the Lord our God?'

**16:11** then you shall say to them, 'Because your fathers forsook Me, says the Lord, and went after foreign gods and served them and worshiped them, and forsook Me and did not keep My law,

**16:12** and you have acted more wickedly than your fathers; and behold, you walk, each one after what pleases your evil heart, so as not to obey Me.

**16:13** And I will cast you away from this land into the land which you and your fathers did not know, and there you shall serve other gods, who will not show you mercy.'"

**16:14** "Therefore, behold, days are coming," says the Lord, "and they shall no longer say, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,'

**16:15** but, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel from the land of the north and from all the countries where they were driven out.' And I will restore them to their own land which I gave to their fathers.

**16:16** Behold, I am sending many fishermen," says the Lord, "and they shall fish for them; and after this I will send many hunters, and they shall hunt them upon every mountain and upon every hill and out of the clefts of the rocks.

**16:17** For My eyes are upon all their ways, and their unrighteous deeds have not been hidden from before My eyes.

**16:18** And I will repay for all their evils and their sins, by which they profaned My land with the carcasses of their abominations and with their lawless deeds, by which they trespassed against My inheritance."

**16:19** O Lord, You are my strength and my help and my refuge in days of evil; to You nations shall come from the end of the earth and say, "How false are the idols our fathers possessed, and there is no profit in them!"

**16:20** Shall a man make gods for himself, when these are not gods?

**16:21** Therefore, behold, I will reveal My hand to them at this time, and I will make known to them My power; and they shall know that My name is the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **16:4** — The Septuagint reads "for an example" (eis paradeigma, a public spectacle) rather than "as refuse" as in the Masoretic Text, and places the beasts and birds before the sword and famine.
- **16:5** — Greek thiasos denotes a ritual or banqueting company, here rendering the Hebrew funerary feasting association (marzeach).
- **16:13** — The Septuagint ends the verse with "other gods, who will not show you mercy," whereas the Masoretic Text has the Lord say "for I will not show you favor."
