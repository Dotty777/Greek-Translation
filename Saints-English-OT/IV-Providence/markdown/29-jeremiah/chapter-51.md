## Text

**51:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews who dwelt in the land of Egypt, and those who resided in Migdol, and in Tahpanhes, and in the land of Pathros, saying:

**51:2** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: "You have seen all the evils which I brought upon Jerusalem and upon the cities of Judah; and behold, they are desolate, without inhabitants,

**51:3** because of their wickedness which they committed to provoke Me, going to burn incense to other gods whom you did not know.

**51:4** And I sent to you My servants the prophets early in the morning, and I sent them, saying, 'Do not do this thing of defilement which I hated.'

**51:5** Yet they did not listen, nor did they incline their ear to turn away from their evils, so as not to burn incense to other gods.

**51:6** Therefore My wrath and My fury poured down, and kindled in the gates of Judah and outside Jerusalem; and they became a desolation and an untrodden waste, as it is this day."

**51:7** And now, thus says the Lord Almighty: "Why do you commit great evils against your own souls, to cut off from you man and woman, infant and nursing child, from the midst of Judah, so that not one of you is left remaining,

**51:8** provoking Me by the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt, where you have come to dwell, that you may be cut off and become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

**51:9** Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, and the evils of the kings of Judah, and the evils of your rulers, and the evils of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and outside Jerusalem?

**51:10** And they have not ceased to this day, nor have they held fast to My ordinances which I set before their fathers."

**51:11** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I set My face

**51:12** to destroy all the remnant who are in Egypt; and they shall fall by the sword and by famine, and they shall perish, from the least to the greatest, and they shall become a reproach, and a destruction, and a curse.

**51:13** And I will punish those who dwell in the land of Egypt, as I punished Jerusalem, by the sword and by famine;

**51:14** and not one of the remnant of Judah who sojourn in the land of Egypt shall be saved to return to the land of Judah, to which they hope in their souls to return; they shall by no means return, except those who escape."

**51:15** Then all the men who knew that their wives burned incense, and all the women, a great assembly, and all the people who dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying,

**51:16** "As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of the Lord, we will not listen to you!

**51:17** For we will surely perform every word that shall proceed from our mouth, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her, just as we did—we and our fathers, our kings and our rulers—in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem; for then we were filled with bread, and we fared well, and we saw no evil.

**51:18** But since we ceased burning incense to the queen of heaven, we have all been brought low, and we have perished by the sword and by famine.

**51:19** And since we burn incense to the queen of heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, did we make cakes for her and pour out drink offerings to her without our husbands?"

**51:20** Then Jeremiah spoke to all the people—to the mighty men, and to the women, and to all the people who had answered him with words—saying,

**51:21** "Did not the Lord remember the incense which we burned in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem—you and your fathers, your kings and your rulers, and the people of the land—and did it not come into His heart?

**51:22** So the Lord could no longer bear it, because of the wickedness of your deeds and because of the abominations which you committed; therefore your land became a desolation, and an untrodden waste, and a curse, as it is on this day,

**51:23** because of the incense you burned and the ways you sinned against the Lord; and you did not listen to the voice of the Lord, nor did you walk in His ordinances, and in the law, and in His testimonies; therefore these evils have overtaken you."

**51:24** Moreover Jeremiah said to the people and to the women, "Hear the word of the Lord."

**51:25** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: "You women have spoken with your mouth and fulfilled it with your hands, saying, 'We will surely perform our vows which we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.' You have surely stood firm in your vows, and you have surely performed them."

**51:26** Therefore hear the word of the Lord, all Judah who dwell in the land of Egypt: "Behold, I have sworn by My great name," says the Lord, "that My name shall no longer be in the mouth of anyone of Judah to say, 'The Lord lives,' in all the land of Egypt.

**51:27** For I am watching over them to harm them and not to do them good; and all Judah who dwell in the land of Egypt shall perish by the sword and by famine, until they are utterly consumed.

**51:28** Yet those who escape the sword shall return to the land of Judah, few in number; and the remnant of Judah who have settled in the land of Egypt to dwell there shall know whose word shall abide.

**51:29** And this shall be the sign to you, that I will punish you for evil."

**51:30** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, I give Hophra king of Egypt into the hands of his enemy and into the hands of those who seek his life, just as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy and the one who sought his life."

**51:31** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in the book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah:

**51:32** Thus says the Lord concerning you, O Baruch:

**51:33** "You said, 'Woe to me, woe to me! For the Lord has added toil to my pain; I lay down in groanings, and I found no rest.'"

**51:34** Say to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'Behold, those whom I built, I tear down; and those whom I planted, I pluck up.

**51:35** And will you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek them; for behold, I am bringing evils upon all flesh,' says the Lord, 'and I will give your life to you as a prize in every place wherever you go.'"

## Translator Notes

- **51:1** — In the Septuagint order of Jeremiah, chapter 51 comprises the oracles corresponding to chapters 44 (verses 1-30) and 45 (verses 31-35) of the Masoretic Text.
- **51:21** — The Greek uses the first-person plural 'which we burned,' though the appositive list immediately shifts to the second person, 'you and your fathers.'
- **51:25** — Unlike the Masoretic Text, which addresses both the men and their wives, the Greek directs this rebuke specifically to 'you women' using feminine participles throughout.
- **51:33** — The Greek reads 'I lay down in groanings,' whereas the Masoretic Text reads 'I am weary with my groaning.'
