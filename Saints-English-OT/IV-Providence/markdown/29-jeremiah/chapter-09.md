## Text

**9:1** Who would give me in the wilderness a farthest lodging place, that I might leave my people and depart from them? For they all commit adultery, an assembly of treacherous men,

**9:2** and they have bent their tongue like a bow. Falsehood, and not faithfulness, has prevailed upon the land; for they have gone forth from evil to evil, and they have not known Me.

**9:3** Beware, each one, of his neighbor, and do not trust in their brethren; for every brother will surely supplant with the heel, and every friend will walk deceitfully.

**9:4** Each one will mock his friend, and they will by no means speak the truth; their tongue has learned to speak falsehoods; they have done wrong and have not ceased so as to return.

**9:5** Usury upon usury, and deceit upon deceit; they refused to know Me.

**9:6** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I will refine them by fire and test them, for I will act because of the wickedness of the daughter of My people.

**9:7** Their tongue is a wounding arrow; deceitful are the words of their mouth; one speaks peaceable words to his neighbor, yet in himself he harbors enmity.

**9:8** Shall I not punish for these things," says the Lord, "or on such a people shall My soul not take vengeance?

**9:9** Take up a wailing over the mountains, and over the paths of the wilderness a lamentation, for they have failed because there are no men; they have not heard the sound of possessions; from the birds of heaven even to the beasts, they have started in dismay; they are gone.

**9:10** And I will give Jerusalem over to exile and to be a dwelling place of dragons, and the cities of Judah I will make a desolation, because they are without inhabitant."

**9:11** Who is the man of understanding? Let him understand this. And he to whom the word of the mouth of the Lord has come, let him declare to you why the land has perished; it has been kindled like a wilderness, with no one passing through it.

**9:12** And the Lord said to me, "Because they have forsaken My law which I set before their face, and have not listened to My voice,

**9:13** but have walked after the desires of their evil heart and after the idols which their fathers taught them."

**9:14** Therefore thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: "Behold, I will feed them afflictions, and give them water of gall to drink;

**9:15** and I will scatter them among the nations which neither they nor their fathers knew, and I will send the sword upon them until I have consumed them by it."

**9:16** Thus says the Lord: "Call for the mourning women, and let them come; and send for the skillful women, and let them sound forth,

**9:17** and let them take up a lamentation over you, and let your eyes bring down tears, and let your eyelids flow with water.

**9:18** For a voice of lamentation is heard in Zion: 'How we are brought to ruin! We are greatly put to shame, because we have forsaken the land and cast away our dwellings!'"

**9:19** Hear now, O women, the word of God, and let your ears receive the words of His mouth; teach your daughters wailing, and each woman her neighbor a lamentation.

**9:20** For death has climbed up through your windows; it has entered into your land to cut off infants from outside and young men from the broad streets.

**9:21** And the corpses of men shall be for an example upon the face of the open field of your land, like grass behind the reaper, and there shall be no one to gather them.

**9:22** Thus says the Lord: "Do not let the wise man boast in his wisdom, and do not let the strong man boast in his strength, and do not let the rich man boast in his wealth;

**9:23** but rather let him who boasts boast in this: that he understands and knows that I am the Lord, who exercises mercy and judgment and righteousness upon the earth; for in these things is My will," says the Lord.

**9:24** "Behold, days are coming," says the Lord, "that I will visit punishment upon all who are circumcised in their foreskins:

**9:25** upon Egypt, and upon Idumea, and upon Edom, and upon the sons of Ammon, and upon the sons of Moab, and upon every one who clips the hair around his face, those who dwell in the wilderness; for all the nations are uncircumcised in the flesh, and all the house of Israel are uncircumcised in their heart."

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — Greek tokos epi tokō ("usury upon usury") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("your dwelling is in the midst of deceit").
- **9:6** — Greek hoti poiēsō ("for I will act") presents a declarative statement rather than the rhetorical question found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:10** — Greek eis metoikian ("over to exile") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("a heap of ruins"), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants.
- **9:25** — The Septuagint names "Idumea" alongside "Edom," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Judah" in place of "Idumea."
