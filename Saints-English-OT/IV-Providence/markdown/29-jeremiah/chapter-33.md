## Text

**33:1** In the beginning of King Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, this word came from the Lord.

**33:2** Thus says the Lord: "Stand in the court of the house of the Lord, and you shall proclaim to all the Judeans and to all who come to worship in the house of the Lord all the words that I have commanded you to proclaim to them; do not take away a word.

**33:3** Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each one from his evil way, and I will cease from the evils that I purpose to do to them on account of their evil practices.

**33:4** And you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: If you do not listen to Me, to walk in My statutes which I have set before your face,

**33:5** to heed the words of My servants the prophets, whom I send to you early in the morning—and I sent them, yet you did not listen to Me—

**33:6** then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make the city a curse to all the nations of all the earth.'"

**33:7** And the priests and the false prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the Lord.

**33:8** And it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking all that the Lord had commanded him to speak to all the people, that the priests and the false prophets and all the people seized him, saying, "You shall surely die,

**33:9** for you have prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, 'This house shall be like Shiloh, and this city shall be made desolate, without inhabitants.'" And all the people were assembled against Jeremiah in the house of the Lord.

**33:10** And the rulers of Judah heard this word, and they went up from the house of the king to the house of the Lord, and sat down at the entrance of the New Gate.

**33:11** And the priests and the false prophets spoke to the rulers and to all the people: "A judgment of death is due this man, for he has prophesied against this city, as you have heard with your own ears."

**33:12** And Jeremiah spoke to the rulers and to all the people, saying, "The Lord sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.

**33:13** And now amend your ways and your deeds, and listen to the voice of the Lord, and the Lord will cease from the evils that He has spoken against you.

**33:14** And behold, I am in your hands; do to me as is fitting and as is better for you.

**33:15** Only know for certain that if you put me to death, you bring innocent blood upon yourselves and upon this city and upon those who dwell in it; for in truth the Lord has sent me to you to speak all these words in your ears."

**33:16** And the rulers and all the people said to the priests and to the false prophets, "No judgment of death is due this man, for in the name of the Lord our God he has spoken to us."

**33:17** And certain men of the elders of the land rose up and spoke to the whole assembly of the people,

**33:18** "Micah the Morasthite lived in the days of Hezekiah, king of Judah, and spoke to all the people of Judah, 'Thus says the Lord: Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall become an untrodden waste, and the mountain of the house a grove of the forest.'

**33:19** Did Hezekiah and all Judah put him to death at all? Was it not that they feared the Lord, and made supplication before the face of the Lord, and the Lord ceased from the evils that He had spoken against them? Yet we have committed great evils against our own souls."

**33:20** There was also a man prophesying in the name of the Lord, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim, and he prophesied concerning this land according to all the words of Jeremiah.

**33:21** And King Jehoiakim and all the rulers heard all his words, and they sought to kill him; and Uriah heard of it and went into Egypt.

**33:22** And the king sent men into Egypt,

**33:23** and they brought him out from there and brought him in to the king; and he struck him with the sword and cast him into the tomb of the sons of his people.

**33:24** Nevertheless, the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that they did not deliver him into the hands of the people to put him to death.

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — Greek reads "all the Judeans" (hapasi tois Ioudaiois), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "all the cities of Judah."
- **33:7** — Here and throughout chapters 33–35 (33:8, 11, 16; 34:9; 35:1), the Septuagint explicitly calls Jeremiah's opponents "false prophets" (pseudoprophetai), where the Hebrew reads simply "prophets."
- **33:18** — Greek eis abaton ("an untrodden waste") renders Jerusalem's desolation in Micah's oracle (cf. Micah 3:12), differing from the Hebrew's "heaps of ruins."
