## Text

**43:1** In the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**43:2** "Take for yourself a scroll of a book, and write on it all the words that I have spoken to you against Jerusalem, and against Judah, and against all the nations, from the day I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah king of Judah, even to this day.

**43:3** Perhaps the house of Judah will hear all the evils that I purpose to do to them, that they may turn back from their wicked way, and I will be merciful to their iniquities and to their sins."

**43:4** Then Jeremiah called Baruch the son of Neriah, and he wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the Lord, which He had spoken to him, upon a scroll of a book.

**43:5** And Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, "I am kept under guard; I am not able to enter into the house of the Lord.

**43:6** And you shall read from this scroll in the hearing of the people in the house of the Lord on a day of fasting; and in the hearing of all Judah who come from their city you shall read to them.

**43:7** Perhaps their plea for mercy will fall before the face of the Lord, and they will turn back from their wicked way; for great are the wrath and the anger of the Lord that He has spoken against this people."

**43:8** And Baruch did according to all that Jeremiah commanded him, reading from the book the words of the Lord in the house of the Lord.

**43:9** Now it came to pass in the eighth year of King Jehoiakim, in the ninth month, that all the people in Jerusalem and the house of Judah proclaimed a fast before the Lord.

**43:10** Then Baruch read from the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the Lord, in the house of Gemariah the son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court at the entrance of the New Gate of the house of the Lord, and in the hearing of all the people.

**43:11** And Micaiah the son of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, heard all the words of the Lord from the book.

**43:12** And he went down to the king's house, into the house of the scribe; and behold, all the princes were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah the son of Shelemiah, and Jonathan the son of Achbor, and Gemariah the son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah the son of Hananiah, and all the princes.

**43:13** Then Micaiah declared to them all the words that he had heard when Baruch read in the hearing of the people.

**43:14** And all the princes sent to Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi, saying, "The scroll from which you read in the hearing of the people, take it in your hand and come." So Baruch took the scroll and went down to them.

**43:15** And they said to him, "Read again in our hearing." So Baruch read.

**43:16** Now it came to pass, when they had heard all the words, that they took counsel, each one with his neighbor, and said, "We shall surely declare to the king all these words."

**43:17** And they questioned Baruch, saying, "Where did you write all these words?"

**43:18** And Baruch said, "From his own mouth Jeremiah declared to me all these words, and I wrote them in a book."

**43:19** Then they said to Baruch, "Go, hide yourself, you and Jeremiah; let no man know where you are."

**43:20** And they went in to the king in the court, and they gave the scroll to be kept in the house of Elisha, and they declared all the words to the king.

**43:21** Then the king sent Jehudi to bring the scroll; and he took it from the house of Elisha, and Jehudi read it in the hearing of the king and in the hearing of all the princes who stood around the king.

**43:22** Now the king was sitting in the winter house, and a brazier of fire was before him.

**43:23** And it came to pass, when Jehudi had read three or four columns, that he cut them off with the scribe's razor and cast them into the fire that was on the brazier, until the entire scroll was consumed in the fire that was on the brazier.

**43:24** And they did not inquire, nor did they tear their garments—the king and his servants who heard all these words.

**43:25** And Elnathan and Gedaliah advised the king to burn the scroll.

**43:26** And the king commanded Jerahmeel the king's son and Seraiah the son of Azriel to seize Baruch and Jeremiah; but they hid themselves.

**43:27** Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, after the king had burned the scroll—all the words that Baruch had written from the mouth of Jeremiah—saying,

**43:28** "Again, take another scroll for yourself, and write all the words that were on the scroll which King Jehoiakim burned;

**43:29** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: You have burned this scroll, saying, "Why have you written on it, saying, 'The king of Babylon shall surely come and destroy this land, and man and beast shall vanish from it'?"

**43:30** Therefore thus says the Lord against Jehoiakim king of Judah: He shall have no one sitting on the throne of David, and his dead body shall be cast out in the heat of the day and in the frost of the night.

**43:31** And I will punish him, and his offspring, and his servants; and I will bring upon him, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and upon the land of Judah, all the evils that I have spoken against them, and they did not listen.'"

**43:32** So Baruch took another scroll and wrote on it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book which Jehoiakim had burned; and still more words like these were added to it.

## Translator Notes

- **43:9** — The Greek text reads "in the eighth year," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 36:9) reads "in the fifth year."
- **43:14** — The Greek text omits the messenger Jehudi here and attaches the lineage "the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi" directly to Baruch the son of Neriah.
- **43:20** — The Greek text reads "Elisha" here and in verse 21, differing from "Elishama" in verse 12.
- **43:25** — The Greek text names "Elnathan and Gedaliah" and states that they advised the king to burn the scroll, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 36:25) lists Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urging him not to burn it.
- **43:26** — The Greek text reads "they hid themselves" without specifying an agent, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 36:26) states that the Lord hid them.
