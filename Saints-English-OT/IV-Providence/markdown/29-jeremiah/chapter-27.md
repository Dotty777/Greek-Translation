## Text

**27:1** The word of the Lord which He spoke against Babylon.

**27:2** Declare among the nations and make it heard, and do not conceal it; say, "Babylon has been captured; Bel, the fearless one, has been put to shame; the delicate one, Maiodak, has been delivered up."

**27:3** For a nation has come up against her from the north; this one shall make her land a desolation, and there shall be no one who dwells in it, from man even to beast.

**27:4** In those days and in that time, the sons of Israel shall come, they and the sons of Judah together; walking and weeping they shall go, seeking the Lord their God.

**27:5** They shall ask the way to Zion, for toward this place they shall set their face; and they shall come and take refuge in the Lord God, for an everlasting covenant shall not be forgotten.

**27:6** My people have become lost sheep; their shepherds have driven them out, upon the mountains they have led them astray; from mountain to hill they have gone, they have forgotten their resting place.

**27:7** All who found them consumed them; their enemies said, "Let us not release them, because they have sinned against the Lord." A pasture of righteousness belongs to Him who gathered their fathers.

**27:8** Estrange yourselves from the midst of Babylon and from the land of the Chaldeans, and go forth, and become like dragons before the sheep.

**27:9** For behold, I am stirring up against Babylon assemblies of nations from the land of the north, and they shall array themselves against her; from there she shall be captured. Like the dart of a skilled warrior, it shall not return empty.

**27:10** And Chaldea shall become a spoil; all who plunder her shall be filled.

**27:11** Because you rejoiced and boasted while plundering My inheritance, because you leaped like calves in the grass and butted with horns like bulls.

**27:12** Your mother has been greatly put to shame, a mother for good things, the last of the nations, a wilderness.

**27:13** Because of the wrath of the Lord she shall not be inhabited, and she shall become wholly a desolation; and everyone who passes through Babylon shall look gloomy, and they shall hiss at all her plague.

**27:14** Array yourselves against Babylon all around, all you who bend the bow; shoot arrows at her, do not spare your arrows.

**27:15** And prevail over her; her hands have grown limp, her battlements have fallen, and her wall has been torn down; for it is vengeance from God. Take vengeance upon her; as she has done, do to her.

**27:16** Utterly destroy the seed from Babylon, the one holding a sickle in the time of harvest; from the face of the Greek sword each one shall turn back to his people, and each one shall flee to his own land.

**27:17** Israel is a straying sheep; lions have driven him out. First the king of Assyria devoured him, and afterward this one, the king of Babylon, devoured his bones.

**27:18** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I am executing vengeance upon him of Babylon and upon his land, just as I executed vengeance upon the king of Assyria.

**27:19** And I will restore Israel to his pasture, and he shall feed on Carmel and on Mount Ephraim and in Gilead, and his soul shall be filled.

**27:20** In those days and in that time, they shall seek the iniquity of Israel, and it shall not exist, and the sins of Judah, and they shall by no means be found; for I will be merciful to those who remain upon the earth," says the Lord.

**27:21** "Bitterly go up against her, and against those who dwell upon her; take vengeance, O sword, and destroy," says the Lord, "and do according to all that I command you."

**27:22** A sound of battle and great destruction is in the land of the Chaldeans.

**27:23** How has the hammer of all the earth been broken and shattered? How has Babylon become a desolation among the nations?

**27:24** They shall come upon you, and you shall not know it, like Babylon, and you shall be captured; you have been found and seized, because you withstood the Lord.

**27:25** The Lord has opened His treasury and has brought forth the weapons of His wrath; for there is a work for the Lord God in the land of the Chaldeans.

**27:26** For her times have come. Open her storehouses, search her like a cave, and utterly destroy her; let there be no remnant of her.

**27:27** Dry up all her fruits, and let them go down to the slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come, and the time of their vengeance.

**27:28** The voice of those who flee and escape from the land of Babylon, to declare in Zion the vengeance from the Lord our God.

**27:29** Summon many against Babylon, everyone who bends the bow; encamp against her all around; let no one of hers escape; repay her according to her works; according to all that she has done, do to her, for she has withstood the Lord, the Holy God of Israel.

**27:30** Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets, and all her men of war shall be cast down, said the Lord.

**27:31** Behold, I am against you, O insolent one, says the Lord, for your day has come, and the time of your vengeance.

**27:32** And your insolence shall grow weak and fall, and there shall be no one who raises her up; and I will kindle a fire in her forest, and it shall devour all that is around her.

**27:33** Thus says the Lord: "The sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been oppressed; together all those who took them captive have oppressed them, because they were unwilling to send them away.

**27:34** Yet He who redeems them is strong; the Lord Almighty is His name. He shall execute judgment against His adversaries, so that He may remove the earth and provoke those who dwell in Babylon."

**27:35** A sword against the Chaldeans, and against those who dwell in Babylon, and against her nobles, and against her wise men.

**27:36** A sword against her warriors, and they shall be weakened; a sword against their horses and against their chariots.

**27:37** A sword against their warriors and against the mixed multitude in her midst, and they shall become like women; a sword against her treasures, and they shall be scattered

**27:38** upon her water, and they shall be put to shame; for it is a land of carved images, and in the islands where they boasted.

**27:39** Therefore phantoms shall dwell in the islands, and daughters of sirens shall dwell in her; she shall no longer be inhabited forever.

**27:40** Just as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and those bordering them, said the Lord, no man shall dwell there, and no son of man shall sojourn there.

**27:41** Behold, a people is coming from the north, and a great nation and many kings shall be stirred up from the farthest part of the earth,

**27:42** holding bow and dagger; it is fierce, and shall show no mercy; their voice shall roar like the sea; they shall ride on horses, arrayed like fire for battle, against you, O daughter of Babylon.

**27:43** The king of Babylon heard the report of them, and his hands grew limp; anguish took hold of him, pangs like those of a woman in labor.

**27:44** Behold, like a lion he shall come up from the Jordan to Gaithan; for I will swiftly drive them away from her, and I will appoint every young man over her. For who is like Me? And who shall withstand Me? And who is this shepherd who shall stand before My face?

**27:45** Therefore hear the counsel of the Lord which He has purposed against Babylon, and His designs which He has devised against the Chaldeans who dwell there: surely the lambs of their sheep shall be destroyed; surely pasture shall be laid waste from them.

**27:46** For at the sound of the capture of Babylon the earth shall be shaken, and an outcry shall be heard among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Septuagint chapter 27 corresponds to Masoretic chapter 50; the Greek adds the epithets "the fearless one" and "the delicate one" (cf. 26:28) and gives the Babylonian deity Merodach as "Maiodak."
- **27:8** — The Septuagint reads "dragons" (Greek drakontes) where the Hebrew text reads "he-goats" leading the flock.
- **27:21** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew place names Merathaim and Pekod by their roots as "Bitterly" and "take vengeance."
- **27:39** — The Septuagint renders the desert creatures of the Hebrew text as "phantoms in the islands" and "daughters of sirens."
- **27:44** — "Gaithan" is the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew phrase for "enduring pasture" as a proper place name.
