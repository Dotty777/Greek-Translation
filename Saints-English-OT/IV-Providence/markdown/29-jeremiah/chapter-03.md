## Text

**3:1** "If a man sends away his wife, and she departs from him and becomes another man's, shall she indeed return to him again? Will not that woman be utterly defiled? Yet you have played the harlot with many shepherds, and would you return to Me?" says the Lord.

**3:2** "Lift up your eyes straight ahead and see: where have you not been defiled? By the roads you sat for them like a forsaken crow, and you defiled the land with your harlotries and with your wicked deeds,

**3:3** and you had many shepherds as a stumbling block to yourself; you took on the countenance of a harlot, you acted shamelessly toward all.

**3:4** Did you not call Me as a house, and Father, and Guide of your maidenhood?

**3:5** Will it remain forever, or be kept to the end? Behold, you have spoken and done these evil things, and you prevailed."

**3:6** And the Lord said to me in the days of Josiah the king, "Have you seen what the habitation of Israel has done to Me? They went upon every high mountain and under every shady tree, and played the harlot there.

**3:7** And I said, after she had committed all these harlotries, 'Return to Me,' yet she did not return; and treacherous Judah saw her treachery.

**3:8** And I saw that for all the things which I also saw—for all the things in which the habitation of Israel was caught committing adultery—I sent her away and gave her a certificate of divorce into her hands; yet treacherous Judah did not fear, but she also went and played the harlot,

**3:9** and her harlotry came to nothing, and she committed adultery with wood and stone.

**3:10** And in all these things treacherous Judah has not turned back to Me with all her heart, but in falsehood."

**3:11** And the Lord said to me, "Israel has justified his soul more than treacherous Judah.

**3:12** Go and read these words toward the north, and you shall say, 'Return to Me, O habitation of Israel,' says the Lord, 'and I will not set My face against you; for I am merciful,' says the Lord, 'and I will not remain angry with you forever.

**3:13** Only acknowledge your iniquity, that you have acted impiously against the Lord your God, and have scattered your ways to strangers under every shady tree, and you have not obeyed My voice,' says the Lord."

**3:14** "Return, O sons who have turned away," says the Lord, "for I will exercise dominion over you; and I will take you, one from a city and two from a family, and I will bring you into Zion.

**3:15** And I will give you shepherds according to My heart, and they shall indeed shepherd you with understanding.

**3:16** And it shall come to pass, when you are multiplied and increased in the land in those days," says the Lord, "they shall no longer say, 'The ark of the covenant of the Holy One of Israel.' It shall not come into the heart, nor shall it be named, nor shall it be visited, nor shall it be made anymore.

**3:17** In those days and in that time they shall call Jerusalem, 'The Throne of the Lord,' and all the nations shall be gathered to her; and they shall no longer walk after the imaginations of their evil heart.

**3:18** In those days the house of Judah shall come together with the house of Israel, and they shall come together from the land of the north and from all the countries to the land that I gave as an inheritance to their fathers."

**3:19** And I said, "Let it be so, Lord!" For I will set you among the nations, and give you a choice land, an inheritance of God Almighty of the nations; and I said, "You shall call Me Father, and from Me you shall not turn away."

**3:20** "Yet as a woman breaks faith with her companion, so has the house of Israel broken faith with Me," says the Lord.

**3:21** A voice from lips was heard, of weeping and supplication of the sons of Israel, because they have done unrighteously in their ways; they have forgotten God their Holy One.

**3:22** "Return, O returning sons, and I will heal your brokenness." "Behold, we shall be servants to You, for You are the Lord our God.

**3:23** Truly for falsehood were the hills and the strength of the mountains, yet through the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.

**3:24** But shame has devoured the labors of our fathers from our youth, their sheep and their calves, and their sons and their daughters.

**3:25** We have lain down in our shame, and our dishonor has covered us, for we have sinned before our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even to this day, and we have not obeyed the voice of the Lord our God."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek reads "that woman" and "with many shepherds," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "that land" and "with many lovers."
- **3:2** — The Greek compares Judah to "a forsaken crow," reflecting a vocalization of the Hebrew consonants as "raven/crow" rather than the Masoretic Text's "Arab in the wilderness."
- **3:6** — The Greek renders the designation as "the habitation of Israel" (also in 3:8 and 3:12), deriving it from the Hebrew root for dwelling rather than turning back ("backsliding Israel").
- **3:19** — The Greek opens with the prayerful exclamation "Let it be so, Lord!" before continuing with God's declaration of "an inheritance of God Almighty of the nations," differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
