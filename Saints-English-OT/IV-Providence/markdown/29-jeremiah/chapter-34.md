## Text

**34:2** Thus says the Lord: "Make bonds and yoke-bars, and put them around your neck,

**34:3** and you shall send them to the king of Idumea, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the sons of Ammon, and to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon, by the hands of their messengers who come to meet them in Jerusalem, to Zedekiah, king of Judah.

**34:4** And you shall command them to say to their masters, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Thus you shall say to your masters:

**34:5** I have made the earth by My great strength and by My uplifted arm, and I will give it to whomever it seems good in My eyes.

**34:6** I have given the earth to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, to serve him, and the wild beasts of the field to labor for him.

**34:8** And whatever nation and kingdom will not put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will visit them with sword and with famine, says the Lord, until they perish by his hand.

**34:9** And as for you, do not listen to your false prophets, nor to those who divine for you, nor to those who dream for you, nor to your omens, nor to your sorcerers, who say, "You shall by no means serve the king of Babylon,"

**34:10** for they prophesy lies to you, so as to remove you far from your land.

**34:11** And the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will leave it upon its own land; and it shall serve him and dwell in it.'"

**34:12** And to Zedekiah, king of Judah, I spoke according to all these words, saying, "Bring your neck and serve the king of Babylon.

**34:14** For they prophesy unjust things to you,

**34:15** for I have not sent them, says the Lord, yet they prophesy in My name unjustly, so as to destroy you; and you shall perish, both you and your prophets who prophesy lies to you unjustly."

**34:16** To you and to all this people and to the priests I spoke, saying, "Thus says the Lord: Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you, saying, 'Behold, the vessels of the house of the Lord shall return from Babylon,' for they prophesy unjust things to you; I have not sent them.

**34:18** If they are prophets, and if the word of the Lord is in them, let them meet Me;

**34:19** for thus says the Lord: Even concerning the remaining vessels,

**34:20** which the king of Babylon did not take when he led Jeconiah into exile from Jerusalem,

**34:22** they shall enter into Babylon, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — Chapters 33–35 in the Septuagint correspond to chapters 26–28 in the Hebrew Bible; chapter 34 is substantially shorter in the Greek text, lacking counterparts to MT 27:1, 7, 13, 17, and 21.
- **34:3** — Greek Idoumaia ("Idumea") is the Septuagint's regular name for Edom, and the phrase "who come to meet them" reflects a distinct Greek reading.
- **34:11** — Greek reads "and it shall serve him" (referring to the king of Babylon), whereas the Hebrew refers to tilling the land ("and it shall till it").
- **34:18** — Greek apantesatosan moi ("let them meet Me") continues first-person divine speech from verse 16, whereas the Hebrew reads "let them intercede with the Lord of hosts."
