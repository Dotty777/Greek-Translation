## Text

**19:1** Then the Lord said to me, "Go and acquire a molded earthen flask, and you shall bring some of the elders of the people and of the priests,

**19:2** and you shall go out to the burial ground of the sons of their children, which is at the entrance of the Gate of Tharseis, and read there all these words that I shall speak to you;

**19:3** and you shall say to them, 'Hear the word of the Lord, O kings of Judah and men of Judah, and inhabitants of Jerusalem, and those who enter through these gates: Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Behold, I am bringing evils upon this place, so that the ears of everyone who hears them shall ring,

**19:4** because they have forsaken Me and estranged this place, and have burned incense in it to strange gods whom they and their fathers did not know; and the kings of Judah have filled this place with innocent blood,

**19:5** and they have built high places to Baal, to burn their sons in fire; I did not command it, nor did I conceive it in My heart.

**19:6** Therefore, behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and this place shall not be called Downfall and Burial Ground of the Son of Hinnom, but rather Burial Ground of the Slaughter.

**19:7** And I will slaughter the counsel of Judah and the counsel of Jerusalem in this place, and I will cast them down by the sword before their enemies and by the hands of those who seek their lives; and I will give their dead bodies as food for the birds of heaven and for the beasts of the earth.

**19:8** And I will bring this city down to desolation and to a hissing; everyone who passes by it shall look gloomy and shall hiss because of all its plague.

**19:9** And they shall eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and each one shall eat the flesh of his neighbor in the enclosure and siege with which their enemies shall besiege them.'

**19:10** And you shall shatter the flask before the eyes of the men who go out with you,

**19:11** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: So will I shatter this people and this city, just as an earthen vessel is shattered, which cannot be mended.

**19:12** For thus will I do, says the Lord, to this place and to those who dwell in it, to give this city over like the one that falls to ruin.

**19:13** And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah shall be like the place that falls to ruin, because of their uncleannesses in all the houses in which they burned incense upon their rooftops to all the host of heaven and poured out drink offerings to strange gods.'"

**19:14** Then Jeremiah came from the Downfall, where the Lord had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the house of the Lord and said to all the people,

**19:15** "Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing upon this city and upon all its cities and upon its villages all the evils that I have spoken against it, because they have hardened their neck so as not to listen to My commandments."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Greek polyandrion ("burial ground" or "common cemetery") renders the Valley of Ben-Hinnom, and "Gate of Tharseis" transliterates the gate name rendered "Potsherd Gate" in the Hebrew.
- **19:6** — Greek Diaptosis ("Downfall" or "Ruin") translates Hebrew Topheth, a wordplay echoed by the related participial forms in verses 12-14.
- **19:7** — Greek sphaxo ("I will slaughter") creates a direct verbal echo of "Burial Ground of the Slaughter" in verse 6, whereas the Hebrew reads "I will empty out" or "make void."
