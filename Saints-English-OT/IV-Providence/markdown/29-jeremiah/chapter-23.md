## Text

**23:1** Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of their pasture!

**23:2** Therefore thus says the Lord against those who shepherd My people: "You have scattered My sheep and driven them away, and have not watched over them; behold, I am executing vengeance upon you according to your wicked practices.

**23:3** And I Myself will gather in the remnant of My people from across all the earth where I drove them, and I will restore them to their pasture, and they shall increase and be multiplied.

**23:4** And I will raise up shepherds for them who shall shepherd them; and they shall no longer fear nor be terrified," says the Lord.

**23:5** "Behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "when I will raise up for David a righteous Dayspring; and a King shall reign and have understanding, and shall execute judgment and righteousness upon the earth.

**23:6** In His days Judah also shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell in confidence; and this is His name which the Lord shall call Him: 'Josedek.'" Concerning the prophets.

**23:8** "And I will bring upon you an everlasting reproach and an everlasting dishonor, which shall not be forgotten. Therefore, behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "when they shall no longer say, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel out of the land of Egypt,' but, 'The Lord lives, who gathered all the seed of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the countries where He had driven them, and restored them to their own land.'"

**23:9** My heart is broken within me; all my bones are shaken; I have become like a broken man, and like a man overcome by wine, because of the presence of the Lord and because of the presence of the splendor of His glory.

**23:10** For because of these the land has mourned; the pastures of the wilderness have dried up. Their forest has become evil, and their strength likewise.

**23:11** "For both priest and prophet are defiled; even in My house I have seen their wicked deeds.

**23:12** Therefore let their way become to them a slippery place in thick darkness, and they shall be tripped up and fall in it; for I will bring calamities upon them in the year of their visitation.

**23:13** And among the prophets of Samaria I saw lawless deeds: they prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel astray.

**23:14** And among the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen horrible things: they commit adultery and walk in lies, and take hold of the hands of many, so that no one turns back from his wicked way. They have all become to Me like Sodom, and her inhabitants like Gomorrah."

**23:15** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I will feed them anguish and give them bitter water to drink; for from the prophets of Jerusalem defilement has gone out into all the land."

**23:16** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Do not listen to the words of the prophets, for they devise a vain vision for themselves; they speak from their own heart, and not from the mouth of the Lord.

**23:17** They say to those who reject the word of the Lord, 'You shall have peace'; and to all who walk in their own desires, to everyone who walks in the error of his heart, they have said, 'No evil shall come upon you.'

**23:18** For who has stood in the council of the Lord and seen His word? Who has given ear and heard?

**23:19** Behold, a tempest from the Lord, and wrath goes forth into a violent shaking; whirling, it shall come upon the ungodly,

**23:20** and the fury of the Lord shall no longer turn back, until He has accomplished it from the purpose of His heart. At the end of the days they shall understand it.

**23:21** I did not send the prophets, yet they ran; nor did I speak to them, yet they prophesied.

**23:22** And if they had stood in My council, and if they had heard My words, then they would have turned My people back from their wicked practices.

**23:23** I am a God who draws near, and not a God afar off.

**23:24** Shall anyone hide himself in secret places, and I shall not see him? Do I not fill heaven and earth?" says the Lord.

**23:25** "I have heard what the prophets speak; they prophesy lies in My name, saying, 'I have dreamed a dream.'

**23:26** How long shall this be in the heart of the prophets who prophesy lies, while they prophesy the desires of their own heart,

**23:27** who scheme to forget My law by their dreams, which each one tells to his neighbor, just as their fathers forgot My name for Baal?

**23:28** The prophet in whom there is a dream, let him tell his dream; and he who has My word with him, let him declare My word in truth. What is the chaff to the wheat?

**23:29** So are My words," says the Lord. "Behold, are not My words like fire, and like an axe splitting rock?"

**23:30** "Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets," says the Lord God, "who steal My words, each one from his neighbor.

**23:31** Behold, I am against the prophets who put forth prophecies of the tongue and slumber their own slumber.

**23:32** Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets who prophesy false dreams, and did not tell them, and led My people astray by their lies and by their deceits; yet I did not send them nor command them, and they shall not profit this people at all.

**23:33** And if this people, or a priest, or a prophet, asks you, 'What is the burden of the Lord?' then you shall say to them, 'You are the burden, and I will cast you down,'" says the Lord.

**23:34** "As for the prophet and the priests and the people who say, 'The burden of the Lord,' I will even bring vengeance upon that man and his house.

**23:35** Thus you shall say, each one to his neighbor and each one to his brother, 'What has the Lord answered? And what has the Lord spoken?'

**23:36** And 'The burden of the Lord' you shall mention no more, for his own word shall be a man's burden."

**23:37** And why has the Lord our God spoken?

**23:38** Therefore thus says the Lord our God: "Because you have spoken this word, 'The burden of the Lord,' when I sent to you, saying, 'You shall not say, The burden of the Lord'—

**23:39** therefore, behold, I am taking up and casting down both you and the city which I gave to you and to your fathers."

## Translator Notes

- **23:6** — Greek reads Κύριος Ἰωσέδεκ ("which the Lord shall call Him: Josedek"), transliterating the Messianic title rather than translating it, and closes the verse with the heading "Concerning the prophets." Also in 23:5, Greek ἀνατολή ("rising" or "dayspring") corresponds to the Hebrew title often rendered "Branch."
- **23:8** — In this Septuagint numbering, verse 23:7 is omitted and verse 23:8 combines the conclusion of the oracle against the prophets (MT 23:40) with the new-exodus promise (MT 23:7–8).
- **23:10** — The Greek text reads ὁ δρυμὸς αὐτῶν ("their forest" or "thicket"), likely an ancient textual variant for δρόμος ("course").
- **23:31** — The Greek reads "slumber their own slumber" (νυστάζοντας νυσταγμὸν αὐτῶν), reading the Hebrew consonants as a root for slumber rather than the Masoretic "utter an oracle."
- **23:33** — Greek λῆμμα ("burden" or "oracle") creates a wordplay with λαμβάνω ("take up") and ῥάσσω ("cast down") in verses 33 and 39.
