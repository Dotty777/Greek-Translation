## Text

**21:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur the son of Malchiah and Zephaniah the son of Manassaeus, the priest, saying,

**21:2** "Inquire of the Lord on our behalf, for the king of Babylon has come against us, whether the Lord will act according to all His wondrous works, and he will depart from us."

**21:3** Then Jeremiah said to them, "Thus you shall say to Zedekiah king of Judah:

**21:4** 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am turning back the weapons of war with which you fight against the Chaldeans, who have shut you in outside the wall, into the midst of this city;

**21:5** and I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a mighty arm, with fury and great wrath.

**21:6** And I will strike all who dwell in this city, both people and beasts, with a great death, and they shall die.

**21:7** And afterward, thus says the Lord: I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, and his servants, and the people who are left in this city from death, and from famine, and from the sword, into the hands of their enemies who seek their lives; and they shall cut them down with the edge of the sword. I will not spare them, nor will I have compassion on them.'"

**21:8** And to this people you shall say, "Thus says the Lord: Behold, I have set before your face the way of life and the way of death.

**21:9** He who abides in this city shall die by the sword and by famine; but he who goes out to surrender to the Chaldeans who have shut you in shall live, and his life shall be as spoil, and he shall live.

**21:10** For I have set My face against this city for evil and not for good; it shall be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire."

**21:11** O house of the king of Judah, hear the word of the Lord:

**21:12** O house of David, thus says the Lord: "Execute judgment in the morning and make it right, and deliver the plundered from the hand of him who wrongs him, lest My wrath be kindled like fire and burn, and there be none to quench it.

**21:13** Behold, I am against you who dwell in the valley of Sor, the plain, who say, 'Who will terrify us? Or who will enter the dwelling place?'

**21:14** And I will kindle a fire in its forest, and it shall devour all things round about it."

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — The Greek reads "Manassaeus" (Manassaiou), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Maaseiah."
- **21:6** — Greek thanatos ("death") regularly renders Hebrew deber ("pestilence") in Septuagint Jeremiah, both here and in 21:7.
- **21:7** — The Greek omits the specific mention of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and places the final verbs in the first person ("I will not spare... nor will I have compassion") rather than the third person as in the Hebrew.
- **21:13** — The Greek treats Hebrew tsur ("rock") as the proper name "Sor" and reads the following verb as "terrify" rather than "come down."
