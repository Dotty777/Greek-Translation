## Text

**28:1** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, I am raising up against Babylon and against the Chaldean inhabitants a scorching, destroying wind.

**28:2** And I will send forth into Babylon insolent men, and they shall deal insolently with her and ravage her land; woe upon Babylon on every side in the day of her affliction!

**28:3** Let him who bends his bow bend it, and let him who has his armor put it on; and do not spare her young men, and utterly destroy all her host.

**28:4** And the wounded shall fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and those pierced through outside of her.

**28:5** For Israel and Judah have not been widowed from their God, from the Lord Almighty, because their land has been filled with unrighteousness from the holy ones of Israel.

**28:6** Flee from the midst of Babylon, and save each one his own life, and do not be cast away in her iniquity; for it is the time of her vengeance from the Lord; He Himself is rendering recompense to her.

**28:7** Babylon was a golden cup in the hand of the Lord, making all the earth drunk; the nations drank of her wine, therefore they were shaken.

**28:8** And suddenly Babylon has fallen and been shattered. Lament over her; take balm for her ruin, if perhaps she may be healed."

**28:9** "We treated Babylon, and she was not healed; let us forsake her, and let us depart, each one to his own land; for her judgment has reached to heaven; it has risen even to the stars."

**28:10** "The Lord has brought forth His judgment; come, and let us declare in Zion the works of the Lord our God."

**28:11** Prepare the arrows, fill the quivers! The Lord has stirred up the spirit of the king of the Medes, for His wrath is against Babylon, to utterly destroy her; for it is the vengeance of the Lord, the vengeance of His people.

**28:12** Lift up a banner upon the walls of Babylon, set up the quivers, rouse the watches, make ready the weapons; for He has taken it in hand, and the Lord will perform what He has spoken against the inhabitants of Babylon,

**28:13** who dwell upon many waters and upon the abundance of her treasures. Your end has truly come into your inward parts.

**28:14** For the Lord has sworn by His arm: "Surely I will fill you with men as with locusts, and those who descend shall shout against you."

**28:15** He who makes the earth by His strength, who prepares the inhabited world by His wisdom, by His understanding stretched out the heaven;

**28:16** at a voice He placed a roar of water in the heaven, and brought up clouds from the end of the earth; He made lightnings for the rain, and brought forth light out of His treasuries.

**28:17** Every man has become foolish without knowledge; every goldsmith has been put to shame by his carved images, for they have cast false things; there is no breath in them.

**28:18** They are futile things, works of mockery; in the time of their visitation they shall perish.

**28:19** Such is not the portion of Jacob, for He who formed all things, He Himself is his inheritance; the Lord is His name.

**28:20** "You scatter for Me weapons of war, and with you I will scatter nations, and from you I will remove kings.

**28:21** And with you I will scatter the horse and its rider,

**28:22** and with you I will scatter chariots and their riders; and with you I will scatter young man and virgin, and with you I will scatter man and woman.

**28:23** And with you I will scatter the shepherd and his flock, and with you I will scatter the farmer and his tilled field, and with you I will scatter your governors and commanders.

**28:24** And I will repay Babylon and all the Chaldean inhabitants for all their evils which they committed against Zion before your eyes," says the Lord.

**28:25** "Behold, I am against you, O corrupted mountain that destroys all the earth; and I will stretch out My hand against you, and roll you down upon the rocks, and make you like a burned mountain.

**28:26** And they shall by no means take from you a stone for a corner, nor a stone for a foundation; for you shall be an utter desolation forever," says the Lord.

**28:27** "Lift up a banner upon the earth, sound the trumpet among the nations! Consecrate nations against her; give orders against her—raise up kings from Me—and to the Ashkenazites; set up siege engines against her; bring up horses against her like a multitude of locusts.

**28:28** Bring up nations against her, the king of the Medes and of all the earth, his governors and all his commanders."

**28:29** The earth shook and writhed in pain, because the purpose of the Lord has risen up against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon an utter desolation, so that it shall not be inhabited.

**28:30** The warrior of Babylon has ceased from fighting; they shall sit there under siege; their might has been broken, they have become like women; her dwellings have been set on fire, her bars have been shattered.

**28:31** Runner shall run to meet runner, and messenger to meet messenger, to report to the king of Babylon that his city has been captured,

**28:32** from the end of his passages they have been seized, and they have burned their reservoirs with fire, and his men of war are going forth.

**28:33** For thus says the Lord: "The houses of the king of Babylon shall be threshed like a threshing floor in season; yet a little while, and her harvest shall come."

**28:34** "He has devoured me, he has divided me, a thin darkness has overtaken me; Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has swallowed me up; like a dragon he has filled his belly from my delicacies.

**28:35** My toils and my miseries have thrust me out into Babylon," the inhabitant of Zion shall say; "and my blood be upon the Chaldean inhabitants," Jerusalem shall say.

**28:36** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I will judge your adversary and execute your vengeance; and I will lay waste her sea and dry up her spring.

**28:37** And Babylon shall become an utter desolation, and shall not be inhabited.

**28:38** For together like lions they have risen up, and like lions' cubs.

**28:39** In their heat I will give them a drink, and I will make them drunk, so that they may become stupefied and sleep a perpetual sleep, and by no means awake," says the Lord.

**28:40** And bring them down like lambs to the slaughter, and like rams with young goats.

**28:41** How has the boast of all the earth been captured and hunted down? How has Babylon become an utter desolation among the nations?

**28:42** The sea has come up over Babylon with the roar of its waves, and she has been covered.

**28:43** Her cities have become like a waterless and untrodden land; not even one shall dwell in it, nor shall any son of man lodge in it.

**28:44** "And I will execute vengeance upon Babylon, and I will bring out of her mouth what she has swallowed; and the nations shall by no means be gathered together to her anymore,

**28:49** and in Babylon the wounded of all the earth shall fall."

**28:50** You who escape from the land, go and do not stand still! You who are from afar, remember the Lord, and let Jerusalem come into your heart.

**28:51** "We have been put to shame, because we have heard our reproach; dishonor has covered our face; foreigners have entered into our sanctuaries, into the house of the Lord."

**28:52** "Therefore, behold, the days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will execute vengeance upon her carved images, and in all her land the wounded shall fall.

**28:53** For though Babylon should ascend like heaven, and though she should fortify the walls with her strength, from Me those who utterly destroy her shall come," says the Lord.

**28:54** The sound of a cry is in Babylon, and great destruction in the land of the Chaldeans,

**28:55** because the Lord has utterly destroyed Babylon, and has destroyed from her a great voice roaring like many waters; He has given her voice over to destruction.

**28:56** For misery has come upon Babylon; her warriors have been captured, their bow is dismayed, for God is repaying them.

**28:57** The Lord is repaying, and He will make her governors, and her wise men, and her commanders drunk with intoxication, says the King; the Lord Almighty is His name.

**28:58** Thus says the Lord: "The wall of Babylon was made broad; it shall be utterly razed, and her lofty gates shall be set on fire; and peoples shall not labor in vain, and nations shall fail in the beginning."

**28:59** The word which the Lord commanded Jeremiah the prophet to speak to Seraiah the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, when he went from Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. Now Seraiah was chief of the gifts.

**28:60** And Jeremiah wrote in a book all the evils that should come upon Babylon, all these words that have been written against Babylon.

**28:61** And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you come to Babylon, then you shall see and read all these words,

**28:62** and you shall say, 'O Lord, Lord, You have spoken against this place to utterly destroy it, and that there should be no inhabitants in it, from man to beast, for it shall be an utter desolation forever.'

**28:63** And it shall be, when you have finished reading this book, that you shall tie a stone to it and cast it into the midst of the Euphrates,

**28:64** and you shall say, 'Thus shall Babylon sink, and shall by no means rise again from the face of the Chaldeans whom I am bringing upon her.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Greek reads ὑβριστάς ("insolent men") and καθυβρίσουσιν ("shall deal insolently with her"), preserving the Septuagint cognate wordplay where the Masoretic Text reads "winnowers" who winnow her.
- **28:5** — Greek reads the plural τῶν ἁγίων Ἰσραήλ (which may mean "the holy ones of Israel" or "the holy things of Israel"), in contrast to the Masoretic Text's singular "the Holy One of Israel."
- **28:16** — Greek reads φῶς ("light") rather than "wind" (as in the Masoretic Text), and renders the opening clause εἰς φωνὴν ἔθετο ἦχος ὕδατος ("at a voice He placed a roar of water").
- **28:20** — Greek renders the opening clause verbally as διασκορπίζεις σύ μοι σκεύη πολέμου ("You scatter for Me weapons of war"), rather than the Masoretic Text's "You are My war-club and weapons of war."
- **28:27** — Where the Masoretic Text lists the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz, the Septuagint reads βασιλεῖς ἄρατε παρ᾽ ἐμοῦ, καὶ τοῖς Ἀσχαναζέοις ("raise up kings from Me, and to the Ashkenazites"), and renders the following term as βελοστάσεις ("siege engines").
- **28:34** — Greek reads κατέλαβέν με σκότος λεπτόν ("a thin darkness has overtaken me"), a distinctive Septuagint reading (widely regarded as an early Greek textual shift from σκεῦος κενόν, "empty vessel") where the Masoretic Text has "he has set me down as an empty vessel."
- **28:44** — Verses 45–48 of the Masoretic Text (Jeremiah 51:45–48) are absent in the Septuagint, so 28:49 follows directly after 28:44 and continues the sentence.
- **28:58** — Greek includes the negation οὐ κοπιάσουσιν ("shall not labor") and reads ἐν ἀρχῇ ("in the beginning"), whereas the Masoretic Text lacks the negation and reads "for fire."
- **28:59** — Greek calls Seraiah ἄρχων δώρων ("chief of the gifts"), reflecting a reading of Hebrew minchah ("gift/tribute") rather than menuchah ("quartermaster"), and states that he went "from Zedekiah" rather than "with Zedekiah."
- **28:64** — Greek reads ἀπὸ προσώπου τῶν Χαλδαίων ("from the face of the Chaldeans") rather than "because of the calamity/evil" (as in the Masoretic Text and some Greek manuscripts), and concludes the chapter without the closing editorial line of Masoretic Jeremiah 51:64.
