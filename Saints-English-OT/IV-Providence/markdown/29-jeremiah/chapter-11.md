## Text

**11:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, saying:

**11:2** "Hear the words of this covenant, and you shall speak to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,

**11:3** and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Cursed is the man who does not listen to the words of this covenant,

**11:4** which I commanded your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, from the iron furnace, saying: Listen to My voice, and do all that I command you; and you shall be My people, and I will be your God,

**11:5** that I may establish My oath which I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day.'" And I answered and said, "So be it, O Lord."

**11:6** And the Lord said to me, "Read aloud these words in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem, saying, 'Hear the words of this covenant and do them.'"

**11:8** And they did not do them.

**11:9** And the Lord said to me, "A conspiracy has been found among the men of Judah and among those who dwell in Jerusalem.

**11:10** They have turned back to the iniquities of their former fathers, who refused to listen to My words; and behold, they themselves walk after foreign gods to serve them. Both the house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken My covenant which I made with their fathers."

**11:11** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I am bringing upon this people calamities from which they shall not be able to come forth; and they shall cry out to Me, and I will not listen to them.

**11:12** Then the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem shall go and cry out to the gods to whom they burn incense, yet they shall by no means save them in the time of their calamities.

**11:13** For according to the number of your cities were your gods, O Judah; and according to the number of the outlets of Jerusalem you have set up altars to burn incense to Baal.

**11:14** And as for you, do not pray for this people, and do not intercede for them with supplication and prayer; for I will not listen in the time when they call upon Me, in the time of their affliction.

**11:15** Why has the beloved one committed an abomination in My house? Shall vows and holy flesh take away your wickednesses from you, or by these shall you escape?

**11:16** A fair olive tree, well-shaded in appearance, the Lord called your name. At the sound of her pruning, fire was kindled against her; great is the affliction upon you; her branches have been made useless,

**11:17** and the Lord who planted you has spoken calamity against you because of the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Judah, for they have done this for themselves, to provoke Me to anger by burning incense to Baal."

**11:18** O Lord, make it known to me, and I shall know; then I saw their practices,

**11:19** but I, like an innocent lamb led to be sacrificed, did not know. Against me they devised an evil scheme, saying, "Come, and let us cast wood into his bread, and let us cut him off from the land of the living, and his name shall no longer be remembered."

**11:20** O Lord, who judges righteously, who tests the inward parts and hearts, let me see Your vengeance upon them, for to You I have revealed my just cause.

**11:21** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the men of Anathoth who seek my life, who say, "You shall by no means prophesy in the name of the Lord, or else you shall die by our hands":

**11:22** "Behold, I will visit punishment upon them: their young men shall die by the sword, and their sons and their daughters shall perish by famine,

**11:23** and there shall be no remnant of them; for I will bring calamities upon those who dwell in Anathoth in the year of their visitation."

## Translator Notes

- **11:8** — Verse 7 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text is absent in the Septuagint, which moves directly from verse 6 to the brief statement of verse 8.
- **11:13** — The Greek places the feminine article tē before "Baal" (as also in 11:17), reflecting the traditional substitution of the feminine Hebrew noun bosheth ("shame") for the name of Baal.
- **11:16** — Greek peritomēs ("pruning" or "circumcision") renders the olive-tree imagery differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("with the roar of a great tumult").
- **11:19** — Greek embalōmen xylon eis ton arton autou ("let us cast wood into his bread") reflects reading the Hebrew noun as "his bread" rather than "its fruit," a rendering often given Christological significance in early Christian interpretation.
