## Text

**6:1** Strengthen yourselves, O sons of Benjamin, out of the midst of Jerusalem, and sound the trumpet in Tekoa, and lift up a signal over Beth-haccerem; for calamity peers forth from the north, and great destruction is coming,

**6:2** and your height shall be taken away, O daughter of Zion.

**6:3** Shepherds and their flocks shall come to her; they shall pitch tents against her all around, and each one shall pasture with his own hand.

**6:4** "Prepare yourselves against her for battle! Arise, and let us go up against her at noon! Woe to us, for the day has declined, for the shadows of the day are fading!

**6:5** Arise, and let us go up against her by night, and let us destroy her foundations!"

**6:6** For thus says the Lord: "Cut down her trees, pour out an army against Jerusalem! O false city, all oppression is within her.

**6:7** As a cistern keeps water cool, so her wickedness keeps cool; ungodliness and misery shall be heard in her, before her face continually.

**6:8** With pain and scourge you shall be disciplined, O Jerusalem, lest My soul depart from you, lest I make you an untrodden land that shall not be inhabited."

**6:9** For thus says the Lord: "Glean, glean like a vine the remnant of Israel; turn back like the grape-gatherer to his basket."

**6:10** To whom shall I speak and bear witness, that he may hear? Behold, their ears are uncircumcised, and they shall not be able to hear. Behold, the word of the Lord has become a reproach to them; they will by no means desire it.

**6:11** "And I have filled My wrath and held it back, and I have not brought them to an end; I will pour it out upon the infants outside, and upon the assembly of young men together; for husband and wife shall be captured, the elder together with the one full of days.

**6:12** And their houses shall be turned over to others, their fields and their wives together; for I will stretch out My hand against those who dwell in this land," says the Lord.

**6:13** "For from the least of them even to the greatest, all have practiced lawless deeds; from priest even to false prophet, all have done falsehood.

**6:14** And they healed the crushing of My people, treating it lightly and saying, 'Peace, peace!'—and where is peace?

**6:15** They were put to shame because they failed; yet not even as those who are ashamed were they put to shame, and they did not know their dishonor. Therefore they shall fall in their downfall, and in the time of visitation they shall perish," the Lord has said.

**6:16** Thus says the Lord: "Stand upon the ways and see, and ask for the everlasting paths of the Lord, and see which is the good way, and walk in it, and you shall find purification for your souls." But they said, "We will not go."

**6:17** "I have appointed watchmen over you; listen to the sound of the trumpet!" But they said, "We will not listen."

**6:18** Therefore the nations have heard, and those who pasture their flocks.

**6:19** "Hear, O earth! Behold, I am bringing calamity upon this people, the fruit of their turning away, because they did not attend to My words, and they cast away My law.

**6:20** Why do you bring Me frankincense from Sheba, and cinnamon from a distant land? Your whole burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices have not been pleasing to Me."

**6:21** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Behold, I am placing weakness upon this people, and fathers and sons shall grow weak together; neighbor and his companion shall perish."

**6:22** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, a people comes from the north, and nations shall be stirred up from the end of the earth;

**6:23** they shall lay hold of bow and spear; he is fierce and shall have no mercy; his voice is like a surging sea; upon horses and chariots he shall draw up in array like fire for battle against you, O daughter of Zion."

**6:24** We have heard the report of them; our hands have grown feeble; distress has laid hold of us, pangs like those of a woman in labor.

**6:25** Do not go out into the field, and do not walk in the roads, for the sword of the enemies dwells all around.

**6:26** O daughter of my people, gird yourself with sackcloth, sprinkle yourself with ashes; make for yourself mourning as for a beloved one, a piteous lamentation, for suddenly misery shall come upon you.

**6:27** I have appointed you as a tester among tested peoples, and you shall know Me when I test their way.

**6:28** All of them are disobedient, walking crookedly; bronze and iron, all of them are corrupted.

**6:29** The bellows have failed from the fire; the lead has failed; in vain the silversmith refines silver; their wickedness has not melted away.

**6:30** Call them rejected silver, because the Lord has rejected them.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Greek reads "and your height shall be taken away, O daughter of Zion," diverging markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("I have likened the daughter of Zion to a lovely and delicate woman").
- **6:11** — Greek places this verse in the Lord's first-person voice ("I have filled My wrath... I will pour it out"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks in the prophet's voice ("I am full of the wrath of the LORD") followed by a command ("Pour it out").
- **6:16** — Greek specifies "the everlasting paths of the Lord" and promises "purification for your souls," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the ancient paths" and "rest for your souls."
- **6:23** — Greek adds "and chariots" and reads "like fire" for the advancing enemy, reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants rendered "like a man" in the Masoretic Text.
