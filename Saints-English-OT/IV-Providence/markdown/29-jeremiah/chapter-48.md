## Text

**48:1** Now it came to pass in the seventh month that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elasa, of the lineage of the king, and ten men with him, came to Gedaliah at Mizpah; and they ate bread together there.

**48:2** Then Ishmael arose, and the ten men who were with him, and struck down Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land,

**48:3** and all the Jews who were with him in Mizpah, and all the Chaldeans who were found there.

**48:4** And it came to pass on the second day after he had struck down Gedaliah, when no one knew it,

**48:5** that men came from Shechem, from Salem, and from Samaria—eighty men, with their beards shaved, their garments torn, and beating their breasts, with manna and frankincense in their hands to bring into the house of the Lord.

**48:6** And Ishmael went out to meet them—they were walking along and weeping—and he said to them, "Come in to Gedaliah."

**48:7** And it came to pass, when they came into the midst of the city, that he slaughtered them into the cistern.

**48:8** But ten men were found there, and they said to Ishmael, "Do not slay us, for we have treasures in the field—wheat and barley, honey and oil." So he passed by and did not slay them among their brethren.

**48:9** Now the cistern into which Ishmael cast all those whom he had struck down—this is a great cistern which King Asa had made because of Baasha king of Israel; this Ishmael filled with the slain.

**48:10** Then Ishmael turned back all the people who remained in Mizpah, and the daughters of the king, whom the captain of the guard had entrusted to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, and he departed to the region beyond the sons of Ammon.

**48:11** Now Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the forces who were with him, heard of all the evil that Ishmael had done.

**48:12** And they led out all their army and went to fight against him, and they found him by the great waters in Gibeon.

**48:13** And it came to pass, when all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan and the commanders of the force that was with him,

**48:14** that they turned back to Johanan.

**48:15** And Ishmael escaped with eight men, and departed to the sons of Ammon.

**48:16** Then Johanan and all the commanders of the forces who were with him took all the remnant of the people whom he had brought back from Ishmael, mighty men of war, and the women, and the rest, and the eunuchs whom he had brought back from Gibeon,

**48:17** and they departed and stayed in Gaberochamaa, which is near Bethlehem, in order to go into Egypt

**48:18** from before the Chaldeans, for they were afraid of them, because Ishmael had struck down Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had appointed in the land.

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — Greek reads "Elasa," whereas the Masoretic Text (Jeremiah 41:1) reads "Elishama."
- **48:5** — Greek reads "Salem" (for Masoretic "Shiloh") and "manna" (a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew term for a grain offering), and describes the men as "beating their breasts" rather than gashing themselves.
- **48:6** — Greek states that the pilgrims ("they") were walking along and weeping, whereas the Masoretic Text describes Ishmael as weeping as he went out.
- **48:17** — Greek reads the place-name "Gaberochamaa," corresponding to "Geruth Chimham" in the Masoretic Text (Jeremiah 41:17).
