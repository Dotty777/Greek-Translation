## Text

**32:15** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: "Take this cup of unmixed wine from My hand, and you shall make all the nations drink, to which I send you;

**32:16** and they shall vomit, and be driven mad from the face of the sword which I send among them."

**32:17** So I took the cup from the hand of the Lord, and made the nations drink, to which the Lord sent me:

**32:18** Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, and the kings of Judah and its rulers, to make them a desolation, and an untrodden waste, and a hissing;

**32:19** and Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, and his nobles,

**32:20** and all his people, and all the mixed peoples, and all the kings of the Philistines—Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;

**32:21** and Edom, and the land of Moab, and the sons of Ammon;

**32:22** and the kings of Tyre, and the kings of Sidon, and the kings who are across the sea;

**32:23** and Dedan, and Tema, and Rhos, and every one who is clipped around his face;

**32:24** and all the mixed peoples who lodge in the wilderness;

**32:25** and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Persians;

**32:26** and all the kings from the east, those far off and those near, each one with his brother, and all the kingdoms that are upon the face of the earth.

**32:27** And you shall say to them, "Thus says the Lord Almighty: 'Drink, be drunk, and you shall vomit and fall, and by no means rise from the face of the sword which I send among you.'"

**32:28** And it shall be, when they are unwilling to receive the cup from your hand to drink, that you shall say, "Thus says the Lord: 'You shall surely drink!

**32:29** For in the city upon which My name has been called I begin to bring calamity, and you shall by no means be acquitted; for I summon a sword upon those who dwell upon the earth.'"

**32:30** And you shall prophesy against them these words, and you shall say: "The Lord shall utter an oracle from on high; from His holy place He shall give forth His voice; He shall proclaim a word over His place, and these shall answer like grape-gatherers. And upon those who dwell on the earth destruction comes,

**32:31** to a part of the earth, for the Lord has a judgment among the nations; He Himself enters into judgment with all flesh, and the ungodly have been given over to the sword, says the Lord."

**32:32** Thus says the Lord: "Behold, evils are coming from nation to nation, and a great tempest goes forth from the end of the earth;

**32:33** and those slain by the Lord in the day of the Lord shall be from one part of the earth even to another part of the earth; they shall by no means be buried; they shall be as dung upon the face of the earth.

**32:34** Wail, you shepherds, and cry out, and beat yourselves in mourning, you rams of the sheep! For your days for slaughter have been fulfilled, and you shall fall like the chosen rams;

**32:35** and flight shall perish from the shepherds, and deliverance from the rams of the sheep.

**32:36** A voice of the outcry of the shepherds, and a wailing of the sheep and of the rams! For the Lord has destroyed their pastures,

**32:37** and the remnants of peace shall cease from the face of the wrath of My anger.

**32:38** He has forsaken His lair like a lion, for their land has become an untrodden waste from the face of the great sword."

## Translator Notes

- **32:15** — In the Septuagint order, chapter 32 begins at verse 15 (parallel to Masoretic Jeremiah 25:15-38); Greek reads "unmixed wine" rather than "wine of wrath."
- **32:23** — Greek reads the distinct proper name Rhos where the Masoretic Text has Buz.
- **32:26** — Greek reads "from the east" rather than "of the north," and in verse 25 reads "the Persians" rather than "the Medes."
- **32:30** — Greek reads the demonstrative "these" (hoide) where the underlying Hebrew has hedad, the shout of grape-treaders; compare 31:33.
- **32:34** — Greek reads "like the chosen rams" rather than the Masoretic "like a precious vessel."
