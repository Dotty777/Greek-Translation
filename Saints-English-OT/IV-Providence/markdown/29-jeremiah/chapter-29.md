## Text

**29:1** Against the Philistines. Thus says the Lord:

**29:2** "Behold, waters rise up from the north and shall become an overflowing torrent; it shall flood the land and its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it. Men shall cry out, and all who inhabit the land shall wail,

**29:3** at the sound of his rushing charge, at the hooves of his feet, and at the shaking of his chariots, the roaring of his wheels. Fathers have not turned back for their sons because of the feebleness of their hands,

**29:4** in the day that is coming to destroy all the Philistines. And I will wipe out Tyre and Sidon and all the remnant of their help, for the Lord will utterly destroy the remnant of the islands.

**29:5** Baldness has come upon Gaza; Ashkelon has been cast away, and the remnant of the Anakim."

**29:6** How long will you strike, O sword of the Lord? How long will you not be quiet? Return into your scabbard; rest, and be lifted up.

**29:7** How shall it be quiet, when the Lord has commanded it to rise up against Ashkelon and against the coastal regions, against those that remain?

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek allophyloi literally means "foreigners" and is the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines.
- **29:4** — The Septuagint reads first-person "I will wipe out" and "the remnant of the islands," whereas the Masoretic Text has "to cut off from Tyre and Sidon" and "the coastland of Caphtor."
- **29:5** — The Septuagint reads "the Anakim" (Enakeim) where the Masoretic Text has "their valley," and in 29:6 uses epartheti ("be lifted up" or "be removed") where the Hebrew has "be still."
