## Text

**4:1** "If Israel returns," says the Lord, "to Me he shall return; if he removes his abominations from his mouth, and stands in awe before the presence,

**4:2** and swears, 'The Lord lives,' with truth, in judgment, and in righteousness, then nations shall bless in him, and in him they shall praise God in Jerusalem."

**4:3** For thus says the Lord to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem: "Break up for yourselves fallow ground, and do not sow upon thorns.

**4:4** Be circumcised to your God, and circumcise your hardness of heart, O men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, lest His wrath go forth like fire and burn, and there be none to quench it, because of the evil of your practices."

**4:5** Declare in Judah, and let it be heard in Jerusalem; speak, sound the trumpet in the land, cry aloud; say, "Assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the fortified cities."

**4:6** Gather up and flee to Zion; make haste, do not stand still, for I am bringing calamity from the north, and a great destruction.

**4:7** A lion has come up from his lair, and the destroyer of nations has set out and gone forth from his place to make the land a desolation; and cities shall be demolished so that they are no longer inhabited.

**4:8** For this, gird yourselves with sackcloth, lament and wail, for the wrath of the Lord has not turned away from you.

**4:9** "And it shall come to pass in that day," says the Lord, "that the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the rulers; and the priests shall be astonished, and the prophets shall marvel."

**4:10** And I said, "O Sovereign Lord, have You indeed utterly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'There shall be peace,' while behold, the sword has reached even to their soul?"

**4:11** At that time they shall say to this people and to Jerusalem, "A spirit of wandering is in the wilderness; the way of the daughter of My people is not toward what is pure, nor toward what is holy.

**4:12** A spirit of fullness shall come to Me; and now I speak judgments against them."

**4:13** Behold, he shall come up like a cloud, and his chariots like a whirlwind; his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are devastated!

**4:14** Wash your heart from wickedness, O Jerusalem, that you may be saved. How long shall the thoughts of your distress remain within you?

**4:15** For a voice of one announcing shall come from Dan, and distress shall be heard from Mount Ephraim.

**4:16** Remind the nations: behold, they have come; proclaim in Jerusalem, "Bands are coming from a distant land, and they have raised their voice against the cities of Judah."

**4:17** "Like watchmen of a field they are all around against her, because you have neglected Me," says the Lord.

**4:18** Your ways and your practices have done these things to you; this is your wickedness, for it is bitter, for it has reached even to your heart.

**4:19** I am in pain in my inward parts, and in the senses of my heart; my soul throbs violently, my heart is convulsed; I will not be silent, for my soul has heard the sound of the trumpet, the cry of war.

**4:20** And it calls upon devastation, upon crushing, for the whole land is devastated; suddenly the tent is devastated, my tent-curtains are torn apart.

**4:21** How long shall I see fugitives, hearing the sound of trumpets?

**4:22** "For the leaders of My people have not known Me; they are foolish sons and without understanding; they are wise to do evil, but to do good they have not known."

**4:23** I looked upon the earth, and behold, nothing; and to heaven, and its lights were not there.

**4:24** I saw the mountains, and they were trembling, and all the hills were shaken in turmoil.

**4:25** I looked, and behold, there was no man, and all the birds of heaven were terrified.

**4:26** I saw, and behold, Carmel was desolate, and all the cities were burned with fire before the presence of the Lord, and before the presence of the anger of His wrath they were blotted out.

**4:27** Thus says the Lord: "All the earth shall be desolate, yet I will by no means make a full end.

**4:28** For this let the earth mourn, and let the heaven above be darkened, because I have spoken and I will not relent; I have set forth and I will not turn back from it."

**4:29** At the sound of the horseman and the bent bow every region has withdrawn; they crept into the caves, and hid themselves in the groves, and climbed up upon the rocks; every city was forsaken; no man dwelt in them.

**4:30** And what will you do if you clothe yourself with scarlet and adorn yourself with ornaments of gold, if you paint your eyes with antimony? In vain is your beautifying; your lovers have rejected you; they seek your life.

**4:31** For I have heard a voice as of a woman in labor, of your groaning, as of one bearing her first child: the voice of the daughter of Zion; she shall grow faint, and let her hands drop: "Woe is me, for my soul faints because of the slain!"

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Greek reads "from his mouth," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from My presence."
- **4:11** — The Greek reads "A spirit of wandering is in the wilderness" and "not toward what is pure, nor toward what is holy," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's scorching wind not meant to winnow or cleanse.
- **4:23** — The Greek summarizes the desolation of the earth concisely as "nothing," rendering the Hebrew phrase "without form and void."
- **4:26** — The Greek takes the Hebrew term as the proper place name "Carmel" rather than "the fruitful land," and describes the cities as "burned with fire" rather than broken down.
