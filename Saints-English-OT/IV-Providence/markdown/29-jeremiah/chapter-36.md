## Text

**36:1** Now these are the words of the book which Jeremiah sent from Jerusalem to the elders of the exile, and to the priests, and to the false prophets—a letter to Babylon for the exiles—and to all the people,

**36:2** after King Jeconiah, and the queen, and the eunuchs, and every free man, and prisoner, and craftsman had departed from Jerusalem,

**36:3** by the hand of Elasah the son of Shaphan and Gemariah the son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to the king of Babylon in Babylon, saying:

**36:4** "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, concerning the exiles whom I carried away from Jerusalem:

**36:5** Build houses and dwell in them, and plant orchards and eat their fruits,

**36:6** and take wives and bring forth sons and daughters, and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, and multiply, and do not be diminished,

**36:7** and seek the peace of the land into which I carried you away, and pray concerning them to the Lord; for in its peace is your peace.

**36:8** For thus says the Lord: Do not let the false prophets who are among you mislead you, and do not let your diviners mislead you, and do not listen to your dreams which you yourselves dream;

**36:9** for they prophesy unrighteous things to you in My name, and I did not send them.

**36:10** For thus says the Lord: When seventy years are about to be fulfilled for Babylon, I will visit you and establish My words upon you, to bring back your people to this place,

**36:11** and I will devise for you a purpose of peace and not evil things, to give these things to you.

**36:12** And pray to Me, and I will hear you;

**36:13** and seek Me earnestly, and you shall find Me; for you shall seek Me with all your heart,

**36:14** and I will appear to you.

**36:15** Because you have said, 'The Lord has appointed prophets for us in Babylon,'

**36:21** thus says the Lord concerning Ahab and concerning Zedekiah: Behold, I am giving them into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall strike them down before your eyes.

**36:22** And they shall take up a curse from them among all the exiles of Judah in Babylon, saying, 'May the Lord do to you as He did to Zedekiah and as He did to Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire,'

**36:23** because of the lawlessness which they committed in Israel, and they committed adultery with the wives of their fellow citizens, and proclaimed a word in My name which I did not command them; and I am a witness, declares the Lord."

**36:24** And to Shemaiah the Elamite you shall say,

**36:25** "I did not send you in My name." And to Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest say,

**36:26** "The Lord has appointed you as priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to be overseer in the house of the Lord over every man who prophesies and every man who is mad, and you shall put him into confinement and into the dungeon.

**36:27** And now why have you reviled Jeremiah of Anathoth, who prophesied to you?

**36:28** Did he not on this account send to you in Babylon, saying, 'It is far off; build houses and dwell in them, and plant gardens and you shall eat their fruit'?"

**36:29** And Zephaniah read the scroll in the ears of Jeremiah.

**36:30** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,

**36:31** "Send to the exiles, saying, 'Thus says the Lord concerning Shemaiah the Elamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you, and I did not send him, and he has caused you to trust in unrighteous things,

**36:32** therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I will punish Shemaiah and his offspring, and there shall not be a man of theirs in your midst to see the good things which I will do for you; they shall not see them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — The Greek text specifies "false prophets" (ψευδοπροφήτας) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (29:1) has "prophets," and places "a letter to Babylon for the exiles" in apposition to "the words of the book."
- **36:11** — The Greek text is significantly briefer than the Hebrew (29:11), reading "a purpose of peace and not evil things, to give these things to you" without "a future and a hope"; verses 16-20 of the Hebrew are also absent from the Septuagint.
- **36:24** — The Greek text identifies Shemaiah as "the Elamite" (τὸν Αἰλαμείτην) where the Hebrew (29:24) reads "the Nehelamite"; the same proper name recurs in verse 31.
- **36:25** — The Greek reads "I did not send you in My name. And to Zephaniah... say," diverging sharply from the Hebrew (29:25), which accuses Shemaiah of sending letters in his own name; likewise, verse 27 omits the negative ("why have you reviled" rather than "why have you not rebuked").
