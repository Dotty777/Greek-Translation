## Text

**38:1** "At that time," says the Lord, "I will be God to the family of Israel, and they shall be My people."

**38:2** Thus says the Lord: "I found warmth in the wilderness with those who perished by the sword; go, and do not destroy Israel."

**38:3** The Lord appeared to him from afar: "With an everlasting love I have loved you; therefore I have drawn you into compassion.

**38:4** For I will build you, and you shall be built, O virgin Israel; you shall take up your timbrel and go forth with the assembly of those who make merry.

**38:5** For you have planted vineyards on the mountains of Samaria; plant and give praise,

**38:6** for there is a day of calling by those who make defense on the mountains of Ephraim: 'Arise, and go up to Zion, to the Lord our God.'"

**38:7** For thus says the Lord to Jacob: "Rejoice and neigh over the head of the nations; make it heard and give praise; say, 'The Lord has saved His people, the remnant of Israel.'

**38:8** Behold, I bring them from the north, and I will gather them from the farthest part of the earth at the feast of Passover; and you shall bear a great multitude, and they shall return here.

**38:9** With weeping they went out, and with consolation I will bring them back, causing them to lodge by channels of waters in a straight way, and they shall by no means go astray in it; for I have become a Father to Israel, and Ephraim is My firstborn."

**38:10** Hear the words of the Lord, O nations, and declare them to the islands afar off; say, "He who winnowed Israel will gather him, and will keep him as one who tends his flock."

**38:11** For the Lord has redeemed Jacob; He has delivered him from the hand of those stronger than he.

**38:12** And they shall come and be glad on Mount Zion, and they shall come to the good things of the Lord, to a land of grain and wine and fruits, and of cattle and sheep; and their soul shall be like a fruitful tree, and they shall hunger no more.

**38:13** Then the virgins shall rejoice in the assembly of young men, and the aged men shall rejoice; and I will turn their mourning into joy, and I will make them glad.

**38:14** I will magnify and abundantly satisfy the soul of the priests, the sons of Levi, and My people shall be filled with My good things.

**38:15** Thus says the Lord: "A voice was heard in Ramah, of lamentation and weeping and wailing; Rachel, weeping bitterly, would not cease for her sons, because they are no more."

**38:16** Thus says the Lord: "Let your voice cease from weeping, and your eyes from your tears; for there is a reward for your works, and they shall return from the land of the enemies,

**38:17** permanence for your children."

**38:18** "I have surely heard Ephraim wailing: 'You have disciplined me, and I was disciplined; I, like a calf, was not trained. Turn me back, and I will return, for You are the Lord my God.

**38:19** For after my captivity I repented, and after I came to know, I groaned over the days of shame, and I showed You that I received reproach from my youth.'

**38:20** Ephraim is a beloved son to Me, a delightful child; for since My words are in him, I will surely remember him. Therefore I have hastened over him; I will surely have mercy on him," says the Lord.

**38:21** Set yourself up, O Zion, make retribution, set your heart to the shoulders; return by the way in which you went, O virgin Israel, return to your cities mourning.

**38:22** How long will you turn away, O dishonored daughter? For the Lord has created salvation for a new planting; in salvation men shall walk about.

**38:23** For thus says the Lord: "Once again they shall speak this word in the land of Judah and in its cities, when I bring back its captivity: 'Blessed be the Lord upon His righteous, holy mountain!'

**38:24** And there shall be those who dwell in the cities of Judah and in all its land together with the farmer, and he shall be lifted up with a flock.

**38:25** For I have abundantly satisfied every thirsty soul, and every hungry soul I have filled."

**38:26** Because of this I awoke and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me.

**38:27** "Therefore, behold, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will sow Israel and Judah with the seed of man and the seed of beast.

**38:28** And it shall come to pass that just as I watched over them to tear down and to afflict, so I will watch over them to build and to plant," says the Lord.

**38:29** In those days they shall by no means say, "The fathers ate unripe grapes, and the teeth of the children were set on edge";

**38:30** rather, each one shall die in his own sin, and the one who eats the unripe grapes—his teeth shall be set on edge.

**38:31** "Behold, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,

**38:32** not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took hold of their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt, because they did not remain in My covenant, and I disregarded them," says the Lord.

**38:33** "For this is My covenant that I will make with the house of Israel: After those days," says the Lord, "I will surely put My laws into their mind, and upon their hearts I will write them; and I will be God to them, and they shall be My people.

**38:34** And they shall not teach, each one his fellow citizen and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' for all of them shall know Me, from the least of them to the greatest of them; for I will be merciful to their iniquities, and their sins I will remember no more."

**38:35** "If heaven is lifted up on high," says the Lord, "and if the foundation of the earth is brought low beneath, still I will not reject the family of Israel," says the Lord, "for all that they have done."

**38:36** Thus says the Lord, who gave the sun for the light of the day, the moon and the stars for the light of the night, and a roaring in the sea, and its waves boomed—the Lord Almighty is His name:

**38:37** "If these laws cease from before Me," says the Lord, "then the family of Israel shall also cease from being a nation before Me all the days."

**38:38** "Behold, days are coming," says the Lord, "when a city shall be built for the Lord from the Tower of Hanameel to the Corner Gate.

**38:39** And its measuring line shall go out opposite them as far as the hills of Gareb, and it shall be encompassed round about with choice stones;

**38:40** and all the Asaremoth as far as Nahal Kidron, as far as the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east, shall be a sanctuary to the Lord; and it shall no longer fail, nor shall it be torn down forever."

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — The Greek reads "warmth" (thermon) and "go, and do not destroy Israel," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("found grace... when Israel went to seek rest").
- **38:8** — The Greek reads "at the feast of Passover" (transliterating the Hebrew word as phasek), where the Masoretic Text reads "among them the blind and the lame."
- **38:22** — The Septuagint reads "the Lord has created salvation for a new planting; in salvation men shall walk about," a markedly different reading from the Masoretic Text ("a woman shall encompass a man").
- **38:32** — The Greek reads "and I disregarded them" (echoed in Hebrews 8:9), where the Masoretic Text reads "though I was a husband to them."
- **38:38** — The Greek reads the proper name "Hanameel" (Hanameel) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Hananel."
- **38:40** — "Asaremoth" (transliterated from a Hebrew term for fields or terraces) and "Nahal Kidron" (transliterating Hebrew nachal, "brook" or "wadi") preserve the Septuagint's own Greek transliterations.
