## Text

**40:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still bound in the court of the prison, saying,

**40:2** "Thus says the Lord, who makes the earth and forms it to establish it—the Lord is His name:

**40:3** Call to Me, and I will answer you, and I will declare to you great and mighty things, which you have not known.

**40:4** For thus says the Lord concerning the houses of this city and concerning the houses of the king of Judah, which have been torn down for ramparts and bulwarks,

**40:5** to fight against the Chaldeans, and to fill it with the dead bodies of the men whom I struck down in My anger and in My wrath, and from whom I turned away My face because of all their evils:

**40:6** Behold, I will bring upon it healing and a cure, and I will reveal to them—and I will heal it—both peace and faithfulness;

**40:7** and I will turn back the captivity of Judah and the captivity of Israel, and I will build them as at the first.

**40:8** And I will cleanse them from all their iniquities by which they have sinned against Me, and I will by no means remember their sins by which they sinned against Me and departed from Me.

**40:9** And it shall be for gladness, and for praise, and for majesty to all the people of the earth, who shall hear of all the good things that I will do; and they shall fear and be embittered concerning all the good things and concerning all the peace that I will make for them.

**40:10** Thus says the Lord: There shall yet be heard in this place, of which you say, 'It is desolate, without man and beasts,' in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem that are laid waste because there is neither man nor beasts,

**40:11** the voice of gladness and the voice of joy, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the voice of those who say, 'Give thanks to the Lord Almighty, for the Lord is good, for His mercy is forever'; and they shall bring gifts into the house of the Lord; for I will turn back all the captivity of that land as at the first," says the Lord.

**40:12** Thus says the Lord of hosts: "There shall be in this desolate place, because there is neither man nor beast, and in all its cities, resting places of shepherds who cause sheep to lie down.

**40:13** In the cities of the hill country, and in the cities of the Shephelah, and in the cities of the Negev, and in the land of Benjamin, and in the places round about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, sheep shall yet pass by the hand of one who counts them," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **40:6** — The Greek places "and I will reveal to them" before "and I will heal it," followed by "both peace and faithfulness," preserving an unusual clause order compared to the Masoretic Text.
- **40:9** — The Greek reads pikranthēsontai ("they shall be embittered") where the Masoretic Text has "they shall tremble."
- **40:10** — The Greek translates the Hebrew word for "streets" as exōthen ("outside Jerusalem").
- **40:13** — The Septuagint text of this chapter ends at verse 13, lacking the royal and Levitical covenant promises found in Masoretic Text Jeremiah 33:14-26.
