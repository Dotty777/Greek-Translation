## Text

**10:1** Hear the word of the Lord which He has spoken concerning you, O house of Israel.

**10:2** Thus says the Lord: "Do not learn according to the ways of the nations, and do not be terrified by the signs of heaven, for they fear them in their countenances.

**10:3** For the customs of the nations are vain: it is wood cut out from the forest, the work of a craftsman and a cast image,

**10:4** adorned with silver and gold; with hammers and nails they have fastened them.

**10:9** They shall set them up, and they shall not be moved; it is chased silver, they shall not walk; plated silver shall come from Tarshish, and gold of Mophaz, and the hand of goldsmiths—all are the works of artisans. With blue and purple they shall clothe them. They must surely be carried, for they cannot take a step. Do not fear them, for they will by no means do evil, nor is there any good in them."

**10:11** Thus you shall say to them: "Let the gods who have not made heaven and earth perish from the earth and from beneath this heaven."

**10:12** It is the Lord who made the earth by His strength, who set upright the inhabited world by His wisdom, and by His understanding stretched out heaven

**10:13** and a multitude of waters in heaven; and He brought up clouds from the farthest end of the earth; He made lightnings for the rain, and brought forth light out of His treasuries.

**10:14** Every man has become foolish, devoid of knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his carved images, for he has cast falsehoods, and there is no breath in them.

**10:15** They are vain, works of mockery; in the time of their visitation they shall perish.

**10:16** Not such is the portion of Jacob, for He who formed all things is Himself his inheritance; the Lord is His name.

**10:17** He has gathered your substance from outside, O you who dwell in choice places.

**10:18** For thus says the Lord: "Behold, I will trip up the inhabitants of this land in affliction, so that your wound may be found."

**10:19** Woe for your ruin! Grievous is your blow! And I said, "Truly this is your wound, and it has overtaken you."

**10:20** Your tent is ruined; it has perished, and all your tent curtains have been torn apart; my sons and my sheep are no more; there is no longer a place for my tent, a place for my curtains.

**10:21** For the shepherds have acted foolishly and have not sought the Lord; therefore the whole pasture has not understood, and they have been scattered.

**10:22** Behold, the sound of a report is coming, and a great shaking from the land of the north, to make the cities of Judah a desolation and a lair of ostriches.

**10:23** I know, O Lord, that a man's way does not belong to him, nor shall a man walk and direct his own course.

**10:24** Chasten us, O Lord, yet in judgment and not in wrath, lest You make us few.

**10:25** Pour out Your wrath upon the nations that do not know You, and upon the generations that have not called upon Your name; for they have devoured Jacob and consumed him, and laid waste his pasture.

## Translator Notes

- **10:9** — The Septuagint omits verses 5–8 and 10 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text and combines the idol polemic here; "Mophaz" is the Greek proper name corresponding to Hebrew "Uphaz."
- **10:13** — Greek phōs ("light") stands where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "wind," and the opening clause connects directly to verse 12 without "when He utters His voice."
- **10:17** — Greek synēgagen ("He has gathered") and en eklektois ("in choice places") differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Gather up your wares from the ground, O inhabitant of the siege").
- **10:20** — Greek kai ta probata mou ("and my sheep") appears here where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "have gone forth from me."
