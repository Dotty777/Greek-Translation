## Text

**2:2** And He said, "Thus says the Lord: 'I have remembered the mercy of your youth and the love of its perfection, that you followed after the Holy One of Israel,' says the Lord.

**2:3** 'Israel is holy to the Lord, the firstfruits of His produce; all who devour him shall trespass; evils shall come upon them,' says the Lord."

**2:4** Hear the word of the Lord, O house of Jacob, and every family of the house of Israel.

**2:5** Thus says the Lord: "What offense did your fathers find in Me, that they departed far from Me, and walked after vain things and became vain?

**2:6** And they did not say, 'Where is the Lord who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, who guided us in the wilderness, in a boundless and untrodden land, in a waterless and barren land, in a land through which nothing passed and where no man dwelt?'

**2:7** And I brought you into Carmel, that you might eat its fruits and its good things; yet you entered and defiled My land, and made My inheritance an abomination.

**2:8** The priests did not say, 'Where is the Lord?' And those who hold fast to the law did not know Me; and the shepherds acted impiously against Me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal and walked after that which is unprofitable.

**2:9** Therefore I will yet contend in judgment with you, and with the sons of your sons I will contend in judgment.

**2:10** For go to the islands of Kittim and see, and send to Kedar and consider carefully, and see whether such things have happened,

**2:11** whether nations will exchange their gods, though they are not gods; yet My people have exchanged their glory for that from which they shall not profit.

**2:12** Heaven was astounded at this and shuddered exceedingly all the more," says the Lord.

**2:13** "For My people have committed two evil things: they have forsaken Me, the fountain of the water of life, and have dug for themselves broken cisterns that will not be able to hold water.

**2:14** Is Israel a servant, or is he homeborn? Why has he become plunder?

**2:15** Lions roared against him and gave forth their voice, who made his land a wilderness, and his cities were demolished so as to be without inhabitant.

**2:16** Also the sons of Memphis and Tahpanhes knew you and mocked you.

**2:17** Has not your forsaking Me done these things to you?" says the Lord your God.

**2:18** "And now what have you to do with the way of Egypt, to drink the water of Gihon? And what have you to do with the way of the Assyrians, to drink the water of rivers?

**2:19** Your apostasy shall chasten you, and your wickedness shall reprove you; and know and see that it is bitter for you to have forsaken Me," says the Lord your God; "and I have not been well pleased with you," says the Lord your God.

**2:20** "For from of old you have broken your yoke; you have burst your bonds and said, 'I will not serve You, but I will go upon every high hill and beneath every shady tree; there I will spread myself out in my harlotry.'

**2:21** Yet I planted you a fruitful vine, wholly true; how have you turned into bitterness, O strange vine?

**2:22** Though you wash yourself with lye and multiply soap for yourself, you are stained in your iniquities before Me," says the Lord.

**2:23** "How will you say, 'I have not been defiled, and I have not walked after Baal'? See your ways in the burial ground, and know what you have done. At evening her voice wailed;

**2:24** she widened her ways to the waters of the wilderness; in the desires of her soul she was carried away by the wind; she was given over. Who shall turn her back? All who seek her shall not grow weary; in her humiliation they shall find her.

**2:25** Turn back your foot from a rough way, and your throat from thirst. But she said, 'I will strengthen myself,' for she had loved strangers, and after them she walked.

**2:26** As is the shame of a thief when he is caught, so shall the sons of Israel be put to shame—they and their kings and their rulers and their priests and their prophets.

**2:27** To the wood they said, 'You are my father,' and to the stone, 'You brought me forth.' Yet they have turned their backs to Me, and not their faces; and in the time of their calamities they will say, 'Arise and save us!'

**2:28** And where are your gods which you made for yourself? Will they arise and save in the time of your affliction? For according to the number of your cities were your gods, O Judah, and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem they sacrificed to Baal.

**2:29** Why do you speak to Me? You have all acted impiously, and you have all acted lawlessly against Me," says the Lord.

**2:30** "In vain I have struck your children; you did not receive discipline; a sword devoured your prophets like a destroying lion, and you did not fear."

**2:31** Hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord: "Have I become a wilderness to Israel, or a barren wasteland? Why have My people said, 'We will not be ruled over, and we will come to You no more'?

**2:32** Will a bride forget her ornaments, or a virgin her breastband? Yet My people have forgotten Me days without number.

**2:33** What fair thing will you yet devise in your ways to seek love? Not so; rather, you yourself have acted wickedly to defile your ways,

**2:34** and on your hands was found the blood of innocent souls; I did not find them in breaches, but at every oak.

**2:35** Yet you said, 'I am innocent; surely let His wrath be turned away from me.' Behold, I enter into judgment with you, because you say, 'I have not sinned.'

**2:36** Why have you made so light of repeating your ways? Indeed, you shall be put to shame by Egypt, just as you were put to shame by Assyria,

**2:37** for from there also you shall go forth with your hands upon your head; for the Lord has rejected your hope, and you shall not prosper in it."

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek omits verse 1, reads "the love of its perfection" (with masculine/neuter pronoun autou, "its/his"), and has "the Holy One of Israel" where the Hebrew reads "after Me in the wilderness, in a land not sown."
- **2:7** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for "orchard/plentiful land" as the proper name "Carmel."
- **2:16** — The Greek reads "knew you and mocked you," diverging from the Hebrew "have broken the crown of your head."
- **2:18** — The Greek names the river "Gihon" (an ancient designation associated with the Nile) where the Hebrew reads "Shihor," and uses plural "rivers" rather than singular "the River."
- **2:23** — In verses 23-24, the Greek departs markedly from the Hebrew animal imagery, reading "in the burial ground" (polyandrion), "At evening her voice wailed," "carried away by the wind," and "in her humiliation."
