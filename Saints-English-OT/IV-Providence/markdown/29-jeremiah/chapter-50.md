## Text

**50:1** Now it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking to the people all the words of the Lord, for which the Lord had sent him to them—all these words—

**50:2** that Azariah the son of Maaseiah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the men who addressed Jeremiah spoke, saying, "You speak falsely! The Lord has not sent you to us, saying, 'Do not enter Egypt to dwell there,'

**50:3** but rather Baruch the son of Neriah is inciting you against us, that you may deliver us into the hands of the Chaldeans, to put us to death and to carry us away captive to Babylon."

**50:4** So Johanan, and all the commanders of the forces, and all the people did not obey the voice of the Lord, to dwell in the land of Judah.

**50:5** And Johanan and all the commanders of the forces took all the remnant of Judah who had returned to dwell in the land—

**50:6** the mighty men, and the women, and the rest, and the daughters of the king, and the souls whom Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, and Jeremiah the prophet, and Baruch the son of Neriah—

**50:7** and they entered Egypt, for they did not obey the voice of the Lord; and they came into Tahpanhes.

**50:8** Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

**50:9** "Take for yourself large stones and hide them in the entranceway, at the gate of the house of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah,

**50:10** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am sending and will bring Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he shall set his throne upon these stones which you have hidden, and he shall lift up his weapons against them.

**50:11** And he shall enter and strike the land of Egypt: such as are for death, to death; and such as are for captivity, to captivity; and such as are for the sword, to the sword.

**50:12** And he shall kindle a fire in the houses of their gods, and he shall burn them and carry them away captive; and he shall delouse the land of Egypt just as a shepherd delouses his garment, and he shall go forth in peace.

**50:13** And he shall shatter the pillars of Heliopolis that are in On, and their houses he shall burn up with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **50:9** — Greek reads "in the entranceway" (en prothyrois), whereas the Masoretic Text (Jeremiah 43:9) reads "in the mortar in the brick pavement."
- **50:10** — Greek reads "which you have hidden" and "he shall lift up his weapons against them," whereas the Masoretic Text has "which I have hidden" and "he will spread his royal canopy over them."
- **50:12** — Greek uses the vivid verb phtheirizo ("delouse"), picturing Nebuchadnezzar stripping Egypt clean as a shepherd picks lice from his garment, whereas the Masoretic Text speaks of wrapping himself in the land.
- **50:13** — Greek specifies "Heliopolis that are in On," using the Greek name for the Egyptian city of the sun alongside its Hebrew name On, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt."
