## Text

**8:1** "At that time," says the Lord, "they shall bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of its rulers, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of those who dwell in Jerusalem, out of their tombs;

**8:2** and they shall lay them out to dry before the sun and the moon, and before all the stars, and before all the host of heaven, which they loved, and which they served, and after which they walked, and to which they clung, and which they worshiped; they shall not be lamented, nor shall they be buried, and they shall become an example upon the face of the earth,

**8:3** because they chose death rather than life—even to all the remnant that are left of that generation, in every place to which I shall drive them out."

**8:4** For thus says the Lord: "Does he who falls not rise again? Or does he who turns away not turn back?

**8:5** Why has this My people turned away with a shameless turning away, and been held fast in their own choice, and refused to return?

**8:6** Give ear now and listen: will they not speak thus? There is no man who repents of his wickedness, saying, 'What have I done?' The runner has ceased from his course, like a sweat-drenched horse in his neighing.

**8:7** Even the stork in the heavens knows her appointed time; the turtledove and the swallow of the field, the sparrows, have observed the times of their coming; but My people do not know the judgments of the Lord.

**8:8** How will you say, 'We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us'? In vain has a lying reed been made for the scribes.

**8:9** The wise have been put to shame, and dismayed, and captured, because they have rejected the law of the Lord; what wisdom is in them?

**8:10** Therefore I will give their wives to others, and their fields to the heirs,

**8:13** and they shall gather their produce," says the Lord. "There are no grapes on the vines, and there are no figs on the fig trees, and the leaves have fallen off."

**8:14** Why are we sitting still? Gather together, and let us enter the fortified cities and be cast away; for God has cast us away and given us water of gall to drink, because we have sinned before Him.

**8:15** We gathered together for peace, and there was no good; for a time of healing, and behold, alarm.

**8:16** From Dan we shall hear the sound of the swiftness of his horses; at the sound of the neighing of the riding of his horses the whole earth has quaked; and he shall come and devour the land and its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it.

**8:17** "For behold, I am sending out among you deadly serpents, which cannot be charmed, and they shall bite you

**8:18** incurably with pain, while your heart is perplexed."

**8:19** Behold, the voice of the cry of the daughter of my people from a land far off: "Is the Lord not in Zion? Or is there no king there?" "Why have they provoked Me to anger with their carved images and with foreign vanities?"

**8:20** "The summer has passed, the harvest has gone by, and we have not been saved."

**8:21** For the shattering of the daughter of my people I have been darkened; in perplexity, birth pangs have overpowered me as of a woman in labor.

**8:22** Is there no resin in Gilead, or is there no physician there? Why has the healing of the daughter of my people not come up?

**8:23** Who will give water to my head, and a fountain of tears to my eyes, that I may weep for this my people day and night, for the wounded of the daughter of my people?

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — The Greek imagery diverges from the Hebrew, depicting a runner who has stopped short in his course "like a sweat-drenched horse in his neighing."
- **8:7** — "Stork" renders Greek asida, a transliteration of the Hebrew name for the bird; the Greek also includes "the sparrows" alongside the turtledove and the swallow of the field.
- **8:10** — The Greek text omits the second half of verse 10 and all of verses 11-12, carrying the sentence directly into verse 13.
- **8:18** — In the Greek text, verse 18 completes the sentence begun by God in verse 17 rather than opening a separate lament by the prophet.
- **8:23** — This verse corresponds to Jeremiah 9:1 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most modern English versions.
