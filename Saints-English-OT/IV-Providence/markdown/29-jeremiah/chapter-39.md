## Text

**39:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah in the tenth year of King Zedekiah—this was the eighteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

**39:2** And the army of the king of Babylon laid siege against Jerusalem, and Jeremiah was kept under guard in the court of the prison, which is in the house of the king,

**39:3** where King Zedekiah had shut him up, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am giving this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it,

**39:4** and Zedekiah shall by no means escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, for he shall surely be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon, and his mouth shall speak to his mouth, and his eyes shall see his eyes,

**39:5** and Zedekiah shall enter into Babylon, and there he shall dwell.'"

**39:6** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,

**39:7** "Behold, Hanamel, the son of Shallum your father's brother, is coming to you, saying, 'Buy for yourself my field which is in Anathoth, for the right is yours to take it for a possession.'"

**39:8** And Hanamel, the son of Samol my father's brother, came to me into the court of the prison and said, "Buy my field which is in the land of Benjamin, which is in Anathoth, for the right is yours to buy it, and you are the elder." And I knew that it was the word of the Lord.

**39:9** And I bought the field of Hanamel, the son of my father's brother, and weighed out to him seven shekels and ten of silver.

**39:10** And I wrote in a scroll and sealed it, and called witnesses to attest it, and weighed the silver on a balance.

**39:11** And I took the scroll of purchase, the one that was sealed,

**39:12** and I gave it to Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, in the sight of Hanamel, the son of my father's brother, and in the sight of the men who stood by and wrote in the scroll of purchase, and in the sight of the Jews who were in the court of the prison.

**39:13** And I charged Baruch in their sight, saying,

**39:14** "Thus says the Lord Almighty: 'Take this scroll of purchase and the scroll that was read, and you shall place it in an earthen vessel, that it may endure for many days.'

**39:15** For thus says the Lord: 'Fields and houses and vineyards shall yet be established in this land.'"

**39:16** And I prayed to the Lord after I had given the scroll of purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah, saying,

**39:17** "O Lord, the One who is, You have made heaven and earth by Your great power and by Your high and uplifted arm; nothing shall by any means be hidden from You,

**39:18** showing mercy to thousands, and repaying the sins of fathers into the bosom of their children after them—the great, the mighty God,

**39:19** the Lord of great counsel and mighty in works, the great God, the Almighty, and the Lord of great name; Your eyes are upon the ways of the sons of men, to give to each one according to his way;

**39:20** You who performed signs and wonders in the land of Egypt even to this day, both in Israel and among the earthborn, and made for Yourself a name, as it is this day,

**39:21** and brought forth Your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and with wonders, with a mighty hand,

**39:22** and with an uplifted arm, and with great visions; and You gave them this land, which You swore to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**39:23** And they entered in and possessed it, yet they did not listen to Your voice, nor walk in Your ordinances; all that You commanded them they did not do, so that all these evils came upon them.

**39:24** Behold, a multitude has come to the city to seize it; and the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans who fight against it, because of the sword and the famine; as You have spoken, so it has come to pass.

**39:25** Yet You say to me, 'Buy for yourself the field with silver'—and I wrote a scroll and sealed it, and called witnesses to attest it—though the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans!"

**39:26** Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**39:27** "I am the Lord, the God of all flesh; shall anything be hidden from Me?

**39:28** Therefore thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: This city shall surely be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;

**39:29** and the Chaldeans who fight against this city shall come and burn this city with fire, and burn down the houses upon whose rooftops they offered incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke Me to anger.

**39:30** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been doing only evil in My sight from their youth;

**39:31** for this city has been for My anger and for My wrath from the day they built it even to this day, that I should remove it from My presence,

**39:32** because of all the evils of the sons of Israel and of Judah which they have done to provoke Me to anger—they, and their kings, and their rulers, and their priests, and their prophets, the men of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem.

**39:33** And they turned their back to Me, and not their face; though I taught them early in the morning and taught them again, yet they did not listen any longer to receive instruction.

**39:34** And they set their defilements in the house upon which My name is called, in their impurities.

**39:35** And they built the altars to Baal which are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to offer up their sons and their daughters to Molech the king—things which I did not command them, nor did it enter My heart—to do this abomination, to cause Judah to sin.

**39:36** And now thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, concerning the city of which you say, 'It shall be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by famine, and by banishment':

**39:37** Behold, I will gather them from all the earth where I have scattered them in My anger and in My wrath and in great indignation; and I will bring them back to this place, and I will cause them to dwell securely.

**39:38** And they shall be to Me a people, and I will be to them a God.

**39:39** And I will give them another way and another heart, to fear Me all their days, and for good to them and to their children after them.

**39:40** And I will make with them an everlasting covenant, which I will by no means turn away from behind them; and I will put My fear into their heart, so that they shall not depart from Me.

**39:41** And I will visit them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, and with all My heart and with all My soul.

**39:42** For thus says the Lord: Just as I have brought upon this people all these great evils, so I will bring upon them all the good things that I have spoken concerning them.

**39:43** And fields shall yet be bought in the land of which you say, 'It shall be untrodden by man and beast, and they have been delivered into the hands of the Chaldeans';

**39:44** and they shall buy fields with silver, and you shall write a scroll and seal it, and call witnesses to attest it, in the land of Benjamin, and round about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the hill country of the Shephelah, and in the cities of the Negev; for I will turn back their captivities."

## Translator Notes

- **39:8** — The Greek text spells Hanamel's father's name Samol here (a metathesis of Shallum in v. 7), and adds "and you are the elder" (presbyteros), indicating seniority in the right of redemption.
- **39:14** — Where the Masoretic Text speaks of "the open deed," the Greek reads to anegnosmenon ("the scroll that was read"), referring to the unsealed copy available for public reading.
- **39:15** — The Greek reads ktisthēsontai ("shall be established/built," from ktizō), differing from the Masoretic Text's "shall be bought" (cf. ktēthēsontai in v. 43).
- **39:17** — The divine address Ho ōn Kyrie ("O Lord, the One who is") echoes the Greek of Exodus 3:14 (ho ōn) in rendering the Hebrew exclamation of prayer.
- **39:39** — The Greek reads "another way and another heart" (hodon heteran kai kardian heteran), contrasting with the Masoretic Text's "one heart and one way."
