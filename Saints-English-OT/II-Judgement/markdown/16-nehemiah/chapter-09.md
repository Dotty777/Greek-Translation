## Text

**9:1** And on the twenty-fourth day of this month the children of Israel assembled with fasting and clad in sackcloth.

**9:2** And the children of Israel separated themselves from every child of a foreigner, and they stood and confessed their own sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

**9:3** They stood in their appointed place and read from the book of the law of their God; and they were confessing to the Lord and worshiping their God.

**9:4** Then Jeshua and the sons of Kadmiel, and Sherebiah the son of Arabiah, stood upon the raised steps of the Levites, and they cried out with a loud voice to the Lord their God.

**9:5** And the Levites Jeshua and Kadmiel said, "Arise, bless the Lord our God from everlasting even to everlasting; and they shall bless the name of Your glory, and shall exalt it above all blessing and praise."

**9:6** And Ezra said, "You Yourself alone are the Lord; You fashioned the heaven of heaven and all their array, the earth and everything that exists upon it, the seas and everything within them, and You give life to all things; and the armies of the heavens bow down in worship to You.

**9:7** You are God; You chose Abram and brought him out from the land of the Chaldeans, and conferred upon him the name Abraham.

**9:8** You found his heart faithful before You, and established a covenant with him to grant to him and to his seed the territory of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Girgashites; and You fulfilled Your words, because You are righteous.

**9:9** You beheld the humiliation of our fathers in Egypt, and You heard their outcry beside the Red Sea.

**9:10** You displayed signs and wonders in Egypt, upon Pharaoh and upon all his servants and upon all the populace of his land, for You knew that they acted arrogantly against them; and You made a name for Yourself, even as on this day.

**9:11** You tore open the sea before him, and they passed through the midst of the sea on dry ground; and those who pursued them You hurled into the depths, like a stone into raging water.

**9:12** With a pillar of cloud You guided them by day, and with a pillar of fire through the night, to illuminate for them the pathway along which they should travel.

**9:13** Upon Mount Sinai You descended and spoke with them from heaven, and gave them upright judgments and laws of truth, good ordinances and commandments.

**9:14** Your holy Sabbath You made known to them, and commandments, ordinances, and law You enjoined upon them through the hand of Moses Your servant.

**9:15** Bread out of heaven You provided them for their sustenance, and water out of a rock You brought forth to them for their thirst; and You told them to enter in and inherit the land over which You had stretched out Your hand to grant it to them.

**9:16** Yet they stiffened their neck, and did not heed Your commandments.

**9:17** They refused to listen, and did not remember Your wondrous works which You had wrought among them; rather, they hardened their neck, and appointed a ruler to return to their bondage in Egypt. Yet You are a merciful and compassionate God, longsuffering and abounding in mercy, and You did not forsake them.

**9:18** Furthermore, they even forged for themselves a molten calf and declared, 'These are the gods who led us out of Egypt,' and they committed great provocations.

**9:19** Yet You, in Your abundant compassions, did not abandon them in the wilderness; You did not turn aside the pillar of cloud from them by day to guide them along the way, nor the pillar of fire by night to illumine for them the path in which they should walk.

**9:20** Your good Spirit You bestowed to instruct them, and Your manna You did not withhold from their mouth, and water You supplied them for their thirst.

**9:21** For forty years You nourished them in the wilderness; You did not fail them; their garments did not wear out, and their feet did not crack open.

**9:22** You handed over to them kings and peoples, and apportioned them out to them; and they inherited the territory of Sihon and that of Og, king of Bashan.

**9:23** Their children You multiplied like the stars of heaven, and led them into the land of which You had spoken to their fathers, and they inherited it.

**9:24** You utterly crushed before them the inhabitants of the land of the Canaanites, and delivered them into their hands—both their kings and the peoples of the land—to deal with them as was pleasing in their sight.

**9:25** And they seized lofty cities, and came into possession of houses filled with all manner of good things, hewn cisterns, vineyards, olive groves, and every fruit-bearing tree in abundance; so they ate and were filled and grew sleek, and delighted themselves in Your great goodness.

**9:26** Yet they raised a defiant cry and turned away from You, and flung Your law behind their body, and slew Your prophets who bore solemn witness against them to bring them back to You; and they committed great provocations.

**9:27** Therefore You handed them over into the hand of those who afflicted them, and they afflicted them; yet they cried aloud to You in the season of their distress, and from Your heaven You heard, and in Your great compassions You gave them saviors and delivered them out of the hand of him who afflicted them.

**9:28** Yet once they had rest, they turned back to practicing evil in Your sight; so You left them in the hands of their adversaries, who held dominion over them. Then again they cried aloud to You, and from heaven You listened and rescued them in Your manifold compassions,

**9:29** and You solemnly warned them to bring them back to Your law; yet they would not listen, but sinned against Your commandments and in Your judgments—which, if a man performs them, he shall live by them—and they presented a stubborn back and stiffened their neck, and refused to listen.

**9:30** Still You bore patiently with them for many years, and testified against them by Your Spirit through the ministry of Your prophets; yet they would not incline their ears, so You gave them over into the hand of the peoples of the land.

**9:31** Nevertheless, in Your abundant compassions You did not bring complete destruction upon them, nor did You forsake them, for You are mighty, merciful, and compassionate.

**9:32** And now, O our God—the mighty, the great, the powerful, and the awesome One, who keeps Your covenant and Your mercy—do not let all the hardship seem small before You that has overtaken us, our kings, our rulers, our priests, our prophets, our fathers, and all Your people, from the days of the kings of Assyria even until this present day.

**9:33** Yet You are righteous concerning all things that have come upon us, because You have acted in truth, whereas we have sinned grievously,

**9:34** and our kings, our rulers, our priests, and our fathers did not carry out Your law, nor did they pay heed to Your commandments and Your testimonies with which You solemnly warned them.

**9:35** Even in Your kingdom, and amid Your abundant goodness which You bestowed upon them, and in the spacious and fertile land which You set before them, they did not serve You, nor did they turn away from their wicked practices.

**9:36** Behold, today we are bondservants; and the land which You granted to our fathers to eat its fruit

**9:37** is given to the kings whom You have placed over us on account of our sins; and they exercise dominion over our bodies and over our livestock according to their pleasure, and we are in great distress."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Septuagint preserves a shorter and distinct list of Levites on the platform: "Jeshua and the sons of Kadmiel, and Sherebiah the son of Arabiah."
- **9:6** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint explicitly introduces the great penitential prayer of verses 6–37 with "And Ezra said."
- **9:11** — The Greek reads the singular pronoun "before him" (treating Israel collectively as a single nation) before continuing with plural verbs and pronouns.
- **9:26** — The Septuagint translates the rebellion literally with "raised a defiant cry" (Greek elalaxan) and "flung Your law behind their body."
- **9:36** — The Septuagint omits the final clause of verse 36 and the opening phrase of verse 37 found in the Hebrew text, connecting "the land" directly to "the kings" across the verse division.
