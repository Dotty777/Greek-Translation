## Text

**13:1** On that day it was read in the book of Moses in the hearing of the people; and it was found written in it that Ammonites and Moabites should not enter into the assembly of God forever,

**13:2** because they did not meet the sons of Israel with bread and with water, and they hired Balaam against him to curse; yet our God turned the curse into a blessing.

**13:3** And it came to pass, when they heard the law, that all the mixed multitude in Israel were separated.

**13:4** Now before this, Eliashib the priest, who dwelt in the storeroom of the house of our God, was closely related to Tobiah.

**13:5** And he made for himself a great storeroom, where formerly they had put the grain offering, and the frankincense, and the vessels, and the tithe of the grain and of the wine and of the oil, the commanded portion of the Levites and of the singers and of the gatekeepers, and the firstfruits of the priests.

**13:6** And during all this I was not in Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon I had gone to the king; and after the end of some days I asked leave of the king,

**13:7** and I came to Jerusalem; and I perceived the evil, in making for him a storeroom in the court of the house of God.

**13:8** And it seemed exceedingly evil to me, and I cast all the furnishings of the house of Tobiah out of the storeroom;

**13:9** and I gave command, and I purified the storerooms, and I returned there the vessels of the house of God, the manna and the frankincense.

**13:10** And I came to know that the portions of the Levites had not been given, and that the Levites and the singers who performed the work had fled, each man to his own field.

**13:11** And I contended and said, "Why has the house of God been forsaken?" And I gathered them together, and they stationed them at their post.

**13:12** And all Judah brought the tithe of the wheat and of the wine and of the oil into the treasuries,

**13:13** into the hand of Shelemiah the priest and Zadok the scribe and Pedaiah of the Levites; and under their hand was Hanan the son of Zaccur, the son of Nathaniah, for they were reckoned faithful, and it rested upon them to distribute to their brethren.

**13:14** Remember me, O God, in this, and may my mercy not be blotted out, which I have shown in the house of the Lord God.

**13:15** In those days I saw in Judah some treading winepresses on the Sabbath, and bringing in sheaves, and loading upon the donkeys both wine and grapes and figs and every kind of burden, and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day; and I testified on the day of their selling.

**13:16** And they dwelt in it, bringing fish and all manner of merchandise, selling on the Sabbath to the sons of Judah and in Jerusalem.

**13:17** And I contended with the freeborn sons of Judah and said to them, "What is this evil thing that you do, and profane the Sabbath day?

**13:18** Did not your fathers do thus, and our God brought upon them and upon us all these evils, and upon this city? And do you add wrath upon Israel, to profane the Sabbath?"

**13:19** And it came to pass, when the gates of Jerusalem were set in place before the Sabbath, that I shut the gates, and I gave word that they should not be opened until after the Sabbath; and I stationed some at the gates, that no burdens should be carried on the Sabbath day.

**13:20** And all of them lodged and carried on trade outside Jerusalem once and twice.

**13:21** And I testified against them and said to them, "Why do you lodge opposite the wall? If you do so a second time, I will stretch out my hand against you." From that time they did not come on the Sabbath.

**13:22** And I spoke to the Levites, who were purifying themselves and coming to guard the gates, sanctifying the Sabbath day. Concerning these things remember me, O God, and spare me according to the multitude of Your mercy.

**13:23** And in those days I saw the Jews who had taken Ashdodite, Ammonite, and Moabite women to dwell with them,

**13:24** and half of their children spoke in the tongue of Ashdod, and they did not know how to speak in the tongue of Judah.

**13:25** And I contended with them and cursed them, and struck some of the men among them, and made them swear by God, "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, nor take any of their daughters for your sons.

**13:26** Did not Solomon king of Israel sin in this very way? Yet among many nations there was no king like him, and he was beloved by God, and God made him king over all Israel; yet even him foreign women caused to turn aside.

**13:27** And shall we listen to you to do all this wickedness, to break faith with our God by taking foreign women to dwell with you?"

**13:28** And one of the sons of Joiada the son of Eliashib the high priest was a son-in-law of Sanballat; and I cast them out from me.

**13:29** Remember them, O God, concerning the kinship of the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood, and the Levites.

**13:30** And I purified them from all that was foreign, and I established divisions for the priests and for the Levites, each man according to his work,

**13:31** and the offering of the wood-bearers at seasons from time to time, and for the firstfruits. Remember me, O our God, for good.

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — "grain offering" renders the Greek transliteration "manaam" (from Hebrew minchah); in verse 9, the Greek instead reads "the manna."
- **13:19** — The Greek omits "until" before "after the Sabbath" (literally "not to be opened after the Sabbath") and omits the explicit object after "stationed."
- **13:25** — "You shall not give... nor take" renders the Semitic oath construction in Greek, literally "If you give... and if you take."
- **13:29** — "kinship" translates Greek "anchisteia," where the translator read the Hebrew root for defilement as the root for kinship or redemption.
- **13:31** — "firstfruits" renders the Greek transliteration "bakchouria" (from Hebrew bikkurim), and the Greek closes with "O our God" rather than "O my God."
