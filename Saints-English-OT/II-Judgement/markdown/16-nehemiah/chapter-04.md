## Text

**4:1** And it came to pass, when Sanballat and Tobiah and the Arabs and the Ammonites heard that the growth had risen upon the walls of Jerusalem, that the breaches had begun to be closed up, it appeared exceedingly evil to them.

**4:2** And all of them gathered together as one to come and wage war in Jerusalem.

**4:3** And we prayed to our God, and set guards against them day and night because of them.

**4:4** And Judah said, "The strength of the enemies is crushed, and there is a great multitude, and we shall not be able to build on the wall."

**4:5** And those who afflicted us said, "They will not know and will not see until we come into their midst and slay them and bring the work to an end."

**4:6** And it came to pass, when the Jews who dwelt near them came, they said to us, "They are coming up from all the places against us."

**4:7** And I stationed men in the lowest parts of the place behind the wall, in the sheltered places, and I stationed the people by clans with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

**4:8** And I looked, and arose and spoke to the nobles and to the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid before them; remember our great and awesome God, and contend in battle for your brethren, your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your houses."

**4:9** And it came to pass, when our enemies heard that it had become known to us, that God thwarted their counsel; and all of us returned to the wall, each man to his own work.

**4:10** And it came to pass from that day onward that half of those who had been shaken out were doing the work, while half of them held fast—and there were spears and shields and the bows and the breastplates, and the rulers were behind the whole house of Judah,

**4:11** who were building on the wall. And those who bore burdens with carrying-straps were under arms: with one hand a man did the work itself, and with one he grasped the javelin.

**4:12** And as for the builders, each man had his sword girded upon his loins as they were building; and the one who sounded the horn stood beside him.

**4:13** And I said to the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, "The work is broad and great, and we are scattered upon the wall, each man far from his brother.

**4:14** In whatever place you hear the sound of the horn, there you shall gather to us, and our God will fight for us."

**4:15** So we were doing the work, while half of them held the spears from the rising up until the dawn, until the coming forth of the stars.

**4:16** And at that time I said to the people, "Lodge in the midst of Jerusalem, and let the night be a guard for you, and the day for work."

**4:17** So I remained, along with the men of the guard behind me, and not a man among us took off his garments.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Greek reads "the growth had risen" (φυή, denoting advancing restoration) and "it appeared exceedingly evil to them" rather than "they became very angry," and does not mention the Ashdodites.
- **4:4** — The Greek reads "the strength of the enemies is crushed, and there is a great multitude," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "the strength of the burden-bearers is failing, and there is much rubble."
- **4:7** — The Greek specifies "in the sheltered places" (ἐν τοῖς σκεπεινοῖς), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to open or exposed places.
- **4:10** — The Greek phrase "those who had been shaken out" (τῶν ἐκτετιναγμένων) renders the Hebrew for "young men/servants" by associating it with the Hebrew verb meaning "to shake out"; the same rendering recurs in 5:15.
