## Text

**11:1** Now the rulers of the people dwelt in Jerusalem; and the rest of the people drew lots to bring one out of ten to dwell in Jerusalem, the holy city, and nine parts in the cities.

**11:2** And the people blessed all the men who willingly offered themselves to dwell in Jerusalem.

**11:3** Now these are the rulers of the province who dwelt in Jerusalem; and in the cities of Judah each man dwelt in his possession in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the sons of the servants of Solomon.

**11:4** And in Jerusalem there dwelt some of the sons of Judah and of the sons of Benjamin. Of the sons of Judah: Athaiah the son of Azed, the son of Zechariah, the son of Samareiah, the son of Shephatiah, the son of Malelem, and of the sons of Perez;

**11:5** and Maaseiah the son of Baruch, the son of Chalea, the son of Hoziah, the son of Dalea, the son of Joiarib, the son of Thezeiah, the son of Delone.

**11:6** All the sons of Seres who dwelt in Jerusalem were four hundred and sixty-eight men of valor.

**11:7** And these are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu the son of Amesula, Joed the son of Phalaiah, the son of Kodaiah, the son of Magael, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jeshaiah;

**11:8** and after him Gabbai, Sallai, nine hundred and twenty-eight.

**11:9** And Joel the son of Zichri was overseer over them, and Judah the son of Hasana was second over the city.

**11:10** Of the priests also: Dadeiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin,

**11:11** Seraiah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Apoboch, opposite the house of God.

**11:12** And their brethren who did the work of the house: Amzi the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah,

**11:13** rulers of fathers' houses, two hundred and forty-two. And Amaseiah the son of Esdriel,

**11:14** and his brethren, mighty men of battle, one hundred and twenty-eight; and the overseer was Badiel.

**11:15** And of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Ezerei;

**11:17** and Mattaniah the son of Mica, and Obeb the son of Shammua,

**11:18** two hundred and eighty-four.

**11:19** And the gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their brethren, one hundred and seventy-two.

**11:22** And the overseer of the Levites was the son of Bani, Uzzi the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mica, of the sons of Asaph, the singers, opposite the work of the house of God.

**11:23** For there was a commandment of the king concerning them.

**11:24** And Pethahiah the son of Baseza was at the king's hand in every matter for the people.

**11:25** And concerning the villages in their field: some of the sons of Judah dwelt in Kiriathar,

**11:26** and in Jeshua,

**11:27** and in Beersheba,

**11:30** and their villages, Lachish and its fields; and they encamped in Beersheba.

**11:31** And the sons of Benjamin were from Gala, Michmash.

**11:36** And of the Levites, divisions of Judah were for Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — The Greek divides and renders several ancestral names differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Chalea, Hoziah, Dalea, Thezeiah, and Delone (as well as Azed, Samareiah, Malelem, and Seres in verses 4 and 6).
- **11:11** — The Greek reads "Apoboch" (for Hebrew "Ahitub") and "opposite the house of God," rendering the Hebrew consonants for "ruler" as the preposition "opposite" (similarly in verse 22).
- **11:15** — Chapter 11 in the Septuagint is substantially shorter than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting verses 16, 20-21, 28-29, and 32-35 as well as several genealogical and geographical clauses within the remaining verses.
- **11:17** — The Greek reads "Obeb" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Abda," and omits the intervening clauses detailing Mattaniah's lineage and Bakbukiah.
- **11:25** — The Greek reads "Kiriathar" (for Hebrew "Kiriath-Arba") and "Gala" in verse 31 (for Hebrew "Geba"), while verse 30 ends simply "and they encamped in Beersheba."
