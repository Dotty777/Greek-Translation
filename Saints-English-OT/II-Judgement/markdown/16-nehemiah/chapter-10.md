## Text

**10:1** And in all these things we make a faithful covenant and write it; and our rulers, our Levites, and our priests set their seal to it.

**10:2** Now over those who sealed were Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah, and Zedekiah

**10:3** the son of Seraiah, and Azariah, and Jeremiah,

**10:4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**10:5** Hattush, Shebani, Malluch,

**10:6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**10:7** Daniel, Tnathoth, Baruch,

**10:8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**10:9** Nadeiah, Belseiah, Shemaiah—these were the priests.

**10:10** And the Levites: Jeshua the son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadab, Kadmiel,

**10:11** and his brethren: Shebaniah, Hodijah, Kanta,

**10:12** Pelaiah, Hanan, Mica, Rehob,

**10:13** Hashabiah, Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**10:14** Hodum, the sons of Benjamin.

**10:15** The rulers of the people: Parosh, Pahath-Moab, Elam, Zattu, the sons of Bani,

**10:16** the sons of Bani, Azgad, Bebai,

**10:17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**10:18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**10:19** Hodijah, Hashum, Bezai,

**10:20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,

**10:21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**10:22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**10:23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Aiah,

**10:24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**10:25** Hallohesh, Phadaeis, Shobek,

**10:26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**10:27** and Ara, Hanan, Enam,

**10:28** Malluch, Harim, Baanah.

**10:29** Now the rest of the people—the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the Nethinim, and everyone who came from the peoples of the land to the law of God, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, everyone who knew and understood—

**10:30** prevailed over their brethren, laid a curse upon them, and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the law of God, which was given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, and to observe and do all our commandments and His judgments,

**10:31** and not to give our daughters to the peoples of the land, and their daughters we will not take for our sons;

**10:32** and as for the peoples of the land who bring wares and every kind of merchandise on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and we will forego the seventh year and the exaction of every hand.

**10:33** And we will lay commandments upon ourselves, to charge ourselves to give a third of a didrachma every year for the service of the house of our God:

**10:34** for the loaves of the Presence and the continual offering, and for the continual burnt offering of the Sabbaths, of the New Moons, for the feasts and for the holy things, and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for the works of the house of our God.

**10:35** And we cast lots concerning the lot of the wood offering—the priests, the Levites, and the people—to bring it into the house of our God, by the house of our fathers, at appointed seasons year by year, to burn upon the altar of our God, as it is written in the book;

**10:36** and to bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of the fruit of every tree, year by year, into the house,

**10:37** and the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the law, and the firstborn of our herds and of our flocks, to bring them into the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God.

**10:38** And the firstfruits of our grain and the fruit of every tree, of wine and of oil, we will bring to the priests, to the treasury of the house of God, and the tithe of our land to the Levites; and the Levites themselves shall collect the tithes in all the cities of our labor.

**10:39** And the priest, Aaron, shall be with the Levite in the tithe of the Levite, and the Levites shall bring up the tithe of the tithe into the house of our God, to the treasuries, to the house of God.

**10:40** For the children of Israel and the Levites shall bring the firstfruits of the grain, of the wine, and of the oil into the storehouses; and there are the holy vessels, and the priests, and the ministers, and the gatekeepers, and the singers; and we will not forsake the house of our God.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek text omits the title "the governor" after Nehemiah and connects Zedekiah directly to the next verse as "the son of Seraiah," rather than listing Seraiah separately as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:7** — The Greek reads "Tnathoth" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Ginnethon"; similar Septuagint-distinct forms appear across this list (including Nadeiah, Belseiah, Kanta, Hodum, the sons of Benjamin, Aiah, Phadaeis, Ara, and Enam).
- **10:30** — The Greek reads "prevailed over their brethren, laid a curse upon them" (reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew term for "their nobles") and "all our commandments and His judgments," omitting "of the Lord our Lord" and "His statutes."
- **10:33** — The Greek specifies "a third of a didrachma," using a Greek silver coin denomination where the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies a third of a shekel.
- **10:39** — The Greek literally reads "the priest, Aaron" (without "the son of") and concludes with "to the house of God" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "to the storehouse."
