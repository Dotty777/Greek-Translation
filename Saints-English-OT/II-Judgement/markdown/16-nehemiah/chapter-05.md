## Text

**5:1** Now there was a great outcry of the people and their wives against their brethren the Jews.

**5:2** And there were some who said, "With our sons and our daughters, we are many; and we shall obtain grain, and we shall eat and live."

**5:3** And there are some who say, "Our fields, and our vineyards, and our houses we are pledging as security; and we shall obtain grain and eat."

**5:4** And there are some who say, "We have borrowed silver for the tribute of the king—our fields, and our vineyards, and our houses.

**5:5** Now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren, and their sons are as our sons; yet behold, we are forcing our sons and our daughters into bondage, and some of our daughters are already held in bondage; and there is no power in our hand, and our fields and our vineyards belong to the nobles."

**5:6** And I was exceedingly grieved when I heard their outcry and these words.

**5:7** And my heart took counsel within me, and I contended with the nobles and the rulers, and said to them, "With exactions you are demanding payment, each man from his brother." And I convened a great assembly against them,

**5:8** and I said to them, "We have purchased our brethren the Jews who were sold to the nations, of our own free will; and do you sell our brethren?" And they kept silent and found no word.

**5:9** And I said, "The thing that you are doing is not good; not in this way, in the fear of our God, shall you depart from the reproach of our enemies.

**5:10** And my brethren and my acquaintances and I have lent them silver and grain, yet we have relinquished this exaction.

**5:11** Restore now yourselves, even today, their fields, their vineyards, their olive groves, and their houses; and from the silver, the grain and the wine, and bring them forth for yourselves."

**5:12** And they said, "We will restore them, and from them we will seek nothing; thus we will do, just as you say." Then I summoned the priests and made them swear an oath to do according to this word.

**5:13** And I shook out my mantle and said, "So may God shake out every man who does not uphold this word from his house and from his labor, and so he shall be shaken out and empty." And all the assembly said, "Amen," and praised the Lord; and the people did according to this word.

**5:14** From the day on which he commanded me to be their ruler in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year even to the thirty-second year of Arsenatha—twelve years—I and our brethren did not eat of their forced exaction.

**5:15** And as for the former exactions which they laid heavily upon them before me, they also took from them in bread and in wine the last silver, forty didrachmas, and their shaken-out ones exercise authority over the people; yet I did not do so because of the fear of God.

**5:16** And in the work of the wall of these people I did not take hold, and a field I did not acquire; and all those who were gathered were there for the work.

**5:17** And the Jews, one hundred and fifty men, as well as those coming to us from the nations around us, were at my table.

**5:18** Now that which was prepared for one day was one calf, and choice sheep and a young goat were prepared for me, and every ten days wine among all in abundance; yet with all this I did not seek the bread of forced exaction, because the servitude was heavy upon this people.

**5:19** Remember me, O God, for good, for all that I have done for this people.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Greek reads "I was exceedingly grieved" (ἐλυπήθην σφόδρα), expressing deep sorrow rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "I became very angry."
- **5:11** — The Greek reads "from the silver, the grain and the wine, and bring them forth for yourselves," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's call to return the hundredth part of the silver, grain, wine, and oil.
- **5:14** — The Greek preserves the distinct royal name "Arsenatha" (Ἀρσεναθά) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Artaxerxes," and renders the governor's allowance with βία ("forced exaction"), a rendering continued in 5:15 and 5:18.
- **5:15** — The Greek reads "the last silver, forty didrachmas" (double drachmas rather than shekels) and again uses "their shaken-out ones" (οἱ ἐκτετιναγμένοι; compare 4:10).
- **5:16** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("I held fast to the work on this wall"), the Greek contains a negation ("I did not take hold") and refers to "all those who were gathered" rather than Nehemiah's servants.
