## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek text here has the truncated dative phrase "of Malluch" and omits verses 4–6; similarly, verse 9 consists solely of "For the divisions," and verse 18 is absent.
- **12:8** — The Greek reads "Machaniah" (for Hebrew Mattaniah) and "over the hands," reflecting a reading of the Hebrew term for thanksgivings as "hands."
- **12:15** — Throughout verses 15–22, several proper names preserve distinctive Greek readings, including Are, Mannas, Adadai, Ganathoth, Edio, Abed, Isdeiu, and the additional name Joa in verse 22.
- **12:25** — Instead of listing the gatekeepers, the Greek reads "when I gathered the gatekeepers"; in verse 27, "thanksgivings" translates the Greek transliteration "tholatha" (from Hebrew todot).
- **12:37** — The Greek reads "Gate of Praise" (for Hebrew Fountain Gate) and plural "cities of David," omits verses 38 and 40–41, and in verse 44 reads "leaders of the cities" where the Hebrew has "fields of the cities."
