## Text

**6:1** And it came to pass, when it was heard by Sanballat and Tobiah, and by Geshem the Arab, and by the rest of our enemies, that the wall had been built, and that no breath was left in them—up to that time I had not set up doors in the gates—

**6:2** that Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, and let us gather together in the villages in the plain of Ono." Yet they were plotting to do evil to me.

**6:3** And I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work, and I shall not be able to come down, lest the work cease; as soon as I have completed it, I will come down to you."

**6:4** And they sent to me according to this same word, and I sent back to them in the same manner.

**6:5** And Sanballat sent his servant to me, with an open letter in his hand.

**6:6** And it was written in it: "It has been heard among the nations that you and the Jews are planning to rebel; therefore you are building the wall, and you shall be king for them.

**6:7** And in addition to these things you have appointed prophets for yourself, so that you may sit in Jerusalem as king over Judah; and now these words will be reported to the king. Come now, therefore, and let us take counsel together."

**6:8** And I sent to him, saying, "Nothing has happened like these words which you speak, for out of your own heart you are fabricating them."

**6:9** For all of them are seeking to frighten us, saying, "Their hands will grow weak from this work, and it will not be done." And now I strengthened my hands.

**6:10** And I entered the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah, the son of Meitael, who was shut in; and he said, "Let us meet together in the house of God, in its midst, and let us shut its doors, for they are coming by night to slay you."

**6:11** And I said, "Who is the man who will enter into the house and live?"

**6:12** And I perceived, and behold, God had not sent him, for the prophecy was a word against me; and Tobiah and Sanballat had hired

**6:13** a multitude against me, in order that I might be afraid and act thus and sin, and become for them an evil name, so that they might reproach me.

**6:14** Remember, O God, Tobiah and Anaballat according to these his deeds, and also Noadiah the prophet and the rest of the priests who were frightening me.

**6:15** So the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of Edud, in fifty-two days.

**6:16** And it came to pass, when our enemies heard of it, that all the nations around us were afraid, and exceeding fear fell in their own eyes; and they knew that it was from our God that this work had been brought to completion.

**6:17** Moreover, in those days letters were going forth from many nobles of Judah to Tobiah, and those of Tobiah were coming to them;

**6:18** for many in Judah were bound by oath to him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah the son of Arah, and Johanan his son had taken the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah as his wife.

**6:19** And they were speaking his words before me, and they were carrying out my words to him; and Tobiah sent letters to frighten me.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek reads "no breath was left in them" (πνοή) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "no breach was left in it."
- **6:3** — Rather than asking rhetorically why the work should stop while he leaves it, the Greek has Nehemiah warn against the work ceasing and add, "as soon as I have completed it, I will come down to you."
- **6:10** — The Greek gives the grandfather's name as "Meitael" (Μειταήλ) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Mehetabel."
- **6:14** — The Greek reads "Anaballat" (Ἀναβαλλάτ, lacking the initial sigma of Sanballat), gives Noadiah the masculine title "the prophet" (τῷ προφήτῃ) rather than "prophetess," and refers to "the rest of the priests" instead of "the rest of the prophets."
- **6:15** — The Greek gives the month name as "Edud" (Ἐδούδ) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Elul."
