## Text

**3:1** Then Eliashib the high priest rose up with his brethren the priests, and they built the Sheep Gate; they consecrated it and set up its doors, and they consecrated it as far as the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**3:2** And next to them were the men of the sons of Jericho, and next to them the sons of Zabaur the son of Amarei.

**3:3** And the Fish Gate was built by the sons of Asan; they roofed it, and they roofed its doors, and its locks, and its bars.

**3:4** And next to them repair was made by Ramoth the son of Uriah, the son of Hakkoz. And next to them Zadok the son of Baana repaired.

**3:5** And next to them the Tekoites repaired, but Adoreem did not put their neck to their service.

**3:6** And the Gate of Isana was repaired by Joeiaa the son of Phasek and Meshullam the son of Badia; they roofed it, and set up its doors, and its locks, and its bars.

**3:8** And next to them Hananiah the son of Joakim made repairs, and they left Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**3:9** And next to them Rephaiah, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired.

**3:10** And next to them Jedaiah the son of Heromath repaired, even opposite his own house. And next to him Hatuth the son of Hasbanam repaired.

**3:11** And second, Malchijah the son of Heram and Hashub the son of Phaab-moab repaired, even as far as the Tower of the Nathureim.

**3:12** And next to him Shallum the son of Heliah, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired, he and his daughters.

**3:13** The Valley Gate was repaired by Hanun and the inhabitants of Zano; they built it, and set up its doors, and its locks, and its bars, and a thousand cubits on the wall as far as the Dung Gate.

**3:14** And the Dung Gate was repaired by Malchijah the son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-acham, he and his sons; and they covered it, and set up its doors, and its locks, and its bars.

**3:15** And the wall of the Pool of the Fleeces, by the king's shearing, and as far as the stairs that go down from the City of David.

**3:16** After him Nehemiah the son of Azabuch, ruler of the district of Besor, repaired as far as the garden of the tomb of David, and as far as the pool that was made, and as far as Beth-abareim.

**3:17** After him the Levites repaired, Basuth the son of Bani. Next to him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, repaired for his district.

**3:18** After him their brethren repaired, Bedei the son of Henadalat, ruler of half the district of Keilah.

**3:19** And next to him Azur the son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired a second measure of the tower of the ascent that joins the corner.

**3:20** After him Baruch the son of Zabu repaired a second measure, from the corner to the door of Beth-elishub the high priest.

**3:21** After him Meramoth the son of Uriah, the son of Hakob, repaired a second measure, from the door of Beth-elishub to the end of Beth-elishub.

**3:22** And after him the priests, the men of Hachechar, repaired.

**3:23** And after him Benjamin and Hashub repaired opposite their house. After him Azariah the son of Madasel, the son of Ananiah, repaired beside his own house.

**3:24** After him Bani the son of Henadad repaired a second measure, from Beth-azariah to the corner and to the turning.

**3:25** Palal the son of Euei repaired opposite the corner, and the upper tower that projects from the king's house, which belongs to the court of the guard. And after him Pedaiah the son of Phoros.

**3:26** Now the Katheneim were dwelling in Ophel, as far as the garden of the Water Gate toward the east, and the projecting tower.

**3:27** After him the Tekoites repaired a second measure, opposite the great projecting tower, and as far as the wall of Ophel.

**3:28** Above the Horse Gate the priests repaired, each man opposite his own house.

**3:29** After him Zadok the son of Immer repaired opposite his own house. And after him Shemaiah the son of Echeniah, the keeper of the house of the east, repaired.

**3:30** After him Hananiah the son of Telemiah, and Hanum the sixth son of Sele, repaired a second measure. After him Meshullam the son of Berechiah repaired opposite his treasury.

**3:31** After him Malchijah the son of Saraphei repaired as far as Beth-anatheim, and the merchants, opposite the Gate of Maphekad, and as far as the middle of the turning.

**3:32** And in the midst of the ascent of the Sheep Gate, the coppersmiths and the merchants repaired.

**3:33** And it came to pass, when Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, that it seemed grievous to him, and he was exceedingly angry, and he mocked the Jews.

**3:34** And he said before his brethren, "Is this the army of Samaria, that these Jews are building their own city?"

**3:35** And Tobiah the Ammonite came beside him, and they said to one another, "Will they sacrifice or eat in their place? Will not a fox go up and tear down their wall of stones?"

**3:36** Hear, O our God, for we have become an object of derision; and turn their reproach upon their own head, and give them over to derision in a land of captivity,

**3:37** and do not cover over iniquity.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Greek leaves the Hebrew word for "their nobles" untranslated as Adoreem; similarly, Greek transliterates several place and group names in this chapter (Isana in 3:6, Nathureim in 3:11, Hachechar in 3:22, and Katheneim in 3:26).
- **3:15** — The Greek text omits the first part of the verse and renders the Hebrew "Pool of Shelah by the king's garden" as "the Pool of the Fleeces, by the king's shearing."
- **3:34** — Greek places "the army of Samaria" inside Sanballat's taunt rather than as part of his audience, and presents a shorter speech than the Hebrew (verses 3:33-37 correspond to 4:1-5 in most English Bibles).
