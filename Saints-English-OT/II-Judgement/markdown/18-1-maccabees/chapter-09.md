## Text

**9:1** And Demetrius heard that Nicanor and his forces had waged war, and he proceeded to send Bacchides and Alcimus a second time into the land of Judah, and the right wing with them.

**9:2** And they went by the way to Gilgal, and encamped against Messaloth, which is in Arbela, and captured it, and he destroyed many lives of men.

**9:3** And in the first month of the one hundred and fifty-second year, they encamped at Jerusalem.

**9:4** And they departed and marched to Berea with twenty thousand men and two thousand horsemen.

**9:5** Now Judas had encamped at Alasa, and three thousand chosen men were with him.

**9:6** And they saw the multitude of the forces, that they were many, and they feared exceedingly; and many slipped away from the camp, so that none of them remained except eight hundred men.

**9:7** And Judas saw that his army had melted away, and the battle pressed hard upon him; and he was broken in heart, because he had no time to gather them together.

**9:8** And he grew faint, and said to those who remained, "Let us arise and go up against our adversaries, if perhaps we may be able to fight against them."

**9:9** And they tried to turn him back, saying, "We shall by no means be able; rather, let us save our own lives for the present; let us return with our brethren and fight against them, for we are few."

**9:10** And Judas said, "Far be it from me to do this thing, to flee from them! And our time has drawn near; let us die with valor for the sake of our brethren, and let us not leave any reproach upon our glory."

**9:11** And the army moved out from the camp, and they stood ready to meet them; and the cavalry was divided into two parts, and the slingers and the archers went before the army, along with the foremost fighters, all the mighty men.

**9:12** Now Bacchides was on the right wing, and the phalanx drew near from both sides, and they sounded with the trumpets; and those who were with Judas also blew the trumpets themselves.

**9:13** And the earth shook because of their armies, and the battle was joined from morning until evening.

**9:14** And Judas saw that Bacchides and the main strength of the army were on the right side, and all who were courageous in heart rallied to him.

**9:15** And the right wing was crushed by them, and he pursued after them as far as Mount Azotus.

**9:16** And those on the left wing saw that the right wing had been crushed, and they turned back close upon the heels of Judas and those who were with him from behind.

**9:17** And the battle grew fierce, and many fell wounded on the one side and on the other.

**9:18** And Judas fell, and the rest fled.

**9:19** And Jonathan and Simon took up Judas his brother, and buried him in the tomb of his fathers in Modein.

**9:20** And they wept for him there, and all Israel lamented for him with a great lamentation, and they mourned many days and said,

**9:21** "How has the mighty one fallen, who saved Israel!"

**9:22** Now the rest of the acts of Judas, and of the wars, and of the valiant deeds which he performed, and of his greatness, were not recorded; for they were exceedingly many.

**9:23** And it came to pass after the death of Judas that the lawless emerged in all the borders of Israel, and all the workers of iniquity sprang up.

**9:24** In those days there came an exceedingly great famine, and the land went over to their side.

**9:25** And Bacchides chose the ungodly men and appointed them masters of the land.

**9:26** And they sought out and searched diligently for the friends of Judas, and brought them to Bacchides; and he took vengeance on them, and they mocked them.

**9:27** And there was great tribulation in Israel, such as had not been since the day that no prophet appeared to them.

**9:28** And all the friends of Judas assembled together and said to Jonathan,

**9:29** "Since your brother Judas has died, there is no man like him to go forth against the enemies and Bacchides, and against those who are hostile to our nation.

**9:30** Now therefore we have chosen you this day to be in his place our ruler and leader, to fight our battle."

**9:31** And Jonathan accepted the leadership at that time, and rose up in place of Judas his brother.

**9:32** And Bacchides learned of it, and sought to slay him.

**9:33** And Jonathan and Simon his brother and all who were with him learned of it, and they fled into the wilderness of Tekoa, and encamped by the water of the cistern of Asphal.

**9:34** And Bacchides learned of it on the Sabbath day, and he and all his army came across the Jordan.

**9:35** And he sent his brother, the leader of the multitude, and appealed to the Nabataeans, his friends, to entrust to them their abundant baggage.

**9:36** And the sons of Jambrin came out from Medeba, and seized John and all that he had, and departed with them.

**9:37** Now after these things they reported to Jonathan and Simon his brother, saying, "The sons of Jambrin are celebrating a great wedding, and are bringing the bride from Nadabath, a daughter of one of the great nobles of Canaan, with a great escort."

**9:38** And they remembered John their brother, and went up and hid themselves under the cover of the mountain.

**9:39** And they lifted up their eyes and looked, and behold, there was a tumult and much baggage; and the bridegroom came out, along with his friends and his brothers, to meet them with timbrels and musical instruments and many weapons.

**9:40** And they rose up against them from the ambush and slew them; and many fell wounded, and the rest fled into the mountain, and they took all their goods.

**9:41** And the wedding was turned into mourning, and the sound of their musical instruments into lamentation.

**9:42** And they executed vengeance for the blood of their brother, and returned to the mountain of the Jordan.

**9:43** And Bacchides heard of it, and came on the Sabbath day as far as the banks of the Jordan with a great force.

**9:44** And Jonathan said to his brethren, "Let us arise now and fight for our lives; for today is not like yesterday and the day before.

**9:45** For behold, the battle is before us and behind us; and the water of the Jordan is on this side and on that side, with marsh and thicket, and there is no place to turn aside.

**9:46** Now therefore cry out to heaven, that you may be delivered from the hand of our enemies."

**9:47** And the battle was joined; and Jonathan stretched out his hand to strike Bacchides, but he turned aside backward away from him.

**9:48** Then Jonathan and those who were with him leaped into the Jordan and swam across to the other side, but they did not cross the Jordan against them.

**9:49** And about one thousand men on the side of Bacchides fell on that day.

**9:50** And they returned to Jerusalem, and built fortified cities in Judea: the stronghold that is in Jericho, and Emmaus, and Beth Horon, and Bethel, and Thamnatha Pharathon, and Tephon, with high walls and gates and bars.

**9:51** And they placed a garrison in them to harass Israel.

**9:52** And they fortified the city, and Beth-zur, and Gazara, and the citadel, and placed forces in them and stores of provisions.

**9:53** And they took the sons of the leaders of the land as hostages, and placed them in the citadel in Jerusalem under guard.

**9:54** And in the one hundred and fifty-third year, in the second month, Alcimus commanded to tear down the wall of the inner court of the sanctuary; and he tore down the works of the prophets, and began to tear it down.

**9:55** At that time Alcimus was stricken, and his works were hindered; and his mouth was stopped, and he was paralyzed, and he was no longer able to speak a word or give command concerning his house.

**9:56** And Alcimus died at that time with great torment.

**9:57** And Bacchides saw that Alcimus had died, and he returned to the king; and the land of Judah had rest for two years.

**9:58** Then all the lawless took counsel together, saying, "Behold, Jonathan and those with him dwell in quietness and confidence; now therefore we will bring Bacchides, and he will seize them all in one night."

**9:59** And they went and took counsel with him.

**9:60** And he set out to come with a great force, and he sent letters secretly to all his allies who were in Judea, that they should seize Jonathan and those with him; but he was not able, because their plot became known to them.

**9:61** And they seized about fifty men from among the men of the country who were leaders of the wickedness, and he slew them.

**9:62** Then Jonathan and Simon and those with him withdrew to Bethbasi, which is in the wilderness, and he rebuilt its ruined parts and fortified it.

**9:63** And Bacchides learned of it, and gathered together all his multitude, and sent orders to those from Judea.

**9:64** And he came and encamped against Bethbasi, and fought against it many days, and made siege engines.

**9:65** And Jonathan left Simon his brother in the city, and went out into the country, and went with a small number.

**9:66** And he struck Odomera and his brothers and the sons of Phasiron in their encampment, and he began to strike and to go up with the forces.

**9:67** And Simon and those with him came out from the city and burned the siege engines.

**9:68** And they fought against Bacchides, and he was crushed by them; and they pressed him exceedingly hard, because his counsel and his assault were in vain.

**9:69** And they were enraged with wrath against the lawless men who had counseled him to come into the country, and they slew many of them; and he resolved to depart to his own land.

**9:70** And Jonathan learned of this, and sent ambassadors to him to make peace with him and to restore the captives to them.

**9:71** And he accepted, and did according to his words, and swore to him that he would not seek harm against him all the days of his life.

**9:72** And he restored to him the captives whom he had previously taken captive from the land of Judah; and he turned back and departed to his own land, and did not come again into their borders.

**9:73** And the sword ceased from Israel; and Jonathan dwelt in Michmash, and Jonathan began to judge the people, and he destroyed the ungodly out of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek text here reads "that Nicanor and his forces had waged war" (following Codex Sinaiticus), whereas other manuscripts read "had fallen in battle."
- **9:10** — The Greek text omits the conditional particle "if," reading directly "And our time has drawn near" rather than "And if our time has drawn near."
- **9:19** — The Greek text reads singular "his brother" after the compound subject "Jonathan and Simon," whereas other witnesses read plural "their brother."
- **9:33** — The Greek text reads "Asphal," whereas other manuscripts read "Asphar" (similarly in v. 5 the Greek reads "Alasa" for "Elasa").
- **9:35** — The implied subject shifts back from Bacchides (v. 34) to Jonathan, whose brother is identified as John in v. 36; in vv. 36–37, the Greek reads "Jambrin" where other witnesses read "Jambri."
- **9:42** — The Greek text reads "the mountain of the Jordan," whereas other manuscripts read "the marsh of the Jordan."
- **9:50** — In vv. 50–53 the Greek text uses plural verbs (referring to Bacchides and his forces) where other witnesses use singular verbs; "Thamnatha Pharathon" appears without a conjunction between the two names, and "Emmaus" renders the Greek variant form Emmaoum.
- **9:65** — "With a small number" renders the literal Greek "by number" (arithmo), a Hebraism indicating a small, easily counted force.
- **9:69** — The Greek text shifts from plural verbs ("they were enraged" and "they slew") to the singular "he resolved" (Bacchides), whereas other witnesses use singular verbs throughout.
