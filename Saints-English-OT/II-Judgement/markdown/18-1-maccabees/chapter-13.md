## Text

**13:1** Now Simon heard that Trypho had gathered a great army to come into the land of Judah to destroy it.

**13:2** And he saw that the people were trembling and terrified; so he went up to Jerusalem and assembled the people.

**13:3** And he encouraged them, and said to them, "You yourselves know all the things that I and my brothers and the house of my father have done for the laws and the sanctuary, and the wars and the distresses.

**13:4** For this cause all my brothers have perished for the sake of Israel, and I alone have been left.

**13:5** And now far be it from me to spare my own life in any time of affliction, for I am no better than my brothers.

**13:6** Rather, I will avenge my nation and the sanctuary and our wives and our children, because all the nations have gathered together to destroy us out of hatred."

**13:7** And the spirit of his people was rekindled upon hearing these words;

**13:8** and they answered with a loud voice, saying, "You are our leader in place of Judas and Jonathan your brother.

**13:9** Fight our battle, and all that you say to us we will do."

**13:10** So he gathered all the men of war, and made haste to finish the walls of Jerusalem, and fortified it all around.

**13:11** And he sent Jonathan the son of Absalom, and with him a considerable force, to Joppa; and he drove out those who were in it, and remained there in it.

**13:12** Then Trypho departed from Ptolemais with a great army to enter the land of Judah, and Jonathan was with him under guard.

**13:13** But Simon encamped in Adida, facing the plain.

**13:14** And Trypho learned that Simon had risen up in place of Jonathan his brother, and that he was about to engage him in battle; so he sent envoys to him, saying,

**13:15** "On account of the silver which Jonathan your brother owed to the royal treasury, because of the offices he held, we are holding him in custody.

**13:16** And now send one hundred talents of silver and two of his sons as hostages, so that when he is released he will not revolt against us, and we will release him."

**13:17** And Simon knew that they were speaking to him with deceit, yet he sent the silver and the boys, lest he should incur great hostility with the people,

**13:18** saying, "Because I did not send him the silver and the boys, he perished."

**13:19** So he sent the boys and the one hundred talents; but he broke his word, and did not release Jonathan.

**13:20** And after these things Trypho came to invade the city and destroy it, and he marched around by the road to Adora; and Simon and his army marched along opposite him to every place wherever he went.

**13:21** Now those from the citadel kept sending messengers, urging him to come to him through the wilderness and to send them provisions.

**13:22** So Trypho prepared all his cavalry to come; but in that night there was exceedingly heavy snow, and he did not come because of the snow; so he departed and came into Gilead.

**13:23** And when he drew near to Bascama, he slew Jonathan, and he was buried there.

**13:24** Then Trypho turned back and departed to his own land.

**13:25** And Simon sent and took the bones of Jonathan his brother, and buried him in Modein, the city of his fathers.

**13:26** And all Israel lamented him with great lamentation, and mourned for him many days.

**13:27** And Simon built a monument over the tomb of his father and his brothers, and raised it high to be seen, with polished stone both behind and before.

**13:28** And he set up seven pyramids, one opposite another, for his father and his mother and his four brothers.

**13:29** And for these he made elaborate devices, setting great pillars all around them; and upon the pillars he made suits of armor for an everlasting name, and beside the suits of armor, carved ships, so that they might be seen by all who sail the sea.

**13:30** This is the tomb which he made in Modein, which stands to this day.

**13:31** Now Trypho dealt treacherously with Antiochus the younger king, and slew him,

**13:32** and reigned in his place, and put on the diadem of Asia, and brought great calamity upon the land.

**13:33** And Simon built up the stronghold of Judea, and walled it around with high towers and great walls and towers and gates and bars, and stored provisions in the strongholds.

**13:34** And Simon chose men and sent them to King Demetrius to grant relief to the country, because all the deeds of Trypho were plunder.

**13:35** And King Demetrius sent word to him according to these words, and answered him, and wrote to him a letter as follows:

**13:36** "King Demetrius to Simon the high priest and friend of kings, and to the elders and nation of the Jews: Greetings.

**13:37** We have received the golden crown and the palm branch which you sent, and we are ready to make a great peace with you, and to write to the officials in charge of affairs to grant you remissions.

**13:38** And whatever we have confirmed to you stands firm, and let the strongholds which you have built belong to you.

**13:39** Furthermore, we remit to you any oversights and offenses up to this present day, and the crown which you owed; and if any other tax was collected in Jerusalem, let it no longer be collected.

**13:40** And if any among you are qualified to be enrolled among our attendants, let them be enrolled, and let there be peace between us."

**13:41** In the one hundred and seventieth year the yoke of the Gentiles was lifted from Israel.

**13:42** And the people of Israel began to write in their documents and contracts, "In the first year of Simon, the great high priest and commander and leader of the Jews."

**13:43** In those days he encamped against Gaza, and surrounded it with encampments; and he built a siege engine and brought it up to the city, and struck one tower and captured it.

**13:44** And those who were in the siege engine leaped out into the city, and there was a great commotion in the city.

**13:45** And those in the city went up with their wives and children upon the wall, having torn their garments, and cried out with a loud voice, pleading with Simon to grant them the right hand of peace.

**13:46** And they said, "Do not deal with us according to our wicked deeds, but according to your mercy."

**13:47** So Simon came to terms with them, and did not fight against them; yet he expelled them from the city, and cleansed the houses in which the idols were, and thus he entered into it singing hymns and offering blessing.

**13:48** And he cast out of it all uncleanness, and settled in it men who observe the law; and he fortified it still more, and built for himself a dwelling place in it.

**13:49** Now those from the citadel in Jerusalem were prevented from going out into the country to buy and to sell; and they hungered exceedingly, and a great number of them perished by famine.

**13:50** Then they cried out to Simon to receive the right hand of peace, and he granted it to them; and he expelled them from there, and cleansed the citadel from its defilements.

**13:51** And he entered into it on the twenty-third day of the second month, in the one hundred and seventy-first year, with praise and palm branches, and with lyres and with cymbals and with harps, and with hymns and with songs, because a great enemy had been crushed out of Israel.

**13:52** And he ordained that they should observe this day every year. And he fortified still more the mountain of the temple which was beside the citadel, and he dwelt there, he and those who were with him.

**13:53** And Simon saw that John his son was a grown man, and he appointed him commander of all the forces; and he dwelt in Gazara.

## Translator Notes

- **13:18** — The Greek text reads the first-person verb "I did not send," reflecting a shift in perspective within Simon's thought; other Greek manuscripts read the third-person "he did not send."
- **13:21** — The Greek text reads the singular "to him," likely an early transcriptional variant for the plural "to them," referring to the garrison in the citadel.
- **13:39** — "The crown" here refers to the crown tax, a customary monetary tribute owed to the Seleucid king.
- **13:43** — The Greek text reads "Gaza," whereas verse 53 names "Gazara"; many interpreters regard "Gaza" here as a textual variant for Gazara.
