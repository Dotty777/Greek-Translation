## Text

**15:1** And Antiochus, the son of King Demetrius, sent letters from the islands of the sea to Simon, priest and ethnarch of the Jews, and to all the nation.

**15:2** And their contents were after this manner: "King Antiochus to Simon, high priest and ethnarch, and to the nation of the Jews, greetings.

**15:3** Whereas certain pestilent men have seized control of the kingdom of our fathers, I purpose to lay claim to the kingdom, that I may restore it as it was before; and I have enlisted a multitude of mercenary forces and fitted out ships of war.

**15:4** Moreover, I intend to disembark into the land, that I may execute vengeance upon those who have ruined our country and those who have laid waste many cities in the kingdom.

**15:5** Now therefore, I confirm to you all the remissions that the kings before me remitted to you, and whatever other remissions they remitted to you;

**15:6** to strike your own stamp as coinage for your country;

**15:7** and that Jerusalem and the sanctuary be free. And all the weapons that you have prepared, and the strongholds that you have built, of which you hold possession, shall remain yours.

**15:8** And every royal debt, and all royal dues that shall come hereafter, from this time forth and for all time, shall be remitted to you.

**15:9** And as soon as we have established our kingdom, we will honor you and your nation and the temple with great glory, so that your glory shall become manifest throughout all the earth."

**15:10** In the one hundred and seventy-fourth year, Antiochus went forth into the land of his fathers, and all the forces assembled to him, so that few were left with Trypho.

**15:11** And King Antiochus pursued him, and he came fleeing to Dor, which lies by the sea;

**15:12** for he knew that calamities had gathered together against him, and the forces had forsaken him.

**15:13** And Antiochus encamped against Dor, and with him were one hundred and twenty thousand men of war and eight thousand cavalry.

**15:14** And he surrounded the city, and the ships closed in from the sea; and he pressed the city hard from the land and from the sea, and did not permit anyone to go out or to come in.

**15:15** Now Numenius and those who were with him came from Rome, bearing letters to the kings and to the countries, in which these words were written:

**15:16** "Lucius, consul of the Romans, to King Ptolemy, greetings.

**15:17** The ambassadors of the Jews have come to us as our friends and allies, renewing the original friendship and alliance, having been sent by Simon the high priest and the people of the Jews;

**15:18** and they have brought a golden shield of five thousand minas.

**15:19** Therefore it has seemed good to us to write to the kings and to the countries, that they should not seek evil against them, nor make war against them and their cities and their lands, and that they should not ally themselves with those who make war against them.

**15:20** Moreover, we have resolved to accept the shield from them.

**15:21** If therefore any pestilent men have fled from their country to you, deliver them over to Simon the high priest, that he may punish them according to their law."

**15:22** And he wrote the same things to King Demetrius, and to Attalus, and to Arathes, and to Arsaces,

**15:23** and to all the countries, and to Sampsake, and to the Spartans, and to Delos, and to Myndus, and to Sicyon, and to Caris, and to Samos, and to Pamphylia and Lycia, and to Halicarnassus, and to Cos, and to Side, and to Aradus, and to Rhodes, and to Phaselis, and Gortyna, and Cnidus, and Cyprus, and Cyrene.

**15:24** And a copy of them he wrote to Simon the high priest.

**15:25** Now King Antiochus encamped against Dor a second time, continually bringing his forces against it and constructing siege engines, and he shut Trypho in from coming in and going out.

**15:26** And Simon sent to him two thousand chosen men to fight as his allies, together with silver and gold and abundant equipment.

**15:27** Yet he would not receive them, but annulled all that he had previously covenanted with him, and became estranged from him.

**15:28** And he sent to him Athenobius, one of his friends, to confer with him, saying, "You hold possession of Joppa and Gazara and the citadel in Jerusalem, cities of my kingdom.

**15:29** You have laid waste their borders, and inflicted great devastation upon the land, and taken dominion over many places in my kingdom.

**15:30** Now therefore, deliver up the cities that you have seized, and the tributes of the places over which you have taken dominion, in the borders outside Judea.

**15:31** Otherwise, give in place of them five hundred talents of silver, and for the destruction that you have wrought and the tributes of the cities, another five hundred talents; otherwise, they will come and make war against you."

**15:32** So Athenobius, the king's friend, came to Jerusalem, and saw the splendor of Simon, and a sideboard with vessels of gold and silver, and a great retinue; and he was astonished, and reported to them the words of the king.

**15:33** And Simon answered and said to him, "We have neither taken foreign land nor seized what belongs to others, but the inheritance of our fathers; at a certain time, however, it was unjustly held in possession by our enemies.

**15:34** But we, having the opportunity, lay firm hold upon our inheritance and that of our fathers.

**15:35** Now concerning Joppa and Gazara, you make demand: these inflicted a great blow upon the people and upon our land; for these we will give one hundred talents." And he did not answer him a word,

**15:36** but returned in anger to the king, and reported to him these words, and the splendor of Simon, and all that he had seen; and the king was filled with great wrath.

**15:37** Meanwhile, Trypho boarded a ship and fled to Orthosia.

**15:38** And the king appointed Cendebeus commander-in-chief of the coastal region, and gave him forces of infantry and cavalry.

**15:39** And he commanded him to encamp facing Judea; and he commanded him to build up Kedron and to fortify the cities, and to wage war against the people; while the king pursued Trypho.

**15:40** So Cendebeus came to Jamnia, and began to provoke the people, and to invade Judea, and to take the people captive and slay them.

**15:41** And he built up Kedron, and stationed horsemen and forces there, that they might go forth and raid the roads of Judea, just as the king had directed him.

## Translator Notes

- **15:6** — The Greek text omits the introductory verb 'and I permit you' found in some manuscripts, continuing the royal grant directly from verse 5.
- **15:18** — The Greek text reads 'five thousand minas' (pentakischilion), whereas many other manuscripts read 'one thousand minas' (compare 14:24).
- **15:22** — 'Arathes' reflects the Greek text's shortened spelling for Ariarathes V, king of Cappadocia.
- **15:23** — 'Sampsake' and 'Caris' reflect the spellings in the Greek text, generally identified with Lampsacus (or Sampsames) and Caria, respectively.
- **15:31** — The Greek text reads the third-person plural 'they will come and make war' (ekpolemesousin) rather than the first-person plural found in some witnesses; similarly in 15:39 it reads 'the cities' (tas poleis) rather than 'the gates.'
