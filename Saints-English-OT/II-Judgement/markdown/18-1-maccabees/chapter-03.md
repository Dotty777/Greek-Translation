## Text

**3:1** Then his son Judas, who was called Maccabeus, arose in his place.

**3:2** And all his brothers helped him, and all who had joined themselves to his father, and they fought the battle of Israel with gladness.

**3:3** And he extended glory for his people; he put on a breastplate like a giant and girded on his weapons of war; he waged battles, shielding the camp with the sword.

**3:4** And he was like a lion in his deeds, and like a young lion roaring for prey.

**3:5** And he pursued the lawless, searching them out, and those who troubled his people he consumed with flames.

**3:6** And the lawless shrank back in fear of him, and all the workers of lawlessness were thrown into confusion, and deliverance prospered in his hand.

**3:7** And he embittered many kings, and made Jacob glad by his deeds, and forever his memorial is for a blessing.

**3:8** And he passed through the cities of Judah, and utterly destroyed the ungodly from it, and turned away wrath from Israel.

**3:9** And he was renowned to the farthest end of the earth, and he gathered together those who were perishing.

**3:10** Then Apollonius gathered Gentiles, and from Samaria a great army, to wage war against Israel.

**3:11** And Judas learned of it, and went out to meet him, and struck him down and slew him; and many fell wounded, and the rest fled.

**3:12** And they took their spoils, and Judas took the sword of Apollonius, and he fought with it all his days.

**3:13** Now Seron, the commander of the army of Syria, heard that Judas had gathered a company and an assembly of faithful men with him, and of those going out to war.

**3:14** And he said, "I will make a name for myself, and I will be glorified in the kingdom, and I will wage war against Judas and those with him, and those who despise the word of the king."

**3:15** And he proceeded, and a strong host of the ungodly went up with him to help him, to execute vengeance upon the children of Israel.

**3:16** And he drew near as far as the ascent of Methoron; and Judas went out to meet him with very few men.

**3:17** And when they saw the host coming to meet him, they said to Judas, "How shall we, being so few, be able to fight against so great a multitude? And we are faint, having eaten nothing today."

**3:18** And Judas said, "It is easy for many to be shut up in the hands of a few; and there is no difference in the sight of Heaven to save by many or by few.

**3:19** For victory in battle does not rest in the multitude of an army, but rather strength is from Heaven.

**3:20** They come against us in an abundance of insolence and lawlessness, to destroy us and our wives and our children, to plunder us.

**3:21** But we fight for our lives and our laws.

**3:22** And He Himself will crush them before our face; and as for you, do not be afraid of them."

**3:23** Now when he ceased speaking, he rushed upon them suddenly, and Seron and his host were crushed before him.

**3:24** And they pursued them down the descent of Beth-horon as far as the plain; and about eight hundred men from among them fell, and the rest fled into the land of the Philistines.

**3:25** And the fear of Judas and his brothers began, and terror fell upon the nations all around them.

**3:26** And his name reached as far as the king, and every nation recounted the battles of Judas.

**3:27** Now when King Antiochus heard these reports, he burned with wrath; and he sent and gathered all the forces of his kingdom, an exceedingly strong host.

**3:28** And he opened his treasury, and gave pay to his forces for the year; and he commanded them to be ready for a year for every need.

**3:29** And he saw that the silver had failed from the treasuries, and that the tributes of the country were small because of the dissension and calamity which he had brought about in the land, to abolish the laws which had been from the earliest days.

**3:30** And he feared lest he might not have enough, as once and twice before, for the expenses and the gifts which he had formerly given with a lavish hand,

**3:31** And he was exceedingly perplexed in his soul; and he resolved to go into Persia, and to collect the tributes of the countries, and to gather much silver.

**3:32** And he left Lysias, an honorable man and of royal lineage, over the affairs of the king, from the river Euphrates to the borders of Egypt,

**3:33** and to bring up his son Antiochus until he returned.

**3:34** And he delivered to him half of the forces and the elephants, and gave him orders concerning all that he desired, and concerning all the inhabitants of Judea and Jerusalem,

**3:35** to send an army against them, to cast out and destroy the strength of Israel and the remnant of Jerusalem, and to remove its memorial from the place,

**3:36** and to settle foreigners within all their borders, and to distribute their land by lot.

**3:37** So the king took the remaining half of the forces and departed from Antioch, the city of his kingdom, in the one hundred and forty-seventh year; and he crossed the Euphrates River and passed through the upper countries.

**3:38** Then Lysias chose Ptolemy the son of Dorymenes, Nicanor, and Gorgias, mighty men among the friends of the king.

**3:39** And he sent with them forty thousand men and seven thousand horsemen, to go into the land of Judah and destroy it according to the word of the king.

**3:40** So they set out with all their army and came, and encamped near Emmaus in the plain country.

**3:41** And the merchants of the country heard of their fame, and took very much silver and gold and servants, and came into the camp to take the children of Israel as servants; and forces of Syria and of the land of the Philistines joined them.

**3:42** Now Judas and his brothers saw that evils had multiplied, and that the armies would encamp within their borders; and they learned of the words of the kingdom which he had commanded to do to the people for destruction and utter ruin.

**3:43** And each one said to his neighbor, "Let us raise up the ruin of our people and of the sanctuary."

**3:44** And the congregation gathered together to be ready for battle, and to pray and ask for mercy and compassion.

**3:45** Now Jerusalem lay uninhabited like a wilderness; there was none of her offspring going in or going out; and the sanctuary was trampled down, and sons of foreigners were in the citadel, a lodging place among the Gentiles; and joy was taken away from Jacob, and the flute and the harp ceased.

**3:46** So they gathered together and came to Mizpah, opposite Jerusalem, because Mizpah had formerly been a place of prayer for Israel.

**3:47** And they fasted on that day, and put on sackcloth and put ashes upon their heads, and tore their garments.

**3:48** And they unrolled the book of the law, concerning those things about which the Gentiles inquired of the likenesses of their idols.

**3:49** And they brought the garments of the priesthood, and the firstfruits, and the tithes; and they raised up the Nazirites who had completed their days;

**3:50** and they cried aloud toward Heaven, saying, "What shall we do with these, and where shall we take them?

**3:51** And Your sanctuary has been trampled down and profaned, and Your priests are in mourning and humiliation.

**3:52** And behold, the Gentiles are gathered together against us to destroy us; You know what they devise against us.

**3:53** How shall we be able to stand before their face, unless You help us?"

**3:54** Then they sounded the trumpets and cried out with a loud voice.

**3:55** And after this Judas appointed leaders of the people: commanders of thousands, and commanders of hundreds, and commanders of fifties, and commanders of tens.

**3:56** And he told those who were building a house, and those who were betrothed to wives, and those who were planting vineyards, and those who were fearful, that each one should return to his own house according to the law.

**3:57** Then the host moved out, and they encamped to the south of Emmaus.

**3:58** And Judas said, "Gird yourselves, and be valiant men; and be ready in the morning to fight against these Gentiles who are assembled against us to destroy us and our sanctuary.

**3:59** For it is better for us to die in battle than to look upon the calamities of our nation and of the sanctuary.

**3:60** Yet as the will in Heaven may be, so He will do."

## Translator Notes

- **3:15** — The verb "proceeded" (Greek prosetheto) reflects a Septuagint Hebraism (from Hebrew yasaf, literally "added"), signifying either that Seron proceeded onward or that a host joined him.
- **3:16** — The Greek text here reads "Methoron" (Methoron), a textual variant for "Beth-horon" (Baithoron), which appears in verse 24.
- **3:18** — Throughout 1 Maccabees, "Heaven" (ho ouranos) serves as a reverent metonymy for God, who is then referred to as "He Himself" in verse 22.
- **3:30** — The idiom "as once and twice" means "as on former occasions," and the Greek sentence continues directly into verse 31.
- **3:41** — The Greek text here reads "servants" (paidas), whereas some other manuscripts read "fetters" (pedas) for binding captives.
- **3:42** — The Greek text reads "the words of the kingdom" (tes basileias), referring to the royal decrees, where other manuscripts read "of the king" (tou basileos).
- **3:43** — This Greek text preserves the shorter reading without the additional clause "and let us fight for our people" found in some manuscripts.
- **3:48** — A compressed Greek clause indicating that the Israelites consulted the book of the law for guidance in contrast to how the Gentiles sought direction from the images of their idols.
