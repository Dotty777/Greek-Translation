## Text

**14:1** Now in the one hundred and seventy-second year, King Demetrius gathered his forces and marched into Media to obtain help for himself, that he might make war against Trypho.

**14:2** And Arsaces, the king of Persia and Media, heard that Demetrius had entered his borders, and he sent one of his commanders to capture him alive.

**14:3** And he went forth and struck down the army of Demetrius, and seized him and brought him to Arsaces, who placed him in prison.

**14:4** And the land had rest all the days of Simon; he sought what was good for his nation, and his authority and his glory pleased them all those days.

**14:5** And along with all his glory, he took Joppa for a harbor and made an entrance to the islands of the sea.

**14:6** He enlarged the borders for his nation and gained mastery over the country.

**14:7** He gathered many captives and held dominion over Gazara, Beth Zur, and the citadel; he removed the defilements from it, and there was none who opposed him.

**14:8** They tilled their land in peace, and the earth yielded its produce, and the trees of the plains their fruit.

**14:9** Elders sat in the broad streets, all speaking together of good things, and the young men put on splendor and garments of war.

**14:10** He supplied provisions for the cities and equipped them with instruments of fortification, until the name of his glory was renowned to the end of the earth.

**14:11** He established peace upon the land, and Israel rejoiced with great gladness.

**14:12** And each man sat beneath his vine and his fig tree, and there was none to terrify them.

**14:13** No one remained in the land to make war against them, and their kings were crushed in those days.

**14:14** He upheld all the humble of his people; he diligently sought the law, and removed every lawless and wicked man.

**14:15** He glorified the sanctuary and multiplied the vessels of the sanctuary.

**14:16** Now it was heard in Rome, and even as far as Sparta, that Jonathan had died, and they were deeply grieved.

**14:17** But when they heard that Simon his brother had become high priest in his place, and that he held dominion over the country and the cities in it,

**14:18** they wrote to him on bronze tablets to renew with him friendship and the alliance which they had established with Judas and Jonathan his brothers.

**14:19** And these were read before the assembly in Jerusalem.

**14:20** And this is the copy of the letters which the Spartans sent: "The rulers and the city of the Spartans, to Simon the great priest, and to the elders, and to the priests, and to the rest of the people of the Jews, our brethren: greetings.

**14:21** The ambassadors who were sent to our people reported to us concerning your glory and honor, and we rejoiced at their coming.

**14:22** And we have recorded the things spoken by them in the decrees of the people as follows: 'Numenius the son of Antiochus and Antipater the son of Jason, ambassadors of the Jews, came to us to renew their friendship with us.

**14:23** And it pleased the people to receive the men with honor, and to place the copy of their words in the designated records of the people, that the people of the Spartans might have a memorial; and they wrote a copy of these things to Simon the high priest.'"

**14:24** After these things, Simon sent Numenius to Rome bearing a great golden shield of the weight of one thousand minas, to confirm the alliance with them.

**14:25** Now when the people heard these words, they said, "What thanks shall we render to Simon and his sons?

**14:26** For he and his brothers and the house of his father have stood firm, and have driven away in battle the enemies of Israel from them, and have established freedom for it."

**14:27** So they inscribed a record on bronze tablets and set them upon a pillar on Mount Zion. And this is the copy of the writing: "On the eighteenth day of Elul, in the one hundred and seventy-second year—that is, the third year under Simon the high priest,

**14:28** in Saramel, in a great assembly of the priests, the people, the rulers of the nation, and the elders of the country, it was made known to us:

**14:29** Whereas wars have often arisen in the country, Simon the son of Mattathias, a descendant of the sons of Joarib, and his brothers gave themselves to peril and withstood the adversaries of their nation, that their sanctuary and the law might be upheld, and with great glory they glorified their nation.

**14:30** Jonathan gathered their nation together and became their high priest, and then was gathered to his people.

**14:31** Then their enemies purposed to invade their country, to destroy their country and to stretch out their hands against their sanctuary.

**14:32** Then Simon arose and fought for his nation; he spent much of his own wealth, furnished arms to the men of the army of his nation, and paid them wages.

**14:33** He also fortified the cities of Judea, and Beth Zur on the borders of Judea, where the arms of the enemies had formerly been, and he stationed there a garrison of Jewish men.

**14:34** He also fortified Joppa by the sea, and Gazara on the borders of Azotus, where the enemies had formerly dwelt, and settled Jews there; and whatever was needed for their restoration he placed in them.

**14:35** And the people saw the faithfulness of Simon and the glory which he purposed to bring to his nation, and they appointed him their leader and high priest because he had done all these things, and for the righteousness and the faithfulness which he kept toward his nation, and because he sought in every way to exalt his people.

**14:36** In his days it prospered in his hands that the Gentiles should be removed from their country, as well as those in the city of David in Jerusalem, who had built themselves a citadel from which they went forth and defiled the surroundings of the sanctuary and inflicted a great wound upon its purity.

**14:37** He settled Jewish men in it and fortified it for the security of the country and the city, and he raised higher the walls of Jerusalem.

**14:38** King Demetrius also confirmed to him the high priesthood in accordance with these things,

**14:39** and made him one of his friends, and honored him with great glory;

**14:40** for it had been heard that the Jews are called friends and allies and brethren by the Romans, and that they had received the ambassadors of Simon with honor;

**14:41** and that the Jews and the priests were well pleased that Simon should be their leader and high priest forever, until a faithful prophet should arise;

**14:42** and that he should be commander over them, and that he should have charge of the sanctuary, and that by his authority men should be appointed over their works, and over the country, and over the arms, and over the strongholds;

**14:43** and that he should have charge of the sanctuary, and that he should be obeyed by all, and that all documents in the country should be written in his name, and that he should be clothed in purple and wear gold.

**14:44** And it shall not be lawful for any of the people or of the priests to annul any of these things, or to contradict what shall be spoken by him, or to convene an assembly in the country without him, or to be clothed in purple, or to wear a golden buckle.

**14:45** And whoever acts contrary to these things, or annuls any of them, shall be held guilty."

**14:46** And all the people consented to establish for Simon that he should act according to these words.

**14:47** And Simon accepted, and consented to serve as high priest, and to be commander and ethnarch of the Jews and the priests, and to preside over all.

**14:48** And they commanded that this writing be placed on bronze tablets, and that they be set up within the precinct of the sanctuary in a secure place;

**14:49** and that copies of them be placed in the treasury, that Simon and his sons might have them.

## Translator Notes

- **14:28** — "Saramel" transliterates Greek Saramel, widely understood to reflect a Hebrew phrase meaning either "prince of the people of God" (a title for Simon) or "court of the people of God." The Greek verb translated "it was made known to us" is literally third-person singular active ("he made known"), used impersonally.
- **14:41** — The decree confers leadership and the high priesthood upon Simon provisionally "until a faithful prophet should arise," reflecting the view that authoritative prophecy was awaited to render a final decision (compare 1 Maccabees 4:46).
- **14:43** — The clause "and that he should have charge of the sanctuary" repeats the nearly identical clause from verse 42, preserved here in accordance with the Greek source text.
- **14:48** — The Greek text reads "in a secure place" (en topo pisto, literally "a trustworthy place"), whereas other manuscripts read "in a conspicuous place" (en topo episemo).
