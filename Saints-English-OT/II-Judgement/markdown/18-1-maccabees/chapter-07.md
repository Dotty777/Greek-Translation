## Text

**7:1** In the fifty-first year Demetrius the son of Seleucus went out from Rome, and went up with a few men to a city by the sea, and reigned there.

**7:2** And it came to pass, as he was entering into the house of the kingdom of his fathers, that the forces seized Antiochus and Lysias to bring them to him.

**7:3** And the matter became known to him, and he said, "Do not show me their faces."

**7:4** And the forces slew them, and Demetrius sat upon the throne of his kingdom.

**7:5** And all the lawless and ungodly men from Israel came to him, and Alcimus was leading them, desiring to serve as priest.

**7:6** And they accused the people before the king, saying, "Judas and his brethren have destroyed all your friends, and he has scattered us from our land.

**7:7** Now therefore send a man whom you trust, and let him go and see all the utter destruction which he has done to us and to the country of the king, and he has punished them and all who help them."

**7:8** Then the king chose Bacchides, one of the king's friends, who ruled in the region beyond the river, and was great in the kingdom and faithful to the king.

**7:9** And he sent him and the ungodly Alcimus, and they established the priesthood for him; and he commanded him to execute vengeance upon the children of Israel.

**7:10** So he departed and came with a great force into the land of Judah; and he sent messengers to Judas and to his brethren with peaceful words in deceit.

**7:11** And they did not give heed to their words, for they saw that they had come with a great force.

**7:12** And an assembly of scribes gathered together to Alcimus and Bacchides to seek what was just.

**7:13** And the Hasideans were the first among the children of Israel, and they sought peace from them;

**7:14** for they said, "A man who is a priest of the seed of Aaron has come with the forces, and he will not wrong us."

**7:15** And he spoke peaceful words with them, and swore to them, saying, "We will not seek evil against you nor against your friends."

**7:16** And they trusted him; and he seized sixty men of them, and slew them in one day, according to the words which he wrote:

**7:17** "The flesh of Your saints and their blood they poured out round about Jerusalem, and there was no one to bury them."

**7:18** Then the fear and trembling of them fell upon all the people, for they said, "There is no truth and justice in them; for they have transgressed the agreement and the oath which they swore."

**7:19** And Bacchides departed from Jerusalem and encamped in Bezeth; and he sent and seized many of the men who had deserted to him, and some of the people, and slaughtered them into the great pit.

**7:20** And he committed the country to Alcimus and left a force with him to help him; and Bacchides departed to the king.

**7:21** And Alcimus strove for the high priesthood.

**7:22** And all who troubled their people gathered to him, and they gained mastery over the land of Judah, and inflicted a great blow in Israel.

**7:23** And Judas saw all the wickedness which Alcimus and those with him had done among the children of Israel, more than the Gentiles;

**7:24** and he went out into all the borders of Judea and round about, and executed vengeance upon the men and upon those who had deserted, and they were restrained from going into the country.

**7:25** Now when Alcimus saw that Judas and those with him had grown strong, and knew that he was able to withstand them, he returned to the king and accused them of wicked things.

**7:26** Then the king sent Nicanor, one of his honored princes, who hated and was hostile to Israel, and commanded him to destroy the people.

**7:27** And Nicanor came to Jerusalem with a great force, and sent to Judas and his brethren deceitfully with peaceful words, saying,

**7:28** "Let there be no battle between me and you; I will come with a few men, that I may see your faces in peace."

**7:29** And he came to Judas, and they greeted one another peacefully; yet the enemies were ready to snatch Judas away.

**7:30** And the matter became known to Judas, that he had come against him with deceit; and he was alarmed by him, and did not wish to see his face anymore.

**7:31** And Nicanor knew that his counsel had been uncovered, and he went out to meet Judas near Charpharsarama.

**7:32** And there fell of those with Nicanor about five thousand men, and they fled into the City of David.

**7:33** And after these things Nicanor went up to Mount Zion; and some of the priests came out from the sanctuary, and some of the elders of the people, to greet him peacefully and to show him the burnt offering that was being offered on behalf of the king.

**7:34** But he mocked them, and laughed them to scorn, and defiled them, and spoke arrogantly.

**7:35** And he swore in wrath, saying, "Unless Judas and his army are delivered into my hands now, then it shall be, if I return in peace, that I will burn up this house." And he went out in wrath.

**7:36** And the priests went in and stood before the altar and the temple, and wept and said,

**7:37** "You chose this house to have Your name called upon it, to be a house of prayer and supplication for Your people.

**7:38** Execute vengeance upon this man and upon his army, and let them fall by the sword; remember their blasphemies, and do not grant them continuance."

**7:39** Then Nicanor went out from Jerusalem, and they encamped in Beth-horon, and a force of Syria met him.

**7:40** And Judas encamped in Adasa with three thousand men; and Judas prayed and said,

**7:41** "When those from the king blasphemed, Your angel went out and struck down among them one hundred and eighty-five thousand.

**7:42** So crush this army before us today, and let the rest know that he has spoken wickedly against Your sanctuary; and judge him according to his wickedness."

**7:43** And the armies joined in battle on the thirteenth day of the month of Adar, and the army of Nicanor was crushed, and he himself fell first in the battle.

**7:44** Now when his army saw that Nicanor had fallen, they threw down their weapons and fled.

**7:45** And they pursued them a day's journey from Adasa until they came to Gazara, and they blew the signal trumpets behind them.

**7:46** And they came out from all the villages of Judea round about and outflanked them; and these turned back upon those, and they all fell by the sword, and not even one of them was left.

**7:47** And they took the spoils and the plunder, and they cut off the head of Nicanor and his right hand, which he had stretched out arrogantly, and brought them and stretched them out near Jerusalem.

**7:48** And the people rejoiced exceedingly, and they kept that day as a great day of gladness.

**7:49** And they decreed to keep this day year by year on the thirteenth day of Adar.

**7:50** And the land of Judah had rest for a few days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek text reads "fifty-first year," omitting "and one hundredth" found in other manuscripts (referring to the one hundred and fifty-first year of the Seleucid era, 162-161 BC).
- **7:7** — The Greek text has the aorist indicative "he has punished" (ekolasato) rather than the imperative "let him punish" (kolasato) found in other manuscripts.
- **7:25** — The Greek text omits the negative particle, reading "that he was able to withstand them," whereas other manuscripts read "that he was not able to withstand them."
- **7:31** — The Greek text reads the place name Charpharsarama, whereas other manuscripts read Capharsalama.
- **7:32** — The Greek text reads "five thousand" (pentakischilioi), whereas other manuscripts read "five hundred" (pentakosioi).
