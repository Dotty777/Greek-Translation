## Text

**2:1** In those days Mattathias the son of John, the son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarim, arose from Jerusalem and dwelt in Modein.

**2:2** And he had five sons: John, who was surnamed Gaddis,

**2:3** Simon, who was called Thassis,

**2:4** Judas, who was called Maccabeus,

**2:5** Eleazar, who was called Avaran, and Jonathan, who was called Sapphus.

**2:6** And he saw the blasphemies that were being committed in Judah and in Jerusalem,

**2:7** and he said, "Woe is me! Why was I born for this, to see the calamities of my people and the ruin of the holy city, and to sit there when it was delivered into the hand of enemies, and the sanctuary into the hand of strangers?

**2:8** Her temple has become like a glorious man;

**2:9** the vessels of glory have been carried away captive; her infants have been slain in her streets, and her young men by the sword of the enemy.

**2:10** What nation has not inherited kingdoms and has not seized her spoils?

**2:11** All her adornment has been taken away; instead of a free woman, she has become a bondwoman.

**2:12** And behold, our sanctuary and our beauty and our glory have been laid waste, and the nations have profaned them.

**2:13** Why should we live any longer?"

**2:14** Then Mattathias and his sons tore their garments, put on sackcloth, and mourned exceedingly.

**2:15** Now the officers from the king who were enforcing the apostasy came to the city of Modein, so that they might offer sacrifice.

**2:16** And many from Israel came to them, and Mattathias and his sons gathered together.

**2:17** Then the officers from the king answered and spoke to Mattathias, saying, "You are a ruler, honorable and great in this city, and strengthened with sons and brethren.

**2:18** Now come forward first and carry out the decree of the king, as all the nations have done, and the rulers of Judah, and those who remain in Jerusalem; and you and your house shall be among the friends of the king, and you and your sons shall be honored with silver and gold and many gifts."

**2:19** And Mattathias answered and said with a loud voice, "Even if all the nations that are in the house of the king's dominion obey him, so that each one departs from the worship of his fathers, and they have chosen his commandments,

**2:20** yet I and my sons and my brethren will walk in the covenant of our fathers.

**2:21** Far be it from us to forsake the law and the ordinances.

**2:22** We will not obey the law of the king, to turn aside from our worship to the right hand or to the left."

**2:23** And when he had finished speaking these words, a Jewish man came forward in the sight of all to burn incense upon the altar in Modein, according to the decree of the king.

**2:24** And Mattathias saw it and was filled with zeal, and his inward parts trembled, and he stirred up his wrath according to the judgment; and he ran and slew him upon the altar.

**2:25** He also killed at that time the king's officer who was compelling them to sacrifice, and he tore down the altar.

**2:26** Thus he was zealous for the law, just as Phinehas did to Zimri the son of Salom.

**2:27** Then Mattathias cried out in the city with a loud voice, saying, "Everyone who is zealous for the law and maintains the covenant, let him come out after me!"

**2:28** So he and his sons fled into the mountains, and left behind all that they had in the city.

**2:29** Then many who were seeking righteousness and justice went down into the wilderness to dwell there,

**2:30** they and their sons, and their wives, and their livestock, because the evils had grown severe upon them.

**2:31** And it was reported to the king's men and to the forces that were in Jerusalem, the city of David, that men who had broken the commandment of the king had gone down into the hiding places in the wilderness.

**2:32** And many ran after them, and having overtaken them, they encamped against them and set battle in array against them on the Sabbath day.

**2:33** And they said to them, "Enough until now! Come out and do according to the word of the king, and you shall live."

**2:34** And they said, "We will not come out, nor will we do the word of the king, to profane the Sabbath day."

**2:35** Then they hastened to wage battle against them.

**2:36** And they did not answer them, nor did they hurl a stone at them, nor did they block up the hiding places,

**2:37** saying, "Let us all die in our innocence; heaven and earth bear witness for us that you destroy us without judgment."

**2:38** So they rose up against them in battle on the Sabbath; and they died, they and their wives and their children and their livestock, up to a thousand souls of men.

**2:39** And Mattathias and his friends learned of it, and they mourned over them exceedingly.

**2:40** And each man said to his neighbor, "If we all do as our brethren have done, and do not fight against the nations for our lives and our ordinances, they will now quickly destroy us from the earth."

**2:41** So they took counsel on that day, saying, "Every man who comes against us to battle on the Sabbath day, let us fight against him, and we shall by no means all die as our brethren died in the hiding places."

**2:42** Then there gathered to them a company of Hasideans, with mighty strength from Israel, everyone who offered himself willingly for the law.

**2:43** And all who were fleeing from the evils were added to them, and became a support to them.

**2:44** So they mustered a force, and struck down sinners in their anger and lawless men in their wrath; and the rest fled to the nations to be saved.

**2:45** And Mattathias and his sons went around and tore down their altars,

**2:46** and they circumcised all the uncircumcised boys whom he found within the borders of Israel, by force.

**2:47** They also pursued the sons of arrogance, and the work prospered in their hand.

**2:48** So they rescued the law out of the hand of the nations and out of the hand of the kings, and they did not give a horn to the sinner.

**2:49** Now the days of Mattathias drew near for him to die, and he said to his sons, "Now arrogance and rebuke have grown strong, and a time of overthrow and fierce wrath.

**2:50** And now, children, be zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of our fathers.

**2:51** Remember the deeds of our fathers which they did in their generations, and receive great glory and everlasting glory.

**2:52** Was not Abraham found faithful in testing, and righteousness was accounted to him?

**2:53** Joseph in the time of his distress kept the commandment, and became lord of Egypt.

**2:54** Phinehas our father, by showing great zeal, received the covenant of a holy priesthood.

**2:55** Joshua, by fulfilling the word, became a judge in Israel.

**2:56** Caleb, by bearing witness before the congregation, received a land as an inheritance.

**2:57** David, by his mercy, inherited the throne of an everlasting kingdom.

**2:58** Elijah, by showing great zeal for the law, was taken up as into heaven.

**2:59** Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael, by believing, were saved out of the flame.

**2:60** Daniel, in his innocence, was delivered from the mouth of lions.

**2:61** And thus consider from generation to generation that all who hope in Him shall not grow weak.

**2:62** And do not fear the words of a sinful man, for his glory comes to dung and to worms.

**2:63** Today he shall be lifted up, and he shall by no means be found, because he has returned to his dust, and his thought has perished.

**2:64** And you, children, be strong and act courageously in your law, for in it you shall be glorified.

**2:65** And behold, Simeon your brother—I know that he is a man of counsel; listen to him all your days; he shall be a father to you.

**2:66** And Judas Maccabeus—he has been mighty in strength from his youth; he shall be to you a commander of the army, and you shall fight the battle of the peoples.

**2:67** And you shall gather to yourselves all the doers of the law, and execute vengeance for your people.

**2:68** Render recompense to the nations, and give heed to the commandments of the law."

**2:69** Then he blessed them, and was gathered to his fathers.

**2:70** And he died in the one hundred and forty-sixth year; and his sons buried him in the tombs of his fathers in Modein, and all Israel mourned for him with great lamentation.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Greek text reads Sapphous (Sapphus) as Jonathan's surname, whereas other manuscripts read Apphous (Apphus).
- **2:8** — The Greek text reads endoxos ("glorious" or "honored"), whereas other manuscripts and ancient versions read adoxos ("inglorious" or "dishonored") in keeping with the surrounding lament.
- **2:22** — The Greek text reads ton nomon ("the law") of the king, whereas other manuscripts read ton logon ("the words") of the king.
- **2:23** — The Greek text reads thymiasai ("to burn incense"), whereas other manuscripts read thysiasai ("to offer sacrifice").
- **2:26** — The Greek text gives the name of Zimri's father as Salom, corresponding to Salu in Numbers 25:14.
- **2:51** — The Greek text repeats doxan ("glory"), reading "great glory and everlasting glory," whereas other manuscripts read "great glory and an everlasting name."
- **2:54** — The Greek text reads hierosynes hagias ("a holy priesthood"), whereas other manuscripts read hierosynes aionias ("an everlasting priesthood").
- **2:63** — The Greek text omits "and tomorrow" before "he shall by no means be found," which appears in other manuscripts.
- **2:66** — The Greek text reads the second-person plural polemesete ("you shall fight") and plural laon ("of the peoples"), whereas other manuscripts read "he shall fight the battle of the people."
