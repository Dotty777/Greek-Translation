## Text

**5:1** Now it came to pass, when the nations round about heard that the altar had been built and the sanctuary dedicated as before, that they were exceedingly angry.

**5:2** And they took counsel to destroy the race of Jacob who were in their midst, and they began to slay among the people and to cut them off.

**5:3** Then Judas waged war against the sons of Esau in Judea at Acrabattene, because they were besieging Israel; and he struck them with a great blow, and humbled them, and took their spoil.

**5:4** He also remembered the wickedness of the sons of Baean, who were a snare and a stumbling block to the people by lying in wait for them along the roads.

**5:5** And they were shut up by him in the towers, and he encamped against them, and devoted them to destruction, and burned its towers with fire, together with all who were in them.

**5:6** Then he crossed over against the sons of Ammon, and found a mighty force and a great host, with Timothy as their commander.

**5:7** And he engaged in many battles against them, and they were crushed before his face, and he struck them down.

**5:8** He also seized Jaze and its daughter towns, and returned to Judea.

**5:9** Now the nations in Gilead gathered together against Israel, against those who were within their borders, to destroy them; and they fled to Dathema, the stronghold.

**5:10** And they sent letters to Judas and his brethren, saying, "The nations round about us have gathered together against us to destroy us,

**5:11** and they are preparing to come and capture the stronghold to which we have fled; and Timothy is the commander of their army.

**5:12** Now therefore, come and deliver us from their hand, for a multitude of us have fallen.

**5:13** And all our brethren who were in the territory of Tubias have been put to death, and they have carried captive their wives and their children and their possessions, and have destroyed there about one thousand men."

**5:14** While the letters were still being read, behold, other messengers arrived from Galilee with their garments torn, reporting according to these words,

**5:15** saying, "Those from Ptolemais and Tyre and Sidon, and all Galilee of the foreigners, have gathered together against them to consume us."

**5:16** Now when Judas and the people heard these words, a great assembly gathered together to take counsel on what they should do for their brethren who were in distress and under attack by him.

**5:17** And Judas said to Simon his brother, "Choose men for yourself, and go and deliver your brethren in Galilee; and I and Jonathan my brother will go into Gilead."

**5:18** And he left Joseph the son of Zechariah, and Azariah, a leader of the people, with the rest of the army in Judea to guard it;

**5:19** and he commanded them, saying, "Take charge of this people, and do not engage in battle against the nations until we return."

**5:20** And three thousand men were allotted to Simon to go into Galilee, and eight thousand men to Judas for Gilead.

**5:21** So Simon went into Galilee and engaged in many battles against the nations; and the nations were crushed before his face.

**5:22** And he pursued them as far as the cities of Ptolemais; and about three thousand men of the nations fell, and he took their spoil.

**5:23** And he took them from Galilee and from Arbacta, together with their wives and children, and all that belonged to them, and they brought them into Judea with great gladness.

**5:24** Now Judas Maccabeus and Jonathan his brother crossed the Jordan and marched a three days' journey in the wilderness.

**5:25** And they met the Nabateans, who received them peaceably and recounted to them all that had happened to their brethren in Gilead:

**5:26** that many of them are shut up in Bossora and Bossor, in Alama, Casphor, Makeb, and Carnein—all these cities are fortified and great—

**5:27** and in the remaining cities of Gilead they are shut up; and for tomorrow they are set to encamp against the strongholds, and to capture and destroy all of them in one day.

**5:28** Then Judas and his army suddenly turned back by way of the wilderness to Bosor; and he captured the city, and slew every male with the edge of the sword, and took all their spoil, and burned it with fire.

**5:29** And he departed from there by night and marched as far as the stronghold.

**5:30** And when morning came, they lifted up their eyes, and behold, a great multitude without number, carrying ladders and siege engines to capture the stronghold; and they were fighting against them.

**5:31** And Judas saw that the battle had begun, and that the cry of the city went up to heaven, with trumpets and a loud shout;

**5:32** and he said to the men of the army, "Fight today for our brethren."

**5:33** Then he went out in three companies behind them; and he sounded the trumpets, and they cried out in prayer.

**5:34** And the army of Timothy recognized that it was Maccabeus, and they fled before his face, and he struck them with a great slaughter; and about eight thousand of their men fell on that day.

**5:35** Then he turned aside to Maapha and fought against it, and captured it, and slew every male in it, and took its spoil, and burned it with fire.

**5:36** From there he departed and seized Chasphoth, Maked, Bosor, and the remaining cities of Gilead.

**5:37** Now after these things Timothy gathered another army, and encamped opposite Raphon across the brook.

**5:38** And Judas sent men to spy out the camp; and they reported to him, saying, "All the nations round about you have gathered together to them, an exceedingly great force.

**5:39** And Arabs have been hired to help them, and they are encamped across the brook, ready to come against you in battle." And Judas went out to meet them.

**5:40** And Timothy said to the commanders of his army, as Judas and his army drew near to the brook of water, "If he crosses over to us first, we will not be able to withstand him, for he will surely prevail against us.

**5:41** But if he is afraid and encamps on the other side of the river, we will cross over to him."

**5:42** Now when Judas drew near to the brook of water, he stationed the scribes of the people by the brook and commanded them, saying, "Do not permit any man to encamp, but let all come into the battle."

**5:43** And he crossed over against them first, and all his people before him; and all the nations were crushed before his face, and they threw away all their weapons and fled to the sacred precinct in Carnain.

**5:44** And they seized the city, and burned the sacred precinct with fire, together with all who were in it; so Carnain was overthrown and could no longer stand before Judas.

**5:45** Then Judas gathered all Israel who were in Gilead, from the least to the greatest, together with their wives and their children and their possessions, an exceedingly great company, to come into the land of Judah.

**5:46** And they came as far as Ephron; now this was a great city at the entrance, exceedingly strong; it was not possible to turn aside from it to the right or to the left, but only to pass through the midst of it.

**5:47** And the people of the city shut them out, and blocked up the gates with stones.

**5:48** Then Judas sent to them with words of peace, saying, "I will pass through your land to go into our own land; and no one will harm you; we will only march through on foot." Yet they would not open to him.

**5:49** So Judas gave orders to proclaim in the camp that each man should encamp in the place where he was.

**5:50** And the men of the city encamped, and they fought against the city all that day and all that night, and the city was delivered into his hands.

**5:51** And he destroyed every male with the edge of the sword, and uprooted it, and took the spoil of the city, and marched through the city over the slain.

**5:52** Then they crossed the Jordan into the great plain opposite Bethshan.

**5:53** And Judas kept gathering together those who lagged behind and encouraging the people all along the way, until he came into the land of Judah.

**5:54** And they went up to Mount Zion with gladness and joy, and offered burnt offerings, because not one of them had fallen until they returned in peace.

**5:55** Now in the days when Judas and Jonathan were in Gilead, and Simon his brother was in Galilee opposite Ptolemais,

**5:56** Joseph the son of Zechariah, and Zechariah, the commanders of the forces, heard of the valiant deeds and the warfare that they had accomplished;

**5:57** and they said, "Let us also make a name for ourselves, and let us go to fight against the nations round about us."

**5:58** So they gave orders to the men of the force who were with them, and marched against Jamnia.

**5:59** And Gorgias and his men came out of the city to meet them in battle.

**5:60** And Joseph and Azariah were routed and pursued as far as the borders of Judea; and about two thousand men of the people of Israel fell on that day.

**5:61** Thus a great rout occurred among the people, because they did not listen to Judas and his brethren, supposing that he would do valiant deeds.

**5:62** But they were not of the seed of those men through whose hand the deliverance of Israel was given.

**5:63** And the man Judas and his brethren were greatly glorified before all Israel and all the nations, wherever their name was heard;

**5:64** and people gathered together to them, praising them.

**5:65** Then Judas and his brethren went forth and fought against the sons of Esau in the land toward the south, and he struck Hebron and its daughter towns; and he tore down its stronghold and burned its towers round about.

**5:66** And he departed to go into the land of the foreigners, and passed through Samaria.

**5:67** On that day priests fell in battle while he desired to act valiantly, when he went out to battle recklessly.

**5:68** Then Judas turned aside to Azotus, the land of the foreigners, and tore down their altars, and burned the carved images of their gods, and plundered the spoil of the cities, and returned to Judea.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Greek text reads "in Judea" (ἐν τῇ Ἰουδαίᾳ), whereas other witnesses read "in Idumea" (Ἰδουμαίᾳ), referring to the border region of Acrabattene.
- **5:8** — The Greek reads "Jaze" (Ἰάζην), a variant form of the Transjordanian city Jazer (Ἰαζήρ); "daughter towns" (θυγατέρας) reflects a Semitic idiom for surrounding dependent villages.
- **5:13** — Literally "in the places of Tubias" (ἐν τοῖς Τουβίου), referring to the region of Tob in Transjordan.
- **5:15** — The Greek shifts from third person ("against them," ἐπ᾽ αὐτούς) to first person ("to consume us," τοῦ ἐξαναλῶσαι ἡμᾶς), blending indirect and direct speech.
- **5:22** — The Greek text reads "the cities of Ptolemais" (τῶν πόλεων Πτολεμαίδος), whereas other manuscripts read "the gates of Ptolemais" (τῶν πυλῶν Πτολεμαίδος).
- **5:43** — The Greek text reads "before him" (ἔμπροσθεν αὐτοῦ), whereas other manuscripts read "behind him" or "after him" (ὄπισθεν αὐτοῦ).
- **5:50** — The Greek text reads "the men of the city" (οἱ ἄνδρες τῆς πόλεως), whereas other witnesses read "the men of the army" (τῆς δυνάμεως).
- **5:56** — The Greek text reads "Zechariah" (Ζαχαρίας) here, whereas other witnesses—and verses 18 and 60—read "Azariah" (Ἀζαρίας).
- **5:66** — The Greek text reads "Samaria" (Σαμαρίαν); many commentators emend this to "Marisa" (Mareshah) on geographical grounds and from Josephus.
- **5:67** — The Greek uses singular pronouns ("he," βουλομένου αὐτοῦ ... αὐτόν), whereas other witnesses read plural ("they"), referring to the priests who went out recklessly.
