## Text

**9:1** For in the twelfth month, on the thirteenth day of the month, which is Adar, the letters written by the king were at hand.

**9:2** On that same day those who opposed the Jews perished; for no one withstood them, fearing them.

**9:3** For the rulers of the satraps and the governors and the royal scribes honored the Jews, because the fear of Mordecai lay upon them.

**9:4** For the king's decree had arrived to be proclaimed throughout all the kingdom.

**9:6** And in the city itself the Jews slew five hundred men,

**9:7** and Pharsan, Nestain, Delphon, Phasga,

**9:8** Pharadatha, Barsa, Sarbacha,

**9:9** Marmasima, Ruphaeus, Arsaeus, and Zabuthaeus,

**9:10** the ten sons of Haman the son of Hammedatha the Bugaean, the enemy of the Jews; and they took plunder

**9:11** on that same day. And the number of those who had perished in Susa was reported to the king.

**9:12** Then the king said to Esther, "The Jews have destroyed five hundred men in the city of Susa; and in the surrounding region, how do you suppose they have acted? What more, then, do you request, and it shall be yours?"

**9:13** And Esther said to the king, "Let it be granted to the Jews to act in the same manner tomorrow, so as to hang the ten sons of Haman."

**9:14** And he permitted it to be done so, and he gave over to the Jews of the city the bodies of the sons of Haman to hang.

**9:15** And the Jews in Susa assembled on the fourteenth day of Adar and slew three hundred men, and they plundered nothing.

**9:16** Now the rest of the Jews who were in the kingdom assembled and defended themselves, and had rest from their enemies; for they destroyed fifteen thousand of them on the thirteenth day of Adar, and they plundered nothing.

**9:17** And they rested on the fourteenth day of that same month, and kept it as a day of rest with joy and gladness.

**9:18** But the Jews who were in the city of Susa assembled also on the fourteenth day and did not rest; and they also kept the fifteenth day with joy and gladness.

**9:19** Therefore, the Jews who are scattered throughout every outlying region keep the fourteenth day of Adar as a good day with gladness, each sending portions to his neighbor. And those who dwell in the chief cities keep also the fifteenth day of Adar as a good day of gladness, sending out portions to their neighbors as well.

**9:20** And Mordecai wrote these words in a book, and sent them out to all the Jews who were in the kingdom of Artaxerxes, to those near and those far off,

**9:21** to establish these good days, and to keep the fourteenth and the fifteenth days of Adar;

**9:22** for in these days the Jews had rest from their enemies; and as for the month in which it was turned for them, which was Adar, from mourning into joy and from sorrow into a good day, they were to keep the whole of it as good days of feasting and gladness, sending out portions to their friends and to the poor.

**9:23** And the Jews accepted what Mordecai had written to them,

**9:24** how Haman the son of Hammedatha, the Macedonian, waged war against them, as he set a decree and a lot to annihilate them,

**9:25** and how he went in to the king, speaking to hang Mordecai; yet all the evil things he undertook to bring upon the Jews came upon himself, and he was hanged, both he and his children.

**9:26** Therefore these days were called Phrourai, on account of the lots, because in their language they are called Phrourai; on account of the words of this letter, and all that they had suffered on account of these things, and all that had happened to them,

**9:27** and he established it. And the Jews took it upon themselves, and upon their seed, and upon those joined to them, nor indeed shall they ever act otherwise. And these days are a memorial celebrated generation after generation, in every city, family, and province.

**9:28** And these days of Phrourai shall be observed for all time, and their memorial shall by no means fail from the generations.

**9:29** And Queen Esther, the daughter of Aminadab, and Mordecai the Jew wrote all that they had done, and the confirmation of the letter of Phrourai.

**9:31** And Mordecai and Queen Esther established it for themselves upon themselves, establishing at that time their counsel also upon their own well-being.

**9:32** And Esther established it by word forever, and it was written for a memorial.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek wording here diverges from the Hebrew (which speaks of Mordecai's growing fame), and verse 9:5 is absent from the Septuagint.
- **9:7** — By dividing the first name into "Pharsan" and "Nestain," the Septuagint lists eleven names in 9:7-9 before calling them "the ten sons of Haman" in 9:10; in 9:10 Haman is also called "the Bugaean" (Hebrew Agagite) and the Greek omits the negative before "they took plunder."
- **9:16** — The Septuagint records "fifteen thousand" slain in the provinces, compared with seventy-five thousand in the Hebrew text.
- **9:26** — "Phrourai" is the Septuagint Greek form corresponding to Purim; in 9:29 Esther's father is likewise given the Septuagint name "Aminadab" (Hebrew Abihail).
- **9:31** — Verse 9:30 is absent from the Greek text, and the syntax of 9:31—literally establishing their counsel "upon [or against] their own well-being"—is difficult and differs notably from the Hebrew.
