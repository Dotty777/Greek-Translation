## Text

**24:1** And the Lord again caused wrath to burn against Israel, and He stirred up David against them, saying, "Go, number Israel and Judah."

**24:2** And the king said to Joab the commander of the force who was with him, "Go now through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan even to Beersheba, and count the people, and I shall know the number of the people."

**24:3** And Joab said to the king, "May the Lord God add to the people as many as they are, and as many as they are a hundredfold, and may the eyes of my lord the king see it; yet why does my lord the king desire this thing?"

**24:4** Nevertheless, the word of the king prevailed against Joab and against the commanders of the host; and Joab and the commanders of the force went out from before the king to count the people of Israel.

**24:5** And they crossed over the Jordan and encamped in Aroel, on the right side of the city that is in the midst of the valley of Gad, and Eliezer.

**24:6** And they came to Gilead and to Thabason, which is Nadasai, and they arrived at Dan Eidan and Oudan, and went around to Sidon;

**24:7** and they came to Mapsar of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivite and of the Canaanite; and they came toward the south of Judah, to Beersheba,

**24:8** and they went about through all the land; and at the end of nine months and twenty days they came to Jerusalem.

**24:9** And Joab gave the number of the registration of the people to the king; and Israel was eight hundred thousand men of might who drew the sword, and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand fighting men.

**24:10** And David's heart struck him after he had numbered the people; and David said to the Lord, "I have sinned greatly in what I have done; now, O Lord, take away, I pray, the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."

**24:11** And David arose in the morning; and the word of the Lord came to Gad the prophet, David's seer, saying,

**24:12** "Go and speak to David, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Three things I am laying upon you; choose for yourself one of them, and I will do it to you.'"

**24:13** And Gad came to David, and told him and said to him, "Choose for yourself what shall come to pass: whether three years of famine shall come upon you in your land, or three months for you to flee before your enemies while they pursue you, or that there be three days of pestilence in your land. Now therefore know and see what word I shall answer to Him who sent me."

**24:14** And David said to Gad, "I am in great distress on every side; I will fall now into the hand of the Lord, for His mercies are exceedingly many, but into the hands of man I will by no means fall."

**24:15** And David chose for himself the pestilence; and it was the days of the wheat harvest, and the Lord sent pestilence upon Israel from the morning until the hour of the midday meal; and the plague began among the people, and there died of the people from Dan even to Beersheba seventy thousand men.

**24:16** And the angel of God stretched out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy it, and the Lord relented concerning the calamity and said to the angel who was destroying among the people, "It is enough now; stay your hand." And the angel of the Lord was by the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.

**24:17** And David spoke to the Lord when he saw the angel striking among the people, and he said, "Behold, it is I who have done wrong; yet these, the sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand, I pray, be against me and against the house of my father."

**24:18** And Gad came to David on that day and said to him, "Go up and set up an altar to the Lord on the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite."

**24:19** And David went up according to the word of Gad, just as the Lord had commanded him.

**24:20** And Orna looked out and saw the king and his servants passing by above him; and Orna went out and bowed down to the king with his face to the ground.

**24:21** And Orna said, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?" And David said, "To purchase the threshing floor from you, in order to build an altar to the Lord." And the plague was restrained from upon the people.

**24:22** And Orna said to David, "Let my lord the king take and offer up to the Lord what is good in his eyes; behold, the oxen for a burnt offering, and the wheels and the gear of the oxen for wood."

**24:23** All these things Orna gave to the king; and Orna said to the king, "May the Lord your God bless you."

**24:24** And the king said to Orna, "No, for I will surely purchase it from you for a price, and I will not offer up to my Lord, my God, a burnt offering without cost." So David purchased the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

**24:25** And David built an altar there to the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings; and Solomon added to the altar afterward, because it was small at the first. And the Lord heeded the land, and the plague was restrained from upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — Verses 5-7 preserve Septuagint-distinct place names: Aroel, Eliezer, Thabason (which is Nadasai), Dan Eidan and Oudan, and Mapsar of Tyre (a transliteration of the Hebrew term for "fortress").
- **24:13** — The Greek reads "three years of famine" (matching 1 Chronicles 21:12) rather than "seven years" as in the Masoretic Text, and uses thanatos ("death/pestilence") here and in verse 15.
- **24:15** — The Greek includes clauses not in the Masoretic Text ("David chose for himself the pestilence," "the days of the wheat harvest," and "the plague began among the people") and reads "until the hour of the midday meal" (Greek horas aristou).
- **24:25** — The Septuagint calls the Jebusite Orna (verses 16-24, corresponding to Araunah) and adds the historical note here that Solomon later enlarged the altar because it was small at first.
