## Text

**6:1** And David again gathered every young man from Israel, about seventy thousand.

**6:2** And David arose and went, along with all the people who were with him from the rulers of Judah on the ascent, to bring up from there the ark of God, over which the name of the Lord of Hosts was called, who sits upon the cherubim upon it.

**6:3** And he placed the ark of the Lord upon a new cart and carried it to the house of Amminadab, who was on the hill; and Uzzah and his brothers, the sons of Amminadab, drove the cart with the ark,

**6:4** and his brothers walked before the ark.

**6:5** And David and the sons of Israel were playing before the Lord on finely tuned instruments, with might and with songs, and with lyres, and with harps, and with tambourines, and with cymbals, and with flutes.

**6:6** And they came to the threshing floor of Nodab; and Uzzah stretched out his hand to the ark of God to steady it, and he grasped it, because the calf drew him aside to take hold of it.

**6:7** And the Lord was angry with Uzzah, and God struck him there; and he died there beside the ark of the Lord before God.

**6:8** And David was disheartened because the Lord had broken out with a breach against Uzzah; and that place was called Breach of Uzzah to this day.

**6:9** And David feared the Lord on that day, saying, "How shall the ark of the Lord come to me?"

**6:10** And David was unwilling to turn aside the ark of the covenant of the Lord to himself into the City of David; so David turned it aside into the house of Abeddara the Gittite.

**6:11** And the ark of the Lord remained in the house of Abeddara the Gittite three months; and the Lord blessed all the house of Abeddara and all that was his.

**6:12** And it was reported to King David, saying, "The Lord has blessed the house of Abeddara and all that is his on account of the ark of God." So David went and brought up the ark of the Lord from the house of Abeddara to the City of David with gladness.

**6:13** And there were with them bearing the ark seven choirs, and as a sacrifice a calf and a lamb.

**6:14** And David was playing on finely tuned instruments before the Lord, and David was clothed in an extraordinary robe.

**6:15** And David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the Lord with shouting and with the sound of a trumpet.

**6:16** Now it came to pass, as the ark came to the City of David, that Michal the daughter of Saul looked down through the window and saw King David dancing and playing music before the Lord; and she despised him in her heart.

**6:17** And they brought the ark of the Lord and set it in its place in the midst of the tent which David had pitched for it; and he offered for it burnt offerings before the Lord, and peace offerings.

**6:18** And David finished offering up the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, and he blessed the people in the name of the Lord of Hosts.

**6:19** And he distributed to all the people, to all the host of Israel from Dan to Beersheba, from man to woman, to each a cake of bread, and a hearth-baked cake, and a wafer from the frying pan; and all the people departed, each one to his own house.

**6:20** And David returned to bless his own house; and Michal the daughter of Saul went out to meet David, and blessed him, and said, "How glorified today is the king of Israel, who uncovered himself today in the eyes of the maidservants of his servants, just as one of the dancers utterly uncovers himself!"

**6:21** And David said to Michal, "Before the Lord I will dance! Blessed be the Lord, who chose me above your father and above all his house, to appoint me as ruler over His people, over Israel; and I will play and dance before the Lord,

**6:22** and I will yet again uncover myself in this way, and I will be worthless in your eyes, and with the maidservants of whom you said that I was glorified."

**6:23** And Michal the daughter of Saul had no child until the day she died.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek reads "about seventy thousand" young men, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "thirty thousand" chosen men.
- **6:3** — The Greek uses singular verbs ("he placed... and carried"), reads "to the house of Amminadab" (Hebrew "from the house of Abinadab"), and translates Ahio as "his brothers."
- **6:6** — The Greek names the threshing floor "Nodab" (Hebrew "Nacon") and states that "the calf drew him aside" rather than that the oxen stumbled.
- **6:10** — The Greek reads "Abeddara" (Hebrew "Obed-edom") here and in 6:11–12, and includes "of the covenant" in "the ark of the covenant of the Lord."
- **6:13** — The Greek describes "seven choirs" bearing the ark with a calf and a lamb as a sacrifice, differing markedly from the Hebrew reading of "six steps" with an ox and a fattened animal.
