## Text

**23:1** Faithful is David the son of Jesse, and faithful is the man whom the Lord raised up over the anointed of the God of Jacob, and lovely are the psalms of Israel.

**23:2** The Spirit of the Lord spoke in me, and His word was upon my tongue.

**23:3** The God of Israel says; to me the Guardian out of Israel spoke, "Speak a parable: 'In man, how shall you strengthen the fear of the anointed?'"

**23:4** And in God, the light of morning, may the sun arise; in the morning the Lord did not pass by from brightness, and as from the rain of fresh grass out of the earth.

**23:5** For is not this my house with the Mighty One? For He has made with me an everlasting covenant, prepared at every season and kept, for this is all my salvation and every desire; for the lawless one shall by no means spring up.

**23:6** Like a thorn cast away are all of these, because they shall not be taken by the hand;

**23:7** and a man shall not toil among them; rather, with fullness of iron and the shaft of a spear, even with burning in fire, they shall be put to their shame.

**23:8** These are the names of the mighty men of David: Jebosthe the Canaanite is chief of the third; Adinon the Asonite, he drew his sword against eight hundred soldiers at one time.

**23:9** And after him was Eleanan the son of his father's brother, the son of Susei, who was among the three mighty men with David; and when he defied the Philistines, they were gathered together there for battle, and the men of Israel cried out.

**23:10** He arose and struck among the Philistines until his hand grew weary, and his hand clung to the sword; and the Lord worked a great deliverance in that day, and the people sat behind him only to strip them.

**23:11** And after him was Samaia the son of Asa the Aruchite; and the Philistines were gathered together at Theria, and there was a plot of the field full of lentils, and the people fled from before the Philistines.

**23:12** But he stood firm in the midst of the plot, and delivered it and struck down the Philistines; and the Lord worked a great deliverance.

**23:13** And three of the thirty went down, and they went down to Kadon to David, to the cave of Adullam; and a company of the Philistines was there, and they encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**23:14** And David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was then in Bethlehem.

**23:15** And David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the cistern in Bethlehem, which is in the gate?" Now the band of the Philistines was then in Bethlehem.

**23:16** And the three mighty men broke through the camp of the Philistines and drew water from the cistern in Bethlehem, which is in the gate, and took it and came to David; yet he would not drink it, and poured it out to the Lord.

**23:17** And he said, "Far be it from me, O Lord, to do this! Shall I drink the blood of the men who went at the peril of their lives?" And he would not drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**23:18** And Abishai the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruiah, he was chief among the three; and he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain men, and he had a name among the three.

**23:19** Of those three he was renowned, and he became a commander over them; yet he did not attain to the three.

**23:20** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, a man abounding in deeds from Kabzeel, he struck down the two sons of Ariel of Moab; and he also went down and struck down the lion in the midst of the pit on the day of snow.

**23:21** He also struck down the Egyptian man, a notable man; and in the hand of the Egyptian was a spear like the beam of a bridge; and he went down to him with a spear, and snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.

**23:22** These things Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**23:23** Of the three he was renowned, yet he did not attain to the three; and David appointed him to his hearing. And these are the names of the mighty men of King David:

**23:24** Asahel the brother of Joab, he was among the thirty; Elhanan the son of Dodei his father's brother, in Bethlehem;

**23:25** Saima the Rudaite;

**23:26** Selles the Kelothite; Ira the son of Iska the Tekoite;

**23:27** Abiezer the Anothite, of the sons of the Anothite;

**23:28** Ellon the Ahohite; Noere the Netophathite.

**23:29** Esthaei the son of Ribai from Gibeah, son of Benjamin the Ephrathite;

**23:31** Asboth the Bardiamite;

**23:32** Emasou the Shaalbonite; the sons of Asan, Jonathan;

**23:33** Samnan the Harodite; Amnan the son of Arai the Saraurite;

**23:34** Eliphelet the son of the Asbite, the son of Machachaachi; Eliab the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite;

**23:35** Asarai the Carmelite, of Ouraiorchi;

**23:36** Gaal the son of Nathan from the host, the son of Galaaddei;

**23:37** Adaoi from the brooks, Gadabiel the son of the Arabothite. Elie the Ammonite; Gelore the Bethorite, who bore the armor of Joab, the son of Zeruiah;

**23:38** Ira the Ithrite; Gerab the Etthenite;

**23:39** Uriah the Hittite: thirty-seven in all.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — The Greek opens with "Faithful is David" and "whom the Lord raised up over the anointed of the God of Jacob," differing from the Masoretic Text ("Now these are the last words of David... raised up on high").
- **23:3** — The Greek reads "Speak a parable: 'In man, how shall you strengthen the fear of the anointed?'" (Greek phobon christou), whereas the Masoretic Text speaks of one who rules justly in the fear of God.
- **23:8** — Throughout this roster of David's mighty men (including Jebosthe the Canaanite and Adinon the Asonite here, as well as numerous names in verses 25-38), the Greek preserves distinctive proper-name forms and places the displaced names of verse 30 into verse 37.
- **23:13** — The Greek reads the place name "Kadon" where the Masoretic Text has "at harvest time"; throughout this passage the Septuagint uses allophyloi ("foreigners") as its standard designation for the Philistines.
