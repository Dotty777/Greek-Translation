## Text

**5:1** And all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said to him, "Behold, we are your bones and your flesh.

**5:2** And in times past, when Saul was king over us, you were the one who led out and brought in Israel; and the Lord said to you, 'You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be leader over Israel.'"

**5:3** And all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David made a covenant with them at Hebron before the Lord; and they anointed David as king over all Israel.

**5:4** David was thirty years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years:

**5:5** Seven years and six months he reigned in Hebron over Judah, and thirty-three years he reigned over all Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

**5:6** And David and his men went to Jerusalem to the Jebusite who dwelt in the land; and it was said to David, "You shall not enter here," for the blind and the lame stood in opposition, saying, "David shall not enter here."

**5:7** And David captured the stronghold of Zion; this is the City of David.

**5:8** And David said on that day, "Whoever strikes a Jebusite, let him attack with a dagger both the lame and the blind, and those who hate the soul of David." Therefore they shall say, "The blind and the lame shall not enter the house of the Lord."

**5:9** And David dwelt in the stronghold, and it was called the City of David; and he built it as a city all around from the citadel, and also his own house.

**5:10** And David continued advancing and growing great, and the Lord Almighty was with him.

**5:11** And Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, along with cedar timber, and workers in wood and workers in stone, and they built a house for David.

**5:12** And David knew that the Lord had prepared him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom was exalted for the sake of His people Israel.

**5:13** And David took more wives and concubines from Jerusalem after he came to Hebron; and more sons and daughters were born to David.

**5:14** And these are the names of those born to him in Jerusalem: Sammus, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon,

**5:15** and Ebear, and Elisus, and Naphek, and Jephies,

**5:16** and Elishama, and Epidae, and Eliphaath, Samae, Jessibath, Nathan, Galamaan, Jebaar, Theesus, Elphalat, Naged, Naphek, Janatha, Leasamys, Baalimath, Eliphaath.

**5:17** And the foreigners heard that David had been anointed king over Israel, and all the foreigners went up to seek David; and David heard of it, and went down to the stronghold.

**5:18** And the foreigners came and converged in the Valley of the Titans.

**5:19** And David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Shall I go up against the foreigners, and will You deliver them into my hands?" And the Lord said to David, "Go up, for I will surely deliver the foreigners into your hands."

**5:20** And David came from Upper Breaches and struck down the foreigners there; and David said, "The Lord has broken through the foreign enemies before me, as waters break through." Therefore the name of that place was called Upper Breaches.

**5:21** And they left their gods behind there, and David and the men who were with him took them.

**5:22** And the foreigners went up yet again and converged in the Valley of the Titans.

**5:23** And David inquired through the Lord, and the Lord said, "You shall not go up to meet them; turn away from them, and you shall come upon them near the place of weeping.

**5:24** And it shall be, when you hear the sound of the closing in from the grove of weeping, then you shall go down against them; for then the Lord will go out before you to strike in the battle of the foreigners."

**5:25** And David did just as the Lord commanded him, and he struck the foreigners from Gibeon as far as the land of Gezer.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Greek states that the blind and the lame themselves stood in opposition, saying David would not enter, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's taunt about removing them.
- **5:8** — The Greek reads "with a dagger" (en paraxiphidi) rather than the Hebrew "by the water shaft," and explicitly adds "of the Lord" after "house."
- **5:13** — The Greek text reads "after he came to Hebron" (eis Chebron) rather than "from Hebron" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:16** — The Greek preserves distinct forms of several names (such as Epidae and Eliphaath) and appends a second list of thirteen additional names of David's sons not present in the Hebrew text of 2 Samuel.
- **5:17** — Greek allophyloi ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines throughout Kingdoms; likewise, the Greek renders Rephaim as "the Titans" (5:18, 22) and Baal-perazim as "Upper Breaches" (5:20).
