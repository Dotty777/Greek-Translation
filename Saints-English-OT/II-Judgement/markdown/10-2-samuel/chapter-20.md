## Text

**20:1** And there was one called there, a lawless man, and his name was Sheba, the son of Bichri, a Jeminite; and he blew the horn and said, "We have no portion in David, nor have we any inheritance in the son of Jesse! Every man to your tents, O Israel!"

**20:2** So all Israel went up from following David, to follow Sheba the son of Bichri; but the men of Judah clung to their king, from the Jordan even to Jerusalem.

**20:3** And David came to his house in Jerusalem. And the king took the ten women, his concubines, whom he had left to keep the house, and put them in a house of custody, and sustained them, and did not go in to them. So they were shut up until the day of their death, living as widows.

**20:4** And the king said to Amasa, "Summon the men of Judah for me within three days, and you yourself stand here."

**20:5** So Amasa went to summon Judah, and he delayed beyond the appointed time which he had set for him.

**20:6** And David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba the son of Bichri will do us greater harm than Absalom; now therefore, take with you the servants of your lord and pursue after him, lest he find for himself fortified cities and cast a shadow over our eyes."

**20:7** So Abishai and the men of Joab went out after him, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites and all the mighty men; and they went out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:8** And when they were beside the great stone that is in Gibeon, Amasa came before them. Now Joab was girded with a cloak, his garment, and over it he was girded with a sword fastened upon his loins in its sheath; and the sword came out—it came out and fell.

**20:9** And Joab said to Amasa, "Are you in health, brother?" And the right hand of Joab took hold of the beard of Amasa to kiss him.

**20:10** And Amasa did not watch out for the sword that was in the hand of Joab; and Joab struck him with it in the abdomen, and his entrails poured out upon the ground, and he did not strike him a second time, and he died. Then Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:11** And a man of the young men of Joab stood over him and said, "Who favors Joab, and who is for David? Follow after Joab!"

**20:12** Now Amasa was wallowing in blood in the middle of the road. And when the man saw that all the people stood still, they moved Amasa from the road into a field, and he cast a garment over him, because he saw that everyone who came up to him stood still.

**20:13** And as soon as he was removed from the road, all the men of Israel passed on after Joab to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:14** And he passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and to Beth Maacah; and all who were in Charri were gathered together, and he came after him.

**20:15** And they came and besieged him in Abel of Beth Maacah, and they poured out a siege mound against the city, and it stood against the outer rampart; and all the people who were with Joab were devising to throw down the wall.

**20:16** And a wise woman cried out from the wall and said, "Hear, hear! Say, I pray, to Joab, 'Draw near to this place,' and I will speak with him."

**20:17** And he drew near to her, and the woman said, "Are you Joab?" And he said, "I am." Then she said to him, "Hear the words of your maidservant." And Joab said, "I am listening."

**20:18** And she spoke, saying, "They spoke a word in former times, saying, 'Inquiry was surely made in Abel and in Dan whether the things which the faithful of Israel established had failed; indeed, they shall surely inquire of one in Abel, and thus, whether they have failed.'

**20:19** I am of the peaceful strongholds of Israel, yet you seek to put to death a city and a mother city in Israel. Why do you swallow up the inheritance of the Lord?"

**20:20** And Joab answered and said, "Far be it from me, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or that I should destroy!

**20:21** This is not the matter. Rather, a man from the hill country of Ephraim, Sheba the son of Bichri by name, has lifted up his hand against King David. Give him to me alone, and I will depart from the city." And the woman said to Joab, "Behold, his head shall be thrown to you over the wall."

**20:22** Then the woman went to all the people, and she spoke to the whole city in her wisdom. And she cut off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri, and took it and threw it to Joab. Then he blew the horn, and they were dispersed from the city away from him, every man to his tents. And Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.

**20:23** Now Joab was over all the army of Israel, and Benaiah the son of Ahilud was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites;

**20:24** and Adoniram was over the tribute, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder,

**20:25** and Joshua was the scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar were priests,

**20:26** and Ira the Jairite was also a priest to David.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — The Greek reads "cast a shadow over our eyes," rendering the underlying Hebrew as derived from "shade" rather than "escape."
- **20:14** — The Septuagint reads "all who were in Charri" where the Masoretic Text refers to the Berites (or Bichrites).
- **20:18** — The Septuagint preserves a longer form of the ancient saying, referring to inquiry made in both Abel and Dan as to whether the customs established by the faithful of Israel had failed.
- **20:23** — The Greek text names "Benaiah the son of Ahilud" (sharing the patronymic of Jehoshaphat in verse twenty-four), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "son of Jehoiada."
- **20:25** — The Septuagint gives the scribe's name as "Joshua" (Greek Iesous), where the Masoretic Text reads "Sheva."
