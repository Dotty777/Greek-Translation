## Text

**17:1** Moreover Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me now choose for myself twelve thousand men, and I will arise and pursue after David tonight.

**17:2** And I will come upon him while he is weary and weak of hands, and I will terrify him, and all the people who are with him will flee, and I will strike down the king alone;

**17:3** and I will bring back all the people to you, as a bride returns to her husband; you seek the life of only one man, and all the people shall be in peace."

**17:4** And the word was right in the eyes of Absalom and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel.

**17:5** Then Absalom said, "Now call Hushai the Archite also, and let us hear what is in his mouth as well."

**17:6** So Hushai came in to Absalom; and Absalom spoke to him, saying, "Ahithophel has spoken after this manner. Shall we act according to his word? If not, you speak."

**17:7** And Hushai said to Absalom, "This counsel that Ahithophel has given is not good at this time."

**17:8** Hushai also said, "You know your father and his men, that they are exceedingly mighty, and they are bitter in their soul, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field, and like a fierce wild sow in the plain; and your father is a man of war, and he will surely not let the people lodge.

**17:9** For behold, even now he is hidden in one of the hills, or in one of the other places; and it shall come to pass, when he falls upon them at the first, that whoever hears of it will hear and say, 'There has been a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom!'

**17:10** And even he who is a man of might, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, will utterly melt away; for all Israel knows that your father is mighty, and those who are with him are men of might.

**17:11** Therefore I surely advise thus: let all Israel be completely gathered to you, from Dan even to Beersheba, like the sand that is by the sea for multitude, with your own presence going in the midst of them;

**17:12** and we shall come upon him in whichever of the places we may find him there, and we shall encamp upon him as the dew falls upon the earth; and of him and of the men who are with him we shall not leave even one.

**17:13** And if he withdraws into a city, then all Israel shall take ropes to that city, and we shall drag it down into the brook, so that not even a stone is left there."

**17:14** And Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the good counsel of Ahithophel." For the Lord had commanded to frustrate the counsel of Ahithophel, so that the Lord might bring all the evil upon Absalom.

**17:15** Then Hushai the Archite said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "Thus and so Ahithophel counseled Absalom and the elders of Israel, and thus and so I have counseled.

**17:16** Now therefore send quickly and tell David, saying, 'Do not lodge tonight in Araboth of the wilderness, but by all means hasten to cross over, lest he prevail upon the king and all the people who are with him.'"

**17:17** Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at the spring of Rogel, and a maidservant went and told them, and they were to go and report to King David, for they could not let themselves be seen entering the city.

**17:18** Yet a young man saw them, and they reported it to Absalom; so both of them departed quickly and entered into the house of a man in Bahurim, who had a cistern in his courtyard, and they went down there.

**17:19** And the woman took and spread a covering over the mouth of the cistern, and dried crushed grain upon it; and the matter was not known.

**17:20** Then the servants of Absalom came to the woman at the house and said, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" And the woman said to them, "They have passed a little beyond the water." And they searched and did not find them, and they returned to Jerusalem.

**17:21** Now it came to pass, after they had departed, that they came up out of the cistern, and went and told King David, and said to David, "Arise and cross over the water quickly, for thus Ahithophel has counseled against you."

**17:22** So David and all the people who were with him arose and crossed the Jordan; by the morning light, not even one was missing who had not crossed over the Jordan.

**17:23** Now when Ahithophel saw that his counsel had not been followed, he saddled his donkey, and arose and went to his house, to his own city; and he gave charge to his household, and hanged himself and died, and he was buried in the tomb of his father.

**17:24** Then David came to Mahanaim, and Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.

**17:25** And Absalom set Amasa over the army in place of Joab; now Amasa was the son of a man whose name was Jether the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigail the daughter of Nahash, the brother of Zeruiah, the mother of Joab.

**17:26** And all Israel and Absalom encamped in the land of Gilead.

**17:27** And it came to pass, when David came to Mahanaim, that Uesbi the son of Nahash from Rabbah of the children of Ammon, and Machir the son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim,

**17:28** brought ten beds, and rugs, and ten basins, and earthen vessels, and wheat, and barley, and flour, and barley meal, and beans, and lentils,

**17:29** and honey, and butter, and sheep, and cheeses of cattle; and they presented them to David and to the people who were with him to eat, for they said, "The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness."

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Greek preserves the distinctive simile 'as a bride returns to her husband; you seek the life of only one man,' differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:8** — The Greek includes the additional simile 'and like a fierce wild sow in the plain,' which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:16** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for plains as Araboth and reads katapeise ('prevail upon' or 'persuade'), where other witnesses and the Hebrew read 'swallow up.'
- **17:25** — The Greek calls Amasa's father Jether the Israelite and uses the masculine noun 'brother' (adelphou), making Nahash the brother of Zeruiah.
- **17:27** — The Greek fuses the Hebrew conjunction and name (we-Shobi) into the single name Uesbi, and in verses 28-29 adds 'ten' before both beds and basins while transliterating the Hebrew term for cattle cheeses as saphoth.
