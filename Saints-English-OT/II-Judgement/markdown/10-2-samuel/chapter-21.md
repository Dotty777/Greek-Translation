## Text

**21:1** Now there was a famine in the days of David for three years, year following year; and David sought the face of the Lord. And the Lord said, "Upon Saul and upon his house is iniquity because of his bloody slaughter, for he put the Gibeonites to death."

**21:2** And King David called the Gibeonites and spoke to them. Now the Gibeonites are not of the children of Israel, but rather of the blood of the Amorites, and the children of Israel had sworn to them; yet Saul sought to strike them down in his zeal for the children of Israel and Judah.

**21:3** And David said to the Gibeonites, "What shall I do for you, and with what shall I make atonement, so that you will bless the inheritance of the Lord?"

**21:4** And the Gibeonites said to him, "We have no silver and gold with Saul and with his house, nor do we have any man to put to death in Israel." And he said, "What do you say, that I may do it for you?"

**21:5** And they said to the king, "The man brought destruction upon us and pursued us, who schemed to destroy us utterly; let us blot him out, that he may not stand in any territory of Israel.

**21:6** Let seven men from his sons be given to us, and we will expose them to the sun for the Lord in Gibeon of Saul, chosen ones of the Lord." And the king said, "I will give them."

**21:7** But the king spared Mephibosheth the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, because of the oath of the Lord that was between them, even between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.

**21:8** So the king took the two sons of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bore to Saul—Armoni and Mephibosheth—and the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul, whom she bore to Serei the son of Barzillai the Meholathite,

**21:9** and he delivered them into the hand of the Gibeonites; and they exposed them to the sun on the mountain before the Lord, and those seven fell together. And they were put to death in the days of harvest, in the first days, at the beginning of the barley harvest.

**21:10** And Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and pitched it for herself against the rock at the beginning of the barley harvest until water dropped upon them from heaven; and she did not allow the birds of heaven to rest upon them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.

**21:11** And it was reported to David all that Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done. And they grew faint, and Dan the son of Joa, of the descendants of the giants, overtook them.

**21:12** And David went and took the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son from the men, the sons of Jabesh Gilead, who had stolen them from the open square of Baith, for the Philistines had set them up there in the day when the Philistines struck down Saul in Gilboa.

**21:13** And he brought up from there the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and he gathered the bones of those who had been exposed to the sun.

**21:14** And they buried the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and of those who had been exposed to the sun, in the land of Benjamin, at the side, in the tomb of Kish his father; and they did all that the king had commanded. And God heeded the land after these things.

**21:15** Now there was again war between the Philistines and Israel; and David went down, and his servants with him, and they fought against the Philistines; and David went on.

**21:16** And Jesbi, who was among the offspring of Rapha—and the weight of his spear was three hundred shekels in weight of bronze, and he was girded with a club—intended to strike down David.

**21:17** But Abishai the son of Zeruiah came to his aid, and struck the Philistine and put him to death. Then the men of David swore, saying, "You shall no longer go out with us to battle, and you shall by no means quench the lamp of Israel."

**21:18** And it came to pass after these things that there was again war in Gath with the Philistines. Then Oebocha the Hastatothite struck down Seph, who was among the offspring of Rapha.

**21:19** And there was war in Rom with the Philistines; and Elhanan the son of Ariorgim the Bethlehemite struck down Godoliah the Hittite, and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam.

**21:20** And there was again war in Gath; and there was a man of Madon, and the fingers of his hands and the toes of his feet were six and six, twenty-four in number; and he also was born to Rapha.

**21:21** And he reproached Israel, and Jonathan the son of Shimei, the brother of David, struck him down.

**21:22** These four were born as descendants of the giants in Gath, the house of Rapha, and they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Greek reads "of the blood of the Amorites" (ek tou haimatos), a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew text has "of the remnant of the Amorites."
- **21:8** — The Greek text names Michal's husband Serei, where the Hebrew text reads Adriel.
- **21:11** — The closing sentence ("And they grew faint, and Dan the son of Joa, of the descendants of the giants, overtook them") is a distinctive Septuagint reading absent from the Hebrew text, anticipating the giant narratives in verses 15-22.
- **21:14** — "At the side" renders the Greek en te pleura, a literal translation of the Hebrew place name Zela ("rib" or "side"); similarly, verse 12 reads simply Baith rather than Beth-shan.
- **21:19** — The Greek preserves several distinctive proper names in verses 16-20, including Jesbi (v. 16), Oebocha the Hastatothite and Seph (v. 18), Rom, Ariorgim, and Godoliah the Hittite (v. 19, where the Hebrew has Gob, Jaare-oregim, and Goliath the Gittite), and Madon (v. 20).
