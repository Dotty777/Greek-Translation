## Text

**2:1** Now it came to pass after these things that David inquired of the Lord, saying, "Shall I go up to one of the cities of Judah?" And the Lord said to him, "Go up." And David said, "Where shall I go up?" And He said, "To Hebron."

**2:2** So David went up there to Hebron, along with both his wives, Ahinoam the Israelitess and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite,

**2:3** and the men who were with him, each man and his household; and they dwelt in the cities of Hebron.

**2:4** Then men of Judea came and anointed David there to reign over the house of Judah. And they reported to David, saying that the men of Jabesh of Gilead had buried Saul.

**2:5** So David sent messengers to the leaders of Jabesh of Gilead and said to them, "Blessed are you by the Lord, because you have shown the mercy of God to your lord Saul and have buried him.

**2:6** And now may the Lord show mercy and truth to you; and I also will do this good thing for you, because you have done this thing.

**2:7** Therefore now let your hands be strengthened, and be sons of valor; for your lord Saul is dead, and moreover the house of Judah has anointed me king over them."

**2:8** Now Abner the son of Ner, commander of Saul's army, took Ishbosheth the son of Saul and brought him up from the camp to Mahanaim,

**2:9** and he made him king over Gilead, and over Thasiri, and over Israel, and over Ephraim, and over Benjamin, and over all Israel.

**2:10** Ishbosheth the son of Saul was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned two years, except for the house of Judah, who followed after David.

**2:11** And the days that David reigned in Hebron over the house of Judah were seven years and six months.

**2:12** Now Abner the son of Ner and the servants of Ishbosheth the son of Saul went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.

**2:13** And Joab the son of Zeruiah and the servants of David went out from Hebron and met them together at the pool of Gibeon; and one side sat down at the pool of Gibeon on this side, and the other at the pool on that side.

**2:14** Then Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men now arise and make sport before us." And Joab said, "Let them arise."

**2:15** So they arose and came forward by number: twelve of the servants of Benjamin belonging to Ishbosheth the son of Saul, and twelve of the servants of David.

**2:16** And each man seized his opponent's head with his hand and drove his sword into his opponent's side, and they fell down together. Therefore the name of that place was called the Portion of the Plotters, which is in Gibeon.

**2:17** And the battle was exceedingly fierce on that day, and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated before the servants of David.

**2:18** Now the three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. And Asahel was swift of foot, like a gazelle in the field.

**2:19** And Asahel pursued after Abner, and did not turn aside to go to the right or to the left from following Abner.

**2:20** Then Abner looked behind him and said, "Are you yourself Asahel?" And he answered, "I am."

**2:21** And Abner said to him, "Turn aside to the right or to the left, and lay hold of one of the young men for yourself and take his armor for yourself." Yet Asahel would not turn aside from following him.

**2:22** So Abner spoke yet again to Asahel, saying, "Turn away from me, lest I strike you down to the ground! And how then shall I lift up my face to Joab?

**2:23** And where do these things lead? Turn back to Joab your brother!" Yet he refused to turn away. Therefore Abner struck him in the loin with the butt end of the spear, so that the spear came out behind him; and he fell there and died on the spot. And it came to pass that everyone who came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died stood still.

**2:24** Joab and Abishai also pursued after Abner; and the sun was going down when they came to the hill of Amman, which is in front of Gai, by the wilderness way of Gibeon.

**2:25** And the sons of Benjamin who followed Abner gathered together, and formed one company, and took their stand on the top of a single hill.

**2:26** Then Abner called to Joab and said, "Shall the sword devour forever? Or do you not know that it will be bitter in the end? How long then will it be before you tell the people to turn back from pursuing our brethren?"

**2:27** And Joab said, "As the Lord lives, if you had not spoken, surely then from the morning the people would have gone up, each man following after his brother."

**2:28** So Joab blew the trumpet, and all the people halted and did not pursue after Israel, nor did they continue to fight any longer.

**2:29** And Abner and his men departed toward the west all that night, and crossed over the Jordan, and marched through all the adjoining region, and came to the camp.

**2:30** And Joab returned from pursuing Abner; and when he had gathered all the people together, nineteen men of the servants of David were missing,

**2:31** and Asahel. And the servants of David had struck down of the sons of Benjamin, of the men of Abner, three hundred and sixty men of his.

**2:32** Then they took up Asahel and buried him in the tomb of his father in Bethlehem. And Joab and the men who were with him marched all night, and the day dawned upon them at Hebron.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek identifies Ahinoam as "the Israelitess" (Israeleitis), whereas the Hebrew reads "the Jezreelitess."
- **2:5** — The Greek reads "the mercy of God" (to eleos tou theou), where the Hebrew reads "this kindness."
- **2:9** — The Greek preserves the distinct name "Thasiri" and reads "Israel" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the Ashurites" and "Jezreel."
- **2:16** — The Greek renders the Hebrew place name Helkath Hazzurim as "the Portion of the Plotters" (Meris ton epiboulon).
- **2:29** — The Greek renders three Hebrew geographic names descriptively: "toward the west" (for the Arabah), "the adjoining region" (for Bithron), and "the camp" (for Mahanaim).
