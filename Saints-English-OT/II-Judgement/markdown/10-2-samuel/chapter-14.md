## Text

**14:1** And Joab the son of Zeruiah knew that the heart of the king was toward Absalom.

**14:2** And Joab sent to Tekoa, and brought from there a wise woman, and said to her, "Mourn now, and put on garments of mourning, and do not anoint yourself with oil; and you shall be like a woman mourning over one who has died for these many days;

**14:3** and you shall come to the king and speak to him according to this word." And Joab put the words in her mouth.

**14:4** And the Tekoite woman went in to the king, and fell on her face to the ground, and bowed down before him, and said, "Save, O king, save!"

**14:5** And the king said to her, "What is the matter with you?" And she said, "Truly, I am a widow woman, and my husband has died.

**14:6** Moreover, your maidservant had two sons, and the two of them fought together in the field, and there was no one to deliver between them; and the one struck the other, his brother, and put him to death.

**14:7** And behold, the whole family has risen up against your maidservant, and they said, 'Give up him who struck his brother, and we will put him to death for the life of his brother whom he slew, and we will cut off even your heir.' And they will quench my ember that remains, so as not to leave to my husband a remnant and a name upon the face of the earth."

**14:8** And the king said, "Go in good health to your house, and I will give command concerning you."

**14:9** And the Tekoite woman said to the king, "Upon me, my lord, O king, be the iniquity, and upon the house of my father; and let the king and his throne be guiltless."

**14:10** And the king said, "Who is the one speaking to you? You shall bring him to me, and he shall not touch him anymore."

**14:11** And she said, "Let the king now remember the Lord his God, though the avenger of blood be multiplied to destroy, and they shall by no means cut off my son." And he said, "As the Lord lives, not one hair of your son shall fall to the ground."

**14:12** And she said, "Let your maidservant now speak a word to my lord the king." And he said, "Speak."

**14:13** And the woman said, "Why have you devised such a thing against the people of God? Or is this word from the mouth of the king as a trespass, in that the king does not bring back his banished one?

**14:14** For we shall surely die, and we are like water poured out to waste upon the earth, which shall not be gathered up again; and God will take a life, even while devising to banish from Himself one who is banished.

**14:15** And now, the reason I have come to speak this word to the king my lord is because the people will see me, and your people will say, 'Let her now speak to the king, if perhaps the king will do the word of his maidservant;'

**14:16** for the king will hear; let him deliver his maidservant out of the hand of the man who seeks to cut off me and my son from the inheritance of God."

**14:17** And the woman said, "May the word of my lord the king now be for sacrifices; for as an angel of God, so is my lord the king to hear the good and the evil. And the Lord your God shall be with you."

**14:18** And the king answered and said to the woman, "Do not hide from me now the matter that I ask you." And the woman said, "Let my lord the king now speak."

**14:19** And the king said, "Is not the hand of Joab with you in all this?" And the woman said to the king, "As your soul lives, my lord, O king, no one can turn to the right hand or to the left from all that my lord the king has spoken; for your servant Joab himself commanded me, and he himself put all these words in the mouth of your maidservant;

**14:20** in order to turn around the appearance of this matter which your servant Joab has done, this craftiness. Yet my lord is wise according to the wisdom of an angel of God, to know all things that are in the earth."

**14:21** And the king said to Joab, "Behold now, I have done for you according to this word of yours; bring back the young man Absalom."

**14:22** And Joab fell on his face to the ground and bowed down and blessed the king; and Joab said, "Today your servant knows that I have found favor in your eyes, my lord, O king, because my lord the king has done the word of his servant."

**14:23** And Joab arose and went to Geshur, and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.

**14:24** And the king said, "Let him return to his own house, and let him not see my face." So Absalom returned to his own house, and did not see the face of the king.

**14:25** And there was no man in all Israel so exceedingly praised as Absalom; from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head there was no blemish in him.

**14:26** And when he sheared his head—for it came to pass from one year's beginning to another that he sheared it, because it was heavy upon him—when he sheared it, he weighed the hair of his head at two hundred shekels according to the royal shekel.

**14:27** And to Absalom were born three sons and one daughter, whose name was Tamar; she was an exceedingly beautiful woman, and she became the wife of Rehoboam the son of Solomon, and bore to him Abiathar.

**14:28** And Absalom dwelt in Jerusalem two full years, and did not see the face of the king.

**14:29** And Absalom sent to Joab, to send him to the king, but he would not come to him; and he sent a second time to him, but he would not come.

**14:30** And Absalom said to his servants, "See, Joab's portion in the field is beside mine, and he has barley there; go and burn it with fire." And the servants of Absalom burned them, even the portion. And the servants of Joab came to him with their garments torn, and said, "The servants of Absalom have burned the portion with fire."

**14:31** And Joab arose and came to Absalom at the house, and said to him, "Why have your servants burned my portion with fire?"

**14:32** And Absalom said to Joab, "Behold, I sent to you, saying, 'Come here, and I will send you to the king to say, Why have I come from Geshur? It was good for me to be there.' And now, behold, I have not seen the face of the king; and if there is unrighteousness in me, then put me to death."

**14:33** And Joab went in to the king and told him, and he called for Absalom. And he went in to the king and bowed down before him, and fell on his face to the ground before the face of the king. And the king kissed Absalom.

## Translator Notes

- **14:14** — The Greek lacks the negations found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reading affirmatively that God will take a life while devising to banish one who is banished.
- **14:17** — The Greek reads 'for sacrifices' (thysias), reflecting Hebrew minchah ('offering') rather than menuchah ('rest, comfort').
- **14:27** — The Greek includes an additional notice that Tamar became the wife of Rehoboam the son of Solomon and bore him Abiathar (cf. Abijam/Abijah in 1 Kings 14:31; 15:1-2).
