## Text

**3:1** Now the war lasted for a long time between the house of Saul and the house of David; and the house of David went on and grew stronger, and the house of Saul went on and grew weaker.

**3:2** And sons were born to David in Hebron: his firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Israelitess;

**3:3** and his second, Daluia, by Abigail the Carmelitess; and the third, Absalom the son of Maacah, the daughter of Thommei, king of Geshur;

**3:4** and the fourth, Orneil the son of Pheggith; and the fifth, Shephatiah, by Abital;

**3:5** and the sixth, Ithream, by Eglah, David's wife. These were born to David in Hebron.

**3:6** And it came to pass, while the war was between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abner was holding sway over the house of Saul.

**3:7** Now Saul had a concubine, Rizpah the daughter of Ial; and Mephibosheth the son of Saul said to Abner, "Why have you gone in to my father's concubine?"

**3:8** Then Abner was exceedingly angry at the word of Mephibosheth, and Abner said to him, "Am I a dog's head? Today I have shown mercy to the house of Saul your father, and to brethren and acquaintances, and I have not deserted to the house of David; and yet today you call me to account concerning the wrongdoing of a woman?

**3:9** May God do thus to Abner, and add more also to him, for just as the Lord swore to David, so indeed I will do for him on this day:

**3:10** to take away the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to set up the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan to Beersheba."

**3:11** And Mephibosheth was no longer able to answer Abner a word, because he feared him.

**3:12** Then Abner sent messengers at once to David at Thailam, where he was, saying, "Make your covenant with me, and behold, my hand is with you to turn all the house of Israel back to you."

**3:13** And David said, "Good, I will make a covenant with you; yet one thing I ask of you, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless you bring Michal, the daughter of Saul, when you come to see my face.'"

**3:14** Then David sent messengers to Mephibosheth the son of Saul, saying, "Restore to me my wife Michal, whom I took for one hundred foreskins of the foreigners."

**3:15** And Mephibosheth sent and took her from her husband, from Paltiel the son of Selles.

**3:16** And her husband went along with her, weeping behind her as far as Barakei. Then Abner said to him, "Go, turn back." And he turned back.

**3:17** Now Abner spoke to the elders of Israel, saying, "In times past you sought David to reign over you.

**3:18** Now therefore do it, for the Lord has spoken concerning David, saying, 'By the hand of My servant David I will save Israel from the hand of the foreigners and from the hand of all their enemies.'"

**3:19** And Abner spoke in the ears of Benjamin; and Abner went to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that was pleasing in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of the whole house of Benjamin.

**3:20** So Abner came to David in Hebron, and twenty men with him; and David made a feast for Abner and for the men who were with him.

**3:21** And Abner said to David, "I will arise now and go, and I will gather all Israel to my lord the king, and I will make a covenant with him, and you shall reign over all that your soul desires." So David sent Abner away, and they departed in peace.

**3:22** And behold, the servants of David and Joab arrived from the raid, and they brought much spoil with them; but Abner was not with David in Hebron, for he had sent him away, and he had departed in peace.

**3:23** And Joab and all his army arrived, and it was reported to Joab, saying, "Abner the son of Ner came to David, and he has sent him away, and he has departed in peace."

**3:24** Then Joab went in to the king and said, "What is this that you have done? Behold, Abner came to you; why then have you sent him away, and he has departed in peace?

**3:25** Or do you not know the malice of Abner the son of Ner, that he came to deceive you, and to know your going out and your coming in, and to know all that you do?"

**3:26** And Joab withdrew from David and sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the well of Seeiram; but David did not know it.

**3:27** And he brought Abner back to Hebron, and Joab drew him aside to the side of the gate to speak to him, lying in wait; and he struck him there in the loin, and he died for the blood of Asahel, the brother of Joab.

**3:28** And David heard of it afterward and said, "I and my kingdom are guiltless before the Lord even forever of the blood of Abner the son of Ner.

**3:29** Let it fall upon the head of Joab and upon all his father's house; and let there not fail from the house of Joab one who has a discharge, or a leper, or one who holds a staff, or one who falls by the sword, or one who lacks bread."

**3:30** Now Joab and Abishai his brother were lying in wait for Abner, because he had put to death Asahel their brother at Gibeon in the battle.

**3:31** Then David said to Joab and to all the people who were with him, "Tear your garments, gird yourselves with sackcloth, and mourn before Abner." And King David walked behind the bier.

**3:32** So they buried Abner in Hebron; and the king lifted up his voice and wept at his grave, and all the people wept over Abner.

**3:33** And the king lamented over Abner and said, "Should Abner die according to the death of Nabal?

**3:34** Your hands were not bound, your feet were not in fetters; he did not draw near like Nabal; before sons of unrighteousness you fell." And all the people were gathered together to weep for him.

**3:35** And all the people came to share a funeral meal of bread with David while it was still day; but David swore, saying, "May God do thus to me, and add more also, for until the sun goes down I will by no means taste bread or anything at all."

**3:36** And all the people took note of it, and all that the king did before the people was pleasing in their sight.

**3:37** So all the people and all Israel knew on that day that it had not been from the king to put Abner the son of Ner to death.

**3:38** And the king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a great leader has fallen on this day in Israel?

**3:39** And that today I am a kinsman and appointed by a king? Yet these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too harsh for me. May the Lord repay the one who does evil according to his wickedness."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — In verses 2–4, the Greek text preserves several distinct forms of proper names and gentilics: "the Israelitess" (MT "the Jezreelitess"), "Daluia" (MT "Chileab"), "Thommei" (MT "Talmai"), "Orneil" (MT "Adonijah"), and "Pheggith" (MT "Haggith").
- **3:7** — Throughout chapters 3–4, the Greek text names Saul's son "Mephibosheth" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Ish-bosheth"), and here calls Rizpah's father "Ial" (MT "Aiah").
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "at Thailam, where he was, at once," reflecting a double rendering of the Hebrew expression for "from where he was," and omits the question "Whose is the land?"
- **3:33** — The Greek renders the Hebrew word for "fool" (naval) as the proper name "Nabal" in verses 33–34, and in verse 34 reads "he did not draw near like Nabal" where the Hebrew reads "as one falls."
- **3:39** — The Greek reads "a kinsman and appointed by a king," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "weak, though anointed king."
