## Text

**15:1** And it came to pass after these things that Absalom provided for himself chariots and horses, and fifty men to run before him.

**15:2** And Absalom rose early and stood beside the way of the gate; and it was so, that whenever any man who had a lawsuit came to the king for judgment, Absalom called to him and said to him, "From what city are you?" And he said, "Your servant is from one of the tribes of Israel."

**15:3** And Absalom said to him, "Behold, your words are good and clear, yet there is no one from the king to hear you."

**15:4** And Absalom said, "Who will appoint me judge in the land, so that every man who has a dispute and a cause may come to me, and I will give him justice?"

**15:5** And it came to pass, when a man drew near to bow down before him, that he stretched out his hand and took hold of him and kissed him.

**15:6** And Absalom did after this manner to all Israel who came to the king for judgment; and Absalom won for himself the heart of the men of Israel.

**15:7** And it came to pass at the end of forty years, that Absalom said to his father, "Let me go now and pay my vows which I have vowed to the Lord in Hebron;

**15:8** for your servant vowed a vow while I dwelt in Geshur in Syria, saying, 'If the Lord indeed brings me back to Jerusalem, then I will serve the Lord.'"

**15:9** And the king said to him, "Go in peace." And he arose and went to Hebron.

**15:10** And Absalom sent spies throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, "When you hear the sound of the horn, then you shall say, 'King Absalom reigns in Hebron!'"

**15:11** And with Absalom went two hundred men out of Jerusalem who were invited; and they went in their simplicity, and did not know anything of the matter.

**15:12** And Absalom sent to Ahithophel the Tekonite, David's counselor, in his city Giloh, while he was offering sacrifices. And there came to be a mighty calamity, and the people kept growing numerous with Absalom.

**15:13** And a messenger came to David, saying, "The heart of the men of Israel has turned after Absalom."

**15:14** And David said to all his servants who were with him in Jerusalem, "Arise, and let us flee, for there is no deliverance for us from the face of Absalom; make haste to depart, lest he make haste and overtake us, and thrust evil upon us and strike the city with the edge of the sword."

**15:15** And the servants of the king said to the king, "According to all that our lord the king chooses, behold, we are your servants."

**15:16** And the king went out, and all his house on foot; and the king left ten women of his concubines to keep the house.

**15:17** And the king went out, and all his servants on foot, and they stood at the Far House.

**15:18** And all his servants passed on beside him, and all the Hittites and all the Pelethites, and they stood by the olive tree in the wilderness; and all the people passed along beside him, and all those about him, and all the mighty men, and all the warriors, six hundred men, were present beside him; and all the Cherethites, and all the Pelethites, and all the Gittites, the six hundred men who had come on foot to Gath, also marched before the face of the king.

**15:19** And the king said to Setthei the Gittite, "Why do you also go with us? Return and dwell with the king, for you are a stranger, and you have also migrated from your place.

**15:20** If you came only yesterday, shall I today make you wander with us? Indeed, you shall change your place of dwelling. Yesterday was your departure, and shall I today move you to go with us, seeing that I go wherever I may go? Return, and take back your brethren with you, and the Lord will deal with you in mercy and truth."

**15:21** And Ittai answered the king and said, "As the Lord lives, and as my lord the king lives, surely in whatever place my lord shall be, whether for death or for life, even there shall your servant be."

**15:22** And the king said to Setthei, "Come, and cross over with me." So Setthei the Gittite passed over, and all his servants, and all the multitude that was with him.

**15:23** And all the land wept with a loud voice; and all the people passed along in the brook of the cedars, and the king crossed over the brook Kidron; and all the people and the king passed along toward the way of the wilderness.

**15:24** And behold, Zadok also came, and all the Levites with him, bearing the ark of the covenant of the Lord from Baithar; and they set down the ark of God, and Abiathar went up until all the people had finished passing out of the city.

**15:25** And the king said to Zadok, "Carry back the ark of God into the city; if I find favor in the eyes of the Lord, He will also bring me back and show me both it and its beauty;

**15:26** and if He says thus, 'I have no delight in you,' behold, here I am; let Him do to me according to what is good in His eyes."

**15:27** And the king said to Zadok the priest, "See, you are returning to the city in peace, and Ahimaaz your son and Jonathan the son of Abiathar, your two sons, with you;

**15:28** see, I am encamped in Araboth of the wilderness until word comes from you to bring me report."

**15:29** And Zadok and Abiathar carried the ark back to Jerusalem, and they remained there.

**15:30** And David went up by the ascent of the olive trees with his head covered, and he went barefoot; and all the people who were with him covered each man his head, and they went up, going up and weeping.

**15:31** And it was told David, saying, "Ahithophel also is among those who are conspiring with Absalom." And David said, "Frustrate now the counsel of Ahithophel, O Lord my God."

**15:32** And David was coming as far as Roos, where he worshiped God there; and behold, Hushai the Archite, David's companion, came to meet him, having torn his tunic and with earth upon his head.

**15:33** And David said to him, "If you pass over with me, then you shall be a burden upon me;

**15:34** but if you return to the city and say to Absalom, 'Your brethren have passed over, and the king your father has passed over behind me; and now I am your servant, O king, let me live; I was the servant of your father formerly and until now, and now I am your servant,' then you shall frustrate for me the counsel of Ahithophel.

**15:35** And behold, Zadok and Abiathar the priests are there with you; and it shall be that whatever word you hear out of the king's house, you shall report it to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests.

**15:36** Behold, there with them are their two sons, Ahimaaz the son of Zadok and Jonathan the son of Abiathar; and by their hand you shall send to me every word that you hear."

**15:37** So Hushai, David's companion, went into the city, and Absalom was entering into Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **15:12** — The Greek calls Ahithophel 'the Tekonite' (rather than 'the Gilonite' as in the Hebrew) and reads 'a mighty calamity' (syntrimma, 'crushing, ruin') where the Hebrew has 'conspiracy'.
- **15:18** — The Greek preserves a doubled account of the procession, naming both the Hittites and the Cherethites, adding a stop by the olive tree in the wilderness, and reading 'to Gath' rather than 'from Gath'.
- **15:19** — The Greek spells the Gittite commander's name 'Setthei' here and in verse 22, and 'Etthei' (Ittai) in verse 21.
- **15:24** — The Greek adds the place name 'from Baithar' to the account of the Levites bearing the ark, a detail absent from the Hebrew text.
- **15:28** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for 'plains' or 'steppes' as 'Araboth' and reads 'am encamped' (strateuomai) where the Hebrew has 'tarrying'.
- **15:32** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for 'summit' (rosh) as the proper name 'Roos' and includes the title 'David's companion' for Hushai.
