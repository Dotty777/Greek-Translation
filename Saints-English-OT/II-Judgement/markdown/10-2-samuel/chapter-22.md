## Text

**22:1** And David spoke the words of this song to the Lord on the day the Lord rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

**22:2** And he said, A song: "O Lord, my rock and my stronghold, and He who delivers me for myself;

**22:3** my God shall be my guardian, I will trust in Him; my shield and the horn of my salvation, my upholder and my refuge of my salvation; from an unjust man You shall save me.

**22:4** I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy of praise, and from my enemies I shall be saved.

**22:5** For the crushings of death surrounded me; torrents of lawlessness terrified me.

**22:6** The pangs of death encircled me; the severities of death overtook me.

**22:7** When I am afflicted I will call upon the Lord, and to my God I will cry aloud; and He shall hear my voice from His temple, and my cry shall be in His ears.

**22:8** And the earth was troubled and shaken, and the foundations of heaven were thrown into turmoil and convulsed, because the Lord was angry with them.

**22:9** Smoke rose up in His wrath, and fire from His mouth shall devour; coals were kindled from Him.

**22:10** And He bowed the heavens and descended, and thick darkness was beneath His feet.

**22:11** And He sat upon cherubim and flew, and He was seen upon the wings of the wind.

**22:12** And He placed the darkness of His hiding place all around Him; His tent was darkness of waters; it thickened in the clouds of the air.

**22:13** From the radiance before Him coals of fire were kindled.

**22:14** The Lord thundered out of heaven, and the Most High gave forth His voice.

**22:15** And He sent forth arrows and scattered them, lightning, and threw them into dismay.

**22:16** And the channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the inhabited world were uncovered, at the rebuke of the Lord, from the blast of the breath of His wrath.

**22:17** He sent from on high and took me; He drew me out of many waters.

**22:18** He rescued me from my enemies of might, from those who hated me, because they had grown stronger than I.

**22:19** The days of my affliction overtook me, and the Lord became my support;

**22:20** and He led me forth into a broad place, and He delivered me, because He was well pleased with me.

**22:21** And the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He recompensed me.

**22:22** For I kept the ways of the Lord, and I did not act impiously away from my God.

**22:23** For all His judgments were before me, and His ordinances—I did not depart from them.

**22:24** And I shall be blameless before Him, and I shall keep watch against my iniquity.

**22:25** And the Lord shall repay me according to my righteousness and according to the cleanness of my hands before His eyes.

**22:26** With the devout You will show Yourself devout, and with a perfect man You will show Yourself perfect;

**22:27** and with the chosen You will be chosen, and with the crooked You will show Yourself crooked.

**22:28** And the poor people You will save, and eyes upon the haughty You will bring low.

**22:29** For You are my lamp, O Lord, and the Lord shall brighten my darkness for me.

**22:30** For by You I shall run girded for action, and by my God I shall scale a wall.

**22:31** As for the Mighty One, His way is blameless; the word of the Lord is strong, refined by fire; He is a shield to all who trust in Him.

**22:32** Who is mighty besides the Lord? And who shall be a Creator besides our God?

**22:33** It is the Mighty One who strengthens me with power, and He shook out my way blameless;

**22:34** making my feet like those of deer, and setting me upon the high places;

**22:35** teaching my hands for war, and breaking a bow of bronze by my arm.

**22:36** And You gave me the defense of my salvation, and Your attentiveness multiplied me, into a broad space for my steps beneath me,

**22:37** and my legs were not shaken.

**22:38** I will pursue my enemies and wipe them out, and I will not turn back until I make an end of them;

**22:39** and I will crush them, and they shall not rise, and they shall fall beneath my feet.

**22:40** And You shall strengthen me with power for war; You shall bend down beneath me those who rise up against me.

**22:41** And You gave me the back of my enemies, those who hated me, and You put them to death.

**22:42** They shall cry out, and there is no helper—even to the Lord, and He did not heed them.

**22:43** And I ground them fine like the dust of the earth; like the mire of the thoroughfares I beat them thin.

**22:44** And You shall rescue me from the strife of peoples; You shall preserve me as the head of nations. A people whom I did not know served me;

**22:45** foreign sons dealt falsely with me; at the hearing of the ear they obeyed me;

**22:46** foreign sons shall be cast away, and they shall stumble out of their enclosures.

**22:47** The Lord lives! And blessed be my guardian, and my God shall be exalted, the guardian of my salvation!

**22:48** Mighty is the Lord, who gives vengeance to me, chastening peoples beneath me,

**22:49** and leading me out from my enemies; and from those who rise up against me You shall lift me on high; from a man of wrongdoings You shall rescue me.

**22:50** Therefore I will give thanks to You, O Lord, among the nations, and in Your name I will sing praises,

**22:51** magnifying the deliverances of His king, and showing mercy to His anointed, to David and to his seed forever." And these are the last words of David.

## Translator Notes

- **22:6** — The Greek reads "of death" in both clauses and renders the second noun as "severities" (sklerotetes), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "cords of Sheol" and "snares of death."
- **22:30** — "Girded for action" translates the rare Greek adjective monozonos (literally "single-girded" or lightly armed for rapid combat), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to a raiding troop.
- **22:32** — The Greek calls God "Creator" (ktistes) rather than "Rock," reflecting a recurring Septuagint preference to avoid literal rock imagery for God (compare "guardian," phylax, in vv. 3 and 47).
- **22:36** — In this Greek text, "into a broad space for my steps beneath me" stands at the end of v. 36 rather than at the start of v. 37, and "Your attentiveness" (hypakoe) refers to God's gracious heeding of David's cry.
- **22:51** — The Greek text attaches the opening heading of the next section ("And these are the last words of David") to the end of 22:51 rather than placing it at the start of 23:1.
