## Text

**13:1** Now it came to pass after this that Absalom the son of David had a sister who was exceedingly fair in appearance, whose name was Tamar; and Amnon the son of David loved her.

**13:2** And Amnon was so distressed that he became ill because of Tamar his sister, for she was a virgin; and it seemed overwhelming in Amnon's eyes to do anything to her.

**13:3** Now Amnon had a companion whose name was Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, David's brother; and Jonadab was a very shrewd man.

**13:4** And he said to him, "What ails you, O son of the king, that you are so weak morning by morning? Will you not tell me?" And Amnon said to him, "I love Tamar, the sister of my brother Absalom."

**13:5** Then Jonadab said to him, "Lie down on your bed and pretend to be ill; and your father will come in to see you, and you shall say to him, 'Please let Tamar my sister come and feed me, and prepare food before my eyes, so that I may see it and eat from her hands.'"

**13:6** So Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill; and the king came in to see him. And Amnon said to the king, "Please let Tamar my sister come to me, and let her make two cakes before my eyes, and I will eat from her hand."

**13:7** Then David sent to Tamar at the house, saying, "Please go to the house of your brother and prepare food for him."

**13:8** So Tamar went to the house of Amnon her brother, and he was lying down. And she took the dough and kneaded it, and shaped cakes before his eyes, and cooked the cakes.

**13:9** And she took the pan and poured them out before him, but he refused to eat. Then Amnon said, "Have every man go out from my presence." And they sent every man out from his presence.

**13:10** Then Amnon said to Tamar, "Bring the food into the inner chamber, that I may eat from your hand." And Tamar took the cakes which she had made, and brought them to Amnon her brother in the bedchamber.

**13:11** And she brought them near to him to eat; and he seized her and said to her, "Come, lie with me, my sister."

**13:12** But she said to him, "No, my brother, do not humiliate me, for such a thing is not to be done in Israel; do not commit this folly.

**13:13** And as for me, where shall I carry my reproach? And you shall be as one of the fools in Israel. Now therefore, please speak to the king, for he will not withhold me from you."

**13:14** However, Amnon would not listen to her voice; and he overpowered her and humiliated her, and lay with her.

**13:15** Then Amnon hated her with an exceedingly great hatred, for the hatred with which he hated her was greater—the latter wickedness was greater than the first—than the love with which he had loved her. And Amnon said to her, "Arise and depart."

**13:16** And Tamar spoke to him concerning this great wickedness, "Greater than the other that you have done to me is to send me away!" Yet Amnon would not listen to her voice.

**13:17** Then he called his young servant who was in charge of the house and said to him, "Put this woman out from me, and bolt the door behind her."

**13:18** Now she had on a long-sleeved tunic, for thus the virgin daughters of the king clothed themselves in their outer garments. And his attendant brought her out and bolted the door behind her.

**13:19** Then Tamar took ashes and put ashes upon her head, and tore the long-sleeved tunic that was on her, and laid her hands upon her head, and went on her way crying aloud.

**13:20** And Absalom her brother said to her, "Has Amnon your brother been with you? Now therefore, my sister, keep silent, for he is your brother; do not set your heart to speak of this matter." So Tamar dwelt as a widow in the house of Absalom her brother.

**13:21** And King David heard all these things and was exceedingly angry; yet he did not grieve the spirit of Amnon his son, because he loved him, for he was his firstborn.

**13:22** And Absalom spoke to Amnon neither evil nor good; for Absalom hated Amnon because he had humiliated Tamar his sister.

**13:23** And it came to pass after two full years that Absalom had sheepshearers in Baal-hazor, which is near Ephraim; and Absalom invited all the king's sons.

**13:24** And Absalom came to the king and said, "Behold, now, your servant has sheepshearers; please let the king and his servants go with your servant."

**13:25** But the king said to Absalom, "No, my son, let us not all go, so that we may not be a burden to you." And he pressed him, yet he would not go, and he blessed him.

**13:26** Then Absalom said, "If not, please let Amnon my brother go with us." And the king said to him, "Why should he go with you?"

**13:27** But Absalom pressed him, so he sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him. And Absalom made a feast like the feast of the king.

**13:28** Now Absalom commanded his young servants, saying, "Watch when the heart of Amnon is merry with wine; and when I say to you, strike Amnon and put him to death. Do not fear, for is it not I who command you? Be courageous and be sons of valor."

**13:29** And the young servants of Absalom did to Amnon just as Absalom had commanded them. Then all the king's sons arose, and each man mounted his mule and fled.

**13:30** And it came to pass, while they were on the way, that the report came to David, saying, "Absalom has struck down all the king's sons, and not even one of them is left."

**13:31** Then the king arose and tore his garments and lay upon the ground; and all his servants who stood around him tore their garments.

**13:32** Then Jonadab the son of Shimeah, David's brother, answered and said, "Do not let my lord the king say that he has put to death all the young men, the king's sons, for Amnon alone is dead; for by the decree of Absalom this was set from the day that he humiliated Tamar his sister.

**13:33** Now therefore, do not let my lord the king take the matter to heart, saying, 'All the king's sons are dead'; for Amnon alone is dead."

**13:34** Meanwhile Absalom fled. And the young man who was the watchman lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, many people were coming along the road behind him from the side of the mountain on the descent. And the watchman came and reported to the king and said, "I have seen men from the road of Oronen, from the side of the mountain."

**13:35** Then Jonadab said to the king, "Behold, the king's sons have arrived; according to the word of your servant, so it has come to pass."

**13:36** And it came to pass, as soon as he had finished speaking, behold, the king's sons came, and they lifted up their voice and wept; and the king also and all his servants wept with a great weeping.

**13:37** But Absalom fled and went to Tholmailem the son of Ammihud, king of Geshur, into Machad. And King David mourned for his son all the days.

**13:38** So Absalom fled and went to Geshur, and was there three years.

**13:39** And King David ceased from going out to Absalom, for he was comforted concerning Amnon, because he was dead.

## Translator Notes

- **13:15** — The Greek text includes the clause 'the latter wickedness was greater than the first,' which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text and anticipates Tamar's words in verse sixteen.
- **13:21** — The Greek text includes 'yet he did not grieve the spirit of Amnon his son, because he loved him, for he was his firstborn,' a reading also supported at Qumran but absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:27** — The Greek text adds the closing sentence 'And Absalom made a feast like the feast of the king,' which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:34** — The Greek text adds the watchman's report to the king along with the distinct place name 'Oronen' (perhaps Horonaim or Beth-horon), which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:37** — The Greek text reads the distinct form 'Tholmailem' (corresponding to Talmai in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and adds the destination 'into Machad' (likely Maacah).
- **13:39** — The Greek verb ekopasen means 'ceased' or 'desisted' from going out against Absalom, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text is often translated 'longed.'
