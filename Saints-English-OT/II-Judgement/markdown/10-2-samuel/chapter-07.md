## Text

**7:1** And it came to pass, when the king sat in his house, and the Lord had given him an inheritance all around from all his surrounding enemies,

**7:2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Behold now, I dwell in a house of cedar, yet the ark of God sits in the midst of the tent."

**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, "All that is in your heart, go and do, for the Lord is with you."

**7:4** And it came to pass in that night that the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,

**7:5** "Go and say to My servant David, 'Thus says the Lord: You shall not build Me a house for Me to dwell in.

**7:6** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought up the sons of Israel out of Egypt until this day, but I have been walking about in a lodging and in a tent.

**7:7** In all the places where I walked among all Israel, have I ever spoken to any one tribe of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedar?"'

**7:8** And now, thus you shall say to My servant David, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: I took you from the sheepfold, that you should be ruler over My people, over Israel,

**7:9** and I was with you wherever you went, and I utterly destroyed all your enemies from before your face, and I made you renowned, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**7:10** And I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, and they shall dwell by themselves and shall be anxious no more; nor shall a son of iniquity afflict them anymore, as from the beginning,

**7:11** from the days that I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will give you rest from all your enemies, and the Lord will declare to you that you shall build a house for Him.

**7:12** And it shall be, when your days are fulfilled and you sleep with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come from your body, and I will prepare his kingdom.

**7:13** He shall build Me a house for My name, and I will establish his throne forever.

**7:14** I will be a Father to him, and he shall be a son to Me. And if his iniquity should come, I will reprove him with a rod of men and with the strokes of the sons of men.

**7:15** Yet My mercy I will not withdraw from him, just as I withdrew it from those whom I removed from before My face.

**7:16** And his house and his kingdom shall be made sure forever before Me, and his throne shall be established forever.'"

**7:17** According to all these words and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**7:18** Then King David went in and sat before the Lord, and he said, "Who am I, my Lord, O Lord, and what is my house, that You have loved me thus far?

**7:19** And I was made very small before You, my Lord, yet You have spoken concerning the house of Your servant for a great while to come; and this is the law of man, my Lord, O Lord.

**7:20** And what more shall David add to speak to You? And now You know Your servant, O Lord.

**7:21** Because of Your servant You have done it; and according to Your own heart You have done all this greatness, to make it known to Your servant, so as to magnify You,

**7:22** O Lord, my Lord; for there is none like You, nor is there any God besides You, in all that we have heard with our ears.

**7:23** And what other nation on the earth is like Your people Israel—how God led them to redeem for Himself a people, for You to establish a name, to work greatness and glorious manifestation, for You to drive out from before Your people, whom You redeemed for Yourself out of Egypt, nations and tents?

**7:24** And You have prepared for Yourself Your people Israel to be a people forever; and You, O Lord, have become their God.

**7:25** And now, my Lord, confirm forever the word which You have spoken concerning Your servant and his house, O Lord Almighty, God of Israel; and now, just as You have spoken,

**7:26** may Your name be magnified forever.

**7:27** O Lord Almighty, God of Israel, You have uncovered the ear of Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you.' Therefore Your servant has found it in his heart to pray this prayer to You.

**7:28** And now, my Lord, O Lord, You are God, and Your words shall be true, and You have spoken these good things concerning Your servant.

**7:29** And now begin and bless the house of Your servant, that it may be forever before You; for it is You, my Lord, O Lord, who have spoken, and from this blessing the house of Your servant shall be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Greek κατεκληρονόμησεν literally means "gave him an inheritance," reflecting the Hebrew root for inheriting where the Masoretic Text reads "gave him rest."
- **7:5** — The Septuagint presents a direct prohibition ("You shall not build Me a house") rather than the rhetorical question found in the Masoretic Text.
- **7:11** — The Greek reads "that you shall build a house for Him," differing from the Masoretic Text ("that the LORD will make you a house"), though 7:27 in the Greek retains "I will build a house for you."
- **7:15** — The Septuagint omits Saul's name and reads broadly "from those whom I removed from before My face," and in 7:16 shifts to the third person ("his house... before Me").
- **7:23** — Greek σκηνώματα ("tents") stands where the Masoretic Text reads "its gods," and ἐπιφάνειαν ("glorious manifestation") translates the Hebrew for awesome deeds.
