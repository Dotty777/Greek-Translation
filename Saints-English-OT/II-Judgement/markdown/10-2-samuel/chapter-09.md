## Text

**9:1** And David said, "Is there still anyone left of the house of Saul, that I may show him mercy for the sake of Jonathan?"

**9:2** Now there was a servant of the house of Saul whose name was Ziba, and they called him to David. And the king said to him, "Are you Ziba?" And he said, "I am your servant."

**9:3** And the king said, "Is there still a man left of the house of Saul, that I may show him the mercy of God?" And Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, stricken in his feet."

**9:4** And the king said, "Where is he?" And Ziba said to the king, "Behold, he is in the house of Machir the son of Amaer, from Lo Debar."

**9:5** Then King David sent and brought him from the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, from Lo Debar.

**9:6** And Mephibosheth the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, came to King David, and he fell on his face and paid homage to him. And David said to him, "Mephibosheth!" And he said, "Behold your servant."

**9:7** And David said to him, "Do not be afraid, for I will surely show mercy to you for the sake of Jonathan your father, and I will restore to you all the land of Saul your father's father; and you shall eat bread at my table continually."

**9:8** Then Mephibosheth paid homage and said, "Who am I, your servant, that you should look upon a dead dog such as I?"

**9:9** And the king called Ziba, the servant of Saul, and said to him, "All that belongs to Saul and to all his house I have given to your master's son.

**9:10** And you shall till the land for him, you and your sons and your servants, and you shall bring in bread for your master's son, and he shall eat bread; yet Mephibosheth, your master's son, shall eat bread continually at my table." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

**9:11** Then Ziba said to the king, "According to all that my lord the king has commanded his servant, so your servant will do." And Mephibosheth ate at the table of David as one of the king's own sons.

**9:12** And Mephibosheth had a young son whose name was Micha. And all who dwelt in the house of Ziba were servants of Mephibosheth.

**9:13** So Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem, for he ate continually at the king's table; and he was lame in both his feet.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek reads "Amaer" (Ἀμαὴρ) in 9:4, whereas 9:5 gives the standard form "Ammiel" (Ἀμειὴλ).
- **9:7** — The Septuagint explicitly reads "your father's father," clarifying Saul's relationship to Mephibosheth where the Masoretic Text simply reads "your father."
- **9:11** — The Septuagint presents the closing clause as third-person narrative ("And Mephibosheth ate at the table of David") rather than a continuation of David's direct speech.
