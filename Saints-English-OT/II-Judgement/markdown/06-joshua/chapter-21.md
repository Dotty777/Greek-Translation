## Text

**21:1** Then the heads of the fathers' houses of the sons of Levi came near to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua the son of Nun, and to the tribal heads of the fathers' houses from the tribes of Israel.

**21:2** And they spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "The Lord commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to dwell in, and the pasturelands for our livestock."

**21:3** So the sons of Israel gave to the Levites, in taking their inheritance by the command of the Lord, the cities and their pasturelands.

**21:4** And the lot came out for the clan of Kohath; and to the sons of Aaron the priests, the Levites, there fell by lot from the tribe of Judah, from the tribe of Simeon, and from the tribe of Benjamin thirteen cities.

**21:5** And to the remaining sons of Kohath there fell by lot out of the tribe of Ephraim, out of the tribe of Dan, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh ten cities.

**21:6** And to the sons of Gershon from the tribe of Issachar, from the tribe of Asher, from the tribe of Naphtali, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan came thirteen cities.

**21:7** And among the sons of Merari according to their clans, from the tribe of Reuben, from the tribe of Gad, and from the tribe of Zebulun, there fell by lot twelve cities.

**21:8** So the sons of Israel gave to the Levites the cities and their pasturelands by lot, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**21:9** And the tribe of the sons of Judah, the tribe of the sons of Simeon, and those from the tribe of the sons of Benjamin gave these cities, and they were assigned by name

**21:10** to the sons of Aaron, from the clan of Kohath of the sons of Levi, for this became the lot.

**21:11** And he gave them Kiriath-arba, the mother city of the Anakim (which is Hebron), in the hill country of Judah; but the pasturelands around it

**21:12** and the fields of the city and its villages Joshua gave to the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh for a possession.

**21:13** And to the sons of Aaron he gave the city of refuge for the manslayer, Hebron and the lands set apart with it, and Libnah and the lands set apart adjoining it,

**21:14** and Ailom and the lands set apart for it, and Tema and the lands set apart for it,

**21:15** and Gella and the lands set apart for it, and Debir and the lands set apart for it,

**21:16** and Asa and the lands set apart for it, and Tany and the lands set apart for it, and Beth-shemesh and the lands set apart for it: nine cities from these two tribes.

**21:17** And from the tribe of Benjamin, Gibeon and the lands set apart for it, and Gatheth and the lands set apart for it,

**21:18** and Anathoth and the lands set apart for it, and Gamala and the lands set apart for it: four cities.

**21:19** All the cities of the sons of Aaron the priests were thirteen.

**21:20** And to the clans of the sons of Kohath, the Levites who remained from the sons of Kohath, there came the city of their priests from the tribe of Ephraim.

**21:21** And they gave them the city of refuge for the manslayer, Shechem and the lands set apart for it, and Gezer with its adjoining places and the lands set apart for it,

**21:22** and Beth-horon and the lands set apart for it: four cities.

**21:23** And out of the tribe of Dan, Elkothaim and the lands set apart for it, and Gethedan and the lands set apart for it,

**21:24** and Aijalon and the lands set apart for it, and Gath-rimmon and the lands set apart for it: four cities.

**21:25** And from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Taanach and the lands set apart for it, and Jebatha and the lands set apart for it: two cities.

**21:26** All the cities were ten, with the set-apart lands adjoining them, for the clans of the remaining sons of Kohath.

**21:27** And to the sons of Gershon, the Levites, they gave out of the half-tribe of Manasseh the cities set apart for the manslayers—Golan in Bashan and the lands set apart for it, and Bosora and the lands set apart for it: two cities.

**21:28** And out of the tribe of Issachar, Kishion and the lands set apart for it, and Debba and the lands set apart for it,

**21:29** and Remmath and the lands set apart for it, and Fountain of Letters and the lands set apart for it: four cities.

**21:30** And out of the tribe of Asher, Basellan and the lands set apart for it, and Dabbon and the lands set apart for it,

**21:31** and Helkath and the lands set apart for it, and Rehob and the lands set apart for it: four cities.

**21:32** And out of the tribe of Naphtali, the city set apart for the manslayer, Kedesh in Galilee and the lands set apart for it, and Nemmath and the lands set apart for it, and Themmon and the lands set apart for it: three cities.

**21:33** All the cities of Gershon according to their clans were thirteen cities.

**21:34** And to the clan of the sons of Merari, the remaining Levites, they gave out of the tribe of Zebulun Maan and its pasturelands, and Kedesh and its pasturelands,

**21:35** and Sella and its pasturelands: three cities.

**21:36** And beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, out of the tribe of Reuben, the city of refuge for the manslayer, Bezer in the wilderness, Miso, and its pasturelands, and Jazer and its pasturelands,

**21:37** and Dekmon and its pasturelands, and Mapha and its pasturelands: four cities.

**21:38** And from the tribe of Gad, the city of refuge for the manslayer, even Ramoth in Gilead and its pasturelands, Kamein and its pasturelands,

**21:39** and Heshbon and its pasturelands, and Jazer and its pasturelands: all of them four cities.

**21:40** All the cities for the sons of Merari according to their clans, who remained from the tribe of Levi—their borders were twelve cities.

**21:41** All the cities of the Levites in the midst of the possession of the sons of Israel were forty-eight cities, and their pasturelands

**21:42** around these cities—a city and the pasturelands around the city for all these cities. And Joshua finished dividing the land within their borders. And the sons of Israel gave a portion to Joshua according to the command of the Lord; they gave him the city for which he asked—Timnath-serah they gave him in the hill country of Ephraim. And Joshua built the city and dwelt in it. And Joshua took the stone knives with which he had circumcised the sons of Israel who were born on the way in the wilderness, and he placed them in Timnath-serah.

**21:43** And the Lord gave to Israel all the land that He swore to give to their fathers, and they received it as an inheritance and dwelt in it.

**21:44** And the Lord granted them rest on every side, just as He had sworn to their fathers; not one stood before them out of all their enemies; the Lord delivered all their enemies into their hands.

**21:45** Not one failed of all the good words that the Lord had spoken to the sons of Israel; every one came to pass.

## Translator Notes

- **21:11** — The Greek reads "mother city of the Anakim" and links "the pasturelands around it" grammatically with v. 12 as part of what Joshua gave to the sons of Caleb, whereas the Hebrew text assigns Hebron's pasturelands to the Levites.
- **21:20** — The Greek text reads "the city of their priests" (polis ton hiereon auton), a distinctive Septuagint textual reading where the Hebrew has "the cities of their lot"; in v. 22 only three Ephraimite cities are named though the total remains four.
- **21:29** — "Fountain of Letters" translates the Greek Pegen grammaton, corresponding to En-gannim in the Hebrew text; several other Levitical town names across vv. 14–38 (such as Ailom, Tema, Gella, Jebatha, Bosora, Maan, Dekmon, and Kamein) likewise preserve distinctive Septuagint forms.
- **21:36** — The Septuagint includes the four Reubenite cities in vv. 36–37, treats the Hebrew term for "tableland" as the proper name Miso, and reads "Jazer" both here and in v. 39 (while vv. 34–35 name three Zebulunite cities though v. 40 sums the Merarite cities as twelve).
- **21:42** — The Septuagint adds an extended conclusion to v. 42 recounting Joshua's completion of the land allotment, his reception of Timnath-serah, and his placement there of the stone knives from the wilderness circumcision (cf. Joshua 5:2–3; 19:49–50).
