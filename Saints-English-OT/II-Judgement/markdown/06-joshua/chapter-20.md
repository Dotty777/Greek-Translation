## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying,

**20:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Appoint the cities of refuge of which I spoke to you through Moses,

**20:3** a refuge for the manslayer who strikes a person unintentionally; and the cities shall be a refuge for you, and the manslayer shall not die by the avenger of blood until he stands before the congregation for judgment.'"

**20:7** And he set apart Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali, and Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and the city of Arbok, which is Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**20:8** And beyond the Jordan he gave Bezer in the wilderness, on the plain, from the tribe of Reuben, and Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan from the tribe of Manasseh.

**20:9** These are the designated cities for the sons of Israel and for the sojourner who dwells among them, that whoever strikes a person unintentionally may flee there, lest he die by the hand of the avenger of blood until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — The Old Greek text moves directly from verse 3 to verse 7, omitting the procedural instructions found in verses 4–6 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text. 'Avenger of blood' renders Greek ho anchisteuon to haima, literally 'the kinsman who claims the blood.'
- **20:7** — The Greek verbs in verses 7–8 are singular ('he set apart,' 'he gave'), making Joshua the subject rather than plural Israel, and verse 7 renders Kiriath-arba as 'the city of Arbok.'
