## Text

**13:1** Now Joshua was old and advanced in days; and the Lord said to Joshua, "You are advanced in days, and much land remains for an inheritance.

**13:2** And this is the land that remains: the territories of the Philistines, the Geshurite, and the Canaanite,

**13:3** from the uninhabited region that faces Egypt as far as the borders of Ekron on the left of the Canaanites, it is reckoned to the five satrapies of the Philistines: the Gazite, the Ashdodite, the Ashkelonite, the Gittite, the Ekronite, and the Hivite;

**13:4** from Teman, and to all the land of Canaan opposite Gaza, and the Sidonians as far as Taphek, as far as the borders of the Amorites;

**13:5** and all the land of Galiath of the Philistines, and all Lebanon toward the rising of the sun, from Galgaa below Mount Hermon as far as the entrance of Hamath;

**13:6** everyone who dwells in the hill country from Lebanon as far as Masereth-memphonmaim, all the Sidonians—I Myself will utterly destroy them from before the face of Israel; only distribute it by lot to Israel, just as I commanded you.

**13:7** And now divide this land as an inheritance to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**13:8** from the Jordan as far as the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun you shall give it; the Great Sea shall be the boundary." To the tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh, to Reuben and to Gad, Moses gave beyond the Jordan; toward the rising of the sun Moses the servant of the Lord gave to them,

**13:9** from Aroer, which is on the bank of the brook Arnon, and the city that is in the midst of the valley, and all Mishor from Daidaban;

**13:10** all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the borders of the sons of Ammon;

**13:11** and Gilead, and the borders of the Geshurite and of the Maachathite, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Acha;

**13:12** all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Edrei and in Ashtaroth; he was left from among the giants, and Moses struck him down and utterly destroyed him.

**13:13** Yet the sons of Israel did not utterly destroy the Geshurite and the Maachathite and the Canaanite; and the king of the Geshurite and the Maachathite dwelt among the sons of Israel to this very day.

**13:14** Only to the tribe of Levi no inheritance was given; the Lord God of Israel, He is their inheritance, just as the Lord said to them. And this is the apportionment which Moses apportioned to the sons of Israel in Araboth of Moab, beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**13:15** And Moses gave to the tribe of Reuben according to their clans.

**13:16** And their borders were from Aroer, which is opposite the valley of Arnon, and the city that is in the valley of Arnon, and all Mishor

**13:17** as far as Heshbon, and all the cities that are in Mishor, and Dibon, and Baimon-baal, and House of Meelboth,

**13:18** and Basan, and Bakedmoth, and Mephaath,

**13:19** and Kiriathaim, and Sibmah, and Serada, and Seion on Mount Enab,

**13:20** and Beth-peor, and Ashdoth-pisgah, and Beth-thasinoth,

**13:21** and all the cities of Mishor, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, whom Moses struck down—him and the leaders of Midian, both Evi and Robok and Zur and Hur and Robe the ruler, spoils of Sihon—and those who dwelt in Sihon.

**13:22** Also Balaam the son of Beor, the diviner, they slew in the rout.

**13:23** And the borders of Reuben were these: the Jordan was the boundary. This is the inheritance of the sons of Reuben according to their clans, their cities and their villages.

**13:24** And Moses gave to the sons of Gad according to their clans.

**13:25** And their borders were Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the sons of Ammon as far as Araba, which is opposite Arad,

**13:26** and from Heshbon as far as Araboth opposite Mizpeh, and Botani and Baan as far as the borders of Dibon,

**13:27** and Enadom and Othargaei and Bainthanabra and Succoth and Zaphon, and the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon; and the Jordan shall be the boundary as far as a part of the Sea of Chinnereth, beyond the Jordan on the east.

**13:28** This is the inheritance of the sons of Gad according to their clans—they shall turn their neck against their enemies, for it came to be according to their clans—their cities and their villages.

**13:29** And Moses gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh according to their clans.

**13:30** And their borders were from Maana, and all the kingdom of Bashan, and all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the villages of Jair which are in Bashan, sixty cities;

**13:31** and half of Gilead, and in Ashtaroth and in Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, for those of Machir, sons of Manasseh, and for half the sons of Machir, sons of Manasseh, according to their clans.

**13:32** These are those to whom Moses distributed an inheritance beyond the Jordan in Araboth of Moab, on the other side of the Jordan opposite Jericho to the east.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Septuagint reads "the uninhabited region" (τῆς ἀοικήτου) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the river "Shihor," and uses "the Hivite" (τῷ Εὐαίῳ) where the Hebrew reads the Avvim.
- **13:5** — "Galiath of the Philistines" and "Galgaa" preserve Septuagint-distinct names where the Hebrew has "the Gebalites" and "Baal-gad"; similarly "Taphek" (13:4), "Masereth-memphonmaim" (13:6), "Daidaban" (13:9), and "Acha" (13:11) reflect the Greek text.
- **13:8** — The opening clause extending the Lord's speech to the Great Sea is a Septuagint addition absent from the Hebrew text.
- **13:21** — The Greek preserves distinctive readings across 13:17–27—including "spoils of Sihon" (ἔναρα Σειών) and "those who dwelt in Sihon" here, "in the rout" (ἐν τῇ ῥοπῇ) in 13:22, and Septuagint place names such as "House of Meelboth," "Serada," "Seion on Mount Enab," "Araba," "Arad," "Enadom," and "Othargaei."
- **13:28** — The clause "they shall turn their neck against their enemies, for it came to be according to their clans" is a distinctive Septuagint addition not present in the Hebrew text.
