## Text

**2:1** And Joshua the son of Naue sent out from Shittim two young men to spy, saying, "Go up and see the land and Jericho." And going forth, the two young men entered into Jericho, and entered into the house of a woman, a harlot, whose name was Rahab, and they lodged there.

**2:2** And it was reported to the king of Jericho, saying, "Men of the sons of Israel have come in here to spy out the land."

**2:3** And the king of Jericho sent and spoke to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who have entered into your house in the night, for they have come to spy out the land."

**2:4** And the woman took the two men and hid them, and she spoke to them, saying, "The men did come in to me;

**2:5** but as the gate was being shut in the darkness, the men also went out. I do not know where they have gone; pursue after them, if you may overtake them."

**2:6** Now she had brought them up onto the roof, and had hidden them in the stalks of flax that were stacked by her upon the roof.

**2:7** And the men pursued after them along the road to the Jordan, to the fords, and the gate was shut.

**2:8** And it came to pass, when those pursuing after them had gone out, and before they themselves lay down to sleep, she went up onto the roof to them

**2:9** and said to them, "I know that the Lord has given you the land, for the terror of you has fallen upon us.

**2:10** For we have heard that the Lord God dried up the Red Sea before your face when you came out of the land of Egypt, and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you utterly destroyed.

**2:11** And when we heard, we were dismayed in our heart, and no spirit remained any longer in any of us before your face; for the Lord your God is He who is in heaven above and upon the earth beneath.

**2:12** And now, swear to me by the Lord God, since I show mercy to you, show mercy also in the house of my father,

**2:13** and preserve alive the house of my father, my mother and my brothers and all my house and all things that belong to them, and you shall deliver my soul from death."

**2:14** And the men said to her, "Our life for yours, even to death." And she said, "When the Lord delivers the city to you, you shall show mercy and truth toward me."

**2:15** And she let them down through the window.

**2:16** And she said to them, "Depart into the hill country, lest those pursuing meet you, and you shall hide yourselves there three days until those pursuing after you return; and after these things you shall depart on your way."

**2:17** And the men said to her, "We are guiltless of this oath of yours:

**2:18** Behold, we are entering into a part of the city, and you shall set the sign: this scarlet cord you shall tie in the window through which you let us down; and your father, and your mother, and your brothers, and all the house of your father you shall gather to yourself into your house.

**2:19** And it shall be that everyone who goes outside the door of your house shall be guilty for himself, and we shall be guiltless of this oath of yours; but as many as are with you in your house, we shall be guilty.

**2:20** Yet if anyone wrongs us or even reveals these words of ours, we shall be guiltless of this oath of yours."

**2:21** And she said to them, "According to your word let it be." And she sent them away.

**2:22** And they went and came into the hill country, and remained there three days; and those pursuing searched out all the roads, and did not find them.

**2:23** And the two young men returned and came down from the mountain, and crossed over to Joshua the son of Naue, and recounted to him all the things that had happened to them.

**2:24** And they said to Joshua, "The Lord has delivered all the land into our hand, and every inhabitant of that land cowers in dread before us."

## Translator Notes

- **2:14** — In the Greek text, Rahab speaks the second half of this verse ("When the Lord delivers the city to you..."), whereas in the Hebrew Masoretic Text the two men continue speaking.
- **2:18** — The Greek reads "into a part of the city," whereas the Hebrew reads "into the land."
- **2:19** — The Greek legal terms "guilty for himself" and "we shall be guilty" correspond to the Hebrew blood-guilt expressions concerning blood being on one's head.
