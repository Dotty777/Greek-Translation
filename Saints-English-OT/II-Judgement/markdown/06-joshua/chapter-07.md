## Text

**7:1** Now the children of Israel committed a great trespass and kept back for themselves from the accursed thing; for Achar the son of Carmi, the son of Zambri, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took from the accursed thing; and the Lord burned with wrath against the children of Israel.

**7:2** And Joshua sent men to Ai, which is by Bethel, saying, "Spy out Ai."

**7:3** So the men went up and spied out Ai, and they returned to Joshua and said to him, "Do not let all the people go up, but let about two thousand or three thousand men go up and take the city by siege; do not lead all the people up there, for they are few."

**7:4** So about three thousand men went up, and they fled from before the men of Ai.

**7:5** And the men of Ai slew about thirty-six of them, and pursued them from the gate and shattered them down the descent; and the heart of the people was dismayed and became like water.

**7:6** Then Joshua tore his garments, and Joshua fell to the earth upon his face before the Lord until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they cast dust upon their heads.

**7:7** And Joshua said, "I pray, O Lord, why did Your servant bring this people across the Jordan, to deliver them to the Amorite to destroy us? If only we had remained and settled by the Jordan!

**7:8** And what shall I say, since Israel has turned his back before his enemy?

**7:9** For when the Canaanite and all the inhabitants of the land hear of it, they will surround us and blot us out from the earth; and what will You do for Your great name?"

**7:10** Then the Lord said to Joshua, "Arise! Why have you fallen thus upon your face?

**7:11** The people have sinned and have transgressed the covenant which I made with them; having stolen from the accursed thing, they have placed it among their goods.

**7:12** The children of Israel shall not be able to stand before their enemies; they shall turn their backs before their enemies, because they have become accursed. I will no longer be with you, unless you remove the accursed thing from among yourselves.

**7:13** Arise, sanctify the people, and tell them to sanctify themselves for tomorrow. Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'The accursed thing is in your midst; you shall not be able to stand against your enemies until you remove the accursed thing from among you.

**7:14** And in the morning you shall all be gathered together by tribes; and it shall be that the tribe which the Lord indicates, you shall bring near by clans; and the clan which the Lord indicates, you shall bring near by household; and the household which the Lord indicates, you shall bring near man by man.

**7:15** And whoever is indicated shall be burned with fire, and all that belongs to him, because he has transgressed the covenant of the Lord and has committed a lawless deed in Israel.'"

**7:16** So Joshua rose early in the morning and brought the people near by tribes, and the tribe of Judah was indicated.

**7:17** And it was brought near by clans, and the clan of the Zerahites was indicated; and it was brought near man by man,

**7:18** and Achar the son of Zambri, the son of Zerah, was indicated.

**7:19** Then Joshua said to Achar, "Give glory today to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession; declare to me what you have done, and do not hide it from me."

**7:20** And Achar answered Joshua and said, "Truly I have sinned before the Lord God of Israel; thus and thus I have done.

**7:21** I saw among the spoil a fine embroidered robe, and two hundred didrachmas of silver, and one wedge of gold of fifty didrachmas, and having coveted them, I took them; and behold, they are hidden in my tent, and the silver is hidden beneath them."

**7:22** So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent in the camp; and these things were hidden in the tent, and the silver was beneath them.

**7:23** And they brought them out from the tent and brought them to Joshua and the elders of Israel, and laid them before the Lord.

**7:24** Then Joshua took Achar the son of Zerah and brought him up to the Valley of Achor, along with his sons and his daughters, his oxen, his beasts of burden, all his sheep, his tent, and all his possessions, and all the people were with him; and he brought them up to Emek-Achor.

**7:25** And Joshua said to Achar, "Why have you brought destruction upon us? May the Lord utterly destroy you, even as today."

**7:26** And all Israel stoned him with stones, and they raised over him a great heap of stones; and the Lord ceased from the fierceness of His wrath. Therefore he named it Emek-Achor, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek text reads "Achar" and "Zambri" (corresponding to "Achan" and "Zabdi" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text); the form "Achar" matches 1 Chronicles 2:7 and directly echoes the place name "Achor" in 7:24-26.
- **7:7** — Following the Greek text, Joshua asks why "Your servant" brought the people across the Jordan, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text addresses God directly ("why have You brought").
- **7:21** — The Greek describes the garment as a "fine embroidered robe" (rather than "Babylonian garment" as in the Hebrew) and measures the silver and gold in "didrachmas," the standard Septuagint equivalent for shekels.
- **7:24** — The Greek text includes both the translated name "the Valley of Achor" and the Hebrew transliteration "Emek-Achor" (also in 7:26) within the same verse.
