## Text

**17:1** Now the borders of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh fell—for he was the firstborn of Joseph—to Machir the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead, for he was a man of war, in the territory of Gilead and in the territory of Bashan.

**17:2** And it also came to the rest of the sons of Manasseh according to their families: to the sons of Jezi, and to the sons of Kelez, and to the sons of Jeziel, and to the sons of Shechem, and to the sons of Symarim, and to the sons of Hepher; these were the males according to their families.

**17:3** Now Zelophehad the son of Hepher had no sons, but only daughters; and these are the names of the daughters of Zelophehad: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**17:4** And they stood before Eleazar the priest and before Joshua and before the rulers, saying, "God commanded by the hand of Moses to give us an inheritance in the midst of our brethren." And by the command of the Lord a lot was given to them among the brethren of their father.

**17:5** And their measured portion fell from Anassa, and the plain of Labek out of Gilead, which is beyond the Jordan,

**17:6** because the daughters of the sons of Manasseh inherited a lot in the midst of their brethren. And the land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the sons of Manasseh.

**17:7** And the borders of the sons of Manasseh were Delanath, which is before the sons of Anath, and they proceed to the borders, to Hiamin and Jassib, to the spring of Thaphthoth;

**17:8** it shall belong to Manasseh, and Thapheth on the borders of Manasseh shall belong to the sons of Ephraim.

**17:9** And the borders shall go down to the valley of Karana southward by the valley of Jariel—a terebinth tree belonging to Ephraim in the midst of the city of Manasseh; and the borders of Manasseh were on the north to the brook. And its outlet shall be the sea,

**17:10** on the south for Ephraim, and on the north for Manasseh; and the sea shall be their borders; and they shall border upon Jaseb on the north, and Issachar on the east.

**17:11** And Manasseh shall have in Issachar and in Asher Kaithoan and their villages, and the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages, and the third part of Mapheta and its villages.

**17:12** Yet the sons of Manasseh were not able to utterly destroy these cities; and the Canaanite began to dwell in this land.

**17:13** And it came to pass, when the sons of Israel grew strong, that they made the Canaanites subject to them, but they did not utterly destroy them.

**17:14** Now the sons of Joseph objected to Joshua, saying, "Why have you given us an inheritance of one lot and one measured portion, whereas I am a numerous people, and God has blessed me?"

**17:15** And Joshua said to them, "If you are a numerous people, go up into the forest and clear a place for yourself, if Mount Ephraim is too narrow for you."

**17:16** And they said, "Mount Ephraim does not please us; and the Canaanite who dwells in it, in Beth Shan and in its villages, in the valley of Israel, has choice horses and iron."

**17:17** And Joshua said to the sons of Joseph, "If you are a numerous people and have great strength, you shall not have one lot;

**17:18** for the forest shall be yours, because it is a forest, and you shall clear it, and it shall be yours even when you utterly destroy the Canaanite, though he has choice horses; for you are stronger than he."

## Translator Notes

- **17:5** — The Greek reads "from Anassa, and the plain of Labek," a notable Septuagint divergence from the Hebrew text ("ten portions fell to Manasseh, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan"); verse 2 likewise preserves the Septuagint-distinct clan names Jezi, Kelez, Jeziel, and Symarim.
- **17:7** — The Greek preserves several Septuagint-distinct geographical names across verses 7 through 11, including Delanath, sons of Anath, Hiamin, Jassib, Thaphthoth, Karana, Jariel, Jaseb (for Asher in verse 10), Kaithoan, and "the third part of Mapheta" (verse 11).
- **17:9** — The Greek reads "a terebinth tree," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonantal text that the Masoretic Text reads as "these [cities]."
- **17:16** — The Greek reads "choice horses and iron" (Hebrew "chariots of iron") and "the valley of Israel" (Hebrew "the valley of Jezreel").
