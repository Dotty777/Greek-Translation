## Text

**15:1** And the border of the tribe of Judah according to their families was from the borders of Judea, from the wilderness of Zin as far as Kadesh toward the southwest.

**15:2** And their border on the southwest was as far as a part of the Salt Sea, from the ridge that faces toward the southwest;

**15:3** and it passes across opposite the ascent of Akrabbim, and goes around Ennak, and goes up on the southwest to Kadesh Barnea, and goes out to Hezron, and goes up to Sarada, and goes out by the way west of Kadesh,

**15:4** and goes out to Selmona, and shall extend as far as the Valley of Egypt, and the outlet of its border shall be at the sea; this is their border on the southwest.

**15:5** And the border on the east is all the Salt Sea as far as the Jordan. And as for their border on the north, both from the ridge of the sea and from the part of the Jordan,

**15:6** the border goes up to Beth-hoglah, and passes along on the north to Beth-arabah, and the border goes up to the stone of Bohan the son of Reuben;

**15:7** and the border goes up to the fourth part of the Valley of Achor, and goes down to Taagad, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, which is to the southwest of the valley, and it shall extend to the water of the Spring of the Sun, and its outlet shall be the Spring of Rogel;

**15:8** and the border goes up into the Valley of Hinnom on the south of Jebus from the southwest—this is Jerusalem—and the border extends to the top of the mountain which is in front of the Valley of Hinnom toward the sea, which is at a part of the land of Rephaim on the north;

**15:9** and the border extends from the top of the mountain to the spring of the water of Maphtho, and extends to Mount Ephron, and the border shall lead to Jebaal—this is the city of Jearim;

**15:10** and the border shall turn around from Baal toward the sea, and shall pass on to Mount Assar along the slope of the city of Jearim on the north—this is Chesalon—and shall go down to the City of the Sun, and shall pass on toward the southwest;

**15:11** and the border shall extend along the slope of Ekron toward the north, and the border shall extend to Succoth, and the border shall pass on toward the southwest, and shall extend to Lemna, and the outlet of the border shall be at the sea.

**15:12** And as for their border on the side of the sea, the Great Sea shall mark the boundary. These are the borders of the sons of Judah all around according to their families.

**15:13** And to Caleb the son of Jephunneh he gave a portion in the midst of the sons of Judah by the command of God; and Joshua gave him the city of Arba, the mother city of Anak—this is Hebron.

**15:14** And Caleb the son of Jephunneh utterly destroyed from there the three sons of Anak—Sheshai, Talmai, and Ahiman.

**15:15** And Caleb went up from there against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir was formerly City of Letters.

**15:16** And Caleb said, "Whoever takes and cuts down the City of Letters and gains mastery over it, I will give him Achsah my daughter as a wife."

**15:17** And Othniel the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, took it; and he gave him Achsah his daughter as a wife.

**15:18** And it came to pass, as she was going out, that she took counsel with him, saying, "I will ask my father for a field." And she cried out from the donkey. And Caleb said to her, "What is the matter with you?"

**15:19** And she said to him, "Give me a blessing, for you have given me into the land of the Negev; give me Boththanis." And he gave her the upper Gonaithla and the lower Gonaithla.

**15:20** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — The Greek text reads "Judea" (Ioudaias) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Edom." Throughout this boundary description, Greek lips ("southwest") represents both southern and western directions.
- **15:7** — The Greek reads "the fourth part of the Valley of Achor" and "Taagad" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Debir from the Valley of Achor" and "Gilgal," and translates En-shemesh literally as "Spring of the Sun" (compare "City of the Sun" in 15:10).
- **15:13** — The Greek reads "by the command of God" and calls the city of Arba the "mother city" (metropolin) of Anak, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "father of Anak."
- **15:19** — The Greek treats the Hebrew expressions for "springs of water" and "the upper springs and the lower springs" as proper names: "Boththanis" and "the upper Gonaithla and the lower Gonaithla."
- **15:21** — The Septuagint list of southern cities in verses 21-32 preserves numerous distinct Greek place-name forms (including Kaibaiseleel, Ara, Ikam, Regma, Aruel, Hasorionain, Mainam, Balmainan, Sen, Salmaa, Serei, Cholaseola, Bakok, Elboudad, Macharim, Sethennak, Labos, Sale, and Eromoth) that differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:44** — The Greek counts ten cities in this district (including Akiezei, Kezib, Bathesar, and Aijalon) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text counts nine; throughout verses 33-62, the Septuagint preserves numerous distinct Greek city-name forms that differ from the Hebrew.
- **15:46** — The Greek renders the Hebrew phrase for "and to the sea" as the place name "Gemna."
- **15:59** — The Septuagint preserves a major additional district of eleven cities headed by Tekoa and Ephrathah (Bethlehem) that is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:62** — The Greek omits the heading "In the wilderness" at verse 61, reads "the cities of Sodom" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "City of Salt," and counts seven cities rather than six.
- **15:63** — The Greek reads "destroy them" (Hebrew "drive them out"), omits "with the sons of Judah," and ends with "until that day" rather than "to this day."
