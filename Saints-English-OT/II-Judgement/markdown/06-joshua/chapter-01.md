## Text

**1:1** Now it came to pass after the death of Moses that the Lord spoke to Joshua the son of Naue, the attendant of Moses, saying,

**1:2** "Moses My servant has died. Now therefore, arise and cross over the Jordan, you and all this people, into the land which I am giving to them.

**1:3** Every place upon which you step with the sole of your feet, to you I will give it, just as I have spoken to Moses:

**1:4** the wilderness and Antilebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, and as far as the uttermost sea; from the setting of the sun shall be your borders.

**1:5** No man shall withstand in your presence all the days of your life; and just as I was with Moses, so I will also be with you, and I will not forsake you nor overlook you.

**1:6** Be strong and valiant; for you shall apportion to this people the land which I swore to your fathers to give to them.

**1:7** Therefore be strong and valiant, to observe and to do according as Moses My servant commanded you, and you shall not turn aside from them to the right nor to the left, that you may have understanding in all that you do.

**1:8** And the book of this law shall not depart from your mouth, and you shall meditate in it day and night, that you may know how to do all the things that are written; then you shall be prospered, and it shall prosper your ways, and then you shall understand.

**1:9** Behold, I have commanded you: be strong and valiant; do not be fainthearted nor be afraid, for the Lord your God is with you in all places wherever you may go."

**1:10** Then Joshua commanded the scribes of the people, saying,

**1:11** "Enter through the midst of the camp of the people, and command the people, saying, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, for yet three days and you are crossing over this Jordan, entering in to take possession of the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving to you.'"

**1:12** And to Reuben and to Gad and to the half-tribe of Manasseh Joshua said,

**1:13** "Remember the word which Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, saying, 'The Lord your God has given you rest and has given you this land.'

**1:14** Let your wives, your little ones, and your livestock dwell in the land which He has given to you; but you shall cross over girded for battle before your brethren, everyone who is strong, and you shall fight alongside them,

**1:15** until the Lord our God gives rest to your brethren even as to you also, and they also inherit the land which the Lord our God is giving to them; and you shall depart, each man to his inheritance, which Moses has given to you beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun."

**1:16** And answering Joshua, they said, "All things whatever you command us we will do, and to every place wherever you send us we will go.

**1:17** According to all things in which we heeded Moses, we will heed you; only may the Lord our God be with you just as He was with Moses.

**1:18** And the man who disobeys you, and whoever does not heed your words according as you command him, let him die. Only be strong and valiant."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek Naue corresponds to Hebrew Nun, the father of Joshua.
- **1:4** — The Greek reads Antilebanon, the mountain range east of Lebanon, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Lebanon, and omits the phrase concerning the land of the Hittites.
- **1:8** — The Greek verb is third-person singular ("it shall prosper your ways," referring to the book of the law), whereas the Hebrew has second-person singular ("you shall make your ways prosperous").
