## Text

**8:1** Then the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear, nor be dismayed; take with you all the men of war, and arise, go up to Ai. Behold, I have delivered into your hands the king of Ai and his land.

**8:2** And you shall do to Ai as you did to Jericho and its king, and the spoil of the livestock you shall take as plunder for yourself. Set an ambush for yourself behind the city."

**8:3** So Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up to Ai; and Joshua chose thirty thousand men mighty in strength, and sent them out by night.

**8:4** And he commanded them, saying, "Lie in ambush behind the city; do not go far from the city, and you shall all be ready.

**8:5** And I and all who are with me will approach the city; and it shall come to pass, when the inhabitants of Ai come out to meet us as previously, that we will flee from before them.

**8:6** And when they come out after us, we will draw them away from the city; and they will say, 'These men are fleeing from before us just as at the first.'

**8:7** Then you shall rise up from the ambush and march into the city.

**8:8** According to this word you shall do; behold, I have commanded you."

**8:9** So Joshua sent them out, and they went to the place of ambush and waited between Bethel and Ai, on the west of Ai.

**8:10** And Joshua rose early in the morning and mustered the people; and they and the elders went up before the people to Ai.

**8:11** And all the people of war who were with him went up, and marching on, they came opposite the city on the east,

**8:12** while the ambush of the city was on the west.

**8:14** Now it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that he hurried and went out directly to battle to meet them, he and all the people who were with him; and he did not know that an ambush was set against him behind the city.

**8:15** And Joshua and Israel saw it and withdrew from before them.

**8:16** And they pursued after the children of Israel, and they themselves drew away from the city;

**8:17** not one man was left in Ai who did not pursue after Israel; and they left the city open and pursued after Israel.

**8:18** Then the Lord said to Joshua, "Stretch out your hand with the javelin that is in your hand toward the city, for into your hands I have delivered it; and the ambush shall rise up swiftly from their place." And Joshua stretched out his hand—the javelin—toward the city;

**8:19** and the ambush rose up swiftly from their place and came forth as soon as he stretched out his hand, and they came upon the city and captured it; and hurrying, they burned the city with fire.

**8:20** And when the inhabitants of Ai looked back behind them, they beheld smoke ascending from the city to heaven; and they no longer had anywhere to flee, this way or that.

**8:21** And Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city ascended to heaven; and turning back, they struck down the men of Ai.

**8:22** And the others came out from the city to meet them, so that they were in the midst of the camp, some on this side and some on that side; and they struck them down until not one of them was left who survived or escaped.

**8:23** But the king of Ai they captured alive, and brought him to Joshua.

**8:24** And when the children of Israel had finished slaying all those of Ai who were in the plains and on the mountain at the descent where they had pursued them from it to the very end, Joshua returned to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.

**8:25** And those who fell on that day, from man even to woman, were twelve thousand—all the inhabitants of Ai.

**8:27** However, all the spoils that were in the city the children of Israel took as plunder for themselves, according to the command of the Lord, just as the Lord had directed Joshua.

**8:28** And Joshua burned the city with fire; he made it an uninhabited mound forever, to this day.

**8:29** And the king of Ai he hanged upon a forked tree, and he remained on the tree until evening; and as the sun was setting, Joshua gave orders, and they took down his body from the tree and cast him into the pit, and raised over him a heap of stones, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **8:12** — The Greek text presents a single ambush of thirty thousand men (8:3) stationed on the west (literally "from the sea," as also in 8:9), omitting the separate five-thousand-man ambush and verse 13 found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:18** — The Greek term denotes a heavy javelin or spear; the Greek text also includes the assurance that the ambush will rise up swiftly as part of the Lord's spoken command.
- **8:25** — The Greek text omits verse 26 (concerning Joshua keeping his javelin outstretched) and moves directly to the taking of the spoils in verse 27.
- **8:29** — The Greek phrase literally means "double wood," indicating a forked tree or two-post gallows; the Greek also states that the king's body was cast "into the pit" rather than at the city gate as in the Hebrew, and places the Mount Ebal passage (Hebrew 8:30-35) after 9:2.
