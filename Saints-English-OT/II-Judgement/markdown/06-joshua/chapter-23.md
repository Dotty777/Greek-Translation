## Text

**23:1** Now it came to pass after many days, after the Lord had given rest to Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua was old and advanced in days.

**23:2** And Joshua summoned all the sons of Israel, and their council of elders, and their rulers, and their scribes, and their judges, and said to them, "I have grown old and am advanced in days.

**23:3** And you have seen all that the Lord our God has done to all these nations before our face, for it is the Lord our God who has fought for us.

**23:4** Behold, just as I spoke to you, these nations that remain to you are in the lots for your tribes, from the Jordan—even all the nations that I have destroyed—and from the Great Sea it shall mark the border toward the setting of the sun.

**23:5** And the Lord our God, He shall destroy them from before our face until they perish; and He shall send the wild beasts against them until He destroys them and their kings from before your face; and inherit their land, just as the Lord our God spoke to you.

**23:6** Be exceedingly strong, therefore, to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, that you may not turn aside to the right hand or to the left;

**23:7** so that you do not enter in among these nations that remain, and the names of their gods shall not be named among you; you shall by no means bow down to them, nor shall you serve them,

**23:8** but to the Lord our God you shall cling, just as you have done until this day.

**23:9** And the Lord shall destroy them from before your face—nations great and strong—and no one has withstood before us until this day.

**23:10** One of you pursued a thousand, for the Lord our God fought for us, just as He spoke to us.

**23:11** And take diligent heed to love the Lord our God.

**23:12** For if you turn back and join yourselves to these remaining nations that are with you, and make marriages with them, and mingle yourselves with them and they with you,

**23:13** know for certain that the Lord shall no longer continue to destroy these nations from before your face; rather, they shall be to you as snares and stumbling blocks, and as nails in your heels and darts in your eyes, until you perish from this good land which the Lord your God has given you.

**23:14** And I am departing the way, even as all who are upon the earth; and you shall know in your heart and in your soul that not one word has fallen from all the words which the Lord our God spoke concerning all things pertaining to us; not one of them has failed.

**23:15** And it shall come to pass that just as all the good words which the Lord spoke to us have come upon us, so shall the Lord God bring upon you all the evil words, until He destroys you from this good land which the Lord has given you,

**23:16** when you transgress the covenant of the Lord our God, which He commanded us, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them."

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — The Greek clause is elliptical and reads "just as I spoke to you," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I have allotted to you."
- **23:5** — The Greek text includes the promise that the Lord will send wild beasts against the remaining nations and their kings, a clause absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:13** — The Greek text renders the imagery as "nails in your heels and darts in your eyes," corresponding to "scourges on your sides and thorns in your eyes" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:16** — The Greek text concludes the chapter here, without the final clause concerning the anger of the Lord burning against Israel found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
