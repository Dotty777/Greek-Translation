## Text

**6:1** Now Jericho was shut up and fortified, and no one went out from it nor came in.

**6:2** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Behold, I deliver Jericho into your hand, and its king who is in it, who are mighty in strength.

**6:3** And you shall station the men of war around it in a circle.

**6:5** And it shall come to pass, when you sound the trumpet, let all the people shout aloud together; and when they shout aloud, the walls of the city shall fall down of themselves, and all the people shall rush in, every man straight ahead into the city."

**6:6** And Joshua the son of Nun went in to the priests

**6:7** and spoke to them, saying, "Command the people to go around and encompass the city, and let the fighting men pass along armed before the Lord;

**6:8** and let seven priests having seven sacred trumpets pass along likewise before the Lord and sound vigorously, and let the ark of the covenant of the Lord follow after.

**6:9** And let the fighting men march in front, and the priests forming the rear guard behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord, sounding the trumpets."

**6:10** Now Joshua commanded the people, saying, "Do not cry out, nor let anyone hear your voice, until the day he himself gives the command to shout aloud; and then you shall shout aloud."

**6:11** And when the ark of the covenant of God had gone around, it immediately returned to the camp and lodged there.

**6:12** And on the second day Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord,

**6:13** and the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets went forward before the Lord; and after this the fighting men marched along, and the rest of the multitude behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord;

**6:14** and the priests sounded the trumpets, and the entire remaining multitude surrounded the city close by and returned again to the camp. Thus they did for six days.

**6:15** And on the seventh day they rose at dawn, and marched around the city six times;

**6:16** and on the seventh circuit the priests sounded the trumpets, and Joshua said to the children of Israel, "Shout aloud, for the Lord has delivered the city to you!

**6:17** And the city shall be devoted to destruction, it and all things that are in it, to the Lord of hosts. Only spare Rahab the harlot, her and all that is in her house.

**6:18** But guard yourselves carefully from what is devoted to destruction, lest, desiring it in your heart, you yourselves take of what is devoted to destruction, and make the camp of the children of Israel a thing devoted to destruction, and utterly destroy us.

**6:19** And all silver or gold or bronze or iron shall be holy to the Lord; it shall be brought into the treasury of the Lord."

**6:20** And the priests sounded the trumpets; and when the people heard the trumpets, all the people shouted together with a great and mighty war cry. And the entire wall fell down all around, and all the people went up into the city.

**6:21** And Joshua devoted it to destruction, and all that was in the city, from man even to woman, from young man even to elder, and even to calf and beast of burden, with the edge of the sword.

**6:22** And to the two young men who had spied, Joshua said, "Go into the house of the woman, and bring her out from there, and all that belongs to her."

**6:23** And the two young men who had spied out the city went into the house of the woman, and brought out Rahab the harlot, and her father, and her mother, and her brothers, and her kindred, and all that belonged to her; and they placed her outside the camp of Israel.

**6:24** And the city was burned with fire, together with all that was in it; only the silver and gold and bronze and iron they gave to be brought into the treasury of the Lord.

**6:25** And Rahab the harlot and all her father's household Joshua spared alive, and she dwelt in Israel to this present day, because she hid the spies whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

**6:26** And Joshua pronounced an oath on that day before the Lord, saying, "Cursed is the man who shall build that city! With his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and with his youngest he shall set up its gates." And so did Ozan of Bethel: with Abiron the firstborn he laid its foundation, and with the youngest who survived he set up its gates.

**6:27** And the Lord was with Joshua, and his name was throughout all the land.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Greek text of verses 6:3-5 is much shorter than the Masoretic Text and omits verse 6:4 entirely, moving directly from 6:3 to 6:5.
- **6:8** — Greek continues Joshua's instructions as direct commands in the imperative mood through verses 6:8-9 (and reads "sacred trumpets"), whereas the Masoretic Text shifts to narrative here.
- **6:15** — Greek reads "six times" here, followed by "on the seventh circuit" in 6:16, whereas the Masoretic Text has "seven times" in 6:15.
- **6:17** — Greek reads "the Lord of hosts" (Kyrio sabaoth), whereas the Masoretic Text has simply "the LORD."
- **6:26** — Greek adds a fulfillment notice absent from the Masoretic Text of Joshua (cf. 1 Kings 16:34), naming "Ozan of Bethel" (for Hiel) and "Abiron" (for Abiram), and rendering Segub as "the youngest who survived" (to elachisto diasothenti).
