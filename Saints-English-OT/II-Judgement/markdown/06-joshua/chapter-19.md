## Text

**19:1** And the second lot came forth for the sons of Simeon; and their inheritance was in the midst of the lots of the sons of Judah.

**19:2** And their lot was Beersheba, Samaa, and Koladam,

**19:3** and Harsola, Bola, and Iason,

**19:4** and Elthoula, Boula, and Hormah,

**19:5** and Ziklag, Beth-machereb, and Sarsousin,

**19:6** and Batharoth, and their fields—thirteen cities and their villages;

**19:7** Eremmon, Thalcha, Ether, and Ashan—four cities and their villages,

**19:8** around their cities as far as Barek, going toward Bameth to the south. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon according to their families.

**19:9** Out of the lot of Judah came the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, for the portion of the sons of Judah was greater than theirs; and the sons of Simeon received an inheritance in the midst of their lot.

**19:10** And the third lot came forth for Zebulun according to their families. The borders of their inheritance shall be Esedekgola; their borders are

**19:11** the sea and Maragelda, and it shall reach to Beth-arabah, into the valley that is before Iekman;

**19:12** and it turned back from Seddouk opposite from the east of Beth-shemesh to the borders of Chaselothaith, and it shall pass through to Dabiroth, and it shall go up to Phangai;

**19:13** and from there it shall go around opposite toward the east to Gebere, to the city of Katasem, and it shall pass through to Remmona Hamathar Haoza;

**19:14** and the borders shall go around to the north to Hamoth, and their outlet shall be at Gaiphael,

**19:15** and Katanath, Nabaal, Symoon, Jericho, and Bethman.

**19:16** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun according to their families, cities and their villages.

**19:17** And for Issachar the fourth lot came forth.

**19:18** And their borders were Iazel, Chasaloth, and Sounan,

**19:19** and Hagin, Siona, Reeroth, and Anachereth,

**19:20** and Dabiron, Kison, and Rebes,

**19:21** and Remmas, Ieon, Tomman, Aimarek, and Beersaphes.

**19:22** And the borders shall reach to Gaithbor and to Salim toward the sea, and Beth-shemesh; and the outlet of its borders shall be the Jordan.

**19:23** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Issachar according to their families, the cities and their villages.

**19:24** And the fifth lot came forth for Asher.

**19:25** And their borders were from Heleketh, Haleph, Baithok, and Keaph,

**19:26** and Elimelech, Amiel, and Maasa; and it shall reach to Carmel toward the sea, and to Seion and Labanath.

**19:27** And it shall turn from the rising of the sun and Baithegeneth, and it shall reach to Zebulun and from Gai and Phthaiel toward the north; and the borders shall enter Saphthaibaithme and Inael, and it shall pass through to Chobamasomel,

**19:28** and Elbon, Raab, Ememaon, and Kanthan, as far as great Sidon;

**19:29** and the borders shall turn back to Ramah and as far as the spring of Masphassat and of the Tyrians; and the borders shall turn back to Iaseiph, and its outlet shall be the sea, and from Leb and Echozob,

**19:30** and Archob, Aphek, and Raau.

**19:31** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Asher according to their families, cities and their villages.

**19:32** And for Naphtali the sixth lot came forth.

**19:33** And their borders were Moolam, Mola, Besemin, Arme, Nabok, and Iephthamai, as far as Dodam; and its outlets were the Jordan.

**19:34** And the borders turn toward the sea to Enath Tabor, and it shall pass through from there to Hiakana; and it shall reach to Zebulun on the south, and it shall reach to Asher toward the sea, and the Jordan from the rising of the sun.

**19:35** And the walled cities of the Tyrians: Tyre and Homatha, Daketh and Kenereth,

**19:36** and Armaith, Harael, and Hazor,

**19:37** and Kedesh, Assarei, and the spring of Hazor,

**19:38** and Keroe and Megala, Harim, Baiththame, and Thessamys.

**19:39** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali.

**19:40** And for Dan the seventh lot came forth.

**19:41** And their borders were Sarath and Asa, the cities of Sammaus,

**19:42** and Salabin, Ammon, and Silatha,

**19:43** and Elon, Timnah, and Ekron,

**19:44** and Alkatha, Begethon, and Gebeelan,

**19:45** and Hazor, Banaibakat, and Gethremmon,

**19:46** and from the sea, Ierakon, a border near Joppa.

**19:47** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Dan according to their families, their cities and their villages. And the sons of Dan did not drive out the Amorite who oppressed them in the hill country; and the Amorites did not permit them to go down into the valley, and they pressed back from them the border of their portion.

**19:48** And the sons of Judah went and fought against Lachish, and took it and struck it with the edge of the sword; and they dwelt in it and called its name Lasenndak. And the Amorite persisted in dwelling in Elom and in Salamin; and the hand of Ephraim grew heavy upon them, and they became tributary to them.

**19:49** And they went to take possession of the land according to their border. And the sons of Israel gave a lot to Joshua the son of Nun among them

**19:50** by the command of God; and they gave him the city that he asked for, Thamarchares, which is in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city and dwelt in it.

**19:51** These are the divisions which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the rulers of the fathers' houses in the tribes of Israel apportioned as an inheritance by lots in Shiloh before the Lord, at the doors of the tabernacle of testimony; and they went to take possession of the land.

## Translator Notes

- **19:6** — The Greek text reads 'Batharoth, and their fields' rather than the Hebrew 'Beth-lebaoth and Sharuhen,' while retaining the total of thirteen cities.
- **19:15** — The Greek text reads 'Jericho' and 'Bethman' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Idalah' and 'Bethlehem,' and omits the Hebrew summary of twelve cities.
- **19:26** — The Greek text reads 'Seion and Labanath' (Greek Seion, a northern border site distinct from Mount Zion) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Shihor-libnath.'
- **19:47** — Here and in verse 48, the Greek text diverges substantially from the Hebrew, incorporating material parallel to Judges 1:34–35 concerning the Amorites pressing Dan in the hill country.
- **19:48** — The Greek text names 'the sons of Judah' fighting 'Lachish' and renaming it 'Lasenndak,' whereas the Hebrew text attributes this campaign to the sons of Dan against 'Leshem,' renamed 'Dan.'
- **19:50** — The Greek text reads 'of God' and 'Thamarchares' (Timnath-heres), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'of the Lord' and 'Timnath-serah.'
