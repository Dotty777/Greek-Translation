## Text

**10:1** Now when Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had taken Ai and had utterly destroyed it—as they had done to Jericho and its king, so they had done to Ai and its king—and that the inhabitants of Gibeon had defected to Joshua and to Israel,

**10:2** they were exceedingly afraid of them; for he knew that Gibeon was a great city, like one of the chief cities, and all its men were mighty.

**10:3** Therefore Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem sent to Elam king of Hebron, and to Pheidon king of Jarmuth, and to Jephthah king of Lachish, and to Dabein king of Adullam, saying,

**10:4** "Come now, come up to me and help me, and let us wage war against Gibeon; for they have defected to Joshua and to the children of Israel."

**10:5** And the five kings of the Jebusites went up—the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Adullam—they and all their people, and they encamped around Gibeon and besieged it.

**10:6** And the inhabitants of Gibeon sent to Joshua at the camp of Israel at Gilgal, saying, "Do not slacken your hands from your servants; come up to us quickly and deliver us and help us, for all the kings of the Amorites who dwell in the hill country have gathered together against us."

**10:7** So Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and all the people of war with him, every man mighty in strength.

**10:8** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for into your hands I have delivered them; not one of them shall be left before you."

**10:9** And when Joshua came upon them suddenly, he had marched all night from Gilgal.

**10:10** And the Lord threw them into panic before the children of Israel, and the Lord crushed them with a great crushing at Gibeon; and they pursued them along the way of the ascent of Horonein, and struck them down as far as Azekah and as far as Makkedah.

**10:11** And as they fled from before the children of Israel on the descent of Horonein, the Lord also cast hailstones upon them from heaven as far as Azekah; and there were more who died because of the hailstones than those whom the children of Israel slew with the sword in the battle.

**10:12** Then Joshua spoke to the Lord on the day that God delivered the Amorite into the hand of Israel, when He crushed them at Gibeon and they were crushed before the children of Israel; and Joshua said, "Let the sun stand still over against Gibeon, and the moon over against the valley of Aijalon."

**10:13** And the sun stood still and the moon remained stationary, until God took vengeance upon their enemies. And the sun stood in the midst of heaven, and did not go forward toward setting until the completion of one day.

**10:14** And there was no day like that, neither before nor afterward, that God heeded a man; for the Lord fought alongside Israel.

**10:16** And these five kings fled and hid themselves in the cave that is in Makkedah.

**10:17** And it was reported to Joshua, saying, "The five kings have been found hidden in the cave that is in Makkedah."

**10:18** And Joshua said, "Roll stones against the mouth of the cave, and set men over them to guard them;

**10:19** but as for you, do not stand still; keep pursuing after your enemies, and overtake their rear guard, and do not let them enter into their cities, for the Lord our God has delivered them into our hands."

**10:20** And it came to pass, when Joshua and every son of Israel had finished striking them with an exceedingly great slaughter to the very end, that those who escaped made their escape into the fortified cities.

**10:21** And all the people returned unharmed to Joshua at Makkedah; and not one of the children of Israel muttered with his tongue.

**10:22** Then Joshua said, "Open the cave, and bring out these five kings from the cave."

**10:23** And they brought out the five kings from the cave: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Adullam.

**10:24** And when they had brought them out to Joshua, Joshua called together all Israel and the commanders of the war who had marched with him, saying to them, "Come forward and put your feet upon their necks." And they drew near and put their feet upon their necks.

**10:25** And Joshua said to them, "Do not fear them, nor be dismayed; be courageous and be strong, for thus the Lord will do to all your enemies against whom you fight."

**10:26** And Joshua slew them and hanged them upon five trees; and they remained hanging upon the trees until evening.

**10:27** And it came to pass toward the setting of the sun that Joshua gave command, and they took them down from the trees and cast them into the cave into which they had fled; and they rolled stones against the cave, to this very day.

**10:28** And they took Makkedah on that day, and slew it with the edge of the sword, and utterly destroyed every breathing thing in it; there was not left in it anyone surviving and escaping. And they did to the king of Makkedah as they had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:29** Then Joshua and all Israel with him departed from Makkedah to Libnah, and he besieged Libnah.

**10:30** And the Lord delivered it into the hands of Israel, and they took it and its king; and he slew it with the edge of the sword, and every breathing thing in it; there was not left in it any surviving and escaping. And they did to its king as they had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:31** Then Joshua and all Israel with him departed from Libnah to Lachish, and he encamped against it and besieged it.

**10:32** And the Lord delivered Lachish into the hands of Israel, and he took it on the second day; and they slew it with the edge of the sword, and utterly destroyed it, just as they had done to Libnah.

**10:33** Then Elam king of Gaza went up to help Lachish; and Joshua struck him with the edge of the sword, along with his people, until there was not left of them anyone surviving and escaping.

**10:34** Then Joshua and all Israel with him departed from Lachish to Adullam, and he encamped against it and besieged it.

**10:35** And the Lord delivered it into the hand of Israel, and he took it on that day, and he slew it with the edge of the sword, and every breathing thing in it they slew, just as they had done to Lachish.

**10:36** Then Joshua and all Israel with him departed to Hebron, and he encamped against it.

**10:37** And he struck it with the edge of the sword, and every breathing thing, all that was in it; there was not one surviving. Just as they had done to Adullam, they utterly destroyed it and all that was in it.

**10:38** Then Joshua and all Israel turned back to Debir, and having encamped against it,

**10:39** they took it and its king and its villages, and struck it with the edge of the sword, and utterly destroyed it and every breathing thing in it, and left in it no one surviving. Just as they had done to Hebron and to its king, so they did to Debir and to its king.

**10:40** And Joshua struck all the land of the hill country, and Nabai, and the lowland, and Asedoth, and its kings; they left none of them surviving, and every breathing thing of life he utterly destroyed, just as the Lord, the God of Israel, had commanded,

**10:41** from Kadesh Barnea as far as Gaza, all Goshen as far as Gibeon.

**10:42** And all their kings and their land Joshua struck at one time, because the Lord, the God of Israel, fought alongside Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Greek names the king of Jerusalem "Adoni-bezek" rather than "Adoni-zedek" as in the Hebrew text.
- **10:3** — The Greek preserves distinct names for the allied kings and one city: Elam (Hebrew Hoham), Pheidon (Piram), Jephthah (Japhia), Dabein (Debir), and Adullam (Eglon; also in 10:5, 23, 34, 37); in 10:5 the Greek calls them "the five kings of the Jebusites" rather than "Amorites."
- **10:10** — The Greek reads "Horonein" (omitting "Beth-" from Hebrew Beth-horon; also in 10:11). In 10:13 the Greek states that God Himself took vengeance and omits the reference to the Book of Jashar, and 10:15 is absent in the Septuagint.
- **10:21** — The Greek reads "not one of the children of Israel muttered with his tongue," whereas the Hebrew reads "no one moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel."
- **10:33** — The Greek reads "Elam king of Gaza" rather than "Horam king of Gezer." In 10:40 the Greek transliterates the Negev and the slopes as "Nabai" and "Asedoth," and 10:43 is absent in the Septuagint.
