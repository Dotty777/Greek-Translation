## Text

**4:1** And when all the people had finished crossing over the Jordan, the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying,

**4:2** "Take men from the people, one from each tribe,

**4:3** and command them, 'Take up from the midst of the Jordan twelve prepared stones, and carrying these across together with yourselves and with them, set them down in your encampment, wherever you shall lodge there for the night.'"

**4:4** And Joshua called twelve distinguished men from the children of Israel, one from each tribe,

**4:5** and said to them, "Go forward before me in the presence of the Lord into the midst of the Jordan, and let each one take up a stone from there and lift it upon his shoulders, according to the number of the twelve tribes of Israel,

**4:6** that these may be for you a sign set up continually; so that when your son asks you tomorrow, saying, 'What are these stones to us?'

**4:7** then you shall declare to your son, saying, 'The Jordan River failed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth, when it crossed over it.' And these stones shall be for you a memorial to the children of Israel forever."

**4:8** And the children of Israel did so, just as the Lord commanded Joshua; and taking twelve stones from the midst of the Jordan, as the Lord had directed Joshua at the completion of the crossing of the children of Israel, they carried them across with themselves to the camp and laid them down there.

**4:9** And Joshua also set up twelve other stones in the Jordan itself, in the place that was beneath the feet of the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and they are there to this present day.

**4:10** Now the priests who bore the ark of the covenant stood in the Jordan until Joshua had finished everything that the Lord commanded him to declare to the people; and the people made haste and crossed over.

**4:11** And it came to pass, when all the people had finished crossing over, that the ark of the covenant of the Lord crossed over, and the stones before them.

**4:12** And the children of Reuben, and the children of Gad, and half of the tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed before the children of Israel, just as Moses had commanded them.

**4:13** Forty thousand men girded for battle crossed over before the Lord to war, toward the city of Jericho.

**4:14** On that day the Lord exalted Joshua in the sight of the whole race of Israel, and they feared him just as they had Moses, as long as he lived.

**4:15** And the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying,

**4:16** "Command the priests who bear the ark of the covenant of the testimony of the Lord to come up out of the Jordan."

**4:17** And Joshua commanded the priests, saying, "Come up out of the Jordan."

**4:18** And it came to pass, when the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord came up out of the Jordan and set their feet upon the ground, that the water of the Jordan rushed back to its place and flowed, as yesterday and the day before, over all its bank.

**4:19** And the people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month; and the children of Israel encamped in Gilgal, on the side toward the rising of the sun from Jericho.

**4:20** And these twelve stones, which he took out of the Jordan, Joshua set up in Gilgal,

**4:21** saying, "When your children ask you, saying, 'What are these stones?'

**4:22** declare to your children that on dry ground Israel crossed over this Jordan,

**4:23** for the Lord our God dried up the water of the Jordan from before them until they had crossed over, just as the Lord our God did to the Red Sea, which the Lord our God dried up before us until we passed through,

**4:24** so that all the nations of the earth may know that the power of the Lord is mighty, and that you may revere the Lord our God in every work."

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — Greek reads "twelve distinguished men" (ton endoxon), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "twelve men whom he had appointed."
- **4:11** — Greek reads "and the stones before them" (kai hoi lithoi emprosthen auton), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "and the priests, in the presence of the people."
- **4:24** — Greek reads "the power of the Lord is mighty" and "in every work" (en panti ergo), where the Masoretic Text reads "the hand of the LORD is mighty" and "forever" (lit. "all the days").
