## Text

**11:1** Now when Jabis king of Hazor heard, he sent to Jobab king of Marron, and to the king of Symoon, and to the king of Azeiph,

**11:2** and to the kings who were toward Sidon the Great, in the hill country, and in Raba opposite Chinneroth, and in the plain, and in Phenaeddor,

**11:3** and to the coastal Canaanites from the east, and to the coastal Amorites, and Hivites, and Jebusites, and Perizzites who were in the mountain, and the Hittites who were below the wilderness toward Maseuman.

**11:4** So they went out, they and their kings with them, like the sand of the sea in multitude, with horses and chariots exceedingly many.

**11:5** And all these kings assembled and came together to one place, and they encamped at the water of Marron to make war against Israel.

**11:6** Then the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid before them, for tomorrow at this hour I will deliver them routed before Israel; you shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire."

**11:7** So Joshua and all the people of war came suddenly against them at the water of Marron, and fell upon them in the hill country.

**11:8** And the Lord delivered them into the hands of Israel, and striking them down they pursued them as far as Sidon the Great, and as far as Maseron, and as far as the plains of Massoch to the east; and they cut them down until not one survivor was left of them.

**11:9** And Joshua did to them just as the Lord had commanded him: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

**11:10** And Joshua turned back at that time and captured Hazor and its king; for Hazor had formerly been the ruler of all these kingdoms.

**11:11** And they slew every breathing thing in it with the sword and utterly destroyed them all, and no breathing thing was left in it; and they burned Hazor with fire.

**11:12** And Joshua took all the cities of the kings and their kings, and slew them with the edge of the sword, and they utterly destroyed them, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded.

**11:13** Yet Israel did not burn any of the cities built upon mounds; only Hazor alone Israel burned.

**11:14** And all its spoil the sons of Israel took as plunder for themselves; but all of them they utterly destroyed with the edge of the sword until he had destroyed them; they did not leave even one breathing thing of them.

**11:15** Just as the Lord had commanded Moses His servant, so likewise Moses commanded Joshua, and thus Joshua did; he did not pass over anything of all that Moses had commanded him.

**11:16** And Joshua took all the hill country, and all of Adeb, and all the land of Goshen, and the lowland, and the region toward the west, and the mountain of Israel and the low places

**11:17** that are beside the mountain, from Mount Achel and that which ascends to Seir, even as far as Baal-gad, and the plains of Lebanon below Mount Hermon; and all their kings he captured, and struck down, and put to death.

**11:18** And for many days Joshua waged war against these kings.

**11:19** There was not a city that Israel did not take; they took all in war,

**11:20** for it came about through the Lord to harden their heart to meet Israel in war, that they might be utterly destroyed, so that mercy might not be given to them, but that they might be utterly destroyed, just as the Lord had spoken to Moses.

**11:21** And Joshua came at that time and utterly destroyed the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, and from Debir, and from Anaboth, and from all the kindred of Israel, and from all the mountain of Judah, together with their cities; and Joshua utterly destroyed them.

**11:22** None of the Anakim was left by the sons of Israel; only in Gaza and in Aseldo were they left.

**11:23** So Joshua took all the land, just as the Lord had commanded Moses; and Joshua gave them as an inheritance to Israel in apportionment according to their tribes. And the land rested from war.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Septuagint reads Jabis, Marron, Symoon, and Azeiph where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Jabin, Madon, Shimron, and Achshaph; similar Septuagint proper-name forms appear throughout chapters 11–12.
- **11:6** — The Greek reads "routed" (tetropomenous) rather than the Hebrew "slain," reflecting a distinct Septuagint textual reading.
- **11:19** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint omits the exception clause regarding the Hivites of Gibeon, stating simply that Israel took every city in war.
- **11:22** — The Septuagint names only Gaza and Aseldo (corresponding to Ashdod) as places where the Anakim remained, omitting Gath found in the Hebrew text.
- **11:23** — The Greek reads the plural pronoun "them" (autous), referring collectively to the conquered lands and territories, where the Hebrew has the singular "it."
