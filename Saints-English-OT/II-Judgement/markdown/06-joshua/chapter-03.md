## Text

**3:1** And Joshua rose early in the morning and departed from Shittim; and they came as far as the Jordan, and lodged there before crossing over.

**3:2** And it came to pass after three days that the scribes went through the camp,

**3:3** and commanded the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord our God, and our priests and the Levites bearing it, you shall depart from your places and go after it.

**3:4** Yet let there be a distance between us and it; about two thousand cubits away you shall stand. Do not draw near to it, that you may know the way by which you are going; for you have not gone this way since yesterday and the third day before."

**3:5** And Joshua said to the people, "Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow, for tomorrow the Lord will do wondrous things among us."

**3:6** And Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and go before the people." And the priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and went before the people.

**3:7** And the Lord said to Joshua, "In this day I begin to exalt you before all the sons of Israel, that they may know that just as I was with Moses, so I will also be with you.

**3:8** And now, command the priests who bear the ark of the covenant, saying, 'When you enter into the midst of the water of the Jordan, you shall stand still in the Jordan.'"

**3:9** And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Draw near here, and hear the word of the Lord our God.

**3:10** By this you shall know that a living God is among you, and He will utterly destroy from before our face the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite, and the Jebusite.

**3:11** Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing over the Jordan.

**3:12** Appoint for yourselves twelve men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe.

**3:13** And it shall come to pass, as soon as the feet of the priests who bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth rest in the water of the Jordan, that the water of the Jordan shall fail, and the water coming down shall stand still."

**3:14** And the people departed from their tents to cross over the Jordan, and the priests bore the ark of the covenant before the people.

**3:15** And as the priests who bore the ark of the covenant entered upon the Jordan, and the feet of the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord were dipped into a part of the water of the Jordan—now the Jordan was full over all its bank, as in the days of wheat harvest—

**3:16** then the waters coming down from above stood still; they stood as one solid mass, standing exceedingly far off, as far as a part of Kathiairein; and that which was going down went down into the sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, until it completely failed; and the people stood opposite Jericho.

**3:17** And the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood upon dry ground in the midst of the Jordan; and all the sons of Israel crossed over on dry ground, until all the people had finished crossing over the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — "Since yesterday and the third day before" is a literal Greek rendering of a Hebrew idiom meaning "heretofore" or "previously."
- **3:8** — The Greek specifies entering "into the midst of the water of the Jordan," whereas the Hebrew reads "to the edge of the water of the Jordan."
- **3:16** — Greek Kathiairein is a distinct place name where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Adam, the city that is beside Zarethan"; the Greek also concludes with the people standing opposite Jericho rather than crossing over.
