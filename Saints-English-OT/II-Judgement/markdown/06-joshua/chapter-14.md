## Text

**14:1** And these are those of the sons of Israel who received an inheritance in the land of Canaan, to whom Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the rulers of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel distributed an inheritance.

**14:2** By lot they received their inheritance, just as the Lord commanded by the hand of Joshua for the nine tribes and the half-tribe,

**14:3** from beyond the Jordan. Yet to the Levites he did not give an allotment among them,

**14:4** for the sons of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim; and no portion in the land was given to the Levites, except cities to dwell in and their set-apart lands for the livestock, and their livestock.

**14:5** Just as the Lord had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

**14:6** Then the sons of Judah drew near to Joshua in Gilgal, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, "You know the word which the Lord spoke to Moses, the man of God, concerning me and you in Kadesh Barnea.

**14:7** For I was forty years old when Moses the servant of God sent me from Kadesh Barnea to spy out the land, and I answered him a word according to his mind.

**14:8** Yet my brethren who went up with me turned aside the heart of the people, but I devoted myself to follow the Lord my God.

**14:9** And Moses swore on that day, saying, 'The land upon which you stepped shall be an inheritance for you and for your children forever, because you devoted yourself to follow after the Lord our God.'

**14:10** And now the Lord has sustained me, just as He said, this forty-fifth year since the Lord spoke this word to Moses, and Israel journeyed in the wilderness. And now, behold, I am eighty-five years old today;

**14:11** I am still as strong today as when Moses sent me; in the same way I am strong now to go in and to go out for war.

**14:12** And now I ask of you this mountain, just as the Lord said on that day; for you heard this word on that day. Now the Anakim are there—cities strong and great; if therefore the Lord is with me, I shall utterly destroy them, just as the Lord said to me."

**14:13** And Joshua blessed him, and gave Hebron to Caleb the son of Jephunneh, the son of Kenaz, as an inheritance.

**14:14** Therefore Hebron became the inheritance of Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he followed the command of the Lord God of Israel.

**14:15** Now the name of Hebron formerly was the city of Argob; this was the mother city of the Anakim. And the land rested from war.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Septuagint reads "by the hand of Joshua" where the Hebrew has "by the hand of Moses," followed by a briefer transition ("from beyond the Jordan") in 14:3.
- **14:7** — "according to his mind" (κατὰ τὸν νοῦν αὐτοῦ) follows the Greek text literally, where the Hebrew reads "as it was in my heart."
- **14:15** — The Septuagint names ancient Hebron "the city of Argob" and calls it "the mother city [μητρόπολις] of the Anakim," where the Hebrew reads "Kiriath-arba; Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim."
