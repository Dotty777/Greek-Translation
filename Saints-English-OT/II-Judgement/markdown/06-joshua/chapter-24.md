## Text

**24:1** And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shiloh, and called together their elders and their scribes and their judges, and he set them before God.

**24:2** And Joshua said to all the people, "Thus says the Lord God of Israel: 'Beyond the river your fathers dwelt from the beginning, Terah the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor; and they served other gods.

**24:3** And I took your father Abraham from beyond the river, and led him throughout all the land; and I multiplied his seed and gave him Isaac,

**24:4** and to Isaac, Jacob and Esau; I gave to Esau Mount Seir to inherit for himself. And Jacob and his sons went down into Egypt, and they became there a great, populous, and mighty nation; and the Egyptians afflicted them.'

**24:5** And they struck Egypt with the things that they did to them. And after these things He brought out

**24:6** our fathers out of Egypt, and you entered into the Red Sea; and the Egyptians pursued after our fathers with chariots and with horses into the Red Sea.

**24:7** And we cried out to the Lord, and He placed a cloud and thick darkness between us and the Egyptians; and He brought the sea upon them and covered them. And your eyes saw all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt; and you were in the wilderness many days.

**24:8** And He brought us into the land of the Amorites who dwelt beyond the Jordan, and the Lord delivered them into our hands; and you inherited their land, and utterly destroyed them from before your face.

**24:9** And Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and arrayed himself in battle against Israel; and he sent and summoned Balaam to curse us.

**24:10** And the Lord your God would not destroy you; indeed, with a blessing He blessed us, and He delivered us out of their hands, and gave them over.

**24:11** And you crossed over the Jordan and came to Jericho; and those who dwelt in Jericho fought against us: the Amorite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hivite and the Jebusite and the Hittite and the Girgashite; and the Lord delivered them into our hands.

**24:12** And He sent out the hornet before us, and drove them out from before our face—twelve kings of the Amorites—not with your sword nor with your bow.

**24:13** And He gave you a land for which you did not labor, and cities which you did not build, and you were settled in them; and of vineyards and olive groves which you did not plant, you shall eat.

**24:14** And now fear the Lord and serve Him in uprightness and in righteousness; and put away the foreign gods which our fathers served beyond the river and in Egypt, and serve the Lord.

**24:15** But if it does not please you to serve the Lord, choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve: whether the gods of your fathers who were beyond the river, or the gods of the Amorites, among whom you dwell in their land; but I and my household will serve the Lord, for He is holy."

**24:16** And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to forsake the Lord so as to serve other gods.

**24:17** The Lord our God, He is God; He brought us and our fathers up out of Egypt, and guarded us throughout all the way in which we journeyed, and among all the nations through whom we passed;

**24:18** and the Lord cast out the Amorite and all the nations dwelling in the land from before our face. Indeed, we also will serve the Lord, for He is our God."

**24:19** And Joshua said to the people, "You will by no means be able to serve the Lord, for He is a holy God; and being jealous, He will not pardon your sins and your iniquities.

**24:20** Whenever you forsake the Lord and serve other gods, He will come upon you and afflict you and utterly consume you, in return for the good He has done for you."

**24:21** And the people said to Joshua, "No, but we will serve the Lord."

**24:22** And Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the Lord, to serve Him.

**24:23** And now put away the foreign gods that are among you, and direct your heart to the Lord God of Israel."

**24:24** And the people said to Joshua, "We will serve the Lord, and we will obey His voice."

**24:25** And Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day, and gave them a law and a judgment in Shiloh, before the tabernacle of the God of Israel.

**24:26** And he wrote these words in a book, the law of God; and Joshua took a great stone and set it up under the terebinth tree before the Lord.

**24:27** And Joshua said to the people, "Behold, this stone shall be among you for a witness, for it has heard all the things spoken to it by the Lord, whatever He has spoken to us today; and this stone shall be among you for a witness in the last days, whenever you deal falsely with the Lord my God."

**24:28** And Joshua sent the people away, and they departed, each one to his own place.

**24:29** And Israel served the Lord all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who prolonged their days after Joshua and who had seen all the works of the Lord which He had done for Israel.

**24:30** And it came to pass after these things that Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died, being one hundred and ten years old.

**24:31** And they buried him by the borders of his inheritance in Timnath-serah, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gilead; there they placed with him in the tomb in which they buried him the stone knives with which he had circumcised the sons of Israel at Gilgal, when He brought them out of Egypt, just as the Lord commanded them; and they are there to this very day.

**24:32** And the sons of Israel brought up the bones of Joseph out of Egypt and buried them in Shechem, in the portion of the field which Jacob had acquired from the Amorites who dwelt in Shechem for one hundred ewe lambs.

**24:33** And it came to pass after these things that Eleazar the son of Aaron, the high priest, died and was buried in Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which he had given him in the hill country of Ephraim. On that day the sons of Israel took the ark of God and carried it about among themselves; and Phinehas served as priest in the place of Eleazar his father until he died, and he was buried in their own Gibeah. And the sons of Israel departed, each one to his own place and to his own city. And the sons of Israel worshiped Astarte and Ashtaroth and the gods of the nations around them; and the Lord delivered them into the hands of Eglon the king of Moab, and he ruled over them eighteen years.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Greek text places the covenant assembly at Shiloh (and again in v. 25, before the tabernacle of the God of Israel), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Shechem.
- **24:5** — Here the Greek shifts from the Lord's first-person speech (vv. 2-4) to Joshua narrating in the third person about the Lord and speaking of 'our fathers' (vv. 5-13), and omits the sending of Moses and Aaron found in the Hebrew.
- **24:12** — The Greek reads 'twelve kings of the Amorites,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'two kings of the Amorites.'
- **24:31** — The Greek places Joshua's burial north of 'Mount Gilead' (Hebrew: 'Mount Gaash') and adds the tradition that the stone knives used for the circumcision at Gilgal were laid in his tomb.
- **24:33** — The Greek concludes the book with an extended epilogue recounting Israel's carrying of the ark, the priesthood and burial of Phinehas, the worship of Astarte and Ashtaroth, and Israel's eighteen-year subjection to Eglon king of Moab (cf. Judges 3:12-14).
