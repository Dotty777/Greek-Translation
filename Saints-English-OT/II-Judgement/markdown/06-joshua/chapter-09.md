## Text

**9:1** Now when the kings of the Amorites who were across the Jordan heard—those in the hill country, those in the lowland, those along all the coast of the Great Sea, and those toward Anti-Lebanon—and the Hittites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites,

**9:2** they assembled together to wage war against Joshua and Israel, all of them at once. Then Joshua built an altar to the Lord, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the law of Moses, an altar of whole stones upon which iron had not been laid; and there he offered up burnt offerings to the Lord, a sacrifice of deliverance. And Joshua wrote upon the stones the repetition of the law, the law of Moses, before the children of Israel. And all Israel, with their elders and the judges and their scribes, passed alongside on this side and on that side opposite the ark, and the priests and the Levites bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, both the proselyte and the native-born, half of whom were near Mount Gerizim and half of whom were near Mount Ebal, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded to bless the people at the first. And afterward, in this manner, Joshua read all the words of this law, the blessings and the curses, according to all that is written in the law of Moses. There was not a word from all that Moses had commanded Joshua which Joshua did not read in the ears of all the assembly of the children of Israel, to the men and the women and the little children, and to the proselytes who accompanied Israel.

**9:3** And the inhabitants of Gibeon heard all that the Lord had done to Jericho and to Ai.

**9:4** And they also acted with craftiness, and came and gathered provisions and made themselves ready; and they took old sacks upon their shoulders, and wineskins that were old and torn and bound up;

**9:5** and the hollows of their shoes and their sandals were old and patched upon their feet, and their garments were worn old upon them; and the bread of their provisions was dry and moldy and eaten away.

**9:6** And they came to Joshua at the camp of Israel at Gilgal, and said to Joshua and Israel, "We have come from a far country; now therefore, make a covenant with us."

**9:7** And the children of Israel said to the Horite, "See lest you dwell among me; and how shall I make a covenant with you?"

**9:8** And they said to Joshua, "We are your servants." And Joshua said to them, "Where are you from, and from where have you come?"

**9:9** And they said, "From a very far country your servants have come, in the name of the Lord your God; for we have heard of His name, and all that He did in Egypt,

**9:10** and all that He did to the kings of the Amorites who were across the Jordan—to Sihon king of the Amorites, and to Og king of Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei.

**9:11** And when they heard, our elders and all the inhabitants of our land spoke to us, saying, 'Take for yourselves provisions for the way and go out to meet them, and you shall say to them, "We are your servants; now therefore, make a covenant with us."'

**9:12** These loaves of bread we packed warm for our journey on the day we set out to come to you, but now they have become dry and eaten away;

**9:13** and these wineskins which we filled when new, even these have been torn; and our garments and our shoes have grown old because of the exceedingly long journey."

**9:14** Then the rulers took some of their provisions, and did not inquire of the Lord.

**9:15** And Joshua made peace with them, and they made a covenant with them to preserve them alive; and the rulers of the congregation swore to them.

**9:16** And it came to pass, after three days after making the covenant with them, that they heard that they were near to them and that they dwelt among them.

**9:17** And the children of Israel set out and came to their cities; now their cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeron, and the cities of Jarein.

**9:18** And the children of Israel did not fight against them, because all the rulers had sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel; and all the congregation murmured against the rulers.

**9:19** Then the rulers said to all the congregation, "We have sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel; now therefore, we shall not be able to touch them.

**9:20** This we will do: we will keep them alive and preserve them, and wrath shall not be against us because of the oath which we swore to them.

**9:21** They shall live, and they shall be cutters of wood and bearers of water for all the congregation," just as the rulers said to them.

**9:22** Then Joshua called them together and said to them, "Why have you deceived me, saying, 'We are exceedingly far from you,' while you are natives, of those who dwell among us?

**9:23** Now therefore, you are accursed; there shall never fail from among you a servant nor a cutter of wood for me and for my God."

**9:24** And they answered Joshua, saying, "It was reported to us all that the Lord your God commanded Moses His servant, to give you this land and to destroy us and all who dwell upon it from before you; and we were exceedingly afraid for our lives because of you, and we did this thing.

**9:25** And now, behold, we are in your hand; as it pleases you and as it seems good to you, do to us."

**9:26** And they did so to them; and Joshua delivered them on that day out of the hands of the children of Israel, and they did not slay them.

**9:27** And on that day Joshua appointed them as cutters of wood and bearers of water for all the congregation and for the altar of God; therefore the inhabitants of Gibeon became cutters of wood and bearers of water for the altar of God to this very day, and for the place which the Lord shall choose.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek reads "Anti-Lebanon" rather than "Lebanon" and includes the Girgashites among the listed peoples.
- **9:2** — In the Septuagint, the Mount Ebal altar and reading of the law (Hebrew 8:30-35) stand here; "the repetition of the law" renders Greek to deuteronomion (literally "the second law").
- **9:4** — The Greek reads "upon their shoulders" rather than "upon their donkeys" as in the Hebrew text.
- **9:7** — The Greek reads "the Horite" rather than "the Hivite" as in the Hebrew text.
- **9:17** — The Greek renders these city names as "Beeron" (Hebrew Beeroth) and translates Kiriath-jearim as "the cities of Jarein."
