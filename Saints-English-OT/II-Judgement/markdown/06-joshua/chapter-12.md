## Text

**12:1** Now these are the kings of the land whom the sons of Israel slew, and whose land they possessed as an inheritance beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the valley of Arnon as far as Mount Hermon, and all the land of Arabah on the east:

**12:2** Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, ruling from Arnon, which is in the valley along part of the valley, and half of Gilead as far as Jabbok, the borders of the sons of Ammon,

**12:3** and Arabah as far as the Sea of Chinnereth on the east, and as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Sea of Salt, on the east, by the way toward Aseimoth, from Thaiman below Medoth Pisgah;

**12:4** and Og king of Bashan—he was left of the giants—who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

**12:5** ruling from Mount Hermon and from Sekchai, and all Bashan as far as the borders of Gergesei and Machei, and half of Gilead, the borders of Sihon king of Heshbon.

**12:6** Moses the servant of the Lord and the sons of Israel struck them down; and Moses gave it as an inheritance to Reuben, and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**12:7** Now these are the kings of the Amorites whom Joshua and the sons of Israel slew beyond the Jordan toward the sea, at Baal-gad in the plain of Lebanon and as far as Mount Chelcha as one ascends to Seir; and Joshua gave it to the tribes of Israel to inherit according to their allotment,

**12:8** in the mountain, and in the plain, and in Arabah, and in Asedoth, and in the wilderness, and in Negeb—the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite;

**12:9** and the king of Jericho, and the king of Ai, which is near Bethel,

**12:10** the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron,

**12:11** the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish,

**12:12** the king of Ailam, the king of Gezer,

**12:13** the king of Debir, the king of Asei,

**12:14** the king of Hermath, the king of Airath, the king of Arath,

**12:15** the king of Libnah, the king of Adullam,

**12:16** the king of Elad,

**12:17** the king of Ataphout, the king of Hepher,

**12:18** the king of Ophek of Arok,

**12:19** the king of Hasom,

**12:20** the king of Symoon, the king of Mamroth, the king of Azeiph,

**12:21** the king of Kadesh, the king of Zakach,

**12:22** the king of Maredoth, and the king of Jekom of Carmel,

**12:23** the king of Eldom of Phenneddor, the king of Geei of Galilee,

**12:24** the king of Tirzah: all these kings were twenty-nine.

## Translator Notes

- **12:4** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew Rephaim as "the giants" (ton giganton) and introduces a finite verb ("he was left").
- **12:7** — The Septuagint specifies "the kings of the Amorites" where the Hebrew reads "the kings of the land," and names the mountain Chelcha (compare Achel in 11:17 and Halak in the Hebrew).
- **12:24** — The Septuagint lists the kings without the repeated Hebrew word "one" and gives the total as "twenty-nine" rather than "thirty-one"; because of a doublet in 12:14 (Airath and Arath), thirty individual kings are named in this Greek list.
