## Text

**16:1** Now the borders of the sons of Joseph went from the Jordan across from Jericho on the east, and they shall go up from Jericho into the hill country, the wilderness, to Bethel Luz,

**16:2** and go out to Bethel, and pass along to the borders of Chatarothi,

**16:3** and pass through toward the sea to the borders of Haptalim as far as the borders of Lower Beth Horon, and their outlet shall be at the sea.

**16:4** And the sons of Joseph, Ephraim and Manasseh, received their inheritance.

**16:5** Now the borders of the sons of Ephraim were according to their families; and the borders of their inheritance on the east were Ashtaroth and Erok as far as Upper Beth Horon and Gazara.

**16:6** And the borders shall come toward the sea to Hicasmon on the north of Therma; they shall go around eastward to Thenasa and Sellesa, and pass along from the east to Janoka,

**16:7** and to Macho and Ashtaroth, and their villages; and they shall come to Jericho, and extend out to the Jordan.

**16:8** And from Taphu the borders shall proceed toward the sea to Chelkana, and their outlet shall be at the sea; this is the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim according to their families.

**16:9** And the cities set apart for the sons of Ephraim were in the midst of the inheritance of the sons of Manasseh, all the cities and their villages.

**16:10** And Ephraim did not destroy the Canaanite who dwelt in Gezer; and the Canaanite dwelt in Ephraim to this day, until Pharaoh king of Egypt went up and took it and burned it with fire, and they pierced through the Canaanites and the Perizzites and those who dwelt in Gezer; and Pharaoh gave it as a dowry to his daughter.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — The Greek reads "to Bethel" (whereas the Hebrew reads "from Bethel to Luz") and preserves the Septuagint-distinct names Chatarothi (Hebrew Archites) and, in verse 3, Haptalim (Hebrew Japhletites).
- **16:6** — Verses 5 through 8 preserve several distinctive Septuagint geographical forms, including Ashtaroth and Erok, Gazara, Hicasmon, Therma, Thenasa and Sellesa, Janoka, Macho, Taphu, and Chelkana.
- **16:10** — The Septuagint adds an account of Pharaoh king of Egypt capturing and burning Gezer and giving it to his daughter as a dowry (paralleling 1 Kings 9:16), in place of the Hebrew statement that the Canaanites became forced laborers.
