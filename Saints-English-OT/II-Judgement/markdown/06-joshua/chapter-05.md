## Text

**5:1** And it came to pass, when the kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan and the kings of Phoenicia who were by the sea heard that the Lord God had dried up the Jordan River from before the children of Israel as they were crossing over, their minds melted and they were dismayed, and there was no understanding at all in them because of the presence of the children of Israel.

**5:2** Now about this time the Lord said to Joshua, "Make for yourself stone knives from sharp flint rock, and sit down and circumcise the children of Israel."

**5:3** And Joshua made sharp stone knives, and circumcised the children of Israel at the place called the Hill of the Foreskins.

**5:4** Now this is the manner in which Joshua thoroughly cleansed the children of Israel: as many as were ever born along the way, and as many as were ever uncircumcised of those who had come out of Egypt,

**5:5** all these Joshua circumcised; for forty-two years Israel had wandered in the wilderness of Madbaritis;

**5:6** therefore most of them were uncircumcised—the fighting men who had come out of the land of Egypt, who disobeyed the commandments of God, concerning whom He also determined that they should not see the land which the Lord swore to our fathers to give, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**5:7** And in their place He raised up their sons, whom Joshua circumcised because they had been born uncircumcised along the way.

**5:8** And when they had been circumcised, they kept quiet, remaining right there in the camp until they were healed.

**5:9** And the Lord said to Joshua the son of Nun, "On this present day I have taken away the reproach of Egypt from you." And he called the name of that place Gilgal.

**5:10** And the children of Israel kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month from evening, on the west of Jericho, beyond the Jordan, in the plain;

**5:11** and they ate of the grain of the land, unleavened bread and fresh produce.

**5:12** On this day the manna ceased after they had eaten of the grain of the land, and there was no longer manna for the children of Israel; but they harvested the crop of the Phoenicians in that year.

**5:13** And it came to pass, when Joshua was at Jericho, that he lifted up his eyes and saw a man standing opposite him, and his sword drawn in his hand. And Joshua approached and said to him, "Are you for us, or for our adversaries?"

**5:14** And he said to him, "I am chief commander of the host of the Lord; I have now come." And Joshua fell on his face to the earth and said to him, "Master, what do you command your servant?"

**5:15** And the chief commander of the Lord said to Joshua, "Loose the sandal from your feet, for the place on which you now stand is holy."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek reads "Phoenicia" here (and "the Phoenicians" in 5:12), where the Masoretic Text refers to the Canaanites and Canaan.
- **5:2** — Greek reads "sit down and circumcise" (kathisas periteme), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew root for "sit" where the Masoretic Text has "circumcise again a second time."
- **5:5** — Greek specifies "forty-two years" (versus Masoretic "forty years") and transliterates Hebrew midbar ("wilderness") as the proper name "Madbaritis."
- **5:14** — Unlike the Masoretic Text, the Greek omits both "No" and "and worshiped," having Joshua fall on his face and address the chief commander as "Master" (Despota).
