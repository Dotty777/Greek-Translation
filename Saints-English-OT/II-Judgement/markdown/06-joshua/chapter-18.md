## Text

**18:1** Now the whole congregation of the sons of Israel assembled together at Shiloh, and they set up the tabernacle of testimony there, and the land was subdued by them.

**18:2** And there remained among the sons of Israel those who had not received their inheritance, seven tribes.

**18:3** And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "How long will you be slack to inherit the land which the Lord our God has given?

**18:4** Give from among you three men from each tribe, and let them arise and go through the land, and describe it before me according as it shall be necessary to pass through it." And they came to him,

**18:5** and he divided for them seven portions. Judah shall stand as a border for them on the south, and the sons of Joseph shall stand for them on the north.

**18:6** "Now you divide the land into seven portions and bring them to me here, and I will draw a lot for you before the Lord our God.

**18:7** For there is no portion for the sons of Levi among you, because the priesthood of the Lord is his portion; and Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan toward the east, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave them."

**18:8** And the men arose and went. And Joshua commanded the men who were going to survey the land, saying, "Go and survey the land, and come back to me; and here I will draw a lot for you before the Lord in Shiloh."

**18:9** And they went and surveyed the land, and saw it, and wrote it down by cities in seven portions in a book, and brought it to Joshua.

**18:10** And Joshua cast a lot for them in Shiloh before the Lord.

**18:11** And the lot of Benjamin came out first according to their families; and the borders of their lot went out between Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**18:12** And their borders on the north were thus: from the Jordan the borders shall go up along the back of Jericho on the north, and go up to the mountain toward the sea, and its outlet shall be Mabdaritis Bethon;

**18:13** and from there the borders shall pass through to Luz, along the back of Luz on its south, which is Bethel. And the borders shall go down to Maatarothorech, to the hill country which is to the south of Lower Beth Horon;

**18:14** and the borders shall pass through and go along to the mountain that looks toward the sea on the south, from the mountain before Beth Horon southward, and its outlet shall be at Kiriath Baal, which is Kiriath Jearim, a city of the sons of Judah; this is the side toward the sea.

**18:15** And the side toward the south is from the part of Kiriath Baal; and the borders shall pass through to Gasin, to the spring of the water of Nephtoah;

**18:16** and the borders shall go down to a part—this is before the glen of Sonnam, which is on the side of Emekraphain on the north; and they shall go down to Gaienna on the south of Jebusai southward, and go down to the spring of Rogel;

**18:17** and they shall pass through to the spring of Beth Shemesh, and pass along to Galiaoth, which is opposite the ascent of Aithamin, and go down to the stone of Baion of the sons of Reuben;

**18:18** and they shall pass through along the back of Beth Arabah on the north, and go down to the borders, to the back of the sea on the north;

**18:19** and the outlet of the borders shall be at the bay of the Salt Sea on the north, toward the part of the Jordan on the south; these are the borders on the south.

**18:20** And the Jordan shall border it on the east side. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, its borders all around according to families.

**18:21** Now the cities of the sons of Benjamin according to their families were Jericho, and Bethegaio, and Amekasis,

**18:22** and Bethabara, and Sara, and Besana,

**18:23** and Ain, and Phara, and Jephratha,

**18:24** and Karapha, and Kephira, and Moni, and Geba—twelve cities and their villages;

**18:25** Gibeon, and Ramah, and Beerotha,

**18:26** and Massema, and Miron, and Hamoke,

**18:27** and Phira, and Kaphan, and Nakan, and Selekan, and Thareela,

**18:28** and Jebus, which is Jerusalem, and cities, and Gabaothjarim—thirteen cities and their villages. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their families.

## Translator Notes

- **18:4** — At the end of verse 4 and into verse 5, the Greek shifts briefly into third-person narrative ("And they came to him, and he divided for them seven portions") before resuming Joshua's direct address in verse 6.
- **18:12** — Mabdaritis Bethon transliterates the Hebrew phrase for "the wilderness of Beth-aven"; verses 13 through 17 also preserve distinctive Septuagint forms such as Maatarothorech, Gasin, Galiaoth, Aithamin, and Baion of the sons of Reuben.
- **18:16** — The Greek transliterates "the valley of the son of Hinnom" and "the Valley of Rephaim" as Sonnam, Emekraphain, and Gaienna (Gehenna).
- **18:22** — The city name Sara directly transliterates the Septuagint-distinct Greek place name Σαρά (corresponding to Hebrew Zemaraim, not the patriarch's wife Sarah), alongside the Septuagint forms Bethabara and Besana.
- **18:28** — Verses 21 through 28 preserve numerous distinctive Septuagint city names (such as Bethegaio, Amekasis, Massema, Miron, Hamoke, Phira, Kaphan, Nakan, Selekan, and Gabaothjarim), render Hebrew Kiriath literally as "cities," and total "thirteen cities" (Hebrew "fourteen").
