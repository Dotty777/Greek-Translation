## Text

**22:1** Then Joshua summoned the sons of Reuben, and the sons of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh,

**22:2** and said to them, "You have listened to all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, and you have heeded my voice according to all that he commanded you.

**22:3** You have not forsaken your brethren these many days; until this present day you have kept the commandment of the Lord your God.

**22:4** And now the Lord our God has given rest to our brethren, just as He spoke to them. Now therefore, turn back and depart to your houses and to the land of your possession, which Moses gave to you beyond the Jordan.

**22:5** Yet take diligent heed to do the commandments and the law which Moses the servant of the Lord commanded us to do: to love the Lord our God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments and to cling to Him, and to serve Him with all your mind and with all your soul."

**22:6** So Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their houses.

**22:7** Now to half the tribe of Manasseh Moses gave an inheritance in the region of Bashan, and to the other half Joshua gave one among their brethren beyond the Jordan by the sea. And when Joshua sent them away to their houses and blessed them,

**22:8** they departed to their houses with much wealth, and very many livestock, and silver, and gold, and iron, and abundant clothing; and they divided the spoil of the enemies with their brethren.

**22:9** So the sons of Reuben, and the sons of Gad, and half the tribe of the sons of Manasseh journeyed from the sons of Israel at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, to go away to Gilead, to the land of their possession, which they had inherited by the command of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

**22:10** And they came to Gilgal of the Jordan, which is in the land of Canaan; and the sons of Gad, and the sons of Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh built there an altar by the Jordan, a great altar to behold.

**22:11** And the sons of Israel heard it said, "Behold, the sons of Gad, and the sons of Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh have built an altar at the borders of the land of Canaan, at Gilead of the Jordan, across from the sons of Israel."

**22:12** Then all the sons of Israel gathered together at Shiloh, to go up and wage war against them.

**22:13** And the sons of Israel sent to the sons of Reuben, and to the sons of Gad, and to the sons of half the tribe of Manasseh, into the land of Gilead, Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the high priest,

**22:14** and ten of the rulers with him, one ruler from each father's house out of all the tribes of Israel; they were rulers of fathers' houses, commanders of thousands of Israel.

**22:15** And they came to the sons of Gad, and to the sons of Reuben, and to those of half the tribe of Manasseh, into Gilead, and they spoke to them, saying,

**22:16** "Thus says the whole congregation of the Lord: What is this trespass that you have committed before the God of Israel, turning away today from the Lord by building for yourselves an altar, that you should become rebels against the Lord?

**22:17** Is the sin of Peor a small thing to us? For we have not been cleansed from it until this day, and a plague came in the congregation of the Lord.

**22:18** And you have turned away today from the Lord! And it shall come to pass, if you rebel today against the Lord, that tomorrow wrath shall be upon all Israel.

**22:19** And now, if the land of your possession is too small, cross over into the land of the possession of the Lord, where the tabernacle of the Lord dwells, and take an inheritance among us; and do not become rebels against God, and do not rebel against the Lord by building an altar apart from the altar of the Lord our God.

**22:20** Behold, did not Achar the son of Zerah surely trespass concerning the accursed thing, and wrath came upon the whole congregation of Israel? And that one man alone died in his own sin."

**22:21** Then the sons of Reuben, and the sons of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh answered and spoke to the commanders of thousands of Israel, saying,

**22:22** "God is God—the Lord! And God, even God, Himself knows, and Israel itself shall know: if in rebellion we have trespassed before the Lord, may He not deliver us on this day;

**22:23** and if we have built for ourselves an altar to depart from the Lord our God, to offer upon it a sacrifice of burnt offerings, or to offer upon it a sacrifice of peace offerings, the Lord shall require it.

**22:24** Rather, out of anxious concern for a matter we have done this, saying, Lest tomorrow your children say to our children, 'What have you to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?

**22:25** And the Lord has set the Jordan as a border between us and you, and you have no portion in the Lord.' And your sons would estrange our sons, so that they would not revere the Lord.

**22:26** And we resolved to do thus, to build this altar—not for burnt offerings, nor for sacrifices,

**22:27** but that this may be a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we may perform the service of the Lord before Him with our burnt offerings, and with our sacrifices, and with our peace offerings; and your children shall not say to our children tomorrow, 'You have no portion in the Lord.'

**22:28** And we said that if it should ever come to pass that they speak to us and to our generations tomorrow, then they shall say, 'Behold the likeness of the altar of the Lord, which our fathers made, not for burnt offerings nor for sacrifices, but it is a witness between you and between us and between our sons.'

**22:29** Far be it from us, therefore, to turn away from the Lord in these present days, to rebel against the Lord so that we should build an altar for burnt offerings, and for the salamin sacrifices, and for the peace offering, besides the altar of the Lord which is before His tabernacle."

**22:30** Now when Phinehas the priest and all the rulers of the congregation of Israel who were with him heard the words that the sons of Reuben, and the sons of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh spoke, it pleased them.

**22:31** And Phinehas the priest said to the sons of Reuben, and to the sons of Gad, and to half the tribe of Manasseh, "Today we know that the Lord is with us, because you have not committed a trespass before the Lord, and because you have delivered the sons of Israel from the hand of the Lord."

**22:32** Then Phinehas the priest and the rulers returned from the sons of Reuben, and from the sons of Gad, and from half the tribe of Manasseh, out of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and reported the words to them.

**22:33** And it pleased the sons of Israel; and they spoke to the sons of Israel, and blessed the God of the sons of Israel, and said no longer to go up against them to war, to destroy the land of the sons of Reuben, and of the sons of Gad, and of half the tribe of Manasseh; and they dwelt upon it.

**22:34** And Joshua named the altar of those of Reuben, and of those of Gad, and of half the tribe of Manasseh, and said, "It is a witness between them that the Lord is their God."

## Translator Notes

- **22:8** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, in which Joshua speaks a parting exhortation to share the spoil, the Greek text narrates in the third person that the returning tribes departed with much wealth and divided the spoil with their brethren.
- **22:10** — The Greek text reads "Gilgal of the Jordan" here (and "Gilead of the Jordan" in verse 11) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Geliloth" (or the region of the Jordan).
- **22:20** — The Greek text preserves the name "Achar" (compare 1 Chronicles 2:7) instead of "Achan," and states "that one man alone died in his own sin" without the negation present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **22:29** — "salamin" reflects the Greek text's transliteration of the Hebrew word shelamim ("peace offerings"), appearing here alongside the Greek equivalent "peace offering" (soterion).
- **22:34** — In the Greek text, Joshua himself names the altar of the eastern tribes and declares it a witness between them, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text attributes the naming to the sons of Reuben and Gad.
