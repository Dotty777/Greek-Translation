## Text

**9:1** Now all Israel had their enrollment, and these were recorded in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah, together with those who were resettled in Babylon in their iniquities.

**9:2** And those who dwelt formerly in their possessions in the cities of Israel were the Levite sons, the priests, and the given ones.

**9:3** And in Jerusalem there dwelt some of the sons of Judah, and some of the sons of Benjamin, and of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh:

**9:4** Gothi, and the son of Sammiu, the son of Omri, sons of the sons of Perez and of the son of Judah.

**9:5** And of the Shilonites: Asaiah his firstborn.

**9:6** Of the sons of Zerah: Epeiel, and their brethren, six hundred and ninety.

**9:7** And of the sons of Benjamin: Shallum the son of Moollam, the son of Hoduiah, the son of Haana;

**9:8** and Banaam the son of Iraam; and these were the sons of Uzzi, the son of Machir; and Masealem the son of Shephatiah, the son of Reuel, the son of Benaiah;

**9:9** and their brethren, according to their generations, nine hundred and fifty-six; all the men were chiefs of fathers' houses according to the houses of their fathers.

**9:10** And of the priests: Jodae, and Joarim, and Jachin,

**9:11** and Azariah, sons of Hilkiah, the son of Mosollom, the son of Zadok, the son of Marmoth, the son of Ahitub, the ruler of the house of God;

**9:12** and Adaiah the son of Iraam, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah; and Maasaiah the son of Adiel, the son of Jediu, the son of Mosollam, the son of Maselmoth, the son of Immer;

**9:13** and their brethren, chiefs of their fathers' houses, one thousand seven hundred and sixty—mighty in strength for the work of the service of the house of God.

**9:14** And of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Esrikan, the son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari;

**9:15** and Bakar, and Raraiel, Galaad, and Manthanias the son of Micah, the son of Zichri, the son of Asaph;

**9:16** and Obadiah the son of Shemaiah, the son of Galaad, the son of Jothon; and Barachi the son of Ossa, the son of Elkanah, who dwelt in the villages of Notephati.

**9:17** The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akum, Tammam, and Aimam, and their brethren; Shallum was the chief.

**9:18** And until this time they were at the king's gate toward the east; these were the gates of the camps of the sons of Levi.

**9:19** And Solomon the son of Koreb, the son of Abiasaph, the son of Korah, and his brethren of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the works of the service, keeping the watches of the tabernacle; and their fathers were over the camp, guarding the entrance.

**9:20** And Phinehas the son of Eleazar had been ruler over them formerly, and these were with him.

**9:21** Zechariah the son of Masalami was gatekeeper of the door of the tabernacle of the testimony.

**9:22** All those chosen for the gates, at the gates, were two hundred and twelve; these were in their courts, according to their enrollment. These David and Samuel the seer appointed in their trust.

**9:23** And they and their sons were over the gates in the house of the Lord, in the house of the tabernacle, to keep watch.

**9:24** According to the four winds were the gates: toward the east, the sea, the north, and the south.

**9:25** And their brethren in their courts were to come in every seven days, from time to time, with these;

**9:26** for four mighty men of the gates are in trust. The Levites were over the chambers, and over the treasuries of the house of God they shall encamp;

**9:27** for upon them was the watch, and they were over the keys, morning by morning, to open the doors of the temple.

**9:28** And some of them were over the vessels of the service, for by number they shall bring them in.

**9:29** And some of them were appointed over the vessels, and over all the holy vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.

**9:30** And some of the sons of the priests were makers of the ointment and for the spices.

**9:31** And Mattathias of the Levites, who was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was in trust over the works of the offering of the pan of the high priest.

**9:32** And Benaiah the Kohathite, of their brethren, was over the loaves of the presentation, to prepare them Sabbath by Sabbath.

**9:33** And these were the singers, chiefs of the fathers' houses of the Levites, arranged in courses; for day and night were upon them in the works.

**9:34** These were the chiefs of the fathers' houses, chiefs according to their generations; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

**9:35** And in Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, and the name of his wife was Maacah.

**9:36** And his firstborn son was Sabadon, and Isir, and Kish, and Baal, and Ner, and Nadab,

**9:37** and Jedur, and a brother, and Zechariah, and Makelloth.

**9:38** And Makelloth fathered Samaa. And these dwelt in the midst of their brethren in Jerusalem, in the midst of their brethren.

**9:39** And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, and Malchishua, and Amminadab, and Asabal.

**9:40** And the son of Jonathan was Merib-Baal, and Merib-Baal fathered Micah.

**9:41** And the sons of Micah were Pithon, and Malach, and Tharach.

**9:42** And Ahaz fathered Jada, and Jada fathered Gameleth, and Gazaoth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Massa.

**9:43** And Massa fathered Baana; Rephaiah was his son, and Esael was his son.

**9:44** And Esel had six sons, and these were their names: Esdrikan his firstborn, and Ishmael, and Seraiah, and Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Esel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Greek reads "the Levite sons, the priests, and the given ones" (οἱ δεδομένοι, the literal Septuagint rendering of the Nethinim, temple servants given to sanctuary service), differing in order from the Hebrew text.
- **9:19** — The Greek names the Korahite leader "Solomon the son of Koreb" rather than the Hebrew "Shallum the son of Kore," and omits "of the Lord" after "the camp."
- **9:20** — The Greek reads "and these were with him" (καὶ οὗτοι μετ᾽ αὐτοῦ), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Lord was with him."
- **9:31** — The Greek adds "of the high priest" (τοῦ μεγάλου ἱερέως) to the pan-offering supervised by Mattathias, and in 9:32 reads the personal name "Benaiah the Kohathite."
- **9:44** — Although the Greek states that Esel had six sons, only five names follow because the Hebrew name Bocheru was rendered as "his firstborn" (πρωτότοκος αὐτοῦ); similarly, in 9:37 the Hebrew name Ahio is translated as the common noun "a brother" (ἀδελφὸς).
