## Text

**23:1** Now David was old and full of days, and he made his son Solomon king in his place over Israel.

**23:2** And he gathered together all the leaders of Israel, and the priests, and the Levites.

**23:3** And the Levites were numbered from thirty years old and upward, and their number by their head count came to thirty-eight thousand men:

**23:4** of the overseers for the work of the house, twenty-four thousand; and scribes and judges, six thousand;

**23:5** and four thousand praising the Lord with the instruments which he made to praise the Lord.

**23:6** And David divided them into divisions among the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**23:7** And of Parosom: Edan and Shimei.

**23:8** The sons of Edan: the chief Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel—three in all.

**23:9** The sons of Shimei: Alothim, Hieiel, and Haidan, three. These were the chiefs of the fathers' houses of Edan.

**23:10** And of the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Ziza, Joash, and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei, four.

**23:11** Now Jahath was the chief, and Ziza the second; but Joash and Beriah did not multiply sons, so they became one father's house in one reckoning.

**23:12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.

**23:13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. And Aaron was set apart to be consecrated for the most holy things, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before the Lord, to minister, and to pronounce blessing in His name forever.

**23:14** Now as for Moses the man of God, his sons were named in the tribe of Levi.

**23:15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**23:16** The sons of Gershom: Shubael the chief.

**23:17** And the sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the chief, and there were no other sons; but the sons of Rehabiah increased exceedingly.

**23:18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomoth the chief.

**23:19** Of the sons of Hebron: Idoud the chief, Amadiah the second, Uzziel the third, and Ikemias the fourth.

**23:20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief, and Isshiah the second.

**23:21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**23:22** And Eleazar died, and he had no sons, only daughters; and the sons of Kish, their brethren, took them.

**23:23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Aidath, and Areimoth, three.

**23:24** These were the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses—the chiefs of their fathers' houses according to their reckoning, by the number of their names, by their head count—who did the work of the ministry of the house of the Lord, from twenty years old and upward.

**23:25** For David said, "The Lord God of Israel has given rest to His people, and He has taken up His dwelling in Jerusalem forever."

**23:26** And the Levites were not carrying the tabernacle and all its vessels for its ministry;

**23:27** for in the last words of David is the number of the sons of Levi from twenty years old and upward,

**23:28** because he stationed them at the hand of Aaron to minister in the house of the Lord—over the courts and over the chambers, and over the purification of all the holy things, and over the work of the ministry of God:

**23:29** for the showbread, for the fine flour of the sacrifice, and for the unleavened wafers, and for the griddle, and for that which is mixed, and for every measure;

**23:30** and to stand in the morning to praise and give thanks to the Lord, and likewise in the evening;

**23:31** and over all the burnt offerings offered up to the Lord on the Sabbaths, and on the new moons, and at the feasts, by number according to the ordinance concerning them, continually to the Lord.

**23:32** And they shall keep the charges of the tabernacle of witness and the charges of the sons of Aaron their brethren, to minister in the house of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — The Greek text omits the four thousand gatekeepers found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, leaving thirty-four thousand itemized out of the thirty-eight thousand total in verse 3.
- **23:7** — Greek Parosom and Edan correspond to the Gershonites and Ladan in the Hebrew text; several personal names in verses 9, 19, and 23 also preserve distinct Septuagint forms (including Alothim, Hieiel, Haidan, Idoud, Amadiah, Ikemias, Aidath, and Areimoth).
- **23:32** — The Greek text names two charges—the tabernacle of witness and the sons of Aaron—without the additional clause concerning the charge of the holy place found in the Hebrew text.
