## Text

**16:1** And they brought in the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent which David had pitched for it; and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**16:2** And when David had finished offering the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.

**16:3** And he distributed to every person of Israel, from man even to woman, to each one a single baker's loaf of bread and a sweet cake.

**16:4** And he appointed certain of the Levites before the ark of the covenant of the Lord to minister, to lift up their voices, and to give thanks and praise the Lord, the God of Israel:

**16:5** Asaph the leader, and second to him Zechariah, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jeiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel with instruments—harps and lyres—and Asaph sounding aloud with cymbals,

**16:6** and Benaiah and Uzziel the priests with the trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God

**16:7** on that day. Then David first appointed to praise the Lord by the hand of Asaph and his brethren. A Song.

**16:8** Give thanks, call upon Him in His name; make known His deeds among the peoples.

**16:9** Sing to Him and sing praises to Him; declare to all His wondrous works which the Lord has done.

**16:10** Praise in His holy name; the heart that seeks His good pleasure shall rejoice.

**16:11** Seek and be strong; seek His face continually.

**16:12** Remember His wondrous works which He has done, wonders and the judgments of His mouth,

**16:13** O seed of Israel, His servants, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**16:14** The Lord is our God; His judgments are in all the earth.

**16:15** We remember His covenant forever, His word which He commanded for a thousand generations,

**16:16** which He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac.

**16:17** He confirmed it to Jacob for a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**16:18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the allotment of your inheritance,"

**16:19** when they were few in number, as they were diminished and sojourned in it.

**16:20** And they went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.

**16:21** He did not permit any man to oppress them, and He rebuked kings for their sakes:

**16:22** "Do not touch My anointed ones; do not act wickedly against My prophets."

**16:23** Sing to the Lord, all the earth; proclaim His salvation from day to day.

**16:25** For the Lord is great and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods.

**16:26** For all the gods of the nations are idols, but our God made heaven.

**16:27** Glory and praise are before His face; strength and exultation are in His place.

**16:28** Give to the Lord, the Father of the nations; give to the Lord glory and strength,

**16:29** take gifts and bring them before His face, and worship the Lord in His holy courts.

**16:30** Let all the earth fear before His face; let the earth be established firmly and not be shaken.

**16:31** Let heaven rejoice, and let the earth be glad; and let them say among the nations, "The Lord reigns."

**16:32** The sea shall roar with its fullness, the tree of the field and all that is in it.

**16:33** Then the trees of the forest shall rejoice before the face of the Lord, for He has come to judge the earth.

**16:34** Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good; for His mercy endures forever.

**16:35** And say, "The God of our salvation has saved us; and deliver us from the nations, to praise Your holy name and to glory in Your praises."

**16:36** Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, from everlasting. And all the people shall say, "Amen." And they praised the Lord.

**16:37** And they left there before the ark of the covenant of the Lord Asaph and his brethren, to minister before the ark continually, as each day required;

**16:38** and Obed-Edom and his brethren, sixty-eight; and Obed-Edom the son of Jeduthun and Hosah as gatekeepers;

**16:39** and Zadok the priest and his brethren the priests before the tabernacle of the Lord at Bama, which is in Gibeon,

**16:40** to offer burnt offerings to the Lord upon the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, and according to all that is written in the law of the Lord, all that He commanded concerning the sons of Israel by the hand of Moses the servant of God.

**16:41** And with him were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest who were chosen by name to praise the Lord, "For His mercy endures forever";

**16:42** and with them were trumpets and cymbals for sounding aloud, and instruments for the songs of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were for the gate.

**16:43** And all the people departed, every man to his own house; and David returned to bless his house.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The Greek lists two items distributed to each person—a baker's loaf of bread and a sweet cake (amoreites)—rather than the three portions found in the Hebrew text.
- **16:23** — Verse 24 of the traditional numbering is absent from this Greek critical text, which moves directly from verse 23 to verse 25.
- **16:28** — The Greek reads 'the Father of the nations' (patri ton ethnon) rather than 'families of the peoples' as in the Hebrew text.
- **16:32** — The Greek reads 'the tree of the field' (xylon agrou) where the Hebrew text reads 'let the field exult.'
- **16:39** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for 'high place' as a proper place name, Bama.
