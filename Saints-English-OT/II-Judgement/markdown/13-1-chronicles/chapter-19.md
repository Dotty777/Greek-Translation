## Text

**19:1** Now it came to pass after this that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and Hanan his son reigned in his place.

**19:2** Then David said, "I will show mercy to Hanan the son of Hanas, as his father showed mercy to me." So David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And the servants of David came into the land of the sons of Ammon to comfort him.

**19:3** And the princes of Ammon said to Hanan, "Is it to honor your father in your sight that he has sent comforters to you? Have his servants not come to you in order to search out the city, to spy out the land?"

**19:4** Therefore Hanan took the servants of David and shaved them, and cut off half of their robes up to the waist, and sent them away.

**19:5** And they came to report to David concerning the men, and he sent to meet them, for they were exceedingly disgraced. And the king said, "Remain in Jericho until your beards have grown, and then return."

**19:6** And the sons of Ammon saw that the people of David had been put to shame; so Hanan and the sons of Ammon sent one thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves chariots and horsemen from Syria of Mesopotamia, from Syria of Maacah, and from Sobal.

**19:7** So they hired for themselves chariots and horsemen—thirty-two thousand chariots—and the king of Maacah and his people, who came and encamped opposite Medeba. Also the sons of Ammon gathered together from their cities and came to battle.

**19:8** And David heard of it, and sent Joab and all the army of the mighty men.

**19:9** Then the sons of Ammon came out and drew up in battle array beside the gate of the city, while the kings who had come encamped by themselves in the plain.

**19:10** And Joab saw that they were arrayed against him to fight, both in front and behind; so he chose from all the young men of Israel, and they drew up in battle array against the Syrians.

**19:11** And the remainder of the people he placed in the hand of Abishai his brother, and they drew up in battle array opposite the sons of Ammon.

**19:12** And he said, "If the Syrians prevail over me, then you shall be my deliverance; and if the sons of Ammon prevail over you, then I will deliver you.

**19:13** And the Lord will do what is good."

**19:14** So Joab and the people who were with him drew up opposite the Syrians for battle, and they fled from him.

**19:15** And when the sons of Ammon saw that the Syrians had fled, they also fled from before Joab and from before his brother, and entered into the city. Then Joab came to Jerusalem.

**19:16** And when the Syrians saw that Israel had routed them, they sent messengers and brought out the Syrians from beyond the river, with Shophar the commander of the army of Hadarezer before them.

**19:17** When this was reported to David, he gathered all Israel together, crossed the Jordan, came upon them, and drew up in battle array against them. And the Syrians drew up in battle array opposite David and fought against him.

**19:18** Then the Syrians fled from before David; and David slew of the Syrians seven thousand chariots and forty thousand foot soldiers, and he also slew Shaphath the commander of the army.

**19:19** And when the servants of Hadarezer saw that they had been defeated before Israel, they made a covenant with David and served him. So the Syrians were no longer willing to help Ammon.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Greek reads 'Hanan the son of Hanas' (and omits the father's name in verse 1), corresponding to Hebrew 'Hanun the son of Nahash.'
- **19:6** — Greek reads 'that the people of David had been put to shame' (Hebrew: 'that they had made themselves odious to David') and 'Sobal' (Hebrew: 'Zobah').
- **19:13** — The Greek text preserves a shorter ending to Joab's speech than the Hebrew, omitting his exhortation to be strong for the people and cities of God.
- **19:16** — Greek names Hadarezer's commander 'Shophar' here and 'Shaphath' in verse 18, both corresponding to Hebrew 'Shophach.'
