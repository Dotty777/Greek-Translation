## Text

**26:1** For the divisions of the gates, the sons of the Korahites: Mosolael the son of Kore; from the sons of Abia, Saphar.

**26:2** And of Mosalea the son of Zechariah, the firstborn was Iderel, Zechariah, Jenonel,

**26:3** Jolam, Jonah, and Elionais the seventh.

**26:4** And to Obed-Edom were sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jozabath, Joath, Sochar, Naas, Jeiel,

**26:5** Ammiel, Issachar, and Japhthoslaathi, for God blessed him.

**26:6** And to Shemaiah his son were born sons of the firstborn Rosai to his father's house, for they were mighty men.

**26:7** The sons of Shemaiah: Gooni, Raphael, Obed, Elezabath, and Achiud—mighty sons—Ennu, Sabchia, and Isbakom.

**26:8** All these were of the sons of Obed-Edom, they and their brethren and their sons, laboring mightily in the work—all of them sixty-two belonging to Obed-Edom.

**26:9** And to Mosomamid were sons and brethren, eighteen mighty men.

**26:10** And to Hosah, of the sons of Merari, were sons guarding the office, for he was not the firstborn; and his father made him chief of the second division.

**26:11** Tablai the third, Zechariah; all these, the sons and brethren of Hosah, were thirteen.

**26:12** To these belonged the divisions of the gates, to the chiefs of the mighty men, having courses just as their brethren, to minister in the house of the Lord.

**26:13** And they cast lots, both the small and the great, according to their fathers' houses, for each gate.

**26:14** And the lot for those toward the east fell to Salameia and Zechariah, sons of Soaz; for Melchia they cast lots, and the lot came forth for the north;

**26:15** to Obed-Edom, the south, opposite the house of Esephin;

**26:16** for the second, to Hosah toward the west, beyond the gate of the chamber of the ascent—watch opposite watch.

**26:17** Toward the east were six a day, on the north four a day, on the south four a day, and at Esephin two

**26:18** as reliefs, and for Hosah toward the west, beyond the gate of the chamber, three. Watch opposite watch of the ascent: toward the east six a day, and on the north four, and on the south four, and at Esephim two as reliefs, and toward the west four, and on the pathway two as reliefs.

**26:19** These were the divisions of the gatekeepers for the sons of Kohath and for those of Merari.

**26:20** And the Levites their brethren were over the treasuries of the house of the Lord and over the treasuries of the consecrated things.

**26:21** These were the sons of Chadan, the Gershonite: to Ladan and Jaieel of Jou; the sons of Jeiel were chiefs of the fathers' houses of Ladan; to the Gershonite, Jeiel.

**26:22** The sons of Jeiel: Zethom and Joel the brothers were over the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**26:23** Of Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel:

**26:24** And Joel the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was over the treasuries.

**26:25** And to his brother Eliezer was Rehabiah his son, and Osaiah, and Joram, and Zichri, and Shelomoth.

**26:26** This Shelomoth and his brethren were over all the treasuries of the holy things, which King David and the chiefs of the fathers' houses, the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and the leaders of the army had consecrated,

**26:27** which he took from the cities and from the spoils, and consecrated from them so that the building of the house of God might not be delayed;

**26:28** and over all the holy things of God—of Samuel the prophet, and of Saul the son of Kish, and of Abner the son of Ner, and of Joab the son of Zeruiah—all that he consecrated was under the hand of Shelomoth and his brethren.

**26:29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and sons were for the outward work over Israel, to serve as scribes and to judge.

**26:30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brethren, mighty men, one thousand seven hundred, were over the oversight of Israel beyond the Jordan toward the west, for every ministry of the Lord and work of the king.

**26:31** Of the Hebronites, Tudeias was the chief of the Hebronites, according to their generations, according to their fathers' houses. In the fortieth year of his reign they were sought out, and a mighty man was found among them in Riazer of Gilead.

**26:32** And his brethren, mighty sons, were two thousand seven hundred chiefs of the fathers' houses; and King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every ordinance of the Lord and matter of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek divides the ancestral name Ebiasaph/Abiasaph into "from the sons of Abia, Saphar," and reads "divisions of the gates" (as also in verse 12) rather than "gatekeepers."
- **26:6** — The Greek reads "of the firstborn Rosai," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes Shemaiah's sons as rulers over their father's house.
- **26:10** — The Greek renders the Hebrew name "Shimri the chief" as "sons guarding the office" and attaches "of the second division" to verse 10, omitting Hilkiah at the start of verse 11.
- **26:18** — "Esephin" (verses 15, 17) and "Esephim" (verse 18) transliterate the Hebrew term for the storehouse (Asuppim); the Greek text of verse 18 also contains an expanded repetition of the guard postings from verses 16–17.
- **26:27** — The Greek reads "from the cities" (rather than "from the battles") and "so that the building of the house of God might not be delayed" (rather than "to maintain the house of the Lord"); verses 20, 24, and 31 likewise preserve distinctive Septuagint readings ("the Levites their brethren," "Joel," and "Tudeias ... in Riazer").
