## Text

**4:1** Now the sons of Judah were Perez, Harson, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.

**4:2** And Rada was his son; and Shobal fathered Ieth, and Ieth fathered Achimei and Laath. These are the generations of Arathi.

**4:3** And these were the sons of Aitan: Azrael, Ragma, and Jabas; and the name of their sister was Heselebbon.

**4:4** And Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Azer was the father of Hosan. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethladen.

**4:5** And Sara the father of Tekoa had two wives, Aoda and Thoada.

**4:6** And Aoda bore him Ochaia, Hephal, Teman, and Aseran. All these were the sons of Aoda.

**4:7** And the sons of Loada were Areth, Saar, and Sennon.

**4:8** And Koe fathered Ennon and Sabatha, and the generations of the brother of Rechab, the son of Jarim.

**4:9** Now Igabes was more honorable than his brethren; and his mother called his name Igabes, saying, "I gave birth as gabes."

**4:10** And Igabes called upon the God of Israel, saying, "If indeed You will bless me, and multiply my borders, and Your hand will be with me, and You will grant knowledge so as not to humble me!" And God brought about all that he had asked.

**4:11** Now Caleb the father of Ascha fathered Machir; he was the father of Assathon.

**4:12** He fathered Bathraia, Bessee, and Teman, the father of the city of Nahash, the brother of Eselon the Kenizzite. These were the men of Rechab.

**4:13** And the sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah; and the sons of Othniel were Hathath.

**4:14** And Manathi fathered Ophrah; and Seraiah fathered Jobab, the father of Ageaddair, for they were craftsmen.

**4:15** And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh were Ir, Adai, and Noom; and the sons of Ada were Kenaz.

**4:16** And the sons of Aleel were Zib, Zepha, Thiria, and Eserel.

**4:17** And the sons of Esrei were Jetheri, Porad, Epher, and Amon. And Jether fathered Maion, Semen, and Mareth, the father of Esthaimon.

**4:18** And his wife, this Adia, bore Jared the father of Gedor, and Abisa the father of Sochon, and Chethiel the father of Zamon. And these were the sons of Gelia, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Noroel took.

**4:19** And the sons of the woman Idouia, the sister of Nacheth, and Dalila the father of Keilah, and Semegon the father of Joman, and of Manahem the father of Keilah: Hatamei and Esthaimone Nochathi.

**4:20** And the sons of Semion were Amnon, Ana the son of Phana, and Inon. And the sons of Seei were Zoan; and the sons of Zoab,

**4:21** the sons of Selom the son of Judah: Er the father of Lecha, and Madath the father of Maicha, and the generations of the houses of ephrath abak, of the house of Esoba;

**4:22** and Joakim, and the men of Sochetha, Joada and Saia, who dwelt in Moab; and he turned them back—abederein athoukiein.

**4:23** These were the potters who dwelt in Azaim and Gabaera with the king; in his kingdom they grew strong and dwelt there.

**4:24** The sons of Simeon were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarin, Zares, and Shaul;

**4:25** Salem his son, Mabasam his son, Masma his son,

**4:26** Shimei his son.

**4:27** Shimei had sixteen sons and three daughters; and their brethren did not have many sons, nor did all their families multiply like the sons of Judah.

**4:28** And they dwelt in Beersheba, Sama, Moalda, and Hesereoulab,

**4:29** Abella, Boosal, and Thoulaem,

**4:30** and Bathun, Herma, and Okla,

**4:31** and Beth-mareimoth, Hemisusesoram, and the house of Braumseorim. These were their cities until King David.

**4:32** And their villages were Aitan, Remmon, Thokka, and Aisar—five cities;

**4:33** and all their villages round about these cities as far as Balat. This was their possession and their enrollment.

**4:34** And Mosobab, Jemoloch, and Joseia the son of Amaziah,

**4:35** and Joel; and he was the son of Isabia, the son of Saraau, the son of Asiel;

**4:36** and Elioenai, Jokaba, Jasuia, and Asia, the sons of Aosal, the son of Saphal,

**4:37** the son of Amon, the son of Idia, the son of Samar, the son of Simeon.

**4:38** These are the ones who made division by the names of princes in their generations; and in the houses of their fathers they multiplied into a multitude.

**4:39** And they went until they came to Gerar, to the east of Gai, to seek pastures for their livestock.

**4:40** And they found rich and good pastures, and the land was wide before them, with peace and quietness; for it belonged to those of the sons of Ham who had dwelt there beforehand.

**4:41** And these who were recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and struck down their houses and the Minaeans whom they found there, and utterly destroyed them to this day; and they dwelt in their place, because there were pastures for their livestock there.

**4:42** And some of them, from the sons of Simeon, five hundred men, went to Mount Seir, with Pelatiah, Noadiah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Jesthen, as their leaders.

**4:43** And they struck down those who were left, the remnant of Amalek, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — The Greek reads "Sara" (Σαρά), a masculine name for the father of Tekoa (corresponding to Hebrew "Ashhur"), distinct from the matriarch Sarah.
- **4:9** — The Septuagint spells the name "Igabes" (Hebrew "Jabez") and renders his mother's explanation by transliterating the Hebrew wordplay as "as gabes" rather than translating it as "in pain."
- **4:10** — Where the Hebrew text petitions God to keep him from harm so that it may not cause him pain, the Septuagint reads "grant knowledge so as not to humble me."
- **4:21** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew phrase for "workers of fine linen" as "ephrath abak," and in verse 22 similarly transliterates "the records are ancient" as "abederein athoukiein."
- **4:27** — The Septuagint counts "three daughters" (rather than "six daughters" as in the Hebrew text) and omits two intermediate generations in verse 26.
- **4:39** — The Greek reads "Gerar" (where the Hebrew text has "Gedor") and treats the Hebrew word for "the valley" as the proper name "Gai."
