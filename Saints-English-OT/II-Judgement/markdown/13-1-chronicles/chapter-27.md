## Text

**27:1** Now the sons of Israel according to their number—the rulers of the fathers' houses, commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and the scribes who ministered to the people and in every matter of the king according to divisions, in every matter of the one coming in and going out month by month, throughout all the months of the year—each single division had twenty-four thousand.

**27:2** And over the first division for the first month was Sobal the son of Zabdiel, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:3** He was from the sons of Perez, ruler of all the commanders of the army for the first month.

**27:4** And over the division for the second month was Dodia the Ekchoch, and in his division were twenty-four thousand, commanders of the army.

**27:5** The third for the third month was Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the priest, the chief, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:6** This Benaiah was mightier than the thirty and was over the thirty, and over his division was Laibazath his son.

**27:7** The fourth for the fourth month was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Obadiah his son and the brethren, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:8** The fifth for the fifth month was the leader Salaoth the Esrae, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:9** The sixth for the sixth month was Oduias the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:10** The seventh for the seventh month was Chesles, who was from Phallus, of the sons of Ephraim, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:11** The eighth for the eighth month was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of Zerah, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:12** The ninth for the ninth month was Abiezer from Anathoth, from the land of Benjamin, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:13** The tenth for the tenth month was Maharai, who was from Netophah, of Zerah, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:14** The eleventh for the eleventh month was Benaiah, who was from Pirathon, of the sons of Ephraim, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:15** The twelfth for the twelfth month was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:16** Now over the tribes of Israel: for those of Reuben, the leader was Eliezer the son of Zichri; for Simeon, Shephatiah the son of Maacah;

**27:17** for Levi, Hashabiah the son of Samuel; for Aaron, Zadok;

**27:18** for Judah, Eliab, one of the brothers of David; for Issachar, Omri the son of Mishael;

**27:19** for Zebulun, Shemaiah the son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth the son of Azriel;

**27:20** for Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Uzziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel the son of Phaladaia;

**27:21** for the half-tribe of Manasseh among those in Gilead, Jaddai the son of Zabdiu; for the sons of Benjamin, Asier the son of Abner;

**27:22** for Dan, Azarel the son of Joram. These were the patriarchs of the tribes of Israel.

**27:23** And David did not take the number of those from twenty years old and under, because the Lord had said He would multiply Israel like the stars of heaven.

**27:24** And Joab the son of Zeruiah began to number among the people, but he did not finish; and because of these things wrath came upon Israel, and the number was not recorded in the book of the accounts of the days of King David.

**27:25** And over the king's treasuries was Azmaveth the son of Adiel; and over the storehouses in the field, and in the villages, and in the hamlets, and in the towers was Jonathan the son of Uzziah.

**27:26** And over those who tilled the ground, those who labored, was Ezri the son of Chobud.

**27:27** And over the fields was Shimei, who was from Rael; and over the storehouses of wine in the fields was Zachri the son of Sephni.

**27:28** And over the olive groves and over the sycamore trees that were in the lowland was Baal-hanan the Gederite; and over the storehouses of oil was Joash.

**27:29** And over the grazing cattle that were in Asidon was Asartais the Sharonite; and over the cattle that were in the valleys was Sophan the son of Adai.

**27:30** And over the camels was Abias the Ishmaelite; and over the donkeys was Jehdeiah, who was from Merathon.

**27:31** And over the sheep was Jaziz the Garite. All these were stewards of the possessions of King David.

**27:32** Also Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding; and Jehiel the son of Hachami was with the king's sons.

**27:33** Ahithophel was the king's counselor, and Hushai was the first friend of the king.

**27:34** And next after this Ahithophel were Jehoiada the son of Benaiah and Abiathar; and Joab was the commander-in-chief of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — The Greek reads "who ministered to the people" (τῷ λαῷ), whereas the Hebrew text reads "who served the king."
- **27:2** — The Septuagint preserves several distinct proper names throughout this chapter—including Sobal (v. 2), Dodia the Ekchoch (v. 4), Laibazath (v. 6), Obadiah (v. 7), Salaoth the Esrae (v. 8), Oduias (v. 9), Chesles from Phallus (v. 10), Samuel (v. 17), Mishael (v. 18), Phaladaia (v. 20), Jaddai son of Zabdiu and Asier (v. 21), Chobud (v. 26), Rael and Zachri son of Sephni (v. 27), Asidon, Asartais, and Sophan son of Adai (v. 29), Abias (v. 30), and the Garite (v. 31)—which differ from the Masoretic Hebrew forms.
- **27:7** — The Greek reads "Obadiah" (Ἀβδείας) rather than "Zebadiah" and "and the brethren" (καὶ οἱ ἀδελφοί), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants rendered "after him" in the Masoretic Text.
- **27:27** — The Greek has "fields" (χωρίων) in both clauses rather than "vineyards" as in the Hebrew text.
- **27:33** — The Greek renders Hushai's title as "the first friend of the king" (ὁ πρῶτος φίλος τοῦ βασιλέως), a recognized Hellenistic royal court rank, rather than "the Archite, the king's friend."
