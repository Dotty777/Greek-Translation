## Text

**2:1** These are the names of the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.

**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah, three; they were born to him by the daughter of Aua the Canaanite woman. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was wicked before the Lord, and He slew him.

**2:4** And Tamar his daughter-in-law bore to him Perez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

**2:5** The sons of Perez: Harson and Jemuel.

**2:6** And the sons of Zerah: Zimri, Etham, Haimuan, Calca, and Dara, five in all.

**2:7** And the sons of Carmi: Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed concerning the devoted thing.

**2:8** And the sons of Etham: Zaria.

**2:9** And the sons of Hezron who were born to him: Jerahmeel, Ram, Chabel, and Aram.

**2:10** And Arran fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, ruler of the house of Judah.

**2:11** And Nahshon fathered Salmon, and Salmon fathered Boaz.

**2:12** And Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse,

**2:13** and Jesse fathered his firstborn Eliab, Amminadab the second, Shammah the third,

**2:14** Nethanel the fourth, Zaddai the fifth,

**2:15** Asom the sixth, David the seventh.

**2:16** And their sister was Zeruiah; and the sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three.

**2:17** And Abigail bore Amessab; and the father of Amessab was Jether the Ishmaelite.

**2:18** And Caleb the son of Hezron fathered Gazuba a wife, and Elioth. And these were her sons: Joasar, Jasub, and Orna.

**2:19** And Gazuba died, and Caleb took for himself Ephrath, and she bore to him Hur.

**2:20** And Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.

**2:21** And after these things Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead; and he took her, and he was sixty years old, and she bore to him Seruch.

**2:22** And Seruch fathered Jair; and he had twenty-three cities in Gilead.

**2:23** And Gedsur and Arran took the villages of Saeir from them, with Kenath and its villages, sixty cities; all these belonged to the sons of Machir the father of Gilead.

**2:24** And after Hezron died, Caleb came to Ephrathah; and the wife of Hezron was Abijah, and she bore to him Ascho, the father of Tekoa.

**2:25** And the sons of Jerahmeel, the firstborn of Hezron, were the firstborn Ran, and Banaia, and Araia, and Ambram, and Asan his brother.

**2:26** And Jerahmeel had another wife, and her name was Atarah; she is the mother of Ozom.

**2:27** And the sons of Aram, the firstborn of Jerahmeel, were Maaz, Jamin, and Akor.

**2:28** And the sons of Ozom were Shammai and Jada; and the sons of Shammai were Nadab and Abishur.

**2:29** And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abichaia, and she bore to him Achabar and Moel.

**2:30** The sons of Nadab: Alsalad and Ephraim; and Salad died having no children.

**2:31** And the sons of Ephraim: Isemiel; and the sons of Isemiel: Sosan; and the sons of Sosan: Achai.

**2:32** And the sons of Iduda: Achisamas, Jether, and Jonathan; and Jether died having no children.

**2:33** And the sons of Jonathan: Thaleth and Ozam. These were the sons of Rameel.

**2:34** Now Sosam had no sons, but only daughters; and Sosam had an Egyptian servant, and his name was Jochel.

**2:35** And Sosan gave his daughter to Jochel his servant as a wife, and she bore to him Etthei.

**2:36** And Etthei fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabed,

**2:37** and Zabed fathered Aphamel, and Aphamel fathered Obed,

**2:38** and Obed fathered Joshua, and Joshua fathered Azariah,

**2:39** and Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Emas,

**2:40** and Emas fathered Sosomai, and Sosomai fathered Shallum,

**2:41** and Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

**2:42** And the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel: Mareshah his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph, and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron.

**2:43** And the sons of Hebron: Korah, Thapus, Rekem, and Shema.

**2:44** And Shema fathered Ramee the father of Jaklan, and Jeklan fathered Shammai.

**2:45** And his son was Meon, and Meon was the father of Gedsur.

**2:46** And Gaiphael, Caleb's concubine, bore Haran, Josan, and Gezue; and Haran fathered Gezue.

**2:47** And the sons of Joshua: Regem, Jotham, Sogar, Phalek, Gaipha, and Sagae.

**2:48** And Caleb's concubine Maachah bore Saber and Tharam.

**2:49** And she bore Sagae the father of Marmena, and Saou the father of Machabena and the father of Gaibal; and the daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

**2:50** These were the sons of Caleb. The sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Sobar the father of Kiriath-jearim,

**2:51** Solomon the father of Baitha, Lammon the father of Bethlehem, Harim the father of Beth-gaidon.

**2:52** And Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim had sons: Haio, Heseira, Monaio,

**2:53** Emospheos, the cities of Jair: Haithalim, Meiphithim, Hesamathim, and Hemasaraim; from these went forth the Sarathites and the sons of Esthaam.

**2:54** The sons of Solomon: Bethlehem, Metophathi, Ataroth of the house of Joab, and half of the Malathi, Hesarei,

**2:55** the families of writings dwelling in Games: Argathiim, Samathiim, and Sokathiim; these are the Kenites who came from Mesema, the father of the house of Recha.

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — The Septuagint lists four sons of Hezron here, adding Aram alongside Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chabel (Hebrew Chelubai), and in verse 10 traces Amminadab's descent through Arran rather than Ram.
- **2:18** — The Greek literally states that Caleb fathered Gazuba a wife and Elioth, reflecting a literal rendering of the Hebrew construction referring to his wives Azubah and Jerioth.
- **2:24** — Where the Hebrew reads 'in Caleb-ephrathah,' the Septuagint reads a verb and subject, 'Caleb came to Ephrathah.'
- **2:38** — In verses 38 and 47, the Septuagint reads 'Joshua' (Greek Iesous) where the Hebrew text has 'Jehu' and 'Jahdai,' respectively.
- **2:53** — The Septuagint transliterates the Hebrew word for 'families' as the proper name Emospheos and reads 'the cities of Jair' rather than 'Kiriath-jearim,' while verse 55 renders 'families of scribes' literally as 'the families of writings.'
