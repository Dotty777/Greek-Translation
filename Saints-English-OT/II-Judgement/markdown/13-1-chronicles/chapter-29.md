## Text

**29:1** And David the king said to all the assembly, "Solomon my son, whom the Lord has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great, for it is not for man, but for the Lord God.

**29:2** With all my strength I have prepared for the house of my God gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, soom stones and stones for setting, and costly and variegated stones, and every precious stone, and Parian marble in abundance.

**29:3** Moreover, because I have set my delight on the house of my God, I have gold and silver that I have acquired for myself, and behold, I have given it to the house of my God over and above, apart from what I have prepared for the house of the holy things:

**29:4** three thousand talents of gold from Souphir and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay with them the walls of the temple

**29:5** by the hand of craftsmen. And who is willing to fill his hands today for the Lord?"

**29:6** Then the rulers of the fathers' houses, and the rulers of the sons of Israel, and the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds, and the overseers of the works, and the builders of the king offered willingly;

**29:7** and they gave for the works of the house of the Lord five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand gold pieces, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

**29:8** And those with whom stones were found gave them to the storehouses of the house of the Lord by the hand of Besiel the Gersomnite.

**29:9** Then the people rejoiced because they had offered willingly, for with a full heart they had offered willingly to the Lord; and David the king rejoiced greatly.

**29:10** And King David blessed the Lord before the assembly, saying,

**29:11** "Blessed are You, O Lord God of Israel, our Father, from everlasting even to everlasting. You, O Lord, are the greatness and the power and the splendor and the victory and the strength, for You exercise dominion over all things in heaven and on earth; before Your presence every king and nation is troubled.

**29:12** From You come riches and glory; You rule over all, O Lord, the Ruler of every dominion, and in Your hand are strength and might; and in Your hand, O Almighty, it is to make great and to strengthen all things.

**29:13** And now, O Lord, we give thanks to You and praise the name of Your splendor.

**29:14** And who am I, and who are Your people, that we have had the strength to offer willingly to You in this manner? For all things belong to You, and from what is Yours we have given to You.

**29:15** For we are sojourners before You, and dwellers, as were all our fathers; like a shadow are our days upon the earth, and there is no abiding.

**29:16** O Lord our God, as for all this abundance that I have prepared for a house to be built for Your holy name, it is from Your hand, and all things belong to You.

**29:17** And I know, O Lord, that You are the One who tests hearts, and You love righteousness. In singleness of heart I have willingly offered all these things; and now I have seen Your people who are found here offering willingly to You with gladness.

**29:18** O Lord God of Abraham, and Isaac, and Israel our fathers, preserve these things in the thoughts of the heart of Your people forever, and direct their hearts toward You.

**29:19** And to Solomon my son give a good heart to do Your commandments, Your testimonies, and Your ordinances, and to bring to completion the building of Your house."

**29:20** Then David said to all the assembly, "Bless the Lord our God." And all the assembly blessed the Lord God of their fathers, and bending their knees, they bowed down before the Lord and the king.

**29:21** And David sacrificed sacrifices to the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings to God on the day after the first day: one thousand young bulls, one thousand rams, one thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**29:22** And they ate and drank before the Lord on that day with joy. And they made Solomon the son of David king, and anointed him to the Lord as king, and Zadok to the priesthood.

**29:23** And Solomon sat upon the throne of David his father, and he was approved, and all Israel obeyed him.

**29:24** The rulers and the mighty men, and the sons of King David his father, submitted themselves to him.

**29:25** And the Lord magnified Solomon from above before all Israel; and they gave him the glory of a king such as had not been upon any king before him.

**29:26** And David the son of Jesse reigned over Israel

**29:27** forty years: in Hebron seven years, in Jerusalem thirty-three years.

**29:28** And he died in a good old age, full of days, with riches and glory; and Solomon his son reigned in his place.

**29:29** Now the rest of the acts of King David, the former and the latter, are written in the words of Samuel the seer, and in the words of Nathan the prophet, and in the words of Gad the seer,

**29:30** concerning all his reign and his might, which came to pass upon him, and upon Israel, and upon all the kingdoms of the earth. Solomon the son of David grew strong over his kingdom; and the Lord his God was with him, and exalted him on high.

## Translator Notes

- **29:2** — Greek "soom stones" transliterates the Hebrew gem name shoham (commonly identified as onyx), and Greek "parion" specifically designates Parian marble.
- **29:4** — Greek reads "Souphir," the characteristic Septuagint form of the place name corresponding to Ophir in the Hebrew text.
- **29:8** — Greek reads "Besiel the Gersomnite," differing from the Hebrew text's Jehiel the Gershonite.
- **29:11** — Greek uses the nominative "You, O Lord" and concludes with the distinctive Septuagint clause "before Your presence every king and nation is troubled," in place of the Hebrew "Yours is the kingdom, O LORD, and You are exalted as head over all."
- **29:30** — The final sentence ("Solomon the son of David grew strong...") is a Septuagint reading that anticipates 2 Chronicles 1:1 as a bridge between the two books.
