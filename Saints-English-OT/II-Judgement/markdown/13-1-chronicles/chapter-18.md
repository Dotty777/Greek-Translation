## Text

**18:1** Now it came to pass after this that David struck the Philistines and routed them, and he took Gath and its villages from the hand of the Philistines.

**18:2** And he struck Moab, and the Moabites became servants to David, bringing gifts.

**18:3** And David struck Hadarezer king of Zobah-Hamath, as he went to establish his power at the river Euphrates.

**18:4** And David captured from them one thousand chariots, seven thousand horses, and twenty thousand men; and David disabled all the chariots, yet left for himself one hundred chariots from among them.

**18:5** And the Syrians from Damascus came to help Hadarezer king of Zobah, and he struck twenty-two thousand men among the Syrians.

**18:6** Then David placed a garrison in Syria of Damascus, and they became servants to David, bringing gifts. And the Lord preserved David wherever he went.

**18:7** And David took the golden collars that were upon the servants of Hadarezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

**18:8** And from Metabechas and from the choice wars of Hadarezer, David took exceedingly much bronze; from it Solomon made the bronze sea, and the pillars, and the vessels of bronze.

**18:9** Now Tou king of Hamath heard that David had struck the entire army of Hadarezer king of Zobah,

**18:10** so he sent Hadoram his son to King David to inquire of his welfare and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadarezer and struck him—for Tou was an adversary in war to Hadarezer—along with all kinds of vessels of silver and gold.

**18:11** These also David consecrated to the Lord, along with the silver and the gold that he had taken from all the nations—from Idumea, and Moab, and from the sons of Ammon, and from the Philistines, and from Amalek.

**18:12** Moreover, Abishai the son of Zeruiah struck Idumea in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand;

**18:13** and he placed garrisons in the valley, and all the Idumeans became servants to David. And the Lord preserved him wherever he went.

**18:14** So David reigned over all Israel, and he was executing judgment and righteousness for all his people.

**18:15** Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahijah was recorder;

**18:16** Zadok the son of Ahitub and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar were the priests; Joshua was scribe;

**18:17** and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the priests and Phalteia; and the sons of David were the chief deputies of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — Here and in verse 11, 'Philistines' renders Greek allophyloi (literally 'foreigners'), the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines.
- **18:7** — Greek reads 'golden collars' (kloious), where the Hebrew text reads 'shields of gold.'
- **18:8** — Greek reads 'Metabechas' (for Hebrew 'Tibhath') and 'choice wars' (eklekton polemon), likely an early scribal shift from 'choice cities' (poleon), where the Hebrew text reads 'Chun.'
- **18:15** — In verses 15-16, the Greek text reads 'Ahijah' (for Hebrew 'Ahilud') and 'Joshua' (Greek Iesous, for Hebrew 'Shavsha').
- **18:17** — Greek reads 'over the priests and Phalteia,' corresponding to the Hebrew text's 'over the Cherethites and the Pelethites.'
