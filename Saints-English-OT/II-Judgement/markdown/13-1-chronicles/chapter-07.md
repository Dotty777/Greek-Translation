## Text

**7:1** And the sons of Issachar were Tholaek, Phut, Jassur, and Shimron—four.

**7:2** And the sons of Thole were Uzzi, Raphara, Reiel, Eikan, Basan, and Isamuel, heads of their fathers' houses belonging to Tholaei, mighty men of valor according to their generations. Their number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

**7:3** And the sons of Zeirrei were Zareia and Michael; and the sons of Zareia were Meibdeia, Rael, and Eisia—five, all of them chiefs.

**7:4** And with them, according to their generations, according to their fathers' houses, were mighty men fit to be arrayed for war, thirty-six thousand; for they had multiplied wives and sons.

**7:5** And their brethren throughout all the families of Issachar, mighty men of valor as well, were eighty-seven thousand, the number of them all.

**7:6** Benjamin, Abeira, and Adeiel—three.

**7:7** And the sons of Badee were Asebon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Areimoth, and Uri—five, heads of fathers' houses, mighty men of valor; and their number was twenty-two thousand and thirty-four.

**7:8** And the sons of Abachei were Amarias, Joash, Eliezer, Eleithainan, Amareia, Auremoth, Abiud, Anathoth, and Gemeeth. All these were the sons of Amacheir,

**7:9** and their number according to their generations, heads of their fathers' houses, mighty men of valor, was twenty thousand two hundred.

**7:10** And the sons of Ariel were Balaan. And the sons of Balaan were Jeush, Benjamin, Aoth, Canaan, Zaithan, Ramessai, and Acheisadar.

**7:11** All these were the sons of Ariel, heads of their families, mighty men of valor, seventeen thousand two hundred, going forth in force to make war;

**7:12** and Sapphin and Happhin, and the sons of Raom, his son Aer.

**7:13** The sons of Naphtali were Jeisiel, Goni, Isseier, and Solomon, the sons of Balam.

**7:14** The sons of Manasseh were Asriel, whom his Syrian concubine bore; she bore Machir the father of Gilead.

**7:15** And Machir took a wife for Hamphin and Mamphin; and the name of his sister was Moocha, and the name of the second was Sapphaad.

**7:16** And Moocha the wife of Machir bore a son, and she called his name Souros; his son was Ulam,

**7:17** Badam. These were the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh.

**7:18** And his sister Malecheth bore Isadek, Abiezer, and Maela.

**7:19** And the sons of Semeira were Jaaim, Shechem, Lakeeim, and Alialeim.

**7:20** And the sons of Ephraim were Sothalath; the sons of Laada: his son Noome,

**7:21** his son Zabed, his sons Ozar and Elaad; and the men of Gath who were born in the land slew him, because they came down to take their livestock.

**7:22** And Ephraim their father mourned many days, and their brethren came to comfort him.

**7:23** And he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Bargaa, because, "It happened in evil in my house

**7:24** and among those who remained." And he built Lower and Upper Beth Horon. And the sons of Ozan were Seera

**7:25** and Raphe, his sons; Saraph and Thalees were his sons; Thaen was his son.

**7:26** To Gedson his son, Jeeth was his son, Zemma was his son,

**7:27** the sons of Nun, the sons of Joshua, his sons.

**7:28** And their possession and their dwelling were Bethel and its villages, toward the east Naarnan, toward the west Gezer and its villages, and Shechem and its villages as far as Gaian and its villages;

**7:29** and as far as the borders of the sons of Manasseh, Beth Shan and its villages, Thalme and its villages; and Balad and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, Dor and its villages. In this dwelt the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel.

**7:30** The sons of Asher were Inina, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriga, and Sore, and their sister.

**7:31** The sons of Bericha were Gaber and Melleie; he was the father of Bezaith.

**7:32** And Chaber fathered Iphamel, Samer, Chothan, and Sola their sister.

**7:33** And the sons of Aphalech were Baisechi, Imabael, and Aseith; these were the sons of Japhalel.

**7:34** And the sons of Semmer were Achiuia, Hobab, Akaran,

**7:35** and Balaam; his brethren were Sochath, Imana, Zeme, and Amaa.

**7:36** The sons of Sophas were Chuchi, Hanarphar, Sula, Sabrei, Imare,

**7:37** Basan, Oa, Sama, Salisa, Jethra, and Beera.

**7:38** And the sons of Jether were Iphina, Phasphai, and Ara;

**7:39** and the sons of Ola were Orech, Hanniel, and Raseia.

**7:40** All these were the sons of Asher, all of them heads of families, choice and mighty men of valor, chief leaders; their number for marshaling to make war—their number was twenty-six thousand men.

## Translator Notes

- **7:6** — The Greek reads simply "Benjamin, Abeira, and Adeiel—three," omitting "the sons of" and diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, and Jediael"). Throughout this chapter, several personal names preserve distinct Septuagint forms (such as Tholaek, Phut, and Jassur in 7:1; Zeirrei in 7:3; and Ariel in 7:10–11).
- **7:13** — The Greek reads "Solomon" and "Balam" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Shallum" and "Bilhah."
- **7:16** — The Greek calls Machir's son "Souros" (Hebrew "Peresh") and passes directly to "his son was Ulam" and "Badam" in 7:17, lacking the intervening clauses found in the Hebrew text.
- **7:24** — The Greek renders the opening clause ("and among those who remained") as a continuation of Ephraim's statement in 7:23 rather than introducing a daughter named Sheerah as in the Hebrew, and it takes "Ozan" and "Seera" as personal names rather than the town Uzzen-sheerah.
- **7:29** — The Greek lists "Thalme" and "Balad" where the Hebrew has "Taanach," and reads singular "in this" (referring to the territory) rather than plural "in these."
