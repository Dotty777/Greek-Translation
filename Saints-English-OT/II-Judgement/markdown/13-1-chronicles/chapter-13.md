## Text

**13:1** And David took counsel with the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds, and with every leader.

**13:2** And David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it has been prospered by the Lord our God, let us send out to your brethren who remain in all the land of Israel, and with them the priests, the Levites, in the cities of their possession, that they may be gathered together to us;

**13:3** and let us bring over the ark of our God to us, for they have not sought it since the days of Saul."

**13:4** And all the assembly said that they would do so, for the word was right in the eyes of all the people.

**13:5** So David assembled all Israel together, from the borders of Egypt even to the entrance of Hamath, to bring in the ark of God from the city of Jearim.

**13:6** And David brought it up; and all Israel went up to the city of David, which belonged to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God the Lord, who sits enthroned upon the cherubim, where His name was called upon.

**13:7** And they set the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Aminadab; and Uzzah and his brethren drove the cart.

**13:8** And David and all Israel were playing before God with all their might, with singers, and with lyres, and with harps, with timbrels, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.

**13:9** And they came to the threshing floor; and Uzzah stretched out his hand to take hold of the ark, and the ox tilted it.

**13:10** And the Lord was angry with Uzzah, and He struck him there because he stretched out his hand to the ark; and he died there before God.

**13:11** And David was downcast because the Lord had broken out with a breach upon Uzzah; and he called that place Breach of Uzzah, to this day.

**13:12** And David feared God that day, saying, "How shall I bring the ark of God in to myself?"

**13:13** So David did not bring the ark back to himself into the city of David, but turned it aside into the house of Abeddara the Gittite.

**13:14** And the ark of God remained in the house of Abeddara three months; and God blessed Abeddaram and all that belonged to him.

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — The Greek reads "the city of David" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text names Baalah (Kirjath Jearim).
- **13:7** — The Greek reads "Aminadab" and "his brethren," where the Hebrew text reads "Abinadab" and "Ahio."
- **13:11** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Perez-Uzzah directly as "Breach of Uzzah," preserving the wordplay with "broken out with a breach."
- **13:13** — Abeddara (spelled Abeddaram in 13:14) is the Septuagint form corresponding to Obed-Edom in the Hebrew text.
