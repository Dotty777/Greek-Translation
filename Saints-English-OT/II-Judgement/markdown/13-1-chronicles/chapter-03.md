## Text

**3:1** Now these were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Israelitess; the second, Damniel, by Abigail the Carmelitess;

**3:2** the third, Absalom the son of Maacah, the daughter of Thoamai king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith;

**3:3** the fifth, Shephatiah, by Sabital; the sixth, Ithream, by Ala his wife.

**3:4** Six were born to him in Hebron. And he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**3:5** And these were born to him in Jerusalem: Saman, Soban, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bathsheba the daughter of Ammiel;

**3:6** and Baar, Elisha, and Eliphelet,

**3:7** and Nogah, Naphath, and Janue,

**3:8** and Elishama, Elida, and Eliphala—nine.

**3:9** All these were the sons of David, besides the sons of the concubines; and Tamar was their sister.

**3:10** The sons of Solomon were Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

**3:11** Joram his son, Uzziah his son, Joash his son,

**3:12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

**3:13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

**3:14** Amnon his son, and Josiah his son.

**3:15** And the sons of Josiah were the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, and the fourth Shallum.

**3:16** And the sons of Jehoiakim were Jeconiah his son, and Zedekiah his son.

**3:17** And the sons of Jeconiah were Assir, Shealtiel his son,

**3:18** Malchiram, Phaldaias, Sanesar, Jekenia, Hosamoth, and Denethi.

**3:19** And the sons of Shealtiel were Zerubbabel; Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister;

**3:20** and Hashubah, Osa, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Arobasok—five.

**3:21** And the sons of Hananiah were Phalleti, and Isaba his son, Raphal his son, Orna his son, Obadiah his son, and Shecaniah his son.

**3:22** And the son of Shecaniah was Shemaiah; and the son of Shemaiah was Hattush, and Joel, Marei, Noadiah, and Shaphat—six.

**3:23** And the sons of Noadiah were Elithana, Hezekiah, and Ezrikan—three.

**3:24** And the sons of Elithenan were Hodoliah, Aseib, Phara, Jakun, Johanan, Delaiah, and Manei—seven.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek reads "the Israelitess" (rather than "the Jezreelitess" of the Hebrew text) and spells David's second son "Damniel" (for "Daniel").
- **3:4** — The Septuagint omits the clause found in the Hebrew text stating that David reigned in Hebron seven years and six months.
- **3:11** — The Greek reads "Uzziah" (Ozeia) where the Hebrew text has "Ahaziah," and similarly reads "Amnon" for "Amon" in verse 14.
- **3:19** — The Septuagint lists Zerubbabel directly as the son of Shealtiel (omitting Pedaiah), matching the genealogy in Ezra 3:2, Haggai 1:1, and Matthew 1:12.
