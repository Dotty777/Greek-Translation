## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — In the Septuagint, chapter 6 begins here (corresponding to Hebrew MT 6:16, as MT 6:1–15 stand at the end of chapter 5); the Greek consistently writes Gedson for Gershon throughout this chapter.
- **6:8** — In 6:6–13 the Greek text preserves several distinct personal names compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Joab, Adei, Jaara, Haresi, Suphi, Kainath, Kohath (in 6:9), Alimoth, Abiathar, Aseri, Idaer, and Sani.
- **6:14** — The Greek omits "of Merari: Mahli" (found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text at 6:29), beginning the verse literally "The sons: Libni his son."
- **6:17** — The Greek reads "the tabernacle of the house of testimony" (MT: "the tabernacle of the tent of meeting"), "with instruments" (MT: "with singing"), and "the house" (MT: "the house of the Lord").
- **6:19** — In the lineages of the singers (6:19–30), the Greek preserves several distinct name forms compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Jeroboam, Tohu, Meth, Amathiu, Abiasar, Aziah, Zammam, Etham, and Asebi.
- **6:37** — In the high-priestly lineage (6:36–38), the Greek preserves the distinct name forms Seraiah (MT: Zerahiah), Mariel (MT: Meraioth), and Ahisama (MT: Ahimaaz).
- **6:42** — In 6:42–45 the Greek preserves several distinct city names compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Selna, Eglam, Gethoron, the additional city Attan (6:44), and Anchoch (6:45).
- **6:48** — The Greek reads "from the tribe of Dan" (Hebrew MT 6:63 reads "from the tribe of Gad"), and in 6:50 lists only Judah and Simeon (omitting Benjamin).
- **6:54** — Compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek lists one city in 6:54 (omitting Gath Rimmon) and one in 6:55 (Amar, omitting Bileam), and in 6:56–62 preserves several distinct place-name forms (including Aseroth, Deberi, Dabor, Jethar, Debir, Maasa, Abaran, Hamoth, and Thachia).
- **6:63** — The Greek reads "and Jericho on the west of the Jordan" (Hebrew MT 6:78 reads "by Jericho on the east side of the Jordan"), omits Jahzah, and in 6:64–66 preserves the place-name forms Kadamos, Maephla, Rammon Gilead, Maanaith, and Gazer.
