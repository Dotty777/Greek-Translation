## Text

**14:1** Now Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and cedar timbers, and builders, and carpenters in wood, to build him a house.

**14:2** And David knew that the Lord had established him over Israel, because his kingdom was exalted on high for the sake of His people Israel.

**14:3** And David took more wives in Jerusalem, and more sons and daughters were born to David.

**14:4** And these are the names of those born to him, who were his in Jerusalem: Samaa, Isoboam, Nathan, Solomon,

**14:5** and Baar, and Ektae, and Eliphaleth,

**14:6** and Nageth, and Naphath, and Janou,

**14:7** and Elisamae, and Balegdae, and Emphalet.

**14:8** Now when the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David; and David heard of it, and went out to meet them.

**14:9** And the Philistines came and converged in the Valley of the Giants.

**14:10** And David inquired through God, saying, "Shall I go up against the Philistines, and will You deliver them into my hands?" And the Lord said to him, "Go up, and I will deliver them into your hands."

**14:11** So he went up to Phaalphathisim, and David struck them there; and David said, "He has broken through my enemies by my hand like a breakthrough of water." Therefore he called the name of that place Breach of Pharisin.

**14:12** And they abandoned their gods, and David gave command to burn them with fire.

**14:13** And the Philistines once again converged in the Valley of the Giants.

**14:14** Therefore David inquired again of God, and God said to him, "You shall not go after them; turn away from them, and come upon them near the pear trees.

**14:15** And it shall be, when you hear the sound of the shaking of the very tops of the pear trees, then you shall go out to battle, for God has gone out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines."

**14:16** So he did just as God commanded him, and he struck the camp of the Philistines from Gibeon as far as Gazara.

**14:17** And the name of David was spread abroad in all the land, and the Lord put the fear of him upon all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **14:9** — "The Valley of the Giants" renders the Septuagint translation of the Hebrew Valley of Rephaim.
- **14:11** — Phaalphathisim and Breach of Pharisin are the Septuagint forms corresponding to Baal-Perazim, translating "Breach" into Greek to reflect David's wordplay.
- **14:14** — "The pear trees" follows the specific Greek reading (apioi), where the Hebrew term (bekha'im) is often rendered "mulberry" or "balsam" trees.
- **14:16** — Gazara is the Septuagint form corresponding to Gezer in the Hebrew text.
