**1:1** Adam, Seth, Enosh,

**1:2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

**1:3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**1:4** Noah. The sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, Japheth.

**1:5** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elishah, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**1:6** And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**1:7** And the sons of Javan: Elishah and Tarshish, the Kittim and the Rhodians.

**1:8** And the sons of Ham: Cush and Mizraim, Put and Canaan.

**1:9** And the sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**1:10** And Cush fathered Nimrod; he began to be a giant hunter upon the earth.

**1:17** The sons of Shem: Elam and Asshur,

**1:24** and Arphaxad, Shelah,

**1:25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**1:26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**1:27** Abraham.

**1:28** Now the sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.

**1:29** Now these are the generations of the firstborn of Ishmael: Nebaioth and Kedar, Adbeel, Massa,

**1:30** Mama, Dumah, Manasseh, Chondan, Tema,

**1:31** Jetur, Naphish, Kedemah; these are the sons of Ishmael.

**1:32** And the sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine—she bore to him Zimran, Jokshan, Midian, Medan, Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan: Dedan and Sheba.

**1:33** And the sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these are the sons of Keturah.

**1:34** And Abraham fathered Isaac. And the sons of Isaac: Jacob and Esau.

**1:35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**1:36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman and Omar, Zophar, Gatam, and Kenaz, and by Timna, Amalek.

**1:37** And the sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

**1:38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, and Onan.

**1:39** And the sons of Lotan: Hori, Heman, Elath, and Namna.

**1:40** The sons of Shobal: Solam, Manahath, Ebal, Shob, and Onan. And the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Sonan.

**1:41** The sons of Sonan: Dishon. And the sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**1:42** And the sons of Ezer: Balaam, Zukam, and Onan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

**1:43** And these are their kings: Balak the son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**1:44** And Balak died, and Jobab the son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.

**1:45** And Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

**1:46** And Husham died, and Hadad the son of Barad, who struck down Midian in the plain of Moab, reigned in his place, and the name of his city was Gethaim.

**1:47** And Hadad died, and in his place reigned—

**1:48** And Hadda died, and Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place; and Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth by the river reigned in his place. And Shaul died. And there were chiefs of Edom: Chief Teman, Chief Gola, Chief Jethet,

**1:49** Baal-Hanan the son of Achbor.

**1:50** And Baal-Hanan the son of Achbor died, and Hadad the son of Barad reigned in his place, and the name of his city was Peor.

**1:51** The chiefs of Edom: Chief Timna, Chief Golada, Chief Jether,

**1:52** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,

**1:53** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**1:54** Chief Magdiel, Chief Zaphoin; these are the chiefs of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **1:10** — The Greek text describes Nimrod as "a giant hunter" (gigas kynegos), echoing Genesis 10:8–9, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads simply "a mighty one."
- **1:17** — Following the shorter Greek manuscript tradition (Codex Vaticanus), verses 11–16 and 17b–23 are absent here, linking Elam and Asshur directly to Arphaxad in verse 24; in verse 5, the Greek also includes Elishah among the sons of Japheth.
- **1:30** — The Greek preserves distinctive proper-name forms here—Mama, Manasseh, and Chondan (with Massa placed in verse 29)—and similarly preserves Jacob before Esau in verse 34 and distinctive Greek forms such as Onan, Elath, Namna, Solam, Shob, Sonan, Balaam, and Zukam in verses 38–42.
- **1:43** — The Greek introduces the Edomite rulers concisely as "their kings" (omitting the reference to kings reigning over Israel) and preserves the Greek name forms Balak here, Barad and Gethaim in verse 46, and Barad and Peor in verse 50.
- **1:47** — Verses 47–51 preserve a textual dislocation found in Codex Vaticanus: verse 47 breaks off mid-sentence, verse 48 introduces Hadda, Samlah, Shaul, and three Edomite chiefs (Teman, Gola, Jethet), verse 49 isolates Baal-Hanan son of Achbor, and verse 54 concludes with Zaphoin (corresponding to Iram in the Hebrew).
