## Text

**28:1** Now David assembled all the rulers of Israel, the rulers of the judges, and the rulers of the divisions who attended the person of the king, and the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the treasurers, and those who were over his possessions, and the mighty men, and the warriors, in Jerusalem.

**28:2** And David stood in the midst of the assembly and said, "Hear me, brethren and my people: It came into my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and a footstool for our Lord, and I have prepared the materials needed for the dwelling.

**28:3** But God said, 'You shall not build a house for Me to call My name upon it, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.'

**28:4** Yet the Lord God of Israel chose me out of all the house of my father to be king over Israel forever; and in Judah He chose the kingdom, and from the house of Judah, the house of my father; and in me He took pleasure, that I should become king over all Israel.

**28:5** And of all my sons—for the Lord has given me many sons—He has chosen Solomon my son to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

**28:6** And God said to me, 'Solomon your son shall inherit My house and My court, for I have chosen him to be My son, and I will be a Father to him.

**28:7** And I will establish his kingdom forever, if he is steadfast to keep My commandments and My judgments, as it is this day.'

**28:8** And now, in the sight of all the assembly of the Lord, seek out the commandments of the Lord our God, that you may inherit the good land for our sons forever.

**28:9** And now, Solomon, know the God of your fathers and serve Him with a perfect heart and a willing soul; for the Lord searches all hearts and knows every thought. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; and if you forsake Him, He will forsake you to the end.

**28:10** See, therefore: the Lord has chosen you to build a house for Him as a sanctuary; be strong and do it."

**28:11** Then David gave to Solomon his son the pattern of the temple, and of its houses, and of its treasuries, and of the upper chambers, and of the inner storehouses, and of the house of atonement,

**28:12** and the pattern that he had in his spirit of the courts of the house of the Lord, and of all the chambers round about, for the storehouses of the Lord, and of the storehouses of the holy things,

**28:13** and of the quarters of the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of the service of the house of the Lord, and of the storehouses of the vessels for service.

**28:14** And he gave him the exact weight of them, both of the gold and of the silver, and the weight of the lampstands

**28:15** and of the lamps.

**28:16** He gave him likewise the weight of the tables of the showbread, for each table of gold, and likewise for those of silver;

**28:17** and of the flesh-hooks, and the libation cups, and the bowls of gold, and the weight of those of gold and of silver, for each weight.

**28:18** And he showed him the weight of the altar of incense of refined gold, and the pattern of the chariot of the cherubim that spread out their wings and overshadowed the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**28:19** All these things in writing from the hand of the Lord David gave to Solomon, according to the understanding that had come to him concerning the workmanship of the pattern.

**28:20** And David said to Solomon his son, "Be strong and of good courage, and do it; do not fear nor be dismayed, for the Lord my God is with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you until you have finished all the work for the service of the house of the Lord. The pattern of the temple and of its house and its treasury, and the upper chambers and the inner storehouses and the house of atonement, and the pattern of the house of the Lord.

**28:21** And behold, the divisions of the priests and the Levites are for all the service of the house of the Lord; and they are with you in every undertaking, along with every willing man in wisdom for every craft; and the rulers and all the people are ready for all your words."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Greek reads "the rulers of the judges" (ἄρχοντας τῶν κριτῶν) where the Hebrew text has "the officials of the tribes."
- **28:6** — The Greek reads "shall inherit My house and My court" (κληρονομήσει... τὴν αὐλήν μου), whereas the Hebrew text reads "shall build My house and My courts."
- **28:11** — "Its treasuries" renders the Septuagint transliteration ζακχώ (and σακχώ in v. 20), derived from a Semitic term for treasuries or storerooms; the Greek also reads "of the temple" (τοῦ ναοῦ) where the Hebrew has "of the vestibule."
- **28:14** — Verses 14 and 15 are substantially shorter in the Greek text than in the Masoretic Hebrew, omitting the detailed repetitions of the weights for the individual vessels and lampstands.
- **28:20** — The concluding sentence of this verse ("The pattern of the temple... house of the Lord.") is a Septuagint doublet repeating elements from verses 11 and 12 within David's closing charge to Solomon.
