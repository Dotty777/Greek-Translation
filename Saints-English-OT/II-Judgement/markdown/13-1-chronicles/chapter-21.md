## Text

**21:1** And the devil stood up in Israel, and incited David to number Israel.

**21:2** And King David said to Joab and to the commanders of the army, "Go now, number Israel from Beersheba even to Dan, and bring it to me, and I shall know their number."

**21:3** And Joab said, "May the Lord add to His people a hundredfold as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king behold it; all of them are servants to my lord. Why does my lord seek this thing? Do not let it become a sin to Israel."

**21:4** And he came to Jerusalem.

**21:5** And Joab gave the number of the muster of the people to David; and all Israel was one million one hundred thousand men who drew the sword.

**21:6** But Levi and Benjamin he did not number among them, because the word of the king prevailed over Joab.

**21:7** And it was evil in the sight of God concerning this matter, and He struck Israel.

**21:8** And David said to God, "I have sinned greatly, because I have done this thing; and now, take away, I pray, the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."

**21:9** And the Lord spoke to Gad the seer,

**21:10** "Go and speak to David, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Three things I lift up against you; choose for yourself one of them, and I shall do it to you.'"

**21:11** And Gad came to David and said to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'Choose for yourself

**21:12** either three years of famine, or three months to flee from before your enemies, with the sword of your enemies to destroy, or three days of the sword of the Lord and pestilence in the land, with the angel of the Lord destroying throughout all the inheritance of Israel.' And now, see what I shall answer Him who sent the word."

**21:13** And David said to Gad, "All three are exceedingly grievous to me; let me fall now into the hands of the Lord, for His mercies are exceedingly great, and let me by no means fall into the hands of men."

**21:14** And the Lord sent pestilence in Israel, and seventy thousand men of Israel fell.

**21:15** And God sent an angel to Jerusalem to destroy it. And as he was destroying, the Lord saw and relented concerning the calamity, and said to the destroying angel, "Let it be enough for you; stay your hand." And the angel of the Lord was standing at the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.

**21:16** And David lifted up his eyes and saw the angel of the Lord standing between the earth and heaven, with his sword drawn in his hand, stretched out over Jerusalem; and David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell upon their faces.

**21:17** And David said to God, "Was it not I who commanded to number the people? And I am the one who has sinned, and I have indeed done wickedly; but these sheep, what have they done? O Lord God, let Your hand be upon me and upon the house of my father, and not upon Your people for destruction, O Lord."

**21:18** And the angel of the Lord spoke to Gad to tell David that he should go up to erect an altar to the Lord at the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.

**21:19** And David went up according to the word of Gad, which he had spoken in the name of the Lord.

**21:20** And Orna turned back and saw the king, and his four sons with him were hiding themselves; now Orna was threshing wheat.

**21:21** And David came to Orna, and Orna went out from the threshing floor and bowed down to David with his face to the ground.

**21:22** And David said to Orna, "Give me your place of the threshing floor, and I shall build upon it an altar to the Lord; give it to me for its full value in silver, and the plague shall cease from the people."

**21:23** And Orna said to David, "Take it for yourself, and let my lord the king do what is good in his sight; behold, I have given the calves for a burnt offering, and the plow for wood, and the wheat for a sacrifice; I have given it all."

**21:24** And King David said to Orna, "No, for I will surely buy it for its full value in silver; for I will by no means take what is yours for the Lord, to offer up a burnt offering without cost to the Lord."

**21:25** So David gave to Orna for his place six hundred shekels of gold by weight.

**21:26** And David built there an altar to the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings; and he cried out to the Lord, and He answered him by fire from heaven upon the altar of burnt offering, and it consumed the burnt offering.

**21:27** And the Lord spoke to the angel, and he put the sword back into the sheath.

**21:28** At that time, when David saw that He had answered him at the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite, he sacrificed there.

**21:29** Now the tabernacle of the Lord, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offerings were at that time in Bamoth in Gibeon.

**21:30** And David was not able to go before it to seek God, for he did not hasten from before the sword of the angel of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — Greek diabolos (without the article) denotes "the devil" or "an adversary," rendering Hebrew satan.
- **21:4** — The Septuagint verse is brief, omitting the account of Joab's circuit through Israel found in the Hebrew text, while placing the statement that the king's word prevailed over Joab in verse 6.
- **21:5** — The Septuagint gives only Israel's muster of one million one hundred thousand men and omits the separate count for Judah found in the Hebrew text.
- **21:20** — The Septuagint reads "the king" rather than "the angel," and transliterates the Hebrew participle meaning "hiding themselves" as methachabein; throughout verses 15-28 it also renders the Jebusite's name as Orna (Hebrew Ornan).
- **21:29** — Greek en Bamoth en Gabaon transliterates the Hebrew term for "the high place" as the place name Bamoth; in verse 30, Greek ou katespeusen ("did not hasten") also differs from the Hebrew ("was terrified").
