## Text

**5:1** Now the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: for he was the firstborn, and when he went up to his father's bed, he gave his blessing to his son Joseph, the son of Israel, and he was not reckoned in the genealogy for the birthright;

**5:2** for Judah was mighty in strength even among his brethren, and from him came a ruler, yet the blessing belonged to Joseph.

**5:3** The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch and Pallu, Harson and Carmi.

**5:4** The sons of Joel were Shimei, and Benaiah his son. And the sons of Gog the son of Shimei:

**5:5** his son Echa, his son Recha, his son Joel,

**5:6** his son Beel, whom Thalgabanasar king of Assyria carried away into exile; he was chief of the Reubenites.

**5:7** And his brethren by his family, in their enrollments according to their generations: the chief was Joel, and Zechariah,

**5:8** and Balec the son of Ozuz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel; he dwelt in Aroer, and as far as Nebo and Beelmasson,

**5:9** and toward the east he dwelt as far as the entrance of the wilderness from the river Euphrates, for he had much livestock in the land of Gilead.

**5:10** And in the days of Saul they made war against the sojourners, and they fell by their hands, dwelling in tents, even all of them toward the east of Gilead.

**5:11** The sons of Gad dwelt opposite them in Bashan as far as Elcha:

**5:12** Joel was the firstborn, and Sabat the second, and Janin the scribe in Bashan.

**5:13** And their brethren, according to the houses of their fathers, were Michael, Meshullam, and Sheba, and Jorai, and Chima, and Zue, and Obed—eight.

**5:14** These were the sons of Abihaia, the sons of Huri, the son of Idai, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Isai, the son of Juri, the son of Zabucham,

**5:15** the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, chief of the house of fathers,

**5:16** of the inhabitants in Gilead, in Bashan and in their villages, and all the surrounding regions of Geriam as far as the border.

**5:17** The enrollment of all these took place in the days of Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

**5:18** The sons of Reuben and Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh, from the sons of valor—men bearing shields and sword, drawing the bow, and trained in war—were forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty, going forth to battle.

**5:19** And they made war with the Hagrites, and the Turites, and the Naphisadaites, and the Nadabites.

**5:20** And they prevailed over them, and the Hagrites and all their tents were delivered into their hands; for they cried out to God in the battle, and He heard them, because they hoped in Him.

**5:21** And they took captive their possessions: five thousand camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand souls of men;

**5:22** for many fell slain, because the war was of God. And they dwelt in their place until the exile.

**5:23** And the half-tribe of Manasseh dwelt from Bashan as far as Baalim and Senir and Mount Hermon; and in Lebanon they multiplied.

**5:24** And these were the heads of their fathers' house: Epher, and Shei, and Eliel, and Esdriel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jeliel—mighty men of valor, men of renown, chiefs of the houses of their fathers.

**5:25** And they acted unfaithfully against the God of their fathers, and played the harlot after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had removed from before them.

**5:26** So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Phaloch king of Assyria, and the spirit of Thagnaphamasar king of Assyria, and he carried away into exile Reuben, and the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Chaach and Habor and to the river Chozar, to this day.

**5:27** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**5:28** And the sons of Kohath: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**5:29** And the children of Amram: Aaron and Moses and Miriam. And the sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**5:30** Eleazar fathered Phinehas; Phinehas fathered Abishua;

**5:31** Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi;

**5:32** Uzzi fathered Zerahiah; Zerahiah fathered Mariel;

**5:33** and Mariel fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub;

**5:34** and Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz;

**5:35** and Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan;

**5:36** and Johanan fathered Azariah; it was he who ministered as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**5:37** And Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub;

**5:38** and Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum;

**5:39** and Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah;

**5:40** and Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.

**5:41** And Jehozadak went into the exile with Judah and Jerusalem by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Greek reads "when he went up to his father's bed, he gave his blessing to his son Joseph" rather than the Hebrew "because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph."
- **5:6** — Several Greek personal and place names in this chapter differ from the Hebrew (including Harson, Benaiah, Echa, Recha, Beel, Balec, Sabat, Chima, Obed, Idai, Zabucham, Geriam, Baalim, and Mariel); Thalgabanasar here and Thagnaphamasar in verse 26 (alongside Phaloch for Pul) reflect the Greek forms of Tiglath-Pileser.
- **5:10** — The Greek translates the name of the Hagrites as a common noun, "the sojourners," while retaining "Hagrites" in verses 19–20.
- **5:13** — Although only seven names are listed, the Greek explicitly gives the total as "eight" (the Hebrew reads "seven").
- **5:21** — The Greek reads "five thousand" camels, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "fifty thousand."
