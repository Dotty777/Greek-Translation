## Text

**15:1** And he made houses for himself in the city of David, and he prepared the place for the ark of God and made a tent for it.

**15:2** Then David said, "No one is to bear the ark of God except the Levites, for the Lord has chosen them, even to minister to Him forever."

**15:3** And David assembled all Israel at Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the Lord to the place which he had prepared for it.

**15:4** And David gathered together the sons of Aaron, the Levites:

**15:5** of the sons of Kohath, Uriel the chief, and his brethren, one hundred and ten;

**15:6** of the sons of Merari, Asai the chief, and his brethren, two hundred and fifty;

**15:7** of the sons of Gershom, Joel the chief, and his brethren, one hundred and fifty;

**15:8** of the sons of Elisaphat, Shemaiah the chief, and his brethren, two hundred;

**15:9** of the sons of Hebron, Ener the chief, and their brethren, eighty;

**15:10** of the sons of Uzziel, Amminadab the chief, and his brethren, one hundred and twelve.

**15:11** And David summoned Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites: Ariel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Enel, and Amminadab.

**15:12** And he said to them, "You are the chiefs of the fathers' houses of the Levites; sanctify yourselves, you and your brethren, and you shall bring up the ark of the God of Israel to the place where I have prepared for it.

**15:13** For because you were not there at the first, our God broke forth against us, because we did not inquire according to the ordinance."

**15:14** So the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves to bring up the ark of the God of Israel.

**15:15** And the sons of the Levites took up the ark of God, as Moses had commanded by the word of God according to the writing, with poles upon themselves.

**15:16** And David said to the chiefs of the Levites, "Appoint their brethren, the singers, with instruments—harps, lyres, and cymbals—to raise a sound on high with a voice of gladness."

**15:17** So the Levites appointed Heman the son of Joel; and of his brethren, Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari his brethren, Ethan the son of Kishaiah;

**15:18** and with them their brethren of the second rank: Zechariah, Uzziel, Shemiramoth, Jeiel, Elioel, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphena, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Uzziah, the gatekeepers.

**15:19** And the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were with cymbals of bronze to make them heard;

**15:20** and Zechariah, Uzziel, Shemiramoth, Eithel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah with harps according to Alamoth;

**15:21** and Mattithiah, Enphanaiah, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Uzziah with lyres on the Amasenith, to lead with strength.

**15:22** And Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was chief of the songs, for he was understanding.

**15:23** And Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.

**15:24** And Somniah, Jehoshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, were blowing the trumpets before the ark of God; and Obed-Edom and Jeiah were gatekeepers for the ark of God.

**15:25** So David, the elders of Israel, and the captains over thousands were going to bring up the ark of the covenant from the house of Obed-Edom with gladness.

**15:26** And it came to pass, when God strengthened the Levites who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, that they sacrificed seven calves and seven rams each.

**15:27** And David was girded with a robe of fine linen, as were all the Levites who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and the singers, and Jechoniah the chief of the songs of the singers; and upon David was a robe of fine linen.

**15:28** Thus all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the Lord with shouting and with the sound of the shofar, with trumpets and with cymbals, sounding aloud with harps and with lyres.

**15:29** And it came to pass, as the ark of the covenant of the Lord came to the city of David, that Michal the daughter of Saul looked down through the window and saw King David dancing and making merry; and she despised him in her soul.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — The Greek numbers for the Levitical divisions in verses 5-7 (110, 250, and 150) and several name forms in verses 8-11 (Elisaphat, Ener, Ariel, and Enel) follow the Septuagint text rather than the Hebrew.
- **15:15** — The Greek reads 'according to the writing' (kata ten graphen), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew term rendered 'on their shoulders' in the Masoretic Text.
- **15:20** — Alamoth (verse 20) and Amasenith (verse 21) are Greek transliterations of ancient Hebrew musical terms; several musician names in verses 18-21 and 24 (including Uzziel, Elioel, Eliphena, Eithel, Enphanaiah, Uzziah, and Somniah) preserve the distinct Greek forms.
- **15:27** — The Greek names Jechoniah rather than Chenaniah as chief of the songs, and describes David as wearing a robe of fine linen rather than a linen ephod.
