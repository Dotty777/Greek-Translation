## Text

**12:1** Now these were the ones who came to David at Sokla, while he was still hemmed in because of Saul the son of Kish; and they were among the mighty men, helpers in war,

**12:2** wielding the bow with both the right hand and the left, and slingers with stones and with bows, of the brethren of Saul from Benjamin:

**12:3** Ahiezer the chief, and Joa the son of Ama the Gebothite, and Joel and Jophalet the sons of Asmoth, and Berchia, and Jehul the Anathothite,

**12:4** and Samaias the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the thirty and over the thirty; Jeremiah, and Jezel, and Johanan, and Joazabab the Gadarathite;

**12:6** Azai, and Arimuth, and Badaia, and Samaraia, and Saphatia the Charaiphite;

**12:7** Elkanah, and Jesuni, and Ozriel, and Jozara, and Sobokam, and the Korahites;

**12:8** and Elia and Zabidia, the sons of Raam, and those of Gedor.

**12:9** And from the Gadites there separated themselves to David from the wilderness strong, mighty men of battle array, bearing shields and spears, whose faces were like the face of a lion, and nimble as gazelles upon the mountains in swiftness:

**12:10** Aza the chief, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,

**12:11** Masemmane the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,

**12:12** Ethoi the sixth, Eliab the seventh,

**12:13** Joan the eighth, Eliazer the ninth,

**12:14** Jeremiah the tenth, Melchabannai the eleventh.

**12:15** These were of the sons of Gad, captains of the host: the least was one over a hundred, and the greatest over a thousand.

**12:16** These are the ones who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it had overflowed all its banks; and they drove out all those who dwelt in the valleys, from the east to the west.

**12:17** And some of the sons of Benjamin and Judah came to the help of David.

**12:18** And David went out to meet them and said to them, "If you have come to me in peace, may my heart be wholly united toward you; but if to deliver me to my enemies, not in truth of hand, may the God of your fathers behold it and rebuke you."

**12:19** Then the Spirit clothed Amasai, chief of the thirty, and he said, "Go forward, you and your people, David son of Jesse! Peace, peace to you, and peace to your helpers! For your God has helped you." So David received them and appointed them captains of the forces.

**12:20** And from Manasseh some went over to David when the Philistines came against Saul to battle; yet they did not help them, for counsel was taken by the commanders of the Philistines, saying, "With the heads of those men he will return to his master Saul."

**12:21** As he went to Soglam, there went over to him from Manasseh: Edna, and Tozabath, and Rhodiel, and Michael, and Josabaith, and Elimuth, and Semathi, who are captains of thousands of Manasseh.

**12:22** And they fought alongside David against the Geddur, for they were all mighty men of valor; and they were leaders in the army, in the host,

**12:23** for day by day they came to David into a great host, like the host of God.

**12:24** Now these are the names of the captains of the army who came to David at Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul over to him, according to the word of the Lord:

**12:25** of the sons of Judah, bearing shields and spears, six thousand eight hundred mighty men of battle array;

**12:26** of the sons of Simeon, mighty men of valor for battle array, seven thousand one hundred;

**12:27** of the sons of Levi, four thousand six hundred;

**12:28** and Toadas, the leader of Aaron, and with him three thousand seven hundred;

**12:29** and Zadok, a young man mighty in valor, and from his father's house twenty-two captains;

**12:30** and of the sons of Benjamin, the brethren of Saul, three thousand; for until then the greater part of them still kept watch over the charge of the house of Saul;

**12:31** and from the sons of Ephraim, twenty thousand eight hundred, mighty in valor, men of renown according to the houses of their fathers;

**12:32** and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, eighteen thousand, who were designated by name to make David king;

**12:33** and from the sons of Issachar, men having understanding of the times, knowing what Israel ought to do, two hundred; and all their brethren were with them;

**12:34** and from Zebulun, those who went forth to battle array with all weapons of war, fifty thousand, to help David not empty-handed;

**12:35** and from Naphtali, one thousand captains, and with them, with shields and spears, thirty-seven thousand;

**12:36** and from the Danites, arrayed for battle, twenty-eight thousand eight hundred;

**12:37** and from Asher, those who went forth to help in battle, forty thousand;

**12:38** and from beyond the Jordan, from Reuben and Gad and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, with all weapons of war, one hundred twenty thousand.

**12:39** All these were men of war, drawing up in battle array with a peaceful soul, and they came to Hebron to make David king over all Israel; and the rest of Israel was also of one soul to make David king.

**12:40** And they were there three days eating and drinking, for their brethren had made preparation.

**12:41** Moreover, those who were near to them, as far as Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, brought to them upon camels and donkeys and mules and oxen provisions—flour, cakes of figs, raisins, wine and oil, and oxen and sheep in abundance—for there was gladness in Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Sokla (spelled Soglam in 12:21) is the Septuagint form corresponding to Ziklag in the Hebrew text.
- **12:4** — In the Greek text, the names that form verse 5 in the Hebrew versification are combined into verse 4.
- **12:18** — The Greek reads "not in truth of hand" and "the God of your fathers," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "though there is no wrong in my hands" and "the God of our fathers."
- **12:20** — "Philistines" translates Greek allophyloi (literally "foreigners"), the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines throughout Chronicles.
- **12:22** — "The Geddur" preserves the Septuagint transliteration of the Hebrew term for a raiding band (ha-gedud) as a proper name.
- **12:5 *(additional)*** — Parallels the closing list of names combined into 12:4 in the site numbering (with minor Greek spelling variants Jeziel and Joazabath).
