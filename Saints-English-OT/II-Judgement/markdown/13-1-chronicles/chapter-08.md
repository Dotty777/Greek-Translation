## Text

**8:1** Now Benjamin fathered Beleleel his firstborn, and Saba the second, Japhael the third,

**8:2** Joa the fourth, and Raphe the fifth.

**8:3** And the sons of Bela were Alei, Gera, and Abihud,

**8:4** and Abisamas, Nooma, and Ahijah,

**8:5** and Gera, Sopharphak, and Hoim.

**8:6** These are the sons of Ehud; these are the heads of the fathers' houses of those who dwelt in Gaber, and they carried them away to Machanathi.

**8:7** And Nooma, Ahijah, and Gera—he, Higaam, fathered Naana and Jachichol.

**8:8** And Saarel fathered children in the plain of Moab after he had sent away Sosin and Ibaada his wife.

**8:9** And by Hada his wife he fathered Jobab, Jebia, Misa, and Melchas,

**8:10** and Idos, Sabia, and Imama. These were heads of the fathers' houses.

**8:11** And by Hosimen he fathered Abitub and Alphaad.

**8:12** And the sons of Alphaad were Obed, Messaam, and Semer; he built Onan and its villages.

**8:13** And Beriga and Sama—these were heads of the fathers' houses of those who dwelt in Ailam, and they drove out the inhabitants of Gath.

**8:14** And his brother Sokel, and Jeremoth,

**8:15** and Azababia, Orer, and Oded,

**8:16** and Michael, Saphan, and Joachan were the sons of Beriga.

**8:17** And Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hazaki, Habar,

**8:18** Samari, Zaria, and Joab were the sons of Elchaad.

**8:19** And Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

**8:20** Elioliaa, Salthi, Elieli,

**8:21** Abijah, Beriga, Beraiah, and Samarath were the sons of Samaith.

**8:22** And Ishpan, Obde, Eliel,

**8:23** Abadon, Zichri, Hanan,

**8:24** Hananiah, Ambri, Elam, Anothaith,

**8:25** Athin, Jepheria, and Pheliel were the sons of Soiek.

**8:26** And Ismasaria, Seraiah, Athaliah,

**8:27** Jasaraia, Seraiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Iraam.

**8:28** These were heads of the fathers' houses, heads according to their generations; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

**8:29** And in Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon, and his wife's name was Molcha.

**8:30** And her firstborn son was Abalon, and Zur, Kish, Baalakaim, Adad,

**8:31** Dur, his brother, Zachur, and Mikloth.

**8:32** And Mikloth fathered Shimeah. And indeed these dwelt opposite their brethren in Jerusalem, with their brethren.

**8:33** And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Amminadab, and Asabal.

**8:34** And the sons of Jonathan: Merib-Baal; and Merib-Baal fathered Michia.

**8:35** And the sons of Michia were Pithon, Melchel, Theree, and Zak.

**8:36** And Zak fathered Jad and Jada; and Jada fathered Salaimath, Salmo, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Maisa.

**8:37** And Maisa fathered Bana; Raphai was his son, Salasath his son, and Esel his son.

**8:38** And Esel had six sons, and these were their names: Ezrikai his firstborn, Ishmael, Seraiah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Esel.

**8:39** And the sons of Asel his brother were Elam his firstborn, Gag the second, and Eliphaleis the third.

**8:40** And the sons of Elam were mighty men of strength, drawing the bow and having many sons and sons' sons, one hundred and fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek text names Benjamin's sons Beleleel (called Bela in v. 3), Saba, Japhael, Joa, and Raphe (v. 2), reflecting the Septuagint forms rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Bela, Ashbel, Aharah, Nohah, Rapha).
- **8:7** — The Greek treats Higaam as a personal name and gives his sons as Naana and Jachichol, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a verb ("he removed them") followed by the names Uzza and Ahihud.
- **8:12** — The Greek text records only Onan and its villages, omitting Lod, which appears alongside Ono in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:38** — Although the Greek states that Esel had six sons, only five names are listed because the second Hebrew name (Bocheru) is translated as "his firstborn."
- **8:39** — The Greek reads Asel for the name of Esel's brother (Hebrew Masoretic Text: Eshek) and names his sons Elam, Gag, and Eliphaleis (Hebrew: Ulam, Jeush, Eliphelet).
