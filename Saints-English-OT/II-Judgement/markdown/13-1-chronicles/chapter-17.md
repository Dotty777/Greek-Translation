## Text

**17:1** Now it came to pass, when David dwelt in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, "Behold, I dwell in a house of cedar, yet the ark of the covenant of the Lord is beneath curtains of skin."

**17:2** And Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your soul, for God is with you."

**17:3** Now it came to pass in that night that the word of the Lord came to Nathan:

**17:4** "Go and say to David My servant, 'Thus says the Lord: You shall not build a house for Me to dwell in.

**17:5** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up Israel to this day,

**17:6** but I have been in a tent and under a covering in all places where I have walked among all Israel. Did I ever speak a word to any one tribe of Israel to shepherd My people, saying, "Why have you not built for Me a house of cedar?"'

**17:7** Now therefore, thus shall you say to My servant David, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: I took you from the sheepfold, from following the flocks, to be ruler over My people Israel,

**17:8** and I have been with you in all places wherever you have gone, and I have utterly destroyed all your enemies from before your face, and I have made for you a name like the name of the great ones who are upon the earth.

**17:9** Moreover, I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, and they shall dwell by themselves and shall be anxious no more; neither shall unrighteousness again humble them as at the beginning,

**17:10** even from the days that I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I have humbled all your enemies, and I will increase you; and the Lord will build you up.

**17:11** And it shall be, when your days are fulfilled and you shall sleep with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come from your own body, and I will prepare his kingdom.

**17:12** He shall build Me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.

**17:13** I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to Me a son; and My mercy I will not withdraw from him, as I withdrew it from those who were before you.

**17:14** And I will confirm him in My house and in his kingdom forever, and his throne shall be established forever.'"

**17:15** According to all these words and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**17:16** Then King David came and sat before the Lord, and said, "Who am I, O Lord God, and what is my house, that You have loved me forever?

**17:17** Yet this was a small thing in Your sight, O God, and You have also spoken concerning the house of Your servant for times afar off; and You have looked upon me as the vision of a man, and have exalted me, O Lord God.

**17:18** What more shall David add to You to glorify You? For You know Your servant,

**17:19** and according to Your own heart You have done all this greatness.

**17:20** O Lord, there is none like You, nor is there any besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**17:21** And there is no other nation upon the earth like Your people Israel, whom God led to redeem for Himself a people, to make for Himself a great and glorious name, to drive out nations from before Your people, whom You redeemed from Egypt.

**17:22** For You have appointed Your people Israel as Your own people forever; and You, O Lord, are God to them.

**17:23** And now, O Lord, let Your word which You have spoken to Your servant and concerning his house be confirmed forever,

**17:24** as men say, 'Lord, Lord Almighty, God of Israel!' And let the house of David Your servant be established before You.

**17:25** For You have opened the ear of Your servant, that You would build him a house; therefore Your servant has found courage to pray before You.

**17:26** And now, O Lord, You Yourself are God, and You have spoken these good things concerning Your servant.

**17:27** And now You have begun to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue forever before You; for You have blessed it, so bless it forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — Greek reads 'any one tribe' (phyle), agreeing with 2 Samuel 7:7, where the Hebrew text of 1 Chronicles reads 'judges.'
- **17:10** — Greek reads 'I will increase you, and the Lord will build you up,' where the Hebrew text reads 'I tell you that the Lord will build you a house.'
- **17:16** — Greek reads 'that You have loved me forever,' whereas the Hebrew text reads 'that You have brought me thus far.'
- **17:23** — In verses 23-24, the Greek text is shorter than the Hebrew, moving directly from 'be confirmed forever' to 'as men say.'
