## Text

**22:1** And David said, "This is the house of the Lord God, and this is the altar for burnt offering for Israel."

**22:2** And David commanded to gather together all the sojourners in the land of Israel; and he appointed stonecutters to hew dressed stones to build a house for God.

**22:3** And David prepared much iron for the nails of the doorways and of the gates and for the hinges, and bronze in abundance; there was no weighing it.

**22:4** And cedar timber, there was no number to it; for the Sidonians and the Tyrians brought cedar timber in abundance to David.

**22:5** And David said, "Solomon my son is a tender young lad, and the house to be built for the Lord is to be of exceeding greatness, for a name and for glory throughout all the earth; I will make preparation for it." So David made preparation in abundance before his death.

**22:6** Then he called Solomon his son, and charged him to build the house for the Lord God of Israel.

**22:7** And David said to Solomon, "Child, it was in my soul to build a house to the name of the Lord God.

**22:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, 'You have shed blood in abundance, and you have made great wars; you shall not build a house to My name, because you have shed much blood upon the earth before Me.

**22:9** Behold, a son is born to you; he shall be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all the enemies round about, for Solomon shall be his name, and I will give peace and quietness upon Israel in his days.

**22:10** He shall build a house to My name, and he shall be to Me a son, and I will be to him a father, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom in Israel forever.'

**22:11** And now, son, the Lord shall be with you, and He shall grant success, and you shall build a house to the Lord your God, as He has spoken concerning you.

**22:12** Only may the Lord give you wisdom and understanding, and strengthen you over Israel, both to keep and to do the law of the Lord your God.

**22:13** Then He will grant success, if you are careful to do the statutes and the judgments which the Lord commanded Moses concerning Israel; be courageous and be strong; do not fear, nor be terrified.

**22:14** And behold, according to my poverty I have prepared for the house of the Lord one hundred thousand talents of gold, and one million talents of silver, and bronze and iron without weight, for it is in abundance; and timber and stones I have prepared, and add to these.

**22:15** And with you add an abundance of those who do work, craftsmen and builders of stone and carpenters of wood, and every wise man in every kind of work,

**22:16** in gold, in silver, in bronze, and in iron, without number. Arise and act, and may the Lord be with you."

**22:17** And David charged the servants, the rulers of Israel, to help Solomon his son,

**22:18** "Is not the Lord with you? And He has given you rest round about, for He has given the inhabitants of the land into your hands, and the land has been subdued before the Lord and before His people.

**22:19** Now set your hearts and your souls to seek the Lord your God, and arise and build a sanctuary to your God, to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord and the holy vessels of God into the house that is to be built to the name of the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:14** — "According to my poverty" translates Greek kata ten ptocheian mou, reflecting the Septuagint's understanding of Hebrew be-onyi ("in my affliction/trouble").
- **22:15** — The Septuagint includes the imperative "add" (prosthes) in this verse, echoing the closing verb of verse 14.
- **22:17** — "The servants, the rulers of Israel" renders Greek tois paisin archousin Israel, likely an early scribal variant for pasin ("all"), as in 20:3.
