## Text

**11:1** And all Israel came to David at Hebron, saying, "Behold, we are your bones and your flesh.

**11:2** And both yesterday and the day before, when Saul was king, you were the one leading out and bringing in Israel. And the Lord of Israel said to you, 'You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be a ruler over Israel.'"

**11:3** And all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the Lord; and they anointed David as king over Israel, according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Samuel.

**11:4** And the king and his men went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus; and there the Jebusites, who dwelt in Jebus,

**11:5** said to David, "You shall not enter here." And he captured the stronghold of Zion, which is the City of David.

**11:6** And David said, "Whoever strikes a Jebusite first shall be chief and commander." And Joab the son of Zeruiah went up against it first, and he became chief.

**11:7** And David dwelt in the stronghold; therefore he called it the City of David.

**11:8** And he built the city all around; and he fought and took the city.

**11:9** And David went on advancing, and the Lord Almighty was with him.

**11:10** And these are the chiefs of the mighty men whom David had, who strengthened themselves with him in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the Lord concerning Israel.

**11:11** And this is the number of the mighty men of David: Jesebada the son of Hachamani, first of the thirty; he drew his sword once against three hundred slain at one time.

**11:12** And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodai, the Archonite; he was among the three mighty men.

**11:13** He was with David at Phasodome, and the foreigners were gathered together there for battle; and there was a portion of the field full of barley, and the people fled from before the foreigners.

**11:14** And he stood in the midst of the portion and delivered it, and he struck down the foreigners; and the Lord brought about a great deliverance.

**11:15** And three of the thirty chiefs went down to the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam, while an encampment of the foreigners was in the Valley of the Giants.

**11:16** And David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the foreigners was then in Bethlehem.

**11:17** And David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the cistern of Bethlehem, which is at the gate?"

**11:18** And the three broke through the encampment of the foreigners, and drew water from the cistern in Bethlehem, which was at the gate, and brought it to David; yet David would not drink it, and poured it out as a drink offering to the Lord,

**11:19** and said, "May God forbid that I should do this thing! Shall I drink the blood of these men at the peril of their lives? For at the peril of their lives they brought it." And he would not drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**11:20** And Abishai the brother of Joab, he was chief of the three; he drew his sword against three hundred slain at one time, and he was renowned among the three.

**11:21** Of the three he was more honorable than the two, and he became their chief; yet he did not attain to the three.

**11:22** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a mighty man, whose deeds were many above Kabzeel—he struck down the two Ariels of Moab, and he went down and struck down the lion in the pit on a day of snow,

**11:23** and he struck down the Egyptian man, a remarkable man five cubits tall; and in the hand of the Egyptian was a spear like a weavers' beam. And Benaiah went down against him with a staff, and snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.

**11:24** These things Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**11:25** He was more honorable than the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three; and David appointed him over his family.

**11:26** And the mighty men of the armies were: Asahel the brother of Joab, Elhanan the son of Dodoe of Bethlehem,

**11:27** Samaoth the Hadite, Chelles the Pelonite,

**11:28** Orai the son of Ektes the Tekoite, Abiezer the Anathothite,

**11:29** Sobochai the Hathite, Eli the Achonite,

**11:30** Neere the Netophathite, Chthaod the son of Nooza the Netophathite,

**11:31** Airei the son of Rebie from the hill of Benjamin, Benaiah the Pirathonite,

**11:32** Hurai from Nachali of Gaash, Abiel the Garabaithite,

**11:33** Azbon the Beerminite, Samaba the Homite;

**11:34** Bennaias the Somologennunite, Jonathan the son of Sola the Harachite,

**11:35** Achim the son of Achar the Hararite, Elphat the son of Sthur,

**11:36** Hophar Mochor, Ahijah the Phedonite,

**11:37** Hesere the Charmadaite, Naarai the son of Azobai,

**11:38** Joel the son of Nathan, Mebaal the son of Hagari,

**11:39** Sele the Ammonite, Nahor the Berthite, the armor-bearer to the son of Zeruiah,

**11:40** Ira the Etherite, Gareobai, Jotheri;

**11:41** Uri the Hittite, Zabet the son of Achaia,

**11:42** Adina the son of Saiza, a chief of Reuben, and with him were thirty,

**11:43** Hanan the son of Moocha, and Jehoshaphat the Baithanite,

**11:44** Uzzia the Ashterathite, Samatha and Jeia, the sons of Kothan the Ararite,

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The Greek reads 'the Lord of Israel said to you' (taking indeclinable Israel as genitive), rather than the Hebrew 'the Lord your God said to you.'
- **11:8** — The Greek reads 'and he fought and took the city,' differing markedly from the Hebrew text, which mentions the Millo and Joab repairing the rest of the city.
- **11:13** — Here and throughout this section (vv. 13-18), the Greek uses allophyloi ('foreigners'), the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines.
- **11:22** — The Greek renders 'from Kabzeel' as 'above Kabzeel' (hyper Kabasael) and leaves the Hebrew title ariel ('lionlike heroes') transliterated as 'Ariels.'
- **11:26** — Throughout verses 11-47, the Greek preserves numerous proper names and gentilics that differ substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (such as Jesebada, Phasodome, Chthaod, Bennaias the Somologennunite, and Sele).
