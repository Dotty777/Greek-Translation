## Text

**24:1** And as for the sons of Aaron by division: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**24:2** And Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no sons; therefore Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron, served as priests.

**24:3** And David divided them, together with Zadok of the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their appointment, according to their ministry, according to their fathers' houses.

**24:4** And the sons of Eleazar were found to be more numerous as chiefs of the mighty men than the sons of Ithamar; and he divided them: for the sons of Eleazar, sixteen chiefs for fathers' houses, and for the sons of Aaron, Ithamar, according to fathers' houses.

**24:5** And he divided them by lot, one with another; for there were leaders of the sanctuary and leaders of the Lord both among the sons of Eleazar and among the sons of Ithamar.

**24:6** And Shemaiah the son of Nethanel, the scribe of Levi, recorded them before the king and the leaders, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the chiefs of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites—one father's house each for Eleazar, and one each for Ithamar.

**24:7** Now the first lot came out for Jarim, the second for Anaideia,

**24:8** the third for Hareb, the fourth for Seorim,

**24:9** the fifth for Malchijah, the sixth for Benjamin,

**24:10** the seventh for Koz, the eighth for Abijah,

**24:11** the ninth for Jeshua, the tenth for Shecaniah,

**24:12** the eleventh for Eliabiei, the twelfth for Jakim,

**24:13** the thirteenth for Ochchopha, the fourteenth for Gelba,

**24:14** the fifteenth for Immer, the sixteenth for Chezin,

**24:15** the seventeenth for Aphese, the eighteenth for Pethahiah,

**24:16** the nineteenth for Ezekiel, the twentieth for Achim,

**24:17** the twenty-first for Gamul, the twenty-second for Adallai,

**24:18** the twenty-third for Maasai.

**24:19** This was their appointment according to their ministry, to enter into the house of the Lord according to their ordinance by the hand of Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded.

**24:20** And as for the remaining sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Jobael and Jadeiah;

**24:21** of Rehabiah, the chief.

**24:22** And of the Izharite, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Inath.

**24:23** The sons of Jedeimou: Amadiah the second, Jase the third, Jokom the fourth.

**24:24** Of Uzziel, Micah; the sons of Micah, Shamir.

**24:25** The brother of Micah, Isshiah; the son of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Ozeia:

**24:27** of Merari, through Ozeia, his sons were Isoam, Zaccur, and Abai.

**24:28** For Mahli: Eleazar and Ithamar; and Eleazar died, and he had no sons.

**24:29** For Kish: the sons of Kish, Jerahmeel.

**24:30** And the sons of Mushi: Mahli, Ela, and Areimoth. These were the sons of the Levites according to their fathers' houses.

**24:31** And they also drew lots, just as their brethren the sons of Aaron, before the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites—the patriarchs of Araab, just as his younger brethren.

## Translator Notes

- **24:4** — The Greek text reads "chiefs of the mighty men" and closes with "for the sons of Aaron, Ithamar, according to fathers' houses," omitting the number eight found in the Hebrew text.
- **24:9** — Greek Benjamin corresponds to Mijamin in the Hebrew text; several other priestly course names in verses 7-18 (including Jarim, Anaideia, Hareb, Eliabiei, Ochchopha, Gelba, Chezin, Aphese, Achim, Adallai, and Maasai) likewise preserve distinct Septuagint forms.
- **24:18** — The Greek text concludes the priestly courses at the twenty-third lot rather than the twenty-fourth, as one course is absent earlier in the list, shifting the numbering from the fourteenth lot onward.
- **24:28** — The Greek text uniquely adds "and Ithamar" as a son of Mahli and states that Eleazar died; verses 20-27 and 30 also preserve condensed clauses and distinct Septuagint forms (including Jobael, Inath, Jedeimou, Jase, Jokom, Ozeia, Isoam, Abai, and Ela).
- **24:31** — Greek Araab reflects a transliteration of the Hebrew word for "head" or "chief," yielding "the patriarchs of Araab" in the Septuagint text.
