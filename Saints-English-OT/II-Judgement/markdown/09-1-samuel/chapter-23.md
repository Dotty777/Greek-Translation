## Text

**23:1** And it was reported to David, saying, "Behold, the foreigners are fighting in Keilah, and they are plundering; they are trampling the threshing floors."

**23:2** Therefore David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Shall I go and strike down these foreigners?" And the Lord said, "Go, and you shall strike among these foreigners, and you shall strike Keilah."

**23:3** And David's men said to him, "Behold, we are afraid here in Judea; and how will it be if we go to Keilah? Shall we enter into the spoils of the foreigners?"

**23:4** Then David inquired once more through the Lord. And the Lord answered him and said to him, "Arise and go down to Keilah, for I am delivering the foreigners into your hands."

**23:5** So David and the men who were with him went to Keilah and fought among the foreigners; and they fled from before him, and he drove away their livestock, and struck them with a great slaughter. Thus David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.

**23:6** Now it came to pass, when Abiathar the son of Abimelech fled to David, that he also went down with David to Keilah, having an ephod in his hand.

**23:7** And it was reported to Saul that David had come to Keilah; and Saul said, "God has sold him into my hands, for he has shut himself in by entering a city of doors and bars."

**23:8** So Saul commanded all the people for war, to go down to Keilah to besiege David and his men.

**23:9** And David knew that Saul was not keeping silent about the evil concerning him; and David said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring near the ephod of the Lord."

**23:10** And David said, "O Lord God of Israel, Your servant has surely heard that Saul seeks to come against Keilah to destroy the city because of me.

**23:11** Will it be shut up? And now, will Saul come down as Your servant has heard? O Lord God of Israel, declare it to Your servant." And the Lord said, "It shall be shut up."

**23:13** Then David and the men who were with him, about four hundred, arose and went out from Keilah, and went wherever they could go. And it was reported to Saul that David had escaped safely from Keilah, so he refrained from going out.

**23:14** And he dwelt in the wilderness in Maserem, in the narrow passes, and remained in the wilderness in the hill country of Ziph, in the parched land. And Saul sought him all the days, yet the Lord did not deliver him into his hands.

**23:15** And David saw that Saul had come out to seek David; and David was in the parched mountain, in Kaine of Ziph.

**23:16** Then Jonathan, the son of Saul, arose and went to David in Kaine, and strengthened his hands in the Lord.

**23:17** And he said to him, "Do not fear, for the hand of Saul my father shall by no means find you. You shall be king over Israel, and I shall be second to you; and Saul my father knows it is so."

**23:18** So the two of them made a covenant before the Lord. And David remained in Kaine, while Jonathan departed to his own house.

**23:19** Then the Ziphites went up from the parched land to Saul on the hill, saying, "Behold, is David not hidden among us in Nessara, in the narrow passes, in Kaine, on the hill of Hachilah, which is on the right of Jeshimon?

**23:20** And now, according to all the desire of the king's soul to come down, let him come down to us; they have shut him up into the hands of the king."

**23:21** And Saul said to them, "Blessed are you by the Lord, because you have grieved for me.

**23:22** Go now and prepare further, and learn his place where his foot will swiftly be, there where you spoke of, lest he act craftily.

**23:23** See therefore and learn, and we shall go with you; and it shall be, if he is in the land, that I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah."

**23:24** So the Ziphites arose and went ahead of Saul. Now David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, toward the west, on the right of Jeshimon.

**23:25** And Saul and his men went to seek him. And they reported it to David, so he went down to the rock that is in the wilderness of Maon. And Saul heard of it, and pursued after David into the wilderness of Maon.

**23:26** And Saul and his men went on one side of the mountain, and David and his men were on the other side of the mountain. And David was taking cover to get away from the presence of Saul, while Saul and his men encamped against David and his men to seize them.

**23:27** But a messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hasten and come, for the foreigners have fallen upon the land!"

**23:28** Therefore Saul turned back from pursuing after David, and went to meet the foreigners; because of this, that place was called the Divided Rock.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — This Greek text reads "and you shall strike Keilah" (likely by assimilation to the preceding verb), whereas the Hebrew text reads "and save Keilah."
- **23:11** — This Greek text compresses verses 11 and 12 (omitting verse 12) and uses the verb apokleio ("shut up" or "deliver up").
- **23:13** — The Greek text reads "about four hundred" men with David, whereas the Hebrew text reads "about six hundred."
- **23:15** — Greek Kaine (literally "New," also in verses 16, 18, and 19) reflects reading Hebrew Horesh as hadashah ("new").
- **23:26** — Greek describes David as "taking cover" while Saul and his men "encamped against" him, where the Hebrew text reads "hurrying" and "closing in on."
