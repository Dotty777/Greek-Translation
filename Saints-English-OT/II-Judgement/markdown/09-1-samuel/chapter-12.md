## Text

**12:1** And Samuel said to all Israel, "Behold, I have listened to your voice in all things that you have said to me, and I have set a king to reign over you.

**12:2** And now, behold, the king goes about before you, while I have grown old and shall sit; and behold, my sons are among you. And behold, I have walked before you from youth even to this day.

**12:3** Behold, here I am; testify against me before the Lord and before His anointed. Whose calf have I taken, or whose donkey have I taken, or whom of you have I oppressed, or whom have I crushed, or from whose hand have I taken a bribe and a sandal? Testify against me, and I will restore it to you."

**12:4** And they said to Samuel, "You have not wronged us, nor have you oppressed or crushed us, and you have not taken anything from anyone's hand."

**12:5** And Samuel said to the people, "The Lord is witness among you, and His anointed is witness today, on this day, that you have not found anything in my hand." And they said, "He is witness."

**12:6** And Samuel spoke to the people, saying, "The Lord is witness, who made Moses and Aaron, who brought our fathers up out of Egypt.

**12:7** And now, take your stand, and I will judge you before the Lord, and I will declare to you all the righteousness of the Lord—the deeds He performed among you and among your fathers:

**12:8** how Jacob and his sons entered into Egypt, and Egypt humbled them; and our fathers cried out to the Lord, and the Lord sent Moses and Aaron, and they brought our fathers out of Egypt, and He settled them in this place.

**12:9** And they forgot the Lord their God, and He sold them into the hands of Sisera, commander of the army of Jabis king of Hazor, and into the hands of foreigners, and into the hands of the king of Moab, and he waged war against them.

**12:10** And they cried out to the Lord and said, 'We have sinned, because we have forsaken the Lord and have served the Baals and the groves; and now deliver us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve You.'

**12:11** And He sent Jeroboam and Barak and Jephthah and Samuel, and He delivered you from the hand of your enemies all around, and you dwelt in safety.

**12:12** And you saw that Nahash, king of the sons of Ammon, came against you, and you said, 'No, rather a king shall reign over us.'

**12:13** And now, behold the king whom you have chosen; and behold, the Lord has given a king over you.

**12:14** If you fear the Lord and serve Him, and listen to His voice, and do not contend against the mouth of the Lord, then both you and the king who reigns over you shall continue walking after the Lord.

**12:15** But if you do not listen to the voice of the Lord, and contend against the mouth of the Lord, then the hand of the Lord shall be against you and against your king.

**12:16** And now, take your stand and see this great thing which the Lord will do before your eyes.

**12:17** Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call upon the Lord, and He will give thunder and rain; and know and see that your wickedness is great, which you have done before the Lord in asking for a king for yourselves."

**12:18** And Samuel called upon the Lord, and the Lord gave thunder and rain on that day; and all the people greatly feared the Lord and Samuel.

**12:19** And all the people said to Samuel, "Pray for your servants to the Lord your God, that we may not die; for we have added wickedness to all our sins, in asking for a king for ourselves."

**12:20** And Samuel said to the people, "Do not fear. You have done all this wickedness; yet do not turn aside from following the Lord, but serve the Lord with all your heart.

**12:21** And do not go astray after things that are nothing, which will accomplish nothing and will not deliver, for they are nothing.

**12:22** For the Lord will not cast off His people for the sake of His great name, because the Lord has graciously taken you to Himself as a people.

**12:23** And as for me, far be it from me that I should sin against the Lord by ceasing to pray for you; and I will serve the Lord, and I will show you the good and straight way.

**12:24** Only fear the Lord and serve Him in truth and with all your heart, for you have seen what great things He has done with you.

**12:25** And if in wickedness you persist in doing evil, both you and your king shall be gathered away."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Greek kathesomai literally means "I shall sit," contrasting Samuel's retirement with the king going about before the people (where the Hebrew text reads "I am gray-headed").
- **12:3** — The Greek text reads "a bribe [lit. propitiation] and a sandal" (exilasma kai hypodema), a distinctive Septuagint reading echoing Amos 2:6 and Sirach 46:19 where the Hebrew speaks of a ransom to blind the eyes.
- **12:9** — The Greek names the king of Hazor as Jabis (Iabeis, corresponding to Jabin) and renders the Philistines here and throughout this book as "foreigners" (allophyloi, literally "people of another tribe").
- **12:11** — The Septuagint lists Jeroboam (where the Hebrew reads Jerubbaal, i.e., Gideon) and Barak (where the Hebrew reads Bedan).
- **12:25** — Greek prostethesesthe literally means "you shall be added," carrying the biblical idiom of being gathered away in death (reflecting the Hebrew root for adding rather than sweeping away).
