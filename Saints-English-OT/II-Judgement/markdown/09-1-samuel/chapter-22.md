## Text

**22:1** So David departed from there and escaped safely, and came to the cave of Adullam; and his brethren and the house of his father heard of it, and they went down to him there.

**22:2** And everyone who was in distress, and everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was deeply grieved in soul gathered to him; and he became a leader over them. And there were with him about four hundred men.

**22:3** Then David departed from there to Mizpah of Moab; and he said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother stay with you, until I know what God will do for me."

**22:4** And he pleaded before the face of the king of Moab, and they dwelt with him all the days that David was in the stronghold.

**22:5** Now Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not dwell in the stronghold; depart, and you shall come into the land of Judah." So David departed, and came and dwelt in the city of Sarik.

**22:6** And Saul heard that David and the men who were with him had been discovered. Now Saul was sitting on the hill below the field that is in Bamah, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing beside him.

**22:7** And Saul spoke to his servants who were standing beside him, and said to them, "Hear now, O sons of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse truly give all of you fields and vineyards, and appoint all of you captains of hundreds and captains of thousands?

**22:8** For all of you have conspired against me, and there is no one who uncovers my ear when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse; and there is none among you who grieves for me and uncovers my ear, that my son has stirred up my servant against me as an enemy, as it is this day."

**22:9** Then Doeg the Syrian, who was set over the mules of Saul, answered and said, "I have seen the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Abimelech the son of Ahitub, the priest;

**22:10** and he inquired of God for him, and gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the foreigner."

**22:11** So the king sent to summon Abimelech the son of Ahitub, and all the sons of his father, the priests who were in Nob; and all of them came to the king.

**22:12** And Saul said, "Hear now, son of Ahitub!" And he said, "Behold, here I am; speak, lord."

**22:13** And Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you have given him bread and a sword, and have inquired of God for him, to set him against me as an enemy, as it is this day?"

**22:14** And he answered the king and said, "And who among all your servants is as faithful as David, who is also the king's son-in-law, and commander over all your bidding, and honored in your house?

**22:15** Have I only today begun to inquire of God for him? Far be it! May the king not lay any charge against his servant, nor against all the house of his father; for your servant knew nothing of all these things, whether a small matter or a great."

**22:16** And King Saul said, "You shall surely die, Abimelech, you and all the house of your father."

**22:17** Then the king said to the runners who stood beside him, "Draw near and put to death the priests of the Lord, because their hand is also with David, and because they knew that he was fleeing and did not uncover my ear." Yet the servants of the king would not lift their hands to sin against the priests of the Lord.

**22:18** And the king said to Doeg, "Turn and fall upon the priests." So Doeg the Syrian turned and put to death the priests of the Lord on that day, three hundred and five men, all bearing an ephod.

**22:19** And he struck Nob, the city of the priests, with the edge of the sword, from man to woman, from infant to nursing child, and calf and donkey and sheep.

**22:20** Now one son of Abimelech the son of Ahitub escaped safely, and his name was Abiathar; and he fled after David.

**22:21** And Abiathar reported to David that Saul had put to death all the priests of the Lord.

**22:22** And David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day that Doeg the Syrian would surely report it to Saul; I am responsible for the lives of the house of your father.

**22:23** Remain with me; do not be afraid; for wherever I seek a place for my own life, I will seek one for your life also, for you are safeguarded with me."

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — Greek reads "in the city of Sarik," whereas the Hebrew text reads "in the forest of Hereth."
- **22:6** — Greek reads "below the field that is in Bamah," whereas the Hebrew text reads "under the tamarisk tree in Ramah."
- **22:18** — The Greek text numbers the slain priests at three hundred and five men, whereas the Hebrew text reads eighty-five.
- **22:23** — Greek reads "wherever I seek a place for my own life, I will seek one for your life also," whereas the Hebrew text reads "he who seeks my life seeks your life."
