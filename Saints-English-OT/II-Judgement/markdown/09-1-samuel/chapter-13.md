## Text

**13:2** And Saul chose for himself three thousand men from the men of Israel; and two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in Mount Bethel, and one thousand were with Jonathan in Geba of Benjamin; and the rest of the people he sent away, each to his own tent.

**13:3** And Jonathan struck down Nasib the foreigner who was on the hill, and the foreigners heard of it. And Saul sounded the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, "The slaves have rebelled!"

**13:4** And all Israel heard people saying, "Saul has struck down Nasib the foreigner," and Israel was put to shame among the foreigners; and the sons of Israel went up after Saul at Gilgal.

**13:5** And the foreigners assembled for war against Israel, and there came up against Israel thirty thousand chariots and six thousand horsemen, and people like the sand beside the sea in multitude; and they came up and encamped in Michmash, opposite Beth Horon to the rear.

**13:6** And the men of Israel saw that they were in distress, so that they could not advance; and the people hid themselves in the caves, and in the enclosures, and in the rocks, and in the pits, and in the cisterns.

**13:7** And those who crossed over crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead. As for Saul, he was still in Gilgal, and all the people were dismayed behind him.

**13:8** And he waited seven days according to the testimony, as Samuel had said; yet Samuel did not arrive at Gilgal, and his people were scattered from him.

**13:9** And Saul said, "Draw near, that I may offer a burnt offering and peace offerings." And he offered up the burnt offering.

**13:10** And it came to pass, as he finished offering up the burnt offering, that Samuel arrived; and Saul went out to meet him, to bless him.

**13:11** And Samuel said, "What have you done?" And Saul said, "Because I saw how the people were scattered from me, and you did not arrive as you had appointed in the testimony of the days, and the foreigners were gathered together at Michmash,

**13:12** and I said, 'Now the foreigners will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not implored the face of the Lord.' So I compelled myself and offered up the burnt offering."

**13:13** And Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly, because you have not kept my commandment which the Lord commanded you; for now the Lord would have established your kingdom forever over Israel.

**13:14** And now your kingdom shall not stand; and the Lord will seek for Himself a man after His own heart, and the Lord will appoint him as ruler over His people, because you have not kept what the Lord commanded you."

**13:15** And Samuel arose and departed from Gilgal on his way; and the remnant of the people went up after Saul to meet the men of war. When they arrived from Gilgal at Gibeah of Benjamin, Saul numbered the people who were found with him, about six hundred men.

**13:16** And Saul and Jonathan his son, and the people and those who were found with them, sat in Geba of Benjamin and wept; and the foreigners had encamped in Michmash.

**13:17** And a destroyer went out from the field of the foreigners in three companies: one company facing the way of Ophrah toward Shual,

**13:18** and one company facing the way of Beth Horon, and one company facing the way of Geba that overlooks Gai of Samein.

**13:19** And no craftsman in iron was found in all the land of Israel, for the foreigners said, "Lest the Hebrews make a sword and a spear."

**13:20** And all Israel went down to the land of the foreigners to forge each man his scythe and tool, and each man his ax and his sickle.

**13:21** And the vintage was ready for harvesting; and the tools were three shekels for a tooth, and for the ax and for the sickle the standing rate was the same.

**13:22** And it came to pass in the days of the battle of Michmash, that neither sword nor spear was found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and with Jonathan; yet they were found with Saul and with Jonathan his son.

**13:23** And some from the garrison of the foreigners went out to the crossing of Michmash; and he did not tell his father.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew word for "garrison/prefect" as a personal name, Nasib, translates Geba as "the hill," and reads Saul's proclamation as "The slaves have rebelled!" (where the Hebrew reads "Let the Hebrews hear!").
- **13:15** — The Septuagint preserves the full narrative of Samuel departing from Gilgal and the remnant following Saul to Gibeah of Benjamin, a passage omitted by scribal error in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:18** — "Gai of Samein" transliterates the Septuagint's distinctive place name Gai ten Samein (corresponding to the Valley of Zeboim in the Hebrew text).
- **13:21** — In this difficult verse, the Septuagint speaks of the vintage being ready for harvest and gives a fixed sharpening rate of three shekels per tooth (or prong), with the same standing rate for the ax and the sickle.
- **13:23** — The closing clause, "and he did not tell his father," stands at the end of 13:23 in the Septuagint (anticipating Jonathan's action in 14:1, where the clause appears in the Hebrew text).
