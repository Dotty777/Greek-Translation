## Text

**7:1** And the men of Kiriath-jearim came and brought up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and brought it into the house of Amminadab on the hill; and they sanctified Eleazar his son to keep the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**7:2** And it came to pass from the day that the ark was in Kiriath-jearim that the days multiplied and became twenty years; and all the house of Israel looked after the Lord.

**7:3** Then Samuel spoke to all the house of Israel, saying, "If you are returning to the Lord with all your heart, put away the foreign gods from among you, and the groves, and prepare your hearts for the Lord, and serve Him alone; and He will deliver you from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:4** So the children of Israel put away the Baals and the groves of Ashtaroth, and served the Lord alone.

**7:5** And Samuel said, "Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray to the Lord for you."

**7:6** And they gathered together to Mizpah, and drew water and poured it out before the Lord upon the ground. And they fasted on that day, and said, "We have sinned before the Lord." And Samuel judged the children of Israel at Mizpah.

**7:7** Now when the Philistines heard that all the children of Israel had gathered together to Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. And the children of Israel heard of it, and were afraid before the Philistines.

**7:8** And the children of Israel said to Samuel, "Do not remain silent on our behalf from crying out to the Lord your God, and He will save us from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:9** And Samuel took one suckling lamb and offered it as a burnt offering to the Lord with all the people; and Samuel cried out to the Lord for Israel, and the Lord heard him.

**7:10** Now as Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to battle against Israel; and the Lord thundered with a loud voice on that day against the Philistines, and they were thrown into confusion and defeated before Israel.

**7:11** And the men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines, and struck them down as far as below Beth-car.

**7:12** Then Samuel took one stone and set it up between Mizpah and the Old, and called its name Ebenezer, Stone of the Helper, and said, "Thus far the Lord has helped us."

**7:13** And the Lord humbled the Philistines, and they did not come anymore into the territory of Israel; and the hand of the Lord was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

**7:14** And the cities which the Philistines had taken from the children of Israel were restored, and they restored them to Israel from Ashkelon to Azob, and they took back the territory of Israel from the hand of the Philistines. And there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**7:15** And Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

**7:16** He went from year to year on a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and judged Israel in all these sanctified places.

**7:17** And his return was to Ramathaim, for his house was there; and there he judged Israel, and there he built an altar to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Septuagint reads "Amminadab" (Ameinadab) where the Masoretic Text has "Abinadab."
- **7:9** — The Septuagint reads "with all the people" (syn panti to lao) where the Masoretic Text has "wholly" or "as a whole burnt offering."
- **7:12** — Where the Masoretic Text names the place Shen, the Septuagint translates "the Old" (tes palaias), and gives Ebenezer both as a transliteration and in translation ("Stone of the Helper").
- **7:14** — The Septuagint reads "from Ashkelon to Azob" where the Masoretic Text has "from Ekron to Gath," and in v. 16 describes the circuit sites as "sanctified places."
