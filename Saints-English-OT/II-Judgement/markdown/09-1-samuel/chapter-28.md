## Text

**28:1** Now it came to pass in those days that the Philistines gathered together in their camps to go out to fight against Israel; and Achish said to David, "Know for certain that you shall go out with me to war, you and your men."

**28:2** And David said to Achish, "So now you shall know what your servant will do." And Achish said to David, "Therefore I will make you chief of my bodyguard all the days."

**28:3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had lamented for him and buried him in Armathaim, in his own city. And Saul had removed the mediums and the diviners from the land.

**28:4** Then the Philistines gathered together, and came and encamped at Soman; and Saul gathered together every man of Israel, and they encamped at Gilboa.

**28:5** And Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, and he was afraid, and his heart was exceedingly dismayed.

**28:6** And Saul inquired through the Lord, and the Lord did not answer him, either by dreams, or by the manifestations, or by the prophets.

**28:7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Seek for me a woman who is a medium, and I will go to her and inquire through her." And his servants said to him, "Behold, there is a woman who is a medium in Aeldor."

**28:8** So Saul disguised himself and put on other garments, and went, he and two men with him; and they came to the woman by night, and he said to her, "Please divine for me by the familiar spirit, and bring up for me whomever I shall tell you."

**28:9** And the woman said to him, "Behold now, you know all that Saul has done, how he has utterly destroyed the mediums and the diviners from the land. Why then do you lay a snare for my life, to put it to death?"

**28:10** And Saul swore to her, saying, "As the Lord lives, no guilt shall come upon you in this matter."

**28:11** Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" And he said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

**28:12** And the woman saw Samuel and cried out with a loud voice; and the woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul!"

**28:13** And the king said to her, "Do not be afraid; tell me whom you have seen." And she said to him, "I have seen gods ascending out of the earth."

**28:14** And he said to her, "What did you perceive?" And she said to him, "An upright man ascending out of the earth, and he is wrapped in a double cloak." And Saul knew that this was Samuel, and he stooped with his face to the ground and bowed down to him.

**28:15** Then Samuel said, "Why have you troubled me, that I should come up?" And Saul said, "I am exceedingly distressed, for the Philistines are waging war against me, and God has departed from me and no longer listens to me, either by the hand of the prophets or by dreams; and now I have called you, to make known to me what I shall do."

**28:16** And Samuel said, "Why do you inquire of me, seeing that the Lord has departed from you and is with your neighbor?

**28:17** And the Lord has done to you just as the Lord spoke by my hand; and the Lord will tear your kingdom out of your hand and give it to your neighbor, to David.

**28:18** Because you did not obey the voice of the Lord nor execute the fierceness of His wrath upon Amalek, therefore the Lord has done this thing to you on this day.

**28:19** And the Lord will deliver Israel with you into the hands of the Philistines; and tomorrow you and your sons with you shall fall, and the Lord will give the camp of Israel into the hands of the Philistines."

**28:20** Then Saul immediately fell at his full height upon the ground, and was exceedingly afraid because of the words of Samuel; and there was no strength left in him, for he had not eaten bread all that day and all that night.

**28:21** And the woman came in to Saul and saw that he was exceedingly agitated; and she said to him, "Behold now, your maidservant has obeyed your voice, and I have placed my life in my hand, and have heeded the words which you spoke to me.

**28:22** Now therefore, please listen to the voice of your maidservant, and I will set before you a morsel of bread, and eat, that there may be strength in you, for you shall go on your way."

**28:23** But he refused to eat. Yet his servants and the woman strongly urged him, and he listened to their voice; so he arose from the ground and sat upon the seat.

**28:24** Now the woman had a pasture-fed heifer in the house; and she hastened and slaughtered it, and she took flour and kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread,

**28:25** and she brought it before Saul and before his servants; and they ate, and they arose and departed that night.

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — Greek reads "Armathaim" (corresponding to Hebrew Ramah); in v. 4, "Soman" corresponds to Shunem, and in v. 7, "Aeldor" corresponds to En Dor.
- **28:6** — Greek delois ("manifestations" or "revelations") is the Septuagint's regular rendering for the Hebrew priestly Urim.
- **28:14** — Greek reads "What did you perceive?" and "An upright man," whereas the Hebrew reads "What is his form?" and "An old man."
- **28:16** — Greek reads "and is with your neighbor" (referring to David, as in v. 17), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and has become your adversary."
- **28:19** — Greek reads "tomorrow you and your sons with you shall fall," whereas the Hebrew reads "tomorrow you and your sons shall be with me."
