## Text

**27:1** And David spoke in his heart, saying, "Now one day I shall be delivered into the hands of Saul; and there is nothing good for me unless I escape safely into the land of the Philistines, and Saul ceases from seeking me within every border of Israel, and I shall be saved out of his hand."

**27:2** So David arose, and the four hundred men who were with him, and went to Achish the son of Ammach, king of Gath.

**27:3** And David dwelt with Achish, he and his men, each man and his household, even David and both his wives, Ahinoam the Israelitess and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**27:4** And it was reported to Saul that David had fled to Gath; so he did not continue to seek him any longer.

**27:5** Then David said to Achish, "If now your servant has found favor in your eyes, let them give me a place in one of the cities of the countryside, and I will dwell there; for why does your servant dwell in a royal city with you?"

**27:6** So he gave him Ziklag on that day; therefore Ziklag has belonged to the king of Judea to this day.

**27:7** And the number of the days that David dwelt along the way of the Philistines was four months.

**27:8** And David and his men went up and raided all the Geshurites and the Amalekites; and behold, the land was inhabited from the bordering fortified places—that region from Gelampsur—even as far as Egypt.

**27:9** And he struck the land, and left neither man nor woman alive; and he took flocks and herds, and donkeys and camels, and apparel, and they returned and came to Achish.

**27:10** Then Achish said to David, "Against whom have you raided today?" And David said to Achish, "Against the south of Judea, and against the south of Jesmega, and against the south of the Kenezite."

**27:11** And neither man nor woman did I leave alive to bring into Gath, saying, "Lest they report in Gath against us, saying, 'Thus David does.'" And this was his custom all the days that David dwelt in the country of the Philistines.

**27:12** And David was greatly trusted by Achish, who said, "He has been utterly put to shame among his people, in Israel; therefore he shall be my servant forever."

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — Throughout chapters 27–28, "Philistines" renders Greek allophyloi (literally "foreigners"), the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines in 1 Kingdoms.
- **27:2** — Greek reads "four hundred men" (Hebrew "six hundred") and "Ammach" (Hebrew "Maoch"); in v. 3, Ahinoam is called "the Israelitess" rather than "the Jezreelitess."
- **27:7** — Greek reads "along the way of the Philistines" for "four months," whereas the Hebrew reads "in the country of the Philistines" for "a year [lit. days] and four months."
- **27:8** — The Greek phrase rendered "from the bordering fortified places—that region from Gelampsur" is syntactically difficult and preserves a conflated place name (Gelampsur) where the Hebrew speaks of ancient inhabitants toward Shur; Greek also omits the Girzites.
- **27:10** — Greek reads "Jesmega" and "the Kenezite" (Hebrew "the Jerahmeelites" and "the Kenites"); in v. 11, the Greek verb shifts abruptly into the first-person singular ("did I leave alive").
