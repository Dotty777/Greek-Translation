## Text

**21:1** And David arose and departed, and Jonathan went into the city.

**21:2** And David came to Nob, to Abimelech the priest; and Abimelech was alarmed at meeting him, and said to him, "Why is it that you are alone, and no one is with you?"

**21:3** And David said to the priest, "The king has commanded me a matter today, and said to me, 'Let no one know the matter concerning which I am sending you, and about which I have commanded you.' And I have solemnly instructed the young men at the place called Faithfulness of God, Phellani Maemoni.

**21:4** And now, are there five loaves of bread under your hand? Give into my hand whatever is found."

**21:5** And the priest answered David and said, "There are no common loaves under my hand, but only holy loaves; if the young men have kept themselves from women, then they shall eat."

**21:6** And David answered the priest and said to him, "Indeed, we have abstained from women since yesterday and the third day; when I went out on the journey, all the young men were purified. And is the journey itself common? For it shall be sanctified today because of my vessels."

**21:7** So Abimelech the priest gave him the loaves of the presentation; for there were no loaves there except the loaves of the Presence, which had been taken away from the presence of the Lord so that warm bread might be set out on the day he took them.

**21:8** Now one of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained—Neessaran—before the Lord; and his name was Doeg the Syrian, tending the mules of Saul.

**21:9** And David said to Abimelech, "See if there is here under your hand a spear or a sword; for I have not taken my sword and weapons in my hand, because the king's matter was urgent."

**21:10** And the priest said, "Behold, the sword of Goliath the foreigner, whom you struck down in the valley of Elah—and it was wrapped in a garment. If you will take this one, take it for yourself, for there is no other besides this one here." And David said, "Behold, there is none like it; give it to me."

**21:11** And he gave it to him. Then David arose and fled on that day from the presence of Saul; and David came to Achish, king of Gath.

**21:12** And the servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? Was it not for this man that the dancing women led the song, saying, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

**21:13** And David laid the words in his heart, and was exceedingly afraid before Achish, king of Gath.

**21:14** So he altered his countenance before him and pretended on that day; and he drummed upon the doors of the city, and staggered about in his hands, and fell against the doors of the gate, and his saliva ran down upon his beard.

**21:15** Then Achish said to his servants, "Behold, you see a man seized with madness! Why have you brought him in to me?

**21:16** Do I lack madmen, that you have brought him in to act madly before me? This man shall not enter into the house."

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Greek text reads Abimelech (corresponding to Hebrew Ahimelech) and Nomba (for Nob) throughout these chapters.
- **21:3** — Greek renders the Hebrew idiom for an unnamed place doubly, translating it as "Faithfulness of God" and transliterating it as the place name Phellani Maemoni.
- **21:8** — Greek doubly renders the Hebrew word for "detained" with a Greek participle and the transliteration Neessaran, and reads "Doeg the Syrian, tending the mules of Saul" where the Hebrew reads "Doeg the Edomite, chief of Saul's herdsmen."
- **21:10** — Throughout 1 Kingdoms, the Septuagint translates the Hebrew term for Philistines as allophyloi ("foreigners"). Here the Greek also does not include "behind the ephod."
