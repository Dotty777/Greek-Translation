## Text

**29:1** Now the Philistines gathered together all their armies to Aphek, and Israel encamped in Aeddon, which is in Israel.

**29:2** And the lords of the Philistines marched past by hundreds and by thousands, while David and his men marched past in the rear with Achish.

**29:3** Then the lords of the Philistines said, "Who are these who are passing through?" And Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel? He has been with us for days—this is the second year—and I have found nothing in him from the day he defected to me even to this day."

**29:4** But the commanders of the Philistines were displeased with him, and they said to him, "Send the man back to his place where you stationed him, and let him not go with us to the battle, and let him not become a plotter against the camp. For with what would this man reconcile himself to his lord? Would it not be with the heads of those men?

**29:5** Is this not David, of whom they led the song in dances, saying, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

**29:6** Then Achish called David and said to him, "As the Lord lives, surely you are upright and good in my eyes, as are your going out and your coming in with me in the camp; for I have found no evil against you from the day you came to me until this present day, and in the eyes of the lords you are good.

**29:7** Now therefore, turn back and go in peace, and you shall do no evil in the eyes of the lords of the Philistines."

**29:8** And David said to Achish, "What have I done to you, and what have you found in your servant from the day I have been before you even to this day, that I should not go to fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

**29:9** And Achish answered David, "I know that you are good in my eyes; yet the lords of the Philistines say, 'He shall not go with us to battle.'

**29:10** Now therefore, rise early in the morning, you and the servants of your lord who have come with you, and go to the place where I stationed you; and do not set a wicked word in your heart, for you are good before me. Rise early on the way, and as soon as it is light for you, depart."

**29:11** So David rose early, he and his men, to depart and to guard the land of the Philistines; and the Philistines went up to make war against Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Greek text reads 'Aeddon, which is in Israel,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the spring which is in Jezreel.' Throughout 1 Kingdoms, the Greek renders 'Philistines' with a term literally meaning 'foreigners.'
- **29:3** — The Greek reads 'these who are passing through' (interpreting the Hebrew word for 'Hebrews' as a participle meaning 'passing over') and specifies 'this is the second year' for David's stay.
- **29:6** — The Greek text lacks the negative found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('you are not good in the eyes of the lords'), reading positively 'in the eyes of the lords you are good.'
- **29:11** — The Greek reads 'to guard the land of the Philistines' and 'to make war against Israel,' whereas the Hebrew reads 'to return to the land of the Philistines' and 'went up to Jezreel.'
