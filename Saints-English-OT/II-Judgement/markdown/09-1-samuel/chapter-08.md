## Text

**8:1** Now it came to pass when Samuel grew old that he appointed his sons as judges for Israel.

**8:2** And these were the names of his sons: the firstborn, Joel, and the name of the second, Abijah; they were judges in Beersheba.

**8:3** Yet his sons did not walk in his way, but turned aside after profit, and took bribes, and perverted justice.

**8:4** Then the men of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramathaim,

**8:5** and said to him, "Behold, you have grown old, and your sons do not walk in your way. Now therefore, appoint for us a king to judge us like the rest of the nations."

**8:6** And the matter was evil in the eyes of Samuel when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." So Samuel prayed to the Lord.

**8:7** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Heed the voice of the people in whatever they speak to you; for they have not rejected you, but they have rejected Me, that I should not reign over them.

**8:8** According to all the deeds which they have done to Me from the day I brought them up out of Egypt until this day, in that they forsook Me and served other gods, so they are doing to you also.

**8:9** Now therefore, heed their voice; yet you shall solemnly testify to them, and declare to them the right of the king who shall reign over them."

**8:10** So Samuel spoke every word of the Lord to the people who were asking a king from him.

**8:11** And he said, "This shall be the right of the king who shall reign over you: he will take your sons and appoint them for his chariots and for his horsemen, and some to run before his chariots;

**8:12** and he will appoint them for himself as commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and to reap his harvest and gather his vintage, and to make his instruments of war and equipment for his chariots.

**8:13** And he will take your daughters to be perfumers, and cooks, and bakers.

**8:14** And he will take your fields, your vineyards, and your olive groves that are good, and give them to his servants.

**8:15** And he will take a tenth of your seed and your vineyards, and give it to his eunuchs and to his servants.

**8:16** And he will take your male servants, your female servants, your herds and your good things, and your donkeys, and take a tenth for his own work.

**8:17** And he will take a tenth of your flocks; and you yourselves shall be his servants.

**8:18** And you will cry out in that day because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves; and the Lord will not hear you in those days, because you yourselves have chosen a king for yourselves."

**8:19** Nevertheless the people refused to listen to Samuel; and they said to him, "No, but a king shall be over us,

**8:20** that we also may be like all the nations, and our king shall judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battle."

**8:21** And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and spoke them in the ears of the Lord.

**8:22** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Heed their voice, and make them a king." And Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Let every man return to his own city."

## Translator Notes

- **8:4** — The Septuagint reads "the men of Israel" rather than "all the elders of Israel," and uses the dual form Ramathaim (Harmathaim, as in 7:17) for Samuel's hometown of Ramah.
- **8:12** — The Septuagint specifies "commanders of hundreds" (rather than fifties) and "to reap his harvest and gather his vintage" (rather than plowing and reaping).
- **8:16** — The Septuagint reads "your herds and your good things" where the Masoretic Text has "your finest young men," and states that the king will "take a tenth" for his work.
