## Text

**9:1** Now there was a man of the sons of Benjamin, and his name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Ared, the son of Bachi, the son of Aphek, the son of a Jeminite man, a mighty man.

**9:2** And he had a son, and his name was Saul, a man of great stature, a goodly man; and among the sons of Israel there was no one more goodly than he—from the shoulders and upward he was taller than all the land.

**9:3** Now the donkeys of Kish, the father of Saul, were lost; and Kish said to Saul his son, "Take one of the young servants with you, and arise, go, and seek the donkeys."

**9:4** So they passed through the hill country of Ephraim and passed through the land of Selcha, and they did not find them; then they passed through the land of Easakem, and they were not there; and they passed through the land of Jakim, and they did not find them.

**9:5** When they came to Seiph, Saul said to his young servant who was with him, "Come, and let us return, lest my father cease caring about the donkeys and become anxious for us."

**9:6** And the young servant said to him, "Behold now, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is held in honor; everything that he speaks will surely come to pass. Now therefore let us go, so that he may tell us our way on which we have gone."

**9:7** Then Saul said to his young servant who was with him, "And behold, we will go, yet what shall we bring to the man of God? For the loaves of bread are spent from our vessels, and there is nothing more with us of what we possess to bring to the man of God."

**9:8** And the young servant answered Saul again and said, "Behold, there is found in my hand a fourth part of a shekel of silver; and you shall give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

**9:9** Now formerly in Israel, each man spoke thus when he went to inquire of God: "Come, let us go to the seer"; for formerly the people called the prophet "the Seer."

**9:10** Then Saul said to his young servant, "The word is good; come, and let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.

**9:11** As they went up the ascent of the city, they found maidens going out to draw water, and they said to them, "Is the seer here?"

**9:12** And the maidens answered them and said to them, "He is; behold, he is right before you. Now because of the day he has come into the city, for the people have a sacrifice today in Bama.

**9:13** As soon as you enter the city, you will at once find him in the city before he goes up to Bama to eat; for the people will by no means eat until he enters, because he blesses the sacrifice, and afterward the guests eat. Now therefore go up, for because of the day you will find him."

**9:14** So they went up to the city. As they were entering into the midst of the city, behold, Samuel came out to meet them, to go up to Bama.

**9:15** Now the Lord had uncovered the ear of Samuel one day before Saul came to him, saying,

**9:16** "About this time tomorrow I will send to you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as ruler over My people Israel, and he shall save My people from the hand of the foreigners; for I have looked upon the affliction of My people, because their cry has come to Me."

**9:17** And Samuel saw Saul, and the Lord answered him, "Behold the man of whom I spoke to you! This one shall rule over My people."

**9:18** Then Saul drew near to Samuel in the midst of the city and said, "Please tell me, which is the house of the seer?"

**9:19** And Samuel answered Saul and said, "I am he. Go up before me to Bama and eat with me today, and in the morning I will send you away and will tell you all that is in your heart.

**9:20** And concerning your donkeys that were lost three days ago today, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. And for whom are the choice things of Israel? Are they not for you and for the house of your father?"

**9:21** And Saul answered and said, "Am I not the son of a Jeminite man, of the small tribe of the stock of Israel, and of the least clan of the whole tribe of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me according to this word?"

**9:22** Then Samuel took Saul and his young servant and brought them into the guest hall, and set a place for them there among the foremost of those who were invited, about seventy men.

**9:23** And Samuel said to the cook, "Give me the portion which I gave you, of which I told you to set it aside with you."

**9:24** So the cook boiled the thigh and set it before Saul; and Samuel said to Saul, "Behold what has been left over; set it before yourself and eat, for as a testimony it has been set for you above the others—cut off pieces." So Saul ate with Samuel on that day.

**9:25** And he came down from Bama into the city; and they spread a bed for Saul on the housetop, and he lay down to sleep.

**9:26** And it came to pass, as the dawn was rising, that Samuel called to Saul on the housetop, saying, "Arise, and I will send you away." So Saul arose, and both he and Samuel went outside.

**9:27** As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Speak to the young man, and let him pass on ahead of us; and you, stand still just now, and hear the word of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Greek names Saul's ancestors Ared, Bachi, and Aphek (differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's Zeror, Becorath, and Aphiah), and in 9:4 reads the place names Selcha, Easakem, and Jakim.
- **9:2** — Greek reads "taller than all the land" (hyper pasan ten gen) where the Masoretic Text reads "all the people."
- **9:12** — Greek transliterates Hebrew bamah ("high place") as the place name Bama throughout chapters 9 to 11.
- **9:22** — Greek numbers the invited guests at "about seventy men" (MT: "about thirty men").
- **9:24** — Greek has the cook "boiled" (hepsesen) the thigh and Samuel describe it as set aside "as a testimony above the others—cut off pieces," and in 9:25 reads that "they spread a bed for Saul on the housetop, and he lay down to sleep."
