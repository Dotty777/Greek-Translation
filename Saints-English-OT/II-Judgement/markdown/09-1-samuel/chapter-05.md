## Text

**5:1** And the foreigners took the ark of God and brought it from Abenner to Ashdod.

**5:2** And the foreigners took the ark of the Lord and brought it into the house of Dagon, and set it beside Dagon.

**5:3** And the Ashdodites rose early and entered the house of Dagon, and they looked, and behold, Dagon had fallen on his face before the ark of God. And they lifted Dagon up and set him in his place. And the hand of the Lord was heavy upon the Ashdodites, and He tormented them and struck them in their secret parts, both Ashdod and its borders.

**5:4** And it came to pass, when they rose early in the morning, that behold, Dagon had fallen on his face before the ark of the covenant of the Lord; and the head of Dagon and both palms of his hands were severed, each of them upon the front of the threshold, and both wrists of his hands had fallen upon the porch; only the spine of Dagon was left.

**5:5** Therefore the priests of Dagon and everyone who enters the house of Dagon do not tread upon the threshold of the house of Dagon in Ashdod to this day, because they always step right over it.

**5:6** And the hand of the Lord was heavy upon Ashdod, and He brought affliction upon them, and it broke out upon them in the ships, and in the midst of its land mice sprang up; and there was a great deadly confusion in the city.

**5:7** And the men of Ashdod saw that it was so, and they said, "The ark of God shall not remain with us, for His hand is harsh upon us and upon Dagon our god."

**5:8** And they sent and gathered the satraps of the foreigners to themselves and said, "What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?" And the Gittites said, "Let the ark of God come over to us." So the ark of God went over to Gath.

**5:9** And it came to pass after it had gone over, that the hand of the Lord came upon the city with an exceedingly great tumult; and He struck the men of the city from small to great, and He struck them in their secret parts. And the Gittites made seats for themselves.

**5:10** And they sent away the ark of God to Ashkelon. And it came to pass, as the ark of God entered Ashkelon, that the Ashkelonites cried out, saying, "Why have you turned the ark of the God of Israel back to us, to put us and our people to death?"

**5:11** So they sent and gathered the satraps of the foreigners and said, "Send away the ark of the God of Israel, and let it settle in its own place, so that it may by no means put us and our people to death." For an exceedingly heavy confusion had come upon the whole city when the ark of the God of Israel entered there.

**5:12** And those who lived and did not die were struck in their secret parts; and the outcry of the city ascended to heaven.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek reads Abenner here instead of Ebenezer (as in 4:1), and uses the Greek name Azotus for Ashdod throughout the chapter.
- **5:4** — Greek renders "threshold" with a transliteration of the Hebrew term (hammiphtan) and preserves a double description of Dagon's severed palms and wrists, leaving only his spine.
- **5:6** — Greek uniquely states that the affliction broke out "in the ships" (eis tas naus) and that mice sprang up in the midst of the land.
- **5:9** — Greek uses hedrai ("seats" or "sitting parts") both for the afflicted body parts ("secret parts" in verses 3, 9, and 12) and for the "seats" the Gittites made for themselves.
- **5:10** — Greek reads Ashkelon and the Ashkelonites, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Ekron and the Ekronites.
