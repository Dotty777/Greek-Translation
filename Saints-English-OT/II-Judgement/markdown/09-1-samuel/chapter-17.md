## Text

**17:1** Now the Philistines gathered their armies for war, and assembled at Socoh of Idumea, and encamped between Socoh and Azekah, at Ephermem.

**17:2** And Saul and the men of Israel assembled and encamped in the valley; they drew up in battle array opposite the Philistines.

**17:3** And the Philistines stood on the mountain on one side, and Israel stood on the mountain on the other side, with a circle between them.

**17:4** And a mighty man came out from the ranks of the Philistines, whose name was Goliath, from Gath; his height was four cubits and a span.

**17:5** And a helmet was upon his head, and he was clothed with a coat of chain mail, and the weight of his coat of mail was five thousand shekels of bronze and iron;

**17:6** and bronze greaves were upon his legs, and a bronze shield was between his shoulders;

**17:7** and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam, and his spearhead was six hundred shekels of iron; and the one who carried his armor went before him.

**17:8** And he stood up and shouted to the ranks of Israel, and said to them, "Why do you come out to draw up in battle array opposite us? Am I not a Philistine, and you Hebrews and Saul? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me.

**17:9** And if he is able to fight against me, and if he strikes me down, then we shall be your servants; but if I prevail and strike him down, you shall be our servants and serve us."

**17:10** And the Philistine said, "Behold, I have reproached the ranks of Israel today, on this very day; give me a man, and we shall both fight in single combat."

**17:11** And Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, and they were dismayed and exceedingly afraid.

**17:32** Then David said to Saul, "Do not let the heart of my lord fail because of him; your servant will go and fight with this Philistine."

**17:33** And Saul said to David, "You are not able to go against the Philistine to fight with him, for you are a lad, and he is a man of war from his youth."

**17:34** And David said to Saul, "Your servant was shepherding for his father in the flock; and whenever the lion and the bear came and took a sheep from the herd,

**17:35** I went out after it and struck it, and snatched the sheep from its mouth; and if it rose up against me, I seized it by its throat, and struck it and put it to death.

**17:36** Your servant has struck both the bear and the lion, and the uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them. Shall I not go and strike him down, and take away reproach from Israel today? For who is this uncircumcised one, who has reproached the ranks of the living God?

**17:37** The Lord, who delivered me from the hand of the lion and from the hand of the bear, He will deliver me from the hand of this uncircumcised Philistine." And Saul said to David, "Go, and the Lord shall be with you."

**17:38** And Saul clothed David with a mantle, and put a bronze helmet about his head,

**17:39** and girded David with his sword over his mantle; and he grew weary after walking once and twice. And David said to Saul, "I cannot walk in these, for I have not tested them." So they removed them from him.

**17:40** And he took his staff in his hand, and chose for himself five perfect stones from the brook, and put them in the shepherd's pouch that he had for gathering, with his sling in his hand; and he drew near to the Philistine man.

**17:42** And Goliath saw David and disdained him, for he was a lad, and he was ruddy, with beauty of eyes.

**17:43** And the Philistine said to David, "Am I like a dog, that you come against me with a staff and stones?" And David said, "No, but worse than a dog." And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.

**17:44** And the Philistine said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of heaven and to the beasts of the earth."

**17:45** Then David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a shield; but I come to you in the name of the Lord God of Sabaoth, of the ranks of Israel, which you have reproached today.

**17:46** And today the Lord will deliver you up into my hand, and I will slay you and take your head from you, and I will give your limbs and the limbs of the camp of the Philistines on this day to the birds of heaven and to the wild beasts of the earth; and all the earth shall know that there is a God in Israel.

**17:47** And all this assembly shall know that the Lord does not save with sword and spear; for the battle belongs to the Lord, and the Lord will deliver you into our hands."

**17:48** And the Philistine arose and went to meet David.

**17:49** And David reached out his hand into the pouch and took one stone from there, and slung it and struck the Philistine upon his forehead; and the stone passed through the helmet into his forehead, and he fell upon his face to the ground.

**17:51** Then David ran and stood over him, and took his sword and put him to death, and cut off his head. And the Philistines saw that their mighty man was dead, and they fled.

**17:52** And the men of Israel and Judah arose and raised a war cry, and pursued after them as far as the entrance of Gath and as far as the gate of Ashkelon; and the wounded of the Philistines fell along the road of the gates, even to Gath and to Ekron.

**17:53** And the men of Israel returned, turning back from pursuing the Philistines, and they trampled their camps.

**17:54** And David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, and he put his armor in his tent.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Greek calls the Philistines allophyloi (literally "foreigners"), its standard designation for them in 1-4 Kingdoms, and reads "Socoh of Idumea" and "Ephermem" where the Masoretic Text has "Socoh, which belongs to Judah" and "Ephes-dammim."
- **17:3** — The Greek reads kyklo ("with a circle"), likely an early Greek textual variation for koilas ("valley").
- **17:4** — The Greek gives Goliath's height as "four cubits and a span" (agreeing with 4QSam-a and Josephus), rather than the Masoretic Text's "six cubits and a span."
- **17:32** — Following the shorter Old Greek text, verses 12-31, 41, and 50 are not present in this chapter, nor are 17:55-18:5.
- **17:43** — The Greek adds "and stones" to Goliath's taunt and preserves David's reply, "No, but worse than a dog," which does not appear in the Masoretic Text.
