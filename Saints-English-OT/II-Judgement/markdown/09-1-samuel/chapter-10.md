## Text

**10:1** Then Samuel took the flask of oil and poured it upon his head, and kissed him and said to him, "Has not the Lord anointed you as ruler over His people, over Israel? And you shall rule over the people of the Lord, and you shall save them from the hand of their enemies all around. And this shall be the sign to you that the Lord has anointed you over His inheritance as ruler:

**10:2** when you depart from me today, you will find two men by the tombs of Rachel in the hill country of Benjamin, leaping greatly, and they will say to you, 'The donkeys which you went to seek have been found; and behold, your father has shaken off the matter of the donkeys and is greatly anxious about you, saying, "What shall I do for my son?"'

**10:3** Then you shall go on from there and come farther, as far as the oak of Tabor, and there you will find three men going up to God at Bethel: one carrying three young goats, and one carrying three vessels of bread, and one carrying a skin of wine.

**10:4** And they will ask after your peace and will give you two firstfruits of bread, and you shall receive them from their hand.

**10:5** After that you shall enter the hill of God, where the garrison of the foreigners is—there is Nasib the foreigner. And it shall be, when you enter there into the city, that you will meet a company of prophets coming down from Bama, and before them a harp, a tambourine, a flute, and a lyre, and they will be prophesying.

**10:6** And the Spirit of the Lord will rush upon you, and you will prophesy with them and be turned into another man.

**10:7** And it shall be, when these signs come upon you, do whatever your hand finds to do, for God is with you.

**10:8** And you shall go down before Gilead; and behold, I am coming down to you to offer up a burnt offering and peace sacrifices. Seven days you shall wait until I come to you, and I will make known to you what you shall do."

**10:9** And it came to pass, as he turned his shoulder to depart from Samuel, that God changed his heart into another heart; and all the signs came to pass on that day.

**10:10** And he came from there to the hill, and behold, a company of prophets came toward him; and the Spirit of God rushed upon him, and he prophesied in their midst.

**10:11** And it happened that all who knew him yesterday and the day before saw him, and behold, he was in the midst of the prophets. Then the people said, each man to his neighbor, "What is this that has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:12** And one of them answered and said, "And who is his father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:13** And when he had finished prophesying, he came to the hill.

**10:14** Then his kinsman said to him and to his young servant, "Where did you go?" And they said, "To seek the donkeys; and we saw that they were not there, so we went in to Samuel."

**10:15** And the kinsman said to Saul, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**10:16** And Saul said to his kinsman, "He plainly told me that the donkeys had been found." But the matter of the kingdom he did not tell him.

**10:17** Then Samuel summoned all the people to the Lord at Mizpah,

**10:18** and said to the sons of Israel, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'I brought up the sons of Israel out of Egypt, and I delivered you from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt and from all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.'

**10:19** Yet today you have rejected God, who Himself is your Savior from all your evils and your afflictions, and you have said, 'No, rather you shall set a king over us.' Now therefore present yourselves before the Lord according to your tribes and according to your clans."

**10:20** So Samuel brought near all the tribes of Israel, and the tribe of Benjamin was taken by lot.

**10:21** Then he brought near the tribe of Benjamin by its clans, and the clan of Mattari was taken by lot; and they brought near the clan of Mattari man by man, and Saul the son of Kish was taken by lot. And he sought him, but he was not found.

**10:22** Therefore Samuel inquired further of the Lord, "Is the man coming here?" And the Lord said, "Behold, he has hidden himself among the baggage."

**10:23** So he ran and took him from there, and set him in the midst of the people; and he stood taller than all the people from the shoulders and upward.

**10:24** And Samuel said to all the people, "Have you seen him whom the Lord has chosen for Himself, that there is none like him among all of you?" And all the people recognized him and said, "Let the king live!"

**10:25** Then Samuel spoke to the people the ordinance of the king, and wrote it in a book and laid it before the Lord. And Samuel sent all the people away, and each man departed to his own place.

**10:26** And Saul also departed to his house at Gibeah; and men of valor whose hearts the Lord had touched went with Saul.

**10:27** But pestilent men said, "How shall this man save us?" And they despised him and brought him no gifts.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek preserves Samuel's fuller declaration of Saul's rule, deliverance of Israel, and the sign of his anointing, which is shorter in the Masoretic Text.
- **10:2** — Greek reads "leaping greatly" (hallomenous megala) where the Masoretic Text has the place name Zelzah.
- **10:5** — Greek contains a doublet for Hebrew netsiv pelishtim, translating it both as "the garrison of the foreigners" and as "Nasib the foreigner"; here and in 9:16 Greek uses allophyloi ("foreigners") for the Philistines.
- **10:8** — Greek reads "before Gilead" (emprosthen tes Galaad) where the Masoretic Text has "before me to Gilgal."
- **10:24** — Greek reads "all the people recognized him" (egnosan) rather than "all the people shouted" as in the Masoretic Text.
