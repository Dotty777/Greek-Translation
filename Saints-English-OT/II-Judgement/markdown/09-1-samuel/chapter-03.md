## Text

**3:1** Now the boy Samuel was ministering to the Lord before Eli the priest. And the word of the Lord was precious in those days; there was no distinct vision.

**3:2** And it came to pass on that day that Eli was sitting in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow heavy, and he could not see.

**3:3** And before the lamp of God was trimmed, Samuel was sleeping in the temple, where the ark of God was.

**3:4** And the Lord called, "Samuel, Samuel!" And he said, "Here I am."

**3:5** And he ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you have called me." And he said, "I have not called you; return and sleep." So he returned and went to sleep.

**3:6** And the Lord called yet again, "Samuel, Samuel!" And he went to Eli a second time and said, "Here I am, for you have called me." And he said, "I have not called you; return and sleep."

**3:7** Now Samuel did not yet know God, nor had the word of the Lord yet been revealed to him.

**3:8** And the Lord called Samuel a third time; and he arose and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you have called me." Then Eli discerned that the Lord had called the boy.

**3:9** And he said, "Return and sleep, child; and it shall be, if He calls you, that you shall say, 'Speak, for Your servant hears.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**3:10** And the Lord came and stood, and called him as once and again. And Samuel said, "Speak, for Your servant hears."

**3:11** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Behold, I am performing My words in Israel; of everyone who hears them, both his ears shall ring.

**3:12** In that day I will raise up against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house; I will begin and I will bring it to completion.

**3:13** And I have declared to him that I am executing vengeance upon his house forever for the iniquities of his sons, because his sons were reviling God, and he did not admonish them,

**3:14** not even so. I have sworn to the house of Eli, 'The iniquity of the house of Eli shall by no means be atoned for with incense and with sacrifices forever.'"

**3:15** And Samuel lay until morning, and he rose early in the morning and opened the doors of the house of the Lord. And Samuel was afraid to declare the vision to Eli.

**3:16** And Eli said to Samuel, "Samuel, child." And he said, "Here I am."

**3:17** And he said, "What is the word that was spoken to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do so to you if you hide from me a single word of all the words that were spoken to you in your ears."

**3:18** So Samuel declared all the words and hid nothing from him. And Eli said, "He is the Lord; He will do what is good in His sight."

**3:19** And Samuel grew up, and the Lord was with him, and none of all his words fell to the ground.

**3:20** And all Israel from Dan even to Beersheba knew that Samuel was trustworthy as a prophet to the Lord.

**3:21** And the Lord continued to be made known in Shiloh, for the Lord was revealed to Samuel; and Samuel was accredited to be a prophet to the Lord for all Israel from one end of the land even to the other. Now Eli was exceedingly old, and his sons kept going on, and their way was wicked before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — Greek reads "before the lamp of God was trimmed" (episkeuasthenai), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "before the lamp of God went out."
- **3:13** — Greek reads "reviling God" (kakologountes theon), preserving an ancient reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "bringing a curse upon themselves."
- **3:14** — Greek reads "with incense and with sacrifices," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "with sacrifice or offering."
- **3:21** — The Greek text includes an expanded conclusion affirming Samuel's accreditation across all Israel and contrasting him with the aged Eli and his wicked sons.
