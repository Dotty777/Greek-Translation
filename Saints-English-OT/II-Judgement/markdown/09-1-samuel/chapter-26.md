## Text

**26:1** Then the Ziphites came from the parched land to Saul at the hill, saying, "Behold, David is hiding himself with us on the hill of Chelmath, which faces Jessemou."

**26:2** And Saul arose and went down into the wilderness of Ziph, and with him three thousand chosen men of Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**26:3** And Saul encamped on the hill of Echela, facing Jessemou, by the road; and David dwelt in the wilderness, and David saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness.

**26:4** And David sent out spies, and knew that Saul had come ready from Keilah.

**26:5** And David arose secretly and came into the place where Saul was sleeping, and Abner the son of Ner, the commander of his army, was there; and Saul was sleeping in a covered chariot, and the people were encamped around him.

**26:6** And David answered and said to Abimelech the Hittite and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, saying, "Who will go in with me to Saul into the camp?" And Abishai said, "I will go in with you."

**26:7** So David and Abishai went in among the people by night; and behold, Saul was fast asleep in a covered chariot, with his spear thrust into the ground by his head, and Abner and his people were sleeping around him.

**26:8** Then Abishai said to David, "Today the Lord has shut up your enemy into your hands; and now I will strike him with the spear to the ground once, and I will not strike him a second time."

**26:9** And David said to Abishai, "Do not bring him low; for who can stretch out his hand against the anointed of the Lord and be guiltless?"

**26:10** And David said, "As the Lord lives, surely the Lord will chasten him, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go down into battle and be taken away.

**26:11** Far be it from me, before the Lord, to stretch out my hand against the anointed of the Lord! And now, please take the spear from by his head and the jug of water, and let us depart by ourselves."

**26:12** So David took the spear and the jug of water from by his head, and they departed by themselves; and no one saw, and no one knew, and no one awoke, for they were all asleep, because a stupor from the Lord had fallen upon them.

**26:13** Then David crossed over to the other side and stood on the top of the mountain afar off, and great was the distance between them.

**26:14** And David called out to the people, and spoke to Abner, saying, "Will you not answer, Abner?" And he said, "Who are you who calls?"

**26:15** And David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? Why then do you not guard your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy the king your lord.

**26:16** And this thing that you have done is not good. As the Lord lives, you are sons of death, you who guard the king your lord, the anointed of the Lord! And now see where the king's spear and the jug of water are—those things that were by his head."

**26:17** Then Saul recognized the voice of David and said, "Is this your voice, my child David?" And David said, "Your servant, my lord, O king."

**26:18** And he said, "Why does my lord thus pursue after his servant? For how have I sinned, and what unrighteousness has been found in me?

**26:19** And now let my lord the king hear the word of his servant: if God stirs you up against me, may He smell the savor of your sacrifice; but if the sons of men, cursed are they before the Lord, for they have cast me out today from being established in the inheritance of the Lord, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

**26:20** And now do not let my blood fall to the earth before the face of the Lord; for the king of Israel has come out to seek my life, just as the night owl hunts in the mountains."

**26:21** Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my child David; for I will do you no harm, because my life was precious in your eyes; and on this day I have acted foolishly and erred exceedingly."

**26:22** And David answered and said, "Behold, the king's spear! Let one of the young men cross over and take it.

**26:23** And the Lord will render to each man his righteous deeds and his faithfulness; for the Lord delivered you today into my hands, and I was not willing to stretch out my hand against the anointed of the Lord.

**26:24** And behold, as your life was magnified on this very day in my eyes, so may my life be magnified before the Lord, and may He shelter me, and He shall deliver me out of every affliction."

**26:25** Then Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, my child; you shall surely accomplish what you do, and you shall surely prevail." So David departed to his place, and Saul returned to his way.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — Greek adds "from the parched land" and "with us," and reads "Chelmath" (and "Echela" in v. 3) facing "Jessemou," corresponding to Hebrew Hachilah facing Jeshimon.
- **26:4** — Greek reads "ready from Keilah," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "for a certainty."
- **26:5** — Greek lampene denotes a covered chariot or royal wagon (also in v. 7), where the Hebrew refers to the camp enclosure; in v. 6, Greek also reads "Abimelech" rather than Hebrew "Ahimelech."
- **26:16** — Greek has the ironic positive participle "you who guard" rather than the Hebrew negative "because you have not guarded."
- **26:20** — Greek reads "to seek my life, just as the night owl hunts in the mountains," whereas the Hebrew reads "to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains."
