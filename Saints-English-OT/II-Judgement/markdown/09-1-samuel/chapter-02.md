## Text

**2:1** "My heart has been established in the Lord; my horn has been exalted in my God. My mouth has been opened wide against enemies; I have rejoiced in Your salvation.

**2:2** For there is none holy like the Lord, and there is none righteous like our God; there is none holy besides You.

**2:3** Do not boast, and do not speak lofty things; do not let arrogant speech come out of your mouth; for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and a God who prepares His own deeds.

**2:4** The bow of the mighty has grown weak, and those who are weak have girded themselves with strength.

**2:5** Those who were full of bread have been brought low, and those who were weak have forsaken the land. For the barren woman has borne seven, and she who had many children has grown weak.

**2:6** The Lord puts to death and makes alive; He brings down to Hades and brings up.

**2:7** The Lord makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and lifts up.

**2:8** He raises the needy from the earth, and lifts the poor from the dunghill, to seat him with the princes of the peoples, and causing them to inherit a throne of glory;

**2:9** granting his vow to the one who vows, and He has blessed the years of the righteous. For by strength no man is mighty.

**2:10** The Lord will make His adversary weak; the Lord is holy. Do not let the prudent man boast in his prudence, and do not let the mighty man boast in his might, and do not let the rich man boast in his riches; rather, let him who boasts boast in this: to understand and know the Lord, and to execute judgment and righteousness in the midst of the earth. The Lord ascended into the heavens and thundered; He Himself will judge the ends of the earth. And He gives strength to our kings, and He will exalt the horn of His anointed."

**2:11** And she left him there before the Lord, and departed to Armathaim. And the child was ministering before the face of the Lord in the presence of Eli the priest.

**2:12** Now the sons of Eli the priest were worthless sons, who did not know the Lord.

**2:13** And this was the priest's due from all the people who offered sacrifice: the priest's servant would come while the meat was boiling, with a three-pronged fleshhook in his hand,

**2:14** and he would thrust it into the great cauldron, or into the copper kettle, or into the pot; whatever came up on the fleshhook, the priest would take for himself. In this manner they did to all Israel who came to sacrifice to the Lord in Shiloh.

**2:15** Also, before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give meat to roast for the priest, for I will by no means take boiled meat from you out of the cauldron."

**2:16** And the man who was sacrificing would say, "Let the fat be burned first, as is fitting, and then take for yourself from all that your soul desires." And he said, "No, for you shall give it now; and if not, I will take it by force."

**2:17** Therefore the sin of the young servants was exceedingly great before the Lord, for they despised the sacrifice of the Lord.

**2:18** Now Samuel was ministering before the Lord, a child girded with a linen ephod.

**2:19** And his mother made him a little robe, and brought it up to him from year to year when she went up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

**2:20** And Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, saying, "May the Lord repay you with offspring from this woman in place of the loan which you lent to the Lord." And the man departed to his own place.

**2:21** And the Lord visited Hannah, and she bore yet three sons and two daughters. And the child Samuel grew great before the Lord.

**2:22** Now Eli was exceedingly old, and he heard what his sons were doing to the sons of Israel;

**2:23** and he said to them, "Why do you do according to this thing which I hear from the mouth of all the people of the Lord?

**2:24** No, children, for the report which I hear is not good; do not do so, for the reports which I hear are not good, so that the people do not serve God.

**2:25** If a man indeed sins against a man, then they will pray for him to the Lord; but if he sins against the Lord, who shall pray for him?" Yet they did not listen to the voice of their father, because the Lord indeed purposed to destroy them.

**2:26** And the child Samuel walked on, and was good both with the Lord and with men.

**2:27** And a man of God came to Eli and said, "Thus says the Lord: I plainly revealed Myself to the house of your father when they were in the land of Egypt, servants to the house of Pharaoh;

**2:28** and I chose the house of your father out of all the tribes of Israel to serve as priests to Me, and to go up upon My altar, and to burn incense, and to bear an ephod; and I gave to the house of your father all the fire offerings of the sons of Israel for food.

**2:29** And why have you looked upon My incense and upon My sacrifice with a shameless eye, and glorified your sons above Me, to bless yourselves with the firstfruits of every sacrifice of Israel before Me?

**2:30** Therefore thus has the Lord God of Israel said: I said, 'Your house and the house of your father shall walk before Me forever.' Yet now the Lord declares: Far be it from Me! For rather, those who glorify Me I will glorify, and he who despises Me shall be dishonored.

**2:31** Behold, days are coming when I will utterly destroy your seed and the seed of your father's house,

**2:32** and you shall not have an old man in My house all the days.

**2:33** And a man I will not utterly destroy from My altar, that his eyes may fail and his soul may waste away; and every survivor of your house shall fall by the sword of men.

**2:34** And this shall be the sign to you that shall come upon these two sons of yours, Hophni and Phinehas: in one day they shall both die.

**2:35** And I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who shall do all the things that are in My heart and in My soul; and I will build him a faithful house, and he shall walk before My anointed all the days.

**2:36** And it shall come to pass that whoever remains in the house shall come to bow down to him for an obol of silver, saying, 'Cast me into one of your priestly offices, to eat bread.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — Greek "those who were weak have forsaken the land" reflects a distinctive LXX rendering of the clause concerning the hungry ceasing to labor.
- **2:10** — The Greek text includes an expanded exhortation (paralleling Jeremiah 9:23-24) against boasting in prudence, might, or riches, and reads "our kings" in the plural.
- **2:18** — Greek "ephoud bar" transcribes the Hebrew phrase for a linen ephod.
- **2:22** — The Greek text omits the clause found in the MT regarding Eli's sons lying with the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
- **2:29** — The Greek reads "with a shameless eye" and "to bless yourselves" (MT: "kick at" and "fatten yourselves"), and in verse 31 reads "your seed" (MT: "your arm").
