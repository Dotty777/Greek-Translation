## Text

**31:1** Now the Philistines fought against Israel; and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines and fell wounded on Mount Gilboa.

**31:2** And the Philistines closed in on Saul and his sons, and the Philistines struck down Jonathan, and Jonadab, and Malchishua, the son of Saul.

**31:3** And the battle grew heavy against Saul, and the javelin-throwers, men who were archers, found him, and he was wounded in the flanks.

**31:4** Then Saul said to his armorbearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me through with it, lest these uncircumcised ones come and pierce me through and mock me." But his armorbearer was not willing, for he was greatly afraid. Therefore Saul took the sword and fell upon it.

**31:5** And when his armorbearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell upon his sword and died with him.

**31:6** Thus Saul and his three sons and his armorbearer died on that day together.

**31:7** And when the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley, and those who were on the other side of the Jordan, saw that the men of Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they forsook their cities and fled; and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

**31:8** And it came to pass on the next day, that the Philistines came to strip the dead; and they found Saul and his three sons fallen on the mountains of Gilboa.

**31:9** And they turned him over and stripped off his armor, and sent it throughout the land of the Philistines all around, proclaiming the glad tidings to their idols and to the people.

**31:10** And they placed his armor in the temple of Astarte, and his body they fastened on the wall of Bethem.

**31:11** And the inhabitants of Jabesh of Gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul.

**31:12** And every valiant man arose and journeyed all night, and they took the body of Saul and the body of Jonathan his son from the wall of Bethsam, and brought them to Jabesh and burned them there.

**31:13** And they took their bones and buried them beneath the field of Jabesh, and fasted seven days. And it came to pass after the death of Saul, that David returned from striking down Amalek, and David stayed in Ziklag two days.

## Translator Notes

- **31:2** — The Greek reads 'Jonadab' (rather than 'Abinadab' as in the Hebrew) and retains the singular 'son of Saul' following the three names.
- **31:3** — The Greek specifies that Saul was 'wounded in the flanks' (literally the soft parts beneath the ribs), where the Hebrew refers more generally to being badly wounded or in anguish.
- **31:9** — The Greek reads 'they turned him over' rather than 'they cut off his head' as in the Hebrew; in verses 10 and 12 the city wall is named Bethem and Bethsam (Hebrew Beth-shan).
- **31:13** — The Greek reads 'beneath the field of Jabesh' (Hebrew 'under the tamarisk tree in Jabesh') and appends the opening sentence of 2 Kingdoms (2 Samuel 1:1) directly to the close of the chapter.
