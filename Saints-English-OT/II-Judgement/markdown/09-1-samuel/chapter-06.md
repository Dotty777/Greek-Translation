## Text

**6:1** And the ark was in the country of the Philistines seven months, and their land swarmed with mice.

**6:2** And the Philistines called their priests, their diviners, and their enchanters, saying, "What shall we do with the ark of the Lord? Make known to us with what we shall send it to its place."

**6:3** And they said, "If you send away the ark of the covenant of God, the Lord of Israel, do not send it away empty, but by all means render to it an offering for the torment; then you shall be healed, and atonement shall be made for you. Will His hand not depart from you?"

**6:4** And they said, "What offering for the torment shall we render to it?" And they said, "According to the number of the lords of the Philistines, five golden seats, because a calamity is among you, and your rulers, and the people,

**6:5** and golden mice, the likeness of your mice that are destroying the land. And you shall give glory to the Lord, so that He may lighten His hand from you, and from your gods, and from your land.

**6:6** And why do you harden your hearts, as Egypt and Pharaoh hardened their heart? When He mocked them, did they not send them away, and they departed?

**6:7** Now therefore, take and make a new cart, and take two cows that have borne their first young, without their young; and yoke the cows to the cart, and lead their young away from behind them back home.

**6:8** And you shall take the ark and set it upon the cart, and the golden articles you shall render to it for the torment, and you shall not put them in a berechthan coffer at its side; and you shall send it away and drive it off, and you shall depart.

**6:9** And watch: if it goes along the way to its own border toward Beth-shemesh, He has done this great evil to us; but if not, then we shall know that His hand has not touched us, but this has happened to us by chance."

**6:10** And the Philistines did so; they took two cows that had borne their first young and yoked them to the cart, and kept their young back at home.

**6:11** And they set the ark upon the cart, and the ergab coffer, and the golden mice.

**6:12** And the cows went straight along the road toward the way of Beth-shemesh; they walked along one path and toiled on, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left. And the lords of the Philistines walked behind it as far as the borders of Beth-shemesh.

**6:13** Now those in Beth-shemesh were reaping the wheat harvest in the valley; and they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark of the Lord, and rejoiced to meet it.

**6:14** And the cart came into the field of Hosea, which was in Beth-shemesh, and they set up a great stone there beside it; and they split the wood of the cart and offered up the cows as a burnt offering to the Lord.

**6:15** And the Levites brought up the ark of the Lord, and the ergab coffer with it, and the golden articles that were upon it, and set them upon the great stone. And the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrifices to the Lord on that day.

**6:16** And the five lords of the Philistines saw it, and returned to Ashkelon on that day.

**6:17** And these are the golden seats which the Philistines rendered to the Lord for the torment: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, one for Ekron;

**6:18** and the golden mice, according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords, from the fortified city even to the village of the Perizzite, and as far as the great stone on which they placed the ark of the covenant of the Lord, which is in the field of Hosea the Beth-shemite.

**6:19** And the sons of Jeconiah were not pleased with the men of Beth-shemesh, because they saw the ark of the Lord; and He struck among them seventy men and fifty thousand men. And the people mourned, because the Lord had struck the people with an exceedingly great plague.

**6:20** And the men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who shall be able to pass before this holy One? And to whom shall the ark of the Lord go up from us?"

**6:21** So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The Philistines have returned the ark of the Lord; come down and bring it up to yourselves."

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Greek uses basanos ("torment") for the reparation offering throughout chapter 6 (vv. 3, 4, 8, 17) and reads "atonement shall be made for you" where the Masoretic Text has "it will be known to you."
- **6:4** — Greek hedras literally means "seats," a euphemism for the afflicted parts or tumors; the Greek also uses satrapai ("lords" or "satraps") for the Philistine rulers.
- **6:8** — The Greek text preserves the negative reading "you shall not put" (ou thesete) and transliterates the Hebrew coffer term as berechthan here and ergab in vv. 11 and 15.
- **6:14** — The Septuagint reads "Hosea" (also in v. 18) where the Masoretic Text has "Joshua," and states actively that they "set up a great stone" beside the cart.
- **6:19** — The Septuagint uniquely specifies that "the sons of Jeconiah were not pleased with the men of Beth-shemesh"; similarly, v. 16 names Ashkelon rather than Ekron, and v. 18 reads "village of the Perizzite" instead of "unwalled village."
