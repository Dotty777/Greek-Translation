## Text

**15:1** And Samuel said to Saul, "The Lord sent me to anoint you as king over Israel; and now, listen to the voice of the Lord.

**15:2** Thus says the Lord of hosts: 'Now I will avenge what Amalek did to Israel, how he met him on the way as he came up out of Egypt.

**15:3** And now go, and you shall strike Amalek and Jerim and all that belongs to him, and you shall not preserve anything of his, but shall utterly destroy him; and you shall devote him and all that belongs to him to destruction, and you shall not spare him, but shall slay from man even to woman, from infant to nursing child, from calf to sheep, and from camel to donkey.'"

**15:4** And Saul summoned the people and mustered them in Gilgal: four hundred thousand troops, and of Judah thirty thousand troops.

**15:5** And Saul came as far as the cities of Amalek, and laid an ambush in the torrent valley.

**15:6** And Saul said to the Kenite, "Depart and turn aside from the midst of the Amalekite, lest I add you with him; for you showed mercy to the sons of Israel when they came up out of Egypt." And the Kenite turned aside from the midst of Amalek.

**15:7** And Saul struck Amalek from Havilah all the way to Asshur, which is before the face of Egypt.

**15:8** And he captured Agag king of Amalek alive, and he slew all the people of Jerim with the edge of the sword.

**15:9** And Saul and all the people preserved Agag alive, and the best of the flocks and of the herds, and of the provisions and of the vineyards, and of all that was good, and he was unwilling to utterly destroy them; but everything that was dishonored and despised they utterly destroyed.

**15:10** Then the word of the Lord came to Samuel, saying,

**15:11** "I regret that I have made Saul king, for he has turned back from following Me and has not kept My words." And Samuel was disheartened, and he cried out to the Lord all the night.

**15:12** And Samuel rose early and went to meet Israel in the morning. And it was reported to Saul, saying, "Samuel has come to Carmel, and he has set up a monument for himself." And he turned the chariot around and went down to Gilgal to Saul; and behold, he was offering up a burnt offering to the Lord, the choicest of the spoils which he had brought from Amalek.

**15:13** And Samuel came to Saul, and Saul said to him, "Blessed are you of the Lord! I have fulfilled all that the Lord spoke."

**15:14** And Samuel said, "What then is this sound of the flock in my ears, and the sound of the cattle which I hear?"

**15:15** And Saul said, "From Amalek I have brought them, which the people spared—the choicest of the flock and of the cattle—in order that they might be sacrificed to the Lord your God; and the rest I have utterly destroyed."

**15:16** And Samuel said to Saul, "Be quiet, and I will declare to you what the Lord spoke to me this night." And he said to him, "Speak."

**15:17** And Samuel said to Saul, "Are you not small before Him, a leader of the scepter of a tribe of Israel? Yet the Lord anointed you as king over Israel.

**15:18** And the Lord sent you on a way and said to you, 'Go and utterly destroy; you shall slay those who sin against Me, even Amalek, and fight against them until you make an end of them.'

**15:19** Why then did you not obey the voice of the Lord, but rushed to seize upon the spoils and did what was evil in the sight of the Lord?"

**15:20** And Saul said to Samuel, "Because I listened to the voice of the people! Yet I walked in the way on which the Lord sent me, and brought Agag king of Amalek, and utterly destroyed Amalek.

**15:21** And the people took from the spoils flocks and herds, the choicest of the things devoted to destruction, before the Lord our God in Gilgal."

**15:22** And Samuel said, "Are burnt offerings and sacrifices as desirable to the Lord as obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, obedience is above a good sacrifice, and giving heed is above the fat of rams.

**15:23** For sin is divination, and grief and toil bring on healing. Because you have rejected the word of the Lord, the Lord will also reject you, so that you shall not be king over Israel."

**15:24** And Saul said to Samuel, "I have sinned, for I have transgressed the word of the Lord and your saying, because I feared the people and listened to their voice.

**15:25** Now therefore, please take away my sin, and return with me, that I may worship the Lord your God."

**15:26** And Samuel said to Saul, "I will not return with you, for you have rejected the word of the Lord, and the Lord will reject you so that you shall not be king over Israel."

**15:27** And Samuel turned his face to depart, and Saul laid hold of the hem of his mantle and tore it.

**15:28** And Samuel said to him, "The Lord has torn your kingdom from Israel out of your hand today, and He will give it to your neighbor who is better than you.

**15:29** And Israel shall be divided in two, and He will not turn back nor relent; for He is not as a man, that He should relent."

**15:30** And Saul said, "I have sinned; yet please honor me before the elders of Israel and before my people, and return with me, and I will worship the Lord your God."

**15:31** So Samuel turned back after Saul, and he worshiped the Lord.

**15:32** And Samuel said, "Bring Agag king of Amalek to me." And Agag came to him trembling. And Agag said, "Is death indeed so bitter?"

**15:33** And Samuel said to Agag, "Just as your sword has made women childless, so shall your mother be made childless among women." And Samuel slaughtered Agag before the Lord in Gilgal.

**15:34** Then Samuel departed to Armathaim, and Saul went up to his house at Gibeah.

**15:35** And Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death, for Samuel mourned over Saul; and the Lord regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The Greek reads "Jerim" as a proper name (also in 15:8), reflecting a transliteration of the Hebrew term for devoting to destruction.
- **15:7** — The Greek reads "Asshur" (Assyria) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Shur."
- **15:23** — The Greek renders the Hebrew word for household idols (terafim) with the similar-sounding Greek word therapeia ("healing" or "service"), producing the clause "grief and toil bring on healing."
- **15:29** — The Greek reads "Israel shall be divided in two," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to "the Glory [or Strength] of Israel."
- **15:34** — "Armathaim" is the distinctive Septuagint name corresponding to Ramah (Ramathaim) in the Hebrew text (also in 16:13).
