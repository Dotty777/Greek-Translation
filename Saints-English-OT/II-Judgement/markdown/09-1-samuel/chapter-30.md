## Text

**30:1** Now it came to pass, when David and his men entered Keilah on the third day, that Amalek had made a raid against the South and against Ziklag, and had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire;

**30:2** and the women and all that were in it, from small to great—they did not put to death either man or woman, but carried them away captive and went on their way.

**30:3** And David and his men came to the city, and behold, it was burned with fire, and their wives, their sons, and their daughters had been carried away captive.

**30:4** Then David and his men lifted up their voice and wept, until there was no longer strength in them to weep.

**30:5** And both of David's wives had been taken captive: Ahinoam the Israelite, and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**30:6** Now David was greatly distressed, for the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was deeply grieved, each man for his sons and for his daughters; yet David was strengthened in the Lord his God.

**30:7** And David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Bring the ephod near."

**30:8** Then David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Shall I pursue after this raiding band? Shall I overtake them?" And He said to him, "Pursue, for you shall surely overtake them, and you shall surely rescue them."

**30:9** So David went, he and the four hundred men with him, and they came to the Brook Besor; and the rest halted.

**30:10** And the rest pursued with four hundred men, while two hundred men stayed behind, who sat down on the other side of the Brook Besor.

**30:11** Then they found an Egyptian man in the field, and they took him and brought him to David in the field; and they gave him bread and he ate, and they gave him water to drink.

**30:12** And they gave him a piece of a fig cake and he ate, and his spirit was restored within him; for he had not eaten bread nor drunk water for three days and three nights.

**30:13** And David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" And the Egyptian young man said, "I am the servant of an Amalekite man, and my master left me behind because I fell ill three days ago today.

**30:14** And we made a raid upon the south of Cholthi, and upon the regions of Judea, and upon Gilboa, and we burned Ziklag with fire."

**30:15** Then David said to him, "Will you lead me down to this raiding band?" And he said, "Swear now to me by God that you will not put me to death and will not deliver me into the hands of my master, and I will lead you down to this raiding band."

**30:16** So he led him down there; and behold, they were spread abroad over the face of all the land, eating and drinking and feasting over all the great spoil which they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

**30:17** And David came upon them and struck them down from dawn until evening and on the next day; and not a man of them escaped, except four hundred young men who mounted camels and fled.

**30:18** And David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and he rescued both of his wives.

**30:19** And nothing of theirs was missing, from small to great, from the spoil even to sons and daughters, and to everything they had taken from them; David brought back all.

**30:20** And he took all the flocks and the herds and drove them ahead of the spoil; and of that spoil it was said, "This is the spoil of David."

**30:21** Now David came to the two hundred men who had grown too weary to follow after David, whom he had seated by the Brook Beana; and they went out to meet David and to meet the people who were with him. And David drew near to the people, and they asked him concerning his welfare.

**30:22** Then every pestilent and wicked man among the men of war who had gone with David answered and said, "Because they did not pursue with us, we will not give them any of the spoil which we have rescued, except that each man may take away his wife and his children and depart."

**30:23** But David said, "You shall not do so after the Lord has given to us and preserved us, and the Lord has delivered the raiding band that came against us into our hands.

**30:24** And who will heed these words of yours? For they are no less than you; for as is the share of him who goes down to battle, so shall be the share of him who stays by the baggage; they shall share alike."

**30:25** And so it was from that day forward, that it became a statute and an ordinance for Israel to this day.

**30:26** Now David came to Ziklag, and he sent some of the spoil to the elders of Judah and to his neighbors, saying, "Behold, from the spoil of the enemies of the Lord"—

**30:27** to those in Bethsur, and to those in Ramah of the South, and to those in Geththor,

**30:28** and to those in Aroer, and to those of Ammadi, and to those in Saphi, and to those in Esthie,

**30:29** and to those in Gath, and to those in Kimath, and to those in Saphek, and to those in Thimath, and to those in Carmel, and to those in the cities of Israel, and to those in the cities of Kenezi,

**30:30** and to those in Jerimuth, and to those in Beersheba, and to those in Noo,

**30:31** and to those in Hebron, and to all the places through which David had passed, he and his men.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — The Greek text names 'Keilah' as the city David entered, whereas the Hebrew reads 'Ziklag' in both halves of the verse.
- **30:8** — 'Raiding band' translates the Greek loanword geddour, a transliteration of the Hebrew term for a marauding troop (recurring in verses 15 and 23).
- **30:9** — The Greek reads 'four hundred men' here rather than 'six hundred' as in the Hebrew, while verse 10 continues with four hundred pursuing and two hundred remaining by the Brook Besor.
- **30:14** — The Greek reads 'Cholthi' and 'Gilboa' where the Hebrew reads 'Cherethites' and 'the Negev of Caleb.'
- **30:27** — In verses 27–30, the Greek list of cities diverges substantially from the Hebrew, naming Bethsur, Geththor, Ammadi, Saphi, Esthie, Gath, Kimath, Saphek, Thimath, Carmel, the cities of Israel and Kenezi, Jerimuth, Beersheba, and Noo (and similarly 'Brook Beana' in verse 21).
