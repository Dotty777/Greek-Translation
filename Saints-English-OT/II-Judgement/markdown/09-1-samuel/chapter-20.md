## Text

**20:1** Then David fled from Auath in Ramah, and came before Jonathan and said, "What have I done, and what is my iniquity, and how have I sinned before your father, that he seeks my life?"

**20:2** And Jonathan said to him, "Far be it from you! You shall by no means die. Behold, my father will by no means do a small thing and not uncover my ear; and why should my father conceal this matter? This is not so."

**20:3** And David answered Jonathan and said, "Your father surely knows that I have found favor in your eyes, and he has said that Jonathan must not know this, lest he be unwilling. But as the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, as he has said, the space between me and death is filled up."

**20:4** And Jonathan said to David, "What does your soul desire, and what shall I do for you?"

**20:5** And David said to Jonathan, "Behold now, tomorrow is the new moon, and I will surely not sit down to eat; so you shall send me away, and I will hide myself in the plain until evening.

**20:6** If your father indeed misses me, then you shall say, 'David earnestly asked leave of me to run to Bethlehem his city, because a sacrifice of the days is there for the whole tribe.'

**20:7** If he says thus, 'It is well,' then there is peace for your servant; but if he answers you harshly, know that evil is fully determined by him.

**20:8** And you shall show mercy to your servant, because you have brought your servant into a covenant of the Lord with yourself. Yet if there is iniquity in your servant, put me to death yourself; for why should you thus bring me to your father?"

**20:9** And Jonathan said, "Far be it from you! For if I surely know that evil is determined by my father to come upon you, even if it is not against your cities, I will tell you."

**20:10** Then David said to Jonathan, "Who will tell me if your father answers harshly?"

**20:11** And Jonathan said to David, "Go and remain in the field." So both of them went out into the field.

**20:12** Then Jonathan said to David, "The Lord God of Israel knows that I will sound out my father about this time on the third day; and behold, if there is good concerning David, and I do not send to you in the field,

**20:13** may God do so to Jonathan, and more also! For I will report the evil against you and uncover your ear, and I will send you away, and you shall depart in peace; and the Lord shall be with you, just as He was with my father.

**20:14** And while I am still living, you shall indeed show mercy to me; and if I surely die,

**20:15** you shall not cut off your mercy from my house forever; and if not, when the Lord cuts off the enemies of David, every one from the face of the earth,

**20:16** may the name of Jonathan be found out from the house of David; and may the Lord seek out the enemies of David."

**20:17** And Jonathan swore yet again to David, because he loved the soul of the one who loved him.

**20:18** And Jonathan said, "Tomorrow is the new moon, and you will be missed, because your seat will be missed.

**20:19** And you shall stay three days and watch carefully, and you shall come to your place where you hid yourself on the workday, and you shall sit beside that Ergab.

**20:20** And I will shoot three times with arrows, sending them forth toward Harmattarei.

**20:21** And behold, I will send the lad, saying, 'Come, find the arrow for me.' If I expressly say to the lad, 'Here is the arrow on this side of you; take it,' then come; for there is peace for you, and there is no word of the Lord, as the Lord lives.

**20:22** But if I say thus to the young man, 'Here is the arrow beyond you,' then go, for the Lord has sent you away.

**20:23** And as for the word which you and I have spoken, behold, the Lord is witness between me and you forever."

**20:24** So David hid himself in the field. And the new moon came, and the king came to the table to eat.

**20:25** And he sat upon his seat, as once and again, upon the seat by the wall; and he came before Jonathan, and Abner sat at Saul's side, and David's place was missed.

**20:26** Yet Saul spoke nothing on that day, for he said, "It appears to be an occurrence—he is not clean, because he has not been purified."

**20:27** And it came to pass on the day after the new moon, on the second day, that David's place was missed. And Saul said to Jonathan his son, "Why has the son of Jesse not come to the table, both yesterday and today?"

**20:28** And Jonathan answered Saul and said to him, "David asked leave of me to go as far as Bethlehem his city,

**20:29** and he said, 'Send me away now, for we have a sacrifice of the tribe in the city, and my brethren have commanded me. And now, if I have found favor in your eyes, let me get away now and see my brethren.' Therefore he has not come to the king's table."

**20:30** Then Saul was exceedingly furious with wrath against Jonathan, and he said to him, "Son of deserting maidens, do I not know that you are a partner with the son of Jesse to your own shame and to the shame of the uncovering of your mother?

**20:31** For all the days that the son of Jesse lives upon the earth, your kingdom shall not be established. Now therefore send and take the young man, for he is a son of death."

**20:32** And Jonathan answered Saul, "Why should he die? What has he done?"

**20:33** And Saul lifted up the spear against Jonathan to put him to death; and Jonathan knew that this evil was fully determined by his father, to put David to death.

**20:34** So Jonathan sprang up from the table in fierce wrath, and he ate no bread on the second day of the month, because his father had fully resolved against him.

**20:35** And it came to pass in the morning that Jonathan went out into the field, just as he had appointed for the testimony of David, and a little lad was with him.

**20:36** And he said to the lad, "Run, find for me the arrows which I shoot." And the lad ran, and he shot the arrow and sent it beyond.

**20:37** And the lad came to the place of the arrow which Jonathan had shot; and Jonathan cried out behind the young man and said, "There is the arrow beyond you!"

**20:38** And Jonathan cried out behind his lad, saying, "Make haste quickly, and do not stand still!" And Jonathan's lad gathered up the arrows to his master.

**20:39** And the lad knew nothing, except Jonathan and David.

**20:40** And Jonathan gave his weapons to his lad, and said to his lad, "Go, enter into the city."

**20:41** And as the lad entered, David arose from the Argab and fell upon his face and bowed down to him three times; and each kissed his companion, and each wept with his companion to a great extremity.

**20:42** And Jonathan said, "Go in peace, and as we both have sworn in the name of the Lord, saying, 'The Lord shall be witness between me and you, and between your seed forever.'"

**20:43** *(after `20:42`)* And David arose and departed, and Jonathan entered into the city.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — The Greek reads "the space between me and death is filled up" (empeplestai), reflecting a different reading from the Hebrew idiom "there is but a step between me and death."
- **20:16** — The Greek syntax of verses 15–16 ("may the name of Jonathan be found out from the house of David") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's statement that Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David.
- **20:19** — "Ergab" (and "Argab" in verse 41) transliterates a Hebrew term for a mound or cairn, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the stone Ezel" (v. 19) and "from beside the south" (v. 41).
- **20:20** — "Harmattarei" transliterates the Hebrew word for "target" as a proper name; in verse 21, "arrow" translates the rare Greek transliteration gouzan (Hebrew chets), while "arrows" elsewhere in the chapter renders schizai (lit. "splinters" or "shafts").
- **20:30** — The Greek reads "Son of deserting maidens" (korasion automolounton), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Son of a perverse, rebellious woman."
