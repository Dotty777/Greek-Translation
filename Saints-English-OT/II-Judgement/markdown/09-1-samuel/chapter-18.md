## Text

**18:6** And the dancing women came out from all the cities of Israel to meet David, with timbrels, with gladness, and with cymbals.

**18:7** And the women led the song and said, "Saul has struck down in his thousands, and David in his ten thousands."

**18:8** And the matter appeared evil in the eyes of Saul concerning this saying, and he said, "To David they have given the ten thousands, and to me they have given the thousands."

**18:9** And Saul eyed David with suspicion from that day and onward.

**18:12** And Saul was afraid before the face of David,

**18:13** and he removed him from himself and appointed him for himself as a commander of a thousand; and he went out and came in before the people.

**18:14** And David acted with understanding in all his ways, and the Lord was with him.

**18:15** And Saul saw that he acted with great understanding, and he stood in dread before his face.

**18:16** And all Israel and Judah loved David, because he came in and went out before the face of the people.

**18:20** Now Michal the daughter of Saul loved David; and it was reported to Saul, and it was right in his eyes.

**18:21** And Saul said, "I will give her to him, and she shall be a stumbling block to him." And the hand of the Philistines was upon Saul.

**18:22** And Saul commanded his servants, saying, "Speak secretly to David, saying, 'Behold, the king delights in you, and all his servants love you; now therefore become son-in-law to the king.'"

**18:23** And the servants of Saul spoke these words in the ears of David. And David said, "Is it a light thing in your eyes to become son-in-law to a king? And I am a lowly man and not held in honor."

**18:24** And the servants of Saul reported to him according to these words that David had spoken.

**18:25** And Saul said, "Thus you shall say to David, 'The king does not desire any dowry, except one hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to take vengeance on the enemies of the king.'" Now Saul planned to cast him into the hands of the Philistines.

**18:26** And the servants of Saul reported these words to David, and the word was right in the eyes of David to become son-in-law to the king.

**18:27** And David arose and went, he and his men, and struck down among the Philistines one hundred men; and he brought up their foreskins to the king, and became son-in-law to the king, and he gave him his daughter Michal as his wife.

**18:28** And Saul saw that the Lord was with David, and that all Israel loved him,

**18:29** and he stood in dread of David yet more.

## Translator Notes

- **18:20** — Greek Melchol is the regular Septuagint spelling of Michal; verses 10-11 and 17-19 are not present in the shorter Old Greek text.
- **18:21** — The Greek concludes the verse with "And the hand of the Philistines was upon Saul," where the Masoretic Text records Saul telling David a second time that he would become his son-in-law.
- **18:27** — The Greek reports that David struck down "one hundred men," matching Saul's demand in verse 25, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "two hundred men."
