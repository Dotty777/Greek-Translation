## Text

**15:1** In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah the son of Amaziah, king of Judah, began to reign.

**15:2** He was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Chalia of Jerusalem.

**15:3** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.

**15:4** Only he did not remove the high places; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**15:5** And the Lord touched the king, so that he was a leper until the day of his death, and he reigned in the house of Apphusoth. And Jonathan the king's son was over the house, judging the people of the land.

**15:6** Now the rest of the acts of Azariah, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**15:7** So Azariah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the City of David; and Jonathan his son reigned in his place.

**15:8** In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah the son of Jeroboam reigned over Israel in Samaria six months.

**15:9** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, as his fathers had done; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:10** And Shallum the son of Jabesh conspired against him, and they struck him down at Keblaam and put him to death; and he reigned in his place.

**15:11** Now the rest of the acts of Zechariah, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**15:12** This was the word of the Lord which He spoke to Jehu, saying, "Sons of the fourth generation shall sit for you upon the throne of Israel." And so it came to pass.

**15:13** And Shallum the son of Jabesh became king; and in the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Shallum reigned for days in Samaria.

**15:14** And Menahem the son of Gadi went up from Tharsila and came to Samaria, and struck down Shallum the son of Jabesh in Samaria, and put him to death.

**15:15** Now the rest of the acts of Shallum, and the conspiracy which he made, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**15:16** Then Menahem struck even Tirzah, and all who were in it, and its borders from Tirzah, because they did not open to him; so he struck it, and ripped open the women who were with child.

**15:17** In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem the son of Gadi reigned over Israel ten years in Samaria.

**15:18** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:19** In his days Phua king of the Assyrians came up against the land; and Menahem gave Phua a thousand talents of silver, that his hand might be with him.

**15:20** And Menahem exacted the silver from Israel, from every man mighty in strength, to give to the king of the Assyrians, fifty shekels from each man. So the king of the Assyrians turned back, and did not remain there in the land.

**15:21** Now the rest of the acts of Menahem, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**15:22** So Menahem slept with his fathers, and Pekahiah his son reigned in his place.

**15:23** In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah the son of Menahem reigned over Israel in Samaria two years.

**15:24** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:25** Then Pekah the son of Remaliah, his captain, conspired against him and struck him down in Samaria before the king's house, with Argob and Arieh with him; and with him were fifty men from the four hundred. And he put him to death, and reigned in his place.

**15:26** Now the rest of the acts of Pekahiah, and all that he did, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**15:27** In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah the son of Remaliah reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty years.

**15:28** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:29** In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Algathphellasar king of the Assyrians came and took Ain, and Abel and Thamaacha, and Anioch, and Kenez, and Hazor, and Gilead, and Galilee, all the land of Naphtali; and he carried them captive to the Assyrians.

**15:30** And Hoshea the son of Elah formed a conspiracy against Pekah the son of Remaliah, and struck him down and put him to death; and he reigned in his place in the twentieth year of Jotham the son of Ahaz.

**15:31** Now the rest of the acts of Pekah, and all that he did, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**15:32** In the second year of Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jonathan the son of Azariah, king of Judah, began to reign.

**15:33** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Erus, the daughter of Zadok.

**15:34** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that Uzziah his father had done.

**15:35** Only he did not remove the high places; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places. He built the Upper Gate of the house of the Lord.

**15:36** Now the rest of the acts of Jotham, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**15:37** In those days the Lord began to send out Rezin king of Syria and Pekah the son of Remaliah.

**15:38** So Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the City of David his father; and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — The Greek reads 'reigned' (Hebrew 'dwelt'), transliterates the Hebrew term for a separate house of isolation as 'Apphusoth', and names the king's son Jonathan (as also in 15:7, 32; cf. Jotham in 15:30, 36, 38).
- **15:10** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew phrase 'before the people' as a place name, Keblaam, and uses plural verbs for the conspirators' attack.
- **15:16** — The Greek reads 'Tirzah' in both places, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Menahem attacking Tiphsah from Tirzah.
- **15:19** — The Greek names the Assyrian king Phua (Hebrew Pul); similarly in 15:29 the Greek reads Algathphellasar (Hebrew Tiglath-pileser) along with the distinct town names Ain, Abel and Thamaacha, Anioch, and Kenez.
- **15:25** — The Greek reads 'fifty men from the four hundred' (Hebrew 'of the Gileadites'); in 15:30 the Greek also reads 'Jotham the son of Ahaz' (Hebrew 'Uzziah').
