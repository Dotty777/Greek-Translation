## Text

**8:1** Now Elisha spoke to the woman whose son he had restored to life, saying, "Arise and go, you and your household, and sojourn wherever you may sojourn; for the Lord has called a famine upon the land, and indeed it has come upon the land for seven years."

**8:2** So the woman arose and did according to the word of Elisha; and she and her household went and sojourned in the land of the Philistines for seven years.

**8:3** And it came to pass, after the end of the seven years, that the woman returned from the land of the Philistines into the city; and she came to cry out to the king concerning her house and concerning her fields.

**8:4** Now the king was speaking to Gehazi, the servant of Elisha the man of God, saying, "Tell me, please, all the great things that Elisha has done."

**8:5** And it came to pass, as he was recounting to the king how he had restored a dead son to life, that behold, the woman whose son Elisha had restored to life was crying out to the king concerning her house and concerning her fields. And Gehazi said, "My lord, O king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha restored to life."

**8:6** And the king asked the woman, and she recounted it to him. So the king assigned a certain eunuch to her, saying, "Restore all her produce of the field, from the day that she left the land until now."

**8:7** Then Elisha came to Damascus, and the son of Hader, king of Syria, was sick. And they reported to him, saying, "The man of God has come even here."

**8:8** And the king said to Hazael, "Take a present in your hand, and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the Lord through him, saying, 'Shall I recover from this sickness of mine?'"

**8:9** So Hazael went to meet him, and took a present in his hand, even all the good things of Damascus, the burden of forty camels, as gifts. And he came and stood before him, and said to Elisha, "Your son, the son of Hader, king of Syria, has sent me to you to inquire, saying, 'Shall I recover from this sickness of mine?'"

**8:10** And Elisha said, "Go, say, 'You shall surely live'; yet the Lord has shown me that you shall surely die."

**8:11** And he stood before his face, and fixed his countenance until he was ashamed; and the man of God wept.

**8:12** And Hazael said, "Why does my lord weep?" And he said, "Because I know all the evil that you will do to the children of Israel: their strongholds you will set on fire, and their chosen men you will slay with the sword, and their little ones you will dash in pieces, and their women with child you will rip open."

**8:13** And Hazael said, "Who is your servant, a dead dog, that he should do this thing?" And Elisha said, "The Lord has shown me you reigning over Israel."

**8:14** So he departed from Elisha and went in to his master, who said to him, "What did Elisha say to you?" And he said, "He told me, 'You shall surely live.'"

**8:15** And it came to pass on the next day that he took the coverlet, and dipped it in water, and spread it over his face, so that he died. And Hazael reigned in his place.

**8:16** In the fifth year of Joram the son of Ahab, king of Israel, while Jehoshaphat was king of Judah, Joram the son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, began to reign.

**8:17** He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem.

**8:18** And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.

**8:19** Yet the Lord was not willing to destroy Judah, for the sake of David His servant, as He had promised to give him a lamp all the days.

**8:20** In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and they made a king to reign over themselves.

**8:21** So Joram went up to Seior, and all the chariots with him. And it came to pass, when he arose, that he struck down Edom, who had surrounded him, and the commanders of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.

**8:22** Yet Edom has revolted from under the hand of Judah to this day. Then Senna revolted at that same time.

**8:23** Now the rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**8:24** So Joram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father. And Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

**8:25** In the twelfth year of Joram the son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah the son of Joram began to reign.

**8:26** Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Israel. And his mother's name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri, king of Israel.

**8:27** And he walked in the way of the house of Ahab, and did evil in the sight of the Lord, just as the house of Ahab had done.

**8:28** And he went with Joram the son of Ahab to war against Hazael, king of the foreigners, at Ramoth Gilead; and the Syrians struck Joram.

**8:29** Then King Joram returned to be healed in Israel from the wounds with which they had struck him at Ramoth, when he fought against Hazael, king of Syria. And Ahaziah the son of Joram went down to see Joram the son of Ahab in Israel, because he was sick.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The Greek renders the royal name Ben-Hadad literally as "son of Hader," reflecting a variant spelling of Hadad; in 8:8–9 "a present" translates the Greek loanword maana (from Hebrew minhah, gift or tribute).
- **8:10** — The Greek verb is second person singular ("you shall surely die"), maintaining direct address rather than shifting to the third person ("he shall surely die").
- **8:13** — The Greek reads "a dead dog" and "reigning over Israel" rather than "over Syria," as found in the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **8:17** — The Greek gives Joram's reign as forty years rather than eight; in 8:15 "coverlet" renders the Greek transliteration chabba, and 8:21–22 preserve the distinct Greek place names "Seior" and "Senna."
- **8:28** — The Greek reads "king of the foreigners" (allophylon, the usual Septuagint term for Philistines) rather than "king of Syria," and in 8:26 and 8:29 reads "Israel" where the Hebrew has "Jerusalem" (8:26) and "Jezreel" (8:29).
