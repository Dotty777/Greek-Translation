## Text

**5:1** Now Naaman, the commander of the host of Syria, was a great man before his lord and admired in person, because by him the Lord had given deliverance to Syria; and the man was mighty in strength, a leper.

**5:2** And Syria went out in raiding bands and took captive from the land of Israel a little maiden, and she was before the wife of Naaman.

**5:3** And she said to her mistress, "Oh, that my lord were before the prophet of God who is in Samaria! Then he would restore him from his leprosy."

**5:4** And she went in and told her lord and said, "Thus and thus spoke the maiden who is from the land of Israel."

**5:5** And the king of Syria said to Naaman, "Go, enter in, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel." So he departed and took in his hand ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of garments.

**5:6** And he brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, "And now, when this letter comes to you, behold, I have sent Naaman my servant to you, and you shall restore him from his leprosy."

**5:7** And it came to pass, when the king of Israel read the letter, that he tore his garments and said, "Am I God, to put to death and to make alive, that this man sends to me to restore a man from his leprosy? Only know now and see that this man is seeking a pretext against me."

**5:8** And it came to pass, when Elisha heard that the king of Israel had torn his garments, that he sent to the king of Israel, saying, "Why have you torn your garments? Let Naaman come now to me, and let him know that there is a prophet in Israel."

**5:9** So Naaman came with horse and chariot, and stood at the door of the house of Elisha.

**5:10** And Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, "Go and wash seven times in the Jordan, and your flesh shall be restored to you, and you shall be cleansed."

**5:11** And Naaman became angry, and departed and said, "Behold, I said, 'He will surely come out to me, and call upon the name of his God, and lay his hand upon the place, and remove the leprosy.'

**5:12** Are not Abana and Apharpha, the rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? May I not go and wash in them and be cleansed?" And he turned aside and departed in wrath.

**5:13** And his servants drew near and spoke to him, "If the prophet had spoken a great word to you, would you not do it? And now when he said to you, 'Wash and be cleansed'?"

**5:14** So Naaman went down and dipped himself in the Jordan seven times, according to the word of Elisha; and his flesh was restored like the flesh of a little child, and he was cleansed.

**5:15** And he returned to Elisha, he and all his company, and came and stood and said, "Behold, I know that there is no God in all the earth except in Israel; and now, take the blessing from your servant."

**5:16** And Elisha said, "As the Lord lives, before whom I stand, I will not take it." And he urged him to take it, but he refused.

**5:17** And Naaman said, "And if not, let there be given now to your servant a gomer, pairs of mules; for your servant will no longer offer burnt offering and sacrifice to other gods, except to the Lord.

**5:18** In this thing also may the Lord pardon your servant: when my lord goes into the house of Rimmon to bow down there, and he leans upon my hand, and I bow down in the house of Rimmon when he bows down in the house of Rimmon, may the Lord indeed pardon your servant in this matter."

**5:19** And Elisha said to Naaman, "Go in peace." And he departed from him for a debratha of the land.

**5:20** And Gehazi, the servant of Elisha, said, "Behold, my lord has spared this Naaman the Syrian, in not receiving from his hand what he brought; as the Lord lives, I will surely run after him and take something from him."

**5:21** So Gehazi pursued after Naaman; and Naaman saw him running after him, and turned back from the chariot to meet him.

**5:22** And he said, "Peace. My lord has sent me, saying, 'Behold, just now two young men from Mount Ephraim, of the sons of the prophets, have come to me; give them now a talent of silver and two changes of garments.'"

**5:23** And Naaman said, "Take two talents of silver." And he took them in two bags, and two changes of garments; and he laid them upon two of his servants, and they carried them before him.

**5:24** And they came to the dark place, and he took them from their hands and stored them in the house, and he sent the men away.

**5:25** And he himself went in and stood beside his lord. And Elisha said to him, "From where have you come, Gehazi?" And Gehazi said, "Your servant has not gone here or there."

**5:26** And Elisha said to him, "Did not my heart go with you when the man turned back from the chariot to meet you? And now you have received the silver, and now you have received the garments, and olive groves and vineyards, and sheep and oxen, and menservants and maidservants;

**5:27** and the leprosy of Naaman shall cling to you and to your seed forever." And he went out from his presence leprous as snow.

## Translator Notes

- **5:12** — "Apharpha" preserves the distinct Septuagint form of the Damascus river name (corresponding to Hebrew Pharpar).
- **5:17** — The Greek literally reads "a gomer, pairs of mules" (where "gomer" is a transliterated dry measure) and does not include the word "earth" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:19** — "Debratha" is an untranslated Greek transliteration of a Hebrew expression denoting a short stretch of distance.
- **5:24** — The Greek reads "to the dark place," interpreting the Hebrew term for a hill or fortified mound (Ophel) as "darkness."
- **5:26** — The Greek presents Elisha's words as a direct accusation ("And now you have received...") rather than the rhetorical question ("Is it a time to receive...?") of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
