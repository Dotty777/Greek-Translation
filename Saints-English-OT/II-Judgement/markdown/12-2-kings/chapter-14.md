## Text

**14:1** In the second year of Joash the son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, began to reign.

**14:2** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Joadim of Jerusalem.

**14:3** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, yet not as David his father; he did according to all that Joash his father had done.

**14:4** Only he did not remove the high places; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**14:5** Now it came to pass, when the kingdom was established in his hand, that he struck down his servants who had struck down his father.

**14:6** Yet he did not put to death the sons of the slayers, as it is written in the book of the laws of Moses, as the Lord commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die for sons, and sons shall not die for fathers, but rather each shall die in his own sins."

**14:7** He struck down ten thousand of Edom in Remele, and he captured the Rock in the battle, and called its name Kathoel, to this day.

**14:8** Then Amaziah sent messengers to Joash the son of Jehoahaz, the son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us look one another in the face."

**14:9** And Joash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thistle that is in Lebanon sent to the cedar that is in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' And the wild beasts of the field that were in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle.

**14:10** You have indeed struck down Idumea, and your heart has lifted you up. Glory in this, remaining in your house; and why do you contend in your mischief? You shall fall, both you and Judah with you."

**14:11** But Amaziah would not listen. So the king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah looked one another in the face at Beth-shemesh, in the land of Judah.

**14:12** And Judah was defeated before Israel, and every man fled to his tent.

**14:13** And Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah the son of Joash, the son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh; and he came to Jerusalem, and broke down four hundred cubits in the wall of Jerusalem, at the Gate of Ephraim even to the Corner Gate.

**14:14** And he took the gold and the silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord and in the treasuries of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

**14:15** Now the rest of the acts of Joash, all that he did in his might, and how he fought with Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**14:16** So Joash slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; and Jeroboam his son reigned in his place.

**14:17** And Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash the son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

**14:18** Now the rest of the acts of Amaziah and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**14:19** And they formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; yet they sent after him to Lachish, and put him to death there.

**14:20** And they brought him on horses, and he was buried at Jerusalem with his fathers in the City of David.

**14:21** And all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and he reigned in place of his father Amaziah.

**14:22** He built Elom and restored it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.

**14:23** In the fifteenth year of Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam the son of Joash reigned over Israel in Samaria forty-one years.

**14:24** And he did what was evil before the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**14:25** He removed the border of Israel from the entrance of Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, according to the word of the Lord God of Israel, which He had spoken by the hand of His servant Jonah the son of Amittai, the prophet who was from Gath-chober.

**14:26** For the Lord saw the affliction of Israel, that it was exceedingly bitter, and that those who were hemmed in were few, diminished, and forsaken, and there was no helper for Israel.

**14:27** And the Lord had not spoken to blot out the seed of Israel from under heaven, and He saved them from the hand of Jeroboam the son of Joash.

**14:28** Now the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, how he warred, and how he restored Damascus and Hamath to Judah in Israel, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**14:29** So Jeroboam slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel; and Azariah the son of Amaziah reigned in place of his father.

## Translator Notes

- **14:7** — The Greek reads Remele (an early corruption of 'Valley of Salt') and Kathoel (corresponding to Hebrew Joktheel).
- **14:13** — The Greek text names Amaziah's grandfather as Jehoahaz, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Ahaziah.
- **14:25** — The Greek verb literally reads 'removed' (probably a textual shortening of 'restored'), and spells Jonah's hometown Gath-chober (Hebrew Gath-hepher).
- **14:27** — The Greek reads 'the seed of Israel' (Hebrew 'name') and 'from the hand of Jeroboam' (Hebrew 'by the hand of Jeroboam').
- **14:29** — The Greek text here reads 'Azariah the son of Amaziah' rather than Jeroboam's son Zechariah (cf. 15:8), an early scribal anticipation of 15:1.
