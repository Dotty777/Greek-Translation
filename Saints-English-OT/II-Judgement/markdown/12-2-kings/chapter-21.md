## Text

**21:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Hephzibah.

**21:2** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, according to the abominations of the nations whom the Lord had removed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:3** And he turned back and built the high places which Hezekiah his father had torn down; and he set up an altar to Baal, and made groves just as Ahab king of Israel had done, and he worshiped all the host of heaven and served them.

**21:4** And he built an altar in the house of the Lord, of which He had said, "In Jerusalem I will place My name."

**21:5** And he built an altar for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.

**21:6** And he made his sons pass through the fire, and practiced divination and read omens, and appointed a medium and multiplied spiritists, to do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**21:7** And he placed the carved image of the grove in the house of which the Lord had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house in Jerusalem I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, and I will place My name forever,

**21:8** and I will not again cause the foot of Israel to be moved from the land which I gave to their fathers, those who will keep all that I have commanded, according to all the commandment which My servant Moses commanded."

**21:9** And they did not listen; and Manasseh led them astray to do evil in the sight of the Lord beyond the nations whom the Lord had destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:10** And the Lord spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets, saying,

**21:11** "Because of all these wicked abominations that Manasseh king of Judah has committed, beyond all that the Amorite before him did, and because he has also caused Judah to sin with their idols,

**21:12** not so; thus says the Lord God of Israel: 'Behold, I am bringing calamities upon Jerusalem and upon Judah, so that both ears of everyone who hears shall ring;

**21:13** and I will stretch over Jerusalem the measure of Samaria and the plummet of the house of Ahab; and I will wipe Jerusalem clean just as an alabaster jar is wiped clean when it is wiped out and overturned upon its face;

**21:14** and I will cast off the remnant of My inheritance, and deliver them into the hands of their enemies, and they shall become a plunder and a spoil to all their enemies,

**21:15** because of all the evil he has done in My sight, and they have been provoking Me to anger from the day that I brought their fathers out of Egypt, even until this day.'"

**21:16** Moreover, Manasseh also shed very much innocent blood, until he had filled Jerusalem from one end to the other, apart from their sins with which he caused Judah to sin, in doing evil in the sight of the Lord.

**21:17** And the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and all that he did, and his sin which he committed, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**21:18** And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza; and Amos his son reigned in his place.

**21:19** Amos was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Meshullam, the daughter of Haruz from Jesebal.

**21:20** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, just as Manasseh his father had done.

**21:21** And he walked in every way that his father had walked, and he served the idols which his father had served, and worshiped them.

**21:22** And he forsook the Lord God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the Lord.

**21:23** And the servants of Amos conspired against him, and put the king himself to death in his house.

**21:24** And the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amos; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

**21:25** And the rest of the acts of Amos, all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**21:26** And they buried him in his tomb in the garden of Uzza; and Josiah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Greek reads plural "his sons" (Hebrew: "his son") and contains the difficult reading ἐλλήν (here rendered "a medium"), a manuscript variant for θελητήν used in the Septuagint for a necromancer or medium.
- **21:12** — "Not so" faithfully translates Greek οὐχ οὕτως, a characteristic kaige-Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew word for "therefore" (laken), also seen in 22:20.
- **21:13** — The Greek uses the vivid image of an alabaster jar (ἀλάβαστρος) wiped clean and overturned, where the Hebrew text speaks of a dish or bowl.
- **21:18** — Here and throughout verses 19-25 the Greek consistently names Manasseh's son Amos (Ἀμώς) rather than Hebrew Amon; similarly, verse 19 preserves the distinct Greek forms Meshullam and Jesebal (Hebrew: Meshullemeth and Jotbah).
