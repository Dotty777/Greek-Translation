## Text

**2:1** And it came to pass, when the Lord was about to take up Elijah in a whirlwind as it were into heaven, that Elijah and Elisha went forth from Jericho.

**2:2** And Elijah said to Elisha, "Behold now, stay here, for God has sent me as far as Bethel." And Elisha said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So he came to Bethel.

**2:3** And the sons of the prophets who were at Bethel came to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the Lord is taking your master away from over your head today?" And he said, "I also know it; keep silent."

**2:4** Then Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, I pray, for the Lord has sent me to Jericho." And he said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not forsake you." So they came to Jericho.

**2:5** And the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the Lord is taking your master away from over your head?" And he said, "Yes, I too know it; keep silent."

**2:6** And Elijah said to him, "Stay here, I pray, for the Lord has sent me as far as the Jordan." And Elisha said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not forsake you." So the two of them went on.

**2:7** And fifty men of the sons of the prophets also stood opposite at a distance, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

**2:8** And Elijah took his sheepskin mantle, rolled it up, and struck the water; and the water was divided this way and that, so that the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

**2:9** And it came to pass, when they had crossed over, that Elijah said to Elisha, "What shall I do for you before I am taken up from you?" And Elisha said, "Let a double portion of your spirit, I pray, come upon me."

**2:10** And Elijah said, "You have asked a hard thing. If you see me being taken up from you, then it shall be so; but if not, it shall not be."

**2:11** And it came to pass, as they were going along—walking and talking—that behold, there was a chariot of fire and horses of fire, and it separated the two of them; and Elijah was taken up in a whirlwind as it were into heaven.

**2:12** And Elisha saw it, and he cried out, "Father, father, the chariot of Israel and its horseman!" And he did not see him anymore; and he took hold of his own garments and tore them into two pieces.

**2:13** And he lifted up the sheepskin mantle of Elijah which had fallen from upon Elisha, and stood by the bank of the Jordan.

**2:14** And he took the sheepskin mantle of Elijah, which had fallen from upon him, and struck the water and said, "Where is the God of Elijah, Appho?" And he struck the waters, and they were parted this way and that; and Elisha crossed over.

**2:15** And the sons of the prophets and those who were in Jericho saw him from opposite, and said, "The spirit of Elijah rests upon Elisha." And they came to meet him and bowed down to him to the ground.

**2:16** And they said to him, "Behold now, there are with your servants fifty men, men of strength. Let them go now and seek your master, lest perhaps the Spirit of the Lord has found him and cast him into the Jordan, or upon one of the mountains, or upon one of the hills." And Elisha said, "You shall not send them."

**2:17** Yet they urged him until he was ashamed, and he said, "Send them." So they sent fifty men, and they searched for three days, and did not find him.

**2:18** And he was dwelling in Jericho; and Elisha said, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

**2:19** And the men of the city said to Elisha, "Behold, the habitation of the city is good, as our lord sees, but the waters are bad and the land is barren."

**2:20** And Elisha said, "Bring me a new water jar, and put salt in it." And they brought it to him.

**2:21** Then Elisha went out to the outlet of the waters and cast salt there, and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'I have healed these waters; from them there shall no longer be death or barrenness.'"

**2:22** So the waters have been healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha which he spoke.

**2:23** And he went up from there to Bethel; and as he was going up along the road, little boys came out from the city and mocked him, and said to him, "Go up, baldhead, go up!"

**2:24** And he turned back behind them and saw them, and cursed them in the name of the Lord. And behold, two bears came out of the woods and tore apart forty-two boys from among them.

**2:25** And he went from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Greek reads ex Iereicho ("from Jericho") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "from Gilgal," and adds hos ("as it were") before "into heaven" (also in 2:11).
- **2:13** — Greek reads epanothen Eleisaie ("from upon Elisha"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "from upon him" (referring to Elijah).
- **2:14** — Greek leaves the Hebrew phrase aph-hu ("even he") untranslated as the transliteration appho ("Appho"), incorporating it into Elisha's invocation; the Greek also records Elisha striking the water twice.
- **2:16** — Greek reads heuren ("has found him") and adds "into the Jordan" alongside "upon one of the mountains, or upon one of the hills."
