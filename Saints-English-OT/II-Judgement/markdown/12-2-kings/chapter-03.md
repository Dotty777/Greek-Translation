## Text

**3:1** And Joram the son of Ahab became king in Israel in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

**3:2** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, yet not like his father and not like his mother; for he removed the pillars of Baal which his father had made.

**3:3** Nevertheless he clung to the sin of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel sin; he did not depart from it.

**3:4** Now Mesha king of Moab was a noked, and he rendered to the king of Israel in the rebellion one hundred thousand lambs and one hundred thousand rams with their fleeces.

**3:5** And it came to pass, after the death of Ahab, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

**3:6** So King Joram went out from Samaria on that day and mustered Israel.

**3:7** And he went and sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me against Moab to battle?" And he said, "I will go up; as I am, so are you, as my people, so are your people, as my horses, so are your horses."

**3:8** And he said, "By which way shall I go up?" And he said, "By the way of the wilderness of Edom."

**3:9** So the king of Israel went, and the king of Judah, and the king of Edom, and they marched on a roundabout journey of seven days; and there was no water for the camp nor for the livestock that followed at their feet.

**3:10** And the king of Israel said, "Alas! For the Lord has called the three kings passing along to deliver them into the hand of Moab."

**3:11** And Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not a prophet of the Lord here, that we may inquire of the Lord by him?" And one of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, "Here is Elisha the son of Jehoshaphat, who poured water on the hands of Elijah."

**3:12** And Jehoshaphat said, "There is a word with him." So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah and the king of Edom went down to him.

**3:13** And Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father." And the king of Israel said to him, "No, for the Lord has called the three kings to deliver them into the hands of Moab."

**3:14** And Elisha said, "As the Lord of hosts lives, before whom I stand, were it not that I regard the presence of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would neither look toward you nor see you.

**3:15** And now, bring me a musician." And it came to pass, as the musician played, that the hand of the Lord came upon him.

**3:16** And he said, "Thus says the Lord: 'Make this stream-bed full of trenches upon trenches.'

**3:17** For thus says the Lord: 'You shall not see wind, and you shall not see rain, yet this stream-bed shall be filled with water, and you shall drink—you, and your herds, and your beasts.

**3:18** And this too is a light thing in the sight of the Lord; and I will deliver Moab into your hand.

**3:19** And you shall strike every fortified city, and every good tree you shall cut down, and all springs of water you shall stop up, and every good portion of land you shall ruin with stones.'"

**3:20** And it came to pass in the morning, as the sacrifice was being offered up, that behold, waters came by the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.

**3:21** And all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them; and they cried out, every one of them girded with a belt, and said, "Alas!" And they took their stand upon the border.

**3:22** And they rose early in the morning, and the sun shone upon the waters; and Moab saw the waters opposite them as red as blood.

**3:23** And they said, "This is blood of the sword! The kings have fought against one another, and each man has struck his neighbor. And now, to the spoil, Moab!"

**3:24** So they came to the camp of Israel; and Israel rose up and struck Moab, and they fled before them. And they entered, advancing and striking Moab.

**3:25** And they tore down the cities; and upon every good portion of land each man cast his stone and filled it, and they stopped up every spring, and they cut down every good tree, until they left the stones of the wall torn down. And the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

**3:26** And when the king of Moab saw that the battle was too fierce for him, he took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword to break through to the king of Edom; but they could not.

**3:27** Then he took his firstborn son, who reigned in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering upon the wall; and great remorse came upon Israel. So they departed from him and returned to the land.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — Greek leaves the Hebrew occupational term noqed ("sheep-breeder") untranslated (rendered here as "noked") and includes the phrase en te epanastasei ("in the rebellion").
- **3:11** — Greek gives Elisha's patronymic as huios Iosaphath ("son of Jehoshaphat"), differing from the Hebrew text's "son of Shaphat."
- **3:21** — Greek reads aneboesan ("they cried out") and adds kai eipon O ("and said, 'Alas!'") before the Moabites take their stand at the border.
- **3:25** — Greek renders tous lithous tou toichou katheremenous ("the stones of the wall torn down"), interpreting as a common noun phrase what appears in the Hebrew text as the place-name Kir-hareseth.
- **3:27** — Greek reads metamelos megas ("great remorse"), expressing regret or revulsion on Israel's part rather than the Hebrew text's "great wrath."
