## Text

**9:1** Now Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets, and said to him, "Gird up your loins, and take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth Gilead.

**9:2** And when you arrive there, look there for Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat, the son of Nimshi, and go in and make him rise up from among his brethren, and bring him into an inner chamber.

**9:3** Then take the flask of oil, and pour it on his head, and say, 'Thus says the Lord: I have anointed you king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee, and do not linger."

**9:4** So the young man, the prophet, went to Ramoth Gilead.

**9:5** And when he came, behold, the commanders of the army were sitting there; and he said, "I have a word for you, O commander." And Jehu said, "For which one of us all?" And he said, "For you, O commander."

**9:6** Then he arose and went into the house. And he poured the oil on his head, and said to him, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'I have anointed you king over the people of the Lord, over Israel.

**9:7** And you shall utterly destroy the house of Ahab your master from before your face, and you shall avenge the blood of My servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the Lord, at the hand of Jezebel,

**9:8** and at the hand of all the house of Ahab; and you shall utterly destroy from the house of Ahab every male, both confined and forsaken in Israel.

**9:9** And I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah.

**9:10** And the dogs shall devour Jezebel in the portion of Israel, and there shall be none to bury her.'" Then he opened the door and fled.

**9:11** Then Jehu came out to the servants of his master, and they said to him, "Is it peace? Why did this madman come in to you?" And he said to them, "You know the man and his babbling."

**9:12** And one said, "False! Tell us now." And Jehu said to them, "Thus and thus he spoke to me, saying"—and he said—"Thus says the Lord: 'I have anointed you king over Israel.'"

**9:13** And when they heard it, they hastened, and each man took his garment and placed it beneath him on the top of the steps; and they blew the horn and proclaimed, "Jehu is king!"

**9:14** So Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat, the son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Now Joram himself had been keeping watch at Ramoth Gilead, he and all Israel, because of Hazael, king of Syria.

**9:15** But King Joram had returned to be healed in Israel from the wounds with which the Syrians had struck him when they fought against Hazael, king of Syria. And Jehu said, "If your soul is with me, let no fugitive go forth from the city to go and report it in Israel."

**9:16** So Jehu rode and went, and came down to Israel; for Joram, king of Israel, was being healed in Israel from the arrow wounds with which the Arameans had shot him at Ramoth in the war against Hazael, king of Syria, because he was mighty and a man of valor. And Ahaziah, king of Judah, had come down to see Joram.

**9:17** Now the watchman went up upon the tower of Israel, and saw the dust cloud of Jehu as he approached, and said, "I see a cloud of dust." And Joram said, "Take a horseman and send him before them, and let him say, 'Is it peace?'"

**9:18** So the horseman went to meet them, and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" And Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Turn behind me." And the watchman reported, saying, "The messenger reached them, and has not returned."

**9:19** Then he sent a second horseman, and he came to him and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" And Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Turn behind me."

**9:20** And the watchman reported, saying, "He reached them, and has not returned; and the driver was driving Jehu the son of Nimshi, for it was in a frenzy."

**9:21** Then Joram said, "Harness!" And he harnessed a chariot. So Joram, king of Israel, and Ahaziah, king of Judah, went out, each man in his chariot; and they went out to meet Jehu, and found him in the portion of Naboth the Israelite.

**9:22** And it came to pass, when Joram saw Jehu, that he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" And Jehu said, "What peace, while the harlotries of Jezebel your mother and her many sorceries still remain?"

**9:23** Then Joram turned his hands around and fled, and said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**9:24** Now Jehu filled his hand with the bow and struck Joram between his arms; and his arrow went out through his heart, and he sank down upon his knees.

**9:25** Then he said to Bidkar his captain, "Cast him into the portion of the field of Naboth the Israelite; for I remember how I and you were riding in pairs behind Ahab his father, when the Lord took up this burden against him:

**9:26** 'Surely I saw yesterday the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons, says the Lord; and I will repay him in this portion, says the Lord.' Now therefore, take him up now and cast him into the portion, according to the word of the Lord."

**9:27** When Ahaziah, king of Judah, saw this, he fled by the way of Bethan. And Jehu pursued after him and said, "Him also!" And he struck him at the chariot at the ascent of Gai, which is Ibleam; and he fled to Megiddo, and died there.

**9:28** And his servants placed him upon the chariot and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb in the city of David.

**9:29** And in the eleventh year of Joram, king of Israel, Ahaziah had begun to reign over Judah.

**9:30** Now Jehu came to Israel; and Jezebel heard of it, and she painted her eyes and adorned her head, and looked out through the window.

**9:31** And as Jehu entered into the city, she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of his master?"

**9:32** And he lifted up his face toward the window and saw her, and said, "Who are you? Come down with me!" And two eunuchs looked down to him.

**9:33** And he said, "Cast her down!" So they cast her down; and some of her blood was spattered upon the wall and upon the horses, and they trampled her underfoot.

**9:34** And he went in and ate and drank, and said, "See now to this accursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

**9:35** So they went to bury her, but they found nothing of her except the skull, and the feet, and the palms of the hands.

**9:36** Therefore they returned and told him. And he said, "This is the word of the Lord, which He spoke by the hand of Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 'In the portion of Israel the dogs shall devour the flesh of Jezebel;

**9:37** and the carcass of Jezebel shall be as refuse upon the face of the field in the portion of Israel, so that they shall not say, Jezebel.'"

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — The Greek uses second person verbs ("you shall avenge" in 9:7 and "you shall utterly destroy" in 9:8) rather than first person; in 9:8 "every male" renders the literal Greek idiom "one who urinates against the wall."
- **9:10** — Here and throughout the chapter (9:15–17, 21, 25, 30, 36–37), the Greek reads "Israel" and "Israelite" where the Hebrew text reads "Jezreel" and "Jezreelite"; in 9:13 "the top of the steps" renders the Greek transliteration garem.
- **9:16** — The Greek preserves an expanded account of Joram's convalescence, specifying his arrow wounds from the Arameans at Ramoth and describing him as "mighty and a man of valor."
- **9:20** — Following the Greek wording faithfully, the watchman reports that the driver was driving Jehu "in a frenzy" rather than comparing the style of driving to Jehu's.
- **9:27** — The Greek preserves the distinct place names "Bethan" and "Gai, which is Ibleam"; in 9:32 the Greek uniquely adds "and saw her" and renders Jehu's words as "Who are you? Come down with me!"
