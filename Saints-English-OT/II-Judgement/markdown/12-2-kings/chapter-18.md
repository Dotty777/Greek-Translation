## Text

**18:1** And it came to pass in the third year of Hoshea, son of Elah, king of Israel, that Hezekiah, son of Ahaz, king of Judah, became king.

**18:2** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Abu, the daughter of Zechariah.

**18:3** And he did what was upright in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.

**18:4** He removed the high places, and shattered the pillars, and utterly destroyed the groves and the bronze serpent that Moses had made; for until those days the children of Israel were burning incense to it, and he called it Nesthali.

**18:5** He put his hope in the Lord God of Israel, and after him there arose none like him among the kings of Judah, nor among those who were before him;

**18:6** and he clung to the Lord; he did not turn away from following Him, and he kept His commandments, all that He had commanded Moses.

**18:7** And the Lord was with him, and in all things that he did he acted with understanding; and he rebelled against the king of the Assyrians and did not serve him.

**18:8** He struck down the Philistines as far as Gaza and as far as its border, from the tower of the watchmen even to the fortified city.

**18:9** And it came to pass in the fourth year of King Hezekiah, which was the seventh year of Hoshea, son of Elah, king of Israel, that Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians came up against Samaria and laid siege against it.

**18:10** And he captured it at the end of three years; in the sixth year of Hezekiah, which was the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel, Samaria was taken.

**18:11** And the king of the Assyrians carried Samaria away captive to the Assyrians, and placed them in Halah, and in Habor, the river of Gozan, and in Ore of the Medes,

**18:12** because they did not heed the voice of the Lord their God, but transgressed His covenant, even all that Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded; and they did not listen, nor did they do them.

**18:13** And in the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib king of the Assyrians came up against the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.

**18:14** And Hezekiah king of Judah sent messengers to the king of the Assyrians at Lachish, saying, "I have sinned; turn away from me. Whatever you lay upon me, I will bear." And the king of the Assyrians imposed upon Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.

**18:15** And Hezekiah gave all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord and in the treasuries of the king's house.

**18:16** At that time Hezekiah cut off the doors of the temple and the supports that Hezekiah king of Judah had overlaid with gold, and gave them to the king of the Assyrians.

**18:17** And the king of the Assyrians sent Thanthan and Raphis and Rabshakeh from Lachish to King Hezekiah with a mighty army against Jerusalem; and they went up and came to Jerusalem, and they stood by the aqueduct of the upper pool, which is on the highway of the fuller's field.

**18:18** And they cried out to Hezekiah, and there came to him Eliakim the son of Hilkiah, the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and Jehoshaphat the recorder.

**18:19** And Rabshakeh said to them, "Say now to Hezekiah, Thus says the great king, the king of the Assyrians: What is this confidence in which you have trusted?

**18:20** You have said—yet they are mere words of the lips—'Counsel and power for war.' Now therefore, in whom have you trusted, that you have rebelled against me?

**18:21** Now behold, you have trusted for yourself upon this bruised staff of reed, upon Egypt; if a man leans upon it, it will enter into his hand and pierce it. So is Pharaoh king of Egypt to all who trust in him.

**18:22** And since you have said to me, 'We have trusted in the Lord God,' has not this very Hezekiah removed His high places and His altars, and said to Judah and to Jerusalem, 'Before this altar you shall worship in Jerusalem'?

**18:23** And now, mingle yourselves with my lord the king of the Assyrians, and I will give you two thousand horses, if you are able on your part to set riders upon them.

**18:24** And how will you turn back the face of a single governor among the least of my lord's servants? Yet you have set your hope for yourself on Egypt for chariots and horsemen.

**18:25** And now, have we come up without the Lord against this place to destroy it? The Lord said to me, 'Go up against this land and destroy it.'"

**18:26** And Eliakim the son of Hilkiah, and Shebna, and Joash said to Rabshakeh, "Speak now to your servants in the Syrian tongue; we understand it, and do not speak with us in the Judean tongue; and why do you speak in the ears of the people who are on the wall?"

**18:27** And Rabshakeh said to them, "Has my lord sent me to your lord and to you to speak these words? Is it not to the men sitting upon the wall, that they may eat their own dung and drink their own urine together with you?"

**18:28** Then Rabshakeh stood and cried out with a loud voice in the Judean tongue, and he spoke and said, "Hear the words of the great king of the Assyrians.

**18:29** Thus says the king: Do not let Hezekiah lift you up with words, for he shall by no means be able to deliver you out of his hand.

**18:30** And do not let Hezekiah cause you to hope in the Lord, saying, 'The Lord will surely deliver; this city shall by no means be given into the hand of the king of the Assyrians.'

**18:31** Do not listen to Hezekiah, for thus says the king of the Assyrians: Make a blessing with me and come out to me, and each man shall drink of his own vine, and each man shall eat of his own fig tree, and shall drink water from his own cistern,

**18:32** until I come and take you to a land like your own land, a land of grain and wine, and of bread and vineyards, a land of oil-bearing olive trees and honey; and you shall live and shall by no means die. And do not listen to Hezekiah, for he deceives you, saying, 'The Lord will rescue you.'

**18:33** Have the gods of the nations truly rescued, each one his own land, out of the hand of the king of the Assyrians?

**18:34** Where is the god of Hamath and Arphal? Where is the god of Sepharumain? And have they indeed delivered Samaria out of my hand?

**18:35** Who among all the gods of the lands are those who have delivered their lands out of my hand, that the Lord should deliver Jerusalem out of my hand?"

**18:36** And they kept silent and did not answer him a word, for it was the king's commandment, saying, "You shall not answer him."

**18:37** Then Eliakim the son of Hilkiah, the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and Joash the son of Shaphan, the recorder, came in to Hezekiah with their garments torn, and reported to him the words of Rabshakeh.

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — The Greek text gives the name of Hezekiah's mother as Abu (Abou), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Abi.
- **18:4** — The Greek text calls the bronze serpent Nesthali (where the Hebrew reads Nehushtan), renders the Asherah poles as groves, and places both the groves and the serpent under the single verb utterly destroyed.
- **18:11** — The Greek states that the king carried away Samaria (rather than Israel) and reads Ore of the Medes, a proper name or reading of mountains where the Hebrew reads cities of the Medes.
- **18:17** — The Greek renders the first two Assyrian official titles as Thanthan and Raphis, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Tartan and Rabsaris.
- **18:18** — The Greek identifies the recorder here as Jehoshaphat, and later as Joash (v. 26) and Joash the son of Shaphan (v. 37), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Joah the son of Asaph.
- **18:23** — Mingle yourselves follows the Greek verb michthete, reflecting a Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew root for associate or mingle rather than give pledges or make a wager.
- **18:34** — The Greek has singular god in both questions, gives the place names as Arphal and Sepharumain (for Hebrew Arpad and Sepharvaim), and does not include Hena and Ivvah.
