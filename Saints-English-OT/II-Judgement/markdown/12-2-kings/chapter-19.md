## Text

**19:1** And it came to pass, when King Hezekiah heard it, that he tore his garments and covered himself with sackcloth, and entered into the house of the Lord.

**19:2** And he sent Eliakim the steward and Somnas the scribe, and the elders of the priests clothed in sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz.

**19:3** And he said to him, "Thus says Hezekiah: 'This day is a day of affliction and rebuke and provocation, for children have come to the birth pangs, and there is no strength in her who is giving birth.

**19:4** It may be that the Lord your God will hear all the words of Rabshakeh, whom his master, the king of the Assyrians, sent to reproach the living God and to blaspheme with the words that the Lord your God has heard; and you shall lift up a prayer for the remnant that is found.'"

**19:5** And the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

**19:6** And Isaiah said to them, "Thus you shall say to your master: 'Thus says the Lord: Do not be afraid because of the words that you have heard, with which the young servants of the king of the Assyrians have blasphemed.

**19:7** Behold, I am putting a spirit in him, and he shall hear a report and return to his own land; and I will strike him down by the sword in his own land.'"

**19:8** Then Rabshakeh returned and found the king of the Assyrians warring against Lomna, for he had heard that he had departed from Lachish.

**19:9** And he heard concerning Thara king of the Ethiopians, saying, "Behold, he has come out to make war with you." So he returned and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,

**19:10** "Do not let your God, in whom you trust, delude you, saying, 'Jerusalem shall by no means be delivered into the hands of the king of the Assyrians.'

**19:11** Behold, you have heard all that the kings of the Assyrians have done to all the lands, devoting them to destruction; and shall you be delivered?

**19:12** Did the gods of the nations deliver them? Did not my fathers destroy both Gozan and Haran, and Rapheis, and the sons of Eden who were in Thaesthen?

**19:13** Where is the king of Math and the king of Arpad? And where are Sepharvaim, Anes, and Oudou?"

**19:14** And Hezekiah received the letters from the hand of the messengers and read them; and Hezekiah went up to the house of the Lord and spread them out before the Lord.

**19:15** And he said, "O Lord, God of Israel, who sit upon the cherubim, You alone are God in all the kingdoms of the earth; You made heaven and earth.

**19:16** Incline Your ear, O Lord, and hear; open Your eyes, O Lord, and see; and hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to reproach the living God.

**19:17** For truly, O Lord, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the nations,

**19:18** and have cast their gods into the fire; for they are not gods, but rather the works of men's hands, wood and stone, and they have destroyed them.

**19:19** And now, O Lord our God, save us from his hand, and all the kingdoms of the earth shall know that You alone are the Lord God."

**19:20** And Isaiah the son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, "Thus says the Lord, the God of hosts, the God of Israel: 'What you have prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib king of the Assyrians, I have heard.'

**19:21** This is the word that the Lord has spoken against him: 'The virgin daughter of Zion has despised you and mocked you; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at you.

**19:22** Whom have you reproached and blasphemed? And against whom have you raised your voice and lifted up your eyes on high? Against the Holy One of Israel.

**19:23** By the hand of your messengers you have reproached your Lord, and you have said, "With the multitude of my chariots I will ascend to the height of the mountains, the flanks of Lebanon; and I cut down the stature of its cedar, the choicest of its cypresses; and he came into the midst of the forest and of Carmel.

**19:24** I cooled myself and drank foreign waters, and with the sole of my foot I dried up all the rivers of siege."

**19:25** I formed it, I gathered it; and it came to be for upheavals from warlike dwellings, fortified cities.

**19:26** And those who dwell in them grew weak of hand; they stumbled and were put to shame. They became grass of the field or green herb, grass of the housetops, and a trampling before one who stands.

**19:27** And I know your sitting down, and your going out, and your coming in, and your fury against Me.

**19:28** Because you have raged against Me, and your insolence has come up into My ears, I will put My hooks in your nostrils and a bridle in your lips, and I will turn you back by the way by which you came.'

**19:29** And this shall be the sign to you: Eat this year what grows of itself, and in the second year what springs up; and in the third year there shall be sowing and reaping and planting of vineyards, and you shall eat their fruit.

**19:30** And that which has escaped of the house of Judah, that which remains, shall again take root downward and bear fruit upward.

**19:31** For out of Jerusalem shall go forth a remnant, and the survivor out of Mount Zion. The zeal of the Lord of hosts shall perform this.

**19:32** Not so! Thus says the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians: 'He shall not enter into this city, nor shoot an arrow there, nor shall a shield come before him, nor shall he pour out a siege mound against it.

**19:33** By the way that he came, by it he shall return; and into this city he shall not enter,' says the Lord.

**19:34** 'And I will defend this city for My own sake and for the sake of David My servant.'"

**19:35** And it came to pass by night that the angel of the Lord went out and struck in the camp of the Assyrians one hundred and eighty-five thousand; and when they rose early in the morning, behold, they were all dead bodies.

**19:36** So Sennacherib king of the Assyrians departed and went away and returned, and dwelt in Nineveh.

**19:37** And it came to pass, as he was worshiping in the house of Esdrach his god, that Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons struck him down with the sword; and they escaped into the land of Ararat. And Asordan his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek text uses the distinct name form Somnas (Heb. Shebna), and similarly preserves distinctive Septuagint name forms throughout the chapter: Lomna (v. 8), Thara (v. 9), Rapheis and Thaesthen (v. 12), Math, Anes, and Oudou (v. 13), and Esdrach and Asordan (v. 37).
- **19:23** — The Greek reads "your Lord" (with the possessive pronoun) and shifts to the third person ("he came into the midst of the forest and of Carmel"); in v. 24, "I cooled myself" reflects a reading of the Hebrew root as "be cool" rather than "dig."
- **19:25** — The Septuagint here is markedly shorter and differently phrased than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting the opening question ("Have you not heard...") and reading "for upheavals from warlike dwellings, fortified cities."
- **19:32** — The Greek reads "Not so!" (ouch houtos), taking the Hebrew consonants lkn as "not so" rather than "therefore."
