## Text

**6:1** And the sons of the prophets said to Elisha, "Behold now, the place in which we dwell before you is too narrow for us.

**6:2** Let us go now to the Jordan, and let us take from there each man one beam, and let us prepare for ourselves there to dwell there." And he said, "Go."

**6:3** And one of them said, "Kindly come with your servants." And he said, "I will go."

**6:4** So he went with them, and they came to the Jordan and were cutting down the trees.

**6:5** And behold, as one of them was felling the beam, the iron head fell out into the water; and he cried out, "Alas, master! And it is hidden!"

**6:6** And the man of God said, "Where did it fall?" And he showed him the place; and he broke off a piece of wood and cast it there, and the iron head floated.

**6:7** And he said, "Lift it up for yourself." So he stretched out his hand and took it.

**6:8** Now the king of Syria was making war against Israel; and he took counsel with his servants, saying, "In such and such a place, Elimoni, I will encamp."

**6:9** And Elisha sent to the king of Israel, saying, "Beware that you do not pass through this place, for there Syria is hidden."

**6:10** And the king of Israel sent to the place of which Elisha had told him, and he kept guard from there not once nor twice.

**6:11** And the soul of the king of Syria was greatly agitated concerning this matter; and he called his servants and said to them, "Will you not tell me who betrays me to the king of Israel?"

**6:12** And one of his servants said, "No, my lord, O king; for Elisha, the prophet who is in Israel, declares to the king of Israel all the words that you speak in the inner room of your bedchamber."

**6:13** And he said, "Go and see where this man is, and I will send and take him." And they told him, saying, "Behold, he is in Dothan."

**6:14** So he sent there horses and chariots and a great force, and they came by night and surrounded the city.

**6:15** And the attendant of Elisha rose early in the morning and went out; and behold, a force was surrounding the city, both horses and chariots. And the young servant said to him, "Alas, master! What shall we do?"

**6:16** And Elisha said, "Do not fear, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them."

**6:17** And Elisha prayed and said, "O Lord, open the eyes of the young servant, that he may see." And the Lord opened his eyes, and he saw; and behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.

**6:18** And they came down to him; and he prayed to the Lord and said, "Strike now this nation with blindness." And He struck them with blindness according to the word of Elisha.

**6:19** And Elisha said to them, "This is not the city, nor is this the way; come after me, and I will lead you to the man whom you seek." And he led them away to Samaria.

**6:20** And it came to pass, when they had entered into Samaria, that Elisha said, "Open now, O Lord, their eyes, that they may see." And He opened their eyes, and they saw; and behold, they were in the midst of Samaria.

**6:21** And the king of Israel said, when he saw them, "Shall I surely strike them down, father?"

**6:22** And he said, "You shall not strike them down, unless you strike those whom you have taken captive with your sword and your bow. Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and let them depart to their master."

**6:23** So he set before them a great feast, and they ate and drank; and he sent them away to their master. And the raiding bands of Syria no longer came into the land of Israel.

**6:24** And it came to pass after these things that the son of Hader, king of Syria, gathered all his army and went up, and they besieged Samaria.

**6:25** And there was a great famine in Samaria; and behold, they besieged it until a donkey's head was sold for fifty pieces of silver, and a fourth of a kab of doves' dung for five pieces of silver.

**6:26** And the king of Israel was passing along on the wall, and a woman cried out to him, saying, "Save me, O lord king!"

**6:27** And he said to her, "May the Lord not save you! From where shall I save you? From the threshing floor, or from the winepress?"

**6:28** And the king said to her, "What is the matter with you?" And the woman said, "This woman said to me, 'Give your son, and we will eat him today; and my son, and we will eat him tomorrow.'

**6:29** So we boiled my son and ate him; and I said to her on the second day, 'Give your son, that we may eat him,' but she has hidden her son."

**6:30** And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the woman, that he tore his garments; and he was passing along on the wall, and the people saw the sackcloth on his flesh underneath.

**6:31** And he said, "May God do so to me, and more also, if the head of Elisha shall remain on him today."

**6:32** Now Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him. And the king had sent a man from before his presence; and before the messenger came to him, he said to the elders, "Did you know that the son of the murderer has sent to take away my head? Look, when the messenger comes, shut the door and press him back at the door. Is not the sound of his master's feet behind him?"

**6:33** While he was still speaking with them, behold, a messenger came down to him and said, "Behold, this calamity is from the Lord; why should I wait for the Lord any longer?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — The Greek reads "hidden" (kekrymmenon), likely an early transcriptional variant for "borrowed" (kechrēmenon, matching the Hebrew).
- **6:8** — "Elimoni" is an untranslated Greek transliteration (elimōni) of the second term in the Hebrew idiom for "such and such."
- **6:24** — "The son of Hader" renders Greek huios Hader, the Septuagint form corresponding to Hebrew Ben-Hadad.
- **6:25** — The Greek reads "fifty" pieces of silver where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "eighty."
- **6:27** — The Greek renders the king's opening exclamation literally as a negative wish ("May the Lord not save you!").
