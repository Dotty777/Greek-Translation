## Text

**17:1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea the son of Elah began to reign in Samaria over Israel for nine years.

**17:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, yet not as the kings of Israel who were before him.

**17:3** Against him came up Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians; and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

**17:4** And the king of the Assyrians found unrighteousness in Hoshea, because he had sent messengers to Segor king of Egypt and had not brought tribute to the king of the Assyrians in that year; therefore the king of the Assyrians besieged him and bound him in a prison house.

**17:5** And the king of the Assyrians went up throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria and besieged it for three years.

**17:6** In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of the Assyrians captured Samaria and carried Israel away captive to the Assyrians, and settled them in Halah and in Habor, by the rivers of Gozar, and in the Mountains of the Medes.

**17:7** Now it came to pass that the sons of Israel sinned against the Lord their God, who brought them up out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and they feared other gods,

**17:8** and they walked in the statutes of the nations whom the Lord had removed from before the sons of Israel, and the kings of Israel, as many as had made them,

**17:9** and as many of the sons of Israel as clothed themselves with words that were not right against the Lord their God; and they built for themselves high places in all their cities, from the tower of the watchmen to the fortified city.

**17:10** And they set up for themselves sacred pillars and groves on every high hill and under every leafy tree;

**17:11** and they burned incense there on all the high places, just as the nations the Lord had carried away from before them; and they made companions and made engravings to provoke the Lord to anger;

**17:12** and they served the idols, of which the Lord had said to them, "You shall not do this thing to the Lord."

**17:13** Yet the Lord testified against Israel and against Judah by the hand of all His prophets, every seer of a word: "Turn back from your evil ways, and keep My commandments and My statutes and all the law which I commanded your fathers, all that I sent to them by the hand of My servants the prophets."

**17:14** Yet they did not listen, and they stiffened their back more than the back of their fathers;

**17:15** and as for His testimonies which He testified to them—they walked after vain things and became vain, and after the nations that were all around them, concerning whom He had commanded them not to do according to these things.

**17:16** They forsook the commandments of the Lord their God, and made for themselves a molten image, two heifers, and made groves, and worshiped all the host of heaven and served Baal;

**17:17** and they made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, practiced divination and omens, and sold themselves to do what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**17:18** Therefore the Lord was exceedingly angry with Israel, and removed them from His presence; and none was left except the tribe of Judah alone.

**17:19** Also Judah did not keep the commandments of God, and they walked in the statutes of Israel which they had made. And they rejected

**17:20** the Lord in all the seed of Israel. And He shook them and gave them into the hand of those who plundered them, until He had cast them out from His presence.

**17:21** For Israel was apart from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king; and Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the Lord and caused them to commit a great sin.

**17:22** And the sons of Israel walked in every sin of Jeroboam which he had committed; they did not depart from it,

**17:23** until the Lord removed Israel from His presence, just as the Lord had spoken by the hand of all His servants the prophets. And Israel was carried away captive from their own land to the Assyrians, to this day.

**17:24** And the king of the Assyrians brought from Babylon, the one from Cuthah, from Aia, and from Hamath and Sepharvaim; and they were settled in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel; and they inherited Samaria and dwelt in its cities.

**17:25** And it came to pass, at the beginning of their dwelling there, that they did not fear the Lord; and the Lord sent lions among them, and they were slaying among them.

**17:26** So they spoke to the king of the Assyrians, saying, "The nations whom you have carried away captive and settled in their place in the cities of Samaria do not know the ordinance of the God of the land; therefore He has sent the lions among them, and behold, they are putting them to death, because they do not know the ordinance of the God of the land."

**17:27** Then the king of the Assyrians commanded, saying, "Lead away from there, and let them go and dwell there, and they shall enlighten them in the ordinance of the land."

**17:28** And they brought one of the priests whom they had carried away captive from Samaria, and he dwelt in Bethel, and he was enlightening them how they should fear the Lord.

**17:29** Yet nation by nation they were making their own gods, and put them in the house of the high places which the Samaritans had made, the nations in their cities in which they dwelt;

**17:30** and the men of Babylon made Rochchoth-baineithei, and the men of Cuth made Ergel, and the men of Hamath made Ashimath,

**17:31** and the Avites made Eblazer and Tartak and Sepharun, when they burned their sons in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech of Sepharun.

**17:32** And they were fearing the Lord, and settled their abominations in the houses of the high places which they had made in Samaria, nation by nation in the city in which they dwelt; and they were fearing the Lord, and appointed priests of the high places, and they officiated for themselves in the house of the high places.

**17:33** The Lord they feared, yet their own gods they served, according to the ordinance of the nations from which he had carried them away captive.

**17:34** To this day they have done according to their ordinance; they fear, and they act according to their statutes and according to their judgment, and according to the law and according to the commandment which the Lord commanded the sons of Jacob, whose name He made Israel.

**17:35** And the Lord made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods nor bow down to them, and you shall not serve them and you shall not sacrifice to them;

**17:36** but rather the Lord, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great strength and with a high arm—Him you shall fear, and to Him you shall bow down; to Him you shall sacrifice.

**17:37** And the statutes, the judgments, the law, and the commandments which He wrote for you, be careful to do all the days; and you shall not fear other gods.

**17:38** And the covenant that He has made with you, you shall not forget; and you shall not fear other gods,

**17:39** but the Lord your God you shall fear, and He shall deliver you from all your enemies;

**17:40** and you shall no longer listen to their ordinance which they practice."

**17:41** So these nations feared the Lord, yet served their carved images; indeed, even the sons and the sons of their sons do just as their fathers did, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — The Greek names the Egyptian king 'Segor' (Hebrew Masoretic Text: 'So'), transliterates the Hebrew term for tribute as manaa (also manach in verse 3), and reads 'in that year' and 'besieged him.'
- **17:6** — The Greek reads 'rivers of Gozar' and 'Mountains of the Medes,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'river of Gozan' and 'cities of the Medes.'
- **17:20** — In the Greek text, the sentence begins at the end of verse 19 with a plural verb making Israel the subject ('And they rejected the Lord in all the seed of Israel'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the Lord rejecting Israel.
- **17:30** — The Greek preserves distinct deity names in verses 30–31—Rochchoth-baineithei (Hebrew: Succoth-Benoth), Ergel (Hebrew: Nergal), Ashimath (Hebrew: Ashima), and Eblazer (Hebrew: Nibhaz)—and treats Sepharun (Hebrew: the Sepharvites) as an idol made by the Avites.
- **17:34** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek omits the negation here ('they fear, and they act according to their statutes...'), and in verse 40 uses a second-person future ('you shall no longer listen') continuing the Lord's speech from verses 35–39.
