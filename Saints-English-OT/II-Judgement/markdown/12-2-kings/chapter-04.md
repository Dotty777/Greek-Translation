## Text

**4:1** And a certain woman from the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant my husband has died, and you know that he was a servant who feared the Lord; and the creditor has come to take my two sons for himself as servants."

**4:2** And Elisha said, "What shall I do for you? What do you have in the house?" And she said, "Your maidservant has nothing at all, except oil with which I may anoint myself."

**4:3** And he said to her, "Go, ask for vessels for yourself outside from all the neighbors, empty vessels; do not ask for only a few.

**4:4** And you shall enter and shut the door behind you and behind your sons, and you shall pour out into these vessels; and whatever is filled you shall set aside."

**4:5** So she departed from him and shut the door behind herself and behind her sons; they brought them near to her, and she kept pouring until the vessels were filled.

**4:6** And she said to her sons, "Bring yet another vessel to me." And they said to her, "There is no longer a vessel." And the oil ceased.

**4:7** And she came and told the man of God. And Elisha said, "Go and sell the oil, and you shall pay your interest, and you and your sons shall live on the remaining oil."

**4:8** Now it came to pass one day that Elisha crossed over to Shunem, and there was a prominent woman there, and she prevailed upon him to eat bread. And it came to pass that as often as he went in, he turned aside to eat there.

**4:9** And the woman said to her husband, "Behold now, I know that this is a holy man of God who continually passes by us.

**4:10** Let us make for him now an upper chamber, a small place, and let us put there for him a bed and a table and a chair and a lampstand; and it shall be, whenever he comes in to us, that he will turn aside there."

**4:11** And there came a day when he entered there, and he turned aside into the upper chamber and slept there.

**4:12** And he said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite woman for me." And he called her, and she stood before him.

**4:13** And he said to him, "Say now to her, 'Behold, you have set aside for us all this possession; what must be done for you? Do you have a word for the king, or for the commander of the host?'" And she said, "I dwell in the midst of the people."

**4:14** And he said, "What must be done for her?" And Gehazi his servant said, "Indeed, she has no son, and her husband is an old man."

**4:15** And he called her, and she stood by the door.

**4:16** And Elisha said to her, "At this season, as the hour lives, you shall be embracing a son." And she said, "No, my lord, do not deceive your maidservant."

**4:17** And the woman conceived and bore a son at this season, as the hour lives, just as Elisha had spoken to her.

**4:18** And the child grew; and it came to pass, when he went out to his father, to the reapers,

**4:19** that he said to his father, "My head, my head!" And he said to the servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**4:20** And he lay upon her knees until noon, and died.

**4:21** And she carried him up and laid him upon the bed of the man of God, and shut the door behind him, and went out.

**4:22** And she called her husband and said, "Send me now one of the servants and one of the donkeys, and I will run to the man of God and return."

**4:23** And he said, "Why are you going to him today? It is neither new moon nor Sabbath." And she said, "Peace."

**4:24** And she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on and go; do not hold back the riding for me, unless I tell you.

**4:25** Go and proceed, and you shall come to the man of God at Mount Carmel." And it came to pass, when Elisha saw her coming, that he said to Gehazi his servant, "Behold now, that Shunammite woman!

**4:26** Run now to meet her, and you shall say, 'Is it peace with you? Is it peace with your husband? Is it peace with the child?'" And she said, "Peace."

**4:27** And she came to Elisha at the mountain and took hold of his feet; and Gehazi drew near to push her away. And Elisha said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is deeply distressed within her, and the Lord has hidden it from me and from you, and has not told me."

**4:28** And she said, "Did I ask a son from my lord? Did I not say, 'You shall not deceive me'?"

**4:29** And Elisha said to Gehazi, "Gird up your loins, and take my staff in your hand, and go; for if you meet a man, you shall not bless him, and if a man blesses you, you shall not answer him; and you shall lay my staff upon the face of the child."

**4:30** And the mother of the child said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So Elisha arose and went after her.

**4:31** And Gehazi passed on before her and laid the staff upon the face of the child, and there was no voice and there was no hearing. So he returned to meet him and told him, saying, "The child has not awakened."

**4:32** And Elisha entered into the house, and behold, the child was dead, laid upon his bed.

**4:33** And Elisha entered into the house and shut the door behind the two of them, and prayed to the Lord.

**4:34** And he went up and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands, and he bent over him; and the flesh of the child grew warm.

**4:35** And he returned and walked back and forth in the house, and went up and bowed over the child up to seven times; and the child opened his eyes.

**4:36** And Elisha cried out to Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite woman." And he called her, and she came in to him. And Elisha said, "Take your son."

**4:37** And the woman came in and fell at his feet and bowed down to the ground, and she took her son and went out.

**4:38** And Elisha returned to Gilgal; and the famine was in the land, and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. And Elisha said to his servant, "Set on the pot, and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

**4:39** And he went out into the field to gather arioth; and he found a vine in the field, and gathered from it wild gourds, his garment full, and cast them into the pot of stew, for they did not know. And he poured out for the men to eat.

**4:40** And it came to pass, as they were eating of the stew, that behold, they cried out and said, "There is death in the pot, O man of God!" And they were not able to eat.

**4:41** And he said, "Take flour and cast it into the pot." And Elisha said to Gehazi the servant, "Pour out for the people, and let them eat." And there was no longer any harmful thing there in the pot.

**4:42** And a man came through from Beth-sarisa and brought to the man of God from the firstfruits twenty barley loaves and fig cakes. And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat."

**4:43** And his attendant said, "What? Shall I set this before one hundred men?" And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat; for thus says the Lord, 'They shall eat and have some left over.'"

**4:44** And they ate and had some left over, according to the word of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Greek reads literally "oil with which I may anoint myself," rendering the underlying Hebrew term for a flask of oil with a verb meaning "anoint."
- **4:13** — The Greek text reads "all this possession" (enktesin), whereas some Greek witnesses read "amazement" or "solicitude" (ekstasin).
- **4:35** — In the Greek text, Elisha himself bows over the child up to seven times before the child opens his eyes, without the sneezing found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:39** — "Arioth" is a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew word for wild herbs or greens.
- **4:42** — "Beth-sarisa" preserves the distinct Septuagint place name (corresponding to Hebrew Baal-shalishah), and the Greek renders the accompanying offering as "fig cakes."
