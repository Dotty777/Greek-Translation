## Text

**22:1** Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Jedidah, the daughter of Edina from Basuroth.

**22:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, and walked in every way of David his father; he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**22:3** And it came to pass in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, in the eighth month, that the king sent Shaphan the son of Eliu, the son of Meshullam, the scribe of the house of the Lord, saying,

**22:4** "Go up to Hilkiah the high priest, and seal up the silver that has been brought into the house of the Lord, which those who keep the threshold have gathered from the people,

**22:5** and let them place it into the hand of the workmen who are appointed in the house of the Lord." And he gave it to the workmen who were in the house of the Lord, to strengthen the breach of the house,

**22:6** to the carpenters and to the builders and to the masons, and to buy timber and hewn stones to reinforce the breach of the house.

**22:7** Yet they did not require an accounting from them for the silver given to them, because they act in faithfulness.

**22:8** And Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found the book of the law in the house of the Lord." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it.

**22:9** And he came in the house of the Lord to the king, and brought back word to the king, and said, "Your servants have melted down the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and have delivered it into the hand of the workmen who are appointed in the house of the Lord."

**22:10** And Shaphan the scribe spoke to the king, saying, "Hilkiah the priest has given me a book." And Shaphan read it before the king.

**22:11** And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the book of the law, that he tore his garments.

**22:12** And the king commanded Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Achbor the son of Micaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king's servant, saying,

**22:13** "Come and inquire of the Lord for me, and for all the people, and for all Judah, and concerning the words of this book that has been found; for great is the wrath of the Lord that has been poured out upon us, because our fathers did not listen to the words of the book, to do according to all that is written against us."

**22:14** So Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophetess, the mother of Shallum the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas, keeper of the wardrobe; and she dwelt in Jerusalem in the Masena; and they spoke to her.

**22:15** And she said, "Thus says the Lord God of Israel: Tell the man who sent you to Me,

**22:16** 'Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing calamities upon this place and upon those who dwell in it, all the words of the book which the king of Judah has read,

**22:17** because they have forsaken Me and burned incense to other gods, that they might provoke Me to anger with the works of their hands; and My wrath shall be kindled in this place and shall not be quenched.'

**22:18** And to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the Lord, thus you shall say to him: 'Thus says the Lord God of Israel: As for the words which you have heard,

**22:19** because your heart was tender, and you humbled yourself before the face of the Lord when you heard all that I spoke against this place and against those who dwell in it, that they should become a desolation and a curse, and you tore your garments and wept before Me, I also have heard, says the Lord.

**22:20** Not so; behold, I am adding you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your tomb in peace, and none of all the calamities which I am bringing upon this place shall be seen by your eyes.'"

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper names Edina and Basuroth (Hebrew: Adaiah and Bozkath).
- **22:3** — The Greek specifies "in the eighth month" (absent from the Hebrew), names Shaphan's father Eliu (Hebrew: Azaliah), and reads "the scribe of the house of the Lord" without a preposition before "house."
- **22:5** — "Breach" renders Greek βέδεκ (also in 22:6), a direct transliteration of the Hebrew term for structural damage; the Greek also shifts from Josiah's command to past-tense narration with "And he gave it."
- **22:14** — The Greek explicitly identifies Huldah as "the mother of Shallum" (Hebrew: "the wife of Shallum") and transliterates the Jerusalem district as "the Masena" (μασενά, Hebrew for the Second Quarter).
- **22:20** — As in 21:12, "Not so" translates Greek οὐχ οὕτως (for Hebrew "therefore"); the Greek chapter also ends at "this place," omitting the closing Hebrew sentence about the messengers returning word to the king.
