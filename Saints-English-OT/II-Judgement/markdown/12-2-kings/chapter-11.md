## Text

**11:1** And Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, and she destroyed all the seed of the kingdom.

**11:2** But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of her brother, and stole him away from the midst of the king's sons who were being put to death, him and his nurse, into the chamber of beds; and she hid him from the presence of Athaliah, and he was not put to death.

**11:3** And he was with her, hidden in the house for six years, while Athaliah was reigning over the land.

**11:4** And in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and took the captains of hundreds, the Chorri and the Rasin, and brought them to himself into the house of the Lord; and he made a covenant of the Lord with them and put them under oath. And Jehoiada showed them the king's son,

**11:5** and commanded them, saying, "This is the thing that you shall do: a third of you shall come in on the Sabbath, and you shall keep the watch of the king's house in the gateway,

**11:6** and a third at the gate of the ways, and a third at the gate behind the guards; and you shall keep the watch of the house.

**11:7** And two companies among you, everyone who goes out on the Sabbath, shall keep the watch of the house of the Lord by the king.

**11:8** And surround the king all around, each man with his weapon in his hand; and whoever enters into the aedoth shall die." And he was with the king when he went out and when he came in.

**11:9** And the captains of hundreds did all that Jehoiada the wise commanded; and each man took his men, even those who came in on the Sabbath, and came to Jehoiada the priest.

**11:10** And the priest gave to the captains of hundreds the spears and the threefold weapons of King David, which were in the house of the Lord.

**11:11** And the guards stood, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and the house, all around the king.

**11:12** And he brought out the king's son, and put upon him the iezer and the testimony; and he made him king and anointed him, and he clapped with his hand and said, "Long live the king!"

**11:13** Now Athaliah heard the sound of the running people, and she came to the people into the house of the Lord.

**11:14** And she looked, and behold, the king stood by the pillar according to the ordinance, and the singers and the trumpets were by the king; and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing with trumpets. Then Athaliah tore her garments and cried out, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**11:15** And Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of hundreds, the overseers of the army, and said to them, "Bring them out from within the aseroth, and whoever follows after her shall surely be put to death by the sword." For the priest said, "Do not let her die in the house of the Lord."

**11:16** So they laid hands upon her, and they went by the way of the horses' entrance into the king's house, and she died there.

**11:17** And Jehoiada made a covenant between the Lord and the king and the people, that they should be a people to the Lord, and also between the king and the people.

**11:18** And all the people of the land entered the house of Baal and tore it down; and its altars and its images they broke thoroughly in pieces, and they slew Magthan the priest of Baal before the altars. And the priest appointed overseers over the house of the Lord.

**11:19** And he took the captains of hundreds, and the Chorri, and the Rassim, and all the people of the land; and they brought the king down from the house of the Lord, and he entered by the way of the gate of the guards into the king's house, and they seated him upon the throne of the kings.

**11:20** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and Athaliah they put to death with the sword in the king's house.

## Translator Notes

- **11:4** — The Greek renders the Hebrew terms for the Carites and the guards as proper-name forms, the Chorri and the Rasin (spelled Rassim in 11:19).
- **11:8** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "ranks" as aedoth here (and aseroth in 11:15, where it also uses the plural "them" before "after her"), and concludes with a third-person past verb ("And he was") rather than a plural command.
- **11:9** — The Greek calls Jehoiada ho synetos ("the wise/discerning") here, whereas the Hebrew reads "the priest."
- **11:12** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "crown/diadem" as iezer, and reads ekratesen (a manuscript spelling reflecting ekrotesen, "clapped") in the singular.
- **11:18** — The Greek spells the name of Baal's priest Magthan, corresponding to Hebrew Mattan.
