## Text

**24:1** In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant for three years; then he turned and rebelled against him.

**24:2** And he sent against him the raiding bands of the Chaldeans, and the raiding bands of Syria, and the raiding bands of Moab, and the raiding bands of the sons of Ammon, and sent them forth into the land of Judah to prevail, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets.

**24:3** Surely because of the wrath of the Lord this came upon Judah, to remove it from His presence, for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he had done.

**24:4** And also he shed innocent blood and filled Jerusalem with innocent blood; and the Lord would not pardon.

**24:5** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**24:6** So Jehoiakim slept with his fathers, and Jehoiakim his son reigned in his place.

**24:7** And the king of Egypt did not come out of his land anymore, for the king of Babylon had taken all that belonged to the king of Egypt, from the brook of Egypt to the river Euphrates.

**24:8** Jehoiakim was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Nestha, the daughter of Ellanatham of Jerusalem.

**24:9** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father had done.

**24:10** At that time Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to Jerusalem, and the city came under siege.

**24:11** And Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came into the city, while his servants were besieging it.

**24:12** And Jehoiakim king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, he and his servants and his mother and his princes and his eunuchs; and the king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.

**24:13** And he carried out from there all the treasures of the house of the Lord and the treasures of the king's house, and cut in pieces all the articles of gold which Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of the Lord, according to the word of the Lord.

**24:14** And he carried away into exile Jerusalem, and all the princes and the mighty men of valor, taking ten thousand captives, and every craftsman and smith; and none remained except the poor of the land.

**24:15** And he carried Jehoiakim away into exile to Babylon, and the king's mother and the king's wives and his eunuchs; and the mighty men of the land he led away into captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon.

**24:16** And all the men of valor, seven thousand, and the craftsmen and the smiths, one thousand, all mighty men who made war—the king of Babylon brought them into exile to Babylon.

**24:17** And the king of Babylon made Matthan his son king in his place, and gave him the name Zedekiah.

**24:18** Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Mitat, the daughter of Jeremiah.

**24:19** And he did what was evil before the Lord, according to all that Jehoiakim had done.

**24:20** For it was because of the wrath of the Lord against Jerusalem and in Judah, until He cast them out from His presence. And Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.

## Translator Notes

- **24:2** — The Greek omits "the Lord" as the explicit subject of "sent" (present in the Hebrew) and reads "to prevail" where the Hebrew has "to destroy it."
- **24:6** — Here and in vv. 8, 12, and 15 the Greek names the son "Jehoiakim," identical to his father, whereas the Hebrew distinguishes him as "Jehoiachin" (v. 8 also preserves the Greek name forms Nestha and Ellanatham).
- **24:17** — The Greek calls the new king "Matthan his son," whereas the Hebrew reads "Mattaniah his uncle"; v. 18 likewise preserves the Greek name "Mitat" (Hebrew "Hamutal") and omits "of Libnah."
