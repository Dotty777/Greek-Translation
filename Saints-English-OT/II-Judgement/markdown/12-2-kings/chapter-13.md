## Text

**13:1** In the twenty-third year of Joash the son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz the son of Jehu reigned in Samaria seventeen years.

**13:2** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked after the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin; he did not depart from it.

**13:3** Then the Lord was moved to wrath against Israel, and He delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Syria, and into the hand of the son of Hader, the son of Hazael, all those days.

**13:4** And Jehoahaz implored the face of the Lord, and the Lord heard him; for He saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Syria oppressed them.

**13:5** And the Lord gave salvation to Israel, and Israel came out from under the hand of Syria; and the children of Israel dwelt in their tents as yesterday and the day before.

**13:6** Yet they did not depart from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who caused Israel to sin, but he walked in it; and moreover the sacred grove remained standing in Samaria.

**13:7** For there was not left to Jehoahaz any people except fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand footmen; for the king of Syria had destroyed them, and they had made them like dust for trampling.

**13:8** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:9** So Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Joash his son became king.

**13:10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Joash the son of Jehoahaz reigned over Israel in Samaria sixteen years.

**13:11** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord; he did not depart from any sin of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, but he walked in it.

**13:12** Now the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds that he performed with Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:13** And Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat with his fathers and in Samaria with the brethren of Israel.

**13:14** Now Elisha fell sick with his illness of which he died; and Joash king of Israel came down to him and wept over his face, and said, "Father, father, the chariot of Israel and its horseman!"

**13:15** And Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." So he took to himself a bow and arrows.

**13:16** Then he said to the king, "Place your hand upon the bow." And Joash placed his hand upon it, and Elisha laid his hands upon the king's hands,

**13:17** and he said, "Open the window toward the east." And he opened it. And he said, "An arrow of salvation of the Lord, and an arrow of salvation in Syria; and it shall strike Syria in Aphek to utter destruction."

**13:18** Then Elisha said to him, "Take bows." And he took them. And he said to the king of Israel, "Strike upon the ground." So the king struck three times, and stopped.

**13:19** And the man of God was grieved with him and said, "If you had struck five or six times, then you would have struck Syria to utter destruction; but now you shall strike Syria three times."

**13:20** Then Elisha died, and they buried him. And raiding bands of Moab came into the land at the coming of the year.

**13:21** And it came to pass, as they were burying a man, behold, they saw the raiding band, and they cast the man into the tomb of Elisha; and he went and touched the bones of Elisha, and he came to life and stood up on his feet.

**13:22** And Hazael oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

**13:23** Yet the Lord had mercy on them and showed compassion to them, and looked upon them because of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; and the Lord was not willing to destroy them, and He did not cast them away from His presence.

**13:24** Now Hazael king of Syria died, and the son of Hader, his son, reigned in his place.

**13:25** And Joash the son of Jehoahaz turned again and took the cities out of the hand of the son of Hader, the son of Hazael, which he had taken out of the hand of Jehoahaz his father in war. Three times Joash struck him and restored the cities of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — "The son of Hader" translates Greek huios Hader (likewise in vv. 24-25), the Septuagint rendering of the Syrian royal name Ben-Hadad.
- **13:13** — The Greek reads "and Jeroboam sat with his fathers and in Samaria with the brethren of Israel," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which states that Jeroboam sat on his throne and Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.
- **13:17** — The Greek omits Elisha's command to shoot and Joash's shooting of the arrow, and uses a third-person future verb ("it shall strike") referring to the arrow where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "you shall strike."
- **13:18** — "Bows" translates Greek plural toxa (which can denote archery gear generally), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies "arrows."
