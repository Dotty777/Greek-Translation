## Text

**23:1** And the kings brought back the word; and the king sent and gathered to himself all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**23:2** And the king went up to the house of the Lord, and every man of Judah and those who dwelt in Jerusalem with him, and the priests and the prophets and all the people, from small even to great; and he read before them all the words of the book of the covenant that was found in the house of the Lord.

**23:3** And the king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord, to keep His commandments and His testimonies and His statutes with all his heart and with all his soul, to establish the words of this covenant, the things written in this book; and all the people stood in the covenant.

**23:4** And the king commanded Hilkiah the high priest, and the priests of the second order, and those who guarded the threshold, to bring out of the temple of the Lord all the vessels that had been made for Baal, and for the grove, and for all the host of heaven; and he burned them outside Jerusalem in Salemoth of Kidron, and took their dust to Bethel.

**23:5** And he burned the Chomarim whom they had given to the king of Judah, and they burned incense on the high places and in the cities of Judah and in the places round about Jerusalem, and those who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, to the moon, to the Mazuroth, and to all the host of heaven.

**23:6** And he brought out the grove from the house of the Lord, outside Jerusalem, to the brook Kidron, and burned it at the brook Kidron and ground it to dust, and cast its dust into the grave of the sons of the people.

**23:7** And he tore down the house of the Kadesim that were in the house of the Lord, where the women wove Chettiin there for the grove.

**23:8** And he brought up all the priests from the cities of Judah, and defiled the high places where the priests had burned incense there, from Gebal even to Beersheba; and he tore down the house of the gates that was by the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the ruler of the gate, which were on a man's left in the gate of the city.

**23:9** Nevertheless, the priests of the high places did not come up to the altar of the Lord in Jerusalem, except that they ate unleavened bread in the midst of their brethren.

**23:10** And you shall defile Topheth in the valley of the son of Hinnom, for a man to make his son pass through, and a man his daughter, to Molech in fire.

**23:11** And he burned the horses that the kings of Judah had given to the sun at the entrance of the house of the Lord, at the treasury of Nathan the king, the eunuch, in Pharurim; and the chariot of the sun he burned with fire.

**23:12** And the altars that were on the roof of the upper chamber of Ahaz, which the king of Judah had made, and the altars which Manasseh had made in the two courts of the house of the Lord, the king tore down and pulled down from there, and cast their dust into the brook Kidron.

**23:13** And the house that was before Jerusalem, on the right of Mount Mosoath, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the offense of the Sidonians, and for Chemosh the offense of Moab, and for Molchol the abomination of the sons of Ammon, the king defiled.

**23:14** And he broke in pieces the pillars and utterly destroyed the groves, and filled their places with the bones of men.

**23:15** Moreover, the high altar that was in Bethel, which Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, had made—even that high altar he pulled down, and broke its stones in pieces and ground it to dust, and burned the grove.

**23:16** And Josiah turned aside and saw the tombs there in the city, and he sent and took the bones out of the tombs and burned them upon the altar, and defiled it according to the word of the Lord which the man of God had spoken when Jeroboam stood at the feast by the altar. And turning around, he lifted up his eyes toward the tomb of the man of God who had spoken these words.

**23:17** And he said, "What is that monument which I see?" And the men of the city said to him, "The man of God who came out of Judah and proclaimed these words which he proclaimed against the altar of Bethel."

**23:18** And he said, "Leave it alone; let no man move his bones." So his bones were spared together with the bones of the prophet who came from Samaria.

**23:19** Moreover, all the houses of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel had made to provoke the Lord to anger, Josiah removed, and did in them all the deeds which he had done in Bethel.

**23:20** And he sacrificed all the priests of the high places who were there upon the altars, and burned the bones of men upon them, and returned to Jerusalem.

**23:21** And the king commanded all the people, saying, "Keep the Passover to the Lord our God, as it is written in the book of this covenant."

**23:22** For this Passover had not been kept from the days of the judges who judged Israel, nor during all the days of the kings of Israel and the kings of Judah,

**23:23** but only in the eighteenth year of King Josiah was the Passover kept to the Lord in Jerusalem.

**23:24** Moreover, the mediums and the spiritists, and the teraphim and the idols, and all the offenses that were in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, Josiah put away, that he might establish the words of the law that were written in the book which Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the Lord.

**23:25** Before him there was no king like him who turned to the Lord with all his heart, and with all his strength, and with all his soul, according to all the law of Moses; nor after him did any arise like him.

**23:26** Nevertheless, the Lord did not turn from the fierceness of His great wrath, with which His anger burned against Judah, because of the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked Him.

**23:27** And the Lord said, "I will also remove Judah from His presence, as I removed Israel, and I will cast off this city which I have chosen, Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, 'My name shall be there.'"

**23:28** Now the rest of the acts of Josiah, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**23:29** In his days Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt went up against the king of the Assyrians to the river Euphrates; and Josiah went to meet him. And Necho put him to death at Megiddo when he saw him.

**23:30** And his servants carried him dead from Megiddo, and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his own tomb. And the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and anointed him, and made him king in place of his father.

**23:31** Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Hamitai, the daughter of Jeremiah of Lemna.

**23:32** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.

**23:33** And Pharaoh Necho removed him at Ablaa in the land of Hamath, so that he would not reign in Jerusalem, and imposed one hundred talents of silver and one hundred talents of gold.

**23:34** And Pharaoh Necho made Eliakim, the son of Josiah king of Judah, reign over them in place of Josiah his father, and changed his name to Jehoiakim. And he took Jehoahaz and brought him into Egypt, and he died there.

**23:35** And Jehoiakim gave the silver and the gold to Pharaoh; nevertheless, they taxed the land to give the silver at the command of Pharaoh; each man according to his assessment, they gave the silver and the gold with the people of the land, to give to Pharaoh Necho.

**23:36** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Jella, the daughter of Edil of Kruma.

**23:37** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — The Greek opens with "the kings brought back the word," whereas the Hebrew closes 22:20 with "they brought back word to the king."
- **23:5** — The Greek reads "burned" (Hebrew "removed") and retains Hebrew terms in transliteration as "Chomarim" (idolatrous priests) and "Mazuroth" (constellations); similar Greek transliterations or forms appear in v. 4 ("Salemoth"), v. 7 ("Kadesim," "Chettiin"), v. 8 ("Gebal"), and v. 11 ("Pharurim").
- **23:10** — The Greek reads second-person plural "you shall defile" and lacks the negative particle before the infinitive, whereas the Hebrew reads "he defiled ... so that no one might make his son or daughter pass through the fire."
- **23:13** — The Greek reads "the house" (Hebrew "the high places") and preserves the distinct forms "Mosoath" (Hebrew "Destruction") and "Molchol" (Hebrew "Milcom").
- **23:33** — The Greek reads "removed him at Ablaa" (Hebrew "imprisoned him at Riblah") and has "one hundred talents of gold" (Hebrew "one talent of gold"); vv. 31 and 36 also preserve distinct Greek name forms (Hamitai, Lemna, Jella, Edil, Kruma).
