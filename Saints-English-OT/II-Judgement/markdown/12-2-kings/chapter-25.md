## Text

**25:1** And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came against Jerusalem, and he encamped against it and built a siege wall against it all around.

**25:2** And the city came under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah, on the ninth day of the month.

**25:3** And the famine grew severe in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.

**25:4** Then the city was breached, and all the men of war went out by night by the way of the gate between the walls, which belongs to the king's garden, while the Chaldeans were against the city all around. And he went by the way of the Arabah.

**25:5** And the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and they overtook him in the Araboth of Jericho, and all his army was scattered away from him.

**25:6** And they seized the king and brought him to the king of Babylon at Jerdeblatha, and he spoke judgment with him.

**25:7** And he slew the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and blinded the eyes of Zedekiah; and he bound him in fetters and brought him to Babylon.

**25:8** And in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, who stood before the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.

**25:9** And he burned the house of the Lord, and the king's house, and all the houses of Jerusalem, and every house the captain of the guard burned.

**25:11** And the remnant of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had deserted to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the support, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away.

**25:12** And of the poor of the land the captain of the guard left behind some to be vinedressers and tabein.

**25:13** And the bronze pillars that were in the house of the Lord, and the mechonoth, and the bronze sea that was in the house of the Lord, the Chaldeans broke in pieces, and they carried their bronze to Babylon.

**25:14** And the cauldrons, and the iamein, and the bowls, and the censers, and all the bronze vessels with which they ministered, he took.

**25:15** And the firepans and the golden and silver bowls the captain of the guard took.

**25:16** The two pillars, the one sea, and the mechonoth which Solomon had made for the house of the Lord—there was no weighing the bronze of all the vessels.

**25:17** Eighteen cubits was the height of the one pillar, and the bronze chothar was upon it; and the height of the chothar was three cubits, with sakacharthai upon the chothar all around, all of bronze; and the second pillar had the same upon the gabacha.

**25:18** And the captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, and Zephaniah the son of the second rank, and the three keepers of the threshold.

**25:19** And from the city he took one eunuch who was overseer of the men of war, and five men of those who saw the king's face who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the army who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.

**25:20** And Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them and led them away to the king of Babylon at Deblatha.

**25:21** And the king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Judah was carried away into exile from its own land.

**25:22** And as for the people who remained in the land of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had left, he appointed over them Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan.

**25:23** And all the commanders of the army, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah; and they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah—even Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Jonah the son of Kareth, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth the Nepphathite, and Ozonias the son of the Achathite, they and their men.

**25:24** And Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, and said to them, "Do not fear the passage of the Chaldeans; dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be well with you."

**25:25** And it came to pass in the seventh month that Manael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the seed of the kings, came, and men with him; and he struck down Gedaliah, and he died, along with the Jews and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah.

**25:26** And all the people, from small even to great, and the commanders of the armies arose and entered Egypt, for they were afraid before the face of the Chaldeans.

**25:27** And it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year of the captivity of Joakim king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, that Evil-Merodach king of Babylon, in the year of his reign, lifted up the head of Joakim king of Judah and brought him out of his prison house.

**25:28** And he spoke kindly with him, and set his throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.

**25:29** And he changed his prison garments, and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.

**25:30** And his provision was a continual provision; it was given to him from the king's house, a daily portion in its day, all the days of his life.

## Translator Notes

- **25:6** — Jerdeblatha (and Deblatha in v. 20) are distinct Septuagint forms of Riblah (v. 21). In v. 8, the title rendered "captain of the guard" is Greek archimageiros (literally "chief cook"), the Septuagint rendering of the Babylonian court office over the royal guard.
- **25:17** — In vv. 12-17 the Greek text retains several untranslated transliterations of Hebrew technical terms: tabein (plowmen), mechonoth (wheeled stands), iamein (shovels), chothar (capital), sakacharthai (latticework and pomegranates), and gabacha (network).
- **25:18** — The Greek reads "son of the second rank" (hyion tes deuteroseos), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the second priest."
- **25:23** — The Septuagint preserves distinctive proper-name forms here ("Jonah the son of Kareth," "the Nepphathite," and "Ozonias the son of the Achathite"), as well as "Manael" in v. 25 (for Ishmael) and "Joakim" in v. 27 (corresponding to Jehoiachin).
- **25:24** — The Greek reads "the passage of the Chaldeans" (parodon), differing from the Masoretic Text's "servants of the Chaldeans."
