## Text

**10:1** Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. And Jehu wrote a letter and sent it to Samaria, to the rulers of Samaria, and to the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab, saying,

**10:2** "And now, as soon as this letter comes to you, since your master's sons are with you, and with you are the chariot and horses, and fortified cities, and armor,

**10:3** look out for the good and upright one among your master's sons, and set him upon his father's throne, and fight for the house of your master."

**10:4** And they were exceedingly afraid, and said, "Behold, the two kings did not stand before him; and how shall we stand?"

**10:5** And those who were over the house, and those who were over the city, and the elders, and the guardians sent to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and whatever you say to us we will do. We will not make any man king; what is good in your eyes we will do."

**10:6** Then he wrote a second letter to them, saying, "If you are mine, and if you listen to my voice, take the heads of the men, the sons of your master, and bring them to me about this time tomorrow to Israel." Now the sons of the king were seventy men; these great men of the city were rearing them.

**10:7** And it came to pass, when the letter came to them, that they took the sons of the king and slaughtered them, seventy men; and they placed their heads in baskets and sent them to him to Israel.

**10:8** And the messenger came and reported, saying, "I have brought the heads of the king's sons." And he said, "Lay them in two heaps by the entrance of the city gate until morning."

**10:9** And it came to pass in the morning that he went out and stood, and said to all the people, "You are righteous. Behold, I myself conspired against my master and slew him; yet who has struck down all these?

**10:10** See then, that nothing of the word of the Lord shall fall to the earth, which the Lord spoke concerning the house of Ahab; for the Lord has done all that He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah."

**10:11** So Jehu struck down all who remained in the house of Ahab in Israel, and all his great men, and his close acquaintances, and his priests, until he left them no remnant.

**10:12** And he arose and went toward Samaria. As he was at Beth-eked of the shepherds on the way,

**10:13** Jehu met the brethren of Ahaziah king of Judah, and said, "Who are you?" And they said, "We are the brethren of Ahaziah, and we have come down for the welfare of the sons of the king and the sons of the ruling queen."

**10:14** And he said, "Take them alive!" And they slaughtered them at Beth-eked, forty-two men; he did not leave a man of them.

**10:15** And he departed from there and received Jonadab the son of Rechab coming to meet him, and he blessed him. And Jehu said to him, "Is your heart right with my heart, as my heart is with your heart?" And Jonadab said, "It is." And Jehu said, "And if it is, give your hand." And he gave his hand, and he brought him up to himself into the chariot.

**10:16** And he said to him, "Come with me, and see my zeal for the Lord." And he seated him in his chariot.

**10:17** And he entered Samaria, and struck down all who remained of Ahab in Samaria until he had destroyed him, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke to Elijah.

**10:18** And Jehu was zealous for all the people, and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.

**10:19** And now, all you prophets of Baal, call to me all his servants and his priests; let no man be missing, for I have a great sacrifice to Baal; everyone who is missing shall not live." Now Jehu did this in craftiness, that he might destroy the servants of Baal.

**10:20** And Jehu said, "Sanctify a sacred festival to Baal." And he proclaimed it.

**10:21** And Jehu sent throughout all Israel, saying, "And now, all his servants and all his priests and all his prophets, let no one be left behind, for I am making a great sacrifice; whoever is left behind shall not live." And all the servants of Baal came, and all his priests, and all his prophets; there was not a man left who did not come. And they entered the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled from mouth to mouth.

**10:22** And he said to the one who was over the house of mesthaal, "Bring out a garment for all the servants of Baal." And the keeper of the robes brought it out to them.

**10:23** Then Jehu and Jonadab the son of Rechab entered the house of Baal, and he said to the servants of Baal, "Search and see whether any of the servants of the Lord are with you, for there must be none except the servants of Baal alone."

**10:24** And he went in to offer the sacrifices and the burnt offerings. Now Jehu had appointed for himself eighty men outside, and said, "If any man escapes from the men whom I bring into your hands, his life shall be for his life."

**10:25** And it came to pass, as soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, that Jehu said to the guards and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let no man come out from among them!" And they struck them down with the edge of the sword; and the guards and the officers cast them out, and went as far as the city of the house of Baal.

**10:26** And they brought out the robe of Baal and burned it.

**10:27** And they tore down the pillars of Baal, and he struck it down into latrines until this day.

**10:28** Thus Jehu destroyed Baal out of Israel.

**10:29** Nevertheless, from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, Jehu did not depart from before them—the golden heifers that were in Bethel and in Dan.

**10:30** And the Lord said to Jehu, "Because you have done well in doing what is upright in My eyes, and have done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in My heart, your sons to the fourth generation shall sit upon the throne of Israel."

**10:31** Yet Jehu did not take care to walk in the law of the Lord God of Israel with all his heart; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, who caused Israel to sin.

**10:32** In those days the Lord began to cut off parts of Israel; and Hazael struck them down throughout all the territory of Israel,

**10:33** from the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, all Gilead—of the Gadites, and of Reuben, and of Manasseh—from Aroer, which is upon the bank of the brook Arnon, both Gilead and Bashan.

**10:34** Now the rest of the acts of Jehu, and all that he did, and all his might, and the engagements which he joined, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**10:35** And Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**10:36** And the days that Jehu reigned over Israel were twenty-eight years in Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — The Greek reads "Israel" here (and in 10:7, 11) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jezreel."
- **10:10** — The Greek reads idete aphpho ("See then"), retaining an untranslated transliteration of the Hebrew particle epho ("then/now") and rendering "see" where the Hebrew has "know."
- **10:18** — The Greek text reads ezelosen ("was zealous for"), differing from the Hebrew "gathered."
- **10:22** — The Greek leaves mesthaal untranslated as a transliteration of the Hebrew term for the wardrobe or vestry.
- **10:26** — The Greek reads stolen ("robe/vestment") rather than stelen ("pillar"), which corresponds to the Hebrew.
