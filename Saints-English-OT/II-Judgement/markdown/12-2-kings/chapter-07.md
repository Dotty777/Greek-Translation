## Text

**7:1** And Elisha said, "Hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord: 'About this time tomorrow, a measure of fine flour shall be sold for a shekel in the gates of Samaria.'"

**7:2** And the officer on whose hand the king leaned answered Elisha and said, "Behold, the Lord will make floodgates in heaven; could this thing be?" And Elisha said, "Behold, you shall see it with your eyes, yet you shall not eat of it."

**7:3** Now there were four leprous men by the entrance of the city; and one man said to his neighbor, "Why do we sit here until we die?

**7:4** If we say, 'Let us enter into the city,' the famine is in the city, and we shall die there; and if we sit here, we shall also die. Now therefore, come, and let us go over to the camp of Syria: if they spare our lives, we shall live; and if they put us to death, we shall die."

**7:5** And they arose in the darkness to enter into the camp of Syria; and they came into the midst of the camp of Syria, and behold, there was no man there.

**7:6** For the Lord had caused the camp of Syria to hear the sound of chariots and the sound of horses, even the sound of a great army; and one man said to his brother, "Now the king of Israel has hired against us the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to come against us!"

**7:7** So they arose and fled in the darkness, and abandoned their tents and their horses and their donkeys in the camp just as it was, and fled for their lives.

**7:8** And these lepers entered as far as the outskirts of the camp, and went into one tent and ate and drank, and carried away from there silver and gold and clothing; and they went away and returned from there, and entered into another tent, and took from there, and went away and hid them.

**7:9** Then one man said to his neighbor, "We should not act thus! This day is a day of good tidings, yet we keep silent; and if we wait until the light of the morning, we shall find iniquity. Now therefore, come, and let us go in and report to the house of the king."

**7:10** So they came and cried out to the gate of the city, and reported to them, saying, "We entered into the camp of Syria, and behold, there is no man there, nor the voice of a man, but only horses tied and donkeys, and their tents just as they are."

**7:11** And the gatekeepers cried out and reported it to the house of the king within.

**7:12** And the king arose in the night and said to his servants, "I will now tell you what Syria has done to us. They know that we are hungry, so they have gone out of the camp and hidden themselves in the field, saying, 'They will come out of the city, and we shall take them alive and enter into the city.'"

**7:13** And one of his servants answered and said, "Let them now take five of the remaining horses that have been left here—behold, they are like all the multitude of Israel that is perishing—and we will send them there and see."

**7:14** So they took two riders of horses, and the king of Israel sent them after the king of Syria, saying, "Go and see."

**7:15** And they went after them as far as the Jordan; and behold, all the way was full of garments and equipment that Syria had cast away in their terror. And the messengers returned and reported to the king.

**7:16** Then the people went out and plundered the camp of Syria; and a measure of fine flour was sold for a shekel, according to the word of the Lord, and two measures of barley for a shekel.

**7:17** Now the king had appointed the officer on whose hand the king leaned over the gate; and the people trampled him in the gate, and he died, just as the man of God had spoken, who spoke when the messenger came down to him.

**7:18** And it came to pass just as Elisha had spoken to the king, saying, "Two measures of barley for a shekel, and a measure of fine flour for a shekel, and it shall be about this time tomorrow in the gate of Samaria."

**7:19** And the officer had answered Elisha and said, "Behold, the Lord makes floodgates in heaven—this thing shall by no means be!" And Elisha had said, "Behold, you shall see it with your eyes, yet you shall by no means eat of it."

**7:20** And so it came to pass, for the people trampled him in the gate, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek mentions only the measure of fine flour in this verse, omitting the two measures of barley that appear later in 7:16 and 7:18.
- **7:5** — The Greek reads "into the midst of" the camp where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to the edge of" the camp.
- **7:14** — The Greek reads "two riders of horses" sent "after the king of Syria," where the Hebrew reads "two chariots with horses" sent "after the army."
- **7:17** — The Greek reads "when the messenger came down to him" (matching 6:33), where the Hebrew reads "when the king came down to him."
