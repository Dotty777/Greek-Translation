## Text

**12:1** Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.

**12:2** In the seventh year of Jehu, Joash began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Abia, from the land of Beersheba.

**12:3** And Joash did what was right before the Lord all the days in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him.

**12:4** Yet the high places were not removed, and there the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**12:5** And Joash said to the priests, "All the silver of the holy things that is brought into the house of the Lord, the silver of valuation, the silver a man receives by valuation, and all the silver that comes upon a man's heart to bring into the house of the Lord,

**12:6** let the priests take for themselves, each man from his sale, and they themselves shall repair the breach of the house, wherever a breach may be found there."

**12:7** And it came to pass in the twenty-third year of King Joash that the priests had not repaired the breach of the house.

**12:8** Then King Joash called Jehoiada the priest and the priests, and said to them, "Why have you not been repairing the breach of the house? Now therefore, do not take silver from your sales, for you shall give it for the breach of the house."

**12:9** And the priests agreed not to receive silver from the people, and not to strengthen the abomination of the house.

**12:10** Then Jehoiada the priest took one chest, and bored a hole in its hole, and placed it beside Iameibein in the house of a man, the house of the Lord; and the priests who guarded the threshold deposited all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord.

**12:11** And it came to pass, when he saw that there was much silver in the chest, that the king's scribe and the high priest went up, and bound up and counted the silver that was found in the house of the Lord.

**12:12** And he gave the silver that had been prepared into the hands of those who did the work, the overseers of the house of the Lord; and they paid it out to the workers in wood and to the builders who labored in the house of the Lord,

**12:13** and to the masons and to the hewers of stone, to buy timber and hewn stones to repair the breach of the house of the Lord, and for all that was expended on the house to strengthen it.

**12:14** However, there shall not be made for the house of the Lord silver doors, nails, bowls, and trumpets, nor any vessel of gold or vessel of silver, from the silver that was brought into the house of the Lord;

**12:15** for they shall give it to those who do the work. And with it they strengthened the house of the Lord.

**12:16** Moreover, they did not require an accounting from the men into whose hands they delivered the silver to give to those who did the work, for they act in their faithfulness.

**12:17** The silver for sin and the silver for trespass, whatever was brought into the house of the Lord, belonged to the priests.

**12:18** Then Hazael king of Syria went up and fought against Gath, and captured it. And Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.

**12:19** So Joash king of Judah took all the holy things that Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, his fathers and kings of Judah, had consecrated, and his own holy things, and all the gold that was found in the treasuries of the house of the Lord and of the king's house, and sent them to Hazael king of Syria; and he went up from Jerusalem.

**12:20** Now the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the days of the kings of Judah?

**12:21** And his servants arose and bound every conspiracy, and struck Joash down in the house of Maalo at Gaalla.

**12:22** And Jezeichar the son of Jemuath, and Jezebuth, his son Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died. And they buried him with his fathers in the city of David; and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek names Joash's mother Abia and adds "from the land of Beersheba," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Zibiah of Beersheba."
- **12:6** — "Sale" translates Greek prasis (likewise "sales" in v. 8), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "acquaintance"; "breach" translates the Greek transliterated loanword bedek, also used in vv. 7, 8, and 13.
- **12:9** — The Greek reads bdelygma ("abomination") here instead of the transliterated loanword bedek ("breach") found in vv. 6-8 and 13.
- **12:10** — The Greek literally reads "bored a hole in its hole" and places the chest "beside Iameibein in the house of a man, the house of the Lord," retaining an untranslated term (Iameibein) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to the right side of the altar as one enters the house of the Lord.
- **12:22** — The Greek gives distinct forms for the conspirators' names ("Jezeichar the son of Jemuath, and Jezebuth") and reads "his son Shomer," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Jozacar son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer."
