## Text

**19:1** And Ahab reported to Jezebel his wife all that Elijah had done, and how he had slain the prophets with the sword.

**19:2** And Jezebel sent to Elijah and said, "If you are Elijah and I am Jezebel, may God do these things to me, and more also, that at this hour tomorrow I will make your life like the life of one of them."

**19:3** And Elijah was afraid, and he arose and departed for his own life; and he came to Beersheba, the land of Judah, and left his young servant there.

**19:4** And he himself went a day's journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down beneath a broom tree. And he asked for his own life that he might die, and said, "Let it be enough now! Take now my life from me, O Lord, for I am no better than my fathers."

**19:5** And he lay down and slept there under a shrub; and behold, someone touched him and said to him, "Arise and eat."

**19:6** And Elijah looked, and behold, by his head was a hearth cake of spelt and a flask of water. And he arose and ate and drank, and turning back, he lay down again.

**19:7** And the angel of the Lord returned a second time, and touched him, and said to him, "Arise, eat, for the journey is too great for you."

**19:8** And he arose and ate and drank; and he went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights as far as Mount Horeb.

**19:9** And he entered into the cave there and lodged there; and behold, the word of the Lord came to him, and He said, "Why are you here, Elijah?"

**19:10** And Elijah said, "With zeal I have been zealous for the Lord Almighty, because the sons of Israel have forsaken You; Your altars they have torn down, and Your prophets they have slain with the sword. And I alone am left, and they seek my life, to take it."

**19:11** And He said, "You shall go forth tomorrow and stand before the Lord on the mountain; behold, the Lord will pass by." And there was a great and mighty wind tearing apart mountains and shattering rocks before the Lord, in the wind of the Lord; and after the wind an earthquake, yet the Lord was not in the earthquake;

**19:12** and after the earthquake a fire, yet the Lord was not in the fire; and after the fire, the sound of a gentle breeze.

**19:13** And it came to pass, when Elijah heard it, that he covered his face with his sheepskin mantle, and went out and stood beneath the cave. And behold, a voice came to him and said, "Why are you here, Elijah?"

**19:14** And Elijah said, "With zeal I have been zealous for the Lord Almighty, because the sons of Israel have forsaken You; Your covenant and Your altars they have pulled down, and Your prophets they have slain with the sword. And I alone am left, and they seek my life, to take it."

**19:15** And the Lord said to him, "Go, return on your way, and you shall come to the way of the wilderness of Damascus; and you shall come and anoint Hazael to be king of Syria;

**19:16** and the son of Jehu, the son of Nimshi, you shall anoint to be king over Israel; and Elisha the son of Shaphat, from Abel Meholah, you shall anoint as prophet in your place.

**19:17** And it shall come to pass that whoever escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall put to death; and whoever escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall put to death.

**19:18** And you shall leave in Israel seven thousand men—all the knees that have not bowed the knee to Baal, and every mouth that has not worshiped him."

**19:19** And he departed from there and found Elisha the son of Shaphat, while he was plowing with oxen, twelve yoke before him, and he was among the twelve. And he came up to him and cast his sheepskin mantle upon him.

**19:20** And Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah, and said, "Let me kiss my father, and I will follow after you." And Elijah said, "Return, for I have done this to you."

**19:21** And he turned back from following him, and took the yokes of oxen and slaughtered them, and boiled them with the gear of the oxen, and gave them to the people, and they ate. Then he arose and went after Elijah, and ministered to him.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek opens Jezebel's oath with "If you are Elijah and I am Jezebel" and refers to "God" in the singular, whereas the Masoretic Text omits the opening clause and reads "the gods."
- **19:11** — Following Codex Vaticanus, this Greek text adds "tomorrow" and reads "in the wind of the Lord" without the negation ("the Lord was not in the wind") found in the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint manuscripts; verse 12 renders the famous Hebrew phrase as "the sound of a gentle breeze."
- **19:16** — Following Codex Vaticanus, this Greek text reads "the son of Jehu, the son of Nimshi" rather than "Jehu the son of Nimshi," though verse 17 names Jehu directly.
- **19:18** — The Greek reads second-person "you shall leave" (where the Masoretic Text has first-person "I have reserved") and "worshiped" (where the Masoretic Text has "kissed").
- **19:20** — The Greek reads "for I have done this to you" as a statement rather than the Masoretic Text's rhetorical question ("for what have I done to you?").
