## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came by the hand of Jehu son of Hanani to Baasha:

**16:2** "Because I exalted you from the earth and appointed you ruler over My people Israel, and you have walked in the way of Jeroboam and have caused My people Israel to sin, to provoke Me to anger with their vanities,

**16:3** behold, I will raise up after Baasha and after his house, and I will make his house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**16:4** The one of Baasha who dies in the city, the dogs shall devour him; and the one of his who dies in the field, the birds of heaven shall devour him."

**16:5** Now the rest of the acts of Baasha, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:6** So Baasha slept with his fathers, and was buried in Tirzah; and Elah his son reigned in his place in the twentieth year of King Asa.

**16:7** And by the hand of Jehu son of Hanani, the Lord spoke against Baasha and against his house concerning all the evil which he did in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger by the works of his hands, in being like the house of Jeroboam, and because he had struck it down.

**16:8** And Elah son of Baasha reigned over Israel two years in Tirzah.

**16:9** And Zimri, the commander of half the cavalry, conspired against him; and he was in Tirzah drinking himself drunk in the house of Osa, the steward in Tirzah.

**16:10** And Zimri went in and struck him and put him to death, and reigned in his place.

**16:11** And it came to pass, when he began to reign, as soon as he sat upon his throne, that he struck down all the house of Baasha,

**16:12** according to the word which the Lord spoke against the house of Baasha and to Jehu the prophet,

**16:13** for all the sins of Baasha and of Elah his son, by which he caused Israel to sin, to provoke the Lord God of Israel to anger with their vanities.

**16:14** Now the rest of the acts of Elah which he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:15** And Zimri reigned seven years in Tirzah; and the camp of Israel was against Gibeon, which belonged to the foreigners.

**16:16** And the people in the camp heard it said, "Zimri has conspired and has struck down the king." Therefore in Israel they made Zimri, the commander of the army, king over Israel that very day in the camp.

**16:17** Then Zimri, and all Israel with him, went up in Gibbethon and laid siege against Tirzah.

**16:18** And it came to pass, when Zimri saw that his city was captured, that they went into the inner vault of the king's house; and the king burned the king's house over him, and he died,

**16:19** because of his sins which he committed in doing what was evil in the sight of the Lord, in walking in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

**16:20** Now the rest of the acts of Zimri, and his conspiracies which he formed, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:21** Then the people of Israel were divided: half of the people followed Tibni son of Ginath to make him king, and half of the people followed Zimri.

**16:22** And the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath were defeated; and Tibni died, and Joram his brother at that time, and Zimri reigned after Tibni.

**16:23** In the thirty-first year of King Asa, Zimri became king over Israel for twelve years; in Tirzah he reigned six years.

**16:24** And Zimri bought the mountain of Samaria from Shemer, the owner of the mountain, for two talents of silver; and he built on the mountain, and they called the name of the mountain which they built, after the name of Shemer the owner of the mountain, Samaria.

**16:25** And Zimri did what was evil in the sight of the Lord; he acted more wickedly than all who were before him.

**16:26** And he walked in all the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin, to provoke to anger with their vanities.

**16:27** Now the rest of the acts of Zimri, and all that he did, and his might, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:28** So Zimri slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria; and Ahab his son reigned in his place. And in the eleventh year of Zimri, Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king; he was thirty-five years old when he began his reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Gabuza, daughter of Selei. And he walked in the way of Asa his father, and did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the sight of the Lord; yet they did not remove the high places; they sacrificed and burned incense on the high places. Now the alliances which Jehoshaphat made, and all the might which he showed, and those with whom he waged war, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah? And the remainder of the corrupt unions which they had undertaken in the days of Asa his father, he removed from the land. And there was no king in Syria; Nasib was the king. He built a ship for Tarshish to go to Sophir, to go for gold; but it did not go, for the ship was shattered at Ezion Geber. Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will send forth your servants and my young men in the ship." But Jehoshaphat was not willing. And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers in the city of David; and Joram his son reigned in his place.

**16:29** In the second year of Jehoshaphat, Ahab son of Zimri became king; he reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.

**16:30** And Ahab did what was evil in the sight of the Lord; he acted more wickedly than all who were before him.

**16:31** And it was not enough for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, but he took as his wife Jezebel daughter of Ethbaal, king of the Sidonians; and he went and served Baal and worshiped him.

**16:32** And he set up an altar to Baal in the house of his abominations, which he had built in Samaria.

**16:33** And Ahab made a grove; and Ahab did even more to commit provocations to provoke his own soul, so as to be utterly destroyed; he did more evil than all the kings of Israel who were before him.

**16:34** Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho; at the cost of Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of Segub his youngest he set up its gates, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of Joshua son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **16:9** — The Greek names the steward Osa (Hebrew Arza); in verse 15 the Greek gives Zimri's reign as seven years (Hebrew seven days) and places the camp at Gibeon rather than Gibbethon.
- **16:16** — Throughout verses 16 through 29, the Greek text reads the name Zimri where the Hebrew introduces Omri as the army commander, king of Israel, and father of Ahab.
- **16:22** — The Greek adds that Tibni's brother Joram also died at that time.
- **16:28** — The Greek places the summary of Jehoshaphat's reign here (paralleling 1 Kings 22:41-50 in the Hebrew), with distinct readings including "Gabuza, daughter of Selei," "Syria" (Hebrew Edom), "Sophir" (Hebrew Ophir), and the transliterated Hebrew title "Nasib" (meaning "deputy").
- **16:33** — Where the Hebrew states that Ahab provoked the Lord God of Israel to anger, the Greek reads that he provoked "his own soul, so as to be utterly destroyed."
