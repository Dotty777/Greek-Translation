## Text

**15:1** And in the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abiu son of Jeroboam became king over Judah.

**15:2** And he reigned six years; and the name of his mother was Maachah, daughter of Absalom.

**15:3** And he walked in the sins of his father which he had committed before him, and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of his father.

**15:4** For because of David, the Lord gave him a remnant, that He might establish his children after him and establish Jerusalem,

**15:5** since David did what was right in the sight of the Lord; he did not turn aside from all that He commanded him all the days of his life.

**15:7** Now the rest of the acts of Abiu, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days for the kings of Judah? And there was war between Abiu and Jeroboam.

**15:8** And Abiu slept with his fathers in the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his place.

**15:9** In the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa became king over Judah,

**15:10** and he reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Ana, daughter of Absalom.

**15:11** And Asa did what was right in the sight of the Lord, as did David his father.

**15:12** And he removed the initiation rites from the land, and banished all the practices which his fathers had practiced.

**15:13** And he removed Ana his mother from being ruler, because she had held an assembly in her grove; and Asa cut down her hiding places and burned them with fire in the brook of the cedars.

**15:14** But the high places he did not remove; nevertheless, the heart of Asa was perfect with the Lord all his days.

**15:15** And he brought in the pillars of his father, and his own pillars he brought into the house of the Lord, of silver and gold, and vessels.

**15:16** And there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all the days.

**15:17** And Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah, and built Ramah, that there might be no one going out or coming in to Asa king of Judah.

**15:18** Then Asa took the silver and the gold that was found in the treasuries of the king's house, and delivered them into the hands of his servants; and King Asa sent them to the son of Hader, son of Taberema, son of Hazin, king of Syria, who dwelt in Damascus, saying,

**15:19** "Make a covenant between me and my father and your father. Behold, I have sent to you gifts of silver and gold. Come, break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, and he will withdraw from me."

**15:20** So the son of Hader heeded King Asa, and sent the commanders of his forces against the cities of Israel; and they struck Ain, Dan, and Adelmath, and all Chezrath, as far as all the land of Naphtali.

**15:21** And it came to pass, when Baasha heard it, that he ceased building Ramah and returned to Tirzah.

**15:22** Then King Asa summoned all Judah to Enakim; and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its timber, with which Baasha had built, and with them King Asa built every hill of Benjamin and the watchtower.

**15:23** Now the rest of the acts of Asa, and all his might which he performed, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days for the kings of Judah? Nevertheless, in the time of his old age he was afflicted in his feet.

**15:24** So Asa slept, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place.

**15:25** Now Nebat son of Jeroboam became king over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned in Israel two years.

**15:26** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of his father, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

**15:27** And Baasha son of Ahijah besieged him at the house of Belaan; and the son of Ahijah struck him down in Gibbethon, which belonged to the foreigners, while Nebat and all Israel were laying siege against Gibbethon.

**15:28** And Baasha put him to death in the third year of King Asa son of Abiu, and reigned.

**15:29** And it came to pass, when he became king, that he struck down the house of Jeroboam, and did not leave any that breathed of Jeroboam until he had utterly destroyed him, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of the servant Ahijah the Shilonite,

**15:30** because of the sins of Jeroboam, by which he caused Israel to sin, and because of his provocation with which he provoked the Lord God of Israel to anger.

**15:31** Now the rest of the acts of Nebat, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of the days for the kings of Israel?

**15:33** And in the third year of the king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king over Israel in Tirzah for twenty-four years.

**15:34** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — The Greek text identifies Abiu (Hebrew Abijam/Abijah) as the son of Jeroboam rather than Rehoboam, and in verse 2 gives his reign as six years rather than three.
- **15:10** — Here and in verse 13 the Greek gives the queen mother's name as Ana where the Hebrew reads Maachah; verse 13 also renders the Kidron Valley literally as "the brook of the cedars."
- **15:18** — The Greek literally translates the name Ben-Hadad as "the son of Hader" (here and in verse 20) and omits the treasuries of the house of the Lord; verse 20 also preserves the distinct Greek place names Ain, Adelmath, and Chezrath.
- **15:22** — The Greek treats the Hebrew phrase "none was exempted" as a place name ("to Enakim") and translates Geba and Mizpah as common nouns ("every hill" and "the watchtower").
- **15:25** — Here and in verses 27 and 31 the Greek gives the name of Jeroboam's son as Nebat (matching Jeroboam's father) where the Hebrew reads Nadab; in verse 27 the Greek also reads "the house of Belaan" where the Hebrew reads "the house of Issachar."
