## Text

**18:1** And it came to pass after many days that the word of the Lord came to Elijah in the third year, saying, "Go and show yourself to Ahab, and I will give rain upon the face of the earth."

**18:2** So Elijah went to show himself to Ahab, and the famine was severe in Samaria.

**18:3** And Ahab called Obadiah the steward; now Obadiah feared the Lord exceedingly.

**18:4** For it came to pass, when Jezebel was striking down the prophets of the Lord, that Obadiah took one hundred men who were prophets and hid them by fifties in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water.

**18:5** And Ahab said to Obadiah, "Come, and let us pass through the land to the springs of water and to the brooks; perhaps we may find grass and preserve the horses and mules alive, and they shall not be destroyed from the tents."

**18:6** So they divided the way between themselves to pass through it: Ahab went by one way, and Obadiah went by another way alone.

**18:7** Now Obadiah was on the way alone, and Elijah came alone to meet him; and Obadiah hastened and fell upon his face and said, "Is it indeed you, my lord Elijah?"

**18:8** And Elijah said to him, "It is I. Go, tell your lord, 'Behold, Elijah!'"

**18:9** And Obadiah said, "How have I sinned, that you are delivering your servant into the hand of Ahab to put me to death?

**18:10** As the Lord your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to seek you; and when they said, 'He is not here,' he even burned the kingdom and its regions because he had not found you.

**18:11** And now you say, 'Go, report to your lord.'

**18:12** And it shall come to pass, as soon as I depart from you, that the Spirit of the Lord will carry you away to a land that I do not know; and I will go in to report to Ahab, and he will slay me. Yet your servant has feared the Lord from his youth.

**18:13** Was it not reported to you, my lord, what I did when Jezebel was slaying the prophets of the Lord, how I hid one hundred men of the prophets of the Lord, fifty to a cave, and fed them with loaves of bread and water?

**18:14** And now you say to me, 'Go, tell your lord, "Behold, Elijah!"' and he will slay me."

**18:15** And Elijah said, "As the Lord of hosts lives, before whom I have stood, today I will surely show myself to him."

**18:16** So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and reported to him; and Ahab ran out and went to meet Elijah.

**18:17** And it came to pass, when Ahab saw Elijah, that Ahab said to Elijah, "Are you yourself the one who perverts Israel?"

**18:18** And Elijah said, "I do not pervert Israel, but rather you and the house of your father, in that you have forsaken the Lord your God, and you have gone after the Baals.

**18:19** Now therefore send and gather to me all Israel to Mount Carmel, and the prophets of shame, four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the groves, four hundred, who eat at the table of Jezebel."

**18:20** So Ahab sent throughout all Israel, and gathered all the prophets together to Mount Carmel.

**18:21** And Elijah drew near to all of them, and Elijah said to them, "How long will you limp on both knees? If the Lord is God, follow after Him; but if Baal, follow after him." And the people did not answer a word.

**18:22** Then Elijah said to the people, "I alone am left a prophet of the Lord, while the prophets of Baal are four hundred and fifty men, and the prophets of the grove are four hundred.

**18:23** Let them give us two oxen, and let them choose one for themselves, and cut it in pieces and lay it on the wood, and put no fire to it; and I will prepare the other ox, and I will by no means put fire to it.

**18:24** And cry out in the name of your gods, and I will call upon the name of the Lord my God; and it shall be that the God who answers by fire, He is God." And all the people answered and said, "Good is the word which you have spoken."

**18:25** And Elijah said to the prophets of shame, "Choose for yourselves one bullock and prepare it first, for you are many; and call upon the name of your god, and put no fire to it."

**18:26** So they took the bullock and prepared it, and called upon the name of Baal from morning until midday, and said, "Hear us, O Baal, hear us!" But there was no voice and no hearing; and they ran about upon the altar which they had made.

**18:27** And midday came, and Elijah the Tishbite mocked them and said, "Call out with a loud voice, for he is a god! For he is occupied in conversation, and perhaps at the same time he is conducting business, or perhaps he is sleeping and will wake up!"

**18:28** So they called out with a loud voice, and cut themselves with knife and lances until blood gushed out upon them,

**18:29** and they prophesied until the afternoon had passed. And it came to pass, about the time for the sacrifice to be offered up, that Elijah spoke to the prophets of the abominations, saying, "Stand aside from now on, and I will prepare my burnt offering." And they stood aside and departed.

**18:30** Then Elijah said to the people, "Draw near to me." And all the people drew near to him.

**18:31** And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of Israel, as the Lord spoke to him, saying, "Israel shall be your name."

**18:32** And he built the stones in the name of the Lord, and repaired the altar that had been torn down; and he made a sea around the altar, holding two measures of seed.

**18:33** And he stacked the split wood upon the altar which he had made, and cut the burnt offering in pieces, and laid on the split wood, and stacked it upon the altar.

**18:34** And he said, "Fetch me four jars of water, and pour it upon the burnt offering and upon the split wood." And they did so. And he said, "Do it a second time," and they did it a second time. And he said, "Do it a third time," and they did it a third time.

**18:35** And the water ran all around the altar, and they filled the sea with water.

**18:36** And Elijah cried aloud to heaven and said, "O Lord, the God of Abraham and Isaac and Israel, hear me, O Lord, hear me today by fire, and let all this people know that You are the Lord, the God of Israel, and I am Your servant, and for Your sake I have done these works.

**18:37** Hear me, O Lord, hear me, that this people may know that You are the Lord God, and that You have turned the heart of this people back again."

**18:38** Then fire fell from the Lord out of heaven, and devoured the burnt offerings and the split wood and the water that was in the sea, and the fire licked up the stones and the dust.

**18:39** And all the people fell upon their faces and said, "Truly, the Lord is God; He is God!"

**18:40** And Elijah said to the people, "Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape!" So they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the brook Kishon and slaughtered them there.

**18:41** And Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, and eat and drink, for there is the sound of the feet of the rain."

**18:42** So Ahab went up to eat and to drink; and Elijah went up to Carmel, and bowed down to the earth and put his face between his knees,

**18:43** and he said to his young servant, "Go up and look toward the sea." And the young servant looked and said, "There is nothing." And Elijah said, "Turn back seven times, and return seven times."

**18:44** And the young servant returned seven times; and it came to pass at the seventh time that behold, a small cloud like a man's footprint was bringing up water. And he said, "Go up and say to Ahab, 'Harness your chariot and go down, lest the rain overtake you.'"

**18:45** And it came to pass meanwhile that the heaven grew dark with clouds and wind, and the rain became great; and Ahab wept and went to Israel.

**18:46** And the hand of the Lord was upon Elijah; and he girded up his loins, and ran before Ahab to Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:10** — The Greek text states that Ahab "burned the kingdom and its regions," whereas the Hebrew reads that he made the kingdom or nation swear an oath.
- **18:19** — Here and in verse 25, the Greek substitutes "shame" for Baal (and "abominations" in verse 29), and renders Asherah as "the groves" (or "grove" in verse 22).
- **18:29** — Rather than stating that no one answered, the Greek records Elijah instructing the prophets of the abominations to stand aside so that he may prepare his burnt offering, followed by their departure.
- **18:32** — Here and in verses 35 and 38, the Greek uses thalassa ("sea") for the water trench or basin around the altar, echoing the bronze sea of the temple.
- **18:45** — Here and in verse 46, the Greek reads "Israel" instead of "Jezreel" (as in the Hebrew), and in verse 45 reports that Ahab "wept" rather than "rode."
