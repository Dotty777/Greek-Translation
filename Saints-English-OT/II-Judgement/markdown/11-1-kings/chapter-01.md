## Text

**1:1** Now King David was old, advanced in days, and they covered him with garments, yet he did not grow warm.

**1:2** And his servants said, "Let them seek a young virgin for the king, and she shall stand before the king and shall cherish him, and lie with him, and my lord the king shall be warmed."

**1:3** So they sought a fair young woman from all the territory of Israel, and they found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.

**1:4** And the young woman was exceedingly fair; and she cherished the king and ministered to him, yet the king did not know her.

**1:5** Then Adonijah the son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, "I will reign." And he prepared for himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.

**1:6** And his father had never restrained him, saying, "Why have you done this?" Moreover, he also was very handsome in appearance, and she bore him after Absalom.

**1:7** And his words were with Joab the son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, and they gave aid following Adonijah.

**1:8** But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei, and Rei, and the mighty sons of David were not following Adonijah.

**1:9** And Adonijah sacrificed sheep and calves and lambs with Aithe of Zoheleth, who was beside Rogel; and he invited his brothers and the notable men of Judah, the servants of the king.

**1:10** But Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, and the mighty men, and Solomon his brother he did not invite.

**1:11** Then Nathan spoke to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon, saying, "Have you not heard that Adonijah the son of Haggith has become king, and our lord David does not know it?

**1:12** And now come, please let me give you counsel, and deliver your life and the life of your son Solomon.

**1:13** Come, go in to King David and say to him, 'Did you not, my lord, O king, swear to your maidservant, saying, "Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne"? Why then has Adonijah become king?'

**1:14** And behold, while you are still speaking there with the king, I also will come in afterward and fulfill your words."

**1:15** So Bathsheba went in to the king into the chamber; and the king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was ministering to the king.

**1:16** And Bathsheba bowed and did obeisance to the king; and the king said, "What do you wish?"

**1:17** And she said, "My lord, you swore by your God to your maidservant, saying, 'Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne.'

**1:18** And now, behold, Adonijah has become king, and you, my lord, O king, did not know it.

**1:19** He has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the sons of the king, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army; but Solomon your servant he did not invite.

**1:20** And as for you, my lord, O king, the eyes of all Israel are upon you, to declare to them who shall sit upon the throne of my lord the king after him.

**1:21** And it shall come to pass, when my lord the king sleeps with his fathers, that I and my son Solomon shall be offenders."

**1:22** And behold, while she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came.

**1:23** And it was told to the king, "Behold, Nathan the prophet." And he went in before the king, and did obeisance to the king with his face to the ground.

**1:24** And Nathan said, "My lord, O king, have you said, 'Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne'?

**1:25** For he has gone down today, and has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the sons of the king, and the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest; and behold, they are eating and drinking before him, and they have said, 'Long live King Adonijah!'

**1:26** But me, even me your servant, and Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Solomon your servant he did not invite.

**1:27** Has this thing been done through my lord the king, and have you not made known to your servant who shall sit upon the throne of my lord the king after him?"

**1:28** Then David answered and said, "Call Bathsheba to me." And she came in before the king and stood before him.

**1:29** And the king swore and said, "As the Lord lives, who has redeemed my soul out of all distress,

**1:30** even as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, 'Solomon your son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne in my place,' surely so I will do this very day."

**1:31** Then Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground, and did obeisance to the king, and said, "May my lord King David live forever!"

**1:32** And King David said, "Call to me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada." So they came in before the king.

**1:33** And the king said to them, "Take with you the servants of your lord, and cause my son Solomon to ride upon my own mule, and bring him down to Gihon;

**1:34** and anoint him there—Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet—as king over Israel, and sound the horn and say, 'Long live King Solomon!'

**1:35** And he shall sit upon my throne and reign in my place, and I have commanded him to be ruler over Israel and Judah."

**1:36** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, "So be it! Thus may the God of my lord the king confirm it.

**1:37** As the Lord was with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and may He magnify his throne above the throne of my lord King David."

**1:38** So Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites went down, and set Solomon upon King David's mule, and brought him to Gihon.

**1:39** And Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon, and he sounded the horn; and all the people said, "Long live King Solomon!"

**1:40** And all the people went up after him, and they danced in dances and rejoiced with great gladness, and the earth was split by their sound.

**1:41** And Adonijah and all his guests heard it as they finished eating; and Joab heard the sound of the horn and said, "What is the sound of the city resounding?"

**1:42** While he was still speaking, behold, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came in; and Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a man of valor, and bring good tidings."

**1:43** And Jonathan answered and said, "Truly, our lord King David has made Solomon king;

**1:44** and the king has sent with him Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and they have set him upon the king's mule;

**1:45** and Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him in Gihon, and they have gone up from there rejoicing, and the city resounded; this is the sound which you have heard.

**1:46** Moreover, Solomon has sat upon the throne of the kingdom,

**1:47** and the servants of the king went in to bless our lord King David, saying, 'May God make the name of Solomon better than your name, and magnify his throne above your throne.' And the king bowed in worship upon the bed;

**1:48** and thus also the king said, 'Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who has given today one from my seed sitting upon my throne, while my eyes behold it.'"

**1:49** Then all the guests of Adonijah rose up, and went, every man to his own way.

**1:50** And Adonijah was afraid before Solomon; and he arose, and went away, and took hold of the horns of the altar.

**1:51** And it was told to Solomon, saying, "Behold, Adonijah fears King Solomon, and he holds fast to the horns of the altar, saying, 'Let King Solomon swear to me today that he will not put his servant to death with the sword.'"

**1:52** And Solomon said, "If he proves to be a son of valor, not one of his hairs shall fall to the ground; but if wickedness is found in him, he shall be put to death."

**1:53** So King Solomon sent and brought him down from upon the altar; and he came in and did obeisance to King Solomon, and Solomon said to him, "Go to your house."

## Translator Notes

- **1:8** — The Greek text reads "the mighty sons of David" rather than the Hebrew "mighty men who belonged to David," and transliterates Rei as Resei.
- **1:9** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by the Stone of Zoheleth, which is by En Rogel," the Septuagint renders a personal name and designation, "with Aithe of Zoheleth, who was beside Rogel," and calls the invited guests "the notable men of Judah."
- **1:36** — Following the Greek text, Benaiah prays that "the God of my lord the king" may "confirm" the royal decree, rather than the Hebrew "so may the LORD, the God of my lord the king, say."
- **1:40** — The Greek text reads "danced in dances," reflecting a Hebrew root for dancing, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "played on flutes."
- **1:48** — The Greek text preserves the phrase "from my seed" before "sitting upon my throne," underscoring David's dynastic succession.
