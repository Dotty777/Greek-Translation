## Text

**5:1** And the appointed officers thus provided for King Solomon, and all the prescribed provisions for the table of the king, each in his month; they do not deviate from a single word. And the barley and the straw for the horses and for the chariots they carried to the place wherever the king might be, each according to his appointed order.

**5:2** And these were the required provisions for Solomon in one day: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal,

**5:3** and ten choice calves, and twenty pasture-fed oxen, and one hundred sheep, besides deer and gazelles and choice fattened fowl.

**5:4** For he was ruler beyond the River, and he had peace from all sides round about.

**5:9** And the Lord gave Solomon understanding and exceedingly great wisdom, and an outpouring of heart like the sand that is beside the sea.

**5:10** And Solomon was increased exceedingly above the understanding of all ancient men and above all the discerning men of Egypt.

**5:11** And he was wiser than all men; and he was wiser than Gaithan the Zareite, and Ainan, and Chalkad, and Darala, the son of Mal.

**5:12** And Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs were five thousand.

**5:13** And he spoke concerning the trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon even to the hyssop that comes forth through the wall; and he spoke concerning the beasts, and concerning the birds, and concerning the creeping things, and concerning the fish.

**5:14** And Solomon built Gezer. And all the peoples came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth, as many as heard of his wisdom. And Solomon took the daughter of Pharaoh for himself as a wife, and brought her into the city of David until he finished the house of the Lord, and his own house, and the wall of Jerusalem. When Pharaoh king of Egypt went up, he seized Gezer and burned it, and the Canaanite dwelling in Mergab, and Pharaoh gave them as a dowry to his daughter, the wife of Solomon.

**5:15** And Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to anoint Solomon in place of David his father, for Hiram had loved David all the days.

**5:16** And Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,

**5:17** "You know David my father, that he was not able to build a house for the name of the Lord my God because of the wars that surrounded him, until He put them under the soles of his feet.

**5:18** And now the Lord my God has given me rest on every side; there is no adversary and there is no evil mishap.

**5:19** And behold, I say, 'I will build a house for the name of the Lord my God,' just as the Lord God spoke to David my father, saying, 'Your son, whom I will set in your place upon your throne, he shall build the house for My name.'

**5:20** And now give command, and let them cut timber for me from Lebanon; and behold, my servants will be with your servants, and for your service I will give to you according to all that you may say; for you know that we do not have the skill of ourselves to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:21** And it came to pass, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced exceedingly and said, "Blessed be God this day, who has given to David a discerning son over this great people."

**5:22** And he sent to Solomon, saying, "I have heard concerning all the things that you have sent to me; I will do all your desire, both timber of cedar and of pine.

**5:23** My servants shall bring them down from Lebanon to the sea; I will form them into rafts as far as the place of which you send word to me, and I will break them apart there, and you shall take them away; and you shall do my desire in giving bread to my household."

**5:24** And Hiram continually gave Solomon cedars and all his desire.

**5:25** And Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat and macheir for his household, and twenty thousand baths of beaten oil; thus Solomon gave to Hiram year by year.

**5:26** And the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon, just as He had spoken to him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between themselves.

**5:27** And the king raised a levy even from all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.

**5:28** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month in shifts; one month they were in Lebanon and two months at their own home; and Adoniram was over the levy.

**5:29** And Solomon had seventy thousand bearers of burdens and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain,

**5:30** besides the chief officers who were appointed over the works of Solomon, three thousand six hundred overseers who carried out the works.

**5:32** And they prepared the stones and the timbers for three years.

## Translator Notes

- **5:11** — The Greek preserves distinct proper-name forms—Gaithan the Zareite, Ainan, Chalkad, and Darala—and reads the singular 'the son of Mal' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol.'
- **5:12** — The Greek reads 'five thousand' songs, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'one thousand and five.'
- **5:14** — In the Septuagint, this verse joins Solomon's marriage to Pharaoh's daughter and Pharaoh's capture of Gezer and Mergab (an LXX-distinct place name) directly to the notice of Solomon's renown, whereas the Hebrew places those events in chapters 3 and 9.
- **5:15** — The Greek states that Hiram sent his servants 'to anoint Solomon,' whereas the Hebrew states that he sent them because he had heard that Solomon had been anointed king.
- **5:25** — 'Macheir' is an untranslated Greek transliteration corresponding to Hebrew 'makkolet' (food or provisions); the Greek also reads 'twenty thousand baths' of oil where the Hebrew reads 'twenty kors.'
