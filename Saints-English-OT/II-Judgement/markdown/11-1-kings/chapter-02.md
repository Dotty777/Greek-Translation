## Text

**2:1** Now the days of David drew near that he should die, and he addressed Solomon his son, saying,

**2:2** "I am going the way of all the earth; and you shall be strong, and become a man,

**2:3** and you shall keep the charge of your God, to walk in His ways, to observe His commandments, and the ordinances, and the judgments written in the law of Moses, that you may understand what you shall do according to all that I command you;

**2:4** that the Lord may establish His word which He spoke, saying, 'If your sons guard their way, to walk before Me in truth with all their heart,' saying, 'There shall not be cut off from you a man from upon the throne of Israel.'

**2:5** Moreover, you also know all that Joab the son of Zeruiah did to me, all that he did to the two commanders of the hosts of Israel, to Abner the son of Ner and to Amasa the son of Jether, and he slew them, and put the blood of war upon his belt that was around his waist and upon his sandal that was on his foot.

**2:6** And you shall do according to your wisdom, and you shall bring down his gray hair in peace to Hades.

**2:7** But to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite you shall show mercy, and they shall be among those who eat at your table; for so they drew near to me when I fled from the presence of Absalom your brother.

**2:8** And behold, with you is Shimei the son of Gera, a son of the Jeminite, from Bahurim; and he cursed me with a grievous curse on the day that I went to the camps, yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'

**2:9** And you shall by no means hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you shall do to him; and you shall bring down his gray hair with blood to Hades."

**2:10** And David slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.

**2:11** And the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years; in Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem thirty-three years.

**2:12** Then Solomon sat upon the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was exceedingly established.

**2:13** And Adonijah came in to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon and bowed down to her. And she said, "Is your coming in peace?" And he said, "In peace.

**2:14** I have a word for you." And she said to him, "Speak."

**2:15** And he said to her, "You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel set their face toward me to be king; yet the kingdom turned about and became my brother's, for it came to him from the Lord.

**2:16** And now I ask one petition of you; do not turn away your face." And Bathsheba said to him, "Speak."

**2:17** And he said to her, "Speak, I pray, to Solomon the king, for he will not turn away his face from you, and he will give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife."

**2:18** And Bathsheba said, "Very well, I will speak for you to the king."

**2:19** So Bathsheba went in to King Solomon to speak to him on behalf of Adonijah. And the king rose up to meet her and kissed her, and sat down upon the throne; and a throne was set for the mother of the king, and she sat at his right hand.

**2:20** And she said to him, "I ask one small petition of you; do not turn away your face." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, and I will not turn you away."

**2:21** And she said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given, I pray, to Adonijah your brother as a wife."

**2:22** And King Solomon answered and said to his mother, "And why have you asked for Abishag for Adonijah? Ask the kingdom for him also; for he is my brother who is older than I, and with him is Abiathar the priest, and with him is Joab the son of Zeruiah, the commander of the host, as his companion."

**2:23** Then King Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, "May God do so to me, and more also, for Adonijah has spoken this word against his own life.

**2:24** And now, as the Lord lives, who has established me and set me upon the throne of David my father, and He Himself has made me a house just as the Lord spoke, surely today Adonijah shall be put to death."

**2:25** So King Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and he slew him, and Adonijah died on that day.

**2:26** And to Abiathar the priest the king said, "Depart to Anathoth, to your field, for you are a man of death on this day; yet I will not put you to death, because you bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord before my father, and because you were afflicted in all things in which my father was afflicted."

**2:27** So Solomon cast out Abiathar from being a priest of the Lord, that the word of the Lord might be fulfilled which He spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

**2:28** Now the report came to Joab the son of Zeruiah, for Joab had turned aside after Adonijah, though he had not turned aside after Absalom. And Joab fled to the tabernacle of the Lord and laid hold of the horns of the altar.

**2:29** And it was told to Solomon, saying, "Joab has fled to the tent of the Lord, and behold, he is holding fast to the horns of the altar." And Solomon sent to Joab, saying, "What has happened to you, that you have fled to the altar?" And Joab said, "Because I was afraid of your presence, and I fled to the Lord." Then Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go and slay him, and bury him."

**2:30** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada came to the tent of the Lord and said to him, "Thus says the king, 'Come out.'" And Joab said, "I will not come out, for here I will die." Then Benaiah the son of Jehoiada returned and spoke to the king, saying, "Thus Joab has spoken, and thus he has answered me."

**2:31** And the king said to him, "Go and do to him just as he has said, and slay him and bury him; and today you shall remove from me and from the house of my father the blood which he shed without cause.

**2:32** And the Lord has returned the blood of his unrighteousness upon his own head; and he fell upon two men more righteous and better than he, and slew them with the sword, while my father David did not know of their blood: Abner the son of Ner, commander of the host of Israel, and Amasa the son of Jether, commander of the host of Judah.

**2:33** And his blood has returned upon his own head and upon the head of his seed forever; but to David, and to his seed, and to his house, and to his throne, may there be peace forever from the Lord."

**2:34** And Jehoiada fell upon Joab and put him to death; and he buried him in his own house in the wilderness.

**2:35** And the king appointed Benaiah the son of Jehoiada in his place over the army; and the kingdom was established in Jerusalem. And Zadok the priest, the king appointed him as first priest in place of Abiathar. And the Lord gave Solomon understanding and exceedingly great wisdom, and breadth of heart like the sand that is beside the sea. And the understanding of Solomon was multiplied above all the ancient sons and beyond all the men of understanding of Egypt. And he took the daughter of Pharaoh and brought her into the city of David until he finished the house of the Lord first and the wall of Jerusalem all around; in seven years he made and finished it. And Solomon had seventy thousand bearing burdens and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain. And Solomon made the sea, and the supports, and the great lavers, and the pillars, and the fountain of the court, and the bronze sea. And he built the citadel as a bulwark upon it; he cut through the city of David. Thus the daughter of Pharaoh went up from the city of David into her house which he had built for her; then he built the citadel. And Solomon offered up three times in the year burnt offerings and peace offerings upon the altar which he had built to the Lord, and he burned incense before the Lord. And he finished the house. And these were the rulers appointed over the works of Solomon: three thousand six hundred overseers of the people who performed the works. And he built Assur, and Magao, and Gezer, and Upper Beth-horon, and Baalath; yet after he built the house of the Lord and the wall of Jerusalem all around, after these things he built these cities. And while David was still alive, he commanded Solomon, saying, "Behold, with you is Shimei the son of Gera, a son of the seed of the Jeminite from Hebron. This man cursed me with a grievous curse on the day on which I went to the camps; yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'He shall not be put to death with the sword.' And now do not hold him guiltless, for you are a man of understanding, and you will know what you shall do to him; and you shall bring down his gray hair with blood to Hades."

**2:36** Then the king summoned Shimei and said to him, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem and dwell there, and you shall not go out from there anywhere.

**2:37** And it shall be, on the day of your going out and when you cross the brook Kidron, know for certain that you shall surely die; your blood shall be upon your own head." And the king made him swear an oath on that day.

**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, "The word that you have spoken is good, my lord the king; so your servant will do." And Shimei dwelt in Jerusalem three years.

**2:39** And it came to pass after the three years that two servants of Shimei fled to Achish the son of Amesa, king of Gath. And it was told to Shimei, saying, "Behold, your servants are in Gath."

**2:40** So Shimei arose and saddled his donkey, and went to Gath to Achish to seek out his servants; and Shimei went and brought his servants from Gath.

**2:41** And it was told to Solomon, saying, "Shimei has gone from Jerusalem to Gath and has brought back his servants."

**2:42** Then the king sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the Lord and solemnly testify to you, saying, 'On whatever day you go out from Jerusalem and go to the right or to the left, know for certain that you shall surely die'?

**2:43** Why then have you not kept the oath of the Lord and the commandment which I commanded concerning you?"

**2:44** And the king said to Shimei, "You know all your wickedness which your heart knows, the things which you did to David my father; and the Lord has repaid your wickedness upon your own head.

**2:45** And King Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the Lord forever."

**2:46** So King Solomon commanded Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and he went out and slew him. And King Solomon was exceedingly prudent and wise; and Judah and Israel were exceedingly many, like the sand that is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking and rejoicing. And Solomon was ruler over all the kingdoms, and they brought gifts and served Solomon all the days of his life. And Solomon began to open the dominions of Lebanon; and he built Thermai in the wilderness. And this was the meal for Solomon: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal, ten choice calves and twenty pasture-fed oxen and one hundred sheep, besides deer, and gazelles, and choice pasture-fed fowl. Was he not ruler over all beyond the River, from Raphi to Gaza, over all the kings beyond the River? And he had peace on all sides around him. And Judah and Israel dwelt in confidence, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, eating and drinking, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon. And these were the rulers of Solomon: Azariah the son of Zadok the priest; and Orniou the son of Nathan, chief of the officers, and he ran to his house; and Suba the scribe; and Baasha the son of Achithalam, the recorder; and Abi the son of Joab, commander of the host; and Achire the son of Edrai, over the levies; and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, over the palace stewardship and over the brickworks; and Kachur the son of Nathan, the counselor. And Solomon had forty thousand brood mares for chariots, and twelve thousand horses; and he was ruler over all the kings from the River even to the land of the foreigners and to the borders of Egypt. Solomon the son of David reigned over Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The Septuagint omits the negative particle found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reading literally "and you shall bring down his gray hair in peace to Hades."
- **2:34** — The Septuagint reads "Jehoiada" alone here, omitting "Benaiah the son of" before his name.
- **2:35** — The Septuagint includes an extended miscellany summarizing Solomon's wisdom, building works, and David's charge regarding Shimei (here placed "from Hebron" rather than Bahurim), with LXX-distinct names Assur (for Hazor), Magao (for Megiddo), and Upper Beth-horon (for Lower Beth-horon).
- **2:39** — The Septuagint names Achish's father "Amesa" rather than "Maacah" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **2:46** — The Septuagint appends an extensive summary of Solomon's reign, royal provisions, territory, and officials, featuring LXX-distinct names including Thermai (for Tadmor/Tamar), Raphi (for Tiphsah), and the officers Orniou, Suba, Baasha son of Achithalam, Abi son of Joab, Achire son of Edrai, and Kachur son of Nathan.
