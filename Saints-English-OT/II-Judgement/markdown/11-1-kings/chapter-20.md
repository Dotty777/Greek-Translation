## Text

**20:1** And Naboth the Israelite had a certain vineyard beside the threshing floor of Ahab king of Samaria.

**20:2** And Ahab spoke to Naboth, saying, "Give me your vineyard, and it shall be for me a garden of herbs, because it is near to my house, and I will give you another vineyard better than it; or if it is pleasing in your sight, I will give you silver in exchange for this vineyard of yours, and it shall be for me a garden of herbs."

**20:3** And Naboth said to Ahab, "Far be it from me, by my God, to give the inheritance of my fathers to you."

**20:4** And the spirit of Ahab became troubled, and he lay down upon his bed and covered his face, and did not eat bread.

**20:5** And Jezebel his wife came in to him and spoke to him, "Why is your spirit troubled, and why are you not eating bread?"

**20:6** And he said to her, "Because I spoke to Naboth the Israelite, saying, 'Give me your vineyard for silver; or if you wish, I will give you another vineyard in its place.' And he said, 'I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers.'"

**20:7** And Jezebel his wife said to him, "Do you now act the king over Israel like this? Arise, eat bread, and be yourself again; I will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Israelite."

**20:8** And she wrote a letter in the name of Ahab and sealed it with his seal, and sent the letter to the elders and the free men who dwelt with Naboth.

**20:9** And it was written in the letters, saying, "Observe a fast, and seat Naboth at the head of the people;

**20:10** and set two men, sons of lawless men."

**20:11** And the men of his city, the elders and the free men who dwelt in his city, did just as Jezebel had sent to them, and according to what was written in the letters that she had sent to them.

**20:12** And they proclaimed a fast, and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

**20:13** And they sat opposite him, and testified against him, saying, "You have blessed God and the king." And they brought him outside the city and stoned him with stones, and he died.

**20:14** And they sent to Jezebel, saying, "Naboth has been stoned and is dead."

**20:15** And it came to pass, when Jezebel heard, that she said to Ahab, "Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Israelite, who did not give it to you for silver; for Naboth is not alive, for he is dead."

**20:16** And it came to pass, when Ahab heard that Naboth the Israelite was dead, that he tore his garments and put on sackcloth. And it came to pass after these things that Ahab arose and went down to the vineyard of Naboth the Israelite to take possession of it.

**20:17** And the Lord said to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

**20:18** "Arise and go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria, for he is in the vineyard of Naboth, because he has gone down there to take possession of it.

**20:19** And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Since you have murdered and also taken possession, therefore thus says the Lord: In every place where the swine and the dogs licked the blood of Naboth, there the dogs shall lick your blood, and the harlots shall wash themselves in your blood.'"

**20:20** And Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, O my enemy?" And he said, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself in vain to do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**20:21** Behold, I am bringing calamity upon you, and I will burn up those after you, and I will utterly destroy from Ahab every one who urinates against a wall, both the confined and the forsaken in Israel;

**20:22** and I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah, because of the provocations with which you have provoked to anger and have caused Israel to sin."

**20:23** And concerning Jezebel the Lord spoke, saying, "The dogs shall devour her in the rampart of Israel.

**20:24** Whoever of Ahab dies in the city, the dogs shall eat; and whoever of his dies in the field, the birds of the sky shall eat."

**20:25** Yet in vain did Ahab act, in that he sold himself to do evil in the sight of the Lord, as Jezebel his wife led him astray;

**20:26** and he acted exceedingly abominably in going after the abominations, according to all that the Amorite had done, whom the Lord utterly destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**20:27** And because of the word, when Ahab was pierced to the heart before the Lord, he went about weeping, and tore his tunic, and girded sackcloth upon his body, and fasted; and he put on sackcloth on the day he struck down Naboth the Israelite, and went his way.

**20:28** And the word of the Lord came by the hand of His servant Elijah concerning Ahab, and the Lord said,

**20:29** "Have you seen how Ahab has been pierced to the heart before Me? I will not bring the calamity in his days, but in the days of his son I will bring the calamity."

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — In the Septuagint, the account of Naboth's vineyard stands as chapter 20 (chapter 21 in the Masoretic Text), consistently calls Naboth "the Israelite" rather than "the Jezreelite" (compare "Israel" in verse 23), and places the vineyard beside Ahab's "threshing floor" rather than his palace.
- **20:10** — Following Codex Vaticanus, this Greek text ends Jezebel's quoted letter here, omitting the further instructions to accuse and stone Naboth that appear in the Masoretic Text and other Greek witnesses.
- **20:13** — "You have blessed God and the king" preserves the literal Greek rendering of the traditional Hebrew euphemism for cursing God and the king.
- **20:16** — The Greek uniquely records Ahab tearing his garments and putting on sackcloth immediately upon hearing of Naboth's death (compare verse 27, where he goes about weeping and puts on sackcloth on the day he struck down Naboth).
- **20:19** — The Greek adds "the swine" alongside the dogs and includes the prophecy that "the harlots shall wash themselves in your blood," anticipating the fulfillment in 1 Kings 22:38.
