## Text

**4:1** And King Solomon was reigning over Israel.

**4:2** And these were the officials whom he had: Azari the son of Zadok,

**4:3** and Eliaph and Ahijah the son of Saba were scribes, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahiliad was recorder,

**4:4** and Zadok and Abiathar were priests,

**4:5** and Ornia the son of Nathan was over the appointed officers, and Zabud the son of Nathan was the king's friend,

**4:6** and Ahi was steward, and Eliak was the steward, and Eliab the son of Saph was over the family, and Adoniram the son of Ephra was over the tribute.

**4:7** And Solomon had twelve appointed officers over all Israel, to provide for the king and his household; it fell to each one to provide for a month in the year.

**4:8** And these were their names: Baior in the hill country of Ephraim, one;

**4:9** the son of Rechas in Machemas and Bethalami and Beth-shemesh and Elom as far as Bethlaman, one;

**4:10** the son of Hesoth, Bernemalusamencha and Respharachein;

**4:11** throughout Dan and throughout Phathei, the husband of Tablethei—a daughter of Solomon was his wife—one;

**4:12** Bakcha the son of Ahimach: Polamach and Megiddo, and all the house of Dan which is beside Sesathan below Esrae, and from Baisaphut, Ebelmaola, as far as Maeber Lukam, one;

**4:13** the son of Geber, Ramoth-gilead—to him belonged the region of Eretabam in Bashan, sixty great walled cities with bars of bronze—one;

**4:14** Ahinaab the son of Achel, Mahanaim;

**4:15** Ahimaaz in Naphtali—he also took Basemath the daughter of Solomon—one;

**4:16** Baana the son of Hushai in Maala, one;

**4:17** the son of Elah in Benjamin;

**4:18** the son of Adai in the land of Gad, the land of Sihon king of Heshbon and of Og king of Bashan; and one naseph in the land of Judah;

**4:19** Jehoshaphat the son of Phuasud in Issachar.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Throughout Solomon's cabinet list (vv. 2-6), the Septuagint preserves several distinct personal names compared to the Hebrew text—including Azari (v. 2), Eliaph, Saba, and Ahiliad (v. 3), Ornia (v. 5), and Ahi, Eliak, Eliab son of Saph, and Ephra (v. 6)—and omits Benaiah from verse 4.
- **4:9** — Numerous district officers and place names in verses 8-16 differ substantially from the Hebrew text, including Baior (v. 8); Rechas, Machemas, Bethalami, Elom, and Bethlaman (v. 9); Bakcha son of Ahimach, Polamach, the house of Dan, Sesathan, Esrae, Baisaphut, Ebelmaola, and Maeber Lukam (v. 12); Eretabam (v. 13); Ahinaab son of Achel (v. 14); and Maala (v. 16).
- **4:10** — "Bernemalusamencha and Respharachein" are untranslated Greek transliterations of Hebrew geographic phrases (corresponding to "in Arubboth; to him belonged Socoh and the land of Hepher"), and "throughout Dan and throughout Phathei, the husband of Tablethei" in verse 11 likewise reflects a distinct Greek form.
- **4:17** — The Septuagint arranges the final three districts differently from the Hebrew text (vv. 17-19), omits the personal name "Shimei" before "the son of Elah" in verse 17, and reads "Phuasud" (for Hebrew Paruah) in verse 19.
- **4:18** — The Greek reads "the son of Adai in the land of Gad" and "king of Heshbon" (where the Hebrew has "Geber the son of Uri in the land of Gilead" and "king of the Amorites"), and leaves the Hebrew word for "officer" untranslated as naseph.
