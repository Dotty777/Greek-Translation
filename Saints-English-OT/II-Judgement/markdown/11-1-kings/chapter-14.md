## Text

**14:21** Now Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned over Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which the Lord had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to place His name there. And his mother's name was Maacham the Ammonitess.

**14:22** And Rehoboam did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and provoked Him to jealousy by all that their fathers had done, in their sins which they had committed.

**14:23** And they built for themselves high places and pillars and groves upon every high hill and beneath every shady tree.

**14:24** And a conspiracy arose in the land, and they practiced some of all the abominations of the nations whom the Lord had removed from before the sons of Israel.

**14:25** And it came to pass in the fifth year of the reign of Rehoboam that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem,

**14:26** and he took all the treasures of the house of the Lord and the treasures of the king's house, and the golden spears which David had taken from the hand of the servants of Hadarezer king of Zobah and brought into Jerusalem—everything, the golden shields which he took.

**14:27** And King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place; and the commanders of the runners, who guarded the gateway of the king's house, placed themselves over him.

**14:28** And it came to pass, whenever the king entered into the house of the Lord, that the runners would carry them, and then deposit them in the guardroom of the runners.

**14:29** Now the rest of the acts of Rehoboam, and all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the days of the kings of Judah?

**14:30** And there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days.

**14:31** And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Abiu his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **14:21** — The Old Greek omits verses 1–20 (placing the story of Jeroboam's son in 12:24g–n) and names Rehoboam's mother Maacham, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Naamah.
- **14:24** — The Septuagint reads "a conspiracy" (Greek syndesmos), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("male cult prostitutes").
- **14:26** — The Septuagint adds the golden spears David captured from the servants of Hadarezer king of Zobah (cf. 2 Samuel 8:7) and omits the Hebrew reference to shields made by Solomon.
- **14:27** — The Greek verb is plural ("placed themselves over him"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("he committed them into the hands of the captains of the guards").
- **14:31** — The Septuagint names Rehoboam's son Abiu, corresponding to Abijam in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Kings (and Abijah in Chronicles).
