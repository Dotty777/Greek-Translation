## Text

**8:1** And it came to pass, when Solomon had finished building the house of the Lord and his own house after twenty years, then King Solomon assembled all the elders of Israel in Zion to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the city of David, which is Zion,

**8:2** in the month of Athamein.

**8:3** And the priests took up the ark,

**8:4** and the tabernacle of testimony, and the holy vessels that were in the tabernacle of testimony.

**8:5** And the king and all Israel were before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen without number.

**8:6** And the priests brought the ark into its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Holy of Holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**8:7** For the cherubim had their wings spread out over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered over the ark and over its holy things from above.

**8:8** And the consecrated things extended beyond, and the ends of the consecrated things were seen from the holy place before the inner sanctuary, yet they were not seen outside.

**8:9** There was nothing in the ark except two tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant which Moses placed at Horeb, which the Lord made with the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**8:10** And it came to pass, when the priests came out of the holy place, that the cloud filled the house.

**8:11** And the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the Lord filled the house.

**8:14** Then the king turned his face around, and the king blessed all Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel stood.

**8:15** And he said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel today, who spoke with His mouth concerning David my father, and with His hands has fulfilled it, saying,

**8:16** 'From the day that I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I did not choose a city from any one tribe of Israel to build a house, that My name might be there; yet I chose Jerusalem for My name to abide there, and I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**8:17** And it was in the heart of my father to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**8:18** And the Lord said to David my father, 'Because it came into your heart to build a house for My name, you did well that it was in your heart.

**8:19** You shall not build the house; rather, your son who comes forth from your loins—he shall build the house for My name.'

**8:20** And the Lord has established His word which He spoke; and I have risen in the place of David my father, and have sat upon the throne of Israel, just as He spoke, and have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**8:21** And there I have appointed a place for the ark, in which is the covenant of the Lord which the Lord made with our fathers when He brought them out of the land of Egypt."

**8:22** Then Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven,

**8:23** and said, "Lord God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven above and on the earth beneath, who keeps covenant and mercy for Your servant who walks before You with all his heart,

**8:24** which You have kept for Your servant David my father; for indeed You spoke with Your mouth, and with Your hands You have fulfilled it, as it is this day.

**8:25** And now, Lord God of Israel, keep for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him, saying, 'A man of yours shall not be cut off from before Me sitting upon the throne of Israel, if only your children guard their ways to walk before Me just as you walked before Me.'

**8:26** And now, Lord God of Israel, let Your word be confirmed, I pray, to David my father.

**8:27** For will God indeed dwell with men upon the earth? If heaven and the heaven of heaven will not suffice for You, how much less this house which I have built for Your name?

**8:28** Yet look upon my supplication, Lord God of Israel, to hear the delight which Your servant prays before You, to You today,

**8:29** that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place of which You said, 'My name shall be there,' to hear the prayer which Your servant prays toward this place day and night.

**8:30** And hear the supplication of Your servant and of Your people Israel, whatever they pray toward this place; and hear in the place of Your dwelling in heaven, and act, and be merciful.

**8:31** In whatever way anyone sins against his neighbor, and if one lays upon him a curse to cause him to swear, and he comes and makes confession before Your altar in this house,

**8:32** then hear from heaven and act, and judge Your people Israel, condemning the lawless, bringing his way upon his own head, and justifying the righteous, giving to him according to his righteousness.

**8:33** When Your people Israel fall before enemies because they sin against You, and they turn back and confess Your name, and pray and make supplication in this house,

**8:34** then hear from heaven and be merciful to the sins of Your servant Israel, and bring them back to the land which You gave to their fathers.

**8:35** When heaven is shut up and there is no rain because they sin against You, and they pray toward this place and confess Your name, and turn from their sins when You humble them,

**8:36** then hear from heaven, and be merciful to the sins of Your servant and of Your people Israel, for You will show them the good way to walk in it, and give rain upon the land which You gave to Your servant for an inheritance.

**8:37** If there is famine, if there is pestilence, when there is scorching heat, if there is locust or mildew, and if his enemy afflicts him in one of his cities, whatever calamity, whatever distress,

**8:38** whatever prayer, whatever supplication is made by any man when each one knows the plague of his own heart, and spreads out his hands toward this house,

**8:39** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and be merciful, and act, and render to each man according to his ways, just as You know his heart, for You alone know the heart of all the sons of men,

**8:40** that they may fear You all the days that they live upon the land which You gave to our fathers.

**8:41** And also concerning the foreigner who is not of Your people,

**8:42** when they come and pray toward this place,

**8:43** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and act according to all for which the foreigner calls upon You, that all the peoples may know Your name and fear You, just as Your people Israel do, and may know that Your name has been called upon this house which I have built.

**8:44** When Your people go out to war against their enemies by the way in which You turn them, and they pray in the name of the Lord toward the city which You have chosen and the house which I have built for Your name,

**8:45** then hear from heaven their supplication and their prayer, and execute justice for them.

**8:46** When they sin against You—for there is no man who will not sin—and You bring them and deliver them before enemies, and their captors carry them away captive to a land far and near,

**8:47** and they turn their hearts in the land to which they were carried away, and they repent in the land of their exile and make supplication to You, saying, 'We have sinned, we have acted unjustly, we have committed lawlessness,'

**8:48** and they return to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their enemies to which You carried them away, and they pray to You toward their land which You gave to their fathers, toward the city which You have chosen, and the house which I have built for Your name,

**8:49** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place,

**8:50** and be merciful to their iniquities by which they have sinned against You, and for all their transgressions by which they have transgressed against You, and grant them compassion before those who hold them captive, that they may have compassion on them;

**8:51** for they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt, from the midst of the iron furnace.

**8:52** And let Your eyes and Your ears be open to the supplication of Your servant and to the supplication of Your people Israel, to hear them in all things for which they call upon You.

**8:53** And You set them apart for Yourself as an inheritance from among all the peoples of the earth, just as You spoke by the hand of Your servant Moses when You brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt, O Lord, Lord." Then Solomon spoke concerning the house when he had finished building it: "The Lord has made known the sun in heaven; He said He would dwell out of thick darkness. Build my house, a splendid house for Yourself, to dwell in anew." Behold, is this not written in the Book of the Song?

**8:54** And it came to pass, when Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and supplication to the Lord, that he arose from before the altar of the Lord, having knelt upon his knees with his hands spread out toward heaven.

**8:55** And he stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying,

**8:56** "Blessed be the Lord today, who has given rest to His people Israel, according to all that He spoke; not one word has failed among all His good words which He spoke by the hand of His servant Moses.

**8:57** May the Lord our God be with us, just as He was with our fathers; may He not forsake us nor turn away from us,

**8:58** that He may incline our hearts to Himself, to walk in all His ways, and to keep all His commandments and His ordinances which He commanded our fathers.

**8:59** And may these words with which I have made supplication before the Lord our God be near to the Lord our God day and night, that He may maintain the just cause of Your servant Israel, the matter of each day in its day of the year,

**8:60** that all the peoples of the earth may know that the Lord God—He is God, and there is no other.

**8:61** And let our hearts be perfect toward the Lord our God, and to walk devoutly in His ordinances and to keep His commandments, as it is this day."

**8:62** And the king and all the sons of Israel offered sacrifice before the Lord.

**8:63** And King Solomon offered the sacrifices of peace offerings which he offered to the Lord, twenty-two thousand oxen; and the king and all the sons of Israel dedicated the house of the Lord.

**8:64** On that day the king consecrated the middle of the court that was in front of the house of the Lord; for there he offered the burnt offering, and the sacrifices, and the fat of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar that was before the Lord was too small to be able to bear the burnt offering and the sacrifices of the peace offerings.

**8:65** And Solomon observed the feast on that day, and all Israel with him, a great assembly from the entrance of Hamath to the river of Egypt, before the Lord our God in the house which he had built, eating and drinking and rejoicing before the Lord our God seven days.

**8:66** And on the eighth day he sent the people away, and he blessed them; and each one departed to his own tents rejoicing, and glad in heart for the good things which the Lord had done for David His servant and for Israel His people.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Greek Athamein corresponds to Hebrew Ethanim (the seventh month); here and in 8:1 (which adds "after twenty years" and locates the assembly "in Zion") the Septuagint differs notably in detail and length from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:7** — The Septuagint reads "its holy things" here and "the consecrated things" in 8:8 (Greek ta hagia / ta hegiasmena) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "its poles"; in 8:6 and 8:8 "inner sanctuary" translates Greek dabeir (transliterated Hebrew debir).
- **8:11** — Verses 8:12–13 are absent at this point in the Septuagint; the corresponding poetic declaration by Solomon appears after 8:53 in the Greek tradition.
- **8:28** — Greek tes terpseos ("the delight") reflects the joyful sense of Hebrew rinnah ("ringing cry" or "shout of joy").
- **8:41** — The Septuagint has a briefer text in 8:41–42 than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting the clause describing the foreigner coming from a distant country on account of the Lord's great name and mighty hand.
- **8:53** — At the close of 8:53 the Septuagint places Solomon's poetic saying (corresponding to 8:12–13 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), uniquely preserving the line "The Lord has made known the sun in heaven," reading "Build my house, a splendid house for Yourself, to dwell in anew," and citing "the Book of the Song" (Greek biblio tes odes).
- **8:63** — The Septuagint lists only "twenty-two thousand oxen" here, without the "one hundred and twenty thousand sheep" present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:65** — The Septuagint adds "in the house which he had built, eating and drinking and rejoicing before the Lord our God" and specifies only "seven days" rather than the fourteen days of the Hebrew Masoretic Text; in 8:66 it similarly reads "he blessed them" rather than "they blessed the king."
