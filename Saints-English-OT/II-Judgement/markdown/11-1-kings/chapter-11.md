## Text

**11:1** Now King Solomon was a lover of women, and he had seven hundred princesses and three hundred concubines; and he took foreign wives, and the daughter of Pharaoh—Moabite, Ammonite, Syrian and Idumean, Hittite and Amorite—

**11:2** from the nations concerning whom the Lord had forbidden the children of Israel, saying, "You shall not go in to them, nor shall they come in to you, lest they turn your hearts aside after their idols." To these Solomon clung in love.

**11:4** And it came to pass in the time of Solomon's old age that his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of David his father; and the foreign wives turned his heart aside after their gods.

**11:5** Then Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, the idol of Moab, and for their king, the idol of the sons of Ammon,

**11:6** and for Astarte, the abomination of the Sidonians.

**11:7** And thus he did for all his foreign wives; they burned incense and offered sacrifices to their idols.

**11:8** So Solomon did evil in the sight of the Lord; he did not walk after the Lord as did David his father.

**11:9** And the Lord was angry with Solomon, because he turned his heart aside from the Lord God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice,

**11:10** and had commanded him concerning this matter not at all to walk after other gods, and to take heed to do what the Lord God had commanded him; neither was his heart perfect with the Lord according to the heart of David his father.

**11:11** Therefore the Lord said to Solomon, "Because these things have been found with you, and you have not kept My commandments and My ordinances which I commanded you, I will surely tear your kingdom out of your hand and give it to your servant.

**11:12** Yet in your days I will not do these things, for the sake of David your father; out of the hand of your son I will take it.

**11:13** However, I will by no means take the whole kingdom; one tribe I will give to your son for the sake of David My servant, and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen."

**11:14** And the Lord raised up an adversary against Solomon: Ader the Idumean, and Esrom the son of Eliada, who was in Raemmaaer, Hadadezer king of Zobah, his lord. And men were gathered to him, and he became captain of a band and seized Damascus beforehand; and they were an adversary to Israel all the days of Solomon. And Ader the Idumean was of the royal seed in Idumea.

**11:15** And it came to pass, when David utterly destroyed Edom, when Joab the commander of the army went to bury the slain, that they cut down every male in Idumea;

**11:16** for six months Joab and all Israel remained there in Idumea, until he had utterly destroyed every male in Idumea.

**11:17** And Ader fled, he and all the Idumeans of his father's servants with him, and they entered into Egypt; and Ader was a little lad.

**11:18** And men arose from the city of Midian and came to Paran, and they took men with them and came to Pharaoh king of Egypt; and Ader went in to Pharaoh, and he gave him a house and appointed bread for him.

**11:19** And Ader found great favor in the sight of Pharaoh, and he gave him as wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Thekemina the elder. And the sister of Thekemina bore to him, to Ader, Genubath her son;

**11:20** and Thekemina raised him in the midst of the sons of Pharaoh, and Genubath was in the midst of the sons of Pharaoh.

**11:21** And Ader heard in Egypt that David slept with his fathers, and that Joab the commander of the army was dead; and Ader said to Pharaoh, "Send me away, and I will return to my own land."

**11:22** Then Pharaoh said to Ader, "What do you lack with me, that behold, you seek to depart to your own land?" And Ader said to him, "Surely, you shall indeed send me away." And Ader returned to his own land.

**11:25** This is the evil which Ader did; and he loathed Israel, and reigned in Edom.

**11:26** Now Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the Ephrathite from Sarira, the son of a widow woman, was the servant of Solomon.

**11:27** And this was the matter in which he lifted up his hands against the king: Solomon built the citadel; he closed up the wall of the city of David his father.

**11:28** And the man Jeroboam was mighty in strength; and Solomon saw the young man, that he was an industrious man, and he set him over the levies of the house of Joseph.

**11:29** And it came to pass at that time, when Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem, that Ahijah the Shilonite, the prophet, found him on the way, and drew him aside from the way; and Ahijah was clad in a new garment, and both of them were in the field.

**11:30** And Ahijah took hold of his new garment that was on him, and tore it into twelve pieces;

**11:31** and he said to Jeroboam, "Take for yourself ten pieces, for thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'Behold, I am tearing the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and I will give to you ten tribes,

**11:32** and two tribes shall be his for the sake of David My servant, and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel,

**11:33** because he has forsaken Me, and has made offerings to Astarte, the abomination of the Sidonians, and to Chemosh and among the idols of Moab, and to their king, the detestable thing of the sons of Ammon, and has not walked in My ways to do what is right in My sight, as did David his father.

**11:34** Yet I will by no means take the whole kingdom out of his hand, for I will surely oppose him all the days of his life, for the sake of David My servant whom I chose.

**11:35** And I will take the kingdom out of the hand of his son, and I will give to you the ten tribes;

**11:36** but to his son I will give the two tribes, so that My servant David may have an established place all the days before Me in Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen for Myself, to place My name there.

**11:37** And I will take you, and you shall reign over all that your soul desires, and you shall be king over Israel.

**11:38** And it shall be, if you keep all that I command you, and walk in My ways, and do what is right in My sight, to observe My commandments and My ordinances, as David My servant did, then I will be with you and build for you a faithful house, as I built for David.'"

**11:40** And Solomon sought to put Jeroboam to death; but he arose and departed and fled to Egypt, to Susakim king of Egypt, and he was in Egypt until Solomon died.

**11:41** Now the rest of the acts of Solomon, and all that he did, and all his understanding, behold, are they not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?

**11:42** And the days that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem were forty years.

**11:43** And Solomon slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David his father. And it came to pass, when Jeroboam the son of Nebat heard of it—while he was still in Egypt, since he had fled from the presence of Solomon and dwelt in Egypt—that he set his course directly, and he came to his own city, to the land of Sarira which is in Mount Ephraim. And King Solomon slept with his fathers, and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — The Greek renders the Ammonite deity Milcom (or Molech) literally as "their king" and orders verses 5–8 differently from the Hebrew text; verse 3 is absent because the count of Solomon's princesses and concubines appears in verse 1.
- **11:14** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "adversary" as satan, preserves the distinct names Ader (Hebrew Hadad) and Esrom (Hebrew Rezon), combines the material of Hebrew verses 23–24 into this verse, and reads "who was in Raemmaaer" where the Hebrew has "who fled from."
- **11:19** — The Greek gives the Egyptian queen's name as Thekemina (Hebrew Tahpenes), and later preserves the distinct names Sarira (11:26, 43; Hebrew Zeredah) and Susakim (11:40; Hebrew Shishak).
- **11:32** — The Greek reads "two tribes" here and in verse 36 (using skeptra, literally "scepters"), whereas verse 13 and the Hebrew text read "one tribe."
- **11:34** — The Greek reads "I will surely oppose him" where the Hebrew has "I will make him ruler," and in verse 36 uses thesis ("an established place") where the Hebrew has "a lamp."
