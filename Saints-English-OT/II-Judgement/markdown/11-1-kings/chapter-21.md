## Text

**21:1** And Son of Hader gathered all his army and went up and besieged Samaria, and thirty-two kings with him, and every horse and chariot; and they went up and besieged Samaria, and fought against it.

**21:2** And he sent to Ahab king of Israel into the city, and said to him, "Thus says Son of Hader:

**21:3** 'Your silver and your gold are mine, and your wives and your children are mine.'"

**21:4** And the king of Israel answered and said, "Just as you have spoken, O lord king, I am yours, and all that is mine."

**21:5** And the messengers returned and said, "Thus says Son of Hader: 'I sent back, saying: Your silver and your gold and your wives you shall give to me;

**21:6** for at this hour tomorrow I will send my servants to you, and they shall search your house and the houses of your servants; and it shall be that the desirable things of their eyes, upon which they lay their hands, they shall take.'"

**21:7** And the king of Israel called all the elders and said, "Know now and see that this man seeks evil; for he has sent to me concerning my wives, and concerning my sons, and concerning my daughters; my silver and my gold I did not withhold from him."

**21:8** And the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen, and do not consent."

**21:9** And he said to the messengers of Son of Hader, "Tell your lord, 'All that you sent for to your servant at the first I will do, but this thing I shall not be able to do.'" And the men departed and brought back word to him.

**21:10** And Son of Hader sent to him, saying, "Thus may God do to me, and thus may He add, if the dust of Samaria shall suffice for the foxes for all the people, my foot soldiers."

**21:11** And the king of Israel answered and said, "Let it suffice; do not let the hunchbacked boast like the upright."

**21:12** And it came to pass, when he answered him this word, that he was drinking, he and all the kings with him, in tents; and he said to his servants, "Build a palisade." And they set a palisade against the city.

**21:13** And behold, a certain prophet came near to the king of Israel and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'Have you seen this great multitude? Behold, I deliver it today into your hands, and you shall know that I am the Lord.'"

**21:14** And Ahab said, "By whom?" And he said, "Thus says the Lord: 'By the young men of the rulers of the provinces.'" And Ahab said, "Who shall join the battle?" And he said, "You."

**21:15** And Ahab mustered the rulers, the young men of the provinces, and they were two hundred and thirty; and after these things he mustered the people, every son of might, sixty.

**21:16** And he went out at noon. And Son of Hader was drinking himself drunk in Succoth, he and the kings, thirty-two, allied kings with him.

**21:17** And the rulers, the young men of the provinces, went out first. And they sent and reported to the king of Syria, saying, "Men have come out from Samaria."

**21:18** "Tell them, for peace—for they do not come out—seize them alive; and for war, seize them alive;

**21:19** and do not let the ruling young men, the rulers of the provinces, come out from the city, and the army behind them."

**21:20** Each one struck the man beside him, and each one struck the man beside him a second time, and Syria fled; and Israel pursued them, and Son of Hader, king of Syria, escaped on a horseman's horse.

**21:21** And the king of Israel went out and took all the horses and the chariots, and struck a great blow in Syria.

**21:22** And the prophet came near to the king of Israel and said, "Be strong, and know and see what you shall do; for at the return of the year Son of Hader, king of Syria, is coming up against you."

**21:23** And the servants of the king of Syria said, "A God of the mountains is the God of Israel, and not a God of the valley; therefore He prevailed over us. But if we fight them on level ground, surely we shall prevail over them.

**21:24** And do this thing: remove the kings, each one to their own place, and set satraps instead of them,

**21:25** and we will replace for you an army like the army that fell, and horse for horse, and chariots for chariots; and we will fight against them on level ground, and we shall prevail over them." And he listened to his voice, and did so.

**21:26** And it came to pass at the return of the year that Son of Hader mustered Syria, and went up to Aphek to war against Israel.

**21:27** And the sons of Israel were mustered, and came to meet them; and Israel encamped opposite them like two flocks of goats, while Syria filled the land.

**21:28** And the man of God came near and said to the king of Israel, "Thus says the Lord: 'Because Syria has said that the Lord, the God of Israel, is a God of the mountains, and He is not a God of the valleys, I will deliver this great army into your hand, and you shall know that I am the Lord;

**21:29** and these shall encamp opposite those for seven days.'" And it came to pass on the seventh day that the battle drew near, and Israel struck Syria, one hundred thousand foot soldiers in one day.

**21:30** And the rest fled to Aphek, into the city; and the wall fell upon twenty-seven thousand men of those who remained. And Son of Hader fled and entered into the house of the bedchamber, into the inner room.

**21:31** And he said to his servants, "I know that the kings of Israel are kings of mercy; let us now put sackcloth upon our loins and ropes upon our heads, and let us still go out to the king of Israel, if perhaps he will preserve our lives."

**21:32** So they girded sackcloth around their loins and put ropes upon their heads, and said to the king of Israel, "Your servant Son of Hader says, 'Let our soul live, I pray.'" And he said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

**21:33** And the men took it as an omen and made a truce, and caught up the word from his mouth and said, "Your brother is Son of Hader." And he said, "Go in and bring him." And Son of Hader came out to him, and they brought him up to him onto the chariot.

**21:34** And he said to him, "The cities which my father took from your father I will restore to you, and you shall establish outlets for yourself in Damascus, just as my father established in Samaria." "And I will send you away with a covenant." And he made a covenant with him, and sent him away.

**21:35** Now a certain man of the sons of the prophets said to his neighbor by the word of the Lord, "Strike me, I pray." And the man was not willing to strike him.

**21:36** Then he said to him, "Because you have not listened to the voice of the Lord, behold, as you go away from me, a lion shall strike you." And he departed from him, and a lion found him and struck him.

**21:37** And he found another man and said, "Strike me, I pray." And the man struck him; striking him, he bruised him.

**21:38** And the prophet went and stood waiting for the king of Israel on the road, and bound up his eyes with a bandage.

**21:39** And it came to pass, as the king was passing by, that this man cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out to the campaign of the war, and behold, a man brought a man out to me and said to me, 'Guard this man; and if he escapes at all, then your life shall be for his life, or you shall weigh out a talent of silver.'

**21:40** And it came to pass that your servant looked around here and there, and he was not there." And the king of Israel said to him, "Behold, you have even slain the ambushes beside me."

**21:41** And he hastened and removed the bandage from his eyes; and the king of Israel recognized him, that he was of the prophets.

**21:42** And he said to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'Because you have let go out of your hand a destructive man, therefore your life shall be for his life, and your people for his people.'"

**21:43** And the king of Israel departed, confounded and dispirited, and came to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — The Septuagint reverses the order of chapters 20 and 21 relative to the Hebrew Masoretic Text and renders the Aramean royal name Ben-Hadad literally as Son of Hader (Greek huios Hader, reflecting an r/d interchange at the end of the name).
- **21:10** — The Greek reads for the foxes (tais alopexin), translating the Hebrew consonants for handfuls (she'alim) as foxes (shu'alim).
- **21:11** — Where the Hebrew proverb speaks of one who puts on armor not boasting like one who takes it off, the Septuagint renders the saying as the hunchbacked (ho kyrtos) not boasting like the upright (ho orthos).
- **21:18** — Verses 18 and 19 feature elliptical Greek syntax with imperatival infinitives (Tell them... seize them alive) and a negative command (do not let... come out) where the Hebrew narrates that the young men went out.
- **21:40** — The Septuagint preserves a markedly different reply from Ahab (Behold, you have even slain the ambushes beside me) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads, So shall your judgment be; you yourself have decided it.
