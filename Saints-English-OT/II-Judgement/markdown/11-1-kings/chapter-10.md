## Text

**10:1** And the queen of Sheba heard of the name of Solomon and the name of the Lord, and she came to test him with riddles.

**10:2** And she came to Jerusalem with a very great retinue, and camels bearing spices, and very much gold, and precious stones; and she went in to Solomon and spoke to him all that was in her heart.

**10:3** And Solomon declared to her all her matters; there was no matter overlooked by the king which he did not declare to her.

**10:4** And the queen of Sheba saw all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he had built,

**10:5** and the food of Solomon, and the seating of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and his apparel, and his cupbearers, and his burnt offering which he offered in the house of the Lord; and she was beside herself.

**10:6** And she said to King Solomon, "True was the report which I heard in my own land concerning your word and concerning your wisdom;

**10:7** yet I did not believe those who spoke to me, until I came and my own eyes saw it; and behold, they are not half as they reported to me, for you have added good things to them beyond all the report which I heard in my own land.

**10:8** Blessed are your wives, blessed are these your servants who stand continually before you, who hear all your wisdom.

**10:9** May the Lord your God be blessed, who delighted in you, to place you on the throne of Israel, because the Lord loves Israel to establish it forever; and He has appointed you king over them, to execute judgment in righteousness and in their judgments."

**10:10** And she gave to Solomon one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and very abundant spices, and precious stones; never again came spices in such abundance as those which the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**10:11** And the ship of Hiram was carrying the gold from Souphir; it brought very much hewn timber and precious stones.

**10:12** And the king made of the hewn timber supports for the house of the Lord and for the house of the king, and harps and lyres for the singers; no such hewn timber had come into the land, nor has any been seen anywhere to this day.

**10:13** And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all that she desired, whatever she asked, besides all that he had given her by the hand of King Solomon; and she turned back and went to her own land, she and all her servants.

**10:14** Now the weight of the gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

**10:15** besides the tributes of the subject peoples, of the merchants, and of all the kings of the region beyond, and of the satraps of the land.

**10:16** And Solomon made three hundred spears of beaten gold; three hundred pieces of gold went upon each spear;

**10:17** and three hundred shields of beaten gold; and three minas of gold went into each shield; and he placed them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.

**10:18** And the king made a great ivory throne, and overlaid it with refined gold.

**10:19** There were six steps to the throne, and busts of calves on the throne behind it, on this side and on that side upon the place of the seat, and two lions standing beside the armrests,

**10:20** and twelve lions standing on the six steps, on this side and on that side; nothing like it has been made in any kingdom.

**10:21** And all the vessels made by Solomon were of gold, and the basins were of gold; all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were encased in gold; there was no silver, for it was not esteemed in the days of Solomon.

**10:22** For the king had a ship of Tarshish on the sea with the ships of Hiram; once every three years a ship came to the king from Tarshish with gold and silver, and engraved and hewn stones. This was the enterprise of the levy which King Solomon raised to build the house of the Lord, and the house of the king, and the wall of Jerusalem, and the citadel, to enclose the fortification of the city of David, and Assour, and Midian, and Gezer, and Upper Baithoram, and Iethermath, and all the cities of the chariots, and all the cities of the horsemen; and the undertaking of Solomon which he undertook to build in Jerusalem and in all the land, so that none might rule over him—all the people who were pointed out from the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, and the Girgashites, who were not of the sons of Israel, their children who remained after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel were not able to utterly destroy—Solomon brought them up into tribute to this day. And from the sons of Israel Solomon imposed no task, for they were the men of war, and his servants, and commanders of his chariots, and his horsemen.

**10:23** And Solomon was magnified above all the kings in riches and in wisdom.

**10:24** And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom which the Lord had given to his heart.

**10:25** And each of them brought his gifts: vessels of gold and garments, myrrh oil and spices, and horses and mules, year by year.

**10:26** And Solomon had four thousand mares for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem. And he was ruler over all the kings from the River even to the land of the Philistines, and to the borders of Egypt.

**10:27** And the king made gold and silver in Jerusalem like stones, and he made cedars like the sycamore trees that are in the lowland for abundance.

**10:28** And Solomon's supply of horsemen came from Egypt, and from Thekoue came the king's merchants, and he received them from Thekoue in exchange;

**10:29** and the supply came up from Egypt: a chariot for one hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for fifty pieces of silver; and thus for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Syria they went forth by sea.

## Translator Notes

- **10:8** — The Greek reads "your wives" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "your men," reflecting a difference in vocalizing the Hebrew consonants.
- **10:11** — The Greek reads Souphir (for Hebrew Ophir) and renders Hebrew almug wood here and in 10:12 as "hewn timber."
- **10:16** — The Greek reads three hundred spears of beaten gold with three hundred pieces of gold per spear, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads two hundred large shields with six hundred shekels of gold per shield.
- **10:22** — The Greek reads "engraved and hewn stones" (for Hebrew "ivory, apes, and peacocks") and places here the account of Solomon's levy and fortifications (cf. Hebrew 9:15–22), with distinctive place-names Assour, Midian, Upper Baithoram, and Iethermath (for Hebrew Hazor, Megiddo, Lower Beth-horon, and Tamar).
- **10:29** — The Greek reads one hundred and fifty pieces of silver (Hebrew: six hundred and one hundred and fifty) and "by sea" (Hebrew: "by their hand"), following 10:26 ("four thousand mares for chariots") and 10:28 (Thekoue for Hebrew Kue).
