## Text

**9:1** And it came to pass, when Solomon had finished building the house of the Lord and the house of the king, and all the undertaking of Solomon, all that he desired to accomplish,

**9:2** that the Lord appeared to Solomon a second time, just as He had appeared in Gabaoth.

**9:3** And the Lord said to him, "I have heard the voice of your prayer and of your supplication which you have made before Me; I have done for you according to all your prayer. I have consecrated this house which you have built, to place My name there forever; and My eyes shall be there forever, and My heart all the days.

**9:4** And as for you, if you walk before Me just as David your father walked, in holiness of heart and in uprightness, and to do according to all that I commanded him, and if you keep My statutes and My commandments,

**9:5** then I will raise up the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, as I spoke to David your father, saying, 'There shall not be cut off from you a man ruling in Israel.'

**9:6** But if you and your children indeed turn away from Me, and do not keep My commandments and My statutes which Moses set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them,

**9:7** then I will cut off Israel from the land which I have given them, and this house which I have consecrated for My name I will cast away from My presence; and Israel shall become a desolation and a byword among all the peoples.

**9:8** And this house shall be the lofty one; everyone who passes through it shall be astonished and shall hiss, and they shall say, 'For what reason has the Lord done thus to this land and to this house?'

**9:9** And they shall say, 'Because they forsook the Lord their God, who brought their fathers out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, and laid hold of foreign gods and worshiped them and served them; therefore He has brought this calamity upon them.'" Then Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David into his house which he had built for himself in those days.

**9:10** During twenty years, in which Solomon built the two houses, the house of the Lord and the house of the king,

**9:11** Hiram king of Tyre assisted Solomon with cedar timber and with pine timber and with gold, and with everything he desired. Then the king gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

**9:12** And Hiram went out from Tyre and journeyed to Galilee to see the cities which Solomon had given him; and it did not please him.

**9:13** And he said, "What are these cities which you have given me, brother?" And he called them Border to this day.

**9:14** And Hiram brought to Solomon one hundred and twenty talents of gold.

**9:26** And in return for this, King Solomon built a ship in Emaeseion Gaber, which is beside Elath on the shore of the farthest sea in the land of Edom.

**9:27** And Hiram sent in the ship some of his servants, seafaring men who knew how to row and knew the sea, with the servants of Solomon.

**9:28** And they came to Sophera, and took from there one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and brought them to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — The Greek text reads "which Moses set before you," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "which I have set before you"; in 9:2 the Greek also has the place-name Gabaoth for Hebrew Gibeon.
- **9:9** — The Greek attaches the notice of Pharaoh's daughter (corresponding to 9:24 in the Hebrew) to the end of 9:9 and omits verses 15–25 here, placing much of the building and forced-labor account in 10:22.
- **9:13** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place-name Cabul as Horion ("Border").
- **9:26** — The Greek reads Emaeseion Gaber (for Hebrew Ezion Geber) and "the farthest sea" (for Hebrew "Sea of Reeds"), and in 9:28 names the destination Sophera (for Ophir) with one hundred and twenty talents of gold (Hebrew: four hundred and twenty).
