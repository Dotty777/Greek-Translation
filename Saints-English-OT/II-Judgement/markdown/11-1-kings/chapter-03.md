## Text

**3:2** They were burning incense on the high places, because a house had not been built for the Lord until now.

**3:3** And Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the statutes of David his father; only he sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**3:4** And he arose and went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for it was the highest and a great high place; Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings upon the altar in Gibeon.

**3:5** And the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream by night; and the Lord said to Solomon, "Ask a request for yourself."

**3:6** And Solomon said, "You have shown great mercy toward Your servant David my father, according as he walked before You in truth and in righteousness and in uprightness of heart with You; and You have kept for him this great mercy, to set his son upon his throne, as it is this day.

**3:7** And now, O Lord my God, You have given Your servant in place of David my father; and I am a little child, and I do not know my going out and my coming in.

**3:8** And Your servant is in the midst of Your people whom You have chosen, a great people who shall not be numbered.

**3:9** And give to Your servant a heart to hear and to judge Your people in righteousness, and to discern between good and evil; for who shall be able to judge this weighty people of Yours?"

**3:10** And it was pleasing in the sight of the Lord that Solomon had asked this thing.

**3:11** And the Lord said to him, "Because you have asked this thing from Me, and have not asked for yourself many days, and have not asked for riches, nor have you asked for the lives of your enemies, but have asked for yourself understanding to hear judgment,

**3:12** behold, I have performed your word; behold, I have given you a discerning and wise heart, so that there has been no one like you before you, and after you none shall arise like you.

**3:13** And I have also given you what you did not ask, both riches and glory, so that there has not been a man like you among the kings;

**3:14** and if you walk in My way to keep My commandments and My statutes, as David your father walked, then I will multiply your days."

**3:15** And Solomon awoke, and behold, it was a dream. And he arose and came to Jerusalem, and stood before the altar that was before the ark of the covenant of the Lord in Zion; and he offered burnt offerings and made peace offerings, and he prepared a great banquet for himself and for all his servants.

**3:16** Then two women who were harlots appeared to the king and stood before him.

**3:17** And the one woman said, "O my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house, and we gave birth in the house.

**3:18** And it came to pass on the third day after I gave birth, that this woman also gave birth; and we were together, and there is no one with us except the two of us in the house.

**3:19** And this woman's son died in the night, because she lay upon him;

**3:20** and she arose in the middle of the night and took my son from my arms and laid him in her bosom, and her dead son she laid in my bosom.

**3:21** And I arose in the morning to nurse my son, and he was dead; and behold, I examined him in the morning, and behold, he was not my son whom I had borne."

**3:22** And the other woman said, "No; rather, my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one." And they spoke before the king.

**3:23** And the king said to them, "You say, 'This is my son, the living one, and the son of this woman is the dead one'; and you say, 'No; rather, my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.'"

**3:24** And the king said, "Fetch a sword." And they brought the sword before the king.

**3:25** And the king said, "Divide the living nursing child in two, and give half of him to this one and half of him to that one."

**3:26** Then the woman whose son was the living one answered and spoke to the king, for her womb was stirred over her son, and she said, "O my lord, give her the child, and by no means put him to death!" But the other said, "Let him be neither mine nor hers; divide him."

**3:27** And the king answered and said, "Give the child to her who said, 'Give him to her, and by no means put him to death'; she is his mother."

**3:28** And all Israel heard of this judgment which the king had judged; and they feared before the face of the king, for they saw that the discernment of God was in him to execute justice.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Septuagint lacks verse 1 here (placing Solomon's marriage to Pharaoh's daughter elsewhere) and opens verse 2 abruptly with the participle "burning incense" (thymiontes).
- **3:15** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek specifies that Solomon stood before the altar that was before the ark of the covenant "in Zion," and that he prepared a great banquet "for himself" as well as all his servants.
- **3:17** — "O my lord" renders the literal Greek idiom en emoi, kyrie ("in me, lord"), a Hebraizing expression of respectful entreaty (also in 3:26).
- **3:27** — The Septuagint uniquely embeds the true mother's plea directly within Solomon's verdict ("to her who said, 'Give him to her...'").
