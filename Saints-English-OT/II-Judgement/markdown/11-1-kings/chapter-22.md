## Text

**22:1** And he remained three years, and there was no war between Syria and Israel.

**22:2** And it came to pass in the third year that Jehoshaphat king of Judah went down to the king of Israel.

**22:3** And the king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth Gilead belongs to us, yet we keep silent from taking it out of the hand of the king of Syria?"

**22:4** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you go up with us to Ramoth Gilead to battle?"

**22:5** And Jehoshaphat said, "As I am, so are you; as my people are, so are your people; as my horses are, so are your horses." And Jehoshaphat king of Judah said to the king of Israel, "Please inquire today of the Lord."

**22:6** Then the king of Israel gathered together all the prophets, about four hundred men, and the king said to them, "Shall I go to Ramoth Gilead to battle, or shall I refrain?" And they said, "Go up, and the Lord will surely deliver it into the hands of the king."

**22:7** And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Is there not a prophet of the Lord here, that we may inquire of the Lord through him?"

**22:8** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is one man through whom to inquire of the Lord, yet I hate him, because he does not speak good concerning me, but only evil—Micaiah the son of Jemiah." And Jehoshaphat king of Judah said, "Do not let the king speak thus."

**22:9** Then the king of Israel summoned a eunuch and said, "Quickly, Micaiah the son of Jemiah!"

**22:10** Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting each man upon his throne, armed, in the gates of Samaria; and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

**22:11** And Zedekiah the son of Chaana made horns of iron for himself and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'With these you shall gore Syria until it is consumed.'"

**22:12** And all the prophets were prophesying in this manner, saying, "Go up to Ramoth Gilead, and it shall prosper; and the Lord will deliver even the king of Syria into your hands."

**22:13** And the messenger who had gone to summon Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Behold now, all the prophets speak with one mouth for good concerning the king. Now let you also be in agreement in your words, like the words of one of these, and speak good."

**22:14** And Micaiah said, "As the Lord lives, whatever the Lord says to me, that I will speak."

**22:15** And he came to the king, and the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall I go up to Ramoth Gilead to battle, or shall I refrain?" And he said, "Go up, and the Lord will prosper it into the hand of the king."

**22:16** And the king said to him, "Five times I adjure you that you speak to me the truth in the name of the Lord."

**22:17** And he said, "Not so! I have seen all Israel scattered upon the mountains like a flock that has no shepherd. And the Lord said, 'Is the Lord not God to these? Let each one return to his house in peace.'"

**22:18** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Did I not say to you, 'This man does not prophesy good to me, but only evil'?"

**22:19** And Micaiah said, "Not so! Not I! Hear the word of the Lord! Not so! I saw the God of Israel sitting upon His throne, and all the host of heaven stood around Him, on His right hand and on His left.

**22:20** And the Lord said, 'Who will deceive Ahab king of Israel, so that he will go up and fall at Ramoth Gilead?' And one spoke in this manner, and another in that manner.

**22:21** And a spirit came forth and stood before the Lord, and said, 'I will deceive him.'

**22:22** And the Lord said to him, 'By what means?' And he said, 'I will go forth and become a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' And He said, 'You shall deceive him, and you shall also prevail. Go forth and do so.'

**22:23** And now, behold, the Lord has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the Lord has spoken evil against you."

**22:24** Then Zedekiah the son of Canaan came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek, and said, "What spirit of the Lord is it that has spoken in you?"

**22:25** And Micaiah said, "Behold, you shall see on that day when you enter into a chamber of the hidden chamber."

**22:26** And the king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Semer the king of the city, and to Joash the son of the king

**22:27** say to place this man in prison, and for him to eat bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace."

**22:28** And Micaiah said, "If you indeed return in peace, the Lord has not spoken by me."

**22:29** So the king of Israel went up, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah with him, to Ramoth Gilead.

**22:30** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "I will disguise myself and enter into the battle, but you put on my robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself and entered into the battle.

**22:31** Now the king of Syria had commanded the thirty-two commanders of his chariots, saying, "Do not fight with small or great, but with the king of Israel alone."

**22:32** And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat king of Judah, that they said, "This appears to be the king of Israel." And they surrounded him to fight; and Jehoshaphat cried out.

**22:33** And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw that this was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from him.

**22:34** And a certain man drew his bow with good aim and struck the king of Israel between the lung and the breastplate. So he said to his charioteer, "Turn your hands around and bring me out of the battle, for I am wounded."

**22:35** And the battle turned on that day; and the king stood upright in the chariot facing Syria from morning until evening, and blood poured out from the wound into the hollow of the chariot. And he died in the evening, and the blood of the rout flowed out to the hollow of the chariot.

**22:36** And the herald of the army stood at the setting of the sun, saying, "Every man to his own city and to his own land,

**22:37** for the king is dead!" And they came to Samaria, and buried the king in Samaria.

**22:38** And they washed off the blood at the spring of Samaria, and the swine and the dogs licked up the blood, and the harlots bathed in the blood, according to the word of the Lord which He had spoken.

**22:39** Now the rest of the acts of Ahab, and all that he did, and the ivory house which he built, and all the cities which he made, behold, these are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**22:40** So Ahab slept with his fathers, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

**22:41** And Jehoshaphat the son of Asa began to reign over Judah; in the fourth year of Ahab he began to reign.

**22:42** Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Azaeba, the daughter of Shimei.

**22:43** And he walked in all the way of Asa his father; he did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of the Lord.

**22:44** Nevertheless, he did not remove the high places; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**22:45** And Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.

**22:46** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, and his mighty deeds, all that he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the records of Jehoshaphat?

**22:51** And he slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David his father; and Joram his son reigned in his place.

**22:52** And Ahaziah the son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned in Israel two years.

**22:53** And he did what was evil before the Lord, and walked in the way of his father Ahab, and in the way of Jezebel his mother, and in the sins of the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**22:54** And he served the Baals and worshiped them, and provoked the Lord God of Israel to anger according to all that had taken place before him. And Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.

## Translator Notes

- **22:10** — The Septuagint describes the kings as sitting armed in the gates of Samaria rather than clothed in robes at a threshing floor as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text. In verses 8-9, 11, and 24, the Greek also preserves the distinct name forms Jemiah (Hebrew Imlah), Chaana, and Canaan (Hebrew Chenaanah).
- **22:16** — The Greek reads 'Five times' rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'How many times,' and in verse 17 renders the Lord's declaration as 'Is the Lord not God to these?' instead of 'These have no master.'
- **22:26** — The Septuagint identifies the city official as 'Semer the king of the city,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Amon the governor of the city.'
- **22:34** — The Greek states that the archer drew his bow 'with good aim' and struck Ahab 'between the lung and the breastplate,' contrasting with the Hebrew 'at random' and 'between the joints of his armor.' Similarly, verse 30 has Ahab instruct Jehoshaphat to put on 'my robes,' and verse 38 adds 'the swine' alongside the dogs.
- **22:42** — The Septuagint gives the name of Jehoshaphat's mother as 'Azaeba, the daughter of Shimei,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.'
