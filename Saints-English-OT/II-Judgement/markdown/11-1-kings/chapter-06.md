## Text

**6:1** And it came to pass in the four hundred and fortieth year of the departure of the sons of Israel from Egypt, in the fourth year, in the second month of the reign of King Solomon over Israel, that they brought great, costly stones for the foundation of the house, and unhewn stones; and the sons of Solomon and the sons of Hiram hewed them and laid them. In the fourth year he laid the foundation of the house of the Lord, in the month of Neiso and the second month. In the eleventh year, in the month of Baad, which is the eighth month, the house was finished according to every specification of it and according to every arrangement of it.

**6:2** And as for the house which the king built for the Lord, its length was forty cubits, and its width twenty cubits, and its height twenty-five cubits.

**6:3** And the vestibule before the temple was twenty cubits in its length according to the width of the house, before the house. And he built the house and finished it.

**6:4** And he made for the house recessed, hidden windows.

**6:5** And against the wall of the house itself he placed chambers round about, for both the temple and the inner sanctuary.

**6:6** The lowest side story was five cubits in its width, and the middle one six, and the third was seven cubits in its width; for he provided a setback for the house round about on the outside of the house, so that they should not take hold of the walls of the house.

**6:7** And the house, while it was being built, was built with quarried, unworked stones; and neither hammer nor axe nor any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.

**6:8** And the doorway of the lowest side story was beneath the right shoulder of the house, and there was a winding ascent to the middle story, and from the middle to the third story.

**6:9** And he built the house and finished it; and he paneled the house with cedars.

**6:10** And he built the framework throughout the whole house, five cubits in its height, and he bound the structure together with cedar timbers.

**6:15** And he built the walls of the house with cedar timbers, from the floor of the house even to the beams and even to the walls; he paneled it continuously with wood on the inside, and he covered the interior of the house with planks of pine.

**6:16** And he built the twenty cubits from the end of the wall, the one side from the floor to the beams; and he made it out of the inner sanctuary for the Holy of Holies.

**6:17** And the temple was forty cubits before the inner sanctuary, in the midst of the house within, to place there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**6:19** To place there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**6:20** Twenty cubits was its length, and twenty cubits its width, and twenty cubits its height.

**6:21** And he overlaid it with sealed gold; and he made an altar before the inner sanctuary and overlaid it with gold.

**6:22** And the whole house he overlaid with gold, until the completion of all the house.

**6:23** And he made in the inner sanctuary two cherubim, ten cubits in measured stature.

**6:24** And its wing was five cubits; and the second was ten cubits from the tip of its wing.

**6:25** Likewise for the second cherub: of one measure and one form; both had one form.

**6:26** And the height of the cherub was by the cubit, and likewise the second cherub.

**6:27** And both cherubim were in the midst of the innermost house; and he spread out their wings, and one wing touched the wall, and a wing touched the second wall; and their wings that were in the midst of the house touched wing to wing.

**6:28** And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.

**6:29** Upon all the walls of the house round about he engraved with a graving tool carved figures of cherubim, and palm trees, both for the inner and for the outer.

**6:30** And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, both of the innermost and of the outermost.

**6:31** And for the doorway of the inner sanctuary he made doors of juniper wood, with pillars fourfold.

**6:34** In both doors was pine wood: two leaves were the one door, with their pivots, and two leaves the second door, pivoting.

**6:35** Carved upon them were cherubim and palm trees and spread-out blossoms, and they were overlaid with gold laid down upon the carved work.

**6:36** And he built the innermost court: three courses of unhewn stones, and a course of dressed cedar round about. And he built a curtain for the court of the vestibule of the house that was before the temple.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek dates the temple's founding to the 'four hundred and fortieth year' of the Exodus (Hebrew: 'four hundred and eightieth') and gives the month names as 'Neiso' and 'Baad' (corresponding to Hebrew 'Ziv' and 'Bul'), combining foundation and completion notices in a single opening verse.
- **6:2** — The Greek gives the temple's length as 'forty cubits' and its height as 'twenty-five cubits,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text gives sixty cubits and thirty cubits respectively.
- **6:3** — 'Vestibule' translates Greek 'ailam' (a transliteration of Hebrew 'ulam'); similarly, in verse 5 and throughout the chapter, 'inner sanctuary' translates Greek 'dabeir' (Hebrew 'debir').
- **6:21** — 'Sealed gold' renders Greek 'chrysio synkekleismeno' (literally 'enclosed' or 'shut-up gold'), a literal rendering of Hebrew 'zahav sagur' denoting pure, choice treasury gold.
- **6:26** — The Greek text omits the numeral 'ten' here, stating only that the height of each cherub was measured 'by the cubit' (compare the ten-cubit stature stated in verse 23).
