## Text

**7:1** And King Solomon sent and brought Hiram from Tyre,

**7:2** the son of a widow woman, and he was from the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a man of Tyre, a craftsman in bronze, filled with skill and understanding and knowledge to perform every work in bronze; and he was brought to King Solomon, and he made all the works.

**7:3** And he cast the porch of the house: eighteen cubits was the height of the pillar, and a circumference of fourteen cubits encircled it; the thickness of the pillar was four fingers, its hollow interior; and thus was the second pillar.

**7:4** And he made two cast capitals to place upon the heads of the pillars; five cubits was the height of the one capital.

**7:5** And he made two networks to cover the capital of the pillars, even a network for the one capital and a network for the second capital.

**7:6** And hanging work, two rows of bronze pomegranates set in network, hanging work, row upon row; and thus he made for the second capital.

**7:7** And he set up the pillars of the porch of the temple; and he set up the one pillar and called its name Jachum, and he set up the second pillar and called its name Balaz.

**7:8** And upon the heads of the pillars was lily work by the porch, of four cubits.

**7:9** And there was a beam upon both of the pillars, and above the sides was a capital, the beam, of a cubit.

**7:10** And he made the sea ten cubits from its wall to its brim, round all about, and five cubits was its height, and gathered around it measured thirty-three cubits.

**7:11** And supports beneath its brim all around encircled it, ten in a cubit all around.

**7:12** And its brim was like the workmanship of the brim of a cup, the blossom of a lily, and its thickness was a handbreadth.

**7:13** And twelve oxen were beneath the sea: three looking toward the north, and three looking toward the west, and three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east; and all their hinder parts were toward the house, and the sea was above upon them.

**7:14** And he made ten bronze bases: five cubits was the length of one base, and four cubits its width, and six cubits its height.

**7:15** And this was the workmanship of the bases: they had an enclosed border, and an enclosed border between the projecting ledges.

**7:16** And upon their enclosed panels between the projecting ledges were lions and oxen and cherubim; and upon the projecting ledges likewise above; and beneath the lions and the oxen were spaces, descending work.

**7:17** And each base had four bronze wheels, and bronze axle supports; and their four corners had shoulder supports beneath the lavers.

**7:18** And there were stays on the wheels in the base; and the height of one wheel was a cubit and a half.

**7:19** And the workmanship of the wheels was the workmanship of chariot wheels; their stays and their rims and their entire structure were all cast.

**7:20** The four shoulder supports were upon the four corners of each base; out of the base itself came its shoulders;

**7:21** and on the top of the base its measure was half a cubit, round all about on the top of the base, along with the head of its stays and its enclosed panels; and it opened upon the four heads of its stays.

**7:22** And its enclosed panels had cherubim and lions and palm trees standing, each adjoining the other facing inward and all around.

**7:23** According to this he made all the ten bases, one order and one measure for them all.

**7:24** And he made ten bronze lavers, each laver holding forty choes by measure; one laver was upon one base for the ten bases.

**7:25** And he placed the five bases on the left side of the house; and the sea was on the right side of the house toward the east, from the quarter of the south.

**7:26** And Hiram made the cauldrons and the firepans and the bowls; and Hiram finished doing all the works that he made for King Solomon in the house of the Lord:

**7:27** two pillars, and the two wreathed ornaments of the pillars upon the heads of the pillars; and the two networks to cover both wreathed ornaments of the carvings that were upon the pillars;

**7:28** the four hundred pomegranates for both networks, two rows of pomegranates for the one network to cover both wreathed ornaments of the base that were upon both pillars;

**7:29** and the ten bases, and the ten lavers upon the bases;

**7:30** and the one sea, and the twelve oxen beneath the sea;

**7:31** and the cauldrons and the firepans and the bowls and all the vessels that Hiram made for King Solomon for the house of the Lord; and the forty-eight pillars of the house of the king and of the house of the Lord; all the works of the king Hiram made wholly of bronze.

**7:32** There was no weighing of the bronze from which he made all these works, because of its exceedingly great abundance; there was no end to the weight of the bronze.

**7:33** In the region around the Jordan he cast them, in the thick earth between Succoth and Seira.

**7:34** And King Solomon took the vessels that he had made in the house: the golden altar, and the golden table upon which were the loaves of the offering,

**7:35** and the lampstands—five on the left and five on the right before the inner sanctuary—of solid gold, and the lamp holders and the lamps and the oil pourers of gold;

**7:36** and the thresholds and the nails and the bowls and the dishes and the censers, of solid gold; and the doorframes of the doors of the innermost house, the Holy of Holies, and the doors of the temple, of gold.

**7:37** And the work that Solomon made for the house of the Lord was completed; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father and all the holy things of Solomon; the silver and the gold he placed into the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**7:38** And Solomon built his own house for thirteen years.

**7:39** And he built the house of the Forest of Lebanon: one hundred cubits was its length, and fifty cubits its width; and it was of three rows of cedar pillars, and there were cedar shoulder beams upon the pillars.

**7:40** And he paneled the house above upon the sides of the pillars; and the number of the pillars was forty-five in the row,

**7:41** and there were three beams, and space opposite space in three tiers.

**7:42** And all the doorways and the spaces were squared and framed with beams, and from doorway to door in three tiers.

**7:43** And the porch of the pillars was fifty in length and fifty in width, joined together, with a porch in front of them, and pillars and a thick beam in front of it for the porches.

**7:44** And the porch of the thrones, where he will judge there, was the porch of judgment.

**7:45** And their house—in a house he will dwell there—had one court winding out from these according to this workmanship; and a house for the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Solomon had taken, according to this porch.

**7:46** All these were of costly stones, hewn at intervals on the inside, and from the foundation to the cornices, and on the outside toward the great court,

**7:47** which was founded with great costly stones, stones of ten cubits and of eight cubits;

**7:48** and above with costly stones according to the measure of unhewn stones, and with cedars.

**7:49** The circuits of the great court were three rows of unhewn stones and a row of joined cedar.

**7:50** And Solomon finished his entire house.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek transliterates the architectural term for the porch as ailam throughout the chapter and gives the pillar circumference as fourteen cubits, compared with twelve cubits in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:7** — The Greek text gives the distinct pillar names Jachum (Iachoum) and Balaz, corresponding to Jachin and Boaz in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and designates them simply as the one pillar and the second pillar.
- **7:10** — The Greek reads from its wall to its brim and gives a circumference of thirty-three cubits, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads from brim to brim and thirty cubits.
- **7:14** — Bases renders the Greek transliteration mechonoth used through verse twenty-nine; the Greek also records the dimensions as five cubits by four cubits by six cubits.
- **7:33** — Seira is the Greek text's distinct proper name for the location corresponding to Zarethan in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
