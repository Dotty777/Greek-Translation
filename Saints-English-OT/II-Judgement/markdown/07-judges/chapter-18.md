## Text

**18:1** In those days there was no king in Israel; and in those days the tribe of Dan was seeking for itself an inheritance to dwell in, for until that day no inheritance had fallen to it in the midst of the tribes of Israel.

**18:2** And the sons of Dan sent from their clans five men, men of valor, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and to search it out, and they said to them, "Go and search out the land." And they came as far as Mount Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and they themselves lodged there,

**18:3** in the house of Micah. And they recognized the voice of the young man, the Levite, and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? And what are you doing in this place? And what do you have here?"

**18:4** And he said to them, "Thus and so Micah has done for me, and he hired me, and I have become a priest to him."

**18:5** And they said to him, "Inquire now of God, and we shall know whether our way in which we are walking will prosper."

**18:6** And the priest said to them, "Go in peace; before the Lord is your way in which you are walking."

**18:7** And the five men departed and came to Laish; and they saw the people who were in its midst dwelling in confidence, after the manner of the Sidonians, living quietly; and there was no one deterring or putting to shame any matter in the land, nor any heir extorting treasure; and they were far from the Sidonians, and had no dealings with any man.

**18:8** And the five men came to their brethren at Zorah and Eshtaol, and said to their brethren, "Why are you sitting still?"

**18:9** And they said, "Arise, and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is exceedingly good. And are you keeping still? Do not hesitate to go and enter in to inherit the land.

**18:10** And whenever you come, you shall enter to a people dwelling in confidence, and the land is broad, for God has given it into your hand—a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

**18:11** And six hundred men of the clans of Dan departed from there, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, girded with weapons of war.

**18:12** And they went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. Therefore that place has been called Camp of Dan to this day; behold, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

**18:13** And they passed on from there to Mount Ephraim, and came as far as the house of Micah.

**18:14** And the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish answered and said to their brethren, "You know that there is in this house an ephod, and teraphim, and a carved image, and a molten image; and now know what you will do."

**18:15** And they turned aside there and entered into the house of the young man the Levite, the house of Micah, and asked him concerning his welfare.

**18:16** And the six hundred men who were girded with their weapons of war, who were of the sons of Dan, stood by the entrance of the gate.

**18:17** And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up,

**18:18** and entered there into the house of Micah, while the priest stood by; and they took the carved image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image. And the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

**18:19** And they said to him, "Hold your peace, put your hand upon your mouth, and come with us, and become a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or for you to become priest to a tribe and a house for a clan of Israel?"

**18:20** And the heart of the priest was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the carved image, and the molten image, and went into the midst of the people.

**18:21** And they turned and departed, and placed the children, and the livestock, and the baggage before them.

**18:22** When they had gone far from the house of Micah, behold, Micah and the men who were in the houses beside the house of Micah cried out and overtook the sons of Dan.

**18:23** And the sons of Dan turned their faces and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have cried out?"

**18:24** And Micah said, "Because you have taken my carved image which I made, and the priest, and have gone away; and what more do I have? And why is this that you say to me, 'Why do you cry out?'"

**18:25** And the sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest men bitter of soul fall upon us, and they add life and the life of your house."

**18:26** And the sons of Dan went on their way; and Micah saw that they were stronger than he, and he returned to his house.

**18:27** And the sons of Dan took what Micah had made, and the priest who belonged to him, and came upon Laish, upon a people living quietly and trusting in confidence; and they struck them with the edge of the sword, and burned the city with fire.

**18:28** And there was no deliverer, because it was far from the Sidonians, and they had no dealings with any man; and it was in the valley of the house of Rahab. And they built the city and dwelt in it,

**18:29** and they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; yet Ulamais was the name of the city formerly.

**18:30** And the sons of Dan set up for themselves the carved image; and Jonathan the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land.

**18:31** And they set up for themselves the carved image which Micah had made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh. And it came to pass in those days that there was no king in Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — The Greek text of Codex Vaticanus uniquely reads "nor any heir extorting treasure," reflecting a reading of the Hebrew consonants as "treasure" rather than "restraint" or "dominion."
- **18:8** — In the Greek text the five scouts speak to their brethren ("Why are you sitting still?"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the brethren asking the scouts what they report.
- **18:25** — The Greek literally reads "fall upon us, and they add life and the life of your house," using the verb "add" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a similar-sounding verb meaning "take away."
- **18:28** — The Greek renders the place name as "the house of Rahab," corresponding to Beth-rehob in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **18:29** — "Ulamais" is a Septuagint-distinct place name formed by transliterating the Hebrew word for "however" together with the city name "Laish."
