## Text

**6:1** And the sons of Israel did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and the Lord gave them into the hand of Midian for seven years.

**6:2** And the hand of Midian prevailed over Israel; and because of Midian the sons of Israel made for themselves the crevices that are in the mountains, and the caves, and the steep crags.

**6:3** And it came to pass, whenever the sons of Israel had sown, that Midian and Amalek would come up, and the sons of the east would come up with them; and they encamped against them,

**6:4** and destroyed their produce as far as the entrance to Gaza, and left no sustenance of life in the land of Israel, nor among the flocks a bull or a donkey.

**6:5** For they and their livestock would come up, and their tents would arrive, like locusts in multitude, and of them and of their camels there was no number; and they would come into the land of Israel and lay it waste,

**6:6** and Israel was greatly impoverished because of Midian.

**6:7** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord because of Midian.

**6:8** And the Lord sent a man, a prophet, to the sons of Israel, and he said to them, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: I am He who brought you up from the land of Egypt, and brought you out of the house of your bondage,

**6:9** and delivered you out of the hand of Egypt and out of the hand of all who oppressed you, and cast them out from before your face, and gave you their land;

**6:10** and I said to you, 'I am the Lord your God; you shall not fear the gods of the Amorite, among whom you shall dwell in their land.' Yet you have not listened to My voice."

**6:11** And the angel of the Lord came and sat under the terebinth tree that was in Ephrathah, in the possession of Joash, the father of Esdri; and Gideon his son was beating out wheat in a winepress, to flee from the face of Midian.

**6:12** And the angel of the Lord appeared to him and said to him, "The Lord is with you, O mighty one of strength."

**6:13** And Gideon said to him, "Pardon me, my lord, and if the Lord is with us, why have these evils found us? And where are all His wondrous deeds which our fathers recounted to us, saying, 'Did not the Lord bring us up from Egypt?' And now He has cast us out and delivered us into the hand of Midian."

**6:14** And the angel of the Lord turned to him and said, "Go in this strength of yours, and you shall save Israel out of the hand of Midian. Behold, I have sent you."

**6:15** And Gideon said to him, "Pardon me, my lord, by what shall I save Israel? Behold, my thousand has grown weak in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's house."

**6:16** And the angel of the Lord said to him, "The Lord shall be with you, and you shall strike Midian as one man."

**6:17** And Gideon said to him, "If now I have found mercy in your eyes, and you will do for me today all that you have spoken with me,

**6:18** do not depart from here until I come to you, and I will bring out the sacrifice and set it before you." And he said, "I myself will sit until you return."

**6:19** And Gideon went in and prepared a kid of the goats, and an ephah of flour as unleavened cakes; and the flesh he put in the basket, and the broth he poured into the pot, and he brought them out to him under the terebinth tree, and drew near.

**6:20** And the angel of God said to him, "Take the flesh and the unleavened cakes and place them upon that rock, and pour out the broth close by." And he did so.

**6:21** And the angel of the Lord stretched out the end of the staff that was in his hand and touched the flesh and the unleavened cakes; and fire went up out of the rock and consumed the flesh and the unleavened cakes; and the angel of the Lord departed from his eyes.

**6:22** And Gideon saw that he was the angel of the Lord; and Gideon said, "Alas, my Lord, O Lord! For I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face."

**6:23** And the Lord said to him, "Peace be to you; do not fear, you shall by no means die."

**6:24** And Gideon built an altar there to the Lord, and called it Peace of the Lord; to this day it is still in Ephrathah, of the father of Esdri.

**6:25** And it came to pass on that night that the Lord said to him, "Take the young bull that belongs to your father, and a second bull seven years old, and you shall tear down the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and the sacred grove that is upon it you shall destroy;

**6:26** and you shall build an altar to the Lord your God upon the summit of this Mauek in proper order, and you shall take the second bull and offer up a whole burnt offering with the wood of the sacred grove which you shall utterly destroy."

**6:27** And Gideon took ten men from his own servants, and did just as the Lord had spoken to him; and it came to pass, because he feared the house of his father and the men of the city too much to do it by day, that he did it by night.

**6:28** And the men of the city rose early in the morning, and behold, the altar of Baal had been torn down, and the sacred grove that was upon it had been destroyed; and they saw the second bull, which he had offered up upon the altar that was built.

**6:29** And each man said to his neighbor, "Who has done this thing?" And they inquired and searched, and knew that Gideon the son of Joash had done this thing.

**6:30** And the men of the city said to Joash, "Bring out your son, and let him die, because he has torn down the altar of Baal, and because he has destroyed the sacred grove that was upon it."

**6:31** And Gideon the son of Joash said to all the men who rose up against him, "Will you now plead for Baal? Or will you save him? Whoever pleads for him, let him be put to death by morning! If he is a god, let him plead for himself, because he has torn down his altar."

**6:32** And he called it on that day Arbaal, saying, "Let Baal plead against him, because his altar was torn down."

**6:33** And all Midian and Amalek and the sons of the east were gathered together, and they encamped in the valley of Jezreel.

**6:34** And the Spirit of the Lord empowered Gideon, and he blew the horn, and Abiezer feared after him.

**6:35** And he sent messengers into all Manasseh, and into Asher, and into Zebulun, and Naphtali; and he went up to meet them.

**6:36** And Gideon said to God, "If You are saving Israel by my hand, just as You have spoken,

**6:37** behold, I am placing the fleece of wool on the threshing floor; if there shall be dew upon the fleece only, and dryness upon all the ground, then I shall know that You will save Israel by my hand, just as You have spoken."

**6:38** And it was so; and he rose early on the next day and squeezed out the fleece, and dew dripped from the fleece, a bowl full of water.

**6:39** And Gideon said to God, "Do not let Your wrath be kindled against me, and I will speak yet once more; let me indeed test yet once more with the fleece: let dryness be upon the fleece only, and upon all the ground let there be dew."

**6:40** And God did so on that night; and there was dryness upon the fleece only, and upon all the ground there was dew.

## Translator Notes

- **6:11** — Codex Vaticanus reads Ἐφραθά ("Ephrathah") for Ophrah and renders the clan name Abiezrite literally as πατρὸς τοῦ Ἐσδρεί ("the father of Esdri"; cf. 6:24).
- **6:26** — Codex Vaticanus transliterates the Hebrew noun for "stronghold" as a proper name, τοῦ Μαουὲκ τούτου ("this Mauek").
- **6:31** — Codex Vaticanus attributes this speech to Γεδεὼν υἱὸς Ἰωάς ("Gideon the son of Joash"), whereas the Hebrew text attributes it to Joash.
- **6:32** — Codex Vaticanus reads neuter αὐτό ("it") and the name Ἀρβάαλ ("Arbaal"), corresponding to Hebrew Jerubbaal, followed by a passive verb ("was torn down").
- **6:34** — Codex Vaticanus reads ἐφοβήθη ("feared") where the Hebrew text reads "was called out" or "rallied."
