## Text

**5:1** And Deborah and Barak the son of Abinoam were saying on that day:

**5:2** "A revelation was revealed in Israel; when the people offered themselves willingly, bless the Lord.

**5:3** Hear, O kings, and give ear, O satraps; I am for the Lord; I, even I, will sing; I will sing praise to the Lord, the God of Israel.

**5:4** O Lord, at Your going forth in Seir, when You departed from the field of Edom, the earth trembled, and heaven dropped dews, and the clouds dropped water.

**5:5** Mountains were shaken before the face of the Lord Eloi, this Sinai, before the face of the Lord, the God of Israel.

**5:6** In the days of Shamgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, they forsook the highways and walked in byways; they walked in crooked paths.

**5:7** Mighty men failed in Israel, they failed, until Deborah arose, until a mother arose in Israel.

**5:8** They chose new gods, when they fought against the cities of rulers; was a shield and a spear seen among forty thousand in Israel?

**5:9** My heart is toward the ordinances for Israel; you who offer yourselves willingly among the people, bless the Lord.

**5:10** You who ride upon a female donkey at midday, you who sit upon the judgment seat, and you who walk upon the ways of the councilors along the road—

**5:11** declare it from the sound of those who strike up music, in the midst of those who draw water; there they shall give acts of righteousness. O Lord, increase acts of righteousness in Israel; then the people of the Lord went down to the cities.

**5:12** Awake, awake, Deborah! Awake, awake, speak a song! Arise, Barak, and take your captivity captive, O son of Abinoam!

**5:13** Then a remnant went down to the strong ones; the people of the Lord went down for Him among the mighty ones from me.

**5:14** Ephraim uprooted them in Amalek; after you, Benjamin, among your peoples; in me, Machir, they went down searching out, and from Zebulun, those who draw with the rod of the recounting of a scribe.

**5:15** And chiefs in Issachar were with Deborah and Barak. So Barak sent forth into the valleys at his feet. To the portions of Reuben there were great ones reaching the heart.

**5:16** Why did they sit in the midst of the double load to hear the piping of messengers? For the divisions of Reuben there were great searchings of heart.

**5:17** Gilead pitched his tent beyond the Jordan; and Dan, why does he sojourn in ships? Asher sat along the shore of the seas, and by his outlets he shall dwell in tents.

**5:18** Zebulun is a people that reproached its soul to death, and Naphtali upon the heights of the field.

**5:19** Their kings came, they drew up in battle array; then the kings of Canaan fought in Taanach by the water of Megiddo; a gift of silver they did not take.

**5:20** From heaven the stars drew up in battle array; from their paths they drew up in battle array against Sisera.

**5:21** The torrent of Kishon swept them away, that torrent of ancient times, the torrent of Kishon; my mighty soul shall trample him down.

**5:22** Then the heels of the horse were entangled; in haste his strong ones hastened.

**5:23** 'Curse Meroz,' said the angel of the Lord, 'curse her! Cursed is everyone who dwells in her, because they did not come to the help of the Lord, to the help among the mighty.'

**5:24** May Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, be blessed among women; above women in tents may she be blessed.

**5:25** He asked for water, she gave milk; in a bowl of nobles she brought forth butter.

**5:26** Her left hand she stretched out to the peg, and her right hand to the hammer of laborers; and she struck Sisera with the hammer, she drove through his head and smote him, she drove through his temple.

**5:27** Between her feet he rolled down, he fell and lay down between her feet; sinking down, he fell; just as he sank down, there he fell, destroyed.

**5:28** Through the window the mother of Sisera leaned out, outside the arrow-slit: 'Why has his chariot been put to shame? Why have the feet of his chariots tarried?'

**5:29** Her wise princesses answered her, and she herself returned her words to herself:

**5:30** 'Will they not find him dividing spoil? Mercifully he will show mercy to the head of a man; spoil of dyed fabrics for Sisera, spoil of dyed fabrics of embroidery, dyed fabrics of embroiderers are they, spoil for his neck.'

**5:31** So may all Your enemies perish, O Lord; and may those who love Him be as the going forth of the sun in its strength." And the land had rest for forty years.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Greek text reads ἦσαν ("were ... saying"), though the uncial ΗΣΑΝ of Codex Vaticanus may also be read as ᾖσαν ("sang").
- **5:2** — Codex Vaticanus spells the verb ἀκουσιασθῆναι, an orthographic variant of ἑκουσιασθῆναι ("to offer oneself willingly"; compare ἑκουσιαζόμενοι in 5:9).
- **5:5** — Codex Vaticanus reads Κυρίου Ἐλωεί ("the Lord Eloi"), preserving a transliterated divine title not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:16** — Codex Vaticanus reads συρισμοῦ ἀγγέλων ("the piping of messengers"), differing from the Hebrew text's "piping for the flocks."
- **5:30** — Codex Vaticanus translates the Hebrew expression for captive maidens through the root meaning "mercy," yielding οἰκτείρμων οἰκτειρήσει ("mercifully he will show mercy").
