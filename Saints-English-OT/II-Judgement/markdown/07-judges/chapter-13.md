## Text

**13:1** And the sons of Israel again did evil in the sight of the Lord, and the Lord delivered them into the hand of the Philistines forty years.

**13:2** Now there was a certain man from Saral, of the clan of the family of the Danites, and his name was Manoah; and his wife was barren and had borne no children.

**13:3** And the Angel of the Lord appeared to the woman and said to her, "Behold, you are barren and have borne no children, yet you shall conceive a son.

**13:4** Now therefore, take heed, and do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean;

**13:5** for behold, you are with child and shall bear a son. And no iron shall come upon his head, for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb; and he shall begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines."

**13:6** So the woman went in and spoke to her husband, saying, "A man of God came to me, and his countenance was like the countenance of an angel of God, exceedingly awesome; and I did not ask him where he was from, nor did he tell me his name.

**13:7** And he said to me, 'Behold, you are with child and shall bear a son. Now therefore, do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean, for the child shall be holy to God from the womb until the day of his death.'"

**13:8** Then Manoah prayed to the Lord and said, "I pray You, O Lord Adonai, let the man of God whom You sent come to us again and teach us what we shall do for the child who is to be born."

**13:9** And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the Angel of God came again to the woman; and she was sitting in the field, and Manoah her husband was not with her.

**13:10** Then the woman hastened and ran and told her husband, and said to him, "Behold, the man who came to me on that day has appeared to me!"

**13:11** So Manoah arose and followed his wife, and came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" And the Angel said, "I am."

**13:12** And Manoah said, "Now your word will come to pass; what shall be the ordinance for the child, and his deeds?"

**13:13** And the Angel of the Lord said to Manoah, "Of all that I have spoken to the woman, she shall take heed:

**13:14** she shall not eat of anything that comes from the vine of wine, nor let her drink wine or strong intoxicating drink, nor let her eat anything unclean; all that I have commanded for him, she shall observe."

**13:15** Then Manoah said to the Angel of the Lord, "Let us detain you here, and let us prepare a young goat before you."

**13:16** And the Angel of the Lord said to Manoah, "Though you detain Me, I will not eat of your bread; and if you prepare a burnt offering, you shall offer it up to the Lord." For Manoah did not know that He was the Angel of the Lord.

**13:17** Then Manoah said to the Angel of the Lord, "What is your name? For may your word come to pass, and we will honor you."

**13:18** And the Angel of the Lord said to him, "Why do you ask this—My name? And it is wonderful."

**13:19** So Manoah took the young goat and the offering and offered them upon the rock to the Lord, and He acted distinctly, while Manoah and his wife looked on.

**13:20** And it came to pass, as the flame went up from above the altar toward heaven, that the Angel of the Lord ascended in the flame of the altar. And Manoah and his wife looked on, and they fell on their faces to the ground.

**13:21** And the Angel of the Lord did not appear again to Manoah and to his wife. Then Manoah knew that He was the Angel of the Lord.

**13:22** And Manoah said to his wife, "We shall surely die, because we have seen God!"

**13:23** But his wife said to him, "If the Lord had desired to put us to death, He would not have accepted a burnt offering and an offering from our hand, nor would He have shown us all these things, nor at this time would He have made us hear these things."

**13:24** So the woman bore a son and called his name Samson; and the child grew, and the Lord blessed him.

**13:25** And the Spirit of the Lord began to go forth with him in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Codex Vaticanus spells the town Saral here, whereas it appears as Saraa (Zorah) in verse 25.
- **13:5** — The Greek reads sideros ("iron") rather than "razor," rendering the Hebrew term broadly.
- **13:14** — Codex Vaticanus reads the masculine pronoun auto ("for him" / "him"), referring to the promised child, whereas the Hebrew text reads feminine ("her").
- **13:19** — Codex Vaticanus translates the Hebrew with diechorisen ("separated/set apart"), reflecting an alternate sense of the Hebrew root rather than "acted wondrously."
- **13:25** — Codex Vaticanus reads synekporeuesthai auto ("to go forth with him"), in contrast to the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "to stir/impel him."
