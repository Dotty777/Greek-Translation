## Text

**1:1** Now it came to pass after the death of Joshua that the children of Israel inquired of the Lord, saying, "Who shall go up for us against the Canaanites as leader, to make war against them?"

**1:2** And the Lord said, "Judah shall go up; behold, I have given the land into his hand."

**1:3** Then Judah said to Simeon his brother, "Come up with me into my lot, and let us do battle against the Canaanites; and I also will go with you into your lot." So Simeon went with him.

**1:4** And Judah went up, and the Lord delivered the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hands; and they cut them down in Bezek, as many as ten thousand men.

**1:5** And they came upon Adoni-Bezek in Bezek and arrayed themselves in battle against him, and they struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**1:6** Then Adoni-Bezek fled, and they pursued after him and captured him, and cut off the extremities of his hands and the extremities of his feet.

**1:7** And Adoni-Bezek said, "Seventy kings, having the extremities of their hands and the extremities of their feet cut off, used to gather what fell beneath my table; therefore, just as I have done, so God has repaid me." And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**1:8** Now the children of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, and set the city ablaze with fire.

**1:9** And afterward the children of Judah went down to make war against the Canaanites who dwelt in the hill country, and in the south, and in the lowland.

**1:10** Then Judah marched against the Canaanites who dwelt in Hebron, and Hebron came out to meet them—now the name of Hebron formerly was Kariarboxepher—and they struck down Sheshai, and Achinaan, and Talmai, the offspring of Anak.

**1:11** And from there they went up against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir formerly was Kiriath-sepher, the City of Letters.

**1:12** And Caleb said, "Whoever strikes the City of Letters and captures it, to him I will give Aza my daughter as a wife."

**1:13** And Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, the younger, captured it; and Caleb gave him Aza his daughter as a wife.

**1:14** Now it came to pass upon her arrival that Othniel urged her to ask her father for a field. And she murmured and cried out from the donkey, "Into a land of the south you have given me away!" And Caleb said to her, "What is the matter with you?"

**1:15** And Achsah said to him, "Give me a blessing, for into a land of the south you have given me away; you shall also give me a redemption of water." So Caleb gave her according to her heart the redemption of the upper places and the redemption of the lower places.

**1:16** Now the children of Jethro the Kenite, the father-in-law of Moses, went up from the City of Palms with the children of Judah into the wilderness that is in the south of Judah, which is at the descent of Arad; and they dwelt with the people.

**1:17** Then Judah went with Simeon his brother, and struck down the Canaanites who dwelt in Sephek, and utterly destroyed them; and he called the name of the city Anathema.

**1:18** And Judah did not inherit Gaza nor its borders, nor Ashkelon nor its borders, nor Ekron nor its borders, nor Ashdod nor its surrounding lands.

**1:19** And the Lord was with Judah, and he inherited the hill country, for they were not able to utterly destroy the inhabitants of the valley, because Rechab had given orders to them.

**1:20** And they gave Hebron to Caleb, just as Moses had spoken; and from there he inherited the three cities of the sons of Anak.

**1:21** Yet the children of Benjamin did not dispossess the Jebusites who dwelt in Jerusalem; so the Jebusites have dwelt with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.

**1:22** Now the children of Joseph, they also went up against Bethel, and the Lord was with them.

**1:23** And they encamped and spied out Bethel; now the name of their city formerly was Luz.

**1:24** And the watchmen looked, and behold, a man was coming out of the city; and they took him and said to him, "Show us the entrance to the city, and we will show mercy to you."

**1:25** So he showed them the entrance to the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword; but the man and his kindred they sent away.

**1:26** And the man went to the land of the Hittites, and built a city there, and called its name Luz; that is its name to this day.

**1:27** Moreover, Manasseh did not remove Beth-shan, which is the City of the Scythians, nor its daughters nor its surrounding towns, nor Taanach nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Dor nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Balak nor its surrounding towns nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Megiddo nor its surrounding towns nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Ibleam nor its surrounding towns nor its daughters; and the Canaanites began to dwell in that land.

**1:28** And it came to pass, when Israel grew strong, that they put the Canaanites under tribute, and did not utterly remove them.

**1:29** And Ephraim did not remove the Canaanites who dwelt in Gezer; so the Canaanites dwelt in his midst in Gezer and became subject to tribute.

**1:30** And Zebulun did not remove the inhabitants of Kedron nor the inhabitants of Domana; so the Canaanites dwelt in their midst and became subject to tribute to them.

**1:31** And Asher did not remove the inhabitants of Acco—and it became subject to tribute to him—nor the inhabitants of Dor, nor the inhabitants of Sidon, nor the inhabitants of Dalaph, nor Aschazi, nor Chebda, nor Naei, nor Ereo.

**1:32** So Asher dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites who inhabited the land, because he was not able to remove them.

**1:33** And Naphtali did not remove the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh nor the inhabitants of Beth-anach; so Naphtali dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites who inhabited the land, and the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and of Beth-anath became subject to tribute to them.

**1:34** And the Amorites pressed the children of Dan into the hill country, for they did not allow them to come down into the valley.

**1:35** And the Amorites began to dwell in the potsherd mountain, in which are the bears and in which are the foxes, in Myrsinon and in Thalabin; yet the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy upon the Amorites, and they became subject to tribute to them.

**1:36** And the border of the Amorites was from the Ascent of Akrabbim, from the Rock and upward.

## Translator Notes

- **1:10** — Codex Vaticanus adds "and Hebron came out to meet them" and "the offspring of Anak," reads Kariarboxepher (apparently conflating Kiriath-arba with Kiriath-sepher), and reads Achinaan for Hebrew Ahiman; similarly, vv. 12–13 read Aza (Achsah in v. 15) and v. 17 reads Sephek and Anathema (Hormah).
- **1:15** — The Greek reads "redemption of water" (lytrosis), reflecting an understanding of the Hebrew word for "springs" from the root meaning "to redeem," and adds "according to her heart."
- **1:18** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reports that Judah took Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, the Septuagint states that Judah did not inherit them and adds Ashdod.
- **1:19** — Codex Vaticanus reads "because Rechab had given orders to them," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "because they had chariots of iron."
- **1:35** — Codex Vaticanus translates Mount Heres as "the potsherd mountain," adds "in which are the bears and in which are the foxes," and reads Myrsinon ("Myrtle-grove") and Thalabin for Aijalon and Shaalbim; compare also the distinct Greek place names Balak, Kedron, Domana, Dalaph, Aschazi, Chebda, Naei, Ereo, and Beth-anach in vv. 27–33.
