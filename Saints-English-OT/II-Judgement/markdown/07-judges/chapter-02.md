## Text

**2:1** Now the Angel of the Lord went up from Gilgal to the Place of Weeping, and to Bethel, and to the house of Israel, and said to them, "Thus says the Lord: I brought you up out of Egypt, and brought you into the land which I swore to your fathers; and I said, 'I will never break My covenant with you,

**2:2** and you shall make no covenant with those who dwell in this land, nor shall you worship their gods, but you shall shatter their carved images and tear down their altars.' Yet you have not obeyed My voice, because you have done these things.

**2:3** Therefore I also said, 'I will by no means remove them from before you; and they shall be for distresses to you, and their gods shall be a snare to you.'"

**2:4** And it came to pass, when the Angel of the Lord spoke these words to all the children of Israel, that the people lifted up their voice and wept.

**2:5** And they named the name of that place Places of Weeping; and they sacrificed there to the Lord.

**2:6** Now Joshua sent the people away, and each man went to his own inheritance to possess the land.

**2:7** And the people served the Lord all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who lived long after Joshua, who knew all the great work of the Lord which He had done in Israel.

**2:8** And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died, being one hundred and ten years old.

**2:9** And they buried him within the border of his inheritance in Timnath-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, on the north side of Mount Gaash.

**2:10** And all that generation also was gathered to their fathers; and another generation arose after them who did not know the Lord nor the work which He had done in Israel.

**2:11** Then the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and served the Baals.

**2:12** And they forsook the Lord, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt; and they walked after other gods from among the gods of the nations that were all around them, and worshiped them; and they provoked the Lord to anger.

**2:13** And they forsook Him, and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.

**2:14** And the Lord was incensed with wrath against Israel, and delivered them into the hands of plunderers, and they plundered them; and He sold them into the hands of their enemies all around, so that they could no longer stand before the face of their enemies in all places wherever they went out.

**2:15** And the hand of the Lord was against them for calamity, just as the Lord had spoken, and just as the Lord had sworn to them; and He afflicted them exceedingly.

**2:16** Then the Lord raised up judges, and the Lord saved them out of the hand of those who plundered them. Yet they did not even obey the judges,

**2:17** for they played the harlot after other gods and worshiped them; and they turned aside quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked, in obeying the words of the Lord; they did not do so.

**2:18** And whenever the Lord raised up judges for them, the Lord was with the judge and saved them out of the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for the Lord was moved with compassion by their groaning because of those who besieged them and afflicted them.

**2:19** Yet it came to pass, when the judge died, that they turned back and again acted more corruptly than their fathers, to walk after other gods, to serve them and worship them; they did not cast away their practices nor their stubborn ways.

**2:20** Then the Lord was incensed with wrath against Israel, and He said, "Because this nation has forsaken My covenant which I commanded their fathers, and has not obeyed My voice,

**2:21** I also will no longer remove a single man from before them of the nations which Joshua the son of Nun left in the land." And He left them

**2:22** to test Israel by them, whether they keep the way of the Lord to walk in it, in the manner that their fathers kept it, or not.

**2:23** Therefore the Lord will leave these nations, so as not to remove them quickly; and He did not deliver them into the hand of Joshua.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Septuagint translates Hebrew Bochim as "the Place of Weeping" (plural "Places of Weeping" in v. 5), adds "and to Bethel, and to the house of Israel" and "Thus says the Lord," and in v. 2 adds "nor shall you worship their gods, but you shall shatter their carved images."
- **2:3** — The Greek renders the Hebrew expression as "for distresses" (synochas) rather than "in your sides," reading the Hebrew consonants as "distresses" or "adversaries."
- **2:21** — Codex Vaticanus reads "left in the land. And He left them" where the Hebrew reads "left when he died"; in v. 23, the Greek also uses the future tense ("will leave").
