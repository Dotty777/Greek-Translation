## Text

**12:1** And the men of Ephraim cried out, and crossed over to the north, and said to Jephthah, "Why did you cross over to do battle against the sons of Ammon, and have not called us to go with you? We will burn your house upon you with fire."

**12:2** And Jephthah said to them, "I was a man of strife, I and my people, and the sons of Ammon exceedingly; and I cried out to you, and you did not save me from their hand.

**12:3** And I saw that you were not a savior, so I put my life in my hand and crossed over to the sons of Ammon, and the Lord gave them into my hand. Why then have you come up against me this day to do battle against me?"

**12:4** Then Jephthah gathered together all the men of Gilead and did battle with Ephraim. And the men of Gilead struck down Ephraim, because the fugitives of Ephraim had said, "You are Gilead in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasseh."

**12:5** And Gilead seized the fords of the Jordan of Ephraim. And the fugitives of Ephraim said to them, "Let us cross over." And the men of Gilead said to them, "Are you an Ephraimite?" And he said, "No."

**12:6** Then they said to him, "Say now, 'Ear of grain.'" And he did not succeed in speaking it thus; so they laid hold of him and slaughtered him at the fords of the Jordan. And there fell at that time from Ephraim forty-two thousand.

**12:7** And Jephthah judged Israel sixty years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in his city in Gilead.

**12:8** And after him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.

**12:9** And he had thirty sons and thirty daughters, whom he sent outside, and thirty daughters he brought in from outside for his sons. And he judged Israel seven years.

**12:10** Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

**12:11** And after him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years.

**12:12** Then Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Elon, in the land of Zebulun.

**12:13** And after him Abdon the son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, judged Israel.

**12:14** And he had forty sons and thirty grandsons who rode on seventy colts. And he judged Israel eight years.

**12:15** Then Abdon the son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, died and was buried in Pirathon, in the land of Ephraim, in the mountain of Amalek.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Greek reads eis borran ("to the north"), translating the Hebrew place-name Zaphon according to its literal directional meaning.
- **12:4** — Codex Vaticanus places "the fugitives of Ephraim" as the subject of "said," whereas the Hebrew text is commonly understood as part of the taunt ("You Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim").
- **12:6** — The Greek translates the Hebrew password Shibboleth into its literal meaning (Stachys, "ear of grain") rather than transliterating it, and omits the mispronounced form Sibboleth.
- **12:7** — Codex Vaticanus reads hexekonta ("sixty") years here, in contrast to the six years of the Hebrew Masoretic Text and Codex Alexandrinus.
