## Text

**17:1** Now there was a man from Mount Ephraim, and his name was Micah.

**17:2** And he said to his mother, "The one thousand one hundred pieces of silver which you took for yourself, and you cursed me and spoke in my ears—behold, the silver is with me; I took it." And his mother said, "Blessed be my son by the Lord."

**17:3** So he restored the one thousand one hundred pieces of silver to his mother; and his mother said, "I have wholly consecrated the silver to the Lord from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a molten image; and now I will return it to you."

**17:4** So he restored the silver to his mother; and his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave it to a silversmith, who made it into a carved image and a molten image; and it was in the house of Micah.

**17:5** And the house of Micah was to him a house of God; and he made an ephod and teraphim, and he filled the hand of one of his sons, who became his priest.

**17:6** Now in those days there was no king in Israel; each man did what was upright in his own eyes.

**17:7** And there was a young man from Bethlehem, of the clan of Judah, who was a Levite; and he was sojourning there.

**17:8** And the man departed from Bethlehem, the city of Judah, to sojourn in whatever place he might find; and he came as far as Mount Ephraim, even to the house of Micah, as he made his journey.

**17:9** And Micah said to him, "From where do you come?" And he said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to sojourn in whatever place I may find."

**17:10** And Micah said to him, "Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a priest, and I will give you ten pieces of silver per day, a suit of garments, and the provisions for your livelihood."

**17:11** And the Levite went and began to sojourn with the man; and the young man was with him like one of his sons.

**17:12** So Micah filled the hand of the Levite, and he became his priest, and he stayed in the house of Micah.

**17:13** Then Micah said, "Now I know that the Lord will do good to me, because the Levite has become my priest."

## Translator Notes

- **17:2** — Codex B reads "which you took for yourself, and you cursed me," differing from the Masoretic Text's passive "which were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse."
- **17:5** — Codex B reads "the house of Micah" (rather than "the man Micah" as in the Masoretic Text); "filled the hand" (eplerosen ten cheira) is the literal consecration idiom meaning to ordain or install as priest (also in v. 12).
- **17:10** — Codex B reads eis hemeran ("per day"), whereas the Masoretic Text has layyamim ("per year").
