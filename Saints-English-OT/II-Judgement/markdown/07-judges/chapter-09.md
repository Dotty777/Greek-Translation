## Text

**9:1** And Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem to his mother's brothers, and spoke to them and to all the kindred of the house of his mother's father, saying,

**9:2** "Speak, I pray, in the ears of all the men of Shechem, 'What is better for you: that seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, should rule over you, or that one man should rule over you? And remember that I am your bone and your flesh.'"

**9:3** And his mother's brothers spoke all these words concerning him in the ears of all the men of Shechem; and their heart inclined after Abimelech, for they said, "He is our brother."

**9:4** And they gave him seventy pieces of silver from the house of Baal-Berith; and Abimelech hired for himself empty and cowardly men, and they followed after him.

**9:5** Then he entered his father's house in Ephrathah and slew his brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, upon one stone. Yet Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, was left, for he hid himself.

**9:6** And all the men of Shechem and all the house of Beth-Maalon gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king by the oak that was found at the pillar in Shechem.

**9:7** Now it was told to Jotham; and he went and stood on the summit of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice and wept, and said to them, "Listen to me, men of Shechem, and God will listen to you.

**9:8** The trees once went forth to anoint a king over themselves, and they said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us!'

**9:9** But the olive tree said to them, 'Should I forsake my richness, by which men will glorify God, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:10** Then the trees said to the fig tree, 'Come, reign over us!'

**9:11** But the fig tree said to them, 'Should I forsake my sweetness and my good produce, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:12** Then the trees said to the vine, 'Come, you reign over us!'

**9:13** But the vine said to them, 'Should I forsake my wine, which gladdens God and men, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:14** Then all the trees said to the bramble, 'Come, you reign over us!'

**9:15** And the bramble said to the trees, 'If in truth you anoint me to reign over you, come, take shelter under my shadow; and if not, let fire come forth from me and devour the cedars of Lebanon!'

**9:16** Now therefore, if you have acted in truth and integrity and have made Abimelech king, and if you have done goodness to Jerubbaal and to his house, and if you have dealt with him according to the recompense of his hand—

**9:17** for my father fought for you, and cast away his life in the open, and delivered you from the hand of Midian,

**9:18** yet you have risen up against my father's house today, and have slain his sons, seventy men, upon one stone, and have made Abimelech, the son of his maidservant, king over the men of Shechem, because he is your brother—

**9:19** if then you have acted in truth and integrity with Jerubbaal and with his house on this day, may you rejoice in Abimelech, and may he also rejoice over you!

**9:20** But if not, may fire come forth from Abimelech and devour the men of Shechem and the house of Beth-Maalon; and may fire come forth from the men of Shechem and from the house of Beth-Maalon and consume Abimelech!"

**9:21** Then Jotham fled and escaped, and went as far as Beer, and dwelt there away from the presence of Abimelech his brother.

**9:22** And Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.

**9:23** Then God sent forth an evil spirit between Abimelech and the men of Shechem; and the men of Shechem dealt treacherously with the house of Abimelech,

**9:24** in order to bring the injustice against the seventy sons of Jerubbaal and their blood, to lay it upon Abimelech their brother who slew them, and upon the men of Shechem, because they strengthened his hands to slay the brothers.

**9:25** And the men of Shechem set men in ambush against him on the tops of the mountains, and they plundered everyone who passed by them along the way; and it was reported to King Abimelech.

**9:26** Now Galaad the son of Jobel and his brothers came and passed over into Shechem, and the men of Shechem put their trust in him.

**9:27** So they went out into the field, and gathered the grapes of their vineyards and trod them, and held festivities of praise; and they brought them into the house of their god, and ate and spoke and cursed Abimelech.

**9:28** And Gaad the son of Jobel said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is the son of Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, and is not Zebul his overseer, his servant together with the men of Hamor the father of Shechem? And why is it that we should serve him?

**9:29** And who would place this people into my hand? Then I would remove Abimelech, and I would say to him, 'Increase your army and come out!'"

**9:30** When Zebul, the ruler of the city, heard the words of Galaad the son of Jobel, he himself burned with wrath.

**9:31** And he sent messengers to Abimelech in secret, saying, "Behold, Galaad the son of Jobel and his brothers are coming to Shechem, and behold, they are besieging the city against you.

**9:32** Now therefore, arise by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in ambush in the field;

**9:33** and it shall be in the morning, as soon as the sun rises, that you shall rise early and rush upon the city; and behold, he and the people who are with him will come out against you, and you shall do to him whatever your hand finds."

**9:34** So Abimelech and all the people with him arose by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four companies.

**9:35** And Galaad the son of Jobel went out and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city; and Abimelech and the people who were with him rose up from the ambush.

**9:36** And Galaad the son of Jobel saw the people, and said to Zebul, "Behold, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains!" And Zebul said to him, "You see the shadow of the mountains as men."

**9:37** And Galaad spoke yet again and said, "Behold, people are coming down toward the sea from beside the navel of the land, and another company is coming by the way of Elon-Maonemin."

**9:38** Then Zebul said to him, "And where is your mouth, as you spoke, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Is this not the people whom you despised? Go out now, I pray, and do battle against him!"

**9:39** So Galaad went out before the men of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.

**9:40** And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled from before his face; and many fell wounded as far as the entrance of the gate.

**9:41** And Abimelech entered into Arumah; and Zebul drove out Galaad and his brothers, so that they would not dwell in Shechem.

**9:42** And it came to pass on the next day that the people went out into the field, and it was told to Abimelech.

**9:43** So he took the people and divided them into three companies, and lay in ambush in the field; and he looked, and behold, the people came out from the city, and he rose up against them and struck them down.

**9:44** And Abimelech and the commanders who were with him rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city, while the two companies rushed upon all who were in the field and struck them down.

**9:45** And Abimelech fought against the city all that day; and he captured the city and slew the people who were in it, and tore down the city and sowed it with salt.

**9:46** Now when all the men of the towers of Shechem heard of it, they came into the gathering place of Beth-Er-Berith.

**9:47** And it was told to Abimelech that all the men of the towers of Shechem were gathered together.

**9:48** Then Abimelech went up to Mount Hermon, and all the people who were with him; and Abimelech took the axes in his hand, and cut down a branch of a tree, and lifted it and laid it upon his shoulders, and said to the people who were with him, "What you have seen me do, quickly do as I have done!"

**9:49** So every man also cut down a branch, and they followed after Abimelech, and laid them upon the gathering place, and burned the gathering place over them with fire; thus all the men of the tower of Shechem also died, about a thousand men and women.

**9:50** Then Abimelech went from Beth-Er-Berith and encamped at Thebez, and captured it.

**9:51** But there was a strong tower in the midst of the city, and all the men and women of the city fled there, and shut it behind themselves, and went up to the roof of the tower.

**9:52** And Abimelech came up to the tower, and they fought against him; and Abimelech drew near to the door of the tower to burn it with fire.

**9:53** Then a certain woman cast a piece of an upper millstone upon Abimelech's head, and crushed his skull.

**9:54** And he cried out quickly to the young man who bore his armor, and said to him, "Draw my sword and put me to death, lest they say, 'A woman slew him!'" So his young man pierced him through, and he died.

**9:55** And when the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, each man departed to his own place.

**9:56** Thus God repaid the wickedness of Abimelech, which he had done to his father in slaying his seventy brothers.

**9:57** And all the wickedness of the men of Shechem God returned upon their own head; and upon them came the curse of Jotham the son of Jerubbaal.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Ephrathah" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Ophrah."
- **9:6** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Beth-Maalon" (MT "Beth-Millo") and describes the landmark as "the oak that was found at the pillar," reflecting a double rendering of the Hebrew term for the pillar.
- **9:26** — Codex Vaticanus names Abimelech's rival "Galaad the son of Jobel" throughout this narrative (spelled "Gaad" in 9:28), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Gaal the son of Ebed."
- **9:27** — "Festivities of praise" translates the Greek transliteration elloulim (from Hebrew hillulim, vintage celebrations); Codex Vaticanus also reads "ate and spoke" where the Hebrew has "ate and drank."
- **9:48** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Mount Hermon" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Mount Zalmon," and in 9:46 and 9:50 refers to the Shechemite sanctuary as "Beth-Er-Berith."
