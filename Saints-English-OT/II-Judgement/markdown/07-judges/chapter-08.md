## Text

**8:1** Now the men of Ephraim said to Gideon, "What is this thing you have done to us, in not calling us when you went to do battle against Midian?" And they contended with him vehemently.

**8:2** And he said to them, "What have I done now in comparison with you? Is not the gleaning of Ephraim better than the vintage of Abiezer?

**8:3** Into your hand the Lord has delivered the princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb; and what was I able to do like you?" Then their spirit subsided toward him when he spoke this word.

**8:4** Then Gideon came to the Jordan and crossed over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, hungry and pursuing.

**8:5** And he said to the men of Succoth, "Please give loaves of bread for food to this people who are at my feet, for they are faint; and behold, I am pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian."

**8:6** And the princes of Succoth said, "Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand? We will not give loaves of bread to your army."

**8:7** And Gideon said, "Therefore, when the Lord gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, then I will thresh your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with the habarkenein."

**8:8** Then he went up from there to Penuel and spoke to them in the same manner; and the men of Penuel answered him just as the men of Succoth had answered.

**8:9** And Gideon said to the men of Penuel, "When I return in peace, I will tear down this tower."

**8:10** Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their camp was with them, about fifteen thousand, all who were left of the entire camp of the foreigners; for those who had fallen were one hundred twenty thousand men who drew the sword.

**8:11** And Gideon went up by the way of those who dwell in tents, east of Nabai and Jegebal; and he struck the camp, while the camp felt secure.

**8:12** And Zebah and Zalmunna fled; and he pursued after them, and captured the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and threw the entire camp into panic.

**8:13** Then Gideon the son of Joash returned from the battle, from above the battle array of Hares.

**8:14** And he captured a young man from among the men of Succoth and questioned him; and he wrote down for him the names of the princes of Succoth and their elders, seventy-seven men.

**8:15** Then Gideon came to the princes of Succoth and said, "Behold, Zebah and Zalmunna, concerning whom you reproached me, saying, 'Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give loaves of bread to the men who are faint?'"

**8:16** And he took the elders of the city with the thorns of the wilderness and the barakeneim, and with them he threshed the men of the city.

**8:17** And he overthrew the tower of Penuel and slew the men of the city.

**8:18** Then he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, "Where are the men whom you slew at Tabor?" And they said, "As you are, so were they, in the likeness of the son of a king."

**8:19** And Gideon said, "They were my brothers and the sons of my mother. As the Lord lives, if you had kept them alive, I would not slay you."

**8:20** So he said to Jether his firstborn, "Arise and slay them!" But the youth did not draw his sword, for he was afraid, because he was still young.

**8:21** Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, "Arise yourself and fall upon us, for your strength is that of a man." So Gideon arose and slew Zebah and Zalmunna, and took the crescent ornaments that were on the necks of their camels.

**8:22** Then the men of Israel said to Gideon, "Lord, rule over us, both you and your son, for you have saved us from the hand of Midian."

**8:23** And Gideon said to them, "I will not rule, nor shall my son rule among you; the Lord shall rule over you."

**8:24** And Gideon said to them, "I will make a request of you: give me, each man, an earring from his plunder." For they had golden earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.

**8:25** And they said, "We will surely give them." So he spread out his cloak, and each man cast there an earring from his plunder.

**8:26** And the weight of the golden earrings that he requested was one thousand five hundred gold pieces, besides the crescent ornaments and the pendants, and the garments and purple robes that were upon the kings of Midian, and apart from the collars that were on the necks of their camels.

**8:27** And Gideon made it into an ephod and set it up in his city, Ephrathah. And all Israel played the harlot after it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and to his house.

**8:28** Thus Midian was subdued before the sons of Israel, and they did not lift up their heads anymore. And the land had rest forty years in the days of Gideon.

**8:29** Then Jerubbaal the son of Joash went and dwelt in his own house.

**8:30** And Gideon had seventy sons who came forth from his loins, for he had many wives.

**8:31** And his concubine was in Shechem, and she also bore him a son, and he called his name Abimelech.

**8:32** And Gideon the son of Joash died in his city, and was buried in the tomb of Joash his father in Ephrathah of Abiesdri.

**8:33** And it came to pass, as soon as Gideon died, that the sons of Israel turned back and played the harlot after the Baals, and made for themselves a covenant with Baal, that he should be their god.

**8:34** And the sons of Israel did not remember the Lord God, who had delivered them from the hand of all who afflicted them on every side.

**8:35** Nor did they show mercy to the house of Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, according to all the good that he had done for Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — Codex Vaticanus transliterates the Hebrew term for briers or threshing-thorns as "habarkenein" here and "barakeneim" in verse 16.
- **8:13** — Codex Vaticanus reads "from above the battle array of Hares," where the Masoretic Text has "from the ascent of Heres."
- **8:18** — Codex Vaticanus translates the Hebrew interrogative as "Where are the men?" rather than "What kind of men were they?"
- **8:26** — Codex Vaticanus gives the weight as "one thousand five hundred gold pieces," compared with "one thousand seven hundred shekels of gold" in the Masoretic Text.
- **8:33** — Codex Vaticanus renders Hebrew "Baal-berith" as "a covenant with Baal." Earlier, in verses 27 and 32, it reads "Ephrathah" (and "Abiesdri") for "Ophrah" (and "the Abiezrites"), and in verse 32 reads "in his city" rather than "in a good old age."
