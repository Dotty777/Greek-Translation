## Text

**10:1** And after Abimelech there arose to save Israel Tola the son of Puah, the son of his father's brother, a man of Issachar; and he dwelt in Shamir in Mount Ephraim.

**10:2** And he judged Israel twenty-three years, and he died and was buried in Shamir.

**10:3** And after him arose Jair of Gilead, and he judged Israel twenty-two years.

**10:4** And he had thirty-two sons who rode on thirty-two colts, and they had thirty-two cities; and they called them the Villages of Jair to this day, in the land of Gilead.

**10:5** And Jair died, and was buried in Ramnon.

**10:6** Then the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of the Lord, and served the Baalim and the Ashtaroth, and the gods of Arad, and the gods of Sidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines; and they forsook the Lord and did not serve Him.

**10:7** And the Lord was fiercely angered against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of the Philistines and into the hand of the children of Ammon.

**10:8** And they afflicted and crushed the children of Israel in that season for eighteen years—all the children of Israel who were beyond the Jordan, in the land of the Amorites in Gilead.

**10:9** Moreover, the children of Ammon crossed over the Jordan to make war against Judah and Benjamin and against Ephraim; and Israel was greatly distressed.

**10:10** And the children of Israel cried out to the Lord, saying, "We have sinned against You, because we have forsaken God and served Baalim."

**10:11** And the Lord said to the children of Israel, "Was it not from Egypt, and from the Amorites, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines,

**10:12** and the Sidonians, and Amalek, and Midian, who afflicted you—and you cried out to Me, and I saved you out of their hand?

**10:13** Yet you have forsaken Me and served other gods; therefore I will no longer save you.

**10:14** Go and cry out to the gods whom you have chosen for yourselves; let them save you in the time of your distress."

**10:15** And the children of Israel said to the Lord, "We have sinned; do to us according to all that is good in Your sight; only deliver us this day."

**10:16** So they put away the foreign gods from among them and served the Lord alone; and His soul was grieved over the misery of Israel.

**10:17** Then the children of Ammon went up and encamped in Gilead, and the children of Israel gathered together and encamped at the Watchtower.

**10:18** And the people, the rulers of Gilead, said, each man to his neighbor, "Who is the man who will begin to make war against the children of Ammon? He shall be ruler over all the inhabitants of Gilead."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Codex Vaticanus reads "the son of his father's brother" (i.e., his uncle), rendering Hebrew Dodo as a common noun rather than a personal name.
- **10:4** — Codex Vaticanus uniquely reads "thirty-two" for Jair's sons, colts, and cities (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "thirty"), and translates the place name Havvoth-jair as "the Villages of Jair."
- **10:5** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Ramnon," corresponding to "Kamon" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:6** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Arad," corresponding to "Aram" (Syria) in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:12** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Midian," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Maon" (the Maonites).
- **10:17** — Codex Vaticanus translates Mizpah as "the Watchtower" here and in 11:29 ("the Watchtower of Gilead"), while transliterating it as "Mizpah" in 11:11 and 11:34.
