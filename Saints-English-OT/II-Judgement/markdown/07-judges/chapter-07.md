## Text

**7:1** And Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, and all the people with him rose early and encamped beside the spring of Arad; and the camp of Midian was to the north of him, from Gabaathamora in the valley.

**7:2** And the Lord said to Gideon, "The people who are with you are too many for Me to deliver Midian into their hand, lest Israel boast against Me, saying, 'My hand has saved me.'

**7:3** And now, speak in the hearing of the people, saying, 'Who is fearful and timid? Let him turn back and depart from Mount Gilead.'" And twenty-two thousand of the people turned back, and ten thousand remained.

**7:4** Then the Lord said to Gideon, "The people are still too many; bring them down to the water, and I will sift them for you there. And it shall be that the one of whom I say to you, 'This one shall go with you,' he shall go with you; and everyone of whom I say to you, 'This one shall not go with you,' he shall not go with you."

**7:5** So he brought the people down to the water. And the Lord said to Gideon, "Everyone who laps from the water with his tongue, as a dog laps, you shall set by himself; and likewise everyone who bows down upon his knees to drink."

**7:6** And the number of those who lapped with their hand to their mouth was three hundred men, and all the rest of the people bowed down upon their knees to drink water.

**7:7** Then the Lord said to Gideon, "By the three hundred men who lapped I will save you and give Midian into your hand; and all the people shall depart, each man to his own place."

**7:8** So they took the provisions of the people in their hand, along with their horns. And he sent away every man of Israel, each man to his tent, and retained the three hundred men. Now the camp of Midian was below him in the valley.

**7:9** And it came to pass on that night that the Lord said to him, "Arise, go down into the camp, for I have delivered it into your hand.

**7:10** Yet if you are afraid to go down, go down, you and Purah your servant, to the camp,

**7:11** and you shall hear what they speak; and afterward your hands shall be strengthened, and you shall go down into the camp." Then he and Purah his servant went down to the outpost of the fifty who were in the camp.

**7:12** Now Midian and Amalek and all the sons of the east were lying in the valley like locusts in multitude; and their camels were without number, but were as the sand upon the shore of the sea in multitude.

**7:13** And Gideon came, and behold, a man was relating a dream to his companion. And he said, "Behold, I have dreamed a dream: and behold, a loaf of barley bread was rolling in the camp of Midian; and it came to the tent and struck it, and it fell, and it turned it upside down, and the tent fell."

**7:14** And his companion answered and said, "This is nothing else than the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel! God has delivered Midian and the whole camp into his hand."

**7:15** And it came to pass, when Gideon heard the telling of the dream and its interpretation, that he worshiped the Lord. And he returned to the camp of Israel and said, "Arise, for the Lord has delivered the camp of Midian into our hand."

**7:16** Then he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put horns into the hand of all of them, with empty pitchers and torches inside the pitchers.

**7:17** And he said to them, "Watch me and do likewise; and behold, I am going to the outskirts of the camp, and it shall be that just as I do, so you shall do.

**7:18** And I will blow the horn, and all of you with me shall blow the horns all around the whole camp, and you shall say, 'For the Lord and for Gideon!'"

**7:19** So Gideon and the hundred men who were with him came to the outskirts of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch, when they had just roused the watchmen; and they blew the horns and dashed to pieces the pitchers that were in their hands.

**7:20** Then the three companies blew the horns and broke the pitchers, and they held the torches in their left hands and the horns in their right hands for blowing; and they cried out, "A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!"

**7:21** And every man stood in his place all around the camp; and the whole camp ran, and sounded the alarm, and fled.

**7:22** And they blew the three hundred horns, and the Lord set every man's sword against his companion throughout the whole camp; and the camp fled as far as Beth-seedta Garagatha, as far as the border of Abomeoula by Tabbath.

**7:23** And the men of Israel from Naphtali, from Asher, and from all Manasseh cried out and pursued after Midian.

**7:24** Then Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim, saying, "Come down to meet Midian, and seize for yourselves the water as far as Bethera and the Jordan." So every man of Ephraim cried out, and they seized the water beforehand as far as Bethera and the Jordan.

**7:25** And they captured the princes of Midian, both Oreb and Zeeb; and they slew Oreb in Sur, and Zeeb they slew in Jakephzeph. And they pursued Midian, and they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon from beyond the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Arad" (corresponding to Hebrew Harod) and transliterates Hebrew "the hill of Moreh" as the single place name "Gabaathamora."
- **7:11** — Codex Vaticanus reads "of the fifty," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants rendered "armed men" in the Masoretic Text.
- **7:22** — Codex Vaticanus preserves the distinct place-name forms "Beth-seedta Garagatha" and "Abomeoula" (corresponding to "Beth-shittah toward Zererah" and "Abel-meholah" in the Masoretic Text); likewise in verse 24 it reads "Bethera" for "Beth-barah."
- **7:25** — Codex Vaticanus transliterates the Hebrew phrases "at the rock of Oreb" and "at the winepress of Zeeb" as the place names "Sur" and "Jakephzeph."
