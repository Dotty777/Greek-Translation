## Text

**11:1** Now Jephthah the Gileadite was exalted in might, and he was the son of a harlot, who bore Jephthah to Gilead.

**11:2** And Gilead's wife bore sons to him; and the sons of his wife grew up, and they drove out Jephthah and said to him, "You shall not inherit in the house of our father, for you are the son of a courtesan."

**11:3** Then Jephthah fled from the face of his brethren and dwelt in the land of Tob; and worthless men gathered to Jephthah and went out with him.

**11:4** And it came to pass, when the children of Ammon made war with Israel,

**11:5** that the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah from the land of Tob;

**11:6** and they said to Jephthah, "Come and be commander for us, and let us make war against the children of Ammon."

**11:7** And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "Did you not hate me, and drive me out from my father's house, and send me away from you? Why then have you come to me now when you are in need?"

**11:8** And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "For this very reason we have turned back to you now, that you may go with us and make war against the children of Ammon, and you shall be ruler for us, over all who dwell in Gilead."

**11:9** And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "If you bring me back to make war against the children of Ammon, and the Lord delivers them before me, shall I indeed be ruler over you?"

**11:10** And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "Let the Lord be a hearer between us if we do not act according to your word."

**11:11** Then Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people set him over themselves as head and as commander; and Jephthah spoke all his words before the Lord in Mizpah.

**11:12** Now Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the children of Ammon, saying, "What is there between me and you, that you have come to me to make war in my land?"

**11:13** And the king of the children of Ammon said to the messengers of Jephthah, "Because Israel took away my land when he came up out of Egypt, from the Arnon even to the Jabbok and to the Jordan; now therefore restore them in peace, and I will depart."

**11:14** And Jephthah again sent messengers to the king of the children of Ammon,

**11:15** and said to him, "Thus says Jephthah: Israel did not take away the land of Moab, nor the land of the children of Ammon.

**11:16** For when they came up out of Egypt, Israel walked in the wilderness as far as the Sea of Suph and came to Kadesh.

**11:17** Then Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, 'Let me pass, I pray, through your land'; but the king of Edom did not listen. And he sent also to the king of Moab, and he did not consent. So Israel remained in Kadesh.

**11:18** And he journeyed in the wilderness, and went around the land of Edom and the land of Moab, and came on the east of the land of Moab, and they encamped on the other side of the Arnon; and he did not enter within the borders of Moab, for the Arnon was the border of Moab.

**11:19** Then Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, king of Heshbon; and Israel said to him, 'Let us pass, we pray, through your land to our place.'

**11:20** But Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his territory; and Sihon gathered all his people together, and they encamped at Jahaz, and he fought against Israel.

**11:21** And the Lord God of Israel delivered Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and he struck him down; and Israel possessed all the land of the Amorites who inhabited that land,

**11:22** from the Arnon even to the Jabbok, and from the wilderness to the Jordan.

**11:23** And now the Lord God of Israel has driven out the Amorites from before His people Israel; and shall you possess them?

**11:24** Will you not possess whatever Hamos your god gives you to possess? So all whom the Lord our God has driven out from before us, them we will possess.

**11:25** And now, are you indeed any better than Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever contend with Israel, or did he ever wage war against him,

**11:26** while he dwelt in Heshbon and in its borders, and in the land of Aroer and in its borders, and in all the cities along the Jordan, for three hundred years? Why then did you not rescue them in that time?

**11:27** And now, I myself have not sinned against you, yet you do evil against me in making war with me. May the Lord, the Judge, judge this day between the children of Israel and the children of Ammon."

**11:28** However, the king of the children of Ammon did not listen to the words of Jephthah which he sent to him.

**11:29** Then the Spirit of the Lord came upon Jephthah, and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh, and passed through the Watchtower of Gilead to the other side of the children of Ammon.

**11:30** And Jephthah made a vow to the Lord, and said, "If You will indeed deliver the children of Ammon into my hand,

**11:31** then it shall be that whoever comes forth from the door of my house to meet me, when I return in peace from the children of Ammon, shall belong to the Lord; I will offer him up as a burnt offering."

**11:32** So Jephthah passed over to the children of Ammon to make war against them, and the Lord delivered them into his hand.

**11:33** And he struck them from Aroer until one comes as far as Arnon—twenty cities in number—and as far as Abel-keramim, with a very great slaughter. Thus the children of Ammon were subdued before the children of Israel.

**11:34** When Jephthah came to Mizpah to his house, behold, his daughter was coming out to meet him with timbrels and dances; and she was an only child; he had no other son or daughter.

**11:35** And it came to pass, when he saw her, that he tore his garments and said, "Alas, alas, my daughter! You have utterly troubled me, and you are in my trouble; for I myself have opened my mouth against you to the Lord, and I cannot turn back."

**11:36** And she said to him, "Father, you have opened your mouth to the Lord; do to me according to what has gone out of your mouth, since the Lord has executed vengeance for you upon your enemies, upon the children of Ammon."

**11:37** Then she said to her father, "Let my father do this thing: leave me alone for two months, that I may go and go down upon the mountains, and weep over my virginity, I and my companions."

**11:38** And he said, "Go." And he sent her away for two months; and she went, she and her companions, and wept over her virginity upon the mountains.

**11:39** And it came to pass at the end of the two months that she returned to her father, and he carried out upon her his vow which he had vowed; and she did not know a man. And it became an ordinance in Israel:

**11:40** from year to year the daughters of Israel went to lament the daughter of Jephthah of Gilead four days in the year.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Codex Vaticanus reads hetairas ("a courtesan"), differing by one letter from heteras ("another woman"), which corresponds to the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **11:16** — "the Sea of Suph" reflects the Greek transliteration thalasses Seiph of Hebrew Yam Suph ("Sea of Reeds," traditionally rendered "the Red Sea").
- **11:24** — Codex Vaticanus spells the Moabite deity's name as "Hamos," corresponding to "Chemosh" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **11:26** — Codex Vaticanus reads "along the Jordan," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "along the Arnon."
- **11:33** — Codex Vaticanus reads "as far as Arnon," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "as far as Minnith."
- **11:35** — Codex Vaticanus explicitly includes "against you" in Jephthah's lament ("opened my mouth against you to the Lord").
