## Text

**4:1** And the children of Israel again did evil before the Lord; and Ehud had died.

**4:2** And the Lord sold the children of Israel into the hand of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; and the commander of his army was Sisera, and he dwelt in Harosheth of the Nations.

**4:3** And the children of Israel cried out to the Lord, for he had nine hundred chariots of iron, and he oppressed Israel with harsh force for twenty years.

**4:4** Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, was judging Israel at that time.

**4:5** And she sat beneath the palm tree of Deborah between Bama and Bethel in Mount Ephraim; and the children of Israel went up to her for judgment.

**4:6** And Deborah sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali, and said to him, "Has not the Lord, the God of Israel, commanded you: 'You shall go to Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men of the sons of Naphtali and of the sons of Zebulun,

**4:7** and I will bring to you at the brook Kishon Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, and his chariots and his multitude, and I will deliver him into your hands'?"

**4:8** And Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go; and if you do not go, I will not go, for I do not know the day on which the Lord prospers the angel with me."

**4:9** And she said, "I will surely go with you; nevertheless, know that the honor shall not be yours on the way that you are going, for into the hand of a woman the Lord will sell Sisera." And Deborah arose and went with Barak from Kedesh.

**4:10** And Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali from Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up at his feet; and Deborah went up with him.

**4:11** Now Heber the Kenite had separated himself from Kain, from the sons of Hobab, the father-in-law of Moses, and pitched his tent as far as the oak of the covetous, which is next to Kedesh.

**4:12** And it was reported to Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.

**4:13** And Sisera summoned all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth of the Nations to the brook Kishon.

**4:14** And Deborah said to Barak, "Arise, for this is the day in which the Lord has delivered Sisera into your hand; for the Lord shall go out before you." So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, with ten thousand men behind him.

**4:15** And the Lord threw Sisera and all his chariots and all his army into panic by the edge of the sword before Barak; and Sisera got down from his chariot and fled on foot.

**4:16** And Barak pursued after the chariots and after the army as far as Harosheth of the Nations; and the entire army of Sisera fell by the edge of the sword—not even one was left.

**4:17** Now Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the companion, the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin, king of Hazor, and the house of Heber the Kenite.

**4:18** And Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not fear." And he turned aside to her into the tent; and she covered him with a blanket.

**4:19** And Sisera said to her, "Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty." So she opened the skin of milk and gave him a drink, and covered him.

**4:20** And Sisera said to her, "Please stand at the door of the tent, and it shall be that if any man comes to you and inquires of you and says, 'Is there a man here?' then you shall say, 'There is not.'"

**4:21** Then Jael, the wife of Heber, took the tent peg and put the hammer in her hand, and went in to him secretly and drove the peg into his temple, and it went right through into the ground; and he, stunned, fainted away and died.

**4:22** And behold, Barak was pursuing Sisera; and Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, and I will show you the man whom you seek." And he went in to her, and behold, Sisera lay prostrate, dead, with the peg in his temple.

**4:23** So God routed Jabin, king of Canaan, on that day before the children of Israel.

**4:24** And the hand of the children of Israel went on growing heavier and harsher against Jabin, king of Canaan, until they utterly destroyed Jabin, king of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Codex Vaticanus reads 'Bama' rather than the Masoretic Text's 'Ramah.'
- **4:8** — The closing clause ('for I do not know the day on which the Lord prospers the angel with me') is a distinctive Septuagint addition not present in the Masoretic Text.
- **4:11** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Zaanannim by its root meaning as 'the oak of the covetous' (dryos pleonektounton).
- **4:17** — Codex Vaticanus includes 'the companion' (hetairou) alongside 'Heber,' reflecting a double rendering of the Hebrew name Cheber both as a proper name and as a common noun.
- **4:21** — Codex Vaticanus reads exestos eskotothe ('stunned, fainted away,' literally 'was darkened'), describing Sisera's collapse rather than his prior sleep and weariness as in the Masoretic Text.
