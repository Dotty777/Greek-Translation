## Text

**21:1** Now the sons of Israel had sworn in Mizpah, saying, "No man from among us shall give his daughter to Benjamin for a wife."

**21:2** And the people came to Bethel, and sat there until evening before God; and they lifted up their voice and wept with great weeping.

**21:3** And they said, "Why, O Lord God of Israel, has this come to pass, that one tribe should be missing from Israel today?"

**21:4** And it came to pass on the next day that the people rose early, and built an altar there, and offered up burnt offerings and complete offerings.

**21:5** And the sons of Israel said, "Who from all the tribes of Israel did not come up in the assembly to the Lord?" For the oath was great against those who had not come up to the Lord to Mizpah, saying, "He shall surely be put to death."

**21:6** And the sons of Israel were moved with compassion toward Benjamin their brother, and said, "One tribe has been cut off today from Israel.

**21:7** What shall we do for wives for them, those who remain, who are left? And we have sworn by the Lord not to give them any of our daughters for wives."

**21:8** And they said, "Which one from the tribes of Israel did not come up to the Lord to Mizpah?" And behold, no man had come to the camp from Jabesh Gilead, to the assembly.

**21:9** And the people were mustered, and there was not a man there from the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead.

**21:10** And the congregation sent twelve thousand men there from the sons of valor, and commanded them, saying, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead with the edge of the sword.

**21:11** And this you shall do: every male and every woman who has known lying with a male you shall devote to destruction, but the virgins you shall keep alive." And they did so.

**21:12** And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead four hundred young virgins, who had not known a man by lying with a male; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

**21:13** And the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the sons of Benjamin at the rock of Rimmon, and called them to peace.

**21:14** And Benjamin returned to the sons of Israel at that time, and the sons of Israel gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the daughters of Jabesh Gilead; and it pleased them so.

**21:15** And the people were moved with compassion for Benjamin, because the Lord had made a breach among the tribes of Israel.

**21:16** And the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since women have been destroyed from Benjamin?"

**21:17** And they said, "There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, and a tribe shall not be blotted out from Israel.

**21:18** For we shall not be able to give them wives from our daughters, because we have sworn among the sons of Israel, saying, 'Cursed is the one who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

**21:19** And they said, "Behold now, there is a feast of the Lord in Shiloh from year to year, which is on the north of Bethel, toward the rising of the sun on the road that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah."

**21:20** And they commanded the sons of Benjamin, saying, "Go, lie in ambush in the vineyards,

**21:21** and watch; and behold, if the daughters of the inhabitants of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then you shall come out from the vineyards and seize for yourselves, every man a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**21:22** And it shall come to pass, when their fathers or their brothers come to contend with you, that we will say to them, 'Show mercy to us concerning them, because we did not take each man his wife in the battle, for you yourselves did not give them to them; as a lot, incur guilt.'"

**21:23** And the sons of Benjamin did so, and took wives according to their number from the dancers whom they had seized; and they went and returned to their inheritance, and built the cities and dwelt in them.

**21:24** And the sons of Israel departed from there at that time, every man to his tribe and to his kindred, and they went out from there, every man to his inheritance.

**21:25** Now in those days there was no king in Israel; every man did what was upright in his own sight.

## Translator Notes

- **21:4** — Greek teleias literally means "complete offerings," Codex Vaticanus's distinctive rendering of the Hebrew peace or fellowship offerings (reflecting the Hebrew root meaning "whole" or "complete").
- **21:11** — Codex Vaticanus includes the explicit directive "but the virgins you shall keep alive. And they did so," while omitting the phrase "with the women and the little ones" in 21:10, differing from the Masoretic Text.
- **21:14** — Codex Vaticanus reads "and it pleased them so" (kai eresen autois houtos), whereas the Masoretic Text reports that they did not find enough wives for them.
- **21:22** — Codex Vaticanus reads "with you" (pros hymas, vs. Masoretic "to us") and ends with the difficult reading "as a lot, incur guilt" (hos kleros plemmelesate), likely an inner-Greek corruption of hos kairos plemmelesete ("at this time you would be guilty").
- **21:25** — Codex Vaticanus reads "in his own sight" (enopion autou, literally "before him") rather than the Masoretic idiom "in his own eyes."
