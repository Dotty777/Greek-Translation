## Text

**20:1** And all the sons of Israel went out, and the congregation was assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba and the land of Gilead, to the Lord at Mizpah.

**20:2** And all the tribes of Israel stood before the Lord in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand men on foot who drew the sword.

**20:3** And the sons of Benjamin heard that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. And the sons of Israel came and said, "Speak, where did this wickedness happen?"

**20:4** And the Levite, the husband of the woman who was murdered, answered and said, "I and my concubine came to Gibeah of Benjamin to lodge for the night.

**20:5** And the men of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house against me by night. They intended to murder me, and they violated my concubine, and she died.

**20:6** And I took hold of my concubine and cut her into pieces, and sent her throughout all the territory of the inheritance of the sons of Israel, because they have committed depravity and outrage in Israel.

**20:7** Behold, all of you, sons of Israel, give for yourselves a word and counsel there."

**20:8** And all the people rose up as one man, saying, "We will not depart, each man to his tent, nor will we return, each man to his house.

**20:9** And now this is the matter that shall be done to Gibeah: we will go up against it by lot.

**20:10** However, we will take ten men out of a hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to gather provisions to bring them to Gibeah of Benjamin, to do to it according to all the outrage that it has committed in Israel."

**20:11** And every man of Israel was gathered together against the city as one man.

**20:12** And the tribes of Israel sent men throughout all the tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this wickedness that has taken place among you?

**20:13** And now hand over the men, the sons of lawless men, who are in Gibeah, and we will put them to death and purge away wickedness from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin were not willing to listen to the voice of their brethren, the sons of Israel.

**20:14** And the sons of Benjamin gathered together from their cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the sons of Israel.

**20:15** And the sons of Benjamin were mustered on that day from the cities, twenty-three thousand men who drew the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who were mustered as seven hundred men,

**20:16** chosen out of all the people, ambidextrous; all these were slingers of stones at a hair and did not miss.

**20:17** And the men of Israel, besides Benjamin, were mustered: four hundred thousand men who drew the sword; all these were men of battle.

**20:18** And they arose and went up to Bethel, and inquired of God. And the sons of Israel said, "Who shall go up for us first to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" And the Lord said, "Judah shall go up first as leader."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel arose in the morning and encamped against Gibeah.

**20:20** And every man of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and they joined battle with them at Gibeah.

**20:21** And the sons of Benjamin came out from Gibeah, and cut down twenty-two thousand men in Israel to the ground on that day.

**20:22** And the men of Israel strengthened themselves, and again joined battle in the place where they had joined battle on the first day.

**20:23** And the sons of Israel went up and wept before the Lord until evening, and inquired of the Lord, saying, "Shall we again draw near to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brethren?" And the Lord said, "Go up against them."

**20:24** And the sons of Israel drew near against the sons of Benjamin on the second day.

**20:25** And the sons of Benjamin went out to meet them from Gibeah on the second day, and cut down eighteen thousand more men of the sons of Israel to the ground.

**20:26** And all the sons of Israel and all the people went up and came to Bethel, and they wept and sat there before the Lord, and fasted on that day until evening, and offered up burnt offerings and complete offerings before the Lord,

**20:27** for there was the ark of the covenant of the Lord God,

**20:28** and Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, stood before it in those days. And the sons of Israel inquired of the Lord, saying, "Shall we yet again go out to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brethren, or shall we cease?" And the Lord said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will deliver them into your hands."

**20:29** And the sons of Israel set ambushes around Gibeah.

**20:30** And the sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day, and joined battle against Gibeah as at the former times.

**20:31** And the sons of Benjamin went out to meet the people and were drawn away from the city, and began to strike down and slay from the people, as at the former times, on the highways—one of which goes up to Bethel and one to Gibeah in the field—about thirty men in Israel.

**20:32** And the sons of Benjamin said, "They are falling before us as at the first." And the sons of Israel said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways." And they did so.

**20:33** And every man rose up from his place, and they joined battle at Baal Tamar; and the ambush of Israel advanced from its place, from Maraagabe.

**20:34** And ten thousand chosen men out of all Israel came over against Gibeah, and the battle was fierce; and they did not know that calamity was coming upon them.

**20:35** And the Lord struck Benjamin before the sons of Israel; and the sons of Israel destroyed twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin on that day. All these drew the sword.

**20:36** And the sons of Benjamin saw that they were struck down; and the men of Israel gave ground to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

**20:37** And as they withdrew, the ambush moved; and they pushed forward against Gibeah, and the ambush poured forth and struck the city with the edge of the sword.

**20:38** And the sons of Israel had a sign with the ambush of the battle, that they should raise up a signal of smoke from the city.

**20:39** And the sons of Israel saw that the ambush had already seized Gibeah, and they stood firm in the battle. And Benjamin began to strike down and slay among the men of Israel about thirty men, for they said, "Again they are surely falling before us, as in the first battle."

**20:40** And the signal rose higher above the city like a pillar of smoke; and Benjamin looked behind him, and behold, the complete destruction of the city ascended to heaven.

**20:41** And the men of Israel turned back; and the men of Benjamin hastened, for they saw that calamity had come upon them.

**20:42** And they turned before the sons of Israel toward the way of the wilderness and fled; and the battle overtook them, and those from the cities were destroying them in their midst.

**20:43** And they cut Benjamin to pieces, and pursued him from Noua hard on his heels as far as opposite Gibeah toward the rising of the sun.

**20:44** And eighteen thousand men fell from Benjamin, all of these men of valor.

**20:45** And the remainder turned and fled into the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and the sons of Israel gleaned five thousand men from among them; and the sons of Israel went down after them as far as Gedan, and struck down two thousand men of them.

**20:46** And all who fell of Benjamin on that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword, all of these men of valor.

**20:47** And the remainder, six hundred men, turned and fled into the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and dwelt at the rock of Rimmon four months.

**20:48** And the sons of Israel turned back against the sons of Benjamin, and struck them with the edge of the sword, from the city of Methla even to the livestock, and even to everything that was found in all the cities; and the cities that were found they burned with fire.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — The Greek text uses the transliteration "zema" (from Hebrew "zimmah," depravity or lewdness) paired with "apoptoma" (literally "downfall" or "lapse," here rendered "outrage").
- **20:15** — Codex Vaticanus reads "twenty-three thousand" for the mustered Benjaminites, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "twenty-six thousand."
- **20:33** — "Maraagabe" is a Septuagint proper-name transliteration of the Hebrew phrase meaning "open ground of Gibeah" or "meadow of Gibeah."
- **20:43** — "Noua" is a Septuagint place-name rendering of the Hebrew term for "resting place" (or "Nohah").
- **20:48** — "Methla" (like "Gedan" in 20:45) preserves a distinctive Septuagint proper-name reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "an entire/inhabited city" (and "Gidom" in 20:45).
