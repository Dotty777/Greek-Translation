## Text

**4:1** Now Boaz went up to the gate and sat down there; and behold, the kinsman-redeemer of whom Boaz had spoken was passing by. And Boaz said to him, "Turn aside and sit down here, O hidden one." So he turned aside and sat down.

**4:2** And Boaz took ten men from the elders of the city and said, "Sit down here." So they sat down.

**4:3** And Boaz said to the kinsman-redeemer, "The portion of the field that belongs to our brother Abimelech, which has been given to Naomi, who has returned from the country of Moab—

**4:4** I also said, 'I will uncover your ear, saying: Acquire it in the presence of those sitting here and in the presence of the elders of my people. If you will redeem it, redeem it; but if you will not redeem it, declare it to me, and I shall know, for there is no one besides you to redeem it, and I am after you.'" And he said, "I am he; I will redeem it."

**4:5** Then Boaz said, "On the day you acquire the field from the hand of Naomi and from Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of the dead man, you must also acquire her, so as to raise up the name of the dead man upon his inheritance."

**4:6** And the kinsman-redeemer said, "I will not be able to redeem it for myself, lest I ruin my own inheritance. Redeem for yourself my right of redemption, for I will not be able to redeem it."

**4:7** Now this was the ordinance in former times in Israel concerning the right of redemption and concerning exchange, to confirm every matter: a man would take off his sandal and give it to his neighbor who was redeeming his right of redemption; and this was a testimony in Israel.

**4:8** So the kinsman-redeemer said to Boaz, "Acquire for yourself my right of redemption." And he took off his sandal and gave it to him.

**4:9** And Boaz said to the elders and to all the people, "You are witnesses this day that I have acquired all that belongs to Abimelech, and all that belongs to Chilion and to Mahlon, from the hand of Naomi;

**4:10** moreover, Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of Mahlon, I have acquired for myself as a wife, to raise up the name of the dead man upon his inheritance, and the name of the dead man shall not be cut off from among his brethren and from the tribe of his people. You are witnesses this day."

**4:11** And all the people who were at the gate said, "We are witnesses." And the elders said, "May the Lord make your wife, who is entering into your house, like Rachel and like Leah, who both built the house of Israel and did valiantly in Ephrathah; and there shall be a name in Bethlehem;

**4:12** and may your house be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, from the seed that the Lord shall give you by this young woman."

**4:13** And Boaz took Ruth, and the Lord gave her conception, and she bore a son.

**4:14** And the women said to Naomi, "Blessed be the Lord, who has not taken away from you a kinsman-redeemer this day, so as to proclaim your name in Israel;

**4:15** and he shall be to you one who restores the soul and sustains your gray hair; for your daughter-in-law, who has loved you, has borne him—she who is better to you than seven sons."

**4:16** And Naomi took the child and laid him in her bosom, and she became a nurse to him.

**4:17** And the neighbor women gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi." And they called his name Obed. He is the father of Jesse, the father of David.

**4:18** Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,

**4:19** and Hezron fathered Arran, and Arran fathered Amminadab,

**4:20** and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon,

**4:21** and Salmon fathered Boaz, and Boaz fathered Obed,

**4:22** and Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Greek vocative κρύφιε ("hidden one") renders the Hebrew idiom for an unnamed person literally as one whose name is concealed.
- **4:3** — The Septuagint reads "Abimelech" (also in v. 9) rather than Hebrew "Elimelech," and reads the passive "which has been given to Naomi" rather than "is selling."
- **4:10** — The Septuagint reads "from the tribe of his people" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from the gate of his place"; in v. 9 the Greek also reverses the order of the brothers to "Chilion and Mahlon."
- **4:14** — The Septuagint reads "your name" (referring to Naomi) rather than Hebrew "his name," and uses the active form καλέσαι ("to proclaim").
- **4:19** — The Septuagint genealogy reads "Arran" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Ram."
