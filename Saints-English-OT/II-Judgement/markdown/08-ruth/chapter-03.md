## Text

**3:1** And she dwelt with her mother-in-law. Then Naomi her mother-in-law said to her, "Daughter, shall I not seek rest for you, that it may be well with you?

**3:2** And now, is not Boaz our acquaintance, with whose maidens you were? Behold, he is winnowing the threshing floor of barley this night.

**3:3** Therefore wash yourself and anoint yourself, and put your apparel upon you, and go up to the threshing floor; do not make yourself known to the man until he has finished drinking and eating.

**3:4** And it shall be, when he lies down, that you shall note the place where he lies; and you shall go in and uncover the place at his feet, and lie down; and he will tell you what you shall do."

**3:5** And Ruth said to her, "All that you say, I will do."

**3:6** So she went down to the threshing floor and did according to all that her mother-in-law had commanded her.

**3:7** And Boaz ate, and his heart was glad, and he went to lie down at the side of the heap of grain; and she came secretly and uncovered the place at his feet.

**3:8** And it came to pass at midnight that the man was startled and troubled; and behold, a woman was lying at his feet.

**3:9** And he said, "Who are you?" And she said, "I am Ruth, your handmaid. Spread the corner of your garment over your handmaid, for you are a kinsman-redeemer."

**3:10** Then Boaz said, "Blessed are you of the Lord God, daughter, for you have made your latter kindness better than the first, in not going after the young men, whether poor or rich.

**3:11** And now, daughter, do not fear; all that you say I will do for you, for every tribe of my people knows that you are a woman of valor.

**3:12** Now it is indeed true that I am a kinsman-redeemer; yet there is a kinsman-redeemer nearer than I.

**3:13** Lodge for the night, and it shall be in the morning that if he will redeem you as kinsman, good, let him redeem you; but if he does not wish to redeem you as kinsman, then I will redeem you as kinsman, as the Lord lives—You are the Lord! Lie down until morning."

**3:14** So she lay at his feet until morning; and she rose before a man could recognize his neighbor. And Boaz said, "Let it not be known that a woman came to the threshing floor."

**3:15** And he said to her, "Bring the mantle that is upon you." And she held it, and he measured out six measures of barley and laid it upon her; and he went into the city.

**3:16** And Ruth came to her mother-in-law; and she said to her, "Daughter." And she told her all that the man had done for her.

**3:17** And she said to her, "These six measures of barley he gave to me, for he said to me, 'Do not go empty to your mother-in-law.'"

**3:18** And she said, "Sit still, daughter, until you know how the matter will not fail; for the man will surely not rest until he completes the matter today."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Following the Septuagint verse division, the sentence "And she dwelt with her mother-in-law" opens 3:1 rather than closing 2:23.
- **3:13** — The Greek text preserves the striking exclamation "as the Lord lives—You are the Lord!" (ze Kyrios, sy ei Kyrios) in Boaz's oath.
- **3:16** — The Greek gives Naomi's greeting simply as "Daughter," without the Hebrew question "Who are you, my daughter?"
- **3:18** — The Greek includes a negation ("how the matter will not fail," literally "will not fall"), expressing confidence that the matter will not come to nothing.
