## Text

**2:1** Now Naomi had an acquaintance of her husband, a man mighty in strength, of the kindred of Abimelech, and his name was Boaz.

**2:2** And Ruth the Moabitess said to Naomi, "Let me go now to the field, and I will gather among the heads of grain after him in whose eyes I may find favor." And she said to her, "Go, daughter."

**2:3** So she went and gleaned in the field behind the reapers; and she happened by chance upon the portion of the field belonging to Boaz, who was of the kindred of Abimelech.

**2:4** And behold, Boaz came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, "The Lord be with you." And they said to him, "May the Lord bless you."

**2:5** Then Boaz said to his servant who was set over the reapers, "Whose young woman is this?"

**2:6** And the servant who was set over the reapers answered and said, "She is the Moabite maiden who returned with Naomi from the countryside of Moab.

**2:7** And she said, 'Let me glean now and gather among the sheaves behind the reapers.' So she came and has continued from morning even until evening; she has not rested in the field even a little."

**2:8** Then Boaz said to Ruth, "Have you not heard, daughter? Do not go to glean in another field, nor shall you depart from here, but stay close here with my maidens.

**2:9** Let your eyes be on the field where they are reaping, and go after them. Behold, I have commanded the young men not to touch you; and whenever you are thirsty, go to the vessels and drink from where the young men draw water."

**2:10** Then she fell on her face and bowed down to the ground, and said to him, "Why is it that I have found favor in your eyes, that you should take notice of me, seeing that I am a foreigner?"

**2:11** And Boaz answered and said to her, "It has been fully reported to me all that you have done for your mother-in-law since the death of your husband, and how you forsook your father and your mother and the land of your birth, and came to a people whom you did not know yesterday or the day before.

**2:12** May the Lord repay your work, and may your reward be full from the Lord, the God of Israel, to whom you have come to trust beneath His wings."

**2:13** And she said, "May I find favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me, and because you have spoken to the heart of your handmaid; and behold, I shall be as one of your maidservants."

**2:14** And Boaz said to her, "It is already the hour to eat; come over here and eat of the bread, and dip your morsel in the vinegar." So Ruth sat beside the reapers, and Boaz heaped up barley meal for her; and she ate and was satisfied, and had some left over.

**2:15** And she rose up to glean. And Boaz commanded his young men, saying, "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not put her to shame.

**2:16** Indeed, carry some for her, and also freely cast aside for her from the heaped-up bundles; and she shall eat and glean, and you shall not rebuke her."

**2:17** So she gleaned in the field until evening, and beat out what she had gleaned; and it came to about an ephah of barley.

**2:18** And she took it up and went into the city; and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned. Then Ruth brought out and gave to her what she had left over after she was satisfied.

**2:19** And her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today, and where did you work? Blessed be the one who took notice of you." And Ruth reported to her mother-in-law where she had worked, and said, "The name of the man with whom I worked today is Boaz."

**2:20** And Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "Blessed is he of the Lord, for He has not forsaken His mercy toward the living and toward the dead." And Naomi said to her, "The man is closely related to us; he is one of our kinsman-redeemers."

**2:21** And Ruth said to her mother-in-law, "He also said to me, 'Keep close to my young men until they have finished all the harvest that belongs to me.'"

**2:22** And Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, "It is good, daughter, that you went with his maidens, and they will not confront you in another field."

**2:23** So Ruth kept close to the maidens of Boaz to glean until she finished the harvest of the barley and of the wheat.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — The Greek reads "from morning even until evening; she has not rested in the field even a little," resolving a difficult clause in the Hebrew text.
- **2:13** — Unlike the Hebrew ("though I am not like one of your maidservants"), the Greek lacks a negation and reads positively, "and behold, I shall be as one of your maidservants."
- **2:16** — The Greek preserves a fuller command from Boaz, adding "carry some for her" and "she shall eat and glean."
