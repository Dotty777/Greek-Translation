## Text

**10:1** I am weary in my soul. Groaning, I will let loose my words against Him; I will speak, oppressed by the bitterness of my soul,

**10:2** and I will say to the Lord, "Do not teach me to be ungodly; and why have You judged me thus?

**10:3** Is it good to You if I do wrong, that I should renounce the works of Your hands, while You have given heed to the counsel of the ungodly?

**10:4** Do You observe as a mortal sees, or will You look as a man sees?

**10:5** Is Your life human, or are Your years those of a man,

**10:6** that You have searched out my iniquity and traced out my sins?

**10:7** For You know that I have not acted ungodly; yet who is there who delivers out of Your hands?

**10:8** Your hands formed me and made me; afterward, turning about, You struck me.

**10:9** Remember that You formed me as clay, and to earth You turn me back again.

**10:10** Did You not milk me like milk, and curdle me like cheese?

**10:11** With skin and flesh You clothed me, and with bones and sinews You wove me together.

**10:12** Life and mercy You placed with me, and Your visitation has guarded my spirit.

**10:13** Having these things within Yourself, I know that You can do all things, and nothing is impossible for You.

**10:14** For if I sin, You watch closely over me, and from iniquity You have not made me guiltless.

**10:15** For if I act ungodly, woe to me! And if I am righteous, I cannot lift up my head, for I am full of dishonor.

**10:16** For I am hunted like a lion for slaughter; for turning again, You dreadfully destroy me,

**10:17** renewing my examination against me; You have dealt with me in great wrath, and You have brought trials upon me.

**10:18** Why then did You bring me forth from the womb, and why did I not die, that no eye might see me,

**10:19** and that I might become as one who does not exist? For why was I not taken away from the womb to the tomb?

**10:20** Is not the span of my time brief? Leave me alone to rest a little,

**10:21** before I go to the place from which I shall not return, to a land dark and gloomy,

**10:22** to a land of everlasting darkness, where there is no light, nor to behold the life of mortals."

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek reads "Do not teach me to be ungodly" (me me asebein didaske), differing from the Hebrew "Do not condemn me."
- **10:3** — In the Greek text, Job is the subject of the first two verbs ("if I do wrong, that I should renounce the works of Your hands"), whereas the Hebrew makes God the subject.
- **10:16** — The Greek uses the passive "I am hunted like a lion for slaughter," portraying Job as the prey rather than comparing God to a hunting lion as in the Hebrew.
