## Text

**12:1** Then Job answered and said:

**12:2** "So then, you are the people; surely wisdom shall die with you.

**12:3** Indeed, I also have a heart like yours.

**12:4** For a righteous and blameless man has become a mockery;

**12:5** for at an appointed time he had been prepared to fall by others, and his houses to be plundered by lawless men.

**12:6** Nevertheless, let no one who is wicked trust that he shall be guiltless—all who provoke the Lord to anger—as though there shall not also be an examination of them.

**12:7** Now ask the four-footed beasts, if they will tell you, and the birds of heaven, if they will declare it to you;

**12:8** speak to the earth, if it will explain to you, and the fish of the sea shall recount it to you.

**12:9** Who then has not known in all these things that the hand of the Lord has made these things?

**12:10** Is not the soul of all living things in His hand, and the spirit of every man?

**12:11** For the mind discerns words, and the throat tastes food.

**12:12** In much time is wisdom, and in long life is knowledge.

**12:13** With Him are wisdom and power; to Him belong counsel and understanding.

**12:14** If He tears down, who shall build up? If He shuts up against men, who shall open?

**12:15** If He withholds the water, He will dry up the earth; and if He sends it forth, He destroys it, overturning it.

**12:16** With Him are might and strength; to Him belong knowledge and understanding.

**12:17** He leads away counselors as captives, and drives the judges of the earth out of their senses.

**12:18** He seats kings upon thrones, and binds their loins with a belt.

**12:19** He sends away priests as captives, and overthrows the mighty men of the earth.

**12:20** He changes the lips of the faithful, and knows the understanding of the elders.

**12:21** He pours out dishonor upon rulers, and heals the lowly.

**12:22** He uncovers deep things out of darkness, and brings the shadow of death out into light.

**12:23** He lays nations low and guides them.

**12:24** He changes the hearts of the rulers of the earth, and causes them to wander in a way that they did not know;

**12:25** may they grope in darkness and not in light, and may they stagger like a drunken man."

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Greek preserves a briefer form of this verse, omitting the parallel clauses found in the Hebrew regarding not being inferior to the friends.
- **12:11** — The Greek reads "the mind" (nous) discerns words rather than "the ear" of the Hebrew text.
- **12:18** — The Greek reads "He seats kings upon thrones" rather than "He loosens the bond of kings" of the Hebrew text.
- **12:21** — The Greek concludes "and heals the lowly" (tapeinous de iasato), differing from the Hebrew "and loosens the belt of the strong."
