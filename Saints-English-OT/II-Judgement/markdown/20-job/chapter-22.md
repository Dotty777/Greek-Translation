## Text

**22:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

**22:2** "Is it not the Lord who teaches understanding and knowledge?

**22:3** For what does it matter to the Lord if you were blameless in your works? Or is it any gain that you put away your way?

**22:4** Or taking account of you, will He reprove you, and enter with you into judgment?

**22:5** Is not your wickedness great, and are not your sins innumerable?

**22:6** For you took pledges from your brethren for nothing, and you stripped away the clothing of the naked;

**22:7** neither did you give the thirsty water to drink, but you deprived the hungry of a morsel;

**22:8** and you showed partiality to certain persons, and you settled those upon the earth;

**22:9** and you sent widows away empty, and you afflicted orphans.

**22:10** Therefore snares have surrounded you, and a fierce war has alarmed you.

**22:11** The light has turned to darkness for you, and when you lay down to sleep, water covered you.

**22:12** Does not He who dwells in the high places look down, and has He not humbled those who are carried along in arrogance?

**22:13** And you said, 'What does the Mighty One know? Or does He judge through the thick darkness?

**22:14** A cloud is His hiding place, and He will not be seen, and He walks along the circuit of heaven.'

**22:15** Will you keep the ancient path which righteous men have trodden,

**22:16** who were seized before their time? Their foundations are an overflowing river;

**22:17** who say, 'What will the Lord do to us? Or what will the Almighty bring upon us?'

**22:18** Yet it was He who filled their houses with good things, and the counsel of the ungodly is far from Him.

**22:19** The righteous saw it and laughed, and the blameless one mocked;

**22:20** truly their substance has been wiped out, and fire shall devour their remnant.

**22:21** Be steadfast now, if you will endure; then your fruit shall be among good things.

**22:22** Bring forth declaration from His mouth, and take up His words in your heart.

**22:23** And if you turn back and humble yourself before the Lord, you have put what is unjust far from your dwelling;

**22:24** you shall be set upon a mound on a rock, and as a rock of the torrent of Sophir.

**22:25** Therefore the Almighty shall be your helper against enemies, and He shall restore you pure as silver refined by fire.

**22:26** Then you shall have boldness before the Lord, looking up cheerfully to heaven;

**22:27** and when you pray to Him, He will hear you, and He will grant you to pay your vows;

**22:28** and He will restore to you a dwelling of righteousness, and upon your ways there shall be radiance.

**22:29** Because you have humbled yourself, you shall also say, 'He acted proudly,' and He will save the one with downcast eyes;

**22:30** He will deliver the innocent, and be saved through your clean hands."

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — The Greek reads aposes ('put away' or 'cast off'), whereas some Septuagint witnesses read haploses ('make plain' or 'straighten').
- **22:15** — The Septuagint reads 'righteous men' (andres dikaioi), contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('wicked men').
- **22:21** — Greek skleros literally means 'hard' or 'firm,' rendered here as 'steadfast,' differing from the Hebrew sense of agreeing or reconciling with God.
- **22:24** — 'Sophir' (Sopheir) is the distinctive Septuagint Greek form of the place name Ophir.
