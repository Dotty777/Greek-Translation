## Text

**32:1** Now his three friends also ceased answering Job further, for Job was righteous before them.

**32:2** Then Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, of the land of Ausitis, became angry; he was exceedingly angry with Job, because he declared himself righteous before the Lord.

**32:3** Against the three friends also he was exceedingly angry, because they were unable to answer Job in rebuttal, and yet they declared him to be ungodly.

**32:4** Now Elihu had waited to give an answer to Job, because they were older than he in days.

**32:5** And Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, and he was provoked in his wrath.

**32:6** Then Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, "I am younger in years, and you are older; therefore I kept silent, fearing to declare to you my own knowledge.

**32:7** And I said, 'It is not time that speaks, and in many years they do not know wisdom;

**32:8** rather, there is a spirit in mortals, and the breath of the Almighty is what teaches;

**32:9** it is not the long-lived who are wise, nor do the aged know judgment.'

**32:10** Therefore I said, 'Hear me, and I will declare to you what I know.'

**32:11** Give ear to my words while you listen, until you search out words.

**32:12** And I will give full attention to you; and behold, there was no one among you who refuted Job, or answered back his words.

**32:13** So that you may not say, 'We have found wisdom by joining ourselves to the Lord;'

**32:14** yet you permitted a man to speak such words.

**32:15** They were dismayed, they answered no more; they let words grow old from them.

**32:16** I waited, for I did not speak; because they stood still and did not answer."

**32:17** Then Elihu answered and said:

**32:18** "Again I will speak; for I am full of words, for the spirit of my belly destroys me;

**32:19** and my belly is like a bound wineskin of fermenting new wine, or like a smith's bellows that has burst.

**32:20** I will speak, that I may find relief, opening my lips;

**32:21** for I will by no means be ashamed before a man, nor indeed will I stand in awe of a mortal;

**32:22** for I do not know how to show partiality to persons; otherwise, moths also will eat me."

## Translator Notes

- **32:1** — The Greek reads "before them" (in the sight of his three friends), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in his own eyes."
- **32:2** — "Ausitis" is the Septuagint name for the land of Uz, added here in the Greek tradition to identify Elihu's region.
- **32:7** — The Septuagint reverses the sense of the Masoretic Hebrew here, having Elihu assert that time does not speak and many years do not know wisdom.
- **32:17** — The Greek introduces a narrative speech heading here ("Then Elihu answered and said"), whereas in the Masoretic Hebrew Elihu continues speaking in the first person.
- **32:22** — The Greek reads "moths also will eat me," diverging from the Masoretic Hebrew ("my Maker would soon take me away").
