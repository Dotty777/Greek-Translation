## Text

**14:1** For a mortal born of woman is short-lived and full of wrath,

**14:2** or like a flower that has blossomed, he falls away, and he flees like a shadow and shall by no means remain.

**14:3** Have You not taken account even of this one, and caused this one to enter into judgment before You?

**14:4** For who shall be clean from defilement? Indeed, not one,

**14:5** even if his life upon the earth is one day; and his months are numbered by Him; You have set a time, and he shall by no means pass beyond it.

**14:6** Withdraw from him, that he may have rest and take pleasure in life, like a hired laborer.

**14:7** For there is hope for a tree: for if it is cut down, it will blossom again, and its tender shoot shall never fail;

**14:8** for though its root grows old in the earth, and in a rock dies

**14:9** its stump, at the scent of water it will bloom and produce a harvest like a young plant.

**14:10** But a man, having died, is gone; and a mortal, having fallen, is no more.

**14:11** For in time a sea is depleted, and a river, laid desolate, dries up.

**14:12** So man, having fallen asleep, shall by no means rise until the heaven is no longer sewn together, and they shall not be awakened from their sleep.

**14:13** Oh, that You had kept me in Hades, and hidden me until Your wrath should cease, and appointed for me a time in which You would remember me!

**14:14** For if a man dies, he shall live, having completed the days of his life; I will wait until I come into being again.

**14:15** Then You will call, and I will answer You; and do not reject the works of Your hands.

**14:16** Yet You have numbered my practices, and not one of my sins shall escape You;

**14:17** and You have sealed up my iniquities in a bag, and You have marked down if I transgressed anything unwillingly.

**14:18** And indeed, a falling mountain shall crumble away, and a rock shall wear away from its place;

**14:19** waters grind smooth the stones, and waters wash away the level surfaces of the soil of the earth; and You have destroyed the endurance of man.

**14:20** You have thrust him away forever, and he is gone; You have set Your face against him and sent him away;

**14:21** and though his sons become many, he does not know it; and if they become few, he does not perceive it;

**14:22** only his flesh suffers pain, and his soul mourns.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The Greek text shifts abruptly from the third-person pronoun ("by Him," παρ᾽ αὐτοῦ) to the second-person verb ("You have set," ἔθου), a shift preserved literally in the translation.
- **14:12** — The unusual Greek phrase "until the heaven is no longer sewn together" (ἕως ἂν ὁ οὐρανὸς οὐ μὴ συνραφῇ) employs a double negative to depict the permanence of death while the heavens hold together.
- **14:14** — Unlike the Hebrew rhetorical question ("If a man dies, shall he live again?"), the Greek text as punctuated here presents an affirmative statement of life after death ("For if a man dies, he shall live... until I come into being again").
