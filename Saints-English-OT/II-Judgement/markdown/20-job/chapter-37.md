## Text

**37:1** "At this also my heart has been troubled, and has slipped away from its place.

**37:2** Hear a sound in the wrath of the Lord's anger, and a meditation shall go forth from His mouth.

**37:3** Beneath all heaven is His dominion, and His light is upon the wings of the earth.

**37:4** After it He shall cry out with a voice; He shall thunder with the voice of His pride, and He shall not exchange them, for you shall hear His voice.

**37:5** The Mighty One shall thunder wondrous things with His voice, for He has done great things which we did not know;

**37:6** commanding the snow, 'Be upon the earth,' and the storm of rain, and the storm of the rains of His might.

**37:7** He sets a seal upon the hand of every man, that every man may know his own weakness.

**37:8** And the wild beasts entered under shelter, and grew quiet in their lair.

**37:9** Out of the chambers come pains, and from the promontories comes cold.

**37:10** And from the breath of the Mighty One He shall give frost, and He steers the water however He wills;

**37:11** and a cloud spreads over what is choice. A cloud shall scatter His light.

**37:12** And He Himself shall turn about the circuits, in theebulathoth for their works; all things whatever He commands them, these have been ordained by Him upon the earth,

**37:13** whether for discipline, whether for His land, whether for mercy, He shall find him.

**37:14** Give ear to these things, Job; stand still, being instructed in the power of the Lord.

**37:15** We know that God has established His works, having made light out of darkness.

**37:16** And He knows the separation of the clouds, and the extraordinary downfalls of the wicked.

**37:17** And your robe is warm; and there is stillness upon the earth.

**37:18** You will make firm with Him for the ancient things, strong as the appearance of a molten casting.

**37:19** Why? Teach me what we shall say to Him; and let us cease speaking many things.

**37:20** Does a book or a scribe stand beside me, that standing up I should silence a man?

**37:21** Yet the light is not visible to all; it shines afar in the ancient things, just as that which is from Him upon the clouds.

**37:22** From the north come clouds shining like gold; upon these, great is the glory and honor of the Almighty.

**37:23** And we do not find another equal to His strength; He who judges righteous things, do you not suppose that He listens?

**37:24** Therefore men shall fear Him, and the wise in heart also shall fear Him."

## Translator Notes

- **37:4** — Greek hybreos literally means "pride" or "loftiness," used here in the Septuagint to express the overwhelming majesty of God's thunderous voice.
- **37:12** — The word "theebulathoth" is left untranslated in the Greek as a transliteration of the Hebrew noun tahbulot, meaning "guidance" or "steering."
- **37:16** — "The extraordinary downfalls of the wicked" is a distinctive Septuagint reading that diverges markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
