## Text

**38:1** Now after Elihu ceased speaking, the Lord said to Job through a whirlwind and clouds:

**38:2** "Who is this who hides counsel from Me, and holds back words in his heart, and thinks to conceal them from Me?

**38:3** Gird your loins like a man; I will question you, and you answer Me.

**38:4** Where were you when I founded the earth? Declare to Me, if you know understanding.

**38:5** Who appointed its measures, if you know? Or who is it who laid a measuring line upon it?

**38:6** Upon what have its rings been fastened? And who is it who laid a cornerstone upon it?

**38:7** When the stars were made, all My angels praised Me with a loud voice.

**38:8** And I hedged the sea with gates when it surged furiously, coming forth from its mother's womb;

**38:9** and I made a cloud its garment, and swaddled it in mist;

**38:10** and I set boundaries for it, placing bars and gates around it;

**38:11** and I said to it, 'Thus far you shall come, and you shall not pass beyond, but within yourself your waves shall be shattered.'

**38:12** Or was it in your days that I commanded the morning light, and the morning star beheld its own appointed station,

**38:13** to take hold of the wings of the earth, to shake the ungodly out of it?

**38:14** Or did you take clay of the earth and fashion a living creature, and set him upon the earth able to speak?

**38:15** And have you taken away the light from the ungodly, and shattered the arm of the proud?

**38:16** And have you come to the spring of the sea, and walked in the tracks of the abyss?

**38:17** Do the gates of death open to you in fear, and did the gatekeepers of Hades cower when they saw you?

**38:18** Have you perceived the breadth of what lies under heaven? Declare now to Me how vast it is.

**38:19** In what land does light dwell, and what is the place of darkness?

**38:20** Could you lead Me to their borders? And do you also know their paths?

**38:21** I know, then, that you were born at that time, and the number of your years is great.

**38:22** Have you come to the treasuries of snow, and have you seen the treasuries of hail?

**38:23** Are they laid up for you against an hour of enemies, for a day of wars and battle?

**38:24** From where does the frost come forth, or the south wind scatter abroad over what lies under heaven?

**38:25** And who has prepared a course for the torrential rain, and a way for the tumults,

**38:26** to bring rain upon a land where there is no man, a wilderness in which no mortal dwells,

**38:27** to satisfy the untrodden and uninhabited waste, and to cause the sprouting of tender grass?

**38:28** Who is the father of the rain? And who is it who has given birth to the drops of dew?

**38:29** From whose womb does the ice come forth? And who has given birth to the frost in heaven,

**38:30** which descends like flowing water? And who has terrified the face of the ungodly?

**38:31** Do you understand the bond of the Pleiades, and have you opened the enclosure of Orion?

**38:32** Or will you open Mazoroth in its season, and the Evening Star by its locks—will you lead them?

**38:33** Do you know the turnings of heaven, or all the things that come to pass in unison under heaven?

**38:34** Will you call a cloud with your voice, and with a violent trembling of water will it obey you?

**38:35** Will you send forth thunderbolts, and will they go? And will they say to you, 'What is it?'

**38:36** And who gave to women the wisdom of weaving, or the knowledge of embroidery?

**38:37** And who is it who numbers the clouds in wisdom, and has bowed heaven down to the earth?

**38:38** Dust is poured out like earth, and I have bonded it as a cube to a stone.

**38:39** Will you hunt prey for the lions, and fill the souls of the dragons?

**38:40** For they shrink in fear in their dens, and sit in the thickets lying in ambush.

**38:41** And who has prepared food for the raven? For its young have cried out to the Lord, wandering about seeking their food."

## Translator Notes

- **38:7** — The Greek text reads "all My angels" rather than the Hebrew "all the sons of God," and speaks of the stars being made without specifying "morning stars."
- **38:14** — Rather than comparing the dawn-lit earth to clay under a seal as in the Masoretic Text, the Septuagint depicts the fashioning of a living creature endowed with speech from clay of the earth.
- **38:30** — The Greek reads "the face of the ungodly" (asebous) where the Hebrew text refers to "the face of the deep," reflecting a notable textual divergence preserved in the Septuagint.
- **38:32** — "Mazoroth" (Greek Mazourouth) transliterates a rare Hebrew astronomical term for a constellation or celestial cycle, paired here with "the Evening Star" (Hesperos).
- **38:36** — Where the Hebrew text speaks obscurely of wisdom in the inward parts or mind, the Septuagint renders the verse as a question about God granting women the wisdom of weaving and the skill of embroidery.
