## Text

**6:1** Then Job answered and said:

**6:2** "Oh, that someone would indeed weigh my wrath, and lift up my sorrows in a balance together,

**6:3** then indeed it would be heavier than the sand of the seashore; yet, as it seems, my words are worthless.

**6:4** For the arrows of the Lord are in my body, whose fury drinks up my blood; whenever I begin to speak, they pierce me.

**6:5** For why? Will a wild donkey cry out in vain, unless it is seeking its food? Or will an ox raise its voice at the manger while it has its fodder?

**6:6** Shall bread be eaten without salt? Or is there indeed any taste in empty words?

**6:7** For my wrath cannot cease; for I perceive my food as a stench, like the smell of a lion.

**6:8** Oh, that He would grant it, and that my request might come to pass, and that the Lord would grant my hope!

**6:9** Having begun, let the Lord wound me, yet let Him not utterly destroy me.

**6:10** May a grave be my city, upon whose walls I leaped. I will not hold back; for I have not falsified the holy words of my God.

**6:11** For what is my strength, that I endure? Or what is my time, that my soul bears up?

**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones? Or is my flesh of bronze?

**6:13** Or did I not trust in Him? Yet help is absent from me.

**6:14** Mercy has renounced me, and the visitation of the Lord has overlooked me.

**6:15** My nearest kin have not looked upon me; like a failing torrent, or like a wave, they have passed me by;

**6:16** those who used to stand in awe of me have now fallen upon me like snow or frozen ice,

**6:17** just as when it melts as heat comes, it is no longer recognized for what it was;

**6:18** so I also have been forsaken by all, and I have perished and become homeless.

**6:19** Look upon the ways of the Temanites, the paths of the Sabeans, you who observe;

**6:20** and those who trust in cities and wealth shall incur shame.

**6:21** Yet you also have come against me without mercy; therefore, seeing my wound, be afraid!

**6:22** For why? Have I asked anything of you? Or do I have need of strength from you,

**6:23** to save me from enemies, or to deliver me from the hand of the mighty?

**6:24** Teach me, and I will be silent; if I have erred in anything, declare it to me.

**6:25** Yet, as it seems, the words of a truthful man are worthless; for I do not ask for strength from you,

**6:26** nor will your reproof stop me with words; for neither will I endure the sound of your speech.

**6:27** Nevertheless, you fall upon an orphan, and you leap upon your friend.

**6:28** And now, looking into your faces, I will not lie.

**6:29** Sit down now, and let there be no injustice; and come together again with what is right.

**6:30** For there is no injustice on my tongue; or does not my throat meditate on understanding?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Greek reads "my blood" and "whenever I begin to speak, they pierce me," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "my spirit" and speaks of the terrors of God arrayed against Job.
- **6:6** — The Greek reads "in empty words," a notable Septuagint rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to the taste of purslane juice or egg white.
- **6:7** — Greek bromon (a variant spelling of bromos, "stench") parallels "the smell of a lion," expressing Job's revulsion at his food.
- **6:9** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, in which Job asks God to cut him off completely, the Greek includes the negative particle me ("not"): "yet let Him not utterly destroy me."
- **6:19** — Greek Thaimanon refers to the "Temanites" (rather than the caravans of Tema as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), echoing Eliphaz the Temanite.
