## Text

**13:1** Behold, my eye has seen these things, and my ear has heard them.

**13:2** And I know as much as you also understand, and I am no less discerning than you.

**13:3** Nevertheless, I will speak to the Lord, and I will reason before Him, if He wills.

**13:4** But you are unjust physicians, and healers of evils, all of you.

**13:5** May it be yours to keep silent, and it shall turn out to be wisdom for you.

**13:6** Hear the reproof of my mouth, and give heed to the judgment of my lips.

**13:7** Do you not speak before the Lord, and utter deceit before Him?

**13:8** Or will you shrink back? Rather, become judges yourselves.

**13:9** For would it be good if He searches you out? For if, doing all things, you attach yourselves to Him,

**13:10** He will reprove you nonetheless; and if even in secret you show partiality,

**13:11** will not His terrors whirl you about? And dread from Him shall fall upon you,

**13:12** and your boasting shall turn out like ashes, and your body made of clay.

**13:13** Keep silent, that I may speak and find rest from wrath,

**13:14** taking up my flesh in my teeth, and I will place my life in my hand.

**13:15** Though the Mighty One overpower me, seeing that He has already begun, yet surely I will speak and reason before Him;

**13:16** and this shall turn out for my salvation, for deceit shall not enter before Him.

**13:17** Hear, hear my words, for I will declare them in your hearing.

**13:18** Behold, I am near to my judgment; I know that I shall be shown righteous.

**13:19** For who is he who will contend with me, that now I shall keep silent and expire?

**13:20** Only grant me two things, and then I will not hide myself from Your face:

**13:21** withhold Your hand from me, and do not let Your dread terrify me.

**13:22** Then You will call, and I will answer You; or You will speak, and I will give You a reply.

**13:23** How many are my sins and my iniquities? Teach me what they are.

**13:24** Why do You hide Yourself from me, and count me as an adversary to You?

**13:25** Will You be wary of a leaf stirred by the wind, or do You set Yourself against me as against grass carried by a gust?

**13:26** For You have written evils against me, and You have laid upon me the sins of youth;

**13:27** and You have set my foot in a restraint, and You have watched all my works, and You have reached to the roots of my feet,

**13:28** which wears out like a wineskin, or like a moth-eaten garment.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Greek text reads "if He wills" (ἐὰν βούληται), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "I desire [to argue with God]."
- **13:15** — The Septuagint differs markedly from the well-known Hebrew reading ("Though He slay me, yet will I hope in Him"), stating instead that even if the Mighty One overpowers Job as He has already begun to do, Job will still speak and reason before Him.
- **13:22** — In the Greek text, God is the speaker in both clauses ("You will call... or You will speak") and Job is the respondent, whereas in the Hebrew text Job proposes speaking first in the second clause.
