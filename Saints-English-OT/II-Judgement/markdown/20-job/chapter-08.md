## Text

**8:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**8:2** "How long will you speak these things? The wind of your mouth is full of many words.

**8:3** Will the Lord act unjustly in judgment? Or will He who made all things confound justice?

**8:4** If your sons sinned before Him, He sent them away into the hand of their iniquity.

**8:5** But you, rise early to seek the Lord Almighty, making supplication.

**8:6** If you are pure and true, He will listen to your supplication, and He will restore to you a habitation of righteousness.

**8:7** Therefore your former things shall be small, but your latter things shall be beyond telling.

**8:8** For inquire of the former generation, and search out according to the lineage of the fathers;

**8:9** for we are of yesterday and do not know, because our life upon the earth is a shadow.

**8:10** Will they not teach you and declare to you, and bring forth words from the heart?

**8:11** Does the papyrus flourish without water? Or will the marsh sedge grow tall without drink?

**8:12** While it is still upon its root and shall by no means be harvested, before drinking does not every herb dry up?

**8:13** So therefore shall be the end of all those who forget the Lord; for the hope of the ungodly shall perish.

**8:14** For his house shall be uninhabited, and his tent shall turn into a spider's web.

**8:15** If he props up his house, it shall by no means stand; and when he takes hold of it, it shall by no means endure;

**8:16** for he is full of sap beneath the sun, and out of his decay his young shoot shall come forth.

**8:17** Upon a heap of stones he lies down, and in the midst of pebbles he shall live.

**8:18** If it swallows him up, the place shall deny him; you have not seen such things.

**8:19** For such is the overthrow of the ungodly, and out of the earth it shall cause another to spring up.

**8:20** For the Lord shall by no means cast off the innocent, nor will He accept any gift of the ungodly.

**8:21** Yet He will fill the mouth of the true with laughter, and their lips with thanksgiving;

**8:22** and their enemies shall be clothed with shame, and the habitation of the ungodly shall be no more."

## Translator Notes

- **8:12** — Follows the Greek wording 'before drinking' (pro tou piein) and 'every herb' (pasa botane), differing from the Masoretic Hebrew 'before any other grass'.
- **8:16** — The Greek reads 'out of his decay' (ek saprias autou), contrasting with the Masoretic Hebrew 'over his garden'.
- **8:18** — The Greek verb katapie ('swallows up') has no explicit object, and the final clause ('you have not seen such things') is second-person singular rather than a first-person denial by the place as in the Hebrew.
