## Text

**16:1** Then Job answered and said:

**16:2** "I have heard many such things; you are all comforters of evils.

**16:3** What then? Is there order in words of wind? Or what will trouble you that you answer?

**16:4** I also will speak just as you do. If indeed your soul were in place of mine, then I would leap upon you with words, and shake my head against you.

**16:5** And may there be strength in my mouth, and I will not spare the movement of my lips.

**16:6** For if I speak, I shall not feel the pain of the wound; and even if I keep silent, how much less shall I be wounded?

**16:7** But now He has made me weary, foolish, decayed, and You have laid hold of me;

**16:8** it has become a testimony; and my falsehood has risen up within me, and has answered back to my face.

**16:9** Dealing in wrath, He has cast me down; He has gnashed His teeth at me; the arrows of His raiders have fallen upon me.

**16:10** With the sharp barbs of His eyes He has leaped upon me; with a sharp point He has struck me on the knees, and with one accord they have rushed down upon me.

**16:11** For the Lord has delivered me into the hands of an unrighteous man, and He has cast me upon the ungodly.

**16:12** While I was at peace, He scattered me; taking me by the hair, He plucked me, and He set me up as a target.

**16:13** They surrounded me with spears, striking into my kidneys; without sparing, they poured out my gall upon the ground;

**16:14** they cast me down, fall upon fall; they rushed against me as mighty men;

**16:15** they sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and my strength has been quenched in the earth.

**16:16** My belly is scorched from weeping, and upon my eyelids is a shadow;

**16:17** yet there was nothing unrighteous in my hands, and my prayer is pure.

**16:18** O earth, do not cover over the blood of my flesh, and let there be no place for my cry.

**16:19** And now, behold, my witness is in the heavens, and He who knows with me is in the highest places.

**16:20** May my supplication reach the Lord, and before Him may my eye shed tears.

**16:21** And may there be a plea for a man before the Lord, and for a son of man with his neighbor.

**16:22** And numbered years have come, and I shall go by the way on which I shall not return."

## Translator Notes

- **16:6** — The Septuagint literally reads "I shall not feel the pain of the wound" rather than the Hebrew "my pain is not assuaged," framing Job's dilemma in Greek as being wounded whether he speaks or remains silent.
- **16:8** — The Greek reads "my falsehood" (to pseudos mou), rendering a Hebrew root that can signify either "leanness" or "falsehood."
- **16:15** — The Septuagint uses the third-person plural ("They sewed sackcloth"), continuing the actions of Job's assailants, whereas the Hebrew has the first-person singular ("I have sewed").
- **16:20** — The Greek reads "May my supplication reach the Lord," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "My friends scorn me."
