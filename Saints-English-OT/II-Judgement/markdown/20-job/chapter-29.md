## Text

**29:1** And Job continued further and said in his discourse:

**29:2** "Who would set me as in a month of former days, in which God watched over me,

**29:3** as when His lamp shone above my head, when by His light I walked in darkness;

**29:4** when I was pressing heavily upon the ways, when God kept oversight of my house;

**29:5** when I was exceedingly luxuriant, and my children were all around me;

**29:6** when my ways flowed with butter, and my mountains flowed with milk;

**29:7** when I went forth early in the morning in the city, and my seat was set in the public squares?

**29:8** Seeing me, the young men hid themselves, and all the elders stood up;

**29:9** the nobles ceased speaking, laying a finger upon their mouth.

**29:10** Those who heard called me blessed, and their tongue clung to their throat;

**29:11** for the ear heard and called me blessed, and the eye, seeing me, turned aside.

**29:12** For I delivered the poor from the hand of the mighty, and I helped the orphan who had no helper;

**29:13** may the blessing of the perishing come upon me, and the mouth of the widow blessed me.

**29:14** I had put on righteousness, and I clothed myself with justice like a mantle.

**29:15** I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame.

**29:16** I was a father to the helpless, and the cause which I did not know I searched out;

**29:17** I crushed the jaw-teeth of the unrighteous; from the midst of their teeth I snatched the prey.

**29:18** And I said, 'My age shall grow old; like the trunk of a palm tree, I shall live a long time;

**29:19** the root is opened out by the water, and the dew shall abide in my harvest;

**29:20** my glory is new with me, and my bow goes forth in his hand.'

**29:21** Having heard me, they gave heed, and they kept silent at my counsel;

**29:22** to my word they added nothing, and they became overjoyed whenever I spoke to them;

**29:23** as thirsty earth awaits the rain, so did these await my speech.

**29:24** If I smiled at them, they would not believe it; and the light of my countenance did not fall away.

**29:25** I chose their way and sat as chief, and I dwelt like a king among warriors, in the manner of one who comforts the afflicted."

## Translator Notes

- **29:5** — Greek hylodes lian ("exceedingly luxuriant," literally "very wooded") is a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew reads "when the Almighty was yet with me."
- **29:7** — Greek orthrios ("early in the morning") reflects reading the Hebrew word for "gate" as "dawn."
- **29:18** — Greek stelechos phoinikos ("trunk of a palm tree") renders Hebrew hol ("sand" or "phoenix") through Greek phoinix, which means both "palm tree" and "phoenix."
- **29:20** — The Greek text reads en cheiri autou ("in his hand"), contrasting with the Hebrew "in my hand"; ancient Greek interpreters sometimes understood this pronoun as referring to God's hand.
