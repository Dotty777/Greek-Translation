## Text

**19:1** Then Job answered and said,

**19:2** "How long will you weary my soul, and tear me down with words? Know only that the Lord has made me thus;

**19:3** you speak against me; without shame before me, you press hard upon me.

**19:4** Yes indeed, in truth I have gone astray, and error lodges with me, to speak words that ought not to be spoken, and my words go astray and are out of season.

**19:5** Come now, surely you magnify yourselves against me, and you leap upon me with reproach.

**19:6** Know therefore that it is the Lord who has troubled me, and He has raised up His stronghold against me.

**19:7** Behold, I laugh at reproach and will not speak; I will cry out, and nowhere is there justice.

**19:8** I am walled in all around, and I can by no means pass through; He has set darkness upon my face.

**19:9** He has stripped glory from me, and taken away the crown from my head.

**19:10** He has torn me apart all around, and I am gone; He has cut down my hope like a tree.

**19:11** Fiercely He has dealt with me in wrath, and He has regarded me as an enemy.

**19:12** With one accord His raiding bands came upon me; those lying in ambush surrounded my ways.

**19:13** My brethren have departed from me; they have recognized strangers rather than me, and my friends have become merciless.

**19:14** My nearest kin have not acknowledged me, and those who knew my name have forgotten me.

**19:15** Neighbors of my house and my maidservants—I was a foreigner before them.

**19:16** I called my servant, and he did not answer; my mouth pleaded,

**19:17** and I implored my wife, and coaxingly called to the sons of my concubines;

**19:18** yet they rejected me forever; whenever I rise up, they speak against me.

**19:19** Those who saw me abhorred me; those whom I had loved have risen up against me.

**19:20** Within my skin my flesh has rotted away, and my bones are held in my teeth.

**19:21** Have mercy on me, have mercy on me, O friends, for it is the hand of the Lord that has touched me.

**19:22** Why do you persecute me just as the Lord also does, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

**19:23** For who would grant that my words might be written, and that they might be placed in a book forever,

**19:24** with an iron stylus and lead?

**19:25** For I know that He who is about to deliver me is eternal;

**19:26** upon the earth may He raise up my skin that endures these things! For from the Lord these things have been accomplished for me,

**19:27** which I myself know within me, which my eye has seen and not another; and all things have been accomplished for me in my bosom.

**19:28** And if you also say, 'What shall we say before him? And we shall find a root of the matter in him,'

**19:29** then you yourselves also beware of concealment; for wrath shall come upon the lawless, and then they shall know where their substance is."

## Translator Notes

- **19:17** — The Septuagint reads "sons of my concubines," whereas the Masoretic Text refers to the sons of Job's own body or mother's womb.
- **19:20** — In place of the Masoretic Text's "by the skin of my teeth," the Greek describes Job's flesh rotting within his skin and his bones being held in his teeth.
- **19:25** — The Septuagint distinctively describes Job's Vindicator as "eternal" (aenaos) and "He who is about to deliver me," followed in verse 26 by the optative prayer that He may raise up Job's enduring skin upon the earth.
- **19:29** — Greek epikalymma ("covering" or "concealment") and hylē ("material" or "substance") are distinctive Septuagint renderings where the Masoretic Text warns of the sword and judgment.
