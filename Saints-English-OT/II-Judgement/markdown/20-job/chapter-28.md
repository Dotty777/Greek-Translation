## Text

**28:1** For there is a place for silver from which it comes, and a place for gold where it is refined;

**28:2** for iron comes from the earth, and copper is quarried like stone.

**28:3** He has set an order for darkness, and he himself thoroughly searches out every boundary—the stone of gloom and the shadow of death,

**28:4** a cleft of a torrent away from limestone; and those who forget the righteous way have grown weak away from mortals.

**28:5** The earth—out of it bread shall come forth; beneath it, it has been overturned like fire.

**28:6** Its stones are the place of sapphire, and it has dust of gold.

**28:7** A path—no bird has known it, and the eye of the vulture has not glanced upon it;

**28:8** and the sons of the arrogant have not trodden it, nor has a lion passed over it.

**28:9** He stretched forth his hand upon the flinty rock, and overturned mountains from their roots;

**28:10** he broke open the whirlpools of rivers, and my eye has seen every precious thing;

**28:11** he uncovered the depths of rivers, and displayed his own power to the light.

**28:12** But from where has wisdom been found? And what is the place of knowledge?

**28:13** A mortal does not know its way, nor indeed has it been found among men.

**28:14** The abyss said, "It is not in me," and the sea said, "It is not with me."

**28:15** One shall not give an enclosure in return for it, and silver shall not be weighed out for it as its exchange;

**28:16** neither shall it be weighed against the gold of Sophir, with precious onyx and sapphire;

**28:17** gold and crystal shall not be equaled to it, nor shall vessels of gold be its exchange.

**28:18** Lofty things and gabis shall not be mentioned; and draw up wisdom above the innermost things.

**28:19** The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be equaled to it; it shall not be weighed against pure gold.

**28:20** But from where has wisdom been found? And what is the place of understanding?

**28:21** It has escaped the notice of every man, and from the birds of heaven it has been concealed.

**28:22** Destruction and Death said, "We have heard its report."

**28:23** God has well established its way, and He Himself knows its place.

**28:24** For He beholds all that is under heaven, knowing all the things upon the earth

**28:25** that He made, the weight of the winds, the measures of water,

**28:26** when He made them; thus, having seen them, He numbered them, and a way in shaking, and voices.

**28:27** Then He saw it and declared to it; having prepared it, He searched it out.

**28:28** And He said to man, "Behold, reverence for God is wisdom, and to abstain from evil is knowledge."

## Translator Notes

- **28:4** — Greek apo konias ("away from limestone" or "from dust") reflects reading the Hebrew consonant group as "chalk/lime" rather than "sojourner," and the second clause ("those who forget the righteous way") is a distinctive Septuagint rendering.
- **28:10** — The Septuagint reads mou ho ophthalmos ("my eye") rather than the Hebrew "his eye," and in verse 11 heautou dynamin ("his own power") rather than "the hidden thing." The unnamed third-person subject in verses 3 and 9-11 refers in context to the miner, though some ancient Greek commentators understood it of God.
- **28:15** — Greek synkleismon ("enclosure") renders the Hebrew term for fine gold etymologically from the root meaning "to shut in."
- **28:16** — Sophir preserves the Septuagint proper name Sopheir (Hebrew "Ophir").
- **28:18** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew gem terms etymologically as "lofty things" (meteora) and "the innermost things" (ta esotata), leaves gabeis ("gabis," crystal) untranslated, and reads an imperative "draw up" (helkyson).
