## Text

**18:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said,

**18:2** "How long will you not cease? Hold back, so that we ourselves may also speak.

**18:3** Why have we been silent before you like four-footed beasts?

**18:4** Wrath has taken hold of you. What then? If you die, will the world under heaven be uninhabited? Or shall mountains be overturned from their foundations?

**18:5** And the light of the ungodly shall be extinguished, and their flame shall not come forth.

**18:6** His light is darkness in his dwelling, and his lamp shall be extinguished beside him.

**18:7** May the least of men prey upon his possessions, and may his counsel fail.

**18:8** His foot has been cast into a snare; may he be entangled in a net.

**18:9** May snares come upon him; He will strengthen the thirsty against him.

**18:10** A cord for him is hidden in the ground, and a trap for him upon the paths.

**18:11** May pains destroy him all around, and may many come about his foot in dire famine,

**18:12** and a dreadful downfall has been prepared for him.

**18:13** May the branches of his feet be devoured, and death shall devour his beauty.

**18:14** May healing be torn away from his dwelling, and may distress seize him on a royal charge.

**18:15** It shall dwell in his tent in his night; his stately possessions shall be strewn with brimstone.

**18:16** From beneath his roots shall dry up, and from above his harvest shall fall down.

**18:17** May his memorial perish from the earth, and a name belongs to him upon the face outside.

**18:18** May He thrust him from light into darkness.

**18:19** He shall not be recognized among his people, nor shall his house be preserved in the world under heaven,

**18:20** but others shall live in his estate. The last groaned over him, and astonishment seized the first.

**18:21** These are the houses of the unrighteous, and this is the place of those who do not know the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **18:3** — Greek sesiōpēkamen ("we have been silent") reflects a different reading of the underlying Hebrew verb, where the Masoretic Text speaks of being regarded as stupid or defiled.
- **18:9** — The Greek leaves the subject of "will strengthen" unstated (understood here as God) and translates the Hebrew term for a snare as dipsōntas ("the thirsty").
- **18:13** — "The branches of his feet" literally translates Greek klōnes podōn, rendering the Hebrew term for limbs as "branches" (referring to the extremities of his feet), while omitting "firstborn of" before "death."
- **18:17** — The Greek critical text lacks a negative particle before hyparchei ("belongs/exists"), literally stating that a name belongs to him "upon the face outside," in contrast to the Masoretic Text's "he has no name in the street."
