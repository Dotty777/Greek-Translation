## Text

**35:1** Then Elihu answered and said:

**35:2** "Why have you considered this in judgment? Who are you that you have said, 'I am righteous before the Lord'?

**35:4** I will give an answer to you, and to your three friends.

**35:5** Look up to heaven and see, and observe the clouds, how high they are above you.

**35:6** If you have sinned, what will you accomplish? And even if you have committed much lawlessness, what are you able to do?

**35:7** And since then you are righteous, what will you give to Him? Or what shall He receive from your hand?

**35:8** Your ungodliness is for a man like yourself, and your righteousness is for a son of man.

**35:9** Because of the multitude, those who are oppressed shall cry out; they shall shout aloud because of the arm of the many.

**35:10** Yet none has said, 'Where is God who made me, who appoints the night watches,

**35:11** who separates me from the four-footed beasts of the earth, and from the birds of heaven?'

**35:12** There they shall cry out, and He will by no means hear, even because of the arrogance of the wicked.

**35:13** For the Lord does not desire to look upon what is wrong; for He Himself, the Almighty, is the beholder

**35:14** of those who carry out lawless deeds, and He shall save me. Now be judged before Him, if you are able to praise Him as He is.

**35:15** And now, because He is not visiting His wrath, and has not greatly taken note of any transgression,

**35:16** Job opens his mouth in vain; in ignorance he makes words heavy."

## Translator Notes

- **35:4** — Verse 35:3 is absent from the Septuagint Greek text, and 35:4 specifically names Job's "three friends" where the Masoretic Text reads "your friends with you."
- **35:10** — The Greek reads "who appoints the night watches" (phylakas nykterinas) rather than the Masoretic Text's "who gives songs in the night."
- **35:14** — The Septuagint diverges markedly from the Masoretic Text here, stating that the Almighty beholds lawless deeds "and He shall save me," and challenging Job to be judged before Him "if you are able to praise Him as He is."
