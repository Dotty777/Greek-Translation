## Text

**30:1** And Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, to come to the house of the Lord at Jerusalem to keep the Passover to the Lord God of Israel.

**30:2** And the king and the rulers and all the assembly that was in Jerusalem took counsel to keep the Passover in the second month.

**30:3** For they were not able to keep it at that time, because the priests had not consecrated themselves in sufficient number, and the people had not been gathered to Jerusalem.

**30:4** And the matter was pleasing in the sight of the king and in the sight of the assembly.

**30:5** So they established a decree to send a proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, that they should come to keep the Passover to the Lord God of Israel at Jerusalem, for a multitude had not kept it according to what was written.

**30:6** And the runners went with the letters from the king and the rulers throughout all Israel and Judah, according to the command of the king, saying, "O children of Israel, return to the God of Abraham and Isaac and Israel, and bring back those who have escaped, who are left from the hand of the king of Assyria.

**30:7** And do not be like your fathers and your brethren, who departed from the Lord God of their fathers, and He gave them over to desolation, as you see.

**30:8** And now do not harden your hearts; give glory to the Lord God, and enter into His sanctuary, which He has sanctified forever, and serve the Lord your God, and He will turn away from you the fierceness of wrath.

**30:9** For when you return to the Lord, your brethren and your children shall find compassion before all who took them captive, and He will bring them back to this land; for the Lord our God is merciful and compassionate, and He will not turn His face away from you, if we return to Him."

**30:10** And the runners passed from city to city in the hill country of Ephraim and Manasseh, and as far as Zebulun; yet they were as ones laughing at them and mocking them.

**30:11** Nevertheless, men of Asher and from Manasseh and from Zebulun turned, and came to Jerusalem and into Judah.

**30:12** And the hand of the Lord was there to give them one heart to come and act according to the commands of the king and the rulers, by the word of the Lord.

**30:13** And many people gathered to Jerusalem to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread in the second month, an exceedingly great assembly.

**30:14** And they arose and tore down the altars that were in Jerusalem, and all the things on which they had burned incense to the false gods they pulled down and cast into the Brook Kidron.

**30:15** And they slaughtered the Passover on the fourteenth day of the second month; and the priests and the Levites were put to shame and consecrated themselves, and brought burnt offerings into the house of the Lord.

**30:16** And they stood at their post according to their ordinance, according to the commandment of Moses the man of God; and the priests received the blood from the hand of the Levites.

**30:17** For a multitude of the assembly had not consecrated themselves; therefore the Levites were in charge of slaughtering the Passover for everyone who was not able to be consecrated to the Lord.

**30:18** For a great part of the people from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun had not consecrated themselves, yet they ate this Passover contrary to what was written. And Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, "May the good Lord make atonement

**30:19** for every heart that sets itself aright to seek the Lord, the God of their fathers, though not according to the purification of the holy things."

**30:20** And the Lord listened to Hezekiah and healed the people.

**30:21** And the children of Israel who were found in Jerusalem kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with great gladness, singing praises to the Lord day by day, both the priests and the Levites with instruments to the Lord.

**30:22** And Hezekiah spoke to the heart of all the Levites and of those who had good understanding for the Lord; and they completed the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days, offering sacrifices of deliverance and giving thanks to the Lord God of their fathers.

**30:23** And the assembly took counsel together to keep another seven days; and they kept seven days with gladness.

**30:24** For Hezekiah gave as an offering to Judah, to the assembly, one thousand calves and seven thousand sheep; and the rulers gave as an offering to the people one thousand calves and ten thousand sheep; and the consecrated offerings of the priests were in great abundance.

**30:25** And all the assembly rejoiced—the priests and the Levites, and all the assembly of Judah, and those who were found from Israel, and the sojourners who came from the land of Israel, and those who dwelt in Judah.

**30:26** And there was great gladness in Jerusalem; since the days of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, there had not been such a feast in Jerusalem.

**30:27** And the priests, the Levites, arose and blessed the people; and their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to His holy dwelling place, to heaven.

## Translator Notes

- **30:6** — The Greek reads an imperative ("bring back those who have escaped") with singular "the king of Assyria" and "the God of Abraham" (without "the Lord"), whereas the Hebrew reads "that He may return to the remnant ... from the hand of the kings of Assyria."
- **30:11** — The Greek places "and into Judah" at the end of verse 11 rather than at the start of verse 12, and uses the verb "turned" (denoting repentance) where the Hebrew has "humbled themselves."
- **30:24** — The Greek reads "to Judah, to the assembly" and ends with "and the consecrated offerings of the priests were in great abundance," differing from the Hebrew ("Hezekiah king of Judah" and "a great number of priests consecrated themselves").
