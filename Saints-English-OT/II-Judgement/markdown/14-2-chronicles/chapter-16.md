## Text

**16:1** And in the thirty-eighth year of the reign of Asa, Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah and built Ramah, so as to allow neither going out nor coming in to Asa king of Judah.

**16:2** Then Asa took gold and silver from the treasuries of the house of the Lord and of the king's house, and sent to the son of Hader, king of Syria, who dwelt in Damascus, saying,

**16:3** "Make a covenant between me and you, and between my father and your father. Behold, I have sent you gold and silver; come and drive away from me Baasha king of Israel, and let him depart from me."

**16:4** And the son of Hader listened to King Asa, and sent to the commanders of his army against the cities of Israel; and he struck Io, and Dano, and Abelman, and all the surrounding regions of Naphtali.

**16:5** And it came to pass, when Baasha heard it, that he left off building Ramah any longer and stopped his work.

**16:6** Then King Asa took all Judah, and took the stones of Ramah and its timber with which Baasha had built, and with them he built Geba and Mizpah.

**16:7** And at that time Hanami the prophet came to Asa king of Judah and said to him, "Because you have trusted in the king of Syria and have not trusted in the Lord your God, therefore the army of Syria has escaped from your hand.

**16:8** Were not the Ethiopians and the Libyans a great army in boldness, with horsemen in exceedingly great multitude? Yet because you trusted in the Lord, He delivered them into your hands.

**16:9** For the eyes of the Lord look over all the earth to strengthen every whole heart that is toward Him. You have acted without understanding in this; from now on there is war with you."

**16:10** And Asa was angry with the prophet, and put him in prison because of this; and Asa maltreated some of the people at that time.

**16:11** And behold, the acts of Asa, the first and the last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**16:12** And Asa became diseased in his feet in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, until he was exceedingly sick; yet in his sickness he did not seek the Lord, but the physicians.

**16:13** And Asa slept with his fathers, and died in the thirtieth year of his reign.

**16:14** And they buried him in the tomb that he had dug for himself in the City of David; and they laid him on the bier and filled it with spices and kinds of ointments made by perfumers, and they made for him an exceedingly great funeral.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Greek reads "thirty-eighth year" here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "thirty-sixth year."
- **16:2** — The Greek renders the Syrian royal name Ben-hadad as "the son of Hader" (here and in v. 4).
- **16:4** — The Greek transmits the northern Israelite town names as Io, Dano, and Abelman (corresponding to Hebrew Ijon, Dan, and Abel-maim) and reads "surrounding regions" for Hebrew "store-cities."
- **16:7** — The Greek names the prophet "Hanami" (Hebrew "Hanani the seer"), and in v. 8 reads "in boldness" where the Hebrew reads "with chariots."
- **16:13** — The Greek text reads "thirtieth year" despite placing Asa's illness in the "thirty-ninth year" in v. 12; the Hebrew and other manuscripts read "forty-first year."
