## Text

**31:1** And when all these things were finished, all Israel who were found in the cities of Judah went out, and shattered the pillars, and cut down the sacred groves, and tore down the high places and the altars from all the land of Judea and Benjamin and Ephraim and from Manasseh, to the very end; and all Israel returned, each man to his inheritance and to their cities.

**31:2** And Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites, and the divisions of each man according to his own ministry, for the priests and for the Levites, for the burnt offering and for the sacrifice of deliverance, and to praise and to give thanks and to minister in the gates in the courts of the house of the Lord.

**31:3** And the king's portion from his own possessions was for the burnt offerings—the morning and the evening—and the burnt offerings for the Sabbaths, and for the new moons, and for the feasts that are written in the law of the Lord.

**31:4** And they told the people who dwelt in Jerusalem to give the portion of the priests and the Levites, that they might be strengthened in the ministry of the house of the Lord.

**31:5** And as he commanded the word, Israel gave in abundance the firstfruits of grain and wine and oil and honey, and all the produce of the field; and the children of Israel and Judah brought all the tithes in great abundance.

**31:6** And those who dwelt in the cities of Judah, they also brought tithes of calves and sheep, and tithes of goats, and consecrated them to the Lord their God, and brought them in and laid them in heaps.

**31:7** In the third month the foundation heaps began to be laid, and in the seventh month they were finished.

**31:8** And Hezekiah and the rulers came and saw the heaps, and they blessed the Lord and His people Israel.

**31:9** And Hezekiah inquired of the priests and the Levites concerning the heaps.

**31:10** And Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, spoke and said, "Since the firstfruits began to be brought into the house of the Lord, we have eaten and drunk and left over; for the Lord has blessed His people, and we have left over to this great abundance."

**31:11** And Hezekiah gave word to prepare chambers in the house of the Lord; and they prepared them.

**31:12** And they brought there the firstfruits and the tithes faithfully; and over them as overseer was Conaniah the Levite, and Shimei his brother was second in charge,

**31:13** and Jehiel, Uzziah, Maeth, Asahel, Jerimoth, Ezabath, Jeiel, Samachiah, Thanai, and Benaiah and his sons were appointed under Conaniah and Shimei his brother, as King Hezekiah and Azariah the ruler of the house of the Lord had commanded.

**31:14** And Kore the son of Heman the Levite, the gatekeeper toward the east, was over the gifts, to distribute the firstfruits to the Lord and the most holy things.

**31:15** By the hand of Odom, Benjamin, Jeshua, Shimei, Marias, and Shecaniah, through the hand of the priests, they were faithfully to distribute to their brethren according to the divisions, both to the great and to the small—

**31:16** each one of the male offspring from three years old and upward, to everyone who entered the house of the Lord, for their daily portion day by day, for the ministry in the divisions of their order.

**31:17** This was the enrollment of the priests according to their fathers' houses, and the Levites in their divisions from twenty years old and upward, in their order,

**31:18** to enroll them with all the offspring of their sons and their daughters, for the whole multitude; for in faithfulness they consecrated the holy thing.

**31:19** And for the sons of Aaron who served as priests, and those from their cities, in every city there were men who were designated by name to give a portion to every male among the priests and to everyone enrolled among the Levites.

**31:20** And thus Hezekiah did throughout all Judah, and he did what was good and right in the sight of the Lord his God.

**31:21** And in every work that he began in the service of the house of the Lord, and in the law and in the commandments, he sought his God with all his soul; and he acted and prospered.

## Translator Notes

- **31:6** — Following the Greek verse division that places "the children of Israel and Judah" at the end of verse 5, verse 6 reads "tithes of goats" where the Hebrew reads "tithes of the holy things."
- **31:13** — The Greek preserves several distinct personal names here (Uzziah, Maeth, Ezabath, Jeiel, Thanai) and adds "and his sons" after Benaiah; verse 12 also spells Conaniah as Chomenias in the Greek text.
- **31:14** — The Greek names Kore's father as Heman (Hebrew: Imnah), and verse 15 preserves the Greek-distinct names Odom, Benjamin, Shimei, and Marias alongside "through the hand of the priests" (Hebrew: "in the cities of the priests").
- **31:21** — The Greek reads "with all his soul" where the Hebrew reads "with all his heart."
