## Text

**29:1** And Hezekiah became king at twenty-five years of age, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Abba, the daughter of Zechariah.

**29:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.

**29:3** And it came to pass, when he was established over his kingdom, in the first month, that he opened the doors of the house of the Lord and repaired them.

**29:4** And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and stationed them on the side toward the east,

**29:5** and said to them, "Hear, O Levites! Now purify yourselves, and purify the house of the Lord, the God of your fathers, and cast out the uncleanness from the holy places.

**29:6** For our fathers have departed and done what is evil before the Lord, and have forsaken Him, and have turned away their face from the tabernacle of the Lord and turned their backs;

**29:7** and they shut up the doors of the temple, and extinguished the lamps, and did not burn incense, and did not offer burnt offerings in the holy place to the God of Israel.

**29:8** And the Lord was angry with wrath against Judah and Jerusalem, and He delivered them over to astonishment and to hissing, as you see with your own eyes.

**29:9** And behold, your fathers have been struck down by the sword, and your sons and your daughters and your wives are in captivity in a land not their own, which is so even now.

**29:10** Because of these things it is now in my heart to make my covenant, a covenant of the Lord, the God of Israel; and He has turned away the wrath of His anger from us.

**29:11** And now do not be negligent, for He has chosen you to stand before Him to minister, and to be His ministers and to burn incense."

**29:12** And the Levites rose up: Mahath the son of Masi and Joel the son of Zechariah, of the sons of Kohath; and of the sons of Merari, Kish the son of Abdi and Zechariah the son of Elle; and of the sons of Gershon, those from Zemmath and Jodan—these were sons of Joah;

**29:13** and of the sons of Elizaphan, both Zambri and Jeiel; and of the sons of Asa, Azariah and Mattaniah;

**29:14** and of the sons belonging to Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and of the sons of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.

**29:15** And he gathered their brethren, and they purified themselves according to the command of the king by the decree of the Lord.

**29:16** And the priests went even into the house of the Lord to purify it, and cast out all the uncleanness that was found in the house of the Lord, even into the court of the house of the Lord; and the Levites received it to cast it out into the brook Kidron.

**29:17** And they began to purify on the third day, the new moon of the first month, and on the eighth day of the month they entered into the temple of the Lord; and they purified the house of the Lord in eight days, and on the thirteenth day of the first month they finished.

**29:18** And they went inside to Hezekiah the king and said, "We have purified all the things in the house of the Lord, the altar of burnt offering and its vessels, and the table of the showbread and its vessels,

**29:19** and all the vessels which Ahaz the king defiled in his reign in his apostasy we have prepared and purified; behold, they are before the altar of the Lord."

**29:20** And Hezekiah the king rose up early and gathered the leaders of the city, and went up to the house of the Lord.

**29:21** And he brought up seven young bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom and for the holy places and for Israel; and he spoke to the sons of Aaron, the priests, to go up to the altar of the Lord.

**29:22** And they slaughtered the young bulls, and the priests received the blood and poured it upon the altar; and they slaughtered the rams, and poured the blood upon the altar.

**29:23** And they brought near the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and they laid their hands upon them.

**29:24** And the priests slaughtered them, and made atonement with their blood at the altar, and made atonement for all Israel; for "For all Israel," said the king, "are the burnt offering and the sin offerings."

**29:25** And he stationed the Levites in the house of the Lord with cymbals, with harps, and with lyres, according to the command of David the king, and of Gad the prophet, and of Nathan the prophet; for the decree was by the command of the Lord through the hand of the prophets.

**29:26** And the Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.

**29:27** And Hezekiah commanded to offer up the burnt offering upon the altar; and when they began to offer up the burnt offering, they began to sing to the Lord, and the trumpets sounded with the instruments of David the king.

**29:28** And the entire assembly worshiped, and the singers were singing and the trumpets were sounding until the burnt offering was finished.

**29:29** And when they had finished offering, the king and all who were present bowed down and worshiped.

**29:30** And Hezekiah the king and the leaders told the Levites to sing praise to the Lord with the words of David and of Asaph the prophet; and they sang praise with gladness, and fell down and worshiped.

**29:31** And Hezekiah answered and said, "Now you have filled your hands to the Lord; draw near and bring sacrifices and offerings of praise into the house of the Lord." And the assembly brought sacrifices and offerings of praise into the house of the Lord, and everyone who was willing in heart brought burnt offerings.

**29:32** And the number of the burnt offerings which the assembly brought was seventy young bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs; all these were for a burnt offering to the Lord.

**29:33** And the consecrated offerings were six hundred young bulls and three thousand five hundred sheep.

**29:34** However, the priests were too few and were not able to skin the burnt offering; so their brethren the Levites helped them until the work was finished and until the priests had purified themselves, for the Levites purified themselves more eagerly than the priests.

**29:35** And the burnt offering was abundant, with the fat portions of the consecration of the peace offering and the drink offerings of the burnt offering; and the work in the house of the Lord was set in order.

**29:36** And Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced because God had made preparation for the people, for the matter had come about suddenly.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Septuagint names Hezekiah's mother "Abba," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Abijah."
- **29:12** — Several Levitical names in 29:12–14 preserve distinctive Septuagint forms—including "Masi," "Zechariah," "Elle," "Zemmath," "Jodan," "Zambri," "Asa," and "Azariah"—and the Greek groups those of Zemmath and Jodan together as sons of Joah.
- **29:17** — The Septuagint dates the start of the purification to "the third day, the new moon of the first month" and its completion to "the thirteenth day," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the first day and the sixteenth day.
- **29:33** — The Septuagint records "three thousand five hundred sheep," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "three thousand."
