## Text

**5:1** And Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father, the silver and the gold and the vessels; he put them into the treasury of the Lord.

**5:2** Then Solomon assembled the elders and all the rulers of the tribes, the leaders of the ancestral houses of the sons of Israel, to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord from the City of David, which is Zion.

**5:3** And all Israel assembled to the king at the feast, which is the seventh month.

**5:4** And all the elders of Israel came, and all the Levites took up the ark,

**5:5** and the tabernacle of the testimony, and all the holy vessels that were in the tabernacle; and the priests and the Levites brought it up.

**5:6** And King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel, and those who feared and those of them who were gathered together before the ark, were sacrificing calves and sheep, which could not be numbered and could not be reckoned for multitude.

**5:7** And the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the Lord into its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Holy of Holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**5:8** And the cherubim had their wings spread out over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered over the ark and over its poles from above.

**5:9** And the poles projected out, and the ends of the poles were seen from the holy place before the inner sanctuary; they were not seen outside. And they were there until this day.

**5:10** There was nothing in the ark except two tablets which Moses placed at Horeb, the things which the Lord covenanted with the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**5:11** And it came to pass when the priests came out of the holy place—for all the priests who were present had sanctified themselves; they were not arranged by division—

**5:12** and all the Levites who were singers, belonging to the sons of Asaph, to Heman, to Jeduthun, and to his sons and to his brethren, clothed in fine linen robes, with cymbals and with harps and with lyres, standing opposite the altar, and with them one hundred and twenty priests sounding with the trumpets,

**5:13** that there was one voice in sounding the trumpets and in singing and in proclaiming with one voice to give thanks and to praise the Lord; and they lifted up their voice with trumpets and with cymbals and with instruments of song, and they said, "Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good, for His mercy endures forever." And the house was filled with a cloud of the glory of the Lord,

**5:14** and the priests were not able to stand to minister because of the presence of the cloud, for the glory of the Lord filled the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Greek includes "and those who feared" alongside "those of them who were gathered together," a distinctive Septuagint reading not present in the Hebrew.
- **5:9** — The Greek states that the ends of the poles were seen "from the holy place" before the inner sanctuary (Greek dabeir), where the Hebrew reads "from the ark."
- **5:10** — The Greek uses a neuter plural relative pronoun ("the things which the Lord covenanted") and specifies "out of the land of Egypt."
- **5:13** — The Greek reads neuter agathon ("for it is good") rather than masculine agathos ("for He is good"), and concludes with "a cloud of the glory of the Lord."
