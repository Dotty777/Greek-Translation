## Text

**3:1** And Solomon began to build the house of the Lord in Jerusalem on Mount Amoria, where the Lord had appeared to David his father, in the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.

**3:2** And the building began in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3:3** And these things Solomon began to build for the house of God: the length in cubits, according to the former measurement, was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**3:4** And the vestibule in front of the house had its length across the width of the house, twenty cubits, and its height was one hundred and twenty cubits; and he overlaid it within with pure gold.

**3:5** And the greater house he paneled with cedar wood, and overlaid it with pure gold, and carved upon it palm trees and chainwork.

**3:6** And he adorned the house with precious stones for glory, and with gold, the gold from Parvaim,

**3:7** and he overlaid the house, and the walls, and the gateways, and the ceilings, and the doors with gold; and he carved cherubim upon the walls.

**3:8** And he made the house of the Holy of Holies: its length across the front, a width of twenty cubits, and the length twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with pure gold for the cherubim, to the amount of six hundred talents.

**3:9** And the weight of the nails, the weight of a single one, was fifty shekels of gold; and he overlaid the upper chamber with gold.

**3:10** And in the house of the Holy of Holies he made two cherubim, a work of wood, and overlaid them with gold.

**3:11** And the wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits in length: the one wing of five cubits touched the wall of the house, and the other wing of five cubits touched the wing of the other cherub.

**3:13** And the wings of the cherubim were spread out twenty cubits; and they stood upon their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

**3:14** And he made the veil of blue and purple and scarlet and fine linen, and wove cherubim into it.

**3:15** And he made before the wall two pillars, thirty-five cubits in height, and their capitals were five cubits.

**3:16** And he made chains in the inner sanctuary and put them upon the capitals of the pillars; and he made one hundred pomegranates and placed them upon the chains.

**3:17** And he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and the other on the left; and he called the name of the one on the right Establishment, and the name of the one on the left Strength.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text preserves the proper-name forms "Amoria" (incorporating the Hebrew definite article into Moriah) and "Orna" (for Ornan/Araunah).
- **3:8** — The Greek text adds "for the cherubim" (eis cheroubein), linking the six hundred talents of pure gold to both the Holy of Holies and the cherubim.
- **3:11** — Verse 12 is absent in the Greek critical text due to an early scribal skip between the parallel clauses describing the wings of the two cherubim.
- **3:16** — "Chains" and "the inner sanctuary" translate the Greek transliterations serseroth and dabeir from Hebrew.
- **3:17** — Rather than transliterating the Hebrew pillar names Jachin and Boaz, the Greek text translates their meanings as Katorthosis ("Establishment") and Ischys ("Strength").
