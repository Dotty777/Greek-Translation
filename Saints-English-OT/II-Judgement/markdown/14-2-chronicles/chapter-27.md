## Text

**27:1** Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Jerusha, the daughter of Sador.

**27:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Uzziah had done, except that he did not enter into the temple of the Lord; yet the people still acted corruptly.

**27:3** He built the High Gate of the house of the Lord, and on his wall of Ophel he built much;

**27:4** in the hill country of Judah, and in the forests both dwellings and towers.

**27:5** He also fought against the king of the sons of Ammon and prevailed against him. And they gave him each year one hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand of barley; these the king of Ammon brought to him each year, in the first year and the second and the third.

**27:6** So Jotham grew mighty, because he prepared his ways before the Lord his God.

**27:7** Now the rest of the acts of Jotham, and his war and his deeds, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**27:9** And Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried in the City of David; and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — The Greek gives the name of Jerusha's father as Sador, corresponding to Zadok in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **27:4** — The Greek text continues the verb "he built" from verse 3 without repeating it and omits the mention of "cities" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **27:5** — The Greek includes "in the first year" along with the second and third years of Ammon's tribute, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists only the second and third years.
- **27:9** — Verse 8, which repeats Jotham's age at accession and length of reign in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, is absent from this Greek text.
