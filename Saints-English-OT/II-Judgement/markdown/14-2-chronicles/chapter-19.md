## Text

**19:1** And Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house in Jerusalem.

**19:2** And Jehu the son of Hanani the prophet went out to meet him, and said to him, "O King Jehoshaphat, do you help a sinner, or show friendship to one who is hated by the Lord? Because of this, wrath from the Lord has come upon you;

**19:3** nevertheless, good things have been found in you, because you have removed the groves from the land of Judah, and have directed your heart to seek the Lord."

**19:4** And Jehoshaphat dwelt there, and again he went out among the people from Beersheba to Mount Ephraim, and brought them back to the Lord God of their fathers.

**19:5** And he appointed judges in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.

**19:6** And he said to the judges, "Take heed to what you are doing, for you do not judge for man, but for the Lord, and with you are the matters of judgment.

**19:7** Now therefore, let the fear of the Lord be upon you; take care and do it, for there is no injustice with the Lord our God, nor showing of partiality, nor taking of gifts."

**19:8** Moreover, in Jerusalem Jehoshaphat appointed some of the priests and of the Levites and of the patriarchs of Israel for the judgment of the Lord and to judge those who dwell in Jerusalem.

**19:9** And he commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall act in the fear of the Lord, in truth and with a whole heart:

**19:10** whatever case of any man comes before you from our brethren who dwell in the midst of bloodshed, and between decree and commandment, and statutes and judgments, you shall strictly warn them, and they shall not sin against the Lord, and wrath shall not come upon you and upon your brethren. Thus you shall do, and you shall not sin.

**19:11** And behold, Amariah the priest is leader over you in every matter of the Lord, and Zebadiah is the leader over the house of Judah in every matter of the king; and the scribes and the Levites are before you. Be strong and act, and the Lord will be with the good."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek includes the direct address "O King Jehoshaphat" and uses the passive "one who is hated by the Lord" where the Hebrew reads "those who hate the LORD."
- **19:8** — The Greek reads "to judge those who dwell in Jerusalem," whereas the Hebrew reads "for controversies; and they returned to Jerusalem."
- **19:10** — The Greek syntax here is compressed, reading "our brethren" (first person) and omitting "in their cities" as well as the second mention of bloodshed found in the Hebrew text.
