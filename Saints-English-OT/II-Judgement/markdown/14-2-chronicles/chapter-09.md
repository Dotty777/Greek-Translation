## Text

**9:1** Now the queen of Sheba heard the name of Solomon, and she came to Jerusalem to test Solomon with riddles, with an exceedingly great retinue, and camels bearing spices, and gold in abundance, and precious stones; and she came to Solomon and spoke to him all that was in her soul.

**9:2** And Solomon declared to her all her matters, and not a matter escaped Solomon—not a matter which he did not declare to her.

**9:3** And the queen of Sheba saw the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he had built,

**9:4** and the food of the tables, and the seating of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers and their apparel, and his cupbearers and their attire, and the burnt offerings which he offered in the house of the Lord; and she was beside herself.

**9:5** And she said to the king, "True is the report which I heard in my land concerning your words and concerning your wisdom.

**9:6** And I did not believe the reports until I came and my eyes saw; and behold, half of the abundance of your wisdom was not declared to me—you have exceeded the report which I heard.

**9:7** Blessed are the men! Blessed are these your servants who stand before you continually and hear your wisdom!

**9:8** Blessed be the Lord your God, who delighted in you to set you upon His throne as king for the Lord your God! Because the Lord your God loved Israel, to establish them forever, therefore He has set you over them as king, to execute judgment and righteousness."

**9:9** And she gave the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices in very great abundance, and precious stones; and there were none like those spices which the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**9:10** Moreover, the servants of Solomon and the servants of Hiram brought gold to Solomon from Suphir, and pine wood, and precious stones.

**9:11** And the king made of the pine wood steps for the house of the Lord and for the house of the king, and lyres and harps for the singers; and nothing like them had been seen before in the land of Judah.

**9:12** And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desires, whatever she asked, besides all that she had brought to King Solomon. And she returned to her own land.

**9:13** Now the weight of the gold that was brought to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

**9:14** besides what the subject men and the merchants brought; and all the kings of Arabia and the satraps of the land brought gold and silver to King Solomon.

**9:15** And King Solomon made two hundred large shields of hammered gold—six hundred pieces of pure gold for one large shield, six hundred gold pieces were upon each large shield;

**9:16** and three hundred shields of hammered gold; and the king placed them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.

**9:17** And the king made a great throne of ivory tusks and overlaid it with refined gold.

**9:18** And there were six steps to the throne, bound with gold, and armrests on either side of the seat of the throne, and two lions standing beside the armrests.

**9:19** And twelve lions stood there upon the six steps, on the one side and on the other; nothing like it was ever made in all the kingdom.

**9:20** And all the vessels of King Solomon were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were overlaid with gold; silver was accounted as nothing in the days of Solomon.

**9:21** For the king's ship went to Tarshish with the servants of Hiram; once every three years ships came from Tarshish to the king, laden with gold, and silver, and ivory tusks, and apes.

**9:22** And Solomon was magnified above all the kings, both in wealth and in wisdom.

**9:23** And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom which God had put in his heart.

**9:24** And each of them brought his gifts: vessels of silver and vessels of gold, and garments, myrrh and spices, horses and mules, year by year.

**9:25** And Solomon had four thousand mares for chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen; and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.

**9:26** And he was ruler over all the kings from the River even to the land of the foreigners, and to the borders of Egypt.

**9:27** And the king made gold and silver in Jerusalem like stones, and the cedars like the sycamores in the lowland for abundance.

**9:28** And the export of Solomon's horses was from Egypt and from all the earth.

**9:29** Now the rest of the acts of Solomon, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the records of Nathan the prophet, and in the records of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Joel the seer concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

**9:30** And King Solomon reigned over all Israel forty years.

**9:31** And Solomon slept, and they buried him in the city of David his father; and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek reads "the burnt offerings which he offered in the house of the Lord," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "his ascent by which he went up to the house of the Lord."
- **9:10** — The Greek reads "Suphir" (for Hebrew "Ophir") and "pine wood" (where Hebrew reads "algum wood"; so also in v. 11).
- **9:25** — The Greek reads "four thousand mares for chariots," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "four thousand stalls for horses and chariots."
- **9:26** — "Foreigners" translates the Greek allophyloi, the Septuagint's standard rendering for the Philistines.
- **9:29** — The Greek reads "Joel the seer," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Iddo the seer."
