## Text

**8:1** And it came to pass after twenty years, during which Solomon built the house of the Lord and his own house,

**8:2** that the cities which Hiram had given to Solomon, Solomon built them and caused the sons of Israel to dwell there.

**8:3** And Solomon went to Baisoba and prevailed against it.

**8:4** And he built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the fortified cities he built in Hamath.

**8:5** And he built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars;

**8:6** and Baalah, and all the fortified cities that belonged to Solomon, and all the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen, and all that Solomon desired according to his desire to build in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and in all his kingdom.

**8:7** All the people who were left of the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who are not of Israel—

**8:8** they were from their sons who were left with him in the land, whom the sons of Israel had utterly destroyed; and Solomon raised them to tribute until this day.

**8:9** But of the sons of Israel Solomon did not appoint any as servants for his kingdom; for behold, they were men of war, and rulers, and mighty men, and commanders of chariots and horsemen.

**8:10** And these were the chief officers of King Solomon's overseers, two hundred and fifty overseeing the labor among the people.

**8:11** Now Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to the house which he had built for her; for he said, "My wife shall not dwell in the city of David, the king of Israel, for the place is holy where the ark of the Lord has entered."

**8:12** Then Solomon offered up burnt offerings to the Lord upon the altar which he had built in front of the temple,

**8:13** and according to the daily requirement, to offer according to the commandments of Moses on the Sabbaths, and on the New Moons, and on the feasts, three times a year—at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, and at the Feast of Tabernacles.

**8:14** And he appointed, according to the ordinance of David, the divisions of the priests for their ministries, and the Levites over their watches to minister and to praise before the priests according to each day's requirement, and the gatekeepers by their divisions at gate after gate; for such were the commandments of David, the man of God.

**8:15** They did not depart from the commandments of the king concerning the priests and the Levites in any matter, or concerning the treasuries.

**8:16** And all the work was prepared from the day its foundation was laid until Solomon completed the house of the Lord.

**8:17** Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Elam by the sea, in the land of Idumea.

**8:18** And Hiram sent by the hand of his servants ships and servants who knew the sea; and they went with the servants of Solomon to Sophira, and took from there the four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and came to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — The Greek text reads "Baisoba" (and in v. 6 "Baalah"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Hamath-zobah" (and in v. 6 "Baalath").
- **8:8** — The Greek reads "with him" and omits the negative particle before "utterly destroyed," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "after them, whom the sons of Israel had not destroyed."
- **8:17** — The Greek reads "Elam" (and in v. 18 "Sophira"), corresponding to Hebrew "Eloth" (and "Ophir").
