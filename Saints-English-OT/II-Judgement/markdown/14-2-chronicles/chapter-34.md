## Text

**34:1** Josiah was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.

**34:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the ways of David his father, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**34:3** And in the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a youth, he began to seek the Lord, the God of David his father; and in the twelfth year of his reign he began to cleanse Judah and Jerusalem from the high places and the groves, and from the cast images.

**34:4** And he tore down before his face the altars of the Baals and the high places that were above them; and he cut down the groves and the carved images, and the cast images he broke in pieces and ground to dust, and cast upon the face of the graves of those who sacrificed to them.

**34:5** And he burned the bones of the priests upon the altars, and cleansed Judah and Jerusalem,

**34:6** and in the cities of Ephraim and Manasseh and Simeon and Naphtali, and in their places round about.

**34:7** And he tore down the groves and the altars, and cut the idols into fine pieces, and cut down all the high places throughout all the land of Israel; and he returned to Jerusalem.

**34:8** Now in the eighteenth year of his reign, to cleanse the land and the house, he sent Shaphan the son of Selia, and Maasa the ruler of the city, and Joah the son of Joach his recorder, to repair the house of the Lord his God.

**34:9** And they came to Hilkiah the high priest and delivered the silver that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites who kept the gate had gathered from the hand of Manasseh and Ephraim, and of the rulers, and from all the remnant in Israel, and of the sons of Judah and Benjamin, and of those who dwelt in Jerusalem.

**34:10** And they put it into the hand of the workmen who were appointed in the house of the Lord, and they gave it to the workmen who were working in the house of the Lord, to mend and strengthen the house.

**34:11** And they gave it to the carpenters and to the builders, to buy squared stones and timber for beams to roof the houses which the kings of Judah had destroyed.

**34:12** And the men were faithful in the work; and over them as overseers were Ie and Obadiah, the Levites of the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam of the sons of Kohath, to oversee; and every Levite, everyone skilled with instruments of song.

**34:13** They were also over the burden-bearers and over all those who did the work in every kind of service; and from the Levites there were scribes and judges and gatekeepers.

**34:14** And as they were bringing out the silver that had been brought into the house of the Lord, Hilkiah the priest found a book of the law of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**34:15** And Hilkiah answered and said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found a book of the law in the house of the Lord." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

**34:16** And Shaphan brought the book to the king, and furthermore brought word back to the king, saying, "All the silver that was given is in the hand of your servants who do the work,

**34:17** and they have melted down the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and he has delivered it into the hand of the overseers who do the work."

**34:18** And Shaphan the scribe told the king, saying, "There is a book which Hilkiah the priest gave to me." And Shaphan read it before the king.

**34:19** And it came to pass, when the king heard the law, that he tore his garments.

**34:20** And the king commanded Hilkiah, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Abdodom the son of Micah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Isaiah the servant of the king, saying,

**34:21** "Go, inquire of the Lord for me, and for all that is left in Israel and Judah, concerning the words of the book that has been found; for great is the wrath of the Lord that has been kindled among us, because our fathers did not listen to the words of the Lord, to do according to all that is written in this book."

**34:22** So Hilkiah and those to whom the king had spoken went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Kathual, the son of Chelles, who kept the commandments—now she dwelt in Jerusalem in Maasanai—and they spoke to her to that effect.

**34:23** And she said to them, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'Tell the man who sent you to Me:

**34:24** Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing calamities upon this place, all the words written in the book which was read before the king of Judah,

**34:25** because they have forsaken Me and burned incense to foreign gods, that they might provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands; and My wrath has been kindled in this place, and it shall not be quenched.

**34:26** And to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the Lord, thus shall you say to him: Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: As for the words which you have heard,

**34:27** and your heart was penitent, and you humbled yourself before My face when you heard My words against this place and against those who dwell in it, and you humbled yourself before Me, and tore your garments and wept before Me—I also have heard, says the Lord.

**34:28** Behold, I will gather you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your graves in peace, and your eyes shall not look upon all the calamities which I am bringing upon this place and upon those who dwell in it.'" And they brought word back to the king.

**34:29** Then the king sent and gathered the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**34:30** And the king went up to the house of the Lord, with all Judah and those who dwelt in Jerusalem, and the priests and the Levites, and all the people from the great to the small; and he read in their hearing all the words of the book of the covenant that were found in the house of the Lord.

**34:31** And the king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk before the Lord, and to keep His commandments and His testimonies and His statutes with all his heart and with all his soul—even the words of the covenant that were written in this book.

**34:32** And he caused all who were found in Jerusalem and Benjamin to take a stand; and the inhabitants of Jerusalem made a covenant in the house of the Lord, the God of their father.

**34:33** And Josiah removed all the abominations from all the land that belonged to the sons of Israel, and caused all who were found in Jerusalem and in Israel to serve the Lord their God all his days; he did not turn aside from following the Lord, the God of his fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **34:8** — The Greek text gives the names "Selia" (for Hebrew Azaliah), "Maasa" (for Maaseiah), and "Joach" (for Joahaz).
- **34:12** — The Greek text reads "Ie" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Jahath."
- **34:20** — The Greek text reads "Abdodom" (for Hebrew Abdon) and "Isaiah" (for Hebrew Asaiah).
- **34:22** — The Greek reads "Kathual" and "Chelles" (for Hebrew Tokhath and Hasrah), applies a feminine participle to Huldah as "who kept the commandments" (where the Hebrew calls Shallum "keeper of the wardrobe"), and transliterates the Jerusalem quarter as "Maasanai" (Hebrew Mishneh, the Second Quarter).
