## Text

**13:1** In the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam, Abijah became king over Israel.

**13:2** He reigned three years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Maacah the daughter of Uriel from Gibeon. And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

**13:3** And Abijah set the battle in array with an army of mighty warriors, four hundred thousand valiant men; and Jeroboam set the battle in array against him with eight hundred thousand, valiant warriors of strength.

**13:4** Then Abijah stood up from Mount Somoron, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, and said, "Hear, O Jeroboam and all Israel!

**13:5** Is it not for you to know that the Lord God of Israel gave a king over Israel forever, to David and to his sons, by a covenant of salt?

**13:6** Yet Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master.

**13:7** And pestilent men, lawless sons, gathered to him, and he rose up against Rehoboam the son of Solomon; and Rehoboam was young and timid in heart, and did not withstand him.

**13:8** And now you say you will withstand the kingdom of the Lord, which is in the hand of the sons of David; and you are a great multitude, and with you are golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods.

**13:9** Have you not cast out the priests of the Lord, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and made for yourselves priests from the people of all the land? Whoever comes to consecrate himself with calves from the herd and seven rams becomes a priest to that which is no god.

**13:10** But as for us, we have not forsaken the Lord our God, and His priests minister to the Lord, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites in their divisions.

**13:11** They burn to the Lord a burnt offering morning and evening, and compounded incense, and the rows of showbread upon the pure table, and the golden lampstand with its lamps for burning to kindle in the evening; for we keep the charges of the Lord the God of our fathers, whereas you have forsaken Him.

**13:12** And behold, with us at our head is the Lord, and His priests, and the trumpets of alarm to sound the signal over us. O sons of Israel, will you fight against the Lord God of our fathers? For you shall not prosper."

**13:13** But Jeroboam sent an ambush around to come upon them from behind; so he was in front of Judah, and the ambush was behind them.

**13:14** And Judah turned back, and behold, the battle was against them both in front and behind; and they cried out to the Lord, and the priests sounded the trumpets.

**13:15** Then the men of Judah shouted; and it came to pass, as the men of Judah shouted, that the Lord struck Jeroboam and Israel before Abijah and Judah.

**13:16** And the sons of Israel fled before Judah, and the Lord delivered them into their hands.

**13:17** And Abijah and his people struck them with a great slaughter, so that five hundred thousand mighty men of Israel fell slain.

**13:18** Thus the sons of Israel were humbled in that day, and the sons of Judah prevailed, because they hoped in the Lord God of their fathers.

**13:19** And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam and captured cities from him: Bethel with its villages, and Cana with its villages, and Ephron with its villages.

**13:20** And Jeroboam did not retain strength any longer all the days of Abijah; and the Lord struck him, and he died.

**13:21** But Abijah grew mighty, and took for himself fourteen wives, and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

**13:22** Now the rest of the acts of Abijah, and his deeds and his words, are written in the book of the prophet Iddo.

**13:23** And Abijah died with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his place. In the days of Asa the land of Judah had rest for ten years.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Greek text reads "over Israel" where the Hebrew reads "over Judah."
- **13:4** — The Greek reads Mount Somoron where the Hebrew reads Mount Zemaraim.
- **13:12** — The Greek reads "over us" (Hebrew: against you) and "our fathers" (Hebrew: your fathers), and omits the negative particle before "fight" (Hebrew: do not fight), rendered here as a rhetorical question.
- **13:19** — The Greek reads Cana where the Hebrew reads Jeshanah.
- **13:23** — In the Greek text this verse concludes chapter thirteen (corresponding to 14:1 in Hebrew numbering), reads "died" rather than "slept," and adds "of Judah" after "the land."
