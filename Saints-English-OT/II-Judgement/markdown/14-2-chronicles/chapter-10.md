## Text

**10:1** And Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel was coming to Shechem to make him king.

**10:2** And it came to pass, when Jeroboam the son of Nebat heard of it—now he was in Egypt, since he had fled from the presence of King Solomon, and Jeroboam had dwelt in Egypt—that Jeroboam returned from Egypt.

**10:3** And he sent and called him; and Jeroboam came, and all the assembly came to Rehoboam, saying,

**10:4** "Your father made our yoke harsh; now therefore lighten the harsh service of your father, and his heavy yoke which he laid upon us, and we will serve you."

**10:5** And he said to them, "Go away for three days, and come to me." So the people departed.

**10:6** Then King Rehoboam assembled the elders who had stood before Solomon his father while he was alive, saying, "How do you advise me to answer a word to this people?"

**10:7** And they spoke to him, saying, "If today you are good to this people, and show them favor, and speak good words to them, they will be your servants all the days."

**10:8** But he forsook the counsel of the elders who had counseled him, and took counsel with the young men who had grown up with him, who stood before him.

**10:9** And he said to them, "What do you advise that I should answer a word to this people, who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke which your father laid upon us'?"

**10:10** And the young men who had grown up with him spoke to him, "Thus you shall speak to the people who spoke to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, so lighten it from us'—thus you shall say, 'My little finger is thicker than my father's loins.

**10:11** And now my father chastised you with a heavy yoke, and I will add to our yoke; my father chastised you with whips, and I will chastise you with scorpions.'"

**10:12** So Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

**10:13** And the king answered harshly; and King Rehoboam forsook the counsel of the elders,

**10:14** and spoke to them according to the counsel of the younger men, saying, "My father made our yoke heavy, and I will add to it; my father chastised you with whips, and I will chastise you with scorpions."

**10:15** So the king did not listen to the people; for there was a turn of events from God, saying, "The Lord has established His word which He spoke by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat

**10:16** and all Israel, because the king did not listen to them." And the people answered the king, saying, "What portion do we have in David, and what inheritance in the son of Jesse? To your tents, O Israel! Now see to your own house, David!" So all Israel went to their tents.

**10:17** And as for the men of Israel and those who dwelt in the cities of Judah, Jeroboam reigned over them.

**10:18** Then Rehoboam sent Adoniram, who was over the tribute, to them; and the sons of Israel stoned him with stones, and he died. And King Jeroboam hastened to mount the chariot to flee to Jerusalem.

**10:19** So Israel rebelled against the house of David until this day.

## Translator Notes

- **10:11** — Both here and in v. 14, the Greek text reads "our yoke" rather than "your yoke."
- **10:15** — The Greek introduces a direct statement ("saying, 'The Lord has established His word...'") continuing into v. 16, whereas the Hebrew expresses purpose ("in order that the Lord might fulfill His word").
- **10:17** — The Greek text names "Jeroboam" rather than "Rehoboam" as the king who reigned over them.
- **10:18** — The Greek reads "Adoniram" (where the Hebrew reads "Hadoram") and again names "King Jeroboam" rather than "King Rehoboam" as fleeing to Jerusalem.
