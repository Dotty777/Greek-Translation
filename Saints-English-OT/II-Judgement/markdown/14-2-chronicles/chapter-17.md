## Text

**17:1** And Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place, and Jehoshaphat strengthened himself against Israel.

**17:2** And he stationed forces in all the fortified cities of Judah, and appointed governors in all the cities of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim, which Asa his father had previously captured.

**17:3** And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the former ways of his father; and he did not seek the idols,

**17:4** but he sought the Lord, the God of his father, and walked in the commandments of his father, and not according to the deeds of Israel.

**17:5** And the Lord established the kingdom in his hand; and all Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat, and he had riches and great honor.

**17:6** And his heart was lifted up in the way of the Lord, and he removed the high places and the groves from the land of Judah.

**17:7** And in the third year of his reign he sent his leaders and the sons of the mighty men—Abiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in the cities of Judah.

**17:8** And with them were the Levites: Samuas, Manthanias, Zebadiah, Jasiel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonijah, and Tobadobiah, who were Levites; and with them were Elishama and Joram, the priests.

**17:9** And they taught in Judah, and with them was the book of the law of the Lord; and they went throughout the cities of Judah and taught the people.

**17:10** And the dread of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the land around Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.

**17:11** And some of the Philistines brought to Jehoshaphat gifts and silver and presents; and the Arabs brought him seven thousand seven hundred rams of the flocks.

**17:12** And Jehoshaphat continued to grow greater to the very heights, and he built dwellings in Judea and fortified cities.

**17:13** And many works were done in Judea, and men of war, mighty men of strength, were in Jerusalem.

**17:14** And this was their number according to their fathers' houses: of Judah, the captains of thousands—Adnah the chief, and with him three hundred thousand mighty sons of valor;

**17:15** and next to him was Johanan the commander, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand;

**17:16** and next to him was Masaiah the son of Zari, who willingly devoted himself to the Lord, and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of valor.

**17:17** And of Benjamin, a mighty man of valor, Eliada, and with him two hundred thousand archers and shield-bearers;

**17:18** and next to him was Jozabad, and with him one hundred and eighty thousand mighty men of war.

**17:19** These were those who served the king, besides those whom the king placed in the fortified cities throughout all Judea.

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — The Greek renders the Hebrew name Ben-Hail literally as "the sons of the mighty men" and reads the distinct name "Abiah" where the Hebrew reads "Obadiah."
- **17:8** — The Greek preserves the distinct Levite names Samuas, Manthanias, Jasiel, and Tobadobiah (where the Hebrew reads Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Asahel, and two separate names, Tobijah and Tobadonijah).
- **17:11** — The Greek mentions only the seven thousand seven hundred rams and omits the seven thousand seven hundred male goats found in the Hebrew text.
- **17:16** — The Greek reads the distinct name "Masaiah the son of Zari" where the Hebrew reads "Amasiah the son of Zichri."
