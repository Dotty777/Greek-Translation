## Text

**24:1** Joash was seven years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Abia from Beersheba.

**24:2** And Joash did what was right in the sight of the Lord all the days of Jehoiada the priest.

**24:3** And Jehoiada took for himself two wives, and fathered sons and daughters.

**24:4** And it came to pass after these things that it came into the heart of Joash to repair the house of the Lord.

**24:5** And he gathered the priests and the Levites and said to them, "Go out to the cities of Judah, and gather silver from all Israel to strengthen the house of the Lord year by year, and make haste to speak." Yet the Levites did not believe.

**24:6** And King Joash called Jehoiada the chief and said to him, "Why have you not inquired concerning the Levites, to bring in from Judah and Jerusalem what was decreed by Moses the man of God, because he assembled Israel to the tabernacle of the testimony?"

**24:7** For Athaliah was that lawless woman, and her sons tore down the house of God, and indeed they used the holy things of the house of the Lord for the Baals.

**24:8** And the king said, "Let a chest be made and placed outside at the gate of the house of the Lord,

**24:9** and let them proclaim in Judah and in Jerusalem to bring to the Lord, just as Moses the servant of God spoke concerning Israel in the wilderness."

**24:10** And all the rulers and all the people gave, and they brought in and cast into the chest until it was filled.

**24:11** And it came to pass, when they brought the chest to the king's overseers by the hand of the Levites, and when they saw that the silver had become plentiful, the king's scribe and the overseer of the high priest came and emptied the chest, and restored it to its place. Thus they did day by day, and gathered much silver.

**24:12** And the king and Jehoiada the priest gave it to those who did the work for the service of the house of the Lord; and they hired stonecutters and carpenters to repair the house of the Lord, and smiths of iron and bronze to repair the house of the Lord.

**24:13** And those who did the works labored, and the length of the works went up in their hands; and they raised up the house of the Lord to its proper state and strengthened it.

**24:14** And when they had finished, they brought the remainder of the silver to the king and to Jehoiada, and they made vessels for the house of the Lord, vessels of ministry for burnt offerings, and censers of gold and silver. And they offered up burnt offerings in the house of the Lord continually all the days of Jehoiada.

**24:15** And Jehoiada grew old and was full of days, and he died; he was one hundred and thirty years old when he died.

**24:16** And they buried him in the City of David among the kings, because he had done good with Israel, and with God and His house.

**24:17** And it came to pass after the death of Jehoiada that the rulers of Judah came in and bowed down to the king; then the king listened to them.

**24:18** And they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, and served the Astartes and the idols; and wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem on that day.

**24:19** And He sent prophets to them to turn them back to the Lord, and they did not listen; and they testified against them, and they did not obey.

**24:20** And the Spirit of God clothed Azariah the son of Jehoiada the priest, and he stood above the people and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'Why do you transgress the commandments of the Lord? And you shall not prosper; because you have forsaken the Lord, He will also forsake you.'"

**24:21** And they conspired against him, and stoned him at the command of Joash the king in the court of the house of the Lord.

**24:22** And Joash did not remember the mercy which Jehoiada his father had shown to him, and he put his son to death. And as he was dying, he said, "May the Lord see and judge!"

**24:23** And it came to pass after the end of the year that the army of Syria came up against him, and came against Judah and against Jerusalem; and they destroyed all the rulers of the people among the people, and sent all their spoil to the king of Damascus.

**24:24** For the army of Syria came with a few men, yet God delivered a very great army into their hands, because they had forsaken the Lord God of their fathers. And with Joash He executed judgments.

**24:25** And after they departed from him, when they left him in great infirmities, his own servants conspired against him because of the blood of the son of Jehoiada the priest, and put him to death on his bed, and he died. And they buried him in the City of David, but they did not bury him in the tomb of the kings.

**24:26** And those who conspired against him were Zabel the son of Sama the Ammonite, and Zozabed the son of Somaioth the Moabite,

**24:27** and all his sons, and the five came to him. And the rest, behold, they are written in the record of the kings. And Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Septuagint preserves the LXX-distinct name Abia for the mother of Joash (Hebrew MT: Zibiah).
- **24:5** — The Greek reads "make haste to speak" and "Yet the Levites did not believe," differing from the Hebrew MT ("hasten the matter; but the Levites did not hasten").
- **24:13** — "The length of the works went up in their hands" renders the literal Greek wording, where the translator read the Hebrew term for "restoration" or "healing" as "length."
- **24:20** — The Septuagint identifies the priest as Azariah the son of Jehoiada, whereas the Hebrew MT calls him Zechariah.
- **24:26** — The Septuagint preserves the distinct names Zabel son of Sama and Zozabed son of Somaioth (Hebrew MT: Zabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shimrith), and continues directly into verse 27 ("and all his sons, and the five came to him"), where the Greek diverges markedly from the Hebrew MT.
