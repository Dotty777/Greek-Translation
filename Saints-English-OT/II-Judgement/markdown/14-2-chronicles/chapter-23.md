## Text

**23:1** And in the eighth year Jehoiada strengthened himself and took the captains of hundreds—Azariah the son of Joram, Ishmael the son of Johanan, Azariah the son of Obed, Maaseiah the son of Azeiah, and Elishaphan the son of Zechariah—with him into the house.

**23:2** And they went around Judah, and he gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah, and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**23:3** And all the assembly of Judah made a covenant in the house of God with the king. And he showed them the king's son and said to them, "Behold, let the king's son reign, just as the Lord spoke concerning the house of David.

**23:4** Now this is the thing which you shall do: a third of you, of the priests and of the Levites, shall enter on the Sabbath, even to the gates of the entrances,

**23:5** and a third shall be at the king's house, and a third at the middle gate; and all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the Lord.

**23:6** And let no one enter the house of the Lord except the priests and the Levites and those of the Levites who minister; they shall enter, for they are holy; and let all the people keep the watches for the Lord.

**23:7** And the Levites shall surround the king all around, every man with his weapon in his hand; and whoever enters into the house shall die. And they shall be with the king as he goes in and as he goes out."

**23:8** And the Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded. And each man took his men from the beginning of the Sabbath until the close of the Sabbath, for Jehoiada did not dismiss the divisions.

**23:9** And he gave the swords and the large shields and the weapons that belonged to King David in the house of God.

**23:10** And he stationed all the people, each man with his weapons, from the right side of the house to the left side of the altar and of the house, all around the king.

**23:11** And he brought out the king's son, and put upon him the royal crown and the testimonies; and Jehoiada and his sons made him king and anointed him, and said, "Long live the king!"

**23:12** And Athaliah heard the voice of the people running and giving thanks and praising the king, and she entered to the king in the house of the Lord.

**23:13** And she looked, and behold, the king was at his station, and at the entrance were the rulers and the trumpets, rulers around the king; and all the people rejoiced and blew the trumpets, and those singing with the instruments, singers and those chanting praise. And Athaliah tore her robe and cried out, "Conspiring, you conspire!"

**23:14** And Jehoiada the priest went out; and Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of hundreds and the commanders of the army, and said to them, "Cast her outside the house, and go in after her, and let her die by the sword." For the priest said, "Do not let her die in the house of the Lord."

**23:15** And they made way for her, and she passed through the gate of the horsemen of the king's house, and they put her to death there.

**23:16** And Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and the people and the king, that they should be a people for the Lord.

**23:17** And all the people of the land entered the house of Baal and tore it down, together with his altars and his idols, and ground them to dust; and they put Mattan the priest of Baal to death before his altars.

**23:18** And Jehoiada the priest entrusted the works of the house of the Lord to the hand of the priests and Levites, and he established the divisions of the priests and of the Levites, which David had appointed over the house of the Lord, and to offer up burnt offerings to the Lord, as it is written in the Law of Moses, with gladness and with songs, by the hand of David.

**23:19** And the gatekeepers stood at the gates of the house of the Lord, and no one unclean in any matter would enter.

**23:20** And he took the heads of the fathers' houses and the mighty men and the rulers of the people and all the people of the land, and they brought the king up into the house of the Lord; and he entered through the inner gate into the king's house, and they seated the king upon the throne of the kingdom.

**23:21** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and they had put Athaliah to death.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — The Greek text reads "eighth year" (Hebrew MT: "seventh year") and "with him into the house" (MT: "into a covenant with him"), and preserves the LXX-distinct names Azeiah (MT: Adaiah) and Elishaphan son of Zechariah (MT: Elishaphat son of Zichri).
- **23:13** — "Conspiring, you conspire!" literally renders the Greek participle-and-verb construction corresponding to the Hebrew exclamation "Treason! Treason!"
- **23:20** — The Septuagint reads "brought the king up into the house of the Lord" and "the inner gate," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "brought the king down from the house of the Lord" and "the Upper Gate."
