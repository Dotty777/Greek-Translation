## Text

**15:1** Now the Spirit of the Lord came upon Azariah the son of Oded.

**15:2** And he went out to meet them in all Judah and Benjamin, and said, "The Lord is with you while we are with Him; and if you seek Him, He will be found by you; and if you forsake Him, He will forsake you.

**15:3** And for many days Israel has been without a true God and without law.

**15:4** Yet He will turn them back to the Lord God of Israel, and He will be found by them.

**15:5** And in that time there is no peace for the one who goes in and the one who goes out, for terror from the Lord is upon all who dwell in the lands.

**15:6** And nation shall make war against nation and city against city, because God has terrified them with every affliction.

**15:7** But as for you, be strong, and do not let your hands grow weak, for there is a reward for your work."

**15:8** And when he heard these words and the prophecy of Adad the prophet, he took courage and cast out the abominations from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had captured in Mount Ephraim; and he renewed the altar of the Lord that was before the temple of the Lord.

**15:9** And he assembled Judah and Benjamin, and the sojourners who dwelt with him from Ephraim and from Manasseh and from Simeon; for many of Israel had joined themselves to him when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.

**15:10** And they were gathered together to Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

**15:11** And he sacrificed to the Lord on that day from what he had brought, seven hundred calves and seven thousand sheep.

**15:12** And he entered into a covenant to seek the Lord God of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul,

**15:13** and that whoever would not seek the Lord God of Israel should die, from the youngest to the oldest, from man to woman.

**15:14** And they swore an oath by the Lord with a loud voice and with trumpets.

**15:15** And all Judah rejoiced concerning the oath, for they had sworn with all their soul, and with all their desire they sought Him, and He was found by them; and the Lord gave them rest on every side.

**15:16** He also removed Maachah his mother from ministering to Astarte, and he cut down the idol and burned it in the brook Kidron.

**15:17** Yet the high places were not removed; they still remained in Israel; nevertheless, the heart of Asa was whole all his days.

**15:18** And he brought in the consecrated things of David his father and the consecrated things of the house of the Lord God—silver and gold and vessels.

**15:19** And there was no war with him until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Greek reads "while we are with Him" (first person plural) rather than the Hebrew "while you are with Him," and places Judah and Benjamin in the narrative rather than as direct address.
- **15:4** — The Greek renders the verb transitively and in the future tense ("He will turn them back"), omitting the Hebrew phrase "in their distress."
- **15:8** — The Greek names the prophet "Adad" here (contrast "Oded" in v. 1) and reads "the temple of the Lord" where the Hebrew has "the vestibule of the Lord."
- **15:16** — The Greek states that Asa removed Maachah "from ministering to Astarte," differing from the Hebrew "from being queen mother because she had made an abominable image for Asherah."
