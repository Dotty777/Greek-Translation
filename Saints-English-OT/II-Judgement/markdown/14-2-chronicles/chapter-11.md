## Text

**11:1** And Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, and he assembled Judah and Benjamin, one hundred and eighty thousand young men who waged war; and he fought against Israel to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.

**11:2** And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,

**11:3** "Speak to Rehoboam the son of Solomon and to all Judah and Benjamin, saying,

**11:4** 'Thus says the Lord: You shall not go up, nor shall you fight against your brethren. Return every man to his house, for this matter has come from Me.'" And they heeded the word of the Lord, and turned back from marching against Jeroboam.

**11:5** And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built walled cities in Judea.

**11:6** He built Bethseem, and Apan, and Tekoa,

**11:7** and Beth-zur, and Socoh, and Adullam,

**11:8** and Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,

**11:9** and Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,

**11:10** and Zorah, and Aldon, and Hebron, which belongs to Judah and Benjamin, walled cities.

**11:11** And he strengthened them as walled cities, and placed commanders in them, and stores of provisions, oil, and wine,

**11:12** in city after city shields and spears, and he made them exceedingly strong; and Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.

**11:13** And the Levites and the priests who were in all Israel gathered to him from all the borders;

**11:14** for the Levites forsook the dwellings of their possession and went to Judah to Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons were casting them out from ministering to the Lord.

**11:15** And he appointed for himself priests for the high places, and for the idols, and for the vain things, and for the calves that Jeroboam had made.

**11:16** And he cast them out from the tribes of Israel, those who set their heart to seek the Lord God of Israel; and they came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the Lord God of their fathers.

**11:17** And they strengthened the kingdom of Judah, and strengthened Rehoboam the son of Solomon for three years, for he walked in the ways of David and Solomon for three years.

**11:18** And Rehoboam took for himself as a wife Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David, Baia the daughter of Elian the son of Jesse.

**11:19** And she bore him sons: Jaouth, Shemariah, and Roollam.

**11:20** And after this he took for himself Maacah the daughter of Absalom, and she bore him Abijah, Jethei, Ziza, and Emmoth.

**11:21** And Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom above all his wives and his concubines, for he had eighteen wives and thirty concubines; and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**11:22** And he appointed Abijah the son of Maacah as chief, to be leader among his brethren, for he intended to make him king.

**11:23** And he was exalted above all his sons throughout all the borders of Judah and Benjamin and in the fortified cities, and he gave them a great abundance of provisions, and requested a multitude of wives.

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — The Greek text reads Bethseem and Apan where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Bethlehem and Etam.
- **11:10** — The Greek text reads Aldon where the Hebrew reads Aijalon.
- **11:16** — The Greek text reads "and he cast them out from the tribes of Israel," whereas the Hebrew reads "and after them, from all the tribes of Israel."
- **11:18** — The Greek reads Baia the daughter of Elian (omitting "and" before her name), where the Hebrew reads "and Abihail the daughter of Eliab"; in verses 19–20 the Greek similarly preserves distinct forms Jaouth, Roollam, Jethei, and Emmoth.
- **11:21** — The Greek text reads thirty concubines, whereas the Hebrew reads sixty concubines.
- **11:23** — The Greek reads "he was exalted above all his sons... and gave them [the fortified cities] provisions," differing from the Hebrew, which describes Rehoboam acting wisely and dispersing his sons.
