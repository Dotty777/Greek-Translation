## Text

**20:1** And after these things, the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and with them some of the Meunites, came against Jehoshaphat to battle.

**20:2** And they came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, "A great multitude has come against you from beyond the sea, from Syria; and behold, they are in Hasam Thamara, which is En Gedi."

**20:3** And Jehoshaphat was afraid, and set his face to seek the Lord, and they proclaimed a fast.

**20:4** And Judah gathered together to seek the Lord; indeed, from all the cities of Judah they came to seek the Lord.

**20:5** And Jehoshaphat stood up in the assembly of Judah in Jerusalem, in the house of the Lord, before the new court,

**20:6** and said, "O Lord God of our fathers, are You not God in heaven above? You rule over all the kingdoms of the nations, and in Your hand is strength of dominion, and none can withstand You.

**20:7** Did You not, O Lord, utterly destroy the inhabitants of this land from before Israel, and give it to the seed of Abraham Your beloved forever?

**20:8** And they dwelt in it, and built in it a sanctuary for Your name, saying,

**20:9** 'If evils come upon us—sword, judgment, death, or famine—we will stand before this house and before You, for Your name is upon this house, and we will cry out to You out of affliction, and You will hear and save.'

**20:10** And now, behold, the sons of Ammon and Mount Seir, through whom You did not permit Israel to pass when they came out of the land of Egypt—for they turned aside from them and did not destroy them—

**20:11** and now, behold, they set their hand against us to come forth to cast us out from our inheritance which You gave us,

**20:12** O Lord our God, will You not judge among them? For we have no strength to withstand this great multitude that has come against us, and we do not know what we should do to them; rather, our eyes are upon You."

**20:13** And all Judah stood before the Lord, with their little children and their wives.

**20:14** Then upon Oziel the son of Zechariah, of the sons of Eleael the son of Manthanias, the Levite, of the sons of Asaph, the Spirit of the Lord came upon him in the assembly.

**20:15** And he said, "Listen, all Judah and you inhabitants of Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat! Thus says the Lord to you yourselves: 'Do not fear nor be dismayed before this great multitude, for the battle is not yours, but God's.

**20:16** Tomorrow go down against them; behold, they are coming up by the ascent of Hasae, and you shall find them at the end of the stream of the wilderness of Jeriel.

**20:17** It is not for you to fight; understand these things and see the salvation of the Lord with you! Behold also Jerusalem! Do not be dismayed nor fear him; go out to meet them, and the Lord is with you.'"

**20:18** And Jehoshaphat bowed down upon his face, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before the Lord to worship the Lord.

**20:19** And the Levites, of the sons of Kohath and of the sons of Korah, stood up to praise the Lord God of Israel with a loud voice to the heights.

**20:20** And they rose early in the morning and went out into the wilderness of Tekoa; and as they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and cried out, "Hear me, O Judah and you inhabitants of Jerusalem: Trust in the Lord your God, and you shall be established; trust in His prophet, and you shall prosper."

**20:21** And he took counsel with the people, and appointed singers and praisers to give thanks and to praise the holy things as they went out before the army; and he said, "Give thanks to the Lord, for His mercy abides forever."

**20:22** And at the beginning of his praise of thanksgiving, the Lord caused the sons of Ammon to wage war against Moab and Mount Seir, who had come out against Judah, and they were routed.

**20:23** For the sons of Ammon and Moab turned against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to utterly destroy and annihilate them; and when they had made an end of the inhabitants of Seir, they rose up against one another to be utterly destroyed.

**20:24** And Judah came to the watchtower of the wilderness, and looked out and saw the multitude; and behold, they were all dead bodies fallen upon the earth, and no one escaped.

**20:25** And Jehoshaphat and his people came to plunder their spoil, and they found much livestock, and goods, and spoils, and precious vessels, and he took spoil among them; and they were three days plundering the spoil, because it was much.

**20:26** And it came to pass on the fourth day that they assembled in the Valley of Blessing, for there they blessed the Lord; therefore they called the name of that place the Valley of Blessing to this day.

**20:27** Then every man of Judah returned to Jerusalem, with Jehoshaphat leading them, in great gladness, for the Lord had made them rejoice over their enemies.

**20:28** And they entered Jerusalem with harps and with lyres and with trumpets, into the house of the Lord.

**20:29** And the dread of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the earth when they heard that the Lord had fought against the adversaries of Israel.

**20:30** And the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was at peace, for his God gave him rest on every side.

**20:31** And Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah; he was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.

**20:32** And he walked in the ways of his father Asa, and did not turn aside from doing what was upright in the sight of the Lord.

**20:33** Yet the high places still remained, and still the people had not directed their heart to the Lord God of their fathers.

**20:34** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the words of Joshua the son of Hanani, who wrote the book of the king of Israel.

**20:35** And after this Jehoshaphat allied himself with Ahaziah king of Israel, and he acted lawlessly

**20:36** in acting and going to him to build ships to go to Tarshish; and he built ships in Ezion Geber.

**20:37** Then Eleiada the son of Odeia, of Mareshah, prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you have joined in friendship with Ahaziah, the Lord has broken your work, and your ships have been shattered." And he was not able to go to Tarshish.

## Translator Notes

- **20:14** — The Greek text gives the lineage as Oziel son of Zechariah, of the sons of Eleael son of Manthanias, differing from the Hebrew text (Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah).
- **20:16** — "Hasae" and "Jeriel" follow the Greek proper names (corresponding to Ziz and Jeruel in the Hebrew text); similarly, 20:2 reads "Hasam Thamara" (for Hazazon Tamar) and 20:17 reads "Behold also Jerusalem."
- **20:22** — Where the Hebrew text mentions "ambushes," the Greek states that the Lord caused the sons of Ammon to wage war against Moab and Mount Seir.
- **20:34** — The Greek text reads "Joshua" (Iesou) rather than "Jehu," and has the singular "who wrote the book of the king of Israel."
- **20:37** — "Eleiada the son of Odeia" preserves the Greek proper names, corresponding to Eliezer the son of Dodavahu in the Hebrew text.
