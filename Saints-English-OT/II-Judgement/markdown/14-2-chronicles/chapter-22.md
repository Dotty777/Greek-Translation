## Text

**22:1** And the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his place, for the band of raiders that had come upon them—the Arabians, the Alimazoneans—had slain all the older ones. So Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.

**22:2** Ahaziah was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri.

**22:3** He also walked in the way of the house of Ahab, for his mother was a counselor to sin.

**22:4** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, like the house of Ahab; for they were his counselors after the death of his father, to destroy him.

**22:5** And he walked in their counsels, and went with Joram the son of Ahab to battle against Hazael king of Syria at Ramoth Gilead; and the archers struck Joram.

**22:6** And Joram returned to Israel to be healed of the wounds with which the Syrians had struck him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael king of Syria. And Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Joram the son of Ahab in Israel, because he was sick.

**22:7** And from God came the downfall of Ahaziah by his coming to Joram; and when he went out, Joram went out with him to the son of Nimshi, the anointed of the Lord concerning the house of Ahab.

**22:8** And it came to pass, as Jehu was executing judgment upon the house of Ahab, that they found the rulers of Judah and the brothers of Ahaziah who ministered to Ahaziah, and he slew them.

**22:9** And he commanded to seek Ahaziah; and they seized him while he was being healed in Samaria, and brought him to Jehu, and he slew him. And they buried him, for they said, "He is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the Lord with all his heart." And there was no one in the house of Ahaziah to maintain power for the kingdom.

**22:10** Now when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the seed of the kingdom in the house of Judah.

**22:11** But Jehoshabeath took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from the midst of the king's sons who were being put to death, and put him and his nurse in a bedchamber. So Jehoshabeath, the daughter of King Jehoram, the sister of Ahaziah and wife of Jehoiada, hid him; she hid him from the face of Athaliah, so that she did not slay him.

**22:12** And he was hidden with him in the house of God six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — "The Alimazoneans" preserves a unique Greek proper name (Aleimazoneis) where the Hebrew text refers to those who came with the Arabians to the camp.
- **22:2** — The Greek text gives Ahaziah's accession age as twenty years (closer to 2 Kings 8:26), rather than forty-two years as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **22:6** — The Greek text reads "Israel" twice here instead of "Jezreel."
- **22:7** — The Greek text is elliptical here, omitting Jehu's name before "the son of Nimshi" and lacking an explicit verb before "the house of Ahab."
