## Text

**6:1** Then Solomon said, "The Lord said that He would dwell in thick darkness.

**6:2** And I have built a house for Your name, holy to You and prepared to dwell in forever."

**6:3** And the king turned his face and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while all the assembly of Israel stood by.

**6:4** And he said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to David my father, and with His hands has fulfilled it, saying,

**6:5** 'From the day that I brought My people up from the land of Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a house that My name might be there, nor did I choose a man to be a leader over My people Israel;

**6:6** yet I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**6:7** Now it was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**6:8** And the Lord said to David my father, 'Because it was in your heart to build a house for My name, it was well that it was in your heart;

**6:9** nevertheless you shall not build the house, for your son who shall come forth from your loins, he shall build the house for My name.'

**6:10** And the Lord has established this word which He spoke, and I have succeeded in the place of David my father and have sat upon the throne of Israel, as the Lord spoke, and I have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel,

**6:11** and I have placed there the ark, in which is the covenant of the Lord which He made with Israel."

**6:12** Then he stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands;

**6:13** for Solomon had made a bronze platform and set it in the midst of the court of the sanctuary, five cubits its length, five cubits its width, and three cubits its height; and he stood upon it, and fell upon his knees in the presence of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven,

**6:14** and said, "O Lord God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven and upon the earth, keeping the covenant and mercy for Your servants who walk before You with a whole heart.

**6:15** You have kept for Your servant David my father the things which You spoke to him; You spoke with Your mouth and have fulfilled them with Your hands, as it is this day.

**6:16** And now, O Lord God of Israel, keep for Your servant David what You spoke to him, saying, 'There shall not fail you a man before My presence sitting upon the throne of Israel, if only your sons guard their way to walk in My name, as you have walked before Me.'

**6:17** And now, O Lord God of Israel, let Your word be confirmed, I pray, which You spoke to Your servant David.

**6:18** For will God truly dwell with men upon the earth? If heaven and the heaven of heaven will not suffice for You, then what is this house which I have built?

**6:19** Yet look upon the prayer of Your servant and upon my supplication, O Lord God, to listen to the supplication and the prayer before You today,

**6:20** that Your eyes may be open upon this house day and night, toward this place of which You said that Your name would be invoked there, to hear the prayer which Your servant prays toward this place.

**6:21** And hear the supplication of Your servant and of the people of Israel, whatever they pray toward this place; and You, listen in the place of Your dwelling, from heaven, and hear and be merciful.

**6:22** If a man sins against his neighbor, and takes upon him an oath to make him swear, and he comes and swears before the altar in this house,

**6:23** then hear from heaven and act, and judge Your servants, repaying the lawless one and bringing his ways back upon his own head, and justifying the righteous, repaying him according to his righteousness.

**6:24** And if Your people Israel are defeated before the enemy if they sin against You, and they turn back and confess Your name, and pray and make supplication before You in this house,

**6:25** then hear from heaven and forgive the sins of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land which You gave to them and to their fathers.

**6:26** When the heaven is shut up and there is no rain because they sin against You, and they pray toward this place and praise Your name, and turn from their sins because You humble them,

**6:27** then hear from heaven and forgive the sins of Your servants and of Your people Israel, for You will show them the good way in which they shall walk; and send rain upon Your land which You have given to Your people for an inheritance.

**6:28** If famine comes upon the land, if pestilence comes upon the land, if there is blight and mildew, locust and caterpillar; if the enemy oppresses them before their cities, in every plague and every distress,

**6:29** then whatever prayer and whatever supplication is made by any man and by all Your people Israel, when a man knows his own affliction and his own sickness, and spreads out his hands toward this house,

**6:30** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and heal, and render to each man according to his ways, as You know his heart—for You alone know the heart of the sons of men—

**6:31** that they may fear all Your ways all the days that they live upon the face of the land which You gave to our fathers.

**6:32** Moreover, concerning every foreigner who is not of the people of Israel, who comes from a far country for the sake of Your great name and Your mighty hand and Your uplifted arm, when they come and pray toward this place,

**6:33** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and do according to all for which the foreigner calls upon You, that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name, and fear You as does Your people Israel, and know that Your name is called upon this house which I have built.

**6:34** And if Your people go out to battle against their enemies by the way that You shall send them, and they pray to You toward this city which You have chosen and the house which I have built for Your name,

**6:35** then hear from heaven their supplication and their prayer, and maintain their just cause.

**6:36** When they sin against You—for there is no man who will not sin—and You strike them and deliver them before enemies, and their captors carry them away captive to a land of enemies, to a land far or near,

**6:37** and they turn their heart in their land to which they were carried away, and turn back and make supplication to You in their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted lawlessly,'

**6:38** and they return to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of those who took them captive, and pray toward their land which You gave to their fathers, and the city which You have chosen, and the house which I have built for Your name,

**6:39** then hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, their prayer and their supplication, and execute judgments, and be merciful to the people who have sinned against You.

**6:40** Now, O Lord, let Your eyes be open, I pray, and Your ears attentive to the supplication of this place.

**6:41** And now arise, O Lord God, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. Let Your priests, O Lord God, be clothed with salvation, and let Your sons rejoice in good things.

**6:42** O Lord God, do not turn away Your face; remember the mercies of David Your servant."

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Greek reads "a house for Your name, holy to You and prepared to dwell in forever," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "house of habitation."
- **6:6** — The Greek omits the first clause of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Yet I have chosen Jerusalem, that My name may be there"), moving directly to the choice of David.
- **6:16** — The Greek reads "to walk in My name" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to walk in My law."
- **6:30** — The Greek reads "and heal" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and forgive."
- **6:41** — The Greek reads "Your sons" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Your godly ones," and similarly in 6:42 reads "do not turn away Your face" rather than "the face of Your anointed one."
