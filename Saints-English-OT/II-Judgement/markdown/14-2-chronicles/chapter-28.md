## Text

**28:1** Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; and he did not do what was right in the sight of the Lord, like David his father.

**28:2** And he walked according to the ways of the king of Israel; for he also made carved images among their idols.

**28:3** He offered sacrifice in Gaibenthom and caused his children to pass through the fire, along with the abominations of the nations whom the Lord had utterly destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**28:4** And he burned incense upon the high places and upon the housetops and beneath every leafy tree.

**28:5** And the Lord his God delivered him by the hand of the kings of Syria, and struck against him, and they carried away from them a great multitude of captives; for He also delivered him into the hands of the kings of Israel, and struck him with a great blow.

**28:6** And Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, sent into Judah in one day one hundred and twenty thousand men mighty in strength, because they had forsaken the Lord, the God of their fathers.

**28:7** And Zichri, the mighty man of Ephraim, slew Maaseiah the king's son, and Egdreikan the commander of his house, and Elkanah the king's successor.

**28:8** And the sons of Israel took captive from their brethren three hundred thousand—women, sons, and daughters—and they plundered much spoil from them, and brought the spoil to Samaria.

**28:9** And there was the prophet of the Lord there, Oded by name; and he went out to meet the army of those coming to Samaria, and said to them, "Behold, the wrath of the Lord, the God of your fathers, was upon Judah, and He delivered them into your hands, and you have slain among them in wrath; it has reached even to the heavens.

**28:10** And now you propose to acquire the sons of Judah and Jerusalem as male and female servants; behold, is it not for you, with yourselves, to bear witness to the Lord our God?

**28:11** And now hear me, and send back the captives whom you have taken captive of your brethren, for the wrath of the Lord is upon you."

**28:12** And leaders from the sons of Ephraim rose up—Oudeia the son of Johanan, and Zechariah the son of Meshillemoth, and Hezekiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Choad—against those who were coming from the war.

**28:13** And they said to them, "You shall by no means bring the captives here to us, for you propose to bring sin against the Lord upon us, to add to our sins and to our ignorance; for our sin is great, and the wrath of the Lord God is upon Israel."

**28:14** So the warriors left the captives and the spoil before the leaders and all the assembly.

**28:15** And the men who were designated by name rose up and took charge of the captives, and from the spoil they covered all who were naked, and clothed them and put sandals on them, and gave them to eat and to anoint themselves; and they also assisted every feeble one with beasts of burden, and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their brethren, and returned to Samaria.

**28:16** At that time Ahaz sent to the king of Assyria to help him,

**28:17** and in this matter also, because the Edomites had attacked and struck Judah and carried away captives.

**28:18** And the Philistines also raided the cities of the lowland and the south of Judah, and took Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Galero, and Soco with its villages, and Galezo with its villages; and they dwelt there.

**28:19** For the Lord humbled Judah on account of Ahaz king of Judah, because he had departed in apostasy from the Lord.

**28:20** And Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria came against him, and they afflicted him.

**28:21** And Ahaz took the things in the house of the Lord and the things in the house of the king and of the leaders, and gave them to the king of Assyria; yet it was not for their help,

**28:22** but rather for his own affliction. And he continued yet more to depart from the Lord; and the king said,

**28:23** "I will seek out the gods of Damascus who strike me." And he said, "The gods of the king of Syria—they will strengthen them; therefore I will sacrifice to them, and they will help me." And they became a stumbling block to him and to all Israel.

**28:24** And Ahaz removed the vessels of the house of the Lord and cut them in pieces, and shut the doors of the house of the Lord, and made for himself altars in every corner,

**28:25** and in every single city in Judah he made high places to burn incense to foreign gods; and they provoked to anger the Lord, the God of their fathers.

**28:26** Now the rest of his words and his deeds, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**28:27** And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David, because they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel; and Hezekiah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — "Gaibenthom" is the Septuagint's transliteration of the Valley of Ben-Hinnom; the Greek also reads "and the abominations" (rendered "along with the abominations") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "according to the abominations."
- **28:6** — The Greek text reads "sent" (apesteilen), a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek witnesses read "slew" (apekteinen).
- **28:8** — The Septuagint specifies "three hundred thousand" captives, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "two hundred thousand."
- **28:10** — The Greek reads "to bear witness to the Lord our God," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to "guilt" against God.
- **28:18** — The Septuagint lists "Galero" and "Galezo" (omitting Timnah) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Gederoth and Gimzo; similarly, distinctive Greek personal names appear in 28:7 ("Egdreikan") and 28:12 ("Oudeia," "Zechariah," and "Choad").
