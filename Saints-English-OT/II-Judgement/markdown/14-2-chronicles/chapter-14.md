## Text

**14:1** And he did what was good and right before the Lord his God.

**14:2** And he removed the altars of the foreigners and the high places, and shattered the pillars, and cut down the groves,

**14:3** and he told Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to do the law and the commandments.

**14:4** And he removed from all the cities of Judah the altars and the idols, and he had peace.

**14:5** There were fortified cities in the land of Judah, for the land was at peace; and he had no war in those years, because the Lord gave him rest.

**14:6** And he said to Judah, "Let us build these cities, and make walls and towers, gates and bars; we shall rule before the land, for just as we sought the Lord our God, He has sought us out, and has given us rest on every side, and has prospered us."

**14:7** And Asa had an army of armed men bearing large shields and spears in the land of Judah, three hundred thousand; and in the land of Benjamin, men bearing small shields and archers, two hundred and fifty thousand; all these were mighty men of war.

**14:8** And Zerah the Ethiopian came out against them with an army, with thousands and with three hundred chariots, and he came as far as Marisel.

**14:9** And Asa went out to meet him, and drew up for battle in the valley to the north of Mareshah.

**14:10** And Asa cried out to the Lord his God and said, "Lord, it is not impossible with You to save with many or with few; strengthen us, O Lord our God, for we have trusted in You, and in Your name we have come against this great multitude. O Lord our God, let no man prevail against You."

**14:11** And the Lord struck the Ethiopians before Judah, and the Ethiopians fled.

**14:12** And Asa and his people pursued them as far as Gedor; and the Ethiopians fell so that there was no survival among them, for they were shattered before the Lord and before His army; and they carried off much spoil.

**14:13** And they destroyed their villages around Gedor, for terror from the Lord came upon them; and they plundered all its cities, for much spoil fell to them.

**14:14** And moreover they destroyed the tents of livestock, the Amazoneis, and took many sheep and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The Greek text lacks a verb before "fortified cities" (likely omitted by scribal eye-skip after "had peace" in v. 4), where the Hebrew and some manuscripts supply "he built."
- **14:8** — The Greek reads simply "with thousands" rather than the Hebrew "a thousand thousands" (one million), and renders the town name as Marisel (contrast Mareshah in v. 9).
- **14:12** — The Greek reads "Gedor" here and in v. 13, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Gerar."
- **14:14** — The Greek adds "the Amazoneis" in apposition to "the tents of livestock," a distinctive Septuagint rendering differing from the Hebrew text.
