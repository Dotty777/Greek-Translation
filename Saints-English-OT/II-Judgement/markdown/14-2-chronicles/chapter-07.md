## Text

**7:1** And when Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offerings and the sacrifices, and the glory of the Lord filled the house.

**7:2** And the priests could not enter into the house of the Lord at that time, because the glory of the Lord had filled the house.

**7:3** And all the sons of Israel saw the fire coming down, and the glory of the Lord upon the house; and they fell on their faces to the ground upon the stone pavement, and worshiped and praised the Lord, saying, "For it is good, for His mercy endures forever."

**7:4** And the king and all the people were offering sacrifices before the Lord.

**7:5** And Solomon offered the sacrifice of twenty-two thousand calves; so the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.

**7:6** And the priests were standing at their posts, and the Levites with the instruments of the songs of the Lord belonging to King David, to give thanks before the Lord, because His mercy endures forever, in the hymns of David by their hand; and the priests were sounding the trumpets before them, and all Israel was standing.

**7:7** And Solomon consecrated the middle of the court that was in the house of the Lord; for there he offered the burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar which Solomon had made was not able to receive the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the fat.

**7:8** And Solomon kept the feast at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly from the entrance of Hamath to the brook of Egypt.

**7:9** And on the eighth day he held a solemn closing assembly, for he kept the dedication of the altar as a feast for seven days.

**7:10** And on the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people away to their tents, rejoicing and glad of heart for the good things which the Lord had done for David, for Solomon, and for Israel His people.

**7:11** Thus Solomon finished the house of the Lord and the house of the king; and all that Solomon had purposed in his soul to do in the house of the Lord and in his own house prospered.

**7:12** And God appeared to Solomon by night, and said to him, "I have heard your prayer, and have chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice.

**7:13** If I shut up heaven and there is no rain, or if I command the locust to devour the trees, or if I send pestilence among My people,

**7:14** and if My people, upon whom My name has been invoked, are humbled and pray and seek My face, and turn back from their evil ways, then I Myself will hear from heaven, and be merciful to their sins, and heal their land.

**7:15** Now My eyes shall be open and My ears attentive to the prayer of this place.

**7:16** And now I have chosen and consecrated this house, that My name may be there forever; and My eyes and My heart shall be there all the days.

**7:17** And as for you, if you walk before Me as David your father did, and do according to all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**7:18** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom, as I covenanted with David your father, saying, 'There shall not be cut off from you a man ruling in Israel.'

**7:19** But if you turn away and forsake My statutes and My commandments which I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them,

**7:20** then I will uproot you from the land which I gave to them; and this house which I have consecrated for My name I will turn away from My presence, and I will make it a proverb and a byword among all the nations.

**7:21** And as for this lofty house, everyone who passes by it will be astonished and say, 'For what reason has the Lord done so to this land and to this house?'

**7:22** And they will say, 'Because they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and laid hold of other gods, and worshiped them and served them; therefore He has brought all this calamity upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek uses the neuter "For it is good" (indicating that praising the Lord is good) rather than the masculine "For He is good."
- **7:5** — The Greek mentions only twenty-two thousand calves and omits the one hundred twenty thousand sheep found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:7** — "The grain offerings" renders the Greek transliterated loanword manaa (from Hebrew minhah).
- **7:13** — The Greek reads "to devour the trees" (literally "the wood") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "to devour the land."
- **7:20** — The Greek shifts to the third person ("which I gave to them") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the second person ("which I gave to you").
