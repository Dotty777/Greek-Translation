## Text

**32:1** And after these matters and this faithfulness, Sennacherib king of the Assyrians came, and he came against Judah and encamped against the walled cities, and purposed to seize them.

**32:2** And Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come, and that his face was set to wage war against Jerusalem.

**32:3** And he took counsel with his elders and the mighty men to stop up the waters of the springs that were outside the city, and they joined to strengthen him.

**32:4** And he gathered many people and stopped up the waters of the springs and the river that runs through the city, saying, "Lest the king of Assyria come and find much water and prevail."

**32:5** And Hezekiah strengthened himself and rebuilt all the wall that had been broken down, and towers, and another outer wall outside, and he strengthened the bastion of the city of David, and prepared many weapons.

**32:6** And he appointed captains of war over the people, and they were gathered to him in the open square of the Valley Gate, and he spoke to their heart, saying,

**32:7** "Be strong and act courageously; do not be terrified before the king of Assyria, nor before all the nation that is with him, for there are more with us than with him.

**32:8** With him are arms of flesh, but with us is the Lord our God, to save and to fight our battle." And the people took courage at the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.

**32:9** And after this Sennacherib king of the Assyrians sent his servants to Jerusalem—while he himself was against Lachish, and all the host with him—and he sent to Hezekiah king of Judah and to all Judah who were in Jerusalem, saying,

**32:10** "Thus says the king of the Assyrians: In what do you trust? Will you remain under siege in Jerusalem?

**32:11** Does Hezekiah not deceive you, to deliver you over to death and to famine and to thirst, saying, 'The Lord our God will save you from the hand of the king of Assyria'?

**32:12** This is Hezekiah who removed His altars and His high places, and spoke to Judah and to those who dwell in Jerusalem, saying, 'Before this altar you shall worship, and upon it you shall burn incense.'

**32:13** Do you not know what I and my fathers did to all the peoples of the lands? Were the gods of the nations of all the earth at all able to save their people from my hand?

**32:14** Who among all the gods of these nations whom my fathers utterly destroyed? Were they able to save their people from my hand, that your God should be able to save you from my hand?

**32:15** Now do not let Hezekiah deceive you, and do not let him cause you to trust in this manner, and do not believe him; for no god of any nation and kingdom shall by any means be able to save his people from my hand and from the hand of my fathers; for your God shall by no means save you from my hand."

**32:16** And his servants spoke still further against the Lord God and against Hezekiah His servant.

**32:17** And he wrote a letter to reproach the Lord God of Israel, and spoke concerning Him, saying, "Just as the gods of the nations of the earth did not deliver their peoples from my hand, so the God of Hezekiah shall by no means deliver His people from my hand."

**32:18** And he cried out with a loud voice in the Judean tongue to the people of Jerusalem who were on the wall, to help them and to pull down, in order that they might seize the city.

**32:19** And he spoke against the God of Jerusalem even as against the gods of the peoples of the earth, the works of the hands of men.

**32:20** And Hezekiah the king and Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, prayed concerning these things and cried out to heaven.

**32:21** And the Lord sent an angel; he wiped out every mighty man and warrior and leader and commander in the camp of the king of Assyria. And he returned with shame of face to his own land. And he went into the house of his god, and some of those who had come forth from his own body struck him down with the sword.

**32:22** And the Lord saved Hezekiah and those who dwelt in Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib king of Assyria and from the hand of all, and He gave them rest on every side.

**32:23** And many brought gifts to the Lord to Jerusalem and presents to Hezekiah king of Judah, and he was greatly exalted in the sight of all the nations after these things.

**32:24** In those days Hezekiah fell sick even to death, and he prayed to the Lord; and He heard him and gave him a sign.

**32:25** And Hezekiah did not repay according to the benefit that He had given him, but his heart was lifted up; and wrath came upon him and upon Judah and Jerusalem.

**32:26** And Hezekiah humbled himself from the loftiness of his heart, both he and those who dwelt in Jerusalem, and the wrath of God did not come upon them in the days of Hezekiah.

**32:27** And Hezekiah had exceedingly great wealth and glory; and he made for himself treasuries for silver and gold and precious stones, and for spices, and armories, and for desirable vessels,

**32:28** and cities for the produce of grain and oil and wine, and stalls for every kind of livestock, and folds for the flocks,

**32:29** and cities which he built for himself, and possessions of sheep and cattle in abundance, for the Lord gave him exceedingly great possessions.

**32:30** Hezekiah himself stopped up the upper outlet of the water of Zion, and directed them straight down to the west of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.

**32:31** And thus, concerning the envoys of the rulers from Babylon who were sent to him to inquire of him about the wonder that had taken place in the land, the Lord forsook him to test him, to know the things that were in his heart.

**32:32** And the rest of the acts of Hezekiah and his mercy, behold, they are written in the prophecy of Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, and in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**32:33** And Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the ascent of the tombs of the sons of David; and all Judah and those who dwelt in Jerusalem gave him glory and honor at his death. And Manasseh his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **32:18** — The Greek reads "to help them and to pull down" (boethesai autois kai kataspasai), likely an early inner-Greek scribal shift from phobesai ("to frighten"), corresponding to "to frighten them and terrify them" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **32:28** — The Greek reads "cities" (poleis) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "storehouses."
- **32:30** — The Greek reads "Zion" (Seion) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Gihon."
- **32:31** — The Greek reads "the Lord" (Kyrios) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "God."
