## Text

**26:1** Then all the people of the land took Ahaziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah.

**26:2** He built Elath; he restored it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.

**26:3** Uzziah was sixteen years old, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Chaia of Jerusalem.

**26:4** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Amaziah had done.

**26:5** And he sought the Lord throughout all the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the fear of the Lord; and in his days he sought the Lord, and the Lord prospered him.

**26:6** And he went forth and made war against the Philistines, and broke down the walls of Gath and the walls of Abner and the walls of Ashdod, and built cities of Ashdod and among the Philistines.

**26:7** And the Lord strengthened him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians who dwelt upon the rock, and against the Meunites.

**26:8** And the Meunites gave gifts to Uzziah, and his name reached even to the entrance of Egypt, for he grew exceedingly strong.

**26:9** Moreover Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem both at the Corner Gate and at the gate of the corner of the valley and at the corners, and fortified them.

**26:10** He also built towers in the wilderness and hewed out many cisterns, for he had much livestock both in the Shephelah and in the plain, and vinedressers in the hill country and on Carmel, for he was a husbandman.

**26:11** And Uzziah had armies that made war and went forth to battle array by number; and their numbering was by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Amasai the judge, under the hand of Hananiah the king's deputy.

**26:12** The entire number of the chief fathers among the mighty men of war was two thousand six hundred.

**26:13** And with them was a fighting army of three hundred and seven thousand five hundred; these were the ones who made war with mighty power to help the king against the adversaries.

**26:14** And Uzziah prepared for them, for all the army, shields and spears and helmets and breastplates and bows and slings for stones.

**26:15** And he made in Jerusalem engines devised by a skillful designer, to be on the towers and on the corners, to shoot arrows and great stones; and their construction was heard of far abroad, for he was marvelously helped until he became strong.

**26:16** But when he became strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction; and he transgressed against the Lord his God, and entered into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense.

**26:17** So Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him eighty priests of the Lord, valiant men.

**26:18** And they withstood King Uzziah and said to him, "It is not for you, Uzziah, to offer sacrifice to the Lord, but for the priests, the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated to offer sacrifice. Go out of the sanctuary, for you have departed from the Lord; and this shall not be for your glory from the Lord God."

**26:19** Then Uzziah became furious, and in his hand was the censer to burn incense in the temple; and while he was furious with the priests, leprosy broke out on his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord, and above the altar of incense.

**26:20** And the chief priest and the priests turned toward him, and behold, he was leprous on his forehead; and they hurried him out from there, and indeed he himself hastened to go out, because the Lord had rebuked him.

**26:21** And King Uzziah was a leper until the day of his death, and he dwelt in a separate house as a leper, because he was cut off from the house of the Lord; and Jotham his son was over his kingdom, judging the people of the land.

**26:22** Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, from first to last, are written by Jesse the prophet.

**26:23** So Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial of the kings, for they said, "He is a leper." Then Jotham his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek text names the new king Ahaziah (Ochozeias) in this verse, whereas from verse 3 onward it calls him Uzziah (Ozeias).
- **26:3** — The Greek gives Uzziah's mother's name as Chaia (Chaaia), corresponding to Jecoliah in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **26:6** — The Greek reads Abner (Abenner) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the Philistine city of Jabneh.
- **26:21** — "A separate house" renders the Greek en oiko aphphousion, which transliterates the Hebrew term for a house of isolation or seclusion.
- **26:22** — The Greek attributes this record to "Jesse the prophet" (Iesseiou), reflecting an early variant for "Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
