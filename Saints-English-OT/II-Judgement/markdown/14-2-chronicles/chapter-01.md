## Text

**1:1** And Solomon the son of David grew strong over his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him and exalted him on high.

**1:2** And Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and to the commanders of hundreds, and to the judges, and to all the rulers before Israel, the rulers of the fathers' houses.

**1:3** And Solomon and all the assembly went to the high place that was in Gibeon, where the tabernacle of testimony of God was, which Moses the servant of the Lord had made in the wilderness.

**1:4** But David had brought up the ark of God from the city of Kiriath-jearim, because he had prepared a tent for it in Jerusalem.

**1:5** And the bronze altar that Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, had made was there before the tabernacle of the Lord; and Solomon and the assembly sought it.

**1:6** And Solomon offered there upon the bronze altar before the Lord, which was in the tabernacle, and he offered upon it a thousand burnt offerings.

**1:7** In that night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I shall give you."

**1:8** And Solomon said to God, "What great mercy You have shown to David my father, and You have made me king in his place.

**1:9** And now, O Lord God, let Your name be confirmed to David my father, for You have made me king over a numerous people, like the dust of the earth.

**1:10** Now give me wisdom and understanding, and I shall go out before this people and come in; for who shall judge this great people of Yours?"

**1:11** And God said to Solomon, "Because this was in your heart, and you have not asked for wealth of possessions, nor glory, nor the life of adversaries, and have not asked for many days, but have asked for wisdom and understanding for yourself, that you may judge My people over whom I have made you king,

**1:12** wisdom and understanding I give to you; and wealth and possessions and glory I will give to you, such that none like you has been among the kings before you, nor after you shall there be the like."

**1:13** And Solomon came from Maba that is in Gibeon, that is in Jerusalem, from before the tabernacle of testimony, and he reigned over Israel.

**1:14** And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen, and he had one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen; and he left them in the cities of the chariots, and the people were with the king in Jerusalem.

**1:15** And the king made gold and silver in Jerusalem like stones, and cedars in Judea like the sycamore trees that are in the lowland in abundance.

**1:16** And the bringing forth of the horsemen for Solomon was from Egypt, and the price of the king's merchants: they went and bought them.

**1:17** And they went up and brought out from Egypt one chariot for six hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty; and thus they brought them by their hands for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Syria.

**1:18** And Solomon resolved to build a house for the name of the Lord and a house for his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **1:9** — The Greek text reads "Your name" (to onoma sou) rather than the Masoretic Text's "Your word."
- **1:13** — The Greek text transliterates and transposes the Hebrew word for "high place" (bamah) as the place-name "Maba," and reads "that is in Jerusalem" rather than "to Jerusalem."
- **1:14** — The Greek text reads "and the people were with the king in Jerusalem," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants for "with" and "people."
- **1:16** — The Greek text interprets the obscure Hebrew term (often understood as the region Kue or a drove of horses) as "price" (time) and renders the closing clause literally.
