## Text

**25:1** Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Jonaa of Jerusalem.

**25:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, yet not with a full heart.

**25:3** Now it came to pass, when the kingdom was established in his hand, that he put to death his servants who had murdered the king his father.

**25:4** Yet their sons he did not slay, according to the covenant of the law of the Lord as it is written, as the Lord commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die for children, nor shall sons die for fathers, but each one shall die for his own sin."

**25:5** Then Amaziah gathered the house of Judah together and appointed them according to their fathers' houses, under captains of thousands and captains of hundreds throughout all Judah and Jerusalem; and he numbered them from twenty years old and upward, and found them to be three hundred thousand, mighty men able to go forth to war, holding spear and shield.

**25:6** He also hired from Israel one hundred thousand mighty men of valor for one hundred talents of silver.

**25:7** And a man of God came to him, saying, "O king, the army of Israel shall not go with you, for the Lord is not with Israel, with all the sons of Ephraim.

**25:8** For if you suppose to prevail by these, then the Lord will put you to flight before the enemies; for it is from the Lord both to prevail and to put to flight."

**25:9** And Amaziah said to the man of God, "And what shall I do about the hundred talents which I gave to the army of Israel?" And the man of God said, "The Lord is able to give you far more than these."

**25:10** So Amaziah separated the army that had come to him from Ephraim, to depart to their own place; and they grew exceedingly angry against Judah, and returned to their own place in fierce wrath.

**25:11** And Amaziah strengthened himself and took his people, and went to the Valley of Salt, and struck down there ten thousand of the sons of Seir.

**25:12** And ten thousand the sons of Judah captured alive, and brought them to the top of the cliff, and cast them down from the top of the cliff, so that they were all dashed to pieces.

**25:13** But the men of the army whom Amaziah had sent back so that they would not be found with him in battle fell upon the cities of Judah from Samaria to Beth Horon, and struck down three thousand among them and carried off great spoil.

**25:14** Now it came to pass, after Amaziah returned from striking down Idumea, that he brought to them the gods of the sons of Seir, and set them up as gods for himself, and stood before them to worship, and himself sacrificed to them.

**25:15** Therefore the wrath of the Lord came upon Amaziah, and He sent prophets to him, and they said to him, "Why have you sought the gods of the people, which did not deliver their own people from your hand?"

**25:16** And it came to pass, as he spoke to him, that he said to him, "Have I appointed you counselor to the king? Take heed, lest you be scourged." Then the prophet kept silent, and said, "I know that He has purposed against you to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."

**25:17** Then Amaziah took counsel.

**25:18** And Joash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thistle in Lebanon sent to the cedar in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' And behold, the wild beasts of the field that are in Lebanon shall come; and the wild beasts came and trampled down the thistle.

**25:19** You have said, 'Behold, you have struck down Idumea,' and your heavy heart lifts you up. Now stay in your house; and why do you meddle in evil, so that you shall fall, you and Judah with you?"

**25:20** But Amaziah did not listen, for it came from the Lord, to deliver him into their hands, because he had sought the gods of the Idumeans.

**25:21** So Joash king of Israel went up, and they faced one another, he and Amaziah king of Judah, at Beth Shemesh, which belongs to Judah.

**25:22** And Judah was routed before Israel, and every man fled to his tent.

**25:23** Then Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, the son of Joash, at Beth Shemesh, and brought them to Jerusalem, and broke down four hundred cubits of the wall of Jerusalem, from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate.

**25:24** And he took all the gold and the silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord and with Obed-Edom, and the treasures of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

**25:25** And Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash the son of Joash, king of Israel.

**25:26** Now the rest of the acts of Amaziah, from first to last, behold, are they not written in the book of Judah and Israel?

**25:27** And from the time that Amaziah turned away from the Lord, they formed a conspiracy against him, and he fled from Jerusalem to Lachish; but they sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

**25:28** And they brought him back upon horses and buried him with his fathers in the City of David.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — The Greek text gives Amaziah's mother's name as Jonaa (Ionaa), corresponding to Jehoaddan in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **25:8** — The Greek reads "if you suppose to prevail by these, then the Lord will put you to flight," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's ironic command to go and be strong in battle.
- **25:17** — The Greek text preserves only this brief clause, omitting Amaziah's message challenging Joash king of Israel found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **25:18** — "Thistle" translates two Greek transliterations (chozei and achouch) of the Hebrew word for thornbush or thistle; the Greek also includes a future-tense clause before the wild beasts trample it.
- **25:25** — The Greek identifies the king of Israel as "Joash the son of Joash," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Joash the son of Jehoahaz."
