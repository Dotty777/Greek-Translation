## Text

**2:1** And Solomon gathered seventy thousand men, and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain, and the overseers over them were three thousand six hundred.

**2:2** And Solomon sent to Hiram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with David my father and sent him cedars to build himself a house to dwell in,

**2:3** so behold, I his son am building a house for the name of the Lord my God, to sanctify it to Him, to burn incense before Him, and for the continual showbread, and to offer burnt offerings continually in the morning and in the evening, and on the Sabbaths and on the new moons and on the feasts of the Lord our God; this is forever upon Israel.

**2:4** And the house that I am building is great, for great is our God above all the gods.

**2:5** And who is able to build Him a house, since heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot bear His glory? And who am I, building Him a house, except only to burn incense before Him?

**2:6** And now send me a wise man who knows how to work in gold and in silver, in bronze and in iron, and in purple and in scarlet, and who understands how to engrave engravings with the wise men who are with me in Judah and in Jerusalem, which David my father prepared.

**2:7** And send me cedar, juniper, and pine timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut timber from Lebanon; and behold, your servants with my servants

**2:8** shall go to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house that I am building is great and glorious.

**2:9** And behold, to the workmen who cut the timber I have given wheat for food as gifts to your servants, twenty thousand kors, and twenty thousand kors of barley, and twenty thousand measures of wine."

**2:10** And Hiram king of Tyre spoke in writing and sent to Solomon, "Because the Lord loved His people, He has made you king over them."

**2:11** And Hiram said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given to David a wise son, versed in understanding and knowledge, who shall build a house for the Lord and a house for his kingdom.

**2:12** And now I have sent to you a wise man, one who knows understanding, Hiram my father;

**2:13** his mother is from the daughters of Dan, and his father is a man of Tyre; he knows how to work in gold and in silver, and in bronze and in iron, in stones and timber, and to weave in purple and in blue and in fine linen and in scarlet, and to engrave engravings, and to devise every design—whatever you may give to him—along with your wise men and the wise men of David my lord, your father.

**2:14** And now the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine, of which my lord has spoken, let him send to his servants.

**2:15** And we will cut timber from Lebanon according to all your need, and we will bring it on rafts to the sea of Joppa, and you shall bring it to Jerusalem."

**2:16** And Solomon gathered all the men who were sojourners in the land of Israel, after the numbering with which David his father had numbered them; and there were found one hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.

**2:17** And he appointed from among them seventy thousand burden-bearers, and eighty thousand stonecutters, and three thousand six hundred over the people.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Greek text adds "His glory" (autou ten doxan), stating that the heavens cannot bear God's glory.
- **2:9** — Unlike the Masoretic Text, the Greek text omits oil from Solomon's list of provisions here, though Hiram mentions oil in verse 14.
- **2:12** — "Hiram my father" renders the Greek literally, reflecting the Hebrew honorific title Huram-abi (denoting a master craftsman or esteemed counselor).
- **2:16** — "Sojourners" translates Greek proselytous (proselytes), the standard Septuagint rendering of Hebrew gerim (resident foreigners).
