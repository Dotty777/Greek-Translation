## Text

**33:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.

**33:2** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, after all the abominations of the nations whom the Lord utterly destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**33:3** And he returned and built the high places which Hezekiah his father had torn down, and he set up pillars for the Baals and made sacred groves, and he worshiped all the host of heaven and served them.

**33:4** And he built altars in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord had said, "In Jerusalem My name shall be forever."

**33:5** And he built altars to all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.

**33:6** And he himself caused his children to pass through fire in Ge-Bane-Hinnom; and he practiced soothsaying and sorcery and augury, and he appointed mediums and enchanters; he multiplied doing evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**33:7** And he set the carved and molten image, the likeness which he had made, in the house of God, of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put My name forever.

**33:8** And I will not again dislodge the foot of Israel from the land which I gave to their fathers, only if they take care to do all that I have commanded them, according to all the law, the statutes, and the judgments by the hand of Moses."

**33:9** And Manasseh led Judah and those who dwelt in Jerusalem astray, to do evil more than all the nations whom the Lord had removed from before the sons of Israel.

**33:10** And the Lord spoke against Manasseh and against his people, and he did not listen.

**33:11** And the Lord brought upon them the captains of the army of the king of Assyria, and they seized Manasseh in bonds and bound him in fetters and led him to Babylon.

**33:12** And when he was afflicted, he sought the face of the Lord his God, and humbled himself greatly before the God of his fathers;

**33:13** and he prayed to Him, and He heard him; and He heard his cry, and brought him back to Jerusalem to his kingdom. And Manasseh knew that the Lord, He is God.

**33:14** And after these things he built a wall outside the city of David on the west along Gihon in the valley, and at the exit of the gate round about, and even to Ophel itself, and he raised it exceedingly high; and he appointed captains of the army in all the walled cities in Judah.

**33:15** And he removed the foreign gods and the carved image from the house of the Lord, and all the altars which he had built on the mountain of the house of the Lord and in Jerusalem and outside the city.

**33:16** And he restored the altar of the Lord, and sacrificed upon it a sacrifice of deliverance and praise, and he told Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel.

**33:17** Nevertheless, the people were still on the high places; yet the Lord was their God.

**33:18** And the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer to God, and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of the God of Israel,

**33:19** behold, they are in the words of his prayer, and He heard him; and all his sins and his apostasies, and the places upon which he built the high places and set up sacred groves and carved images there before he turned back, behold, they are written in the words of the seers.

**33:20** And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the garden of his house; and Amos his son reigned in his place.

**33:21** Amos was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.

**33:22** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasseh his father had done; and to all the idols which Manasseh his father had made, Amos sacrificed and served them.

**33:23** And he did not humble himself before the Lord as Manasseh his father had humbled himself, for his son Amos multiplied trespass.

**33:24** And his servants set upon him and struck him down in his house.

**33:25** And the people of the land struck down those who had set upon King Amos; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — "Ge-Bane-Hinnom" preserves the Septuagint transliteration (ge bane Hennom) of the Hebrew phrase meaning "the valley of the sons of Hinnom."
- **33:14** — The Greek reads "at the exit of the gate round about, and even to Ophel [Greek Opla] itself," omitting "Fish" before "Gate" as found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **33:17** — The Greek is elliptical, omitting the verb "sacrificed" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and placing "the Lord their God" in the nominative case.
- **33:19** — The Greek reads "in the words of the seers" (ton horonton) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the chronicles of Hozai."
- **33:20** — Here and throughout verses 21-25, the Greek names Manasseh's son "Amos" (Amos) rather than "Amon" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
