## Text

**4:1** And he made the altar of bronze, twenty cubits in length, and the width twenty cubits, ten cubits in height.

**4:2** And he made the molten sea, ten cubits in diameter, round all around, and five cubits in height, and thirty cubits in circumference.

**4:3** And beneath it was a likeness of calves; all around they surround it, ten cubits encompass the laver all around; they cast the calves in two kinds in their casting,

**4:4** by which they made them twelve calves: three facing north, and three west, and three south, and three toward the east; and the sea was upon them above, and their hindquarters were inward.

**4:5** And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim was like the brim of a cup, carved with buds of a lily, holding three thousand measures; and he finished it.

**4:6** And he made ten lavers, and placed five on the right and five on the left, to wash in them the works of the burnt offerings and to rinse in them; and the sea was for the priests to wash themselves in it.

**4:7** And he made the ten golden lampstands according to their ordinance, and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left.

**4:8** And he made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left; and he made one hundred golden bowls.

**4:9** And he made the court of the priests and the great court, and doors for the court, and their doorframes overlaid with bronze.

**4:10** And he placed the sea at the corner of the house on the right side, facing opposite toward the east.

**4:11** And Hiram made the fleshhooks, and the firepans, and the grate of the altar, and all its vessels. And Hiram finished doing all the work which he made for King Solomon in the house of God:

**4:12** two pillars, and upon them the bowls for the capital upon the tops of the two pillars, and two networks to cover the tops of the capitals which were upon the tops of the pillars,

**4:13** and four hundred golden bells for the two networks, and kinds of pomegranates on each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals which were above the pillars.

**4:14** And he made the ten stands, and he made the lavers upon the stands,

**4:15** and the one sea, and the twelve calves beneath it,

**4:16** and the footed pots, and the scoops, and the cauldrons, and the fleshhooks, and all their vessels, which Hiram made and brought up to King Solomon in the house of the Lord, of pure bronze.

**4:17** In the plain of the Jordan the king cast them, in the thickness of the ground, in the house of Succoth and Anamesirdathai.

**4:18** And Solomon made all these vessels in exceedingly great abundance, for the weight of the bronze did not fail.

**4:19** And Solomon made all the vessels of the house of the Lord, and the golden altar, and the tables, and upon them the loaves of showbread,

**4:20** and the lampstands and the lamps of light according to the ordinance, and before the inner sanctuary, of pure gold,

**4:21** and their tongs and their lamps, and the bowls, and the incense dishes, and the firepans, of pure gold,

**4:22** and the inner door of the house into the Holy of Holies, for the golden doors of the house of the temple. And all the work which Solomon made in the house of the Lord was finished.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Greek reads "ten cubits encompass the laver all around" (where the Hebrew has "ten to a cubit") and uses gene ("kinds") where the Hebrew has "rows."
- **4:12** — Here and in verses 13, 14, and 20, the Greek retains transliterated Hebrew architectural terms (golath "bowls," chothareth "capitals," mechonoth "stands," and dabeir "inner sanctuary"), and specifies "the tops of the capitals."
- **4:13** — The Greek reads "four hundred golden bells" (where the Hebrew has "four hundred pomegranates") and refers to "kinds of pomegranates" without specifying "two."
- **4:17** — The Greek reads "in the house of Succoth and Anamesirdathai," a distinctive Septuagint form corresponding to the Hebrew "between Succoth and Zeredathah."
- **4:22** — The concluding clause ("And all the work which Solomon made in the house of the Lord was finished") stands at the end of chapter 4 in the Greek, corresponding to the beginning of 5:1 in the Hebrew.
