## Text

**35:1** Now Josiah kept the Passover to the Lord his God; and they slaughtered the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**35:2** And he appointed the priests to their watches, and encouraged them for the works of the house of the Lord.

**35:3** And he spoke to the Levites who were capable in all Israel, that they should sanctify themselves to the Lord; and they placed the holy ark in the house which Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, had built. And the king said, "It is not for you to bear anything upon your shoulders; now therefore minister to the Lord your God and to His people Israel,

**35:4** and prepare yourselves according to your fathers' houses and according to your divisions, according to the writing of David king of Israel and by the hand of King Solomon his son;

**35:5** and stand in the house according to the divisions of your fathers' houses for your brethren, the sons of the people, and a portion of a father's house for the Levites;

**35:6** and slaughter the Passover, and prepare for your brethren, to do according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses."

**35:7** And Josiah contributed to the sons of the people sheep and lambs and kids from the young of the goats, all for the Passover, and for all who were found present, to the number of thirty thousand, and three thousand calves; these were from the king's possessions.

**35:8** And his rulers contributed to the people, and to the priests and the Levites. Hilkiah and Zechariah and Jehiel, the rulers of the house of God, gave to the priests, and they gave for the Passover two thousand six hundred sheep and lambs and kids, and three hundred calves.

**35:9** And Conaniah, and Benaiah, and Shemaiah, and Nethanel his brother, and Hashabiah, and Joel, and Jozabad, rulers of the Levites, contributed to the Levites for the Passover five thousand, and five hundred calves.

**35:10** So the service was set in order, and the priests stood in their place, and the Levites in their divisions, according to the command of the king.

**35:11** And they slaughtered the Passover; and the priests poured out the blood from their hand, while the Levites skinned them.

**35:12** And they prepared the burnt offering to deliver it to them according to the divisions by fathers' houses, to the sons of the people, to offer to the Lord, as it is written in the book of Moses; and likewise for the morning.

**35:13** And they roasted the Passover in fire according to the ordinance; and the holy offerings they boiled in the bronze vessels and in the caldrons, and it prospered; and they ran to all the sons of the people.

**35:14** And after they had prepared for them and for the priests—because the priests were occupied in offering up the fat and the burnt offerings until night—the Levites prepared for themselves and for their brethren, the sons of Aaron.

**35:15** And the singers, the sons of Asaph, were at their station according to the commands of David, and Asaph and Heman and Jeduthun, the prophets of the king; and as for the rulers and the gatekeepers at every gate, it was not for them to depart from the ministry of the holy things, because their brethren the Levites prepared for them.

**35:16** So all the service of the Lord was set in order and prepared on that day, to keep the Passover and to offer the burnt offerings upon the altar of the Lord, according to the command of King Josiah.

**35:17** And the sons of Israel who were present kept the Passover at that time, and the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days.

**35:18** And there had been no Passover like it in Israel; from the days of Samuel the prophet and of every king of Israel, they had not kept such a Passover as Josiah kept, with the priests and the Levites, and all Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to the Lord,

**35:19** in the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah. Moreover, King Josiah burned up the mediums and the spiritists, and the teraphim and the idols and the karasim that were in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, that he might establish the words of the law that were written in the book which Hilkiah the priest found in the house of the Lord. There was no one like him before him who turned to the Lord with all his heart and with all his soul and with all his strength, according to all the law of Moses, nor after him did any arise like him. Yet the Lord did not turn away from the fierceness of His great wrath, with which the Lord was angered in wrath against Judah because of all the ordinances with which Manasseh had provoked Him. And the Lord said, "I will also remove Judah from sight, just as I removed Israel, and I have rejected the city which I chose, Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, 'My name shall be there.'"

**35:20** And Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt went up against the king of the Assyrians to the river Euphrates; and King Josiah went out to meet him.

**35:21** And he sent messengers to him, saying, "What have I to do with you, king of Judah? I have not come against you today to wage war, and God has told me to make haste. Beware of God who is with me, lest He destroy you."

**35:22** Yet Josiah did not turn his face from him, but strengthened himself to fight against him, and did not listen to the words of Necho from the mouth of God; and he came to fight in the plain of Megiddo.

**35:23** And the archers shot at King Josiah; and the king said to his servants, "Take me away, for I am severely wounded."

**35:24** So his servants took him out of the chariot and put him in the second chariot that he had, and brought him to Jerusalem. And he died, and was buried with his fathers; and all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah.

**35:25** And Jeremiah lamented for Josiah, and all the rulers and the female rulers spoke a lament over Josiah to this day; and they made it an ordinance in Israel, and behold, it is written in the Laments.

**35:26** Now the acts of Josiah and his hope were written in the law of the Lord,

**35:27** and his deeds, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **35:3** — The Greek describes the Levites as "capable in all Israel" (Hebrew "who taught all Israel") and narrates the placing of the ark as an accomplished act ("they placed") rather than a command.
- **35:9** — The Greek includes "Benaiah" in the list of Levite leaders and reads "Joel" where the Hebrew has "Jeiel."
- **35:12** — The Greek reads "for the morning" (reflecting unpointed Hebrew labboqer rather than labbaqar, "for the cattle"); similarly in 35:13, "and it prospered" reflects the verb tzalach ("prosper") rather than "in pans."
- **35:19** — The Greek preserves an extended section paralleling 2 Kings 23:24-27 not found in the Hebrew of 2 Chronicles, including the untranslated term "karasim" (for abominations) and "ordinances" where 2 Kings 23:26 has "provocations."
- **35:25** — The Greek reads "all the rulers and the female rulers," reflecting Hebrew sarim/sarot ("rulers") rather than sharim/sharot ("singers").
