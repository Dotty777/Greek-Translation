## Text

**5:1** Then Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo prophesied a prophecy to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel who was over them.

**5:2** So Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak arose and began to build the house of God which is in Jerusalem; and with them were the prophets of God, helping them.

**5:3** At that same time Thananai, the governor beyond the River, and Satharbouzana and their fellow servants came to them and spoke thus to them: "Who issued a decree for you to build the house and to complete this furnishing?"

**5:4** Then they said these things to them: "What are the names of the men who are building this city?"

**5:5** But the eyes of God were upon the captivity of Judah, and they did not stop them until a report was brought to Darius; and then it was sent to the tax collector concerning this matter.

**5:6** The copy of the letter which Thanthanas, governor of the region beyond, and Satharbouzana and their fellow servants, the Apharsachites beyond the River, sent to King Darius;

**5:7** they sent a report to him, and thus it was written in it:

**5:8** "To King Darius, all peace. Let it be known to the king that we went into the province of Judea, to the house of the great God; and it is being built with choice stones, and timber is being laid in the walls, and that work is going forward skillfully and prospering in their hands.

**5:9** Then we questioned those elders and spoke thus to them: 'Who issued a decree for you to build this furnishing and to complete it?'

**5:10** We also asked them for their names to inform you, so that we might write down the names of the men who were their leaders.

**5:11** And this word they answered us, saying, 'We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are building the house that was built many years before this; and a great king of Israel built it and completed it for them.

**5:12** But since our fathers provoked the God of heaven to wrath, He gave them into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he destroyed this house and carried the people away captive to Babylon.

**5:13** However, in the first year of Cyrus the king, King Cyrus issued a decree that this house of God should be built.

**5:14** Also the silver and gold vessels of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought out from the house that was in Jerusalem and carried to the temple of the king, these he gave to Bagasar the treasurer, who was over the treasury,

**5:15** and said to him, "Take all the vessels; go, put them in the house that is in Jerusalem, in their own place."

**5:16** Then that Sarbagar came and laid the foundations of the house of God which is in Jerusalem; and from that time until now it has been in building, and has not been finished.'

**5:17** And now, if it seems good to the king, let a search be made in the treasure house of the king of Babylon, so that you may know that a decree was issued by King Cyrus to build that house of God which is in Jerusalem; and when the king has learned concerning this matter, let him send to us."

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — "Thananai" (spelled "Thanthanas" in 5:6) corresponds to Tattenai, and "Satharbouzana" to Shethar-bozenai. The Greek word rendered "furnishing" (also in 5:9) translates an obscure Aramaic architectural term often rendered "structure" or "wall."
- **5:4** — The Greek reads the third-person plural "they said" and "this city," where the Aramaic text has "we said" and "this building."
- **5:14** — "Bagasar" (spelled "Sarbagar" in 5:16) corresponds to Sheshbazzar; the Greek describes him as "the treasurer, who was over the treasury" rather than "governor."
- **5:17** — The Greek reads "of the king of Babylon" and "that a decree was issued," and concludes with "when the king has learned concerning this matter" rather than "his pleasure."
