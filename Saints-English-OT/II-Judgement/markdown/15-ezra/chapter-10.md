## Text

**10:1** Now as Ezra prayed, and as he confessed, weeping and praying before the house of God, a very great assembly from Israel was gathered to him, men and women and youths; for the people wept and lifted up their weeping.

**10:2** And Shecaniah the son of Jehiel, of the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, "We have broken faith with our God and have caused foreign wives from the peoples of the land to dwell with us; yet now there is patient hope for Israel concerning this.

**10:3** Now therefore, let us make a covenant with our God to put away all the wives and those born of them, according as you desire; arise and put them in fear by the commandments of our God, and let it be done according to the law.

**10:4** Arise, for the matter rests upon you, and we are with you; be strong and act."

**10:5** Then Ezra arose and made the rulers, the Levites, and all Israel swear an oath to do according to this word; and they swore.

**10:6** And Ezra rose up from before the house of God and went into the treasury chamber of Johanan the son of Eliashib, and he went there; he ate no bread and drank no water, for he mourned over the faithlessness of the captivity.

**10:7** And they sent forth a proclamation to Judah and in Jerusalem,

**10:8** "Whoever does not come within three days, according to the counsel of the rulers and the elders, all his possessions shall be placed under the ban, and he himself shall be separated from the assembly of the captivity."

**10:9** Then all the men of Judah and Benjamin were gathered to Jerusalem within the three days; this was the ninth month. On the twentieth day of the month, all the people sat in the open square of the house of God, because of their agitation over the matter and because of the winter storm.

**10:10** And Ezra the priest arose and said to them, "You have broken faith and have caused foreign wives to dwell with you, to add to the trespass of Israel.

**10:11** Now therefore, give praise to the God of our fathers, and do what is pleasing before Him, and separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign wives."

**10:12** Then all the assembly answered and said, "Great is this word of yours for us to do.

**10:13** Yet the people are many, and the place is wintry, and there is no strength to stand outside; nor is the work for one day or for two, for we have greatly multiplied our wrongdoing in this matter.

**10:14** Let our rulers now stand; and for all those in our cities, whoever has caused foreign wives to dwell with him, let them come at appointed times from the gatherings, and with them the elders of every city and the judges, to turn away the fierce wrath of our God from us concerning this matter."

**10:15** Only Jonathan the son of Asahel and Lazeia the son of Hilkiah were with me concerning this, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite were helping them.

**10:16** And the sons of the captivity did so. And Ezra the priest and men who were heads of ancestral houses by house, and all of them by name, were set apart, for they returned on a day of the tenth month to examine the matter.

**10:17** And they finished with all the men who had caused foreign wives to dwell with them by the first day of the first month.

**10:18** Now among the sons of the priests who had caused foreign wives to dwell with them were found: of the sons of Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brethren: Meesel, Eliezer, Jarim, and Gedaliah.

**10:19** And they gave their hand to put away their wives, and for their trespass a ram from the flock concerning their guilt.

**10:20** And of the sons of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah.

**10:21** And of the sons of Harim: Masael, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah.

**10:22** And of the sons of Pashhur: Eliona, Maaseiah, Samael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah.

**10:23** And of the Levites: Jozabad, Samu, Kelaiah—that is, Kolieu—Pedaiah, Jodom, and Eliezer;

**10:24** and of the singers: Elisaph; and of the gatekeepers: Gellem, Telem, and Odouth.

**10:25** And of Israel, of the sons of Parosh: Ramiah, Aziah, Malchijah, Amamin, Eleazar, and Benaiah.

**10:26** And of the sons of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdiah, Jeremoth, and Elijah.

**10:27** And of the sons of Zathuia: Eliona, Eliashib, Alathaniah, Amon, Zabadab, and Uzziah.

**10:28** And of the sons of Bebai: Johanan, Niana, Zabu, and Thali.

**10:29** And of the sons of Banui: Melusham, Alum, Ada, Jasud, Shaluiah, and Memon.

**10:30** And of the sons of Pahath-Moab: Aidaine, Chael, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Thanui, and Manasseh.

**10:31** And of the sons of Harim: Eliezer, Isshiah, Malchijah, Shemaiah, Shimeon,

**10:32** Benjamin, Baluch, and Shemariah.

**10:33** Of the sons of Hashum: Mattaniah, Atha, Zabel, Eliphaneth, Jeramim, Manasseh, and Shimei.

**10:34** Of the sons of Ani: Modediah, Mari, Uel,

**10:35** Benaiah, Baraiah, Hilkiah,

**10:36** Vaniah, Meremoth, Eliasiph,

**10:37** Mattaniah, and Mathanan; and they did so.

**10:38** The sons of Banui and the sons of Shimei,

**10:39** and Shelemiah, Nathan, and Adaiah,

**10:40** Machadnabu, Shashai, Sariu,

**10:41** Azarel, Shelemiah, Shemariah,

**10:42** Shallum, Mariah, and Joseph.

**10:43** Of the sons of Nebo: Jeiel, Thamathiah, Sedem, Zanbina, Dia, Joel, and Benaiah.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — The Greek reads 'according as you desire; arise and put them in fear by the commandments of our God,' diverging from the Masoretic Hebrew ('according to the counsel of my lord and of those who tremble at the commandment of our God').
- **10:7** — The Greek text here is shorter than the Masoretic Hebrew, omitting the clause about summoning all the returned exiles to assemble at Jerusalem.
- **10:15** — The Greek reads 'Lazeia the son of Hilkiah' and states that Jonathan and Lazeia 'were with me' concerning this matter, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads 'Jahzeiah the son of Tikvah' and portrays them as opposing it.
- **10:23** — Throughout verses 18–43, the Greek preserves several distinctive personal-name forms that differ from the Masoretic Hebrew, including Meesel (v. 18), Kolieu and Jodom (v. 23), Gellem and Odouth (v. 24), Alathaniah and Amon (v. 27), Baluch (v. 32), and Thamathiah, Sedem, and Dia (v. 43).
- **10:37** — The Greek renders the final word as a verb, 'and they did so,' whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads a personal name (Jaasu/Jaasai); similarly, verse 38 reads 'the sons of Banui and the sons of Shimei' rather than individual names.
