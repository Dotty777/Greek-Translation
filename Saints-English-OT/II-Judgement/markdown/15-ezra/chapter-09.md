## Text

**9:1** Now when these things were finished, the leaders drew near to me, saying, "The people of Israel and the priests and the Levites have not separated themselves from the peoples of the lands in their abominations—those of the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, the Ammonite, Moab, the Moseri, and the Amorite.

**9:2** For they have taken some of their daughters for themselves and for their sons, so that the holy seed has been led astray among the peoples of the lands; and the hand of the leaders is in this unfaithfulness."

**9:3** And when I heard this word, I tore my garments and trembled, and I plucked out some of the hair of my head and of my beard, and sat down in silence.

**9:4** Then everyone who pursued the word of the God of Israel was gathered to me because of the unfaithfulness of the exile; and I sat in silence until the evening sacrifice.

**9:5** And at the evening sacrifice I arose from my humiliation; and having torn my garments, I trembled, and I bowed upon my knees and spread out my hands to God,

**9:6** and I said: "I am ashamed and humiliated to lift up my face to You, for our iniquities have multiplied above our head, and our trespasses have grown great even to heaven.

**9:7** From the days of our fathers we have been in great trespass to this day; and because of our iniquities we, our kings, and our sons have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the nations, to the sword and to captivity, to plunder and to shame of our face, as it is this day.

**9:8** And now our God has made provision for us to leave us for deliverance, and to give us a firm support in the place of His sanctuary, to enlighten our eyes and to give a little reviving in our bondage.

**9:9** For we are bondservants; yet in our bondage the Lord our God did not forsake us, and He inclined mercy toward us before the kings of Persia, to give us reviving, that they might raise up the house of our God and restore its desolate places, and to give us a hedge in Judah and in Jerusalem.

**9:10** What shall we say, O our God, after this? For we have forsaken Your commandments,

**9:11** which You gave us by the hand of Your servants the prophets: 'The land which you are entering to inherit is a land unsettled by the shifting of the peoples of the nations, in their abominations, with which they have filled it from mouth to mouth in their uncleannesses.

**9:12** Now therefore, do not give your daughters to their sons, nor take any of their daughters for your sons, and you shall not seek their peace or their good forever, that you may be strong and eat the good things of the land, and leave it as an inheritance to your sons forever.'

**9:13** And after all that has come upon you for your evil deeds and for your great trespass—for there is none like our God, because You have lightened our iniquities and have given us deliverance—

**9:14** because we have turned back to break Your commandments and to intermarry with the peoples, do not be provoked to anger against us to the point of utter destruction, so that there would be no remnant and no survivor.

**9:15** O Lord God of Israel, You are righteous, for we have been left as survivors, as it is this day. We are before You in our trespasses, for no one can stand before You because of this."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — "Abominations" translates a rare Greek term meaning things to be kept far away or estrangements, and "the Moseri" transliterates the Hebrew gentilic for "the Egyptian."
- **9:3** — The Greek reads "and trembled" (here and in verse 5) where the Hebrew has "and my robe," and "in silence" where the Hebrew has "appalled."
- **9:11** — The Greek reads "unsettled by the shifting" where the Hebrew has "impure/unclean," and renders the idiom for "from one end to the other" literally as "from mouth to mouth."
- **9:13** — The Greek shifts to the second-person plural ("upon you... your") and reads "for there is none like our God" before acknowledging that God has lightened their iniquities.
