## Text

**6:1** Then Darius the king issued a decree, and he made search in the archives where the treasure is laid up in Babylon.

**6:2** And there was found in the city, in the fortress, one scroll, and this memorandum was written in it:

**6:3** "In the first year of Cyrus the king, Cyrus the king issued a decree concerning the holy house of God which is in Jerusalem, and the place where they offer the sacrifices. And he set an elevation, sixty cubits in height,

**6:4** and three courses of stone, and one course of timber; and the expense shall be given from the house of the king.

**6:5** And the vessels of the house of God, of silver and of gold, which Nebuchadnezzar brought out from the house which is in Jerusalem, were set in their place in the house of God."

**6:6** "Now you shall give, O governors beyond the River, Shethar-Boznai and their fellow servants the Apharsachites beyond the River, keeping far away from there:

**6:7** leave alone the work of the house of God; and let the elders of the Jews build that house of God in its place.

**6:8** And by me a decree has been issued lest you ever do anything with the elders of the Jews for the building of that house of God; and from the possessions of the king, of the tribute beyond the River, let the expense be diligently given to those men, so that they may not be hindered.

**6:9** And whatever is lacking—both young bulls and rams and lambs for burnt offerings to the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, and oil, according to the word of the priests who are in Jerusalem—let it be given to them day by day, whatever they may ask,

**6:10** that they may offer sweet aromas to the God of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and of his sons.

**6:11** Also by me a decree has been issued that every man who alters this word, a timber shall be pulled down from his house, and being set upright he shall be struck upon it; and his house shall be dealt with according to my will.

**6:12** And the God whose name dwells there shall overthrow every king and people who stretches out his hand to alter or to destroy the house of God which is in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have issued a decree; it shall be done diligently."

**6:13** Then Tattenai, governor beyond the River, Shethar-Boznai, and his fellow servants, according to what Darius the king had sent, so they diligently did.

**6:14** And the elders of the Jews and the Levites were building through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo; and they built and finished it by the decree of the God of Israel, and by the decree of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes, kings of the Persians.

**6:15** And they finished this house by the third day of the month of Adar, which was the sixth year of the reign of Darius the king.

**6:16** And the children of Israel, the priests and the Levites and the rest of the children of the captivity, kept the dedication of the house of God with gladness.

**6:17** And they offered at the dedication of the house of God one hundred young bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and as a sin offering for all Israel twelve male goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

**6:18** And they appointed the priests in their division and the Levites in their courses for the service of God who is in Jerusalem, according to the writing of the book of Moses.

**6:19** And the children of the captivity kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**6:20** For the priests and the Levites had purified themselves; all of them to a man were clean; and they slaughtered the Passover for all the children of the captivity, and for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.

**6:21** And the children of Israel who were from the captivity ate the Passover, together with everyone who separated himself with respect to the uncleannesses of the nations of the land to join them, to seek the Lord God of Israel.

**6:22** And they kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with gladness, for He made them glad, and the heart of the king of Assyria turned toward them, to strengthen their hands in the works of the house of the God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Greek text reads "in the city, in the fortress," without naming Ecbatana (Achmetha) or the province of Media as in the Aramaic text.
- **6:6** — The Greek reads "Now you shall give" (nyn dosete), translating the proper name Tattenai as a form of the verb "to give," whereas the governor's name appears in 6:13.
- **6:11** — The Greek reads "shall be struck upon it" and "his house shall be dealt with according to my will" (to kat' eme), differing from the Aramaic "impaled" and "made a refuse heap."
- **6:14** — The Greek includes "and the Levites" as builders alongside the elders, whereas the Aramaic text has a verb meaning "prospered."
- **6:21** — The Greek literally reads eis akatharsias ("with respect to the uncleannesses") rather than "from the uncleanness" as in the Hebrew text.
