## Text

**7:1** Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of the Persians, Ezra went up, the son of Seraiah, the son of Zariah, the son of Hilkiah,

**7:2** the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,

**7:3** the son of Samariah, the son of Esriah, the son of Mareroth,

**7:4** the son of Zerahiah, the son of Saouia, the son of Bukki,

**7:5** the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the ancestral priest.

**7:6** He went up from Babylon, and he was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God of Israel had given; and the king granted to him, because the hand of his God was upon him, in all that he sought.

**7:7** And some of the children of Israel, and of the priests, and of the Levites, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.

**7:8** And they came to Jerusalem in the fifth month; this was the seventh year of the king.

**7:9** For on the first day of the fifth month they came to Jerusalem, because the good hand of his God was upon him.

**7:10** For Ezra had set it in his heart to seek the law, and to do it, and to teach statutes and judgment in Israel.

**7:11** Now this is the copy of the decree which Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the book of the words of the commandments of the Lord and of His statutes to Israel:

**7:12** "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra, scribe of the law of the God of heaven: The matter and the reply have been completed.

**7:13** By me a decree has been issued that everyone in my kingdom who is willing, of the people of Israel and of the priests and Levites, to go to Jerusalem, may go with you.

**7:14** From the presence of the king and of the seven counselors it has been sent to make inquiry concerning Judea and Jerusalem, by the law of their God which is in your hand,

**7:15** and for the house of the Lord, silver and gold which the king and the counselors have freely offered to the Lord of Israel, who dwells in Jerusalem,

**7:16** and all the silver and gold that you may find in all the province of Babylon, together with the freewill offering of the people and of the priests who offer willingly for the house of God which is in Jerusalem.

**7:17** And everything that comes in, enter it promptly in this book: young bulls, rams, lambs, and their offerings and their drink offerings; and you shall offer them upon the altar of the house of your God which is in Jerusalem.

**7:18** And furthermore, has it seemed good to you and to your brethren to do anything with the rest of the silver and the gold? Do as is pleasing to your God.

**7:19** And the vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of God, deliver before God in Jerusalem.

**7:20** And the rest of the need of the house of God, whatever it may seem fit to you to give, you shall give from the treasure houses of the king.

**7:21** And from me—I myself, Artaxerxes the king—I have issued a decree to all the treasuries that are beyond the River, that whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, may ask of you, let it be done promptly,

**7:22** up to talents of silver, and up to one hundred kors of wheat, and up to one hundred storehouses of wine, and salt of which there is no written limit.

**7:23** Everything that is in the decree of the God of heaven, let it be done. Take heed lest you lay hands on anything against the house of the God of heaven, lest wrath come upon the kingdom of the king and of his sons.

**7:24** Also it is made known to you concerning all the priests and the Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, and ministers of this house of God: let there be no tribute for you; you shall have no authority to bring them into bondage.

**7:25** And you, Ezra, in whose hand is the wisdom of God, appoint scribes and judges, that they may judge all the people who are beyond the River, all who know the law of God; and to him who does not know, you shall make it known.

**7:26** And everyone who does not promptly do the law of God and the law of the king, judgment shall be executed upon him, whether to death, or to chastisement, or to forfeiture of property, or to imprisonment."

**7:27** Blessed be the Lord God of our fathers, who has put such a thing in the heart of the king, who glorified the house which is in Jerusalem,

**7:28** and has extended mercy to me in the sight of the king and of his counselors, and of all the exalted princes of the king. And I was strengthened as the good hand of God was upon me, and I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek preserves distinct forms of several ancestral names in Ezra's genealogy—Zariah (7:1), Samariah, Esriah, Mareroth (7:3), and Saouia (7:4)—corresponding to Azariah, Amariah, Azariah, Meraioth, and Uzzi in the Hebrew text.
- **7:5** — The Greek describes Aaron as "the ancestral priest" (tou patroou) rather than "the chief priest" as in the Hebrew text.
- **7:9** — The Greek records only the arrival on the first day of the fifth month, omitting the departure on the first day of the first month found in the Hebrew text.
- **7:17** — The Greek directs Ezra to "enter it promptly in this book" (entaxon en biblio touto) rather than "buy with this money" as in the Aramaic text.
- **7:22** — The Greek omits the numeral "one hundred" before "talents of silver," renders the wine measure as "storehouses" (apothekon), and omits the allotment of oil found in the Aramaic text.
