## Text

**8:1** Now these are the leaders of their fathers' houses, the guides who went up with me in the reign of Artaxerxes the king of Babylon:

**8:2** of the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; of the sons of David, Tous;

**8:3** of the sons of Sanachia, of the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him the company of one hundred and fifty;

**8:4** of the sons of Pahath-Moab, Eliana the son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred males;

**8:5** and of the sons of Zathoe, Shecaniah the son of Aziel, and with him three hundred males.

**8:6** And of the sons of Adin, Obeth the son of Jonathan, and with him fifty males;

**8:7** of the sons of Elah, Josiah the son of Atheli, and with him seventy males;

**8:8** of the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah the son of Michael, and with him eighty males;

**8:9** of the sons of Joab, Adiah the son of Iema, and with him two hundred and eighteen males;

**8:10** of the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and with him one hundred and sixty males;

**8:11** and of the sons of Bebai, Azariah the son of Bebai, and with him seventy-eight males;

**8:12** of the sons of Astad, Johanan the son of Hakkatan, and with him one hundred and ten males;

**8:13** of the sons of Adonikam, the last ones, and these are their names: Eliphat, Euia, and Shemaiah, sixty males;

**8:14** and of the sons of Bago, Uthai, and with him eighty males.

**8:15** And I gathered them to the river that flows to Euim, and we encamped there three days. And I looked closely among the people and among the priests, and I found none of the sons of Levi there.

**8:16** Then I sent for the men Eleazar, Ariel, Shemaiah, Alonam, Areb, Elnatham, Nathan, Zechariah, and Mesuam, and for Arib and Eanathan, men of understanding.

**8:17** And I brought them out to the ruler in the silver of the place, and I put words in their mouth to speak to their brethren of the Athanim in the silver of the place, to bring us singers for the house of our God.

**8:18** And there came to us, as the good hand of our God was upon us, a man, Sachoch, of the sons of Mahli the son of Levi, the son of Israel; and at the beginning came his sons and his brethren, eighteen;

**8:19** and Hashabiah and Osaiah of the sons of Merari, his brethren and their sons, twenty;

**8:20** and of the Nethinim, whom David and the leaders had given for the service of the Levites, two hundred and twenty Nethinim; all of them were gathered by name.

**8:21** Then I proclaimed a fast there at the river Thue, that we might humble ourselves before our God, to seek from Him a straight way for us and for our children and for all our possessions.

**8:22** For I was ashamed to ask of the king an armed force and horsemen to save us from the enemy on the way, because we had spoken to the king, saying, "The hand of our God is upon all those who seek Him for good, and His power and His wrath are against all those who forsake Him."

**8:23** So we fasted and sought from our God concerning this matter, and He heard us.

**8:24** And I set apart twelve of the leaders of the priests: Seraiah, Hashabiah, and with them twelve of their brethren.

**8:25** And I weighed out to them the silver and the gold and the vessels, the offering of the house of our God, which the king and his counselors and his officials and all Israel who were found there had offered up.

**8:26** I weighed into their hands six hundred and fifty talents of silver, and one hundred silver vessels, and one hundred talents of gold;

**8:27** and twenty golden bowls, for the way one thousand chamaneim, and choice vessels of fine gleaming bronze, precious as gold.

**8:28** And I said to them, "You are holy to the Lord, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are freewill offerings to the Lord God of our fathers.

**8:29** Watch and keep guard until you stand before the leaders of the priests and the Levites and the leaders of the fathers' houses in Jerusalem, at the tents of the house of the Lord."

**8:30** So the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver and the gold and the vessels, to bring them to Jerusalem to the house of our God.

**8:31** Then we departed from the river Thue on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem. And the hand of our God was upon us, and He delivered us from the hand of the enemy and the adversary along the way.

**8:32** So we came to Jerusalem, and we remained there three days.

**8:33** Now it came to pass on the fourth day that we weighed out the silver and the gold and the vessels in the house of our God into the hand of Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest, and with him was Eleazar the son of Phinehas, and with them were Jozabad the son of Jeshua and Noadi of Ebannaia, the Levites—

**8:34** everything by number and by weight; and all the weight was written down.

**8:35** At that time, those who had come out of the captivity, the sons of the exile, offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve young bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats for a sin offering—all of them as burnt offerings.

**8:36** And they delivered the king's decree to the king's administrators and to the governors beyond the River, and they honored the people and the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek reads "the guides" where the Hebrew has "their genealogy," and designates Artaxerxes as "the king of Babylon" rather than "from Babylon."
- **8:5** — The Greek preserves the ancestral name "of the sons of Zathoe" (corresponding to Zattu), which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:15** — The river name "Euim" here—and "Thue" in verses 21 and 31—reflects distinct Septuagint forms corresponding to Ahava in the Hebrew text.
- **8:17** — The Greek translates the place name Casiphia literally as "the silver of the place" (from the Hebrew word for silver), names the temple servants "the Athanim" (Nethinim), and asks for "singers" rather than "ministers."
- **8:27** — "Bowls" translates a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew term for covered bowls, and the Greek renders the Hebrew expression for "a thousand darics" as "for the way one thousand chamaneim."
