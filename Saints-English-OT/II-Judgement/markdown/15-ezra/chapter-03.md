## Text

**3:1** And the seventh month arrived, and those of Israel were in their cities; then the people gathered together as one man to Jerusalem.

**3:2** Then Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brethren arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings on it, according to what is written in the law of Moses, the man of God.

**3:3** And they set the altar upon its prepared base, and a burnt offering went up upon it to the Lord in the morning and toward evening.

**3:4** They also kept the Feast of Tabernacles as it is written, with burnt offerings day by day in number according to the ordinance, the daily portion for each day;

**3:5** and afterward the continual burnt offerings, both for the new moons and for all the consecrated feasts, and for everyone who willingly presented a freewill offering to the Lord.

**3:6** On the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the Lord, though the house of the Lord had not been founded.

**3:7** They also gave silver to the stonecutters and to the carpenters, and food, drink, and oil to the Sedamein and to the Sorein, to bring cedar timber from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, according to the grant of Cyrus king of the Persians to them.

**3:8** Now in the second year of their coming to the house of God at Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and the rest of the brethren, the priests and the Levites, and all who came from the captivity to Jerusalem, made a beginning and appointed the Levites from twenty years old and above over the works of the Lord.

**3:9** Then Jeshua and his sons and his brethren, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, stood over those doing the work in the house of God—the sons of Henaad, their sons and their brethren, the Levites.

**3:10** And they laid the foundation to build the house of the Lord; and the priests stood vested with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, to praise the Lord according to the direction of David king of Israel.

**3:11** And they responded in praise and thanksgiving to the Lord, "For it is good, for His mercy is forever upon Israel." And all the people raised a great shout to praise the Lord at the laying of the foundation of the house of the Lord.

**3:12** Yet many of the priests and the Levites and heads of the fathers' houses, the elders who had seen the first house at its foundation, and saw this house before their eyes, wept with a loud voice; while a multitude shouted with gladness to lift up a song.

**3:13** And the people could not distinguish the sound of the shout of gladness from the sound of the weeping, for the people cried out with a loud voice, and it was heard even from afar.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Greek text names only Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brethren as builders of the altar, omitting the priests and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel with his brethren found in the Hebrew.
- **3:7** — The Greek forms "Sedamein" and "Sorein" are transliterations corresponding to the Sidonians and Tyrians.
- **3:10** — The Greek reads "laid the foundation to build the house of the Lord," taking the Hebrew term for "builders" as an infinitive, and omits "with cymbals."
- **3:11** — The Greek uses the neuter "For it is good" rather than the masculine "For He is good."
