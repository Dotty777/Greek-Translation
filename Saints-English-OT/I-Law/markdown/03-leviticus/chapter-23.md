## Text

**23:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**23:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'The feasts of the Lord, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, these are My feasts.

**23:3** Six days you shall do work, and on the seventh day is a Sabbath of rest, a holy convocation to the Lord; you shall do no work; it is a Sabbath to your Lord in every dwelling of yours.

**23:4** These are the feasts to the Lord, and these, proclaim them holy in their appointed seasons.

**23:5** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the evenings, is the Passover to the Lord.

**23:6** And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the Lord; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**23:7** And the first day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work;

**23:8** and you shall bring burnt offerings to the Lord for seven days, and the seventh day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work.'"

**23:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:10** "Tell the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'When you enter the land which I give to you and reap its harvest, then you shall bring a sheaf, the firstfruits of your harvest, to the priest;

**23:11** and he shall offer up the sheaf before the Lord to be accepted for you; on the day after the first day the priest shall offer it up.

**23:12** And you shall prepare on the day on which you bring the sheaf an unblemished year-old sheep for a burnt offering to the Lord,

**23:13** and its offering shall be two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil—an offering to the Lord, a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord—and its drink offering shall be a fourth of a hin of wine.

**23:14** And you shall eat neither bread nor roasted new grain until this very day, until you yourselves have brought the gifts to your God; it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations in every dwelling of yours.

**23:15** And you shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day on which you bring the sheaf of the presentation offering, seven complete weeks;

**23:16** until the day after the last week you shall count fifty days, and you shall offer a new offering to the Lord.

**23:17** From your dwelling you shall bring loaves as a presentation offering, two loaves; they shall be of two tenths of fine flour, and they shall be baked with leaven, as firstfruits to the Lord.

**23:18** And he shall bring with the loaves seven unblemished year-old lambs, and one calf from the herd, and two unblemished rams; they shall be a burnt offering to the Lord, together with their offerings and their drink offerings, an offering of sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord.

**23:19** And they shall prepare one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, and two year-old lambs for a sacrifice of deliverance, with the loaves of the firstfruits;

**23:20** and the priest shall present them with the loaves of the firstfruits as a presentation offering before the Lord, with the two lambs; they shall be holy to the Lord; to the priest who offers them, it shall belong to him.

**23:21** And you shall proclaim this day a convocation; it shall be holy to you; you shall do no servile work on it; it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling.

**23:22** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish reaping the remainder of the harvest of your field when you reap, nor shall you gather what falls from your harvest; for the poor and for the sojourner you shall leave it. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:24** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying: 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there shall be a rest for you, a memorial of trumpets; it shall be a holy convocation to you.

**23:25** You shall do no servile work, and you shall bring a burnt offering to the Lord.'"

**23:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:27** "Also on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement; it shall be a holy convocation to you, and you shall humble your souls and bring a burnt offering to the Lord.

**23:28** You shall do no work on this very day, for it is a day of atonement for you, to make atonement for you before the Lord your God.

**23:29** Every soul that is not humbled on this very day shall be utterly destroyed from its people.

**23:30** And every soul that does any work on this very day, that soul shall perish from its people.

**23:31** You shall do no work; it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**23:32** It shall be a Sabbath of Sabbaths to you, and you shall humble your souls; from the ninth day of the month, from evening to evening, you shall keep your Sabbath."

**23:33** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:34** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying: 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days to the Lord.

**23:35** And the first day is a holy convocation; you shall do no servile work.

**23:36** For seven days you shall bring burnt offerings to the Lord; and the eighth day shall be a holy convocation to you, and you shall bring burnt offerings to the Lord; it is a closing festival; you shall do no servile work.

**23:37** These are the feasts to the Lord which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, to bring altar offerings to the Lord: burnt offerings and their sacrifices and their drink offerings, each day's portion on its day,

**23:38** besides the Sabbaths of the Lord, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides your freewill offerings which you give to the Lord.

**23:39** And on the fifteenth day of this seventh month, when you have finished gathering the produce of the land, celebrate a feast to the Lord for seven days; on the first day there shall be rest, and on the eighth day there shall be rest.

**23:40** And you shall take on the first day choice fruit of a tree, and fronds of palm trees, and thick branches of a tree, and branches of willow and chaste-tree from a brook, to rejoice before the Lord your God seven days of the year;

**23:41** it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations. In the seventh month you shall celebrate it;

**23:42** in booths you shall dwell seven days; every native-born in Israel shall dwell in booths,

**23:43** so that your generations may see that I caused the sons of Israel to dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:44** And Moses declared the feasts of the Lord to the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — The Greek reads 'and these, proclaim them holy' as an imperative clause, differing from the Hebrew relative clause ('holy convocations, which you shall proclaim').
- **23:11** — The Greek specifies 'on the day after the first day' (referring to the first day of Unleavened Bread), reflecting an early interpretive tradition for the Hebrew phrase 'the day after the Sabbath.'
- **23:36** — The Greek term exodion ('closing festival' or 'day of departure') renders the Hebrew designation for the solemn concluding assembly at the end of the Feast of Tabernacles.
- **23:40** — The Greek lists branches of both willow and chaste-tree from a brook and attaches 'of the year' to the end of verse 40 rather than verse 41.
