## Text

**8:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Take Aaron and his sons, and his garments, and the anointing oil, and the young bull for the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread,

**8:3** and assemble all the congregation at the door of the tabernacle of witness."

**8:4** And Moses did just as the Lord commanded him, and he assembled the congregation at the door of the tabernacle of witness.

**8:5** And Moses said to the congregation, "This is the thing—this is what the Lord commanded to do."

**8:6** And Moses brought Aaron and his sons near, and washed them with water.

**8:7** And he clothed them with the tunic and girded them with the sash; and he clothed him with the robe and put the ephod upon him,

**8:8** and he girded him according to the workmanship of the ephod and bound him tightly with it; and he placed the oracle upon it, and upon the oracle he placed the Manifestation and the Truth.

**8:9** And he placed the turban upon his head, and upon the front of the turban he placed the golden plate, consecrated and holy, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:10** And Moses took some of the anointing oil,

**8:11** and sprinkled some of it upon the altar seven times; and he anointed the altar and sanctified it, and all its utensils, and the laver and its base, and sanctified them; and he anointed the tabernacle and all that was in it, and sanctified it.

**8:12** And Moses poured some of the anointing oil upon the head of Aaron, and anointed him and sanctified him.

**8:13** And Moses brought the sons of Aaron near, and clothed them with tunics, and girded them with sashes, and put headdresses upon them, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:14** And Moses brought forward the young bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the young bull for the sin offering.

**8:15** And he slew it; and Moses took some of the blood and put it upon the horns of the altar all around with his finger, and cleansed the altar; and he poured out the blood at the base of the altar, and sanctified it to make atonement upon it.

**8:16** And Moses took all the fat that was upon the entrails, and the lobe from the liver, and both kidneys and the fat that was upon them, and Moses offered them up upon the altar.

**8:17** But the young bull, and its hide, and its flesh, and its dung, he burned completely with fire outside the camp, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:18** And Moses brought forward the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram. And Moses slew the ram, and Moses poured out the blood upon the altar all around.

**8:20** And he cut the ram into pieces, and Moses offered up the head, and the pieces, and the fat.

**8:21** And he washed the inward parts and the legs with water, and Moses offered up the entire ram upon the altar; it was a burnt offering for a sweet-smelling aroma, an offering made by fire to the Lord, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:22** And Moses brought forward the second ram, the ram of consecration; and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram.

**8:23** And he slew it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it upon the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and upon the tip of his right hand, and upon the tip of his right foot.

**8:24** And Moses brought the sons of Aaron near; and Moses put some of the blood upon the lobes of their right ears, and upon the tips of their right hands, and upon the tips of their right feet; and Moses poured out the blood upon the altar all around.

**8:25** And he took the fat, and the loin, and the fat that was upon the inward parts, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that was upon them, and the right shoulder;

**8:26** and from the basket of consecration that was before the Lord he took one unleavened loaf, and one loaf made with oil, and one wafer, and placed them upon the fat and placed the right shoulder upon them;

**8:27** and he placed all of them upon the hands of Aaron and upon the hands of his sons, and offered them up as a dedicated offering before the Lord.

**8:28** And Moses took them from their hands, and Moses offered them up upon the altar, upon the burnt offering of consecration, which is a sweet-smelling aroma; it is an offering made by fire to the Lord.

**8:29** And Moses took the breast and separated it as a presentation offering before the Lord from the ram of consecration; and it became Moses' portion, just as the Lord commanded Moses. And Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was upon the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron and his garments and his sons.

**8:30** And Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was upon the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron, and his garments, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him, and the garments of his sons with him.

**8:31** And Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the flesh in the court of the tabernacle of witness, in a holy place, and there you shall eat it and the loaves in the basket of consecration, just as it has been commanded to me, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat these.'

**8:32** And what remains of the flesh and of the loaves shall be burned completely with fire.

**8:33** And you shall not go out from the door of the tabernacle of witness for seven days, until the day of your consecration is fulfilled; for seven days He will consecrate your hands,

**8:34** just as He has done on this day, which the Lord commanded to be done to make atonement for you.

**8:35** And you shall abide at the door of the tabernacle of witness seven days, day and night; you shall keep the charges of the Lord, lest you die; for so the Lord God has commanded me."

**8:36** And Aaron and his sons did all the words that the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The Greek uses plural pronouns ("them") for the tunic and sash before shifting to the singular ("him") for Aaron's robe and ephod, differing from the singular pronouns throughout in the Hebrew text.
- **8:8** — The Septuagint renders the breastpiece as "the oracle" (logion) and translates the Urim and Thummim as "the Manifestation and the Truth" (delosis and aletheia).
- **8:11** — In the Septuagint, the anointing of the tabernacle and all its contents appears at the end of verse 11 rather than in verse 10 as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:18** — In this Greek text, the clauses corresponding to verse 19 in standard versification are joined directly to the end of verse 18.
- **8:30** — The Greek source repeats the sprinkling clause from the end of verse 29 and duplicates the final phrase "and the garments of his sons with him," reflecting textual overlap in the source witness.
