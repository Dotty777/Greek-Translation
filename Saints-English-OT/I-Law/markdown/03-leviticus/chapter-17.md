## Text

**17:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'This is the word which the Lord has commanded, saying:

**17:3** Any man of the sons of Israel, or of the proselytes, or of those joined among you, who slaughters a calf or a sheep or a goat in the camp, and who slaughters outside the camp,

**17:4** and does not bring it to the door of the tabernacle of testimony to offer it as a burnt offering or an offering of deliverance to the Lord, acceptable for a sweet aroma—and whoever slaughters outside and does not bring it to the door of the tabernacle of testimony, so as not to present a gift to the Lord before the tabernacle of the Lord—blood shall be reckoned to that man; he has shed blood, and that soul shall be utterly destroyed from its people,

**17:5** so that the sons of Israel may bring up their sacrifices, whatever they themselves would slaughter in the fields, and they shall bring them to the Lord at the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, to the priest, and shall sacrifice them as a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord.

**17:6** And the priest shall pour the blood upon the altar all around before the Lord, beside the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and shall offer up the fat for a sweet aroma to the Lord.

**17:7** And they shall no longer sacrifice their sacrifices to the vain things after which they themselves play the harlot. It shall be an everlasting statute for you throughout your generations.'

**17:8** And you shall say to them, 'Any man of the sons of Israel, and from the sons of the proselytes who are joined among you, who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice,

**17:9** and does not bring it to the door of the tabernacle of testimony to offer it to the Lord, that man shall be utterly destroyed from his people.

**17:10** And any man of the sons of Israel, or of the proselytes who are joined among you, who eats any blood, I will set My face against the soul that eats the blood, and I will destroy it from its people.

**17:11** For the life of all flesh is its blood, and I have given it to you upon the altar to make atonement for your souls; for its blood shall make atonement in place of the soul.

**17:12** Therefore I have said to the sons of Israel, "No soul among you shall eat blood, nor shall the proselyte who is joined among you eat blood."

**17:13** And any man of the sons of Israel, and of the proselytes who are joined among you, who hunts down game, a wild beast or a bird that is eaten, shall pour out the blood and cover it with earth.

**17:14** For the life of all flesh is its blood; and I said to the sons of Israel, "You shall not eat the blood of any flesh, for the life of all flesh is its blood; everyone who eats it shall be utterly destroyed."

**17:15** And every soul who eats that which died of itself or that which was torn by wild beasts, whether among the native-born or among the proselytes, shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening; then he shall be clean.

**17:16** But if he does not wash his garments and does not bathe his body in water, he shall bear his iniquity.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Greek explicitly includes proselytes (proselutos, resident foreigners who have joined Israel) and those attached among them, and specifies a calf (moschos) where the Hebrew reads an ox.
- **17:4** — The Septuagint contains a notable expansion specifying burnt offerings and offerings of deliverance (soterion, the Greek rendering for the peace offering) as well as a repeated clause on slaughtering outside the camp.
- **17:7** — Greek tois mataiois ("the vain things") is a standard Septuagint designation for worthless idols, standing where the Hebrew reads "goat-demons."
- **17:11** — The Greek states that the life of all flesh "is its blood" (rather than "is in the blood") and uses the substitutionary preposition anti ("in place of the soul") for the blood's atoning role.
