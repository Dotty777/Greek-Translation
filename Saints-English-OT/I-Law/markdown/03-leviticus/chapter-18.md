## Text

**18:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**18:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'I am the Lord your God.

**18:3** According to the practices of the land of Egypt, in which you dwelt, it shall not be done; and according to the practices of the land of Canaan, into which I am bringing you, you shall not do, nor shall you walk in their statutes.

**18:4** You shall do My judgments and keep My ordinances, to walk in them; I am the Lord your God.

**18:5** And you shall keep all My ordinances and all My judgments, and shall do them; the man who does them shall live by them. I am the Lord your God.

**18:6** No man shall approach any close relative of his flesh to uncover nakedness; I am the Lord.

**18:7** The nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother you shall not uncover; for she is your mother, and you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:8** The nakedness of your father's wife you shall not uncover; it is your father's nakedness.

**18:9** The nakedness of your sister, whether by your father or by your mother, born within the household or outside, her nakedness you shall not uncover.

**18:10** The nakedness of your son's daughter or of your daughter's daughter, their nakedness you shall not uncover, for it is your own nakedness.

**18:11** The nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife you shall not uncover; she is your sister by the same father, and you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:12** The nakedness of your father's sister you shall not uncover; for she is a close relative of your father.

**18:13** The nakedness of your mother's sister you shall not uncover; for she is a close relative of your mother.

**18:14** The nakedness of your father's brother you shall not uncover, and you shall not go in to his wife; for she is your kinswoman.

**18:15** The nakedness of your daughter-in-law you shall not uncover; for she is your son's wife, and you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:16** The nakedness of your brother's wife you shall not uncover; it is your brother's nakedness.

**18:17** The nakedness of a woman and of her daughter you shall not uncover; you shall not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to uncover their nakedness, for they are your close relatives; it is an impiety.

**18:18** You shall not take a woman in addition to her sister as a rival, to uncover her nakedness beside her while she is still living.

**18:19** And you shall not approach a woman in the separation of her uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.

**18:20** And you shall not lie with your neighbor's wife to give your seed, to be defiled with her.

**18:21** And you shall not give any of your seed to serve a ruler, and you shall not profane the holy name; I am the Lord.

**18:22** And with a male you shall not lie as with a woman; for it is an abomination.

**18:23** And you shall not lie with any four-footed beast for the giving of seed, to be defiled with it; nor shall a woman stand before any four-footed beast to be mated with it, for it is a loathsome thing.

**18:24** Do not defile yourselves with any of these things; for with all these things the nations have been defiled, which I am driving out before your face.

**18:25** And the land was defiled; therefore I have repaid them for unrighteousness on its account, and the land loathed those who dwelt upon it.

**18:26** And you shall keep all My statutes and all My ordinances, and you shall not do any of all these abominations—neither the native of the land nor the proselyte who has joined himself among you;

**18:27** for all these abominations the men of the land who were before you have done, and the land was defiled;

**18:28** and lest the land loathe you when you defile it, in the same way that it loathed the nations that were before you.

**18:29** For whoever commits any of all these abominations, the souls that commit them shall be utterly destroyed from their people.

**18:30** And you shall keep My ordinances, so that you do not practice any of all the abominable customs which were done before you, and you shall not be defiled by them; for I am the Lord your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — Throughout this chapter, the Septuagint renders the Hebrew idiom "nakedness" with the modest term aschemosyne (literally "indecency" or "shame").
- **18:17** — The Greek reads "your close relatives" (sou), whereas the Hebrew text reads "her near kinswomen."
- **18:21** — The Septuagint renders this prohibition as giving offspring "to serve a ruler" (latreuein archonti) and profaning "the holy name," where the Hebrew refers to passing children through the fire to Molech and profaning "the name of your God."
- **18:25** — Here and in verse 28, the Septuagint renders the vivid Hebrew metaphor of the land "vomiting out" its inhabitants with prosochthizo ("to loathe" or "be disgusted with").
