## Text

**7:1** "And this is the law of the ram for the trespass offering: it is most holy.

**7:2** In the place where they slay the burnt offering, they shall slay the ram of the trespass offering before the Lord; and he shall pour out the blood upon the base of the altar all around.

**7:3** And he shall offer from it all its fat: both the loin and all the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat that is on the entrails,

**7:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is by the thighs, and the lobe that is on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove them.

**7:5** And the priest shall offer them up on the altar as an offering made by fire to the Lord; it is for a trespass.

**7:6** Every male among the priests shall eat them; in a holy place they shall eat them; they are most holy.

**7:7** As is the sin offering, so also is the trespass offering; there is one law for them: the priest who makes atonement, it shall belong to him.

**7:8** And the priest who brings near a man's burnt offering, the hide of the burnt offering which he himself offers shall belong to him.

**7:9** And every sacrifice that is prepared in the oven, and every one that is prepared on a hearth or on a griddle, belongs to the priest who offers it; it shall be his.

**7:10** And every sacrifice mixed with oil, and every one not mixed, shall belong to all the sons of Aaron, to each one an equal share.

**7:11** This is the law of the sacrifice of deliverance which they shall offer to the Lord:

**7:12** If he offers it for praise, then he shall offer with the sacrifice of praise loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and fine flour kneaded with oil.

**7:13** With leavened loaves he shall offer his gifts, along with the sacrifice of praise of deliverance.

**7:14** And he shall bring near one from all his gifts as a set-apart portion to the Lord; to the priest who pours out the blood of the deliverance offering, to him it shall belong.

**7:15** And the flesh of the sacrifice of praise of deliverance shall belong to him; and on the day it is presented as a gift, it shall be eaten; they shall not leave any of it until morning.

**7:16** And if he sacrifices his gift as a vow or a freewill offering, on the day that he brings near his sacrifice it shall be eaten, and on the next day.

**7:17** And what remains of the flesh of the sacrifice until the third day shall be burned up with fire.

**7:18** But if he indeed eats any of the flesh on the third day, it shall not be accepted for him who offers it; it shall not be credited to him; it is a defilement, and the soul that eats of it shall bear the sin.

**7:19** And any flesh that touches anything unclean shall not be eaten; it shall be burned up with fire. Everyone who is clean shall eat flesh.

**7:20** But the soul that eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance, which belongs to the Lord, while his uncleanness is upon him, that soul shall perish from among its people.

**7:21** And a soul that touches any unclean thing, whether of human uncleanness, or of the unclean four-footed animals, or of any unclean abomination, and eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance, which belongs to the Lord, that soul shall perish from among its people."

**7:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:23** "Speak to the sons of Israel, 'You shall not eat any fat of oxen, or of sheep, or of goats.

**7:24** And the fat of what dies of itself and what is caught by wild beasts shall be used for any work, and for food it shall not be eaten.

**7:25** Everyone who eats fat from the livestock of which one brings near an offering made by fire to the Lord, that soul shall perish from its people.

**7:26** You shall not eat any blood in all the land of your dwelling, whether of birds or of livestock.

**7:27** Every soul that eats blood, that soul shall perish from its people.'"

**7:28** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:29** "And to the sons of Israel you shall speak, saying, 'He who offers a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord shall bring his gift to the Lord also from the sacrifice of deliverance.

**7:30** His own hands shall offer the offerings made by fire to the Lord: the fat that is on the breast and the lobe of the liver, he shall offer them so as to place a gift before the Lord.

**7:31** And the priest shall offer up the fat upon the altar, and the breast shall belong to Aaron and shall belong to his sons.

**7:32** And the right shoulder you shall give as a set-apart portion to the priest from the sacrifices of your deliverance offerings.

**7:33** He among the sons of Aaron who offers the blood of the deliverance offering and the fat, to him shall belong the right shoulder as a portion.

**7:34** For the breast of the presentation offering and the shoulder of the set-apart portion I have taken from the sons of Israel, from the sacrifices of your deliverance offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a perpetual statute from the sons of Israel.'"

**7:35** This is the anointing portion of Aaron and the anointing portion of his sons from the offerings made by fire to the Lord, on the day He brought them near to minister as priests to the Lord,

**7:36** just as the Lord commanded to be given to them from the sons of Israel on the day He anointed them, a perpetual statute throughout their generations.

**7:37** This is the law of the burnt offerings, and of the sacrifice, and of the sin offering, and of the trespass offering, and of the consecration, and of the sacrifice of deliverance,

**7:38** in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day He commanded the sons of Israel to offer their gifts before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Septuagint explicitly specifies "of the ram" here and in verse two, identifying the animal required for the trespass offering.
- **7:11** — Greek soterion literally means "deliverance" or "salvation," serving as the Septuagint term for the peace or fellowship offering.
- **7:15** — The Septuagint includes "shall belong to him," assigning the flesh of the sacrifice of praise to the officiating priest.
- **7:30** — The Septuagint adds "and the lobe of the liver" alongside the fat on the breast, and uses "place a gift" (epitheinai doma) for the presentation rite.
