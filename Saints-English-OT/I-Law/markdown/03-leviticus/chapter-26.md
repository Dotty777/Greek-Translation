## Text

**26:1** I am the Lord your God. You shall not make for yourselves handmade idols nor carved images, nor shall you set up a pillar for yourselves, nor shall you place a stone to gaze upon in your land to bow down to it; I am the Lord your God.

**26:2** You shall keep My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuaries; I am the Lord.

**26:3** If you walk in My ordinances, and keep My commandments and perform them,

**26:4** then I will give you the rain in its season, and the land shall yield its produce, and the trees of the fields shall yield their fruit.

**26:5** And for you the harvest shall overtake the vintage, and the vintage shall overtake the sowing; you shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in security upon your land, and war shall not pass through your land.

**26:6** And I will give peace in your land, and you shall lie down, and there shall be no one to terrify you; and I will destroy evil beasts out of your land.

**26:7** And you shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you by slaughter.

**26:8** And five of you shall pursue one hundred, and one hundred of you shall pursue tens of thousands, and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

**26:9** And I will look upon you, and I will cause you to increase and multiply you, and I will establish My covenant with you.

**26:10** And you shall eat old store, and the oldest of the old, and you shall bring out the old before the new.

**26:11** And I will set My covenant among you, and My soul shall not abhor you.

**26:12** And I will walk among you and be God to you, and you shall be My people.

**26:13** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt when you were slaves; and I shattered the bond of your yoke and led you forth with boldness.

**26:14** But if you do not obey Me, nor perform these ordinances of Mine,

**26:15** but disobey them, and your soul loathes My judgments, so that you do not perform all My commandments, so as to break My covenant,

**26:16** then I also will do thus to you: I will bring upon you perplexity, and the scab and jaundice, with your eyes wasting away and your soul melting away; and you shall sow your seeds in vain, and your adversaries shall eat them.

**26:17** And I will set My face against you, and you shall fall before enemies; and those who hate you shall pursue you, and you shall flee when no one pursues you.

**26:18** And if even up to this point you do not obey Me, then I will chastise you yet sevenfold for your sins.

**26:19** And I will shatter the arrogance of your pride, and I will make the sky iron for you and your earth like bronze.

**26:20** And your strength shall be in vain, and your land shall not yield its seed, and the tree of your field shall not yield its fruit.

**26:21** And if after these things you walk contrary and are not willing to obey Me, I will add to you seven plagues according to your sins.

**26:22** And I will send upon you the wild beasts of the earth, and they shall devour you and consume your livestock; and I will make you few in number, and your roads shall be made desolate.

**26:23** And if by these things you are not disciplined, but walk contrary to Me,

**26:24** then I also will walk with you in contrary wrath, and I also will strike you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:25** And I will bring upon you a sword avenging the justice of the covenant, and you shall flee for refuge into your cities; and I will send forth death among you, and you shall be delivered into the hands of enemies.

**26:26** When I afflict you with a scarcity of bread, ten women shall bake your loaves in one oven, and they shall return your loaves by weight; and you shall eat, and shall not be satisfied.

**26:27** But if in spite of these things you do not obey Me, and walk contrary to Me,

**26:28** then I Myself will walk with you in contrary wrath, and I will chastise you sevenfold according to your sins.

**26:29** And you shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.

**26:30** And I will lay waste your pillars, and I will utterly destroy your wooden handmade idols, and I will place your carcasses upon the carcasses of your idols; and My soul shall loathe you.

**26:31** And I will make your cities desolate, and I will lay waste your sanctuaries, and I will by no means smell the aroma of your sacrifices.

**26:32** And I Myself will lay waste your land, and your enemies who dwell in it shall be astonished at it.

**26:33** And I will scatter you among the nations, and the advancing sword shall consume you; and your land shall be desolate, and your cities shall be desolate.

**26:34** Then the land shall enjoy its Sabbaths, even all the days of its desolation,

**26:35** and you shall be in the land of your enemies; then the land shall keep Sabbath, and the land shall enjoy its Sabbaths. All the days of its desolation it shall keep the Sabbath rest which it did not keep during your Sabbaths, when you dwelt in it.

**26:36** And upon those who are left of you I will bring bondage into their heart in the land of their enemies; and the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them, and they shall flee as those fleeing from war, and they shall fall when no one pursues.

**26:37** And brother shall disregard brother as in war when no one pursues, and you shall not be able to withstand your enemies.

**26:38** And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall devour you.

**26:39** And those who are left of you shall decay because of your sins; in the land of their enemies they shall pine away.

**26:40** And they shall confess their sins and the sins of their fathers, that they transgressed and disregarded Me, and that they walked contrary against Me,

**26:41** and I walked with them in contrary wrath; and I will destroy them in the land of their enemies. Then their uncircumcised heart shall be ashamed, and then they shall accept their sins.

**26:42** And I will remember the covenant of Jacob, and the covenant of Isaac and the covenant of Abraham I will remember; and the land I will remember.

**26:43** And the land shall be forsaken by them; then the land shall accept its Sabbaths when it is made desolate because of them, and they themselves shall accept their iniquities, because they disregarded My judgments and loathed My ordinances in their soul.

**26:44** And yet even so, while they are in the land of their enemies, I did not disregard them, nor did I loathe them so as to consume them utterly, to break My covenant with them; for I am the Lord their God.

**26:45** And I will remember for them the former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, in the sight of the nations, to be their God; I am the Lord.

**26:46** These are My judgments and My ordinances, and the law which the Lord gave between Himself and the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek text begins with the declaration 'I am the Lord your God' and renders the forbidden cultic stone as 'lithon skopon' (literally a lookout stone or stone set up to gaze upon), reflecting an interpretation of the Hebrew term as connected with looking or viewing.
- **26:11** — The Septuagint reads 'My covenant' (diatheken) here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'My dwelling' or 'My tabernacle.'
- **26:21** — Here and in verses 23, 24, 27, 28, 40, and 41, the Greek uses 'plagios' (literally sideways, crooked, or contrary) both for Israel's willful opposition and for the Lord's responding wrath.
- **26:36** — The Greek critical text reads 'doulian' ('bondage/servitude'), whereas other Septuagint manuscripts read 'deilian' ('timidity/faintheartedness').
- **26:41** — The Septuagint reads the future 'I will destroy them' (apolo) rather than the Masoretic Text's 'I brought them,' and uses 'eudokesousin' ('accept' or 'acquiesce in,' i.e., the penalty of their sins), echoing the same verb used for the land enjoying its Sabbaths in verses 34-35.
