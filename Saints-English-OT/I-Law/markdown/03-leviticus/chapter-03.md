## Text

**3:1** Now if his gift to the Lord is a sacrifice of deliverance, if he brings it from his herds, whether male or female, he shall bring it without blemish before the Lord.

**3:2** And he shall lay his hands upon the head of the gift and slay it before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood upon the altar of whole burnt offerings all around.

**3:3** And they shall bring from the sacrifice of deliverance an offering to the Lord: the fat that covers the belly and all the fat that is upon the belly,

**3:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and the lobe that is on the liver he shall remove along with the kidneys.

**3:5** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall offer them up upon the altar, upon the whole burnt offerings, upon the wood that is on the fire upon the altar: an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**3:6** And if his gift is from the flocks, a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, male or female, he shall bring it without blemish.

**3:7** If he brings a lamb as his gift, he shall bring it before the Lord;

**3:8** and he shall lay his hands upon the head of his gift and slay it beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood upon the altar all around.

**3:9** And he shall bring from the sacrifice of deliverance an offering to God: the fat and the unblemished loin—he shall remove it along with the flanks—and the fat of the belly,

**3:10** and both kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and having removed the lobe that is on the liver along with the kidneys,

**3:11** the priest shall offer them up upon the altar: an aroma of sweet fragrance, an offering to the Lord.

**3:12** And if his gift is from the goats, then he shall bring it before the Lord;

**3:13** and he shall lay his hands upon its head, and they shall slay it before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood upon the altar all around.

**3:14** And he shall offer up upon it an offering to the Lord: the fat that covers the belly and all the fat that is upon the belly,

**3:15** and both kidneys and all the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and the lobe of the liver he shall remove along with the kidneys.

**3:16** And the priest shall offer them up upon the altar: an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord. All the fat belongs to the Lord.

**3:17** It shall be a statute forever throughout your generations in all your dwellings: you shall eat neither any fat nor any blood.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Greek thysia soteriou ('sacrifice of deliverance') is the Septuagint's characteristic rendering of the Hebrew peace or well-being offering.
- **3:2** — The Greek specifies plural 'hands' (also in 3:8 and 3:13) and uniquely names 'the altar of whole burnt offerings' in this verse.
- **3:9** — The Greek reads 'to God' rather than 'to the Lord' and renders the fat tail of the sheep as 'the unblemished loin' removed along with 'the flanks' (psoai, the loin muscles).
- **3:11** — Here and in 3:16 the Greek reads 'an aroma of sweet fragrance' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'food' or 'bread.'
