## Text

**16:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses after the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they offered strange fire before the Lord and died;

**16:2** and the Lord said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron your brother, and let him not enter at all times into the holy place within the veil, before the mercy seat which is upon the ark of the testimony, and he shall not die; for I will appear in a cloud upon the mercy seat.

**16:3** Thus Aaron shall enter into the holy place: with a calf from the herd as a sin offering, and a ram as a burnt offering.

**16:4** And he shall put on a consecrated linen tunic, and linen trousers shall be upon his flesh; and he shall be girded with a linen sash, and shall wear a linen turban. These are holy garments, and he shall bathe all his body in water and put them on.

**16:5** And from the congregation of the children of Israel he shall take two kids of the goats as a sin offering, and one ram as a burnt offering.

**16:6** And Aaron shall present the calf for his sin offering, and make atonement for himself and for his house.

**16:7** And he shall take the two goats and set them before the Lord at the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**16:8** And Aaron shall place upon the two goats one lot for the Lord and one lot for the scapegoat.

**16:9** And Aaron shall bring forward the goat on which the lot for the Lord fell, and offer it as a sin offering.

**16:10** But the goat on which the lot of the scapegoat fell he shall set alive before the Lord, to make atonement over it, so as to send it away for dismissal; he shall release it into the wilderness.

**16:11** And Aaron shall present the calf for the sin offering which is for himself and for his house alone, and make atonement for himself and for his house, and he shall slaughter the calf for the sin offering which is for himself.

**16:12** And he shall take the censer full of burning coals of fire from the altar that is before the Lord, and fill his hands with finely compounded incense, and bring it within the veil.

**16:13** And he shall put the incense upon the fire before the Lord, and the vapor of the incense shall cover the mercy seat that is upon the testimonies, and he shall not die.

**16:14** And he shall take some of the blood of the calf and sprinkle it with his finger upon the mercy seat on the east side; and in front of the mercy seat he shall sprinkle some of the blood seven times with his finger.

**16:15** And he shall slaughter the goat of the sin offering which is for the people before the Lord, and bring some of its blood within the veil, and do with its blood as he did with the blood of the calf, and sprinkle its blood upon the mercy seat, in front of the mercy seat.

**16:16** And he shall make atonement for the holy place because of the uncleannesses of the children of Israel, and because of their wrongdoings concerning all their sins; and so he shall do for the tabernacle of testimony which is established among them in the midst of their uncleanness.

**16:17** And no person shall be in the tabernacle of testimony when he enters to make atonement in the holy place, until he comes out; and he shall make atonement for himself and for his house, and for all the congregation of the children of Israel.

**16:18** And he shall go out to the altar that is before the Lord and make atonement upon it; and he shall take some of the blood of the calf and some of the blood of the goat, and put it on the horns of the altar all around.

**16:19** And he shall sprinkle some of the blood upon it with his finger seven times, and cleanse it and sanctify it from the uncleannesses of the children of Israel.

**16:20** And he shall finish making atonement for the holy place and the tabernacle of testimony and the altar, and he shall make cleansing for the priests; and he shall bring forward the live goat.

**16:21** And Aaron shall lay his hands on the head of the live goat, and confess over it all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their wrongdoings, and all their sins, and he shall lay them upon the head of the live goat, and send it away by the hand of a ready man into the wilderness.

**16:22** And the goat shall bear upon itself their wrongdoings to an untrodden land, and he shall send the goat away into the wilderness.

**16:23** And Aaron shall enter into the tabernacle of testimony, and take off the linen robe which he had put on when he entered into the holy place, and shall leave it there.

**16:24** And he shall bathe his body in water in a holy place and put on his own robe, and coming out he shall offer his whole burnt offering and the whole burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for his house and for the people, as for the priests.

**16:25** And the fat of the sin offerings he shall offer up upon the altar.

**16:26** And he who sends away the goat that was set apart for release shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and afterward he shall enter into the camp.

**16:27** And the calf for the sin offering and the goat for the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, they shall carry outside the camp, and they shall burn them completely with fire, both their skins and their flesh and their dung.

**16:28** And he who burns them shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and afterward he shall enter into the camp.

**16:29** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you: in the seventh month humble your souls, and you shall do no work at all, neither the native-born nor the proselyte who dwells among you.

**16:30** For on this day he shall make atonement for you, to cleanse you from all your sins before the Lord; and you shall be cleansed.

**16:31** This shall be a Sabbath of Sabbaths of rest to you, and you shall humble your souls—a perpetual statute.

**16:32** And the priest whom they anoint and whose hands they consecrate to serve as priest after his father shall make atonement, and shall put on the linen robe, a holy robe.

**16:33** And he shall make atonement for the Holy of Holies, and for the tabernacle of testimony and for the altar he shall make atonement, and for the priests and for all the congregation he shall make atonement.

**16:34** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you, to make atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins." Once a year it shall be done, as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Septuagint explicitly adds "strange fire" (pur allotrion, echoing Leviticus 10:1), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "when they drew near before the Lord."
- **16:8** — Greek to apopompaio (lit. "for the one to be sent away," traditionally "for the scapegoat") renders the Hebrew term Azazel; see also "for dismissal" (eis ten apopompen) in 16:10 and "set apart for release" (ton diestalmenon eis aphesin) in 16:26.
- **16:20** — The Septuagint includes the additional clause "and he shall make cleansing for the priests" (compare also the Septuagint additions "and for his house" and "as for the priests" in 16:24).
- **16:29** — This Greek text reads "in the seventh month" without the words "on the tenth day of the month" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek witnesses.
- **16:34** — The Septuagint reads a future passive verb, "Once a year it shall be done" (poiethesetai), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text concludes with the active narrative statement "And he did as the Lord commanded Moses."
