## Text

**5:1** "And if a soul sins and hears the voice of an adjuration, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or knows of it—if he does not report it, he shall bear the sin.

**5:2** Or if a soul touches any unclean thing, whether a carcass or an unclean animal torn by wild beasts, or any of the carcasses or of the unclean abominations or of the carcasses of unclean beasts,

**5:3** or touches any uncleanness of man, of all his uncleanness by touching which he becomes defiled, and it was hidden from him, yet afterward he comes to know it and incurs guilt;

**5:4** or the lawless soul who pronounces with his lips to do evil or to do good, according to whatever a man pronounces with an oath, and it escapes him before his eyes, and he comes to know it and sins in any one of these things—

**5:5** then he shall confess the sin concerning the things in which he has sinned regarding it,

**5:6** and he shall bring for the things in which he has trespassed against the Lord, for his sin which he has sinned, a female from the flock, a ewe lamb or a young she-goat from the goats, for a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin which he has sinned, and the sin shall be forgiven him.

**5:7** And if his hand cannot afford enough for the sheep, he shall bring for his sin which he has sinned two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the Lord, one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.

**5:8** And he shall bring them to the priest, and the priest shall offer the one for the sin offering first; and the priest shall wring its head at the neck, yet he shall not divide it.

**5:9** And he shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering upon the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood he shall drain out at the base of the altar; for it is a sin offering.

**5:10** And the second he shall prepare as a burnt offering as is prescribed; and the priest shall make atonement concerning his sin which he has sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.

**5:11** And if his hand cannot find a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring as his gift for that in which he has sinned the tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a sin offering; he shall not pour oil upon it, nor shall he put frankincense upon it, because it is for a sin offering.

**5:12** And he shall bring it to the priest. And the priest, taking a full handful from it, shall place its memorial portion upon the altar of burnt offerings to the Lord; it is a sin offering.

**5:13** And the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin which he has sinned in any one of these things, and it shall be forgiven him. And what remains shall belong to the priest, like the offering of fine flour."

**5:14** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:15** "If a soul genuinely forgets and sins unintentionally regarding the holy things of the Lord, then he shall bring for his trespass to the Lord a ram without blemish from the flock, valued in shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for that in which he has trespassed.

**5:16** And what he has sinned regarding the holy things he shall repay, and he shall add one-fifth to it, and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and it shall be forgiven him.

**5:17** And the soul who sins and does one of all the commandments of the Lord which ought not to be done, and did not know it, yet incurs guilt and bears the sin,

**5:18** then he shall bring a ram without blemish from the flock, valued in silver for a trespass offering, to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his ignorance in which he erred ignorantly, though he himself did not know it, and it shall be forgiven him;

**5:19** for he has surely committed a trespass before the Lord."

**5:20** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:21** "If a soul sins and willfully disregards the commandments of the Lord, and deals falsely in matters toward his neighbor concerning a deposit, or concerning a partnership, or concerning robbery, or has wronged his neighbor in anything,

**5:22** or has found a lost thing and lies concerning it, and swears unjustly concerning any one of all the things that a man may do so as to sin in them:

**5:23** then it shall be, whenever he sins and incurs guilt, that he shall restore the plunder which he robbed, or what he took by wronging him, or the deposit which was entrusted to him, or the lost thing which he found,

**5:24** concerning any matter about which he swore unjustly, he shall repay the principal itself, and he shall add one-fifth to it; to the one whose it is, to him he shall restore it on the day he is convicted.

**5:25** And for his trespass he shall bring to the Lord a ram without blemish from the flock, according to the valuation, for that in which he has trespassed.

**5:26** And the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord, and it shall be forgiven him concerning any one of all the things which he has done and incurred guilt in it."

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Septuagint expands the list of sources of uncleanness to include an unclean animal torn by wild beasts and carcasses of unclean beasts, while omitting the Masoretic clause stating that the matter was hidden from him.
- **5:4** — The Septuagint uniquely reads "the lawless soul" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "if a soul swears."
- **5:15** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "commits a trespass," the Septuagint reads "genuinely forgets" (literally "it escapes him with forgetfulness"), reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew root.
- **5:20** — In the Septuagint, Leviticus 5:20-26 continues chapter 5; in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most English translations, these verses begin chapter 6 (Leviticus 6:1-7).
- **5:24** — The Septuagint reads "on the day he is convicted," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "on the day of his guilt offering."
