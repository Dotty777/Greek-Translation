## Text

**15:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'When any man has a discharge from his body, his discharge is unclean.

**15:3** And this is the law of his uncleanness: when he flows with an issue from his body, from the discharge by which his body is stopped up through the discharge, this is his uncleanness in him; all the days of the discharge of his body, by which his body is stopped up through the discharge, it is his uncleanness.

**15:4** Every bed on which he who has a discharge lies is unclean, and every object on which he who has a discharge sits shall be unclean.

**15:5** And anyone who touches his bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:6** And he who sits on the object on which he who has a discharge sat shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:7** And he who touches the flesh of him who has a discharge shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:8** And if he who has a discharge spits on one who is clean, he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:9** And every saddle of a donkey on which he who has a discharge rides shall be unclean until evening.

**15:10** And everyone who touches anything that was under him shall be unclean until evening; and he who carries those things shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:11** And everyone whom he who has a discharge touches, when he has not rinsed his hands, shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:12** And an earthen vessel which he who has a discharge touches shall be broken; and a wooden vessel shall be rinsed with water, and it shall be clean.

**15:13** And if he who has a discharge is cleansed from his discharge, then he shall count for himself seven days for his cleansing, and wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and he shall be clean.

**15:14** And on the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them before the Lord to the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and give them to the priest.

**15:15** And the priest shall offer them, one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord because of his discharge.

**15:16** And if a man has an emission of seed go out from him, then he shall bathe all his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:17** And every garment and every skin on which there is an emission of seed shall be washed with water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:18** And as for a woman, if a man lies with her with an emission of seed, they shall bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:19** And if a woman has a flow of blood, and her discharge is in her body, she shall be seven days in her separation; and everyone who touches her shall be unclean until evening.

**15:20** And everything on which she lies during her separation shall be unclean, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean.

**15:21** And everyone who touches her bed shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:22** And everyone who touches any object on which she sat shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:23** And if, while she is on her bed or on the object on which she sits, he touches her, he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:24** And if anyone indeed lies with her, and her uncleanness comes upon him, then he shall be unclean seven days; and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

**15:25** And if a woman has a discharge of blood for many days, not at the time of her separation, or if it flows beyond her separation, all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness shall be as the days of separation; she shall be unclean.

**15:26** And every bed on which she lies all the days of the discharge shall be to her as the bed of separation, and every object on which she sits shall be unclean, according to the uncleanness of separation.

**15:27** Everyone who touches her shall be unclean, and shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:28** And if she is cleansed from the discharge, then she shall count for herself seven days, and after that she shall be cleansed.

**15:29** And on the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them to the priest, to the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**15:30** And the priest shall offer the one as a sin offering and the one as a burnt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for her before the Lord because of the discharge of her uncleanness.'

**15:31** And you shall make the children of Israel cautious of their uncleannesses, and they shall not die because of their uncleanness, when they defile My tabernacle that is among them."

**15:32** This is the law of him who has a discharge, and of anyone from whom an emission of seed goes out so that he is defiled by it,

**15:33** and of her who has a flow of blood in her separation, and of the one who has a discharge in his discharge, for the male or for the female, and for the man who lies with a woman who is in separation.

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — Greek rheon gonon ("flowing with an issue/seed") introduces the discharge terminology used throughout the chapter (gonorrhues). The Septuagint reads sunesteken ("is stopped up") and includes an expanded final clause not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:9** — The Septuagint specifies "of a donkey" (onou) and adds "until evening" (heos hesperas), both absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:23** — The Septuagint reads a genitive absolute ("while she is on her bed") and feminine autes ("when he touches her"), followed by the neuter akatharton ("unclean"), translated "he shall be unclean" in agreement with the preceding masculine pronoun auton.
- **15:31** — Greek eulabeis poiesete ("make cautious" or "make reverent") reflects a distinctive Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew verb commonly translated "separate" or "warn."
