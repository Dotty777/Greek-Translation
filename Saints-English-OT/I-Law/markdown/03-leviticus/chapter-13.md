## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

**13:2** If any man has on the skin of his flesh a bright scar of a mark, and it becomes on the skin of his flesh a plague of leprosy, then he shall be brought to Aaron the priest, or to one of his sons the priests.

**13:3** And the priest shall examine the plague on the skin of his flesh; and if the hair in the plague has turned white, and the appearance of the plague is lower than the skin of the flesh, it is a plague of leprosy; and the priest shall look upon it and pronounce him unclean.

**13:4** But if there is a bright white spot on the skin of his flesh, and its appearance is not lower than his skin, and his hair has not turned to white hair, and it is dim, then the priest shall isolate the plague for seven days.

**13:5** And the priest shall examine the plague on the seventh day; and behold, the plague remains before him, and the plague has not spread on the skin, then the priest shall isolate him for seven days a second time.

**13:6** And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day a second time; and behold, the plague is dim, and the plague has not spread on the skin, the priest shall pronounce him clean; for it is a mark; and after washing his garments, he shall be clean.

**13:7** But if the mark changes and spreads on the skin after the priest has examined him to pronounce him clean, then he shall appear a second time before the priest;

**13:8** and the priest shall examine him; and behold, the mark has spread on the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:9** And if a plague of leprosy is on a man, then he shall come to the priest;

**13:10** and the priest shall examine him; and behold, there is a white scar on the skin, and it has turned the hair white, and there is some of the sound, living flesh in the scar,

**13:11** it is an old leprosy; it is on the skin of the flesh; and the priest shall pronounce him unclean and isolate him, because he is unclean.

**13:12** And if the leprosy breaks out abundantly on the skin, and the leprosy covers all the skin of the plague from head to foot, as far as the entire sight of the priest reaches,

**13:13** then the priest shall look, and behold, the leprosy has covered all the skin of the flesh, and the priest shall pronounce him clean of the plague; because all of it has turned white, it is clean.

**13:14** And on whatever day living flesh appears on him, he shall be unclean;

**13:15** and the priest shall examine the sound flesh, and the sound flesh shall defile him, because it is unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:16** But if the sound flesh is restored and turns white, then he shall come to the priest;

**13:17** and the priest shall examine him, and behold, the plague has turned white, then the priest shall pronounce the plague clean; he is clean.

**13:18** And if the flesh has a sore on its skin, and it is healed,

**13:19** and in the place of the sore there arises a white scar, or a bright spot that is whitish or reddish, then he shall appear before the priest.

**13:20** And the priest shall examine it; and behold, its appearance is lower than the skin, and its hair has turned white, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:21** But if the priest examines it, and behold, there is no white hair in it, and it is not lower than the skin of the flesh, and it is dim, the priest shall isolate him for seven days.

**13:22** And if it spreads on the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:23** But if the bright spot remains in its place and does not spread, it is the scar of the sore, and the priest shall pronounce him clean.

**13:24** And if the flesh has a burn of fire on its skin, and the healed part of the burn on its skin becomes a shining, bright white spot, somewhat reddish or very white,

**13:25** then the priest shall examine him; and behold, the hair in the shining spot has turned white, and its appearance is lower than the skin, it is leprosy; it has broken out in the burn; and the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy.

**13:26** But if the priest examines it, and behold, there is no white hair in the shining spot, and it is not lower than the skin, and it is dim, then the priest shall isolate him for seven days.

**13:27** And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day; and if it indeed spreads on the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:28** But if the shining spot remains in its place and has not spread on the skin, and it is dim, it is the scar of the burn, and the priest shall pronounce him clean; for it is the imprint of the burn.

**13:29** And if either a man or a woman has a plague of leprosy on the head or on the beard,

**13:30** then the priest shall examine the plague; and behold, its appearance is deeper than the skin, and in it there is thin yellowish hair, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is a scale; it is leprosy of the head or leprosy of the beard.

**13:31** And if the priest examines the plague of the wound, and behold, its appearance is not deeper than the skin, and there is no yellowish hair in it, then the priest shall isolate the plague of the scale for seven days;

**13:32** and the priest shall examine the plague on the seventh day; and behold, the scale has not spread, and there is no yellowish hair in it, and the appearance of the scale is not sunken below the skin,

**13:33** then the skin shall be shaved, but the scale shall not be shaved, and the priest shall isolate the scale for seven days a second time.

**13:34** And the priest shall examine the scale on the seventh day; and behold, the scale has not spread on the skin after he was shaved, and the appearance of the scale is not sunken below the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him clean; and after washing his garments, he shall be clean.

**13:35** But if the scale indeed spreads on the skin after he has been pronounced clean,

**13:36** then the priest shall examine him; and behold, the scale has spread on the skin, the priest shall not inspect for yellow hair, because he is unclean;

**13:37** but if the scale remains in its place before him and black hair grows up in it, the scale has healed; it is clean, and the priest shall pronounce him clean.

**13:38** And if a man or a woman has on the skin of his flesh a shining spot, shining whitish spots,

**13:39** then the priest shall examine him; and behold, on the skin of his flesh there are gleaming, whitish spots, it is a white rash; he is clean; it will break out on the skin of his flesh; he is clean.

**13:40** And if anyone loses the hair of his head, he is bald; he is clean;

**13:41** and if his head loses its hair toward the front, he is bald on the forehead; he is clean.

**13:42** But if there arises on his bald head or on his bald forehead a white or reddish plague, it is leprosy on his bald head or on his bald forehead;

**13:43** and the priest shall examine him; and behold, the appearance of the plague is reddish-white on his bald head or on his bald forehead, like the appearance of leprosy on the skin of his flesh,

**13:44** he is a leprous man; the priest shall surely pronounce him unclean; his plague is on his head.

**13:45** And as for the leper in whom the plague is, let his garments be loosened, and his head uncovered, and let him put a covering around his mouth, and he shall be called unclean;

**13:46** all the days that the plague is upon him, being unclean, he shall be unclean; he shall dwell apart; outside the camp shall be his dwelling.

**13:47** And if a garment has a plague of leprosy in it, whether in a woolen garment or in a flaxen garment,

**13:48** or in the warp or in the woof, either of linen or of wool, or in leather, or in any wrought leather,

**13:49** and the plague becomes greenish or reddish in the leather, or in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any wrought article of leather, it is a plague of leprosy, and he shall show it to the priest.

**13:50** And the priest shall examine the plague, and the priest shall isolate the plague for seven days.

**13:51** And the priest shall examine the plague on the seventh day; and if the plague has spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in the leather, in all leathers that may be made in workmanship, the plague is a persistent leprosy; it is unclean.

**13:52** He shall burn the garment, or the warp, or the woof, whether of wool or of linen, or any article of leather in which the plague is; because it is leprosy, it shall be burned in fire.

**13:53** But if the priest examines it, and the plague has not spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of leather,

**13:54** then the priest shall command, and he shall wash that upon which the plague is, and the priest shall isolate the plague for seven days a second time.

**13:55** And the priest shall examine the plague after it has been washed; and if this plague has not changed its appearance, and the plague does not spread, it is unclean; it shall be burned in fire; it is fixed fast in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof.

**13:56** And if the priest examines it, and the plague is dim after it has been washed, he shall tear it out from the garment, or from the leather, or from the warp, or from the woof.

**13:57** But if it still appears in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of leather, it is leprosy breaking out; that in which the plague is shall be burned in fire.

**13:58** And as for the garment, or the warp, or the woof, or any article of leather that is washed, and the plague departs from it, then it shall be washed a second time; it shall be clean.

**13:59** This is the law of the plague of leprosy of a woolen or flaxen garment, or of warp or woof, or of any article of leather, to pronounce it clean or to pronounce it unclean.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Greek phrase oule semasias telauges ("a bright scar of a mark") presents a single composite description rather than the three separate skin conditions listed in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:4** — The Greek text reads the feminine possessive pronoun autes ("her/its") with "flesh" and "skin" before switching to masculine autou ("his hair"); "his" is used here for English coherence. The Septuagint also uniquely includes "and it is dim" and directs the priest to isolate "the plague."
- **13:11** — The Septuagint reads affirmatively "and isolate him," in contrast to the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which states that the priest shall not shut him up.
- **13:15** — In the Septuagint, "the sound flesh" (rendered with hygies, "sound/healthy," corresponding to the "living flesh" of v. 14) is the grammatical subject that defiles the person.
- **13:27** — The Septuagint concludes this verse with "it has broken out in the sore" (echoing v. 22) even within the regulations concerning a burn (vv. 24–28).
- **13:31** — The Greek text reads traumatos ("of the wound") in the first clause and thrausmatos ("of the scale") in the second, and specifies "yellowish hair" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "black hair."
- **13:45** — The Septuagint directs the leper to put a covering "around his mouth" and uses the passive "and he shall be called unclean," rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's active cry "Unclean, unclean."
- **13:55** — The Septuagint concludes with "it is fixed fast in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof," in place of the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to a depression on the back or front of the fabric.
