## Text

**6:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:2** "Command Aaron and his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the burnt offering: the burnt offering itself shall remain on its burning place on the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not be put out.

**6:3** And the priest shall put on a linen tunic, and he shall put linen trousers upon his body, and he shall take up the consumed remains which the fire has consumed—the burnt offering—from the altar, and place them beside the altar.

**6:4** Then he shall take off his garment and put on another garment, and carry the consumed remains outside the camp to a clean place.

**6:5** And fire upon the altar shall be kept burning from it, and it shall not be put out; and the priest shall burn wood on it in the morning, and stack the burnt offering upon it, and place upon it the fat of the deliverance offering.

**6:6** And fire shall burn continually upon the altar; it shall not be put out.

**6:7** This is the law of the sacrifice which the sons of Aaron shall bring near before the Lord, in front of the altar:

**6:8** He shall take from it with his handful some of the fine flour of the sacrifice, with its oil and with its frankincense that are upon the sacrifice, and he shall offer it up upon the altar as an offering made by fire—a sweet aroma, its memorial portion to the Lord.

**6:9** And the remainder of it Aaron and his sons shall eat; it shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place—in the court of the tabernacle of testimony they shall eat it.

**6:10** It shall not be baked leavened. I have given it to them as a portion from the offerings made by fire to the Lord; it is most holy, like the sin offering and like the trespass offering.

**6:11** Every male among the priests shall eat it. It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations from the offerings made by fire to the Lord. Everyone who touches them shall be made holy.'"

**6:12** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:13** "This is the gift of Aaron and of his sons, which they shall offer to the Lord on the day when you anoint him: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a perpetual sacrifice, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

**6:14** It shall be prepared on a griddle with oil; kneaded well he shall bring it—rolled pieces, a sacrifice of broken pieces, a sacrifice as a sweet aroma to the Lord.

**6:15** The priest from among his sons who is anointed in his place shall prepare it. It is a perpetual law; the whole of it shall be completely offered.

**6:16** And every sacrifice of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten."

**6:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:18** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the sin offering: In the place where they slay the burnt offering, they shall slay the sin offering before the Lord; it is most holy.

**6:19** The priest who offers it up shall eat it. In a holy place it shall be eaten, in the court of the tabernacle of testimony.

**6:20** Everyone who touches its flesh shall be made holy; and whoever has any of its blood sprinkled upon a garment—that upon which it is sprinkled shall be washed in a holy place.

**6:21** And an earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; but if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, he shall scour it and rinse it out with water.

**6:22** Every male among the priests shall eat them; they are most holy to the Lord.

**6:23** And all sin offerings from which any of their blood is brought into the tabernacle of testimony to make atonement in the holy place shall not be eaten; they shall be burned up with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — Greek katakarposis literally denotes the burnt-up offering or consumed remains, corresponding to the Hebrew term for fatty ashes.
- **6:7** — Throughout Septuagint Leviticus, standalone thysia ("sacrifice") is used specifically for the grain or tribute offering.
- **6:14** — Greek helikta ("rolled pieces") refers to pastry or dough pieces rolled or twisted before being broken.
- **6:15** — Greek epitelesthesetai ("shall be completely offered") emphasizes the full completion of the rite, whereas the Hebrew reads "shall be wholly burned."
