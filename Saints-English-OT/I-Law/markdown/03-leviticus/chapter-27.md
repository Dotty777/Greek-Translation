## Text

**27:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**27:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Whoever vows a vow as a valuation of his soul to the Lord,

**27:3** the valuation of the male from twenty years old to sixty years old shall be—his valuation shall be fifty didrachmas of silver by the holy weight.

**27:4** And for the female, the assessed valuation shall be thirty didrachmas.

**27:5** And if from five years old to twenty years, the valuation of the male shall be twenty didrachmas, and of the female fourteen didrachmas.

**27:6** And from one month old to five years old, the valuation of the male shall be five didrachmas of silver, and of the female three didrachmas.

**27:7** And if from sixty years old and above, if it is a male, the valuation shall be fifteen didrachmas of silver; and if a female, ten didrachmas.

**27:8** But if he is too poor for the valuation, he shall stand before the priest, and the priest shall value him; just as the hand of him who vowed is able, the priest shall value him.

**27:9** Now if it is of the beasts from which a gift is offered to the Lord, whoever gives from these to the Lord, it shall be holy.

**27:10** He shall not exchange it, a good one for a bad one, nor a bad one for a good one; and if he does indeed exchange it, beast for beast, both it and the exchange shall be holy.

**27:11** And if it is any unclean beast, of those from which a gift is not offered to the Lord, he shall set the beast before the priest;

**27:12** and the priest shall value it between good and bad; and just as the priest values it, so shall it stand.

**27:13** But if he indeed redeems it, he shall add the fifth part to its valuation.

**27:14** And whatever man consecrates his house to be holy to the Lord, then the priest shall value it between good and bad; as the priest values it, so shall it stand.

**27:15** But if he who consecrated it redeems his house, he shall add to it the fifth part of the silver of the valuation, and it shall be his.

**27:16** And if a man consecrates to the Lord part of the field of his possession, then the valuation shall be according to its seed: for a kor of barley, fifty didrachmas of silver.

**27:17** And if he consecrates his field from the year of release, according to its valuation it shall stand.

**27:18** But if he consecrates his field later, after the release, the priest shall reckon to him the silver according to the remaining years until the year of release, and it shall be deducted from its assessed valuation.

**27:19** And if he who bought it redeems the field, he shall add the fifth part of the silver to its valuation, and it shall be his.

**27:20** But if he does not redeem the field, and sells the field to another man, he shall no longer redeem it;

**27:21** rather, the field, when the release goes out, shall be holy to the Lord, like the land that is set apart; it shall be a possession for the priest.

**27:22** And if he consecrates to the Lord part of a field which he has acquired, which is not of the field of his possession,

**27:23** the priest shall reckon to him the full amount of the valuation from the year of release, and he shall pay the valuation on that day as a holy thing to the Lord;

**27:24** and in the year of release the field shall be restored to the man from whom he acquired it, to whom the possession of the land belonged.

**27:25** And every valuation shall be by holy weights; twenty obols shall be the didrachma.

**27:26** And every firstborn that is born among your beasts shall belong to the Lord, and no one shall consecrate it, whether calf or sheep; it belongs to the Lord.

**27:27** But if it is of the unclean four-footed beasts, he shall exchange it according to its valuation and shall add the fifth part to it, and it shall be his; but if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold according to its valuation.

**27:28** Yet every devoted thing that a man devotes to the Lord from all that belongs to him, from man to beast, and from the field of his possession, shall not be sold nor redeemed; every devoted thing shall be most holy to the Lord.

**27:29** And whatever is devoted from among men shall not be redeemed, but shall surely be put to death.

**27:30** Every tithe of the land, of the seed of the land and of the fruit of the trees, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord.

**27:31** And if a man redeems his tithe by ransom, he shall add the fifth part to it, and it shall be his.

**27:32** And every tithe of oxen and of sheep, even everything that passes in the counting under the rod, the tenth shall be holy to the Lord.

**27:33** You shall not exchange a good one for a bad one; and if you do indeed exchange it, then its exchange also shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.'"

**27:34** These are the commandments which the Lord commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Greek text renders the Hebrew sanctuary shekel as the didrachma (a two-drachma silver coin, valued at twenty obols in verse 25) and refers to the sanctuary standard as the holy weight.
- **27:5** — The Greek text reads fourteen didrachmas for a female aged five to twenty, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads ten shekels.
- **27:19** — The Greek text reads he who bought it, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads he who consecrated it.
- **27:23** — The Greek text uses release throughout this chapter for the Jubilee year and here reads from the year of release, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads until the year of Jubilee.
