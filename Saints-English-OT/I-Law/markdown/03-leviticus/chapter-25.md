## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'When you enter into the land which I give to you, then the land which I give to you shall rest a Sabbath to the Lord.

**25:3** Six years you shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vineyard and gather its fruit;

**25:4** but in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of rest for the land, a Sabbath to the Lord; you shall not sow your field, and you shall not prune your vineyard,

**25:5** and what springs up of itself in your field you shall not reap, and the grapes of your sanctification you shall not gather; it shall be a year of rest for the land.

**25:6** And the Sabbath of the land shall be food for you, and for your manservant, and for your maidservant, and for your hired worker, and for the sojourner who abides with you;

**25:7** and for your livestock and for the wild beasts that are in your land, all its produce shall be for food.

**25:8** And you shall count for yourself seven rests of years, seven years seven times; and seven weeks of years shall be to you forty-nine years.

**25:9** And you shall make proclamation with the sound of a trumpet throughout all your land, in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month; on the Day of Atonement you shall make proclamation with a trumpet throughout all your land.

**25:10** And sanctify the fiftieth year, and you shall proclaim release upon the land to all who dwell in it; this shall be to you a year, a signal of release, and each one shall depart to his possession, and each of you shall depart to his homeland.

**25:11** This is a signal of release; the fiftieth year shall be a year to you; you shall not sow, nor reap what springs up of itself in it, and you shall not gather its consecrated fruit;

**25:12** for it is a signal of release; it shall be holy to you; from the fields you shall eat its produce.

**25:13** In the year of release, at its signal, he shall return to his possession.

**25:14** And if you make a sale to your neighbor, or if you also buy from your neighbor, let no man oppress his neighbor.

**25:15** According to the number of years after the signal you shall buy from the neighbor; according to the number of years of produce he shall sell to you.

**25:16** According as the greater number of years increases his purchase, and according as the lesser number of years diminishes his possession—for the number of his produce he shall thus sell to you.

**25:17** Let no man oppress his neighbor, and you shall fear the Lord your God; I am the Lord your God.

**25:18** And you shall do all My ordinances and all My judgments, and keep them and do them, and you shall dwell in confidence;

**25:19** and the land shall yield its produce, and you shall eat to fullness, and you shall dwell upon it in confidence.

**25:20** And if you say, "What shall we eat in this seventh year, if we do not sow nor gather in our produce?"

**25:21** then I will send My blessing to you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth its produce for the three years.

**25:22** And you shall sow in the eighth year, and you shall eat what is old from the produce; until the ninth year, until its produce comes in, you shall eat old of the old.

**25:23** And the land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is Mine; because you are strangers and sojourners before Me;

**25:24** and throughout all the land of your possession you shall grant redemption for the land.

**25:25** And if your brother who is with you becomes poor and sells some of his possession, then his kinsman who is nearest to him shall come and shall redeem what his brother sold.

**25:26** And if anyone has no kinsman-redeemer, yet he prospers by his hand and enough is found for him for his redemption,

**25:27** then he shall calculate the years of his sale, and restore what he has to the man to whom he sold himself, and he shall depart to his possession.

**25:28** But if his hand is not found to have enough to restore them, then the sale shall remain with the one who bought them until the sixth year of the release; and it shall go out in the release, and he shall depart into his rest.

**25:29** And if anyone sells a dwelling house in a walled city, then there shall be redemption for it; until a year of days is completed, there shall be redemption for it.

**25:30** But if it is not redeemed until a whole year is completed for it, then the house that is in the city having a wall shall be confirmed permanently to the one who bought it, throughout his generations, and it shall not go out in the release.

**25:31** But the houses that are in villages, which have no wall around them, let them be reckoned as the field of the land; they shall be redeemable at all times, and in the release they shall go out.

**25:32** And as for the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession shall be redeemable at all times for the Levites;

**25:33** and whoever redeems from the Levites, then their sale of the houses of the city of their possession shall go out in the release, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**25:34** And the fields set apart for their cities shall not be sold, because this is their everlasting possession.

**25:35** And if your brother becomes poor and grows weak in his hands beside you, you shall uphold him as a stranger and a sojourner, and your brother shall live with you.

**25:36** You shall not take from him interest nor with increase, and you shall fear your God; I am the Lord; and your brother shall live with you.

**25:37** You shall not give him your silver at interest, and you shall not give him your food for increase.

**25:38** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan, so as to be your God.

**25:39** And if your brother is brought low beside you and is sold to you, he shall not serve you with the service of a household servant;

**25:40** as a hired worker or a sojourner he shall be with you; until the year of release he shall work beside you,

**25:41** and he shall go out in the release, and his children with him, and he shall depart to his family; to his ancestral possession he shall hasten back.

**25:42** Because these are My household servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt, he shall not be sold in the sale of a household servant.

**25:43** You shall not wear him down with toil, and you shall fear the Lord your God.

**25:44** And as for manservant and maidservant, as many as you may have from the nations that are all around you, from them you shall acquire a male slave and a female slave.

**25:45** And from the sons of the sojourners who are among you, from these you shall acquire, and from their kindred, as many as are born in your land; let them be to you for a possession.

**25:46** And you shall apportion them to your children after you, and they shall be held as your possession forever; as for your brethren, the sons of Israel, each one shall not wear down his brother with toils.

**25:47** And if the hand of the stranger or of the sojourner beside you finds means, and your brother, being destitute, is sold to the stranger or to the sojourner beside you, to a stranger from birth,

**25:48** after he has been sold to him, there shall be redemption for him; one of your brothers shall redeem him.

**25:49** His father's brother or a son of his father's brother shall redeem him, or one from the kindred of his flesh from his tribe shall redeem him; or if, prospering by his hands, he redeems himself,

**25:50** then he shall reckon with the one who bought him from the year in which he sold himself to him until the year of release; and the silver of his sale shall be as that of a hired worker; year by year he shall be with him.

**25:51** And if more of the years remain for anyone, in proportion to these he shall repay his redemption from the silver of his sale;

**25:52** and if only a little remains of the years until the year of release, then he shall reckon with him according to his years, and he shall repay his redemption. As a hired worker,

**25:53** year by year he shall be with him; you shall not wear him down with toil in your sight.

**25:54** And if he is not redeemed after these things, he shall go out in the year of release, he and his children with him.

**25:55** For to Me the sons of Israel are household servants; these are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — The Greek 'grapes of your sanctification' reflects the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew term for untrimmed vines as something consecrated, akin to a Nazirite vow (cf. verse eleven).
- **25:10** — The phrase 'signal of release' is the Septuagint's rendering of the Jubilee, highlighting the trumpet-signal announcing liberty throughout the land (cf. verses eleven through thirteen and fifteen).
- **25:27** — The Septuagint reads 'what he has' and 'to whom he sold himself,' differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'the surplus' and 'to whom he sold it.'
- **25:28** — The Greek uniquely reads 'until the sixth year of the release' and 'into his rest,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'until the year of jubilee' and 'to his possession.'
- **25:55** — The Septuagint concludes the chapter with the Exodus reminder and omits the closing formula 'I am the Lord your God' found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
