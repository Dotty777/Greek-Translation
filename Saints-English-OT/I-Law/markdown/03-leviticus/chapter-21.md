## Text

**21:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and you shall say to them, 'They shall not be defiled for the dead among their nation,

**21:2** except for their nearest relative, for father and mother, and for sons and daughters, for a brother,

**21:3** and for a virgin sister who is near to him, who has not been given to a husband; for these he may be defiled.

**21:4** He shall not be defiled suddenly among his people, so as to profane himself.

**21:5** And you shall not shave a bald spot on your head for the dead, and they shall not shave the appearance of their beard, nor shall they make cuttings in their flesh.

**21:6** They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God; for they offer the sacrifices of the Lord, the gifts of their God, and they shall be holy.

**21:7** They shall not take a woman who is a harlot and profaned, nor a woman cast out from her husband; he is holy to the Lord his God,

**21:8** and he shall sanctify him; he offers the gifts of the Lord your God. He shall be holy, for I, the Lord who sanctifies them, am holy.

**21:9** And if the daughter of a man who is a priest is profaned by playing the harlot, she profanes the name of her father; she shall be burned with fire.

**21:10** And the high priest among his brethren, upon whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to wear the garments, shall not remove the turban from his head nor tear his garments,

**21:11** nor shall he go in to any dead person; he shall not be defiled for his father nor for his mother,

**21:12** nor shall he go out from the holy place, nor profane the sanctuary of his God; for the holy anointing oil of God is upon him. I am the Lord.

**21:13** He shall take a virgin from his own lineage;

**21:14** but a widow, or a woman cast out, or one profaned, or a harlot—these he shall not take; rather, he shall take a virgin from his own people as a wife,

**21:15** and he shall not profane his offspring among his people; I am the Lord who sanctifies him.'"

**21:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**21:17** "Speak to Aaron, 'A man from your lineage throughout your generations, whoever has a blemish in him, shall not approach to offer the gifts of his God.

**21:18** Every man in whom there is a blemish shall not approach: a man who is lame or blind,

**21:19** or mutilated in the nose or cut in the ear, a man who has a broken hand or a broken foot,

**21:20** or hunchbacked, or having white spots in the eye, or blear-eyed, or a man in whom there is a malignant itch, or a scaly eruption, or having only one testicle—

**21:21** everyone in whom there is a blemish from the offspring of Aaron the priest shall not draw near to offer the sacrifices to your God; because there is a blemish in him, he shall not approach to offer the gifts of God.

**21:22** The gifts of God, the most holy things and from the holy things, he may eat;

**21:23** only he shall not approach the veil, nor draw near to the altar, because he has a blemish; and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his God, for I am the Lord who sanctifies them.'"

**21:24** And Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons, and to all the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **21:4** — Greek exapina ("suddenly") is a distinctive Septuagint rendering where the Masoretic Text has a difficult Hebrew phrase often understood as "as a leader" or "as a husband."
- **21:8** — Greek hagiasei auton ("he shall sanctify him") is third-person singular rather than the second-person singular of the Masoretic Text; it may also be understood reflexively ("sanctify himself") or with the Lord as subject.
- **21:10** — Greek apokidarosei specifically refers to removing the priestly turban (kidaris) from the head, interpreting the Hebrew command not to leave the head uncovered or disheveled.
- **21:20** — The Greek medical terms ephelos ("having white spots in the eye"), ptilos ("blear-eyed"), and monorchis ("having only one testicle") represent specific Septuagint interpretations of rare Hebrew blemish terms.
