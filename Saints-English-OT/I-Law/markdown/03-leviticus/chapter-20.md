## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "You shall also speak to the sons of Israel:

**20:2** 'If anyone of the sons of Israel, or of the sojourners who have come to be in Israel, gives of his seed to a ruler, he shall surely be put to death; the nation upon the land shall stone him with stones.

**20:3** And I will set My face against that man, and will destroy him from among his people, because he gave of his seed to a ruler, in order to defile My holy places and to profane the name of those sanctified to Me.

**20:4** And if the native-born of the land completely overlook that man with their eyes when he gives of his seed to a ruler, so as not to kill him,

**20:5** then I will set My face against that man and against his kindred, and I will destroy him and all who are of one mind with him, so that he plays the harlot after the rulers, from among their people.

**20:6** And the soul that follows after mediums or enchanters, to play the harlot after them, I will set My face against that soul and will destroy it from among its people.

**20:7** And you shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord your God;

**20:8** and you shall keep My ordinances and perform them; I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**20:9** Whoever speaks evil of his father or his mother shall surely be put to death; he has spoken evil of his father or his mother; he shall be guilty.

**20:10** A man who commits adultery with the wife of a man, or who commits adultery with the wife of his neighbor, both the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

**20:11** If anyone lies with his father's wife, he has uncovered his father's nakedness; he shall be put to death; both of them are guilty.

**20:12** And if anyone lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death; for they have committed impiety; they are guilty.

**20:13** And whoever lies with a male as with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination; they shall be put to death; they are guilty.

**20:14** Whoever takes a woman and her mother, it is a lawless deed; they shall burn up both him and them in fire, so that there shall be no lawlessness among you.

**20:15** And whoever lies carnally with a four-footed animal shall surely be put to death, and you shall kill the four-footed animal.

**20:16** And any woman who approaches any beast to be mounted by it, you shall kill the woman and the beast; she shall surely be put to death; they are guilty.

**20:17** Whoever takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness, it is a reproach; they shall be utterly destroyed before the sons of their kindred; he has uncovered his sister's nakedness; they shall bear sin.

**20:18** And a man who lies with a woman in her menstrual separation and uncovers her nakedness has uncovered her fountain, and she has uncovered the flow of her blood; both of them shall be utterly destroyed from among their kindred.

**20:19** And you shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister or of your mother's sister; for he has uncovered his close kin; they shall bear sin.

**20:20** Whoever lies with his kinswoman has uncovered the nakedness of his kinswoman; they shall die childless.

**20:21** Whoever takes his brother's wife, it is his uncleanness; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall die childless.

**20:22** And keep all My ordinances and My judgments, and perform them; and the land into which I am bringing you to dwell upon it shall by no means loathe you.

**20:23** And you shall not walk in the customs of the nations whom I am casting out from before you; for they committed all these things, and I abhorred them.

**20:24** And I said to you, "You, inherit their land, and I will give it to you for a possession, a land flowing with milk and honey." I am the Lord your God, who has separated you from all the nations.

**20:25** And you shall distinguish them, between the clean beasts and the unclean beasts, and between the clean birds and the unclean; and you shall not make your souls detestable with the beasts, and with the birds, and with all the creeping things of the earth, which I have set apart for you in uncleanness.

**20:26** And you shall be holy to Me, for I am holy, the Lord your God, who has separated you from all the nations to be Mine.

**20:27** And a man or a woman, whichever of them becomes a medium or an enchanter, both of them shall surely be put to death; stone them with stones; they are guilty.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — Here and in 20:3-5, the Septuagint renders the Hebrew name Molech as 'ruler' (archonti; plural 'rulers' in 20:5).
- **20:3** — The Greek reads 'the name of those sanctified to Me' (to onoma ton hegiasmenon moi), whereas the Hebrew text has 'My holy name.'
- **20:9** — Here and in 20:11-13, 16, and 27, the Septuagint renders the Hebrew idiom 'his/their blood shall be upon him/them' as 'he shall be guilty' or 'they are guilty' (enochos).
- **20:20** — The Septuagint uses the broader term 'his kinswoman' (tes syngenous autou) where the Hebrew specifies 'his uncle's wife.'
