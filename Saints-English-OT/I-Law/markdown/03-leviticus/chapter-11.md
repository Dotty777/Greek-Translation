## Text

**11:1** Now the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**11:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'These are the beasts which you may eat from among the beasts that are on the earth:

**11:3** Every beast that parts the hoof, dividing the clefts into two hooves, and brings up the cud, among the beasts—these you may eat.

**11:4** Nevertheless, of these you shall not eat, among those that bring up the cud and among those that part the hooves and divide the clefts: the camel, because it brings up the cud, yet does not part the hoof—it is unclean to you;

**11:5** and the hare, because it brings up the cud and does not part the hoof—it is unclean to you;

**11:6** and the rock badger, because it brings up the cud and does not part the hoof—it is unclean to you.

**11:7** And the swine, because it parts the hoof and divides the clefts of the hoof, yet does not bring up the cud—it is unclean to you.

**11:8** Of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch; these are unclean to you.

**11:9** And these you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the streams, these you may eat.

**11:10** And all that do not have fins or scales in the water, or in the seas and in the streams, of all things that the waters bring forth and of every living creature that is in the water, are an abomination; and abominations they shall be

**11:11** to you; of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall hold in abomination.

**11:12** And whatever does not have fins and scales among those in the water, this is an abomination to you.

**11:13** And these are what you shall hold in abomination among the birds, and they shall not be eaten—they are an abomination: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the sea eagle,

**11:14** the vulture, and the kite and those like it,

**11:16** the ostrich, the owl, the gull and those like it, and the hawk and those like it,

**11:17** the night raven, the cormorant, and the ibis,

**11:18** the purple water hen, the pelican, and the swan,

**11:19** the owl, the heron, the plover and those like it, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**11:20** And all winged creeping things that go on all fours are abominations to you.

**11:21** Yet these you may eat among the winged creeping things that go on all fours—such as has legs above its feet to leap with them upon the earth.

**11:22** And these of them you may eat: the wingless locust and those like it, the cricket and those like it, the locust and those like it, and the grasshopper and those like it.

**11:23** Every creeping thing among the winged creatures that has four feet—these are abominations to you.

**11:24** And by these you shall be defiled: whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening,

**11:25** and whoever carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening.

**11:26** And among all the beasts, every one that parts the hoof and divides the clefts, yet does not chew the cud, shall be unclean to you; whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening.

**11:27** And whatever walks on its paws, among all the wild beasts that go on all fours, these shall be unclean to you; whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening,

**11:28** and whoever carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening; these are unclean to you.

**11:29** And these are unclean to you among the creeping things that are upon the earth: the weasel, the mouse, and the land crocodile,

**11:30** the shrew, the chameleon, the gecko, the lizard, and the mole.

**11:31** These are unclean to you among all the creeping things that are upon the earth; whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening.

**11:32** And anything upon which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean, whether any vessel of wood, or garment, or skin, or sackcloth; every vessel with which any work is done shall be dipped into water, and it shall be unclean until evening, and then it shall be clean.

**11:33** And every earthen vessel into which any of these falls, whatever is inside it shall be unclean, and it shall be broken.

**11:34** And every food that is eaten, upon which water comes, shall be unclean; and every drink that is drunk in any vessel shall be unclean.

**11:35** And everything upon which any part of their carcasses falls shall be unclean; ovens and pot stands shall be broken down; they are unclean, and they shall be unclean to you.

**11:36** Nevertheless, springs of water, and a cistern, and a gathering of water shall be clean; but whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean.

**11:37** And if any of their carcasses falls upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean;

**11:38** but if water has been poured upon any seed, and any of their carcasses falls upon it, it is unclean to you.

**11:39** And if any of the beasts dies which is yours to eat, whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening;

**11:40** and whoever eats of these carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening; and whoever carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**11:41** And every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth—this shall be an abomination to you; it shall not be eaten.

**11:42** And every creature that goes on its belly, and every creature that goes continually on all fours, whatever has many feet among all the creeping things that creep upon the earth—you shall not eat it, for it is an abomination to you.

**11:43** And you shall by no means make your souls abominable with any of the creeping things that creep upon the earth, nor shall you defile yourselves with these, nor shall you become unclean by them.

**11:44** For I am the Lord your God; therefore you shall be sanctified and you shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy; and you shall not defile your souls with any of the creeping things that move upon the earth.

**11:45** For I am the Lord who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; therefore you shall be holy, for I the Lord am holy.'"

**11:46** This is the law concerning the beasts, and the birds, and every creature that moves in the water, and every creature that creeps upon the earth,

**11:47** to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the creatures that bring forth living young that may be eaten and those that bring forth living young that may not be eaten.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — In verses 5 and 6, the Septuagint reverses the order of the hare (dasypoda, v. 5) and the rock badger (choirogryllion, v. 6) compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **11:14** — Verse 15 (listing the raven in the Hebrew text) is absent from this Septuagint text, and the Greek bird name glaux ("owl") occurs in both verse 16 and verse 19.
- **11:22** — The four Greek insect names—brouchos, attakes, akris, and ophiomaches (literally "serpent-fighter")—denote varieties of locusts and grasshoppers, rendered here as wingless locust, cricket, locust, and grasshopper.
- **11:47** — The Septuagint uses the participle zoogonounton ("that bring forth living young") where the Hebrew has the general term for living creatures.
