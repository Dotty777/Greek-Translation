## Text

**19:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**19:2** "Speak to the congregation of the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'You shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord your God.

**19:3** Each one shall fear his father and his mother, and you shall keep My Sabbaths; I am the Lord your God.

**19:4** You shall not follow after idols, nor shall you make for yourselves molten gods; I am the Lord your God.

**19:5** And if you offer a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, you shall sacrifice your tenth.

**19:6** On whatever day you sacrifice it, it shall be eaten, and on the next day; and if any remains until the third day, it shall be burned up in fire.

**19:7** And if it is indeed eaten on the third day, it is unfit for sacrifice; it shall not be accepted.

**19:8** And the one who eats it shall bear sin, because he has profaned the holy things of the Lord; and the souls that eat it shall be utterly destroyed from among their people.

**19:9** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish reaping your harvest of your field, nor shall you gather what falls from your harvest.

**19:10** And you shall not glean your vineyard a second time, nor shall you gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and for the sojourner; I am the Lord your God.

**19:11** You shall not steal, you shall not lie, nor shall anyone falsely accuse his neighbor.

**19:12** And you shall not swear by My name unjustly, nor shall you profane the name of your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:13** You shall not wrong your neighbor, nor rob him; and the wages of a hired servant shall by no means remain overnight with you until morning.

**19:14** You shall not speak evil of the deaf, nor put a stumbling block before the blind; but you shall fear the Lord your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:15** You shall do no injustice in judgment; you shall not show partiality to the poor, nor defer to the person of the mighty; in righteousness you shall judge your neighbor.

**19:16** You shall not walk in deceit among your nation; you shall not rise up against the blood of your neighbor; I am the Lord your God.

**19:17** You shall not hate your brother in your mind; you shall surely reprove your neighbor, and you shall not incur sin because of him.

**19:18** And your hand shall not take vengeance, nor shall you bear a grudge against the sons of your people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself; I am the Lord.

**19:19** You shall keep My law. You shall not breed your livestock with another kind, and you shall not sow your vineyard with mixed seed, nor shall you put upon yourself an adulterated garment woven of two materials.

**19:20** And if anyone lies carnally with a woman who is a maidservant reserved for a man, and she has not been redeemed with a ransom, nor has freedom been given to her, there shall be an inquiry concerning them; they shall not die, because she was not set free.

**19:21** And he shall bring for his trespass to the Lord, to the door of the tabernacle of testimony, a ram for a trespass offering.

**19:22** And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the Lord for the sin which he has sinned, and the sin which he has sinned shall be forgiven him.

**19:23** Now when you enter into the land which the Lord God gives you, and plant every kind of tree for food, then you shall cleanse away its uncleanness; for three years its fruit shall be unpurified to you; it shall not be eaten.

**19:24** And in the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, praiseworthy to the Lord;

**19:25** and in the fifth year you shall eat the fruit, its produce being an increase for you; I am the Lord your God.

**19:26** Do not eat upon the mountains, and you shall not practice augury nor observe birds for omens.

**19:27** You shall not make a rounded haircut from the hair of your head, nor shall you mar the appearance of your beard.

**19:28** And you shall not make incisions in your body for a departed soul, nor shall you make tattooed marks upon yourselves; I am the Lord your God.

**19:29** You shall not profane your daughter to make her a harlot; and the land shall not play the harlot, lest the land be filled with lawlessness.

**19:30** You shall keep My Sabbaths, and you shall revere My holy places; I am the Lord.

**19:31** You shall not follow after mediums, nor shall you cling to enchanters, to be defiled by them; I am the Lord your God.

**19:32** You shall rise up before the gray-headed, and honor the presence of an elder, and fear your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:33** And if any sojourner comes to you in your land, you shall not oppress him.

**19:34** The sojourner who comes to you shall be to you as the native-born among you, and you shall love him as yourself; for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt; I am the Lord your God.

**19:35** You shall do no injustice in judgment, in measures, in weights, and in balances.

**19:36** You shall have just balances, just weights, and a just chous; I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.

**19:37** And you shall keep all My law and all My ordinances, and perform them; I am the Lord your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — The Septuagint places 'his father' before 'his mother,' reversing the order found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **19:5** — The Greek reads 'a sacrifice of deliverance' (thysian soterioy) and 'your tenth' (dekaten hymon, i.e., tithe), whereas some other Greek witnesses read 'acceptable for you' (dekten hymin), closer to the Hebrew.
- **19:26** — The Septuagint reads 'upon the mountains' (epi ton oreon), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's prohibition against eating 'with the blood.'
- **19:36** — The Greek specifies a 'chous' (chous), a standard Greek liquid measure of roughly three liters, where the Hebrew lists an ephah and a hin.
