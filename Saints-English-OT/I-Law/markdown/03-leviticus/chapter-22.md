## Text

**22:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:2** "Speak to Aaron and his sons, and let them take heed regarding the holy things of the sons of Israel, and they shall not profane My holy name in whatever things they sanctify to Me; I am the Lord.

**22:3** Say to them, 'Throughout your generations, every man from your offspring who approaches the holy things which the sons of Israel sanctify to the Lord, while his uncleanness is upon him, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from Me; I am the Lord your God.

**22:4** And any man from the offspring of Aaron the priest who has leprosy or a discharge shall not eat of the holy things until he is cleansed. And whoever touches any uncleanness of a dead person, or a man from whom an emission of seed goes out,

**22:5** whoever touches any unclean creeping thing that defiles him, or a man by whom he is defiled according to all his uncleanness,

**22:6** the soul who touches them shall be unclean until evening; he shall not eat from the holy things unless he washes his body with water

**22:7** and the sun sets, and he shall be clean; and then he may eat of the holy things, because it is his bread.

**22:8** That which dies of itself and that which is torn by wild beasts he shall not eat, so as to be defiled by them; I am the Lord.

**22:9** And they shall keep My ordinances, lest they incur sin because of them and die because of it; and if they profane them, I am the Lord God who sanctifies them.

**22:10** And no outsider shall eat holy things; a sojourner with a priest or a hired servant shall not eat holy things.

**22:11** But if a priest acquires a person purchased with silver, that one may eat of his bread; and those born in his house, they also may eat of his bread.

**22:12** And if the daughter of a man who is a priest is married to an outsider, she shall not eat of the firstfruits of the sanctuary.

**22:13** And if a priest's daughter becomes a widow, one cast out, and has no offspring, she shall return to her father's house as in her youth; she may eat from her father's bread, and no outsider shall eat from them.

**22:14** And a man who eats holy things in ignorance shall add one-fifth of it to it and give the holy thing to the priest.

**22:15** And they shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel, which they set apart to the Lord,

**22:16** and bring upon themselves the iniquity of trespass when they eat their holy things; for I am the Lord who sanctifies them.'"

**22:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:18** "Speak to Aaron and his sons, and to all the congregation of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Any man from the sons of Israel, or from the sons of the proselytes who are joined to them in Israel, who offers his gifts according to all their pledges or according to all their freewill offerings, whatever they offer to God for a whole burnt offering,

**22:19** acceptable for you, shall be unblemished males from the herds and from the sheep and from the goats.

**22:20** Whatever has a blemish in it they shall not bring to the Lord, because it shall not be acceptable for you.

**22:21** And a man who offers a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, having specified a vow according to freewill choice or in your feasts, from the herds or from the sheep, it shall be unblemished to be acceptable; there shall be no blemish in it.

**22:22** What is blind, or broken, or cut in the tongue, or having warts, or having a malignant itch, or having scaly eruptions—they shall not bring these to the Lord, and you shall not give any of them as a burnt offering upon the altar to the Lord.

**22:23** And a calf or a sheep that is cut in the ear or docked in the tail, you may make them as slaughter-offerings for yourself, but for your vow it shall not be accepted.

**22:24** What is crushed, or bruised, or castrated, or torn off—you shall not bring them to the Lord, nor shall you do so in your land.

**22:25** And from the hand of a foreigner you shall not offer the gifts of your God from any of these, because there is corruption in them, a blemish is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.'"

**22:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:27** "A calf or a sheep or a goat, when it is born, shall remain seven days under its mother; and on the eighth day and beyond it shall be accepted for gifts, a burnt offering to the Lord.

**22:28** And a heifer or a sheep, her and her young ones, you shall not slay in one day.

**22:29** And if you sacrifice a sacrifice, a vow of gladness to the Lord, you shall sacrifice it so as to be acceptable for you;

**22:30** on that very day it shall be eaten; you shall not leave any of the meat until the morning. I am the Lord.

**22:31** And you shall keep My commandments and do them.

**22:32** And you shall not profane the name of the Holy One, and I shall be sanctified in the midst of the sons of Israel; I am the Lord who sanctifies you,

**22:33** who brought you out of the land of Egypt so as to be your God; I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — The Greek text places the conditional clause "and if they profane them" before the closing divine formula and includes the title "God" in "the Lord God who sanctifies them."
- **22:13** — The Greek text reads chera ekbeblemene ("a widow, one cast out") without a conjunction ("or") between the two descriptors.
- **22:23** — Greek sphagia poieseis auta seauto ("you may make them as slaughter-offerings for yourself") permits domestic slaughter rather than a freewill offering as in the Masoretic Text, and kolobokerkon specifies an animal docked in the tail.
- **22:29** — Greek thusian euchen charmosunes ("a sacrifice, a vow of gladness") emphasizes a joyful votive offering where the Masoretic Text reads "a sacrifice of thanksgiving."
