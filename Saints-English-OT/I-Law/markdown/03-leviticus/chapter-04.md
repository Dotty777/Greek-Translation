## Text

**4:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'If a soul sins unintentionally before the Lord against any of the ordinances of the Lord which ought not to be done, and does any one of them:

**4:3** if the anointed high priest sins, causing the people to sin, then he shall bring for his sin which he has sinned a young bull from the herd, without blemish, to the Lord for his sin.

**4:4** And he shall bring the young bull to the door of the tabernacle of testimony before the Lord, and he shall lay his hand upon the head of the young bull before the Lord, and he shall slay the young bull in the presence of the Lord.

**4:5** And the anointed priest whose hands have been consecrated shall take some of the blood of the young bull and bring it into the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:6** And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the Lord toward the holy veil.

**4:7** And the priest shall put some of the blood of the young bull upon the horns of the altar of compound incense which is before the Lord in the tabernacle of testimony; and all the blood of the young bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offerings, which is by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:8** And all the fat of the young bull of the sin offering he shall remove from it, the fat that covers the inward parts and all the fat that is upon the inward parts,

**4:9** and the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, which is by the flanks, and the lobe that is on the liver with the kidneys, he shall remove it,

**4:10** just as you take it away from the young bull of the sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall offer it up upon the altar of the offering by fire.

**4:11** And the hide of the young bull and all its flesh, with its head and its extremities, and its belly and its dung—

**4:12** they shall carry the whole young bull outside the camp to a clean place where they pour out the ashes, and they shall burn it up on wood with fire; at the place where the ashes are poured out it shall be burned.

**4:13** And if the whole congregation of Israel errs in ignorance unintentionally, and the matter is hidden from the eyes of the congregation, and they do one of all the commandments of the Lord which shall not be done, and they incur guilt,

**4:14** and the sin which they have sinned in it becomes known to them, then the congregation shall bring a young bull from the herd, without blemish, for the sin, and shall bring it to the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:15** And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands upon the head of the young bull before the Lord, and they shall slay the young bull before the Lord.

**4:16** And the anointed priest shall bring some of the blood of the young bull into the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:17** And the priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood of the young bull, and sprinkle seven times before the Lord directly in front of the holy veil.

**4:18** And some of the blood the priest shall put upon the horns of the altar of compound incense, which is before the Lord, which is in the tabernacle of testimony; and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of offerings by fire, which is at the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:19** And all the fat he shall remove from it and offer it up upon the altar.

**4:20** And he shall do with the young bull just as he did with the young bull of the sin offering; so shall it be done. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and the sin shall be forgiven them.

**4:21** And they shall carry the young bull outside the camp, and they shall burn up the young bull just as they burned up the first young bull; it is a sin offering of the congregation.

**4:22** And if the ruler sins and does one of all the commandments of the Lord their God which shall not be done, unintentionally, and sins and incurs guilt,

**4:23** and the sin which he has sinned in it becomes known to him, then he shall bring his gift, a kid from the goats, a male without blemish.

**4:24** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the kid, and they shall slay it in the place where they slay the burnt offerings before the Lord; it is a sin offering.

**4:25** And the priest shall put some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger upon the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offerings.

**4:26** And all its fat he shall offer up upon the altar, like the fat of a sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin, and it shall be forgiven him.

**4:27** And if one soul from the people of the land sins unintentionally in doing one of all the commandments of the Lord which shall not be done, and incurs guilt,

**4:28** and the sin which he has sinned in it becomes known to him, then he shall bring a young she-goat from the goats; a female without blemish he shall bring for the sin which he has sinned.

**4:29** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his sin offering, and they shall slay the young she-goat of the sin offering in the place where they slay the burnt offerings.

**4:30** And the priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it upon the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

**4:31** And all the fat he shall remove, just as fat is removed from a sacrifice of deliverance, and the priest shall offer it up upon the altar for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord; and the priest shall make atonement for him, and it shall be forgiven him.

**4:32** And if he brings a sheep as his gift for a sin offering, he shall bring it as a female without blemish.

**4:33** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering, and they shall slay it in the place where they slay the burnt offerings.

**4:34** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it upon the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering.

**4:35** And all its fat he shall remove, just as the fat of a sheep is removed from the sacrifice of deliverance, and the priest shall place it upon the altar upon the burnt offering of the Lord; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning the sin which he has sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.'"

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Septuagint reads "the anointed high priest" and "causing the people to sin," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the anointed priest" and "bringing guilt on the people."
- **4:5** — The Septuagint adds "whose hands have been consecrated" (literally "perfected as to his hands"), a traditional Greek idiom for priestly ordination not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:10** — The Septuagint reads the second-person plural "just as you take it away" and renders the Hebrew peace offering throughout Leviticus as "sacrifice of deliverance."
- **4:12** — Here and in verses 21, 24, 29, and 33, the Septuagint uses third-person plural verbs ("they shall carry," "they shall burn," "they shall slay") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the singular.
