## Text

**2:1** Now if a soul brings a gift, a sacrifice to the Lord, his gift shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil upon it and put frankincense upon it. It is a sacrifice.

**2:2** And he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests; and taking from it a full handful of the fine flour with the oil, and all its frankincense, the priest shall place its memorial upon the altar: a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:3** And the remainder of the sacrifice shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, a most holy thing from the sacrifices of the Lord.

**2:4** And if he brings a gift, a sacrifice baked from frankincense, a gift to the Lord of fine flour—unleavened loaves mixed with oil and unleavened wafers anointed with oil.

**2:5** And if your gift is a sacrifice from a pan, fine flour mixed with oil, it is unleavened;

**2:6** and you shall break them into pieces and pour oil upon them. It is a sacrifice to the Lord.

**2:7** And if your gift is a sacrifice from a hearth, it shall be made with oil.

**2:8** And he shall bring to the Lord the sacrifice that he makes from these things, and he shall bring it to the priest; and having drawn near to the altar,

**2:9** the priest shall take from the sacrifice its memorial, and the priest shall place it upon the altar: an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:10** Now that which remains of the sacrifice shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, most holy things from the offerings of the Lord.

**2:11** No sacrifice that you bring to the Lord shall you make leavened; for of any leaven or any honey you shall bring none to offer by fire to the Lord.

**2:12** As a gift of firstfruits you shall bring them to the Lord; yet upon the altar they shall not be brought up for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:13** And every gift of your sacrifice shall be salted with salt; you shall not let the salt of the covenant of the Lord cease from your sacrifices; upon every gift of yours you shall bring salt to the Lord your God.

**2:14** And if you bring a sacrifice of firstfruits to the Lord, it shall be tender—new, roasted, crushed groats to the Lord; and you shall bring the sacrifice of the firstfruits,

**2:15** and you shall pour oil upon it and put frankincense upon it. It is a sacrifice.

**2:16** And the priest shall offer up its memorial from the groats with the oil, and all its frankincense. It is an offering to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Throughout chapter 2, the Septuagint uses thysia ('sacrifice') for the grain offering and adds the closing declaration 'It is a sacrifice' here.
- **2:4** — The Greek text reads ek libanou ('from frankincense'), a well-known Septuagint reading resulting from the omission of the initial letter of klibanou ('oven').
- **2:14** — Greek chidra ('groats') refers to fresh grains of unripe wheat that have been roasted and crushed.
