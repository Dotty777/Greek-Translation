## Text

**24:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and let them take for Me pure beaten olive oil for the light, to keep a lamp burning continually

**24:3** outside the veil in the tabernacle of testimony; Aaron and his sons shall keep it burning from evening until morning before the Lord continually; it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations.

**24:4** Upon the pure lampstand you shall keep the lamps burning before the Lord until morning.

**24:5** And you shall take fine flour and make it into twelve loaves; each loaf shall be of two tenths.

**24:6** And you shall place them in two rows, six loaves in one row, upon the pure table before the Lord.

**24:7** And you shall put upon the row pure frankincense and salt, and they shall be for loaves as a memorial, set forth to the Lord.

**24:8** On the Sabbath day it shall be set forth before the Lord continually, before the sons of Israel as an everlasting covenant.

**24:9** And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, and they shall eat them in a holy place; for this is most holy to them from the things sacrificed to the Lord, an everlasting statute."

**24:10** And the son of an Israelite woman went out, and he was the son of an Egyptian among the sons of Israel. And the son of the Israelite woman and the Israelite man fought in the camp,

**24:11** and the son of the Israelite woman, having named the Name, cursed. And they brought them to Moses; and his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**24:12** And they put him in custody, to judge him by the command of the Lord.

**24:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:14** "Bring out the one who cursed outside the camp, and all who heard shall lay their hands upon his head, and all the congregation shall stone him.

**24:15** And speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'Any man who curses God shall bear sin,

**24:16** and whoever names the name of the Lord shall surely be put to death; all the congregation of Israel shall stone him with stones; whether sojourner or native-born, when he names the name of the Lord, let him die.

**24:17** And any man who strikes the life of a man, and he dies, shall surely be put to death.

**24:18** And whoever strikes an animal, and it dies, shall make restitution, life for life.

**24:19** And if anyone inflicts a blemish on his neighbor, as he has done to him, so shall it be done to him in return:

**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; just as he has inflicted a blemish on the man, so shall it be inflicted on him.

**24:21** Whoever strikes a man, and he dies, shall surely be put to death.

**24:22** There shall be one judgment for the sojourner and for the native of the land, for I am the Lord your God.'"

**24:23** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they brought the one who cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones; and the sons of Israel did just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The Greek adds 'and salt' alongside pure frankincense upon the loaves, harmonizing with the requirement of salt for offerings in Leviticus 2:13.
- **24:11** — The Greek describes the offense as 'having named the Name, cursed' and uses the plural 'brought them,' bringing both combatants before Moses.
- **24:16** — The Greek renders the prohibition as 'whoever names the name of the Lord,' a wording historically linked to the reverent Jewish custom of not pronouncing the divine Name aloud.
- **24:21** — The Greek contains only the clause concerning striking a human being, omitting the repetition of the animal-restitution rule already given in verse 18.
