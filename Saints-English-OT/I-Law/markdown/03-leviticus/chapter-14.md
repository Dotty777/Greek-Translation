## Text

**14:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**14:2** "This is the law of the leper, on whatever day he is cleansed: he shall be brought to the priest,

**14:3** and the priest shall go forth outside the camp; and the priest shall look, and behold, the plague of leprosy is healed from the leper.

**14:4** Then the priest shall command, and they shall take for the one who has been cleansed two living clean birds, and cedar wood, and spun scarlet, and hyssop;

**14:5** and the priest shall command, and they shall slay the one bird into an earthen vessel over living water;

**14:6** and as for the living bird, he shall take it, and the cedar wood, and the spun scarlet, and the hyssop, and he shall dip them and the living bird into the blood of the bird that was slain over living water;

**14:7** and he shall sprinkle upon the one cleansed from the leprosy seven times, and he shall be clean; and he shall send away the living bird into the open field.

**14:8** And the one who has been cleansed shall wash his garments, and shave off all his hair, and bathe in water, and he shall be clean; and after these things he shall enter into the camp, and he shall abide outside his house seven days.

**14:9** And it shall be on the seventh day that he shall shave off all his hair—his head, and his beard, and his eyebrows, even all his hair he shall shave off—and he shall wash the garments and bathe his body with water, and he shall be clean.

**14:10** And on the eighth day he shall take two yearling lambs without blemish, and a yearling sheep without blemish, and three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for an offering, and one cotyla of oil;

**14:11** and the priest who cleanses shall set the man who is being cleansed, and these things, before the Lord, at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony.

**14:12** And the priest shall take the one lamb and offer it for the trespass offering, along with the cotyla of oil, and he shall set it apart as a separated offering before the Lord.

**14:13** And they shall slay the lamb in the place where they slay the burnt offerings and the sin offerings, in a holy place; for the sin offering is like the trespass offering; it belongs to the priest; it is most holy.

**14:14** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the trespass offering, and the priest shall put it upon the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and upon the tip of the right hand, and upon the tip of the right foot;

**14:15** and the priest shall take some from the cotyla of oil and pour it upon the left hand of the priest,

**14:16** and he shall dip his right finger into some of the oil that is upon his left hand, and sprinkle seven times with his finger before the Lord.

**14:17** And the remaining oil that is in his hand the priest shall put upon the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and upon the tip of the right hand, and upon the tip of the right foot, upon the place of the blood of the trespass offering;

**14:18** and the remaining oil that is upon the hand of the priest, the priest shall put upon the head of the one who has been cleansed; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord.

**14:19** And the priest shall offer the sin offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him, the unclean one who is being cleansed from his sin; and after this the priest shall slay the burnt offering.

**14:20** And the priest shall offer up the burnt offering and the offering upon the altar before the Lord; and the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be cleansed.

**14:21** But if his hand is poor and cannot find enough, he shall take one lamb for that in which he trespassed, for an offering set apart, so as to make atonement for him, and a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for an offering, and one cotyla of oil,

**14:22** and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, as much as his hand has found; and the one shall be for a sin offering, and the other for a burnt offering;

**14:23** and he shall bring them on the eighth day, for his cleansing, to the priest, at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, before the Lord.

**14:24** And the priest shall take the lamb of the trespass offering and the cotyla of oil, and present them as a presentation offering before the Lord.

**14:25** And he shall slay the lamb of the trespass offering, and the priest shall take some of the blood of the trespass offering, and put it upon the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and upon the tip of the right hand, and upon the tip of the right foot.

**14:26** And some of the oil the priest shall pour upon the left hand of the priest;

**14:27** and the priest shall sprinkle with his right finger some of the oil that is in his left hand seven times before the Lord;

**14:28** and the priest shall put some of the oil that is upon his hand upon the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and upon the tip of his right hand, and upon the tip of his right foot, upon the place of the blood of the trespass offering;

**14:29** and what remains of the oil that is upon the hand of the priest, he shall put upon the head of the one who has been cleansed; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord.

**14:30** And he shall offer one of the turtledoves or from the young pigeons, according as his hand has found,

**14:31** the one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering, with the offering; and the priest shall make atonement for the one being cleansed before the Lord.

**14:32** This is the law for him in whom is the plague of leprosy, and who cannot find with his hand enough for his cleansing."

**14:33** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**14:34** "When you enter into the land of the Canaanites, which I give to you for a possession, and I put a plague of leprosy in the houses of the land possessed by you,

**14:35** then he to whom the house belongs shall come and report to the priest, saying, 'Something like a plague has been seen in my house.'

**14:36** And the priest shall command them to empty the house before the priest enters to look at the house, so that whatever is in the house may by no means become unclean; and after these things the priest shall enter to examine the house.

**14:37** And he shall look at the plague in the walls of the house, greenish hollows, and their appearance is lower than the walls;

**14:38** and the priest shall go out of the house to the door of the house, and the priest shall set the house apart for seven days.

**14:39** And the priest shall return on the seventh day and look at the house, and behold, the plague has not spread in the walls of the house;

**14:40** and the priest shall command, and they shall take out the stones—there is the plague—and they shall cast them outside the city into an unclean place.

**14:41** And they shall scrape the house inside all around, and they shall pour out the mortar outside the city into an unclean place.

**14:42** And they shall take solid scraped stones and put them in place of the stones, and they shall take other mortar and plaster the house.

**14:43** Now if a plague comes again and breaks out in the house after the stones have been taken out, and after the house has been scraped, and after it has been plastered,

**14:44** then the priest shall enter and look; if the plague has spread in the house, it is an abiding leprosy in the house; it is unclean.

**14:45** And they shall tear down the house, and its timbers and its stones and all the mortar they shall carry forth outside the city into an unclean place;

**14:46** and whoever enters into the house all the days that it is set apart shall be unclean until evening,

**14:47** and whoever sleeps in the house shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until evening; and whoever eats in the house shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until evening.

**14:48** But if the priest comes and enters and looks, and behold, the plague does not spread at all in the house after the house has been plastered, then the priest shall cleanse the house, because the plague has been healed.

**14:49** And to purify the house he shall take two living clean birds, and cedar wood, and spun scarlet, and hyssop;

**14:50** and he shall slay the one bird into an earthen vessel over living water,

**14:51** and he shall take the cedar wood, and the spun scarlet, and the hyssop, and the living bird, and dip it into the blood of the bird that was slain over living water, and sprinkle with them upon the house seven times.

**14:52** And he shall purify the house with the blood of the bird, and with the living water, and with the living bird, and with the cedar wood, and with the hyssop, and with the spun scarlet;

**14:53** and he shall send away the living bird outside the city into the open field; and he shall make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

**14:54** This is the law concerning every plague of leprosy and of scale,

**14:55** and of the leprosy of a garment and of a house,

**14:56** and of a scar, and of a mark, and of a bright spot,

**14:57** and to declare on what day a thing is unclean, and on what day it shall be cleansed. This is the law of leprosy."

## Translator Notes

- **14:10** — The Greek measure 'cotyla' (roughly half a pint) renders the Hebrew liquid measure 'log.' The Greek also specifies 'yearling' for both the two lambs and the sheep.
- **14:19** — The Greek text reads 'the unclean one who is being cleansed from his sin,' whereas the Masoretic Text reads 'the one being cleansed from his uncleanness.'
- **14:37** — The Greek source text mentions only 'greenish hollows,' without the additional 'or reddish' found in the Masoretic Text.
- **14:39** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek source text reads 'has not spread,' in contrast to the Masoretic Text and other witnesses that read 'has spread.'
- **14:42** — The Greek text specifies 'solid scraped stones' where the Masoretic Text reads 'other stones.'
