## Text

**1:1** And the Lord called Moses and spoke to him from the tent of testimony, saying,

**1:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'If any man among you brings gifts to the Lord, from the livestock, and from the herds, and from the flocks you shall bring your gifts.

**1:3** If his gift is a whole burnt offering from the herds, he shall bring a male without blemish; to the door of the tent of testimony he shall bring it, acceptable for him before the Lord.

**1:4** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the offering, acceptable for him to make atonement for him.

**1:5** And they shall slay the calf before the Lord; and the priests, the sons of Aaron, shall bring the blood and pour the blood upon the altar all around, which is at the doors of the tent of testimony.

**1:6** And having skinned the whole burnt offering, they shall cut it into its pieces.

**1:7** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall put fire upon the altar and pile wood upon the fire.

**1:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pile the cut pieces, and the head, and the fat upon the wood that is on the fire, which is upon the altar;

**1:9** but its entrails and the feet they shall wash with water; and the priests shall place all of them upon the altar. It is an offering, a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**1:10** Now if his gift to the Lord is from the flocks, both from the lambs and from the kids, for a whole burnt offering, he shall bring it, a male without blemish,

**1:11** and he shall lay his hand upon its head. And they shall slay it at the side of the altar toward the north, before the Lord; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour its blood upon the altar all around.

**1:12** And they shall divide it into its pieces, together with the head and the fat; and the priests shall pile them upon the wood that is on the fire, which is upon the altar;

**1:13** and the entrails and the feet they shall wash with water. And the priest shall bring all of them and place them upon the altar. It is an offering of sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**1:14** And if you bring an offering from the birds as a gift to the Lord, then he shall bring his gift from the turtledoves or from the doves.

**1:15** And the priest shall bring it to the altar, and pinch off the head, and the priest shall place it upon the altar, and he shall drain out the blood at the base of the altar.

**1:16** And he shall remove the crop with the feathers and cast it beside the altar toward the east, into the place of the ashes.

**1:17** And he shall break it open by its wings, yet he shall not divide it; and the priest shall place it upon the altar, upon the wood that is on the fire. It is an offering, a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Septuagint consistently renders the Hebrew 'tent of meeting' as 'tent of testimony' (skene tou martyriou), associating the meeting place with the testimony of the covenant.
- **1:3** — Greek dekton auto ('acceptable for him', also in 1:4) emphasizes the offering's acceptance before the Lord rather than the offerer's voluntary will as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:4** — Greek karpoma ('offering') designates an offering consumed by fire on the altar, distinct here from holokautoma ('whole burnt offering') and doron ('gift').
- **1:11** — The Greek preserves the explicit instruction 'and he shall lay his hand upon its head,' which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
