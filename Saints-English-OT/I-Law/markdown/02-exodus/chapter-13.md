## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Sanctify to Me every firstborn, first-produced, opening every womb among the sons of Israel, from man even to beast; it is Mine."

**13:3** And Moses said to the people, "Remember this day in which you came out from the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage; for with a mighty hand the Lord brought you out from here, and leaven shall not be eaten.

**13:4** For on this day you are going out, in the month of the new crops.

**13:5** And it shall be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Hivites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Jebusites, which He swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, that you shall perform this service in this month.

**13:6** Six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a feast of the Lord.

**13:7** Unleavened bread you shall eat seven days; nothing leavened shall be seen with you, nor shall there be leaven with you in all your borders.

**13:8** And you shall declare to your son in that day, saying, 'Because of this the Lord God did for me when I was coming out of Egypt.'

**13:9** And it shall be to you a sign upon your hand and a memorial before your eyes, so that the law of the Lord may be in your mouth; for with a mighty hand the Lord God brought you out of Egypt.

**13:10** And you shall keep this law from days to days.

**13:11** And it shall be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land of the Canaanites, in the manner He swore to your fathers, and gives it to you,

**13:12** that you shall set apart every opener of the womb, the males, to the Lord; every opener of the womb from the herds or among your beasts, as many as shall be born to you, the males you shall sanctify to the Lord.

**13:13** Every opener of the womb of a donkey you shall exchange with a sheep; and if you do not exchange it, you shall redeem it. Every firstborn of man among your sons you shall redeem.

**13:14** And if your son asks you hereafter, saying, 'What is this?' then you shall say to him, 'With a mighty hand the Lord brought us out from the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage;

**13:15** and when Pharaoh hardened himself against sending us away, He slew every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of men even to the firstborn of beasts; therefore I sacrifice every firstborn to the Lord, every opener of the womb, the males, and every firstborn of my sons I will redeem.'

**13:16** And it shall be for a sign upon your hand, and an immovable token before your eyes; for with a mighty hand the Lord brought you out of Egypt."

**13:17** Now when Pharaoh sent the people away, God did not lead them by the way of the land of the Philistines, because it was near; for God said, "Lest at any time the people change their mind upon seeing war, and return to Egypt."

**13:18** And God led the people around by the way into the wilderness, to the Red Sea; and in the fifth generation the sons of Israel went up out of the land of Egypt.

**13:19** And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him; for he had bound the sons of Israel by an oath, saying, "The Lord will surely visit you, and you shall carry up my bones from here with you."

**13:20** And setting out from Succoth, the sons of Israel encamped at Etham, beside the wilderness.

**13:21** And God went before them, by day in a pillar of cloud to show them the way, and by night in a pillar of fire.

**13:22** And the pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not fail before all the people.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — The Greek renders the Hebrew month name Abib descriptively as "the month of the new crops" (en mēni tōn neōn), referring to the spring harvest of fresh grain.
- **13:5** — The Greek text lists all seven nations of Canaan, including the Girgashites and Perizzites, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text names five here.
- **13:6** — The Greek reads "Six days" for eating unleavened bread prior to the seventh-day feast, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Seven days."
- **13:13** — The Greek commands "you shall redeem it" (lytrōsē auto) if a firstborn donkey is not exchanged, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text commands breaking its neck.
- **13:18** — The Greek reads "in the fifth generation" (pemptē de genea), interpreting the underlying Hebrew term from the root for "five" rather than "armed" or "in battle array."
