## Text

**16:1** Then they departed from Elim, and the whole assembly of the sons of Israel came into the wilderness of Sin, which lies between Elim and Sinai. On the fifteenth day of the second month after they had come out of the land of Egypt,

**16:2** the entire assembly of the sons of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron;

**16:3** and the sons of Israel spoke to them, "If only we had died struck down by the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat beside the cauldrons of meat and ate loaves of bread to our fill! For you have led us out into this wilderness to put this whole assembly to death by famine."

**16:4** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Behold, I am raining down loaves of bread for you from heaven; and the people shall go out and gather each day's portion for that day, so that I may test them whether they will walk in My law or not.

**16:5** And it shall come to pass on the sixth day that they shall prepare whatever they bring in, and it shall be twice as much as what they gather day by day."

**16:6** So Moses and Aaron said to the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, "At evening you shall know that the Lord brought you out of the land of Egypt,

**16:7** and in the morning you shall behold the glory of the Lord, because He has heard your murmuring against God; for what are we, that you murmur against us?"

**16:8** Moses also said, "This shall be when the Lord gives you meat to eat at evening and loaves of bread in the morning to your fill, because the Lord has heard your murmuring with which you murmur against us; for what are we? Indeed, your murmuring is not against us, but rather against God."

**16:9** Then Moses said to Aaron, "Say to the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, 'Draw near before God, for He has heard your murmuring.'"

**16:10** Now while Aaron was speaking to the entire assembly of the sons of Israel, they turned toward the wilderness, and the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud.

**16:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:12** "I have heard the murmuring of the sons of Israel; speak to them and say, 'Toward evening you shall eat meat, and in the morning you shall be filled with bread; and you shall know that I am the Lord your God.'"

**16:13** So evening came, and quail came up and covered the encampment; and in the morning it came about, as the dew settled all around the encampment,

**16:14** behold, upon the surface of the wilderness there lay something fine like coriander, white like frost upon the earth.

**16:15** When the sons of Israel saw it, they asked one another, "What is this?" For they did not know what it was. And Moses said to them, "This is the bread that the Lord has given you to eat.

**16:16** This is the word that the Lord has commanded: 'Gather from it, each person for those belonging to him; an omer per head, according to the number of your souls, each of you along with your tentmates shall collect.'"

**16:17** And the sons of Israel did so, and they gathered, one a large amount and another a smaller amount.

**16:18** Yet when they measured it by the omer, the one with much had no surplus, and the one with little suffered no lack; each had gathered for those belonging to his own household.

**16:19** Then Moses instructed them, "Let no one leave any of it behind until the morning."

**16:20** Yet they did not heed Moses, for certain ones left some of it behind until the morning; and it teemed with worms and gave off a stench. And Moses grew bitter against them.

**16:21** So they gathered it in the morning; and whenever the sun grew hot, it melted away.

**16:22** Now it happened on the sixth day that they gathered twice the needed portion, two omers for each person; and all the rulers of the assembly came in and reported it to Moses.

**16:23** And the Lord said to them, "Is this not the word that the Lord spoke? Tomorrow is a Sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; whatever you would bake, bake, and whatever you would boil, boil; and all that remains over, set it aside for storage until the morning."

**16:24** So they set some of it aside until the morning, just as Moses had commanded them; and it did not give off a stench, nor was there any worm found in it.

**16:25** Then Moses said, "Eat it today, for today is a Sabbath to the Lord; it will not be found in the plain.

**16:26** Six days you shall gather, yet on the seventh day is a Sabbath, because there shall be none on it."

**16:27** Yet it came about on the seventh day that some of the people went out to gather, and they found nothing.

**16:28** Then the Lord said to Moses, "How long will you refuse to heed My commandments and My law?

**16:29** Behold, for the Lord has given you this day; on this account He Himself gave you bread for two days on the sixth day. Remain seated, each one in your own houses; let no one go forth from his place on the seventh day."

**16:30** So the people kept the Sabbath rest on the seventh day.

**16:31** And the sons of Israel called its name manna; now it was white like coriander seed, and its taste was like a wafer made with honey.

**16:32** Then Moses said, "This is the word that the Lord has commanded: 'Fill an omer with the manna for safekeeping throughout your generations, so that they may see the bread that you ate in the wilderness when the Lord led you out of the land of Egypt.'"

**16:33** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take one golden jar, and place into it a full omer of manna, and you shall lay it up before God for preservation throughout your generations,"

**16:34** in the manner that the Lord had commanded Moses; and Aaron laid it up before God for preservation.

**16:35** Now the sons of Israel ate the manna for forty years, until they entered the inhabited land; they ate the manna until they reached a region of Phoenicia.

**16:36** Now the omer was the tenth part of the three measures.

## Translator Notes

- **16:23** — The Greek text explicitly names "the Lord" as the speaker and casts the opening clause as a rhetorical question ("Is this not the word that the Lord spoke?"), whereas the Hebrew has Moses speaking declaratively.
- **16:33** — The Greek specifies "one golden jar" (a detail echoed in Hebrews 9:4), whereas the Hebrew text mentions a jar without specifying gold.
- **16:35** — The Greek reads "a region of Phoenicia" where the Hebrew reads "the border of the land of Canaan," reflecting Hellenistic Septuagint geographical terminology.
- **16:36** — The Greek transliterates the dry measure as gomor (here rendered "omer") and defines it as "the tenth part of the three measures," corresponding to the Hebrew ephah of three seahs.
