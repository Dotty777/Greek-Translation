## Text

**33:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Depart, go up from here, you and your people whom you brought out of the land of Egypt, to the land which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give it.'

**33:2** And I will send My angel along before your face, and you shall drive out the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Girgashite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.

**33:3** And I will bring you into a land flowing with milk and honey; for I will by no means go up with you, because you are a stiff-necked people, lest I consume you on the way."

**33:4** And when the people heard this grievous word, they mourned deeply in mourning attire.

**33:5** And the Lord said to the sons of Israel, "You are a stiff-necked people; beware lest I bring another plague upon you and consume you; now therefore take off your robes of glory and your ornaments, and I will show you what I will do to you."

**33:6** So the sons of Israel took off their ornaments and their robes from Mount Horeb.

**33:7** And Moses took his tent and pitched it outside the camp, far off from the camp, and it was called the Tent of Testimony; and it came to pass that everyone who sought the Lord went out to the tent outside the camp.

**33:8** And whenever Moses went into the tent outside the camp, all the people stood watching, each man at the door of his tent, and they watched Moses as he departed until he entered into the tent.

**33:9** And whenever Moses entered into the tent, the pillar of the cloud descended and stood at the door of the tent, and He spoke to Moses.

**33:10** And all the people saw the pillar of the cloud standing at the door of the tent; and all the people rose up and worshiped, each man from the door of his tent.

**33:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses face to face, as if a man should speak to his own friend. Then he would return to the camp, but his attendant Joshua the son of Nave, a young man, did not depart from the tent.

**33:12** And Moses said to the Lord, "Behold, You say to me, 'Bring up this people,' yet You have not made known to me whom You will send along with me; and You have said to me, 'I know you above all others, and you have favor with Me.'

**33:13** If therefore I have found favor before You, manifest Yourself to me; let me see You clearly, so that I may have found favor before You, and that I may know that this great nation is Your people."

**33:14** And He said, "I Myself will go before you, and I will give you rest."

**33:15** And he said to Him, "If You Yourself do not go, do not bring me up from here.

**33:16** And how shall it truly be known that I have found favor with You, both I and Your people, except by Your going along with us? Then both I and Your people shall be glorified above all the nations that are upon the earth."

**33:17** And the Lord said to Moses, "I will also do for you this word which you have spoken; for you have found favor before Me, and I know you above all others."

**33:18** And he said, "Manifest Yourself to me."

**33:19** And He said, "I will pass by before you in My glory, and I will speak by My name, 'the Lord,' before you; and I will show mercy to whomever I show mercy, and I will have compassion on whomever I have compassion."

**33:20** And He said, "You will not be able to see My face; for no man shall see My face and live."

**33:21** And the Lord said, "Behold, there is a place by Me; you shall stand upon the rock;

**33:22** and when My glory passes by, I will place you in a cleft of the rock, and I will shield you with My hand until I have passed by;

**33:23** and I will remove My hand, and then you shall see My back, but My face shall not be seen by you."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — The Greek reads "you shall drive out" (second-person singular) and lists six nations, including the Girgashite and omitting the Canaanite found in the Hebrew text.
- **33:5** — The Greek addresses the sons of Israel directly and warns "lest I bring another plague upon you" and "I will show you what I will do to you," differing from the Hebrew ("in a single moment" and "that I may know").
- **33:11** — "Nave" preserves the distinct Septuagint proper name (Naue) where the Hebrew text has "Nun."
- **33:18** — The Greek reads "Manifest Yourself to me" (echoing 33:13) rather than "Show me Your glory" as in the Hebrew; in 33:19 the Greek likewise reads "in My glory" where the Hebrew has "all My goodness."
