## Text

**23:1** You shall not receive a vain report; you shall not join in agreement with the unrighteous to become an unrighteous witness.

**23:2** You shall not be with the many in evil; you shall not side with a multitude to turn aside with the many, so as to shut out justice.

**23:3** And you shall not pity a poor man in judgment.

**23:4** And if you meet your enemy's ox or his beast of burden going astray, you shall turn it back and restore it to him.

**23:5** And if you see your enemy's beast of burden fallen beneath its burden, you shall not pass it by, but you shall lift it up together with him.

**23:6** You shall not pervert the judgment of a poor man in his cause.

**23:7** From every unjust matter you shall keep far away; the innocent and righteous you shall not slay, and you shall not justify the ungodly for the sake of bribes.

**23:8** And you shall not accept bribes; for bribes blind the eyes of those who see, and corrupt righteous words.

**23:9** And you shall not oppress a sojourner; for you know the soul of a sojourner, since you yourselves were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**23:10** Six years you shall sow your land and gather in its produce;

**23:11** but in the seventh you shall grant a release and leave it uncultivated, and the poor of your nation shall eat; and what is left over the wild beasts shall eat. Likewise you shall do with your vineyard and with your olive grove.

**23:12** Six days you shall do your works, but on the seventh day there shall be rest, that your ox and your beast of burden may rest, and that the son of your maidservant and the sojourner may rest.

**23:13** Observe all things that I have spoken to you; and you shall not call to mind the name of other gods, nor shall it be heard from your mouth.

**23:14** Three times in the year you shall keep a feast to Me.

**23:15** You shall take care to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread: seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of new crops, for in it you came out of Egypt. You shall not appear before Me empty-handed.

**23:16** And you shall keep the Feast of Harvest of the firstfruits of your labors that you sow in your field, and the Feast of Completion at the outgoing of the year, in the gathering in of your labors from your field.

**23:17** Three times in the year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord your God.

**23:18** For when I cast out nations from before your face and enlarge your borders, you shall not sacrifice the blood of My incense-offering upon leaven, nor shall the fat of My feast remain overnight until morning.

**23:19** The firstfruits of your first-produce you shall bring into the house of the Lord your God. You shall not boil a lamb in its mother's milk.

**23:20** And behold, I send My angel before your face to guard you in the way, that he may bring you into the land which I have prepared for you.

**23:21** Take heed to yourself and listen to him, and do not disobey him; for he will by no means shrink back from you, for My name is upon him.

**23:22** If you indeed listen to My voice, and do all things that I command you, and keep My covenant, you shall be to Me a treasured people from among all the nations; for all the earth is Mine, and you shall be to Me a royal priesthood and a holy nation. These words you shall speak to the sons of Israel. If you indeed listen to My voice, and do all things that I say to you, I will be an enemy to your enemies and an adversary to your adversaries.

**23:23** For My angel shall go leading before you, and he shall bring you in to the Amorite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Canaanite, and the Girgashite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite, and I will blot them out.

**23:24** You shall not bow down to their gods, nor shall you serve them; you shall not do according to their works, but you shall utterly demolish them and shatter their pillars in pieces.

**23:25** And you shall serve the Lord your God, and I will bless your bread, and your wine, and your water, and I will turn sickness away from you.

**23:26** There shall be neither childless nor barren one in your land; I will fulfill the number of your days.

**23:27** And I will send dread leading before you, and I will throw into dismay all the nations to whom you come, and I will make all your adversaries fugitives.

**23:28** And I will send the hornets ahead of you, and you shall drive out the Amorites, and the Hivites, and the Canaanites, and the Hittites from before you.

**23:29** I will not drive them out in one year, lest the land become desolate and the wild beasts of the land become many against you;

**23:30** little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you increase and inherit the land.

**23:31** And I will set your borders from the Red Sea even to the sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness even to the great river Euphrates; and I will deliver into your hands those who dwell in the land, and I will drive them out from before you.

**23:32** You shall not make a covenant with them or with their gods;

**23:33** and they shall not dwell in your land, lest they cause you to sin against Me; for if you serve their gods, these will become a stumbling block to you.

## Translator Notes

- **23:7** — The Greek addresses the judge directly ('you shall not justify the ungodly for the sake of bribes'), whereas the Masoretic Text has a first-person divine declaration ('for I will not acquit the wicked').
- **23:18** — The Septuagint opens with a clause echoing Exodus 34:24 ('For when I cast out nations... and enlarge your borders') and reads 'My incense-offering' (thymiamatos mou) where the Hebrew has 'My sacrifice.'
- **23:19** — The Greek specifies 'a lamb' (arna), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies a young goat.
- **23:22** — The first half of this verse in the Septuagint contains an expanded covenant promise closely paralleling Exodus 19:5-6 before resuming the promise of divine aid against Israel's enemies.
