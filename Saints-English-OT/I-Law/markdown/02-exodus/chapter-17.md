## Text

**17:1** And all the congregation of the sons of Israel departed from the wilderness of Sin, according to their encampments, by the word of the Lord, and encamped in Rephidim; yet there was no water for the people to drink.

**17:2** And the people reviled Moses, saying, "Give us water, that we may drink." And Moses said to them, "Why do you revile me, and why do you test the Lord?"

**17:3** And the people thirsted there for water, and the people murmured there against Moses, saying, "Why is this that you have brought us up out of Egypt, to slay us and our children and the livestock with thirst?"

**17:4** Then Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "What shall I do with this people? Yet a little while, and they will stone me!"

**17:5** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go on before this people, and take with yourself some of the elders of the people; and take in your hand the rod with which you struck the river, and you shall go.

**17:6** Behold, I stand there before you upon the rock in Horeb; and you shall strike the rock, and water shall come forth out of it, and My people shall drink." And Moses did so in the sight of the sons of Israel.

**17:7** And he named the name of that place Testing and Reviling, because of the reviling of the sons of Israel, and because they tested the Lord, saying, "Is the Lord among us or not?"

**17:8** Now Amalek came and fought against Israel in Rephidim.

**17:9** And Moses said to Joshua, "Choose for yourself mighty men, and go out and array yourself in battle against Amalek tomorrow; and behold, I will stand upon the top of the hill, with the rod of God in my hand."

**17:10** And Joshua did just as Moses had told him, and went out and arrayed himself in battle against Amalek; and Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

**17:11** And it came to pass, whenever Moses lifted up his hands, that Israel prevailed; and whenever he let down his hands, Amalek prevailed.

**17:12** Now the hands of Moses were heavy; so they took a stone and placed it under him, and he sat upon it. And Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on this side and one on that side; and the hands of Moses were held steady until the setting of the sun.

**17:13** And Joshua routed Amalek and all his people with the slaughter of the sword.

**17:14** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Write this down for a memorial in a book, and deliver it into the ears of Joshua, that I will utterly blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven."

**17:15** And Moses built an altar to the Lord, and named its name The Lord Is My Refuge;

**17:16** for with a hidden hand the Lord wages war against Amalek from generations to generations.

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — The Septuagint translates the place names into Greek as Peirasmos ("Testing") and Loidoresis ("Reviling"), corresponding to Hebrew Massah and Meribah.
- **17:9** — The Greek text uses Iesous, the standard Septuagint form of the name Joshua, and specifies "mighty men" (andras dynatous) where the Masoretic Text reads simply "men."
- **17:15** — The Septuagint names the altar "The Lord Is My Refuge" (Kyrios kataphyge mou), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "The Lord Is My Banner."
- **17:16** — The Septuagint reads "with a hidden hand" (en cheiri kryphaia), differing from the Masoretic Text's difficult phrase usually rendered "a hand upon the throne of the Lord."
