## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke all these words, saying:

**20:2** "I am the Lord your God, who brought you forth from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**20:3** You shall have no other gods besides Me.

**20:4** You shall not make for yourself an idol, nor a likeness of anything—whatever is in heaven above, and whatever is on the earth beneath, and whatever is in the waters underneath the earth.

**20:5** You shall not bow down to them nor shall you serve them; for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate Me,

**20:6** and showing mercy to thousands, to those who love Me and to those who keep My ordinances.

**20:7** You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain; for the Lord your God will by no means acquit the one who takes His name in vain.

**20:8** Remember the Sabbath day, to sanctify it.

**20:9** Six days you shall labor and perform all your works,

**20:10** but on the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God; you shall not do any work in it—you and your son and your daughter, your manservant and your maidservant, your ox and your beast of burden and all your cattle, and the stranger who sojourns among you.

**20:11** For in six days the Lord made the heaven and the earth and all things that are in them, and He rested on the seventh day; therefore the Lord blessed the seventh day and sanctified it.

**20:12** Honor your father and mother, that it may be well with you, and that you may live long upon the good land which the Lord your God is giving to you.

**20:13** You shall not commit adultery.

**20:14** You shall not steal.

**20:15** You shall not murder.

**20:16** You shall not testify with false witness against your neighbor.

**20:17** You shall not covet your neighbor's wife; you shall not covet your neighbor's house, nor his field, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his beast of burden, nor any of his cattle, nor whatever belongs to your neighbor."

**20:18** And all the people saw the voice and the flashes and the sound of the trumpet and the mountain smoking; and all the people were afraid and stood afar off,

**20:19** and they said to Moses, "You speak to us, and do not let God speak to us, lest we die."

**20:20** And Moses said to them, "Take courage; for God has come to you in order to test you, so that the fear of Him may be in you, that you may not sin."

**20:21** And the people stood afar off, while Moses entered into the thick darkness where God was.

**20:22** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the house of Jacob and declare to the sons of Israel: 'You yourselves have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.

**20:23** You shall not make for yourselves gods of silver, and gods of gold you shall not make for yourselves.

**20:24** An altar of earth you shall make for Me, and you shall sacrifice upon it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, and your sheep and your calves, in every place where I shall name My name there; and I will come to you and bless you.

**20:25** And if you make an altar of stones for Me, you shall not build them hewn; for you have laid your hand tool upon them, and they have been defiled.

**20:26** You shall not go up by steps to My altar, so that you may not uncover your nakedness upon it.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:11** — The Greek text omits the sea from the summary of creation and states that the Lord blessed the seventh day rather than the Sabbath day, differing from the Masoretic Hebrew.
- **20:13** — The Septuagint orders verses 13 through 15 as adultery, theft, and murder, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew places them in the order murder, adultery, and theft.
- **20:17** — The Greek text places coveting a neighbor's wife before coveting his house and adds his field to the list.
- **20:18** — The Greek literally says the people saw the voice and the torches (or flashes), expressing the thunder and lightning of Sinai in vivid visual terms.
