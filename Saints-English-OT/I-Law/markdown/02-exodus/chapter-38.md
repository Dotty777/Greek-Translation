## Text

**38:1** And Bezalel made the ark,

**38:2** and overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside.

**38:3** And he cast four golden rings for it, two on the one side and two on the second side,

**38:4** wide for the poles, so as to carry it with them.

**38:5** And he made the mercy seat of gold above the ark,

**38:6** and the two golden cherubim:

**38:7** one cherub at the end of the mercy seat and one cherub at the second end of the mercy seat,

**38:8** overshadowing the mercy seat with their wings.

**38:9** And he made the table set forth of pure gold,

**38:10** and cast four rings for it, two on the one side and two on the second side, wide so as to carry it with the poles in them.

**38:11** And he made the poles of the ark and of the table, and overlaid them with gold.

**38:12** And he made the vessels of the table, the dishes, and the censers, and the cups, and the libation bowls with which he shall pour out drink offerings, of gold.

**38:13** And he made the lampstand that gives light of gold,

**38:14** solid in its shaft, and the branches from both of its sides;

**38:15** from its branches the buds extending out, three from the one side and three from the other, equal to one another;

**38:16** and their lamp-cups that are upon the ends, nut-shaped, out of them; and the sockets out of them, so that the lamps might be upon them, and the seventh socket from the end of the lamp-cup upon the top above, wholly solid, of gold;

**38:17** and seven golden lamps upon it, and its tongs of gold, and their oil vessels of gold.

**38:18** He overlaid the pillars with silver, and cast golden rings for the pillar, and gilded the bars with gold, and overlaid the pillars of the veil with gold, and made the hooks of gold.

**38:19** He also made the clasps of the tabernacle of gold, and the clasps of the court and clasps for stretching out the covering above, of bronze.

**38:20** He cast the silver capitals of the tabernacle, and the bronze capitals of the door of the tabernacle, and the gate of the court; and he made silver hooks for the pillars upon the pillars; he overlaid them with silver.

**38:21** He also made the pegs of the court of bronze.

**38:22** He made the bronze altar out of the bronze censers that belonged to the men who rebelled with the congregation of Korah.

**38:23** He made all the utensils of the altar, its firepan, and the base, and the basins, and the flesh-hooks of bronze.

**38:24** He made for the altar a grating, a work of network, beneath the firepan, under it up to the middle of it; and he placed upon it four bronze rings on the four sides of the grating of the altar, wide for the bars, so as to carry the altar with them.

**38:25** He made the holy anointing oil and the composition of the incense, the pure work of a perfumer.

**38:26** He made the bronze laver and its bronze base from the mirrors of the women who fasted, who fasted by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony on the day that he pitched it;

**38:27** and he made the laver so that Moses and Aaron and his sons might wash their hands and feet from it when they entered into the tabernacle of testimony; or whenever they drew near to the altar to minister, they washed from it, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **38:18** — Verses 18–26 shift from the connecting phrase "And he" to an emphatic demonstrative pronoun (houtos, literally "this man"), highlighting Bezalel's craftsmanship across these furnishings.
- **38:20** — The Septuagint renders the tabernacle sockets or bases as "capitals" (kephalidas), a distinctive architectural term carried through into 39:4–5.
- **38:22** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint explicitly states that the bronze altar was made from the censers of the men who rebelled with Korah (compare Numbers 16:36–40).
- **38:26** — The Greek reads "the women who fasted, who fasted," preserving a distinctive Septuagint reading in place of the Hebrew "women who served."
