## Text

**21:1** "And these are the ordinances which you shall set before them:

**21:2** If you acquire a Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve you; and in the seventh year he shall depart free, without payment.

**21:3** If he comes in alone, he shall also go out alone; and if a wife comes in with him, the wife shall also go out with him.

**21:4** And if the master gives him a wife, and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and the children shall belong to his master, and he himself shall go out alone.

**21:5** But if the servant answers and says, 'I have loved my master and my wife and my children; I will not depart free,'

**21:6** then his master shall bring him to the judgment seat of God, and then he shall bring him to the door, to the doorpost; and his master shall pierce his ear with the awl, and he shall serve him forever.

**21:7** And if anyone sells his own daughter as a household servant, she shall not depart as the female slaves depart.

**21:8** If she whom he betrothed to himself does not please her master, he shall let her be redeemed; yet he has no authority to sell her to a foreign nation, because he has broken faith with her.

**21:9** And if he betroths her to his son, he shall deal with her according to the ordinance for daughters.

**21:10** And if he takes another wife, he shall not withhold her necessities, and her clothing, and her marital companionship.

**21:11** And if he does not do these three things for her, she shall go out free, without silver.

**21:12** And if anyone strikes someone so that he dies, he shall surely be put to death;

**21:13** but if he did not do so willingly, rather God delivered him into his hands, then I will give you a place to which the one who killed may flee.

**21:14** But if anyone sets upon his neighbor to kill him by guile, and flees for refuge, you shall take him from My altar to put him to death.

**21:15** Whoever strikes his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.

**21:16** The one who speaks evil of his father or his mother shall surely die.

**21:17** Whoever steals any one of the sons of Israel and, having overpowered him, sells him, and he is found in his possession, shall surely die.

**21:18** And if two men quarrel and strike a neighbor with a stone or with a fist, and he does not die, but is laid up upon his bed,

**21:19** if the man rises up and walks about outside upon a staff, then the one who struck him shall be guiltless; only he shall pay for his loss of labor and the healing expenses.

**21:20** And if anyone strikes his manservant or his maidservant with a rod, and he dies under his hands, he shall surely be punished with justice.

**21:21** Yet if he survives one day or two, he shall not be punished; for he is his silver.

**21:22** And if two men fight and strike a woman who is with child, and her child comes out not yet formed, he shall surely be fined; according to whatever the woman's husband imposes, he shall pay with an assessment.

**21:23** But if it was formed, he shall give life for life,

**21:24** eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

**21:25** burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

**21:26** And if anyone strikes the eye of his manservant or the eye of his maidservant and blinds it, he shall send them away free for their eye.

**21:27** And if he knocks out the tooth of his manservant or the tooth of his maidservant, he shall send them away free for their tooth.

**21:28** And if a bull gores a man or a woman, and that person dies, the bull shall surely be stoned with stones, and its flesh shall not be eaten; and the owner of the bull shall be guiltless.

**21:29** But if the bull was given to goring before yesterday and before the third day, and testimony was given to its owner, and he did not do away with it, and it kills a man or a woman, the bull shall be stoned and its owner shall be put to death as well.

**21:30** Yet if a ransom is imposed upon him, he shall pay as a ransom for his life whatever they impose upon him.

**21:31** And if it gores a son or a daughter, according to this ordinance they shall do to him.

**21:32** And if the bull gores a manservant or a maidservant, he shall pay thirty didrachmas of silver to their master, and the bull shall be stoned.

**21:33** And if anyone opens a pit, or hews out a pit, and does not cover it, and a calf or a donkey falls into it,

**21:34** the owner of the pit shall make restitution; he shall pay silver to their owner, and the dead animal shall belong to him.

**21:35** And if someone's bull gores his neighbor's bull, and it dies, they shall sell the living bull and divide its silver, and the dead bull they shall also divide.

**21:36** But if the bull is known to be given to goring before yesterday and before the third day, and testimony has been given to its owner, and he has not done away with it, he shall pay bull for bull, and the dead one shall belong to him.

**21:37** And if anyone steals a calf or a sheep and slaughters it or sells it, he shall pay five calves for the calf and four sheep for the sheep."

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Greek interprets the Hebrew term for God or judges specifically as the judgment seat (tribunal) of God.
- **21:16** — The Septuagint places the statute on reviling parents in verse 16 and the statute on kidnapping in verse 17, reversing the Masoretic sequence.
- **21:22** — In verses 22 and 23, the Septuagint distinguishes between a child not yet formed and one already formed, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew focuses on whether further harm follows.
- **21:29** — Literally "before yesterday and before the third day," a Septuagint idiom for "in times past" (likewise in verse 36).
- **21:37** — This verse concludes chapter 21 in the Septuagint, whereas in the Masoretic Text and most English Bibles it is numbered as Exodus 22:1.
