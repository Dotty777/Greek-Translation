## Text

**3:1** Now Moses was tending the sheep of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the sheep beyond the wilderness and came to Mount Horeb.

**3:2** And the Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire out of the bush; and he saw that the bush was burning with fire, yet the bush was not being consumed.

**3:3** Then Moses said, "I will turn aside and see this great sight, why the bush is not consumed."

**3:4** And when the Lord saw that he drew near to look, the Lord called him out of the bush, saying, "Moses, Moses!" And he said, "What is it?"

**3:5** And He said, "Do not draw near here; loose the sandal from your feet, for the place on which you stand is holy ground."

**3:6** And He said, "I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." And Moses turned away his face, for he was reverently afraid to gaze before God.

**3:7** And the Lord said to Moses, "I have surely seen the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their outcry because of the taskmasters; for I know their anguish;

**3:8** and I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them out of that land, and to bring them into a good and spacious land, into a land flowing with milk and honey, into the place of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Girgashites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

**3:9** And now, behold, the outcry of the children of Israel has come to Me, and I have seen the oppression with which the Egyptians oppress them.

**3:10** And now come, let Me send you to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall bring My people, the children of Israel, out of the land of Egypt."

**3:11** And Moses said to God, "Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and that I should bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt?"

**3:12** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "Certainly I will be with you; and this shall be the sign to you that I shall send you forth: when you have brought My people out of Egypt, you shall serve God on this mountain."

**3:13** Then Moses said to God, "Behold, I shall go out to the children of Israel and say to them, 'The God of our fathers has sent me to you.' And they will ask me, 'What is His name?' What shall I say to them?"

**3:14** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "I am He who is." And He said, "Thus you shall say to the children of Israel, 'He who is has sent me to you.'"

**3:15** And God said again to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the children of Israel, 'The Lord, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.' This is My everlasting name, and a memorial to generations of generations.

**3:16** Go, therefore, gather the council of elders of the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'The Lord, the God of your fathers, has appeared to me, the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, saying, "I have surely visited you and looked upon all that has happened to you in Egypt."

**3:17** And He said, "I will bring you up out of the affliction of the Egyptians into the land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Girgashites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, into a land flowing with milk and honey."'

**3:18** And they will listen to your voice; and you and the council of elders of Israel shall go in to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'The God of the Hebrews has summoned us; let us therefore go a journey of three days into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to our God.'

**3:19** Yet I know that Pharaoh king of Egypt will not let you go, except by a mighty hand;

**3:20** and stretching out My hand, I will strike the Egyptians with all My wonders which I will perform among them, and after these things he will send you forth.

**3:21** And I will give favor to this people in the sight of the Egyptians; and when you depart in haste, you shall not go away empty-handed:

**3:22** each woman shall ask of her neighbor and of the woman dwelling in her house for articles of silver and gold, and for clothing, and you shall put them upon your sons and upon your daughters; and plunder the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — The Septuagint renders Moses's response as "What is it?" rather than "Here I am."
- **3:8** — Here and in verse 17, the Septuagint lists seven nations by including the Girgashites, whereas the Masoretic Text lists six.
- **3:14** — The Septuagint translates God's self-revelation using the masculine present participle "ho on" ("He who is" or "the Existing One"), expressing His eternal, self-existent being.
- **3:17** — The Septuagint reads the third person ("And He said"), continuing Moses's quotation of God to the elders, whereas the Masoretic Text reads the first person ("And I said").
