## Text

**34:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Carve for yourself two stone tablets like the first ones, and come up to Me onto the mountain, and I will write upon the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you shattered.

**34:2** And be ready for the morning, and you shall ascend Mount Sinai, and stand before Me there on the summit of the mountain.

**34:3** And let no one ascend with you, nor let anyone be seen on all the mountain; and let neither the sheep nor the cattle graze near that mountain."

**34:4** And he carved two stone tablets just like the first ones; and rising early in the morning, Moses ascended Mount Sinai, just as the Lord had commanded him, and Moses took the two stone tablets.

**34:5** And the Lord descended in a cloud and stood beside him there, and called upon the name of the Lord.

**34:6** And the Lord passed before his face, and proclaimed, "The Lord God, compassionate and merciful, longsuffering and abounding in mercy and true,

**34:7** and keeping righteousness and mercy for thousands, taking away iniquities and unrighteousness and sins; and He will not acquit the guilty, bringing the iniquities of fathers upon children and upon children's children, to the third and fourth generation."

**34:8** And Moses hastened, bowed down to the earth, and worshiped,

**34:9** and said, "If I have found favor before You, let my Lord go along with us; for the people are stiff-necked, and You shall take away our sins and our iniquities, and we shall be Yours."

**34:10** And the Lord said to Moses, "Behold, I establish a covenant with you before all your people; I will perform glorious deeds that have not been done in all the earth or in any nation; and all the people among whom you are shall see the works of the Lord, for wondrous are the things that I will do for you.

**34:11** Give heed yourself to all things that I command you; behold, I am driving out before your face the Amorite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hittite and the Hivite and the Girgashite and the Jebusite.

**34:12** Take heed to yourself, lest you ever make a covenant with those who dwell in the land into which you are entering, lest it become a stumbling block to you in your midst.

**34:13** Their altars you shall tear down, and their pillars you shall shatter, and their groves you shall cut down, and the carved images of their gods you shall burn completely in fire.

**34:14** For you shall by no means worship other gods; for the Lord God, a jealous name, is a jealous God;

**34:15** lest you make a covenant with those who dwell among foreigners in the land, and they play the harlot after their gods, and sacrifice to their gods, and invite you and you eat of their sacrifices,

**34:16** and you take of their daughters for your sons, and give of your daughters to their sons, and your daughters play the harlot after their gods, and your sons play the harlot after their gods.

**34:17** And you shall not make for yourself molten gods.

**34:18** And you shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I have commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of new produce; for in the month of new produce you came out of Egypt.

**34:19** Everything that opens the womb—the males are Mine, every firstborn of a calf and firstborn of a sheep.

**34:20** And the firstborn of a beast of burden you shall redeem with a sheep; and if you do not redeem it, you shall pay a price. Every firstborn of your sons you shall redeem; you shall not appear before Me empty-handed.

**34:21** Six days you shall labor, but on the seventh there shall be rest; in sowing and in harvest there shall be rest.

**34:22** And you shall keep a Feast of Weeks for Me, the beginning of the wheat harvest, and the beginning of the ingathering in the middle of the year.

**34:23** Three times in the year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord, the God of Israel.

**34:24** For when I drive out the nations before your face and enlarge your borders, no one shall covet your land whenever you go up to appear before the Lord your God three times in the year.

**34:25** You shall not slaughter the blood of My incense offerings with leaven, nor shall the sacrifices of the Feast of the Passover remain overnight until the morning.

**34:26** The firstfruits of your land you shall place in the house of the Lord your God. You shall not offer a lamb in its mother's milk."

**34:27** And the Lord said to Moses, "Write for yourself these words; for upon these words I have established a covenant with you and with Israel."

**34:28** And Moses was there before the Lord forty days and forty nights; bread he did not eat, and water he did not drink. And he wrote these words upon the tablets of the covenant, the ten words.

**34:29** Now as Moses was coming down from the mountain, and the two tablets were in the hands of Moses, as he was coming down from the mountain, Moses did not know that the appearance of the skin of his face had been glorified while he was speaking with Him.

**34:30** And Aaron and all the elders of Israel saw Moses, and the appearance of the skin of his face was glorified; and they were afraid to draw near to him.

**34:31** And Moses called to them, and Aaron and all the rulers of the congregation turned back to him; and Moses spoke to them.

**34:32** And afterward all the sons of Israel drew near to him, and he commanded them all things that the Lord had commanded him on Mount Sinai.

**34:33** And when he had finished speaking to them, he placed a veil upon his face.

**34:34** But whenever Moses went in before the Lord to speak with Him, he would remove the veil until he came out; and having come out, he would speak to all the sons of Israel whatever the Lord had commanded him.

**34:35** And the sons of Israel saw the face of Moses, that it was glorified; and Moses put a veil over his face until he went in to speak with Him.

## Translator Notes

- **34:11** — The Septuagint lists seven nations here, adding the Girgashite, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists six.
- **34:18** — The Greek phrase "month of new produce" (literally "of the new things") is the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew month name Abib.
- **34:20** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text commands breaking the neck of an unredeemed donkey, the Septuagint specifies paying a price ("you shall pay a price").
- **34:26** — The Septuagint reads "offer a lamb" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "boil a young goat."
- **34:29** — The Septuagint renders the radiance of Moses' face as "the appearance of the skin of his face had been glorified," vocabulary later echoed in 2 Corinthians 3:7-13.
