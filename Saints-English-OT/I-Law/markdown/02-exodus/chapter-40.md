## Text

**40:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**40:2** "On the first day of the first month, at the new moon, you shall set up the tabernacle of testimony.

**40:3** And you shall cover the ark of testimony with the veil.

**40:4** And you shall bring in the table and set out its presentation; and you shall bring in the lampstand and place its lamps.

**40:5** And you shall set the golden altar for burning incense before the ark, and you shall place a covering of the veil over the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**40:6** And the altar of burnt offerings you shall set beside the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.

**40:8** And you shall enclose the tabernacle, and you shall sanctify all that belongs to it all around; and you shall anoint the altar of burnt offerings and all its vessels.

**40:9** And you shall take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it, and you shall sanctify it and all its vessels, and it shall be holy.

**40:10** And you shall sanctify the altar, and it shall be most holy.

**40:12** And you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and wash them with water.

**40:13** And you shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him and sanctify him to serve as priest to Me.

**40:14** And you shall bring his sons forward and clothe them with tunics,

**40:15** and you shall anoint them just as you anointed their father, and they shall serve as priests to Me; and it shall come to pass that an anointing of priesthood shall belong to them forever, throughout their generations."

**40:16** And Moses did all that the Lord commanded him; so he did.

**40:17** And it came to pass in the first month, in the second year of their going out from Egypt, at the new moon, that the tabernacle was set up.

**40:18** And Moses set up the tabernacle, and placed the capitals, and inserted the bars, and set up the pillars.

**40:19** And he spread out the curtains over the tabernacle, and placed the covering of the tabernacle upon it from above, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:20** And taking the testimonies, he put them into the ark, and placed the poles beneath the ark,

**40:21** and he brought the ark into the tabernacle, and placed the covering of the veil, and covered the ark of testimony, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:22** And he placed the table in the tabernacle of testimony, on the north side of the tabernacle of testimony, outside the veil of the tabernacle,

**40:23** and he set out upon it loaves of presentation before the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:24** And he put the lampstand in the tabernacle of testimony, on the south side of the tabernacle,

**40:25** and he placed its lamps before the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:26** And he put the golden altar in the tabernacle of testimony opposite the veil,

**40:27** and he offered upon it the compounded incense, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:29** And the altar of burnt offerings he set beside the doors of the tabernacle,

**40:33** and he set up the court all around the tabernacle and the altar; and Moses finished all the works.

**40:34** And the cloud covered the tabernacle of testimony, and the tabernacle was filled with the glory of the Lord.

**40:35** And Moses was not able to enter into the tabernacle of testimony, because the cloud overshadowed it, and the tabernacle was filled with the glory of the Lord.

**40:36** Now whenever the cloud ascended from the tabernacle, the sons of Israel would break camp with their baggage;

**40:37** but if the cloud did not ascend, they would not break camp until the day on which the cloud ascended.

**40:38** For a cloud was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was upon it by night, in the sight of all Israel, throughout all their journeys.

## Translator Notes

- **40:8** — The Septuagint omits verses 7 and 11 (concerning the washbasin) and places the command to anoint the altar of burnt offerings here rather than in verse 10, while reading "enclose the tabernacle" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to setting up the court.
- **40:17** — The Greek text adds "of their going out from Egypt" and specifies "at the new moon," expanding on the date formula in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **40:18** — The Greek reads "placed the capitals" (the tops of the pillars), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions laying the bases and setting up the frames.
- **40:33** — The Septuagint omits verses 28 and 30–32 (concerning the entrance screen and the bronze basin) and uses the plural "all the works" for Moses' completion of the sanctuary.
- **40:38** — The Greek reads "a cloud" (rather than "the cloud of the Lord") and states that fire was "upon it" (the tabernacle) by night, rather than "in it" (the cloud) as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
