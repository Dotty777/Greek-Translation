## Text

**1:1** These are the names of the sons of Israel who came into Egypt together with Jacob their father; each one of them entered with his whole household:

**1:2** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah,

**1:3** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

**1:4** Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**1:5** Now Joseph was in Egypt; and all the souls who came from Jacob were seventy-five.

**1:6** And Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation.

**1:7** But the children of Israel increased and multiplied, and became a teeming throng, and grew exceedingly mighty; and the land multiplied them.

**1:8** Now another king arose over Egypt, who did not know Joseph.

**1:9** And he said to his nation, "Behold, the race of the children of Israel is a great multitude, and is stronger than we;

**1:10** come, therefore, let us deal craftily with them, lest they multiply, and when war happens to us, these also will be joined to our adversaries, and having waged war against us, they will go out from the land."

**1:11** Therefore he set overseers of labors over them, that they might afflict them in their labors; and they built fortified cities for Pharaoh, namely Pithom and Raamses and On, which is Heliopolis.

**1:12** Yet the more they humbled them, so much the more numerous they became, and they grew exceedingly mighty; and the Egyptians abhorred the children of Israel.

**1:13** And the Egyptians oppressed the children of Israel by force,

**1:14** and they made their life grievous in hard labors, in clay and in brickmaking, and in all the labors in the fields, in all the works with which they enslaved them with force.

**1:15** Then the king of the Egyptians spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews, to the one whose name was Shiphrah, and the name of the second was Puah;

**1:16** and he said, "When you serve as midwives to the Hebrew women and they are about to give birth, if it is a male, slay him; but if it is a female, preserve her alive."

**1:17** But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt commanded them, and they kept the male children alive.

**1:18** So the king of Egypt called the midwives and said to them, "Why is it that you have done this thing, and have kept the male children alive?"

**1:19** And the midwives said to Pharaoh, "The Hebrew women are not like the women of Egypt, for they give birth before the midwives come in to them, and they gave birth."

**1:20** Therefore God dealt well with the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly strong.

**1:21** Because the midwives feared God, they made houses for themselves.

**1:22** Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, "Every male that is born to the Hebrews, cast into the river, and every female, keep alive."

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — The Septuagint counts seventy-five souls descending from Jacob (compare Acts 7:14), whereas the Masoretic Text reads seventy.
- **1:11** — The Septuagint adds "and On, which is Heliopolis" as a third fortified city alongside Pithom and Raamses.
- **1:21** — The Septuagint uses a third-person plural verb ("they made houses for themselves"), whereas the Masoretic Text states that God established households for the midwives.
