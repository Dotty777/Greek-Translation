## Text

**27:1** And you shall make an altar of incorruptible wood, five cubits in length and five cubits in width; the altar shall be square, and its height shall be three cubits.

**27:2** And you shall make the horns upon the four corners; the horns shall be of one piece with it, and you shall overlay them with bronze.

**27:3** And you shall make a rim for the altar, and its cover, and its basins, and its fleshhooks, and its firepan; and all its utensils you shall make of bronze.

**27:4** And you shall make for it a bronze grate of network; and you shall make for the grate four bronze rings under the four sides.

**27:5** And you shall place them underneath the grate of the altar below; and the grate shall extend to halfway up the altar.

**27:6** And you shall make poles for the altar of incorruptible wood, and overlay them with bronze.

**27:7** And you shall insert the poles into the rings; and the poles shall be along the sides of the altar when carrying it.

**27:8** Hollow with boards you shall make it; according to what was shown to you on the mountain, so you shall make it.

**27:9** And you shall make a court for the tabernacle: for the side toward the southwest there shall be hangings of the court, one hundred cubits in length for the one side;

**27:10** and their pillars shall be twenty, and their twenty bases of bronze; and their hooks and their bands shall be of silver.

**27:11** Likewise for the side toward the east there shall be hangings one hundred cubits in length; and their pillars shall be twenty, and their twenty bases of bronze; and the hooks and the bands of the pillars and their bases shall be overlaid with silver.

**27:12** And for the width of the court toward the sea there shall be hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten.

**27:13** And for the width of the court toward the south there shall be hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten.

**27:14** And fifteen cubits shall be the height of the hangings on the one side; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:15** And for the second side, fifteen cubits shall be the height of the hangings; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:16** And for the gate of the court there shall be a covering, twenty cubits in height, of blue, and purple, and twisted scarlet, and twisted fine linen, with the variegated work of an embroiderer; their pillars shall be four, and their bases four.

**27:17** All the pillars of the court all around shall be overlaid with silver; their capitals shall be of silver, and their bases of bronze.

**27:18** And the length of the court shall be one hundred by one hundred, and the width fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of twisted fine linen, and their bases of bronze.

**27:19** And all the equipment, and all the instruments, and the pegs of the court shall be of bronze.

**27:20** And you, command the sons of Israel, and let them take for you olive oil free of dregs, pure and beaten, for light to burn, that a lamp may burn continually

**27:21** in the tabernacle of the testimony, outside the veil that is over the covenant; Aaron and his sons shall burn it from evening until morning before the Lord, an everlasting statute throughout your generations from the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Septuagint specifies a "rim" (stephane) and "cover" (kalypter) for the altar, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists pots for ashes and shovels.
- **27:9** — In verses 9–13, the Septuagint orients the court's sides differently from the Masoretic Text, placing the two hundred-cubit sides toward the southwest (lips) and east (apeliotes), and the two fifty-cubit sides toward the sea (west) and south (notos).
- **27:14** — In verses 14–16, the Septuagint refers to "the height" (to hypsos) of the gate hangings as fifteen and twenty cubits, whereas verse 18 states the court's height as five cubits (in the Masoretic Text, fifteen and twenty cubits are horizontal widths).
- **27:18** — The Septuagint reads "one hundred by one hundred" for the length of the court, paralleling "fifty by fifty" for the width, where the Masoretic Text has "one hundred cubits."
