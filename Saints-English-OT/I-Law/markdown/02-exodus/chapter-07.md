## Text

**7:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Behold, I have appointed you as a god to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother shall be your prophet.

**7:2** You shall speak to him all the things that I command you, and Aaron your brother shall speak to Pharaoh, that he send away the sons of Israel out of his land.

**7:3** Yet I will harden the heart of Pharaoh, and I will multiply My signs and wonders in the land of Egypt.

**7:4** And Pharaoh will not listen to you; and I will lay My hand upon Egypt, and I will bring out My people, the sons of Israel, with My power out of the land of Egypt with great vengeance.

**7:5** And all the Egyptians shall know that I am the Lord, when I stretch out My hand upon Egypt; and I will bring out the sons of Israel from their midst."

**7:6** So Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord commanded them; thus they did.

**7:7** Now Moses was eighty years old, and Aaron his brother was eighty-three years old, when he spoke to Pharaoh.

**7:8** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**7:9** "And if Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, 'Give us a sign or a wonder,' then you shall say to Aaron your brother, 'Take the rod and cast it upon the ground before Pharaoh and before his attendants, and it shall become a dragon.'"

**7:10** So Moses and Aaron went in before Pharaoh and his attendants, and they did thus, just as the Lord had commanded them; and Aaron cast down the rod before Pharaoh and before his attendants, and it became a dragon.

**7:11** Then Pharaoh summoned the wise men of Egypt and the sorcerers; and the enchanters of the Egyptians also did likewise with their sorceries,

**7:12** and each one cast down his rod, and they became dragons; yet the rod of Aaron swallowed up their rods.

**7:13** And the heart of Pharaoh grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded them.

**7:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "The heart of Pharaoh is weighed down, so that he will not send away the people.

**7:15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning; behold, he is going out to the water, and you shall stand to meet him upon the bank of the river; and the rod that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand,

**7:16** and you shall say to him, 'The Lord God of the Hebrews has sent me to you, saying, "Send away My people, that they may serve Me in the wilderness"; and behold, you have not listened until now.

**7:17** Thus says the Lord, "By this you shall know that I am the Lord: behold, I strike with the rod that is in My hand upon the water that is in the river, and it shall turn into blood;

**7:18** and the fish that are in the river shall die, and the river shall grow foul, and the Egyptians shall not be able to drink water from the river."'"

**7:19** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron your brother, 'Take your rod in your hand, and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, and over their rivers, and over their canals, and over their marshes, and over all their standing water, and it shall become blood.'" And there was blood throughout all the land of Egypt, both in the wood and in the stone.

**7:20** And Moses and Aaron did thus, just as the Lord had commanded them; and lifting up his rod, he struck the water that was in the river before Pharaoh and before his attendants, and all the water that was in the river turned into blood.

**7:21** And the fish that were in the river died, and the river grew foul, and the Egyptians could not drink water from the river; and the blood was throughout all the land of Egypt.

**7:22** Yet the enchanters of the Egyptians also did likewise with their sorceries; and the heart of Pharaoh grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had said.

**7:23** Then Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and he did not set his mind even upon this.

**7:24** And all the Egyptians dug all around the river so as to drink water from the river, yet they could not drink water from the river.

**7:25** And seven days were fulfilled after the Lord struck the river.

**7:26** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, "Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**7:27** Yet if you are not willing to send them away, behold, I strike all your borders with frogs.

**7:28** And the river shall pour forth frogs; and coming up they shall enter into your houses, and into the inner chambers of your bedrooms, and upon your beds, and into the houses of your attendants and of your people, and into your dough and into your ovens;

**7:29** and upon you, and upon your attendants, and upon your people the frogs shall come up."'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:9** — The Septuagint uses "dragon" (drakon) here and in 7:10 and 7:12, distinguishing it from "serpent" (ophis) in 7:15.
- **7:13** — The Septuagint reads "just as the Lord had commanded them" where the Masoretic Text has "just as the Lord had spoken."
- **7:19** — The final clause shifts to a past-tense narrative verb ("And there was blood") in the Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Text continues the Lord's future-tense command.
- **7:26** — Verses 7:26-29 in the Septuagint correspond to 8:1-4 in the Masoretic Text and most English translations.
