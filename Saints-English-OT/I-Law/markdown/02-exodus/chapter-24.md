## Text

**24:1** And He said to Moses, "Come up to the Lord, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel, and they shall worship the Lord from afar.

**24:2** And Moses alone shall draw near to God, but they shall not draw near; nor shall the people go up with them."

**24:3** Then Moses went in and recounted to the people all the words of God and the ordinances; and all the people answered with one voice, saying, "All the words which the Lord has spoken we will do, and we will heed."

**24:4** And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord. And rising early in the morning, Moses built an altar beneath the mountain, and twelve stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.

**24:5** And he sent forth the young men of the sons of Israel, and they offered up whole burnt offerings, and sacrificed young calves as a sacrifice of salvation to God.

**24:6** And Moses took half of the blood and poured it into basins, and half of the blood he poured out against the altar.

**24:7** And taking the book of the covenant, he read it in the ears of the people; and they said, "All things that the Lord has spoken we will do, and we will heed."

**24:8** And Moses took the blood, sprinkled it upon the people, and said, "Behold, the blood of the covenant which the Lord has made with you concerning all these words."

**24:9** Then Moses went up, with Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the council of elders of Israel;

**24:10** and they saw the place where the God of Israel stood; and what was beneath His feet was as a work of sapphire brick, and as the appearance of the firmament of heaven in its purity.

**24:11** And of the chosen ones of Israel, not even one perished; and they appeared in the place of God, and they ate and drank.

**24:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Come up to Me into the mountain and be there; and I will give you the stone tablets, the law and the commandments which I have written, to give as law to them."

**24:13** So Moses arose, with Joshua who attended him, and they went up into the mountain of God.

**24:14** And to the elders they said, "Remain quietly here until we return to you; and behold, Aaron and Hur are with you. If a dispute arises for anyone, let them go to them."

**24:15** And Moses and Joshua went up into the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

**24:16** And the glory of God descended upon Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days; and on the seventh day the Lord called Moses out of the midst of the cloud.

**24:17** Now the appearance of the glory of the Lord was like a blazing fire on the summit of the mountain before the sons of Israel.

**24:18** And Moses entered into the midst of the cloud and went up into the mountain; and he was there in the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Septuagint reads a third-person plural future, "they shall worship," rather than a second-person command, and transcribes Abihu as Greek Abioud (here rendered by the standard English name Abihu).
- **24:10** — The Septuagint reads "the place where the God of Israel stood" rather than directly saying they saw God, softening the anthropomorphism of the Hebrew text.
- **24:11** — The Septuagint translates "not even one perished" (Greek diephonesen) and "they appeared in the place of God," continuing the reverent phrasing of verse 10.
