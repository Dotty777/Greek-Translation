## Text

**9:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people, that they may serve Me;

**9:2** for if you are not willing to send away My people, but still hold fast to them,

**9:3** behold, the hand of the Lord shall be upon your livestock that are in the fields—upon the horses, and upon the beasts of burden, and the camels, and the oxen, and the sheep—a very great pestilence.

**9:4** And I will make a wonderful distinction at that time between the livestock of the Egyptians and the livestock of the sons of Israel; not a single thing shall die of all that belongs to the sons of Israel.'"

**9:5** And God set an appointed time, saying, "Tomorrow the Lord will do this thing in the land."

**9:6** And the Lord did this thing on the next day, and all the livestock of the Egyptians died; but of the livestock of the sons of Israel, not one died.

**9:7** But when Pharaoh saw that not one of all the livestock of the sons of Israel had died, the heart of Pharaoh became heavy, and he did not send away the people.

**9:8** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, "Take for yourselves handfuls of soot from a furnace, and let Moses scatter it toward heaven before Pharaoh and before his servants,

**9:9** and let it become dust over all the land of Egypt; and upon men and upon four-footed beasts there shall be boils, erupting blisters, both upon men and upon four-footed beasts and throughout all the land of Egypt."

**9:10** So he took the soot of the furnace before Pharaoh, and Moses scattered it toward heaven; and boils, erupting blisters, came upon men and upon four-footed beasts.

**9:11** And the sorcerers could not stand before Moses because of the boils; for the boils were upon the sorcerers and throughout all the land of Egypt.

**9:12** But the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had appointed.

**9:13** And the Lord said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**9:14** For at this present time I am sending forth all My afflictions into your heart and that of your servants and of your people, that you may know that there is no other like Me in all the earth.

**9:15** For now, stretching out My hand, I will strike you and put your people to death, and you shall be blotted out from the earth;

**9:16** and for this very reason you have been preserved, that I may display My strength in you, and that My name may be proclaimed in all the earth.

**9:17** Do you then still lay claim to My people, so as not to send them away?

**9:18** Behold, at this very hour tomorrow I will rain down exceedingly heavy hail, such as has never been in Egypt from the day it was founded until this day.

**9:19** Now therefore, hasten to gather your livestock and all that you have in the field; for all the men and the livestock that you have in the field, and that do not enter into a house, when the hail falls upon them, they shall die.'"

**9:20** He who feared the word of the Lord among the servants of Pharaoh gathered his livestock into the houses;

**9:21** but he who did not give heed in his mind to the word of the Lord left the livestock in the fields.

**9:22** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward heaven, and there shall be hail upon all the land of Egypt, both upon men and upon livestock and upon every plant that is upon the earth."

**9:23** And Moses stretched out his hand toward heaven, and the Lord sent thunderings and hail, and fire ran across the earth; and the Lord rained hail upon all the land of Egypt.

**9:24** So there was hail, and fire blazing within the hail; and the hail was exceedingly heavy, such as had never been in Egypt from the day a nation came into being upon it.

**9:25** And the hail struck throughout all the land of Egypt from man to livestock; and the hail struck every plant that was in the field, and the hail shattered all the trees that were in the fields.

**9:26** Only in the land of Goshen, where the sons of Israel were, there was no hail.

**9:27** Then Pharaoh sent and called Moses and Aaron, and said to them, "I have sinned this time; the Lord is righteous, and I and my people are ungodly.

**9:28** Pray therefore for me to the Lord, and let the thunderings of God and hail and fire cease to be; and I will send you away, and you shall no longer continue to remain."

**9:29** And Moses said to him, "As soon as I go out of the city, I will spread out my hands, and the thunderings shall cease, and the hail and the rain shall be no more, that you may know that the earth is the Lord's.

**9:30** Yet as for you and your servants, I know that you do not yet fear God."

**9:31** Now the flax and the barley were struck, for the barley was standing ripe, and the flax was in seed;

**9:32** but the wheat and the spelt were not struck, for they were late.

**9:33** So Moses went out from Pharaoh, outside the city, and stretched out his hands to the Lord; and the thunderings and the hail ceased, and the rain dropped no more upon the earth.

**9:34** And when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunderings had ceased, he continued to sin, and he made heavy his own heart and that of his servants.

**9:35** And the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not send away the sons of Israel, just as the Lord had spoken to Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **9:14** — Greek synantēmata ("afflictions"), literally "encounters" or "visitations," denotes the calamities sent by the Lord upon Pharaoh and Egypt.
- **9:15** — The Septuagint employs direct future verbs ("I will strike... put to death... you shall be blotted out"), expressing an imminent warning of judgment rather than a past counterfactual condition.
- **9:17** — Greek enpoiē ("lay claim to") means to assert ownership or hold onto, reflecting the Septuagint's distinctive wording in this rebuke.
- **9:23** — Greek phōnai ("thunderings"), literally "voices" or "sounds," is used throughout verses 23–34 for peals of thunder, including "thunderings of God" in 9:28.
