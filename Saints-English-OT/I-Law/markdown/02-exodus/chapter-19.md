## Text

**19:1** Now in the third month of the departure of the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt, on this very day they came into the wilderness of Sinai.

**19:2** For they departed from Rephidim and came into the wilderness of Sinai, and Israel encamped there opposite the mountain.

**19:3** And Moses went up to the mountain of God; and God called to him out of heaven, saying, "Thus you shall say to the house of Jacob, and declare to the sons of Israel:

**19:4** 'You yourselves have seen all that I have done to the Egyptians, and how I took you up as upon wings of eagles, and brought you to Myself.

**19:5** And now, if you will indeed heed My voice and keep My covenant, you shall be to Me a treasured people above all the nations; for all the earth is Mine,

**19:6** and you shall be for Me a royal priesthood and a holy nation.' These words you shall speak to the sons of Israel."

**19:7** So Moses came and spoke to the elders of the people, and set before them all these words which God had commanded him.

**19:8** And all the people answered with one accord and said, "All that God has spoken we will do, and we will obey." And Moses brought back these words to God.

**19:9** And the Lord said to Moses, "Behold, I am coming to you in a pillar of cloud, that the people may hear when I speak to you, and may believe you forever." And Moses reported the words of the people to the Lord.

**19:10** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Go down and solemnly charge the people, and purify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments,

**19:11** and let them be ready for the third day; for on the third day the Lord will come down upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people.

**19:12** And you shall set bounds for the people round about, saying, 'Take heed to yourselves not to go up onto the mountain or touch any part of it; everyone who touches the mountain shall surely die.

**19:13** No hand shall touch him, for he shall surely be stoned with stones or shot through with a dart; whether beast or man, it shall not live.' When the sounds and the trumpets and the cloud depart from the mountain, they shall go up upon the mountain."

**19:14** So Moses went down from the mountain to the people and sanctified them, and they washed their garments.

**19:15** And he said to the people, "Be ready for three days; do not come near a woman."

**19:16** And it came to pass on the third day, as it drew toward dawn, that there came thunderings and lightnings and a dark cloud upon Mount Sinai, and the sound of the trumpet resounded loudly; and all the people who were in the camp were terrified.

**19:17** And Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stood at the foot of Mount Sinai.

**19:18** Now Mount Sinai was altogether in smoke, because God had descended upon it in fire, and the smoke ascended like the smoke of a furnace; and all the people were exceedingly astounded.

**19:19** And the sounds of the trumpet went on growing exceedingly louder; Moses spoke, and God answered him with a voice.

**19:20** And the Lord came down upon Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain; and the Lord called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.

**19:21** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "Go down and solemnly charge the people, lest they draw near to God to gaze intently, and a multitude of them fall;

**19:22** and let the priests who draw near to the Lord God be sanctified, lest the Lord depart from them."

**19:23** And Moses said to God, "The people will not be able to come up to Mount Sinai; for You Yourself solemnly charged us, saying, 'Set bounds around the mountain and sanctify it.'"

**19:24** And the Lord said to him, "Go, descend, and come up, you and Aaron with you; but do not let the priests and the people force their way to come up to God, lest the Lord destroy some of them."

**19:25** So Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — The Septuagint states that God called Moses "out of heaven" (ek tou ouranou), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "from the mountain."
- **19:6** — The Septuagint renders the covenant title "a royal priesthood" (basileion hierateuma, later echoed in 1 Peter 2:9), where the Masoretic Text reads "a kingdom of priests."
- **19:9** — The Septuagint reads "in a pillar of cloud" (en stylo nepheles), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "in a thick cloud."
- **19:13** — The Septuagint specifies that the people may ascend "when the sounds and the trumpets and the cloud depart from the mountain," differing from the Masoretic Text's "when the ram's horn sounds long."
- **19:18** — The Septuagint reads "all the people were exceedingly astounded" (exeste pas ho laos sphodra), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "the whole mountain trembled greatly."
