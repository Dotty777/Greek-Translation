## Text

**4:1** Then Moses answered and said, "If they do not believe me nor listen to my voice—for they will say, 'God has not appeared to you'—what shall I say to them?"

**4:2** And the Lord said to him, "What is this that is in your hand?" And he said, "A staff."

**4:3** And He said, "Cast it upon the ground." So he cast it upon the ground, and it became a serpent; and Moses fled from it.

**4:4** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand and take hold of its tail." So stretching out his hand, he took hold of its tail, and it became a staff in his hand—

**4:5** "that they may believe you, that the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you."

**4:6** And the Lord said to him again, "Put your hand into your bosom." So he put his hand into his bosom; and he brought his hand out of his bosom, and his hand became like snow.

**4:7** And He said again, "Put your hand into your bosom." And he put his hand into his bosom; and he brought it out of his bosom, and it was restored again to the color of its flesh.

**4:8** "Now if they do not believe you nor listen to the voice of the first sign, they will believe you at the voice of the latter sign.

**4:9** And it shall be, if they do not believe you in these two signs nor listen to your voice, that you shall take from the water of the river and pour it out upon the dry ground; and the water that you take from the river shall be blood upon the dry ground."

**4:10** Then Moses said to the Lord, "I pray, Lord, I am not capable—neither before yesterday nor before the third day, nor since You began to speak to Your servant; I am weak-voiced and slow of tongue."

**4:11** And the Lord said to Moses, "Who has given man a mouth, and who makes the hard of hearing and the mute, the seeing and the blind? Is it not I, God?

**4:12** Now therefore go, and I will open your mouth, and I will instruct you in what you are to speak."

**4:13** And Moses said, "I pray, Lord, appoint another capable one whom You will send."

**4:14** Then the Lord was kindled with wrath against Moses and said, "Is there not Aaron your brother, the Levite? I know that he will surely speak for you; and behold, he himself will come out to meet you, and when he sees you, he will rejoice within himself.

**4:15** And you shall speak to him and put My words into his mouth; and I will open your mouth and his mouth, and I will instruct you in what you shall do.

**4:16** And he shall speak for you to the people, and he shall be your mouth; and you shall be to him in the things pertaining to God.

**4:17** And this staff that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand, with which you shall perform the signs."

**4:18** Then Moses went and returned to Jethro his father-in-law, and said, "I will go and return to my brethren who are in Egypt, and see whether they are still alive." And Jethro said to Moses, "Go in good health." Now after those many days, the king of Egypt died.

**4:19** And the Lord said to Moses in Midian, "Go, depart to Egypt; for all those who were seeking your life have died."

**4:20** So Moses took his wife and his children, set them upon the beasts of burden, and returned to Egypt; and Moses took the staff from God in his hand.

**4:21** And the Lord said to Moses, "As you go and return to Egypt, see to all the wonders that I have put in your hands, that you perform them before Pharaoh; yet I will harden his heart, and he will by no means send the people away.

**4:22** And you shall say to Pharaoh, 'Thus says the Lord: Israel is My firstborn son;

**4:23** and I said to you, Send My people away that they may serve Me; now if you are unwilling to send them away, see then, I will slay your firstborn son.'"

**4:24** Now it came to pass on the way, at the lodging place, that an angel of the Lord met him and sought to kill him.

**4:25** Then Zipporah took a stone and circumcised the foreskin of her son, and fell down at his feet and said, "The blood of the circumcision of my child has ceased."

**4:26** And he departed from him, because she had said, "The blood of the circumcision of my child has ceased."

**4:27** Now the Lord said to Aaron, "Go into the wilderness to meet Moses." So he went and met him at the mountain of God, and they kissed one another.

**4:28** And Moses reported to Aaron all the words of the Lord that He had sent, and all the sayings that He had commanded him.

**4:29** Then Moses and Aaron went and gathered together the council of elders of the sons of Israel.

**4:30** And Aaron spoke all these words that God had spoken to Moses, and he performed the signs before the people.

**4:31** So the people believed, and rejoiced that God had visited the sons of Israel and that He had seen their affliction; and the people bowed down and worshiped.

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — The Greek text reads simply "like snow" (ὡσεὶ χιών), without the mention of leprosy found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:16** — The Greek "in the things pertaining to God" (τὰ πρὸς τὸν θεόν) clarifies the Hebrew idiom "as God," emphasizing Moses' role as mediator of God's word to Aaron.
- **4:24** — The Greek attributes this encounter to "an angel of the Lord" (ἄγγελος Κυρίου), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text names the Lord directly.
- **4:25** — Instead of the Hebrew "bridegroom of blood," the Greek renders Zipporah's words as "The blood of the circumcision of my child has ceased" (ἔστη τὸ αἷμα τῆς περιτομῆς τοῦ παιδίου μου), explaining why the angel withdrew in verse 26.
