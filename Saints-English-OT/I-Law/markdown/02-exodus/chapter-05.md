## Text

**5:1** And after these things Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and said to him, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, 'Send My people away, that they may hold a feast to Me in the wilderness.'"

**5:2** And Pharaoh said, "Who is He whose voice I should listen to, so as to send away the sons of Israel? I do not know the Lord, and I will not send Israel away."

**5:3** And they said to him, "The God of the Hebrews has summoned us; therefore we will go a journey of three days into the wilderness, so that we may sacrifice to our God, lest death or slaughter meet us."

**5:4** And the king of Egypt said to them, "Why, Moses and Aaron, do you turn my people away from their labors? Depart, each of you, to his labors."

**5:5** And Pharaoh said, "Behold, now the people are numerous; let us not therefore give them rest from their labors."

**5:6** So Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters of the people and the scribes, saying,

**5:7** "Straw shall no longer be given to the people for brickmaking, as yesterday and the third day; let them go themselves and gather straw for themselves.

**5:8** And the quota of brickmaking that they make each day you shall lay upon them; you shall take away nothing. For they are idle; therefore they cry out, saying, 'Let us arise and sacrifice to our God.'

**5:9** Let the labors of these men be made heavy, and let them concern themselves with these things, and let them not concern themselves with empty words."

**5:10** Then the taskmasters and the scribes pressed them hard, and spoke to the people, saying, "Thus says Pharaoh: 'I will no longer give you straw;

**5:11** go yourselves and gather straw for yourselves wherever you may find it, for nothing is taken away from your quota.'"

**5:12** So the people were scattered throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.

**5:13** And the taskmasters pressed them hard, saying, "Fulfill your appointed labors each day, just as when straw was given to you."

**5:14** And the scribes of the lineage of the sons of Israel, who had been set over them by Pharaoh's overseers, were scourged, and they said, "Why have you not fulfilled your quotas of brickmaking, as yesterday and the third day, and that of today?"

**5:15** Then the scribes of the sons of Israel came in and cried out to Pharaoh, saying, "Why do you deal thus with your servants?

**5:16** Straw is not given to your servants, yet they tell us to make bricks; and behold, your servants have been scourged. You will therefore wrong your own people."

**5:17** And he said to them, "You are idle, you are idlers! Therefore you say, 'Let us go, let us sacrifice to our God.'

**5:18** Now therefore go and work; for straw shall not be given to you, yet you shall deliver the quota of brickmaking."

**5:19** And the scribes of the sons of Israel saw themselves in distress, when it was said, "You shall not fall short of the daily allotment of brickmaking."

**5:20** And they met Moses and Aaron coming to meet them as they came out from Pharaoh,

**5:21** and said to them, "May God look upon you and judge, because you have made our odor detestable before Pharaoh and before his servants, putting a sword into his hands to slay us."

**5:22** Then Moses returned to the Lord and said, "I pray, Lord, why have You afflicted this people? And why have You sent me?

**5:23** For ever since I went to Pharaoh to speak in Your name, he has afflicted this people, and You have not delivered Your people."

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Greek reads "has summoned us" (προσκέκληται ἡμᾶς) rather than "has met with us," and renders the threatened judgments as "death or slaughter" (θάνατος ἢ φόνος).
- **5:14** — The phrase "yesterday and the third day" (ἐχθὲς καὶ τρίτην ἡμέραν, also in 4:10 and 5:7) preserves the Septuagint's literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for "previously."
- **5:16** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the fault is with your own people," the Greek translates the clause as an active future verb: "You will therefore wrong your own people" (ἀδικήσεις οὖν τὸν λαόν σου).
