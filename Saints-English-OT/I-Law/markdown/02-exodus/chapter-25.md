## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and take firstfruits from all to whom it seems good in their heart; and you shall receive My firstfruits.

**25:3** And this is the firstfruit offering which you shall receive from them: gold, and silver, and bronze,

**25:4** and blue, and purple, and double-dyed scarlet, and spun fine linen, and goats' hair,

**25:5** and rams' skins dyed red, and blue-dyed skins, and incorruptible wood,

**25:7** and sardius stones, and stones for engraving, for the shoulder-garment and the full-length robe.

**25:8** And you shall make Me a sanctuary, and I will appear among you.

**25:9** And you shall make for Me according to all things that I show you on the mountain: the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings; thus you shall make it.

**25:10** And you shall make an ark of testimony of incorruptible wood: two and a half cubits its length, and one and a half cubits its breadth, and one and a half cubits its height.

**25:11** And you shall overlay it with pure gold; outside and inside you shall gild it; and you shall make for it wreathed moldings of gold all around.

**25:12** And you shall beat out for it four gold rings and put them upon the four sides: two rings on the one side, and two rings on the second side.

**25:13** And you shall make carrying poles of incorruptible wood, and you shall overlay them with gold;

**25:14** and you shall insert the carrying poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark with them.

**25:15** In the rings of the ark the carrying poles shall remain immovable.

**25:16** And you shall place into the ark the testimonies which I shall give you.

**25:17** And you shall make a mercy seat, a cover of pure gold: two and a half cubits its length, and one and a half cubits its breadth.

**25:18** And you shall make two cherubim of wrought gold, and you shall set them on both sides of the mercy seat;

**25:19** they shall be made one cherub on this side and one cherub on the second side of the mercy seat; and you shall make the two cherubim upon the two sides.

**25:20** The cherubim shall stretch out their wings above, overshadowing the mercy seat with their wings, and their faces shall be toward one another; toward the mercy seat shall the faces of the cherubim be.

**25:21** And you shall set the mercy seat upon the ark above, and into the ark you shall place the testimonies which I shall give you.

**25:22** And I will make Myself known to you from there, and I will speak to you from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubim which are upon the ark of the testimony, and concerning all things that I shall command you for the sons of Israel.

**25:23** And you shall make a golden table of pure gold: two cubits its length, and one cubit its width, and one and a half cubits its height.

**25:24** And you shall make for it wreathed moldings of gold all around; and you shall make for it a rim of a handbreadth all around;

**25:25** and you shall make a wreathed molding for the rim all around.

**25:26** And you shall make four gold rings, and you shall set the four rings upon the four parts of its feet beneath the rim;

**25:27** and the rings shall be as holders for the carrying poles, so as to bear the table with them.

**25:28** And you shall make the carrying poles of incorruptible wood, and you shall overlay them with pure gold, and the table shall be borne with them.

**25:29** And you shall make its dishes, and its censers, and its libation bowls, and its ladles, with which you shall pour drink offerings; of pure gold you shall make them.

**25:30** And you shall set upon the table loaves of the Presence before Me continually.

**25:31** And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold; of wrought work you shall make the lampstand: its shaft, and its branches, and its cups, and its knobs, and its lilies shall be of one piece with it.

**25:32** And six branches shall proceed from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from its second side.

**25:33** And there shall be three cups shaped like nuts on the one branch, a knob and a lily; thus for the six branches proceeding out of the lampstand.

**25:34** And on the lampstand there shall be four cups shaped like nuts; on the one branch, knobs and its lilies.

**25:35** The knob beneath the two branches shall be of one piece with it, and a knob beneath the four branches shall be of one piece with it; thus for the six branches proceeding out of the lampstand. And on the lampstand there shall be four cups shaped like nuts.

**25:36** The knobs and the branches shall be of one piece with it; the whole of it shall be wrought from one piece of pure gold.

**25:37** And you shall make its seven lamps; and you shall set on the lamps, and they shall give light from the one front.

**25:38** And its oil funnel and its trays you shall make of pure gold;

**25:39** all these furnishings shall be a talent of pure gold.

**25:40** See that you make them according to the pattern which was shown to you on the mountain."

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew acacia wood as "incorruptible wood" (xyla asepta) and tahash skins as "blue-dyed skins"; verse 6 of the Hebrew text (listing oil and spices) is omitted in the Septuagint here.
- **25:22** — The Septuagint reads "I will make Myself known to you" (gnosthesomai), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew verb from the root "to know" rather than "to meet."
- **25:23** — The Septuagint describes "a golden table of pure gold" rather than a wooden table overlaid with gold, and accordingly omits the instruction to overlay it in verse 24.
- **25:35** — The Septuagint reads "under the four branches" (rather than repeating "under the two branches") and repeats the clause regarding the four nut-shaped cups on the lampstand from verse 34.
