## Text

**30:1** And you shall make an altar of incense of incorruptible wood.

**30:2** And you shall make it a cubit in length and a cubit in width; it shall be foursquare; and two cubits in height; its horns shall be of one piece with it.

**30:3** And you shall overlay them with pure gold—its hearth, its sides all around, and its horns—and you shall make for it a twisted crown of gold all around.

**30:4** And you shall make two rings of pure gold beneath its twisted crown for the two flanks; you shall make them on the two sides, and they shall be holders for the poles, so as to carry it with them.

**30:5** And you shall make poles of incorruptible wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

**30:6** And you shall set it before the veil that is over the ark of the testimonies, in which I will be made known to you from there.

**30:7** And Aaron shall offer upon it fine compounded incense; morning by morning, when he trims the lamps, he shall burn incense upon it.

**30:8** And when Aaron lights the lamps in the evening, you shall burn incense upon it, a perpetual incense continually before the Lord throughout their generations.

**30:9** And he shall not offer up upon it strange incense, nor a burnt offering, nor a sacrifice; and you shall not pour a drink offering upon it.

**30:10** And Aaron shall make atonement for it upon its horns once a year; from the blood of the purification he shall cleanse it throughout their generations. It is most holy to the Lord.

**30:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:12** "When you take the reckoning of the sons of Israel in their numbering, each one shall give a ransom for his soul to the Lord, and there shall be no downfall among them in their numbering.

**30:13** And this is what all who pass through the numbering shall give: half a didrachma, which is according to the sacred didrachma—twenty obols are the didrachma—and the half didrachma shall be an offering to the Lord.

**30:14** Everyone who passes into the numbering, from twenty years old and above, shall give the offering to the Lord.

**30:15** The rich shall not add to, and the poor shall not diminish from, the half didrachma when giving the offering to the Lord to make atonement for your souls.

**30:16** And you shall receive the silver of the offering from the sons of Israel, and you shall give it for the work of the tabernacle of testimony; and it shall be a memorial for the sons of Israel before the Lord, and to make atonement for your souls."

**30:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:18** "Make a bronze laver and a bronze base for it, for washing; and you shall place it between the tabernacle of testimony and the altar, and you shall pour water into it.

**30:19** And Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it with water.

**30:20** When they enter the tabernacle of testimony, they shall wash with water and shall by no means die; when they approach the altar to minister and to offer up the whole burnt offerings to the Lord,

**30:21** they shall wash their hands and their feet with water; when they enter the tabernacle of testimony, they shall wash with water, lest they die; and it shall be a perpetual statute for them, for him and for his generations after him."

**30:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:23** "And you, take spices: of the flower of choice myrrh, five hundred shekels; and of fragrant cinnamon half of that, two hundred and fifty; and of fragrant calamus, two hundred and fifty;

**30:24** and of iris, five hundred shekels of the sanctuary; and a hin of olive oil.

**30:25** And you shall make it a holy anointing oil, a perfumed ointment by the skill of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil.

**30:26** And with it you shall anoint the tabernacle of testimony and the ark of testimony,

**30:27** and the lampstand and all its vessels, and the tabernacle of testimony and all its vessels, and the altar of incense,

**30:28** and the altar of whole burnt offerings and all its vessels, and the table and all its vessels, and the laver.

**30:29** And you shall sanctify them, and they shall be most holy; everyone who touches them shall be sanctified.

**30:30** And you shall anoint Aaron and his sons, and sanctify them to serve as priests to Me.

**30:31** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'This shall be a holy oil, an ointment of anointing to you throughout your generations.

**30:32** It shall not be anointed upon human flesh, nor shall any be made for yourselves like it according to this composition; it is holy, and it shall be a holy thing to you.

**30:33** Whoever makes any like it, and whoever gives any of it to an outsider, shall be utterly destroyed from his people.'"

**30:34** And the Lord said to Moses, "Take for yourself spices: stacte, onycha, fragrant galbanum, and clear frankincense; there shall be an equal amount of each.

**30:35** And they shall make with it a perfumed incense, the work of a perfumer, blended together, a pure and holy work.

**30:36** And you shall beat some of these very fine, and set it before the testimonies in the tabernacle of testimony, from which place I will be made known to you; it shall be most holy to you.

**30:37** You shall not make for yourselves incense according to this composition; it shall be a holy thing to you for the Lord.

**30:38** Whoever makes any like it so as to smell it shall perish from his people."

## Translator Notes

- **30:6** — The Greek reads 'the ark of the testimonies' (plural) and omits the Hebrew clause referring to the mercy seat over the testimony.
- **30:13** — The Greek renders the sanctuary weights with Hellenistic coinage terms, 'didrachma' and 'obols,' rather than the Hebrew 'shekel' and 'gerahs.'
- **30:24** — The Greek specifies 'iris' (ireos), whereas the Hebrew text lists cassia.
- **30:27** — The Greek repeats 'the tabernacle of testimony and all its vessels' here and lists the table in verse 28, where it also omits the base of the laver.
