## Text

**39:1** All the gold that was worked for the tasks according to all the workmanship of the sanctuary was of the gold of the firstfruits offering: twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and twenty shekels, according to the holy shekel.

**39:2** And the offering of silver from the numbered men of the congregation was one hundred talents and one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels; one drachma per head, half of a shekel, according to the holy shekel.

**39:3** Everyone who passed through the numbering, from twenty years old and upward, amounted to six hundred thousand, and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

**39:4** And the one hundred talents of silver were for the casting of the one hundred capitals of the tabernacle and for the one hundred capitals of the veil, one hundred capitals to the one hundred talents, a talent to each capital.

**39:5** And the one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels they made into the hooks for the pillars, and he overlaid their capitals with gold and adorned them.

**39:6** And the bronze of the offering was seventy talents and one thousand five hundred shekels.

**39:7** And from them he made the bases of the door of the tabernacle of testimony,

**39:8** and the bases of the gate all around, and the bases of the gate of the court, and the pegs of the tabernacle, and the pegs of the court all around,

**39:9** and the bronze grating of the altar, and all the utensils of the altar, and all the implements of the tabernacle of testimony.

**39:10** And the children of Israel did just as the Lord commanded Moses; so they did.

**39:11** And the remaining gold of the offering they made into vessels to minister with them before the Lord;

**39:12** and the blue, and purple, and scarlet that were left over they made into garments of ministry for Aaron, so as to minister in them in the sanctuary.

**39:13** And they brought the garments to Moses, and the tabernacle and its utensils, and the bases, and its bars, and the pillars,

**39:15** and the altar and all its utensils, and the anointing oil, and the compounded incense, and the pure lampstand,

**39:16** and its lamps, the lamps for burning, and the oil for the light,

**39:17** and the table of showbread and all its utensils, and the loaves set forth,

**39:18** and the garments of the sanctuary that belong to Aaron, and the garments of his sons for the priesthood,

**39:19** and the hangings of the court and the pillars, and the veil of the door of the tabernacle and of the gate of the court,

**39:20** and all the utensils of the tabernacle and all its implements, and the hides, rams' skins dyed red, and the coverings, blue skins, and the coverings of the remaining things, and the pegs and all the implements for the works of the tabernacle of testimony.

**39:22** That which the Lord commanded Moses, so the children of Israel made all the equipment.

**39:23** And Moses saw all the works, and they had done them; in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses, so they did them; and Moses blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — The Septuagint specifies "seven hundred and twenty shekels," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads seven hundred and thirty shekels.
- **39:4** — The Greek explicitly reads "one hundred capitals of the veil," a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies four sockets for the veil.
- **39:6** — The Septuagint gives "one thousand five hundred shekels" of bronze here, differing from the two thousand four hundred shekels in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **39:13** — Because the Septuagint arranges the tabernacle construction account differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, standard LXX versification skips numbers 14 and 21 in this chapter.
- **39:15 *(additional)*** — Appears in the Greek critical text as an unplaced verse between 39:13 and 39:15, matching the consecration command of 40:8.
