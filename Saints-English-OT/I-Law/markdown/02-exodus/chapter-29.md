## Text

**29:1** And these are the things which you shall do to them: you shall sanctify them so that they may minister as priests to Me. You shall take one young bull from the herd and two rams without blemish,

**29:2** and unleavened loaves mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil; of fine wheat flour you shall make them.

**29:3** And you shall place them in one basket, and you shall bring them in the basket, along with the young bull and the two rams.

**29:4** And Aaron and his sons you shall bring to the doors of the tabernacle of witness, and you shall wash them with water.

**29:5** And taking the garments, you shall clothe Aaron your brother with the full-length tunic, and the shoulder-piece, and the oracle against the shoulder-piece.

**29:6** And you shall put the turban upon his head, and you shall place the plate of holiness upon the turban.

**29:7** And you shall take some of the anointing oil and pour it upon his head, and anoint him.

**29:8** And you shall bring his sons forward, and clothe them with tunics.

**29:9** And you shall gird them with the sashes, and put the headdresses upon them; and the priesthood to Me shall be theirs forever. And you shall consecrate the hands of Aaron and the hands of his sons.

**29:10** And you shall bring the bull to the doors of the tabernacle of witness, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the bull before the Lord beside the doors of the tabernacle of witness.

**29:11** And you shall slaughter the bull before the Lord beside the doors of the tabernacle of witness.

**29:12** And you shall take some of the blood of the bull, and put it upon the horns of the altar with your finger; and all the remaining blood you shall pour out beside the base of the altar.

**29:13** And you shall take all the fat that is upon the belly, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, and place them upon the altar.

**29:14** But the flesh of the bull, and the hide, and the dung you shall burn up with fire outside the camp; for it is a sin offering.

**29:15** And you shall take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.

**29:16** And you shall slaughter it, and taking the blood, you shall pour it against the altar all around.

**29:17** And you shall cut the ram into pieces according to its limbs, and wash the inward parts and the feet with water, and lay the cut pieces together with the head.

**29:18** And you shall offer up the whole ram upon the altar, a whole burnt offering to the Lord for a sweet-smelling aroma; it shall be an incense offering to the Lord.

**29:19** And you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.

**29:20** And you shall slaughter it, and take some of its blood, and put it upon the lobe of the right ear of Aaron, and upon the tip of his right hand, and upon the tip of his right foot, and upon the lobes of the right ears of his sons, and upon the tips of their right hands, and upon the tips of their right feet.

**29:21** And you shall take some of the blood that is from the altar and some of the oil of anointing, and sprinkle it upon Aaron and upon his garment, and upon his sons and upon the garments of his sons with him; and he shall be sanctified, and his garment, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him. And the blood of the ram you shall pour against the altar all around.

**29:22** And you shall take from the ram its fat, and the fat that covers the belly, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, and the right shoulder; for this is a consecration;

**29:23** and one loaf made with oil and one wafer from the basket of the unleavened loaves that are set forth before the Lord.

**29:24** And you shall put all these things upon the hands of Aaron and upon the hands of his sons, and you shall set apart for them a separated offering before the Lord.

**29:25** And you shall take them from their hands, and offer them up upon the altar of burnt offering for a sweet-smelling aroma before the Lord; it is an offering by fire to the Lord.

**29:26** And you shall take the breast from the ram of consecration, which belongs to Aaron, and set it apart as a separated offering before the Lord; and it shall be yours as a portion.

**29:27** And you shall sanctify the breast as a separated offering and the shoulder of the contribution, which has been set apart and which has been taken up, from the ram of consecration, from that which belongs to Aaron and from that which belongs to his sons.

**29:28** And it shall be for Aaron and for his sons a perpetual statute from the children of Israel; for this is a separated offering, and it shall be a contribution from the children of Israel, from the sacrifices of deliverance of the children of Israel, a contribution to the Lord.

**29:29** And the apparel of the sanctuary which belongs to Aaron shall be for his sons after him, that they may be anointed in them and consecrate their hands.

**29:30** For seven days the priest who takes his place from among his sons shall put them on, who enters into the tabernacle of witness to minister in the sanctuary.

**29:31** And you shall take the ram of consecration and boil the flesh in a holy place.

**29:32** And Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the loaves that are in the basket beside the doors of the tabernacle of witness.

**29:33** They shall eat those things by which they were sanctified, to consecrate their hands, to sanctify them; and an outsider shall not eat of it, for they are holy.

**29:34** And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of consecration and of the loaves remains until morning, you shall burn up what remains with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is a holy thing.

**29:35** And thus you shall do to Aaron and to his sons, according to all that I have commanded you; for seven days you shall consecrate their hands.

**29:36** And you shall offer the young bull of the sin offering on the day of purification; and you shall purify the altar when you sanctify upon it, and you shall anoint it so as to sanctify it.

**29:37** For seven days you shall purify the altar and sanctify it, and the altar shall be most holy; everyone who touches the altar shall be sanctified.

**29:38** Now these are the things which you shall offer upon the altar: two lambs a year old, without blemish, each day upon the altar continually, a continual offering by fire.

**29:39** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer toward evening;

**29:40** and one-tenth of fine flour mixed with beaten oil, one-fourth of a hin, and as a drink offering one-fourth of a hin of wine, for the one lamb.

**29:41** And the second lamb you shall offer toward evening; according to the morning sacrifice and according to its drink offering you shall offer it, a sweet-smelling aroma, an offering by fire to the Lord,

**29:42** a continual sacrifice throughout your generations, at the doors of the tabernacle of witness before the Lord, in which I will be made known to you from there, so as to speak to you.

**29:43** And I will give orders there to the children of Israel, and I will be sanctified in My glory.

**29:44** And I will sanctify the tabernacle of witness and the altar; and Aaron and his sons I will sanctify to minister as priests to Me.

**29:45** And I will be called upon among the children of Israel, and I will be their God.

**29:46** And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, to be called upon by them and to be their God.

## Translator Notes

- **29:5** — The Greek text adds 'your brother' and renders the high priest's vestments distinctly as the 'full-length tunic' (chitona ton podere), the 'shoulder-piece' (epomida, corresponding to the Hebrew ephod), and the 'oracle' (logion, corresponding to the breastpiece).
- **29:20** — The Septuagint applies blood to Aaron's right ear, the tip (akron) of his right hand, and the tip of his right foot before his sons, and places the pouring of the ram's blood around the altar at the end of verse 21.
- **29:24** — The Greek renders the ritual presentation terms as 'separated offering' (aphorisma, vv. 24, 26-28, corresponding to the Hebrew wave offering), 'contribution' (aphairema, vv. 27-28, corresponding to the heave offering), and 'sacrifices of deliverance' (thymata soterion, v. 28).
- **29:43** — The Septuagint preserves distinctive readings in verses 43 and 45-46: 'I will give orders' (taxomai), the first-person 'I will be sanctified in My glory' (hagiasthēsomai), and 'be called upon' (epiklēthēsomai/epiklēthēnai) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'meet,' 'it shall be sanctified,' and 'dwell.'
