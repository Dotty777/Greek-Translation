## Text

**10:1** Now the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Go in to Pharaoh; for I have hardened his heart and the hearts of his servants, that these signs may come upon them in succession,

**10:2** in order that you may recount in the ears of your children and to your children's children all the ways I have mocked the Egyptians, and My signs which I have performed among them; and you shall know that I am the Lord."

**10:3** So Moses and Aaron went in before Pharaoh and said to him, "Thus says the Lord God of the Hebrews: 'How long are you unwilling to revere Me? Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**10:4** Yet if you are unwilling to send away My people, behold, tomorrow at this hour I will bring locusts in abundance upon all your borders;

**10:5** and they shall cover the face of the land, and you shall not be able to see the land; and they shall devour every remnant of the land that was left behind, which the hail left for you, and they shall devour every tree that grows for you upon the land;

**10:6** and your houses shall be filled, and the houses of your servants, and all the houses in all the land of the Egyptians—such things as neither your fathers nor their forefathers have ever seen, from the day they came to be upon the earth until this day.'" And Moses turned aside and went out from Pharaoh.

**10:7** Then the servants of Pharaoh said to him, "How long shall this be a stumbling block to us? Send the men away, so that they may serve their God. Or do you wish to know that Egypt has perished?"

**10:8** So they brought both Moses and Aaron back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, "Go and serve your God; but who and who are those who are going?"

**10:9** And Moses said, "With the young men and the elders we shall go, with our sons and daughters and sheep and cattle; for it is a feast of the Lord."

**10:10** And he said to them, "Let it be so, the Lord be with you; as I send you away, surely not your dependents also? See, for evil lies before you.

**10:11** Not so! Rather, let the men go and serve God, for this is what you yourselves were seeking." And they drove them out from the presence of Pharaoh.

**10:12** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt, and let the locusts come up upon the land, and they shall devour every herb of the land and all the fruit of the trees which the hail left behind."

**10:13** And Moses lifted up the staff toward heaven, and he brought a south wind upon the land all that day and all the night; morning came, and the south wind took up the locusts,

**10:14** and brought them up over all the land of Egypt, and they settled upon all the borders of Egypt in exceeding multitude; before them there had never been such locusts, nor after them shall it be so again.

**10:15** And they covered the face of the land, and the land was ruined; and they devoured every herb of the land and all the fruit of the trees which had been left from the hail; nothing green was left on the trees or among any herb of the field in the land of Egypt.

**10:16** Then Pharaoh made haste to summon Moses and Aaron, saying, "I have sinned before the Lord your God and against you;

**10:17** therefore forgive my sin yet this once, and pray to the Lord your God, and let Him take away this death from me."

**10:18** So Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to God.

**10:19** And the Lord turned a mighty wind from the sea, and it took up the locusts and cast them into the Red Sea; and not one locust remained in all the land of Egypt.

**10:20** But the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he did not send away the sons of Israel.

**10:21** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward heaven, and let there be darkness over the land of Egypt, a darkness that can be felt."

**10:22** So Moses stretched out his hand toward heaven, and there came darkness, gloom, and tempest over all the land of Egypt for three days.

**10:23** And no one saw his brother for three days, and no one rose up from his bed for three days; but for all the sons of Israel there was light in all the places where they dwelt.

**10:24** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron, saying, "Go, serve the Lord your God; only leave behind the sheep and the cattle; and let your dependents depart with you."

**10:25** And Moses said, "Indeed, you yourself shall also give us burnt offerings and sacrifices, which we shall offer to the Lord our God,

**10:26** and our livestock shall go with us, and we shall not leave a single hoof behind; for from them we shall take to serve the Lord our God, and we do not know with what we shall serve the Lord our God until we come there."

**10:27** But the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he was unwilling to send them away.

**10:28** And Pharaoh said, "Depart from me! Take heed to yourself lest you see my face again; for on whatever day you appear before me, you shall die."

**10:29** And Moses said, "You have spoken it; I shall no longer appear before your face."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Greek reads "in succession" (hexes) and "upon them," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "these signs of Mine in his midst."
- **10:7** — The Greek uses the neuter "this" (touto) for the "stumbling block" (skolon) and asks "Or do you wish to know that Egypt has perished?" rather than the Hebrew "Do you not yet know?"
- **10:13** — The Greek specifies "a south wind" (anemon noton) rather than an east wind, has Moses lift his staff "toward heaven," and omits an explicit subject "the Lord" before "brought."
- **10:22** — The Greek intensifies the plague with an asyndetic triad—"darkness, gloom, and tempest" (skotos gnophos thyella)—and in 10:23 specifies that no one rose "from his bed."
