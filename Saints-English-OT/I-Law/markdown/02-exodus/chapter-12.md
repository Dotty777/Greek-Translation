## Text

**12:1** Now the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,

**12:2** "This month shall be the beginning of months for you; it is the first for you among the months of the year.

**12:3** Speak to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, 'On the tenth day of this month, let each one take a sheep according to the houses of their fathers, each one a sheep for a household.

**12:4** And if those in the household are too few so as not to be enough for a sheep, he shall take along with himself his neighbor who is nearest to him; according to the number of souls, each one shall reckon what is sufficient for himself toward a sheep.

**12:5** It shall be to you an unblemished sheep, a male a year old; you shall take it from the lambs and from the young goats.

**12:6** And it shall be kept by you until the fourteenth day of this month; and the entire multitude of the congregation of the sons of Israel shall slaughter it toward evening.

**12:7** And they shall take some of the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel in the houses in which they shall eat them.

**12:8** And they shall eat the flesh on this night, roasted with fire, and they shall eat unleavened bread with bitter herbs.

**12:9** You shall not eat any of them raw, nor boiled in water, but only roasted with fire—the head together with the feet and the inward parts.

**12:10** Nothing shall be left of it until morning, and a bone shall not be broken from it; but what remains of it until morning you shall burn thoroughly with fire.

**12:11** And thus you shall eat it: with your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staffs in your hands; and you shall eat it in haste. It is a Passover to the Lord.

**12:12** And I will come into the land of Egypt on this night and will strike every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from man to beast; and upon all the gods of the Egyptians I will execute vengeance. I am the Lord.

**12:13** And the blood shall be for you as a sign upon the houses in which you are; and I will see the blood and will shelter you, and there shall not be among you a plague to destroy you when I strike in the land of Egypt.

**12:14** And this day shall be to you a memorial, and you shall celebrate it as a feast to the Lord throughout all your generations; you shall celebrate it as an everlasting ordinance.

**12:15** Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread; and from the first day you shall put away leaven out of your houses. Everyone who eats leaven, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from Israel, from the first day until the seventh day.

**12:16** And the first day shall be called holy, and the seventh day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work on them, except whatever shall be prepared for every soul—that alone shall be prepared for you.

**12:17** And you shall keep this commandment; for on this very day I will bring out your host from the land of Egypt. And you shall observe this day throughout your generations as an everlasting ordinance.

**12:18** Beginning on the fourteenth day of the first month, from evening you shall eat unleavened bread, until the twenty-first day of the month, until evening.

**12:19** For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses; everyone who eats anything leavened, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from the congregation of Israel, both among the sojourners and the native-born of the land.

**12:20** You shall eat nothing leavened; in every dwelling place of yours you shall eat unleavened bread.'"

**12:21** Then Moses called the entire council of elders of the sons of Israel and said to them, "Go and take for yourselves a sheep according to your kindred, and sacrifice the Passover.

**12:22** And you shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dipping it in the blood that is beside the door, you shall touch the lintel and both doorposts with some of the blood that is beside the door; and none of you shall go out through the door of his house until morning.

**12:23** And the Lord will pass through to strike the Egyptians, and He will see the blood on the lintel and on both doorposts; and the Lord will pass over the door and will not permit the destroyer to enter your houses to strike.

**12:24** And you shall keep this word as an ordinance for yourself and for your sons forever.

**12:25** And when you enter the land which the Lord shall give you, just as He spoke, you shall keep this service.

**12:26** And it shall come to pass, when your sons say to you, 'What is this service?'

**12:27** then you shall say to them, 'This Passover is a sacrifice to the Lord, because He sheltered the houses of the sons of Israel in Egypt when He struck the Egyptians and delivered our houses.'" And the people bowed down and worshiped.

**12:28** And the sons of Israel went and did just as the Lord had commanded Moses; so they did.

**12:29** Now it came to pass at midnight that the Lord struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat on the throne to the firstborn of the captive woman who was in the dungeon, and even to the firstborn of every beast.

**12:30** And Pharaoh rose up in the night, he and his servants and all the Egyptians; and there arose a great outcry in all the land of Egypt, for there was not a house in which there was not someone dead.

**12:31** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron by night and said to them, "Rise up and go out from among my people, both you and the sons of Israel; go and serve the Lord your God, just as you say.

**12:32** Take along both your sheep and your oxen and depart, and bless me also."

**12:33** And the Egyptians pressed the people hard to drive them out of the land in haste, for they said, "We are all dying."

**12:34** So the people took up their dough before it was leavened, with their batches of dough bound up in their garments upon their shoulders.

**12:35** And the sons of Israel did just as Moses had instructed them, and they asked from the Egyptians articles of silver and gold, and clothing.

**12:36** And the Lord gave favor to His people in the sight of the Egyptians, and they lent to them; thus they plundered the Egyptians.

**12:37** Then the sons of Israel set out from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides the dependents.

**12:38** And a great mixed multitude went up with them, along with sheep and oxen and exceedingly many livestock.

**12:39** And they baked the dough which they had brought out of Egypt into unleavened hearth-cakes, for it was not leavened; because the Egyptians had driven them out and they could not delay, nor had they prepared provisions for themselves for the journey.

**12:40** Now the dwelling of the sons of Israel, which they dwelt in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan, was four hundred and thirty-five years.

**12:41** And it came to pass after the four hundred and thirty-five years that the entire host of the Lord went out from the land of Egypt by night.

**12:42** It is a vigil to the Lord for bringing them out of the land of Egypt; that very night is a vigil to the Lord, to be for all the sons of Israel throughout their generations.

**12:43** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, "This is the law of the Passover: no foreigner shall eat of it;

**12:44** and every household servant or one bought with silver, you shall circumcise him, and then he shall eat of it.

**12:45** A temporary resident or a hired servant shall not eat of it.

**12:46** In one house it shall be eaten; you shall not carry any of the flesh outside the house, and you shall not break a bone of it.

**12:47** All the congregation of the sons of Israel shall keep it.

**12:48** And if any proselyte comes to you to keep the Passover to the Lord, you shall circumcise every male belonging to him, and then he shall draw near to keep it, and he shall be just like the native-born of the land; no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.

**12:49** There shall be one law for the native and for the proselyte who has come among you."

**12:50** And the sons of Israel did just as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron concerning them; so they did.

**12:51** And it came to pass on that day that the Lord brought the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt with their host.

## Translator Notes

- **12:10** — The Greek text includes the clause "and a bone shall not be broken from it" (absent from the Masoretic Text here, though present in 12:46), and uses middle/passive verb forms that may also reflect orthographic variation for second-person plural imperatives.
- **12:13** — Here (and in 12:27) the Septuagint renders the Passover verb with Greek skepazo ("shelter, cover, protect"), whereas in 12:23 it uses parerchomai ("pass over" or "pass by").
- **12:19** — Greek giorais ("sojourners") is a rare Septuagint transliteration of the Aramaic word for a resident alien or proselyte.
- **12:22** — The Septuagint reads para ten thyran ("beside the door") twice, interpreting the Hebrew word saph as "threshold/doorway" rather than "basin."
- **12:40** — The Septuagint specifies that the dwelling was "in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan," and this Greek text (following Codex Vaticanus) gives the duration as "four hundred and thirty-five years" here and in 12:41 rather than four hundred and thirty.
