## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Behold, I have called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**31:3** And I have filled him with a divine spirit of wisdom and understanding and knowledge in every work,

**31:4** to devise designs and to act as a master craftsman, to work in gold and silver and bronze, and in blue and purple and spun scarlet,

**31:5** and in stoneworking, and in the carpentry works of wood, to labor in accordance with all the works.

**31:6** And I have appointed him and Eliab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and to everyone who is understanding in heart I have given understanding;

**31:7** and they shall make all things that I have commanded you: the tabernacle of testimony, and the ark of the covenant, and the mercy seat that is upon it, and the furnishings of the tabernacle,

**31:8** and the altars, and the table and all its vessels, and the pure lampstand and all its vessels,

**31:9** and the laver and its base,

**31:10** and the ministering garments of Aaron and the garments of his sons to serve as priests to Me,

**31:11** and the anointing oil and the compounded incense of the sanctuary; according to all that I have commanded you, they shall make them."

**31:12** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:13** "And you, command the sons of Israel, saying, 'Take heed and keep My Sabbaths; it is a sign with Me and among you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**31:14** And you shall keep the Sabbaths, for this is a holy thing of the Lord for you; he who profanes it shall surely be put to death; everyone who does work on it, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from the midst of his people.

**31:15** Six days you shall do work, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; everyone who does work on the seventh day shall be put to death.

**31:16** And the sons of Israel shall keep the Sabbaths, to observe them throughout their generations—

**31:17** an everlasting covenant between Me and the sons of Israel. It is an everlasting sign with Me; for in six days the Lord made the heaven and the earth, and on the seventh day He ceased and rested.'"

**31:18** And He gave to Moses, when He had finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the testimony, tablets of stone written by the finger of God.

## Translator Notes

- **31:3** — The Greek reads 'a divine spirit' (pneuma theion) rather than the Hebrew 'the Spirit of God.'
- **31:4** — The Greek adds 'and in blue and purple and spun scarlet,' a textile clause absent from the Hebrew text.
- **31:6** — The Greek names Bezalel's co-worker 'Eliab,' whereas the Hebrew text reads 'Oholiab.'
- **31:8** — The Greek groups both altars together as 'the altars' here and has a shorter verse 9 naming only the laver and its base.
