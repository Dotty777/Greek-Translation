## Text

**15:1** Then Moses and the sons of Israel sang this song to God, speaking these words: "Let us sing to the Lord, for gloriously has He been glorified; both horse and rider He has cast into the sea.

**15:2** A helper and a defender has He become to me for deliverance; this is my God, and I shall glorify Him—the God of my father, and I shall exalt Him.

**15:3** The Lord is the One who shatters wars; the Lord is His name.

**15:4** The chariots of Pharaoh and his armed force He has cast into the sea, along with chosen officers who rode upon them; they were swallowed up in the Red Sea.

**15:5** With the open deep He covered them over; they sank downward into the abyss like a stone.

**15:6** Your right hand, O Lord, has been magnified in might; Your right hand, O Lord, has shattered foes.

**15:7** And in the abundance of Your glory You crushed the adversaries; You dispatched Your wrath, and it devoured them like dry stubble.

**15:8** And by the blast of Your indignation the water parted; the waters congealed like a wall, and the billows hardened in the midst of the sea.

**15:9** The foe declared, 'Pursuing them, I will overtake them; I will divide the plunder, I will satisfy my soul, I will slay with my sword, and my hand shall exercise dominion.'

**15:10** You sent forth Your breath, and the sea covered them; they plunged like lead into the surging water.

**15:11** Who is like You among the gods, O Lord? Who is like You? Glorified among the holy ones, wondrous in splendors, performing marvels.

**15:12** You stretched out Your right hand; the earth swallowed them down.

**15:13** In Your righteousness You guided this people of Yours whom You redeemed; by Your strength You summoned them onward to Your holy lodging place.

**15:14** Nations heard and grew furious; birth pangs seized the inhabitants of Philistia.

**15:15** Then the chieftains of Edom hastened in alarm; and as for the rulers of the Moabites, trembling gripped them; all the inhabitants of Canaan melted away.

**15:16** May terror and dread fall upon them; by the greatness of Your arm let them be turned to stone, until Your people pass through, O Lord, until this people of Yours whom You acquired pass through.

**15:17** Bring them in and plant them firmly upon the mountain of Your inheritance, in Your prepared habitation which You fashioned, O Lord—the sanctuary, O Lord, which Your hands made ready.

**15:18** The Lord is King forever, and to the age and even beyond!"

**15:19** For Pharaoh's cavalry entered the sea together with his chariots and horsemen, and the Lord brought the water of the sea back upon them; yet the sons of Israel journeyed across dry ground in the midst of the sea.

**15:20** Then Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took the timbrel in her hand, and all the women went forth behind her with timbrels and dancing.

**15:21** And Miriam led them in song, declaring, "Let us sing to the Lord, for gloriously has He been glorified; both horse and rider He has cast into the sea."

**15:22** Now Moses led the sons of Israel away from the Red Sea and brought them into the wilderness of Shur; and they traveled three days in the wilderness without finding water to drink.

**15:23** Then they arrived at Marah, yet they were unable to drink from Marah, for it was bitter; on account of this he named that place Bitterness.

**15:24** And the people murmured against Moses, saying, "What are we to drink?"

**15:25** So Moses cried aloud to the Lord, and the Lord showed him a piece of wood, and he threw it into the water, and the water grew sweet. There He established for him ordinances and judgments, and there He put him to the test,

**15:26** and He said, "If you truly listen to the voice of the Lord your God, and perform what is pleasing in His sight, and give ear to His commandments, and keep all His ordinances, I will not bring upon you any of the diseases that I brought upon the Egyptians; for I am the Lord your God who heals you."

**15:27** Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy trunks of palm trees; and they encamped there beside the waters.

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The Greek text describes the Lord as "the One who shatters wars" (literally "crushing wars"), a distinctive Septuagint rendering compared to the Hebrew "man of war."
- **15:13** — The Greek reads "in Your righteousness" and uses a verb meaning "summoned" or "encouraged," preserving the Septuagint wording rather than the Hebrew "in Your steadfast love" and "You guided."
- **15:23** — The Greek translates the name of the site directly as Pikria ("Bitterness"), explaining the bitter waters of Marah (Greek Merra).
