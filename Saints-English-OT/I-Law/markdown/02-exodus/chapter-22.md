## Text

**22:1** And if the thief is found breaking in, and he is struck and dies, there is no bloodguilt for him;

**22:2** but if the sun has risen upon him, he is guilty; he shall die in return. And if he has nothing, let him be sold for the theft.

**22:3** And if what was stolen is seized and found in his hand, from donkey even to sheep, alive, he shall restore them twofold.

**22:4** And if anyone grazes a field or a vineyard, and lets his livestock loose to graze another field, he shall make restitution from his own field according to its yield; and if he grazes the entire field, he shall restore the best of his own field and the best of his own vineyard.

**22:5** And if fire breaks out and catches thorns, and also burns up threshing floors, or ears of grain, or a field, the one who kindled the fire shall make restitution.

**22:6** And if anyone gives silver or articles to his neighbor to keep, and it is stolen from the man's house, if the one who stole it is found, he shall pay twofold;

**22:7** but if the one who stole it is not found, the master of the house shall come before God and shall swear that he surely has not acted wickedly regarding any part of his neighbor's deposit.

**22:8** In every stated case of wrongdoing—whether concerning a calf, or a beast of burden, or a sheep, or a garment, or any lost thing for which a claim is made, whatever it may be—the case of both parties shall come before God, and the one convicted through God shall pay twofold to his neighbor.

**22:9** And if anyone gives to his neighbor a beast of burden, or a calf, or a sheep, or any livestock to keep, and it is maimed, or dies, or is taken captive, and no one knows,

**22:10** an oath of God shall be between both of them, that he surely has not acted wickedly at all regarding his neighbor's deposit; and so its owner shall accept it, and he shall not make restitution.

**22:11** But if it is stolen from him, he shall make restitution to the owner;

**22:12** and if it is seized by a wild beast, he shall bring him to the prey, and he shall not make restitution.

**22:13** And if anyone borrows from his neighbor, and it is maimed, or dies, or is taken captive, while its owner is not with it, he shall make restitution;

**22:14** but if the owner is with it, he shall not make restitution; and if it is hired, it shall be to him in place of its hire.

**22:15** And if anyone seduces a virgin who is not betrothed and lies with her, he shall surely pay the bride-price for her to be his wife.

**22:16** But if her father utterly refuses and is not willing to give her to him as a wife, he shall pay silver to the father according to the amount of the bride-price of virgins.

**22:17** You shall not permit sorcerers to live.

**22:18** Everyone who lies with a beast, you shall surely put them to death.

**22:19** The one who sacrifices to gods, except to the Lord alone, shall surely be destroyed by death.

**22:20** And you shall not mistreat a sojourner, nor shall you oppress him; for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**22:21** You shall not mistreat any widow or orphan;

**22:22** and if you grievously mistreat them, and crying out they cry aloud to Me, I will surely hear their voice;

**22:23** and I will be enraged with wrath, and I will slay you with the sword, and your wives shall become widows and your children orphans.

**22:24** And if you lend silver to the poor brother who is with you, you shall not press hard upon him; you shall not lay interest upon him.

**22:25** And if you indeed take your neighbor's garment as a pledge, you shall return it to him before the setting of the sun;

**22:26** for this is his covering, this alone is the garment for his nakedness; in what shall he sleep? Therefore if he cries aloud to Me, I will hear him, for I am merciful.

**22:27** You shall not revile gods, nor speak evil of the rulers of your people.

**22:28** You shall not delay the firstfruits of your threshing floor and your winepress; the firstborn of your sons you shall give to Me.

**22:29** Likewise you shall do with your calf, and your sheep, and your beast of burden: seven days it shall be under its mother, and on the eighth day you shall render it to Me.

**22:30** And you shall be holy men to Me; and flesh seized by a wild beast you shall not eat; cast it to the dog.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Greek reads 'he shall die in return' (antapothaneitai), imposing capital liability on one who kills a thief after sunrise, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of making restitution.
- **22:4** — The Septuagint preserves a two-part distinction not found in the Masoretic Text: partial grazing requires restitution according to the field's yield, whereas grazing an entire field requires restitution from the best of one's field and vineyard.
- **22:12** — The Greek reads 'he shall bring him to the prey' (axei auton epi ten theran), referring to leading the owner to the torn carcass rather than bringing remains as evidence as in the Masoretic Text.
- **22:27** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew plural 'elohim' explicitly as plural 'gods' (theous) alongside plural 'rulers' (archontas), where other traditions render 'God' or 'judges.'
