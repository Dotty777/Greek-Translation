## Text

**26:1** And you shall make the tabernacle of ten curtains of twisted fine linen, and blue, and purple, and twisted scarlet; you shall make them with cherubim, the work of a weaver.

**26:2** The length of one curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of one curtain shall be four cubits; all the curtains shall have the same measure.

**26:3** Now five curtains shall be joined together, one to another, and five curtains shall be coupled one to another.

**26:4** And you shall make for them loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain on the one side at the joining; and thus you shall also do on the edge of the outermost curtain at the second joining.

**26:5** Fifty loops you shall make on the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the side of the curtain at the joining of the second, facing and answering one another, each to each.

**26:6** And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and join the curtains one to another with the clasps; and the tabernacle shall be one.

**26:7** And you shall make curtains of hair as a covering over the tabernacle; eleven curtains you shall make them.

**26:8** The length of one curtain shall be thirty cubits, and four cubits the width of one curtain; the eleven curtains shall have the same measure.

**26:9** And you shall join five curtains together, and six curtains together; and you shall fold over the sixth curtain at the front of the tabernacle.

**26:10** And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain that is in the middle at the joining, and fifty loops you shall make on the edge of the second joining curtain.

**26:11** And you shall make fifty clasps of bronze, and join the clasps into the loops, and join the curtains together, and it shall be one.

**26:12** And you shall place beneath the excess of the curtains of the tabernacle; you shall cover underneath at the rear of the tabernacle:

**26:13** a cubit on this side and a cubit on that side, of what overhangs of the curtains, of the length of the curtains of the tabernacle; it shall cover over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side, that it may cover it.

**26:14** And you shall make a covering for the tabernacle of rams' skins dyed red, and outer coverings of blue skins above them.

**26:15** And you shall make pillars for the tabernacle of incorruptible wood.

**26:16** Ten cubits you shall make one pillar, and one cubit and a half shall be the width of one pillar;

**26:17** two tenons for one pillar, set opposite one another; thus you shall do for all the pillars of the tabernacle.

**26:18** And you shall make pillars for the tabernacle, twenty pillars on the side toward the north.

**26:19** And forty bases of silver you shall make for the twenty pillars, two bases for one pillar on both sides of it.

**26:20** And for the second side, toward the south, twenty pillars,

**26:21** and their forty bases of silver, two bases for one pillar on both sides of it.

**26:22** And for the rear of the tabernacle, on the side toward the sea, you shall make six pillars.

**26:23** And two pillars you shall make at the corners of the tabernacle at the rear,

**26:24** and it shall be even from below; in the same way they shall be equal at their tops into one joining; thus you shall do for both of them, and they shall be for the two corners.

**26:25** And there shall be eight pillars, and their sixteen bases of silver: two bases for one pillar on both sides of it, and two bases for the next pillar.

**26:26** And you shall make bars of incorruptible wood: five for the one pillar on the one side of the tabernacle,

**26:27** and five bars for the pillar on the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the rear pillar on the side of the tabernacle toward the sea.

**26:28** And the middle bar in the midst of the pillars shall pass through from the one side to the other side.

**26:29** And you shall overlay the pillars with gold, and you shall make the rings of gold into which you shall insert the bars; and you shall overlay the bars with gold.

**26:30** And you shall raise up the tabernacle according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain.

**26:31** And you shall make a veil of blue, and purple, and twisted scarlet, and spun fine linen; of woven work you shall make it with cherubim.

**26:32** And you shall place it upon four pillars of incorruptible wood overlaid with gold; and their capitals shall be of gold, and their four bases of silver.

**26:33** And you shall set the veil upon the pillars, and you shall bring in there, within the veil, the ark of the testimony; and the veil shall divide for you between the Holy Place and the Holy of Holies.

**26:34** And you shall conceal with the veil the ark of the testimony in the Holy of Holies.

**26:35** And you shall place the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the side of the tabernacle toward the south; and the table you shall set on the side of the tabernacle toward the north.

**26:36** And you shall make a screen of blue, and purple, and twisted scarlet, and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer.

**26:37** And you shall make for the veil five pillars, and overlay them with gold; and their capitals shall be of gold; and you shall cast for them five bases of bronze.

## Translator Notes

- **26:12** — The Septuagint omits the Hebrew Masoretic Text's mention of the remaining half curtain, stating simply that the excess of the curtains is placed beneath to cover the rear of the tabernacle.
- **26:14** — The Septuagint renders the outermost covering as "blue skins" (dermata hyakinthina), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the debated term tachash (often translated as badger, sea cow, or fine leather).
- **26:15** — Throughout Exodus 26 and 27, the Septuagint renders Hebrew shittim (acacia) wood as "incorruptible wood" (xyla asepta) and refers to the tabernacle's vertical frames as "pillars" (styloi).
- **26:18** — The Septuagint reverses the north-south order relative to the Hebrew Masoretic Text, specifying the first twenty pillars on the north side (v. 18) and the second twenty on the south side (v. 20).
- **26:34** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text commands placing the mercy seat upon the ark, the Septuagint instructs that the ark of the testimony in the Holy of Holies be concealed with the veil.
