## Text

**14:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**14:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, that they turn back and encamp opposite the farmstead, between Migdol and the sea, over against Baal Zephon; before them you shall encamp by the sea,

**14:3** and Pharaoh will say to his people, 'These sons of Israel are wandering in the land; for the wilderness has shut them in.'

**14:4** And I will harden the heart of Pharaoh, and he shall pursue after them; and I shall be glorified in Pharaoh and in all his army, and all the Egyptians shall know that I am the Lord." And they did so.

**14:5** And it was reported to the king of the Egyptians that the people had fled; and the heart of Pharaoh and the heart of his servants turned against the people, and they said, "What is this we have done, to send away the sons of Israel from serving us?"

**14:6** Therefore Pharaoh harnessed his chariots, and took all his people along with him,

**14:7** taking six hundred chosen chariots, and all the cavalry of the Egyptians, and officers over all of them.

**14:8** And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt and of his servants, and he pursued after the sons of Israel; and the sons of Israel were going out with a high hand.

**14:9** And the Egyptians pursued after them, and found them encamped by the sea, all the cavalry and the chariots of Pharaoh, and his horsemen and his army, opposite the farmstead, over against Baal Zephon.

**14:10** And Pharaoh drew near; and the sons of Israel lifted up their eyes and saw, and the Egyptians had encamped behind them, and they were exceedingly afraid. And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord;

**14:11** and they said to Moses, "Because there were no graves in the land of Egypt, have you brought us out to put us to death in the wilderness? What is this you have done to us, bringing us out of Egypt?

**14:12** Was this not the word that we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, 'Leave us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it is better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in this wilderness."

**14:13** And Moses said to the people, "Take courage! Stand firm and see the salvation from God, which He will work for us today; for in the manner that you have seen the Egyptians today, you shall never again see them forevermore.

**14:14** The Lord shall fight for you, and you shall be silent."

**14:15** And the Lord said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to Me? Speak to the sons of Israel, and let them break camp;

**14:16** and you, lift up your rod, and stretch out your hand over the sea and divide it, and let the sons of Israel go into the midst of the sea on dry ground.

**14:17** And behold, I will harden the heart of Pharaoh and of all the Egyptians, and they shall go in after them; and I shall be glorified in Pharaoh and in all his army, and in his chariots and in his horses.

**14:18** And all the Egyptians shall know that I am the Lord, when I am glorified in Pharaoh and in his chariots and horses."

**14:19** And the angel of God, who went before the camp of the sons of Israel, moved and went behind them; and the pillar of cloud also moved from before them and stood behind them.

**14:20** And it entered between the Egyptians and the camp of Israel, and stood; and there came darkness and gloom, and the night passed, and they did not mingle with one another all the night.

**14:21** And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea; and the Lord drove back the sea with a strong south wind all the night, and made the sea dry land, and the water was divided.

**14:22** And the sons of Israel went into the midst of the sea on the dry ground, and the water was to them a wall on the right hand and a wall on the left.

**14:23** And the Egyptians pursued, and went in after them, even every horse of Pharaoh, and the chariots, and the riders, into the midst of the sea.

**14:24** And it came to pass in the morning watch that the Lord looked down upon the camp of the Egyptians in the pillar of fire and cloud, and threw the camp of the Egyptians into confusion,

**14:25** and bound fast the axles of their chariots, and drove them with difficulty. And the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from the face of Israel, for the Lord is fighting for them against the Egyptians."

**14:26** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the water may be restored and cover over the Egyptians, both upon the chariots and the riders."

**14:27** And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the water was restored toward daybreak to its place; and the Egyptians fled beneath the water, and the Lord shook off the Egyptians in the midst of the sea.

**14:28** And the returning water covered the chariots and the riders and all the host of Pharaoh that had gone in after them into the sea; and not even one of them was left.

**14:29** But the sons of Israel walked through dry land in the midst of the sea, and the water was to them a wall on the right hand and a wall on the left.

**14:30** And the Lord delivered Israel in that day from the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead upon the shore of the sea.

**14:31** And Israel saw the mighty hand, the things which the Lord had done to the Egyptians; and the people feared the Lord, and believed in God and in Moses His servant.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Pi-hahiroth as a common noun, "the farmstead" (tēs epauleōs), here and in verse nine.
- **14:10** — The Greek states that the Egyptians "had encamped" (estratopedeusan) behind Israel, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes them marching after Israel.
- **14:20** — The Greek reads "there came darkness and gloom, and the night passed," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which states that the cloud gave light to the night.
- **14:21** — The Greek specifies a "south wind" (anemō notō), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies an "east wind."
- **14:25** — The Greek reads that the Lord "bound fast the axles of their chariots" (synedēsen tous axonas), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of removing or clogging their chariot wheels.
