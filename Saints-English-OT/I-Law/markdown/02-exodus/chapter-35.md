## Text

**35:1** And Moses assembled the entire congregation of the sons of Israel and said, "These are the words that the Lord has spoken, to do them:

**35:2** Six days you shall do works, but on the seventh day there shall be rest, a holy thing, a Sabbath of rest to the Lord; everyone who does work on it shall die.

**35:3** You shall not kindle a fire in any dwelling place of yours on the Sabbath day; I am the Lord."

**35:4** And Moses spoke to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the word that the Lord has commanded, saying,

**35:5** 'Take from among yourselves an offering for the Lord; everyone who is willing in heart shall bring the firstfruits to the Lord: gold, silver, bronze,

**35:6** blue, purple, twice-dyed spun scarlet, twisted fine linen, and goat hair,

**35:7** and ram skins dyed red, and blue-dyed skins, and incorruptible wood,

**35:9** and sardius stones, and stones for engraving for the ephod and the full-length robe.

**35:10** And let every wise-hearted person among you come and make all things that the Lord has commanded:

**35:11** the tabernacle, and the coverings, and the braces, and the bars, and the pillars,

**35:12** and the ark of the testimony, and its poles, and its mercy seat, and the veil, and the curtains of the court, and its pillars, and the emerald stones, and the incense, and the anointing oil,

**35:13** and the table and all its vessels,

**35:14** and the lampstand of the light and all its vessels,

**35:16** and the altar and all its vessels,

**35:19** and the holy garments of Aaron the priest, and the garments in which they shall minister, and the tunics of the priesthood for the sons of Aaron, and the anointing oil, and the compounded incense.'"

**35:20** And the entire congregation of the sons of Israel departed from Moses.

**35:21** And each one whose heart moved him, and as many as it pleased in their soul, brought up an offering; and they brought an offering to the Lord for all the works of the tabernacle of the testimony, and for all its workmanship, and for all the garments of the sanctuary.

**35:22** And the men brought from the women—everyone to whose mind it seemed good brought signets, and earrings, and rings, and hair ornaments, and armlets, every article of gold;

**35:23** even all those who brought offerings of gold to the Lord. And everyone with whom was found fine linen, and blue-dyed skins, and ram skins dyed red, brought them.

**35:24** And everyone who set apart an offering brought silver and bronze, the offerings to the Lord; and those with whom incorruptible wood was found brought it also for all the works of the construction.

**35:25** And every woman wise in mind to spin with her hands brought spun yarn: the blue, and the purple, and the scarlet, and the fine linen.

**35:26** And all the women to whose mind it seemed good in wisdom spun the goat hair.

**35:27** And the rulers brought the emerald stones and the stones for setting for the ephod and the oracle,

**35:28** and the spice mixtures, and the oil of anointing, and the composition of the incense.

**35:29** And every man and woman whose mind moved them to come in to do all the works that the Lord had commanded to be done through Moses—the sons of Israel brought an offering to the Lord.

**35:30** And Moses said to the sons of Israel, "Behold, God has called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**35:31** and has filled him with a spirit of wisdom and understanding and knowledge of all things,

**35:32** to act as master craftsman in all the works of craftsmanship, to work the gold and the silver and the bronze,

**35:33** and to carve the stone, and to work the wood, and to labor in every work of wisdom;

**35:34** and He has also put it in his mind to instruct, both him and Eliab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan;

**35:35** He has filled them with wisdom and understanding of mind, to understand how to do all the works of the sanctuary, and to weave the woven and embroidered works with the scarlet and the fine linen, to perform every work of artistic craftsmanship."

## Translator Notes

- **35:7** — The Septuagint consistently renders the Hebrew coverings and acacia wood as "blue-dyed skins" and "incorruptible wood."
- **35:9** — The Septuagint's shorter tabernacle summary omits verses 8, 15, 17, and 18 of the Masoretic numbering, and here reads "sardius stones," "stones for engraving," and "the full-length robe."
- **35:22** — The Greek reads "the men brought from the women," differing from the Hebrew phrasing "men as well as women."
- **35:27** — The Greek word rendered "oracle" is the Septuagint's standard term for the high priest's breastpiece of judgment.
- **35:34** — The Septuagint preserves the distinct proper name "Eliab" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Oholiab."
