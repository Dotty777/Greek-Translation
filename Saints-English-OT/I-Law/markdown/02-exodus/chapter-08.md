## Text

**8:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron your brother, 'Stretch out your staff with your hand over the rivers and over the canals and over the marshes, and bring up the frogs.'"

**8:2** And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt and brought up the frogs; and the frog was brought up and covered the land of Egypt.

**8:3** Then the enchanters of the Egyptians also did likewise with their sorceries, and brought up the frogs upon the land of Egypt.

**8:4** And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said, "Pray for me to the Lord, and let Him take away the frogs from me and from my people, and I will send them away, and they shall sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:5** And Moses said to Pharaoh, "Appoint for me when I should pray for you and for your servants and for your people, to destroy the frogs from you and from your people and from your houses; only in the river shall they remain."

**8:6** And he said, "For tomorrow." So he said, "As you have spoken, that you may see that there is no other besides the Lord;

**8:7** and the frogs shall be taken away from you and from your houses and from the courtyards and from your servants and from your people; only in the river shall they remain."

**8:8** Then Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh; and Moses cried out to the Lord concerning the appointed limit for the frogs, as Pharaoh had arranged.

**8:9** And the Lord did just as Moses had said, and the frogs died out of the houses and out of the courtyards and out of the fields.

**8:10** And they gathered them in heaps upon heaps, and the land stank.

**8:11** But when Pharaoh saw that there was relief, his heart became heavy, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had spoken.

**8:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff with your hand and strike the dust of the earth, and there shall be gnats both on men and on four-footed beasts and in all the land of Egypt.'"

**8:13** So Aaron stretched out the staff with his hand and struck the dust of the earth, and the gnats came upon men and upon four-footed beasts; and in all the dust of the earth there were gnats.

**8:14** Then the enchanters also did likewise with their sorceries to bring forth the gnat, and they could not; and the gnats were on men and on four-footed beasts.

**8:15** So the enchanters said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." Yet the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had spoken.

**8:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh; and behold, he will come out to the water, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord: Send away My people, that they may serve Me in the wilderness;

**8:17** but if you are not willing to send away My people, behold, I am sending upon you and upon your servants and upon your people and upon your houses the dog-fly; and the houses of the Egyptians shall be filled with the dog-fly, and also the land upon which they are.

**8:18** And I will wonderfully distinguish in that day the land of Goshen, upon which My people are, where the dog-fly shall not be, that you may know that I am the Lord, the Lord of all the earth.

**8:19** And I will set a distinction between My people and your people; and tomorrow this shall be upon the land.'"

**8:20** And the Lord did so, and the dog-fly came in a multitude into the houses of Pharaoh and into the houses of his servants and into all the land of Egypt; and the land was ruined by the dog-fly.

**8:21** Then Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, saying, "Go, sacrifice to your God in the land."

**8:22** And Moses said, "It is not possible for this matter to be done thus, for we shall sacrifice the abominations of the Egyptians to the Lord our God; for if we sacrifice the abominations of the Egyptians before them, we shall be stoned.

**8:23** We will go a journey of three days into the wilderness, and we will sacrifice to our God just as the Lord has said to us."

**8:24** And Pharaoh said, "I will send you away, and sacrifice to your God in the wilderness; only you shall not extend your journey far away. Pray therefore for me to the Lord."

**8:25** And Moses said, "Behold, I will go out from you and pray to God, and the dog-fly shall depart from you and from your servants and your people tomorrow; do not continue any longer, Pharaoh, to deceive, so as not to send away the people to sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:26** Then Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to God.

**8:27** And the Lord did just as Moses had said, and He took away the dog-fly from Pharaoh and from his servants and from his people, and not even one was left.

**8:28** And Pharaoh made his heart heavy at this time also, and he was not willing to send away the people.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek preserves both the plural "brought up the frogs" and a collective singular passive clause "and the frog was brought up," a distinctive Septuagint reading.
- **8:11** — Greek uses the verb barynō ("become heavy" / "make heavy") here and in 8:28, 9:7, and 9:34, distinct from sklērynō ("harden") in 8:15, 9:12, and 9:35; here the Septuagint uses the passive "his heart became heavy."
- **8:17** — Greek kynomyia ("dog-fly") is the Septuagint's specific rendering of the fourth plague as a biting fly, used as a collective singular throughout verses 17–27.
- **8:18** — "Goshen" renders the Greek form Gesem; the Septuagint also concludes with the distinctive declaration "the Lord, the Lord of all the earth."
