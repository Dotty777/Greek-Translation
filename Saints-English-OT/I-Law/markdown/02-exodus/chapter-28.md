## Text

**28:1** And you, bring near to yourself both Aaron your brother and his sons from among the sons of Israel, to minister as priests to Me—Aaron, and Nadab and Abihu and Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron.

**28:2** And you shall make a holy vestment for Aaron your brother, for honor and glory.

**28:3** And you, speak to all those who are wise in understanding, whom I have filled with a spirit of perception, and they shall make the holy vestment of Aaron for the sanctuary, in which he shall minister as priest to Me.

**28:4** And these are the vestments which they shall make: the breastpiece, and the ephod, and the full-length tasseled tunic, and a turban, and a sash. And they shall make holy vestments for Aaron and for his sons, to minister as priests to Me.

**28:5** And they shall take the gold, and the blue, and the purple, and the scarlet, and the fine linen.

**28:6** And they shall make the ephod of twisted fine linen, the work of an embroidering weaver.

**28:7** It shall have two shoulder-pieces joining one to the other, fitted upon the two sides.

**28:8** And the woven band of the shoulder-pieces, which is upon it, shall be of the same workmanship and of one piece with it, of gold, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

**28:9** And you shall take the two stones, emerald stones, and you shall engrave upon them the names of the sons of Israel:

**28:10** six names upon the one stone, and the remaining six names upon the second stone, according to their births.

**28:11** As the work of a stonecutter's craft, like the engraving of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones upon

**28:12** the shoulders of the ephod. They are stones of memorial for the sons of Israel; and Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel before the Lord upon his two shoulders, as a memorial concerning them.

**28:13** And you shall make small shields of pure gold.

**28:14** And you shall make two tassels of pure gold, intermingled with flowers, of braided work; and you shall place the braided tassels upon the small shields along their shoulder-pieces on the front.

**28:15** And you shall make an oracle of judgments, the work of an embroiderer; according to the pattern of the ephod you shall make it; of gold, and blue, and purple, and twisted scarlet, and twisted fine linen

**28:16** you shall make it. It shall be square, doubled; a span shall be its length, and a span its breadth.

**28:17** And you shall weave into it a stone-set fabric of four rows. There shall be a row of stones: sardius, topaz, and emerald shall be the first row;

**28:18** and the second row: carbuncle, and sapphire, and jasper;

**28:19** and the third row: ligure, agate, and amethyst;

**28:20** and the fourth row: chrysolite, and beryl, and onyx. They shall be encased in gold, bound together in gold, according to their row.

**28:21** And the stones shall be from the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, according to their names; like engravings of signets, each according to the name, they shall be for twelve tribes.

**28:22** And you shall make upon the oracle interwoven tassels, chainwork of pure gold.

**28:29** And Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel upon the oracle of judgment on his breast when he enters into the sanctuary, as a memorial before God. And you shall place the tassels upon the oracle of judgment; the chainwork you shall set upon both sides of the oracle, and the two small shields you shall set upon both shoulders of the ephod on the front.

**28:30** And you shall place upon the oracle of judgment the Manifestation and the Truth; and it shall be upon the breast of Aaron whenever he enters into the sanctuary before the Lord. And Aaron shall bear the judgments of the sons of Israel upon his breast before the Lord continually.

**28:31** And you shall make a full-length under-robe wholly of blue.

**28:32** And the neck opening shall be in the middle of it, having a border all around the opening, the work of a weaver, with the seam woven together out of it, lest it be torn.

**28:33** And beneath the hem of the under-robe at the bottom you shall make small pomegranates as of a blossoming pomegranate, of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen, upon the hem of the under-robe all around; of the same form, small golden pomegranates, and bells between them all around:

**28:34** beside a small golden pomegranate, a bell and a blossom, upon the hem of the under-robe all around.

**28:35** And while Aaron ministers, its sound shall be heard when he enters into the sanctuary before the Lord and when he goes out, lest he die.

**28:36** And you shall make a plate of pure gold, and you shall engrave upon it, like the engraving of a signet: "Holiness of the Lord."

**28:37** And you shall place it upon twisted blue, and it shall be upon the miter; upon the front of the miter it shall be.

**28:38** And it shall be upon the forehead of Aaron, and Aaron shall take away the sins of the holy things, whatever the sons of Israel may consecrate in every gift of their holy things; and it shall be upon the forehead of Aaron continually, acceptable for them before the Lord.

**28:39** And the tassels of the tunics shall be of fine linen; and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and a sash you shall make, the work of an embroiderer.

**28:40** And for the sons of Aaron you shall make tunics and sashes, and turbans you shall make for them, for honor and glory.

**28:41** And you shall clothe Aaron your brother with them, and his sons with him; and you shall anoint them, and fill their hands, and sanctify them, that they may minister as priests to Me.

**28:42** And you shall make for them linen breeches to cover the nakedness of their flesh; from the loins to the thighs they shall reach.

**28:43** And Aaron and his sons shall wear them whenever they enter into the tabernacle of testimony, when they approach to minister at the altar of the sanctuary; and they shall not bring sin upon themselves, lest they die. It shall be an everlasting ordinance for him and for his seed after him.

## Translator Notes

- **28:11** — In the Greek text, the clause bridges verses 11 and 12 without the additional gold-setting instructions found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **28:15** — Greek logion ('oracle') presents the breastpiece as an instrument of divine revelation; the sentence continues across the verse boundary into verse 16.
- **28:29** — The Greek text omits verses 23 through 28 of the Hebrew numbering and summarizes the attachment of the chainwork and small shields at the end of verse 29.
- **28:30** — Greek 'the Manifestation and the Truth' (delosis and aletheia) interprets the Hebrew Urim and Thummim conceptually rather than transliterating them.
- **28:36** — Greek hagiasma Kyriou uses the genitive ('Holiness of the Lord'), corresponding to 'Holiness to the Lord' in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
