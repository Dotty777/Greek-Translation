## Text

**18:1** Now Jethro, the priest of Midian, the father-in-law of Moses, heard all that the Lord had done for Israel His own people, for the Lord had brought Israel out of Egypt.

**18:2** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took Zipporah, the wife of Moses, after her dismissal,

**18:3** and her two sons, the name of one of them being Gershom, saying, "I was a sojourner in a foreign land";

**18:4** and the name of the second was Eliezer, saying, "For the God of my father is my helper, and He delivered me out of the hand of Pharaoh."

**18:5** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, and the sons and the wife came out to Moses into the wilderness, where he encamped at the mountain of God.

**18:6** And it was reported to Moses, saying, "Behold, your father-in-law Jethro is coming to you, and your wife and your two sons with him."

**18:7** So Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, and bowed down before him and kissed him; and they greeted one another, and he brought him into the tent.

**18:8** And Moses recounted to his father-in-law all that the Lord had done to Pharaoh and to all the Egyptians for the sake of Israel, and all the hardship that had come upon them along the way, and how the Lord had delivered them out of the hand of Pharaoh and out of the hand of the Egyptians.

**18:9** And Jethro was amazed at all the good things which the Lord had done for them, in that He had delivered them out of the hand of the Egyptians and out of the hand of Pharaoh.

**18:10** And Jethro said, "Blessed be the Lord, because He has delivered them out of the hand of the Egyptians and out of the hand of Pharaoh.

**18:11** Now I know that the Lord is great above all the gods, on account of this, that they set upon them."

**18:12** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took whole burnt offerings and sacrifices for God; and Aaron and all the elders of Israel came to eat bread together with the father-in-law of Moses before God.

**18:13** And it came to pass after the next day that Moses sat down to judge the people; and all the people stood before Moses from morning until evening.

**18:14** And when Jethro saw all that he was doing for the people, he said, "What is this that you are doing for the people? Why do you sit alone, while all the people stand before you from morning until evening?"

**18:15** And Moses said to his father-in-law, "Because the people come to me to seek judgment from God.

**18:16** For whenever a dispute arises among them and they come to me, I judge each one, and I instruct them in the ordinances of God and His law."

**18:17** And the father-in-law of Moses said to him, "You are not doing this thing rightly;

**18:18** you will surely waste away with unendurable exhaustion, both you and all the people who are with you; this matter is too heavy for you, and you will not be able to perform it alone.

**18:19** Now therefore listen to me, and I will give you counsel, and God shall be with you. Stand for the people in the things pertaining to God, and you shall bring their matters before God;

**18:20** and you shall solemnly testify to them the ordinances of God and His law, and you shall show them the ways in which they shall walk, and the works which they shall do.

**18:21** And seek out for yourself from all the people capable men, fearing God, righteous men, hating arrogance, and you shall appoint over them rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, and rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens;

**18:22** and they shall judge the people at all times; the matter that is too great they shall bring to you, but the small judgments they themselves shall judge; and they shall lighten the burden from you and help bear it with you.

**18:23** If you do this thing, God shall strengthen you, and you will be able to endure, and all this people shall come to their own place in peace."

**18:24** So Moses heeded the voice of his father-in-law, and did all that he had said to him.

**18:25** And Moses chose capable men out of all Israel, and made them over them rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, and rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens;

**18:26** and they judged the people at all times; every weighty matter they brought to Moses, but every light matter they themselves judged.

**18:27** Then Moses sent his father-in-law on his way, and he departed into his own land.

## Translator Notes

- **18:9** — The Septuagint reads "was amazed" (exeste), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "rejoiced."
- **18:13** — The Greek phrase meta ten epaurion literally means "after the next day," a hyper-literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for "on the next day."
- **18:21** — The Septuagint specifies "righteous men, hating arrogance" (andras dikaious, misountas hyperephanian), whereas the Masoretic Text has "men of truth, hating dishonest gain."
- **18:23** — The Septuagint reads "God shall strengthen you" (katischysei se ho theos), where the Masoretic Text reads "and God so commands you."
