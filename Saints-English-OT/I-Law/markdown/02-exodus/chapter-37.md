## Text

**37:1** And they made ten curtains for the tabernacle;

**37:2** the length of one curtain was twenty-eight cubits—they were all the same—and the width of one curtain was four cubits.

**37:3** And he made the veil of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine twisted linen, the work of a weaver, with cherubim;

**37:4** and they placed it upon four incorruptible pillars overlaid with gold; and their capitals were of gold, and their four bases were of silver.

**37:5** And they made the veil of the door of the tabernacle of the testimony of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine twisted linen, woven work of cherubim,

**37:6** and their five pillars and the rings; and they overlaid their capitals with gold, and the five bases were of bronze.

**37:7** And they made the court: on the side toward the southwest, hangings of the court of fine twisted linen, one hundred by one hundred;

**37:8** and their pillars were twenty, and their bases were twenty.

**37:9** And the side toward the north was one hundred by one hundred, and the side toward the south was one hundred by one hundred; and their pillars were twenty, and their bases were twenty.

**37:10** And on the side toward the sea were curtains of fifty cubits; their pillars were ten, and their bases were ten.

**37:11** And the side toward the east was fifty cubits, the part along the flank;

**37:12** and their pillars were three, and their bases were three.

**37:13** And upon the second flank, on this side and on that side by the gate of the court, were curtains of one hundred and fifty cubits; their pillars were three, and their bases were three.

**37:14** All the curtains of the tabernacle were of fine twisted linen;

**37:15** and the bases of their pillars were of bronze, and their hooks were of silver, and their capitals were overlaid with silver, and the pillars were overlaid with silver, all the pillars of the court.

**37:16** And the veil of the gate of the court was the work of an embroiderer, of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine twisted linen; twenty cubits was the length, and the height and the width were five cubits, matching the hangings of the court;

**37:17** and their pillars were four, and their four bases were of bronze, and their hooks were of silver, and their capitals were overlaid with silver.

**37:18** And they themselves were overlaid with silver, and all the pegs of the court all around were of bronze.

**37:19** And this is the arrangement of the tabernacle of the testimony, just as it was commanded to Moses, that the ministry should be that of the Levites through Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

**37:20** And Bezalel, the son of Uri, of the tribe of Judah, did just as the Lord commanded Moses,

**37:21** and Eliab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, who was the master craftsman of the woven works, and the needlework, and the embroidered works, to weave with the scarlet and with the fine linen.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — In the Septuagint arrangement, chapter 37 recounts the tabernacle curtains, veils, and courtyard (corresponding to parts of Hebrew Exodus 36 and 38), while the ark and furnishings follow in chapter 38.
- **37:4** — Greek aseptos literally means "incorruptible" or "imperishable," the Septuagint's standard designation for acacia wood.
- **37:7** — The Septuagint states the court's measurement as "one hundred by one hundred" (here and in verse 9) and names both the southwest (lips) and the south (notos) in addition to the north, sea (west), and east.
- **37:13** — The Greek text reads "one hundred and fifty cubits," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "fifteen cubits."
