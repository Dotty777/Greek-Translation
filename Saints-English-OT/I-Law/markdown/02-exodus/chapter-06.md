## Text

**6:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh; for by a mighty hand he will send them away, and by an uplifted arm he will cast them out from his land."

**6:2** Now God spoke to Moses and said to him, "I am the Lord.

**6:3** And I appeared to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, being their God, yet My name, the Lord, I did not make known to them.

**6:4** And I established My covenant with them, so as to give to them the land of the Canaanites, the land in which they have sojourned, upon which they also sojourned.

**6:5** And I have heard the groaning of the sons of Israel, whom the Egyptians hold in bondage, and I have remembered your covenant.

**6:6** Go, speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'I am the Lord, and I will bring you out from the dominion of the Egyptians, and I will deliver you from bondage, and I will redeem you with an uplifted arm and with great judgment.

**6:7** And I will take you to Myself as a people for Me, and I will be your God; and you shall know that I am the Lord your God, who brought you out from the oppression of the Egyptians.

**6:8** And I will lead you forth into the land toward which I stretched out My hand to give it to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, and I will give it to you as an inheritance: I am the Lord.'"

**6:9** So Moses spoke thus to the sons of Israel; yet they did not listen to Moses, because of their faintness of spirit and because of the harsh labors.

**6:10** Then the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:11** "Go in, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he send away the sons of Israel out of his land."

**6:12** But Moses spoke before the Lord, saying, "Behold, the sons of Israel have not listened to me, and how will Pharaoh listen to me? For I am lacking in speech."

**6:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, and gave them a charge to Pharaoh king of Egypt, so as to send forth the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt.

**6:14** And these are the heads of their fathers' houses. The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi; this is the kindred of Reuben.

**6:15** And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar, and Shaul, the son of the Phoenician woman; these are the paternal families of the sons of Simeon.

**6:16** And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their kindreds: Gershon and Kohath and Merari; and the years of the life of Levi were one hundred and thirty-seven.

**6:17** And these are the sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, the houses of their paternal family.

**6:18** And the sons of Kohath: Amram and Issachar, Hebron and Uzziel; and the years of the life of Kohath were one hundred and thirty years.

**6:19** And the sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the houses of the paternal families of Levi according to their kindred.

**6:20** And Amram took Jochebed, the daughter of his father's brother, to himself as a wife, and she bore to him Aaron and Moses and Miriam their sister; and the years of the life of Amram were one hundred and thirty-two years.

**6:21** And the sons of Izhar: Korah and Nepheg and Zichri.

**6:22** And the sons of Uzziel: Elzaphan and Sithri.

**6:23** Now Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, sister of Nahshon, to himself as a wife, and she bore to him Nadab and Abihu and Eleazar and Ithamar.

**6:24** And the sons of Korah: Assir and Elkanah and Abiasaph; these are the generations of Korah.

**6:25** And Eleazar, the son of Aaron, took one of the daughters of Putiel to himself as a wife, and she bore to him Phinehas; these are the heads of the paternal house of the Levites according to their generations.

**6:26** This is that Aaron and Moses to whom God said to bring out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt with their host.

**6:27** These are the ones who spoke with Pharaoh king of Egypt; and they brought out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt—the same Aaron and Moses.

**6:28** On the day when the Lord spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

**6:29** then the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the Lord; speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I speak to you."

**6:30** And Moses said before the Lord, "Behold, I am faltering of speech, and how will Pharaoh listen to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Septuagint reads "being their God" here rather than the Masoretic "as God Almighty."
- **6:12** — The Greek renders the Hebrew idiom "uncircumcised of lips" interpretively as "lacking in speech" (alogos) here and "faltering of speech" (ischnophonos) in 6:30.
- **6:15** — The Septuagint identifies Shaul's mother as "the Phoenician woman" rather than the Masoretic "Canaanite woman."
- **6:18** — The Septuagint lists Kohath's second son as Issachar here (compare Izhar in 6:21) and gives Kohath's lifespan as one hundred and thirty years (Masoretic Text: one hundred and thirty-three).
- **6:20** — The Septuagint identifies Jochebed as Amram's cousin ("the daughter of his father's brother" rather than his aunt), includes Miriam among her children, and records Amram's lifespan as one hundred and thirty-two years (Masoretic Text: one hundred and thirty-seven).
