## Text

**11:1** And you shall love the Lord your God, and keep His charges, and His statutes, and His judgments all the days.

**11:2** And you shall know today that it is not your children, who have neither known nor seen the discipline of the Lord your God, and His great deeds, and His mighty hand, and His uplifted arm,

**11:3** and His signs and His wonders which He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land,

**11:4** and what He did to the army of the Egyptians, their chariots and their horses and their force, how He flooded the water of the Red Sea over their face as they pursued after you, and the Lord destroyed them even to this day,

**11:5** and what He did for us in the wilderness until you came to this place,

**11:6** and what He did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab the son of Reuben, whom the earth opened its mouth and swallowed up, together with their households and their tents and all their substance that was with them, in the midst of all Israel;

**11:7** for your eyes have seen all the works of the Lord which He has done for you today.

**11:8** And you shall keep all His commandments which I command you today, that you may live and be multiplied, and go in and inherit the land into which you are crossing over the Jordan to inherit it;

**11:9** that you may prolong your days upon the land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed after them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**11:10** For the land into which you are entering to inherit it is not like the land of Egypt, from which you came out, when they sow the seed and water it with their feet like a garden of herbs;

**11:11** but the land into which you are entering to inherit it is a land of mountains and plains; it shall drink water from the rain of heaven;

**11:12** a land which the Lord your God watches over continually; the eyes of the Lord your God are upon it from the beginning of the year even to the end of the year.

**11:13** And if you diligently heed all the commandments which I command you today, to love the Lord your God and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,

**11:14** then He will give the rain for your land in its season, both the early and the latter rain, and you shall gather in your grain, and your wine, and your oil;

**11:15** and He will give grass in your fields for your livestock. And when you have eaten and are satisfied,

**11:16** take heed to yourself, lest your heart be enlarged, and you transgress and serve other gods and worship them;

**11:17** and the Lord be provoked to wrath against you and shut up the heaven, so that there is no rain and the earth does not yield its fruit, and you perish swiftly from the good land which the Lord has given you.

**11:18** And you shall lay these words in your heart and in your soul, and you shall bind them as a sign upon your hand, and it shall be unmoved before your eyes;

**11:19** and you shall teach them to your children, speaking of them when you sit in your house and when you walk by the way, when you lie down and when you rise up;

**11:20** and you shall write them upon the doorposts of your houses and upon your gates,

**11:21** that you may prolong your days, and the days of your sons, upon the land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them, as the days of heaven upon the earth.

**11:22** And it shall come to pass, if you diligently heed all these commandments which I command you today to do them—to love the Lord our God, and to walk in all His ways, and to cleave to Him—

**11:23** then the Lord will cast out all these nations from before your face, and you shall inherit nations greater and stronger than yourselves.

**11:24** Every place where the sole of your foot shall tread shall be yours: from the wilderness and Antilebanon, and from the great river, the river Euphrates, even to the western sea shall your borders be.

**11:25** No man shall stand before your face; the Lord your God shall put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the face of all the land on which you tread, just as He spoke to you.

**11:26** Behold, I set before you today the blessing and the curse:

**11:27** the blessing, if you heed the commandments of the Lord your God which I command you today;

**11:28** and the curses, if you do not heed the commandments of the Lord your God, all that I command you today, and wander astray from the way which I have commanded you, going to serve other gods whom you do not know.

**11:29** And it shall be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land into which you are crossing over to inherit it, that you shall put a blessing upon Mount Gerizim and the curse upon Mount Ebal.

**11:30** Behold, are they not beyond the Jordan, behind the way of the setting sun, in the land of Canaan, which dwells toward the west, beside Gilgal, near the lofty oak?

**11:31** For you are crossing over the Jordan, going in to inherit the land which the Lord your God is giving you as an inheritance all the days, and you shall dwell in it.

**11:32** And you shall be careful to do all His ordinances and these judgments which I set before you today.

## Translator Notes

- **11:16** — "be enlarged" translates Greek platynomai ("widen" or "enlarge"), where the Septuagint reads the underlying Hebrew verb as widening rather than being enticed or deceived.
- **11:18** — "unmoved" renders Greek asaleuton ("immovable" or "unshakable"), the Septuagint's distinctive rendering of the Hebrew term traditionally translated "frontlets."
- **11:24** — "Antilebanon" preserves the Septuagint's distinct proper name (Antilibanou), referring to the eastern mountain range, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Lebanon."
- **11:30** — "near the lofty oak" reflects the Septuagint's plesion tes dryos tes hypseles, rendering Hebrew "oaks of Moreh" descriptively; the Greek also transliterates Gilgal as Golgol and locates Canaan "toward the west."
