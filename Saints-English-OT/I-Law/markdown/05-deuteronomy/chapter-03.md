## Text

**3:1** Then we turned and went up by the way to Bashan; and Gog king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.

**3:2** And the Lord said to me, "Do not fear him, for I have delivered him and all his people and all his land into your hands; and you shall do to him just as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon."

**3:3** So the Lord our God delivered him into our hands, even Og king of Bashan and all his people; and we struck him down until we left no seed of his.

**3:4** And we captured all his cities at that time; there was not a city that we did not take from them—sixty cities, all the adjoining regions of Argob, belonging to King Og in Bashan;

**3:5** all fortified cities, with high walls, gates, and bars, besides the cities of the Perizzites, which were exceedingly many.

**3:6** We utterly destroyed them, just as we did to Sihon king of Heshbon, and we utterly destroyed every city in turn, and the women and the little children,

**3:7** and all the livestock; and the spoil of the cities we took as plunder for ourselves.

**3:8** And at that time we took the land from the hands of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the brook Arnon even as far as Hermon—

**3:9** the Phoenicians call Hermon Sanior, and the Amorites called it Senir—

**3:10** all the cities of Misor, and all Gilead, and all Bashan as far as Elcha and Edrei, royal cities of Og in Bashan.

**3:11** For only Og king of Bashan was left of the Rephaim. Behold, his bed was a bed of iron; behold, it is in the citadel of the sons of Ammon; nine cubits is its length and four cubits its width, according to the cubit of a man.

**3:12** And that land we took as plunder at that time, from Aroer, which is on the bank of the brook Arnon, and half of Mount Gilead; and its cities I gave to Reuben and to Gad.

**3:13** And the rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Gog, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh, even all the region of Argob; all that land of Bashan shall be reckoned a land of Rephaim.

**3:14** And Jair the son of Manasseh took all the region of Arbok as far as the borders of Gartasi and Homachathi; he named them after his own name—Basemath, Havvoth Jair—until this day.

**3:15** And to Machir I gave Gilead.

**3:16** And to Reuben and to Gad I have given from Gilead as far as the brook Arnon, and as far as the Jabbok—the brook being the border for the sons of Ammon—

**3:17** and the Arabah and the Jordan, the border of Machanareth, even as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, below Ashdoth Pisgah on the east.

**3:18** And I commanded you at that time, saying, "The Lord our God has given you this land as an inheritance; armed, you shall go before the face of your brethren, the sons of Israel, every mighty man.

**3:19** Only your wives and your children and your livestock—I know that you have much livestock—let them dwell in your cities which I have given you, until the Lord

**3:20** your God gives rest to your brethren as well as to you, and they also inherit the land which the Lord our God is giving to them beyond the Jordan; and you shall return, each man to his inheritance which I have given you."

**3:21** And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, "Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God has done to these two kings; thus the Lord our God shall do to all the kingdoms to which you are crossing over there;

**3:22** you shall not fear, for the Lord our God Himself fights for you."

**3:23** And I pleaded before the Lord at that time, saying,

**3:24** "O Lord God, You have begun to show Your servant Your strength and Your power, and Your mighty hand and Your uplifted arm; for what god is there in heaven or on earth who will do according to what You have done and according to Your strength?

**3:25** Let me cross over therefore and see this good land that is beyond the Jordan, the good mountain and Antilebanon."

**3:26** Yet the Lord disregarded me on account of you, and did not listen to me; and the Lord said to me, "Let it be enough for you; do not speak this word anymore.

**3:27** Go up to the summit of the Hewn Peak, and having lifted up your eyes toward the sea and the north and the south and the east, look with your eyes; for you shall not cross over this Jordan.

**3:28** And command Joshua, and strengthen him and encourage him; for he shall cross over before the face of this people, and he himself shall cause them to inherit the land which you have seen."

**3:29** And we dwelt in the valley near the house of Peor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — This Greek text reads "Gog" here and in 3:13, while reading "Og" in 3:3, 3:4, 3:10, and 3:11.
- **3:5** — The Greek renders the Hebrew expression for "unwalled villages" as the ethnic name "the Perizzites."
- **3:9** — The Greek reads "the Phoenicians" and "Sanior" where the Hebrew reads "the Sidonians" and "Sirion."
- **3:14** — This Greek text preserves the distinct names "Arbok" (for "Argob"), "Gartasi" and "Homachathi" (for "Geshurites" and "Maachathites"), and "Basemath" (for "Bashan"); similarly, 3:10 transliterates "Misor" (Hebrew "the plain") and 3:17 reads "Machanareth" (Hebrew "from Chinnereth").
- **3:27** — "The Hewn Peak" translates the Greek epithet rendering Hebrew "Pisgah"; likewise, 3:25 specifies "Antilebanon" where the Hebrew reads "Lebanon," and 3:26 reads "disregarded" where the Hebrew reads "was angry with."
