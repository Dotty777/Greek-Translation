## Text

**18:1** The priests, the Levites—the whole tribe of Levi—shall have no portion nor inheritance with Israel; the offerings by fire of the Lord are their inheritance, and they shall eat them.

**18:2** And they shall have no inheritance among their brethren; the Lord Himself is his inheritance, as He spoke to him.

**18:3** And this shall be the due of the priests—what comes from the people, from those who offer sacrifices, whether a calf or a sheep: he shall give to the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the stomach.

**18:4** And the firstfruits of your grain and your wine and your oil, and the firstfruits of the shearings of your sheep, you shall give to him.

**18:5** For the Lord has chosen him out of all your tribes to stand before the Lord God, to minister and to bless in His name, he and his sons among the sons of Israel.

**18:6** Now if a Levite comes from one of the cities out of all the sons of Israel where he sojourns, according as his soul desires, to the place that He chooses,

**18:7** he shall minister in the name of the Lord his God, just like all his brethren the Levites who stand there before the Lord your God.

**18:8** He shall eat an apportioned share, besides the sale according to his father's house.

**18:9** When you enter into the land which the Lord your God gives you, you shall not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.

**18:10** There shall not be found among you anyone who purifies his son and his daughter in fire, or practices divination, or interprets omens and practices augury, or with spells

**18:11** chants an incantation, or a ventriloquist and an observer of portents, or one who inquires of the dead.

**18:12** For everyone who does these things is an abomination to the Lord your God; and because of these abominations the Lord will utterly destroy them from before you.

**18:13** You shall be blameless before the Lord your God.

**18:14** For these nations whom you are dispossessing listen to omens and divinations; but to you the Lord your God has not given it to be so.

**18:15** The Lord your God will raise up for you a Prophet like me from among your brethren; Him you shall hear,

**18:16** according to all that you asked of the Lord your God in Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, "We will not again hear the voice of the Lord your God, nor will we see this great fire anymore, so that we may not die."

**18:17** And the Lord said to me, "All that they have spoken to you is well spoken.

**18:18** I will raise up for them a Prophet like you from among their brethren, and I will put My words in His mouth, and He shall speak to them according to all that I command Him.

**18:19** And as for the man who does not hear whatever that Prophet speaks in My name, I Myself will require it of him.

**18:20** Yet the prophet who acts ungodly to speak a word in My name which I have not commanded to be spoken, and whoever speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die."

**18:21** And if you say in your heart, "How shall we know the word that the Lord has not spoken?"

**18:22** whatever that prophet speaks in the name of the Lord, and it does not happen and does not come to pass, that is the word which the Lord has not spoken; in ungodliness that prophet has spoken it, and you shall not spare him.

## Translator Notes

- **18:5** — The Greek adds "to stand before the Lord God" and "to bless," and concludes with "among the sons of Israel" rather than "all the days."
- **18:10** — The Greek renders "make pass through the fire" as "purifies in fire" (perikathairōn) and ends the verse with dative pharmakois ("with spells/potions"), which governs "chants an incantation" in verse 11.
- **18:11** — Greek engastrimythos literally means "ventriloquist" (belly-speaker) and is the Septuagint term for a necromantic spirit-medium.
- **18:22** — The Greek reads "you shall not spare him" (literally "you shall not hold back from him"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "you shall not dread him."
