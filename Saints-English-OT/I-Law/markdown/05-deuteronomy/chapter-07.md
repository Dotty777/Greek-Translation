## Text

**7:1** Now when the Lord your God brings you into the land where you are entering to inherit it, and casts out great nations from before your face, the Hittite and the Amorite and the Girgashite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hivite and the Jebusite, seven nations numerous and mightier than you,

**7:2** and the Lord your God delivers them into your hands and you strike them, you shall utterly destroy them; you shall not make a covenant with them, nor shall you show mercy to them,

**7:3** nor shall you intermarry with them; you shall not give your daughter to his son, and you shall not take his daughter for your son;

**7:4** for he will turn your son away from Me, and he will serve other gods; and the Lord will be angry with wrath against you, and He will swiftly destroy you.

**7:5** But thus you shall do to them: you shall tear down their altars, and shatter their pillars, and cut down their groves, and burn the graven images of their gods with fire.

**7:6** For you are a holy people to the Lord your God, and the Lord your God has chosen you to be to Him a treasured people above all the nations that are upon the face of the earth.

**7:7** It is not because you are more numerous than all the nations that the Lord preferred you and chose you, for you are the fewest among all the nations;

**7:8** but because the Lord loves you, and because He keeps the oath which He swore to your fathers, the Lord brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of bondage, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

**7:9** And you shall know today that the Lord your God—He is God, the faithful God, who keeps covenant and mercy for those who love Him and for those who keep His commandments to a thousand generations,

**7:10** and who repays those who hate Him to their face, to destroy them; and He will not delay toward those who hate Him; to their face He will repay them.

**7:11** And you shall keep His commandments and His statutes and His judgments, all that I command you today to do.

**7:12** And it shall come to pass, if you listen to these statutes and keep and do them, that the Lord your God will preserve for you the covenant and the mercy which He swore to your fathers;

**7:13** and the Lord will love you and bless you and multiply you; and He will bless the offspring of your womb and the fruit of your land—your grain and your wine and your oil, the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep—upon the land, as the Lord swore to your fathers to give to you.

**7:14** You shall be blessed above all the nations; there shall not be among you any who is sterile or barren, nor among your livestock.

**7:15** And the Lord will take away from you every infirmity; and all the evil diseases of Egypt which you have seen and all that you have known, He will not lay upon you, but He will lay them upon all who hate you.

**7:16** And you shall consume all the spoils of the nations which the Lord your God gives to you; your eye shall not spare them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that is a stumbling block to you.

**7:17** Now if you say in your mind, "This nation is more numerous than I; how shall I be able to destroy them?"

**7:18** you shall not fear them; you shall surely remember all that the Lord your God did to Pharaoh and to all the Egyptians:

**7:19** the great trials which your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the mighty hand and the uplifted arm, as the Lord your God brought you out; so shall the Lord our God do to all the nations before whose face you are afraid.

**7:20** Moreover, the Lord your God will send the hornets among them, until those who remain and those who are hidden from you are wiped out.

**7:21** You shall not be wounded before their face, for the Lord your God is among you, a great and mighty God.

**7:22** And the Lord your God will consume these nations from before your face little by little; you will not be able to utterly consume them swiftly, lest the land become desolate and the wild beasts multiply against you.

**7:23** And the Lord your God will deliver them into your hands, and you shall destroy them with a great destruction until He utterly destroys them;

**7:24** and He will deliver their kings into your hands, and their name shall perish from their place; no one shall stand before your face, until He utterly destroys them.

**7:25** The graven images of their gods you shall burn in fire; you shall not covet silver and gold from them, nor take it for yourself, lest you stumble because of it, for it is an abomination to the Lord your God;

**7:26** and you shall not bring an abomination into your house, and become an accursed thing like it; you shall utterly abhor it and utterly detest it, for it is an accursed thing.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Septuagint lists the Amorite before the Girgashite (whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places the Girgashite before the Amorite) and reads "great nations" rather than "many nations."
- **7:16** — The Greek reads "all the spoils of the nations" (panta ta skyla ton ethnon), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "all the peoples."
- **7:21** — The Greek verb trothese literally means "be wounded" (rendering Hebrew ta'arots, "dread" or "be terrified"), and describes God as "mighty" (krataios) rather than "awesome."
- **7:24** — The Greek reads "from their place" (ek tou topou ekeinon; compare standard Septuagint ekeinou, "from that place"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "from under heaven."
