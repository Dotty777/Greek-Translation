## Text

**25:1** Now if a dispute arises between men, and they come forward to judgment, and they vindicate what is right and condemn the ungodly man,

**25:2** then it shall be, if the one acting ungodly is worthy of stripes, you shall seat him before them.

**25:3** And they shall scourge him with the number forty; they shall not add more; for if you add to scourge him with more stripes than these, your brother will be disgraced before you.

**25:4** You shall not muzzle a threshing ox.

**25:5** Now if brothers dwell together, and one of them dies, and he had no seed, the wife of the deceased shall not belong outside to a man who is not near of kin; her husband's brother shall go in to her and take her to himself as a wife, and dwell with her.

**25:6** And it shall be that the child whom she bears shall be established by the name of the one who has died, and his name shall not be blotted out from Israel.

**25:7** But if the man does not wish to take his brother's wife, then the woman shall go up to the gate, to the council of elders, and shall say, "My husband's brother refuses to raise up the name of his brother in Israel; my husband's brother has not been willing."

**25:8** And the council of elders of his city shall summon him and speak to him; and if he stands and says, "I do not wish to take her,"

**25:9** then his brother's wife shall come forward before the council of elders, and she shall loose his one sandal from his foot, and spit in his face, and answering she shall say, "Thus shall they do to the man who will not build up the house of his brother in Israel."

**25:10** And his name shall be called in Israel, "The House of Him Whose Sandal Was Loosed."

**25:11** Now if men fight together, a man with his brother, and the wife of one of them comes near to rescue her husband from the hand of the one striking him, and stretching out her hand she seizes his private parts,

**25:12** you shall cut off her hand; your eye shall not spare her.

**25:13** You shall not have in your bag a weight and a weight, a large or a small;

**25:14** there shall not be in your house a measure and a measure, a large or a small;

**25:15** a true and just weight you shall have, that you may live many days upon the land which the Lord your God gives you as an inheritance.

**25:16** For everyone who does these things, everyone who practices injustice, is an abomination to the Lord your God.

**25:17** Remember all that Amalek did to you on the way as you were coming out of the land of Egypt,

**25:18** how he withstood you on the way and cut off your rear guard, those who were weary behind you, while you were hungry and weary, and he did not fear God.

**25:19** And it shall come to pass, when the Lord your God has given you rest from all your enemies around you in the land which the Lord your God gives you to inherit, you shall blot out the name of Amalek from the earth under heaven; and you shall by no means forget.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — The Greek uses the neuter "what is right" (to dikaion) in contrast to the masculine "the ungodly man" (tou asebous).
- **25:2** — Greek "you shall seat him before them" (kathieis) differs from the Hebrew "cause him to lie down," and omits the clause about beating him according to the measure of his guilt.
- **25:6** — The Greek reads simply "the child whom she bears" (to paidion), without specifying "the firstborn" as in the Hebrew.
- **25:15** — The Greek includes only the command for a true and just weight, without repeating the parallel clause for a measure found in the Hebrew.
