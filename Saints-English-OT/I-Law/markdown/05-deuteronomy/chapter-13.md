## Text

**13:1** Every word that I command you today, this you shall take care to do; you shall not add to it, nor take away from it.

**13:2** And if there arises among you a prophet or one who dreams a dream, and he gives you a sign or a wonder,

**13:3** and the sign or the wonder comes to pass of which he spoke to you, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," whom you do not know,

**13:4** you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or of the one who dreams that dream; for the Lord your God is testing you, to know whether you love your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**13:5** Walk after the Lord your God, and Him you shall fear, and to His voice you shall listen, and to Him you shall hold fast.

**13:6** And that prophet or that dreamer of the dream shall die; for he spoke to lead you astray from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, who redeemed you from bondage, to thrust you out from the way in which the Lord your God commanded you to walk; and you shall purge the wicked person from among yourselves.

**13:7** And if your brother by your father or by your mother, or your son or your daughter, or the wife in your bosom, or a friend who is equal to your own soul, urges you, saying secretly, "Let us go and serve other gods," whom neither you nor your fathers have known,

**13:8** from among the gods of the nations that are all around you, those near to you or those far off from you, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth,

**13:9** you shall not consent to him, nor listen to him; neither shall your eye spare him, nor shall you yearn over him, nor shall you conceal him.

**13:10** You shall surely report concerning him; your hands shall be upon him first to put him to death, and the hands of all the people afterward.

**13:11** And they shall stone him with stones, and he shall die, because he sought to turn you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

**13:12** And all Israel shall hear and fear, and shall no longer continue to do according to this wicked thing among you.

**13:13** And if you hear in one of your cities, which the Lord your God gives you to dwell there, people saying,

**13:14** "Lawless men have gone out from among you and have turned away all who dwell in their land, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,' whom you have not known,"

**13:15** then you shall inquire and search out diligently; and behold, if the matter is truly clear and this abomination has been committed among you,

**13:16** you shall surely slay all who dwell in that land with the slaughter of the sword; you shall utterly devote it and all that is in it to destruction.

**13:17** And all its plunder you shall gather into its streets, and you shall burn the city with fire and all its plunder completely before the Lord your God; and it shall be uninhabited forever; it shall not be rebuilt again.

**13:18** And nothing of the devoted thing shall cling to your hand, that the Lord may turn from the fierceness of His wrath, and He will show you mercy and have compassion on you and multiply you, just as He swore to your fathers,

**13:19** if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep His commandments which I command you today, to do what is pleasing and good before the Lord your God.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — In the Greek numbering this verse opens chapter thirteen, whereas in the Hebrew Masoretic Text it concludes chapter twelve (12:32), shifting the remaining verses of chapter thirteen by one.
- **13:7** — The Greek text explicitly includes "by your father or" alongside "by your mother," whereas the Hebrew text mentions only "the son of your mother."
- **13:10** — The Greek reads "you shall surely report concerning him" (anangellon anangeleis), reflecting a different verbal reading from the Hebrew "you shall surely kill him."
- **13:14** — Here and in verse sixteen the Greek refers to "their land" and "that land" (ge), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "their city" and "that city."
