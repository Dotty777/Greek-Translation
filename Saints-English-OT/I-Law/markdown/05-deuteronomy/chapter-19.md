## Text

**19:1** Now when the Lord your God destroys the nations whose land God is giving to you, and you inherit them and dwell in their cities and in their houses,

**19:2** you shall set apart three cities for yourself in the midst of your land, which the Lord your God is giving to you.

**19:3** Measure out the road for yourself, and you shall divide into three parts the borders of your land, which the Lord your God apportions to you, and there shall be a refuge there for every manslayer.

**19:4** And this shall be the ordinance of the manslayer who flees there and shall live: whoever strikes his neighbor unknowingly, and he was not hating him before yesterday and the third day.

**19:5** And whoever enters with his neighbor into the forest to gather wood, and his hand swings aside with the axe as he cuts the wood, and the iron slips off from the wood and strikes his neighbor, and he dies, that man shall flee to one of these cities and live;

**19:6** lest the avenger of blood pursue after the slayer, because he is heated in his heart, and overtake him, if the way is too long, and strike his life; yet for this man there is no judgment of death, because he was not hating him before yesterday nor before the third day.

**19:7** Therefore I command you this word, saying, "You shall set apart three cities for yourself."

**19:8** And if the Lord your God enlarges your borders, in the manner He swore to your fathers, and the Lord gives you all the land which He said He would give to your fathers,

**19:9** if you listen to do all these commandments which I command you today, to love the Lord your God and to walk in all His ways all the days, then you shall add for yourself three more cities in addition to these three;

**19:10** and innocent blood shall not be shed in the land which the Lord your God is giving to you as an inheritance, and there shall not be among you one guilty of blood.

**19:11** But if there is among you a man who hates his neighbor, and lies in wait for him, and rises up against him and strikes his life so that he dies, and flees to one of these cities,

**19:12** then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there, and deliver him into the hands of the avengers of blood, and he shall die.

**19:13** Your eye shall not spare him, and you shall cleanse the innocent blood from Israel, and it shall be well with you.

**19:14** You shall not move the boundaries of your neighbor, which your fathers set up in the inheritance that you have received in the land which the Lord your God is giving to you as an allotment.

**19:15** One witness shall not stand to testify against a man concerning any iniquity or concerning any offense or concerning any sin that he may commit; upon the mouth of two witnesses and upon the mouth of three witnesses every word shall be established.

**19:16** And if an unrighteous witness stands up against a man, charging him with ungodliness,

**19:17** then the two men who have the dispute shall know before the Lord, and before the priests, and before the judges who shall be in those days,

**19:18** and the judges shall inquire diligently, and behold, an unrighteous witness has testified unrighteous things—he has stood up against his brother—

**19:19** then you shall do to him in the manner he wickedly schemed to do against his brother; and you shall remove the wicked one from among yourselves.

**19:20** And the rest shall hear and fear, and shall no longer continue to act according to this wicked word among you.

**19:21** Your eye shall not spare him: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — The Greek idiom 'before yesterday and the third day' (also in 19:6) reflects a Semitic expression meaning 'previously' or 'in time past.'
- **19:17** — The Greek text here reads 'shall know' (gnōsontai, following Codex Vaticanus) rather than 'shall stand' (stēsontai), which appears in other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew.
- **19:19** — The Greek uses the masculine singular 'the wicked one' (ton ponēron), pointing directly to the false witness rather than evil in the abstract.
