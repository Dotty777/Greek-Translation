## Text

**31:1** And Moses finished speaking all these words to all the sons of Israel.

**31:2** And he said to them, "I am one hundred and twenty years old today; I shall no longer be able to go in and go out. And the Lord said to me, 'You shall not cross over this Jordan.'

**31:3** The Lord your God is He who goes before your face; He shall utterly destroy these nations from before your face, and you shall take possession of them; and Joshua is he who goes before your face, just as the Lord has spoken.

**31:4** And the Lord your God shall do to them as He did to Sihon and Og, the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, and to their land, even as He utterly destroyed them,

**31:5** and the Lord delivered them over to you; and you shall do to them just as I have commanded you.

**31:6** Be courageous and strong; do not fear, nor be dismayed, nor be terrified before their face; for the Lord your God is He who goes forward with you, among you; He will by no means fail you, nor will He abandon you."

**31:7** And Moses called Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be courageous and strong; for you shall enter before the face of this people into the land which the Lord swore to our fathers to give to them, and you shall give it as an inheritance to them.

**31:8** And the Lord, who journeys along with you, will not fail you nor by any means abandon you; do not fear, nor be dismayed."

**31:9** And Moses wrote the words of this law in a book, and gave it to the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to the elders of the sons of Israel.

**31:10** And Moses commanded them in that day, saying, "After seven years, at the appointed time of the year of release, at the Feast of Tabernacles,

**31:11** when all Israel gathers together to appear before the Lord your God in the place which the Lord shall choose, you shall read this law before all Israel in their hearing,

**31:12** assembling the people—the men and the women and the little ones, and the sojourner who is in your cities—that they may hear and that they may learn to fear the Lord your God, and they shall listen to perform all the words of this law;

**31:13** and their sons, who do not know, shall hear and learn to fear the Lord your God all the days that they live upon the land into which you are crossing over the Jordan there to inherit it."

**31:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "Behold, the days of your death have drawn near; call Joshua, and stand by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and I will give him a charge." And Moses and Joshua went to the tabernacle of testimony, and they stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.

**31:15** And the Lord descended in a cloud, and stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony; and the pillar of the cloud stood by the doors of the tabernacle.

**31:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Behold, you are about to sleep with your fathers; and this people will rise up and play the harlot after foreign gods of the land into which it is entering, and they will forsake Me and break My covenant which I made with them.

**31:17** And I will be angry with wrath against them in that day, and I will forsake them and turn away My face from them, and it shall become prey to be devoured; and many evils and afflictions shall find it, and it will say in that day, 'Because the Lord my God is not in me, these evils have found me.'

**31:18** And I will surely turn away My face from them in that day on account of all the evils which they have done, because they turned away to foreign gods.

**31:19** And write the words of this song, and teach it to the sons of Israel, and put it into their mouth, that this song may be for Me face to face, bearing witness among the sons of Israel.

**31:20** For I will bring them into the good land which I swore to their fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey; and they shall eat, and having been filled they shall be satiated, and they shall turn to foreign gods and provoke Me and break My covenant.

**31:21** And this song shall stand against them, bearing witness face to face; for it shall by no means be forgotten from the mouth of their seed. For I know their wickedness, all that they are doing here today, before I bring them into the good land which I swore to their fathers."

**31:22** And Moses wrote this song in that day, and taught it to the sons of Israel.

**31:23** And he charged Joshua and said, "Be courageous and strong; for you shall bring the sons of Israel into the land which the Lord swore to them, and He Himself will be with you."

**31:24** Now when Moses had finished writing all the words of this law in a book until the very end,

**31:25** then he commanded the Levites who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord, saying,

**31:26** "Take the book of this law and place it at the side of the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, and it shall be there among you for a witness;

**31:27** for I know your provocation and your stiff neck. For while I am still living with you today, you have been provoking God to bitterness; how shall it not be so even after my death?

**31:28** Assemble to me your tribal leaders and your judges and your scribes, that I may speak all these words in their hearing, and I call both heaven and earth to witness against them.

**31:29** For I know that after my passing you will surely act lawlessly and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you—evils at the end of the days—because you will do what is wicked in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger by the works of your hands."

**31:30** And Moses spoke in the hearing of the entire assembly the words of this song until the very end.

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — The Greek text reads 'finished speaking' (syntelesen lalon), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'went and spoke.'
- **31:6** — The Septuagint uniquely includes three verbs of fear ('do not fear, nor be dismayed, nor be terrified') and the doubled phrase 'with you, among you' (meth' hymon en hymin).
- **31:21** — The Greek text omits the opening clause found in the Hebrew ('and when many evils and troubles have come upon them') and renders Hebrew 'inclination' as 'wickedness' (ten ponerian).
- **31:23** — By referring to the Lord in the third person ('which the Lord swore... He Himself'), the Greek text presents Moses rather than the Lord as the speaker commissioning Joshua here.
- **31:29** — The Greek critical text omits a verb before 'evils at the end of the days,' preserving an abrupt elliptical clause found in major Septuagint witnesses such as Codex Vaticanus.
