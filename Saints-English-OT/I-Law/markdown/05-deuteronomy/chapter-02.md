## Text

**2:1** Then we turned back and set out into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea, just as the Lord spoke to me, and we went around Mount Seir for many days.

**2:2** And the Lord said to me,

**2:3** "Let it be enough for you to go around this mountain; turn therefore toward the north.

**2:4** And command the people, saying, 'You are passing through the borders of your brethren, the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir, and they will fear you and dread you exceedingly.

**2:5** Do not engage in war against them, for I will by no means give you any of their land, not even a footstep, because I have given Mount Seir as an inheritance to the sons of Esau.

**2:6** Buy food from them with silver and you shall eat, and obtain water from them by measure for silver and you shall drink;

**2:7** for the Lord our God has blessed you in every work of your hands. Consider how you passed through that great and fearful wilderness; behold, for forty years the Lord your God has been with you, and you have not lacked a thing.'"

**2:8** And we passed by your brethren, the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, along the way of the Arabah from Ailon and from Ezion Geber; and having turned, we passed along the wilderness way of Moab.

**2:9** And the Lord said to me, "Do not be hostile toward the Moabites, and do not engage in war against them; for I will by no means give you any of their land as an inheritance, because I have given Seir to the sons of Lot to inherit."

**2:10** The Emim formerly dwelt in it, a great and numerous nation, and mighty like the Anakim;

**2:11** these also shall be reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim; and the Moabites call them Emim.

**2:12** And the Horites formerly dwelt in Seir, and the sons of Esau destroyed them and blotted them out from before their face, and settled in their place—just as Israel did to the land of his inheritance, which the Lord gave to them.

**2:13** "Now therefore, arise and set out, and cross over the valley of Zered." So we crossed over the valley of Zered.

**2:14** And the days in which we journeyed from Kadesh Barnea until we crossed over the valley of Zered were thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of the men of war perished, dying out from the camp, just as God had sworn to them.

**2:15** And the hand of God was against them to consume them from the midst of the camp, until they perished.

**2:16** And it came to pass, when all the men of war had perished, dying out from the midst of the people,

**2:17** that the Lord spoke to me, saying,

**2:18** "Today you shall pass over the borders of Moab, even Seir,

**2:19** and you shall draw near to the sons of Ammon. Do not be hostile toward them, and do not engage with them in war; for I will by no means give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon as an inheritance, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as an inheritance."

**2:20** It shall be reckoned a land of Rephaim, for the Rephaim dwelt in it formerly, and the Ammonites call them Zochomin,

**2:21** a great and numerous nation, and mightier than you, like the Anakim; yet the Lord destroyed them before their face, and they took possession and settled in their place until this day—

**2:22** just as they did for the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, in the manner that they blotted out the Horites from before their face, and took possession and settled in their place until this day;

**2:23** and as for the Hivites who dwelt in Asedoth as far as Gaza, the Cappadocians who came out of Cappadocia blotted them out and settled in their place.

**2:24** "Now therefore, arise and set out, and cross over the valley of Arnon. Behold, I have delivered into your hands Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to inherit it, and engage in war against him. On this day

**2:25** begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the face of all the nations under heaven, who upon hearing your name shall be troubled, and shall have pangs of anguish from before your face."

**2:26** And I sent envoys from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,

**2:27** "I will pass through your land; I will pass along the road, and I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**2:28** You shall sell me food for silver and I shall eat, and you shall sell me water for silver and I shall drink; only I will pass through on foot,

**2:29** just as the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Aroer did for me, until I cross over the Jordan into the land which the Lord our God is giving to us."

**2:30** Yet Sihon king of Heshbon was not willing for us to pass by him, because the Lord our God hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, so that he might be delivered into your hands, as on this day.

**2:31** And the Lord said to me, "Behold, I have begun to deliver before your face Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to inherit his land."

**2:32** Then Sihon king of Heshbon came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Jahaz.

**2:33** And the Lord our God delivered him before our face, and He struck down him and his sons and all his people.

**2:34** And we captured all his cities at that time, and we utterly destroyed every city in turn, and their women and their children; we left no one alive.

**2:35** Only the livestock we took as plunder, and the spoil of the cities we seized,

**2:36** from Aroer, which is along the bank of the brook Arnon, and the city that is in the valley, even as far as Mount Gilead; there was not a city that escaped us, for the Lord our God delivered them all into our hands.

**2:37** Only we did not come near the sons of Ammon—all the areas bordering the brook Jabbok and the cities in the hill country—just as the Lord our God commanded us.

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — The Greek text reads "Seir" here and in 2:18, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Ar" (and "Aroer" in 2:29).
- **2:20** — "Zochomin" preserves the distinct Greek name form in this text corresponding to Hebrew "Zamzummim."
- **2:23** — The Greek text reads "Hivites" (for Hebrew "Avvim"), "Asedoth" (for Hebrew "Hazerim"), and "Cappadocians who came out of Cappadocia" (for Hebrew "Caphtorim who came out of Caphtor").
- **2:25** — The Greek uses a second-person imperative ("begin to put") addressed to Israel, whereas the Hebrew has first-person ("I will begin to put"); the phrase "On this day" stands at the end of 2:24 in this Greek text.
- **2:33** — The Greek verb is third-person singular ("He struck down"), making the Lord the subject, whereas the Hebrew reads first-person plural ("we struck down").
