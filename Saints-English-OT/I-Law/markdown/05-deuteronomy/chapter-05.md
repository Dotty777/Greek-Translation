## Text

**5:1** And Moses summoned all Israel and said to them, "Hear, O Israel, the ordinances and the judgments, all that I speak in your ears on this day; and you shall learn them and take care to do them.

**5:2** The Lord your God established a covenant with you at Horeb.

**5:3** Not with your fathers did the Lord establish this covenant, but with you—you who are all here alive today.

**5:4** Face to face the Lord spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire,

**5:5** and I stood between the Lord and you at that time to declare to you the words of the Lord, because you were afraid before the fire and did not go up into the mountain, saying,

**5:6** 'I am the Lord your God, who brought you forth from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**5:7** You shall have no other gods before My face.

**5:8** You shall not make for yourself an idol, nor a likeness of anything that is in heaven above, and that is on the earth beneath, and that is in the waters underneath the earth.

**5:9** You shall not bow down to them, nor shall you serve them; for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate Me,

**5:10** and showing mercy to thousands, to those who love Me and to those who keep My decrees.

**5:11** You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain; for the Lord will by no means acquit the one who takes His name in vain.

**5:12** Guard the Sabbath day to sanctify it, in the manner that the Lord your God commanded you.

**5:13** Six days you shall labor and perform all your works,

**5:14** but on the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God. You shall not do any work on it: you, and your sons, and your daughter, your manservant and your maidservant, your ox and your beast of burden and all your livestock, the stranger who sojourns among you; for in six days the Lord made both heaven and earth and the sea and all that is in them; so that your manservant and your maidservant may rest, just as you also do.

**5:15** And you shall remember that you were a bondservant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God brought you forth from there with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm; therefore the Lord your God directed you to guard the Sabbath day and to sanctify it.

**5:16** Honor your father and your mother, in the manner that the Lord your God commanded, that it may be well with you, and that you may live long upon the land which the Lord your God is giving you.

**5:17** You shall not commit adultery.

**5:18** You shall not murder.

**5:19** You shall not steal.

**5:20** You shall not give false testimony against your neighbor.

**5:21** You shall not covet the wife of your neighbor; you shall not covet the house of your neighbor, nor his field, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his beast of burden, nor any of his livestock, nor anything that belongs to your neighbor.'

**5:22** These words the Lord spoke to all your congregation on the mountain from the midst of the fire—darkness, thick gloom, tempest, a great voice—and He added no more; and He wrote them upon two tablets and gave them to me.

**5:23** And it came to pass, when you heard the voice from the midst of the fire while the mountain was blazing with fire, that you drew near to me, all the leaders of your tribes and your council of elders;

**5:24** and you said, 'Behold, the Lord our God has shown His glory, and we have heard His voice from the midst of the fire; on this day we have seen that God will speak to man, and he shall live.

**5:25** And now let us not die, for this great fire will utterly consume us; if we continue to hear the voice of the Lord our God any longer, then we shall die.

**5:26** For what flesh is there that has heard the voice of the living God speaking from the midst of the fire, as we have, and shall live?

**5:27** You draw near and hear all that the Lord our God may say; and you shall speak to us everything that the Lord our God speaks to you, and we will hear and do it.'

**5:28** And the Lord heard the voice of your words when you spoke to me, and the Lord said to me, 'I have heard the voice of the words of this people, all that they have spoken to you; all that they have spoken is right.

**5:29** Who will grant that their heart might be thus within them, so as to fear Me and to keep My commandments all the days, that it may be well with them and with their sons forever?

**5:30** Go, say to them, "Return to your houses."

**5:31** But as for you, stand here with Me, and I will speak to you the commandments, the ordinances, and the judgments which you shall teach them; and let them perform them in the land which I am giving them as an inheritance.'

**5:32** And you shall take care to do in the manner that the Lord your God commanded you; you shall not turn aside to the right hand or to the left,

**5:33** according to all the way which the Lord your God commanded you to walk in, so that He may give you rest, and it may be well with you, and you may prolong your days upon the land which you shall inherit."

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Greek text uses second-person plural pronouns ("your fathers... with you... you who are all here") in 5:2-3, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses first-person plural ("our God... our fathers... us").
- **5:14** — This Greek witness includes the creation clause ("for in six days the Lord made both heaven and earth and the sea and all that is in them"), paralleling Exodus 20:11 alongside the Exodus redemption motive in 5:15.
- **5:17** — The Greek Septuagint places the prohibition against adultery (5:17) before murder (5:18), reversing the Hebrew Masoretic order (cf. Luke 18:20; Romans 13:9).
- **5:33** — The Greek reads "so that He may give you rest" (katapause), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "that you may live."
