## Text

**32:1** Give heed, O heaven, and I will speak; and let the earth hear words from my mouth.

**32:2** Let my utterance be awaited like rain, and let my words descend like dew, like a heavy shower upon field grass, and like falling snow upon the green herb.

**32:3** For I have called upon the name of the Lord; give greatness to our God.

**32:4** God—His works are true, and all His ways are judgments; a faithful God, and there is no unrighteousness; righteous and holy is the Lord.

**32:5** They have sinned—not children to Him, blemished ones; a crooked and perverse generation.

**32:6** Do you thus repay the Lord these things, O foolish people and not wise? Is not this very One your Father who acquired you and made you?

**32:7** Remember the days of old; understand the years of generations to generations. Ask your father, and he will declare it to you; your elders, and they will tell you.

**32:8** When the Most High divided the nations, when He scattered the sons of Adam, He established the boundaries of the nations according to the number of the angels of God.

**32:9** And His people Jacob became the portion of the Lord, Israel the measured allotment of His inheritance.

**32:10** He sustained him in the wilderness, in the thirst of burning heat, in a waterless land; He encircled him and instructed him, and guarded him as the pupil of an eye.

**32:11** As an eagle shelters its nest and yearned over its young, spreading out its wings, it received them and took them up upon its back.

**32:12** The Lord alone led them; there was no strange god with them.

**32:13** He brought them up upon the strength of the earth; He fed them the produce of the fields; they sucked honey from the rock, and oil from the solid rock;

**32:14** butter of cattle and milk of sheep, with the fat of lambs and rams, of young bulls and male goats, with the fat of the kidneys of wheat; and he drank wine, the blood of the grape.

**32:15** And Jacob ate and was filled, and the beloved one kicked; he grew fat, he grew thick, he grew broad; and he forsook God who made him, and departed from God his Savior.

**32:16** They provoked Me to anger over strange gods; with their abominations they embittered Me.

**32:17** They sacrificed to demons and not to God, to gods whom they did not know; new and recent ones have come, whom their fathers did not know.

**32:18** You forsook God who fathered you, and you forgot God who nourishes you.

**32:19** And the Lord saw and was moved to jealousy, and He was provoked because of anger at His sons and daughters.

**32:20** And He said, "I will turn away My face from them, and I will show what shall happen to them in the last days; for it is a perverted generation, sons in whom there is no faithfulness.

**32:21** They have provoked Me to jealousy by that which is not God; they have angered Me with their idols. And I will provoke them to jealousy by that which is not a nation; by a nation without understanding I will move them to wrath.

**32:22** For a fire has been kindled from My wrath; it shall burn down to Hades below; it shall devour the earth and its produce; it shall set ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

**32:23** I will gather evils against them, and I will wage war upon them with My arrows.

**32:24** Wasting away with famine and the devouring of birds, and incurable convulsion—I will send the teeth of wild beasts against them, with the venom of creatures crawling upon the earth.

**32:25** Outside, the sword shall make them childless, and from the inner chambers, terror—young man together with virgin, nursing infant with the aged elder.

**32:26** I said, 'I will scatter them, and I will cause the remembrance of them to cease from among men,'

**32:27** were it not for the wrath of enemies, lest they endure long, lest the adversaries join together in attack, lest they say, 'Our high hand, and not the Lord, has done all these things.'"

**32:28** It is a nation that has lost counsel, and there is no understanding in them.

**32:29** They did not have the wisdom to understand; let them receive these things for the time to come.

**32:30** How shall one pursue a thousand, and two put ten thousands to flight, unless God had sold them, and the Lord had delivered them up?

**32:31** For their gods are not like our God; and our enemies are senseless.

**32:32** For their vine is from the vine of Sodom, and their vine branch is from Gomorrah; their grape is a grape of gall, a cluster of bitterness to them;

**32:33** their wine is the venom of serpents, and the incurable venom of asps.

**32:34** Behold, are not these things gathered together with Me, and sealed up in My treasuries?

**32:35** In the day of vengeance I will repay, at the time when their foot slips; for the day of destruction is near for them, and the things prepared for you are at hand.

**32:36** For the Lord will judge His people, and He will be moved to compassion over His servants; for He saw them enfeebled, and failing in calamity, and utterly spent.

**32:37** And the Lord said, "Where are their gods, in whom they trusted,

**32:38** the fat of whose sacrifices you ate, and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them arise and help you, and let them become protectors for you.

**32:39** See, see that I am He, and there is no god besides Me; I put to death, and I will make alive; I will strike, and I will heal; and there is no one who shall deliver out of My hands.

**32:40** For I will lift up My hand to heaven, and I will swear by My right hand, and I will say, 'I live forever.'

**32:41** For I will sharpen My sword like lightning, and My hand shall lay hold on judgment; and I will render justice to the enemies, and I will repay those who hate Me;

**32:42** I will make My arrows drunk with the blood of the wounded and of captivity, from the head of the rulers of the enemies."

**32:43** Rejoice, O heavens, together with Him, and let the sons of God worship Him; rejoice, O nations, with His people, and let all the angels of God be strong in Him; for He avenges the blood of His sons, and He will take vengeance, and He will repay justice to the enemies; and He will repay those who hate Him, and the Lord will cleanse the land of His people.

**32:44** And Moses wrote this song on that day, and taught it to the sons of Israel; and Moses entered and spoke all the words of this law into the ears of the people, he and Joshua the son of Nun.

**32:45** And Moses finished speaking to all Israel;

**32:46** and he said to them, "Set your heart upon all these words which I testify to you today, which you shall command your sons, to keep and to do all the words of this law.

**32:47** For this is not an empty word for you, because this is your life; and on account of this word you shall prolong your days upon the land into which you are crossing over the Jordan to inherit."

**32:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses on this day, saying,

**32:49** "Go up to Mount Abarim, this Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Canaan opposite Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I give to the sons of Israel;

**32:50** and die on the mountain to which you are going up, and be added to your people, just as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was added to his people;

**32:51** because you disobeyed My word among the sons of Israel at the water of Strife of Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not sanctify Me among the sons of Israel.

**32:52** You shall see the land from opposite it, and you shall not enter there."

## Translator Notes

- **32:8** — The Greek reads "according to the number of the angels of God," an important Septuagint reading supported by early Dead Sea Scrolls witnesses, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the sons of Israel."
- **32:15** — "The beloved one" translates Greek ho egapemenos, the Septuagint rendering of the poetic Hebrew title Jeshurun for Israel.
- **32:35** — The Greek reads "In the day of vengeance I will repay," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Vengeance is Mine, and recompense."
- **32:43** — The Septuagint preserves a fuller hymnic conclusion than the Masoretic Text, calling upon the heavens, the sons of God, the nations, and all the angels of God to rejoice and worship Him (cf. Romans 15:10; Hebrews 1:6).
- **32:49** — The Greek reads "in the land of Canaan" for the location of Mount Nebo, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the land of Moab."
