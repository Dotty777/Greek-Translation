## Text

**26:1** And it shall come to pass, when you enter into the land which the Lord your God gives you to inherit, and you possess it and dwell upon it,

**26:2** then you shall take from the firstfruits of the fruits of your land which the Lord your God gives you, and put them into a basket, and go to the place which the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there;

**26:3** and you shall come to the priest who shall be in those days, and say to him, "I declare today to the Lord my God that I have entered into the land which the Lord swore to our fathers to give to us."

**26:4** And the priest shall take the basket from your hands, and set it before the altar of the Lord your God.

**26:5** And answering he shall say before the Lord your God, "My father forsook Syria and went down into Egypt, and sojourned there with a small number, and became there a great nation and a large multitude.

**26:6** And the Egyptians mistreated us, and humbled us, and laid hard labors upon us.

**26:7** And we cried out to the Lord our God, and the Lord heard our voice, and saw our humiliation and our affliction.

**26:8** And the Lord Himself brought us out of Egypt with His great strength and with a mighty hand and a lofty arm, and with great visions and with signs and with wonders;

**26:9** and He brought us into this place, and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**26:10** And now, behold, I have brought the firstfruits of the produce of the land which You have given me, O Lord, a land flowing with milk and honey." And you shall worship before the Lord your God;

**26:11** and you and your household, and the Levite and the sojourner who is among you, shall rejoice in all the good things which the Lord your God has given you.

**26:12** Now when you have finished tithing all the tithe of your produce in the third year, you shall give the second tithe to the Levite and to the sojourner and to the orphan and to the widow, and they shall eat in your cities and rejoice.

**26:13** And you shall say before the Lord your God, "I have cleared out the holy things from my house, and have given them to the Levite and to the sojourner and to the orphan and to the widow, according to all the commandments which You commanded me; I have not transgressed Your commandment, nor have I forgotten.

**26:14** And I have not eaten of them in my grief, I have not offered of them for an unclean thing, I have not given of them to the dead; I have obeyed the voice of the Lord our God, I have obeyed just as You commanded me.

**26:15** Look down from Your holy house, from heaven, and bless Your people Israel and the land which You have given to them, just as You swore to our fathers to give to us a land flowing with milk and honey."

**26:16** On this day the Lord your God has commanded you to do all the statutes and the judgments; and you shall keep and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.

**26:17** You have chosen God today to be your God, and to walk in all His ways, and to keep the statutes and the judgments, and to obey His voice;

**26:18** and the Lord has chosen you today to become for Him a treasured people, just as He spoke, to keep His commandments,

**26:19** and for you to be high above all the nations, as He has made you renowned, and a boast, and glorious—that you may be a holy people to the Lord your God, just as He has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **26:5** — The Greek uses the third-person verb "he shall say" (erei) and renders the confession "My father forsook Syria" (Syrian apebalen ho pater mou), contrasting with the Hebrew "you shall say" and "a wandering Aramean was my father."
- **26:8** — Greek "with great visions" (en horamasin megalois) reflects a reading of the Hebrew root for "vision" rather than "terror."
- **26:12** — The Greek explicitly specifies "the second tithe" (to deuteron epidekaton) rather than the Hebrew "the year of tithing."
- **26:17** — The Greek translates the covenantal verbs in verses 17 and 18 with "chosen" (heilou and heilato) and reads "God" rather than "the Lord" in verse 17.
