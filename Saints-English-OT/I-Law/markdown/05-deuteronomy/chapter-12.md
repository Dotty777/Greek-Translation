## Text

**12:1** And these are the ordinances and the judgments which you shall be careful to do in the land which the Lord, the God of our fathers, is giving you as an inheritance, all the days that you live upon the earth.

**12:2** You shall utterly destroy all the places where those whom you are dispossessing served their gods, upon the high mountains and upon the hills and beneath a leafy tree;

**12:3** and you shall tear down their altars, and shatter their pillars, and cut down their groves, and burn the carved images of their gods with fire; and their name shall perish from that place.

**12:4** You shall not do thus to the Lord your God;

**12:5** but to the place which the Lord your God shall choose in one of your cities, to name His name there to be called upon, even there you shall seek;

**12:6** and you shall bring your burnt offerings, and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herds and of your flocks;

**12:7** and there you shall eat before the Lord your God, and you shall rejoice in all to which you put your hand, you and your households, according as the Lord your God has blessed you.

**12:8** You shall not do all the things which we are doing here today, every man doing what is pleasing in his own sight;

**12:9** for as yet you have not come to the rest and to the inheritance which the Lord our God is giving you.

**12:10** And you shall cross over the Jordan and dwell in the land which the Lord our God gives you to inherit, and He shall give you rest from all your enemies round about, and you shall dwell in safety.

**12:11** Then there shall be the place which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there; you shall bring all that I command you today: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the firstfruits of your hands, and every choice thing of your gifts, whatever you vow to God;

**12:12** and you shall rejoice before the Lord your God, you and your sons and your daughters, your menservants and your maidservants, and the Levite who is at your gates, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you.

**12:13** Take heed to yourself lest you offer up your burnt offerings in every place that you see;

**12:14** but rather in the place which the Lord your God shall choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer up your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I command you today.

**12:15** Yet according to all your desire you may slaughter and eat meat, according to the blessing of the Lord your God which He has given you in every city; the unclean among you and the clean shall eat it alike, as the gazelle or the deer;

**12:16** only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out upon the earth like water.

**12:17** You may not eat within your cities the tithe of your grain, and of your wine, and of your oil, nor the firstborn of your herds, nor any vows which you vow, nor your freewill offerings, nor the firstfruits of your hands;

**12:18** but you shall eat it before the Lord your God in the place which the Lord your God shall choose for Himself, you and your son and your daughter, your manservant and your maidservant, and the sojourner who is in your cities; and you shall rejoice before the Lord your God in all to which you put your hand.

**12:19** Take heed to yourself lest you forsake the Levite all the time that you live upon the land.

**12:20** And if the Lord your God enlarges your borders, just as He spoke to you, and you say, "I will eat meat," if your soul desires to eat meat, according to all the desire of your soul you may eat meat.

**12:21** And if the place which the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there is far from you, then you shall slaughter from your herds and from your flocks which God gives you, in the manner that I have commanded you, and you shall eat within your cities according to the desire of your soul;

**12:22** just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you shall eat it; the unclean among you and the clean shall eat alike.

**12:23** Take earnest heed not to eat blood, for its blood is the life; the life shall not be eaten with the meat;

**12:24** you shall not eat it; you shall pour it out upon the earth like water;

**12:25** you shall not eat it, that it may be well with you and with your sons after you, if you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

**12:26** Only your holy things, if you have any, and your vows you shall take and come to the place which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there;

**12:27** and you shall offer your burnt offerings; and the meat you shall eat.

**12:28** Be watchful and listen, and you shall do all the words which I command you, that it may be well with you and with your sons forever, if you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

**12:29** And when the Lord your God utterly destroys from before your face the nations among whom you are entering to inherit their land, and you inherit it and dwell in their land,

**12:30** take heed to yourself lest you seek to follow after them after they have been utterly destroyed from before your face, saying, "How do these nations act toward their gods? I also will do so."

**12:31** You shall not do thus to your God; for the abominations of the Lord, which He hates, they have done with their gods; for their sons and their daughters they burn in fire to their gods.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — Following the Septuagint, Israel is commanded to cut down the groves and burn the carved images—reversing the verbs of the Hebrew Masoretic Text—and "their name shall perish" is intransitive.
- **12:6** — The Codex Vaticanus Septuagint text here is shorter than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, listing burnt offerings, freewill offerings (homologias), and the firstborn of herds and flocks; the fuller list appears in 12:11.
- **12:18** — "the sojourner" translates Greek proselytos ("sojourner" or "resident alien"), which stands in the Septuagint here where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Levite."
- **12:27** — The Septuagint text here is markedly shorter than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting the instructions regarding placing the flesh and pouring the blood upon the altar.
