## Text

**23:1** A man shall not take his father's wife, nor shall he uncover his father's covering.

**23:2** Neither a eunuch who is crushed nor one who is castrated shall enter into the assembly of the Lord.

**23:3** One born of a harlot shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord.

**23:4** An Ammonite or a Moabite shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord; even to the tenth generation he shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord, even forever,

**23:5** because they did not meet you with bread and water on the way when you were coming out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam the son of Beor from Mesopotamia to curse you;

**23:6** yet the Lord your God would not listen to Balaam, and the Lord your God turned the curses into a blessing, because the Lord your God loved you.

**23:7** You shall not speak peaceable words to them nor what is beneficial to them all your days forever.

**23:8** You shall not abhor an Idumean, for he is your brother; you shall not abhor an Egyptian, for you were a sojourner in his land;

**23:9** if sons are born to them, in the third generation they shall enter into the assembly of the Lord.

**23:10** And if you go out to encamp against your enemies, then you shall guard yourself from every evil thing.

**23:11** If there is among you a man who is not clean because of his emission by night, then he shall go out into the camp;

**23:12** and it shall be toward evening that he shall wash his body with water, and when the sun has set, he shall enter into the camp.

**23:13** And you shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there.

**23:14** And you shall have a peg upon your belt, and it shall be that when you sit down outside, you shall dig with it, and drawing earth over it, you shall cover your indecency;

**23:15** for the Lord your God walks about in your camp to deliver you and to hand over your enemy before your face; and your camp shall be holy, and no unseemliness of any matter shall be seen in you, lest He turn away from you.

**23:16** You shall not hand over a servant to his master, who has attached himself to you from his master;

**23:17** he shall dwell with you, among you; he shall dwell wherever it pleases him; you shall not oppress him.

**23:18** There shall not be a harlot from the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a fornicator from the sons of Israel; there shall not be a female cult initiate from the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a male cult initiate from the sons of Israel.

**23:19** You shall not bring the hire of a harlot or the price of a dog into the house of the Lord your God for any vow, for both of these are an abomination to the Lord your God.

**23:20** You shall not lend at interest to your brother—interest on silver and interest on food and interest on anything that you may lend out;

**23:21** to a foreigner you may lend at interest, but to your brother you shall not lend at interest, that the Lord your God may bless you in all your works upon the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**23:22** And if you vow a vow to the Lord your God, you shall not delay to fulfill it, for the Lord your God will surely require it from you, and there will be sin in you;

**23:23** but if you choose not to vow, there is no sin in you.

**23:24** You shall guard what goes forth through your lips, and you shall perform just as you vowed to God the gift that you spoke with your mouth.

**23:25** And if you enter into your neighbor's standing grain, you may gather heads of grain with your hands, but you shall by no means put a sickle to your neighbor's standing grain.

**23:26** And if you enter into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes enough to satisfy your soul, but you shall not put any into a vessel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — In the Septuagint, chapter 23 begins with this verse (22:30 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), shifting the subsequent verse numbers by one.
- **23:8** — The Greek has Idoumaion ("Idumean"), the Septuagint designation for an Edomite.
- **23:11** — The Greek text here reads "he shall go out into the camp," a notable Codex Vaticanus reading where other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read "outside the camp, and he shall not enter into the camp."
- **23:18** — The Septuagint preserves a fourfold prohibition, rendering the Hebrew terms both as "harlot" and "fornicator" and as female and male initiates (telesphoros and teliskomenos) of pagan mystery cults.
- **23:25** — In the Septuagint, the laws regarding a neighbor's standing grain (v. 25) and vineyard (v. 26) appear in reverse order from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
