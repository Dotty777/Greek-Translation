## Text

**14:1** You are sons of the Lord your God; you shall not make a baldness between your eyes for the dead.

**14:2** For you are a holy people to the Lord your God, and the Lord your God has chosen you to be a treasured people for Himself from all the nations that are on the face of the earth.

**14:3** You shall not eat any abominable thing.

**14:4** These are the beasts that you shall eat: the calf from the cattle, and the lamb from the sheep, and the kid from the goats,

**14:5** the deer and the gazelle and the pygarg, the oryx and the giraffe;

**14:6** every beast that parts the hoof and divides the hoof into two claws, and brings up the cud among the beasts—these you shall eat.

**14:7** Yet these you shall not eat of those that bring up the cud and of those that part the hooves and divide the cloven hoof: the camel, and the hare, and the rock hyrax; for they bring up the cud, yet do not part the hoof; they are unclean for you.

**14:8** And the swine, because it parts the hoof and divides the cloven hoof, yet does not chew the cud, it is unclean for you; of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch.

**14:9** And these you shall eat of all that are in the water: all that have fins and scales you shall eat.

**14:10** And all that do not have fins and scales you shall not eat; they are unclean for you.

**14:11** Every clean bird you shall eat.

**14:12** But these of them you shall not eat: the eagle, and the bearded vulture, and the sea eagle,

**14:13** and the vulture, and the kite and those like it,

**14:15** and the ostrich, and the owl, and the gull,

**14:16** and the heron, and the swan, and the ibis,

**14:17** and the cormorant, and the hawk and those like it, and the hoopoe, and the night raven,

**14:18** and the pelican, and the plover and those like it, and the purple gallinule, and the bat.

**14:19** All winged creeping things are unclean for you; you shall not eat of them.

**14:20** Every clean winged creature you shall eat.

**14:21** You shall not eat any carcass; it shall be given to the sojourner who is in your cities, and he shall eat it, or you shall sell it to a foreigner; for you are a holy people to the Lord your God. You shall not boil a lamb in its mother's milk.

**14:22** You shall surely tithe a tenth of all the produce of your seed, the produce of your field, year by year.

**14:23** And you shall eat it in the place which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there; you shall bring the tithes of your grain and of your wine and of your oil, and the firstborn of your cattle and of your sheep, that you may learn to fear the Lord your God all the days.

**14:24** And if the way is too far from you, so that you are not able to carry them up, because the place is far from you which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there, because the Lord your God will bless you,

**14:25** then you shall sell them for silver, and take the silver in your hands, and go to the place which the Lord your God shall choose;

**14:26** and you shall spend silver for whatever your soul desires; and you shall eat there before the Lord your God, and you shall rejoice, you and your son,

**14:27** and the Levite who is in your cities, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you.

**14:28** After three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your produce. In that year you shall deposit it in your cities.

**14:29** And the Levite shall come—because he has no portion nor inheritance with you—and the stranger, and the orphan, and the widow who is in your cities, and they shall eat and be filled, that the Lord your God may bless you in all the works that you do.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — The Greek text states only the prohibition against making baldness between the eyes for the dead, without the Hebrew clause forbidding ritual self-laceration.
- **14:5** — The Greek lists five permitted wild animals—including the pygarg (white-rumped antelope) and the giraffe (kamelopardalis)—where the Hebrew text lists seven.
- **14:13** — Verse fourteen of the Hebrew text ("every raven according to its kind") is absent from this Greek witness, so the numbering proceeds directly from verse thirteen to verse fifteen.
- **14:21** — The Greek specifies "a lamb" (arna) rather than the Hebrew "a young goat."
- **14:26** — The Greek omits the list of oxen, sheep, wine, and strong drink, reads "your son" (ho huios sou) rather than "your household," and connects verse twenty-seven directly to the rejoicing without the Hebrew clause "you shall not forsake him."
