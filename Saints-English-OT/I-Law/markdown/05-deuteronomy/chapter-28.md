## Text

**28:1** And it shall come to pass, if you diligently heed the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and to perform all these commandments which I command you today, that the Lord your God shall set you high above all the nations of the earth.

**28:2** And all these blessings shall come upon you and find you, if you diligently heed the voice of the Lord your God.

**28:3** Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field.

**28:4** Blessed are the offspring of your womb, the herds of your cattle, and the flocks of your sheep.

**28:5** Blessed are your storehouses and your reserves.

**28:6** Blessed are you in your coming in, and blessed are you in your going out.

**28:7** May the Lord your God deliver your enemies who stand against you, crushed before your face; by one way they shall come out against you, and by seven ways they shall flee from before your face.

**28:8** May the Lord send the blessing upon you in your storerooms and upon everything to which you put your hand, upon the land which the Lord your God gives you.

**28:9** May the Lord raise you up for Himself as a holy people, just as He swore to your fathers, if you heed the voice of the Lord your God and walk in all His ways.

**28:10** And all the nations of the earth shall see you, that the name of the Lord is called upon you, and they shall fear you.

**28:11** And the Lord your God shall multiply you for good, in the offspring of your womb, and in the produce of your land, and in the offspring of your livestock, upon the land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give you.

**28:12** May the Lord open to you His good treasury, the heaven, to give the rain to your land in season, to bless all the works of your hands; and you shall lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow.

**28:13** May the Lord your God appoint you as the head and not as the tail, and then you shall be above and shall not be beneath, if you heed the voice of the Lord your God in all things that I command you today to keep.

**28:14** You shall not turn aside from any of the commandments which I command you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

**28:15** And it shall come to pass, if you do not heed the voice of the Lord your God, to keep all His commandments which I command you today, that all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you.

**28:16** Cursed are you in the city, and cursed are you in the field.

**28:17** Cursed are your storehouses and your reserves.

**28:18** Cursed are the offspring of your womb and the produce of your land, the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep.

**28:19** Cursed are you in your going out, and cursed are you in your coming in.

**28:20** May the Lord send upon you want, and famine, and consumption upon everything to which you put your hand, until He utterly destroys you, and until He makes you perish swiftly because of your evil practices, because you have forsaken Me.

**28:21** May the Lord cause death to cling to you, until He completely consumes you from the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**28:22** May the Lord strike you with distress, and fever, and chill, and inflammation, and blight, and mildew; and they shall pursue you until they destroy you.

**28:23** And the heaven that is over your head shall be bronze to you, and the earth that is beneath you shall be iron.

**28:24** May the Lord your God make the rain for your land fine dust, and dust shall come down from heaven, until He wipes you out, and until He destroys you swiftly.

**28:25** May the Lord make you a visitation before the enemies; by one way you shall go out against them, and by seven ways you shall flee from before their face, and you shall be a dispersion in all the kingdoms of the earth.

**28:26** And your dead bodies shall be food for the birds of heaven and for the beasts of the earth, and there shall be no one to frighten them away.

**28:27** May the Lord strike you with an Egyptian boil upon the seat, and with a malignant scab, and with an itch, so that you cannot be healed.

**28:28** May the Lord strike you with madness of blindness and with bewilderment of mind.

**28:29** And you shall grope at noon, as a blind man might grope in the darkness, and He shall not prosper your ways; and then you shall be wronged and plundered all the days, and there shall be no one to help.

**28:30** You shall take a wife, and another man shall have her; you shall build a house, and you shall not dwell in it; you shall plant a vineyard, and you shall not harvest it.

**28:31** Your calf shall be slaughtered before you, and you shall not eat of it; your donkey shall be seized from you, and shall not be restored to you; your sheep shall be given to your enemies,

**28:32** and your eyes shall gaze upon them, failing with anguish; your hand shall have no strength.

**28:33** A nation which you do not know shall eat the yield of your land and all your labors, and you shall be wronged and crushed all the days.

**28:34** And you shall be driven mad because of the sights of your eyes which you shall see.

**28:35** May the Lord strike you with a grievous boil upon the knees and upon the legs, so that you cannot be healed, from the sole of your feet to the crown of your head.

**28:36** May the Lord lead you away, and your rulers whom you may set over yourself, to a nation which neither you nor your fathers know; and there you shall serve other gods, wood and stone.

**28:37** And you shall be there as a riddle, and a proverb, and a tale among all the nations to which the Lord leads you away.

**28:38** You shall carry out much seed into the field, and you shall bring in little, for the locust shall devour it.

**28:39** You shall plant a vineyard and cultivate it, yet you shall neither drink wine nor rejoice from it, for the worm shall devour it.

**28:40** You shall have olive trees in all your borders, yet you shall not anoint yourself with oil, for your olive shall drop off.

**28:41** You shall bring forth sons and daughters, yet they shall not be, for they shall go away in captivity.

**28:42** The rust shall completely consume all your trees and the produce of your land.

**28:43** The sojourner who is among you shall ascend higher and higher, but you shall descend lower and lower.

**28:44** He shall lend to you, but you shall not lend to him; he shall be the head, and you shall be the tail.

**28:45** And all these curses shall come upon you and pursue you and overtake you, until He utterly destroys you and until He makes you perish, because you did not heed the voice of the Lord your God, to keep His commandments and the ordinances which He commanded you.

**28:46** And they shall be signs and wonders upon you, in your seed forever,

**28:47** because you did not serve the Lord your God with gladness and a good mind, for the abundance of all things.

**28:48** And you shall serve your enemies, whom the Lord shall send against you, in hunger and in want of all things; and He shall put an iron collar upon your neck, until He utterly destroys you.

**28:49** The Lord shall bring upon you a nation from afar, from the end of the earth, like the swoop of an eagle, a nation whose voice you shall not understand,

**28:50** a nation shameless in countenance, which shall not respect the person of the old man, nor show mercy to the young.

**28:51** And it shall devour the offspring of your livestock and the produce of your land, so as not to leave you grain, wine, oil, the herds of your cattle, and the flocks of your sheep, until it destroys you

**28:52** and wipes you out in your cities, until the high and fortified walls in which you trust are torn down throughout all your land; and it shall afflict you in your cities which He gave you.

**28:53** And you shall eat the offspring of your womb, the flesh of your sons and of your daughters, as many as He gave you, in your distress and in your affliction with which your enemy shall afflict you.

**28:54** The tender and exceedingly delicate man among you shall look with a begrudging eye upon his brother, and the wife in his bosom, and the remaining children who may be left,

**28:55** so as not to give to any one of them from the flesh of his children whom he may devour, because nothing is left to him in your distress and in your affliction with which your enemies shall afflict you in all your cities.

**28:56** And the tender and delicate woman among you, whose foot has not ventured to tread upon the earth because of her delicacy, shall look with a begrudging eye upon her husband in her bosom, and her son, and her daughter,

**28:57** and upon her afterbirth which comes forth from between her thighs, and upon the child whom she may bear; for she shall devour them in secret because of the want of all things, in your distress and in your affliction with which your enemy shall afflict you in your cities.

**28:58** If you do not heed to perform all the words of this law which are written in this book, to fear this honored and wondrous name, the Lord your God,

**28:59** then the Lord shall make your plagues extraordinary, and the plagues of your seed, great and wondrous plagues, and evil and persistent diseases.

**28:60** And He shall bring back every evil affliction of Egypt, which you dreaded before their face, and they shall cling to you.

**28:61** And every sickness and every plague which is not written in the book of this law, the Lord shall bring upon you, until He utterly destroys you.

**28:62** And you shall be left small in number, whereas you were as the stars of heaven in multitude, because you did not heed the voice of the Lord your God.

**28:63** And it shall come to pass, just as the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, so the Lord shall rejoice over you to utterly destroy you; and you shall be swiftly removed from the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**28:64** And the Lord your God shall scatter you among all the nations, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth, and there you shall serve other gods, wood and stone, whom neither you nor your fathers knew.

**28:65** Moreover, even among those nations He shall not give you rest, nor shall there be a resting place for the sole of your foot; and there the Lord shall give you another, disobedient heart, and failing eyes, and a melting soul.

**28:66** And your life shall be hanging before your eyes, and you shall fear day and night, and you shall not trust in your life.

**28:67** In the morning you shall say, "How might it become evening?" and in the evening you shall say, "How might it become morning?" because of the fear of your heart which you shall fear, and because of the sights of your eyes which you shall see.

**28:68** And the Lord shall bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said, "You shall not see it again"; and there you shall be sold to your enemies as bondmen and bondwomen, and there shall be no one to buy you.

**28:69** These are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to establish with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant which He made with them in Horeb.

## Translator Notes

- **28:4** — The Greek text here omits "and the produce of your land" (which appears in 28:18 and in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), listing only offspring, cattle, and sheep.
- **28:5** — Greek apothekai ("storehouses") and enkataleimmata ("reserves" or "what remains," also in 28:17) represent the Septuagint's distinctive rendering of the Hebrew terms commonly translated "basket" and "kneading bowl."
- **28:7** — Throughout the blessings (28:7-13) and curses (28:20-28, 35), the Greek regularly uses optative forms ("May the Lord..."), casting these declarations as solemn liturgical wishes rather than simple future statements.
- **28:25** — The Greek text reads episkopen ("a visitation" or "reckoning," as in Codex Vaticanus), whereas other Greek witnesses read enkopen ("defeat" or "rout").
- **28:32** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek text omits the clause about sons and daughters given to another nation (likely lost through similar endings with 28:31), so neuter plural auta ("them") refers directly back to "your sheep" in 28:31.
- **28:36** — The Greek text reads plural archontas ("your rulers") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has singular "your king."
- **28:48** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek text specifies only "in hunger and in want of all things," without "in thirst and in nakedness" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and other Greek witnesses.
- **28:65** — The Greek text reads kardian heteran apeithousan ("another, disobedient heart," as in Codex Vaticanus), whereas other Greek manuscripts read "a despondent heart" and the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a trembling heart."
- **28:69** — In the Septuagint and Hebrew versification, this summary statement concludes chapter twenty-eight as 28:69; most English Bibles number it as 29:1.
