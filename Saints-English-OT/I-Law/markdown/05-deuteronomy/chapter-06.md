## Text

**6:1** Now these are the commandments, the ordinances, and the judgments which our God commanded to teach you, that you may do thus in the land into which you are entering to inherit it,

**6:2** that you may fear the Lord your God; guard all His ordinances and His commandments which I command you today—you, and your sons, and the sons of your sons—all the days of your life, that you may prolong your days.

**6:3** And hear, O Israel, and take care to perform them, so that it may be well with you, and that you may be greatly multiplied, just as the Lord, the God of your fathers, spoke to give you a land flowing with milk and honey.

**6:4** And these are the ordinances and the judgments which the Lord commanded the sons of Israel when they went forth from the land of Egypt: Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord;

**6:5** and you shall love the Lord your God with all your mind, and with all your soul, and with all your might.

**6:6** And these words which I command you today shall be in your heart and in your soul;

**6:7** and you shall diligently instruct your sons, and you shall speak of them when sitting in the house, and when walking on the way, and when lying down, and when rising up.

**6:8** And you shall fasten them as a sign upon your hand, and it shall be immovable before your eyes;

**6:9** and you shall write them upon the doorposts of your houses and of your gates.

**6:10** And it shall come to pass, when the Lord your God brings you into the land which the Lord swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give you—great and fair cities which you did not build,

**6:11** houses full of all good things which you did not fill, hewn cisterns which you did not hew out, vineyards and olive groves which you did not plant—and when you have eaten and are filled, take heed to yourself lest you forget

**6:12** the Lord your God, who brought you forth from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**6:13** The Lord your God you shall fear, and Him you shall serve, and to Him you shall cling, and by His name you shall swear.

**6:14** You shall not walk after other gods from among the nations that are all around you,

**6:15** for the Lord your God in your midst is a jealous God—lest the Lord your God be provoked to wrath against you and utterly destroy you from the face of the earth.

**6:16** You shall not test the Lord your God, as you tested Him at Testing.

**6:17** You shall diligently guard the commandments of the Lord your God, the testimonies and the ordinances which He commanded you;

**6:18** and you shall do what is pleasing and good before the Lord your God, that it may be well with you, and that you may enter and inherit the good land which the Lord swore to your fathers,

**6:19** to drive out all your enemies from before your face, just as the Lord has spoken.

**6:20** And it shall come to pass, when your son asks you in days to come, saying, "What are the testimonies, the ordinances, and the judgments which the Lord our God has commanded us?"

**6:21** then you shall say to your son, "We were bondservants to Pharaoh in the land of Egypt, and the Lord brought us forth from there with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm;

**6:22** and the Lord gave signs and wonders, great and grievous, in Egypt, upon Pharaoh and upon his house, in our sight;

**6:23** and He brought us forth from there to give us this land which He swore to give to our fathers.

**6:24** And the Lord commanded us to perform all these ordinances, to fear the Lord our God, that it may be well with us all the days, that we may live, just as we do today;

**6:25** and there shall be mercy for us if we take care to perform all these commandments before the Lord our God, just as the Lord has commanded us."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Greek text prefixes an introductory superscription ("And these are the ordinances and the judgments which the Lord commanded the sons of Israel when they went forth from the land of Egypt") before the Shema, absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **6:5** — The Greek reads "with all your mind" (dianoia), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "with all your heart" (cf. Matthew 22:37; Mark 12:30).
- **6:8** — Greek asaleuton literally means "immovable" or "unshaken," rendering the rare Hebrew term totaphot ("frontlets" or "phylacteries").
- **6:16** — Greek en to Peirasmo ("at Testing") translates the Hebrew place name Massah ("testing") rather than transliterating it.
- **6:25** — Greek eleemosyne ("mercy" or "charitable righteousness") renders Hebrew tsedaqah ("righteousness"), reflecting a frequent Septuagint equivalence in Deuteronomy.
