## Text

**22:1** You shall not see your brother's calf or his sheep going astray in the way and overlook them; you shall surely bring them back to your brother and restore them to him.

**22:2** And if your brother is not near you, or if you do not know him, you shall bring it inside your house, and it shall be with you until your brother seeks them, and you shall restore them to him.

**22:3** So you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do concerning every lost thing of your brother; whatever is lost from him and you find, you may not overlook it.

**22:4** You shall not see your brother's donkey or his calf fallen in the way; do not overlook them; you shall surely lift them up with him.

**22:5** The articles of a man shall not be upon a woman, nor shall a man put on a woman's garment; for everyone who does these things is an abomination to the Lord your God.

**22:6** And if you come upon a nest of birds before you in the way, whether in any tree or upon the ground, with fledglings or eggs, and the mother is brooding over the fledglings or over the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young;

**22:7** you shall surely send the mother away, but the young ones you may take for yourself, that it may go well with you and that you may live many days.

**22:8** If you build a new house, then you shall make a parapet for your roof, and you shall not cause bloodshed in your house if anyone should fall from it.

**22:9** You shall not sow your vineyard with a double crop, lest the produce and the seed that one sows be consecrated together with the produce of your vineyard.

**22:10** You shall not plow with a calf and a donkey together.

**22:11** You shall not put on a mixed garment, wool and linen together.

**22:12** You shall make twisted tassels for yourself upon the four corners of your cloaks with which you cover yourself.

**22:13** And if any man takes a wife and lives with her, and hates her,

**22:14** and lays unfounded accusations against her, and brings down an evil name upon her, and says, "I took this woman, and when I approached her, I did not find the tokens of her virginity";

**22:15** then the father and the mother of the maiden shall take and bring out the tokens of the maiden's virginity to the council of elders at the gate,

**22:16** and the father of the maiden shall say to the council of elders, "I gave this daughter of mine to this man as a wife, and having hated her,

**22:17** now this man lays unfounded accusations against her, saying, 'I did not find tokens of virginity in your daughter.' Yet these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity." And they shall spread out the garment before the council of elders of the city.

**22:18** And the council of elders of that city shall take that man and chastise him,

**22:19** and they shall fine him one hundred shekels and give them to the father of the young woman, because he brought out an evil name upon an Israelite virgin; and she shall be his wife; he may not send her away for all time.

**22:20** But if this matter is true, and tokens of virginity are not found for the young woman,

**22:21** then they shall bring out the young woman to the doors of her father, and they shall stone her with stones and she shall die, because she has committed folly among the sons of Israel, defiling the house of her father with harlotry; and you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.

**22:22** And if a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, you shall put both of them to death, the man who lay with the woman and the woman; and you shall remove the evil one from Israel.

**22:23** And if there is a virgin maiden betrothed to a husband, and a man finds her in a city and lies with her,

**22:24** you shall bring both of them out to the gate of their city, and they shall be stoned with stones and shall die—the young woman, because she did not cry out in the city, and the man, because he humbled his neighbor's wife; and you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.

**22:25** But if a man finds the betrothed maiden in a plain and forces her and lies with her, you shall put to death only the one who lay with her,

**22:26** and in the young woman there is no sin worthy of death. As when a man rises up against his neighbor and murders his life, so is this matter;

**22:27** for he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, and there was no one to help her.

**22:28** And if any man finds a virgin maiden who is not betrothed, and forces her and lies with her, and he is found out,

**22:29** then the man who lay with her shall give to the father of the young woman fifty didrachmas of silver, and she shall be his wife, because he humbled her; he may not send her away for all time.

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — The Greek reads "with a double crop" (diphoron) and "be consecrated" (hagiasthē, i.e., forfeited to the sanctuary), rather than the Hebrew "two kinds of seed."
- **22:21** — The Greek uses the masculine singular "the evil one" (ton ponēron, also in verses 22 and 24), the exact Septuagint phrasing later quoted in 1 Corinthians 5:13.
- **22:29** — The Greek specifies "fifty didrachmas of silver," using the Alexandrian two-drachma coin as the equivalent of the shekel.
