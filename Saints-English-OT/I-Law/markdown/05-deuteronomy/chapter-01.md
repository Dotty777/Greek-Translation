## Text

**1:1** These are the words which Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness, toward the west, near the Red Sea, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Aulon, and Catachrysea.

**1:2** Eleven days in Horeb is the way to Mount

**1:3** Seir, as far as Kadesh Barnea. And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke to all the sons of Israel according to all that the Lord had commanded him for them, after striking down

**1:4** Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

**1:5** on the far side of the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to expound this law, saying:

**1:6** The Lord our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying, "Let it suffice for you to dwell at this mountain.

**1:7** Turn and set out, and enter into the mountain of the Amorites, and to all those who dwell around the Arabah, to the mountain and the plain, and toward the south, and the coastal land of the Canaanites, and Antilibanus, as far as the great river, the Euphrates.

**1:8** Behold, He has delivered the land before you; go in and inherit the land which I swore to your fathers, to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, to give to them and to their seed after them."

**1:9** And I spoke to you at that time, saying, "I shall not be able to bear you alone.

**1:10** The Lord your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are today like the stars of heaven in multitude.

**1:11** May the Lord, the God of your fathers, add to you a thousandfold more than you are, and bless you just as He has spoken to you.

**1:12** How shall I alone be able to bear your toil, and your burden, and your contentions?

**1:13** Provide for yourselves wise, knowledgeable, and discerning men for your tribes, and I will appoint them over you as your leaders."

**1:14** And you answered me and said, "The word which you have spoken to do is good."

**1:15** And I took from among you wise, knowledgeable, and discerning men, and appointed them to rule over you: commanders of thousands, and commanders of hundreds, and commanders of tens, and recorders for your judges.

**1:16** And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, "Give a full hearing between your brethren, and judge righteously between a man and a brother, and his sojourner.

**1:17** You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall judge the small and the great alike; you shall by no means shrink before the face of man, for judgment belongs to God. And whatever matter is too hard for you, you shall bring it up to me, and I will hear you."

**1:18** And I commanded you at that time all the words which you should perform.

**1:19** And setting out from Horeb, we journeyed through all that great and fearful wilderness which you saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorite, just as the Lord our God commanded us; and we came as far as Kadesh Barnea.

**1:20** And I said to you, "You have come as far as the mountain of the Amorite, which the Lord our God is giving you.

**1:21** Behold, the Lord our God has delivered the land to you before your face; go up and inherit it, in the manner that the Lord, the God of our fathers, has spoken to you. Do not fear, nor be dismayed."

**1:22** And you all approached me and said, "Let us send men ahead of us, and let them explore the land for us, and bring back a report to us concerning the way by which we shall go up, and the cities into which we shall enter."

**1:23** And the word was pleasing in my sight, and I took from among you twelve men, one man for each tribe.

**1:24** And turning, they went up into the mountain, and came as far as the Valley of the Cluster, and spied it out.

**1:25** And they took in their hands some of the fruit of the land and brought it down to us, and said, "Good is the land which the Lord our God is giving us."

**1:26** Yet you were not willing to go up, and you disobeyed the word of the Lord our God;

**1:27** and you murmured in your tents and said, "Because the Lord hates us, He brought us out of the land of Egypt to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites, to utterly destroy us.

**1:28** Where are we going up?" Your brethren turned away your heart, saying, "A nation great and numerous and mightier than you, and cities great and fortified up to heaven; moreover, we have also seen sons of giants there."

**1:29** And I said to you, "Do not be terrified, nor be afraid of them.

**1:30** The Lord your God, who goes before your face, He Himself shall fight against them together with you, according to all that He did for us in the land of Egypt, and in this wilderness which you saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorite, how the Lord

**1:31** your God nurtured you, as a man nurtures his own son, along all the way in which you walked until you came to this place."

**1:32** Yet in this matter you did not trust in the Lord our God,

**1:33** who goes ahead of you in the way to choose a place for you, guiding you in fire by night, showing you the way in which you should walk, and in a cloud by day.

**1:34** And the Lord heard the voice of your words, and being provoked to wrath, He swore, saying,

**1:35** "Surely not one of these men shall see this good land which I swore to their fathers,

**1:36** except Caleb the son of Jephunneh; he shall see it, and to him I will give the land on which he walked, and to his sons, because he was devoted to the things pertaining to the Lord."

**1:37** The Lord was also angry with me on your account, saying, "You also shall by no means enter there.

**1:38** Joshua the son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall enter there; strengthen him, for he shall apportion it as an inheritance to Israel.

**1:39** And every young child who today does not know good or evil, these shall enter there, and to them I will give it, and they shall inherit it."

**1:40** And you turned and encamped toward the wilderness, by the way to the Red Sea.

**1:41** And you answered me and said, "We have sinned before the Lord our God; we will go up and fight according to all that the Lord our God has commanded us." And each man took up his weapons of war, and gathering together, you went up into the mountain.

**1:42** And the Lord said to me, "Say to them, 'You shall not go up, nor shall you fight, for I am not with you; and you shall not be crushed before your enemies.'"

**1:43** And I spoke to you, and you did not listen to me; and you transgressed the word of the Lord, and forcing your way, you went up into the mountain.

**1:44** And the Amorite who dwelt in that mountain came out to meet you, and they pursued you just as bees would do, and wounded you from Seir as far as Hormah.

**1:45** And sitting down, you wept before the Lord our God; yet the Lord did not listen to your voice, nor did He pay attention to you.

**1:46** And you abode in Kadesh many days, as many days as you abode there.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek Septuagint renders the Hebrew geographical terms as "toward the west" (for Arabah), "near the Red Sea" (for Suph), and the Greek place names "Aulon" and "Catachrysea" (translating Hazeroth and Di-zahab).
- **1:7** — The Greek text reads "Antilibanus" (the Anti-Lebanon mountain range) rather than Lebanon.
- **1:8** — The Greek verb "He has delivered" stands in the third person singular before shifting to first person ("which I swore") within the divine speech.
- **1:28** — The Greek uses second-person pronouns ("Your brethren turned away your heart"), indicating that the people's direct lament ends after "Where are we going up?" and Moses resumes addressing Israel; "sons of giants" renders the Greek for Hebrew Anakites.
- **1:40** — The Greek uses an aorist indicative ("you turned and encamped") rather than an imperative, making this verse Moses's narrative report of Israel's departure after God's speech ends in verse 39.
