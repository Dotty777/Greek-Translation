## Text

**10:1** At that time the Lord spoke to me, "Hew for yourself two tablets of stone like the first, and come up to Me on the mountain, and you shall make for yourself an ark of wood;

**10:2** and you shall write on the tablets the words which were on the first tablets which you shattered, and you shall put them into the ark."

**10:3** So I made an ark of incorruptible wood, and hewed the tablets of stone like the first, and went up into the mountain, and the two tablets were in my hands.

**10:4** And He wrote on the tablets, according to the first writing, the ten words which the Lord spoke to you on the mountain out of the midst of the fire; and the Lord gave them to me.

**10:5** Then I turned and came down from the mountain, and put the tablets into the ark which I had made; and they were there, just as the Lord commanded me.

**10:6** And the sons of Israel departed from Beeroth of the sons of Jakim to Misadai; there Aaron died, and there he was buried, and Eleazar his son served as priest in his place.

**10:7** From there they departed to Gadgad, and from Gadgad to Taibatha, a land of brooks of water.

**10:8** At that time the Lord set apart the tribe of Levi to bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord, to stand before the Lord, to minister and to invoke blessings in His name, until this day.

**10:9** Therefore the Levites have no portion and inheritance among their brethren; the Lord Himself is his inheritance, just as He spoke to him.

**10:10** And I stood on the mountain forty days and forty nights, and the Lord heard me at this time also, and the Lord was not willing to destroy you utterly.

**10:11** And the Lord spoke to me, "Go, depart before this people, and let them go in and inherit the land which I swore to their fathers to give to them."

**10:12** And now, O Israel, what does the Lord your God ask of you, except to fear the Lord your God, and to walk in all His ways, and to love Him, and to serve the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul,

**10:13** to keep the commandments of the Lord your God and His ordinances, all that I command you today, that it may be well with you?

**10:14** Behold, to the Lord your God belong heaven and the heaven of heaven, the earth and all things that are in it.

**10:15** Yet the Lord preferred your fathers, to love them, and He chose their seed after them—even you—above all the nations, as it is this day.

**10:16** And you shall circumcise the hardness of your heart, and you shall not stiffen your neck.

**10:17** For the Lord your God—He is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great and mighty and awesome God, who does not show partiality, nor will He by any means take a bribe;

**10:18** executing justice for the sojourner and the orphan and the widow, and He loves the sojourner, giving him bread and clothing.

**10:19** And you shall love the sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**10:20** You shall fear the Lord your God, and Him you shall serve, and to Him you shall cling, and by His name you shall swear.

**10:21** He is your boast, and He is your God, who has done among you these great and glorious things which your eyes have seen.

**10:22** With seventy souls your fathers went down to Egypt, but now the Lord your God has made you like the stars of heaven in multitude.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek text reads the second-person future "you shall write" (matching Exod 34:27), whereas the Hebrew text reads first-person "I will write" (compare 10:4).
- **10:3** — Greek "incorruptible wood" (literally wood that does not rot) is the standard Septuagint rendering of Hebrew acacia wood.
- **10:6** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper-name forms "Beeroth of the sons of Jakim" and "Misadai," corresponding to Hebrew Beeroth Bene-jaakan and Moserah.
- **10:7** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper-name forms "Gadgad" and "Taibatha," corresponding to Hebrew Gudgodah and Jotbathah.
- **10:16** — The Greek renders the idiom directly as "the hardness of your heart," where the Hebrew reads literally "the foreskin of your heart."
