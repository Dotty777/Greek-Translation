## Text

**15:1** Every seven years you shall make a release.

**15:2** And this is the ordinance of the release: you shall remit every personal debt that your neighbor owes you, and you shall not exact it of your brother, for the release has been proclaimed to the Lord your God.

**15:3** Of a foreigner you shall exact whatever of yours is with him, but for your brother you shall grant a release of your debt;

**15:4** for there shall be no needy person among you, because the Lord your God will surely bless you in the land which the Lord your God gives you as an inheritance to possess it,

**15:5** if indeed you diligently listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and to do all these commandments that I command you today.

**15:6** For the Lord your God has blessed you just as He spoke to you; and you shall lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow; and you shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.

**15:7** And if there is among you a needy one of your brethren in one of your cities in the land which the Lord your God gives you, you shall not turn away your heart, nor shall you tightly shut your hand from your needy brother;

**15:8** you shall surely open your hands to him, and shall surely lend him a loan as much as he needs, according to what he lacks.

**15:9** Take heed to yourself lest there be a hidden thought in your heart, a lawless thing, saying, "The seventh year, the year of release, is drawing near," and your eye be evil toward your needy brother, and you do not give to him, and he cries out against you to the Lord, and it becomes a great sin in you.

**15:10** You shall surely give to him, and you shall surely lend him a loan as much as he needs, according to what he lacks; and you shall not be grieved in your heart when you give to him, because for this matter the Lord your God will bless you in all the works and in all to which you set your hand.

**15:11** For a needy person shall never cease from the land; therefore I command you to do this thing, saying, "You shall surely open your hands to your brother, to the poor and to the needy who is in your land."

**15:12** And if your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, is sold to you, he shall serve you six years, and in the seventh you shall send him out free from you.

**15:13** And when you send him out free from you, you shall not send him out empty-handed;

**15:14** you shall generously supply him with provisions from your sheep, and from your grain, and from your wine; just as the Lord your God has blessed you, you shall give to him.

**15:15** And you shall remember that you were a servant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God redeemed you from there; therefore I command you to do this thing.

**15:16** And if he says to you, "I will not go out from you," because he has loved you and your house, because it is well with him beside you,

**15:17** then you shall take the awl and pierce his ear against the door, and he shall be your servant forever; and to your maidservant you shall do likewise.

**15:18** It shall not seem hard in your sight when they are sent out free from you, for he has served you six years for the yearly wage of a hired servant; and the Lord your God will bless you in all that you do.

**15:19** Every firstborn that is born among your cattle and among your sheep—the males—you shall sanctify to the Lord your God; you shall not work with your firstborn calf, nor shall you shear the firstborn of your sheep.

**15:20** Before the Lord you shall eat it year by year in the place which the Lord your God shall choose, you and your household.

**15:21** And if there is a blemish in it, lame or blind, an evil blemish, you shall not sacrifice it to the Lord your God.

**15:22** Within your cities you shall eat it; the unclean among you and the clean alike shall eat it, as a gazelle or a deer.

**15:23** Only you shall not eat blood; upon the earth you shall pour it out as water.

## Translator Notes

- **15:10** — The Greek repeats the injunction from verse eight to lend the needy brother as much as he requires according to his lack.
- **15:18** — The Greek reads "the yearly wage of a hired servant" (ephetion misthon), whereas the Hebrew text reads "double the wage of a hired servant."
