## Text

**33:1** And this is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the sons of Israel before his death.

**33:2** And he said, "The Lord has come from Sinai, and has appeared to us from Seir; He hastened from Mount Paran with myriads of Kadesh; at His right hand were angels with Him.

**33:3** And He spared His people, and all the sanctified ones are under Your hands; these also are beneath You, and he received from His words,

**33:4** a law which Moses commanded you, an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob.

**33:5** And there shall be a ruler in the beloved, when the rulers of the peoples are gathered together with the tribes of Israel.

**33:6** Let Reuben live and not die, and let him be many in number."

**33:7** And this is for Judah: "Hear, O Lord, the voice of Judah, and may You come to his people; his hands shall contend for him, and You shall be a helper from the enemies."

**33:8** And of Levi he said, "Give to Levi his manifestations and his truth to the holy man, whom they tested at Testing, and whom they reviled at the water of Contradiction,

**33:9** who says to his father and to his mother, 'I have not seen you,' and did not acknowledge his brethren, and disowned his sons; he kept Your oracles, and preserved Your covenant.

**33:10** They shall declare Your ordinances to Jacob, and Your law to Israel; they shall place incense in Your wrath continually upon Your altar.

**33:11** Bless, O Lord, his strength, and accept the works of his hands; shatter the loins of the enemies who have risen up against him, and let those who hate him not rise up."

**33:12** And of Benjamin he said, "Beloved by the Lord, he shall dwell in confidence; God overshadows him all the days, and between his shoulders He has rested."

**33:13** And of Joseph he said, "By the blessing of the Lord is his land, from the seasons of heaven and dew, and from the depths of springs beneath,

**33:14** and according to the season of the produce of the turnings of the sun, and from the conjunctions of the months,

**33:15** from the summit of the mountains of the beginning, and from the summit of the everlasting hills,

**33:16** and according to the season of the fullness of the earth; and may the favors of Him who appeared in the bush come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of him who was glorified among his brethren.

**33:17** A firstborn of a bull is his beauty, and the horns of a unicorn are his horns; with them he shall gore the nations together even to the end of the earth. These are the myriads of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasseh."

**33:18** And of Zebulun he said, "Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in his tents.

**33:19** They shall utterly destroy nations; and there you shall call out, and you shall offer a sacrifice of righteousness; for the wealth of the sea shall nurse you, and the commerce of those who dwell along the coast."

**33:20** And of Gad he said, "Blessed is He who enlarges Gad; like a lion he has rested, having crushed arm and ruler.

**33:21** And he saw his firstfruits, because there the land of the rulers was divided, when they were gathered together with the leaders of the peoples; the Lord executed righteousness, and His judgment with Israel."

**33:22** And of Dan he said, "Dan is a lion's cub, and he shall leap forth from Bashan."

**33:23** And of Naphtali he said, "Naphtali is an abundance of favors, and let him be filled with blessing from the Lord; he shall inherit the sea and the southwest."

**33:24** And of Asher he said, "Blessed above children is Asher, and he shall be acceptable to his brethren. He shall dip his foot in oil;

**33:25** iron and bronze shall be his sandal; as your days, so is your strength.

**33:26** There is none like the God of the beloved; He who rides upon heaven is your helper, and the Majestic One of the firmament.

**33:27** And the rule of God shall shelter you, and under the strength of everlasting arms; He shall cast out the enemy from before your face, saying, 'Perish!'

**33:28** And Israel shall dwell in confidence, alone upon the land of Jacob, upon grain and wine; and the heaven for you shall be clouded with dew.

**33:29** Blessed are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the Lord? Your helper shall shield you, and the sword is your boast; your enemies shall deal falsely with you, and you shall tread upon their neck."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — The Greek text reads "myriads of Kadesh" and "angels with Him," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "ten thousands of holy ones" and "a fiery law for them."
- **33:5** — "The beloved" translates Greek ho egapemenos (also in 33:26), the Septuagint rendering of the poetic Hebrew name Jeshurun.
- **33:8** — Greek "manifestations" and "truth" translate the Hebrew Urim and Thummim, while "Testing" (Peira) and "Contradiction" (Antilogia) translate the place names Massah and Meribah.
- **33:10** — The Greek text reads "in Your wrath continually," reflecting a rendering of Hebrew be'appekha ("in Your nostril/wrath") and taking the following term to mean "continually" rather than "whole burnt offering."
- **33:19** — The Greek text reads "They shall utterly destroy nations," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("They shall call peoples to the mountain").
