## Text

**9:1** Hear, O Israel: you are crossing over this Jordan today to go in to inherit nations great and much stronger than you, great cities walled up to heaven,

**9:2** a great and numerous and tall people, the sons of Anak, whom you know, and you have heard, "Who can stand before the face of the sons of Anak?"

**9:3** And you shall know today that the Lord your God—He goes before your face; He is a consuming fire. He shall utterly destroy them, and He shall turn them back from before your face, and you shall destroy them, just as the Lord spoke to you.

**9:4** Do not say in your heart, when the Lord your God has consumed these nations from before you, saying, "Because of my righteousness the Lord has brought me in to inherit this good land."

**9:5** It is not because of your righteousness nor because of the holiness of your heart that you are going in to inherit their land, but because of the ungodliness of these nations the Lord will utterly destroy them from before your face, and that He may establish His covenant which He swore to your fathers, to Abraham and to Isaac and to Jacob.

**9:6** And you shall know today that it is not because of your righteous deeds that the Lord your God gives you this good land to inherit, for you are a stiff-necked people.

**9:7** Remember, do not forget how much you provoked the Lord your God in the wilderness; from the day you went out from Egypt and came to this place, you have continually been disobedient toward the Lord.

**9:8** Also at Horeb you provoked the Lord, and He was filled with wrath against you to destroy you utterly,

**9:9** when I went up into the mountain to receive the tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant which the Lord made with you. And I remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights; I did not eat bread, and I did not drink water.

**9:10** And the Lord gave to me the two tablets of stone, written by the finger of God, and upon them had been written all the words which the Lord spoke to you on the mountain in the day of the assembly.

**9:11** And it came to pass after forty days and forty nights that the Lord gave to me the two tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant.

**9:12** And the Lord spoke to me, "Arise, go down quickly from here, for your people whom you brought out of the land of Egypt have acted lawlessly; they have quickly transgressed from the way which you commanded them, and have made for themselves a molten image."

**9:13** And the Lord spoke to me, "I have spoken to you once and twice, saying, 'I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people';

**9:14** and now let Me alone that I may utterly destroy them, and I will blot out their name from under heaven, and I will make you into a nation great and strong and far more numerous than this."

**9:15** And I turned and came down from the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire up to heaven, and the two tablets were in my two hands.

**9:16** And when I saw that you had sinned before the Lord your God, and had made for yourselves a molten image, and had transgressed from the way which the Lord commanded you,

**9:17** then I took hold of the two tablets and cast them out of my two hands, and shattered them before you.

**9:18** And I made supplication before the Lord a second time, just as at the first, forty days and forty nights; I did not eat bread and I did not drink water, because of all your sins which you had sinned, in doing evil before the Lord God to provoke Him.

**9:19** And I am terrified because of the wrath and the fury, for the Lord was provoked against you to destroy you utterly; yet the Lord listened to me at this time also.

**9:20** And against Aaron He was filled with wrath to destroy him utterly, and I prayed for Aaron also at that time.

**9:21** And your sin which you had made, the calf, I took it and burned it completely in fire, and broke it in pieces, grinding it thoroughly until it became fine; and it became like dust, and I cast the dust into the brook that came down from the mountain.

**9:22** Also at the Burning, and at the Testing, and at the Graves of Craving, you were provoking the Lord.

**9:23** And when the Lord sent you out from Kadesh Barnea, saying, "Go up and inherit the land which I give to you," then you disobeyed the word of the Lord your God, and you did not believe Him, and you did not listen to His voice.

**9:24** You have been disobedient toward the Lord from the day He became known to you.

**9:25** And I made supplication before the Lord forty days and forty nights, as long as I made supplication, for the Lord had spoken to destroy you utterly.

**9:26** And I prayed to God and said, "O Lord, King of the gods, do not utterly destroy Your people and Your portion whom You have redeemed, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt with Your great strength, and with Your mighty hand, and with Your uplifted arm.

**9:27** Remember Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, Your servants, to whom You swore by Yourself; do not look upon the hardness of this people, nor their ungodly deeds, nor their sins,

**9:28** lest those who dwell in the land from which You brought us out speak, saying, 'Because the Lord was not able to bring them into the land of which He spoke to them, and because He hated them, He brought them out into the wilderness to slay them.'

**9:29** Yet these are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt with Your great strength, and with Your mighty hand, and with Your uplifted arm."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek text adds "good" ("this good land") and omits the closing clause found in the Hebrew ("rather it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the Lord is driving them out before you").
- **9:13** — The Greek includes "I have spoken to you once and twice, saying," emphasizing the Lord's repeated warnings; this clause is absent from the Hebrew text.
- **9:22** — Rather than transliterating the Hebrew place names (Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-hattaavah), the Greek translates them by their meanings: "the Burning," "the Testing," and "the Graves of Craving."
- **9:24** — The Greek reads passively, "from the day He became known to you," whereas the Hebrew reads actively, "from the day I knew you."
- **9:26** — The Greek addresses God as "O Lord, King of the gods," corresponding to Hebrew Adonai YHWH.
