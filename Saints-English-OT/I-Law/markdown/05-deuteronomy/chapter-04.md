## Text

**4:1** And now, O Israel, listen to the statutes and the judgments, all that I teach you today to do, that you may live and be multiplied, and enter in and inherit the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, gives to you.

**4:2** You shall not add to the word which I command you, nor shall you take away from it; keep the commandments of the Lord your God, all that I command you today.

**4:3** Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did to Baal-peor; for every man who went after Baal-peor, the Lord your God destroyed him from among us.

**4:4** But you who hold fast to the Lord your God are all alive on this day.

**4:5** See, I have shown you statutes and judgments, just as the Lord commanded me, to do thus in the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**4:6** And you shall keep and do them; for this is your wisdom and your understanding before all the nations, as many as shall hear all these statutes and shall say, "Behold, this great nation is a wise and understanding people."

**4:7** For what great nation is there that has a god drawing near to them, as the Lord our God is in all things for which we call upon Him?

**4:8** And what great nation is there that has righteous statutes and judgments according to all this law which I give to you today?

**4:9** Take heed to yourself and diligently guard your soul, lest you forget all the things which your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life; and you shall instruct your sons and the sons of your sons—the day on which you stood before the Lord our God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when

**4:10** the Lord said to me, "Assemble the people to Me, and let them hear My words, that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live upon the earth, and they shall teach their sons."

**4:11** And you came near and stood beneath the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire up to heaven—darkness, gloom, tempest, a great voice.

**4:12** And the Lord spoke to you out of the midst of the fire a sound of words which you heard, and you saw no likeness, but only a voice.

**4:13** And He declared to you His covenant which He commanded you to perform, the ten words; and He wrote them upon two tablets of stone.

**4:14** And the Lord commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and judgments, that you should do them in the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**4:15** And you shall diligently guard your souls, for you saw no likeness on the day when the Lord spoke to you at Horeb on the mountain out of the midst of the fire,

**4:16** lest you act lawlessly and make for yourselves a carved likeness, any image, the likeness of male or female,

**4:17** the likeness of any beast of those that are upon the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies beneath heaven,

**4:18** the likeness of any creeping thing that creeps upon the earth, the likeness of any fish, of those that are in the waters beneath the earth;

**4:19** and lest, lifting up your eyes to heaven and seeing the sun and the moon and the stars and all the array of heaven, you be led astray and bow down to them and serve them, which the Lord your God has apportioned to all the nations that are beneath heaven.

**4:20** But God has taken you and brought us out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be to Him a people of inheritance, as on this day.

**4:21** And the Lord was angry with me concerning the things spoken by you, and swore that I should not cross over this Jordan, and that I should not enter the land which the Lord your God gives to you as an inheritance.

**4:22** For I am dying in this land, and I am not crossing over the Jordan; but you are crossing over and shall inherit this good land.

**4:23** Take heed to yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of the Lord your God which He made with you, and make for yourselves a carved likeness of any of the things concerning which the Lord your God has given command;

**4:24** for the Lord your God is a devouring fire, a jealous God.

**4:25** And if you have sons and the sons of your sons, and remain a long time upon the land, and act lawlessly and make a carved likeness of anything, and do evil things before the Lord

**4:26** your God, to provoke Him to anger, I call both heaven and earth to witness against you today that you shall utterly perish from the land into which you are crossing over the Jordan to inherit it; you shall not live many days upon it, but shall surely be utterly destroyed.

**4:27** And the Lord shall scatter you among all the nations, and you shall be left few in number among the nations into which the Lord shall lead you.

**4:28** And there you shall serve other gods, the works of men's hands, wood and stone, which shall neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

**4:29** And there you shall seek the Lord our God, and you shall find Him, whenever you seek Him out with all your heart and with all your soul in your affliction.

**4:30** And all these things shall find you at the end of the days, and you shall return to the Lord your God and heed His voice;

**4:31** for the Lord your God is a compassionate God; He will not forsake you nor destroy you, nor will He forget the covenant of your fathers which He swore to them.

**4:32** Inquire of the former days that were before you, since the day that God created man upon the earth, and from one end of heaven to the other end of heaven, whether anything has happened like this great thing, or whether such a thing has been heard of; whether any nation has heard the voice

**4:33** of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as you have heard, and lived;

**4:34** or whether God has attempted to go and take for Himself a nation from the midst of another nation, by trial, and by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand, and by an uplifted arm, and by great visions, according to all that our God did in Egypt before your eyes as you watched—

**4:35** so that you might know that the Lord your God—He is God, and there is no other besides Him.

**4:36** Out of heaven His voice was made audible to instruct you, and upon the earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words out of the midst of the fire.

**4:37** Because He loved your fathers, He also chose their seed after them—even you—and He Himself brought you out of Egypt by His great strength,

**4:38** to utterly destroy nations great and stronger than you before your face, to bring you in, and to give you their land to inherit, as you have it today.

**4:39** And you shall know today, and consider in your mind, that the Lord your God—He is God in heaven above and upon the earth beneath, and there is no other besides Him.

**4:40** And keep His statutes and His commandments, all that I command you today, that it may be well with you and with your sons after you, and that you may live many days upon the land which the Lord your God gives to you all the days.

**4:41** Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun,

**4:42** that the manslayer might flee there, who kills his neighbor unwittingly, not having hated him before yesterday or before the third day; and he shall flee into one of these cities and live:

**4:43** Bezer in the wilderness, in the plain country, for Reuben; and Ramoth in Gilead for Gaddi; and Golan in Bashan for Manasseh.

**4:44** This is the law which Moses set before the sons of Israel;

**4:45** these are the testimonies, and the statutes, and the judgments which Moses spoke to the sons of Israel when they had come out of the land of Egypt,

**4:46** beyond the Jordan, in the valley near the house of Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck down when they had come out of the land of Egypt.

**4:47** And they inherited his land and the land of Gog king of Bashan, two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun,

**4:48** from Aroer, which is on the bank of the brook Arnon, even to Mount Sihon, which is Hermon,

**4:49** all the Arabah beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from Asedoth the hewn.

## Translator Notes

- **4:34** — The Greek reads "great visions" (horamasin megalois), reflecting a Hebrew reading related to sight/visions where the Masoretic Text reads "great terrors."
- **4:43** — "Gaddi" (Gaddei) transliterates the Hebrew gentilic for the Gadites, standing in the Greek as a proper name parallel to Reuben and Manasseh.
- **4:47** — The Greek text explicitly reads "Gog" (Gog) here where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Og."
- **4:48** — The Greek text reads "Mount Sihon" (Seon), matching the spelling of king Sihon in verse 46, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Mount Sion."
- **4:49** — "Asedoth the hewn" (Asedoth ten laxeuten) combines a transliteration of Hebrew Ashdoth with a Greek rendering of Pisgah as "the hewn," corresponding to "the slopes of Pisgah" in the Masoretic Text.
