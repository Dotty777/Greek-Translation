## Text

**29:1** And Moses called all the sons of Israel and said to them, "You have seen all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt before you, to Pharaoh and to his servants and to all his land,

**29:2** the great trials which your eyes have seen, the signs and those great wonders.

**29:3** Yet the Lord God has not given you a heart to know and eyes to see and ears to hear, to this day.

**29:4** And He led you in the wilderness forty years; your garments did not grow old, and your sandals did not wear out from your feet.

**29:5** You did not eat bread, nor did you drink wine or strong drink, that you might know that I am the Lord your God.

**29:6** And you came to this place, and Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to meet you in battle;

**29:7** and we struck them down and took their land, and I gave it as an inheritance to Reuben and to the Gadites and to the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**29:8** Therefore you shall be careful to do all the words of this covenant, that you may understand all that you shall do.

**29:9** All of you stand today before the Lord your God: your tribal leaders and your council of elders and your judges and your officers, every man of Israel,

**29:10** your wives and your offspring, and the sojourner who is in the midst of your camp, from your woodcutter even to your water-carrier,

**29:11** to enter into the covenant of the Lord your God and into His curses, which the Lord your God is making with you today,

**29:12** that He may establish you for Himself as a people, and that He shall be your God, just as He spoke to you, and just as He swore to your fathers, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob.

**29:13** And not with you alone do I make this covenant and this curse,

**29:14** but both with those who are here with us today before the Lord your God, and with those who are not here today.

**29:15** For you know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the midst of the nations, as you passed through.

**29:16** And you saw their abominations and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which are among them;

**29:17** lest there be among you a man or a woman or a family or a tribe whose mind has turned away from the Lord your God to go and serve the gods of those nations; lest there be among you a root springing up in gall and bitterness;

**29:18** and it shall come to pass, when he hears the words of this curse, that he blesses himself in his heart, saying, 'May good things come to me, for I shall walk in the wandering of my heart,' so that the sinner may not destroy the sinless along with himself—

**29:19** God will by no means be willing to pardon him, but then the wrath of the Lord and His jealousy shall blaze forth against that man, and all the curses of this covenant that are written in this book shall cling to him, and the Lord shall blot out his name from under heaven.

**29:20** And the Lord shall separate him for calamity from all the sons of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant that are written in the book of this law.

**29:21** And the next generation shall say—your sons who shall arise after you, and the foreigner who comes from a distant land—and they shall see the plagues of that land and its diseases which the Lord sent upon it,

**29:22** brimstone and scorched salt; all its land shall not be sown, nor shall it sprout, nor shall any green thing spring up upon it, just as Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim were overthrown, which the Lord overthrew in fury and wrath.

**29:23** And all the nations shall say, 'Why has the Lord done thus to this land? What is this great fury of wrath?'

**29:24** And they shall say, 'Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord, the God of their fathers, the things which He covenanted with their fathers when He brought them out of the land of Egypt,

**29:25** and they went and served other gods whom they did not know, and whom He had not allotted to them;

**29:26** and the Lord was angry in fury against that land, to bring upon it according to all the curses that are written in the book of this law;

**29:27** and the Lord removed them from their land in fury and wrath and very great indignation, and cast them out into another land, as it is now.'

**29:28** The secret things belong to the Lord your God, but the things revealed belong to you and to your children forever, to do all the words of this law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:7** — The Greek reads first-person singular "I gave" (where the Masoretic Text has "we gave") and uses the gentilic transliteration Gaddei ("the Gadites") alongside "Reuben."
- **29:18** — The Septuagint renders the sinner's self-blessing as "May good [literally 'holy/favorable'] things come to me" and interprets the closing clause ethically as "so that the sinner may not destroy the sinless along with himself" (where the Hebrew metaphor speaks of sweeping away the watered with the dry).
- **29:28** — The Greek consistently uses second-person plural pronouns ("your God," "to you and to your children"), whereas the Masoretic Text uses first-person plural ("our God," "to us and to our children").
