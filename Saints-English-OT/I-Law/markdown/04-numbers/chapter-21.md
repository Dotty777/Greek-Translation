## Text

**21:1** Now the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who dwelt by the wilderness, heard—for Israel had come by the way of Atharim—and he fought against Israel, and they took captives from among them.

**21:2** So Israel vowed a vow to the Lord and said, "If You deliver this people into my hand, I will devote him and his cities to destruction."

**21:3** And the Lord heard the voice of Israel and delivered the Canaanite into his hand; and he devoted him and his cities to destruction, and they called the name of that place Anathema.

**21:4** Then they departed from Mount Hor by the way to the Red Sea, and went around the land of Edom; and the people became fainthearted along the way.

**21:5** And the people spoke against God and against Moses, saying, "Why have you brought us out of Egypt like this, to slay us in the wilderness? For there is no bread and no water, and our soul loathes this empty bread."

**21:6** So the Lord sent the deadly serpents among the people, and they bit the people; and many people of the sons of Israel died.

**21:7** Therefore the people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned, because we have spoken against the Lord and against you; pray therefore to the Lord, and let Him take away the serpent from us."

**21:8** So Moses prayed to the Lord for the people. And the Lord said to Moses, "Make for yourself a serpent, and set it on a standard; and it shall be that if a serpent bites a man, everyone who is bitten, when he looks upon it, shall live."

**21:9** So Moses made a bronze serpent and set it up on a standard; and it came to pass that whenever a serpent bit a man, and he looked upon the bronze serpent, he lived.

**21:10** Then the sons of Israel departed and encamped in Oboth.

**21:11** And departing from Oboth, they encamped at Chalgaei on the other side, in the wilderness that is opposite Moab, toward the rising of the sun.

**21:12** From there they departed, and encamped in the Valley of Zared.

**21:13** Departing from there, they encamped on the other side of the Arnon in the wilderness that extends from the borders of the Amorites; for the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorite.

**21:14** Therefore it is said in the book, War of the Lord, "He set Zoob ablaze, and the torrents of Arnon;

**21:15** and He appointed the torrents to settle Ar, and it lies adjacent to the borders of Moab."

**21:16** And from there to the well; this is the well of which the Lord spoke to Moses, "Gather the people together, and I will give them water to drink."

**21:17** Then Israel sang this song at the well: "Lead the song to it:

**21:18** A well! Princes dug it, kings of the nations hewed it out, in their reign, when they held dominion." And from the well to Manthanain;

**21:20** and from Manthanain to Manael; and from Manael to Bamoth; and from Bamoth to Janen, which is in the plain of Moab, from the summit of the hewn height that looks out toward the face of the wilderness.

**21:21** Then Moses sent envoys to Sihon, king of the Amorites, with words of peace, saying, "We will pass through your land; we will travel along the road; we will not turn aside either into a field or into a vineyard,

**21:22** we will not drink water from your well; we will travel by the royal highway until we have passed through your borders."

**21:23** But Sihon did not grant Israel to pass through his borders; instead Sihon gathered all his people together and went out into the wilderness to do battle against Israel, and he came to Jahaz and arrayed himself in battle against Israel.

**21:24** And Israel struck him with the slaughter of the sword, and they took dominion over his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the sons of Ammon; for Jazer is the border of the sons of Ammon.

**21:25** So Israel took all these cities; and Israel dwelt in all the cities of the Amorites, in Heshbon and in all those adjoining it.

**21:26** For Heshbon is the city of Sihon, king of the Amorites; and he had fought against the king of Moab formerly, and they took all his land from Aroer as far as the Arnon.

**21:27** Therefore the speakers of riddles say, "Come to Heshbon, that the city of Sihon may be built and established.

**21:28** For fire went forth from Heshbon, a flame from the city of Sihon; and it devoured as far as Moab, and swallowed up the pillars of Arnon.

**21:29** Woe to you, Moab! You have perished, O people of Chemosh! Their sons were given up to flee for safety, and their daughters as captives, to Sihon, king of the Amorites.

**21:30** And their seed shall perish, Heshbon as far as Dibon, and the women have yet further kindled a fire against Moab."

**21:31** Thus Israel dwelt in all the cities of the Amorites.

**21:32** And Moses sent to spy out Jazer, and they captured it and its villages, and drove out the Amorite who dwelt there.

**21:33** Then they turned and went up by the way to Bashan; and Og, king of Bashan, went out to meet them, and all his people, to battle at Edrei.

**21:34** And the Lord said to Moses, "Do not fear him, for I have delivered him into your hands, and all his people and all his land; and you shall do to him just as you did to Sihon, king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon."

**21:35** So he struck him and his sons and all his people, until no survivor of his was left; and they inherited their land.

## Translator Notes

- **21:3** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew place name Hormah as "Anathema," signifying something devoted to destruction.
- **21:11** — "Chalgaei on the other side" preserves the Septuagint's distinct proper name (Chalgaei) corresponding to the Hebrew Iye-abarim.
- **21:14** — "He set Zoob ablaze" preserves the Septuagint's distinct proper name (Zoob) and verbal rendering of a difficult poetic line (Hebrew Masoretic Text: "Waheb in Suphah").
- **21:18** — The Septuagint renders the Song of the Well as "kings of the nations hewed it out, in their reign, when they held dominion" (Masoretic Text: "nobles of the people, with the scepter and with their staffs"). Verse 21:19 is merged into 21:20 in the Greek text, which preserves the distinct Septuagint names Manthanain, Manael, and Janen (for Hebrew Mattanah, Nahaliel, and "the valley") along with "the hewn height" for Pisgah.
- **21:24** — The Septuagint reads "Jazer" as the border of the sons of Ammon where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "strong." Likewise, in 21:26 the Septuagint reads "from Aroer" (Masoretic Text: "from his hand"), and in 21:30 it reads "the women have yet further kindled a fire against Moab."
- **21:20 *(additional)*** — Matches the opening clause of 21:21 in the Greek critical text (reading preabeis as an orthographic slip for presbeis, 'envoys').
