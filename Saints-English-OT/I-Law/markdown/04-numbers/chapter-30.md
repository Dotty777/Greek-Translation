## Text

**30:1** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded Moses.

**30:2** And Moses spoke to the leaders of the tribes of Israel, saying, "This is the word which the Lord has commanded:

**30:3** Any man who vows a vow to the Lord, or swears an oath with a binding pledge, or binds himself concerning his soul, shall not profane his word; he shall do all that proceeds out of his mouth.

**30:4** And if a woman vows a vow to the Lord, or binds herself by a pledge in the house of her father in her youth,

**30:5** and her father hears her vows and her pledges which she has bound upon her soul, and her father holds his peace toward her, then all her vows shall stand, and all the pledges which she has bound upon her soul shall remain upon her.

**30:6** But if her father expressly forbids her on the day that he hears all her vows and the pledges which she has bound upon her soul, they shall not stand; and the Lord shall cleanse her, because her father forbade her.

**30:7** And if she indeed becomes married to a husband, while her vows are upon her according to the declaration of her lips, which she has bound upon her soul,

**30:8** and her husband hears of it, and holds his peace toward her on the day that he hears, then thus all her vows shall stand, and her pledges which she has bound upon her soul shall stand.

**30:9** But if her husband expressly forbids her on the day that he hears, all her vows and her pledges which she has bound upon her soul shall not remain, because her husband has refused her; and the Lord shall cleanse her.

**30:10** And the vow of a widow or of a divorced woman, whatever she vows upon her soul, shall remain upon her.

**30:11** And if her vow, or the pledge upon her soul with an oath, is in the house of her husband,

**30:12** and her husband hears of it, and holds his peace toward her and does not forbid her, then all her vows shall stand, and all her pledges which she has bound upon her soul shall stand against her.

**30:13** But if her husband completely annuls them on the day that he hears, all that proceeds out of her lips concerning her vows and concerning the pledges upon her soul shall not remain upon her; her husband has annulled them, and the Lord shall cleanse her.

**30:14** Every vow and every oath of a bond to afflict the soul, her husband may establish for her, and her husband may annul it.

**30:15** And if he altogether holds his peace toward her from day to day, then he shall establish for her all her vows, and the pledges that are upon her he shall establish for her, because he held his peace toward her on the day that he heard.

**30:16** But if he completely annuls them for her after the day that he heard, then he shall bear his sin."

**30:17** These are the ordinances which the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, and between a father and a daughter in her youth in her father's house.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — Following the Septuagint division, this verse opens chapter 30, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and many English Bibles number it as 29:40.
- **30:6** — Greek kathariei (also in vv. 9 and 13) literally means "shall cleanse" or "clear," signifying that the Lord absolves the woman from obligation rather than the Hebrew "forgive."
- **30:16** — The Greek text reads ten hamartian autou ("his sin"), attributing the guilt to the husband who annuls the vow after the day he heard it, whereas the Hebrew reads "her iniquity."
