## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Send out men for yourself, and let them spy out the land of the Canaanites, which I am giving to the sons of Israel for a possession; one man from each tribe, sending them according to the clans of their fathers' houses, every one a leader among them."

**13:3** So Moses sent them forth from the wilderness of Paran by the voice of the Lord; all these men were leaders of the sons of Israel.

**13:4** And these are their names: of the tribe of Reuben, Samuel the son of Zaccur;

**13:5** of the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat the son of Suri;

**13:6** of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh;

**13:7** of the tribe of Issachar, Ilaal the son of Joseph;

**13:8** of the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Nun;

**13:9** of the tribe of Benjamin, Palti the son of Raphu;

**13:10** of the tribe of Zebulun, Gudiel the son of Sodi;

**13:11** of the tribe of Joseph, of the sons of Manasseh, Gaddi the son of Susi;

**13:12** of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the son of Gamai;

**13:13** of the tribe of Asher, Sethur the son of Michael;

**13:14** of the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi the son of Jabi;

**13:15** of the tribe of Gad, Gudiel the son of Machi.

**13:16** These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses gave Hoshea the son of Nun the name Joshua.

**13:17** Then Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, and said to them, "Go up by this wilderness, and you shall go up into the mountain;

**13:18** and you shall see the land, what it is, and the people who dwell upon it, whether they are stronger or weak, or whether they are few or many;

**13:19** and what the land is in which they dwell, whether it is good or bad; and what the cities are in which they abide, whether in walled cities or in unwalled ones;

**13:20** and what the land is, whether rich or exhausted, whether there are trees in it or not. And being steadfast, you shall take from the fruits of the land." Now the days were the days of spring, the firstfruits of the grape.

**13:21** So they went up and spied out the land from the wilderness of Zin to Rehob, at the entrance of Ephaath.

**13:22** And they went up through the wilderness and came to Hebron; and there were Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak. Now Hebron was built seven years before Tanis of Egypt.

**13:23** And they came to the Valley of the Cluster and spied it out; and from there they cut off vine branches and one cluster of grapes upon it, and they carried it on poles, along with some of the pomegranates and of the cucumbers.

**13:24** That place they named Valley of the Cluster, because of the cluster which the sons of Israel cut off from there.

**13:25** And after spying out the land, they returned from there after forty days.

**13:26** And they departed and came to Moses and Aaron and to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, into the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh; and they brought back word to them and to all the congregation, and showed the fruit of the land.

**13:27** And they recounted it to him and said, "We came into the land to which you sent us, a land flowing with milk and honey, and this is its fruit;

**13:28** yet the nation that dwells upon it is bold, and the cities are strong, walled, and exceedingly great; and we saw the descendants of Anak there;

**13:29** and Amalek dwells in the land toward the south, and the Hittite and the Hivite and the Jebusite and the Amorite dwell in the hill country, and the Canaanite dwells by the sea."

**13:30** Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said to him, "No, rather we shall surely go up and inherit it, for we are indeed able to prevail against them."

**13:31** But the men who had gone up with him said, "We are not going up, for we are by no means able to go up against the nation, for it is far stronger than we."

**13:32** And they brought dismay concerning the land which they had spied out to the sons of Israel, saying, "The land through which we passed to spy it out is a land that devours those who dwell upon it; all the people whom we saw in it are men of exceeding stature there;

**13:33** and there we saw the giants, and we were before them like locusts; and indeed so we were before them."

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — Several names in verses 4-15 reflect distinct Greek forms compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text: Samuel (13:4, MT Shammua), Suri (13:5, MT Hori), Ilaal (13:7, MT Igal), Gudiel in both 13:10 and 13:15 (MT Gaddiel and Geuel), Gamai (13:12, MT Gemalli), and Jabi (13:14, MT Vophsi).
- **13:21** — The Greek text reads "Ephaath" (Ephaath) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Hamath."
- **13:22** — The Septuagint uses the Greek geographical name "Tanis" (Tanin) for the Egyptian city called "Zoan" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:23** — The Greek translates Eshcol as "Valley of the Cluster" (Pharanx botryos) and reads "cucumbers" (sikyōn, an ancient Septuagint reading) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "figs."
- **13:32** — The Greek reads "dismay" (ekstasin) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a bad report," and in 13:33 it repeats "before them" twice rather than contrasting "in our own sight" with "in their sight."
