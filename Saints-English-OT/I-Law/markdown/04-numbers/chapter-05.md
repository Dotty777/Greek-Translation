## Text

**5:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:2** "Command the children of Israel, and let them send out from the camp every leper, and everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is unclean because of a dead body;

**5:3** from male to female, send them outside the camp, and they shall by no means defile their camps, in which I dwell among them."

**5:4** And the children of Israel did so, and sent them outside the camp; just as the Lord spoke to Moses, so the children of Israel did.

**5:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:6** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'A man or a woman, whoever commits any of the sins of mankind, and willfully disregards, and that soul surely commits a trespass,

**5:7** he shall confess the sin which he has committed, and he shall restore the trespass, the principal sum, and add its fifth part to it, and give it to the one against whom he trespassed.

**5:8** But if the man has no near kinsman to whom to restore the trespass, the trespass that is restored to the Lord shall belong to the priest, besides the ram of atonement through which he shall make atonement with it on his behalf.

**5:9** And every firstfruits offering according to all the things consecrated among the children of Israel, whatever they offer to the Lord, to the priest, shall belong to him.

**5:10** And each man's consecrated things shall belong to him; whatever a man gives to the priest shall belong to him.'"

**5:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:12** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'If any man's wife goes astray and disregards him in contempt,

**5:13** and a man lies with her carnally, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband and she conceals it, yet she is defiled, and there was no witness with her and she has not been caught;

**5:14** and a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he becomes jealous of his wife, and she is indeed defiled, or a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he becomes jealous of his wife, yet she is not defiled;

**5:15** then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and he shall bring the offering for her, one-tenth of an ephah of barley flour; he shall pour no oil upon it nor put frankincense upon it, for it is an offering of jealousy, an offering of remembrance, bringing sin to remembrance.

**5:16** And the priest shall bring her near, and set her before the Lord;

**5:17** and the priest shall take pure living water in an earthen vessel, and of the earth that is on the floor of the tabernacle of testimony the priest shall take and cast it into the water;

**5:18** and the priest shall set the woman before the Lord, and uncover the head of the woman, and place in her hands the offering of remembrance, the offering of jealousy; and in the hand of the priest shall be this water of conviction that brings a curse.

**5:19** And the priest shall put her under oath and say to the woman, "If no man has lain with you, and if you have not gone astray to be defiled while under your husband, be free from this water of conviction that brings a curse.

**5:20** But if you have gone astray while under your husband, or have been defiled, and some man besides your husband has lain with you—"

**5:21** then the priest shall put the woman under oath with the words of this curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, "May the Lord make you a curse and an oath in the midst of your people, when the Lord causes your thigh to waste away and your belly to swell;

**5:22** and this water that brings a curse shall enter into your inward parts, to make your belly swell and your thigh waste away." And the woman shall say, "So be it, so be it."

**5:23** Then the priest shall write these curses in a book, and blot them out into the water of conviction that brings a curse;

**5:24** and he shall make the woman drink the water of conviction that brings a curse, and the water of conviction that brings a curse shall enter into her.

**5:25** And the priest shall take the offering of jealousy out of the woman's hand, and present the offering before the Lord and bring it to the altar;

**5:26** and the priest shall take a handful from the offering as its memorial portion and offer it up upon the altar, and afterward he shall make the woman drink the water.

**5:27** And it shall be, if she has been defiled and has utterly concealed it from her husband, then the water of conviction that brings a curse shall enter into her, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall waste away, and the woman shall become a curse to her people;

**5:28** but if the woman has not been defiled and is clean, then she shall be free and shall conceive seed.

**5:29** This is the law of jealousy, when a woman goes astray while under her husband and is defiled,

**5:30** or when a spirit of jealousy comes upon a man and he becomes jealous of his wife; then he shall set his wife before the Lord, and the priest shall execute upon her all this law;

**5:31** and the man shall be free from sin, but that woman shall bear her sin.'"

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Greek phrase rendered "because of a dead body" is literally "upon a soul," a Septuagint idiom for ritual defilement caused by a corpse.
- **5:17** — The Septuagint specifies "pure living water," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "holy water."
- **5:18** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of "bitter water," the Septuagint reads "water of conviction," emphasizing legal exposure and proof of guilt.
