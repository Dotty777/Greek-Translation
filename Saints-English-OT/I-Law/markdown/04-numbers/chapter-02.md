## Text

**2:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**2:2** "Every man keeping close by his own, according to division, according to ensigns, according to the houses of their fathers, let the children of Israel encamp; facing round about the tabernacle of the testimony, the children of Israel shall encamp.

**2:3** And those encamping toward the south, toward the east, shall be the division of the camp of Judah with their host, and the ruler of the children of Judah shall be Nahshon the son of Amminadab;

**2:4** his host, those who were numbered, is seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**2:5** And those encamping next to them shall be of the tribe of Issachar, and the ruler of the children of Issachar shall be Nethanel the son of Zuar;

**2:6** his host, those who were numbered, is fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**2:7** And those encamping next to them shall be of the tribe of Zebulun, and the ruler of the children of Zebulun shall be Eliab the son of Helon;

**2:8** their host, those who were numbered, is fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**2:9** All who were numbered from the camp of Judah are one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred, with their host; they shall set out first.

**2:10** The division of the camp of Reuben shall have their host toward the south, and the ruler of the children of Reuben shall be Elizur the son of Shedeur;

**2:11** his host, those who were numbered, is forty-six thousand five hundred.

**2:12** And those encamping next to him shall be of the tribe of Simeon, and the ruler of the children of Simeon shall be Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**2:13** His host, those who were numbered, is fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**2:14** And those encamping next to him shall be the tribe of Gad, and the ruler of the children of Gad shall be Eliasaph the son of Reuel;

**2:15** his host, those who were numbered, is forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**2:16** All who were numbered from the camp of Reuben are one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty, with their host; they shall set out second.

**2:17** And the tabernacle of the testimony shall be taken up, and the camp of the Levites shall be in the midst of the camps; just as they encamp, so also shall they set out, each one in order according to leadership.

**2:18** The division of the camp of Ephraim shall be by the sea with their host, and the ruler of the children of Ephraim shall be Elishama the son of Ammihud.

**2:19** His host, those who were numbered, is forty thousand five hundred.

**2:20** And those encamping next to them shall be of the tribe of Manasseh, and the ruler of the children of Manasseh shall be Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;

**2:21** his host, those who were numbered, is thirty-two thousand four hundred.

**2:22** And those encamping next to them shall be of the tribe of Benjamin, and the ruler of the children of Benjamin shall be Abidan the son of Gideoni;

**2:23** his host, those who were numbered, is thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**2:24** All who were numbered of the camp of Ephraim are one hundred eight thousand one hundred, with their host; they shall set out third.

**2:25** The division of the camp of Dan shall be toward the north with their host, and the ruler of the children of Dan shall be Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;

**2:26** his host, those who were numbered, is sixty-two thousand five hundred.

**2:27** And those encamping next to him shall be of the tribe of Asher, and the ruler of the children of Asher shall be Pagiel the son of Ocran;

**2:28** his host, those who were numbered, is forty-one thousand five hundred.

**2:29** And those encamping next to them shall be of the tribe of Naphtali, and the ruler of the children of Naphtali shall be Ahira the son of Enan;

**2:30** his host, those who were numbered, is fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**2:31** All who were numbered of the camp of Dan are one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred; they shall set out last, according to their division."

**2:32** This is the numbering of the children of Israel according to the houses of their fathers; the whole numbering of the camps with their hosts was six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**2:33** But the Levites were not numbered together among them, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**2:34** And the children of Israel did all that the Lord directed Moses; thus they encamped according to their division, and thus they set out, each keeping in order according to their clans, according to the houses of their fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew phrase "tent of meeting" as "tabernacle of the testimony" (skene tou martyriou) and translates Hebrew degel ("standard") as tagma ("division").
- **2:3** — The Greek reads "toward the south, toward the east" (kata noton kat' anatolas), a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "eastward, toward the sunrise."
- **2:18** — "By the sea" (para thalassan) is the literal Greek rendering of the directional idiom for westward, looking toward the Mediterranean Sea.
- **2:21** — The Septuagint numbers Manasseh at thirty-two thousand four hundred, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads thirty-two thousand two hundred, though the camp subtotal in verse 24 remains one hundred eight thousand one hundred.
- **2:26** — The Septuagint numbers Dan at sixty-two thousand five hundred, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads sixty-two thousand seven hundred; this offsets the extra two hundred in Manasseh (verse 21), preserving the overall total of six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty in verse 32.
