## Text

**23:1** And Balaam said to Balak, "Build for me here seven altars, and prepare for me here seven calves and seven rams."

**23:2** And Balak did just as Balaam had spoken to him, and he offered up a calf and a ram upon the altar.

**23:3** And Balaam said to Balak, "Stand beside your sacrifice, and I will go, to see if God will appear to meet me; and whatever word He shows me, I will declare to you." And Balak stood beside his sacrifice,

**23:4** and Balaam went to inquire of God, and he went straight ahead. And God appeared to Balaam, and Balaam said to Him, "I have prepared the seven altars, and I have offered up a calf and a ram upon the altar."

**23:5** And God put a word into the mouth of Balaam and said, "Return to Balak, and thus you shall speak."

**23:6** And he returned to him, and there he stood beside his burnt offerings, and all the princes of Moab were with him. And the Spirit of God came upon him;

**23:7** and taking up his parable, he said, "From Mesopotamia Balak sent for me, the king of Moab from the mountains of the east, saying, 'Come, curse Jacob for me, and come, invoke a curse upon Israel for me.'

**23:8** Why should I curse him whom the Lord does not curse? Or why should I curse him whom God does not curse?

**23:9** For from the summit of the mountains I shall see him, and from the hills I shall behold him; behold, a people shall dwell alone, and among the nations it shall not be reckoned.

**23:10** Who has accurately calculated the seed of Jacob? And who shall count the peoples of Israel? May my soul die among the souls of the righteous, and may my seed be as the seed of these!"

**23:11** And Balak said to Balaam, "What have you done to me? I summoned you to curse my enemies, and behold, you have blessed them with a blessing!"

**23:12** And Balaam said to Balak, "Whatever God puts into my mouth, shall I not take care to speak this?"

**23:13** And Balak said to him, "Come again with me to another place, from which you shall not see him, except that you shall see a part of him, and all of them you shall by no means see; and curse him for me from there."

**23:14** And he took him to the lookout of a field, to the summit of the Hewn Rock, and he built seven altars there, and offered up a calf and a ram upon the altar.

**23:15** And Balaam said to Balak, "Stand beside your sacrifice, and I will go to inquire of God."

**23:16** And God met Balaam, and put a word into his mouth, and said, "Return to Balak, and thus you shall speak."

**23:17** And he returned to him, and he was standing beside his burnt offering, and all the princes of Moab were with him. And Balak said to him, "What has the Lord spoken?"

**23:18** And taking up his parable, he said, "Arise, Balak, and hear; give ear as a witness, son of Zippor!

**23:19** God is not as a man, to be deceived, nor as a son of man, to be threatened; having spoken, shall He not perform it? Shall He speak, and not abide by it?

**23:20** Behold, I have been commissioned to bless; I will bless, and I will by no means turn back.

**23:21** There shall be no hardship in Jacob, nor shall toil be seen in Israel; the Lord his God is with him, and the glories of rulers are in him.

**23:22** It is God who brought them out from Egypt; as the glory of a unicorn is his.

**23:23** For there is no augury in Jacob, nor divination in Israel; in due season it shall be told to Jacob and to Israel what God shall accomplish.

**23:24** Behold, a people shall rise up as a lion cub, and as a lion it shall exult; it shall not lie down until it devours the prey, and it shall drink the blood of the slain."

**23:25** And Balak said to Balaam, "Neither curse him for me with curses, nor bless him at all."

**23:26** And Balaam answered and said to Balak, "Did I not speak to you, saying, 'The word which God speaks, that I will do'?"

**23:27** And Balak said to Balaam, "Come, let me take you to another place, if it will be pleasing to God, and curse him for me from there."

**23:28** And Balak took Balaam to the summit of Peor, which extends toward the wilderness.

**23:29** And Balaam said to Balak, "Build for me here seven altars, and prepare for me here seven calves and seven rams."

**23:30** And Balak did just as Balaam had spoken to him, and he offered up a calf and a ram upon the altar.

## Translator Notes

- **23:6** — The Septuagint adds "And the Spirit of God came upon him" before Balaam's first oracle, paralleling Numbers 24:2.
- **23:10** — The Septuagint reads "seed" in both halves of the verse—corresponding to "dust" and "end" in the Hebrew—and translates the Hebrew "fourth part" as "peoples."
- **23:14** — The Greek translates the place name Pisgah literally as "the Hewn Rock" (Lelaxeumenou, from a verb meaning to hew stone).
- **23:19** — The Greek renders the parallel infinitives passively as "to be deceived" and "to be threatened," in contrast to the Hebrew "to lie" and "to relent."
- **23:22** — "Unicorn" translates the Greek monokerotos ("one-horned beast"), the standard Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew re'em ("wild ox").
