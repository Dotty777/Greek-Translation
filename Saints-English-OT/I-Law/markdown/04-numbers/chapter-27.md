## Text

**27:1** Then the daughters of Zelophehad the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, of the family of Manasseh of the sons of Joseph, came near; and these were their names: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**27:2** And they stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the rulers, and before all the congregation, at the door of the tabernacle of testimony, saying,

**27:3** "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation that banded together against the Lord in the congregation of Korah; for he died for his own sin, and he had no sons. May the name of our father not be blotted out from the midst of his family because he has no son; give us a possession among our father's brethren."

**27:5** And Moses brought their case before the Lord.

**27:6** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**27:7** "The daughters of Zelophehad have spoken rightly; you shall surely give them as a gift a possession of inheritance among their father's brethren, and you shall transfer the allotment of their father to them. And you shall speak to the sons of Israel.

**27:8** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'If a man dies and has no son, you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter.

**27:9** And if he has no daughter, you shall give the inheritance to his brother.

**27:10** And if he has no brothers, you shall give the inheritance to his father's brother.

**27:11** And if his father has no brothers, you shall give the inheritance to his nearest kinsman from his tribe; he shall inherit what is his.'" And this shall be to the sons of Israel a statute of judgment, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**27:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go up to the mountain that is in the region beyond, this Mount Nebo, and behold the land of Canaan, which I give to the sons of Israel for a possession.

**27:13** And you shall see it, and you also shall be gathered to your people, just as Aaron your brother was gathered on Mount Hor,

**27:14** because you transgressed My word in the Wilderness of Zin, when the congregation contended, to sanctify Me—you did not sanctify Me at the water before them." This is the water of Contention at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin.

**27:15** Then Moses spoke to the Lord, saying,

**27:16** "Let the Lord, the God of the spirits and of all flesh, appoint a man over this congregation,

**27:17** who shall go out before them and who shall come in before them, and who shall lead them out and who shall bring them in, and the congregation of the Lord shall not be like sheep that have no shepherd."

**27:18** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Take to yourself Joshua the son of Nun, a man who has the Spirit in him, and you shall lay your hands on him;

**27:19** and you shall set him before Eleazar the priest, and you shall charge him before all the congregation, and you shall give a charge concerning him in their sight;

**27:20** and you shall bestow some of your glory upon him, so that the sons of Israel may listen to him.

**27:21** And he shall stand before Eleazar the priest, and they shall inquire of him for the judgment of the Manifestations before the Lord; at his word they shall go out, and at his word they shall come in, he and the sons of Israel with one accord, and all the congregation."

**27:22** So Moses did just as the Lord commanded him; and taking Joshua, he set him before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation.

**27:23** And he laid his hands on him and commissioned him, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Greek text includes the petition traditionally numbered as verse 4 directly within verse 3, leaving no separate verse 4.
- **27:7** — The Greek text repeats the clause "and you shall speak to the sons of Israel" across the boundary of verses 7 and 8.
- **27:12** — Where the Hebrew text reads "Mount Abarim," the Greek renders the phrase as "the mountain that is in the region beyond" and explicitly identifies it as "this Mount Nebo."
- **27:16** — The Greek text reads "of the spirits and of all flesh," including "and" between the two phrases.
- **27:21** — Greek "ton delon" ("the Manifestations" or "the Clear Signs") translates the Hebrew Urim, and the Greek uses a plural verb ("they shall inquire of him").
