## Text

**8:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to Aaron,

**8:2** and you shall say to him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light from the side in front of the lampstand.'"

**8:3** And Aaron did so; on the one side in front of the lampstand he kindled its lamps.

**8:4** And this was the construction of the lampstand: solid gold; its shaft and its lilies were wholly solid. According to the form which the Lord showed Moses, so he made the lampstand.

**8:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:6** "Take the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, and you shall purify them.

**8:7** And thus you shall perform their purification for them: you shall sprinkle water of purification upon them, and a razor shall pass over all their body, and they shall wash their garments, and they shall be clean.

**8:8** And they shall take one calf from the herd and its offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and you shall take a year-old calf from the herd for a sin offering.

**8:9** And you shall present the Levites before the tabernacle of the testimony, and you shall assemble all the congregation of the sons of Israel.

**8:10** And you shall present the Levites before the Lord, and the sons of Israel shall lay their hands upon the Levites.

**8:11** And Aaron shall set apart the Levites as a gift before the Lord from the sons of Israel, and they shall be appointed to perform the works of the Lord.

**8:12** And the Levites shall lay their hands upon the heads of the calves, and he shall offer the one for a sin offering and the one for a burnt offering to the Lord, to make atonement for them.

**8:13** And you shall set the Levites before the Lord, before Aaron and before his sons, and you shall present them as a gift before the Lord.

**8:14** And you shall separate the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, and they shall be Mine.

**8:15** And after these things the Levites shall enter to perform the works of the tabernacle of the testimony; and you shall cleanse them, and present them before the Lord.

**8:16** For as a gift these have been given to Me from the midst of the sons of Israel; in place of all the firstborn who open every womb from among the sons of Israel, I have taken them for Myself.

**8:17** For every firstborn among the sons of Israel is Mine, from men even to livestock; on the day when I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified them for Myself.

**8:18** And I have taken the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel.

**8:19** And I have presented the Levites as a gift given to Aaron and to his sons from the midst of the sons of Israel, to perform the works of the sons of Israel in the tabernacle of the testimony and to make atonement for the sons of Israel; and there shall not be among the sons of Israel anyone drawing near to the holy things."

**8:20** And Moses and Aaron and all the congregation of the sons of Israel did to the Levites just as the Lord commanded Moses concerning the Levites; so the sons of Israel did to them.

**8:21** And the Levites purified themselves and washed their garments; and Aaron presented them as a gift before the Lord, and Aaron made atonement for them to purify them.

**8:22** And after these things the Levites went in to perform their ministry in the tabernacle of the testimony before Aaron and before his sons; just as the Lord directed Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them.

**8:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:24** "This is what pertains to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward they shall enter to work in the tabernacle of the testimony;

**8:25** and from fifty years old he shall withdraw from the ministry and shall no longer work,

**8:26** and his brother shall minister in the tabernacle of the testimony to keep the charges, but he shall not perform work. Thus you shall do to the Levites in their charges."

## Translator Notes

- **8:8** — The Greek specifies "a year-old calf" (μόσχον ἐνιαύσιον) for the sin offering, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a second young bull."
- **8:11** — Throughout this passage (vv. 11, 13, 16, 19, 21), the Septuagint renders the Hebrew wave-offering terminology as setting apart or presenting the Levites as a "gift" (ἀπόδομα) before the Lord.
- **8:19** — The Greek reads "there shall not be among the sons of Israel anyone drawing near to the holy things" (προσεγγίζων πρὸς τὰ ἅγια), without the Masoretic Text's reference to a plague.
- **8:26** — The Greek makes "his brother" (ὁ ἀδελφὸς αὐτοῦ) the nominative subject who ministers in the tabernacle of the testimony, whereas the Masoretic Text states that the retired Levite may assist his brothers.
