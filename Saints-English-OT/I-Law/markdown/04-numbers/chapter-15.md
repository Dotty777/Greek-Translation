## Text

**15:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Speak to the children of Israel,

**15:2** and you shall say to them, 'When you enter the land of your habitation, which I am giving to you,

**15:3** and you shall offer burnt offerings to the Lord, a whole burnt offering or a sacrifice, to magnify a vow or as a freewill offering, or in your feasts, to make a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord, whether from the cattle or from the sheep;

**15:4** then he who brings his gift to the Lord shall present an offering of fine flour, a tenth of an ephah, mixed with oil, a fourth of a hin;

**15:5** and wine for a drink offering, a fourth of a hin, you shall prepare with the burnt offering or with the sacrifice; for each lamb you shall prepare this much, an offering made by fire, a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord.

**15:6** And for a ram, when you offer it either for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice, you shall prepare an offering of fine flour, two-tenths, mixed with oil, a third of a hin;

**15:7** and wine for a drink offering, a third of a hin, you shall present for a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord.

**15:8** And if you offer from the cattle for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice, to magnify a vow as a peace offering to the Lord,

**15:9** then he shall present with the calf an offering of fine flour, three-tenths, mixed with oil, half a hin,

**15:10** an offering made by fire, a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord.

**15:11** Thus you shall do for each calf, or for each ram, or for each lamb from the sheep or from the goats;

**15:12** according to the number that you prepare, so you shall do for each one according to their number.

**15:13** Everyone who is native-born shall do such things in this manner, to present offerings made by fire for a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord.

**15:14** And if a proselyte joins you in your land, or whoever is among you throughout your generations, and shall prepare an offering made by fire, a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord, in the manner that you do, so shall the congregation do to the Lord.

**15:15** There shall be one law for you and for the proselytes who are joined among you, an everlasting law throughout your generations; as you are, so also shall the proselyte be before the Lord.

**15:16** There shall be one law and one ordinance also for the proselyte who is joined among you.'"

**15:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:18** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'When you come into the land to which I am bringing you,

**15:19** then it shall be, when you eat of the bread of the land, that you shall set aside a contribution, a separated offering to the Lord;

**15:20** the firstfruits of your dough, a loaf as a contribution, you shall set it apart; as a contribution from the threshing floor, so shall you set it aside,

**15:21** the firstfruits of your dough; and you shall give to the Lord a contribution throughout your generations.

**15:22** And when you err and do not perform all these commandments which the Lord spoke to Moses,

**15:23** just as the Lord commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day that the Lord commanded you and onward throughout your generations;

**15:24** then it shall be, if it is done unintentionally out of the sight of the congregation, that all the congregation shall offer one calf from the cattle without blemish for a burnt offering, for a sweet-smelling aroma to the Lord, and its offering and its drink offering according to the ordinance, and one kid from the goats for a sin offering.

**15:25** And the priest shall make atonement for it, for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them, for it is unintentional; and they have brought their gift, an offering made by fire to the Lord, for their sin before the Lord, for their unintentional errors.

**15:26** And it shall be forgiven throughout all the congregation of the children of Israel and to the proselyte who is joined to you, because for all the people it was unintentional.

**15:27** And if one soul sins unintentionally, he shall bring one female goat a year old for a sin offering;

**15:28** and the priest shall make atonement for the soul who erred unintentionally and sinned unintentionally before the Lord, to make atonement for him.

**15:29** For the native among the children of Israel and for the proselyte who is joined among them, there shall be one law for them, for whoever acts unintentionally.

**15:30** And the soul who acts with a hand of arrogance, whether from the native-born or from the proselytes, that one provokes God; that soul shall be utterly destroyed from among his people.

**15:31** Because he has despised the words of the Lord and has broken His commandments, that soul shall surely be blotted out; his sin is upon him.'"

**15:32** And the children of Israel were in the wilderness, and they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.

**15:33** And those who found him gathering wood on the Sabbath day brought him to Moses and Aaron, and to all the congregation of the children of Israel.

**15:34** And they placed him under guard, for they had not determined what they should do to him.

**15:35** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "The man shall surely be put to death; stone him with stones, all the congregation."

**15:36** And all the congregation brought him outside the camp; and all the congregation stoned him with stones outside the camp, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**15:37** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**15:38** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, and let them make for themselves fringes on the corners of their garments throughout their generations; and you shall put upon the fringes of the corners a cord of blue.

**15:39** And it shall be for you in the fringes, and you shall look upon them and remember all the commandments of the Lord and perform them, and you shall not be turned aside after your thoughts and eyes, after which you play the harlot; so that you may remember and perform all My commandments,

**15:40** and you shall be holy to your God.

**15:41** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God; I am the Lord your God."

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The Greek reads "to magnify a vow" (also in 15:8), rendering the Hebrew idiom for fulfilling a special vow, and uses "burnt offerings" where the Masoretic Text has "an offering made by fire."
- **15:10** — Following the Greek critical text, this verse omits the clause specifying half a hin of wine for the drink offering that appears in the Masoretic Text and some manuscripts.
- **15:14** — The Greek places "the congregation" at the close of verse 14 ("so shall the congregation do to the Lord") rather than at the opening of verse 15 as in the Masoretic Text.
- **15:30** — The Greek reads "with a hand of arrogance" and "provokes God," corresponding to "with a high hand" and "blasphemes the LORD" in the Masoretic Text.
- **15:39** — In the Greek verse division, "so that you may remember and perform all My commandments" concludes verse 39 rather than beginning verse 40, and Greek "thoughts" corresponds to "heart" in the Masoretic Text.
