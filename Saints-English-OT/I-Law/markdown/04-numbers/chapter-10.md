## Text

**10:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Make for yourself two trumpets;

**10:2** of hammered silver you shall make them for yourself, and they shall be for you to summon the congregation and to move the camps.

**10:3** And you shall sound them, and all the congregation shall be gathered to the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**10:4** But if they sound with one, all the rulers, the leaders of Israel, shall come to you.

**10:5** And you shall sound an alarm, and the camps that encamp toward the east shall set out.

**10:6** And you shall sound a second alarm, and the camps that encamp toward the south shall set out; and you shall sound a third alarm, and the camps that encamp by the sea shall set out; and you shall sound a fourth alarm, and the camps that encamp toward the north shall set out; they shall sound with an alarm at their setting out.

**10:7** Yet when you gather the congregation together, you shall sound the trumpets, and not with an alarm.

**10:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall sound the trumpets; and it shall be to you an everlasting ordinance throughout your generations.

**10:9** And if you go out to war in your land against the adversaries who oppose you, then you shall sound an alarm with the trumpets, and you shall be remembered before the Lord, and you shall be delivered from your enemies.

**10:10** Also in the days of your gladness, and in your feasts, and at your new moons, you shall sound the trumpets over your whole burnt offerings and over the sacrifices of your deliverance; and it shall be a memorial for you before your God: I am the Lord your God."

**10:11** Now it came to pass in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, that the cloud ascended from the tabernacle of testimony.

**10:12** And the sons of Israel set out with their belongings in the wilderness of Sinai; and the cloud stood still in the wilderness of Paran.

**10:13** And they first set out by the voice of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

**10:14** And the division of the camp of the sons of Judah set out first with their host, and over their host was Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

**10:15** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel the son of Zuar.

**10:16** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun was Eliab the son of Helon.

**10:17** And they shall take down the tabernacle, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari shall set out, carrying the tabernacle.

**10:18** And the division of the camp of Reuben set out with their host, and over their host was Elizur the son of Shedeur.

**10:19** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Simeon was Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**10:20** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Gad was Eliasaph the son of Reuel.

**10:21** And the sons of Kohath shall set out, carrying the holy things; and they shall set up the tabernacle before they arrive.

**10:22** And the division of the camp of Ephraim shall set out with their host; over their host was Elishama the son of Ammihud.

**10:23** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh was Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

**10:24** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin was Abidan the son of Gideoni.

**10:25** And the division of the camp of the sons of Dan shall set out, the last of all the camps, with their host; and over their host was Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

**10:26** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Asher was Pagiel the son of Ocran.

**10:27** And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali was Ahira the son of Enan.

**10:28** These were the armies of the sons of Israel, and they set out with their host.

**10:29** Then Moses said to Hobab the son of Reuel the Midianite, the father-in-law of Moses, "We are setting out for the place of which the Lord said, 'This I will give to you.' Come with us, and we will do good to you, for the Lord has spoken good things concerning Israel."

**10:30** And he said to him, "I will not go, but only to my land and to my kindred."

**10:31** And he said, "Do not forsake us, because you were with us in the wilderness, and you shall be an elder among us;

**10:32** and it shall be, if you go with us, that whatever good things the Lord shall bestow upon us, we also will do good to you."

**10:33** So they set out from the mountain of the Lord a journey of three days; and the ark of the covenant of the Lord went ahead before them a journey of three days, to search out a resting place for them.

**10:34** And it came to pass, when the ark set out, that Moses said, "Arise, O Lord, let Your enemies be scattered, and let all who hate You flee!"

**10:35** And when it rested, he said, "Return, O Lord, the thousands and ten thousands in Israel."

**10:36** And the cloud became a shade over them by day, and when they set out from the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — The Greek text preserves trumpet alarms for all four cardinal directions (east, south, west by the sea, and north), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions only the east and south.
- **10:17** — Here and in verses 21, 22, and 25, the Greek shifts from past narrative verbs to the future tense ("shall take down," "shall set out"), reflecting the prescribed marching order.
- **10:31** — The Greek reads "because you were with us in the wilderness, and you shall be an elder among us," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "since you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you shall be eyes for us."
- **10:34** — In the Greek text, Moses's prayers at the ark's setting out and resting appear in verses 34-35, followed by the cloud shading the camp in verse 36 (reversing the Hebrew Masoretic order of verses 34 and 35-36).
