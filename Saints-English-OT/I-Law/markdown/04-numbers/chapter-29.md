## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek hemera semasias literally means "a day of signaling," referring to the ceremonial sounding of trumpets on the first day of the seventh month.
- **29:6** — Greek synkrisis (translated "ordinance" throughout this chapter) is the Septuagint rendering for the prescribed ritual regulation or standard.
- **29:11** — The Greek text includes "to make atonement for you" and concludes with "according to the ordinance, for a sweet aroma, an offering made by fire to the Lord," where the Hebrew tradition is shorter.
- **29:35** — Greek exodion denotes a solemn closing festival or assembly concluding the seven-day Feast of Tabernacles.
- **29:39** — Greek soteria literally means "offerings of deliverance" (or "salvation offerings"), the Septuagint equivalent for peace or fellowship offerings.
