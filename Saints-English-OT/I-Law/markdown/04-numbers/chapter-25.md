## Text

**25:1** Now Israel lodged in Shittim; and the people were profaned to commit harlotry with the daughters of Moab.

**25:2** And they invited them to the sacrifices of their idols, and the people ate of their sacrifices and bowed down to their idols,

**25:3** and Israel was initiated into Baal-Peor; and the Lord was angry in wrath against Israel.

**25:4** And the Lord said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and make a public example of them to the Lord before the sun, and the wrath of the Lord shall be turned away from Israel."

**25:5** And Moses said to the tribes of Israel, "Let each man slay his kinsman who has been initiated into Baal-Peor."

**25:6** And behold, a man of the sons of Israel came and brought his brother to the Midianite woman before Moses and before all the congregation of the sons of Israel, while they were weeping beside the door of the tabernacle of testimony.

**25:7** And when Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from the midst of the congregation, and taking a spear in his hand,

**25:8** he went in after the Israelite man into the vaulted chamber, and pierced through both of them, both the Israelite man and the woman through her womb; and the plague ceased from the sons of Israel.

**25:9** And those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.

**25:10** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:11** "Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has caused My wrath to cease from the sons of Israel, in that he was zealous with My zeal among them, so that I did not consume the sons of Israel in My zeal.

**25:12** Thus say: 'Behold, I give to him a covenant of peace,

**25:13** and it shall be to him and to his seed after him a covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel.'"

**25:14** Now the name of the Israelite man who was struck down, who was struck down with the Midianite woman, was Zimri the son of Salmon, a ruler of a father's house of the Simeonites.

**25:15** And the name of the Midianite woman who was struck down was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur, ruler of the nation of Ommoth; he is of a father's house of the Midianites.

**25:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying,

**25:17** 'Be hostile toward the Midianites and strike them down,

**25:18** for they themselves are hostile toward you in deceit, in all the ways they act deceitfully toward you because of Peor, and because of Cozbi, the daughter of a ruler of Midian, their sister, who was struck down in the day of the plague because of Peor.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — The Septuagint reads "were profaned," reflecting a different sense of the Hebrew verb root than the Masoretic Text's "began."
- **25:6** — The Septuagint literally reads "brought his brother to the Midianite woman," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "brought to his brethren a Midianite woman."
- **25:8** — The Greek word translated "vaulted chamber" literally denotes a domed furnace or kiln, used here for a vaulted alcove or tent-chamber.
- **25:14** — The Septuagint gives the father's name as "Salmon," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Salu."
- **25:15** — The Septuagint treats the Hebrew plural noun for "peoples" or "tribes" as the proper name "Ommoth."
