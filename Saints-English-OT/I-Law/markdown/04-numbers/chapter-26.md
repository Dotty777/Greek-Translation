## Text

**26:1** And it came to pass after the plague that the Lord spoke to Moses and to Eleazar the priest, saying,

**26:2** "Take the sum of all the congregation of the sons of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, according to the houses of their fathers, every one who goes forth to battle in Israel."

**26:3** And Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke in Araboth of Moab, by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,

**26:4** "From twenty years old and upward, just as the Lord commanded Moses—you and the sons of Israel who came out of Egypt."

**26:5** Reuben was the firstborn of Israel. Now the sons of Reuben were: Hanoch, and the family of Hanoch; of Pallu, the family of the Palluites;

**26:6** of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites; of Carmi, the family of the Carmites.

**26:7** These are the families of Reuben; and their numbering was forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**26:8** And the sons of Pallu were Eliab.

**26:9** And the sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. These were chosen men of the congregation; these are the ones who rose up against Moses and Aaron in the assembly of Korah, in the uprising against the Lord;

**26:10** and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and Korah in his assembly, when the fire devoured the two hundred and fifty, and they became a sign.

**26:11** Yet the sons of Korah did not die.

**26:12** And the sons of Simeon—the family of the sons of Simeon: of Nemuel, the family of the Nemuelites; of Jamin, the family of the Jaminites; of Jachin, the family of the Jachinites;

**26:13** of Zerah, the family of the Zerahites; of Shaul, the family of the Shaulites.

**26:14** These are the families of Simeon from their numbering: twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**26:15** Now the sons of Judah were Er and Onan; and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

**26:16** And the sons of Judah according to their families were: of Shelah, the family of the Shelanites; of Perez, the family of Perez; of Zerah, the family of the Zerahites.

**26:17** And the sons of Perez were: of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites; of Jamun, the family of the Jamunites.

**26:18** These are the families of Judah according to their numbering: seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**26:19** And the sons of Issachar according to their families were: of Tola, the family of the Tolaites; of Puah, the family of the Puaites;

**26:20** of Jashub, the family of the Jashubites; of Samaram, the family of the Samaranites.

**26:21** These are the families of Issachar from their numbering: sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26:22** The sons of Zebulun according to their families were: of Sered, the family of the Seredites; of Elon, the family of the Elonites; of Jahleel, the family of the Jahleelites.

**26:23** These are the families of Zebulun from their numbering: sixty thousand five hundred.

**26:24** The sons of Gad according to their families were: of Zephon, the family of the Zephonites; of Haggi, the family of the Haggites; of Shuni, the family of the Shunites;

**26:25** of Ozni, the family of the Oznites; of Addi, the family of the Addites;

**26:26** of Arodi, the family of the Arodites; of Ariel, the family of the Arielites.

**26:27** These are the families of the sons of Gad from their numbering: forty-four thousand five hundred.

**26:28** The sons of Asher according to their families were: of Jamin, the family of the Jaminites; of Ishvi, the family of the Ishvites; of Beriah, the family of the Beriites;

**26:29** of Heber, the family of the Heberites; of Melliel, the family of the Mellielites.

**26:30** And the name of the daughter of Asher was Kara.

**26:31** These are the families of Asher from their numbering: forty-three thousand six hundred.

**26:32** The sons of Joseph according to their families were Manasseh and Ephraim.

**26:33** The sons of Manasseh were: of Machir, the family of the Machirites; and Machir fathered Gilead; of Gilead, the family of the Gileadites.

**26:34** And these are the sons of Gilead: Ahiezer, the family of the Ahiezerites; of Helek, the family of the Helekites;

**26:35** of Asriel, the family of the Asrielites; of Shechem, the family of the Shechemites;

**26:36** of Symaer, the family of the Symaerites; and Hepher, the family of the Hepherites.

**26:37** Now Zelophehad the son of Hepher had no sons, but only daughters; and these are the names of the daughters of Zelophehad: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**26:38** These are the families of Manasseh from their numbering: fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**26:39** And these are the sons of Ephraim: of Shuthelah, the family of the Shuthelahites; of Tanach, the family of the Tanachites.

**26:40** These are the sons of Shuthelah: of Eden, the family of the Edenites.

**26:41** These are the families of Ephraim from their numbering: thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the families of the sons of Joseph according to their families.

**26:42** The sons of Benjamin according to their families were: of Bela, the family of the Belaites; of Ashbel, the family of the Ashbelites; of Jachiran, the family of the Jachiranites;

**26:43** of Shupham, the family of the Shuphamites.

**26:44** And the sons of Bela were Adar and Noemani.

**26:45** These are the sons of Benjamin according to their families from their numbering: thirty-five thousand five hundred.

**26:46** And the sons of Dan according to their families were: of Shami, the family of the Shamites. These are the families of Dan according to their families.

**26:47** All the families of the Shamites according to their numbering were sixty-four thousand six hundred.

**26:48** The sons of Naphtali according to their families were: of Sael, the family of the Saelites; of Guni, the family of the Gunites;

**26:49** of Jezer, the family of the Jezerites; of Shillem, the family of the Shillemites.

**26:50** These are the families of Naphtali from their numbering: thirty thousand three hundred.

**26:51** This was the numbering of the sons of Israel: six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**26:52** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**26:53** "To these the land shall be divided to inherit according to the number of names.

**26:54** To the larger number you shall increase the inheritance, and to the smaller you shall diminish their inheritance; to each one, just as they were numbered, their inheritance shall be given.

**26:55** By lots the land shall be divided according to the names; according to the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit.

**26:56** By the lot you shall divide their inheritance between the many and the few."

**26:57** And the sons of Levi according to their families were: of Gershon, the family of the Gershonites; of Kohath, the family of the Kohathites; of Merari, the family of the Merarites.

**26:58** These are the families of the sons of Levi: the family of the Libnites, the family of the Hebronites, the family of Korah, and the family of the Mushites. And Kohath fathered Amram.

**26:59** And the name of his wife was Jochebed, the daughter of Levi, who bore these to Levi in Egypt; and she bore to Amram Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister.

**26:60** And to Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, and Eleazar and Ithamar.

**26:61** And Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

**26:62** And those from their numbering were twenty-three thousand, every male from a month old and upward; for they were not numbered together in the midst of the sons of Israel, because no portion is given to them in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**26:63** And this was the numbering by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered the sons of Israel in Araboth of Moab, by the Jordan opposite Jericho;

**26:64** and among these there was not a man of those numbered by Moses and Aaron, whom they numbered of the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai;

**26:65** for the Lord had said to him, "They shall surely die in the wilderness." And not even one of them was left, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for plains as the place name Araboth ("Araboth of Moab"). In 26:4, the Greek also uniquely includes "you" ("you and the sons of Israel") in the direct address.
- **26:15** — In the Septuagint census, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun are listed before Gad and Asher, differing from the tribal order in the Hebrew Masoretic Text. The Greek also preserves distinct clan names here: Jamun in 26:17 (Hebrew Hamul) and Samaram in 26:20 (Hebrew Shimron).
- **26:25** — For Gad's clans, the Greek reads Addi (Hebrew Eri) and in 26:26 reads Ariel (Hebrew Areli). In 26:27, the Greek census total for Gad is 44,500, compared with 40,500 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **26:30** — The Greek names Asher's daughter Kara, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Serah. Earlier in Asher's list, the Greek reads Jamin in 26:28 (Hebrew Jimnah) and Melliel in 26:29 (Hebrew Malchiel), without the Masoretic subheading introducing Heber and Malchiel as sons of Beriah.
- **26:31** — The Greek text gives Asher's total as 43,600 (using the Greek alphabetic numeral chi with keraia for 600), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (26:47) records 53,400.
- **26:39** — The Greek lists two sons of Ephraim—Shuthelah and Tanach (Hebrew Tahan)—omitting Becher, and reads Eden in 26:40 (Hebrew Eran). Earlier in Manasseh's list, the Greek also reads Ahiezer in 26:34 (Hebrew Iezer) and Symaer in 26:36 (Hebrew Shemida).
- **26:45** — The Greek gives Benjamin's census total as 35,500, compared with 45,600 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text. In 26:42-44, the Greek also omits Hupham and reads Jachiran (Hebrew Ahiram), Adar (Hebrew Ard), and Noemani (Hebrew Naaman).
- **26:47** — For Dan, the Greek reads the clan name Shami (Hebrew Shuham) and records a total of 64,600 (using the Greek alphabetic numeral chi with keraia for 600), compared with 64,400 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **26:50** — The Greek text records Naphtali's total as 30,300, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 45,400. In 26:48, the Greek also reads Sael where the Hebrew reads Jahzeel.
- **26:59** — The Greek reads actively "who bore these to Levi in Egypt," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads passively "who was born to Levi in Egypt." In 26:58, the Greek also omits the family of the Mahlites.
