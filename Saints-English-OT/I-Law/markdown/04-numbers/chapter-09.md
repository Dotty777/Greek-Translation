## Text

**9:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, in the first month, saying,

**9:2** "Speak, and let the sons of Israel keep the Passover in its season.

**9:3** On the fourteenth day of the first month, toward evening, you shall keep it at the appointed times; according to its law and according to its ordinance you shall keep it."

**9:4** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel to keep the Passover, beginning on the fourteenth day of the month in the wilderness of Sinai;

**9:5** just as the Lord directed Moses, so the sons of Israel did.

**9:6** And the men who were unclean on account of a human corpse came forward, and they were not able to keep the Passover on that day; and they drew near before Moses and Aaron on that day.

**9:7** And those men said to him, "We are unclean on account of a human corpse; should we therefore fail to offer the gift to the Lord at its appointed time in the midst of the sons of Israel?"

**9:8** And Moses said to them, "Stand here, and I will hear what the Lord will command concerning you."

**9:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**9:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Any man who becomes unclean on account of a human corpse, or is on a journey far away, among you or among your generations, yet he shall keep the Passover to the Lord; in the second month, on the fourteenth day,

**9:11** toward evening, they shall keep it. With unleavened bread and bitter herbs they shall eat it;

**9:12** they shall not leave any of it until the morning, nor shall they break a bone of it; according to the law of the Passover they shall keep it.

**9:13** And the man who is clean and is not on a distant journey, and fails to keep the Passover, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from its people; because he did not offer the gift to the Lord at its appointed time, that man shall bear his sin.

**9:14** And if a proselyte comes to you in your land and keeps the Passover to the Lord, according to the law of the Passover and according to its regulation he shall keep it; there shall be one law for you, both for the proselyte and for the native of the land.'"

**9:15** And on the day when the tabernacle was set up, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the house of the testimony; and in the evening there was upon the tabernacle like the appearance of fire until morning.

**9:16** So it was continually: the cloud covered it by day, and the appearance of fire by night.

**9:17** And whenever the cloud went up from the tabernacle, then after that the sons of Israel set out; and in the place where the cloud stood, there the sons of Israel encamped.

**9:18** By the command of the Lord the sons of Israel shall encamp; by the command of the Lord they shall set out. All the days in which the cloud overshadows the tabernacle, the sons of Israel shall encamp.

**9:19** And whenever the cloud lingers upon the tabernacle for many days, then the sons of Israel shall keep the charge of God and shall by no means depart.

**9:20** And it shall be, whenever the cloud covers the tabernacle for a number of days, by the voice of the Lord they shall encamp, and by the command of the Lord they shall set out.

**9:21** And it shall be, whenever the cloud remains from evening until morning, and the cloud goes up in the morning, then they shall set out, by day or by night.

**9:22** For a month, or a day, while the cloud lingers longer overshadowing it, the sons of Israel shall encamp and shall by no means set out.

**9:23** For by the command of the Lord they shall set out—the charge of the Lord which they kept by the command of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek places the temporal clause with the unusual participle "beginning" (ἐναρχομένου) in verse 4, omitting the fuller statement of observance found in verse 5 of the Masoretic Text.
- **9:6** — Literally "on account of a soul of a man" (ἐπὶ ψυχῇ ἀνθρώπου), the standard Septuagint idiom for ritual defilement through contact with a human corpse (similarly in vv. 7, 10).
- **9:18** — From verse 18 through verse 23, the Septuagint casts the Israelites' responses to the cloud in the future tense ("shall encamp," "shall set out") and places encampment before departure in verse 18.
- **9:22** — The Greek reads simply "for a month, or a day" (μηνός, ἡμέρας), differing from the Masoretic Text's "two days, or a month, or a year," and omits the closing clause about departing when the cloud lifted.
