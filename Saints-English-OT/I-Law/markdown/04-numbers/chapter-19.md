## Text

**19:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**19:2** "This is the ordinance of the law, all that the Lord has commanded, saying, 'Speak to the children of Israel, and let them bring to you a red heifer without blemish, which has no blemish in her and upon which no yoke has been laid.

**19:3** And you shall give her to Eleazar the priest; and they shall bring her forth outside the camp to a clean place, and they shall slaughter her before him;

**19:4** and Eleazar shall take some of her blood, and shall sprinkle some of her blood seven times opposite the front of the tabernacle of the testimony.

**19:5** And they shall burn her up before him; both her hide and her flesh and her blood, together with her dung, shall be burned up.

**19:6** And the priest shall take cedar wood and hyssop and scarlet, and they shall cast them into the midst of the burning of the heifer.

**19:7** Then the priest shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp; and the priest shall be unclean until evening.

**19:8** And the one who burns her up shall wash his garments and bathe his body, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**19:9** And a clean man shall gather up the ashes of the heifer and deposit them outside the camp in a clean place. And it shall be for the congregation of the children of Israel for safekeeping; it is water of sprinkling, it is a purification.

**19:10** And the one who gathers the ashes of the heifer shall wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until evening; and it shall be a perpetual statute for the children of Israel and for the proselytes who are joined to them.

**19:11** He who touches the dead body of any human soul shall be unclean seven days;

**19:12** this one shall be purified on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall be clean; but if he is not purified on the third day and on the seventh day, he shall not be clean.

**19:13** Everyone who touches the dead body of a human soul, if one dies, and is not purified, has defiled the tabernacle of the Lord; that soul shall be wiped out from Israel, because the water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him; he is unclean, his uncleanness is still in him.

**19:14** And this is the law: if a man dies in a house, everyone who enters into the house and all that is in the house shall be unclean seven days;

**19:15** and every open vessel that does not have a fastening bound upon it is unclean.

**19:16** And everyone who touches on the face of the open field one slain, or a corpse, or a human bone, or a grave, shall be unclean seven days.

**19:17** And for the unclean person they shall take some of the burned ashes of the purification, and they shall pour living water upon them into a vessel;

**19:18** and a clean man shall take hyssop and dip it into the water, and shall sprinkle it upon the house, and upon the vessels, and upon the souls, as many as are there, and upon the one who has touched the human bone, or the slain one, or the dead body, or the grave;

**19:19** and the clean person shall sprinkle upon the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall be purified on the seventh day; and he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**19:20** And any man who is defiled and is not purified, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from the midst of the congregation, because he has defiled the holy things of the Lord; because the water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him, he is unclean.

**19:21** And it shall be a perpetual statute for you; and the one who sprinkles the water of sprinkling shall wash his garments, and the one who touches the water of sprinkling shall be unclean until evening;

**19:22** and whatever the unclean person touches shall be unclean, and the soul that touches it shall be unclean until evening.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — Greek adds "to a clean place" (εἰς τόπον καθαρόν) and uses plural verbs ("they shall bring her forth... they shall slaughter her"), as also in verse 6 ("they shall cast").
- **19:14** — Greek consistently renders "house" (οἰκία here and οἶκος in verse 18) where the Masoretic Hebrew has "tent."
- **19:19** — Greek reads "and he shall be unclean until evening" (ἀκάθαρτος ἔσται ἕως ἑσπέρας), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "and he shall be clean at evening."
