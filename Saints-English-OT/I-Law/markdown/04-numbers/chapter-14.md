## Text

**14:1** And all the congregation lifted up their voice and cried out, and the people wept all that night.

**14:2** And all the sons of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron, and all the congregation said to them,

**14:3** "If only we had died in the land of Egypt, or if we had died in this wilderness! And why is the Lord bringing us into this land to fall in war? Our wives and little ones will become plunder; now therefore it is better for us to return to Egypt."

**14:4** And they said one to another, "Let us appoint a leader and return to Egypt."

**14:5** Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the congregation of the sons of Israel.

**14:6** And Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, who were of those who had spied out the land, tore their garments,

**14:7** and spoke to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "The land which we spied out is exceedingly, exceedingly good.

**14:8** If the Lord chooses us, He will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land which flows with milk and honey.

**14:9** Yet do not become rebels against the Lord; and do not fear the people of the land, for they are food for us; for the season has departed from them, but the Lord is among us; do not fear them."

**14:10** And all the congregation said to stone them. Then the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud upon the tabernacle of testimony to all the sons of Israel.

**14:11** And the Lord said to Moses, "How long will this people provoke Me? And how long will they not believe Me, for all the signs which I have done among them?

**14:12** I will strike them with death and destroy them, and I will make you and the house of your father into a great and much more numerous nation than this."

**14:13** And Moses said to the Lord, "Then Egypt will hear, for by Your might You brought this people out from among them;

**14:14** moreover, all who dwell upon this land have heard that You are the Lord among this people, You who are seen eye to eye, O Lord; and Your cloud stands over them, and in a pillar of cloud You go along before them by day, and in a pillar of fire by night.

**14:15** Now if You wipe out this people as one man, then the nations, all who have heard Your name, will speak, saying,

**14:16** 'Because the Lord was not able to bring this people into the land which He swore to them, He laid them low in the wilderness.'

**14:17** And now let Your strength be exalted, O Lord, just as You spoke, saying,

**14:18** 'The Lord is longsuffering and abounding in mercy and true, taking away lawlessness and unrighteousness and sins; and He will by no means clear the guilty, repaying the sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation.'

**14:19** Forgive the sin of this people according to Your great mercy, just as You have been gracious to them from Egypt even until now."

**14:20** And the Lord said to Moses, "I am gracious to them according to your word;

**14:21** yet as I live, and as My name lives, the glory of the Lord shall indeed fill all the earth:

**14:22** for all the men who have seen My glory and the signs which I performed in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have tested Me this tenth time and have not heeded My voice,

**14:23** surely they shall not see the land which I swore to their fathers; rather, their children who are with Me here, as many as do not know good or evil, every inexperienced youth—to these I will give the land; but all who provoked Me shall not see it.

**14:24** But My servant Caleb, because another spirit was in him and he followed Me, I will bring him into the land into which he entered, and his seed shall inherit it.

**14:25** Now Amalek and the Canaanite dwell in the valley; tomorrow turn back and depart into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea."

**14:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**14:27** "How long shall I bear with this wicked congregation? They murmur against Me; I have heard the murmuring of the sons of Israel, which they have murmured concerning you.

**14:28** Say to them, 'As I live, says the Lord, surely just as you have spoken in My ears, so I will do to you:

**14:29** in this wilderness your carcasses shall fall, and all your census and those of you who were numbered from twenty years old and upward, as many as murmured against Me.

**14:30** You shall by no means enter into the land over which I stretched out My hand to cause you to dwell upon it, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun.

**14:31** But the little ones, whom you said would become plunder, I will bring them into the land, and they shall inherit the land from which you turned away.

**14:32** And your carcasses shall fall in this wilderness;

**14:33** and your sons shall be pasturing in the wilderness forty years, and shall bear your harlotry, until your carcasses are consumed in the wilderness.

**14:34** According to the number of the days in which you spied out the land, forty days, for each day a year, you shall bear your sins forty years, and you shall know the fury of My wrath.

**14:35** I the Lord have spoken: surely I will do this to this wicked congregation that has banded together against Me; in this wilderness they shall be utterly consumed, and there they shall die.'"

**14:36** Now the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land, who returned and murmured against it to the congregation, so as to bring forth evil words concerning the land,

**14:37** even those men who spoke evil against the land died by the plague before the Lord.

**14:38** But Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh remained alive of those men who had gone to spy out the land.

**14:39** And Moses spoke these words to all the sons of Israel, and the people mourned exceedingly.

**14:40** And rising early in the morning, they went up to the top of the mountain, saying, "Behold, here we are; we will go up to the place of which the Lord spoke, for we have sinned."

**14:41** And Moses said, "Why do you transgress the word of the Lord? It will not go well for you.

**14:42** Do not go up, for the Lord is not with you, and you will fall before the face of your enemies.

**14:43** For Amalek and the Canaanite are there before you, and you shall fall by the sword; because you turned away, disobeying the Lord, the Lord will not be among you."

**14:44** Yet forcing their way, they went up to the top of the mountain; but the ark of the covenant of the Lord and Moses did not move from the camp.

**14:45** Then Amalek and the Canaanite who dwelt in that mountain came down, and routed them and cut them down as far as Hormah; and they returned to the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — Following the Septuagint verse division, the opening wish begins verse three rather than concluding verse two, and the Greek reads "to fall in war" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by the sword."
- **14:9** — The Septuagint reads "the season" (or appointed time) has departed from them, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "their protection" (literally, "their shade").
- **14:23** — The Septuagint includes an extensive promise not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, granting the land to the children present who do not know good or evil, echoing Deuteronomy 1:39.
- **14:27** — The Septuagint reads that Israel murmured "concerning you" (referring to Moses and Aaron), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "against Me."
- **14:34** — The Septuagint renders the final phrase as "the fury of My wrath," translating a rare Hebrew noun often understood as "My opposition" or "My displeasure."
