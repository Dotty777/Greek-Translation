## Text

**3:1** And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses, in the day that the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.

**3:2** And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: the firstborn Nadab, and Abiud, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**3:3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whose hands they consecrated to minister as priests.

**3:4** And Nadab and Abiud died before the Lord, as they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children; and Eleazar and Ithamar ministered as priests with Aaron their father.

**3:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:6** "Take the tribe of Levi, and you shall set them before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him.

**3:7** And they shall keep his charges and the charges of the sons of Israel before the tabernacle of the testimony, to perform the works of the tabernacle;

**3:8** and they shall keep all the furnishings of the tabernacle of the testimony and the charges of the sons of Israel, according to all the works of the tabernacle.

**3:9** And you shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons the priests; these are given as a gift to Me from the sons of Israel.

**3:10** And you shall appoint Aaron and his sons over the tabernacle of the testimony, and they shall keep their priesthood and all things concerning the altar and within the veil; and the outsider who touches it shall die."

**3:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:12** "And behold, I Myself have taken the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb from among the sons of Israel; they shall be their ransom, and the Levites shall be Mine.

**3:13** For every firstborn is Mine; in the day that I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified to Myself every firstborn in Israel; from man to beast, they shall be Mine. I am the Lord."

**3:14** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

**3:15** "Number the sons of Levi by the houses of their fathers, by their families; every male from a month old and upward, number them."

**3:16** And Moses and Aaron numbered them by the voice of the Lord, in the manner that the Lord commanded them.

**3:17** And these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gedson, Kohath, and Merari.

**3:18** And these are the names of the sons of Gedson by their families: Libni and Shimei.

**3:19** And the sons of Kohath by their families: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**3:20** And the sons of Merari by their families: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by the houses of their fathers.

**3:21** Of Gedson was the family of Libni and the family of Shimei; these are the families of Gedson.

**3:22** Their numbering, according to the count of every male from a month old and upward, their numbering was seven thousand five hundred.

**3:23** And the sons of Gedson shall encamp behind the tabernacle by the sea;

**3:24** and the leader of the father's house of the family of Gedson was Eliasaph the son of Dael.

**3:25** And the charge of the sons of Gedson in the tabernacle of the testimony was the tabernacle and the covering, and the curtain of the door of the tabernacle of the testimony,

**3:26** and the hangings of the court, and the veil of the gate of the court which is by the tabernacle, and the remainder of all its works.

**3:27** Of Kohath was the family of the Amramites, and the family of the Izharites, and the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites; these are the families of Kohath by number,

**3:28** every male from a month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, keeping the charges of the holy things.

**3:29** The families of the sons of Kohath shall encamp on the side of the tabernacle toward the south;

**3:30** and the leader of the house of the fathers of the families of Kohath was Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.

**3:31** And their charge was the ark, and the table, and the lampstand, and the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary with which they minister, and the curtain, and all their works.

**3:32** And the leader over the leaders of the Levites was Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, appointed to keep the charges of the holy things.

**3:33** Of Merari was the family of Mahli and the family of Mushi; these are the families of Merari.

**3:34** Their numbering by count, every male from a month old and upward, was six thousand fifty;

**3:35** and the leader of the father's house of the family of Merari was Zuriel the son of Abihail; they shall encamp on the side of the tabernacle toward the north.

**3:36** The oversight and charge of the sons of Merari was the capitals of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets, and all their furnishings, and their works,

**3:37** and the pillars of the court all around, and their sockets, and their pegs, and their cords.

**3:38** And those who encamp in front of the tabernacle of the testimony on the east are Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charges of the sanctuary for the charges of the sons of Israel; and the outsider who touches it shall die.

**3:39** All the numbering of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the voice of the Lord by their families, every male from a month old and upward, was twenty-two thousand.

**3:40** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Number every firstborn male of the sons of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number by name;

**3:41** and you shall take the Levites for Me—I am the Lord—in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the sons of Israel."

**3:42** And Moses numbered, in the manner that the Lord commanded, every firstborn among the sons of Israel;

**3:43** and all the firstborn males, according to the number by name from a month old and upward from their numbering, were twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three.

**3:44** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:45** "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock, and the Levites shall be Mine. I am the Lord.

**3:46** And as for the ransom, the two hundred seventy-three who exceed the Levites from among the firstborn of the sons of Israel,

**3:47** and you shall take five shekels per head; according to the holy didrachm you shall take them, twenty obols to the shekel;

**3:48** and you shall give the silver to Aaron and to his sons, the ransom of those who exceed among them."

**3:49** And Moses took the silver, the ransom of those who exceeded, for the redemption of the Levites;

**3:50** from the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the silver, one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels, according to the holy shekel.

**3:51** And Moses gave the ransom of those who exceeded to Aaron and to his sons, by the voice of the Lord, in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Greek reads Abiud here and in verse 4, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Abihu.
- **3:10** — The Septuagint includes the fuller phrases "over the tabernacle of the testimony" and "all things concerning the altar and within the veil," and reads "who touches" where the Hebrew reads "who draws near."
- **3:17** — The Greek reads Gedson here and throughout the chapter (as well as Dael in verse 24), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Gershon (and Lael in verse 24).
- **3:34** — The Greek gives the number of the Merarites as six thousand fifty, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads six thousand two hundred.
- **3:47** — The Greek uses Hellenistic coinage terms here, rendering the sanctuary standard as "the holy didrachm" (compare "the holy shekel" in verse 50) and the smaller unit as "obols."
