## Text

**1:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tabernacle of the testimony, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying,

**1:2** "Take the sum of all the congregation of Israel, according to kindreds, according to fathers' houses, according to the number by name, head by head:

**1:3** every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host of Israel, muster them with their host; you and Aaron shall muster them.

**1:4** And with you there shall be one for each tribe, each of the leaders; and they shall be according to fathers' houses.

**1:5** And these are the names of the men who shall stand with you: from the sons of Reuben, Elizur the son of Shedeur;

**1:6** from Simeon, Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai;

**1:7** from Judah, Nahshon the son of Amminadab;

**1:8** from Issachar, Nethanel the son of Zuar;

**1:9** from Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon; from the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama the son of Ammihud;

**1:10** from Manasseh, Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;

**1:11** from Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gideoni;

**1:12** from Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;

**1:13** from Asher, Pagiel the son of Ocran;

**1:14** from Gad, Eliasaph the son of Reuel;

**1:15** from Naphtali, Ahira the son of Enan."

**1:16** These are the chosen of the congregation, leaders of the tribes according to their fathers' houses; they are the commanders of thousands in Israel.

**1:17** And Moses and Aaron took these men who had been called by name,

**1:18** and they assembled all the congregation on the first day of the month of the second year, and registered them according to their lineages, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, from twenty years old and upward, every male head by head,

**1:19** just as the Lord had commanded Moses; and they were mustered in the wilderness of Sinai.

**1:20** And the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:21** their muster from the tribe of Reuben was forty-six thousand five hundred.

**1:22** For the sons of Simeon, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:23** their muster from the tribe of Simeon was fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**1:24** For the sons of Judah, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:25** their muster from the tribe of Judah was seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**1:26** For the sons of Issachar, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:27** their muster from the tribe of Issachar was fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**1:28** For the sons of Zebulun, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:29** their muster from the tribe of Zebulun was fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**1:30** For the sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:31** their muster from the tribe of Ephraim was forty thousand five hundred.

**1:32** For the sons of Manasseh, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:33** their muster from the tribe of Manasseh was thirty-two thousand three hundred.

**1:34** For the sons of Benjamin, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:35** their muster from the tribe of Benjamin was thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**1:36** For the sons of Gad, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:37** their muster from the tribe of Gad was forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

**1:38** For the sons of Dan, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:39** their muster from the tribe of Dan was sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**1:40** For the sons of Asher, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:41** their muster from the tribe of Asher was forty-one thousand five hundred.

**1:42** For the sons of Naphtali, according to their kindreds, according to their families, according to their fathers' houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, all the males from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth in the host:

**1:43** their muster from the tribe of Naphtali was fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**1:44** This is the muster which Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel mustered, twelve men; they were one man for each tribe, according to the tribe of their fathers' houses.

**1:45** And the entire muster of the sons of Israel in their host, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to array for battle in Israel, was

**1:46** six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

**1:47** But the Levites, from the tribe of their fathers' house, were not mustered among the sons of Israel.

**1:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**1:49** "See that you do not muster the tribe of Levi with them, nor take their number in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**1:50** And you shall appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its vessels, and over all things that are in it; they shall bear the tabernacle and all its vessels, and they shall minister in it, and they shall encamp around the tabernacle.

**1:51** And when the tabernacle is to set out, the Levites shall take it down; and when the tabernacle is to encamp, they shall raise it up; and the outsider who draws near shall die.

**1:52** And the sons of Israel shall encamp, each man in his own order and each man according to his own command, with their host;

**1:53** but let the Levites encamp before the Lord around the tabernacle of the testimony, and there shall be no sin among the sons of Israel; and the Levites themselves shall keep the watch of the tabernacle of the testimony."

**1:54** And the sons of Israel did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron; thus they did.

## Translator Notes

- **1:14** — The Greek reads "Reuel" (Rhagouel, agreeing with Numbers 2:14), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Deuel" in this verse.
- **1:18** — The Greek reads "on the first day of the month of the second year" and uses the rare verb epexonousan (literally "enrolled on tablets" or "registered by lineage"), rendered "registered them."
- **1:24** — In the Greek text, Judah is listed third here (vv. 24-25) and Gad is placed ninth after Benjamin (vv. 36-37), differing from the order in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:33** — The Greek text reads "thirty-two thousand three hundred" (triakosioi), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "thirty-two thousand two hundred."
- **1:53** — The Greek includes "before the Lord" and reads "there shall be no sin" (hamartema), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "wrath."
