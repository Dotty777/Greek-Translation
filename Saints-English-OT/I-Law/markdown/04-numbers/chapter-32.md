## Text

**32:1** Now the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad had a multitude of livestock, an exceedingly great multitude; and they saw the region of Jazer and the region of Gilead, and the place was a place for livestock.

**32:2** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad came and spoke to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the rulers of the congregation, saying,

**32:3** "Ataroth, and Dibon, and Jazer, and Nimrah, and Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Sibmah, and Nebo, and Beon,

**32:4** the land which the Lord has delivered before the children of Israel, is a land for raising livestock, and your servants have livestock."

**32:5** And they said, "If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be given to your servants as a possession, and do not bring us across the Jordan."

**32:6** And Moses said to the sons of Gad and to the sons of Reuben, "Do your brethren go to war, and shall you sit here?

**32:7** And why do you turn aside the minds of the children of Israel from crossing over into the land which the Lord is giving them?

**32:8** Did your fathers not do thus when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to inspect the land?

**32:9** And they went up to the Valley of the Cluster, and inspected the land, and turned away the heart of the children of Israel, so that they should not enter into the land which the Lord gave them.

**32:10** And the Lord was provoked to wrath on that day, and swore, saying,

**32:11** 'Surely none of these men who came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and above, who know evil and good, shall see the land which He swore to Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob; for they have not wholly followed after Me,

**32:12** except Caleb the son of Jephunneh, the one who was set apart, and Joshua the son of Nun, because they have wholly followed after the Lord.'

**32:13** And the Lord was provoked to wrath against Israel, and made them wander about in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation was consumed, those who did evil things before the Lord.

**32:14** Behold, you have risen up in place of your fathers, a band of sinful men, to add yet more to the fury of the wrath of the Lord against Israel;

**32:15** for you will turn away from Him, so as to leave them yet again in the wilderness, and you will act lawlessly against this whole congregation."

**32:16** And they came near to him and said, "Let us build sheepfolds here for our livestock, and cities for our little ones;

**32:17** and we ourselves, armed as a vanguard, will go before the children of Israel, until we have brought them into their own place; and our little ones shall dwell in walled cities because of the inhabitants of the land.

**32:18** We will by no means return to our houses until the children of Israel have been apportioned each to his own inheritance.

**32:19** And we shall no longer inherit among them from across the Jordan and beyond, because we have received our portions on the other side of the Jordan toward the east."

**32:20** Then Moses said to them, "If you do according to this word, if you arm yourselves before the Lord for war,

**32:21** and every armed man of yours crosses over the Jordan before the Lord until His enemy is destroyed from before His face,

**32:22** and the land is subdued before the Lord, then afterward you shall return; and you shall be guiltless before the Lord and toward Israel, and this land shall be yours as a possession before the Lord.

**32:23** But if you do not do so, you will sin before the Lord, and you shall know your sin when evils overtake you.

**32:24** And you shall build for yourselves cities for your little ones and folds for your livestock; and that which proceeds from your mouth, this you shall do."

**32:25** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad spoke to Moses, saying, "Your servants will do just as our lord commands.

**32:26** Our little ones, and our wives, and all our livestock shall be in the cities of Gilead;

**32:27** but your servants will all cross over, armed and arrayed before the Lord for battle, just as our lord says."

**32:28** And Moses appointed over them Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the rulers of the fathers' houses of the tribes of Israel,

**32:29** and Moses said to them, "If the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad cross over the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the Lord, and you subdue the land before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead as a possession;

**32:30** but if they do not cross over armed with you to battle before the Lord, bring their little ones, and their wives, and their livestock across ahead of you into the land of Canaan, and they shall inherit together with you in the land of Canaan."

**32:31** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad answered, saying, "Whatever the Lord says to His servants, so we shall do;

**32:32** we will cross over armed before the Lord into the land of Canaan, and you shall give us the possession on the other side of the Jordan."

**32:33** So Moses gave to them—to the sons of Gad, and to the sons of Reuben, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh of the sons of Joseph—the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land and the cities with its borders, the cities of the land round about.

**32:34** And the sons of Gad built Dibon, and Ataroth, and Aroer,

**32:35** and Sophar, and Jazer, and raised them up;

**32:36** and Namram, and Beth Haran, fortified cities and sheepfolds.

**32:37** And the sons of Reuben built Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,

**32:38** and Baal Meon, encircled, and Sibmah; and they named the names of the cities which they built according to their own names.

**32:39** And the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, went to Gilead and took it, and destroyed the Amorite who dwelt in it.

**32:40** And Moses gave Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh, and he dwelt there.

**32:41** And Jair the son of Manasseh went and took their settlements, and called them the Settlements of Jair.

**32:42** And Nobah went and took Kaath and its villages, and called them Naboth after his own name.

## Translator Notes

- **32:11** — The Septuagint includes the additional clause "who know evil and good" (cf. Deuteronomy 1:39), absent from the Masoretic Text, and shifts from third-person "He swore" to first-person "after Me."
- **32:12** — Where the Masoretic Text calls Caleb "the Kenizzite," the Septuagint translates the epithet as "the one who was set apart."
- **32:23** — The Septuagint reads "you shall know your sin when evils overtake you," in contrast to the Masoretic Text's "be sure your sin will find you out."
- **32:30** — The Septuagint includes an expanded command to bring their little ones, wives, and livestock across ahead of the Israelites into Canaan, which is not in the Masoretic Text.
- **32:35** — The Septuagint reads "Sophar" (for Hebrew "Atroth Shophan") and translates the Hebrew place name "Jogbehah" (from a root meaning "to be high") as the verb phrase "and raised them up"; distinct Septuagint proper-name forms also appear in vv. 36 (Namram) and 42 (Kaath, Naboth).
