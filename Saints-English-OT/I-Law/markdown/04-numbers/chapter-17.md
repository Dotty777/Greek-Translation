## Text

**17:1** And the Lord said to Moses

**17:2** and to Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, "Take up the bronze censers out of the midst of those who were burned, and scatter this strange fire over there, for they have consecrated the censers of these sinners at the cost of their lives;

**17:3** and make them into hammered plates as a covering for the altar, because they were offered before the Lord and were sanctified, and they have become a sign to the sons of Israel."

**17:4** And Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest took the bronze censers which those who were burned had offered, and they affixed them as a covering for the altar,

**17:5** as a memorial to the sons of Israel, so that no outsider, who is not of the seed of Aaron, should draw near to place incense before the Lord, and that he may not become like Korah and his faction, just as the Lord spoke to him by the hand of Moses.

**17:6** And on the next day the sons of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have slain the people of the Lord."

**17:7** And it came to pass, as the congregation was banding together against Moses and Aaron, that they rushed toward the tabernacle of testimony; and as for it, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.

**17:8** And Moses and Aaron went before the tabernacle of testimony.

**17:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**17:10** "Depart from the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them at once." And they fell on their faces.

**17:11** Then Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, and put fire in it from the altar, and lay incense upon it, and carry it swiftly into the camp, and it shall make atonement for them; for wrath has gone forth from the presence of the Lord, and it has begun to strike down the people."

**17:12** And Aaron took it just as Moses had spoken to him, and ran into the congregation; and already the plague had begun among the people. And he put on the incense and made atonement for the people;

**17:13** and he stood between the dead and the living, and the plague ceased among the people.

**17:14** Now those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died on account of Korah.

**17:15** And Aaron returned to Moses at the door of the tabernacle of testimony, and the plague ceased.

**17:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:17** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and take from them a rod—a rod for each of their fathers' houses from all their leaders, according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods; and write each man's name upon the rod.

**17:18** And write Aaron's name upon the rod of Levi; for there is one rod; according to the tribe of their fathers' house they shall give it.

**17:19** And you shall place them in the tabernacle of testimony before the testimony, where I will be made known to you there.

**17:20** And it shall be that the man whom I choose, his rod shall sprout; and I will take away from Me the murmuring of the sons of Israel, which they murmur against you."

**17:21** So Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and all their leaders gave him a rod—a rod for each leader, one for every leader, according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods; and the rod of Aaron was among their rods.

**17:22** And Moses laid up the rods before the Lord in the tabernacle of testimony.

**17:23** And it came to pass on the next day that Moses and Aaron went into the tabernacle of testimony, and behold, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted, and brought forth buds, and put forth blossoms, and yielded nuts.

**17:24** Then Moses brought out all the rods from the presence of the Lord to all the sons of Israel; and they looked, and each man took his own rod.

**17:25** And the Lord said to Moses, "Lay up the rod of Aaron before the testimonies for safekeeping, as a sign to the sons of the disobedient; and let their murmuring cease from Me, so that they shall not die."

**17:26** And Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord commanded Moses; so they did.

**17:27** And the sons of Israel spoke to Moses, saying, "Behold, we are consumed, we have perished, we are utterly spent!

**17:28** Everyone who touches the tabernacle of the Lord dies. Shall we die to the very end?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — In the Septuagint, chapter 17 opens with the bronze censers and the plague (vv. 1-15, corresponding to 16:36-50 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) before continuing with Aaron's rod (vv. 16-28, corresponding to 17:1-13 MT).
- **17:7** — The Greek uses a proleptic feminine demonstrative (tende, "this one") referring back to the tabernacle of testimony ahead of the resumptive pronoun "it."
- **17:19** — The Greek reads "I will be made known to you" (reflecting the Hebrew root "to know") where the Masoretic Text has "I will meet with you."
- **17:23** — The Greek uses the general term karya ("nuts") for the fruit yielded by Aaron's rod, corresponding to "almonds" in the Hebrew text.
