## Text

**11:1** Now the people were murmuring wicked things before the Lord; and the Lord heard it and was kindled with wrath, and fire from the Lord broke out among them and consumed a part of the camp.

**11:2** Then the people cried out to Moses; and Moses prayed to the Lord, and the fire abated.

**11:3** And the name of that place was called Burning, because fire from the Lord had broken out among them.

**11:4** Now the mixed multitude who were among them craved with a strong craving; and the sons of Israel also sat down and wept, and said, "Who will feed us flesh?

**11:5** We remember the fish which we ate freely in Egypt, and the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlic;

**11:6** but now our soul is parched; there is nothing at all, only upon the manna are our eyes."

**11:7** Now the manna is like coriander seed, and its appearance is the appearance of crystal.

**11:8** And the people went about and gathered it, and ground it in the mill and crushed it in the mortar, and boiled it in the pot and made it into hearth-cakes; and its savor was like the taste of a cake made with oil.

**11:9** And whenever the dew fell upon the camp by night, the manna fell upon it.

**11:10** And Moses heard them weeping throughout their families, each man at his door; and the Lord was exceedingly kindled with wrath, and before Moses it was evil.

**11:11** And Moses said to the Lord, "Why have You afflicted Your servant? And why have I not found favor in Your sight, that You have laid the impulse of this people upon me?

**11:12** Did I conceive all this people, or did I give birth to them, that You say to me, 'Take them into your bosom, as a nurse carries the nursing child,' to the land which You swore to their fathers?

**11:13** Where am I to get flesh to give to all this people? For they weep before me, saying, 'Give us flesh, that we may eat.'

**11:14** I will not be able by myself alone to bear this people, because this matter is too heavy for me.

**11:15** And if You deal thus with me, put me utterly to death, if I have found mercy with You, that I may not see my affliction."

**11:16** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Gather to Me seventy men from the elders of Israel, whom you yourself have perceived to be elders of the people and their scribes; and you shall bring them to the tabernacle of testimony, and they shall stand there with you.

**11:17** And I will come down and speak with you there, and I will take from the Spirit who is upon you and lay the same upon them; and they shall share with you the impulse of the people, and you shall not bear them yourself alone.

**11:18** And to the people you shall say, 'Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow, and you shall eat flesh; for you have wept before the Lord, saying, "Who will feed us flesh? For it is well with us in Egypt." Therefore the Lord will give you flesh to eat, and you shall eat flesh.

**11:19** You shall not eat one day, nor two, nor five days, nor ten days, nor twenty days,

**11:20** for a month of days you shall eat, until flesh comes out of your nostrils and becomes nausea to you; because you have disobeyed the Lord who is among you, and have wept before Him, saying, "Why did we come out of Egypt?"'"

**11:21** And Moses said to the Lord, "Six hundred thousand footmen are the people among whom I am, and You have said, 'Flesh I will give them to eat, and they shall eat for a month of days.'

**11:22** Shall sheep and oxen be slaughtered for them, and will it suffice for them? Or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered together for them, and will it suffice for them?"

**11:23** And the Lord said to Moses, "Will the hand of the Lord not suffice? Now you shall know whether My word shall overtake you or not."

**11:24** So Moses went out and spoke to the people the words of the Lord, and he gathered seventy men from the elders of the people and stationed them around the tabernacle.

**11:25** And the Lord came down in a cloud and spoke to him, and He took from the Spirit who was upon him and laid the same upon the seventy men, the elders; and as soon as the Spirit rested upon them, they prophesied, and they did not do so anymore.

**11:26** Now two men remained in the camp, the name of the one was Eldad and the name of the second was Medad, and the Spirit rested upon them; and these were among those who had been enrolled, yet they had not come to the tabernacle, and they prophesied in the camp.

**11:27** And the young man ran and reported to Moses, and spoke, saying, "Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp."

**11:28** And Joshua the son of Nun, who attended Moses, the chosen one, answered and said, "Lord Moses, restrain them."

**11:29** And Moses said to him, "Are you jealous for my sake? And who would grant that all the people of the Lord were prophets, whenever the Lord puts His Spirit upon them?"

**11:30** Then Moses departed into the camp, both he and the elders of Israel.

**11:31** And a wind went forth from the Lord and brought quail across from the sea, and cast them upon the camp, a day's journey on this side and a day's journey on the other side, all around the camp, and about two cubits above the ground.

**11:32** And the people rose up all that day, and all that night, and all the next day, and gathered the quail; he who gathered the least gathered ten cors; and they slaughtered them to dry for themselves all around the camp.

**11:33** The flesh was still between their teeth, before it was exhausted, when the Lord was kindled with wrath against His people; and the Lord struck the people with an exceedingly great plague.

**11:34** And the name of that place was called Graves of Craving; there they buried the people who craved.

**11:35** From Graves of Craving the people set out to Hazeroth, and the people were at Hazeroth.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Taberah as Enpyrismos ("Burning").
- **11:7** — The Greek describes the manna's appearance as "crystal" (krystallos), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text compares it to bdellium.
- **11:28** — The Greek identifies Joshua as "the chosen one" (eklektos), reading the underlying Hebrew differently from the Masoretic Text's "from his youth."
- **11:32** — The Greek reads "slaughtered them to dry for themselves" (esphaxan... psygmous) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "spread them out"; a "cor" (Greek koros) is a dry measure equivalent to a homer.
- **11:34** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Kibroth Hattaavah as Mnemata tes epithymias ("Graves of Craving").
