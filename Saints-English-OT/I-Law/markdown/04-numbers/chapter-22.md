## Text

**22:1** And the sons of Israel set out and encamped on the west of Moab, beside the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**22:2** And Balak the son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorite,

**22:3** and Moab feared the people exceedingly, because they were many; and Moab was vexed at the presence of the sons of Israel.

**22:4** And Moab said to the council of elders of Midian, "Now this congregation will lick up all those around us, as the calf licks up the green herbage from the field." And Balak the son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.

**22:5** And he sent ambassadors to Balaam the son of Beor at Pethor, which is upon the river of the land of the sons of his people, to summon him, saying, "Behold, a people has come out from Egypt, and behold, it has covered the face of the earth; and this people sits settled close to me.

**22:6** And now come, curse this people for me, for it is stronger than we are, so that we may perhaps be able to strike some of them, and I shall drive them out from the land; for I know that those whom you bless are blessed, and those whom you curse are cursed."

**22:7** And the council of elders of Moab and the council of elders of Midian departed, and the divinations were in their hands; and they came to Balaam and spoke to him the words of Balak.

**22:8** And he said to them, "Lodge here through the night, and I will answer you the matters which the Lord shall speak to me." And the princes of Moab remained with Balaam.

**22:9** And God came to Balaam and said to him, "Why are these men with you?"

**22:10** And Balaam said to God, "Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent them to me, saying,

**22:11** 'Behold, a people has come out from Egypt, and behold, it has covered the face of the earth, and this people sits settled close to me; and now come, curse it for me, if indeed I may be able to strike it and drive it out from the land.'"

**22:12** And God said to Balaam, "You shall not go with them, nor shall you curse the people, for it is blessed."

**22:13** And Balaam arose in the morning and said to the princes of Balak, "Return quickly to your lord; God does not permit me to go with you."

**22:14** And the princes of Moab arose, and came to Balak, and said, "Balaam is not willing to go with us."

**22:15** And Balak sent yet again princes more numerous and more honorable than these.

**22:16** And they came to Balaam and said to him, "Thus says Balak the son of Zippor: 'I urge you, do not hesitate to come to me;

**22:17** for I will honor you with great honor, and whatever you say I will do for you; and come, curse this people for me.'"

**22:18** And Balaam answered and said to the princes of Balak, "Though Balak should give me his house full of silver and gold, I shall not be able to transgress the word of the Lord God, to make it small or great in my own mind.

**22:19** And now stay here, you also, this night, and I shall know what further the Lord will speak to me."

**22:20** And God came to Balaam and said to him, "If these men have come to summon you, arise and follow them; yet the word which I shall speak to you, that you shall do."

**22:21** And Balaam arose in the morning, saddled his donkey, and went with the princes of Moab.

**22:22** And God was provoked with wrath because he went, and the Angel of God stood up to oppose him; now he was riding upon his donkey, and two of his servants were with him.

**22:23** And when the donkey saw the Angel of God standing against him in the road, with His sword drawn in His hand, the donkey turned aside out of her road and went into the field; and he struck the donkey with the staff, to turn her straight back into the road.

**22:24** And the Angel of God stood in the furrows of the vineyards, a fence on this side and a fence on that side;

**22:25** and when the donkey saw the Angel of God, she pressed him against the wall and crushed the foot of Balaam; and he went on to lash her yet again.

**22:26** And the Angel of God went further, and departing stood in a narrow place, where there was no way to turn aside to the right or to the left.

**22:27** And when the donkey saw the Angel of God, she lay down beneath Balaam; and Balaam became furious and kept beating the donkey with the staff.

**22:28** And God opened the mouth of the donkey, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you, that you have struck me this third time?"

**22:29** And Balaam said to the donkey, "Because you have mocked me; and if I had a sword in my hand, I would already have pierced you through."

**22:30** And the donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey, upon which you have ridden from your youth until this very day? Have I ever acted thus toward you in utter disregard?" And he said, "No."

**22:31** Then God uncovered the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the Angel of the Lord standing against him in the road, with His sword drawn in His hand; and he bowed down and worshiped upon his face.

**22:32** And the Angel of God said to him, "Why have you struck your donkey this third time? Behold, I have come forth to oppose you, because your way is not pleasing before Me; and when the donkey saw Me, she turned aside from Me this third time.

**22:33** And had she not turned aside, surely now I would have slain you, and spared her alive."

**22:34** And Balaam said to the Angel of the Lord, "I have sinned, for I did not know that You stood against me in the road to meet me; and now, if it is not pleasing to You, I will turn back."

**22:35** And the Angel of God said to Balaam, "Go with the men; yet the word which I shall speak to you, that you shall take care to speak." And Balaam went with the princes of Balak.

**22:36** And when Balak heard that Balaam had arrived, he went out to meet him to a city of Moab, which is upon the borders of the Arnon, which is at the edge of the borders.

**22:37** And Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not send to you to summon you? Why did you not come to me? Shall I not truly be able to honor you?"

**22:38** And Balaam said to Balak, "Behold, I have come to you now; shall I be able to speak anything? The word which God puts into my mouth, that I shall speak."

**22:39** And Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Cities of Villages.

**22:40** And Balak sacrificed sheep and calves, and sent to Balaam and to the princes who were with him.

**22:41** And it came to pass in the morning that Balak took Balaam and brought him up to the pillar of Baal, and from there he showed him a part of the people.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — The Septuagint reads "on the west of Moab" (taking the Hebrew term for "plains" in the sense of "west/evening") rather than "in the plains of Moab."
- **22:30** — The Greek idiom literally reads "disregarding with disregard," rendering the Hebrew phrase for being accustomed to act in a certain way as acting with disdainful disregard.
- **22:39** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Kiriath-huzoth literally as "Cities of Villages" (Poleis epayleon).
- **22:41** — The Septuagint reads "the pillar of Baal" rather than the Hebrew "Bamoth-baal" ("high places of Baal"), and explicitly states that Balak showed Balaam a part of the people.
