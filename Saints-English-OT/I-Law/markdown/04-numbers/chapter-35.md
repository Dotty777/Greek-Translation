## Text

**35:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses on the west of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,

**35:2** "Command the children of Israel, and they shall give to the Levites from the portions of their possession cities to dwell in; and the outskirts of the cities around them they shall give to the Levites.

**35:3** And the cities shall be theirs to dwell in, and their set-apart grounds shall be for their cattle and for all their four-footed beasts.

**35:4** And the adjoining lands of the cities which you shall give to the Levites shall extend from the wall of the city and outward two thousand cubits all around.

**35:5** And you shall measure outside the city on the east side two thousand cubits, and on the south side two thousand cubits, and on the side toward the sea two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits; and the city in the midst of this shall be yours, and the bordering lands of the cities.

**35:6** And the cities which you shall give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall give for the manslayer to flee there, and in addition to these, forty-two cities.

**35:7** All the cities which you shall give to the Levites shall be forty-eight cities, these and their outskirts.

**35:8** And as for the cities which you shall give from the possession of the children of Israel, from those who have many you shall give many, and from those who have fewer, fewer; each according to his inheritance which they shall inherit, they shall give of the cities to the Levites."

**35:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**35:10** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You are crossing the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**35:11** and you shall set apart cities for yourselves; they shall be places of refuge for you, that the manslayer may flee there, everyone who strikes a person unintentionally.

**35:12** And the cities shall be places of refuge for you from the avenger of blood, so that the manslayer shall by no means die until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

**35:13** And the cities which you shall give, the six cities, shall be places of refuge for you:

**35:14** three cities you shall give beyond the Jordan, and the three cities you shall give in the land of Canaan.

**35:15** There shall be a refuge for the children of Israel, and for the proselyte who sojourns among you; these cities shall be for a place of refuge, for everyone who strikes a person unintentionally to flee there.

**35:16** But if he strikes him with an instrument of iron so that he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:17** And if he strikes him with a stone in the hand by which one may die, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:18** And if he strikes him with a wooden instrument in the hand by which one may die, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:19** The avenger of blood himself shall slay the murderer; whenever he meets him, he himself shall slay him.

**35:20** And if out of hatred he pushes him and hurls any object upon him from ambush, so that he dies,

**35:21** or in wrath he struck him with his hand, so that he dies, the one who struck him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death. The avenger of blood shall slay the murderer when he meets him.

**35:22** But if suddenly, not out of hatred, he pushes him, or hurls any object upon him without ambush,

**35:23** or with any stone by which one may die, without knowing, and it falls upon him so that he dies, while he was not his enemy nor seeking to do him harm,

**35:24** then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the avenger of blood according to these judgments.

**35:25** And the congregation shall deliver the manslayer from the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge to which he had fled; and he shall dwell there until the high priest dies, whom they anointed with the holy oil.

**35:26** But if the manslayer at all goes outside the borders of the city to which he fled,

**35:27** and the avenger of blood finds him outside the borders of the city of his refuge, and the avenger of blood slays the manslayer, he is not guilty;

**35:28** for he shall dwell in the city of refuge until the high priest dies; after the death of the high priest, the manslayer shall return to the land of his possession.

**35:29** And these things shall be to you for a statute of judgment throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**35:30** Whoever strikes a person, by witnesses you shall slay the murderer; and one witness shall not testify against a person so that he should die.

**35:31** And you shall not accept a ransom for the life from the murderer who is liable to be put to death, for he shall surely be put to death.

**35:32** You shall not accept a ransom for fleeing to a city of refuge, to dwell again upon the land, until the high priest dies.

**35:33** And you shall by no means stain with murder the land in which you dwell, for this blood stains the land with murder; and atonement shall not be made for the land for the blood that was shed upon it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**35:34** And you shall not defile the land on which you dwell, upon which I will dwell among you; for I am the Lord who dwells in the midst of the children of Israel.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:1** — The Greek reads "on the west of Moab" (epi dysmon Moab), reflecting a reading of Hebrew aravot ("plains") in connection with erev ("evening/west").
- **35:4** — The Greek specifies "two thousand cubits" here (matching v. 5), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "one thousand cubits."
- **35:12** — "The avenger of blood" translates Greek ho anchisteuon to haima, literally "the next of kin for the blood."
- **35:32** — The Greek uses an infinitive construction ("for fleeing to a city of refuge"), whereas the Hebrew refers to the person "who has fled to his city of refuge."
- **35:33** — "Stain with murder" renders the rare Septuagint verb phonoktoneo ("defile with murder/bloodshed"), where the Hebrew uses a verb meaning "pollute" or "defile."
