## Text

**28:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**28:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'My offerings, My gifts, My offerings made by fire, for a sweet aroma, you shall be careful to offer to Me at My feasts.'

**28:3** And you shall say to them, 'These are the offerings made by fire which you shall bring to the Lord: two unblemished lambs a year old each day, for a continual burnt offering.

**28:4** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer toward evening,

**28:5** and you shall offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a sacrifice, mixed with oil, a fourth of a hin—

**28:6** a perpetual burnt offering, which was ordained on Mount Sinai for a sweet aroma to the Lord.

**28:7** And its drink offering shall be a fourth of a hin for the one lamb; in the holy place you shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the Lord.

**28:8** And the second lamb you shall offer toward evening; according to its sacrifice and according to its drink offering you shall offer it, for a sweet aroma to the Lord.

**28:9** And on the Sabbath day you shall bring two unblemished lambs a year old, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for a sacrifice, and a drink offering—

**28:10** a burnt offering of the Sabbaths on the Sabbaths, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**28:11** And at the new moons you shall bring burnt offerings to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, and one ram, and seven unblemished lambs a year old;

**28:12** three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each young bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for the one ram,

**28:13** and a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for each lamb, a sacrifice, a sweet aroma, an offering made by fire to the Lord.

**28:14** Their drink offering shall be half a hin for each young bull, and a third of a hin for the one ram, and a fourth of a hin of wine for each lamb. This is the burnt offering month by month for the months of the year.

**28:15** And one male goat from the goats for a sin offering to the Lord; it shall be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**28:16** And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the Passover of the Lord.

**28:17** And on the fifteenth day of this month is a feast; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**28:18** And the first day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work.

**28:19** And you shall bring burnt offerings, offerings made by fire to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, and seven lambs a year old; they shall be unblemished for you.

**28:20** And their sacrifice shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each young bull and two tenths for the one ram;

**28:21** one tenth each you shall offer for every lamb of the seven lambs;

**28:22** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you;

**28:23** besides the continual burnt offering of the morning, which is a perpetual burnt offering.

**28:24** These things, in this same manner, you shall offer each day for the seven days, a gift, an offering made by fire for a sweet aroma to the Lord; in addition to the continual burnt offering you shall offer its drink offering.

**28:25** And the seventh day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work on it.

**28:26** And on the day of the new produce, when you offer a new sacrifice of the Weeks to the Lord, it shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work.

**28:27** And you shall bring burnt offerings for a sweet aroma to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, and seven unblemished lambs a year old;

**28:28** their sacrifice shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each young bull and two tenths for the one ram,

**28:29** one tenth each for every lamb of the seven lambs;

**28:30** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you, besides the burnt offering that is

**28:31** continual; and you shall offer their sacrifice to Me—they shall be unblemished for you—together with their drink offerings.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — The Greek text enumerates three terms ("My offerings, My gifts, My offerings made by fire") and concludes with "at My feasts" rather than "at its appointed time."
- **28:26** — Greek "te hemera ton neon" ("the day of the new produce") corresponds to the Feast of Firstfruits, also called the Feast of Weeks.
- **28:30** — The Greek text splits the phrase "the continual burnt offering" ("tou holokautomatos tou dia pantos") across the boundary between verses 30 and 31.
