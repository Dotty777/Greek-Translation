## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Execute the vengeance of the sons of Israel upon the Midianites, and afterward you shall be added to your people."

**31:3** And Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Arm men from among you to array for battle before the Lord against Midian, to render vengeance from the Lord upon Midian.

**31:4** A thousand from a tribe, a thousand from a tribe—from all the tribes of Israel send them out to array for battle."

**31:5** So they numbered out from the thousands of Israel a thousand from each tribe: twelve thousand, armed for battle.

**31:6** And Moses sent them, a thousand from a tribe, a thousand from a tribe, with their host, and Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest; and the holy vessels and the signaling trumpets were in their hands.

**31:7** And they arrayed for battle against Midian, just as the Lord commanded Moses, and they slew every male.

**31:8** And they slew the kings of Midian along with their slain: Evi, and Zur, and Rekem, and Hur, and Robok, five kings of Midian; and Balaam the son of Beor they slew with the sword along with their slain.

**31:9** And they took as plunder the women of Midian, and their little ones, and their livestock, and all their possessions, and their wealth they plundered.

**31:10** And all their cities in their dwelling places, and their encampments, they burned with fire.

**31:11** And they took all the plunder and all their spoils, from man to beast.

**31:12** And they brought to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to all the sons of Israel, the captives and the spoils and the plunder, to the camp at Araboth of Moab, which is by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**31:13** And Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and all the rulers of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp.

**31:14** And Moses was angry with the overseers of the host, the captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, who were coming from the battle array of the war.

**31:15** And Moses said to them, "Why have you kept every female alive?

**31:16** For these were the ones who, according to the word of Balaam, caused the sons of Israel to turn away and disregard the word of the Lord on account of Peor, and the plague came upon the congregation of the Lord.

**31:17** Now therefore slay every male among all the little ones, and every woman who has known the bed of a male you shall slay;

**31:18** but all the little ones among the women who have not known the bed of a male, keep them alive.

**31:19** And as for you, encamp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has slain anyone, and whoever touches the slain, shall be purified on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.

**31:20** And every garment, and every article of leather, and every work of goats' hair, and every article of wood, you shall purify."

**31:21** Then Eleazar the priest said to the men of the host who were coming from the battle array of the war, "This is the ordinance of the law which the Lord commanded Moses:

**31:22** Only the gold, and the silver, and bronze, and iron, and lead, and tin—

**31:23** every item shall pass through fire, and it shall be cleansed, yet with the water of purification it shall be purified; and all things that do not pass through fire shall pass through water.

**31:24** And you shall wash your garments on the seventh day, and you shall be cleansed; and after these things you shall enter into the camp."

**31:25** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:26** "Take the sum of the spoils of the captivity, from man to beast, you and Eleazar the priest and the rulers of the fathers' houses of the congregation;

**31:27** and you shall divide the spoils between the warriors who went out to the battle array and all the congregation.

**31:28** And you shall set apart a tribute for the Lord from the men of war who went out to the battle array: one soul out of five hundred, of the people, and of the livestock, and of the oxen, and of the sheep, and of the goats;

**31:29** and from their half you shall take it, and you shall give it to Eleazar the priest as the firstfruits of the Lord.

**31:30** And from the half belonging to the sons of Israel you shall take one out of fifty, of the people, and of the oxen, and of the sheep, and of the donkeys, and of all the livestock, and you shall give them to the Levites who keep the charges in the tabernacle of the Lord."

**31:31** And Moses and Eleazar the priest did just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:32** And the remainder of the plunder which the men of war had plundered was, of the sheep, six hundred and seventy-five thousand;

**31:33** and oxen, seventy-two thousand;

**31:34** donkeys, sixty-one thousand;

**31:35** and human souls, of the women who had not known the bed of a man, all the souls were thirty-two thousand.

**31:36** And the half, the portion of those who had gone out to war, was, of the number of the sheep, three hundred and thirty-five thousand five hundred.

**31:37** And the tribute for the Lord from the sheep was six hundred and seventy-five;

**31:38** and the oxen were thirty-six thousand, and the tribute for the Lord was seventy-two;

**31:39** and the donkeys were thirty thousand five hundred, and the tribute for the Lord was sixty-one;

**31:40** and the human souls were sixteen thousand, and their tribute for the Lord was thirty-two souls.

**31:41** And Moses gave the tribute for the Lord, the set-apart offering of God, to Eleazar the priest, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:42** From the half belonging to the sons of Israel, whom Moses separated from the men of war—

**31:43** now the half belonging to the congregation was, of the sheep, three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred,

**31:44** and oxen, thirty-six thousand,

**31:45** donkeys, thirty thousand five hundred,

**31:46** and human souls, sixteen thousand.

**31:47** And Moses took from the half belonging to the sons of Israel the one out of fifty, of the people and of the livestock, and gave them to the Levites who keep the charges of the tabernacle of the Lord, in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:48** And all those appointed over the divisions of thousands of the host, the captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, came near to Moses;

**31:49** and they said to Moses, "Your servants have taken the count of the men of war who are with us, and not even one of them is missing.

**31:50** Therefore we have brought the offering to the Lord, whatever article of gold each man found—anklet, and bracelet, and ring, and armlet, and hair ornament—to make atonement for ourselves before the Lord."

**31:51** And Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from them, every wrought article.

**31:52** And all the gold, the set-apart offering which they set apart for the Lord, was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from the captains of thousands and from the captains of hundreds.

**31:53** And the men of war had taken plunder, each man for himself.

**31:54** And Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the captains of thousands and from the captains of hundreds, and brought it into the tabernacle of testimony as a memorial for the sons of Israel before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **31:8** — The Greek reads Robok for the fifth Midianite king, where the Hebrew text reads Reba, and adds "along with their slain" after the slaying of Balaam.
- **31:12** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew expression for "the plains of Moab" as the place name Araboth of Moab.
- **31:28** — The Greek lists five categories here—people, livestock, oxen, sheep, and goats—whereas the Hebrew text omits livestock and has donkeys rather than goats.
- **31:36** — The Greek text reads three hundred and thirty-five thousand five hundred sheep here, whereas verse forty-three and the Hebrew text read three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred.
- **31:54** — The Greek renders the Hebrew "tent of meeting" as "tabernacle of testimony," the standard Septuagint designation throughout Numbers.
