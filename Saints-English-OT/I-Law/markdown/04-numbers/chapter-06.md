## Text

**6:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the children of Israel,

**6:2** and you shall say to them, 'Any man or woman who solemnly vows a vow to consecrate himself in purity to the Lord

**6:3** from wine and strong drink, he shall keep himself pure from wine, and vinegar made from wine and vinegar made from strong drink he shall not drink, and whatever is produced from the grape he shall not drink, and fresh grapes and raisins he shall not eat.

**6:4** All the days of his vow, of all things that come from the vine—wine, from the grape skins even to the seed—he shall not eat.

**6:5** All the days of the consecration no razor shall come upon his head; until the days are fulfilled which he vowed to the Lord, he shall be holy, letting the locks of the hair of his head grow long.

**6:6** All the days of the vow to the Lord he shall not go near any dead body. For father and for mother,

**6:7** and for brother and for sister, he shall not defile himself for them when they die, because the vow of his God is upon him, upon his head;

**6:8** all the days of his vow he shall be holy to the Lord.

**6:9** And if anyone dies very suddenly beside him, immediately the head of his vow shall be defiled, and he shall shave his head on the day he is cleansed; on the seventh day he shall be shaved.

**6:10** And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the doors of the tabernacle of testimony,

**6:11** and the priest shall offer one as a sin offering and one for a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning what he sinned regarding the dead body, and he shall sanctify his head on that day on which he was consecrated to the Lord for the days of the vow,

**6:12** and he shall bring a year-old male lamb for a trespass offering; and the former days shall be void, because the head of his vow was defiled.

**6:13** And this is the law of the one who has made a vow: on the day when he fulfills the days of his vow, he himself shall present himself beside the doors of the tabernacle of testimony;

**6:14** and he shall bring his gift to the Lord: one year-old male lamb without blemish for a burnt offering, and one year-old ewe lamb without blemish for a sin offering, and one ram without blemish for a peace offering,

**6:15** and a basket of unleavened bread, loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and their grain offering and their drink offering.

**6:16** And the priest shall bring them before the Lord, and shall offer his sin offering and his burnt offering;

**6:17** and the ram he shall offer as a sacrifice of a peace offering to the Lord, with the basket of unleavened bread; and the priest shall offer his grain offering and his drink offering.

**6:18** And the one who has made the vow shall shave the head of his vow beside the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and shall put the hair upon the fire which is under the sacrifice of the peace offering.

**6:19** And the priest shall take the boiled shoulder from the ram, and one unleavened loaf from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and shall place them upon the hands of the one who made the vow, after he has shaved his vow;

**6:20** and the priest shall offer them as a presented offering before the Lord; it shall be holy for the priest, in addition to the breast of the presented offering and in addition to the shoulder of the portion set apart; and after these things the one who made the vow shall drink wine.

**6:21** This is the law of the one who has made a vow, who vows to the Lord his gift to the Lord concerning the vow, besides whatever else his hand may afford, according to the measure of his vow which he vows, according to the law of consecration.'"

**6:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:23** "Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying, 'In this manner you shall bless the children of Israel, saying to them'—and they shall place My name upon the children of Israel, and I, the Lord, will bless them:

**6:24** 'May the Lord bless you and guard you,

**6:25** and may the Lord cause His face to shine upon you and have mercy upon you;

**6:26** may the Lord lift up His countenance upon you and grant you peace.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Rather than transliterating the Hebrew term "Nazirite," the Septuagint translates it throughout this chapter as making a vow of consecration or purity to the Lord.
- **6:11** — The Septuagint includes the clause "on which he was consecrated to the Lord for the days of the vow" at the end of verse 11, rather than beginning verse 12 as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **6:23** — The Septuagint places the promise about placing God's name on Israel here before the priestly blessing and includes "the Lord," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places it after the blessing as verse 27.
- **6:25** — The Septuagint begins this line with "and" and renders the verb as "have mercy upon you" rather than "be gracious to you."
