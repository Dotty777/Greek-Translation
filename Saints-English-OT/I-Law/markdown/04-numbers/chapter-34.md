## Text

**34:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:2** "Command the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You are entering into the land of Canaan; this shall be to you for the inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders.

**34:3** And your side toward the south shall be from the wilderness of Zin extending alongside Edom, and your border toward the south shall be from the edge of the Salt Sea on the east;

**34:4** and the border shall turn around you from the south toward the Ascent of Akrabbim, and pass along to Ennak, and its outlet shall be to the south of Kadesh Barnea; then it shall go out to the village of Arad, and pass on to Azmon.

**34:5** And the border shall turn from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and the outlet shall be the sea.

**34:6** And you shall have the border of the sea: the Great Sea shall mark the boundary; this shall be to you the border of the sea.

**34:7** And this shall be your border toward the north: from the Great Sea you shall measure off for yourselves along the mountain, the mountain;

**34:8** and from the mountain, the mountain you shall measure off for them to the entrance of Hamath, and its outlet shall be the border of Saradak;

**34:9** and the border shall go out to Dephrona, and its outlet shall be Harsenaim; this shall be your border on the north.

**34:10** And you shall measure off for yourselves the eastern border from Harsenain to Sepphamar;

**34:11** and the border shall go down from Sepphamar to Bela on the east to the springs, and the border of Bela shall go down to the slope of the Sea of Chinnereth on the east.

**34:12** And the border shall go down to the Jordan, and the outlet shall be the Salt Sea. This shall be your land and its borders all around.'"

**34:13** Then Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying, "This is the land which you shall inherit by lot, just as the Lord commanded Moses to give it to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh;

**34:14** for the tribe of the sons of Reuben and the tribe of the sons of Gad, according to their fathers' houses, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their portions.

**34:15** Two tribes and a half-tribe have received their portions beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, on the south toward the east."

**34:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:17** "These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun.

**34:18** And you shall take one leader from each tribe to apportion the land to you as an inheritance.

**34:19** And these are the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh;

**34:20** of the tribe of Simeon, Salamiel the son of Semiud;

**34:21** of the tribe of Benjamin, Eldad the son of Chislon;

**34:22** of the tribe of Dan, a leader, Bakchir the son of Jogli;

**34:23** of the sons of Joseph, of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, a leader, Hanniel the son of Suphi;

**34:24** of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, a leader, Kemuel the son of Sabatha;

**34:25** of the tribe of Zebulun, a leader, Elizaphan the son of Parnach;

**34:26** of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, a leader, Paltiel the son of Oza;

**34:27** of the tribe of the sons of Asher, a leader, Achior the son of Shelomi;

**34:28** of the tribe of Naphtali, a leader, Pedahel the son of Beniamiud."

**34:29** These are the ones whom the Lord commanded to make the apportionment for the children of Israel in the land of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **34:4** — The Greek text reads Ennak (where the Hebrew has Zin) and translates Hebrew hazar as a common noun while reading Arad, yielding "the village of Arad" (where the Hebrew has Hazar-addar).
- **34:7** — Here and in v. 8 the Greek renders Hebrew Hor ha-har ("Mount Hor") literally twice as "the mountain, the mountain" (to oros to oros).
- **34:11** — In vv. 8–11 the Greek preserves several distinct border place names compared with the Hebrew, including Saradak (for Zedad), Dephrona (for Ziphron), Harsenaim/Harsenain (for Hazar-enan), Sepphamar (for Shepham), and Bela (for Riblah), and translates Hebrew Ain as "the springs."
- **34:15** — The Greek reads "on the south toward the east" (apo notou kat' anatolas), where the Hebrew reads "eastward toward the sunrise."
- **34:20** — In vv. 20–28 the Greek preserves several distinct forms of the tribal leaders' and fathers' names compared with the Hebrew: Salamiel son of Semiud, Eldad, Bakchir, Suphi, Sabatha, Oza, Achior, and Beniamiud.
