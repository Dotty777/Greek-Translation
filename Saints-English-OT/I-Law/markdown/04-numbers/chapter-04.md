## Text

**4:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**4:2** "Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses,

**4:3** from twenty-five years old and upward, even to fifty years old, everyone who enters to minister, to perform all the works in the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:4** And these are the works of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of testimony: what is most holy.

**4:5** And Aaron and his sons shall enter whenever the camp sets out, and they shall take down the overshadowing veil and cover the ark of the testimony with it;

**4:6** and they shall place upon it a covering of blue leather, and spread over it a cloth wholly of blue on top, and insert the carrying poles.

**4:7** And upon the table set forth they shall spread a cloth wholly of purple, and place upon it the dishes, the censers, the cups, and the libation bowls with which he pours drink offerings; and the continual loaves shall be upon it.

**4:8** And they shall spread over it a scarlet cloth, and cover it with a covering of blue leather, and insert the carrying poles through it.

**4:9** And they shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand that gives light, and its lamps, its tongs, its oil funnels, and all the vessels of oil with which they minister;

**4:10** and they shall put it and all its utensils into a covering of blue leather, and set it upon carrying poles.

**4:11** And over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, and cover it with a covering of blue leather, and insert its carrying poles.

**4:12** And they shall take all the utensils of ministry with which they minister in the sanctuary, and put them into a blue cloth, and cover them with a covering of blue leather, and set them upon carrying poles.

**4:13** And he shall place the cover upon the altar, and they shall spread over it a cloth wholly of purple;

**4:14** and they shall place upon it all the utensils with which they minister upon it—the firepans, the fleshhooks, the bowls, the cover, and all the utensils of the altar—and they shall spread over it a covering of blue leather, and insert its carrying poles. And they shall take a purple cloth and cover the laver and its base, and put it into a covering of blue leather, and set it upon carrying poles.

**4:15** And Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy things and all the holy utensils when the camp sets out; and after these things the sons of Kohath shall enter to carry them, and they shall not touch the holy things, lest they die. These things the sons of Kohath shall carry in the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:16** Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest is overseer: the oil for the light, the compounded incense, the daily sacrifice, and the anointing oil—the oversight of the entire tabernacle and all that is in it, in the sanctuary, in all the works."

**4:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**4:18** "Do not destroy the clan of the tribe of Kohath from among the Levites.

**4:19** Do this for them, and they shall live and shall by no means die when they approach the most holy things: let Aaron and his sons go in, and they shall appoint each of them according to his burden.

**4:20** And they shall by no means enter to look suddenly upon the holy things, or they shall die."

**4:21** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:22** "Take the count of the sons of Gershon, them also, according to their fathers' houses, according to their clans;

**4:23** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old you shall number them, everyone who enters to minister, to perform his works in the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:24** This is the ministry of the clan of Gershon, to minister and to carry:

**4:25** they shall carry the curtains of the tabernacle, and the tabernacle of testimony, its covering, the blue covering that is upon it above, and the covering of the door of the tabernacle of testimony,

**4:26** and the hangings of the court that are by the tabernacle of testimony, and the remaining things; and all the utensils of ministry with which they minister, they shall attend to.

**4:27** By the word of Aaron and his sons shall be the ministry of the sons of Gershon, according to all their ministries and according to all the works performed by them; and you shall assign to them by name all the things to be carried by them.

**4:28** This is the ministry of the sons of Gershon in the tabernacle of testimony, and their charge shall be under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

**4:29** As for the sons of Merari, according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses, number them;

**4:30** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old you shall number them, everyone who enters to minister in the works of the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:31** And these are the charges of the things carried by them, according to all the works in the tabernacle of testimony: the capitals of the tabernacle, the bars, its pillars, its bases, and the covering, and their bases and their pillars, and the covering of the door of the tabernacle,

**4:32** and the pillars of the court all around, and their bases, and the pillars of the veil of the gate of the court and their bases, and their pegs and their cords, and all their utensils and all their services; by name you shall number them and all the utensils of the charge of the things carried by them.

**4:33** This is the ministry of the clan of the sons of Merari, in all their works in the tabernacle of testimony, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest."

**4:34** And Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel numbered the sons of Kohath according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses,

**4:35** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered to minister and to work in the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:36** And their numbering according to their clans was two thousand two hundred and fifty.

**4:37** This was the numbering of the clan of Kohath, everyone who ministered in the tabernacle of testimony, just as Moses and Aaron numbered them by the voice of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

**4:38** And the sons of Gershon were numbered according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses,

**4:39** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered to minister and to perform the works in the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:40** And their numbering according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses, was two thousand six hundred and thirty.

**4:41** This was the numbering of the clan of the sons of Gershon, everyone who ministered in the tabernacle of testimony, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the voice of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

**4:42** And the clan of the sons of Merari was also numbered according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses,

**4:43** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered to minister for the works of the tabernacle of testimony.

**4:44** And their numbering according to their clans, according to their fathers' houses, was three thousand two hundred.

**4:45** This was the numbering of the clan of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the voice of the Lord through the hand of Moses.

**4:46** All those who were numbered, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel numbered—the Levites—according to clans and according to fathers' houses,

**4:47** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered for the work of the works and the works of carrying in the tabernacle of testimony;

**4:48** and those who were numbered were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

**4:49** By the voice of the Lord they were numbered through the hand of Moses, man by man, over their works and over what they carry; and they were numbered in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Greek text gives the Levitical service age as twenty-five to fifty years old throughout this chapter (compare Numbers 8:24), whereas the Masoretic Text reads thirty to fifty.
- **4:6** — Greek hyakinthinon ("blue" or "hyacinth-blue") describes both the inner cloth and the outer leather covering across verses 6 through 14; in verse 7 the Greek also specifies a cloth wholly of purple for the table, where the Masoretic Text reads blue.
- **4:13** — The Greek reads "he shall place the cover upon the altar" (echoing "the cover" in verse 14), whereas the Masoretic Text directs that they remove the ashes from the altar.
- **4:14** — The closing instruction concerning the covering and transport of the laver and its base is preserved in the Septuagint (and Samaritan Pentateuch) but does not appear in the Masoretic Text.
- **4:36** — The Greek text numbers the Kohathites at two thousand two hundred and fifty (compared to two thousand seven hundred and fifty in the Masoretic Text), while the combined Levitical total in verse 48 remains eight thousand five hundred and eighty.
