## Text

**36:1** And the leaders of the tribe of the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, from the tribe of the sons of Joseph, came near and spoke before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the leaders of the fathers' houses of the sons of Israel.

**36:2** And they said, "The Lord commanded our lord to assign the land of the inheritance by lot to the sons of Israel, and the Lord directed our lord to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother to his daughters.

**36:3** And they will become wives to one from the tribes of the sons of Israel, and their lot will be taken away from the possession of our fathers, and it will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to whom they may become wives, and from the lot of our inheritance it will be taken away.

**36:4** And when the release of the sons of Israel comes, then their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to whom they may become wives, and from the inheritance of the tribe of our lineage their inheritance will be taken away."

**36:5** And Moses commanded the sons of Israel by the ordinance of the Lord, saying, "Thus the tribe of the sons of Joseph speak.

**36:6** This is the word which the Lord has directed for the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, 'Where it is pleasing in their sight, let them become wives; only from the clan of their father let them become wives.'

**36:7** And no inheritance of the sons of Israel shall be transferred from tribe to tribe, for the sons of Israel shall each cleave to the inheritance of the tribe of his lineage.

**36:8** And every daughter who possesses an inheritance from the tribes of the sons of Israel shall become a wife to one of those from the clan of her father, so that the sons of Israel may each possess his paternal inheritance.

**36:9** And no lot shall be transferred from one tribe to another tribe, but the sons of Israel shall each cleave to his own inheritance."

**36:10** Just as the Lord directed Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did.

**36:11** And Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, Noah, and Mahlah, the daughters of Zelophehad, were married to their cousins.

**36:12** From the clan of Manasseh, of the sons of Joseph, they became wives, and their inheritance remained with the tribe of the clan of their father.

**36:13** These are the commandments and the ordinances and the judgments which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses on the west of Moab, by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

## Translator Notes

- **36:4** — Greek aphesis ("release") is the Septuagint's standard rendering for the Year of Jubilee (cf. Leviticus 25:10).
- **36:5** — Greek houtos ("Thus") renders the underlying Hebrew ken literally in its adverbial sense rather than "rightly."
- **36:8** — The Greek verb anchisteuo ("possess") specifically denotes inheriting or succeeding to property by right of kinship as next of kin.
- **36:11** — The Septuagint places Mahlah last in the list of Zelophehad's daughters (rather than first as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and renders "sons of their uncles" concisely as anepsiois ("cousins").
- **36:13** — The Septuagint lists three legal terms ("commandments and the ordinances and the judgments") and renders the geography as epi dysmon Moab ("on the west of Moab"), reflecting an association of Hebrew arvot ("plains") with erev ("evening/west").
