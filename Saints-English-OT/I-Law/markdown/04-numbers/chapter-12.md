## Text

**12:1** Then Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom Moses had taken, for he had taken an Ethiopian woman.

**12:2** And they said, "Has the Lord spoken to Moses alone? Has He not spoken to us also?" And the Lord heard.

**12:3** Now the man Moses was exceedingly meek, more than all the men who were upon the earth.

**12:4** And the Lord said to Moses, Miriam, and Aaron, "Come out, you three, to the tabernacle of testimony." And the three went out to the tabernacle of testimony.

**12:5** Then the Lord came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the door of the tabernacle of testimony; and Aaron and Miriam were called, and both of them came forth.

**12:6** And He said to them, "Hear My words: If there is a prophet of yours for the Lord, I will make Myself known to him in a vision, and I will speak to him in sleep.

**12:7** Not so with My servant Moses; he is faithful in all My house.

**12:8** Mouth to mouth I will speak to him, in visible form and not through riddles, and he has beheld the glory of the Lord. Why then were you not afraid to speak against My servant Moses?"

**12:9** And the fierce wrath of the Lord was upon them, and He departed.

**12:10** And the cloud withdrew from the tabernacle, and behold, Miriam was leprous, like snow. And Aaron looked upon Miriam, and behold, she was leprous.

**12:11** Then Aaron said to Moses, "I implore you, lord, do not lay sin upon us, because we acted in ignorance in that we have sinned.

**12:12** Do not let her become like one equal to death, like an untimely birth coming forth from a mother's womb; it devours half of her flesh."

**12:13** So Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "O God, I implore You, heal her."

**12:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "If her father had surely spat in her face, would she not be ashamed seven days? Let her be set apart seven days outside the camp, and after that she shall come in."

**12:15** So Miriam was set apart outside the camp seven days; and the people did not set out until Miriam was cleansed.

**12:16** And after that the people set out from Hazeroth, and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Septuagint identifies Moses' wife as an Ethiopian woman (Aithiopissa) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Cushite.
- **12:6** — The Greek links "for the Lord" (Kyrio) to "a prophet of yours" and reads "in sleep" (en hypno) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in a dream."
- **12:8** — The Septuagint reads "the glory of the Lord" (ten doxan Kyriou) with an aorist verb ("has beheld") rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "form" or "likeness."
- **12:15** — The Septuagint reads "until Miriam was cleansed" (ekatharisthe) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "until Miriam was brought in again."
