## Text

**20:1** And the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people remained in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there.

**20:2** Now there was no water for the congregation, and they assembled together against Moses and Aaron.

**20:3** And the people railed against Moses, saying, "If only we had died in the destruction of our brethren before the Lord!

**20:4** And why have you brought up the congregation of the Lord into this wilderness, to kill us and our livestock?

**20:5** And why have you brought us up out of Egypt like this, to come to this place? It is a place where nothing is sown, nor are there figs, nor vines, nor pomegranates; neither is there water to drink."

**20:6** Then Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the congregation to the door of the tabernacle of testimony, and fell on their faces; and the glory of the Lord appeared to them.

**20:7** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:8** "Take the rod, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before them, and it shall yield its waters; and you shall bring forth water for them out of the rock, and give drink to the congregation and their livestock."

**20:9** So Moses took the rod that was before the Lord, just as the Lord commanded.

**20:10** And Moses and Aaron gathered the congregation together before the rock, and he said to them, "Hear me, you disobedient ones! Shall we bring forth water for you out of this rock?"

**20:11** Then Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock twice with the rod; and abundant water came forth, and the congregation and their livestock drank.

**20:12** Then the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not believe so as to sanctify Me before the sons of Israel, therefore you shall not bring this congregation into the land which I have given them."

**20:13** This is the water of Contradiction, because the sons of Israel contended before the Lord, and He was sanctified among them.

**20:14** Now Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom, saying, "Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the hardship that has befallen us,

**20:15** and our fathers went down into Egypt, and we sojourned in Egypt for many days, and the Egyptians afflicted us and our fathers;

**20:16** and we cried out to the Lord, and the Lord heard our voice, and sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt; and now we are in Kadesh, a city on the edge of your borders.

**20:17** We will pass through your land; we will not go through fields or through vineyards, nor will we drink water from your cistern; we will travel by the royal highway, and we will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your borders."

**20:18** Then Edom said to him, "You shall not pass through me; otherwise I will come out in war to meet you."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel said to him, "We will pass along by the mountain; and if I and the livestock drink of your water, I will pay you the price; yet the matter is nothing—we will pass along by the mountain."

**20:20** But he said, "You shall not pass through me." And Edom came out to meet him with a heavy multitude and with a strong hand.

**20:21** Thus Edom would not grant Israel to pass through his borders; so Israel turned away from him.

**20:22** Now they departed from Kadesh; and the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor.

**20:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron at Mount Hor, on the borders of the land of Edom, saying,

**20:24** "Let Aaron be gathered to his people, for you shall by no means enter into the land which I have given to the sons of Israel, because you provoked Me at the water of Reviling.

**20:25** Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up onto Mount Hor before all the congregation;

**20:26** and strip Aaron of his robe and put it on Eleazar his son; and let Aaron be gathered and die there."

**20:27** So Moses did just as the Lord commanded, and he brought him up onto Mount Hor before all the congregation.

**20:28** And he stripped Aaron of his garments and clothed Eleazar his son with them; and Aaron died on the summit of the mountain, and Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.

**20:29** And all the congregation saw that Aaron had departed; and all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.

## Translator Notes

- **20:13** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew place name Meribah as "Contradiction" (Greek antilogia) here, and renders it as "Reviling" (Greek loidoria) in 20:24.
- **20:19** — The Septuagint reads "along by the mountain" twice, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by the highway" and then "on foot."
- **20:24** — The Septuagint uses the second-person plural ("you shall by no means enter"), addressing both Moses and Aaron, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the third-person singular ("he shall not enter").
