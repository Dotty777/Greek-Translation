## Text

**33:1** And these are the stations of the children of Israel, as they went out from the land of Egypt with their host by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

**33:2** And Moses wrote down their departures and their stations by the word of the Lord. And these are the stations of their journey.

**33:3** They departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the Passover the children of Israel went out with a high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians,

**33:4** while the Egyptians were burying all the dead from among themselves whom the Lord had struck, every firstborn in the land of Egypt; and upon their gods the Lord executed vengeance.

**33:5** And the children of Israel, having departed from Rameses, encamped at Succoth.

**33:6** And they departed from Succoth and encamped at Buthan, which is a part of the wilderness.

**33:7** And they departed from Buthan and encamped by the mouth of Epiroth, which is opposite Baal Zephon, and they encamped opposite Migdol.

**33:8** And they departed from before Hiroth and passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness; and they walked a journey of three days through the wilderness and encamped at Bitterness.

**33:9** And they departed from Bitterness and came to Elim; and at Elim were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees; and they encamped there by the water.

**33:10** And they departed from Elim and encamped by the Red Sea.

**33:11** And they departed from the Red Sea and encamped in the Wilderness of Sin.

**33:12** And they departed from the Wilderness of Sin and encamped at Raphaca.

**33:13** And they departed from Raphaca and encamped at Elim.

**33:14** And they departed from Elim and encamped at Rephidim, and there was no water for the people to drink there.

**33:15** And they departed from Rephidim and encamped in the Wilderness of Sinai.

**33:16** And they departed from the Wilderness of Sinai and encamped at Graves of Craving.

**33:17** And they departed from Graves of Craving and encamped at Hazeroth.

**33:18** And they departed from Hazeroth and encamped at Rithmah.

**33:19** And they departed from Rithmah and encamped at Rimmon Perez.

**33:20** And they departed from Rimmon Perez and encamped at Lemona.

**33:21** And they departed from Lemona and encamped at Dessa.

**33:22** And they departed from Dessa and encamped at Makellath.

**33:23** And they departed from Makellath and encamped at Shepher.

**33:24** And they departed from Shepher and encamped at Haradath.

**33:25** And they departed from Haradath and encamped at Makheloth.

**33:26** And they departed from Makheloth and encamped at Kataath.

**33:27** And they departed from Kataath and encamped at Tarath.

**33:28** And they departed from Tarath and encamped at Mithkah.

**33:29** And they departed from Mithkah and encamped at Zalmonah.

**33:30** And they departed from Zalmonah and encamped at Moseroth.

**33:31** And they departed from Moseroth and encamped at Banaia.

**33:32** And they departed from Banaia and encamped at Mount Gadgad.

**33:33** And they departed from Mount Gadgad and encamped at Setebatha.

**33:34** And they departed from Setebatha and encamped at Sebrona.

**33:35** And they departed from Sebrona and encamped at Ezion Geber.

**33:36** And they departed from Ezion Geber and encamped in the Wilderness of Zin; and they departed from the Wilderness of Zin and encamped in the Wilderness of Paran, that is, Kadesh.

**33:37** And they departed from Kadesh and encamped at Mount Hor, near the land of Edom.

**33:38** And Aaron the priest went up by the command of the Lord, and died there in the fortieth year of the departure of the children of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.

**33:39** And Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**33:40** And the Canaanite, king of Arad—and he dwelt in the land of Canaan—heard when the children of Israel were coming in.

**33:41** And they departed from Mount Hor and encamped at Zalmonah.

**33:42** And they departed from Zalmonah and encamped at Phino.

**33:43** And they departed from Phino and encamped at Soboth.

**33:44** And they departed from Soboth and encamped at Gai, on the far side, at the borders of Moab.

**33:45** And they departed from Gai and encamped at Dibon Gad.

**33:46** And they departed from Dibon Gad and encamped at Almon Diblathaim.

**33:47** And they departed from Almon Diblathaim and encamped upon the mountains of Abarim, opposite Nebo.

**33:48** And they departed from the mountains of Abarim and encamped on the west of Moab, by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**33:49** And they encamped by the Jordan between Aesimoth as far as Belsa, which is toward the west of Moab.

**33:50** And the Lord spoke to Moses on the west of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,

**33:51** "Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You are crossing the Jordan into the land of Canaan;

**33:52** and you shall destroy all those who dwell in the land from before your face, and you shall remove their watchtowers, and all their molten idols you shall destroy, and all their pillars you shall remove.

**33:53** And you shall destroy all those who dwell in the land and dwell in it, for to you I have given their land for an inheritance.

**33:54** And you shall divide their land for an inheritance by lot according to their tribes; to the larger you shall increase their possession, and to the smaller you shall diminish their possession; wherever his name goes forth, there it shall be his; according to the tribes of your families you shall inherit.

**33:55** But if you do not destroy those who dwell upon the land from before your face, then it shall be that those whom you leave of them shall be thorns in your eyes and darts in your sides, and they shall be hostile upon the land in which you shall dwell;

**33:56** and it shall come to pass that just as I had resolved to do to them, I will do to you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — The Greek text preserves several distinct place names across this itinerary compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Buthan (v. 6, for Etham), the mouth of Epiroth and Hiroth (vv. 7–8, translating Pi- as 'mouth'), Raphaca (v. 12), Elim (v. 13, for Alush), Lemona (v. 20), Dessa (v. 21), Makellath (v. 22), Kataath (v. 26), Zalmonah (v. 29, for Hashmonah), Banaia (v. 31), Setebatha (v. 33), Sebrona (v. 34), Phino (v. 42), Soboth (v. 43), Gai on the far side (v. 44), and Aesimoth to Belsa (v. 49).
- **33:8** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Marah literally as Pikriai ('Bitterness'), and similarly renders Kibroth Hattaavah in verses 16–17 as Mnemasin epithymias ('Graves of Craving').
- **33:36** — The Septuagint records an extra stage here, distinguishing the encampment in the Wilderness of Zin from a further departure and encampment in the Wilderness of Paran, which is Kadesh.
- **33:48** — Literally 'on the west of Moab' (also in vv. 49–50), where the Greek interprets the Hebrew word for plains or steppes by the related term for evening or west.
- **33:52** — The Greek uses 'destroy' (also in vv. 53, 55) rather than 'drive out,' and renders the Canaanite cultic sites as 'watchtowers' and 'pillars.'
