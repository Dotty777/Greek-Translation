## Text

**18:1** And the Lord said to Aaron, saying, "You and your sons and the house of your ancestral family shall receive the firstfruits of the holy things; you and your sons shall bear the sins of your priesthood.

**18:2** And your brethren, the tribe of Levi, the clan of your father, bring near to yourself, and let them be joined to you and minister to you; and you and your sons with you shall be before the tabernacle of the testimony.

**18:3** And they shall keep your charges and the charges of the tabernacle; only they shall not draw near to the holy vessels and to the altar, and neither they nor you shall die.

**18:4** And they shall be joined to you, and they shall keep the charges of the tabernacle of the testimony according to all the ministries of the tabernacle; and the outsider shall not draw near to you.

**18:5** And you shall keep the charges of the holy things and the charges of the altar, and there shall be no wrath among the children of Israel.

**18:6** And I have taken your brethren the Levites from the midst of the children of Israel, a gift given to the Lord, to perform the ministries of the tabernacle of the testimony;

**18:7** and you and your sons with you shall guard your priesthood, in every manner concerning the altar and that which is within the veil, and you shall perform the ministries as a gift of your priesthood; and the outsider who comes near shall die."

**18:8** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, "And behold, I have given you the keeping of the firstfruits from all the things consecrated to Me by the children of Israel; to you I have given them as a gift of honor, and to your sons after you, as a perpetual statute.

**18:9** And this shall be yours from the consecrated holy things of the offerings made by fire: from all their gifts, and from all their sacrifices, and from all their trespass offerings, and from all the sin offerings, whatever they render to Me from all the holy things, shall belong to you and to your sons.

**18:10** In the Holy of Holies you shall eat them; every male shall eat them, you and your sons; they shall be holy to you.

**18:11** And this shall be yours of the firstfruits of their gifts, from all the presentation offerings of the children of Israel; to you I have given them, and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:12** Every firstfruit of olive oil and every firstfruit of wine, their firstfruit of grain, whatever they give to the Lord, to you I have given them.

**18:13** All the first produce, whatever is in their land, whatever they bring to the Lord, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:14** Everything devoted among the children of Israel shall be yours.

**18:15** And everything that opens the womb of all flesh, which they offer to the Lord, from man to beast, shall be yours; nevertheless, the firstborn of men shall surely be redeemed with a ransom, and the firstborn of unclean beasts you shall redeem.

**18:16** And its redemption shall be from one month old; the valuation shall be five shekels according to the holy shekel, which is twenty obols.

**18:17** However, the firstborn of calves and the firstborn of sheep and the firstborn of goats you shall not redeem; they are holy; and you shall pour out their blood against the altar, and the fat you shall offer up as an offering made by fire for a sweet aroma to the Lord;

**18:18** and the flesh shall be yours; even the breast of the presentation offering, and as with the right shoulder, it shall be yours.

**18:19** Every contribution of the holy things, whatever the children of Israel set apart to the Lord, I have given to you and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute; it is a covenant of perpetual salt before the Lord for you and for your seed after you."

**18:20** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, "In their land you shall have no inheritance, and there shall be no portion for you among them, because I am your portion and your inheritance in the midst of the children of Israel.

**18:21** And to the sons of Levi, behold, I have given every tithe in Israel as an allotment in return for their ministries, all the ministry that they perform in the tabernacle of the testimony.

**18:22** And the children of Israel shall no longer draw near to the tabernacle of the testimony, to bear deadly sin.

**18:23** And the Levite himself shall perform the ministry of the tabernacle of the testimony, and they shall bear their sins, a perpetual statute throughout their generations; and in the midst of the children of Israel they shall not inherit an inheritance.

**18:24** For the tithes of the children of Israel, whatever they separate to the Lord as a contribution, I have given to the Levites as an allotment; therefore I have said to them, 'In the midst of the children of Israel they shall not inherit an allotment.'"

**18:25** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:26** "And to the Levites you shall speak and say to them, 'When you receive from the children of Israel the tithe which I have given you from them as an allotment, then you yourselves shall set apart from it a contribution to the Lord, a tithe from the tithe.

**18:27** And your contributions shall be reckoned to you as grain from the threshing floor and as a contribution from the winepress.

**18:28** Thus you yourselves shall also set them apart from the contributions of the Lord, from all your tithes which you receive from the children of Israel; and from them you shall give a contribution to Aaron the priest.

**18:29** From all your gifts you shall set apart a contribution to the Lord, or from all the firstfruits the consecrated part of it.'

**18:30** And you shall say to them, 'When you set apart the firstfruit from it, then it shall be reckoned to the Levites as produce from the threshing floor and as produce from the winepress,

**18:31** and you shall eat it in every place, you and your households, because this is your wages in return for your ministries in the tabernacle of the testimony;

**18:32** and you shall not bear sin on account of it, when you set apart the firstfruit from it; and the holy things of the children of Israel you shall not profane, lest you die.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — Greek reads "shall receive the firstfruits of the holy things" (τὰς ἀπαρχὰς τῶν ἁγίων), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary."
- **18:8** — Greek γέρας means a "gift of honor" or privileged portion, rendering the Hebrew term for a consecrated/anointed share.
- **18:16** — Greek renders the Hebrew weight gerah with ὀβολοί ("obols"), a Greek coin unit here valued at twenty to the holy shekel.
- **18:28** — Greek includes the pronoun αὐτούς ("them") before "you yourselves" and omits "of the Lord" before "a contribution to Aaron the priest."
