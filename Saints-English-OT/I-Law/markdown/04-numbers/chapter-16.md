## Text

**16:1** Now Korah the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram the sons of Eliab, and On the son of Peleth, the son of Reuben, spoke;

**16:2** and they rose up before Moses, with two hundred and fifty men of the sons of Israel, leaders of the congregation, summoned to the council, and men of renown.

**16:3** They banded together against Moses and Aaron and said, "Let it be enough for you, for all the congregation—every one of them—are holy, and the Lord is among them. Why then do you rise up over the congregation of the Lord?"

**16:4** And when Moses heard this, he fell on his face,

**16:5** and he spoke to Korah and to all his congregation, saying, "God has examined and knows those who are His and those who are holy, and He has brought them near to Himself; and those whom He has chosen for Himself, He has brought near to Himself.

**16:6** Do this: take censers for yourselves, Korah and all his congregation,

**16:7** and put fire in them, and place incense upon them before the Lord tomorrow; and it shall be that the man whom the Lord chooses, he is holy. Let it be enough for you, sons of Levi."

**16:8** And Moses said to Korah, "Hear me, sons of Levi.

**16:9** Is this a small thing to you, that the God of Israel has separated you from the congregation of Israel and has brought you near to Himself, to perform the services of the tabernacle of the Lord and to stand before the tabernacle to serve them,

**16:10** and that He has brought you near, and all your brethren the sons of Levi with you? And do you seek to serve as priests also?

**16:11** Thus you and all your congregation are gathered together against God; and who is Aaron, that you murmur against him?"

**16:12** And Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram the sons of Eliab; and they said, "We will not come up.

**16:13** Is this a small thing, that you have brought us up into a land flowing with milk and honey, to slay us in the wilderness, that you altogether rule over us?

**16:14** Even if you had brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey and had given us an inheritance of field and vineyards, you would have put out the eyes of those men. We will not come up."

**16:15** Then Moses was exceedingly indignant, and said to the Lord, "Do not pay regard to their offering; I have not taken anything desirable from any one of them, nor have I harmed any one of them."

**16:16** And Moses said to Korah, "Sanctify your congregation, and be ready before the Lord, you and they, and Aaron, tomorrow;

**16:17** and take, each of you, his censer, and you shall place incense upon them, and you shall bring before the Lord each one his censer, two hundred and fifty censers; and you and Aaron, each one his censer."

**16:18** So each man took his censer, and they put fire in them, and laid incense upon it; and Moses and Aaron stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.

**16:19** And Korah assembled all his congregation against them by the door of the tabernacle of testimony. Then the glory of the Lord appeared to all the congregation.

**16:20** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**16:21** "Separate yourselves from the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them at once."

**16:22** And they fell on their faces and said, "O God, God of the spirits and of all flesh, if one man has sinned, shall the wrath of the Lord be upon all the congregation?"

**16:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:24** "Speak to the congregation, saying, 'Withdraw from all around the congregation of Korah.'"

**16:25** Then Moses arose and went to Dathan and Abiram, and all the elders of Israel went with him.

**16:26** And he spoke to the congregation, saying, "Separate yourselves from the tents of these harsh men, and do not touch anything of all that belongs to them, lest you perish together with all your belongings."

**16:27** So they withdrew from all around the tent of Korah; and Dathan and Abiram came out and stood by the doors of their tents, with their wives, their children, and their dependents.

**16:28** And Moses said, "By this you shall know that the Lord has sent me to do all these works, for they are not from myself:

**16:29** if these men die according to the death of all men, and if their visitation shall be according to the visitation of all men, then the Lord has not sent me.

**16:30** But if the Lord shows it by a marvel, and the earth opens its mouth and swallows them up, with their households and their tents and all that belongs to them, and they go down alive into Hades, then you shall know that these men have provoked the Lord."

**16:31** Now as he finished speaking all these words, the earth split apart beneath them,

**16:32** and the earth opened and swallowed them up, and their households, and all the men who were with Korah, and their livestock.

**16:33** So they and all that was theirs went down alive into Hades; and the earth covered them, and they perished from the midst of the congregation.

**16:34** And all Israel who were around them fled at their cry, for they were saying, "Lest the earth swallow us up!"

**16:35** And fire came forth from the Lord and devoured the two hundred and fifty men who were offering the incense.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Greek text reads "spoke" rather than the Hebrew "took," presenting Korah and his companions as initiating their challenge through speech.
- **16:5** — The Greek renders the Hebrew consonants for "in the morning" as a verb, yielding "God has examined and knows those who are His," phrasing later echoed in 2 Timothy 2:19.
- **16:13** — Unlike the Hebrew ("out of a land"), the Greek reads "into a land flowing with milk and honey," and continues in v. 14 with a contrafactual clause ("Even if you had brought us...").
- **16:15** — The Greek reads "anything desirable" (epithymema) where the Hebrew reads "a single donkey," reflecting a difference between similar Hebrew roots.
- **16:26** — The Greek text here reads "your belongings" (apartia hymon) rather than the variant "their sin" (hamartia auton), warning the congregation not to perish with all their possessions.
