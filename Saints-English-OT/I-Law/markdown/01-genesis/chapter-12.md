## Text

**12:1** Now the Lord said to Abram, "Go forth from your land, and from your kindred, and from your father's house, to the land which I shall show you.

**12:2** And I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you and magnify your name, and you shall be blessed;

**12:3** and I will bless those who bless you, and those who curse you I will curse; and in you all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed."

**12:4** So Abram departed just as the Lord had spoken to him, and Lot went with him. Now Abram was seventy-five years old when he went out from Haran.

**12:5** And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all their possessions which they had acquired, and every soul whom they had acquired from Haran, and they went forth to journey into the land of Canaan; and they came into the land of Canaan.

**12:6** And Abram passed through its length as far as the place of Shechem, to the high oak; and the Canaanites then dwelt in the land.

**12:7** Then the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "To your seed I will give this land." And there Abram built an altar to the Lord, who had appeared to him.

**12:8** And he departed from there to the mountain east of Bethel, and pitched his tent there, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east; and there he built an altar to the Lord and called upon the name of the Lord.

**12:9** Then Abram set out, and journeying on, he encamped in the wilderness.

**12:10** Now there was a famine in the land; and Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine grew severe in the land.

**12:11** And it came to pass, when Abram drew near to enter Egypt, that Abram said to Sarai his wife, "I know that you are a woman of fair countenance;

**12:12** therefore it shall come to pass, when the Egyptians see you, that they will say, 'This is his wife'; and they will slay me, but you they will keep alive.

**12:13** Say therefore, 'I am his sister,' that it may go well with me because of you, and my soul shall live on account of you."

**12:14** And it came to pass, when Abram entered Egypt, that the Egyptians beheld his wife, that she was exceedingly fair,

**12:15** and the princes of Pharaoh saw her and praised her to Pharaoh, and they brought her in to Pharaoh.

**12:16** And they dealt well with Abram on her account; and he came to have sheep and calves and donkeys, menservants and maidservants, mules and camels.

**12:17** But God afflicted Pharaoh and his house with great and grievous afflictions concerning Sarai, the wife of Abram.

**12:18** Then Pharaoh called Abram and said, "What is this you have done to me, that you did not declare to me that she is your wife?

**12:19** Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her to myself as a wife? Now therefore, behold, your wife is before you; take her and be gone."

**12:20** And Pharaoh commanded men concerning Abram to escort him away, along with his wife and all that belonged to him, and Lot with him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek reads "and you shall be blessed" (an adjective describing Abram's state), whereas the Hebrew text reads "and be a blessing."
- **12:6** — The Greek renders the location as "the high oak," interpreting rather than transliterating the Hebrew name "Moreh."
- **12:16** — The Greek uses the plural "they dealt well" and lists "calves" and "mules," where the Hebrew text has the singular "he dealt well" with "oxen" and "female donkeys."
