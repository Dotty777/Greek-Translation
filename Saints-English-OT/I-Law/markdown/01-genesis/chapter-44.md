## Text

**44:1** And Joseph commanded him who was over his house, saying, "Fill the men's sacks with provisions, as much as they are able to carry, and put each man's silver in the mouth of his sack,

**44:2** and put my silver cup into the sack of the youngest, and the price of his grain." And it was done according to the word of Joseph, just as he had spoken.

**44:3** The morning dawned, and the men were sent away, they and their donkeys.

**44:4** Now when they had gone out of the city and had not gone far, Joseph said to him who was over his house, saying, "Arise, pursue after the men, and you shall overtake them and say to them, 'Why have you repaid me evil for good?

**44:5** Why have you stolen my silver cup? Is this not the one from which my lord drinks? And he surely practices divination by it; you have accomplished evil in what you have done.'"

**44:6** So when he found them, he spoke to them according to these words.

**44:7** And they said to him, "Why does my lord speak according to these words? Far be it from your servants to do this thing.

**44:8** If indeed we returned to you from the land of Canaan the silver which we found in our sacks, how then would we steal silver or gold from the house of your lord?

**44:9** With whomever of your servants the cup is found, let him die; and we also shall be servants to our lord."

**44:10** And he said, "Now also as you say, so shall it be: the man with whom the cup is found, he shall be my servant, and you shall be innocent."

**44:11** And they hastened, and each man lowered his sack to the ground, and each man opened his sack.

**44:12** And he searched, beginning with the eldest until he came to the youngest; and he found the cup in Benjamin's sack.

**44:13** And they tore their garments, and each man placed his sack upon his donkey, and they returned to the city.

**44:14** Then Judah and his brethren came in to Joseph, while he was still there; and they fell to the ground before him.

**44:15** And Joseph said to them, "What is this deed you have done? Do you not know that a man such as I surely practices divination?"

**44:16** And Judah said, "What shall we answer my lord, or what shall we speak, or how shall we be justified? God has found out the iniquity of your servants. Behold, we are household servants to our lord, both we and he with whom the cup was found."

**44:17** And Joseph said, "Far be it from me to do this thing! The man with whom the cup was found, he shall be my servant; as for you, go up in safety to your father."

**44:18** Then Judah drew near to him and said, "I plead with you, my lord, let your servant speak a word before you, and do not be angry with your servant; for you are next after Pharaoh.

**44:19** My lord, you asked your servants, saying, 'Do you have a father or a brother?'

**44:20** And we said to my lord, 'We have an aged father, and a younger child of his old age; and his brother died, and he alone was left to his father, and his father has loved him.'

**44:21** Then you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me, and I will care for him.'

**44:22** And we said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father; and if he leaves his father, he will die.'

**44:23** But you said to your servants, 'Unless your youngest brother comes down with you, you shall not see my face again.'

**44:24** And it came to pass when we went up to your servant, our father, that we reported to him the words of my lord.

**44:25** And our father said to us, 'Go again, buy us a little food.'

**44:26** But we said, 'We shall not be able to go down; yet if our youngest brother goes down with us, we will go down; for we shall not be able to see the face of the man if our youngest brother is not with us.'

**44:27** Then your servant our father said to us, 'You know that my wife bore me two sons,

**44:28** and the one went out from me, and you said, "He has been devoured by wild beasts," and I have not seen him since.

**44:29** Therefore if you take this one also from my presence, and infirmity befalls him along the way, then you will bring down my old age with sorrow to Hades.'

**44:30** Now therefore, if I go in to your servant, our father, and the lad is not with us, and his soul hangs upon this one's soul,

**44:31** then it shall come to pass, when he sees that the lad is not with us, that he will die; and your servants will bring down the old age of your servant, our father, with grief to Hades.

**44:32** For your servant became surety for the boy from my father, saying, 'If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, I shall have sinned against my father all my days.'

**44:33** Now therefore, I will remain with you as a servant in place of the boy, a household servant of my lord; and let the boy go up with his brethren.

**44:34** For how shall I go up to my father if the boy is not with us, lest I see the evils that will find my father?"

## Translator Notes

- **44:4** — The Greek text includes the additional question "Why have you stolen my silver cup?"—whose Greek interrogative phrase is split across the boundary of verses 4 and 5—which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **44:18** — The Greek reads "you are next after Pharaoh" (literally "after Pharaoh"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you are like Pharaoh."
- **44:20** — The Greek reads "he alone was left to his father," contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "of his mother."
- **44:29** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for harm as "infirmity" (sickness) and uses "old age" and "Hades" where the Hebrew has gray hair and Sheol.
