## Text

**7:1** And the Lord God said to Noah, "Enter into the ark, you and all your household, for I have seen you to be righteous before Me in this generation.

**7:2** Of the clean livestock bring in to yourself seven by seven, male and female; and of the livestock that are not clean, two by two, male and female;

**7:3** also of the clean birds of heaven seven by seven, male and female, and of all the birds that are not clean two by two, male and female, to sustain seed upon all the earth.

**7:4** For after seven days more I am bringing rain upon the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out every living being that I have made from the face of the earth."

**7:5** And Noah did all things that the Lord God commanded him.

**7:6** Now Noah was six hundred years old, and the flood was upon the earth.

**7:7** And Noah, and his sons, and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him entered into the ark because of the water of the flood.

**7:8** And of the birds, and of the clean livestock, and of the livestock that are not clean, and of the birds, and of all the creeping things upon the earth,

**7:9** two by two they entered to Noah into the ark, male and female, just as God had commanded him.

**7:10** And it came to pass after the seven days that the water of the flood came upon the earth.

**7:11** In the six hundredth year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, on that day all the fountains of the abyss burst forth, and the floodgates of heaven were opened;

**7:12** and the rain was upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.

**7:13** On that same day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and the wife of Noah and the three wives of his sons with him, entered into the ark.

**7:14** And all the wild beasts according to kind, and all the livestock according to kind, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth according to kind, and every bird according to kind,

**7:15** entered to Noah into the ark, two by two, male and female, of all flesh in which is the spirit of life.

**7:16** And those that were entering, male and female of all flesh, went in just as God had commanded Noah; and the Lord God shut the ark from outside him.

**7:17** And the flood came upon the earth for forty days and forty nights upon the earth; and the water increased and lifted up the ark, and it was raised up from the earth.

**7:18** And the water prevailed and increased exceedingly upon the earth; and the ark was borne upon the water.

**7:19** And the water prevailed most exceedingly upon the earth, and it covered all the high mountains that were under heaven.

**7:20** Fifteen cubits upward the water rose, and it covered all the high mountains.

**7:21** And all flesh that moves upon the earth died, of birds and of livestock and of wild beasts, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth, and every man;

**7:22** and all things that have the breath of life, and everyone who was upon the dry land, died.

**7:23** And He blotted out every living being that was upon the face of all the earth, from man to livestock, and creeping things, and the birds of heaven; and they were blotted out from the earth. And Noah alone was left, and those who were with him in the ark.

**7:24** And the water was raised high upon the earth for one hundred and fifty days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — The rare Greek noun anastema (literally "that which rises" or "stands," rendered "living being") also appears in verse 23 to encompass all living creatures upon the earth.
- **7:11** — The Greek dates the onset of the flood to the twenty-seventh day of the second month—and likewise the ark's resting in 8:4 to the twenty-seventh day of the seventh month—differing from the seventeenth day in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
