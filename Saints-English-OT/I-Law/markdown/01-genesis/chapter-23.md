## Text

**23:1** Now the life of Sarah was one hundred and twenty-seven years.

**23:2** And Sarah died in the city of Arbok, which is in the valley; this is Hebron in the land of Canaan. And Abraham came to lament for Sarah and to mourn.

**23:3** Then Abraham arose from his dead, and Abraham spoke to the sons of Heth, saying,

**23:4** "I am an alien and a sojourner with you; therefore give me possession of a burial place with you, and I will bury my dead there."

**23:5** And the sons of Heth answered Abraham, saying,

**23:6** "Not so, my lord, but listen to us: you are a king from God among us. In our choicest tombs bury your dead; for none of us will withhold his tomb from you, to bury your dead there."

**23:7** Then Abraham rose up and bowed down to the people of the land, the sons of Heth.

**23:8** And Abraham spoke to them, saying, "If you have it in your soul that I should bury my dead out of my sight, listen to me, and speak for me to Ephron the son of Saar,

**23:9** and let him give me the double cave which belongs to him, which is in a part of his field; for its full worth in silver give it to me among you as the possession of a tomb."

**23:10** Now Ephron was sitting in the midst of the sons of Heth; and Ephron the Hittite answered and spoke to Abraham in the hearing of the sons of Heth and of all who entered the city, saying,

**23:11** "Attend to me, my lord, and listen to me: the field and the cave that is in it I give to you; in the presence of my citizens I have given it to you; bury your dead."

**23:12** And Abraham bowed down before the people of the land.

**23:13** And he spoke in the ears of Ephron before all the people of the land, "Since you are on my side, listen to me: take the silver for the field from me, and bury my dead there."

**23:14** And Ephron answered Abraham, saying,

**23:15** "No, my lord, for I have heard: four hundred didrachmas of silver—between me and you, what could this be? As for you, bury your dead."

**23:16** And Abraham listened to Ephron; and Abraham rendered to Ephron the silver of which he had spoken in the hearing of the sons of Heth, four hundred didrachmas of silver, approved by merchants.

**23:17** And the field of Ephron, which was at the double cave that is before Mamre—the field and the cave that was in it, and every tree that was in the field, which is within its borders all around—was established

**23:18** to Abraham as a possession in the presence of the sons of Heth and of all who entered the city.

**23:19** After these things Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the double cave of the field, which is opposite Mamre; this is Hebron in the land of Canaan.

**23:20** And the field and the cave that was in it were confirmed to Abraham as the possession of a burial place by the sons of Heth.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Greek reads "city of Arbok" (corresponding to Hebrew Kiriath-arba) and adds the clarifying phrase "which is in the valley."
- **23:9** — The Greek translates the Hebrew name Machpelah literally as "the double cave" (also in vv. 17, 19); in v. 8 it spells Ephron's father's name "Saar" (Hebrew Zohar).
- **23:13** — The Greek text reads the second-person imperative "bury" (thapson) rather than the first-person "I will bury" (thapso) found in some manuscripts and the Hebrew.
- **23:15** — The Greek reads "for I have heard" rather than "listen to me," and states the price in "didrachmas" (two-drachma silver coins, also in v. 16), corresponding to Hebrew shekels.
