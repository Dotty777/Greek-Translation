## Text

**39:1** Now Joseph was brought down to Egypt; and Potiphar, the eunuch of Pharaoh, the chief cook, an Egyptian man, purchased him from the hands of the Ishmaelites, who had brought him down there.

**39:2** And the Lord was with Joseph, and he was a prosperous man; and he was in the house with the Egyptian master.

**39:3** And his master knew that the Lord was with him, and that whatever he did, the Lord caused to prosper in his hands.

**39:4** So Joseph found favor before his master, and he was pleasing to him; and he appointed him over his house, and all things that belonged to him he placed into the hand of Joseph.

**39:5** Now it came to pass, after he was appointed over his house and over all that belonged to him, that the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian for the sake of Joseph; and the blessing of the Lord was upon all his possessions, in the house and in the field.

**39:6** And he turned over all that belonged to him into the hands of Joseph, and he knew nothing of his own affairs except the bread which he himself ate. Now Joseph was handsome in form and exceedingly fair in appearance.

**39:7** And it came to pass after these things that his master's wife cast her eyes upon Joseph, and said, "Lie with me."

**39:8** But he was unwilling, and said to his master's wife, "If my master knows nothing in his house because of me, and has given all that belongs to him into my hands,

**39:9** and surpasses me in nothing in his house, nor has he withheld anything from me except you, because you are his wife—how then shall I do this wicked thing and sin before God?"

**39:10** Now whenever she spoke to Joseph day after day, he did not listen to her to sleep with her, to be with her.

**39:11** And it came to pass on a certain such day that Joseph went into the house to do his work, and no one was inside the house.

**39:12** And she caught him by his garments, saying, "Lie with me." But leaving his garments behind, he fled and went outside.

**39:13** And it came to pass, when she saw that he had left his garments in her hands and had fled and gone outside,

**39:14** that she called those who were in the house and spoke to them, saying, "See, he has brought in to us a Hebrew servant to mock us; he came in to me, saying, 'Lie with me,' and I cried out with a loud voice.

**39:15** And when he heard that I lifted up my voice and cried out, leaving his garments with me, he fled and went outside."

**39:16** And she kept the garments with her until the master came into his house.

**39:17** And she spoke to him according to these words, saying, "The Hebrew servant, whom you brought in to us, came in to me to mock me, and said to me, 'Lie with me.'

**39:18** And when he heard that I lifted up my voice and cried out, he left his garments with me, and fled and went outside."

**39:19** Now it came to pass, when the master heard the words of his wife, which she spoke to him, saying, "Thus your servant has done to me," that he was enraged with wrath.

**39:20** And the master took Joseph and cast him into the stronghold, into the place where the prisoners of the king are held there in the stronghold.

**39:21** Yet the Lord was with Joseph and poured out mercy upon him, and granted him favor before the chief jailer.

**39:22** And the chief jailer committed the prison into the hand of Joseph, and all the captives who were in the prison, and all that they did there.

**39:23** The chief jailer knew nothing because of him; for all things were in the hand of Joseph, because the Lord was with him; and whatever he did, the Lord caused to prosper in his hands.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — The Greek renders Potiphar (Πετεφρῆς) as Pharaoh's "eunuch" (εὐνοῦχος) and "chief cook" (ἀρχιμάγειρος), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text designates him an officer and captain of the guard.
- **39:4** — The Greek reads "he was pleasing to him" (εὐηρέστει δὲ αὐτῷ), rather than "he served him" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **39:8** — The Greek opens Joseph's reply with a conditional "If" (Εἰ) and reads "because of me" (δι᾽ ἐμέ), linking verses 8 and 9 into a single sentence.
- **39:20** — The Greek terms the royal prison "the stronghold" (τὸ ὀχύρωμα; also in 40:14) and includes the concluding phrase "there in the stronghold."
