## Text

**9:1** And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth and have dominion over it.

**9:2** And the dread and the fear of you shall be upon all the wild beasts of the earth, and upon all the birds of heaven, and upon all things that move upon the earth, and upon all the fish of the sea; into your hands I have given them.

**9:3** And every creeping thing that lives shall be for food for you; just as the green herbs, I have given all things to you.

**9:4** Yet flesh with the blood of life you shall not eat;

**9:5** for indeed, your own blood of your lives I will require; from the hand of all the wild beasts I will require it, and from the hand of a brother man I will require the life of man.

**9:6** Whoever sheds the blood of man, in return for his blood shall it be shed; for in the image of God I made man.

**9:7** But as for you, increase and multiply, and fill the earth and multiply upon the earth."

**9:8** And God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying,

**9:9** "As for Me, behold, I establish My covenant with you and with your seed after you,

**9:10** and with every living soul that is with you—of birds and of livestock, and with all the wild beasts of the earth, as many as are with you, from all that came out of the ark.

**9:11** And I will establish My covenant with you: no flesh shall die anymore from the water of the flood, and there shall no longer be a flood of water to destroy all the earth."

**9:12** And the Lord God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant which I give between Me and you, and between every living soul that is with you, for everlasting generations:

**9:13** I set My bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of a covenant between Me and the earth.

**9:14** And it shall come to pass, when I gather clouds over the earth, that My bow shall appear in the cloud;

**9:15** and I will remember My covenant which is between Me and you and between every living soul in all flesh; and the water shall no longer become a flood, so as to blot out all flesh.

**9:16** And My bow shall be in the cloud, and I will look upon it to remember an everlasting covenant between Me and every living soul in all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:17** And God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant which I have made between Me and all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:18** Now the sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was the father of Canaan.

**9:19** These three are the sons of Noah, and from them people were scattered over all the earth.

**9:20** And Noah began to be a man cultivating the ground, and he planted a vineyard.

**9:21** And he drank of the wine and became drunk, and he lay uncovered in his house.

**9:22** And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and going out he told his two brothers outside.

**9:23** And Shem and Japheth took the garment, laid it upon both their backs, and walked backward and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were turned backward, and they did not see the nakedness of their father.

**9:24** Now Noah became sober from the wine, and he knew all that his younger son had done to him.

**9:25** And he said, "Cursed be Canaan; a house servant shall he be to his brothers."

**9:26** And he said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem, and Canaan shall be his servant.

**9:27** May God enlarge Japheth, and let him dwell in the houses of Shem; and let Canaan become their servant."

**9:28** And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.

**9:29** So all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek text includes the closing command "and have dominion over it," echoing Genesis 1:28, which does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:6** — Where the Hebrew reads "by man shall his blood be shed; for in the image of God He made man," the Greek reads "in return for his blood shall it be shed" and uses the first-person verb "I made."
- **9:21** — The Greek reads "in his house" (and "in the houses of Shem" in verse 27), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "tent" and "tents."
- **9:25** — The Greek renders the superlative Hebrew idiom "a servant of servants" as "a house servant" (pais oiketes).
