## Text

**14:1** Now it came to pass in the reign of Amraphel king of Shinar, that Arioch king of Ellasar, and Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Thalga king of nations,

**14:2** made war with Balla king of Sodom, and with Barsa king of Gomorrah, and Sennaar king of Admah, and Symobor king of Zeboiim, and with the king of Balak—this is Zoar.

**14:3** All these united in accord at the Salt Ravine—this is the Sea of Salt.

**14:4** Twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

**14:5** And in the fourteenth year Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him came, and cut down the giants in Ashtaroth Karnaim, and strong nations together with them, and the Somites in the city of Shaveh,

**14:6** and the Horites in the mountains of Seir, as far as the terebinth of Paran, which is in the wilderness.

**14:7** And turning back, they came to the Spring of Judgment—this is Kadesh—and cut down all the rulers of Amalek, and also the Amorites who dwelt in Hazazon Tamar.

**14:8** Then the king of Sodom, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Balak—this is Zoar—went out and arrayed themselves for battle against them in the Salt Valley,

**14:9** against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Thalgal king of nations, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar—these four kings against the five.

**14:10** Now the Salt Valley was filled with pits of asphalt; and the king of Sodom and the king of Gomorrah fled and fell there, and those who were left fled to the hill country.

**14:11** And he took all the cavalry of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their provisions, and they departed.

**14:12** And they also took Lot, the son of Abram's brother, and his belongings, and went away; for he was dwelling in Sodom.

**14:13** Then one of those who had escaped came and reported to Abram the migrant; now he was dwelling by the oak of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and the brother of Aunan, who were sworn allies of Abram.

**14:14** And when Abram heard that Lot his brother had been taken captive, he numbered his own homeborn servants, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued after them as far as Dan.

**14:15** And he fell upon them by night, he and his servants, and struck them, and pursued them as far as Chobal, which is on the left of Damascus.

**14:16** So he brought back all the cavalry of Sodom; and he brought back Lot his brother and all his possessions, as well as the women and the people.

**14:17** And the king of Sodom went out to meet him, after his return from the slaughter of Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him, to the Valley of Shaveh—this was the plain of the king.

**14:18** And Melchizedek king of Salem brought out loaves of bread and wine; and he was priest of God Most High.

**14:19** And he blessed Abram and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, who created heaven and earth;

**14:20** and blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies into your hand." And he gave him a tenth of all.

**14:21** Then the king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the men, and take the cavalry for yourself."

**14:22** And Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I will stretch out my hand to God Most High, who created heaven and earth,

**14:23** that I will not take from a thread to a sandal strap of all that is yours, lest you should say, 'I have made Abram rich'—

**14:24** except only what the young men have eaten, and the portion of the men who went with me, Eshcol, Aunan, and Mamre; these shall take a portion."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — The Greek reads "in the reign of Amraphel" (MT: "in the days of") and presents distinct forms of several royal names here and in v. 2: Thalga (v. 9: Thalgal; MT: Tidal), Balla (Bera), Barsa (Birsha), Sennaar (Shinab), Symobor (Shemeber), and Balak (Bela).
- **14:5** — The Greek translates Rephaim as "giants," renders "the Zuzim in Ham" as "strong nations together with them," and reads "the Somites" (corresponding to MT "the Emim") in "the city of Shaveh" (translating Kiriathaim as "the city").
- **14:11** — Here and in vv. 16 and 21, the Greek reads "cavalry" (collective feminine hippos), reflecting a Hebrew reading meaning "steeds" rather than the Masoretic "goods/possessions."
- **14:13** — The Greek renders "the Hebrew" etymologically as "the migrant" (perates, one who crosses over) and gives the ally's name as Aunan (MT: Aner); similarly, v. 15 reads Chobal where the MT has Hobah.
- **14:22** — The Greek uses the future tense "I will stretch out" (MT: "I have lifted") and reads "God Most High" without "the Lord."
