## Text

**19:1** Now the two angels came to Sodom in the evening, and Lot was sitting by the gate of Sodom. And when Lot saw them, he rose to meet them, and he bowed down with his face to the ground;

**19:2** and he said, "Behold, lords, turn aside to the house of your servant and lodge, and wash your feet; and rising early, you shall depart on your way." And they said, "No, rather we shall lodge in the open square."

**19:3** And he pressed them earnestly, and they turned aside to him. And they entered into his house; and he prepared a banquet for them and baked unleavened loaves for them, and they ate

**19:4** before lying down. And the men of the city, the Sodomites, surrounded the house, from young man to elder, all the people together.

**19:5** And they called out for Lot, and said to him, "Where are the men who came in to you this night? Bring them out to us, that we may be intimate with them."

**19:6** And Lot went out to them, and shut the door behind him.

**19:7** And he said to them, "By no means, brethren, do not act wickedly.

**19:8** Now I have two daughters who have not known a man; I will bring them out to you, and deal with them as it pleases you; only to these men do not do anything unjust, seeing that they have come under the shelter of my beams."

**19:9** And they said, "Stand back over there! You came in to sojourn; would you also pass judgment? Now therefore we will harm you more than them." And they pressed hard against the man, Lot, and drew near to break down the door.

**19:10** But the men stretched out their hands and pulled Lot inside to themselves into the house, and shut the door of the house;

**19:11** and the men who were at the door of the house they struck with blindness, from small to great; and they grew exhausted searching for the door.

**19:12** And the men said to Lot, "Is there anyone belonging to you here—sons-in-law, or sons, or daughters, or if there is anyone else belonging to you in the city—bring them out of this place;

**19:13** for we are about to destroy this place, because their outcry has risen high before the Lord, and the Lord has sent us to blot it out."

**19:14** And Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who had taken his daughters, and said, "Arise and go out of this place, for the Lord is about to blot out the city." But he seemed to be jesting before his sons-in-law.

**19:15** And when dawn was coming on, the angels hurried Lot, saying, "Arise, take your wife and your two daughters whom you have, and go out, lest you perish together in the iniquities of the city."

**19:16** And they were troubled; and the angels took hold of their hand, and of the hand of his wife, and of the hands of his two daughters, because the Lord spared him.

**19:17** And it came to pass, when they had brought them outside, that they said, "Surely save your own life; do not look around behind you, nor stand still in all the surrounding region; escape to the mountain, lest you be caught up with them."

**19:18** And Lot said to them, "I implore you, lord,

**19:19** since your servant has found mercy before you, and you have magnified your righteousness, which you show toward me in keeping my soul alive—yet I shall not be able to escape safely to the mountain, lest the calamities overtake me and I die;

**19:20** behold, this city is near enough for me to flee there, and it is small; there I shall be saved—is it not small?—and my soul shall live on your account."

**19:21** And he said to him, "Behold, I have regarded your countenance in this matter also, so as not to overthrow the city concerning which you have spoken;

**19:22** make haste therefore to escape there; for I cannot do a thing until you arrive there." Therefore he named the name of that city Zoar.

**19:23** The sun had risen upon the earth, and Lot entered into Zoar,

**19:24** and the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven,

**19:25** and He overthrew these cities in which Lot dwelt, and all the surrounding region, and all those dwelling in the cities, and all that sprang up from the earth.

**19:26** And his wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

**19:27** Now Abraham rose early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before the Lord,

**19:28** and he looked out over the face of Sodom and Gomorrah and over the face of the land of the surrounding region, and he saw; and behold, a flame of the earth was ascending like the vapor of a furnace.

**19:29** And it came to pass, when the Lord blotted out all the cities of the surrounding region, that God remembered Abraham and sent Lot out from the midst of the overthrow, when the Lord overthrew the cities in which Lot dwelt.

**19:30** And Lot went out of Zoar and settled in the mountain, and his two daughters with him; for he feared to dwell in Zoar. And he dwelt in the cave, he and his two daughters with him.

**19:31** And the elder said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no one upon the earth who will come in to us as is fitting for all the earth.

**19:32** Come therefore, let us give our father wine to drink and lie with him, and let us raise up seed from our father."

**19:33** And they gave their father wine to drink on that night; and the elder went in and lay with her father that night, and he did not know when she lay down and when she arose.

**19:34** And it came to pass on the next day that the elder said to the younger, "Behold, I lay yesterday with our father; let us give him wine to drink tonight also, and go in and lie with him, and let us raise up seed from our father."

**19:35** And they gave their father wine to drink on that night also; and the younger went in and lay with her father, and he did not know when she lay down and when she arose.

**19:36** And the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.

**19:37** And the elder bore a son, and she called his name Moab, saying, "From my father." He is the father of the Moabites to this present day.

**19:38** And the younger also bore a son, and she called his name Amman, "The son of my kindred." He is the father of the Ammanites to this present day.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — In the Greek text, "before lying down" completes the sentence from verse 3 ("and they ate before lying down"), rather than introducing the gathering of the men of Sodom as in the Masoretic Text.
- **19:16** — The Greek text reads "and they were troubled" and "took hold of their hand" (with plural verbs and pronoun), whereas the Masoretic Text describes Lot lingering and the messengers seizing his hand.
- **19:18** — Although Lot speaks to both angels ("to them"), he addresses them with the singular vocative "lord" (Greek kyrie), and the dialogue continues in the singular through verse 22.
- **19:38** — The Septuagint preserves the distinct name "Amman" with the explanatory gloss "the son of my kindred" (and "Ammanites"), corresponding to Hebrew Ben-Ammi and paralleling the explanatory etymology added in verse 37 ("From my father").
