## Text

**25:1** And Abraham again took a wife, whose name was Keturah.

**25:2** And she bore to him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.

**25:3** And Jokshan fathered Sheba, Teman, and Dedan; and the sons of Dedan were Reuel, Nabdeel, Asshurim, Datusieim, and Leummim.

**25:4** And the sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abira, and Thergama. All these were the sons of Keturah.

**25:5** And Abraham gave all his possessions to Isaac his son;

**25:6** and to the sons of his concubines Abraham gave gifts, and sent them away from Isaac his son while he was still living, eastward to the land of the east.

**25:7** Now these are the years of the days of Abraham's life which he lived: one hundred and seventy-five years.

**25:8** And breathing his last, Abraham died in a good old age, an old man and full of days, and was gathered to his people.

**25:9** And Isaac and Ishmael, his two sons, buried him in the double cave, in the field of Ephron the son of Zohar the Hittite, which faces Mamre,

**25:10** the field and the cave which Abraham had purchased from the sons of Heth; there they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife.

**25:11** And it came to pass after Abraham died that God blessed Isaac his son; and Isaac dwelt by the Well of Vision.

**25:12** Now these are the generations of Ishmael the son of Abraham, whom Hagar the maidservant of Sarah bore to Abraham.

**25:13** And these are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by name according to his generations: the firstborn of Ishmael was Nebaioth, and Kedar, and Nabdeel, and Mibsam,

**25:14** and Mishma, and Iduma, and Massa,

**25:15** and Choddan, and Tema, and Jetur, and Naphish, and Kedemah.

**25:16** These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names in their tents and in their encampments—twelve princes according to their nation.

**25:17** And these are the years of the life of Ishmael: one hundred and thirty-seven years; and breathing his last, he died and was gathered to his kindred.

**25:18** And he dwelt from Havilah as far as Suel, which faces Egypt until one comes to the Assyrians; in the presence of all his brethren he dwelt.

**25:19** And these are the generations of Isaac the son of Abraham: Abraham fathered Isaac.

**25:20** Now Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Syrian from Mesopotamia, the sister of Laban the Syrian, to himself as wife.

**25:21** And Isaac pleaded with the Lord concerning Rebekah his wife, because she was barren; and God listened to him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.

**25:22** But the children leaped within her; and she said, "If it is going to be this way with me, why is this happening to me?" So she went to inquire of the Lord.

**25:23** And the Lord said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples shall be separated from your belly; one people shall surpass the other people, and the greater shall serve the lesser."

**25:24** And the days were fulfilled for her to give birth, and behold, there were twins in her womb.

**25:25** And the firstborn son came out ruddy, altogether shaggy like a hairy hide; and she named him Esau.

**25:26** And after this his brother came out, with his hand grasping the heel of Esau; and she called his name Jacob. Now Isaac was sixty years old when Rebekah bore them.

**25:27** And the young men grew up; and Esau was a man skilled in hunting, a man of the fields, while Jacob was a guileless man, dwelling in a house.

**25:28** And Isaac loved Esau, because his game was food for him; but Rebekah loved Jacob.

**25:29** Now Jacob boiled a stew; and Esau came from the plain, fainting.

**25:30** And Esau said to Jacob, "Give me a taste of this red stew, for I am fainting." Therefore his name was called Edom.

**25:31** And Jacob said to Esau, "Sell to me this day your birthright."

**25:32** And Esau said, "Behold, I am about to die, so what is this birthright to me?"

**25:33** And Jacob said to him, "Swear to me this day." And he swore to him; and Esau sold the birthright to Jacob.

**25:34** Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew; and he ate and drank, and arose and went away. Thus Esau despised the birthright.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — The Septuagint includes Teman among Jokshan's sons and adds Reuel and Nabdeel to the sons of Dedan, also reading Datusieim where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Letushim (and similarly Thergama in v. 4 for Eldaah).
- **25:18** — The Septuagint reads Suel where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Shur, and renders Ishmael's settlement in the presence of his brethren with the verb "dwelt."
- **25:27** — Greek aplastos ("guileless" or "unfeigned") and "dwelling in a house" reflect the Septuagint's distinctive rendering of Jacob's character and abode (compared with "dwelling in tents" in the Masoretic Text).
