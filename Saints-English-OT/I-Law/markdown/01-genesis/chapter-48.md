## Text

**48:1** Now it came to pass after these things that it was reported to Joseph, "Your father is ill." And taking his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, he came to Jacob.

**48:2** And it was told to Jacob, saying, "Behold, your son Joseph is coming to you." And Israel gathered his strength and sat up on the bed.

**48:3** And Jacob said to Joseph, "My God appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan and blessed me,

**48:4** and He said to me, 'Behold, I will cause you to increase and multiply, and I will make you into assemblies of nations, and I will give this land to you and to your seed after you for an everlasting possession.'

**48:5** Now therefore your two sons who were born to you in Egypt before I came to you in Egypt are mine; Ephraim and Manasseh shall be mine, as Reuben and Simeon.

**48:6** But the offspring whom you may father after these shall be under the name of their brethren; they shall be called in their inheritances.

**48:7** And as for me, when I was coming from Mesopotamia of Syria, Rachel your mother died in the land of Canaan, as I was drawing near to the hippodrome of Chabratha of the land to come to Ephrathah; and I buried her on the road of the hippodrome—this is Bethlehem."

**48:8** And when Israel saw the sons of Joseph, he said, "Who are these to you?"

**48:9** And Joseph said to his father, "They are my sons, whom God has given me here." And Jacob said, "Bring them near to me, that I may bless them."

**48:10** Now the eyes of Israel were dim from old age, and he could not see. And he brought them near to him, and he kissed them and embraced them.

**48:11** And Israel said to Joseph, "Behold, I have not been deprived of your face; and behold, God has also shown me your seed."

**48:12** And Joseph brought them out from his knees, and they bowed down to him with their faces to the earth.

**48:13** And taking his two sons, Ephraim in his right hand, at Israel's left, and Manasseh at his left, at Israel's right, Joseph brought them near to him.

**48:14** And Israel stretched out his right hand and laid it upon the head of Ephraim—now he was the younger—and his left hand upon the head of Manasseh, crossing his hands.

**48:15** And he blessed them and said, "The God before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac were well-pleasing, the Lord who sustains me from youth until this day,

**48:16** the Angel who delivers me from all evils, may He bless these children; and my name shall be called upon them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and may they be multiplied into a great multitude upon the earth."

**48:17** Now when Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand upon the head of Ephraim, it seemed grievous to him; and Joseph took hold of his father's hand to remove it from the head of Ephraim to the head of Manasseh.

**48:18** And Joseph said to his father, "Not so, father, for this one is the firstborn; place your right hand upon his head."

**48:19** And he was unwilling, but said, "I know, child, I know. He also shall become a people, and he also shall be exalted; yet his younger brother shall be greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations."

**48:20** And he blessed them on that day, saying, "In you shall Israel be blessed, saying, 'May God make you as Ephraim and as Manasseh.'" And he set Ephraim before Manasseh.

**48:21** And Israel said to Joseph, "Behold, I am dying, and God shall be with you and bring you back to the land of your fathers.

**48:22** And I give to you Shechem, a choice portion above your brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorites with my sword and bow."

## Translator Notes

- **48:3** — The Greek reads "my God" (ho theos mou) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic "God Almighty" (El Shaddai).
- **48:7** — The Greek renders the Hebrew measure of distance both as "hippodrome" (racecourse) and by transliterating it as "Chabratha," and reads "Mesopotamia of Syria" where the Hebrew has "Paddan."
- **48:15** — The Greek reads "blessed them" and "the Lord" (ho kyrios) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "blessed Joseph" and "the God."
- **48:22** — The Greek treats the Hebrew word for "ridge" or "portion" (shekhem) as the city name Sikima (Shechem), pairing it with a feminine relative pronoun ("which I took").
