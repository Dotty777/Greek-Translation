## Text

**1:1** In the beginning God made heaven and the earth.

**1:2** Now the earth was unseen and unformed, and darkness lay over the abyss; and the Spirit of God was borne above the water.

**1:3** And God said, "Let light come into being"; and light came into being.

**1:4** And God beheld the light, that it was good; and God divided between the light and the darkness.

**1:5** And God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. And evening came, and morning came, one day.

**1:6** And God said, "Let a firmament come into being in the midst of the water, and let it divide between water and water." And it came to pass so.

**1:7** And God made the firmament, and God divided between the water which was beneath the firmament and the water which was above the firmament.

**1:8** And God called the firmament Heaven; and God saw that it was good. And evening came, and morning came, a second day.

**1:9** And God said, "Let the water which is beneath heaven be gathered together into one gathering, and let the dry land appear." And it came to pass so. And the water which was beneath heaven was gathered together into its gatherings, and the dry land appeared.

**1:10** And God called the dry land Earth, and the gatherings of the waters He called Seas; and God saw that it was good.

**1:11** And God said, "Let the earth sprout forth herbage of grass, sowing seed according to kind and according to likeness, and the fruit-bearing tree yielding fruit, whose seed is in itself according to kind for likeness upon the earth." And it came to pass so.

**1:12** And the earth brought forth herbage of grass, sowing seed according to kind and according to likeness, and the fruit-bearing tree yielding fruit, whose seed is in itself according to kind upon the earth. And God saw that it was good.

**1:13** And evening came, and morning came, a third day.

**1:14** And God said, "Let luminaries come into being in the firmament of heaven for the lighting of the earth, and to rule the day and the night, and to divide between the day and the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and for years;

**1:15** and let them be for lighting in the firmament of heaven, so as to shine upon the earth." And it came to pass so.

**1:16** And God made the two great luminaries, the greater luminary for the rule of the day and the lesser luminary for the rule of the night, and the stars.

**1:17** And God set them in the firmament of heaven, so as to shine upon the earth,

**1:18** and to rule the day and the night, and to divide between the light and the darkness; and God saw that it was good.

**1:19** And evening came, and morning came, a fourth day.

**1:20** And God said, "Let the waters bring forth creeping things of living souls, and winged creatures flying upon the earth across the firmament of heaven." And it came to pass so.

**1:21** And God made the great sea creatures, and every soul of creeping animals which the waters brought forth according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to kind; and God saw that they were good.

**1:22** And God blessed them, saying, "Increase and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds be multiplied upon the earth."

**1:23** And evening came, and morning came, a fifth day.

**1:24** And God said, "Let the earth bring forth a living soul according to kind, four-footed beasts and creeping things and wild beasts of the earth according to kind." And it came to pass so.

**1:25** And God made the wild beasts of the earth according to kind, and the cattle according to kind, and all the creeping things of the earth according to their kind; and God saw that they were good.

**1:26** And God said, "Let Us make man according to Our image and according to likeness; and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the birds of heaven, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over all the creeping things that creep upon the earth."

**1:27** And God made man; according to the image of God He made him; male and female He made them.

**1:28** And God blessed them, saying, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it; and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the birds of heaven, and over all the cattle, and over all the earth, and over all the creeping things that creep upon the earth."

**1:29** And God said, "Behold, I have given to you every seed-bearing herb sowing seed, which is upon all the earth, and every tree that has in itself fruit of seed for sowing; to you it shall be for food,

**1:30** and to all the wild beasts of the earth, and to all the birds of heaven, and to every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth, which has in itself a soul of life, even every green herb for food." And it came to pass so.

**1:31** And God beheld all things that He had made, and behold, they were exceedingly good. And evening came, and morning came, a sixth day.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek reads epoiesen ('made') and singular ton ouranon ('heaven'), distinct from the Hebrew 'created the heavens.'
- **1:2** — Greek aoratos kai akataskeuastos literally means 'unseen and unformed/unfurnished,' reflecting the Septuagint's own rendering of the Hebrew pair.
- **1:6** — Greek stereoma denotes a firm, solid expanse ('firmament').
- **1:9** — The second half of the verse, explicitly narrating the waters gathering into their gatherings and the dry land appearing, is a Septuagint plus retained from the Greek text.
- **1:30** — Greek psychen zoes literally means 'a soul of life,' rendered literally here to distinguish the genitive phrase from 'living soul' (psyche zosa) in verses 20 and 24.
