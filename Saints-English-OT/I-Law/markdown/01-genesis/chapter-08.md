## Text

**8:1** And God remembered Noah, and all the wild beasts, and all the livestock, and all the birds, and all the creeping things that were with him in the ark; and God brought a wind upon the earth, and the water abated;

**8:2** and the fountains of the abyss and the floodgates of heaven were uncovered, and the rain from heaven was restrained,

**8:3** and the water kept receding as it departed from the earth; the water receded and diminished after one hundred and fifty days.

**8:4** And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

**8:5** And the water continually diminished until the tenth month; and in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, the summits of the mountains were seen.

**8:6** And it came to pass after forty days that Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made,

**8:7** and he sent out the raven to see whether the water had abated; and going out, it did not return until the water had dried up from the earth.

**8:8** And he sent out the dove after it, to see whether the water had abated from the earth.

**8:9** And the dove, finding no resting place for its feet, returned to him into the ark, for water was upon the whole face of all the earth; and stretching out his hand, he took it and brought it in to himself into the ark.

**8:10** And waiting yet another seven days, he again sent out the dove from the ark.

**8:11** And the dove returned to him toward evening, and it had a sprig of an olive leaf in its mouth; and Noah knew that the water had abated from the earth.

**8:12** And waiting yet another seven days, he again sent out the dove, and it did not return to him anymore.

**8:13** And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of the life of Noah, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the water vanished from the earth; and Noah uncovered the roof of the ark and saw that the water had vanished from the face of the earth.

**8:14** And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dried.

**8:15** And the Lord God spoke to Noah, saying,

**8:16** "Go out from the ark, you and your wife and your sons and the wives of your sons with you,

**8:17** and bring out with you all the wild beasts that are with you, and all flesh from birds to livestock, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth; and increase and multiply upon the earth."

**8:18** And Noah went out, and his wife, and his sons, and the wives of his sons with him;

**8:19** and all the wild beasts, and all the livestock, and every bird, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth, according to their kind, went out from the ark.

**8:20** And Noah built an altar to God, and took of all the clean livestock and of all the clean birds, and offered up a whole burnt offering upon the altar.

**8:21** And the Lord God smelled a fragrance of sweet savor, and the Lord God spoke, having pondered it, "I will never again curse the earth because of the works of men, for the mind of man is intently set upon evil things from youth; therefore I will never again strike down all living flesh as I have done.

**8:22** All the days of the earth, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and spring shall not cease day and night."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek text here reads apekalyphthēsan ("were uncovered"), whereas other Septuagint witnesses read epekalyphthēsan ("were covered over" or "closed"), agreeing with the Hebrew sense of shutting off the waters.
- **8:5** — The Greek places the sighting of the mountain summits in the eleventh month, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text dates it to the tenth month.
- **8:7** — The Greek explicitly states that the raven "did not return" (ouch hypestrepsen) until the water dried up, differing from the Hebrew reading that it went out to and fro.
- **8:22** — The Greek pairs "summer and spring" (theros kai ear) rather than "summer and winter," and places "day and night" (hēmeran kai nykta) in the adverbial accusative modifying "shall not cease."
