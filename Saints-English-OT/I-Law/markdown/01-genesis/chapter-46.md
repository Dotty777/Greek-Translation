## Text

**46:1** Now Israel departed, he and all that belonged to him, and they came to the Well of the Oath; and there he offered a sacrifice to the God of his father Isaac.

**46:2** And God spoke to Israel in a vision of the night, saying, "Jacob, Jacob!" And he said, "What is it?"

**46:3** And He said, "I am the God of your fathers; do not fear to go down to Egypt, for I will make you into a great nation there.

**46:4** And I Myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I will bring you up again in the end; and Joseph shall lay his hands upon your eyes."

**46:5** Then Jacob rose up from the Well of the Oath; and the sons of Israel lifted up their father, and their dependents, and their wives onto the wagons which Joseph had sent to carry him.

**46:6** And taking up their goods and all the possessions which they had acquired from the land of Canaan, Jacob entered into Egypt, and all his seed with him,

**46:7** sons and the sons of his sons with him, daughters and daughters of his sons with him; and he brought all his seed into Egypt.

**46:8** Now these are the names of the sons of Israel who entered into Egypt—Jacob and his sons: the firstborn of Jacob was Reuben.

**46:9** And the sons of Reuben were Hanoch and Phallud, Hezron and Carmi.

**46:10** And the sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachim, Zohar, and Samuel the son of the Canaanite woman.

**46:11** And the sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**46:12** And the sons of Judah were Er, Onan, Shelom, Perez, and Zerah; but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. And the sons of Perez were Hezron and Jemuel.

**46:13** And the sons of Issachar were Tola, Puvah, Jasuph, and Zambram.

**46:14** And the sons of Zebulun were Sered, Hasron, and Jahleel.

**46:15** These are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Mesopotamia of Syria, along with Dinah his daughter; all the souls, sons and daughters, were thirty-three.

**46:16** And the sons of Gad were Zephon, Haggi, Shuni, Thasoban, Aedis, Arodi, and Areli.

**46:17** And the sons of Asher were Jimnah, Jessai, Jeul, Beriah, and Saar their sister. And the sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel.

**46:18** These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob—sixteen souls.

**46:19** And the sons of Rachel, the wife of Jacob, were Joseph and Benjamin.

**46:20** And sons were born to Joseph in the land of Egypt, whom Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of Heliopolis, bore to him: Manasseh and Ephraim. And sons were born to Manasseh, whom his Syrian concubine bore to him: Machir; and Machir fathered Gilead. And the sons of Ephraim, the brother of Manasseh, were Sutalaam and Taam; and the sons of Sutalaam: Edem.

**46:21** And the sons of Benjamin were Bela, Chobor, and Ashbel. And the sons of Bela were Gera, Naaman, Anchis, Rosh, Mamphein, and Hophimin; and Gera fathered Arad.

**46:22** These are the sons of Rachel, whom Jacob fathered; all the souls were eighteen.

**46:23** And the sons of Dan: Hushim.

**46:24** And the sons of Naphtali were Jahzeel, Guni, Issaar, and Shillem.

**46:25** These are the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to Rachel his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob; all the souls were seven.

**46:26** Now all the souls who entered with Jacob into Egypt, those who came forth from his loins, besides the wives of the sons of Jacob, all the souls were sixty-six.

**46:27** And the sons of Joseph who were born to him in the land of Egypt were nine souls. All the souls of the house of Jacob who entered into Egypt were seventy-five.

**46:28** Now he sent Judah ahead of them to Joseph, to meet him near Heroopolis, into the land of Rameses.

**46:29** And Joseph yoked his chariots and went up to meet Israel his father near Heroopolis; and appearing to him, he fell upon his neck and wept with abundant weeping.

**46:30** And Israel said to Joseph, "From now on I may die, since I have seen your face, for you are still alive."

**46:31** Then Joseph said to his brethren, "I will go up and report to Pharaoh, and I will say to him, 'My brethren and the house of my father, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me;

**46:32** and the men are shepherds, for they have been keepers of livestock; and they have brought their livestock, and their cattle, and all that belongs to them.'

**46:33** Therefore, if Pharaoh calls you and says to you, 'What is your occupation?'

**46:34** you shall say, 'We, your servants, are keepers of livestock from youth until now, both we and our fathers,' that you may dwell in the land of Goshen of Arabia; for every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — "The Well of the Oath" translates Greek to phrear tou horkou, the Septuagint's literal rendering of the Hebrew place name Beersheba.
- **46:10** — Several genealogical names in vv. 9–24 preserve distinct Septuagint forms differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including Phallud (MT Pallu), Jachim (MT Jachin), Samuel (MT Shaul), Shelom (MT Shelah), Jemuel in v. 12 (MT Hamul), Jasuph and Zambram (MT Job and Shimron), Hasron in v. 14 (MT Elon), Thasoban and Aedis (MT Ezbon and Eri), Jessai, Jeul, and Saar (MT Ishvah, Ishvi, and Serah), Chobor and Anchis (MT Becher and Ehi), and Issaar (MT Jezer).
- **46:20** — "Heliopolis" translates Greek Heliou polis (City of the Sun), the Septuagint name for Hebrew On. The Septuagint also records additional descendants of Manasseh and Ephraim (Machir, Gilead, Sutalaam, Taam, and Edem) not listed here in the Masoretic Text.
- **46:27** — In keeping with the expanded genealogy of v. 20, the Septuagint counts nine sons born to Joseph in Egypt and a total of seventy-five souls of the house of Jacob entering Egypt (matching Acts 7:14), whereas the Masoretic Text reads two and seventy.
- **46:28** — "Heroopolis" (Heroon polis) is the Septuagint place name in vv. 28–29 where the Masoretic Text has Goshen; in v. 34, the Septuagint likewise specifies "the land of Goshen of Arabia."
