## Text

**45:1** And Joseph could not endure all those who stood by him, but he said, "Send everyone out from me!" And no one stood by Joseph any longer when Joseph made himself known to his brethren.

**45:2** And he uttered a cry with weeping; and all the Egyptians heard it, and it was heard in the house of Pharaoh.

**45:3** And Joseph said to his brethren, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt. Is my father still alive?" And the brethren could not answer him, for they were troubled.

**45:4** And he said, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.

**45:5** Now therefore, do not be grieved, nor let it seem harsh to you that you sold me here; for God sent me before you for life.

**45:6** For this is the second year of famine upon the earth, and there remain yet five years in which there shall be neither plowing nor reaping.

**45:7** For God sent me before you to leave a remnant of you upon the earth, and to nourish a great survival of you.

**45:8** Now therefore, it was not you who sent me here, but God; and He has made me as a father to Pharaoh, and lord of all his house, and ruler of all the land of Egypt.

**45:9** Hasten therefore, go up to my father, and say to him, 'Thus says your son Joseph: God has made me lord of all the land of Egypt; come down therefore to me, and do not delay.

**45:10** And you shall dwell in the land of Goshen of Arabia, and you shall be near me—you and your sons and your sons' sons, your sheep and your cattle, and all that is yours there.

**45:11** And I will nourish you there—for there are still five years of famine—lest you and your sons and all your possessions be wiped out.'

**45:12** Behold, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my mouth that speaks to you.

**45:13** Report therefore to my father all my glory in Egypt and all that you have seen, and make haste and bring my father down here."

**45:14** And falling upon the neck of Benjamin his brother, he fell upon him; and Benjamin wept upon his neck.

**45:15** And having kissed all his brethren, he wept over them; and after these things his brethren spoke with him.

**45:16** And the report was spread abroad to the house of Pharaoh, saying, "Joseph's brethren have come!" And Pharaoh and his attendants rejoiced.

**45:17** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Say to your brethren, 'Do this: fill your carts and depart to the land of Canaan,

**45:18** and taking your father and your possessions, come to me; and I will give you of all the good things of Egypt, and you shall eat the marrow of the land.'

**45:19** And you, command these things: to take for themselves wagons out of the land of Egypt for your little ones and wives, and taking up your father, come;

**45:20** and do not let your eyes spare your goods, for all the good things of Egypt shall be yours."

**45:21** And the sons of Israel did so. And Joseph gave them wagons according to the things spoken by Pharaoh the king, and he gave them provisions for the journey.

**45:22** And to all of them he gave two robes each, and to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of gold and five changes of robes.

**45:23** And to his father he sent in like manner, and ten donkeys carrying of all the good things of Egypt, and ten mules carrying bread for his father for the journey.

**45:24** So he sent his brethren away, and they departed; and he said to them, "Do not be angry along the way."

**45:25** And they went up out of Egypt, and came to the land of Canaan to Jacob their father,

**45:26** and they reported to him, saying, "Your son Joseph is alive, and he rules over all the land of Egypt." And Jacob's mind was stunned, for he did not believe them.

**45:27** But they spoke to him all the words spoken by Joseph, whatever he had said to them; and when he saw the wagons which Joseph had sent to take him up, the spirit of Jacob their father revived.

**45:28** And Israel said, "It is a great thing to me if my son Joseph is still alive; I will go and see him before I die."

## Translator Notes

- **45:3** — In the Greek text, Joseph identifies himself as "your brother, whom you sold into Egypt" already in verse 3 as well as in verse 4, and verse 4 omits the command to come near found in the Hebrew.
- **45:10** — The Greek reads "Gesem of Arabia" (rendered here as "Goshen of Arabia"), identifying Goshen with the Arabian district of eastern Egypt.
- **45:14** — The Greek text here reads "he fell upon him" rather than "he wept," while stating that Benjamin wept upon Joseph's neck.
- **45:22** — The Greek specifies "three hundred pieces of gold" given to Benjamin, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies pieces of silver.
