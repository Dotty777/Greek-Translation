## Text

**37:1** Now Jacob dwelt in the land where his father had sojourned, in the land of Canaan. And these are the generations of Jacob.

**37:2** Joseph, being seventeen years old, was shepherding the sheep with his brethren, being a youth, with the sons of Bilhah and with the sons of Zilpah, his father's wives; and they brought an evil report against Joseph to Israel their father.

**37:3** Now Jacob loved Joseph above all his sons, because he was the son of his old age; and he made him a tunic of many colors.

**37:4** And when his brethren saw that his father loved him more than all his sons, they hated him and could not speak anything peaceable to him.

**37:5** Now Joseph dreamed a dream and told it to his brethren.

**37:6** And he said to them, "Hear this dream which I have dreamed.

**37:7** I thought that you were binding sheaves in the midst of the field, and my sheaf arose and stood upright; and your sheaves turned around and bowed down to my sheaf."

**37:8** And his brethren said to him, "Shall you indeed reign as king over us, or shall you indeed have dominion over us?" And they hated him yet the more because of his dreams and because of his words.

**37:9** Then he saw another dream, and recounted it to his father and to his brethren, and said, "Behold, I have dreamed another dream: it was as though the sun and the moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me."

**37:10** And his father rebuked him and said, "What is this dream that you have dreamed? Shall we indeed come—both I and your mother and your brethren—to bow down to you to the earth?"

**37:11** And his brethren envied him, but his father kept the word.

**37:12** Now his brethren went to pasture their father's sheep in Shechem.

**37:13** And Israel said to Joseph, "Are not your brethren shepherding in Shechem? Come, let me send you to them." And he said to him, "Behold, here I am."

**37:14** And Israel said to him, "Go and see whether your brethren and the sheep are well, and bring back word to me." So he sent him out of the valley of Hebron, and he came to Shechem.

**37:15** And a man found him wandering in the field; and the man asked him, saying, "What do you seek?"

**37:16** And he said, "I seek my brethren; tell me where they are pasturing."

**37:17** And the man said to him, "They have departed from here; for I heard them saying, 'Let us go to Dothan.'" So Joseph went after his brethren and found them at Dothan.

**37:18** Now they saw him from afar before he drew near to them, and they went to slay him.

**37:19** And each said to his brother, "Behold, that dreamer is coming.

**37:20** Now therefore, come, let us slay him and cast him into one of the pits, and we shall say, 'An evil wild beast has devoured him.' And we shall see what his dreams are."

**37:21** But when Reuben heard it, he delivered him out of their hands and said, "We shall not strike him to take his life."

**37:22** And Reuben said to them, "Do not shed blood, but cast him into one of the pits that are in the wilderness, and do not lay a hand on him"—that he might deliver him out of their hands and restore him to his father.

**37:23** And it came to pass, when Joseph came to his brethren, that they stripped Joseph of the tunic of many colors that was upon him,

**37:24** and they took him and cast him into the pit; and that pit had no water.

**37:25** Then they sat down to eat bread; and lifting up their eyes they looked, and behold, Ishmaelite travelers were coming from Gilead, and their camels were laden with incense, resin, and myrrh, and they were going to carry them down to Egypt.

**37:26** And Judah said to his brethren, "What profit is it if we slay our brother and conceal his blood?

**37:27** Come, let us sell him to these Ishmaelites, and do not let our hands be upon him, for he is our brother and our flesh." And his brethren listened.

**37:28** And the Midianite men, the merchants, passed by; and they drew and lifted Joseph up out of the pit, and sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of gold; and they brought Joseph down to Egypt.

**37:29** Then Reuben returned to the pit, and he did not see Joseph in the pit; and he tore his garments.

**37:30** And he returned to his brethren and said, "The lad is no more; and as for me, where shall I go now?"

**37:31** Then they took Joseph's tunic, slaughtered a kid of the goats, and stained the tunic with blood.

**37:32** And they sent the tunic of many colors and brought it to their father, and said, "We have found this; examine whether it is your son's tunic or not."

**37:33** And he recognized it and said, "It is my son's tunic! An evil wild beast has devoured him; a wild beast has carried off Joseph."

**37:34** Then Jacob tore his garments, and put sackcloth upon his loins, and mourned for his son for some days.

**37:35** And all his sons and daughters gathered together and came to comfort him, yet he refused to be comforted, saying, "I shall go down to my son in mourning to Hades." Thus his father wept for him.

**37:36** Meanwhile the Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, the eunuch of Pharaoh, the chief cook.

## Translator Notes

- **37:2** — The Greek uses a plural verb ("they brought") with Joseph as object, stating that Joseph's brothers brought an evil report against him to Israel, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text makes Joseph the one bringing an evil report about them.
- **37:28** — The Greek reads "twenty pieces of gold," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "twenty pieces of silver."
- **37:34** — The Greek text here reads "for some days," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and many Greek manuscripts) reads "for many days."
- **37:36** — The Greek renders Potiphar's titles literally as "eunuch" and "chief cook," reflecting the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew court titles often translated "officer" and "captain of the guard."
