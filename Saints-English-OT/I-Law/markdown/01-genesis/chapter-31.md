## Text

**31:1** Now Jacob heard the words of the sons of Laban, saying, "Jacob has taken away all that belonged to our father, and from what belonged to our father he has acquired all this glory."

**31:2** And Jacob beheld the countenance of Laban, and behold, it was not toward him as yesterday and the day before.

**31:3** Then the Lord said to Jacob, "Return to the land of your father and to your kindred, and I will be with you."

**31:4** So Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field where the flocks were,

**31:5** and said to them, "I see your father's countenance, that it is not with me as yesterday and the day before; yet the God of my father was with me.

**31:6** And you yourselves know that with all my strength I have served your father.

**31:7** Yet your father has defrauded me and changed my wages of the ten lambs, but God did not permit him to do me harm.

**31:8** If he spoke thus, 'The speckled shall be your wages,' then all the sheep would bear speckled; and if he said, 'The white shall be your wages,' then all the sheep would bear white.

**31:9** And God has taken away all the livestock of your father and has given them to me.

**31:10** And it came to pass, at the time when the sheep were conceiving, that I saw with my eyes in a dream, and behold, the he-goats and the rams mounting upon the sheep and the goats were streaked with white, and speckled, and ash-gray dappled.

**31:11** Then the angel of God said to me in a dream, 'Jacob, Jacob!' And I said, 'What is it?'

**31:12** And He said, 'Lift up your eyes and see the he-goats and the rams mounting upon the sheep and the goats, streaked with white, and speckled, and ash-gray dappled; for I have seen all that Laban is doing to you.

**31:13** I am the God who appeared to you in the place where you anointed a pillar to Me there and vowed a vow to Me there; now therefore arise, depart from this land, and go to the land of your birth, and I will be with you.'"

**31:14** Then Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, "Is there still any portion or inheritance for us in our father's house?

**31:15** Are we not reckoned by him as strangers? For he has sold us, and has utterly devoured our silver.

**31:16** All the riches and the glory which God has taken away from our father shall be ours and our children's; now therefore, whatever God has said to you, do."

**31:17** Then Jacob arose and placed his wives and his children upon the camels,

**31:18** and he led away all his possessions and all his goods which he had acquired in Mesopotamia, and all that was his, to go to Isaac his father in the land of Canaan.

**31:19** Now Laban had gone to shear his sheep; and Rachel stole the idols of her father.

**31:20** And Jacob hid the matter from Laban the Syrian, by not telling him that he was fleeing.

**31:21** So he fled, he and all that was his, and crossed the river, and hastened toward the mountain of Gilead.

**31:22** Now it was reported to Laban the Syrian on the third day that Jacob had fled.

**31:23** And taking all his brethren with him, he pursued after him a journey of seven days, and overtook him in the mountain of Gilead.

**31:24** Now God came to Laban the Syrian in a dream by night and said to him, "Guard yourself, lest you ever speak evil with Jacob."

**31:25** So Laban overtook Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the mountain, and Laban stationed his brethren in the mountain of Gilead.

**31:26** And Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done? Why have you fled in secret, and robbed me by stealth, and led away my daughters like captives of the sword?

**31:27** And if you had told me, I would have sent you away with gladness and with music, with timbrels and harp.

**31:28** I was not counted worthy to kiss my children and my daughters; now indeed you have acted foolishly.

**31:29** And now my hand has power to do you harm; yet the God of your father spoke to me yesterday, saying, 'Guard yourself, lest you ever speak evil with Jacob.'

**31:30** Now therefore you have departed, for with great longing you have longed to go away to your father's house; yet why have you stolen my gods?"

**31:31** Then Jacob answered and said to Laban, "For I said, 'Lest you take away your daughters from me, and all that is mine.'"

**31:32** And Jacob said to him, "With whomever you find your gods, he shall not live before our brethren; recognize what of yours is with me, and take it." And he recognized nothing with him; for Jacob did not know that Rachel his wife had stolen them.

**31:33** Then Laban went in and searched the house of Leah, and did not find them; and going out of the house of Leah, he searched the house of Jacob and the house of the two maidservants, and did not find them; and he went also into the house of Rachel.

**31:34** Now Rachel had taken the idols and put them into the saddlebags of the camel, and sat upon them.

**31:35** And she said to her father, "Do not be displeased, lord; I cannot rise before you, for the custom of women is upon me." So Laban searched throughout the whole house, and did not find the idols.

**31:36** Then Jacob grew angry and contended with Laban; and Jacob answered and said to Laban, "What is my wrongdoing, and what is my sin, that you have hotly pursued after me?

**31:37** And since you have searched all the goods of my house, what have you found of all the goods of your house? Set it here before your brethren and my brethren, and let them judge between the two of us.

**31:38** These twenty years I have been with you; your sheep and your goats have not miscarried, and the rams of your sheep I have not eaten.

**31:39** That which was taken by wild beasts I did not bring to you; I made restitution for it myself, for thefts by day and thefts by night.

**31:40** By day I was scorched by the burning heat, and by the frost of the night, and my sleep departed from my eyes.

**31:41** These twenty years I have been in your house; I served you fourteen years for your two daughters, and six years for your sheep, and you have defrauded me of my wages by ten ewe-lambs.

**31:42** Unless the God of my father Abraham and the Fear of Isaac had been with me, surely now you would have sent me away empty; God has beheld my affliction and the labor of my hands, and rebuked you yesterday."

**31:43** Then Laban answered and said to Jacob, "Your daughters are my daughters, and your sons are my sons, and your livestock is my livestock, and all that you see is mine; yet what can I do today to these my daughters, or to their children whom they have borne?

**31:44** Now therefore, come, let us make a covenant, I and you, and it shall be for a witness between me and you." And Jacob said to him, "Behold, no one is with us; see, God is witness between me and you."

**31:45** Then Jacob took a stone and set it up as a pillar.

**31:46** And Jacob said to his brethren, "Gather stones." And they gathered stones and made a heap; and they ate and drank there upon the heap. And Laban said to him, "This heap bears witness between me and you today."

**31:47** And Laban called it Witness Heap, but Jacob called it Heap Bears Witness.

**31:48** And Laban said to Jacob, "Behold this heap and this pillar which I have set up between me and you; this heap bears witness, and this pillar bears witness." Therefore its name was called Heap Bears Witness,

**31:49** and The Vision, of which he said, "May God watch between me and you, because we shall depart one from another.

**31:50** If you afflict my daughters, if you take wives besides my daughters, see, no one is with us.

**31:52** For if I do not pass over to you, neither shall you pass over to me beyond this heap and this pillar for harm.

**31:53** The God of Abraham and the God of Nahor shall judge between us." And Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.

**31:54** Then Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain, and called his brethren, and they ate and drank, and slept on the mountain.

## Translator Notes

- **31:7** — The Septuagint reads "of the ten lambs" here (and "by ten ewe-lambs" in 31:41), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "ten times."
- **31:13** — The Greek text reads "in the place where you anointed a pillar to Me there," without the name Bethel found in the Hebrew text, and concludes with "and I will be with you."
- **31:33** — Following the Septuagint, Laban searches Leah's house first (rather than Jacob's) before searching the houses of Jacob, the two maidservants, and Rachel; the Greek renders the tents as "house" throughout.
- **31:47** — Rather than transliterating the Aramaic and Hebrew names Jegar-sahadutha and Galeed, the Septuagint translates them directly into Greek as "Witness Heap" and "Heap Bears Witness."
- **31:49** — The Septuagint translates the place name Mizpah into Greek as "The Vision," and rearranges the covenant verses (31:44–54) compared to the Hebrew text, so that verse 31:51 does not appear as a separate verse.
