## Text

**11:1** And all the earth was of one lip, and all had one voice.

**11:2** And it came to pass, as they moved from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar, and dwelt there.

**11:3** Then each man said to his neighbor, "Come, let us mold bricks and bake them with fire." And brick served them for stone, and asphalt was their mortar.

**11:4** And they said, "Come, let us build for ourselves a city and a tower whose top shall reach to heaven, and we shall make a name for ourselves, before we are scattered over the face of all the earth."

**11:5** And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower which the sons of men had built.

**11:6** And the Lord said, "Behold, there is one race and one lip for all; and this they have begun to do, and now nothing shall fail them of all that they undertake to do.

**11:7** Come, let Us go down and confound their tongue there, so that each may not understand the voice of his neighbor."

**11:8** So the Lord scattered them from there over the face of all the earth, and they ceased building the city and the tower.

**11:9** Therefore its name was called Confusion, because there the Lord confounded the lips of all the earth; and from there the Lord God scattered them over the face of all the earth.

**11:10** Now these are the generations of Shem: Shem was one hundred years old when he fathered Arphaxad, in the second year after the flood.

**11:11** And Shem lived five hundred years after he fathered Arphaxad, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:12** And Arphaxad lived one hundred and thirty-five years, and fathered Cainan.

**11:13** And Arphaxad lived four hundred and thirty years after he fathered Cainan, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died. And Cainan lived one hundred and thirty years, and fathered Shelah. And Cainan lived three hundred and thirty years after he fathered Shelah, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:14** And Shelah lived one hundred and thirty years, and fathered Eber.

**11:15** And Shelah lived three hundred and thirty years after he fathered Eber, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:16** And Eber lived one hundred and thirty-four years, and fathered Peleg.

**11:17** And Eber lived three hundred and seventy years after he fathered Peleg, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:18** And Peleg lived one hundred and thirty years, and fathered Reu.

**11:19** And Peleg lived two hundred and nine years after he fathered Reu, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:20** And Reu lived one hundred and thirty-two years, and fathered Serug.

**11:21** And Reu lived two hundred and seven years after he fathered Serug, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:22** And Serug lived one hundred and thirty years, and fathered Nahor.

**11:23** And Serug lived two hundred years after he fathered Nahor, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:24** And Nahor lived seventy-nine years, and fathered Terah.

**11:25** And Nahor lived one hundred and twenty-nine years after he fathered Terah, and he fathered sons and daughters, and died.

**11:26** And Terah lived seventy years, and fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

**11:27** Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran fathered Lot.

**11:28** And Haran died in the presence of Terah his father in the land where he was born, in the country of the Chaldeans.

**11:29** Then Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves: the name of Abram's wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, who was both the father of Milcah and the father of Iscah.

**11:30** Now Sarai was barren, and she did not bear children.

**11:31** And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his son's son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, his son's wife, and brought them out from the country of the Chaldeans to go into the land of Canaan; and he came as far as Haran, and dwelt there.

**11:32** And the days of Terah in Haran were two hundred and five years; and Terah died in Haran.

## Translator Notes

- **11:9** — The Greek text translates the name of the tower as "Confusion" (Synchysis) rather than transliterating "Babel," matching the verb "confounded" in the same verse and the mention of both "the city and the tower" in verse 8.
- **11:12** — The Septuagint genealogy includes an additional generation, Cainan (cf. Luke 3:36), along with distinct patriarchal ages and the closing refrain "and died" for each patriarch.
- **11:28** — Here and in verse 31 the Greek reads "the country of the Chaldeans," without the city name "Ur" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **11:29** — Here and through chapter 16 the Greek text spells her name "Sara" (later changed to "Sarra" in 17:15), rendered with the standard English pre-renaming form "Sarai."
