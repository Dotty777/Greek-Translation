## Text

**24:1** And Abraham was aged, advanced in days, and the Lord blessed Abraham in all things.

**24:2** And Abraham said to his servant, the eldest of his household, who ruled over all that was his, "Place your hand under my thigh,

**24:3** and I will make you swear by the Lord, the God of heaven and of the earth, that you shall not take a wife for my son Isaac from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell;

**24:4** but you shall go to my land where I was born and to my tribe, and you shall take a wife for my son Isaac from there."

**24:5** And the servant said to him, "Perhaps the woman will not be willing to go back with me to this land; shall I return your son to the land from which you came out?"

**24:6** And Abraham said to him, "Take heed to yourself that you do not return my son there.

**24:7** The Lord, the God of heaven and the God of the earth, who took me from the house of my father and from the land where I was born, who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, 'To you I will give this land, and to your seed'—He will send His angel before you, and you shall take a wife for my son Isaac from there.

**24:8** And if the woman is not willing to go with you to this land, you shall be clear from this oath; only do not return my son there."

**24:9** And the servant placed his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and swore to him concerning this matter.

**24:10** And the servant took ten camels from the camels of his master, and of all the good things of his master with him; and he arose and journeyed to Mesopotamia, to the city of Nahor.

**24:11** And he made the camels lie down outside the city beside the well of water toward evening, at the time when the women who draw water go out.

**24:12** And he said, "O Lord, the God of my master Abraham, grant success before me today, and show mercy to my master Abraham.

**24:13** Behold, I stand by the spring of water, and the daughters of those who dwell in the city are coming out to draw water.

**24:14** And let it be that the maiden to whom I say, 'Lower your water jar that I may drink,' and she says to me, 'Drink, and I will also water your camels until they cease drinking'—she is the one You have prepared for Your servant Isaac; and by this I shall know that You have shown mercy to my master Abraham."

**24:15** And it came to pass, before he had finished speaking in his mind, that behold, Rebekah came out—she who was born to Bethuel, the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, the brother of Abraham—with her water jar upon her shoulders.

**24:16** Now the maiden was exceedingly fair in appearance; she was a virgin, and no man had known her. And she went down to the spring, filled her water jar, and came up.

**24:17** And the servant ran to meet her and said, "Give me a little water to drink from your water jar."

**24:18** And she said, "Drink, my lord." And she hastened and lowered the water jar onto her arm, and gave him drink until he ceased drinking.

**24:19** And she said, "I will draw water for your camels also, until all of them have drunk."

**24:20** And she hastened and emptied the water jar into the watering trough, and ran to the well to draw water, and drew water for all the camels.

**24:21** And the man observed her closely and kept silent, in order to know whether the Lord had prospered his way or not.

**24:22** And it came to pass, when all the camels had ceased drinking, that the man took golden earrings, each a drachma in weight, and two bracelets for her hands; their weight was ten gold pieces.

**24:23** And he questioned her and said, "Whose daughter are you? Tell me, is there a place with your father for us to lodge?"

**24:24** And she said to him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor."

**24:25** And she said to him, "There is both straw and much fodder with us, and a place to lodge."

**24:26** And the man, being well pleased, worshiped the Lord and said,

**24:27** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who has not forsaken righteousness and truth from my master; as for me, the Lord has guided me prosperously to the house of my master's brother."

**24:28** And the girl ran and reported to her mother's house according to these words.

**24:29** Now Rebekah had a brother whose name was Laban; and Laban ran out to the man at the spring.

**24:30** And it came to pass, when he saw the earrings and the bracelets on the hands of his sister, and when he heard the words of his sister Rebekah, saying, "Thus the man has spoken to me," that he came to the man as he stood by the camels at the spring.

**24:31** And he said to him, "Come, enter in—blessed be the Lord! Why do you stand outside? For I have prepared the house and a place for the camels."

**24:32** And the man entered into the house, and he unloaded the camels; and he gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water for his feet and for the feet of the men who were with him.

**24:33** And he set bread before them to eat. And he said, "I will certainly not eat until I have spoken my words." And they said, "Speak."

**24:34** And he said, "I am Abraham's servant.

**24:35** And the Lord has blessed my master exceedingly, and he has been exalted; and He has given him sheep and calves, silver and gold, male servants and female servants, camels and donkeys.

**24:36** And Sarah my master's wife bore one son to my master after he had grown old; and he has given him all that was his.

**24:37** And my master made me swear, saying, 'You shall not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I sojourn in their land;

**24:38** but rather you shall go to the house of my father and to my tribe, and take a wife for my son from there.'

**24:39** And I said to my master, 'Perhaps the woman will not go with me.'

**24:40** And he said to me, 'The Lord God, before whom I have been well pleasing—He will send His angel with you and prosper your way; and you shall take a wife for my son from my tribe and from the house of my father.

**24:41** Then you shall be guiltless from my curse; for when you come to my tribe and they do not give her to you, then you shall be guiltless from my oath.'

**24:42** And having come today to the spring, I said, 'O Lord, the God of my master Abraham, if You indeed prosper my way on which I now go,

**24:43** behold, I stand by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city will come out to draw water; and let it be that the maiden to whom I say, "Give me a little water to drink from your water jar,"

**24:44** and she says to me, "Drink, and I will draw water for your camels also"—she is the woman whom the Lord has prepared for His own servant Isaac; and by this I shall know that You have shown mercy to my master Abraham.'

**24:45** And it came to pass, before I had finished speaking in my mind, that immediately Rebekah came out with her water jar upon her shoulders, and she went down to the spring and drew water. And I said to her, 'Give me a drink.'

**24:46** And she hastened and lowered the water jar from herself onto her arm, and said, 'Drink, and I will water your camels also.' So I drank, and she watered my camels.

**24:47** And I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you? Tell me.' And she said, 'I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.' And I put the earrings on her and the bracelets on her hands;

**24:48** and being well pleased, I worshiped the Lord, and blessed the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who guided me prosperously in the way of truth, to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

**24:49** Now therefore, if you will show mercy and righteousness toward my master, tell me, that I may turn to the right hand or to the left."

**24:50** Then Laban and Bethuel answered and said, "This decree has come forth from the Lord; therefore we cannot speak against you, evil for good.

**24:51** Behold, Rebekah is before you; take her and depart, and let her be a wife to your master's son, even as the Lord has spoken."

**24:52** And it came to pass, when Abraham's servant heard these words, that he worshiped the Lord, bowing to the earth.

**24:53** And the servant brought out articles of silver and gold, and garments, and gave them to Rebekah; and he gave gifts to her brother and to her mother.

**24:54** And they ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and slept. And rising up in the morning, he said, "Send me away, that I may depart to my master."

**24:55** And her brothers and her mother said, "Let the maiden abide with us about ten days, and after that she shall depart."

**24:56** And he said to them, "Do not detain me, since the Lord has prospered my way; send me away, that I may depart to my master."

**24:57** And they said, "Let us call the girl and inquire at her mouth."

**24:58** And they called Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" And she said, "I will go."

**24:59** So they sent away Rebekah their sister and her possessions, and Abraham's servant and those who were with him.

**24:60** And they blessed Rebekah their sister and said to her, "You are our sister; become thousands of ten thousands, and may your seed inherit the cities of the adversaries."

**24:61** Then Rebekah and her maidservants arose and mounted the camels, and went with the man; and the servant took Rebekah and departed.

**24:62** Now Isaac was walking through the wilderness by the Well of Vision; for he dwelt in the land toward the south.

**24:63** And Isaac went out to meditate in the field toward evening; and lifting up his eyes, he saw camels coming.

**24:64** And Rebekah lifted up her eyes and saw Isaac, and she leaped down from the camel.

**24:65** And she said to the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?" And the servant said, "This is my master." Then she took her veil and covered herself.

**24:66** And the servant recounted to Isaac all the things that he had done.

**24:67** And Isaac entered into the house of his mother and took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her; and Isaac was comforted concerning Sarah his mother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The Greek text reads "the God of heaven and the God of the earth" and "To you I will give this land, and to your seed" (adding "and the God of the earth" and "To you" compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and explicitly names Isaac here as in verses 3 and 4.
- **24:22** — The Greek specifies plural "golden earrings, each a drachma in weight" (rather than a single nose ring weighing half a shekel as in the Hebrew text) and gives the weight of the two bracelets as "ten gold pieces."
- **24:26** — The Greek uses the participle eudokesas ("being well pleased"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "bowed his head."
- **24:27** — The Greek renders the divine attribute as dikaiosyne ("righteousness") rather than "mercy" or "lovingkindness," and has singular "brother" rather than plural "brothers."
- **24:31** — The Greek text reads the nominative eulogetos Kyrios ("blessed be the Lord") as an exclamation of praise, rather than the genitive "blessed of the Lord."
- **24:36** — The Greek specifies "one son" and reads the masculine pronoun "after he had grown old" (referring to Abraham), whereas the Hebrew text reads "after she was old."
- **24:50** — The Greek reads the accusative-dative pair kakon kalo ("evil for good") rather than "bad or good," and uses prostagma ("decree") for the matter proceeding from the Lord.
- **24:59** — The Greek reads ta hyparchonta autes ("her possessions"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "her nurse."
- **24:62** — The Greek adds "through the wilderness" and translates the place name Beer Lahai Roi as "the Well of Vision."
