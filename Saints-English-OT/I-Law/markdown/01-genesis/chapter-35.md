## Text

**35:1** Now God said to Jacob, "Arise, go up to the place Bethel, and dwell there; and make an altar there to God, who appeared to you when you fled from the face of Esau your brother."

**35:2** And Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, "Take away the foreign gods from your midst, and purify yourselves, and change your garments;

**35:3** and let us arise and go up to Bethel, and let us make there an altar to God, who heard me in the day of affliction, who was with me and preserved me safely in the way in which I went."

**35:4** So they gave to Jacob the foreign gods which were in their hands, and the earrings which were in their ears; and Jacob hid them beneath the terebinth which is in Shechem, and destroyed them until this day.

**35:5** And Israel departed from Shechem; and the fear of God came upon the cities round about them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Israel.

**35:6** And Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, which is Bethel, he and all the people who were with him.

**35:7** And he built an altar there, and called the name of the place Bethel, for there God appeared to him when he fled from the face of Esau his brother.

**35:8** Now Deborah, the nurse of Rebekah, died below Bethel beneath the oak; and Jacob called its name Oak of Mourning.

**35:9** And God appeared to Jacob again in Luz, when he arrived from Mesopotamia of Syria, and God blessed him.

**35:10** And God said to him, "Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name."

**35:11** And God said to him, "I am your God; increase and multiply, and nations and assemblies of nations shall come from you, and kings shall go forth from your loins.

**35:12** And the land which I have given to Abraham and Isaac, to you I have given it; and to your seed after you I will give this land."

**35:13** Then God went up from him from the place where He had spoken with him.

**35:14** And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where He had spoken with him, a pillar of stone; and he poured out a drink offering upon it, and poured oil upon it.

**35:15** And Jacob called the name of the place in which God had spoken with him there, Bethel.

**35:16** Now Jacob departed from Bethel and pitched his tent beyond the tower of Gader. And it came to pass when he drew near to Chabratha to enter the land of Ephrathah, that Rachel gave birth, and she had hard labor in childbirth.

**35:17** And it came to pass, as she was in hard labor in giving birth, that the midwife said to her, "Take courage, for this one also is a son."

**35:18** And it came to pass, as she was yielding up her soul—for she was dying—that she called his name Son of my sorrow; but his father called him Benjamin.

**35:19** So Rachel died, and was buried on the way to Ephrathah; this is Bethlehem.

**35:20** And Jacob set up a pillar over her tomb; this is the pillar of the tomb of Rachel to this day.

**35:22** And it came to pass when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father's concubine; and Israel heard, and it appeared evil before him. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve:

**35:23** the sons of Leah: the firstborn of Jacob, Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun;

**35:24** and the sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin;

**35:25** and the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's maidservant: Dan and Naphtali;

**35:26** and the sons of Zilpah, Leah's maidservant: Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Mesopotamia of Syria.

**35:27** And Jacob came to Isaac his father to Mamre, to the city of the plain—this is Hebron—in the land of Canaan, where Abraham and Isaac had sojourned.

**35:28** Now the days of Isaac which he lived were one hundred and eighty years.

**35:29** And expiring, he died and was gathered to his kindred, old and full of days; and Esau and Jacob his sons buried him.

## Translator Notes

- **35:4** — The Septuagint includes the final clause "and destroyed them until this day," which is absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **35:8** — The Greek text omits "and she was buried," explicitly names Jacob as the one who named the tree, and translates the Hebrew name Allon-bacuth as "Oak of Mourning."
- **35:11** — The Septuagint reads "I am your God" rather than Hebrew El Shaddai ("God Almighty"), and uses plural "nations and assemblies of nations."
- **35:16** — The Septuagint incorporates the tent pitched beyond the tower of Gader (MT "Eder" in 35:21) here, so verse 35:21 is absent in Greek; "Chabratha" transliterates a Hebrew term for a stretch of distance.
- **35:22** — The Septuagint includes "and it appeared evil before him" after "Israel heard," completing the well-known mid-verse break in the Masoretic Text.
- **35:27** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew place name Kiriath-arba as "the city of the plain" (polin tou pediou).
