## Text

**33:1** Now Jacob lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, Esau his brother was coming, he and four hundred men with him; and Jacob divided the children among Leah, and Rachel, and the two maidservants;

**33:2** and he placed the two maidservants and their sons first, and Leah and her children behind them, and Rachel and Joseph last.

**33:3** But he himself passed on ahead of them, and bowed down to the ground seven times, until he drew near to his brother.

**33:4** And Esau ran to meet him, and embracing him, kissed him, and fell upon his neck; and both of them wept.

**33:5** And looking up, he saw the women and the children, and said, "What are these to you?" And he said, "The children with whom God has shown mercy to your servant."

**33:6** Then the maidservants and their children drew near and bowed down;

**33:7** and Leah and her children drew near and bowed down; and after this Rachel and Joseph drew near and bowed down.

**33:8** And he said, "What are these to you—all these encampments which I have met?" And he said, "That your servant may find favor in your eyes, lord."

**33:9** But Esau said, "I have much, brother; let what is yours remain yours."

**33:10** And Jacob said, "If I have found favor in your sight, receive the gifts from my hands; for on this account I have seen your face as one might see the face of God, and you will be pleased with me.

**33:11** Take my blessings which I have brought to you, because God has shown mercy to me, and I have everything." And he urged him strongly, and he took them.

**33:12** And he said, "Let us set out and go straight ahead."

**33:13** But he said to him, "My lord knows that the children are quite tender, and the sheep and the cows are bearing young in my care; therefore, if I drive them hard for one day, all the livestock will die.

**33:14** Let my lord go on ahead before the servant, and I will gather strength along the way at the leisure of the journey before me and at the pace of the little children, until I come to my lord in Seir."

**33:15** And Esau said, "I will leave with you some of the people who are with me." But he said, "Why this? It is enough that I have found favor in your sight, lord."

**33:16** So Esau returned on that day on his way to Seir.

**33:17** And Jacob set out to Booths; and he made houses for himself there, and for his livestock he made booths; therefore he called the name of that place Booths.

**33:18** And Jacob came to Salem, a city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Mesopotamia of Syria; and he encamped before the city.

**33:19** And he acquired the portion of the field where he had pitched his tent, from Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred lambs;

**33:20** and he erected an altar there and called upon the God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **33:10** — The Greek reads the future indicative ("and you will be pleased with me"), whereas the Hebrew has a past verb ("and you have received me favorably").
- **33:17** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Succoth as Skenai ("Booths" or "Tents"), preserving the wordplay with the booths Jacob made for his livestock.
- **33:18** — The Greek takes Hebrew shalem ("safely") as a place name, "Salem, a city of Shechem," and renders Paddan-aram as "Mesopotamia of Syria."
- **33:19** — The Greek translates the rare Hebrew unit qesitah as amnon ("lambs"), reading "one hundred lambs."
