## Text

**5:1** This is the book of the generations of mankind. In the day that God made Adam, according to the image of God He made him;

**5:2** male and female He made them, and He blessed them; and He called their name Adam, in the day that He made them.

**5:3** Now Adam lived two hundred and thirty years, and fathered one according to his form and according to his image; and he called his name Seth.

**5:4** And the days of Adam after he fathered Seth were seven hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:5** So all the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.

**5:6** Now Seth lived two hundred and five years, and fathered Enosh.

**5:7** And Seth lived after he fathered Enosh seven hundred and seven years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:8** So all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.

**5:9** And Enosh lived one hundred and ninety years, and fathered Cainan.

**5:10** And Enosh lived after he fathered Cainan seven hundred and fifteen years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:11** So all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years, and he died.

**5:12** And Cainan lived one hundred and seventy years, and fathered Mahalalel.

**5:13** And Cainan lived after he fathered Mahalalel seven hundred and forty years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:14** So all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.

**5:15** And Mahalalel lived one hundred and sixty-five years, and fathered Jared.

**5:16** And Mahalalel lived after he fathered Jared seven hundred and thirty years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:17** So all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.

**5:18** And Jared lived one hundred and sixty-two years, and fathered Enoch.

**5:19** And Jared lived after he fathered Enoch eight hundred years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:20** So all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.

**5:21** And Enoch lived one hundred and sixty-five years, and fathered Methuselah.

**5:22** And Enoch was well-pleasing to God after he fathered Methuselah two hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:23** So all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.

**5:24** And Enoch was well-pleasing to God; and he was not found, because God transferred him.

**5:25** And Methuselah lived one hundred and eighty-seven years, and fathered Lamech.

**5:26** And Methuselah lived after he fathered Lamech seven hundred and eighty-two years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:27** So all the days of Methuselah that he lived were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.

**5:28** And Lamech lived one hundred and eighty-eight years, and fathered a son.

**5:29** And he called his name Noah, saying, "This one shall give us rest from our works and from the sorrows of our hands, and from the ground which the Lord God has cursed."

**5:30** And Lamech lived after he fathered Noah five hundred and sixty-five years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:31** So all the days of Lamech were seven hundred and fifty-three years, and he died.

**5:32** And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — Throughout Genesis 5, the Septuagint preserves a longer patriarchal chronology than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, often giving an age one hundred years higher at the birth of the named son (here two hundred and thirty years rather than one hundred and thirty) with a corresponding reduction in the remaining years.
- **5:24** — The Greek reads "was well-pleasing to God" (also in 5:22) and "he was not found, because God transferred him," wording quoted directly in Hebrews 11:5.
- **5:28** — For Lamech, the Septuagint records one hundred and eighty-eight years before Noah's birth, five hundred and sixty-five years afterward (5:30), and seven hundred and fifty-three years in total (5:31), compared with one hundred and eighty-two, five hundred and ninety-five, and seven hundred and seventy-seven in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:29** — The Greek renders Lamech's wordplay on Noah's name with "give us rest" (dianapausei), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "comfort us."
