## Text

**49:1** And Jacob called his sons and said to them, "Gather together, that I may declare to you what shall befall you in the last days.

**49:2** Gather together and hear me, sons of Jacob; hear Israel, hear your father.

**49:3** Reuben, my firstborn, you are my strength and the beginning of my children—hard to bear and harsh, self-willed.

**49:4** You acted insolently like water—do not boil over; for you went up to your father's bed; then you defiled the couch where you went up.

**49:5** Simeon and Levi are brethren; they accomplished injustice by their own choice.

**49:6** Into their counsel may my soul not enter, and in their confederacy may my liver not contend; for in their wrath they slew men, and in their passion they hamstrung a bull.

**49:7** Cursed be their wrath, for it is self-willed, and their fury, for it has been hardened; I will divide them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel.

**49:8** Judah, may your brethren praise you; your hands shall be upon the back of your enemies; your father's sons shall bow down to you.

**49:9** A lion's whelp is Judah; from a shoot, my son, you have gone up. Lying down, you slept as a lion and as a whelp; who shall rouse him?

**49:10** A ruler shall not fail from Judah, nor a leader from his thighs, until the things laid up for him come; and he is the expectation of the nations.

**49:11** Binding his colt to the vine, and the colt of his donkey to the tendril, he shall wash his robe in wine, and his garment in the blood of the grape.

**49:12** His eyes are more gladdening than wine, and his teeth whiter than milk.

**49:13** Zebulun shall dwell by the sea, and he shall be beside a haven of ships, and he shall extend as far as Sidon.

**49:14** Issachar desired that which is good, resting in the midst of the inheritances;

**49:15** and seeing the rest, that it was good, and the land, that it was fertile, he bowed his shoulder to labor and became a tiller of the soil.

**49:16** Dan shall judge his own people, as also one tribe in Israel.

**49:17** And let Dan be a serpent on the road, lying in wait upon the path, biting the heel of the horse, and the horseman shall fall backward,

**49:18** awaiting the salvation of the Lord.

**49:19** Gad—a raiding band shall raid him, yet he shall raid them at their heels.

**49:20** Asher—rich shall be his bread, and he shall yield delicacies to rulers.

**49:21** Naphtali is a spreading tree trunk, bestowing beauty in its produce.

**49:22** Joseph is a son increased, my envied son increased; my youngest son, return to me.

**49:23** Against whom, taking counsel, they railed, and masters of arrows held a grudge against him;

**49:24** and their bows were shattered with might, and the sinews of the arms of their hand were loosed by the hand of the Mighty One of Jacob—from there is He who strengthened Israel—from the God of your father.

**49:25** And my God helped you, and He blessed you with the blessing of heaven from above, and the blessing of the earth possessing all things, on account of the blessing of breasts and womb,

**49:26** the blessing of your father and of your mother; it has prevailed above the blessings of the enduring mountains and above the blessings of the everlasting hills. They shall be upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the brethren whom he led.

**49:27** Benjamin is a ravenous wolf; in the morning he shall still eat, and toward evening he gives food."

**49:28** All these are the twelve sons of Jacob, and these things their father spoke to them; and their father blessed them, each according to his blessing he blessed them.

**49:29** And he said to them, "I am being gathered to my people; bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,

**49:30** in the double cave that is opposite Mamre in the land of Canaan, the cave which Abraham acquired from Ephron the Hittite for the possession of a tomb.

**49:31** There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; there they buried Leah,

**49:32** in the possession of the field and of the cave that is in it, from the sons of Heth."

**49:33** And Jacob finished commanding his sons; and lifting up his feet onto the bed, he breathed his last and was gathered to his people.

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — The Greek reads "they accomplished injustice by their own choice," differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to weapons of violence.
- **49:6** — The Greek reads "my liver" (ta hepata mou, a seat of passion in ancient idiom) and "not contend," reflecting a different reading from the Hebrew Masoretic "my glory" and "not be united."
- **49:10** — The Greek interprets the difficult Hebrew term Shiloh as "the things laid up for him" (ta apokeimena auto) and concludes "he is the expectation of the nations."
- **49:18** — Rather than an abrupt first-person exclamation as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek uses a participle ("awaiting the salvation of the Lord") that directly continues the saying about Dan in verse 17.
- **49:22** — The Greek oracle for Joseph differs substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, describing him as "my envied son increased" and ending with an appeal, "my youngest son, return to me."
