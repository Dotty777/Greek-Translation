## Text

**36:1** Now these are the generations of Esau; he is Edom.

**36:2** And Esau took wives for himself from the daughters of the Canaanites: Adah, the daughter of Elom the Hittite, and Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah the son of Zibeon the Hivite,

**36:3** and Basemath, the daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebaioth.

**36:4** And Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau, and Basemath bore Reuel,

**36:5** and Oholibamah bore Jeush, and Jalam, and Korah. These are the sons of Esau who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

**36:6** And Esau took his wives, his sons, and his daughters, and all the persons of his household, and all his possessions and his livestock, and all that he had acquired and all that he had gained in the land of Canaan; and he departed from the land of Canaan away from the presence of his brother Jacob.

**36:7** For their possessions were too great for them to dwell together, and the land of their sojourning could not sustain them because of the multitude of their possessions.

**36:8** So Esau dwelt in Mount Seir; Esau himself is Edom.

**36:9** Now these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in Mount Seir.

**36:10** And these are the names of the sons of Esau: Eliphaz, the son of Adah the wife of Esau, and Reuel, the son of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:11** And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Oman, Sophar, Gatam, and Kenaz.

**36:12** Now Timna was the concubine of Eliphaz, the son of Esau, and she bore Amalek to Eliphaz. These are the sons of Adah, the wife of Esau.

**36:13** And these are the sons of Reuel: Nahom, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These were the sons of Masemath, the wife of Esau.

**36:14** And these are the sons of Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah the son of Zibeon, the wife of Esau; and she bore to Esau Jeush, and Jalam, and Korah.

**36:15** These are the chiefs of the son of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Sophar, Chief Kenaz,

**36:16** Chief Korah, Chief Gotha, Chief Amalek. These are the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Idumea; these are the sons of Adah.

**36:17** And these are the sons of Reuel, the son of Esau: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Mizzah, Chief Shammah. These are the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Masemath, the wife of Esau.

**36:18** And these are the sons of Oholibamah, the wife of Esau: Chief Jeul, Chief Jalam, Chief Korah. These are the chiefs of Oholibamah.

**36:19** And these are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs; these are their chiefs, the sons of Edom.

**36:20** Now these are the sons of Seir the Horite, who inhabited the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

**36:21** and Dishon, and Saar, and Rison. These are the chiefs of the Horite, the son of Seir, in the land of Edom.

**36:22** And the sons of Lotan were Hori and Heman; and the sister of Lotan was Timna.

**36:23** And these are the sons of Shobal: Golon, and Manahath, and Ebal, Soph, and Oman.

**36:24** And these are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Onan. This is the Onas who found Jamin in the wilderness when he was pasturing the beasts of burden of his father Zibeon.

**36:25** And these are the sons of Anah: Dishon, and Oholibamah the daughter of Anah.

**36:26** And these are the sons of Dishon: Hamada, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

**36:27** And these are the sons of Saar: Bilhan, and Zukam, and Joukam, and Ukan.

**36:28** And these are the sons of Rison: Uz and Aram.

**36:29** These are the chiefs of Hori: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,

**36:30** Chief Dishon, Chief Saar, Chief Rison. These are the chiefs of Hori in their chiefdoms in the land of Edom.

**36:31** Now these are the kings who reigned in Edom before any king reigned in Jerusalem.

**36:32** And Balak the son of Beor reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**36:33** And Balak died, and Jobad the son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.

**36:34** And Jobab died, and Husham, from the land of the Temanites, reigned in his place.

**36:35** And Husham died, and Hadad the son of Barad, who struck down Midian in the plain of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Gethaim.

**36:36** And Hadad died, and Salama, from Masekka, reigned in his place.

**36:37** And Salama died, and Saul, from Rehoboth by the river, reigned in his place.

**36:38** And Saul died, and Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his place.

**36:39** And Baal-hanan the son of Achbor died, and Harath the son of Barath reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Phogor, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matraith the son of Mezoob.

**36:40** These are the names of the chiefs of Esau in their tribes, according to their place, in their lands and in their nations: Chief Timna, Chief Gola, Chief Jeber,

**36:41** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Phines,

**36:42** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mazar,

**36:43** Chief Metodiel, Chief Zaphoei. These are the chiefs of Edom in the built-up settlements in the land of their possession. This is Esau, the father of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — The Greek text reads "Anah the son of Zibeon" (also in 36:14), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "daughter of Zibeon," and it names the Hittite father Elom (Hebrew Elon).
- **36:21** — The Greek names the last two Horite chiefs Saar and Rison (Hebrew Ezer and Dishan), and likewise preserves distinct proper-name forms elsewhere in the chapter, including Sophar (36:11), Nahom and Masemath (36:13), Jeul (36:18), and four sons of Saar (36:27).
- **36:24** — The Greek treats the obscure Hebrew word often translated "hot springs" as a proper name, "Jamin," and names the finder Onan/Onas (Hebrew Anah).
- **36:31** — This Greek text reads "in Jerusalem," where the Masoretic Text reads "over the children of Israel."
- **36:39** — The Greek names the king "Harath the son of Barath" (Hebrew "Hadar," without a father's name), calls his city Phogor (Hebrew Pau), and reads "Matraith the son of Mezoob" (Hebrew "Matred, daughter of Mezahab"); similar Greek-distinct names appear in 36:32–36 (Balak, Jobad, Barad, Gethaim, Salama, Masekka) and 36:40–43 (Gola, Jeber, Phines, Mazar, Metodiel, Zaphoei).
