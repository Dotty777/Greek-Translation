## Text

**41:1** Now it came to pass after two full years that Pharaoh saw a dream: he supposed that he was standing by the river,

**41:2** and behold, as it were out of the river there came up seven cows, fair in appearance and choice in flesh, and they grazed in the reed grass.

**41:3** And seven other cows came up after them out of the river, ugly in appearance and lean in flesh, and the cows fed beside the bank of the river in the reed grass.

**41:4** And the seven cows that were ugly and lean in flesh devoured the seven cows that were fair in appearance and choice. Then Pharaoh awoke.

**41:5** And he dreamed a second time; and behold, seven ears of grain came up on one stalk, choice and good.

**41:6** And seven other ears of grain, thin and blighted by the wind, sprang up after them.

**41:7** And the seven thin and wind-blighted ears of grain swallowed up the seven choice and full ears of grain. Then Pharaoh awoke, and it was a dream.

**41:8** Now it came to pass in the morning that his soul was troubled; and he sent and called all the interpreters of Egypt and all its wise men. And Pharaoh recounted the dream to them, but there was no one who could declare it to Pharaoh.

**41:9** Then the chief cupbearer spoke to Pharaoh, saying, "I call my sin to remembrance this day.

**41:10** Pharaoh was angry with his servants, and put us in custody in the house of the chief keeper of the prison, both me and the chief baker.

**41:11** And we saw a dream in one night, both he and I; each of us saw according to his own dream.

**41:12** Now there was with us there a young man, a Hebrew servant of the chief cook; and we told him, and he interpreted for us.

**41:13** And it came to pass that just as he interpreted for us, so indeed it happened: I was restored to my office, while he was hanged."

**41:14** Then Pharaoh sent and called Joseph, and brought him out of the stronghold; and they shaved him and changed his garment, and he came to Pharaoh.

**41:15** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have seen a dream, and there is no one who can interpret it; yet I have heard it said concerning you that upon hearing dreams you interpret them."

**41:16** And Joseph answered Pharaoh and said, "Apart from God, no answer of welfare shall be given to Pharaoh."

**41:17** Then Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, saying, "In my sleep I supposed that I was standing upon the bank of the river;

**41:18** and as it were out of the river there came up seven cows, fair in appearance and choice in flesh, and they fed in the reed grass.

**41:19** And behold, seven other cows came up behind them out of the river, wretched and ugly in appearance and lean in flesh, and they fed in the reed grass—such as I have never seen uglier in all Egypt.

**41:20** And the seven ugly and lean cows devoured the first seven fair and choice cows,

**41:21** and they went into their bellies; yet it was not apparent that they had gone into their bellies, and their looks were ugly, just as at the beginning. Then I awoke and fell asleep.

**41:22** And I saw again in my sleep, and as it were seven ears of grain came up on one stalk, full and good.

**41:23** And seven other ears of grain, thin and blighted by the wind, sprang up next to them.

**41:24** And the seven thin and wind-blighted ears of grain swallowed up the seven good and full ears of grain. So I told the interpreters, and there was no one who could declare it to me."

**41:25** And Joseph said to Pharaoh, "The dream of Pharaoh is one; all that God is doing, He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:26** The seven fair cows are seven years, and the seven good ears of grain are seven years; the dream of Pharaoh is one.

**41:27** And the seven lean cows that came up behind them are seven years, and the seven thin and wind-blighted ears of grain—they shall be seven years of famine.

**41:28** Now as for the word which I have spoken to Pharaoh: all that God is doing, He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:29** Behold, seven years are coming of great plenty in all the land of Egypt;

**41:30** and seven years of famine shall come after these, and they shall forget the abundance in all the land of Egypt, and the famine shall consume the land.

**41:31** And the plenty shall not be recognized upon the land because of the famine that shall come after these, for it shall be exceedingly severe.

**41:32** And concerning the repeating of the dream to Pharaoh twice, it is because the word from God shall be true, and God will hasten to perform it.

**41:33** Now therefore, look out for a prudent and discerning man, and set him over the land of Egypt.

**41:34** And let Pharaoh act and appoint district governors over the land, and let them collect a fifth part of all the produce of the land of Egypt during the seven years of plenty,

**41:35** and let them gather all the food of these seven good years that are coming; and let the grain be gathered under the hand of Pharaoh, and let food be gathered in the cities.

**41:36** And the food shall be kept in store for the land against the seven years of famine which shall be in the land of Egypt, and the land shall not be destroyed in the famine."

**41:37** Now the words were pleasing in the sight of Pharaoh and in the sight of all his servants.

**41:38** And Pharaoh said to all his servants, "Shall we find such a man as this, who has the Spirit of God in him?"

**41:39** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has shown you all these things, there is no man more prudent and discerning than you.

**41:40** You shall be over my house, and at your word all my people shall obey; only in regard to the throne will I be greater than you."

**41:41** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Behold, I appoint you this day over all the land of Egypt."

**41:42** And taking the ring from his hand, Pharaoh put it upon the hand of Joseph, and clothed him in a robe of fine linen, and placed a golden collar around his neck.

**41:43** And he made him ride upon the second of his chariots, and a herald made proclamation before him; and he set him over all the land of Egypt.

**41:44** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh; without you no one shall lift up his hand in all the land of Egypt."

**41:45** And Pharaoh called Joseph's name Psonthomphanech; and he gave him Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of Heliopolis, to be his wife.

**41:46** Now Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and went throughout all the land of Egypt.

**41:47** And during the seven years of plenty the land brought forth sheaves.

**41:48** And he gathered all the food of the seven years in which there was plenty in the land of Egypt, and he laid up the food in the cities; the food of the fields of the city that were around it—On—he laid up in it.

**41:49** And Joseph gathered grain like the sand of the sea, exceedingly much, until he could not count it, for it was beyond number.

**41:50** Now to Joseph were born two sons before the seven years of famine came, whom Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of Heliopolis, bore to him.

**41:51** And Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh, saying, "For God has made me forget all my toils and all that belongs to my father."

**41:52** And the name of the second he called Ephraim, "For God has exalted me in the land of my humiliation."

**41:53** Then the seven years of plenty which were in the land of Egypt passed away,

**41:54** and the seven years of famine began to come, just as Joseph had said. And there was famine in all the earth, and in all the land of Egypt there was no bread.

**41:55** And all the land of Egypt hungered, and all the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread. Then Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, "Go to Joseph, and whatever he says to you, do."

**41:56** And the famine was over the face of all the earth; and Joseph opened all the granaries and sold to all the Egyptians.

**41:57** And all the countries came to Egypt to buy from Joseph, for the famine prevailed in all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **41:16** — The Greek literally reads 'Apart from God, no answer of welfare (or deliverance) shall be given to Pharaoh,' expressing Joseph's dependence on God in a different construction from the Hebrew ('It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh an answer of peace').
- **41:45** — The Greek preserves the Septuagint form Psonthomphanech for Joseph's Egyptian name (Hebrew 'Zaphnath-paaneah'), spells Potiphera as Petrephes (Petrephe), and renders the Egyptian city of On by its Greek name Heliopolis ('City of the Sun').
- **41:48** — This Greek text uniquely includes the proper name 'On' (the Egyptian name for Heliopolis) in describing the grain stored from the surrounding fields.
- **41:52** — This Greek reading has 'exalted me' (hypsosen) rather than 'increased me' (standard Septuagint) or 'made me fruitful' (Hebrew), sharpening the contrast with 'the land of my humiliation.'
- **41:54** — This Greek text reads 'there was no bread' in Egypt (in contrast to the Hebrew Masoretic Text, 'there was bread'), directly anticipating the people's cry for bread in verse 55.
