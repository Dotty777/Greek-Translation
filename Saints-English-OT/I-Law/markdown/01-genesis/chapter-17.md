## Text

**17:1** Now Abram was ninety-nine years old, and the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "I am your God; be well-pleasing before Me, and become blameless.

**17:2** And I will establish My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you exceedingly."

**17:3** And Abram fell upon his face,

**17:4** and God spoke to him, saying, "And as for Me, behold, My covenant is with you, and you shall be a father of a multitude of nations.

**17:5** And no longer shall your name be called Abram, but your name shall be Abraham, for I have appointed you a father of many nations.

**17:6** And I will cause you to increase exceedingly, and I will make you into nations, and kings shall come forth from you.

**17:7** And I will establish My covenant between Me and you and your seed after you throughout their generations, for an everlasting covenant, to be God to you and to your seed after you.

**17:8** And I will give to you and to your seed after you the land in which you sojourn, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession; and I will be God to them."

**17:9** And God said to Abraham, "Now as for you, you shall diligently keep My covenant, you and your seed after you throughout their generations.

**17:10** And this is the covenant which you shall keep, between Me and you and your seed after you throughout their generations: every male among you shall be circumcised.

**17:11** And you shall be circumcised in the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall be for a sign of a covenant between Me and you.

**17:12** And a child eight days old—every male among you shall be circumcised throughout your generations: the one born in your house and the one bought with silver from any son of a foreigner, who is not of your seed,

**17:13** shall surely be circumcised—both the one born in your house and the one bought with silver. And My covenant shall be upon your flesh for an everlasting covenant.

**17:14** And an uncircumcised male who is not circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin on the eighth day—that soul shall be utterly destroyed from his people, because he has broken My covenant."

**17:15** Then God said to Abraham, "As for Sarai your wife, her name shall not be called Sarai, but Sarah shall be her name.

**17:16** And I will bless her, and I will give you a child from her; and I will bless him, and he shall become nations, and kings of nations shall come from him."

**17:17** Then Abraham fell upon his face and laughed, and spoke in his mind, saying, "Shall a son be born to a man one hundred years old, and shall one be born to Sarah, who is ninety years old?"

**17:18** And Abraham said to God, "Let this Ishmael live before You."

**17:19** And God said to Abraham, "Yes; behold, Sarah your wife shall bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac. And I will establish My covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, and for his seed after him.

**17:20** And concerning Ishmael, behold, I have heard you, and I have blessed him, and I will cause him to increase and will multiply him exceedingly. He shall father twelve nations, and I will make him into a great nation.

**17:21** But My covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear to you at this time in the next year."

**17:22** Then He finished speaking to him, and God went up from Abraham.

**17:23** And Abraham took Ishmael his son, and all those born in his house, and all those bought with silver, and every male among the men who were in the house of Abraham, and circumcised their foreskins at the time of that day, just as God had spoken to him.

**17:24** Now Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin;

**17:25** and Ishmael his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

**17:26** At the time of that day Abraham was circumcised, and Ishmael his son;

**17:27** and all the men of his house, both those born in the house and those bought with silver from foreign nations—he circumcised them.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Greek text reads "I am your God" rather than the Hebrew "God Almighty" (El Shaddai), and renders "walk before Me" as "be well-pleasing before Me."
- **17:14** — The Greek text includes the phrase "on the eighth day," which is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **17:15** — The Greek text distinguishes the old and new names as Sara and Sarra, doubling the r rather than changing from Hebrew Sarai to Sarah.
- **17:16** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which uses feminine pronouns referring to Sarah, the Greek uses masculine pronouns ("bless him ... he shall become nations ... from him"), directing the second blessing to the promised child.
- **17:20** — The Greek reads "twelve nations" rather than the Hebrew "twelve princes."
