## Text

**32:1** Now Laban arose in the morning, kissed his sons and his daughters, and blessed them; and Laban turned back and departed to his own place.

**32:2** And Jacob departed on his own way; and lifting up his eyes, he saw an encampment of God encamped, and the angels of God met him.

**32:3** And Jacob said when he saw them, "This is an encampment of God." And he called the name of that place Encampments.

**32:4** Now Jacob sent messengers to Esau his brother, to the land of Seir, to the country of Edom,

**32:5** and he commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall speak to my lord Esau: 'Thus says your servant Jacob: I have sojourned with Laban and tarried until now;

**32:6** and I have acquired oxen, donkeys, sheep, male servants, and female servants; and I have sent to bring word to my lord Esau, that your servant may find favor in your sight.'"

**32:7** And the messengers returned to Jacob, saying, "We came to your brother Esau, and he is coming to meet you, and four hundred men are with him."

**32:8** Then Jacob was exceedingly afraid and was distressed; and he divided the people who were with him, and the oxen and the sheep, into two encampments.

**32:9** And Jacob said, "If Esau comes to the one encampment and cuts it down, then the second encampment will remain to be saved."

**32:10** Then Jacob said, "O God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, O Lord, who said to me, 'Hasten back to the land of your birth, and I will deal well with you,'

**32:11** You are sufficient for me by all the righteousness and by all the truth which You have performed for Your servant; for with my staff I crossed over this Jordan, and now I have become two encampments.

**32:12** Deliver me from the hand of my brother Esau, for I fear him, lest he come and strike me, and the mother with the children.

**32:13** Yet You said, 'I will surely deal well with you, and I will make your seed like the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered for multitude.'"

**32:14** And he slept there that night. And he took gifts from what he had brought, and sent them out to Esau his brother:

**32:15** two hundred female goats, twenty male goats, two hundred ewes, twenty rams,

**32:16** thirty nursing camels and their young, forty cows, ten bulls, twenty donkeys, and ten colts.

**32:17** And he delivered them by the hand of his servants, each flock by itself. And he said to his servants, "Go forward ahead of me, and put a space between flock and flock."

**32:18** And he commanded the first, saying, "When Esau my brother meets you and asks you, saying, 'To whom do you belong, and where are you going? And whose are these going ahead of you?'

**32:19** then you shall say, 'They belong to your servant Jacob; he has sent gifts to my lord Esau, and behold, he himself is behind us.'"

**32:20** And he commanded the first, and the second, and the third, and all who went forward behind these flocks, saying, "According to this word you shall speak to Esau when you find him,

**32:21** and you shall say, 'Behold, your servant Jacob is coming behind us.'" For he said, "I will appease his face with the gifts that go ahead of him, and after this I shall see his face; for perhaps he will accept my face."

**32:22** So the gifts went forward before him, but he himself slept that night in the encampment.

**32:23** Now rising up that night, he took the two wives, and the two maidservants, and his eleven children, and crossed over the ford of the Jabbok.

**32:24** And he took them and crossed over the brook, and he sent across all that belonged to him.

**32:25** Then Jacob was left alone, and a Man wrestled with him until morning.

**32:26** Now He saw that He could not prevail against him, and He touched the broad part of his thigh; and the broad part of Jacob's thigh grew numb as he wrestled with Him.

**32:27** And He said to him, "Let Me go, for the dawn has risen." But he said, "I will by no means let You go unless You bless me."

**32:28** So He said to him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Jacob."

**32:29** And He said to him, "Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name; for you have prevailed with God, and with men you shall be mighty."

**32:30** Then Jacob asked and said, "Declare to me Your name." And He said, "Why is it that you ask My name?" And He blessed him there.

**32:31** And Jacob called the name of that place Form of God, "For I have seen God face to face, and my soul has been saved."

**32:32** Now the sun rose upon him as he passed by the Form of God, and he himself limped upon his thigh.

**32:33** For on account of this the sons of Israel do not eat the sinew that grew numb, which is upon the broad part of Jacob's thigh, until this day, because He touched the broad part of Jacob's thigh, the sinew, and it grew numb.

## Translator Notes

- **32:2** — The Septuagint numbering of chapter 32 begins one verse earlier than the Hebrew Masoretic Text (32:1 corresponds to 31:55), and 32:2 uniquely adds that Jacob lifted up his eyes and saw "an encampment of God encamped," leading into the Greek translation of Mahanaim as Parembolai ("Encampments") in 32:3.
- **32:11** — The Greek source reads hikanousai moi ("You are sufficient for me"; other Greek witnesses read hikanoutai moi, "it is sufficient for me"), differing from the Hebrew "I am not worthy of all the mercies and all the truth."
- **32:29** — The final Greek clause omits the verb (literally "and with men mighty"), here rendered "and with men you shall be mighty."
- **32:31** — The Greek renders the Hebrew place name Peniel as Eidos theou ("Form of God," and "the Form of God" in 32:32), preserving a Greek wordplay with eidon ("I have seen") in the clause that follows.
