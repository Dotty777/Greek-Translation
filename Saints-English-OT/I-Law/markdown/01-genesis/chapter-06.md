## Text

**6:1** And it came to pass, when men began to grow numerous upon the earth and daughters were born to them,

**6:2** that the angels of God, seeing the daughters of men that they were fair, took for themselves wives from all whom they chose.

**6:3** And the Lord God said, "My Spirit shall by no means abide in these men forever, because they are flesh; yet their days shall be one hundred and twenty years."

**6:4** Now the giants were upon the earth in those days, and after that, whenever the sons of God went in to the daughters of men and they bore children to them. Those were the giants who were from of old, the men of renown.

**6:5** Then the Lord God saw that the evils of men were multiplied upon the earth, and that everyone meditates in his heart intently upon wicked things all the days.

**6:6** And God laid it to heart that He had made man upon the earth, and He pondered it.

**6:7** And God said, "I will blot out man whom I have made from the face of the earth, from man to livestock, and from creeping things to the birds of heaven; for I am angered that I made them."

**6:8** But Noah found grace before the Lord God.

**6:9** Now these are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, being perfect in his generation; Noah was well-pleasing to God.

**6:10** And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**6:11** Now the earth was corrupted before God, and the earth was filled with unrighteousness.

**6:12** And the Lord God saw the earth, and it was corrupted, for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth.

**6:13** And God said to Noah, "The time of every man has come before Me, for the earth has been filled with unrighteousness because of them; and behold, I am destroying them and the earth.

**6:14** Make for yourself, therefore, an ark of squared timbers; you shall make compartments in the ark, and you shall coat it inside and outside with bitumen.

**6:15** And thus you shall make the ark: three hundred cubits shall be the length of the ark, and fifty cubits the breadth, and thirty cubits its height.

**6:16** Tapering it together you shall make the ark, and to a cubit you shall finish it at the top; and the door of the ark you shall make on the side; with lower, second, and third stories you shall make it.

**6:17** And behold, I Myself am bringing the flood of water upon the earth, to destroy all flesh in which is the spirit of life under heaven; and all things that are upon the earth shall perish.

**6:18** And I will establish My covenant with you; and you shall enter into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and the wives of your sons with you.

**6:19** And of all livestock, and of all creeping things, and of all wild beasts, and of all flesh, two by two of all you shall bring into the ark, that you may sustain them with yourself; male and female they shall be.

**6:20** Of all winged birds according to kind, and of all livestock according to kind, and of all creeping things that creep upon the earth according to their kind, two by two of all shall come in to you to be sustained with you, male and female.

**6:21** And you shall take for yourself of all foods that you shall eat, and gather them to yourself; and it shall be for you and for them to eat."

**6:22** And Noah did all things that the Lord God commanded him; so he did.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Greek reads "the angels of God" (hoi angeloi tou theou) here, whereas verse 4 reads "the sons of God" (hoi huioi tou theou), reflecting an ancient Septuagint interpretive tradition identifying these beings as angelic.
- **6:6** — Rather than describing God as repenting or grieving, the Greek uses two verbs of reflection—enethymēthē ("laid it to heart") and dienoēthē ("pondered it")—and in verse 7 uses ethymōthēn ("I am angered").
- **6:14** — The Greek specifies "squared timbers" (xylōn tetragōnōn) rather than Hebrew "gopher wood," and renders "compartments" literally as "nests" (nossias).
- **6:16** — The Greek participle episynagōn ("tapering it together") describes the ark as narrowing upward to a one-cubit crown rather than specifying a window or roof opening.
