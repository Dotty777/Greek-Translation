## Text

**26:1** Now there came a famine upon the land, besides the former famine that happened in the time of Abraham; and Isaac went to Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar.

**26:2** And the Lord appeared to him and said, "Do not go down to Egypt, but dwell in the land of which I shall tell you.

**26:3** And sojourn in this land, and I will be with you and bless you; for to you and to your seed I will give all this land, and I will establish My oath which I swore to Abraham your father.

**26:4** And I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and I will give to your seed all this land, and in your seed all the nations of the earth shall be blessed,

**26:5** because Abraham your father obeyed My voice, and kept My ordinances, My commandments, My statutes, and My laws."

**26:6** So Isaac dwelt in Gerar.

**26:7** And the men of the place asked about Rebekah his wife, and he said, "She is my sister"; for he was afraid to say, "She is my wife," lest the men of the place should slay him on account of Rebekah, because she was fair in appearance.

**26:8** Now he remained there a long time; and Abimelech the king of Gerar looked through the window and saw Isaac playing with Rebekah his wife.

**26:9** Then Abimelech called Isaac and said to him, "Surely she is your wife! Why then did you say, 'She is my sister'?" And Isaac said to him, "Because I said, 'Lest I die on account of her.'"

**26:10** And Abimelech said to him, "What is this that you have done to us? One of my kindred almost lay with your wife, and you brought a sin of ignorance upon us."

**26:11** So Abimelech charged all his people, saying, "Everyone who touches this man or his wife shall be liable to death."

**26:12** And Isaac sowed in that land, and in that year he found a hundredfold harvest of barley; and the Lord blessed him.

**26:13** And the man was exalted, and advancing he became greater until he became exceedingly great;

**26:14** and he came to have flocks of sheep and herds of cattle and many tilled lands. And the Philistines envied him;

**26:15** and all the wells which the servants of his father had dug in the time of his father, the Philistines stopped them up and filled them with earth.

**26:16** And Abimelech said to Isaac, "Depart from us, for you have become far mightier than we."

**26:17** So Isaac departed from there and encamped in the valley of Gerar, and dwelt there.

**26:18** And Isaac dug again the wells of water which the servants of Abraham his father had dug and which the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham his father died; and he named them with names according to the names which Abraham his father had named them.

**26:19** And the servants of Isaac dug in the valley of Gerar, and found there a well of living water.

**26:20** And the shepherds of Gerar quarreled with the shepherds of Isaac, claiming that the water was theirs; and he called the name of that well Injustice, because they had wronged him.

**26:21** Then Isaac moved on from there and dug another well; and they contended over that one also, and he named it Enmity.

**26:22** And moving on from there he dug another well, and they did not quarrel over it; and he named it Broad Space, saying, "Because now the Lord has made room for us and has increased us upon the earth."

**26:23** And he went up from there to the Well of the Oath.

**26:24** And the Lord appeared to him in that night and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father; do not fear, for I am with you, and I will bless you and multiply your seed for the sake of Abraham your father."

**26:25** And he built an altar there and called upon the name of the Lord, and pitched his tent there; and there the servants of Isaac dug a well.

**26:26** Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar, along with Ahuzzath his bridal escort and Phicol the commander-in-chief of his army.

**26:27** And Isaac said to them, "Why have you come to me, seeing that you hated me and sent me away from you?"

**26:28** And they said, "We have surely seen that the Lord was with you, and we said, 'Let there be a sworn oath between us and you, and we will make a covenant with you

**26:29** that you will do us no harm, just as we did not abhor you, and as we treated you well and sent you away in peace.' And now you are blessed by the Lord."

**26:30** So he made a feast for them, and they ate and drank.

**26:31** And rising early in the morning, they swore an oath each man to his neighbor; and Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in safety.

**26:32** Now it came to pass on that day that the servants of Isaac arrived and reported to him concerning the well which they had dug, and they said, "We have not found water."

**26:33** And he called its name Oath; therefore he called the name of the city Well of Oath, to this very day.

**26:34** Now Esau was forty years old, and he took as wife Judith, the daughter of Beer the Hittite, and Masemmath, the daughter of Elon the Hivite;

**26:35** and they were contentious with Isaac and Rebekah.

## Translator Notes

- **26:10** — Greek agnoia ("ignorance") is used in the Septuagint to denote an unwitting offense or sin of ignorance, rendering the Hebrew term for guilt.
- **26:12** — The Septuagint reads "barley" (krithen), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants rendered as "measures" in the Masoretic Text.
- **26:20** — Here and in vv. 21-23 and 33, the Septuagint translates the well names into Greek (Injustice, Enmity, Broad Space, and Well of the Oath) rather than transliterating the Hebrew names Esek, Sitnah, Rehoboth, and Beersheba.
- **26:32** — The Septuagint reads "We have not found water," taking the Hebrew consonants read as "to him" in the Masoretic Text ("We have found water") as the negative particle "not."
- **26:34** — The Septuagint gives the names Beer and Masemmath (for Hebrew Beeri and Basemath) and identifies Elon as "the Hivite" rather than "the Hittite."
