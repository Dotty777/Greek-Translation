## Text

**47:1** Then Joseph came and reported to Pharaoh, saying, "My father and my brethren, and their livestock and their cattle, and all that belongs to them, have come out of the land of Canaan; and behold, they are in the land of Goshen."

**47:2** And from among his brethren he took five men and set them before Pharaoh.

**47:3** And Pharaoh said to the brethren of Joseph, "What is your occupation?" And they said to Pharaoh, "Your servants are shepherds of sheep, both we and our fathers."

**47:5** And they said to Pharaoh, "We have come to sojourn in the land; for there is no pasture for the livestock of your servants, because the famine has grown severe in the land of Canaan. Now therefore, we shall dwell in the land of Goshen." And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Let them dwell in the land of Goshen; and if you know that there are capable men among them, appoint them rulers over my livestock." Now Jacob and his sons came into Egypt to Joseph; and Pharaoh, king of Egypt, heard of it. And Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, saying, "Your father and your brethren have come to you;

**47:6** behold, the land of Egypt is before you; settle your father and your brethren in the best of the land."

**47:7** Then Joseph brought in Jacob his father and set him before Pharaoh; and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

**47:8** And Pharaoh said to Jacob, "How many are the years of the days of your life?"

**47:9** And Jacob said to Pharaoh, "The days of the years of my life during which I sojourn are one hundred and thirty years; short and evil have been the days of the years of my life; they have not attained to the days of the years of the life of my fathers, in the days that they sojourned."

**47:10** And having blessed Pharaoh, Jacob went out from him.

**47:11** And Joseph settled his father and his brethren, and gave them a possession in the land of Egypt, in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, just as Pharaoh had commanded.

**47:12** And Joseph measured out to his father, and to his brethren, and to all the house of his father, grain according to each person.

**47:13** Now there was no grain in all the land, for the famine grew exceedingly severe; and the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan fainted because of the famine.

**47:14** And Joseph gathered up all the silver that was found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan, for the grain which they were buying, and he measured out grain to them; and Joseph brought all the silver into the house of Pharaoh.

**47:15** And all the silver failed from the land of Egypt and from the land of Canaan; and all the Egyptians came to Joseph, saying, "Give us bread! And why should we die before you? For our silver has failed."

**47:16** Then Joseph said to them, "Bring your livestock, and I will give you bread in exchange for your livestock, if your silver has failed."

**47:17** So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for the horses, and for the sheep, and for the cattle, and for the donkeys; and he nourished them with bread in exchange for all their livestock in that year.

**47:18** Now that year passed away, and they came to him in the second year and said to him, "Let us not be wiped out from before our lord! For if our silver has failed, and our goods and our livestock have passed to you, our lord, then nothing is left to us before our lord except our own body and our land.

**47:19** Lest we die before you and the land become desolate, buy us and our land in exchange for bread, and we and our land will be servants of Pharaoh; give seed that we may sow and not die, and the land will become desolate."

**47:20** So Joseph bought all the land of the Egyptians for Pharaoh; for the Egyptians sold their land to Pharaoh, because the famine prevailed over them; and the land became Pharaoh's,

**47:21** and he enslaved the people to him as servants, from one end of the borders of Egypt to the other,

**47:22** except the land of the priests alone; Joseph did not buy this, for by a grant Pharaoh gave a gift to the priests, and they ate the portion which Pharaoh gave them; therefore they did not sell their land.

**47:23** Then Joseph said to all the Egyptians, "Behold, I have bought you and your land today for Pharaoh; take seed for yourselves and sow the land.

**47:24** And it shall be that of its produce you shall give the fifth part to Pharaoh; and the four parts shall be for yourselves, for seed for the land, and for food for you and for all your households."

**47:25** And they said, "You have saved us; we have found favor before our lord, and we will be servants of Pharaoh."

**47:26** And Joseph established it for them as an ordinance until this day over the land of Egypt, to pay a fifth to Pharaoh, except for the land of the priests alone; it did not belong to Pharaoh.

**47:27** So Israel dwelt in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen; and they received an inheritance in it, and multiplied exceedingly.

**47:28** And Jacob lived on in the land of Egypt seventeen years; and the days of Jacob, of the years of his life, were one hundred and forty-seven years.

**47:29** Now the days of Israel drew near for him to die; and he called his son Joseph and said to him, "If I have found favor before you, place your hand under my thigh, and you shall show mercy and truth to me, so as not to bury me in Egypt;

**47:30** but I shall sleep with my fathers, and you shall carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their tomb." And he said, "I will do according to your word."

**47:31** And he said, "Swear to me." And he swore to him; and Israel worshiped upon the top of his staff.

## Translator Notes

- **47:5** — This Septuagint versification combines the material of vv. 4–5 (with no separate v. 4) and places Pharaoh's grant of Goshen ahead of the notice that Jacob and his sons came to Egypt.
- **47:19** — The Greek text here omits a negative before "will become desolate" (kai he ge eremothesetai), whereas the Masoretic Text and some Greek witnesses read "and the land will not become desolate."
- **47:21** — The Septuagint reads "enslaved the people to him as servants" (agreeing with the Samaritan Pentateuch), whereas the Masoretic Text reads that Joseph moved the people into cities.
- **47:31** — The Septuagint reads "upon the top of his staff" (quoted in Hebrews 11:21), whereas the Masoretic Text vocalizes the Hebrew word to mean "the head of the bed."
