## Text

**42:1** Now when Jacob saw that there was selling in Egypt, he said to his sons, "Why are you idle?

**42:2** Behold, I have heard that there is grain in Egypt; go down there and buy a little food for us, that we may live and not die."

**42:3** So the ten brothers of Joseph went down to buy grain from Egypt.

**42:4** But Benjamin, Joseph's brother, he did not send with his brothers; for he said, "Lest sickness befall him."

**42:5** And the sons of Israel came to buy with those who were coming, for the famine was in the land of Canaan.

**42:6** Now Joseph was ruler of the land; it was he who sold to all the people of the land. And Joseph's brothers came and bowed down before him with their faces to the ground.

**42:7** When Joseph saw his brothers, he recognized them, yet he acted as a stranger toward them and spoke harshly to them. And he said to them, "Where have you come from?" And they said, "From the land of Canaan to buy food."

**42:8** Now Joseph recognized his brothers, but they did not recognize him.

**42:9** And Joseph remembered the dreams which he himself had seen, and he said to them, "You are spies! You have come to observe the tracks of the country."

**42:10** And they said, "No, my lord; your servants have come to buy food.

**42:11** We are all sons of one man; we are men of peace; your servants are not spies."

**42:12** But he said to them, "No, rather you have come to see the tracks of the land."

**42:13** And they said, "Your servants are twelve brothers in the land of Canaan; and behold, the youngest is with our father today, and the other is no more."

**42:14** Then Joseph said to them, "This is what I spoke to you, saying that you are spies.

**42:15** By this you shall be made manifest: by the health of Pharaoh, you shall by no means depart from here unless your youngest brother comes here.

**42:16** Send one from among you, and fetch your brother; and as for you, be led away until your words are made manifest, whether you speak the truth or not; otherwise, by the health of Pharaoh, surely you are spies."

**42:17** And he put them in prison for three days.

**42:18** Then he said to them on the third day, "Do this and you shall live, for I fear God:

**42:19** if you are men of peace, let one of your brothers be detained in the prison; but as for you, go and carry away the purchase of your grain supply,

**42:20** and bring your youngest brother down to me, and your words shall be believed; otherwise, you shall die." And they did so.

**42:21** Then each said to his brother, "Indeed, we are in sin concerning our brother, because we disregarded the distress of his soul when he pleaded with us, and we did not listen to him; for this reason this distress has come upon us."

**42:22** And Reuben answered and said to them, "Did I not speak to you, saying, 'Do not wrong the lad'? Yet you did not listen to me; and behold, his blood is required."

**42:23** Now they did not know that Joseph understood, for the interpreter was between them.

**42:24** And Joseph turned away from them and wept. Then he came to them again and spoke to them; and he took Simeon from them and bound him in their sight.

**42:25** Then Joseph commanded to fill their vessels with grain, and to restore each man's silver into his sack, and to give them provisions for the journey. And thus it was done for them.

**42:26** So they loaded the grain upon their donkeys and departed from there.

**42:27** But as one of them untied his bag to give fodder to his donkeys where they lodged, he saw the bundle of his silver, and it was at the top of the mouth of the bag.

**42:28** And he said to his brothers, "My silver has been restored to me, and behold, here it is in my bag!" And their hearts failed them, and they were troubled among themselves, saying, "What is this that God has done to us?"

**42:29** Then they came to Jacob their father in the land of Canaan, and reported to him all the things that had befallen them, saying,

**42:30** "The man, the lord of the land, spoke harshly to us, and put us in prison as those spying out the land.

**42:31** But we said to him, 'We are men of peace; we are not spies.

**42:32** We are twelve brothers, sons of our father; the one is no more, and the youngest is with our father today in the land of Canaan.'

**42:33** Then the man, the lord of the land, said to us, 'By this we shall know that you are men of peace: leave one brother here with me, and take the purchase of your grain supply and depart,

**42:34** and bring your youngest brother to me; then I shall know that you are not spies, but that you are men of peace. And I will restore your brother to you, and you may trade in the land.'"

**42:35** Now it came to pass, as they emptied their sacks, that each man's bundle of silver was in his sack; and when they and their father saw the bundles of their silver, they were afraid.

**42:36** And Jacob their father said to them, "You have made me childless! Joseph is no more, Simeon is no more, and you will take Benjamin! All these things have come upon me."

**42:37** Then Reuben spoke to his father, saying, "Slay my two sons if I do not bring him to you; give him into my hand, and I will bring him back to you."

**42:38** But he said, "My son shall not go down with you; for his brother is dead, and he alone is left. And it will come to pass that he will fall sick along the way in which you go, and you will bring down my old age with sorrow to Hades."

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — The Septuagint reads "selling" (prasis) rather than "grain," and "Why are you idle?" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Why do you look at one another?"
- **42:4** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew word for "harm" as "sickness" (malakia), corresponding to "fall sick" (malakisthenai) in 42:38.
- **42:9** — "The tracks of the country" (ta ichne, also "tracks of the land" in 42:12) is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew idiom "the nakedness of the land," referring to its vulnerable approaches.
- **42:20** — The Septuagint concludes Joseph's condition with a warning ("otherwise, you shall die"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and you shall not die."
- **42:38** — "Hades" translates the Greek Hades, the standard Septuagint rendering of Hebrew Sheol, the realm of the dead.
