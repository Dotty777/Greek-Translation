## Text

**27:1** Now it came to pass, after Isaac had grown old and his eyes had become dim so that he could not see, that he called Esau his elder son and said to him, "My son." And he said to him, "Behold, here I am."

**27:2** And he said, "Behold, I have grown old, and I do not know the day of my death.

**27:3** Now therefore, take your equipment, both the quiver and the bow, and go out into the field and hunt game for me;

**27:4** and make savory dishes for me such as I love, and bring them to me that I may eat, so that my soul may bless you before I die."

**27:5** Now Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau his son. And Esau went into the field to hunt game for his father.

**27:6** Then Rebekah said to Jacob her younger son, "Behold, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother, saying,

**27:7** 'Bring me game and make savory dishes for me, that I may eat and bless you before the Lord before I die.'

**27:8** Now therefore, son, listen to me just as I command you:

**27:9** Go to the flock and take for me from there two tender and good young goats, and I will prepare them as savory dishes for your father, such as he loves;

**27:10** and you shall bring them in to your father, and he shall eat, so that your father may bless you before he dies."

**27:11** And Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Esau my brother is a hairy man, whereas I am a smooth man.

**27:12** Perhaps my father will feel me, and I shall be before him as one showing contempt, and I shall bring upon myself a curse and not a blessing."

**27:13** And his mother said to him, "Upon me be your curse, child; only obey my voice, and go and bring them to me."

**27:14** So he went and took them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made savory dishes just as his father loved.

**27:15** Then Rebekah took the fine robe of Esau her elder son, which was with her in the house, and clothed Jacob her younger son with it;

**27:16** and she put the skins of the young goats upon his arms and upon the bare parts of his neck;

**27:17** and she gave the savory dishes and the loaves of bread which she had made into the hands of Jacob her son.

**27:18** And he brought them in to his father, and he said, "My father." And he said, "Behold, here I am. Who are you, child?"

**27:19** And Jacob his son said to his father, "I am Esau your firstborn; I have done just as you spoke to me. Arise, sit and eat of my game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:20** And Isaac said to his son, "What is this that you have found so quickly, O child?" And he said, "That which the Lord God delivered before me."

**27:21** Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Draw near to me, and I will feel you, child, whether you are my son Esau or not."

**27:22** So Jacob drew near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is the voice of Jacob, yet the hands are the hands of Esau."

**27:23** And he did not recognize him, for his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau his brother; and he blessed him.

**27:24** And he said, "Are you my son Esau?" And he said, "I am."

**27:25** And he said, "Bring it near to me, and I will eat of your game, child, so that my soul may bless you." So he brought it to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.

**27:26** Then Isaac his father said to him, "Draw near to me and kiss me, child."

**27:27** And he drew near and kissed him; and he smelled the fragrance of his garments, and blessed him and said, "Behold, the fragrance of my son is like the fragrance of a full field which the Lord has blessed.

**27:28** And may God give you of the dew of heaven from above, and of the fatness of the earth, and an abundance of grain and wine.

**27:29** And let nations serve you, and rulers shall bow down to you; and be lord of your brother, and the sons of your father shall bow down to you. He who curses you is cursed, and he who blesses you is blessed."

**27:30** Now it came to pass, after Isaac had finished blessing Jacob his son, and it came to pass as Jacob went out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother came from his hunting.

**27:31** And he also made savory dishes and brought them to his father; and he said to his father, "Let my father arise and eat of his son's game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:32** And Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" And he said, "I am your firstborn son, Esau."

**27:33** Then Isaac was struck with an exceedingly great dismay and said, "Who then is he who hunted game for me and brought it in to me, and I ate of all of it before you came in, and I blessed him? And let him be blessed."

**27:34** Now it came to pass, when Esau heard the words of Isaac his father, that Esau cried out with an exceedingly loud and bitter voice, and said, "Bless me, even me also, father!"

**27:35** And he said to him, "Your brother came with deceit and has taken your blessing."

**27:36** And he said, "Rightly was his name called Jacob, for he has supplanted me now this second time: he has taken my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing." And Esau said to his father, "Have you not reserved a blessing for me, father?"

**27:37** And Isaac answered and said to Esau, "If I have made him lord over you, and all his brethren I have made his household servants, and with grain and wine I have sustained him, what then shall I do for you, child?"

**27:38** And Esau said to Isaac his father, "Do you have only one blessing, father? Bless me, even me also, father!"

**27:39** Then Isaac his father answered and said to him, "Behold, of the fatness of the earth shall your dwelling be, and of the dew of heaven from above;

**27:40** and by your sword you shall live, and you shall serve your brother. Yet it shall come to pass that when you cast it down, you shall loosen his yoke from your neck."

**27:41** And Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him; and Esau said in his mind, "Let the days of mourning for my father draw near, so that I may slay Jacob my brother."

**27:42** And the words of Esau her elder son were reported to Rebekah. So she sent and called Jacob her younger son and said to him, "Behold, Esau your brother threatens you, to slay you.

**27:43** Now therefore, child, listen to my voice: arise and flee into Mesopotamia to Laban my brother, to Haran,

**27:44** and dwell with him a few days, until the fury

**27:45** and the wrath of your brother turn away from you, and he forgets what you have done to him; then I will send and summon you from there, lest I be bereaved of both of you in one day."

**27:46** Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am weary of my life because of the daughters of the sons of Heth. If Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of this land, of what use is life to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **27:12** — Greek kataphronon ("showing contempt" or "despising") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "a mocker" or "deceiver."
- **27:29** — The Greek reads "rulers" (archontes) and "the sons of your father," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "peoples" and "the sons of your mother."
- **27:36** — Greek epterniken (literally "tripped by the heel," here rendered "supplanted") preserves the wordplay on the name Jacob.
- **27:40** — Greek henika ean katheles kai eklyseis uses katheles ("pull down" or "cast down") with kai introducing the result clause ("you shall loosen").
