## Text

**38:1** Now it came to pass at that time that Judah went down from his brethren and came to a certain Adullamite man whose name was Hirah.

**38:2** And there Judah saw a Canaanite man's daughter, whose name was Shua; and he took her and went in to her.

**38:3** And she conceived and bore a son, and called his name Er.

**38:4** And she conceived again and bore a son, and called his name Onan.

**38:5** And she bore yet another son, and called his name Shelah; now she was in Chezib when she bore them.

**38:6** And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, whose name was Tamar.

**38:7** Now Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked before the Lord, and God slew him.

**38:8** Then Judah said to Onan, "Go in to your brother's wife and perform the duty of a brother-in-law to her, and raise up seed for your brother."

**38:9** But Onan knew that the seed would not be his; and it came to pass, whenever he went in to his brother's wife, that he poured it out upon the ground so as not to give seed to his brother.

**38:10** And the deed appeared wicked before God, that he did this, and He put him to death also.

**38:11** Then Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Remain a widow in your father's house until Shelah my son is grown." For he said, "Lest he also die like his brethren." So Tamar departed and remained in her father's house.

**38:12** And the days were multiplied, and Shua the wife of Judah died; and when Judah was comforted, he went up to those who were shearing his sheep, he and Hirah his shepherd, the Adullamite, to Timnah.

**38:13** And it was told to Tamar his daughter-in-law, saying, "Behold, your father-in-law has gone up to Timnah to shear his sheep."

**38:14** So she put off from herself the garments of her widowhood, wrapped herself in a veil, and adorned herself, and sat at the gates of Enan, which is on the way to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah his son was grown, yet he had not given her to him as a wife.

**38:15** And when Judah saw her, he supposed her to be a harlot, for she had covered her face, and he did not recognize her.

**38:16** And he turned aside from the way to her and said to her, "Let me come in to you," for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, "What will you give me if you come in to me?"

**38:17** And he said, "I will send you a kid of the goats from the flock." And she said, "If you give a pledge until you send it."

**38:18** And he said, "What pledge shall I give you?" And she said, "Your signet ring, and the necklace, and the staff that is in your hand." So he gave them to her and went in to her, and she conceived by him.

**38:19** Then she arose and departed, and took off the veil from herself, and put on the garments of her widowhood.

**38:20** And Judah sent the kid of the goats by the hand of his shepherd, the Adullamite, to receive the pledge back from the woman; but he did not find her.

**38:21** Then he asked the men of that place, "Where is the harlot who was in Enan by the wayside?" And they said, "There was no harlot here."

**38:22** So he returned to Judah and said, "I did not find her; and the men of that place say that there is no harlot here."

**38:23** And Judah said, "Let her keep them, lest we be mocked; I indeed have sent this kid, and you have not found her."

**38:24** And it came to pass after three months that it was told to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the harlot, and behold, she is with child by harlotry." And Judah said, "Bring her out and let her be burned!"

**38:25** When she was brought out, she sent to her father-in-law, saying, "By the man to whom these belong, I am with child." And she said, "Examine whose are the signet ring, and the necklace, and this staff."

**38:26** And Judah recognized them and said, "Tamar is justified rather than I, because I did not give her to Shelah my son." And he did not know her again.

**38:27** Now it came to pass, when she gave birth, that there were twins in her womb.

**38:28** And it came to pass, while she was giving birth, that the one put out his hand; and the midwife took a scarlet thread and bound it upon his hand, saying, "This one will come out first."

**38:29** Then as he drew back his hand, immediately his brother came out. And she said, "Why has a barrier been broken through because of you?" And she called his name Perez.

**38:30** And after this his brother came out, on whose hand was the scarlet thread; and she called his name Zerah.

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — In the Greek text the feminine relative pronoun makes Shua the name of Judah's wife herself (as also in verse 12), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text identifies Shua as her father.
- **38:5** — The Greek reads "she was in Chezib when she bore them," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to Judah ("he") being at Chezib when she bore Shelah ("him").
- **38:12** — Here and in verse 20, the Greek identifies Hirah as Judah's "shepherd," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic reading "friend."
- **38:14** — The Greek renders the place name as Enan (compare Hebrew "Enaim") and states that Tamar "adorned herself."
