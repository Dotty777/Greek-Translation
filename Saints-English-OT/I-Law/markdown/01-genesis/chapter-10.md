## Text

**10:1** Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and sons were born to them after the flood.

**10:2** The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elishah, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**10:3** And the sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**10:4** And the sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittim, and the Rhodians.

**10:5** From these the islands of the nations were separated in their land, each according to his tongue, in their tribes and in their nations.

**10:6** And the sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**10:7** And the sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca; and the sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan.

**10:8** Now Cush fathered Nimrod; he began to be a giant upon the earth.

**10:9** He was a giant hunter before the Lord God; therefore they say, "Like Nimrod, a giant hunter before the Lord."

**10:10** And the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

**10:11** From that land Asshur went forth, and he built Nineveh, and Rehoboth City, and Calah,

**10:12** and Dasem, between Nineveh and Calah; this is the great city.

**10:13** And Mizraim fathered the Ludim, the Nephthalim, the Anamim, and the Lehabim,

**10:14** and the Pathrusim and the Casluhim, from whom the Philistines came forth, and the Caphtorim.

**10:15** And Canaan fathered Sidon, the firstborn, and the Hittite,

**10:16** and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

**10:17** and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

**10:18** and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite. And afterward the tribes of the Canaanites were scattered abroad.

**10:19** And the borders of the Canaanites extended from Sidon as you go to Gerar and Gaza, as you go as far as Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, as far as Dasa.

**10:20** These are the sons of Ham in their tribes, according to their tongues, in their lands, and in their nations.

**10:21** And to Shem also children were born—the father of all the sons of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder.

**10:22** The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.

**10:23** And the sons of Aram were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

**10:24** And Arphaxad fathered Cainan, and Cainan fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

**10:25** And to Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided, and the name of his brother was Joktan.

**10:26** And Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**10:27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**10:28** Abimael, Sheba,

**10:29** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

**10:30** And their dwelling place extended from Mesha as you go toward Sephar, the mountain of the east.

**10:31** These are the sons of Shem in their tribes, according to their tongues, in their lands, and in their nations.

**10:32** These are the tribes of the sons of Noah according to their generations, according to their nations; from these the islands of the nations were scattered upon the earth after the flood.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek includes Elishah among the sons of Japheth here as well as among the sons of Javan in verse 4; in verse 4, the Greek also reads "the Rhodians" where the Hebrew reads Dodanim.
- **10:8** — In verses 8–9, the Greek describes Nimrod as a "giant" (gigas) rather than "mighty one," and in verse 9 adds "God" to the first occurrence of "the Lord."
- **10:12** — The Greek reads the city name "Dasem" where the Hebrew reads Resen; similarly, in verse 19 the Greek reads "Dasa" where the Hebrew reads Lasha.
- **10:24** — Following the Septuagint genealogy echoed in Luke 3:36, the Greek includes Cainan between Arphaxad and Shelah.
- **10:28** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which lists Obal before Abimael, the Greek lists only Abimael and Sheba in this verse; in verse 23, the Greek also reads "Meshech" (Mosoch) where the Hebrew has Mash.
