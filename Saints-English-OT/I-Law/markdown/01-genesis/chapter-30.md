## Text

**30:1** Now when Rachel saw that she had not borne children to Jacob, Rachel became envious of her sister and said to Jacob, "Give me children; otherwise I shall die."

**30:2** Then Jacob became angry with Rachel and said to her, "Am I in the place of God, who has deprived you of the fruit of the womb?"

**30:3** And Rachel said to Jacob, "Behold, my maidservant Bilhah; go in to her, and she shall bear upon my knees, that I also may obtain children through her."

**30:4** So she gave him her maidservant Bilhah to be his wife, and Jacob went in to her.

**30:5** And Bilhah, Rachel's maidservant, conceived and bore a son to Jacob.

**30:6** And Rachel said, "God has judged for me, and has heard my voice, and has given me a son." Therefore she called his name Dan.

**30:7** And Bilhah, Rachel's maidservant, conceived again and bore a second son to Jacob.

**30:8** And Rachel said, "God has come to my aid, and I have contended with my sister and have prevailed." And she called his name Naphtali.

**30:9** Now Leah saw that she had ceased bearing, and she took her maidservant Zilpah and gave her to Jacob as a wife.

**30:10** And Jacob went in to her; and Zilpah, Leah's maidservant, conceived and bore a son to Jacob.

**30:11** And Leah said, "With good fortune!" And she gave him the name Gad.

**30:12** And Zilpah, Leah's maidservant, conceived again and once more bore a second son to Jacob.

**30:13** And Leah said, "Blessed am I, for all the women count me blessed!" And she called his name Asher.

**30:14** Now Reuben went in the days of the wheat harvest and found mandrake apples in the field, and he brought them to Leah his mother. And Rachel said to Leah, "Give me some of your son's mandrakes."

**30:15** And Leah said, "Is it not enough for you that you have taken my husband? Will you also take my son's mandrakes?" And Rachel said, "Not so; let him lie with you this night in exchange for your son's mandrakes."

**30:16** Now Jacob came in from the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him and said, "You shall come in to me today, for I have hired you in exchange for my son's mandrakes." And he lay with her that night.

**30:17** And God heard her, and she conceived and bore a fifth son to Jacob.

**30:18** And Leah said, "God has given me my reward, because I gave my maidservant to my husband." And she called his name Issachar, which means Reward.

**30:19** And Leah conceived again and bore a sixth son to Jacob.

**30:20** And Leah said, "God has bestowed upon me a good gift; at this present time my husband will choose me, for I have borne him six sons." And she called his name Zebulun.

**30:21** And after this she bore a daughter, and she called her name Dinah. And she ceased bearing.

**30:22** Then God remembered Rachel, and God heard her and opened her womb.

**30:23** And she conceived and bore a son to Jacob. And Rachel said, "God has taken away my reproach."

**30:24** And she called his name Joseph, saying, "May God add to me another son."

**30:25** Now it came to pass, when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said to Laban, "Send me away, that I may go to my own place and to my own land.

**30:26** Give me the wives and the children for whom I have served you, that I may depart; for you know the service with which I have served you."

**30:27** And Laban said to him, "If I have found favor in your sight, I would take an omen; for God has blessed me at your coming in.

**30:28** Specify your wages to me, and I will give them to you."

**30:29** And Jacob said to him, "You know how I have served you, and how many livestock of yours were with me.

**30:30** For what was before me was little, and it has increased to a multitude; and the Lord has blessed you at my foot. Now therefore, when shall I also make a house for myself?"

**30:31** And Laban said to him, "What shall I give you?" And Jacob said to him, "You shall give me nothing. If you will do this thing for me, I will again shepherd your sheep and keep them.

**30:32** Let your sheep pass by today, and separate from there every dusky sheep among the lambs and every speckled and white one among the goats; it shall be my wages.

**30:33** And my righteousness shall answer for me tomorrow, because my wages are before you: everything that is not speckled and marked with white among the goats, and dusky among the lambs, shall be counted as stolen with me."

**30:34** And Laban said to him, "Let it be according to your word."

**30:35** And on that day he set apart the male goats that were speckled and marked with white, and all the female goats that were speckled and marked with white, and every one that was white among them, and every one that was dusky among the lambs, and delivered them into the hand of his sons.

**30:36** And he set a distance of a three days' journey between them and Jacob, while Jacob shepherded the remaining sheep of Laban.

**30:37** Now Jacob took for himself green rods of storax, and of walnut, and of plane tree, and Jacob peeled white strips in them, stripping off the green; and the white that he had peeled appeared variegated upon the rods.

**30:38** And he placed the rods that he had peeled in the troughs of the watering places, so that whenever the sheep came to drink before the rods—when they came to drink—the sheep would conceive in front of the rods.

**30:39** And the sheep brought forth young that were marked with white, and variegated, and ash-colored speckled.

**30:40** And Jacob separated the lambs, and he set before the sheep a ram marked with white and every variegated one among the lambs; and he set apart flocks for himself alone, and did not mingle them with the sheep of Laban.

**30:41** Now it came to pass, at the time when the sheep were in heat, conceiving in the womb, that Jacob placed the rods before the sheep in the troughs, so that they might conceive by the rods.

**30:42** For whenever the sheep gave birth, he did not place them; thus the unmarked ones became Laban's, and the marked ones became Jacob's.

**30:43** And the man became exceedingly, exceedingly rich, and he came to have much livestock, and oxen, and male servants, and female servants, and camels, and donkeys.

## Translator Notes

- **30:8** — The Greek reads "God has come to my aid, and I have contended with my sister," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "with mighty wrestlings I have wrestled with my sister."
- **30:15** — The Greek renders Rachel's opening reply as "Not so" (Ouch houtos), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Therefore."
- **30:27** — The Greek uses the potential expression "I would take an omen" (oionisamen an) and refers to "God" rather than the divine name found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **30:32** — In the Greek text Jacob instructs Laban to have the sheep pass by and separate them, whereas in the Hebrew Masoretic Text Jacob speaks in the first person ("I will pass through... removing").
- **30:42** — The Greek reads "whenever the sheep gave birth" and contrasts "unmarked" (asema) with "marked" (episema) animals, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text contrasts feeble and strong flocks.
