## Text

**18:1** Now God appeared to him by the oak of Mamre, as he was sitting at the door of his tent at midday.

**18:2** And lifting up his eyes, he looked, and behold, three men were standing above him; and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the door of his tent and bowed down to the ground,

**18:3** and said, "Lord, if indeed I have found favor before You, do not pass by Your servant.

**18:4** Let water now be brought, and let someone wash your feet, and cool yourselves beneath the tree.

**18:5** And I will bring bread, and you shall eat; and after this you shall pass on along your way, since you have turned aside to your servant." And He said, "Do just as you have spoken."

**18:6** So Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said to her, "Hurry and knead three measures of fine flour, and make hearth cakes."

**18:7** And Abraham ran to the herd and took a tender and good calf, and gave it to the servant, and he hastened to prepare it.

**18:8** Then he took butter and milk and the calf which he had prepared, and set them before them, and they ate; while he himself stood beside them under the tree.

**18:9** Then He said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" And he answered and said, "Behold, in the tent."

**18:10** And He said, "I will surely return to you at this time next year, and Sarah your wife shall have a son." Now Sarah was listening at the door of the tent, being behind Him.

**18:11** Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in days; and the manner of women had ceased to be with Sarah.

**18:12** So Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "It has not yet happened to me until now, and my lord is old."

**18:13** And the Lord said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh within herself, saying, 'Shall I indeed truly bear a child, though I have grown old?'

**18:14** Is any matter impossible with God? At this time next year I will return to you, and Sarah shall have a son."

**18:15** But Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid. And He said, "No, but you did laugh."

**18:16** Then the men rose up from there and looked down toward the face of Sodom and Gomorrah, and Abraham walked along with them to escort them on their way.

**18:17** And the Lord said, "Shall I hide from Abraham My servant the things which I am doing?

**18:18** For Abraham shall surely become a great and numerous nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him.

**18:19** For I knew that he will command his sons and his house after him, and they shall keep the ways of the Lord, to do righteousness and justice, so that the Lord may bring upon Abraham all the things which He has spoken concerning him."

**18:20** Then the Lord said, "The outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah has been multiplied, and their sins are exceedingly great.

**18:21** Therefore I will go down and see whether they are acting altogether according to their outcry that comes to Me; and if not, that I may know."

**18:22** And the men turned away from there and went to Sodom, while Abraham was standing before the Lord.

**18:23** And Abraham drew near and said, "Do not destroy the righteous together with the ungodly, so that the righteous shall be as the ungodly.

**18:24** If there are fifty righteous in the city, will You destroy them? Will You not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous, if they are in it?

**18:25** By no means shall You do such a thing as this, to slay the righteous with the ungodly, so that the righteous shall be as the ungodly—by no means! You who judge all the earth, shall You not execute justice?"

**18:26** And the Lord said, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous within the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake."

**18:27** Then Abraham answered and said, "Now I have begun to speak to the Lord, though I am earth and ashes:

**18:28** if the fifty righteous should fall short by five, will You destroy the whole city on account of the five?" And He said, "I will by no means destroy it, if I find forty-five there."

**18:29** And he spoke to Him yet again and said, "And if forty are found there?" And He said, "I will by no means destroy it for the sake of the forty."

**18:30** And he said, "Do not be displeased, Lord, if I speak: what if thirty are found there?" And He said, "I will by no means destroy it, if thirty are found there."

**18:31** And he said, "Since I am able to speak to the Lord, what if twenty are found there?" And He said, "I will by no means destroy it for the sake of the twenty."

**18:32** And he said, "Do not be displeased, Lord, if I speak yet once more: what if ten are found there?" And He said, "I will by no means destroy it for the sake of the ten."

**18:33** So the Lord departed when He ceased speaking to Abraham, and Abraham returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — The Greek reads "God," the singular "oak of Mamre," and "at midday" rather than the Hebrew "YHWH," "oaks of Mamre," and "in the heat of the day."
- **18:5** — The Greek uses the singular verb "He said" here and in verse 9, highlighting the Lord as the chief speaker among the three visitors.
- **18:12** — Instead of the Hebrew question about experiencing pleasure in old age, the Greek reads literally, "It has not yet happened to me until now, and my lord is old."
- **18:30** — The Greek opens with the elliptical deferential plea "Not anything, Lord" (repeated in verse 32), rendered here as "Do not be displeased, Lord."
