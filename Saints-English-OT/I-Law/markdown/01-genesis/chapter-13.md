## Text

**13:1** Then Abram went up from Egypt—he and his wife, and all that was his, and Lot with him—into the wilderness.

**13:2** Now Abram was exceedingly rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold.

**13:3** And he journeyed from where he had come into the wilderness as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the first, between Bethel and Ai,

**13:4** to the place of the altar, where he had made the tent; and there Abram called upon the name of the Lord.

**13:5** And Lot also, who journeyed with Abram, had sheep, and oxen, and livestock.

**13:6** Now the land could not contain them to dwell together, for their possessions were many, and they were not able to dwell together.

**13:7** And there arose strife between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and the herdsmen of Lot's livestock; and the Canaanites and the Perizzites then dwelt in the land.

**13:8** Then Abram said to Lot, "Let there be no strife between me and you, nor between your herdsmen and my herdsmen, for we men are brethren.

**13:9** And behold, the whole land is before you; separate yourself from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; and if you go to the right, I will go to the left."

**13:10** And Lot lifted up his eyes and beheld all the surrounding region of the Jordan, that it was all well-watered—before God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah—like the paradise of God and like the land of Egypt, all the way to Zoar.

**13:11** So Lot chose for himself all the surrounding region of the Jordan, and Lot set out from the east; and they separated each man from his brother.

**13:12** Now Abram dwelt in the land of Canaan, while Lot dwelt in a city of the surrounding regions and pitched his tent in Sodom.

**13:13** Now the men who were in Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful before God.

**13:14** And God said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, "Lift up your eyes and look from the place where you now are, to the north and south and east and west;

**13:15** for all the land which you see, to you I will give it and to your seed forever.

**13:16** And I will make your seed like the sand of the earth; if anyone is able to number the sand of the earth, then your seed also shall be numbered.

**13:17** Arise, walk through the land along its length and along its breadth, for to you I will give it, and to your seed forever."

**13:18** Then Abram moved his tent, and came and dwelt beside the oak of Mamre, which was in Hebron, and built there an altar to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — This Greek text reads "where he had made the tent" (ten skenen), a variant reading preserved in Septuagint witnesses alongside "at the beginning" (ten archen).
- **13:14** — "West" renders Greek thalassan, literally "sea" (the Mediterranean Sea), the standard biblical directional idiom for west (as also in 12:8).
- **13:16** — The Greek compares Abram's offspring to "the sand of the earth" rather than "the dust of the earth" as in the Hebrew text, and verse 17 adds "and to your seed forever."
