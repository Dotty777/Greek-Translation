## Text

**15:1** Now after these things the word of the Lord came to Abram in a vision, saying, "Do not fear, Abram; I shield you; your reward shall be exceedingly great."

**15:2** And Abram said, "Master, Lord, what will You give me, seeing I am departing childless, and the son of Masek my homeborn maidservant is this Damascus Eliezer?"

**15:3** And Abram said, "Since You have given no seed to me, indeed my homeborn servant will be my heir."

**15:4** And immediately the voice of the Lord came to him, saying, "This one shall not be your heir, but one who shall come forth from you, he shall be your heir."

**15:5** Then He brought him outside and said to him, "Look up now toward heaven and count the stars, if you are able to number them." And He said, "So shall your seed be."

**15:6** And Abram believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness.

**15:7** And He said to him, "I am God, who brought you out of the land of the Chaldeans, to give you this land to inherit."

**15:8** And he said, "Master, Lord, by what shall I know that I will inherit it?"

**15:9** And He said to him, "Take for Me a three-year-old heifer, and a three-year-old goat, and a three-year-old ram, and a turtledove, and a pigeon."

**15:10** So he took all these for Him, and divided them down the middle, and placed them opposite one another; but he did not divide the birds.

**15:11** And birds came down upon the bodies, their severed halves; and Abram sat down with them.

**15:12** Now about sunset a trance fell upon Abram; and behold, a great dark dread fell upon him.

**15:13** And it was said to Abram, "Know for certain that your seed shall be a sojourner in a land not their own, and they will mistreat them, and enslave them, and afflict them four hundred years.

**15:14** Yet the nation whom they shall serve, I will judge; and afterward they shall come out here with abundant possessions.

**15:15** But you shall depart to your fathers in peace, nourished in a good old age.

**15:16** And in the fourth generation they shall return here, for the sins of the Amorites are not yet filled up until now."

**15:17** Now when the sun was near setting, a flame appeared; and behold, a smoking furnace and torches of fire, which passed through the midst of these severed halves.

**15:18** There God made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your seed I will give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the Euphrates:

**15:19** the Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kelmonites,

**15:20** and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaim,

**15:21** and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Hivites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Greek takes Masek as the personal name of Abram's homeborn maidservant ("the son of Masek my homeborn maidservant"), differing from the difficult Masoretic phrase usually rendered "heir/steward of my house."
- **15:6** — The Greek specifies "Abram believed God" (MT: "he believed the LORD") and uses the passive verb "it was reckoned" (MT: active "He counted"), the wording quoted in the New Testament (Rom. 4:3; Gal. 3:6; Jas. 2:23).
- **15:11** — The Greek reads "Abram sat down with them," reflecting a different Hebrew verbal root ("sit") than the Masoretic Text ("drove them away").
- **15:15** — The Greek text here reads trapheis ("nourished") rather than tapheis ("buried," as in the MT and some Greek witnesses).
- **15:19** — "Kelmonites" preserves the Greek textual form corresponding to the Masoretic "Kadmonites"; in v. 21 the Greek also adds "the Hivites," bringing the list of nations to eleven.
