## Text

**43:1** Now the famine grew severe upon the land.

**43:2** And it came to pass, when they had finished eating the grain which they had brought from Egypt, that their father said to them, "Go again and buy a little food for us."

**43:3** But Judah spoke to him, saying, "The man solemnly warned us, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless your youngest brother comes down to me.'

**43:4** If therefore you send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you;

**43:5** but if you do not send our brother with us, we will not go; for the man spoke to us, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless your youngest brother is with you.'"

**43:6** Then Israel said, "Why did you deal ill with me, reporting to the man whether you had a brother?"

**43:7** And they said, "The man closely questioned us concerning ourselves and our kindred, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have a brother?' And we reported to him according to his questioning. Could we have known that he would say to us, 'Bring your brother'?"

**43:8** Then Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the lad with me, and we will arise and go, that we may live and not die, both we and you and our households.

**43:9** I myself will be surety for him; from my hand require him. If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, then I shall have sinned against you for all days.

**43:10** For if we had not delayed, by now we would have returned twice."

**43:11** Then Israel their father said to them, "If it is so, do this: take some of the fruits of the land in your vessels and carry down gifts to the man—some resin and some honey, incense and stacte, and terebinth and walnuts.

**43:12** And take double silver in your hands; the silver that was returned in your bags, return it with you; perhaps it was an oversight.

**43:13** Take your brother also, and arise and go down to the man.

**43:14** And may my God grant you favor before the man, that he may send back your one brother and Benjamin. For as for me, just as I have been made childless, I am made childless."

**43:15** So the men took these gifts, and they took double silver in their hands, and Benjamin; and they arose and went down to Egypt, and stood before Joseph.

**43:16** When Joseph saw them, and Benjamin his brother by the same mother, he commanded the one over his house to bring the men into the house: "And slaughter beasts and make ready, for the men shall eat bread with me at noon."

**43:17** And the man did just as Joseph said, and he brought the men into Joseph's house.

**43:18** Now when the men saw that they were brought into Joseph's house, they said, "It is because of the silver that was returned in our bags the first time that we are brought in, that he may falsely accuse us and fall upon us, to take us as servants and our donkeys."

**43:19** So they drew near to the man who was over Joseph's house, and spoke to him at the gateway of the house,

**43:20** saying, "We plead with you, my lord: we came down the first time to buy food;

**43:21** and it came to pass, when we came to the lodging place and opened our bags, that here was each man's silver in his bag. We have now returned our silver in full weight in our bags,

**43:22** and other silver we have brought with us to buy food; we do not know who put the silver into our bags."

**43:23** But the man said to them, "Mercy be to you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your fathers has given you treasures in your bags; and your approved silver I have received in full." And he brought Simeon out to them.

**43:24** And he brought water to wash their feet, and he brought fodder for their donkeys.

**43:25** Then they made ready the gifts until Joseph came at noon, for they had heard that he was about to dine there.

**43:26** And Joseph came into the house, and they brought to him the gifts which they had in their hands into the house, and bowed down before him with their faces to the ground.

**43:27** Then he asked them, "How are you?" And he said to them, "Is your aged father well, of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?"

**43:28** And they said, "Your servant our father is well; he is still alive." And he said, "Blessed is that man by God." And they bowed low and paid homage.

**43:29** Then Joseph lifted up his eyes and saw Benjamin, his brother by the same mother, and said to them, "Is this your youngest brother, whom you spoke of bringing to me?" And he said, "May God have mercy on you, child."

**43:30** And Joseph was troubled, for his inward parts were stirred over his brother, and he sought to weep; so he went into the inner chamber and wept there.

**43:31** Then he washed his face, came out, and restrained himself, and said, "Set out the bread."

**43:32** So they set out for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians who dined with him by themselves; for the Egyptians could not eat bread together with the Hebrews, because every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians.

**43:33** And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men were astonished, each one toward his brother.

**43:34** And they carried a portion from him to them; and the portion of Benjamin was enlarged beyond the portions of all, fivefold compared to theirs. And they drank and became drunk with him.

## Translator Notes

- **43:14** — The Septuagint reads "my God" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "God Almighty" (El Shaddai), and "your one brother" rather than "your other brother."
- **43:23** — "Mercy be to you" renders the Greek idiom hileos hymin (literally "gracious to you," an elliptic invocation of divine favor), corresponding to Hebrew "Peace be to you."
- **43:28** — The Septuagint includes Joseph's blessing, "Blessed is that man by God," which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **43:32** — The Septuagint specifies that "every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians" (cf. 46:34), expanding the shorter reading of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("for that is an abomination to the Egyptians").
