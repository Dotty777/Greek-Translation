## Text

**29:1** And Jacob lifted up his feet and journeyed to the land of the east, to Laban the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

**29:2** And he looked, and behold, a well in the field; and three flocks of sheep were resting there together, for out of that well they watered the flocks, and a great stone was upon the mouth of the well.

**29:3** Now all the flocks were gathered there, and they would roll away the stone from the mouth of the well and water the sheep, and restore the stone upon the mouth of the well to its place.

**29:4** And Jacob said to them, "Brethren, where are you from?" And they said, "We are from Haran."

**29:5** And he said to them, "Do you know Laban the son of Nahor?" And they said, "We know him."

**29:6** Then he said to them, "Is he well?" And they said, "He is well." While he was still speaking, behold, Rachel his daughter was coming with her father's sheep, for she tended her father's sheep.

**29:7** And Jacob said, "Much of the day still remains; it is not yet time for the livestock to be gathered together. Water the sheep, and go and pasture them."

**29:8** But they said, "We will not be able until all the shepherds are gathered together and roll away the stone from the mouth of the well; then we will water the sheep."

**29:9** While he was still speaking with them, Rachel the daughter of Laban came with her father's sheep, for she tended her father's sheep.

**29:10** Now it came to pass, when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban, his mother's brother, that Jacob drew near and rolled away the stone from the mouth of the well, and watered the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother.

**29:11** And Jacob kissed Rachel, and cried out with his voice and wept.

**29:12** And he declared to Rachel that he was her father's kinsman, and that he was the son of Rebekah; and she ran and reported to her father according to these words.

**29:13** And it came to pass, when Laban heard the name of Jacob, his sister's son, that he ran to meet him, embraced him, kissed him, and brought him into his house. And he recounted to Laban all these words.

**29:14** And Laban said to him, "You are of my bones and of my flesh." And he remained with him a month of days.

**29:15** Then Laban said to Jacob, "Because you are my kinsman, you shall not serve me for nothing; tell me what your wages are."

**29:16** Now Laban had two daughters: the name of the elder was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel.

**29:17** Now the eyes of Leah were weak, but Rachel was fair in form and lovely in countenance.

**29:18** And Jacob loved Rachel, and he said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."

**29:19** And Laban said to him, "It is better for me to give her to you than for me to give her to another man; dwell with me."

**29:20** And Jacob served seven years for Rachel.

**29:21** Then Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days are fulfilled, that I may go in to her."

**29:22** So Laban gathered together all the men of the place and made a wedding feast.

**29:23** And evening came, and he took Leah his daughter and brought her in to Jacob; and Jacob went in to her.

**29:24** And Laban gave Zilpah his maidservant to Leah his daughter to be her maidservant.

**29:25** Now morning came, and behold, it was Leah! And Jacob said to Laban, "What is this that you have done to me? Did I not serve you for Rachel? Why then have you deceived me?"

**29:26** And Laban said, "It is not done thus in our place, to give the younger before the elder;

**29:27** therefore complete her seven days, and I will give you this one also in return for the labor that you shall perform with me yet another seven years."

**29:28** And Jacob did so, and fulfilled her seven days; and Laban gave Rachel his daughter to him as a wife.

**29:29** And Laban gave Bilhah his maidservant to Rachel his daughter to be her maidservant.

**29:30** And he went in to Rachel, and he loved Rachel more than Leah; and he served him another seven years.

**29:31** Now when the Lord God saw that Leah was hated, He opened her womb; but Rachel was barren.

**29:32** And Leah conceived and bore a son to Jacob, and she called his name Reuben, saying, "Because the Lord has seen my humiliation; now my husband will love me."

**29:33** And Leah conceived again and bore a second son to Jacob, and she said, "The Lord has heard that I am hated, and has given me this one also." And she called his name Simeon.

**29:34** And she conceived yet again and bore a son, and she said, "At this present time my husband will be on my side, for I have borne him three sons." Therefore his name was called Levi.

**29:35** And conceiving yet again, she bore a son, and she said, "Now this time also I will give thanks to the Lord." Therefore she called his name Judah. And she ceased bearing.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Greek text adds Jacob's explicit destination ("to Laban the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau"), which is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **29:6** — The Greek places Rachel's arrival with her father's sheep in the narrator's voice already here ("While he was still speaking..."), before resuming the notice in verse 9, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text includes the mention of her approach within the shepherds' reply.
- **29:12** — Literally "brother of her father" (Greek adelphos, as also in verse 15), reflecting the broad Semitic usage of "brother" for a close male kinsman.
- **29:20** — This Greek critical text concludes after "seven years," without the additional clause found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Septuagint witnesses stating that the years seemed like a few days because of Jacob's love for Rachel.
- **29:27** — Greek ta hebdoma ("seven days" or "week," also in verse 28) refers to the traditional seven-day bridal celebration for Leah.
