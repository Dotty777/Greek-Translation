## Text

**16:1** Now Sarai, the wife of Abram, had borne him no children; and she had an Egyptian maidservant whose name was Hagar.

**16:2** So Sarai said to Abram, "Behold, the Lord has shut me up from bearing children; go in therefore to my maidservant, that you may have children by her." And Abram heeded her voice.

**16:3** And Sarai, the wife of Abram, took Hagar the Egyptian, her maidservant, after Abram had dwelt ten years in the land of Canaan, and gave her to Abram her husband to be his wife.

**16:4** So he went in to Hagar, and she conceived; and when she saw that she was with child, her mistress was dishonored in her sight.

**16:5** Then Sarai said to Abram, "I am wronged because of you! I gave my maidservant into your bosom; and when she saw that she was with child, I was dishonored in her sight. May God judge between me and you!"

**16:6** And Abram said to Sarai, "Behold, your maidservant is before you; deal with her as it may please you." And Sarai mistreated her, and she fled from her presence.

**16:7** Now the angel of the Lord God found her by the spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Shur.

**16:8** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?" And she said, "I am fleeing from the presence of Sarai my mistress."

**16:9** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Return to your mistress, and humble yourself under her hands."

**16:10** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "I will exceedingly multiply your seed, and it shall not be numbered for multitude."

**16:11** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Behold, you are with child, and you shall bear a son, and you shall call his name Ishmael, because the Lord has given ear to your affliction.

**16:12** He shall be a wild man; his hands shall be against all, and the hands of all against him, and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren."

**16:13** Then Hagar called the name of the Lord who spoke to her, "You are the God who looked upon me"; for she said, "Indeed, I have seen face to face Him who appeared to me."

**16:14** Therefore she called the well "The Well of Him Whom I Saw Face to Face"; behold, it is between Kadesh and Bered.

**16:15** So Hagar bore Abram a son; and Abram called the name of his son, whom Hagar bore to him, Ishmael.

**16:16** Now Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Greek spells her name Sara here (distinguished from Sarra after the name change in Genesis 17:15), rendered with the standard English pre-covenant spelling Sarai.
- **16:2** — The Greek uses the second-person verb "that you may have children by her," whereas the Masoretic Text has the first-person "that I may obtain children by her."
- **16:7** — This Greek text reads "the angel of the Lord God," whereas the Masoretic Text and many Greek witnesses read simply "the angel of the Lord."
- **16:13** — The Greek clarifies the difficult Hebrew saying as "I have seen face to face Him who appeared to me," and in v. 14 translates the well's name accordingly ("The Well of Him Whom I Saw Face to Face," MT: Beer-lahai-roi).
