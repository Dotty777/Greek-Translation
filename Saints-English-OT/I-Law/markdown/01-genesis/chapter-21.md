## Text

**21:1** And the Lord visited Sarah even as He had said; and the Lord did for Sarah according to what He had spoken.

**21:2** So Sarah conceived and bore a son to Abraham in old age, at the time just as the Lord had spoken to him.

**21:3** And Abraham called the name of his son who had been born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.

**21:4** Then Abraham circumcised Isaac on the eighth day, just as God had commanded him.

**21:5** Now Abraham was one hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.

**21:6** And Sarah said, "The Lord has made laughter for me; for whoever hears will rejoice with me."

**21:7** And she said, "Who will declare to Abraham that Sarah is nursing a child? For I have borne a son in my old age."

**21:8** And the child grew and was weaned; and Abraham held a great banquet on the day that his son Isaac was weaned.

**21:9** Now Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, who was born to Abraham, playing with her own son Isaac,

**21:10** and she said to Abraham, "Cast out this bondwoman and her son; for the son of the bondwoman shall by no means inherit with my son Isaac."

**21:11** And the matter appeared exceedingly harsh in the sight of Abraham concerning his son Ishmael.

**21:12** But God said to Abraham, "Do not let the matter be harsh in your sight concerning the child and concerning the bondwoman; in all that Sarah says to you, heed her voice, for in Isaac your seed shall be called.

**21:13** Yet the son of this bondwoman also I will make into a great nation, because he is your seed."

**21:14** So Abraham rose early in the morning, and took loaves of bread and a skin of water, and gave them to Hagar; and he set them upon her shoulder, together with the child, and sent her away. And having departed, she wandered through the wilderness by the Well of the Oath.

**21:15** And the water ran out from the skin, and she cast the child beneath a single fir tree.

**21:16** Then she went away and sat down opposite him at a distance, about a bowshot off; for she said, "I will by no means look upon the death of my child." So she sat opposite him afar off; and the child cried aloud and wept.

**21:17** And God heard the voice of the child from the place where he was; and the angel of God called to Hagar out of heaven and said to her, "What is it, Hagar? Do not fear, for God has heard the voice of your child from the place where he is.

**21:18** Arise, take up the child, and hold him fast with your hand; for I will make him into a great nation."

**21:19** Then God opened her eyes, and she beheld a well of living water; and she went and filled the skin with water, and gave the child to drink.

**21:20** And God was with the child; and he grew and dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer.

**21:21** And he dwelt in the wilderness; and his mother took a wife for him from the land of Egypt.

**21:22** Now it came to pass at that time that Abimelech, and Ahuzzath his bridal escort, and Phicol the commander-in-chief of his army, spoke to Abraham, saying, "God is with you in all things that you do.

**21:23** Now therefore, swear to me by God that you will not deal unjustly with me, nor with my seed, nor with my name; but according to the righteousness that I have shown toward you, so you shall deal with me, and with the land in which you have sojourned."

**21:24** And Abraham said, "I will swear."

**21:25** Then Abraham reproved Abimelech concerning the wells of water which the servants of Abimelech had taken away.

**21:26** And Abimelech said to him, "I do not know who has done this thing; neither did you tell me, nor have I heard of it except today."

**21:27** So Abraham took sheep and calves and gave them to Abimelech, and both of them made a covenant.

**21:28** And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock apart by themselves.

**21:29** Then Abimelech said to Abraham, "What are these seven ewe lambs of the flock that you have set apart by themselves?"

**21:30** And Abraham said, "You shall receive these seven ewe lambs from me, that they may be for a witness that I dug this well."

**21:31** Therefore he called the name of that place Well of Swearing, because there both of them swore an oath.

**21:32** So they made a covenant at the Well of the Oath. Then Abimelech rose up, and Ahuzzath his bridal escort, and Phicol the commander-in-chief of his army, and they returned to the land of the Philistines.

**21:33** And Abraham planted a field at the Well of the Oath, and there he called upon the name of the Lord, Everlasting God.

**21:34** And Abraham sojourned in the land of the Philistines many days.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Greek reads "will rejoice with me" (synchareitai moi) rather than "will laugh with me," emphasizing Sarah's joyful vindication; in v. 7 the Greek also reads "in my old age" rather than "in his old age."
- **21:9** — The Greek explicitly adds "with her own son Isaac," specifying with whom Ishmael was playing.
- **21:16** — Whereas the Hebrew has Hagar lift up her voice and weep, the Greek attributes the crying aloud and weeping to the child, directly preparing for God's hearing the child's voice in v. 17; likewise, v. 15 specifies a fir tree and v. 19 specifies living water.
- **21:22** — Both here and in v. 32 the Greek includes Ahuzzath (Greek Ochozath), Abimelech's bridal escort (nymphagogos), who appears only in Genesis 26:26 in the Hebrew text.
- **21:31** — Here the Greek translates Beersheba as "Well of Swearing" (Phrear horkismou), and in vv. 14, 32, 33, and 22:19 as "the Well of the Oath"; in v. 33 the Greek also renders the Hebrew tamarisk tree as "a field" (aroura).
