## Text

**34:1** Now Dinah the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to observe the daughters of the inhabitants of the land.

**34:2** And Shechem the son of Hamor the Horite, the ruler of the land, saw her; and he took her and lay with her, and humbled her.

**34:3** And he became devoted to the soul of Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and loved the maiden, and spoke to her according to the mind of the maiden.

**34:4** And Shechem spoke to Hamor his father, saying, "Take this young girl for me as a wife."

**34:5** Now Jacob heard that the son of Hamor had defiled Dinah his daughter; and his sons were with his livestock in the field, so Jacob kept silent until they came.

**34:6** And Hamor the father of Shechem went out to Jacob to speak with him.

**34:7** And the sons of Jacob came from the field; and when they heard, the men were deeply pierced in heart, and it was exceedingly grievous to them, because Shechem had done a disgraceful thing in Israel by lying with the daughter of Jacob; and it shall not be so.

**34:8** And Hamor spoke to them, saying, "Shechem my son has chosen your daughter in his soul; therefore give her to him as a wife.

**34:9** Intermarry with us: give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for your sons.

**34:10** And dwell among us, and behold, the land is broad before you; dwell and trade upon it, and acquire possessions in it."

**34:11** Then Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, "May I find favor before you, and whatever you say to us, we will give.

**34:12** Multiply the dowry exceedingly, and I will give according as you say to me; and you shall give me this girl as a wife."

**34:13** And the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit, and spoke to them, because they had defiled Dinah their sister.

**34:14** And Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah and sons of Leah, said to them, "We will not be able to do this, to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin, for it is a reproach to us.

**34:15** In this we will become like you and dwell among you, if you also become as we are, in that every male among you is circumcised;

**34:16** then we will give our daughters to you, and from your daughters we will take wives for ourselves; and we will dwell with you, and we will be as one kindred.

**34:17** But if you do not listen to us to be circumcised, we will take our daughters and depart."

**34:18** And the words were pleasing before Hamor and before Shechem the son of Hamor.

**34:19** And the young man did not delay to do this thing, for he was deeply attached to the daughter of Jacob; and he was the most honored of all those in the house of his father.

**34:20** And Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city, and spoke to the men of their city, saying,

**34:21** "These men are peaceful with us; let them dwell upon the land and trade in it, and behold, the land is broad before them. We will take their daughters for ourselves as wives, and we will give our daughters to them.

**34:22** Only in this will the men become like us to dwell with us, so as to be one people: in that every male among us is circumcised, just as they also are circumcised.

**34:23** And will not their livestock and possessions and their four-footed beasts be ours? Only in this let us become like them, and they will dwell with us."

**34:24** And all who went out of the gate of their city listened to Hamor and Shechem his son; and they were circumcised in the flesh of their foreskin, every male.

**34:25** Now it came to pass on the third day, when they were in pain, that the two sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, each took his sword and entered into the city securely, and slew every male.

**34:26** And they slew both Hamor and Shechem his son with the edge of the sword, and took Dinah out of the house of Shechem, and went out.

**34:27** And the sons of Jacob came upon the wounded, and plundered the city in which they had defiled Dinah their sister.

**34:28** And their sheep and their oxen and their donkeys, both all that was in the city and all that was in the field, they took.

**34:29** And all their persons and all their household goods and their wives they took captive; and they plundered both all that was in the city and all that was in the houses.

**34:30** Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have made me hateful, so that I am evil to the inhabitants of the land, both among the Canaanites and the Perizzites; and I am few in number, and gathering together against me they will strike me down, and I will be destroyed, I and my house."

**34:31** And they said, "But should they treat our sister like a harlot?"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The Greek text reads "the Horite" (ho Chorraios), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Hivite."
- **34:14** — The Septuagint explicitly names "Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah and sons of Leah" as the speakers, whereas the Masoretic Text refers generally to the sons of Jacob.
- **34:29** — Greek somata ("persons") is a common Hellenistic term for captives or household servants, and aposkeue ("household goods") frequently translates the Hebrew word for dependents or little ones.
