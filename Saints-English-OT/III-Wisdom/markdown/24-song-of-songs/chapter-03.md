## Text

**3:1** Upon my bed in the nights I sought him whom my soul loves; I sought him, and I did not find him; I called him, and he did not answer me.

**3:2** "I will arise now and go about in the city, in the marketplaces and in the broad streets, and I will seek him whom my soul loves." I sought him, and I did not find him.

**3:3** The watchmen who go about in the city found me: "Have you seen him whom my soul loves?"

**3:4** Scarcely had I passed from them when I found him whom my soul loves. I took hold of him and did not let him go, until I brought him into the house of my mother and into the inner chamber of her who conceived me.

**3:5** I have adjured you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the powers and by the forces of the field, that you neither stir up nor awaken love until it pleases.

**3:6** Who is this coming up from the wilderness like columns of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and frankincense, from all the powders of the perfumer?

**3:7** Behold, the couch of Solomon: sixty mighty men are around it, from the mighty men of Israel,

**3:8** all of them holding a sword, trained in warfare, each man with his sword upon his thigh because of terror in the nights.

**3:9** King Solomon made for himself a litter from the timbers of Lebanon;

**3:10** its pillars he made of silver, and its backrest of gold; its seat was purple, its interior paved with stone—love from the daughters of Jerusalem.

**3:11** Go forth and gaze upon King Solomon, upon the crown with which his mother crowned him in the day of his wedding and in the day of the gladness of his heart.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek includes the closing clause "I called him, and he did not answer me" (cf. 5:6), which is absent from the Masoretic Text here.
- **3:10** — Greek lithostroton literally means "paved with stone" (or mosaic-paved), followed directly by "love" in apposition.
- **3:11** — Unlike the Hebrew ("O daughters of Zion"), the Greek text does not specify an addressee for the commands "Go forth and gaze."
