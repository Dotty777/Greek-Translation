## Text

**36:1** Have mercy upon us, O Master, God of all, and look upon us, and cast Your fear upon all the nations.

**36:2** Lift up Your hand against foreign nations, and let them see Your mighty power.

**36:3** Just as You were sanctified in us before them, so be magnified in them before us;

**36:4** and let them know You, just as we also have known, that there is no God besides You, O Lord.

**36:5** Renew signs and work fresh wonders.

**36:6** Glorify Your hand and Your right arm; arouse fury and pour out wrath; remove the adversary and wipe out the enemy.

**36:7** Hasten the time and remember the oath, and let them declare Your mighty deeds.

**36:8** Let him who escapes be consumed in the wrath of fire, and may those who wrong Your people find destruction.

**36:9** Crush the heads of the enemy rulers who say, "There is none besides us."

**36:10** Gather all the tribes of Jacob—I also, the last, kept watch—and You shall give them their inheritance, as from the beginning.

**36:11** Have mercy, O Lord, upon the people called by Your name, and upon Israel, whom You have likened to a firstborn.

**36:12** Have compassion on the city of Your sanctuary, Jerusalem, the city of Your rest.

**36:13** Fill Zion with the praise of Your mighty deeds, and Your people with Your glory.

**36:14** Give testimony to those You created in the beginning, and raise up the prophecies spoken in Your name.

**36:15** Give reward to those who wait for You, and let Your prophets be proved trustworthy.

**36:16** Hear, O Lord, the supplication of Your suppliants, according to the blessing of Aaron concerning Your people; and all who are upon the earth shall know that You are the Lord, the God of the ages.

**36:18** The stomach will eat every kind of food, yet one food is better than another.

**36:19** The palate tastes foods of the hunt; so an understanding heart discerns false words.

**36:20** A perverse heart will bring grief, but a man of much experience will repay him.

**36:21** A woman will accept any man, yet one daughter is better than another.

**36:22** The beauty of a woman brightens the countenance, and it surpasses every desire of man.

**36:23** If mercy and gentleness are upon her tongue, her husband is not like other sons of men.

**36:24** He who acquires a wife makes the beginning of a possession—a helper suited to him and a pillar of rest.

**36:25** Where there is no hedge, the property will be plundered; and where there is no wife, a man will groan as a wanderer.

**36:26** For who will trust a nimble robber who roams from city to city? So it is with a man who has no nest and lodges wherever evening overtakes him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:7** — The Greek reads "oath" (horkismou), whereas the Hebrew text reads "appointed time."
- **36:10** — The clause "I also, the last, kept watch" is preserved in this Greek text as a textual seam remnant from the ancient manuscript transposition involving Sirach 33:16.
- **36:13** — The Greek text reads "Your people" (ton laon sou), whereas the Hebrew and certain other witnesses read "Your temple."
- **36:23** — Literally "her husband is not according to the sons of men," an idiom meaning that he is uniquely favored beyond ordinary men.
