## Text

**49:1** The remembrance of Josiah is like a blending of incense, prepared by the work of a perfumer; in every mouth it shall be sweet as honey, and like music at a banquet of wine.

**49:2** He was directed aright in the turning back of the people, and he removed the abominations of lawlessness.

**49:3** He directed his heart toward the Lord; in the days of lawless men he strengthened godliness.

**49:4** Except for David and Hezekiah and Josiah, all committed grave trespass; for they forsook the law of the Most High, and the kings of Judah came to an end.

**49:5** For they gave their horn to others, and their glory to a foreign nation.

**49:6** They burned the chosen city of the sanctuary and made her streets desolate, by the hand of Jeremiah;

**49:7** for they afflicted him, though he was sanctified in the womb as a prophet, to root out and to afflict and to destroy, and likewise to build and to plant.

**49:8** It was Ezekiel who saw a vision of glory, which He showed him upon a chariot of cherubim;

**49:9** for indeed he remembered the enemies in a rainstorm, and to do good to those who make their ways straight.

**49:10** And may the bones of the twelve prophets flourish again from their place; for He comforted Jacob and redeemed them in faithful hope.

**49:11** How shall we magnify Zerubbabel? Even he is like a signet ring upon the right hand.

**49:12** So also was Joshua the son of Jozadak, who in their days built the house and exalted a holy people to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory.

**49:13** And far and wide they bestow the remembrance of him who raised up for us the fallen walls, and set up gates and bars, and rebuilt our ruins.

**49:14** Not even one was created upon the earth such as Enoch, for he indeed was taken up from the earth.

**49:15** And Joseph was born a man, a leader of brethren, a support of the people, and his bones were cared for.

**49:16** Shem and Seth were glorified among men, and above every living creature in creation was Adam.

## Translator Notes

- **49:6** — The Greek text uses a singular verb ("it burned," matching "a foreign nation" in verse 5) followed by a plural verb ("they made desolate"). "By the hand of Jeremiah" refers to the fulfillment of the prophetic word spoken through Jeremiah.
- **49:9** — The Greek text reads "the enemies" (ton echthron), where the Hebrew text of Ben Sira refers to Job, whose Hebrew consonants closely resemble the word for "enemy."
- **49:10** — The Greek text has singular verbs ("comforted ... redeemed"), rendered here with the Lord as subject, whereas some Greek manuscripts read plural verbs referring to the twelve prophets.
- **49:12** — The Greek text reads "a holy people" (laon hagion), whereas some manuscripts read "a holy temple" (naon hagion).
- **49:13** — The Greek text reads the verb nemousin ("they bestow") rather than the proper name Neemiou ("of Nehemiah") found in other manuscripts, though the description of rebuilding the fallen walls, gates, and bars clearly refers to Nehemiah.
