## Text

**16:1** Do not desire a multitude of unprofitable children, nor rejoice over ungodly sons.

**16:2** Though they multiply, do not rejoice over them; if the fear of the Lord is not with them,

**16:3** do not trust in their life, and do not rely upon their place; for one is better than a thousand, and to die childless than to have ungodly children.

**16:4** For by one man of understanding a city shall be populated, but a tribe of lawless ones shall be made desolate.

**16:5** Many such things I have seen with my eyes, and mightier things than these my ear has heard.

**16:6** In an assembly of sinners a fire shall be kindled, and in a disobedient nation wrath was kindled.

**16:7** He did not pardon the giants of old, who rebelled in their strength;

**16:8** He did not spare those among whom Lot sojourned, whom He abhorred because of their arrogance;

**16:9** He did not have mercy on the nation of destruction, those who were swept away in their sins,

**16:10** and likewise six hundred thousand foot soldiers, those who were gathered together in their hardness of heart.

**16:11** Even if there is one stiff-necked man, it is a wonder if he shall go unpunished; for mercy and wrath are with Him—mighty in forgiveness, and pouring out wrath.

**16:12** According to His great mercy, so also is His great reproof; He will judge a man according to his works.

**16:13** A sinner shall not escape with his plunder, and He will by no means delay the endurance of the godly.

**16:14** He will make a place for every act of mercy; each one shall find according to his works.

**16:17** Do not say, "I shall be hidden from the Lord, and who from on high will remember me? Among so great a people I shall by no means be remembered, for what is my soul in a measureless creation?"

**16:18** Behold, heaven and the heaven of the heaven of God, the deep and the earth, shall be shaken at His visitation.

**16:19** Together the mountains and the foundations of the earth quake with trembling when He looks upon them.

**16:20** And upon these things no heart will ponder; and who will consider His ways?

**16:21** And a tempest, which no man shall see—and the greater part of His works are in hidden places.

**16:22** Who will declare the works of righteousness, or who will wait for them? For the covenant is far off.

**16:23** One who is lacking in heart ponders these things, and a foolish and erring man ponders foolish things.

**16:24** Hear me, child, and learn knowledge, and attend to my words with your heart.

**16:25** I bring forth instruction by weight, and with exactness I declare knowledge.

**16:26** In the judgment of the Lord were His works from the beginning, and from their making He divided their portions.

**16:27** He set His works in order forever, and their dominions throughout their generations; they neither hungered nor grew weary, and they did not cease from their works.

**16:28** Not one pressed upon his neighbor, and forever they shall not disobey His word.

**16:29** And after these things the Lord looked upon the earth and filled it with His good things.

**16:30** With the life of every living creature He covered its face, and to it is their return.

## Translator Notes

- **16:9** — "Nation of destruction" translates Greek ethnos apoleias (reflecting the nations devoted to destruction), and "swept away" renders exermenous, which can also mean "lifted up" in arrogance.
- **16:14** — Verses 16:15–16 are absent from the primary Greek critical text and are omitted accordingly.
- **16:27** — Greek archas can mean either "dominions" (referring to the appointed realms of the heavenly luminaries) or "beginnings."
