## Text

**8:1** Do not contend with a mighty man, lest you fall into his hands.

**8:2** Do not quarrel with a rich man, lest he outweigh you in the balance; for gold has destroyed many, and has turned aside the hearts of kings.

**8:3** Do not contend with a loud-tongued man, and do not pile wood upon his fire.

**8:4** Do not jest with an undisciplined man, lest your forefathers be dishonored.

**8:5** Do not reproach a man who turns away from sin; remember that we are all under penalty.

**8:6** Do not dishonor a man in his old age, for some of us also are growing old.

**8:7** Do not rejoice over a dead man; remember that we all die.

**8:8** Do not neglect the discourse of the wise, and occupy yourself with their proverbs; for from them you shall learn instruction, and how to serve great men.

**8:9** Do not pass over the discourse of the aged, for they themselves learned from their fathers; for from them you shall learn understanding, and how to give an answer in the time of need.

**8:10** Do not kindle the coals of a sinner, lest you be burned in the fire of his flame.

**8:11** Do not rise up in the presence of an insolent man, lest he lie in wait as an ambush for your mouth.

**8:12** Do not lend to a man stronger than yourself; and if you lend, count yourself as one who has lost it.

**8:13** Do not become surety beyond your means; and if you become surety, take thought as one who must pay.

**8:14** Do not go to law against a judge, for according to his honor they will judge in his favor.

**8:15** Do not travel on the road with a reckless man, lest he become a heavy burden upon you; for he will act according to his own will, and by his folly you shall perish along with him.

**8:16** Do not enter into strife with a wrathful man, and do not travel with him through the wilderness; for blood is as nothing in his eyes, and where there is no help he will strike you down.

**8:17** Do not take counsel with a fool, for he will not be able to keep a word secret.

**8:18** Before a stranger do not do a secret thing, for you do not know what he will bring forth.

**8:19** Do not reveal your heart to every man, and do not let him return you a favor.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — "Outweigh you in the balance" renders a weighing-scale metaphor (literally "counterbalance your weight"), referring to a wealthy person's power to sway judgment.
- **8:5** — "Under penalty" (Greek en epitimois) means liable to punishment or judgment for one's own sins.
- **8:11** — "Rise up in the presence of" reflects the Greek idiom for reacting with provocation or confrontation, warning that an insolent person lies in wait to trap one's words.
- **8:19** — The second clause literally reads "let him not bring back a favor to you," warning against placing oneself under obligation to an untrustworthy confidant.
