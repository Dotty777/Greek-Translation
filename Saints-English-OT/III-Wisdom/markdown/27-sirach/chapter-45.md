## Text

**45:1** And He brought forth from him a man of mercy, finding favor in the eyes of all flesh, beloved by God and men—Moses, whose memorial is in blessings.

**45:2** He made him equal in glory to the holy ones, and magnified him in the terrors of enemies.

**45:3** By his words he caused signs to cease; He glorified him in the presence of kings; He gave him commandments for His people, and showed him of His glory.

**45:4** In his faithfulness and meekness He sanctified him; He chose him out of all flesh.

**45:5** He caused him to hear His voice, and led him into the thick darkness, and gave him commandments face to face, a law of life and knowledge, to teach Jacob a covenant, and His judgments to Israel.

**45:6** He exalted Aaron, a holy man like him, his brother, of the tribe of Levi.

**45:7** He appointed him to an everlasting covenant, and gave him the priesthood of the people; He blessed him with stately adornment, and girded him with a robe of glory.

**45:8** He clothed him with the perfection of splendor, and strengthened him with emblems of might: the linen breeches, and the full-length robe, and the ephod.

**45:9** And He encircled him with pomegranates, and with very many golden bells all around, to ring out a sound at his footsteps, to make the chime heard in the temple for a memorial to the sons of His people;

**45:10** with a holy garment, with gold and blue and purple, works of an embroiderer, with the breastpiece of judgment, with the manifestations of truth,

**45:11** with spun scarlet, the work of a craftsman, with precious stones of seal engraving in a setting of gold, works of a jeweler, for a memorial in carved inscription according to the number of the tribes of Israel;

**45:12** and a crown of gold upon the turban, an engraved seal of holiness, a splendor of honor, a work of might, delights of the eyes richly adorned, things of beauty.

**45:13** Before him there had never been such things; forever no outsider has put them on, except his sons only, and his descendants continually.

**45:14** His sacrifice shall be wholly consumed every day continually, twice.

**45:15** Moses filled his hands and anointed him with holy oil; it became to him an everlasting covenant, and to his seed for the days of heaven, to minister to Him and also to serve in the priesthood, and to bless His people in the Name.

**45:16** He chose him out of every living person, to offer sacrifice to the Lord, incense and a sweet aroma for a memorial, to make atonement for your people.

**45:17** He placed him over His commandments, giving authority in covenants of judgments, to teach Jacob the testimonies, and in His law to proclaim to Israel.

**45:18** Outsiders conspired together against him and envied him in the wilderness—the men who were with Dathan and Abiram, and the assembly of Korah, in wrath and anger.

**45:19** The Lord saw it and was not pleased, and they were consumed in the fury of wrath; He performed wonders upon them, to consume them in the fire of His flame.

**45:20** And He added glory to Aaron, and gave him an inheritance; He apportioned to them the firstfruits of the first produce, and in the first place He prepared bread in abundance.

**45:21** For indeed they shall eat the sacrifices of the Lord, which He gave both to him and to his seed.

**45:22** Yet in the land of the people he shall not inherit, and he has no portion among the people; for He Himself is your portion and inheritance.

**45:23** And Phinehas the son of Eleazar is third in glory, because he was zealous in the fear of the Lord, and stood firm when the people turned away, in the goodness of the readiness of his soul; and he made atonement for Israel.

**45:24** Therefore a covenant of peace was established with him, that he should be guardian of the sanctuary and of his people, so that to him and to his seed might belong the majesty of the priesthood forever.

**45:25** And a covenant was made with David, a son of the tribe of Judah: the inheritance of the king passes from son to son alone, whereas the inheritance of Aaron belongs also to his seed.

**45:26** May He give you wisdom in your heart to judge His people in righteousness, so that their good things may not perish, and their glory may endure throughout their generations.

## Translator Notes

- **45:3** — The Greek reads κατέπαυσεν ("caused to cease"), referring to Moses ending the plagues in Egypt by his word, whereas some manuscripts read κατέσπευσεν ("hastened").
- **45:10** — The Greek phrase δήλοις ἀληθείας ("manifestations of truth") is the Septuagint rendering corresponding to the Urim and Thummim on the high priest's breastpiece (cf. Exodus 28:30).
- **45:15** — "Filled his hands" renders the biblical Greek idiom ἐπλήρωσεν τὰς χεῖρας, denoting priestly consecration and ordination (cf. Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:33).
- **45:16** — The Greek abruptly shifts to second person (τοῦ λαοῦ σου, "your people"), addressing Aaron directly as in 45:22, whereas some manuscripts harmonize to third person.
- **45:17** — Major Greek witnesses read φωνῆσαι ("to proclaim to" or "to summon"), whereas other witnesses read φωτίσαι ("to enlighten").
- **45:25** — The Greek reads simply "a son of the tribe of Judah" without naming Jesse, though some later witnesses supply "son of Jesse."
