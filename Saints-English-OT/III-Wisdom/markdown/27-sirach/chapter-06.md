## Text

**6:3** You shall devour your leaves and destroy your fruits, and leave yourself like a dry tree.

**6:4** An evil soul will destroy the one who possesses it, and will make him a laughingstock to enemies.

**6:5** A sweet throat will multiply its friends, and a well-spoken tongue will multiply courteous greetings.

**6:6** Let those who are at peace with you be many, but your counselors one out of a thousand.

**6:7** If you acquire a friend, acquire him through testing, and do not quickly put your trust in him.

**6:8** For there is a friend for his own occasion, and he will by no means abide in the day of your affliction;

**6:9** and there is a friend who turns to enmity, and he will expose a quarrel to your reproach;

**6:10** and there is a friend who is a companion at tables, and he will by no means abide in the day of your affliction;

**6:11** and in your prosperity he will be like you, and over your household servants he will speak boldly;

**6:12** if you are brought low, he will be against you, and he will hide himself from your face.

**6:13** Separate yourself from your enemies, and beware of your friends.

**6:14** A faithful friend is a mighty shelter; and he who has found him has found a treasure.

**6:15** For a faithful friend there is no equivalent, and there is no weight to measure his excellence.

**6:16** A faithful friend is a medicine of life, and those who fear the Lord shall find him.

**6:17** He who fears the Lord directs his friendship aright, for as he is, so also is his neighbor.

**6:18** Child, from your youth choose instruction, and until gray hairs you shall find wisdom;

**6:19** like one who plows and one who sows, draw near to her, and wait for her good fruits; for in cultivating her you shall toil a little, and quickly you shall eat of her produce.

**6:20** How exceedingly swift she is to the uninstructed, and the senseless man will not abide in her;

**6:21** like a heavy stone of testing she shall be upon him, and he will not delay to cast her away.

**6:22** For wisdom is according to her name, and she is not manifest to many.

**6:23** Listen, child, and receive my judgment, and do not refuse my counsel;

**6:24** and put your feet into her fetters, and your neck into her collar;

**6:25** put your shoulder under her and bear her, and do not be vexed at her bonds;

**6:26** with all your soul draw near to her, and with all your strength keep her ways.

**6:27** Search her out and seek her, and she shall be made known to you; and having taken hold of her, do not let her go;

**6:28** for at the last you shall find her rest, and she shall be turned for you into gladness;

**6:29** and the fetters shall be to you a shelter of strength, and her branches a robe of glory.

**6:30** For a golden ornament is upon her, and her bonds are a cord of blue;

**6:31** you shall put her on as a robe of glory, and you shall set upon yourself a crown of exultation.

**6:32** If you are willing, child, you shall be instructed, and if you apply your soul, you shall be prudent;

**6:33** if you love to listen, you shall receive it, and if you incline your ear, you shall be wise.

**6:34** Stand in the multitude of elders; and who is wise? Cling to him.

**6:35** Be willing to hear every godly discourse, and do not let proverbs of understanding escape you.

**6:36** If you see a man of understanding, rise early to go to him, and let your foot wear out the steps of his doors.

**6:37** Reflect upon the ordinances of the Lord, and meditate continually in His commandments; He Himself shall establish your heart, and your desire for wisdom shall be given to you.

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — Literally "a sweet throat" (λάρυγξ γλυκὺς), a Semitic idiom for pleasant, gracious speech.
- **6:20** — The Greek text reads ταχεῖα ("swift"), preserved by major uncial manuscripts, whereas other witnesses read τραχεῖα ("harsh" or "rough").
- **6:22** — Wisdom's hiddenness "according to her name" reflects a Hebrew wordplay between musar ("discipline" or "instruction") and mustar ("hidden") that lies behind the Greek rendering.
- **6:29** — The Greek text reads οἱ κλάδοι αὐτῆς ("her branches"), whereas other manuscripts read οἱ κλοιοὶ αὐτῆς ("her collars"), echoing verse 6:24.
