## Text

**48:1** And Elijah the prophet arose like fire, and his word burned like a torch.

**48:2** He brought a famine upon them, and by his zeal he made them few in number.

**48:3** By the word of the Lord he held back heaven, and thus three times he brought down fire.

**48:4** How you were glorified, O Elijah, in your wondrous deeds! And who can boast like you?

**48:5** He who raised up a dead man from death and from Hades by the word of the Most High;

**48:6** who brought kings down to destruction, and men of renown from their bed;

**48:7** who heard reproof at Sinai, and judgments of vengeance at Horeb;

**48:8** who anointed kings for retribution, and prophets as successors after him;

**48:9** who was taken up in a whirlwind of fire, in a chariot of fiery horses;

**48:10** who was recorded in reproofs for appointed times, to appease wrath before fury, and to turn the heart of a father to a son, and to restore the tribes of Jacob.

**48:11** Blessed are those who saw you and those who have been adorned in love, for we also shall surely live.

**48:12** Elijah was covered in a whirlwind, and Elisha was filled with his spirit; in his days he was not shaken by any ruler, and no one overpowered him.

**48:13** No matter was too great for him, and in sleep his body prophesied.

**48:14** In his life he performed wonders, and in death his works were marvelous.

**48:15** In all these things the people did not repent, and they did not depart from their sins, until they were carried off as plunder from their land and scattered throughout all the earth; and the people were left very few in number, yet with a ruler for the house of David.

**48:16** Some of them did what was pleasing, while others multiplied sins.

**48:17** Hezekiah fortified his city and brought Gog into their midst; he hewed the hard rock with iron and built cisterns for waters.

**48:18** In his days Sennacherib came up and sent Rabshakeh, and departed; and his hand was lifted up against Zion, and he boasted greatly in his arrogance.

**48:19** Then their hearts and hands were shaken, and they suffered anguish like women in labor.

**48:20** And they called upon the Lord, the Merciful One, stretching out their hands toward Him; and the Holy One swiftly heard them from heaven and delivered them by the hand of Isaiah.

**48:21** He struck the camp of the Assyrians, and His angel wiped them out.

**48:22** For Hezekiah did what was pleasing to the Lord and stood firm in the ways of David his father, which Isaiah the prophet commanded, who was great and faithful in his vision.

**48:23** In his days the sun went backward, and he added life to the king.

**48:24** By a mighty spirit he saw the last things, and he comforted those who mourned in Zion.

**48:25** He revealed the things that were to come even to the end of the age, and the hidden things before they came to pass.

## Translator Notes

- **48:11** — The Greek text reads "adorned in love" (kekosmemenoi), whereas some commentators conjecture "fallen asleep in love" (kekoimemenoi) from the Hebrew. "Shall surely live" renders the Hebraic cognate construction "shall live with life."
- **48:17** — The Greek text reads "Gog," an inner-Greek transmission variant for the spring Gihon, whose waters Hezekiah channeled into Jerusalem through rock (cf. 2 Chronicles 32:30).
- **48:18** — The Greek text has a nominative subject ("his hand") with an active verb, rendered passively here as "his hand was lifted up," where some manuscripts read the accusative ("he lifted up his hand").
