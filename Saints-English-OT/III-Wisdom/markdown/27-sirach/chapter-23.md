## Text

**23:1** O Lord, Father and Master of my life, do not forsake me to their counsel, and do not let me fall because of them.

**23:2** Who will set scourges over my thoughts, and the discipline of wisdom over my heart, that they may not spare my errors, and that it may not pass over their sins,

**23:3** so that my errors may not multiply and my sins may not abound, lest I fall before my adversaries and my enemy rejoice over me?

**23:4** O Lord, Father and God of my life, do not give me haughtiness of eyes,

**23:5** and turn lust away from me.

**23:6** Do not let appetite of the belly and fleshly passion overtake me, and do not give me over to a shameless soul.

**23:7** Discipline of the mouth. Hear the discipline of the mouth, children, and he who keeps it shall by no means be caught; by his own lips

**23:8** the sinner and the reviler and the arrogant shall be caught; they shall stumble by them.

**23:9** Do not accustom your mouth to an oath, and do not make a habit of naming the Holy One.

**23:10** For just as a servant who is continually examined under punishment will not be free from bruises, so also he who swears and continually names Him shall by no means be cleansed from sin.

**23:11** A man of many oaths shall be filled with lawlessness, and the scourge shall not depart from his house. If he offends, his sin is upon him; and if he disregards it, he has sinned doubly; and if he has sworn in vain, he shall not be acquitted, for his house shall be filled with calamities.

**23:12** There is a manner of speech clothed about with death; may it not be found in the inheritance of Jacob, for all these things shall be far from the godly, and they shall not wallow in sins.

**23:13** Do not accustom your mouth to foul coarseness, for in it there is a word of sin.

**23:14** Remember your father and your mother, for among great men one sits in council; lest you forget yourself before them and by your habit act the fool, and wish that you had not been born, and lest you curse the day of your birth.

**23:15** A man accustomed to words of reproach shall by no means be disciplined in all his days.

**23:17** Two kinds multiply sins, and the third shall bring on wrath. A passionate soul is like a burning fire; it shall by no means be quenched until it is consumed. A fornicator in the body of his flesh shall by no means cease until he kindles a fire. To a fornicator all bread is sweet; he shall by no means desist until he dies.

**23:18** A man who transgresses from his own bed, saying in his soul, "Who sees me? Darkness is around me, and the walls conceal me, and no one sees me; why should I be afraid? The Most High will by no means remember my sins."

**23:19** And the eyes of men are his fear, and he does not know that the eyes of the Lord are ten thousand times brighter than the sun, looking upon all the ways of men and perceiving into hidden places.

**23:20** Before all things were created, they were known to Him; and so also after they were brought to completion.

**23:21** This man shall be punished in the streets of the city, and where he did not expect it, he shall be seized.

**23:22** So also is a woman who forsakes her husband and brings forth an heir by another man.

**23:23** For first, she has disobeyed the law of the Most High; and second, she has trespassed against her own husband; and third, she has committed adultery in fornication, and has brought forth children by another man.

**23:24** She shall be brought out before the assembly, and there shall be a visitation upon her children.

**23:25** Her children shall not strike root, and her branches shall not yield fruit.

**23:26** She shall leave behind her memory for a curse, and her reproach shall not be blotted out.

**23:27** And those who remain shall know that nothing is better than the fear of the Lord, and nothing is sweeter than to give heed to the commandments of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Greek reads "their sins" (auton), referring to the thoughts and heart (or the unguarded lips of 22:27), with the singular verb pare ("pass over") agreeing with "discipline."
- **23:7** — "Discipline of the mouth" is an ancient Greek section heading preserved within the text of 23:7, and the closing phrase "by his own lips" continues directly into 23:8.
- **23:8** — Greek kataleiphthesetai (literally "will be left behind") reflects a common orthographic variant for katalephthesetai ("will be caught" or "overtaken"), paralleling "caught" in 23:7.
- **23:14** — The Greek text has third-person singular synedreuei ("one sits in council"), where some manuscripts read second-person "you sit," and resumes the warning at the end with me katarase ("lest you curse").
- **23:17** — The lines numbered 23:16 in some editions are joined to the beginning of 23:17 in this Greek versification.
