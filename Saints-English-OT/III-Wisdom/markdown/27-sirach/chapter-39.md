## Text

**39:1** He shall seek out the wisdom of all the ancients, and shall be occupied in prophecies.

**39:2** He shall preserve the discourses of men of renown, and shall enter into the subtleties of parables.

**39:3** He shall seek out the hidden things of proverbs, and shall be conversant with the riddles of parables.

**39:4** He shall serve in the midst of great men, and shall appear before a ruler; he shall pass through the land of foreign nations, for he has tested good and evil among men.

**39:5** He shall give his heart to rise early to the Lord who made him, and shall make supplication before the Most High; he shall open his mouth in prayer, and shall make supplication for his sins.

**39:6** If the great Lord wills, he shall be filled with the spirit of understanding; he himself shall pour forth words of wisdom, and in prayer he shall give thanks to the Lord.

**39:7** He himself shall direct his counsel and knowledge, and shall meditate upon His hidden things.

**39:8** He displays the discipline of his instruction, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.

**39:9** Many shall praise his understanding, and forever it shall not be blotted out; his memorial shall not depart, and his name shall live to generations of generations.

**39:10** Nations shall recount his wisdom, and the assembly shall proclaim his praise.

**39:11** If he remains, he shall leave a name greater than a thousand; and if he rests, it suffices for him.

**39:12** Having meditated yet further, I will declare it, for like the full moon I have been filled.

**39:13** Listen to me, O holy sons, and bud forth like a rose growing by a stream of the field,

**39:14** and like frankincense send forth a sweet aroma, and put forth a blossom like a lily; spread abroad a fragrance and sing a song of praise. Bless the Lord for all the works,

**39:15** give greatness to His name, and give thanks in His praise, with songs of the lips and with harps; and thus you shall say in thanksgiving:

**39:16** "All the works of the Lord are exceedingly good, and every command shall come to pass in its time.

**39:17** One must not say, 'What is this? Why is this?' for everything shall be sought out in its time. At His word the water stood as a heap, and at the utterance of His mouth the reservoirs of waters.

**39:18** At His command is all good pleasure, and there is no one who shall diminish His salvation.

**39:19** The works of all flesh are before Him, and it is not possible to be hidden from His eyes.

**39:20** From everlasting to everlasting He looks, and nothing is astonishing before Him.

**39:21** One must not say, 'What is this? Why is this?' for all things have been created for their uses.

**39:22** His blessing has covered over like a river, and like a flood has drenched the dry land;

**39:23** so shall the nations inherit His wrath, just as He turned waters into brine.

**39:24** His ways are straight for the holy, even so they are stumbling blocks for the lawless.

**39:25** Good things were created for the good from the beginning, even so evil things for sinners.

**39:26** The foremost of every need for the life of man are water, fire, and iron, and salt, and fine flour, wheat, and honey, and milk, the blood of the grape, and oil, and clothing.

**39:27** All these things are for good to the godly; even so to sinners they shall be turned into evil.

**39:28** There are spirits that were created for vengeance, and in their fury they have made firm their scourges; and in the time of consummation they shall pour forth strength, and shall appease the wrath of Him who made them.

**39:29** Fire and hail, and famine and death—all these were created for vengeance;

**39:30** the teeth of wild beasts, and scorpions, and vipers, and a sword executing vengeance upon the ungodly to destruction;

**39:31** in His commandment they shall rejoice, and upon the earth they shall be made ready for needs, and in their seasons they shall not transgress the word."

**39:32** Therefore from the beginning I was firmly convinced, and I meditated, and I left it in writing.

**39:33** All the works of the Lord are good, and He shall supply every need in its hour.

**39:34** And one must not say, "This is worse than that," for all things shall be approved in due time.

**39:35** And now with all heart and mouth we have sung praise; and bless the name of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **39:11** — The Greek omits the comparative before "than a thousand," and the verb rendered "it suffices for him" reflects an unusual Greek rendering of an expression meaning to have enough or be satisfied.
- **39:13** — The Greek text reads "by a stream of the field," whereas some other witnesses read "by a stream of water."
- **39:28** — Greek pneumata can mean either "spirits" or "winds"; the masculine plural pronouns and personified fury favor "spirits" as agents of divine judgment.
- **39:35** — The Greek text has the first-person plural "we have sung praise" alongside the imperative "bless," whereas many manuscripts read the imperative "sing praise" in the first clause as well.
