## Text

**46:1** Mighty in wars was Joshua the son of Nun, and the successor of Moses in prophecies, who became, according to his name, great for the salvation of His chosen ones, to execute vengeance upon enemies who rose up, so that he might give Israel their inheritance.

**46:2** How glorified he was when he lifted up his hands and turned the sword against cities!

**46:3** Who before him stood so firm? For the Lord Himself brought the enemies forward.

**46:4** Did not the sun turn back by his hand, and did not one day become as two?

**46:5** He called upon the Most High Mighty One when enemies pressed him hard on every side, and the great Lord answered them,

**46:6** with hailstones of mighty power. He hurled down battle upon the nation, and at the descent He destroyed those who withstood them, so that the nations might know their full armor, that his war was before the Lord; for indeed he followed after the Mighty One.

**46:7** And in the days of Moses he showed mercy, he and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, by standing against the enemy, restraining the people from sin, and quieting the murmuring of wickedness.

**46:8** And these two alone were preserved out of six hundred thousand men on foot, to lead them into their inheritance, into a land flowing with milk and honey.

**46:9** And the Lord gave strength to Caleb, and it remained with him until old age, so that he ascended the heights of the land, and his seed took possession of an inheritance,

**46:10** so that all the sons of Israel might see that it is good to walk after the Lord.

**46:11** And the judges also, each by his own name, all whose heart did not play the harlot and all who did not turn away from the Lord—may their memorial be in blessings.

**46:12** May their bones flourish again from their resting place, and may their name be renewed upon the sons of those glorified among them.

**46:13** Beloved by his Lord, the prophet of the Lord established a king and anointed rulers over His people.

**46:14** By the law of the Lord he judged the congregation, and the Lord visited Jacob.

**46:15** By his faithfulness he was proved a true prophet, and in his faithfulness he was known to be trustworthy in vision.

**46:16** And he called upon the Lord, the Mighty One, when his enemies pressed him on every side, with the offering of a nursing lamb.

**46:17** And the Lord thundered from heaven, and with a great sound He made His voice heard,

**46:18** and He crushed the leaders of the Tyrians and all the rulers of the Philistines.

**46:19** And before the time of his eternal sleep, he bore witness before the Lord and the anointed one: "Wealth, even down to sandals, I have not taken from any flesh." And no man accused him.

**46:20** And after he had fallen asleep, he prophesied and declared to the king his end; and he lifted up his voice from the earth in prophecy, to blot out the iniquity of the people.

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — The phrase "according to his name, great for the salvation of His chosen ones" alludes to the Hebrew meaning of the name Joshua ("the Lord saves").
- **46:3** — Major Greek witnesses read τοὺς πολεμίους Κύριος αὐτὸς ἐπήγαγεν ("the Lord Himself brought the enemies forward"), whereas other witnesses read τοὺς πολέμους Κυρίου αὐτὸς ἐπήγαγεν ("he himself waged the wars of the Lord").
- **46:13** — Major Greek witnesses omit the name "Samuel" here and read singular βασιλέα ("a king") rather than βασιλείαν ("a kingdom"), though the passage clearly refers to the prophet Samuel.
- **46:18** — "Tyrians" follows the Septuagint Greek Τυρίων, which renders Hebrew tsar (meaning either "Tyre" or "adversary") as the people of Tyre.
