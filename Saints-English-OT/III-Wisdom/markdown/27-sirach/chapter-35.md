## Text

**35:1** He who keeps the law multiplies offerings; he who heeds the commandments offers a peace offering.

**35:2** He who repays a kindness offers fine flour, and he who gives alms offers a sacrifice of praise.

**35:3** To depart from wickedness is the good pleasure of the Lord, and to turn away from unrighteousness is an atonement.

**35:4** Do not appear before the face of the Lord empty-handed, for all these things are on account of the commandment.

**35:5** The offering of a righteous man enriches the altar, and its sweet aroma is before the Most High.

**35:6** The sacrifice of a righteous man is acceptable, and its memorial shall not be forgotten.

**35:7** With a generous eye glorify the Lord, and do not diminish the firstfruits of your hands.

**35:8** In every gift make your countenance cheerful, and with gladness consecrate the tithe.

**35:9** Give to the Most High according to His gift, and with a generous eye according to what your hand has found.

**35:10** For the Lord is One who repays, and He will repay you sevenfold.

**35:12** Do not offer a bribe, for He will not accept it; and do not trust in an unrighteous sacrifice, for the Lord is Judge, and with Him there is no partiality.

**35:13** He will not show partiality against a poor man, and He will listen to the supplication of the one who has been wronged.

**35:14** He will by no means disregard the plea of the fatherless, nor the widow when she pours out her complaint.

**35:15** Do not the tears of the widow run down her cheek, and is not her outcry against him who makes them fall?

**35:16** He who serves in good pleasure shall be accepted, and his supplication shall reach to the clouds.

**35:17** The prayer of the humble pierces the clouds, and until it draws near he will not be comforted; and he will not depart until the Most High visits,

**35:21** and judges righteously and executes judgment. And the Lord will not delay, nor will He be patient with them, until He crushes the loins of the merciless and repays vengeance to the nations; until He takes away the multitude of the insolent and shatters the scepters of the unrighteous;

**35:22** until He repays man according to his deeds and the works of men according to their intentions; until He judges the cause of His people and makes them rejoice in His mercy.

**35:24** Timely is mercy in the time of their affliction, like clouds of rain in a time of drought.

## Translator Notes

- **35:17** — In this Greek versification, verses seventeen through twenty-two span material sometimes subdivided across additional verse numbers; in verse twenty-one, "righteously" translates the adverbial reading dikaios rather than the dative variant "for the righteous."
- **35:24** — The Greek literally reads "in the time of his affliction," with the singular pronoun referring collectively back to "His people" in verse twenty-two.
