## Text

**3:1** Hear me, your father, O children, and act accordingly, that you may be saved.

**3:2** For the Lord has honored a father over children, and has established the authority of a mother over sons.

**3:3** He who honors a father will make atonement for sins;

**3:4** and he who glorifies his mother is like one who stores up treasure.

**3:5** He who honors a father will be gladdened by children, and he who glorifies his mother is like one who stores up treasure; and in the day of his prayer he will be heard.

**3:6** He who glorifies a father will have long life, and he who obeys the Lord will give rest to his mother,

**3:7** and as to masters, he will serve those who brought him forth.

**3:8** In deed and in word honor your father, that a blessing may come upon you from him;

**3:9** for a father's blessing establishes the houses of children, but a mother's curse uproots foundations.

**3:10** Do not glory in the dishonor of your father, for there is no glory for you in dishonor;

**3:11** for a man's glory is from the honor of his father, and a mother in disgrace is a reproach to children.

**3:12** Child, care for your father in his old age, and do not grieve him during his lifetime;

**3:13** and even if he fails in understanding, have forbearance, and do not dishonor him in all your strength.

**3:14** For mercy toward a father will not be forgotten, and in place of sins it will be built up for you;

**3:15** in the day of your affliction you will be remembered; as fair weather upon frost, so your sins will melt away.

**3:16** He who forsakes a father is like a blasphemer, and he who provokes his mother to anger is cursed by the Lord.

**3:17** Child, perform your works in meekness, and you will be loved by an acceptable man;

**3:18** the greater you are, the more humble yourself, and before the Lord you will find favor;

**3:20** for great is the might of the Lord, and by the humble He is glorified.

**3:21** Do not seek things too hard for you, and do not examine things too strong for you.

**3:22** Reflect upon the things that have been commanded you, for you have no need of the hidden things.

**3:23** Do not meddle in matters beyond your works, for more than the understanding of men has been shown to you.

**3:24** For their own presumption has led many astray, and wicked conjecture has caused their minds to slip.

**3:26** And he who loves danger will fall into it; a stubborn heart will suffer evil at the last, a stubborn heart will be weighed down at the last.

**3:27** A stubborn heart will be weighed down with troubles, and the sinner will add sin upon sins.

**3:28** For the affliction of the proud there is no healing, for a plant of wickedness has taken root in him.

**3:29** The heart of a prudent man will ponder a parable, and a listening ear is the desire of a wise man.

**3:30** Water will quench a blazing fire, and almsgiving will make atonement for sins.

**3:31** He who repays favors is mindful of what comes afterward, and in the time of falling he will find support.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — The middle clause ("and he who glorifies his mother is like one who stores up treasure") repeats verse 4 verbatim in the Greek critical text.
- **3:10** — The Greek text reads "in dishonor" (pros atimian) rather than the variant "a father's dishonor" (patros atimia) found in some manuscripts.
- **3:14** — Literally "will be built up additionally for you," signifying that mercy shown to a father stands as a lasting credit offsetting sins.
- **3:26** — The Greek critical text places the clause on loving danger first with "will fall into it" (rather than "will perish") and doubles the clause concerning a stubborn heart at the last.
