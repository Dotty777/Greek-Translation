## Text

**44:1** A Hymn of the Fathers. Let us now praise glorious men, and our fathers in their lineage.

**44:2** Great glory the Lord created, His majesty from eternity.

**44:3** Those ruling in their kingdoms, and men of renown in power; they shall give counsel in their understanding, having declared things in prophecies;

**44:4** leaders of the people by counsels, and by understanding of the learning of the people; wise were the words in their instruction;

**44:5** those searching out melodies of music, and setting forth verses in writing;

**44:6** rich men furnished with strength, living peaceably in their habitations.

**44:7** All these were honored in their generations, and were a boast in their days.

**44:8** There are some of them who left behind a name, so that praises might be declared.

**44:9** And there are some of whom there is no memorial, and they perished as though they had never existed, and became as though they had never been born, and their children after them.

**44:10** Yet these were men of mercy, whose righteous deeds have not been forgotten.

**44:11** With their seed shall abide a good inheritance, even their offspring; in the covenants

**44:12** their seed stood firm, and their children for their sake.

**44:13** Their seed shall abide forever, and their glory shall not be blotted out.

**44:14** Their bodies were buried in peace, and their name lives through generations.

**44:15** Peoples shall declare their wisdom, and the assembly proclaims their praise.

**44:16** Enoch pleased the Lord and was taken up, an example of repentance to the generations.

**44:17** Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he became a ransom; therefore a remnant was left to the earth; therefore the flood came.

**44:18** Everlasting covenants were established with him, that all flesh should not be blotted out by a flood.

**44:19** Abraham was a great father of a multitude of nations, and no one was found like him in glory,

**44:20** who kept the law of the Most High, and entered into covenant with Him; and in his flesh he established the covenant, and in testing he was found faithful.

**44:21** Therefore with an oath He assured him that the nations would be blessed in his seed, that He would multiply him like the dust of the earth, and exalt his seed like the stars, and cause them to inherit from sea to sea, and from the river to the end of the earth.

**44:22** And in Isaac He likewise established, for the sake of Abraham his father, the blessing of all men and the covenant,

**44:23** and He caused them to rest upon the head of Jacob; He acknowledged him in His blessings, and gave it to him as an inheritance; and He divided his portions, and distributed them among twelve tribes.

## Translator Notes

- **44:3** — The Greek text uses the future verb bouleusontai ("they shall give counsel"), whereas other witnesses read the participle bouleuontes ("giving counsel").
- **44:11** — The prepositional phrase "in the covenants" (en tais diathekais) stands at the end of verse 11 in the Greek text and completes its clause in verse 12.
- **44:17** — The Greek text repeats dia touto ("therefore the flood came"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read hote ("when the flood came").
