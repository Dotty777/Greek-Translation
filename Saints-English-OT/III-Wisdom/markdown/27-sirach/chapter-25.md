## Text

**25:1** In three things I was adorned, and I stood forth beautiful before the Lord and men: the harmony of brethren, and the friendship of neighbors, and a wife and husband who walk together in agreement.

**25:2** And three kinds my soul hates, and I greatly loathe their way of life: a proud poor man, and a rich liar, an old adulterer lacking in understanding.

**25:3** In youth you have not gathered, and how could you find in your old age?

**25:4** How comely is judgment for gray hairs, and for elders to know counsel!

**25:5** How comely is the wisdom of the aged, and understanding and counsel for those held in honor!

**25:6** The crown of the aged is rich experience, and their glory is the fear of the Lord.

**25:7** Nine thoughts I have called blessed in my heart, and the tenth I will speak with my tongue: a man who rejoices over his children, living and looking upon the downfall of enemies;

**25:8** blessed is he who dwells with a wife of understanding, and he who has not slipped with his tongue, and he who has not served one unworthy of himself;

**25:9** blessed is he who has found prudence, and he who speaks to the ears of those who listen;

**25:10** how great is he who has found wisdom! Yet there is none above him who fears the Lord.

**25:11** The fear of the Lord surpasses everything; he who holds fast to it, to whom shall he be likened?

**25:13** Any wound, but not a wound of the heart; and any wickedness, but not the wickedness of a woman;

**25:14** any affliction, but not affliction from those who hate; and any vengeance, but not the vengeance of enemies.

**25:15** There is no head above the head of a serpent, and there is no wrath above the wrath of an enemy.

**25:16** I would rather dwell with a lion and a dragon than live in a house with a wicked woman.

**25:17** The wickedness of a woman alters her appearance, and darkens her countenance like sackcloth.

**25:18** In the midst of his neighbors her husband shall recline at table, and upon hearing, he groans bitterly.

**25:19** Small is every malice compared to the malice of a woman; may the lot of a sinner fall upon her!

**25:20** Like a sandy ascent to the feet of an old man, so is a talkative wife to a quiet husband.

**25:21** Do not fall before the beauty of a woman, and do not long after a woman.

**25:22** There is wrath and shamelessness and great shame if a wife supports her husband.

**25:23** A dejected heart, a gloomy countenance, and a wound of the heart—this is a wicked wife; drooping hands and feeble knees—she who does not make her husband happy.

**25:24** From a woman was the beginning of sin, and because of her we all die.

**25:25** Do not give water an outlet, nor authority to a wicked woman.

**25:26** If she does not walk according to your hand, cut her off from your own flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — Greek horaisthan ("I was adorned") and anesten horaia ("I stood forth beautiful") use the feminine form, representing the speaker's soul (cf. he psyche mou in v. 2).
- **25:9** — Greek phronesin means "prudence" or "good sense"; the surviving Hebrew text reads "a true friend."
- **25:15** — Greek kephale ("head") literally renders Hebrew rosh, a word that means both "head" and "venom/poison."
- **25:17** — Greek hos sakkon means "like sackcloth"; the surviving Hebrew text reads "like a bear."
