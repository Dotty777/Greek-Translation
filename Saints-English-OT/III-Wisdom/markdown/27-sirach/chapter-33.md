## Text

**33:1** No evil shall befall him who fears the Lord, but in trial He will deliver him again and again.

**33:2** A wise man will not hate the law, but he who plays the hypocrite in it is like a ship in a tempest.

**33:3** A man of understanding will trust in the law, and the law is trustworthy to him as an inquiry of the righteous.

**33:4** Prepare your speech, and so you shall be heard; bind together instruction, and then answer.

**33:5** The heart of a fool is like a cart wheel, and his reasoning is like a turning axle.

**33:6** A stallion is like a mocking friend; beneath everyone who sits upon him he neighs.

**33:7** Why does one day excel another, when all the light of every day of the year is from the sun?

**33:8** By the knowledge of the Lord they were separated, and He varied seasons and feasts.

**33:9** Some of them He exalted and sanctified, and some of them He set into the number of days.

**33:10** And all men are from the ground, and out of the earth Adam was created.

**33:11** In fullness of knowledge the Lord separated them, and He varied their ways.

**33:12** Some of them He blessed and exalted, and some of them He sanctified and brought near to Himself; some of them He cursed and brought low, and overturned them from their station.

**33:13** As the potter's clay is in his hand—all its ways are according to his good pleasure—so are men in the hand of Him who made them, to render to them according to His judgment.

**33:14** Opposite evil is good, and opposite death is life; so opposite the godly is the sinner.

**33:15** And so look upon all the works of the Most High: two by two, one opposite the other.

**33:17** As one who gleans behind the grape gatherers, by the blessing of the Lord I got ahead,

**33:18** and like a grape gatherer I filled a winepress. Consider that I have not labored for myself alone, but for all those who seek instruction.

**33:19** Hear me, O great men of the people, and you rulers of the assembly, give ear.

**33:20** To son and wife, to brother and friend, do not give authority over you during your life; and do not give your possessions to another, lest you regret it and have to beg for them.

**33:21** While you still live and breath is in you, do not give yourself in exchange to any flesh.

**33:22** For it is better that your children should ask of you than that you should look to the hands of your sons.

**33:23** In all your works remain preeminent; do not bring a blemish upon your honor.

**33:24** In the day of the completion of the days of your life, and at the time of your end, distribute your inheritance.

**33:25** Fodder and a rod and burdens for a donkey; bread and discipline and work for a servant.

**33:26** Keep a servant at work, and you shall find rest; leave his hands idle, and he will seek liberty.

**33:27** Yoke and thong will bow the neck, and for an evildoing servant there are racks and torments.

**33:28** Put him to work, that he may not be idle, for idleness teaches much wickedness.

**33:29** Set him to tasks as is fitting for him, and if he does not obey, make his fetters heavy.

**33:30** Yet do not go to excess with any flesh, and without judgment do nothing.

**33:31** If you have a servant, let him be as yourself, because you bought him with blood.

**33:32** If you have a servant, treat him as yourself, for you have need of him as of your own soul; if you mistreat him and he departs and runs away, on what road will you search for him?

## Translator Notes

- **33:3** — This Greek text reads dikaion ('an inquiry of the righteous'), whereas other Greek witnesses and editions read delon ('an inquiry of the Urim').
- **33:17** — In this Greek text, numbering moves directly from verse fifteen to verse seventeen without the opening clause ('And I, the last, kept watch') found in some manuscripts.
- **33:31** — 'With blood' is an idiom expressing that a servant was acquired at heavy personal cost, comparable to one's own lifeblood or livelihood.
- **33:32** — This Greek text reads 'as yourself' (rather than 'as a brother' in some witnesses) and literally says 'as your soul, there is need of him.'
