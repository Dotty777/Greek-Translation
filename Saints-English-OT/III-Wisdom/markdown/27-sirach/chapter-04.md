## Text

**4:1** Child, do not deprive the poor of his livelihood, and do not keep needy eyes waiting.

**4:2** Do not grieve a hungry soul, and do not provoke a man to anger in his distress.

**4:3** Do not add trouble to a heart provoked to anger, and do not delay a gift to one in need.

**4:4** Do not reject a suppliant in affliction, and do not turn your face away from a poor man.

**4:5** Do not turn your eye away from one in need, and do not give a man occasion to curse you;

**4:6** for when he curses you in the bitterness of his soul, He who made him will hear his supplication.

**4:7** Make yourself beloved to the assembly, and bow your head low before a great man.

**4:8** Incline your ear to the poor, and answer him with peaceful words in meekness.

**4:9** Deliver the wronged from the hand of the wrongdoer, and do not be fainthearted when you judge.

**4:10** Be like a father to orphans, and instead of a husband to their mother; and you will be like a son of the Most High, and He will love you more than your mother does.

**4:11** Wisdom has exalted her own sons, and she takes hold of those who seek her.

**4:12** He who loves her loves life, and those who rise early for her will be filled with gladness;

**4:13** he who holds fast to her will inherit glory, and wherever he enters, the Lord will bestow His blessing;

**4:14** those who serve her will minister to the Holy One, and those who love her the Lord loves;

**4:15** he who obeys her will judge nations, and he who draws near to her will dwell in confidence.

**4:16** If you trust, you will inherit her, and his generations will remain in possession;

**4:17** for at first she walks with him by crooked paths, and she will bring fear and dread upon him, and torment him with her discipline until she trusts his soul, and she will test him with her ordinances;

**4:18** and again she will return straight to him and make him glad, and she will reveal to him her hidden things.

**4:19** If he goes astray, she will forsake him, and deliver him into the hands of his downfall.

**4:20** Observe the right time and guard yourself from evil, and concerning your soul do not be ashamed;

**4:21** for there is a shame that brings sin, and there is a shame that is glory and grace.

**4:22** Do not show partiality against your own soul, and do not feel shame to your own downfall.

**4:23** Do not withhold speech in a time of salvation;

**4:24** for by speech wisdom will be known, and instruction by the word of the tongue.

**4:25** Do not speak against the truth, and be ashamed of your lack of instruction;

**4:26** do not be ashamed to confess your sins, and do not force the current of a river.

**4:27** And do not subject yourself to a foolish man, and do not show partiality to a ruler.

**4:28** Strive for the truth even to death, and the Lord God will fight for you.

**4:29** Do not be harsh in your tongue, and sluggish and slack in your works.

**4:30** Do not be like a lion in your house, and given to wild fancies among your household servants.

**4:31** Do not let your hand be stretched out to receive, and drawn back when it is time to repay.

## Translator Notes

- **4:13** — The subject of "enters" is unexpressed in Greek and may refer either to the one who holds fast to Wisdom or to Wisdom herself.
- **4:16** — The Greek text shifts abruptly from the second person ("If you trust, you will inherit her") to the third person ("his generations"), continuing in the third person through verse 19.
- **4:23** — Some later Greek witnesses add a second line warning not to hide wisdom in its beauty, which is absent from the Greek critical text translated here.
- **4:30** — The rare Greek participle phantasiokopon literally denotes being given to wild fancies or delusions, referring in context to erratic, suspicious, or frantic behavior toward one's household.
