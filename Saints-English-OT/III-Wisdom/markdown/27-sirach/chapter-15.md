## Text

**15:1** He who fears the Lord will do this, and he who holds fast to the law shall obtain her.

**15:2** And she shall come to meet him like a mother, and like a virgin bride she shall welcome him.

**15:3** She shall feed him with the bread of understanding, and give him the water of wisdom to drink.

**15:4** He shall lean upon her and shall not be swayed, and he shall hold fast to her and shall not be put to shame.

**15:5** And she shall exalt him above his neighbors, and in the midst of the assembly she shall open his mouth.

**15:6** He shall inherit gladness and a crown of rejoicing and an everlasting name.

**15:7** Men without understanding shall by no means lay hold of her, and sinful men shall not see her.

**15:8** She is far from pride, and lying men shall never remember her.

**15:9** Praise is not fitting in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent from the Lord.

**15:10** For praise shall be spoken in wisdom, and the Lord will make it prosper.

**15:11** Do not say, "Because of the Lord I fell away"; for what He hates, you shall not do.

**15:12** Do not say, "He Himself led me astray"; for He has no need of a sinful man.

**15:13** The Lord hates every abomination, and it is not beloved by those who fear Him.

**15:14** He Himself from the beginning made man, and left him in the hand of his own counsel.

**15:15** If you choose, you shall keep the commandments, and to practice faithfulness is a matter of good will.

**15:16** He has set fire and water before you; to whichever you desire, you shall stretch out your hand.

**15:17** Before men are life and death, and whichever one chooses shall be given to him.

**15:18** For great is the wisdom of the Lord; He is mighty in power and beholds all things.

**15:19** And His eyes are upon those who fear Him, and He Himself shall know every work of man.

**15:20** And He has commanded no one to act ungodly, and He has given no one license to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:11** — The critical Greek text reads second-person ou poieseis ("you shall not do"), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew text read third-person "He will not do."
- **15:14** — Greek diabouliou ("counsel") reflects the Hebrew term for inward inclination or moral deliberation, emphasizing human free choice.
- **15:15** — The compact Greek phrase pistin poiesai eudokias ("to practice faithfulness is a matter of good will") can denote either acting faithfully by one's own willing choice or practicing the faithfulness that pleases God.
