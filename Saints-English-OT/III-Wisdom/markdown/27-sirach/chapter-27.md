## Text

**27:1** For the sake of a trifle many have sinned, and he who seeks to increase will turn away his eye.

**27:2** Between the joints of stones a peg shall be driven fast, and between selling and buying sin shall be squeezed in.

**27:3** If he does not hold fast diligently in the fear of the Lord, swiftly his house shall be overthrown.

**27:4** In the shaking of a sieve the refuse remains; so does a man's filth in his reasoning.

**27:5** The furnace tests the potter's vessels, and the testing of a man is in his discourse.

**27:6** The fruit of a tree reveals its cultivation; so does speech reveal the thought of a man's heart.

**27:7** Before discourse, do not praise a man, for this is the testing of men.

**27:8** If you pursue righteousness, you shall attain it, and you shall put it on like a robe of glory.

**27:9** Birds will lodge with their own kind, and truth will return to those who practice it.

**27:10** A lion lies in wait for prey; so do sins for those who practice unrighteousness.

**27:11** The discourse of a godly man is always wisdom, but the fool changes like the moon.

**27:12** In the midst of those without understanding watch the time, but in the midst of a thoughtful man remain continually.

**27:13** The discourse of fools is an offense, and their laughter is in the wantonness of sin.

**27:14** The talk of one who swears many oaths makes the hair stand on end, and their strife is a stopping of the ears.

**27:15** The strife of the proud is a shedding of blood, and their reviling is grievous to hear.

**27:16** He who reveals secrets has destroyed trust, and he shall never find a friend after his own soul.

**27:17** Love a friend and keep faith with him; but if you reveal his secrets, you shall not pursue after him.

**27:18** For as a man has destroyed his enemy, so you have destroyed the friendship of your neighbor.

**27:19** And as you have let a bird go from your hand, so you have let your neighbor go, and you shall not catch him.

**27:20** Do not pursue him, for he has gone far away, and has escaped like a gazelle from a snare.

**27:21** For a wound can be bound up, and after reviling there is reconciliation; but he who has revealed secrets has lost all hope.

**27:22** He who winks with the eye devises evil things, and no one will keep himself away from him.

**27:23** Before your eyes he will sweeten your mouth and marvel at your words, but afterward he will twist his mouth and set a stumbling block in your words.

**27:24** Many things I have hated, yet I have compared none to him; and the Lord shall hate him.

**27:25** He who casts a stone on high casts it upon his own head, and a deceitful blow shall deal out wounds.

**27:26** He who digs a pit shall fall into it, and he who sets a snare shall be caught in it.

**27:27** He who does evil—it shall roll back upon him, and he shall not know from where it has come to him.

**27:28** Mockery and reproach belong to the proud, and vengeance, like a lion, shall lie in wait for him.

**27:29** Those who rejoice at the fall of the godly shall be caught in a snare, and anguish shall consume them before their death.

**27:30** Rage and wrath—these also are abominations, and a sinful man shall hold fast to them.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — The Greek text reads "a trifle" (literally "an indifferent thing"); some manuscripts read "profit" or "gain."
- **27:18** — The Greek text reads "his enemy"; some manuscripts and versions read "his dead" (i.e., someone lost to death).
- **27:23** — The Greek text reads "your mouth" (speaking sweetly to please you); some manuscripts read "his mouth."
