## Text

**17:1** The Lord created man out of the earth, and turned him back to it again.

**17:2** He gave them numbered days and an appointed time, and He gave them authority over the things upon it.

**17:3** Befitting themselves He clothed them with strength, and according to His own image He made them.

**17:4** And He placed the fear of him upon all flesh, so that he should have dominion over wild beasts and birds.

**17:6** Discretion, and a tongue, and eyes, ears, and a heart to think He gave to them.

**17:7** He filled them with knowledge of understanding, and showed them good and evil.

**17:8** He set His eye upon their hearts, to show them the majesty of His works,

**17:10** that they might declare the mighty deeds of His works. And they shall praise the name of holiness.

**17:11** He added knowledge to them, and bequeathed to them a law of life.

**17:12** He established an everlasting covenant with them, and showed them His judgments.

**17:13** Their eyes beheld the majesty of glory, and their ear heard the glory of their voice.

**17:14** And He said to them, "Beware of all unrighteousness," and He commanded each one of them concerning his neighbor.

**17:15** Their ways are continually before Him; they shall not be hidden from His eyes.

**17:17** Over every nation He appointed a ruler, and Israel is the Lord's portion.

**17:19** All their works are like the sun before Him, and His eyes are continually upon their ways.

**17:20** Their iniquities have not been hidden from Him, and all their sins are before the Lord.

**17:22** A man's act of mercy is like a signet with Him, and He will preserve a man's kindness as the apple of the eye.

**17:23** Afterward He will rise up and repay them, and He will return their recompense upon their own head.

**17:24** Yet to those who repent He granted a return, and He comforted those whose endurance was failing.

**17:25** Turn to the Lord and forsake sins; make supplication before His face, and lessen offense.

**17:26** Return to the Most High and turn away from iniquity, and intensely hate abomination.

**17:27** Who will praise the Most High in Hades, instead of the living and those who render thanksgiving?

**17:28** From the dead, as from one who does not exist, thanksgiving perishes; he who is alive and whole shall praise the Lord.

**17:29** How great is the mercy of the Lord, and forgiveness to those who turn to Him!

**17:30** For all things cannot be in men, because a son of man is not immortal.

**17:31** What is brighter than the sun? Yet this too fails; and an evil man will ponder flesh and blood.

**17:32** He Himself inspects the host of the height of heaven, and all men are earth and ashes.

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Greek text reads the plural kath' heautous ("befitting themselves" or "according to themselves"), whereas some manuscripts read the singular kath' heauton ("like Himself").
- **17:8** — The Greek text reads ton ophthalmon autou ("His eye"); some later manuscripts read "His fear." Verses 17:5, 17:9, 17:16, 17:18, and 17:21 are absent from the primary Greek critical text.
- **17:13** — The Greek critical text literally reads phones auton ("their voice"), whereas other witnesses read phones autou ("His voice").
- **17:31** — The Greek syntax places poneros ("an evil man") in the nominative and sarka kai haima ("flesh and blood") in the accusative; some versions invert the grammar to "flesh and blood devises evil."
