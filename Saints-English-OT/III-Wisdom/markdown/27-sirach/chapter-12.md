## Text

**12:1** If you do good, know to whom you do it, and there shall be gratitude for your good deeds.

**12:2** Do good to a godly man, and you shall find repayment, and if not from him, yet from the Most High.

**12:3** No good belongs to the one who persists in evil, nor to the one who does not bestow alms.

**12:4** Give to the godly man, and do not assist the sinner.

**12:5** Do good to the humble, and do not give to the ungodly; withhold his bread and do not give to him, lest through it he overpower you; for you shall find double evil for all the good that you do for him.

**12:6** For the Most High also hates sinners, and will repay vengeance upon the ungodly.

**12:7** Give to the good man, and do not assist the sinner.

**12:8** A friend will not be vindicated in prosperity, and an enemy will not be hidden in adversity.

**12:9** In a man's prosperity his enemies are in grief, and in his adversity even his friend will withdraw.

**12:10** Never trust your enemy; for as bronze corrodes, so does his wickedness.

**12:11** Even if he humbles himself and walks bowed down, keep your soul alert and guard yourself from him; and you shall be toward him like one who has wiped clean a mirror, and you shall know that it has not completely corroded.

**12:12** Do not set him beside yourself, lest having overthrown you, he stand in your place; do not seat him at your right hand, lest he seek your seat, and at the last you shall recognize my words and be pierced with remorse at my sayings.

**12:13** Who will pity a charmer bitten by a serpent, and all who draw near to wild beasts?

**12:14** So it is with one who draws near to a sinful man and becomes entangled in his sins.

**12:15** For an hour he will remain with you, yet if you turn aside, he will by no means stand firm.

**12:16** With his lips the enemy will speak sweetly, yet in his heart he will plot to overthrow you into a pit; with his eyes the enemy will weep, yet if he finds an opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.

**12:17** If calamities meet you, you shall find him there before you, and as though helping, he will trip up your heel;

**12:18** he will shake his head and clap his hands, and whisper many things, and change his countenance.

## Translator Notes

- **12:8** — The Greek critical text reads ekdikethesetai ("will be vindicated" or "avenged"), whereas the Hebrew text and some Greek witnesses read "will not be known."
- **12:11** — This difficult Greek clause literally says "it has not completely corroded," picturing the vigilance needed to see through an enemy's humble pretense as one inspects a wiped bronze mirror.
- **12:15** — Greek ou me karterese ("will by no means stand firm") can also carry the sense "will not hold back" from turning against you when you stumble.
