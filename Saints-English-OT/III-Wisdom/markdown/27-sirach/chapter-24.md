## Text

**24:1** The praise of Wisdom. Wisdom shall praise her own soul, and in the midst of her people she shall glory.

**24:2** In the assembly of the Most High she shall open her mouth, and before His host she shall glory:

**24:3** "I came forth from the mouth of the Most High, and like a mist I covered the earth.

**24:4** I dwelt in the high places, and my throne was in a pillar of cloud.

**24:5** Alone I encompassed the circuit of heaven, and in the depth of the abysses I walked.

**24:6** In the waves of the sea and in all the earth, and in every people and nation, I gained a possession.

**24:7** Among all these I sought a resting place, and in whose inheritance shall I abide?

**24:8** Then the Creator of all things commanded me, and He who created me caused my tent to rest and said, 'Make your dwelling in Jacob, and receive your inheritance in Israel.'

**24:9** Before eternity, from the beginning, He created me, and to eternity I shall never cease.

**24:10** In the holy tabernacle I ministered before Him, and so in Zion I was established.

**24:11** In the beloved city likewise He gave me rest, and in Jerusalem was my authority.

**24:12** And I took root among a glorified people, in the portion of the Lord, His inheritance.

**24:13** Like a cedar I grew tall in Lebanon, and like a cypress on the mountains of Hermon.

**24:14** Like a palm tree I grew tall on the shores, and like rose plants in Jericho; like a fair olive tree in the plain, and I grew tall like a plane tree.

**24:15** Like cinnamon and aromatic aspalathus I gave forth a scent, and like choice myrrh I spread abroad a sweet aroma, like galbanum, and onycha, and stacte, and like the vapor of frankincense in the tabernacle.

**24:16** Like a terebinth I stretched out my branches, and my branches are branches of glory and grace.

**24:17** I am like a vine putting forth grace, and my blossoms are the fruit of glory and riches.

**24:19** Come to me, you who desire me, and be filled with my produce.

**24:20** For the remembrance of me is sweeter than honey, and my inheritance is sweeter than the honeycomb.

**24:21** Those who eat me shall still hunger, and those who drink me shall still thirst.

**24:22** He who obeys me shall not be put to shame, and those who labor in me shall not sin."

**24:23** All these things are the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the law that Moses commanded as an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob.

**24:25** It overflows with wisdom like the Pishon, and like the Tigris in the days of new fruits;

**24:26** it fills up understanding like the Euphrates, and like the Jordan in the days of harvest;

**24:27** it makes instruction shine forth like light, like the Gihon in the days of vintage.

**24:28** The first man did not finish knowing her, and likewise the last has not searched her out.

**24:29** For her thought is fuller than the sea, and her counsel deeper than the great abyss.

**24:30** And I, like a canal from a river, and like a water conduit, went forth into an orchard.

**24:31** I said, "I will water my garden, and I will drench my flower bed." And behold, my canal became a river, and my river became a sea.

**24:32** I will yet make instruction shine like the dawn, and I will reveal these things far away.

**24:33** I will yet pour out teaching like prophecy, and I will leave it for generations forever.

**24:34** See that I have not labored for myself alone, but for all who diligently seek it.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Greek ektesamen means "I gained a possession" (or "acquired"); some manuscripts read hegesamen ("I held sway" or "ruled").
- **24:14** — Greek en aigialois literally means "on the shores" (or "along the banks"), whereas other textual traditions read "in En-gedi."
- **24:27** — Greek hos phos literally means "like light," likely reflecting a Hebrew wordplay or confusion between ye'or ("the Nile") and 'or ("light") alongside the other great rivers in vv. 25-27.
- **24:34** — Greek auten ("it" or "her") refers back grammatically to teaching (didaskalian, v. 33) and more broadly to Wisdom throughout the chapter.
