## Text

**3:1** But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and torment shall by no means touch them.

**3:2** In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to have died,

**3:3** and their departure was reckoned as affliction, and their going from us as destruction; but they are in peace.

**3:4** For even if in the sight of men they are punished, their hope is full of immortality;

**3:5** and having been chastened a little, they shall receive great good, because God tested them and found them worthy of Himself.

**3:6** Like gold in a smelting furnace He proved them, and like a whole burnt sacrifice He accepted them.

**3:7** And in the time of their visitation they shall shine forth, and they shall run like sparks through the stubble.

**3:8** They shall judge nations and have dominion over peoples, and the Lord shall reign over them forever.

**3:9** Those who trust in Him shall understand truth, and the faithful shall abide with Him in love, because grace and mercy are for His chosen ones.

**3:10** But the ungodly shall receive punishment according to what they reasoned, who disregarded the righteous man and turned away from the Lord.

**3:11** For wretched is he who despises wisdom and discipline; their hope is empty, their labors are unprofitable, and their works are useless.

**3:12** Their wives are foolish, and their children are wicked;

**3:13** cursed is their offspring. For blessed is the barren woman who is undefiled, who has not known a bed in transgression; she shall have fruit in the visitation of souls.

**3:14** And blessed is the eunuch who has worked no lawless deed with his hand, nor thought evil things against the Lord; for to him shall be given a choice favor for his faithfulness, and a more pleasing lot in the temple of the Lord.

**3:15** For the fruit of good labors is glorious, and the root of understanding does not fail.

**3:16** But the children of adulterers shall not come to maturity, and the seed of an unlawful bed shall vanish away.

**3:17** For even if they live long, they shall be counted as nothing, and at the last their old age shall be without honor.

**3:18** And if they die swiftly, they have no hope, nor consolation in the day of decision.

**3:19** For grievous are the ends of an unrighteous generation.

## Translator Notes

- **3:9** — Some manuscripts add "and visitation is upon His holy ones" at the end of the verse, which is absent from the Greek text translated here.
- **3:14** — Greek τῆς πίστεως χάρις ἐκλεκτή ("a choice favor for his faithfulness") echoes the promise to faithful eunuchs in Isaiah 56:4–5.
- **3:18** — Greek διάγνωσις denotes a formal judicial examination or verdict ("day of decision").
