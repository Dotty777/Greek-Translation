## Text

**1:1** Love righteousness, you who judge the earth; think of the Lord with goodness, and in singleness of heart seek Him.

**1:2** For He is found by those who do not put Him to the test, and manifests Himself to those who do not distrust Him.

**1:3** For crooked reasonings separate from God, and His power, when put to the test, reproves the foolish.

**1:4** For wisdom shall not enter a soul that devises evil, nor dwell in a body indebted to sin.

**1:5** For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and will withdraw from senseless thoughts, and will be repelled when unrighteousness approaches.

**1:6** For wisdom is a spirit that loves mankind, yet will not acquit a blasphemer for his lips; for God is a witness of his inward parts, a true overseer of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.

**1:7** For the Spirit of the Lord has filled the world, and that which holds all things together has knowledge of speech.

**1:8** Therefore no one who utters unrighteous things shall escape notice, nor indeed shall justice, when it convicts, pass him by.

**1:9** For inquiry shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly, and the report of his words shall come to the Lord for the conviction of his lawless deeds.

**1:10** For a jealous ear listens to all things, and the sound of murmurings is not hidden.

**1:11** Guard yourselves therefore against unprofitable murmuring, and spare your tongue from slander; for a secret utterance shall not go forth in vain, and a lying mouth slays the soul.

**1:12** Do not invite death in the error of your life, nor draw destruction upon yourselves by the works of your hands;

**1:13** for God did not make death, nor does He delight in the destruction of the living.

**1:14** For He created all things that they might exist, and the generations of the world are wholesome, and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor is the dominion of Hades upon the earth;

**1:15** for righteousness is immortal.

**1:16** But the ungodly by their hands and words summoned him; considering him a friend, they wasted away, and they made a covenant with him, because they are worthy to belong to his portion.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Greek ἐλεγχθήσεται literally means "will be put to shame" or "convicted," here conveying that the holy spirit of discipline is repelled and will not abide when unrighteousness approaches.
- **1:14** — Greek γενέσεις can mean "generations," "creatures," or "generative forces" of the world.
- **1:16** — The pronouns "him" and "his" refer back to personified Death (vv. 12–13), with v. 15 standing as a parenthetical statement.
