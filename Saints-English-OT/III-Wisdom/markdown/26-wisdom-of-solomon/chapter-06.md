## Text

**6:1** Hear therefore, O kings, and understand; learn, you judges of the ends of the earth.

**6:2** Give ear, you who rule over a multitude and take pride in throngs of nations.

**6:3** For dominion was given to you from the Lord, and sovereignty from the Most High, who will examine your works and search out your counsels.

**6:4** Because, though you were ministers of His kingdom, you did not judge rightly, nor did you keep the law, nor did you walk according to the counsel of God.

**6:5** Terribly and swiftly He will come upon you, because a stern judgment comes upon those in high places.

**6:6** For the lowliest man may be pardoned in mercy, but the mighty shall be mightily tested.

**6:7** For the Sovereign Lord of all will not shrink before anyone's presence, nor will He stand in awe of greatness; for He Himself made both small and great, and He cares for all alike.

**6:8** But upon the powerful a strict inquiry impends.

**6:9** To you therefore, O monarchs, are my words directed, that you may learn wisdom and not fall away.

**6:10** For those who have kept holy things in holiness shall be made holy, and those who have been taught them will find a defense.

**6:11** Set your desire therefore upon my words; long for them, and you shall be instructed.

**6:12** Radiant and unfading is wisdom, and she is readily beheld by those who love her.

**6:13** She anticipates those who desire her, making herself known to them first.

**6:14** He who rises early for her will not grow weary, for he will find her sitting beside his gates.

**6:15** For to reflect upon her is the perfection of understanding, and he who keeps vigil for her sake will quickly be free from anxiety.

**6:16** Because she herself goes about seeking those who are worthy of her, and in the pathways she graciously appears to them, and in every thought she meets them.

**6:17** For her truest beginning is the desire for instruction, and care for instruction is love,

**6:18** and love is the keeping of her laws, and giving heed to her laws is the assurance of incorruption,

**6:19** and incorruption brings one near to God;

**6:20** therefore the desire for wisdom leads up to a kingdom.

**6:21** If therefore you delight in thrones and scepters, O monarchs of the peoples, honor wisdom, that you may reign forever.

**6:22** Now what wisdom is and how she came into being I will declare, and I will not hide mysteries from you; rather, I will trace her out from the beginning of creation and bring the knowledge of her into the open, and I will by no means pass by the truth.

**6:23** Nor indeed will I travel in company with wasting envy, because such envy will have no fellowship with wisdom.

**6:24** But a multitude of the wise is the salvation of the world, and a discerning king is the stability of the people.

**6:25** Therefore be instructed by my words, and you shall be profited.

## Translator Notes

- **6:9** — Greek tyrannoi here refers to absolute sovereigns or monarchs rather than carrying a strictly pejorative sense.
- **6:12** — This critical text does not include the clause "and is found by those who seek her" that appears in some other editions.
- **6:17** — Verses 6:17-20 form a classical Greek sorites (chain argument), linking the desire for instruction step by step to royal dominion.
