## Text

**16:1** Therefore they were deservedly punished through like things, and through a multitude of noxious creatures they were tormented.

**16:2** Instead of that punishment, You showed kindness to Your people, and for the longing of their appetite You prepared an unusual delicacy, quail for food,

**16:3** so that the former, though desiring food, might turn away even from their necessary appetite because of the sight displayed of the creatures sent upon them, while the latter, having been in want for a little while, might partake even of an unusual delicacy.

**16:4** For it was necessary that relentless want should come upon those who acted as tyrants, but to these it should only be shown how their enemies were tormented.

**16:5** For even when the fierce fury of wild beasts came upon them, and they were perishing by the bites of writhing serpents, Your wrath did not continue to the end.

**16:6** Instead, they were troubled for a little while as an admonition, having a token of salvation as a reminder of the commandment of Your law;

**16:7** for the one who turned to look was not saved by the thing beheld, but by You, the Savior of all.

**16:8** And by this also You convinced our enemies that You are the One who delivers from every evil.

**16:9** For the bites of locusts and flies slew them, and no healing was found for their life, because they were worthy to be punished by such creatures;

**16:10** but not even the fangs of venomous serpents conquered Your sons, for Your mercy intervened and healed them.

**16:11** For they were stung to remind them of Your oracles, and swiftly rescued, lest falling into deep forgetfulness they should become unmindful of Your kindness.

**16:12** For neither herb nor poultice cured them, but Your word, O Lord, which heals all things.

**16:13** For You have authority over life and death; You lead down to the gates of Hades and bring up again.

**16:14** A man indeed slays in his malice, yet he does not bring back the spirit that has gone forth, nor does he release a soul once taken away.

**16:15** Yet it is impossible to escape Your hand.

**16:16** For the ungodly who refused to know You were scourged by the strength of Your arm, pursued by strange rains and hail and relentless downpours, and consumed by fire.

**16:17** For most wondrous of all, in the water that quenches all things the fire raged all the more fiercely, for the world is the defender of the righteous.

**16:18** For at one time the flame was tamed, so that it would not burn up the living creatures sent against the ungodly, but that seeing this they themselves might know that they were pursued by the judgment of God;

**16:19** and at another time, even in the midst of water, it blazes beyond the power of fire, to destroy the produce of an unrighteous land.

**16:20** Instead of these things, You fed Your people with the food of angels, and without toil You sent them bread made ready from heaven, able to yield every delight and suited to every taste.

**16:21** For Your sustenance manifested Your sweetness toward Your children; and serving the desire of him who partook of it, it was transformed into whatever each one wished.

**16:22** Now snow and ice endured fire and did not melt, so that they might know that fire, blazing in the hail and flashing through the rains, was destroying the crops of the enemies;

**16:23** yet this same fire, on the other hand, so that the righteous might be nourished, has forgotten even its own power.

**16:24** For creation, serving You who made it, strains itself for punishment against the unrighteous, and relaxes into kindness on behalf of those who trust in You.

**16:25** Therefore even then, transformed into all things, it ministered to Your all-nourishing bounty according to the wish of those in need,

**16:26** so that Your sons, whom You loved, O Lord, might learn that it is not the yielding of crops that nourishes man, but Your word preserves those who trust in You.

**16:27** For what was not destroyed by fire simply melted when warmed by a fleeting ray of the sun,

**16:28** so that it might be known that one must rise before the sun to give thanks to You, and pray to You at the dawning of the light.

**16:29** For the hope of the ungrateful will melt away like wintry frost, and will flow away like useless water.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The Greek phrase literally reads "because of the [one] shown of those sent upon them," with a feminine noun such as "appearance" or "sight" implied; some critics conjecture the similar-looking noun aeideian ("loathsomeness").
- **16:11** — Greek aperispastoi literally means "undistracted" or "unconcerned," used here with the genitive to mean becoming unresponsive to or unmindful of God's kindness.
- **16:21** — Greek hypostasis here means "sustenance" (referring to the manna provided by God), though the term can also denote "substance" or "nature."
