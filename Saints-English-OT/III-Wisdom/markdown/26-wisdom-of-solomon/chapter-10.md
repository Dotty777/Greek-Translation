## Text

**10:1** She guarded the first-formed father of the world, when he was created alone, and delivered him from his own transgression,

**10:2** and gave him strength to have dominion over all things.

**10:3** But when an unrighteous man departed from her in his wrath, he perished along with his brother-slaying fury.

**10:4** Because of him, when the earth was overwhelmed by flood, Wisdom saved it again, steering the righteous man by means of humble wood.

**10:5** She also, when the nations were confounded in the accord of their wickedness, found the righteous man, preserved him blameless before God, and kept him strong against his tender affection for his child.

**10:6** She rescued a righteous man while the ungodly were perishing, as he fled the fire descending upon Pentapolis,

**10:7** as a witness to whose wickedness a smoking wasteland still stands, and plants bearing fruit in unfulfilled seasons, and a standing pillar of salt, a memorial of an unbelieving soul.

**10:8** For having passed by Wisdom, they not only suffered the harm of not knowing what is good, but also left to human life a memorial of their folly, so that in the things in which they stumbled they might not even escape notice.

**10:9** But Wisdom rescued those who served her out of their troubles.

**10:10** She guided a righteous man fleeing from a brother's wrath in straight paths; she showed him the kingdom of God and gave him knowledge of holy things; she prospered him in his toils and multiplied the fruits of his labors;

**10:11** in the greed of those who oppressed him, she stood by him and made him rich;

**10:12** she guarded him from enemies and kept him safe from those who lay in wait, and in a fierce contest she awarded him the victory, that he might know that godliness is mightier than all.

**10:13** She did not forsake a righteous man when he was sold, but delivered him from sin;

**10:14** she went down with him into the pit, and in bonds she did not leave him, until she brought him the scepter of a kingdom and authority over those who tyrannized him; she also exposed as liars those who accused him, and gave him everlasting glory.

**10:15** She delivered a holy people and a blameless seed from a nation of oppressors.

**10:16** She entered into the soul of a servant of the Lord, and withstood fearsome kings with wonders and signs.

**10:17** She gave to the holy ones the reward of their labors, guided them along a marvelous way, and became for them a shelter by day and a flame of stars through the night.

**10:18** She brought them across the Red Sea, and led them through deep water;

**10:19** but their enemies she drowned, and cast them up from the depth of the abyss.

**10:20** Therefore the righteous plundered the ungodly, and sang hymns to Your holy name, O Lord, and praised Your defending hand with one accord;

**10:21** for Wisdom opened the mouth of the mute, and made the tongues of infants articulate.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — Pentapolis (literally 'Five Cities') is the Greek proper name used here for the five cities of the plain—Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Zoar (Genesis 14:2; 19:24–25).
- **10:10** — Greek hagion may be understood either as neuter ('holy things') or masculine ('holy ones,' i.e., angels), alluding to Jacob's vision at Bethel (Genesis 28:12–17).
- **10:12** — The 'fierce contest' alludes to Jacob's wrestling at the Jabbok (Genesis 32:24–30), employing athletic imagery (ebrabeusen, 'awarded the prize').
