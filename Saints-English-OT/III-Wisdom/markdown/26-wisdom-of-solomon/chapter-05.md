## Text

**5:1** Then the righteous man will stand with great boldness before the face of those who afflicted him and of those who made light of his labors.

**5:2** When they see him, they will be shaken with dreadful fear, and they will be astounded at the unexpectedness of salvation.

**5:3** They will say among themselves in repentance, and through anguish of spirit they will groan, "This was the one whom we once held in derision and as a byword of reproach,

**5:4** we fools! We accounted his life to be madness, and his end without honor.

**5:5** How has he been numbered among the sons of God, and how is his lot among the saints?

**5:6** Surely we have strayed from the way of truth, and the light of righteousness did not shine upon us, and the sun did not rise for us.

**5:7** We had our fill in the paths of lawlessness and destruction, and we journeyed through trackless deserts, but the way of the Lord we did not know.

**5:8** What has arrogance profited us? And what has wealth with boasting brought to us?

**5:9** All those things have passed away like a shadow, and like a message hurrying by;

**5:10** like a ship passing through billowing water, of which, when it has gone by, no trace can be found, nor the track of its keel in the waves;

**5:11** or as when a bird flies through the air, no proof of its course is found, but the light air, lashed by the stroke of its pinions and cloven by the force of its rushing flight, is traversed by the beating of its wings, and afterward no sign of its passage is found in it;

**5:12** or as when an arrow is shot at a target, the parted air immediately closes back into itself, so that one cannot discern its pathway:

**5:13** so we also, as soon as we were born, ceased to be, and we had no sign of virtue to show, but in our wickedness we were utterly consumed."

**5:14** For the hope of the ungodly is like chaff carried by the wind, and like thin frost driven by a tempest; like smoke it is dispersed by the wind, and it passes away like the remembrance of a guest who lodges for a single day.

**5:15** But the righteous live forever, and their reward is in the Lord, and the care of them is with the Most High.

**5:16** Therefore they shall receive the royal crown of splendor and the diadem of beauty from the hand of the Lord, because with His right hand He will shelter them, and with His arm He will shield them.

**5:17** He will take His zeal as His full armor, and will arm creation for vengeance against His enemies.

**5:18** He will put on righteousness as a breastplate, and wear unfeigned judgment as a helmet.

**5:19** He will take holiness as an invincible shield,

**5:20** and He will sharpen stern wrath into a sword, and the world will join Him in battle against the senseless.

**5:21** Well-aimed shafts of lightning will go forth, and as from a well-drawn bow of the clouds they will leap to the target,

**5:22** and from a catapult hailstones full of fury will be hurled; the water of the sea will rage against them, and rivers will relentlessly overwhelm them.

**5:23** A mighty blast will rise up against them, and like a whirlwind it will winnow them away; and all lawlessness will lay waste the earth, and evildoing will overturn the thrones of rulers.

## Translator Notes

- **5:14** — This critical text reads "frost" (pachne), whereas some manuscripts read "spray" or "foam" (achne).
- **5:23** — This critical text reads nominative "all" (pasa) modifying "lawlessness," whereas other witnesses read accusative "all" (pasan) modifying "the earth."
