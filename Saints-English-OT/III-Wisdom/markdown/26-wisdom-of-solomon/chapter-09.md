## Text

**9:1** "O God of the fathers and Lord of Your mercy, who made all things by Your word,

**9:2** and by Your wisdom formed man, that he should have dominion over the creatures that were made by You,

**9:3** and govern the world in holiness and righteousness, and render judgment in uprightness of soul;

**9:4** give me wisdom, who sits by Your thrones, and do not reject me from among Your children.

**9:5** For I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant, a weak man and short-lived, and lacking in the understanding of judgment and laws.

**9:6** For even if someone is perfect among the sons of men, yet without the wisdom that comes from You, he shall be counted as nothing.

**9:7** You chose me to be king of Your people and judge of Your sons and daughters;

**9:8** You gave command to build a temple on Your holy mountain, and an altar in the city of Your dwelling, a copy of the holy tabernacle which You prepared beforehand from the beginning.

**9:9** And with You is wisdom, who knows Your works and was present when You made the world, and who understands what is pleasing in Your sight and what is right in Your commandments.

**9:10** Send her forth from the holy heavens, and from the throne of Your glory send her, that being present with me she may labor, and that I may know what is well-pleasing before You.

**9:11** For she knows and understands all things, and she shall guide me prudently in my deeds and guard me in her glory.

**9:12** Then my works shall be acceptable, and I shall judge Your people righteously, and I shall be worthy of the thrones of my father.

**9:13** For what man can know the counsel of God? Or who can conceive what the Lord wills?

**9:14** For the reasonings of mortals are timid, and our designs are uncertain;

**9:15** for a perishable body weighs down the soul, and the earthly tent burdens a mind full of cares.

**9:16** And we scarcely guess at the things upon earth, and what is at hand we find with labor; but the things in the heavens, who has traced out?

**9:17** And who has known Your counsel, unless You gave wisdom and sent Your Holy Spirit from on high?

**9:18** And thus the paths of those on earth were made straight, and men were taught the things that are pleasing to You, and by wisdom they were saved."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek text literally reads "Lord of Your mercy" (Kyrie tou eleous sou), retaining the possessive pronoun attested in major Septuagint witnesses.
- **9:4** — The Greek uses the plural "Your thrones" (paralleled by "the thrones of my father" in verse 12), and paidon ("children") may also be rendered "servants."
- **9:15** — The metaphor of the perishable body as an "earthly tent" weighing down the soul draws on Hellenistic philosophical idiom to express mortal limitation rather than an inherently evil body.
