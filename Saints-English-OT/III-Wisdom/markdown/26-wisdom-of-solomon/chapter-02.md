## Text

**2:1** For they said among themselves, reasoning unsoundly: "Short and sorrowful is our life, and there is no remedy at the end of a man, and no one has been known who returned from Hades.

**2:2** For we were born by mere chance, and hereafter we shall be as though we had never existed; for the breath in our nostrils is smoke, and reason is a spark in the beating of our heart,

**2:3** which being extinguished, the body shall turn to ashes, and the spirit shall be dispersed like empty air.

**2:4** And our name shall be forgotten in time, and no one shall remember our works; and our life shall pass away like the traces of a cloud, and shall be scattered like a mist, driven away by the rays of the sun and weighed down by its heat.

**2:5** For our life is the passing of a shadow, and there is no turning back of our end; for it has been sealed, and no one returns.

**2:6** Come therefore, and let us enjoy the good things that exist, and let us use creation eagerly as in youth.

**2:7** Let us take our fill of costly wine and perfumes, and let no flower of the air pass us by;

**2:8** let us crown ourselves with rosebuds before they wither;

**2:9** let none of us be without a share in our revelry; everywhere let us leave behind tokens of our gladness, for this is our portion, and this is our lot.

**2:10** Let us oppress the righteous poor man; let us not spare the widow, nor show reverence for the gray hairs of the aged of many years.

**2:11** But let our might be the law of righteousness, for that which is weak is proved useless.

**2:12** Let us lie in wait for the righteous man, because he is troublesome to us, and opposes our works, and reproaches us for sins against the law, and charges us with sins against our upbringing.

**2:13** He professes to have knowledge of God, and calls himself a child of the Lord.

**2:14** He has become to us a reproof of our thoughts;

**2:15** he is grievous to us even to look upon, because his life is unlike that of others, and his paths are different.

**2:16** We are counted by him as counterfeit, and he keeps away from our ways as from uncleanness; he calls the end of the righteous blessed, and boasts that God is his Father.

**2:17** Let us see if his words are true, and let us test what shall happen at his departure.

**2:18** For if the righteous man is a son of God, He will uphold him, and will deliver him from the hand of those who oppose him.

**2:19** Let us examine him with outrage and torment, that we may know his gentleness and judge his forbearance.

**2:20** Let us condemn him to a shameful death, for there shall be a visitation for him according to his words."

**2:21** These things they reasoned, and they were led astray; for their own wickedness blinded them, and they did not know the mysteries

**2:22** of Him, nor did they hope for the wages of holiness, nor did they discern the prize of blameless souls.

**2:23** For God created man for incorruption, and made him the image of His own nature;

**2:24** but through the envy of the devil death entered into the world, and those who belong to his portion experience it.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — The Greek text reads ἄνθος ἀέρος ("flower of the air"); some manuscripts and emendations read ἄνθος ἔαρος ("flower of spring").
- **2:13** — Greek παῖδα can mean either "child" or "servant" of the Lord.
- **2:22** — The verse division splits the Greek phrase μυστήρια αὐτοῦ ("the mysteries of Him," referring to God); some manuscripts read θεοῦ ("of God") instead of αὐτοῦ.
- **2:23** — The Greek text reads ἰδιότητος ("nature" or "proper being"); some manuscripts read ἀϊδιότητος ("eternity").
