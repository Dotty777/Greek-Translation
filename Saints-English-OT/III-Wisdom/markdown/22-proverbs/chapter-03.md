## Text

**3:1** Son, do not forget my statutes, but let your heart keep my words;

**3:2** for they shall add to you length of lifetime and years of life and peace.

**3:3** Do not let deeds of mercy and faithfulness forsake you, but bind them upon your neck, and you shall find favor;

**3:4** and take thought for honorable things before the Lord and men.

**3:5** Be confident in God with your whole heart, and do not exalt yourself upon your own wisdom;

**3:6** in all your ways acknowledge her, so that she may cut your paths straight.

**3:7** Do not be wise in your own judgment, but fear God and turn away from all evil;

**3:8** then there shall be healing for your body and care for your bones.

**3:9** Honor the Lord from your righteous labors, and offer to Him firstfruits from your fruits of righteousness,

**3:10** so that your storehouses may be filled to abundance with grain, and your winepresses may gush forth with wine.

**3:11** Son, do not regard lightly the discipline of the Lord, nor grow faint when you are reproved by Him;

**3:12** for whom the Lord loves He reproves, and He scourges every son whom He receives.

**3:13** Blessed is the man who has found wisdom, and the mortal who has seen understanding.

**3:14** For it is better to trade in her than in treasures of gold and silver.

**3:15** And she is more precious than costly stones; nothing evil shall oppose her. She is well known to all who draw near to her, and nothing precious is worthy of her.

**3:16** For length of lifetime and years of life are in her right hand, and in her left hand are wealth and glory; out of her mouth righteousness goes forth, and she bears law and mercy upon her tongue.

**3:17** Her ways are fair ways, and all her paths are in peace;

**3:18** she is a tree of life to all who hold fast to her, and to those who lean upon her as upon the Lord.

**3:19** God by wisdom founded the earth, and by understanding He prepared the heavens;

**3:20** by perception the deeps broke open, and the clouds poured forth dews.

**3:21** Son, do not slip away, but keep my counsel and insight,

**3:22** so that your soul may live, and grace may be around your neck. And there shall be healing for your flesh and care for your bones,

**3:23** that you may walk confidently in peace in all your ways, and your foot shall by no means stumble.

**3:24** For if you sit down, you shall be fearless, and if you sleep, you shall slumber sweetly;

**3:25** and you shall not fear terror when it comes, nor the onslaughts of the ungodly approaching;

**3:26** for the Lord shall be over all your ways, and He shall steady your foot so that you may not be shaken.

**3:27** Do not refrain from doing good to the needy, whenever your hand is able to help.

**3:28** Do not say, "Go back and come again, tomorrow I will give," when you are able to do good; for you do not know what the coming day shall bring forth.

**3:29** Do not devise evil things against your friend, who dwells beside you and trusts in you.

**3:30** Do not be hostile toward a man without cause, lest he do some evil to you.

**3:31** Do not acquire the reproaches of wicked men, nor envy their ways.

**3:32** For every lawless man is unclean before the Lord, and he does not sit in council among the righteous.

**3:33** The curse of God is in the houses of the ungodly, but the dwellings of the righteous are blessed.

**3:34** The Lord opposes the proud, but to the humble He gives grace.

**3:35** The wise shall inherit glory, but the ungodly have exalted dishonor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — The Greek reads "in God" and "do not exalt yourself upon your own wisdom," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in the LORD" and "do not lean on your own understanding."
- **3:6** — In the Greek, the feminine pronoun ("her") and verb ("she may cut... straight") refer back to wisdom from verse 5 rather than to God.
- **3:15** — This verse and verse 16 contain expanded Septuagint clauses ("nothing evil shall oppose her. She is well known to all who draw near to her" and "out of her mouth righteousness goes forth, and she bears law and mercy upon her tongue") not found in the Masoretic Text.
- **3:18** — The Greek ends with the distinctive comparison "as upon the Lord," where the Masoretic Hebrew concludes with a beatitude on those who hold fast to wisdom.
