## Text

**4:1** Hear, children, the discipline of a father, and give heed to know insight.

**4:2** For I bestow upon you a good gift; do not forsake my law.

**4:3** For I also was a son obedient to a father, and beloved in the presence of a mother,

**4:4** who spoke and taught me, "Let our word be firmly fixed in your heart; keep commandments,

**4:5** do not forget, nor disregard the utterance of my mouth,

**4:6** nor forsake her, and she shall uphold you; love her ardently, and she shall guard you.

**4:8** Fence her about, and she shall exalt you; honor her, so that she may embrace you,

**4:9** that she may give to your head a crown of graces, and with a crown of delight she may shield you."

**4:10** Hear, son, and receive my words, and the years of your life shall be multiplied, so that many paths of living may be yours.

**4:11** For I teach you ways of wisdom, and I lead you onto straight paths.

**4:12** For if you walk, your steps shall not be hemmed in; and if you run, you shall not grow weary.

**4:13** Take hold of my discipline, do not let it go, but guard it for yourself for your life.

**4:14** Do not enter upon the ways of the ungodly, nor envy the ways of the lawless;

**4:15** in whatever place they encamp, do not go there, but turn away from them and pass by.

**4:16** For they shall by no means slumber unless they do evil; their sleep has been taken away, and they do not sleep;

**4:17** for these feed upon the food of ungodliness, and with lawless wine they grow drunk.

**4:18** But the ways of the righteous shine like light; they advance and give light until the day is established.

**4:19** But the ways of the ungodly are dark; they do not know how they stumble.

**4:20** Son, give heed to my utterance, and incline your ear to my words,

**4:21** so that your springs may not fail you; guard them in your heart;

**4:22** for it is life to those who find it, and healing to all flesh.

**4:23** With all vigilance guard your heart, for from these are the issues of life.

**4:24** Remove from yourself a crooked mouth, and thrust unjust lips far away from you.

**4:25** Let your eyes look upon what is straight, and let your eyelids incline toward what is righteous.

**4:26** Make straight courses for your feet, and guide your ways straight.

**4:27** Do not turn aside to the right or to the left, but turn your foot away from an evil way. For God knows the ways on the right, but those on the left are crooked; and He Himself shall make your courses straight, and shall lead your journeys forward in peace.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek uses the plural relative pronoun ("who") and possessive ("our word"), presenting both father and mother together as the speaker's teachers.
- **4:8** — Verse 7 of the Masoretic Hebrew is absent from the Septuagint; the rare Greek verb pericharakoo ("fence about") pictures fortifying wisdom like a walled city.
- **4:21** — The Greek reads "your springs" (ai pegai sou), diverging notably from the Masoretic Hebrew "your eyes."
- **4:27** — The second half of the verse ("For God knows the ways on the right...") is a substantial Septuagint expansion absent from the Masoretic Hebrew.
