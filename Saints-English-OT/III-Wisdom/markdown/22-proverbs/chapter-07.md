## Text

**7:1** Son, keep my words, and hide my commandments within yourself; son, honor the Lord and you shall be strong, and besides Him do not fear another.

**7:2** Guard my commandments and you shall live, and my words as the pupils of your eyes;

**7:3** bind them around your fingers, and inscribe them upon the breadth of your heart.

**7:4** Say that wisdom is your sister, and acquire prudence as a familiar friend for yourself,

**7:5** that she may guard you from a strange and wicked woman, if she should assail you with words spoken for favor.

**7:6** For peering down from a window out of her house into the streets,

**7:7** whomever she sees among the foolish children, a young man devoid of understanding,

**7:8** passing along beside the corner in the passageways of her houses, and speaking

**7:9** in the evening darkness, whenever there is the stillness of night and thick gloom,

**7:10** then the woman meets him, having the appearance of a harlot, she who causes the hearts of young men to take flight.

**7:11** Now she is restless and dissolute, and her feet do not remain still at home;

**7:12** for at one time she wanders outside, and at another time she lies in ambush in the streets at every corner.

**7:13** Then taking hold of him, she kissed him, and with a shameless countenance she spoke to him:

**7:14** "I have a peace offering; today I fulfill my vows.

**7:15** Because of this I came out to meet you; longing for your face, I have found you.

**7:16** I have strung my bed with cords, and I have spread it with double-woven tapestries from Egypt;

**7:17** I have sprinkled my couch with saffron, and my house with cinnamon;

**7:18** come, and let us enjoy love until dawn; come, and let us entwine ourselves in passion.

**7:19** For my husband is not at home, but he has gone on a long journey,

**7:20** having taken a bundle of silver in his hand; after many days he will return to his house."

**7:21** So she led him astray with much conversation, and with the snares from her lips she drove him off course;

**7:22** and he followed after her, easily beguiled, just as an ox is led to the slaughter, and as a dog to bonds,

**7:23** or like a stag struck in the liver by an arrow; and he hastens like a bird into a trap, not knowing that he runs at the cost of his life.

**7:24** Now therefore, son, listen to me, and pay heed to the words of my mouth.

**7:25** Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways;

**7:26** for she has wounded and cast down many, and countless are those whom she has slain;

**7:27** her house is the ways of Hades, leading down to the inner chambers of death.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Septuagint includes a second couplet ("son, honor the Lord and you shall be strong, and besides Him do not fear another") not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:6** — Unlike the Hebrew text where the speaker looks out his own window, the Greek makes the strange woman the subject peering down from the window of her house into the streets.
- **7:17** — The Greek reads "saffron" for the couch and "cinnamon" for "my house," differing from the Hebrew sequence of myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
- **7:22** — The Greek uses a rare word meaning "easily beguiled" (alluding to an easily trapped seabird) and likens the young man to "a dog to bonds" rather than a fool.
