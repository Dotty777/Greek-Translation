## Text

**81:1** A Psalm of Asaph. God has taken His stand in the assembly of gods, and in the midst He judges the gods.

**81:2** How long will you judge unrighteousness and show partiality to sinners? Interlude.

**81:3** Do justice for the orphan and the poor; vindicate the humble and the needy.

**81:4** Rescue the needy and the poor; deliver them from the hand of the sinner.

**81:5** They did not know, nor did they understand; they walk on in darkness; all the foundations of the earth shall be shaken.

**81:6** I said, "You are gods, and all of you are sons of the Most High."

**81:7** Yet indeed you die like men, and fall like one of the princes.

**81:8** Arise, O God, judge the earth; for You shall inherit among all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **81:1** — The Greek reads "assembly of gods" and makes "gods" the direct object of "judges," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "congregation of the mighty" and "in the midst of the gods He judges."
- **81:2** — Greek diapsalma is the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew selah, denoting a musical pause or interlude, and is translated "Interlude" throughout.
