## Text

**82:1** A Song of a Psalm of Asaph.

**82:2** O God, who shall be likened to You? Do not keep silent, nor remain still, O God.

**82:3** For behold, Your enemies have made an uproar, and those who hate You have lifted up their head.

**82:4** Against Your people they have craftily devised a plan, and they have taken counsel against Your holy ones.

**82:5** They said, "Come, and let us utterly destroy them from being a nation, and the name of Israel shall never again be remembered."

**82:6** For they have taken counsel together with one accord; against You they have made a covenant:

**82:7** the tents of the Edomites and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites,

**82:8** Naibal and Ammon and Amalek, and foreigners with the inhabitants of Tyre.

**82:9** For Assyria also has joined with them; they have become a help to the sons of Lot. Interlude.

**82:10** Do to them as to Midian and to Sisera, as to Jabin at the brook Kishon.

**82:11** They were utterly destroyed at Endor; they became like dung for the earth.

**82:12** Make their princes like Oreb and Zeeb, and Zebah and Zalmunna—all their princes,

**82:13** who said, "Let us inherit for ourselves the altar of God."

**82:14** O my God, make them like a wheel, like stubble before the face of the wind.

**82:15** As fire that shall burn up a forest, as if a flame should consume mountains,

**82:16** so You shall pursue them in Your tempest, and in Your wrath You shall trouble them.

**82:17** Fill their faces with dishonor, and they shall seek Your name, O Lord.

**82:18** Let them be put to shame and troubled forever and ever, and let them be disgraced and perish.

**82:19** And let them know that Your name is the Lord—You alone are Most High over all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **82:2** — The Greek reads "who shall be likened to You?" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "do not keep silent."
- **82:4** — The Greek reads "Your holy ones," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Your treasured ones" or "Your sheltered ones."
- **82:8** — This Greek text reads the proper name "Naibal," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and other Greek manuscripts read "Gebal." Greek allophyloi ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard term for the Philistines.
- **82:13** — This Greek text reads "the altar of God," whereas other Greek manuscripts read "the sanctuary of God" and the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the pastures" or "habitations of God."
