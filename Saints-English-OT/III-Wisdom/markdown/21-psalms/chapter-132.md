## Text

**132:1** A Song of Ascents. Behold now, what is good or what is pleasant, except for brethren to dwell together as one?

**132:2** It is like fragrant oil upon the head, running down upon the beard, the beard of Aaron, running down upon the border of his garment;

**132:3** like the dew of Hermon, descending upon the mountains of Zion; for there the Lord commanded the blessing and life forevermore.

## Translator Notes

- **132:1** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek heading omits "of David" and frames the verse as a rhetorical question ("what is good or what is pleasant, except...").
