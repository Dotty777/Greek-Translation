## Text

**14:1** A psalm of David. O Lord, who shall sojourn in Your tabernacle, and who shall dwell on Your holy mountain?

**14:2** He who walks blamelessly and practices righteousness, speaking truth in his heart;

**14:3** who has not dealt deceitfully with his tongue, nor done evil to his neighbor, and has not taken up a reproach against those nearest to him;

**14:4** in his sight an evildoer is despised, yet he honors those who fear the Lord; he who swears to his neighbor and does not break faith;

**14:5** he has not lent his silver at interest, and he has not accepted bribes against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be shaken.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — The Greek verb edolosen ("dealt deceitfully") reflects a different understanding of the underlying Hebrew than the Masoretic Text's "slanders."
- **14:4** — The Septuagint reads "to his neighbor" (to plesion autou), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's "to his own hurt."
