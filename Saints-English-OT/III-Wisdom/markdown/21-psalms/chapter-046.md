## Text

**46:1** For the end; a psalm for the sons of Korah.

**46:2** All you nations, clap your hands; shout aloud to God with a voice of exultation.

**46:3** For the Lord Most High is to be feared, a great King over all the earth.

**46:4** He has subjected peoples to us, and nations beneath our feet;

**46:5** He has chosen for us His inheritance, the beauty of Jacob, which He loved. Interlude.

**46:6** God has ascended with a shout, the Lord with the voice of a trumpet.

**46:7** Sing psalms to our God, sing psalms; sing psalms to our King, sing psalms;

**46:8** for God is King of all the earth; sing psalms with understanding.

**46:9** God has reigned over the nations; God sits upon His holy throne.

**46:10** The rulers of the peoples have gathered together with the God of Abraham, for God's mighty ones of the earth have been exceedingly exalted.

## Translator Notes

- **46:5** — The Septuagint reads "His inheritance" (ten kleronomian autou) rather than the Hebrew "our inheritance," and renders Hebrew ga'on ("majesty/pride") as kallone ("beauty").
- **46:10** — The Septuagint reads "with the God of Abraham" (reflecting Hebrew 'im "with" rather than 'am "people") and uses a plural verb ("have been exceedingly exalted") whose subject is "God's mighty ones of the earth," whereas the Hebrew has a singular verb referring to God.
