## Text

**141:1** Of understanding, of David, when he was in the cave; a prayer.

**141:2** With my voice I cried out to the Lord; with my voice I made supplication to the Lord.

**141:3** I will pour out my supplication before Him; I will declare my affliction in His presence.

**141:4** When my spirit failed within me, then You knew my paths; in this way in which I walked, they hid a snare for me.

**141:5** I looked to the right and watched, for there was no one who recognized me; escape perished from me, and there is no one who cares for my soul.

**141:6** To You, O Lord, I cried out, and I said, "You are my hope, my portion in the land of the living."

**141:7** Attend to my supplication, for I have been brought exceedingly low; deliver me from those who pursue me, for they have grown stronger than I.

**141:8** Bring my soul out of prison, that I may give thanks to Your name, O Lord; the righteous shall wait for me until You reward me.

## Translator Notes

- **141:1** — "Of understanding" translates Greek syneseos, the Septuagint equivalent of the Hebrew superscription term Maskil.
- **141:5** — The Greek uses first-person past verbs ("I looked... and watched") rather than second-person imperatives.
- **141:8** — The Greek reads "wait for me" (hypomenousin), differing from textual traditions that read "surround me."
