## Text

**33:1** For David, when he altered his countenance before Abimelech, and he sent him away, and he departed.

**33:2** I will bless the Lord in every season; continually shall His praise be in my mouth.

**33:3** In the Lord my soul shall be praised; let the meek hear and be glad.

**33:4** Magnify the Lord along with me, and let us lift up His name together.

**33:5** I sought out the Lord, and He heard me and rescued me from all my sojournings.

**33:6** Draw near to Him and be enlightened, and your faces shall by no means be put to shame.

**33:7** This poor man cried aloud, and the Lord heard him and saved him out of all his afflictions.

**33:8** The angel of the Lord will encamp around those who fear Him and will rescue them.

**33:9** Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the man who hopes in Him.

**33:10** Fear the Lord, all you His saints, for there is no lack for those who fear Him.

**33:11** The rich have become poor and have hungered, but those who seek out the Lord shall not be deprived of any good thing. Interlude.

**33:12** Come, children, hear me; I will teach you the fear of the Lord.

**33:13** Who is the man who desires life, who loves to see good days?

**33:14** Restrain your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit.

**33:15** Turn aside from evil and do good; seek after peace and pursue it.

**33:16** For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears are toward their supplication.

**33:17** But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil, to utterly destroy their remembrance from the earth.

**33:18** The righteous cried aloud, and the Lord heard them and rescued them out of all their afflictions.

**33:19** The Lord is near to those who are crushed in heart, and He will save the lowly in spirit.

**33:20** Many are the afflictions of the righteous, and out of them all He will rescue them.

**33:21** The Lord guards all their bones; not one of them shall be broken.

**33:22** The death of sinners is evil, and those who hate the righteous one shall incur guilt.

**33:23** The Lord will redeem the souls of His servants, and none of those who hope in Him shall by any means incur guilt.

## Translator Notes

- **33:3** — The Greek passive verb epainesthesetai ("shall be praised") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reflexive sense ("shall make its boast").
- **33:5** — The Greek reads "my sojournings" (paroikion mou), interpreting the Hebrew root in the sense of "sojourn" rather than "terror" or "fear" as in the Masoretic Text.
- **33:6** — The Greek uses plural imperatives ("Draw near... and be enlightened"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has third-person past verbs ("They looked... and were radiant").
- **33:11** — The Greek reads "the rich" (plousioi) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "young lions," and adds diapsalma ("Interlude") at the close of the verse.
