## Text

**122:1** A Song of Ascents. To You I have lifted up my eyes, O You who dwell in heaven.

**122:2** Behold, as the eyes of servants are toward the hands of their masters, as the eyes of a maidservant are toward the hands of her mistress, so our eyes are toward the Lord our God, until He has compassion on us.

**122:3** Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us, for we have been abundantly filled with contempt.

**122:4** Our soul has been filled all the more—reproach to those who prosper, and contempt to the proud.

## Translator Notes

- **122:1** — The superscription literally means "A Song of the Steps" (or Ascents). The Greek reads singular "in heaven" rather than plural "in the heavens."
- **122:2** — The Greek uses plural "hands" in both comparisons and employs a verb for "compassion" distinct from "have mercy" in verse 3.
- **122:4** — The Greek uses nominative nouns with datives ("reproach to those who prosper, and contempt to the proud"), which can be read either as a verdict against them or as a literal rendering of the Hebrew preposition describing their scorn.
