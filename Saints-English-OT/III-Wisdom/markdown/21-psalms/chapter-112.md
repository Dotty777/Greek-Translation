## Text

**112:1** Hallelujah. Praise the Lord, O servants; praise His name.

**112:2** May the name of the Lord be blessed from this time onward and forevermore.

**112:3** From the rising of the sun to its going down, the name of the Lord is praised.

**112:4** High above all the nations is the Lord; above the heavens is His glory.

**112:5** Who is like the Lord our God, who dwells in the heights,

**112:6** and looks upon the lowly things in heaven and on the earth?

**112:7** He raises the poor from the earth, and lifts up the needy from the dunghill,

**112:8** to seat him with rulers, with the rulers of His people.

**112:9** He causes the barren woman to dwell in a home, a mother of rejoicing children.

## Translator Notes

- **112:1** — Greek paides can mean either "servants" or "children"; unlike the Hebrew text ("servants of the Lord ... the name of the Lord"), the Greek reads simply "servants" and "His name."
- **112:3** — The Greek verb aineitai is present passive indicative ("is praised"), expressing ongoing universal praise rather than the gerundive sense ("is to be praised") in the Hebrew text and some Greek witnesses.
- **112:9** — The Greek participle euphrainomenon is genitive plural modifying "children" ("rejoicing children"), whereas the Hebrew text and other Greek manuscripts make the mother the joyful one.
