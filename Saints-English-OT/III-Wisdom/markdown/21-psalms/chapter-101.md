## Text

**101:1** A prayer for the poor man, when he grows faint and pours out his supplication before the Lord.

**101:2** O Lord, hear my prayer, and let my outcry come to You.

**101:3** Do not turn Your face away from me; in whatever day I am afflicted, incline Your ear to me; in whatever day I call upon You, hear me swiftly.

**101:4** For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones have been scorched like dry kindling.

**101:5** I have been struck down like grass, and my heart has withered, for I forgot to eat my bread.

**101:6** Because of the sound of my groaning, my bone has clung to my flesh.

**101:7** I have grown like a pelican of the wilderness; I have become as a night-raven in a ruined dwelling.

**101:8** I have lain awake and have become like a solitary sparrow upon a housetop.

**101:9** All day long my enemies reproached me, and those who praised me swore against me.

**101:10** For I have eaten ashes like bread and mingled my drink with weeping,

**101:11** from before Your wrath and Your indignation; for having lifted me up, You have dashed me down.

**101:12** My days have declined like a shadow, and I have withered away like grass.

**101:13** Yet You, O Lord, abide forever, and Your remembrance is from generation to generation.

**101:14** You shall arise and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to show compassion to her, for the season has come.

**101:15** For Your servants have taken pleasure in her stones, and they shall pity her dust.

**101:16** And the nations shall fear Your name, O Lord, and all the kings Your glory;

**101:17** because the Lord will build Zion, and He will be seen in His glory.

**101:18** He has looked upon the prayer of the humble, and has not despised their supplication.

**101:19** Let this be written for another generation, and a people being created shall praise the Lord;

**101:20** for He has looked forth from His holy height; from heaven the Lord has gazed upon the earth,

**101:21** to hear the groaning of those bound in fetters, to loose the sons of those who have been put to death,

**101:22** to proclaim in Zion the name of the Lord, and His praise in Jerusalem,

**101:23** when peoples are gathered together into one, and kingdoms, to serve the Lord.

**101:24** He answered Him in the way of his strength, "Declare to me the fewness of my days.

**101:25** Do not take me away in the half of my days; Your years are throughout all generations.

**101:26** At the beginning You, O Lord, founded the earth, and the heavens are the works of Your hands;

**101:27** they shall perish, while You endure continually; and all of them shall grow old like a garment, and like a mantle You shall roll them up, and they shall be changed;

**101:28** yet You are the very same, and Your years shall not fail.

**101:29** The sons of Your servants shall have their dwelling, and their seed shall be directed aright forever."

## Translator Notes

- **101:9** — The Greek reads "those who praised me" (hoi epainountes me), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "those who deride/rage against me."
- **101:24** — The Greek reads "He answered Him in the way of his strength," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb ("answered" rather than "afflicted/weakened") and introducing the closing appeal to God as direct speech.
- **101:27** — The Greek reads "You shall roll them up" (helixeis), a reading also reflected in the New Testament quotation of this passage at Hebrews 1:12.
