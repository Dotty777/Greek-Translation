## Text

**144:1** A Praise of David. I will exalt You, my God, my King, and I will bless Your name forever and ever.

**144:2** Every day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name.

**144:3** Great is the Lord and exceedingly to be praised, and to His greatness there is no end.

**144:4** Generation after generation shall praise Your works, and they shall proclaim Your power;

**144:5** and they shall speak of the majesty of the glory of Your holiness, and they shall recount Your wondrous works;

**144:6** and they shall tell of the power of Your awesome deeds, and as for Your greatness, I will recount it; and they shall speak of Your might.

**144:7** They shall pour forth the remembrance of the abundance of Your goodness, and in Your righteousness they shall exult.

**144:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord, longsuffering and abounding in mercy.

**144:9** Good is the Lord to those who wait patiently, and His tender mercies are over all His works.

**144:10** Let all Your works give thanks to You, O Lord, and let Your devout ones bless You.

**144:11** They shall tell of the glory of Your kingdom, and they shall speak of Your might,

**144:12** to make known to the sons of men Your might, and the glory of the majesty of Your kingdom.

**144:13** Your kingdom is a kingdom of all the ages, and Your dominion is through every generation and generation. Faithful is the Lord in His words, and holy in all His works.

**144:14** The Lord upholds all who are falling, and lifts up all who are cast down.

**144:15** The eyes of all hope in You, and You give them their food in due season;

**144:16** You open Your hands, and fill every living creature with goodwill.

**144:17** Righteous is the Lord in all His ways, and holy in all His works.

**144:18** Near is the Lord to all who call upon Him, to all who call upon Him in truth.

**144:19** He will grant the desire of those who fear Him, and He will hear their supplication and save them.

**144:20** The Lord guards all who love Him, and all the sinners He will utterly destroy.

**144:21** The praise of the Lord my mouth shall speak, and let all flesh bless His holy name forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **144:9** — The Greek critical text reads "to those who wait patiently" (or "endure"), whereas some Septuagint witnesses and the Hebrew Masoretic Text read "to all."
- **144:13** — The second sentence ("Faithful is the Lord in His words, and holy in all His works") supplies the missing nun-line of the Hebrew alphabetical acrostic, preserved in the Septuagint and Dead Sea Scrolls but absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **144:16** — The Greek reads plural "Your hands" and "with goodwill" (or "good pleasure"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Your hand" and "the desire of every living thing."
