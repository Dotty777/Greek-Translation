## Text

**68:1** For the end, concerning those who shall be changed; of David.

**68:2** Save me, O God, for the waters have entered even to my soul.

**68:3** I have sunk fast into the mire of the deep, and there is no foothold; I have come into the depths of the sea, and a tempest has submerged me.

**68:4** I have grown weary with crying out, my throat has become hoarse; my eyes have failed from drawing near to my God.

**68:5** Those who hate me without cause have multiplied beyond the hairs of my head; my enemies who persecute me unjustly have grown strong; what I did not seize, then I repaid.

**68:6** O God, You know my foolishness, and my trespasses have not been hidden from You.

**68:7** Do not let those who wait upon You be put to shame because of me, O Lord of hosts; do not let those who seek You be disgraced because of me, O God of Israel.

**68:8** For because of You I have borne reproach; dishonor has covered my face.

**68:9** I have become estranged from my brethren, and a stranger to the sons of my mother;

**68:10** for the zeal of Your house shall consume me, and the reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen upon me.

**68:11** And I bowed down my soul with fasting, and it became a reproach to me;

**68:12** and I made sackcloth my garment, and I became a proverb to them.

**68:13** Those who sit in the gate spoke against me, and those who drink wine sang songs against me.

**68:14** But as for me, my prayer is to You, O Lord; it is a time of favor, O God; in the multitude of Your mercy give ear, in the truth of Your salvation.

**68:15** Save me from the mire, lest I sink fast; may I be delivered from those who hate me, and out of the depth of the waters.

**68:16** Do not let a tempest of water submerge me, nor let the deep swallow me up, nor let the pit shut its mouth over me.

**68:17** Hear me, O Lord, for Your mercy is good; according to the multitude of Your compassions look upon me.

**68:18** For I am in distress; quickly hear me.

**68:19** Attend to my soul and redeem it; because of my enemies, deliver me.

**68:20** For You know my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonor; all those who afflict me are before You.

**68:21** My soul expected reproach and misery; and I waited for one to grieve with me, and there was none, and for a comforter, and I found none.

**68:22** And they put gall into my food, and for my thirst they made me drink vinegar.

**68:23** Let their table before them become a snare, and a recompense, and a stumbling block;

**68:24** let their eyes be darkened so that they do not see, and bow down their back continually.

**68:25** Pour out Your wrath upon them, and may the fierceness of Your anger overtake them.

**68:26** Let their habitation become desolate, and let no one dwell in their tents;

**68:27** for they persecuted him whom You struck, and they added to the pain of my wounds.

**68:28** Add iniquity to their iniquity, and do not let them enter into Your righteousness;

**68:29** let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and do not let them be written with the righteous.

**68:30** I am poor and in pain, and the salvation of Your countenance has upheld me.

**68:31** I will praise the name of God with a song; I will magnify Him with praise,

**68:32** and it shall please God more than a young calf bringing forth horns and hooves.

**68:33** Let the poor see and be glad; seek God earnestly, and you shall live.

**68:34** For the Lord has heard the needy, and He has not despised His prisoners.

**68:35** Let the heavens and the earth praise Him, the sea and all things that move in them.

**68:36** For God will save Zion, and the cities of Judea shall be built, and they shall dwell there and inherit it;

**68:37** and the seed of His servants shall possess it, and those who love His name shall dwell in it.

## Translator Notes

- **68:1** — The Septuagint superscription reads "concerning those who shall be changed," rendering the Hebrew musical term usually translated "according to Lilies."
- **68:4** — The Greek text reads "from drawing near to my God" (eggizein), a notable textual variant alongside the more widespread Septuagint reading "from hoping in my God" (elpizein).
- **68:10** — The Greek text uses the future tense "shall consume me," matching the quotation in John 2:17, rather than the past tense found in the Hebrew and some Septuagint witnesses.
- **68:18** — The Greek text preserves only this brief petition, omitting the preceding clause ("do not turn away Your face from Your servant") found in the Hebrew and other Septuagint manuscripts.
- **68:21** — The Septuagint reads "my soul expected reproach and misery," taking the Hebrew verb usually rendered "reproach has broken my heart" in the sense of waiting or expecting.
