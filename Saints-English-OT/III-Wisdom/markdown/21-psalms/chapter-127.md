## Text

**127:1** A Song of Ascents. Blessed are all who fear the Lord, who walk in His ways.

**127:2** You shall eat the fruits of your labors; you are blessed, and it shall be well with you.

**127:3** Your wife shall be like a flourishing vine along the sides of your house; your sons like newly planted olive shoots around your table.

**127:4** Behold, so shall the man who fears the Lord be blessed.

**127:5** May the Lord bless you from Zion, and may you see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of your life,

**127:6** and may you see the sons of your sons. Peace upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **127:2** — Greek tous karpous ton ponon sou ("the fruits of your labors") corresponds to "the labor of your hands" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **127:3** — Greek en tois klitesi ("along the sides," indicating the inner sides or walls of a house) and neophyta ("newly planted shoots") render the domestic imagery literally.
- **127:5** — Greek uses optative verbs ("May the Lord bless... may you see," continuing into verse 6) for a formal priestly blessing and renders Jerusalem's prosperity with the plural ta agatha ("the good things").
