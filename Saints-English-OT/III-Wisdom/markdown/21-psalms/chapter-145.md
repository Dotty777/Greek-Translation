## Text

**145:1** Hallelujah. Of Haggai and Zechariah. Praise the Lord, O my soul.

**145:2** I will praise the Lord in my life; I will sing praises to my God as long as I exist.

**145:3** Do not trust in rulers, nor in sons of men, in whom there is no salvation.

**145:4** His spirit shall go forth, and he shall return to his earth; in that day all their thoughts shall perish.

**145:5** Blessed is he whose helper is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the Lord his God,

**145:6** who made the heaven and the earth, the sea and all things that are in them; who guards truth forever,

**145:7** who executes justice for those who are wronged, who gives food to the hungry. The Lord looses those who are fettered.

**145:8** The Lord lifts up those who are cast down; the Lord makes the blind wise; the Lord loves the righteous.

**145:9** The Lord guards the sojourners; He will sustain the orphan and the widow, and the way of sinners He will blot out.

**145:10** The Lord shall reign forever, your God, O Zion, from generation to generation.

## Translator Notes

- **145:1** — The attribution to the post-exilic prophets Haggai and Zechariah is distinctive to the Septuagint tradition and does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **145:8** — The Septuagint places "lifts up those who are cast down" first and reads "makes the blind wise," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places "opens the eyes of the blind" first.
- **145:9** — The Greek word rendered "sojourners" (proselytous) denotes resident aliens or proselytes living among Israel.
