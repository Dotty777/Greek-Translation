## Text

**12:2** For the end; a psalm of David. How long, O Lord, will You forget me to the end? How long will You turn Your face away from me?

**12:3** How long shall I lay up counsels in my soul, sorrows in my heart by day? How long shall my enemy be exalted over me?

**12:4** Look, hear me, O Lord my God; enlighten my eyes, lest I ever fall asleep in death,

**12:5** lest my enemy ever say, "I have prevailed against him"; those who afflict me will exult if I am shaken. But I have hoped in Your mercy; my heart shall exult in Your salvation;

**12:6** I will sing to the Lord, who has done good to me, and I will sing psalms to the name of the Lord Most High.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**12:4** *(after `12:4`)* lest my enemy ever say, "I have prevailed against him"; those who afflict me will exult if I am shaken.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — In this Greek versification, the superscription and opening questions appear together as verse two, and the Greek asks how long the Lord will "turn Your face away" (apostrepseis) rather than "hide" it.
- **12:3** — The Greek text reads simply "by day" (hemeras), without the expansion "and by night" found in some later Greek witnesses.
- **12:6** — The closing clause, "and I will sing psalms to the name of the Lord Most High," is a distinctive Septuagint addition not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **12:4 *(additional)*** — Matches the opening clauses of 12:5 in the site numbering (reading optative eipoi in this witness).
