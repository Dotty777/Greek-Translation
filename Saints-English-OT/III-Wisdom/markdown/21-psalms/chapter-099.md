## Text

**99:1** A Psalm for thanksgiving. Raise a joyful shout to the Lord, all the earth.

**99:2** Serve the Lord with gladness; enter into His presence with exultation.

**99:3** Know that the Lord—He is God; He made us, and not we ourselves, His people and the sheep of His pasture.

**99:4** Enter His gates with thanksgiving, His courts with hymns; give thanks to Him, praise His name.

**99:5** For the Lord is good; His mercy is forever, and His truth endures to generation and generation.

## Translator Notes

- **99:2** — The Greek agalliasis means "exultation" or "exceeding joy," where the Masoretic Hebrew (Psalm 100:2) has a term often rendered "singing."
- **99:3** — The Greek "and not we ourselves" (kai ouch hemeis) corresponds to the written Hebrew (ketib) rather than the marginal reading (qere) "and we are His."
- **99:4** — The Greek reads "with hymns" (en hymnois) and "praise" (aineite) His name, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew has "with praise" and "bless" His name.
