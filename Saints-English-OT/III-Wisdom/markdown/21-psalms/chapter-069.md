# Psalm 69 — Saints Traditional English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**69:1** For the end; of David, for a remembrance, that the Lord may save me.

**69:2** O God, attend to my help.

**69:3** May those who seek my soul be ashamed and disgraced; may those who desire evil for me be turned back and put to shame;

**69:4** may those who say to me, "Aha, aha!" be turned back immediately in shame.

**69:5** Let all those who seek You exult and be glad in You; and let those who love Your salvation say continually, "Let God be magnified!"

**69:6** But I am poor and needy; O God, help me. You are my helper and my deliverer; O Lord, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **69:1** — The Septuagint expands the superscription with the clause "that the Lord may save me," which is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **69:2** — The Greek text consists solely of the opening plea, omitting the second clause ("O Lord, make haste to help me") found in the Hebrew and other Septuagint witnesses.
