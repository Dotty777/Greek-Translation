## Text

**102:1** Of David. O my soul, bless the Lord, and all my inward parts, His holy name.

**102:2** O my soul, bless the Lord, and do not forget all His praises;

**102:3** who pardons all your iniquities, who heals all your sicknesses;

**102:4** who redeems your life from corruption, who crowns you with mercy and tender compassions,

**102:5** who fills your desire with good things; your youth shall be renewed like that of an eagle.

**102:6** The Lord performs deeds of mercy and judgment for all who suffer wrong.

**102:7** He made known His ways to Moses, to the sons of Israel His will.

**102:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord, longsuffering and rich in mercy.

**102:9** He will not remain angry to the end, nor will He hold His wrath forever;

**102:10** Not according to our sins has He done to us, nor according to our iniquities has He repaid us.

**102:11** For according to the height of heaven above the earth, the Lord has made His mercy mighty over those who fear Him;

**102:12** as far as the east stands distant from the west, He has put our iniquities far from us.

**102:13** And just as a father shows compassion to his sons, the Lord has shown compassion to those who fear Him,

**102:14** for He Himself knows our frame; remember that we are dust.

**102:15** Man—his days are like grass; like a flower of the field, thus shall he bloom.

**102:16** For a wind passes through him, and he shall be no more, and he shall no longer recognize his place.

**102:17** Yet the mercy of the Lord is from everlasting even to everlasting upon those who fear Him, and His righteousness upon sons of sons,

**102:18** to those who keep His covenant, and to those who are mindful of His commandments to perform them.

**102:19** The Lord has prepared His throne in heaven, and His kingdom holds dominion over all.

**102:20** Bless the Lord, all His angels, mighty in strength, who carry out His word, to heed the voice of His words.

**102:21** Bless the Lord, all His hosts, His ministers who perform His will.

**102:22** Bless the Lord, all His works, in every place of His dominion; O my soul, bless the Lord.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**102:29** *(after `102:22`)* For David.

## Translator Notes

- **102:2** — The Greek text here reads "all His praises" (pasas tas aineseis autou), whereas other Septuagint manuscripts and the Hebrew Masoretic Text read "all His benefits/recompenses."
- **102:14** — The Greek shifts to an imperative addressed directly to God, "remember that we are dust" (mnestheti), in contrast to the third-person indicative ("He remembers") in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **102:16** — The Greek makes the man the subject and "his place" (ton topon autou) the direct object ("he shall no longer recognize his place"), reversing the subject and object of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("its place knows it no more").
