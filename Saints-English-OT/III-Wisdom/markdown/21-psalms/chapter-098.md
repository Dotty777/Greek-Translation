## Text

**98:1** A Psalm of David. The Lord reigns; let the peoples be enraged. He who sits upon the cherubim—let the earth be shaken.

**98:2** Great is the Lord in Zion, and He is lofty above all the peoples.

**98:3** Let all give thanks to Your great name, for it is awesome and holy.

**98:4** And the honor of a king loves judgment; You have prepared uprightness; You have performed judgment and righteousness in Jacob.

**98:5** Exalt the Lord our God, and bow down to the footstool of His feet.

**98:6** He is holy. Moses and Aaron were among His priests, and Samuel was among those who call upon His name; they called upon the Lord, and He Himself heard them.

**98:7** In a pillar of cloud He spoke to them; they kept His testimonies, and the ordinances that He gave them.

**98:8** O Lord our God, You heard them; O God, You were merciful to them, yet taking vengeance upon all their practices.

**98:9** Exalt the Lord our God, and bow down toward His holy mountain, for holy is the Lord our God.

## Translator Notes

- **98:1** — The Greek superscription "A Psalm of David" is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew (Psalm 99). The verb rendered "be enraged" (orgizesthosan) reflects the Septuagint reading, whereas the Hebrew is commonly translated "tremble."
- **98:4** — The Greek reads "honor" (time) of a king rather than the Masoretic "strength," and "uprightness" renders the plural Greek noun euthytetas.
- **98:6** — In this Greek text, "He is holy" (hagios estin) stands at the beginning of verse 6 without "for," concluding the liturgical refrain of verse 5 (compare verses 3 and 9); other Greek witnesses attach "for He is holy" to the end of verse 5.
- **98:8** — The Greek reads "all their practices" (panta ta epitedeumata auton), including "all," which is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew.
