## Text

**60:1** For the end, in hymns; of David.

**60:2** Hear my supplication; give heed to my prayer.

**60:3** From the ends of the earth I cried out to You when my heart grew faint; upon a rock You lifted me up, You guided me,

**60:4** for You have become my hope, a tower of strength from before the enemy.

**60:5** I will sojourn in Your tabernacle forever. Interlude. I will be sheltered in the shelter of Your wings.

**60:6** For You, O God, have heard my prayers; You have given an inheritance to those who fear Your name.

**60:7** You will add days to the days of the king, his years until the day of generation and generation.

**60:8** He shall abide forever before God; His mercy and truth—who will seek them out?

**60:9** So I will sing praise to Your name forever and ever, that I may pay my vows day by day.

## Translator Notes

- **60:2** — The Greek text opens the petition directly without the vocative "O God."
- **60:3** — The Greek uses past-tense indicatives ("You lifted me up, You guided me") rather than the imperative prayer found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **60:8** — The Greek frames the second clause as a rhetorical question ("who will seek them out?"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads an imperative ("appoint mercy and truth").
