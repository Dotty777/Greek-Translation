## Text

**115:1** Hallelujah. I believed, therefore I spoke; yet I was brought exceedingly low.

**115:2** I said in my dismay, "Every man is a liar."

**115:3** What shall I render to the Lord for what He has rendered to me?

**115:4** I will receive the cup of salvation, and I will call upon the name of the Lord.

**115:6** Precious before the Lord is the death of His holy ones.

**115:7** O Lord, I am Your servant; I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant; You have burst my bonds.

**115:8** To You I will offer a sacrifice of praise;

**115:9** my vows I will pay to the Lord before all His people,

**115:10** in the courts of the house of the Lord, in your midst, O Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **115:2** — Greek ekstasis denotes sudden alarm, bewilderment, or dismay, here translated "dismay."
- **115:3** — This Greek text reads peri hon ("for what") without the word "all" found in some Septuagint manuscripts.
- **115:6** — Verse 115:5 (which repeats the vow clause of 115:9 in some witnesses) is absent from this Greek text, following major Septuagint manuscripts.
- **115:8** — This Greek text concludes after "a sacrifice of praise," omitting the additional clause ("and I will call upon the name of the Lord") present in other witnesses.
