## Text

**36:1** Of David. Do not be stirred to envy over those who act wickedly, nor be jealous of those who practice lawlessness;

**36:2** for like grass they shall swiftly dry up, and like green herbs they shall swiftly fall away.

**36:3** Hope in the Lord and practice goodness, and dwell in the land, and you shall be shepherded upon its wealth.

**36:4** Take delight in the Lord, and He shall give you the petitions of your heart.

**36:5** Reveal your way to the Lord and hope in Him, and He shall act;

**36:6** and He shall bring forth your righteousness as light, and your judgment as the noonday.

**36:7** Submit to the Lord and implore Him; do not be stirred to envy over the one who prospers in his life, over a man who commits transgressions.

**36:8** Cease from anger and forsake wrath; do not be stirred to envy so as to act wickedly.

**36:9** For those who act wickedly shall be utterly destroyed, but those who wait upon the Lord—they shall inherit the earth.

**36:10** And yet a little while, and a sinner shall surely be no more; and you shall search for his place, and you shall by no means find it.

**36:11** But the meek shall inherit the earth, and they shall take delight in a multitude of peace.

**36:12** The sinner shall watch closely for the righteous one, and he shall gnash his teeth against him;

**36:13** but the Lord shall laugh him to scorn, for He foresees that his day shall come.

**36:14** Sinners have drawn the sword, they have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, to slay the upright in heart;

**36:15** may their sword enter into their own heart, and may their bows be shattered.

**36:16** Better is a little for the righteous one than the great wealth of sinners.

**36:17** For the arms of sinners shall be shattered, but the Lord upholds the righteous.

**36:18** The Lord knows the ways of the blameless, and their inheritance shall be forever;

**36:19** they shall not be put to shame in an evil time, and in days of famine they shall be filled.

**36:20** For sinners shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord, at the very moment they are glorified and exalted, vanishing like smoke, have utterly vanished.

**36:21** The sinner borrows and shall not repay, but the righteous one shows compassion and gives.

**36:22** For those who bless Him shall inherit the earth, but those who curse Him shall be utterly destroyed.

**36:23** By the Lord the steps of a man are directed aright, and He shall take pleasure in his way;

**36:24** when he falls, he shall not be dashed down, for the Lord steadies his hand.

**36:25** I was young, and indeed I have grown old, and I have not seen a righteous one forsaken, nor his seed seeking bread.

**36:26** All the day he shows mercy and lends, and his seed shall be for a blessing.

**36:27** Turn aside from evil and do good, and dwell forever and ever.

**36:28** For the Lord loves justice, and He shall not forsake His holy ones; they shall be guarded forever; the blameless shall be avenged, and the seed of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.

**36:29** But the righteous shall inherit the earth, and they shall dwell upon it forever and ever.

**36:30** The mouth of a righteous one shall meditate on wisdom, and his tongue shall speak justice;

**36:31** the law of his God is in his heart, and his steps shall not slip.

**36:32** The sinner keeps watch over the righteous one, and seeks to put him to death;

**36:33** but the Lord shall by no means abandon him into his hands, nor shall He condemn him when he is judged against him.

**36:34** Wait patiently for the Lord and keep His way, and He shall exalt you to inherit the earth; when sinners are utterly destroyed, you shall see it.

**36:35** I saw an ungodly man exceedingly exalted, and lifting himself up like the cedars of Lebanon;

**36:36** and I passed by, and behold, he was no more; and I sought him, and his place was not found.

**36:37** Guard innocence and behold uprightness, for there is a remnant for a man of peace.

**36:38** But transgressors shall be utterly destroyed all together; the remnants of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.

**36:39** Yet the salvation of the righteous is from the Lord, and He is their defender in a time of affliction;

**36:40** and the Lord shall help them and deliver them; He shall rescue them from sinners and save them, because they have hoped in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:3** — The Septuagint reads a passive verb ("you shall be shepherded") and "upon its wealth," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "feed on faithfulness."
- **36:20** — The Septuagint renders this clause as "at the very moment they are glorified and exalted," differing from the Masoretic Text's "like the splendor of the pastures."
- **36:22** — The Greek uses active participles ("those who bless Him ... those who curse Him," referring to the Lord, though the pronoun could also refer to the righteous one of verse 21), whereas the Masoretic Text has passives ("those blessed/cursed by Him").
- **36:28** — The clause "the blameless shall be avenged" in the Greek critical text corresponds to the missing ayin-line of the Hebrew acrostic; some Greek manuscripts instead read "the lawless shall be driven out."
- **36:35** — The Septuagint specifies "like the cedars of Lebanon," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "like a native green tree."
