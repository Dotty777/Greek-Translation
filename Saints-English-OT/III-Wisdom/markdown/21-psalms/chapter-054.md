## Text

**54:1** For the end, in hymns; of understanding, for David.

**54:2** Give ear, O God, to my prayer, and do not overlook my supplication;

**54:3** attend to me and hear me. I was grieved in my meditation, and I was troubled

**54:4** at the voice of an enemy and at the affliction of a sinner; for they turned iniquity upon me, and in wrath they harbored malice against me.

**54:5** My heart was troubled within me, and the dread of death fell upon me.

**54:6** Fear and trembling came upon me, and darkness covered me.

**54:7** And I said, "Who will give me wings like a dove, and I will fly away and find rest?"

**54:8** Behold, I went far off in flight, and I lodged in the wilderness. Pause.

**54:9** I awaited Him who saves me from faintheartedness and tempest.

**54:10** Submerge, O Lord, and divide their tongues, for I have seen lawlessness and contention in the city.

**54:11** Day and night it shall encircle her upon her walls; iniquity and toil are in her midst

**54:12** and unrighteousness; and trouble and deceit have not ceased from her streets.

**54:13** For if an enemy had reproached me, I would have borne it; and if he who hates had spoken boastfully against me, I would have hidden myself from him.

**54:14** But it was you, a man of like soul, my guide and my familiar friend,

**54:15** who together sweetened meals; in the house of God we walked in concord.

**54:16** Let death come upon them, and let them go down into Hades alive; for evils are in their dwellings, in their midst.

**54:17** As for me, I cried out to God, and the Lord heard me.

**54:18** At evening and morning and noonday I will declare; I will proclaim, and He shall hear my voice.

**54:19** He shall redeem my soul in peace from those who draw near to me, for among many they were with me.

**54:20** God shall hear and humble them, He who exists before the ages. Pause.

**54:21** For there is no exchange for them, and they did not fear God. He stretched forth His hand in recompense; they profaned His covenant.

**54:22** They were divided from the wrath of His countenance, and his heart drew near; his words were softer than oil, yet they are darts.

**54:23** Cast your care upon the Lord, and He Himself shall nourish you; He shall never give wavering to the righteous.

**54:24** But You, O God, shall bring them down into the pit of corruption; men of blood and deceit shall by no means live out half their days; but as for me, I will hope in You, O Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:9** — Following the Greek text, this verse reads "I awaited Him who saves me from faintheartedness and tempest," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest."
- **54:15** — The Greek "edesmata" ("meals" or "foods") reflects an understanding of shared table fellowship, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "sweet counsel."
- **54:19** — "For among many they were with me" preserves the literal Greek phrasing ("en pollois esan syn emoi"), denoting numerous adversaries contending with the psalmist.
- **54:21** — In the Greek text, God stretches forth His hand in recompense ("en to apodidonai") because the wicked profaned His covenant (plural), and the pronoun referent shifts back to the treacherous companion in the second half of 54:22 ("his heart," "his words").
