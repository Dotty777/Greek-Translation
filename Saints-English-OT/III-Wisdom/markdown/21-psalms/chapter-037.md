## Text

**37:1** A Psalm of David; for a remembrance concerning the Sabbath.

**37:2** O Lord, do not reprove me in Your wrath, nor chasten me in Your anger.

**37:3** For Your arrows have pierced into me, and You have laid Your hand heavily upon me;

**37:4** and there is no healing in my flesh because of Your anger; there is no peace in my bones because of my sins.

**37:5** For my iniquities have risen above my head; like a heavy burden they have weighed heavily upon me.

**37:6** My wounds have grown foul and festered because of my foolishness;

**37:7** I have been afflicted with misery and bowed down to the uttermost; all the day I walked about with a downcast countenance;

**37:8** for my soul has been filled with mockeries, and there is no healing in my flesh.

**37:9** I have been afflicted and humbled exceedingly; I roared aloud from the groaning of my heart.

**37:10** And before You is all my desire, and my groaning has not been hidden from You.

**37:11** My heart has been troubled, my strength has forsaken me, and the light of my eyes is not with me.

**37:12** My friends and my neighbors drew near opposite me and stood, and those nearest to me stood afar off;

**37:13** and those who seek my soul used violence, and those who seek evil against me spoke vanities, and all the day they meditated deceits.

**37:14** Yet I, like a deaf man, did not hear, and like a mute man who does not open his mouth;

**37:15** and I became like a man who does not hear, and who has no reproofs in his mouth.

**37:16** For in You I have hoped, O Lord; You shall hear, O Lord my God.

**37:17** For I said, "Let my enemies never gloat over me"; and when my feet were shaken, they spoke boastfully against me.

**37:18** For I am ready for scourges, and my pain is continually before me.

**37:19** For I shall declare my iniquity, and I shall be anxious over my sin.

**37:20** Yet my enemies live and have grown stronger than I, and those who hate me unjustly have been multiplied;

**37:21** those who repay evil for good slandered me, because I pursued righteousness.

**37:22** Do not forsake me, O Lord; O my God, do not depart from me;

**37:23** attend to my help, O Lord of my salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The liturgical phrase "concerning the Sabbath" is distinctive to the Septuagint superscription and is absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **37:8** — The Greek critical text reads "my soul has been filled with mockeries" (some manuscripts read "loins" instead of "soul"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "my loins are filled with burning."
- **37:18** — The Septuagint renders the noun as "scourges," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "ready to stumble" or "halting."
