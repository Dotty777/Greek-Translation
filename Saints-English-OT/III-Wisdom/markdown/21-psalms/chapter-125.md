## Text

**125:1** A Song of Ascents. When the Lord turned back the captivity of Zion, we became like those who have been comforted.

**125:2** Then our mouth was filled with joy, and our tongue with exultation; then they shall say among the nations, "The Lord has done great deeds with them."

**125:3** The Lord has done great deeds with us; we became glad.

**125:4** Turn back our captivity, O Lord, like the torrent in the south.

**125:5** Those who sow in tears shall reap in exultation.

**125:6** Going forth, they went on and wept, bearing their seeds; yet coming, they shall indeed come in exultation, bearing their sheaves.

## Translator Notes

- **125:1** — Greek parakeklemenoi ("who have been comforted") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("who dream"), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants.
- **125:2** — Greek charas ("joy") corresponds to "laughter" in the Hebrew text, and the future verb erousin ("they shall say") casts the nations' confession forward in time.
- **125:4** — Greek uses the singular ho cheimarrous ("the torrent," a winter-flowing stream) and renders the Hebrew Negev as to noto ("the south").
- **125:6** — Greek employs plural verbs and participles throughout rather than the Hebrew singular, and pairs participles with finite verbs to mirror the emphatic Hebrew idiom.
