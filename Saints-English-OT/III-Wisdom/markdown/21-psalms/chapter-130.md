## Text

**130:1** A Song of Ascents. O Lord, my heart has not been lifted up, nor have my eyes been raised on high; nor have I walked in great matters, nor in wondrous things beyond me,

**130:2** but rather I was humble-minded. And I lifted up my soul, like a weaned child toward its mother, until You recompense my soul.

**130:3** Let Israel hope in the Lord from now and forevermore.

## Translator Notes

- **130:1** — This Greek text omits the attribution "of David" found in the superscription of the Hebrew tradition and some manuscripts.
- **130:2** — This Greek text differs notably from both the Hebrew and some Septuagint manuscripts, punctuating after "humble-minded" (literally "if I was not humble-minded," functioning idiomatically as an adversative affirmation after the preceding negatives) and reading "and I lifted up my soul" and "until You recompense my soul."
