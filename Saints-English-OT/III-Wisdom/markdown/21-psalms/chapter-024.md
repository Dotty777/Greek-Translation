## Text

**24:1** A psalm of David. To You, O Lord, I have lifted up my soul,

**24:2** O my God; in You I have trusted; may I not be put to shame, nor let my enemies laugh at me.

**24:3** For indeed, all who wait patiently for You shall by no means be put to shame; let those be ashamed who practice lawlessness in vain.

**24:4** Make Your ways known to me, O Lord, and teach me Your paths.

**24:5** Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are God my Savior, and for You I have waited all the day long.

**24:6** Remember Your compassions and Your mercies, for they are from everlasting.

**24:7** Do not remember the sins of my youth and of ignorance; according to Your mercy remember me, for the sake of Your goodness, O Lord.

**24:8** Good and upright is the Lord; therefore He will give law to sinners in the way.

**24:9** He will guide the meek in judgment; He will teach the meek His ways.

**24:10** All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth to those who diligently seek His covenant and His testimonies.

**24:11** For the sake of Your name, O Lord, You shall also pardon my sin, for it is great.

**24:12** Who is the man who fears the Lord? He will give him law in the way that he has chosen.

**24:13** His soul shall abide among good things, and his seed shall inherit the land.

**24:14** The Lord is the stronghold of those who fear Him, and the name of the Lord belongs to those who fear Him, and His covenant is to make it manifest to them.

**24:15** My eyes are continually toward the Lord, for He Himself shall pluck my feet out of the snare.

**24:16** Look upon me and have mercy on me, for I am solitary and poor.

**24:17** The afflictions of my heart have been multiplied; bring me out of my distresses.

**24:18** Behold my lowliness and my toil, and forgive all my sins.

**24:19** Behold my enemies, for they have been multiplied, and with an unjust hatred they have hated me.

**24:20** Guard my soul and deliver me; may I not be put to shame, for I have hoped in You.

**24:21** The innocent and the upright clung to me, for I waited patiently for You, O Lord.

**24:22** Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his afflictions.

## Translator Notes

- **24:8** — Greek νομοθετησει ("will give law to," also in v. 12) interprets the Hebrew verb for "instruct" specifically in terms of lawgiving.
- **24:14** — Greek renders Hebrew "counsel" as κραταιωμα ("stronghold") and includes the additional clause "and the name of the Lord belongs to those who fear Him."
- **24:21** — Greek has personal plural subjects "The innocent and the upright clung to me" (ακακοι και ευθεις εκολλωντο μοι) and adds "O Lord," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads abstract nouns "integrity and uprightness."
