## Text

**71:1** For Solomon. O God, give Your judgment to the king, and Your righteousness to the son of the king,

**71:2** to judge Your people in righteousness, and Your poor in judgment.

**71:3** Let the mountains lift up peace for Your people, and the hills.

**71:4** In righteousness he will judge the poor of the people; and You will save the sons of the needy, and he will humble the slanderer.

**71:5** And he shall endure with the sun, and before the moon, through generations of generations.

**71:6** And he shall come down like rain upon a fleece, and like drops dripping upon the earth.

**71:7** In his days righteousness shall spring forth, and abundance of peace until the moon is taken away.

**71:8** And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the inhabited world.

**71:9** Before him the Ethiopians shall fall down, and his enemies shall lick the dust.

**71:10** The kings of Tarshish and the islands shall offer gifts; the kings of the Arabs and of Sheba shall bring gifts.

**71:11** And all the kings shall bow down to him; all the nations shall serve him.

**71:12** For he delivered the poor from the hand of the mighty, and the needy who had no helper.

**71:13** He will spare the poor and the needy, and he will save the souls of the needy.

**71:14** From usury and from unrighteousness he will redeem their souls, and honorable shall their name be before him.

**71:15** And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Arabia; and they shall pray for him continually; all the day they shall bless him.

**71:16** There shall be a firm support in the earth upon the tops of the mountains; its fruit shall be lifted high above Lebanon, and they shall flourish out of the city like the grass of the earth.

**71:17** Let his name be blessed forever; before the sun his name shall abide; and in him all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed; all the nations shall call him blessed.

**71:18** Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who alone does wondrous things,

**71:19** and blessed be the name of His glory forever, and forever and ever; and all the earth shall be filled with His glory. So be it, so be it.

**71:20** The hymns of David, the son of Jesse, have come to an end.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

## Translator Notes

- **71:4** — The Greek places "In righteousness" at the beginning of verse 4 and shifts briefly to the second person ("You will save") between third-person verbs referring to the king.
- **71:9** — The Greek reads "Ethiopians" (Aithiopes) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "desert dwellers."
- **71:14** — The Greek reads "usury" (tokou) and "their name" (to onoma auton) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "oppression" and "their blood."
- **71:20** — The Greek reads "the hymns" (hoi hymnoi) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the prayers."
