## Text

**138:1** For the end; a psalm of David. O Lord, You have examined me and known me;

**138:2** You know my sitting down and my rising up; You perceive all my thoughts from afar;

**138:3** You have traced out my path and my measuring line, and You have foreseen all my ways,

**138:4** for there is no unrighteous word on my tongue; behold, O Lord, You have known all things,

**138:5** the last things and the ancient things; You fashioned me and laid Your hand upon me.

**138:6** Your knowledge has become too wondrous for me; it has grown mighty; I cannot attain to it.

**138:7** Where shall I go from Your Spirit? And from Your presence where shall I flee?

**138:8** If I ascend into heaven, You are there; if I descend into Hades, You are present;

**138:9** if I take up my wings straight ahead and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea,

**138:10** for even there Your hand shall guide me, and Your right hand shall hold me fast.

**138:11** And I said, "Surely darkness shall trample me down, and night shall be illumination in my delight."

**138:12** As its darkness is, so also is its light.

**138:13** For You possessed my inward parts, O Lord; You upheld me from my mother's womb.

**138:14** I will give thanks to You, for You have been fearfully made wondrous; marvelous are Your works, and my soul knows full well.

**138:15** My bone was not hidden from You, which You made in secret, nor my substance in the lowest parts of the earth;

**138:16** My eyes saw Your unwrought work, and in Your book all shall be written; day by day they shall be fashioned, and there is not one among them.

**138:17** But to me Your friends are exceedingly honored, O God; their dominions have grown exceedingly mighty;

**138:18** I will count them, and they shall be multiplied more than the sand; I awoke, and I am still with You.

**138:19** If only You would slay sinners, O God! Men of blood, depart from me;

**138:20** for You will say concerning reasoning, "They shall take Your cities in vain."

**138:21** Have I not hated those who hate You, O Lord, and wasted away over Your enemies?

**138:22** With a perfect hatred I hated them; they became enemies to me.

**138:23** Examine me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my paths;

**138:24** and see if You have seen iniquities in me, and guide me in the everlasting way.

## Translator Notes

- **138:9** — This Greek text reads kat' orthon ("straight ahead"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read kat' orthron ("at dawn").
- **138:12** — This Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus) preserves the shorter reading, omitting the clauses "for darkness shall not be darkened from You, and night shall be illuminated as day" found in other Greek manuscripts and the Masoretic Text.
- **138:16** — This Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus) reads "My eyes saw Your unwrought work," reversing the pronouns found in other Greek manuscripts and the Masoretic Text ("Your eyes saw my unformed substance").
- **138:20** — The Greek reflects a notably different reading of the underlying Hebrew consonants, yielding "You will say concerning reasoning, 'They shall take Your cities in vain.'"
- **138:24** — This Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus) reads ei ides anomias ("if You have seen iniquities"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read ei hodos anomias ("if there is a way of iniquity").
