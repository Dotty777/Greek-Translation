# Psalm 128 — Saints Traditional English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**128:1** A Song of Ascents. "Many times they have warred against me from my youth," let Israel now say—

**128:2** "Many times they have warred against me from my youth, yet indeed they could not prevail against me."

**128:3** Upon my back the sinners hammered; they prolonged their iniquity.

**128:4** The righteous Lord has severed the neck of sinners.

**128:5** Let them be put to shame and turned backward, all who hate Zion.

**128:6** Let them become like grass of the housetops, which withers before it is plucked up,

**128:7** with which the harvester does not fill his hand, nor he who gathers the sheaves his bosom;

**128:8** and those who pass by do not say, "The blessing of the Lord be upon us; we will bless you in the name of the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **128:3** — The Greek reads "the sinners hammered" (or "worked as craftsmen") and "prolonged their iniquity," differing from the Hebrew tradition's "the plowers plowed" and "made long their furrows."
- **128:4** — The Greek reads "the neck of sinners," whereas the Hebrew tradition reads "the cords of the wicked."
- **128:8** — This Greek text reads "upon us" and the future "we will bless you," rather than "upon you" and "we bless you."
