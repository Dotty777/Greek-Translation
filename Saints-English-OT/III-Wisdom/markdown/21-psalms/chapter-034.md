## Text

**34:1** Of David. Judge, O Lord, those who wrong me; wage war against those who make war against me.

**34:2** Take hold of armor and shield, and rise up to my help.

**34:3** Pour out a sword and shut the way against those who pursue me; say to my soul, "I am your salvation."

**34:4** Let those who seek my soul be shamed and confounded; let those who devise evil against me be turned back and put to utter shame.

**34:5** Let them become like dust before the face of the wind, and let the angel of the Lord press them hard.

**34:6** Let their way become darkness and slipperiness, and let the angel of the Lord pursue them.

**34:7** For without cause they hid for me the destruction of their snare; in vain they reproached my soul.

**34:8** Let a snare which they do not know come upon them, and let the trap which they hid seize them; and into that very snare they shall fall.

**34:9** Then my soul shall exult in the Lord; it shall delight in His salvation.

**34:10** All my bones shall say, "O Lord, who is like You, delivering the poor from the hand of those stronger than he, and the poor and needy from those who plunder him?"

**34:11** Unjust witnesses rose up; they questioned me concerning things I did not know.

**34:12** They repaid me evil for good, and bereavement to my soul.

**34:13** Yet as for me, when they troubled me, I clothed myself in sackcloth, and I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer shall return to my own bosom.

**34:14** As toward a neighbor, as toward our brother, so I sought to please; as one mourning and downcast, so I humbled myself.

**34:15** Yet against me they rejoiced and were gathered together; scourges were gathered together against me, and I did not know it; they were torn apart and felt no remorse.

**34:16** They tested me, they derided me with derision, they gnashed their teeth at me.

**34:17** O Lord, when will You look on? Restore my soul from their malice, my only one from the lions.

**34:18** I will give thanks to You, O Lord, in a great assembly; among a mighty people I will praise You.

**34:19** Do not let those who are wrongfully hostile to me gloat over me—those who hate me without cause and wink with their eyes.

**34:20** For to me they spoke words of peace, yet in wrath they plotted deceits.

**34:21** And they opened wide their mouth against me; they said, "Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen it."

**34:22** You have seen it, O Lord; do not keep silent; O Lord, do not depart from me.

**34:23** Awake, O Lord, and attend to my judgment, my God and my Lord, to my cause.

**34:24** Judge me, O Lord, according to Your righteousness, O Lord my God, and do not let them gloat over me.

**34:25** Do not let them say in their hearts, "Aha, aha, for our soul!" and do not let them say, "We have swallowed him up."

**34:26** Let those who gloat over my troubles be shamed and confounded together; let those who speak boastfully against me be clothed with shame and disgrace.

**34:27** Let those who desire my righteousness exult and be glad, and let them say continually, "May the Lord be magnified," who desire the peace of His servant.

**34:28** And my tongue shall rehearse Your righteousness, and Your praise all the day long.

## Translator Notes

- **34:3** — Greek "Pour out a sword and shut the way" preserves the Septuagint's literal rendering of the Hebrew imperatives for drawing a weapon and barring the path, taking the second verb as "close/shut" rather than a noun for a weapon.
- **34:7** — The Septuagint reads "the destruction of their snare" (taking the Hebrew noun for "pit" in its sense of destruction/corruption) and "reproached" (reading the Hebrew root for shame/reproach rather than "dug").
- **34:15** — The Septuagint reads "scourges" and two passive verbs, "they were torn apart and felt no remorse," reflecting a different vocalization and interpretation from the Masoretic Text.
- **34:20** — Unlike the Masoretic Text ("they do not speak peace"), the Septuagint reads "to me they spoke words of peace, yet in wrath they plotted deceits," contrasting deceptive peaceful speech with inward hostility.
