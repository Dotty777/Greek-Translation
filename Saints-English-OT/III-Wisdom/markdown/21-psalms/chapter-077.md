## Text

**77:1** Of understanding, for Asaph. Give heed, my people, to my law; incline your ear to the words of my mouth.

**77:2** I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter riddles from the beginning,

**77:3** all that we have heard and known, and that our fathers have recounted to us.

**77:4** They were not hidden from their children to another generation, declaring the praises of the Lord and His mighty deeds, and His wondrous works that He has done.

**77:5** And He raised up a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to make known to their sons,

**77:6** that another generation might know, the sons who are to be born, and they shall arise and declare them to their sons;

**77:7** that they may set their hope upon God, and not forget the works of God, and shall diligently seek His commandments;

**77:8** that they may not become like their fathers, a crooked and provoking generation, a generation that was not upright in its heart, and whose spirit was not faithful with God.

**77:9** The sons of Ephraim, bending and shooting the bow, turned back in the day of battle.

**77:10** They did not keep the covenant of God, and were not willing to walk in His law.

**77:11** And they forgot His benefits and His wondrous works that He had shown them,

**77:12** the wondrous works that He did before their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the plain of Tanis.

**77:13** He split the sea and led them through; He made the waters stand like a waterskin.

**77:14** And He guided them with a cloud by day, and all the night with the light of fire.

**77:15** He split a rock in the wilderness, and gave them drink as in a great abyss;

**77:16** and He brought forth water out of a rock, and brought down waters like rivers.

**77:17** And they continued still further to sin against Him; they provoked the Most High in a waterless place,

**77:18** and they put God to the test in their hearts, by asking for food for their souls.

**77:19** And they spoke against God and said, "Will God be able to prepare a table in the wilderness?

**77:20** Since He struck a rock, and waters flowed, and torrents overflowed, will He also be able to give bread, or prepare a table for His people?"

**77:21** Therefore the Lord heard and delayed, and a fire was kindled in Jacob, and wrath rose up against Israel,

**77:22** because they did not believe in God, nor did they hope in His salvation.

**77:23** And He commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven;

**77:24** and He rained down manna for them to eat, and gave them bread of heaven.

**77:25** Man ate the bread of angels; He sent them provisions to the full.

**77:26** And He raised up the south wind from heaven, and by His might He brought on the southwest wind,

**77:27** and He rained flesh upon them like dust, and winged birds like the sand of the seas;

**77:28** and they fell into the midst of their camp, all around their tents.

**77:29** And they ate and were exceedingly filled, and He brought them their desire.

**77:30** They were not deprived of their desire; while still the food

**77:31** of theirs was in their mouth, the wrath of God rose up against them and slew among the greater part of them, and fettered the chosen ones of Israel.

**77:32** In all these things they sinned still more, and did not believe in

**77:33** His wondrous works; and their days failed in vanity, and their years with haste.

**77:34** Whenever He slew them, they sought Him, and they turned back and

**77:35** rose early to seek God; and they remembered that God is their helper, and God the Most High is their redeemer.

**77:36** And they loved Him with their mouth, and with their tongue

**77:37** they lied to Him; for their heart was not upright with Him, nor were they faithful in His covenant.

**77:38** Yet He is compassionate and will pardon their sins, and will not destroy; and He will many times turn away His anger, and will not kindle all His wrath.

**77:39** And He remembered that they are flesh, a breath passing away and not returning.

**77:40** How often did they provoke Him in the wilderness, and anger

**77:41** Him in a waterless land? And they turned back and tested God, and exasperated the Holy One of Israel.

**77:42** They did not remember His hand, the day when He redeemed them from the hand of the oppressor,

**77:43** how He set His signs in Egypt, and

**77:44** His wonders in the plain of Tanis; and He turned their rivers into blood, and their rainwaters so that they could not drink;

**77:45** He sent forth the dog-fly against them, and it devoured them, and the frog, and it destroyed them;

**77:46** and He gave their fruit to the blight, and their labors to the locust;

**77:47** He slew their vine with hail, and their sycamore trees with

**77:48** frost; and He delivered their cattle to hail, and their possessions to fire;

**77:49** He sent forth against them the wrath of His fury, fury and wrath and affliction, a sending through

**77:50** evil angels; He made a path for His wrath, He did not spare their souls from death, and He shut up their cattle to death;

**77:51** and He struck every firstborn in Egypt, the firstfruits of their labors in the tents of Ham.

**77:52** And He brought forth His people like sheep, and led them like

**77:53** a flock in the wilderness; and He guided them in hope, and they were not afraid, and the sea covered their enemies.

**77:54** And He brought them into the mountain of His sanctuary, this mountain which

**77:55** His right hand acquired; and He drove out nations from before their face, and apportioned them by a line of inheritance, and made the tribes of Israel dwell in their tents.

**77:56** Yet they tested and provoked God the Most High, and did not keep His testimonies;

**77:57** and they turned back and broke covenant just like their fathers, and were turned into a crooked bow;

**77:58** and they angered Him upon their hills, and provoked Him to jealousy with their carved images.

**77:59** God heard, and disdained, and despised Israel exceedingly;

**77:60** and He cast off the tabernacle of Shiloh, His tent

**77:61** where He dwelt among men; and He delivered their strength into captivity, and His beauty into the hands of an enemy;

**77:62** and He shut up His people to the sword, and disdained His inheritance.

**77:63** Fire devoured their young men, and their virgins did not mourn;

**77:64** their priests fell by the sword, and their widows shall not be wept for.

**77:65** And the Lord awoke as one who sleeps, as a mighty man heavy-headed

**77:66** from wine; and He struck their enemies from behind; He gave them an everlasting reproach.

**77:67** And He cast off the tent of Joseph, and He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim;

**77:68** and He chose the tribe of Judah; Mount Zion He loved.

**77:69** And He built His sanctuary like that of unicorns; in the earth He founded it forever.

**77:70** And He chose David His servant, and took him up from the

**77:71** flocks of sheep; from behind the nursing ewes He took him, to shepherd Jacob His servant and Israel His inheritance.

**77:72** And he shepherded them in the innocence of his heart, and by the understanding of his hands he guided them.

## Translator Notes

- **77:12** — The Greek uses Tanis, the Hellenistic name for the Egyptian city of Zoan in the eastern Nile Delta.
- **77:13** — The Greek askos means "waterskin" or "wineskin," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew from the Masoretic reading "heap."
- **77:21** — The Greek anebaleto literally means "delayed" or "held back," differing from the Masoretic Text's "was full of wrath."
- **77:31** — The Greek phrase "among the greater part of them" (en tois pleiosin auton) corresponds to the Masoretic "among their stoutest men," and "fettered" (synepodisen) literally means "bound the feet of."
- **77:36** — The Greek reads "loved Him" (egapesan auton), whereas the Masoretic Text has "flattered Him"; the clause continues directly into verse 37.
- **77:46** — The Greek erysibe means "blight" or "mildew," differing from the Masoretic Text's "caterpillar."
- **77:63** — The Greek reads "did not mourn" (ouk epenthesan) and in verse 64 uses a passive ("shall not be wept for"), whereas the Masoretic Text speaks of virgins having no wedding songs and widows making no lamentation.
- **77:65** — The Greek participle kekraipalekos (completed by "from wine" in verse 66) denotes being heavy-headed or stuporous from wine, contrasting with the Masoretic "shouting because of wine."
- **77:69** — The Greek hos monokeroton literally means "like [that] of unicorns," arising from reading the Hebrew consonants as re'emim ("wild oxen/unicorns") rather than the Masoretic ramim ("heights").
