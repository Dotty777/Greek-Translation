## Text

**9:1** For the end, concerning the hidden things of the son; a psalm of David.

**9:2** I will give thanks to You, O Lord, with all my heart; I will recount all Your wondrous works.

**9:3** I will be glad and exult in You; I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High.

**9:4** When my enemy is turned back, they shall falter and perish from before Your presence.

**9:5** For You have executed my judgment and my cause; You sat upon a throne, judging righteousness.

**9:6** You have rebuked the nations, and the ungodly has perished; You have blotted out their name forever and ever.

**9:7** The swords of the enemy have utterly failed, and You have torn down cities; their memorial has perished with a resounding noise;

**9:8** and the Lord abides forever; He has prepared His throne in judgment.

**9:9** And He Himself shall judge the inhabited world in righteousness; He shall judge the peoples in uprightness.

**9:10** And the Lord has become a refuge for the needy, a helper in due seasons, in affliction.

**9:11** And let those who know Your name put their hope in You, for You have not forsaken those who seek You out, O Lord.

**9:12** Sing praises to the Lord, who dwells in Zion; proclaim His deeds among the nations.

**9:13** For He who requires their blood has remembered; He has not forgotten the supplication of the needy.

**9:14** Have mercy on me, O Lord; behold my humiliation from my enemies, You who lift me up from the gates of death,

**9:15** so that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion; I will exult in Your salvation.

**9:16** The nations have been caught fast in the destruction which they made; in this snare which they hid, their foot has been seized.

**9:17** The Lord is known as executing judgments; in the works of his own hands the sinner has been caught. A song of interlude.

**9:18** Let sinners be turned back to Hades, all the nations that forget God.

**9:19** For the poor shall not be forgotten to the end; the patient endurance of the needy shall not perish forever.

**9:20** Arise, O Lord; do not let man grow strong; let the nations be judged before You.

**9:21** Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them; let the nations know that they are men. Interlude.

**9:22** Why, O Lord, do You stand far off? Do You look away in due seasons, in affliction?

**9:23** While the ungodly acts arrogantly, the poor is consumed with fire; they are caught in the schemes which they devise.

**9:24** For the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul, and the one who does unrighteousness is blessed.

**9:25** The sinner has provoked the Lord; according to the abundance of his wrath he will not inquire; God is not before him.

**9:26** His ways are profaned at all times; Your judgments are taken away from before his face; he shall rule over all his enemies.

**9:27** For he said in his heart, "I shall by no means be shaken, from generation to generation, without evil."

**9:28** His mouth is full of cursing and bitterness and deceit; beneath his tongue are toil and trouble.

**9:29** He sits in ambush with the rich in secret places to slay the innocent; his eyes look intently toward the needy.

**9:30** He lies in ambush in a secret place, like a lion in his den; he lies in ambush to seize the poor, to seize the poor as he draws him in; in his snare

**9:31** he will bring him low; he will stoop and fall when he gains dominion over the needy.

**9:32** For he said in his heart, "God has forgotten; He has turned away His face so as not to see to the end."

**9:33** Arise, O Lord God; let Your hand be lifted up; do not forget the needy.

**9:34** Why has the ungodly provoked God? For he said in his heart, "He will not inquire."

**9:35** You see, for You shall regard trouble and fury, to deliver them into Your hands. To You, therefore, the poor has been left; to the orphan You have been a helper.

**9:36** Break the arm of the sinner and wicked man; his sin shall be sought out, and he shall by no means be found because of it.

**9:37** The Lord shall reign forever and ever; you shall perish, O nations, from His land.

**9:38** The Lord has heard the desire of the needy; Your ear has attended to the readiness of their heart,

**9:39** to render justice for the orphan and the humble, so that man upon the earth may no longer boast arrogantly.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek superscription reads "concerning the hidden things of the son," interpreting the Hebrew musical heading al-muth labben differently from the Masoretic vocalization.
- **9:17** — Greek ode diapsalmatos ("A song of interlude") corresponds to Higgaion Selah in the Hebrew text.
- **9:21** — The Greek reads "a lawgiver" (nomotheten), vocalizing the Hebrew consonantal text as "teacher/lawgiver" rather than "terror."
- **9:22** — From 9:22 through 9:39, the Septuagint continues Psalm 9 as one composition where the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins Psalm 10.
- **9:36** — The Greek concludes with di' auten ("because of it"), indicating that the sinner will disappear on account of his sin when it is sought out.
