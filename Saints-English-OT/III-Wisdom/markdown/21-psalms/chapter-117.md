## Text

**117:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy abides forever.

**117:2** Let the house of Israel now say that He is good, that His mercy abides forever;

**117:3** let the house of Aaron now say that He is good, that His mercy abides forever.

**117:4** Let all those who fear the Lord now say that He is good, that His mercy abides forever.

**117:5** In affliction I called upon the Lord, and He heard me, bringing me into a broad place.

**117:6** The Lord is a helper to me; I shall not be afraid. What will man do to me?

**117:7** The Lord is a helper to me, and I shall look upon my enemies.

**117:8** It is better to put confidence in the Lord than to put confidence in man;

**117:9** it is better to hope in the Lord than to hope in rulers.

**117:10** All the nations surrounded me, and by the name of the Lord I repelled them;

**117:11** surrounding, they surrounded me, and by the name of the Lord I repelled them;

**117:12** they surrounded me like bees around a honeycomb, and they blazed up like fire among thorns; and by the name of the Lord I repelled them.

**117:13** Having been pushed, I was overturned so as to fall, and the Lord upheld me.

**117:14** My strength and my song of praise is the Lord, and He has become salvation to me.

**117:15** The voice of exultation and salvation is in the tents of the righteous; the right hand of the Lord has acted mightily,

**117:16** the right hand of the Lord has exalted me.

**117:17** I shall not die, but I shall live, and I shall recount the works of the Lord.

**117:18** Chastening, the Lord chastened me, yet He did not deliver me to death.

**117:19** Open for me the gates of righteousness; entering through them, I shall give thanks to the Lord.

**117:20** This is the gate of the Lord; the righteous shall enter through it.

**117:21** I will give thanks to You, for You have heard me and have become salvation to me.

**117:22** The stone that the builders rejected, this one has become the head of the corner;

**117:23** from the Lord this has come to pass, and it is wondrous in our eyes.

**117:24** This is the day which the Lord has made; let us exult and be joyful in it.

**117:25** O Lord, save now; O Lord, grant prosperity now.

**117:26** Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord; from the house of the Lord we have blessed you.

**117:27** The Lord is God, and He has appeared to us; appoint a feast with thick boughs, even to the horns of the altar.

**117:28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to You; You are my God, and I will exalt You; I will give thanks to You, for You have heard me and have become salvation to me.

**117:29** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy abides forever.

## Translator Notes

- **117:1** — The Septuagint opens this psalm with "Hallelujah" (absent in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Psalm 118) and includes the clause "that He is good" in verses 2 through 4.
- **117:12** — The Greek uniquely adds "around a honeycomb" and reads "they blazed up" (contrasting with the Hebrew "were extinguished"); throughout verses 10 through 12, the Greek verb means to repel or ward off.
- **117:16** — The Greek critical text preserves the shorter form of this verse, reading "exalted me" transitively and omitting the repeated clause "the right hand of the Lord has acted mightily."
- **117:27** — The Septuagint reads "appeared to us" (ἐπέφανεν) and "appoint a feast with thick boughs," differing from the Masoretic Text's "given us light" and "bind the festival sacrifice with cords."
- **117:28** — The Greek text includes a third clause repeating the thanksgiving formula of verse 21, which is not present in the Masoretic Text.
