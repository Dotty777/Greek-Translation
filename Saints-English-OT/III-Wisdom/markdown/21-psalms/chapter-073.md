## Text

**73:1** Of understanding, for Asaph. Why have You cast us off, O God, forever? Has Your wrath raged against the sheep of Your pasture?

**73:2** Remember Your congregation, which You acquired from the beginning; You redeemed the rod of Your inheritance, this Mount Zion in which You have dwelt.

**73:3** Lift up Your hands against their arrogance forever, because of all the evil the enemy has done in Your holy places.

**73:4** And those who hate You have boasted in the midst of Your feast,

**73:5** as at the entrance above; as in a thicket of trees with axes they cut down

**73:6** its doors; altogether with hatchet and chisel they smashed it down.

**73:7** They burned Your sanctuary with fire; to the ground they profaned the tabernacle of Your name.

**73:8** Their kindred altogether said in their heart, "Come, and let us put an end to the feasts of the Lord from the earth."

**73:9** We have not seen our signs; there is no longer a prophet, and He will know us no more.

**73:10** How long, O God, shall the enemy reproach? Shall the adversary provoke Your name forever?

**73:11** Why do You turn away Your hand, and Your right hand from the midst of Your bosom forever?

**73:12** Yet our King from before the age has wrought salvation in the midst of the earth.

**73:13** You made firm the sea by Your power; You shattered the heads of the dragons upon the water.

**73:14** You gave him as food to the Ethiopian peoples.

**73:15** You broke open springs and torrents.

**73:16** Yours is the day, and Yours is the night; You fashioned the sun and the moon.

**73:17** You made all the borders of the earth; summer and spring You made.

**73:18** Remember this Your creation. An enemy has reproached the Lord, and a foolish people has provoked His name.

**73:19** Do not deliver to the wild beasts a soul that confesses You; do not forget the souls of Your needy forever.

**73:20** Look upon Your covenant, for the darkened places of the earth have been filled with houses of iniquities.

**73:21** Do not let the humbled and shamed be turned away; the poor and the needy shall praise Your name.

**73:22** Arise, O God, judge Your cause; remember the reproaches against You by a fool all the day.

**73:23** Do not forget the voice of Your suppliants; may the pride of those who hate You ascend continually to You.

## Translator Notes

- **73:9** — The Greek reads "and He will know us no more" (kai hemas ou gnosetai eti) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "nor is there any among us who knows how long."
- **73:14** — The Greek text here consists solely of this clause (without the line about crushing the heads of Leviathan) and renders the recipients as "the Ethiopian peoples" (laois tois Aithiopsin).
- **73:15** — The Greek text here preserves only the opening clause about breaking open springs and torrents, without the additional clause about drying up rivers.
- **73:23** — The Greek reads "Your suppliants" (ton hiketon sou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Your adversaries."
