## Text

**72:1** A Psalm of Asaph. How good God is to Israel, to the upright in heart!

**72:2** But as for me, my feet had almost been shaken; my steps had nearly been poured out.

**72:3** For I was envious of the lawless, when I beheld the peace of sinners.

**72:4** For there is no respite in their death, and there is firmness in their scourge.

**72:5** They are not in the toils of men, and they shall not be scourged with men.

**72:6** Therefore pride has taken hold of them; they have clothed themselves with their unrighteousness and ungodliness.

**72:7** Their unrighteousness shall come forth as out of fatness; they have passed into the disposition of the heart.

**72:8** They have schemed and spoken in wickedness; unrighteousness they have spoken to the height.

**72:9** They have set their mouth against heaven, and their tongue has passed over the earth.

**72:10** Therefore my people shall return here, and full days shall be found for them.

**72:11** And they said, "How does God know? And is there knowledge in the Most High?"

**72:12** Behold, sinners—and they prosper; forever they have laid hold of wealth.

**72:13** And I said, "Surely in vain I have justified my heart, and washed my hands among the innocent,

**72:14** and I have been scourged all the day, and my reproof is in the mornings."

**72:15** If I had said, "I will declare thus," behold, I would have broken faith with the generation of Your sons.

**72:16** And I undertook to understand; this was a toil before me,

**72:17** until I entered into the sanctuary of God, and understood the last things.

**72:18** Nevertheless, because of their deceits You have appointed it for them.

**72:19** How have they come to desolation? Suddenly they have vanished; they have perished because of their lawlessness.

**72:20** Like the dream of one who awakens, O Lord, in Your city You shall despise their image.

**72:21** For my heart was gladdened, and my inward parts were changed;

**72:22** and I was brought to nothing and did not know; I became like a beast before You.

**72:23** Yet I am continually with You; You have taken hold of my right hand;

**72:24** in Your counsel You have guided me, and with glory You have received me.

**72:25** For what do I have in heaven? And besides You, what have I desired upon the earth?

**72:26** My heart and my flesh have failed, O God of my heart; and God is my portion forever.

**72:27** For behold, those who remove themselves far from You shall perish; You have utterly destroyed everyone who commits harlotry away from You.

**72:28** But as for me, it is good to cling to the Lord, to place my hope in the Lord, that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **72:4** — The Greek phrasing "no respite in their death, and firmness in their scourge" (ananeusis ... stereoma en te mastigi auton) reflects a distinctive Septuagint rendering that differs markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **72:18** — The Greek reads "because of their deceits" rather than "in slippery places," and the critical text here consists of this single clause without the additional line about casting them down to ruin.
- **72:20** — The Greek reads "in Your city" (en te polei sou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "when You awake."
- **72:21** — The Greek reads "my heart was gladdened" (euphranthe) rather than "was embittered"; "inward parts" renders the literal Greek "my kidneys" (hoi nephroi mou).
- **72:28** — The Greek adds the closing phrase "in the gates of the daughter of Zion," which does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
