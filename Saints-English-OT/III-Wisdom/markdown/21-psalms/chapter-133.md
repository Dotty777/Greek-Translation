## Text

**133:1** A Song of Ascents. Behold now, bless the Lord, all you servants of the Lord, who stand in the courts of the house of our God.

**133:2** In the nights, lift up your hands toward the sanctuary, and bless the Lord.

**133:3** The Lord shall bless you from Zion, He who made heaven and earth.

## Translator Notes

- **133:1** — The Greek text reads "who stand in the courts of the house of our God" without the additional clause "in the house of the Lord," and places "in the nights" at the opening of verse 2.
