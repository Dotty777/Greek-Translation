## Text

**3:1** A psalm of David, when he fled from the face of Absalom his son.

**3:2** O Lord, why have those who afflict me been multiplied? Many are rising up against me;

**3:3** many say to my soul, "There is no salvation in his God." Interlude.

**3:4** But You, O Lord, are my protector, my glory, and the One who lifts up my head.

**3:5** With my voice I cried aloud to the Lord, and He heard me from His holy mountain. Interlude.

**3:6** I lay down and fell asleep; I awoke, for the Lord will uphold me.

**3:7** I shall not fear ten thousands of people who assail me on every side.

**3:8** Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God! For You have struck all who are hostile to me without cause; You have shattered the teeth of sinners.

**3:9** To the Lord belongs salvation, and upon Your people is Your blessing.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — In the Septuagint, the psalm superscription is numbered as verse 1, shifting the remaining verse numbers ahead by one compared to most English translations based on the Hebrew text.
- **3:4** — The Greek term means "protector" or "helper" rather than "shield" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **3:8** — The Septuagint reads "all who are hostile to me without cause" rather than "all my enemies on the cheekbone" as in the Hebrew text.
