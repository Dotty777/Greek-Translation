## Text

**135:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy abides forever.

**135:2** Give thanks to the God of gods, for His mercy abides forever.

**135:3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:4** to Him who alone works great wonders, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:5** to Him who made the heavens with understanding, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:6** to Him who made the earth firm upon the water, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:7** to Him who alone made great lights, for His mercy abides forever—

**135:8** the sun for dominion over the day, for His mercy abides forever,

**135:9** the moon and the stars for dominion over the night, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:10** to Him who struck Egypt together with their firstborn, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:11** and brought Israel out from their midst, for His mercy abides forever,

**135:12** with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:13** to Him who divided the Red Sea into divisions, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:14** and led Israel through the midst of it, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:15** and shook off Pharaoh and his host into the Red Sea, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:16** to Him who led His people through the wilderness, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:17** to Him who struck great kings, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:18** and slew mighty kings, for His mercy abides forever—

**135:19** Sihon king of the Amorites, for His mercy abides forever,

**135:20** and Og king of Bashan, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:21** and gave their land as an inheritance, for His mercy abides forever,

**135:22** an inheritance to Israel His servant, for His mercy abides forever.

**135:23** For in our lowliness the Lord remembered us, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:24** and redeemed us from the hand of our enemies, for His mercy abides forever;

**135:25** He who gives food to all flesh, for His mercy abides forever.

**135:26** Give thanks to the Lord of heaven, for His mercy abides forever.

## Translator Notes

- **135:6** — The Greek uses the singular "water" with the verb stereosanti ("made firm"), echoing the creation vocabulary of Genesis 1.
- **135:7** — The Greek adds "alone" (mono), paralleling verse 4 and not present here in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **135:10** — The Greek reads "Egypt together with their firstborn" rather than "Egypt in their firstborn."
- **135:26** — This Greek text reads "the Lord of heaven" (to kyrio tou ouranou) rather than "the God of heaven" found in the Masoretic Text and some Septuagint witnesses.
