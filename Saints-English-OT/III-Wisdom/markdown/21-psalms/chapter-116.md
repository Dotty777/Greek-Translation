## Text

**116:1** Hallelujah. Praise the Lord, all you nations; let all the peoples praise Him;

**116:2** for His mercy has grown mighty upon us, and the truth of the Lord abides forever.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**116:10** *(after `116:2`)* Hallelujah.

## Translator Notes

- **116:1** — This Greek text uses the third-person imperative ainesatosan ("let all the peoples praise Him," matching Romans 15:11) rather than the second-person imperative found in some manuscripts.
