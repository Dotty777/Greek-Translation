## Text

**44:1** For the end, concerning those who shall be changed; for the sons of Korah, for understanding; a song concerning the Beloved.

**44:2** My heart has poured forth a good word; I declare my works to the King; my tongue is the pen of a swift-writing scribe.

**44:3** Fair in beauty beyond the sons of men, grace has been poured forth upon Your lips; for this reason God has blessed You forever.

**44:4** Gird Your sword upon Your thigh, O Mighty One, in Your comeliness and Your beauty,

**44:5** and bend the bow, and prosper, and reign for the sake of truth and meekness and righteousness, and Your right hand shall guide You wondrously.

**44:6** Your arrows are sharpened, O Mighty One; peoples shall fall beneath You, in the heart of the enemies of the King.

**44:7** Your throne, O God, is forever and ever; a scepter of uprightness is the scepter of Your kingdom.

**44:8** You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; for this reason God, Your God, has anointed You with the oil of exultation beyond Your companions.

**44:9** Myrrh and stacte and cassia are from Your garments, from ivory palaces, out of which they have made You glad.

**44:10** Daughters of kings are in Your honor; the queen stood at Your right hand, arrayed in gold-woven apparel, richly embroidered.

**44:11** Listen, O daughter, and see, and incline your ear, and forget your people and the house of your father;

**44:12** for the King has desired your beauty, for He is your Lord.

**44:13** And the daughters of Tyre shall worship Him with gifts; the rich of the people of the earth shall supplicate your countenance.

**44:14** All her glory is that of the daughter of the king of Heshbon; in golden fringes she is arrayed, richly embroidered.

**44:15** Virgins shall be brought to the King behind her; her companions shall be brought to You;

**44:16** they shall be brought with gladness and exultation; they shall be led into the temple of the King.

**44:17** In place of Your fathers, sons have been born to You; You shall appoint them rulers over all the earth.

**44:18** They shall remember Your name in every generation and generation; therefore peoples shall give thanks to You forever, and forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **44:1** — The Greek phrase rendered "concerning those who shall be changed" reflects the Septuagint translation of the Hebrew superscription term shoshannim ("lilies") from a verbal root meaning "to change," while "For the end" is the standard Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew musical heading.
- **44:5** — The imperative enteinon literally means "stretch tight" or "draw," commonly used in the Septuagint for bending a bow (or by extension pressing forward), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonantal text.
- **44:14** — The reading "Heshbon" (Esebon) is a notable textual variant found in major Septuagint uncial manuscripts such as Sinaiticus and Vaticanus, whereas other Greek manuscripts read esothen ("within"), matching the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **44:18** — The Greek text here reads the third-person plural mnesthesontai ("They shall remember"), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew text read first-person singular ("I will cause Your name to be remembered").
