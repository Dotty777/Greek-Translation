## Text

**79:1** For the end, concerning those who shall be changed; a testimony of Asaph, a psalm concerning the Assyrian.

**79:2** Give heed, O You who shepherd Israel, You who guide Joseph like sheep; You who are seated upon the cherubim, appear.

**79:3** Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh, stir up Your mighty power, and come to save us.

**79:4** O God, restore us, and make Your countenance shine, and we shall be saved.

**79:5** O Lord God of hosts, how long will You be angry against the prayer of Your servant?

**79:6** Will You feed us bread of tears, and give us tears to drink by measure?

**79:7** You have made us a cause of strife to our neighbors, and our enemies have mocked us.

**79:8** O Lord God of hosts, restore us, and make Your countenance shine, and we shall be saved. Interlude.

**79:9** You brought a vine out of Egypt; You drove out nations and planted it.

**79:10** You cleared a way before it, and You planted its roots, and the land was filled.

**79:11** Its shadow covered mountains, and its climbing boughs the cedars of God.

**79:12** It stretched out its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the river.

**79:13** Why have You broken down its hedge, so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit?

**79:14** A boar from the forest has ravaged it, and a wild donkey has grazed upon it.

**79:15** O God of hosts, turn back now; look down from heaven and behold, and visit this vine,

**79:16** and restore that which Your right hand has planted, and look upon the son of man whom You have made strong for Yourself.

**79:17** It is burned with fire and dug up; at the rebuke of Your countenance they shall perish.

**79:18** Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, and upon the son of man whom You have made strong for Yourself;

**79:19** and we will never turn away from You; You will give us life, and we will call upon Your name.

**79:20** O Lord God of hosts, restore us, and make Your countenance shine, and we shall be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **79:1** — The Septuagint superscription reads 'concerning those who shall be changed' (interpreting Hebrew Shoshannim, 'lilies') and adds 'concerning the Assyrian,' which does not appear in the Masoretic Text.
- **79:5** — Greek reads the singular 'Your servant,' whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads 'Your people.'
- **79:8** — Greek diapsalma denotes a musical pause or interlude, corresponding to the Hebrew Selah.
- **79:14** — Greek onos agrios reads 'a wild donkey,' differing from the Masoretic Hebrew phrase for creatures moving in the field.
