## Text

**88:1** Of understanding, for Ethan the Israelite.

**88:2** I will sing of Your mercies, O Lord, forever; from generation to generation I will declare Your truth with my mouth.

**88:3** For You said, "Mercy shall be built up forever"; in the heavens Your truth shall be established.

**88:4** "I have made a covenant with My chosen ones; I have sworn to David My servant,

**88:5** 'Forever I will establish your seed, and I will build up your throne from generation to generation.'" Interlude.

**88:6** The heavens shall praise Your wonders, O Lord, and Your truth in the assembly of the holy ones.

**88:7** For who in the clouds shall be equaled to the Lord? And who shall be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?

**88:8** God is glorified in the council of the holy ones, great and awesome above all those who are around Him.

**88:9** O Lord, God of hosts, who is like You? Mighty are You, O Lord, and Your truth is all around You.

**88:10** You rule over the might of the sea, and You calm the surging of its waves.

**88:11** You humbled the proud one like a wounded man, and with the arm of Your power You scattered Your enemies.

**88:12** Yours are the heavens, and Yours is the earth; You have founded the inhabited world and its fullness.

**88:13** You created the north and the seas; Tabor and Hermonim shall rejoice greatly in Your name.

**88:14** Yours is the arm with sovereign might; let Your hand be strengthened; let Your right hand be exalted.

**88:15** Righteousness and judgment are the foundation of Your throne; mercy and truth shall go before Your face.

**88:16** Blessed is the people that knows the joyful shout; O Lord, they shall walk in the light of Your countenance,

**88:17** and in Your name they shall rejoice all the day, and in Your righteousness they shall be exalted.

**88:18** For You are the boast of their strength, and in Your good pleasure our horn shall be exalted.

**88:19** For protection belongs to the Lord, and to the Holy One of Israel, our King.

**88:20** Then You spoke in a vision to Your sons and said, "I have laid help upon a mighty one; I have exalted a chosen one out of My people.

**88:21** I have found David My servant; with holy mercy I have anointed him.

**88:22** For My hand shall assist him, and My arm shall strengthen him.

**88:23** No enemy shall gain advantage over him, and no son of lawlessness shall again afflict him.

**88:24** And I will cut down his enemies from before his face, and I will put to flight those who hate him.

**88:25** And My truth and My mercy shall be with him, and in My name his horn shall be exalted.

**88:26** And I will set his hand upon the sea, and his right hand upon the rivers.

**88:27** He shall call upon Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the Protector of my salvation.'

**88:28** And I will appoint him firstborn, high above the kings of the earth.

**88:29** Forever I will keep My mercy for him, and My covenant shall remain faithful to him.

**88:30** And I will establish his seed forever and ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.

**88:31** If his sons forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments,

**88:32** if they profane My ordinances and do not keep My commandments,

**88:33** I will visit their iniquities with a rod, and their sins with scourges.

**88:34** Yet My mercy I will by no means disperse from him, nor will I deal falsely in My truth,

**88:35** nor will I profane My covenant, and what goes forth through My lips I will by no means set aside.

**88:36** Once I have sworn by My holiness—I will by no means lie to David:

**88:37** His seed shall abide forever, and his throne as the sun before Me,

**88:38** and as the moon established forever; and the witness in heaven is faithful." Interlude.

**88:39** But You have cast off and despised, You have put off Your anointed one.

**88:40** You have overturned the covenant of Your servant; You have profaned his sanctuary to the ground.

**88:41** You have pulled down all his enclosures; You have turned his strongholds into terror.

**88:42** All who pass along the way have plundered him; he has become a reproach to his neighbors.

**88:43** You have exalted the right hand of his enemies; You have caused all his enemies to rejoice.

**88:44** You have turned back the help of his sword, and You did not uphold him in battle.

**88:45** You have removed him from purification; You have dashed his throne down to the ground.

**88:46** You have shortened the days of his throne; You have poured shame upon him. Interlude.

**88:47** How long, O Lord, will You turn away forever? Shall Your wrath burn like fire?

**88:48** Remember what my substance is; for have You created all the sons of men in vain?

**88:49** Who is the man who shall live and shall not see death? Shall he deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Interlude.

**88:50** Where are Your ancient mercies, O Lord, which You swore to David in Your truth?

**88:51** Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants, which You sustained in my bosom from many nations,

**88:52** with which Your enemies have reproached, O Lord, with which they have reproached the recompense of Your anointed one.

**88:53** Blessed is the Lord forever. So be it, so be it.

## Translator Notes

- **88:3** — The Septuagint reads second-person eipas ("You said") rather than first-person "I said," while retaining second-person "Your truth" in the following clause.
- **88:11** — Greek hyperephanon ("the proud one") renders the Hebrew name Rahab descriptively rather than as a proper name.
- **88:13** — The Greek reads thalassas ("the seas") rather than "the south," and uses the plural transliterated name Hermonim (Hermonieim) for Mount Hermon.
- **88:21** — The Greek text reads en eleei hagioi ("with holy mercy") rather than the similar-sounding elaioi ("oil"), a well-attested Septuagint reading preserved faithfully here.
- **88:51** — The Greek text reads second-person hypeschou ("You sustained") rather than the first-person variant hypeschon ("I bore").
