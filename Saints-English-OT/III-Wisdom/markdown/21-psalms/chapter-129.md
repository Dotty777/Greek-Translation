## Text

**129:1** A Song of Ascents. From the depths I have cried out to You, O Lord;

**129:2** Lord, listen to my voice; let Your ears give heed to the voice of my supplication.

**129:3** If You should observe iniquities, O Lord—O Lord, who shall endure?

**129:4** For with You there is propitiation. For the sake of Your name,

**129:5** I have waited, O Lord; my soul has waited for His law.

**129:6** My soul has hoped in the Lord from the morning watch until night;

**129:7** for with the Lord is mercy, and abundant redemption is with Him,

**129:8** and He Himself shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.

## Translator Notes

- **129:4** — The Greek term hilasmos denotes propitiation or forgiveness. The Greek lacks the Hebrew clause "that You may be feared," instead opening the next sentence with "For the sake of Your name" at the end of verse 4.
- **129:5** — The Greek reads "for His law" rather than "in His word," shifting briefly from direct address ("O Lord") to third-person reference ("His law").
- **129:6** — Following the Greek critical text, this verse reads "from the morning watch until night," without the Hebrew comparison to watchmen waiting for the morning or the repeated line found in some manuscripts.
