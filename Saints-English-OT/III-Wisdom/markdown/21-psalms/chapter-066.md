## Text

**66:1** For the end, in hymns; a psalm of David.

**66:2** May God have compassion on us and bless us; may He cause His countenance to shine upon us, Interlude.

**66:3** that Your way may be known upon the earth, and Your salvation among all nations.

**66:4** Let peoples give thanks, O God; let all peoples give thanks.

**66:5** Let nations be glad and exult, for You shall judge peoples in uprightness, and You shall guide nations upon the earth. Interlude.

**66:6** Let peoples give thanks to You, O God; let all peoples give thanks to You.

**66:7** The earth has yielded its fruit; may God, our God, bless us,

**66:8** may God bless us, and let all the ends of the earth fear Him.

## Translator Notes

- **66:4** — The Greek text omits σοι ("to You") in this verse, whereas it includes σοι in the matching refrain at 66:6.
- **66:7** — The aorist ἔδωκεν ("has yielded") expresses thanksgiving for a harvest already granted.
