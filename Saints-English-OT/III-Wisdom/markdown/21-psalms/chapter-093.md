## Text

**93:1** A Psalm of David, for the fourth day of the week. The Lord is the God of vengeance; the God of vengeance has revealed Himself boldly.

**93:2** Be exalted, O You who judge the earth; render recompense to the proud.

**93:3** How long shall sinners, O Lord, how long shall sinners boast?

**93:4** Shall they utter and speak unrighteousness; shall all who work lawlessness speak?

**93:5** Your people, O Lord, they have humbled, and Your inheritance they have afflicted;

**93:6** the widow and the orphan they have slain, and the sojourner they have murdered;

**93:7** and they said, "The Lord will not see, nor will the God of Jacob understand."

**93:8** Understand now, you senseless ones among the people; and you fools, at last be wise.

**93:9** He who planted the ear, does He not hear? Or He who formed the eyes, does He not discern?

**93:10** He who disciplines the nations, shall He not reprove—He who teaches man knowledge?

**93:11** The Lord knows the reasonings of men, that they are vain.

**93:12** Blessed is the man whom You discipline, O Lord, and teach from Your law,

**93:13** to grant him calm from evil days, until a pit is dug for the sinner.

**93:14** For the Lord will not reject His people, and His inheritance He will not abandon,

**93:15** until righteousness returns to judgment, and holding fast to it are all the upright in heart. Interlude.

**93:16** Who will rise up for me against evildoers, or who will stand beside me against those who work lawlessness?

**93:17** Unless the Lord had helped me, my soul would nearly have dwelt in Hades.

**93:18** If I said, "My foot has been shaken," Your mercy, O Lord, helps me.

**93:19** O Lord, according to the multitude of my sorrows in my heart, Your consolations have loved my soul.

**93:20** Shall a throne of lawlessness be joined with You, which fashions trouble by a decree?

**93:21** They will hunt for the soul of the righteous, and they will condemn innocent blood.

**93:22** And the Lord has become a refuge for me, and my God the helper of my hope;

**93:23** and He will repay to them their lawlessness and their wickedness; the Lord our God will utterly destroy them.

## Translator Notes

- **93:1** — The Septuagint superscription assigns this psalm to the fourth day of the week (Wednesday), and eparresiasato ("has revealed Himself boldly") conveys open, unrestrained divine manifestation.
- **93:15** — In the Greek text, "righteousness" is the subject that returns to "judgment" (reversing the order in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and "Interlude" translates diapsalma, the Septuagint liturgical direction corresponding to Hebrew selah.
- **93:17** — The Greek reads to hade ("in Hades," the realm of the dead) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in silence."
- **93:19** — Greek egapesan literally means "loved" or "cherished," expressing affectionate comfort where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "delighted."
