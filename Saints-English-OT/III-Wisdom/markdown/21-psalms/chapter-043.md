## Text

**43:1** For the end; for the sons of Korah, for understanding, a Psalm.

**43:2** O God, we have heard with our ears, our fathers have declared to us the work which You performed in their days, in days of old.

**43:3** Your hand utterly destroyed nations, and You planted them; You afflicted peoples and cast them out.

**43:4** For they did not inherit the land by their sword, nor did their arm save them; but Your right hand, and Your arm, and the light of Your countenance, because You were well pleased in them.

**43:5** You Yourself are my King and my God, who commands the deliverances of Jacob.

**43:6** In You we shall gore our enemies, and in Your name we shall bring to nothing those who rise up against us.

**43:7** For I will not place my hope in my bow, and my sword shall not save me;

**43:8** for You have saved us from those who afflict us, and You have put to shame those who hate us.

**43:9** In God we shall be praised all day long, and in Your name we shall give thanks forever. Interlude.

**43:10** Yet now You have cast us off and put us to shame, and You will not go forth with our hosts.

**43:11** You have turned us back before our enemies, and those who hate us plundered for themselves;

**43:12** You have given us up like sheep for food, and among the nations You have scattered us;

**43:13** You sold Your people without price, and there was no abundance in their shoutings;

**43:14** You have made us a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and a derision to those around us;

**43:15** You have made us a proverb among the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.

**43:16** All day long my disgrace is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me,

**43:17** because of the voice of the reproacher and the reviler, because of the presence of the enemy and the persecutor.

**43:18** All these things have come upon us, yet we have not forgotten You, and we have not dealt unrighteously in Your covenant;

**43:19** and our heart has not turned back, yet You turned aside our paths from Your way.

**43:20** For You have humbled us in a place of affliction, and the shadow of death has covered us over.

**43:21** If we had forgotten the name of our God, and if we had stretched out our hands to a foreign god,

**43:22** would not God search these things out? For He Himself knows the hidden things of the heart.

**43:23** For on Your account we are put to death all the day long; we have been reckoned as sheep for slaughter.

**43:24** Awake! Why do You sleep, O Lord? Arise, and do not cast us off to the end.

**43:25** Why do You turn away Your face? Do You forget our poverty and our affliction?

**43:26** For our soul has been humbled to the dust; our belly has clung to the earth.

**43:27** Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for the sake of Your name.

## Translator Notes

- **43:9** — "Interlude" translates the Greek musical term diapsalma (corresponding to Hebrew selah), and "we shall be praised" preserves the Greek passive voice (epainesthesometha).
- **43:13** — "In their shoutings" renders the Septuagint's alalagmasin ("shoutings" or "battle cries"), differing from the Masoretic Hebrew reading "in their prices."
- **43:19** — The Greek text has a second-person singular verb ("yet You turned aside our paths"), contrasting with the Hebrew reading that states the people's own steps had not turned aside.
- **43:20** — "In a place of affliction" translates the Septuagint reading en topo kakoseos, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in a place of jackals."
- **43:25** — "Our poverty" reflects the Septuagint reading tes ptochias hemon, whereas the Hebrew reads "our affliction."
