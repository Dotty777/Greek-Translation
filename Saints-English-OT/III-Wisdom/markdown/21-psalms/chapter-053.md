## Text

**53:1** For the end, in hymns; of understanding, for David,

**53:2** when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Behold, is not David hidden with us?"

**53:3** O God, by Your name save me, and by Your power judge me.

**53:4** O God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.

**53:5** For strangers have risen against me, and mighty men have sought my soul; they have not placed God before them. Pause.

**53:6** For behold, God helps me, and the Lord is the upholder of my soul.

**53:7** He shall turn back the evils upon my enemies; in Your truth utterly destroy them.

**53:8** Willingly I will sacrifice to You; I will give thanks to Your name, for it is good;

**53:9** for out of every affliction You have delivered me, and upon my enemies my eye has looked.

## Translator Notes

- **53:1** — Psalm 53 in the Septuagint corresponds to Psalm 54 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text. "For the end" translates the Greek superscription "eis to telos," and "of understanding" renders "syneseos."
- **53:5** — "Pause" translates the Greek "diapsalma," a musical interlude corresponding to the Hebrew "Selah."
- **53:8** — The Greek text reads "I will give thanks to Your name, for it is good," without the vocative "O Lord" present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
