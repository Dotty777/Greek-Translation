## Text

**58:1** To the end: "Do not destroy." Of David, for a pillar inscription, when Saul sent and watched his house to put him to death.

**58:2** Rescue me from my enemies, O God, and from those who rise up against me, redeem me.

**58:3** Deliver me from those who work lawlessness, and save me from men of blood.

**58:4** For behold, they have hunted my soul; mighty men have set upon me—neither is it my iniquity nor my sin, O Lord.

**58:5** Without iniquity I ran and kept a straight course; awake to meet me, and see,

**58:6** and You, O Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, give heed to visit all the nations; do not have compassion on any who work lawlessness. Interlude.

**58:7** They shall return at evening, and they shall hunger like a dog and go around a city.

**58:8** Behold, they will speak forth with their mouth, and a sword is on their lips; "For who has heard?"

**58:9** And You, O Lord, shall laugh at them; You shall hold all the nations in contempt.

**58:10** My strength I will keep for You, for You are God, my protector.

**58:11** My God—His mercy shall go before me; my God shall let me look upon my enemies.

**58:12** Do not slay them, lest they forget Your law; scatter them by Your power and bring them down, O Lord, my defender.

**58:13** For the sin of their mouth and the word of their lips, let them be caught in their pride; and because of cursing and falsehood there shall be proclaimed

**58:14** complete destructions, in the wrath of complete destruction, and they shall be no more; and they shall know that the God of Jacob rules over the ends of the earth. Interlude.

**58:15** They shall return at evening, and they shall hunger like a dog and go around a city;

**58:16** they shall be scattered abroad to eat, and if they are satisfied, they shall also murmur.

**58:17** But I will sing of Your power, and in the morning I will exult in Your mercy; for You have become my protector and a refuge in a day of affliction.

**58:18** O my helper, to You I will make melody, O my God; for You are my protector, O my God, my mercy.

## Translator Notes

- **58:5** — The Greek uses first-person singular verbs ("I ran and kept a straight course"), affirming the psalmist's innocence, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses third-person plural verbs referring to the enemies.
- **58:12** — The Greek reads "lest they forget Your law" (tou nomou sou), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "lest my people forget."
- **58:14** — The Greek construes "of Jacob" with "God" ("the God of Jacob rules over the ends of the earth"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "God rules in Jacob to the ends of the earth."
- **58:16** — The Greek text here has no negative particle ("if they are satisfied, they shall also murmur"), depicting chronic discontent even when fed; the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek manuscripts read "if they are not satisfied."
