## Text

**123:1** A Song of Ascents. "Had it not been that the Lord was among us," let Israel now say—

**123:2** "Had it not been that the Lord was among us when men rose up against us,

**123:3** then they would have swallowed us alive when their wrath raged against us;

**123:4** then the water would have drowned us; our soul would have passed through a torrent;

**123:5** then our soul would have passed through the irresistible water."

**123:6** Blessed be the Lord, who did not give us for prey to their teeth.

**123:7** Our soul was delivered like a sparrow from the snare of the hunters; the snare was shattered, and we were delivered.

**123:8** Our help is in the name of the Lord, the One who made heaven and earth.

## Translator Notes

- **123:1** — The Greek superscription does not include "of David," and the text reads "among us" (en hemin) rather than "on our side."
- **123:4** — The Greek reads singular "the water" and makes "our soul" the grammatical subject passing through the torrent (and likewise in verse 5), whereas the Hebrew has the torrent passing over the soul.
- **123:5** — Greek anupostaton literally denotes that which cannot be withstood or endured, hence "irresistible" or "overwhelming."
- **123:7** — The Greek specifically reads "a sparrow" (strouthion) and "the hunters" (ton thereuonton), and uses the passive verb "was delivered / were delivered" rather than "escaped."
