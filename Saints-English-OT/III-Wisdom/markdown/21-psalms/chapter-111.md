## Text

**111:1** Hallelujah. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord; in His commandments he delights exceedingly.

**111:2** Mighty in the earth shall his seed be; the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

**111:3** Glory and wealth are in his house, and his righteousness abides forever and ever.

**111:4** Light has dawned in darkness for the upright; he is merciful, and compassionate, and righteous.

**111:5** Good is the man who shows compassion and lends; he shall order his words with judgment.

**111:6** For he shall never be shaken; the righteous shall be held in eternal remembrance.

**111:7** He shall not be afraid of an evil report; his heart is prepared to hope in the Lord.

**111:8** His heart is firmly established; he shall not fear until he looks upon his enemies.

**111:9** He has scattered abroad, he has given to the needy; his righteousness abides forever and ever; his horn shall be exalted in glory.

**111:10** A sinner shall see it and be angered; he shall gnash his teeth and melt away; the desire of sinners shall perish.

## Translator Notes

- **111:3** — The Greek reads "glory and wealth" (doxa kai ploutos), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "wealth and riches."
- **111:4** — The three adjectives "merciful, and compassionate, and righteous" appear without an explicit subject in the Greek; they may describe the righteous man of the psalm or, as often understood liturgically, the Lord Himself.
- **111:5** — Greek logous ("words") reflects a Hebrew term that also denotes "affairs" or "matters," pointing to ordering one's speech or dealings with justice.
