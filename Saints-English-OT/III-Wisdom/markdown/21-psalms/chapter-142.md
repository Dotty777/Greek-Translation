## Text

**142:1** A Psalm of David, when his son pursued him. O Lord, hear my prayer; give ear to my supplication in Your truth; answer me in Your righteousness.

**142:2** And do not enter into judgment with Your servant, for no one living shall be justified in Your sight.

**142:3** He has brought my life low to the earth; he has made me dwell in dark places, like the dead of ages past.

**142:4** And my spirit grew despondent within me; inside me my heart was troubled.

**142:5** I remembered days of old, and I meditated on all Your works; on the deeds of Your hands I pondered.

**142:6** I stretched out my hands to You; my soul is like a waterless land before You. Interlude.

**142:7** Hear me swiftly, O Lord; my spirit has failed. Do not turn Your face away from me, lest I become like those who go down into the pit.

**142:8** Cause me to hear Your mercy in the morning, for in You I have put my hope; make known to me, O Lord, the way in which I shall walk, for to You I have lifted up my soul.

**142:9** Rescue me from my enemies, O Lord, for to You I have fled for refuge.

**142:10** Teach me to do Your will, for You are my God; Your Holy Spirit shall guide me in the straight way.

**142:11** For the sake of Your name, O Lord, You shall revive me in Your righteousness; You shall bring my soul out of affliction.

**142:12** And in Your mercy You shall cut off my enemies, and You shall destroy all those who afflict my soul; for I am Your servant.

## Translator Notes

- **142:1** — The superscription includes the Septuagint historical notice "when his son pursued him," linking the psalm to Absalom's rebellion.
- **142:3** — This Greek text begins directly with "He has brought my life low," without the opening clause ("For the enemy has pursued my soul") present in other witnesses.
- **142:6** — "Interlude" translates Greek diapsalma, a liturgical or musical pause corresponding to Hebrew Selah.
- **142:10** — This Greek text reads "Your Holy Spirit" (to hagion) rather than "Your good Spirit," and "in the straight way" renders en te eutheia without an explicit noun for "land."
