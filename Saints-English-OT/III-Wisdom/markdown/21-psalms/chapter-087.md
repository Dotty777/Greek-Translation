## Text

**87:1** A song of a psalm for the sons of Korah; for the end, concerning Mahalath, to answer; of understanding, for Heman the Israelite.

**87:2** O Lord, God of my salvation, by day I have cried out, and in the night before You.

**87:3** Let my prayer enter before You; incline Your ear to my supplication, O Lord.

**87:4** For my soul has been filled with evils, and my life has drawn near to Hades.

**87:5** I have been reckoned with those who descend into the pit; I have become like a man without help,

**87:6** free among the dead, like wounded men cast away, sleeping in the tomb, whom You remembered no longer, and they were thrust away from Your hand.

**87:7** They have placed me in the lowest pit, in dark places and in the shadow of death.

**87:8** Your wrath has rested heavily upon me, and You have brought all Your surging waves upon me. Interlude.

**87:9** You have put my acquaintances far from me; they have made me an abomination to themselves; I was delivered up, and I did not go forth.

**87:10** My eyes have grown weak from poverty; and I have cried out to You, O Lord, all the day; I have stretched out my hands.

**87:11** Will You perform wonders for the dead? Or will physicians raise them up, and will they give praise to You?

**87:12** Will anyone recount Your mercy in the tomb, and Your truth in destruction?

**87:13** Will Your wonders be known in the darkness, and Your righteousness in a forgotten land?

**87:14** And I, O Lord, have cried out to You, and in the morning my prayer shall come before You.

**87:15** Why, O Lord, do You cast off my prayer, and turn away Your face from me?

**87:16** I am poor and in toils from my youth; yet having been lifted up, I was humbled and brought to despair.

**87:17** Your outbursts of wrath have passed over me, and Your terrors have troubled me.

**87:18** They surrounded me like water all the day; they encompassed me altogether.

**87:19** You have put a friend far from me, and my acquaintances because of misery.

## Translator Notes

- **87:1** — The superscription preserves the Septuagint phrasing "for the end" (Greek eis to telos), the transliterated musical term "Mahalath" (maeleth), and the Greek gentilic "the Israelite" (toi Israeleitei) rather than "Ezrahite."
- **87:8** — "Surging waves" renders Greek meteorismous (lit. "upliftings" or "swells"), and "Interlude" translates Greek diapsalma, denoting a musical pause throughout the Septuagint Psalter.
- **87:11** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew term for departed shades (rephaim) as iatroi ("physicians"), asking whether human healers can raise the dead to give praise.
- **87:15** — The Greek text reads ten proseuchen mou ("my prayer") rather than "my soul," and in verse 19 apo talaiporias ("because of misery") rather than "darkness."
