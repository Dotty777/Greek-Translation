## Text

**149:1** Hallelujah. Sing to the Lord a new song; His praise is in the congregation of the devout.

**149:2** Let Israel be glad in Him who made him, and let the sons of Zion exult in their King.

**149:3** Let them praise His name with dancing; with timbrel and psaltery let them make melody to Him.

**149:4** For the Lord is well pleased with His people, and He will exalt the meek with salvation.

**149:5** The devout shall exult in glory, and they shall rejoice upon their beds.

**149:6** The exaltations of God are in their throat, and two-edged swords in their hands,

**149:7** to execute vengeance among the nations, rebukes among the peoples,

**149:8** to bind their kings with fetters and their nobles with iron manacles,

**149:9** to carry out upon them the written judgment; this glory belongs to all His devout ones.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**149:14** *(after `149:9`)* Hallelujah.

## Translator Notes

- **149:4** — The Septuagint reads 'will exalt' (hypsosei), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'will adorn' or 'beautify.'
- **149:6** — The Greek literally reads 'the exaltations of God' (echoing 'exalt' in verse four), 'in their throat' (singular), and 'two-edged swords' (plural).
- **149:8** — The Greek distinguishes between fetters for the feet (pedais) and iron manacles for the hands (cheiropedais siderais).
