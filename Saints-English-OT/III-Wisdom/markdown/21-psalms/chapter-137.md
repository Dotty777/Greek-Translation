## Text

**137:1** Of David. I will give thanks to You, O Lord, with my whole heart, for You have heard the words of my mouth; and before the angels I will sing praises to You.

**137:2** I will worship toward Your holy temple, and give thanks to Your name for Your mercy and Your truth; for You have magnified Your holy name above all things.

**137:3** In whatever day I call, hear me swiftly; You will greatly care for me in my soul with great strength.

**137:4** Let all the kings of the earth give thanks to You, O Lord, for they have heard all the words of Your mouth;

**137:5** and let them sing in the ways of the Lord, for the glory of the Lord is great;

**137:6** for the Lord is high, and He looks upon the lowly, and the lofty He knows from afar.

**137:7** Though I walk in the midst of affliction, You will keep me alive; against the wrath of enemies You stretched out my hands, and Your right hand saved me.

**137:8** O Lord, You will recompense on my behalf; O Lord, Your mercy endures forever; O Lord, do not disregard the works of Your hands.

## Translator Notes

- **137:1** — The Greek includes "for You have heard the words of my mouth" and reads "before the angels" (angelon), whereas the Masoretic Text omits the former clause and reads "before the gods."
- **137:2** — The Greek reads "You have magnified Your holy name above all things" (also syntactically possible as "Your holy sanctuary above every name"), differing from the Masoretic Text ("Your word above all Your name").
- **137:7** — This Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus) reads "against the wrath of enemies You stretched out my hands," whereas other Greek witnesses and the Masoretic Text read "of my enemies You stretched out Your hand."
