## Text

**106:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy abides forever.

**106:2** Let those redeemed by the Lord speak, whom He redeemed from the hand of an enemy.

**106:3** From the lands He gathered them, from the east and the west, and from the north and the sea.

**106:4** They wandered in the wilderness in a waterless place; they did not find a way to a city of habitation.

**106:5** Hungering and thirsting, their soul failed within them.

**106:6** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He delivered them from their distresses;

**106:7** and He guided them into a straight way, that they might walk to a city of habitation.

**106:8** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies, and for His wondrous works to the sons of men,

**106:9** for He satisfied an empty soul, and He filled a hungry soul with good things.

**106:10** Those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, bound in poverty and in iron,

**106:11** because they rebelled against the oracles of God, and provoked the counsel of the Most High.

**106:12** And their heart was humbled in labors; they grew weak, and there was no one to help.

**106:13** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He saved them from their distresses;

**106:14** and He brought them out of darkness and out of the shadow of death, and He tore their bonds apart.

**106:15** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies, and for His wondrous works to the sons of men,

**106:16** for He shattered gates of bronze, and He broke bars of iron in pieces.

**106:17** He helped them out of the way of their iniquity, for because of their iniquities they were humbled.

**106:18** Their soul loathed all food, and they drew near even to the gates of death.

**106:19** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He saved them from their distresses;

**106:20** He sent forth His word and healed them, and He delivered them from their destructions.

**106:21** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies, and for His wondrous works to the sons of men,

**106:22** and let them offer a sacrifice of praise, and proclaim His works with exultation.

**106:23** Those who go down to the sea in ships, carrying on their work in many waters,

**106:24** they themselves saw the works of the Lord, and His wondrous works in the deep.

**106:25** He spoke, and a tempestuous wind arose, and its waves were lifted high;

**106:26** they ascend to the heavens, and they descend to the depths; their soul was melting away in calamities;

**106:27** they were troubled and reeled like a drunken man, and all their wisdom was swallowed up.

**106:28** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He brought them out of their distresses,

**106:29** and He stilled its tempest, and its waves fell silent;

**106:30** and they rejoiced because they grew quiet, and He guided them by the care of His will.

**106:31** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies, and for His wondrous works to the sons of men,

**106:32** let them exalt Him in the assemblies of the people, and in the seats of the elders let them praise Him.

**106:33** He turned rivers into a wilderness, and outlets of waters into thirst,

**106:34** a fruitful land into saltiness, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it.

**106:35** He turned a wilderness into havens of waters,

**106:36** and there He settled the hungry, and they established a city of habitation,

**106:37** and they sowed fields and planted vineyards, and they yielded fruit of increase.

**106:38** And He blessed them, and they were multiplied exceedingly, and He did not diminish their cattle.

**106:39** And they became few and were afflicted through oppression of evils and sorrow.

**106:40** Contempt was poured out upon rulers, and He caused them to wander in a trackless place where there is no way.

**106:41** And He helped the poor man out of poverty, and He made families like sheep.

**106:42** The upright shall see and rejoice, and all iniquity shall shut its mouth.

**106:43** Who is wise and will observe these things? And they shall understand the mercies of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **106:3** — The Greek literally reads "the sea" (thalasses), preserving the Septuagint directional term where the Masoretic Text is often rendered "the south."
- **106:17** — The Greek reads "He helped them" (antelabeto auton), reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic Text's "fools."
- **106:30** — The Greek text here reads epimelia ("by the care") rather than the variant epi limena ("to a harbor") attested in some Septuagint witnesses.
- **106:35** — The Greek reads limenas ("havens," a variant of limnas, "pools") and omits the second clause ("and a dry land into watersprings") found in other witnesses and the Masoretic Text.
