## Text

**42:1** A Psalm of David. Judge me, O God, and plead my cause against an unholy nation; deliver me from an unjust and deceitful man.

**42:2** For You are God, my strength; why have You cast me off? And why do I go about with a sorrowful countenance while my enemy afflicts me?

**42:3** Send forth Your light and Your truth; they have guided me and brought me to Your holy mountain and to Your tabernacles.

**42:4** And I will go in to the altar of God, to God who gladdens my youth; I will give thanks to You, O Lord my God, upon the harp.

**42:5** Why are you deeply sorrowful, O soul, and why do you trouble me? Hope in God, for I shall give thanks to Him, the salvation of my countenance, my God.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — Greek Psalm 42 (Hebrew Psalm 43) includes the superscription "A Psalm of David," which does not appear in the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **42:3** — The Septuagint translates both verbs as past aorists ("they have guided me and brought me"), rather than jussive petitions ("let them lead me and bring me") as in the Hebrew.
- **42:4** — The Greek addresses "O Lord my God" (Kyrie ho theos mou), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "O God, my God."
