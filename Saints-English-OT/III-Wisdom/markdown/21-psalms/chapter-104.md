## Text

**104:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord and call upon His name; proclaim His works among the nations.

**104:2** Sing to Him and sing psalms to Him; recount all His wondrous works.

**104:3** Exult in His holy name; let the heart of those who seek the Lord be glad.

**104:4** Seek the Lord and be strengthened; seek His face continually.

**104:5** Remember His marvelous deeds that He has done, His wonders and the judgments of His mouth,

**104:6** O seed of Abraham, His servants, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**104:7** He Himself is the Lord our God; His judgments are throughout all the earth.

**104:8** He has remembered His covenant forever, the word which He commanded for a thousand generations,

**104:9** which He covenanted with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac,

**104:10** and He established it to Jacob as a statute, and to Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**104:11** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan, the measured portion of your inheritance,"

**104:12** when they were small in number, very few, and sojourners in it.

**104:13** And they passed from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people.

**104:14** He did not permit any man to wrong them, and He rebuked kings on their behalf:

**104:15** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do not deal wickedly with My prophets."

**104:16** And He summoned a famine upon the land; every support of bread He broke.

**104:17** He sent a man ahead of them; Joseph was sold as a slave.

**104:18** They afflicted his feet with fetters; his soul passed through iron,

**104:19** until his word came to pass; the oracle of the Lord refined him by fire.

**104:20** The king sent and loosed him, the ruler of peoples, and set him free.

**104:21** He appointed him lord of his house and ruler of all his possessions,

**104:22** to instruct his princes as himself, and to make his elders wise.

**104:23** And Israel entered into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

**104:24** And He increased His people exceedingly, and made them stronger than their enemies.

**104:25** And He turned their heart to hate His people, to deal craftily with His servants.

**104:26** He sent forth Moses His servant, Aaron whom He had chosen.

**104:27** He placed among them the words of His signs and wonders in the land of Ham.

**104:28** He sent forth darkness, and it grew dark; and they embittered His words.

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.

**104:30** Their land swarmed forth frogs, in the inner chambers of their kingdoms.

**104:31** He spoke, and the dog-fly came, and gnats throughout all their borders.

**104:32** He made their rains into hail, a consuming fire in their land.

**104:33** And He struck their vines and their fig trees, and shattered every tree of their territory.

**104:34** He spoke, and the locust came, and the crawling locust, of which there was no number,

**104:35** and devoured all the herbage in their land, and devoured the fruit of their land.

**104:36** And He struck every firstborn from their land, the firstfruits of all their labor.

**104:37** And He brought them out with silver and gold, and there was none who was feeble among their tribes.

**104:38** Egypt was glad at their departure, for the fear of them had fallen upon them.

**104:39** He spread out a cloud as a shelter for them, and fire to illuminate the night for them.

**104:40** They asked, and the quail came, and He filled them with the bread of heaven.

**104:41** He split the rock open, and waters flowed out; rivers ran in waterless places.

**104:42** For He remembered His holy word to Abraham His servant.

**104:43** And He brought out His people with exultation, and His chosen ones with gladness.

**104:44** And He gave them the territories of the nations, and they inherited the labors of the peoples,

**104:45** so that they might keep His ordinances and diligently seek His law.

## Translator Notes

- **104:18** — The Greek literally reads "his soul passed through iron," with "his soul" as the grammatical subject and "iron" as the object, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **104:22** — The Greek reads "to instruct his princes as himself," reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew from the Masoretic Text ("to bind his princes at his pleasure").
- **104:28** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("they did not rebel against His word"), the Greek has no negation and reads "they embittered His words."
- **104:30** — The Greek text here reads "kingdoms" (basileion) rather than "kings" (basileon).
- **104:36** — The Greek "labor" (ponou) reflects a different understanding of the underlying Hebrew term rendered "strength" in the Masoretic Text.
