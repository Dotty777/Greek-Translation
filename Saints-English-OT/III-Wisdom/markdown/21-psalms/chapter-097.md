## Text

**97:1** A Psalm of David. Sing to the Lord a new song, for the Lord has done marvelous things; His right hand and His holy arm have brought Him salvation.

**97:2** The Lord has made known His salvation before the nations; He has revealed His righteousness.

**97:3** He has remembered His mercy toward Jacob and His truth toward the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

**97:4** Shout aloud to God, all the earth; sing, exult, and make melody.

**97:5** Make melody to the Lord with the harp, with the harp and the voice of a psalm,

**97:6** with hammered trumpets and the sound of a trumpet of horn; shout aloud before the King, the Lord.

**97:7** Let the sea be shaken, and its fullness, the world and those who dwell in it.

**97:8** Rivers shall clap their hands together; the mountains shall exult

**97:9** for He has come to judge the earth; He shall judge the world in righteousness, and peoples in uprightness.

## Translator Notes

- **97:1** — The Septuagint superscription adds "of David" to the Hebrew heading "A Psalm" (Psalm 98).
- **97:2** — The Greek critical text groups "before the nations" with the first clause ("has made known His salvation before the nations") rather than with the second clause as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **97:3** — The Septuagint includes "toward Jacob" in parallel with "toward the house of Israel," an addition not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **97:6** — The Greek specifies elatais ("hammered" or beaten-metal) trumpets and ends with the dative Kyrio ("to the Lord") alongside "before the King," unifying the acclamation directed to the Lord as King.
