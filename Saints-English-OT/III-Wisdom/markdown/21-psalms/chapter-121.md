## Text

**121:1** A Song of the Ascents. Of David. I rejoiced at those who said to me, "We shall go into the house of the Lord."

**121:2** Our feet were standing in the courts of Jerusalem.

**121:3** Jerusalem is built as a city whose fellowship is together as one.

**121:4** For there the tribes of the Lord went up, a testimony of Israel, to give thanks to the name of the Lord;

**121:5** for there thrones sat for judgment, thrones over the house of David,

**121:6** and prosperity for those who love you; ask now for the things pertaining to peace for Jerusalem.

**121:7** May your peace indeed be in your strength, and prosperity in your fortified towers,

**121:8** for the sake of my brethren and my neighbors. And I spoke peace concerning you;

**121:9** for the sake of the house of the Lord our God I sought it out for you.

## Translator Notes

- **121:2** — The Greek reads "in the courts of Jerusalem" (en tais aulais Ierousalem) without a possessive pronoun, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "within your gates, O Jerusalem."
- **121:3** — The Greek metoche ("sharing, participation, fellowship") with epi to auto ("together, as one") portrays Jerusalem's unity in terms of communal fellowship rather than architectural compactness.
- **121:6** — This Greek text reverses the usual clause order of verse 6, placing "and prosperity for those who love you" before the command to seek Jerusalem's peace, and uses the dative "for Jerusalem" (te Ierousalem).
- **121:7** — The Greek includes the possessive pronoun in "your peace" (eirene sou), renders the Hebrew word for "rampart" as dynamei ("strength"), and uses the rare compound pyrgobaresin ("fortified towers").
- **121:9** — This Greek text reads exezetesa soi without agatha ("good things," found in other witnesses), leaving the object of "sought out" implied from "peace" in verse 8.
