## Text

**113:1** Hallelujah. When Israel went forth out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a foreign-speaking people,

**113:2** Judea became His sanctuary, Israel His dominion.

**113:3** The sea beheld and fled; the Jordan was turned backward.

**113:4** The mountains leaped like rams, and the hills like lambs of the flock.

**113:5** What is it, O sea, that you fled? And you, O Jordan, that you retreated backward?

**113:6** O mountains, did you leap like rams?

**113:7** At the presence of the Lord the earth was shaken, at the presence of the God of Jacob,

**113:8** who turned the rock into pools of water, and the flinty rock into springs of water.

**113:9** Not to us, O Lord, not to us, but to Your name give glory, because of Your mercy and Your truth.

**113:10** Lest the nations ever say, "Where is their God?"

**113:11** But our God is in heaven above; in the heavens and upon the earth He has done all that He willed.

**113:12** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the works of the hands of men.

**113:13** They have a mouth, and they do not speak; they have eyes, and they shall not see.

**113:14** They have ears, and they shall not hear; they have noses, and they shall not smell.

**113:15** They have hands, and they shall not feel; they have feet, and they shall not walk; they shall not utter a sound through their throat.

**113:16** May those who make them become like them, and all who put their trust in them.

**113:17** The house of Israel has hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their defender.

**113:18** The house of Aaron has hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their defender.

**113:19** Those who fear the Lord have hoped in Him; He is their helper and their defender.

**113:20** The Lord has remembered us and blessed us; He has blessed the house of Israel; He has blessed the house of Aaron.

**113:21** He has blessed those who fear the Lord, the small together with the great.

**113:22** May the Lord grant increase upon you, upon you and upon your sons.

**113:23** You are blessed by the Lord, who made heaven and earth.

**113:24** The heaven of heaven belongs to the Lord, but the earth He has given to the sons of men.

**113:25** The dead shall not praise You, O Lord, nor any who go down into Hades,

**113:26** but let us who live bless the Lord from this time onward and forevermore.

## Translator Notes

- **113:2** — The Greek reads Ioudaia ("Judea") where the Hebrew text reads "Judah."
- **113:6** — This Greek text preserves a shorter reading, omitting the parallel clause about the hills found in the Hebrew text and other Greek manuscripts.
- **113:7** — The Greek verb esaleuthe is aorist passive indicative ("was shaken"), narrating a past event rather than issuing the imperative ("Tremble!") of the Hebrew text.
- **113:9** — In Septuagint numbering, Psalm 113 unites what the Hebrew Masoretic Text divides into Psalm 114 (vv. 1-8) and Psalm 115 (vv. 9-26).
- **113:25** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which speaks of the Lord in the third person and of descending into "silence," the Greek addresses the Lord directly ("You, O Lord") and names the realm of the dead as "Hades."
