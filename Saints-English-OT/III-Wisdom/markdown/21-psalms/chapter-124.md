## Text

**124:1** A Song of Ascents. Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion; he who dwells in Jerusalem shall never be shaken.

**124:2** Mountains are around her, and the Lord is around His people, from now on and forevermore.

**124:3** For He will not leave the rod of sinners upon the lot of the righteous, lest the righteous stretch out their hands in iniquity.

**124:4** Do good, O Lord, to the good, and to the upright in heart.

**124:5** But those who turn aside into crooked ways the Lord will lead away with those who work iniquity. Peace be upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **124:1** — Unlike the Hebrew verse division, the Greek places "Jerusalem" at the end of verse 1 as the object of "he who dwells in," making the inhabitant of Jerusalem the subject of "shall never be shaken."
- **124:3** — The Greek makes the Lord the active subject ("He will not leave") and reads "of sinners" rather than the abstract "of wickedness."
