## Text

**109:1** A psalm of David. The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand until I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet."

**109:2** The Lord shall send forth a scepter of power from Zion; and have dominion in the midst of Your enemies.

**109:3** With You is dominion in the day of Your power, in the splendor of the holy ones; from the womb, before the morning star, I have begotten You.

**109:4** The Lord has sworn and will not change His mind: "You are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek."

**109:5** The Lord at Your right hand has shattered kings in the day of His wrath;

**109:6** He shall judge among the nations, He shall heap up corpses, He shall shatter heads across a vast earth.

**109:7** He shall drink from a brook along the way; therefore He shall lift up His head.

## Translator Notes

- **109:1** — In this messianic psalm (Psalm 110 in the Hebrew numbering), the Septuagint uses kyrios for both "the Lord" (God) and "my Lord" (the Davidic king).
- **109:3** — The Septuagint reads "With You is dominion... in the splendor of the holy ones; from the womb, before the morning star, I have begotten You," a distinctive reading differing from the vocalized Hebrew text and widely cited in early Christian theology.
- **109:6** — "He shall heap up corpses" translates the concise Greek expression plerosei ptoma (literally "He shall fill [with] fallen bodies" or "complete the downfall"), and the Septuagint reads plural "heads" rather than singular "head."
