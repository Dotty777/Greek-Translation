## Text

**52:1** For the end, concerning Maeleth; of understanding, of David.

**52:2** A fool has said in his heart, "There is no God." They have become corrupt and have become abominable in iniquities; there is no one who does good.

**52:3** God looked down from heaven upon the sons of men, to see if there is anyone who understands, or who seeks after God.

**52:4** All have turned aside; together they have become unprofitable; there is no one who does good, there is not even one.

**52:5** Shall not all who work iniquity know, who eat my people as a meal of bread? They have not called upon God.

**52:6** There they shall fear with dread where there was no fear, for God has scattered the bones of those who please men; they have been put to shame, because God has despised them.

**52:7** Who will give out of Zion the salvation of Israel? When the Lord turns back the captivity of His people, Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad.

## Translator Notes

- **52:1** — "Maeleth" transliterates the Greek musical or liturgical term maeleth (corresponding to Hebrew Mahalath).
- **52:6** — The Septuagint reads "the bones of those who please men" (Greek anthropareskon), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the bones of him who encamps against you."
