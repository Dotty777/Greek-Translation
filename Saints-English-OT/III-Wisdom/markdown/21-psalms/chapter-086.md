## Text

**86:1** For the sons of Korah; a psalm of a song. His foundations are in the holy mountains.

**86:2** The Lord loves the gates of Zion above all the tabernacles of Jacob.

**86:3** Glorious things have been spoken concerning you, O city of God. Interlude.

**86:4** "I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to those who know Me; and behold, foreigners and Tyre and the people of the Ethiopians—these were born there."

**86:5** "Mother Zion," a man shall say, and a Man was born in her, and He Himself, the Most High, has founded her.

**86:6** The Lord shall recount in the register of peoples and of princes—of these who have been born in her. Interlude.

**86:7** As of all who rejoice, the dwelling is in you.

## Translator Notes

- **86:1** — The masculine pronoun autou ("His") refers to the Lord named in verse two, whereas the feminine pronouns in verses five and six refer to Zion.
- **86:4** — Greek allophyloi (literally "foreigners") is the standard Septuagint rendering for the Philistines.
- **86:5** — The Septuagint reads "Mother Zion" rather than "of Zion" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; the capitalization of "Man" reflects the Greek source's traditional Christological reading.
- **86:6** — The Greek adds "and of princes" in the Lord's register, a phrase not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **86:7** — The Septuagint reads "the dwelling" of "all who rejoice," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "singers and players... all my springs."
