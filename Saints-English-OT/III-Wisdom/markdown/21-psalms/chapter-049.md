## Text

**49:1** A Psalm of Asaph. The God of gods, the Lord, has spoken and summoned the earth from the rising of the sun even to its setting.

**49:2** Out of Zion is the splendor of His beauty.

**49:3** God shall come manifestly, even our God, and He shall not keep silent; a fire shall burn before Him, and all around Him a mighty tempest.

**49:4** He shall summon the heaven above and the earth, to judge His people.

**49:5** Gather to Him His holy ones, those who establish His covenant over sacrifices.

**49:6** And the heavens shall proclaim His righteousness, for God is Judge. Interlude.

**49:7** "Hear, My people, and I will speak to you; O Israel, and I will bear solemn witness to you: I am God, your God.

**49:8** I will not reprove you on account of your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are continually before Me;

**49:9** I will not accept young bulls from your house, nor male goats from your flocks.

**49:10** For Mine are all the wild beasts of the forest, the cattle upon the mountains, and the oxen;

**49:11** I know all the birds of heaven, and the beauty of the field is with Me.

**49:12** If I should hunger, I would by no means tell you; for Mine is the world and its fullness.

**49:13** Shall I eat the flesh of bulls? Or shall I drink the blood of goats?

**49:14** Sacrifice to God a sacrifice of praise, and render your vows to the Most High;

**49:15** and call upon Me in the day of affliction, and I will rescue you, and you shall glorify Me." Interlude.

**49:16** But to the sinner God said, "Why do you recount My ordinances, and take up My covenant with your mouth?

**49:17** Yet you hated discipline, and cast My words behind you.

**49:18** If you saw a thief, you ran along with him, and with adulterers you placed your portion;

**49:19** your mouth multiplied malice, and your tongue wove deceit;

**49:20** sitting down, you slandered your brother, and against the son of your mother you laid a stumbling block.

**49:21** These things you did, and I kept silent; you supposed lawlessness, that I would be like you. I will reprove you and set it before your face.

**49:22** Understand these things now, you who forget God, lest He snatch you away, and there be none to deliver.

**49:23** A sacrifice of praise shall glorify Me, and there is the way by which I will show him the salvation of God."

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — The Septuagint uses third-person pronouns ("Gather to Him His holy ones"), continuing the heraldic summons rather than shifting into first-person divine speech as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **49:10** — The Septuagint reads "and the oxen" (kai boes), and in verse 11 "birds of heaven" and "beauty of the field," differing from the Masoretic Text ("a thousand hills," "of the mountains," and "all that moves in the field").
- **49:21** — "You supposed lawlessness" translates the Greek literally (hupelabes anomian), where the Septuagint supplies an explicit noun for iniquity in rendering the Hebrew idiom.
- **49:23** — "And there is the way" (kai ekei hodos) reflects the Septuagint reading of the Hebrew consonants as "and there is a way" rather than the Masoretic "and to him who orders his way."
