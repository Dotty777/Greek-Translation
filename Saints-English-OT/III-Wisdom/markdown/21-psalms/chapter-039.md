## Text

**39:1** For the end; a psalm of David.

**39:2** Waiting patiently, I waited for the Lord, and He attended to me and heard my supplication.

**39:3** And He brought me up from a pit of misery and from the clay of the mire; He set my feet upon a rock and made my steps straight.

**39:4** And He put a new song into my mouth, a hymn to our God; many shall see and fear, and shall hope in the Lord.

**39:5** Blessed is the man whose hope is the name of the Lord, and who has not looked upon vanities and false frenzies.

**39:6** Many are Your wondrous works that You have done, O Lord my God, and to Your thoughts there is no one who shall be likened; I declared and spoke of them, and they have been multiplied beyond number.

**39:7** Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but You have prepared a body for me; whole burnt offering and offering for sin You did not require.

**39:8** Then I said, "Behold, I have come; in the scroll of the book it is written concerning me;

**39:9** to do Your will, O my God, I have desired, and Your law is in the midst of my heart."

**39:10** I have proclaimed good tidings of righteousness in a great assembly; behold, I will by no means restrain my lips. O Lord, You have known

**39:11** my righteousness; I have not hidden Your truth in my heart, and I have spoken of Your salvation; I have not concealed Your mercy and Your truth from a numerous congregation.

**39:12** But You, O Lord, do not put Your tender mercies far from me; Your mercy and Your truth have continually upheld me.

**39:13** For evils of which there is no number have surrounded me; my iniquities have overtaken me, and I was not able to see; they have been multiplied beyond the hairs of my head, and my heart has forsaken me.

**39:14** Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me; O Lord, give heed to help me.

**39:15** Let them be put to shame and confounded together who seek my soul to take it away; let them be turned backward and disgraced who wish evil for me.

**39:16** Let them immediately receive their shame who say to me, "Aha, aha!"

**39:17** Let all who seek You exult and be glad in You, O Lord; and let them say continually, "Let the Lord be magnified," those who continually love Your salvation.

**39:18** But I am poor and needy; the Lord will care for me. You are my helper and my defender; O my God, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **39:7** — The Septuagint reads "You have prepared a body for me" (soma de katertiso moi) rather than the Hebrew "ears You have dug for me"; this Greek wording is cited in Hebrews 10:5.
- **39:11** — Following the punctuation and reading of the Greek text, "my righteousness" (ten dikaiosynen mou) is the direct object of "O Lord, You have known" at the end of verse 10, followed by "I have not hidden Your truth in my heart."
- **39:12** — Greek antelabonto is an aorist indicative ("have continually upheld me"), affirming God's ongoing help rather than expressing a petition as in the Hebrew.
