## Text

**118:1** Hallelujah. Blessed are the blameless in the way, those who walk in the law of the Lord.

**118:2** Blessed are those who search out His testimonies; with a whole heart they seek Him diligently.

**118:3** For those who work lawlessness have not walked in His ways.

**118:4** You have commanded that Your commandments be kept strictly.

**118:5** Oh, that my ways might be directed straight to keep Your ordinances!

**118:6** Then I shall by no means be put to shame, when I look upon all Your commandments.

**118:7** I will give thanks to You, O Lord, with uprightness of heart, when I have learned the judgments of Your righteousness.

**118:8** Your ordinances I will keep; do not forsake me utterly.

**118:9** By what means shall a young man set his way straight? By guarding Your words.

**118:10** With a whole heart I have sought You diligently; do not thrust me away from Your commandments.

**118:11** In my heart I have hidden Your oracles, so that I may not sin against You.

**118:12** Blessed are You, O Lord; teach me Your ordinances.

**118:13** With my lips I have proclaimed all the judgments of Your mouth.

**118:14** In the way of Your testimonies I have delighted, as over all wealth.

**118:15** I will ponder Your commandments, and I will diligently seek out Your ways.

**118:16** In Your ordinances I will meditate; I will not forget Your words.

**118:17** Recompense Your servant; I shall live, and I will keep Your words.

**118:18** Uncover my eyes, and I will perceive Your wonders out of Your law.

**118:19** I am a sojourner in the earth; do not turn Your commandments away from me.

**118:20** My soul has yearned to desire Your ordinances at every season.

**118:21** You have rebuked the proud; accursed are those who turn aside from Your commandments.

**118:22** Take away from me reproach and contempt, for I have diligently sought out Your testimonies.

**118:23** For indeed rulers sat and spoke against me, yet Your servant pondered Your ordinances.

**118:24** For indeed Your testimonies are my meditation, and Your ordinances are my counsels.

**118:25** My soul has clung to the ground; I shall live according to Your oracle.

**118:26** Your ways I have declared, and You have heard me; teach me Your ordinances.

**118:27** And make me understand the way of Your ordinances, and I will ponder Your wonders.

**118:28** My soul has grown drowsy from despondency; confirm me in Your words.

**118:29** Remove the way of unrighteousness from me, and by Your law have mercy on me.

**118:30** The way of truth I have chosen; Your judgments I have not forgotten.

**118:31** I have clung to Your testimonies; O Lord, do not put me to shame.

**118:32** The way of Your commandments I ran, when You enlarged my heart.

**118:33** Set forth as law for me, O Lord, the way of Your ordinances, and I will diligently seek it continually.

**118:34** Give me understanding, and I will diligently seek Your law, and I will keep it with my whole heart.

**118:35** Guide me in the path of Your commandments, for I have desired it.

**118:36** Incline my heart to Your testimonies, and not to covetousness.

**118:37** Turn away my eyes from seeing vanity; in Your way give me life.

**118:38** Establish Your servant in Your oracle, for the fear of You.

**118:39** Take away my reproach, which I have dreaded, for Your judgments are good.

**118:40** Behold, I have desired Your commandments; in Your righteousness give me life.

**118:41** And may Your mercy come upon me, O Lord, Your salvation according to Your mercy.

**118:42** And I will answer a word to those who reproach me, for I have hoped in Your words.

**118:43** And do not take the word of truth utterly out of my mouth, for I have set my hope upon Your judgments.

**118:44** And I will keep Your law continually, forever and to the age of the age.

**118:45** And I walked in a broad place, for I diligently sought out Your commandments.

**118:46** And I spoke of Your testimonies before kings, and I was not ashamed.

**118:47** And I meditated on Your commandments, which You have loved exceedingly.

**118:48** And I lifted up my hands toward Your commandments, which You have loved exceedingly, and I pondered Your ordinances.

**118:49** Remember Your word concerning Your servants, in which You have caused me to hope.

**118:50** This comforted me in my humiliation, for I have not turned aside from Your oracle.

**118:51** The proud transgressed the law utterly, yet from Your law I did not turn aside.

**118:52** I remembered Your judgments, O Lord, from of old, and I was comforted.

**118:53** Despair took hold of me because of sinners who forsake Your law.

**118:54** Your ordinances were songs to me in the place of my sojourning.

**118:55** I remembered Your name in the night, O Lord, and I kept Your law.

**118:56** This became mine; Your ordinances I diligently sought out.

**118:57** You are my portion, O Lord; I said that I would keep Your commandments.

**118:58** I implored Your face with my whole heart; have mercy on me according to Your oracle.

**118:59** For I considered according to Your ways, and I turned my feet back to Your testimonies.

**118:60** I prepared myself and was not troubled, to keep Your commandments.

**118:61** The cords of sinners entwined around me, and I did not forget Your law.

**118:62** At midnight I rose up to give thanks to You for the judgments of Your righteousness.

**118:63** I am a companion of all those who fear You and who keep Your commandments.

**118:64** All the earth is full of Your mercy, O Lord; teach me Your ordinances, O Lord.

**118:65** You have shown goodness to Your servant, O Lord, according to Your oracle.

**118:66** Teach me goodness and discipline and knowledge, for I have believed in Your commandments.

**118:67** Before I was humbled, I erred; therefore I have kept Your oracle.

**118:68** You are good, O Lord, and in Your goodness teach me Your ordinances.

**118:69** The unrighteousness of the proud has been multiplied against me, yet with my whole heart I will search out Your commandments.

**118:70** Their heart has curdled like milk, yet I have meditated on Your law.

**118:71** It is good for me that You have humbled me, so that I may learn Your ordinances.

**118:72** Better for me is the law of Your mouth than thousands of gold and silver.

**118:73** Your hands formed me and prepared me; give me understanding, and I will learn Your commandments.

**118:74** Those who fear You will see me and rejoice, for I have set my hope upon Your words.

**118:75** I know, O Lord, that in righteousness and truth Your judgments have humbled me.

**118:76** Now let Your mercy be to comfort me, and Your oracle for Your servant.

**118:77** Let Your compassions come to me, and I shall live; for Your law is my meditation.

**118:78** Let the proud be put to shame, for unjustly they have acted lawlessly against me; yet I will ponder Your commandments.

**118:79** Let those who fear You return, and those who know Your testimonies.

**118:80** Let my heart be blameless in Your ordinances, so that I may not be put to shame.

**118:81** My soul faints for Your salvation, and I have set my hope upon Your word.

**118:82** My eyes have failed for Your oracle, saying, "They have comforted me."

**118:83** For I have become like a wineskin in frost; Your ordinances I have not forgotten.

**118:84** How many are the days of Your servant? When will You execute judgment for me upon those who persecute me?

**118:85** Lawless men have recounted idle tales to me, yet not like Your law, O Lord.

**118:86** All Your commandments are truth; unjustly they have persecuted me; help me.

**118:87** They nearly made an end of me on the earth, yet I did not forsake Your commandments.

**118:88** According to Your mercy I shall live, and I will keep the testimonies of Your mouth.

**118:89** Forever, O Lord, Your word abides in heaven.

**118:90** To generation and generation is Your truth; You laid the foundation of the earth, and it abides.

**118:91** By Your decree the day abides, for all things are Your servants.

**118:92** Were it not that Your law is my meditation, then I would have perished in my humiliation.

**118:93** Forever I shall by no means forget Your ordinances, for by them You have given me life, O Lord.

**118:94** Yours I am, O Lord; save me, for I have diligently sought out Your ordinances.

**118:95** Yet Your testimonies I have understood.

**118:96** Of every completion I have seen a limit; Your commandment is exceedingly broad.

**118:97** How I have loved Your law, O Lord! All the day long it is my meditation.

**118:98** Above my enemies You have made me wise in Your commandments, for they are mine forever.

**118:99** Above all those who teach me I have gained understanding, for Your testimonies are my meditation.

**118:100** Above the elders I have gained understanding, for I have sought Your commandments.

**118:101** From every evil way I have restrained my feet, so that I may keep Your words.

**118:102** From Your regions I have not turned aside, for You Yourself have laid down the law for me.

**118:103** How sweet to my throat are Your oracles, sweeter than honey and honeycomb to my mouth!

**118:104** From Your commandments I have gained understanding; therefore I have hated every way of unrighteousness, for You have laid down the law for me.

**118:105** A lamp to my feet is Your law, and a light to my paths.

**118:106** I have sworn and confirmed to keep the judgments of Your righteousness.

**118:107** I have been humbled utterly; O Lord, give me life according to Your oracle.

**118:108** Bless the freewill offerings of my mouth, O Lord, and teach me Your judgments.

**118:109** My soul is in my hands continually, yet I have not forgotten Your law.

**118:110** Sinners have set a snare for me, yet I have not strayed from Your commandments.

**118:111** I have inherited Your testimonies forever, for they are the exultation of my heart.

**118:112** I have inclined my heart to perform Your ordinances forever, continually as a recompense.

**118:113** Lawless men I have hated, and Your law I have loved.

**118:114** You are my helper and my protector, and in Your people I have hoped.

**118:115** Turn away from me, you evildoers, and I will search out the commandments of my God.

**118:116** Come to my aid according to Your oracle and give me life, and do not put me to shame in my expectation.

**118:117** Help me, and I shall be saved, and I will meditate on Your ordinances continually.

**118:118** You have scorned all those who fall away from Your ordinances, for their intent is unrighteous.

**118:119** As transgressors I have reckoned all the sinners of the earth; therefore I have loved Your testimonies continually.

**118:120** Nail down my flesh with the fear of You, for I have been afraid of Your judgments.

**118:121** I have done judgment and righteousness; do not deliver me over to those who wrong me.

**118:122** Receive Your servant for good things; do not let the proud falsely accuse me.

**118:123** My eyes have failed for Your salvation, and for the oracle of Your righteousness.

**118:124** Deal with Your servant according to Your oracle, and teach me according to Your ordinances.

**118:125** I am Your servant; give me understanding, and I will know Your testimonies.

**118:126** It is time for the Lord to act; they have shattered Your law.

**118:127** Therefore I have loved Your commandments above gold and topaz.

**118:129** Wondrous are Your testimonies; therefore my soul has searched them out.

**118:130** The manifestation of Your words will give light, and will give understanding to infants.

**118:131** I opened my mouth and drew in breath, for I yearned for Your commandments.

**118:132** Look upon me and have mercy on me, according to the judgment of those who love Your name.

**118:133** Direct my steps straight according to Your oracle, and do not let any lawlessness have dominion over me.

**118:134** Redeem me from the false accusation of men, and I will keep Your commandments.

**118:144** Your testimonies are righteousness forever; give me understanding, and give me life.

## Translator Notes

- **118:1** — The Septuagint opens Psalm 118 (corresponding to Psalm 119 in Hebrew numbering) with the heading "Hallelujah" (Greek Halleluia), which is absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **118:25** — The Greek text reads the future indicative zesomai ("I shall live"), whereas the Masoretic Text and some Septuagint witnesses read an imperative ("give me life").
- **118:26** — The Greek text reads tas hodous sou ("Your ways"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "my ways."
- **118:38** — The Greek text preserves the preposition eis before to logion sou (literally "establish for Your servant in Your oracle"), an early Septuagint reading that differs from manuscripts taking "Your oracle" as the direct object.
- **118:41** — The Greek text repeats kata to eleos sou ("according to Your mercy") at the end of the verse, whereas the Masoretic Text and other Greek witnesses read "according to Your word."
- **118:47** — Both verses 47 and 48 read the second-person verb egapesas ("which You have loved exceedingly"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read the first person ("which I have loved").
- **118:50** — The Greek text reads hoti to logion sou ouk exeklina ("for I have not turned aside from Your oracle"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read "for Your word has given me life."
- **118:75** — The Greek text makes "Your judgments" the subject of the plural verb etapeinosan ("have humbled me") with two adverbial datives ("in righteousness and truth"), differing from witnesses that read "Your judgments are righteousness, and in truth You have humbled me."
- **118:82** — The Greek text reads the third-person plural indicative Parekalesan me ("They have comforted me"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read a question, "When will You comfort me?"
- **118:85** — The Septuagint reads "recounted idle tales" (adoleschias) rather than the Masoretic Text's "dug pits," and adds the vocative "O Lord" at the end of the verse.
- **118:95** — The Greek text contains only the second clause ("Yet Your testimonies I have understood"), omitting the opening clause ("Sinners waited for me to destroy me") found in the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses.
- **118:102** — The Greek text reads klimaton ("regions" or "inclinations"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read krimaton ("judgments").
- **118:114** — The Greek text reads ton laon sou ("Your people"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read ton logon sou ("Your word").
- **118:120** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew verb for shuddering in fear as katheloson ("nail down" or "pierce with nails").
- **118:124** — The Greek text reads kata to logion sou ("according to Your oracle") and adds kata ("according to") before "Your ordinances," whereas the Masoretic Text and other Greek witnesses read "according to Your mercy" and "teach me Your statutes."
- **118:139** — The Greek text reads "zeal for Your house" (ho zelos tou oikou sou) and "Your commandments," differing from the Masoretic Text ("my zeal" and "Your words").
- **118:147** — The Greek text reads the third-person plural proephthasan me ("they came upon me" or "they overtook me"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read the first-person singular ("I rose before dawn").
- **118:148** — The Greek text reads pros se orthroun ("rising early toward You"), differing from witnesses that read pros orthron ("before dawn").
- **118:149** — The Greek text reads kata to logion sou ("according to Your oracle"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read "according to Your mercy."
- **118:176** — The Greek text reads zeson ton doulon sou ("give life to Your servant"), whereas the Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses read zeteson ("seek Your servant").
