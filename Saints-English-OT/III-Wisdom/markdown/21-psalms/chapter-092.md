## Text

**92:1** For the day before the Sabbath, when the earth was settled; a song of praise of David. The Lord reigns; He has clothed Himself with splendor; the Lord has clothed Himself with power and girded Himself; for indeed He has firmly established the inhabited world, which shall not be shaken.

**92:2** Your throne is prepared from of old; from everlasting You are.

**92:3** The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices.

**92:4** More than the voices of many waters, wondrous are the surges of the sea; wondrous in the heights is the Lord.

**92:5** Your testimonies have been proven exceedingly sure; holiness befits Your house, O Lord, for length of days.

## Translator Notes

- **92:1** — The Septuagint superscription assigns this psalm to the day before the Sabbath (Friday), linking it to the sixth day of creation when the earth was settled; this title is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **92:4** — The opening phrase renders Greek apo phonon reflecting a Hebrew comparative construction (also readable causally as "from the voices of many waters"), and meteorismoi denotes the surging swells of the sea.
