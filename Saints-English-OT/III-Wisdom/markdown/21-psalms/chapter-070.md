## Text

**70:1** Of David, of the sons of Jonadab and of the first who were taken captive. O God, in You I have hoped; may I not be put to shame forever.

**70:2** In Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline Your ear to me and save me.

**70:3** Be to me a God of protection, a fortified place to save me; for You are my stronghold and my refuge.

**70:4** O God, deliver me out of the hand of the sinner, out of the hand of the transgressor and the wrongdoer;

**70:5** for You are my expectation, O Lord; the Lord is my hope from my youth.

**70:6** Upon You I have been supported from the womb; from the womb of my mother You are my protector; in You is my hymn of praise continually.

**70:7** I have become as a wonder to the many, and You are a mighty helper.

**70:8** Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may sing of Your glory, and all the day long of Your majesty.

**70:9** Do not cast me off in the time of old age; when my strength fails, do not forsake me.

**70:10** For my enemies have spoken against me, and those who watch for my soul have taken counsel together,

**70:11** saying, "God has forsaken him; pursue and overtake him, for there is none to deliver."

**70:12** O God, do not be far from me.

**70:13** Let those who slander my soul be put to shame and fail; let those who seek evil for me be clothed with shame and dishonor.

**70:14** But as for me, I will hope continually, and I will add to all Your praise.

**70:15** My mouth shall declare Your righteousness, and all the day long Your salvation, for I have not known business dealings.

**70:16** I will enter in the might of the Lord; O Lord, I will remember Your righteousness alone.

**70:17** You have taught me, O God, from my youth, and until now I will declare Your wondrous works,

**70:18** and even to old age and advanced years; O God, do not forsake me until I proclaim Your arm to every generation that is to come, Your mighty power,

**70:19** and Your righteousness, O God, even to the highest, the great things which You have done. O God, who is like You?

**70:20** For You have shown me many afflictions and evils, and turning again You gave me life, and from the depths of the earth You brought me up again,

**70:21** multiplying Your righteousness; and turning again You comforted me, and from the depths You brought me up again.

**70:22** For I will also give thanks to You with an instrument of psalmody for Your truth; I will sing praise to You, O God, with the harp, O Holy One of Israel.

**70:23** My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing praise to You, and my soul which You have redeemed.

**70:24** Moreover, my tongue also shall meditate on Your righteousness all the day long, when those who seek evil for me are put to shame and disgraced.

## Translator Notes

- **70:1** — The Septuagint supplies a superscription linking the psalm to David, the sons of Jonadab, and the first captives, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has no title.
- **70:12** — The Greek text preserves only the opening petition, omitting the second clause ("my God, make haste to help me") found in the Hebrew and other Septuagint witnesses.
- **70:15** — The Greek text reads "business dealings" (pragmatias), differing both from the Septuagint variant "writings/learning" (grammateias) and the Hebrew "numbers."
- **70:21** — The Greek text connects directly to verse 20 with the participle "multiplying" and repeats the closing clause "and from the depths You brought me up again," preserving a distinctive early manuscript reading.
