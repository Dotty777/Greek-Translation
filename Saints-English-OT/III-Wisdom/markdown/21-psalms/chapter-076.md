## Text

**76:1** For the end, on behalf of Jeduthun; a psalm for Asaph.

**76:2** With my voice I cried out to the Lord, and my voice was to God, and He gave heed to me.

**76:3** In the day of my affliction I sought out God, with my hands by night before Him, and I was not deceived; my soul refused to be comforted.

**76:4** I remembered God and was gladdened; I meditated, and my spirit grew faint. Interlude.

**76:5** My enemies seized the watches beforehand; I was troubled and did not speak.

**76:6** I considered the days of old, and the everlasting years

**76:7** I remembered and meditated; by night I mused with my heart, and my spirit searched diligently.

**76:8** Will the Lord cast off forever, and will He not be well-pleased anymore?

**76:9** Or will He utterly cut off mercy from generation to generation?

**76:10** Or will God forget to have compassion, or will He hold back His compassions in His wrath? Interlude.

**76:11** And I said, "Now I have begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High."

**76:12** I remembered the works of the Lord, for I will remember Your wondrous works from the beginning,

**76:13** and I will meditate on all Your works, and I will ponder Your deeds.

**76:14** O God, Your way is in holiness; what god is great like our God?

**76:15** You are our God who works wonders; You have made known Your power among Your peoples;

**76:16** with Your arm You redeemed Your people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Interlude.

**76:17** The waters saw You, O God; the waters saw You and were afraid, and the depths were troubled;

**76:18** a multitude of the roar of water; the clouds gave forth a voice, for indeed Your arrows pass through;

**76:19** the voice of Your thunder was in the wheel; the earth was shaken and began to tremble.

**76:20** In the sea is Your way, and Your paths in many waters, and Your footprints shall not be known.

**76:21** You led Your people like sheep by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

## Translator Notes

- **76:1** — "Jeduthun" renders the Greek transliteration Idithum.
- **76:3** — The Greek reads "and I was not deceived" (ouk epatethen), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "without growing weary."
- **76:5** — This Greek text reads "my enemies" (hoi echthroi mou) rather than the variant "my eyes" (hoi ophthalmoi mou) found in other Septuagint manuscripts and the Masoretic Text.
- **76:11** — The Greek reads "Now I have begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High," interpreting the Hebrew consonantal text differently from the Masoretic tradition.
- **76:19** — "In the wheel" (en to trocho) literally translates the Greek rendering of Hebrew baggalgal (often understood as "whirlwind"); this Greek text also lacks the clause "Your lightnings lit up the world" found in other manuscripts.
