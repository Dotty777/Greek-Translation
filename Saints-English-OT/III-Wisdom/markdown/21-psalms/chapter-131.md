## Text

**131:1** A Song of Ascents. Remember, O Lord, David and all his meekness,

**131:2** how he swore to the Lord, and vowed to the God of Jacob:

**131:3** "Surely I will not enter the dwelling of my house, nor ascend upon the couch of my bed;

**131:4** I will not give sleep to my eyes, nor slumber to my eyelids, nor rest to my temples,

**131:5** until I find a place for the Lord, a dwelling for the God of Jacob."

**131:6** Behold, we heard of it in Ephrathah; we found it in the thickets of the forest.

**131:7** I will enter into His dwellings; we will worship at the place where His feet stood.

**131:8** Arise, O Lord, into Your rest, You and the ark of Your holiness.

**131:9** Your priests have clothed themselves with righteousness, and Your holy ones shall rejoice.

**131:10** For the sake of David Your servant, do not turn away the face of Your anointed.

**131:11** The Lord swore truth to David, and He will by no means annul it: "From the fruit of your body I will set one upon your throne.

**131:12** If your sons keep My covenant and these testimonies of Mine which I will teach them, their sons also shall sit upon your throne forever."

**131:13** For the Lord has selected Zion; He has chosen her as a habitation for Himself:

**131:14** "This is My resting place forever and ever; here I will dwell, for I have chosen her.

**131:15** I will surely bless her widow; her poor I will satisfy with bread.

**131:16** I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her holy ones shall rejoice with exultation.

**131:17** There I will cause a horn to spring forth for David; I have prepared a lamp for your anointed.

**131:18** I will clothe his enemies with shame, but upon him My holiness shall blossom."

## Translator Notes

- **131:1** — The Greek reads "meekness" (πρᾳύτητος), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "affliction" or "hardship."
- **131:4** — The Greek text includes the third clause "nor rest to my temples," which is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **131:6** — The Greek text reads "in the thickets of the forest" (ἐν ταῖς δασέσι τοῦ δρυμοῦ), differing from other Septuagint witnesses ("plains of the forest") and the Hebrew ("fields of the woods/Jaar").
- **131:7** — The Greek text shifts from first-person singular ("I will enter," εἰσελεύσομαι) to first-person plural ("we will worship") and reads "at the place where His feet stood" where the Hebrew has "at His footstool."
- **131:15** — The Greek text reads "her widow" (τὴν χήραν αὐτῆς), a well-attested Septuagint reading alongside "her hunt/catch" (θήραν) in other Greek manuscripts and "her provision" in the Hebrew.
- **131:17** — The Greek text reads "for your anointed" (τῷ χριστῷ σου), shifting to second-person address within the divine promise, whereas other Septuagint witnesses and the Hebrew read "for My anointed."
