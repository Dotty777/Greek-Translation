## Text

**65:1** For the end; a song of a psalm of resurrection. Shout joyfully to God, all the earth,

**65:2** sing psalms indeed to His name; give glory to His praise.

**65:3** Say to God, "How awesome are Your works! In the multitude of Your power Your enemies shall lie to You;

**65:4** let all the earth worship You and sing psalms to You; let them sing psalms to Your name." Interlude.

**65:5** Come, children, and see the works of God; He is awesome in counsels beyond the sons of men.

**65:6** He turns the sea into dry land; through the river they shall pass on foot. There we shall rejoice in Him,

**65:7** who rules by the dominion of His eternity. His eyes look upon the nations; let the rebellious not be exalted in themselves. Interlude.

**65:8** Bless our God, O nations, and make the voice of His praise heard,

**65:9** who has set my soul in life, and has not given my feet over to shaking.

**65:10** For You have proved us, O God, and You have tried us by fire as silver is tried.

**65:11** You brought us into the snare; You laid afflictions before us.

**65:12** You caused men to ride over our heads; we passed through fire and water, and You brought us out into refreshment.

**65:13** I will enter into Your house with whole burnt offerings; I will pay You my vows,

**65:14** which my lips distinctly uttered and my mouth spoke in my affliction.

**65:15** I will offer up to You whole burnt offerings full of marrow, with incense and rams; I will offer to You oxen with male goats. Interlude.

**65:16** Come, hear, and I will recount, all you who fear the Lord, all that He has done for my soul.

**65:17** To Him I cried aloud with my mouth, and I exalted Him beneath my tongue.

**65:18** If I had regarded unrighteousness in my heart, may the Lord not hear.

**65:19** Therefore God has heard me; He has attended to the voice of my prayer.

**65:20** Blessed be God, who has not turned away my prayer, nor His mercy from me.

## Translator Notes

- **65:1** — The Septuagint superscription adds ἀναστάσεως ("of resurrection"), which is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **65:4** — "Interlude" translates Greek διάψαλμα, indicating a musical pause or interlude corresponding to the Hebrew Selah.
- **65:7** — The Greek critical text reads ἐν τῇ δυναστίᾳ τοῦ αἰῶνος αὐτοῦ ("by the dominion of His eternity"), differing from manuscripts that read "by His dominion forever."
- **65:11** — The Greek critical text reads ἐνώπιον ἡμῶν ("before us"), where other Septuagint witnesses and the Hebrew text read "upon our back."
