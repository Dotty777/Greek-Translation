## Text

**8:1** For the end, concerning the winepresses; a psalm of David.

**8:2** O Lord, our Lord, how wondrous is Your name in all the earth! For Your magnificence has been lifted up above the heavens.

**8:3** From the mouth of infants and nursing babes You have prepared praise because of Your enemies, to overthrow the enemy and the avenger.

**8:4** For I will behold the heavens, the works of Your fingers, the moon and the stars, which You have founded;

**8:5** what is man that You remember him, or a son of man that You watch over him?

**8:6** You made him a little lower than the angels; with glory and honor You crowned him.

**8:7** And You set him over the works of Your hands; all things You subjected beneath his feet,

**8:8** sheep and all cattle, and moreover the beasts of the field,

**8:9** the birds of heaven and the fish of the sea, those that pass through the paths of the seas.

**8:10** O Lord, our Lord, how wondrous is Your name in all the earth!

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek superscription renders the musical heading as "For the end, concerning the winepresses," corresponding to Hebrew lamnatseach al-haggittith.
- **8:3** — The Greek reads "praise" (ainon, as quoted in Matthew 21:16), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "strength."
- **8:6** — The Greek translates Hebrew elohim as "angels" (angelous, quoted in Hebrews 2:7), and brachy ti can mean either "a little" in status or "for a little while" in duration.
