## Text

**105:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy abides forever.

**105:2** Who shall speak of the mighty deeds of the Lord, or cause all His praises to be heard?

**105:3** Blessed are those who keep judgment and practice righteousness at all times.

**105:4** Remember us, O Lord, in the good pleasure toward Your people; visit us in Your salvation,

**105:5** that we may look upon the goodness of Your chosen ones, that we may rejoice in the gladness of Your nation, that we may be praised together with Your inheritance.

**105:6** We have sinned with our fathers; we have acted lawlessly; we have done unrighteousness.

**105:7** Our fathers in Egypt did not understand Your wonders, and did not remember the multitude of Your mercy, but rebelled bitterly as they went up at the Red Sea.

**105:8** Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, in order to make known His might.

**105:9** And He rebuked the Red Sea, and it was dried up; and He led them in the deep as in a wilderness.

**105:10** And He saved them from the hands of those who hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

**105:11** And water covered those who afflicted them; not a single one of them remained.

**105:12** And they believed in His words, and praised Him with His praise.

**105:13** They made haste, they forgot His works; they did not wait for His counsel.

**105:14** And they lusted with a craving in the wilderness, and put God to the test in a waterless place.

**105:15** And He granted them their request, and sent forth fullness into their soul.

**105:16** And they provoked Moses to anger in the camp, and Aaron, the holy one of the Lord.

**105:17** The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered over the assembly of Abiram.

**105:18** And a fire was kindled in their assembly, and a flame burned up sinners.

**105:19** And they made a calf at Horeb, and worshiped the carved image.

**105:20** And they exchanged their glory for the likeness of a calf that eats grass.

**105:21** They forgot God who saves them, who performed great things in Egypt,

**105:22** wondrous deeds in the land of Ham, and fearful things at the Red Sea.

**105:23** And He spoke of destroying them, had not Moses, His chosen one, stood in the breach before Him, to turn Him away from the fury of His wrath, lest He destroy them.

**105:24** And they despised a desirable land, and did not believe His word;

**105:25** and they murmured in their tents; they did not heed the voice of the Lord.

**105:26** And He lifted up His hand against them, to cast them down in the wilderness,

**105:27** and to cast down their seed among the nations, and to scatter them in the lands.

**105:28** And they were initiated to Baal-Peor, and ate sacrifices of the dead;

**105:29** and they provoked Him to anger with their practices, and destruction was multiplied upon them.

**105:30** And Phinehas stood up and made atonement, and the plague abated.

**105:31** And it was reckoned to him for righteousness, to generation and generation forevermore.

**105:32** And they provoked wrath at the water of Contradiction, and Moses suffered ill on their account,

**105:33** because they embittered his spirit, and he spoke explicitly with his lips.

**105:34** They did not destroy the nations concerning which the Lord had spoken,

**105:35** but they mingled among the nations and learned their works;

**105:36** and they served their carved images, and it became a stumbling block to them;

**105:37** and they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons,

**105:38** and shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters; they sacrificed to the carved images of Canaan; and the land was defiled with murder by the blood,

**105:39** and it was defiled by their works, and they played the harlot in their practices.

**105:40** And the Lord was enraged with wrath against His people, and He abhorred His inheritance.

**105:41** And He delivered them into the hands of the nations, and those who hated them held dominion over them.

**105:42** And their enemies oppressed them, and they were humbled beneath their hands.

**105:43** Many times He rescued them, yet they rebelled bitterly in their counsel, and were brought low in their iniquities.

**105:44** Yet He looked upon them when they were afflicted, when He heard their supplication;

**105:45** and He remembered His covenant, and relented according to the multitude of His mercy,

**105:46** and He granted them compassion in the sight of all who had taken them captive.

**105:47** Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us together from among the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name and glory in Your praise.

**105:48** Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, from everlasting and to everlasting; and all the people shall say, "So be it."

## Translator Notes

- **105:7** — The Greek reads 'as they went up' (anabainontes), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's 'by the sea.'
- **105:15** — The Greek has 'fullness' (plesmonen, meaning satiety), contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'leanness' or 'wasting disease.'
- **105:28** — The Greek verb etelesthesan specifically denotes ritual initiation into the cultic rites of Baal-Peor.
- **105:32** — The Greek translates Hebrew Meribah as 'Contradiction' (Antilogias), highlighting the people's contentious opposition.
- **105:48** — The Greek concludes the doxology with a single 'So be it' (Genoito, translating Hebrew Amen), without the concluding 'Hallelujah' found in the Masoretic Text.
