## Text

**32:1** For David. Exult, you righteous, in the Lord; praise is fitting for the upright.

**32:2** Give thanks to the Lord with the lyre; with a ten-stringed harp make melody to Him.

**32:3** Sing to Him a new song; make melody skillfully with a shout of joy.

**32:4** For the word of the Lord is upright, and all His works are in faithfulness.

**32:5** He loves mercy and judgment; the earth is full of the lovingkindness of the Lord.

**32:6** By the word of the Lord the heavens were established, and by the breath of His mouth all their host.

**32:7** He gathers the waters of the sea as in a waterskin; He lays up the deeps in storehouses.

**32:8** Let all the earth fear the Lord, and let all who inhabit the world tremble before Him.

**32:9** For He spoke, and they came into being; He commanded, and they were created.

**32:10** The Lord scatters the counsels of the nations; He sets aside the designs of the peoples, and sets aside the counsels of rulers.

**32:11** But the counsel of the Lord abides forever, the designs of His heart from generation to generation.

**32:12** Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, a people whom He chose for Himself as an inheritance.

**32:13** From heaven the Lord looked down; He beheld all the sons of men.

**32:14** From His prepared dwelling place He looked upon all who inhabit the earth,

**32:15** He who fashioned their hearts one by one, He who understands all their works.

**32:16** A king is not saved by great might, and a giant shall not be saved by the abundance of his strength.

**32:17** A horse is deceptive for deliverance, and by the abundance of its strength it shall not be saved.

**32:18** Behold, the eyes of the Lord are upon those who fear Him, upon those who hope in His mercy,

**32:19** to rescue their souls from death, and to nourish them in famine.

**32:20** Our soul waits patiently for the Lord, for He is our helper and our protector.

**32:21** For in Him our heart shall be glad, and in His holy name we have hoped.

**32:22** Let Your mercy, O Lord, be upon us, just as we have hoped in You.

## Translator Notes

- **32:5** — The Greek reads "mercy" (eleemosynen) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "righteousness."
- **32:7** — The Greek reads "as in a waterskin" (hos askon), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic Text's "as a heap."
- **32:9** — The Greek uses plural passive verbs ("they came into being... they were created"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses singular verbs.
- **32:10** — The Greek preserves a third parallel clause, "and sets aside the counsels of rulers," which is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **32:15** — The Greek phrase kata monas ("one by one" or "individually") contrasts with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "all together."
