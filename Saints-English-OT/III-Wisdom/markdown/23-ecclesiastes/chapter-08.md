## Text

**8:1** The wisdom of a man shall illumine his countenance, and the one who is shameless in his countenance shall be hated.

**8:2** Keep the mouth of the king, and concerning the matter of an oath of God

**8:3** do not be hasty; you shall depart from his presence; do not stand in an evil matter, for all that he may desire he shall do,

**8:4** just as a king who exercises authority; and who shall say to him, "What are you doing?"

**8:5** He who keeps a commandment will know no evil thing, and the heart of a wise man knows a time of judgment;

**8:6** for to every matter there is a time and judgment, because the knowledge of man is great upon him;

**8:7** for there is no one who knows what is to come; for how it shall come to pass, who will declare to him?

**8:8** There is no man who has authority over the spirit to restrain the spirit, and there is no authority in the day of death, and there is no discharge in the day of war; neither shall ungodliness deliver the one who belongs to it.

**8:9** And all this I saw, and I gave my heart to every work that has been done under the sun, in all the ways that man has ruled over man to harm him;

**8:10** and then I saw the ungodly brought into tombs, even from the holy place; and they departed and were praised in the city because they had done thus. This also is vanity.

**8:11** Because no contradiction is swiftly made from those who do evil, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

**8:12** He who has sinned has done evil from that time and from their long continuance; yet indeed I know that it is good for those who fear God, that they may fear before His face;

**8:13** and it shall not be good for the ungodly, neither shall he prolong days in shadow, who does not fear before the face of God.

**8:14** There is a vanity that has been done upon the earth, that there are righteous men upon whom it comes like the work of the ungodly, and there are ungodly men to whom it comes like the work of the righteous; I said that this also is vanity.

**8:15** And I praised gladness, because there is nothing good for man under the sun except to eat and to drink and to be glad; and this shall abide with him in his toil during the days of his life, as many as God has given him under the sun.

**8:16** When I gave my heart to know wisdom and to see the travail that has been done upon the earth, for both by day and by night one does not see sleep with his eyes,

**8:17** then I beheld all the works of God, that man will not be able to discover the work that is done under the sun; however much a man may toil to search it out, yet he will not find it; and indeed, though a wise man speak of knowing it, he will not be able to discover it; for all this I laid to my heart, and with my heart I saw all this:

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek reads 'shall be hated' (μισηθήσεται), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew verb than the Masoretic Text's 'is changed.'
- **8:6** — The Greek reads 'the knowledge of man' (γνῶσις), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'the misery/evil of man' through a Hebrew consonant interchange.
- **8:10** — The Greek reads 'and were praised' (ἐπῃνέθησαν), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'and were forgotten.'
- **8:17** — The closing clause ('for all this I laid to my heart, and with my heart I saw all this') corresponds to the opening of Ecclesiastes 9:1 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
