## Text

**3:1** For all things there is a time, and a season for every matter under heaven.

**3:2** A season to give birth and a season to die; a season to plant and a season to pluck up what has been planted;

**3:3** A season to slay and a season to heal; a season to tear down and a season to build;

**3:4** A season to weep and a season to laugh; a season to mourn and a season to dance;

**3:5** A season to cast stones and a season to gather stones; a season to embrace and a season to keep far from embracing;

**3:6** A season to seek and a season to lose; a season to keep and a season to cast away;

**3:7** A season to rend and a season to sew; a season to keep silent and a season to speak;

**3:8** A season to love and a season to hate; a season of war and a season of peace.

**3:9** What advantage has the worker in those things in which he toils?

**3:10** I saw all the preoccupation which God has given to the sons of men, to be preoccupied with it.

**3:11** All things that He has made are beautiful in their season; moreover, He has put all eternity in their heart, so that man may not find out the work which God has wrought from the beginning even to the end.

**3:12** I perceived that there is no good among them, except to be glad and to do good in his life.

**3:13** And indeed, every man who shall eat and drink and see good in all his toil—this is a gift of God.

**3:14** I knew that all things that God has done, they shall be forever; to it nothing can be added, and from it nothing can be taken away; and God has done it so that they should fear before His face.

**3:15** That which has been already is, and all that is to come has already been; and God will seek out the one who is pursued.

**3:16** And furthermore I saw under the sun the place of judgment—there was the ungodly one; and the place of the righteous—there was the godly one.

**3:17** And I said in my heart, "God will judge both the righteous and the ungodly, for there is a season for every matter and for every work."

**3:18** And I said in my heart concerning the speech of the sons of man, that God will discern them, and to show that they themselves are beasts.

**3:19** And indeed for them, is not the event of the sons of man also the event of the beast? One event is theirs: as the death of the one, so is the death of the other, and there is one spirit for all. And what advantage has man over the beast? Nothing, for all things are vanity.

**3:20** All things go to one place; all things came from the dust, and all things shall return to the dust.

**3:21** And who knows the spirit of the sons of man, whether it ascends upward, and the spirit of the beast, whether it descends downward into the earth?

**3:22** And I saw that there is no good thing except that a man should rejoice in his works, for that is his portion; for who shall bring him to see what will come to pass after him?

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Greek tou tekein is active ("to give birth") rather than passive ("to be born"). In 3:1-8, the Greek distinguishes chronos ("time," 3:1) from kairos ("season" or appointed moment, 3:1-8).
- **3:11** — Greek ton aiōna ("all eternity" or "the whole age") renders Hebrew ha-olam, which can signify either eternity or the world.
- **3:15** — Greek ton diōkomenon ("the one who is pursued" or "the persecuted one") is masculine personal, whereas the Hebrew is often taken neutrally as "what has passed."
- **3:16** — The Greek reads "there was the godly one" (ho eusebēs) in the place of the righteous, in contrast to the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "there was wickedness."
