## Text

**2:1** Aleph. How the Lord in His anger has shrouded the daughter of Zion in darkness! He has cast down from heaven to the earth the glory of Israel, and did not remember the footstool of His feet.

**2:2** Beth. In the day of His anger the Lord has engulfed without sparing; He has torn down in His wrath all the beautiful things of Jacob. He has pressed the strongholds of the daughter of Judah to the ground; He has profaned her kings and her princes.

**2:3** Gimel. In the wrath of His anger He has called together every horn of Israel; He has drawn back His right hand from before the face of the enemy, and He has kindled a flame like fire in Jacob, and it has devoured all things round about.

**2:4** Daleth. He has bent His bow like an opposing enemy; He has steadied His right hand like an adversary, and He has slain all the desirable things of my eyes in the tent of the daughter of Zion; He has poured out His wrath like fire.

**2:5** He. The Lord has become like an enemy; He has engulfed Israel. He has engulfed her palaces; He has destroyed his strongholds, and for the daughter of Judah He has multiplied her being humbled and brought low.

**2:6** Vav. And He has spread apart His tabernacle like a vine; He has destroyed His feast. The Lord has forgotten what He made in Zion of feast and Sabbath, and in the indignation of His anger He has provoked king and priest and prince.

**2:7** Zayin. The Lord has cast off His altar; He has shaken off His sanctuary; He has shattered with His hand the wall of her palaces. They lifted up a voice in the house as on a feast day.

**2:8** Heth. And He turned to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion; He stretched out a measure; He did not turn back His hand from trampling down. And the rampart mourned, and the wall languished together with it.

**2:9** Teth. Her gates have sunk into the earth; He has destroyed and shattered her bars, and her king and her princes among the nations. There is no law; indeed, her prophets also did not see a vision from the Lord.

**2:10** Yod. The elders of the daughter of Zion sat upon the ground and kept silent; they cast dust upon their heads; they girded themselves with sackcloth; they brought down to the earth the chief virgins in Jerusalem.

**2:11** Kaph. My eyes have failed with tears; my heart is troubled; my glory is poured out upon the earth because of the shattering of the daughter of my people, as infant and nursing child faint in the streets of the city.

**2:12** Lamed. To their mothers they said, "Where are grain and wine?" as they fainted like wounded men in the streets of the city, as their souls were poured out into the bosom of their mothers.

**2:13** Mem. What shall I testify to you, or to what shall I liken you, O daughter of Jerusalem? Who shall save and comfort you, O virgin daughter of Zion? For the cup of your shattering has been magnified; who shall heal you?

**2:14** Nun. Your prophets have seen for you vain things and folly, and they have not uncovered your iniquity so as to turn back your captivity; rather, they have seen for you vain oracles and expulsions.

**2:15** Samekh. All who pass by the way have clapped their hands at you; they have hissed and wagged their heads at the daughter of Jerusalem: "This is the city," they shall say, "the crown of gladness of all the earth."

**2:16** Ayin. All your enemies have opened their mouths wide against you; they have hissed and gnashed their teeth, and said, "We have swallowed her up! Surely this is the day for which we waited; we have found it, we have seen it."

**2:17** Pe. The Lord has done what He purposed; He has fulfilled His words which He commanded from the days of old. He has torn down and has not spared; and He has caused the enemy to rejoice over you; He has exalted the horn of him who afflicts you.

**2:18** Tsade. Their heart cried out to the Lord. O walls of Zion, pour down tears like torrents day and night; give yourself no respite; do not let your eye keep silent, O daughter.

**2:19** Qoph. Arise, exult in the night at the beginnings of your watch; pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord. Lift up your hands to Him for the life of the infants who faint with hunger at the head of all the ways out.

**2:20** Resh. Behold, O Lord, and consider whom You have gleaned thus! Shall women eat the fruit of their womb? The butcher has made a gleaning! Shall infants nursing at the breasts be slain? Will You kill priest and prophet in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**2:21** Shin. Young boy and old man lay down in the street; my virgins and my young men have gone into captivity. By sword and by famine You have slain; in the day of Your anger You butchered; You did not spare.

**2:22** Tav. He has summoned as a feast day my sojournings round about, and in the day of the anger of the Lord there was neither one escaping nor one left remaining, as I strengthened and multiplied all my enemies.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Greek text here reads synekalesen ("called together") rather than syneklasen ("broke in pieces").
- **2:10** — The Greek reads "brought down to the earth the chief virgins" (archegous parthenous), rendering the Hebrew word for "their head" as "leaders/chief ones" modifying the virgins.
- **2:19** — The Greek verb agalliasai ("exult") literally renders a Hebrew verb for crying aloud that commonly denotes joyful shouting.
- **2:20** — The Greek reads "The butcher [mageiros, literally 'cook/butcher'] has made a gleaning," continuing the vine-gleaning metaphor of 1:22 and linking lexically with "You butchered" (emageireusas) in 2:21.
- **2:22** — The Greek opens in the third person ("He has summoned") with "my sojournings" (paroikias mou) and closes with "as I strengthened and multiplied all my enemies," differing substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
