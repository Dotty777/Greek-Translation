## Text

**1:1** And it came to pass, after Israel was taken captive and Jerusalem was laid waste, that Jeremiah sat weeping, and lamented this lamentation over Jerusalem, and said: Aleph. How does the city sit solitary, she who abounded with peoples? She has become like a widow—she who was multiplied among the nations; a princess among the provinces, she has been brought under tribute.

**1:2** Beth. Weeping, she wept in the night, and her tears are upon her cheeks; there is none who comforts her among all who love her. All her friends have broken faith with her; they have become enemies to her.

**1:3** Gimel. Judea has been carried into exile because of her affliction and because of the multitude of her servitude. She dwelt among the nations; she did not find rest. All who pursued her overtook her in the midst of the oppressors.

**1:4** Daleth. The ways of Zion mourn, because there are none who come at the feast; all her gates are laid waste, her priests groan, her virgins are led away, and she herself is embittered within herself.

**1:5** He. Those who afflict her have become the head, and her enemies prospered, because the Lord humbled her for the multitude of her ungodly deeds; her little children went into captivity before the face of the oppressor.

**1:6** Vav. And all her beauty has been taken away from the daughter of Zion; her princes have become like rams finding no pasture, and they walked without strength before the face of the pursuer.

**1:7** Zayin. Jerusalem remembered the days of her humiliation and her rejections, all her desirable things that were from the days of old, when her people fell into the hands of the oppressor, and there was none who helped her. Seeing this, her enemies laughed at her habitation.

**1:8** Heth. Jerusalem has sinned a sin; therefore she has come into turmoil. All who glorified her have humbled her, for they saw her unseemliness; indeed, she herself groans and has turned backward.

**1:9** Teth. Her uncleanness is at her feet; she did not remember her latter end, and she brought down her lofty things; there is none who comforts her. Behold, O Lord, my affliction, for the enemy has magnified himself.

**1:10** Yod. The oppressor has stretched out his hand over all her desirable things; for she saw the nations entering into her sanctuary, concerning whom You commanded that they should not enter into Your assembly.

**1:11** Kaph. All her people groan, seeking bread; they have given her desirable things for food to restore the soul. Behold, O Lord, and look, for she has become dishonored.

**1:12** Lamed. To you, all who pass by the way, turn and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow which has come to pass; having spoken against me, the Lord has humbled me in the day of the wrath of His anger.

**1:13** Mem. From His height He sent fire; into my bones He brought it down. He spread a net for my feet; He turned me backward; He made me desolate, grieving in pain all the day.

**1:14** Nun. Watch was kept over my ungodly deeds; in my hands they were intertwined; they came up upon my neck. My strength has grown weak, for the Lord has put pains into my hands; I shall not be able to stand.

**1:15** Samekh. The Lord has removed all my mighty ones from my midst; He has summoned an appointed time against me to crush my chosen ones. The Lord has trodden the winepress for the virgin daughter of Judah; for these things I weep.

**1:16** Ayin. My eye has poured down water, because he who comforts me, who restores my soul, is far removed from me; my sons have become desolate, because the enemy has prevailed.

**1:17** Pe. Zion has spread out her hands; there is none who comforts her. The Lord has given command concerning Jacob: his oppressors are all around him; Jerusalem has become like an unclean woman in their midst.

**1:18** Tsade. The Lord is righteous, for I have provoked His mouth. Hear now, all peoples, and behold my sorrow: my virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.

**1:19** Qoph. I called to my lovers, but they deceived me; my priests and my elders perished in the city, for they sought food for themselves that they might restore their souls, and they did not find it.

**1:20** Resh. Behold, O Lord, for I am afflicted; my inward parts are troubled, and my heart is overturned within me, for in provoking I have been sorely embittered. Outdoors the sword has bereaved of children, just as death within the house.

**1:21** Shin. Hear now that I groan; there is none who comforts me. All my enemies have heard of my calamities and have rejoiced, because You have done it. You have brought on the day; You have summoned the appointed time; they have become like me.

**1:22** Tav. May all their wickedness come before Your face, and glean them bare in the manner that they made a gleaning because of all my sins; for my groanings are many, and my heart is grieved.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek opens with a prose superscription attributing the lament to Jeremiah after Israel's captivity and Jerusalem's desolation, and retains the Hebrew acrostic letter names (Aleph through Tav) at the start of each verse.
- **1:7** — The Greek reads "at her habitation" (katoikesia), reflecting a reading of the underlying Hebrew from the root "to dwell" rather than "her downfall."
- **1:12** — The opening "To you, all who pass by the way" follows the Greek literal rendering (hoi pros hymas), and the following clause reads "having spoken against me" (phthengxamenos en emoi).
- **1:14** — The Greek reads "Watch was kept" (egregorethe), "in my hands they were intertwined," and "has put pains into my hands," diverging markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:22** — The Greek employs the vine-gleaning metaphor ("glean them bare" and third-person plural "they made a gleaning"), an idiom that recurs in 2:20.
