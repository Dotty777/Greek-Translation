## Text

**5:1** Remember, O Lord, what has come upon us; look down and behold our reproach.

**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our houses to foreigners.

**5:3** We have become orphans; there is no father; our mothers are like widows.

**5:4** From our days, our wood came in exchange; upon our neck

**5:5** we were pursued; we toiled, and we were given no rest.

**5:6** Egypt gave a hand, Assyria to their fill.

**5:7** Our fathers sinned, and they are no more; we have borne their iniquities.

**5:8** Servants have ruled over us; there is no one who redeems from their hand.

**5:9** At the peril of our lives we shall bring in our bread, because of the sword of the wilderness.

**5:10** Our skin has grown livid like an oven; they were shriveled up because of the tempests of famine.

**5:11** They humbled women in Zion, virgins in the cities of Judah.

**5:12** Rulers were hanged by their hands; elders were not honored.

**5:13** Chosen men took up weeping, and young men grew weak under wood.

**5:14** And aged men have ceased from the gate; chosen men have ceased from their psalms.

**5:15** The joy of our heart has ceased; our dance has been turned into mourning.

**5:16** The crown of our head has fallen. Woe indeed to us, for we have sinned!

**5:17** Because of this our heart has become a grievous sorrow; because of this our eyes have grown dark.

**5:18** Because of Mount Zion, for it has been laid desolate, foxes have passed through it.

**5:19** Yet You, O Lord, shall dwell forever; Your throne endures from generation to generation.

**5:20** Why will You utterly forget us? Will You forsake us for length of days?

**5:21** Turn us back, O Lord, to You, and we shall be turned back; and renew our days as before.

**5:22** For You have utterly rejected us; You have been exceedingly angry against us.

## Translator Notes

- **5:4** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek reads "From our days" (omitting the Hebrew clause about drinking water for money) and breaks the clause across verses 4 and 5 ("upon our neck / we were pursued").
- **5:6** — The Greek places "Egypt" in the nominative as the subject of the singular verb "gave a hand," and omits "bread" after "to their fill."
- **5:13** — The Greek reads "Chosen men took up weeping" (klauthmon), where the Hebrew refers to young men carrying the millstone.
