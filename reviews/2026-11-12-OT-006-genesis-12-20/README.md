# Genesis 12–20 — Greek-led working comparison

**OT-006 · 12 November 2026 · 215 units in nine chapters · provisional AI work**

Every existing unit in Genesis 12–20 was compared with the publisher's complete Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 running Greek. [Sources and actual access scope](sources.json) identify all nine chapter pages; the endings of chapters 18 and 19 were read in follow-up retrieval after the initial combined output was truncated. No critical-apparatus or Göttingen access is claimed.

[The review records](focused-reviews.json) contain the exact baseline text, stable reference and anchor, candidate and SHA-256 hash, verse-specific analysis, unresolved questions, and close/spoken/literary working drafts. Retained wording may serve all three stages where the scoped comparison supports it; identical draft fields are not proof of finished review. The original Genesis files and accepted Genesis 1–11 benchmark remain untouched.

Two revisions are proposed; 213 units retain their wording provisionally:

| Passage | Proposed change | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12:19 | Separate Pharaoh's question from “And I took her as my wife.” | Preserve simple Greek coordination without making the taking an expressly intended purpose through “so that.” |
| 19:21 | “I have shown you favour in this matter also” | Express the face-regarding idiom through the favor granted in context. The literal image remains recorded as an alternative. |

These proposals are contextual translation judgments. No specialist lexical consultation was performed for this batch. The inherited text already preserves such selected Greek features as horses in Genesis 14, Abram sitting in 15:11, masculine child pronouns in 17:16, twelve nations in 17:20, plural trouble in 19:16, and the repeated Lord in 19:24.

Prominent unresolved passages include the household-name syntax of **15:2**, Hagar's seeing expressions and well name in **16:13–14**, the completion verb and clause structure in **18:21**, and the awkward “from outside” expression in **20:18**. The prohibitive alternative to the initial question in **18:23**, pronoun-number changes, kinship wording, names, ancient measures and other lexical issues are also recorded. Retention does not mark these questions resolved.

It writes only this review folder using UTF-8 and LF endings. [Validation](validation.json) checks coverage, identity and hashes.

Full token/construction accounting, relevant apparatus and Göttingen comparison, specialist lexical and syntactic review, independent human Greek review, human reading aloud, editorial acceptance and any website publication remain pending. None of these 215 units is counted as a completed fully reviewed translation.
