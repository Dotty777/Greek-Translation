## Key Literal Audit Proposals Flagged for Vince's Ruling

1. **Prohibited `"all"` Insertions Removed (`John 3:7, 11`):** Removed unattested `"You all"` for simple 2nd-plural Greek (`δεῖ ὑμᾶς`, `οὐ λαμβάνετε`) per `TRANSLATION-STANDARD.md` §1 & §3.
2. **Skeptical / Pre-Confessional Speaker Pronouns (`John 2:18, 20; 3:2`):** Replaced self-contradictory `"Thou hast ... unless God is with him"` (`3:2`) and hostile challengers' `"Thou"` (`2:18, 20`) with ordinary `"you"`, while preserving devotional `"Thou/Thy"` in direct prayer to God (`John 2:17`; `Psalm 22:4–6 LXX`).
3. **Ungrounded Additions & Omissions Fixed (`John 2–3`):** Removed unattested `"this"` (`2:4`), `"His"` before `τῶν σημείων` (`2:11`), `"there"` (`2:14`), and `"their"` (`2:15`); restored omitted `δὲ`/`καὶ` conjunctions, `καὶ εἶπαν`/`εἶπεν` (`2:18, 19; 3:9, 10, 27`), `αὐτοῦ τὰ σημεῖα` (`"His signs"`, `2:23`), cognate dative `χαρᾷ χαίρει` (`"rejoices with joy"`, `3:29`), and repeated `τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ... ἐν τῷ ἀνθρώπῳ` (`"concerning man ... in man"`, `2:25`).
4. **Psalm 22:1–6 LXX (`GT-004`):** Restored omitted `ἐκεῖ` (`"there He made me dwell"`, `22:2`), resumptive `αὐταί` (`"they comforted me"`, `22:4`), comparative `ὡς κράτιστον` (`"is as the very best"`, `22:5`), and articular infinitive `καὶ τὸ κατοικεῖν με` (`"and my dwelling shall be"`, `22:6`).
5. **Isaiah 53:1–12 LXX (`GT-004B`):** Removed erroneous object `"him"` at `53:10` (`βούλεται κύριος ἀφελεῖν ἀπὸ τοῦ πόνου τῆς ψυχῆς αὐτοῦ` = `"wills to take away from the toil of his soul"`), removed unattested `"O"` (`53:1`), and unified `πληγή` (`"stroke"`, vv. 3, 4, 10) vs. `κάκωσις` (`"affliction"`, vv. 4, 7).
6. **1 Maccabees 1:1–64 (`OT-1MA-001`):** Restored `Καὶ ἐγένετο` (`1:1`) and over fifty stripped paratactic `καί` clauses, preserved Greek proper names `Chettiim` (`1:1`) and `Chaseleu` (`1:54`), replaced `"prevent"` with `"forbid"` (`1:45`), and distinguished `βωμός` (`"altar"`) from `θυσιαστήριον` (`"altar of sacrifice"`) without unattested `"pagan"` (`1:47, 54, 59`).
