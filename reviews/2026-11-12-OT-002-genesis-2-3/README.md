# Genesis 2–3: Greek comparison and Saints English proposals

**OT-002 · 12 November 2026 · Provisional AI review; no editorial acceptance**

All 49 anchored units in Genesis 2–3 were compared with the publisher's [Genesis 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/GEN.2) and [Genesis 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/GEN.3) Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 running text, accessed on 12 November 2026. The inherited English is pinned to repository commit `39a2bb990f252f9c867879f14942b200c4a86aa5`.

The [49 individual records](focused-reviews.json) preserve each reference, anchor, exact inherited text, candidate text, source locator, focused rationale and review limits. This is a substantive verse-level comparison, not exhaustive Greek token accounting or specialist certification. The original accepted Genesis 1–11 benchmark has not been edited.

Five proposed revisions are separate from that benchmark:

| Passage | Proposal | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genesis 2:9 | “God **also** caused” | Represent the additive word left unexpressed in the inherited wording. |
| Genesis 2:22 | “**built** the rib … into a woman” | Retain the source's building image, distinct from earlier forming language. |
| Genesis 3:5 | “you **eat** … **will** be opened … **will** be as gods” | Make the serpent's still-future prediction clear while retaining “God knew.” |
| Genesis 3:7 | “coverings **about their waists**” | Preserve the more specific garment image. |
| Genesis 3:22 | Remove “also” before “from the tree of life” | Avoid an additional additive assertion not separately expressed in this clause. |

Forty-four units retain their inherited wording provisionally. Retention is not acceptance of an exhaustive analysis. Significant open decisions include the plural address in 2:17, stones in 2:12, rib/side in 2:21–22, the scope of the serpent's question in 3:1, sound/voice in 3:8 and 3:10, the repeated watching verb and seed pronoun in 3:15, and turning toward the husband in 3:16. Source-specific details retained include the sixth day in 2:2, face in 2:7, plural eating in 3:6, the name Life in 3:20, and Adam's settlement opposite the garden in 3:24.

Only the running text was consulted. Full word accounting, specialist lexical study, the critical apparatus, Göttingen comparison, independent qualified Greek review, human read-aloud review, editorial acceptance and publication remain pending. No New Testament text or comparison was edited or audited. No website changes were made.

`build_reviews.py` reconstructs the records from the unchanged local Genesis benchmark and the explicitly authored observations in the script. It does not fetch or translate Greek automatically. The full publisher Greek text is not redistributed.
