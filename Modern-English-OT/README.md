## Overview

This directory contains the **Saints Modern English** translation of the Greek Old Testament (Septuagint), translated fresh from `Saints-Greek-Critical-Text` under the locked style policy ([`STYLE.md`](STYLE.md)) and shared term glossary ([`glossary.json`](glossary.json)).

- **Method:** Formal-equivalent (literal) Modern English smoothed for natural reading, with zero archaisms, own-source Greek Septuagint fidelity (`κύριος` → `the Lord`), and original wording.
- **Saints.Bible versification:** Chapter and verse keys match `Saints-Greek-Critical-Text` and the Saints.Bible Parallel Reader.
- **Related directories:** Complete 27/27 NT in [`../Modern-English-NT/`](../Modern-English-NT/README.md); original reviewed pilot (incl. Ps 22, Isa 53, Dan 2 & projected layers) in [`../Modern-English-Pilot/`](../Modern-English-Pilot/README.md).
