## Text

**9:1** And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth and exercise dominion over it.

**9:2** And the dread of you and the fear will be upon all the wild animals of the earth, and upon all the birds of the sky, and upon all that moves upon the earth, and all the fish of the sea; I have given them under your hands.

**9:3** And every creeping thing that lives will be for you as food; like green herbs I have given everything to you.

**9:4** However, flesh with blood of life you must not eat;

**9:5** for indeed, your own blood of your lives I will require; from the hand of all the wild animals I will require it, and from the hand of a brother man I will require the life of the man.

**9:6** The one who sheds a man's blood, in return for his blood it will be shed, because in the image of God I made man.

**9:7** As for you, increase and multiply, and fill the earth and multiply upon the earth."

**9:8** And God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying,

**9:9** "Look, I am establishing My covenant with you and with your offspring after you,

**9:10** and with every living creature that is with you, from birds and from livestock, and with all the wild animals of the earth that are with you, from all that came out of the ark.

**9:11** And I will establish My covenant with you, and no flesh will die again from the water of the flood, and there will no longer be a flood of water to destroy all the earth."

**9:12** And the Lord God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant which I am giving between Me and you, and between every living creature that is with you, for everlasting generations:

**9:13** I place My bow in the cloud, and it will be for a sign of a covenant between Me and the earth.

**9:14** And it will happen when I gather clouds over the earth, My bow will be seen in the cloud,

**9:15** and I will remember My covenant, which is between Me and you and between every living creature in all flesh; and the water will no longer become a flood so as to wipe out all flesh.

**9:16** And My bow will be in the cloud, and I will look to remember an everlasting covenant between Me and every living creature in all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:17** And God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between Me and all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:18** Now the sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; Ham was the father of Canaan.

**9:19** These three are the sons of Noah; from these they were scattered over all the earth.

**9:20** And Noah began as a man who tilled the earth, and he planted a vineyard.

**9:21** And he drank from the wine and became drunk, and he was uncovered in his house.

**9:22** And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and going out he reported it to his two brothers outside.

**9:23** And Shem and Japheth took the garment and placed it upon both of their backs, and walked backward and covered the nakedness of their father; and their face was turned backward, and they did not see the nakedness of their father.

**9:24** And Noah sobered up from the wine, and came to know all that his younger son had done to him.

**9:25** And he said, "Cursed be Canaan; a household servant he will be to his brothers."

**9:26** And he said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Shem, and Canaan will be his servant.

**9:27** May God make room for Japheth, and let him dwell in the houses of Shem; and let Canaan become their servant."

**9:28** And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.

**9:29** And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Septuagint adds "and exercise dominion over it" at the end of the blessing (echoing Gen 1:28), a clause absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:6** — The Septuagint reads "in return for his blood it will be shed" (where the Hebrew has "by man his blood shall be shed") and uses the first-person "I made" rather than the third-person "he made."
- **9:21** — The Septuagint reads "in his house" here (and "in the houses of Shem" in 9:27) where the Hebrew text reads "tent(s)."
- **9:25** — Greek "pais oiketes" ("a household servant") renders the Hebrew idiom "servant of servants."
