## Text

**35:1** Now God said to Jacob, "Rise, go up to the place Bethel, and dwell there, and make an altar there to God, who appeared to you when you were fleeing from the face of Esau your brother."

**35:2** And Jacob said to his house and to all those who were with him, "Remove the foreign gods from your midst, and purify yourselves, and change your garments;

**35:3** and rising, let us go up to Bethel, and let us make an altar there to God, who heard me in the day of distress, who was with me and preserved me on the way that I traveled."

**35:4** And they gave to Jacob the foreign gods that were in their hands and the earrings that were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the terebinth that was in Shechem and destroyed them until the present day.

**35:5** And Israel departed from Shechem, and a fear of God came upon the cities around them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Israel.

**35:6** And Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Bethel, he and all the people who were with him.

**35:7** And he built an altar there and called the name of the place Bethel, for there God had appeared to him when he was fleeing from the face of Esau his brother.

**35:8** Now Deborah, the nurse of Rebekah, died below Bethel under the oak, and Jacob called its name Oak of Mourning.

**35:9** And God appeared to Jacob again in Luz, when he arrived from Mesopotamia of Syria, and God blessed him.

**35:10** And God said to him, "Your name will no longer be called Jacob, but Israel will be your name."

**35:11** And God said to him, "I am your God; increase and multiply, and nations and assemblies of nations will come from you, and kings will go forth from your loins.

**35:12** And the land that I have given to Abraham and Isaac, to you I have given it, and to your seed after you I will give this land."

**35:13** Then God went up from him from the place where He had spoken with him.

**35:14** And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where He had spoken with him, a pillar of stone; and he poured out a drink offering upon it and poured oil upon it.

**35:15** And Jacob called the name of the place where God had spoken with him there Bethel.

**35:16** Now Jacob, departing from Bethel, pitched his tent beyond the tower of Gader. And it came about when he drew near Chabratha to come into the land of Ephrathah, that Rachel gave birth, and she had a difficult labor in childbirth.

**35:17** And it came about while she was giving birth with difficulty that the midwife said to her, "Take courage, for this one too is a son."

**35:18** And it came about as she was giving up her soul, for she was dying, that she called his name Son of My Pain; but his father called him Benjamin.

**35:19** So Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrathah, that is, Bethlehem.

**35:20** And Jacob set up a pillar upon her tomb; this is the pillar of the tomb of Rachel until the present day.

**35:22** And it came about when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, the concubine of his father; and Israel heard, and it appeared evil before him. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve:

**35:23** the sons of Leah: Jacob's firstborn Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun;

**35:24** and the sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin;

**35:25** and the sons of Bilhah, the maidservant of Rachel: Dan and Naphtali;

**35:26** and the sons of Zilpah, the maidservant of Leah: Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Mesopotamia of Syria.

**35:27** And Jacob came to Isaac his father to Mamre, to the city of the plain, that is, Hebron in the land of Canaan, where Abraham and Isaac had sojourned.

**35:28** And the days of Isaac that he lived were one hundred eighty years.

**35:29** And expiring, he died and was added to his people, old and full of days; and Esau and Jacob his sons buried him.

## Translator Notes

- **35:4** — The concluding clause "and destroyed them until the present day" is present in the Greek text but absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **35:11** — The Greek reads "your God" (ὁ θεός σου) rather than the Hebrew "God Almighty" (El Shaddai), and uses the plural "nations and assemblies of nations."
- **35:16** — In the Greek text, Jacob's pitching of his tent beyond the tower of Gader (Hebrew: Eder) appears here rather than at v. 21, which is absent in Greek; "Chabratha" transliterates the Hebrew term for a short measure of distance.
- **35:22** — The clause "and it appeared evil before him" completes the sentence in the Greek text, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text breaks off after "Israel heard."
- **35:27** — The Greek renders the Hebrew place name Kiriath-arba as "the city of the plain" (πόλιν τοῦ πεδίου).
