## Text

**34:1** Now Dinah, the daughter of Leah whom she bore to Jacob, went out to observe the daughters of the native inhabitants.

**34:2** And Shechem the son of Hamor the Horite, the ruler of the land, saw her; and taking her, he lay with her and humiliated her.

**34:3** And he became attached to the soul of Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the maiden, and he spoke to her according to the mind of the maiden.

**34:4** And Shechem spoke to Hamor his father, saying, "Take this girl for me as a wife."

**34:5** Now Jacob heard that the son of Hamor had defiled Dinah his daughter, and his sons were with his livestock in the field; so Jacob remained silent until they came.

**34:6** Then Hamor the father of Shechem went out to Jacob to speak with him.

**34:7** Now the sons of Jacob came from the field, and when they heard, the men were deeply stung, and it was exceedingly grievous to them, because Shechem had done a disgraceful thing in Israel by lying with the daughter of Jacob; and it shall not be so.

**34:8** And Hamor spoke to them, saying, "Shechem my son has chosen your daughter in his soul; therefore give her to him as a wife.

**34:9** Intermarry with us; give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for your sons.

**34:10** And dwell among us, and look, the land is wide before you; dwell and trade upon it, and acquire property in it."

**34:11** And Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, "May I find favor before you, and whatever you tell us, we will give.

**34:12** Increase the bride-price greatly, and I will give according to whatever you tell me, and you will give me this girl as a wife."

**34:13** And the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit and spoke to them, because they had defiled Dinah their sister.

**34:14** And Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah and sons of Leah, said to them, "We will not be able to do this, to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin, for it is a reproach to us.

**34:15** In this we will become like you and dwell among you, if you also become like us by having every male among you circumcised;

**34:16** then we will give our daughters to you, and from your daughters we will take wives for ourselves, and we will dwell with you, and we will be as one people.

**34:17** But if you do not listen to us to be circumcised, taking our daughters we will depart."

**34:18** And the words were pleasing before Hamor and before Shechem the son of Hamor.

**34:19** And the young man did not delay to do this thing, for he was deeply attached to the daughter of Jacob; and he himself was the most honored of all those in the house of his father.

**34:20** So Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city, and they spoke to the men of their city, saying,

**34:21** "These men are peaceful with us; let them dwell upon the land and trade in it, and look, the land is wide before them. We will take their daughters for ourselves as wives, and we will give our daughters to them.

**34:22** Only in this will the men become like us to dwell with us so as to be one people: by having every male among us circumcised, just as they themselves are circumcised.

**34:23** And their livestock and their possessions and their four-footed animals--will they not be ours? Only let us become like them in this, and they will dwell with us."

**34:24** And all those who went out of the gate of their city listened to Hamor and Shechem his son, and they were circumcised in the flesh of their foreskin, every male.

**34:25** Now it came about on the third day, when they were in pain, that the two sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, each took his sword and entered the city securely and killed every male;

**34:26** both Hamor and Shechem his son they killed by the edge of the sword, and they took Dinah out of the house of Shechem and went out.

**34:27** And the sons of Jacob came upon the wounded men and plundered the city in which they had defiled Dinah their sister;

**34:28** and they took their sheep and their cattle and their donkeys, both whatever was in the city and whatever was in the field.

**34:29** And all their persons and all their dependents and their wives they took captive, and they plundered both whatever was in the city and whatever was in the houses.

**34:30** And Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have made me hateful, so that I am offensive to those who inhabit the land, both among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. And I am very few in number, and having gathered against me, they will strike me down, and I will be wiped out, I and my house."

**34:31** And they said, "But should they treat our sister like a prostitute?"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The Greek text identifies Hamor as "the Horite" (Χορραῖος), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Hivite."
- **34:14** — The Greek text explicitly names Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah and sons of Leah, as the speakers, whereas the Hebrew text leaves the subject unspecified.
- **34:15** — The Greek verb rendered "become like" (ὁμοιόω, also in vv. 22-23) translates a Hebrew verb meaning "consent," emphasizing assimilation into one people.
- **34:29** — The Greek terms σώματα ("persons") and ἀποσκευή ("dependents") reflect Hellenistic usage for captives and non-combatant household members; the Greek also adds "whatever was in the city" to the plunder.
