## Text

**23:1** Now the life of Sarah was one hundred twenty-seven years.

**23:2** And Sarah died in the city of Arbok, which is in the valley; this is Hebron in the land of Canaan. And Abraham came to lament for Sarah and to mourn.

**23:3** And Abraham rose up from his dead, and Abraham spoke to the sons of Heth, saying,

**23:4** "I am a resident alien and a temporary resident with you; therefore give me a possession for a grave with you, and I will bury my dead there."

**23:5** And the sons of Heth answered Abraham, saying,

**23:6** "No, lord; instead, listen to us. You are a king from God among us; bury your dead in our choice tombs, for none of us will withhold his tomb from you for burying your dead there."

**23:7** Then Abraham rose up and bowed down to the people of the land, the sons of Heth.

**23:8** And Abraham spoke to them, saying, "If you have it in your soul to bury my dead from before my face, listen to me and speak concerning me to Ephron the son of Zohar,

**23:9** and let him give me the double cave that belongs to him, which is in a portion of his field; for its worth in silver, give it to me among you as a possession for a tomb."

**23:10** Now Ephron was sitting in the midst of the sons of Heth; and Ephron the Hittite answered and spoke to Abraham, in the hearing of the sons of Heth and of all who were entering the city, saying,

**23:11** "Be with me, lord, and listen to me. The field and the cave that is in it I give to you; before my citizens I have given it to you; bury your dead."

**23:12** And Abraham bowed down before the people of the land.

**23:13** And he spoke to Ephron within hearing before all the people of the land, "Since you are on my side, listen to me: take the silver for the field from me, and bury my dead there."

**23:14** And Ephron answered Abraham, saying,

**23:15** "No, lord, for I have heard: four hundred didrachmas of silver—between me and you, what would this be? But you, bury your dead."

**23:16** And Abraham listened to Ephron; and Abraham paid to Ephron the silver which he had spoken in the hearing of the sons of Heth, four hundred didrachmas of silver, approved by merchants.

**23:17** And the field of Ephron stood confirmed, which was in the double cave, which is facing Mamre—the field and the cave that belonged to it, and every tree that was in the field, which is within its borders all around—

**23:18** to Abraham as a possession before the sons of Heth and all who were entering the city.

**23:19** After these things, Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the double cave of the field, which is opposite Mamre; this is Hebron in the land of Canaan.

**23:20** And the field and the cave that belonged to it were ratified to Abraham as a possession for a grave from the sons of Heth.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Greek reads 'city of Arbok' (πόλει Ἀρβόκ) corresponding to Hebrew Kiriath-arba, and uniquely includes 'in the valley' (ἐν τῷ κοιλώματι, literally 'in the hollow').
- **23:9** — The Greek renders Hebrew Machpelah descriptively as 'the double cave' (τὸ σπήλαιον τὸ διπλοῦν; also in vv. 17, 19), reads 'in a portion' (ἐν μερίδι) rather than 'at the end', and uses the plural imperative 'give' (δότε).
- **23:13** — The Greek text reads the second-person imperative 'bury' (θάψον) rather than the first-person future 'I will bury' (cf. v. 4 and the Hebrew text).
- **23:15** — The Greek reads 'for I have heard' (ἀκήκοα γάρ) without explicitly mentioning 'land', and specifies the currency as 'didrachmas' (δίδραχμα; also in v. 16) rather than Hebrew shekels.
