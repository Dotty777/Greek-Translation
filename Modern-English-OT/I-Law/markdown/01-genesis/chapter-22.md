## Text

**22:1** And it happened after these things that God tested Abraham, and said to him, "Abraham, Abraham." And he said, "Here I am."

**22:2** And He said, "Take your beloved son, whom you have loved, Isaac, and go to the high land, and offer him up there as a whole burnt offering on one of the mountains of which I will tell you."

**22:3** So Abraham rose in the morning and saddled his donkey. And he took with him two servants and Isaac his son, and having split wood for a whole burnt offering, he rose and went. And he came to the place of which God had told him on the third day.

**22:4** And Abraham looked up with his eyes and saw the place from a distance.

**22:5** And Abraham said to his servants, "Sit here with the donkey, while I and the child go over there; and having worshiped, we will return to you."

**22:6** And Abraham took the wood of the whole burnt offering and laid it on Isaac his son; and he also took the fire in his hand and the knife, and the two of them went together.

**22:7** And Isaac spoke to Abraham his father, saying, "Father." And he said, "What is it, child?" And he said, "Look, the fire and the wood; where is the sheep for the whole burnt offering?"

**22:8** And Abraham said, "God will see for Himself a sheep for a whole burnt offering, child." And both of them, going together,

**22:9** came to the place of which God had told him. And there Abraham built an altar and laid the wood on it; and having bound the feet of Isaac his son, he laid him on the altar on top of the wood.

**22:10** And Abraham stretched out his hand to take the knife, to slaughter his son.

**22:11** And the angel of the Lord called to him from heaven and said to him, "Abraham, Abraham." And he said, "Here I am."

**22:12** And he said, "Do not lay your hand on the boy, nor do anything to him; for now I know that you fear God, and have not spared your beloved son because of Me."

**22:13** And Abraham looked up with his eyes and saw, and look, one ram caught by its horns in a sabek plant. And Abraham went and took the ram, and offered it up as a whole burnt offering in place of Isaac his son.

**22:14** And Abraham called the name of that place, "The Lord Saw," so that they say today, "On the mountain the Lord appeared."

**22:15** And the angel of the Lord called to Abraham a second time from heaven,

**22:16** saying, "By Myself I have sworn, says the Lord: because you have done this thing and have not spared your beloved son because of Me,

**22:17** surely blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply your offspring like the stars of heaven and like the sand along the shore of the sea; and your offspring will inherit the cities of the adversaries.

**22:18** and in your offspring all the nations will be blessed, because you obeyed My voice."

**22:19** So Abraham returned to his servants, and rising up, they went to the Well of the Oath; and Abraham lived at the Well of the Oath.

**22:20** Now it happened after these things that it was reported to Abraham, saying, "Look, Milcah also has borne sons to your brother Nahor:

**22:21** Uz the firstborn, and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of the Syrians,

**22:22** and Chesed, and Hazo, and Pildash, and Jidlaph, and Bethuel."

**22:23** And Bethuel fathered Rebekah. These are the eight sons whom Milcah bore to Nahor, the brother of Abraham.

**22:24** And his concubine, whose name was Reera, also bore Tebah, and Taam, and Tahash, and Maacah.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Greek reads "your beloved son" (for Hebrew "your only one") and translates "the land of Moriah" as "the high land."
- **22:3** — The Greek places "on the third day" at the end of v. 3, attaching it to Abraham's arrival at the place rather than to the start of v. 4.
- **22:13** — The Greek reads "one ram" caught in "a sabek plant," transliterating the Hebrew word for "thicket" (sebak) as a plant name.
- **22:24** — The Greek text preserves the distinct name forms "Reera" (for Hebrew Reumah) and "Taam" (for Hebrew Gaham); in v. 21 it also renders "Aram" as "the Syrians."
