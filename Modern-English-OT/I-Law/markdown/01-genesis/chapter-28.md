## Text

**28:1** Then Isaac summoned Jacob and blessed him, and commanded him, saying, "You must not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan.

**28:2** Rise, flee to Mesopotamia of Syria, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and take for yourself from there a wife from the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother.

**28:3** And may my God bless you and make you grow and multiply you, and you will become assemblies of nations;

**28:4** and may He give you the blessing of Abraham your father, to you and to your seed after you, to inherit the land of your sojourning, which God has given to Abraham."

**28:5** So Isaac sent Jacob away, and he went to Mesopotamia, to Laban the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

**28:6** Now Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and that he had departed to Mesopotamia of Syria to take a wife for himself, as he blessed him and commanded him, saying, "You must not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan,"

**28:7** and that Jacob listened to his father and his mother and went to Mesopotamia.

**28:8** And Esau saw that the daughters of Canaan were displeasing before Isaac his father,

**28:9** so Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalath, the daughter of Ishmael the son of Abraham, the sister of Nebaioth, as a wife in addition to his wives.

**28:10** And Jacob went out from the Well of the Oath and went toward Haran.

**28:11** And he came upon a place and lay down to sleep there, for the sun had set; and he took one of the stones of the place and put it at his head, and he lay down to sleep in that place.

**28:12** And he dreamed, and look, a ladder was fixed on the earth, whose top reached into heaven; and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it.

**28:13** And the Lord stood firmly upon it and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. Do not be afraid: the land on which you are sleeping, to you I will give it and to your seed.

**28:14** And your seed will be like the sand of the sea, and will spread out toward the sea and toward the south and toward the north and toward the east; and in you all the tribes of the earth will be blessed, and in your seed.

**28:15** And look, I am with you, guarding you along the entire way wherever you may go, and I will bring you back to this land; for I will by no means forsake you until I have done all that I have spoken to you."

**28:16** Then Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, "The Lord is in this place, and I did not know it."

**28:17** And he was afraid and said, "How fearful is this place! This is nothing other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven."

**28:18** And Jacob rose in the morning, and took the stone that he had put there at his head and set it up as a pillar, and poured oil on the top of it.

**28:19** And Jacob called the name of that place House of God; and Oulammaus was the name of the city previously.

**28:20** And Jacob vowed a vow, saying, "If the Lord God is with me, and guards me on this way that I am going, and gives me bread to eat and a garment to put on,

**28:21** and brings me back in safety to the house of my father, then the Lord will be God to me;

**28:22** and this stone, which I have set up as a pillar, will be a house of God to me; and of all things that You may give me, I will surely tithe a tenth of them to You."

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — The Septuagint reads "my God" rather than "God Almighty" (Hebrew El Shaddai).
- **28:10** — The Septuagint translates the place name Beersheba literally as "the Well of the Oath."
- **28:14** — The Septuagint compares Jacob's offspring to "the sand of the sea" (Hebrew Masoretic Text: "the dust of the earth") and lists the compass directions as sea (west), south, north, and east.
- **28:19** — The Septuagint translates Bethel as "House of God" and gives the city's former name as "Oulammaus," which reflects a conflation of the Hebrew particle "ulam" ("however") with the city name "Luz."
