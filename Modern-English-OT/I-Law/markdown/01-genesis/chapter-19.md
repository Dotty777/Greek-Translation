## Text

**19:1** Now the two angels came to Sodom in the evening, and Lot was sitting beside the gate of Sodom. When Lot saw them, he rose up to meet them and bowed down with his face to the ground.

**19:2** And he said, "Look, lords, turn aside to the house of your servant and stay the night, and wash your feet; and rising early, you will depart on your way." But they said, "No, rather we will stay the night in the square."

**19:3** And he strongly urged them, and they turned aside to him. And they entered into his house, and he made a banquet for them and baked unleavened loaves for them, and they ate

**19:4** before lying down. And the men of the city, the Sodomites, surrounded the house, from young man to old man, all the people together.

**19:5** And they were calling out for Lot and saying to him, "Where are the men who came in to you during the night? Bring them out to us, so that we may have relations with them."

**19:6** Then Lot went out to them, and shut the door behind him.

**19:7** And he said to them, "By no means, brothers, do not act wickedly.

**19:8** Now I have two daughters who have not known a man; I will bring them out to you, and treat them as it pleases you; only do nothing unjust to these men, because they have come under the shelter of my roof beams."

**19:9** But they said, "Stand back over there! You came in to reside as a foreigner; surely not also to pass judgment? Now therefore we will harm you more than them." And they pressed very hard against the man, Lot, and came near to break down the door.

**19:10** But the men reached out their hands and pulled Lot inside to themselves into the house, and shut the door of the house.

**19:11** And the men who were at the door of the house they struck with blindness, from small to great; and they became exhausted searching for the door.

**19:12** Then the men said to Lot, "Do you have anyone here—sons-in-law, or sons, or daughters, or if you have anyone else in the city—bring them out of this place,

**19:13** because we are destroying this place, since their outcry has risen high before the Lord, and the Lord has sent us to wipe it out."

**19:14** So Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law who had taken his daughters, and said, "Get up and go out from this place, because the Lord is wiping out the city." But he seemed to be joking before his sons-in-law.

**19:15** Now when dawn was coming, the angels were hurrying Lot, saying, "Get up, take your wife and your two daughters whom you have, and go out, so that you do not perish together with the lawless deeds of the city."

**19:16** And they were troubled; and the angels took hold of their hand, and the hand of his wife, and the hands of his two daughters, because the Lord spared him.

**19:17** And it happened, when they had brought them outside, that they said, "Be sure to save your own life; do not look around behind you, nor stop in all the surrounding region; get to safety in the mountain, so that you are not swept away with them."

**19:18** And Lot said to them, "I beg you, lord,

**19:19** since your servant has found mercy before you, and you have magnified your righteousness, which you are doing toward me, so that my soul may live; yet I will not be able to reach safety in the mountain, for fear that the evils overtake me and I die;

**19:20** look, this city is near for me to flee there, which is small; there I will be saved. Is it not small? And my soul will live because of you."

**19:21** And he said to him, "Look, I have shown regard for your face in this matter also, so as not to overthrow the city about which you spoke;

**19:22** hurry, therefore, to reach safety there; for I will not be able to do anything until you arrive there." Therefore he named the name of that city Zoar.

**19:23** The sun came out upon the earth, and Lot entered into Zoar,

**19:24** and the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah sulfur and fire from the Lord out of heaven,

**19:25** and He overthrew these cities in which Lot was dwelling, and all the surrounding region, and all those dwelling in the cities, and all things springing up from the earth.

**19:26** And his wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

**19:27** Now Abraham rose early in the morning to go to the place where he had stood before the Lord,

**19:28** and looked toward the face of Sodom and Gomorrah and toward the face of the land of the surrounding region, and he saw; and look, a flame of the earth was rising up like the vapor of a furnace.

**19:29** And it happened, when the Lord wiped out all the cities of the surrounding region, that God remembered Abraham and sent Lot out from the midst of the overthrow, when the Lord overthrew the cities in which Lot was dwelling.

**19:30** And Lot went out from Zoar and settled in the mountain, and his two daughters with him, for he was afraid to dwell in Zoar; and he dwelt in the cave, he and his two daughters with him.

**19:31** Then the older one said to the younger one, "Our father is old, and there is no one on the earth who will come in to us as is fitting for all the earth.

**19:32** Come, therefore, let us give our father wine to drink and let us lie with him, and let us raise up seed from our father."

**19:33** So they gave their father wine to drink on that night, and the older one went in and lay with her father during that night; and he did not know when she lay down or when she rose up.

**19:34** And it happened on the next day that the older one said to the younger one, "Look, yesterday I lay with our father; let us give him wine to drink this night also, and go in and lie with him, and let us raise up seed from our father."

**19:35** So on that night also they gave their father wine to drink, and the younger one went in and lay with her father; and he did not know when she lay down or when she rose up.

**19:36** And the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.

**19:37** And the older one gave birth to a son, and called his name Moab, saying, "From my father." He is the father of the Moabites to the present day.

**19:38** And the younger one also gave birth to a son, and called his name Amman, the son of my kindred; he is the father of the Ammanites to the present day.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — In the Greek text, "before lying down" concludes the preceding sentence ("and they ate before lying down"), whereas the Hebrew MT attaches it to the following clause ("Before they lay down, the men of the city...").
- **19:5** — The Greek uses the verb synginomai ("be with" / "have relations with"), a standard Greek euphemism for sexual intercourse, rather than translating the Hebrew idiom "know" literally.
- **19:16** — The Greek reads plural "they were troubled" (where the Hebrew MT has singular "he lingered") and plural "their hand" alongside the singular possessives "his wife" and "his two daughters."
- **19:25** — The Greek includes the clause "in which Lot was dwelling" (echoing 19:29), which is absent from the Hebrew MT.
- **19:38** — The Greek names the child "Amman" (with "the son of my kindred" as an explanatory appositive) and his descendants "Ammanites," corresponding to Hebrew "Ben-Ammi" and "Ammonites."
