## Text

**31:1** Now Jacob heard the words of the sons of Laban, saying, "Jacob has taken everything that belonged to our father, and from what belonged to our father he has made all this glory."

**31:2** And Jacob saw the face of Laban, and look, it was not toward him as yesterday and the day before.

**31:3** Then the Lord said to Jacob, "Return to the land of your father and to your family, and I will be with you."

**31:4** So Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field where the flocks were,

**31:5** and said to them, "I see the face of your father, that it is not with me as yesterday and the day before; yet the God of my father was with me.

**31:6** And you yourselves know that with all my strength I have served your father.

**31:7** Yet your father cheated me and changed my wages of the ten lambs, and God did not allow him to harm me.

**31:8** If he spoke like this, 'The speckled ones will be your wages,' then all the sheep would bear speckled ones; and if he said, 'The white ones will be your wages,' then all the sheep would bear white ones.

**31:9** And God took away all the livestock of your father and gave them to me.

**31:10** And it came about at the time when the sheep were conceiving, that I saw with my eyes in my sleep, and look, the male goats and the rams were mounting the sheep and the goats, streaked with white, and speckled, and ash-colored dappled.

**31:11** And the angel of God said to me in a dream, 'Jacob, Jacob.' And I said, 'What is it?'

**31:12** And He said, 'Look up with your eyes and see the male goats and the rams mounting the sheep and the goats, streaked with white, and speckled, and ash-colored dappled; for I have seen all that Laban is doing to you.

**31:13** I am the God who appeared to you in the place where you anointed a pillar to Me there and vowed a vow to Me there. Now therefore, arise and go out from this land, and go to the land of your birth, and I will be with you.'"

**31:14** And Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, "Is there still any portion or inheritance for us in the house of our father?

**31:15** Have we not been counted as foreigners by him? For he has sold us and has completely devoured our silver.

**31:16** All the wealth and the glory that God has taken away from our father will belong to us and to our children. Now therefore, do whatever God has said to you."

**31:17** Then Jacob arose and took his wives and his children onto the camels,

**31:18** and led away all his possessions and all his goods that he had acquired in Mesopotamia, and all that was his, to go to Isaac his father to the land of Canaan.

**31:19** Now Laban had gone to shear his sheep, and Rachel stole the idols of her father.

**31:20** And Jacob concealed it from Laban the Syrian, not telling him that he was fleeing.

**31:21** So he fled, he and all that was his, and crossed the river, and hurried toward the mountain of Gilead.

**31:22** Now it was reported to Laban the Syrian on the third day that Jacob had fled.

**31:23** And taking all his brothers with him, he pursued after him a journey of seven days, and overtook him in the mountain of Gilead.

**31:24** And God came to Laban the Syrian in a dream during the night and said to him, "Guard yourself so that you never speak evil things with Jacob."

**31:25** And Laban overtook Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent on the mountain, and Laban stationed his brothers on the mountain of Gilead.

**31:26** Then Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done? Why did you flee secretly and rob me, and carry off my daughters like captives of the sword?

**31:27** And if you had told me, I would have sent you away with gladness and with music, tambourines, and lyre.

**31:28** I was not counted worthy to kiss my children and my daughters. Now you have acted foolishly.

**31:29** And now my hand has strength to harm you; but the God of your father spoke to me yesterday, saying, 'Guard yourself so that you never speak evil things with Jacob.'

**31:30** Now then, you have gone away, for you deeply longed to go to the house of your father; yet why did you steal my gods?"

**31:31** And Jacob answered and said to Laban, "Because I said, 'Perhaps you might take away your daughters from me and all that is mine.'"

**31:32** And Jacob said to him, "The one with whom you find your gods shall not live before our brothers. Identify what of yours is with me, and take it." And he identified nothing with him; now Jacob did not know that Rachel his wife had stolen them.

**31:33** So Laban went in and searched the house of Leah, and did not find them; and coming out of the house of Leah, he searched the house of Jacob and the house of the two female servants, and did not find them; and he also went into the house of Rachel.

**31:34** Now Rachel had taken the idols and put them into the saddlebags of the camel, and sat on them.

**31:35** And she said to her father, "Do not be displeased, lord; I cannot rise before you, because what is according to the custom of women is upon me." So Laban searched throughout the whole house, and did not find the idols.

**31:36** Then Jacob became angry and quarreled with Laban; and Jacob answered and said to Laban, "What is my wrongdoing and what is my sin, that you have pursued after me?

**31:37** And since you have searched all the articles of my house, what have you found from all the articles of your house? Set it here before your brothers and my brothers, and let them decide between the two of us.

**31:38** These twenty years I have been with you; your sheep and your goats have not miscarried, and rams of your sheep I have not eaten.

**31:39** What was caught by wild beasts I did not bring to you; I paid it back myself, thefts of the day and thefts of the night.

**31:40** By day I was scorched by the heat and by frost at night, and my sleep departed from my eyes.

**31:41** These twenty years I have been in your house; I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years among your sheep, and you defrauded me of my wages by ten ewe-lambs.

**31:42** If the God of my father Abraham and the Fear of Isaac had not been for me, now you would have sent me away empty-handed. God saw my affliction and the labor of my hands, and rebuked you yesterday."

**31:43** And Laban answered and said to Jacob, "Your daughters are my daughters, and your sons are my sons, and your livestock are my livestock, and everything that you see is mine. Yet what can I do today to these daughters of mine, or to their children whom they have borne?

**31:44** Now therefore, come, let us make a covenant, you and I, and it will be for a witness between me and you." And Jacob said to him, "Look, no one is with us; see, God is witness between me and you."

**31:45** Then Jacob took a stone and set it up as a pillar.

**31:46** And Jacob said to his brothers, "Gather stones." So they gathered stones and made a heap, and they ate and drank there on the heap. And Laban said to him, "This heap testifies between me and you today."

**31:47** And Laban called it Witness Heap, while Jacob called it Heap Testifies.

**31:48** And Laban said to Jacob, "Look, this heap and this pillar that I have set up between me and you; this heap testifies, and this pillar testifies." Therefore its name was called Heap Testifies,

**31:49** and The Vision, of which he said, "May God watch between me and you, because we will part one from the other.

**31:50** If you afflict my daughters, if you take wives in addition to my daughters, see, no one is with us.

**31:52** For if I do not cross over to you, neither may you cross over to me past this heap and this pillar for harm.

**31:53** The God of Abraham and the God of Nahor will judge between us." And Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.

**31:54** And Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain, and called his brothers, and they ate and drank, and slept on the mountain.

## Translator Notes

- **31:7** — Greek ton deka amnon ("of the ten lambs"; cf. deka amnasin, "ten ewe-lambs," in v. 41) reflects the Septuagint reading of the Hebrew expression elsewhere rendered "ten times."
- **31:33** — The Septuagint uses oikos ("house") rather than "tent" in vv. 33–37 and orders Laban's search differently from the Masoretic Text, beginning with Leah's house before Jacob's and that of the two female servants.
- **31:47** — Rather than transliterating the Aramaic and Hebrew names (Jegar-sahadutha and Galeed), the Septuagint translates both into Greek as Bounos martys ("Witness Heap") and Bounos martyrei ("Heap Testifies").
- **31:49** — The Septuagint translates the place name Mizpah into Greek as He horasis ("The Vision"); in this Greek text, the material corresponding to Masoretic v. 51 is integrated into vv. 48–50 and 52, so verse numbering skips from v. 50 to v. 52.
