## Text

**29:1** And Jacob lifted up his feet and went to the land of the east, to Laban the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

**29:2** And he looked, and see, there was a well in the plain; and there were three flocks of sheep resting there together, for from that well they used to water the flocks, and a large stone was on the mouth of the well.

**29:3** And all the flocks would be gathered there, and they would roll the stone away from the mouth of the well, and water the sheep, and put the stone back on the mouth of the well in its place.

**29:4** And Jacob said to them, "Brothers, where are you from?" And they said, "We are from Haran."

**29:5** And he said to them, "Do you know Laban the son of Nahor?" And they said, "We know him."

**29:6** And he said to them, "Is he well?" And they said, "He is well." While he was still speaking, look, Rachel his daughter was coming with her father's sheep, for she herself was pasturing her father's sheep.

**29:7** And Jacob said, "There is still much day left; it is not yet time for the livestock to be gathered. Water the sheep, and go pasture them."

**29:8** And they said, "We will not be able to until all the shepherds are gathered and they roll the stone away from the mouth of the well, and we will water the sheep."

**29:9** While he was still speaking to them, Rachel the daughter of Laban was coming with her father's sheep, for she herself was pasturing her father's sheep.

**29:10** And it happened when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban, his mother's brother, that Jacob came near and rolled the stone away from the mouth of the well, and watered the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother.

**29:11** And Jacob kissed Rachel, and cried out with his voice and wept.

**29:12** And he told Rachel that he was her father's brother and that he was Rebekah's son. And she ran and reported to her father according to these words.

**29:13** And it happened when Laban heard the name of Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him, and embraced him and kissed him, and brought him into his house. And he recounted to Laban all these matters.

**29:14** And Laban said to him, "You are of my bones and of my flesh." And he was with him a month of days.

**29:15** And Laban said to Jacob, "Because you are my brother, you will not serve me for nothing; tell me what your wages are."

**29:16** Now Laban had two daughters: the name of the older was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel.

**29:17** And Leah's eyes were weak, but Rachel was lovely in form and beautiful in appearance.

**29:18** Now Jacob loved Rachel, and he said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."

**29:19** And Laban said to him, "It is better for me to give her to you than for me to give her to another man; live with me."

**29:20** And Jacob served seven years for Rachel.

**29:21** And Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days have been fulfilled, so that I may go in to her."

**29:22** And Laban gathered all the men of the place and held a wedding.

**29:23** And evening came, and taking Leah his daughter, he brought her in to Jacob, and Jacob went in to her.

**29:24** And Laban gave his maidservant Zilpah to Leah his daughter as a maidservant for her.

**29:25** And morning came, and look, it was Leah. And Jacob said to Laban, "What is this you have done to me? Did I not serve you for Rachel? And why did you deceive me?"

**29:26** And Laban said, "It is not so in our place, to give the younger before the older;

**29:27** complete then the seven days of this one, and I will give you this one also in return for the work that you will work with me yet another seven years."

**29:28** And Jacob did so, and fulfilled the seven days of this one; and Laban gave Rachel his daughter to him as a wife.

**29:29** And Laban gave his maidservant Bilhah to Rachel his daughter as a maidservant for her.

**29:30** And he went in to Rachel, and he loved Rachel more than Leah; and he served him another seven years.

**29:31** Now when the Lord God saw that Leah was hated, He opened her womb; but Rachel was barren.

**29:32** And Leah conceived and bore a son to Jacob, and she called his name Reuben, saying, "Because the Lord has seen my humiliation; now my husband will love me."

**29:33** And Leah conceived again and bore a second son to Jacob, and she said, "The Lord has heard that I am hated, and He has given me this one also." And she called his name Simeon.

**29:34** And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, "At the present time my husband will be on my side, for I have borne him three sons." Therefore his name was called Levi.

**29:35** And conceiving again, she bore a son, and she said, "Now yet this time I will give thanks to the Lord." Therefore she called his name Judah. And she stopped giving birth.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Septuagint expands Jacob's destination beyond the Hebrew text by adding "to Laban the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau"; Greek "Syrian" (Syros) regularly translates Hebrew "Aramean."
- **29:6** — Unlike the Hebrew text, where the shepherds point out Rachel's arrival, the Septuagint shifts to narration with "While he was still speaking" and anticipates the description of Rachel approaching with her father's sheep repeated in verse 9.
- **29:20** — The Septuagint concludes the verse here without the clause present in the Masoretic Text ("and they seemed to him like a few days because of his love for her").
- **29:27** — Greek "ta hebdoma" (literally "the sevens") refers to the seven-day bridal week of Leah's wedding celebration.
- **29:34** — Greek "pros emou" ("on my side" or "attached to me") reflects the Hebrew wordplay on Levi ("joined"), and the Septuagint uses the passive verb "was called" rather than "she called."
