## Text

**32:1** Now Laban rose in the morning, kissed his sons and his daughters, and blessed them; and Laban turned back and departed to his place.

**32:2** And Jacob went away on his own way; and looking up with his eyes, he saw a camp of God encamped, and the angels of God met him.

**32:3** And Jacob said, when he saw them, "This is a camp of God"; and he called the name of that place Camps.

**32:4** Now Jacob sent messengers to Esau his brother, into the land of Seir, into the country of Edom,

**32:5** and commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall say to my lord Esau: 'Thus says your servant Jacob: I have sojourned with Laban and lingered until now;

**32:6** and I have acquired cattle and donkeys and sheep, and male servants and female servants; and I have sent to report to my lord Esau, so that your servant may find favor before you.'"

**32:7** And the messengers returned to Jacob, saying, "We came to your brother Esau, and he is coming to meet you, and four hundred men are with him."

**32:8** Then Jacob was exceedingly afraid and was at a loss; and he divided the people who were with him, and the cattle and the sheep, into two camps.

**32:9** And Jacob said, "If Esau comes to one camp and cuts it down, the second camp will be saved."

**32:10** Then Jacob said, "God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, Lord, who said to me, 'Hurry back to the land of your birth, and I will treat you well';

**32:11** You are sufficient for me from all the righteousness and from all the truth that You have done for Your servant; for with my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.

**32:12** Deliver me from the hand of my brother Esau, for I am afraid of him, for fear that he may come and strike me, and mother with children.

**32:13** Yet You said, 'I will surely treat you well, and I will make your seed like the sand of the sea, which will not be numbered because of its multitude.'"

**32:14** And he slept there that night. And from what he was bringing he took gifts and sent them out to Esau his brother:

**32:15** two hundred female goats, twenty male goats, two hundred sheep, twenty rams,

**32:16** thirty nursing camels and their young, forty cows, ten bulls, twenty donkeys, ten colts.

**32:17** And he delivered them into the hand of his servants, each flock by itself. And he said to his servants, "Go on ahead before me, and put a space between flock and flock."

**32:18** And he commanded the first, saying, "If my brother Esau meets you and asks you, saying, 'Whose are you? And where are you going? And whose are these going ahead of you?'

**32:19** then you shall say, 'Your servant Jacob's; he has sent gifts to my lord Esau, and look, he himself is behind us.'"

**32:20** And he commanded the first and the second and the third and all those proceeding behind these flocks, saying, "According to this word speak to Esau when you find him,

**32:21** and you shall say, 'Look, your servant Jacob is coming behind us.'" For he said, "I will appease his face with the gifts going ahead of him, and after this I will see his face; for perhaps he will accept my face."

**32:22** So the gifts went on ahead before his face; and he himself slept that night in the camp.

**32:23** Now rising up that night, he took the two wives and the two female servants and his eleven children, and crossed the ford of the Jabbok.

**32:24** And he took them and crossed the stream, and he sent across everything that was his.

**32:25** And Jacob was left behind alone, and a man wrestled with him until morning.

**32:26** And he saw that he could not prevail against him, and he touched the flat of his thigh, and the flat of Jacob's thigh grew numb as he wrestled with him.

**32:27** And he said to him, "Let me go, for the dawn has risen." But he said, "I will by no means let you go unless you bless me."

**32:28** And he said to him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Jacob."

**32:29** And he said to him, "Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name; for you have prevailed with God, and with men you are powerful."

**32:30** Then Jacob asked and said, "Tell me your name." And he said, "Why do you ask my name?" And he blessed him there.

**32:31** And Jacob called the name of that place Appearance of God, "For I saw God face to face, and my soul was saved."

**32:32** Now the sun rose upon him when he passed by the Appearance of God; and he himself was limping on his thigh.

**32:33** For on account of this the sons of Israel will not eat the sinew that grew numb, which is on the flat of Jacob's thigh, until this day, because he touched the flat of Jacob's thigh, at the sinew, and it grew numb.

## Translator Notes

- **32:2** — The Greek includes "looking up with his eyes, he saw a camp of God encamped" before the angels meet Jacob, and in 32:3 translates Hebrew Mahanaim as Parembolai ("Camps").
- **32:11** — The Greek reads hikanousai moi ("You are sufficient for me", a textual variant alongside hikanoutai moi, "it is enough for me") where the Hebrew text reads "I am unworthy of all the steadfast love and faithfulness."
- **32:26** — The Greek describes "the flat of his thigh" growing "numb" (enarkesen, also in 32:33) rather than being dislocated as in the Hebrew text.
- **32:31** — The Greek translates Hebrew Peniel as Eidos theou ("Appearance of God", and "the Appearance of God" in 32:32), creating a wordplay with eidon ("I saw").
