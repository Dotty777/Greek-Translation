## Text

**11:1** And all the earth was of one lip, and there was one speech for all.

**11:2** And it happened as they moved from the east that they found a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.

**11:3** And a man said to his neighbor, "Come, let us make bricks and bake them with fire." And brick became stone for them, and asphalt was mortar for them.

**11:4** And they said, "Come, let us build for ourselves a city and a tower whose top will be up to heaven, and we will make a name for ourselves before being scattered over the face of all the earth."

**11:5** And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower that the sons of men built.

**11:6** And the Lord said, "Look, there is one people and one lip of all; and this they have begun to do, and now nothing will fail them of all that they set themselves to do.

**11:7** Come, let Us go down and confuse their tongue there, so that each one may not understand the speech of his neighbor."

**11:8** And the Lord scattered them from there over the face of all the earth, and they stopped building the city and the tower.

**11:9** Because of this its name was called Confusion, because there the Lord confused the lips of all the earth, and from there the Lord God scattered them over the face of all the earth.

**11:10** And these are the generations of Shem. Shem was one hundred years old when he fathered Arphaxad, in the second year after the flood.

**11:11** And Shem lived five hundred years after he fathered Arphaxad, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:12** And Arphaxad lived one hundred thirty-five years, and he fathered Cainan.

**11:13** And Arphaxad lived four hundred thirty years after he fathered Cainan, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died. And Cainan lived one hundred thirty years, and he fathered Shelah. And Cainan lived three hundred thirty years after he fathered Shelah, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:14** And Shelah lived one hundred thirty years, and he fathered Eber.

**11:15** And Shelah lived three hundred thirty years after he fathered Eber, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:16** And Eber lived one hundred thirty-four years, and he fathered Peleg.

**11:17** And Eber lived three hundred seventy years after he fathered Peleg, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:18** And Peleg lived one hundred thirty years, and he fathered Reu.

**11:19** And Peleg lived two hundred nine years after he fathered Reu, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:20** And Reu lived one hundred thirty-two years, and he fathered Serug.

**11:21** And Reu lived two hundred seven years after he fathered Serug, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:22** And Serug lived one hundred thirty years, and he fathered Nahor.

**11:23** And Serug lived two hundred years after he fathered Nahor, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:24** And Nahor lived seventy-nine years, and he fathered Terah.

**11:25** And Nahor lived one hundred twenty-nine years after he fathered Terah, and he fathered sons and daughters, and he died.

**11:26** And Terah lived seventy years, and he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

**11:27** Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot.

**11:28** And Haran died in the presence of Terah his father in the land in which he was born, in the country of the Chaldeans.

**11:29** And Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves; the name of Abram's wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah, daughter of Haran, both the father of Milcah and the father of Iscah.

**11:30** And Sarai was barren and did not bear children.

**11:31** And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, the son of his son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of his son, and he led them out from the country of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan; and he came as far as Haran and settled there.

**11:32** And the days of Terah in Haran were two hundred five years, and Terah died in Haran.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Greek cheilos literally means "lip," preserving the Hebrew idiom for "language" (likewise in 11:6 and 11:9).
- **11:4** — The Greek reads "before being scattered" (pro tou diaspareinai), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "lest we be scattered."
- **11:9** — The Septuagint translates the name into Greek as Synchysis ("Confusion") rather than transliterating "Babel," and in 11:8 adds "and the tower."
- **11:13** — The Septuagint includes Cainan as a generation between Arphaxad and Shelah (cf. Luke 3:36) and gives longer ages before fatherhood across 11:12-25 than the Masoretic Text.
- **11:28** — The Greek reads "the country of the Chaldeans" (also in 11:31) rather than "Ur of the Chaldeans" as in the Masoretic Text.
