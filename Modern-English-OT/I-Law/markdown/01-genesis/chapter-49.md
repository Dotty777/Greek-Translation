## Text

**49:1** Now Jacob called his sons and said to them, "Gather together, so that I may declare to you what will happen to you in the last days.

**49:2** Gather together and listen to me, sons of Jacob; listen to Israel, listen to your father.

**49:3** Reuben, my firstborn, you are my strength and the beginning of my children; hard to bear and hard, self-willed.

**49:4** You acted insolently like water; do not boil over! For you went up onto your father's bed; then you defiled the couch where you went up.

**49:5** Simeon and Levi are brothers; they accomplished injustice by their own choice.

**49:6** Into their council may my soul not enter, and at their gathering may my liver not contend; because in their wrath they killed men, and in their passion they hamstrung a bull.

**49:7** Cursed is their wrath because it is self-willed, and their fury because it has been hardened; I will divide them in Jacob, and I will scatter them in Israel.

**49:8** Judah, may your brothers praise you; your hands will be on the back of your enemies; the sons of your father will bow down to you.

**49:9** Judah is a lion's cub; from a shoot, my son, you have gone up. Lying down, you slept like a lion and like a cub; who will rouse him?

**49:10** A ruler will not fail from Judah, nor a leader from his thighs, until the things reserved for him come, and he is the expectation of nations.

**49:11** Tying his colt to a vine, and the colt of his donkey to the tendril, he will wash his robe in wine, and his cloak in the blood of the grape.

**49:12** His eyes are more gladdening than wine, and his teeth are whiter than milk.

**49:13** Zebulun will dwell by the sea, and he himself beside a harbor of ships, and he will extend as far as Sidon.

**49:14** Issachar desired what is good, resting in the midst of the allotments;

**49:15** and seeing the resting place, that it was good, and the land, that it was rich, he put his shoulder under to labor and became a farming man.

**49:16** Dan will judge his own people, just like one tribe in Israel.

**49:17** And let Dan become a serpent on a road, lying in wait upon a path, biting a horse's heel, and the rider will fall backward,

**49:18** waiting for the salvation of the Lord.

**49:19** Gad, a raiding band will raid him, but he himself will raid them at their heels.

**49:20** Asher, rich is his bread, and he himself will provide delicacies for rulers.

**49:21** Naphtali is a spreading trunk, yielding beauty in its produce.

**49:22** Joseph is a grown son, my enviable grown son, my youngest son; return to me,

**49:23** against whom, plotting together, they hurled insults, and masters of arrows held a grudge against him.

**49:24** And their bows were shattered with might, and the sinews of the arms of their hand were loosened by the hand of the Mighty One of Jacob; from there is the one who strengthened Israel, from the God of your father.

**49:25** And my God helped you, and He blessed you with a blessing of heaven above, and a blessing of the earth that holds all things, on account of the blessing of breasts and womb,

**49:26** the blessing of your father and your mother; it has prevailed over the blessings of enduring mountains and over the blessings of everlasting hills. They will be upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the brothers whom he led.

**49:27** Benjamin is a ravenous wolf; in the morning he will still eat, and toward evening he gives food."

**49:28** All these are the twelve sons of Jacob, and these things their father spoke to them; and their father blessed them, each one according to his own blessing he blessed them.

**49:29** And he said to them, "I am being added to my people. Bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,

**49:30** in the double cave that is opposite Mamre in the land of Canaan, the cave that Abraham acquired from Ephron the Hittite as a possession for a tomb.

**49:31** There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; there they buried Leah,

**49:32** in the possession of the field and of the cave that is in it, from the sons of Heth."

**49:33** And Jacob finished instructing his sons, and lifting up his feet onto the bed, he expired and was added to his people.

## Translator Notes

- **49:6** — The Greek reads "my liver" (ta hepata mou), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew consonants as kaved ("liver") rather than kavod ("glory").
- **49:10** — The Greek reads "the things reserved for him" (ta apokeimena auto), understanding the Hebrew Shiloh as shelo ("that which belongs to him"), and renders the final clause as "the expectation of nations."
- **49:14** — The Greek reads "desired what is good" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "a strong donkey," and translates the final noun as "allotments" (kleroi).
- **49:21** — The Greek reads the Hebrew consonants as a "trunk" (stelechos) yielding beauty in its produce, rather than the Masoretic Text's "doe let loose" bearing beautiful fawns.
- **49:24** — The Greek reads "their bows were shattered with might" and "the one who strengthened Israel" (ho katischysas Israel, which may be understood either as a divine title parallel to "the Mighty One of Jacob," i.e., "the One who strengthened Israel," or as referring to Joseph), differing from the Masoretic Text's "his bow remained steady" and "the Shepherd, the Stone of Israel."
