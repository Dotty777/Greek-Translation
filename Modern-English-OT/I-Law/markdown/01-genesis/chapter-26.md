## Text

**26:1** Now a famine occurred on the land, apart from the earlier famine that happened in the time of Abraham; and Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines, to Gerar.

**26:2** And the Lord appeared to him and said, "Do not go down to Egypt; rather, settle in the land that I tell you.

**26:3** And sojourn in this land, and I will be with you and bless you; for to you and to your seed I will give all this land, and I will establish My oath that I swore to Abraham your father.

**26:4** And I will multiply your seed like the stars of heaven, and I will give to your seed all this land, and in your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed,

**26:5** because Abraham your father obeyed My voice and kept My ordinances and My commandments and My statutes and My laws."

**26:6** And Isaac settled in Gerar.

**26:7** And the men of the place asked about Rebekah his wife, and he said, "She is my sister," for he was afraid to say, "She is my wife," for fear that the men of the place might kill him on account of Rebekah, because she was beautiful in appearance.

**26:8** And he stayed there a long time; and Abimelech the king of Gerar, peering through the window, saw Isaac playing with Rebekah his wife.

**26:9** And Abimelech called Isaac and said to him, "So then, she is your wife! Why did you say, 'She is my sister'?" And Isaac said to him, "Because I said, 'I might die on account of her.'"

**26:10** And Abimelech said to him, "What is this you have done to us? One of my kindred nearly slept with your wife, and you would have brought ignorance upon us."

**26:11** And Abimelech commanded all his people, saying, "Everyone who touches this man or his wife will be liable to death."

**26:12** And Isaac sowed in that land, and in that year he found a hundredfold of barley; and the Lord blessed him.

**26:13** And the man was exalted, and advancing he kept becoming greater until he became very great.

**26:14** And he had livestock of sheep and livestock of cattle and many tilled fields; and the Philistines envied him.

**26:15** And all the wells that the servants of his father had dug in the time of his father, the Philistines stopped them up and filled them with earth.

**26:16** And Abimelech said to Isaac, "Depart from us, because you have become much more powerful than we."

**26:17** And Isaac departed from there and encamped in the valley of Gerar, and settled there.

**26:18** And Isaac again dug the wells of water that the servants of Abraham his father had dug, and which the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham his father died; and he gave them names according to the names that Abraham his father had named them.

**26:19** And the servants of Isaac dug in the valley of Gerar, and found there a well of living water.

**26:20** And the shepherds of Gerar quarreled with the shepherds of Isaac, claiming that the water was theirs; and he called the name of that well Injustice, for they treated him unjustly.

**26:21** And departing from there, Isaac dug another well; and they disputed about that one also, and he gave it the name Enmity.

**26:22** And departing from there, he dug another well, and they did not quarrel over it; and he gave it the name Broad Space, saying, "Because now the Lord has made room for us and has increased us on the land."

**26:23** And he went up from there to the Well of the Oath.

**26:24** And the Lord appeared to him in that night and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not be afraid, for I am with you, and I will bless you and multiply your seed for the sake of Abraham your father."

**26:25** And he built an altar there and called upon the name of the Lord, and he pitched his tent there; and there the servants of Isaac dug a well.

**26:26** And Abimelech went to him from Gerar, along with Ahuzzath his bridal attendant and Phicol the commander of his army.

**26:27** And Isaac said to them, "Why have you come to me? Yet you hated me and sent me away from you."

**26:28** And they said, "We have clearly seen that the Lord was with you, and we said, 'Let there be a curse between us and you, and we will make a covenant with you

**26:29** not to do evil to us, just as we did not detest you, and in the way we treated you well and sent you away with peace.' And now you are blessed by the Lord."

**26:30** And he made a feast for them, and they ate and drank.

**26:31** And rising up in the morning, they swore each man to his neighbor; and Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in safety.

**26:32** And it came about on that day that the servants of Isaac arrived and reported to him about the well that they had dug, and they said, "We did not find water."

**26:33** And he called its name Oath; therefore he called the name of the city Well of Oath, to the present day.

**26:34** Now Esau was forty years old, and he took as a wife Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hivite;

**26:35** and they were quarreling with Isaac and Rebekah.

## Translator Notes

- **26:10** — Greek agnoian literally means "ignorance," used in the Septuagint for an unwitting sin or cultic guilt (corresponding to Hebrew "guilt").
- **26:12** — The Greek text specifies "a hundredfold of barley," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants ("barley" rather than "measures").
- **26:32** — The Greek text explicitly reads "We did not find water" (ouch heuromen hydor), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "We have found water."
- **26:34** — The Greek text identifies Elon as "the Hivite" (rather than "the Hittite" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and in verse 35 describes Esau's wives as "quarreling" with Isaac and Rebekah.
