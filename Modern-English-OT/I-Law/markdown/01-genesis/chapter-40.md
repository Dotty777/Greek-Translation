## Text

**40:1** Now it came about after these things that the chief cupbearer of the king of Egypt and the chief baker sinned against their lord, the king of Egypt.

**40:2** And Pharaoh was angry with his two eunuchs, with the chief cupbearer and with the chief baker;

**40:3** and he put them in custody with the chief jailer, into the prison, into the place where Joseph had been led away.

**40:4** And the chief warden entrusted them to Joseph, and he attended to them; and they were in custody for some days.

**40:5** And both of them saw a dream, each one a dream in one night, a vision of his dream--the chief cupbearer and the chief baker who belonged to the king of Egypt, who were in the prison.

**40:6** Now Joseph came in to them in the morning, and he saw them, and they were troubled.

**40:7** And he asked the eunuchs of Pharaoh who were with him in custody with his master, saying, "Why is it that your faces are gloomy today?"

**40:8** And they said to him, "We have seen a dream, and there is no one to interpret it." And Joseph said to them, "Is not their explanation through God? Tell me, then."

**40:9** And the chief cupbearer told his dream to Joseph and said, "In my sleep there was a vine before me,

**40:10** and on the vine were three stems; and it was flourishing, having brought forth shoots; the clusters of grapes were ripe.

**40:11** And the cup of Pharaoh was in my hand; and I took the grapes and squeezed them into the cup, and I gave the cup into the hands of Pharaoh."

**40:12** And Joseph said to him, "This is its interpretation: the three stems are three days.

**40:13** Three days more, and Pharaoh will remember your office and restore you to your office of chief cupbearer, and you will give the cup of Pharaoh into his hand according to your former office, as when you were serving as cupbearer.

**40:14** But remember me by yourself when it goes well for you, and show mercy toward me, and make mention of me to Pharaoh, and bring me out of this stronghold.

**40:15** For by theft I was stolen from the land of the Hebrews; and here I have done nothing, yet they threw me into this pit."

**40:16** And the chief baker saw that he had interpreted rightly, and he said to Joseph, "I too saw a dream, and I thought I was carrying three baskets of coarse-wheat loaves on my head;

**40:17** and in the top basket were some of all the foods that King Pharaoh eats, the work of a baker; and the birds of the sky were eating them from the basket on top of my head."

**40:18** And Joseph answered and said to him, "This is its interpretation: the three baskets are three days.

**40:19** Within three days more, Pharaoh will remove your head from you and hang you on a tree, and the birds of the sky will eat your flesh from you."

**40:20** Now it came about on the third day--it was Pharaoh's birthday--that he made a banquet for all his servants; and he remembered the office of the chief cupbearer and the office of the chief baker in the midst of his servants.

**40:21** And he restored the chief cupbearer to his office, and he gave the cup into the hand of Pharaoh;

**40:22** but the chief baker he hanged, just as Joseph had interpreted to them.

**40:23** Yet the chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph, but forgot him.

## Translator Notes

- **40:4** — The Greek uses the rare compound ἀρχιδεσμώτης ("chief warden") here, varying from ἀρχιδεσμοφύλαξ ("chief jailer") in 39:21-23 and 40:3.
- **40:5** — The Greek reads "a vision of his dream" (ὅρασις τοῦ ἐνυπνίου αὐτοῦ), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "each according to the interpretation of his dream."
- **40:13** — Here and in verse 20, the Greek reads "remember your office" (μνησθήσεται τῆς ἀρχῆς σου), interpreting the Hebrew idiom "lift up your head."
- **40:16** — The Greek χονδριτῶν refers specifically to loaves made of coarse wheat or groats, where the Hebrew has "baskets of white bread" or "perforated baskets."
