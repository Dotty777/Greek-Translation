## Text

**24:1** Now Abraham was old, advanced in days, and the Lord blessed Abraham in all things.

**24:2** And Abraham said to his servant, the oldest of his household, who ruled over all that was his, "Put your hand under my thigh,

**24:3** and I will make you swear by the Lord, the God of heaven and of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son Isaac from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell;

**24:4** but you will go to my land where I was born and to my tribe, and you will take a wife for my son Isaac from there."

**24:5** And the servant said to him, "Perhaps the woman will not be willing to go back with me to this land. Should I take your son back to the land from which you came out?"

**24:6** And Abraham said to him, "Be careful that you do not take my son back there.

**24:7** The Lord, the God of heaven and the God of the earth, who took me from my father's house and from the land where I was born, who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, 'To you I will give this land and to your seed,' He Himself will send His angel before you, and you will take a wife for my son Isaac from there.

**24:8** But if the woman is not willing to go with you to this land, you will be clear of this oath; only do not take my son back there."

**24:9** So the servant put his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and swore to him concerning this matter.

**24:10** And the servant took ten camels from his master's camels, and some of all his master's good things with him, and rising up, he went to Mesopotamia, to the city of Nahor.

**24:11** And he made the camels lie down outside the city beside the well of water toward evening, at the time when the women who draw water come out.

**24:12** And he said, "Lord, the God of the master Abraham, grant success before me today, and show mercy to my master Abraham.

**24:13** Look, I am standing at the spring of water, and the daughters of those who dwell in the city are coming out to draw water.

**24:14** And let it be that the young woman to whom I say, 'Tilt your water jar so that I may drink,' and she says to me, 'You drink, and I will water your camels also until they finish drinking,' she is the one You have prepared for Your servant Isaac; and by this I will know that You have shown mercy to my master Abraham."

**24:15** And it happened, before he had finished speaking in his mind, that look, Rebekah was coming out, who was born to Bethuel, the son of Milcah the wife of Nahor, the brother of Abraham, with her water jar on her shoulders.

**24:16** Now the young woman was very beautiful in appearance; she was a virgin; no man had known her. And going down to the spring, she filled her water jar and came up.

**24:17** Then the servant ran to meet her and said, "Give me a little water to drink from your water jar."

**24:18** And she said, "Drink, sir." And she hurried and lowered the water jar onto her arm, and gave him a drink until he finished drinking.

**24:19** And she said, "I will draw water for your camels also until they have all drunk."

**24:20** And she hurried and emptied the water jar into the watering trough, and ran to the well to draw water, and drew water for all the camels.

**24:21** Meanwhile, the man was observing her closely, and kept silent in order to know whether the Lord had made his way successful or not.

**24:22** And it happened, when all the camels had finished drinking, that the man took gold earrings, each a drachma in weight, and two bracelets for her hands; their weight was ten gold pieces.

**24:23** And he questioned her and said, "Whose daughter are you? Tell me: is there a place with your father for us to lodge?"

**24:24** And she said to him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Milcah, whom she bore to him, Nahor."

**24:25** And she said to him, "Both straw and much fodder are with us, and a place to lodge."

**24:26** And being pleased, the man worshiped the Lord and said,

**24:27** "Blessed is the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who has not forsaken righteousness and truth from my master; as for me, the Lord has led me successfully to the house of my master's brother."

**24:28** And the girl ran and reported to her mother's house according to these words.

**24:29** Now Rebekah had a brother whose name was Laban; and Laban ran out to the man at the spring.

**24:30** And it happened, when he saw the earrings and the bracelets on his sister's hands, and when he heard the words of Rebekah his sister, saying, "Thus the man has spoken to me," that he came to the man as he was standing by the camels at the spring;

**24:31** and he said to him, "Come, enter! Blessed is the Lord. Why are you standing outside? For I have prepared the house and a place for the camels."

**24:32** So the man entered the house, and he unloaded the camels; and he gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water for his feet and for the feet of the men who were with him,

**24:33** and he set loaves of bread before them to eat. And he said, "I will certainly not eat until I have spoken my words." And they said, "Speak."

**24:34** And he said, "I am Abraham's servant.

**24:35** And the Lord has blessed my master greatly, and he has been exalted; and He has given him sheep and calves, and silver and gold, male servants and female servants, camels and donkeys.

**24:36** And Sarah, my master's wife, bore one son to my master after he had grown old; and he has given to him all that was his.

**24:37** And my master made me swear, saying, 'You will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I sojourn in their land;

**24:38** but you will go to my father's house and to my tribe, and you will take a wife for my son from there.'

**24:39** And I said to my master, 'Perhaps the woman will not go with me.'

**24:40** And he said to me, 'The Lord God, before whom I have been well-pleasing, He Himself will send out His angel with you and will make your way successful; and you will take a wife for my son from my tribe and from my father's house.

**24:41** Then you will be guiltless of my curse; for when you come to my tribe and they do not give her to you, then you will be guiltless of my oath.'

**24:42** And coming today to the spring, I said, 'Lord, the God of my master Abraham, if You are making my way successful, on which I am now going,

**24:43** look, I am standing at the spring of water, and the daughters of the people of the city will come out to draw water; and let it be that the young woman to whom I say, "Give me a little water to drink from your water jar,"

**24:44** and she says to me, "You drink, and I will draw water for your camels also," she is the woman whom the Lord has prepared for His own servant Isaac; and by this I will know that You have shown mercy to my master Abraham.'

**24:45** And it happened, before I had finished speaking in my mind, immediately Rebekah was coming out with the water jar on her shoulders, and she went down to the spring and drew water. And I said to her, 'Give me a drink.'

**24:46** And hurrying, she lowered the water jar from herself onto her arm and said, 'You drink, and I will water your camels also.' So I drank, and she watered my camels.

**24:47** And I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you? Tell me.' And she said, 'I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.' And I put the earrings on her, and the bracelets on her hands;

**24:48** and being pleased, I worshiped the Lord, and blessed the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who led me successfully in the way of truth to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

**24:49** Therefore, if you are showing mercy and righteousness toward my master, tell me, so that I may turn to the right or to the left."

**24:50** Then Laban and Bethuel answered and said, "This decree has come out from the Lord; therefore we will not be able to speak against you evil for good.

**24:51** Look, Rebekah is before you; take her and hurry off, and let her be a wife to your master's son, just as the Lord has spoken."

**24:52** And it happened, when the servant of Abraham heard these words, that he bowed down to the ground in worship to the Lord.

**24:53** And the servant brought out articles of silver and gold and clothing, and gave them to Rebekah; and he gave gifts to her brother and to her mother.

**24:54** And they ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and they slept. And rising up in the morning, he said, "Send me away so that I may depart to my master."

**24:55** But her brothers and her mother said, "Let the young woman stay with us about ten days, and after that she will depart."

**24:56** And he said to them, "Do not detain me, since the Lord has made my way successful; send me away so that I may depart to my master."

**24:57** And they said, "Let us call the girl and question her mouth."

**24:58** And they called Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" And she said, "I will go."

**24:59** So they sent away Rebekah their sister and her possessions, and the servant of Abraham and those with him.

**24:60** And they blessed Rebekah their sister and said to her, "You are our sister; become thousands of ten thousands, and may your seed inherit the cities of the adversaries."

**24:61** Then Rebekah and her maidservants arose and mounted the camels, and went with the man; and the servant took Rebekah and departed.

**24:62** Now Isaac was journeying through the wilderness by the Well of Vision; for he was dwelling in the land toward the south.

**24:63** And Isaac went out to meditate in the field toward evening; and looking up with his eyes, he saw camels coming.

**24:64** And Rebekah, looking up with her eyes, saw Isaac and leaped down from the camel,

**24:65** and she said to the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?" And the servant said, "This is my master." So she took the veil and wrapped herself in it.

**24:66** And the servant recounted to Isaac all the things that he had done.

**24:67** Then Isaac entered the house of his mother, and he took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her; and Isaac was comforted concerning Sarah his mother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The Greek text reads "the God of heaven and the God of the earth" and "to you I will give this land and to your seed," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits "and the God of the earth" and "to you."
- **24:22** — The Greek reads plural "gold earrings, each a drachma in weight," whereas the Hebrew has a single gold nose-ring weighing half a shekel.
- **24:24** — The Greek phrasing "whom she bore to him, Nahor" uses an appositional dative (αὐτῷ Ναχώρ) identifying Nahor as the father to whom Milcah bore Bethuel.
- **24:26** — The Greek participle εὐδοκήσας ("being pleased") differs from the Hebrew "bowed his head."
- **24:31** — The Greek reads nominative εὐλογητὸς Κύριος ("Blessed is the Lord") as an exclamation of praise, rather than the Hebrew "blessed of the LORD" addressed to the servant.
- **24:36** — The Greek reads "one son" and "after he had grown old" (masculine αὐτόν, referring to Abraham), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a son" and "after her old age" (referring to Sarah).
- **24:50** — The Greek idiom ἀντειπεῖν κακὸν καλῷ ("speak against you evil for good") differs from the Hebrew "speak to you bad or good."
- **24:59** — The Greek reads "her possessions" (τὰ ὑπάρχοντα αὐτῆς), whereas the Hebrew has "her nurse."
- **24:62** — The Greek renders the place name Beer-lahai-roi descriptively as "the Well of Vision" (τὸ φρέαρ τῆς ὁράσεως) and adds "through the wilderness."
- **24:67** — The Greek reads "Isaac entered the house of his mother" and ends with "concerning Sarah his mother," whereas the Hebrew reads "brought her into the tent of Sarah his mother" and "after his mother's death."
