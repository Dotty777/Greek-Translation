## Text

**36:1** Now these are the generations of Esau; he is Edom.

**36:2** Now Esau took wives for himself from the daughters of the Canaanites: Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah the son of Zibeon the Hivite,

**36:3** and Basemath, daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebaioth.

**36:4** And Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau, and Basemath bore Reuel,

**36:5** and Oholibamah bore Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These are the sons of Esau who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

**36:6** And Esau took his wives and his sons and his daughters and all the persons of his household, and all the possessions and the livestock, and everything that he had acquired and everything that he had gained in the land of Canaan; and he went out of the land of Canaan away from the presence of Jacob his brother.

**36:7** For their possessions were too many for them to dwell together, and the land of their sojourning could not support them because of the abundance of their possessions.

**36:8** So Esau dwelt in the hill country of Seir; Esau, he is Edom.

**36:9** Now these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in the hill country of Seir.

**36:10** And these are the names of the sons of Esau: Eliphaz, the son of Adah the wife of Esau, and Reuel, the son of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:11** Now the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zophar, Gatam, and Kenaz.

**36:12** Now Timna was a concubine of Eliphaz the son of Esau, and she bore Amalek to Eliphaz. These are the sons of Adah the wife of Esau.

**36:13** And these are the sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah; these were the sons of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:14** And these are the sons of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah the son of Zibeon, the wife of Esau; and she bore to Esau Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**36:15** These are the chiefs of the son of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zophar, Chief Kenaz,

**36:16** Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, Chief Amalek; these are the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Idumea; these are the sons of Adah.

**36:17** And these are the sons of Reuel, the son of Esau: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Mizzah, Chief Shammah; these are the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Basemath, the wife of Esau.

**36:18** And these are the sons of Oholibamah, the wife of Esau: Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, Chief Korah; these are the chiefs of Oholibamah.

**36:19** And these are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs; these are their chiefs, the sons of Edom.

**36:20** Now these are the sons of Seir the Horite, who inhabited the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

**36:21** and Dishon, Saar, and Rison; these are the chiefs of the Horite, the son of Seir, in the land of Edom.

**36:22** And the sons of Lotan were Hori and Heman; and Lotan's sister was Timna.

**36:23** And these are the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Oman.

**36:24** And these are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Onan; this is the Onas who found Jamin in the wilderness when he was pasturing the beasts of burden of Zibeon his father.

**36:25** And these are the sons of Anah: Dishon, and Oholibamah the daughter of Anah.

**36:26** And these are the sons of Dishon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**36:27** And these are the sons of Saar: Bilhan, Zukam, Jukam, and Ukan.

**36:28** And these are the sons of Rison: Uz and Aram.

**36:29** These are the chiefs of Hori: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,

**36:30** Chief Dishon, Chief Saar, Chief Rison; these are the chiefs of Hori in their chiefdoms in the land of Edom.

**36:31** And these are the kings who reigned in Edom before a king reigned in Jerusalem.

**36:32** And Balak the son of Beor reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**36:33** Then Balak died, and Jobab the son of Zerah from Bozrah reigned in his place.

**36:34** Then Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

**36:35** Then Husham died, and Hadad the son of Barad, who struck down Midian in the plain of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Gethaim.

**36:36** Then Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.

**36:37** Then Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth by the river reigned in his place.

**36:38** Then Shaul died, and Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his place.

**36:39** Then Baal-hanan the son of Achbor died, and Arath the son of Barath reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Peor, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the son of Me-zahab.

**36:40** These are the names of the chiefs of Esau in their tribes, according to their place, in their regions and in their nations: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jeber,

**36:41** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Phines,

**36:42** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**36:43** Chief Metodiel, Chief Zaphoi; these are the chiefs of Edom in the built-up places in the land of their possession. This is Esau, the father of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — The Greek text identifies Anah as 'the son of Zibeon' (likewise in 36:14), aligning with 36:24-25, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the daughter of Zibeon'.
- **36:21** — The Greek text names Seir's last two sons Saar and Rison (repeated in 36:27-30, where Saar is given four sons: Bilhan, Zukam, Jukam, and Ukan), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Ezer (with three sons) and Dishan.
- **36:24** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to Anah finding 'the hot springs' (or an uncertain term) while tending donkeys, the Greek reads 'Onan / Onas' finding a person named 'Jamin' while pasturing beasts of burden.
- **36:31** — This Greek text reads 'in Jerusalem', a manuscript reading distinct from the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most Septuagint witnesses, which read 'in Israel' (or 'over the Israelites').
- **36:32** — The Greek reads Balak (Βάλακ) here and in 36:33 where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Bela; this Edomite king is distinct from Balak king of Moab in Numbers 22–24.
- **36:39** — The Greek text names this king 'Arath the son of Barath' (with city Peor and Matred as 'the son of Me-zahab'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Hadar' without a patronymic (with city Pau and 'daughter of Me-zahab'); several other names in this chapter also reflect distinct Greek forms (Zophar in 36:11, 15; Balak in 36:32; Barad and Gethaim in 36:35; Jeber, Phines, Metodiel, and Zaphoi in 36:40-43).
