## Text

**7:1** And the Lord God said to Noah, "Enter into the ark, you and all your household, because I have seen you to be righteous before Me in this generation.

**7:2** From the clean livestock bring in to yourself seven by seven, male and female, and from the livestock that are not clean two by two, male and female;

**7:3** and from the clean birds of the sky seven by seven, male and female, and from all the birds that are not clean two by two, male and female, to nourish seed upon all the earth.

**7:4** For after seven more days, I am bringing rain upon the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe out every living creature that I have made from the face of the earth."

**7:5** And Noah did everything that the Lord God commanded him.

**7:6** Now Noah was six hundred years old, and the flood was upon the earth.

**7:7** And Noah, and his sons, and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him, entered into the ark because of the water of the flood.

**7:8** And from the birds, and from the clean livestock, and from the livestock that are not clean, and from the birds, and from all the creeping things that are on the earth,

**7:9** two by two they came in to Noah into the ark, male and female, just as God had commanded him.

**7:10** And it came about after the seven days that the water of the flood came upon the earth.

**7:11** In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, on this day all the springs of the abyss burst open, and the floodgates of the sky were opened.

**7:12** And the rain came upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.

**7:13** On this day Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with him entered into the ark.

**7:14** And all the wild animals according to kind, and all the livestock according to kind, and every creeping thing that moves on the earth according to kind, and every bird according to kind

**7:15** entered in to Noah into the ark, two by two, male and female, from all flesh in which there is a spirit of life.

**7:16** And those that were going in entered, male and female from all flesh, just as God had commanded Noah; and the Lord God shut the ark from outside him.

**7:17** And the flood came upon the earth for forty days and forty nights upon the earth; and the water multiplied and lifted up the ark, and it was raised up from the earth.

**7:18** And the water prevailed and multiplied exceedingly upon the earth, and the ark was carried along over the water.

**7:19** And the water prevailed most exceedingly upon the earth, and it covered all the high mountains that were under the sky.

**7:20** Fifteen cubits upward the water rose, and it covered all the high mountains.

**7:21** And all flesh that moves on the earth died: of the birds, and of the livestock, and of the wild animals, and every creeping thing that moves on the earth, and every person;

**7:22** and everything that has the breath of life, and every one that was on the dry land, died.

**7:23** And He wiped out every living creature that was upon the face of all the earth, from man to livestock and creeping things and the birds of the sky; and they were wiped out from the earth. And Noah alone was left, and those who were with him in the ark.

**7:24** And the water was raised high upon the earth for one hundred fifty days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — Greek anastema (also in 7:23) literally refers to that which stands upright or a living structure, serving as the Septuagint term for every living creature.
- **7:11** — The Septuagint dates the outbreak of the flood to the twenty-seventh day of the second month (matching 8:4), ten days later than the Masoretic Text's seventeenth day.
- **7:17** — The Greek text repeats "upon the earth" and includes "and forty nights" alongside "forty days," harmonizing with 7:4 and 7:12.
