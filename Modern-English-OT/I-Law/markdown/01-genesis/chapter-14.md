## Text

**14:1** Now it came about in the reign of Amraphel king of Shinar, that Arioch king of Ellasar, and Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Thalga king of nations

**14:2** made war with Balla king of Sodom, and with Birsha king of Gomorrah, and Sennaar king of Admah, and Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and with the king of Balak; this is Zoar.

**14:3** All these united in agreement at the Salt Ravine; this is the Sea of Salt.

**14:4** For twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

**14:5** And in the fourteenth year Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him came, and they cut down the giants who were in Ashtaroth-karnaim, and strong nations with them, and the Somaites who were in the city of Shaveh,

**14:6** and the Horites who were in the mountains of Seir, as far as the terebinth of Paran, which is in the wilderness.

**14:7** And turning back, they came to the Spring of Judgment (this is Kadesh), and they cut down all the rulers of Amalek and the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.

**14:8** Then the king of Sodom and the king of Gomorrah and the king of Admah and the king of Zeboiim and the king of Balak (this is Zoar) went out, and they lined up against them for war in the Salt Valley,

**14:9** against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Thalgal king of nations, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar, these four kings against the five.

**14:10** Now the Salt Valley was pits of asphalt. And the king of Sodom and the king of Gomorrah fled, and they fell in there; and those who were left behind fled to the hill country.

**14:11** And he took all the cavalry of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their provisions, and they departed.

**14:12** They also took Lot, the son of Abram's brother, and his possessions, and went away; for he was living in Sodom.

**14:13** Then one of those who had escaped came and reported to Abram the migrant; now he was living by the oak of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and the brother of Aunan, who were sworn allies of Abram.

**14:14** And when Abram heard that Lot his brother had been taken captive, he numbered his own houseborn men, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued after them as far as Dan.

**14:15** And he fell upon them by night, he and his servants, and struck them, and pursued them as far as Chobal, which is on the left of Damascus.

**14:16** And he brought back all the cavalry of Sodom; and he brought back Lot his brother, and all his belongings, and the women, and the people.

**14:17** Then the king of Sodom went out to meet him, after he returned from the slaughter of Chedorlaomer and of the kings who were with him, to the Valley of Shaveh; this was the Plain of the King.

**14:18** And Melchizedek king of Salem brought out loaves of bread and wine; now he was priest of the Most High God.

**14:19** And he blessed Abram and said, "Blessed be Abram by the Most High God, who created heaven and earth;

**14:20** and blessed be the Most High God, who delivered your enemies into your hands." And he gave him a tenth of everything.

**14:21** Then the king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the men, and take the cavalry for yourself."

**14:22** And Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I will stretch out my hand to the Most High God, who created heaven and earth,

**14:23** that I will not take from a thread to a sandal strap of anything that is yours, so that you may not say, 'I made Abram rich,'

**14:24** except what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me, Eshcol, Aunan, Mamre; these will take a share."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — The Greek reads "in the reign" (Hebrew "in the days") and has distinct proper name forms here and in 14:2, 9: Thalga (Thalgal in 14:9; Hebrew Tidal), Balla (Hebrew Bera), Sennaar (spelled identically to Shinar in Greek; Hebrew Shinab), and Balak (Hebrew Bela).
- **14:5** — The Greek translates Hebrew Rephaim as "giants," renders Hebrew "Zuzim in Ham" as "strong nations with them," and reads "the Somaites in the city of Shaveh" (Hebrew "Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim").
- **14:11** — Here and in 14:16, 21, the Greek reads "the cavalry" (τὴν ἵππον), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew term for "possessions" (rekhush) as "steeds" (rekhesh).
- **14:13** — "The migrant" renders Greek τῷ περάτῃ (literally "the one who crossed over"), the Septuagint's translation of Hebrew "the Hebrew"; Greek also has Aunan (Hebrew Aner) here and in 14:24, and Chobal (Hebrew Hobah) in 14:15.
