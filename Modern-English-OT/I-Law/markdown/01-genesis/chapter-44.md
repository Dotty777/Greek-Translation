## Text

**44:1** And Joseph commanded the one who was over his house, saying, "Fill the men's bags with as much food as they are able to carry, and put each one's silver at the mouth of his bag,

**44:2** and put my silver cup into the bag of the youngest, and the price of his grain." And it was done according to the word of Joseph, just as he spoke.

**44:3** The morning dawned, and the men were sent away, they and their donkeys.

**44:4** And when they had gone out of the city, they had not gone far away, and Joseph spoke to the one over his house, saying, "Get up, pursue after the men, and you will overtake them and say to them, 'Why have you repaid me evil for good?

**44:5** Why did you steal my silver cup? Is this not the one from which my lord drinks? And he indeed practices divination with it. You have accomplished evil things in what you have done.'"

**44:6** And having found them, he spoke to them according to these words.

**44:7** And they said to him, "Why does my lord speak according to these words? Far be it from your servants to do this thing.

**44:8** If we returned to you from the land of Canaan the silver that we found in our bags, how could we steal silver or gold from the house of your lord?

**44:9** The one of your servants with whom the cup is found, let him die; and we also will be servants to our lord."

**44:10** And he said, "And now, as you say, so it will be: the man with whom the cup is found, he will be my servant, and you will be innocent."

**44:11** And they hurried and each one lowered his bag to the ground, and each one opened his bag.

**44:12** And he searched, beginning from the oldest until he came to the youngest, and he found the cup in Benjamin's bag.

**44:13** And they tore their garments, and each one placed his bag upon his donkey, and they returned to the city.

**44:14** And Judah and his brothers came in to Joseph, while he was still there, and they fell before him to the ground.

**44:15** And Joseph said to them, "What is this deed you have done? Do you not know that a man such as I will surely practice divination?"

**44:16** And Judah said, "What shall we answer my lord, or what shall we speak, or how shall we be justified? God has found the wrongdoing of your servants. Look, we are household servants to our lord, both we and the one with whom the cup was found."

**44:17** And Joseph said, "Far be it from me to do this thing. The man with whom the cup was found, he will be my servant; but as for you, go up in safety to your father."

**44:18** And Judah drew near to him and said, "I beg you, my lord, let your servant speak a word before you, and do not be angry with your servant, for you are after Pharaoh.

**44:19** My lord, you asked your servants, saying, 'Do you have a father or a brother?'

**44:20** And we said to my lord, 'We have an elderly father, and a younger child of his old age; and his brother died, and he alone was left to his father, and his father loved him.'

**44:21** And you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me, and I will take care of him.'

**44:22** And we said to my lord, 'The child will not be able to leave his father; and if he leaves his father, he will die.'

**44:23** But you said to your servants, 'Unless your youngest brother comes down with you, you will not see my face again.'

**44:24** And it came about when we went up to your servant, our father, that we reported to him the words of my lord.

**44:25** And our father said to us, 'Go again, buy us a little food.'

**44:26** And we said, 'We will not be able to go down; rather, if our youngest brother goes down with us, we will go down, for we will not be able to see the face of the man while our youngest brother is not with us.'

**44:27** And your servant our father said to us, 'You know that my wife bore me two.

**44:28** And the one went out from me, and you said, "He has been devoured by a wild beast," and I have not seen him since.

**44:29** Therefore, if you take this one also from my presence and sickness befalls him on the way, then you will bring down my old age with grief to Hades.'

**44:30** Now therefore, if I go in to your servant, our father, and the boy is not with us, since his soul hangs upon this one's soul,

**44:31** then it will happen when he sees that the boy is not with us, he will die, and your servants will bring down the old age of your servant, our father, with sorrow to Hades.

**44:32** For your servant has become surety for the child from my father, saying, 'If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, I will have sinned against my father all the days.'

**44:33** Now therefore, I will remain with you as a servant instead of the child, a household servant of my lord; and let the child go up with the brothers.

**44:34** For how shall I go up to my father when the child is not with us? So that I may not see the evils that will find my father."

## Translator Notes

- **44:5** — The question "Why did you steal my silver cup?" is a Septuagint addition not in the Masoretic Text; in the Greek source, the verse division splits the idiom hina ti ("why") between 44:4 and 44:5.
- **44:18** — Greek reads meta Pharao ("after Pharaoh," i.e., second only to Pharaoh), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "like Pharaoh."
- **44:28** — Greek reads second-person plural eipate ("you said"), attributing the words to the brothers, where the Masoretic Text has first-person singular ("I said").
- **44:29** — Greek renders Hebrew "harm" with malakia ("sickness" or "infirmity") and "Sheol" with Haidou ("Hades," also in 44:31).
