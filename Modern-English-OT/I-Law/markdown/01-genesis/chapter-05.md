## Text

**5:1** This is the book of the origin of human beings. On the day God made Adam, according to the image of God He made him;

**5:2** male and female He made them, and He blessed them. And He called their name Adam, on the day He made them.

**5:3** Now Adam lived two hundred thirty years, and he fathered one according to his form and according to his image; and he called his name Seth.

**5:4** And the days of Adam after he fathered Seth were seven hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:5** And all the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred thirty years, and he died.

**5:6** Now Seth lived two hundred five years, and he fathered Enosh.

**5:7** And Seth lived seven hundred seven years after he fathered Enosh, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:8** And all the days of Seth were nine hundred twelve years, and he died.

**5:9** And Enosh lived one hundred ninety years, and he fathered Kenan.

**5:10** And Enosh lived seven hundred fifteen years after he fathered Kenan, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:11** And all the days of Enosh were nine hundred five years, and he died.

**5:12** And Kenan lived one hundred seventy years, and he fathered Mahalalel.

**5:13** And Kenan lived seven hundred forty years after he fathered Mahalalel, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:14** And all the days of Kenan were nine hundred ten years, and he died.

**5:15** And Mahalalel lived one hundred sixty-five years, and he fathered Jared.

**5:16** And Mahalalel lived seven hundred thirty years after he fathered Jared, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:17** And all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred ninety-five years, and he died.

**5:18** And Jared lived one hundred sixty-two years, and he fathered Enoch.

**5:19** And Jared lived eight hundred years after he fathered Enoch, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:20** And all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty-two years, and he died.

**5:21** And Enoch lived one hundred sixty-five years, and he fathered Methuselah.

**5:22** And Enoch pleased God for two hundred years after he fathered Methuselah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:23** And all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty-five years.

**5:24** And Enoch pleased God, and he was not found, because God transferred him.

**5:25** And Methuselah lived one hundred eighty-seven years, and he fathered Lamech.

**5:26** And Methuselah lived seven hundred eighty-two years after he fathered Lamech, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:27** And all the days of Methuselah that he lived were nine hundred sixty-nine years, and he died.

**5:28** And Lamech lived one hundred eighty-eight years, and he fathered a son.

**5:29** And he called his name Noah, saying, "This one will give us rest from our works and from the pains of our hands and from the ground which the Lord God cursed."

**5:30** And Lamech lived five hundred sixty-five years after he fathered Noah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:31** And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred fifty-three years, and he died.

**5:32** And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Septuagint has the plural "human beings" (anthropon) rather than singular "Adam" in the opening title.
- **5:3** — Throughout Genesis 5, the Septuagint preserves a distinct chronology with higher pre-fatherhood ages for most patriarchs (such as 230 for Adam and 188 for Lamech) and corresponding differences in remaining years compared to the Masoretic Text.
- **5:24** — In 5:22 and 5:24, the Septuagint translates Enoch's walking with God as "pleased God" and adds "was not found, because God transferred him," the wording echoed in Hebrews 11:5.
- **5:29** — The Septuagint reads "will give us rest" (dianapausei) rather than "will comfort us" (Masoretic Text), emphasizing the wordplay on the name Noah.
