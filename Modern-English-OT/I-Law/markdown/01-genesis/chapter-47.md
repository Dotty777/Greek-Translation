## Text

**47:1** Then Joseph came and reported to Pharaoh, saying, "My father and brothers, and their livestock and cattle and all that is theirs, have come from the land of Canaan; and look, they are in the land of Goshen."

**47:2** And from among his brothers he took five men and set them before Pharaoh.

**47:3** And Pharaoh said to Joseph's brothers, "What is your occupation?" And they said to Pharaoh, "Your servants are shepherds of sheep, both we and our fathers."

**47:5** And they said to Pharaoh, "We have come to sojourn in the land, for there is no pasture for the livestock of your servants, because the famine has grown severe in the land of Canaan. Now therefore we will dwell in the land of Goshen." And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Let them dwell in the land of Goshen; and if you know that there are capable men among them, appoint them rulers over my livestock." Now Jacob and his sons came into Egypt to Joseph, and Pharaoh king of Egypt heard. And Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, saying, "Your father and your brothers have come to you;

**47:6** look, the land of Egypt is before you. Settle your father and your brothers in the best land."

**47:7** Then Joseph brought in Jacob his father and set him before Pharaoh; and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

**47:8** And Pharaoh said to Jacob, "How many are the years of the days of your life?"

**47:9** And Jacob said to Pharaoh, "The days of the years of my life that I sojourn are one hundred thirty years. Short and evil have the days of the years of my life been; they have not reached the days of the years of the life of my fathers, the days that they sojourned."

**47:10** And having blessed Pharaoh, Jacob went out from him.

**47:11** And Joseph settled his father and his brothers and gave them a possession in the land of Egypt, in the best land, in the land of Rameses, just as Pharaoh had commanded.

**47:12** And Joseph measured out grain to his father and to his brothers and to all the house of his father, according to each person.

**47:13** Now there was no grain in all the land, for the famine grew exceedingly severe; and the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan failed because of the famine.

**47:14** And Joseph gathered all the silver that was found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan for the grain that they were buying, and he measured out grain to them; and Joseph brought all the silver into the house of Pharaoh.

**47:15** And all the silver failed from the land of Egypt and from the land of Canaan. Then all the Egyptians came to Joseph, saying, "Give us bread! And why should we die before you? For our silver has failed."

**47:16** And Joseph said to them, "Bring your livestock, and I will give you bread in exchange for your livestock, if your silver has failed."

**47:17** So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for the horses and for the sheep and for the cattle and for the donkeys; and he nourished them with bread in exchange for all their livestock in that year.

**47:18** And that year came to an end, and they came to him in the second year and said to him, "Let us not be wiped out from our lord; for if our silver and possessions and livestock have failed to you, the lord, then nothing is left to us before the lord except our own body and our land.

**47:19** So that we may not die before you and the land become desolate, acquire us and our land in exchange for bread, and we and our land will be servants of Pharaoh. Give seed so that we may sow and not die, and the land will become desolate."

**47:20** So Joseph acquired all the land of the Egyptians for Pharaoh, for the Egyptians sold their land to Pharaoh, because the famine prevailed over them; and the land became Pharaoh's,

**47:21** and he enslaved the people to him as servants, from one end of the borders of Egypt to the other,

**47:22** apart from the land of the priests alone; Joseph did not acquire this, for by a grant Pharaoh gave a gift to the priests, and they ate the grant that Pharaoh gave them; therefore they did not sell their land.

**47:23** Then Joseph said to all the Egyptians, "Look, I have acquired you and your land today for Pharaoh. Take seed for yourselves and sow the land;

**47:24** and when its produce comes, you will give the fifth part to Pharaoh; and the four parts will be for you yourselves, for seed for the land and for food for you and for all your households."

**47:25** And they said, "You have saved us! We have found favor before our lord, and we will be servants of Pharaoh."

**47:26** And Joseph established it for them as a statute until this day over the land of Egypt, to pay a fifth to Pharaoh, apart from the land of the priests alone; it did not belong to Pharaoh.

**47:27** Now Israel dwelt in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen; and they gained an inheritance in it, and they multiplied exceedingly.

**47:28** And Jacob lived on in the land of Egypt seventeen years; and the days of Jacob, of the years of his life, were one hundred forty-seven years.

**47:29** Now the days of Israel drew near for him to die, and he called his son Joseph and said to him, "If I have found favor before you, place your hand under my thigh, and you will show mercy and truth toward me, not to bury me in Egypt;

**47:30** rather, I will sleep with my fathers, and you will carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their tomb." And he said, "I will do according to your word."

**47:31** And he said, "Swear to me." And he swore to him. And Israel bowed in worship upon the top of his staff.

## Translator Notes

- **47:5** — The Septuagint has no separate v. 4 and arranges the dialogue in a different order, placing Pharaoh's grant of Goshen and his instruction regarding capable men before a transitional notice that Jacob and his sons arrived and Pharaoh heard of it.
- **47:18** — The Greek verb ektribomen ("be wiped out") interprets the underlying Hebrew root k-ch-d in the sense of destruction rather than concealment ("hide from my lord" in the Masoretic text).
- **47:19** — The Greek source text has no negative particle before the final future verb, reading literally "and the land will become desolate" rather than "and the land will not become desolate."
- **47:21** — The Septuagint reads "enslaved the people to him as servants" (agreeing with the Samaritan Pentateuch), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew text reads "removed them to the cities."
- **47:31** — The Septuagint reads "upon the top of his staff" (quoted in Heb 11:21), reflecting the vocalization matteh ("staff") rather than the Masoretic mittah ("bed").
