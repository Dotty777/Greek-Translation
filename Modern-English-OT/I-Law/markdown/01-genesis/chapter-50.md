## Text

**50:1** And Joseph fell upon his father's face and wept over him and kissed him.

**50:2** And Joseph commanded his servants the embalmers to embalm his father; and the embalmers embalmed Israel.

**50:3** And he completed forty days for them, for in this way the days of burial are numbered; and Egypt mourned for him seventy days.

**50:4** Now when the days of mourning had passed, Joseph spoke to the officials of Pharaoh, saying, "If I have found favor before you, speak concerning me in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,

**50:5** 'My father made me swear, saying, "In the tomb that I dug for myself in the land of Canaan, there you will bury me." Now therefore, I will go up and bury my father, and I will return.'"

**50:6** And Pharaoh said, "Go up, bury your father, just as he made you swear."

**50:7** And Joseph went up to bury his father; and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh and the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,

**50:8** and all the household of Joseph, and his brothers, and all his father's house, and his relatives; and they left behind the sheep and the cattle in the land of Goshen.

**50:9** And both chariots and horsemen went up with him, and the company was very great.

**50:10** And they came to the threshing floor of Atad, which is beyond the Jordan, and they lamented for him with a great and very strong lamentation; and they observed the mourning for his father seven days.

**50:11** And the inhabitants of the land of Canaan saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad and said, "This is a great mourning for the Egyptians." Therefore he called its name Mourning of Egypt, which is beyond the Jordan.

**50:12** And his sons did so for him, and they buried him there.

**50:13** And his sons carried him up into the land of Canaan and buried him in the double cave, which cave Abraham acquired as a possession for a tomb from Ephron the Hittite, opposite Mamre.

**50:14** And Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brothers and those who had gone up with him to bury his father.

**50:15** Now when Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, "Perhaps Joseph will bear a grudge against us and will surely repay us for all the evils that we did to him."

**50:16** And they came to Joseph, saying, "Your father made us swear before he died, saying,

**50:17** 'Say this to Joseph, "Forgive them their wrongdoing and their sin, because they did evil things to you."' And now, accept the wrongdoing of the servants of the God of your father." And Joseph wept while they were speaking to him.

**50:18** And coming to him, they said, "Here we are, household servants."

**50:19** And Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid, for I belong to God.

**50:20** You planned against me for evil, but God planned concerning me for good, so that it might happen as it is today, in order that many people might be sustained."

**50:21** And he said to them, "Do not be afraid; I will sustain you and your households." And he comforted them and spoke to their heart.

**50:22** And Joseph resided in Egypt, he and his brothers and all the household of his father; and Joseph lived one hundred and ten years.

**50:23** And Joseph saw Ephraim's children to the third generation; and the sons of Machir, the son of Manasseh, were born on Joseph's thighs.

**50:24** And Joseph spoke to his brothers, saying, "I am dying, but God will surely visit you and will bring you up out of this land into the land that God swore to our fathers, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob."

**50:25** And Joseph made the sons of Israel swear, saying, "In the visitation with which God will visit you, you will also carry up my bones from here with you."

**50:26** And Joseph died, being one hundred and ten years old; and they buried him and placed him in the coffin in Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **50:3** — The Greek reads a singular active verb with a plural object ("he completed forty days for them"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads passively ("forty days were fulfilled for him").
- **50:11** — The Septuagint translates the place name into Greek (Πένθος Αἰγύπτου, "Mourning of Egypt") rather than transliterating the Hebrew Abel-mizraim.
- **50:13** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew Machpelah descriptively as "the double cave" (τὸ σπήλαιον τὸ διπλοῦν) and specifies "the cave" rather than "the field" as the property Abraham acquired.
- **50:19** — The Septuagint renders Joseph's response as a statement ("for I belong to God," τοῦ γὰρ θεοῦ ἐγὼ εἰμί), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a rhetorical question ("Am I in the place of God?").
- **50:26** — The Greek verb ἔθαψαν ("buried" or "prepared for burial") corresponds here to the Hebrew verb for embalming, followed by Joseph's placement in the coffin in Egypt.
