## Text

**17:1** Now Abram was ninety-nine years old, and the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "I am your God; be well-pleasing before Me, and become blameless,

**17:2** and I will make My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you exceedingly."

**17:3** And Abram fell on his face,

**17:4** and God spoke to him, saying, "And as for Me, look, My covenant is with you, and you will be the father of a multitude of nations.

**17:5** And your name will no longer be called Abram, but Abraham will be your name, because I have made you the father of many nations.

**17:6** And I will increase you exceedingly, and I will make you into nations, and kings will come out from you.

**17:7** And I will establish My covenant between Me and you and your seed after you for their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be your God and the God of your seed after you.

**17:8** And I will give to you and to your seed after you the land in which you live as a stranger, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession, and I will be God to them."

**17:9** And God said to Abraham, "Now as for you, you shall keep My covenant, you and your seed after you for their generations.

**17:10** And this is the covenant which you shall keep between Me and you, and your seed after you for their generations: every male among you shall be circumcised.

**17:11** And you shall be circumcised in the flesh of your foreskin, and it will be a sign of the covenant between Me and you.

**17:12** And every male among you, a child eight days old, shall be circumcised for your generations: the one born in your house and the one bought with silver from any son of a foreigner, who is not of your seed,

**17:13** shall surely be circumcised, the one born in your house and the one bought with silver. And My covenant will be on your flesh for an everlasting covenant.

**17:14** And an uncircumcised male, who is not circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin on the eighth day, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from his people, because he has broken My covenant."

**17:15** And God said to Abraham, "As for Sara your wife, her name will not be called Sara, but Sarah will be her name.

**17:16** And I will bless her, and I will give you a child from her. And I will bless him, and he will become nations, and kings of nations will be from him."

**17:17** And Abraham fell on his face and laughed, and spoke in his mind, saying, "Will a son be born to a hundred-year-old man, and will one be born to Sarah at ninety years old?"

**17:18** And Abraham said to God, "Let this Ishmael live before You."

**17:19** And God said to Abraham, "Yes; look, Sarah your wife will bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac; and I will establish My covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, and for his seed after him.

**17:20** Now concerning Ishmael, look, I have heard you; and I have blessed him, and I will increase him and multiply him exceedingly; he will father twelve nations, and I will make him into a great nation.

**17:21** But My covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this time in the next year."

**17:22** And He finished speaking to him, and God went up from Abraham.

**17:23** And Abraham took Ishmael his son, and all those born in his house, and all those bought with silver, and every male of the men who were in the house of Abraham, and circumcised their foreskins at the time of that day, just as God had spoken to him.

**17:24** Now Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin;

**17:25** and Ishmael his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

**17:26** At the time of that day, Abraham and Ishmael his son were circumcised;

**17:27** and all the men of his house, both those born in the house and those bought with silver from foreign nations—he circumcised them.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Greek reads "your God" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "El Shaddai") and "be well-pleasing" (for Hebrew "walk").
- **17:14** — The Greek specifies "on the eighth day," a phrase also found in the Samaritan Pentateuch though absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:15** — The Greek changes the name from Sara (Σάρα) to Sarra (Σάρρα, rendered with the standard English spelling "Sarah"), corresponding to the Hebrew shift from Sarai to Sarah.
- **17:16** — The Greek reads "a child" and shifts to masculine pronouns ("bless him," "he will become," "from him"), referring to the promised son rather than to Sarah as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:20** — The Greek reads "twelve nations" (δώδεκα ἔθνη), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "twelve princes."
