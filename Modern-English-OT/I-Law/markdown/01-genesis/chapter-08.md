## Text

**8:1** And God remembered Noah, and all the wild animals, and all the livestock, and all the birds, and all the creeping things that were with him in the ark; and God brought a wind upon the earth, and the water subsided.

**8:2** And the springs of the abyss and the floodgates of the sky were uncovered, and the rain from the sky was held back.

**8:3** And the water was receding, going away from the earth; the water was receding and diminishing after one hundred fifty days.

**8:4** And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

**8:5** Now the water continually diminished until the tenth month; and in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.

**8:6** And it came about after forty days that Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made,

**8:7** and he sent out the raven to see whether the water had subsided; and having gone out, it did not return until the water had dried up from the earth.

**8:8** And he sent out the dove after it to see whether the water had subsided from the earth.

**8:9** And the dove, not finding a resting place for its feet, returned to him into the ark, because water was on the whole face of all the earth; and stretching out his hand, he took it and brought it in to himself into the ark.

**8:10** And having waited yet another seven days, he again sent out the dove from the ark.

**8:11** And the dove returned to him toward evening, and it had an olive leaf, a twig, in its mouth; and Noah knew that the water had subsided from the earth.

**8:12** And having waited yet another seven days, he again sent out the dove, and it did not return to him anymore.

**8:13** And it came about in the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the water vanished from the earth; and Noah uncovered the roof of the ark and saw that the water had vanished from the face of the earth.

**8:14** And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dried up.

**8:15** And the Lord God spoke to Noah, saying,

**8:16** "Come out of the ark, you and your wife and your sons and the wives of your sons with you;

**8:17** and all the wild animals that are with you, and all flesh from birds to livestock, and every creeping thing that moves on the earth, bring out with you; and increase and multiply on the earth."

**8:18** And Noah went out, and his wife, and his sons, and the wives of his sons with him;

**8:19** and all the wild animals, and all the livestock, and every bird, and every creeping thing that moves on the earth, according to their kind, went out from the ark.

**8:20** And Noah built an altar to God, and took from all the clean livestock and from all the clean birds, and offered up a whole burnt offering on the altar.

**8:21** And the Lord God smelled a scent of sweet fragrance, and the Lord God, having considered, said, "I will not again curse the earth because of the deeds of people, because the mindset of man is intently set upon evil things from youth; therefore I will not again strike down all living flesh as I have done.

**8:22** All the days of the earth, seed and harvest, cold and heat, summer and spring, day and night, will not cease."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek source reads apekalyphthēsan ("were uncovered"), an early transcriptional variant attested in witnesses such as Codex Alexandrinus for epekalyphthēsan ("were covered over" or "closed").
- **8:5** — The Septuagint places the appearance of the mountaintops in the eleventh month rather than the tenth month as in the Masoretic Text.
- **8:7** — The Septuagint adds "to see whether the water had subsided" and states that the raven "did not return" until the water dried up from the earth.
- **8:22** — The Septuagint pairs "summer and spring" (theros kai ear) rather than "summer and winter."
