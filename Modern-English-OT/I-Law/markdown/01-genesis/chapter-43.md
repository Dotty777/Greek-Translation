## Text

**43:1** Now the famine grew severe upon the land.

**43:2** And it happened when they had finished eating up the grain that they had brought from Egypt, that their father said to them, "Go again and buy a little food for us."

**43:3** And Judah spoke to him, saying, "The man solemnly warned us, saying, 'You will not see my face unless your youngest brother comes down to me.'

**43:4** So if you send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you;

**43:5** but if you do not send our brother with us, we will not go, for the man spoke to us, saying, 'You will not see my face unless your youngest brother is with you.'"

**43:6** And Israel said, "Why did you treat me badly, by reporting to the man whether you had a brother?"

**43:7** And they said, "The man closely questioned us and about our family, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have a brother?' And we reported to him according to his questioning. Could we have known whether he would say to us, 'Bring your brother'?"

**43:8** And Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the boy with me, and we will arise and go, so that we may live and not die, both we and you and our dependents.

**43:9** I myself take responsibility for him; require him from my hand. If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, I will have sinned against you all the days.

**43:10** For if we had not delayed, we would already have returned twice."

**43:11** Then Israel their father said to them, "If it is so, do this: take some of the fruits of the land in your vessels, and bring down gifts to the man—some resin and honey, incense and stacte and terebinth and walnuts.

**43:12** And take double the silver in your hands; return with you the silver that was returned in your bags; perhaps it is an oversight.

**43:13** And take your brother, and arise and go down to the man.

**43:14** And may my God grant you favor before the man, and send back your one brother and Benjamin; for as for me, just as I have been made childless, I am made childless."

**43:15** So the men took these gifts, and they took double the silver in their hands, and Benjamin; and rising up, they went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.

**43:16** And Joseph saw them and Benjamin, his brother by the same mother, and he commanded the one over his house to bring the men into the house: "And slaughter animals and prepare them, for the men will eat bread with me at midday."

**43:17** And the man did just as Joseph had said, and brought the men into the house of Joseph.

**43:18** And when the men saw that they had been brought into the house of Joseph, they said, "Because of the silver that was returned in our bags the first time, we are being brought in, to accuse us falsely and to attack us, to take us as servants, and our donkeys."

**43:19** So approaching the man who was over the house of Joseph, they spoke to him at the gateway of the house,

**43:20** saying, "We beg you, lord: we came down the first time to buy food;

**43:21** and it happened when we came to the lodging place and opened our bags, here was each one's silver in his bag. Our silver by weight we have now returned in our bags,

**43:22** and we have brought other silver with us to buy food; we do not know who put the silver into our bags."

**43:23** And the man said to them, "Be at ease; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your fathers has given you treasures in your bags; and I have received your approved silver in full." And he brought Simeon out to them.

**43:24** And he brought water to wash their feet, and he brought fodder for their donkeys.

**43:25** And they prepared the gifts until Joseph came at midday, for they had heard that he was going to dine there.

**43:26** Now Joseph entered the house, and they brought to him into the house the gifts that they had in their hands, and they bowed down to him face down to the ground.

**43:27** And he asked them, "How are you?" And he said to them, "Is your aged father of whom you spoke in good health? Is he still alive?"

**43:28** And they said, "Your servant our father is in good health; he is still alive." And he said, "Blessed is that man by God." And bending low, they bowed down.

**43:29** And lifting up his eyes, Joseph saw Benjamin, his brother by the same mother, and said to them, "Is this your youngest brother, whom you spoke of bringing to me?" And he said, "May God have mercy on you, child."

**43:30** And Joseph was troubled, for his inward parts were churning over his brother, and he sought to weep; so entering the inner room, he wept there.

**43:31** And having washed his face, he came out and restrained himself, and said, "Set out bread."

**43:32** And they set it before him by himself, and before them by themselves, and before the Egyptians who dined with him by themselves; for the Egyptians could not eat bread together with the Hebrews, for every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians.

**43:33** And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men were astonished, each one toward his brother.

**43:34** And they carried a portion from him to them; and Benjamin's portion was enlarged five times beyond the portions of all in comparison with theirs. And they drank and became drunk with him.

## Translator Notes

- **43:11** — The Septuagint lists "terebinth" (τερέμινθον) and "walnuts" (κάρυα) among the gifts, corresponding to "pistachios" and "almonds" in the Masoretic Text.
- **43:14** — The Septuagint reads "my God" (ὁ θεός μου) where the Masoretic Text reads "God Almighty" (El Shaddai).
- **43:23** — The steward's greeting "Be at ease" translates the Greek idiom ἵλεως ὑμῖν (literally "gracious/merciful to you"), rendering the Hebrew greeting of peace.
- **43:28** — The sentence "And he said, 'Blessed is that man by God'" is present in the Septuagint and not found in the Masoretic Text.
- **43:32** — The Septuagint specifies "for every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians" (matching Genesis 46:34), whereas the Masoretic Text reads simply "for that is an abomination to the Egyptians."
