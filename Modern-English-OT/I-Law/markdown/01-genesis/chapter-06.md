## Text

**6:1** And it came about, when people began to become numerous on the earth, that daughters were born to them.

**6:2** Then the angels of God saw the daughters of men, that they were beautiful, and they took for themselves wives from all whom they chose.

**6:3** And the Lord God said, "My Spirit will certainly not remain in these people forever, because they are flesh; rather, their days will be one hundred twenty years."

**6:4** Now the giants were on the earth in those days, and after that, whenever the sons of God went in to the daughters of men and they bore children for themselves. Those were the giants from ancient times, the men of renown.

**6:5** Now the Lord God saw that the evils of people had multiplied on the earth, and that every person intently directed his thoughts in his heart toward evil things every day.

**6:6** And God took it to heart that He had made man on the earth, and He thought it over.

**6:7** And God said, "I will wipe out man whom I have made from the face of the earth, from man to livestock and from creeping things to the birds of the sky, because I have become angry that I made them."

**6:8** But Noah found favor before the Lord God.

**6:9** Now these are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, being blameless in his generation; Noah was well-pleasing to God.

**6:10** And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**6:11** Now the earth was corrupted before God, and the earth was filled with injustice.

**6:12** And the Lord God saw the earth, and it was corrupted, because all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth.

**6:13** And God said to Noah, "The time of every person has come before Me, because the earth has been filled with injustice because of them; and look, I am about to destroy them and the earth.

**6:14** Therefore, make for yourself an ark out of squared timbers; you will make nests in the ark, and you will coat it inside and outside with asphalt.

**6:15** And this is how you will make the ark: three hundred cubits the length of the ark, and fifty cubits the width, and thirty cubits its height.

**6:16** You will make the ark tapering together, and you will finish it to a cubit above; and you will make the door of the ark on the side; you will make it with lower, second, and third stories.

**6:17** As for Me, look, I am bringing the flood of water upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which there is a spirit of life under the sky; and everything that is on the earth will die.

**6:18** And I will establish My covenant with you; and you will enter into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and the wives of your sons with you.

**6:19** And from all the livestock and from all the creeping things and from all the wild animals and from all flesh, two by two from all of them you will bring into the ark, so that you may nourish them with yourself; they will be male and female.

**6:20** From all the winged birds according to kind, and from all the livestock according to kind, and from all the creeping things that creep on the earth according to their kind, two by two from all of them will come in to you to be nourished with you, male and female.

**6:21** And as for you, take for yourself from all the foods that you will eat, and gather them to yourself, and it will be for you and for them to eat."

**6:22** And Noah did everything that the Lord God commanded him; so he did.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Septuagint reads "the angels of God" (hoi angeloi tou theou) here, whereas 6:4 reads "the sons of God" (hoi huioi tou theou), reflecting an early interpretive rendering of the Hebrew phrase.
- **6:6** — Greek enethymēthē ("took it to heart") and dienoēthē ("thought it over"), followed by ethymōthēn ("I have become angry") in 6:7, soften the Hebrew anthropomorphisms of divine regret and grief.
- **6:14** — Greek xylōn tetragōnōn ("squared timbers") and nossias ("nests," i.e., compartments) represent the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew terms for the ark's wood and rooms.
- **6:16** — Greek episynagōn ("tapering together") interprets the rare Hebrew term for the ark's roof or opening as narrowing the upper structure to a one-cubit finish.
