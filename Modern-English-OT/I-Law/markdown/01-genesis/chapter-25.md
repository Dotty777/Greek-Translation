## Text

**25:1** Now Abraham again took a wife, whose name was Keturah.

**25:2** And she bore to him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.

**25:3** And Jokshan fathered Sheba, Teman, and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan were Reuel, Nabdeel, Asshurim, Datusieim, and Leummim.

**25:4** And the sons of Midian were Gephar, Epher, Hanoch, Abira, and Thergama. All these were sons of Keturah.

**25:5** Now Abraham gave all his possessions to Isaac his son.

**25:6** And to the sons of his concubines Abraham gave gifts, and he sent them away from Isaac his son while he was still living, eastward to the land of the east.

**25:7** And these are the years of the days of Abraham's life that he lived: one hundred seventy-five years.

**25:8** And expiring, Abraham died in a good old age, an old man and full of days, and he was added to his people.

**25:9** And Isaac and Ishmael, his two sons, buried him in the double cave, in the field of Ephron the son of Zohar the Hittite, which is opposite Mamre,

**25:10** the field and the cave that Abraham acquired from the sons of Heth; there they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife.

**25:11** And it came about after Abraham died that God blessed Isaac his son; and Isaac settled beside the Well of Vision.

**25:12** Now these are the generations of Ishmael the son of Abraham, whom Hagar, the female servant of Sarah, bore to Abraham.

**25:13** And these are the names of the sons of Ishmael, according to the name of his generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; and Kedar, and Nabdeel, and Mibsam,

**25:14** and Mishma, and Iduma, and Massa,

**25:15** and Choddan, and Teman, and Jetur, and Naphish, and Kedemah.

**25:16** These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names in their tents and in their encampments: twelve rulers according to their nation.

**25:17** And these are the years of the life of Ishmael: one hundred thirty-seven years. And expiring, he died and was added to his kindred.

**25:18** And he settled from Havilah as far as Suel, which is facing Egypt, until one comes to the Assyrians; facing all his brothers he settled.

**25:19** And these are the generations of Isaac the son of Abraham: Abraham fathered Isaac.

**25:20** And Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Syrian from Mesopotamia, sister of Laban the Syrian, for himself as a wife.

**25:21** And Isaac pleaded with the Lord concerning Rebekah his wife, because she was barren; and God listened to him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.

**25:22** And the children were leaping inside her; and she said, "If it is going to be this way for me, why is this happening to me?" And she went to inquire of the Lord.

**25:23** And the Lord said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples will be separated from your belly; and one people will surpass the other people, and the greater will serve the lesser."

**25:24** And the days for her to give birth were fulfilled, and here there were twins in her womb.

**25:25** And the firstborn son came out reddish, completely hairy like a hide; and she gave him the name Esau.

**25:26** And after this his brother came out, and his hand was grasping Esau's heel; and she called his name Jacob. Now Isaac was sixty years old when Rebekah bore them.

**25:27** And the young men grew up; and Esau was a man who knew how to hunt, a man of the fields, while Jacob was a plain man, living in a house.

**25:28** And Isaac loved Esau, because his game was food for him; but Rebekah loved Jacob.

**25:29** Now Jacob cooked a stew, and Esau came from the plain, fainting.

**25:30** And Esau said to Jacob, "Give me a taste of this red stew, for I am fainting." Therefore his name was called Edom.

**25:31** And Jacob said to Esau, "Sell me today your birthright, to me."

**25:32** And Esau said, "Look, I am going to die, so what is this birthright to me?"

**25:33** And Jacob said to him, "Swear to me today." And he swore to him; and Esau sold the birthright to Jacob.

**25:34** Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew; and he ate and drank, and got up and went away. Thus Esau despised the birthright.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — The Greek text includes Teman among Jokshan's sons and adds Reuel and Nabdeel among Dedan's sons, along with Datusieim (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Letushim).
- **25:4** — The Greek text reads the distinct names Gephar, Abira, and Thergama (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Ephah, Abida, and Eldaah).
- **25:15** — In verses 13-15, the Greek text preserves the distinct name forms Nabdeel, Iduma, Choddan, and Teman (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Adbeel, Dumah, Hadad, and Tema).
- **25:18** — The Greek text reads Suel where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Shur.
