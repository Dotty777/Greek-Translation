## Text

**13:1** And Abram went up from Egypt, he and his wife and all that was his, and Lot with him, into the wilderness.

**13:2** Now Abram was exceedingly rich in livestock, and in silver, and in gold.

**13:3** And he went from where he had come into the wilderness as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been previously, between Bethel and Ai,

**13:4** to the place of the altar where he had made the tent there; and there Abram called on the name of the Lord.

**13:5** And Lot, who was traveling with Abram, had sheep and oxen and livestock.

**13:6** And the land did not have room for them to live together, because their possessions were many, and they were not able to live together.

**13:7** And strife arose between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and the herdsmen of Lot's livestock. Now the Canaanites and the Perizzites were then inhabiting the land.

**13:8** And Abram said to Lot, "Let there be no strife between me and you, and between your herdsmen and my herdsmen, because we are men who are brothers.

**13:9** And look, all the land is before you; separate yourself from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; and if you go to the right, I will go to the left."

**13:10** And Lot lifted up his eyes and saw all the surrounding region of the Jordan, that all of it was watered (before God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah), like the garden of God and like the land of Egypt, until one comes to Zoar.

**13:11** And Lot chose for himself all the surrounding region of the Jordan, and Lot set out from the east; and they separated, each man from his brother.

**13:12** Now Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled in a city of the surrounding regions and pitched his tent in Sodom.

**13:13** Now the men in Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful before God.

**13:14** And God said to Abram after Lot had separated from him, "Look up with your eyes and see from the place where you now are, toward the north and the south and the east and the sea,

**13:15** because all the land that you see, to you I will give it and to your seed forever.

**13:16** And I will make your seed like the sand of the earth; if anyone is able to count out the sand of the earth, then your seed also will be counted.

**13:17** Get up, travel through the land both to its length and to its width, because to you I will give it and to your seed forever."

**13:18** And having moved his tent, Abram came and settled beside the oak of Mamre, which was in Hebron; and he built an altar there to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — This Greek text reads "where he had made the tent there" (ten skenen), a notable variant alongside manuscripts reading "at the first" (ten archen).
- **13:5** — The Septuagint reads "livestock" (ktene) where the Masoretic Text reads "tents."
- **13:10** — The Septuagint reads "God" here and in 13:13-14 where the Masoretic Text has the divine name YHWH.
- **13:16** — The Septuagint reads "the sand of the earth" where the Masoretic Text has "the dust of the earth," and in 13:17 adds "and to your seed forever."
