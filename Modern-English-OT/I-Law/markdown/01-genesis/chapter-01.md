## Text

**1:1** In the beginning God made heaven and earth.

**1:2** But the earth was unseen and unformed, and darkness was over the abyss, and the Spirit of God was moving above the water.

**1:3** And God said, "Let light come to be." And light came to be.

**1:4** And God saw the light, that it was good. And God divided between the light and the darkness.

**1:5** And God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. And evening came and morning came, one day.

**1:6** And God said, "Let a vault come to be in the middle of the water, and let it be dividing between water and water." And it was so.

**1:7** And God made the vault, and God divided between the water which was under the vault and the water that was above the vault.

**1:8** And God called the vault Heaven. And God saw that it was good. And evening came and morning came, a second day.

**1:9** And God said, "Let the water that is under heaven be gathered into one gathering, and let the dry land appear." And it was so. And the water that was under heaven was gathered into their gatherings, and the dry land appeared.

**1:10** And God called the dry land Earth, and the collections of the waters He called Seas. And God saw that it was good.

**1:11** And God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation of plants, sowing seed according to kind and according to likeness, and the fruit-bearing tree making fruit whose seed is in it according to kind, for likeness, on the earth." And it was so.

**1:12** And the earth brought out vegetation of plants, sowing seed according to kind and according to likeness, and the fruit-bearing tree making fruit whose seed is in it according to kind on the earth. And God saw that it was good.

**1:13** And evening came and morning came, a third day.

**1:14** And God said, "Let luminaries come to be in the vault of heaven for illumination of the earth, to rule the day and the night and to divide between the day and the night; and let them be for signs and for seasons and for days and for years;

**1:15** and let them be for illumination in the vault of heaven, so as to shine on the earth." And it was so.

**1:16** And God made the two great luminaries, the great luminary for rule of the day and the lesser luminary for rule of the night, and the stars.

**1:17** And God placed them in the vault of heaven, so as to shine on the earth,

**1:18** and to rule the day and the night, and to divide between the light and the darkness. And God saw that it was good.

**1:19** And evening came and morning came, a fourth day.

**1:20** And God said, "Let the waters bring out creeping things of living creatures, and birds flying above the earth across the vault of heaven." And it was so.

**1:21** And God made the great sea creatures and every living creature of the creeping animals, which the waters brought out according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to kind. And God saw that they were good.

**1:22** And God blessed them, saying, "Increase and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth."

**1:23** And evening came and morning came, a fifth day.

**1:24** And God said, "Let the earth bring out the living creature according to kind, four-footed animals and creeping things and wild animals of the earth according to kind." And it was so.

**1:25** And God made the wild animals of the earth according to kind and the livestock according to kind and all the creeping things of the earth according to their kind. And God saw that they were good.

**1:26** And God said, "Let Us make man according to Our image and according to likeness, and let them rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of heaven and the livestock and all the earth and all the creeping things that creep on the earth."

**1:27** And God made man; according to the image of God He made him; male and female He made them.

**1:28** And God blessed them, saying, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth and gain mastery over it, and rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of heaven and all the livestock and all the earth and all the creeping things that creep on the earth."

**1:29** And God said, "See, I have given you every seed-bearing plant sowing seed which is upon all the earth, and every tree which has in itself fruit of seed for sowing; it will be food for you,

**1:30** and for all the wild animals of the earth and for all the birds of heaven and for every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, which has in itself a soul of life, every green plant for food." And it was so.

**1:31** And God saw all the things that He had made, and look, they were very good. And evening came and morning came, a sixth day.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek has the singular τὸν οὐρανόν, so this reads "heaven" (singular) and not "the heavens." The verb ἐποίησεν means "made."
- **1:2** — ἀόρατος καὶ ἀκατασκεύαστος means "unseen (invisible) and unformed (not yet furnished)," which is the Greek's own interpretation of the Hebrew pair. πνεῦμα θεοῦ is rendered "the Spirit of God"; the phrase can also mean "a wind of God."
- **1:6** — στερέωμα means a firm or solid structure. "Vault" keeps that sense without the archaic "firmament" and is used for the word throughout.
- **1:9** — The second half of the verse (the water gathered and the dry land appearing) is a plus in the Greek and is translated as it stands.
- **1:20** — ψυχὴ ζῶσα (literally "living soul") is rendered "living creature" throughout Genesis 1–2, including 2:7, so the reader can see the same phrase recur.
- **1:26** — ἄνθρωπος is the generic word for a human being. It is rendered "man" to keep the singular collective, which the following "let them rule" picks up. The Greek has no "our" before "likeness."
- **1:30** — ψυχὴν ζωῆς is literally "a soul of life"; it is kept literal to show the Greek's distinct phrase.
