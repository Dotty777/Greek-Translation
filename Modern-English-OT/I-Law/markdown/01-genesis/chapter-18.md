## Text

**18:1** Now God appeared to him by the oak of Mamre, while he was sitting at the door of his tent at midday.

**18:2** And looking up with his eyes, he saw, and look, three men were standing above him. And when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the door of his tent, and bowed down to the ground;

**18:3** and he said, "Lord, if indeed I have found favor before You, do not pass by Your servant.

**18:4** Please let water be fetched, and let someone wash your feet, and cool yourselves under the tree.

**18:5** And I will fetch bread, and you shall eat; and after this you may pass on along your way, since for this reason you have turned aside to your servant." And He said, "Do so, just as you have said."

**18:6** And Abraham hurried to the tent to Sarah and said to her, "Hurry and knead three measures of fine flour and make hearth cakes."

**18:7** And Abraham ran to the cattle, and they took a tender and good calf, and he gave it to the servant, and he hurried to prepare it.

**18:8** And he took butter and milk and the calf that he had prepared, and set them before them, and they ate; and he himself stood beside them under the tree.

**18:9** And He said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" And answering, he said, "Here, in the tent."

**18:10** And He said, "Returning, I will come to you at this time in due season, and Sarah your wife will have a son." Now Sarah heard at the door of the tent, being behind Him.

**18:11** Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in days; and the manner of women had ceased to happen to Sarah.

**18:12** And Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "It has not yet happened to me until now, and my lord is old."

**18:13** And the Lord said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh within herself, saying, 'Will I truly give birth? Yet I have grown old'?

**18:14** Is any matter impossible with God? At this time I will return to you in due season, and Sarah will have a son."

**18:15** And Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid. And He said, "No, but you did laugh."

**18:16** Then the men rose up from there and looked down toward the face of Sodom and Gomorrah; and Abraham was walking with them, sending them on their way.

**18:17** And the Lord said, "Shall I hide from Abraham My servant the things that I am doing?

**18:18** Now Abraham will surely become a great and numerous nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him.

**18:19** For I knew that he will command his sons and his house after him, and they will guard the ways of the Lord, to do righteousness and justice, so that the Lord may bring upon Abraham all the things that He has spoken concerning him."

**18:20** And the Lord said, "The outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah has been multiplied, and their sins are exceedingly great.

**18:21** Therefore I will go down and see whether they are acting to the full according to their outcry that is coming to Me; and if not, so that I may know."

**18:22** And turning away from there, the men went to Sodom; but Abraham was standing before the Lord.

**18:23** And Abraham drew near and said, "Do not destroy a righteous person together with an ungodly person, and the righteous will be like the ungodly.

**18:24** If there are fifty righteous people in the city, will You destroy them? Will You not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous people, if they are in it?

**18:25** By no means shall You act like this matter, to kill a righteous person with an ungodly person, and the righteous will be like the ungodly; by no means. You who judge all the earth, will You not do justice?"

**18:26** And the Lord said, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous people in the city, I will spare the whole place on their account."

**18:27** And answering, Abraham said, "Now I have begun to speak to the Lord, though I am earth and ashes;

**18:28** now if the fifty righteous people are fewer by five, will You destroy the whole city because of the five?" And He said, "I will certainly not destroy it if I find forty-five there."

**18:29** And he continued yet further to speak to Him and said, "What if forty are found there?" And He said, "I will certainly not destroy it for the sake of the forty."

**18:30** And he said, "Let it be nothing, Lord, if I speak; what if thirty are found there?" And He said, "I will certainly not destroy it if thirty are found there."

**18:31** And he said, "Since I am able to speak to the Lord, what if twenty are found there?" And He said, "I will certainly not destroy it for the sake of the twenty."

**18:32** And he said, "Let it be nothing, Lord, if I speak just once more; what if ten are found there?" And He said, "I will certainly not destroy it for the sake of the ten."

**18:33** And the Lord departed when He stopped speaking to Abraham, and Abraham returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — The Greek source text reads the plural verb "they took" (ἔλαβον, as in Codex Alexandrinus) before returning to singular verbs for Abraham, whereas other witnesses read singular "he took" (ἔλαβεν).
- **18:10** — The Greek idiom "in due season" (εἰς ὥρας, also in 18:14) denotes the return of the same season in the following year.
- **18:12** — The Greek reads "It has not yet happened to me until now," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Shall I have pleasure?").
- **18:23** — The Greek frames Abraham's opening appeal as a prohibition ("Do not destroy") and includes "and the righteous will be like the ungodly" here as well as in 18:25.
- **18:30** — "Let it be nothing, Lord" renders an elliptical Greek apologetic idiom (Μή τι, κύριε, also in 18:32) asking the Lord not to take offense.
