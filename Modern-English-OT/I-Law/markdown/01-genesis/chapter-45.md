## Text

**45:1** And Joseph was not able to restrain himself before all those standing beside him, but he said, "Send everyone away from me." And no one stood beside Joseph any longer when Joseph made himself known to his brothers.

**45:2** And he let out a cry with weeping; and all the Egyptians heard, and it became heard in the house of Pharaoh.

**45:3** And Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt. Is my father still alive?" And the brothers were not able to answer him, for they were dismayed.

**45:4** And he said, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.

**45:5** Now therefore, do not be grieved, nor let it seem hard to you that you sold me here, for God sent me before you for life.

**45:6** For this is the second year of famine upon the earth, and there are still five years remaining in which there will be neither plowing nor harvest.

**45:7** For God sent me before you to leave behind a remnant of you on the earth, and to nourish a great survival of you.

**45:8** Now therefore, it was not you who sent me here, but God; and He made me as a father to Pharaoh, and lord of all his house, and ruler of all the land of Egypt.

**45:9** Hurry therefore and go up to my father, and say to him, 'Thus says your son Joseph: God has made me lord of all the land of Egypt; come down therefore to me and do not delay.

**45:10** And you will dwell in the land of Goshen of Arabia, and you will be near me, you and your sons and your sons' sons, your sheep and your cattle and all that belongs to you there.

**45:11** And I will nourish you there, for there are still five years of famine, so that you may not be wiped out, you and your sons and all your possessions.'

**45:12** Look, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my mouth that is speaking to you.

**45:13** Therefore report to my father all my glory in Egypt and all that you have seen, and hurry and bring my father down here."

**45:14** And falling upon the neck of Benjamin his brother, he fell upon him; and Benjamin wept upon his neck.

**45:15** And having kissed all his brothers, he wept over them; and after these things his brothers spoke to him.

**45:16** And the report was spread abroad into the house of Pharaoh, saying, "Joseph's brothers have come." And Pharaoh and his attendants rejoiced.

**45:17** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Say to your brothers, 'Do this: load your carts and go away to the land of Canaan,

**45:18** and taking your father and your possessions, come to me; and I will give you of all the good things of Egypt, and you will eat the marrow of the land.'

**45:19** And you, command these things: for them to take wagons from the land of Egypt for your little ones and your wives, and taking up your father, come.

**45:20** And do not spare your goods with your eyes, for all the good things of Egypt will be yours."

**45:21** And the sons of Israel did so. And Joseph gave them wagons according to the things spoken by Pharaoh the king, and he gave them provisions for the way.

**45:22** And to all he gave double robes, and to Benjamin he gave three hundred gold pieces and five changes of robes.

**45:23** And to his father he sent in the same manner, and ten donkeys carrying of all the good things of Egypt, and ten mules carrying bread for his father for the way.

**45:24** And he sent away his brothers, and they went; and he said to them, "Do not be angry on the way."

**45:25** And they went up out of Egypt and came to the land of Canaan, to Jacob their father,

**45:26** and they reported to him, saying, "Your son Joseph is alive, and he rules over all the land of Egypt." And Jacob's mind was stunned, for he did not believe them.

**45:27** And they spoke to him all the things spoken by Joseph, all that he had said to them; and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to take him up, the spirit of Jacob their father revived.

**45:28** And Israel said, "It is a great thing to me if my son Joseph is still alive; I will go and see him before I die."

## Translator Notes

- **45:3** — In this Greek text, "your brother, whom you sold into Egypt" already appears in verse 3, and verse 4 omits Joseph's invitation for the brothers to draw near.
- **45:10** — Greek Gesem Arabias ("Goshen of Arabia") adds the geographical qualifier "of Arabia" to Goshen.
- **45:14** — This Greek text reads epepesen ep' auto ("he fell upon him") rather than "he wept upon him" as in other Septuagint witnesses and the Masoretic Text.
- **45:17** — Greek poria denotes carts or conveyances, corresponding to Hebrew "beasts."
- **45:22** — Greek reads triakosious chrysous ("three hundred gold pieces"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "three hundred pieces of silver."
