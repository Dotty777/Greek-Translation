## Text

**41:1** Now it happened after two years of days that Pharaoh saw a dream. He thought he was standing by the river,

**41:2** and look, as if out of the river seven cows were coming up, fine in appearance and choice in flesh, and they were grazing in the reed-grass.

**41:3** And seven other cows were coming up after them out of the river, ugly in appearance and thin in flesh, and the cows were feeding beside the bank of the river in the reed-grass.

**41:4** And the seven cows that were ugly and thin in flesh devoured the seven cows that were fine in appearance and choice. Then Pharaoh woke up.

**41:5** And he dreamed a second time, and look, seven ears of grain were coming up on one stalk, choice and fine.

**41:6** And seven other ears of grain, thin and wind-blasted, were sprouting up after them.

**41:7** And the seven thin and wind-blasted ears of grain swallowed up the seven choice and full ears of grain. Then Pharaoh woke up, and it was a dream.

**41:8** Now morning came, and his soul was troubled, and he sent and called all the interpreters of Egypt and all its wise men; and Pharaoh recounted the dream to them, and there was no one to declare it to Pharaoh.

**41:9** And the chief cupbearer spoke to Pharaoh, saying, "I recall my sin today.

**41:10** Pharaoh was angry with his servants, and he put us in custody in the house of the chief jailer, both me and the chief baker.

**41:11** And we saw a dream in one night, I and he; each of us saw according to his own dream.

**41:12** Now there was there with us a young man, a Hebrew servant of the chief cook, and we told him, and he interpreted for us.

**41:13** And it came about just as he interpreted for us, so it also happened: I was restored to my office, and the other man was hanged."

**41:14** Then Pharaoh sent and called Joseph, and he brought him out of the stronghold; and they shaved him and changed his garment, and he came to Pharaoh.

**41:15** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have seen a dream, and there is no one to interpret it; and I have heard people saying about you that when you hear dreams, you interpret them."

**41:16** And Joseph answered Pharaoh and said, "Apart from God, the welfare of Pharaoh will not be answered."

**41:17** Then Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, saying, "In my sleep I thought I was standing on the bank of the river,

**41:18** and as if out of the river seven cows were coming up, fine in appearance and choice in flesh, and they were feeding in the reed-grass.

**41:19** And look, seven other cows were coming up behind them out of the river, bad and ugly in appearance and thin in flesh, and they were feeding in the reed-grass; such as these I never saw any uglier in all Egypt.

**41:20** And the seven ugly and thin cows devoured the first seven fine and choice cows,

**41:21** and they entered into their bellies, yet it was not apparent that they had entered into their bellies, and their appearance was ugly just as at the beginning. Then I woke up and fell asleep.

**41:22** And I saw again in my sleep, and as it were, seven ears of grain were coming up on one stalk, full and fine.

**41:23** And seven other ears of grain, thin and wind-blasted, were sprouting up next to them.

**41:24** And the seven thin and wind-blasted ears of grain swallowed up the seven fine and full ears of grain. So I told the interpreters, and there was no one to declare it to me."

**41:25** And Joseph said to Pharaoh, "The dream of Pharaoh is one; all that God is doing He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:26** The seven fine cows are seven years, and the seven fine ears of grain are seven years; the dream of Pharaoh is one.

**41:27** And the seven thin cows coming up behind them are seven years, and the seven thin and wind-blasted ears of grain; they will be seven years of famine.

**41:28** Now the word that I have spoken to Pharaoh: all that God is doing He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:29** Look, seven years are coming, great abundance in all the land of Egypt;

**41:30** and seven years of famine will come after them, and they will forget the plenty in all the land of Egypt, and the famine will consume the land.

**41:31** And the abundance will not be known upon the land because of the famine that will come after these things, for it will be exceedingly severe.

**41:32** And concerning the repeating of the dream to Pharaoh twice, it is because the word from God will be true, and God will hasten to do it.

**41:33** Now therefore look out for a prudent and discerning man, and appoint him over the land of Egypt;

**41:34** and let Pharaoh act and appoint district governors over the land, and let them take a fifth of all the produce of the land of Egypt during the seven years of abundance,

**41:35** and let them gather all the food of these seven fine years that are coming; and let the grain be gathered under the hand of Pharaoh, let food be gathered in the cities.

**41:36** And the food will be kept for the land for the seven years of the famine that will be in the land of Egypt, and the land will not be wiped out in the famine."

**41:37** Now the words were pleasing before Pharaoh and before all his servants.

**41:38** And Pharaoh said to all his servants, "Can we find a man like this, who has the spirit of God in him?"

**41:39** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has shown you all these things, there is no man more prudent and more discerning than you.

**41:40** You will be over my house, and at your mouth all my people will obey; only in the throne will I surpass you."

**41:41** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "See, I appoint you today over all the land of Egypt."

**41:42** And Pharaoh took off the ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand, and he clothed him in a fine linen garment, and he placed a gold collar around his neck.

**41:43** And he had him mount the second of his chariots, and a herald proclaimed before him; and he appointed him over the whole land of Egypt.

**41:44** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh; without you no one will lift up his hand in all the land of Egypt."

**41:45** And Pharaoh called Joseph's name Psonthomphanech; and he gave to him Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of Heliopolis, as a wife.

**41:46** Now Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and passed through all the land of Egypt.

**41:47** And during the seven years of abundance the land produced sheaves;

**41:48** and he gathered all the food of the seven years in which the abundance was in the land of Egypt, and he placed the food in the cities; the food of the fields of the city around it, On, he placed in it.

**41:49** And Joseph gathered grain like the sand of the sea, exceedingly much, until he was unable to count it, for there was no number.

**41:50** Now to Joseph two sons were born before the seven years of the famine came, whom Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of Heliopolis, bore to him.

**41:51** And Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh, saying, "Because God has made me forget all my toils and all the things of my father."

**41:52** And the name of the second he called Ephraim, "Because God has exalted me in the land of my humiliation."

**41:53** Then the seven years of abundance that occurred in the land of Egypt passed away,

**41:54** and the seven years of the famine began to come, just as Joseph had said. And there was famine in all the earth, and in all the land of Egypt there was no bread.

**41:55** And all the land of Egypt grew hungry, and all the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread; and Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, "Go to Joseph, and whatever he says to you, do it."

**41:56** And the famine was over the face of all the earth; and Joseph opened all the granaries and was selling to all the Egyptians.

**41:57** And all the countries came to Egypt to buy from Joseph, for the famine prevailed in all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **41:16** — The Greek literally reads "Apart from God, the welfare of Pharaoh will not be answered," differing from the Hebrew text ("It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh a favorable answer").
- **41:38** — Greek pneuma theou ("spirit of God," without the article) can be understood either as a divinely given spirit or as "the Spirit of God" (compare Gen 1:2).
- **41:45** — The Greek preserves distinct forms for the Egyptian names: "Psonthomphanech" (Hebrew "Zaphenath-paneah"), "Petrephe" (Πετρεφῆ, rendered with standard English "Potiphera" as in 46:20; most Greek witnesses read "Petephres"), and "Heliopolis" ("City of the Sun," rendering Hebrew "On").
- **41:48** — This Greek text contains the name "On" after "around it," likely an early gloss identifying the city or an uncial misreading of a relative pronoun.
- **41:52** — This Greek text reads "exalted" (Greek hypsosen) rather than the more common Septuagint reading "increased/made fruitful" (euxesen), forming a direct contrast with "humiliation."
- **41:54** — This Greek text reads "there was no bread" (Greek ouk esan artoi), whereas the Hebrew text and other Greek witnesses state that "there was bread" in all the land of Egypt.
