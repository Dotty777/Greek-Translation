## Text

**15:1** Now after these words, the word of the Lord came to Abram in a vision, saying, "Do not fear, Abram; I shield you; your reward will be exceedingly great."

**15:2** And Abram said, "Master Lord, what will You give me? For I am departing childless, and the son of Masek my houseborn woman, this is Damascus Eliezer."

**15:3** And Abram said, "Since You have not given me seed, my houseborn servant will be my heir."

**15:4** And immediately the voice of the Lord came to him, saying, "This one will not be your heir; instead, the one who will come out from you, he will be your heir."

**15:5** And He brought him outside and said to him, "Look up now toward heaven and count the stars, if you are able to number them." And He said, "So will your seed be."

**15:6** And Abram believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness.

**15:7** And He said to him, "I am God who brought you out from the country of the Chaldeans, so as to give you this land to inherit."

**15:8** And he said, "Master Lord, by what will I know that I will inherit it?"

**15:9** And He said to him, "Take for Me a three-year-old heifer, and a three-year-old female goat, and a three-year-old ram, and a turtledove, and a pigeon."

**15:10** So he took for Him all these, and divided them down the middle, and placed them opposite one another; but the birds he did not divide.

**15:11** And birds came down upon the carcasses, their divided pieces; and Abram sat down with them.

**15:12** Now around the setting of the sun, a trance fell upon Abram, and look, a great dark dread falls upon him.

**15:13** And it was said to Abram, "You will surely know that your seed will be a sojourner in a land not its own, and they will mistreat it, and enslave them, and humble them for four hundred years.

**15:14** But the nation whom they may serve I will judge; and after these things they will come out here with many possessions.

**15:15** As for you, you will depart to your fathers in peace, nourished in a good old age.

**15:16** And in the fourth generation they will return here; for the sins of the Amorites have not yet been filled up until now."

**15:17** And when the sun was approaching its setting, a flame came to be; and look, a smoking oven and torches of fire, which passed through between these divided pieces.

**15:18** There God made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your seed I will give this land, from the river of Egypt as far as the great river Euphrates:

**15:19** the Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kelmonites,

**15:20** and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaim,

**15:21** and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Hivites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Where the Hebrew has an obscure phrase usually rendered "heir of my house," the Greek reads "the son of Masek my houseborn woman," treating the Hebrew word mesheq as a personal name.
- **15:6** — The Greek reads "God" and the passive "it was reckoned" (Hebrew has the divine name and active "he reckoned it"), the wording later quoted in Romans 4:3 and Galatians 3:6.
- **15:11** — The Greek reads "Abram sat down with them" (συνεκάθισεν), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants vocalized as "drove away" in the Masoretic Text.
- **15:15** — The Greek reads "nourished" (τραφεὶς) where Hebrew has "buried," and in 15:17 reads "a flame" (φλὸξ) where Hebrew has "darkness"; in 15:19 the Greek spells "Kelmonites" (Hebrew Kadmonites) and includes the Hivites in 15:21.
