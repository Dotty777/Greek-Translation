## Text

**39:1** Now Joseph was brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, the eunuch of Pharaoh, the chief cook, an Egyptian man, acquired him from the hands of the Ishmaelites who brought him down there.

**39:2** And the Lord was with Joseph, and he was a successful man; and he was in the house with the Egyptian master.

**39:3** Now his master knew that the Lord was with him, and that whatever he did, the Lord prospered in his hands.

**39:4** And Joseph found favor before his master, and he was pleasing to him; and he appointed him over his house, and everything that belonged to him he gave into Joseph's hand.

**39:5** Now it came about after he was appointed over his house and over everything that belonged to him, that the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian on account of Joseph; and the blessing of the Lord came upon all his possessions, in the house and in the field.

**39:6** And he turned over everything that belonged to him into the hands of Joseph, and he knew nothing of his own affairs except the bread that he himself ate. And Joseph was handsome in form and very attractive in appearance.

**39:7** And it came about after these things that his master's wife cast her eyes upon Joseph, and she said, "Lie with me."

**39:8** But he was not willing, and he said to his master's wife, "If my master knows nothing in his house because of me, and everything that belongs to him he has given into my hands,

**39:9** and he does not surpass me in anything in his house, nor has he withheld anything from me except you, because you are his wife, then how shall I do this wicked thing and sin before God?"

**39:10** And when she spoke to Joseph day after day, he did not listen to her to sleep with her, to be with her.

**39:11** Now a certain such day came about: Joseph went into the house to do his work, and no one was inside the house.

**39:12** And she pulled him by his garments, saying, "Lie with me." And leaving his garments, he fled and went outside.

**39:13** And it came about when she saw that he had left his garments in her hands and had fled and gone outside,

**39:14** that she called those who were in the house and spoke to them, saying, "Look, he brought in to us a Hebrew servant to mock us! He came in to me, saying, 'Lie with me,' and I cried out with a loud voice.

**39:15** And when he heard that I raised my voice and cried out, leaving his garments beside me, he fled and went outside."

**39:16** And she left the garments beside her until the master came into his house.

**39:17** And she spoke to him according to these words, saying, "The Hebrew servant whom you brought in to us came in to me to mock me, and he said to me, 'Lie with me.'

**39:18** And when he heard that I raised my voice and cried out, he left his garments beside me and fled and went outside."

**39:19** Now it came about when the master heard the words of his wife, all that she spoke to him, saying, "This is what your servant did to me," that he became furious with anger.

**39:20** And the master took Joseph and threw him into the stronghold, into the place where the king's prisoners are held there in the stronghold.

**39:21** And the Lord was with Joseph and poured down mercy upon him, and He gave him favor before the chief jailer.

**39:22** And the chief jailer gave the prison into Joseph's hand, and all those led away who were in the prison, and everything that they do there.

**39:23** The chief jailer knew nothing because of him, for everything was in Joseph's hand, because the Lord was with him; and whatever he himself did, the Lord prospered in his hands.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — The Greek reads "eunuch" (εὐνοῦχος) and "chief cook" (ἀρχιμάγειρος), reflecting the Septuagint's literal rendering of titles often translated from the Hebrew as "officer" and "captain of the guard."
- **39:8** — The Greek opens Joseph's reply with conditional "If" (εἰ), rendering the Hebrew interjection hen ("look") as a conditional clause answered by "then how..." in verse 9.
- **39:12** — Throughout verses 12-18, the Greek uses the plural "garments" (τὰ ἱμάτια) where the Hebrew has the singular "garment," and omits "in her hand" here while retaining it in verse 13.
- **39:20** — The Greek renders Joseph's place of confinement here and in 40:14 as "stronghold" (ὀχύρωμα), distinct from "prison" (δεσμωτήριον) in 39:22 and 40:3, 5.
