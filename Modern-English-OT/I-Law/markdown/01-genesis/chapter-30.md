## Text

**30:1** Now when Rachel saw that she had not borne children to Jacob, Rachel envied her sister and said to Jacob, "Give me children, or else I will die."

**30:2** And Jacob became angry with Rachel and said to her, "Am I in the place of God, who has deprived you of the fruit of the womb?"

**30:3** And Rachel said to Jacob, "Here is my female servant Bilhah; go in to her, and she will give birth on my knees, so that I too may have children through her."

**30:4** And she gave him her female servant Bilhah as a wife, and Jacob went in to her.

**30:5** And Bilhah, Rachel's female servant, conceived and bore a son to Jacob.

**30:6** And Rachel said, "God has judged in my favor, and has heard my voice, and has given me a son." Therefore she called his name Dan.

**30:7** And Bilhah, Rachel's female servant, conceived again and bore a second son to Jacob.

**30:8** And Rachel said, "God has helped me, and I have contended with my sister and prevailed." And she called his name Naphtali.

**30:9** Now Leah saw that she had stopped bearing children, and she took her female servant Zilpah and gave her to Jacob as a wife.

**30:10** And Jacob went in to her, and Zilpah, Leah's female servant, conceived and bore a son to Jacob.

**30:11** And Leah said, "With good fortune!" And she named him Gad.

**30:12** And Zilpah, Leah's female servant, conceived again and bore yet a second son to Jacob.

**30:13** And Leah said, "Blessed am I, for all the women call me blessed." And she called his name Asher.

**30:14** Now Reuben went out in the days of the wheat harvest and found mandrake apples in the field, and he brought them to his mother Leah. And Rachel said to Leah, "Give me some of your son's mandrakes."

**30:15** And Leah said, "Is it not enough for you that you have taken my husband? Will you also take my son's mandrakes?" And Rachel said, "Not so; let him lie with you tonight in exchange for your son's mandrakes."

**30:16** Now Jacob came in from the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him and said, "You must come in to me today, for I have hired you in exchange for my son's mandrakes." And he lay with her that night.

**30:17** And God heard her, and she conceived and bore a fifth son to Jacob.

**30:18** And Leah said, "God has given me my wages because I gave my female servant to my husband." And she called his name Issachar, which means "Wages."

**30:19** And Leah conceived again and bore a sixth son to Jacob.

**30:20** And Leah said, "God has granted me a good gift; at this present time my husband will choose me, for I have borne him six sons." And she called his name Zebulun.

**30:21** And after this she bore a daughter and called her name Dinah. And she stopped bearing children.

**30:22** Now God remembered Rachel, and God heard her and opened her womb.

**30:23** And she conceived and bore a son to Jacob. And Rachel said, "God has taken away my reproach."

**30:24** And she called his name Joseph, saying, "May God add to me another son."

**30:25** Now it came about, when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said to Laban, "Send me away so that I may go to my place and to my land.

**30:26** Give me the wives and the children for whom I have served you, so that I may depart; for you know the service with which I have served you."

**30:27** And Laban said to him, "If I have found favor before you, I would have observed omens; for God has blessed me at your coming in.

**30:28** Specify your wages to me, and I will give it to you."

**30:29** And Jacob said to him, "You know how I have served you, and how much livestock of yours was with me.

**30:30** For what was before me was little, and it increased to a multitude, and the Lord blessed you at my foot. Now then, when will I also make a house for myself?"

**30:31** And Laban said to him, "What should I give you?" And Jacob said to him, "You will give me nothing; if you do this thing for me, I will again shepherd your sheep and keep them.

**30:32** Let your sheep pass by today, and separate from there every dark-gray sheep among the lambs and every speckled and white one among the goats; it will be my wages.

**30:33** And my righteousness will answer for me tomorrow, because my wages are before you: every one that is not speckled and white-streaked among the goats and dark-gray among the lambs will be stolen with me."

**30:34** And Laban said to him, "Let it be according to your word."

**30:35** And he separated on that day the speckled and white-streaked male goats, and all the speckled and white-streaked female goats, and every one that was white among them, and every one that was dark-gray among the lambs, and gave them into the hand of his sons.

**30:36** And he put a three days' journey between them and Jacob, while Jacob shepherded the sheep of Laban that were left behind.

**30:37** Now Jacob took for himself a fresh rod of storax, and of walnut, and of plane tree, and Jacob peeled white strips on them, stripping away the green; and on the rods the white that he had peeled appeared variegated.

**30:38** And he set the rods that he had peeled in the troughs of the watering places of water, so that whenever the sheep came to drink in front of the rods, and when they came to drink, the sheep would come into heat toward the rods.

**30:39** And the sheep bore white-streaked and variegated and ash-colored speckled young.

**30:40** And Jacob separated the lambs, and set in front of the sheep a white-streaked ram and every variegated one among the lambs; and he set apart flocks for himself by himself, and did not mix them with Laban's sheep.

**30:41** Now it came about at the time when the sheep were in heat, conceiving, that Jacob placed the rods in front of the sheep in the troughs, so that they would come into heat facing the rods;

**30:42** for whenever the sheep gave birth, he would not place them; so the unmarked ones became Laban's, and the marked ones became Jacob's.

**30:43** And the man became very, very rich, and he came to have much livestock, and cattle, and male servants and female servants, and camels and donkeys.

## Translator Notes

- **30:8** — The Septuagint reads "God has helped me, and I have contended with my sister" (συνεβάλετό μοι ὁ θεός, καὶ συνανεστράφην τῇ ἀδελφῇ μου), rendering the Naphtali wordplay differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "with mighty wrestlings I have wrestled."
- **30:15** — The Septuagint reads "Not so" (οὐχ οὕτως), reflecting a different vocalization or reading of the Hebrew consonant group that the Masoretic Text reads as "Therefore" (laken).
- **30:27** — The Septuagint construes οἰωνισάμην ἄν ("I would have observed omens") as the potential conclusion to "If I have found favor before you," whereas the Masoretic Text treats the verb as a statement ("I have learned by divination").
- **30:32** — The Septuagint has Jacob instruct Laban with "Let your sheep pass by today, and separate from there," whereas the Masoretic Text has Jacob speak in the first person ("Let me pass through all your flock today, removing from there").
- **30:42** — In vv. 41-42 the Septuagint contrasts when the sheep were "conceiving" versus "giving birth," and calls the resulting offspring "unmarked" (ἄσημα) and "marked" (ἐπίσημα), diverging from the Masoretic Text's "stronger" and "feebler" flocks.
