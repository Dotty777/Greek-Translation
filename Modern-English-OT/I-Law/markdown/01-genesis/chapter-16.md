## Text

**16:1** Now Sarai, the wife of Abram, was not bearing children to him; and she had an Egyptian maidservant whose name was Hagar.

**16:2** And Sarai said to Abram, "Look, the Lord has shut me up from bearing; go in, therefore, to my maidservant, so that you may have children from her." And Abram listened to her voice.

**16:3** And Sarai, the wife of Abram, took Hagar the Egyptian, her own maidservant, after ten years of Abram's dwelling in the land of Canaan, and gave her to Abram her husband as a wife for him.

**16:4** And he went in to Hagar, and she conceived; and she saw that she was pregnant, and the mistress was dishonored before her.

**16:5** And Sarai said to Abram, "I am wronged because of you. I gave my maidservant into your embrace, and when she saw that she was pregnant, I was dishonored before her. May God judge between me and you."

**16:6** And Abram said to Sarai, "Look, your maidservant is before you; treat her as it may be pleasing to you." And Sarai mistreated her, and she fled from her presence.

**16:7** And an angel of the Lord God found her by the spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the road to Shur.

**16:8** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, where are you coming from, and where are you going?" And she said, "From the presence of Sarai my mistress I am fleeing."

**16:9** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Return to your mistress and humble yourself under her hands."

**16:10** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "I will surely multiply your seed, and it will not be counted because of the multitude."

**16:11** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Look, you are pregnant, and you will bear a son, and you will call his name Ishmael, because the Lord has listened to your humiliation.

**16:12** He will be a wild man; his hands will be against all, and the hands of all against him, and facing all his brothers he will dwell."

**16:13** And Hagar called the name of the Lord who was speaking to her, "You are the God who looked upon me"; for she said, "For indeed I saw in person the One who appeared to me."

**16:14** Because of this she called the well, "Well of the One Whom I Saw in Person"; look, it is between Kadesh and Bered.

**16:15** And Hagar bore a son to Abram; and Abram called the name of his son, whom Hagar bore to him, Ishmael.

**16:16** Now Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Greek spells her name Sara (Σάρα) throughout Genesis 11–16 before lengthening it to Sarra (Σάρρα) in 17:15; standard modern English Sarai / Sarah is used consistently across chapters 11–17.
- **16:12** — "A wild man" translates Greek ἄγροικος ἄνθρωπος (literally "a rustic" or "untamed man"), corresponding to Hebrew "a wild donkey of a man."
- **16:13** — Both here and in the well's name in 16:14 (Φρέαρ οὗ ἐνώπιον εἶδον, corresponding to Hebrew Beer-lahai-roi), the Greek uses ἐνώπιον ("in person" or "face to face") to express Hagar's marvel at having seen God directly.
