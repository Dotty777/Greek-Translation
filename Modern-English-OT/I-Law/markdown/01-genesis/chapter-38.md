## Text

**38:1** Now it came about at that time that Judah went down from his brothers and came as far as a certain Adullamite man whose name was Hirah.

**38:2** And there Judah saw a Canaanite man's daughter whose name was Shua; and he took her and went in to her.

**38:3** And she conceived and gave birth to a son, and called his name Er.

**38:4** And she conceived again and gave birth to a son, and called his name Onan.

**38:5** And once more she gave birth to a son, and called his name Shelah; and she was in Chezib when she gave birth to them.

**38:6** And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, whose name was Tamar.

**38:7** Now Er, the firstborn of Judah, was wicked before the Lord, and God killed him.

**38:8** Then Judah said to Onan, "Go in to your brother's wife and marry her as brother-in-law, and raise up offspring for your brother."

**38:9** And Onan, knowing that the offspring would not be his, it happened that whenever he went in to his brother's wife, he poured it out on the ground so as not to give offspring to his brother.

**38:10** And the thing appeared wicked before God that he did this, and He put this one to death also.

**38:11** Then Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Remain a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up"; for he said, "Perhaps this one also will die like his brothers." So Tamar went away and remained in her father's house.

**38:12** Now the days multiplied, and Shua, Judah's wife, died; and when Judah was comforted, he went up to those who were shearing his sheep, he and Hirah his shepherd, the Adullamite, to Timnah.

**38:13** And it was reported to Tamar his daughter-in-law, saying, "Look, your father-in-law has gone up to Timnah to shear his sheep."

**38:14** And taking off her widowhood clothes from herself, she wrapped herself in a veil and adorned herself, and she sat by the gates of Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah; for she saw that his son Shelah had grown up, yet he had not given her to him as a wife.

**38:15** And when Judah saw her, he supposed her to be a prostitute, for she had covered her face, and he did not recognize her.

**38:16** And he turned aside from the road to her and said to her, "Let me come in to you"; for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, "What will you give me if you come in to me?"

**38:17** And he said, "I will send you a young goat from the flock." And she said, "If you give a pledge until you send it."

**38:18** And he said, "What pledge shall I give you?" And she said, "Your signet ring and the necklace and the staff that is in your hand." And he gave them to her, and went in to her, and she conceived by him.

**38:19** And getting up, she went away, and took off the veil from herself, and put on her widowhood clothes.

**38:20** Then Judah sent the young goat by the hand of his shepherd, the Adullamite, to recover the pledge from the woman; but he did not find her.

**38:21** And he asked the men who were in that place, "Where is the prostitute who was in Enaim by the road?" And they said, "There was no prostitute here."

**38:22** And he returned to Judah and said, "I did not find her, and the men from the place say that there is no prostitute here."

**38:23** And Judah said, "Let her keep them, so that we may not be ridiculed; I indeed have sent this young goat, but you have not found her."

**38:24** And it came about after three months that it was reported to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has acted as a prostitute, and look, she is pregnant from prostitution." And Judah said, "Bring her out and let her be burned."

**38:25** But as she was being led out, she sent to her father-in-law, saying, "I am pregnant by the man to whom these belong." And she said, "Identify whose are the signet ring and the necklace and this staff."

**38:26** And Judah recognized them and said, "Tamar has been justified more than I, because I did not give her to my son Shelah." And he did not know her again.

**38:27** And it came about, when she gave birth, that she had twins in her womb.

**38:28** And it came about, while she was giving birth, that one put out his hand first; and the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his hand, saying, "This one will come out first."

**38:29** But as he drew back his hand, immediately his brother came out. And she said, "Why was a barrier broken through because of you?" And she called his name Perez.

**38:30** And after this his brother came out, on whose hand was the scarlet thread; and she called his name Zerah.

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — Using the feminine relative pronoun (ᾗ), the Greek identifies Shua as the name of Judah's Canaanite wife (cf. 38:12), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text makes Shua the name of her father.
- **38:5** — The Greek reads 'she was in Chezib when she gave birth to them' (plural), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('he was in Chezib when she bore him').
- **38:12** — The Greek identifies Hirah as Judah's 'shepherd' (ποιμήν; also in 38:20), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's 'friend.'
- **38:29** — The Greek renders the Hebrew wordplay on the name Perez ('breach') with the verb 'was broken through' (διεκόπη) and the noun 'barrier' or 'fence' (φραγμός).
