## Text

**33:1** Now Jacob looked up and saw, and look, Esau his brother was coming, he and four hundred men with him; and Jacob divided the children among Leah and Rachel and the two female servants.

**33:2** And he placed the two female servants and their sons first, and Leah and her children behind, and Rachel and Joseph last.

**33:3** And he himself went on ahead of them, and bowed down to the ground seven times until he came near to his brother.

**33:4** And Esau ran to meet him, and embracing him, kissed him and fell on his neck; and both of them wept.

**33:5** And looking up, he saw the women and the children, and said, "What are these to you?" And he said, "The children with whom God has shown mercy to your servant."

**33:6** Then the female servants and their children came near and bowed down;

**33:7** and Leah and her children came near and bowed down; and after this Rachel and Joseph came near and bowed down.

**33:8** And he said, "What are these to you, all these camps that I have met?" And he said, "So that your servant may find favor in your eyes, lord."

**33:9** But Esau said, "I have much, brother; let what is yours be yours."

**33:10** Then Jacob said, "If I have found favor before you, accept the gifts from my hands; for this reason I have seen your face as one might see the face of God, and you will be pleased with me.

**33:11** Take my blessings which I have brought to you, because God has shown mercy to me and I have everything." And he urged him strongly, and he took them.

**33:12** And he said, "Let us set out and go straight ahead."

**33:13** But he said to him, "My lord knows that the children are rather tender, and the sheep and the cattle are giving birth in my care; if then I drive them hard for one day, all the livestock will die.

**33:14** Let my lord go on ahead before the servant; and I will gain strength along the way at the leisurely pace of the journey before me and at the pace of the young children, until I come to my lord in Seir."

**33:15** Then Esau said, "I will leave with you some of the people who are with me." But he said, "Why this? It is enough that I have found favor before you, lord."

**33:16** So Esau returned on that day on his way to Seir.

**33:17** And Jacob set out for Tents; and he made houses for himself there, and for his livestock he made tents; therefore he called the name of that place Tents.

**33:18** And Jacob came to Salem, a city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Mesopotamia of Syria; and he encamped in front of the city.

**33:19** And he acquired the portion of the field where he pitched his tent from Hamor the father of Shechem for a hundred lambs.

**33:20** And he set up an altar there and called upon the God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **33:14** — The Greek reads "I will gain strength (enischyso) along the way at the leisurely pace of the journey," differing from the Hebrew idiom "I will lead on slowly."
- **33:17** — The Greek translates Hebrew Succoth as Skenai ("Tents"), echoing the tents made for Jacob's livestock, and reads plural "houses" rather than singular "house."
- **33:18** — The Greek takes Hebrew shalem ("safely") as a proper name, "Salem, a city of Shechem," and renders Paddan-aram as "Mesopotamia of Syria."
- **33:19** — The Greek reads "from Hamor the father of Shechem" (without "the sons of") and renders the monetary unit Hebrew qesitah as "lambs."
- **33:20** — The Greek reads "called upon the God of Israel" (epekalesato ton theon Israel) rather than naming the altar El-Elohe-Israel as in the Hebrew text.
