## Text

**21:1** And the Lord visited Sarah, just as He had said; and the Lord did for Sarah just as He had spoken.

**21:2** And having conceived, Sarah bore a son to Abraham in old age, at the time just as the Lord had spoken to him.

**21:3** And Abraham called the name of his son who was born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.

**21:4** And Abraham circumcised Isaac on the eighth day, just as God had commanded him.

**21:5** Now Abraham was one hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.

**21:6** And Sarah said, "The Lord has made laughter for me; for whoever hears will rejoice with me."

**21:7** And she said, "Who will report to Abraham that Sarah is nursing a child? For I have borne a son in my old age."

**21:8** And the child grew and was weaned; and Abraham made a great feast on the day on which his son Isaac was weaned.

**21:9** Now Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, who was born to Abraham, playing with her own son Isaac,

**21:10** and she said to Abraham, "Cast out this maidservant and her son; for the son of the maidservant will certainly not inherit with my son Isaac."

**21:11** And the matter appeared very harsh before Abraham concerning his son Ishmael.

**21:12** But God said to Abraham, "Do not let the matter be harsh before you concerning the child and concerning the maidservant; whatever Sarah says to you, listen to her voice, because in Isaac offspring will be called for you.

**21:13** And as for the son of this maidservant also, I will make him into a great nation, because he is your offspring."

**21:14** So Abraham rose in the morning, and took loaves of bread and a skin of water and gave them to Hagar, and placed them on her shoulder along with the child, and sent her away. And departing, she wandered in the wilderness near the Well of the Oath.

**21:15** And the water ran out from the skin, and she cast the child under one fir tree.

**21:16** And going away, she sat opposite him farther off, about a bowshot away; for she said, "I will certainly not see the death of my child." And she sat opposite him at a distance; and crying out, the child wept.

**21:17** And God heard the voice of the child from the place where he was, and the angel of God called to Hagar from heaven and said to her, "What is the matter, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has listened to the voice of your child from the place where he is.

**21:18** Get up and take the child, and hold him firmly with your hand; for I will make him into a great nation."

**21:19** And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of living water; and she went and filled the skin with water and gave the child a drink.

**21:20** And God was with the child, and he grew up and lived in the wilderness; and he became an archer.

**21:21** And he lived in the wilderness, and his mother took a wife for him from the land of Egypt.

**21:22** Now it happened at that time that Abimelech, along with Ahuzzath his bridal attendant and Phicol the commander of his army, spoke to Abraham, saying, "God is with you in everything that you do.

**21:23** Now therefore, swear to me by God that you will not wrong me, nor my offspring, nor my name; but according to the righteousness that I have done with you, you will do with me, and to the land in which you have lived as a foreigner."

**21:24** And Abraham said, "I will swear."

**21:25** And Abraham reproved Abimelech concerning the wells of water that the servants of Abimelech had seized.

**21:26** And Abimelech said to him, "I did not know who did this thing; neither did you tell me, nor did I hear of it except today."

**21:27** And Abraham took sheep and calves and gave them to Abimelech; and both of them made a covenant.

**21:28** And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves.

**21:29** And Abimelech said to Abraham, "What are the seven ewe lambs of these sheep that you set by themselves?"

**21:30** And Abraham said, "You will receive these seven ewe lambs from me, so that they may be a witness that I dug this well."

**21:31** Therefore he named the name of that place Well of Swearing, because there both of them swore.

**21:32** And they made a covenant at the Well of the Oath. Then Abimelech rose up, along with Ahuzzath his bridal attendant and Phicol the commander of his army, and they returned to the land of the Philistines.

**21:33** And Abraham planted a field at the Well of the Oath, and called there on the name of the Lord, Eternal God.

**21:34** And Abraham lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines many days.

## Translator Notes

- **21:9** — The Greek text explicitly adds "playing with her own son Isaac," specifying the object of Ishmael's playing (absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text).
- **21:14** — Here and in vv. 32-33 (as well as 22:19), the Greek text translates the place name Beersheba as "the Well of the Oath" (and "Well of Swearing" in v. 31).
- **21:16** — The Greek text reads that the child cried out and wept rather than Hagar; similarly, v. 15 specifies "one fir tree" rather than a shrub, and v. 21 omits the name "Paran."
- **21:33** — The Greek renders the Hebrew word for "tamarisk tree" as "a field" (aroura, arable land); earlier, in vv. 22 and 32, the Greek also includes "Ahuzzath his bridal attendant" alongside Abimelech and Phicol (cf. 26:26).
