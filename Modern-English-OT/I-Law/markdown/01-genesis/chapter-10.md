## Text

**10:1** Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and sons were born to them after the flood.

**10:2** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Elishah, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

**10:3** And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

**10:4** And the sons of Javan: Elishah and Tarshish, Kittim, and Rhodians.

**10:5** From these the islands of the nations were separated in their land, each according to language, in their tribes and in their nations.

**10:6** And the sons of Ham: Cush and Mizraim, Put and Canaan.

**10:7** And the sons of Cush: Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabteca. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**10:8** And Cush fathered Nimrod; he began to be a giant upon the earth.

**10:9** He was a giant hunter before the Lord God; therefore they will say, "Like Nimrod, a giant hunter before the Lord."

**10:10** And the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

**10:11** Out of that land Asshur went forth, and he built Nineveh, and the city of Rehoboth, and Calah,

**10:12** and Dasem, between Nineveh and Calah; this is the great city.

**10:13** And Mizraim fathered the Ludim, and the Naphtuhim, and the Anamim, and the Lehabim,

**10:14** and the Pathrusim, and the Casluhim, from whom the Philistines came out, and the Caphtorim.

**10:15** And Canaan fathered Sidon the firstborn, and the Hittite,

**10:16** and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

**10:17** and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

**10:18** and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite. And after this the tribes of the Canaanites were scattered.

**10:19** And the borders of the Canaanites were from Sidon until coming to Gerar and Gaza, until coming as far as Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, as far as Dasa.

**10:20** These are the sons of Ham in their tribes, according to their languages, in their lands, and in their nations.

**10:21** And to Shem also children were born, the father of all the sons of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder.

**10:22** The sons of Shem: Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram.

**10:23** And the sons of Aram: Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

**10:24** And Arphaxad fathered Cainan, and Cainan fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

**10:25** And to Eber two sons were born; the name of the one was Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and the name of his brother was Joktan.

**10:26** And Joktan fathered Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

**10:27** and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

**10:28** and Abimael, and Sheba,

**10:29** and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were sons of Joktan.

**10:30** And their dwelling place was from Mesha until coming to Sephar, a mountain of the east.

**10:31** These are the sons of Shem in their tribes, according to their languages, in their lands, and in their nations.

**10:32** These are the tribes of the sons of Noah according to their generations, according to their nations; from these the islands of the nations were scattered upon the earth after the flood.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Septuagint includes Elishah among the sons of Japheth here (in addition to listing him as a son of Javan in 10:4), whereas the Hebrew text lists seven sons in 10:2.
- **10:4** — Greek "Rhodioi" ("Rhodians") reflects the form "Rodanim" (compare 1 Chr 1:7) rather than "Dodanim" in the Hebrew text of Genesis 10:4.
- **10:8** — Greek "gigas" ("giant," also in 10:9) translates the Hebrew "gibbor" ("mighty one").
- **10:12** — Greek "Dasem" corresponds to "Resen" in the Hebrew text; similarly, Greek "Dasa" in 10:19 corresponds to Hebrew "Lasha."
- **10:24** — The Septuagint includes Cainan (Greek "Kainam") between Arphaxad and Shelah, as in Luke 3:36, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits him (note also that the Septuagint reads "Meshech" for Hebrew "Mash" in 10:23 and omits "Obal" in 10:28).
