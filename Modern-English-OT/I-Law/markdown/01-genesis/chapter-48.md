## Text

**48:1** Now it happened after these things that it was reported to Joseph, "Your father is ill." And taking along his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, he came to Jacob.

**48:2** And it was reported to Jacob, saying, "Look, your son Joseph is coming to you." And Israel strengthened himself and sat up on the bed.

**48:3** And Jacob said to Joseph, "My God appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan and blessed me,

**48:4** and He said to me, 'Look, I will cause you to grow and multiply you, and I will make you into assemblies of nations, and I will give this land to you and to your seed after you for an everlasting possession.'

**48:5** Now therefore, your two sons who were born to you in Egypt before I came to Egypt to you are mine; Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, just like Reuben and Simeon.

**48:6** But the children whom you may father after these will be under the name of their brothers; they will be called in their brothers' allotments.

**48:7** And as for me, when I was coming from Mesopotamia of Syria, Rachel your mother died in the land of Canaan, as I was approaching the racecourse of Chabratha of the land, to come to Ephrathah; and I buried her on the road of the racecourse; this is Bethlehem."

**48:8** And when Israel saw the sons of Joseph, he said, "Who are these to you?"

**48:9** And Joseph said to his father, "They are my sons, whom God has given me here." And Jacob said, "Bring them to me so that I may bless them."

**48:10** Now the eyes of Israel had grown dim from old age, and he was not able to see. And he brought them near to him, and he kissed them and embraced them.

**48:11** And Israel said to Joseph, "Look, I have not been deprived of your face, and look, God has shown me your seed as well."

**48:12** And Joseph brought them out from his knees, and they bowed down to him with their faces to the ground.

**48:13** And Joseph took his two sons, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel's left, and Manasseh on his left toward Israel's right, and brought them near to him.

**48:14** But Israel stretched out his right hand and laid it upon the head of Ephraim (now he was the younger), and his left hand upon the head of Manasseh, crossing his hands.

**48:15** And he blessed them and said, "The God whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac pleased in His sight, the Lord who has nourished me from youth until this day,

**48:16** the angel who delivers me from all evils, may He bless these boys; and my name will be called upon them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and may they be multiplied into a great multitude upon the earth."

**48:17** And when Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand upon the head of Ephraim, it appeared grievous to him; and Joseph took hold of his father's hand to lift it from the head of Ephraim onto the head of Manasseh.

**48:18** And Joseph said to his father, "Not so, father; for this one is the firstborn. Place your right hand upon his head."

**48:19** And he was not willing, but said, "I know, child, I know. He too will become a people, and he too will be exalted; yet his younger brother will be greater than he, and his offspring will become a multitude of nations."

**48:20** And he blessed them on that day, saying, "In you Israel will be blessed, saying, 'May God make you like Ephraim and like Manasseh.'" And he placed Ephraim before Manasseh.

**48:21** And Israel said to Joseph, "Look, I am dying, and God will be with you and will bring you back to the land of your fathers.

**48:22** And as for me, I give you Shechem, singled out above your brothers, which I took from the hand of the Amorites with my sword and bow."

## Translator Notes

- **48:3** — The Greek reads "my God," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "God Almighty" (El Shaddai).
- **48:7** — The Greek renders the Hebrew distance phrase (kibrat ha-aretz) with both the gloss "the racecourse" (hippodromos) and the transliteration "Chabratha."
- **48:22** — The Greek treats the Hebrew word for "ridge" or "portion" (shekhem) as the city name Shechem (Sikima), confirmed by the feminine relative pronoun "which" (hen).
