## Text

**46:1** Now Israel set out, he and all that was his, and they came to the Well of the Oath, and there he offered a sacrifice to the God of his father Isaac.

**46:2** And God spoke to Israel in a vision of the night, saying, "Jacob, Jacob." And he said, "What is it?"

**46:3** And He said, "I am the God of your fathers. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you into a great nation there;

**46:4** and I will go down with you to Egypt, and I will bring you up in the end; and Joseph will lay his hands upon your eyes."

**46:5** Then Jacob rose up from the Well of the Oath, and the sons of Israel took up their father and the dependents and their wives onto the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him.

**46:6** And having taken their possessions and all the property that they had acquired from the land of Canaan, Jacob entered into Egypt, and all his offspring with him,

**46:7** sons and the sons of his sons with him, daughters and daughters of his sons with him; and he brought all his offspring into Egypt.

**46:8** Now these are the names of the sons of Israel who entered into Egypt, Jacob and his sons: the firstborn of Jacob was Reuben.

**46:9** And the sons of Reuben: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**46:10** And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar and Samuel, the son of the Canaanite woman.

**46:11** And the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**46:12** And the sons of Judah: Er and Onan and Shelah and Perez and Zerah. Now Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. And the sons of Perez were Hezron and Jemuel.

**46:13** And the sons of Issachar: Tola and Puvah and Jashub and Zambram.

**46:14** And the sons of Zebulun: Sered and Hezron and Jahleel.

**46:15** These are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Mesopotamia of Syria, and Dinah his daughter; all the souls, sons and daughters, were thirty-three.

**46:16** And the sons of Gad: Ziphion and Haggi and Shuni and Thasoban and Eri and Arodi and Areli.

**46:17** And the sons of Asher: Imnah and Jessai and Jeul and Beriah, and Serah their sister. And the sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel.

**46:18** These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob, sixteen souls.

**46:19** And the sons of Rachel, the wife of Jacob: Joseph and Benjamin.

**46:20** Now sons were born to Joseph in the land of Egypt, whom Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of Heliopolis, bore to him: Manasseh and Ephraim. And sons were born to Manasseh, whom the Syrian concubine bore to him: Machir; and Machir fathered Gilead. And the sons of Ephraim, the brother of Manasseh: Sutalaam and Taam. And the sons of Sutalaam: Edem.

**46:21** And the sons of Benjamin: Bela and Chobor and Ashbel. And the sons of Bela were Gera and Naaman and Ehi and Rosh and Muppim and Huppim; and Gera fathered Ard.

**46:22** These are the sons of Rachel whom Jacob fathered; all the souls were eighteen.

**46:23** And the sons of Dan: Hushim.

**46:24** And the sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel and Guni and Jezer and Shillem.

**46:25** These are the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to Rachel his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob; all the souls were seven.

**46:26** Now all the souls who entered with Jacob into Egypt, those who came out from his loins, apart from the wives of Jacob's sons, all the souls were sixty-six.

**46:27** And the sons of Joseph who were born to him in the land of Egypt were nine souls. All the souls of the house of Jacob who entered into Egypt were seventy-five.

**46:28** Now he sent Judah ahead of them to Joseph to meet him at Heroonpolis, into the land of Rameses.

**46:29** And Joseph hitched his chariots and went up to meet Israel his father at Heroonpolis; and when he appeared to him, he fell upon his neck and wept with abundant weeping.

**46:30** And Israel said to Joseph, "I may die from now on, since I have seen your face, for you are still alive."

**46:31** And Joseph said to his brothers, "I will go up and report to Pharaoh, and I will say to him, 'My brothers and the house of my father, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me;

**46:32** and the men are shepherds, for they were men who keep livestock; and they have brought their livestock and their cattle and all that is theirs.'

**46:33** So if Pharaoh calls you and says to you, 'What is your occupation?'

**46:34** you will say, 'We, your servants, are men who keep livestock from youth until now, both we and our fathers,' so that you may dwell in the land of Goshen of Arabia; for every shepherd of sheep is an abomination to the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **46:10** — The Greek genealogy preserves several Septuagint-distinct personal names where the Masoretic Hebrew text differs, including Samuel here (Hebrew Shaul), Jemuel in v. 12 (Hamul), Zambram in v. 13 (Shimron), Hezron in v. 14 (Elon), Thasoban in v. 16 (Ezbon), Jessai and Jeul in v. 17 (Ishvah and Ishvi), and Chobor in v. 21 (Becher).
- **46:20** — The Septuagint expands Joseph's genealogy with five additional descendants through Manasseh and Ephraim (Machir, Gilead, Sutalaam, Taam, and Edem; cf. Num 26:29–36), which accounts for Rachel's total of eighteen in v. 22.
- **46:27** — The Septuagint counts nine souls born to Joseph in Egypt and seventy-five total for Jacob's house (the figure cited in Acts 7:14), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew text counts two and seventy.
- **46:28** — The Septuagint names the meeting place Heroonpolis (Greek Heroon polis, "City of Heroes") in the land of Rameses, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew text reads Goshen.
- **46:34** — The Septuagint specifies "Goshen of Arabia," referring to the eastern Egyptian border nome known in Hellenistic geography as Arabia, where the Hebrew reads simply "Goshen."
