## Text

**27:1** Now it came about, after Isaac had grown old, that his eyes grew too dim to see; and he called Esau, his older son, and said to him, "My son." And he said to him, "Here I am."

**27:2** And he said, "Look, I have grown old, and I do not know the day of my death.

**27:3** Now therefore, take your gear, both your quiver and your bow, and go out into the field and hunt game for me,

**27:4** and make savory dishes for me as I love, and bring them to me so that I may eat, in order that my soul may bless you before I die."

**27:5** Now Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau his son. And Esau went into the field to hunt game for his father.

**27:6** And Rebekah said to Jacob, her younger son, "See, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother, saying,

**27:7** 'Bring me game and make savory dishes for me, so that when I have eaten I may bless you before the Lord before I die.'

**27:8** Now therefore, son, listen to me just as I command you:

**27:9** go to the flock and take for me from there two tender and fine young goats, and I will make them into savory dishes for your father as he loves;

**27:10** and you will bring them in to your father and he will eat, in order that your father may bless you before he dies."

**27:11** Then Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Esau my brother is a hairy man, while I am a smooth man.

**27:12** Perhaps my father will feel me, and I will be before him as one showing contempt, and I will bring a curse upon myself and not a blessing."

**27:13** And his mother said to him, "Upon me be your curse, child; only obey my voice, and go and bring them to me."

**27:14** So he went and took them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made savory dishes just as his father loved.

**27:15** Then Rebekah took the fine robe of Esau, her older son, which was with her in the house, and put it on Jacob, her younger son;

**27:16** and she put the skins of the young goats around his arms and over the bare parts of his neck;

**27:17** and she gave the savory dishes and the loaves of bread that she had made into the hands of Jacob her son.

**27:18** And he brought them in to his father, and said, "My father." And he said, "Here I am. Who are you, child?"

**27:19** And Jacob his son said to his father, "I am Esau your firstborn. I have done just as you spoke to me. Rise, sit up, and eat of my game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:20** And Isaac said to his son, "What is this that you have found so quickly, child?" And he said, "It is what the Lord God delivered before me."

**27:21** Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Come near to me and I will feel you, child, whether you are my son Esau or not."

**27:22** So Jacob came near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is the voice of Jacob, yet the hands are the hands of Esau."

**27:23** And he did not recognize him, for his hands were hairy like the hands of his brother Esau; so he blessed him.

**27:24** And he said, "Are you my son Esau?" And he said, "I am."

**27:25** And he said, "Bring it to me and I will eat from your game, child, so that my soul may bless you." And he brought it to him, and he ate; and he brought wine in to him, and he drank.

**27:26** Then Isaac his father said to him, "Come near to me and kiss me, child."

**27:27** And he came near and kissed him. And he smelled the scent of his garments, and he blessed him and said, "Look, the scent of my son is like the scent of a full field that the Lord has blessed.

**27:28** And may God give you from the dew of heaven above, and from the richness of the earth, and an abundance of grain and wine.

**27:29** And let nations serve you, and rulers will bow down to you; and be lord over your brother, and the sons of your father will bow down to you. The one who curses you is cursed, and the one who blesses you is blessed."

**27:30** And it came about, after Isaac had finished blessing Jacob his son, and it came about as Jacob went out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother came in from his hunting.

**27:31** And he also made savory dishes and brought them to his father; and he said to his father, "Let my father rise and eat of his son's game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:32** And Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" And he said, "I am your firstborn son, Esau."

**27:33** Then Isaac was struck with an exceedingly great astonishment and said, "Who then is the one who hunted game for me and brought it in to me, and I ate from all of it before you came in, and I blessed him? And let him be blessed."

**27:34** Now it came about, when Esau heard the words of Isaac his father, that Esau cried out with a loud and exceedingly bitter cry, and said, "Bless me too, please, father."

**27:35** And he said to him, "Your brother came with deceit and took your blessing."

**27:36** And he said, "Rightly was his name called Jacob, for he has tripped me up now this second time: he has taken my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing." And Esau said to his father, "Have you not kept back a blessing for me, father?"

**27:37** And Isaac answered and said to Esau, "If I have made him lord over you, and all his brothers I have made his household servants, and with grain and wine I have sustained him, then what will I do for you, child?"

**27:38** And Esau said to Isaac his father, "Do you have only one blessing, father? Bless me too, please, father."

**27:39** And Isaac his father answered and said to him, "Look, from the richness of the earth will be your dwelling, and from the dew of heaven above;

**27:40** and by your sword you will live, and you will serve your brother. Yet it will come about that whenever you tear down, you will also loosen his yoke from your neck."

**27:41** And Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him. And Esau said in his mind, "Let the days of mourning for my father draw near, so that I may kill Jacob my brother."

**27:42** Now the words of Esau, her older son, were reported to Rebekah; so she sent and called Jacob, her younger son, and said to him, "Look, Esau your brother is threatening you to kill you.

**27:43** Now therefore, child, listen to my voice, and rise and flee to Mesopotamia, to Laban my brother, to Haran,

**27:44** and stay with him for some days, until the fury

**27:45** and the anger of your brother turn away from you, and he forgets what you have done to him; then I will send and summon you from there, so that I may not be made childless of both of you in a single day."

**27:46** And Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am disgusted with my life because of the daughters of the sons of Heth. If Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of this land, what is living to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **27:12** — Greek "ὡς καταφρονῶν" ("as one showing contempt") renders the sense of a mocker or deceiver as someone treating his father with disdain.
- **27:29** — The Septuagint reads "rulers" (for Hebrew "peoples"), singular "your brother," and "the sons of your father" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the sons of your mother").
- **27:36** — "Tripped me up" renders Greek "ἐπτέρνικεν" (literally, "struck at the heel" or "supplanted"), preserving the wordplay on the name Jacob.
- **27:39** — The Greek preposition "ἀπό" ("from") mirrors the phrasing of 27:28 and can be understood either as source ("from the richness") or separation ("away from the richness").
