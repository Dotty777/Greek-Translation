## Text

**12:1** And the Lord said to Abram, "Go out from your land, and from your relatives, and from the house of your father, to the land that I will show you.

**12:2** And I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you, and I will magnify your name, and you will be blessed.

**12:3** And I will bless those who bless you, and those who curse you I will curse; and in you all the tribes of the earth will be blessed."

**12:4** And Abram went just as the Lord had spoken to him, and Lot went with him. Now Abram was seventy-five years old when he went out from Haran.

**12:5** And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot the son of his brother, and all their possessions that they had acquired, and every soul that they had acquired from Haran, and they went out to go to the land of Canaan; and they came to the land of Canaan.

**12:6** And Abram traveled through the length of it as far as the place of Shechem, to the tall oak. Now the Canaanites were then inhabiting the land.

**12:7** And the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "To your seed I will give this land." And there Abram built an altar to the Lord who had appeared to him.

**12:8** And he moved away from there to the mountain east of Bethel, and he pitched his tent there in Bethel toward the sea, with Ai toward the east; and he built an altar there to the Lord and called upon the name of the Lord.

**12:9** And Abram set out, and having traveled on, he encamped in the wilderness.

**12:10** And a famine came upon the land; and Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, because the famine grew severe upon the land.

**12:11** Now it happened when Abram drew near to enter Egypt, Abram said to Sarai his wife, "I know that you are a woman of fair appearance.

**12:12** Therefore it will happen, as soon as the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'This woman is his wife,' and they will kill me, but they will keep you alive.

**12:13** Say therefore, 'I am his sister,' so that it may go well with me because of you, and my soul will live on account of you."

**12:14** Now it happened when Abram entered Egypt that the Egyptians saw his wife, that she was exceedingly beautiful,

**12:15** and the officials of Pharaoh saw her and praised her to Pharaoh, and they brought her in to Pharaoh.

**12:16** And they treated Abram well because of her, and he came to have sheep and calves and donkeys, male servants and female servants, mules and camels.

**12:17** And God afflicted Pharaoh and his house with great and grievous afflictions on account of Sarai, the wife of Abram.

**12:18** And Pharaoh called Abram and said, "What is this you have done to me, that you did not tell me that she is your wife?

**12:19** Why did you say, 'She is my sister'? And I took her for myself as a wife. And now, look, your wife is before you; take her and hurry away."

**12:20** And Pharaoh gave orders to men concerning Abram to escort him on his way, and his wife, and all that belonged to him, and Lot with him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:6** — The Septuagint translates the location as "the tall oak" (ten dryn ten hypseilen) rather than transliterating "Moreh."
- **12:8** — "Toward the sea" (kata thalassan) is the biblical idiom for westward, toward the Mediterranean Sea (likewise in 13:14).
- **12:9** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew geographical term Negev as "the wilderness" (also in 13:1 and 13:3).
- **12:16** — The Greek lists "mules" (hemionoi) where the Masoretic Text has "female donkeys," and in 12:20 adds "and Lot with him."
