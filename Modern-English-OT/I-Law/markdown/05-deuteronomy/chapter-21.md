## Text

**21:1** Now if someone struck down is found in the land that the Lord your God is giving you to inherit, fallen in the open field, and they do not know the one who struck him,

**21:2** your council of elders and your judges shall go out and measure out to the cities that are around the person struck down,

**21:3** and it shall be the city that is nearest to the person struck down: the council of elders of that city shall take a heifer from the cattle, which has not been worked and which has not pulled a yoke;

**21:4** and the council of elders of that city shall bring the heifer down into a rough ravine, which has not been worked nor is sown, and they shall hamstring the heifer in the ravine.

**21:5** And the priests, the Levites, shall come forward, because the Lord God chose them to stand before Him and to bless in His name, and by their mouth shall be every dispute and every assault.

**21:6** And the entire council of elders of that city, those who are nearest to the person struck down, shall wash their hands over the head of the heifer that was hamstrung in the ravine,

**21:7** and they shall answer and say, "Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen.

**21:8** Be merciful to Your people Israel, whom You redeemed, Lord, so that innocent blood may not be among Your people Israel." And the blood shall be atoned for on their behalf.

**21:9** So you shall remove the innocent blood from among yourselves, if you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

**21:10** Now when you go out to war against your enemies, and the Lord your God delivers them into your hands, and you take their plunder,

**21:11** and you see among the plunder a woman beautiful in appearance, and you desire her and take her for yourself as a wife,

**21:12** then you shall bring her inside into your house, and you shall shave her head and trim her nails,

**21:13** and you shall remove the garments of captivity from her, and she shall sit in your house and weep for her father and her mother for a month of days; and after this you shall go in to her and be joined in marriage to her, and she shall be your wife.

**21:14** And it shall be, if you do not want her, you shall send her away free, and she shall certainly not be sold for silver; you shall not deal treacherously with her, because you have humbled her.

**21:15** Now if a man has two wives, one of them loved and one of them hated, and the loved one and the hated one give birth for him, and the firstborn son belongs to the hated one,

**21:16** then it shall be, on the day when he bequeaths his possessions to his sons, that he shall not be able to grant firstborn rights to the son of the loved one, overlooking the son of the hated one, the firstborn;

**21:17** rather, he shall acknowledge the firstborn son of the hated one by giving him a double portion of everything that is found belonging to him, because this one is the beginning of his children, and to him belong the rights of the firstborn.

**21:18** Now if someone has a disobedient and defiant son, not obeying the voice of his father and the voice of his mother, and they discipline him, yet he does not listen to them,

**21:19** then his father and his mother shall take hold of him and bring him out to the council of elders of his city and to the gate of the place,

**21:20** and they shall say to the men of their city, "This son of ours disobeys and acts defiantly; he does not obey our voice; he carouses and drinks to excess."

**21:21** Then the men of his city shall stone him with stones, and he shall die; and you shall remove the evil person from among yourselves, and the rest shall hear and be afraid.

**21:22** Now if there is in someone a sin with a sentence of death, and he dies, and you hang him on a tree,

**21:23** his body shall not remain overnight on the tree; rather, you shall certainly bury it on that day, because everyone hanging on a tree is cursed by God; and you shall not defile the land that the Lord your God is giving you as an allotment.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — "Someone struck down" (and "the person struck down" in 21:2, 3, 6) translates Greek τραυματίας, literally "wounded person," used in the Septuagint for someone fatally wounded or killed.
- **21:4** — "Rough ravine" renders φάραγγα τραχεῖαν (distinct from Hebrew interpretations of a perennial stream), and "hamstring" translates Greek νευροκοπέω (literally "cut the sinews," also in 21:6), where the Hebrew refers to breaking the heifer's neck.
- **21:12** — Here and in 21:13 the Greek uses second-person verbs ("you shall shave... trim... remove"), whereas the Hebrew places these actions in the third person ("she shall...").
- **21:17** — The Greek reads ἀρχὴ τέκνων αὐτοῦ ("the beginning of his children"), whereas the Hebrew has "the firstfruits of his vigor."
