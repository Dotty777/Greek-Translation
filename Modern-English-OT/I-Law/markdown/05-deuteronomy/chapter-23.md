## Text

**23:1** A man shall not take his father's wife, and he shall not uncover his father's covering.

**23:2** Neither one with crushed testicles nor one who is cut off shall enter the assembly of the Lord.

**23:3** One born of a prostitute shall not enter the assembly of the Lord.

**23:4** Neither an Ammonite nor a Moabite shall enter the assembly of the Lord; even to the tenth generation he shall not enter the assembly of the Lord, even forever,

**23:5** because they did not meet you with bread and water on the road as you were coming out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam son of Beor from Mesopotamia to curse you;

**23:6** and the Lord your God was not willing to listen to Balaam, and the Lord your God turned the curses into a blessing, because the Lord your God loved you.

**23:7** You shall not speak peaceful things to them or beneficial things to them all your days forever.

**23:8** You shall not abhor an Idumean, because he is your brother; you shall not abhor an Egyptian, because you were a resident alien in his land.

**23:9** If sons are born to them, in the third generation they shall enter the assembly of the Lord.

**23:10** And if you go out to encamp against your enemies, then you shall guard yourself from every evil thing.

**23:11** If there is among you a man who is not clean from his discharge at night, then he shall go out into the camp.

**23:12** And it shall be toward evening that he shall wash his body with water, and when the sun has set, he shall enter the camp.

**23:13** And you shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go outside there.

**23:14** And you shall have a peg on your belt, and it shall be, when you squat down outside, that you shall dig with it and cover over your indecency;

**23:15** for the Lord your God walks about in your camp to deliver you and to hand over your enemy before you; and your camp shall be holy, and no indecent thing shall be seen among you, or He will turn away from you.

**23:16** You shall not hand over to his master a servant who has attached himself to you from his master.

**23:17** He shall live with you, among you; he shall live wherever it pleases him; you shall not oppress him.

**23:18** There shall not be a prostitute from the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a man practicing prostitution from the sons of Israel; there shall not be a ritual initiate from the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be one being initiated from the sons of Israel.

**23:19** You shall not bring the wage of a prostitute or the exchange price of a dog into the house of the Lord your God for any vow, for both of them are an abomination to the Lord your God.

**23:20** You shall not charge your brother interest: interest on silver, or interest on food, or interest on anything that you may lend out.

**23:21** To a foreigner you may charge interest, but to your brother you shall not charge interest, so that the Lord your God may bless you in all your works in the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**23:22** And if you vow a vow to the Lord your God, you shall not delay to pay it, for the Lord your God will surely require it from you, and there will be sin in you.

**23:23** But if you do not wish to make a vow, there is no sin in you.

**23:24** You shall keep what goes out through your lips, and you shall do just as you vowed to God, a gift that you spoke with your mouth.

**23:25** And if you enter your neighbor's standing grain, you may gather heads of grain with your hands, but you shall not put a sickle to your neighbor's standing grain.

**23:26** And if you enter your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes as much as fills your appetite, but you shall not put any into a vessel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — In both the Greek and the Hebrew numbering chapter 23 begins with this verse (numbered 22:30 in another common versification, which counts 23:2-26 as 23:1-25).
- **23:8** — Greek Idoumaion ("Idumean") is the Septuagint's Greek designation corresponding to Hebrew "Edomite" (similarly, 23:5 reads "from Mesopotamia" where the Hebrew MT has "from Pethor of Aram-naharaim").
- **23:11** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek text reads "he shall go out into the camp," reflecting an early scribal omission (parablepsis) of the clause "outside the camp, and he shall not enter" preserved in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **23:18** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew's two prohibitions as four clauses, pairing prostitution terms (pornē / porneuōn) with Hellenistic mystery-cult initiation terms (telesphoros / teliskomenos).
- **23:25** — The Septuagint reverses the order of the standing-grain law (23:25) and the vineyard law (23:26) compared with the Hebrew MT, where the vineyard law comes first.
