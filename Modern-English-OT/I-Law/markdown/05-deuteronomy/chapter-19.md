## Text

**19:1** Now when the Lord your God wipes out the nations whose land God is giving you, and you take possession of them and live in their cities and in their houses,

**19:2** you shall set apart three cities for yourself in the middle of your land, which the Lord your God is giving you.

**19:3** Measure out the road for yourself, and divide into three parts the territory of your land which the Lord your God is apportioning to you, and there shall be a refuge there for every killer.

**19:4** Now this shall be the ordinance for the killer who flees there and will live: whoever strikes his neighbor unknowingly, and did not hate him in the past,

**19:5** and whoever goes with his neighbor into the forest to gather wood, and his hand swings out with the axe as he cuts the wood, and the iron head slips off the wood and hits his neighbor, and he dies—this person shall flee for refuge to one of these cities and live,

**19:6** so that the kinsman-avenger of blood does not pursue after the one who killed, because his heart is inflamed, and overtake him if the road is too long, and strike him fatally—yet for this person there is no sentence of death, because he had not hated him in the past.

**19:7** Therefore I am commanding you this word, saying, "You shall set apart three cities for yourself."

**19:8** Now if the Lord your God enlarges your territory, just as He swore to your fathers, and the Lord gives you all the land that He said He would give to your fathers,

**19:9** if you listen so as to do all these commandments which I am commanding you today, to love the Lord your God, to walk in all His ways all the days, then you shall add three more cities for yourself in addition to these three,

**19:10** and innocent blood shall not be shed in the land that the Lord your God is giving you as an allotment, and there shall not be among you anyone guilty of blood.

**19:11** But if there is among you a man who hates his neighbor, and lies in ambush for him, and rises up against him and strikes him fatally so that he dies, and flees to one of these cities,

**19:12** then the council of elders of his city shall send and take him from there, and deliver him into the hands of the kinsman-avengers of blood, and he shall die.

**19:13** Your eye shall not spare him, and you shall cleanse away the innocent blood from Israel, and it shall go well with you.

**19:14** You shall not move a neighbor's boundaries, which your fathers set, in the inheritance that you have inherited in the land that the Lord your God is giving you as an allotment.

**19:15** A single witness shall not stand to testify against a person for any injustice, or for any offense, or for any sin that he may commit; by the mouth of two witnesses and by the mouth of three witnesses every matter shall stand.

**19:16** Now if an unjust witness comes forward against a person, accusing him of ungodliness,

**19:17** then the two people who have the dispute shall know before the Lord, and before the priests and before the judges who are in place in those days;

**19:18** and the judges shall investigate thoroughly, and look, an unjust witness testified unjustly; he stood against his brother;

**19:19** and you shall do to him just as he plotted with evil intent to do against his brother, and you shall remove the evil person from among yourselves.

**19:20** And the rest shall hear and be afraid, and they shall not again act according to this evil thing among you.

**19:21** Your eye shall not spare him: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — Greek στόχασαι literally means "measure out" or "survey" (distinct from the Hebrew "prepare"), fitting the division of the territory into three parts to keep the refuge cities accessible.
- **19:4** — "In the past" renders the literal Greek idiom "before yesterday and the third [day]" (likewise in 19:6), an expression meaning previously.
- **19:6** — "Kinsman-avenger of blood" translates Greek ὁ ἀγχιστεύων τοῦ αἵματος, literally "the one acting as next of kin for the blood" (plural in 19:12); "strike him fatally" renders the literal idiom "strike his life" (also in 19:11).
- **19:17** — The Greek text reads γνώσονται ("shall know"), matching Codex Vaticanus, where other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read "shall stand."
