## Text

**1:1** These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness, toward the west, near the Red Sea, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Aulon, and Catachrysea.

**1:2** It is an eleven-day journey in Horeb to Mount

**1:3** Seir as far as Kadesh-barnea. And it happened in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke to all the sons of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded him for them, after striking down

**1:4** Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og, king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei.

**1:5** Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law, saying:

**1:6** The Lord our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying, "Let it be enough for you to live at this mountain.

**1:7** Turn and set out yourselves, and go into the hill country of the Amorites and to all the neighbors of Arabah, into hill country and plain, and toward the south, and the coastland, the land of the Canaanites, and Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river Euphrates.

**1:8** See, He has delivered the land before you; go in and inherit the land that I swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give to them and to their seed after them."

**1:9** And I spoke to you at that time, saying, "I will not be able to bear you alone.

**1:10** The Lord your God has multiplied you, and look, today you are like the stars of heaven in multitude.

**1:11** May the Lord, the God of your fathers, add to you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you just as He spoke to you.

**1:12** How will I alone be able to bear your trouble and your burden and your disputes?

**1:13** Provide for yourselves wise, knowledgeable, and discerning men for your tribes, and I will appoint them over you as your leaders."

**1:14** And you answered me and said, "The thing that you have spoken is good to do."

**1:15** And I took from among you wise, knowledgeable, and discerning men, and I appointed them to lead over you, commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds and commanders of tens, and recorders for your judges.

**1:16** And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, "Give a full hearing between your brothers, and judge righteously between a man and a brother and his resident alien.

**1:17** You must not show partiality in judgment; you will judge the small and the great alike; you must never shrink back before the face of a man, for the judgment is God's. And whatever matter is too hard for you, you will bring it up to me, and I will hear you."

**1:18** And I commanded you at that time all the words that you were to do.

**1:19** And setting out from Horeb, we traveled through all that great and fearsome wilderness that you saw, by the way to the hill country of the Amorite, just as the Lord our God commanded us, and we came as far as Kadesh-barnea.

**1:20** And I said to you, "You have come as far as the hill country of the Amorite, which the Lord our God is giving to you.

**1:21** See, the Lord our God has delivered the land to you before your face; go up and inherit it, just as the Lord, the God of our fathers, said to you. Do not fear nor be dismayed."

**1:22** And all of you approached me and said, "Let us send men ahead of us, and let them scout out the land for us and bring back a report to us: the way by which we will go up and the cities into which we will enter."

**1:23** And the matter was pleasing before me, and I took from among you twelve men, one man for each tribe.

**1:24** And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came as far as the Valley of the Cluster, and spied it out.

**1:25** And they took in their hands some of the fruit of the land and brought it down to us, and they said, "The land that the Lord our God is giving to us is good."

**1:26** Yet you were not willing to go up, and you disobeyed the word of the Lord our God.

**1:27** And you grumbled in your tents and said, "Because the Lord hates us, He brought us out of the land of Egypt to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites, to destroy us.

**1:28** Where are we going up?" Your brothers turned away your heart, saying, "A nation great and numerous and stronger than you, and cities great and walled up to heaven; moreover, we have even seen sons of giants there."

**1:29** And I said to you, "Do not be terrified nor be afraid of them.

**1:30** The Lord your God, who goes ahead before your face, He Himself will fight them with you according to all that He did for us in the land of Egypt, and in this wilderness that you saw by the way to the hill country of the Amorite, how He nurtured you, the Lord

**1:31** your God, as a man would nurture his son, along all the way on which you traveled until you came to this place."

**1:32** And in this matter you did not trust the Lord our God,

**1:33** who goes ahead of you on the way to choose a place for you, guiding you in fire by night, showing you the way along which you travel, and in a cloud by day.

**1:34** And the Lord heard the sound of your words, and being provoked, He swore, saying,

**1:35** "Surely not one of these men will see this good land that I swore to their fathers,

**1:36** except Caleb son of Jephunneh; he will see it, and to him I will give the land on which he set foot, and to his sons, because he was devoted to the things concerning the Lord."

**1:37** And the Lord was also angry with me because of you, saying, "You also will by no means enter there.

**1:38** Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, he will enter there. Strengthen him, for he will cause Israel to inherit it.

**1:39** And every young child who today does not know good or evil, these will enter there, and to them I will give it, and they will inherit it."

**1:40** And you turned and encamped in the wilderness, by the way to the Red Sea.

**1:41** And you answered me and said, "We have sinned before the Lord our God; we will go up and fight according to all that the Lord our God commanded us." And each of you took up his weapons of war, and gathering together, you began going up into the hill country.

**1:42** And the Lord said to me, "Say to them, 'You must not go up, nor fight, for I am not with you, and you will not be crushed before your enemies.'"

**1:43** And I spoke to you, and you did not listen to me; and you transgressed the word of the Lord, and forcing your way, you went up into the hill country.

**1:44** And the Amorite who lived in that hill country came out to meet you, and they chased you as bees would do, and kept wounding you from Seir as far as Hormah.

**1:45** And sitting down, you wept before the Lord our God, and the Lord did not listen to your voice, nor did He pay attention to you.

**1:46** And you remained in Kadesh many days, as many days as you remained there.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek preserves two LXX-distinct place names, Aulon (MT "Hazeroth") and Catachrysea (lit. "gold-covered places," translating MT "Di-zahab"). It also reads "toward the west" (MT "in the Arabah") and "near the Red [Sea]" with "Sea" implied.
- **1:7** — Greek specifically names the Anti-Lebanon range (Antilibanos), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "Lebanon."
- **1:8** — Greek reads the third-person perfect "He has delivered" (paradedoken) before shifting to the first-person "I swore" (omosa), whereas the Hebrew MT uses first person in both clauses.
- **1:24** — Greek translates the Hebrew place name "Valley of Eshcol" literally into Greek as "the Valley of the Cluster" (Pharangos botryos).
- **1:40** — Greek uses the aorist indicative "you encamped" (estratopedeusate), resuming Moses's narration after the Lord's speech ends in v. 39, rather than an imperative command as in the Hebrew MT.
