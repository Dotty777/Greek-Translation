## Text

**3:1** And we turned and went up by the way to Bashan; and Gog king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.

**3:2** And the Lord said to me, "Do not fear him, because I have handed him and all his people and all his land over into your hands; and you will do to him just as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."

**3:3** And the Lord our God handed him over into our hands, even Og king of Bashan and all his people; and we struck him until we left no seed of his.

**3:4** And we captured all his cities at that time; there was not a city that we did not take from them: sixty cities, all the adjoining region of Argob of King Og in Bashan,

**3:5** all fortified cities, with high walls, gates, and bars, besides the cities of the Perizzites, which were very many.

**3:6** We utterly destroyed them, just as we did to Sihon king of Heshbon, and we utterly destroyed every city in succession, and the women and the little children,

**3:7** and all the livestock; and we took the spoil of the cities as plunder for ourselves.

**3:8** And at that time we took the land from the hands of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the brook Arnon even as far as Hermon;

**3:9** the Phoenicians call Hermon Sanior, and the Amorites called it Senir;

**3:10** all the cities of Misor, and all Gilead, and all Bashan as far as Elcha and Edrei, royal cities of Og in Bashan;

**3:11** for only Og king of Bashan was left of the Rephaim. Look, his bed is an iron bed; look, it is in the citadel of the sons of Ammon: nine cubits is its length and four cubits its width, by the cubit of a man.

**3:12** And we plundered that land at that time, from Aroer, which is on the bank of the brook Arnon, and half of Mount Gilead; and its cities I gave to Reuben and to Gad.

**3:13** And the rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Gog, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh, and all the region of Argob, all that land of Bashan; it will be reckoned a land of Rephaim.

**3:14** And Jair son of Manasseh took all the region of Arbok as far as the borders of Gartasei and Homachathei; he named them after his own name, Bassemath, Havvoth-jair, until this day.

**3:15** And to Machir I gave Gilead.

**3:16** And to Reuben and to Gad I have given from Gilead as far as the brook Arnon, and as far as the Jabbok; the brook is a border for the sons of Ammon;

**3:17** and the Arabah and the Jordan, the border of Machanareth and as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, below Ashdoth-pisgah to the east.

**3:18** And I commanded you at that time, saying, "The Lord our God has given you this land as an inheritance; armed, go ahead before your brothers, the sons of Israel, every mighty man.

**3:19** Only your wives and your children and your livestock (I know that you have much livestock), let them live in your cities which I gave you, until the Lord

**3:20** your God gives rest to your brothers just as He has to you, and they also take possession of the land which the Lord our God is giving to them beyond the Jordan; then you will return, each one to his inheritance which I gave you."

**3:21** And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, "Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did to these two kings; so the Lord our God will do to all the kingdoms against which you are crossing over there.

**3:22** You shall not fear, because the Lord our God Himself fights for you."

**3:23** And I pleaded before the Lord at that time, saying,

**3:24** "Lord God, You have begun to show Your servant Your strength and Your power and the mighty hand and the uplifted arm; for what god is there in heaven or on earth who will do according to what You have done and according to Your strength?

**3:25** Having crossed over, then, I will see this good land that is beyond the Jordan, the good mountain and Anti-Lebanon."

**3:26** And the Lord overlooked me on account of you and did not listen to me; and the Lord said to me, "Let it be enough for you; do not continue to speak of this matter.

**3:27** Go up to the summit of the Hewn One, and looking up with your eyes toward the sea and north and south and east, see with your eyes, because you will not cross over this Jordan.

**3:28** And command Joshua, and strengthen him and encourage him, because he will cross over before this people, and he himself will cause them to inherit the land which you have seen."

**3:29** And we stayed in a valley near the house of Peor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text reads "Gog" here and in 3:13, while using "Og" in vv. 3, 4, 10, and 11.
- **3:5** — The Greek text reads "cities of the Perizzites" (interpreting Hebrew perazi as a gentilic), whereas the Hebrew MT refers to unwalled towns.
- **3:9** — The Greek text reads "Phoenicians" and "Sanior" (vs. MT "Sidonians" and "Sirion"), and in v. 10 treats "Misor" (MT "the tableland") as a proper name and reads "Elcha" (MT "Salecah").
- **3:12** — The Greek text reads "we plundered" (epronomeusamen), where the Hebrew MT has "we took possession of"; in v. 11, "in the citadel" translates Hebrew "in Rabbah."
- **3:14** — The Greek text reads the distinct names "Arbok" (vs. "Argob" in vv. 4, 13), "Gartasei," "Homachathei," and "Bassemath" (corresponding to MT "Geshurites," "Maacathites," and "Bashan").
- **3:17** — "Machanareth" reflects a fusion of the Hebrew preposition with "Chinnereth," and "Ashdoth-pisgah" (Greek Asedoth) transliterates Hebrew "slopes of Pisgah."
- **3:27** — "The Hewn One" (Lelaxeumenou) renders the name "Pisgah" as a Greek participle; similarly, v. 25 reads "Antilebanon" (MT "Lebanon") and v. 26 reads "overlooked" (MT "was angry with").
