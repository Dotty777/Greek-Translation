## Text

**31:1** And Moses finished speaking all these words to all the sons of Israel.

**31:2** And he said to them, "I am one hundred and twenty years old today; I will no longer be able to go in and go out, and the Lord said to me, 'You shall not cross this Jordan.'

**31:3** The Lord your God is the One who goes ahead before you; He will utterly destroy these nations from before you, and you will dispossess them. And Joshua is the one who goes ahead before you, just as the Lord spoke.

**31:4** And the Lord your God will do to them just as He did to Sihon and Og, the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, and to their land, as He utterly destroyed them,

**31:5** and the Lord delivered them to you; and you shall do to them just as I commanded you.

**31:6** Be courageous and be strong; do not fear, nor be fainthearted, nor be terrified before them, for the Lord your God is the One who goes ahead with you, among you; He will by no means let go of you, nor by any means abandon you."

**31:7** And Moses called Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be courageous and be strong, for you will go in before this people into the land that the Lord swore to our fathers to give to them, and you will apportion it as an inheritance to them.

**31:8** And the Lord, the One who goes along with you, will not let go of you, nor by any means abandon you. Do not fear, nor be fainthearted."

**31:9** And Moses wrote the words of this law in a book, and gave it to the priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to the elders of the sons of Israel.

**31:10** And Moses commanded them on that day, saying, "After seven years, at the time of the year of release, at the Festival of Tent-Pitching,

**31:11** when all Israel comes together to appear before the Lord your God in the place that the Lord chooses, you shall read this law in the presence of all Israel, in their hearing,

**31:12** having assembled the people, the men and the women and the children, and the resident alien who is in your cities, so that they may hear and so that they may learn to fear the Lord your God, and they will listen so as to do all the words of this law;

**31:13** and their sons, who do not know, will hear and will learn to fear the Lord your God all the days that they live on the land into which you are crossing the Jordan to inherit it there."

**31:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, the days of your death have drawn near; call Joshua, and stand beside the doors of the tent of testimony, and I will command him." And Moses and Joshua went to the tent of testimony, and stood beside the doors of the tent of testimony.

**31:15** And the Lord came down in a cloud, and stood beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the pillar of the cloud stood beside the doors of the tent.

**31:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, you are about to sleep with your fathers, and this people will rise up and prostitute itself after foreign gods of the land into which it is entering, and they will forsake Me and break My covenant that I made with them.

**31:17** And I will become angry with wrath against them on that day, and I will forsake them and turn My face away from them, and it will become prey; and many evils and afflictions will find it, and it will say on that day, 'Because the Lord my God is not in me, these evils have found me.'

**31:18** But I will surely turn My face away from them on that day on account of all the evil deeds that they did, because they turned away to foreign gods.

**31:19** And write the words of this song, and you shall teach it to the sons of Israel, and you shall put it into their mouth, so that this song may be for Me, testifying face to face among the sons of Israel.

**31:20** For I will bring them into the good land that I swore to their fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey; and they will eat and, having been filled, they will be satiated, and they will turn to foreign gods and provoke Me and break My covenant.

**31:21** And this song will stand in opposition, testifying face to face; for it will by no means be forgotten from the mouth of their seed. For I know their wickedness, all that they are doing here today before I bring them into the good land that I swore to their fathers."

**31:22** And Moses wrote this song on that day, and taught it to the sons of Israel.

**31:23** And he commanded Joshua and said, "Be courageous and be strong, for you will bring the sons of Israel into the land that the Lord swore to them, and He will be with you."

**31:24** Now when Moses finished writing all the words of this law in a book to the end,

**31:25** then he commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, saying,

**31:26** "Take the book of this law and place it at the side of the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, and it will be there among you as a testimony;

**31:27** for I know your provocation and your stiff neck. For while I am still living with you today, you have been rebellious in matters toward God; how will it not also be so after my death?

**31:28** Assemble to me your tribal leaders and your judges and your recorders, so that I may speak all these words in their hearing, and I call both heaven and earth to witness against them.

**31:29** For I know that after my death you will surely act lawlessly, and you will turn aside from the way that I commanded you—the evils at the end of the days, because you will do what is wicked in the sight of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger by the works of your hands."

**31:30** And Moses spoke in the hearing of the whole assembly the words of this song to the end.

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — The Greek reads "Moses finished speaking" (συνετέλεσεν Μωυσῆς λαλῶν), aligning with the Dead Sea Scrolls manuscript 1QDeut-b rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "Moses went and spoke."
- **31:6** — The Greek includes both "with you" and "among you" (μεθ᾽ ὑμῶν ἐν ὑμῖν), a distinctive Codex Vaticanus doublet, and lists three verbs of fear where the Hebrew has two.
- **31:21** — The Greek opens directly with the song standing in opposition as a witness, without the opening temporal clause ("when many evils and troubles have come upon them") found in the Masoretic Hebrew, and renders the people's "inclination" as "wickedness" (πονηρίαν).
- **31:23** — With third-person phrasing ("that the Lord swore to them, and He will be with you"), the Greek continues Moses from v. 22 as the speaker commissioning Joshua, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew attributes this verse to the Lord speaking in the first person.
- **31:29** — The Greek source text reads the verbless phrase "the evils at the end of the days" (τὰ κακὰ ἔσχατον τῶν ἡμερῶν), lacking "and [evils] will befall you" found in other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew—likely an early scribal eye-skip between two occurrences of ὑμῖν ("you").
