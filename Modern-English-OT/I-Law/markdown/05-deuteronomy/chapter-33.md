## Text

**33:1** And this is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the sons of Israel before his death.

**33:2** And he said, "The Lord has come from Sinai, and appeared from Seir to us; and He hastened down from Mount Paran with tens of thousands of Kadesh; at His right hand were angels with Him.

**33:3** And He spared His people, and all the sanctified ones are under Your hands; and these are under You, and he received from His words,

**33:4** a law which Moses commanded you, an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob.

**33:5** And there will be a ruler in the beloved one, when the rulers of the peoples are gathered together with the tribes of Israel.

**33:6** Let Reuben live and not die, and let him be many in number."

**33:7** And this is for Judah: "Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and may You come to his people; and his hands will judge for him, and You will be a helper against the enemies."

**33:8** And to Levi he said, "Give to Levi his manifestations, and his truth to the devout man, whom they tested at Testing; they reviled him at the water of Contention.

**33:9** Saying to his father and to his mother, 'I have not seen you,' he also did not recognize his brothers, and he disowned his sons; he guarded Your oracles, and preserved Your covenant.

**33:10** They will make known Your ordinances to Jacob, and Your law to Israel; they will place incense in Your wrath continually upon Your altar.

**33:11** Bless, Lord, his strength, and accept the works of his hands; shatter the loins of enemies who have risen up against him, and let those who hate him not rise."

**33:12** And to Benjamin he said, "Beloved by the Lord, he will dwell in confidence, and God overshadows him all the days, and between his shoulders He rested."

**33:13** And to Joseph he said, "Under the blessing of the Lord is his land, from the seasons of heaven and dew, and from the deeps of springs below,

**33:14** and according to the season of the yields of the sun's turnings, and from the conjunctions of the months,

**33:15** from the summit of mountains of the beginning, and from the summit of everlasting hills,

**33:16** and according to the season of the fullness of the earth; and may the things acceptable to the One who appeared in the bush come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of one glorified among brothers.

**33:17** A firstborn of a bull is his beauty; the horns of a unicorn are his horns; with them he will gore nations together as far as the end of the earth. These are the tens of thousands of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasseh."

**33:18** And to Zebulun he said, "Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar in his tents.

**33:19** They will utterly destroy nations; and you will call out there, and you will sacrifice a sacrifice of righteousness; because the wealth of the sea will nurse you, and the markets of those who inhabit the coast."

**33:20** And to Gad he said, "Blessed is the One who enlarges Gad; like a lion he rested, having crushed an arm and a ruler.

**33:21** And he saw his firstfruits, because there was apportioned a land of rulers gathered together with leaders of peoples; the Lord did righteousness, and His judgment with Israel."

**33:22** And to Dan he said, "Dan is a lion's cub, and he will leap out from Bashan."

**33:23** And to Naphtali he said, "Naphtali is a fullness of acceptable things, and let him be filled with blessing from the Lord; he will inherit the sea and the south."

**33:24** And to Asher he said, "Blessed above children is Asher, and he will be acceptable to his brothers. He will dip his foot in oil;

**33:25** iron and bronze will be his sandal; as your days are, so is your strength.

**33:26** There is none like the God of the beloved one; the One who rides upon heaven is your helper, and the Majestic One of the vault.

**33:27** And the rule of God will shelter you, and you are under the strength of everlasting arms; He will drive out an enemy from before you, saying, 'Perish!'

**33:28** And Israel will dwell in confidence, alone upon the land of Jacob, with grain and wine, and heaven is clouded with dew for you.

**33:29** Blessed are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the Lord? Your helper will shield you, and the sword is your boast; and your enemies will lie to you, and you will tread upon their neck."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — Greek reads "Kadesh" (transliterating the Hebrew word for "holy ones") and "angels with Him" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a difficult phrase often rendered "a fiery law for them."
- **33:5** — "The beloved one" translates the Septuagint rendering of the poetic name Jeshurun for Israel (also in 33:26), and Greek reads "ruler" rather than "king."
- **33:8** — Greek renders Hebrew Urim and Thummim as "manifestations" and "truth," and translates the place names Massah and Meribah as "Testing" and "Contention."
- **33:10** — Greek translates the Hebrew word meaning either "nostrils" or "anger" as "in Your wrath" and renders the term for "whole burnt offering" as "continually."
- **33:17** — Greek monokerotos ("unicorn," literally "one-horned animal") is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew term for a wild ox.
