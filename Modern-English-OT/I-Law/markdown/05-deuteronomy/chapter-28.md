## Text

**28:1** And it will be, if you indeed listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and to do all these commandments that I am commanding you today, then the Lord your God will place you high over all the nations of the earth.

**28:2** And all these blessings will come upon you and find you, if you indeed listen to the voice of the Lord your God.

**28:3** Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field.

**28:4** Blessed are the offspring of your womb, the herds of your cattle, and the flocks of your sheep.

**28:5** Blessed are your storehouses and your reserves.

**28:6** Blessed are you in your coming in, and blessed are you in your going out.

**28:7** May the Lord your God hand over your enemies who stand against you, shattered before your face; by one way they will come out against you, and by seven ways they will flee from your presence.

**28:8** May the Lord send the blessing upon you in your storerooms and upon everything to which you put your hand, on the land that the Lord your God is giving you.

**28:9** May the Lord raise you up for Himself as a holy people, just as He swore to your fathers, if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God and walk in all His ways.

**28:10** And all the nations of the earth will see you, that the name of the Lord has been called upon you, and they will fear you.

**28:11** And the Lord your God will multiply you for good things in the offspring of your womb, and in the produce of your land, and in the offspring of your livestock, on the land that the Lord swore to your fathers to give you.

**28:12** May the Lord open for you His good treasury, the sky, to give the rain to your land in season, to bless all the works of your hands; and you will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow.

**28:13** May the Lord your God appoint you as head and not as tail, and you will then be above and will not be below, if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep all that I am commanding you today.

**28:14** You will not turn aside from any of the commandments that I am commanding you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

**28:15** And it will be, if you do not heed the voice of the Lord your God, to keep all His commandments that I am commanding you today, then all these curses will come upon you and overtake you.

**28:16** Cursed are you in the city, and cursed are you in the field.

**28:17** Cursed are your storehouses and your reserves.

**28:18** Cursed are the offspring of your womb and the produce of your land, the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep.

**28:19** Cursed are you in your going out, and cursed are you in your coming in.

**28:20** May the Lord send upon you scarcity and starvation and consumption, upon everything to which you put your hand, until He exterminates you, and until He destroys you quickly, on account of your evil practices, because you forsook Me.

**28:21** May the Lord make death cling to you until He completely consumes you from the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**28:22** May the Lord strike you with distress and fever and chill and inflammation and wind-blight and mildew, and they will pursue you until they destroy you.

**28:23** And the sky over your head will be bronze to you, and the earth below you iron.

**28:24** May the Lord your God make the rain for your land dust, and loose earth will come down from the sky, until He wipes you out and until He destroys you quickly.

**28:25** May the Lord give you over to visitation before the enemies; by one way you will go out against them, and by seven ways you will flee from their presence, and you will be a dispersion in all the kingdoms of the earth.

**28:26** And your dead bodies will be food for the birds of the sky and for the wild beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to scare them away.

**28:27** May the Lord strike you with an Egyptian ulcer on the buttocks, and with fierce scab and itch, so that you cannot be healed.

**28:28** May the Lord strike you with derangement of blindness and with bewilderment of mind.

**28:29** And you will be groping at midday, as if a blind person were to grope in the darkness, and He will not prosper your ways; and you will then be wronged and plundered all the days, and there will be no one to help.

**28:30** You will take a wife, and another man will have her; you will build a house, and you will not live in it; you will plant a vineyard, and you will not harvest it.

**28:31** Your calf will be slaughtered before you, and you will not eat from it; your donkey will be snatched away from you, and will not be given back to you; your sheep will be given to your enemies,

**28:32** and your eyes will look at them, wasting away; your hand will have no strength.

**28:33** A nation that you do not know will eat the crops of your land and all your labors, and you will be wronged and crushed all the days.

**28:34** And you will be deranged because of the sights of your eyes that you will see.

**28:35** May the Lord strike you with a malignant ulcer on the knees and on the shins, so that you cannot be healed, from the sole of your feet to the crown of your head.

**28:36** May the Lord lead you away, and your rulers whom you appoint over yourself, to a nation that you and your fathers do not know, and there you will serve other gods, wood and stone.

**28:37** And there you will be a riddle and a proverb and a tale among all the nations to which the Lord may lead you away.

**28:38** You will carry much seed out into the open field, and you will bring in little, because the locust will devour it.

**28:39** You will plant a vineyard and cultivate it, and you will not drink wine nor be gladdened from it, because the worm will devour it.

**28:40** You will have olive trees in all your borders, and you will not anoint yourself with oil, because your olives will drop off.

**28:41** You will father sons and daughters, and they will not be; for they will go away in captivity.

**28:42** The blight will completely consume all your wooden things and the produce of your land.

**28:43** The resident alien who is among you will go up higher and higher, but you will go down lower and lower.

**28:44** He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him; he will be head, but you will be tail.

**28:45** And all these curses will come upon you and pursue you and overtake you, until He exterminates you and until He destroys you, because you did not heed the voice of the Lord your God, to keep His commandments and the statutes that He commanded you.

**28:46** And there will be signs and wonders in you, in your seed forever,

**28:47** because you did not serve the Lord your God with gladness and a good mind on account of the abundance of all things.

**28:48** And you will serve your enemies, whom the Lord will send upon you, in hunger and in the lack of all things; and He will put an iron collar on your neck, until He exterminates you.

**28:49** The Lord will bring upon you a nation from afar, from the end of the earth, like the swoop of an eagle, a nation whose voice you will not understand,

**28:50** a nation shameless in face, which will not respect the face of an old man and will not show mercy to a young man.

**28:51** And it will devour the offspring of your livestock and the produce of your land, so as not to leave you grain, wine, oil, the herds of your cattle, and the flocks of your sheep, until it destroys you

**28:52** and wipes you out in your cities, until the high and fortified walls in which you have trusted are torn down in all your land; and it will afflict you in your cities that He gave you.

**28:53** And you will eat the offspring of your womb, the flesh of your sons and your daughters, all whom He gave you, in your distress and in your affliction, with which your enemy will afflict you.

**28:54** The tender and very delicate man among you will begrudge with his eye his brother and the wife in his embrace and the remaining children who may be left,

**28:55** so as not to give to any one of them from the flesh of his children whom he eats, because nothing has been left to him in your distress and in your affliction, with which your enemies will afflict you in all your cities.

**28:56** And the tender and delicate woman among you, whose foot has not ventured to step on the ground because of her delicacy, will begrudge with her eye her husband in her embrace and her son and her daughter,

**28:57** and her afterbirth that comes out from between her thighs and the child that she may bear; for she will devour them secretly on account of the scarcity of all things, in your distress and in your affliction, with which your enemy will afflict you in your cities.

**28:58** If you do not listen to do all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this honored, wondrous name, the Lord your God,

**28:59** then the Lord will make extraordinary your plagues and the plagues of your seed, great and wondrous plagues, and malignant and lasting diseases.

**28:60** And He will bring back all the malignant suffering of Egypt, which you dreaded before them, and they will cling to you.

**28:61** And the Lord will bring upon you every sickness and every plague that is not written in the book of this law, until He exterminates you.

**28:62** And you will be left in a small number, whereas you were like the stars of the sky in multitude, because you did not heed the voice of the Lord your God.

**28:63** And it will be, just as the Lord rejoiced over you to do good to you, so the Lord will rejoice over you to exterminate you; and you will be removed quickly from the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**28:64** And the Lord your God will scatter you into all the nations, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth, and there you will serve other gods, wood and stone, whom you and your fathers did not know.

**28:65** Yet even among those nations He will not give you rest, nor will there ever be a standing place for the sole of your foot; and there the Lord will give you a different, disobedient heart, and failing eyes, and a melting soul.

**28:66** And your life will be hanging in front of your eyes, and you will be afraid day and night, and you will not trust in your life.

**28:67** In the morning you will say, "If only evening would come!" and in the evening you will say, "If only morning would come!" because of the fear of your heart that you will fear, and because of the sights of your eyes that you will see.

**28:68** And the Lord will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said, "You will never see it again"; and there you will be sold to your enemies as male servants and female servants, and there will be no one to buy.

**28:69** These are the words of the covenant that the Lord commanded Moses to establish with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant that He made with them at Horeb.

## Translator Notes

- **28:5** — Greek ἀποθῆκαι ("storehouses") and ἐνκαταλίμματα ("reserves," literally "things left over," also in v. 17) differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "basket" and "kneading bowl."
- **28:7** — Here and in the other covenant invocations of blessing and curse (vv. 8-9, 12-13, 20-22, 24-25, 27-28, 35-36), the Greek uses the volitive/optative mood ("May the Lord..."), contrasting with the future indicatives used for the resulting outcomes.
- **28:25** — The Greek reads ἐπισκοπήν ("visitation," in the sense of divine judgment or punishment) and διασπορά ("dispersion"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "defeated" and "a horror."
- **28:28** — The Greek text binds the first two afflictions into a single genitive phrase, παραπληξίᾳ ἀορασίας ("derangement of blindness"), rather than separating them with "and" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("madness and blindness").
- **28:32** — Following the shorter Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus), vv. 31-32 lack the clause about having no helper and sons and daughters being given to another nation; thus neuter plural αὐτά ("them") refers back to "your sheep" in v. 31, and σφακελίζοντες depicts eyes wasting away in anguish.
- **28:36** — Greek τοὺς ἄρχοντάς σου ("your rulers," plural) differs from the singular "your king" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **28:48** — The Greek text omits "in thirst and in nakedness" (present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek witnesses) and reads κλοιὸν σιδηροῦν ("an iron collar" rather than "an iron yoke").
- **28:55** — Greek βασκανεῖ ... ὥστε δοῦναι lacks an explicit negative particle before the infinitive because βασκαίνω itself conveys grudgingly withholding ("begrudge ... so as not to give").
- **28:65** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek reads καρδίαν ἑτέραν ἀπειθοῦσαν ("a different, disobedient heart"), differing from other Greek manuscripts ("a despondent heart") and the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("a trembling heart").
- **28:69** — The Saints Greek Critical Text numbers this verse 29:1 and the rest of the chapter 29:2-29. The English keeps the Saints.Bible verse slots (28:69 and 29:1-28) shared with the Hebrew and Latin editions, so Greek 29:n appears at 29:n-1 in this chapter.
