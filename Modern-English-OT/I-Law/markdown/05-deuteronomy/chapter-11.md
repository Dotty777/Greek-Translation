## Text

**11:1** And you shall love the Lord your God, and you shall keep His requirements and His statutes and His judgments all the days.

**11:2** And you shall know today that it is not your children, who do not know and have not seen the discipline of the Lord your God, and His great deeds, and the mighty hand and the uplifted arm,

**11:3** and His signs and His wonders, all that He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and to all his land,

**11:4** and all that He did to the army of the Egyptians, their chariots and their cavalry and their army, how He flooded the water of the Red Sea over their faces while they were pursuing behind you, and the Lord destroyed them until this day,

**11:5** and all that He did for us in the wilderness until you came to this place,

**11:6** and all that He did to Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, son of Reuben, whom the earth, opening its mouth, swallowed—them and their households and their tents and all their possessions that were with them in the midst of all Israel;

**11:7** for your eyes have seen all the works of the Lord that He has done for you today.

**11:8** And you shall keep all His commandments that I am commanding you today, so that you may live and be multiplied, and you will go in and inherit the land into which you are crossing the Jordan to inherit it,

**11:9** so that you may live long on the land that the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed after them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**11:10** For the land into which you are entering to inherit it is not like the land of Egypt, from which you have come out, when they sow the seed and water it with their feet like a vegetable garden;

**11:11** but the land into which you are entering to inherit it is a land of mountains and plains; from the rain of heaven it will drink water;

**11:12** a land that the Lord your God watches over continually; the eyes of the Lord your God are upon it from the beginning of the year even until the end of the year.

**11:13** Now if you diligently listen to all the commandments that I am commanding you today, to love the Lord your God and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,

**11:14** then He will give the rain for your land in its season, early and late, and you will bring in your grain and your wine and your oil;

**11:15** and He will give fodder in your fields for your livestock. And when you have eaten and been filled,

**11:16** watch yourself that your heart is not widened, and that you do not transgress and serve other gods and bow down to them;

**11:17** and the Lord will become enraged with anger against you and shut up heaven, and there will be no rain, and the land will not give its fruit, and you will perish quickly from the good land that the Lord has given you.

**11:18** And you shall place these words in your heart and in your soul, and you shall fasten them as a sign upon your hand, and it shall be immovable before your eyes;

**11:19** and you shall teach them to your children, to speak of them when you sit at home and when you walk on the way, and when you sleep and when you rise up;

**11:20** and you shall write them on the doorposts of your houses and of your gates,

**11:21** so that you may live long, and the days of your sons on the land that the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them may be like the days of heaven upon the earth.

**11:22** And it will be that if you diligently listen to all these commandments that I am commanding you today to do, to love the Lord our God and to walk in all His ways, to cling to Him,

**11:23** then the Lord will drive out all these nations from before you, and you will inherit nations greater and stronger than you.

**11:24** Every place where the sole of your foot treads will be yours; from the wilderness and Anti-Lebanon, and from the great river, the river Euphrates, even to the sea on the west will be your borders.

**11:25** No one will take a stand before you; the Lord your God will put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the face of all the land on which you set foot, just as He spoke to you.

**11:26** See, I am setting before you today the blessing and the curse:

**11:27** the blessing, if you listen to the commandments of the Lord your God that I am commanding you today;

**11:28** and the curses, if you do not listen to the commandments of the Lord your God, all that I am commanding you today, and stray from the way that I commanded you, going to serve other gods whom you do not know.

**11:29** And it will be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land into which you are crossing to inherit it, that you shall give a blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

**11:30** Look, are these not beyond the Jordan, behind the way of the setting sun, in the land of Canaan, which dwells toward the west, adjoining Gilgal, near the high oak?

**11:31** For you are crossing the Jordan, going in to inherit the land that the Lord your God is giving you as an inheritance all the days, and you will dwell in it.

**11:32** And you shall be careful to do all His ordinances and these judgments, all that I am setting before you today.

## Translator Notes

- **11:14** — In vv. 13-15 the Greek keeps Moses speaking in the third person ("all the commandments," "He will give"), whereas the Hebrew MT shifts to God speaking in the first person ("My commandments," "I will give").
- **11:16** — Greek πλατυνθῇ ("be widened") renders the Hebrew verb in the sense of "be wide/enlarged" rather than "be enticed/deceived," and the Greek links the participles at the end of v. 15 directly to the warning here.
- **11:18** — Greek ἀσάλευτον literally means "immovable" or "unshakable," rendering Hebrew totafot ("frontlets").
- **11:24** — The Greek text names Anti-Lebanon (Ἀντιλιβάνου) rather than the Hebrew MT's "Lebanon," and adds "the great" before "river Euphrates."
- **11:30** — The Greek reads "in the land of Canaan, which dwells toward the west" (MT: "of the Canaanites who dwell in the Arabah"), spells Gilgal as "Golgol," and renders Hebrew "oaks of Moreh" as singular "the high oak."
