## Text

**9:1** Hear, Israel: you are crossing this Jordan today to go in to inherit nations great and far stronger than you, cities great and walled up to heaven,

**9:2** a people great and numerous and tall, sons of Anak, whom you know, and you yourself have heard, "Who will stand in the face of the sons of Anak?"

**9:3** And you shall know today that the Lord your God, He goes ahead before you; He is a consuming fire. He will utterly destroy them, and He will turn them back from before you, and you will destroy them just as the Lord told you.

**9:4** Do not say in your heart, when the Lord your God utterly consumes these nations from sight, saying, "Because of my righteousness the Lord has brought me in to inherit this good land."

**9:5** It is not because of your righteousness nor because of the holiness of your heart that you are going in to inherit their land, but because of the ungodliness of these nations the Lord will utterly destroy them from before you, and so that He may establish His covenant which He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

**9:6** And you shall know today that it is not because of your righteous deeds that the Lord your God is giving you this good land to inherit, for you are a stiff-necked people.

**9:7** Remember, do not forget how much you provoked the Lord your God in the wilderness. From the day you came out of Egypt and came to this place, you have continued being disobedient toward the Lord.

**9:8** Even at Horeb you provoked the Lord, and He was furious with you, so as to utterly destroy you,

**9:9** when I was going up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant which the Lord made with you. And I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights; I ate no bread and drank no water.

**9:10** And the Lord gave me the two stone tablets, written with the finger of God, and on them were written all the words which the Lord spoke to you on the mountain on the day of the assembly.

**9:11** And it came about after forty days and forty nights that the Lord gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant.

**9:12** And the Lord said to me, "Rise, go down quickly from here, because your people whom you brought out of the land of Egypt have acted lawlessly. They have quickly turned aside from the way that you commanded them, and they have made for themselves a cast image."

**9:13** And the Lord said to me, "I have spoken to you once and twice, saying, 'I have seen this people, and look, it is a stiff-necked people.'

**9:14** And now let Me utterly destroy them, and I will blot out their name from under heaven, and I will make you into a nation great and strong and far more numerous than this one."

**9:15** And I turned and went down from the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire up to heaven, and the two tablets were in my two hands.

**9:16** And when I saw that you had sinned before the Lord your God, and had made for yourselves a cast image, and had turned aside from the way that the Lord had commanded you,

**9:17** I took hold of the two tablets and threw them from my two hands, and I shattered them before you.

**9:18** And I pleaded before the Lord a second time, just as the first time, forty days and forty nights—I ate no bread and drank no water—because of all your sins that you had sinned, in doing what was evil before the Lord God to provoke Him.

**9:19** And I am terrified because of the wrath and the fury, because the Lord was provoked against you, so as to utterly destroy you. And the Lord listened to me at this time also.

**9:20** And He was furious with Aaron, so as to utterly destroy him, and I prayed for Aaron also at that time.

**9:21** And your sin which you had made, the calf—I took it and burned it up in fire, and crushed it, grinding it thoroughly until it became fine; and it became like dust, and I threw the dust into the stream coming down from the mountain.

**9:22** And at the Burning, and at the Testing, and at the Graves of Craving, you were provoking the Lord.

**9:23** And when the Lord sent you out from Kadesh-barnea, saying, "Go up and inherit the land which I am giving you," you disobeyed the word of the Lord your God, and you did not believe Him, and you did not listen to His voice.

**9:24** You have been disobedient toward the Lord from the day He became known to you.

**9:25** And I pleaded before the Lord for the forty days and forty nights that I pleaded, for the Lord had said He would utterly destroy you.

**9:26** And I prayed to God and said, "Lord, King of the gods, do not utterly destroy Your people and Your portion which You redeemed, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt with Your great strength and with Your mighty hand and with Your uplifted arm.

**9:27** Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, Your servants, to whom You swore by Yourself; do not look upon the stubbornness of this people and their ungodly deeds and their sins,

**9:28** so that those who inhabit the land from which You brought us out may not say, 'Because the Lord was not able to bring them into the land of which He spoke to them, and because He hated them, He brought them out into the wilderness to kill them.'

**9:29** And these are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt with Your great strength and with Your mighty hand and with Your uplifted arm."

## Translator Notes

- **9:12** — The Greek verb is second person singular ("that you commanded them"), matching "your people whom you brought out," where the Hebrew text reads "which I commanded them."
- **9:13** — The Greek adds the Lord's introductory words "I have spoken to you once and twice, saying," before the quotation, a distinctive Septuagint reading absent from the Hebrew text.
- **9:22** — The Greek renders the three wilderness place names (Hebrew Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-hattaavah) by their literal meanings: "the Burning," "the Testing," and "the Graves of Craving."
- **9:24** — The Greek passive verb literally reads "from the day He became known to you" (referring to the Lord), whereas the Hebrew text reads "from the day I knew you."
- **9:26** — The Greek addresses God as "Lord, King of the gods," where the Hebrew text reads "Lord GOD."
