## Text

**8:1** All the commandments that I command you today you shall be careful to do, so that you may live and be multiplied, and enter and inherit the land that the Lord your God swore to your fathers.

**8:2** And you shall remember all the way along which the Lord your God led you in the wilderness, so that He might afflict you and thoroughly test you, and the things in your heart might be discerned, whether you would keep His commandments or not.

**8:3** And He afflicted you and made you suffer hunger, and fed you the manna that your fathers did not know, so that He might declare to you that man will not live on bread alone, but on every word that goes out through the mouth of God man will live.

**8:4** Your garments did not wear out off you, your sandals did not wear away off you, your feet did not become calloused—look, forty years.

**8:5** And you will know in your heart that as if a man were to discipline his son, so the Lord your God will discipline you.

**8:6** And you shall keep the commandments of the Lord your God, to walk in His ways and to fear Him.

**8:7** For the Lord your God is bringing you into a good and large land, where there are streams of water, springs of the deep going out through the plains and through the mountains;

**8:8** a land of wheat and barley, vines, fig trees, pomegranates, a land of olive trees of oil and honey;

**8:9** a land on which you will eat your bread without poverty, and you will not lack anything on it; a land whose stones are iron, and from its mountains you will mine copper.

**8:10** And you will eat and be filled, and you will bless the Lord your God on the good land that He gave you.

**8:11** Watch yourself so that you do not forget the Lord your God by not keeping His commandments and the judgments and His statutes, all that I command you today;

**8:12** so that, when you have eaten and been filled, and have built fine houses and lived in them,

**8:13** and when your cattle have multiplied and your sheep have multiplied, when silver and gold have multiplied for you, and all that you will have has multiplied for you,

**8:14** you do not become lifted up in heart and forget the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery;

**8:15** who led you through that great and fearsome wilderness, where there was biting serpent and scorpion and thirst, where there was no water; who brought out for you from sheer rock a spring of water;

**8:16** who fed you the manna in the wilderness, which your fathers did not know, so that He might afflict you and thoroughly test you, and do good to you at the end of your days.

**8:17** Do not say in your heart, "My strength and the might of my hand produced for me this great power."

**8:18** And you shall remember the Lord your God, for He Himself gives you the strength to produce power, and so that He may establish the covenant that the Lord swore to your fathers, as it is today.

**8:19** And it will be, if with forgetfulness you forget the Lord your God, and walk after other gods and serve them, I solemnly testify to you today that by destruction you will perish;

**8:20** just as also the remaining nations that the Lord is destroying before your face, so you will perish, because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord your God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Unlike the Hebrew, the Greek omits "these forty years" here (placing "look, forty years" in v. 4) and uses a passive verb, "might be discerned" (διαγνωσθῇ).
- **8:3** — The Greek omits "which you did not know," supplies "word" (ῥήματι), and reads "through the mouth of God" (διὰ στόματος θεοῦ) rather than "of the Lord."
- **8:4** — The Greek adds the clause about sandals not wearing away, reads "your feet did not become calloused" (οὐκ ἐτυλώθησαν) rather than "your foot did not swell," and concludes with "look, forty years" (ἰδοὺ τεσσεράκοντα ἔτη).
- **8:15** — The Greek reads "biting serpent" (ὄφις δάκνων, for Hebrew "fiery serpent"), "thirst" (δίψα, for "thirsty ground"), and "a spring of water" (πηγὴν ὕδατος, for "water"); "sheer rock" renders πέτρας ἀκροτόμου.
- **8:17** — Greek δύναμις ("power," also in v. 18) renders a Hebrew term spanning both "power" and "wealth," and the Greek adds "great" (τὴν μεγάλην).
