## Text

**22:1** Do not see your brother's calf or his sheep straying on the road and overlook them; you shall surely bring them back to your brother and return them to him.

**22:2** And if your brother is not near you, or you do not know him, you shall gather it inside your house, and it shall be with you until your brother searches for them, and you shall return them to him.

**22:3** So you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do with every lost thing of your brother; whatever may be lost from him that you find, you may not overlook it.

**22:4** You shall not see your brother's donkey or his calf fallen on the road and overlook them; you shall surely lift them up with him.

**22:5** Articles of a man shall not be on a woman, nor shall a man put on a woman's garment; for everyone who does these things is an abomination to the Lord your God.

**22:6** And if you come upon a nest of birds before you on the road, either in any tree or on the ground, with chicks or eggs, and the mother is brooding over the chicks or over the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young;

**22:7** you shall surely send the mother away, but you may take the young ones for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may have many days.

**22:8** If you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, and you shall not cause murder in your house if anyone who falls should fall from it.

**22:9** You shall not sow your vineyard for a double yield, so that the produce and the seed that someone may sow, together with the produce of your vineyard, may not be consecrated.

**22:10** You shall not plow with a calf and a donkey together.

**22:11** You shall not wear anything adulterated, wool and linen together.

**22:12** You shall make twisted cords for yourself on the four borders of your cloaks that you wrap around yourself.

**22:13** And if anyone takes a wife and lives with her, and hates her,

**22:14** and lays pretextual charges against her, and brings an evil name against her, and says, "I took this woman, and when I approached her, I did not find her signs of virginity,"

**22:15** then the father of the girl and the mother shall take and bring out the girl's signs of virginity to the council of elders at the gate.

**22:16** And the father of the girl shall say to the council of elders, "I gave this daughter of mine to this man as a wife, and having come to hate her,

**22:17** now this man lays pretextual charges against her, saying, 'I did not find signs of virginity in your daughter,' yet these are the signs of virginity of my daughter." And they shall spread out the garment before the council of elders of the city.

**22:18** And the council of elders of that city shall take that man and discipline him,

**22:19** and they shall fine him one hundred shekels and give them to the father of the young woman, because he brought out an evil name against an Israelite virgin; and she shall be his wife; he may not send her away for all time.

**22:20** But if this charge proves true, and signs of virginity are not found for the young woman,

**22:21** then they shall bring the young woman out to the doors of her father, and they shall stone her with stones, and she shall die, because she committed folly among the sons of Israel by prostituting her father's house; and you shall remove the evil person from among yourselves.

**22:22** And if a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, you shall put both of them to death, the man lying with the woman and the woman; and you shall remove the evil person from Israel.

**22:23** And if there is a virgin girl betrothed to a man, and a man finds her in a city and lies with her,

**22:24** you shall bring both of them out to the gate of their city, and they shall be stoned with stones and die: the young woman, because she did not cry out in the city, and the man, because he humiliated his neighbor's wife; and you shall remove the evil person from among yourselves.

**22:25** But if a man finds the betrothed girl in open country and forces her and lies with her, you shall put to death only the one lying with her.

**22:26** And for the young woman there is no sin worthy of death. Just as if a man rises up against his neighbor and murders his life, so is this matter;

**22:27** for he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, and there was no one to help her.

**22:28** And if anyone finds a virgin girl who is not betrothed, and forces her and lies with her, and he is found out,

**22:29** the man who lay with her shall give to the father of the young woman fifty didrachmas of silver, and she shall be his wife, because he humiliated her; he may not send her away for all time.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — The Septuagint consistently renders Hebrew shor ("ox") as moschos ("calf") in 22:1, 4, 10, and here includes "on the road" and "and return them to him" beyond the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **22:9** — Greek diphoron literally means "double-yielding" (bearing two crops), translating Hebrew kilayim ("two kinds"); hagiasthē ("be consecrated") indicates forfeiture to the sanctuary.
- **22:21** — The Greek omits "of the house" after "her father" and "the men of her city" before "shall stone her" (both in the Hebrew MT), and uses masculine singular ton ponēron ("the evil person," echoed in 1 Cor 5:13) here and in 22:22, 24 rather than an abstract neuter "evil."
- **22:29** — The Septuagint uses the Greek silver coin didrachma ("fifty didrachmas of silver") here, whereas 22:19 uses the transliterated siklous ("one hundred shekels").
