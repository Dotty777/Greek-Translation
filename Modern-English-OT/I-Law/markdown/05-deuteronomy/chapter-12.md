## Text

**12:1** And these are the ordinances and the judgments that you shall be careful to do in the land that the Lord, the God of our fathers, is giving you as an inheritance all the days that you live upon the earth.

**12:2** You shall utterly destroy all the places where those whom you are inheriting served their gods, upon the high mountains and upon the hills and under a leafy tree;

**12:3** and tear down their altars, and you shall shatter their pillars, and their sacred groves you shall cut down, and burn the carved images of their gods with fire; and their name will perish from that place.

**12:4** You shall not do so to the Lord your God;

**12:5** rather, to the place that the Lord your God chooses in one of your cities, to name His name there to be called upon—seek there,

**12:6** and you shall bring your burnt offerings, and your pledged offerings, the firstborn of your cattle and of your sheep;

**12:7** and you shall eat there before the Lord your God, and you shall rejoice in everything to which you put your hand, you and your households, as the Lord your God has blessed you.

**12:8** You shall not do all the things that we are doing here today, each one what is pleasing in his own sight;

**12:9** for until now you have not come into the rest and into the inheritance that the Lord our God is giving you.

**12:10** And you will cross the Jordan and dwell in the land that the Lord our God is giving you as an inheritance, and He will give you rest from all your enemies all around, and you will dwell in security.

**12:11** And it will be, to the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there, you shall bring all that I am commanding you today: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, and your tithes and the firstfruits of your hands, and every choice one of your gifts, all that you vow to God;

**12:12** and you shall rejoice before the Lord your God, you and your sons and your daughters, your male servants and your female servants, and the Levite who is at your gates, because he has no portion or inheritance with you.

**12:13** Watch yourself that you do not offer up your burnt offerings in every place that you see,

**12:14** but rather in the place that the Lord your God chooses in one of your tribes; there you shall offer up your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I am commanding you today.

**12:15** However, with all your desire you may slaughter and eat meat, according to the blessing of the Lord your God that He has given you in every city; the unclean among you and the clean alike may eat it, like a gazelle or a deer;

**12:16** only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out on the ground like water.

**12:17** You may not eat in your cities the tithe of your grain and of your wine and of your oil, the firstborn of your cattle, and all vows that you vow, and your pledged offerings, and the firstfruits of your hands;

**12:18** rather, before the Lord your God you shall eat it in the place that the Lord your God chooses for Himself, you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the proselyte who is in your cities; and you shall rejoice before the Lord your God in everything to which you put your hand.

**12:19** Watch yourself that you do not forsake the Levite all the time that you live on the land.

**12:20** Now if the Lord your God enlarges your borders, just as He spoke to you, and you say, "I will eat meat," if your soul desires to eat meat, with all the desire of your soul you may eat meat.

**12:21** And if the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there is far away from you, then you may slaughter from your cattle and from your sheep that God gives you, just as I commanded you, and you may eat in your cities according to the desire of your soul;

**12:22** just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you may eat it; the unclean among you and the clean may likewise eat.

**12:23** Be very careful not to eat blood, because its blood is life; the life shall not be eaten with the meat.

**12:24** You shall not eat it; you shall pour it out on the ground like water.

**12:25** You shall not eat it, so that it may go well with you and with your sons after you, if you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

**12:26** Only you shall take your holy things, if you have any, and your vows, and come to the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there;

**12:27** and you shall offer your burnt offerings; but the meat you shall eat.

**12:28** Be careful and listen, and you shall do all the words that I am commanding you, so that it may go well with you and with your sons forever, if you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

**12:29** Now if the Lord your God utterly destroys from before you the nations among whom you are entering to inherit their land, and you inherit it and dwell in their land,

**12:30** watch yourself that you do not seek to follow after them after they have been utterly destroyed from before you, saying, "How do these nations act toward their gods? I also will do so."

**12:31** You shall not do so to your God; for the abominations of the Lord, which He hated, they have done with their gods, because they burn their sons and their daughters in fire to their gods.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — Compared with the Hebrew MT, the Greek reverses the verbs for sacred groves ("cut down") and carved images ("burn with fire"), and ends intransitively with "their name will perish."
- **12:5** — The Greek specifies "in one of your cities" (MT: "out of all your tribes"), and v. 6 follows a shorter Greek text mentioning only burnt offerings, pledged offerings (ὁμολογίας), and the firstborn of cattle and sheep.
- **12:18** — The Greek reads "the proselyte" (ὁ προσήλυτος, resident alien/convert) "in your cities," where the Hebrew MT has "the Levite who is within your gates."
- **12:23** — Greek ψυχή ("soul/life") here carries the sense "life"; the Greek also adds "its" before "blood" and states the prohibition passively ("the life shall not be eaten with the meat").
- **12:27** — The Greek text here is markedly shorter than the Hebrew MT, omitting the clauses concerning the altar and the pouring out of the blood of sacrifices.
