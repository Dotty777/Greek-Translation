## Text

**6:1** And these are the commandments and the statutes and the judgments that our God commanded to teach you to do so in the land into which you are entering to inherit it,

**6:2** so that you may fear the Lord your God; keep all His statutes and His commandments that I am commanding you today—you and your sons and the sons of your sons—all the days of your life, so that you may prolong your days.

**6:3** And hear, Israel, and be careful to do so, so that it may go well with you, and so that you may be greatly multiplied, just as the Lord, the God of your fathers, spoke to give you a land flowing with milk and honey.

**6:4** And these are the statutes and the judgments that the Lord commanded the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt. Hear, Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord;

**6:5** and you shall love the Lord your God with all your mind and with all your soul and with all your strength.

**6:6** And these words that I am commanding you today shall be in your heart and in your soul;

**6:7** and you shall instruct your sons, and you shall speak of them when sitting in the house and when walking on the road, and when lying down and when rising up;

**6:8** and you shall fasten them as a sign upon your hand, and it shall be immovable before your eyes;

**6:9** and you shall write them upon the doorposts of your houses and of your gates.

**6:10** And it shall be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land that the Lord swore to your fathers, to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, to give you great and fine cities that you did not build,

**6:11** houses full of all good things that you did not fill, hewn cisterns that you did not hew, vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant, and when you have eaten and been filled, watch yourself that you do not forget

**6:12** the Lord your God, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery.

**6:13** The Lord your God you shall fear, and Him you shall serve, and to Him you shall cling, and by His name you shall swear.

**6:14** You shall not go after other gods from the nations that are all around you,

**6:15** for the Lord your God among you is a jealous God; otherwise the Lord your God, becoming angry, may be enraged against you; He will utterly destroy you from the face of the earth.

**6:16** You shall not test the Lord your God, just as you tested Him at the Testing.

**6:17** You shall carefully keep the commandments of the Lord your God, the testimonies and the statutes that He commanded you;

**6:18** and you shall do what is pleasing and what is fine before the Lord your God, so that it may go well with you, and you may enter and inherit the good land that the Lord swore to your fathers,

**6:19** to drive out all your enemies from before your face, just as the Lord spoke.

**6:20** And it shall be, when your son asks you tomorrow, saying, "What are the testimonies and the statutes and the judgments that the Lord our God commanded us?"

**6:21** then you shall say to your son, "We were household servants to Pharaoh in the land of Egypt, and the Lord led us out from there with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm;

**6:22** and the Lord gave signs and wonders, great and grievous, in Egypt, against Pharaoh and against his house, before us,

**6:23** and He led us out from there to give us this land that He swore to give to our fathers;

**6:24** and the Lord commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the Lord our God, so that it may go well with us all the days, so that we may live, just as today;

**6:25** and there will be mercy for us if we are careful to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, just as the Lord commanded us."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Greek text opens this verse with an introductory superscription ("And these are the statutes... out of the land of Egypt") not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, before introducing the Shema ("Hear, Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord").
- **6:5** — "Mind" translates the Greek διανοίας (compare Matthew 22:37; Mark 12:30), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "heart."
- **6:8** — "Immovable" translates the Greek ἀσάλευτον (neuter singular, "fixed/unshaken"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "frontlets/bands."
- **6:16** — "The Testing" translates the Greek place-name τῷ Πειρασμῷ, which renders the Hebrew name Massah by its meaning and creates a wordplay with the verb "test" (ἐκπειράζω).
- **6:25** — "Mercy" translates the Greek ἐλεημοσύνη ("mercy/compassion"), the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew term usually translated "righteousness."
