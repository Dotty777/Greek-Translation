## Text

**32:1** Pay attention, heaven, and I will speak; and let the earth hear words from my mouth.

**32:2** Let my utterance be awaited like rain, and let my words come down like dew, like a shower upon wild grass, and like snowfall upon vegetation.

**32:3** For I have called on the name of the Lord; give greatness to our God.

**32:4** God, His works are true, and all His ways are judgments; a faithful God, and there is no unrighteousness; righteous and holy is the Lord.

**32:5** They have sinned, not His children, blemished ones; a crooked and distorted generation.

**32:6** Do you repay the Lord these things in this way, foolish people and not wise? Did not He Himself, your Father, acquire you and make you?

**32:7** Remember the days of old; understand the years of generations to generations. Ask your father, and he will declare to you; your elders, and they will tell you.

**32:8** When the Most High divided the nations, as He scattered the sons of Adam, He set the boundaries of the nations according to the number of the angels of God.

**32:9** And the Lord's portion became His people Jacob, the measured allotment of His inheritance, Israel.

**32:10** He provided sufficiently for him in the wilderness, in the thirst of scorching heat, in a waterless land; He encircled him and instructed him, and guarded him like the pupil of an eye.

**32:11** As an eagle shelters its nest and yearns over its young, spreading out His wings He received them and took them up upon His back.

**32:12** The Lord alone led them; there was no foreign god with them.

**32:13** He brought them up upon the strength of the land; He fed them the produce of fields; they sucked honey from a rock and oil from a solid rock;

**32:14** butter of cattle and milk of sheep, with fat of lambs and rams, young of bulls and male goats, with fat of kidneys of wheat; and the blood of the grape he drank as wine.

**32:15** And Jacob ate and was filled, and the beloved one kicked; he grew fat, he grew thick, he grew broad; and he forsook God who made him, and turned away from God his Savior.

**32:16** They provoked Me with foreign ones; with their abominations they embittered Me.

**32:17** They sacrificed to demons and not to God, to gods whom they had not known; new, recent ones have come, whom their fathers had not known.

**32:18** You forsook God who fathered you, and you forgot God who nourishes you.

**32:19** And the Lord saw and became jealous, and was provoked because of the anger of His sons and daughters.

**32:20** And He said, "I will turn My face away from them, and I will show what will happen to them in the last days. For it is a perverted generation, sons in whom there is no faithfulness.

**32:21** They made Me jealous with what is not a god; they provoked Me with their idols. And I will make them jealous with what is not a nation; with a nation without understanding I will rouse them to anger.

**32:22** For a fire has been kindled from My wrath; it will burn as far as Hades below; it will devour the earth and its produce; it will set ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

**32:23** I will gather evils against them, and with My arrows I will wage war against them.

**32:24** Wasting away with famine and with the devouring of birds, and an incurable spasm; I will send the teeth of wild beasts against them, with the venom of creatures crawling upon the earth.

**32:25** From outside a sword will make them childless, and from the inner chambers, terror: young man with virgin, nursing infant with an old man of settled age.

**32:26** I said, 'I will scatter them, and I will make the remembrance of them cease from among people,'

**32:27** were it not for the anger of enemies, so that they would not endure long, so that the adversaries would not join in attacking, and would not say, 'Our exalted hand, and not the Lord, has done all these things.'"

**32:28** It is a nation that has lost counsel, and there is no knowledge in them.

**32:29** They did not have the sense to understand; let them receive these things for the coming time.

**32:30** How will one pursue a thousand, and two dislodge ten thousands, unless God had sold them and the Lord had handed them over?

**32:31** For their gods are not like our God; and our enemies are senseless.

**32:32** For their vine is from the vine of Sodom, and their vine branch is from Gomorrah; their grape is a grape of gall, a cluster of bitterness is theirs.

**32:33** Their wine is the venom of dragons, and the incurable venom of asps.

**32:34** Look, have not these things been gathered with Me, and sealed in My treasuries?

**32:35** In the day of vengeance I will repay, when their foot slips; for the day of destruction is near for them, and what is prepared is at hand for you.

**32:36** For the Lord will judge His people, and He will be comforted over His servants; for He saw them paralyzed, and failing in affliction, and exhausted.

**32:37** And the Lord said, "Where are their gods, in whom they had trusted,

**32:38** the fat of whose sacrifices you ate, and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them rise up and help you, and let them become protectors for you.

**32:39** See, see that I am, and there is no god besides Me; I kill and I will make alive; I will strike, and I will heal; and there is no one who will rescue from My hands.

**32:40** For I will lift up My hand to heaven, and I will swear by My right hand and say, 'I live forever.'

**32:41** For I will sharpen My sword like lightning, and My hand will take hold of judgment, and I will render justice to the enemies, and to those who hate I will repay;

**32:42** I will make My arrows drunk from the blood of the wounded and of captivity, from the head of the rulers of enemies."

**32:43** Rejoice, heavens, together with Him, and let the sons of God worship Him; rejoice, nations, with His people, and let all the angels of God grow strong in Him; for the blood of His sons is avenged, and He will take vengeance and repay justice to the enemies, and to those who hate He will repay, and the Lord will cleanse the land of His people.

**32:44** And Moses wrote this song on that day and taught it to the sons of Israel; and Moses went in and spoke all the words of this law into the ears of the people, he and Joshua the son of Nun.

**32:45** And Moses finished speaking to all Israel.

**32:46** And he said to them, "Pay attention with your heart to all these words which I testify to you today, which you will command your sons, to keep and to do all the words of this law;

**32:47** for this is not an empty word for you, because this is your life; and because of this word you will live long upon the land into which you are crossing the Jordan to inherit it."

**32:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses on this day, saying,

**32:49** "Go up to Mount Abarim, this Mount Nebo which is in the land of Canaan opposite Jericho, and see the land of Canaan which I am giving to the sons of Israel;

**32:50** and die on the mountain to which you are going up, and be added to your people, just as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was added to his people;

**32:51** because you disobeyed My word among the sons of Israel at the water of Contradiction at Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not sanctify Me among the sons of Israel.

**32:52** You will see the land opposite, and you will not enter there."

## Translator Notes

- **32:8** — The Greek text reads "according to the number of the angels of God," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "sons of Israel" and a Dead Sea Scrolls manuscript (4QDeut j) reads "sons of God."
- **32:23** — Translates the Greek reading συνπολεμήσω ("wage war with," as in Codex Vaticanus), rather than συντελέσω ("spend / use up") found in other Greek manuscripts and corresponding to the Hebrew.
- **32:43** — The Greek preserves an expanded poetic conclusion calling on the heavens, the sons of God, the nations, and all the angels of God to rejoice and worship Him (clauses cited in Rom 15:10 and Heb 1:6).
- **32:49** — The Greek text (as in Codex Vaticanus) locates Mount Nebo "in the land of Canaan," whereas other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew text read "in the land of Moab."
- **32:51** — "Contradiction" translates the Greek place name Ἀντιλογίας (lit. "contradiction" or "dispute"), the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew Meribah.
