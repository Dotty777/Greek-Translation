## Text

**13:1** Every word that I command you today, this you shall take care to do; you shall not add to it, nor shall you take away from it.

**13:2** Now if a prophet or one who dreams a dream arises among you and gives you a sign or a wonder,

**13:3** and the sign or the wonder comes about of which he spoke to you, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," whom you do not know,

**13:4** you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or of the one who dreams that dream; for the Lord your God is testing you, to know whether you love your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**13:5** Walk after the Lord your God, and you shall fear Him, and you shall listen to His voice, and to Him you shall be joined.

**13:6** And that prophet or that one who dreams the dream shall die; for he spoke to lead you astray from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, who redeemed you from slavery, to drive you out from the way in which the Lord your God commanded you to walk; and you shall eliminate the wicked person from among yourselves.

**13:7** Now if your brother from your father or from your mother, or your son, or your daughter, or the wife who is in your bosom, or a friend who is equal to your soul, urges you secretly, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," whom you and your fathers did not know,

**13:8** from the gods of the nations around you, those near to you or those far from you, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth,

**13:9** you shall not consent to him, and you shall not listen to him, and your eye shall not spare him; you shall not long for him, nor shall you in any way conceal him.

**13:10** You shall surely report about him; your hands shall be upon him first to kill him, and the hands of all the people afterward;

**13:11** and they shall stone him with stones, and he shall die, because he sought to turn you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

**13:12** And all Israel will hear and be afraid, and will not again act according to this wicked thing among you.

**13:13** Now if you hear in one of your cities, which the Lord your God is giving you to dwell there, people saying,

**13:14** "Lawless men have gone out from among you and have turned away all those who dwell in their land, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,' whom you did not know,"

**13:15** then you shall inquire and investigate thoroughly; and look, the report is truly and clearly the case, this abomination has been done among you:

**13:16** you shall surely kill all those who dwell in that land by the slaughter of the sword; you shall utterly devote it to destruction and everything that is in it;

**13:17** and you shall gather all its plunder into its thoroughfares, and burn the city with fire and all its plunder publicly before the Lord your God; and it shall be uninhabited forever, it shall not be rebuilt again.

**13:18** And nothing from what is devoted to destruction shall cling to your hand, so that the Lord may turn away from the fury of His anger; and He will grant you mercy and have mercy on you and multiply you, just as He swore to your fathers,

**13:19** if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep His commandments, all that I command you today, to do what is pleasing and good before the Lord your God.

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — This Greek text omits the clauses about keeping God's commandments and serving Him that appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some other Greek witnesses.
- **13:6** — The Greek uses masculine singular 'the wicked person' (ton poneron) rather than neuter 'the evil', and reads simply 'from slavery' without 'house of' (contrast v. 11).
- **13:10** — The Greek reads 'you shall surely report about him' (anangellon anangeleis), requiring formal denunciation, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'you shall surely kill him'.
- **13:17** — The Greek adverb pandemei ('with the whole people / publicly') renders the Hebrew term for a whole offering; earlier in vv. 14 and 16 the Greek also refers to 'their land' and 'that land' rather than 'city'.
