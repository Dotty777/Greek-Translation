## Text

**29:1** And Moses called all the sons of Israel and said to them, "You have seen all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt before you to Pharaoh and to his attendants and to all his land,

**29:2** the great trials which your eyes have seen, those great signs and wonders.

**29:3** Yet the Lord God has not given you a heart to know and eyes to see and ears to hear up to this day.

**29:4** And He led you in the wilderness forty years; your garments did not grow old, and your sandals did not wear out from your feet.

**29:5** You did not eat bread; you did not drink wine or strong drink, so that you might know that I am the Lord your God.

**29:6** And you came as far as this place, and Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to meet you in battle;

**29:7** and we struck them down, and we took their land, and I gave it as an inheritance to Reuben and to Gad and to the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**29:8** And you shall be careful to do all the words of this covenant, so that you may understand all that you will do.

**29:9** You are all standing today before the Lord your God: your tribal leaders and your council of elders and your judges and your recorders, every man of Israel,

**29:10** your wives and your offspring and the resident alien who is in the midst of your camp, from your woodcutter even to your water-carrier,

**29:11** to enter into the covenant of the Lord your God and into His curses, all that the Lord your God is making with you today,

**29:12** so that He may establish you for Himself as a people, and He Himself will be your God, just as He spoke to you and just as He swore to your fathers, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob.

**29:13** And not with you alone am I making this covenant and this curse,

**29:14** but both with those who are here with us today before the Lord your God, and with those who are not here today.

**29:15** For you know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, how we passed through the midst of the nations, as you passed through.

**29:16** And you saw their abominations and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which are among them.

**29:17** Let there not be among you a man or woman or clan or tribe whose mind has turned away from the Lord your God, going to serve the gods of those nations; let there not be among you a root sprouting upward in gall and bitterness.

**29:18** And it will be, if he hears the words of this curse and blesses himself in his heart, saying, 'May holy things be mine, for I will walk in the wandering of my heart,' so that the sinner may not destroy the sinless along with himself,

**29:19** God will by no means be willing to pardon him, but rather then the anger of the Lord and His jealousy will blaze against that man, and all the curses of this covenant that are written in this book will cling to him, and the Lord will blot out his name from under heaven.

**29:20** And the Lord will separate him for calamities from all the sons of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant that are written in the book of this law.

**29:21** And the next generation, your sons who will rise up after you, and the foreigner who comes from a distant land will say, and they will see the plagues of that land and its diseases which the Lord sent upon it:

**29:22** sulfur and burned salt; its whole land will not be sown, nor will it sprout, nor will any green thing come up upon it, just as Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim were overthrown, which the Lord overthrew in fury and anger.

**29:23** And all the nations will say, 'Why has the Lord done thus to this land? What is this great fury of anger?'

**29:24** And they will say, 'Because they abandoned the covenant of the Lord, the God of their fathers, which He made with their fathers when He brought them out of the land of Egypt,

**29:25** and they went and served other gods whom they did not know and whom He did not allot to them;

**29:26** and the Lord became angry with fury against that land, to bring upon it according to all the curses that are written in the book of this law;

**29:27** and the Lord removed them from their land in fury and anger and very great indignation, and cast them out into another land, as it is now.'

**29:28** The hidden things belong to the Lord your God, but the revealed things belong to you and to your children forever, to do all the words of this law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — In this chapter, Saints.Bible verse numbering follows the 28-verse division (with Greek 29:1 placed at 28:69), so verses 29:1-28 correspond to Greek Deuteronomy 29:2-29 (a -1 offset throughout chapter 29; see note on 28:69).
- **29:4** — The Greek reads third-person 'He led,' aligning with Moses as narrator, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads first-person 'I led' (though v. 5 still shifts to divine first-person speech, 'I am the Lord your God').
- **29:7** — The Greek reads singular 'I gave' (Hebrew: 'we gave') and writes Gaddei, transliterating the Hebrew gentilic 'the Gadite,' paired here with the tribal name Reuben.
- **29:17** — The Greek 'a root sprouting upward in gall and bitterness' differs from the Hebrew 'a root bearing poison and wormwood' and is closely echoed in Hebrews 12:15.
- **29:18** — Greek hosia moi genoito ('May holy things be mine') renders Hebrew 'I shall have peace,' and the closing purpose clause ('so that the sinner may not destroy the sinless along with himself') diverges markedly from the Hebrew idiom 'to sweep away the watered with the dry.'
- **29:28** — The Greek uses second-person plural pronouns throughout ('your God ... to you and to your children'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads first-person plural ('our God ... to us and to our sons').
