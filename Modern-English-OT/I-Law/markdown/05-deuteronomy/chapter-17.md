## Text

**17:1** You shall not sacrifice to the Lord your God a calf or a sheep in which there is a blemish, any bad thing, because it is an abomination to the Lord your God.

**17:2** Now if there is found in one of your cities that the Lord your God is giving you a man or a woman who does what is evil before the Lord your God, transgressing His covenant,

**17:3** and they go and serve other gods and bow down to them—to the sun or the moon or anything from the array of heaven, things which He did not command—

**17:4** and it is reported to you, then you shall inquire thoroughly; and look, the matter has truly happened, this abomination has occurred in Israel;

**17:5** and you shall take out that man or that woman, and you shall stone them with stones, and they shall die.

**17:6** On the basis of two witnesses or on the basis of three witnesses, the one who is to die shall die; he shall not die on the basis of one witness.

**17:7** And the hand of the witnesses shall be against him first to put him to death, and the hand of the people afterward; and you shall remove the evil person from among yourselves.

**17:8** Now if a matter in judgment is too difficult for you—between blood and blood, and between judgment and judgment, and between affliction and affliction, and between dispute and dispute, matters of judgment in your cities—then you shall arise and go up to the place that the Lord your God chooses there,

**17:9** and you shall come to the judge who arises in those days, and they will inquire and declare the judgment to you.

**17:10** And you shall do according to the matter that they declare to you from the place that the Lord your God chooses, and you shall be careful to do all that is legislated for you.

**17:11** According to the law and according to the judgment that they tell you, you shall act; you shall not turn aside from the word that they declare to you, to the right or to the left.

**17:12** And the person who acts in arrogance so as not to obey the priest who stands to minister in the name of the Lord your God, or the judge who is there in those days—that person shall die, and you shall remove the evil person from Israel.

**17:13** And all the people will hear and fear, and will not act impiously anymore.

**17:14** Now if you enter the land that the Lord your God is giving you, and inherit it and dwell upon it, and say, "I will appoint a ruler over myself, just like the rest of the nations around me,"

**17:15** you shall surely appoint over yourself a ruler whom the Lord God chooses. From among your brothers you shall appoint a ruler over yourself; you may not appoint a foreign man over yourself, because he is not your brother.

**17:16** For he shall not multiply horses for himself, nor shall he turn the people back to Egypt, so that he does not multiply horses for himself; and the Lord said, "You shall never again return by this way."

**17:17** And he shall not multiply wives for himself, nor shall his heart turn away; and he shall not greatly multiply silver and gold for himself.

**17:18** And when he is seated over his rule, he shall write for himself this second law in a book from the priests, the Levites,

**17:19** and it shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the Lord your God and to keep all these commandments and to do these statutes,

**17:20** so that his heart may not be lifted up above his brothers, so that he may not depart from the commandments to the right or to the left, in order that he may continue long over his rule, he and his sons among the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — Greek tou kosmou tou ouranou ("the array of heaven") renders Hebrew "host of heaven," and LXX reads third-person "which He did not command" (ou prosetaxen) rather than MT's first-person "which I did not command."
- **17:7** — LXX reads masculine ton poneron ("the evil person," also in v. 12 and quoted in 1 Cor 5:13) rather than neuter "the evil" as in MT.
- **17:9** — Following Codex Vaticanus, the Greek text mentions only "the judge" here (omitting the Levitical priests found in MT and other Greek witnesses), though the following verbs remain plural and the priest appears in v. 12.
- **17:14** — Throughout vv. 14-20, LXX uses archon ("ruler") and arche ("rule") rather than basileus ("king") and "throne/kingdom" as in MT.
- **17:18** — Greek to deuteronomion touto ("this second law") translates Hebrew mishneh hattorah hazzot ("a copy of this law") and is the source of the book's English title, Deuteronomy.
