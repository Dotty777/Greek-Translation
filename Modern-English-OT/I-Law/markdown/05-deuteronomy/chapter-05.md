## Text

**5:1** And Moses called all Israel and said to them, "Hear, Israel, the statutes and the judgments that I am speaking in your ears on this day, and you shall learn them and be careful to do them.

**5:2** The Lord your God made a covenant with you at Horeb.

**5:3** The Lord did not make this covenant with your fathers, but with you—you who are all alive here today.

**5:4** Face to face the Lord spoke to you on the mountain out of the midst of the fire,

**5:5** and I was standing between the Lord and you at that time to declare to you the words of the Lord, because you were afraid before the fire and did not go up onto the mountain, saying:

**5:6** 'I am the Lord your God, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery.

**5:7** You shall not have other gods before My face.

**5:8** You shall not make for yourself an idol, nor a likeness of anything—of all that is in heaven above, and all that is on the earth below, and all that is in the waters beneath the earth.

**5:9** You shall not bow down to them, nor serve them at all; for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation for those who hate Me,

**5:10** and showing mercy to thousands, to those who love Me and to those who keep My decrees.

**5:11** You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain; for the Lord will by no means clear the one who takes His name in vain.

**5:12** Keep the Sabbath day to sanctify it, just as the Lord your God commanded you.

**5:13** Six days you shall labor and do all your works,

**5:14** but on the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God; you shall not do any work on it—you and your sons and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, your ox and your draft animal and all your livestock, the resident alien who lives among you; for in six days the Lord made both heaven and earth, and the sea, and all that is in them; so that your male servant and your female servant may rest just as you do.

**5:15** And you shall remember that you were a household servant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God led you out from there with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm; for this reason the Lord your God ordered you to keep the Sabbath day and to sanctify it.

**5:16** Honor your father and your mother, just as the Lord your God commanded, so that it may go well with you, and so that you may live long upon the land that the Lord your God is giving you.

**5:17** You shall not murder.

**5:18** You shall not commit adultery.

**5:19** You shall not steal.

**5:20** You shall not testify falsely against your neighbor with false testimony.

**5:21** You shall not covet the wife of your neighbor; you shall not covet the house of your neighbor, nor his field, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his draft animal, nor any of his livestock, nor anything that belongs to your neighbor.'

**5:22** These words the Lord spoke to all your assembly on the mountain out of the midst of the fire—darkness, gloom, storm, a loud voice—and He added no more; and He wrote them on two tablets and gave them to me.

**5:23** And it happened, when you heard the voice out of the midst of the fire, and the mountain was burning with fire, that you approached me, all the leaders of your tribes and your council of elders;

**5:24** and you were saying, 'Look, the Lord our God has shown His glory, and we have heard His voice out of the midst of the fire; on this day we have seen that God will speak to a man, and he will live.

**5:25** And now let us not die, for this great fire will consume us; if we ourselves continue to hear the voice of the Lord our God any longer, then we will die.

**5:26** For what flesh is there that has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as we have, and will live?

**5:27** You approach and hear all that the Lord our God may say, and you shall speak to us everything that the Lord our God may speak to you, and we will hear and we will do.'

**5:28** And the Lord heard the voice of your words as you were speaking to me, and the Lord said to me, 'I have heard the voice of the words of this people, all that they have spoken to you; everything that they have spoken is right.

**5:29** Who will grant that their heart be like this within them, so as to fear Me and to keep My commandments all the days, so that it may go well with them and with their sons forever?

**5:30** Go, say to them, "Return to your houses."

**5:31** But you, stand here with Me, and I will speak to you the commandments and the statutes and the judgments that you shall teach them; and let them do them in the land that I am giving them as an inheritance.'

**5:32** And you shall be careful to do just as the Lord your God commanded you; you shall not turn aside to the right nor to the left,

**5:33** in all the way that the Lord your God commanded you, to walk in it, so that He may give you rest, and it may go well with you, and you may prolong your days upon the land that you will inherit."

## Translator Notes

- **5:14** — The Greek text includes the creation rationale ("for in six days the Lord made both heaven and earth, and the sea, and all that is in them"), paralleling Exodus 20:11, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits this clause here.
- **5:17** — In the Greek manuscript tradition of Deuteronomy 5:17-18, some witnesses (including Codex Vaticanus/Swete) place adultery (v. 17) before murder (v. 18), while the Sixtine/Brenton order adopted here places murder (v. 17) before adultery (v. 18) and theft (v. 19); compare the distinct Septuagint order in Exodus 20:13-15 (adultery, theft, murder).
- **5:29** — "Who will grant" translates the literal Greek idiom τίς δώσει, reflecting a Hebrew expression of earnest wish ("Oh that...").
- **5:33** — "So that He may give you rest" translates the Greek καταπαύσῃ σε, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "so that you may live."
