## Text

**2:1** And we turned and set out into the wilderness, by the way of the Red Sea, just as the Lord spoke to me, and we went around Mount Seir for many days.

**2:2** And the Lord said to me,

**2:3** "Let it be enough for you to go around this mountain; turn, therefore, toward the north.

**2:4** And command the people, saying, 'You are passing through the territory of your brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, and they will fear you and be very wary of you.

**2:5** Do not engage in battle with them, for I will certainly not give you any of their land, not even a footstep, because I have given Mount Seir to the sons of Esau as an inheritance.

**2:6** Buy food from them for silver and you will eat, and you will obtain water from them by measure for silver and you will drink.'"

**2:7** For the Lord our God has blessed you in every work of your hands. Consider how you passed through that great and fearsome wilderness; look, for forty years the Lord your God has been with you; you have not lacked a thing.

**2:8** And we passed by your brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, along the way of the Arabah from Ailon and from Ezion-geber; and we turned and passed along the wilderness way of Moab.

**2:9** And the Lord said to me, "Do not be hostile to the Moabites, and do not engage in battle with them, for I will certainly not give you any of their land as an inheritance, for I have given Seir to the sons of Lot to inherit."

**2:10** The Emim formerly resided on it, a great and numerous nation, and strong like the Anakim.

**2:11** These also will be reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim; and the Moabites call them Emim.

**2:12** And in Seir the Horites resided previously, and the sons of Esau destroyed them and wiped them out from their presence, and settled in their place, just as Israel did to the land of his inheritance, which the Lord has given to them.

**2:13** "Now then, rise and set out yourselves, and cross over the valley of Zered." And we crossed over the valley of Zered.

**2:14** And the days during which we traveled from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed over the valley of Zered were thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of men of war perished, dying from the camp, just as God swore to them.

**2:15** And the hand of God was upon them to consume them from the midst of the camp, until they perished.

**2:16** And it came about, when all the men of war had perished, dying from the midst of the people,

**2:17** that the Lord spoke to me, saying,

**2:18** "You will pass by the borders of Moab, Seir, today,

**2:19** and you will draw near to the sons of Ammon. Do not be hostile to them and do not engage with them in battle, for I will certainly not give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon as an inheritance, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as an inheritance."

**2:20** It will be reckoned a land of Rephaim, for the Rephaim also lived on it previously, and the Ammonites call them Zochomein,

**2:21** a great and numerous nation, and mightier than you, like the Anakim. And the Lord destroyed them before their face, and they took possession and settled in their place until this day;

**2:22** just as they did for the sons of Esau who live in Seir, in the way they wiped out the Horites from their presence, and they took possession and settled in their place until this day.

**2:23** And as for the Hivites who lived in Hasedoth as far as Gaza, the Cappadocians who came out from Cappadocia wiped them out and settled in their place.

**2:24** "Now then, rise and set out, and cross over the valley of Arnon yourselves. See, I have handed over into your hands Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and his land. Begin to take possession; engage in battle with him. On this day

**2:25** begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the face of all the nations under heaven, who, upon hearing your name, will be thrown into turmoil, and pangs will seize them at your presence."

**2:26** And I sent envoys from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with peaceful words, saying,

**2:27** "I will pass through your land; I will pass along the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**2:28** You will sell me food for silver and I will eat, and you will sell me water for silver and I will drink; only I will pass through on foot,

**2:29** just as the sons of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Aroer did for me, until I cross over the Jordan into the land which the Lord our God is giving to us."

**2:30** And Sihon king of Heshbon was not willing for us to pass by him, because the Lord our God hardened his spirit and strengthened his heart, so that he might be handed over into your hands, as on this day.

**2:31** And the Lord said to me, "See, I have begun to hand over before you Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and his land; begin to take possession of his land."

**2:32** And Sihon king of Heshbon came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Jahaz.

**2:33** And the Lord our God handed him over before us, and He struck him and his sons and all his people.

**2:34** And we captured all his cities at that time, and we utterly destroyed every city in succession, and their women and their children; we left no survivor.

**2:35** Only we plundered the livestock and took the spoil of the cities,

**2:36** from Aroer, which is beside the bank of the brook Arnon, and the city that is in the valley, and as far as Mount Gilead; there was not a city that escaped us; the Lord our God handed all of them over into our hands.

**2:37** Only we did not come near the sons of Ammon, all that borders the brook Jabbok and the cities in the hill country, just as the Lord our God commanded us.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — The Greek text begins with "the Lord our God" and reads the imperative "Consider how you passed through that great and fearsome wilderness," whereas the Hebrew MT has "the Lord your God" and "He knows your wandering through this great wilderness."
- **2:8** — The Greek text reads "Ailon" (for MT "Elath") and "your brothers" (for MT "our brothers").
- **2:9** — The Greek text reads "Seir" here and in 2:18, and "Aroer" in 2:29, where the Hebrew MT reads "Ar."
- **2:20** — "Zochomein" is the Greek text's distinct name for the people called "Zamzummim" in the Hebrew MT.
- **2:23** — The Greek text identifies these peoples and places as the "Hivites," "Hasedoth," and "Cappadocians" from "Cappadocia," corresponding to MT "Avvim," "Hazerim," and "Caphtorim" from "Caphtor."
- **2:25** — The Greek text reads the second-person imperative "begin to put" (continuing the imperative in v. 24) and "your name," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "I will begin to put" and "the report of you."
