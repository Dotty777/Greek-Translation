## Text

**20:1** Now when you go out to war against your enemies and see horse and rider and a people more numerous than you, you shall not be afraid of them, because the Lord your God is with you, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt.

**20:2** And it shall be, when you draw near to the battle, that the priest shall come forward and speak to the people,

**20:3** and he shall say to them, "Listen, Israel: you are going today into battle against your enemies. Do not let your heart grow faint; do not be afraid, nor be broken, nor turn aside from before them,

**20:4** because it is the Lord your God who goes ahead with you, to fight your enemies together with you, to save you."

**20:5** And the scribes shall speak to the people, saying, "Who is the man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return to his house, or else he may die in the battle and another man will dedicate it.

**20:6** And who is the man who has planted a vineyard and has not taken enjoyment from it? Let him go and return to his house, or else he may die in the battle and another man will take enjoyment from it.

**20:7** And who is the man who is engaged to a woman and has not taken her? Let him go and return to his house, or else he may die in the battle and another man will take her."

**20:8** And the scribes shall speak further to the people and say, "Who is the man who is fearful and cowardly in heart? Let him go and return to his house, so that he does not make his brother's heart cowardly like his own."

**20:9** And it shall be, when the scribes finish speaking to the people, that they shall appoint commanders of the army to lead the people.

**20:10** Now when you approach a city to make war against them, call them out with peace.

**20:11** If they answer you peacefully and open to you, then all the people who are found in it shall be under tribute to you and subject to you;

**20:12** but if they do not obey you and make war against you, you shall besiege it,

**20:13** until the Lord your God delivers it into your hands, and you shall strike down every male of it with the slaughter of the sword,

**20:14** except the women and the dependents; and whatever is in the city and all the movable goods you shall plunder for yourself, and you shall eat all the plunder of your enemies whom the Lord your God is giving you.

**20:15** In this way you shall treat all the cities that are very far away from you, not from the cities of these nations

**20:16** whose land the Lord your God is giving you to inherit; you shall not keep alive anything that breathes,

**20:17** rather, you shall utterly devote them to destruction—the Hittite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite—just as the Lord your God commanded you,

**20:18** so that they do not teach you to do all their abominations that they have done for their gods, and you sin before the Lord your God.

**20:19** Now when you besiege one city for many days, to make war against it for its capture, you shall not destroy its trees by putting iron to them; rather, you may eat from it, but you shall not cut it down. Is the tree in the field a human being, that it should enter the siegework from before you?

**20:20** However, a tree that you know does not bear edible fruit—this you shall destroy and cut down, and you shall build siegeworks against the city that is making war against you, until it is delivered up.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Greek reads ἵππον καὶ ἀναβάτην ("horse and rider"), whereas the Hebrew has "horses and chariots."
- **20:5** — "Scribes" translates Greek γραμματεῖς (also in 20:8-9), which the Septuagint uses here for the Hebrew term denoting military officers or officials.
- **20:16** — Unlike the Hebrew, the Greek text does not repeat "only from the cities of these peoples" at the start of verse 16, linking "not from the cities of these nations" in 20:15 directly into this clause.
- **20:19** — The Greek reads σίδηρον ("iron") rather than "axe," and frames the rhetorical question literally as whether a tree in the field is a human being to enter the siegework from before you.
