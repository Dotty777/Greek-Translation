## Text

**7:1** Now when the Lord your God brings you into the land into which you are entering there to inherit it, He will remove great nations from before you—the Hittite, the Amorite, the Girgashite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite, seven nations, numerous and stronger than you—

**7:2** and the Lord your God will deliver them into your hands, and you will strike them; with obliteration you shall obliterate them. You shall not make a covenant with them, nor shall you show mercy to them,

**7:3** nor shall you intermarry with them; you shall not give your daughter to his son, and you shall not take his daughter for your son.

**7:4** For he will turn your son away from Me, and he will serve other gods; and the Lord will be angered with wrath against you, and He will utterly destroy you quickly.

**7:5** Instead, you shall do this to them: you shall tear down their altars, and you shall shatter their pillars, and you shall cut down their sacred groves, and you shall burn up the carved images of their gods with fire.

**7:6** For you are a holy people to the Lord your God, and the Lord your God selected you to be a special people for Him above all the nations that are on the face of the earth.

**7:7** It is not because you are more numerous than all the nations that the Lord selected you and chose you, for you are the fewest of all the nations;

**7:8** rather, because the Lord loves you, and keeping the oath that He swore to your fathers, the Lord brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

**7:9** And you will know today that the Lord your God, He is God, a faithful God, who keeps covenant and mercy for those who love Him and for those who keep His commandments, to a thousand generations,

**7:10** and who repays those who hate to their face, to utterly destroy them; and He will not delay toward those who hate; to their face He will repay them.

**7:11** And you shall keep His commandments and His ordinances and His judgments, all that I command you today to do.

**7:12** And it will be, if you listen to these ordinances and keep and do them, that the Lord your God will faithfully keep for you the covenant and the mercy that He swore to your fathers;

**7:13** and the Lord will love you and bless you and multiply you, and He will bless the offspring of your womb and the fruit of your land, your grain and your wine and your oil, the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep, on the land, as the Lord swore to your fathers to give to you.

**7:14** You will be blessed above all the nations; there will not be among you a sterile male or a barren female, nor among your livestock.

**7:15** And the Lord will take away from you every sickness; and all the evil diseases of Egypt that you have seen and all that you knew, He will not lay upon you, but He will lay them upon all those who hate you.

**7:16** And you will eat all the spoils of the nations that the Lord your God is giving to you; your eye shall not spare them, and you shall not serve their gods, for this is a stumbling block to you.

**7:17** Now if you say in your mind, "This nation is more numerous than I; how will I be able to utterly destroy them?"

**7:18** you shall not fear them; with remembrance you shall remember all that the Lord your God did to Pharaoh and to all the Egyptians,

**7:19** the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the mighty hand and the uplifted arm, how the Lord your God brought you out. So the Lord our God will do to all the nations whom you fear before their face.

**7:20** And the Lord your God will send the hornets among them, until those who are left behind and those who are hidden from you are wiped out.

**7:21** You will not be wounded before them, for the Lord your God is among you, a great and mighty God.

**7:22** And the Lord your God will consume these nations from before you little by little; you will not be able to completely consume them quickly, so that the land may not become desolate and the wild beasts multiply against you.

**7:23** And the Lord your God will deliver them into your hands, and you will destroy them with a great destruction until He utterly destroys them.

**7:24** And He will deliver their kings into your hands, and their name will perish from the place of those people; no one will resist before your face until He utterly destroys them.

**7:25** You shall burn the carved images of their gods in fire; you shall not covet silver and gold from them; you shall not take it for yourself, so that you do not stumble because of it, for it is an abomination to the Lord your God.

**7:26** And you shall not bring an abomination into your house, and become an accursed thing just like it; with loathing you shall loathe it and with detestation you shall detest it, for it is an accursed thing.

## Translator Notes

- **7:10** — The Greek reads "those who hate" (τοῖς μισοῦσιν) twice without an explicit object ("Him"); "to their face" (κατὰ πρόσωπον) denotes direct, personal repayment.
- **7:16** — Where the Hebrew reads "all the peoples," the Greek reads "all the spoils of the nations" (πάντα τὰ σκῦλα τῶν ἐθνῶν); "stumbling block" translates σκῶλον (lit. a thorn or pointed stake).
- **7:19** — The Greek shifts to "the Lord our God" (where the Hebrew has "your God") and reads "uplifted arm" (βραχίονα τὸν ὑψηλόν) rather than "outstretched arm."
- **7:21** — The Greek reads "you will not be wounded" (οὐ τρωθήσῃ, from τιτρώσκω) where the Hebrew has "you shall not dread," and "mighty" (κραταιός) where the Hebrew has "awesome."
- **7:24** — The Greek reads "from the place of those people" (ἐκ τοῦ τόπου ἐκείνων, lit. "from the place of those ones") rather than the Hebrew "from under heaven," and has third-person singular "until He utterly destroys them" (as in v. 23).
