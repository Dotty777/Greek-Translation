## Text

**4:1** And now, Israel, listen to the statutes and the judgments, all that I am teaching you today to do, so that you may live and be multiplied, and enter and inherit the land that the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving to you.

**4:2** You shall not add to the word that I command you, and you shall not take away from it; keep the commandments of the Lord your God, all that I command you today.

**4:3** Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did to Baal-peor, for every man who walked after Baal-peor, the Lord your God wiped him out from among us;

**4:4** but you who hold fast to the Lord your God are all alive on this day.

**4:5** Look, I have shown you statutes and judgments, just as the Lord commanded me, to do so in the land into which you are entering to inherit it;

**4:6** and you shall keep and do them, for this is your wisdom and understanding before all nations, as many as hear all these statutes, and they will say, "Look, this great nation is a wise and knowledgeable people."

**4:7** For what great nation is there that has a god drawing near to them like the Lord our God in all things in which we call upon Him?

**4:8** And what great nation is there that has righteous statutes and judgments like all this law that I am giving to you today?

**4:9** Watch yourself and guard your soul diligently, so that you do not forget all the words that your eyes have seen, and let them not depart from your heart all the days of your life; and you shall instruct your sons and the sons of your sons: the day you stood before the Lord our God at Horeb on the day of the assembly,

**4:10** when the Lord said to me, "Assemble the people to Me, and let them hear My words, so that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live on the earth, and they shall teach their sons."

**4:11** And you came near and stood beneath the mountain; and the mountain was burning with fire up to heaven, darkness, gloom, storm, a loud voice.

**4:12** And the Lord spoke to you from the midst of the fire a voice of words that you heard, and you saw no likeness, only a voice;

**4:13** and He declared to you His covenant that He commanded you to do, the ten words, and He wrote them on two stone tablets.

**4:14** And the Lord commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and judgments, for you to do them in the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**4:15** And you shall guard your souls diligently, because you saw no likeness on the day when the Lord spoke to you at Horeb on the mountain from the midst of the fire;

**4:16** so that you do not act lawlessly and make for yourselves a carved likeness, any image, the likeness of male or female,

**4:17** the likeness of any beast among those on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies under heaven,

**4:18** the likeness of any creeping thing that creeps on the earth, the likeness of any fish among those in the waters beneath the earth;

**4:19** and so that, looking up to heaven and seeing the sun and the moon and the stars and all the array of heaven, being led astray, you do not bow down to them and serve them, which the Lord your God apportioned to all the nations under heaven.

**4:20** But God took you and brought us out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be for Him a heritage people, as on this day.

**4:21** And the Lord was angry with me concerning the things spoken by you, and He swore that I should not cross this Jordan, and that I should not enter the land that the Lord your God is giving to you as an inheritance.

**4:22** For I am dying in this land, and I am not crossing the Jordan; but you are crossing over and will inherit this good land.

**4:23** Watch yourselves, so that you do not forget the covenant of the Lord your God that He made with you, and make for yourselves a carved likeness of anything that the Lord your God commanded;

**4:24** because the Lord your God is a fire that consumes, a jealous God.

**4:25** Now if you father sons and sons of your sons, and you remain a long time in the land, and act lawlessly and make a carved likeness of anything, and do what is evil before the Lord

**4:26** your God to provoke Him to anger, I call both heaven and earth to witness against you today that you will utterly perish from the land you are crossing the Jordan to inherit; you will not prolong days on it, but instead you will be utterly wiped out.

**4:27** And the Lord will scatter you among all the nations, and you will be left few in number among the nations into which the Lord will bring you.

**4:28** And there you will serve other gods, works of human hands, wood and stone, which will not see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

**4:29** And there you will seek the Lord our God, and you will find Him, whenever you search for Him with all your heart and with all your soul in your affliction;

**4:30** and all these words will find you at the end of the days, and you will turn back to the Lord your God and heed His voice;

**4:31** for the Lord your God is a compassionate God; He will not abandon you nor will He wipe you out; He will not forget the covenant of your fathers that He swore to them.

**4:32** Inquire about former days that were before you, from the day when God created man on the earth, and toward one end of heaven as far as the other end of heaven, whether anything like this great matter has happened, whether such a thing has been heard; whether a nation has heard the voice

**4:33** of the living God speaking from the midst of the fire, in the way that you yourself have heard and lived;

**4:34** whether God has tried to go in and take for Himself a nation from the midst of a nation, with testing and with signs and with wonders and with war and with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm and with great visions, according to all that our God did in Egypt before you as you watched;

**4:35** so that you might know that the Lord your God, He is God, and there is no other besides Him.

**4:36** Out of heaven His voice was made audible to discipline you, and on the earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words from the midst of the fire.

**4:37** Because He loved your fathers, He also chose their seed after them—you—and He Himself brought you out of Egypt by His great strength,

**4:38** and to utterly destroy nations great and stronger than you before your face, to bring you in, to give you their land to inherit, just as you have today.

**4:39** And you shall know today and turn back in your mind that the Lord your God, He is God in heaven above and on the earth below, and there is no other besides Him;

**4:40** and keep His statutes and His commandments, all that I command you today, so that it may go well with you and with your sons after you, so that you may be long-lived in the land that the Lord your God is giving to you for all the days.

**4:41** Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun,

**4:42** for the killer to flee there who kills his neighbor unknowingly, and who did not hate him before yesterday or before the third day; and he will flee for refuge into one of these cities and live:

**4:43** Bezer in the wilderness, in the level land, for Reuben; and Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadite; and Golan in Bashan for Manasseh.

**4:44** This is the law that Moses placed before the sons of Israel;

**4:45** these are the testimonies and the statutes and the judgments, all that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt,

**4:46** in the region beyond the Jordan, in a valley near the house of Peor, in the land of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck down when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**4:47** And they inherited his land and the land of Gog, king of Bashan, two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun;

**4:48** from Aroer, which is on the edge of the torrent of Arnon, and up to Mount Seon, which is Hermon,

**4:49** all the Arabah beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from Ashdoth the hewn.

## Translator Notes

- **4:9** — The Greek includes "on the day of the assembly" (absent in the Hebrew MT) and places the verse break mid-clause between the verb ("when ... said") and its subject ("the Lord") in v. 10, which English word order groups at the start of v. 10.
- **4:20** — This Greek text shifts from "you" to "us," repeats "out of Egypt," and translates the Hebrew "people of inheritance" with the adjective eukleron ("heritage" or "favored").
- **4:34** — Where the Hebrew MT has "great terrors," the Greek reads "great visions" (horamasin megalois, deriving the Hebrew from the root "to see"), and writes "our God" without "the Lord."
- **4:47** — The Greek text (following Codex Vaticanus) reads "Gog" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Og."
- **4:48** — The Greek text reads "Seon" (representing Hebrew Si'on, an alternate name for Mount Hermon, distinct from Mount Zion in Jerusalem).
- **4:49** — The Greek omits the Hebrew MT phrase "as far as the sea of the Arabah" and renders Hebrew "at the foot of the slopes of Pisgah" as "from Ashdoth the hewn" (apo Asedoth ten laxeuten), translating Pisgah etymologically as "hewn."
