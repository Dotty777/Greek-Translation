## Text

**30:1** "And it will be, when all these words come upon you, the blessing and the curse which I have set before you, and you receive them into your heart among all the nations where the Lord scatters you,

**30:2** and you return to the Lord your God and listen to His voice according to all that I am commanding you today, with all your heart and with all your soul,

**30:3** then the Lord will heal your sins, and He will have mercy on you, and He will again gather you from all the nations into which the Lord scattered you.

**30:4** If your dispersion is from the end of heaven to the end of heaven, from there the Lord your God will gather you, and from there the Lord your God will take you.

**30:5** And your God will bring you in from there into the land which your fathers inherited, and you will inherit it; and He will do good to you, and He will make you more numerous than your fathers.

**30:6** And the Lord will thoroughly cleanse your heart and the heart of your descendants, to love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul, so that you may live.

**30:7** And the Lord your God will put these curses upon your enemies and upon those who hate you, who persecuted you.

**30:8** And you will return to the Lord and listen to the voice of the Lord your God, and do His commandments which I am commanding you today.

**30:9** And the Lord your God will bless you in every work of your hands, in the offspring of your womb and in the produce of your land and in the offspring of your livestock. For the Lord your God will return to rejoice over you for good, just as He rejoiced over your fathers,

**30:10** if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes and His judgments that are written in the book of this law, if you return to the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**30:11** For this commandment which I am commanding you today is not too burdensome, nor is it far from you.

**30:12** It is not in heaven above, for one to say, 'Who will ascend for us into heaven and take it for us, and when we have heard it, we will do it?'

**30:13** Nor is it beyond the sea, for one to say, 'Who will cross over for us to the other side of the sea and take it for us and make it heard by us, and we will do it?'

**30:14** The word is very near you, in your mouth and in your heart and in your hands, to do it.

**30:15** Look, I have set before you today life and death, good and evil.

**30:16** If you listen to the commandments of the Lord your God which I am commanding you today, to love the Lord your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His statutes and His judgments, then you will live and become many, and the Lord your God will bless you in all the land into which you are entering to inherit it.

**30:17** And if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and being led astray you bow down to other gods and serve them,

**30:18** I declare to you today that you will certainly perish, and you will by no means live many days upon the land into which you are crossing the Jordan to inherit it.

**30:19** I call both heaven and earth to witness against you today: I have set life and death before you, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, so that you and your descendants may live,

**30:20** to love the Lord your God, to listen to His voice, and to hold fast to Him; for this is your life and the length of your days, that you may dwell upon the land which the Lord swore to your fathers, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, to give to them."

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — The Greek reads 'the Lord will heal your sins,' a notable theological rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the LORD your God will restore your fortunes' (or 'return your captivity').
- **30:6** — The Greek reads 'the Lord will thoroughly cleanse your heart,' interpreting the metaphor where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'will circumcise your heart.'
- **30:14** — The Greek adds 'and in your hands' after 'in your mouth and in your heart,' a phrase absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text and from Paul's quotation in Romans 10:8.
- **30:20** — The Greek uses the neuter pronoun 'this' (referring to loving, listening to, and holding fast to the Lord), whereas the Hebrew pronoun can be read either as 'He is your life' or 'that is your life.'
