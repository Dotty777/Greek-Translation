## Text

**14:1** You are sons of the Lord your God; you shall not make a bald spot between your eyes for a dead person,

**14:2** for you are a holy people to the Lord your God, and the Lord your God chose you to become a treasured people for Himself out of all the nations that are on the face of the earth.

**14:3** You shall not eat any abomination.

**14:4** These are the animals that you may eat: a calf from the cattle, and a lamb from the sheep, and a kid from the goats,

**14:5** a deer, and a gazelle, and a pygarg, an oryx, and a giraffe.

**14:6** Every animal that divides the hoof and splits clefts of two hooves and brings up the cud among the animals, these you may eat.

**14:7** And these you shall not eat from among those that bring up the cud and from among those that divide the hooves and split clefts: the camel, and the hare, and the rock hyrax; because they bring up the cud and do not divide the hoof, these are unclean to you.

**14:8** And the pig, because it divides the hoof and splits clefts of the hoof, yet it does not chew the cud, it is unclean to you; you shall not eat from their flesh, you shall not touch their carcasses.

**14:9** And these you may eat from all that are in the water: everything that has fins and scales you may eat.

**14:10** And everything that does not have fins and scales you shall not eat; they are unclean to you.

**14:11** Every clean bird you may eat.

**14:12** And these from among them you shall not eat: the eagle, and the bearded vulture, and the sea eagle,

**14:13** and the vulture, and the kite and those like it,

**14:15** and the ostrich, and the owl, and the gull,

**14:16** and the heron, and the swan, and the ibis,

**14:17** and the cormorant, and the hawk and those like it, and the hoopoe, and the night raven,

**14:18** and the pelican, and the plover and those like it, and the purple gallinule, and the bat.

**14:19** All creeping winged creatures are unclean to you; you shall not eat of them.

**14:20** Every clean winged creature you may eat.

**14:21** You shall not eat any carcass; it shall be given to the resident alien who is in your cities, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to the foreigner; for you are a holy people to the Lord your God. You shall not boil a lamb in the milk of its mother.

**14:22** You shall surely tithe all the produce of your seed, the produce of your field year by year,

**14:23** and you shall eat it in the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there; you shall bring the tithes of your grain and of your wine and of your oil, the firstborn of your cattle and of your sheep, so that you may learn to fear the Lord your God all the days.

**14:24** Now if the way is far from you and you are unable to carry them up, because the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there is far from you, because the Lord your God will bless you,

**14:25** then you shall sell them for silver, and you shall take the silver in your hands and go to the place that the Lord your God chooses;

**14:26** and you shall spend silver on everything that your soul desires; and you shall eat there before the Lord your God, and you shall rejoice, you and your son,

**14:27** and the Levite who is in your cities, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you.

**14:28** After three years you shall bring out the entire tithe of your produce. In that year you shall place it in your cities.

**14:29** And the Levite shall come, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you, and the proselyte, and the orphan, and the widow who is in your cities, and they shall eat and be filled, so that the Lord your God may bless you in all the works that you do.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — This Greek text omits the prohibition against cutting or gashing oneself for the dead found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek witnesses.
- **14:5** — The Greek lists five wild animals rather than the seven in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, notably identifying the last animal as the giraffe (kamelopardalis).
- **14:13** — Verse 14 (the raven in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) is absent from this Greek text, and the Greek bird list in vv. 12-18 follows a distinctive order and identifications including swan, ibis, pelican, and purple gallinule.
- **14:21** — The Greek specifies 'a lamb' (arna) rather than 'a young goat' as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and uses the passive 'it shall be given'.
- **14:26** — This Greek text omits the list of purchasable items (cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink) and reads 'you and your son' rather than 'your household', continuing directly into v. 27 without 'you shall not forsake him'.
