## Text

**16:1** Observe the month of new produce, and you shall keep the Passover to the Lord your God, because in the month of new produce you came out of Egypt by night.

**16:2** And you shall sacrifice the Passover to the Lord your God, sheep and cattle, in the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there.

**16:3** You shall not eat leaven with it; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread with it, bread of affliction, because you came out of Egypt in haste, so that you may remember the day of your departure from the land of Egypt all the days of your life.

**16:4** Leaven shall not be seen with you within all your borders for seven days, and none of the meat that you sacrifice in the evening on the first day shall remain overnight until morning.

**16:5** You may not sacrifice the Passover in any of your cities that the Lord your God is giving you,

**16:6** but only at the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there; you shall sacrifice the Passover in the evening toward sunset, at the time when you came out of Egypt.

**16:7** And you shall boil and roast and eat in the place that the Lord your God chooses; and in the morning you shall turn back and go to your houses.

**16:8** For six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there is a closing assembly, a festival to the Lord your God; you shall not do any work on it, except whatever is to be done for a person.

**16:9** You shall count out seven weeks for yourself; from when you begin with the sickle in the harvest, you shall begin to count out seven weeks.

**16:10** And you shall keep the Festival of Weeks to the Lord your God according to what your hand is able, as much as the Lord your God gives.

**16:11** And you shall rejoice before your God, and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the Levite and the resident alien and the orphan and the widow who is among you, in the place that the Lord your God chooses for His name to be called upon there.

**16:12** And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and you shall keep and do these commandments.

**16:13** You shall keep the Festival of Tents for yourself for seven days when you gather in from your threshing floor and from your winepress.

**16:14** And you shall rejoice in your festival, you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the Levite and the resident alien and the orphan and the widow who is in your cities.

**16:15** For seven days you shall celebrate a festival to the Lord your God in the place that the Lord your God chooses for Himself; and if the Lord your God blesses you in all your produce and in every work of your hands, then you shall be rejoicing.

**16:16** Three times a year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord your God in the place that the Lord chooses: at the Festival of Unleavened Bread, and at the Festival of Weeks, and at the Festival of Tent-Pitching. You shall not appear before the Lord your God empty-handed;

**16:17** each one according to the ability of your hands, according to the blessing of the Lord your God that He has given you.

**16:18** You shall appoint judges and recorders for yourself in your cities that the Lord your God is giving you, tribe by tribe, and they shall judge the people with righteous judgment.

**16:19** They shall not show partiality, nor shall they accept a bribe; for bribes blind the eyes of the wise and will remove the words of the righteous.

**16:20** Justly you shall pursue what is just, so that you may live and enter and inherit the land that the Lord your God is giving you.

**16:21** You shall not plant a sacred grove for yourself; you shall not make any tree for yourself beside the altar of your God.

**16:22** You shall not set up a pillar for yourself, things that the Lord your God hates.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Greek ton mena ton neon ("the month of new produce") renders Hebrew Abib, and LXX uses the second-person verb "you came out of Egypt by night" rather than MT's "the Lord your God brought you out."
- **16:7** — LXX combines both "boil and roast" (hepseseis kai opteseis, harmonizing with Exod 12:8-9) where MT has only "boil/cook," and reads "your houses" (tous oikous sou) where MT has "your tents."
- **16:8** — LXX adds both "a festival" (heorte) and the exception clause "except whatever is to be done for a person" (lit. "for a soul," psyche, echoing Exod 12:16), neither of which appears in MT.
- **16:18** — Greek grammatoeisagogeis ("court clerks" or "recorders") is a Hellenistic administrative term used in the LXX to translate Hebrew shoterim ("officers").
- **16:21** — Greek alsos ("sacred grove") is the LXX rendering of Hebrew Asherah; LXX also frames the second clause as a separate prohibition ("you shall not make for yourself") rather than a relative clause modifying the altar as in MT.
