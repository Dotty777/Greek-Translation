## Text

**18:1** The priests, the Levites—the whole tribe of Levi—shall have no portion or inheritance with Israel; the offerings of the Lord are their inheritance; they shall eat them.

**18:2** And they shall have no inheritance among their brothers; the Lord Himself is his inheritance, just as He said to him.

**18:3** And this is the due right of the priests—the things from the people, from those sacrificing the sacrifices, whether a calf or a sheep: he shall give to the priest the shoulder and the cheeks and the stomach.

**18:4** And the firstfruits of your grain and your wine and your oil, and the firstfruit of the shearings of your sheep, you shall give to him;

**18:5** because the Lord chose him out of all your tribes to stand before the Lord God, to minister and to bless in His name, he and his sons among the sons of Israel.

**18:6** Now if the Levite comes from one of the cities from among all the sons of Israel where he lives as a resident alien, just as his soul desires, to the place that He chooses,

**18:7** he shall minister in the name of the Lord his God just like all his brothers the Levites who stand there before the Lord your God.

**18:8** He shall eat an apportioned portion, apart from the sale according to paternal family.

**18:9** Now when you enter the land that the Lord your God is giving you, you shall not learn to act according to the abominations of those nations.

**18:10** There shall not be found among you one who purifies his son and his daughter by fire, one who practices divination, one who observes omens and practices augury, one who with charms

**18:11** chants an incantation, a medium and an observer of portents, one who inquires of the dead.

**18:12** For everyone who does these things is an abomination to the Lord your God; for on account of these abominations the Lord will utterly destroy them away from you.

**18:13** You shall be perfect before the Lord your God.

**18:14** For these nations that you are dispossessing—they will listen to omens and divinations; but to you the Lord your God has not given it this way.

**18:15** A prophet from among your brothers like me the Lord your God will raise up for you; to him you shall listen;

**18:16** according to all that you asked from the Lord your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, "We will not hear the voice of the Lord your God again, and we will not see this great fire anymore, nor shall we die."

**18:17** And the Lord said to me, "Everything that they have spoken to you is right;

**18:18** I will raise up a prophet for them from among their brothers like you, and I will put My words in his mouth, and he will speak to them just as I command him.

**18:19** And the person who does not listen to whatever that prophet speaks in My name—I Myself will exact retribution from him.

**18:20** However, the prophet who acts impiously to speak a word in My name that I did not command to speak, and who speaks in the name of other gods—that prophet shall die."

**18:21** And if you say in your heart, "How will we know the word that the Lord has not spoken?"

**18:22** Whatever that prophet speaks in the name of the Lord, and it does not happen and does not come about, this is the word that the Lord has not spoken; that prophet has spoken in impiety; you shall not spare him.

## Translator Notes

- **18:5** — LXX adds "before the Lord God" and "and to bless" (cf. Deut 10:8), and concludes with "among the sons of Israel" where MT reads "all the days" ("forever").
- **18:8** — The terse phrase "apart from the sale according to paternal family" (plen tes praseos tes kata patrian) is difficult in both Greek and Hebrew, generally referring to separate proceeds from the sale of ancestral family property.
- **18:10** — LXX renders "making pass through fire" as perikathairon ... en pyri ("purifying ... by fire"); the dative pharmakois ("with charms" or "with sorcerers") at the end of v. 10 links directly to "chants an incantation" in v. 11 (where other Greek manuscripts read nominative pharmakos, "a sorcerer").
- **18:16** — LXX reads "the Lord your God" (sou, the people addressing Moses) where MT has "the Lord my God."
- **18:22** — Greek ouk aphexesthe autou ("you shall not spare him" or "hold back from him") differs from MT's "you shall not be afraid of him."
