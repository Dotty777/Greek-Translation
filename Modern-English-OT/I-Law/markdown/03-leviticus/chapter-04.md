## Text

**4:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'If a person sins before the Lord unintentionally against any of the ordinances of the Lord which must not be done, and does any one of them:

**4:3** If the anointed high priest sins so that the people sin, then he shall bring for his sin which he sinned a calf from the cattle without blemish to the Lord for his sin.

**4:4** And he shall bring the calf to the door of the tent of testimony before the Lord, and he shall lay his hand on the head of the calf before the Lord, and he shall slaughter the calf before the Lord.

**4:5** And the anointed priest whose hands have been consecrated shall take some of the blood of the calf and bring it into the tent of testimony;

**4:6** and the priest shall dip his finger into the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the Lord toward the holy veil;

**4:7** and the priest shall put some of the blood of the calf on the horns of the altar of compounded incense before the Lord, which is in the tent of testimony; and all the blood of the calf he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offerings, which is at the doors of the tent of testimony.

**4:8** And all the fat of the calf of the sin offering he shall remove from it: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

**4:9** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs, and the lobe that is on the liver—along with the kidneys he shall remove it,

**4:10** in the same way you remove it from the calf of the sacrifice of deliverance; the priest shall bring it up onto the altar of the whole burnt offering.

**4:11** And the hide of the calf and all its flesh, with the head and the extremities and the belly and the dung,

**4:12** they shall carry out the whole calf outside the camp to a clean place where they pour out the ashes, and they shall burn it up on wood with fire; at the place of the pouring out of the ashes it shall be burned.

**4:13** Now if the entire congregation of Israel acts in ignorance unintentionally, and a matter escapes from the eyes of the congregation, and they do any one of all the commandments of the Lord which is not to be done and incur guilt,

**4:14** and the sin which they sinned in it becomes known to them, then the congregation shall bring a calf from the cattle without blemish for the sin offering, and shall bring it to the doors of the tent of testimony.

**4:15** And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the head of the calf before the Lord, and they shall slaughter the calf before the Lord;

**4:16** and the anointed priest shall bring some of the blood of the calf into the tent of testimony;

**4:17** and the priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood of the calf and sprinkle it seven times before the Lord, in front of the holy veil;

**4:18** and the priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of compounded incense, which is before the Lord, which is in the tent of testimony; and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of whole burnt offerings, which is at the door of the tent of testimony.

**4:19** And all the fat he shall remove from it and offer up on the altar;

**4:20** and he shall do to the calf in the same way he did to the calf of the sin offering; so it shall be done. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and the sin will be forgiven them.

**4:21** And they shall carry out the calf outside the camp and burn up the calf in the same way they burned up the first calf; it is a sin offering of the congregation.

**4:22** Now if the ruler sins and does any one of all the commandments of the Lord their God which is not to be done, unintentionally, and sins and incurs guilt,

**4:23** and the sin which he sinned in it becomes known to him, then he shall offer as his gift a young goat from the goats, a male without blemish.

**4:24** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the young goat, and they shall slaughter it in the place where they slaughter the burnt offerings before the Lord; it is a sin offering.

**4:25** And the priest shall put some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offerings.

**4:26** And all its fat he shall offer up on the altar, like the fat of a sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin, and it will be forgiven him.

**4:27** Now if one person from the people of the land sins unintentionally by doing any one of all the commandments of the Lord which is not to be done, and incurs guilt,

**4:28** and the sin which he sinned in it becomes known to him, then he shall bring a young goat from the goats; a female without blemish he shall bring for the sin which he sinned.

**4:29** And he shall lay his hand on the head of his sin offering, and they shall slaughter the young goat of the sin offering in the place where they slaughter the burnt offerings.

**4:30** And the priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

**4:31** And all the fat he shall remove in the same way fat is removed from a sacrifice of deliverance, and the priest shall offer it up on the altar for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord; and the priest shall make atonement for him, and it will be forgiven him.

**4:32** And if he offers a sheep as his gift for a sin offering, he shall offer it, a female without blemish.

**4:33** And he shall lay his hand on its head, the sin offering, and they shall slaughter it in the place where they slaughter the burnt offerings.

**4:34** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering.

**4:35** And all its fat he shall remove in the same way the fat of a sheep is removed from the sacrifice of deliverance, and the priest shall put it on the altar upon the burnt offering of the Lord; and the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin which he sinned, and it will be forgiven him.'"

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — Greek 'skenes tou martyriou' ("tent of testimony") is the standard Septuagint rendering of Hebrew 'ohel moed' ("tent of meeting"), connecting the phrase with witness or testimony.
- **4:5** — The Greek adds "whose hands have been consecrated" (lit. "perfected in his hands"), a Septuagint idiom for priestly ordination not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of this verse.
- **4:10** — "Sacrifice of deliverance" translates Greek 'thysias tou soteriou' (lit. "sacrifice of salvation/deliverance"), the Septuagint term for the peace or well-being offering. The verb 'aphaireite' is second-person plural ("you remove"), likely an itacistic spelling of third-person singular passive 'aphaireitai' ("is removed").
- **4:22** — The Greek reads "the Lord their God" (plural 'auton'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "his God" (singular).
- **4:29** — The Greek reads literally "the head of his sin/offense" ('tou hamartematos autou'), using the term for sin to designate the sin-offering animal, as 'hamartia' does throughout chapters 4-5.
