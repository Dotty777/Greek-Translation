## Text

**21:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Tell the priests, the sons of Aaron, and you shall say to them, 'They shall not be defiled for dead persons among their nation,

**21:2** except for the relative closest to them, for father and mother, and sons and daughters, for a brother

**21:3** and a virgin sister who is close to him, who has not been given in marriage to a man; for these he may be defiled.

**21:4** He shall not be defiled suddenly among his people to his profanation.

**21:5** And you shall not shave a bald spot on your head for a dead person, and they shall not shave the appearance of their beard, and upon their flesh they shall not cut incisions.

**21:6** They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God; for they themselves offer the sacrifices of the Lord, the gifts of their God, and they shall be holy.

**21:7** They shall not take a woman who is a prostitute and profaned, nor a woman cast out from her husband. He is holy to the Lord his God,

**21:8** and he shall sanctify him; he offers the gifts of the Lord your God; he shall be holy, because I am holy, the Lord who sanctifies them.

**21:9** And if a daughter of a man who is a priest is profaned by committing prostitution, she profanes the name of her father. She shall be burned up upon fire.

**21:10** And the great priest from among his brothers, upon whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to put on the garments, shall not remove the turban from his head and shall not tear his garments,

**21:11** and he shall not go in to any dead person; neither for his father nor for his mother shall he be defiled,

**21:12** and he shall not go out from the sanctuary and shall not profane the sanctified place of his God, because the holy anointing oil of God is upon him; I am the Lord.

**21:13** He shall take a virgin from his own lineage;

**21:14** but a widow, and one cast out, and one profaned, and a prostitute—these he shall not take; rather, he shall take a virgin from his own people as a wife;

**21:15** and he shall not profane his seed among his people; I am the Lord who sanctifies him.'"

**21:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**21:17** "Tell Aaron, 'A man from your lineage throughout your generations who has a blemish in him shall not approach to offer the gifts of his God.

**21:18** No man who has a blemish in him shall approach: a man who is lame or blind,

**21:19** or with a mutilated nose or with a cut ear, a man who has a fracture of a hand or a fracture of a foot,

**21:20** or hunchbacked, or having a white spot in the eye, or bleary in the eyes, or a man who has a malignant scab or a scaly eruption, or one with only one testicle—

**21:21** no one from the seed of Aaron the priest who has a blemish in him shall come near to offer the sacrifices to your God; because a blemish is in him, he shall not approach to offer the gifts of God.

**21:22** He may eat the gifts of God, the most holy things and from the holy things;

**21:23** however, he shall not approach the curtain, and he shall not come near to the altar, because he has a blemish; and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his God, because I am the Lord who sanctifies them.'"

**21:24** And Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons and to all the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — Greek en tais psychais, literally "for the souls," uses the Septuagint idiom for deceased persons or corpses; unlike the singular Hebrew, the Greek uses the plural.
- **21:4** — The Greek reads exapina ("suddenly"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads ba'al ("husband" or "chief/leader").
- **21:8** — The Greek reads third-person singular hagiasei ("he shall sanctify him") rather than second-person ("you shall sanctify him"); the unexpressed subject may be an unspecified Israelite, the Lord, or reflexive.
- **21:10** — Greek apokidarōsei specifically means "remove the turban from" (from kidaris, the priestly turban), whereas the Hebrew refers to unbinding or uncovering the head.
- **21:20** — Several Septuagint defect terms specify conditions differently from the Hebrew, including ephēlos ("having a white spot in the eye"), ptilos tous ophthalmous ("bleary in the eyes"), and monorchis ("one with only one testicle"), along with ōtotmētos ("with a cut ear") and hand before foot in 21:19.
