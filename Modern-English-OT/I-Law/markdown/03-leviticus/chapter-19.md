## Text

**19:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**19:2** "Speak to the assembly of the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You shall be holy, because I am holy, the Lord your God.

**19:3** Let each one fear his father and his mother, and you shall keep My Sabbaths; I am the Lord your God.

**19:4** You shall not follow after idols, and you shall not make for yourselves molten gods; I am the Lord your God.

**19:5** And if you sacrifice a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, you shall sacrifice your tithe.

**19:6** On whatever day you sacrifice it, it shall be eaten, and on the next day; and if it is left over until the third day, it shall be burned up in fire.

**19:7** But if it is eaten at all on the third day, it is unfit for sacrifice; it shall not be accepted.

**19:8** And the one who eats it shall incur sin, because he has profaned the holy things of the Lord; and the souls that eat shall be utterly destroyed from their people.

**19:9** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish reaping your harvest of your field, and you shall not gather what falls from your harvest;

**19:10** and you shall not harvest your vineyard a second time, nor shall you gather the single grapes of your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and for the resident alien; I am the Lord your God.

**19:11** You shall not steal; you shall not lie; each one shall not falsely accuse his neighbor.

**19:12** And you shall not swear by My name unjustly, and you shall not profane the name of your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:13** You shall not wrong your neighbor, and you shall not rob; and the wages of the hired worker shall by no means remain overnight with you until morning.

**19:14** You shall not speak evil of a deaf person, and in front of a blind person you shall not place a stumbling block; and you shall fear the Lord your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:15** You shall not commit injustice in judgment; you shall not favor a poor person, nor admire a mighty one; in righteousness you shall judge your neighbor.

**19:16** You shall not walk with deceit among your nation; you shall not stand up against the blood of your neighbor; I am the Lord your God.

**19:17** You shall not hate your brother in your mind; you shall surely reprove your neighbor, and you shall not incur sin because of him.

**19:18** And your hand shall not take vengeance, and you shall not harbor anger against the sons of your people, and you shall love your neighbor as yourself; I am the Lord.

**19:19** You shall keep My law: you shall not breed your livestock with a different kind, and you shall not sow your vineyard with different kinds, and you shall not put on yourself an adulterated garment woven from two materials.

**19:20** And if anyone lies with a woman with an emission of seed, and she is a female household servant reserved for a man, and she has not been ransomed with a ransom or freedom has not been given to her, there shall be an inquiry concerning them; they shall not die, because she was not set free.

**19:21** And he shall bring for his trespass to the Lord, beside the door of the tent of testimony, a ram of trespass;

**19:22** and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of trespass before the Lord for the sin which he sinned, and the sin which he sinned shall be forgiven him.

**19:23** Now when you enter into the land which the Lord God is giving you, and you plant any edible tree, then you shall cleanse away its uncleanness; its fruit shall be uncleansed to you for three years; it shall not be eaten.

**19:24** And in the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, praiseworthy to the Lord,

**19:25** but in the fifth year you shall eat the fruit; its produce shall be an increase for you; I am the Lord your God.

**19:26** Do not eat upon the mountains, and you shall not practice augury nor observe birds for omens.

**19:27** You shall not make a rounded cut from the hair of your head, nor shall you spoil the appearance of your beard.

**19:28** And you shall not make incisions for a soul in your body, and you shall not make tattooed marks on yourselves. I am the Lord your God.

**19:29** You shall not profane your daughter to prostitute her; and the land shall not commit prostitution, and the land shall be filled with lawlessness.

**19:30** You shall keep My Sabbaths, and you shall fear My sanctuary; I am the Lord.

**19:31** You shall not follow after mediums, and you shall not attach yourselves to enchanters so as to be defiled by them. I am the Lord your God.

**19:32** In the presence of a gray-haired person you shall rise up, and you shall honor the person of an elder; and you shall fear your God; I am the Lord your God.

**19:33** And if any resident alien comes to you in your land, you shall not oppress him.

**19:34** The resident alien who comes to you shall be like the native among you, and you shall love him as yourself, because you became resident aliens in the land of Egypt; I am the Lord your God.

**19:35** You shall not commit injustice in judgment, in measures and in weights and in balances.

**19:36** You shall have just balances and just weights and a just chous; I am the Lord your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt.

**19:37** And you shall keep all My law and all My ordinances, and you shall do them; I am the Lord your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — The Greek text reads δεκάτην ("tithe" or "tenth"), a notable Septuagint manuscript reading where other witnesses have δεκτὴν ("acceptable," matching the Hebrew).
- **19:23** — The Greek adds the clause "which the Lord God is giving you" and uses purification language ("cleanse away its uncleanness," "uncleansed") rather than the Hebrew's circumcision imagery.
- **19:26** — The Greek reads "upon the mountains" (ἐπὶ τῶν ὀρέων), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's "with the blood."
- **19:29** — Following Hebrew negative coordination literally, the Greek does not repeat the negative before "and the land shall be filled with lawlessness," though the negation extends to both clauses.
- **19:36** — A chous (χοῦς) is a Greek liquid measure of roughly three liters; the Greek lists three measuring standards where the Hebrew lists four.
