## Text

**1:1** And the Lord summoned Moses and spoke to him from the tent of testimony, saying,

**1:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'If a person among you brings gifts to the Lord, from the livestock, both from the cattle and from the sheep, you shall present your gifts.

**1:3** If his gift is a whole burnt offering from the cattle, he shall bring an unblemished male; he shall present it at the door of the tent of testimony, acceptable for him before the Lord.

**1:4** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the fire offering, acceptable for him, to make atonement on his behalf.

**1:5** And they shall slaughter the calf before the Lord; and the priests, the sons of Aaron, shall present the blood and pour out the blood all around on the altar that is at the doors of the tent of testimony.

**1:6** And having skinned the whole burnt offering, they shall cut it up piece by piece.

**1:7** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall place fire on the altar, and they shall stack wood on the fire.

**1:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall stack the cut pieces and the head and the fat on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar;

**1:9** but they shall wash its entrails and its feet with water; and the priests shall place all of it on the altar. It is a fire offering, a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**1:10** Now if his gift to the Lord is from the sheep, from both the lambs and the young goats, for a whole burnt offering, he shall bring it as an unblemished male,

**1:11** and he shall lay his hand on its head. And they shall slaughter it on the side of the altar toward the north before the Lord; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour out its blood all around on the altar.

**1:12** And they shall divide it piece by piece, and the head and the fat; and the priests shall stack them on the wood that is on the fire on the altar,

**1:13** and they shall wash the entrails and the feet with water; and the priest shall present all of it and place it on the altar. It is a fire offering of sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**1:14** Now if you present a fire offering from the birds as a gift to the Lord, then he shall present his gift from the turtledoves or from the pigeons.

**1:15** And the priest shall bring it to the altar and pinch off its head; and the priest shall place it on the altar, and he shall squeeze out the blood against the base of the altar.

**1:16** And he shall remove the crop with the feathers, and throw it out beside the altar toward the east into the place of the ashes.

**1:17** And he shall break it open by the wings and shall not divide it; and the priest shall place it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a fire offering, a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek skenes tou martyriou ("tent of testimony") is the standard Septuagint rendering used throughout Leviticus where the Hebrew has "tent of meeting."
- **1:4** — Greek karpoma ("fire offering") is a cultic term for an offering consumed by fire on the altar; here and in 1:14 the Greek uses it where the Hebrew has "burnt offering."
- **1:5** — The Greek shifts to third-person plural verbs ("they shall slaughter," continuing in 1:6, 1:9, and 1:11-13) where the Hebrew uses singular verbs for the offerer's actions.
- **1:10** — Greek ton probaton ("the sheep") is used broadly in the Septuagint for small flock livestock, here explicitly encompassing both lambs and young goats (where the Hebrew reads "or").
- **1:16** — Greek syn tois pterois ("with the feathers") reflects an ancient interpretation of the rare Hebrew term often rendered "contents" or "plumage."
