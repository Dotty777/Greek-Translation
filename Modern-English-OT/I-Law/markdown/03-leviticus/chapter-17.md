## Text

**17:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'This is the word that the Lord has commanded, saying,

**17:3** "Any person of the sons of Israel, or of the resident aliens, or of those settled among you, who slaughters a calf or a sheep or a goat in the camp, and whoever slaughters outside the camp,

**17:4** and does not bring it to the door of the tent of testimony so as to make it a burnt offering or a deliverance offering to the Lord, acceptable for a fragrant aroma, and whoever slaughters outside and does not bring it to the door of the tent of testimony so as not to present a gift to the Lord before the tent of the Lord, blood shall be reckoned to that person. He has shed blood; that soul shall be utterly destroyed from its people;

**17:5** so that the sons of Israel may bring up their sacrifices, as many as they themselves slaughter in the fields, and they shall bring them to the Lord to the doors of the tent of testimony, to the priest, and they shall sacrifice them as a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord.

**17:6** And the priest shall pour the blood onto the altar all around before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony, and he shall offer up the fat for a fragrant aroma to the Lord.

**17:7** And they shall no longer sacrifice their sacrifices to the worthless things after which they prostitute themselves. It shall be an eternal statute for you throughout your generations."'

**17:8** And you shall say to them, 'Any person of the sons of Israel, and from the sons of the resident aliens who are settled among you, who makes a burnt offering or a sacrifice,

**17:9** and does not bring it to the door of the tent of testimony to make it for the Lord, that person shall be utterly destroyed from his people.

**17:10** And any person of the sons of Israel, or of the resident aliens who are settled among you, who eats any blood, I will set My face against the soul that eats the blood, and I will destroy it from its people.

**17:11** For the life of all flesh is its blood, and I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your lives; for its blood will make atonement in place of the life.

**17:12** Therefore I have said to the sons of Israel, "No soul from among you shall eat blood, and the resident alien who is settled among you shall not eat blood."

**17:13** And any person of the sons of Israel and of the resident aliens who are settled among you, who hunts game—a wild animal or a bird—that is eaten, shall pour out the blood and cover it with earth.

**17:14** For the life of all flesh is its blood, and I said to the sons of Israel, "You shall not eat the blood of any flesh, because the life of all flesh is its blood; everyone who eats it shall be utterly destroyed."

**17:15** And every soul that eats what died of itself or what was torn by wild beasts, among the native-born or among the resident aliens, shall wash his clothes and bathe himself with water, and shall be unclean until evening; and he shall be clean.

**17:16** But if he does not wash his clothes and does not bathe his body with water, he shall bear his lawlessness.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — The Septuagint includes an expanded doublet naming the burnt offering and deliverance offering (σωτήριον, corresponding to the Hebrew peace/fellowship offering) and renders the sanctuary as the 'tent of testimony' (σκηνὴ τοῦ μαρτυρίου) rather than 'tent of meeting'.
- **17:7** — Greek τοῖς ματαίοις ('the worthless things', a standard Septuagint expression for idols) corresponds to 'goat-demons' in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:8** — The Septuagint reads 'the sons of the resident aliens' (τῶν υἱῶν τῶν προσηλύτων), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads simply 'the sojourners'.
- **17:11** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'is in the blood' and 'by the life', the Septuagint equates the life (ψυχή, 'life/soul') directly with 'its blood' and states that its blood makes atonement 'in place of' (ἀντί) the life.
