## Text

**11:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**11:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'These are the animals that you may eat from among the animals that are on the earth:

**11:3** Every animal that divides the hoof and splits hoof-clefts of two claws and brings up the cud, among the animals, these you may eat.

**11:4** However, from these you shall not eat, from those that bring up the cud and from those that divide the hooves and split hoof-clefts: the camel, because it brings up the cud yet does not divide the hoof, it is unclean to you;

**11:5** and the hare, because it brings up the cud and does not divide the hoof, it is unclean to you;

**11:6** and the rock badger, because it brings up the cud and does not divide the hoof, it is unclean to you.

**11:7** And the pig, because it divides the hoof and splits the claws of the hoof, yet it does not bring up the cud, it is unclean to you.

**11:8** You shall not eat from their meat, and you shall not touch their carcasses; they are unclean to you.

**11:9** And these are what you may eat from all that are in the waters: everything that has fins and scales, in the waters and in the seas and in the streams, these you may eat.

**11:10** And everything that does not have fins or scales, in the water or in the seas and in the streams, from all that the waters bring forth and from every living creature that is in the water, is a detestable thing; and they shall be detestable things

**11:11** for you; you shall not eat from their meat, and their carcasses you shall detest.

**11:12** And everything that does not have fins and scales among those in the water, it is a detestable thing to you.

**11:13** And these are what you shall detest among the winged creatures, and they shall not be eaten; they are a detestable thing: the eagle and the griffon vulture and the sea eagle,

**11:14** and the vulture and the kite and those like it,

**11:16** and the ostrich and the owl and the gull and those like it, and the hawk and those like it,

**11:17** and the night raven and the cormorant and the ibis,

**11:18** and the purple waterhen and the pelican and the swan,

**11:19** and the owl and the heron and the plover and those like it, and the hoopoe and the bat.

**11:20** And all the creeping things among the winged creatures that go on all fours are detestable things to you.

**11:21** Yet these you may eat from the creeping things among the winged creatures that go on all fours: any that has legs above its feet to leap with them on the earth.

**11:22** And these of them you may eat: the wingless locust and those like it, and the attakes and those like it, and the locust and those like it, and the snake-fighter and those like it.

**11:23** Every creeping thing among the winged creatures that has four feet, they are detestable things to you.

**11:24** And by these you will be defiled: everyone who touches their carcasses is unclean until evening,

**11:25** and everyone who carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until evening.

**11:26** And among all the animals, any that divides the hoof and splits hoof-clefts and does not chew the cud, they shall be unclean to you; everyone who touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening.

**11:27** And every one that goes on paws among all the wild animals that go on all fours, they shall be unclean to you; everyone who touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening,

**11:28** and the one who carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until evening; these are unclean to you.

**11:29** And these are unclean to you among the creeping things that are on the earth: the weasel and the mouse and the land crocodile,

**11:30** the shrew and the chameleon and the gecko and the lizard and the mole.

**11:31** These are unclean to you among all the creeping things that are on the earth; everyone who touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening.

**11:32** And anything upon which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean, whether any wooden article, or garment, or skin, or sackcloth; every article with which work is done shall be dipped into water, and it shall be unclean until evening, and it shall be clean.

**11:33** And every clay vessel into the inside of which any of these falls, whatever is inside shall be unclean, and it itself shall be shattered.

**11:34** And every food that is eaten, upon which water comes, shall be unclean; and every drink that is drunk in any container shall be unclean.

**11:35** And anything upon which any of their carcasses falls shall be unclean; ovens and pot-stands shall be torn down; they are unclean, and they shall be unclean to you.

**11:36** However, springs of water and a cistern and a gathering of water shall be clean; but the one who touches their carcasses shall be unclean.

**11:37** And if any of their carcasses falls upon any sowing seed that is to be sown, it shall be clean;

**11:38** but if water is poured upon any seed, and any of their carcasses falls upon it, it is unclean to you.

**11:39** And if one of the animals that you may eat dies, the one who touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening;

**11:40** and the one who eats from these carcasses shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until evening; and the one who carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and shall bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**11:41** And every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, it shall be a detestable thing to you; it shall not be eaten.

**11:42** And every one that goes on its belly, and every one that goes on all fours continually, any that has many feet among all the creeping things that creep on the earth, you shall not eat it, because it is a detestable thing to you.

**11:43** And you shall certainly not make your souls detestable with any of the creeping things that creep on the earth, and you shall not be defiled by these, and you shall not be unclean by them.

**11:44** For I am the Lord your God; and you shall be sanctified and you shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord your God; and you shall not defile your souls with any of the creeping things that move on the earth;

**11:45** for I am the Lord who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; and you shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord.'"

**11:46** This is the law concerning the animals and the winged creatures and every creature that moves in the water and every creature that creeps on the earth,

**11:47** to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between those that bring forth alive what is eaten and those that bring forth alive what is not eaten.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — The Greek text reverses the order of the Hebrew MT in vv. 5-6, listing δασύποδα ('hare') in v. 5 and χοιρογρύλλιον ('rock badger') in v. 6 (MT: rock badger in v. 5, hare in v. 6).
- **11:14** — Verse 11:15 (listing the raven in the Hebrew MT) is absent from this Greek text, while γλαῦκα ('owl') appears twice in the bird list (vv. 16 and 19).
- **11:22** — The four edible insects in Greek are βροῦχον ('wingless locust'), ἀττάκην ('attakes', an unidentified kind of locust), ἀκρίδα ('locust'), and ὀφιομάχην (literally 'snake-fighter', a kind of locust or grasshopper).
- **11:26** — The Greek text omits the negation before 'splits hoof-clefts' (ὀνυχιστῆρας ὀνυχίζει), whereas the Hebrew MT reads 'is not cloven-footed.'
- **11:47** — 'Those that bring forth alive what is eaten' literally translates Greek τῶν ζωογονούντων τὰ ἐσθιόμενα (taking the neuter plural as the direct object of ζωογονέω), whereas the Hebrew MT reads 'the living creature that may be eaten.'
