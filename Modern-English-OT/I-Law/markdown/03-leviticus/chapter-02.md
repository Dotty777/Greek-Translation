## Text

**2:1** Now if a person presents a gift, a sacrifice, to the Lord, his gift shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil on it, and he shall place frankincense on it. It is a sacrifice.

**2:2** And he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests; and having grasped from it a full handful of the fine flour with the oil, and all its frankincense, the priest shall place its memorial on the altar: a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:3** And the remainder from the sacrifice belongs to Aaron and to his sons, a most holy thing from the sacrifices of the Lord.

**2:4** Now if one presents a gift, a sacrifice baked from frankincense, it shall be a gift to the Lord from fine flour: unleavened loaves kneaded with oil and unleavened flat cakes spread with oil.

**2:5** And if your gift is a sacrifice from a frying pan, it is fine flour kneaded with oil, unleavened;

**2:6** and you shall break it into pieces and pour oil on it; it is a sacrifice to the Lord.

**2:7** And if your gift is a sacrifice from a hearth, it shall be made with oil.

**2:8** And he shall present to the Lord the sacrifice that he makes from these, and he shall bring it to the priest; and having come near to the altar,

**2:9** the priest shall remove from the sacrifice its memorial, and the priest shall place it on the altar: a fire offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:10** Now what is left over from the sacrifice belongs to Aaron and to his sons, most holy things from the fire offerings of the Lord.

**2:11** No sacrifice that you present to the Lord shall you make leavened; for of all leaven and all honey, you shall not present any of it to offer as a fire offering to the Lord.

**2:12** As a gift of firstfruits you shall present them to the Lord; but they shall not be brought up onto the altar for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:13** And every gift of your sacrifice shall be salted with salt; you shall not let the salt of the covenant of the Lord cease from your sacrificial offerings; upon every gift of yours you shall present salt to the Lord your God.

**2:14** Now if you present a sacrifice of first-produce to the Lord, it shall be tender, fresh, roasted, crushed groats to the Lord; and you shall present the sacrifice of the first-produce,

**2:15** and you shall pour oil on it, and you shall place frankincense on it. It is a sacrifice.

**2:16** And the priest shall offer up its memorial from the groats with the oil and all its frankincense. It is a fire offering to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Throughout chapter 2, the Greek uses thysia ("sacrifice") for what the Hebrew designates specifically as a grain offering (minchah), and here adds "It is a sacrifice" at the end of the verse.
- **2:4** — The Greek text literally reads ek libanou ("from frankincense"), an early copyist omission of kappa from ek klibanou ("from an oven," matching the Hebrew).
- **2:7** — Greek escharas ("hearth," or portable brazier/grate) stands where the Hebrew names a cooking pan, and the Greek omits the mention of "fine flour" found in the Hebrew.
- **2:14** — Greek chidra ("groats") denotes coarsely crushed, roasted unripe wheat grains; the translation preserves the Septuagint's stacked modifiers ("tender, fresh, roasted, crushed groats").
