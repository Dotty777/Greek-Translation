## Text

**3:1** Now if his gift to the Lord is a sacrifice of deliverance, if he brings it from his cattle, whether a male or a female, he shall bring it unblemished before the Lord.

**3:2** And he shall lay his hands on the head of the gift, and he shall slaughter it before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour out the blood all around on the altar of the whole burnt offerings.

**3:3** And they shall bring from the sacrifice of deliverance a fire offering to the Lord: the fat that covers the belly and all the fat that is on the belly,

**3:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and he shall remove the lobe that is on the liver along with the kidneys.

**3:5** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall offer them up on the altar, upon the whole burnt offerings, on the wood that is on the fire on the altar: a fire offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**3:6** Now if his gift is from the sheep, a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, male or female, he shall present it unblemished.

**3:7** If he brings a lamb as his gift, he shall bring it before the Lord;

**3:8** and he shall lay his hands on the head of his gift, and he shall slaughter it beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour out the blood all around on the altar.

**3:9** And he shall present from the sacrifice of deliverance a fire offering to God: the fat and the unblemished loin—he shall remove it along with the loin muscles—and the fat of the belly,

**3:10** and both kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and having removed the lobe that is on the liver along with the kidneys,

**3:11** the priest shall offer them up on the altar: an aroma of sweet fragrance, a fire offering to the Lord.

**3:12** Now if his gift is from the goats, then he shall bring it before the Lord;

**3:13** and he shall lay his hands on its head, and they shall slaughter it before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour out the blood all around on the altar.

**3:14** And he shall offer up upon it a fire offering to the Lord: the fat that covers the belly and all the fat that is on the belly,

**3:15** and both kidneys and all the fat that is on them, which is on the thighs; and he shall remove the lobe of the liver along with the kidneys.

**3:16** And the priest shall offer them up on the altar: a fire offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord. All the fat belongs to the Lord.

**3:17** It is a statute forever for your generations in every dwelling of yours: you shall not eat any fat or any blood.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Greek thysia soteriou ("sacrifice of deliverance," literally "of salvation/preservation") is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew peace or well-being offering (shelamim).
- **3:2** — The Greek specifies plural "his hands" (also in 3:8 and 3:13, contrasting with singular "hand" in chapter 1 and the Hebrew) and explicitly names "the altar of the whole burnt offerings."
- **3:9** — The Greek reads "to God" rather than "to the Lord," and specifies "the unblemished loin" removed "along with the loin muscles" (psoai), where the Hebrew specifies the entire fat tail removed close to the backbone.
- **3:14** — The Greek text reads ep' autou ("upon it," referring to the altar at the end of 3:13), whereas some Greek manuscripts read ap' autou ("from it," matching the Hebrew).
