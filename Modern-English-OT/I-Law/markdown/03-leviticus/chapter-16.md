## Text

**16:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses after the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they presented strange fire before the Lord, and they died.

**16:2** And the Lord said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron your brother, and let him not enter at all times into the holy place inside the curtain, in front of the mercy seat which is on the ark of testimony, and he shall not die; for I will appear in a cloud over the mercy seat.

**16:3** In this way Aaron shall enter the holy place: with a calf from the herd for a sin offering, and a ram for a burnt offering.

**16:4** And he shall put on a consecrated linen tunic, and linen undergarments shall be on his skin, and he shall wrap himself with a linen sash, and he shall put on a linen turban; they are holy garments, and he shall bathe his entire body in water and put them on.

**16:5** And from the congregation of the sons of Israel he shall take two male goats from the goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

**16:6** And Aaron shall present the calf for his sin offering, and make atonement for himself and his household.

**16:7** And he shall take the two male goats and set them before the Lord by the door of the tent of testimony.

**16:8** And Aaron shall place on the two male goats one lot for the Lord and one lot for the one to be sent away.

**16:9** And Aaron shall present the male goat on which the lot for the Lord fell, and offer it for a sin offering.

**16:10** And the male goat on which the lot of the one to be sent away fell, he shall set it alive before the Lord to make atonement over it, so as to dispatch it for the sending away; he shall release it into the wilderness.

**16:11** And Aaron shall present the calf for the sin offering that is for himself and his household alone, and make atonement for himself and his household, and slaughter the calf for the sin offering that is for himself.

**16:12** And he shall take the censer full of coals of fire from the altar that is before the Lord, and he shall fill his hands with incense of fine composition and bring it inside the curtain,

**16:13** and he shall put the incense on the fire before the Lord; and the vapor of the incense shall cover the mercy seat that is over the testimonies, and he shall not die.

**16:14** And he shall take some of the blood of the calf and sprinkle with his finger on the mercy seat toward the east; in front of the mercy seat he shall sprinkle some of the blood seven times with his finger.

**16:15** And he shall slaughter the male goat for the sin offering that is for the people before the Lord, and bring some of its blood inside the curtain, and do with its blood just as he did with the blood of the calf, and sprinkle its blood on the mercy seat, in front of the mercy seat,

**16:16** and he shall make atonement for the holy place from the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel and from their wrongdoings, for all their sins; and so he shall do for the tent of testimony that is established among them in the midst of their uncleanness.

**16:17** And no person shall be in the tent of testimony when he enters to make atonement in the holy place, until he comes out; and he shall make atonement for himself and his household, and for the entire congregation of the sons of Israel.

**16:18** And he shall go out to the altar that is before the Lord and make atonement over it; and he shall take some of the blood of the calf and some of the blood of the male goat and put it on the horns of the altar all around,

**16:19** and he shall sprinkle some of the blood on it with his finger seven times and cleanse it, and consecrate it from the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel.

**16:20** And he shall finish making atonement for the holy place and the tent of testimony and the altar, and he shall make cleansing for the priests; and he shall present the live male goat.

**16:21** And Aaron shall lay his hands on the head of the live male goat, and confess over it all the lawless deeds of the sons of Israel and all their unrighteous deeds and all their sins, and lay them on the head of the live male goat, and send it away by the hand of a ready man into the wilderness;

**16:22** and the male goat shall bear upon itself their unrighteous deeds to an untrodden land, and he shall send the male goat away into the wilderness.

**16:23** And Aaron shall enter the tent of testimony, and take off the linen robe which he had put on when he entered the holy place, and lay it aside there.

**16:24** And he shall bathe his body in water in a holy place and put on his robe, and he shall go out and offer his whole burnt offering and the whole burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for his household and for the people, as for the priests.

**16:25** And he shall offer up the fat for the sin offerings on the altar.

**16:26** And the one who sends away the male goat that was set apart for release shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and afterward he shall enter the camp.

**16:27** And the calf for the sin offering and the male goat for the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, they shall carry them outside the camp, and they shall burn them up in fire, both their hides and their flesh and their dung.

**16:28** And the one who burns them up shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and afterward he shall enter the camp.

**16:29** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you: in the seventh month, humble your souls and you shall not do any work, the native and the proselyte who is joined among you.

**16:30** For on this day he shall make atonement for you, to cleanse you from all your sins before the Lord; and you shall be cleansed.

**16:31** This shall be a Sabbath of Sabbaths, a rest for you, and you shall humble your souls; it is a perpetual statute.

**16:32** The priest whom they anoint and whose hands they consecrate to serve as priest after his father shall make atonement, and he shall put on the linen robe, a holy robe;

**16:33** and he shall make atonement for the most holy place, and he shall make atonement for the tent of testimony and the altar, and he shall make atonement for the priests and for the entire congregation.

**16:34** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you, to make atonement for the sons of Israel from all their sins." Once a year it shall be done, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Septuagint explicitly adds "strange fire" (pyr allotrion, echoing Lev 10:1), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads only "when they drew near before the Lord."
- **16:8** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Azazel," the Septuagint translates with to apopompaio ("the one to be sent away," i.e., the scapegoat; cf. apopompe, "sending away," in 16:10 and eis aphesin, "for release," in 16:26).
- **16:24** — As in 16:20 ("make cleansing for the priests"), the Septuagint expands the atonement formula here by adding "and for his household" and "as for the priests" (hos peri ton hiereon).
- **16:29** — Following the Codex Vaticanus tradition, the Greek text omits "on the tenth day of the month" after "in the seventh month" and uses an aorist imperative ("humble your souls") rather than a future verb; it also renders the resident alien as proselutos ho proskeimenos ("the proselyte who is joined").
- **16:34** — The Septuagint renders the closing verb as future passive poiethesetai ("Once a year it shall be done"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a narrative past tense ("And he did as the Lord commanded Moses").
