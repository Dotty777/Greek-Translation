## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'When you enter the land which I am giving to you, then the land which I am giving to you shall rest a Sabbath to the Lord.

**25:3** Six years you shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vine and gather its fruit;

**25:4** but in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath rest for the land, a Sabbath to the Lord; you shall not sow your field and you shall not prune your vine,

**25:5** and you shall not reap what grows up of itself from your field, and you shall not gather the grapes of your consecration; it shall be a year of rest for the land.

**25:6** And the Sabbath of the land shall be food for you, and for your male servant, and for your female servant, and for your hired worker, and for the sojourner who resides with you;

**25:7** and for your livestock and for the wild animals that are in your land, all its crop shall be for food.

**25:8** And you shall count out for yourself seven rests of years, seven years seven times; and seven weeks of years shall be for you forty-nine years.

**25:9** And you shall proclaim with the sound of a trumpet in all your land, in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month; on the Day of Atonement you shall proclaim with a trumpet in all your land.

**25:10** And consecrate the year, the fiftieth year, and you shall proclaim release upon the land to all who inhabit it; this shall be for you a year, a signal of release, and each one shall go back to his property, and each of you shall go back to his ancestral home.

**25:11** This is a signal of release; the fiftieth year shall be a year for you; you shall not sow, nor shall you harvest what grows up of itself from it, and you shall not gather what is consecrated of it;

**25:12** because it is a signal of release, it shall be holy to you; from the fields you shall eat its crops.

**25:13** In the year of release, at its signal, he shall return to his property.

**25:14** And if you make a sale to your neighbor, or if you also acquire from your neighbor, let no person oppress his neighbor;

**25:15** according to the number of years after the signal you shall acquire from your neighbor; according to the number of years of crops he shall sell to you.

**25:16** According as there are more of the years, he shall increase his acquisition, and according as there are fewer of the years, he shall decrease his property; for thus he shall sell to you a number of his crops.

**25:17** Let no person oppress his neighbor, and you shall fear the Lord your God; I am the Lord your God.

**25:18** And you shall do all My statutes and all My judgments, and keep them and you shall do them, and you shall dwell in confidence;

**25:19** and the land shall give its yield, and you shall eat to fullness, and you shall dwell in confidence upon it.

**25:20** And if you say, "What shall we eat in this seventh year, if we do not sow and do not gather our crops?"

**25:21** then I am sending My blessing to you in the sixth year, and it shall produce its crops for the three years.

**25:22** And you shall sow in the eighth year, and you shall eat what is old from the crops; until the ninth year, until its crop comes, you shall eat old of the old.

**25:23** And the land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is Mine; because you are resident aliens and sojourners before Me;

**25:24** and throughout all the land of your possession you shall grant redemption for the land.

**25:25** And if your brother who is with you becomes poor and sells from his possession, and the kinsman who is nearest to him comes, then he shall redeem what his brother sold.

**25:26** And if someone does not have a kinsman, and he prospers by his hand and enough is found for him for his redemption,

**25:27** then he shall calculate the years of his sale, and he shall pay back what he has to the man to whom he sold himself, and he shall go back to his possession.

**25:28** And if his hand is not found with enough to pay it back, then what was sold shall belong to the one who acquired it until the sixth year of release, and it shall go out at the release, and he shall go back to his rest.

**25:29** And if anyone sells a dwelling house in a walled city, then there shall be redemption for it; until a year of days is completed, there shall be redemption for it.

**25:30** And if it is not redeemed until a whole year for it is completed, the house that is in the city that has a wall shall be confirmed permanently to the one who acquired it throughout his generations, and it shall not go out in the release.

**25:31** But the houses that are in villages, which do not have a wall around them, let them be reckoned with the field of the land; they shall be redeemable at all times, and in the release they shall go out.

**25:32** And the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession, shall be redeemable at all times for the Levites;

**25:33** and whoever redeems from the Levites, then their sale of houses of the city of their possession shall go out in the release, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**25:34** And the fields set apart for their cities shall not be sold, because this is their eternal possession.

**25:35** And if your brother becomes poor and becomes weak in his hands with you, you shall support him as a resident alien and a sojourner, and your brother shall live with you.

**25:36** You shall not take interest from him nor on increase, and you shall fear your God; I am the Lord; and your brother shall live with you.

**25:37** You shall not give your silver to him at interest, and you shall not give him your food for increase.

**25:38** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to give to you the land of Canaan so as to be your God.

**25:39** And if your brother is brought low with you and is sold to you, he shall not serve you with the service of a household servant;

**25:40** like a hired worker or a sojourner he shall be to you; until the year of release he shall work with you,

**25:41** and he shall go out at the release, and his children with him, and he shall go back to his family; he shall hasten back to the ancestral possession.

**25:42** Because these are My household servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt, he shall not be sold in a sale of a household servant.

**25:43** You shall not wear him down with toil, and you shall fear the Lord your God.

**25:44** And a male servant and a female servant, as many as may become yours from the nations, as many as are around you, from them you shall acquire a male servant and a female servant.

**25:45** And from the sons of the sojourners who are among you, from these you shall acquire, and from their relatives, as many as are born in your land; let them be for you as a possession.

**25:46** And you shall apportion them to your children after you, and they shall be held in possession by you forever; among your brothers the sons of Israel, each one shall not wear down his brother with toils.

**25:47** And if the hand of the resident alien or of the sojourner who is with you finds means, and your brother, having become destitute, is sold to the resident alien or to the sojourner who is with you, a resident alien from birth,

**25:48** after he has been sold to him, there shall be redemption for him; one of your brothers shall redeem him.

**25:49** The brother of his father or a son of the brother of his father shall redeem him, or one from the close relatives of his flesh from his tribe shall redeem him; and if, having prospered in his hands, he redeems himself,

**25:50** then he shall calculate with the one who acquired him from the year in which he sold himself to him until the year of release, and the silver of his sale shall be like that of a hired laborer; year by year he shall be with him.

**25:51** And if for anyone there are more of the years, according to these he shall pay back his redemption price from the silver of his sale;

**25:52** and if little is left of the years until the year of release, then he shall calculate with him according to his years, and he shall pay back his redemption price. Like a hired worker

**25:53** year by year he shall be with him; you shall not wear him down with toil in your sight.

**25:54** And if he is not redeemed after these things, he shall go out in the year of release, he and his children with him.

**25:55** For to Me the sons of Israel are household servants; these are My servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:10** — Greek ἀφέσεως σημασία ("signal of release") renders the Hebrew Jubilee (yobel) with reference to the trumpet signal proclaiming the fiftieth year of release, repeated in vv. 11-13.
- **25:27** — The Greek source reads ὅπερ ἔχει ("what he has," rather than ὃ ὑπερέχει, "the remainder") and explicitly includes ἑαυτὸν ("to whom he sold himself").
- **25:28** — The Greek source distinctly reads τοῦ ἕκτου ἔτους τῆς ἀφέσεως ("the sixth year of release") and κατάπαυσιν ("rest") rather than κατάσχεσιν ("possession").
- **25:33** — The elliptical Greek construction ὃς ἂν λυτρωσάμενος παρὰ τῶν Λευειτῶν ("whoever redeems from the Levites") uses a participle with ὃς ἂν to render a notoriously debated legal clause on Levitical house redemption.
- **25:55** — The Greek text concludes with the exodus statement and does not include the closing formula "I am the Lord your God" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
