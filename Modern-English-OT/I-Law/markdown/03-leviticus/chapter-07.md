## Text

**7:1** "And this is the law of the ram for trespass: it is most holy.

**7:2** In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering, they shall slaughter the ram of trespass before the Lord, and he shall pour out the blood on the base of the altar all around;

**7:3** and he shall offer all its fat from it, and the loin, and all the fat that covers the inward parts, and all the fat that is on the inward parts,

**7:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, that which is on the thighs, and the lobe that is on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove them;

**7:5** and the priest shall offer them up on the altar as a fire offering to the Lord; it is for trespass.

**7:6** Every male from among the priests shall eat it; in a holy place they shall eat it; it is most holy.

**7:7** Just as the sin offering is, so also is the trespass offering; there is one law for them: as for the priest who makes atonement, it shall be in his possession.

**7:8** And as for the priest who presents a person's burnt offering, the hide of the burnt offering that he himself offers shall belong to him.

**7:9** And every sacrifice that is prepared in the oven, and every one that is prepared on a hearth or on a griddle, belongs to the priest who offers it; it shall be his.

**7:10** And every sacrifice mixed with oil and not mixed shall belong to all the sons of Aaron, to each an equal share.

**7:11** This is the law of the sacrifice of deliverance that they shall offer to the Lord.

**7:12** If he offers it for praise, then he shall offer with the sacrifice of praise loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened flat cakes spread with oil, and fine flour kneaded with oil;

**7:13** with leavened loaves he shall offer his gifts, along with a deliverance sacrifice of praise.

**7:14** And he shall present one from all his gifts as a set-apart portion to the Lord; it shall belong to the priest who pours out the blood of the deliverance offering.

**7:15** And the flesh of the deliverance sacrifice of praise shall belong to him; and on the day it is given, it shall be eaten; they shall not leave any of it until the morning.

**7:16** And if he sacrifices his gift as a vow or a freewill offering, on whatever day he presents his sacrifice it shall be eaten, and on the next day;

**7:17** and what is left over from the flesh of the sacrifice until the third day shall be burned up in fire.

**7:18** But if he actually eats from the flesh on the third day, it shall not be accepted for him who offers it; it shall not be credited to him; it is a defilement; and the soul that eats from it shall bear the sin.

**7:19** And whatever flesh touches anything unclean shall not be eaten; it shall be burned up in fire; everyone who is clean shall eat flesh.

**7:20** But the soul that eats from the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance that belongs to the Lord, while his uncleanness is on him, that soul shall be destroyed from its people.

**7:21** And a soul that touches any unclean thing, whether from the uncleanness of a person, or of unclean four-footed animals, or any unclean abomination, and eats from the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance that belongs to the Lord, that soul shall be destroyed from its people."

**7:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:23** "Speak to the sons of Israel: 'You shall not eat any fat of cattle and sheep and goats.

**7:24** And the fat of animals that die naturally, and what is torn by wild beasts, may be used for any work, yet for food it shall not be eaten;

**7:25** as for everyone who eats fat from the livestock from which one presents a fire offering of the Lord, that soul shall be destroyed from its people.

**7:26** You shall not eat any blood in all the land, your dwelling place, both from the birds and from the livestock.

**7:27** Every soul that eats blood, that soul shall be destroyed from its people.'"

**7:28** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:29** "And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'The one who offers a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord shall bring his gift to the Lord, even from the sacrifice of deliverance;

**7:30** his hands shall offer the fire offerings to the Lord: the fat that is on the breast and the lobe of the liver, he shall offer them so as to place them as a gift before the Lord.

**7:31** And the priest shall offer up the fat on the altar, and the breast shall belong to Aaron and shall belong to his sons.

**7:32** And you shall give the right shoulder as a set-apart portion to the priest from your sacrifices of deliverance;

**7:33** as for the one who offers the blood of the deliverance offering and the fat, that which is from the sons of Aaron, to him the right shoulder shall belong as a portion.

**7:34** For I have taken the breast of the placed offering and the shoulder of the set-apart portion from the sons of Israel, from your sacrifices of deliverance, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as an eternal statute from the sons of Israel.'"

**7:35** This is the anointing of Aaron and the anointing of his sons from the fire offerings of the Lord, on the day when He brought them near to serve as priests to the Lord,

**7:36** just as the Lord commanded to give to them on the day when He anointed them, from the sons of Israel; it is an eternal statute for their generations.

**7:37** This is the law of the burnt offerings, and of the sacrifice, and of the offering for sin, and of the trespass offering, and of consecration, and of the sacrifice of deliverance,

**7:38** in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day when He commanded the sons of Israel to offer their gifts before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — Greek ten osphyn ("the loin") appears here where the Masoretic Text specifies the animal's fat tail.
- **7:11** — Greek thysia soteriou ("sacrifice of deliverance") renders the Hebrew peace or fellowship offering throughout Leviticus.
- **7:15** — Unlike the Masoretic Text, the Septuagint includes auto estai ("shall belong to him"), assigning the flesh of the deliverance sacrifice of praise to the officiating priest.
- **7:30** — The Septuagint adds "and the lobe of the liver" (absent from the Masoretic Text) and renders the Hebrew "wave offering" as a "placed" gift (epithenai doma, corresponding to epithema in 7:34; similarly brachion, "shoulder/arm," in 7:32-34 corresponds to Hebrew "thigh").
- **7:33** — The Greek text reads neuter to ("that which is from the sons of Aaron"), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Masoretic Text read masculine ("the one from among the sons of Aaron").
