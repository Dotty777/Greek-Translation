## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "You shall also speak to the sons of Israel:

**20:2** 'If anyone from the sons of Israel or from those who have become resident aliens in Israel gives any of his seed to a ruler, he shall surely be put to death; the nation that is upon the land shall stone him with stones.

**20:3** And I will set My face against that man and I will destroy him from his people, because he gave of his seed to a ruler, so as to defile My holy places and profane the name of those consecrated to Me.

**20:4** And if the natives of the land completely look away with their eyes from that man when he gives of his seed to a ruler, so as not to kill him,

**20:5** then I will set My face against that man and his relatives, and I will destroy him and all who agree with him, so that he commits prostitution toward the rulers, from their people.

**20:6** And whatever soul follows after mediums or enchanters so as to commit prostitution after them, I will set My face against that soul and I will destroy it from its people.

**20:7** And you shall be holy, because I am holy, the Lord your God;

**20:8** and you shall keep My ordinances and do them; I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**20:9** Every person who speaks evil of his father or his mother shall surely be put to death; he has spoken evil of his father or his mother; he shall be guilty.

**20:10** A man who commits adultery with a man's wife, or who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife—the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

**20:11** If anyone lies with his father's wife, he has uncovered his father's nakedness; he shall be put to death; both are guilty.

**20:12** And if anyone lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death; for they have acted impiously; they are guilty.

**20:13** And whoever lies with a male as one lies with a woman, both of them have done an abomination; they shall be put to death; they are guilty.

**20:14** Whoever takes a woman and her mother, it is a lawless deed; they shall burn up him and them in fire, and there shall not be lawlessness among you.

**20:15** And whoever gives his intercourse to a four-footed animal shall surely be put to death, and you shall kill the four-footed animal.

**20:16** And any woman who approaches any animal to be mounted by it, you shall kill the woman and the animal; she shall surely be put to death; they are guilty.

**20:17** Whoever takes his sister, from his father or from his mother, and sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace; they shall be utterly destroyed before the sons of their kin; he has uncovered his sister's nakedness; they shall bear sin.

**20:18** And a man who lies with a menstruating woman and uncovers her nakedness, he has uncovered her fountain, and she has uncovered the flow of her blood; both of them shall be utterly destroyed from their kin.

**20:19** And you shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister and of your mother's sister; for he has uncovered close kinship; they shall bear sin.

**20:20** Whoever lies with his female relative, he has uncovered the nakedness of his female relative; they shall die childless.

**20:21** Whoever takes his brother's wife, it is his uncleanness; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall die childless.

**20:22** And keep all My ordinances and My judgments, and you shall do them; and the land into which I am bringing you to dwell there upon it shall by no means be disgusted with you.

**20:23** And you are not to walk in the statutes of the nations, whom I am sending away from you; because they did all these things, and I detested them.

**20:24** And I said to you, "You inherit their land, and I will give it to you as a possession, a land flowing with milk and honey." I am the Lord your God, who set you apart from all the nations.

**20:25** And you shall separate them between the clean animals and the unclean animals, and between the clean birds and the unclean; and you shall not make your souls detestable by the animals and by the birds and by all the creeping things of the earth, which I separated for you in uncleanness.

**20:26** And you shall be holy to Me, because I am holy, the Lord your God, who separated you from all the nations to be Mine.

**20:27** And a man or a woman among them who becomes a medium or an enchanter, both of them shall surely be put to death; stone them with stones; they are guilty.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — Here and in verses 3-5, the Greek translates the Hebrew as "a ruler" (ἄρχοντι) or "the rulers" (τοὺς ἄρχοντας), reading it from the root for "king/ruler" rather than as the proper name Molech.
- **20:3** — The Greek reads "the name of those consecrated to Me" (τῶν ἡγιασμένων μοι), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "My holy name."
- **20:9** — Here and in verses 11-13, 16, and 27, the Greek renders the Hebrew idiom "his/their blood is upon him/them" as "guilty" (ἔνοχος/ἔνοχοι).
- **20:20** — The Greek twice reads "his female relative" (τῆς συγγενοῦς αὐτοῦ) and omits "they shall bear their sin," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's references to an uncle's wife and uncle.
- **20:22** — The Greek reads "be disgusted with" (προσοχθίσῃ) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "vomit you out."
