## Text

**26:1** "I am the Lord your God. You shall not make for yourselves handmade objects or carved images, nor shall you set up a pillar for yourselves, nor shall you place a lookout stone in your land to bow down to it; I am the Lord your God.

**26:2** You shall keep My Sabbaths, and you shall fear My holy places; I am the Lord.

**26:3** If you walk in My ordinances, and keep My commandments and do them,

**26:4** then I will give you the rain in its season, and the land will yield its produce, and the trees of the plains will yield their fruit.

**26:5** And for you the reaping will overtake the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will overtake the sowing; and you will eat your bread to the full, and you will dwell in security in your land, and war will not pass through your land.

**26:6** And I will give peace in your land, and you will lie down to sleep and there will be no one to terrify you, and I will destroy evil beasts from your land.

**26:7** And you will pursue your enemies, and they will fall before you by slaughter.

**26:8** And five of you will pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you will pursue tens of thousands, and your enemies will fall before you by the sword.

**26:9** And I will look upon you, and I will increase you and multiply you, and I will establish My covenant with you.

**26:10** And you will eat what is old and old of the old, and you will carry out the old from before the new.

**26:11** And I will set My covenant among you, and My soul will not abhor you.

**26:12** And I will walk about among you and will be God to you, and you will be My people.

**26:13** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt when you were servants; and I shattered the bond of your yoke and led you with boldness.

**26:14** But if you do not obey Me, and do not do these ordinances of Mine,

**26:15** but disobey them, and your soul loathes My judgments so that you do not do all My commandments, so as to break My covenant,

**26:16** then I also will do this to you, and I will bring upon you distress, both scabies and jaundice, with your eyes festering and your soul melting away; and you will sow your seeds in vain, and your adversaries will eat them.

**26:17** And I will set My face against you, and you will fall before the enemies, and those who hate you will pursue you, and you will flee when no one is pursuing you.

**26:18** And if up to this point you do not obey Me, then I will further discipline you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:19** And I will shatter the insolence of your pride, and I will make the sky iron for you and your land like bronze.

**26:20** And your strength will be in vain, and your land will not yield its seed, and the tree of your field will not yield its fruit.

**26:21** And if after these things you walk sideways and are unwilling to obey Me, I will add to you seven plagues according to your sins.

**26:22** And I will send upon you the wild beasts of the land, and they will devour you and consume your livestock, and I will make you few in number, and your roads will be made desolate.

**26:23** And if despite these things you are not disciplined, but walk sideways toward Me,

**26:24** then I also will walk with you in sideways wrath, and I also will strike you sevenfold in return for your sins.

**26:25** And I will bring upon you a sword exacting the vengeance of the covenant, and you will flee for refuge into your cities; and I will send out death among you, and you will be handed over into the hands of enemies.

**26:26** When I afflict you with a scarcity of bread, ten women will bake your loaves in one oven, and they will give back your loaves by weight; and you will eat and will by no means be filled.

**26:27** But if despite these things you do not obey Me and walk sideways toward Me,

**26:28** then I Myself will walk with you in sideways wrath, and I will discipline you sevenfold according to your sins.

**26:29** And you will eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you will eat.

**26:30** And I will make your pillars desolate, and I will utterly destroy your wooden handmade objects, and I will place your corpses upon the corpses of your idols, and My soul will loathe you.

**26:31** And I will make your cities desolate, and I will lay waste your holy places, and I will by no means smell the aroma of your sacrifices.

**26:32** And I Myself will lay waste your land, and your enemies who dwell in it will be astonished at it.

**26:33** And I will scatter you among the nations, and the advancing sword will consume you; and your land will be desolate, and your cities will be desolate.

**26:34** Then the land will delight in its Sabbaths even all the days of its desolation,

**26:35** and you will be in the land of your enemies; then the land will keep Sabbath, and the land will delight in its Sabbaths. All the days of its desolation it will keep the Sabbaths that it did not keep during your Sabbaths, when you were dwelling in it.

**26:36** And as for those who are left of you, I will bring bondage into their heart in the land of their enemies, and the sound of a driven leaf will pursue them, and they will flee as those fleeing from war, and they will fall when no one is pursuing.

**26:37** And brother will disregard brother as in war, when no one is chasing, and you will not be able to stand against your enemies.

**26:38** And you will perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will devour you.

**26:39** And those who are left of you will decay because of your sins; in the land of their enemies they will melt away.

**26:40** And they will confess their sins and the sins of their fathers, that they transgressed and disregarded Me, and that they walked sideways against Me,

**26:41** and I walked with them in sideways wrath. And I will destroy them in the land of their enemies; then their uncircumcised heart will be put to shame, and then they will accept their sins.

**26:42** And I will remember the covenant of Jacob, and the covenant of Isaac and the covenant of Abraham I will remember, and the land I will remember.

**26:43** And the land will be forsaken by them; then the land will receive its Sabbaths when it is made desolate because of them, and they themselves will receive their own lawless deeds, because they disregarded My judgments and loathed My ordinances in their soul.

**26:44** And not even so, when they were in the land of their enemies, did I disregard them, nor did I loathe them so as to consume them, to break My covenant with them; for I am the Lord their God.

**26:45** And I will remember their former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, in the sight of the nations, to be their God; I am the Lord.

**26:46** These are My judgments and My ordinances, and the law which the Lord gave between Himself and the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses."

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Septuagint opens with "I am the Lord your God" (absent from the Masoretic Text) and translates Hebrew even maskit ("figured stone") as lithon skopon ("lookout stone," literally a stone set as a watch-post or visual mark).
- **26:11** — The Greek text reads "My covenant" (ten diatheken mou), differing from the Masoretic Text's "My dwelling/tabernacle."
- **26:21** — Greek plagios, repeated seven times in vv. 21-41, literally means "sideways" or "oblique," expressing contrary hostility both in Israel's walk toward God and in God's responsive "sideways wrath."
- **26:36** — The Greek text reads doulian ("bondage/slavery," matching v. 45) rather than deilian ("faintness/cowardice," which corresponds to the Masoretic Text).
- **26:41** — The Greek reads apolo ("I will destroy them") rather than the Masoretic Text's "I brought them," and uses eudokesousin ("accept," literally "be well pleased with") to translate Hebrew ratsah ("accept the penalty of / make amends for").
- **26:46** — The Septuagint unanimously reads first-person possessives "My judgments and My ordinances" (ta krimata mou kai ta prostagmata mou) before shifting mid-verse into the third-person Sinai colophon ("which the Lord gave ... by the hand of Moses"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits "My" and treats the entire verse as a third-person narrator colophon.
