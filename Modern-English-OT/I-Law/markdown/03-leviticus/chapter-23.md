## Text

**23:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**23:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'The feasts of the Lord, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, these are My feasts.

**23:3** Six days you shall do work, and on the seventh day is a Sabbath, a rest, a holy convocation to the Lord; you shall not do any work; it is a Sabbath to your Lord in every dwelling of yours.

**23:4** These are the feasts to the Lord, and these; proclaim them holy at their appointed times.

**23:5** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the evenings, is the Passover to the Lord.

**23:6** And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the Lord; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**23:7** And the first day shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work.

**23:8** And you shall offer burnt offerings to the Lord for seven days, and the seventh day shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work.'"

**23:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:10** "Tell the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'When you enter the land that I am giving to you and reap its harvest, then you shall bring a sheaf, the firstfruits of your harvest, to the priest.

**23:11** And he shall lift up the sheaf before the Lord to be acceptable for you; on the day after the first day the priest shall lift it up.

**23:12** And on the day on which you bring the sheaf, you shall prepare an unblemished year-old sheep for a burnt offering to the Lord,

**23:13** and its sacrifice: two-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil; a sacrifice to the Lord, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord; and its drink offering, a fourth of a hin of wine.

**23:14** And you shall not eat bread or roasted fresh grain until this very day, until you yourselves present the gifts to your God; it is an eternal statute throughout your generations in every dwelling of yours.

**23:15** And you yourselves shall count from the day after the Sabbath, from the day on which you present the sheaf of the placed offering, seven complete weeks;

**23:16** until the day after the last week you shall count fifty days, and you shall present a new sacrifice to the Lord.

**23:17** From your dwelling you shall present loaves as a placed offering, two loaves; they shall be of two-tenths of fine flour; they shall be baked leavened, of the first produce to the Lord.

**23:18** And he shall offer with the loaves seven unblemished year-old lambs, and one calf from the herd, and two unblemished rams; they shall be a burnt offering to the Lord, along with their sacrifices and their drink offerings; a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**23:19** And they shall prepare one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, and two year-old lambs for a sacrifice of deliverance with the loaves of the first produce.

**23:20** And the priest shall place them with the loaves of the first produce as a placed offering before the Lord, with the two lambs; they shall be holy to the Lord; to the priest who presents them, to him it shall belong.

**23:21** And you shall proclaim this day a convocation; it shall be holy for you; you shall not do any servile work on it; it is an eternal statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling.

**23:22** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish the remainder of the harvest of your field when you reap, and you shall not gather what falls from your harvest; you shall leave it for the poor and for the resident alien. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:24** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there shall be a rest for you, a memorial of trumpets; it shall be a holy convocation for you.

**23:25** You shall not do any servile work, and you shall offer a burnt offering to the Lord.'"

**23:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:27** "And on the tenth day of this seventh month is a day of atonement; it shall be a holy convocation for you; and you shall humble your souls, and you shall offer a burnt offering to the Lord.

**23:28** You shall not do any work on this very day; for this is a day of atonement for you, to make atonement for you before the Lord your God.

**23:29** Every soul that is not humbled on this very day shall be utterly destroyed from its people.

**23:30** And every soul that does work on this very day, that soul shall perish from its people.

**23:31** You shall not do any work; it is an eternal statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**23:32** It shall be a Sabbath of Sabbaths for you, and you shall humble your souls; from the ninth day of the month, from evening until evening, you shall observe your Sabbath."

**23:33** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:34** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths for seven days to the Lord.

**23:35** And the first day is a holy convocation; you shall not do any servile work.

**23:36** For seven days you shall offer burnt offerings to the Lord; and the eighth day shall be a holy convocation for you, and you shall offer burnt offerings to the Lord; it is a closing ceremony; you shall not do any servile work.

**23:37** These are the feasts to the Lord which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, so as to present altar offerings to the Lord: burnt offerings and their sacrifices and their drink offerings, the daily portion for each day,

**23:38** besides the Sabbaths of the Lord, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides your freewill offerings that you give to the Lord.

**23:39** And on the fifteenth day of this seventh month, when you finish the produce of the land, celebrate a feast to the Lord for seven days; on the first day is a rest, and on the eighth day is a rest.

**23:40** And on the first day you shall take beautiful fruit of a tree, and fronds of palm trees, and thick branches of a tree, and branches of willow and of chaste tree from a stream, to rejoice before the Lord your God seven days of the year.

**23:41** It is an eternal statute throughout your generations. In the seventh month you shall celebrate it.

**23:42** You shall dwell in booths for seven days; every native in Israel shall dwell in booths,

**23:43** so that your generations may see that I caused the sons of Israel to dwell in booths when I brought them out from the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:44** And Moses spoke the feasts of the Lord to the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — The Greek reads "and these; proclaim them holy" (καὶ αὗται, ἁγίας καλέσατε αὐτάς) rather than "holy convocations which you shall proclaim" as in other manuscripts and the Hebrew Masoretic Text, likely reflecting an early scribal shift from κληταί ("convocations") to καὶ αὗται ("and these").
- **23:11** — The Greek specifies "on the day after the first day" (τῇ ἐπαύριον τῆς πρώτης), referring to the day after the first day of Unleavened Bread rather than "the day after the Sabbath" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (compare 23:15, where the Greek retains "the Sabbath").
- **23:15** — Greek ἐπίθεμα ("placed offering," literally something laid upon; also in 23:17, 20) corresponds to what the Hebrew Masoretic Text calls a "wave offering." In 23:13 and 23:17, "two-tenths" denotes two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, and a "hin" is a liquid measure.
- **23:40** — The Greek adds branches of "chaste tree" (ἄγνος) alongside willow from a stream, and places "of the year" (τοῦ ἐνιαυτοῦ) at the end of 23:40 while 23:41 omits the Hebrew Masoretic Text's opening clause about celebrating the feast for seven days in the year.
- **23:43** — The Greek reads ἴδωσιν ("may see") rather than εἰδῶσιν ("may know"), though the two forms were pronounced identically in Koine Greek.
