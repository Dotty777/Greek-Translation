## Text

**10:1** And the two sons of Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, each taking his firepan, put fire on it and placed incense on it, and offered strange fire before the Lord, which the Lord had not commanded them.

**10:2** And fire came out from the Lord and devoured them, and they died before the Lord.

**10:3** And Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the Lord spoke, saying, 'Among those who draw near to Me I will be sanctified, and in the whole congregation I will be glorified.'" And Aaron was deeply stricken.

**10:4** And Moses called Misadai and Elizaphan, sons of Aziel, sons of the brother of Aaron's father, and said to them, "Come forward and carry your brothers from before the sanctuary outside the camp."

**10:5** And they came forward and carried them in their tunics outside the camp, just as Moses had said.

**10:6** And Moses said to Aaron and Eleazar and Ithamar, his remaining sons, "You shall not remove the turban from your head and you shall not tear your garments, so that you will not die, nor wrath come upon the whole congregation; your brothers, the whole house of Israel, shall weep for the burning with which they were burned by the Lord.

**10:7** And you shall not go out from the door of the tent of testimony, so that you will not die, for the anointing oil from the Lord is upon you." And they did according to the word of Moses.

**10:8** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, saying,

**10:9** "Wine and strong drink you shall not drink, you and your sons with you, whenever you enter the tent of testimony, or when you approach the altar, and you will certainly not die; it is an eternal ordinance throughout your generations,

**10:10** to distinguish between the holy and the profane, and between the unclean and the clean.

**10:11** And you shall teach the sons of Israel all the ordinances that the Lord spoke to them by the hand of Moses."

**10:12** And Moses said to Aaron and to Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron who were left, "Take the sacrifice that was left from the fire offerings of the Lord, and eat it unleavened beside the altar; it is most holy.

**10:13** And you shall eat it in a holy place, for this is an eternal ordinance for you and an ordinance for your sons from the fire offerings of the Lord; for so it has been commanded to me.

**10:14** And the breast of the separated offering and the shoulder of the contribution you shall eat in a holy place, you and your sons and your household with you; for it has been given as an ordinance for you and an ordinance for your sons from the sacrifices of deliverance of the sons of Israel.

**10:15** The shoulder of the contribution and the breast of the separated offering they shall bring along with the fire offerings of the fat portions, to set apart as a separated offering before the Lord; and it shall be for you and for your sons and for your daughters with you an eternal ordinance, just as the Lord commanded Moses."

**10:16** Now Moses searched diligently for the male goat of the sin offering, and look, it had been burned up; and Moses became angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, the remaining sons of Aaron, saying,

**10:17** "Why did you not eat the sin offering in a holy place? For since it is most holy, He gave this to you to eat, so that you might take away the sin of the congregation and make atonement for them before the Lord.

**10:18** For none of its blood was brought into the sanctuary; you shall eat it right inside, in a holy place, just as the Lord commanded me."

**10:19** And Aaron spoke to Moses, saying, "If today they have offered their sin offerings and their whole burnt offerings before the Lord, and these things have happened to me, and I eat the sin offerings today, would it be pleasing to the Lord?"

**10:20** And Moses heard, and it pleased him.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — 'Deeply stricken' translates Greek κατενύχθη ('pierced/stunned with grief'), where the Hebrew MT reads 'kept silent'; likewise 'in the whole congregation' renders ἐν πάσῃ συναγωγῇ (MT: 'before all the people').
- **10:4** — The Greek text reads Μισαδαὶ ('Misadai') and Ἀζιήλ ('Aziel'), where the Hebrew MT has Mishael and Uzziel.
- **10:6** — 'Remove the turban from your head' translates the rare Greek verb ἀποκιδαρώσετε (literally 'un-turban', from κίδαρις, the priestly turban), whereas the Hebrew MT refers to leaving the hair unbound.
- **10:14** — The Septuagint terms ἀφόρισμα ('separated offering'), ἀφαίρεμα ('contribution'), βραχίων ('shoulder'), and θυσίαι τοῦ σωτηρίου ('sacrifices of deliverance') correspond to the Hebrew MT's wave offering, heave offering, thigh, and peace offerings; the Greek also reads 'in a holy place' and 'your household' (MT: 'in a clean place' and 'your daughters').
- **10:18** — The Greek joins κατὰ πρόσωπον ἔσω ('right inside') to the command to eat the offering and concludes 'just as the Lord commanded me' (Hebrew MT: 'just as I commanded').
