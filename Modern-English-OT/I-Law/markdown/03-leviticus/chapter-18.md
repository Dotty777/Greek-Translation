## Text

**18:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**18:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'I am the Lord your God.

**18:3** According to the practices of the land of Egypt, in which you lived, it shall not be done; and according to the practices of the land of Canaan, into which I am bringing you, you shall not do so, and you shall not walk in their statutes.

**18:4** You shall do My judgments and keep My ordinances, to walk in them; I am the Lord your God.

**18:5** And you shall keep all My ordinances and all My judgments, and do them; a person who does them will live in them; I am the Lord your God.

**18:6** No person shall approach any close relatives of his flesh to uncover nakedness; I am the Lord.

**18:7** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother; for she is your mother, and you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:8** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife; it is the nakedness of your father.

**18:9** The nakedness of your sister from your father or your mother, born at home or outside—you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:10** The nakedness of your son's daughter or of your daughter's daughter—you shall not uncover their nakedness, because it is your own nakedness.

**18:11** You shall not uncover the nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife; she is your sister by the same father; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:12** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister; for she is a close relative of your father.

**18:13** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister; for she is a close relative of your mother.

**18:14** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's brother, and you shall not go in to his wife; for she is your relative.

**18:15** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law; for she is your son's wife; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:16** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife; it is the nakedness of your brother.

**18:17** You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and of her daughter; you shall not take her son's daughter and her daughter's daughter to uncover their nakedness; for they are close relatives of yours; it is impiety.

**18:18** You shall not take a woman in addition to her sister as a rival, to uncover her nakedness alongside her while she is still alive.

**18:19** And you shall not approach a woman during the separation of her uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.

**18:20** And you shall not give intercourse of your seed to your neighbor's wife, to be thoroughly defiled with her.

**18:21** And you shall not give any of your offspring to serve a ruler, and you shall not profane the holy name; I am the Lord.

**18:22** And with a male you shall not lie as in intercourse with a woman; for it is an abomination.

**18:23** And you shall not give your intercourse to any four-footed animal for emission of seed, to be thoroughly defiled with it; and a woman shall not stand before any four-footed animal to be mounted; for it is loathsome.

**18:24** Do not defile yourselves by any of these things; for by all these things the nations that I am sending out before your face were defiled.

**18:25** And the land was defiled, and I repaid them for iniquity because of it, and the land became disgusted with those living on it.

**18:26** And you shall keep all My statutes and all My ordinances, and you shall not do any of all these abominations—neither the native of the land nor the resident alien who has come among you;

**18:27** for the people of the land who were before you did all these abominations, and the land was defiled;

**18:28** and so that the land may not become disgusted with you when you defile it, in the way it became disgusted with the nations that were before you.

**18:29** For everyone who does any of all these abominations—the souls that do them shall be utterly destroyed from their people.

**18:30** And you shall keep My ordinances, so that you may not do any of all the abominable statutes that has been done before you, and you shall not be defiled by them; for I am the Lord your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:3** — The Septuagint uses the passive οὐ ποιηθήσεται ('it shall not be done') in the first clause before shifting to the active οὐ ποιήσετε ('you shall not do so') in the second.
- **18:6** — Greek ἀσχημοσύνη (literally 'unseemliness' or 'shameful nakedness') is used throughout this chapter as the Septuagint euphemism for nakedness in the idiom 'uncover nakedness'.
- **18:21** — Greek λατρεύειν ἄρχοντι ('to serve a ruler') corresponds to the Hebrew phrase commonly translated 'to pass through [the fire] to Molech', and τὸ ὄνομα τὸ ἅγιον reads 'the holy name' rather than 'the name of your God'.
- **18:25** — Greek προσώχθισεν ('became disgusted with', also in v. 28) replaces the physical metaphor of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('vomited out').
- **18:28** — The Septuagint reads the plural 'the nations' (τοῖς ἔθνεσιν), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the singular 'the nation'.
