## Text

**9:1** And it happened on the eighth day that Moses called Aaron and his sons and the council of elders of Israel.

**9:2** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take for yourself a tender calf from the cattle for a sin offering, and a ram for a whole burnt offering, without blemish, and present them before the Lord;

**9:3** and speak to the council of elders of Israel, saying, 'Take one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, and a calf and a year-old lamb for a whole fire offering, without blemish,

**9:4** and a young bull and a ram for a sacrifice of deliverance before the Lord, and fine flour kneaded with oil; for today the Lord will appear among you.'"

**9:5** And they took them, just as Moses commanded, in front of the tent of testimony; and all the congregation approached and stood before the Lord.

**9:6** And Moses said, "Do this word that the Lord spoke, and the glory of the Lord will appear among you."

**9:7** And Moses said to Aaron, "Approach the altar, and offer your sin offering and your whole burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself and your house; and offer the gifts of the people, and make atonement for them, just as the Lord commanded Moses."

**9:8** And Aaron approached the altar, and slaughtered the calf for the sin offering.

**9:9** And the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he dipped his finger into the blood and put it on the horns of the altar, and poured out the blood at the base of the altar.

**9:10** And the fat, and the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver of the sin offering he offered up on the altar, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**9:11** And the flesh and the hide, he burned them up with fire outside the camp.

**9:12** And he slaughtered the whole burnt offering; and the sons of Aaron also brought the blood to him, and he poured it upon the altar all around.

**9:13** And the whole burnt offering, they brought it in pieces, them and the head, and he placed them on the altar.

**9:14** And he washed the belly and the feet with water, and placed them upon the whole burnt offering on the altar.

**9:15** And they presented the gift of the people; and he took the male goat for the sin offering of the people, and slaughtered it just as the first one.

**9:16** And he presented the whole burnt offering, and offered it as is proper.

**9:17** And he presented the sacrifice, and filled his hands from it, and placed it on the altar besides the morning whole burnt offering.

**9:18** And he slaughtered the young bull and the ram of the sacrifice of deliverance of the people; and the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he poured it against the altar all around.

**9:19** And the fat from the young bull and the ram, the loin, and the fat that covered the belly, and the two kidneys and the fat that was on them, and the lobe that was on the liver;

**9:20** and he placed the fat portions upon the breasts, and they offered up the fat portions on the altar.

**9:21** And the breast and the right shoulder Aaron set apart as a set-apart offering before the Lord, just as the Lord ordered Moses.

**9:22** And lifting up his hands toward the people, Aaron blessed them, and he came down after offering the sin offering, and the whole burnt offerings, and the offerings of deliverance.

**9:23** And Moses and Aaron entered into the tent of testimony, and coming out they blessed all the people; and the glory of the Lord appeared to all the people.

**9:24** And fire came out from the Lord, and consumed the things on the altar, both the whole burnt offerings and the fat portions; and all the people saw and were amazed, and they fell on their faces.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — Greek θυσία σωτηρίου ("sacrifice of deliverance") is the characteristic Septuagint rendering for the Hebrew peace or fellowship offering.
- **9:7** — The Septuagint reads "and your house" (τοῦ οἴκου σου) rather than the Hebrew "and for the people," and includes "to Moses" (τῷ Μωυσῇ) at the end of the verse.
- **9:20** — The Greek reverses the verb numbers of the Hebrew text, reading singular ἐπέθηκεν ("he placed") followed by plural ἀνήνεγκαν ("they offered up").
- **9:24** — Greek ἐξέστη ("were amazed" or "were astonished") expresses a Septuagint reaction of awe rather than the Hebrew "shouted for joy."
