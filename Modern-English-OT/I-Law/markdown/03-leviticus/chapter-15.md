## Text

**15:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'For any man who has a discharge from his body, his discharge is unclean.

**15:3** And this is the law of his uncleanness: when he flows with semen from his body, from the discharge in which his body is obstructed through the discharge, this is his uncleanness in him; all the days of the discharge of his body in which his body is obstructed through the discharge, it is his uncleanness.

**15:4** Every bed on which the one with a discharge lies is unclean, and every object on which the one with a discharge sits shall be unclean.

**15:5** And any person who touches his bed shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:6** And the one who sits on the object on which the one with a discharge has sat shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:7** And the one who touches the skin of the one with a discharge shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:8** And if the one with a discharge spits on one who is clean, then that one shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:9** And every saddle of a donkey on which the one with a discharge rides shall be unclean until evening.

**15:10** And everyone who touches whatever things are beneath him shall be unclean until evening; and the one who carries them shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:11** And everyone whom the one with a discharge touches without having rinsed his hands shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:12** And an earthenware vessel that the one with a discharge touches shall be broken, and a wooden vessel shall be rinsed with water and shall be clean.

**15:13** And if the one with a discharge is cleansed from his discharge, he shall count off for himself seven days for his cleansing, and he shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he shall be clean.

**15:14** And on the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them before the Lord to the doors of the tent of testimony, and give them to the priest.

**15:15** And the priest shall offer them, one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord from his discharge.

**15:16** And a man from whom an emission of semen goes out shall bathe his entire body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:17** And every garment and every hide on which there is an emission of semen shall be washed with water and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:18** And as for a woman, if a man lies with her with an emission of semen, they shall bathe in water and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:19** And a woman who is flowing with blood, her discharge being in her body, shall be in her menstruation for seven days; everyone who touches her shall be unclean until evening,

**15:20** and everything on which she lies during her menstruation shall be unclean, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean.

**15:21** And everyone who touches her bed shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:22** And everyone who touches any object on which she sits shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:23** And if, while she is in her bed or on the object on which she sits, he touches her, it shall be unclean until evening.

**15:24** And if someone indeed lies with her and her uncleanness comes upon him, he shall be unclean seven days; and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

**15:25** And if a woman flows with a discharge of blood for many days not at the time of her menstruation, if she also flows after her menstruation, all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness shall be like the days of menstruation; she shall be unclean.

**15:26** And every bed on which she lies all the days of the discharge shall be to her like the bed of menstruation, and every object on which she sits shall be unclean like the uncleanness of menstruation.

**15:27** Everyone who touches her shall be unclean, and shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

**15:28** And if she is cleansed from the discharge, she shall count off for herself seven days, and afterward she shall be cleansed.

**15:29** And on the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them to the priest to the door of the tent of testimony.

**15:30** And the priest shall offer the one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for her before the Lord from the discharge of her uncleanness.'

**15:31** And you shall make the sons of Israel cautious of their uncleannesses, and they shall not die because of their uncleanness when they defile My tent that is among them.

**15:32** This is the law of the one with a discharge, and if an emission of semen goes out from anyone so as to be defiled by it,

**15:33** and for the woman having a flow of blood in her menstruation, and for the one with a discharge in his discharge, for the male or the female, and for the man who lies with a menstruating woman."

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The Greek uses medical terminology (gonos, "semen/genital fluid," the root of gonorrhues, "the one with a discharge," used throughout the chapter) and synesteken ("is obstructed/congealed"). Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Septuagint omits "or" between the two conditions and adds the closing clause "all the days of the discharge of his body... it is his uncleanness."
- **15:9** — The Septuagint specifies "saddle of a donkey" (episagma onou) and adds "until evening" (heos hesperas), both absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:23** — The Greek syntax is notably difficult: the feminine participle ouses ("while she is") and feminine pronoun autes ("her") refer to the woman rather than to an object on the bed or seat as in the Hebrew, while the Codex Vaticanus text concludes with the neuter akatharton ("it shall be unclean").
- **15:31** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text commands Moses and Aaron to "separate" the Israelites from their uncleanness, the Septuagint renders the verb as eulabeis poiesete ("make cautious" or "circumspect").
