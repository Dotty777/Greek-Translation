## Text

**27:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**27:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Whoever makes a vow as a valuation of his life to the Lord,

**27:3** the valuation of a male from twenty years old to sixty years old shall be—his valuation shall be fifty didrachmas of silver by the holy weight.

**27:4** And the assessment of a female shall be thirty didrachmas.

**27:5** And if it is from five years old to twenty years, the valuation of a male shall be twenty didrachmas, and of a female, fourteen didrachmas.

**27:6** And from one month old to five years old, the valuation of a male shall be five didrachmas of silver, and of a female, three didrachmas.

**27:7** And if it is from sixty years old and above, if it is a male, the valuation shall be fifteen didrachmas of silver, and if a female, ten didrachmas.

**27:8** And if he is too poor for the valuation, he shall stand before the priest, and the priest shall assess him; just as the hand of the one who vowed is able, the priest shall assess him.

**27:9** Now if it is from the animals from which a gift is offered to the Lord, whoever gives from these to the Lord, it shall be holy.

**27:10** He shall not exchange it, good for bad, nor bad for good; and if he actually exchanges it, animal for animal, it and the substitute shall be holy.

**27:11** And if it is any unclean animal from which a gift is not offered to the Lord, he shall set the animal before the priest,

**27:12** and the priest shall assess it between good and bad; and according to whatever the priest assesses, so it shall stand.

**27:13** And if he actually redeems it, he shall add one-fifth to its valuation.

**27:14** And as for a person who consecrates his house as holy to the Lord, the priest shall assess it between good and bad; just as the priest assesses it, so it shall stand.

**27:15** And if the one who consecrated it redeems his house, he shall add to it one-fifth of the silver of the valuation, and it shall be his.

**27:16** Now if a person consecrates to the Lord part of the field of his possession, then the valuation shall be according to its seed: for a kor of barley, fifty didrachmas of silver.

**27:17** And if he consecrates his field from the year of release, it shall stand according to its valuation.

**27:18** And if he consecrates his field later, after the release, the priest shall calculate the silver for him for the remaining years until the year of release, and it shall be deducted from its assessment.

**27:19** And if the one who bought it redeems the field, he shall add one-fifth of the silver to its valuation, and it shall be his.

**27:20** And if he does not redeem the field, and he sells the field to another person, he shall never again redeem it;

**27:21** but the field, when the release has gone out, shall be holy to the Lord, like the land that has been set apart; it shall be a possession for the priest.

**27:22** And if he consecrates to the Lord part of the field that he has acquired, which is not part of the field of his possession,

**27:23** the priest shall calculate for him the total of the valuation from the year of release, and he shall pay the valuation on that day, holy to the Lord;

**27:24** and in the year of release, the field shall be returned to the person from whom he acquired it, to whom the possession of the land belonged.

**27:25** And every valuation shall be by holy weights; the didrachma shall be twenty obols.

**27:26** And every firstborn that is born among your animals shall belong to the Lord, and no one shall consecrate it, whether calf or sheep; it belongs to the Lord.

**27:27** And if it is of the unclean four-footed animals, he shall exchange it according to its valuation and add one-fifth to it, and it shall be his; and if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold according to its assessed value.

**27:28** But as for every devoted thing that a person devotes to the Lord from all that belongs to him, from human to animal and from a field of his possession, he shall not sell or redeem it; every devoted thing shall be most holy to the Lord.

**27:29** And everyone who is devoted from among human beings shall not be redeemed, but shall surely be put to death.

**27:30** Every tithe of the land, from the seed of the land and the fruit of the tree, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord.

**27:31** And if a person indeed redeems his tithe, he shall add one-fifth to it, and it shall be his.

**27:32** And every tithe of cattle and sheep, and everything that comes in the counting under the rod, the tenth one shall be holy to the Lord.

**27:33** You shall not exchange good for bad; and if you actually exchange it, its substitute also shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.'"

**27:34** These are the commandments that the Lord commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **27:5** — The Greek text reads "fourteen didrachmas" for a female aged five to twenty, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and some Greek manuscripts) reads ten.
- **27:17** — Throughout verses 17-24, the Greek renders the jubilee year as "the year of release" (aphesis), emphasizing the remission and return of property.
- **27:19** — The Greek text reads "the one who bought it" (ho agorasas), contrasting with "the one who consecrated it" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **27:23** — The Greek reads "from the year of release" (ek) rather than "until the year of jubilee" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and uses telos ("total" or "full sum") for the calculated valuation.
- **27:25** — Throughout this chapter, the Greek expresses sanctuary weight standards using Greek equivalents: "didrachma" (didrachmon) for the shekel and "obols" (oboloi) for gerahs, alongside "kor" (koros) in verse 16.
