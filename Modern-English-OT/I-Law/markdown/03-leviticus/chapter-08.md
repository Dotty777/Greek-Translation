## Text

**8:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Take Aaron and his sons and his garments, and the anointing oil, and the young bull for the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;

**8:3** and assemble all the congregation at the door of the tent of testimony."

**8:4** And Moses did just as the Lord ordered him, and he assembled the congregation at the door of the tent of testimony.

**8:5** And Moses said to the congregation, "This is the word, this is what the Lord commanded to do."

**8:6** And Moses presented Aaron and his sons, and washed them with water.

**8:7** And he clothed them with the tunic and fastened the sash around them; and he clothed him with the robe and placed the shoulder-garment upon him,

**8:8** and he wrapped him according to the workmanship of the shoulder-garment and fastened him tightly with it; and he placed the oracle upon it, and placed the manifestation and the truth upon the oracle.

**8:9** And he placed the turban upon his head, and he placed upon the turban, in front of his face, the golden plate, dedicated as holy, just as the Lord ordered Moses.

**8:10** And Moses took some of the anointing oil,

**8:11** and he sprinkled some of it upon the altar seven times; and he anointed the altar and sanctified it, and all its utensils, and the basin and its base, and sanctified them; and he anointed the tent and all that was in it, and sanctified it.

**8:12** And Moses poured some of the anointing oil upon the head of Aaron, and he anointed him and sanctified him.

**8:13** And Moses brought forward the sons of Aaron, and clothed them with tunics, and fastened sashes around them, and put headdresses on them, just as the Lord ordered Moses.

**8:14** And Moses brought forward the young bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the young bull of the sin offering.

**8:15** And he slaughtered it; and Moses took some of the blood and put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger, and purified the altar; and he poured out the blood at the base of the altar, and sanctified it to make atonement upon it.

**8:16** And Moses took all the fat that was on the inward parts, and a lobe from the liver, and both kidneys and the fat that was on them, and Moses offered them up on the altar.

**8:17** And the young bull and its hide and its flesh and its dung, he burned them up with fire outside the camp, just as the Lord ordered Moses.

**8:18** And Moses brought forward the ram for a whole burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram. And Moses slaughtered the ram, and Moses poured the blood upon the altar all around.

**8:19** And he cut up the ram into pieces, and Moses offered up the head and the pieces and the fat. And he washed the belly and the feet with water,

**8:20** and Moses offered up the whole ram on the altar; a whole burnt offering that is for an aroma of sweet fragrance, it is a fire offering to the Lord, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:21** And Moses brought forward the second ram, a ram of consecration; and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

**8:22** And he slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and on the tip of his right hand, and on the tip of his right foot.

**8:23** And Moses brought forward the sons of Aaron; and Moses put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, and on the tips of their right hands, and on the tips of their right feet; and Moses poured the blood upon the altar all around.

**8:24** And he took the fat, and the loin, and the fat that was on the belly, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that was on them, and the right shoulder.

**8:25** And from the basket of consecration that was before the Lord he took one unleavened loaf, and one loaf made with oil, and one flat cake, and he placed them on the fat, and he placed the right shoulder.

**8:26** And he placed all of them upon the hands of Aaron and upon the hands of his sons, and he offered them up as a set-apart offering before the Lord.

**8:27** And Moses took them from their hands, and Moses offered them up on the altar, upon the whole burnt offering of consecration, which is an aroma of sweet fragrance; it is a fire offering to the Lord.

**8:28** And taking the breast, Moses set it apart as a placed offering before the Lord from the ram of consecration; and it became a portion for Moses, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**8:29** And Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron, and his garments, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him.

**8:30** And he sanctified Aaron, and his garments, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him.

**8:31** And Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the flesh in the courtyard of the tent of testimony in a holy place, and there you shall eat it and the loaves in the basket of consecration, just as it has been ordered to me, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat these.'

**8:32** And what is left over of the flesh and of the loaves shall be burned up in fire.

**8:33** And you shall not go out from the door of the tent of testimony for seven days, until the day of your consecration is fulfilled; for seven days he will consecrate your hands,

**8:34** just as he did on this day, on which the Lord commanded to do so, in order to make atonement for you.

**8:35** And you shall remain at the door of the tent of testimony for seven days, day and night; you shall keep the observances of the Lord, so that you may not die; for so the Lord God commanded me."

**8:36** And Aaron and his sons did all the words that the Lord ordered Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The Septuagint reads plural αὐτούς ("them") for the tunic and sash before switching to singular αὐτόν ("him") for the robe and shoulder-piece, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the singular throughout.
- **8:8** — The Greek renders the Hebrew priestly vestments as ἐπωμίς ("shoulder-piece," for ephod), λόγιον ("oracle," for breastpiece), and τὴν δήλωσιν καὶ τὴν ἀλήθειαν ("the manifestation and the truth," for Urim and Thummim).
- **8:11** — In the Septuagint order, the anointing of the tent and its contents follows the sprinkling and anointing of the altar and basin at the end of verse 11 rather than appearing in verse 10 as in the Hebrew text.
- **8:18** — In this Greek text, verse 18 incorporates the material traditionally numbered as verse 19 ("And Moses slaughtered the ram, and Moses poured the blood upon the altar all around").
