## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**13:2** "If a bright scar of a mark occurs on any person in the skin of his flesh, and an affliction of leprosy occurs in the skin of his flesh, then he shall be brought to Aaron the priest or one of his sons, the priests.

**13:3** And the priest shall see the affliction in the skin of his flesh, and if the hair in the affliction has turned white and the appearance of the affliction is lower than the skin of the flesh, it is an affliction of leprosy; and the priest shall see and declare him unclean.

**13:4** But if a bright white spot is in the skin of its flesh, and its appearance is not lower than its skin, and its hair has not turned to white hair, and it is dim, then the priest shall isolate the affliction for seven days.

**13:5** And the priest shall see the affliction on the seventh day, and look, the affliction remains before him; the affliction has not changed in the skin; then the priest shall isolate him for seven days a second time.

**13:6** And the priest shall see him on the seventh day a second time, and look, the affliction is dim; the affliction has not changed in the skin; the priest shall declare him clean, for it is a mark; and after washing his garments, he shall be clean.

**13:7** But if the mark turns and changes in the skin after the priest has seen him to declare him clean, then he shall appear before the priest a second time.

**13:8** And the priest shall see him, and look, the mark has changed in the skin; then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:9** And if an affliction of leprosy occurs on a person, then he shall come to the priest.

**13:10** And the priest shall see, and look, there is a white scar in the skin, and it has turned hair white, and some of the healthy part of the living flesh is in the scar,

**13:11** it is long-standing leprosy; it is in the skin of the flesh; and the priest shall declare him unclean and isolate him, because he is unclean.

**13:12** But if the leprosy breaks out thoroughly in the skin, and the leprosy covers all the skin of the affliction from head to feet throughout the entire sight of the priest,

**13:13** then the priest shall see, and look, the leprosy has covered all the skin of the flesh; and the priest shall declare him clean of the affliction; because it has all turned white, it is clean.

**13:14** And on whatever day living flesh appears on him, he shall be defiled.

**13:15** And the priest shall see the healthy flesh, and the healthy flesh shall defile him, because it is unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:16** But if the healthy flesh is restored and turns white, then he shall come to the priest.

**13:17** And the priest shall see, and look, the affliction has turned to white; then the priest shall declare the affliction clean; he is clean.

**13:18** And as for flesh, if a sore occurs in its skin, and it is healed,

**13:19** and in the place of the sore there occurs a white scar or a bright spot that is whitish or reddish, then he shall appear before the priest.

**13:20** And the priest shall see, and look, the appearance is lower than the skin, and its hair has turned to white; then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:21** But if the priest sees, and look, there is no white hair in it, and it is not lower than the skin of the flesh, and it is dim, the priest shall isolate him for seven days.

**13:22** And if it spreads in the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction of leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:23** But if the bright spot remains in its place and does not spread, it is a scar of the sore, and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:24** And as for flesh, if a burn from fire occurs in its skin, and in its skin the healed part of the burn becomes a bright white shining spot, somewhat reddish or very white,

**13:25** then the priest shall see him, and look, hair has turned white in the shining spot, and its appearance is lower than the skin; it is leprosy; it has broken out in the burn. And the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction of leprosy.

**13:26** But if the priest sees, and look, there is no white hair in the shining spot, and it is not lower than the skin, and it is dim, then the priest shall isolate him for seven days.

**13:27** And the priest shall see him on the seventh day; and if it spreads widely in the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction of leprosy; it has broken out in the sore.

**13:28** But if the shining spot remains in its place and does not spread in the skin, and it is dim, it is the scar of the burn, and the priest shall declare him clean, for it is the imprint of the burn.

**13:29** And as for a man and a woman, if an affliction of leprosy occurs on them on the head or on the beard,

**13:30** then the priest shall see the affliction, and look, its appearance is more sunken than the skin, and in it there is thin yellowish hair; then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a scab; it is leprosy of the head or leprosy of the beard.

**13:31** And if the priest sees the affliction of the wound, and look, the appearance is not more sunken than the skin, and there is no yellowish hair in it, then the priest shall isolate the affliction of the scab for seven days.

**13:32** And the priest shall see the affliction on the seventh day, and look, the scab has not spread, and there is no yellowish hair in it, and the appearance of the scab is not sunken below the skin,

**13:33** then the skin shall be shaved, but the scab shall not be shaved; and the priest shall isolate the scab for seven days a second time.

**13:34** And the priest shall see the scab on the seventh day, and look, the scab has not spread in the skin after he has been shaved, and the appearance of the scab is not sunken below the skin; then the priest shall declare him clean; and after washing his garments, he shall be clean.

**13:35** But if the scab spreads widely in the skin after he has been declared clean,

**13:36** then the priest shall see, and look, the scab has spread in the skin; the priest shall not inspect for the yellow hair, because he is unclean.

**13:37** But if the scab remains in view in its place and black hair has sprung up in it, the scab has healed; it is clean, and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:38** And as for a man or a woman, if a gleaming spot occurs in the skin of his flesh, shining whitish spots,

**13:39** then the priest shall see, and look, in the skin of his flesh there are gleaming spots, shining and whitish; it is a white rash, he is clean; it will break out in the skin of his flesh, he is clean.

**13:40** And if anyone's head loses its hair, he is bald; he is clean.

**13:41** And if his head loses its hair at the front, he is bald on the forehead; he is clean.

**13:42** But if a white or reddish affliction occurs on his bald head or on his bald forehead, it is leprosy on his bald head or on his bald forehead.

**13:43** And the priest shall see him, and look, the appearance of the affliction is reddish-white on his bald head or on his bald forehead, like the form of leprosy in the skin of his flesh,

**13:44** he is a leprous person; the priest shall surely declare him unclean; his affliction is on his head.

**13:45** And as for the leper on whom the affliction is, let his garments be loosened and his head uncovered, and let him cover around his mouth, and he shall be called unclean.

**13:46** All the days that the affliction is on him, being unclean, he shall be unclean; he shall dwell separated; his dwelling shall be outside the camp.

**13:47** And as for a garment, if an affliction of leprosy occurs in it, in a woolen garment or in a flax garment,

**13:48** or in warp or in weft, or in linen or in wool, or in leather or in any worked leather,

**13:49** and the affliction is greenish or reddish in the leather or in the garment or in the warp or in the weft or in any worked article of leather, it is an affliction of leprosy, and he shall show it to the priest.

**13:50** And the priest shall see the affliction, and the priest shall isolate the affliction for seven days.

**13:51** And the priest shall see the affliction on the seventh day; and if the affliction spreads in the garment or in the warp or in the weft or in the leather, according to all leather things that may be made in the work, the affliction is a persistent leprosy; it is unclean.

**13:52** He shall burn up the garment or the warp or the weft, in wool or in linen or in any leather article in which the affliction is; because it is leprosy, it shall be burned up in fire.

**13:53** But if the priest sees, and the affliction does not spread in the garment or in the warp or in the weft or in any leather article,

**13:54** then the priest shall command, and he shall wash that on which the affliction is, and the priest shall isolate the affliction for seven days a second time.

**13:55** And the priest shall see the affliction after it has been washed, and if this affliction has not changed its appearance, and the affliction does not spread, it is unclean; it shall be burned up in fire; it is firmly set in the garment or in the warp or in the weft.

**13:56** And if the priest sees, and the affliction is dim after it has been washed, he shall tear it away from the garment or from the leather or from the warp or from the weft.

**13:57** But if it still appears in the garment or in the warp or in the weft or in any leather article, it is leprosy breaking out; that in which the affliction is shall be burned up in fire.

**13:58** And as for the garment or the warp or the weft or any leather article that is washed and from which the affliction departs, it shall be washed a second time; it shall be clean.

**13:59** This is the law of an affliction of leprosy in a woolen or flax garment, or warp or weft, or any leather article, to declare it clean or to declare it unclean."

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Greek "a bright scar of a mark" forms a single phrase rather than the three alternatives ("swelling, eruption, or bright spot") of the Hebrew text. Throughout this chapter, Greek "leprosy" (lepra) covers a range of scaly or discoloring skin conditions rather than solely modern Hansen's disease.
- **13:4** — The Greek text uses the feminine pronoun ("its" or "her") for "its flesh" and "its skin" (matching the feminine gender of "bright spot" or "affliction") and includes the clause "and it is dim," which is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew here.
- **13:11** — The Greek text reads positively "and isolate him," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "he shall not shut him up."
- **13:15** — The Greek renders the Hebrew for "raw/living flesh" as "healthy flesh" (also in vv. 10, 16) and takes "the healthy flesh" as the subject of "defile," where the Masoretic Hebrew divides the clauses with the priest as subject.
- **13:27** — Although vv. 24-28 concern burns, the Greek text includes the clause "it has broken out in the sore" (echoing v. 22), which is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew here.
- **13:31** — The Greek text reads "of the wound" (traumatos) in the first clause before "of the scab" (thrausmatos) in the second, and reads "yellowish hair" where the Masoretic Hebrew has "black hair."
- **13:39** — Greek alphos refers to a benign dull-white skin rash; the Greek text also uses the future tense "it will break out" and repeats "he is clean."
- **13:45** — The Greek text reads "loosened" (not "torn"), "head uncovered" (not "hair disheveled"), "around his mouth" (not "upper lip"), and passive "he shall be called unclean" where the Masoretic Hebrew reads active "he shall cry out, 'Unclean, unclean!'"
- **13:55** — The Greek text reads "it is firmly set in the garment or in the warp or in the weft," diverging from the Masoretic Hebrew ("it is a corrosion, whether on the back or on the front of it").
