## Text

**24:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and let them take for Me pure beaten olive oil for light, to burn a lamp continually

**24:3** outside the curtain in the tent of testimony; Aaron and his sons shall burn it from evening until morning in the presence of the Lord constantly; it is an eternal statute throughout your generations.

**24:4** Upon the pure lampstand you shall burn the lamps before the Lord until the morning.

**24:5** And you shall take fine flour and make it into twelve loaves; each loaf shall be of two-tenths.

**24:6** And you shall place them in two stacks, six loaves in each stack, upon the pure table before the Lord.

**24:7** And you shall place pure frankincense and salt upon the stack, and they shall be for the loaves as a memorial, set forth to the Lord.

**24:8** On the Sabbath day it shall be set forth before the Lord continually, in the presence of the sons of Israel, as an eternal covenant.

**24:9** And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, and they shall eat them in a holy place; for this is holy of holies, theirs from the things sacrificed to the Lord, an eternal statute."

**24:10** And a son of an Israelite woman went out, and he was the son of an Egyptian among the sons of Israel. And the one from the Israelite woman and the Israelite man fought in the camp,

**24:11** and the son of the Israelite woman, having named the Name, cursed. And they brought them to Moses; and the name of his mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**24:12** And they placed him in custody, to judge him by the command of the Lord.

**24:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:14** "Bring the one who cursed outside the camp, and all who heard shall lay their hands upon his head, and the whole congregation shall stone him.

**24:15** And speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'Any person who curses God shall bear sin,

**24:16** and the one who names the name of the Lord shall surely be put to death; the whole congregation of Israel shall stone him with stones; whether resident alien or native, when he names the name of the Lord, let him die.

**24:17** And a person who strikes the life of a person, and he dies, shall surely be put to death.

**24:18** And whoever strikes an animal, and it dies, shall pay life for life.

**24:19** And if anyone inflicts a blemish on a neighbor, just as he did to him, in the same way it shall be done in return to him:

**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; just as he inflicts a blemish on the person, so it shall be inflicted on him.

**24:21** Whoever strikes a person, and he dies, shall surely be put to death.

**24:22** There shall be one judgment for the resident alien and for the native of the land, for I am the Lord your God.'"

**24:23** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they brought the one who cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones; and the sons of Israel did just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The Greek explicitly adds "and salt" (καὶ ἅλα) alongside pure frankincense on the stack of loaves, and describes them as "set forth" (προκείμενα) rather than as an offering by fire as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **24:11** — The Greek renders the offense as "having named the Name" (ἐπονομάσας ... τὸ ὄνομα) and cursing rather than "blasphemed" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; it also has plural "they brought them" (αὐτούς) to Moses.
- **24:16** — As in 24:11, the Greek forbids "naming" (ὀνομάζων / ὀνομάσαι) the name of the Lord rather than "blaspheming" it as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and explicitly adds "of Israel" and "of the Lord."
- **24:21** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek omits the opening clause regarding restitution for killing an animal (already stated in 24:18) and retains only the clause on striking a person.
