## Text

**22:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:2** "Tell Aaron and his sons, and let them be careful regarding the holy things of the sons of Israel, whatever they themselves sanctify to Me, and they shall not profane My holy name; I am the Lord.

**22:3** Tell them, 'Throughout your generations, any man from your offspring who approaches the holy things, whatever the sons of Israel sanctify to the Lord, while his uncleanness is upon him, that person shall be utterly destroyed from Me; I am the Lord your God.

**22:4** And any man from the offspring of Aaron the priest who has leprosy or a discharge shall not eat of the holy things until he is cleansed; and whoever touches any uncleanness from a dead person, or a man from whom an emission of semen goes out,

**22:5** whoever touches any unclean creeping thing that will defile him, or a person by whom he will defile himself according to all his uncleanness,

**22:6** the person who touches them shall be unclean until evening; he shall not eat from the holy things unless he bathes his body in water

**22:7** and the sun sets, and he shall be clean; and then he may eat of the holy things, because it is his bread.

**22:8** He shall not eat what has died of itself or what has been seized by wild beasts, so as to be defiled by them; I am the Lord.

**22:9** And they shall keep My charges, so that they do not bear sin because of them and die because of it; and if they profane them, I am the Lord God who sanctifies them.

**22:10** And no outsider shall eat holy things; a temporary resident of a priest or a hired worker shall not eat holy things.

**22:11** But if a priest acquires a person purchased with silver, he may eat from his loaves; and those born in his house, they too may eat of his loaves.

**22:12** And if a daughter of a man who is a priest marries a man who is an outsider, she shall not eat of the firstfruits of the sanctuary.

**22:13** And if a daughter of a priest becomes a widow cast out, and she had no offspring, she shall return to her paternal house as in her youth; she may eat from the loaves of her father. And no outsider shall eat from them.

**22:14** And if a man eats holy things in ignorance, he shall add its fifth part to it and shall give the holy thing to the priest.

**22:15** And they shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel, which they set apart to the Lord,

**22:16** and bring upon themselves iniquity of trespass when they eat their holy things; because I am the Lord who sanctifies them.'"

**22:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:18** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to the entire congregation of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Any man from the sons of Israel or from the sons of the proselytes who are joined to them in Israel who offers his gifts according to any pledge of theirs or according to any freewill choice of theirs, whatever they offer to God for a whole burnt offering,

**22:19** they shall be acceptable for you, unblemished males from the cattle and from the sheep and from the goats.

**22:20** Anything that has a blemish in it they shall not present to the Lord, because it will not be acceptable for you.

**22:21** And if a man offers a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord, specifying a vow according to freewill choice or at your feasts, from the cattle or from the sheep, it shall be unblemished to be acceptable; there shall be no blemish at all in it.

**22:22** One that is blind, or fractured, or with a cut tongue, or having warts, or having a malignant scab, or having scaly eruptions—these they shall not present to the Lord, and you shall not give any of them for a burnt offering upon the altar to the Lord.

**22:23** And a calf or a sheep with a cut ear or with a docked tail, you may make them animals for slaughter for yourself, but for your vow it shall not be accepted.

**22:24** One with crushed testicles, and one squeezed, and one castrated, and one torn—you shall not present them to the Lord, and in your land you shall not do so.

**22:25** And from the hand of an outsider you shall not offer the gifts of your God from any of these, because defects are in them, a blemish is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.'"

**22:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:27** "A calf or a sheep or a goat, when it is born, shall be seven days under its mother; but on the eighth day and beyond, it shall be accepted for gifts, a burnt offering to the Lord.

**22:28** And a calf or a sheep, her and her young ones, you shall not slaughter in one day.

**22:29** And if you sacrifice a sacrifice, a vow of joy, to the Lord, you shall sacrifice it to be acceptable for you;

**22:30** on that very day it shall be eaten; you shall not leave any of the meat until morning; I am the Lord.

**22:31** And you shall keep My commandments and do them.

**22:32** And you shall not profane the name of the Holy One, and I will be sanctified in the midst of the sons of Israel; I am the Lord who sanctifies you,

**22:33** who brought you out of the land of Egypt so as to be your God; I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — The Greek reads ean de bebēlōsousin auta ("and if they profane them") as a separate conditional clause and adds ho theos ("God") in "the Lord God who sanctifies them."
- **22:13** — The Greek reads chēra ekbeblēmenē ("a widow cast out") without a conjunction ("or"), differing from the Hebrew "a widow or a divorced woman."
- **22:23** — The Greek specifies ōtotmēton ("with a cut ear") and kolobokerkon ("with a docked tail") and permits making them sphagia ... seautō ("animals for slaughter for yourself"), whereas the Hebrew permits offering such an animal as a freewill offering.
- **22:29** — The Greek adds euchēn ("a vow") and translates the Hebrew "thanksgiving" with charmosynēs ("of joy").
- **22:32** — The Greek reads to onoma tou hagiou ("the name of the Holy One") rather than "My holy name" (cf. 22:2).
