## Text

**22:1** And the sons of Israel set out and encamped on the west of Moab, beside the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**22:2** And Balak son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites,

**22:3** and Moab feared the people greatly, because they were many; and Moab was filled with loathing at the presence of the sons of Israel.

**22:4** And Moab said to the council of elders of Midian, "Now this assembly will lick up all those around us, just as a calf licks up the greenery from the field." Now Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.

**22:5** So he sent envoys to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, which is by the river of the land of the sons of his people, to summon him, saying, "Look, a people has come out from Egypt, and see, it has covered the face of the land, and it is settled right next to me.

**22:6** And now come, curse this people for me, because it is stronger than we are, if we may be able to strike some of them, and I will drive them out of the land; for I know that those whom you bless are blessed, and those whom you curse are cursed."

**22:7** And the council of elders of Moab and the council of elders of Midian went, with the divinations in their hands; and they came to Balaam and spoke to him the words of Balak.

**22:8** And he said to them, "Stay here for the night, and I will answer you with the matters that the Lord speaks to me." So the officials of Moab stayed with Balaam.

**22:9** And God came to Balaam and said to him, "Why are these men with you?"

**22:10** And Balaam said to God, "Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, sent them to me, saying,

**22:11** 'Look, a people has come out from Egypt, and see, it has covered the face of the land, and it is settled right next to me; and now come, curse it for me, if perhaps I will be able to strike it and drive it out from the land.'"

**22:12** And God said to Balaam, "You shall not go with them, nor shall you curse the people, for it is blessed."

**22:13** And Balaam got up in the morning and said to the officials of Balak, "Hurry back to your lord; God does not permit me to go with you."

**22:14** And the officials of Moab got up and came to Balak and said, "Balaam is not willing to go with us."

**22:15** And Balak again sent officials, more numerous and more honorable than these.

**22:16** And they came to Balaam and said to him, "Thus says Balak son of Zippor: 'I urge you, do not hesitate to come to me,

**22:17** for I will honor you with great honor, and whatever you say I will do for you; and come, pronounce a curse on this people for me.'"

**22:18** And Balaam answered and said to the officials of Balak, "If Balak should give me his house full of silver and gold, I will not be able to transgress the word of the Lord God, to make it small or great in my mind.

**22:19** And now you also stay here this night, and I will know what more the Lord will speak to me."

**22:20** And God came to Balaam and said to him, "If these men are here to summon you, get up and follow them; yet the word that I speak to you, this you shall do."

**22:21** And Balaam got up in the morning, saddled his donkey, and went with the officials of Moab.

**22:22** And God became furious with anger because he went, and the angel of God stood up to oppose him; and he himself was riding on his donkey, and two of his servants were with him.

**22:23** And when the donkey saw the angel of God standing in opposition on the road, with the sword drawn in his hand, the donkey turned aside from her road and went into the field; and he struck the donkey with the staff to set her straight on the road.

**22:24** And the angel of God stood in the furrows of the vineyards, a fence on this side and a fence on that side;

**22:25** and when the donkey saw the angel of God, she pressed him against the wall and crushed Balaam's foot, and he whipped her once again.

**22:26** And the angel of God went on further and took his stand in a narrow place, where there was no room to turn aside to the right or to the left.

**22:27** And when the donkey saw the angel of God, she sank down underneath Balaam; and Balaam became enraged and kept beating the donkey with the staff.

**22:28** And God opened the mouth of the donkey, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you, that you have struck me this third time?"

**22:29** And Balaam said to the donkey, "Because you have mocked me; and if I had a sword in my hand, I would already have run you through."

**22:30** And the donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey, on which you have ridden from your youth until this day? Have I, in utter disregard, done this to you?" And he said, "No."

**22:31** Then God uncovered the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the angel of the Lord standing in opposition on the road, with the sword drawn in his hand; and bowing low, he prostrated himself on his face.

**22:32** And the angel of God said to him, "Why have you struck your donkey this third time? And look, I came out to oppose you, because your way is not pleasing before me. And when the donkey saw me, she turned aside from me this third time;

**22:33** and if she had not turned aside, then by now I would have killed you, yet I would have spared her."

**22:34** And Balaam said to the angel of the Lord, "I have sinned, for I did not know that you were standing in opposition to me on the road to meet me; and now, if it does not please you, I will turn back."

**22:35** And the angel of God said to Balaam, "Go with the men; only the word that I speak to you, this you shall be careful to speak." So Balaam went with the officials of Balak.

**22:36** And when Balak heard that Balaam had arrived, he went out to meet him at a city of Moab, which is on the borders of the Arnon, which is at the edge of the borders.

**22:37** And Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not send to you to summon you? Why did you not come to me? Will I not truly be able to honor you?"

**22:38** And Balaam said to Balak, "Look, I have come to you now; will I be able to speak anything? The word that God puts into my mouth, this I will speak."

**22:39** And Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Cities of Villages.

**22:40** And Balak sacrificed sheep and calves, and sent to Balaam and to the officials who were with him.

**22:41** And morning came, and Balak took Balaam and brought him up to the pillar of Baal, and from there he showed him a part of the people.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — The Greek text reads "on the west of Moab" (epi dysmōn Mōab), reflecting the Hebrew consonantal root for "west/evening" rather than the Masoretic Text's "plains of Moab."
- **22:25** — Unlike the Masoretic Text ("she pressed herself against the wall"), the Greek text uses the masculine pronoun auton ("him"), indicating that the donkey pressed Balaam himself against the wall.
- **22:30** — The Greek text reads "from your youth" (apo neotētos sou) and "in utter disregard" (hyperorasei hyperidousa), where the Masoretic Text has "all your life" and "been in the habit of."
- **22:39** — Greek Poleis epauleōn ("Cities of Villages") translates the Hebrew place name Kiriath-huzoth literally rather than transliterating it.
- **22:41** — The Greek text reads "the pillar of Baal" (tēn stēlēn tou Baal) and "he showed him" (edeixen autō), where the Masoretic Text has "Bamoth-baal" ("high places of Baal") and "he saw."
