## Text

**21:1** And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who lived along the wilderness, heard—for Israel had come by the way of Atharim—and he fought against Israel, and they carried off captives from among them.

**21:2** And Israel vowed a vow to the Lord and said, "If You deliver this people under my hand, I will devote them and their cities to destruction."

**21:3** And the Lord listened to the voice of Israel and delivered the Canaanite under his hand; and he devoted him and his cities to destruction, and they called the name of that place Anathema.

**21:4** And setting out from Mount Hor by the way to the Red Sea, they went around the land of Edom; and the people lost heart on the way.

**21:5** And the people spoke against God and against Moses, saying, "Why is it that you have brought us out of Egypt to kill us in the wilderness? For there is no bread and no water, and our soul loathes the empty bread."

**21:6** And the Lord sent the deadly serpents among the people, and they bit the people; and many people of the sons of Israel died.

**21:7** And the people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned, because we spoke against the Lord and against you; pray, therefore, to the Lord, and let Him take away the serpent from us."

**21:8** And Moses prayed to the Lord for the people. And the Lord said to Moses, "Make for yourself a serpent, and set it on a standard; and it will be that if a serpent bites a person, everyone who has been bitten, when he sees it, will live."

**21:9** And Moses made a bronze serpent, and set it up on a standard; and it happened that whenever a serpent bit a person, and he looked upon the bronze serpent, he lived.

**21:10** And the sons of Israel set out and camped in Oboth.

**21:11** And setting out from Oboth, they camped in Chalgai on the far side, in the wilderness that faces Moab toward the rising of the sun.

**21:12** From there they set out and camped in the valley of Zered.

**21:13** Setting out from there, they camped on the other side of the Arnon in the wilderness, which extends from the borders of the Amorites; for the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorite.

**21:14** Therefore it is said in the book War of the Lord: "He set Zoob ablaze and the streams of the Arnon;

**21:15** and He appointed the streams to settle Ar, and it adjoins the borders of Moab."

**21:16** And from there, the well; this is the well about which the Lord said to Moses, "Gather the people, and I will give them water to drink."

**21:17** Then Israel sang this song over the well: "Strike up the song to it:

**21:18** A well—princes dug it, kings of nations hewed it out, in their kingdom, when they ruled over them." And from the well to Manthanain;

**21:19** and from Manthanain to Manael; and from Manael to Bamoth; and from Bamoth to Ianen, which is in the plain of Moab, from the top of the hewn rock, which looks toward the face of the wilderness.

**21:20** And Moses sent envoys to Sihon king of the Amorites with words of peace, saying,

**21:21** And Moses sent envoys to Sihon king of the Amorites with words of peace, saying, "We will pass through your land; we will travel along the road; we will not turn aside either into a field or into a vineyard,

**21:22** we will not drink water from your well; we will travel by the royal road until we pass through your borders."

**21:23** And Sihon did not allow Israel to pass through his borders; and Sihon gathered all his people, and went out to engage Israel in battle in the wilderness; and he came to Jahaz and engaged Israel in battle.

**21:24** And Israel struck him with the slaughter of the sword, and they gained mastery over his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the sons of Ammon; for Jazer is the border of the sons of Ammon.

**21:25** And Israel took all these cities; and Israel settled in all the cities of the Amorites, in Heshbon and in all those adjoining it.

**21:26** For Heshbon is the city of Sihon the king of the Amorites; and he had fought against the king of Moab previously, and they took all his land from Aroer to the Arnon.

**21:27** Therefore the riddle-makers will say, "Come to Heshbon, that the city of Sihon may be built and prepared;

**21:28** for fire went out from Heshbon, a flame from the city of Sihon; and it devoured as far as Moab, and swallowed up the pillars of the Arnon.

**21:29** Woe to you, Moab! You have perished, people of Chemosh! Their sons were given over to escape safely, and their daughters as captives, to the king of the Amorites, Sihon.

**21:30** And their offspring will perish, Heshbon as far as Dibon, and the women still further kindled fire upon Moab."

**21:31** Now Israel settled in all the cities of the Amorites.

**21:32** And Moses sent to spy out Jazer, and they captured it and its villages, and drove out the Amorite who lived there.

**21:33** And turning, they went up by the way to Bashan; and Og king of Bashan came out to meet them, and all his people, for battle at Edrei.

**21:34** And the Lord said to Moses, "Do not fear him, for into your hands I have delivered him and all his people and all his land; and you shall do to him just as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."

**21:35** And he struck him and his sons and all his people, until he left no survivor of his; and they inherited their land.

## Translator Notes

- **21:3** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew place name Hormah into Greek as Anathema ("devoted to destruction" or "accursed"), preserving the wordplay with the preceding verb.
- **21:11** — Greek "Chalgai on the far side" (Chalgai ek tou peran) is a distinct Septuagint form corresponding to Hebrew Iye-abarim.
- **21:14** — In this citation of the singular "War of the Lord" (Hebrew "Wars of the Lord"), the Greek reads the verb "He set ablaze" with the distinct place name Zoob, where the Hebrew has the obscure phrase "Waheb in Suphah."
- **21:19** — This verse gives the whole itinerary and preserves the distinct Greek forms Manthanain (Hebrew Mattanah), Manael (Hebrew Nahaliel), and Ianen (a proper name where the Hebrew has 'the valley'), and reads 'the hewn rock' (τοῦ λελαξευμένου) where the Hebrew has Pisgah.
- **21:20** — The Saints Greek Critical Text has this clause here and again at the opening of verse 21; both are translated as they stand.
- **21:24** — The Septuagint reads the place name "Jazer" where the Hebrew reads "strong" ("for the border of the sons of Ammon was strong").
- **21:30** — The Greek diverges markedly from the Hebrew ("we have shot at them... to Nophah, which reaches to Medeba"), reading "their offspring will perish" and "the women still further kindled fire upon Moab."
