## Text

**30:1** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded Moses.

**30:2** And Moses spoke to the leaders of the tribes of Israel, saying, "This is the word that the Lord has prescribed:

**30:3** Any man who vows a vow to the Lord, or swears an oath with an obligation, or imposes an obligation concerning his soul shall not profane his word; he shall do all that goes out from his mouth.

**30:4** Now if a woman vows a vow to the Lord, or imposes an obligation in her father's house in her youth,

**30:5** and her father hears her vows and her obligations that she imposed upon her soul, and her father keeps silent toward her, then all her vows shall stand, and all the obligations that she imposed upon her soul shall remain upon her.

**30:6** But if her father firmly disapproves on the day he hears all her vows and the obligations that she imposed upon her soul, they shall not stand; and the Lord will cleanse her, because her father disapproved.

**30:7** And if she indeed marries a husband, and her vows are upon her according to the utterance of her lips, which she imposed upon her soul,

**30:8** and her husband hears and keeps silent toward her on the day he hears, then so all her vows shall stand, and her obligations that she imposed upon her soul shall stand.

**30:9** But if her husband firmly disapproves on the day he hears, all her vows and her obligations that she imposed upon her soul shall not remain, because her husband disapproved of her; and the Lord will cleanse her.

**30:10** And the vow of a widow and of a divorced woman, all that she vows upon her soul, shall remain upon her.

**30:11** And if her vow or the obligation upon her soul with an oath is in her husband's house,

**30:12** and her husband hears, and keeps silent toward her and does not disapprove of her, then all her vows shall stand, and all her obligations that she imposed upon her soul shall stand against her.

**30:13** But if her husband completely removes them on the day he hears, all that goes out from her lips concerning her vows and concerning the obligations upon her soul shall not remain upon her; her husband has removed them, and the Lord will cleanse her.

**30:14** Every vow and every binding oath to afflict a soul, her husband may establish it for her, and her husband may remove it.

**30:15** But if he keeps completely silent toward her from day to day, then he shall establish for her all her vows, and he shall establish for her the obligations that are upon her, because he kept silent toward her on the day he heard.

**30:16** But if he completely removes them from her after the day he heard, then he shall bear his sin."

**30:17** These are the ordinances that the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, and between a father and a daughter in youth in a father's house.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — Following Septuagint and Hebrew versification, chapter 30 begins here (29:40 in an alternative versification), shifting the chapter's verse numbers ahead by one.
- **30:6** — Greek kathariei ("will cleanse," also in vv. 9, 13) declares the woman clear of guilt, where the Hebrew text reads "will forgive."
- **30:7** — Greek kata ten diastolen ton cheileon autes ("according to the utterance of her lips") links to the vows as a qualifying phrase rather than the Hebrew alternative "or the rash utterance of her lips."
- **30:13** — Greek periaireo (literally "remove" or "take away," also in vv. 14, 16) denotes canceling or annulling the binding force of a vow.
- **30:16** — The Greek text reads "his sin" (ten hamartian autou), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "her iniquity."
