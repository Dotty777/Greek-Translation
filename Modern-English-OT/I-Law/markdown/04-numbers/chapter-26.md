## Text

**26:1** And it happened after the plague that the Lord spoke to Moses and to Eleazar the priest, saying,

**26:2** "Take the count of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, according to their fathers' houses, everyone who goes out to draw up for battle in Israel."

**26:3** And Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke in Araboth of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,

**26:4** "From twenty years old and upward, just as the Lord commanded Moses, you and the sons of Israel who came out of Egypt."

**26:5** Reuben was the firstborn of Israel. Now the sons of Reuben were: Hanoch, and the clan of Hanoch; of Pallu, the clan of the Palluites;

**26:6** of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Carmi, the clan of the Carmites.

**26:7** These are the clans of Reuben; and their numbering was forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**26:8** And the sons of Pallu: Eliab.

**26:9** And the sons of Eliab: Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. These were the summoned men of the congregation; these are the ones who rose up against Moses and Aaron in the congregation of Korah, in the uprising against the Lord.

**26:10** And the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and Korah in his congregation, when the fire devoured the two hundred and fifty, and they became a sign.

**26:11** However, the sons of Korah did not die.

**26:12** And the sons of Simeon, the clan of the sons of Simeon: of Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; of Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; of Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites;

**26:13** of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; of Shaul, the clan of the Shaulites.

**26:14** These are the clans of Simeon from their numbering, twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**26:15** Now the sons of Judah were Er and Onan; and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

**26:16** And the sons of Judah according to their clans were: of Shelah, the clan of the Shelanites; of Perez, the clan of Perez; of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites.

**26:17** And the sons of Perez were: of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Jamun, the clan of the Jamunites.

**26:18** These are the clans of Judah according to their registration, seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**26:19** And the sons of Issachar according to their clans: of Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; of Puvah, the clan of the Puvaites;

**26:20** of Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; of Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites.

**26:21** These are the clans of Issachar from their numbering, sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26:22** The sons of Zebulun according to their clans: of Sered, the clan of the Seredites; of Elon, the clan of the Elonites; of Allel, the clan of the Allelites.

**26:23** These are the clans of Zebulun from their numbering, sixty thousand five hundred.

**26:24** The sons of Gad according to their clans: of Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; of Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; of Shuni, the clan of the Shunites;

**26:25** of Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; of Addi, the clan of the Addites;

**26:26** of Arodi, the clan of the Arodites; of Ariel, the clan of the Arielites.

**26:27** These are the clans of the sons of Gad from their numbering, forty-four thousand five hundred.

**26:28** The sons of Asher according to their clans: of Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; of Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; of Beriah, the clan of the Beriites.

**26:29** Of Heber, the clan of the Heberites; of Melliel, the clan of the Mellielites.

**26:30** And the name of the daughter of Asher was Kara.

**26:31** These are the clans of Asher from their numbering, forty-three thousand six hundred.

**26:32** The sons of Joseph according to their clans: Manasseh and Ephraim.

**26:33** The sons of Manasseh: of Machir, the clan of the Machirites; and Machir fathered Gilead; of Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites.

**26:34** And these were the sons of Gilead: Ahiezer, the clan of the Ahiezerites; of Helek, the clan of the Helekites;

**26:35** of Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; of Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites;

**26:36** of Symaer, the clan of the Symaerites; and Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.

**26:37** And Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, but only daughters; and these were the names of the daughters of Zelophehad: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**26:38** These are the clans of Manasseh from their numbering, fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**26:39** And these were the sons of Ephraim: of Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; of Tanach, the clan of the Tanachites.

**26:40** These were the sons of Shuthelah: of Eden, the clan of the Edenites.

**26:41** These are the clans of Ephraim from their numbering, thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the clans of the sons of Joseph according to their clans.

**26:42** The sons of Benjamin according to their clans: of Bela, the clan of the Belaites; of Asyber, the clan of the Asyberites; of Jachiran, the clan of the Jachiranites;

**26:43** of Sophan, the clan of the Sophanites.

**26:44** And the sons of Bela were Adar and Naamani.

**26:45** These are the sons of Benjamin according to their clans from their numbering, thirty-five thousand five hundred.

**26:46** And the sons of Dan according to their clans: of Sami, the clan of the Samites. These are the clans of Dan according to their clans.

**26:47** All the clans of Sami according to their registration were sixty-four thousand six hundred.

**26:48** The sons of Naphtali according to their clans: of Sael, the clan of the Saelites; of Guni, the clan of the Gunites;

**26:49** of Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; of Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites.

**26:50** These are the clans of Naphtali from their numbering, thirty thousand three hundred.

**26:51** This is the numbering of the sons of Israel, six hundred one thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**26:52** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**26:53** "To these the land shall be divided to inherit by the number of names;

**26:54** to the more numerous you shall increase the inheritance, and to the fewer you shall decrease their inheritance; to each one, just as they were numbered, their inheritance shall be given.

**26:55** Through lots the land shall be divided by the names; according to the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit;

**26:56** by the lot you shall divide their inheritance between many and few."

**26:57** And the sons of Levi according to their clans: of Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; of Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; of Merari, the clan of the Merarites.

**26:58** These are the clans of the sons of Levi: the clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of Korah, and the clan of the Mushites. And Kohath fathered Amram.

**26:59** And the name of his wife was Jochebed, daughter of Levi, who bore these to Levi in Egypt; and she bore to Amram Aaron, Moses, and Miriam their sister.

**26:60** And to Aaron were born Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**26:61** And Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

**26:62** And from their numbering they were twenty-three thousand, every male from a month old and upward; for they were not numbered together in the midst of the sons of Israel, because no allotment is given to them in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**26:63** And this is the numbering of Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered the sons of Israel in Araboth of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**26:64** And among these there was not a man of those who had been numbered by Moses and Aaron when they numbered the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.

**26:65** For the Lord had said to him, "They will certainly die in the wilderness." And not even one was left of them, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew term for "plains" as the place name "Araboth" (Araboth of Moab).
- **26:4** — The Greek text preserves an elliptical command containing the second-person pronoun "you" ("you and the sons of Israel who came out of Egypt"), differing from the Hebrew.
- **26:10** — Greek reads "in his congregation" where the Hebrew has "when that company died," and reads simply "the two hundred and fifty" without "men."
- **26:15** — In vv. 15-31 the Septuagint orders the tribes as Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Gad, and Asher, differing from the Hebrew order (where Gad appears at vv. 15-18 and Asher at vv. 44-47).
- **26:17** — Greek reads the distinct name Jamun (and Jamunites), corresponding to Hebrew Hamul (and Hamulites).
- **26:22** — Greek reads the distinct name Allel (and Allelites), corresponding to Hebrew Jahleel (and Jahleelites).
- **26:25** — Greek reads the distinct name Addi (and Addites), corresponding to Hebrew Eri (and Erites).
- **26:26** — Greek reads Arodi (as in Gen 46:16) and the distinct name Ariel (and Arielites), corresponding to Hebrew Arod and Areli.
- **26:27** — Greek gives Gad's census total as 44,500, whereas the Hebrew reads 40,500.
- **26:28** — Greek reads the distinct name Jamin (and Jaminites) for Asher's first son, corresponding to Hebrew Imnah (and Imnites).
- **26:29** — Greek omits "of the sons of Beriah" before Heber and reads the distinct name Melliel (and Mellielites), corresponding to Hebrew Malchiel.
- **26:30** — Greek reads the distinct name Kara (likely from scribal confusion of uncial sigma and kappa for Sara), corresponding to Hebrew Serah.
- **26:31** — Greek gives Asher's census total as 43,600 (using the Greek alphabetic numeral chi for 600), whereas the Hebrew reads 53,400.
- **26:34** — Greek reads the distinct name Ahiezer (and Ahiezerites), corresponding to Hebrew Iezer (and Iezerites).
- **26:36** — Greek reads the distinct name Symaer (and Symaerites), corresponding to Hebrew Shemida (and Shemidaites).
- **26:39** — Greek omits Becher (present in the Hebrew) and reads the distinct name Tanach (and Tanachites), corresponding to Hebrew Tahan (and Tahanites).
- **26:40** — Greek reads the distinct name Eden (and Edenites), corresponding to Hebrew Eran (and Eranites).
- **26:42** — Greek reads the distinct names Asyber (and Asyberites) and Jachiran (and Jachiranites), corresponding to Hebrew Ashbel and Ahiram.
- **26:43** — Greek lists only Sophan (and Sophanites), corresponding to Hebrew Shephupham while omitting Hupham.
- **26:44** — Greek reads Adar and Naamani (corresponding to Hebrew Ard and Naaman) and omits their clan formulas.
- **26:45** — Greek gives Benjamin's census total as 35,500, whereas the Hebrew reads 45,600.
- **26:46** — Greek reads the distinct name Sami (and Samites), corresponding to Hebrew Shuham (and Shuhamites).
- **26:47** — Greek gives Dan's census total as 64,600 (using the Greek alphabetic numeral chi for 600), whereas the Hebrew reads 64,400.
- **26:48** — Greek reads the distinct name Sael (and Saelites), corresponding to Hebrew Jahzeel (and Jahzeelites).
- **26:50** — Greek gives Naphtali's census total as 30,300, whereas the Hebrew reads 45,400.
- **26:58** — Greek lists four Levitical clans (omitting the Mahlites of the Hebrew) and places the clan of Korah before the Mushites.
- **26:59** — Greek literally reads "who bore these to Levi in Egypt," whereas the Hebrew reads "who was born to Levi in Egypt."
- **26:65** — Greek reads singular "to him" (referring to Moses), whereas the Hebrew reads plural "of them."
