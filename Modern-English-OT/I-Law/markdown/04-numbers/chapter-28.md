## Text

**28:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**28:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'My gifts, My offerings, My fire-offerings, for an aroma of sweet fragrance, you shall be careful to offer to Me at My feasts.'

**28:3** And you shall say to them, 'These are the fire-offerings which you shall bring to the Lord: two year-old lambs without blemish each day for a whole burnt offering continually;

**28:4** the one lamb you shall prepare in the morning, and the second lamb you shall prepare toward evening.

**28:5** And you shall prepare one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a sacrifice, mixed with oil, one-fourth of a hin,

**28:6** a continual whole burnt offering, which was made on Mount Sinai for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord;

**28:7** and its drink offering, one-fourth of a hin for each lamb; in the holy place you shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the Lord.

**28:8** And the second lamb you shall prepare toward evening; according to its sacrifice and according to its drink offering you shall prepare it for an aroma of sweet fragrance for the Lord.

**28:9** And on the Sabbath day you shall bring two year-old lambs without blemish, and two-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for a sacrifice, and a drink offering,

**28:10** a whole burnt offering of the Sabbath on the Sabbaths, in addition to the perpetual whole burnt offering, and its drink offering.

**28:11** And at the new moons you shall bring whole burnt offerings to the Lord: two young bulls from the cattle and one ram, seven year-old lambs without blemish;

**28:12** three-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each young bull, and two-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for the one ram,

**28:13** one-tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for each lamb, a sacrifice, an aroma of sweet fragrance, a fire-offering to the Lord.

**28:14** Their drink offering shall be one-half of a hin for each young bull, and one-third of a hin shall be for the one ram, and one-fourth of a hin of wine shall be for each lamb; this is the whole burnt offering month by month for the months of the year.

**28:15** And one male goat from the goats for a sin offering for the Lord; it shall be prepared in addition to the perpetual whole burnt offering, and its drink offering.

**28:16** And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the Passover of the Lord.

**28:17** And on the fifteenth day of this month is a feast; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**28:18** And the first day shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work;

**28:19** and you shall bring whole burnt offerings, fire-offerings to the Lord: two young bulls from the cattle, one ram, seven year-old lambs; they shall be without blemish for you;

**28:20** and their sacrifice shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for each young bull and two-tenths for the one ram,

**28:21** one-tenth each you shall prepare for each lamb of the seven lambs;

**28:22** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you,

**28:23** besides the perpetual whole burnt offering of the morning, which is a continual whole burnt offering.

**28:24** You shall prepare these things in the same manner each day for the seven days, a gift, a fire-offering for an aroma of sweet fragrance for the Lord; in addition to the perpetual whole burnt offering you shall prepare its drink offering.

**28:25** And the seventh day shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**28:26** And on the day of the new produce, when you offer to the Lord a new sacrifice of the Weeks, it shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work.

**28:27** And you shall bring whole burnt offerings for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord: two young bulls from the cattle, one ram, seven year-old lambs without blemish;

**28:28** their sacrifice shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for each young bull and two-tenths for the one ram,

**28:29** one-tenth each for each lamb of the seven lambs;

**28:30** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you, besides the whole burnt offering

**28:31** that is perpetual; and you shall prepare their sacrifice for Me (they shall be without blemish for you), and their drink offerings.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Where the Hebrew text speaks of "My food ... at its appointed time," the Greek reads "My offerings ... at My feasts."
- **28:5** — Throughout chapter 28, Greek thysia ("sacrifice") designates the flour offering corresponding to the Hebrew grain offering (minchah), while karpoma ("fire-offering") designates an offering burned on the altar.
- **28:26** — Instead of a term for "firstfruits," the Greek reads "the day of the new produce" (literally "of the new things"), paralleling "a new sacrifice" in the same verse.
- **28:31** — The Greek verse boundary splits "the whole burnt offering | that is perpetual" across 28:30-31, and 28:31 specifies "their sacrifice for Me" where the Hebrew reads "its grain offering."
