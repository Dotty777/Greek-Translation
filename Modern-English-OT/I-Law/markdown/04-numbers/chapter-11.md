## Text

**11:1** And the people were grumbling evil things before the Lord; and the Lord heard and became furious with anger, and fire from the Lord blazed up among them and consumed a part of the camp.

**11:2** And the people cried out to Moses; and Moses prayed to the Lord, and the fire died down.

**11:3** And the name of that place was called Burning, because fire from the Lord blazed up among them.

**11:4** And the mixed crowd that was among them craved a craving; and sitting down, the sons of Israel also wept and said, "Who will feed us meat?

**11:5** We remember the fish that we used to eat in Egypt for free, and the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlic;

**11:6** but now our soul is dried up; there is nothing, except our eyes are on the manna."

**11:7** Now the manna is like coriander seed, and its appearance is the appearance of crystal.

**11:8** And the people went about and gathered it, and ground it in the mill and pounded it in the mortar, and boiled it in the pot and made it into hearth cakes; and its flavor was like the taste of a pastry made with oil.

**11:9** And whenever the dew came down upon the camp at night, the manna came down upon it.

**11:10** And Moses heard them weeping according to their clans, each at his doorway; and the Lord became exceedingly furious with anger, and before Moses it was evil.

**11:11** And Moses said to the Lord, "Why have You afflicted Your servant? And why have I not found favor before You, that You lay the impulse of this people upon me?

**11:12** Did I conceive all this people, or did I give birth to them, that You say to me, 'Take him into your bosom, as a nurse would carry the nursing child,' to the land that You swore to their fathers?

**11:13** Where am I to get meat to give to all this people? For they weep at me, saying, 'Give us meat so that we may eat.'

**11:14** I alone will not be able to bear this people, because this matter is too heavy for me.

**11:15** And if You deal with me this way, kill me outright, if I have found mercy with You, so that I may not see my affliction."

**11:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Gather for Me seventy men from the elders of Israel, of whom you yourself have seen that they are elders of the people and their scribes. And you shall bring them to the tent of the testimony, and they will stand there with you.

**11:17** And I will come down and speak there with you, and I will take some of the Spirit on you and place some on them; and they will help bear the impulse of the people with you, and you will not bear them alone.

**11:18** And to the people you shall say, 'Purify yourselves for tomorrow, and you will eat meat; because you wept before the Lord, saying, "Who will feed us meat? For it is good for us in Egypt." And the Lord will give you meat to eat, and you will eat meat.

**11:19** You will not eat one day, nor two, nor five days, nor ten days, nor twenty days;

**11:20** up to a month of days you will eat, until meat comes out of your nostrils, and it will become cholera to you; because you disobeyed the Lord who is among you, and wept before Him, saying, "Why did we come out of Egypt?"'"

**11:21** And Moses said to the Lord, "Six hundred thousand on foot are the people among whom I am, and You have said, 'I will give them meat to eat, and they will eat for a month of days.'

**11:22** Will sheep and cattle be slaughtered for them, and will it be enough for them? Or will all the fish of the sea be gathered for them, and will it be enough for them?"

**11:23** And the Lord said to Moses, "Will the hand of the Lord not be sufficient? Now you will know whether My word will overtake you or not."

**11:24** And Moses went out and spoke to the people the words of the Lord, and he gathered seventy men from the elders of the people and stationed them around the tent.

**11:25** And the Lord came down in a cloud and spoke to him, and took some of the Spirit on him and placed some upon the seventy men, the elders; and when the Spirit rested upon them, they prophesied, and they did not do so again.

**11:26** And two men were left behind in the camp; the name of the one was Eldad, and the name of the second was Medad, and the Spirit rested upon them; and these were among those who had been registered, and they did not come to the tent; and they prophesied in the camp.

**11:27** And the young man ran up and reported to Moses and spoke, saying, "Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp."

**11:28** And Joshua the son of Nun, who stood beside Moses, the chosen one, answered and said, "Master Moses, stop them."

**11:29** And Moses said to him, "Are you jealous for me? And who would grant all the people of the Lord to be prophets, whenever the Lord puts His Spirit upon them?"

**11:30** And Moses went away into the camp, he and the elders of Israel.

**11:31** And a wind went out from the Lord and brought across quail from the sea, and cast them upon the camp a day's journey on this side and a day's journey on that side, around the camp, about two cubits above the ground.

**11:32** And the people rose up all the day and all the night and all the next day, and gathered the quail; the one who gathered the least gathered ten kors; and they slaughtered drying heaps for themselves around the camp.

**11:33** The meat was still in their teeth before it ran out, and the Lord became furious against His people; and the Lord struck the people with an exceedingly great plague.

**11:34** And the name of that place was called Graves of Craving; there they buried the craving people.

**11:35** From Graves of Craving the people set out to Hazeroth, and the people were in Hazeroth.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Taberah by its meaning as Enpyrismos ("Burning"); similarly, in vv. 34-35 it translates Kibroth-hattaavah as Mnemata tes epithymias ("Graves of Craving").
- **11:11** — Greek horme literally means "impulse," "impetus," or "onrush" (also in v. 17), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "burden."
- **11:20** — Greek cholera denotes an acute bilious sickness accompanied by violent vomiting and nausea, rendering Hebrew "a loathsome thing."
- **11:28** — Greek ho eklektos ("the chosen one") reflects a reading of the Hebrew root for "chosen," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from his youth."
- **11:32** — The Greek renders the dry measure as "kors" (korous; MT "homers") and reads "they slaughtered drying heaps" (esphaxan ... psygmous) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "they spread them out."
