## Text

**12:1** And Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses on account of the Ethiopian woman whom Moses had taken, because he had taken an Ethiopian woman.

**12:2** And they said, "Has the Lord spoken to Moses alone? Did He not also speak to us?" And the Lord heard.

**12:3** Now the man Moses was very gentle, more than all the people who were on the earth.

**12:4** And the Lord said to Moses, Miriam, and Aaron, "Come out, you three, to the tent of testimony." And the three went out to the tent of testimony.

**12:5** And the Lord came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the door of the tent of testimony; and Aaron and Miriam were called, and both of them came out.

**12:6** And He said to them, "Hear My words: If there is a prophet of yours for the Lord, in a vision I will make Myself known to him, and in sleep I will speak to him.

**12:7** Not so is My attendant Moses; in all My house he is faithful.

**12:8** Mouth to mouth I will speak to him, in visible form and not through riddles, and he has seen the glory of the Lord. And why were you not afraid to speak against My attendant Moses?"

**12:9** And the wrath of the Lord's fury was upon them, and He departed.

**12:10** And the cloud withdrew from the tent, and look, Miriam was leprous, like snow. And Aaron looked at Miriam, and look, she was leprous.

**12:11** And Aaron said to Moses, "I beg you, lord, do not lay sin upon us, for we acted in ignorance, in that we have sinned.

**12:12** Let her not become like something equal to death, like a stillborn child coming out from a mother's womb; it devours half of her flesh."

**12:13** And Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "God, I beg You, heal her."

**12:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "If her father had indeed spit in her face, would she not be ashamed for seven days? Let her be separated for seven days outside the camp, and after this she will come in."

**12:15** And Miriam was separated outside the camp for seven days; and the people did not set out until Miriam was cleansed.

**12:16** And after this the people set out from Hazeroth and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Greek text reads Aithiopissa ("Ethiopian woman"), rendering the Hebrew "Cushite woman" with the Septuagint's standard geographic equivalent.
- **12:4** — The Greek text uses skene tou martyriou ("tent of testimony") rather than the Hebrew "tent of meeting," and lists Miriam before Aaron.
- **12:6** — The Greek text reads Kyrio ("for the Lord") in the dative and en hypno ("in sleep") rather than "in a dream," resolving a difficult Hebrew clause; in v. 8 it likewise reads "the glory of the Lord" where Hebrew has "the form of the Lord."
- **12:15** — The Greek text reads ekatharisthe ("was cleansed"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "was brought back in."
