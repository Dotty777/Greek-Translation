## Text

**6:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel

**6:2** and you shall say to them, 'A man or a woman who solemnly vows a vow to keep himself pure in purity to the Lord

**6:3** from wine and strong drink, he shall keep himself pure from wine, and vinegar from wine and vinegar from strong drink he shall not drink, and whatever is made from grapes he shall not drink, and fresh grapes and raisins he shall not eat.

**6:4** All the days of his vow, from everything that is produced from the vine, wine from pressed grape skins to a grape seed he shall not eat.

**6:5** All the days of the purification, a razor shall not come upon his head; until the days which he vowed to the Lord are fulfilled, he shall be holy, letting the locks of hair of the head grow.

**6:6** All the days of the vow to the Lord, he shall not go in to any dead person. For a father and for a mother

**6:7** and for a brother and for a sister, he shall not be defiled for them when they die, because the vow of his God is upon him, upon his head;

**6:8** all the days of his vow he shall be holy to the Lord.

**6:9** But if someone dies suddenly beside him, immediately the head of his vow shall be defiled, and he shall shave his head on whatever day he is cleansed; on the seventh day he shall be shaved.

**6:10** And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the doors of the tent of testimony,

**6:11** and the priest shall offer one for sin and one for a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning what he sinned regarding the corpse, and he shall consecrate his head on that day on which he was consecrated to the Lord for the days of the vow,

**6:12** and he shall bring a year-old male lamb for a trespass offering; and the former days shall be uncounted, because the head of his vow was defiled.

**6:13** And this is the law of the one who has vowed: on whatever day he fulfills the days of his vow, he himself shall offer at the doors of the tent of testimony;

**6:14** and he shall bring his gift to the Lord: one year-old unblemished male lamb for a whole burnt offering, and one year-old unblemished female lamb for a sin offering, and one unblemished ram for a deliverance offering,

**6:15** and a basket of unleavened bread of fine flour, loaves kneaded with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and their sacrifice and their drink offering.

**6:16** And the priest shall present them before the Lord, and shall offer his sin offering and his burnt offering;

**6:17** and he shall offer the ram as a sacrifice of deliverance to the Lord with the basket of unleavened bread, and the priest shall offer its sacrifice and its drink offering.

**6:18** And the one who has vowed shall shave the head of his vow at the doors of the tent of testimony, and he shall put the hair upon the fire that is under the sacrifice of deliverance.

**6:19** And the priest shall take the boiled shoulder from the ram, and one unleavened loaf from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and shall place them upon the hands of the one who has vowed, after he has shaved his vow;

**6:20** and the priest shall present them as a placed offering before the Lord; it shall be holy for the priest, in addition to the breast of the placed offering and in addition to the shoulder of the set-apart offering; and after these things the one who has vowed may drink wine.

**6:21** This is the law of the one who has vowed, who vows to the Lord, his gift to the Lord concerning the vow, apart from whatever his hand may find, according to the extent of his vow which he vows, according to the law of purity.'"

**6:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:23** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'In this way you shall bless the sons of Israel, saying to them:

**6:24** "May the Lord bless you and guard you,

**6:25** and may the Lord make His face shine upon you and have mercy on you;

**6:26** may the Lord lift up His face upon you and give you peace."'"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**6:27** *(after `6:26`)* "And they shall put My name upon the sons of Israel, and I, the Lord, will bless them."

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Throughout this chapter the Septuagint never transliterates "Nazirite," rendering the Hebrew root with terms for vowing (euche) and ritual purity (hagneia, hagnismos).
- **6:4** — The Greek text reads accusative oinon ("wine") in apposition with the grape products rather than a genitive modifier ("vine of wine") as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **6:11** — The Septuagint joins the clause "on which he was consecrated to the Lord for the days of the vow" to v. 11 with a passive verb, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins v. 12 with an active rededication.
- **6:14** — Greek soterion ("deliverance [offering]") is the Septuagint's standard rendering of the Hebrew peace/fellowship offering (similarly in vv. 17, 18).
- **6:24** — Greek reads 'bless you and guard' without repeating 'you' after 'guard' (supplied in English), and in verse 25 'have mercy on you' (ἐλεήσαι σε).
- **6:27 *(additional)*** — In the Saints Greek Critical Text this verse follows the blessing (vv. 24-26); it has no separate place in the Saints.Bible numbering, so it is shown after 6:26.
