## Text

**10:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Make for yourself two trumpets;

**10:2** of hammered silver you shall make them for yourself. And they will be for you to summon the congregation and to move out the camps.

**10:3** And you shall blow on them, and all the congregation will be gathered to the doorway of the tent of the testimony;

**10:4** but if they blow on one, all the rulers, the leaders of Israel, will come to you.

**10:5** And you shall blow a signal, and the camps encamping on the east will set out;

**10:6** and you shall blow a second signal, and the camps encamping on the south will set out; and you shall blow a third signal, and the camps encamping by the sea will set out; and you shall blow a fourth signal, and the camps encamping toward the north will set out; they shall blow with a signal at their setting out.

**10:7** And whenever you gather the congregation, you shall blow, and not with a signal.

**10:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets, and it will be for you a perpetual statute for your generations.

**10:9** And if you go out to war in your land against the adversaries who oppose you, then you shall sound a signal with the trumpets, and you will be remembered before the Lord, and you will be saved from your enemies.

**10:10** And in the days of your gladness, and at your feasts, and at your new moons, you shall blow the trumpets over the whole burnt offerings and over the sacrifices of your deliverance, and it will be a remembrance for you before your God; I am the Lord your God."

**10:11** And it happened in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, that the cloud went up from the tent of the testimony,

**10:12** and the sons of Israel set out with their baggage in the wilderness of Sinai; and the cloud stood still in the wilderness of Paran.

**10:13** And they set out first at the voice of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**10:14** And the division of the camp of the sons of Judah set out first with their force, and over their force was Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**10:15** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar;

**10:16** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon.

**10:17** And they will take down the tent, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari will set out, carrying the tent.

**10:18** And the division of the camp of Reuben set out with their force, and over their force was Elizur son of Shedeur;

**10:19** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**10:20** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Gad was Eliasaph the son of Reuel.

**10:21** And the sons of Kohath will set out, carrying the holy things, and they will set up the tent by the time they arrive.

**10:22** And the division of the camp of Ephraim will set out with their force; over their force is Elishama son of Ammihud;

**10:23** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh is Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;

**10:24** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin is Abidan the son of Gideoni.

**10:25** And the division of the camp of the sons of Dan will set out last of all the camps with their force, and over their force is Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;

**10:26** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Asher is Pagiel son of Ochran;

**10:27** and over the force of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan.

**10:28** These are the armies of the sons of Israel, and they set out with their force.

**10:29** And Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, the father-in-law of Moses, "We are setting out to the place of which the Lord said, 'This I will give to you'; come with us, and we will do good to you, for the Lord has spoken good things concerning Israel."

**10:30** And he said to him, "I will not go, but rather to my land and to my family."

**10:31** And he said, "Do not leave us, because you were with us in the wilderness, and you will be an elder among us;

**10:32** and it will be, if you go with us, that whatever those good things are with which the Lord does good to us, we will do good to you as well."

**10:33** And they set out from the mountain of the Lord a three days' journey; and the ark of the covenant of the Lord was going ahead before them a three days' journey, to search out a resting place for them.

**10:34** And it happened, when the ark set out, that Moses said, "Rise up, Lord, let Your enemies be scattered, let all who hate You flee."

**10:35** And at the resting he said, "Return, Lord, thousands, ten thousands in Israel."

**10:36** And the cloud was overshadowing them by day and when they set out from the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — The Greek includes third and fourth trumpet signals for the camps by the sea (west) and toward the north, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions only the first two signals (east and south).
- **10:17** — Here and in vv. 21, 22, and 25, the Greek shifts from past aorist verbs (vv. 14, 18, 28) to future verbs ("will take down," "will set out," "will set up"), reflecting a prescriptive or habitual rendering of Hebrew weqatal forms.
- **10:20** — The Greek reads "Reuel" (Ragouel) as the father of Eliasaph (matching MT Num 2:14), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text here reads "Deuel."
- **10:31** — The Greek reads "because you were with us in the wilderness, and you will be an elder among us," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "because you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you will serve as eyes for us."
- **10:34** — Verses 34-36 follow the Septuagint verse order, placing Moses's words at the ark's departure and resting (vv. 34-35; MT vv. 35-36) before the notice of the overshadowing cloud (v. 36; MT v. 34). In v. 35, Greek "thousands, ten thousands" (chiliadas myriadas) stands in the accusative without a preposition.
