## Text

**17:1** And the Lord said to Moses

**17:2** and to Eleazar the son of Aaron, the priest, "Take up the bronze censers from the midst of those who were burned up, and scatter this strange fire there, because they sanctified the censers of these sinners with their lives;

**17:3** and make them into hammered plates, a covering for the altar, because they were offered before the Lord and were sanctified, and they became a sign to the sons of Israel."

**17:4** And Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, took the bronze censers that those who were burned up had offered, and they placed them as a covering for the altar,

**17:5** a memorial to the sons of Israel, so that no outsider who is not from the offspring of Aaron may approach to put incense before the Lord, and he will not become like Korah and his faction; just as the Lord spoke to him by the hand of Moses.

**17:6** And the sons of Israel grumbled the next day against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have killed the people of the Lord."

**17:7** And it happened, while the congregation was banding together against Moses and Aaron, that they rushed toward the tent of the testimony; and this one, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.

**17:8** And Moses and Aaron went in before the tent of the testimony;

**17:9** and the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**17:10** "Depart from the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them all at once." And they fell on their faces.

**17:11** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, and put fire on it from the altar, and lay incense on it, and carry it quickly into the camp, and it will make atonement for them; for wrath has gone out from the presence of the Lord; it has begun to shatter the people."

**17:12** And Aaron took it just as Moses had spoken to him, and ran into the congregation; and the plague had already begun among the people. And he laid on the incense and made atonement for the people;

**17:13** and he stood between the dead and the living, and the plague ceased among the people.

**17:14** And those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died on account of Korah.

**17:15** And Aaron returned to Moses at the door of the tent of the testimony, and the plague ceased.

**17:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:17** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and take from them a staff, a staff according to ancestral houses, from all their rulers, according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs; and write each one's name on the staff.

**17:18** And write the name of Aaron on the staff of Levi; for there is one staff; they will give it according to the tribe of their ancestral house.

**17:19** And you will place them in the tent of the testimony, in front of the testimony, where I will be made known to you there.

**17:20** And it will be that the man whom I choose, his staff will sprout; and I will remove from Myself the grumbling of the sons of Israel, which they grumble against you."

**17:21** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel; and all their rulers gave him a staff, for the one ruler a staff, ruler by ruler, according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs; and the staff of Aaron was among their staffs.

**17:22** And Moses deposited the staffs before the Lord in the tent of the testimony.

**17:23** And it happened on the next day that Moses and Aaron entered the tent of the testimony, and look, the staff of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted, and brought forth a sprout, and bloomed with blossoms, and produced nuts.

**17:24** And Moses brought out all the staffs from the presence of the Lord to all the sons of Israel; and they saw, and each one took his staff.

**17:25** And the Lord said to Moses, "Deposit the staff of Aaron before the testimonies for safekeeping, a sign to the sons of the disobedient; and let their grumbling cease from Me, and they will certainly not die."

**17:26** And Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord commanded Moses; so they did.

**17:27** And the sons of Israel spoke to Moses, saying, "Look, we have been consumed, we have perished, we are utterly consumed;

**17:28** everyone who touches the tent of the Lord dies; must we die to the very end?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — In Septuagint versification, chapter 17 begins here (corresponding to 16:36 in Hebrew Bibles), and the Lord addresses both Moses and Eleazar directly rather than telling Moses to speak to Eleazar.
- **17:7** — The Greek reads "rushed" (hormesan) rather than the Hebrew "turned," and renders the Hebrew exclamation with the deictic pronoun "this one" (tende), referring to the tent.
- **17:11** — The Greek text has the third-person singular future "it will make atonement" (exilasetai, referring to the incense) rather than an imperative addressed to Aaron, and uses the verb "shatter" (thrauein, cognate with thrausis, "plague," in verses 12-15).
- **17:23** — The Greek reads "nuts" (karya) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies "almonds," and includes Aaron alongside Moses entering the tent.
