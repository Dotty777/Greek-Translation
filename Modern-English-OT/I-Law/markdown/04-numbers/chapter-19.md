## Text

**19:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**19:2** "This is the ordinance of the law, all that the Lord has commanded, saying, 'Speak to the sons of Israel, and let them bring to you a red heifer without blemish, which has no blemish in it and upon which a yoke has not been laid.

**19:3** And you shall give it to Eleazar the priest; and they shall lead it outside the camp to a clean place, and they shall slaughter it before him;

**19:4** and Eleazar shall take some of its blood, and he shall sprinkle some of its blood opposite the front of the tent of the testimony seven times.

**19:5** And they shall burn it completely before him; and the hide and its flesh and its blood, together with its dung, shall be burned completely.

**19:6** And the priest shall take cedar wood and hyssop and scarlet, and they shall throw them into the midst of the burning of the heifer.

**19:7** And the priest shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and after these things he shall enter into the camp; and the priest shall be unclean until evening.

**19:8** And the one who burns it completely shall wash his garments and bathe his body, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**19:9** And a clean person shall gather the ashes of the heifer and deposit them outside the camp in a clean place. And it shall be for the congregation of the sons of Israel for safekeeping; water of sprinkling—it is a purification.

**19:10** And the one who gathers the ashes of the heifer shall wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until evening; and it shall be for the sons of Israel and for the attached proselytes an eternal statute.

**19:11** The one who touches the dead body of any human person shall be unclean for seven days;

**19:12** this one shall be purified on the third day and on the seventh day, and shall be clean; but if he is not purified on the third day and on the seventh day, he shall not be clean.

**19:13** Everyone who touches the dead body from a human person, if one dies, and is not purified, has defiled the tent of the Lord; that person shall be wiped out from Israel, because water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him; he is unclean; his uncleanness is still in him.

**19:14** And this is the law: if a person dies in a house, everyone who enters into the house and all things that are in the house shall be unclean for seven days;

**19:15** and every open vessel that does not have a fastening bound upon it is unclean.

**19:16** And everyone who touches on the face of the open field one killed or a corpse or a human bone or a grave shall be unclean for seven days.

**19:17** And they shall take for the unclean person some of the burned ashes of the purification, and they shall pour living water upon them into a vessel;

**19:18** and a clean man shall take hyssop and dip it into the water, and he shall sprinkle upon the house and upon the vessels and upon the persons, however many are there, and upon the one who has touched the human bone or the one killed or the one who has died or the grave;

**19:19** and the clean person shall sprinkle upon the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall be purified on the seventh day; and he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**19:20** And a person who becomes defiled and is not purified, that person shall be utterly destroyed from the midst of the congregation, because he has defiled the holy things of the Lord; because water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him, he is unclean.

**19:21** And it shall be to you an eternal statute; and the one who sprinkles water of sprinkling shall wash his garments, and the one who touches the water of sprinkling shall be unclean until evening;

**19:22** and everything that the unclean person touches shall be unclean, and the person who touches it shall be unclean until evening.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — The Greek adds "to a clean place" (absent in the Hebrew MT here, though present in 19:9) and uses plural verbs ("they shall lead... they shall slaughter") for the actions carried out before Eleazar.
- **19:9** — Greek "water of sprinkling" (hydor rantismou) and "purification" (hagnisma) correspond to Hebrew expressions commonly rendered "water for impurity" and "sin offering," highlighting the rite's cleansing function.
- **19:14** — The Greek renders the dwelling as "house" (oikia here and oikos in 19:18) rather than "tent" as in the Hebrew MT, adapting the law to settled domestic contexts.
- **19:16** — Greek traumatias ("one killed" or "fatally wounded") corresponds to the Hebrew phrase for one killed by a sword, without mentioning a sword.
- **19:19** — The Greek reads "he shall be unclean until evening" (matching the phrasing of vv. 7, 8, 10, 21, and 22), whereas the Hebrew MT states positively "he shall be clean at evening."
