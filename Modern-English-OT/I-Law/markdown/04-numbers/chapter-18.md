## Text

**18:1** And the Lord said to Aaron, saying, "You and your sons and the house of your ancestral family shall receive the firstfruits of the holy things; you and your sons shall bear the sins of your priesthood.

**18:2** And bring near to yourself your brothers, the tribe of Levi, the clan of your father, and let them be joined to you and serve you; and you and your sons with you shall be before the tent of testimony.

**18:3** And they shall keep your duties and the duties of the tent; however, they shall not come near to the holy vessels and to the altar, and neither they nor you shall die.

**18:4** And they shall be joined to you, and they shall keep the duties of the tent of testimony for all the services of the tent, and an outsider shall not come near to you.

**18:5** And you shall keep the duties of the holy things and the duties of the altar, and there shall be no wrath among the sons of Israel.

**18:6** And I have taken your brothers the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, a gift given to the Lord, to perform the services of the tent of testimony;

**18:7** and you and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for every matter of the altar and what is inside the curtain, and you shall perform the services as a gift of your priesthood; and the outsider who comes near shall die."

**18:8** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, "And look, I have given to you the keeping of the firstfruits from all the things consecrated to Me by the sons of Israel; to you I have given them as a gift of honor, and to your sons after you, as an eternal statute.

**18:9** And let this be yours from the consecrated holy things of the fire offerings, from all their gifts and from all the sacrifices and from every guilt offering of theirs and from all the sin offerings: whatever they render to Me from all the holy things shall belong to you and to your sons.

**18:10** In the holy of holies you shall eat them; every male shall eat them, you and your sons; they shall be holy to you.

**18:11** And this shall be yours from the firstfruits of their gifts, from all the laid-on offerings of the sons of Israel; to you I have given them, and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as an eternal statute; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:12** Every firstfruit of oil and every firstfruit of wine, their firstfruit of grain—whatever they give to the Lord, to you I have given them.

**18:13** All the first produce, whatever is in their land, whatever they bring to the Lord, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:14** Everything devoted among the sons of Israel shall be yours.

**18:15** And everything that opens a womb from all flesh, which they offer to the Lord, from human to animal, shall be yours; however, the firstborn of humans shall be redeemed with a ransom, and the firstborn of the unclean animals you shall redeem.

**18:16** And its redemption shall be from one month old; the valuation is five shekels according to the holy shekel, which is twenty obols.

**18:17** However, the firstborn of calves and the firstborn of sheep and the firstborn of goats you shall not redeem; they are holy. And you shall pour out their blood against the altar, and the fat you shall offer up as a fire offering, for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord;

**18:18** and the flesh shall be yours; and the breast of the laid-on offering, and as with the right shoulder, it shall be yours.

**18:19** Every contribution of the holy things, whatever the sons of Israel set aside for the Lord, I have given to you and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as an eternal statute; it is a covenant of eternal salt before the Lord for you and for your offspring after you."

**18:20** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, "In their land you shall not inherit, and there shall be no portion for you among them, because I am your portion and your inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**18:21** And to the sons of Levi, look, I have given every tithe in Israel as an allotment in return for their services, whatever service they perform in the tent of testimony.

**18:22** And the sons of Israel shall no longer come near to the tent of testimony, to bear fatal sin.

**18:23** And the Levite himself shall perform the service of the tent of testimony, and they shall bear their sins, an eternal statute for their generations; and in the midst of the sons of Israel they shall not inherit an inheritance.

**18:24** For the tithes of the sons of Israel, whatever they set apart for the Lord as a contribution, I have given to the Levites as an allotment; therefore I have said to them, 'In the midst of the sons of Israel they shall not inherit an allotment.'"

**18:25** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:26** "And to the Levites you shall speak and say to them, 'When you receive from the sons of Israel the tithe that I have given to you from them as an allotment, then you yourselves shall set aside from it a contribution for the Lord, a tithe from the tithe.

**18:27** And your contributions shall be reckoned to you as grain from a threshing floor and a contribution from a winepress.

**18:28** In this way you also shall set them aside from the contributions of the Lord, from all your tithes, whatever you receive from the sons of Israel, and from them you shall give a contribution to Aaron the priest.

**18:29** From all your gifts you shall set aside a contribution for the Lord, or from all the firstfruits, the consecrated part from it.'

**18:30** And you shall say to them, 'Whenever you set aside the firstfruit from it, then it shall be reckoned to the Levites as produce from a threshing floor and as produce from a winepress,

**18:31** and you shall eat it in every place, you and your households, because this is your wage in return for your services in the tent of testimony;

**18:32** and you shall not bear sin because of it, whenever you set aside the firstfruit from it; and the holy things of the sons of Israel you shall not profane, so that you may not die.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — The Greek reads "shall receive the firstfruits of the holy things," differing from the Hebrew MT ("bear the iniquity of the sanctuary"), while using the same verb ("receive/bear") in the second clause for "bear the sins of your priesthood."
- **18:11** — Greek epithemata literally means "things laid on" ("laid-on offerings"), rendering the Hebrew wave offering; the same term recurs in 18:18 alongside "right shoulder" (brachion, where the Hebrew MT has "thigh").
- **18:16** — The Greek uses "obols" (oboloi), a Greek unit of weight and coinage, as the equivalent of the Hebrew gerah (one-twentieth of a holy shekel).
- **18:19** — Greek aphairema ("contribution") literally means a portion taken off or set aside; "a covenant of eternal salt" preserves the Greek genitive agreement (halos aioniou), denoting an enduring salt-ratified covenant.
- **18:28** — The Greek includes a difficult masculine plural pronoun ("set them aside from the contributions of the Lord"), likely an early textual variation within the Greek tradition where the Hebrew MT reads "you also shall present a contribution to the Lord."
