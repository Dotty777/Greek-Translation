## Text

**5:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and let them send away from the camp every leper, and everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is unclean because of a corpse;

**5:3** from male to female, send them away outside the camp, and they will by no means defile their camps, in which I dwell among them."

**5:4** And the sons of Israel did so, and they sent them away outside the camp; just as the Lord spoke to Moses, so the sons of Israel did.

**5:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:6** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'A man or a woman—whoever commits any of the human sins and acts with utter disregard, and that person surely commits a trespass—

**5:7** he shall confess the sin that he committed, and shall repay the trespass, the principal, and shall add its fifth part to it, and shall repay the one against whom he trespassed.

**5:8** But if the person has no next of kin so as to repay the trespass to him, the trespass that is repaid to the Lord shall belong to the priest, apart from the ram of atonement through which he shall make atonement for him.

**5:9** And every firstfruit offering pertaining to all the things consecrated among the sons of Israel, whatever they present to the Lord, to the priest, shall belong to him.

**5:10** And to each one his consecrated things shall belong; a man who gives to the priest—it shall belong to him.'"

**5:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:12** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'If any man's wife transgresses and utterly disregards him,

**5:13** and someone lies with her in sexual intercourse, and it escapes the eyes of her husband and she keeps it secret, though she is defiled, and there was no witness with her and she has not been caught;

**5:14** and a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he is jealous of his wife, and she is defiled, or a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he is jealous of his wife, and she is not defiled;

**5:15** then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and shall present the gift for her, one-tenth of an ephah, barley flour; he shall not pour oil upon it, nor shall he put frankincense upon it, for it is a sacrifice of jealousy, a memorial sacrifice bringing sin to remembrance.

**5:16** And the priest shall bring her forward and set her before the Lord;

**5:17** and the priest shall take clean, living water in an earthenware vessel, and some of the earth that is on the floor of the tent of testimony, and the priest shall take it and put it into the water;

**5:18** and the priest shall set the woman before the Lord, and uncover the head of the woman, and place upon her hands the memorial sacrifice, the sacrifice of jealousy; and in the hand of the priest shall be the water of this conviction that brings a curse.

**5:19** And the priest shall put her under oath and say to the woman, "If no one has lain with you, if you have not transgressed so as to be defiled while under your own husband, be unharmed by this water of conviction that brings a curse.

**5:20** But if you have transgressed, being under a husband, or have been defiled, and someone has had sexual intercourse with you besides your husband—"

**5:21** and the priest shall put the woman under oath with the words of this curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, "May the Lord make you a curse and an oath in the midst of your people, when the Lord makes your thigh fall away and your belly swell;

**5:22** and this water that brings a curse shall enter into your belly, to make the abdomen swell and your thigh fall away." And the woman shall say, "May it be so, may it be so."

**5:23** And the priest shall write these curses in a scroll, and wash them off into the water of conviction that brings a curse;

**5:24** and he shall make the woman drink the water of conviction that brings a curse, and the water of conviction that brings a curse shall enter into her.

**5:25** And the priest shall take from the hand of the woman the sacrifice of jealousy, and shall place the sacrifice before the Lord and present it to the altar;

**5:26** and the priest shall take a handful from the sacrifice, its memorial, and offer it up upon the altar, and after these things he shall make the woman drink the water.

**5:27** And it shall be, if she is defiled and completely escapes her husband's notice, then the water of conviction that brings a curse shall enter into her, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall fall away, and the woman shall become a curse to her people;

**5:28** but if the woman has not been defiled and is clean, then she shall be unharmed and shall bring forth seed.

**5:29** This is the law of jealousy, when a woman transgresses, being under a husband, and is defiled,

**5:30** or a man upon whom a spirit of jealousy comes and he is jealous of his wife; and he shall set his wife before the Lord, and the priest shall carry out for her all this law;

**5:31** and the man shall be innocent of sin, and that woman shall bear her sin.'"

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Greek psyche ("soul") is used in Septuagint purity laws to denote a dead body or corpse (compare 6:6, 11).
- **5:9** — The Greek text has a double dative ("to the Lord, to the priest"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads only "to the priest."
- **5:17** — The Septuagint reads "clean, living water" and "the tent of testimony," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "holy water" and "the tabernacle."
- **5:18** — Greek elegmos ("conviction" or "reproof") reflects a Septuagint rendering distinct from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "bitterness" (similarly in vv. 19, 23, 24, 27).
- **5:27** — Greek lethe lathe ("completely escapes ... notice") echoes v. 13, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "has acted unfaithfully against her husband."
