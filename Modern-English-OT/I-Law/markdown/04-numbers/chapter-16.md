## Text

**16:1** And Korah, son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On, son of Peleth, son of Reuben, spoke;

**16:2** and they rose up before Moses, along with two hundred and fifty men of the sons of Israel, leaders of the congregation, summoned to the council, and renowned men;

**16:3** They banded together behind Moses and Aaron and said, "Let it be enough for you, because all the congregation, all of them, are holy, and the Lord is among them; and why do you rise up against the congregation of the Lord?"

**16:4** And when Moses heard, he fell on his face,

**16:5** and he spoke to Korah and to all his congregation, saying, "God has examined and known those who are His and the holy ones, and He brought them near to Himself, and those whom He chose for Himself, He brought near to Himself.

**16:6** Do this: take censers for yourselves, Korah and all his congregation,

**16:7** and put fire on them, and put incense on them before the Lord tomorrow; and it will be that the man whom the Lord chooses, this one is holy; let it be enough for you, sons of Levi."

**16:8** And Moses said to Korah, "Listen to me, sons of Levi.

**16:9** Is this a small thing to you, that the God of Israel separated you from the congregation of Israel and brought you near to Himself to perform the services of the tent of the Lord, and to stand before the tent to serve them,

**16:10** and brought you near, and all your brothers, the sons of Levi, with you? And do you also seek to serve as priests?

**16:11** Thus you and all your congregation are gathered together against God; and who is Aaron, that you grumble against him?"

**16:12** And Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab. And they said, "We are not going up;

**16:13** is this a small thing, that you brought us up into a land flowing with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness, that you rule over us as a ruler?

**16:14** Even if you had brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, and had given us an inheritance of a field and vineyards, you would have gouged out the eyes of those men; we are not going up."

**16:15** And Moses was exceedingly indignant, and said to the Lord, "Do not have regard for their sacrifice; I have not taken any desirable thing from any of them, nor have I harmed any one of them."

**16:16** And Moses said to Korah, "Sanctify your congregation, and be ready before the Lord, you and they and Aaron, tomorrow;

**16:17** and each of you take his censer, and you will put incense on them, and each of you will bring his censer before the Lord, two hundred and fifty censers, and you and Aaron, each his censer."

**16:18** And each one took his censer, and they put fire on them, and laid incense on it; and Moses and Aaron stood by the doors of the tent of testimony.

**16:19** And Korah assembled all his congregation against them by the door of the tent of testimony; and the glory of the Lord appeared to all the congregation.

**16:20** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**16:21** "Separate yourselves from the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them all at once."

**16:22** And they fell on their faces and said, "God, God of the spirits and of all flesh, if one man has sinned, is the wrath of the Lord upon all the congregation?"

**16:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:24** "Speak to the congregation, saying, 'Withdraw from around the congregation of Korah.'"

**16:25** And Moses rose up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and all the elders of Israel went along with him.

**16:26** And he spoke to the congregation, saying, "Separate yourselves from the tents of these harsh men, and do not touch anything of all that belongs to them, so that you do not perish together with all your belongings."

**16:27** And they withdrew from around the tent of Korah; and Dathan and Abiram came out and were standing by the doors of their tents, and their wives and their children and their dependents.

**16:28** And Moses said, "By this you will know that the Lord sent me to do all these works, that it is not from myself;

**16:29** if these men die according to the death of all people, and if according to the visitation of all people there is a visitation for them, the Lord has not sent me.

**16:30** But the Lord will show it by a portent, and the earth will open its mouth and swallow them up, and their houses and their tents and all that belongs to them, and they will go down alive into Hades, and you will know that these men have provoked the Lord."

**16:31** And when he finished speaking all these words, the earth split open beneath them,

**16:32** and the earth opened and swallowed them up, and their houses, and all the people who were with Korah, and their livestock.

**16:33** And they and all that belonged to them went down alive into Hades, and the earth covered them, and they perished from the midst of the congregation.

**16:34** And all Israel who were around them fled from their cry, for they were saying, "The earth might swallow us up."

**16:35** And fire went out from the Lord and devoured the two hundred and fifty men who were offering the incense.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Greek reads "spoke" (elalesen) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "took," which lacks an explicit object in Hebrew.
- **16:5** — The Greek reads "God has examined" (epeskeptai) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the morning" (boqer), reflecting a verbal reading of the same Hebrew consonants.
- **16:13** — The Greek text reads "into a land" (eis gen) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "out of a land," and verse 14 continues as a contrafactual conditional ("Even if you had brought us... you would have gouged out").
- **16:15** — The Greek reads "desirable thing" (epithymema) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "donkey" (chamor), reflecting a single-consonant difference in the underlying Hebrew.
- **16:26** — The Greek text reads "these harsh men" (skleron) and "with all your belongings" (en pase te apartia hymon); the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "wicked" and "in all their sins," and some Greek manuscripts read hamartia auton ("their sin").
