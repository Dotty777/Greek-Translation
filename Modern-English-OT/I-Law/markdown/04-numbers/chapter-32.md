## Text

**32:1** Now the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad had a multitude of livestock, a very great multitude; and they saw the region of Jazer and the region of Gilead, and the place was a place for livestock.

**32:2** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad approached and spoke to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the congregation, saying,

**32:3** "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sibmah, Nebo, and Beon,

**32:4** the land which the Lord has delivered over before the sons of Israel, is a land for raising livestock, and your servants have livestock."

**32:5** And they said, "If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be given to your household servants as a possession, and do not bring us across the Jordan."

**32:6** And Moses said to the sons of Gad and to the sons of Reuben, "Are your brothers going to war, and will you sit here?

**32:7** And why are you turning aside the minds of the sons of Israel, so that they do not cross over into the land which the Lord is giving to them?

**32:8** Did not your fathers act in this way when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to inspect the land?

**32:9** And they went up to the Valley of the Cluster, and inspected the land, and turned away the heart of the sons of Israel, so that they would not enter into the land which the Lord gave to them.

**32:10** And the Lord became angry with fury on that day, and swore, saying,

**32:11** 'Surely these men who came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, who know evil and good, will not see the land which He swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, for they did not follow closely after Me,

**32:12** except Caleb son of Jephunneh, the one set apart, and Joshua the son of Nun, because he followed closely after the Lord.'

**32:13** And the Lord became angry with fury against Israel, and made them wander around in the wilderness forty years, until the entire generation was consumed, those who were doing evil before the Lord.

**32:14** Look, you have risen up in place of your fathers, a band of sinful men, to add still more to the fury of the anger of the Lord against Israel;

**32:15** for you will turn away from Him, so as once more to leave it in the wilderness, and you will act lawlessly against this whole congregation."

**32:16** And they approached him and said, "Let us build sheepfolds here for our livestock and cities for our dependents,

**32:17** and we ourselves will arm as a vanguard ahead of the sons of Israel, until we bring them to their own place; and our dependents will dwell in walled cities because of those who dwell in the land.

**32:18** We will by no means return to our houses until the sons of Israel have been apportioned, each one to his inheritance.

**32:19** And we will no longer inherit among them from across the Jordan and beyond, because we have received our allotments across the Jordan on the east."

**32:20** And Moses said to them, "If you do according to this word, if you arm yourselves before the Lord for war,

**32:21** and every armed man among you crosses over the Jordan before the Lord until His enemy is wiped out from His presence

**32:22** and the land is subdued before the Lord, then afterward you will return, and you will be guiltless before the Lord and toward Israel, and this land will be yours as a possession before the Lord.

**32:23** But if you do not do so, you will sin before the Lord, and you will know your sin when evils overtake you.

**32:24** And build for yourselves cities for your dependents and folds for your livestock, and what goes out from your mouth, this you shall do."

**32:25** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad spoke to Moses, saying, "Your servants will do just as our lord will command.

**32:26** Our dependents, our wives, and all our livestock will be in the cities of Gilead;

**32:27** but your servants will cross over, all armed and arrayed before the Lord for the war, just as our lord says."

**32:28** And Moses appointed for them Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the ancestral houses of the tribes of Israel,

**32:29** and Moses said to them, "If the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad cross over the Jordan with you, every man armed for war before the Lord, and you subdue the land before you, then you shall give to them the land of Gilead as a possession;

**32:30** but if they do not cross over armed with you for the war before the Lord, bring their dependents, their wives, and their livestock across ahead of you into the land of Canaan, and they will be allotted an inheritance together among you in the land of Canaan."

**32:31** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad answered, saying, "Whatever our lord says to his attendants, so we will do;

**32:32** we will cross over armed before the Lord into the land of Canaan, and you will give the possession to us across the Jordan."

**32:33** And Moses gave to them, to the sons of Gad, to the sons of Reuben, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh of the sons of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land and the cities with its borders, the cities of the land all around.

**32:34** And the sons of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, and Aroer,

**32:35** and Sophar and Jazer, and they raised them up;

**32:36** and Namram and Beth-haran, fortified cities, and sheepfolds.

**32:37** And the sons of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,

**32:38** and Baal-meon, encircled, and Sibmah; and they named the names of the cities which they built according to their own names.

**32:39** And a son of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead and took it, and destroyed the Amorite who dwelt in it.

**32:40** And Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he dwelt there.

**32:41** And Jair the son of Manasseh went and took their settlements, and named them Settlements of Jair.

**32:42** And Nabau went and took Kaath and its villages, and named them Naboth after his own name.

## Translator Notes

- **32:9** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name Eshcol ("cluster") literally as "Valley of the Cluster" (Φάραγγα βότρυος).
- **32:12** — The Greek renders the Hebrew gentilic "the Kenizzite" as ὁ διακεχωρισμένος ("the one set apart") and uses the singular verb συνεπηκολούθησεν ("he followed closely"), following v. 11 where it also includes the Septuagint plus "who know evil and good" and reads third-person "which He swore" before "after Me."
- **32:23** — The Greek reads "you will know your sin when evils overtake you" (ὅταν ὑμᾶς καταλάβῃ τὰ κακά), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "know that your sin will find you out."
- **32:30** — The Greek includes the substantial directive "for the war before the Lord, bring their dependents, their wives, and their livestock across ahead of you into the land of Canaan," absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (here and throughout the chapter ἀποσκευή denotes non-combatant dependents and household belongings). In v. 31, the Greek also reads arthrous ὁ κύριος ("our lord," referring to Moses as in vv. 25 and 27) rather than the Hebrew divine name.
- **32:35** — The Greek reads "Sophar" (Σωφάρ, lacking the element "Atroth-" of Hebrew Atroth-shophan) and renders the Hebrew place name Jogbehah as the verb phrase "and they raised them up" (καὶ ὕψωσαν αὐτάς). Similarly, v. 36 reads "Namram" (Ναμράμ) without "Beth-," and v. 38 omits Nebo while describing the cities as "encircled" (περικεκυκλωμένας).
- **32:42** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper names Nabau (Νάβαυ), Kaath (Καάθ), and Naboth (Νάβωθ) with the plural pronoun "them" (αὐτάς), corresponding to Nobah, Kenath, and Nobah ("it") in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
