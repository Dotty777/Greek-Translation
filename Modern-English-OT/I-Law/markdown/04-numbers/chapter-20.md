## Text

**20:1** And the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there.

**20:2** And there was no water for the congregation, and they gathered together against Moses and Aaron.

**20:3** And the people quarreled with Moses, saying, "If only we had died in the destruction of our brothers before the Lord!

**20:4** And why have you brought up the congregation of the Lord into this wilderness to kill us and our livestock?

**20:5** And why is it that you have brought us up out of Egypt to come to this place? A place where nothing is sown, nor are there figs or vines or pomegranates, nor is there water to drink."

**20:6** And Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the congregation to the door of the tent of testimony, and they fell on their faces; and the glory of the Lord appeared to them.

**20:7** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:8** "Take the staff, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before them, and it will give its waters; and you will bring out water for them from the rock, and you will give drink to the congregation and their livestock."

**20:9** And Moses took the staff that was before the Lord, just as the Lord commanded.

**20:10** And Moses and Aaron assembled the congregation in front of the rock, and he said to them, "Listen to me, you disobedient ones! Shall we bring out water for you from this rock?"

**20:11** And raising his hand, Moses struck the rock with the staff twice, and much water came out, and the congregation and their livestock drank.

**20:12** And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not believe so as to sanctify Me before the sons of Israel, therefore you yourselves will not bring this congregation into the land that I have given them."

**20:13** This is the water of dispute, because the sons of Israel quarreled before the Lord, and He was sanctified among them.

**20:14** And Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom, saying, "This is what your brother Israel says: You yourself know all the hardship that has found us;

**20:15** and our fathers went down to Egypt, and we sojourned in Egypt for many days, and the Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers;

**20:16** and we cried out to the Lord, and the Lord listened to our voice, and sending an angel, He brought us out of Egypt; and now we are in Kadesh, a city at the edge of your borders.

**20:17** We will pass through your land; we will not go through fields or through vineyards, nor will we drink water from your cistern; we will travel by the royal road, and we will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we pass through your borders."

**20:18** And Edom said to him, "You shall not pass through me; otherwise, I will come out in war to meet you."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel said to him, "We will pass along the mountain; and if we drink any of your water, both I and the livestock, I will give you payment; yet the matter is nothing; we will pass along the mountain."

**20:20** But he said, "You shall not pass through me." And Edom came out to meet him with a heavy throng and with a strong hand.

**20:21** And Edom was not willing to allow Israel to pass through his borders; and Israel turned aside from him.

**20:22** And they set out from Kadesh; and the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor.

**20:23** And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron at Mount Hor, at the borders of the land of Edom, saying,

**20:24** "Let Aaron be added to his people, for you shall certainly not enter into the land that I have given to the sons of Israel, because you provoked Me at the water of quarreling.

**20:25** Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up onto Mount Hor before the whole congregation;

**20:26** and strip Aaron of his robe and clothe Eleazar his son with it, and let Aaron be added and die there."

**20:27** And Moses did just as the Lord commanded, and he brought him up onto Mount Hor before the whole congregation.

**20:28** And he stripped Aaron of his garments and clothed Eleazar his son with them; and Aaron died on the top of the mountain, and Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.

**20:29** And the whole congregation saw that Aaron had departed; and the entire house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.

## Translator Notes

- **20:12** — The Greek reads "you did not believe so as to sanctify Me," omitting "in Me" (present in the Hebrew) and taking the infinitive directly as the complement of the verb.
- **20:13** — The Septuagint translates Hebrew Meribah here as "water of dispute" (antilogia) and in 20:24 as "water of quarreling" (loidoria), highlighting Israel's verbal contention.
- **20:19** — Where the Hebrew reads "by the highway" and "on foot," the Greek twice reads "along the mountain" (para to oros).
- **20:24** — The Greek uses the second-person plural ("you shall certainly not enter") addressing both Moses and Aaron, and reads "you provoked Me" rather than the Hebrew "you rebelled against My word."
