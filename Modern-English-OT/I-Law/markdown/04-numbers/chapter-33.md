## Text

**33:1** And these are the stopping places of the sons of Israel, as they went out from the land of Egypt with their force by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

**33:2** And Moses wrote down their departures and their stopping places by the word of the Lord. And these are the stopping places of their journey.

**33:3** They departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the Passover the sons of Israel went out with a high hand before all the Egyptians.

**33:4** And the Egyptians were burying all of their own who had died, whom the Lord had struck, every firstborn in the land of Egypt; and on their gods the Lord executed vengeance.

**33:5** And having departed from Rameses, the sons of Israel encamped at Succoth.

**33:6** And they departed from Succoth and encamped at Buthan, which is part of the wilderness.

**33:7** And they departed from Buthan and encamped at the mouth of Epiroth, which is opposite Baal-zephon, and they encamped opposite Migdol.

**33:8** And they departed from opposite Hiroth and crossed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness; and they traveled a journey of three days through the wilderness and encamped at Bitterness.

**33:9** And they departed from Bitterness and came to Elim; and at Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy trunks of palm trees; and they encamped there beside the water.

**33:10** And they departed from Elim and encamped by the Red Sea.

**33:11** And they departed from the Red Sea and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.

**33:12** And they departed from the wilderness of Sin and encamped at Raphaka.

**33:13** And they departed from Raphaka and encamped at Elim.

**33:14** And they departed from Elim and encamped at Rephidim, and there was no water for the people to drink there.

**33:15** And they departed from Rephidim and encamped in the wilderness of Sinai.

**33:16** And they departed from the wilderness of Sinai and encamped at Graves of Craving.

**33:17** And they departed from Graves of Craving and encamped at Hazeroth.

**33:18** And they departed from Hazeroth and encamped at Rithmah.

**33:19** And they departed from Rithmah and encamped at Rimmon-perez.

**33:20** And they departed from Rimmon-perez and encamped at Lemona.

**33:21** And they departed from Lemona and encamped at Dessa.

**33:22** And they departed from Dessa and encamped at Makellath.

**33:23** And they departed from Makellath and encamped at Shepher.

**33:24** And they departed from Shepher and encamped at Haradah.

**33:25** And they departed from Haradah and encamped at Makheloth.

**33:26** And they departed from Makheloth and encamped at Kataath.

**33:27** And they departed from Kataath and encamped at Tarath.

**33:28** And they departed from Tarath and encamped at Mithkah.

**33:29** And they departed from Mithkah and encamped at Zalmonah.

**33:30** And they departed from Zalmonah and encamped at Moseroth.

**33:31** And they departed from Moseroth and encamped at Banaia.

**33:32** And they departed from Banaia and encamped at Mount Gadgad.

**33:33** And they departed from Mount Gadgad and encamped at Setebatha.

**33:34** And they departed from Setebatha and encamped at Sebrona.

**33:35** And they departed from Sebrona and encamped at Ezion-geber.

**33:36** And they departed from Ezion-geber and encamped in the wilderness of Zin; and they departed from the wilderness of Zin and encamped in the wilderness of Paran, that is, Kadesh.

**33:37** And they departed from Kadesh and encamped at Mount Hor, near the land of Edom.

**33:38** And Aaron the priest went up by the command of the Lord and died there in the fortieth year of the exodus of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first of the month.

**33:39** And Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**33:40** And the Canaanite, king of Arad, heard—and he was dwelling in the land of Canaan—when the sons of Israel were entering.

**33:41** And they departed from Mount Hor and encamped at Zalmonah.

**33:42** And they departed from Zalmonah and encamped at Punon.

**33:43** And they departed from Punon and encamped at Soboth.

**33:44** And they departed from Soboth and encamped at Gai, on the other side, at the borders of Moab.

**33:45** And they departed from Gai and encamped at Dibon-gad.

**33:46** And they departed from Dibon-gad and encamped at Almon-diblathaim.

**33:47** And they departed from Almon-diblathaim and encamped on the mountains of Abarim, opposite Nebo.

**33:48** And they departed from the mountains of Abarim and encamped on the west of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho.

**33:49** And they encamped beside the Jordan between Aesimoth as far as Belsa, which is toward the west of Moab.

**33:50** And the Lord spoke to Moses on the west of Moab, beside the Jordan across from Jericho, saying,

**33:51** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'You are crossing the Jordan into the land of Canaan.

**33:52** And you shall destroy all those dwelling in the land before you, and you shall remove their watchtowers, and all their cast idols you shall destroy, and all their pillars you shall remove.

**33:53** And you shall destroy all those dwelling in the land and you shall dwell in it, for to you I have given their land as an allotment.

**33:54** And you shall inherit their land by lot according to their tribes; to the more numerous you shall increase their possession, and to the fewer you shall decrease their possession; wherever his name comes out there, it shall be his; according to the tribes of your families you shall inherit.

**33:55** But if you do not destroy those dwelling on the land from before you, then those of them whom you leave behind will be thorns in your eyes and darts in your sides, and they will be hostile on the land on which you will dwell.

**33:56** And it will be that just as I had determined to do to them, I will do to you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — The Greek itinerary preserves several Septuagint-distinct place names and literal translations where the Hebrew differs, including Buthan (vv. 6-7, Hebrew Etham), the mouth of Epiroth and Hiroth (vv. 7-8, Pi-hahiroth), Bitterness (vv. 8-9, Marah), Raphaka (vv. 12-13, Dophkah), Elim repeated here instead of Hebrew Alush (vv. 13-14), and Graves of Craving (vv. 16-17, Kibroth-hattaavah).
- **33:20** — Several wilderness station names in vv. 20-35 follow distinct Septuagint forms rather than the Masoretic Hebrew: Lemona (Libnah), Dessa (Rissah), Makellath (Kehelathah), Shepher without Mount (Mount Shepher), Kataath (Tahath), Tarath (Terah), Zalmonah repeated from vv. 41-42 (Hashmonah), Banaia (Bene-jaakan), Mount Gadgad (Hor-haggidgad), Setebatha (Jotbathah), and Sebrona (Abronah).
- **33:36** — Unlike the Hebrew, which identifies the wilderness of Zin directly with Kadesh, the Septuagint adds an extra stage from the wilderness of Zin to the wilderness of Paran (Kadesh); in v. 38 it omits Mount Hor, and in v. 40 it omits in the Negev while leaving heard as a suspended participle.
- **33:48** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew plains of Moab as west of Moab (vv. 48-50, connecting Hebrew araboth with evening/west) and preserves distinct final station forms: Soboth (vv. 43-44, Oboth), Gai on the other side (vv. 44-45, Iye-abarim), and between Aesimoth as far as Belsa (v. 49, from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim).
- **33:52** — In the closing command (vv. 52-55), the Septuagint translates Hebrew drive out/dispossess as destroy, figured stones as watchtowers, high places as pillars, the lot as his name (v. 54, along with their tribes instead of your clans), and thorns in your sides as darts in your sides (v. 55).
