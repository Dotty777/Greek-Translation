## Text

**14:1** Then the whole congregation raised and let out a cry, and the people wept all that night.

**14:2** And all the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole congregation said to them,

**14:3** "If only we had died in the land of Egypt, or if only we had died in this wilderness! And why is the Lord bringing us into this land to fall in war? Our wives and little ones will become plunder. So now it is better for us to turn back to Egypt."

**14:4** And they said to one another, "Let us appoint a leader and turn back to Egypt."

**14:5** Then Moses and Aaron fell facedown before the whole congregation of the sons of Israel.

**14:6** And Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, from among those who had spied out the land, tore their garments.

**14:7** And they spoke to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "The land that we spied out is exceedingly, exceedingly good.

**14:8** If the Lord favors us, He will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land that is flowing with milk and honey.

**14:9** Only do not become rebels against the Lord; and you, do not fear the people of the land, for they are food for us. For the opportunity has departed from them, but the Lord is among us; do not fear them."

**14:10** And the whole congregation said to stone them. And the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud over the tent of testimony to all the sons of Israel.

**14:11** Then the Lord said to Moses, "How long will this people provoke Me? And how long will they not believe Me in spite of all the signs that I have performed among them?

**14:12** I will strike them with death and destroy them, and I will make you and the house of your father into a nation great and more numerous than this one."

**14:13** And Moses said to the Lord, "Then Egypt will hear, for You brought this people out from among them by Your strength;

**14:14** moreover, all those who dwell on this land have also heard that You are the Lord among this people—You who are seen eye to eye, Lord. And Your cloud stands over them, and in a pillar of cloud You go along ahead of them by day, and in a pillar of fire by night.

**14:15** And You will wipe out this people like one man, and the nations, all who have heard Your name, will speak, saying,

**14:16** 'Because the Lord was not able to bring this people into the land that He swore to them, He laid them low in the wilderness.'

**14:17** And now, let Your strength be exalted, Lord, just as You spoke, saying,

**14:18** 'The Lord is slow to anger and abundant in mercy and true, taking away lawless deeds and injustices and sins; yet He will by no means clear the guilty, repaying sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation.'

**14:19** Forgive the sin of this people according to Your great mercy, just as You have been gracious to them from Egypt until now."

**14:20** And the Lord said to Moses, "I am gracious to them according to your word.

**14:21** Yet as I live and as My name lives, the glory of the Lord will fill all the earth.

**14:22** For all the men who have seen My glory and the signs that I performed in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have tested Me this tenth time and have not listened to My voice,

**14:23** they will certainly not see the land that I swore to their fathers. Rather, their children who are with Me here, all who do not know good or evil, every inexperienced youth—to these I will give the land; but all who provoked Me will not see it.

**14:24** But as for My servant Caleb, because there was a different spirit in him and he followed closely after Me, I will bring him into the land that he entered, and his seed will inherit it.

**14:25** Now Amalek and the Canaanite dwell in the valley. Tomorrow turn back and set out into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea."

**14:26** Then the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**14:27** "How long must I bear with this evil congregation? They are grumbling before Me. I have heard the grumbling of the sons of Israel, which they grumbled about you.

**14:28** Say to them, 'As I live, says the Lord, surely just as you have spoken in My ears, so I will do to you:

**14:29** In this wilderness your corpses will fall, and all your census and those of you who were numbered, from twenty years old and upward, all who grumbled against Me.

**14:30** You will certainly not enter the land concerning which I stretched out My hand to settle you upon it, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

**14:31** But as for the little ones, who you said would be plunder, I will bring them into the land, and they will inherit the land from which you turned away.

**14:32** And your corpses will fall in this wilderness.

**14:33** And your sons will be pasturing in the wilderness for forty years, and they will bear your prostitution until your corpses are consumed in the wilderness.

**14:34** According to the number of the days that you spied out the land, forty days—a day for a year—you will bear your sins for forty years, and you will know the fury of My wrath.

**14:35** I, the Lord, have spoken: surely I will do so to this evil congregation that has banded together against Me; in this wilderness they will be completely consumed, and there they will die.'"

**14:36** And the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land, who upon returning grumbled against it to the congregation, putting forth evil words about the land—

**14:37** the men who spoke evil things against the land died in the plague before the Lord.

**14:38** But Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh remained alive from among those men who had gone to spy out the land.

**14:39** Then Moses spoke these words to all the sons of Israel, and the people mourned greatly.

**14:40** And rising early in the morning, they went up to the top of the mountain, saying, "Look, here we are; we will go up to the place of which the Lord spoke, for we have sinned."

**14:41** And Moses said, "Why are you transgressing the word of the Lord? It will not go well for you.

**14:42** Do not go up, for the Lord is not with you, and you will fall before your enemies.

**14:43** For Amalek and the Canaanite are there in front of you, and you will fall by the sword; because you have turned away, disobeying the Lord, the Lord will not be among you."

**14:44** Yet forcing their way forward, they went up to the top of the mountain; but the ark of the covenant of the Lord and Moses did not move from the camp.

**14:45** Then Amalek and the Canaanite who was settled in that mountain came down, and they routed them and cut them down as far as Hormah; and they turned back to the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **14:9** — The Greek reads "the opportunity" (ὁ καιρός, also "season" or "favorable time"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "their shadow" (i.e., protection).
- **14:18** — Greek katharismo ou kathariei literally reads "with cleansing He will not cleanse," a forensic idiom for "will by no means clear/acquit" (compare Exod 20:7; 34:7; Deut 5:11).
- **14:23** — The Greek includes the extended promise granting the land to the children who do not yet know good or evil (paralleling Deuteronomy 1:39), a clause absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text here.
- **14:27** — The Greek reads "about you" (περὶ ὑμῶν, addressed to Moses and Aaron), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "against Me."
- **14:33** — The Greek middle/passive participle νεμόμενοι ("pasturing" or "grazing") portrays the next generation subsisting in the wilderness like a flock, where the Hebrew has "shepherds."
- **14:45** — The Greek renders Hormah as Ἑρμάν (Herman) and concludes with the clause "and they turned back to the camp," which is not in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
