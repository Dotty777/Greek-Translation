## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Avenge the vengeance of the sons of Israel on the Midianites, and afterward you will be added to your people."

**31:3** And Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Arm men from among you to draw up for battle before the Lord against Midian, to render vengeance from the Lord to Midian.

**31:4** A thousand from a tribe, a thousand from a tribe; from all the tribes of Israel send them to draw up for battle."

**31:5** And they counted out from the thousands of Israel a thousand from a tribe: twelve thousand, armed for battle array.

**31:6** And Moses sent them out, a thousand from a tribe, a thousand from a tribe, with their force, and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest; and the holy vessels and the trumpets of signaling were in their hands.

**31:7** And they drew up for battle against Midian, just as the Lord commanded Moses, and they killed every male.

**31:8** And they killed the kings of Midian along with their slain: Evi, Zur, Rekem, Hur, and Reba, five kings of Midian; and they killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword together with their slain.

**31:9** And they took as plunder the women of Midian and their dependents, and their livestock and all their possessions and their wealth they plundered.

**31:10** And they burned with fire all their cities in their dwellings, and their encampments.

**31:11** And they took all the plunder and all their spoils, from human to animal.

**31:12** And they brought to Moses and to Eleazar the priest and to all the sons of Israel the captives and the spoils and the plunder, to the camp at Araboth of Moab, which is by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**31:13** And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp.

**31:14** And Moses became angry with the overseers of the force, commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, who were coming from the battle array of the war.

**31:15** And Moses said to them, "Why have you kept every female alive?

**31:16** For these were to the sons of Israel, according to the word of Balaam, a cause to turn away and disregard the word of the Lord because of Peor, and the plague occurred in the congregation of the Lord.

**31:17** And now kill every male among all the dependents, and kill every woman who has known the bed of a male.

**31:18** All the dependents among the women who have not known the bed of a male, keep them alive.

**31:19** And you, encamp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has killed and whoever touches the slain shall be purified on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives;

**31:20** and every garment and every leather article and every work of goat's hair and every wooden article you shall purify."

**31:21** And Eleazar the priest said to the men of the force who were coming from the battle array of the war, "This is the ordinance of the law that the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:22** Besides the gold and the silver and bronze and iron and lead and tin,

**31:23** every item shall pass through fire, and it shall be cleansed; yet it shall be purified with the water of purification. And all things that do not pass through fire shall pass through water.

**31:24** And you shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you shall be cleansed; and after these things you shall enter the camp."

**31:25** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:26** "Take the total of the spoils of the captivity, from human to animal, you and Eleazar the priest and the leaders of the ancestral houses of the congregation;

**31:27** and you shall divide the spoils between the warriors who go out to the battle array and all the congregation.

**31:28** And you shall set apart a tribute for the Lord from the fighting men who have gone out to the battle array: one soul out of five hundred, from the people and from the livestock and from the cattle and from the sheep and from the goats;

**31:29** and from their half you shall take and give to Eleazar the priest the firstfruits of the Lord;

**31:30** and from the half belonging to the sons of Israel, you shall take one out of fifty, from the people and from the cattle and from the sheep and from the donkeys and from all the livestock, and you shall give them to the Levites who keep the watches in the tabernacle of the Lord."

**31:31** And Moses and Eleazar the priest did just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:32** And the remainder of the plunder that the fighting men had plundered was, from the sheep, six hundred seventy-five thousand;

**31:33** and cattle, seventy-two thousand;

**31:34** donkeys, sixty-one thousand;

**31:35** and human souls, from the women who had not known the bed of a man, all the souls: thirty-two thousand.

**31:36** And the half-share, the portion of those who had gone out to the war, was, from the number of the sheep, three hundred thirty-five thousand five hundred.

**31:37** And the tribute for the Lord from the sheep was six hundred seventy-five;

**31:38** and cattle, thirty-six thousand, and the tribute for the Lord, seventy-two;

**31:39** and donkeys, thirty thousand five hundred, and the tribute for the Lord, sixty-one;

**31:40** and human souls, sixteen thousand, and their tribute for the Lord, thirty-two souls.

**31:41** And Moses gave the tribute for the Lord, the set-apart offering of God, to Eleazar the priest, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:42** From the half-share of the sons of Israel, whom Moses separated from the fighting men;

**31:43** and the half-share from the congregation was, from the sheep, three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred,

**31:44** and cattle, thirty-six thousand,

**31:45** donkeys, thirty thousand five hundred,

**31:46** and human souls, sixteen thousand.

**31:47** And Moses took from the half-share of the sons of Israel the one out of fifty, from the people and from the livestock, and gave them to the Levites who keep the watches of the tabernacle of the Lord, in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

**31:48** And all those appointed over the divisions of thousands of the force, commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, approached Moses,

**31:49** and they said to Moses, "Your servants have taken the total of the fighting men who are with us, and not even one of them is missing;

**31:50** and we have brought the gift for the Lord, whatever gold article each man found, bangle and bracelet and ring and armband and braided ornament, to make atonement for us before the Lord."

**31:51** And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, every crafted article.

**31:52** And all the gold, the set-apart offering that they set apart for the Lord, was sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty shekels, from the commanders of thousands and from the commanders of hundreds.

**31:53** And the fighting men plundered, each for himself.

**31:54** And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of thousands and from the commanders of hundreds, and brought it into the tabernacle of the testimony, a memorial of the sons of Israel before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **31:6** — The Greek repeats 'a thousand from a tribe,' adds 'son of Aaron,' and reads plural 'in their hands' where the Hebrew MT reads singular 'in his hand.'
- **31:12** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew 'arboth' ('plains') as the proper place-name 'Araboth of Moab,' and reads 'all the sons of Israel' where the Hebrew MT has 'the congregation of the sons of Israel.'
- **31:17** — Greek 'apartia' literally means movable household goods or baggage, used here and in v. 18 (like 'aposkeue' in v. 9) to designate dependents or children.
- **31:22** — Greek 'plen' ('besides, apart from') with the genitive renders Hebrew 'ak' ('only') and reverses the order of the last two metals to 'lead and tin'; v. 23 then condenses the clause to 'every item shall pass through fire.'
- **31:36** — The Greek reads 335,500 ('five thousand' rather than 'seven thousand'), whereas v. 43 and the Hebrew MT read 337,500 (matching the tribute of 675 in v. 37).
