## Text

**15:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel

**15:2** and you shall say to them, 'When you enter the land of your dwelling, which I am giving to you,

**15:3** and you make burnt offerings to the Lord, a whole offering or a sacrifice, to magnify a vow or voluntarily, or at your feasts to make an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord, whether from the cattle or from the sheep,

**15:4** then the one presenting his gift to the Lord shall present a sacrifice of fine flour, a tenth of an ephah, mixed with oil, a fourth of a hin;

**15:5** and you shall prepare wine for a drink offering, a fourth of a hin, with the burnt offering or with the sacrifice; for each lamb you shall prepare this much, an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**15:6** And for a ram, when you prepare it either for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice, you shall prepare a sacrifice of fine flour, two tenths, mixed with oil, a third of a hin;

**15:7** and you shall present wine for a drink offering, a third of a hin, for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**15:8** Now if you prepare from the cattle for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice, to magnify a vow for a deliverance offering to the Lord,

**15:9** then he shall present with the calf a sacrifice of fine flour, three tenths, mixed with oil, half of a hin,

**15:10** an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**15:11** In this way you shall do for each calf, or for each ram, or for each lamb from the sheep or from the goats.

**15:12** According to the number of those that you prepare, so you shall do for each one according to their number.

**15:13** Every native-born person shall do such things in this way, to present offerings for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**15:14** And if a resident alien comes among you in your land, or whoever comes to be among you in your generations, and makes an offering, an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord, in the way that you do, so the congregation shall do to the Lord.

**15:15** There shall be one law for you and for the resident aliens dwelling among you, an eternal law throughout your generations; as you are, the resident alien shall also be before the Lord.

**15:16** There shall be one law and there shall be one ordinance also for the resident alien dwelling among you.'"

**15:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:18** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, 'When you enter the land into which I am bringing you,

**15:19** then it shall be that when you eat from the bread of the land, you shall set aside a contribution, a set-apart portion to the Lord.

**15:20** As the firstfruits of your dough, you shall set apart a loaf as a contribution; like a contribution from a threshing floor, so you shall set it aside,

**15:21** the firstfruits of your dough; and you shall give to the Lord a contribution throughout your generations.

**15:22** Now when you err and do not do all these commandments that the Lord spoke to Moses,

**15:23** just as the Lord commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day on which the Lord commanded you and onward throughout your generations,

**15:24** then it shall be, if it is done unintentionally away from the eyes of the congregation, that the entire congregation shall prepare one unblemished calf from the cattle for a burnt offering, for an aroma of sweet fragrance to the Lord, and its sacrifice and its drink offering according to the regulation, and one male goat from the goats for sin.

**15:25** And the priest shall make atonement for it, for the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them, because it is unintentional; and they themselves brought their gift, an offering to the Lord for their sin before the Lord, for their unintentional acts.

**15:26** And it shall be forgiven throughout the entire congregation of the sons of Israel and to the resident alien dwelling with you, because for all the people it is unintentional.

**15:27** And if one person sins unintentionally, he shall bring one year-old female goat for sin;

**15:28** and the priest shall make atonement for the person who acted unintentionally and sinned unintentionally before the Lord, to make atonement for him.

**15:29** For the native among the sons of Israel and for the resident alien dwelling among them, there shall be one law for them, whoever acts unintentionally.

**15:30** And a person who acts with a hand of arrogance, from the native-born or from the resident aliens, this one provokes God; that person shall be destroyed from among his people.

**15:31** Because he scorned the words of the Lord and broke His commandments, that person shall be completely wiped out; his sin is in him.'"

**15:32** And the sons of Israel were in the wilderness, and they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.

**15:33** And those who found him gathering wood on the Sabbath day brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel.

**15:34** And they placed him in custody, for they had not decided what they should do to him.

**15:35** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "The man shall surely be put to death; stone him with stones, all the congregation."

**15:36** And the entire congregation brought him outside the camp, and the entire congregation stoned him with stones outside the camp, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**15:37** And the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**15:38** "Speak to the sons of Israel and you shall say to them, and let them make for themselves tassels on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and you shall put a blue cord on the tassels of the corners.

**15:39** And it shall be for you in the tassels, and you shall look at them, and you shall remember all the commandments of the Lord and do them, and you shall not turn aside after your thoughts and eyes, after which you prostitute yourselves, so that you may remember and do all My commandments,

**15:40** and you shall be holy to your God.

**15:41** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God; I am the Lord your God."

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The phrase "to magnify a vow" (also in 15:8) translates the Greek literally, reflecting a distinctive Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew idiom for making or fulfilling a special vow.
- **15:10** — The Greek text omits the prescription of half a hin of wine for a drink offering found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, likely through an early scribal omission after "half of a hin" at the end of v. 9.
- **15:14** — The Greek text places "the congregation" at the end of v. 14 as the subject of the final clause ("so the congregation shall do to the Lord"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places "the assembly" at the beginning of v. 15.
- **15:30** — "With a hand of arrogance" is a literal rendering of the Greek idiom for defiant or presumptuous action; the Greek also reads "provokes God" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "blasphemes the Lord."
- **15:39** — Following the Greek verse division, "so that you may remember and do all My commandments" concludes v. 39 rather than opening v. 40 as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and "it shall be for you in the tassels" refers to the blue cord placed in them.
