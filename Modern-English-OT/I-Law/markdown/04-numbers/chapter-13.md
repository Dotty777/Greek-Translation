## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Send men for yourself, and let them scout out the land of the Canaanites, which I am giving to the sons of Israel for a possession; sending them one man for each tribe according to the clans of their fathers' houses, every one a leader from among them."

**13:3** And Moses sent them out from the wilderness of Paran by the voice of the Lord; all of these men were leaders of the sons of Israel.

**13:4** And these are their names: of the tribe of Reuben, Samuel son of Zaccur;

**13:5** of the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Suri;

**13:6** of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**13:7** of the tribe of Issachar, Ilaal son of Joseph;

**13:8** of the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;

**13:9** of the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;

**13:10** of the tribe of Zebulun, Gudiel son of Sodi;

**13:11** of the tribe of Joseph, of the sons of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi;

**13:12** of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gamai;

**13:13** of the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;

**13:14** of the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Jabi;

**13:15** of the tribe of Gad, Gudiel son of Maki.

**13:16** These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to scout out the land. And Moses named Hoshea son of Nun Joshua.

**13:17** And Moses sent them to scout out the land of Canaan and said to them, "Go up through this wilderness, and you will go up into the mountain;

**13:18** and you will see the land, what it is like, and the people who are settled on it, whether they are stronger or weak, whether they are few or many;

**13:19** and what the land is like on which they are settled, whether it is good or bad; and what the cities are like in which they dwell, whether in walled ones or in unwalled ones;

**13:20** and what the land is like, whether rich or depleted, whether there are trees in it or not. And persevere and take from the fruits of the land." Now the days were days of spring, forerunners of the grape.

**13:21** And they went up and scouted out the land from the wilderness of Zin to Rahab, as one enters Hephaath.

**13:22** And they went up through the wilderness and came as far as Hebron, and there were Ahiman, Sheshai, and Thelamin, offspring of Anak. Now Hebron was built seven years before Tanis of Egypt.

**13:23** And they came as far as the Valley of the Cluster and scouted it out; and they cut from there vine branches and one cluster of grapes on it, and they carried it on poles, and some of the pomegranates and some of the cucumbers.

**13:24** They named that place Valley of the Cluster, because of the cluster that the sons of Israel cut from there.

**13:25** And they returned from there, having scouted out the land, after forty days.

**13:26** And they went and came to Moses and Aaron and to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, into the wilderness of Paran, Kadesh; and they answered them and the whole congregation with a word, and showed the fruit of the land.

**13:27** And they related to him and said, "We came into the land to which you sent us, a land flowing with milk and honey, and this is its fruit;

**13:28** except that the nation that dwells on it is bold, and the cities are fortified, walled, and very large, and we have seen the offspring of Anak there;

**13:29** and Amalek dwells in the land toward the south, and the Hittite, the Hivite, the Jebusite, and the Amorite dwell in the hill country, and the Canaanite dwells beside the sea."

**13:30** And Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said to him, "No, rather we will surely go up and inherit it, for we will certainly prevail against them."

**13:31** And the men who had gone up with him said, "We are not going up, because we will by no means be able to go up against the nation, for it is much stronger than we are."

**13:32** And they brought out to the sons of Israel dismay concerning the land that they had scouted out, saying, "The land through which we passed to scout it out is a land that devours those who dwell on it; all the people whom we have seen in it are exceedingly tall men there;

**13:33** and there we have seen the giants, and we were before them like locusts; and indeed, even so we were before them."

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — Several names in the Septuagint scout list (vv. 4-15) differ genuinely from the Hebrew text: Samuel (v. 4, Heb. Shammua), Suri (v. 5, Heb. Hori), Ilaal (v. 7, Heb. Igal), Naue (vv. 8, 16, Heb. Nun), Gudiel (vv. 10, 15, Heb. Gaddiel and Geuel), Gamai (v. 12, Heb. Gemalli), and Jabi (v. 14, Heb. Vophsi).
- **13:21** — The Greek text reads Rhaab ("Rahab") and Hephaath ("Hephaath") where the Hebrew text reads "Rehob" and "Hamath" (Lebo-hamath).
- **13:22** — The Greek text reads Thelamin (Heb. Talmai) and uses the Greek name Tanin ("Tanis") for the Egyptian city called "Zoan" in the Hebrew text.
- **13:23** — The Greek text translates the place name Eshcol literally as Pharanx botryos ("Valley of the Cluster," vv. 23-24), adds "and scouted it out," and reads sikyon ("cucumbers") instead of sykon ("figs," as in Hebrew).
- **13:29** — The Greek text includes "the Hivite" among the hill-country inhabitants and omits "and along the Jordan" at the end of the verse; in v. 32 it reads ekstasin ("dismay") rather than "bad report," and in v. 33 repeats "before them" twice without "in our own sight."
