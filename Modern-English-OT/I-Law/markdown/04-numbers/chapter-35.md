## Text

**35:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses at the west of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,

**35:2** "Instruct the sons of Israel, and they shall give to the Levites from the allotments of their possession cities to live in, and they shall give to the Levites the outskirts of the cities all around them;

**35:3** and the cities shall be for them to live in, and their set-apart grounds shall be for their livestock and for all their four-footed animals.

**35:4** And the adjoining areas of the cities which you shall give to the Levites shall be from the wall of the city and outward two thousand cubits all around;

**35:5** and you shall measure outside the city the side toward the east, two thousand cubits, and the side toward the south, two thousand cubits, and the side toward the sea, two thousand cubits, and the side toward the north, two thousand cubits; and the city in the middle of this shall be for you, and the bordering areas of the cities.

**35:6** And you shall give the cities to the Levites: the six cities of refuge which you shall give for the killer to flee there, and in addition to these, forty-two cities;

**35:7** you shall give all the cities to the Levites, even forty-eight cities, these and their outskirts.

**35:8** And as for the cities which you shall give from the possession of the sons of Israel: from those who have many, many, and from those who are fewer, fewer; each according to his inheritance which they shall inherit, they shall give from the cities to the Levites."

**35:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**35:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You are crossing the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**35:11** and you shall set apart cities for yourselves; they shall be places of refuge for you, for the killer to flee there, everyone who strikes down a person unintentionally.

**35:12** And the cities shall be places of refuge for you from the kinsman-avenger of blood, and the killer shall certainly not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

**35:13** And the cities which you shall give, the six cities, shall be places of refuge for you;

**35:14** three cities you shall give beyond the Jordan, and the three cities you shall give in the land of Canaan.

**35:15** It shall be a refuge for the sons of Israel, and for the proselyte, the resident alien who is among you; these cities shall be for a place of refuge, for everyone who strikes down a person unintentionally to flee there.

**35:16** But if he strikes him with an iron implement and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death.

**35:17** And if he strikes him with a stone from the hand, by which he would die, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death.

**35:18** And if he strikes him with a wooden implement from the hand, from which he would die, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death;

**35:19** the kinsman-avenger of blood himself shall kill the killer; when he himself meets him, he shall kill him.

**35:20** And if out of enmity he pushes him and throws any implement at him from ambush, and he dies,

**35:21** or out of wrath he strikes him with his hand, and he dies, the one who struck him must surely be put to death; he is a murderer; the killer must surely be put to death; the kinsman-avenger of blood shall kill the killer when he meets him.

**35:22** But if suddenly, not out of enmity, he pushes him or throws any implement at him not from ambush,

**35:23** or with any stone by which he would die, without knowing, and it falls upon him, and he dies, though he was not his enemy nor seeking to harm him,

**35:24** then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the kinsman-avenger of blood according to these judgments.

**35:25** And the congregation shall rescue the killer from the kinsman-avenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge where he fled for refuge; and he shall live there until the high priest dies, whom they anointed with the holy oil.

**35:26** But if the killer ever goes out beyond the boundaries of the city to which he fled for refuge,

**35:27** and the kinsman-avenger of blood finds him outside the boundaries of the city of his refuge, and the kinsman-avenger of blood kills the killer, he is not guilty;

**35:28** for he must live in the city of refuge until the high priest dies; after the high priest dies, the killer shall return to the land of his possession.

**35:29** And these things shall be for you as a statute of judgment throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.

**35:30** As for everyone who strikes down a person, by witnesses you shall kill the killer, and one witness shall not testify against a person so that he dies;

**35:31** and you shall not take a ransom for the life from the killer who is liable to be executed, for he shall surely be put to death.

**35:32** You shall not take a ransom for fleeing to a city of refuge, to live again on the land until the high priest dies.

**35:33** And you shall certainly not stain with murder the land in which you live, for this blood stains the land with murder; and the land shall not be atoned for from the blood that was shed upon it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**35:34** And you shall not defile the land upon which you live, upon which I will dwell among you; for I am the Lord who dwells in the midst of the sons of Israel.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:1** — Greek epi dysmon Moab ("at the west of Moab") reflects interpreting Hebrew arbot ("plains") in connection with ereb ("west/sunset").
- **35:4** — The Greek specifies "two thousand cubits" here (matching v. 5), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "one thousand cubits" in v. 4.
- **35:21** — The Greek repeats the capital sentence ("the killer must surely be put to death") after "he is a murderer," an expansion absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **35:32** — Greek tou phygein ("for fleeing") renders the Hebrew verb as an infinitive rather than a participle ("for one who has fled"), and specifies "the high priest" where the Hebrew has "the priest."
- **35:33** — Greek phonoktoneo ("stain with murder") is a rare compound verb used here to emphasize pollution specifically through bloodshed, distinct from miaino ("defile") in v. 34.
