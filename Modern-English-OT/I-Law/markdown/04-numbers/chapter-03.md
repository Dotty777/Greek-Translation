## Text

**3:1** And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day when the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.

**3:2** And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: the firstborn, Nadab, and Abiud, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**3:3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whose hands were consecrated to serve as priests.

**3:4** And Nadab and Abiud died before the Lord while they were offering strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children; and Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests with Aaron their father.

**3:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:6** "Take the tribe of Levi, and you shall set them before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him.

**3:7** And they shall keep his charges and the charges of the sons of Israel before the tabernacle of testimony, to perform the works of the tabernacle;

**3:8** and they shall keep all the articles of the tabernacle of testimony and the charges of the sons of Israel, according to all the works of the tabernacle.

**3:9** And you shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons the priests; these are given as a gift to Me from the sons of Israel.

**3:10** And you shall appoint Aaron and his sons over the tabernacle of testimony, and they shall keep their priesthood and all things pertaining to the altar and inside the curtain; and the outsider who touches it shall die."

**3:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:12** "And look, I have taken the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb from the sons of Israel; they shall be their ransom, and the Levites shall be Mine.

**3:13** For every firstborn is Mine; on the day when I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified to Myself every firstborn in Israel; from man to livestock, they shall be Mine; I am the Lord."

**3:14** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

**3:15** "Enroll the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses, according to their clans; every male from a month old and upward, enroll them."

**3:16** And Moses and Aaron enrolled them by the voice of the Lord, just as the Lord directed them.

**3:17** And these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gedson, Kohath, and Merari.

**3:18** And these are the names of the sons of Gedson according to their clans: Libni and Shimei.

**3:19** And the sons of Kohath according to their clans: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**3:20** And the sons of Merari according to their clans: Mahli and Omushi. These are the clans of the Levites according to their fathers' houses.

**3:21** To Gedson belonged the clan of Libni and the clan of Shimei; these are the clans of Gedson.

**3:22** Their enrollment according to the number of every male from a month old and upward, their enrollment was seven thousand five hundred.

**3:23** And the sons of Gedson shall encamp behind the tabernacle toward the sea;

**3:24** and the leader of the father's house of the clan of Gedson was Eliasaph son of Dael.

**3:25** And the charge of the sons of Gedson in the tabernacle of testimony was the tabernacle and the covering, and the screen of the door of the tabernacle of testimony,

**3:26** and the hangings of the courtyard, and the curtain of the gate of the courtyard that is by the tabernacle, and the remainder of all its works.

**3:27** To Kohath belonged the Amramite clan, and the Izharite clan, and the Hebronite clan, and the Uzzielite clan; these are the clans of Kohath according to number,

**3:28** every male from a month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, keeping the charges of the holy things.

**3:29** The clans of the sons of Kohath shall encamp on the side of the tabernacle toward the south;

**3:30** and the leader of the fathers' house of the clans of Kohath was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

**3:31** And their charge was the ark and the table and the lampstand and the altars, and the articles of the sanctuary, all those with which they minister, and the screen and all their works.

**3:32** And the leader over the leaders of the Levites was Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, appointed to keep the charges of the holy things.

**3:33** To Merari belonged the Mahlite clan and the Mushite clan; these are the clans of Merari.

**3:34** Their enrollment according to number, every male from a month old and upward, was six thousand and fifty;

**3:35** and the leader of the father's house of the clan of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail; they shall encamp on the side of the tabernacle toward the north.

**3:36** The oversight, the charge of the sons of Merari was the capitals of the tabernacle and its bars and its pillars and its bases, and all their articles and their works,

**3:37** and the pillars of the courtyard all around and their bases, and the pegs and their cords.

**3:38** And those who encamp in front of the tabernacle of testimony on the east are Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charges of the sanctuary for the charges of the sons of Israel; and the outsider who touches it shall die.

**3:39** The entire enrollment of the Levites whom Moses and Aaron enrolled by the voice of the Lord according to their clans, every male from a month old and upward, was twenty-two thousand.

**3:40** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Enroll every firstborn male of the sons of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number by name;

**3:41** and you shall take the Levites for Me—I am the Lord—in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the sons of Israel."

**3:42** And Moses enrolled, just as the Lord commanded, every firstborn among the sons of Israel;

**3:43** and all the firstborn males, according to number by name from a month old and upward from their enrollment, came to twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.

**3:44** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:45** "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock, and the Levites shall be Mine; I am the Lord.

**3:46** And as for the ransom—the two hundred and seventy-three who are in excess of the Levites from the firstborn of the sons of Israel—

**3:47** then you shall take five shekels per head; according to the holy didrachma you shall take them, twenty obols to the shekel;

**3:48** and you shall give the silver to Aaron and to his sons as the ransom of those who are in excess among them."

**3:49** And Moses took the silver, the ransom of those who were in excess, for the redemption of the Levites;

**3:50** from the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the silver, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five shekels, according to the holy shekel.

**3:51** And Moses gave the ransom of those who were in excess to Aaron and to his sons by the voice of the Lord, just as the Lord directed Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Greek reads Abiud (also in 3:4), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Abihu.
- **3:17** — Greek consistently reads Gedson throughout this chapter (a dalet/resh variation), corresponding to Hebrew Gershon.
- **3:20** — Greek reads Omushi (reflecting the Hebrew conjunction u- transliterated onto the name), whereas the clan name in 3:33 is Mushi (Mushite).
- **3:24** — Greek reads Dael (from a delta/lambda or dalet/lamed interchange), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Lael.
- **3:34** — Greek gives the Merarite count as 6,050, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 6,200; in both traditions the sum of the three clans exceeds the stated total of 22,000 in 3:39.
- **3:47** — Greek renders the sanctuary weight standard with Ptolemaic monetary terms ("holy didrachma" and "twenty obols to the shekel"), where the Hebrew reads "shekel of the sanctuary" and "twenty gerahs to the shekel."
