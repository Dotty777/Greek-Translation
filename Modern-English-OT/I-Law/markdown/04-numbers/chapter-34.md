## Text

**34:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'You are entering the land of Canaan; this shall be for you as the inheritance, the land of Canaan with its boundaries.

**34:3** And the side toward the south shall be for you from the wilderness of Zin as far as bordering Edom, and the boundary toward the south shall be for you from the edge of the Salt Sea on the east;

**34:4** and the boundary shall turn around you from the south toward the ascent of Akrabbim, and pass by Ennak, and its outlet shall be south of Kadesh-barnea; and it shall go out to the village of Arad, and pass by Azmon.

**34:5** And the boundary shall turn around from Azmon to the brook of Egypt, and the outlet shall be the sea.

**34:6** And the boundary of the sea shall be for you: the Great Sea shall mark the boundary; this shall be for you the boundary of the sea.

**34:7** And this shall be the boundary for you toward the north: from the Great Sea you shall measure out for yourselves alongside the mountain, the mountain;

**34:8** and from the mountain, the mountain you shall measure out for them as they enter Hamath, and their outlet shall be the boundary of Saradak;

**34:9** and the boundary shall go out to Dephrona, and its outlet shall be Harsenaim; this shall be a boundary for you on the north.

**34:10** And you shall measure out for yourselves the boundary of the east from Harsenain to Sepphamar;

**34:11** and the boundary shall go down from Sepphamar to Bela, on the east toward springs; and the boundary of Bela shall go down upon the back of the Sea of Chenara on the east.

**34:12** And the boundary shall go down to the Jordan, and the outlet shall be the Salt Sea. This shall be the land for you, and its boundaries all around.'"

**34:13** And Moses commanded the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the land which you shall inherit by lot, just as the Lord instructed Moses to give it to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh;

**34:14** for the tribe of the sons of Reuben and the tribe of the sons of Gad have received theirs according to the houses of their fathers, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their allotments.

**34:15** Two tribes and a half-tribe have received their allotments beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, from the south toward the east."

**34:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:17** "These are the names of the men who shall allot the land to you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun.

**34:18** And you shall take one leader from each tribe to allot the land to you as an inheritance.

**34:19** And these are the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**34:20** of the tribe of Simeon, Salamiel son of Semiud;

**34:21** of the tribe of Benjamin, Eldad son of Chislon;

**34:22** of the tribe of Dan, a leader, Bakchir son of Egli;

**34:23** of the sons of Joseph, of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, a leader, Hanniel son of Suphi;

**34:24** of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, a leader, Kemuel son of Sabatha;

**34:25** of the tribe of Zebulun, a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnach;

**34:26** of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, a leader, Paltiel son of Oza;

**34:27** of the tribe of the sons of Asher, a leader, Achior son of Shelomi;

**34:28** of the tribe of Naphtali, a leader, Pedahel son of Beniamiud."

**34:29** These are the ones whom the Lord commanded to apportion shares to the sons of Israel in the land of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **34:4** — The Greek reads Ennak (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Zin) and translates Hazar-addar as "the village of Arad" (epaulin Arad).
- **34:7** — Greek to oros to oros ("the mountain, the mountain," repeated in v. 8) renders the Hebrew place name Hor ha-har ("Mount Hor") literally as a doubled common noun.
- **34:11** — In vv. 8-11 the Greek preserves several distinctive place names and renderings: Saradak (MT Zedad), Dephrona (MT Ziphron), Harsenaim/Harsenain (MT Hazar-enan), Sepphamar (MT Shepham), Bela (MT Riblah, repeated in the second clause), "springs" (MT Ain), and Chenara (MT Chinnereth).
- **34:15** — The Greek reads "from the south toward the east" (apo notou kat' anatolas), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "eastward toward the sunrise."
- **34:20** — Throughout vv. 20-28 the Greek preserves distinctive forms for several tribal leaders and fathers compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text: Salamiel son of Semiud (v. 20; MT Shemuel son of Ammihud), Eldad (v. 21; MT Elidad), Bakchir son of Egli (v. 22; MT Bukki son of Jogli), Suphi (v. 23; MT Ephod), Sabatha (v. 24; MT Shiphtan), Oza (v. 26; MT Azzan), Achior (v. 27; MT Ahihud), and Beniamiud (v. 28; MT Ammihud).
