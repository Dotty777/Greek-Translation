## Text

**4:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**4:2** "Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from the midst of the sons of Levi, according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses,

**4:3** from twenty-five years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who enters to minister, to do all the works in the tent of the testimony.

**4:4** And these are the works of the sons of Kohath in the tent of the testimony: the holy of holies.

**4:5** And Aaron and his sons shall go in when the camp sets out, and they shall take down the screening curtain and cover the ark of the testimony with it.

**4:6** And they shall place on it a covering of blue leather, and lay over it an all-blue cloth on top, and insert the carrying poles.

**4:7** And on the table that is set forth they shall lay an all-purple cloth, and the dishes, and the incense cups, and the ladles, and the libation bowls with which he pours drink offerings; and the continual loaves shall be on it.

**4:8** And they shall lay over it a scarlet cloth, and cover it with a blue leather covering, and insert the carrying poles through it.

**4:9** And they shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand that gives light, and its lamps, and its tongs, and its oil funnels, and all the oil vessels with which they minister.

**4:10** And they shall put it and all its articles into a blue leather covering, and place it on carrying poles.

**4:11** And over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, and cover it with a blue leather covering, and insert its carrying poles.

**4:12** And they shall take all the articles of ministry with which they minister in the holy place, and put them into a blue cloth, and cover them with a blue leather covering, and place them on carrying poles.

**4:13** And he shall place the cover on the altar, and they shall spread an all-purple cloth over it.

**4:14** And they shall place on it all the articles with which they minister on it, and the firepans, and the meat forks, and the bowls, and the cover, and all the articles of the altar; and they shall lay over it a blue leather covering, and insert its carrying poles. And they shall take a purple cloth and cover up the washbasin and its base, and put it into a blue leather covering, and place it on carrying poles.

**4:15** And Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy things and all the holy articles when the camp sets out; and after these things the sons of Kohath shall go in to carry them, and they shall not touch the holy things, so that they will not die. These things the sons of Kohath shall carry in the tent of the testimony.

**4:16** Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is overseer: the oil of the light, and the compounded incense, and the daily sacrifice, and the anointing oil, the oversight of the whole tent and all that is in it in the holy place, in all the works."

**4:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**4:18** "Do not destroy the tribe's clan of Kohath from the midst of the Levites.

**4:19** Do this for them, and they shall live and by no means die when they approach the most holy things: let Aaron and his sons approach, and they shall appoint them, each according to his burden.

**4:20** And they shall by no means go in to look suddenly at the holy things, or they will die."

**4:21** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:22** "Take the total of the sons of Gershon, these also, according to their ancestral houses, according to their clans;

**4:23** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old register them, everyone who enters to minister, to do his works in the tent of the testimony.

**4:24** This is the ministry of the clan of Gershon, to minister and to carry:

**4:25** and it shall carry the curtains of the tent, and the tent of the testimony and its covering, and the blue covering that is over it on top, and the covering of the doorway of the tent of the testimony,

**4:26** and the hangings of the courtyard, all that are at the tent of the testimony, and the remaining things; and all the articles of ministry with which they minister they shall attend to.

**4:27** By the mouth of Aaron and his sons shall be the ministry of the sons of Gershon, according to all their ministries and according to all the works by them; and you shall register them by name for all the things to be carried by them.

**4:28** This is the ministry of the sons of Gershon in the tent of the testimony, and their charge is in the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

**4:29** As for the sons of Merari, according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses, register them;

**4:30** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old register them, everyone who enters to minister in the works of the tent of the testimony.

**4:31** And these are the charges of the things carried by them, according to all the works of the things in the tent of the testimony: the capitals of the tent, and the bars, and its pillars, and its bases, and the covering, and their bases, and their pillars, and the covering of the doorway of the tent,

**4:32** and the pillars of the courtyard all around, and their bases, and the pillars of the curtain of the gate of the courtyard and their bases, and their pegs, and their cords, and all their articles, and all their ministries; by name register them and all the articles of the charge of the things carried by them.

**4:33** This is the ministry of the clan of the sons of Merari in all their works in the tent of the testimony, in the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

**4:34** And Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered the sons of Kohath according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses,

**4:35** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who enters to minister and to work in the tent of the testimony.

**4:36** And their registration according to their clans was two thousand two hundred fifty.

**4:37** This is the registration of the clan of Kohath, everyone who ministers in the tent of the testimony, just as Moses and Aaron registered them by the voice of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.

**4:38** And the sons of Gershon were registered according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses,

**4:39** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who enters to minister and to do the works in the tent of the testimony.

**4:40** And their registration according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses, was two thousand six hundred thirty.

**4:41** This is the registration of the clan of the sons of Gershon, everyone who ministers in the tent of the testimony, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.

**4:42** And the clan of the sons of Merari was also registered according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses,

**4:43** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who enters to minister for the works of the tent of the testimony.

**4:44** And their registration according to their clans, according to their ancestral houses, was three thousand two hundred.

**4:45** This is the registration of the clan of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.

**4:46** All those who were registered, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered—the Levites—according to clans and according to ancestral houses,

**4:47** from twenty-five years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who enters for the work of the works and the works that are carried in the tent of the testimony;

**4:48** and those who were registered were eight thousand five hundred eighty.

**4:49** By the voice of the Lord he registered them by the hand of Moses, man by man, over their works and over what they themselves carry; and they were registered in the manner the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Greek text consistently sets the starting age for Levitical service at twenty-five (also in vv. 23, 30, 35, 39, 43, and 47), matching Numbers 8:24 rather than the thirty years found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:14** — In vv. 13-14 the Greek directs placing "the cover" (καλυπτήρ) on the altar rather than removing its ashes, and adds an instruction absent from the Masoretic Text for wrapping the washbasin and its base in a purple cloth and blue leather covering.
- **4:22** — The Greek text reads Γεδσών (Gedson), the standard Septuagint form of Gershon (also in vv. 24, 27, 28, 38, and 41), rendered here with its standard modern English spelling.
- **4:26** — The Greek reads "the remaining things" (τὰ περισσά), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew root as "remainder" rather than "their cords," and omits the courtyard gate screen present in the Masoretic Text.
- **4:36** — The Greek text gives 2,250 for the Kohathites (whereas the Masoretic Text reads 2,750), while keeping the combined Levitical total of 8,580 in v. 48.
