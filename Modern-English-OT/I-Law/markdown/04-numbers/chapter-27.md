## Text

**27:1** And the daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, of the clan of Manasseh of the sons of Joseph, came forward, and these are their names: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah;

**27:2** and standing before Moses and before Eleazar the priest and before the rulers and before all the congregation at the doorway of the tent of testimony, they said,

**27:3** "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation that banded together before the Lord in the congregation of Korah, for he died because of his sin, and he had no sons. Let the name of our father not be blotted out from the midst of his clan because he has no son; give us a possession in the midst of our father's brothers."

**27:4** And Moses brought their case before the Lord.

**27:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**27:6** "The daughters of Zelophehad have spoken rightly; you shall give them as a gift a possession of inheritance in the midst of their father's brothers, and you shall transfer their father's allotment to them. And you shall speak to the sons of Israel.

**27:7** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel,

**27:8** saying, 'If a man dies and has no son, you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter;

**27:9** and if he has no daughter, you shall give the inheritance to his brother;

**27:10** and if he has no brothers, you shall give the inheritance to his father's brother;

**27:11** and if his father has no brothers, you shall give the inheritance to the relative nearest to him from his tribe; he shall inherit what is his.'" And this shall be to the sons of Israel a statute of judgment, just as the Lord directed Moses.

**27:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go up to the mountain on the other side, this Mount Nebo, and see the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel as a possession;

**27:13** and you shall see it, and you also shall be added to your people, just as Aaron your brother was added on Mount Hor,

**27:14** because you transgressed My word in the wilderness of Zin when the congregation resisted, to sanctify Me; you did not sanctify Me at the water before them." This is the water of contention in Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin.

**27:15** And Moses said to the Lord,

**27:16** "May the Lord, the God of the spirits and of all flesh, appoint a man over this congregation,

**27:17** who will go out before them and who will come in before them, and who will lead them out and who will bring them in, and the congregation of the Lord will not be like sheep that have no shepherd."

**27:18** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Take to yourself Joshua son of Nun, a man who has spirit in himself, and you shall lay your hands on him;

**27:19** and you shall set him before Eleazar the priest, and you shall command him before all the congregation, and you shall give a command concerning him before them;

**27:20** and you shall place some of your glory on him, so that the sons of Israel may listen to him.

**27:21** And he shall stand before Eleazar the priest, and they shall inquire of him for the judgment of the Manifestations before the Lord; at his word they shall go out and at his word they shall come in, he and the sons of Israel with one accord and the whole congregation."

**27:22** And Moses did just as the Lord commanded him; and taking Joshua, he set him before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation,

**27:23** and he laid his hands on him and appointed him, just as the Lord directed Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Greek text combines MT verses 3 and 4 into a single verse and frames the request as a third-person imperative prohibition ("Let the name of our father not be blotted out") rather than the Hebrew rhetorical question ("Why should the name of our father be withdrawn?").
- **27:7** — The Saints Greek Critical Text repeats the closing clause of verse 6 ('And you shall speak to the sons of Israel') as verse 7; both are translated as they stand.
- **27:12** — Instead of transliterating Hebrew Abarim, the Greek renders it geographically as "the mountain on the other side" and explicitly identifies it as "this Mount Nebo" (compare Deut 32:49), while also adding "of Canaan" and "as a possession."
- **27:14** — Rather than transliterating Hebrew Meribah, the Greek translates it as "water of contention" and adds the explicit clause "you did not sanctify Me at the water before them."
- **27:21** — The Greek renders Hebrew Urim etymologically as "the Manifestations" (Greek ton delon) and uses a plural verb ("they shall inquire of him") where the Hebrew has singular ("he shall inquire for him").
