## Text

**24:1** And when Balaam saw that it was good before the Lord to bless Israel, he did not go as was customary to meet the omens, and he turned his face toward the wilderness.

**24:2** And Balaam lifted up his eyes and observed Israel encamped by tribes, and the Spirit of God came to be in him.

**24:3** And taking up his parable, he said, "Balaam son of Beor says, the man who truly sees says,

**24:4** the one who hears the oracles of God says, who saw a vision of God in sleep, his eyes uncovered:

**24:5** How beautiful are your houses, Jacob, your tents, Israel,

**24:6** like shady valleys, and like a garden beside rivers, and like tents that the Lord pitched, like cedars beside waters.

**24:7** A man will come forth from his seed, and he will rule over many nations; and a kingdom will be exalted higher than Gog, and his kingdom will be increased.

**24:8** God led him out of Egypt; like the glory of a unicorn is his; he will devour the nations of his enemies, and he will drain the marrow from their fat parts, and with his arrows he will shoot down an enemy.

**24:9** He lay down and rested like a lion and like a cub; who will rouse him? Those who bless you are blessed, and those who curse you are cursed."

**24:10** And Balak became angry with Balaam, and he struck his hands together; and Balak said to Balaam, "I have called you to curse my enemy, and look, you have surely blessed him this third time.

**24:11** Now therefore, flee to your place. I said, 'I will honor you,' and now the Lord has deprived you of glory."

**24:12** And Balaam said to Balak, "Did I not also speak to your messengers whom you sent to me, saying,

**24:13** 'If Balak should give me his house full of silver and gold, I will not be able to transgress the word of the Lord, to make it evil or good on my own; whatever things God says, those I will speak'?

**24:14** And now, look, I am hurrying away to my place; come, I will advise you what this people will do to your people at the end of the days."

**24:15** And taking up his parable, he said,

**24:16** "Balaam son of Beor says, the true man who sees says, hearing the oracles of God, knowing knowledge from the Most High, and having seen a vision of God in sleep, his eyes uncovered:

**24:17** I will show to him, yet not now; I pronounce blessed, yet he does not draw near: a star will rise out of Jacob, and a man will arise out of Israel, and he will shatter the leaders of Moab, and he will plunder all the sons of Seth.

**24:18** And Edom will be an inheritance, and Esau his enemy will be an inheritance; and Israel acted in strength.

**24:19** And one will be raised up out of Jacob, and will destroy anyone escaping from a city."

**24:20** And seeing Amalek and taking up his parable, he said, "Amalek is the beginning of the nations, and their seed will perish."

**24:21** And seeing the Kenite and taking up his parable, he said, "Strong is your dwelling place; and if you place your nest in a rock,

**24:22** and if a nest of craftiness comes to be for Beor, Assyrians will take you captive."

**24:23** And seeing Og and taking up his parable, he said, "Alas, alas, who will live when God establishes these things?

**24:24** And one will come forth from the hand of the Kittim, and they will afflict Asshur, and they will afflict the Hebrews, and they themselves will perish together."

**24:25** And Balaam rose up and departed, returning to his place; and Balak departed to his own home.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The Greek reads "A man will come forth from his seed" and names "Gog" (where the Hebrew MT reads "water shall flow from his buckets" and "Agag"). Because the Greek lacks "his" on the first "kingdom," the clause may also be read "he will be exalted higher than the kingdom of Gog."
- **24:8** — Greek monokeros ("unicorn," literally "one-horned") renders Hebrew re'em ("wild ox"), and pache ("fat parts") renders Hebrew atzmot ("bones").
- **24:17** — The Greek renders "a man" (for MT "a scepter") and "the leaders of Moab" (for MT "the forehead/corners of Moab"), and opens with "I will show to him" and "I pronounce blessed" (for MT "I see him" and "I behold him"). In 24:18, the Greek also names "Esau" where the Hebrew MT has "Seir."
- **24:22** — Where the Hebrew MT reads "Kain will be consumed" (leba'er Qayin), the Greek reads the proper name "Beor" (le-Beor) and "nest" (qen), rendering "a nest of craftiness" for Beor.
- **24:23** — The Greek adds "and seeing Og" (absent from the Hebrew MT), and in 24:24 renders "the Hebrews" (for MT "Eber") without "ships" before "from the hand of the Kittim."
