## Text

**25:1** And Israel lodged at Shittim; and the people were profaned by committing sexual immorality with the daughters of Moab.

**25:2** And they invited them to the sacrifices of their idols, and the people ate from their sacrifices and bowed down to their idols,

**25:3** and Israel was initiated to Baal-peor; and the Lord became angry with fury against Israel.

**25:4** And the Lord said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and make a public example of them to the Lord facing the sun, and the anger of the Lord will turn away from Israel."

**25:5** And Moses said to the tribes of Israel, "Each of you, kill his own relative who has been initiated to Baal-peor."

**25:6** And look, a man of the sons of Israel came and brought his brother to the Midianite woman before Moses and before the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, while they were weeping beside the door of the tent of testimony.

**25:7** And when Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this, he rose up from the midst of the congregation, and taking a spear in his hand,

**25:8** he went in behind the Israelite man into the furnace, and pierced both of them, both the Israelite man and the woman through her womb; and the plague ceased from the sons of Israel.

**25:9** And those who had died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.

**25:10** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:11** "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has caused My fury to cease from the sons of Israel by being zealous with My zeal among them, and I did not consume the sons of Israel in My zeal.

**25:12** So say, 'Look, I am giving him a covenant of peace,

**25:13** and it will be for him and for his seed after him an eternal covenant of priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel.'"

**25:14** Now the name of the Israelite man who had been struck, who was struck with the Midianite woman, was Zimri, son of Salmon, ruler of a father's house of the Simeonites.

**25:15** And the name of the Midianite woman who had been struck was Cozbi, daughter of Zur, ruler of the nation of Ommoth; he is of a father's house of the Midianites.

**25:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying,

**25:17** 'Be hostile to the Midianites and strike them,

**25:18** because they themselves are hostile to you with deceit, in all the ways they deceive you on account of Peor, and on account of Cozbi, daughter of a ruler of Midian, their sister, who was struck on the day of the plague on account of Peor.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — Greek ebebelothe ("were profaned") reflects reading the Hebrew root chalal as "profane" rather than "begin" (MT "the people began").
- **25:6** — The Greek reverses the objects relative to the Hebrew MT ("brought the Midianite woman to his brothers"), reading "brought his brother to the Midianite woman."
- **25:8** — Greek kaminon literally means "furnace" or "oven," rendering Hebrew qubbah (a domed tent or vaulted alcove); metras specifically means "womb."
- **25:14** — The Greek gives the father's name as "Salmon," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "Salu."
- **25:15** — The Greek both translates Hebrew ummoth ("peoples/tribes") as "nation" and transliterates it as the proper name "Ommoth."
