## Text

**1:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of the testimony, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt, saying,

**1:2** "Take the sum of the whole congregation of Israel by kinship groups, by houses of ancestral families, by number, by name, head by head among them;

**1:3** every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army of Israel, muster them with their army; you and Aaron, muster them.

**1:4** And with you shall be each man by the tribe of each of the leaders, and they shall be by houses of ancestral families.

**1:5** And these are the names of the men who will stand with you: from the sons of Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;

**1:6** from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**1:7** from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**1:8** from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;

**1:9** from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; from the sons of Joseph, from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud,

**1:10** from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

**1:11** from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;

**1:12** from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

**1:13** from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran;

**1:14** from Gad, Eliasaph son of Reuel;

**1:15** from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

**1:16** These are the summoned ones of the congregation, leaders of the tribes by ancestral families, commanders of thousands of Israel.

**1:17** And Moses and Aaron took these men who were called by name,

**1:18** and they assembled the whole congregation on the first day of the month of the second year, and they registered them by their lineages, by their ancestral families, by the number of their names, from twenty years old and upward, every male, head by head among them,

**1:19** just as the Lord directed Moses. And they were mustered in the wilderness of Sinai.

**1:20** And the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:21** their mustering from the tribe of Reuben was forty-six thousand five hundred.

**1:22** For the sons of Simeon, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:23** their muster from the tribe of Simeon was fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**1:24** For the sons of Judah, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:25** their muster from the tribe of Judah was seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**1:26** For the sons of Issachar, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:27** their muster from the tribe of Issachar was fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**1:28** For the sons of Zebulun, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:29** their muster from the tribe of Zebulun was fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**1:30** For the sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:31** their muster from the tribe of Ephraim was forty thousand five hundred.

**1:32** For the sons of Manasseh, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:33** their muster from the tribe of Manasseh was thirty-two thousand three hundred.

**1:34** For the sons of Benjamin, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:35** their muster from the tribe of Benjamin was thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**1:36** For the sons of Gad, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:37** their muster from the tribe of Gad was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**1:38** For the sons of Dan, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:39** their muster from the tribe of Dan was sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**1:40** For the sons of Asher, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:41** their muster from the tribe of Asher was forty-one thousand five hundred.

**1:42** For the sons of Naphtali, by their kinship groups, by their clans, by the houses of their ancestral families, by the number of their names, head by head among them, all males from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out in the army:

**1:43** their muster from the tribe of Naphtali was fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**1:44** This is the muster that Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel conducted, twelve men; they were one man for each tribe, by the tribe of the houses of an ancestral family.

**1:45** And the whole muster of the sons of Israel in their army, from twenty years old and upward, everyone going out to draw up for battle in Israel, came to

**1:46** six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**1:47** But the Levites from the tribe of their ancestral family were not mustered among the sons of Israel.

**1:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**1:49** "See, you shall not include the tribe of Levi in the muster, and you shall not take their number in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**1:50** And you, appoint the Levites over the tent of the testimony and over all its furnishings and over all that is in it. They shall carry the tent and all its furnishings, and they shall minister in it, and they shall encamp around the tent.

**1:51** And when the tent is to set out, the Levites shall take it down, and when the tent is to encamp, they shall set it up; and the outsider who comes near must die.

**1:52** And the sons of Israel shall encamp, each man in his own rank and each man according to his own command, with their army;

**1:53** but let the Levites encamp before the Lord around the tent of the testimony, and there will be no sin among the sons of Israel. And the Levites themselves shall keep the watch of the tent of the testimony."

**1:54** And the sons of Israel did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron; so they did.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Greek syntax ("each man by the tribe of each of the leaders, and they shall be by houses of ancestral families") diverges notably from the Hebrew MT ("one man from each tribe, each being head of his father's house").
- **1:14** — The Greek reads Rhagouel (Reuel), matching Numbers 2:14, whereas the Hebrew MT here reads Deuel.
- **1:18** — The Greek date reads "of the month of the second year" (where the Hebrew MT reads "of the second month"), and the rare verb epexonoysan refers to registering names on official tablets or rolls (axones).
- **1:33** — The Greek gives Manasseh's count as 32,300 (Hebrew MT: 32,200), though the total in v. 46 remains 603,550; the Greek also places Gad at vv. 36-37 after Benjamin rather than at vv. 24-25 before Judah as in MT.
- **1:53** — The Greek adds "before the Lord" and reads "sin" (hamartema) among the sons of Israel, whereas the Hebrew MT reads "wrath" upon the congregation of the sons of Israel.
