## Text

**2:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**2:2** "Let the sons of Israel encamp, each man next to him, by division, by standards, by the houses of their fathers; the sons of Israel will encamp facing all around the tent of testimony.

**2:3** And those who encamp toward the south, toward the east are the division of the camp of Judah with their force, and the leader of the sons of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**2:4** His force, those who were registered, is seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**2:5** And those who encamp next are of the tribe of Issachar, and the leader of the sons of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar.

**2:6** His force, those who were registered, is fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**2:7** And those who encamp next are of the tribe of Zebulun, and the leader of the sons of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon.

**2:8** Their force, those who were registered, is fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**2:9** All those who were registered from the camp of Judah are one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred, with their force; they will set out first.

**2:10** The division of the camp of Reuben: toward the south is their force, and the leader of the sons of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur.

**2:11** His force, those who were registered, is forty-six thousand five hundred.

**2:12** And those who encamp next to him are of the tribe of Simeon, and the leader of the sons of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

**2:13** His force, those who were registered, is fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**2:14** And those who encamp next to him are the tribe of Gad, and the leader of the sons of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel.

**2:15** His force, those who were registered, is forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**2:16** All those who were registered from the camp of Reuben are one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty, with their force; they will set out second.

**2:17** And the tent of testimony will be lifted up, and the camp of the Levites in the middle of the camps; just as they encamp, so also they will set out, each one next in order according to leadership.

**2:18** The division of the camp of Ephraim is by the sea with their force, and the leader of the sons of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud.

**2:19** His force, those who were registered, is forty thousand five hundred.

**2:20** And those who encamp next are of the tribe of Manasseh, and the leader of the sons of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

**2:21** His force, those who were registered, is thirty-two thousand four hundred.

**2:22** And those who encamp next are of the tribe of Benjamin, and the leader of the sons of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni.

**2:23** His force, those who were registered, is thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**2:24** All those who were registered of the camp of Ephraim are one hundred eight thousand one hundred, with their force; they will set out third.

**2:25** The division of the camp of Dan is toward the north with their force, and the leader of the sons of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

**2:26** His force, those who were registered, is sixty-two thousand five hundred.

**2:27** And those who encamp next to him are of the tribe of Asher, and the leader of the sons of Asher is Pagiel son of Ocran.

**2:28** His force, those who were registered, is forty-one thousand five hundred.

**2:29** And those who encamp next are of the tribe of Naphtali, and the leader of the sons of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan.

**2:30** His force, those who were registered, is fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**2:31** All those who were registered of the camp of Dan are one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred; they will set out last by their division."

**2:32** This is the registration of the sons of Israel by the houses of their fathers; the entire registration of the camps with their forces is six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**2:33** But the Levites were not registered together among them, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**2:34** And the sons of Israel did all that the Lord ordered Moses; so they encamped by their division, and so they set out, each one next in order by their clans, by the houses of their fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Greek reads 'each man next to him' (anthropos echomenos autou) for the ordered placement of the tribes, and consistently renders 'tent of meeting' as 'the tent of testimony' (he skene tou martyriou).
- **2:3** — The Greek text uniquely reads 'toward the south, toward the east' (kata noton kat' anatolas), where the Hebrew has 'eastward, toward the sunrise.'
- **2:18** — 'By the sea' (para thalassan) is a literal Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew directional idiom for 'westward' (toward the Mediterranean Sea).
- **2:21** — The Greek text reads 'thirty-two thousand four hundred' (tetrakosioi), whereas the Hebrew has 32,200 (which aligns with the division total of 108,100 in 2:24).
- **2:26** — The Greek text reads 'sixty-two thousand five hundred' (pentakosioi), whereas the Hebrew has 62,700 (which aligns with the division total of 157,600 in 2:31, where the Greek text uses the numeral abbreviation 'n' for fifty).
