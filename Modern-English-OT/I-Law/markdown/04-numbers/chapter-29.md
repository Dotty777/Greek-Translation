## Text

**29:1** And in the seventh month, on the first of the month, there shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work. It shall be a day of signaling for you.

**29:2** And you shall prepare whole burnt offerings as a sweet aroma to the Lord: one young bull from the cattle, one ram, seven year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:3** their sacrifice: fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for the one young bull and two tenths for the one ram,

**29:4** one tenth for each lamb, for the seven lambs;

**29:5** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you,

**29:6** besides the whole burnt offerings of the new moon, and their sacrifices and their drink offerings, and the continual whole burnt offerings and their sacrifices and their drink offerings, according to their regulation, as a sweet aroma for the Lord.

**29:7** And on the tenth of this month there shall be a holy convocation for you; and you shall afflict your souls, and you shall not do any work.

**29:8** And you shall offer whole burnt offerings as a sweet aroma, fire offerings to the Lord: one young bull from the cattle, one ram, seven year-old lambs; they shall be without blemish for you.

**29:9** Their sacrifice: fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for the one young bull and two tenths for the one ram,

**29:10** one tenth for each lamb, for the seven lambs;

**29:11** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, to make atonement for you, besides the sin offering of atonement, and the continual whole burnt offering, its sacrifice and its drink offering, according to the regulation, as a sweet aroma, a fire offering to the Lord.

**29:12** And on the fifteenth day of this seventh month there shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall not do any servile work, and celebrate it as a festival of the Lord for seven days.

**29:13** And you shall present whole burnt offerings, fire offerings as a sweet aroma for the Lord: on the first day, thirteen young bulls from the cattle, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs; they shall be without blemish.

**29:14** Their sacrifices: fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for each young bull, for the thirteen young bulls, and two tenths for each ram, for the two rams,

**29:15** one tenth for each lamb, for the fourteen lambs;

**29:16** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:17** And on the second day: twelve young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:18** their sacrifice and their drink offering for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:19** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:20** On the third day: eleven young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:21** their sacrifice and their drink offering for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:22** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:23** On the fourth day: ten young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:24** their sacrifices and their drink offerings for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:25** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:26** On the fifth day: nine young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:27** their sacrifices and their drink offerings for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:28** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:29** On the sixth day: eight young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:30** their sacrifices and their drink offerings for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:31** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:32** On the seventh day: seven young bulls, two rams, fourteen year-old lambs;

**29:33** their sacrifices and their drink offerings for the young bulls and for the rams and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:34** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:35** And on the eighth day there shall be a closing festival for you; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**29:36** And you shall present whole burnt offerings as a sweet aroma, fire offerings to the Lord: one young bull, one ram, seven year-old lambs without blemish;

**29:37** their sacrifices and their drink offerings for the young bull and for the ram and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their regulation;

**29:38** and one male goat from the goats for a sin offering, besides the continual whole burnt offering, their sacrifices and their drink offerings.

**29:39** These things you shall prepare for the Lord at your festivals, besides your vows and your freewill offerings, and your whole burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, and your drink offerings, and your deliverance offerings.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek hemera semasias ("day of signaling") refers to the sounding of trumpet signals, corresponding to the Hebrew Festival of Trumpets (teruah).
- **29:3** — In Septuagint Numbers, Greek thysia ("sacrifice") is the regular term for the accompanying grain offering (Hebrew minchah), here specified as fine flour mixed with oil.
- **29:32** — Unlike the other days of the festival (vv. 17, 20, 23, 26, 29), the Greek text omits "without blemish" (amomous) here.
- **29:35** — Greek exodion ("closing festival") denotes the concluding observance at the end of the seven-day feast, corresponding to the Hebrew "solemn assembly" (atseret).
- **29:39** — Greek soteria ("deliverance offerings") is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew peace or fellowship offerings (shelamim).
