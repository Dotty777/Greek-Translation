## Text

**23:1** And Balaam said to Balak, "Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven calves and seven rams for me here."

**23:2** And Balak did just as Balaam had told him, and he offered up a calf and a ram on the altar.

**23:3** And Balaam said to Balak, "Stand beside your sacrifice, and I will go, if perhaps God will appear to meet me; and whatever word He shows me I will report to you." And Balak stood beside his sacrifice;

**23:4** and Balaam went to inquire of God, and he went straight on. And God appeared to Balaam, and Balaam said to Him, "I have prepared the seven altars, and I have brought up a calf and a ram onto the altar."

**23:5** And God put a word into Balaam's mouth and said, "Return to Balak, and this is how you shall speak."

**23:6** And he returned to him; and he was standing beside his burnt offerings, and all the officials of Moab were with him. And the Spirit of God came upon him;

**23:7** and taking up his parable, he said, "From Mesopotamia Balak sent for me, the king of Moab from the mountains of the east, saying, 'Come, curse Jacob for me, and come, pronounce a curse on Israel for me.'

**23:8** How shall I curse one whom the Lord does not curse? Or how shall I curse one whom God does not curse?

**23:9** For from the summit of mountains I will see him, and from hills I will observe him. Look, a people will dwell alone and will not be reckoned among the nations.

**23:10** Who has precisely calculated the offspring of Jacob? And who will number the clans of Israel? May my soul die among the souls of the righteous, and may my offspring become like the offspring of these."

**23:11** And Balak said to Balaam, "What have you done to me? I have summoned you to curse my enemies, and look, you have blessed them with a blessing."

**23:12** And Balaam said to Balak, "Whatever God puts into my mouth, will I not be careful to speak this?"

**23:13** And Balak said to him, "Come again with me to another place, from where you will not see it, except that you will see a part of it, while all of them you will certainly not see; and curse it for me from there."

**23:14** And he took him to a field's lookout, to the summit of the Hewn Place, and built seven altars there, and brought up a calf and a ram onto the altar.

**23:15** And Balaam said to Balak, "Stand beside your sacrifice, and I will go to inquire of God."

**23:16** And God met Balaam and put a word into his mouth and said, "Return to Balak, and these things you shall speak."

**23:17** And he returned to him; and he was standing beside his burnt offering, and all the officials of Moab were with him. And Balak said to him, "What has the Lord spoken?"

**23:18** And taking up his parable, he said, "Rise, Balak, and hear; give ear, witness, son of Zippor.

**23:19** God is not like a man, to be misled, nor like a son of man, to be threatened; having spoken, will He not do it? Will He speak and not stand by it?

**23:20** Look, I have been brought to bless; I will bless, and I will certainly not turn back.

**23:21** There will be no hardship in Jacob, nor will distress be seen in Israel; the Lord his God is with him, the glories of rulers are in him.

**23:22** God is the One who brought them out of Egypt; his is like the glory of a unicorn.

**23:23** For there is no augury in Jacob, nor divination in Israel. In due time it will be told to Jacob and to Israel what God will accomplish.

**23:24** Look, a people will rise up like a lion cub, and like a lion it will exult; it will not sleep until it eats prey, and it will drink the blood of the wounded."

**23:25** And Balak said to Balaam, "Neither curse it for me with curses, nor bless it at all."

**23:26** And Balaam answered and said to Balak, "Did I not speak to you, saying, 'The word that God speaks, this I will do'?"

**23:27** And Balak said to Balaam, "Come, let me take you to another place; if it will please God, then curse it for me from there."

**23:28** And Balak took Balaam to the summit of Peor, which stretches out toward the wilderness.

**23:29** And Balaam said to Balak, "Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven calves and seven rams for me here."

**23:30** And Balak did just as Balaam had told him, and he offered up a calf and a ram on the altar.

## Translator Notes

- **23:7** — Greek parabolē ("parable") here denotes an elevated poetic oracle, and "Mesopotamia" reflects the Greek reading Ek Mesopotamias (MT: "from Aram").
- **23:10** — The Greek text differs notably from the Masoretic Text, reading "offspring" (sperma, MT: "dust"), "clans" (dēmous, MT: "fourth part"), "among the souls of the righteous" (en psychais dikaiōn), and "my offspring" (to sperma mou, MT: "my end").
- **23:14** — The Greek text translates both Hebrew place names literally: agrou skopian ("a field's lookout," MT: "field of Zophim") and Lelaxeumenou ("the Hewn Place," a participle rendering Hebrew "Pisgah").
- **23:19** — The Greek text uses passive infinitives—diartēthēnai ("to be misled / kept in suspense") and apeilēthēnai ("to be threatened")—where the Masoretic Text reads "that he should lie" and "that he should repent."
- **23:22** — Greek monokerōs ("unicorn," literally "one-horned animal") is the Septuagint's standard rendering of Hebrew re'em (probably the wild ox), and doxa ("glory") renders a rare Hebrew noun denoting strength or horns.
