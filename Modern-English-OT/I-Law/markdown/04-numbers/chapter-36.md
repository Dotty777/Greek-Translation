## Text

**36:1** And the leaders of the tribe of the sons of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the tribe of the sons of Joseph, came forward and spoke before Moses and before Eleazar the priest and before the leaders of the ancestral houses of the sons of Israel.

**36:2** And they said, "The Lord commanded our lord to assign the land of the inheritance by lot to the sons of Israel, and the Lord ordered our lord to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother to his daughters.

**36:3** And they will become wives to one from the tribes of the sons of Israel, and their allotment will be taken away from the possession of our fathers, and it will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to whom they become wives, and from the allotment of our inheritance it will be taken away.

**36:4** And if the release of the sons of Israel comes, then their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to whom they become wives, and their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of the tribe of our ancestral family."

**36:5** And Moses commanded the sons of Israel by the decree of the Lord, saying, "The tribe of the sons of Joseph speaks rightly.

**36:6** This is the word that the Lord ordered for the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, 'Where it is pleasing in their sight, let them become wives; only to those from the clan of their father let them become wives.

**36:7** And an inheritance for the sons of Israel shall not shift from tribe to tribe, for the sons of Israel shall indeed cling, each one to the inheritance of the tribe of his ancestral family.

**36:8** And every daughter who succeeds to an inheritance from the tribes of the sons of Israel shall become a wife to one of those from the clan of her father, so that the sons of Israel may each succeed to the ancestral inheritance.

**36:9** And an allotment shall not shift from one tribe to another tribe, but the sons of Israel shall cling, each one to his inheritance.'"

**36:10** In the manner that the Lord ordered Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did.

**36:11** And Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, Noah, and Mahlah, the daughters of Zelophehad, were married to their cousins.

**36:12** They became wives to those from the clan of Manasseh, of the sons of Joseph, and their inheritance remained with the tribe of the clan of their father.

**36:13** These are the commandments, the statutes, and the judgments that the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses at the west of Moab, by the Jordan opposite Jericho.

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — The Septuagint uniquely includes "and before Eleazar the priest," which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (cf. Num 27:2), and refers to the Gileadite group as a "tribe" rather than a clan.
- **36:4** — Greek aphesis ("release") is the Septuagint's regular term for the Hebrew Jubilee (cf. Lev 25:10).
- **36:5** — Greek houtos ("Thus") renders Hebrew ken with its sense "so/thus" rather than "rightly" (contrast Num 27:7, where the Septuagint renders ken as orthos, "rightly").
- **36:11** — The Septuagint orders the five daughters of Zelophehad with Mahlah last rather than first as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and uses the idiom "became to their cousins" (i.e., "were married to their cousins") without an explicit word for "wives."
- **36:13** — The Septuagint includes "the statutes" alongside commandments and judgments, omits "to the sons of Israel," and renders Hebrew arbot ("plains") as "the west" (lit. "sunsets") of Moab.
