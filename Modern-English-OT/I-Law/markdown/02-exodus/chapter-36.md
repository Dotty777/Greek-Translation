## Text

**36:1** And Bezalel and Eliab and everyone wise in mind, to whom wisdom and knowledge were given in them to understand how to do all the works according to the holy duties, worked according to all that the Lord commanded.

**36:2** And Moses called Bezalel and Eliab and all those who had the wisdom, to whom God gave knowledge in the heart, and all those willingly desiring to come forward to the works so as to complete them.

**36:3** And they received from Moses all the contributions that the sons of Israel brought for all the works of the sanctuary, to do them; and they were still receiving the things offered from those bringing them in the morning.

**36:4** And all the wise ones who were doing the works of the sanctuary came, each one according to his own work which they themselves were doing.

**36:5** And he said to Moses, "The people are bringing an abundance for the works that the Lord commanded to do."

**36:6** And Moses gave an order, and he proclaimed in the camp, saying, "Let man and woman no longer work for the firstfruits of the sanctuary." And the people were restrained from offering any more.

**36:7** And the works were sufficient for them to make the construction, and they left some over in addition.

**36:8** And every wise person among those doing the work made the robes of the sanctuary, which are for Aaron the priest, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:9** And he made the shoulder-garment of gold, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

**36:10** And the plates of gold were cut as hairs, so as to weave together with the blue, and the purple, and the thoroughly spun scarlet, and with the twisted fine linen; they made it a woven work,

**36:11** shoulder-pieces joining together from both sides, a woven work, intertwined with one another by itself.

**36:12** Out of it they made it, of gold, and blue, and purple, and thoroughly spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:13** And they made both the emerald stones fastened with clasps, and bordered around, and engraved with the engraving of a signet after the names of the sons of Israel;

**36:14** and he placed them upon the shoulders of the shoulder-garment, stones of memorial of the sons of Israel, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:15** And they made an oracle, a woven work with embroidery, according to the work of the shoulder-garment, of gold, and blue, and purple, and thoroughly spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen;

**36:16** they made the oracle square and doubled, a span its length and a span its width, doubled.

**36:17** And a four-rowed weaving set with stones was woven into it, a row of stones: sardius, and topaz, and emerald, the first row;

**36:18** and the second row: carbuncle, and sapphire, and jasper;

**36:19** and the third row: ligure, and agate, and amethyst.

**36:20** And the fourth row: chrysolite, and beryl, and onyx, encircled with gold and bound together with gold.

**36:21** And the stones were twelve after the names of the sons of Israel, inscribed as signets after their names, each one after its own name, for the twelve tribes.

**36:22** And they made upon the oracle intertwined fringes, a work of braiding, of pure gold.

**36:23** And they made two small gold shields and two gold rings. And they placed the two gold rings upon both ends of the oracle;

**36:24** and they placed the braids of gold upon the rings on both sides of the oracle, and into them the two braids,

**36:25** and they placed them upon the two small shields; and they placed them upon the shoulders of the shoulder-garment opposite on the front.

**36:26** And they made two gold rings, and they placed them upon the two corners on the edge of the oracle and upon the edge of the back of the shoulder-garment on the inside.

**36:27** And they made two gold rings, and they placed them upon both shoulders of the shoulder-garment.

**36:28** And he bound the oracle tightly from the rings that were on it to the rings of the shoulder-garment, held together with the blue, intertwined into the weaving of the shoulder-garment, so that the oracle would not become loose from the shoulder-garment, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:29** And they made the under-robe beneath the shoulder-garment, a woven work, wholly blue;

**36:30** and the opening of the under-robe in the middle was woven through and intertwined, having a border around the opening so as not to come apart.

**36:31** And they made upon the hem of the under-robe underneath small pomegranates as of a blossoming pomegranate, of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

**36:32** And they made gold bells, and they placed the bells upon the hem of the under-robe all around between the small pomegranates;

**36:33** a gold bell and a small pomegranate upon the hem of the under-robe all around, for ministering, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:34** And they made tunics of fine linen, a woven work, for Aaron and for his sons,

**36:35** and the headdresses of fine linen, and the turban of fine linen, and the undergarments of twisted fine linen,

**36:36** and their sashes of fine linen, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, the work of an embroiderer, in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

**36:37** And they made the gold plate, a dedication of the sanctuary, of pure gold; and he wrote upon it letters engraved as of a signet, "Holiness to the Lord";

**36:38** and they placed it upon the blue band, so as to rest upon the turban above, in the manner that the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — The Greek text names Bezalel's companion Eliab here and in 36:2 and 37:21, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Oholiab.
- **36:8** — From 36:8 onward, the Septuagint places the account of making the priestly garments here (corresponding to Hebrew Exodus 39:1-31), before the curtains and courtyard in chapter 37.
- **36:9** — Greek epomis (literally 'shoulder-garment') is the Septuagint term corresponding to the Hebrew ephod; in the plural (36:11) it refers to the shoulder-pieces.
- **36:15** — Greek logion ('oracle') is the Septuagint rendering for the high priest's breastpiece (Hebrew choshen).
- **36:24** — The Greek phrase rendered 'and into them the two braids' (kai eis tas ta dyo emplokia) lacks a noun after the feminine plural article tas, likely due to an early scribal omission (some witnesses supply 'couplings').
