## Text

**34:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Chisel for yourself two stone tablets just like the first ones, and come up to Me onto the mountain, and I will write upon the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you shattered.

**34:2** And be ready for the morning, and you shall go up onto Mount Sinai, and stand before Me there on the top of the mountain.

**34:3** And let no one go up with you, nor be seen anywhere on the whole mountain; and let the sheep and the cattle not graze near that mountain."

**34:4** And he chiseled two stone tablets just like the first ones; and rising early in the morning, Moses went up onto Mount Sinai, just as the Lord had commanded him; and Moses took the two stone tablets.

**34:5** And the Lord came down in a cloud and stood beside him there, and called by the name of the Lord.

**34:6** And the Lord passed by before his face, and called out, "The Lord God, compassionate and merciful, slow to anger and abounding in mercy and true,

**34:7** and keeping righteousness and mercy to thousands, taking away lawless deeds and injustices and sins, and He will not acquit the guilty, bringing the lawless deeds of fathers upon children and upon children's children, to the third and fourth generation."

**34:8** And hurrying, Moses bowed down to the ground and worshiped,

**34:9** and he said, "If I have found favor before You, let my Lord go along with us; for the people are stiff-necked, and You will take away our sins and our lawless deeds, and we will be Yours."

**34:10** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, I am making a covenant with you before all your people; I will do glorious things that have not happened in all the earth and in any nation; and all the people among whom you are will see the works of the Lord, for the things that I will do for you are marvelous.

**34:11** Pay attention to all that I am commanding you; look, I am driving out before your face the Amorite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hittite and the Hivite and the Girgashite and the Jebusite.

**34:12** Watch yourself, so that you never make a covenant with those dwelling on the land into which you are entering, so that it does not become a stumbling block to you among you.

**34:13** You shall tear down their altars, and you shall shatter their pillars, and you shall cut down their groves, and you shall burn up the carved images of their gods with fire.

**34:14** For you shall by no means worship other gods; for the Lord God, a jealous name, is a jealous God.

**34:15** Never make a covenant with those dwelling among foreigners on the land, or else they will prostitute themselves after their gods, and sacrifice to their gods, and invite you and you eat from their sacrifices,

**34:16** and you take some of their daughters for your sons, and give some of your daughters to their sons, and your daughters prostitute themselves after their gods, and your sons prostitute themselves after their gods.

**34:17** And you shall not make molten gods for yourself.

**34:18** And you shall keep the Festival of Unleavened Bread; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I have commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of new things; for in the month of new things you came out of Egypt.

**34:19** Everything that opens the womb—the males are Mine, every firstborn of a calf and firstborn of a sheep.

**34:20** And the firstborn of a beast of burden you shall redeem with a sheep; but if you do not redeem it, you shall pay a price. Every firstborn of your sons you shall redeem; you shall not appear before Me empty-handed.

**34:21** Six days you shall work, but on the seventh there is rest; during sowing and during harvesting, there is rest.

**34:22** And you shall observe a Festival of Weeks for Me, the beginning of the wheat harvest, and the beginning of the ingathering in the middle of the year.

**34:23** Three times of the year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord, the God of Israel.

**34:24** For when I drive out the nations before your face and enlarge your borders, no one will covet your land whenever you go up to appear before the Lord your God three times of the year.

**34:25** You shall not slaughter the blood of My incense offerings with leaven, and the sacrifices of the Festival of the Passover shall not remain overnight until the morning.

**34:26** The firstfruits of your land you shall place in the house of the Lord your God. You shall not offer a lamb in its mother's milk."

**34:27** And the Lord said to Moses, "Write for yourself these words; for on the basis of these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel."

**34:28** And Moses was there before the Lord forty days and forty nights; he did not eat bread and he did not drink water; and he wrote these words upon the tablets of the covenant, the ten words.

**34:29** Now as Moses was coming down from the mountain, the two tablets were in the hands of Moses; and as he was coming down from the mountain, Moses did not know that the appearance of the complexion of his face had been glorified while he was speaking to Him.

**34:30** And Aaron and all the elders of Israel saw Moses, and the appearance of the complexion of his face was glorified. And they were afraid to come near him.

**34:31** And Moses called them, and Aaron and all the rulers of the congregation turned back to him; and Moses spoke to them.

**34:32** And after this all the sons of Israel came near to him. And he commanded them all that the Lord had commanded him on Mount Sinai.

**34:33** And when he finished speaking to them, he put a veil over his face.

**34:34** But whenever Moses would go in before the Lord to speak to Him, he would take off the veil until he came out; and having come out, he would speak to all the sons of Israel whatever the Lord had commanded him.

**34:35** And the sons of Israel saw the face of Moses, that it was glorified; and Moses put a veil over his face until he should go in to speak with Him.

## Translator Notes

- **34:18** — "Month of new things" translates Greek menì ton neon (referring to new crops or produce), the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew month name Abib.
- **34:20** — "You shall pay a price" reflects the Septuagint reading (timen doseis), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you shall break its neck."
- **34:26** — "You shall not offer a lamb" reflects the Septuagint wording (ou prosoiseis arna), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you shall not boil a young goat."
- **34:29** — "Had been glorified" (dedoxastai) translates the Septuagint rendering for the radiance of Moses's face (later echoed in 2 Corinthians 3:7-11), where the Hebrew verb denotes shining or sending out rays.
