## Text

**30:1** "And you shall make an altar of incense from incorruptible wood.

**30:2** And you shall make it a cubit in length and a cubit in width—it shall be square—and two cubits in height; its horns shall be from it.

**30:3** And you shall overlay them with pure gold—its hearth and its walls all around and its horns—and you shall make for it a twisted gold molding all around.

**30:4** And you shall make two pure gold rings under its twisted molding on the two sides; you shall make them on the two flanks, and they shall be holders for the poles so as to carry it with them.

**30:5** And you shall make poles from incorruptible wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

**30:6** And you shall place it in front of the veil that is over the ark of the testimonies, in which I will make Myself known to you from there.

**30:7** And Aaron shall offer fine compounded incense on it; morning by morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn incense on it.

**30:8** And when Aaron lights the lamps in the evening, you shall burn incense on it, a continual incense perpetually before the Lord throughout their generations.

**30:9** And he shall not offer up on it other incense, an offering by fire, or a sacrifice; you shall not pour out a drink offering on it.

**30:10** And Aaron shall make atonement for it on its horns once a year; with the blood of the purification he shall purify it throughout their generations; it is a holy of holies to the Lord."

**30:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:12** "When you take the reckoning of the sons of Israel at their inspection, then each one shall give a ransom for his soul to the Lord, and there shall not be a downfall among them at their inspection.

**30:13** And this is what they shall give, as many as pass through the inspection: half of the didrachma, which is according to the sacred didrachma—twenty obols is the didrachma—and the half of the didrachma is a contribution to the Lord.

**30:14** Everyone who passes into the inspection from twenty years old and upward, they shall give the contribution to the Lord.

**30:15** The one who is rich shall not add and the one who is poor shall not give less than the half of the didrachma, in giving the contribution to the Lord to make atonement for your souls.

**30:16** And you shall receive the silver of the contribution from the sons of Israel, and you shall give it for the work of the tent of the testimony; and it shall be to the sons of Israel a memorial before the Lord, and to make atonement for your souls."

**30:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:18** "Make a bronze basin and a bronze base for it, for washing; and you shall place it between the tent of the testimony and the altar, and you shall pour water into it.

**30:19** And Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it with water.

**30:20** Whenever they enter into the tent of the testimony, they shall wash with water and they shall by no means die; whenever they approach the altar to minister and to offer up the whole burnt offerings to the Lord,

**30:21** they shall wash their hands and their feet with water; whenever they enter into the tent of the testimony, they shall wash with water so that they may not die; and it shall be to them an eternal statute, to him and to his generations after him."

**30:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:23** "And you, take spices: the flower of choice myrrh, five hundred shekels, and of fragrant cinnamon, half of this, two hundred fifty, and of fragrant calamus, two hundred fifty,

**30:24** and of iris, five hundred shekels of the sanctuary, and oil from olives, a hin;

**30:25** and you shall make it a holy anointing oil, a perfumed ointment by the craft of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil.

**30:26** And you shall anoint with it the tent of the testimony, and the ark of the testimony,

**30:27** and the lampstand and all its utensils, and the tent of the testimony and all its utensils, and the altar of incense,

**30:28** and the altar of whole burnt offerings and all its utensils, and the table and all its utensils, and the basin.

**30:29** And you shall sanctify them, and they shall be holy of holies; everyone who touches them shall be sanctified.

**30:30** And you shall anoint Aaron and his sons, and you shall sanctify them to serve as priests to Me.

**30:31** And to the sons of Israel you shall speak, saying, 'This shall be to you a holy oil, an ointment of anointing, throughout your generations.

**30:32** On human flesh it shall not be anointed, and according to this composition it shall not be made for yourselves in the same way; it is holy, and it shall be a holy thing to you.

**30:33** Whoever makes one in the same way, and whoever gives any of it to an outsider, shall be utterly destroyed from his people.'"

**30:34** And the Lord said to Moses, "Take for yourself spices: stacte, onycha, fragrant galbanum, and translucent frankincense; it shall be equal part for equal part;

**30:35** and they shall make with it a perfumed incense, the work of a perfumer, blended, a pure, holy work.

**30:36** And you shall crush some of these finely, and you shall place it in front of the testimonies in the tent of the testimony, where I will make Myself known to you from there; it shall be a holy of holies to you.

**30:37** Incense according to this composition you shall not make for yourselves; it shall be to you a holy thing for the Lord.

**30:38** Whoever makes it in the same way so as to smell it shall perish from his people."

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — The Greek has "its hearth" (escharan) where the Hebrew text reads "its top," referring to the upper burning surface of the incense altar.
- **30:13** — The Greek expresses the sanctuary weights in Hellenistic currency units—didrachmas and obols—rather than Hebrew shekels and gerahs.
- **30:24** — The Greek specifies "iris" (ireos) where the Hebrew text has "cassia."
- **30:27** — The Greek text repeats "the tent of the testimony and all its utensils" here and orders the furnishings in verses 27-28 differently from the Hebrew text.
