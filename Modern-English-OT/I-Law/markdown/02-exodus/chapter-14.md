## Text

**14:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**14:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and having turned back, let them encamp opposite the farmstead, between Migdol and the sea, directly facing Baal-zephon; in front of them you shall encamp by the sea;

**14:3** and Pharaoh will say to his people, 'These sons of Israel are wandering around in the land; for the wilderness has shut them in.'

**14:4** And I will harden the heart of Pharaoh, and he will pursue after them; and I will be glorified in Pharaoh and in all his army, and all the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord." And they did so.

**14:5** And it was reported to the king of the Egyptians that the people had fled; and the heart of Pharaoh and the heart of his servants turned against the people, and they said, "What is this we have done, sending out the sons of Israel so that they do not serve us?"

**14:6** So Pharaoh yoked his chariots, and led away all his people with him,

**14:7** and having taken six hundred chosen chariots and all the cavalry of the Egyptians, and officers over all of them.

**14:8** And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt and of his servants, and he pursued after the sons of Israel; now the sons of Israel were going out with a high hand.

**14:9** And the Egyptians pursued after them and found them encamped beside the sea; and all the cavalry and the chariots of Pharaoh and the horsemen and his army were opposite the farmstead, directly facing Baal-zephon.

**14:10** And Pharaoh was drawing near; and having looked up with their eyes, the sons of Israel saw, and the Egyptians had encamped behind them, and they were greatly afraid. And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord,

**14:11** and they said to Moses, "Because there were no graves in the land of Egypt, have you brought us out to put us to death in the wilderness? What is this you have done to us, having brought us out of Egypt?

**14:12** Was not this the word that we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, 'Leave us alone so that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it is better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in this wilderness."

**14:13** And Moses said to the people, "Take courage! Stand firm and see the salvation from God, which He will accomplish for us today; for in the way you have seen the Egyptians today, you will never see them again forever.

**14:14** The Lord will fight for you, and you shall keep silent."

**14:15** And the Lord said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to Me? Speak to the sons of Israel, and let them break camp;

**14:16** and you, lift up your staff, and stretch out your hand over the sea and split it, and let the sons of Israel enter into the midst of the sea on the dry ground.

**14:17** And look, I will harden the heart of Pharaoh and of all the Egyptians, and they will go in after them; and I will be glorified in Pharaoh and in all his army, and in his chariots and in his horses.

**14:18** And all the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord, when I am glorified in Pharaoh and in his chariots and his horses."

**14:19** And the angel of God, who was going ahead of the camp of the sons of Israel, moved and went to the rear; and the pillar of cloud also moved from before them and stood behind them.

**14:20** And it entered between the Egyptians and the camp of Israel, and stood; and there came darkness and gloom, and the night passed, and they did not come together with one another all night.

**14:21** And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea; and the Lord drove back the sea with a violent south wind all night, and made the sea dry, and the water was divided.

**14:22** And the sons of Israel entered into the midst of the sea on the dry ground, and the water was a wall for them on the right and a wall on the left.

**14:23** And the Egyptians pursued, and went in after them, even every horse of Pharaoh and the chariots and the riders, into the midst of the sea.

**14:24** And it came about in the morning watch that the Lord looked upon the camp of the Egyptians in a pillar of fire and cloud, and threw the camp of the Egyptians into confusion,

**14:25** and He bound the axles of their chariots and led them with force. And the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from the face of Israel, for the Lord is fighting for them against the Egyptians."

**14:26** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, and let the water return and cover over the Egyptians, both upon the chariots and the riders."

**14:27** And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and toward daybreak the water returned to its place; and the Egyptians fled under the water, and the Lord shook off the Egyptians in the midst of the sea.

**14:28** And turning back, the water covered the chariots and the riders and all the force of Pharaoh, those who had entered after them into the sea; and not even one of them was left.

**14:29** But the sons of Israel walked through dry land in the midst of the sea, and the water was a wall for them on the right and a wall on the left.

**14:30** And the Lord rescued Israel on that day from the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead along the shore of the sea.

**14:31** And Israel saw the great hand, what the Lord did to the Egyptians; and the people feared the Lord, and they believed in God and in Moses His servant.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew place name Pi-hahiroth as the common noun "the farmstead" (tes epauleos), here and in 14:9.
- **14:20** — The Septuagint describes darkness and gloom as the night passed, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions the cloud giving light by night to Israel.
- **14:21** — The Septuagint specifies a "south wind" (anemo noto), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies an "east wind."
- **14:25** — The Septuagint reads that the Lord "bound the axles" (synedesen tous axonas) and "led them with force" (egagen autous meta bias), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "clogged/removed their wheels so that they drove with difficulty."
