## Text

**24:1** And He said to Moses, "Come up to the Lord, you and Aaron and Nadab and Abihu and seventy of the elders of Israel, and they shall worship the Lord from a distance.

**24:2** And Moses alone shall draw near to God, but they shall not draw near; and the people shall not go up with them."

**24:3** Then Moses came in and recounted to the people all the words of God and the ordinances; and all the people answered with one voice, saying, "All the words that the Lord has spoken we will do and heed."

**24:4** And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord. Rising early in the morning, Moses built an altar beneath the mountain and twelve stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.

**24:5** And he sent out the young men of the sons of Israel, and they offered up whole burnt offerings, and sacrificed young calves as a sacrifice of deliverance to God.

**24:6** Taking half of the blood, Moses poured it into bowls; and the other half of the blood he poured out against the altar.

**24:7** And taking the book of the covenant, he read it in the ears of the people, and they said, "All that the Lord has spoken we will do and heed."

**24:8** Then Moses took the blood, sprinkled it over the people, and said, "Look, the blood of the covenant that the Lord has established with you concerning all these words."

**24:9** And Moses and Aaron and Nadab and Abihu and seventy of the council of elders of Israel went up,

**24:10** and they saw the place where the God of Israel stood; and what was under His feet was like a work of sapphire brick, and like the appearance of the vault of heaven in clearness.

**24:11** And of the chosen ones of Israel not even one perished; and they appeared in the place of God, and they ate and drank.

**24:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Come up to Me on the mountain and remain there; and I will give you the stone tablets, the law and the commandments that I have written to give as law to them."

**24:13** And rising up, Moses and Joshua his attendant went up into the mountain of God.

**24:14** And to the elders they said, "Remain quietly here until we return to you; and look, Aaron and Hur are with you; if a legal dispute happens to anyone, let them approach them."

**24:15** And Moses and Joshua went up into the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

**24:16** And the glory of God descended upon Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days; and the Lord called Moses on the seventh day from the midst of the cloud.

**24:17** Now the appearance of the glory of the Lord was like a blazing fire on the summit of the mountain before the sons of Israel.

**24:18** And Moses entered into the midst of the cloud and went up into the mountain, and he was there on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — "Sacrifice of deliverance" translates Greek thysia sōtēriou, the standard Septuagint rendering for the peace/fellowship offering.
- **24:10** — The Greek reads "they saw the place where the God of Israel stood" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "they saw the God of Israel."
- **24:11** — The Greek reads "of the chosen ones of Israel not even one perished; and they appeared in the place of God," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("He did not stretch out His hand against the nobles... and they saw God").
- **24:14** — Here ("they said") and in 24:15 ("Moses and Joshua went up"), the Greek explicitly includes Joshua alongside Moses where the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks only of Moses.
