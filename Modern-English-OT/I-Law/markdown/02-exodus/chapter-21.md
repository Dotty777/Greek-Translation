## Text

**21:1** And these are the ordinances that you will set before them:

**21:2** If you acquire a Hebrew servant, he will serve you for six years, and in the seventh year he will depart free, without payment.

**21:3** If he comes in alone, he will also go out alone; but if a wife comes in with him, the wife will also go out with him.

**21:4** But if the master gives him a wife, and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and the children will belong to his master, and he himself will go out alone.

**21:5** But if the servant answers and says, "I love my master and my wife and the children; I will not go away free,"

**21:6** his master will bring him to the judgment court of God, and then he will bring him to the door, to the doorpost, and his master will pierce his ear with the awl, and he will serve him forever.

**21:7** And if someone sells his own daughter as a household servant, she will not depart just as the female slaves go away.

**21:8** If she does not please her master who betrothed her to himself, he will release her by ransom; but he has no authority to sell her to a foreign nation, because he has broken faith with her.

**21:9** And if he betroths her to his son, he will deal with her according to the ordinance for daughters.

**21:10** And if he takes another, he must not withhold the necessities, the clothing, and her marital intimacy.

**21:11** And if he does not do these three things for her, she will go out without payment, without money.

**21:12** And if someone strikes someone else and he dies, he must surely be put to death.

**21:13** But as for the one who did not do it intentionally, rather God delivered him into his hands, I will give you a place to which the one who killed may flee.

**21:14** But if someone attacks his neighbor to kill him by treachery and flees for refuge, you will take him from My altar to put him to death.

**21:15** Whoever strikes his father or his mother must surely be put to death.

**21:16** The one who speaks evil of his father or his mother will surely die.

**21:17** Whoever steals someone from the sons of Israel and, having overpowered him, sells him, and he is found with him, must surely die.

**21:18** And if two men quarrel and strike their neighbor with a stone or with a fist, and he does not die, but lies down upon his bed,

**21:19** if the man gets up and walks about outside on a staff, the one who struck him will be innocent; only he will pay for his loss of work and the medical expenses.

**21:20** And if someone strikes his male servant or his female servant with a rod, and the servant dies under his hands, it must be avenged with justice.

**21:21** But if the servant survives for one day or two, it will not be avenged, for the servant is his money.

**21:22** And if two men fight and strike a pregnant woman, and her child comes out not fully formed, he will surely be fined; according to whatever the woman's husband imposes, he will pay with an assessment.

**21:23** But if it was fully formed, he will give life for life,

**21:24** eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

**21:25** burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

**21:26** And if someone strikes the eye of his household servant or the eye of his female attendant and blinds it, he will send them away free in place of their eye.

**21:27** And if he knocks out the tooth of the household servant or the tooth of his female attendant, he will send them away free in place of their tooth.

**21:28** And if a bull gores a man or a woman and the person dies, the bull will be stoned with stones, and its meat must not be eaten; but the owner of the bull will be innocent.

**21:29** But if the bull has been a gorer before yesterday and before the third day, and they have warned its owner, and he has not removed it, and it kills a man or a woman, the bull will be stoned, and its owner will also die.

**21:30** Yet if a ransom is imposed on him, he will pay as a ransom for his life whatever they impose on him.

**21:31** And if it gores a son or a daughter, they will do to him according to this ordinance.

**21:32** And if the bull gores a male servant or a female servant, he will pay thirty didrachmas of silver to their master, and the bull will be stoned.

**21:33** And if someone opens a pit or quarries one and does not cover it, and a calf or a donkey falls in there,

**21:34** the owner of the pit will make restitution; he will pay money to their owner, and the dead animal will belong to him.

**21:35** And if someone's bull gores a neighbor's bull and it dies, they will sell the living bull and divide its money, and they will divide the dead bull.

**21:36** But if the bull is known to be a gorer before yesterday and before the third day, and they have testified to the owner, and he has not removed it, he will pay back bull for bull, and the dead one will belong to him.

**21:37** And if someone steals a calf or a sheep and slaughters or sells it, he will pay back five calves for the calf and four sheep for the sheep.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — Greek to kriterion tou theou ("the judgment court of God") renders the Hebrew ha-elohim specifically as a judicial tribunal under God's authority.
- **21:16** — The Septuagint reverses verses 16 and 17 relative to the Masoretic Text, placing speaking evil of parents in verse 16 and kidnapping in verse 17.
- **21:22** — In verses 22–23, the Greek distinguishes between a child not yet fully formed (me exeikonismenon) and one fully formed (exeikonismenon), whereas the Masoretic Text speaks generally of "harm."
- **21:29** — "Before yesterday and before the third day" is a literal Greek rendering of the Hebrew idiom for "previously," and Greek aphanise means "remove" or "do away with" rather than "keep confined."
- **21:32** — The Greek converts the Hebrew weight unit into thirty didrachmas of silver; note also that verse 37 of this chapter corresponds to Exodus 22:1 in the Masoretic Text.
