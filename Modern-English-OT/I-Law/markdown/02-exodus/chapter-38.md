## Text

**38:1** And Bezalel made the ark,

**38:2** and he overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside.

**38:3** And he cast for it four gold rings, two on the one side and two on the second side,

**38:4** wide for the carrying poles so as to carry it with them.

**38:5** And he made the mercy seat above the ark out of gold,

**38:6** and the two cherubim of gold:

**38:7** one cherub on the end of the mercy seat and one cherub on the second end of the mercy seat,

**38:8** overshadowing the mercy seat with their wings.

**38:9** And he made the table that is set forth from pure gold,

**38:10** and he cast for it four rings, two on the one side and two on the second side, wide so as to carry with the carrying poles in them.

**38:11** And he made the carrying poles of the ark and of the table, and he overlaid them with gold.

**38:12** And he made the utensils of the table, both the dishes and the incense cups and the ladles and the libation bowls with which he will pour drink offerings, of gold.

**38:13** And he made the lampstand that gives light of gold,

**38:14** solid in its shaft, and the branches from both of its sides;

**38:15** from its branches the buds projecting, three from this side and three from that side, equal to one another;

**38:16** and their lamp-cups that are on the ends, shaped like almonds, out of them; and the sockets out of them, so that the lamps might be on them, and the seventh socket from the end of the lamp-cup at the top above, solid, wholly of gold;

**38:17** and seven gold lamps on it, and its gold tongs, and their gold oil-ladles.

**38:18** This man overlaid the pillars with silver, and he cast gold rings for each pillar, and he gilded the bars with gold, and he overlaid the pillars of the curtain with gold, and he made the hooks of gold.

**38:19** This man also made the rings of the tent of gold, and the rings of the courtyard and rings for stretching out the covering above of bronze.

**38:20** This man cast the silver capitals of the tent, and the bronze capitals of the door of the tent, and the gate of the courtyard; and he made silver hooks for the pillars on the pillars; this man overlaid them with silver.

**38:21** This man also made the pegs of the courtyard of bronze.

**38:22** This man made the bronze altar out of the bronze firepans that belonged to the men who rebelled with the congregation of Korah.

**38:23** This man made all the utensils of the altar, both its firepan and the base and the bowls and the fleshhooks, of bronze.

**38:24** This man made for the altar an attachment, a work of network beneath the firepan under it, up to its middle; and he put on it four bronze rings at the four sides of the attachment of the altar, wide for the bars so as to carry the altar with them.

**38:25** This man made the holy anointing oil and the compound of incense, the pure work of a perfumer.

**38:26** This man made the washbasin of bronze and its base of bronze out of the mirrors of the women who fasted, who fasted beside the doors of the tent of testimony on the day he pitched it;

**38:27** and he made the washbasin so that Moses and Aaron and his sons might wash their hands and feet from it when they entered the tent of testimony; or whenever they approached the altar to minister, they would wash from it, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **38:16** — Greek lampadia ('lamp-cups') and enthemia ('sockets') describe the almond-shaped cups and lamp-holders on the ends of the lampstand's branches and its central top.
- **38:20** — The Greek uses kephalides ('capitals,' normally pillar-tops) here and in 39:4-5 where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'bases/sockets' in the parallel passages.
- **38:22** — The Septuagint distinctively states that Bezalel made the bronze altar from the firepans of the men who rebelled with Korah's congregation (cf. Numbers 16:36-40), a detail absent from the Hebrew text of Exodus.
- **38:26** — Greek reads 'women who fasted' (nesteusason), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to women who 'served' or 'assembled' at the entrance of the tent.
