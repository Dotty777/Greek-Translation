## Text

**1:1** These are the names of the sons of Israel who went into Egypt together with Jacob their father; each one entered with his whole household:

**1:2** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah,

**1:3** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

**1:4** Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**1:5** Now Joseph was in Egypt; and all the souls from Jacob were seventy-five.

**1:6** And Joseph died, and all his brothers, and all that generation.

**1:7** But the sons of Israel increased and multiplied and teemed and grew very, very strong; and the land multiplied them.

**1:8** Then another king arose over Egypt, who did not know Joseph.

**1:9** And he said to his nation, "Look, the race of the sons of Israel is a great multitude, and is stronger than we are.

**1:10** Come, therefore, let us outwit them, so that it does not multiply, and whenever war happens to us, these also will be joined to the adversaries, and having waged war against us, they will depart from the land."

**1:11** So he set overseers of the works over them, so that they might afflict them in the works; and they built fortified cities for Pharaoh: Pithom, and Rameses, and On, which is Heliopolis.

**1:12** Yet the more they humbled them, so much the more numerous they became, and they grew very, very strong; and the Egyptians recoiled in disgust from the sons of Israel.

**1:13** And the Egyptians oppressed the sons of Israel by force,

**1:14** and made their life painful with harsh works, in clay and brickmaking and in all the works in the fields, according to all the works with which they enslaved them with force.

**1:15** And the king of the Egyptians spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews, to the one of them whose name was Shiphrah, and the name of the second was Puah;

**1:16** and he said, "When you act as midwives for the Hebrew women and they are about to give birth, if it is a male, kill it; but if it is a female, preserve it alive."

**1:17** But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt commanded them, and they kept the males alive.

**1:18** So the king of Egypt summoned the midwives and said to them, "Why is it that you have done this thing and kept the males alive?"

**1:19** And the midwives said to Pharaoh, "The Hebrew women are not like the women of Egypt, for they give birth before the midwives come in to them, and they gave birth."

**1:20** And God dealt well with the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew very strong.

**1:21** Because the midwives feared God, they made houses for themselves.

**1:22** Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, "Every male that is born to the Hebrews, throw into the river; and every female, keep it alive."

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — LXX counts seventy-five persons (matching 4QExod-a and Acts 7:14) rather than the seventy of the Masoretic Hebrew text, and places the notice about Joseph first.
- **1:7** — LXX uses chydaioi egenonto ("teemed," literally "became abundant in crowds") and concludes with an active clause, "and the land multiplied them," where the Hebrew reads "the land was filled with them."
- **1:11** — LXX renders "fortified cities" (instead of "store cities") and adds a third city, "On, which is Heliopolis," not present in the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **1:21** — LXX has a third-person plural verb ("they made houses for themselves"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew has God as the singular subject ("He made houses for them").
