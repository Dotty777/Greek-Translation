## Text

**33:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go, go up from here, you and your people whom you brought out from the land of Egypt, into the land that I swore to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, saying, 'To your offspring I will give it.'

**33:2** And I will send My angel along before your face, and you will drive out the Amorite and the Hittite and the Perizzite and the Girgashite and the Hivite and the Jebusite.

**33:3** And I will bring you into a land flowing with milk and honey; for I will certainly not go up with you, because you are a stiff-necked people, so that I may not consume you on the way."

**33:4** And when the people heard this bad word, they mourned deeply in mourning garments.

**33:5** And the Lord said to the sons of Israel, "You are a stiff-necked people; watch out that I do not bring another plague upon you and consume you. Now therefore, take off the robes of your glories and your adornment, and I will show you what I will do to you."

**33:6** And the sons of Israel took off their adornment and their attire from Mount Horeb.

**33:7** And Moses took his tent and pitched it outside the camp, far away from the camp, and it was called the tent of testimony; and it happened that everyone who sought the Lord would go out to the tent outside the camp.

**33:8** And whenever Moses went into the tent outside the camp, all the people would stand watching, each one beside the doors of his tent; and they would observe Moses as he departed until he entered into the tent.

**33:9** And whenever Moses entered into the tent, the pillar of the cloud would come down and stand at the door of the tent, and He would speak to Moses.

**33:10** And all the people would see the pillar of the cloud standing at the door of the tent; and all the people, standing up, would worship, each one from the door of his tent.

**33:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses face to face, as if someone should speak to his own friend. And he would return to the camp; but the attendant Joshua, son of Nun, a young man, would not depart from the tent.

**33:12** And Moses said to the Lord, "Look, You say to me, 'Lead this people up,' yet You have not made known to me whom You will send along with me; and You have said to me, 'I know you above all, and you have favor with Me.'

**33:13** Therefore, if I have found favor before You, reveal Yourself to me; let me see You clearly, so that I may have found favor before You, and so that I may know that this great nation is Your people."

**33:14** And He said, "I Myself will go before you and will give you rest."

**33:15** And he said to Him, "If You Yourself do not go, do not lead me up from here.

**33:16** And how will it truly be known that I have found favor with You, both I and Your people, except by Your going along with us? And both I and Your people will be glorified beyond all the nations that are on the earth."

**33:17** And the Lord said to Moses, "This word also that you have spoken I will do for you; for you have found favor before Me, and I know you above all."

**33:18** And he said, "Reveal Yourself to me."

**33:19** And He said, "I will pass by before you in My glory, and I will speak by My name, 'the Lord,' before you; and I will have mercy on whomever I have mercy, and I will show compassion to whomever I show compassion."

**33:20** And He said, "You will not be able to see My face; for no human being will see My face and live."

**33:21** And the Lord said, "Look, there is a place beside Me; you will stand on the rock.

**33:22** And when My glory passes by, then I will place you into a cleft of the rock, and I will cover over you with My hand until I have passed by.

**33:23** And I will take away My hand, and then you will see My back, but My face will not be seen by you."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — The Greek uses the second-person singular "you will drive out" (addressing Moses) and includes the Girgashite where the Hebrew text lists the Canaanite.
- **33:11** — The Greek spells the name of Joshua's father Naue (Ναυή) throughout the Pentateuch, rendered here with the standard modern English form Nun.
- **33:13** — The Greek reads "reveal Yourself to me; let me see You clearly" (γνωστῶς, literally "knowingly") instead of the Hebrew "show me your ways," and likewise reads "Reveal Yourself to me" in 33:18.
- **33:19** — The Septuagint reads "in My glory" (τῇ δόξῃ μου) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "all my goodness."
