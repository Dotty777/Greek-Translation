## Text

**27:1** And you shall make an altar of incorruptible wood, five cubits in length and five cubits in width; the altar shall be square, and its height shall be three cubits.

**27:2** And you shall make the horns on the four corners; the horns shall be part of it, and you shall cover them with bronze.

**27:3** And you shall make a rim for the altar, and its cover, and its bowls, and its meat forks, and its firepan; and all its utensils you shall make of bronze.

**27:4** And you shall make for it a bronze grate of network, and you shall make for the grate four bronze rings under the four sides.

**27:5** And you shall place them under the grate of the altar below; and the grate shall extend to halfway up the altar.

**27:6** And you shall make carrying poles for the altar of incorruptible wood, and you shall overlay them with bronze.

**27:7** And you shall insert the carrying poles into the rings, and let the carrying poles be along the sides of the altar when carrying it.

**27:8** You shall make it hollow with boards; according to what was shown to you on the mountain, so you shall make it.

**27:9** And you shall make a courtyard for the tabernacle; on the side toward the southwest there shall be hangings for the courtyard, a length of one hundred cubits for the one side.

**27:10** And their pillars shall be twenty, and their bases twenty of bronze, and their rings and the bands shall be of silver.

**27:11** Likewise on the side toward the east, hangings of one hundred cubits in length; and their pillars shall be twenty, and their bases twenty of bronze, and the rings and the bands of the pillars and their bases shall be overlaid with silver.

**27:12** And for the width of the courtyard toward the sea, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten.

**27:13** And for the width of the courtyard toward the south, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten.

**27:14** And fifteen cubits shall be the height of the hangings for the one side; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:15** And for the second side, fifteen cubits shall be the height of the hangings; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:16** And for the gate of the courtyard there shall be a covering, twenty cubits in height, of blue and purple and twisted scarlet and twisted fine linen with the embroidery of a needleworker; their pillars shall be four, and their bases four.

**27:17** All the pillars of the courtyard all around shall be overlaid with silver, and their capitals shall be of silver, and their bases of bronze.

**27:18** And the length of the courtyard shall be one hundred by one hundred, and the width fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of twisted fine linen, and their bases of bronze.

**27:19** And all the equipment and all the implements and the pegs of the courtyard shall be of bronze.

**27:20** And you, command the sons of Israel, and let them take for you oil from olives, clear, pure, beaten, to burn for light, so that a lamp may burn continually

**27:21** in the tent of testimony, outside the curtain that is over the covenant; Aaron and his sons shall burn it from evening until morning before the Lord. It shall be an eternal statute for your generations from the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Greek lists "a rim" (στεφάνην) and "its cover" (καλυπτῆρα) among the altar's bronze fittings, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists pots for ashes and shovels.
- **27:5** — Greek αὐτούς ("them," masculine plural) refers back to the four bronze rings of verse 4, placing the rings beneath the grate, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places the grate beneath the altar's ledge.
- **27:9** — In verses 9–13 the Greek gives the four sides of the courtyard as southwest (λίβα), east (ἀπηλιώτην), the sea/west (θάλασσαν), and south (νότον), differing from the south, north, west, and east sequence of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **27:14** — In verses 14–16 the Greek explicitly specifies τὸ ὕψος ("the height") for the fifteen-cubit and twenty-cubit dimensions flanking and spanning the gate, whereas verse 18 gives the courtyard's height as five cubits.
- **27:21** — Greek τῆς διαθήκης ("the covenant") varies from τοῦ μαρτυρίου ("the testimony") in the same verse and in 26:33–34, reflecting the Septuagint's rendering of the ark's designation here.
