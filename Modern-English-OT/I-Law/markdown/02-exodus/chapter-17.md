## Text

**17:1** And all the congregation of the sons of Israel departed from the wilderness of Sin according to their encampments by the word of the Lord, and they encamped in Rephidim; and there was no water for the people to drink.

**17:2** And the people were reviling Moses, saying, "Give us water so that we may drink." And Moses said to them, "Why do you revile me, and why do you test the Lord?"

**17:3** And the people thirsted there for water, and the people grumbled there against Moses, saying, "Why is this that you brought us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and the livestock with thirst?"

**17:4** And Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "What shall I do with this people? A little longer and they will stone me to death."

**17:5** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go ahead of this people, and take with yourself some of the elders of the people; and take in your hand the staff with which you struck the river, and you shall go.

**17:6** Here I stand there before you upon the rock in Horeb; and you shall strike the rock, and water shall come out from it, and My people shall drink." And Moses did so before the sons of Israel.

**17:7** And he named the name of that place Testing and Reviling, because of the reviling of the sons of Israel and because of testing the Lord, saying, "Is the Lord among us or not?"

**17:8** And Amalek came and fought against Israel in Rephidim.

**17:9** And Moses said to Joshua, "Choose for yourself able men, and go out and engage in battle against Amalek tomorrow; and look, I stand on the top of the hill, with the staff of God in my hand."

**17:10** And Joshua did just as Moses had told him, and he went out and engaged in battle against Amalek; and Moses and Aaron and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

**17:11** And it happened that whenever Moses lifted up his hands, Israel prevailed; but whenever he lowered his hands, Amalek prevailed.

**17:12** Now the hands of Moses were heavy; and taking a stone they placed it under him, and he sat on it; and Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on this side and one on that side; and the hands of Moses remained supported until the setting of the sun.

**17:13** And Joshua routed Amalek and all his people with the slaughter of the sword.

**17:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "Write this down as a memorial in a book, and put it into the ears of Joshua, that I will utterly blot out the memorial of Amalek from under heaven."

**17:15** And Moses built an altar to the Lord, and he named its name "The Lord is my refuge,"

**17:16** for with a hidden hand the Lord wages war against Amalek from generations to generations.

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — The Greek reads "before the sons of Israel," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "in the sight of the elders of Israel."
- **17:7** — Rather than transliterating the Hebrew place names Massah and Meribah, the Septuagint translates them into Greek as Peirasmos ("Testing") and Loidoresis ("Reviling").
- **17:15** — The Septuagint renders the altar's name "The Lord is my refuge" (Kyrios kataphyge mou), differing from the Hebrew MT "Yahweh-Nissi" ("The Lord is my banner").
- **17:16** — The Greek reads "with a hidden hand" (en cheiri kryphaia), whereas the Hebrew MT has the difficult phrase "a hand upon the throne of Yah."
