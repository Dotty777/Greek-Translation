## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and take firstfruits from all to whom it seems good in their heart; and you shall receive My firstfruits.

**25:3** And this is the firstfruit that you shall receive from them: gold and silver and bronze,

**25:4** and blue and purple and double-dyed scarlet and spun fine linen and goat hair,

**25:5** and ram skins dyed red and blue skins and incorruptible wood,

**25:7** and sardius stones and stones for engraving, for the shoulder-garment and the full-length robe.

**25:8** And you shall make Me a sanctuary, and I will appear among you;

**25:9** and you shall make it for Me according to all that I show you on the mountain, the pattern of the tent and the pattern of all its articles. So you shall make it.

**25:10** And you shall make an ark of testimony of incorruptible wood, two and a half cubits in length, and a cubit and a half in width, and a cubit and a half in height.

**25:11** And you shall overlay it with pure gold; outside and inside you shall gild it; and you shall make for it twisted golden wave-moldings all around.

**25:12** And you shall hammer out for it four golden rings and place them on the four sides: two rings on the one side, and two rings on the second side.

**25:13** And you shall make carrying poles of incorruptible wood, and overlay them with gold;

**25:14** and you shall insert the carrying poles into the rings that are on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark with them.

**25:15** In the rings of the ark the carrying poles shall remain immovable.

**25:16** And you shall put into the ark the testimonies that I will give you.

**25:17** And you shall make an atonement cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits in length, and a cubit and a half in width.

**25:18** And you shall make two cherubim of hammered gold, and place them on both sides of the atonement cover;

**25:19** they shall be made one cherub on this side and one cherub on the second side of the atonement cover; and you shall make the two cherubim on the two sides.

**25:20** The cherubim shall be stretching out their wings above, overshadowing the atonement cover with their wings, with their faces toward one another; toward the atonement cover shall be the faces of the cherubim.

**25:21** And you shall place the atonement cover on top of the ark, and into the ark you shall put the testimonies that I will give you.

**25:22** And I will make Myself known to you from there, and I will speak to you from above the atonement cover, between the two cherubim that are upon the ark of the testimony, and according to all that I command you for the sons of Israel.

**25:23** And you shall make a golden table of pure gold, two cubits in length, and a cubit in breadth, and a cubit and a half in height.

**25:24** And you shall make for it twisted golden wave-moldings all around; and you shall make for it a rim of a handbreadth all around;

**25:25** and you shall make a twisted wave-molding for the rim all around.

**25:26** And you shall make four golden rings, and you shall place the four rings on the four parts of its legs under the rim;

**25:27** and the rings shall serve as holders for the carrying poles, so as to carry the table with them.

**25:28** And you shall make the carrying poles of incorruptible wood, and overlay them with pure gold, and the table shall be carried with them.

**25:29** And you shall make its dishes and the censers and the libation bowls and the ladles with which you will pour drink offerings; of pure gold you shall make them.

**25:30** And you shall place on the table loaves of presence before Me continually.

**25:31** And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work you shall make the lampstand; its shaft and the branches and the cups and the knobs and the lilies shall be of one piece with it.

**25:32** And six branches shall go out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from the second side.

**25:33** And three cups shaped like small nuts, on the one branch a knob and a lily; so for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

**25:34** And on the lampstand four cups shaped like small nuts; on the one branch, knobs and its lilies.

**25:35** The knob under the two branches shall be of one piece with it, and a knob under the four branches of one piece with it; so for the six branches going out from the lampstand. And on the lampstand four cups shaped like small nuts.

**25:36** The knobs and the branches shall be of one piece with it; all of it hammered work, of one piece of pure gold.

**25:37** And you shall make its lamps seven; and you shall set up the lamps, and they shall shine from the one front.

**25:38** And its oil-pourer and its trays you shall make of pure gold;

**25:39** all these articles shall be a talent of pure gold.

**25:40** See that you make them according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain."

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — "Incorruptible wood" renders Greek xyla asēpta (literally "non-rotting wood"), the Septuagint term for acacia wood. Verse 6 of the Hebrew text (listing oil, spices, and incense) is absent in the Greek critical text.
- **25:17** — "Atonement cover" renders Greek hilastērion epithema (literally "propitiatory cover"), shortened to hilastērion in verses 18-22 and traditionally known as the "mercy seat."
- **25:22** — The Greek reads "I will make Myself known to you from there" (gnōsthēsomai), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I will meet with you there."
- **25:23** — The Greek specifies "a golden table of pure gold," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes a table of acacia wood overlaid with gold.
- **25:35** — The Greek specifies a knob under "the two branches" and a knob under "the four branches," and repeats the clause about four nut-shaped cups on the lampstand, differing from the three pairs of two branches in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
