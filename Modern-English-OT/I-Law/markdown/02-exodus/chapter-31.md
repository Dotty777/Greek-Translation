## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Look, I have called by name Bezalel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**31:3** and I have filled him with a divine spirit of wisdom and understanding and knowledge in every work,

**31:4** to devise and to design, to work gold and silver and bronze, and blue and purple and spun scarlet,

**31:5** and stonework, and for the carpentry works of wood, to work according to all the works.

**31:6** And I have given him and Eliab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and to everyone understanding in heart I have given understanding;

**31:7** and they shall make all that I have commanded you: the tent of testimony, and the ark of the covenant and the mercy seat that is on it, and the furnishings of the tent,

**31:8** and the altars, and the table and all its utensils, and the pure lampstand and all its utensils,

**31:9** and the basin and its base,

**31:10** and the ministry robes of Aaron and the robes of his sons, to serve as priests to Me,

**31:11** and the oil of anointing, and the incense of the composition of the sanctuary; according to all that I have commanded you, they shall make them."

**31:12** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:13** "And you, command the sons of Israel, saying, 'See to it and keep My Sabbaths; it is a sign with Me and among you throughout your generations, so that you may know that I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**31:14** And you shall keep the Sabbaths, for this is a holy thing of the Lord for you; the one who profanes it shall surely be put to death; everyone who does work on it, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from the midst of his people.

**31:15** Six days you shall do work, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; everyone who does work on the seventh day shall be put to death.

**31:16** And the sons of Israel shall keep the Sabbaths, to observe them throughout their generations;

**31:17** an eternal covenant between Me and the sons of Israel, it is an eternal sign with Me; for in six days the Lord made the heaven and the earth, and on the seventh day He rested and ceased.'"

**31:18** And He gave to Moses, when He finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the testimony, stone tablets written with the finger of God.

## Translator Notes

- **31:3** — The Greek reads "a divine spirit" (pneuma theion), using an adjective rather than the construct phrase "Spirit of God" in the Hebrew text.
- **31:4** — The Greek includes "blue and purple and spun scarlet" among Bezalel's crafts here, in addition to the metals listed in the Hebrew text.
- **31:6** — The Greek gives the name "Eliab" where the Hebrew text reads "Oholiab," and reads "I have given him and Eliab" rather than "I have appointed with him Oholiab."
- **31:17** — The Greek uses two verbs of cessation, "He rested and ceased" (katepausen kai epausato), where the Hebrew text reads "He rested and was refreshed."
