## Text

**12:1** Now the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,

**12:2** "This month is for you the beginning of months; it is the first for you among the months of the year.

**12:3** Speak to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, 'On the tenth of this month let each of them take a sheep according to fathers' houses, each one a sheep according to a household.

**12:4** And if those in the house are too few to be sufficient for a sheep, he shall take along with himself his neighbor who is near to him; according to the number of souls, each shall reckon what is enough for himself for a sheep.

**12:5** It shall be for you a perfect sheep, a male a year old; you shall take it from the lambs and the young goats.

**12:6** And it shall be kept by you until the fourteenth of this month, and all the multitude of the congregation of the sons of Israel shall slaughter it toward evening.

**12:7** And they shall take some of the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel, on the houses in which they eat them.

**12:8** And they shall eat the flesh on this night, roasted with fire, and they shall eat unleavened bread with bitter herbs.

**12:9** You shall not eat any of it raw nor boiled in water, but only roasted with fire, the head with the feet and the inner parts.

**12:10** You shall not leave any of it until morning, and you shall not break a bone of it; and what remains of it until morning you shall burn with fire.

**12:11** And in this way you shall eat it: your waists girded, and your sandals on your feet, and your staffs in your hands; and you shall eat it with haste. It is a Passover to the Lord.

**12:12** And I will go through the land of Egypt on this night, and I will strike every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from man to livestock, and against all the gods of the Egyptians I will execute vengeance; I am the Lord.

**12:13** And the blood shall be for you as a sign on the houses where you are; and I will see the blood and will shelter you, and there shall not be among you a plague of destruction when I strike in the land of Egypt.

**12:14** And this day shall be to you a memorial, and you shall celebrate it as a feast to the Lord throughout all your generations; as a perpetual statute you shall celebrate it.

**12:15** Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and from the first day you shall remove leaven from your houses; everyone who eats leaven, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from Israel, from the first day until the seventh day.

**12:16** And the first day shall be called holy, and the seventh day shall be a holy convocation to you; you shall do no servile work on them, except whatever shall be done for every soul, that alone shall be done by you.

**12:17** And you shall keep this commandment, for on this day I will bring out your host from the land of Egypt; and you shall observe this day throughout your generations as a perpetual statute.

**12:18** Beginning on the fourteenth day of the first month, from evening, you shall eat unleavened bread until the twenty-first day of the month, until evening.

**12:19** Seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses; everyone who eats anything leavened, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from the congregation of Israel, both among the proselytes and among the natives of the land.

**12:20** You shall not eat anything leavened, but in every dwelling place of yours you shall eat unleavened bread.'"

**12:21** Then Moses called all the council of elders of the sons of Israel and said to them, "Go and take for yourselves a sheep according to your kindred, and you shall sacrifice the Passover.

**12:22** And you shall take a bunch of hyssop, and having dipped it into the blood that is beside the door, you shall touch the lintel and both of the doorposts with some of the blood that is beside the door; and none of you shall go out of the door of his house until morning.

**12:23** And the Lord will pass by to strike the Egyptians, and He will see the blood on the lintel and on both of the doorposts; and the Lord will pass by the door, and He will not allow the destroyer to enter into your houses to strike.

**12:24** And you shall keep this word as a statute for yourself and for your sons forever.

**12:25** And when you enter into the land which the Lord gives you, just as He spoke, you shall keep this service.

**12:26** And it shall be, when your sons say to you, 'What is this service?'

**12:27** that you shall say to them, 'This Passover is a sacrifice to the Lord, as He sheltered the houses of the sons of Israel in Egypt when He struck the Egyptians, but delivered our houses.'" And the people bowed down and worshiped.

**12:28** And the sons of Israel went and did just as the Lord commanded Moses; so they did.

**12:29** Now it came about at midnight that the Lord struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat on the throne to the firstborn of the captive woman who was in the pit, and to the firstborn of all livestock.

**12:30** And Pharaoh rose up in the night, he and his attendants and all the Egyptians, and there was a great outcry in all the land of Egypt, for there was not a house in which there was not someone dead.

**12:31** And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron in the night and said to them, "Rise up and go out from among my people, both you and the sons of Israel; go and serve the Lord your God, just as you say.

**12:32** And taking both your sheep and your cattle, go on your way, and bless me also."

**12:33** And the Egyptians were urgently pressing the people to drive them out of the land in haste, for they said, "We are all dying."

**12:34** So the people took up the dough before it was leavened, their batches of dough bound up in their garments on their shoulders.

**12:35** And the sons of Israel did just as Moses had instructed them, and they asked from the Egyptians articles of silver and gold, and clothing.

**12:36** And the Lord gave favor to His people before the Egyptians, and they lent to them; and they plundered the Egyptians.

**12:37** Now the sons of Israel set out from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides the dependents.

**12:38** And a great mixed crowd went up with them, and sheep and cattle and very much livestock.

**12:39** And they baked the dough which they had brought out of Egypt into unleavened hearth cakes, for it had not been leavened; for the Egyptians drove them out, and they were not able to linger, nor had they prepared provisions for themselves for the journey.

**12:40** Now the dwelling of the sons of Israel, which they dwelt in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan, was four hundred thirty-five years.

**12:41** And it came about after the four hundred thirty-five years that all the host of the Lord went out from the land of Egypt by night.

**12:42** It is a vigil to the Lord for bringing them out from the land of Egypt; that very night is a vigil to the Lord, to be for all the sons of Israel throughout their generations.

**12:43** Now the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, "This is the law of the Passover: no foreigner shall eat of it.

**12:44** And as for every household servant or one bought with silver, you shall circumcise him, and then he shall eat of it.

**12:45** A temporary resident or a hired worker shall not eat of it.

**12:46** In one house it shall be eaten, and you shall not carry any of the flesh outside from the house, and you shall not break a bone of it.

**12:47** All the congregation of the sons of Israel shall observe it.

**12:48** And if any proselyte comes to you to observe the Passover to the Lord, you shall circumcise every male belonging to him; and then he shall come near to observe it, and he shall be just like the native of the land. No uncircumcised person shall eat of it.

**12:49** There shall be one law for the native-born and for the proselyte who has come among you."

**12:50** And the sons of Israel did just as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron concerning them; so they did.

**12:51** And it came about on that day that the Lord brought the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt with their host.

## Translator Notes

- **12:10** — The clause "and you shall not break a bone of it" appears here in the Septuagint in addition to 12:46 (absent from the Masoretic Text in v. 10). The Greek spelling of the first two verbs (-εται) is an orthographic variant for the second-person plural future (-ετε), matching the third verb and 12:46.
- **12:13** — Greek σκεπάσω ("I will shelter") renders the Passover verb here and in 12:27, emphasizing protective covering (compare παρελεύσεται, "will pass by," in 12:23).
- **12:19** — Greek γιώραις is a transliteration of the Aramaic term giyora ("proselyte" or "resident alien"), corresponding to προσήλυτος in 12:48–49.
- **12:22** — Greek παρὰ τὴν θύραν ("beside the door," twice) reflects the Septuagint's understanding of the Hebrew term as "threshold" rather than "basin."
- **12:40** — The Septuagint specifies the dwelling as "in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan" and gives the duration as "four hundred thirty-five years" (here and in 12:41, which also adds "by night"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads 430 years in Egypt alone.
