## Text

**10:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Go in to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and that of his attendants, so that these signs may come upon them in succession,

**10:2** in order that you may recount in the ears of your children and to the children of your children all the ways I have mocked the Egyptians, and My signs that I performed among them; and you will know that I am the Lord."

**10:3** And Moses and Aaron went in before Pharaoh and said to him, "Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: 'How long are you unwilling to show reverence to Me? Send away My people so that they may serve Me.

**10:4** But if you are not willing to send away My people, look, at this hour tomorrow I am bringing abundant locusts upon all your borders;

**10:5** and they will cover the face of the land, and you will not be able to see the land; and they will devour all the remainder of the land that was left behind, which the hail left for you, and they will devour every tree growing for you upon the land;

**10:6** and your houses will be filled, and the houses of your attendants, and all the houses in all the land of the Egyptians—things that neither your fathers nor their great-grandfathers have ever seen, from the day they came to be upon the earth until this day.'" And Moses turned away and went out from Pharaoh.

**10:7** And the attendants of Pharaoh said to him, "How long will this be a snare to us? Send away the people so that they may serve their God. Or do you want to know that Egypt is destroyed?"

**10:8** And they brought both Moses and Aaron back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, "Go and serve your God. But who and who are the ones going?"

**10:9** And Moses said, "With our young men and elders we will go, with our sons and daughters and sheep and cattle, for it is a festival of the Lord."

**10:10** And he said to them, "So be it—the Lord be with you! Just as I am sending you away, shall I send your dependents too? See, evil lies before you.

**10:11** Not so! Instead, let the men go and serve God, for this is what you yourselves were seeking." And they drove them out from the presence of Pharaoh.

**10:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt, and let locusts come up upon the land, and they will devour every plant of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail left behind."

**10:13** And Moses lifted up the staff toward heaven and brought a south wind upon the land all that day and all the night; morning came, and the south wind took up the locusts,

**10:14** and brought them up over all the land of Egypt, and they settled upon all the borders of Egypt, exceedingly numerous; before them there have never been such locusts, and after these things it will not be so.

**10:15** And they covered the face of the land, and the land was ruined; and they devoured every plant of the land and all the fruit of the trees that was left from the hail; nothing green was left on the trees or in any plant of the field in the land of Egypt.

**10:16** And Pharaoh hurried to summon Moses and Aaron, saying, "I have sinned before the Lord your God and against you.

**10:17** Therefore forgive my sin just this once more, and pray to the Lord your God, and let Him remove this death from me."

**10:18** And Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to God.

**10:19** And the Lord shifted a strong wind from the sea, and it took up the locusts and cast them into the Red Sea; and not a single locust was left in all the land of Egypt.

**10:20** And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he did not send away the sons of Israel.

**10:21** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward heaven, and let there be darkness over the land of Egypt, a palpable darkness."

**10:22** And Moses stretched out his hand toward heaven, and there came darkness, gloom, tempest over all the land of Egypt for three days;

**10:23** and no one saw his brother for three days, and no one rose up from his bed for three days; but for all the sons of Israel there was light in all the places where they lived.

**10:24** And Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron, saying, "Go, serve the Lord your God; only leave behind the sheep and the cattle, and let your dependents go away with you."

**10:25** And Moses said, "Rather, you yourself will also give us burnt offerings and sacrifices that we will offer to the Lord our God,

**10:26** and our livestock will go with us, and we will not leave behind a hoof; for from them we will take to serve the Lord our God, and we do not know with what we are to serve the Lord our God until we arrive there."

**10:27** And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he was not willing to send them away.

**10:28** And Pharaoh said, "Go away from me! Watch yourself against seeing my face again, and on whatever day you appear before me, you will die."

**10:29** And Moses said, "You have spoken it; I will no longer appear before your face."

## Translator Notes

- **10:7** — The Greek reads "Or do you want to know that Egypt is destroyed?" (without the "not yet" of the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and uses σκῶλον (lit. "thorn, stake, stumbling block") for "snare."
- **10:10** — Greek ἀποσκευή ("baggage, household train, dependents") is the Septuagint's standard rendering for the non-combatant family members and little ones accompanying the men (so also in 10:24).
- **10:13** — The Septuagint reads "a south wind" (ἄνεμον νότον) rather than the Hebrew "east wind," and has no explicit subject "the Lord" before "brought."
- **10:22** — The Greek intensifies the plague with an asyndetic triad—"darkness, gloom, tempest" (σκότος γνόφος θύελλα)—and in 10:23 specifies "from his bed" (κοίτης) where the Hebrew has "from his place."
