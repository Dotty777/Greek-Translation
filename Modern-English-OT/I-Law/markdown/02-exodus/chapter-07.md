## Text

**7:1** And the Lord said to Moses, saying, "Look, I have given you as a god to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother will be your prophet.

**7:2** And you will speak to him all the things that I command you, and Aaron your brother will speak to Pharaoh so that he may send out the sons of Israel from his land.

**7:3** But I will harden the heart of Pharaoh, and I will multiply My signs and wonders in the land of Egypt.

**7:4** And Pharaoh will not listen to you; and I will lay My hand upon Egypt, and with My power I will bring out My people, the sons of Israel, from the land of Egypt with great vengeance.

**7:5** And all the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord, as I stretch out My hand upon Egypt; and I will bring out the sons of Israel from their midst."

**7:6** And Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord commanded them; so they did.

**7:7** Now Moses was eighty years old, and Aaron his brother was eighty-three years old, when he spoke to Pharaoh.

**7:8** And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**7:9** "And if Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, 'Give us a sign or a wonder,' then you will say to Aaron your brother, 'Take the staff and throw it upon the ground before Pharaoh and before his attendants,' and it will become a dragon."

**7:10** So Moses and Aaron went in before Pharaoh and his attendants, and they did so, just as the Lord commanded them; and Aaron threw the staff before Pharaoh and before his attendants, and it became a dragon.

**7:11** Then Pharaoh called together the wise men of Egypt and the sorcerers; and the enchanters of the Egyptians also did the same by their sorceries,

**7:12** and each one threw his staff, and they became dragons; and Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs.

**7:13** And the heart of Pharaoh grew strong, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded them.

**7:14** Then the Lord said to Moses, "The heart of Pharaoh is weighed down so as not to send out the people.

**7:15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning; look, he is going out to the water, and you will be meeting him at the bank of the river; and you will take in your hand the staff that was turned into a snake,

**7:16** and you will say to him, 'The Lord, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying, "Send out My people so that they may serve Me in the wilderness"; and look, you have not listened until now.

**7:17** Thus says the Lord: "By this you will know that I am the Lord: look, I am striking the water that is in the river with the staff that is in My hand, and it will turn into blood;

**7:18** and the fish that are in the river will die, and the river will stink, and the Egyptians will not be able to drink water from the river."'"

**7:19** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron your brother, 'Take your staff in your hand, and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, and over their rivers, and over their canals, and over their marshes, and over all their standing water, and it will become blood.'" And blood came to be in all the land of Egypt, both in the wood and in the stone.

**7:20** And Moses and Aaron did so, just as the Lord commanded them; and lifting up his staff, he struck the water that was in the river before Pharaoh and before his attendants, and all the water that was in the river turned into blood.

**7:21** And the fish that were in the river died, and the river stank, and the Egyptians were not able to drink water from the river, and the blood was in all the land of Egypt.

**7:22** And the enchanters of the Egyptians also did the same by their sorceries; and the heart of Pharaoh hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had said.

**7:23** And Pharaoh turned around and went into his house, and he did not set his mind even on this.

**7:24** And all the Egyptians dug around the river so as to drink water from the river, and they were not able to drink water from the river.

**7:25** And seven days were fulfilled after the Lord struck the river.

**7:26** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, and you will say to him, 'Thus says the Lord: "Send out My people so that they may serve Me.

**7:27** And if you are not willing to send them out, look, I am striking all your borders with frogs.

**7:28** And the river will pour forth frogs; and coming up, they will enter into your houses, and into the inner chambers of your bedrooms, and upon your beds, and into the houses of your attendants and of your people, and in your dough and in your ovens;

**7:29** and upon you and upon your attendants and upon your people the frogs will come up."'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:9** — The Greek uses δράκων ("dragon" or great serpent) here and in 7:10, 7:12, distinct from ὄφις ("snake") in 7:15.
- **7:13** — The Greek reads "just as the Lord had commanded them" (ἐνετείλατο αὐτοῖς), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "just as the Lord had spoken."
- **7:19** — The final clause shifts to an aorist indicative in Greek ("And blood came to be"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text continues the future prediction ("and there shall be blood").
- **7:26** — Verses 7:26–29 follow the Greek and Hebrew verse numbering, corresponding to 8:1–4 in most English versions.
