## Text

**9:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people so that they may serve Me;

**9:2** if then you are not willing to send away My people, but still hold onto them,

**9:3** look, the hand of the Lord will be upon your livestock that are in the fields, both on the horses and on the beasts of burden and the camels and cattle and sheep, a very great death.

**9:4** And at that time I will make a wondrous distinction between the livestock of the Egyptians and the livestock of the sons of Israel; not a single thing will die from all that belongs to the sons of Israel.'"

**9:5** And God set a time limit, saying, "Tomorrow the Lord will do this thing upon the land."

**9:6** And the Lord did this thing the next day, and all the livestock of the Egyptians died; but of the livestock of the sons of Israel not one died.

**9:7** And when Pharaoh saw that not one of all the livestock of the sons of Israel had died, Pharaoh's heart became heavy, and he did not send away the people.

**9:8** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, "Take your hands full of furnace soot, and let Moses scatter it toward the sky before Pharaoh and before his servants,

**9:9** and let it become fine dust over all the land of Egypt; and upon the people and upon the four-footed animals there will be sores, erupting blisters, both on the people and on the four-footed animals and in all the land of Egypt."

**9:10** And he took the soot of the furnace before Pharaoh, and Moses scattered it toward the sky; and there came to be sores, erupting blisters, on the people and on the four-footed animals.

**9:11** And the sorcerers could not stand before Moses because of the sores, for the sores were on the sorcerers and in all the land of Egypt.

**9:12** But the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded.

**9:13** And the Lord said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people so that they may serve Me.

**9:14** For at this present time I am sending out all My afflictions against your heart and that of your servants and of your people, so that you may know that there is no other like Me in all the earth.

**9:15** For now, stretching out My hand, I will strike you, and I will put your people to death, and you will be wiped out from the earth;

**9:16** and for this reason you were preserved, so that I may display My strength in you, and so that My name may be proclaimed in all the earth.

**9:17** Do you then still lay claim to My people, so as not to send them away?

**9:18** Look, at this hour tomorrow I am raining down very heavy hail, such as has not occurred in Egypt from the day it was founded until this day.

**9:19** Now therefore hurry to gather your livestock and all that you have in the field; for all the people and the livestock that belong to you in the field and do not enter a house, when the hail falls on them, they will die.'"

**9:20** The one among the servants of Pharaoh who feared the word of the Lord gathered his livestock into the houses;

**9:21** but whoever did not pay attention in his mind to the word of the Lord left the livestock in the fields.

**9:22** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, and there will be hail over all the land of Egypt, both on the people and the livestock and on every plant that is on the earth."

**9:23** And Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky, and the Lord gave thunders and hail, and fire ran along the earth; and the Lord rained hail upon all the land of Egypt.

**9:24** And there was the hail, and the fire flaming within the hail; and the hail was very heavy, such as had not occurred in Egypt from the day a nation came into being upon it.

**9:25** And the hail struck in all the land of Egypt, from human to livestock, and the hail struck every plant that was in the field, and the hail shattered all the trees that were in the fields;

**9:26** only in the land of Goshen, where the sons of Israel were, there was no hail.

**9:27** And Pharaoh sent and summoned Moses and Aaron and said to them, "I have sinned this time; the Lord is righteous, and I and my people are ungodly.

**9:28** Pray therefore for me to the Lord, and let there cease to be thunders of God and hail and fire; and I will send you away, and you will no longer continue to stay."

**9:29** And Moses said to him, "As soon as I go out of the city, I will spread out my hands, and the thunders will cease, and the hail and the rain will be no more, so that you may know that the earth belongs to the Lord.

**9:30** But as for you and your servants, I know that you do not yet fear God."

**9:31** Now the flax and the barley were struck, for the barley was standing ripe and the flax was producing seed;

**9:32** but the wheat and the spelt were not struck, for they were late.

**9:33** And Moses went out from Pharaoh outside the city and stretched out his hands to the Lord; and the thunders and the hail ceased, and the rain no longer dripped upon the earth.

**9:34** But when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunders had ceased, he continued to sin, and he made his own heart heavy, and that of his servants.

**9:35** And Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not send away the sons of Israel, just as the Lord had spoken to Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — "A single thing" renders Greek rheton (literally "a specified thing"), reflecting the Hebrew idiom for "anything."
- **9:15** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text is commonly read as a counterfactual past ("I could have stretched out my hand..."), the Septuagint uses future indicatives ("I will strike... I will put to death... you will be wiped out").
- **9:17** — "Lay claim to" translates the Greek middle verb empoieomai with the genitive, meaning to assert ownership over or detain.
- **9:23** — "Thunders" renders Greek phonai (literally "voices" or "sounds"), a Septuagint Hebraism for thunder that recurs in 9:28, 29, 33, and 34.
