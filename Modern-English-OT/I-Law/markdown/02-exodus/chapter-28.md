## Text

**28:1** And you, bring near to yourself both Aaron your brother and his sons from among the sons of Israel to serve as priests to Me: Aaron, and Nadab and Abihu and Eleazar and Ithamar, sons of Aaron.

**28:2** And you shall make a holy garment for Aaron your brother, for honor and glory.

**28:3** And you, speak to all who are wise in mind, whom I have filled with a spirit of perception, and they shall make the holy garment of Aaron for the sanctuary, in which he shall serve as priest to Me.

**28:4** And these are the garments which they shall make: the breastpiece and the shoulder-garment and the full-length tasseled tunic and a turban and a sash. And they shall make holy garments for Aaron and for his sons to serve as priests to Me.

**28:5** And they shall take the gold and the blue and the purple and the scarlet and the fine linen.

**28:6** And they shall make the shoulder-garment of twisted fine linen, the work of an embroidering weaver.

**28:7** Two shoulder-pieces joining one to the other shall be on it, fitted on the two sides;

**28:8** and the woven work of the shoulder-pieces, which is upon it, according to its workmanship shall be of one piece with it, of gold and blue and purple and spun scarlet and twisted fine linen.

**28:9** And you shall take the two stones, stones of emerald, and you shall engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel:

**28:10** six names on the one stone, and the remaining six names on the second stone, according to their births,

**28:11** the work of a stone-carving craft; as the engraving of a seal you shall engrave the two stones upon

**28:12** the shoulders of the shoulder-garment. They are stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel; and Aaron shall carry the names of the sons of Israel before the Lord upon his two shoulders, a remembrance concerning them.

**28:13** And you shall make small shields of pure gold;

**28:14** and you shall make two fringes of pure gold, intermingled with flowers, the work of braiding; and you shall place the braided fringes upon the small shields along their shoulder-edges on the front.

**28:15** And you shall make an oracle of judgments, the work of an embroiderer; according to the pattern of the shoulder-garment you shall make it; of gold and blue and purple and twisted scarlet and twisted fine linen

**28:16** you shall make it. It shall be square, doubled; a span its length and a span its width.

**28:17** And you shall weave into it a four-rowed woven work set with stones. There shall be a row of stones: sardius, topaz, and emerald, the first row;

**28:18** and the second row, carbuncle and sapphire and jasper;

**28:19** and the third row, ligure, agate, amethyst;

**28:20** and the fourth row, chrysolite and beryl and onyx; covered around with gold, bound together in gold, let them be according to their row.

**28:21** And let the stones be twelve from the names of the sons of Israel, according to their names; engravings of seals, each according to the name, let them be for twelve tribes.

**28:22** And you shall make on the oracle intertwined fringes, chain-work of pure gold.

**28:29** And Aaron shall take the names of the sons of Israel upon the oracle of judgment on his chest, when entering into the sanctuary, a remembrance before God. And you shall put the fringes on the oracle of judgment; the chains you shall place on both sides of the oracle, and the two small shields you shall place on both shoulders of the shoulder-garment in front.

**28:30** And you shall place upon the oracle of judgment the manifestation and the truth; and it shall be upon the chest of Aaron whenever he enters into the sanctuary before the Lord; and Aaron shall bear the judgments of the sons of Israel upon his chest before the Lord continually.

**28:31** And you shall make a full-length under-robe, entirely of blue.

**28:32** And the neck-opening shall be in the middle of it, having a border around the neck-opening, the work of a weaver, the seam woven together from it, so that it may not tear.

**28:33** And you shall make under the hem of the under-robe below, as of a blossoming pomegranate, small pomegranates of blue and purple and spun scarlet and twisted fine linen, upon the hem of the under-robe all around; in the same form, small gold pomegranates, and bells in between them all around;

**28:34** next to a small gold pomegranate, a bell and a floral ornament upon the hem of the under-robe all around.

**28:35** And for Aaron while ministering, its sound shall be audible as he enters into the sanctuary before the Lord and as he goes out, so that he may not die.

**28:36** And you shall make a plate of pure gold, and you shall stamp on it the impression of a seal: "Holiness of the Lord."

**28:37** And you shall place it upon twisted blue, and it shall be upon the miter; on the front of the miter it shall be.

**28:38** And it shall be upon the forehead of Aaron, and Aaron shall take away the sins of the holy things, whatever the sons of Israel may consecrate, of every gift of their holy things; and it shall be upon the forehead of Aaron continually, acceptable for them before the Lord.

**28:39** And the tasseled parts of the tunics shall be of fine linen; and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and you shall make a sash, the work of an embroiderer.

**28:40** And for the sons of Aaron you shall make tunics and sashes, and turbans you shall make for them, for honor and glory.

**28:41** And you shall put them on Aaron your brother and his sons with him; and you shall anoint them, and you shall fill their hands, and you shall consecrate them, so that they may serve as priests to Me.

**28:42** And you shall make for them linen breeches to cover the nakedness of their flesh; from waist to thighs they shall be.

**28:43** And Aaron and his sons shall wear them whenever they enter into the tent of the testimony, when they approach to minister at the altar of the sanctuary; and they shall not bring sin upon themselves, so that they may not die; an eternal statute for him and for his seed after him.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Greek spells Aaron's second son Abiud (Ἀβιούδ), rendered with standard modern English Abihu throughout Exodus–Numbers.
- **28:11** — In the Septuagint text, verses 11–12 omit the instructions for gold filigree settings found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, so the preposition 'upon' at the end of verse 11 links directly into verse 12.
- **28:15** — Greek logion literally means 'oracle' (rendered here as 'oracle of judgments'), corresponding to the breastpiece of judgment in the Hebrew text.
- **28:29** — Verses 23–28, which detail the gold rings and cords attaching the breastpiece in the Hebrew text, are absent in the Septuagint; the fastening is summarized at the end of verse 29.
- **28:30** — Greek delosis ('manifestation') and aletheia ('truth') translate the Hebrew Urim and Thummim.
