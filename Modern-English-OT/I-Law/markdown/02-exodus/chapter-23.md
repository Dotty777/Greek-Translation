## Text

**23:1** You shall not accept a vain report; you shall not side with the unjust person to become an unjust witness.

**23:2** You shall not be with the majority in wickedness; you shall not join with a crowd to turn aside with the majority, so as to shut out justice.

**23:3** And you shall not show pity to a poor person in judgment.

**23:4** Now if you encounter your enemy's ox or his pack animal wandering astray, turning it back, you shall return it to him.

**23:5** And if you see your enemy's pack animal fallen under its load, you shall not pass by it, but you shall lift it up together with him.

**23:6** You shall not pervert the judgment of a poor person in his case.

**23:7** From every unjust matter you shall stay away; the innocent and the righteous you shall not kill, and you shall not acquit the ungodly for the sake of gifts.

**23:8** And you shall not take gifts; for gifts blind the eyes of those who see and ruin righteous words.

**23:9** And you shall not oppress a resident alien; for you know the soul of the resident alien, because you yourselves were resident aliens in the land of Egypt.

**23:10** Six years you shall sow your land and gather its produce;

**23:11** but in the seventh you shall make a release and let it lie fallow, and the poor of your nation shall eat; and what is left over the wild beasts shall eat. So you shall do with your vineyard and your olive grove.

**23:12** Six days you shall do your works, and on the seventh day there shall be rest, so that your ox and your pack animal may rest, and so that the son of your female servant and the resident alien may rest.

**23:13** Observe everything that I have spoken to you; and the name of other gods you shall not bring to mind, nor shall it be heard from your mouth.

**23:14** Three times of the year you shall celebrate a festival to Me.

**23:15** You shall be careful to keep the festival of unleavened bread: seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of new crops, for in it you came out of Egypt. You shall not appear before Me empty-handed.

**23:16** And you shall keep the festival of the harvest of the first produce of your labors, of whatever you sow in your field, and the festival of completion at the outgoing of the year, at the gathering in of your labors from your field.

**23:17** Three times of the year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord your God.

**23:18** For when I cast out nations from before your face and enlarge your borders, you shall not offer the blood of My incense-offering with leaven, nor shall the fat of My festival remain overnight until morning.

**23:19** The firstfruits of your first produce you shall bring into the house of the Lord your God. You shall not boil a lamb in its mother's milk.

**23:20** And look, I am sending My angel before your face so that he may guard you on the way, in order that he may bring you into the land which I have prepared for you.

**23:21** Pay attention to yourself and listen to him, and do not disobey him; for he will by no means hold back from you, for My name is upon him.

**23:22** If you truly listen to My voice, and do all things that I command you, and keep My covenant, you shall be to Me a treasured people above all the nations; for all the earth is Mine, and you shall be to Me a royal priesthood and a holy nation. These words you shall speak to the sons of Israel. If you truly listen to My voice, and do all things that I say to you, I will be an enemy to your enemies and oppose those who oppose you.

**23:23** For My angel will go leading you, and will bring you to the Amorite and the Hittite and the Perizzite and the Canaanite and the Girgashite and the Hivite and the Jebusite, and I will wipe them out.

**23:24** You shall not bow down to their gods, nor shall you serve them; you shall not do according to their works, but you shall utterly tear them down and completely shatter their pillars.

**23:25** And you shall serve the Lord your God, and I will bless your bread and your wine and your water, and I will turn sickness away from you.

**23:26** There shall not be one childless or barren in your land; I will fill up the number of your days.

**23:27** And I will send terror leading ahead of you, and I will throw into panic all the nations among whom you enter, and I will make all your adversaries fugitives.

**23:28** And I will send the hornets ahead of you, and you shall drive out the Amorites and the Hivites and the Canaanites and the Hittites away from you.

**23:29** I will not drive them out in a single year, so that the land does not become desolate and the wild beasts of the land do not become many against you;

**23:30** little by little I will drive them out from you, until you increase and inherit the land.

**23:31** And I will set your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the great river Euphrates; and I will hand over into your hands those who dwell in the land, and I will drive them out from you.

**23:32** You shall not make a covenant with them and their gods.

**23:33** And they shall not dwell in your land, so that they do not cause you to sin against Me; for if you serve their gods, these will be a stumbling block to you.

## Translator Notes

- **23:15** — The Greek "month of new crops" (μηνὸς τῶν νέων) translates the Hebrew month name Abib (meaning fresh ears of grain).
- **23:18** — The Greek begins with an expansion paralleling Exodus 34:24 ("For when I cast out nations...") and renders "My sacrifice" as "My incense-offering" (θυμιάματός μου).
- **23:19** — The Greek specifies "a lamb" (ἄρνα), whereas the Hebrew specifies a young goat.
- **23:22** — The Greek includes a major expansion drawn from Exodus 19:5-6 ("you shall be to Me a treasured people... These words you shall speak to the sons of Israel") before resuming the conditional promise of protection.
