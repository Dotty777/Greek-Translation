## Text

**37:1** And they made ten curtains for the tabernacle;

**37:2** twenty-eight cubits was the length of the one curtain; it was the same for all of them; and four cubits was the width of the one curtain.

**37:3** And he made the curtain of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen, the work of a weaver, cherubim;

**37:4** and they placed it upon four incorruptible pillars overlaid with gold; and their capitals were gold, and their four bases were silver.

**37:5** And they made the curtain of the door of the tent of testimony of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen, a woven work of the cherubim,

**37:6** and their five pillars and the rings; and they overlaid their capitals with gold, and the five bases were bronze.

**37:7** And they made the courtyard: the parts toward the southwest, hangings of the courtyard of twisted fine linen, a hundred by a hundred;

**37:8** and their pillars were twenty, and their bases were twenty.

**37:9** And the side toward the north was a hundred by a hundred, and the side toward the south was a hundred by a hundred; and their pillars were twenty, and their bases were twenty.

**37:10** And on the side toward the sea, curtains of fifty cubits; their pillars were ten, and their bases were ten.

**37:11** And the side toward the east was fifty cubits, the part at the back;

**37:12** and their pillars were three, and their bases were three.

**37:13** And on the second back, on this side and on that side by the gate of the courtyard, curtains of a hundred and fifty cubits; their pillars were three, and their bases were three.

**37:14** All the curtains of the tabernacle were of twisted fine linen;

**37:15** and the bases of their pillars were bronze, and their hooks were silver, and their capitals were overlaid with silver, and the pillars were overlaid with silver, all the pillars of the courtyard.

**37:16** And the curtain of the gate of the courtyard was the work of an embroiderer, of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and twisted fine linen, twenty cubits in length, and the height and the width were five cubits, matching the hangings of the courtyard;

**37:17** and their pillars were four, and their four bases were bronze, and their hooks were silver, and their capitals were overlaid with silver.

**37:18** And they themselves were overlaid with silver, and all the pegs of the courtyard all around were bronze.

**37:19** And this is the arrangement of the tent of testimony, just as was commanded to Moses, that the ministry belong to the Levites through Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

**37:20** And Bezalel the son of Uri, from the tribe of Judah, did just as the Lord commanded Moses,

**37:21** and Eliab the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, who designed as master craftsman the woven works and the needlework and the embroidered works, to weave with the scarlet and the fine linen.

## Translator Notes

- **37:4** — Greek aseptos (literally 'incorruptible' or 'rot-resistant') is the Septuagint's regular rendering for acacia wood.
- **37:5** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text (36:37), which describes the entrance screen as an embroiderer's work, the Greek text specifies 'a woven work of the cherubim.'
- **37:7** — The Greek text gives the dimensions as 'a hundred by a hundred' here and in 37:9 (where it lists both a north and a south side in addition to the southwest side in 37:7), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'a hundred cubits.'
- **37:13** — The Greek text reads 'a hundred and fifty cubits' for the curtains on the second flank (notos, literally 'back') by the courtyard gate, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'fifteen cubits.'
