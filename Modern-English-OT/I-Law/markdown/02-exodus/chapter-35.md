## Text

**35:1** And Moses gathered together all the congregation of the sons of Israel and said, "These are the words that the Lord spoke, to do them:

**35:2** Six days you shall do works, but on the seventh day there is rest—a holy thing, a Sabbath, a rest to the Lord; everyone who does work on it shall die.

**35:3** You shall not kindle a fire in any dwelling of yours on the day of the Sabbaths; I am the Lord."

**35:4** And Moses spoke to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the word that the Lord commanded, saying,

**35:5** 'Take from among yourselves a contribution for the Lord; everyone who is willing in heart shall bring the firstfruits to the Lord: gold, silver, bronze,

**35:6** blue, purple, double-dyed spun scarlet, twisted fine linen, and goat hair,

**35:7** and ram skins dyed red, and blue-dyed skins, and rot-resistant wood,

**35:9** and sardius stones and stones for engraving for the ephod and the full-length robe.

**35:10** And let everyone wise in heart among you come and make all that the Lord has commanded:

**35:11** the tabernacle and the coverings and the crossbars and the bars and the pillars,

**35:12** and the ark of the testimony and its carrying poles and its mercy seat and the curtain, and the hangings of the courtyard and its pillars, and the emerald stones, and the incense, and the anointing oil,

**35:13** and the table and all its utensils,

**35:14** and the lampstand of light and all its utensils,

**35:16** and the altar and all its utensils,

**35:19** and the holy robes of Aaron the priest, and the robes in which they will minister, and the tunics of the priesthood for the sons of Aaron, and the anointing oil, and the compounded incense.'"

**35:20** And all the congregation of the sons of Israel went out from Moses.

**35:21** And they brought up—each one whose heart moved them, and all to whose soul it seemed good—a contribution; and they brought a contribution to the Lord for all the works of the tabernacle of the testimony and for all its workmanship and for all the robes of the sanctuary.

**35:22** And the men brought from the women—everyone to whose mind it seemed good brought signet rings and earrings and finger rings and hair clasps and armlets, every gold article;

**35:23** and all those who brought contributions of gold to the Lord. And everyone with whom fine linen and blue-dyed skins and ram skins dyed red were found brought them.

**35:24** And everyone setting apart the contribution brought silver and bronze, the contributions to the Lord; and those with whom rot-resistant wood was found brought it for all the works of the construction.

**35:25** And every woman wise in mind to spin with her hands brought spun goods: the blue and the purple and the scarlet and the fine linen;

**35:26** and all the women to whose mind it seemed good in wisdom spun the goat hair.

**35:27** And the rulers brought the emerald stones and the stones for setting for the ephod and the oracle,

**35:28** and the compounds and the oil of anointing and the compounding of the incense.

**35:29** And every man and woman whose mind moved them to go in to do all the works that the Lord had commanded to be done through Moses—the sons of Israel brought a contribution to the Lord.

**35:30** And Moses said to the sons of Israel, "Look, God has called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah,

**35:31** and He has filled him with a spirit of wisdom and understanding and knowledge of all things,

**35:32** to design according to all the works of master craftsmanship, to work the gold and the silver and the bronze,

**35:33** and to carve stone, and to fashion the wood, and to work in every work of wisdom;

**35:34** and indeed He has put it in his mind to instruct—both to him and to Eliab the son of Ahisamach from the tribe of Dan;

**35:35** He has filled them with wisdom and understanding of mind, to understand how to do all the works of the sanctuary, and to weave the woven and embroidered works with scarlet and fine linen, to do every work of master craftsmanship in embroidery."

## Translator Notes

- **35:7** — "Blue-dyed skins" (dermata hyakinthina) and "rot-resistant wood" (xyla asepta) reflect the Septuagint renderings for the Hebrew tahash skins and acacia wood.
- **35:9** — The Septuagint omits verses 8, 15, 17, and 18 found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and presents a shorter, rearranged inventory of the tabernacle furnishings in verses 9-19.
- **35:27** — "The oracle" (to logion) is the Septuagint term for the high priest's breastpiece of judgment, emphasizing its function in divine consultation.
- **35:34** — "Eliab" (Eliab) preserves the Septuagint form of the name of the artisan from Dan, corresponding to Oholiab in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
