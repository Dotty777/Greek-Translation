## Text

**29:1** And these are the things that you shall do for them: you shall sanctify them so that they may serve as priests to Me. You shall take one young calf from the cattle and two unblemished rams,

**29:2** and unleavened loaves mixed with oil, and unleavened flatbreads spread with oil; you shall make them of fine flour from wheat.

**29:3** And you shall place them in one basket. And you shall present them in the basket, and the young calf and the two rams.

**29:4** And you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the doors of the tent of testimony, and you shall wash them with water.

**29:5** And taking the robes, you shall clothe Aaron your brother with the full-length tunic and the shoulder-garment and the oracle against the shoulder-garment.

**29:6** And you shall put the turban on his head, and you shall place the holy plate on the turban.

**29:7** And you shall take some of the anointing oil and pour it on his head, and you shall anoint him.

**29:8** And you shall bring his sons forward, and you shall clothe them with tunics.

**29:9** And you shall bind them with the sashes, and you shall put the headdresses on them; and the priesthood to Me shall be theirs forever. And you shall consecrate the hands of Aaron and the hands of his sons.

**29:10** And you shall bring the calf to the doors of the tent of testimony, and Aaron and his sons shall place their hands on the head of the calf before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony.

**29:11** And you shall slaughter the calf before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony.

**29:12** And you shall take some of the blood of the calf and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger; and all the remaining blood you shall pour out beside the base of the altar.

**29:13** And you shall take all the fat that is on the belly, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and you shall place them on the altar.

**29:14** But the flesh of the calf and the hide and the dung you shall burn up with fire outside the camp; for it is a sin offering.

**29:15** And you shall take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons shall place their hands on the head of the ram.

**29:16** And you shall slaughter it, and taking the blood, you shall pour it against the altar all around.

**29:17** And you shall cut the ram into pieces according to its parts, and you shall wash the entrails and the feet with water, and you shall place the cut pieces together with the head.

**29:18** And you shall offer up the whole ram on the altar, a whole burnt offering to the Lord for a fragrant aroma; it shall be an incense offering to the Lord.

**29:19** And you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall place their hands on the head of the ram.

**29:20** And you shall slaughter it, and you shall take some of its blood and place it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and on the tip of his right hand, and on the tip of his right foot, and on the lobes of the right ears of his sons, and on the tips of their right hands, and on the tips of their right feet.

**29:21** And you shall take some of the blood that is from the altar and some of the anointing oil, and you shall sprinkle it on Aaron and on his robe, and on his sons and on the robes of his sons with him; and he and his robe shall be sanctified, and his sons and the robes of his sons with him; and the blood of the ram you shall pour against the altar all around.

**29:22** And you shall take from the ram its fat and the fat that covers the belly, and the lobe of the liver and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and the right shoulder (for this is a consecration),

**29:23** and one loaf made with oil and one flatbread from the basket of the unleavened loaves set before the Lord.

**29:24** And you shall place all of them on the hands of Aaron and on the hands of his sons, and you shall set apart for them a separated offering before the Lord.

**29:25** And you shall take them from their hands, and you shall offer them up on the altar of the whole burnt offering for a fragrant aroma before the Lord; it is a fire offering to the Lord.

**29:26** And you shall take the breast from the ram of consecration that belongs to Aaron, and you shall set it apart as a separated offering before the Lord, and it shall be for you as a portion.

**29:27** And you shall sanctify the breast as a separated offering and the shoulder of the contribution, which has been set apart and which has been taken away, from the ram of consecration, from Aaron and from his sons.

**29:28** And it shall be for Aaron and his sons an eternal statute from the sons of Israel; for this is a separated offering; and it shall be a contribution from the sons of Israel, from the sacrifices of deliverance of the sons of Israel, a contribution to the Lord.

**29:29** And the robe of the sanctuary that belongs to Aaron shall be for his sons after him, for them to be anointed in them and to consecrate their hands.

**29:30** For seven days the priest among his sons who is in his place shall put them on, who will enter into the tent of testimony to minister in the holy places.

**29:31** And you shall take the ram of consecration and boil the flesh in a holy place.

**29:32** And Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the loaves that are in the basket beside the doors of the tent of testimony.

**29:33** They shall eat those things by which they were sanctified, to consecrate their hands, to sanctify them; and an outsider shall not eat from it, for they are holy.

**29:34** And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of consecration and of the loaves is left over until morning, you shall burn up the remainder with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is a holy thing.

**29:35** And you shall do in this way to Aaron and to his sons, according to everything that I commanded you; for seven days you shall consecrate their hands.

**29:36** And you shall offer the young calf of the sin offering on the day of purification; and you shall purify the altar when you sanctify upon it, and you shall anoint it so as to sanctify it.

**29:37** For seven days you shall purify the altar and sanctify it, and the altar shall be most holy; everyone who touches the altar shall be sanctified.

**29:38** And these are the things that you shall offer on the altar: two unblemished year-old lambs each day on the altar continually, a continual fire offering.

**29:39** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer in the evening,

**29:40** and a tenth of fine flour mixed with beaten oil, a fourth of a hin, and a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of wine, for the one lamb.

**29:41** And the second lamb you shall offer in the evening; according to the morning sacrifice and according to its drink offering you shall offer it, as a fragrant aroma, a fire offering to the Lord,

**29:42** a continual sacrifice throughout your generations, at the doors of the tent of testimony before the Lord, in which I will be made known to you from there, so as to speak to you.

**29:43** And I will give orders there to the sons of Israel, and I will be sanctified in My glory.

**29:44** And I will sanctify the tent of testimony and the altar, and I will sanctify Aaron and his sons to serve as priests to Me.

**29:45** And I will be called upon among the sons of Israel, and I will be their God.

**29:46** And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt, to be called upon by them and to be their God.

## Translator Notes

- **29:5** — Greek ἐπωμίδα ("shoulder-garment") and λόγιον ("oracle") are the Septuagint terms corresponding to the Hebrew ephod and breastpiece; the Greek text here also lacks the Masoretic Text's clause about girding Aaron with the woven band of the ephod.
- **29:9** — Greek τελειόω ("consecrate," literally "complete" or "perfect") used with "the hands" is the Septuagint technical idiom for priestly ordination, recurring throughout the chapter (vv. 22, 26-35).
- **29:20** — Greek τὸ ἄκρον ("the tip" or "extremity") of the right hand and right foot corresponds to the thumb and big toe; the command to pour the ram's blood against the altar is placed at the end of v. 21 in the Septuagint rather than v. 20.
- **29:27** — Greek ἀφόρισμα ("separated offering") and ἀφαίρεμα ("contribution," literally "that which is taken away") are the Septuagint renderings corresponding to the wave offering and heave offering; similarly, θυμάτων τῶν σωτηρίων in v. 28 is literally "sacrifices of deliverance."
- **29:45** — Greek ἐπικληθήσομαι ("I will be called upon," echoed by ἐπικληθῆναι in v. 46) differs from the Masoretic Text's "I will dwell"; likewise, the Septuagint reads γνωσθήσομαί ("I will be made known") in v. 42 and τάξομαι ("I will give orders") in v. 43.
