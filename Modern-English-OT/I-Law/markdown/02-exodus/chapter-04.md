## Text

**4:1** Then Moses answered and said, "If they do not believe me and do not listen to my voice—for they will say, 'God has not appeared to you'—what shall I say to them?"

**4:2** And the Lord said to him, "What is this that is in your hand?" And he said, "A staff."

**4:3** And He said, "Throw it onto the ground." And he threw it onto the ground, and it became a serpent; and Moses fled from it.

**4:4** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand and take hold of the tail." So he stretched out his hand and took hold of the tail, and it became a staff in his hand—

**4:5** "so that they may believe you that the God of their fathers has appeared to you, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob."

**4:6** And the Lord said to him again, "Put your hand into your bosom." And he put his hand into his bosom; and he brought his hand out of his bosom, and his hand became like snow.

**4:7** And He said again, "Put your hand into your bosom." And he put his hand into his bosom; and he brought it out of his bosom, and it was restored again to the color of its flesh.

**4:8** "And if they do not believe you and do not listen to the voice of the first sign, they will believe you at the voice of the latter sign.

**4:9** And it will be that if they do not believe you through these two signs and do not listen to your voice, you shall take from the water of the river and pour it out onto the dry ground, and the water that you take from the river will be blood upon the dry ground."

**4:10** Then Moses said to the Lord, "Please, Lord, I am not competent—neither before yesterday, nor before the third day, nor since You began to speak to Your servant; I am weak-voiced and slow-tongued."

**4:11** And the Lord said to Moses, "Who gave a mouth to man, and who made the hard of hearing and the deaf, the seeing and the blind? Is it not I, God?

**4:12** And now go, and I will open your mouth, and I will instruct you in what you are to speak."

**4:13** And Moses said, "Please, Lord, appoint another capable person whom You will send."

**4:14** And the Lord, becoming enraged with anger against Moses, said, "Look, is there not Aaron your brother, the Levite? I know that he will surely speak for you; and look, he himself will come out to meet you, and when he sees you, he will rejoice in himself.

**4:15** And you shall speak to him and put My words into his mouth; and I will open your mouth and his mouth, and I will instruct you in what you shall do.

**4:16** And he himself will speak for you to the people, and he will be your mouth, while you will be for him in the matters pertaining to God.

**4:17** And this staff that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand, with which you shall perform the signs."

**4:18** Then Moses went and returned to Jethro his father-in-law and said, "I will go and return to my brothers who are in Egypt, and I will see if they are still alive." And Jethro said to Moses, "Go in good health." And after those many days, the king of Egypt died.

**4:19** And the Lord said to Moses in Midian, "Go, depart to Egypt; for all those seeking your life have died."

**4:20** So Moses took his wife and children, mounted them on the beasts of burden, and returned to Egypt; and Moses took the staff from God in his hand.

**4:21** And the Lord said to Moses, "As you go and return to Egypt, see all the wonders that I have put in your hands—you shall perform them before Pharaoh; but I will harden his heart, and he will by no means send the people away.

**4:22** And you shall say to Pharaoh, 'Thus says the Lord: Israel is My firstborn son,

**4:23** and I said to you: Send My people away so that they may serve Me; now if you are not willing to send them away, see then, I am going to kill your firstborn son.'"

**4:24** And it happened on the way, at the lodging place, that an angel of the Lord met him and sought to kill him.

**4:25** And Zipporah took a stone and circumcised the foreskin of her son, and she fell down at the feet and said, "The blood of the circumcision of my child has stopped."

**4:26** And he departed from him, because she said, "The blood of the circumcision of my child has stopped."

**4:27** And the Lord said to Aaron, "Go into the wilderness to meet Moses." And he went and met him at the mountain of God, and they kissed one another.

**4:28** And Moses reported to Aaron all the words of the Lord that He had sent, and all the sayings that He had commanded him.

**4:29** Then Moses and Aaron went and gathered the council of elders of the sons of Israel.

**4:30** And Aaron spoke all these words that God had spoken to Moses, and he performed the signs before the people.

**4:31** And the people believed and rejoiced that God had visited the sons of Israel and that He had seen their affliction; and bowing down, the people worshiped.

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — The Greek text reads only "like snow" (ὡσεὶ χιών), without the word "leprous" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **4:16** — Literally "the things pertaining to God" (τὰ πρὸς τὸν θεόν), a Septuagint phrasing that expresses Moses' mediatorial role rather than the Hebrew "as God."
- **4:18** — The closing sentence ("And after those many days, the king of Egypt died") appears here in the Septuagint to introduce v. 19, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits it at this point.
- **4:24** — The Greek text specifies "an angel of the Lord" (ἄγγελος Κυρίου), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Lord" directly.
- **4:25** — The Septuagint reads "she fell down at the feet and said, 'The blood of the circumcision of my child has stopped,'" differing markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "a bridegroom of blood."
