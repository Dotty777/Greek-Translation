## Text

**39:1** All the gold that was worked for the works, throughout all the workmanship of the holy things, came from the gold of the firstfruits: twenty-nine talents and seven hundred twenty shekels, according to the holy shekel.

**39:2** And the contribution of silver from the numbered men of the congregation was one hundred talents and one thousand seven hundred seventy-five shekels; one drachma per head, half a shekel, according to the holy shekel.

**39:3** Everyone passing through the numbering, from twenty years old and upward, amounted to six hundred thousand and three thousand five hundred fifty.

**39:4** And the one hundred talents of silver went into the casting of the one hundred capitals of the tent and into the one hundred capitals of the curtain,

**39:5** one hundred capitals for the one hundred talents, a talent per capital.

**39:6** And the one thousand seven hundred seventy-five shekels they made into the hooks for the pillars, and he overlaid their capitals with gold and adorned them.

**39:7** And the bronze of the contribution was seventy talents and one thousand five hundred shekels.

**39:8** And out of them he made the bases of the door of the tent of testimony,

**39:9** and the bases of the gate all around, and the bases of the gate of the courtyard, and the pegs of the tent, and the pegs of the courtyard all around,

**39:10** and the bronze attachment of the altar, and all the utensils of the altar, and all the implements of the tent of testimony.

**39:11** And the sons of Israel did just as the Lord commanded Moses; so they did.

**39:12** Now the remaining gold of the contribution they made into utensils for ministering with them before the Lord;

**39:13** and the blue and purple and scarlet that were left over they made into ministering garments for Aaron, so as to minister in them in the sanctuary.

**39:14** And they brought the garments to Moses, and the tent and its utensils and the bases and its bars and the pillars, and the ark of the covenant and its carrying poles,

**39:15** and the altar and all its utensils,

**39:16** and the anointing oil and the compounded incense, and the pure lampstand

**39:17** and its lamps, the lamps for burning, and the oil for the light,

**39:18** and the table of presentation and all its utensils and the loaves that are set forth,

**39:19** and the garments of the sanctuary that belong to Aaron, and the garments of his sons for the priesthood,

**39:20** and the hangings of the courtyard and the pillars, and the curtain of the door of the tent and of the gate of the courtyard,

**39:22** What the Lord commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel made all the equipment.

**39:23** And Moses saw all the works, and they had done them in the manner the Lord commanded Moses; so they did them. And Moses blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — The Septuagint gives seven hundred twenty shekels of gold (Hebrew Masoretic Text: seven hundred thirty) and refers to 'the holy shekel.'
- **39:2** — Greek renders the half-shekel weight (Hebrew beka) with the Hellenistic measure 'one drachma.'
- **39:4** — Greek repeats 'one hundred' before both the capitals of the tent and the capitals of the curtain; verse 5 then gives the total of one hundred capitals for one hundred talents.
- **39:7** — The Septuagint records seventy talents and one thousand five hundred shekels of bronze, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads two thousand four hundred shekels.
- **39:21 *(additional)*** — Translated from the Saints Greek Critical Text; this verse has no separate place in the Saints.Bible numbering, so it is shown after 39:20.
