## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke all these words, saying,

**20:2** "I am the Lord your God, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from a house of slavery.

**20:3** You must not have other gods besides Me.

**20:4** You must not make for yourself an idol, nor a likeness of anything—whatever is in heaven above, and whatever is on the earth below, and whatever is in the waters beneath the earth.

**20:5** You must not bow down to them nor ever serve them, for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation for those who hate Me,

**20:6** and showing mercy to thousands for those who love Me and for those who keep My commands.

**20:7** You must not take the name of the Lord your God in vain, for the Lord your God will by no means clear the one who takes His name in vain.

**20:8** Remember the Sabbath day, to sanctify it.

**20:9** Six days you will labor and do all your work,

**20:10** but on the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God. You must not do any work on it—you, and your son, and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, your ox and your beast of burden and all your livestock, and the resident alien who resides among you.

**20:11** For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth and all the things in them, and rested on the seventh day. For this reason the Lord blessed the seventh day and sanctified it.

**20:12** Honor your father and mother, so that it may go well with you and so that you may live long upon the good land that the Lord your God is giving you.

**20:13** You must not commit adultery.

**20:14** You must not steal.

**20:15** You must not murder.

**20:16** You must not testify falsely against your neighbor with false testimony.

**20:17** You must not covet your neighbor's wife; you must not covet your neighbor's house, nor his field, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his beast of burden, nor any of his livestock, nor anything that belongs to your neighbor."

**20:18** And all the people were seeing the voice and the flashes and the sound of the trumpet and the smoking mountain; and having become afraid, all the people stood at a distance,

**20:19** and they said to Moses, "You speak to us, and let God not speak to us, so that we do not die."

**20:20** And Moses said to them, "Take courage, for God has come to you in order to test you, so that the fear of Him may be in you, so that you do not sin."

**20:21** So the people stood at a distance, while Moses entered into the thick darkness where God was.

**20:22** Then the Lord said to Moses, "This is what you will say to the house of Jacob and announce to the sons of Israel: 'You yourselves have seen that from heaven I have spoken to you.

**20:23** You must not make for yourselves gods of silver, and gods of gold you must not make for yourselves.

**20:24** You will make an altar of earth for Me, and you will sacrifice upon it your whole burnt offerings and your deliverance offerings and your sheep and your calves, in every place where I name My name; and I will come to you and bless you.

**20:25** And if you make an altar of stones for Me, you must not build them as cut stones, for you have laid your hand tool upon them, and they have been defiled.

**20:26** You must not go up by steps onto My altar, so that you may not uncover your indecency upon it.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:12** — The Greek includes "so that it may go well with you" and "the good land," aligning with the phrasing of Deuteronomy 5:16.
- **20:13** — In the Septuagint, verses 13–15 order the prohibitions as adultery, theft, and murder, differing from the Masoretic Text order (murder, adultery, theft).
- **20:17** — The Greek places "your neighbor's wife" first before "your neighbor's house" and adds "his field," paralleling Deuteronomy 5:21.
- **20:22** — The Greek expands the introduction with "to the house of Jacob and announce to the sons of Israel," echoing Exodus 19:3.
