## Text

**19:1** Now in the third month of the departure of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, on this day they came into the wilderness of Sinai.

**19:2** And they departed from Rephidim and came into the wilderness of Sinai, and Israel encamped there opposite the mountain.

**19:3** And Moses went up to the mountain of God; and God called him from heaven, saying, "Thus you shall say to the house of Jacob and declare to the sons of Israel:

**19:4** 'You yourselves have seen all that I have done to the Egyptians, and I took you up as on wings of eagles, and brought you to Myself.

**19:5** And now if you truly listen to My voice and keep My covenant, you shall be to Me a treasured people from among all the nations; for all the earth is Mine,

**19:6** and you shall be to Me a royal priesthood and a holy nation.' These are the words that you shall speak to the sons of Israel."

**19:7** And Moses came and spoke to the elders of the people, and set before them all these words which God had commanded him.

**19:8** And all the people answered with one accord and said, "All that God has spoken we will do and we will listen." And Moses brought these words back to God.

**19:9** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, I am coming to you in a pillar of cloud, so that the people may hear as I speak to you, and may believe you forever." And Moses reported the words of the people to the Lord.

**19:10** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go down and solemnly charge the people, and purify them today and tomorrow; and let them wash their garments,

**19:11** and let them be ready for the third day; for on the third day the Lord will come down upon Mount Sinai before all the people.

**19:12** And you shall mark off the people all around, saying, 'Guard yourselves against going up onto the mountain or touching any part of it; everyone who touches the mountain shall surely die.

**19:13** No hand shall touch him; for with stones he shall be stoned or with a dart he shall be shot down; whether beast or man, he shall not live.' When the sounds and the trumpets and the cloud depart from the mountain, they shall go up onto the mountain."

**19:14** And Moses went down from the mountain to the people, and sanctified them; and they washed their garments.

**19:15** And he said to the people, "Be ready for three days; do not come near a woman."

**19:16** And it happened on the third day, as it grew toward dawn, that there were sounds and flashes of lightning and a dark cloud upon Mount Sinai, and the sound of the trumpet resounded loudly; and all the people who were in the camp were terrified.

**19:17** And Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stood at the foot of Mount Sinai.

**19:18** And Mount Sinai was smoking entirely because God had come down upon it in fire, and the smoke went up like the smoke of a furnace; and all the people were exceedingly amazed.

**19:19** And the sounds of the trumpet went on growing exceedingly stronger; Moses spoke, and God answered him with a voice.

**19:20** And the Lord came down upon Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain; and the Lord called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.

**19:21** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "Go down and solemnly charge the people, so that they do not draw near to God to observe, and a multitude of them fall;

**19:22** and let the priests who draw near to the Lord God be sanctified, so that the Lord does not depart from them."

**19:23** And Moses said to God, "The people will not be able to go up onto Mount Sinai; for You have solemnly charged us, saying, 'Mark off the mountain and sanctify it.'"

**19:24** And the Lord said to him, "Go, go down, and come up, you and Aaron with you; but let not the priests and the people force their way to come up to God, so that the Lord does not destroy some of them."

**19:25** And Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — The Septuagint states that God called Moses "from heaven" (ek tou ouranou), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "out of the mountain."
- **19:13** — The Greek instructs that they may ascend when "the sounds and the trumpets and the cloud depart from the mountain," differing significantly from the Hebrew MT "when the ram's horn sounds a long blast."
- **19:18** — The Septuagint reads "all the people were exceedingly amazed," where the Hebrew MT has "the whole mountain trembled violently."
- **19:22** — The Greek apallaxe ap' auton most naturally means "depart from them" (though some render it "remove some of them"), differing from the Hebrew MT "break out against them."
