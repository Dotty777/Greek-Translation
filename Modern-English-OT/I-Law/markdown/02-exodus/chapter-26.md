## Text

**26:1** And you shall make the tabernacle of ten curtains of twisted fine linen and blue and purple and twisted scarlet; you shall make them with cherubim, the work of a weaver.

**26:2** The length of one curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of one curtain shall be four cubits; there shall be the same measure for all the curtains.

**26:3** And five curtains shall be joined to one another, one to the other, and five curtains shall be joined together, one to the other.

**26:4** And you shall make for them blue loops on the edge of the one curtain, on the one side for the joining; and so you shall do on the edge of the outermost curtain at the second joining.

**26:5** You shall make fifty loops for the one curtain, and you shall make fifty loops on the side of the curtain at the joining of the second, facing opposite and matching one another, each to each.

**26:6** And you shall make fifty gold rings, and you shall join the curtains one to the other with the rings; and the tabernacle shall be one.

**26:7** And you shall make cloths of hair as a covering over the tabernacle; you shall make them eleven cloths.

**26:8** The length of one cloth shall be thirty cubits, and four cubits shall be the width of one cloth; there shall be the same measure for the eleven cloths.

**26:9** And you shall join the five cloths together, and the six cloths together; and you shall fold the sixth cloth double at the front of the tabernacle.

**26:10** And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the one cloth that is in the middle at the joining, and you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the second joining cloth.

**26:11** And you shall make fifty bronze rings, and you shall join the rings to the loops, and you shall join the cloths, and it shall be one.

**26:12** And you shall place underneath what is left over in the cloths of the tabernacle; you shall cover underneath behind the tabernacle.

**26:13** A cubit on this side and a cubit on that side, from what extends beyond of the cloths, from the length of the cloths of the tabernacle, shall cover over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side, so that it may cover.

**26:14** And you shall make a covering for the tabernacle of rams' skins dyed red, and top coverings of blue skins above.

**26:15** And you shall make pillars for the tabernacle of incorruptible wood.

**26:16** Ten cubits you shall make the one pillar, and one and a half cubits shall be the breadth of the one pillar.

**26:17** There shall be two tenons for the one pillar, matching one to the other; so you shall do for all the pillars of the tabernacle.

**26:18** And you shall make pillars for the tabernacle, twenty pillars on the side toward the north.

**26:19** And you shall make forty silver bases for the twenty pillars, two bases for the one pillar for both of its sides.

**26:20** And for the second side, toward the south, twenty pillars,

**26:21** and their forty silver bases, two bases for the one pillar for both of its sides.

**26:22** And at the back of the tabernacle, on the side toward the sea, you shall make six pillars.

**26:23** And you shall make two pillars at the corners of the tabernacle at the back,

**26:24** and it shall be equal at the bottom; in the same way they shall be equal at their heads into one joining; so you shall do for both; let them be for the two corners.

**26:25** And there shall be eight pillars, and their bases shall be sixteen silver ones: two bases for the one pillar for both of its sides, and two bases for the one pillar.

**26:26** And you shall make bars of incorruptible wood, five for the one pillar on the one side of the tabernacle,

**26:27** and five bars for the one pillar on the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the rear pillar on the side of the tabernacle toward the sea.

**26:28** And let the middle bar in the midst of the pillars reach through from the one side to the other side.

**26:29** And you shall overlay the pillars with gold, and you shall make the rings of gold into which you shall insert the bars, and you shall overlay the bars with gold.

**26:30** And you shall erect the tabernacle according to the form that was shown to you on the mountain.

**26:31** And you shall make a curtain of blue and purple and twisted scarlet and spun fine linen; you shall make it a woven work with cherubim.

**26:32** And you shall put it upon four incorruptible pillars overlaid with gold; and their capitals shall be of gold, and their four bases shall be of silver.

**26:33** And you shall place the curtain upon the pillars, and you shall bring in there, inside the curtain, the ark of the testimony; and the curtain shall separate for you between the holy place and the Holy of Holies.

**26:34** And you shall cover the ark of the testimony with the curtain in the Holy of Holies.

**26:35** And you shall place the table outside the curtain, and the lampstand opposite the table on the side of the tabernacle toward the south; and the table you shall put on the side of the tabernacle toward the north.

**26:36** And you shall make a screen of blue and purple and twisted scarlet and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer.

**26:37** And you shall make five pillars for the curtain, and you shall overlay them with gold; and their capitals shall be of gold; and you shall cast five bronze bases for them.

## Translator Notes

- **26:14** — Greek δέρματα ὑακίνθινα ("blue skins") reflects the Septuagint's color-based rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to tachash skins.
- **26:15** — Throughout verses 15–29 the Greek uses στύλους ("pillars") and ξύλων ἀσήπτων ("incorruptible wood"), corresponding to "boards" (or "frames") and "acacia wood" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **26:18** — The Greek places the first twenty pillars on the north side (πρὸς βορρᾶν) and the second twenty on the south side (πρὸς νότον, v. 20), reversing the south-then-north order of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **26:32** — Greek κεφαλίδες ("capitals," also in 26:37 and 27:17) denotes decorative pillar tops, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "hooks."
- **26:34** — The Greek instructs covering the ark of the testimony with the curtain (κατακαλύψεις τῷ καταπετάσματι), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text instructs placing the mercy seat upon the ark.
