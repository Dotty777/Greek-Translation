## Text

**3:1** Now Moses was shepherding the sheep of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the sheep toward the wilderness and came to Mount Horeb.

**3:2** And an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a fire of flame out of the bush; and he saw that the bush was burning with fire, yet the bush was not being burned up.

**3:3** And Moses said, "I will turn aside and see this great sight, why the bush is not burned up."

**3:4** And when the Lord saw that he was drawing near to look, the Lord called him out of the bush, saying, "Moses, Moses." And he said, "What is it?"

**3:5** And He said, "Do not draw near here; untie the sandal from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground."

**3:6** And He said, "I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob." And Moses turned away his face, for he was afraid to gaze before God.

**3:7** And the Lord said to Moses, "I have surely seen the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their cry because of the taskmasters; for I know their anguish,

**3:8** and I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them out of that land, and to bring them into a good and vast land, into a land flowing with milk and honey, into the place of the Canaanites and Hittites and Amorites and Perizzites and Girgashites and Hivites and Jebusites.

**3:9** And now, look, the cry of the sons of Israel has come to Me, and I have seen the oppression with which the Egyptians are oppressing them.

**3:10** And now, come, let Me send you to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall bring My people, the sons of Israel, out of the land of Egypt."

**3:11** And Moses said to God, "Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and that I should bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt?"

**3:12** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "I will be with you; and this will be the sign for you that I will send you out: when you bring My people out of Egypt, you will worship God on this mountain."

**3:13** And Moses said to God, "Look, I will go out to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'The God of our fathers has sent me to you.' They will ask me, 'What is His name?' What shall I say to them?"

**3:14** And God spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the One who is." And He said, "Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'The One who is has sent me to you.'"

**3:15** And God said again to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'The Lord, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.' This is My eternal name, and a memorial to generations of generations.

**3:16** Go, therefore, gather the council of elders of the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'The Lord, the God of your fathers, has appeared to me, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob, saying, "I have surely watched over you and all that has happened to you in Egypt,"

**3:17** and He said, "I will bring you up out of the affliction of the Egyptians into the land of the Canaanites and Hittites and Amorites and Perizzites and Girgashites and Hivites and Jebusites, into a land flowing with milk and honey."'

**3:18** And they will listen to your voice; and you and the council of elders of Israel shall go in to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'The God of the Hebrews has summoned us; let us go, therefore, a three days' journey into the wilderness, so that we may sacrifice to our God.'

**3:19** But I know that Pharaoh king of Egypt will not let you go, except by a mighty hand;

**3:20** and stretching out My hand, I will strike the Egyptians with all My wonders which I will do among them, and after these things he will send you out.

**3:21** And I will give favor to this people before the Egyptians; and when you depart, you will not go away empty-handed;

**3:22** a woman shall ask from her neighbor and housemate for articles of silver and gold and clothing, and you shall put them on your sons and on your daughters; and plunder the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — LXX renders the Hebrew response "Here I am" idiomatically as "What is it?" (Ti estin;), and uses "the Lord" (Kyrios) in both clauses where the Hebrew has YHWH followed by Elohim.
- **3:14** — LXX translates the Hebrew divine self-disclosure ontologically with the present participle ho on, "the One who is" (or "the Existing One").
- **3:17** — LXX reads third-person "and He said" (continuing Moses' report to the elders) rather than first-person "and I said"; as in v. 8, LXX lists seven nations by including the Girgashites.
- **3:19** — LXX reads "except by a mighty hand" (ean me meta cheiros krataias), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "and not [even] by a mighty hand."
