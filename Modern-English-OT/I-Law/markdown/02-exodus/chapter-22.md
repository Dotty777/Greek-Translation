## Text

**22:1** Now if the thief is found breaking in and is struck and dies, there is no guilt of murder for him.

**22:2** But if the sun has risen upon him, he is guilty; he shall die in return. And if he has nothing, let him be sold in exchange for what was stolen.

**22:3** And if what was stolen is caught and found alive in his hand, from a donkey to a sheep, he shall repay them double.

**22:4** Now if someone grazes down a field or a vineyard, and lets his animal loose to graze down another field, he shall repay from his own field according to its produce; and if he grazes down the whole field, he shall repay the best of his own field and the best of his own vineyard.

**22:5** Now if fire breaks out and catches thorns, and burns up threshing floors or ears of grain or a field, the one who kindled the fire shall repay.

**22:6** Now if someone gives his neighbor silver or articles to keep, and it is stolen from the man's house, if the one who stole it is found, he shall repay double.

**22:7** But if the one who stole it is not found, the owner of the house shall come before God and shall swear that he himself has certainly not acted wickedly with regard to any of his neighbor's deposit.

**22:8** Regarding every stated wrongdoing—whether concerning a calf, a pack animal, a sheep, a garment, or any lost thing that is claimed, whatever it may be—the case of both shall come before God, and the one convicted by God shall repay double to his neighbor.

**22:9** Now if someone gives his neighbor a pack animal, or a calf, or a sheep, or any animal to keep, and it is injured, or dies, or is taken captive, and no one knows,

**22:10** an oath of God shall be between both of them that he himself has certainly not acted wickedly at all with regard to his neighbor's deposit; and so its owner shall accept it, and he shall not repay.

**22:11** But if it is stolen from him, he shall repay the owner.

**22:12** And if it is caught by a wild beast, he shall lead him to the prey, and he shall not repay.

**22:13** Now if someone borrows from his neighbor, and it is injured, or dies, or is taken captive, and the owner is not with it, he shall repay.

**22:14** But if the owner is with it, he shall not repay; and if he is a hired worker, it shall be for him in place of his hire.

**22:15** Now if someone seduces an unbetrothed virgin and lies with her, he shall surely pay the bride-price for her to be his wife.

**22:16** But if her father utterly refuses and is not willing to give her to him as a wife, he shall repay silver to the father according to the amount of the bride-price of virgins.

**22:17** You shall not keep sorcerers alive.

**22:18** Anyone who lies with an animal—you shall surely put them to death.

**22:19** The one who sacrifices to gods, except to the Lord alone, shall be destroyed by death.

**22:20** And you shall not mistreat a resident alien, nor shall you oppress him; for you were resident aliens in the land of Egypt.

**22:21** You shall not mistreat any widow or orphan.

**22:22** And if you harshly mistreat them, and crying out they cry aloud to Me, I will surely listen to their voice,

**22:23** and I will grow furious with anger and will kill you with the sword, and your wives will become widows and your children orphans.

**22:24** Now if you lend silver to the poor brother with you, you shall not be pressing him hard; you shall not impose interest upon him.

**22:25** And if you indeed take your neighbor's garment as a pledge, you shall return it to him before sunset;

**22:26** for this is his covering; this alone is the garment for his nakedness. In what will he sleep? Therefore, if he cries aloud to Me, I will listen to him, for I am merciful.

**22:27** You shall not revile gods, and you shall not speak evil of rulers of your people.

**22:28** You shall not delay the firstfruits of your threshing floor and winepress; the firstborn of your sons you shall give to Me.

**22:29** So you shall do with your calf and your sheep and your pack animal: seven days it shall be under its mother, and on the eighth day you shall give it back to Me.

**22:30** And you shall be holy men to Me; and you shall not eat meat caught by a wild beast; throw it away to the dog.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Greek reads "he shall die in return" (ἀνταποθανεῖται), assigning capital liability to one who kills a thief in daylight, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text stipulates restitution by the thief.
- **22:12** — The Greek reads "he shall lead him to the prey" (ἄξει αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τὴν θήραν), referring to the carcass left by a predator, whereas the Hebrew directs bringing the torn animal as evidence.
- **22:17** — The Greek uses the masculine plural "sorcerers" (φαρμακούς), whereas the Hebrew uses the feminine singular "sorceress."
- **22:27** — The Greek has the plural "gods" (θεούς) for Hebrew Elohim.
