## Text

**16:1** And they departed from Elim, and the entire congregation of the sons of Israel came to the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai. And on the fifteenth day of the second month after they had gone out from the land of Egypt,

**16:2** the entire congregation of the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron.

**16:3** And the sons of Israel said to them, "If only we had died, struck down by the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the pots of meat and ate bread to fullness, because you brought us out into this wilderness to kill this entire congregation with famine."

**16:4** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, I am raining bread for you from heaven, and the people will go out and gather the day's portion day by day, so that I may test them, whether they will walk in My law or not.

**16:5** And it will be on the sixth day that they will prepare whatever they bring in, and it will be double whatever they gather day by day."

**16:6** And Moses and Aaron said to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, "At evening you will know that the Lord brought you out from the land of Egypt,

**16:7** and in the morning you will see the glory of the Lord, when He hears your grumbling against God; and what are we, that you grumble against us?"

**16:8** And Moses said, "When the Lord gives you meat in the evening to eat and bread in the morning to fullness, because the Lord has heard your grumbling which you grumble against us—and what are we? For your grumbling is not against us, but against God."

**16:9** And Moses said to Aaron, "Say to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, 'Approach before God, for He has heard your grumbling.'"

**16:10** And as Aaron was speaking to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, they turned toward the wilderness, and the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud.

**16:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:12** "I have heard the grumbling of the sons of Israel; speak to them, saying, 'Toward evening you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be filled with bread; and you will know that I am the Lord your God.'"

**16:13** And evening came, and quail came up and covered the camp; in the morning it happened, as the dew settled around the camp,

**16:14** look, on the surface of the wilderness was something fine like coriander, white like frost on the ground.

**16:15** And when the sons of Israel saw it, they said to one another, "What is this?" For they did not know what it was. And Moses said to them, "This is the bread which the Lord has given you to eat.

**16:16** This is the word which the Lord commanded: 'Gather from it, each one for those belonging to him; an omer per head, according to the number of your souls, each one with your tentmates, gather.'"

**16:17** And the sons of Israel did so, and they gathered, one much and another less.

**16:18** And when they measured with the omer, the one with much had no excess, and the one with less had no lack; each gathered for those with him who belonged to him.

**16:19** And Moses said to them, "Let no one leave any of it until the morning."

**16:20** And they did not listen to Moses, but some left part of it until the morning; and it swarmed with worms and stank; and Moses became embittered against them.

**16:21** And they gathered it in the morning; and when the sun grew warm, it melted.

**16:22** And it happened on the sixth day that they gathered double the necessary amount, two omers for each one; and all the rulers of the congregation came in and reported to Moses.

**16:23** And the Lord said to them, "Is this not the word which the Lord spoke? Tomorrow is a Sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; whatever you bake, bake, and whatever you boil, boil; and all that is left over, leave it in storage until the morning."

**16:24** And they left some of it until the morning, just as Moses commanded them; and it did not stink, nor did a worm appear in them.

**16:25** And Moses said, "Eat today, for today is a Sabbath to the Lord; it will not be found in the plain.

**16:26** Six days you will gather, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath, because there will be none on it."

**16:27** And it happened on the seventh day that some of the people went out to gather, and they found none.

**16:28** And the Lord said to Moses, "How long are you unwilling to listen to My commandments and My law?

**16:29** See, for the Lord has given you this day; therefore He Himself gave you bread for two days on the sixth day. Each of you will remain in your houses; let no one go out from his place on the seventh day."

**16:30** And the people kept the Sabbath on the seventh day.

**16:31** And the sons of Israel named its name manna; and it was like coriander seed, white, and its taste was like a cake in honey.

**16:32** And Moses said, "This is the word which the Lord commanded: 'Fill the omer with the manna for storage for your generations, so that they may see the bread which you ate in the wilderness when the Lord brought you out from the land of Egypt.'"

**16:33** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take one golden jar and put into it a full omer of manna, and you will set it aside before God for safekeeping for your generations."

**16:34** Just as the Lord commanded Moses, Aaron set it aside before God for safekeeping.

**16:35** And the sons of Israel ate the manna forty years, until they came to the inhabited land. They ate the manna until they arrived at a region of Phoenicia.

**16:36** Now the omer was a tenth of the three measures.

## Translator Notes

- **16:14** — The Greek describes the substance as "like coriander, white" (hōsei korion leukon), a Septuagint reading that differs from the Hebrew "flake-like."
- **16:16** — "Omer" represents the Greek transliteration gomor (defined in 16:36 as "a tenth of the three measures"), and "those belonging to him" renders Greek tous kathēkontas.
- **16:23** — The Greek text reads "the Lord said to them" (eipen de Kyrios pros autous) rather than "Moses," followed by the question "Is this not the word which the Lord spoke?"
- **16:31** — "Manna" translates the shorter Greek form man used throughout Exodus 16.
- **16:35** — The Greek reads "a region of Phoenicia" (meros tēs Phoinikēs) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the border of the land of Canaan."
