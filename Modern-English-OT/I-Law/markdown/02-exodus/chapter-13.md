## Text

**13:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Consecrate to Me every firstborn, first-offspring, opening every womb among the sons of Israel, from man to livestock; it is Mine."

**13:3** And Moses said to the people, "Remember this day on which you went out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery; for with a mighty hand the Lord brought you out from here, and leaven shall not be eaten.

**13:4** For on this day you are going out, in the month of the new produce.

**13:5** And it will be, whenever the Lord your God brings you into the land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Hivites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Jebusites, which He swore to your fathers to give to you, a land flowing with milk and honey, then you shall perform this service in this month.

**13:6** Six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a feast of the Lord.

**13:7** Unleavened bread you shall eat for seven days; nothing leavened shall be seen with you, nor shall leaven be with you within all your borders.

**13:8** And you shall report to your son on that day, saying, 'Because of this the Lord God acted for me, when I was going out of Egypt.'

**13:9** And it will be to you a sign upon your hand and a memorial before your eyes, so that the law of the Lord may be in your mouth; for with a mighty hand the Lord God brought you out of Egypt.

**13:10** And you shall keep this law from days to days.

**13:11** And it will be, when the Lord your God brings you into the land of the Canaanites, in the way He swore to your fathers, and gives it to you,

**13:12** then you shall set apart everything opening a womb, the males, to the Lord; everything opening a womb from the herds or among your livestock, as many as you may have, the males you shall consecrate to the Lord.

**13:13** Everything opening the womb of a donkey you shall exchange for a sheep; and if you do not exchange it, you shall redeem it. Every firstborn of man among your sons you shall redeem.

**13:14** And if your son asks you after these things, saying, 'What is this?' then you shall say to him, 'With a mighty hand the Lord brought us out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery;

**13:15** and when Pharaoh hardened himself against sending us out, He killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of men to the firstborn of livestock. Because of this I sacrifice every firstborn to the Lord, everything opening a womb, the males, and every firstborn of my sons I will redeem.'

**13:16** And it will be for a sign upon your hand, and something immovable before your eyes; for with a mighty hand the Lord brought you out of Egypt."

**13:17** Now when Pharaoh sent the people out, God did not lead them by the way of the land of the Philistines, because it was near; for God said, "The people might have regret upon seeing war, and turn back to Egypt."

**13:18** And God led the people around by the way into the wilderness, to the Red Sea; and in the fifth generation the sons of Israel went up from the land of Egypt.

**13:19** And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him; for with an oath he had made the sons of Israel swear, saying, "The Lord will surely visit you, and you shall carry up my bones from here with you."

**13:20** And having departed from Succoth, the sons of Israel encamped at Etham beside the wilderness.

**13:21** And God was leading them, by day in a pillar of cloud to show them the way, and by night in a pillar of fire.

**13:22** And the pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not fail before all the people.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew month name Abib as "the new things" (ton neon), referring to newly ripening grain and produce.
- **13:6** — The Septuagint reads "six days" before the seventh-day feast, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "seven days."
- **13:13** — The Septuagint reads "you shall redeem it" (lytrose auto), contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "you shall break its neck."
- **13:16** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew term totafot (often translated "frontlets") with asaleuton ("something immovable" or "unshakable").
- **13:18** — The Septuagint reads "in the fifth generation" (pempte genea), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (chamushim) is usually translated "equipped for battle" or "in battle array."
