## Text

**6:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh; for with a mighty hand he will send them out, and with an uplifted arm he will drive them out of his land."

**6:2** Now God spoke to Moses and said to him, "I am the Lord.

**6:3** And I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, being their God, and I did not make My name, the Lord, known to them.

**6:4** And I established My covenant with them so as to give them the land of the Canaanites, the land in which they have sojourned, on which they also sojourned.

**6:5** And I heard the groaning of the sons of Israel, whom the Egyptians are enslaving, and I remembered your covenant.

**6:6** Go, speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'I am the Lord, and I will bring you out from the domination of the Egyptians, and I will rescue you from slavery, and I will redeem you with an uplifted arm and great judgment.

**6:7** And I will take you to Myself as a people for Me, and I will be your God, and you will know that I am the Lord your God, who brought you out from the oppression of the Egyptians.

**6:8** And I will bring you out into the land toward which I stretched out My hand to give it to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and I will give it to you as an inheritance. I am the Lord.'"

**6:9** And Moses spoke in this way to the sons of Israel, but they did not listen to Moses because of faintheartedness and because of the harsh labors.

**6:10** Then the Lord said to Moses, saying,

**6:11** "Go in, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, so that he may send out the sons of Israel from his land."

**6:12** But Moses spoke before the Lord, saying, "Look, the sons of Israel did not listen to me, and how will Pharaoh listen to me? And I am inarticulate."

**6:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, and He gave them a charge to Pharaoh king of Egypt, so as to send out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt.

**6:14** And these are the leaders of the houses of their clans. The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi; this is the kindred of Reuben.

**6:15** And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar and Shaul, the one from the Phoenician woman; these are the clans of the sons of Simeon.

**6:16** And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their kindreds: Gershon and Kohath and Merari; and the years of the life of Levi were one hundred thirty-seven.

**6:17** And these are the sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, houses of their clan.

**6:18** And the sons of Kohath: Amram and Issachar, Hebron and Uzziel; and the years of the life of Kohath were one hundred thirty years.

**6:19** And the sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the houses of the clans of Levi according to their kindred.

**6:20** And Amram took Jochebed, the daughter of his father's brother, for himself as a wife, and she bore to him both Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister; and the years of the life of Amram were one hundred thirty-two years.

**6:21** And the sons of Izhar: Korah and Nepheg and Zichri.

**6:22** And the sons of Uzziel: Elizaphan and Segri.

**6:23** And Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, for himself as a wife, and she bore to him Nadab and Abihu and Eleazar and Ithamar.

**6:24** And the sons of Korah: Assir and Elkanah and Abiasar; these are the generations of Korah.

**6:25** And Eleazar, the son of Aaron, took one of the daughters of Putiel for himself as a wife, and she bore Phinehas to him. These are the heads of the clan of the Levites according to their generations.

**6:26** This is Aaron and Moses, to whom God spoke, to bring out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt with their force.

**6:27** These are the ones who spoke with Pharaoh king of Egypt; and they brought out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, this same Aaron and Moses.

**6:28** On the day when the Lord spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

**6:29** the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the Lord. Speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I am speaking to you."

**6:30** And Moses said before the Lord, "Look, I am weak-voiced, and how will Pharaoh listen to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Greek reads "being their God" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic "as God Almighty" (El Shaddai).
- **6:15** — The Greek identifies Shaul's mother as "the Phoenician woman" (Φοινίσσης), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Canaanite woman."
- **6:18** — The Greek names Kohath's second son "Issachar" (Ἰσσαχάρ, compared to "Izhar" in 6:21) and gives Kohath's lifespan as 130 years (Hebrew MT: 133).
- **6:20** — The Greek describes Jochebed as the daughter of Amram's paternal uncle (his cousin; Hebrew MT: his father's sister), includes "Miriam their sister," and gives Amram's lifespan as 132 years (Hebrew MT: 137).
- **6:22** — The Greek lists only two sons of Uzziel—omitting Mishael—and reads "Segri" (Σεγρεί) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Sithri."
- **6:24** — The Greek reads "Abiasar" (Ἀβιασάρ) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Abiasaph."
