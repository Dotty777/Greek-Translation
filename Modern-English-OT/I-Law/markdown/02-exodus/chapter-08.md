## Text

**8:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron your brother, 'Stretch out your staff with your hand over the rivers and over the canals and over the marshes, and bring up the frogs.'"

**8:2** And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and he brought up the frogs; and the frogs were brought up and covered the land of Egypt.

**8:3** And the enchanters of the Egyptians also did likewise with their sorceries, and they brought up the frogs upon the land of Egypt.

**8:4** And Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Pray for me to the Lord, and let Him remove the frogs from me and from my people, and I will send them away, and let them sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:5** And Moses said to Pharaoh, "Appoint for me when I should pray for you and for your servants and for your people, to wipe out the frogs from you and from your people and from your houses; only in the river will they be left."

**8:6** And he said, "For tomorrow." So he said, "As you have spoken, so that you may see that there is no other besides the Lord;

**8:7** and the frogs will be removed from you and from your houses and from the courtyards and from your servants and from your people; only in the river will they be left."

**8:8** And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh; and Moses cried out to the Lord concerning the appointed time of the frogs, as Pharaoh had appointed.

**8:9** And the Lord did just as Moses said, and the frogs died out of the houses and out of the courtyards and out of the fields;

**8:10** and they gathered them in heaps upon heaps, and the land stank.

**8:11** But when Pharaoh saw that relief had come, his heart became heavy, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had spoken.

**8:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff with your hand and strike the dust of the ground, and there will be gnats both on the people and on the four-footed animals and in all the land of Egypt.'"

**8:13** So Aaron stretched out his staff with his hand and struck the dust of the ground, and the gnats came to be on the people and on the four-footed animals; and in all the dust of the ground the gnats came to be.

**8:14** And the enchanters also did likewise with their sorceries to bring forth the gnats, and they were not able; and the gnats were on the people and on the four-footed animals.

**8:15** So the enchanters said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." Yet Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had spoken.

**8:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh; and look, he will go out to the water, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord: Send away My people so that they may serve Me in the wilderness;

**8:17** but if you are not willing to send away My people, look, I am sending upon you and upon your servants and upon your people and upon your houses dog-flies, and the houses of the Egyptians will be filled with the dog-flies, and also the ground on which they are.

**8:18** And on that day I will wondrously set apart the land of Goshen, on which My people are, where the dog-flies will not be, so that you may know that I am the Lord, the Lord of all the earth.

**8:19** And I will make a distinction between My people and your people; and tomorrow this will happen upon the land.'"

**8:20** And the Lord did so, and the dog-flies came in a multitude into the houses of Pharaoh and into the houses of his servants and into all the land of Egypt; and the land was ruined by the dog-flies.

**8:21** And Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron, saying, "Go, sacrifice to your God in the land."

**8:22** And Moses said, "It is not possible for this matter to happen this way, for we will sacrifice the abominations of the Egyptians to the Lord our God; for if we sacrifice the abominations of the Egyptians before them, we will be stoned.

**8:23** We will go a three days' journey into the wilderness, and we will sacrifice to our God just as the Lord told us."

**8:24** And Pharaoh said, "I am sending you, and sacrifice to your God in the wilderness; only you shall not extend your journey far. Pray therefore for me to the Lord."

**8:25** And Moses said, "Look, I will go out from you and pray to God, and the dog-flies will depart from you and from your servants and your people tomorrow; do not continue any longer, Pharaoh, to deceive by not sending away the people to sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:26** And Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to God.

**8:27** And the Lord did just as Moses said, and He removed the dog-flies from Pharaoh and his servants and his people, and not even one was left.

**8:28** And Pharaoh made his heart heavy at this time as well, and he was not willing to send away the people.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Chapter 8 verse numbering follows the Septuagint, where 8:1 corresponds to 8:5 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (the preceding four verses close chapter 7).
- **8:8** — The Greek reads "concerning the appointed time" (horismos) of the frogs, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("concerning the frogs which He had brought upon Pharaoh").
- **8:17** — "Dog-flies" translates the collective singular Greek kynomyia (literally "dog-fly"), a biting fly, where the Hebrew has "swarms."
- **8:18** — "Goshen" represents the Septuagint spelling Gesem; the verse also concludes with the Septuagint reading "the Lord, the Lord of all the earth."
