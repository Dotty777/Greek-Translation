## Text

**18:1** Now Jethro, the priest of Midian, the father-in-law of Moses, heard of all that the Lord had done for Israel His own people, for the Lord had brought Israel out of Egypt.

**18:2** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took Zipporah, the wife of Moses, after her dismissal,

**18:3** and her two sons; the name of one of them was Gershom, saying, "I was a sojourner in a foreign land";

**18:4** and the name of the second was Eliezer, saying, "For the God of my father is my helper, and He delivered me from the hand of Pharaoh."

**18:5** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, and the sons and the wife came out to Moses into the wilderness, where he was encamped at the mountain of God.

**18:6** And it was reported to Moses, saying, "Look, your father-in-law Jethro is coming to you, and your wife and your two sons with him."

**18:7** And Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, and bowed down to him and kissed him, and they greeted one another; and he brought him into the tent.

**18:8** And Moses recounted to his father-in-law all that the Lord had done to Pharaoh and to all the Egyptians for the sake of Israel, and all the hardship that had come upon them on the way, and that the Lord had delivered them from the hand of Pharaoh and from the hand of the Egyptians.

**18:9** And Jethro was amazed at all the good things which the Lord had done for them, that He had delivered them from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of Pharaoh.

**18:10** And Jethro said, "Blessed be the Lord, because He delivered them from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of Pharaoh.

**18:11** Now I know that the Lord is great above all the gods, because of this, that they attacked them."

**18:12** And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took whole burnt offerings and sacrifices to God; and Aaron and all the elders of Israel came to eat bread together with the father-in-law of Moses before God.

**18:13** And it happened after the next day that Moses sat down to judge the people; and all the people stood beside Moses from morning until evening.

**18:14** And when Jethro saw all that he was doing for the people, he said, "What is this that you are doing for the people? Why do you sit alone, while all the people stand beside you from morning until evening?"

**18:15** And Moses said to his father-in-law, "Because the people come to me to seek a judgment from God.

**18:16** For whenever a dispute arises among them and they come to me, I judge each one, and I instruct them in the ordinances of God and His law."

**18:17** And the father-in-law of Moses said to him, "You are not doing this matter rightly;

**18:18** you will surely waste away with unbearable ruin, both you and all the people who are with you; this matter is heavy for you, you will not be able to do it alone.

**18:19** Now therefore listen to me, and I will advise you, and God will be with you. Be for the people in the things pertaining to God, and you shall bring their matters up to God;

**18:20** and you shall solemnly testify to them the ordinances of God and His law, and you shall make known to them the ways in which they shall walk and the works which they shall do.

**18:21** And look out for yourself from all the people able men, God-fearing, righteous men, hating arrogance, and you shall appoint over them rulers of thousands and rulers of hundreds and rulers of fifties and rulers of tens,

**18:22** and they shall judge the people at every hour; and the weighty matter they shall bring up to you, but the minor judgments they shall judge themselves, and they will relieve you and assist you.

**18:23** If you do this matter, God will strengthen you and you will be able to endure, and all this people will come to its own place in peace."

**18:24** And Moses listened to the voice of his father-in-law, and did all that he had said to him.

**18:25** And Moses chose able men from all Israel, and made them over them rulers of thousands and rulers of hundreds and rulers of fifties and rulers of tens,

**18:26** and they judged the people at every hour; and every weighty matter they brought up to Moses, but every light matter they judged themselves.

**18:27** And Moses sent off his father-in-law, and he departed to his own land.

## Translator Notes

- **18:9** — The Greek uses exeste ("was amazed" or "astonished"), differing from the Hebrew MT "rejoiced."
- **18:13** — The Greek literally says "after the next day" (meta ten epaurion), a formal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for "on the next day."
- **18:18** — The Septuagint adds anypomoneto ("unbearable") to intensify the warning of ruin and exhaustion.
- **18:21** — The Greek reads "hating arrogance" (misountas hyperephanian), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "hating dishonest gain."
