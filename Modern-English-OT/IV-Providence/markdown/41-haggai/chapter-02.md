## Text

**2:1** In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the Lord spoke by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:2** "Speak now to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the tribe of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to all the remnant of the people, saying,

**2:3** 'Who is there among you who saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? As though it does not exist before you.

**2:4** And now be strong, Zerubbabel, says the Lord, and be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and let all the people of the land be strong, says the Lord, and work; for I am with you, says the Lord Almighty,

**2:5** and My Spirit stands in your midst; take courage.

**2:6** For the Lord Almighty says: Once more I will shake heaven and earth and the sea and the dry land,

**2:7** and I will shake all the nations, and the choice things of all the nations will come; and I will fill this house with glory, says the Lord Almighty.

**2:8** Mine is the silver and Mine is the gold, says the Lord Almighty.

**2:9** For the latter glory of this house will be greater than the first, says the Lord Almighty; and in this place I will give peace, says the Lord Almighty, and peace of soul as a possession to everyone who builds to raise up this temple.'"

**2:10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year under Darius, the word of the Lord came to Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:11** "Thus says the Lord Almighty: Ask the priests about the law, saying,

**2:12** 'If a man takes holy meat in the corner of his garment, and the corner of his garment touches bread or stew or wine or oil or any food, will it become holy?'" And the priests answered and said, "No."

**2:13** And Haggai said, "If one who is defiled and unclean by a dead body touches any of these, will it be defiled?" And the priests answered and said, "It will be defiled."

**2:14** And Haggai answered and said, "So is this people, and so is this nation before Me, says the Lord, and so are all the works of their hands; and whoever draws near there will be defiled because of their early-morning gains; they will suffer pain on account of their labors, and you hated those who reprove in the gates.

**2:15** And now take to heart, from this day and earlier, before laying a stone upon a stone in the temple of the Lord,

**2:16** what you were like, when you would put twenty sata of barley into a bin, and it became ten sata of barley, and you would go into the wine vat to draw out fifty metretai, and there were twenty.

**2:17** I struck you with barrenness and with blighting wind and with hail, all the works of your hands, and you did not return to Me, says the Lord.

**2:18** Submit your hearts now from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, and from the day on which the foundation of the temple of the Lord was laid; take to heart

**2:19** whether it will be recognized on the threshing floor, and whether the vine and the fig tree and the pomegranate and the olive trees still bear no fruit; from this day I will bless."

**2:20** And the word of the Lord came a second time to Haggai the prophet on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,

**2:21** "Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the tribe of Judah, saying, 'I am shaking heaven and earth and the sea and the dry land,

**2:22** and I will overthrow thrones of kings, and I will destroy the power of the kings of the nations, and I will overthrow chariots and riders, and horses and their riders will go down, each by the sword against his brother.

**2:23** On that day, says the Lord Almighty, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, My servant, says the Lord, and I will set you as a signet ring, because I have chosen you, says the Lord Almighty.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Greek text is shorter than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, omitting the opening reference to the covenant made at the Exodus, and ends with the positive command "take courage" rather than "do not fear."
- **2:9** — The final clause ("and peace of soul as a possession to everyone who builds to raise up this temple") is a Septuagint addition not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **2:14** — The Greek text reads "whoever draws near there will be defiled" and adds a closing passage not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, referring to "early-morning gains" (profit or takings) and echoing Amos 5:10 ("hated those who reprove in the gates").
- **2:16** — The Greek text opens with "what you were like" and specifies "barley" measured in dry "sata" (about 12-13 liters each) alongside liquid "metretai" (about 39 liters each), differing from the more elliptical Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **2:19** — The Greek text reads "whether it will be recognized on the threshing floor" (with an unexpressed subject referring to the harvest) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Is the seed still in the barn?", and smooths an elliptical Greek participial clause before "from this day I will bless."
