## Text

**1:1** In the second year under Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying, "Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the tribe of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,

**1:2** 'This is what the Lord Almighty says: This people says, "The time has not come to build the house of the Lord."'"

**1:3** And the word of the Lord came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**1:4** "Is it time for you to live in your paneled houses, while your house lies desolate?"

**1:5** And now this is what the Lord Almighty says: "Direct your hearts, then, to your ways.

**1:6** You sowed much and brought in little; you ate, yet not to fullness; you drank, yet not to drunkenness; you put on clothes, yet you were not warmed by them; and the one who gathers wages gathered them into a pierced pouch."

**1:7** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "Set your hearts on your ways.

**1:8** Go up to the mountain and cut timber and build the house, and I will take pleasure in it and be glorified," said the Lord.

**1:9** "You looked for much, and it became little; and it was brought into the house, and I blew it away." Therefore this is what the Lord Almighty says: "Because My house is desolate, while you rush, each to his own house,

**1:10** therefore heaven will hold back dew, and the earth will withhold its produce.

**1:11** And I will bring a sword upon the land and upon the mountains, and upon the grain and upon the wine and upon the oil and whatever the land brings forth, and upon the people and upon the livestock, and upon all the labors of their hands."

**1:12** And Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the tribe of Judah, and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of the Lord their God and to the words of Haggai the prophet, just as the Lord their God had sent him to them; and the people feared before the Lord.

**1:13** And Haggai, a messenger of the Lord among the messengers of the Lord, said to the people, "I am with you," says the Lord.

**1:14** And the Lord roused the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the tribe of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of the remnant of all the people; and they came in and began doing work in the house of the Lord Almighty, their God,

**1:15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year under Darius the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text identifies Zerubbabel as "from the tribe of Judah" (also in 1:12, 14; 2:2, 21), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "governor of Judah."
- **1:4** — The Greek text reads "your house" (referring to the community's temple), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and some Greek manuscripts read "this house."
- **1:8** — The Greek text reads "cut timber," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "bring wood."
- **1:11** — The Greek text reads "a sword" (reflecting a different vocalization of the unpointed Hebrew consonants), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a drought."
- **1:13** — The Greek text reads "a messenger of the Lord among the messengers of the Lord," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the messenger of the Lord, in the message of the Lord."
