## Text

**1:1** And the word of the Lord came to Micah the son of Morasthi in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, concerning what he saw regarding Samaria and regarding Jerusalem.

**1:2** Hear words, peoples, and let the earth pay attention, and all who are in it; and the Lord, the Lord will be among you as a witness, the Lord from His holy house.

**1:3** For look, the Lord is coming out from His place, and He will come down upon the high places of the earth,

**1:4** and the mountains will be shaken beneath Him, and the valleys will melt like wax before fire, and like water rushing down a slope.

**1:5** All these things are because of the ungodliness of Jacob, and because of the sin of the house of Israel. What is the ungodliness of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what is the sin of the house of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

**1:6** And I will make Samaria into a fruit-watcher's hut of a field and into a planting of a vineyard; and I will pull down her stones into a chasm, and I will uncover her foundations.

**1:7** And they will cut to pieces all her carved images, and they will burn all her wages in fire, and I will make all her idols into a desolation; for from the wages of prostitution she gathered them, and from the wages of prostitution she collected them.

**1:8** Because of this she will wail and lament, she will go barefoot and naked; she will make a wailing like that of dragons and mourning like that of daughters of sirens,

**1:9** for her wound has prevailed, because it has come as far as Judah and has reached as far as the gate of My people, as far as Jerusalem.

**1:10** You who are in Gath, do not exalt yourselves; you who are in Akim, do not rebuild mockery from a house; sprinkle earth upon your mockery.

**1:11** She who inhabits her cities well, she who inhabits Sennaar, did not go out to wail for the house next to her; she will receive from you a wound of pain.

**1:12** Who began toward good things for her who inhabits pains? Because evils came down from the Lord upon the gates of Jerusalem,

**1:13** a sound of chariots and horsemen. She who inhabits Lachish, she is the originator of sin for the daughter of Zion, for in you Israel's acts of ungodliness were found.

**1:14** Therefore she will give those who are sent away as far as the inheritance of Gath, vain houses; they came to nothing for the kings of Israel,

**1:15** until they bring the heirs, you who inhabit Lachish; an inheritance will come as far as Adullam, the glory of the daughter of Israel.

**1:16** Shave yourself and cut your hair for your delicate children; enlarge your widowhood like an eagle, for they have been taken captive away from you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Greek reads "words" (logous) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "all of you," and uses the doubled divine title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord").
- **1:8** — Unlike the Hebrew MT (first person, "jackals," "ostriches"), the Greek uses third-person feminine singular ("she") and renders the mourning creatures as "dragons" (drakonton) and "daughters of sirens" (thygateron seirenon).
- **1:10** — "Akim" (Greek Akeim) is a Septuagint-distinct proper name corresponding to the Hebrew wordplay on Acco/weeping, and "mockery from a house" renders a Greek common-noun reading where Hebrew MT has the place name Beth-le-aphrah.
- **1:11** — "Sennaar" (Greek Sennaar) is a Septuagint-distinct proper name corresponding to Zaanan in the Hebrew MT, while other place names in verses 11-15 are translated in Greek as common nouns.
- **1:16** — The Greek reads "your widowhood" (ten cherian sou) where the Hebrew MT has "your baldness."
