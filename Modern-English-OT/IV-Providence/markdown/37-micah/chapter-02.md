## Text

**2:1** They came to be devising troubles and working evil things on their beds, and at daybreak they carried them out, because they did not lift up their hands to God.

**2:2** And they coveted fields, and they plundered orphans, and they oppressed houses, and they plundered a man and his house, and a man and his inheritance.

**2:3** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Look, I am devising evils against this tribe, from which you will by no means lift up your necks, and you will by no means walk upright suddenly, for it is an evil time."

**2:4** In that day a proverb will be taken up against you, and a lament will be lamented in melody, saying, "We have suffered utter misery; the portion of my people has been measured out with a cord, and there was no one to hinder him from turning away; our fields have been divided up."

**2:5** Therefore you will have no one casting a cord by lot in the assembly of the Lord.

**2:6** Do not weep with tears, nor let them shed tears over these things; for he will not ward off reproaches,

**2:7** the one who says, "The house of Jacob has provoked the Spirit of the Lord." Are these His practices? Are not His words good with him, and have they walked uprightly?

**2:8** And formerly My people stood up in enmity; opposite his peace they stripped off his skin, so as to take away hope, a crushing of war.

**2:9** The leaders of My people will be cast out from the houses of their luxury; because of their wicked practices they were driven out. Draw near to everlasting mountains.

**2:10** Rise and go, for this is not rest for you because of uncleanness. You have been destroyed with destruction,

**2:11** you have been pursued with no one pursuing; a spirit set up falsehood, it dripped to you for wine and strong drink. And it will come about from the drop of this people,

**2:12** Jacob will surely be gathered with all; I will surely receive the remnant of Israel, together I will set his return; like sheep in distress, like a flock in the midst of their resting place, they will leap out from among people.

**2:13** Through the breach before them they broke through, and they passed through a gate and went out through it; and their king went out before them, and the Lord will lead them.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Unlike the Hebrew MT ("Woe to those... because it is in the power of their hand"), the Greek begins "They came to be" (Egenonto) and closes "because they did not lift up their hands to God."
- **2:6** — The Greek renders the Hebrew verb for "preach/drip" as "weep with tears" (klaiete dakrysin) and connects the end of verse 6 syntactically to "the one who says" (ho legon) in verse 7.
- **2:8** — The Greek reads "his peace" (tes eirenes autou) where the Hebrew MT has "garment," and "a crushing of war" (syntrimmon polemou) where MT has "those averse to war."
- **2:9** — The Greek reads "leaders of My people" (hegoumenoi laou mou) where the Hebrew MT has "women of My people," and concludes with the distinct command "Draw near to everlasting mountains."
- **2:12** — The Greek reads "in distress" (en thlipsei) where the Hebrew MT has "of Bozrah / in a fold," and "they will leap out" (exalountai) where MT describes the noise of a multitude.
