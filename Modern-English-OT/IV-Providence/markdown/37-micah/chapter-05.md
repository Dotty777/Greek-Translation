## Text

**5:1** And you, Bethlehem, house of Ephrathah, are very small to be among the thousands of Judah, from whom He will go out for Me to be a ruler of Israel, and His goings out are from the beginning, from days of old.

**5:2** Therefore He will give them up until the time of her who is giving birth; she will give birth, and the rest of their brothers will return to the sons of Israel.

**5:3** And He will stand and see, and the Lord will shepherd His flock in strength, and in the glory of the name of the Lord their God they will exist; because now He will be magnified to the ends of the earth.

**5:4** And this will be peace: when Assyria comes upon your land and when it treads upon your country, then seven shepherds and eight bites of men will be raised up against it;

**5:5** and they will shepherd Assyria with a sword and the land of Nimrod in its trench; and He will deliver from Assyria when it comes upon your land and when it treads upon your borders.

**5:6** And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like dew falling from the Lord and like lambs upon grass, so that no one may be gathered together nor stand firm among the sons of men.

**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among beasts in the forest and like a young lion among flocks of sheep, just as when it passes through and separates and seizes, and there is no one who rescues.

**5:8** Your hand will be lifted up against those who afflict you, and all your enemies will be utterly destroyed.

**5:9** "And it will be in the day," says the Lord, "I will utterly destroy the horses from your midst and destroy your chariots,

**5:10** and I will utterly destroy the cities of your land and I will remove all your strongholds,

**5:11** and I will utterly destroy your sorceries from your hands, and diviners will not be in you;

**5:12** and I will utterly destroy your carved images and your pillars from your midst, and you will no longer bow down to the works of your hands,

**5:13** and I will cut down the groves from your midst, and I will wipe out your cities,

**5:14** and I will execute vengeance in anger and in wrath among the nations, because they did not listen."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Greek text reads 'ex hou' ('from whom' or 'out of which'), whereas other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew text read 'out of you.' Pronouns for the promised ruler ('He/His' here and in vv. 3, 5) are capitalized following traditional messianic reading; Greek makes no case distinction.
- **5:3** — In the Greek text, 'will see' (opsetai) stands alongside 'will shepherd,' and 'the Lord' (Kyrios) is nominative as the subject ('the Lord will shepherd') rather than genitive ('in the strength of the Lord') as in the Hebrew.
- **5:4** — Greek 'degmata anthropon' ('bites of men') reflects reading the Hebrew word for 'princes' (nesikei) as 'bites' (neshikei).
- **5:6** — The Greek reads 'like lambs (arnes) upon grass' and 'so that no one may be gathered together nor stand firm,' whereas the Hebrew reads 'like showers upon grass, which do not wait for man.'
