## Text

**4:1** And it will be in the last days that the mountain of the Lord will be visible, ready on the summits of the mountains, and it will be lifted up above the hills; and peoples will hasten to it,

**4:2** and many nations will go and say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord and to the house of the God of Jacob; and they will show us His way, and we will walk in His paths." For from Zion law will go out, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem,

**4:3** and He will judge between many peoples, and He will rebuke strong nations even far away; and they will hammer their swords into plows and their spears into sickles, and nation will no longer raise a sword against nation, and they will no longer learn to wage war;

**4:4** and each one will rest beneath his vine, and each one beneath his fig tree, and there will be no one causing fear, because the mouth of the Lord Almighty has spoken these things.

**4:5** For all the peoples will walk, each one his own way, but we will walk in the name of the Lord our God forever and beyond.

**4:6** "In that day," says the Lord, "I will gather her who has been crushed, and I will receive her who has been driven out, and those whom I cast off;

**4:7** and I will make her who has been crushed into a remnant, and her who has been cast off into a mighty nation, and the Lord will reign over them on Mount Zion from now and forever."

**4:8** And you, gloomy tower of the flock, daughter of Zion, to you it will come, and the first dominion will enter, a kingdom from Babylon to the daughter of Jerusalem.

**4:9** And now why have you known evils? Was there not a king for you? Or has your counsel perished, that birth pains have seized you like a woman giving birth?

**4:10** Be in labor and be courageous and draw near, daughter of Zion, like a woman giving birth; because now you will go out from the city and camp in the plain, and you will come as far as Babylon; from there the Lord your God will deliver you from the hand of your enemies.

**4:11** And now many nations have been gathered together against you, saying, "We will gloat, and our eyes will look upon Zion."

**4:12** Yet they did not know the thought of the Lord, and they did not understand His counsel, for He has gathered them like sheaves of a threshing floor.

**4:13** Arise and thresh them, daughter of Zion, for I will make your horns iron, and I will make your hooves bronze; and you will melt down many peoples, and you will dedicate their multitude to the Lord, and their strength to the Lord of all the earth.

**4:14** Now the daughter will be hedged in with a hedge; he has set a siege against you; with a rod they will strike the gates of Israel on the cheek.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Greek reads plural 'they will show us His way,' whereas the Hebrew text reads singular 'He will teach us of His ways.'
- **4:8** — Greek 'auchmodes' ('gloomy' or 'murky') reflects taking the Hebrew term for 'hill' (ophel) as 'darkness'; the Greek also reads 'from Babylon' where the Hebrew has the verb 'shall come.'
- **4:13** — The Greek reads 'you will melt down' (katatexeis) and 'you will dedicate their multitude' (to plethos auton), whereas the Hebrew reads 'you will crush' and 'I will devote their unjust gain.'
- **4:14** — The Greek reads 'the daughter will be hedged in with a hedge' and 'the gates (pylas) of Israel,' whereas other Greek manuscripts read 'the tribes (phylas) of Israel' and the Hebrew reads 'the judge of Israel.'
