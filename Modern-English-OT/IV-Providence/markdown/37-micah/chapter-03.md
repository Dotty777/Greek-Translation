## Text

**3:1** And he will say, "Listen now to these things, rulers of the house of Jacob and remnant of the house of Israel. Is it not for you to know justice?

**3:2** You who hate good things and seek wicked things, tearing their skin off them and their flesh off their bones.

**3:3** Just as they devoured the flesh of my people, and stripped their skin off them, and crushed their bones and cut them in pieces like flesh for a cauldron and like meat for a pot,

**3:4** so they will cry out to the Lord, and He will not listen to them; and He will turn His face away from them at that time, because they acted wickedly in their practices against them."

**3:5** Thus says the Lord concerning the prophets who lead My people astray, who bite with their teeth and proclaim peace upon him, and when nothing was given into their mouth, they raised up war against him:

**3:6** "Therefore there will be night for you without vision, and there will be darkness for you without divination; and the sun will set upon the prophets, and the day will grow dark over them.

**3:7** And those who see dreams will be put to shame, and the diviners will be mocked, and all of them will speak against them, for there will be no one who listens to them."

**3:8** Unless I fill up strength by the Spirit of the Lord and of justice and might, to declare to Jacob his acts of ungodliness and to Israel his sins.

**3:9** Listen now to these things, leaders of the house of Jacob and remnant of the house of Israel, who detest justice and distort all upright things,

**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with injustices.

**3:11** Her leaders judged for gifts, and her priests gave answers for wages, and her prophets practiced divination for silver; and they leaned upon the Lord, saying, "Is not the Lord among us? Evils will by no means come upon us."

**3:12** Therefore, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem will be like a fruit-watcher's hut, and the mountain of the house will become a forest grove.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek opens with third-person future "And he will say" (Kai erei) where the Hebrew MT has "And I said," and reads "remnant" (hoi kataloipoi) here and in 3:9 where MT has "rulers."
- **3:7** — The Greek expands "seers" to "those who see dreams," reads "will speak against them" where Hebrew MT has "will cover their mustache," and reads "no one who listens to them" where MT has "no answer from God."
- **3:8** — The Greek opens with the conditional "Unless" (ean me) and the active verb "I fill up strength" (empleso ischyn), contrasting elliptically with the false prophets of verses 6-7 (Hebrew MT: "On the other hand, I am filled with power"). The genitives "of justice and might" (krimatos kai dynasteias) may depend either on anarthrous pneumati ("a spirit / the Spirit of the Lord and of justice and might") or directly on "fill up" as genitives of content ("with justice and might").
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "fruit-watcher's hut" (oporophylakion, as in 1:6) where the Hebrew MT has "heaps of ruins," and "a forest grove" (alsos drymou) where MT has "high places of a forest."
