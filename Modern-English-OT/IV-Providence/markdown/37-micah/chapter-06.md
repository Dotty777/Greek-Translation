## Text

**6:1** Hear now a word. The Lord, the Lord has said, "Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice."

**6:2** Hear, peoples, the judgment of the Lord, and you ravines, foundations of the earth, for the Lord has a judgment against His people, and with Israel He will enter into dispute.

**6:3** My people, what have I done to you, or how have I grieved you, or how have I troubled you? Answer Me.

**6:4** Because I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and from the house of slavery I redeemed you, and I sent forth before your face Moses and Aaron and Miriam.

**6:5** My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab planned against you, and what Balaam son of Beor answered him, from the reeds as far as Gilgal, so that the righteousness of the Lord may be known.

**6:6** With what shall I reach the Lord, lay hold of my God Most High? Shall I reach Him with burnt offerings, with year-old calves?

**6:7** Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of rams, or with ten thousands of fat torrents? Shall I give my firstborn for ungodliness, the fruit of my womb for the sin of my soul?

**6:8** Has it been told to you, man, what is good? Or what does the Lord seek from you except to do justice, and to love mercy, and to be ready to walk with the Lord your God?

**6:9** The voice of the Lord will be called out to the city, and He will save those who fear His name. Hear, tribe, and who will adorn the city?

**6:10** Is there fire, and a house of a lawless one treasuring up lawless treasures, and with arrogance, injustice?

**6:11** Will a lawless one be justified with a scale, and weights of deceit in a bag?

**6:12** From which they filled their wealth with ungodliness, and those who dwell in it were speaking lies, and their tongue, be lifted up in their mouth.

**6:13** And I will begin to strike you; I will wipe you out in your sins.

**6:14** You will eat and will not be filled, and it will grow dark in you and turn aside, and you will not be saved, and as many as are saved will be handed over to a sword;

**6:15** you will sow and will not reap; you will press the olive and will not anoint yourself with oil, and wine, and you will not drink; and the ordinances of My people will be wiped out.

**6:16** And you kept the statutes of Zimri and all the works of the house of Ahab, and you walked in their ways, so that I may hand you over to desolation and those who dwell in it to hissing, and you will bear the reproaches of peoples.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek text reads 'Hear now a word. The Lord, the Lord (kyrios Kyrios) has said,' whereas other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew text read 'Hear the word of the Lord; the Lord has said' or 'Hear what the Lord says.'
- **6:5** — Greek 'apo ton schoinon' ('from the reeds') renders the Hebrew place name Shittim as a common plant noun.
- **6:8** — The Greek opens with a passive question ('Has it been told to you') and renders the final clause 'to be ready to walk with the Lord your God,' whereas the Hebrew reads 'He has told you... to walk humbly with your God.'
- **6:12** — The Greek reads 'their tongue, be lifted up (hypsotheti) in their mouth,' using a second-person passive imperative; other Greek manuscripts read the indicative 'was lifted up,' and the Hebrew reads 'their tongue is deceitful.'
- **6:16** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper name Zambrei (Zimri) where the Hebrew text reads 'Omri'; the closing clause of 6:15 ('and the ordinances of My people will be wiped out') is also unique to the Greek.
