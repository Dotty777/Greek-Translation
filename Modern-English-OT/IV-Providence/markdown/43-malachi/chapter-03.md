## Text

**3:1** "Look, I am sending out My messenger, and he will survey a way before Me; and suddenly the Lord whom you seek will come to His own temple, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire; look, He is coming," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:2** And who will endure the day of His entrance? Or who will stand firm at His appearing? For He enters like the fire of a smelting furnace and like the soap-herb of launderers.

**3:3** He will sit smelting and purifying as with silver and as with gold, and He will purify the sons of Levi and cast them like gold and like silver; and they will be offering a sacrifice to the Lord in righteousness,

**3:4** and the sacrifice of Judah and Jerusalem will please the Lord, just as in the days of old and just as in the former years.

**3:5** "And I will draw near to you in judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorceresses and against the adulteresses, and against those who swear by My name falsely, and against those who defraud a hired worker of wages, and those who oppress a widow, and those who beat orphans, and those who turn aside the justice of a proselyte, and those who do not fear Me," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:6** "For I am the Lord your God, and I have not changed."

**3:7** "And you, sons of Jacob, do not refrain from the injustices of your fathers; you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the Lord Almighty. "Yet you said, 'In what way shall we return?'

**3:8** Will a human cheat God? For you are cheating Me! And you will say, 'In what way have we cheated You?' Because the tithes and the firstfruits are with you;

**3:9** and looking away, you look away, and Me you are cheating.

**3:10** The year has been completed, and you brought all the produce into the storehouses, and his plunder will be in his house. Examine now in this," says the Lord Almighty, "whether I will not open for you the floodgates of heaven and pour out My blessing for you until it is enough;

**3:11** and I will appoint for you for food, and I will by no means destroy the fruit of your land, and your vine in the field will by no means grow weak," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:12** "And all the nations will call you blessed, because you yourselves will be a desirable land," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:13** "You have made your words heavy against Me," says the Lord. "Yet you said, 'In what way have we spoken against You?'

**3:14** You said, 'Futile is the one who serves God, and what advantage is it that we kept His charges, and that we walked as suppliants before the Lord Almighty?

**3:15** And now we ourselves call foreigners blessed, and all who do lawless things are built up, and they opposed God and were saved.'"

**3:16** Those who fear the Lord spoke these things, each to his neighbor; and the Lord paid attention and listened, and He wrote a book of remembrance before Himself for those who fear the Lord and who revere His name.

**3:17** "And they will be Mine," says the Lord Almighty, "for the day that I am making, as a treasured possession, and I will choose them in the way a man chooses his son who serves him.

**3:18** And you will return and see between a righteous person and a lawless person, and between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve.

**3:19** For look, a day is coming, burning like an oven, and it will set them ablaze, and all the foreigners and all who do lawless things will be stubble, and the coming day will kindle them," says the Lord Almighty, "and neither root nor branch will by any means be left from them.

**3:20** And for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise, and healing will be in its wings; and you will go out and leap like young calves released from bonds.

**3:21** And you will trample down lawless ones, for they will be ashes beneath your feet on the day that I am making," says the Lord Almighty.

**3:22** "And look, I am sending to you Elijah the Tishbite before the great and illustrious day of the Lord comes,

**3:23** who will restore the heart of a father to a son, and the heart of a person to his neighbor, so that I do not come and strike the land utterly."

**3:24** "Remember the law of Moses My servant, just as I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, decrees and ordinances."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek verb epiblepsetai means 'will survey' or 'will inspect' rather than Hebrew 'will clear/prepare' the way.
- **3:5** — The first two nouns in the Greek list are feminine plural ('the sorceresses and ... the adulteresses'), whereas the Hebrew has masculine plural forms; the Greek also specifies swearing 'by My name.'
- **3:8** — The Greek verb pternizo ('cheat,' literally 'trip by the heel' or 'supplant,' echoing Jacob in Genesis 27:36) replaces Hebrew 'rob,' and 3:9 reads 'looking away, you look away' instead of Hebrew 'you are cursed with a curse.'
- **3:10** — The Greek diverges substantially from the Hebrew here and in 3:11: 3:10 opens with declarative past actions ('The year has been completed, and you brought all the produce into the storehouses, and his plunder will be in his house'), and 3:11 reads 'I will appoint for you for food, and I will by no means destroy' (first-person singular) instead of Hebrew 'I will rebuke the devourer ... so that it will not destroy.'
- **3:24** — In the Septuagint order, the command to remember Moses' law stands last (3:24) after the Elijah prophecy (3:22-23), reversing the order of the Hebrew MT (3:22-24 / English 4:4-6). The Greek also specifies 'Elijah the Tishbite' (3:22, vs. Hebrew 'the prophet') and 'the heart of a person to his neighbor' (3:23, vs. Hebrew 'the hearts of children to their fathers').
