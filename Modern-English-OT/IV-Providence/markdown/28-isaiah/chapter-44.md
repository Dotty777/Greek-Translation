## Text

**44:1** "But now listen, Jacob My servant, and Israel whom I have chosen,"

**44:2** thus says the Lord God who made you and who formed you from the womb: "You will yet be helped; do not be afraid, My servant Jacob, and beloved Israel whom I have chosen.

**44:3** For I will give water in thirst to those who walk in a waterless land; I will put My Spirit upon your seed, and My blessings upon your children,

**44:4** and they will spring up like grass in the midst of water, and like a willow by flowing water.

**44:5** This one will say, 'I belong to God,' and this one will cry out by the name of Jacob, and another will write with his hand, 'I belong to God,' and by the name of Israel he will cry out."

**44:6** Thus says the King of Israel and the One who rescued him, God Sabaoth: "I am first, and I am after these things; besides Me there is no God.

**44:7** Who is like Me? Let him stand and call out and declare, and let him prepare for Me from the time I made humanity forever, and let them declare to you the things that are coming before they arrive.

**44:8** Do not hide yourselves and do not be led astray; did you not give ear from the beginning, and I declared it to you? You are witnesses: is there a God besides Me?"

**44:9** And they did not listen then. Those who form and those who carve are all futile, making their cherished things, which will not profit them; instead, they will be put to shame.

**44:10** Those who form a god and carve one are all unprofitable,

**44:11** and all from whom they came have dried up, and they are deaf from among people; let them all be gathered and stand together, and let them be disgraced and put to shame together.

**44:12** Because a craftsman sharpened iron, with an adze he worked it and with a drill he set it, and he worked it with the arm of his strength; and he will grow hungry and grow weak and will by no means drink water.

**44:13** Having chosen wood, a craftsman set it by measure, and shaped it with glue, and made it like the form of a man and like the beauty of a human being, to set it up in a house.

**44:14** He cut down wood from the forest which the Lord planted, and rain made it grow tall,

**44:15** so that it might be for burning for people; and taking from it, he warmed himself, and burning them, they baked loaves upon them; but the rest they worked into gods, and they bow down to them.

**44:16** Half of which he burned up in fire, and upon half of it he baked loaves on the coals, and roasting meat upon it, he ate and was filled, and having warmed himself, he said, "How pleasant for me, because I have warmed myself and seen a fire!"

**44:17** And the rest he made into a carved god, and he bows down and prays to it, saying, "Rescue me, because you are my god."

**44:18** They did not know how to understand, because they were darkened from seeing with their eyes and from perceiving with their heart.

**44:19** And he did not consider in his soul, nor did he recognize in his understanding that half of it he burned up in fire, and baked loaves on its coals, and having roasted meat, ate it, and the rest of it he made into an abomination, and they bow down to it.

**44:20** Know that their heart is ashes, and they are led astray, and no one is able to rescue his soul; look, you will not say, "There is a lie in my right hand."

**44:21** "Remember these things, Jacob and Israel, because you are My servant; I formed you as My servant, and you, Israel, do not forget Me.

**44:22** For look, I have wiped away your lawless deeds like a cloud, and your sins like a dark cloud; turn back to Me, and I will redeem you."

**44:23** Be glad, heavens, because God has had mercy on Israel; sound the trumpet, foundations of the earth; cry out with gladness, mountains, the hills and all the trees that are in them, because God has redeemed Jacob, and Israel will be glorified.

**44:24** Thus says the Lord who redeems you and forms you from the womb: "I am the Lord who accomplishes all things; I stretched out heaven alone, and I made the earth firm.

**44:25** Who else will scatter the signs of ventriloquists and divinations from the heart, turning wise men backward and making their counsel foolish,

**44:26** and establishing the words of His servant, and proving the counsel of His messengers true? The One who says to Jerusalem, 'You will be inhabited,' and to the cities of Idumea, 'You will be built,' and its deserted places will spring up;

**44:27** the One who says to the abyss, 'You will be made desolate, and I will dry up your rivers';

**44:28** the One who tells Cyrus to be wise, and, 'He will do all My wishes'; the One who says to Jerusalem, 'You will be built, and I will lay the foundation of My holy house.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:2** — Greek "beloved Israel" translates the Hebrew poetic name "Jeshurun," and the Greek places "You will yet be helped" at the start of the direct address.
- **44:6** — Greek "God Sabaoth" leaves Hebrew "Sabaoth" (hosts/armies) transliterated rather than translated, and reads "after these things" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the last."
- **44:11** — Greek "deaf ones from among people" likely arose from reading Hebrew harashim ("craftsmen") as hereshim ("deaf ones"), and "all from whom they came have dried up" differs from the Hebrew "all his associates will be put to shame."
- **44:26** — The Greek text reads "the cities of Idumea" (Edom) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the cities of Judah."
- **44:28** — Greek "tells Cyrus to be wise" (phronein) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "says of Cyrus, 'He is My shepherd,'" and Greek "I will lay the foundation" is first-person active rather than passive.
