## Text

**14:1** And the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will still choose Israel, and they will rest upon their land; and the proselyte will be joined to them, and will be joined to the house of Jacob,

**14:2** and nations will take them and bring them into their place, and they will receive an inheritance, and they will be multiplied upon the land as male slaves and female slaves; and those who took them captive will be captives, and those who ruled over them will be ruled over.

**14:3** And it will be in that day that the Lord will give you rest from your pain and your fury, your hard slavery with which you served them.

**14:4** And you will take up this lament over the king of Babylon, and you will say, "How the exactor has rested, and the taskmaster has rested!

**14:5** The Lord has shattered the yoke of sinners, the yoke of rulers,

**14:6** having struck a nation in fury with an incurable blow, beating a nation with a blow of fury that did not spare, He rested confident.

**14:7** The whole earth shouts with gladness,

**14:8** and the trees of Lebanon were glad over you, and the cedar of Lebanon: 'Since you have fallen asleep, the one who cuts us down has not come up.'

**14:9** Hades below was embittered at meeting you; all the giants who ruled the earth were roused together for you, who raised up from their thrones all the kings of nations.

**14:10** All will answer and say to you, 'You too have been captured just as we were, and you have been counted among us.'

**14:11** Your glory has gone down to Hades, your great gladness; beneath you they will spread decay, and your covering is the worm.

**14:12** How the morning star, who rises early, has fallen out of heaven! He who sent to all the nations has been shattered to the earth.

**14:13** But you said in your mind, 'I will ascend into heaven; above the stars of heaven I will set my throne; I will sit on a high mountain, upon the high mountains toward the north;

**14:14** I will ascend above the clouds; I will be like the Most High.'

**14:15** Now, however, you will go down to Hades and to the foundations of the earth.

**14:16** Those who see you will marvel at you and will say, 'This is the man who provoked the earth, shaking kings,

**14:17** who made the whole inhabited world desolate, and he tore down his cities; he did not release those in captivity.'

**14:18** All the kings of the nations slept in honor, each person in his house;

**14:19** but you will be cast out on the mountains, like a loathsome corpse, with many who have died, pierced with swords, going down to Hades.

**14:20** Just as a garment stained with blood will not be clean, so neither will you be clean, because you destroyed my land and killed my people; you will by no means remain forever, evil seed.

**14:21** Prepare your children to be slaughtered for the sins of their father, so that they may not rise up and inherit the earth and fill the earth with wars."

**14:22** "And I will rise up against them," says the Lord Sabaoth, "and I will destroy their name and remnant and seed," thus says the Lord.

**14:23** "And I will make Babylonia desolate so that hedgehogs dwell there, and it will become nothing; and I will make it a pit of mud for destruction."

**14:24** Thus says the Lord Sabaoth: "Just as I have spoken, so it will be, and just as I have planned, so it will remain,

**14:25** to destroy the Assyrians on My land and on My mountains; and they will be for trampling, and their yoke will be taken away from them, and their renown will be taken away from their shoulders."

**14:26** This is the plan that the Lord has planned against the whole inhabited world, and this is the uplifted hand against all the nations.

**14:27** For what the holy God has planned, who will thwart? And His uplifted hand, who will turn back?

**14:28** In the year in which King Ahaz died, this word came.

**14:29** Do not be glad, all you foreigners, for the yoke of the one beating you has been shattered; for from the seed of a serpent will come forth offspring of asps, and their offspring will come forth as flying serpents.

**14:30** And the poor will be pastured through him, and poor people will rest in peace; but he will kill your seed with famine, and your remnant he will kill.

**14:31** Wail, gates of cities; let troubled cities cry out, all you foreigners, because smoke is coming from the north, and there is no way to exist.

**14:32** And what will the kings of the nations answer? That the Lord has founded Zion, and through Him the humble of the people will be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Greek γιώρας is a Semitic loanword (from Aramaic gîyōrā / Hebrew gēr) denoting a proselyte or resident alien.
- **14:6** — The LXX divides vv. 6-7 differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, rendering "is at rest and quiet" as masculine singular ("He rested confident"), grammatically continuing the Lord as subject from v. 5 (though some interpret it of the oppressor from v. 4).
- **14:12** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text's second-person address, the LXX shifts to third person ("How the morning star ... has fallen") and reads "who sent to all the nations" for "who laid low the nations."
- **14:20** — The LXX reads first-person "my land" and "my people" (reflecting Israel's lament from v. 4, or God speaking) in place of the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "your land" and "your people."
- **14:29** — Here and in v. 31, the LXX uses οἱ ἀλλόφυλοι ("the foreigners") for the Philistines/Philistia of the Hebrew text; in v. 31, "there is no way to exist" renders the difficult Greek clause οὐκ ἔστιν τοῦ εἶναι.
