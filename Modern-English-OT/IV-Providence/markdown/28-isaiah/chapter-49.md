## Text

**49:1** Listen to me, islands, and pay attention, nations: "After a long time it will stand," says the Lord. From my mother's womb He called my name,

**49:2** and He made my mouth like a sharp sword, and under the shelter of His hand He hid me; He made me like a chosen arrow, and in His quiver He hid me,

**49:3** and He said to me, "You are My servant, Israel, and in you I will be glorified."

**49:4** And I said, "In vain I have labored; for emptiness and for nothing I have given my strength; therefore my justice is with the Lord, and my toil is before my God."

**49:5** And now, thus says the Lord, who formed me from the womb as a servant for Himself, to gather Jacob to Him and Israel—I will be gathered and glorified before the Lord, and God will be my strength—

**49:6** and He said to me, "It is a great thing for you to be called My servant, to raise up the tribes of Jacob and to bring back the dispersion of Israel; look, I have given you as a covenant for a people, as a light for nations, so that you may be for salvation to the end of the earth."

**49:7** Thus says the Lord, who redeems you, the God of Israel: "Sanctify the one who despises his own soul, the one who is abhorred by the nations, the servants of the rulers; kings will see him, and rulers will stand up and bow down to him for the sake of the Lord; because the Holy One of Israel is faithful, and I have chosen you."

**49:8** Thus says the Lord: "In an acceptable time I listened to you, and on a day of salvation I helped you; and I formed you and gave you as a covenant for nations, to establish the land and to inherit desolate inheritances,

**49:9** saying to those in bonds, 'Come out,' and to those in the darkness to be revealed. Along all the roads they will graze, and on all paths will be their pasture;

**49:10** they will not hunger, nor will they thirst, nor will scorching heat strike them, nor the sun, but the One who has mercy on them will comfort them, and through springs of waters He will lead them;

**49:11** and I will make every mountain into a road, and every path into pasture for them.

**49:12** Look, these will come from afar; these from the north and the sea, and others from the land of the Persians."

**49:13** Be glad, heavens, and let the earth rejoice; let the mountains break forth with gladness, because God has had mercy on His people, and He has comforted the humble of His people.

**49:14** But Zion said, "The Lord has forsaken me, and the Lord has forgotten me."

**49:15** "Can a woman forget her little child, or not have mercy on the offspring of her womb? Yet even if a woman should forget these, I will not forget you," said the Lord.

**49:16** Look, on My hands I have painted your walls, and you are before Me continually;

**49:17** and quickly you will be built by those who tore you down, and those who laid you waste will go out from you.

**49:18** Lift up your eyes all around and see them all; look, they have gathered together and come to you. "As I live," says the Lord, "surely you will clothe yourself with all of them like an ornament, and you will put them around yourself like an ornament, like a bride."

**49:19** For your desolate places and your ruined places and your fallen places will now be cramped because of the inhabitants, and those who swallow you up will be far away from you.

**49:20** For your sons whom you have lost will say in your ears, "The place is too cramped for me; make a place for me so that I may dwell."

**49:21** And you will say in your heart, "Who bore these for me? Yet I am childless and a widow. And who raised these for me? I was left alone, and where were these for me?"

**49:22** Thus says the Lord: "Look, I am lifting up My hand to the nations, and to the islands I will lift up My signal; and they will bring your sons in their bosom, and your daughters they will carry on their shoulders;

**49:23** and kings will be your foster fathers, and their princesses your nurses; upon the face of the earth they will bow down to you, and they will lick the dust of your feet; and you will know that I am the Lord, and those who wait for Me will not be put to shame."

**49:24** Will anyone take plunder from a giant? And if anyone takes captives unjustly, will he be saved?

**49:25** Thus says the Lord: "If anyone takes a giant captive, he will take plunder; and one taking from a strong man will be saved; and I will judge your case, and I will redeem your sons;

**49:26** and those who afflicted you will eat their own flesh, and they will drink their own blood like new wine and become drunk; and all flesh will perceive that I am the Lord, who redeems you and upholds the strength of Jacob."

## Translator Notes

- **49:1** — The Septuagint translates Hebrew "from afar" temporally as "after a long time" and supplies "it will stand, says the Lord."
- **49:5** — Greek syntax shifts abruptly after "and Israel" into the Servant's parenthetical declaration ("I will be gathered and glorified..."), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("though Israel be not gathered").
- **49:6** — The Septuagint reads "It is a great thing for you" (against Hebrew "It is too small a thing") and includes "as a covenant for a people" before "as a light for nations."
- **49:7** — The Septuagint opens the divine speech with the imperative "Sanctify" (against Hebrew "to the despised of soul") and renders "servant of rulers" as plural "servants of the rulers" in apposition to "the nations."
- **49:12** — The Septuagint identifies the distant region as "the land of the Persians" (corresponding to "Sinim" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and "the sea" reflects the Hebrew directional idiom for the west.
