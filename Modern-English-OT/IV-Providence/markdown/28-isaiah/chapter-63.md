## Text

**63:1** Who is this coming from Edom, with redness of garments from Bozrah? So splendid in a robe, with force, with strength? I declare righteousness and judgment of salvation.

**63:2** Why are Your garments red, and Your clothing as from a trodden winepress?

**63:3** I am full of a trampled winepress, and of the nations there is no man with Me; and I trampled them in My wrath, and I crushed them like earth, and I brought down their blood.

**63:4** For a day of retribution has come upon them, and a year of redemption is here.

**63:5** And I looked, and there was no helper; and I observed, and no one was coming to aid; and My arm rescued them, and My wrath stood firm.

**63:6** And I trampled them in My anger, and I brought down their blood to the earth.

**63:7** I remembered the mercy of the Lord, the excellences of the Lord in all that He repays to us. The Lord is a good judge to the house of Israel; He brings to us according to His mercy and according to the abundance of His righteousness.

**63:8** And He said, "Are they not My people? Children, they will certainly not break faith," and He became salvation to them

**63:9** out of all their affliction. Not an envoy nor an angel, but He Himself saved them, because He loved them and spared them; He Himself redeemed them and took them up, and exalted them all the days of old.

**63:10** But they disobeyed and provoked His Holy Spirit; and He turned to enmity against them, He Himself fought against them.

**63:11** And he remembered days of old: Where is the One who brought up from the sea the shepherd of the sheep? Where is the One who put the Holy Spirit within them?

**63:12** The One who led Moses by the right hand, the arm of His glory? He overpowered water before him, to make for Himself an everlasting name.

**63:13** He led them through the deep like a horse through a wilderness, and they did not grow weary,

**63:14** and like cattle through a plain; the Spirit came down from the Lord and guided them. Thus You led Your people, to make for Yourself a name of glory.

**63:15** Turn back from heaven and look from Your holy house and Your glory; where is Your zeal and Your strength? Where is the abundance of Your mercy and Your compassions, that You have held back from us?

**63:16** For You are our Father, because Abraham did not know us, and Israel did not recognize us; but You, Lord, are our Father; rescue us; from the beginning Your name is upon us.

**63:17** Why have You caused us to wander, Lord, from Your way? Have You hardened our hearts so as not to fear You? Turn back for the sake of Your servants, for the sake of the tribes of Your inheritance,

**63:18** so that we may inherit a little of Your holy mountain.

**63:19** We have become as from the beginning, when You did not rule over us, nor was Your name called upon us. If You open heaven, trembling will seize mountains from You, and they will melt

## Translator Notes

- **63:1** — Greek "Bosor" is the standard LXX rendering of Bozrah; LXX reads "redness of garments," "with force," and "judgment of salvation" where MT has "crimsoned garments," "marching," and "mighty to save."
- **63:3** — LXX reads "full of a trampled [winepress]" (a feminine singular participle, καταπεπατημένης, agreeing with the winepress, ληνός, of 63:2) where MT has "I have trodden the winepress alone."
- **63:9** — LXX reads "Not an envoy nor an angel [or 'messenger'], but He Himself saved them" where MT has "In all their affliction He was afflicted, and the angel of His presence saved them."
- **63:14** — Greek πνεῦμα παρὰ Κυρίου is anarthrous and can be rendered either "the Spirit ... from the Lord" or "a spirit from the Lord."
- **63:18** — LXX reads "so that we may inherit a little [or 'for a little while'] of Your holy mountain" and omits the second half of the verse found in MT.
- **63:19** — In LXX, the second half of 63:19 corresponds to MT 64:1a and breaks mid-sentence ("and they will melt") before continuing into 64:1.
