## Text

**66:1** Thus says the Lord, "Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet; what sort of house will you build for Me? And what is the place of My rest?

**66:2** For My hand made all these things, and all these things are Mine," says the Lord; "and upon whom will I look, except upon the humble and quiet person, and the one who trembles at My words?

**66:3** But the lawless one who sacrifices a calf to Me is like one who kills a dog; the one who offers fine flour, like pig's blood; the one who gives frankincense for a memorial, like a blasphemer. And they themselves chose their ways, and their soul desired their abominations,

**66:4** and I will receive their mockeries, and I will pay back their sins to them; because I called them and they did not listen to Me, I spoke and they did not hear, and they did what was evil before Me, and chose what I did not desire."

**66:5** Hear the words of the Lord, you who tremble at His word: Say, our brothers, to those who hate you and detest you, that the name of the Lord may be glorified and appear in their gladness, and they will be put to shame.

**66:6** A sound of outcry from the city, a sound from the temple, a sound of the Lord paying back repayment to those who oppose.

**66:7** Before the woman in labor gave birth, before the pain of birth pangs came, she escaped and gave birth to a male child.

**66:8** Who has heard such a thing, and who has seen in this way? Has a land been in labor in one day, or has a nation even been born all at once? Because Zion was in labor and gave birth to her children.

**66:9** "And I gave this expectation, and you did not remember Me," said the Lord. "Look, did I not make the one who gives birth and the barren woman?" said your God.

**66:10** Be glad, Jerusalem, and celebrate a festival in her, all you who love her; rejoice with joy alongside her, all you who mourn over her,

**66:11** so that you may nurse and be filled from the breast of her comfort, so that, having nursed fully, you may delight yourselves from the entrance of her glory.

**66:12** For thus says the Lord, "Look, I am turning toward them like a river of peace, and like a torrent flooding the glory of the nations; their children will be carried on shoulders and comforted on knees.

**66:13** Just as if a mother comforts someone, so I also will comfort you, and in Jerusalem you will be comforted.

**66:14** And you will see, and your heart will rejoice, and your bones will sprout like grass; and the hand of the Lord will be known to those who fear Him, and He will threaten those who disobey."

**66:15** For look, the Lord will come like fire, and His chariots like a whirlwind, to repay His vengeance in wrath and His utter rejection in a flame of fire.

**66:16** For by the fire of the Lord all the earth will be judged, and by His sword all flesh; many will be wounded by the Lord.

**66:17** Those who sanctify themselves and purify themselves for the gardens, and in the porches eat pig's flesh and the abominations and the mouse, they will be consumed together, said the Lord.

**66:18** And I—their works and their thought; I am coming to gather all the nations and the tongues, and they will come and see My glory.

**66:19** And I will leave a sign upon them, and I will send out saved ones from among them to the nations, to Tarshish and Put and Lud and Meshech, and to Tubal and to Greece, and to the distant islands, who have not heard My name nor seen My glory; and they will declare My glory among the nations.

**66:20** And they will bring your brothers from all the nations as a gift to the Lord, with horses and chariots, in covered wagons of mules with sunshades, to the holy city Jerusalem, said the Lord, just as the sons of Israel would bring their sacrifices to Me with psalms into the house of the Lord.

**66:21** And from them I will take priests and Levites, said the Lord.

**66:22** For just as the new heaven and the new earth that I am making remain before Me, says the Lord, so your seed and your name will stand.

**66:23** And it will be that month after month and Sabbath after Sabbath, all flesh will come to worship before Me in Jerusalem, said the Lord.

**66:24** And they will go out and see the corpses of the people who transgressed against Me; for their worm will not die, and their fire will not be extinguished, and they will be a spectacle to all flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **66:3** — The Septuagint introduces the verse with 'the lawless one' (ho anomos) and reads 'like one who kills a dog,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins 'He who slaughters an ox is like one who strikes down a man.'
- **66:5** — The Greek renders this as an imperative ('Say, our brothers, to those who hate you...') followed by a purpose clause, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reports the enemies' speech ('Your brothers who hate you... say, "May the Lord be glorified"').
- **66:9** — The Septuagint reads 'I gave this expectation, and you did not remember Me,' differing markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('Shall I bring to the point of birth and not cause to bring forth?').
- **66:19** — The Septuagint lists Put (Phoud), Meshech (Mosoch), and Greece (Hellas) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Pul,' 'who draw the bow,' and 'Javan.'
- **66:20** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'on dromedaries' and 'in a clean vessel,' the Septuagint reads 'in covered wagons of mules with sunshades' and 'to Me with psalms.'
