## Text

**59:1** Is the hand of the Lord not strong enough to save? Or has His ear grown heavy so as not to listen?

**59:2** Rather, your sinful deeds divide between you and God, and because of your sins He turned His face away from you so as not to have mercy.

**59:3** For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers are in sins; your lips have spoken lawlessness, and your tongue meditates on injustice.

**59:4** No one speaks righteous things, nor is there true justice; they trust in futile things and speak empty things, because they conceive trouble and give birth to lawlessness.

**59:5** They have broken open eggs of asps, and they weave a spider's web; and the one who is about to eat from their eggs, upon crushing one, found an addled egg, and in it was a basilisk.

**59:6** Their web will not become a garment, nor will they clothe themselves from their works; for their works are works of lawlessness.

**59:7** And their feet run toward wickedness, swift to shed blood, and their thoughts come from murders; ruin and misery are in their ways,

**59:8** and they do not know a way of peace, and there is no justice in their ways; for the paths that they travel are crooked, and they do not know peace.

**59:9** Therefore justice has departed from them, and righteousness will never overtake them; while they waited for light, darkness came to them; waiting for brightness, they walked in gloom.

**59:10** They will grope for a wall like the blind, and as though having no eyes they will grope; they will fall at midday as at midnight; like those who are dying they will groan,

**59:11** like a bear and like a dove they will go together; we waited for justice, and there is none; salvation stands far off from us.

**59:12** For our lawlessness is great before You, and our sins have stood against us; for our lawless deeds are within us, and our unjust deeds we have known;

**59:13** we acted ungodly and lied, and turned away from behind our God; we spoke unjust things and disobeyed, we were conceiving and devising unjust words from our heart,

**59:14** and we drove justice back, and righteousness stands far off; for truth has been consumed in their ways, and they were not able to pass through a straight course.

**59:15** And truth has been taken away, and they removed their mind from understanding; and the Lord saw, and it did not please Him, because there was no justice.

**59:16** And He saw, and there was no man, and He observed, and there was no one to assist; and He defended them with His arm, and with mercy He sustained Himself.

**59:17** And He put on righteousness like a breastplate, and placed a helmet of salvation upon His head, and clothed Himself with a garment of vengeance and His cloak,

**59:18** as One about to repay a repayment, a reproach to the adversaries.

**59:19** And those from the west will fear the name of the Lord, and those from the rising of the sun the glorious name; for wrath from the Lord will come like a violent river; it will come with fury.

**59:20** And the Deliverer will come for the sake of Zion, and He will turn away ungodly deeds from Jacob.

**59:21** And this is the covenant for them from Me, said the Lord: My Spirit that is upon you, and the words that I have put into your mouth, will never fail from your mouth and from the mouth of your seed; for the Lord has said, from now and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **59:5** — Greek ourion specifically denotes a "wind-egg" (an unfertilized or addled egg), differing from MT "that which is crushed."
- **59:11** — LXX keeps the third-person plural ("they will go together") from vv. 9-10 before shifting to first-person plural ("we waited"), and turns MT's "we all growl like bears and moan like doves" into walking together.
- **59:16** — Greek emynato autous means "He defended them" (as in Acts 7:24, matching Heb. "saved"), though in Greek it can also mean "retaliated against them"; "with mercy" (te eleemosyne) renders MT "His righteousness."
- **59:18** — LXX joins v. 18 directly to the end of v. 17, reading "a reproach to the adversaries" while lacking MT's "zeal" (v. 17) and the recompense to the coastlands (v. 18b).
- **59:20** — LXX reads "for the sake of Zion" (heneken Seion), differing from MT "to Zion" and Paul's citation "out of Zion" in Romans 11:26.
