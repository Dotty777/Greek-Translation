## Text

**57:1** See how the righteous one has perished, and no one takes it to heart, and righteous men are taken away, and no one considers. For from the face of unrighteousness the righteous one has been taken away;

**57:2** his burial will be in peace; he has been taken away from the midst.

**57:3** But you, come near here, lawless sons, offspring of adulterers and a prostitute;

**57:4** in whom did you delight? And against whom did you open your mouth? And against whom did you let loose your tongue? Are you not children of destruction, lawless offspring?

**57:5** Those who entreat the idols under leafy trees, slaughtering their children in the valleys in the midst of the rocks.

**57:6** That is your portion, this is your lot, and to those you poured out drink offerings, and to these you offered up sacrifices; over these things, then, will I not be angry?

**57:7** Upon a high and lofty mountain, there is your bed, and there you brought up sacrifices;

**57:8** and behind the posts of your door you placed your memorials; you thought that if you turned away from Me, you would have something more; you loved those who sleep with you,

**57:9** and you multiplied your prostitution with them, and you made those far from you many, and you sent envoys beyond your borders, and you were brought low as far as Hades.

**57:10** By your many journeys you grew weary, and you did not say, "I will stop," growing strong because you did these things; because of this you yourself did not entreat Me.

**57:11** Whom did you dread and fear, that you lied to Me and did not remember, nor did you take Me into your mind or into your heart? And I, seeing you, look past you, and you did not fear Me.

**57:12** And I will declare your righteousness and your evil deeds, which will not benefit you;

**57:13** when you cry out, let them rescue you in your affliction; for wind will take all these, and a storm will carry them away. But those who hold fast to Me will possess the land, and will inherit My holy mountain,

**57:14** and they will say, "Clear ways from before him, and take away stumbling blocks from the way of My people."

**57:15** Thus says the Most High in the high places, inhabiting eternity, Holy in the holy places is His name, Most High resting in the holy places, and giving patience to the fainthearted, and giving life to those crushed in heart:

**57:16** "I will not take vengeance on you forever, nor will I be angry with you continually; for a spirit will go forth from Me, and I Myself made every breath.

**57:17** Because of sin I grieved him for a little while, and I struck him and turned My face away from him; and he was grieved, and went downcast in his ways.

**57:18** I have seen his ways, and I healed him and comforted him, and I gave him true comfort,

**57:19** peace upon peace to those who are far off and to those who are near." And the Lord said, "I will heal them";

**57:20** but the unrighteous will be tossed like waves and will not be able to rest.

**57:21** "There is no rejoicing," said God, "for the ungodly."

## Translator Notes

- **57:2** — The Septuagint explicitly mentions "his burial" (ἡ ταφὴ αὐτοῦ) and repeats the verb ἦρται ("has been taken away") from v. 1.
- **57:5** — Greek παρακαλοῦντες τὰ εἴδωλα ("entreat/comfort the idols") reflects reading the Hebrew verb from nḥm ("comfort/appeal to") rather than ḥmm ("be inflamed with lust").
- **57:8** — The clause "you thought that if you turned away from Me, you would have something more" is a distinctive Septuagint rendering diverging from the Masoretic Text.
- **57:15** — The phrase ἐν ἁγίοις can mean either "in the holy places" (parallel to ἐν ὑψηλοῖς, "in the high places") or "among the holy ones"; "giving patience to the fainthearted" also differs from Hebrew "to revive the spirit of the lowly."
- **57:21** — The Septuagint reads χαίρειν ("rejoicing") instead of Hebrew "peace," and ὁ θεός ("God") rather than "my God."
