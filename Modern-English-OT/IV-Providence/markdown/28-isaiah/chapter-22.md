## Text

**22:1** The word of the valley of Zion. What has happened to you that now you have all gone up onto vain rooftops?

**22:2** The city was filled with those shouting; your wounded are not wounded by swords, nor are your dead, dead by war.

**22:3** All your rulers have fled, and having been captured, they are harshly bound; and those who are strong in you have fled far away.

**22:4** Because of this I said, "Leave me alone, I will weep bitterly; do not insist on comforting me over the shattering of the daughter of my people."

**22:5** For it is a day of turmoil and destruction, and trampling and wandering, from the Lord Sabaoth; in the valley of Zion they wander, from small to great they wander upon the mountains.

**22:6** And the Elamites took quivers, men mounted on horses, and a gathering of battle-array.

**22:7** And your choice valleys will be—they will be filled with chariots, and the horsemen will block your gates,

**22:8** and they will uncover the gates of Judah; and they will look in that day into the choice houses of the city,

**22:9** and they will uncover the hidden things of the houses of the citadel of David. And they saw that they are many, and that he turned back the water of the ancient pool into the city,

**22:10** and that they tore down the houses of Jerusalem for fortifications of a wall for the city.

**22:11** And you made for yourselves water between the two walls, inside the ancient pool, and you did not look to the One who made it from the beginning, and the One who created it you did not see.

**22:12** And the Lord, the Lord Sabaoth, called in that day for weeping and lamentation and shaving and girding with sackcloth,

**22:13** but they made gladness and exultation, slaughtering calves and sacrificing sheep, so as to eat meat and drink wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."

**22:14** And these things are uncovered in the ears of the Lord Sabaoth, that this sin will not be forgiven you until you die.

**22:15** Thus says the Lord Sabaoth: "Go into the chamber to Shebna the treasurer and say to him,

**22:16** 'Why are you here, and what do you have here, that you have hewed out a tomb for yourself here, and made a tomb for yourself on high, and carved a tent for yourself in a rock?

**22:17** Look now, the Lord Sabaoth casts out and will crush a man, and He will remove your robe and your glorious crown,

**22:18** and He will hurl you into a large and measureless land, and there you will die; and He will put your fine chariot to dishonor, and the house of your ruler,

**22:19** and you will be removed from your stewardship and from your station.

**22:20** And it will be in that day that I will call My servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah,

**22:21** and I will clothe him with your robe, and your crown I will give to him with power, and your stewardship I will give into his hands; and he will be like a father to those who live in Jerusalem and to those who live in Judah.

**22:22** And I will give the glory of David to him, and he will rule, and there will be no one who opposes, and he will shut, and there is no one who opens.

**22:23** And I will establish him as a ruler in a faithful place, and he will become a throne of glory of the house of his father.

**22:24** And every glorious one in the house of his father will be trusting in him, from small to great, and they will be hanging upon him

**22:25** in that day.'" Thus says the Lord Sabaoth: "The man who has been firmly fixed in a faithful place will be moved, and he will be removed and will fall, and the glory that is upon him will be utterly destroyed," for the Lord has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — Greek reads 'The word (to rhema) of the valley of Zion (Seion),' whereas the Hebrew MT has 'The oracle of the valley of vision.'
- **22:6** — Greek renders the final clause as 'and a gathering of battle-array' (synagoge parataxeos), whereas the Hebrew MT reads 'and Kir uncovered the shield.'
- **22:12** — Greek preserves the double divine title kyrios Kyrios sabaoth ('the Lord, the Lord Sabaoth'), corresponding to Hebrew Adonay YHWH sebaot.
- **22:15** — The Greek spells the treasurer's name Somnas (accusative Σόμναν, corresponding to Σόβνας in 36:3 and Hebrew Shebna, modernized consistently to Shebna) and adds 'into the chamber' (eis to pastophorion).
- **22:22** — Greek reads 'the glory of David' (ten doxan Dauid) where the Hebrew MT has 'the key of the house of David,' and reads 'no one opposing' instead of 'none will open.'
