## Text

**65:1** I became manifest to those who do not inquire of Me, I was found by those who do not seek Me; I said, "Here I am," to the nation who did not call My name.

**65:2** I stretched out My hands all day long to a disobedient and contradicting people, to those who walk in a way that is not good, but after their sins.

**65:3** This people is the one who provokes Me before Me continually; they sacrifice in the gardens and burn incense upon the bricks to the demons that do not exist,

**65:4** in the tombs and in the caves they sleep for dreams, those who eat pig's flesh and broth of sacrifices—all their vessels are defiled,

**65:5** those who say, "Far from me, do not come near me, for I am pure"; this is smoke of My wrath; a fire burns in it all the days.

**65:6** Look, it is written before Me: "I will not keep silent until I repay into their bosom

**65:7** their sins and those of their fathers," says the Lord; those who burned incense on the mountains and reviled Me on the hills—I will repay their works into their bosom.

**65:8** Thus says the Lord, "Just as a grape is found in the cluster, and they say, 'Do not destroy it, for a blessing is in it,' so I will do for the sake of him who serves Me; for this reason I will by no means destroy everyone.

**65:9** And I will bring out the seed from Jacob and from Judah, and it will inherit My holy mountain; and My chosen ones and My servants will inherit it, and they will dwell there.

**65:10** And in the forest there will be folds for flocks, and the Valley of Achor for a resting place of herds, for My people who sought Me.

**65:11** But you who forsake Me and forget My holy mountain, and prepare a table for the demon and fill a mixed drink for Fortune,

**65:12** I will hand you over to the sword; all of you will fall in slaughter, because I called you and you did not listen, I spoke and you disregarded, and you did what was evil before Me and chose what I did not desire."

**65:13** Therefore thus says the Lord, "Look, those who serve Me will eat, but you will go hungry; look, those who serve Me will drink, but you will be thirsty; look, those who serve Me will be glad, but you will be put to shame;

**65:14** look, those who serve Me will exult in gladness, but you will cry out because of pain of heart, and from crushing of your spirit you will wail.

**65:15** For you will leave your name for satiety to My chosen ones, and the Lord will put you to death. But for those who serve Me, a new name will be called,

**65:16** which will be blessed on the earth; for they will bless the true God, and those who swear on the earth will swear by the true God. For they will forget the former affliction, and it will not come up into their heart.

**65:17** For the heaven will be new and the earth new, and they will by no means remember the former things, nor will they ever come upon their heart,

**65:18** but gladness and exultation they will find in it; for look, I am making Jerusalem exultation and My people gladness.

**65:19** And I will exult over Jerusalem, and I will be glad over My people; and no longer will the sound of weeping or the sound of crying out be heard in her,

**65:20** nor will there ever again be there one dying prematurely or an old man who will not fill up his time; for the youth will be a hundred years old, and the sinner dying at a hundred years will also be accursed.

**65:21** And they will build houses and they themselves will inhabit them, and they will plant vineyards and they themselves will eat their produce;

**65:22** they will by no means build and others inhabit them, and they will by no means plant and others eat. For like the days of the tree of life will be the days of My people; for they will wear out the works of their labors.

**65:23** My chosen ones will not toil in vain, nor will they bear children for a curse, because they are a seed blessed by God, and their descendants with them.

**65:24** And it will be that before they cry out, I will listen to them; while they are still speaking, I will say, 'What is it?'

**65:25** Then wolves and lambs will graze together, and a lion will eat straw like an ox, and a serpent, earth like bread. They will not harm nor destroy upon My holy mountain," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **65:8** — The Septuagint reads singular 'him who serves Me' (tou douleuontos moi) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has plural 'My servants.'
- **65:10** — Greek en tō drymō ('in the forest') translates the Hebrew place name 'Sharon' as a common noun.
- **65:11** — Greek 'the demon' (tō daimoniō) and 'Fortune' (tē tychē) render the deity names Gad and Meni in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **65:15** — Greek eis plēsmonēn ('for satiety' or 'to surfeit') reflects Hebrew śābʿâ ('satiety') where the Masoretic Text reads šəbûʿâ ('oath/curse').
- **65:22** — The Septuagint reads 'the tree of life' (tou xylou tēs zōēs), adding 'of life' compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'a tree.'
