## Text

**34:1** Draw near, nations, and hear, rulers; let the earth hear and those who are in it, the inhabited world and the people who are in it.

**34:2** For the wrath of the Lord is against all the nations, and anger against their number, to destroy them and to hand them over to slaughter.

**34:3** And their wounded will be cast out, and the dead, and their stench will go up, and the mountains will be drenched with their blood.

**34:4** And all the powers of the heavens will melt, and heaven will be rolled up like a scroll, and all the stars will fall like leaves from a vine, and as leaves fall from a fig tree.

**34:5** My sword has become drunk in heaven; look, it will come down upon Idumea and upon the people of destruction with judgment.

**34:6** The sword of the Lord has been filled with blood; it has been fattened from fat, from the blood of male goats and lambs, and from the fat of male goats and rams. For there is a sacrifice to the Lord in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in Idumea,

**34:7** and the mighty ones will fall with them, and the rams and the bulls; and the land will become drunk from the blood, and from their fat it will be filled.

**34:8** For it is a day of the Lord's judgment, and a year of recompense for the judgment of Zion.

**34:9** And its ravines will be turned into pitch, and its land into sulfur; and its land will be like burning pitch

**34:10** night and day, and it will not be quenched for eternal time, and its smoke will rise upward; for its generations it will be made desolate, and for a long time.

**34:11** Birds and hedgehogs and ibises and ravens will dwell in it; and a cord of desert surveying will be cast upon it, and onocentaurs will live in it.

**34:12** Its rulers will be no more; for its kings and its nobles will come to destruction.

**34:13** And thorny trees will spring up in their cities and in its strongholds, and it will be dwellings of sirens and a court of ostriches.

**34:14** And demons will meet with onocentaurs, and they will cry out one to another; there onocentaurs will rest, having found rest for themselves.

**34:15** There a hedgehog made its nest, and the land saved its young in safety; there deer met and saw one another's faces.

**34:16** They passed by in number, and not one of them perished; one did not seek another, because the Lord commanded them, and His Spirit gathered them.

**34:17** And He Himself will cast lots for them, and His hand apportioned it for grazing; for eternal time you will inherit it; for generations of generations they will rest upon it.

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — Greek 'their number' (ton arithmon auton) translates the Hebrew for 'their host/army' (compare v. 16, 'in number').
- **34:3** — Greek 'will be drenched' (brachesetai) differs from the Masoretic Text ('will melt').
- **34:6** — The Greek uses the Septuagint proper name 'Idumea' (Idoumaia, also in v. 5) for Edom, and spells Bozrah as 'Bosor' (Βοσόρ, rendered with standard English Bozrah as in 63:1).
- **34:11** — Here and in vv. 13-15 the Septuagint renders desert wildlife with terms including 'ibises,' 'sirens' (v. 13), 'demons' (v. 14), 'onocentaurs' (vv. 11, 14), and 'deer' (v. 15), alongside 'a cord of desert surveying.'
- **34:17** — The Greek shifts abruptly to the second-person plural 'you will inherit' (kleronomesete) before returning to third-person plural 'they will rest.'
