## Text

**6:1** And it came about in the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting on a high and exalted throne, and the house was full of His glory.

**6:2** And seraphim were standing around Him; one had six wings, and another had six wings: with two they were covering their faces, with two they were covering their feet, and with two they were flying.

**6:3** And one cried out to another, and they were saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Sabaoth; all the earth is full of His glory."

**6:4** And the lintel was lifted up at the voice with which they cried out, and the house was filled with smoke.

**6:5** And I said, "Oh, wretched am I, for I am cut to the heart! Because being a man and having unclean lips, I live in the midst of a people having unclean lips, and I have seen the King, the Lord Sabaoth, with my eyes."

**6:6** And one of the seraphim was sent to me, and in its hand it had a live coal that it had taken with the tongs from the altar,

**6:7** and it touched my mouth and said, "Look, this has touched your lips, and will take away your iniquities and completely cleanse your sins."

**6:8** And I heard the voice of the Lord saying, "Whom shall I send, and who will go to this people?" And I said, "Here I am; send me."

**6:9** And He said, "Go and say to this people, 'Hearing you will hear, and you will by no means understand; and looking you will look, and you will by no means see.'

**6:10** For the heart of this people has grown calloused, and with their ears they heard heavily, and they closed their eyes, so that they might never see with their eyes and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart and turn back, and I will heal them."

**6:11** And I said, "How long, Lord?" And He said, "Until cities are laid waste because they are not inhabited, and houses because there are no people, and the land will be left desolate.

**6:12** And after these things God will remove the people far away, and those who are left will be multiplied on the land,

**6:13** and still on it is the tenth part, and again it will be for plunder, like a terebinth and like an acorn when it falls out of its husk."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Greek reads "and the house was full of His glory" (cf. John 12:41), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (MT) refers to the hem of His robe filling the temple.
- **6:3** — Greek Κύριος σαβαώθ (Kyrios Sabaoth) transliterates the Hebrew title "the Lord of hosts" rather than translating it, as also in 6:5, 7:7, and 8:18.
- **6:4** — Greek reads "the lintel was lifted up," whereas the MT reads "the foundations of the thresholds shook."
- **6:10** — Greek uses past and active indicatives ("has grown calloused ... they closed") where the MT has imperatives, a form echoed in Matthew 13:15 and Acts 28:27.
- **6:12** — Greek adds "after these things" and reads "those who are left will be multiplied on the land" (MT: "the forsaken places are many"), and 6:13 ends without the MT's "holy seed" clause.
