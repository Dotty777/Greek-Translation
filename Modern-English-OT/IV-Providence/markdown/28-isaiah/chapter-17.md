## Text

**17:1** The word against Damascus. Look, Damascus will be removed from among cities, and it will become a ruin,

**17:2** abandoned forever, for a resting place of flocks and for rest, and there will be no one pursuing;

**17:3** and there will no longer be a stronghold for Ephraim to take refuge, and no longer a kingdom in Damascus nor the remainder of the Syrians; for you are no better than the sons of Israel and their glory. Thus says the Lord of Sabaoth:

**17:4** "In that day there will be a failing of the glory of Jacob, and the rich parts of his glory will be shaken.

**17:5** And it will be just as when someone gathers a standing harvest and reaps seed of heads of grain, and it will be just as when someone gathers a head of grain in a hard ravine,

**17:6** and stubble is left in it, or like two or three olive berries on a lofty top, or four or five are left on their branches." Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel.

**17:7** On that day man will trust in the One who made him, and his eyes will look to the Holy One of Israel,

**17:8** and they will by no means trust in the altars nor in the works of their hands, which their fingers made, and they will not look upon the trees nor their abominations.

**17:9** On that day your cities will be abandoned, just as the Amorites and the Hivites abandoned them from the presence of the sons of Israel, and they will be desolate;

**17:10** because you abandoned God your Savior, and you did not remember the Lord your Helper. Therefore you will plant an unfaithful planting and an unfaithful seed;

**17:11** but on the day that you plant, you will go astray; and in the morning, if you sow, it will blossom for harvest on whatever day you inherit, and like a father of a man you will allot an inheritance to your sons.

**17:12** Woe, multitude of many nations; like a surging sea, so you will be thrown into turmoil, and the back of many nations will resound like water;

**17:13** like much water are many nations, like much water driven by force. And He will cast him off, and will pursue him far away like fine chaff of those who winnow before the wind, and like dust of a wheel that a whirlwind carries.

**17:14** Toward evening there will be mourning; before morning he will be no more. This is the portion of those who plundered you, and an inheritance for those who inherited you.

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Greek ends the Damascus oracle with "you are no better than the sons of Israel and their glory" (for Hebrew "they will be like the glory of the sons of Israel") and transliterates Hebrew sebaoth ("hosts") as "Sabaoth."
- **17:5** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name "Rephaim" as an adjective ("in a hard ravine"), describing rocky or unyielding terrain.
- **17:8** — The Greek renders Hebrew Asherim (sacred poles) literally as "the trees" and incense altars as "their abominations."
- **17:9** — Where the Hebrew has "the forsaken places of the forest and the summit" (or Horesh and Amir), the Greek reads the gentilic proper names "the Amorites and the Hivites."
- **17:11** — Interpreting the Hebrew words for "sickness" as "inheritance" and "incurable pain" as "like a father of a man," the Greek speaks of inheriting and allotting an inheritance to sons.
