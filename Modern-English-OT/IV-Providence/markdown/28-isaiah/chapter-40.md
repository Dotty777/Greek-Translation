## Text

**40:1** "Comfort, comfort My people," says God.

**40:2** Priests, speak to the heart of Jerusalem, comfort her, because her humiliation has been fulfilled; her sin has been pardoned, because she received from the hand of the Lord double for her sins.

**40:3** A voice of one crying out in the wilderness: "Prepare the way of the Lord; make the paths of our God straight."

**40:4** Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill will be brought low; and all the crooked places will become straight, and the rough ground into plains,

**40:5** and the glory of the Lord will be seen, and all flesh will see the salvation of God, for the Lord has spoken.

**40:6** A voice of one saying, "Cry out." And I said, "What should I cry out?" "All flesh is grass, and all the glory of humanity is like a flower of grass.

**40:7** The grass has dried up, and the flower has fallen off,

**40:8** but the word of our God remains forever."

**40:9** Go up onto a high mountain, you who bring good news to Zion; lift up your voice with strength, you who bring good news to Jerusalem; lift it up, do not be afraid; say to the cities of Judah, "Look, your God!"

**40:10** Look, the Lord, the Lord comes with strength, and the arm with authority; look, His reward is with Him, and the work before Him.

**40:11** Like a shepherd He will shepherd His flock, and with His arm He will gather lambs, and those with young He will comfort.

**40:12** Who measured the water with the hand, and heaven with a span, and all the earth with a handful? Who weighed the mountains on a scale and the wooded valleys on a balance?

**40:13** Who has known the mind of the Lord, and who became His counselor, who will instruct Him?

**40:14** Or with whom did He take counsel, and who instructed Him? Or who showed Him justice? Or who showed Him the way of understanding?

**40:15** If all the nations were counted as a drop from a bucket and as a tilt of a balance, they will be counted as spittle;

**40:16** and Lebanon is not sufficient for burning, and all the four-footed animals are not sufficient for a whole burnt offering,

**40:17** and all the nations are as nothing and were counted for nothing.

**40:18** To whom have you likened the Lord, and to what likeness have you likened Him?

**40:19** Did a craftsman make an image, or did a goldsmith melt gold and overlay it with gold? Did he construct it as a likeness?

**40:20** For a craftsman chooses unrotting wood, and skillfully seeks how he will set up his image and that it may not be shaken.

**40:21** Will you not know? Will you not hear? Was it not declared to you from the beginning? Did you not know the foundations of the earth?

**40:22** He who holds the circle of the earth, and those who dwell in it are like locusts; He who set up heaven like a vault and stretched it out like a tent to dwell in,

**40:23** He who gives rulers to rule as nothing, and made the earth as nothing.

**40:24** For they will by no means plant, nor will they sow, nor will their root be rooted in the earth; He blew upon them and they dried up, and a storm will take them like brushwood.

**40:25** "Now therefore, to whom have you likened Me, and I will be exalted?" said the Holy One.

**40:26** Lift up your eyes to the height and see: who displayed all these things? He who brings out His array by number will call them all by name from great glory, and in the might of strength nothing escaped your notice.

**40:27** For do not say, Jacob, and why did you speak, Israel, "My way has been hidden from God, and my God has taken away justice and departed"?

**40:28** And now, have you not known? Have you not heard? An eternal God, the God who constructed the ends of the earth, will not hunger nor grow weary, nor is there any finding out of His understanding,

**40:29** giving strength to those who hunger, and sorrow to those who are not suffering pain.

**40:30** For youths will grow hungry, and young men will grow weary, and chosen ones will be without strength;

**40:31** but those who wait for God will renew strength; they will grow wings like eagles; they will run and not grow weary; they will walk and not grow hungry.

## Translator Notes

- **40:2** — The Greek text explicitly addresses "Priests" (ἱερεῖς), reads "her humiliation" rather than the Hebrew "her hard service," and omits "all" before "her sins."
- **40:10** — The Greek text renders the Hebrew double divine title with repeated κύριος ("the Lord, the Lord") and reads "the arm with authority" (μετὰ κυρίας), omitting "His" and echoing the root of κύριος.
- **40:15** — The Greek text introduces the sentence conditionally with "If" (εἰ) and renders the final clause as "they will be counted as spittle," differing from the Hebrew text.
- **40:26** — The second-person singular pronoun σε ("your notice") in the closing clause is debated as addressing either Israel (anticipating Jacob in v. 27) or God in an abrupt apostrophe, differing from the Hebrew "not one is missing."
- **40:29** — Where the Hebrew text speaks of increasing power for the weak, the Greek text reads "sorrow to those who are not suffering pain," reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew root for strength/trouble.
