## Text

**11:1** And a rod will come forth from the root of Jesse, and a blossom will come up from the root.

**11:2** And the Spirit of God will rest upon Him, a spirit of wisdom and understanding, a spirit of counsel and strength, a spirit of knowledge and godliness;

**11:3** a spirit of the fear of God will fill Him. He will not judge according to appearance, nor will He reprove according to report,

**11:4** but He will judge judgment for a humble one, and He will reprove the humble of the earth, and He will strike the earth with the word of His mouth, and with breath through His lips He will kill an ungodly one;

**11:5** and He will be girded around His waist with righteousness, and wrapped around His sides with truth.

**11:6** And a wolf will graze together with a lamb, and a leopard will rest together with a young goat, and a calf and a bull and a lion will graze together, and a small child will lead them;

**11:7** and an ox and a bear will graze together, and their young will be together, and a lion like an ox will eat straw;

**11:8** and an infant child over holes of asps, even upon a den of the offspring of asps will lay its hand.

**11:9** And they will by no means do harm, nor will they be able to destroy anyone upon My holy mountain, because the entire earth has been filled with knowing the Lord, as much water covers seas.

**11:10** And it will be on that day, the root of Jesse and the One who rises up to rule nations—in Him nations will hope; and His resting place will be honor.

**11:11** And it will be on that day the Lord will again show His hand to be zealous for the remaining remnant of the people, whatever is left by the Assyrians and from Egypt and from Babylonia and Ethiopia and from the Elamites and from the rising of the sun and from Arabia.

**11:12** And He will lift up a signal to the nations, and He will gather together the lost ones of Israel, and He will gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four wings of the earth.

**11:13** And the jealousy of Ephraim will be taken away, and the enemies of Judah will perish; Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah, and Judah will not afflict Ephraim.

**11:14** And they will fly in ships of foreigners; together they will plunder the sea and those from the rising of the sun and Idumea, and upon Moab first they will lay their hands, and the sons of Ammon will obey first.

**11:15** And the Lord will make desolate the sea of Egypt, and He will lay His hand upon the river with a violent wind, and He will strike seven ravines so that it may be crossed in sandals;

**11:16** and there will be a passageway for My people who are left in Egypt, and it will be for Israel like the day when it came out from the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Whereas the Hebrew repeats 'fear of the Lord' in both v. 2 and v. 3, the Septuagint renders the first occurrence as 'godliness' (εὐσεβείας) and the second as 'fear of God,' yielding seven distinct spiritual attributes.
- **11:3** — Greek δόξα here carries its classical sense of 'appearance' or 'opinion' (rendering Hebrew 'what His eyes see'), and 'will fill Him' translates ἐμπλήσει αὐτόν where the Hebrew has 'His delight.'
- **11:10** — The Septuagint reads 'who rises up to rule nations' and 'in Him nations will hope,' where the Hebrew has 'stands as a signal for the peoples' and 'the nations will seek Him.'
- **11:14** — 'Foreigners' translates Greek ἀλλοφύλων (the usual Septuagint term for the Philistines), and 'Idumea' is the Greek form of Edom; the Septuagint also reads 'in ships' and 'will plunder the sea' where the Hebrew has 'on the slope of the Philistines toward the west.'
- **11:16** — The Septuagint reads 'in Egypt' where the Hebrew has 'from Assyria,' just as v. 11 differs in its list by including 'Babylonia,' 'from the rising of the sun,' and 'Arabia.'
