## Text

**19:1** A vision of Egypt. Look, the Lord is sitting on a swift cloud and will come into Egypt, and the handmade things of Egypt will be shaken before His presence, and their heart will be defeated within them.

**19:2** And Egyptians will be stirred up against Egyptians, and a man will fight against his brother and a man against his neighbor, city against city and district against district.

**19:3** And the spirit of the Egyptians will be troubled within them, and I will scatter their counsel; and they will inquire of their gods and their images and those who speak from the ground and the mediums.

**19:4** And I will deliver Egypt into the hands of men, harsh masters, and harsh kings will rule over them. This is what the Lord Sabaoth says.

**19:5** And the Egyptians will drink the water beside the sea, and the river will fail and be dried up;

**19:6** and the rivers and the canals of the river will fail, and every gathering of water will be dried up, and in every marsh of reed and papyrus,

**19:7** and all the green reed-grass around the river and what is sown along the river will be dried up, wind-blasted.

**19:8** And the fishermen will groan, and all who cast hooks into the river will groan, and those who cast dragnets and the net-casters will mourn.

**19:9** And shame will seize those who work split flax and those who work fine linen,

**19:10** and those who work them will be in pain, and all who make beer will be grieved and will ache in their souls.

**19:11** And the rulers of Tanis will be fools; the wise counselors of the king—their counsel will be made foolish. How will you say to the king, "We are sons of the discerning, sons of kings from the beginning"?

**19:12** Where are your wise men now? And let them declare to you and say what the Lord Sabaoth has purposed against Egypt.

**19:13** The rulers of Tanis have failed, and the rulers of Memphis have been exalted; and they will lead Egypt astray by tribes.

**19:14** For the Lord mixed for them a spirit of error, and they led Egypt astray in all their works, as the one who is drunk and the one who vomits stagger together.

**19:15** And there will be no work for the Egyptians that will make head and tail, and beginning and end.

**19:16** Now on that day the Egyptians will be like women in fear and in trembling before the hand of the Lord Sabaoth, which He Himself will lay upon them.

**19:17** And the land of the Judeans will be a terror to the Egyptians; everyone who mentions it to them—they will be afraid because of the counsel that the Lord has purposed against it.

**19:18** On that day there will be five cities in Egypt speaking the Canaanite language and swearing by the name of the Lord; the one city will be called City of Asedek.

**19:19** On that day there will be an altar to the Lord in the land of the Egyptians, and a pillar at its border to the Lord,

**19:20** and it will be for a sign forever to the Lord in the land of Egypt; for they will cry out to the Lord because of those who afflict them, and He will send them a man who will save them; judging, he will save them.

**19:21** And the Lord will be known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians will know the Lord on that day, and they will offer sacrifices, and they will vow vows to the Lord and fulfill them.

**19:22** And the Lord will strike the Egyptians with a blow, and He will heal them with healing; and they will return to the Lord, and He will listen to them and heal them.

**19:23** On that day there will be the road of Egypt to the Assyrians, and Assyrians will enter into Egypt, and Egyptians will go to the Assyrians, and Egyptians will serve the Assyrians.

**19:24** On that day Israel will be third among the Egyptians and among the Assyrians, blessed in the land

**19:25** that the Lord Sabaoth blessed, saying, "Blessed is My people that is in Egypt and that is among the Assyrians, and the land, My inheritance, Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — The Greek reads "will drink the water beside the sea," interpreting the underlying Hebrew verb as "drink" rather than "be dried up."
- **19:11** — Greek "Tanis" is the Hellenistic Egyptian name corresponding to Hebrew "Zoan" in the Masoretic Text.
- **19:13** — Greek "Memphis" corresponds to Hebrew "Noph" in the Masoretic Text, and "have been exalted" reflects a reading of the Hebrew verb as "lifted up" rather than "deceived."
- **19:18** — Greek "asedek" transliterates Hebrew ha-tsedeq ("the righteousness"), reading "City of Asedek" where the Masoretic Text reads "City of Destruction" (or "City of the Sun").
- **19:25** — The Greek applies "My people" to those residing in Egypt and among the Assyrians and identifies "the land, My inheritance" as Israel, differing from the Masoretic Text, which calls Egypt "My people" and Assyria "the work of My hands."
