## Text

**16:1** I will send like creeping things upon the earth; is the mountain of the daughter of Zion a desolate rock?

**16:2** For you will be like a nestling taken away from a bird flying away; you will be, daughter of Moab; on this side, Arnon, further

**16:3** take counsel, make a shelter of mourning for her continually; in midday darkness they flee, they are bewildered; do not be led away.

**16:4** The fugitives of Moab will sojourn with you; they will be a shelter for you from the presence of a pursuer, because your alliance has been removed, and the ruler who tramples down has perished from the earth.

**16:5** And a throne will be set upright with mercy, and one will sit on it with truth in the tent of David, judging and seeking out justice and hastening righteousness.

**16:6** We have heard of the arrogance of Moab; he is exceedingly arrogant; I have removed the pride. Not so is your divination, not so.

**16:7** Moab will wail, for in Moabitis all will wail; you will be concerned for those who inhabit Deseth, and you will not be ashamed.

**16:8** The fields of Heshbon will mourn, the vine of Sibmah; you who swallow up the nations, you will trample down its vines as far as Jazer; you will by no means catch up, wander the wilderness; those who were sent were abandoned, for they crossed the sea.

**16:9** Therefore I will weep, like the weeping of Jazer, for the vine of Sibmah; Heshbon and Elealeh struck down your trees, because upon your harvest and upon your vintage I will trample, and all things will fall.

**16:10** And gladness and exultation will be removed from the vineyards, and in your vineyards they will by no means be glad, and they will by no means tread wine into the wine vats, for it has ceased.

**16:11** Therefore my belly will resound like a harp over Moab, and my inward parts you renewed like a wall.

**16:12** And it will result in your being ashamed, because Moab grew weary at the altars and will enter into its handmade things to pray, and they will by no means be able to rescue it.

**16:13** This is the word that the Lord spoke concerning Moab, when He spoke.

**16:14** And now I say, "In three years of the years of a hired worker, the glory of Moab will be dishonored with all the great wealth, and it will be left very small in number and not honored."

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Where the Hebrew speaks of sending a lamb from Sela to Zion, the Greek reads "I will send like creeping things upon the earth" and turns the second clause into a question about Zion being a desolate rock.
- **16:2** — The Greek addresses "Arnon" in the vocative ("on this side, Arnon, further / take counsel" across vv. 2-3), whereas the Hebrew refers to "the fords of the Arnon."
- **16:6** — The Greek reads "I have removed the pride" and "your divination," interpreting the Hebrew term for idle boasts as divination.
- **16:7** — "Deseth" is an LXX-distinct proper name corresponding to Hebrew "Kir-hareseth," and the verse replaces the Hebrew reference to raisin cakes and moaning with concern for its inhabitants and not being ashamed.
- **16:11** — Reading the Hebrew place name Kir-heres as "wall" and the root for "renew," the Greek has "my inward parts you renewed like a wall."
