## Text

**13:1** A vision that Isaiah son of Amoz saw against Babylon.

**13:2** On a level mountain lift up a signal, raise your voice to them, summon with the hand; open, you rulers.

**13:3** I command them, even I; giants are coming to fulfill My fury, rejoicing and behaving arrogantly at the same time.

**13:4** A sound of many nations on the mountains, like that of many nations, a sound of kings and nations gathered together. The Lord Sabaoth has commanded a warrior nation

**13:5** to come from a land far away, from the farthest foundation of heaven, the Lord and His warriors, to destroy the whole inhabited world.

**13:6** Wail, for the day of the Lord is near, and destruction from God will come.

**13:7** Therefore every hand will go limp, and every human soul will be afraid;

**13:8** the envoys will be troubled, and labor pains will seize them like a woman giving birth; and they will lament to one another and be astounded, and they will change their face like a flame.

**13:9** For look, the day of the Lord is coming, incurable, of fury and wrath, to make the inhabited world desolate and to destroy the sinners from it.

**13:10** For the stars of heaven and Orion and all the array of heaven will not give light, and it will grow dark as the sun rises, and the moon will not give its light.

**13:11** And I will command evils upon the whole inhabited world, and their sins upon the ungodly; and I will destroy the arrogance of the lawless, and the arrogance of the proud I will humble.

**13:12** And those who are left will be more precious than gold untouched by fire, and a human being will be more precious than the stone that is in Souphir.

**13:13** For heaven will become furious, and the earth will be shaken from its foundations because of the fury of the wrath of the Lord Sabaoth, in the day when His fury comes.

**13:14** And those who are left will be like a fleeing gazelle and like a straying sheep, and there will be no one gathering them, so that each person turns back to his own people, and each person will rush to his own country.

**13:15** For whoever is captured will be defeated, and all who have been gathered together will fall by the sword.

**13:16** And they will dash their children down before them, and they will plunder their houses, and they will take their wives.

**13:17** Look, I am stirring up the Medes against you, who do not value silver nor have need of gold.

**13:18** They will shatter the arrows of young men, and they will certainly not have mercy on your children, nor will their eyes spare your children.

**13:19** And Babylon, which is called glorious by the king of the Chaldeans, will be just as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah;

**13:20** it will not be inhabited forever, nor will anyone by any means enter it through many generations, nor will Arabs by any means pass through it, nor will shepherds by any means rest in it.

**13:21** And wild beasts will rest there, and the houses will be filled with noise; and sirens will rest there, and demons will dance there,

**13:22** and onocentaurs will dwell there, and hedgehogs will make nests in their houses. It is coming quickly and will not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The LXX translates "bare mountain" as "level mountain" (ὄρους πεδινοῦ) and renders the final clause as an imperative, "open, you rulers," without an object such as "the gates."
- **13:4** — Greek σαβαώθ (Sabaoth) transliterates the Hebrew title meaning "of hosts/armies," preserved untranslated throughout LXX Isaiah.
- **13:8** — The LXX reads οἱ πρέσβεις ("the envoys"), interpreting the Hebrew consonants ṣîrîm as "messengers" rather than "pangs," and uses the rare verb συμφοράζω ("lament together").
- **13:12** — Souphir (Σουφείρ) is the distinctive Septuagint form corresponding to Ophir in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:21** — In vv. 21-22, the LXX employs Greek mythological and creature terms ("sirens," "demons," "onocentaurs," and "hedgehogs") to render the desert animals of the Hebrew text.
