## Text

**58:1** Cry out with strength and do not spare; like a trumpet, lift up your voice, and announce to My people their sinful deeds, and to the house of Jacob their lawless deeds.

**58:2** Day after day they seek Me and desire to know My ways, like a people that has practiced righteousness and has not forsaken the justice of its God; now they ask Me for a righteous judgment, and they desire to draw near to God,

**58:3** saying, "Why did we fast, and You did not see? We humbled our souls, and You did not know?" For in the days of your fasts you find your wishes, and you goad all those subject to you.

**58:4** If you fast for disputes and fights and strike a humble person with fists, why do you fast for Me as today, for your voice to be heard in an outcry?

**58:5** I did not choose this fast, nor a day for a person to humble his soul; not even if you bend your neck like a ring, and spread sackcloth and ashes under yourself, not even so will you call it an acceptable fast.

**58:6** I did not choose such a fast, says the Lord; rather, loose every bond of injustice, untie the knots of coercive transactions, send away those who are crushed in release, and tear apart every unjust contract.

**58:7** Break your bread for the hungry, and bring the homeless poor into your house; if you see someone who is naked, clothe him, and you shall not disregard the relatives of your seed.

**58:8** Then your light will break forth early, and your healings will quickly rise up, and your righteousness will go ahead before you, and the glory of God will enfold you.

**58:9** Then you will cry out, and God will listen to you; while you are still speaking, He will say, "Look, I am here." If you remove from yourself the bond and the pointing of the hand and a word of grumbling,

**58:10** and give the hungry the bread from your soul, and satisfy a humbled soul, then your light will rise in the darkness, and your darkness will be like midday,

**58:11** and your God will be with you continually; and you will be satisfied just as your soul desires, and your bones will be fattened, and they will be like a saturated garden and like a spring whose water has not failed.

**58:12** And your age-old desolate places will be built, and your foundations will be eternal for generations of generations; and you will be called "Builder of Fences," and you will cause your paths in the midst to cease.

**58:13** If you turn your foot away from the Sabbath so as not to do your wishes on the holy day, then you will call the Sabbath delightful, holy to God; you will not lift your foot for work, nor speak a word in anger from your mouth,

**58:14** and you will trust in the Lord, and He will bring you up upon the good things of the land, and feed you the inheritance of Jacob your father; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

## Translator Notes

- **58:4** — LXX reads "strike a humble person with fists" (tapeinon) and "in an outcry" (en krauge), where the Hebrew MT has "with a wicked fist" and "on high."
- **58:6** — LXX uses ouchi ... alla ("I did not choose such a fast ... rather, loose...") as a negative contrast with v. 5, whereas MT frames v. 6 as an affirmative rhetorical question ("Is not this the fast that I choose...?").
- **58:9** — Greek cheirotonia (normally "stretching out the hand to vote/appoint") here renders Hebrew "pointing the finger" as an accusatory gesture, and "a word of grumbling" (rhema gongysmou) corresponds to MT "speaking wickedness."
- **58:12** — LXX "you will cause your paths in the midst to cease" renders the Hebrew infinitive lasebet ("to dwell") as if derived from sbt ("to cease").
- **58:13** — Greek shifts after "If you turn" (subjunctive) to future indicatives ("will call ... will not lift ... nor speak") and specifies "for work" and "in anger," which are absent from MT.
