## Text

**51:1** Listen to Me, you who pursue what is right and seek the Lord: look at the solid rock that you quarried, and at the hole of the pit that you dug.

**51:2** Look at Abraham your father, and at Sarah who was in labor with you; for he was one, and I called him, and I blessed him and loved him.

**51:3** And now I will comfort you, Zion, and I have comforted all her desolate places, and I will make her desolate places like a paradise, and the regions toward the west like the paradise of the Lord; gladness and exultation they will find in her, thanksgiving and a voice of praise.

**51:4** Listen to Me, listen to Me, My people, and you kings, give ear to Me; for a law will go out from Me, and My justice for a light of nations.

**51:5** My righteousness draws near quickly, and My salvation also will go out like a light, and in My arm nations will hope; islands will wait for Me, and in My arm they will hope.

**51:6** Lift up your eyes to heaven, and look at the earth below; for heaven was made firm like smoke, and the earth will grow old like a garment, and the inhabitants will die just like these things, but My salvation will be forever, and My righteousness will never fail.

**51:7** Listen to Me, you who know justice, a people in whose heart is My law: do not fear the reproach of men, and do not be defeated by their contempt.

**51:8** For like a garment it will be eaten by time, and like wool it will be eaten by a moth, but My righteousness will be forever, and My salvation for generations of generations.

**51:9** Awake, awake, Jerusalem, and put on the strength of your arm; awake as in the beginning of a day, as a generation of eternity.

**51:10** Are you not the one who makes the sea desolate, water of the abyss, an abundance? The one who made the depths of the sea a way of crossing for those being rescued

**51:11** and those who have been redeemed? For by the Lord they will be brought back, and they will come to Zion with gladness and everlasting exultation; for upon their head is praise, and gladness will overtake them; pain and grief and sighing have fled away.

**51:12** I am, I am the One who comforts you; know who you are, that you were afraid of mortal man and of a son of man, who withered like grass.

**51:13** And you forgot God who made you, who made heaven and laid the foundation of the earth; and you feared continually all the days the face of the wrath of the one who afflicts you, for how he plotted to take you away; and now where is the wrath of the one who afflicts you?

**51:14** For when you are being saved, it will not stand nor delay;

**51:15** for I am your God, who stirs up the sea and makes its waves roar; the Lord Sabaoth is My name.

**51:16** I will put My words into your mouth, and under the shadow of My hand I will shelter you, by which I established heaven and laid the foundation of the earth; and He will say to Zion, "You are My people."

**51:17** Awake, awake, rise up, Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the Lord the cup of His wrath; for the cup of downfall, the goblet of wrath, you drank up and emptied out,

**51:18** and there was no one comforting you from among all your children whom you bore, and there was no one taking hold of your hand, not even from among all your sons whom you raised up.

**51:19** These two things are opposed to you: who will grieve with you? Ruin and destruction, famine and sword; who will comfort you?

**51:20** Your sons, who are at a loss, who sleep at the head of every exit like a half-boiled beet, who are full of the wrath of the Lord, exhausted by the Lord God.

**51:21** Therefore listen, you who have been humbled and who are drunk, not from wine.

**51:22** Thus says the Lord God, who judges His people: "Look, I have taken out of your hand the cup of downfall, the goblet of My wrath, and you will not again drink it;

**51:23** and I will give it into the hands of those who wronged you, those who humbled you, who said to your soul, 'Bow down, so that we may pass over,' and you made your middle even with the ground outside for those passing by."

## Translator Notes

- **51:3** — The Greek renders the Hebrew region name ("her Arabah") as "the regions toward the west" (τὰ πρὸς δυσμάς) and uses παράδεισος ("paradise") for both Hebrew "Eden" and "garden."
- **51:9** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which addresses the arm of the Lord, the Greek explicitly addresses Jerusalem (Ἰερουσαλήμ), which also governs the feminine participles in 51:10.
- **51:15** — The title Κύριος σαβαώθ ("the Lord Sabaoth") leaves the Hebrew title ṣəbāʾôt ("hosts" or "armies") transliterated in Greek; the same title recurs in 54:5.
- **51:16** — Because Σειών ("Zion") is indeclinable, it functions here as a bare dative "to Zion" (as in 52:7, λέγων Σειών), though grammatically it could also be construed as the subject ("and Zion will say").
- **51:20** — The Greek uniquely renders the Hebrew simile ("like an antelope in a net") as ὡς σευτλίον ἡμίεφθον ("like a half-boiled beet").
