## Text

**47:1** Come down, sit on the ground, virgin daughter of Babylon; sit on the ground, daughter of the Chaldeans, for you will no longer continue to be called tender and delicate.

**47:2** Take a millstone, grind flour; uncover your veil, uncover your gray hairs, bare your legs, cross over rivers;

**47:3** your shame will be uncovered, your reproaches will appear; I will take justice from you; no longer will I yield to human beings.

**47:4** The One who rescues you is the Lord Sabaoth; His name is the Holy One of Israel.

**47:5** Sit stunned, enter into the darkness, daughter of the Chaldeans; no longer will you be called the strength of a kingdom.

**47:6** I was provoked against My people; you defiled My inheritance; I gave them into your hand, yet you gave them no mercy; you made the yoke of the aged exceedingly heavy,

**47:7** and you said, "Forever I will be a ruler." You did not consider these things in your heart, nor did you remember the last things.

**47:8** Now then, hear these things, you delicate one, who sits, who is confident, who says in her heart, "I am, and there is no other; I will not sit as a widow, nor will I know bereavement."

**47:9** But now it will come suddenly upon you in your sorcery, in the strength of your enchantments, exceedingly,

**47:10** in the hope of your wickedness. For you said, "I am, and there is no other." Know: the understanding of these things and your prostitution will be a shame to you; and you said in your heart, "I am, and there is no other."

**47:11** And destruction will come upon you, and you will certainly not know it; a pit, and you will fall into it; and misery will come upon you, and you will certainly not be able to become clean; and destruction will come upon you suddenly, and you will not know.

**47:12** Stand now in your enchantments and your abundant sorcery, which you were learning from your youth, if you will be able to be helped.

**47:13** You have labored in your counsels; let the astrologers of heaven stand and save you; let those who watch the stars declare to you what is about to come upon you.

**47:14** Look, all of them will be burned up like brushwood on a fire, and they will certainly not rescue their life from the flame; for you have coals of fire—sit upon them!

**47:15** These will be a help to you; you have labored in trade from youth; each person went astray on his own; but for you there will be no salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **47:2** — "Your gray hairs" (τὰς πολιάς) is a distinct Septuagint reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the skirt/train."
- **47:3** — The verb παραδῶ ("yield" or "hand over") appears without an expressed direct object in the Greek (differing from the Hebrew "I will not spare any man"), and 47:4 shifts to singular "rescues you" (σε) against the Hebrew plural "our Redeemer."
- **47:9** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("these two things... in one day, loss of children and widowhood"), the Greek omits the explicit subject and leaves ἥξει ("it will come") with an implied subject carried over from 47:8.
- **47:14** — The Greek reverses the sense of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("there is no coal for warming, no fire to sit before"), reading instead an ironic command: "for you have coals of fire—sit upon them!"
