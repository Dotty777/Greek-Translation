## Text

**32:1** For look, a righteous king will reign, and rulers will rule with judgment.

**32:2** And the man will be hiding his words, and he will be hidden as from rushing water; and he will appear in Zion like a glorious rushing river in a thirsty land.

**32:3** And no longer will they be trusting in human beings, but they will give their ears to hear;

**32:4** and the heart of the weak will draw near to hear, and the stammering tongues will quickly learn to speak peace;

**32:5** and no longer will they tell the fool to rule, and no longer will your servants say, "Be silent!"

**32:6** For the fool will speak foolish things, and his heart will think futile things, to accomplish lawless deeds and to speak delusion against the Lord, to scatter hungry souls, and he will make thirsty souls empty.

**32:7** For the plan of the wicked will plan lawless things, to destroy the humble with unjust words and to disperse the words of the humble in judgment.

**32:8** But the godly planned prudent things, and this plan will remain.

**32:9** Wealthy women, arise and hear my voice; daughters in hope, listen to my words.

**32:10** Make remembrance for days of a year in pain with hope; the vintage is consumed; it has ceased; no longer will it come.

**32:11** Be dismayed, be grieved, you confident women; strip yourselves, become naked, gird your waists

**32:12** and beat upon your breasts, over a field of desire and a vine of produce.

**32:13** The land of my people—thorn and grass will come up, and from every house gladness will be taken away;

**32:14** wealthy city, abandoned houses will leave behind the wealth of a city, houses of desire; and the villages will become caves forever, a gladness of wild donkeys, pastures of shepherds,

**32:15** until a spirit comes upon you from on high. And Chermel will be a wilderness, and Chermel will be reckoned as a forest.

**32:16** And justice will rest in the wilderness, and righteousness will dwell in Carmel;

**32:17** and the works of righteousness will be peace, and righteousness will hold fast to rest, and they will be confident forever;

**32:18** and His people will dwell in a city of peace and will reside in confidence, and they will rest with wealth.

**32:19** And if the hail comes down, it will not come upon you. And those who reside in the forests will be confident, as in the plain.

**32:20** Blessed are those who sow beside every water, where ox and donkey tread.

## Translator Notes

- **32:2** — The Septuagint diverges markedly from the Hebrew text here, speaking of the man hiding his words and appearing in Zion like a glorious rushing river rather than leaders serving as a shelter from wind and storm.
- **32:14** — Where the Hebrew text mentions "hill and watchtower," the Septuagint reads "the villages" (hai kōmai), and portrays abandoned houses leaving behind a city's wealth.
- **32:15** — Greek transliterates Hebrew karmel as "Chermel" in 32:15 (making Chermel the subject that becomes a wilderness), while rendering the same term as "Carmel" in 32:16.
- **32:19** — Unlike the Hebrew forecast of destructive hail, the Septuagint promises safety ("if the hail comes down, it will not come upon you") and confidence for those residing in the forests.
