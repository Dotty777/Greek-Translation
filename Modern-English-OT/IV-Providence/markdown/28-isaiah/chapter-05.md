## Text

**5:1** I will sing now to the beloved a song of my beloved for my vineyard. A vineyard belonged to the beloved on a horn in a fertile place.

**5:2** And I put a fence around it and enclosed it with stakes, and I planted a Sorek vine, and I built a tower in the middle of it, and I dug a wine-vat in it; and I waited for it to produce grapes, and it produced thorns.

**5:3** And now, you who dwell in Jerusalem and man of Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard.

**5:4** What more shall I do for My vineyard that I have not done for it? Because I waited for it to produce grapes, yet it produced thorns.

**5:5** Now then I will declare to you what I will do to My vineyard: I will take away its fence and it will be for plunder, and I will tear down its wall and it will be for trampling.

**5:6** And I will abandon My vineyard, and it will by no means be pruned nor dug, and thorns will come up upon it as upon barren ground; and I will command the clouds not to rain down rain upon it.

**5:7** For the vineyard of the Lord Sabaoth is the house of Israel, and the man of Judah is a beloved new planting; I waited for it to produce justice, yet it produced lawlessness, and not righteousness, but an outcry.

**5:8** Woe to those who join house to house and bring field near to field, so that they may take away something from their neighbor! Surely you will not dwell alone upon the land?

**5:9** For these things were heard in the ears of the Lord Sabaoth: for if houses become many, they will become a desolation; great and fine, yet there will be no one dwelling in them.

**5:10** For where ten pairs of oxen work, it will produce one jar, and he who sows six artabas will produce three measures.

**5:11** Woe to those who rise in the morning and pursue strong drink, who stay into the evening; for wine will burn them up.

**5:12** For with lyre and harp and tambourines and flutes they drink the wine, yet they do not look at the works of the Lord, and they do not consider the works of His hands.

**5:13** Therefore My people became captive because they did not know the Lord, and a multitude of the dead came to be because of famine and thirst for water.

**5:14** And Hades enlarged its soul and opened wide its mouth without ceasing, and down will go the glorious ones and the great ones and the rich ones and her pestilent ones.

**5:15** And a person will be humbled, and a man will be dishonored, and the lofty eyes will be humbled;

**5:16** and the Lord Sabaoth will be exalted in judgment, and the holy God will be glorified in righteousness;

**5:17** and those who were plundered will graze like bulls, and lambs will eat the desolate places of those who have been taken away.

**5:18** Woe to those who drag sins along as with a long rope, and lawless deeds as with the yoke strap of a heifer,

**5:19** who say, "Quickly let what He will do draw near, so that we may see, and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come, so that we may know."

**5:20** Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who make darkness light and light darkness, who make bitter sweet and sweet bitter.

**5:21** Woe to those who are intelligent in themselves and knowledgeable in their own sight.

**5:22** Woe to your strong men who drink wine, and the mighty men who mix strong drink,

**5:23** who justify the ungodly for the sake of bribes, and take away the right of the righteous one.

**5:24** Therefore just as stubble will be burned by a coal of fire, and will be burned up by an unleashed flame, their root will be like chaff, and their blossom will go up like dust; for they did not desire the law of the Lord Sabaoth, but provoked the oracle of the Holy One of Israel.

**5:25** And the Lord Sabaoth became furious with anger against His people, and He laid His hand upon them and struck them; and the mountains were provoked, and their corpses became like dung in the middle of a road. And in all these things His fury has not turned away, but His hand is still high.

**5:26** Therefore He will lift up a signal among the nations far away and will whistle to them from the end of the earth, and look, swiftly, lightly they come.

**5:27** They will not hunger nor grow weary, nor slumber nor sleep, nor untie their belts from their waist, nor will the straps of their sandals be broken;

**5:28** whose arrows are sharp and their bows are strung. The feet of their horses are like solid rock; the wheels of their chariots have been reckoned like a whirlwind.

**5:29** They rage like lions, they have taken their stand like a lion's cubs; and he will seize, and roar like a wild beast, and cast out, and there will be no one to rescue them;

**5:30** and he will roar because of them in that day like the sound of a surging sea; and they will look to the land, and look, harsh darkness in their distress.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The LXX shifts to first-person 'for my vineyard' (τῷ ἀμπελῶνί μου) in 5:1 and continues with first-person singular verbs in 5:2 ('I put... I planted...'), whereas the Masoretic Text uses the third person in 5:1-2. In 5:2, 'Sorek' (σωρήκ) is a Greek transliteration of a choice red-grape variety.
- **5:10** — For the Masoretic Text's 'homer' and 'ephah' (a ten-to-one yield ratio), the LXX specifies 'six artabas' producing 'three measures,' using Graeco-Egyptian dry measures.
- **5:17** — The LXX reads 'those who were plundered will graze like bulls' and 'of those who have been taken away' (ἀπειλημμένων, from ἀπολαμβάνω), differing markedly from the Masoretic Text ('lambs will graze as in their pasture... waste places of the rich').
- **5:19** — The Greek text spells εἰδῶμεν ('so that we may see'), an itacistic spelling of ἴδωμεν (compare Σειών for Σιών) common in Codex Vaticanus rather than a repetition of 'know' with γνῶμεν.
- **5:29** — The verb ὀργιῶσιν ('they rage,' from ὀργιάω) shifts mid-verse to singular future verbs ('he will seize... roar... cast out'), continuing into 5:30 and portraying the attacking foe collectively as a single predator.
