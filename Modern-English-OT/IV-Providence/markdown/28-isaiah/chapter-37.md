## Text

**37:1** And it happened, when King Hezekiah heard, that he tore his garments and wrapped himself in sackcloth, and went up into the house of the Lord.

**37:2** And he sent Eliakim the steward and Shebna the scribe and the elders of the priests, wrapped in sackcloth, to Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet;

**37:3** and they said to him, "Thus says Hezekiah: 'This day is a day of distress and reproach and rebuke and wrath; for labor pain has come to the woman giving birth, yet she has no strength to give birth.

**37:4** May the Lord your God listen to the words of Rabshakeh, which the king of the Assyrians sent to reproach the living God and to reproach with words that the Lord your God has heard; and pray to your Lord concerning these who are left.'"

**37:5** And the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah,

**37:6** and Isaiah said to them, "Thus you shall say to your master, 'Thus says the Lord: Do not be afraid of the words that you heard, with which the envoys of the king of the Assyrians reproached Me.

**37:7** Look, I am putting a spirit in him, and when he hears a report he will turn back to his own country, and he will fall by the sword in his own land.'"

**37:8** And Rabshakeh turned back and found the king of the Assyrians besieging Libnah, and he heard that he had departed from Lachish.

**37:9** And Tirhakah, king of the Ethiopians, went out to besiege him; and when he heard, he turned back and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,

**37:10** "Thus you shall say to Hezekiah, king of Judea, 'Do not let your God, in whom you trust, deceive you, saying, "Jerusalem will by no means be handed over into the hand of the king of the Assyrians."

**37:11** Have you not heard what the kings of the Assyrians did to all the earth, how they destroyed it? And will you be delivered?

**37:12** Did the gods of the nations whom my fathers destroyed deliver them—both Gozan and Haran and Rapheth, which are in the country of Theemath?

**37:13** Where are the kings of Hamath, and where are those of Arpad? And where are those of the city of Epharvaim, Anag, Ugava?'"

**37:14** And Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers and read it, and he went up into the house of the Lord and opened it before the Lord.

**37:15** And Hezekiah prayed to the Lord, saying,

**37:16** "Lord Sabaoth, the God of Israel, who sits upon the cherubim, You alone are God of every kingdom of the world; You made heaven and earth.

**37:17** Incline Your ear, Lord; listen, Lord! Open Your eyes, Lord; look, Lord, and see the words of Sennacherib that he sent to reproach the living God.

**37:18** For truly the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the whole world and their country,

**37:19** and threw their idols into the fire—for they were not gods, but works of human hands, wood and stones—and cast them away.

**37:20** But now, Lord our God, save us from his hand, so that every kingdom of the earth may know that You alone are God."

**37:21** And Isaiah the son of Amoz was sent to Hezekiah and said to him, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'I have heard what you prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib, king of the Assyrians.'"

**37:22** This is the word that God spoke concerning him: "The virgin daughter of Zion has despised you and mocked you; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at you.

**37:23** Whom have you reproached and provoked? Or against whom have you raised your voice? And did you not lift up your eyes on high toward the Holy One of Israel?

**37:24** Because through messengers you reproached the Lord; for you said, 'With the multitude of chariots I went up to the height of mountains and to the farthest parts of Lebanon, and I cut down the height of its cedar and the beauty of the cypress, and I entered into the height of part of the forest,

**37:25** and I set a bridge, and I laid waste waters and every gathering of water.'

**37:26** Did I not hear long ago what I did? From ancient days I ordained it, and now I have shown it—to utterly lay waste nations in strongholds and those dwelling in fortified cities;

**37:27** I slackened the hands, and they dried up, and became like dry grass on rooftops and like couch grass.

**37:28** But now I know your resting and your going out and your coming in;

**37:29** and your rage with which you raged and your bitterness has come up to Me, and I will put a muzzle in your nose and a bridle on your lips, and I will turn you back by the way on which you came.

**37:30** And this will be the sign for you: eat this year what you have sown, and in the second year what is left, and in the third year sow and reap, and plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**37:31** And those who are left will be in Judea; they will put forth root downward and produce seed upward.

**37:32** For out of Jerusalem will be those who are left, and those who are saved out of Mount Zion; the zeal of the Lord Sabaoth will do these things."

**37:33** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians: "He will by no means enter this city, nor shoot an arrow against it, nor bring a shield against it, nor surround it with a siege rampart;

**37:34** but by the way on which he came, by it he will turn back, and into this city he will by no means enter," thus says the Lord.

**37:35** "I will shield this city to save it, for My own sake and for the sake of David My servant."

**37:36** And the angel of the Lord went out and killed one hundred eighty-five thousand from the camp of the Assyrians; and when they rose in the morning, they found all the bodies dead.

**37:37** And Sennacherib, king of the Assyrians, turned back and departed, and lived in Nineveh.

**37:38** And while he was worshiping his ancestral god in the house of Nisroch, Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons struck him down with swords, and they escaped safely to Armenia; and Esarhaddon his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **37:4** — LXX directs Isaiah to 'pray to your Lord' (anarthrous 'kyrion sou') concerning those who are left, referring to the Lord God named earlier in the verse.
- **37:12** — LXX reads 'Rapheth' and 'in the country of Theemath' where the Hebrew MT has 'Rezeph, and the people of Eden who were in Telassar'; similarly, 37:13 reads 'Epharvaim, Anag, Ugava' for Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah.
- **37:16** — 'Sabaoth' is the Greek transliteration of the Hebrew title meaning 'of hosts' (armies), left untranslated in LXX Isaiah (also in 37:32).
- **37:25** — LXX reads 'I set a bridge, and I laid waste waters and every gathering of water' where MT has 'I dug wells and drank waters, and dried up all the streams of Egypt'; in 37:26, the Greek reads first-person 'ekousa' ('Did I not hear') rather than second-person ('Have you not heard').
- **37:38** — LXX renders 'his god' with 'ton patrarchon autou' ('his ancestral god' or 'patron deity') and reads 'Armenia' where the Hebrew MT has 'the land of Ararat.'
