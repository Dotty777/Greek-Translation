## Text

**55:1** You who are thirsty, go to the water, and all you who have no silver, go, buy, and eat wine and fat without silver and price.

**55:2** Why do you spend silver, and your labor not for satisfaction? And you will eat good things, and your soul will delight in good things.

**55:3** Pay attention with your ears and follow My ways; listen to Me, and your soul will live in good things, and I will make an everlasting covenant with you, the faithful holy things of David.

**55:4** Look, I have given him as a testimony among nations, a ruler and commander for nations.

**55:5** Nations that do not know you will call upon you, and peoples who are not acquainted with you will flee to you for refuge, because of the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, for He has glorified you.

**55:6** Seek the Lord, and upon finding Him, call upon Him; and whenever He draws near to you,

**55:7** let the ungodly one forsake his ways, and a lawless man his plans, and let him turn back to the Lord, and he will be shown mercy, for He will greatly forgive your sins.

**55:8** "For My plans are not like your plans, nor like your ways are My ways," says the Lord,

**55:9** "but as far as heaven is from the earth, so far is My way from your ways, and your thoughts from My thought.

**55:10** For as the rain or snow comes down from heaven, and does not return until it drenches the earth, and brings forth and sprouts, and gives seed to the sower and bread for food,

**55:11** so will My word be that goes out from My mouth: it will not return until all that I willed is accomplished, and I will prosper your ways and My commands."

**55:12** For in gladness you will go out, and in joy you will be taught; for the mountains and the hills will leap up as they welcome you in joy, and all the trees of the field will clap with their branches,

**55:13** and instead of the thornbush a cypress will come up, and instead of the fleabane a myrtle will come up; and the Lord will be for a name and for an everlasting sign, and will not fail.

## Translator Notes

- **55:1** — The Greek has genitive οἴνου ("of wine") alongside accusative στέαρ ("fat," reading the Hebrew consonants ḥlb as "fat" rather than "milk").
- **55:3** — The phrase τὰ ὅσια Δαυὶδ τὰ πιστά (literally "the faithful holy things of David") translates Hebrew "faithful mercies of David" and is quoted in this Septuagint form in Acts 13:34.
- **55:11** — The Septuagint reads "and I will prosper your ways and My commands," differing from the Masoretic Text ("and it will succeed in the thing for which I sent it").
- **55:13** — Greek στοιβή (thorny burnet/thornbush) and κόνυζα (fleabane) identify specific Mediterranean plants, and "the Lord" (Κύριος) is the grammatical subject of "will be" rather than "for the Lord" as in Hebrew.
