## Text

**50:1** Thus says the Lord: "What is the certificate of your mother's divorce, with which I sent her away? Or to what debtor did I sell you? Look, because of your sins you were sold, and because of your lawless deeds I sent your mother away.

**50:2** Why is it that I came, and there was no one? I called, and there was no one who heeded? Is My hand not strong enough to redeem? Or am I not strong enough to rescue? Look, by My rebuke I will lay waste the sea, and I will make rivers desolate, and their fish will dry up because there is no water, and they will die in thirst.

**50:3** I will clothe heaven with darkness, and I will make its covering like sackcloth."

**50:4** The Lord gives me a tongue of instruction to know when one ought to speak a word; He set it for me in the morning, He added to me an ear to hear,

**50:5** and the instruction of the Lord, the Lord, opens my ears, and I do not disobey, nor do I speak against it.

**50:6** I gave my back to scourges, and my cheeks to slaps, and my face I did not turn away from the shame of spitting,

**50:7** and the Lord, the Lord, became my helper; therefore I was not disgraced, but I set my face like a solid rock, and I knew that I will never be put to shame.

**50:8** For the One who justified me draws near; who is the one contending with me? Let him stand against me at the same time; and who is the one contending with me? Let him draw near to me.

**50:9** Look, the Lord will help me; who will harm me? Look, all of you will wear out like a garment, and a moth will devour you.

**50:10** Who among you is the one who fears the Lord? Let him heed the voice of His servant; those who walk in darkness, and there is no light for them—trust in the name of the Lord and rely on God.

**50:11** Look, all of you light a fire and strengthen a flame; walk by the light of your fire and by the flame that you have kindled. Because of Me these things happened to you; in sorrow you will lie down.

## Translator Notes

- **50:1** — Greek hypochreō literally means "debtor" (here translating the Hebrew word for "creditor"), and Poion asks "What is" rather than Hebrew "Where is."
- **50:4** — Instead of the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "how to sustain the weary with a word," the Septuagint reads "to know when one ought to speak a word," and pairs "He set" (ethēken) with "He added" (prosethēken) where Hebrew repeats "He awakens."
- **50:5** — The Septuagint specifies "the instruction (paidia) of the Lord, the Lord, opens my ears," explicitly linking this verse with the "tongue of instruction" in v. 4.
- **50:11** — Following the Septuagint, v. 11 reads "strengthen a flame" (for Hebrew "gird yourselves with firebrands"), casts "these things happened to you" in the past tense, and ends with "in sorrow" (lypē) rather than "in torment."
