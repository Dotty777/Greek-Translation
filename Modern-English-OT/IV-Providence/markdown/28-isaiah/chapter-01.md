## Text

**1:1** A vision that Isaiah son of Amoz saw, which he saw against Judea and against Jerusalem, in the reign of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judea.

**1:2** Hear, heaven, and listen closely, earth, for the Lord has spoken: "I fathered sons and exalted them, but they rejected Me.

**1:3** An ox knows its owner, and a donkey its master's feeding trough; but Israel does not know Me, and the people do not understand Me."

**1:4** Woe, sinful nation, people full of sins, evil offspring, lawless sons! You have abandoned the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger.

**1:5** Why should you still be struck while adding lawlessness? Every head is in pain, and every heart is in grief.

**1:6** From feet to head there is neither wound nor bruise nor festering blow; there is no salve to apply, nor oil, nor bandages.

**1:7** Your land is desolate, your cities are burned with fire; your country—aliens are devouring it before you, and it has been made desolate, overthrown by alien peoples.

**1:8** The daughter of Zion will be abandoned like a tent in a vineyard, and like a watchman's hut in a cucumber field, like a besieged city.

**1:9** And if the Lord Sabaoth had not left us offspring, we would have become like Sodom, and we would have been made like Gomorrah.

**1:10** Hear the word of the Lord, rulers of Sodom; pay attention to the law of God, people of Gomorrah.

**1:11** "What to Me is the multitude of your sacrifices?" says the Lord. "I am full of whole burnt offerings of rams, and I do not want the fat of lambs and the blood of bulls and goats,

**1:12** not even if you come to appear before Me. For who required these things from your hands? To trample My court

**1:13** you will not continue; if you bring fine flour, it is worthless; incense—it is an abomination to Me; your new moons and the Sabbaths and a great day I do not endure; fasting and idleness

**1:14** and your new moons and your festivals My soul hates; you have become a surfeit to Me; I will no longer forgive your sins.

**1:15** When you stretch out your hands, I will turn My eyes away from you; and if you multiply prayer, I will not listen to you, for your hands are full of blood.

**1:16** Wash yourselves, become clean, remove the evil deeds from your souls before My eyes; cease from your evil deeds,

**1:17** learn to do good, seek justice, rescue the one being wronged, render justice for the orphan, and vindicate the widow."

**1:18** "And come, let us settle our case," says the Lord. "Even if your sins are like crimson, I will make them white like snow; and if they are like scarlet, I will make them white like wool.

**1:19** And if you are willing and listen to Me, you will eat the good things of the land;

**1:20** but if you are not willing and do not listen to Me, a sword will devour you," for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

**1:21** How has the faithful city Zion, full of justice, become a prostitute? In it righteousness lodged, but now murderers!

**1:22** Your silver is debased; your tavern-keepers mix the wine with water.

**1:23** Your rulers are disobedient, companions of thieves, loving gifts, pursuing repayment, not rendering justice for orphans, and not paying attention to the case of widows.

**1:24** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Sovereign Sabaoth: "Woe to the mighty ones of Israel! For My wrath will not cease against the adversaries, and I will execute judgment on My enemies.

**1:25** And I will bring My hand upon you and refine you to purity; the disobedient I will destroy, and I will remove all lawless ones from you;

**1:26** and I will establish your judges as before, and your counselors as from the beginning; and after these things you will be called, 'City of Righteousness, Faithful Metropolis Zion.'"

**1:27** For with judgment her captivity will be saved, and with mercy;

**1:28** and the lawless and the sinners will be crushed together, and those who abandon the Lord will be consumed;

**1:29** for they will be put to shame because of their idols, which they themselves wanted, and they were put to shame over the gardens that they desired.

**1:30** For they will be like a terebinth that has shed its leaves, and like a garden having no water;

**1:31** and their strength will be like a stalk of tow, and their works like sparks, and the lawless and the sinners will be burned up together, and there will be no one to quench them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Septuagint reads Judea (corresponding to Judah in the Hebrew text), repeats the clause "which he saw," and uses the preposition "against" (kata) rather than "concerning."
- **1:6** — Unlike the Hebrew text ("there is no soundness in it; wounds and bruises..."), the Greek negates all three injury terms ("neither wound nor bruise nor festering blow"), emphasizing either the lack of ordinary treatable injuries or total devastation beneath the surface.
- **1:9** — "Sabaoth" preserves the Septuagint transliteration of the Hebrew title meaning "hosts" or "armies," and "offspring" renders Greek sperma (literally "seed"), where the Hebrew text reads "survivor."
- **1:18** — "I will make them white" renders the Septuagint first-person singular active verb (leukano) in both clauses, whereas the Hebrew text uses third-person plural ("they will be white" / "will be like wool").
- **1:27** — "Her captivity will be saved, and with mercy" follows the Septuagint (he aichmalosia autes and eleemosynes), differing from the Hebrew text ("Zion will be redeemed with justice, and her repentant ones with righteousness").
