## Text

**54:1** Rejoice, barren one, you who do not bear; break forth and shout, you who are not in labor, for the children of the desolate one are many, more than of the one who has the husband, for the Lord said.

**54:2** Widen the place of your tent and of your curtains; fasten it, do not spare; lengthen your cords, and strengthen your stakes,

**54:3** spread out further to the right and to the left; and your seed will inherit nations, and you will resettle desolated cities.

**54:4** Do not fear because you were put to shame, nor be ashamed because you were reproached; for you will forget everlasting shame, and the reproach of your widowhood you will never remember.

**54:5** For the Lord is the One who makes you, the Lord Sabaoth is His name; and the One who rescued you will Himself be called the God of Israel by all the earth.

**54:6** Not like a forsaken and fainthearted woman has the Lord called you, nor like a woman hated from youth, your God said.

**54:7** For a little time I forsook you, and with great mercy I will have mercy on you;

**54:8** in a little wrath I turned My face away from you, and with everlasting mercy I will have mercy on you, said the Lord who rescued you.

**54:9** From the water in the time of Noah this is to Me, just as I swore to him at that time, to the earth, not to be angry against you anymore, nor that in threatening you the mountains will be removed,

**54:10** nor will your hills be moved; so neither will the mercy from Me to you fail, nor will the covenant of your peace ever be removed; for he said, "Merciful to you, Lord."

**54:11** Humbled and unsettled, you were not comforted; look, I am preparing for you carbuncle as your stone, and sapphire as your foundations,

**54:12** and I will make your battlements jasper, and your gates stones of crystal, and your surrounding wall choice stones,

**54:13** and all your sons taught by God, and your children in great peace.

**54:14** And in righteousness you will be built; keep away from unrighteousness, and you will not fear, and trembling will not draw near to you.

**54:15** Look, proselytes will come to you through Me, and they will sojourn with you and flee to you for refuge.

**54:16** Look, I created you, not like a smith blowing on coals and bringing out an instrument for work; but I created you not for destruction, to ruin.

**54:17** No instrument fashioned against you will I prosper; and every voice that rises against you for judgment, all of them you will defeat, and those guilty against you will be in it. There is an inheritance for those who serve the Lord, and you will be righteous to Me, says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:9** — The Greek reads "From the water in the time of Noah" (ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕδατος) rather than "Like the waters of Noah," and inserts the dative τῇ γῇ ("to the earth") before the oath about the mountains (54:9-10).
- **54:10** — The Greek closes with εἶπεν γάρ Ἵλεώς σοι, Κύριε ("for he said, 'Merciful to you, Lord'"), with vocative Κύριε (contrast nominative Κύριος in some witnesses) rendering Hebrew "says the LORD who has compassion on you."
- **54:15** — Greek προσήλυτοι προσελεύσονταί ("proselytes will come") introduces a wordplay on proselytes joining Zion through God (δι᾽ ἐμοῦ), differing substantially from the Hebrew warning against attackers.
- **54:16** — The Greek makes Zion itself the object of ἔκτισά σε ("I created you") rather than the smith, and negates the closing purpose clause ("not for destruction, to ruin").
- **54:17** — In "those guilty against you will be in it" (οἱ δὲ ἔνοχοί σου ἔσονται ἐν αὐτῇ), the feminine pronoun αὐτῇ ("it") refers back to κρίσιν ("judgment").
