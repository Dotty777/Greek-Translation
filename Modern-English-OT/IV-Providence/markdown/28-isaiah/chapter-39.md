## Text

**39:1** At that time Merodach-baladan, the son of Baladan, the king of Babylonia, sent letters and envoys and gifts; for he heard that he had become ill to the point of death and had recovered.

**39:2** And Hezekiah rejoiced over them, and he showed them the house of nechotha, and of silver, and of gold, and of stacte, and of incense, and of perfumed oil, and all the houses of the vessels of the treasure, and everything that was in his treasuries; and there was nothing that Hezekiah did not show in his house.

**39:3** And Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and said to him, "What do these men say, and from where have they come to you?" And Hezekiah said, "From a land far away they have come to me, from Babylon."

**39:4** And Isaiah said, "What did they see in your house?" And Hezekiah said, "All the things in my house they saw, and there is nothing in my house that they did not see, but also the things in my treasuries."

**39:5** And Isaiah said to him, "Hear the word of the Lord Sabaoth:

**39:6** Look, days are coming, and they will take everything that is in your house, and all that your fathers gathered until this day will come to Babylon, and they will certainly leave nothing behind; and God said,

**39:7** 'Even from your children whom you will father they will take, and they will make them eunuchs in the house of the king of the Babylonians.'"

**39:8** And Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "Good is the word of the Lord that He spoke; let there indeed be peace and righteousness in my days."

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — The Greek text adds "and envoys" (καὶ πρέσβεις) to the items sent by Merodach-baladan, king of Babylonia.
- **39:2** — "Nechotha" transliterates the Greek νεχωθά, which leaves the Hebrew term (nĕkōt, "treasure house" or "spicery") untranslated; the verse also omits the concluding phrase "or in all his realm" found in the Hebrew text.
- **39:5** — "Sabaoth" retains the Greek transliteration σαβαώθ of the Hebrew divine title meaning "of hosts" or "of armies" (compare Rom 9:29; Jas 5:4).
- **39:6** — In the Greek text, "and God said" (εἶπεν δὲ ὁ θεὸς) links forward via ὅτι directly to the prophecy in verse 7, whereas in the Hebrew text "says the LORD" closes verse 6.
