## Text

**29:1** Woe, Ariel, city against which David fought! Gather produce year upon year; eat, for you will eat with Moab.

**29:2** For I will afflict Ariel, and her strength and wealth will be Mine.

**29:3** And I will circle around against you like David, and I will throw up a palisade around you, and I will set towers around you,

**29:4** and your words will be brought low to the earth, and into the earth your words will sink; and your voice will be like those who call out from the earth, and toward the ground your voice will grow weak.

**29:5** And the wealth of the ungodly will be like dust from a wall, and the multitude of those who oppress you like chaff carried along, and it will be like an instant, suddenly

**29:6** from the Lord Sabaoth; for there will be a visitation with thunder and earthquake and a loud voice, a rushing storm and a devouring flame of fire.

**29:7** And like one dreaming in sleep at night will be the wealth of all the nations as many as campaigned against Jerusalem, and all those campaigning against Jerusalem, and all those gathered against her, and those who afflict her.

**29:8** And like those who in sleep drink and eat, and when they wake up, the dream is vain; and just as the thirsty one dreams as if drinking, and having woken up, he is still thirsty, and his soul has hoped for nothing, so will be the wealth of all the nations as many as campaigned against Mount Zion.

**29:9** Grow faint and be beside yourselves; be intoxicated, not from strong drink nor from wine;

**29:10** for the Lord has made you drink a spirit of stupor, and He will shut their eyes, and those of their prophets and of their rulers, those who see the hidden things.

**29:11** And all these sayings will be to you like the words of the sealed book, which if they give it to a man who knows letters, saying, "Read these things," and he will say, "I cannot read, for it has been sealed."

**29:12** And this book will be given into the hands of a man who does not know letters, and someone will say to him, "Read this." And he will say, "I do not know letters."

**29:13** And the Lord said, "This people draws near to Me with its mouth, and with their lips they honor Me, yet their heart stays far away from Me; and in vain they worship Me, teaching commandments of men and teachings.

**29:14** Therefore, look, I will proceed to remove this people, and I will remove them, and I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of the intelligent I will hide."

**29:15** Woe to those who make counsel deeply, and their works will be in darkness, and they will say, "Who has seen us? And who will know us, or what we ourselves are doing?"

**29:16** Will you not be reckoned like the potter's clay? Will the thing formed say to the One who formed it, "It was not You who formed me"? Or the thing made to the One who made it, "You did not make me with understanding"?

**29:17** Not much longer, and Lebanon will be changed like Mount Carmel, and Carmel will be reckoned as a forest.

**29:18** And in that day the deaf will hear words of a book, and the eyes of the blind, those in the darkness and those in the gloom, will see,

**29:19** and the poor will exult because of the Lord in gladness, and the hopeless among men will be filled with gladness.

**29:20** The lawless one has come to an end, and the arrogant one has perished, and those who act lawlessly for evil have been utterly destroyed,

**29:21** and those who cause men to sin by a word; and they will make all who reprove in the gates a stumbling block, because they turned aside a righteous man with unjust things.

**29:22** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the house of Jacob, whom He set apart from Abraham: "Not now will Jacob be put to shame, nor now will he change his face;

**29:23** but when their children see My works, because of Me they will sanctify My name, and they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and they will fear the God of Israel;

**29:24** and those who go astray in spirit will know understanding, and those who grumble will learn to obey, and the stammering tongues will learn to speak peace."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Unlike the MT ("let the feasts go around"), the LXX concludes v. 1 with "eat, for you will eat with Moab," and in v. 2 replaces "mourning and lamentation" with "her strength and wealth."
- **29:13** — The LXX differs slightly from NT citations (Matt 15:8–9; Mark 7:6–7) by including "draws near to Me with its mouth" and coordinating "commandments of men and teachings"; in v. 14 it reads "remove" (μεταθεῖναι) where the MT has "deal wonderfully" and "I will hide" (κρύψω) where 1 Cor 1:19 has "I will set aside."
- **29:17** — The LXX treats Χερμέλ (Carmel) as a proper mountain name ("Mount Carmel" and "Carmel") rather than a common noun ("fruitful field").
- **29:21** — The Greek uses a double accusative construction ("make all who reprove in the gates a stumbling block"), diverging from the MT's "lay a snare for him who reproves in the gate."
- **29:22** — The LXX reads "whom He set apart from Abraham" (ὃν ἀφώρισεν ἐξ Ἀβραάμ), where the MT has "who redeemed Abraham."
