## Text

**45:1** Thus says the Lord God to My anointed Cyrus, whose right hand I took hold of so that nations may obey before him, and I will break apart the strength of kings; I will open doors before him, and cities will not be shut:

**45:2** "I will go before you and level mountains; I will shatter bronze doors and break apart iron bars,

**45:3** and I will give you dark, hidden treasures; I will open unseen ones to you, so that you may know that I am the Lord God, who calls your name, the God of Israel.

**45:4** For the sake of My servant Jacob and Israel, My chosen one, I will call you by My name and I will accept you; yet you did not know Me.

**45:5** For I am the Lord God, and there is no other besides Me; I strengthened you, and you did not know Me,

**45:6** so that those from the rising of the sun and those from the west may know that there is none besides Me. I am the Lord God, and there is no other,

**45:7** I am the One who fashioned light and made darkness, who makes peace and creates evils; I am the Lord God, who does all these things.

**45:8** Let heaven above rejoice, and let the clouds sprinkle righteousness; let the earth spring up and sprout mercy, and let it declare righteousness at the same time; I am the Lord, who created you.

**45:9** What better thing have I fashioned like potter's clay? Will the one who plows plow the ground all day long? Will the clay say to the potter, 'What are you making, for you do not work, nor do you have hands?' Will the thing formed answer the one who formed it?

**45:10** The one who says to a father, 'What will you beget?' and to a mother, 'What are you in labor with?'"

**45:11** For thus says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel, who made the things that are coming: "Ask Me about My sons, and concerning the works of My hands, command Me.

**45:12** I made the earth and man upon it; I, with My hand, made heaven firm; I commanded all the stars.

**45:13** I raised him up as king with righteousness, and all his ways are straight; this one will build My city, and he will bring back the captivity of My people, not with ransom nor with gifts," said the Lord Sabaoth.

**45:14** Thus says the Lord Sabaoth: "Egypt has labored, and the merchandise of the Ethiopians, and the Sabeans, tall men, will cross over to you, and they will be slaves to you, and they will follow behind you bound in manacles; and they will cross over to you and bow down to you, and in you they will pray: 'God is in you, and there is no God besides You.

**45:15** For You are God, and we did not know, the God of Israel.'

**45:16** All who oppose Him will be put to shame and humiliated, and they will go in shame. Be renewed toward Me, islands.

**45:17** Israel is saved by the Lord with an eternal salvation; they will not be put to shame, nor will they at all be humiliated forever."

**45:18** Thus says the Lord who made heaven—this is the God who displayed the earth and made it; He Himself marked it out; He did not make it in vain, but formed it to be inhabited: "I am, and there is no other.

**45:19** I have not spoken in secret, nor in a dark place of the earth; I did not say to the seed of Jacob, 'Seek what is vain'; I am, I am the Lord, who speaks righteousness and declares truth.

**45:20** Gather together and come; take counsel together, you who are being saved from the nations. They do not know—those who carry the wood, their carved image, and who pray to gods that do not save.

**45:21** If they will declare it, let them draw near, so that they may know together who made these things heard from the beginning; then it was declared to you: 'I am God, and there is no other besides Me, righteous and a Savior; there is none apart from Me.'

**45:22** Turn to Me and you will be saved, you from the end of the earth; I am God, and there is no other.

**45:23** By Myself I swear that righteousness will surely go out from My mouth; My words will not be turned back, for to Me every knee will bow, and every tongue will swear by God,

**45:24** saying, 'Righteousness and glory will come to Him,' and all who separate themselves will be put to shame;

**45:25** by the Lord they will be justified, and in God all the seed of the sons of Israel will be glorified."

## Translator Notes

- **45:4** — In the Greek Septuagint, God tells Cyrus "I will call you by My name" (τῷ ὀνόματί μου) and "accept you," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("call you by your name" and "give you a title of honor").
- **45:7** — The plural κακά ("evils") contrasts with "peace" (εἰρήνην) and denotes calamities, adversity, or disaster rather than moral wickedness.
- **45:9** — The Greek opens with the distinct Septuagint reading "What better thing have I fashioned like potter's clay?" and includes the plowman question (cf. Isaiah 28:24) as well as the closing question echoed in Romans 9:20.
- **45:14** — "Sabaoth" (σαβαώθ) is the Septuagint transliteration of Hebrew tseva'ot ("of hosts/armies"), and "Ethiopians" (Αἰθιόπων) renders Hebrew Cush. In the nations' prayer, "besides You" shifts from addressing Zion ("in you") to addressing God directly (continued in 45:15).
- **45:23** — The clause "εἰ μὴ ἐξελεύσεται..." reflects the Septuagint's Hebraistic oath formula after "I swear" (ὀμνύω), expressing positive certainty ("that... will surely go out"); the Greek also explicitly specifies "by God" (τὸν θεόν) at the end of the verse.
