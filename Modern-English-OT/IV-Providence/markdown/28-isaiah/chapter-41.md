## Text

**41:1** Renew yourselves toward Me, islands, for the rulers will renew strength; let them draw near and speak together, then let them declare judgment.

**41:2** Who raised up righteousness from the east, called it at His feet, and it will go? He will give it before nations, and He will dismay kings; and He will give their swords to the earth, and their bows like driven brushwood;

**41:3** and He will pursue them; the way of His feet will pass through in peace.

**41:4** Who worked and did these things? He who calls it from the generations of the beginning called it: I, God, am first, and for the things to come, I am.

**41:5** Nations saw and feared; the ends of the earth drew near and came together,

**41:6** each deciding to help his neighbor and his brother, and he will say,

**41:7** "A craftsman has grown strong, and a smith striking with a hammer, beating at the same time." Sometimes he will say, "The joint is good"; he made them firm with nails; they will set them up, and they will not be moved.

**41:8** But you, Israel, My servant Jacob whom I chose, offspring of Abraham whom I loved,

**41:9** whom I took hold of from the ends of the earth, and from its watchtowers I called you and said to you, "You are My servant; I chose you and did not forsake you."

**41:10** Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be led astray, for I am your God who strengthened you, and I helped you and made you secure with My righteous right hand.

**41:11** Look, all who oppose you will be ashamed and disgraced, for they will be as those who do not exist, and all your adversaries will perish.

**41:12** You will seek them, and you will by no means find the people who will act abusively toward you; for they will be as those who do not exist, and those who wage war against you will not exist.

**41:13** For I am your God, who grasps your right hand, who says to you,

**41:14** "Do not fear, Jacob, tiny Israel; I helped you," says God, who redeems Israel.

**41:15** Look, I have made you like new, saw-toothed threshing wheels of a cart, and you will thresh mountains and grind hills fine, and make them like chaff,

**41:16** and you will winnow; and a wind will take them, and a whirlwind will scatter them. But you will rejoice in the holy ones of Israel,

**41:17** and the poor and the needy will exult. For they will seek water and there will be none; their tongue has dried up from thirst. I am the Lord God; I, the God of Israel, will hear and will not forsake them,

**41:18** rather, I will open rivers upon the mountains and springs in the midst of plains; I will make the wilderness into marshes of water and the thirsty land in water channels;

**41:19** I will place in the waterless land cedar and box tree, myrtle and cypress and white poplar,

**41:20** so that they may see and know and consider and understand together that the hand of the Lord did these things, and the Holy One of Israel revealed them.

**41:21** "Your judgment draws near," says the Lord God; "your counsels have drawn near," says the King of Jacob.

**41:22** Let them draw near and declare to you what will happen, or tell what the former things were, and we will apply our mind, and we will know what the last things are, and tell us the things to come;

**41:23** Declare to us the things to come at the end, and we will know that you are gods. Do good and do harm, and we will marvel, and we will see together;

**41:24** for where are you from, and where is your work from? From the earth! They chose you an abomination.

**41:25** But I raised up the one from the north and the one from the rising of the sun; they will be called by My name. Let rulers come, and like potter's clay and like a potter trampling the clay, so you will be trampled.

**41:26** For who will declare the things from the beginning, so that we may know, and the things beforehand, and we will say that they are true? There is no one who foretells, nor anyone who hears your words.

**41:27** I will give a beginning to Zion, and I will comfort Jerusalem.

**41:28** For from the nations, look, there is no one, and from their idols there was no one declaring; and if I ask them, "Where are you from?" they will by no means answer Me.

**41:29** For there are those who make you, and in vain are those who lead you astray.

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — The Greek reads "Renew yourselves" (Ἐγκαινίζεσθε) and "rulers" (ἄρχοντες), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Keep silence" and "peoples").
- **41:2** — In Greek, "righteousness" (δικαιοσύνην) is feminine (referred to by feminine "it" here and in v. 4), leaving "Who" (God, v. 4) as the grammatical antecedent for the masculine pronouns and verbs in vv. 2-3.
- **41:7** — Following "and he will say" at the end of v. 6, the Greek introduces the craftsman's words directly at the start of v. 7, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Be strong!" in v. 6 and begins narrative action in v. 7.
- **41:14** — The Greek reads "tiny Israel" (ὀλιγοστὸς Ἰσραήλ, smallest or fewest in number) rather than "you worm Jacob / men of Israel" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **41:16** — The Greek has plural "the holy ones of Israel" (τοῖς ἁγίοις Ἰσραήλ), contrasting with the singular "the Holy One of Israel" in v. 20 and in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
