## Text

**24:1** Look, the Lord is destroying the inhabited world, and will lay it waste and uncover its face and scatter those who dwell in it.

**24:2** And the people will be like the priest, and the servant like the master, and the maidservant like the mistress; the one who buys will be like the one who sells, the one who lends like the one who borrows, and the one who owes like the one to whom he owes.

**24:3** With destruction the earth will be destroyed, and with plunder the earth will be plundered; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.

**24:4** The earth mourned, and the inhabited world was destroyed; the lofty ones of the earth mourned.

**24:5** And the earth acted lawlessly because of those who inhabit it, because they transgressed the law and changed the ordinances, an everlasting covenant.

**24:6** Therefore a curse will devour the earth, because those who inhabit it sinned; therefore those who dwell on the earth will be poor, and few people will be left.

**24:7** Wine will mourn, the vine will mourn; all who are glad in soul will groan.

**24:8** The gladness of tambourines has ceased, the arrogance and wealth of the ungodly has ceased, the sound of the lyre has ceased.

**24:9** They were put to shame; they did not drink wine; strong drink became bitter to those who drink it.

**24:10** Every city was laid waste; one will shut a house so that no one may enter.

**24:11** Wail over the wine everywhere; all gladness has ceased.

**24:12** Cities will be left desolate, and abandoned houses will perish.

**24:13** All these things will be on the earth in the midst of the nations: just as when someone gleans an olive tree, so they will glean them, and when the grape harvest has ceased.

**24:14** These will cry out with a shout, but those who are left on the earth will be glad together with the glory of the Lord; the water of the sea will be troubled.

**24:15** Therefore the glory of the Lord will be in the islands of the sea; the name of the Lord will be glorious.

**24:16** Lord, God of Israel, from the wings of the earth we have heard wonders: "Hope for the godly!" And they will say, "Woe to those who rebel!" You who rebel against the law,

**24:17** fear and a pit and a snare are upon you who dwell on the earth.

**24:18** And it will be that the one who flees from the fear will fall into the pit, and the one who comes out of the pit will be caught by the snare; for windows from heaven were opened, and the foundations of the earth will be shaken.

**24:19** With trouble the earth will be troubled, and with perplexity the earth will be perplexed.

**24:20** It tilted like one who is drunk and reeling, and the earth will be shaken like a fruit-watcher's hut; for lawlessness prevailed upon it, and it will fall and will by no means be able to rise.

**24:21** And God will bring His hand upon the array of heaven and upon the kings of the earth.

**24:22** And they will gather its gathering into a prison and shut them up in a stronghold; after many generations there will be a visitation of them.

**24:23** And the brick will melt, and the wall will fall; for the Lord will reign from Zion and to Jerusalem, and before the elders He will be glorified.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — The Septuagint reads "will be poor" (ptochoi esontai) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "are scorched" or "diminished."
- **24:8** — The Septuagint reads "the arrogance and wealth of the ungodly" where the Masoretic Text reads "the noise of revelers."
- **24:14** — The Septuagint renders the final clause "the water of the sea will be troubled," differing from the Masoretic Text ("they shout from the sea/west").
- **24:21** — The Septuagint reads "the array [lit. adornment/order] of heaven" for the Hebrew "host of the height" and uses "bring His hand upon" for "punish."
- **24:23** — The Septuagint reads "the brick will melt, and the wall will fall" where the Masoretic Text has "the moon will be confounded and the sun ashamed," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants.
