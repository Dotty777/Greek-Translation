## Text

**43:1** And now thus says the Lord God, who made you, Jacob, and who formed you, Israel: "Do not be afraid, because I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine.

**43:2** And if you cross through water, I am with you, and rivers will not flood over you; and if you pass through fire, you will by no means be burned up; flame will not burn you up.

**43:3** For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, who saves you; I have made Egypt and Ethiopia an exchange for you, and Syene in your place.

**43:4** Since you became precious in My sight, you have been glorified, and I have loved you; and I will give people for you and rulers for your head.

**43:5** Do not be afraid, because I am with you; from the east I will bring your offspring, and from the west I will gather you.

**43:6** I will say to the north, 'Bring them,' and to the southwest, 'Do not hold back; bring My sons from a land far away, and My daughters from the ends of the earth,

**43:7** all who have been called by My name.' For in My glory I fashioned him and formed him and made him,

**43:8** and I brought out a blind people, and eyes are likewise blind, and deaf ones who have ears.

**43:9** All the nations have been gathered together, and rulers from among them will be gathered. Who will declare these things? Or who will declare to you the things from the beginning? Let them bring their witnesses and be justified, and let them hear, and let them speak true things.

**43:10** Become witnesses for Me, and I am a witness," says the Lord God, "and the servant whom I have chosen, so that you may know and believe and understand that I am; before Me no other god came to be, and after Me there will be none.

**43:11** I am God, and apart from Me there is no one who saves.

**43:12** I declared and I saved; I reproached, and there was no stranger among us; you are witnesses for Me, and I am the Lord God

**43:13** even from the beginning, and there is no one who rescues out of My hands; I will act, and who will turn it back?"

**43:14** Thus says the Lord God who redeems you, the Holy One of Israel: "For your sake I will send to Babylon and will stir up all who flee, and the Chaldeans will be bound in ships.

**43:15** I am the Lord God, your Holy One, who appointed Israel as your king."

**43:16** Thus says the Lord, who gives a way in the sea and a path in mighty water,

**43:17** who brought out chariots and horse and a mighty crowd—but they will lie down and will not rise; they were quenched like quenched flax:

**43:18** "Do not remember the former things, and do not ponder the ancient things.

**43:19** Look, I am doing new things that will now spring up, and you will know them. And I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the waterless land.

**43:20** The wild beasts of the field will bless Me, sirens and daughters of ostriches, because I gave water in the wilderness and rivers in the waterless land, to give drink to My chosen race,

**43:21** My people whom I acquired for Myself to recount My praises.

**43:22** It was not now that I called you, Jacob, nor did I make you grow weary, Israel;

**43:23** your sheep of your whole burnt offering are not for Me, nor did you glorify Me in your sacrifices, nor did I make you weary with frankincense,

**43:24** nor did you buy an offering for Me with silver, nor did I desire the fat of your sacrifices; instead, in your sins you stood before Me, and in your unrighteous deeds.

**43:25** I am, I am the One who blots out your lawless deeds for My sake, and your sins, and I will not remember them.

**43:26** But you, remember, and let us enter into judgment; you state your lawless deeds first, so that you may be justified.

**43:27** Your fathers first and your rulers acted lawlessly against Me,

**43:28** and the rulers defiled My holy things; and I gave Jacob over to be destroyed, and Israel to reproach."

## Translator Notes

- **43:3** — The Greek text reads "Syene" (ancient Aswan in Upper Egypt) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Seba."
- **43:6** — Greek "to the southwest" (from Greek lips, the southwest wind or quarter) stands here where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "to the south."
- **43:12** — Greek "I reproached" and "among us" (following the first-person plural pronoun in the Greek critical text) differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "I proclaimed" and "among you."
- **43:15** — The Greek participial construction ("who appointed Israel as your king") places "Israel" and "your king" in the accusative as object and complement, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Creator of Israel, your King").
- **43:20** — The Greek text renders the desert creatures literally as "sirens and daughters of ostriches," traditional Septuagint designations for Hebrew "jackals and ostriches."
