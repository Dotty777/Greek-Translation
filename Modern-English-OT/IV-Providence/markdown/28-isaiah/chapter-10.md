## Text

**10:1** Woe to those who write wickedness; for writing, they write wickedness,

**10:2** turning aside the justice of the poor, seizing the judgment of the needy of My people, so that a widow becomes prey for them, and an orphan becomes plunder.

**10:3** And what will they do in the day of visitation? For affliction will come to you from afar; and to whom will you flee for refuge to be helped? And where will you leave your glory behind so as not to fall into captivity?

**10:4** For all these things the wrath has not turned away, but the hand is still uplifted.

**10:5** Woe to the Assyrians, the rod of My fury, and the wrath is in their hands.

**10:6** I will send My wrath against a lawless nation, and I will command My people to take spoils and plunder, and to trample the cities and turn them into dust.

**10:7** But he did not think so, and in his soul he has not reasoned so; rather, his mind will change, even to destroy not a few nations.

**10:8** And if they say to him, "You alone are ruler,"

**10:9** then he will say, "Did I not take the region above Babylon and Calneh, where the tower was built? And I took Arabia and Damascus and Samaria;

**10:10** just as I took these, I will also take all the realms. Wail, you carved images in Jerusalem and in Samaria;

**10:11** for just as I did to Samaria and to its things made with hands, so I will do also to Jerusalem and to its idols."

**10:12** And it will be, when the Lord finishes doing everything on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will bring punishment upon the great mind, upon the ruler of the Assyrians, and upon the loftiness of the glory of his eyes.

**10:13** For he said, "By strength I will act, and by the wisdom of understanding I will remove boundaries of nations, and I will plunder their strength;

**10:14** and I will shake inhabited cities, and I will seize the whole inhabited world with my hand like a nest, and like abandoned eggs I will take them up; and there is no one who will escape me or speak against me."

**10:15** Will an axe be glorified without the one who chops with it? Or will a saw be lifted up without the one who pulls it? As when someone lifts up a rod or a piece of wood—and is it not so?

**10:16** But the Lord Sabaoth will send upon your honor dishonor, and upon your glory a burning fire will burn;

**10:17** and the light of Israel will become fire, and will sanctify him in burning fire, and will devour the brushwood like grass.

**10:18** On that day the mountains and the hills and the forests will be extinguished, and it will devour from soul even to flesh; and the one who flees will be like one fleeing from a burning flame;

**10:19** and those who are left from them will be a number, and a child will write them down.

**10:20** And it will be on that day, what is left of Israel will no longer be added to, and those of Jacob who were saved will no longer trust in those who wronged them, but they will trust in God, the Holy One of Israel, in truth.

**10:21** And what is left of Jacob will be upon a mighty God.

**10:22** And even if the people of Israel become like the sand of the sea, their remnant will be saved;

**10:23** completing and cutting short a word in righteousness, because the Lord will execute a word cut short in the whole inhabited world.

**10:24** Therefore thus says the Lord Sabaoth: "Do not be afraid, My people who dwell in Zion, of the Assyrians, because he will strike you with a rod; for I am bringing a blow upon you, to see the way of Egypt.

**10:25** For yet a little while, and the wrath will cease, and My fury will be against their counsel."

**10:26** And God will stir up against them according to the blow of Midian in a place of affliction, and His fury on the way by the sea, into the way toward Egypt.

**10:27** And it will be on that day his yoke will be taken away from your shoulder, and the fear of him from you, and the yoke will be destroyed from your shoulders.

**10:28** For he will come to the city of Aggai, and he will pass on to Megiddo, and in Michmash he will deposit his baggage;

**10:29** and he will pass through a ravine and he will come to Aggai; fear will seize Ramah, the city of Saul. She will flee,

**10:30** the daughter of Gallim; she will listen in Sa, she will listen in Anathoth;

**10:31** and Madebena was dismayed, and those who dwell in Gibbir.

**10:32** Exhort today on the road to remain; with your hand exhort the mountain, the daughter of Zion, and you hills that are in Jerusalem.

**10:33** Look, the Master, the Lord Sabaoth, throws the glorious ones into confusion with strength, and those who are lofty in arrogance will be crushed, and the lofty will be humbled,

**10:34** and lofty ones will fall by the sword, and Lebanon will fall with the lofty ones.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — Unlike the Hebrew ('Against a godless nation I send him...'), the Septuagint makes 'My wrath' the object of 'I will send' and has God command 'My people' rather than the Assyrian.
- **10:9** — The Septuagint differs substantially from the Hebrew ('Is not Calno like Carchemish?...'), introducing Babylon, Calneh (Greek Χαλαννή, matching Gen 10:10, where the Hebrew here has Calno), a reference to the tower built there, and Arabia.
- **10:15** — The final clause translates the Septuagint Greek literally ('and is it not so?'), where the Hebrew reads 'not wood.'
- **10:28** — The Septuagint presents distinct place-name forms in vv. 28–31: 'Aggai' (Ἀγγαί, for Hebrew Aiath in v. 28 and Geba in v. 29), 'Megiddo' (Μαγεδώ, for Hebrew Migron), 'the city of Saul' (for Gibeah of Saul in v. 29), 'in Sa' (ἐν Σά, for Laishah in v. 30), 'Madebena' (for Madmenah in v. 31), and 'Gibbir' (for Gebim in v. 31).
- **10:29** — Greek φεύξεται ('will flee') stands at the end of v. 29 with its subject ἡ θυγάτηρ Γαλείμ ('the daughter of Gallim') at the beginning of v. 30, following the Septuagint's clause division rather than the Masoretic verse break.
