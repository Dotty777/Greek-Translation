## Text

**15:1** The word against Moabitis. By night Moabitis will be destroyed, for by night the wall of Moabitis will be destroyed.

**15:2** Grieve for yourselves, for Dibon also will be destroyed, where your altar is; there you will go up to weep, to Nebo of Moabitis. Wail; on every head is baldness, all arms are gashed;

**15:3** in her squares wrap yourselves in sackcloth, and lament on her rooftops and in her streets; all of you, wail with weeping.

**15:4** Because Heshbon and Elealeh have cried aloud, their voice was heard as far as Jahaz; therefore the loins of Moabitis cry out, her soul will know.

**15:5** The heart of Moabitis cries out within her as far as Zoar, for she is a three-year-old heifer; and up the ascent of Luhith they will go up to you weeping along the road of Horonaim; destruction and shaking cry out,

**15:6** the water of Nimrim will be a desolation, and its grass will fail; for there will be no green grass.

**15:7** Is she going to be saved even like this? For I will bring Arabs upon the ravine, and they will take it.

**15:8** For the outcry has reached the border of Moabitis, of Eglaim, and her wailing as far as the well of Elim.

**15:9** And the water of Dimon will be filled with blood; for I will bring Arabs upon Dimon, and I will remove the seed of Moab and Ariel and the remnant of Adama.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — The Greek uses the feminine territorial name "Moabitis" (here and in 15:2, 4, 5, 8; 16:7, alongside "Moab") for the land of Moab, omits "Ar," and renders Hebrew qir as "wall."
- **15:2** — The Greek reads "all arms are gashed" (referring to mourning lacerations), whereas the Hebrew text reads "every beard is cut off."
- **15:4** — Reading the Hebrew consonants as "loins" and "will know," the Greek renders "the loins of Moabitis cry out; her soul will know" where the Hebrew has "armed men" and "trembles."
- **15:7** — Instead of the Hebrew statement about carrying wealth over "the brook of the willows," the Greek presents a rhetorical question and a warning that "Arabs" will be brought upon the ravine.
- **15:9** — The Greek again reads "Arabs" and treats the Hebrew nouns for "lion" and "land" as the proper names "Ariel" and "Adama."
