## Text

**61:1** The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He anointed Me; He has sent Me to announce good news to the poor, to heal those crushed in heart, to proclaim release to captives and recovery of sight to the blind,

**61:2** to declare an acceptable year of the Lord and a day of retribution, to comfort all who mourn,

**61:3** that to those who mourn in Zion, to them, glory may be given instead of ashes, oil of gladness to those who mourn, a robe of glory instead of a spirit of dejection; and they will be called generations of righteousness, a planting of the Lord for glory.

**61:4** And they will build ancient desolate places; those previously laid desolate they will raise up; and they will renew desolate cities, laid desolate for generations.

**61:5** And foreigners will come shepherding your sheep, and aliens as plowmen and vinedressers;

**61:6** but you will be called priests of the Lord, ministers of God; you will consume the strength of nations, and in their wealth you will be marveled at.

**61:7** Thus they will inherit the land a second time, and everlasting gladness will be over their head.

**61:8** For I am the Lord who loves righteousness and hates plunder from unrighteousness; and I will give their labor to the righteous, and an everlasting covenant I will make with them.

**61:9** And their seed will be known among the nations, and their descendants in the midst of the peoples; everyone who sees them will recognize them, that these are a seed blessed by God,

**61:10** and with gladness they will be glad in the Lord. Let my soul exult in the Lord; for He has clothed me with a garment of salvation and a tunic of gladness; He has placed a turban on me as on a bridegroom, and as a bride He has adorned me with ornament.

**61:11** And as the earth causes its blossom to grow, and as a garden its seeds, so the Lord, the Lord will cause righteousness and exultation to spring up before all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **61:1** — The speaker is the Anointed One (Messiah), whose pronouns are capitalized accordingly (compare Luke 4:18-19); LXX has "recovery of sight to the blind" where MT reads "opening of the prison to those who are bound."
- **61:3** — LXX renders MT's "oaks of righteousness" as "generations of righteousness" and reads "to those who mourn" (dative) rather than "instead of mourning."
- **61:7** — LXX differs markedly from MT ("instead of your shame you will have a double portion"), reading "Thus they will inherit the land a second time."
- **61:10** — The opening clause ("and with gladness they will be glad in the Lord") continues the third-person plural description of 61:9 in LXX before shifting to the first-person speaker.
- **61:11** — Greek doubles the title as kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), corresponding to Hebrew Adonai YHWH.
