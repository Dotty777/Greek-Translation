## Text

**52:1** Awake, awake, Zion; put on your strength, Zion, and you, put on your glory, Jerusalem, the holy city; no longer will anyone uncircumcised and unclean pass through you again.

**52:2** Shake off the dust and rise up, sit down, Jerusalem; loose the bond of your neck, captive daughter of Zion.

**52:3** For thus says the Lord: "You were sold for nothing, and not with silver will you be redeemed."

**52:4** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: "To Egypt My people went down at first to sojourn there, and to the Assyrians they were led by force;

**52:5** and now why are you here?" Thus says the Lord: "Because My people was taken for nothing, marvel and wail." Thus says the Lord: "Because of you My name is continually blasphemed among the nations.

**52:6** Therefore My people will know My name in that day, that I Myself am the One who speaks; I am present like a season upon the mountains,

**52:7** like the feet of one bringing good news, a report of peace, like one bringing good news of good things, for I will make your salvation heard, saying to Zion, 'Your God will reign.'"

**52:8** For the voice of those who guard you has been lifted up, and with the voice together they will rejoice; for eyes will look to eyes when the Lord has mercy on Zion.

**52:9** Let the desolate places of Jerusalem break forth together into gladness, for the Lord has had mercy on her and has rescued Jerusalem.

**52:10** And the Lord will reveal His holy arm before all the nations, and all the ends of the earth will see the salvation from our God.

**52:11** Depart, depart, go out from there and touch nothing unclean; go out from the midst of her, separate yourselves, you who carry the vessels of the Lord;

**52:12** for not with turmoil will you go out, nor will you go in flight; for the Lord will go first before you, and the God of Israel, the One who gathers you together.

**52:13** Look, My servant will understand, and he will be lifted up and glorified exceedingly.

**52:14** Just as many will be astonished at you, so your appearance will be without glory from men, and your glory from men.

**52:15** So many nations will marvel at him, and kings will shut their mouth; for those to whom it was not announced concerning him will see, and those who have not heard will understand.

## Translator Notes

- **52:4** — The doubled title κύριος Κύριος ("the Lord, the Lord") translates Hebrew ʾădōnāy YHWH, and βίᾳ ἤχθησαν ("they were led by force") diverges from the Hebrew clause about oppression without cause.
- **52:6** — Greek ὡς ὥρα ("like a season") denotes a timely season, springtime, or bloom/beauty, corresponding to the Hebrew exclamation "How lovely" at the beginning of 52:7.
- **52:14** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which shifts to the third person in the second half of the verse, the Greek retains second-person singular pronouns (σέ, σου) throughout and uses Hebraic comparative ἀπό ("from" or "beyond").
- **52:15** — Greek θαυμάσονται ("will marvel") corresponds to Hebrew yazzeh ("he will sprinkle"), continuing the motif of astonishment from 52:14.
