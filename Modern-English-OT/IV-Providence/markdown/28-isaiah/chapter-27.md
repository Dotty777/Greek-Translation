## Text

**27:1** In that day God will bring the holy and great and strong sword against the dragon, a fleeing serpent, against the dragon, a crooked serpent; He will kill the dragon.

**27:2** In that day, a fine vineyard; a desire to begin against her.

**27:3** I am a fortified city, a city being besieged; in vain I will water her, for she will be captured by night, and by day a wall will fall.

**27:4** There is not one that has not taken hold of her; who will set Me to guard stubble in a field? Because of this hostility I have rejected her. Therefore, because of this the Lord has done everything that He commanded. "I have been burned up,"

**27:5** those who dwell in her will cry out, "Let us make peace."

**27:6** Those who are coming, children of Jacob, Israel will sprout and blossom, and the inhabited world will be filled with his fruit.

**27:7** He will not also be struck just as he himself struck, will he? And as he himself killed, will he be killed like that?

**27:8** Fighting and reproaching, He will send them away; were you not devising with the harsh spirit to kill them with a spirit of wrath?

**27:9** Therefore the lawlessness of Jacob will be taken away, and this is his blessing, when I take away his sin, when they make all the stones of the altars shattered like fine dust; and their trees will certainly not remain, and their idols will be cut down like a forest far away.

**27:10** The inhabited flock will be abandoned, like a forsaken flock; and for a long time it will be for pasture, and there they will rest.

**27:11** And after a time there will not be anything green in her because it has dried up. Women coming from a sight, come! For it is not a people having understanding; therefore He who made them will certainly not have compassion on them, nor will He who formed them by any means have mercy.

**27:12** And it will be in that day that God will fence in from the canal of the river as far as Rhinocorura; and you, gather the sons of Israel one by one.

**27:13** And it will be in that day that they will sound the great trumpet, and those who were lost in the land of the Assyrians and those who were lost in Egypt will come, and they will worship the Lord on the holy mountain, Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The LXX diverges sharply from the Hebrew MT, reading "I am a fortified city, a city being besieged" where the MT has "I, the Lord, am its keeper."
- **27:4** — The concluding verb (κατακέκαυμαι, "I have been burned up") is punctuated and framed in the LXX as the opening cry of the city's inhabitants, continuing directly into v. 5.
- **27:8** — The second-person pronoun (σύ, "you") is syntactically ambiguous in Greek; in this context it most naturally addresses the hostile adversary who plotted to kill Israel, though some understand it as addressing God regarding His fierce wind or spirit (cf. MT).
- **27:12** — The LXX reads "fence in" (συμφράξει) instead of the MT's "beat out" or "thresh," and names the border city "Rhinocorura" (Ῥινοκορούρων, modern El-Arish) where the MT has "the Brook of Egypt."
