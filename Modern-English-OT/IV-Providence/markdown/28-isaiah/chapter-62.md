## Text

**62:1** For Zion's sake I will not be silent, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not let up, until her righteousness goes forth like light, and My salvation will burn like a torch.

**62:2** And nations will see your righteousness, and kings your glory, and one will call you the new name that the Lord will name.

**62:3** And you will be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a diadem of kingship in the hand of your God.

**62:4** And you will no longer be called "Forsaken," and your land will no longer be called "Desolate"; for you will be called "My Delight," and your land "Inhabited," because the Lord has delighted in you, and your land will be settled.

**62:5** And as a young man dwells with a virgin, so your sons will dwell; and it will be that in the way a bridegroom will be glad over a bride, so the Lord will be glad over you.

**62:6** And upon your walls, Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen all the day and all the night, who continually will not be silent, remembering the Lord.

**62:7** For there is no one like you; if He establishes, and makes Jerusalem a boast on the earth.

**62:8** The Lord has sworn by His glory and by the strength of His arm: "I will certainly no longer give your grain and food to your enemies, and foreign sons will no longer drink your wine for which you labored;

**62:9** but those who gathered them will eat them and praise the Lord, and those who gathered them will drink them in My holy courts."

**62:10** Go through My gates, and make a way for My people, and cast the stones out of the way; lift up a signal to the nations.

**62:11** For look, the Lord has made it heard to the end of the earth; say to the daughter of Zion, "Look, the Savior has come to you, having His own reward, and His work before His face."

**62:12** And one will call them "Holy People, Redeemed by the Lord," but you will be called "Sought-After City" and "Not Forsaken."

## Translator Notes

- **62:1** — LXX reads "My salvation" (spoken by the Lord) where MT has "her salvation."
- **62:4** — LXX renders Hebrew Hephzibah ("My delight is in her") as "My Delight" (lit. "My Will/Desire") and Beulah ("Married") as "Inhabited," linking the verse to the dwelling wordplay in 62:5.
- **62:7** — LXX differs from MT ("and give Him no rest until He establishes..."), reading "there is no one like you" and leaving an open conditional clause ("if He establishes," lit. "sets straight").
- **62:8** — Greek introduces the negative oath with the Hebraistic conditional idiom ei eti (lit. "if still/again"), meaning "I will certainly no longer."
