## Text

**60:1** Be illuminated, be illuminated, Jerusalem, for your light has come, and the glory of the Lord has risen upon you.

**60:2** Look, darkness will cover the earth, and thick darkness will be upon the nations; but upon you the Lord will appear, and His glory will be seen upon you.

**60:3** And kings will walk by your light, and nations by your brightness.

**60:4** Lift up your eyes all around and see your children gathered together; all your sons have come from afar, and your daughters will be carried on shoulders.

**60:5** Then you will see, and you will fear and be amazed in your heart, because the wealth of the sea and of nations and of peoples will turn to you. And herds of camels will come to you,

**60:6** and camels of Midian and Ephah will cover you; all from Sheba will come bringing gold, and they will bring frankincense, and they will proclaim the good news of the salvation of the Lord;

**60:7** and all the sheep of Kedar will be gathered together, and rams of Nebaioth will come, and acceptable things will be offered up upon My altar, and the house of My prayer will be glorified.

**60:8** Who are these that fly like clouds, and like doves with nestlings to Me?

**60:9** For Me the islands have waited, and ships of Tarshish among the first, to bring your children from afar, and their silver and their gold with them, and the holy name of the Lord, and because the Holy One of Israel is glorious.

**60:10** And foreigners will build your walls, and their kings will attend you; for because of My wrath I struck you, and because of mercy I loved you.

**60:11** And your gates will be opened continually; day and night they will not be shut, to bring in to you the power of nations and their kings being led.

**60:12** For the nations and the kings who will not serve you will perish, and the nations will be made utterly desolate.

**60:13** And the glory of Lebanon will come to you, with cypress and pine and cedar together, to glorify My holy place.

**60:14** And the sons of those who humbled you and provoked you will come to you in fear, and you will be called "City of the Lord, Zion of the Holy One of Israel."

**60:15** Because you have become forsaken and hated, and there was no one to help, I will make you an eternal exultation, a joy for generations of generations.

**60:16** And you will nurse on the milk of nations, and you will eat the wealth of kings, and you will know that I am the Lord who saves you and rescues you, the God of Israel.

**60:17** Instead of bronze I will bring you gold, and instead of iron I will bring you silver, and instead of wood I will bring you bronze, and instead of stones, iron. And I will appoint your rulers in peace, and your overseers in righteousness;

**60:18** and injustice will no longer be heard in your land, nor ruin nor misery within your borders; rather, your walls will be called "Salvation," and your gates "Carving."

**60:19** And the sun will no longer be light by day for you, nor will the rising of the moon illuminate the night for you; rather, the Lord will be an eternal light for you, and God your glory.

**60:20** For the sun will not set for you, and the moon will not fail you; for the Lord will be an eternal light for you, and the days of your mourning will be fulfilled.

**60:21** And your people will all be righteous; forever they will inherit the land, guarding the planting, the works of His hands, for glory.

**60:22** The smallest one will become thousands, and the least one a great nation; I, the Lord, in due time will gather them.

## Translator Notes

- **60:1** — LXX doubles the passive/middle imperative "Be illuminated, be illuminated" and explicitly names Jerusalem, where MT reads "Arise, shine."
- **60:6** — Greek reads Gaipha (representing the initial Hebrew consonant ayin with gamma) and Saba, transliterating Ephah and Sheba.
- **60:9** — Greek "and the holy name of the Lord" lacks a preposition (corresponding to Heb. lesem, "for the name"), and "because the Holy One of Israel is glorious" differs from MT "because He has glorified you."
- **60:18** — LXX reads Glymma ("Carving" or "carved work") for your gates, where MT reads "Praise" (tehillah).
- **60:21** — LXX "guarding the planting, the works of His hands" reads Heb. neser ("branch/shoot") as noser ("guarding") and shifts first-person "My planting, the work of My hands" to third-person "His hands" (cf. 61:3).
