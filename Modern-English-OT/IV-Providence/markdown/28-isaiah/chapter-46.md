## Text

**46:1** Bel has fallen; Nebo has been shattered; their carved images have come to be for wild beasts and the livestock; carry them bound up as a load for one who is weary,

**46:2** faint and hungry, altogether lacking strength; who will not be able to be saved from war, but they themselves were led away captive.

**46:3** Listen to Me, house of Jacob and all the remnant of Israel, who are carried from the womb and instructed from childhood:

**46:4** even to old age I am, and until you grow old, I am; I bear with you; I made, and I will forgive; I will take up and save you.

**46:5** To whom have you likened Me? See, contrive, you who go astray,

**46:6** those who contribute gold from a pouch and silver on a balance; they will weigh it on a scale, and having hired a goldsmith, they made things made with hands, and stooping down, they worship them.

**46:7** They lift it onto the shoulder and walk along; and if they set it down, it remains in its place; it will certainly not move; and whoever cries out to it, it will certainly not listen; from evils it will certainly not save him.

**46:8** Remember these things and groan; repent, you who have gone astray, return in heart,

**46:9** and remember the former things from of old, for I am God, and there is no other besides Me,

**46:10** declaring beforehand the last things before they happen, and at once it was accomplished; and I said, "All My counsel will stand, and all that I have purposed I will do";

**46:11** calling a bird from the east, and from a land far away concerning the things I have purposed; I spoke and brought it, I created and made it, I brought him and prospered his way.

**46:12** Listen to Me, you who have lost heart, you who are far from righteousness.

**46:13** I have brought My righteousness near, and the salvation from Me I will not delay; I have given salvation in Zion to Israel for glory.

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — The Greek phrase ἐγένετο ... εἰς ("have come to be for") reflects the Hebrew idiom meaning they became burdens placed upon wild beasts and livestock.
- **46:4** — The repeated ἐγώ εἰμι ("I am") echoes the absolute divine self-designation in 45:18-19; ἀνήσω ("I will forgive/spare/release") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("I will carry/bear").
- **46:10** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "things that have not yet been done," the Greek reads καὶ ἅμα συνετελέσθη ("and at once it was accomplished").
- **46:12** — "You who have lost heart" translates the distinct Septuagint reading οἱ ἀπολωλεκότες τὴν καρδίαν, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("stout/stubborn of heart").
