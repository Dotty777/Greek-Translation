## Text

**12:1** And you will say in that day, "I bless You, Lord, because You were angry with me, and You turned away Your fury and had mercy on me.

**12:2** Look, my God is my Savior; I will have confidence in Him and will not be afraid, because the Lord is my glory and my praise, and He has become salvation for me."

**12:3** And you will draw water with gladness from the springs of salvation.

**12:4** And you will say in that day, "Sing praise to the Lord, call out His name, declare His glorious deeds among the nations; remember that His name has been exalted.

**12:5** Sing praise to the name of the Lord, because He has done lofty things; declare these things in all the earth.

**12:6** Exult and be glad, you who inhabit Zion, because the Holy One of Israel has been exalted in her midst."

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The LXX reads present-tense "I bless You" (Εὐλογῶ σε), contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "I will give thanks to You."
- **12:2** — The LXX reads "my God is my Savior" and "my glory and my praise" (with explicit "in Him" and "for me"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "God is my salvation" and "my strength and my song."
