## Text

**28:1** Woe to the crown of arrogance, the hired workers of Ephraim, the flower fallen from glory on the summit of the rich mountain, those who are drunk without wine!

**28:2** Look, strong and harsh is the wrath of the Lord, like hail pouring down with no shelter, pouring down with force; like a great abundance of water sweeping away a region, it will make rest for the earth; with hands

**28:3** and with feet the crown of arrogance will be trampled down, the hired workers of Ephraim.

**28:4** And the flower fallen from the hope of life on the top of the high mountain will be like an early fig; the one who sees it, before taking it into his hand, will want to swallow it.

**28:5** In that day the Lord Sabaoth will be the crown of hope, woven of glory, for the remnant of the people;

**28:6** they will be left with a spirit of judgment for judgment and strength, restraining from killing.

**28:7** For these have erred with wine; they went astray because of strong drink, priest and prophet were beside themselves because of strong drink, they were swallowed up because of wine, they were shaken from drunkenness, they went astray; this is a vision.

**28:8** A curse will devour this counsel; for this counsel is on account of greed.

**28:9** To whom have we announced evil things, and to whom have we announced a message? Those who have been weaned from milk, those who have been drawn away from the breast.

**28:10** Expect affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little while, yet a little while,

**28:11** on account of contempt of lips, through another tongue, for they will speak to this people,

**28:12** saying to them, "This is the rest for the one who is hungry, and this is the crushing," and they were not willing to listen.

**28:13** And the oracle of the Lord will be to them: affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little while, yet a little while, so that they may go and fall backward, and they will be crushed and be in danger and be captured.

**28:14** Therefore hear the word of the Lord, afflicted men and rulers of this people who are in Jerusalem,

**28:15** because you said, "We have made a covenant with Hades, and with death an agreement; if a rushing storm passes through, it will certainly not come upon us; we have made falsehood our hope, and by falsehood we will be sheltered."

**28:16** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: "Look, I am placing into the foundations of Zion a costly, chosen, honored cornerstone into her foundations, and the one who believes will certainly not be put to shame.

**28:17** And I will set judgment for hope, and My mercy for scales, and those who trust in vain in falsehood; that a storm may not sweep over you,

**28:18** nor also take away your covenant of death, and your hope toward Hades will certainly not remain; if a rushing storm comes upon you, you will be trampled down by it.

**28:19** Whenever it passes through, it will take you; morning by morning it will pass through by day, and in the night there will be an evil hope."

**28:20** Learn to listen, you who are hemmed in: we are not able to fight, and we ourselves are too weak for us to be gathered together.

**28:21** Like a mountain of the ungodly the Lord will rise up, and He will be in the valley of Gibeon; with wrath He will do His works, a work of bitterness, and His wrath will deal strangely, and His rottenness is strange.

**28:22** And as for you, may you not be glad, nor let your bonds grow strong; because completed and cut-short matters I have heard from the Lord Sabaoth, which He will do upon all the earth.

**28:23** Give ear and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my words.

**28:24** Will the one who plows plow all day long? Or will he prepare seed before working the ground?

**28:25** When he has leveled its surface, does he not then sow a little black cumin and cumin, and again sow wheat, barley, and millet, and spelt in your borders?

**28:26** And you will be instructed by the judgment of God, and you will be glad.

**28:27** For black cumin is not cleaned with harshness, nor will a wagon wheel roll over the cumin; but black cumin is shaken with a rod, and the cumin

**28:28** will be eaten with bread. For I will not be angry with you forever, nor will the voice of My bitterness trample you down.

**28:29** And these wonders have gone forth from the Lord Sabaoth; take counsel, lift up vain comfort.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The LXX reads "hired workers of Ephraim" (οἱ μισθωτοὶ Ἐφράιμ) and "drunk without wine" (οἱ μεθύοντες ἄνευ οἴνου), whereas the MT has "drunkards of Ephraim" and "overcome with wine."
- **28:5** — "Sabaoth" (σαβαώθ, also in 28:22, 28:29, 29:6) retains the Greek transliteration of the Hebrew divine title meaning "of hosts" or "of armies."
- **28:8** — The LXX differs substantially from the MT ("all tables are full of vomit and filth"), reading instead "A curse will devour this counsel; for this counsel is on account of greed" (and similarly "afflicted men" in v. 14 instead of "scoffers").
- **28:16** — The double title κύριος Κύριος ("the Lord, the Lord") reflects the Greek literal rendering of the Hebrew Adonai YHWH; unlike NT citations (Rom 9:33; 1 Pet 2:6), LXX Isaiah has no pronoun ("on it" or "on Him") after "the one who believes."
- **28:20** — In vv. 20–21 and 27–28, the LXX departs markedly from the MT, speaking of being "too weak to be gathered together" (v. 20), "a mountain of the ungodly" and "His rottenness" (v. 21), and "cumin will be eaten with bread" followed by God's promise not to be angry forever (vv. 27–28).
