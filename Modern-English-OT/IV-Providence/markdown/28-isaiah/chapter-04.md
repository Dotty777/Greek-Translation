## Text

**4:1** And seven women will take hold of one man, saying, "We will eat our own bread and put on our own garments; only let your name be called upon us; take away our reproach."

**4:2** But in that day God will shine forth in counsel with glory upon the earth, to exalt and glorify what is left of Israel;

**4:3** and it will be that what remains in Zion and what is left in Jerusalem—all who have been written for life in Jerusalem will be called holy.

**4:4** For the Lord will wash away the filth of the sons and of the daughters of Zion, and will cleanse the blood from their midst by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.

**4:5** And He will come, and it will be that a cloud will overshadow every place of Mount Zion and all things around it by day, and as of smoke and of light of a fire burning by night, and with all the glory it will be sheltered;

**4:6** and it will be for a shade from heat, and in shelter and in a hiding place from severity and rain.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Instead of the Masoretic Text's 'branch of the Lord,' the LXX reads 'God will shine forth in counsel with glory,' and adds 'to exalt and glorify' before 'what is left of Israel.'
- **4:4** — The LXX includes 'the sons and' alongside 'the daughters of Zion,' where the Masoretic Text mentions only the daughters.
- **4:5** — Reading 'He will come' (ἥξει) rather than 'will create,' and 'with all the glory it will be sheltered' (σκεπασθήσεται) rather than 'over all the glory will be a canopy,' the LXX preserves a distinct prophetic sequence.
