## Text

**21:1** The vision of the wilderness. As a storm might pass through a wilderness, coming from a wilderness, out of a land. Fearful

**21:2** is the vision, and harsh, that was announced to me. The treacherous one acts treacherously, the lawless one acts lawlessly. Against me are the Elamites, and the envoys of the Persians are coming against me. Now I will groan and comfort myself.

**21:3** Because of this my loins were filled with faintness, and birth pangs seized me like a woman giving birth; I did wrong not to hear, I hurried not to see.

**21:4** My heart wanders, and lawlessness overwhelms me; my soul stands in fear.

**21:5** Prepare the table, eat, drink; rise up, you rulers, prepare shields,

**21:6** for thus the Lord said to me: "Go, station a lookout for yourself, and whatever you see, announce."

**21:7** And I saw two mounted horsemen, and a rider of a donkey, and a rider of a camel. Listen with great attention,

**21:8** and call Uriah to the lookout post. The Lord said, "I stood continually by day, and at the camp I stood all the night,

**21:9** and look, he himself is coming, a rider of a two-horse chariot." And answering he said, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon, and all her statues and her handmade things were shattered to the ground."

**21:10** Hear, you who are left behind and you who are in pain, hear what I heard from the Lord Sabaoth; the God of Israel has announced it to us.

**21:11** The vision of Idumea. To me someone calls from Seir, "Guard the battlements."

**21:12** I guard morning and night; if you seek, seek, and dwell with me;

**21:13** in the forest at evening you will sleep, on the road of Dedan.

**21:14** To meet one who is thirsty, bring water, you who live in the land of Teman; with loaves, meet those who are fleeing

**21:15** because of the multitude of those killed, and because of the multitude of those wandering, and because of the multitude of the sword, and because of the multitude of the drawn bows, and because of the multitude of those who have fallen in the war.

**21:16** Because thus the Lord said to me: "Yet a year, like a year of a hired worker, the glory of the sons of Kedar will fail,

**21:17** and the remainder of the bows of the mighty sons of Kedar will be small," for the Lord, the God of Israel, has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Greek reads 'the envoys of the Persians' (hoi presbeis ton Person) where the Hebrew MT has 'besiege, O Media,' apparently reading Hebrew sir ('envoy') instead of sur ('besiege').
- **21:8** — Greek uniquely reads 'call Uriah (Oureian) to the lookout post' and 'The Lord said,' whereas the Hebrew MT reads 'and he cried, A lion: upon the watchtower, O Lord, I stand.'
- **21:10** — Greek transliterates Hebrew sebaot ('hosts/armies') as the title 'Sabaoth' (Sabaoth) throughout this section, and addresses 'you who are left behind and you who are in pain' (MT: 'my threshed one and son of my threshing floor').
- **21:11** — Greek identifies the oracle as 'of Idumea' (Edom) rather than MT 'Dumah' and translates 'Guard the battlements' rather than MT 'Watchman, what of the night?'
