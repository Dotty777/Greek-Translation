## Text

**8:1** And the Lord said to me, "Take for yourself a new, large scroll, and write on it with a man's stylus, 'To swiftly make plunder of spoils'—for it is at hand—

**8:2** and make faithful men witnesses for Me, Uriah and Zechariah son of Berechiah."

**8:3** And I approached the prophetess, and she conceived in the womb and gave birth to a son. And the Lord said to me, "Call his name 'Quickly Spoil, Swiftly Plunder';

**8:4** for before the child knows how to call out father or mother, one will take the wealth of Damascus and the spoils of Samaria before the king of the Assyrians."

**8:5** And the Lord spoke to me yet again:

**8:6** "Because this people does not want the water of Siloam that flows quietly, but wants to have Rezin and the son of Remaliah as king over you,

**8:7** therefore look, the Lord is bringing up upon you the strong and abundant water of the river, the king of the Assyrians and his glory; and he will go up over every ravine of yours, and he will walk over every wall of yours,

**8:8** and he will take away from Judea any man who will be able to lift a head or is able to accomplish anything; and his camp will be such as to fill the breadth of your country. God is with us."

**8:9** Know, nations, and be defeated; give ear as far as the end of the earth; having grown strong, be defeated; for if you grow strong again, again you will be defeated.

**8:10** And whatever plan you plan, the Lord will scatter, and whatever word you speak will by no means remain with you, because God is with us.

**8:11** Thus says the Lord: "With the strong hand they refuse the course of the way of this people, saying,

**8:12** 'May they never say anything hard, for everything that this people says is hard; yet by no means fear their fear, nor be troubled.

**8:13** Sanctify the Lord Himself, and He Himself will be your fear.

**8:14** And if you are trusting in Him, He will be a sanctuary to you, and you will not encounter Him as a stumbling of a stone, nor as a falling of a rock. But the houses of Jacob are in a snare, and sitting in a hollow in Jerusalem;

**8:15** therefore many among them will be powerless, and they will fall and be shattered, and people in security will draw near and be captured.'"

**8:16** Then those who seal up the law so as not to learn will be revealed.

**8:17** And he will say, "I will wait for God, who has turned His face away from the house of Jacob, and I will trust in Him.

**8:18** Here I am, and the children whom God has given me. And there will be signs and wonders in the house of Israel from the Lord Sabaoth, who dwells on Mount Zion."

**8:19** And if they say to you, "Seek the mediums and those who speak from the ground, the empty talkers who speak out of the belly"—will not a nation seek out its God? Why do they seek out the dead concerning the living?

**8:20** For He gave a law for help, so that they might speak not like this word, concerning which there are no gifts to give for it.

**8:21** And a severe famine will come upon you, and it will happen when you grow hungry, you will be grieved and speak evil of the ruler and the ancestral customs, and they will look up to heaven above,

**8:22** and look down at the earth below; and look, narrow perplexity and darkness, affliction and distress and darkness so as not to see, and the one who is in distress will not be at a loss until a time.

**8:23** Drink this first, act quickly, country of Zebulun, the land of Naphtali, and the rest, those along the seacoast and beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Greek uses the genitive "of a new, large [sheet]" (τόμον καινοῦ μεγάλου) and translates the symbolic name Maher-shalal-hash-baz in 8:1 and 8:3 rather than transliterating it; in 8:2, Zechariah's father is Berechiah (MT: Jeberechiah).
- **8:6** — Greek reads "wants to have Rezin and the son of Remaliah as king over you," diverging from the MT ("rejoices in Rezin and the son of Remaliah").
- **8:11** — Greek reads "they refuse the course of the way of this people" and in 8:12 uses "hard" (σκληρόν) where the MT has "conspiracy."
- **8:14** — Greek introduces a condition and negation ("if you are trusting in Him ... you will not encounter Him as a stumbling of a stone"), whereas the MT makes Him a stumbling stone to both houses of Israel.
- **8:23** — Corresponding to 9:1 in Hebrew/English Bibles; Greek begins "Drink this first, act quickly" (MT: "In the former time He brought into contempt") and leaves the participle before the accusative "the seacoast" implied.
