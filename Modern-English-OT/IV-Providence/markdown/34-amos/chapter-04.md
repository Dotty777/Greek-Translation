## Text

**4:1** Hear this word, heifers of Bashan who are on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the poor and trample the needy, who say to their masters, "Give to us so that we may drink."

**4:2** The Lord swears by His holy things: "Look, days are coming upon you, and they will take you with weapons, and fiery pests will throw those with you into cauldrons heated from below,

**4:3** and you will be carried out naked opposite one another, and you will be cast away to Mount Romman," says the Lord.

**4:4** You entered Bethel and acted ungodly, and at Gilgal you multiplied acting ungodly; and you brought your sacrifices in the morning, your tithes for the three-day period;

**4:5** and they read a law outside and called for confessions. Proclaim that the sons of Israel loved these things, says the Lord God.

**4:6** And I will give you aching of teeth in all your cities, and a lack of bread in all your places, and you did not turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**4:7** And I withheld the rain from you three months before the vintage; and I will send rain upon one city, but upon another city I will not send rain; one portion will be rained upon, and a portion upon which I will not send rain will dry up.

**4:8** And two and three cities will gather together into one city to drink water, and they will certainly not be satisfied, and you did not turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**4:9** I struck you with scorching heat and with blight; you multiplied your gardens; the caterpillar devoured your vineyards and your fig groves and your olive groves, and not even so did you turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**4:10** I sent out death among you in the way of Egypt, and I killed your young men with the sword along with the captivity of your horses, and I brought up your camps in fire in your anger, and not even so did you turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**4:11** I overthrew you just as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a firebrand snatched out of fire, and not even so did you turn back to Me, says the Lord.

**4:12** Therefore I will do thus to you, Israel; yet because I will do thus to you, prepare yourself to call upon your God, Israel.

**4:13** For He who establishes thunder and creates wind and declares His anointed to people, who makes dawn and mist, treading upon the heights of the earth: the Lord God Almighty is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Greek τῶν ἁγίων αὐτοῦ is plural and may mean neuter "His holy things" (sanctuary) or masculine "His holy ones," where the Hebrew has "His holiness." The Greek also reads "with weapons" and "fiery pests will throw those with you into cauldrons heated from below," differing markedly from the Hebrew wording.
- **4:3** — Greek Ῥομμάν ("Romman") is a Septuagint-distinct place name where the Masoretic Text has Harmon.
- **4:5** — The Septuagint reads "they read a law outside and called for confessions," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's leavened thank offering and freewill offerings.
- **4:10** — Greek "in fire ... in your anger" differs from the Masoretic Text ("the stench ... into your nostrils") through reading the Hebrew consonants as "fire" and translating the word for "nose/anger" in its sense "anger."
- **4:13** — The Septuagint reads βροντήν ("thunder") where the Hebrew has "mountains," and τὸν χριστὸν αὐτοῦ ("His anointed," also readable as "His Christ") where the Masoretic Text has "what is his thought." Greek πνεῦμα can mean either "wind" or "spirit."
