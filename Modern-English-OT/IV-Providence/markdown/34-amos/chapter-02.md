## Text

**2:1** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of Moab, and for the four, I will not turn away from it, because they burned up the bones of the king of Idumea into lime;

**2:2** and I will send out fire upon Moab, and it will devour the foundations of its cities, and Moab will die in weakness, with a shout and with the sound of a trumpet;

**2:3** and I will utterly destroy a judge from it, and I will kill all who belong to it with him," says the Lord.

**2:4** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of the sons of Judah, and for the four, I will not turn away from him, because they rejected the law of the Lord, and did not keep His ordinances, and their vain things which they made led them astray, after which their fathers followed;

**2:5** and I will send out fire upon Judah, and it will devour the foundations of Jerusalem."

**2:6** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of Israel, and for the four, I will not turn away from him, because they sold a righteous man for silver, and a needy man for sandals,

**2:7** which tread upon the dust of the earth; and they struck the heads of the poor with their fists, and turned aside the way of the humble, and a son and his father went in to the same servant girl, so that they might profane the name of their God;

**2:8** and binding their garments with cords, they made curtains next to the altar, and they drank wine from extortion in the house of their God.

**2:9** Yet I removed the Amorite from before them, whose height was like the height of a cedar, and he was strong as an oak; and I dried up his fruit from above and his roots from below;

**2:10** and I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and I led you around in the wilderness forty years to inherit the land of the Amorites;

**2:11** and I took from your sons for prophets, and from your young men for consecration. Are these things not so, sons of Israel?" says the Lord.

**2:12** "And you gave the consecrated ones wine to drink, and you commanded the prophets, saying, 'You must by no means prophesy.'

**2:13** Therefore, look, I roll beneath you in the way the wagon full of straw rolls;

**2:14** and flight will perish from a runner, and the mighty man will by no means keep hold of his strength, and the warrior will by no means save his life,

**2:15** and the archer will by no means stand firm, and the one swift with his feet will by no means escape safely, nor will the horseman by any means save his life,

**2:16** and the mighty man will by no means find his heart in acts of power; the naked man will pursue on that day," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Greek text reads simply "all who belong to it" (literally "all of hers"), omitting the noun "officials/princes" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **2:7** — In Greek, "which tread upon the dust of the earth" is neuter plural modifying "sandals" at the end of verse 6, followed by "struck the heads of the poor with their fists" and "the name of their God" (Hebrew "My holy name").
- **2:13** — The Greek translates the verb as "roll" ("I roll beneath you in the way the wagon full of straw rolls"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes pressing down or groaning beneath a cart loaded with sheaves.
- **2:16** — The Greek reads "will by no means find his heart in acts of power; the naked man will pursue on that day," using the active future of "pursue" (unlike the Hebrew "the stout of heart among the mighty will flee away naked").
