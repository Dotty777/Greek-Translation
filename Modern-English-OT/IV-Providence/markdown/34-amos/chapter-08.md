## Text

**8:2** and look, a bird-catcher's basket. And He said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A bird-catcher's basket." And the Lord said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will no longer pass them by.

**8:3** And the paneled ceilings of the temple will wail in that day," says the Lord, the Lord. "Many are the fallen in every place; I will cast silence."

**8:4** Hear these things now, you who crush the needy in the morning and oppress the poor from the land,

**8:5** saying, "When will the month pass by so that we may sell, and the Sabbaths so that we may open a storehouse to make a small measure, and to enlarge the weight and make an unjust scale,

**8:6** to acquire even the poor with silver, and a lowly person in exchange for sandals, and from all produce we will trade?"

**8:7** The Lord swears by the arrogance of Jacob: "Surely all your deeds will not be forgotten forever,

**8:8** and because of these things, will the earth not be troubled, and will everyone who dwells in it not mourn? And destruction will rise like a river, and it will go down like the river of Egypt."

**8:9** "And it will happen in that day," says the Lord, the Lord, "that the sun will set at noon, and the light will grow dark upon the earth in the daytime;

**8:10** and I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring sackcloth upon every waist, and baldness upon every head; and I will make him like the mourning for a beloved one, and those with him like a day of pain."

**8:11** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when I will send out a famine upon the land—not a famine of bread nor a thirst for water, but a famine for hearing the word of the Lord.

**8:12** And the waters of the sea will be shaken, and from the north to the east they will run about, seeking the word of the Lord, and they will certainly not find it.

**8:13** In that day the beautiful virgins and the young men will faint from thirst,

**8:14** those who swear by the propitiation of Samaria, and those who say, 'Your God lives, Dan, and your God lives, Beersheba!' And they will fall and never rise again."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Greek 'angos ixeutou' ('a bird-catcher's basket') differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'basket of summer fruit,' which puns on the Hebrew word for 'end.'
- **8:3** — Greek 'phatnomata' ('paneled ceilings') differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'songs,' and the final clause reads a first-person future verb ('I will cast silence').
- **8:7** — The Greek conditional particle 'ei' reflects a Hebrew oath idiom carrying strong negation ('surely ... not'), and 'eis nikos' (lit. 'to victory') is a standard Septuagint idiom meaning 'forever' or 'completely.'
- **8:10** — Greek 'agapetou' ('a beloved one') renders the Hebrew for 'an only son,' and 'those with him' ('tous met' autou') differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'its end.'
- **8:12** — The Greek reads 'the waters of the sea will be shaken,' diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'they shall wander from sea to sea.'
