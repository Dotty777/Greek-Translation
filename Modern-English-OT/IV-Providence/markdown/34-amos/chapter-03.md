## Text

**3:1** Hear this word that the Lord spoke against you, house of Israel, and against every tribe that I brought up out of the land of Egypt, saying,

**3:2** "Only you have I known out of all the tribes of the earth; therefore I will execute vengeance upon you for all your sins."

**3:3** Will two walk together at all unless they become acquainted with one another?

**3:4** Will a lion roar from its forest when it has no prey? Will a young lion give its voice from its den at all unless it has seized something?

**3:5** Will a bird fall to the ground without a bird-catcher? Will a trap be sprung upon the ground without catching anything?

**3:6** Will a trumpet sound in a city and people not be terrified? Will there be evil in a city that the Lord has not done?

**3:7** For the Lord God will by no means do a thing unless He reveals instruction to His servants the prophets.

**3:8** A lion will roar, and who will not fear? The Lord God has spoken, and who will not prophesy?

**3:9** "Proclaim to the regions among the Assyrians and over the regions of Egypt, and say, 'Gather together upon the mountain of Samaria, and see many astonishing things in her midst and the oppression that is in her.'

**3:10** And she did not know what will be before her," says the Lord, "those who store up injustice and misery in their regions."

**3:11** Therefore, thus says the Lord God: "Tyre, your land will be laid waste all around, and he will bring down your strength from you, and your regions will be plundered."

**3:12** Thus says the Lord: "Just as when the shepherd snatches from the mouth of the lion two legs or a lobe of an ear, so the sons of Israel who dwell in Samaria will be snatched out opposite the tribe, and priests in Damascus."

**3:13** "Hear and testify against the house of Jacob," says the Lord God Almighty,

**3:14** "for on the day when I execute vengeance for the impieties of Israel upon him, I will also execute vengeance upon the altars of Bethel, and the horns of the altar will be torn down and will fall to the ground;

**3:15** I will demolish and strike the colonnaded house upon the summer house, and ivory houses will perish, and many other houses will be added," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek reads "unless they become acquainted with one another" (based on the Hebrew root for "know") rather than "unless they have agreed to meet," and in verse 7 reads "reveals instruction" where the Hebrew has "reveals His secret counsel."
- **3:9** — The Greek reads "among the Assyrians" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "in Ashdod"), "many astonishing things," and in verse 10 "she did not know what will be before her" (Hebrew "they do not know how to do right").
- **3:11** — The Greek reads the proper name "Tyre" (interpreting the Hebrew word for "adversary" as the city name), leaving the subject of "he will bring down" unexpressed.
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "opposite the tribe, and priests in Damascus," interpreting the Hebrew terms for a couch and damask bed as "tribe," "Damascus," and "priests."
- **3:15** — The Greek reads "demolish and strike the colonnaded house" (Hebrew "winter house") and "many other houses will be added" (reading the Hebrew verb as "be added" rather than "come to an end").
