## Text

**1:1** The words of Amos, which came to be in Akkarim in Tekoa, which he saw concerning Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**1:2** And he said, "The Lord sounded forth from Zion, and from Jerusalem He gave His voice; and the pastures of the shepherds mourned, and the summit of Carmel dried up."

**1:3** And the Lord said, "For the three impieties of Damascus, and for the four, I will not turn away from it, because they sawed with iron saws the pregnant women of those in Gilead;

**1:4** and I will send out fire into the house of Hazael, and it will devour the foundations of the son of Hader;

**1:5** and I will shatter the bars of Damascus, and I will utterly destroy the inhabitants from the plain of On, and I will cut down a tribe from the men of Haran, and the chosen people of Syria will be taken captive," says the Lord.

**1:6** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of Gaza, and for the four, I will not turn away from them, because they took captive the captives of Solomon, to shut them up into Idumea;

**1:7** and I will send out fire upon the walls of Gaza, and it will devour its foundations.

**1:8** And I will utterly destroy the inhabitants from Ashdod, and a tribe will be removed from Ashkelon, and I will bring My hand upon Ekron, and the rest of the foreigners will perish," says the Lord.

**1:9** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of Tyre, and for the four, I will not turn away from it, because they shut up the captives of Solomon into Idumea, and did not remember a covenant of brothers;

**1:10** and I will send out fire upon the walls of Tyre, and it will devour its foundations."

**1:11** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of Idumea, and for the four, I will not turn away from them, because they pursued his brother with a sword, and he ravaged a mother upon the earth, and he seized his horror for a testimony, and he kept his onslaught for victory;

**1:12** and I will send out fire into Teman, and it will devour the foundations of its walls."

**1:13** Thus says the Lord: "For the three impieties of the sons of Ammon, and for the four, I will not turn away from him, because they ripped open the pregnant women of the Gileadites, so that they might enlarge their own borders;

**1:14** and I will kindle fire upon the walls of Rabbah, and it will devour its foundations with a shout in the day of war, and it will be shaken in the days of its end;

**1:15** and its kings will go in captivity, their priests and their rulers together," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text reads "which came to be in Akkarim" (transliterating the Hebrew word for "shepherds" as a place name) and "concerning Jerusalem," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "who was among the shepherds" and "concerning Israel."
- **1:3** — Throughout chapters 1-2, the Greek formula "I will not turn away from it/him/them" renders the Hebrew expression for not revoking punishment, and here specifies sawing "the pregnant women of those in Gilead" with iron saws (compare Hebrew "threshed Gilead with iron sledges").
- **1:5** — The Greek preserves distinct proper names and readings here and in verse 4: "son of Hader" (Hebrew "Ben-hadad"), "plain of On" (Hebrew "Valley of Aven"), "a tribe from the men of Haran" (Hebrew "the scepter-bearer from Beth-eden"), and "the chosen people of Syria" (Hebrew "to Kir").
- **1:6** — Here and in verse 9, the Greek reads "captives of Solomon" (reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew for "a whole captivity") and uses the Greek territorial name "Idumea" for Edom; in verse 8, "foreigners" is the Septuagint's standard rendering for the Philistines.
- **1:11** — The Greek diverges sharply from the Hebrew, reading "ravaged a mother upon the earth," "seized his horror for a testimony," and "kept his onslaught for victory," followed in verse 12 by "the foundations of its walls" (where the Hebrew has "the citadels of Bozrah").
