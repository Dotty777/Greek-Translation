## Text

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing on the altar, and He said, "Strike upon the mercy seat, and the gateways will be shaken, and cut through onto the heads of all; and the remnant of them I will kill with the sword. No one who flees from among them will escape, and no survivor from among them will be saved.

**9:2** If they hide themselves in Hades, from there My hand will pull them up; and if they go up into heaven, from there I will bring them down;

**9:3** if they hide themselves on the peak of Carmel, from there I will search out and take them; and if they sink down from My eyes into the depths of the sea, there I will command the dragon, and it will bite them;

**9:4** and if they go in captivity before the face of their enemies, there I will command the sword, and it will kill them; and I will fix My eyes upon them for evil and not for good."

**9:5** And the Lord, the Lord God Almighty, the One who touches the earth and shakes it, and all who dwell in it will mourn; and its destruction will rise like a river, and it will go down like the river of Egypt;

**9:6** the One who builds His ascent into heaven, and founds His promise upon the earth, the One who summons the water of the sea and pours it out upon the face of the earth; the Lord Almighty is His name.

**9:7** "Are you not like the sons of the Ethiopians to Me, sons of Israel?" says the Lord. "Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, and the foreigners from Cappadocia, and the Syrians from a pit?"

**9:8** "Look, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the kingdom of the sinners, and I will remove it from the face of the earth; yet I will not completely remove the house of Jacob," says the Lord.

**9:9** "For I am commanding, and I will winnow the house of Israel among all the nations, just as winnowing is done in a winnowing basket, and not a fragment will fall to the ground.

**9:10** By the sword all the sinners of My people will die, those who say, 'Evils will certainly not draw near nor come upon us.'"

**9:11** "In that day I will raise up the tent of David that has fallen, and I will rebuild its fallen parts, and I will raise up its ruins, and I will rebuild it just as in the ancient days,

**9:12** so that the remnant of mankind may seek, and all the nations upon whom My name has been called," says the Lord who does these things.

**9:13** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when the harvest will overtake the vintage, and the grape will ripen at sowing time, and the mountains will drip sweetness, and all the hills will be thickly wooded;

**9:14** and I will bring back the captivity of My people Israel, and they will build the destroyed cities and dwell in them, and they will plant a vineyard and drink their wine, and they will plant gardens and eat their fruits;

**9:15** and I will plant them upon their land, and they will never again be uprooted from the land that I have given them," says the Lord God Almighty.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Greek 'hilasterion' ('mercy seat', the term used for the lid of the ark) replaces the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'capital' of a pillar.
- **9:6** — Greek 'epangelian' ('promise') diverges from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'vault' or 'band.'
- **9:7** — The Greek uses 'foreigners' ('allophyloi', its standard rendering for the Philistines), 'Cappadocia' (for Hebrew Caphtor), and 'from a pit' (for Hebrew 'from Kir').
- **9:12** — The Greek reads 'so that the remnant of mankind may seek' (reflecting the text quoted in Acts 15:17, without an explicit object after 'seek' here), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'that they may possess the remnant of Edom.'
- **9:13** — The Greek reads 'the harvest will overtake the vintage, and the grape will ripen at sowing time' and 'thickly wooded' ('symphytoi'), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's imagery of the plowman, grape-treader, and melting hills.
