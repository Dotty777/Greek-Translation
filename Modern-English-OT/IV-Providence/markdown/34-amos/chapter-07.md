## Text

**7:1** Thus the Lord God showed me, and look, a brood of locusts coming at dawn, and look, one wingless locust, Gog the King.

**7:2** And it will be, if it finishes devouring the grass of the land—and I said, "Lord, Lord, be merciful! Who will raise up Jacob? For he is very small;

**7:3** relent, Lord, concerning this." "This also will not be," says the Lord.

**7:4** Thus the Lord showed me, and look, the Lord summoned judgment by fire, and it devoured the great abyss, and it devoured the portion of the Lord.

**7:5** And I said, "Lord, please cease! Who will raise up Jacob? For he is very small;

**7:6** relent, Lord, concerning this." "This also will certainly not happen," says the Lord.

**7:7** Thus the Lord showed me, and look, He was standing on a wall of adamant, and in His hand was adamant.

**7:8** And the Lord said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "Adamant." And the Lord said to me, "Look, I am setting adamant in the midst of My people Israel; I will never again pass them by;

**7:9** and the altars of laughter will be destroyed, and the rites of Israel will be laid waste, and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

**7:10** And Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, "Amos is forming conspiracies against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land will not be able to bear all his words.

**7:11** For thus says Amos: 'By the sword Jeroboam will die, and Israel will be led captive from its land.'"

**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, go, depart into the land of Judah, and live out your life there, and there you will prophesy;

**7:13** but at Bethel you will no longer prophesy, for it is a king's sanctuary, and it is a house of the kingdom."

**7:14** And Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I was not a prophet, nor a son of a prophet; rather, I was a goatherd and one who nips sycamore figs.

**7:15** And the Lord took me from the sheep, and the Lord said to me, 'Go, prophesy to My people Israel.'

**7:16** And now, hear the word of the Lord: You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and you must never stir up a crowd against the house of Jacob.'

**7:17** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Your wife will become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword, and your land will be measured out with a cord, and you yourself will die in an unclean land, and Israel will be led captive from its land.'" Thus the Lord, the Lord showed me,

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek reads 'Gog the King' for the leading wingless locust, diverging sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'the king's mowings.'
- **7:3** — Here (and again in 7:6) the Greek has an imperative plea from Amos, 'relent, Lord, concerning this,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a narrative statement that the Lord relented.
- **7:7** — Greek 'adamas' ('adamant', the hardest metal or stone) corresponds to the rare Hebrew term usually rendered 'plumb line' or 'tin.'
- **7:9** — The Greek translates the Hebrew name Isaac literally as 'laughter' ('altars of laughter') and renders 'sanctuaries' as 'rites' ('teletai').
- **7:17** — The title 'the Lord, the Lord' renders the doubled Greek 'kyrios Kyrios.' The final clause ('Thus the Lord, the Lord showed me,') begins the fourth vision—usually numbered 8:1—so chapter 8 begins at 8:2 in this Greek text.
