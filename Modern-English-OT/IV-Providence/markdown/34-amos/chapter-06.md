## Text

**6:1** Woe to those who despise Zion and to those who trust in the mountain of Samaria! They harvested the foremost of the nations, and they themselves entered. House of Israel,

**6:2** cross over, all of you, and see, and pass through from there to Hamath Rabbah, and go down from there to Gath of the foreigners, the mightiest of all these kingdoms, whether their borders are greater than your borders.

**6:3** Those who come to an evil day, who draw near and take hold of false sabbaths,

**6:4** who sleep on ivory beds and live in luxury on their couches, and eat young goats from flocks and suckling calves from the midst of herds,

**6:5** who clap to the sound of the instruments; they reckoned them as enduring and not as fleeting;

**6:6** who drink strained wine and anoint themselves with the finest perfumes, and suffered nothing over the ruin of Joseph.

**6:7** Therefore now they will be captives at the head of the rulers, and the neighing of horses will be removed from Ephraim.

**6:8** For the Lord swore by Himself: "I abhor all the arrogance of Jacob, and I have hated his lands, and I will remove a city with all who inhabit it."

**6:9** And it will be that if ten are left in one house, they will die, and the remaining ones will be left,

**6:10** and their relatives will take them and strive hard to carry their bones out of the house; and he will say to those in charge of the house, "Is there still anyone with you?" And he will say, "No longer." And he will say, "Be silent, so as not to name the name of the Lord."

**6:11** For look, the Lord commands, and He will strike the great house with fractures and the small house with cracks.

**6:12** Will horses race on rocks? Will they keep silent among mares? Yet you turned justice into fury, and the fruit of righteousness into bitterness,

**6:13** you who rejoice over nothing, who say, "Did we not gain horns by our strength?"

**6:14** For look, I am raising up a nation against you, house of Israel, says the Lord of armies, and they will oppress you so that you cannot enter Hamath, and as far as the torrent of the west.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Greek πάντες ("all of you") stands where the Masoretic Text has the city Calneh, and Ἑμὰθ Ῥαββά transliterates "Hamath Rabbah" (Hebrew "Hamath the great"). Greek ἀλλοφύλων ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines.
- **6:3** — Greek "who come to an evil day" and "false sabbaths" (σαββάτων ψευδῶν) differ from the Masoretic Text ("who put far away the day of disaster" and "the seat of violence").
- **6:7** — Greek "the neighing of horses will be removed from Ephraim" differs sharply from the Masoretic Text ("the revelry of those who stretch out shall pass away").
- **6:12** — Greek εἰ παρασιωπήσονται ἐν θηλείαις ("Will they keep silent among mares/females?") reflects reading the Hebrew root as "be silent" rather than "plow" (Masoretic Text: "or does one plow there with oxen?").
- **6:14** — Greek "so that you cannot enter Hamath" and "the torrent of the west" (δυσμῶν) translate the Hebrew place expressions Lebo-hamath and the Brook of the Arabah as descriptive phrases, with ὡς functioning for ἕως ("as far as").
