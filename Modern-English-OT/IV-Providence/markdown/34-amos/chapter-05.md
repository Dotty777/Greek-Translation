## Text

**5:1** Hear this word of the Lord which I take up over you as a lamentation: "The house of Israel

**5:2** has fallen; it will never rise again; the virgin of Israel has stumbled upon his land; there is no one who will raise her up."

**5:3** Therefore, this is what the Lord, the Lord says: "The city from which a thousand went out will have a hundred left, and the one from which a hundred went out will have ten left for the house of Israel."

**5:4** For this is what the Lord says to the house of Israel: "Seek Me, and you will live;

**5:5** and do not seek Bethel, and do not enter Gilgal, and do not cross over to the Well of the Oath, because Gilgal will surely be taken captive, and Bethel will be as though it does not exist."

**5:6** Seek the Lord and live, so that the house of Joseph may not blaze up like fire and devour it, and there will be no one to quench it for the house of Israel.

**5:7** He who does justice on high, and placed righteousness on the earth;

**5:8** He who makes all things and transforms them, and turns shadow into the morning, and darkens day into night; He who summons the water of the sea and pours it out upon the face of the earth, the Lord is His name;

**5:9** He who apportions ruin upon strength, and brings misery upon a fortress.

**5:10** They hated one who reproves in the gates, and they abhorred a devout word.

**5:11** Therefore, because they were beating the poor with fists, and you accepted choice gifts from them, you built houses of hewn stone, yet you will certainly not dwell in them; you planted desirable vineyards, yet you will certainly not drink the wine from them.

**5:12** For I know your many acts of ungodliness, and your sins are strong, trampling a righteous person, taking bribes and turning aside the needy in the gates.

**5:13** Therefore the one who understands will keep silent in that time, because it is a time of evils.

**5:14** Seek good and not evil, so that you may live, and thus the Lord God Almighty will be with you; just as you said,

**5:15** "We have hated evil things and loved good things," so restore justice in the gates, so that the Lord God Almighty may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.

**5:16** Therefore thus says the Lord God Almighty: "In all the squares there will be wailing, and in all the streets it will be said, 'Woe, woe!' A farmer will be called to mourning and wailing, and to those who know lamentation,

**5:17** and in all streets there will be wailing, because I will pass through your midst," said the Lord.

**5:18** Woe to you who desire the day of the Lord! Why is this day of the Lord for you? And it is darkness and not light,

**5:19** just as when a man flees from before a lion and a bear falls upon him, and he leaps into his house and leans his hands against the wall, and a snake bites him.

**5:20** Is not the day of the Lord darkness and not light? And is it not gloom having no brightness?

**5:21** I have hated, I have rejected your festivals, and I will by no means smell sacrifices in your festive assemblies.

**5:22** For if you bring Me burnt offerings and your sacrifices, I will not accept them, and I will not look upon your displays of deliverance.

**5:23** Remove from Me the sound of your songs, and I will not listen to the music of your instruments;

**5:24** and justice will roll like water, and righteousness like an impassable torrent.

**5:25** Did you offer Me slain beasts and sacrifices in the wilderness for forty years, house of Israel?

**5:26** And you took up the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Rephan, their images which you made for yourselves;

**5:27** and I will deport you beyond Damascus, says the Lord; God Almighty is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — Greek κύριος Κύριος ("the Lord, the Lord") literally reflects the double Hebrew divine title Adonai YHWH.
- **5:5** — Greek τὸ φρέαρ τοῦ ὅρκου ("the Well of the Oath") is the Septuagint's literal translation of the place name Beersheba.
- **5:7** — The Septuagint renders this verse in the singular as part of the hymn to God (continuing into 5:8-9) and reads "on high" (εἰς ὕψος) where the Masoretic Text addresses human oppressors turning justice "to wormwood." In 5:8 the Greek likewise has "all things and transforms them" where the Hebrew names Pleiades and Orion.
- **5:15** — The Septuagint links "just as you said" at the end of 5:14 to first-person plural perfects here ("We have hated ... and loved"), quoting Israel's claim rather than issuing imperatives as in the Masoretic Text.
- **5:26** — The Septuagint reads "the tent of Moloch" and "the star of your god Rephan" (Greek Ῥαιφάν), a well-known Septuagint reading quoted in Acts 7:43 where the Masoretic Text has Sikkuth and Kiyyun.
