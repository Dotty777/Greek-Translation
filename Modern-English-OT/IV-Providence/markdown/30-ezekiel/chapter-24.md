## Text

**24:1** And the word of the Lord came to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, saying,

**24:2** "Son of man, write for yourself for a day from this day, from which the king of Babylon pressed against Jerusalem, from this very day;

**24:3** and speak a parable against the provoking house, and you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Set the cauldron on, and pour water into it,

**24:4** and throw into it the cut pieces, every good cut piece, thigh and shoulder, stripped from the bones,

**24:5** taken from choice livestock, and burn the bones underneath them. It boiled, and her bones have been cooked in her midst.

**24:6** Therefore thus says the Lord: O city of bloodshed, cauldron in which there is rust, and the rust did not go out from her; piece by piece of her she brought out; no lot fell upon her.

**24:7** For her bloodshed is in her midst; upon a smooth rock I have set it; I have not poured it out upon the earth to cover it with earth,

**24:8** so that wrath may rise up for vengeance, to be avenged; I have placed her blood upon a smooth rock, so as not to cover it.

**24:9** Therefore thus says the Lord: I also will make the people great,

**24:10** and I will multiply the wood and kindle the fire, so that the meat may melt away and the broth may be reduced

**24:11** and she may stand upon the coals, so that her bronze may be scorched and heated and melt in the midst of her uncleanness, and her rust may disappear,

**24:12** and her abundant rust will by no means go out from her; her rust will be put to shame,

**24:13** because you yourself were defiling yourself. And what if you are not cleansed any longer until I satisfy My wrath?

**24:14** I, the Lord, have spoken, and it will come, and I will act; I will not hold back nor will I ever have mercy. According to your ways and according to your devices I will judge you, says the Lord. Therefore I will judge you according to your bloodshed, and according to your devices I will judge you, unclean one, renowned and great in provoking.'"

**24:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:16** "Son of man, look, I am taking from you the desires of your eyes in battle; you shall by no means lament, nor shall you weep.

**24:17** You will be a groaning of blood, of loins, of mourning; your hair will not be braided upon you, and your sandals will be on your feet; you shall by no means be comforted by their lips, and you shall by no means eat the bread of men."

**24:18** And I spoke to the people in the morning just as He commanded me in the evening, and I did in the morning just as it was ordered to me.

**24:19** And the people said to me, "Will you not tell us what these things are that you are doing?"

**24:20** And I said to them, "The word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:21** 'Say to the house of Israel, Thus says the Lord: Look, I am profaning My sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the desires of your eyes, and the things for which your souls feel pity. And your sons and your daughters whom you left behind will fall by the sword,

**24:22** and you will do just as I have done: you will not be comforted by their mouth, and you will not eat the bread of men,

**24:23** and your hair will be upon your head, and your sandals on your feet; you will neither lament nor weep, and you will melt away in your iniquities, and each one will comfort his brother.

**24:24** And Ezekiel will be a portent to you; according to all that you have done you will do when these things come; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**24:25** "And you, son of man, will it not be on the day when I take the strength from them, the lifting up of their boasting, the desires of their eyes and the lifting up of their soul, their sons and their daughters,

**24:26** on that day the survivor will come to you to report to you in your ears?

**24:27** On that day your mouth will be opened to the survivor; you will speak, and you will never again be mute, and you will be a portent to them; and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **24:9** — The Greek reads "the people" (ton laon), likely an early scribal shift from "the firebrand" (ton dalon), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "the pile."
- **24:12** — The Greek reads "will be put to shame" (kataischynthesetai), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants rendered "in the fire" in the MT.
- **24:17** — The Greek ("a groaning of blood, of loins, of mourning you will be; your hair will not be braided upon you... nor be comforted by their lips") diverges markedly from the Hebrew MT ("groan silently; make no mourning for the dead; bind on your turban... do not cover your mustache"). Greek omits a repeated verb before "and your sandals on your feet," which in context (compare v. 23) stands outside the preceding negation.
- **24:18** — The Greek omits the report of Ezekiel's wife's death found in the Hebrew MT ("and in the evening my wife died"), reading instead "just as He commanded me in the evening."
- **24:24** — The Greek reads second-person plural "you have done" (epoiesate), where the Hebrew MT has third-person singular "he has done."
