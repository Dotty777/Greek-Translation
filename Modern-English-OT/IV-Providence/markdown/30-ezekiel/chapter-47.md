## Text

**47:1** And he brought me to the doorway of the house, and look, water was flowing out from beneath the atrium toward the east, for the front of the house faced east; and the water was flowing down from the right side, from the south, toward the altar.

**47:2** And he brought me out by the way of the gate toward the north, and he led me around by the outside way to the gate of the court facing east, and look, the water was streaming down from the right side,

**47:3** like the going out of a man from the opposite side. And there was a measure in his hand; and he measured a thousand with the measure, and he passed through in the water, water of release;

**47:4** and he measured a thousand, and he passed through in the water, water up to the thighs; and he measured a thousand, and he passed through water up to the waist.

**47:5** And he measured a thousand, and he was not able to pass through, for it was surging wildly, a torrent that they will not cross.

**47:6** And he said to me, "Have you seen, son of man?" And he led me to the bank of the river

**47:7** upon my return; and look, on the bank of the river there were very many trees on this side and on that.

**47:8** And he said to me, "This water that flows out into Galilee toward the east, and went down to Arabia, and came all the way to the sea, to the water of the outlet, and it will heal the waters,

**47:9** and it shall be that every soul of the creatures that swarm, in all places where the river comes there, shall live; and there shall be very many fish there, because this water has come there, and it will heal and live; everything to which the river comes there shall live.

**47:10** And fishermen shall stand there from En-gedi to En-eglaim; it shall be a drying place for dragnets, it shall be by itself; and its fish shall be like the fish of the Great Sea, a very great multitude.

**47:11** And in its outlet and in its turning back and in its overflowing, they shall certainly not heal; they have been given over to salt.

**47:12** And by the river there shall grow up, on its bank on this side and on that, every tree for food; it shall certainly not grow old upon it, nor shall its fruit fail; in its newness it shall bear firstfruits, because their waters, these flow out from the sanctuary; and their fruit shall be for food, and their growth shall be for healing."

**47:13** Thus says the Lord God: "These are the borders of the land that you shall inherit: for the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel, an addition of a measured portion.

**47:14** And you shall inherit it, each one just like his brother, concerning which I lifted up My hand to give it to their fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

**47:15** And these are the borders of the land toward the north: from the Great Sea that goes down and splits around, the entrance of Hamath, Seldamma,

**47:16** Abthera, Sibraim, Heliam, between the borders of Damascus and the borders of Hamath, the court of Saunan, which are above the borders of Auranitis.

**47:17** These are the borders from the sea: from the court of Enan, the borders of Damascus, and the parts toward the north.

**47:18** And the parts toward the east: between Loranitis and Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel, the Jordan marks the boundary to the sea east of Palm Grove; these are the parts toward the east.

**47:19** And the parts toward the south and southwest: from Teman and Palm Grove, to the water of Marimoth-kadem, extending along to the Great Sea;

**47:20** this part is south and southwest. This part of the Great Sea marks the boundary, as far as opposite the entrance of Hamath, as far as its entrance; these are the parts toward the sea of Hamath.

**47:21** And you shall divide this land among them, the tribes of Israel.

**47:22** You shall cast it by lot for yourselves and for the proselytes who reside in your midst, who have fathered sons in your midst, and they shall be yours like native-born among the sons of Israel; with you they shall eat in an inheritance in the midst of the tribes of Israel,

**47:23** and they shall be in a tribe of proselytes, among the proselytes who are with them; there you shall give them an inheritance," says the Lord God.

## Translator Notes

- **47:3** — In vv. 3-5 the Greek verbs describe the guide himself passing through the stream rather than leading Ezekiel through, and read "water of release" (Greek apheseos) here and "up to the thighs" in v. 4 (where the Hebrew MT has "ankle-deep" and "knee-deep").
- **47:8** — The Greek renders the geographical terms as the proper names "Galilee" and "Arabia" (corresponding to "the eastern region" and "the Arabah" in the Hebrew MT).
- **47:12** — The Greek reads "it shall certainly not grow old," "in its newness it shall bear firstfruits," and "their growth shall be for healing" (where the Hebrew MT speaks of leaves not withering, bearing fruit every month, and leaves for healing).
- **47:13** — The Greek reads "an addition of a measured portion," taking the Hebrew name Yosef ("Joseph") as the verb "to add" (where the Hebrew MT reads "Joseph shall have two portions").
- **47:16** — The Greek border description in vv. 15-20 preserves several Septuagint-distinct place names and renderings—including Seldamma (v. 15), Abthera, Heliam, the court of Saunan, Auranitis (v. 16), Loranitis and Palm Grove (Greek Phoinikon, rendering Tamar in vv. 18-19), and Marimoth-kadem (v. 19)—and vv. 22-23 read "they shall eat in an inheritance" and "in a tribe of proselytes."
