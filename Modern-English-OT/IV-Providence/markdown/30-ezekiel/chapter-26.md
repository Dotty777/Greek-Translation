## Text

**26:1** And it happened in the eleventh year, on the first of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**26:2** "Son of man, because Tyre said against Jerusalem, 'Aha! She has been shattered; the nations have perished; she has turned to me; she who was full has been laid desolate!'

**26:3** therefore thus says the Lord: Look, I am against you, Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea rises up with its waves.

**26:4** And they will throw down the walls of Tyre, and they will throw down your towers, and I will winnow her dust from her, and I will make her into a smooth rock;

**26:5** she will be a drying place for dragnets in the midst of the sea, for I have spoken, says the Lord. And she will be for plunder to the nations,

**26:6** and her daughters in the plain will be killed by the sword; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**26:7** For thus says the Lord: Look, I am bringing against you, Tyre, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the north; he is king of kings, with horses and chariots and horsemen and an assembly of very many nations.

**26:8** He will kill your daughters who are in the plain by the sword, and he will set an outpost against you and build around you, and he will make a palisade all around against you and a ring of weapons, and he will set his spears opposite you;

**26:9** your walls and your towers he will throw down with his swords.

**26:10** From the multitude of his horses their dust will cover you, and from the sound of his horsemen and of the wheels of his chariots your walls will shake, as he enters your gates like one entering a city from a plain.

**26:11** With the hooves of his horses they will trample all your streets; he will kill your people with the sword, and he will bring down the foundation of your strength to the ground.

**26:12** And he will plunder your wealth, and he will loot your possessions, and he will throw down your walls and tear down your desirable houses, and your stones and your timber and your dust he will throw into the midst of your sea.

**26:13** And he will put an end to the multitude of your musicians, and the sound of your harps will by no means be heard any longer.

**26:14** And I will make you a smooth rock; you will be a drying place for dragnets; you will never be built again, for I, the Lord, have spoken, says the Lord.

**26:15** For thus says the Lord, the Lord, to Tyre: At the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when a sword is drawn in your midst, will not the islands shake?

**26:16** And all the rulers from the nations of the sea will come down from their thrones, and they will remove the turbans from their heads, and they will strip off their embroidered clothing; with dismay they will be dismayed, they will sit upon the ground and fear their destruction, and they will groan over you;

**26:17** and they will take up a lamentation over you and say to you, 'How have you been destroyed from the sea, the praised city, who put her terror on all who inhabit her?'

**26:18** And the islands will be afraid from the day of your fall.

**26:19** For thus says the Lord, the Lord: When I make you a desolated city, like the cities that will not be inhabited, when I bring up the abyss upon you, and much water covers you,

**26:20** then I will bring you down to those who go down to the pit, to a people of old, and I will make you dwell in the depths of the earth, like an eternal desolation, with those who go down to the pit, so that you may not be inhabited nor rise up upon the land of life.

**26:21** I will make you a destruction, and you will exist no longer forever, says the Lord, the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **26:2** — Throughout this chapter the Greek uses the transliterated form "Sor" for Tyre; here it also reads "the nations have perished... she who was full has been laid desolate" where the Hebrew MT has "the gate of the peoples... I shall be filled, now that she is laid waste."
- **26:10** — Greek "from a plain" (ek pediou) reflects a different reading of the Hebrew root translated "a breached city" in the MT.
- **26:12** — The Greek uses third-person singular verbs throughout vv. 12-13 ("he will plunder... he will put an end to") and reads "your sea" (MT: "the waters"), whereas the Hebrew MT has third-person plural verbs in v. 12 and first-person singular in v. 13.
- **26:15** — Greek reads kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") here and in vv. 19 and 21, reflecting the doubled divine title in the Hebrew; "when a sword is drawn" also differs from the MT ("when slaughter is made").
- **26:20** — Greek "nor rise up upon the land of life" diverges from the Hebrew MT ("and I will set glory in the land of the living"); v. 21 likewise reads "a destruction" where the MT has "terrors" and lacks the final MT clause.
