## Text

**1:1** And it came about in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth of the month, while I was in the midst of the captivity by the river Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**1:2** On the fifth of the month, this was the fifth year of the captivity of King Jehoiakim,

**1:3** and the word of the Lord came to Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar. And the hand of the Lord came upon me.

**1:4** And I looked, and look, a sweeping wind was coming from the north, and a great cloud was in it, and brightness all around it, and flashing fire, and in the midst of it something like an appearance of amber in the midst of the fire, and brightness in it.

**1:5** And in the midst was something like a likeness of four living creatures; and this was their appearance: a likeness of a man was upon them.

**1:6** And each one had four faces, and each one had four wings.

**1:7** And their legs were straight, and their feet were winged, and there were sparks like flashing bronze; and their wings were light,

**1:8** and a man's hand was underneath their wings on their four sides.

**1:9** And the faces of the four of them did not turn as they walked; each one went straight before their face.

**1:10** And the likeness of their faces: a face of a man, and a face of a lion on the right for the four, and a face of a calf on the left for the four, and a face of an eagle for the four;

**1:11** and their wings were stretched out above for the four: each one had two joined to one another, and two covered over their body.

**1:12** And each one went straight before its face; wherever the spirit was going, they went, and they did not turn.

**1:13** And in the midst of the living creatures was an appearance like burning coals of fire, like the sight of torches whirling around in the midst of the living creatures, and brightness of the fire; and out of the fire lightning was going forth.

**1:15** And I looked, and look, one wheel was on the earth beside the living creatures, for the four.

**1:16** And the appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of tharsis, and the four had one likeness; and their workmanship was as if a wheel were within a wheel.

**1:17** Toward their four sides they went; they did not turn as they went,

**1:18** nor did their backs, and they had height; and I saw them, and their backs were full of eyes all around for the four.

**1:19** And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures rose up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.

**1:20** Wherever the cloud was, there was the spirit to go; the wheels went and were lifted up with them, because a spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:21** When they went, they went, and when they stood, they stood, and when they rose up from the earth, they were lifted up with them, for a spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:22** And a likeness was over the head of the living creatures like an expanse, like an appearance of crystal, stretched out over their wings above.

**1:23** And under the expanse their wings were stretched out, flapping one against the other; each one had two covering their bodies.

**1:24** And I heard the sound of their wings as they went, like the sound of much water; and when they stood, their wings rested.

**1:25** And look, there was a voice from above the expanse that was over their head.

**1:26** Like an appearance of sapphire stone, a likeness of a throne was upon it; and upon the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the appearance of a man above.

**1:27** And I saw something like the sight of amber from the appearance of the waist and upward; and from the appearance of the waist and all the way downward I saw something like an appearance of fire, and its brightness was all around.

**1:28** Like the appearance of a bow when it is in the cloud on days of rain, so was the state of the brightness all around.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Greek reads "Ioakeim" (corresponding to Jehoiakim), a form the Septuagint frequently uses for both Jehoiakim and Jehoiachin (the latter being named in the Hebrew text here).
- **1:7** — The Greek text uniquely describes the creatures' feet as "winged" and adds that "their wings were light," differing substantially from the Hebrew description of calf-like feet.
- **1:13** — Verse 14 of the Hebrew text (describing the living creatures darting to and fro like lightning) is absent in the Septuagint.
- **1:16** — Greek "tharseis" transliterates the Hebrew gemstone term "tarshish" (often identified as beryl or chrysolite).
- **1:18** — Greek literally renders the wheel rims as "backs" ("notoi"), connects them to the preceding verse ("nor did their backs"), and reads "and I saw them" where the Hebrew text reads "and they were awesome."
