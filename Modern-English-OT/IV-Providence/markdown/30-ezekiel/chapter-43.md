## Text

**43:1** And he led me to the gate that faces toward the east, and he brought me out.

**43:2** And look, the glory of the God of Israel was coming by the way toward the east, and the sound of the encampment was like the sound of many redoubling; and the earth shone forth like radiance from the glory all around.

**43:3** And the vision that I saw was like the vision that I saw when I went in to anoint the city, and the vision of the chariot that I saw was like the vision that I saw by the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.

**43:4** And the glory of the Lord entered into the house by the way of the gate that faces toward the east.

**43:5** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and look, the house was full of the glory of the Lord.

**43:6** And I stood, and look, there was a voice from the house of One speaking to me, and the man was standing beside me.

**43:7** And He said to me, "You have seen, son of man, the place of My throne and the place of the sole of My feet, in which My name will dwell in the midst of the house of Israel forever. And the house of Israel will no longer profane My holy name, they and their rulers, in their prostitution and in the murders of the rulers in their midst,

**43:8** by their setting My threshold with their thresholds and My doorposts next to their doorposts; and they made My wall as adjoining between Me and them, and they profaned My holy name in their lawless deeds that they practiced; and I destroyed them in My wrath and in slaughter.

**43:9** And now let them put away their prostitution and the murders of their rulers from Me, and I will dwell in their midst forever.

**43:10** And you, son of man, show the house to the house of Israel, and they will cease from their sins; and its appearance and its arrangement,

**43:11** and they themselves will bear their punishment for all that they have done. And you shall outline the house and its exits and its foundation, and all its ordinances and all its statutes you shall make known to them and outline before them, and they shall keep all My decrees and all My ordinances and do them.

**43:12** And the outline of the house on the top of the mountain: all its boundaries all around are most holy."

**43:13** And these are the measurements of the altar by the cubit of a cubit and a handbreadth: a hollow of a cubit in depth, and a cubit the width, and a rim on its edge all around of a span. And this is the height of the altar:

**43:14** from the depth of the beginning of its hollow to this great propitiatory below is two cubits, and the width is a cubit. And from the small propitiatory to the great propitiatory is four cubits, and the width is a cubit.

**43:15** And the ariel is four cubits, and from the ariel and above the horns is a cubit.

**43:16** And the ariel is twelve cubits in length by twelve cubits, square on its four sides.

**43:17** And the propitiatory is fourteen cubits in length by fourteen cubits in width on its four sides, and the rim for it all around, encircling it, is half a cubit, and its circuit is a cubit all around; and its steps face toward the east.

**43:18** And He said to me, "Son of man, thus says the Lord God of Israel: These are the ordinances of the altar on the day of its making, to offer up burnt offerings on it and to pour blood against it.

**43:19** And you shall give to the priests, the Levites, who are from the offspring of Zadok, who draw near to Me, says the Lord God, to minister to Me, a young bull from the herd for a sin offering.

**43:20** And they shall take from its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar and on the four corners of the propitiatory and on the base all around, and they shall make atonement for it.

**43:21** And they shall take the young bull for the sin offering, and it shall be burned up in the set-apart place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**43:22** And on the second day they shall take two unblemished kids of the goats for a sin offering, and they shall make atonement for the altar just as they made atonement with the young bull.

**43:23** And after you finish the atonement, they shall present an unblemished young bull from the herd and an unblemished ram from the sheep,

**43:24** and you shall present them before the Lord; and the priests shall cast salt on them and offer them up as burnt offerings to the Lord.

**43:25** For seven days you shall prepare a kid for a sin offering each day, and a young bull from the herd and a ram from the sheep; unblemished ones they shall prepare

**43:26** for seven days. And they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and they shall fill their hands.

**43:27** And it shall be from the eighth day and onward, the priests shall prepare on the altar your burnt offerings and your offerings of deliverance; and I will accept you, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **43:2** — The Greek reads "the sound of the encampment was like the sound of many redoubling" (τῆς παρεμβολῆς ... διπλασιαζόντων πολλῶν), differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "like the sound of many waters."
- **43:3** — The Greek reads "to anoint the city" (τοῦ χρῖσαι) and "the vision of the chariot" (τοῦ ἅρματος), where the Masoretic Text has "to destroy the city" and "visions."
- **43:7** — Here and in v. 9 the Greek reads "in the murders of the rulers" (ἐν τοῖς φόνοις τῶν ἡγουμένων), whereas the Masoretic Text refers to "the corpses of their kings."
- **43:14** — The Greek renders the altar's tiers or ledges as ἱλαστήριον ("propitiatory," place of atonement) throughout vv. 14-20, and here reads "this great propitiatory below" before contrasting the small and great propitiatories.
- **43:15** — "Ariel" (ἁριήλ/ἀριήλ) is a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew term for the altar hearth; the Greek also reads "above the horns is a cubit" rather than specifying four horns, and in v. 26 uses "fill their hands," the biblical idiom for consecration or ordination.
