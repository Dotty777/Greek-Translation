## Text

**12:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:2** "Son of man, you dwell in the midst of their injustices, those who have eyes to see and do not see, and have ears to hear and do not hear, because it is a provoking house.

**12:3** And you, son of man, make for yourself baggage for captivity by day before them, and you will be taken captive from your place to another place before them, so that they may know that it is a provoking house.

**12:4** And you will bring out your baggage, baggage for captivity, by day before their eyes; and you yourself will go out in the evening as a captive goes out

**12:5** before them. Dig for yourself into the wall, and you will pass out through it.

**12:6** Before them you will be lifted up on shoulders, and you will go out concealed; you will cover your face and will by no means see the land, because I have given you as a portent to the house of Israel."

**12:7** And I did so, according to all that He commanded me, and I brought out baggage for captivity by day, and in the evening I dug through the wall for myself and went out concealed; I was lifted up on shoulders before them.

**12:8** And the word of the Lord came to me in the morning, saying,

**12:9** "Son of man, did not the house of Israel, the provoking house, say to you, 'What are you doing?'

**12:10** Say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: The prince and the leader in Jerusalem, and to all the house of Israel who are in their midst.'

**12:11** Say, 'I am performing portents; just as I have done, so it will be to him; in exile and in captivity they will go.

**12:12** And the prince in their midst will be lifted up on shoulders, and he will go out concealed through the wall, and he will dig through so that he may go out through it. He will cover his face so that he may not be seen by an eye, and he himself will not see the land.

**12:13** And I will spread out My net over him, and he will be caught in My enclosure, and I will bring him to Babylon, to the land of the Chaldeans, and he will not see it, and there he will die.

**12:14** And all those around him, his helpers, and all those who support him, I will disperse to every wind, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**12:15** And they will know that I am the Lord when I scatter them among the nations, and I will disperse them in the countries.

**12:16** And I will leave behind from them men few in number from the sword and from famine and from death, so that they may recount all their lawless deeds among the nations where they entered, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

**12:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:18** "Son of man, you will eat your bread with anguish, and you will drink water with torment and affliction.

**12:19** And you will say to the people of the land, 'Thus says the Lord to the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They will eat their loaves with scarcity, and they will drink their water with desolation, so that the land may be made desolate along with its fullness, for all those who dwell in it are in ungodliness.

**12:20** And their inhabited cities will be laid waste, and the land will become a desolation; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**12:21** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:22** "Son of man, what is the proverb you have in the land of Israel, saying, 'The days are long, vision has perished'?

**12:23** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: I will turn back this proverb, and the house of Israel will no longer say this proverb,' for you will speak to them, 'The days have drawn near, and the word of every vision.

**12:24** For there will no longer be any false vision or one who divines flattering things in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**12:25** Because I, the Lord, will speak My words; I will speak and I will do it, and I will no longer delay; for in your days, provoking house, I will speak a word and I will do it, says the Lord.'"

**12:26** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**12:27** "Son of man, look, the provoking house of Israel keep saying, 'The vision that this man sees is for many days, and for distant times this man prophesies.'

**12:28** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: None of My words that I speak will be delayed any longer; I will speak and I will do it, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Greek text reads "in the midst of their injustices" (en meso ton adikion auton), whereas the Hebrew reads "in the midst of the rebellious house."
- **12:6** — The Greek reads the passive "you will be lifted up on shoulders" and "concealed" (also in vv. 7 and 12), whereas the Hebrew speaks of carrying baggage on the shoulder "in the dark."
- **12:10** — The Greek uses the double divine title "kyrios Kyrios" ("the Lord, the Lord") and reads "The prince and the leader" (Ho archon kai ho aphegoumenos), where the Hebrew has "This oracle concerns the prince."
