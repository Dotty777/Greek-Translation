## Text

**18:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**18:2** "Son of man, what is this proverb to you among the sons of Israel, saying, 'The fathers ate unripe grapes, and the teeth of the children were set on edge'?

**18:3** As I live, says the Lord, never again will this proverb be spoken in Israel.

**18:4** For all souls are Mine; just as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son—they are Mine; the soul that sins, that one will die.

**18:5** Now the man who will be righteous, who practices righteousness,

**18:6** will not eat upon the mountains, and will by no means lift up his eyes to the devices of the house of Israel, and will by no means defile the wife of his neighbor, and will not approach a woman who is in her menstrual period,

**18:7** and will by no means oppress any person, will return the pledge of a debtor and will not seize plunder, will give his bread to the hungry and will clothe the naked,

**18:8** and will not lend his money at interest and will not take increase, and will turn back his hand from unrighteousness, will execute righteous judgment between a man and his neighbor,

**18:9** and has walked in My statutes, has kept My ordinances to do them—this one is righteous; he will surely live, says the Lord.

**18:10** And if he fathers a pestilent son, who sheds blood and commits a sin—

**18:11** he did not walk in the way of his righteous father, but even ate upon the mountains and defiled the wife of his neighbor,

**18:12** and oppressed the poor and the needy, and seized plunder, and did not return a pledge, and set his eyes upon the idols; he has committed lawlessness,

**18:13** he lent with interest and took increase—this one will surely not live; he committed all these lawless deeds; he will surely be put to death; his blood will be upon him.

**18:14** Now if he fathers a son, and he sees all the sins of his father which he committed, and fears and does not act according to them—

**18:15** he has not eaten upon the mountains, and did not set his eyes upon the devices of the house of Israel, and did not defile the wife of his neighbor,

**18:16** and did not oppress any person, and did not take a pledge, and did not seize plunder, gave his bread to the hungry and clothed the naked,

**18:17** and turned back his hand from unrighteousness, took neither interest nor increase, practiced righteousness and walked in My statutes—he will not die in the unrighteous deeds of his father; he will surely live.

**18:18** As for his father, if he severely oppresses and seizes plunder—he did contrary things in the midst of My people, and he will die in his unrighteousness.

**18:19** And you will say, 'Why has the son not borne the unrighteousness of the father?' Because the son has practiced righteousness and mercy, has carefully kept all My laws and done them; he will surely live.

**18:20** Now the soul that sins will die; the son will not bear the unrighteousness of the father, nor will the father bear the unrighteousness of the son; the righteousness of a righteous one will be upon him, and the lawlessness of a lawless one will be upon him.

**18:21** And if the lawless one turns away from all his lawless deeds which he committed, and keeps all My commandments and practices righteousness and mercy, he will surely live; he will by no means die.

**18:22** All his trespasses, as many as he committed, will not be remembered; in his righteousness which he practiced he will live.

**18:23** Do I really desire the death of the lawless one, says the Lord, as much as that he should turn away from the evil way and live?

**18:24** But when a righteous one turns away from his righteousness and commits unrighteousness according to all the lawless deeds which the lawless one committed, all his righteous deeds which he practiced will by no means be remembered; in his trespass by which he trespassed and in his sins which he sinned, in them he will die.

**18:25** And you said, 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' Hear now, house of Israel: is My way not straight? Is it not your way that is not straight?

**18:26** When the righteous one turns away from his righteousness and commits a trespass, and dies in the trespass which he committed, in it he will die.

**18:27** And when a lawless one turns away from his lawlessness which he committed and practices judgment and righteousness, this one has preserved his soul.

**18:28** And he turned away from all his impieties which he committed; he will surely live; he will by no means die.

**18:29** And the house of Israel says, 'The way of the Lord is not upright.' Is My way not upright, house of Israel? Is it not your way that is not upright?

**18:30** Each one according to his way I will judge you, house of Israel, says the Lord; turn back and turn away from all your impieties, and they will not be to you for a punishment of unrighteousness.

**18:31** Cast away from yourselves all your impieties by which you acted impiously against Me, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; and why are you dying, house of Israel?

**18:32** For I do not desire the death of the one who dies, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — Here and in v. 15 the Greek renders Hebrew "idols" with ta enthymemata ("the devices" or "contrivances"), distinct from ta eidola ("the idols") in v. 12.
- **18:9** — "He will surely live" renders the emphatic Greek dative idiom zoe zesetai (literally "with life he will live"), which mirrors the Hebrew infinitive absolute throughout this chapter.
- **18:11** — The Greek reads "he did not walk in the way of his righteous father," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("though he himself did none of these things").
- **18:32** — The Greek text ends the chapter after "says the Lord," without the closing imperative "so turn and live" present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
