## Text

**22:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:2** "And you, son of man, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then show her all her lawless deeds,

**22:3** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: "O city shedding blood in her midst so that her time may come, and making devices against herself to defile herself!

**22:4** By their blood that you have shed you have transgressed, and by your devices that you were making you defiled yourself; and you have brought your days near, and you have brought the time of your years. Therefore I have given you as a reproach to the nations and as a mockery to all the countries, those near to you

**22:5** and those far distant from you; and they will mock at you, you unclean one, renowned and abounding in lawless deeds!

**22:6** Look, the leaders of the house of Israel, each one with his relatives, mingled together in you so that they might shed blood.

**22:7** Father and mother they reviled in you, and toward a resident alien they conducted themselves with injustices in you; orphan and widow they oppressed;

**22:8** and My holy things they despised, and My Sabbaths they profaned in you.

**22:9** Men who are robbers are in you, so that they may shed blood in you; and on the mountains they ate in you; unholy deeds they practiced in your midst.

**22:10** The shame of a father they uncovered in you, and her who was set apart in her impurities they humbled in you.

**22:11** Each one acted lawlessly with the wife of his neighbor, and each one defiled his daughter-in-law in impiety, and each one humbled his sister, the daughter of his father, in you.

**22:12** Gifts they took in you in order to shed blood; interest and increase they took in you; and you brought to completion the full measure of your wickedness in oppression, but Me you have forgotten," says the Lord.

**22:13** "Now if I bring My hand against what you have completed that you have done, and against your bloodshed that has taken place in your midst,

**22:14** will your heart endure? Will your hands be strong in the days in which I act in you? I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will act.

**22:15** And I will scatter you among the nations, and I will disperse you among the countries, and your uncleanness will cease from you.

**22:16** And I will take possession in you before the eyes of the nations, and you will know that I am the Lord."'"

**22:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:18** "Son of man, look, the house of Israel has become to Me all mixed with bronze and iron and tin and lead; in the midst of silver it is mixed.

**22:19** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: "Because you have become one mixture, therefore I am taking you into the midst of Jerusalem.

**22:20** Just as silver and bronze and iron and tin and lead are taken into the midst of a furnace, to blow fire onto it in order to be melted, so in My wrath I will take you in, and I will gather and melt you;

**22:21** and I will blow upon you in the fire of My wrath, and you will be melted in its midst.

**22:22** As silver is melted in the midst of a furnace, so you will be melted in its midst; and you will know that I, the Lord, have poured out My fury upon you."'"

**22:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:24** "Son of man, say to her, 'You are a land that is not watered, nor has rain come upon you in the day of wrath,

**22:25** whose leaders in her midst are like roaring lions seizing prey, devouring souls by force and taking valuables; and your widows have been multiplied in your midst.

**22:26** And her priests have set aside My law and profaned My holy things; between the holy and the profane they made no distinction, and between the unclean and the clean they made no distinction, and from My Sabbaths they covered their eyes, and I was profaned in their midst.

**22:27** Her rulers in her midst are like wolves seizing prey to shed blood, so that with greed they may make greedy gain.

**22:28** And her prophets, daubing them, will fall, seeing empty things, divining falsehoods, saying, "Thus says the Lord," when the Lord has not spoken;

**22:29** extorting the people of the land with injustice and carrying off plunder, oppressing the poor and the needy, and not conducting themselves with justice toward the resident alien.

**22:30** And I was seeking from among them a man conducting himself uprightly and standing completely before My face in the time of the land, so that I would not completely blot it out, and I found none.

**22:31** And I poured out My fury upon her in the fire of My wrath, to make an end; their ways I have put upon their heads,'" says the Lord, the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — Greek ἐνθυμήματα ("devices" or "schemes," also in 22:4) is LXX Ezekiel's characteristic rendering of Hebrew gillulim ("idols").
- **22:16** — LXX reads a first-person active verb κατακληρονομήσω ("I will take possession"), diverging from MT's "you will be profaned" (or "take possession for yourself").
- **22:25** — LXX reads "whose leaders" (ἧς οἱ ἀφηγούμενοι), diverging from MT's "a conspiracy of her prophets," and in v. 24 has "not watered" (οὐ βρεχομένη) where MT reads "not cleansed."
- **22:29** — LXX places "the people of the land" (λαὸν τῆς γῆς) in the accusative as the victims being extorted by the ruling classes, rather than as the grammatical subject as in MT. In v. 28, LXX reads "will fall" (πεσοῦνται) where MT has "whitewash."
