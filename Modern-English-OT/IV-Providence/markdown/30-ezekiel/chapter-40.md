## Text

**40:1** And it happened in the twenty-fifth year of our captivity, in the first month, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was captured, on that day the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He brought me

**40:2** in a vision of God into the land of Israel, and He set me on a very high mountain, and on it was something like the structure of a city opposite.

**40:3** And He brought me in there, and look, a man, and his appearance was like the appearance of gleaming bronze, and in his hand was a builders' cord and a measuring reed; and he was standing at the gate.

**40:4** And the man said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? See with your eyes, and hear with your ears, and set in your heart all that I am showing you, because for the sake of showing you, you have come in here, and you will show all that you see to the house of Israel."

**40:5** And look, an enclosure wall on the outside of the house all around, and in the hand of the man was a measuring reed of six cubits by a cubit and a handbreadth; and he measured the outer wall, in width equal to the reed, and its height equal to the reed.

**40:6** And he went into the gate facing toward the east by seven steps, and he measured the porch of the gate, equal to the reed;

**40:7** and the guardroom was equal to the reed in length and equal to the reed in width, and the porch between the guardrooms was six cubits; and the second guardroom was equal to the reed in width and equal to the reed in length, and the porch was five cubits;

**40:8** and the third guardroom was equal to the reed in width and equal to the reed in length;

**40:9** and the porch of the gateway near the porch of the gate was eight cubits, and the jambs were two cubits; and the porches of the gate were on the inside;

**40:10** and the guardrooms of the gate were guardroom opposite guardroom, three on this side and three on that side, and one measurement for the three, one measurement for the porches on this side and on that side.

**40:11** And he measured the width of the door of the gateway, ten cubits, and the breadth of the gateway, thirteen cubits.

**40:12** And there was a narrowing cubit in front of the guardrooms on this side and on that side, and the guardroom was six cubits on this side and six cubits on that side.

**40:13** And he measured the gate from the wall of the guardroom to the wall of the guardroom, a width of twenty-five cubits; this was gate opposite gate.

**40:14** And the forecourt of the porch of the gate on the outside was twenty cubits, guardrooms of the gate all around.

**40:15** And the forecourt of the gate on the outside to the forecourt of the porch of the gate on the inside was fifty cubits.

**40:16** And there were hidden windows upon the guardrooms and upon the porches inside the gate of the court all around; and likewise for the porches, windows all around on the inside, and upon the porch were palm trees on this side and on that side.

**40:17** And he brought me into the inner court, and look, chambers and colonnades of the court all around, thirty chambers in the colonnades.

**40:18** And the porticoes were at the back of the gates; according to the length of the gates was the lower colonnade.

**40:19** And he measured the width of the court from the forecourt of the outer gate on the inside to the forecourt of the gate facing outward, a hundred cubits, the one facing toward the east. And he led me to the north,

**40:20** and look, a gate facing toward the north for the outer court, and he measured it, both its length and its width,

**40:21** and the guardrooms, three on this side and three on that side, and the jambs and the vestibules and its palm trees. And it was according to the measurements of the gate facing toward the east: fifty cubits was its length, and twenty-five cubits was its breadth.

**40:22** And its windows and the vestibules and its palm trees were just like the gate facing toward the east; and by seven stairs they went up to it, and the porches were on the inside.

**40:23** And there was a gate for the inner court facing the gate of the north, in the same manner as the gate facing toward the east; and he measured the court from gate to gate, a hundred cubits.

**40:24** And he led me toward the south, and look, a gate facing toward the south, and he measured it and the guardrooms and the jambs and the vestibules according to these measurements.

**40:25** And its windows and the vestibules all around were just like the windows of the porch; fifty cubits was its length, and twenty-five cubits was its breadth.

**40:26** And it had seven stairs and vestibules on the inside, and it had palm trees, one on this side and one on that side upon the jambs.

**40:27** And there was a gate opposite the gate of the inner court toward the south; and he measured the court from gate to gate, a hundred cubits in breadth toward the south.

**40:28** And he brought me into the inner court of the gate toward the south, and he measured the gate according to these measurements,

**40:29** and the guardrooms and the jambs and the vestibules according to these measurements, and there were windows for it and for the vestibule all around; fifty cubits was its length, and the breadth twenty-five cubits

**40:31** of the porch toward the outer court, and palm trees on the jamb, and eight stairs.

**40:32** And he brought me into the gate facing toward the east, and he measured it according to these measurements,

**40:33** and the guardrooms and the jambs and the vestibules according to these measurements, and there were windows for it and for vestibules all around; fifty cubits was its length, and the breadth was twenty-five cubits.

**40:34** And vestibules were toward the inner court, and palm trees upon the jamb on this side and on that side, and it had eight stairs.

**40:35** And he brought me into the gate toward the north, and he measured according to these measurements,

**40:36** and the guardrooms and the jambs and the vestibules, and there were windows for it all around, and for its vestibule; fifty cubits was its length, and the breadth was twenty-five cubits;

**40:37** and the vestibules were toward the outer court, and palm trees on the jamb on this side and on that side, and it had eight stairs.

**40:38** Its chambers and its doorways and its vestibules were at the second gate; there was a drain,

**40:39** so that they may slaughter in it the offerings for sin and for ignorance.

**40:40** And at the back of the channel of the burnt offerings of the gate facing toward the north were two tables toward the east at the back of the second, and of the porch of the gate were two tables toward the east.

**40:41** Four on this side and four on that side at the back of the gate; upon them they will slaughter the sacrifices, opposite the eight tables of the sacrifices.

**40:42** And four tables of the burnt offerings were of hewn stone, a cubit and a half in width, and two and a half cubits in length, and a cubit in height; upon them they will place the instruments with which they slaughter there the burnt offerings and the sacrifices.

**40:43** And they will have a hewn rim of a handbreadth on the inside all around, and over the tables above, coverings to be sheltered from the rain and from the dryness.

**40:44** And he brought me into the inner court, and look, two halls in the inner court, one at the back of the gate facing toward the north, opening toward the south, and one at the back of the gate toward the south, yet facing toward the north.

**40:45** And he said to me, "This hall that faces toward the south is for the priests who keep the watch of the house;

**40:46** and the hall that faces toward the north is for the priests who keep the watch of the altar; those are the sons of Zadok, who draw near from Levi to the Lord to minister to Him."

**40:47** And he measured the court, a hundred cubits in length and a hundred cubits in breadth, on its four sides, and the altar was opposite the house.

**40:48** And he brought me into the porch of the house. And he measured the jamb of the porch, five cubits in width on this side and five cubits on that side, and the breadth of the doorway was fourteen cubits, and the shoulders of the door of the porch were three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side.

**40:49** And the length of the porch was twenty cubits, and the breadth was twelve cubits; and by ten steps they went up to it, and there were pillars at the porch, one on this side and one on that side.

## Translator Notes

- **40:7** — Throughout chapter 40, the Greek retains transliterated Hebrew architectural terms: thee/theeim (and here the unique form thaielatha) rendered 'guardroom(s)', ailam rendered 'porch', ailammon rendered 'vestibule(s)', and ail/aileu rendered 'jamb(s)'. In vv. 7-8, the Greek text explicitly measures three guardrooms in sequence.
- **40:17** — The Greek text reads 'the inner court' (as also in v. 34) and 'colonnades' (peristyla), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the outer court' and 'pavement'.
- **40:31** — Verse 30 is absent from the Greek text, so the final clause of v. 29 runs directly into the genitive phrase 'of the porch toward the outer court' at the beginning of v. 31.
- **40:38** — Verses 38-40 follow a distinctive Greek text mentioning 'the second gate', a 'drain' (ekrysis), and 'the channel of the burnt offerings' (rhoax), with v. 39 omitting the porch tables described in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **40:43** — The Greek text describes a handbreadth hewn rim and overhead coverings for shelter from rain and dryness, differing substantially from the hooks and sacrificial flesh of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
