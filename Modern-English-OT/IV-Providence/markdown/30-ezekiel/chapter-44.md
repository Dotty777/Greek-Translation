## Text

**44:1** And he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary that faces toward the east, and it was shut.

**44:2** And the Lord said to me, "This gate shall be shut; it shall not be opened, and no one shall pass through it, because the Lord, the God of Israel, will enter through it, and it shall be shut.

**44:3** For the ruler, he shall sit in it to eat bread before the Lord; he shall enter by the way of the porch of the gate, and by its way he shall go out."

**44:4** And he brought me in by the way of the gate toward the north, in front of the house; and I looked, and look, the house of the Lord was full of glory, and I fell on my face.

**44:5** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, set it in your heart, and see with your eyes, and hear with your ears all that I speak with you concerning all the ordinances of the house of the Lord and concerning all its statutes; and you shall set your heart on the entrance of the house, according to all its exits in all the holy places.

**44:6** And you shall say to the provoking house, to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord God: Let it be enough for you from all your lawless deeds, house of Israel,

**44:7** in your bringing in foreign sons, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in My sanctuary; and they were profaning it when you presented loaves, flesh, and blood, and you were transgressing My covenant in all your lawless deeds,

**44:8** and you gave orders to keep charges in My sanctuary.

**44:9** Therefore thus says the Lord God: No foreign son, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter into My sanctuary, among all the sons of foreigners who are in the midst of the house of Israel,

**44:10** but rather the Levites who sprang away from Me when Israel went astray from Me after their own devices—and they shall bear their unrighteousness,

**44:11** and they shall be in My sanctuary ministering, gatekeepers at the gates of the house and ministering to the house; they shall slaughter the burnt offerings and the sacrifices for the people, and they shall stand before the people to minister to them.

**44:12** Because they ministered to them before the face of their idols, and it became to the house of Israel a punishment of unrighteousness, on account of this I lifted up My hand against them, says the Lord God,

**44:13** and they shall not draw near to Me to serve as priests to Me, nor to approach the holy things of the sons of Israel nor My most holy things; and they shall bear their dishonor in the straying by which they went astray.

**44:14** And they shall bring them down to keep charges of the house, for all its works and for all that they may do.

**44:15** The priests, the Levites, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charges of My sanctuary when the house of Israel went astray from Me, these shall approach Me to minister to Me, and they shall stand before My face to present to Me sacrifice, fat and blood, says the Lord God.

**44:16** These shall enter into My sanctuary, and these shall come near to My table to minister to Me, and they shall keep My charges.

**44:17** And it shall be, when they enter the gates of the inner court, they shall put on linen robes; and they shall not put on wool while they minister from the gate of the inner court.

**44:18** And they shall have linen turbans on their heads, and they shall have linen breeches on their loins, and they shall not gird themselves by force.

**44:19** And when they go out into the outer court to the people, they shall take off their robes in which they minister, and they shall place them in the chambers of the sanctuary, and they shall put on other robes, and they shall by no means sanctify the people with their robes.

**44:20** And they shall not shave their heads, and they shall not strip bare their hair; they shall surely cover their heads.

**44:21** And no priest shall drink wine when they enter into the inner court.

**44:22** And they shall not take for themselves as a wife a widow or a divorced woman, but only a virgin from the seed of Israel; yet a widow, if she is from a priest, they may take.

**44:23** And they shall teach My people the difference between the holy and the profane, and they shall make known to them the difference between the clean and the unclean.

**44:24** And over a judgment of blood these shall stand to decide; they shall uphold My decrees, and they shall judge My judgments, and they shall keep My statutes and My ordinances in all My feasts, and they shall sanctify My Sabbaths.

**44:25** And they shall not go in to the soul of a person to be defiled; except for a father and a mother, and for a son and for a daughter, and for a brother and for his sister who has not belonged to a husband, he may be defiled.

**44:26** And after he is cleansed, he shall count out seven days for himself;

**44:27** and on whatever day they enter into the inner court to minister in the holy place, they shall present a propitiation, says the Lord God.

**44:28** And it shall be to them for an inheritance: I am an inheritance to them; and no possession shall be given to them among the sons of Israel, because I am their possession.

**44:29** And the sacrifices and the offerings for sin and the offerings for ignorance they themselves shall eat, and every set-apart thing in Israel shall be theirs.

**44:30** The firstfruits of all things and the firstborn of all things and all the contributions from all your firstfruits shall belong to the priests. And your first produce you shall give to the priest, to place your blessings upon your houses.

**44:31** And anything that dies of itself or is caught by wild beasts, from the birds or from the livestock, the priests shall not eat.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:2** — The Greek uses the future tense "will enter" (εἰσελεύσεται), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the past tense "has entered."
- **44:3** — "Porch" renders αἰλάμ (ailam), a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew architectural term for the porch of the gate.
- **44:14** — The Greek uses the third-person plural "they shall bring them down" (κατάξουσιν), whereas the Masoretic Text has the first-person singular "I will appoint them."
- **44:20** — The Greek reads "strip bare their hair" and "surely cover their heads" (καλύπτοντες καλύψουσιν), differing from the Masoretic Text's command to trim the hair; similarly in v. 18 the Greek reads "by force" (βίᾳ) where the Hebrew refers to sweat.
- **44:25** — "The soul of a person" (ψυχὴν ἀνθρώπου) is a Septuagint idiom denoting a human corpse, corresponding to "a dead person" in the Hebrew.
