## Text

**41:1** And he brought me into the temple, in which he measured the vestibule, six cubits in width on one side,

**41:2** and six cubits the breadth of the vestibule on the other side, and the breadth of the gateway was ten cubits, and the shoulders of the gateway were five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other side. And he measured its length, forty cubits, and the breadth, twenty cubits.

**41:3** And he went into the inner court, and he measured the jamb of the doorway, two cubits, and the doorway, six cubits, and the shoulders of the doorway, seven cubits on one side and seven cubits on the other side.

**41:4** And he measured the length of the doors, forty cubits, and a breadth of twenty cubits in front of the temple. And he said, "This is the Holy of Holies."

**41:5** And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits, and the breadth of the side room, four cubits all around,

**41:6** and the side rooms were side room upon side room, thirty, three times twice; and there was an interval in the wall of the house, in the side rooms all around, so that those taking hold might be able to see, in order that they might not touch the walls of the house at all.

**41:7** And the breadth of the upper one of the side rooms was according to the addition from the wall, toward the upper one all around the house, so that it might be widened above, and from the lower ones they might go up to the upper stories and from the cornices to the third stories,

**41:8** and the thrael of the house was a height all around; the interval of the side rooms was equal to the reed of six cubits. The intervals

**41:9** and the breadth of the wall of the side room on the outside were five cubits, and the remaining spaces between the side rooms of the house

**41:10** and between the chambers had a breadth of twenty cubits, that which surrounded the house all around.

**41:11** And the doors of the chambers were toward the remaining space of the one door toward the north; and the one door was toward the south, and the breadth of the light of the remaining space was five cubits in width all around.

**41:12** And the open-air area in front of the remaining space toward the west was seventy cubits, the width of the wall of the partition was five cubits, in breadth all around, and its length was ninety cubits.

**41:13** And he measured opposite the house a length of a hundred cubits, and the remaining spaces and the partitions and their walls, a length of a hundred cubits,

**41:14** and the breadth in front of the house and the remaining spaces opposite, a hundred cubits.

**41:15** And he measured the length of the partition in front of the remaining space of the parts behind that house, and the remaining spaces on one side and on the other, a hundred cubits in length. And the temple and the corners and the outer vestibule were paneled.

**41:16** And there were latticed windows, narrow openings all around for the three so as to look through; and the house and the adjacent parts were covered with wood all around, and the floor and from the floor up to the windows, and the windows opened out threefold to look through.

**41:17** And up to near the inner one and up to the outer one, and upon the whole wall all around, on the inside and on the outside,

**41:18** were carved cherubim, and palm trees between cherub and cherub. The cherub had two faces,

**41:19** the face of a man toward the palm tree on one side and on the other, and the face of a lion toward the palm tree on one side and on the other; the house was carved throughout all around;

**41:20** from the floor up to the paneling, the cherubim and the palm trees were carved throughout.

**41:21** And the holy place and the temple, opening out, were square; in front of the sanctuary was a vision like the appearance

**41:22** of a wooden altar, its height three cubits, and the length two cubits, and the breadth two cubits; and it had horns, and its base and its walls were wooden. And he said to me, "This is the table that is before the face." And the temple had two doors,

**41:23** and the holy place had two doors;

**41:24** for the two swinging doors, there were two doors for the one, and two doors for the second door.

**41:25** And there was carving on them, and on the doors of the temple were cherubim and palm trees according to the carving of the sanctuary, and sturdy timbers in front of the vestibule on the outside,

**41:26** and hidden windows. And he measured on one side and on the other, to the roofings of the vestibule, and the side rooms of the house were yoked together.

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — Greek uses the Hebrew architectural loanword ailam ("vestibule" or "porch") here and in vv. 2, 15, and 25-26, where the Hebrew of v. 1 refers to pilasters or jambs.
- **41:4** — The Greek reads "the length of the doors, forty cubits," whereas the Hebrew reads "its length, twenty cubits."
- **41:6** — The Greek wording is difficult and diverges from the Hebrew, giving the count literally as "thirty, three times twice" and describing the wall interval as enabling "those taking hold" to see so as not to touch the house walls.
- **41:8** — "Thrael" transliterates an obscure Greek term of uncertain meaning, likely arising from the underlying Hebrew text; in v. 7 the Greek also reads "from the cornices" (geison) rather than "from the middle ones."
- **41:22** — The Greek omits "of the Lord" after "before the face" (found in the Hebrew and some Greek manuscripts), adds the breadth of two cubits, and reads "horns" and "its base."
