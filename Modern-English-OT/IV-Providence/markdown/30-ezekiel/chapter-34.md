## Text

**34:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**34:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel; prophesy and say to the shepherds, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: O shepherds of Israel, do shepherds feed themselves? Do the shepherds not feed the sheep?

**34:3** Look, you consume the milk and clothe yourselves with the wool and slaughter the fat one, yet you do not feed My sheep.

**34:4** The weak you did not strengthen, and the sick you did not restore to health, and the broken you did not bind up, and the straying you did not bring back, and the lost you did not seek, and the strong you wore down with toil.

**34:5** And My sheep were scattered because there were no shepherds, and they became food for all the wild beasts of the field.

**34:6** And My sheep were scattered on every mountain and upon every high hill, and over the face of the earth they were scattered, and there was no one seeking them out nor anyone bringing them back.

**34:7** Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the Lord:

**34:8** As I live, says the Lord, the Lord, surely because My sheep became plunder, and My sheep became food for all the wild beasts of the plain since there were no shepherds, and the shepherds did not seek out My sheep, and the shepherds fed themselves, while My sheep they did not feed;

**34:9** because of this, shepherds,

**34:10** thus says the Lord, the Lord: Look, I am against the shepherds, and I will demand My sheep from their hands, and I will turn them away from shepherding My sheep, and the shepherds will no longer feed them; and I will rescue My sheep from their mouth, and they will no longer be food for them.

**34:11** For thus says the Lord: Look, I will seek out My sheep and watch over them.

**34:12** Just as a shepherd seeks his flock on a day when there is gloom and cloud in the midst of separated sheep, so I will seek out My sheep and drive them out from every place where they were scattered on a day of cloud and gloom.

**34:13** And I will bring them out from the nations and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them into their land, and I will feed them on the mountains of Israel and in the ravines and in every dwelling place of the land;

**34:14** in good pasture I will feed them, on the high mountain of Israel. And their sheepfolds will be there and they will lie down, and there they will rest in good delight, and in rich pasture they will be fed on the mountains of Israel.

**34:15** I Myself will feed My sheep, and I Myself will give them rest, and they will know that I am the Lord. Thus says the Lord, the Lord:

**34:16** The lost I will seek, and the straying I will bring back, and the broken I will bind up, and the failing I will strengthen, and the strong I will guard, and I will feed them with judgment.

**34:17** And as for you, sheep, thus says the Lord, the Lord: Look, I will judge between sheep and sheep, rams and male goats.

**34:18** And is it not enough for you that you grazed on the fine pasture, yet you trampled down the remainder of your pasture with your feet? And you drank the settled water, yet the rest you stirred up with your feet?

**34:19** And My sheep grazed on what was trampled by your feet, and they drank the water stirred up by your feet?

**34:20** Therefore, thus says the Lord, the Lord: Look, I will judge between a strong sheep and a weak sheep.

**34:21** With your sides and your shoulders you pushed, and with your horns you butted, and every failing one you pressed out.

**34:22** And I will save My sheep, and they will never again be plunder, and I will judge between ram and ram.

**34:23** And I will raise up over them one shepherd, and he will shepherd them—My servant David—and he will be their shepherd;

**34:24** and I, the Lord, will be God to them, and David will be ruler in their midst; I, the Lord, have spoken.

**34:25** And I will make with David a covenant of peace, and I will remove evil wild beasts from the land, and they will dwell in the wilderness and sleep in the forests.

**34:26** And I will place them around My mountain; and I will give the rain to you, a rain of blessing.

**34:27** And the trees in the plain will give their fruit, and the land will give its strength, and they will dwell upon their land in hope of peace, and they will know that I am the Lord when I break their yoke; and I will rescue them from the hand of those who enslaved them,

**34:28** and they will no longer be plunder for the nations, and the wild beasts of the land will never again devour them; and they will dwell in hope, and there will be no one terrifying them.

**34:29** And I will raise up for them a plant of peace, and they will no longer perish from famine on the land, and they will never again bear the reproach of nations.

**34:30** And they will know that I am the Lord their God, and they are My people. House of Israel, says the Lord,

**34:31** you are My sheep and the sheep of My flock, and I am the Lord your God, says the Lord, the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — Greek repeats the title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord"), representing the Hebrew formula Adonai YHWH. Unlike the Hebrew MT ("Woe, shepherds of Israel who have been feeding themselves"), the Greek begins with a vocative ("O shepherds of Israel") followed by two rhetorical questions.
- **34:3** — Greek reads "the milk" (to gala), reflecting the Hebrew consonantal text vocalized as chalav ("milk") rather than MT chelev ("fat").
- **34:16** — Greek reads "and the strong I will guard" (phylaxo, reflecting Hebrew shmr "guard"), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "and the fat and the strong I will destroy" (shmd).
- **34:25** — Greek explicitly names "David" ("I will make with David a covenant of peace"), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "with them."
- **34:29** — Greek reads "a plant of peace" (phyton eirenes, reflecting Hebrew shalom), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "a planting of renown" (leshem).
