## Text

**33:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**33:2** "Son of man, speak to the sons of your people and you shall say to them, 'As for a land upon which I may bring a sword, if the people of the land take one man from among them and set him for themselves as a watchman,

**33:3** and he sees the sword coming upon the land and blows the trumpet and signals to the people,

**33:4** and a hearer hears the sound of the trumpet and does not guard himself, and the sword comes and overtakes him, his blood shall be upon his head.

**33:5** Because he heard the sound of the trumpet and did not guard himself, his blood shall be upon him; whereas this one, because he guarded himself, delivered his soul.

**33:6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not signal with the trumpet, and the people do not guard themselves, and the sword comes and takes a soul from among them, that soul was taken because of its own lawlessness, yet the blood I will require from the hand of the watchman.'

**33:7** And you, son of man, I have set you as a watchman for the house of Israel, and you shall hear a word from My mouth.

**33:8** When I say to the sinner, 'You shall surely be put to death,' and you do not speak so that the ungodly one may guard himself from his way, that lawless one shall die in his lawlessness, but his blood I will require from your hand.

**33:9** But if you forewarn the ungodly one of his way, to turn back from it, and he does not turn back from his way, that one shall die in his ungodliness, and you have delivered your own soul.

**33:10** And you, son of man, say to the house of Israel, 'Thus you have spoken, saying, "Our errors and our lawless deeds are upon us, and in them we are wasting away; and how shall we live?"'

**33:11** Say to them, 'As I live, thus says the Lord, I do not desire the death of the ungodly one, as much as that the ungodly one turn back from his way and live. Turn back completely from your way! And why are you dying, house of Israel?'

**33:12** Say to the sons of your people, 'The righteousness of a righteous one shall by no means deliver him on whatever day he errs, and the lawlessness of an ungodly one shall by no means harm him on whatever day he turns back from his lawlessness; and a righteous one shall by no means be able to be saved.

**33:13** When I say to the righteous one, "This one has trusted in his righteousness," and he does lawlessness, all his righteous deeds shall by no means be remembered; in his unrighteousness that he has done, in it he shall die.

**33:14** And when I say to the ungodly one, "You shall surely be put to death," and he turns back from his sin and does justice and righteousness,

**33:15** and returns a pledge and pays back things taken by robbery, walks in ordinances of life so as not to do what is unrighteous, he shall surely live and shall by no means die;

**33:16** all his sins that he has sinned shall by no means be remembered; because he has done justice and righteousness, in them he shall live.'

**33:17** Yet the sons of your people will say, 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' Yet this way of theirs is not straight.

**33:18** When a righteous one turns back from his righteousness and does lawless deeds, then he shall die in them.

**33:19** And when the sinner turns back from his lawlessness and does justice and righteousness, in them he himself shall live.

**33:20** And this is what you have said: 'The way of the Lord is not straight.' I will judge you, each one in his ways, house of Israel."

**33:21** And it happened in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the fifth of the month, of our captivity, the one who had escaped came to me from Jerusalem, saying, "The city has been captured!"

**33:22** Now the hand of the Lord had come upon me in the evening before he came, and He opened my mouth until he came to me in the morning; and when my mouth was opened, it was no longer held shut.

**33:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**33:24** "Son of man, those who dwell in the desolate places on the land of Israel are saying, 'Abraham was one, and he possessed the land; yet we are more—to us the land has been given as a possession.'

**33:25** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord:

**33:27** As I live, surely those who are in the desolate places shall fall by swords, and those who are on the face of the plain shall be given to the wild beasts of the field for food, and those in the fortified places and those in the caves I will kill with death.

**33:28** And I will render the land desolate, and the arrogance of its strength shall perish, and the mountains of Israel shall be made desolate because there is no one passing through.

**33:29** And they shall know that I am the Lord; and I will make their land desolate, and it shall be made desolate because of all their abominations that they have done.'

**33:30** And you, son of man, the sons of your people who speak about you by the walls and in the gateways of the houses—they speak, one to his brother, saying, 'Let us come together and hear the things going forth from the Lord.'

**33:31** They come to you as a people comes together, and they sit before you and hear your words, yet they will by no means do them; for falsehood is in their mouth, and their heart is after defilements.

**33:32** And you become to them like the sound of a sweet-voiced, harmonious harp, and they will hear your words, yet they will by no means do them.

**33:33** And when it comes, they will say, 'Look, it has come!' and they shall know that a prophet was in their midst."

## Translator Notes

- **33:13** — The Greek omits the Hebrew clause "he shall surely live," placing "This one has trusted in his righteousness" directly after "When I say to the righteous one."
- **33:21** — The Greek dates the survivor's arrival to the twelfth month, whereas the Hebrew reads the tenth month.
- **33:25** — The Greek moves directly from the messenger formula here to the oath in v. 27, omitting the indictments found in the Hebrew of vv. 25b-26.
- **33:27** — Greek thanato ("with death") reflects the Septuagint's standard rendering of Hebrew deber ("pestilence").
