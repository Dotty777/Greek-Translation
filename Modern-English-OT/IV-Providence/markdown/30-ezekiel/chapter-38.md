## Text

**38:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**38:2** "Son of man, set your face against Gog and the land of Magog, ruler of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal, and prophesy against him,

**38:3** and say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord:

**38:4** Look, I am against the ruler of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal, and I will gather you and all your force, horses and horsemen, all clothed in breastplates, with a large assembly—shields and helmets and swords;

**38:5** Persians and Ethiopians and Libyans, all with helmets and shields,

**38:6** Gomer and all those around him, the house of Togarmah from the farthest north and all those around him, and many nations with you.

**38:7** Be prepared, prepare yourself, you and all your assembly, those gathered with you, and you will be a guard for Me.

**38:8** After many days he will be prepared, and at the end of years he will come and arrive in the land brought back from the sword, of those gathered from many nations, upon the land of Israel, which became completely desolate; and he has come out from the nations, and they will all dwell in peace.

**38:9** And you will go up like rain, and he will come like a cloud to cover the land, and you will be there, you and all those around you, and many nations with you.

**38:10** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: And it will be in that day that words will come up into your heart, and you will think evil thoughts,

**38:11** and you will say, "I will go up against a cast-off land; I will come against those who are at rest in quietness and dwelling in peace, all of them dwelling in a land in which there is no wall nor bars, and they have no gates,

**38:12** to take plunder and to seize their spoil, to turn my hand against the desolate land that has been settled, and against a nation gathered from many nations, who have acquired possessions, dwelling upon the navel of the earth."

**38:13** Sheba and Dedan and the Carthaginian merchants and all their villages will say to you, "Are you coming for plunder, to plunder and to seize spoil? Have you gathered your assembly to take silver and gold, to carry away possessions, to seize spoil?"'

**38:14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog, 'Thus says the Lord: In that day, when My people Israel are settled in peace, will you not be roused?

**38:15** And you will come from your place, from the farthest north, and many nations with you, all of them riders of horses, a great assembly and a large force;

**38:16** and you will go up against My people Israel like a cloud to cover the land. It will be in the last days, and I will bring you up against My land, so that all the nations may know Me when I am sanctified in you before them.

**38:17** Thus says the Lord, the Lord to Gog: You are the one about whom I spoke before the former days through the hand of My servants the prophets of Israel, in those days and years, to bring you against them.

**38:18** And it will be in that day, on the day when Gog comes against the land of Israel, says the Lord, the Lord, that My fury will rise up,

**38:19** and My zeal. In the fire of My wrath I have spoken: Surely in that day there will be a great earthquake upon the land of Israel,

**38:20** and the fish of the sea and the birds of the sky and the wild beasts of the field and all the creeping things that creep upon the earth and all the people who are upon the face of the earth will be shaken from the presence of the Lord; and the mountains will be split apart, and the ravines will fall, and every wall will fall to the ground.

**38:21** And I will summon against it even every terror, says the Lord; the sword of a man will be against his brother.

**38:22** And I will judge him with death and blood and flooding rain and hailstones, and I will rain fire and sulfur upon him and upon all those with him and upon many nations with him.

**38:23** And I will be magnified and sanctified and glorified and made known before many nations, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — The Greek treats Rosh as a proper name alongside Meshech and Tubal (whereas the Hebrew term is sometimes understood as 'chief') and reads 'Gog and the land of Magog.'
- **38:3** — The Septuagint double title kyrios Kyrios ('the Lord, the Lord') renders the Hebrew compound title Adonai YHWH and recurs in 38:10, 17, 18; 39:8, 25, 29.
- **38:11** — The Greek reads 'a cast-off land' (gen aperrimmenen), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'land of unwalled villages.'
- **38:13** — The Greek uses the distinct proper name 'Carthaginian' (Karchedonioi) where the Hebrew has 'Tarshish,' and reads 'villages' (komai) where the Hebrew has 'young lions.'
- **38:21** — The Greek reads 'against it even every terror' (ep' auto kai pan phobon), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'for a sword against him on all my mountains.'
