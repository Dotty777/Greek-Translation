## Text

**46:1** Thus says the Lord God: "The gate in the inner court that faces east shall be shut during the six working days; on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.

**46:2** And the ruler shall enter by the way of the vestibule of the inner gate and stand at the doorway of the gate, and the priests shall prepare his burnt offerings and his deliverance offerings.

**46:3** And he shall worship at the doorway of the gate and go out, and the gate shall not be shut until evening; and the people of the land shall worship at the doorway of that gate both on the Sabbaths and at the new moons before the Lord.

**46:4** And the ruler shall offer the burnt offerings to the Lord on the Sabbath day: six unblemished lambs and an unblemished ram,

**46:5** and a grain offering, a cake for the ram, and for the lambs a sacrifice, a gift of his hand, and a hin of oil for the cake.

**46:6** And on the day of the new moon, an unblemished calf and six lambs, and there shall be an unblemished ram,

**46:7** and a cake for the ram and a cake for the calf shall be a grain offering, and for the lambs as much as his hand is able, and a hin of oil for the cake.

**46:8** And when the ruler enters, he shall enter by the way of the vestibule of the gate, and by the way of the gate he shall go out.

**46:9** And whenever the people of the land enter before the Lord at the feasts, the one who enters by the way of the gate facing north to worship shall go out by the way of the gate toward the south, and the one who enters by the way of the gate toward the south shall go out by the way of the gate toward the north; he shall not return by the gate into which he has entered, but shall go out straight opposite it.

**46:10** And the ruler in their midst, when they enter, shall enter with them, and when they go out, he shall go out.

**46:11** And at the feasts and at the festive assemblies, the grain offering shall be a cake for the calf and a cake for the ram, and for the lambs as much as his hand is able, and a hin of oil for the cake.

**46:12** And if the ruler prepares a freewill offering, a burnt offering of deliverance to the Lord, then he shall open for himself the gate facing east, and he shall prepare his burnt offering and his deliverance offerings in the same manner as he does on the Sabbath day; and he shall go out, and he shall shut the doors after he goes out.

**46:13** And he shall prepare a year-old unblemished lamb for a burnt offering daily to the Lord; in the morning he shall prepare it.

**46:14** And he shall prepare a grain offering with it in the morning, a sixth of the measure, and a third of a hin of oil to mix the fine flour, a grain offering to the Lord, a perpetual statute.

**46:15** You shall prepare the lamb, and you shall prepare the grain offering and the oil in the morning, a continual burnt offering."

**46:16** Thus says the Lord God: "If the ruler gives a gift to one of his sons from his inheritance, this shall belong to his sons as a possession by inheritance.

**46:17** But if he gives a gift to one of his servants, it shall belong to him until the year of release, and he shall return it to the ruler; only as for the inheritance of his sons, it shall belong to them.

**46:18** And the ruler shall certainly not take from the inheritance of the people to oppress them; from his own possession he shall give an inheritance to his sons, so that My people may not be scattered, each one from his possession."

**46:19** And he brought me into the entrance behind the gate, into the chamber of the holy things of the priests facing north, and look, there was a place set apart there.

**46:20** And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall boil the offerings for ignorance and the offerings for sin, and there they shall bake the grain offering, so as not to carry them out at all into the outer court to sanctify the people."

**46:21** And he brought me out into the outer court, and he led me around to the four parts of the court, and look, there was a court at the sides of the court;

**46:22** at each side a court, a court at the four parts, and of the court there was a small court, forty cubits in length and thirty cubits in width, one measure for the four.

**46:23** And there were chambers all around in them, all around the four; and kitchens were made underneath the chambers all around.

**46:24** And he said to me, "These are the houses of the cooks, where those who minister in the house shall boil the sacrifices of the people."

## Translator Notes

- **46:2** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew ulam ("vestibule") as ailam (also v. 8) and specifies "the inner gate" where the Hebrew MT reads "from outside."
- **46:12** — The Greek joins "freewill offering" and "burnt offering of deliverance" without "or" and uses active verbs so that the ruler himself opens the gate and shuts "the doors."
- **46:19** — The Greek has a singular "chamber of the holy things of the priests" and "a place set apart" (where the Hebrew MT has plural chambers and "at the far western end").
- **46:20** — The Greek renders the guilt offering as "the offerings for ignorance" and uses the adverbial phrase to parapan with the negation ("so as not to carry them out at all").
