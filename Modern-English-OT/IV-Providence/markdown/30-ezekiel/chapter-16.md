## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**16:2** "Son of man, testify to Jerusalem of her lawless deeds,

**16:3** and you shall say, Thus says the Lord to Jerusalem: Your root and your birth are from the land of Canaan; your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.

**16:4** And as for your birth, on the day you were born you did not bind your breasts, and you were not washed in water, nor were you salted with salt, and you were not swaddled in swaddling cloths,

**16:5** nor did My eye spare you out of all these things, so as to feel anything for you; and you were cast out onto the face of the field in the crookedness of your soul on the day you were born.

**16:6** And I passed by you and saw you defiled in the blood, and I said to you, 'Out of your blood, life;

**16:7** multiply!' Like the growth of the field I have made you; and you multiplied and grew great, and you entered into cities of cities. Your breasts stood upright, and your hair sprang up, yet you were naked and indecent.

**16:8** And I passed by you and saw you, and look, it was your time, and a time of those who lodge; and I spread out My wings over you and covered your indecency, and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, says the Lord, and you became Mine.

**16:9** And I washed you in water, and I washed off your blood from you, and I anointed you with oil;

**16:10** and I clothed you with embroidered garments, and I shod you with hyacinth-blue, and I girded you with fine linen, and I wrapped you in fine-spun cloth;

**16:11** and I adorned you with ornament, and I placed bracelets around your hands and a necklace around your neck;

**16:12** and I put a ring in your nostril and round earrings on your ears and a crown of boasting on your head;

**16:13** and you were adorned with gold and silver, and your wraps were of fine linen and fine-spun cloth and embroidered work; fine flour and oil and honey you ate, and you became exceedingly beautiful.

**16:14** And a name went out for you among the nations for your beauty, because it was perfected in splendor, in the loveliness that I placed upon you, says the Lord.

**16:15** You trusted in your beauty and prostituted yourself because of your name, and you poured out your prostitution upon every passerby.

**16:16** And you took from your garments and made for yourself stitched idols, and you prostituted yourself upon them; and you shall by no means enter, nor shall it come to be.

**16:17** And you took the articles of your boasting from My gold and from My silver, from what I gave you, and you made for yourself male images and prostituted yourself with them,

**16:18** and you took your embroidered clothing and wrapped them, and My oil and My incense you set before their face,

**16:19** and My loaves that I gave you—fine flour and oil and honey I fed you—you set them before their face for an aroma of sweet fragrance. And it happened, says the Lord,

**16:20** and you took your sons and your daughters whom you bore, and sacrificed them to them for consumption; as though you had prostituted yourself little,

**16:21** and you slaughtered your children and gave them when you offered them in appeasement to them.

**16:22** This was beyond all your prostitution, and you did not remember your infancy, the day when you were naked and indecent; defiled in your blood, you lived.

**16:23** And it happened after all your evil deeds, says the Lord,

**16:24** and you built for yourself a house of prostitution, and you made for yourself a public stand in every square,

**16:25** and at the head of every road you built your brothels and ruined your beauty, and you parted your legs to every passerby and multiplied your prostitution,

**16:26** and you prostituted yourself with the sons of Egypt, who border on you, those of great flesh, and in many ways you prostituted yourself, to provoke Me to anger.

**16:27** But if I stretch out My hand against you, then I will remove your ordinances and deliver you into the souls of those who hate you, the daughters of the foreigners who turn you aside from your way. You acted impiously

**16:28** and you prostituted yourself with the daughters of Assyria, and not even so were you satisfied; and you prostituted yourself and were not being satisfied.

**16:29** And you multiplied your covenants with the land of the Chaldeans, and not even with these were you satisfied.

**16:30** How should I deal with your daughter, says the Lord, when you have done all these things, the deeds of a prostitute woman? And you prostituted yourself threefold among your daughters;

**16:31** you built the brothel at the head of every road, and you made your pedestal in every square. And you became like a prostitute gathering wages.

**16:32** The woman who commits adultery is like you; receiving wages from her husband, she gave wages in addition to all those who prostituted her;

**16:33** and you have given wages to all your lovers, and you loaded them down so that they would come to you from all around in your prostitution.

**16:34** And something perverted happened in you contrary to the women in your prostitution, and with you they have committed prostitution in your giving wages, and wages were not given to you, and perverted things happened in you.

**16:35** Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of the Lord:

**16:36** Thus says the Lord: Because you poured out your bronze, and your shame will be uncovered in your prostitution toward your lovers and toward all the devices of your lawless deeds and in the blood of your children whom you gave to them,

**16:37** therefore, look, I am gathering together all your lovers with whom you mingled, and all whom you loved together with all whom you hated; and I will gather them against you from all around and uncover your evil deeds to them, and they will see all your shame;

**16:38** and I will punish you with the punishment of an adulteress, and I will place you in blood of wrath and jealousy.

**16:39** And I will deliver you into their hands, and they will tear down your brothel and pull down your pedestal, and they will strip you of your garments and take the articles of your boasting, and they will leave you naked and indecent.

**16:40** And they will bring crowds against you, and they will stone you with stones, and they will slaughter you with their swords,

**16:41** and they will burn your houses with fire, and execute punishments on you before many women; and I will turn you back from prostitution, and you shall by no means give wages anymore.

**16:42** And I will let loose My wrath upon you, and My jealousy will be removed from you; and I will rest, and I will by no means be anxious anymore.

**16:43** Because you did not remember your infancy, and you kept grieving Me in all these things, look, I too have put your ways upon your head, says the Lord; and thus you committed impiety in addition to all your lawless deeds.

**16:44** These are all the things that they said against you in a proverb, saying, 'Like the mother,

**16:45** so the daughter.' You are the daughter of your mother, the one who rejected her husband and her children, and sisters of your sisters, those who rejected their husbands and their children; your mother was a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.

**16:46** Your older sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who dwells on your left; and your sister younger than you, who dwells on your right, is Sodom and her daughters.

**16:47** And not even so did you walk in their ways, nor did you act according to their lawless deeds; in a little while you even surpass them in all your ways.

**16:48** As I live, says the Lord, Sodom, she and her daughters, have surely not done in the way that you and your daughters did.

**16:49** However, this was the lawlessness of your sister Sodom: arrogance; in fullness of bread and in prosperity they lived in luxury, she and her daughters; this belonged to her and to her daughters; and they did not take hold of the hand of the poor and needy,

**16:50** and they boasted arrogantly and committed acts of lawlessness before Me, and I removed them just as I saw.

**16:51** And Samaria did not sin according to half of your sins; and you multiplied your lawless deeds beyond them, and you justified your sisters in all your lawless deeds that you committed.

**16:52** And you, bear your torment in which you corrupted your sisters by your sins in which you acted lawlessly beyond them, and you justified them beyond yourself; and you, be ashamed and take your dishonor in your justifying your sisters.

**16:53** And I will turn back their turnings away, the turning away of Sodom and her daughters, and I will turn back the turning away of Samaria and her daughters, and I will turn back your turning away in their midst,

**16:54** so that you may bear your torment and be dishonored because of all that you did in provoking Me to anger.

**16:55** And your sister Sodom and her daughters will be restored just as they were from the beginning; and you and your daughters will be restored just as you were from the beginning.

**16:56** And if your sister Sodom was not a report in your mouth in the days of your arrogance,

**16:57** before your evil deeds were uncovered, in the way that you are now a reproach of the daughters of Syria and of all those around her, the daughters of the foreigners who surround you all around,

**16:58** your impieties and your lawless deeds, you have borne them, says the Lord.

**16:59** Thus says the Lord: And I will do to you just as you did, since you dishonored these things so as to transgress My covenant.

**16:60** And I Myself will remember My covenant with you in the days of your infancy, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant.

**16:61** And you will remember your way, and you will be utterly dishonored when you receive your sisters who are older than you together with those who are younger than you, and I will give them to you for building up and not from your covenant.

**16:62** And I Myself will establish My covenant with you, and you will know that I am the Lord;

**16:63** so that you may remember and be ashamed, and it may no longer be yours to open your mouth because of your dishonor, when I make atonement for you according to all that you did, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **16:4** — Greek 'ouk edesas tous mastous sou' ("you did not bind your breasts") reflects a distinctive Septuagint reading where the Masoretic Hebrew has "your umbilical cord was not cut."
- **16:7** — Greek 'eis poleis poleon' ("into cities of cities") reflects a Septuagint reading of Hebrew 'cities' where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "ornament of ornaments."
- **16:8** — Greek 'kairos katalyonton' literally means "a time of those who lodge" (or "of those who unbind/dissolve"), a difficult Septuagint rendering where the Masoretic Hebrew has "a time of love."
- **16:27** — Greek 'ean de' ("But if"), 'allophylon' ("foreigners," the standard Septuagint rendering for "Philistines"), and 'tas ekklinousas se' ("who turn you aside") reflect distinctive Septuagint readings; 'esebesas' ("You acted impiously") begins the clause completed in verse 28.
- **16:30** — Greek 'ti diatho ten thygatera sou' ("How should I deal with your daughter") and 'trissos en tais thygatrasin sou' ("threefold among your daughters") reflect distinctive Septuagint readings differing markedly from the Masoretic Hebrew.
- **16:36** — Greek 'ton chalkon sou' ("your bronze," also used for copper money) reflects the Septuagint reading of Hebrew 'nehoshet' as bronze/money rather than "lust/nakedness"; Greek 'enthymemata' ("devices/thoughts") regularly renders Hebrew "idols" in Ezekiel.
- **16:42** — Greek 'ou me merimneso' ("I will by no means be anxious") is a distinctive Septuagint rendering where the Masoretic Hebrew has "I will no longer be angry."
- **16:53** — Greek 'apostrepso tas apostrophas' ("I will turn back their turnings away") renders the Hebrew idiom for restoring fortunes or captivity with a literal Greek cognate phrase.
- **16:59** — Greek 'tauta' ("these things") reflects a Septuagint reading of the Hebrew consonants as "these" rather than the Masoretic vocalization "oath."
- **16:61** — Greek 'eis oikodomen' ("for building up") reflects a Septuagint reading of the Hebrew consonants from the root "to build" rather than the Masoretic vocalization "for daughters."
