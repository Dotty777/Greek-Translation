## Text

**21:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:2** "Son of man, set your face toward Teman and look toward Dagon, and prophesy against the leading forest of the Negev,

**21:3** and you shall say to the forest of the Negev, 'Hear the word of the Lord. Thus says the Lord, the Lord: "Look, I am kindling a fire in you, and it will devour in you a green tree and every dry tree; the flame that is kindled will not be quenched, and every face will be burned up in it, from the east to the north.

**21:4** And all flesh will know that I, the Lord, have kindled it; it will not be quenched."'"

**21:5** And I said, "By no means, Lord, Lord! They are saying to me, 'Is this not a parable being spoken?'"

**21:6** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:7** "Therefore prophesy, son of man; set your face toward Jerusalem and look toward their holy places, and you shall prophesy against the land of Israel.

**21:8** And you shall say to the land of Israel, 'Look, I am against you, and I will draw My dagger out of its sheath, and I will destroy from you the unrighteous and the lawless.

**21:9** Because I will destroy from you the unrighteous and the lawless, so My dagger will go out from its sheath against all flesh from the east to the north,

**21:10** and all flesh will know that I, the Lord, have drawn My dagger out of its sheath; it will not return anymore.'

**21:11** And you, son of man, groan deeply with the crushing of your loins, and in pains you shall groan before their eyes.

**21:12** And it will be, if they say to you, 'Why are you groaning?' then you shall say, 'Because of the report, for it is coming; and every heart will be broken, and all hands will be paralyzed, and all flesh and every spirit will faint, and all thighs will be defiled with moisture. Look, it is coming,'" says the Lord, the Lord.

**21:13** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:14** "Son of man, prophesy and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord.' Say, 'A sword, a sword, be sharpened and be enraged,

**21:15** so that you may slaughter victims; be sharpened so that you may come to a gleam, ready for paralysis; slaughter, despise, reject every tree.

**21:16** And He gave it ready for His hand to hold; the sword has been sharpened; it is ready to be given into the hand of a slayer.'

**21:17** Cry out and wail, son of man, for it has come among My people, it is among all the leaders of Israel; they will sojourn; upon a sword it has come among My people; therefore clap upon your hand.

**21:18** For it has been justified; and should a tribe be rejected? It will not be," says the Lord, the Lord.

**21:19** "And you, son of man, prophesy and clap hand upon hand, and double the sword; the third is a sword of the wounded, the great sword of the wounded. And you will dismay them,

**21:20** so that the heart may not be broken, and the weak may be multiplied at every gate; they have been handed over as victims of the sword; it has been made well for slaughter, it has been made well for gleaming.

**21:21** Pass through, be sharpened on the right and on the left, wherever your face is roused.

**21:22** And I also will clap My hand to My hand and I will let loose My fury; I, the Lord, have spoken."

**21:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:24** "And you, son of man, mark out for yourself two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to enter; out of one country the two will go forth. And a hand is at the head of a road to a city; at the head of the road you shall mark it out for a sword to enter against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and against Judea, and against Jerusalem in its midst,

**21:25** for a sword to enter against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and against Judea, and against Jerusalem, in its midst.

**21:26** For the king of Babylon will stand at the ancient road, at the head of the two roads, to practice divination: to shake a rod, and to inquire by the carved images, and to inspect the liver on his right.

**21:27** The divination came against Jerusalem: to throw up a palisade, to open the mouth with a shout, to lift up the voice with an outcry, to throw up a palisade against her gates, and to cast up a mound, and to build siege batteries.

**21:28** And he is to them like one practicing divination before them, and he is calling to mind his injustices to be remembered.

**21:29** Therefore thus says the Lord: 'Because you have called your injustices to mind in that your impieties were uncovered, so that your sins were seen in all your impieties and in your practices; because you have called them to mind, in these you will be captured.'

**21:30** And you, lawless, profane leader of Israel, whose day has come in the time of injustice, the end,

**21:31** thus says the Lord: 'You have taken off the turban and put on the crown; for her it will not be like this; you have humbled what is high, and you have exalted what is low.

**21:32** Injustice, injustice, injustice I will make her, woe to her! Such she will be until he comes to whom it belongs, and I will hand it over to him.'

**21:33** And you, son of man, prophesy and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord concerning the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach.' And you shall say, 'A sword, a sword, drawn for slaughter and drawn for complete destruction; rouse yourself so that you may gleam,

**21:34** in your empty vision and in your divining falsehoods, to deliver you onto the necks of the lawless wounded, the day has come in the time of injustice, the end.

**21:35** Turn back; do not lodge in this place in which you were born; in your own land I will judge you.

**21:36** And I will pour out My wrath upon you; in the fire of My wrath I will blow upon you, and I will deliver you into the hands of barbarous men who craft destructions.

**21:37** In fire you will be food; your blood will be in the midst of your land; there will by no means be any remembrance of you, for I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — LXX reads "Dagon" (Δαγών) where Hebrew MT has "south" (darom), and "leading" (ἡγούμενον) modifying "forest" where MT has "of the field." LXX also begins chapter 21 here (corresponding to MT 20:45-21:32).
- **21:3** — The Greek doubles the divine title (κύριος Κύριος, "the Lord, the Lord") to represent Hebrew Adonai YHWH. Greek ἀπηλιώτου literally means "east" (also in 21:9), where Hebrew MT reads "south."
- **21:8** — LXX reads "the unrighteous and the lawless" (ἄδικον καὶ ἄνομον, also in 21:9) rather than MT's "the righteous and the wicked." Greek uses ἐγχειρίδιον ("dagger") in vv. 8-10 before switching to ῥομφαία ("sword") in vv. 14-33.
- **21:18** — Verses 17-20 preserve notably difficult LXX readings, including "they will sojourn" and "clap upon your hand" (vs. MT "thigh") in v. 17, "it has been justified; and should a tribe be rejected?" in v. 18, and the negation "so that the heart may not be broken" in v. 20.
- **21:25** — The Greek source repeats the final clause of v. 24 here. In v. 26, LXX reads "ancient road" and singular "rod" (vs. MT "arrows"), and in vv. 31-32 reads indicative verbs ("you have taken off... put on") and "injustice" (vs. MT "ruin").
