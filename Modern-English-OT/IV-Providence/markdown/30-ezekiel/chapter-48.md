## Text

**48:1** And these are the names of the tribes, from the beginning on the north, along the side of the descent of that which cuts around to the entrance of Hamath, the court of Ailam, the border of Damascus toward the north along the side of Hamath, the court; and the east side as far as the sea shall belong to them: Dan, one.

**48:2** And from the borders of Dan, the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Asher, one.

**48:3** And from the borders of Asher, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Naphtali, one.

**48:4** And from the borders of Naphtali, from the east as far as the side toward the sea: Manasseh, one.

**48:5** And from the borders of Manasseh, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Ephraim, one.

**48:6** And from the borders of Ephraim, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Reuben, one.

**48:7** And from the borders of Reuben, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Judah, one.

**48:8** And from the borders of Judah, from the east side it shall remain; the firstfruit of the setting apart shall be twenty-five thousand in width, and in length like one of the portions from the east side and as far as the side toward the sea, and the holy place shall be in the midst of them.

**48:9** The firstfruit which they shall set apart to the Lord shall be twenty-five thousand in length and twenty-five thousand in width.

**48:10** Of these shall be the firstfruit of the holy places for the priests: toward the north twenty-five thousand, and toward the sea ten thousand, and toward the south twenty-five thousand; and the mountain of the holy places shall be in the midst of it;

**48:11** for the consecrated priests, the sons of Zadok, who keep the charges of the house, who did not go astray in the straying of the sons of Israel, in the way that the Levites went astray.

**48:12** And the firstfruit shall be given to them from the firstfruits of the land, a holy of holies, from the borders of the Levites.

**48:13** And for the Levites, what is adjacent to the borders of the priests shall be twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width; the entire length shall be twenty-five thousand, and the width twenty thousand.

**48:14** None of it shall be sold, nor measured out, nor shall the first produce of the land be taken away, for it is holy to the Lord.

**48:15** And as for the five thousand that remain in breadth alongside the twenty-five thousand, it shall be an outer wall for the city, for dwelling and for its open space; and the city shall be in the midst of it.

**48:16** And these are its measurements: on the north side, four thousand five hundred; and on the south side, four thousand five hundred; and on the east side, four thousand five hundred; and on the side toward the sea, four thousand five hundred.

**48:17** And there shall be an open space for the city: toward the north two hundred fifty, and toward the south two hundred fifty, and toward the east two hundred fifty, and toward the sea two hundred fifty.

**48:18** And the remainder of the length, adjacent to the firstfruits of the holy places, shall be ten thousand toward the east and ten thousand toward the sea; and they shall be the firstfruits of the holy place, and its produce shall be for bread for those who work the city.

**48:19** And those who work the city shall work it from all the tribes of Israel.

**48:20** The entire firstfruit shall be twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; as a square you shall set apart there the beginning of the holy place, from the possession of the city.

**48:21** And the remainder shall belong to the ruler, on this side and on that side, from the firstfruits of the holy place and to the possession of the city, along twenty-five thousand in length as far as the borders toward the east, and toward the sea along twenty-five thousand as far as the borders toward the sea, adjacent to the portions of the ruler; and the firstfruit of the holy places and the sanctuary of the house shall be in the midst of it.

**48:22** And from the Levites, from the possession of the city, it shall be in the midst of the rulers, between the borders of Judah and the borders of Benjamin; it shall belong to the rulers.

**48:23** And as for the remainder of the tribes, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Benjamin, one.

**48:24** And from the borders of Benjamin, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Simeon, one.

**48:25** And from the borders of Simeon, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Issachar, one.

**48:26** And from the borders of Issachar, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Zebulun, one.

**48:27** And from the borders of Zebulun, from the east side as far as the side toward the sea: Gad, one.

**48:28** And from the borders of Gad as far as the side toward the southwest, then its borders shall be from Teman and the water of Barimoth Kadesh, of an inheritance, as far as the Great Sea.

**48:29** This is the land which you shall cast by lot for the tribes of Israel, and these are their divisions, says the Lord God.

**48:30** And these are the exits of the city: those toward the north, four thousand five hundred by measure.

**48:31** And the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes of Israel; three gates toward the north: the gate of Reuben, one; and the gate of Judah, one; and the gate of Levi, one.

**48:32** And the east side, four thousand five hundred; and three gates: the gate of Joseph, one; and the gate of Benjamin, one; and the gate of Dan, one.

**48:33** And the south side, four thousand five hundred by measure; and three gates: the gate of Simeon, one; and the gate of Issachar, one; and the gate of Zebulun, one.

**48:34** And the side toward the sea, four thousand five hundred by measure; and three gates: the gate of Gad, one; and the gate of Asher, one; and the gate of Naphtali, one.

**48:35** The circumference shall be eighteen thousand. And the name of the city, from whatever day it comes into being, shall be its name.

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — The Greek reads 'the descent of that which cuts around' where the Hebrew has 'the way of Hethlon,' and 'the court of Ailam' as an LXX-distinct proper name where the Hebrew has 'Hazar-enan.'
- **48:10** — The Greek omits the eastern measurement found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and reads 'the mountain of the holy places' (Hebrew: 'the sanctuary of the Lord'). Earlier in v. 9 the Greek also gives a width of twenty-five thousand (and twenty thousand in v. 13) where the Hebrew has ten thousand.
- **48:15** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for 'common use' with a word meaning 'outer wall' or 'outwork,' apparently reading the similar Hebrew word for a rampart.
- **48:28** — The Greek preserves several distinct geographical readings: 'Teman' (Hebrew: 'Tamar'), the LXX-distinct name 'Barimoth Kadesh' (Hebrew: 'Meribath-kadesh'), and 'of an inheritance' (reading the Hebrew word for 'toward the brook' as 'inheritance').
- **48:35** — Instead of the Hebrew Masoretic Text's city name 'the Lord Is There,' the Greek reflects a different reading and vocalization of the Hebrew consonants, rendering 'from whatever day it comes into being, shall be its name.'
