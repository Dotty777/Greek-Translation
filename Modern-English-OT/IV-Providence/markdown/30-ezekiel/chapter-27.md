## Text

**27:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**27:2** "Son of man, you too, take up a lamentation over Tyre,

**27:3** and you shall say to Tyre, who dwells at the entrance of the sea, the trading center of the peoples from many islands, 'Thus says the Lord to Tyre:

**27:4** You said, "I have placed my beauty around myself." In the heart of the sea, for Beelim, your sons placed beauty around you.

**27:5** Cedar from Senir was built for you; strips of cypress planks from Lebanon were taken to make fir masts for you;

**27:6** from Bashan they made your oars; your sanctuaries they made of ivory, wooded houses from the islands of Kittim.

**27:7** Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt became bedding for you, to place glory around you and to clothe you with blue and purple from the islands of Elishah, and they became your coverings.

**27:8** And your rulers who dwell in Sidon and the Arvadites became your rowers; your wise men, Tyre, who were in you, these were your helmsmen;

**27:9** the elders of Byblos and their wise men were in you; these were strengthening your counsel; and all the ships of the sea and their rowers were yours to the west of the west.

**27:10** Persians and Lydians and Libyans were in your force; your fighting men hung shields and helmets in you; these gave your glory.

**27:11** The sons of the Arvadites and your force were on your walls; guards were in your towers; they hung their quivers on your anchorages all around; these perfected your beauty.

**27:12** The Carthaginians were your merchants from the abundance of all your strength; silver and gold and iron and tin and lead they gave for your market.

**27:13** Greece, and the whole world, and the bordering regions, these traded with you in human lives, and bronze vessels they gave for your trade;

**27:14** from the house of Togarmah, horses and horsemen supplied your market.

**27:15** Sons of the Rhodians were merchants; from the islands they multiplied your trade with ivory tusks, and for the things brought in you gave your wages in return;

**27:16** human beings as your trade from the abundance of your mixed wares, oil of myrrh and embroideries from Tarshish; and Lamoth and Chorchor supplied your market.

**27:17** Judah and the sons of Israel, these were your merchants in the sale of wheat and perfumes, and cassia; and finest honey and oil and resin they gave into your mixed wares.

**27:18** Damascus was your merchant from the abundance of all your power; wine from Helbon and wool from Miletus, and wine they gave into your market.

**27:19** From Asel is worked iron, and a wheel is in your mixed wares.

**27:20** Dedan were your merchants with choice beasts for chariots.

**27:21** Arabia and all the rulers of Kedar, these were your merchants by your hand: camels and rams and lambs, in which they trade with you.

**27:22** The merchants of Sheba and Raamah, these were your merchants with the finest spices and precious stones, and gold they gave for your market.

**27:23** Haran and Canneh, these were your merchants.

**27:24** Asshur and Charman were your merchants, bringing blue cloth as trade, and choice treasures bound with cords, and cypress wood.

**27:25** Ships were your merchants in the abundance, in your mixed wares; and you were filled and exceedingly weighed down in the heart of the sea.

**27:26** In much water your rowers led you. The south wind shattered you in the heart of the sea.

**27:27** There were your forces, and your wage and that of your mixed wares, and your rowers and your helmsmen and your counselors, and your mixed people from your mixed wares, and all your fighting men who are in you; and all your assembly in the midst of you will fall in the heart of the sea in the day of your fall.

**27:28** At the cry of your voice, your helmsmen will be terrified with fear,

**27:29** and all the rowers and the passengers will go down from the ships, and the lookouts of the sea will stand on the land,

**27:30** and they will wail over you with their voice and cry out bitterly, and they will put earth on their head and spread ashes for themselves.

**27:32** And their sons will take up a lamentation over you, a lament for Tyre.

**27:33** How great a wage did you find from the sea? You filled nations from your abundance, and from your mixed wares you enriched all the kings of the earth.

**27:34** Now you were shattered in the sea; in the depth of the water are your mixed wares, and all your assembly is in the midst of you; all your rowers fell.

**27:35** All those who dwell in the islands grew gloomy over you, and their kings were astounded with astonishment, and their face shed tears.

**27:36** Merchants from the nations hissed at you; you became destruction, and you will be no more forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:4** — Throughout chapter 27 the Greek calls Tyre Sor (transliterating Hebrew Tsor, versus Tyros in chapter 28). Here the Greek also reads the proper name Beelim and "your sons," whereas the Hebrew MT has "your borders" and "your builders."
- **27:9** — Greek uses the classical name Byblos for Hebrew Gebal, reads "strengthening your counsel" (MT "caulking your seams"), and renders Hebrew ma'arab ("merchandise") as "to the west of the west" (epi dysmas dysmon).
- **27:12** — Greek renders Hebrew Tarshish as Carthaginians here (contrast Tarshish in v. 16); likewise in v. 13 Greek renders Hebrew Javan, Tubal, and Meshech as "Greece, and the whole world, and the bordering regions," and in v. 15 reads Rhodians where the MT has Dedan.
- **27:16** — Greek reads "human beings," Tarshish, and the proper names Lamoth and Chorchor (where the MT has Aram/Edom, linen, coral, and rubies), and renders Hebrew ma'arab as "mixed wares" (symmiktos, vv. 16-34). Similarly, Greek reads Miletus in v. 18, Asel and "a wheel" (trochias) in v. 19, and Charman in v. 24 (MT Chilmad).
- **27:32** — Verse 31 of the Hebrew MT is absent from this Greek text, and here Greek reads "their sons" and omits the opening question of the lament ("Who is like Tyre?").
