## Text

**28:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:2** "And you, son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre, 'Thus says the Lord: Because your heart was lifted up, and you said, "I am a god; I have dwelt in the dwelling of a god in the heart of the sea," yet you are a man and not a god, and you set your heart like the heart of a god,

**28:3** are you really wiser than Daniel? Did wise men not instruct you with their knowledge?

**28:4** Did you really, by your knowledge or your understanding, make power for yourself, and gold and silver in your treasuries?

**28:5** By your great knowledge and trade you multiplied your power; your heart was lifted up in your power.

**28:6** Therefore, thus says the Lord: Since you have set your heart like the heart of a god,

**28:7** for this reason, look, I am bringing against you foreigners, pestilent ones from the nations, and they will draw out their swords against you and against the beauty of your knowledge, and they will lay your beauty low to destruction,

**28:8** and they will bring you down, and you will die the death of the wounded in the heart of the sea.

**28:9** Will you really say, "I am a god," before those who are killing you? Yet you are a man and not a god.

**28:10** Among a multitude of the uncircumcised you will perish by the hands of foreigners, for I have spoken, says the Lord.'"

**28:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:12** "Son of man, take up a lamentation over the ruler of Tyre and say to him, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: You, a seal of likeness

**28:13** and a crown of beauty, were in the delight of the paradise of God. You have bound on yourself every precious stone: sardius and topaz and emerald, and ruby and sapphire and jasper, and silver and gold, and jacinth and agate and amethyst, and chrysolite and beryl and onyx; and with gold you filled your treasuries and your storehouses in you.

**28:14** From the day you were created with the cherub, I placed you on the holy mountain of God; you were in the midst of fiery stones,

**28:15** you were blameless in your days, from the day you were created until the wrongdoings were found in you.

**28:16** From the abundance of your trade you filled your inner chambers with lawlessness, and you sinned and were wounded from the mountain of God, and the cherub led you out from the midst of fiery stones.

**28:17** Your heart was lifted up because of your beauty; your knowledge was corrupted with your beauty; because of the multitude of your sins I cast you to the ground; before kings I gave you to be made a public example.

**28:18** Because of the multitude of your sins and the injustices of your trade, I profaned your sanctuaries, and I will bring out fire from the midst of you; this will devour you; and I will make you ashes upon your land before all who see you.

**28:19** And all who know you among the nations will groan over you; you became destruction, and you will exist no longer forever.'"

**28:20** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**28:21** "Son of man, set your face toward Sidon and prophesy against her,

**28:22** and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am against you, Sidon, and I will be glorified in you, and you will know that I am the Lord when I execute judgments in you, and I will be sanctified in you.

**28:23** Blood and death will be in your streets, and those wounded by swords will fall in you all around you; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**28:24** And there will no longer be in the house of Israel a brier of bitterness and a thorn of pain from those all around them who dishonored them; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**28:25** Thus says the Lord, the Lord: And I will gather Israel from the nations where they were scattered, and I will be sanctified in them and before the peoples and the nations.

**28:26** And they will dwell upon their land, which I have given to My servant Jacob; and they will dwell upon it in hope, and they will build houses and plant vineyards, and they will dwell in hope, when I execute judgment among all those who dishonored them, among those around them; and they will know that I am the Lord their God and the God of their fathers.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Greek theos without the article is rendered "a god" here and in vv. 6, 9 to match the contrast with "a man," though it can also be translated "God." In v. 3 Greek asks two rhetorical questions rather than making the declarative statements of the Hebrew MT.
- **28:12** — Greek reads the double divine title kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord," also v. 25) and addresses "the ruler of Tyre" (as in v. 2), where the MT here has "king."
- **28:13** — Greek lists all twelve gemstones of the high priest's breastpiece (cf. Exod 28:17-20) along with silver and gold, whereas the Hebrew MT lists nine stones.
- **28:14** — Greek reads "with the cherub" (meta tou cheroub), distinguishing the ruler of Tyre from the cherub who leads him out in v. 16, whereas the Hebrew MT reads "you were an anointed guardian cherub." In v. 15 Greek reads "in your days" (MT "in your ways"), and in v. 18 "I profaned" (MT "you profaned").
