## Text

**6:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:2** "Son of man, set your face firmly against the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them,

**6:3** and you shall say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord to the mountains and to the hills and to the ravines and to the valleys: Look, I am bringing a sword upon you, and your high places will be utterly destroyed,

**6:4** and your altars and your shrines will be broken, and I will cast down your wounded before your idols,

**6:5** and I will scatter your bones around your altars.

**6:6** In all your habitation the cities will be laid waste and the high places will be devastated, so that your altars may be utterly destroyed and your idols broken and your shrines removed,

**6:7** and wounded ones will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**6:8** When there are those of you who escape from the sword among the nations, and in your dispersion in the countries,

**6:9** then those of you who escape will remember Me among the nations where they were taken captive; I have sworn to their heart that prostitutes itself away from Me and to their eyes that prostitute themselves after their practices, and they will beat their faces for all their abominations;

**6:10** and they will know that I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

**6:11** Thus says the Lord: "Clap with your hand and stamp with your foot and say, 'Aha, aha!' over all the abominations of the house of Israel; by sword and by death and by famine they will fall.

**6:12** The one who is near will fall by sword, and the one who is far off will perish by death, and the one who is besieged will be brought to an end by famine, and I will bring My anger to an end upon them.

**6:13** And you will know that I am the Lord, when your wounded are in the midst of your idols around your altars, on every high hill and under a shady tree, where they gave a fragrant aroma to all their idols.

**6:14** And I will stretch out My hand against them, and I will make the land a desolation and a destruction from the wilderness of Deblatha, from all the habitation; and you will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Greek temene ("shrines" or "sacred precincts") renders Hebrew "incense altars," and traumatias (literally "wounded") is the standard Septuagint equivalent for Hebrew "slain" (also in 6:7, 13).
- **6:9** — Greek omomoka ("I have sworn") reflects reading the Hebrew consonants for "I was broken" as "I have sworn," and "their practices" and "beat their faces" follow the distinctive Septuagint text.
- **6:11** — Greek euge euge ("Aha, aha!", literally "Well done, well done!") expresses mocking exultation, contrasting with the Hebrew interjection "Alas!"
- **6:14** — Deblatha is the Septuagint proper name (Deblatha) corresponding to Hebrew Diblathah ("toward Diblah," often emended to Riblah); the Greek reads "from the wilderness of Deblatha" rather than the Masoretic Text's "more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblah."
