## Text

**9:1** And He cried out in my ears with a loud voice, saying, "The vengeance of the city has drawn near." And each one had the instruments of destruction in his hand.

**9:2** And look, six men were coming from the way of the high gate facing north, and each one's axe was in his hand; and one man in their midst was clothed in a full-length robe, and a sash of sapphire was on his waist; and they went in and stood next to the bronze altar.

**9:3** And the glory of the God of Israel went up from the cherubim, which was upon them, to the open court of the house.

**9:4** And He called the man clothed in the full-length robe, who had a sash on his waist, and said to him, "Pass through the midst of Jerusalem, and put a mark on the foreheads of the men who groan deeply and who are deeply grieved over all the lawless deeds being done in their midst."

**9:5** And to these He said in my hearing, "Go after him into the city, and strike, and do not spare with your eyes, and do not show mercy;

**9:6** kill old man and young man and virgin and infants and women to extermination, but do not draw near to any on whom the mark is; begin from My holy places." And they began from the elder men who were inside the house.

**9:7** And He said to them, "Defile the house, and fill the roads with the dead as you go out, and strike."

**9:8** And it happened, while they were striking, that I fell on my face, and I cried aloud and said, "Alas, Lord, are You wiping out those remaining of Israel when You pour out Your fury upon Jerusalem?"

**9:9** And He said to me, "The injustice of the house of Israel and Judah has grown very, very great, because the land was filled with many peoples, and the city was filled with injustice and uncleanness; because they said, 'The Lord has forsaken the land; the Lord does not look on.'

**9:10** And My eye will not spare, nor will I show mercy; I have put their ways upon their heads."

**9:11** And look, the man clothed in the full-length robe and girded at his waist with the sash answered, saying, "I have done just as You commanded me."

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Greek reads "a full-length robe" and "a sash of sapphire," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "linen" and "a scribe's writing case."
- **9:3** — The Greek reads "open court" (aithrion) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "threshold."
- **9:9** — The Greek reads "many peoples" and "injustice and uncleanness" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "blood" and "perversity."
