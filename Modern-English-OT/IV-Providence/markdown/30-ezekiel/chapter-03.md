## Text

**3:1** And He said to me, "Son of man, eat up this scroll, and go and speak to the sons of Israel."

**3:2** And He opened my mouth, and He fed me the scroll.

**3:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, your mouth shall eat, and your belly shall be filled with this scroll that has been given to you." And I ate it, and it became in my mouth like sweet-tasting honey.

**3:4** And He said to me, "Son of man, walk and go in to the house of Israel and speak My words to them,

**3:5** because you are not being sent out to a people of unintelligible speech—to the house of Israel—

**3:6** nor to many peoples of foreign voice or foreign tongue, nor heavy in tongue, whose words you will not understand; and if I had sent you out to such as these, these would have listened to you.

**3:7** But the house of Israel will by no means be willing to listen to you, because they do not wish to listen to Me; for all the house of Israel are contentious and hard-hearted.

**3:8** And look, I have made your face strong against their faces, and your contention I will strengthen against their contention,

**3:9** and it shall be continually stronger than rock. Do not be afraid of them nor be terrified before their face, because it is a provoking house."

**3:10** And He said to me, "Son of man, all the words that I have spoken with you, receive into your heart and hear with your ears,

**3:11** and walk, go in to the captivity, to the sons of your people, and you shall speak to them, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord,' whether indeed they hear, whether indeed they yield."

**3:12** And the Spirit took me up, and I heard behind me, and I heard a sound of a great earthquake: "Blessed be the glory of the Lord from His place."

**3:13** And I saw the sound of the wings of the living creatures flapping one against the other, and the sound of the wheels beside them, and the sound of the earthquake.

**3:14** And the Spirit lifted me up and took me up, and I went in the impulse of my spirit, and the hand of the Lord was strong upon me.

**3:15** And I entered into the captivity aloft, and I went around those dwelling by the river Chebar, those who were there; and I sat there seven days, moving about in their midst.

**3:16** And it came about after the seven days that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**3:17** "Son of man, I have given you as a watchman to the house of Israel, and you shall hear a word from My mouth, and you shall threaten them from Me.

**3:18** When I say to the lawless one, 'You will surely be put to death,' and you do not warn him nor speak to warn the lawless one to turn from his ways so that he may live, that lawless one will die by his unrighteousness, and I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:19** And if you warn the lawless one, and he does not turn from his lawlessness and the way, that lawless one will die in his unrighteousness, and you will deliver your soul.

**3:20** And when a righteous one turns from his righteous deeds and commits a transgression, and I put the torment before his face, he will die, because you did not warn him; and in his sins he will die, because his righteous deeds will certainly not be remembered; and I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:21** But if you warn the righteous one not to sin, and he does not sin, the righteous one will surely live, because you warned him; and you will deliver your own soul."

**3:22** And the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He said to me, "Arise and go out into the plain, and there it will be spoken to you."

**3:23** And I arose and went out into the plain, and look, there the glory of the Lord was standing, just as the vision and just as the glory that I saw by the river Chebar; and I fell upon my face.

**3:24** And the Spirit came upon me and stood me upon my feet, and He spoke to me and said to me, "Go in and be shut up in the midst of your house.

**3:25** And you, son of man, look, bonds have been put upon you, and they will bind you with them, and you shall certainly not go out from their midst.

**3:26** And I will bind up your tongue, and you will become mute, and you will not be to them a man who reproves, because it is a provoking house.

**3:27** And when I speak to you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Let the one who hears hear, and let the one who disobeys disobey,' because it is a provoking house."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Greek reads the third-person verb "He opened my mouth," whereas the Hebrew text reads the first-person "I opened my mouth."
- **3:8** — Greek uses "contentious" (3:7) and "contention" (3:8), followed by "continually stronger than rock" (3:9), differing from the Hebrew references to a hard forehead and emery/flint.
- **3:13** — Greek literally reads "I saw the sound of the wings," supplying the verb "I saw" before the sound of the flapping wings.
- **3:15** — Instead of the Hebrew place-name "Tel-abib" and "stunned," Greek reads "aloft" ("meteoros"), adds "I went around," and concludes with "moving about in their midst."
- **3:20** — Greek renders the Hebrew term for "stumbling block" as "the torment" ("ten basanon"), a characteristic Septuagint Ezekiel rendering; in 3:17, Greek likewise uses the stronger verb "threaten" ("diapeileomai") alongside "warn" ("diastellomai") in 3:18-21.
