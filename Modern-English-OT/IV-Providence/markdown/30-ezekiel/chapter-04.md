## Text

**4:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a brick and you shall place it before your face, and you shall sketch on it a city, Jerusalem,

**4:2** and you shall set a blockade against it, and build ramparts against it, and throw up a palisade against it, and set camps against it, and position the missile emplacements all around.

**4:3** And you, take for yourself an iron pan and you shall place it as an iron wall between you and the city, and you shall prepare your face against it, and it will be under siege, and you shall besiege it. This is a sign to the sons of Israel.

**4:4** And you shall lie down on your left side, and you shall lay the iniquities of the house of Israel upon it according to the number of the days, one hundred and fifty, that you will lie on it, and you shall bear their iniquities.

**4:5** And I have given you their two iniquities as a number of days, one hundred and ninety days, and you shall bear the iniquities of the house of Israel.

**4:6** And you shall complete these, and you shall lie down on your right side, and you shall bear the iniquities of the house of Judah for forty days; I have appointed for you a day for a year.

**4:7** And toward the siege of Jerusalem you shall prepare your face, and you shall strengthen your arm, and you shall prophesy against it.

**4:8** And look, I have placed bonds upon you, and do not turn from your one side to your other side until the days of your siege are completed.

**4:9** And you, take for yourself wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet and spelt, and you shall put them into one earthenware vessel, and you shall make them for yourself into loaves, and according to the number of the days that you sleep on your side, one hundred and ninety days, you shall eat them.

**4:10** And you shall eat your food by weight, twenty shekels a day; from time to time you shall eat them.

**4:11** And you shall drink water by measure; a sixth of a hin you shall drink from time to time.

**4:12** And you shall eat them as a barley ash-cake; you shall bake them in dung of human excrement before their eyes,

**4:13** and you shall say, 'This is what the Lord God of Israel says: In this way the sons of Israel will eat unclean things among the nations.'"

**4:14** And I said, "By no means, Lord God of Israel! Surely my soul has not been defiled with uncleanness, and I have not eaten anything torn by wild beasts or anything that died of itself from my birth until now, nor has any stale meat entered my mouth."

**4:15** And He said to me, "Look, I have given you cow dung instead of human dung, and you shall make your loaves over it."

**4:16** And He said to me, "Son of man, look, I am breaking the support of bread in Jerusalem, and they will eat bread by weight and in want, and they will drink water by measure and in desolation,

**4:17** so that they may be in want of bread and water; and a man and his brother will be devastated, and they will waste away in their iniquities."

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Septuagint specifies 150 days for Israel here and 190 days in 4:5 and 4:9 (combining Israel's 150 days and Judah's 40 days in 4:6), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has no number in 4:4 and 390 days in 4:5 and 4:9.
- **4:5** — Greek "their two iniquities" (tas duo adikias auton) reflects reading the unvocalized Hebrew consonants for "years of" as "two," referring to the combined periods of Israel and Judah totaling 190 days.
- **4:7** — Greek "you shall strengthen your arm" (stereoseis) differs from the Masoretic Text's "with your arm bared."
- **4:14** — Greek ei here serves as an asseverative particle ("surely"), likely an itacistic spelling of e ("truly, surely"); "from my birth" and "stale meat" follow the Septuagint wording rather than the Hebrew's "from my youth" and "tainted meat."
