## Text

**37:1** And the hand of the Lord came upon me, and the Lord brought me out in the Spirit and set me in the middle of the plain, and it was full of human bones.

**37:2** And He led me around them all the way around, and look, there were very many upon the face of the plain, very dry.

**37:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, will these bones live?" And I said, "Lord, You know these things."

**37:4** And He said to me, "Prophesy to these bones, and you will say to them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of the Lord:

**37:5** Thus says the Lord to these bones: Look, I am bringing a spirit of life into you,

**37:6** and I will place sinews upon you, and I will bring flesh up upon you, and I will stretch skin over you, and I will put My Spirit into you, and you will live; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**37:7** And I prophesied just as He commanded me. And it happened while I prophesied that, look, there was a shaking, and the bones came together, each one to its joint.

**37:8** And I looked, and see, sinews and flesh were growing upon them, and skin was coming up over them on top, and there was no spirit in them.

**37:9** And He said to me, "Prophesy to the spirit; prophesy, son of man, and say to the spirit, 'Thus says the Lord: Come from the four winds and breathe into these dead ones, and let them live.'"

**37:10** And I prophesied just as He commanded me; and the spirit entered into them and they lived, and they stood upon their feet, a very great assembly.

**37:11** And the Lord spoke to me, saying, "Son of man, these bones are all the house of Israel, and they say, 'Our bones have become dry, our hope has perished, we are lost.'

**37:12** Therefore prophesy and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am opening your tombs, and I will bring you up out of your tombs, and I will bring you into the land of Israel,

**37:13** and you will know that I am the Lord, when I open your graves to bring My people up out of the graves.

**37:14** And I will put My Spirit into you, and you will live, and I will place you upon your land, and you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken and will do it, says the Lord.'"

**37:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**37:16** "Son of man, take for yourself a rod and write upon it, 'Judah and the sons of Israel who are joined to him'; and you will take a second rod for yourself and write on it, 'For Joseph, the rod of Ephraim, and all the sons of Israel who are added to him.'

**37:17** And you will join them to one another for yourself into one rod to bind them, and they will be in your hand.

**37:18** And it will happen when the sons of your people say to you, 'Will you not tell us what these things mean to you?'

**37:19** then you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I will take the tribe of Joseph, which is by the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel that are joined to him, and I will place them upon the tribe of Judah, and they will become one rod in the hand of Judah.'

**37:20** And the rods on which you wrote will be in your hand before them,

**37:21** and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord, the Lord says: Look, I am taking all the house of Israel from the midst of the nations where they went, and I will gather them from all those around them and bring them into the land of Israel,

**37:22** and I will make them into a nation in My land and on the mountains of Israel; and one ruler will be theirs, and they will no longer be two nations, nor will they by any means be divided any longer into two kingdoms,

**37:23** so that they may no longer be defiled by their idols. And I will rescue them from all their lawless deeds in which they sinned, and I will cleanse them, and they will be a people to Me, and I, the Lord, will be God to them.

**37:24** And My servant David will be ruler in their midst; there will be one shepherd of all; for they will walk in My ordinances, and they will keep My judgments and do them.

**37:25** And they will dwell upon their land that I have given to My servant Jacob, where their fathers dwelt; and they themselves will dwell upon it. And David My servant will be ruler forever.

**37:26** And I will make a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant with them. And I will place My sanctuary in their midst forever,

**37:27** and My dwelling will be among them, and I will be God to them, and they will be My people.

**37:28** And the nations will know that I am the Lord who sanctifies them, when My sanctuary is in their midst forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The Greek text makes Kyrios ("the Lord") the nominative subject ("the Lord brought me out in the Spirit"), reads pedion ("plain") rather than "valley," and specifies anthropinon ("human") bones.
- **37:9** — Throughout 37:1-14 Greek uses the single noun pneuma for God's Spirit (including "My Spirit" in v. 6, where the MT lacks "My"), animating spirit or breath (vv. 5, 8-10), and the "four winds" (v. 9).
- **37:10** — Greek reads synagoge ("assembly"), where the Hebrew MT has "army."
- **37:19** — Greek uses phyle ("tribe") rather than "stick" for Joseph and Judah, and concludes "in the hand of Judah" where the Hebrew MT reads "in My hand."
- **37:22** — Greek uses archon ("ruler") here and in vv. 24-25, whereas the Hebrew MT has "king" in vv. 22, 24 and "prince" in v. 25.
