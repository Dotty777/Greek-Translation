## Text

**11:1** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the gate of the house of the Lord that is opposite, which faces east. And at the entrance of the gate were about twenty-five men, and I saw in their midst Jeconiah the son of Ezer and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, the leaders of the people.

**11:2** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise futile things and plan wicked counsel in this city,

**11:3** who say, 'Have the houses not recently been built? This is the pot, and we are the meat.'

**11:4** Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man."

**11:5** And the Spirit fell upon me and said to me, "Say, 'Thus says the Lord: So you have spoken, house of Israel, and the thoughts of your spirit I know.

**11:6** You have multiplied your dead in this city, and you have filled their streets with those struck down.

**11:7** Therefore, thus says the Lord: Your dead whom you struck down in its midst, they are the meat, and this is the pot, and I will bring you out from its midst.

**11:8** You fear a sword, and a sword I will bring upon you, says the Lord.

**11:9** And I will bring you out from its midst, and I will hand you over into the hands of foreigners, and I will execute judgments among you.

**11:10** By the sword you will fall; on the mountains of Israel I will judge you, and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**11:13** And it happened, as I was prophesying, that Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. And I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Alas, alas, Lord! Are You bringing the remnant of Israel to a complete end?"

**11:14** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**11:15** "Son of man, your brothers and the men of your captivity and all the house of Israel have been brought to an end, to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem said, 'Keep far away from the Lord; to us the land has been given as an inheritance.'

**11:16** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: I will cast them off among the nations, and I will scatter them into every land, and I will be to them a small sanctuary in the countries wherever they enter.'

**11:17** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: I will also receive them from the nations, and I will gather them from the countries where I dispersed them, and I will give them the land of Israel.

**11:18** And they will enter there, and they will remove all its abominations and all its lawless deeds from it.

**11:19** And I will give them a different heart, and a new spirit I will put within them; and I will pull out the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

**11:20** so that they may walk in My statutes, and keep My ordinances and carry them out; and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them.

**11:21** And toward the heart of their abominations and their lawless deeds, as their heart was going, I have put their ways upon their heads, says the Lord.'"

**11:22** And the cherubim lifted up their wings, and the wheels were beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them, above them.

**11:23** And the glory of the Lord went up from the midst of the city and stood upon the mountain that was opposite the city.

**11:24** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the land of the Chaldeans, to the captivity, in a vision by the Spirit of God. And I went up from the vision that I saw,

**11:25** and I spoke to the captivity all the words of the Lord that He had shown me.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Greek text names "Jeconiah the son of Ezer" (Iechonian ton tou Ezer), whereas the Hebrew text reads "Jaazaniah the son of Azzur."
- **11:3** — The Greek reads "Have the houses not recently been built?" rather than the Hebrew "the time is not near to build houses."
- **11:10** — The Greek reads "on the mountains of Israel" (epi ton oreon tou Israel, vs. Hebrew "at the border of Israel"), and verses 11-12 of the Hebrew text are absent from the Greek text.
- **11:15** — The Greek reads "the men of your captivity" (vs. Hebrew "your kindred") and "have been brought to an end" (syntetelestai), reflecting a reading of Hebrew kallah ("be finished") rather than kulloh ("all of it").
- **11:19** — The Greek text reads "a different heart" (kardian heteran), whereas the Hebrew text reads "one heart."
- **11:24** — The Greek text reads "And I went up from the vision that I saw" (first person), whereas the Hebrew reads "Then the vision that I had seen went up from me."
