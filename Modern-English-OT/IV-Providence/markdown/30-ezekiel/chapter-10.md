## Text

**10:1** And I saw, and look, above the expanse that was over the heads of the cherubim, like a sapphire stone, a likeness of a throne was upon them.

**10:2** And He said to the man clothed in the robe, "Go into the midst of the wheels that are beneath the cherubim, and fill your hands with coals of fire from the midst of the cherubim, and scatter them over the city." And he went in before me.

**10:3** And the cherubim were standing on the right side of the house as the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.

**10:4** And the glory of the Lord departed from the cherubim to the open court of the house, and the cloud filled the house, and the court was filled with the brightness of the glory of the Lord.

**10:5** And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God Shaddai speaking.

**10:6** And it happened, when He commanded the man clothed in the holy robe, saying, "Take fire from the midst of the wheels, from the midst of the cherubim," that he went in and stood beside the wheels.

**10:7** And he stretched out his hand into the midst of the fire that was in the midst of the cherubim, and he took some and put it into the hands of the one clothed in the holy robe, and he took it and went out.

**10:8** And I saw the cherubim, a likeness of men's hands beneath their wings.

**10:9** And I saw, and look, four wheels were standing beside the cherubim, one wheel beside one cherub, and the appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of a carbuncle stone.

**10:10** And their appearance was one likeness for the four, just as when a wheel is in the midst of a wheel.

**10:11** When they went, they went in their four directions; they did not turn as they went, because to whatever place the one head looked, they went, and they did not turn as they went.

**10:12** And their backs and their hands and their wings and the wheels were full of eyes all around on the four wheels.

**10:13** And these wheels were called Gelgel in my hearing.

**10:15** And the cherubim were this living creature that I saw by the river Chebar.

**10:16** And when the cherubim went, the wheels went, and these were beside them; and when the cherubim lifted up their wings to rise from the earth, their wheels did not turn.

**10:17** When they stood, these stood, and when they rose, these rose with them, because a spirit of life was in them.

**10:18** And the glory of the Lord went out from the house and mounted upon the cherubim,

**10:19** and the cherubim lifted up their wings and rose from the earth before me; as they went out, the wheels also were beside them; and they stood at the entrance of the gate of the house of the Lord that is opposite, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.

**10:20** This is the living creature that I saw beneath the God of Israel by the river Chebar, and I knew that they were cherubim.

**10:21** Each one had four faces, and each one had eight wings, and a likeness of a man's hands was beneath their wings.

**10:22** And as for the likeness of their faces, these are the faces that I saw beneath the glory of the God of Israel by the river Chebar, and they each went straight before their faces.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — Greek "theou Saddai" transliterates the Hebrew divine epithet El Shaddai ("God Shaddai") rather than translating it as "Almighty."
- **10:7** — The Greek text leaves the subject of "stretched out his hand" unexpressed (whereas the Hebrew specifies "the cherub"), and speaks of "the holy robe" here and in v. 6 rather than "linen."
- **10:13** — Greek "Gelgel" transliterates Hebrew galgal ("whirling wheels" or "wheelwork"); verse 14 of the Hebrew text, which describes the four faces, is absent from the Greek text.
- **10:21** — The Greek text reads "eight wings" (okto pteryges), whereas the Hebrew text reads "four wings."
