## Text

**25:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**25:2** "Son of man, set your face against the sons of Ammon and prophesy against them,

**25:3** and you will say to the sons of Ammon, 'Hear the word of the Lord! Thus says the Lord: Because you gloated over My sanctuary that it was profaned, and over the land of Israel that it was laid waste, and over the house of Judah that they went into captivity,

**25:4** therefore, look, I am handing you over to the sons of Kedem for an inheritance, and they will encamp with their gear in you, and they will set their tents in you; they themselves will eat your fruits, and they themselves will drink your fatness.

**25:5** And I will make the city of Ammon into pastures for camels, and the sons of Ammon into a pasture for sheep; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**25:6** For thus says the Lord: Because you clapped your hand and stamped with your foot, and gloated from your soul over the land of Israel,

**25:7** therefore I will stretch out My hand against you and give you for plunder among the nations, and I will exterminate you from the peoples, and I will destroy you from the lands with destruction; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**25:8** Thus says the Lord: "Because Moab said, 'Look, is not the house of Israel and Judah just like all the nations?'

**25:9** therefore, look, I am paralyzing the shoulder of Moab from his frontier cities, a choice land, the house of Thasimouth, of the ascent of a coastal city,

**25:10** the sons of Kedem against the sons of Ammon; I have given it to him for an inheritance, so that there may be no remembrance of the sons of Ammon.

**25:11** And against Moab I will execute vengeance, and they will know that I am the Lord."

**25:12** Thus says the Lord: "Because Idumea acted in taking vengeance against the house of Judah, and they held a grudge and exacted retribution,

**25:13** therefore thus says the Lord: I will also stretch out My hand against Idumea, and I will exterminate from her man and beast, and I will make her desolate, and those pursued from Teman will fall by the sword;

**25:14** and I will lay My vengeance upon Idumea by the hand of My people Israel, and they will act in Idumea according to My anger and according to My wrath; and they will know My vengeance," says the Lord.

**25:15** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Because the foreigners acted in vengeance and raised up vengeance, gloating from the soul to wipe out down to the last one,

**25:16** therefore thus says the Lord: Look, I will stretch out My hand against the foreigners, and I will exterminate the Cretans, and I will destroy the remnant who inhabit the seacoast;

**25:17** and I will execute great acts of vengeance among them, and they will know that I am the Lord, when I lay My vengeance upon them."

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — Greek "Kedem" transliterates the Hebrew word for "East" ("people of the East"), here and in v. 10; "your fatness" (pioteta) reflects a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants for "your milk."
- **25:9** — Greek "the house of Thasimouth, of the ascent of a coastal city" corresponds to the three Moabite cities named in the Hebrew MT (Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim).
- **25:12** — Greek "Idumea" (vv. 12-14) corresponds to Hebrew "Edom"; in v. 13 Greek "those pursued from Teman" differs from the Hebrew MT ("from Teman even to Dedan").
- **25:15** — Greek "the foreigners" (hoi allophyloi, vv. 15-16) is the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines, and in v. 16 Greek renders Hebrew "Cherethites" as "Cretans" (Kretas).
