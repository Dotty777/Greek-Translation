## Text

**13:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**13:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel, and you shall prophesy and say to them, 'Hear the word of the Lord:

**13:3** Thus says the Lord: Woe to those who prophesy from their own heart and do not see at all.

**13:4** Like foxes in the deserts are your prophets, Israel.

**13:5** They did not stand in steadfastness, and they gathered flocks against the house of Israel; those who say, "In the day of the Lord," did not rise up,

**13:6** seeing falsehoods, divining vain things, those who say, "The Lord says," yet the Lord has not sent them, and they began to establish a word.

**13:7** Have you not seen a false vision and spoken vain divinations?'

**13:8** Therefore say, 'Thus says the Lord: Because your words are false and your divinations are vain, therefore, look, I am against you, says the Lord,

**13:9** and I will stretch out My hand against the prophets who see falsehoods and who utter vain things. They will not be in the instruction of My people, nor will they be written in the register of the house of Israel, and they will not enter into the land of Israel; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**13:10** Because they led My people astray, saying, "Peace," and there was no peace; and this one builds a wall, and they plaster it; it will fall.'

**13:11** Say to those who plaster, 'It will fall, and there will be a flooding rain, and I will put catapult stones into their joints, and they will fall, and a sweeping wind, and it will be torn apart.

**13:12** And look, the wall has fallen, and will they not say to you, "Where is your plaster that you plastered?"

**13:13** Therefore thus says the Lord: And I will break loose a sweeping blast with wrath, and there will be a flooding rain in My anger, and in wrath I will bring the catapult stones to complete destruction,

**13:14** and I will tear down the wall that you plastered; it will fall. And I will lay it on the ground, and its foundations will be uncovered, and it will fall, and you will be brought to an end with rebukes; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**13:15** And I will complete My wrath upon the wall and upon those who plaster it; it will fall. And I said to you, "The wall is no more, nor those who plaster it,"

**13:16** the prophets of Israel, who prophesy concerning Jerusalem and who see peace for her, and there is no peace, says the Lord.'

**13:17** And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy from their own heart, and prophesy against them,

**13:18** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: Woe to the women who sew pillows under every elbow of a hand and who make veils upon every head of every stature, in order to pervert souls. The souls of My people were perverted, and they were preserving souls alive,

**13:19** and they were profaning Me among My people for a handful of barley and for pieces of bread, to put to death souls that should not die and to preserve alive souls that should not live, by your uttering to a people who listen to vain utterances.

**13:20** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: Look, I am against your pillows, upon which you round up souls there, and I will tear them from your arms, and I will send away the souls, whose souls you twist away, into dispersion,

**13:21** and I will tear apart your veils, and I will deliver My people from your hand, and they will no longer be in your hands for rounding up; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**13:22** Because you were perverting the heart of a righteous man, when I was not perverting him, and to strengthen the hands of a lawless man, not to turn back at all from his evil way so that he might live,

**13:23** therefore you shall by no means see falsehoods, and you shall no longer divine divinations, and I will deliver My people from your hand; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — The Greek text differs notably from the Hebrew ("in the breaches" / "built up a wall"), reading "in steadfastness" (en stereomati) and "they gathered flocks" (synegagon poimnia), and omitting "in battle" before "In the day of the Lord."
- **13:9** — Greek paideia ("instruction" or "discipline") stands where the Hebrew has "council," and the final verb is third person plural ("they will know") rather than second person.
- **13:10** — Here and in vv. 11, 14, and 15, the Greek renders the Hebrew word for "whitewash" as the future verb "it will fall" (peseitai).
- **13:14** — Greek met' elenchon ("with rebukes") reflects a different reading of the Hebrew consonants underlying "in its midst."
- **13:20** — Greek kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord," also in 14:6) reflects the doubled Hebrew divine title Adonai YHWH. Greek systrephete ("round up"), ekstrephete ("twist away"), and "into dispersion" (eis diaskorpismon) also diverge from the Hebrew bird-hunting imagery.
