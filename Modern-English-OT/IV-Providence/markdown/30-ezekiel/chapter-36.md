## Text

**36:1** And you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel, and say to the mountains of Israel, "Hear the word of the Lord:

**36:2** This is what the Lord, the Lord says: Because the enemy has said against you, 'Aha! Everlasting wastes have become a possession for us,'"

**36:3** therefore prophesy and say, "This is what the Lord, the Lord says: Because you have been dishonored and hated by those around you, so that you became a possession for the remaining nations, and you came up as talk on the tongue and as a reproach to the nations,

**36:4** therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord: This is what the Lord says to the mountains and to the hills and to the ravines and to the torrents and to the desolated and ruined places and to the forsaken cities, and they have become a plunder and a trampling for the remaining nations all around:

**36:5** therefore, this is what the Lord, the Lord says: Surely in the fire of My wrath I have spoken against the rest of the nations and against all Idumea, because they gave My land to themselves for a possession with gladness, dishonoring souls so as to lay waste in plunder."

**36:6** Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains and to the hills and to the ravines and to the valleys, "This is what the Lord says: Look, I have spoken in My jealousy and in My wrath, because you have borne the reproaches of nations.

**36:7** Therefore I will lift up My hand against the nations that are all around you; they themselves will receive their dishonor.

**36:8** But your grapes, mountains of Israel, and your fruit My people will eat, for they are hoping to come.

**36:9** For look, I am for you, and I will look upon you, and you will be cultivated and sown,

**36:10** and I will multiply people upon you, all the house of Israel completely; and the cities will be inhabited, and the desolate place will be built.

**36:11** And I will multiply people and livestock upon you, and I will settle you as in your beginning, and I will do good to you just as in your former times; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**36:12** And I will bring forth people upon you, My people Israel, and they will inherit you, and you will be a possession for them; and you will by no means be bereaved of them any longer."

**36:13** This is what the Lord, the Lord says: "Because they said to you, 'You are one who devours people, and you have become bereaved by your nation,'

**36:14** therefore you will no longer eat people, and you will no longer bereave your nation," says the Lord, the Lord.

**36:15** "And the dishonor of nations will no longer be heard against you, and you will by no means bear the reproaches of peoples," says the Lord, the Lord.

**36:16** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**36:17** "Son of man, the house of Israel dwelt upon their land, and they defiled it by their way and by their idols and their impurities, and like the impurity of a menstruating woman their way became before My face.

**36:18** And I poured out My wrath upon them,

**36:19** and I scattered them among the nations, and I dispersed them through the countries; according to their way and according to their sin I judged them.

**36:20** And they went into the nations where they went, and they profaned My holy name when it was said of them, 'These are the people of the Lord, and they have gone out from His land.'

**36:21** And I spared them for the sake of My holy name, which the house of Israel profaned among the nations where they went.

**36:22** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord says: It is not for you that I am acting, house of Israel, but rather for the sake of My holy name, which you profaned among the nations where you went.

**36:23** And I will sanctify My great name that was profaned among the nations, which you profaned in their midst; and the nations will know that I am the Lord, when I am sanctified in you before their eyes.

**36:24** And I will take you from the nations and gather you from all the lands, and I will bring you into your land.

**36:25** And I will sprinkle clean water upon you, and you will be cleansed from all your impurities and from all your idols, and I will cleanse you,

**36:26** and I will give you a new heart, and I will place a new spirit within you, and I will take away the stony heart from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh.

**36:27** And I will place My Spirit within you, and I will cause you to walk in My statutes and to keep My judgments and do them.

**36:28** And you will dwell upon the land that I gave to your fathers, and you will be a people to Me, and I will be God to you.

**36:29** And I will save you from all your impurities, and I will summon the grain and multiply it, and I will not bring famine upon you;

**36:30** and I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field, so that you may not receive the reproach of famine among the nations.

**36:31** And you will remember your evil ways and your practices that were not good, and you will feel loathing before their face because of your lawless deeds and over their abominations.

**36:32** It is not because of you that I am acting, says the Lord, the Lord; it is known to you. Be ashamed and humiliated because of your ways, house of Israel.'"

**36:33** This is what Adonai the Lord says: "On the day when I cleanse you from all your lawless deeds, I will also settle the cities, and desolate places will be built;

**36:34** and the ruined land will be worked, whereas it had become ruined before the eyes of everyone passing by.

**36:35** And they will say, 'That ruined land has become like a garden of delight, and the desolate and ruined and demolished cities have become fortified and inhabited.'

**36:36** And the nations, as many as are left around you, will know that I, the Lord, have built the torn-down places and planted the ruined places; I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it."

**36:37** This is what Adonai the Lord says: "Yet for this I will be sought by the house of Israel, to do it for them: I will multiply them with people like sheep,

**36:38** like holy sheep, like the sheep of Jerusalem at her feasts; so the desolate cities will be full of flocks of people, and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — Greek doubles the divine title (kyrios Kyrios, "the Lord, the Lord") here and elsewhere in Ezekiel to render Hebrew Adonai YHWH. Greek also reads erema aionia ("everlasting wastes") where the Hebrew MT has "ancient heights."
- **36:5** — Greek uses Idoumaia ("Idumea"), the Hellenistic geographical name corresponding to Edom in the Hebrew text. The closing clause ("dishonoring souls so as to lay waste in plunder") reflects a distinct Septuagint reading.
- **36:31** — The Greek text has third-person plural pronouns ("before their face" and "over their abominations") where the Hebrew MT reads second-person plural ("your").
- **36:33** — Here and in 36:37 the Greek text retains the Hebrew transliteration Adonai before Kyrios ("Adonai the Lord"), marking a notable translational shift within 36:23b-38.
- **36:35** — Greek translates Hebrew Eden by its sense as kepos tryphes ("a garden of delight") rather than transliterating it as a proper name.
