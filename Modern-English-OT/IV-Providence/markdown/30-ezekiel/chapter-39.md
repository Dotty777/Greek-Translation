## Text

**39:1** "And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog and say, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am against you, Gog, ruler of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal.

**39:2** And I will gather you and guide you and bring you up at the farthest north, and I will bring you up onto the mountains of Israel.

**39:3** And I will destroy your bow from your left hand and your arrows from your right hand, and I will cast you down upon the mountains of Israel,

**39:4** and you will fall, you and all those around you, and the nations with you will be given to multitudes of birds; to every winged creature and to all the wild beasts of the field I have given you to be devoured.

**39:5** Upon the face of the field you will fall, for I have spoken, says the Lord.

**39:6** And I will send fire upon Gog, and the islands will be inhabited in peace, and they will know that I am the Lord.

**39:7** And My holy name will be made known in the midst of My people Israel, and My holy name will no longer be profaned; and the nations will know that I am the Lord, holy in Israel.

**39:8** Look, it has come, and you will know that it will be, says the Lord, the Lord; this is the day on which I spoke.

**39:9** And those who dwell in the cities of Israel will go out and burn the weapons—shields and pikes and bows and arrows and hand staffs and spears—and they will burn fire with them for seven years;

**39:10** and they will by no means take wood from the field nor cut from the forests, but rather they will burn up the weapons with fire; and they will plunder those who plundered them, and they will despoil those who despoiled them, says the Lord.

**39:11** And it will be in that day that I will give Gog a renowned place, a tomb in Israel, the burial ground of those who approached by the sea, and they will build a wall around the mouth of the ravine;

**39:12** and they will bury Gog there and all his multitude, and it will be called Te, the Burial Ground of Gog. And the house of Israel will bury them so that the land may be cleansed in seven months,

**39:13** and all the people of the land will bury them, and it will be renowned for them on the day I was glorified, says the Lord.

**39:14** And they will continually set apart men going throughout the land to bury those left behind upon the face of the land, to cleanse it after the seven months, and they will search thoroughly.

**39:15** And everyone who passes through the land and sees a human bone will build a marker beside it, until the buriers bury it in Gai, the Burial Ground of Gog.

**39:16** For the name of the city is also Burial Ground; and the land will be cleansed.'

**39:17** And you, son of man, say, 'Thus says the Lord: Speak to every winged bird and to all the wild beasts of the field, "Gather together and come, gather together from all around to My sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you, a great sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, and you will eat flesh and drink blood.

**39:18** You will eat the flesh of giants and drink the blood of the rulers of the earth—rams and calves and male goats, and the calves are all fattened.

**39:19** And you will eat fat to fullness, and you will drink blood to drunkenness from My sacrifice which I sacrificed for you.

**39:20** And you will be filled at My table with horse and rider and giant and every man of war," says the Lord.

**39:21** And I will set My glory among you, and all the nations will see My judgment which I executed and My hand which I brought upon them.

**39:22** And the house of Israel will know that I am the Lord their God from this day and onward.

**39:23** And all the nations will know that because of their sins the house of Israel was taken captive, because they acted faithlessly against Me, and I turned My face away from them and delivered them into the hands of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword.

**39:24** According to their impurities and according to their lawless deeds I dealt with them, and I turned My face away from them.

**39:25** Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord: Now I will turn back the captivity in Jacob, and I will have mercy on the house of Israel, and I will be zealous for My holy name.

**39:26** And they will bear their dishonor and the wrongdoing which they committed, when they are settled upon their land in peace, and there will be no one to cause fear,

**39:27** when I bring them back from the nations and gather them from the lands of the nations, and I will be sanctified in them before the nations;

**39:28** and they will know that I am the Lord their God, when I appear to them among the nations.

**39:29** And I will no longer turn My face away from them, because I have poured out My fury upon the house of Israel, says the Lord, the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **39:12** — 'Te' (Greek To te, a variant transcription of Hebrew ge, 'valley'; compare 'Gai' in 39:15) is paired with polyandrion ('Burial Ground,' literally 'place of many men,' also the city name in 39:16), which translates Hebrew Hamon ('multitude') as a common burial site.
- **39:18** — The Greek renders the Hebrew term for mighty warriors as 'giants' (giganton; also singular giganta in 39:20) and omits 'of Bashan.'
- **39:21** — The Greek reads 'among you' (en hymin), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'among the nations.'
- **39:28** — The Greek concludes with 'when I appear to them among the nations' (en to epiphanenai me autois en tois ethnesin), lacking the longer Masoretic statement about sending Israel into exile and leaving none behind.
- **39:29** — The Greek reads 'My fury' (ton thymon mou), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'My Spirit.'
