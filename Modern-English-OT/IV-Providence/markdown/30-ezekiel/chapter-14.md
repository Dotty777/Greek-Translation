## Text

**14:1** And men from the elders of Israel came to me, and sat before my face.

**14:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:3** "Son of man, these men have set their thoughts upon their hearts, and they have placed the punishment of their iniquities before their face; should I indeed answer them?

**14:4** Therefore speak to them, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Any man from the house of Israel who sets his thoughts upon his heart, and places the punishment of his iniquity before his face, and comes to the prophet, I, the Lord, will answer him in the things in which his mind is entangled,

**14:5** so that it may turn aside the house of Israel according to their hearts that are estranged from Me in their devices.'

**14:6** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: Turn back and turn away from your practices and from all your impieties, and turn back your faces.

**14:7** For any man from the house of Israel and from the sojourners who sojourn in Israel who becomes estranged from Me, and sets his devices upon his heart, and places the punishment of his iniquity before his face, and comes to the prophet to inquire of him concerning Me, I, the Lord, will answer him in that in which he is entangled.

**14:8** And I will set My face against that man, and I will make him a wasteland and a desolation, and I will remove him from the midst of My people; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**14:9** And if the prophet misleads and speaks, I, the Lord, have misled that prophet, and I will stretch out My hand against him and wipe him out from the midst of My people Israel.

**14:10** And they will bear their iniquity; according to the wrongdoing of the one who inquires, likewise according to the wrongdoing it will be for the prophet,

**14:11** so that the house of Israel may no longer go astray from Me, and that they may no longer be defiled in all their transgressions; and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them, says the Lord.'"

**14:12** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:13** "Son of man, whatever land sins against Me by committing a transgression, I will stretch out My hand against it and break its support of bread, and I will send out famine upon it and remove from it man and beasts.

**14:14** And if these three men are in its midst, Noah and Daniel and Job, they themselves will be saved by their righteousness, says the Lord.

**14:15** If I also bring evil wild animals upon the land and I punish it, and it becomes a desolation, and there is no one passing through because of the wild animals,

**14:16** and these three men are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, neither sons nor daughters will be saved, but rather they alone will be saved, and the land will be for destruction.

**14:17** Or if I also bring a sword upon that land and say, 'Let a sword pass through the land,' and I remove from it man and beast,

**14:18** and these three men are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, they will by no means deliver sons or daughters; they alone will be saved.

**14:19** Or if I also send death upon that land and pour out My wrath upon it in blood to utterly destroy from it man and beast,

**14:20** and Noah and Daniel and Job are in its midst, as I live, says the Lord, neither sons nor daughters will be left behind; they themselves by their righteousness will deliver their own souls.

**14:21** Thus says the Lord: Even if I also send out My four evil acts of vengeance, sword and famine and evil wild animals and death, upon Jerusalem to utterly destroy from her man and beast,

**14:22** yet look, left behind in her are those of her who have been saved, who lead out from her sons and daughters; look, they are coming out to you, and you will see their ways

**14:23** and their devices, and you will know that I have not done in vain all that I did in her, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — Throughout vv. 3-7, the Greek renders Hebrew "idols" with "thoughts" (dianoemata, vv. 3-4) and "devices" (enthymemata, vv. 5, 7), and translates "stumbling block" as "punishment" (kolasin).
- **14:5** — Greek plagiase ("may turn aside" or "outflank") is third person singular (continuing "his mind" from v. 4, or an indefinite subject) where the Hebrew has "so that I may seize."
- **14:9** — Greek planese is active ("misleads," or intransitively "goes astray") where the Hebrew has a passive verb ("is deceived").
- **14:19** — Here and in v. 21, the Greek translates Hebrew "pestilence" as "death" (thanaton).
- **14:23** — The Greek text is substantially shorter than the Hebrew, omitting the clauses about being comforted when seeing the survivors' ways and deeds; in v. 22, Greek exagousin ("lead out") is also active rather than passive.
