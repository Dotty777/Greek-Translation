## Text

**5:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a sword sharper than a barber's razor; you shall acquire it for yourself, and you shall bring it over your head and over your beard. And you shall take a balance of weights and divide them:

**5:2** a fourth you shall burn in fire in the middle of the city at the completion of the days of the siege, and you shall take a fourth and burn it up in its midst, and a fourth you shall cut up with a sword around it, and a fourth you shall scatter to the wind, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**5:3** And you shall take from there a few in number and wrap them up in your cloak.

**5:4** And again you shall take some of these and throw them into the middle of the fire, and burn them up in fire; from it fire will go out. And you shall say to the whole house of Israel,

**5:5** 'This is what the Lord says: This is Jerusalem; in the midst of the nations I have set her, and the countries around her.

**5:6** And you shall speak My ordinances to the lawless one from the nations, and My statutes from the countries around her, because they rejected My ordinances, and in My statutes they did not walk in them.

**5:7** Therefore, this is what the Lord says: Because your impulse is from the nations around you, and you did not walk in My statutes, and you did not do My ordinances, nor have you even acted according to the ordinances of the nations around you,

**5:8** therefore, this is what the Lord says: Look, I am against you, and I will execute judgment in your midst before the nations,

**5:9** and I will do in you things that I have not done and the like of which I will not do again, according to all your abominations.

**5:10** Therefore, fathers will eat children in your midst, and children will eat fathers; and I will execute judgments in you, and I will scatter all your survivors to every wind.

**5:11** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, surely because you defiled My holy things with all your abominations, I too will reject you; My eye will not spare, and I too will show no mercy.

**5:12** A fourth of you will be consumed by death, and a fourth of you will be brought to an end by famine in your midst, and a fourth of you, I will scatter them to every wind, and a fourth of you will fall by sword around you, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**5:13** And My wrath and My anger will be brought to an end upon them, and you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken in My zeal, when I bring My anger to an end upon them.

**5:14** And I will make you a wasteland, and your daughters around you, before everyone who passes by,

**5:15** and you will be lamented and wretched among the nations around you, when I execute judgments in you in the vengeance of My wrath; I, the Lord, have spoken;

**5:16** And when I send out My arrows of famine upon them, they will be for extinction, and I will break your support of bread.

**5:17** And I will send out upon you famine and evil wild beasts, and I will punish you, and death and blood will pass through upon you, and I will bring a sword upon you from all around; I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Septuagint divides the hair into four fourths (to tetarton four times, matching 5:12), including a doubled burning clause, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text divides it into three thirds.
- **5:6** — Greek "you shall speak My ordinances to the lawless one" reflects reading the Hebrew verb "she rebelled" as "you shall speak" and rendering "wickedness" as a personal dative.
- **5:12** — Greek thanatos ("death") is the Septuagint's regular rendering of Hebrew "pestilence" in the judgment triad of sword, famine, and death (see also 5:17; 6:11-12).
- **5:14** — Greek "and your daughters around you" (referring to Jerusalem's surrounding satellite towns) differs from the Masoretic Text's "and a reproach among the nations around you."
- **5:15** — The rare Greek adjective pair stenakte kai delaiste ("lamented and wretched") includes the Septuagint hapax legomenon delaiste, glossed in ancient lexica as "wretched" or "miserable."
