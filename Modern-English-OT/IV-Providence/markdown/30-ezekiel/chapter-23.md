## Text

**23:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**23:2** "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother,

**23:3** and they prostituted themselves in Egypt in their youth; there their breasts fell, there they were deflowered.

**23:4** And their names were Oholah the older and Oholibah her sister. And they became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. And their names: Samaria was Oholah, and Jerusalem was Oholibah.

**23:5** And Oholah prostituted herself away from Me and threw herself upon her lovers, upon the Assyrians who were drawing near to her,

**23:6** clothed in blue-purple, rulers and commanders; young men and choice ones, all of them horsemen riding on horses.

**23:7** And she gave her prostitution to them—all of them choice sons of the Assyrians—and with all upon whom she threw herself, she was defiling herself with all her devices.

**23:8** And she did not abandon her prostitution from Egypt, because they lay with her in her youth, and they themselves deflowered her and poured out their prostitution upon her.

**23:9** Therefore I handed her over into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the sons of the Assyrians, upon whom she was throwing herself.

**23:10** They uncovered her shame, took her sons and daughters, and killed her with a sword; and she became a subject of talk among women, and they executed punishments upon her for the daughters.

**23:11** And her sister Oholibah saw, and she corrupted her advances more than she did, and her prostitution more than the prostitution of her sister.

**23:12** She threw herself upon the sons of the Assyrians, rulers and commanders who were near her, clothed in finely bordered garments, horsemen riding on horses—all of them choice young men.

**23:13** And I saw that she was defiled; the two of them had one way.

**23:14** And she added to her prostitution, and she saw men painted on the wall, images of Chaldeans, painted with a stylus,

**23:15** girded with multicolored fabrics around their waists, dyed cloths also on their heads, a threefold appearance of all of them, a likeness of the sons of the Chaldeans, of the land of his homeland.

**23:16** And she threw herself upon them at the sight of her eyes, and she sent out messengers to them to the land of the Chaldeans.

**23:17** And the sons of Babylon came to her, into a bed of lodgers, and they were defiling her with her prostitution; and she was defiled by them, and her soul turned away from them.

**23:18** And she uncovered her prostitution, and she uncovered her shame; and My soul turned away from her, just as My soul had turned away from her sister.

**23:19** And you multiplied your prostitution to call to mind the day of your youth, in which you prostituted yourself in Egypt,

**23:20** and you threw yourself upon the Chaldeans, whose flesh is like that of donkeys, and their genitals are the genitals of horses,

**23:21** and you revisited the lawlessness of your youth, the things that you were doing in Egypt in your lodging, where the breasts of your youth were.

**23:22** Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord says: 'Look, I am rousing your lovers against you, from whom your soul turned away, and I will bring them against you from all around:

**23:23** the sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, Phakouk, Soue, and Hychoue, and all the sons of the Assyrians with them—choice young men, governors and commanders, all of them threefold and renowned, riding on horses.

**23:24** And they will all come against you from the north, chariots and wheels with a crowd of peoples, large shields and small shields, and he will set a guard against you all around.

**23:25** And I will set judgment before their face, and they will punish you by their judgments. And I will direct My jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in furious wrath; they will remove your nose and your ears, and they will strike down your survivors with a sword. They will take your sons and your daughters, and fire will devour your survivors.

**23:26** And they will strip you of your clothing, and they will take the articles of your boasting.

**23:27** And I will turn away your impieties from you and your prostitution from the land of Egypt, and you will by no means lift up your eyes to them, and you will never remember Egypt again.'

**23:28** For this is what the Lord, the Lord says: 'Look, I am handing you over into the hands of those whom you hate, from whom your soul turned away.

**23:29** And they will deal with you in hatred, and they will take all your labors and your toils, and you will be naked and shamed, and the shame of your prostitution and your impiety will be uncovered. And your prostitution

**23:30** did these things to you, in your prostituting yourself after nations, and you were defiling yourself with their devices.

**23:31** You walked in the way of your sister, and I will put her cup into your hands.'

**23:32** This is what the Lord says: 'You will drink the cup of your sister, the deep and wide and overflowing one, to bring drunkenness to completion,

**23:33** and you will be filled with exhaustion; and the cup of ruin is the cup of your sister, and you will drink it,

**23:34** and I will turn away her festivals and her new moons; because I have spoken,' says the Lord.

**23:35** Therefore, this is what the Lord says: 'Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your body, you too, bear your impiety and your prostitution.'"

**23:36** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, will you not judge Oholah and Oholibah? And declare to them their lawless deeds,

**23:37** because they were committing adultery, and blood is on their hands; they were committing adultery with their devices, and their children whom they bore to Me they passed through sacrificial fires for them.

**23:38** Even these things too they did to Me: they were defiling My sanctuaries and profaning My Sabbaths,

**23:39** and when they were slaughtering their children to their idols, they were also entering My sanctuaries to profane them. And because they were acting in this way in the midst of My house,

**23:40** and because for the men coming from afar, to whom they were sending out messengers, as soon as they arrived you immediately bathed yourself and painted your eyes and adorned yourself with ornaments,

**23:41** and you sat on a spread couch, with a table arranged before it; and the incense and My oil—they were delighting in them,

**23:42** and they were striking up a sound of harmony, even for men from a multitude of people who had come from the wilderness; and they were putting bracelets on their hands and a crown of boasting on their heads.

**23:43** And I said, 'Are they not committing adultery in these things?' And with the deeds of a prostitute she herself also prostituted herself;

**23:44** and they were going in to her; just as they go in to a woman who is a prostitute, so they were going in to Oholah and to Oholibah to commit lawlessness.

**23:45** And righteous men—they themselves will indeed punish them with the punishment of an adulteress and with the punishment of blood, because they are adulteresses, and blood is on their hands.

**23:46** This is what the Lord, the Lord says: 'Bring up a crowd against them, and put turmoil and plunder among them,

**23:47** and stone them with the stones of crowds, and pierce them through with their swords; they will kill their sons and their daughters, and they will burn down their houses.

**23:48** And I will turn away impiety from the land, and all the women will be disciplined and will by no means act according to their impieties.

**23:49** And your impiety will be placed upon you, and you will bear the sins of your devices; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — Greek epitithemai epi (literally "set/throw oneself upon") is used throughout this chapter (vv. 5, 7, 9, 12, 16, 20; cognate noun epithesis in v. 11) for passionate or lustful pursuit.
- **23:7** — Greek enthymemata ("devices" or "thoughts," also in vv. 30, 37, 49) is the Septuagint's characteristic rendering in Ezekiel for the Hebrew term for idols, distinct from eidola ("idols") in v. 39. The Greek also reads the singular pronoun "her devices" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "their idols."
- **23:15** — Greek trissos ("threefold," also in v. 23) reflects the Septuagint's rendering of the Hebrew term for military officers (shalishim) by its connection to the number three. The Greek text also preserves the singular pronoun in "his homeland."
- **23:23** — "Phakouk, Soue, and Hychoue" reflect the Greek proper-name forms Phakouk, Soue, and Hychoue, corresponding to Pekod, Shoa, and Koa in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:34** — The Greek text reads "and I will turn away her festivals and her new moons," differing substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which speaks of draining the cup, gnawing its sherds, and tearing the breasts.
