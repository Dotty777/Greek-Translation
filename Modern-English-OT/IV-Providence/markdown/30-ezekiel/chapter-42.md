## Text

**42:1** And he brought me into the inner court toward the east, opposite the gate toward the north; and he brought me in, and look, five chambers adjoining the remaining space and adjoining the partition toward the north,

**42:2** for a hundred cubits in length toward the north, and the width fifty,

**42:3** laid out in the same way as the gates of the inner court and in the same way as the colonnades of the outer court, arranged in rows facing one another, threefold porticoes.

**42:4** And opposite the chambers was a walkway ten cubits in width, for a hundred cubits in length; and their doorways were toward the north,

**42:5** and the upper walkways were likewise; for the colonnade projected from it, from the lower colonnade, and the interval; thus there was a colonnade and an interval, and thus two porticoes.

**42:6** For they were triple, and they did not have pillars like the pillars of the outer ones; for this reason they projected from the lower ones and the middle ones from the ground.

**42:7** And there was a light on the outside, in the same way as the chambers of the outer court that face opposite the chambers toward the north, a length of fifty cubits.

**42:8** For the length of the chambers facing toward the outer court was fifty cubits, and these are the ones facing opposite those; the total was a hundred cubits.

**42:9** And the doors of these chambers were of the entrance toward the east, for going in through them from the outer court

**42:10** according to the light of the walkway at the beginning; and the parts toward the south were in front of the south, in front of the remaining space and in front of the partition; and the chambers

**42:11** and the walkway in front of them were according to the measurements of the chambers toward the north, and according to their length, and according to their breadth, and according to all their exits, and according to all their turnings, and according to their lights, and according to their doorways,

**42:12** of the chambers toward the south, and according to the doorways from the beginning of the walkway toward a light of a reed's interval, and toward the east for going in through them.

**42:13** And he said to me, "The chambers toward the north and the chambers toward the south, which are in front of the intervals, these are the chambers of the holy place, in which the priests, the sons of Zadok, who draw near to the Lord, will eat the most holy things there; and there they will place the most holy things, and the sacrifice, and the offerings for sin, and the offerings for ignorance, because the place is holy.

**42:14** No one shall enter there except the priests; they shall not go out from the holy place into the outer court, so that those who approach may be continually holy; and let them not touch their vestments in which they minister, because they are holy; and they shall put on other garments whenever they touch the people."

**42:15** And the measurement of the house on the inside was completed. And he brought me out by the way of the gate that faces toward the east, and he measured the pattern of the house all around in order.

**42:16** And he stood at the back of the gate that faces toward the east, and he measured five hundred with the measuring reed.

**42:17** And he turned toward the north and measured the side facing the north, five hundred cubits with the measuring reed.

**42:18** And he turned toward the west and measured the side facing the west, five hundred with the measuring reed.

**42:19** And he turned toward the south and measured opposite the south, five hundred with the measuring reed,

**42:20** the four sides of the same reed; and he arranged it and their enclosure all around, five hundred toward the east and five hundred cubits in breadth, to separate between the sanctuary and the outer wall that was in the arrangement of the house.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — The Greek reads "into the inner court toward the east" and "five chambers," where the Hebrew reads "out to the outer court" and a singular "chamber."
- **42:7** — Here and in 41:11 and 42:10-12, the Greek uses phos ("light") for an unroofed space or architectural light opening, where the Hebrew refers to a wall or place.
- **42:13** — The Greek adds "the sons of Zadok" and renders the final offering category as "the offerings for ignorance" (agnoia, where the Hebrew has "guilt offering").
- **42:14** — The Greek differs substantially from the Hebrew, stating that none may enter except the priests, that those who approach may be continually holy, and that their vestments must not be touched.
- **42:17** — The Greek specifies "five hundred cubits" here and in v. 20 (with cubits implied in vv. 16 and 18-19), whereas the Hebrew measures five hundred reeds; in vv. 18-19 the Greek also orders west (lit. "the sea") before south, and in v. 20 reads "the outer wall" (proteichisma) rather than "the common/profane."
