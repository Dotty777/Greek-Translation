## Text

**35:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**35:2** "Son of man, turn your face toward Mount Seir and prophesy against it,

**35:3** and say, 'Thus says the Lord, the Lord: Look, I am against you, Mount Seir, and I will stretch out My hand against you and make you a desolation, and you will be made desolate,

**35:4** and I will make a desolation of your cities, and you will be desolate, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**35:5** Because you became an everlasting enemy, and lay in wait for the house of Israel with deceit, by the hand of enemies with a sword, in the time of injustice, at the end;

**35:6** therefore, as I live, says the Lord, the Lord, surely you have sinned against blood, and blood will pursue you.

**35:7** And I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a waste, and I will destroy from it people and livestock,

**35:8** and I will fill hills and your ravines with the wounded, and in all your plains those wounded by the sword will fall in you.

**35:9** I will make you an everlasting desolation, and your cities will never again be inhabited, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**35:10** Because you said, "The two nations and the two countries will be mine, and I will inherit them," though the Lord is there;

**35:11** therefore, as I live, says the Lord, I will do to you according to your enmity, and I will be made known to you when I judge you,

**35:12** and you will know that I am the Lord. I have heard the voice of your blasphemies, that you said, "The mountains of Israel are desolate; they have been given to us for food,"

**35:13** and you spoke boastfully against Me with your mouth; I Myself have heard.

**35:14** Thus says the Lord: In the rejoicing of all the earth I will make you desolate;

**35:15** you will be desolate, Mount Seir, and all Idumea, and it will be completely consumed; you will know that I am the Lord their God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:5** — Greek reads "lay in wait for the house of Israel with deceit, by the hand of enemies with a sword," diverging substantially from the Hebrew MT ("delivered the sons of Israel to the power of the sword").
- **35:6** — Greek has a notably shorter text than the Hebrew MT and reads literally "you have sinned against [or 'with respect to'] blood" (eis haima hemartes).
- **35:7** — Greek reads "people and livestock" (anthropous kai ktene), where the Hebrew MT reads "the one who passes through and returns."
- **35:15** — Greek uses the Hellenistic geographical name "Idumea" (Idoumaia, corresponding to Hebrew Edom), adds "and it will be completely consumed" (reading Hebrew klh as a verb rather than "all of it"), and closes with the singular "you will know" plus "their God."
