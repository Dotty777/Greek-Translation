## Text

**30:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:2** "Son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord says: Alas, alas, the day,

**30:3** for a day is near, the day of the Lord; it will be the end of nations.

**30:4** And a sword will come upon the Egyptians, and there will be turmoil in the land of Ethiopia, and the wounded will collapse in Egypt, and its foundations will collapse.

**30:5** Persians and Cretans and Lydians and Libyans and all the mixed peoples, and some of the sons of My covenant, will fall by the sword in it.

**30:6** And the supports of Egypt will fall, and the arrogance of its strength will come down from Migdol to Syene; they will fall by the sword in it, says the Lord.

**30:7** And it will be desolated in the midst of desolated countries, and their cities will be in the midst of desolated cities.

**30:8** And they will know that I am the Lord, when I put fire upon Egypt and all those who help it are shattered.

**30:9** In that day messengers will go out hurrying to wipe out Ethiopia, and there will be turmoil among them in the day of Egypt, for look, it is coming.

**30:10** This is what the Lord, the Lord says: And I will destroy the multitude of the Egyptians by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon,

**30:11** him and his people, pestilent ones from the nations sent to destroy the land; and they will all unsheathe their swords against Egypt, and the land will be filled with the wounded.

**30:12** And I will make their rivers desolate and destroy the land and its fullness by the hands of foreigners; I, the Lord, have spoken.

**30:13** For this is what the Lord, the Lord says: And I will destroy nobles from Memphis and rulers of Memphis from the land of Egypt, and they will be no more.

**30:14** And I will destroy the land of Pathros, and I will put fire upon Tanis, and I will execute vengeance in Diospolis.

**30:15** And I will pour out My wrath upon Sais, the strength of Egypt, and I will destroy the multitude of Memphis.

**30:16** And I will put fire upon Egypt, and Syene will be troubled with turmoil, and in Diospolis there will be a breach, and waters will be poured out.

**30:17** The young men of Heliopolis and Bubastis will fall by the sword, and the women will go into captivity,

**30:18** and in Tahpanhes the day will grow dark, when I shatter the scepters of Egypt there, and the arrogance of its strength will perish there; and a cloud will cover it, and its daughters will be led away captive.

**30:19** And I will execute judgment in Egypt, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

**30:20** And it happened in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:21** "Son of man, I have shattered the arms of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and look, he was not bound up to be given healing, to have a poultice put on him, to be given strength to take hold of a sword.

**30:22** Therefore, this is what the Lord, the Lord says: Look, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I will shatter his arms, the strong ones and the outstretched ones, and I will cast his sword down from his hand.

**30:23** And I will scatter Egypt into the nations and winnow them into the countries,

**30:24** and I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put My sword into his hand, and he will bring it upon Egypt and he will plunder its plunder and despoil its spoils.

**30:25** And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon; the arms of Pharaoh will fall. And they will know that I am the Lord, when I put My sword into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will stretch it out against the land of Egypt.

**30:26** And I will scatter Egypt into the nations, and winnow them into the countries; and all will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **30:5** — The Greek lists "Persians and Cretans and Lydians and Libyans" (differing from the Hebrew list beginning with Cush and Put) and reads "some of the sons of My covenant" (tōn huiōn tēs diathēkēs mou).
- **30:13** — The Greek reads "nobles from Memphis and rulers of Memphis" where Hebrew has "idols" and "a prince"; "Memphis" is the Greek city name corresponding to Hebrew Noph.
- **30:14** — "Tanis" and "Diospolis" are the Septuagint's Greek city names for Hebrew Zoan and No (Thebes), just as "Sais" (v. 15), "Heliopolis", and "Bubastis" (v. 17) correspond to Hebrew Sin, Aven/On, and Pi-beseth.
- **30:16** — "Waters will be poured out" (diachythēsetai hydata) is a notable Septuagint reading where the Hebrew text speaks of Memphis having daily adversaries.
- **30:21** — The Greek has plural "arms" (singular in Hebrew) and the verb katedeēthē, a variant passive form of katadeō ("bind up", though formally resembling katadeomai, "ask for"); in v. 22, the Greek describes the arms as "strong and outstretched" rather than "strong and broken".
