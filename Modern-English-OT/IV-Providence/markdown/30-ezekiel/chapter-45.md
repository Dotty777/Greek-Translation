## Text

**45:1** And when you measure out the land for an inheritance, you shall set apart a firstfruit offering to the Lord, a holy portion from the land, twenty-five thousand in length and twenty thousand in width; it shall be holy within all its borders all around.

**45:2** And from this there shall be a sanctuary, five hundred by five hundred, square all around, and fifty cubits of open space for it all around.

**45:3** And from this measurement you shall measure a length of twenty-five thousand and a width of twenty thousand, and in it shall be the Holy of Holies.

**45:4** From the land it shall belong to the priests who minister in the holy place, and it shall belong to those who draw near to minister to the Lord; and it shall be a place for them for houses set apart for their sanctification,

**45:5** twenty-five thousand in length and twenty thousand in width. And for the Levites who minister in the house, they shall have cities to live in as a possession.

**45:6** And you shall give the possession of the city, five thousand in width and twenty-five thousand in length, just like the firstfruit offering of the sanctuary; they shall belong to the whole house of Israel.

**45:7** And for the ruler, on one side and on the other toward the firstfruit offerings of the sanctuary, toward the possession of the city, facing the firstfruit offerings of the sanctuary and facing the possession of the city, the parts toward the sea and from the parts toward the sea toward the east; and the length shall be like one of the portions, from the borders toward the sea, and the length to the borders toward the east of the land.

**45:8** And it shall be for him as a possession in Israel, and the rulers of Israel shall no longer oppress My people; and the house of Israel shall inherit the land according to their tribes.

**45:9** Thus says the Lord God: "Let it be enough for you, rulers of Israel! Put away injustice and misery; do justice and righteousness; remove oppression from My people," says the Lord God.

**45:10** "You shall have a just balance and a just measure and a just choenix of the measure,

**45:11** and the choenix shall likewise be one for holding: a tenth of the homer is a choenix, and a tenth of the homer; according to the homer shall be the equivalent.

**45:12** And the weights shall be twenty obols; five shekels are five, and ten shekels, and fifty shekels shall be the mina for you.

**45:13** And this is the firstfruit offering that you shall set apart: a sixth of a measure from the homer of wheat, and the sixth of the ephah itself from the kor of barley.

**45:14** And the statute for the oil: a cotyle of oil from ten cotyles, for the ten cotyles are a homer.

**45:15** And a sheep from the sheep, out of ten, a portion set apart from all the families of Israel, for sacrifices and for burnt offerings and for deliverance offerings, to make atonement for you," says the Lord God.

**45:16** And all the people shall give this firstfruit offering to the ruler of Israel.

**45:17** And through the ruler shall be the burnt offerings and the sacrifices and the drink offerings at the feasts and at the new moons and on the Sabbaths and at all the feasts of the house of Israel; he himself shall prepare the offerings for sin and the sacrifice and the burnt offerings and the deliverance offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel.

**45:18** Thus says the Lord God: "In the first month, on the first of the month, you shall take an unblemished calf from the cattle to make atonement for the holy place.

**45:19** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the atonement, and he shall put it on the doorposts of the house and on the four corners of the temple and on the altar and on the doorposts of the gate of the inner court.

**45:20** And thus you shall do in the seventh month, on the first of the month, a portion from each one, and you shall make atonement for the house.

**45:21** And in the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, the Passover shall be a feast for you; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**45:22** And on that day the ruler shall prepare, for himself and the house and for all the people of the land, a calf for sin.

**45:23** And during the seven days of the feast he shall prepare burnt offerings to the Lord: seven calves and seven rams, unblemished, daily for the seven days, and for sin a kid of the goats daily, and a sacrifice.

**45:24** And you shall prepare a cake for the calf and a cake for the ram, and a hin of oil for the cake.

**45:25** And in the seventh month, on the fifteenth of the month, at the feast, you shall do the same for seven days: just like the offerings for sin, and just like the burnt offerings, and just like the grain offering, and just like the oil."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The Greek text gives the width as twenty thousand here and in vv. 3 and 5 (where the Hebrew MT reads ten thousand), and leaves the unit of measurement unstated. In v. 5 the Greek likewise reads "cities to live in" where the Hebrew MT has "twenty chambers."
- **45:12** — The Greek uses Greek units of measure and weight—choenix (vv. 10-11), obol (v. 12), and cotyle (v. 14)—and sets the mina at fifty shekels in a compressed numerical sequence (contrast the Hebrew MT's twenty gerahs and sixty-shekel mina).
- **45:15** — The Greek specifies a levy of one sheep out of ten from all the families of Israel (where the Hebrew MT reads one out of two hundred from the well-watered pastures of Israel).
- **45:20** — The Greek prescribes a second sanctuary cleansing on the first day of the seventh month with "a portion from each one" (where the Hebrew MT sets it on the seventh day of the first month for anyone who errs unintentionally).
- **45:24** — Here and in 46:5, 7, 11 the Greek reads "cake" (Greek pemma, a baked offering) where the Hebrew MT reads the dry measure "ephah." In v. 25 and ch. 46 the Greek uses the Hebrew loanword manaa for "grain offering."
