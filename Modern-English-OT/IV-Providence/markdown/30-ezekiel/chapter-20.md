## Text

**20:1** And it happened in the seventh year, on the fifteenth of the month, that men from the elders of the house of Israel came to inquire of the Lord, and they sat before my face.

**20:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**20:3** "Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel, and you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Are you coming to inquire of Me? As I live, I will certainly not answer you, says the Lord,

**20:4** nor will I avenge them with vengeance.' Son of man, testify to them concerning the lawless deeds of their fathers,

**20:5** and you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: From the day I chose the house of Israel, I was made known to the offspring of the house of Jacob, and I was known to them in the land of Egypt, and I took hold of them with My hand, saying, "I am the Lord your God."

**20:6** On that day I took hold of them with My hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt into the land that I prepared for them, a land flowing with milk and honey; it is a honeycomb beyond all the earth.

**20:7** And I said to them, "Let each one cast away the abominations of his eyes, and do not defile yourselves with the practices of Egypt; I am the Lord your God."

**20:8** And they turned away from Me, and they were not willing to listen to Me; they did not cast away the abominations of their eyes, and they did not forsake the practices of Egypt. And I said I would pour out My wrath upon them, to bring My anger to completion among them in the midst of the land of Egypt.

**20:9** And I acted so that My name might not be profaned at all in the sight of the nations in whose midst they are, among whom I was known to them in their sight by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.

**20:10** And I led them into the wilderness,

**20:11** and I gave them My ordinances, and I made My decrees known to them, all of which, if a person does them, he will live by them.

**20:12** And I gave them My Sabbaths to be a sign between Me and them, so that they might know that I am the Lord who sanctifies them.

**20:13** And I said to the house of Israel in the wilderness, "Walk in My ordinances." Yet they did not walk in them, and they rejected My decrees, which, if a person does them, he will live by them, and they greatly profaned My Sabbaths. And I said I would pour out My wrath upon them in the wilderness to consume them.

**20:14** And I acted so that My name might not be profaned at all in the sight of the nations before whose eyes I brought them out.

**20:15** I also lifted up My hand against them in the wilderness not to bring them at all into the land that I gave them, a land flowing with milk and honey; it is a honeycomb beyond all the earth—

**20:16** because they rejected My decrees, and they did not walk in My ordinances, and they were profaning My Sabbaths, and they were walking after the designs of their heart.

**20:17** Yet My eye spared them so as not to wipe them out, and I did not bring them to a complete end in the wilderness.

**20:18** And I said to their children in the wilderness, "Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, and do not keep their decrees, and do not mingle with their practices and do not defile yourselves.

**20:19** I am the Lord your God; walk in My ordinances, and keep My decrees and do them,

**20:20** and sanctify My Sabbaths, and let them be a sign between Me and you, to know that I am the Lord your God."

**20:21** And their children also embittered Me; they did not walk in My ordinances, and they did not keep My decrees to do them, which, if a person does them, he will live by them, and they were profaning My Sabbaths. And I said I would pour out My wrath upon them in the wilderness to bring My anger to completion upon them.

**20:22** And I acted so that My name might not be profaned at all in the sight of the nations, and I brought them out before their eyes.

**20:23** And I lifted up My hand against them in the wilderness to scatter them among the nations, to disperse them in the countries,

**20:24** because they did not do My decrees, and they rejected My ordinances, and they were profaning My Sabbaths, and their eyes were after the designs of their fathers.

**20:25** I also gave them ordinances that were not good and decrees by which they will not live.

**20:26** And I will defile them by their gifts, when I pass through everything that opens a womb, so that I may destroy them.'

**20:27** Therefore speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: Up to this point your fathers provoked Me to anger by their trespasses in which they trespassed against Me.

**20:28** And I brought them into the land that I lifted My hand to give them, and they saw every high hill and every shady tree, and they sacrificed there to their gods, and they set there an aroma of sweet fragrance, and they poured out their drink offerings there.

**20:29** And I said to them, "What is Habana, that you go in there?" And they called its name Habana until this present day.'

**20:30** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord: Are you defiling yourselves in the lawless deeds of your fathers, and are you prostituting yourselves after their abominations?

**20:31** And in the firstfruits of your gifts, in the separated offerings by which you defile yourselves with all your designs until this present day? And should I answer you, house of Israel? As I live, says the Lord, I will certainly not answer you, nor will this come up upon your spirit.

**20:32** And it will not be as you say, "We will be like the nations and like the tribes of the earth, to worship wood and stones."

**20:33** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, with a mighty hand and with a high arm and with poured-out wrath I will reign over you,

**20:34** and I will bring you out from the peoples, and I will receive you from the countries where you were scattered, with a mighty hand and a high arm and with poured-out wrath.

**20:35** And I will lead you into the wilderness of the peoples, and I will enter into judgment with you there face to face.

**20:36** Just as I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will judge you, says the Lord.

**20:37** And I will lead you through under My rod, and I will bring you in by number,

**20:38** and I will convict from among you the ungodly and those who have turned away, because I will bring them out from their place of sojourning, and they will not enter into the land of Israel; and you will know that I am the Lord, the Lord.

**20:39** And you, house of Israel, thus says the Lord, the Lord: Each one remove his practices; and after these things, if you do not listen to Me, yet My holy name you will no longer profane with your gifts and with your practices,

**20:40** because on My holy mountain, on a high mountain, says the Lord, the Lord, there all the house of Israel will serve Me to the end. And there I will accept, and there I will attend to, your firstfruits and the firstfruits of your separated offerings with all your holy things.

**20:41** With an aroma of sweet fragrance I will accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples and receive you from the countries in which you were scattered, and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the peoples.

**20:42** And you will know that I am the Lord, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land that I lifted My hand to give to your fathers.

**20:43** And there you will remember your ways and your practices in which you were defiling yourselves, and you will beat your faces over all your evils.

**20:44** And you will know that I am the Lord, when I act in this way toward you so that My name may not be profaned according to your evil ways and according to your corrupted practices, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:4** — Greek ei ekdikeso autous ekdikesei continues the negative oath formula from v. 3 ("As I live, I will certainly not...") with a shift to the third person ("them") and a cognate dative ("avenge them with vengeance"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a second-person question ("Will you judge them?").
- **20:6** — Greek kerion ("honeycomb") is a distinctive Septuagint reading (also in v. 15) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "glory/ornament," and Greek hetoimasa reads "prepared" rather than "searched out."
- **20:26** — The Greek uses a future verb ("I will defile") and makes God the subject of diaporeuesthai ("when I pass through everything that opens a womb"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's past tense and causal reference to passing firstborn through fire, and omits the closing recognition clause.
- **20:29** — Habana (Greek habana/Habana) is a Septuagint transliteration of the Hebrew habbamah ("the high place"), kept here as an LXX-distinct proper name.
- **20:38** — Greek kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") is a doubled divine title (recurring in vv. 39 and 40) rendering Hebrew Adonai YHWH.
