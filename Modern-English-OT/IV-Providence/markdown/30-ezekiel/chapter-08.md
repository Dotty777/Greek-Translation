## Text

**8:1** And it happened in the sixth year, in the fifth month, on the fifth of the month, that I was sitting in the house, and the elders of Judah were sitting before me. And the hand of the Lord came upon me,

**8:2** and I looked, and look, a likeness of a man: from His waist and downward was fire, and from His waist upward was like the appearance of amber.

**8:3** And He stretched out a likeness of a hand and took me up by the crown of my head, and the Spirit lifted me up between the earth and heaven and brought me to Jerusalem in a vision of God, to the entrance of the gate facing north, where the pillar of the acquirer was.

**8:4** And look, there was the glory of the Lord God of Israel, according to the vision that I saw in the plain.

**8:5** And He said to me, "Son of man, look up with your eyes toward the north." And I looked up with my eyes toward the north, and look, from the north toward the gate of the one facing east.

**8:6** And He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen what these people are doing? They are doing great lawless deeds here, so as to keep away from My holy places; and you will yet see greater lawless deeds."

**8:7** And He brought me in to the entrance of the court,

**8:8** and He said to me, "Son of man, dig." And I dug, and look, a door.

**8:9** And He said to me, "Go in and see the lawless deeds that these people are doing here."

**8:10** And I went in and looked, and look, vain abominations and all the idols of the house of Israel depicted on it all around.

**8:11** And there were seventy men from the elders of the house of Israel; Jeconiah son of Shaphan was standing in their midst before them, and each one had his censer in his hand, and the vapor of the incense was rising.

**8:12** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel are doing, each of them in their secret bedchamber? For they said, 'The Lord does not see; the Lord has forsaken the land.'"

**8:13** And He said to me, "You will yet see greater lawless deeds that these people are doing."

**8:14** And He brought me in to the entrance of the gate of the house of the Lord facing north, and look, women were sitting there, mourning for Tammuz.

**8:15** And He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen? And you will yet see practices greater than these."

**8:16** And He brought me into the inner court of the house of the Lord, and at the entrance of the temple of the Lord, between the porticoes and the altar, were about twenty men, with their backs toward the temple of the Lord and their faces in the opposite direction, and they were bowing down to the sun.

**8:17** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? Is it a small thing for the house of Judah to do the lawless deeds that they have done here? For they filled the land with lawlessness, and look, they are like those who sneer.

**8:18** And I will act toward them with fury; My eye will not spare, nor will I show mercy."

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Septuagint dates this vision to the "fifth month," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the "sixth month."
- **8:3** — The Greek "pillar of the acquirer" reflects a different reading or vocalization of the Hebrew behind the Masoretic Text's "image of jealousy."
- **8:5** — The Greek omits the Hebrew's mention of the "image of jealousy" here and reads "facing east" where the Hebrew reads "of the altar."
- **8:11** — The Greek gives the name "Jeconiah" (Iechonias), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jaazaniah."
- **8:16** — The Greek reads "about twenty men" rather than the Hebrew's "about twenty-five men," and renders "porticoes" with the plural transliteration ton ailam.
