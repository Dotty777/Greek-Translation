## Text

**32:1** And it happened in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the first of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**32:2** "Son of man, take up a lament over Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'You have been likened to a lion of the nations, and like a dragon that is in the sea; and you were butting in your rivers, and troubling water with your feet, and trampling your rivers.

**32:3** Thus says the Lord: And I will cast over you nets of many peoples, and I will bring you up with My hook,

**32:4** and I will stretch you out upon the ground; plains shall be filled with you, and I will cause all the birds to settle upon you, and I will fill all the wild beasts of all the earth;

**32:5** and I will place your flesh upon the mountains, and I will fill them with your blood;

**32:6** and the land shall be watered with your discharges, from your multitude upon the mountains; ravines I will fill with you.

**32:7** And when you are extinguished, I will cover over heaven and darken its stars; the sun I will cover with a cloud, and the moon shall not shine its light.

**32:8** All the things shining light in heaven shall grow dark over you, and I will put darkness upon the earth, says the Lord, the Lord.

**32:9** And I will provoke to anger the heart of many peoples, when I bring your captivity to the nations, into a land that you did not know.

**32:10** And many nations shall be appalled over you, and their kings shall be astounded with astonishment when My sword flies before their faces, awaiting their fall from the day of your fall.

**32:11** For thus says the Lord: The sword of the king of Babylon shall come upon you

**32:12** with swords of giants, and I will cast down your strength; all of them are pestilent ones from the nations, and they shall destroy the arrogance of Egypt, and all her strength shall be shattered.

**32:13** And I will destroy all her livestock from much water, and the foot of man shall no longer trouble it, and the tread of livestock shall not trample it.

**32:14** Thus then their waters shall be calm, and their rivers shall run like oil, says the Lord.

**32:15** When I give Egypt over to destruction and the land is made desolate along with its fullness, when I scatter all those dwelling in it, then they shall know that I am the Lord.'

**32:16** It is a lament, and you shall lament it, and the daughters of the nations shall lament it; over Egypt and over all her strength they shall lament her, says the Lord, the Lord."

**32:17** And it happened in the twelfth year, in the first month, on the fifteenth of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**32:18** "Son of man, lament over the strength of Egypt, and the nations shall bring her daughters down dead into the depth of the earth, to those going down into the pit.

**32:20** In the midst of those wounded by the sword they shall fall with him, and all his strength shall lie down.

**32:21** And the giants shall say to you, 'Be in the depth of tumult! Than whom are you better? And go down and lie with the uncircumcised in the midst of those wounded by the sword.'

**32:22** There is Assyria and all its assembly; all of them wounded, there they were placed, and their burial is in the depth of the pit, and its assembly came to be around its tomb, all the wounded who have fallen by the sword,

**32:23** who laid their terror upon the land of life.

**32:24** There is Elam and all its force around its tomb, all the wounded who have fallen by the sword and those going down uncircumcised into the depth of the earth, who had laid their terror upon life; and they received their torment with those going down into the pit

**32:25** in the midst of the wounded.

**32:26** There Meshech and Tubal and all their strength were placed around its tomb, all its wounded, all uncircumcised, wounded by the sword, who had laid their terror upon life;

**32:27** and they lay down with the giants who have fallen from ancient times, who went down to Hades with weapons of war and placed their swords under their heads; and their lawless deeds came to be upon their bones, because they terrified everyone in their life.

**32:28** And you shall lie down in the midst of the uncircumcised with those who have been wounded by the sword.

**32:29** There the rulers of Assyria were placed, who gave its strength to the wound of the sword; these lay down with the wounded, with those going down into the pit.

**32:30** There are the rulers of the north, all the commanders of Assyria, who go down wounded; with their terror and their strength they lay down uncircumcised with those wounded by the sword, and they bore their torment with those going down into the pit.

**32:31** King Pharaoh shall see them, and he shall be comforted over all their strength, says the Lord, the Lord.

**32:32** For I have laid his terror upon life, and he shall lie down in the midst of the uncircumcised with those wounded by the sword, Pharaoh and all his multitude with him, says the Lord, the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **32:1** — The Greek dates this oracle to the tenth month (whereas the Hebrew reads the twelfth month); similarly, v. 17 specifies the first month where the Hebrew omits the month.
- **32:8** — Greek kyrios Kyrios ("the Lord, the Lord") doubles kyrios to represent the Hebrew compound divine title (Adonai YHWH) without adding theos ("God"), a recurring idiom in Septuagint Ezekiel.
- **32:20** — Greek traumatias literally means "wounded one" and is regularly used in the Septuagint for those mortally wounded or slain in battle. Verse 19 is absent as a separate verse in the Greek, with its wording appearing in v. 21.
- **32:21** — Greek reads "in the depth of tumult" (thorybou), likely an early inner-Greek transposition of bothrou ("pit").
- **32:29** — Here and in v. 30 the Greek reads "Assyria" (Assour) where the Hebrew names Edom (v. 29) and the Sidonians (v. 30). Also in v. 27 the Greek reads positively ("they lay down with the giants"), lacking the Hebrew negation.
