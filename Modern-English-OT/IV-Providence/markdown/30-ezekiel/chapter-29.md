## Text

**29:1** In the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:2** "Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt,

**29:3** and say, 'This is what the Lord says: Look, I am against Pharaoh, the great dragon who sits in the midst of his rivers, who says, "Mine are the rivers, and I made them."

**29:4** And I will put snares into your jaws, and I will make the fish of your river cling to your fins, and I will bring you up from the midst of your river,

**29:5** and I will cast you down quickly, and all the fish of your river. On the face of the plain you will fall, and you will by no means be gathered and by no means be prepared for burial; to the wild beasts of the earth and to the birds of the sky I have given you for food.

**29:6** And all those who inhabit Egypt will know that I am the Lord, because you became a reed staff to the house of Israel.

**29:7** Because they took hold of you with their hand, you were crushed; and when every hand clapped against them, and when they leaned upon you, you were shattered, and you broke every loin of theirs.

**29:8** Therefore, this is what the Lord says: Look, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will destroy people and beasts from you,

**29:9** and the land of Egypt will become destruction and a wasteland; and they will know that I am the Lord, because you say, "The rivers are mine, and I made them."

**29:10** Therefore, look, I am against you and against all your rivers, and I will give the land of Egypt over to wasteland, sword, and destruction, from Migdol and Syene even to the borders of the Ethiopians.

**29:11** The foot of a person will by no means pass through it, and the foot of a beast will by no means pass through it, and it will not be inhabited for forty years.

**29:12** And I will make its land a destruction in the midst of a desolated land, and its cities will be in the midst of desolated cities for forty years. And I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and I will winnow them into the countries.

**29:13** This is what the Lord says: After forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the nations where they were dispersed,

**29:14** and I will turn back the captivity of the Egyptians, and I will settle them in the land of Pathros, in the land from which they were taken; and it will be a lowly dominion

**29:15** compared to all the dominions; it will by no means be exalted any longer over the nations, and I will make them few in number so that they will not be more numerous among the nations.

**29:16** And they will no longer be a hope for the house of Israel, bringing lawlessness to mind when they follow after them; and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

**29:17** And it happened in the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:18** "Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon subjected his army to great servitude against Tyre; every head was bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare; yet no wages came to him or to his army against Tyre, nor for the servitude with which they served against it.

**29:19** This is what the Lord, the Lord says: Look, I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will plunder its plunder and despoil its spoils, and it will be wages for his army.

**29:20** In return for his service with which he served against Tyre, I have given him the land of Egypt. This is what the Lord, the Lord says:

**29:21** In that day a horn will spring up for all the house of Israel, and to you I will give an opened mouth in their midst, and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Greek text dates this oracle to the twelfth year, tenth month, first day, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the tenth year, tenth month, twelfth day.
- **29:4** — Greek pagidas ("snares") and pterygas ("fins", literally "wings") differ from the Hebrew text's "hooks" and "scales"; in v. 5, the Greek also reads "quickly" (en tachei) where Hebrew has "into the wilderness".
- **29:7** — The Greek reads "when every hand clapped against them" (ekrotēsen ep' autous pasa cheir), diverging from the Hebrew text ("you tore open every shoulder for them").
- **29:10** — Greek rhomphaian ("sword") reflects reading the Hebrew consonants for "drought/desolation" as "sword"; "Ethiopians" renders Greek Aithiopes, the Septuagint name corresponding to Hebrew Cush (also "Ethiopia" in 30:4, 9).
- **29:19** — "The Lord, the Lord" translates the doubled Greek title kyrios Kyrios (corresponding to Hebrew Adonai YHWH), which also appears at the end of v. 20 introducing v. 21 in the Greek text.
