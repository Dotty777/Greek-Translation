## Text

**1:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,

**1:2** "Rise and go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim in it, for the outcry of its evil has come up to Me."

**1:3** And Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish, and paid the fare for it and went aboard it to sail with them to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord.

**1:4** And the Lord stirred up a wind upon the sea, and a great tempest arose in the sea, and the ship was in danger of being shattered.

**1:5** And the sailors were afraid and cried out, each one to his god, and they jettisoned the goods that were in the ship into the sea, so as to be lightened of them; but Jonah had gone down into the hold of the ship, and was sleeping and snoring.

**1:6** And the first mate came to him and said to him, "Why are you snoring? Rise and call upon your god, so that God may rescue us and we may not perish."

**1:7** And each one said to his companion, "Come, let us cast lots and find out on whose account this evil is among us." And they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

**1:8** And they said to him, "Tell us, what is your occupation, and where do you come from, and from what country and from what people are you?"

**1:9** And he said to them, "I am a servant of the Lord, and I revere the Lord God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land."

**1:10** And the men feared with a great fear and said to him, "What is this you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the Lord, because he had told them.

**1:11** And they said to him, "What shall we do to you so that the sea will subside from us?" For the sea kept advancing and stirring up a tempest all the more.

**1:12** And Jonah said to them, "Lift me up and cast me into the sea, and the sea will subside from you; for I know that because of me this great tempest is upon you."

**1:13** And the men strove hard to turn back to the land, and they were not able, because the sea kept advancing and was being stirred up all the more against them.

**1:14** And they cried out to the Lord and said, "By no means, Lord! Let us not perish on account of this man's life, and do not lay righteous blood upon us, for You, Lord, have done just as You desired."

**1:15** And they took Jonah and cast him out into the sea, and the sea ceased from its tossing.

**1:16** And the men feared the Lord with great fear, and they offered sacrifices to the Lord and vowed vows.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Greek reads "in it" (ἐν αὐτῇ) rather than "against it," and includes "the outcry" (ἡ κραυγή) before "of its evil," reflecting distinctive Septuagint wording compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:6** — Greek πρωρεύς denotes the prow officer or first mate (second in command of a vessel), and ῥέγχεις ("snoring," as in 1:5) specifically depicts Jonah's heavy sleep as snoring.
- **1:9** — The Greek reads "a servant of the Lord" (Δοῦλος Κυρίου) rather than "a Hebrew" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reflecting a different reading of the underlying Hebrew consonants.
- **1:14** — Greek Μηδαμῶς ("By no means") renders a Hebrew particle of entreaty as a strong negation, and αἷμα δίκαιον specifies "righteous blood" rather than "innocent blood."
