## Text

**8:1** And the word of the Lord Almighty came, saying:

**8:2** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "I have been jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with great jealousy, and with great wrath I have been jealous for her."

**8:3** This is what the Lord says: "I will return to Zion, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and Jerusalem will be called the True City, and the mountain of the Lord Almighty, Holy Mountain."

**8:4** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "Old men and old women will yet sit in the squares of Jerusalem, each one holding his staff in his hand because of an abundance of days;

**8:5** and the squares of the city will be filled with boys and girls playing in its squares."

**8:6** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "If it will be impossible in the sight of the remnant of this people in those days, will it also be impossible in My sight?" says the Lord Almighty.

**8:7** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "Look, I am saving My people from the land of the east and from the land of the west,

**8:8** and I will bring them in, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them, in truth and in righteousness."

**8:9** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "Let your hands be strong, you who hear in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets, from the day the house of the Lord Almighty was founded, and from the time the temple was built.

**8:10** For before those days the wage of people will not be of benefit, and the wage of livestock will not exist, and for the one going out and the one coming in there will be no peace from the affliction; and I will send out all people, each one against his neighbor.

**8:11** And now, not as in the former days am I dealing with the remnant of this people," says the Lord Almighty,

**8:12** "but rather I will show peace: the vine will give its fruit, and the earth will give its produce, and the sky will give its dew, and I will give all these things as an inheritance to the remnant of this people of Mine.

**8:13** And it will be that just as you were under a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will rescue you, and you will be in blessing; take courage, and be strong in your hands."

**8:14** For this is what the Lord Almighty says: "Just as I purposed to do harm to you when your fathers provoked Me to anger," says the Lord Almighty, "and I did not relent,

**8:15** so I have prepared and purposed in these days to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah; take courage.

**8:16** These are the things that you are to do: speak truth, each one to his neighbor; render truth and peaceful judgment in your gates,

**8:17** and do not, each one of you, devise evil against his neighbor in your hearts, and do not love a false oath; for all these things I have hated," says the Lord Almighty.

**8:18** And the word of the Lord Almighty came to me:

**8:19** The Lord Almighty says: "The fourth fast and the fifth fast and the seventh fast and the tenth fast will become for the house of Judah joy and gladness and good festivals, and you will be glad; and love truth and peace."

**8:20** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "Many peoples and inhabitants of many cities will yet come,

**8:21** and inhabitants of five cities will come together to one city, saying, 'Let us go to entreat the face of the Lord and to seek the face of the Lord Almighty; I too will go.'

**8:22** And many peoples and many nations will come to seek the face of the Lord Almighty in Jerusalem and to appease the face of the Lord."

**8:23** This is what the Lord Almighty says: "In those days, if ten men from all the languages of the nations take hold, and take hold of the hem of a Jewish man, saying, 'We will go with you, because we have heard that God is with you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **8:8** — The Greek has the first-person singular future verb kataskenoso ("I will dwell," echoing 8:3, though possibly transitive "I will settle [them]") rather than the Hebrew third-person plural ("they will dwell").
- **8:10** — All four verbs in the Greek are future tense ("will not be ... will not exist ... will not be ... I will send out") despite "before those days," whereas the Hebrew uses past tenses; the Greek also reads "from the affliction" rather than "from the foe."
- **8:12** — The Greek reads "but rather I will show peace" (all' e deixo eirenen), differing from the Hebrew "for there will be a sowing of peace."
- **8:21** — The Greek reads "inhabitants of five cities will come together to one city" (pente poleis), whereas the Hebrew reads "the inhabitants of one city will go to another."
