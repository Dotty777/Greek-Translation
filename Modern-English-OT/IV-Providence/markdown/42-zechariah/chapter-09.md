## Text

**9:1** An oracle of the word of the Lord in the land of Sedrach, and of Damascus is His sacrifice, because the Lord watches over people and all the tribes of Israel,

**9:2** and in Hamath within its borders; Tyre and Sidon, because they were very wise.

**9:3** And Tyre built strongholds for herself, and stored up silver like dust, and gold like mud of the streets.

**9:4** And because of this the Lord will inherit them, and He will strike her power into the sea, and she herself will be consumed in fire.

**9:5** Ashkelon will see and be afraid, and Gaza also, and she will be in great anguish, and Ekron, because she was put to shame over her transgression; and a king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will certainly not be inhabited.

**9:6** And foreigners will dwell in Ashdod, and I will bring down the arrogance of the Philistines,

**9:7** and I will remove their blood from their mouth, and their abominations from between their teeth; and these too will be left behind for our God, and they will be like a commander of a thousand in Judah, and Ekron like the Jebusite;

**9:8** and I will set up a garrison for My house so that no one passes through or turns back, and one who drives out will never again come upon them, for now I have seen with My eyes.

**9:9** Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion! Proclaim, daughter of Jerusalem! Look, your king is coming to you; righteous and saving is He, gentle and mounted on a beast of burden and a young colt.

**9:10** And He will destroy chariots from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war will be destroyed; and there will be abundance and peace from the nations; and He will rule over waters as far as the sea, and over rivers to the outlets of the earth.

**9:11** And you, by the blood of your covenant, sent forth your prisoners from a pit that has no water.

**9:12** You will sit in strongholds, prisoners of the assembly, and for one day of your foreign residence I will repay you double;

**9:13** for I have bent you, Judah, as a bow for Myself, I have filled Ephraim, and I will rouse your children, Zion, against the children of the Greeks, and I will handle you like a warrior's sword;

**9:14** and the Lord will be over them, and a dart will go forth like lightning; and the Lord Almighty will blow a trumpet and will march in the surge of His threat.

**9:15** The Lord Almighty will shield them; and they will consume them, and they will bury them with sling stones, and they will drink them down like wine, and they will fill the bowls like an altar.

**9:16** And the Lord their God will save them on that day, His people like sheep, because holy stones roll upon His land.

**9:17** For whatever is good of His, and whatever is beautiful of His, is grain for young men and fragrant wine for virgins.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek preserves the distinct place name Sedrach (Hebrew "Hadrach") and reads "of Damascus is His sacrifice" (thysia autou), where the Hebrew has "Damascus is its resting place."
- **9:6** — "The Philistines" renders Greek allophylon (literally "foreigners"), the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines, distinct from allogeneis ("foreigners") earlier in the verse.
- **9:9** — Following the Greek text, the verse reads "Proclaim" (kerysse, vs. Hebrew "Shout"), the active participle "saving" (sozon, vs. Hebrew "saved/victorious"), and "a beast of burden and a young colt" (hypozygion kai polon neon).
- **9:10** — The Greek reads third-person "He will destroy" (exolethreusei, vs. Hebrew "I will cut off"), "abundance and peace from the nations" (vs. Hebrew "he will speak peace to the nations"), and "waters as far as the sea, and over rivers to the outlets of the earth" (vs. Hebrew "from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth").
- **9:12** — The Greek reads "You will sit in strongholds, prisoners of the assembly, and for one day of your foreign residence I will repay you double," differing from the Hebrew "Return to the stronghold, prisoners of hope; even today I declare that I will restore double to you."
