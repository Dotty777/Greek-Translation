## Text

**6:1** And I turned again and lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, four chariots going out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**6:2** With the first chariot were red horses, and with the second chariot black horses,

**6:3** and with the third chariot white horses, and with the fourth chariot dappled, ash-gray horses.

**6:4** And I answered and said to the angel speaking in me, "What are these, sir?"

**6:5** And the angel speaking in me answered and said, "These are the four winds of heaven; they go out to stand before the Lord of all the earth."

**6:6** As for the chariot with which the black horses were, they went out to the land of the north, and the white ones went out behind them, and the dappled ones went out to the land of the south,

**6:7** and the ash-gray ones went out and were looking to go and travel through the earth. And He said, "Go and travel through the earth." And they traveled through the earth.

**6:8** And they cried out, and He spoke to me, saying, "Here are those going out to the land of the north, and they have put My wrath to rest in the land of the north."

**6:9** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:10** "Take the things from the captivity, from the rulers and from its useful ones and from those who have known it, and you yourself will enter on that day into the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah, who has come from Babylon,

**6:11** and you will take silver and gold and make crowns, and you will place them upon the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest,

**6:12** and you will say to him, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: Here is a man; Rising is his name, and from beneath him he will rise up, and he will build the house of the Lord;

**6:13** and he himself will receive excellence, and he will sit and rule upon his throne, and the priest will be on his right, and peaceful counsel will be between both of them.'

**6:14** Now the crown will be for those who endure, and for its useful ones, and for those who have known it, and for the favor of the son of Zephaniah, and for a psalm in the house of the Lord.

**6:15** And those who are far from them will come and build in the house of the Lord, and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you; and it will happen if you earnestly listen to the voice of the Lord your God."

## Translator Notes

- **6:8** — The Greek begins with a plural verb "they cried out" (aneboesan) and reads "My wrath" (ton thymon mou) where the Masoretic Text has "My spirit."
- **6:10** — Instead of transliterating the Masoretic Text's personal names (Heldai, Tobijah, Jedaiah), the Greek translates them as common descriptors ("the rulers," "its useful ones," and "those who have known it," referring back to "the captivity") and uses a singular participle ("who has come from Babylon").
- **6:12** — "Rising" translates Greek Anatole (meaning "rising," "dawn," or "shoot," rendering Hebrew "Branch"), preserving the Greek wordplay with "will rise up" (anatelei) and the literal expression "from beneath him" (hypokatothen autou).
- **6:13** — The Greek omits the Masoretic Text's repeated clause about building the house, reads "receive excellence" (lempsetai areten), and clearly distinguishes two figures by placing "the priest on his right" (ek dexion autou).
- **6:14** — Continuing the pattern of v. 10, the Greek translates the Masoretic Text's personal names as descriptors ("those who endure," "its useful ones," "those who have known it," and "for the favor" rather than "Hen") and reads "for a psalm" (eis psalmon) instead of "for a memorial."
