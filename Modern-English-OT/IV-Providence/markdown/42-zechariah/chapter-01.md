## Text

**1:1** In the eighth month of the second year under Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,

**1:2** "The Lord was angry with your fathers with great anger.

**1:3** And you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: Return to Me, says the Lord of powers, and I will return to you, says the Lord of powers.

**1:4** And do not become like your fathers, whom the prophets formerly reproached, saying, "Thus says the Lord Almighty: Turn away from your evil ways and from your evil practices." And they did not listen, and they did not pay attention to listen to Me, says the Lord.

**1:5** Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, will they live forever?

**1:6** Yet receive My words and My statutes, all that I command by My Spirit to My servants the prophets, that overtook your fathers. And they answered and said, "Just as the Lord Almighty has resolved to do to us according to our ways and according to our practices, so He did to us."'"

**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Shebat, in the second year under Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying:

**1:8** I saw in the night, and look, a man mounted on a red horse, and he was standing among the shady mountains, and behind him were red, ash-gray, dappled, and white horses.

**1:9** And I said, "What are these, sir?" And the angel speaking in me said to me, "I will show you what these are."

**1:10** And the man standing among the mountains answered and said to me, "These are the ones whom the Lord sent out to travel through the earth."

**1:11** And they answered the angel of the Lord standing among the mountains and said, "We have traveled through all the earth, and look, all the earth is inhabited and quiet."

**1:12** And the angel of the Lord answered and said, "Lord Almighty, how long will You not have mercy on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, which You have overlooked this seventieth year?"

**1:13** And the Lord Almighty answered the angel speaking in me with good words and comforting words.

**1:14** And the angel speaking in me said to me, "Cry out, saying, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: I have been jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with great jealousy,

**1:15** and with great anger I am angry with the nations that join in attacking, because while I was angry a little, they joined in attacking for evil.

**1:16** Therefore thus says the Lord: I will look upon Jerusalem with compassion, and My house will be rebuilt in it, says the Lord Almighty, and a measure will yet be stretched out over Jerusalem.'"

**1:17** And the angel speaking in me said to me, "Cry out, saying, 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: Cities will yet overflow with good things, and the Lord will yet have mercy on Zion and will choose Jerusalem.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Greek shifts within this verse from "the Lord Almighty" (Kyrios Pantokrator) to "the Lord of powers" (Kyrios ton dynameon), as in 7:4.
- **1:6** — The Greek reads an imperative ("receive") and adds "by My Spirit" (en pneumati mou), whereas the Hebrew MT has a rhetorical question with no mention of the Spirit. It also reads "answered" (apekrithesan) where the MT has "repented/returned."
- **1:8** — The Greek reads "shady mountains" (oreon ton kataskion) where the Hebrew MT has "myrtle trees in the ravine," and lists four colors of horses rather than three.
- **1:15** — The Greek uses the same verb twice for the nations "that join in attacking" and "joined in attacking" (synepitithemai), whereas the Hebrew MT describes them first as "at ease" and then as "furthering" disaster.
