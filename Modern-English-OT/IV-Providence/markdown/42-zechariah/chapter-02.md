## Text

**2:1** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, four horns.

**2:2** And I said to the angel speaking in me, "What are these, sir?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that scattered Judah and Israel and Jerusalem."

**2:3** And the Lord showed me four craftsmen.

**2:4** And I said, "What are these coming to do?" And he said, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, and they shattered Israel, and none of them lifted up his head; and these have come out to sharpen them into their hands—the four horns, the nations that lift up a horn against the land of the Lord to scatter it."

**2:5** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, a man, and in his hand was a measuring cord.

**2:6** And I said to him, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see how great its width is and how great its length."

**2:7** And look, the angel speaking in me was standing there, and another angel was going out to meet him,

**2:8** and he said to him, "Run and speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem will be fruitfully inhabited because of the multitude of people and livestock in its midst;

**2:9** and I will be to it, says the Lord, a wall of fire all around, and I will be glory in its midst.'"

**2:10** "Ah, ah! Flee from the land of the north," says the Lord, "for from the four winds of heaven I will gather you," says the Lord;

**2:11** "Escape to Zion, you who dwell in daughter Babylon."

**2:12** For thus says the Lord Almighty: "After glory He has sent me to the nations that plundered you, for the one who touches you is like one who touches the pupil of His eye.

**2:13** For look, I am bringing My hand upon them, and they will be plunder to those who serve them, and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me."

**2:14** "Delight and rejoice, daughter of Zion, for look, I am coming and will dwell in your midst," says the Lord.

**2:15** And many nations will flee for refuge to the Lord in that day, and they will be a people for Him, and they will dwell in your midst, and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me out to you.

**2:16** And the Lord will inherit Judah as His portion upon the holy land, and will yet choose Jerusalem.

**2:17** Let all flesh stand in awe at the presence of the Lord, for He has risen up from His holy clouds.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The Greek diverges sharply from the Hebrew MT here: it reads "shattered" Israel, renders the craftsmen's task as "to sharpen them into their hands" (reflecting alternative readings of Hebrew words for "terrify" and "cast down"), and specifies "the land of the Lord" instead of "the land of Judah."
- **2:8** — The Greek reads "fruitfully" (katakarpos), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "without walls" (as unwalled villages).
- **2:10** — The Greek promises gathering ("from the four winds of heaven I will gather you," synaxo), whereas the Hebrew MT speaks of past scattering ("I have scattered you like the four winds of heaven").
- **2:12** — The abrupt shift from "Thus says the Lord Almighty" to "He has sent me" leaves debated whether the one sent "after glory" is the prophet himself or a divine messenger.
- **2:17** — The Greek reads "stand in awe" (eulabeistho) and "from His holy clouds" (ek nephelon hagion autou), whereas the Hebrew MT has "be silent" and "from His holy dwelling."
