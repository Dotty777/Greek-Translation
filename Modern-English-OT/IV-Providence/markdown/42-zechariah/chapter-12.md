## Text

**12:1** An oracle of the word of the Lord concerning Israel. The Lord, who stretches out heaven, and lays the foundation of the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him, says:

**12:2** "Look, I am making Jerusalem like shaken doorways for all the peoples all around, and in Judea there will be a siege against Jerusalem.

**12:3** And it will be on that day that I will make Jerusalem a trampled stone for all the nations; everyone who tramples her will surely mock, and all the nations of the earth will be gathered together against her.

**12:4** On that day," says the Lord Almighty, "I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness; but upon the house of Judah I will open My eyes, and all the horses of the peoples I will strike with blindness.

**12:5** And Judah's commanders of thousands will say in their hearts, 'We will find for ourselves the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the Lord Almighty their God.'

**12:6** On that day I will make Judah's commanders of thousands like a brand of fire among wood and like a torch of fire in stubble, and they will devour on the right and on the left all the peoples all around; and Jerusalem will still dwell by herself in Jerusalem.

**12:7** And the Lord will save the tents of Judah as from the beginning, so that the boast of the house of David and the exaltations of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not be magnified over Judah.

**12:8** And it will be on that day that the Lord will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and the one who is weak among them on that day will be like David, and the house of David like the house of God, like the angel of the Lord before them.

**12:9** And it will be on that day that I will seek to remove all the nations that come against Jerusalem,

**12:10** and I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and compassion; and they will look to Me because they mocked in triumph, and they will mourn with mourning for Him as for a beloved one, and they will grieve with grief as for the firstborn.

**12:11** On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be magnified like the mourning of a pomegranate grove being cut down in a plain,

**12:12** and the land will mourn, tribe by tribe: the tribe of the house of David by itself, and their wives by themselves; the tribe of the house of Nathan by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**12:13** the tribe of the house of Levi by itself, and their wives by themselves; the tribe of Simeon by itself, and their wives by themselves.

**12:14** All the remaining tribes, each tribe by itself, and their wives by themselves."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Greek prothyra saleuomena means 'shaken doorways' (reading Hebrew saph as 'threshold/porch' rather than 'cup'), and the Greek specifies 'in Judea there will be a siege.'
- **12:3** — The Septuagint reads 'a trampled stone' and active/middle empaizon empaixetai ('will surely mock'), where the Hebrew reads 'a heavy stone; all who lift it will severely injure themselves.'
- **12:10** — Greek anth' hon katorchesanto literally means 'because they danced in mockery/triumph,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and John 19:37) reads 'whom they pierced.'
- **12:11** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew 'Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo' as 'a pomegranate grove being cut down in a plain.'
- **12:13** — The Greek reads the LXX-distinct proper name Symeon ('Simeon'), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Shimei.'
