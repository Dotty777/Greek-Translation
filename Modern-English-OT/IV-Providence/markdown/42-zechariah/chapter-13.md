## Text

**13:1** On that day every place will be opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for removal and for separation.

**13:2** "And it will happen on that day," says the Lord Sabaoth, "that I will utterly destroy the names of the idols from the land, and there will no longer be any remembrance of them; and I will remove the false prophets and the unclean spirit from the land."

**13:3** And it will happen, if a man still prophesies, that his father and his mother, who brought him forth, will say to him, "You will not live, because you spoke lies in the name of the Lord." And his father and his mother, who brought him forth, will bind him when he prophesies.

**13:4** And it will happen on that day that the prophets will be put to shame, each one because of his vision when he prophesies, and they will put on a hairy hide because they lied.

**13:5** And he will say, "I am not a prophet, for I am a man who works the ground, because a man brought me forth from my youth."

**13:6** And someone will say to him, "What are these wounds between your hands?" And he will say, "Those with which I was wounded in my beloved house."

**13:7** "Sword, awake against My shepherds and against a man who is My fellow citizen," says the Lord Almighty. "Strike the shepherds and pull out the sheep, and I will bring My hand upon the little ones."

**13:8** "And it will happen in all the land," says the Lord, "that two parts of it will be utterly destroyed and perish, but the third part will be left behind in it.

**13:9** And I will bring the third part through fire, and I will refine them as silver is refined, and I will test them as gold is tested. It will call upon My name, and I will listen to it; and I will say, 'This is My people,' and it will say, 'The Lord is my God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Greek reads "every place" where the Hebrew MT has "a fountain," and "for removal and for separation" corresponding to Hebrew "for sin and for impurity."
- **13:2** — Here alone in Zechariah the Greek transliterates Hebrew sabaoth ('hosts/armies') directly as Κύριος σαβαώθ ('the Lord Sabaoth') rather than translating it as Κύριος Παντοκράτωρ ('the Lord Almighty').
- **13:3** — Greek sympodizo ("bind" or "tie the feet of") differs markedly from the Hebrew MT reading "pierce him through."
- **13:4** — Unlike the Hebrew MT ("they will not wear a hairy cloak in order to deceive"), the Greek lacks the negation and reads positively that they will put on a hairy hide because they lied.
- **13:5** — Greek egennesen ("brought forth / fathered") reflects taking the Hebrew verb qanah ("acquired/bought") in its sense of "produce/beget."
- **13:7** — The Greek reads plural "shepherds" and plural imperatives "Strike ... and pull out" (ekspasate), differing from both the Hebrew MT ("strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered") and the New Testament citation in Matthew 26:31 and Mark 14:27.
