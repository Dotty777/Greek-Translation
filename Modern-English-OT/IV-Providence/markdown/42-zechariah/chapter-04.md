## Text

**4:1** And the angel speaking in me returned and raised me up, just as when a man is awakened from his sleep.

**4:2** And he said to me, "What do you see?" And I said, "I have seen, and look, a lampstand entirely of gold, and the lamp-bowl on top of it, and seven lamps on top of it, and seven oil funnels for the lamps that are on top of it,

**4:3** and two olive trees above it, one on the right of its lamp-bowl and one on the left."

**4:4** And I asked and said to the angel speaking in me, saying, "What are these, sir?"

**4:5** And the angel speaking in me answered and said to me, saying, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, sir."

**4:6** And he answered and said to me, saying, "This is the word of the Lord to Zerubbabel, saying, 'Not by great power nor by strength, but only by My Spirit,' says the Lord Almighty.

**4:7** 'Who are you, great mountain before the face of Zerubbabel, to succeed? And I will bring out the stone of the inheritance, equality of grace, its grace.'"

**4:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**4:9** "The hands of Zerubbabel—he laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will complete it, and you will recognize that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you.

**4:10** For who has despised small days? And they will rejoice and see the tin stone in the hand of Zerubbabel. These seven are eyes that look over all the earth."

**4:11** And I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on the left?"

**4:12** And I asked a second time and said to him, "What are the two branches of the olive trees that are in the hands of the two golden spouts that pour into and supply the golden oil funnels?"

**4:13** And he said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, sir."

**4:14** And he said, "These are the two sons of richness; they stand before the Lord of all the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Greek "speaking in me" (ho lalon en emoi) literally reflects the Hebrew idiom for speaking with someone; the verb exeren reads "raised/lifted me up" rather than "awakened me."
- **4:7** — The Greek diverges sharply from the Masoretic Text, reading an infinitive "to succeed" (tou katorthosai), a first-person verb "I will bring out" (exoiso), "the stone of the inheritance" (tes kleronomias), and the appositional phrase "equality of grace, its grace" (isoteta charitos charita autes).
- **4:10** — "The tin stone" (ton lithon ton kassiterinon) literally denotes a metal plummet or plumb weight; unlike the Masoretic Text, the Greek text omits "of the Lord" after "eyes."
- **4:14** — "Sons of richness" renders Greek huioi tes piotetos (literally "sons of fatness/richness"), referring to the rich oil of the olive tree.
