## Text

**14:1** Look, days of the Lord are coming, and your plunder will be divided within you.

**14:2** And I will gather all nations against Jerusalem for war, and the city will be captured, and the houses will be plundered, and the women will be defiled, and half of the city will go out into captivity, but the rest of My people will by no means be utterly destroyed from the city.

**14:3** And the Lord will go out and do battle against those nations, like a day of His battle on a day of war.

**14:4** And His feet will stand on that day on the Mount of Olives, which is opposite Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives will be split, half of it toward the east and the sea, an exceedingly great chasm; and half of the mountain will lean toward the north, and half of it toward the south.

**14:5** And the valley of My mountains will be blocked up, and the valley of the mountains will be joined as far as Iasod, and it will be blocked up just as it was blocked up in the days of the earthquake, in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. And the Lord my God will come, and all the holy ones with Him.

**14:6** And it will happen on that day that there will be no light, and there will be cold and frost.

**14:7** It will be for one day, and that day is known to the Lord, neither day nor night; and toward evening there will be light.

**14:8** And on that day living water will go out from Jerusalem, half of it into the first sea and half of it into the last sea; both in summer and in spring it will be so.

**14:9** And the Lord will be king over all the earth; on that day the Lord will be one, and His name one,

**14:10** encircling all the land and the wilderness from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem. But Ramah will remain in place, from the Gate of Benjamin to the place of the First Gate, to the Gate of the Corners, and to the Tower of Hanamel, to the king's wine vats.

**14:11** They will dwell in it, and there will no longer be a curse, and Jerusalem will dwell confidently.

**14:12** And this will be the calamity with which the Lord will strike all the peoples who marched against Jerusalem: their flesh will melt away while they are standing on their feet, and their eyes will flow out of their sockets, and their tongue will melt away in their mouth.

**14:13** And it will happen on that day that there will be a great panic from the Lord upon them; and each one will seize the hand of his neighbor, and his hand will be locked against the hand of his neighbor.

**14:14** And Judah will do battle in Jerusalem, and will gather the strength of all the peoples all around: gold and silver and clothing in very great abundance.

**14:15** And this will be the calamity of the horses and the mules and the camels and the donkeys and all the livestock that are in those camps, like this calamity.

**14:16** And it will happen that as many as are left from all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year by year to worship the King, the Lord Almighty, and to celebrate the Feast of Booths.

**14:17** And it will happen that as many as do not go up from all the tribes of the earth to Jerusalem to worship the King, the Lord Almighty, these also will be added to those.

**14:18** And if the tribe of Egypt does not go up or come, upon these also will be the calamity with which the Lord will strike all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths.

**14:19** This will be the sin of Egypt and the sin of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths.

**14:20** On that day what is on the bridle of the horse will be holy to the Lord Almighty; and the pots in the house of the Lord will be like bowls before the altar.

**14:21** And every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah will be holy to the Lord Almighty; and all who sacrifice will come and take from them and boil in them, and there will no longer be a Canaanite in the house of the Lord Almighty on that day.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The Greek reads "will be blocked up" (phrachthesetai / enphrachthesetai) where the Hebrew MT has "you will flee," uses the LXX-distinct place name "Iasod" (Hebrew "Azal"), and closes with "with Him" (Hebrew "with you").
- **14:8** — The Greek reads "in spring" (en eari) where the Hebrew MT has "in winter," and renders the eastern and western seas literally as "the first sea" and "the last sea."
- **14:10** — The Greek begins with the participle "encircling" continuing from 14:9, reads the Hebrew verb for "be lifted up" as the place name "Ramah," and reads "Hanamel" (Anamel) where the Hebrew MT has "Hananel."
- **14:17** — Instead of the Hebrew MT's "upon them there will be no rain," the Greek reads "these also will be added to those" (linking them with the afflicted nations) and likewise omits any mention of rain in 14:18.
- **14:20** — The Greek reads "what is on the bridle of the horse" where the Hebrew MT has "the bells of the horses," and treats "holy to the Lord Almighty" as a predicate adjective parallel to 14:21.
