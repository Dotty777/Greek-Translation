## Text

**10:1** Ask rain from the Lord in season, early and late; the Lord has made manifestations, and He will give them winter rain, to each one vegetation in the field.

**10:2** For those who utter oracles spoke troubles, and the diviners false visions, and they were speaking false dreams; they comforted in vain. Therefore they were dried up like sheep and were afflicted, because there was no healing.

**10:3** "Against the shepherds My wrath was provoked, and I will call the lambs to account; and the Lord God Almighty will visit His flock, the house of Judah, and will station them as His stately horse in war.

**10:4** And from him He looked, and from him He stationed, and from him a bow in wrath; from him will go forth everyone who drives out, together.

**10:5** And they will be like warriors treading mud in the streets in war, and they will draw up for battle, because the Lord is with them; and riders of horses will be put to shame.

**10:6** And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will settle them, because I have loved them; and they will be as though I had not turned away from them, for I am the Lord their God, and I will listen to them.

**10:7** And they will be like warriors of Ephraim, and their heart will rejoice as with wine; and their children will see and be glad, and their heart will rejoice in the Lord.

**10:8** I will signal to them and receive them in, because I will redeem them; and they will be multiplied just as they were many.

**10:9** And I will sow them among peoples, and those far away will remember Me; they will raise their children and return.

**10:10** And I will bring them back from the land of Egypt, and from the Assyrians I will receive them in; and I will bring them into Gilead and into Lebanon, and not even one of them will be left behind.

**10:11** And they will pass through a narrow sea, and they will strike waves in the sea, and all the depths of rivers will be dried up; and all the pride of the Assyrians will be taken away, and the scepter of Egypt will be removed.

**10:12** And I will strengthen them in the Lord their God, and in His name they will boast," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek phantasias literally means 'appearances' or 'manifestations' (sky-phenomena or visions), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'storm clouds' or 'lightning flashes.'
- **10:2** — The Septuagint reads exeranthesan ('were dried up') and iasis ('healing'), where the Hebrew text reads 'wandered' and 'shepherd.'
- **10:4** — The Greek renders this verse with two finite verbs ('looked' and 'stationed') and 'a bow in wrath,' diverging from the Hebrew nouns 'cornerstone,' 'tent peg,' and 'battle bow.'
- **10:10** — The Greek reads 'not even one of them will be left behind,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'no room will be found for them.'
