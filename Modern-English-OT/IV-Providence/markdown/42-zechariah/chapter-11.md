## Text

**11:1** Open your doors, Lebanon, and let fire devour your cedars.

**11:2** Let the pine tree wail, for the cedar has fallen, because the great ones were greatly devastated; wail, oaks of Bashan, because the dense forest has been torn down.

**11:3** A sound of lamenting shepherds, because their greatness has been devastated; a sound of roaring lions, because the pride of the Jordan has been devastated.

**11:4** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Shepherd the sheep of slaughter,

**11:5** which those who bought them were slaughtering and felt no remorse, and those who sold them were saying, 'Blessed be the Lord, and we have become rich,' and their shepherds felt nothing for them.

**11:6** Therefore I will no longer spare the inhabitants of the land," says the Lord, "and look, I am handing over the people, each one into the hands of his neighbor and into the hands of his king; and they will strike down the land, and I will by no means rescue them from their hand."

**11:7** And I will shepherd the sheep of slaughter into Canaan; and I will take for myself two staffs: the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Allotment; and I will shepherd the sheep.

**11:8** And I will remove the three shepherds in one month, and my soul will be weighed down against them, for their souls also roared against me.

**11:9** And I said, "I will not shepherd you; what is dying, let it die, and what is failing, let it fail, and as for the rest, let each one devour the flesh of his neighbor."

**11:10** And they will take my beautiful staff, and I will cast it away to annul my covenant which I made with all the peoples.

**11:11** And it will be annulled on that day, and the Canaanites will know the sheep that are guarded for me; for it is the word of the Lord.

**11:12** And I will say to them, "If it is good in your sight, give my wages, or refuse." And they weighed out my wages, thirty silver pieces.

**11:13** And the Lord said to me, "Drop them into the smelting furnace, and I will examine whether it is genuine, just as it was tested for them." And I took the thirty silver pieces and threw them into the house of the Lord, into the smelting furnace.

**11:14** And I cast away the second staff, Allotment, to annul the possession between Judah and Israel.

**11:15** And the Lord said to me, "Take for yourself once more the shepherding gear of an inexperienced shepherd.

**11:16** For look, I am raising up a shepherd over the land; what is failing he will by no means attend to, and what is scattered he will by no means seek, and what is crushed he will by no means heal, and what is sound he will by no means guide; and he will devour the meat of the choice ones, and he will dislocate their anklebones.

**11:17** Woe to those who shepherd vain things and have abandoned the sheep! A sword upon his arms and upon his right eye! His arm will be completely dried up, and his right eye will be completely blinded."

## Translator Notes

- **11:7** — The Greek reads the proper name 'into Canaan' (Chanaanitin, and 'the Canaanites' in 11:11) where the Hebrew has 'therefore, the afflicted of [the flock]'; Schoinisma ('Allotment') literally denotes a portion measured out with a cord, corresponding to Hebrew 'Union.'
- **11:10** — The Saints Greek text reads third-person plural lempsontai ('they will take') before first-person aporripso ('I will cast away'), reflecting the broader alternation between future and past tenses in the Septuagint of 11:7-14.
- **11:11** — The Greek uses the LXX-distinct proper name hoi Chananaiioi ('the Canaanites') as the subject and takes 'the sheep that are guarded for me' as the object, followed by dioti ('for').
- **11:13** — Greek choneuterion means 'smelting furnace' (where the Hebrew reads 'the potter'), and third-person singular edokimasthe ('it was tested') matches the metal-assaying imagery rather than Hebrew first-person 'I was valued.'
- **11:14** — Greek kataschesin means 'possession' or 'allotted holding,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'brotherhood.'
