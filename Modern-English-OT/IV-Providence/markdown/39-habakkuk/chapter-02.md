## Text

**2:1** I will stand upon my watchpost and climb onto a rock, and I will keep watch to see what He will speak in me, and what I should answer concerning my reproof.

**2:2** And the Lord replied to me and said, "Write a vision, and clearly on a tablet, so that the one who reads them may run.

**2:3** For a vision is still for an appointed time, and it will rise to the end and not in vain; if he lags behind, wait for him, because he will indeed come and will certainly not delay.

**2:4** If he shrinks back, My soul takes no pleasure in him; but the righteous one will live by My faithfulness.

**2:5** Now the conceited despiser will accomplish nothing at all—he who enlarged his soul like Hades, and this one, like death, is not satisfied; and he will gather together to himself all the nations, and will take in to himself all the peoples.

**2:6** Will not all these take up a proverb against him and a riddle as an account about him? And they will say, 'Woe to the one who multiplies for himself things that are not his own—how long?—and weighs down his collar heavily.

**2:7** For suddenly those who bite him will rise up, and those who plot against you will wake up, and you will become plunder for them.

**2:8** Because you plundered many nations, all the remaining peoples will plunder, because of the bloodshed of human beings and the acts of ungodliness of land and city and all those who inhabit it.

**2:9** Alas for the one who greedily takes evil gain for his house, to set his nest on high, to be snatched away from the hand of evils.

**2:10** You devised shame for your house; you finished off many peoples, and your soul has sinned.

**2:11** For a stone will call out from a wall, and a beetle from timber will utter these things.

**2:12** Woe to the one who builds a city with bloodshed, and prepares a city with injustices.

**2:13** Are not these things from the Lord Almighty? And numerous peoples failed in fire, and many nations grew fainthearted.

**2:14** For the earth will be filled with knowing the glory of the Lord, as water will cover them.

**2:15** Alas for the one who gives his neighbor to drink with turbid overthrow, and makes him drunk, so that he may look upon their caves.

**2:16** Drink a fullness of dishonor rather than glory, you too; be shaken in heart and quake! The cup of the right hand of the Lord has come around upon you, and dishonor has been gathered upon your glory.

**2:17** For the ungodliness of Lebanon will cover you, and the misery of wild beasts will terrify you, because of the bloodshed of human beings and the acts of ungodliness of land and city and all those who inhabit it.

**2:18** What profit is a carved image, that they carved it? He molded it as a cast image, a false illusion, because the one who molded it has trusted in his molded work, to make mute idols.

**2:19** Woe to the one who says to the wood, "Wake up, rise up!" and to the stone, "Be lifted up!" And it itself is an illusion; and this is a beaten plate of gold and silver, and there is no spirit at all in it.'

**2:20** But the Lord is in His holy temple; let all the earth stand in awe before His presence."

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The Septuagint reads "by My faithfulness" (or "by faith in Me") rather than MT "by his faithfulness," and together with the masculine "him" in 2:3 forms the wording cited in Hebrews 10:37-38.
- **2:6** — The Greek renders the rare Hebrew term for heavy pledges/debt as "his collar," referring to a heavy neck-collar.
- **2:11** — The Greek reads "a beetle" (a wood-boring beetle) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a beam" or "rafter."
- **2:14** — The Greek reads "as water will cover them" (referring to the peoples and nations of 2:13) rather than MT "as the waters cover the sea."
- **2:15** — The Greek translates "their caves" (reading Hebrew me'arot) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "their nakedness."
