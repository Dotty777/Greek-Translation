## Text

**1:1** The pronouncement that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

**1:2** How long, Lord, will I cry out, and You will not listen? Will I call out to You while being wronged, and You will not save?

**1:3** Why have You shown me troubles and hardships to look upon, misery and ungodliness? Judgment has come about opposite me, and the judge takes.

**1:4** Therefore law has been shattered, and judgment is not carried through to completion, because an ungodly one oppresses the righteous one; for this reason judgment will go out perverted.

**1:5** See, you despisers, and look closely, and marvel at marvelous things and vanish; for I am performing a work in your days that you will certainly not believe if anyone recounts it.

**1:6** For look, I am raising up the Chaldeans, the bitter and swift nation, which marches across the breadths of the earth to inherit dwellings not its own.

**1:7** It is terrifying and imposing; from itself its judgment will come, and its pronouncement will go out from itself.

**1:8** And its horses will leap more than leopards, and are fiercer than the wolves of Arabia; and its horsemen will ride out and will rush from afar, and they will fly like an eagle eager to eat.

**1:9** Complete destruction will come upon ungodly ones who resist with their faces opposite, and it will gather captives like sand.

**1:10** And it will make sport of kings, and tyrants are its playthings; and it will mock at every stronghold, and it will throw up a mound and capture it.

**1:11** Then it will change its spirit, and will pass through and make atonement; this strength belongs to my God.

**1:12** Are You not from the beginning, Lord God, my Holy One? And we will certainly not die. Lord, You have appointed it for judgment, and He formed me to reprove its discipline.

**1:13** Pure is the eye, so as not to see evil things or look upon hardships of pain; why do You look upon those who despise? Will You remain silent while an ungodly one swallows up the righteous one?

**1:14** And will You make human beings like the fish of the sea, and like the creeping things that have no leader?

**1:15** He drew up complete destruction with a hook, and dragged him in a casting net, and gathered him in his dragnets. For this reason his heart will be glad and rejoice.

**1:16** For this reason he will sacrifice to his dragnet and burn incense to his casting net, because by them he fattened his portion, and his foods are choice.

**1:17** Therefore he will cast his casting net, and he will not refrain from continually killing nations.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Greek text reads "and the judge takes" (implying bribery), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and strife arises."
- **1:5** — The Septuagint addresses "you despisers" (reflecting Hebrew bogedim rather than MT baggoyim, "among the nations") and adds "and vanish"; this Greek wording is quoted in Acts 13:41.
- **1:8** — The Greek translates the Hebrew consonants for "evening" (erev) as the proper name "Arabia" (Arav).
- **1:11** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "be guilty" and "to his god," the Greek reads "make atonement" and "to my God," turning the final clause into a confession of the prophet's God.
- **1:12** — The Greek shifts to the third person ("He formed me to reprove its discipline"), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants behind MT "O Rock, You have established it for reproof."
