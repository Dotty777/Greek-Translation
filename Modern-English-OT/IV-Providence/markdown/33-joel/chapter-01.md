## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Joel the son of Bethuel.

**1:2** Hear these things, elders, and listen closely, all who inhabit the land. Have such things happened in our days, or in the days of your fathers?

**1:3** Give an account of them to your children, and your children to their children, and their children to another generation.

**1:4** What the caterpillar left, the locust devoured; and what the locust left, the wingless locust devoured; and what the wingless locust left, the blight devoured.

**1:5** Sober up, drunkards, from their wine, and weep; lament, all who drink wine, to drunkenness, because gladness and joy have been taken away from your mouth.

**1:6** For a nation has come up against My land, strong and innumerable; its teeth are the teeth of a lion, and its molars are those of a young lion.

**1:7** It has reduced My vine to desolation, and My fig trees to splinters; it has thoroughly searched it out and thrown it away; it has whitened its branches.

**1:8** Lament to Me more than a bride girded with sackcloth over the husband of her virginity.

**1:9** Sacrifice and drink offering have been taken away from the house of the Lord; mourn, priests who minister at the altar.

**1:10** Because the plains are devastated, let the land mourn; because grain is devastated, wine has dried up, oil has diminished,

**1:11** farmers have dried up. Lament, estates, over wheat and barley, because the harvest has perished from the field;

**1:12** the vine has dried up, and the fig trees have diminished; pomegranate and palm and apple and all the trees of the field have dried up, because the sons of men have put joy to shame.

**1:13** Gird yourselves and wail, priests; lament, you who minister at the altar. Come in, sleep in sackcloth, you who minister to God, because sacrifice and drink offering have been kept back from the house of your God.

**1:14** Sanctify a fast, proclaim a service, gather elders, all who inhabit the land, into the house of your God, and cry out to the Lord earnestly:

**1:15** "Alas, alas, alas for the day, for the day of the Lord is near, and like devastation from devastation it will come."

**1:16** Before your eyes food has been utterly destroyed; from the house of your God, gladness and joy.

**1:17** Heifers have leaped at their mangers, storehouses have been laid waste, vats have been torn down, because grain has dried up.

**1:18** What will we store up for ourselves? Herds of cattle have wept, because there was no pasture for them, and the flocks of sheep have been wiped out.

**1:19** To You, Lord, I will cry out, because fire has consumed the lovely places of the wilderness, and a flame has set ablaze all the trees of the field;

**1:20** and the beasts of the plain have looked up to You, because channels of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the lovely places of the wilderness.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek reads Bathouel (standard English "Bethuel"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Pethuel."
- **1:4** — The Greek identifies the four scourges as caterpillar (kampe), locust (akris), wingless locust (brouchos), and blight (erysibe), rendering four Hebrew terms for locusts.
- **1:15** — The Greek renders the Hebrew wordplay "destruction from Shaddai (the Almighty)" as "devastation from devastation" (talaiporia ek talaiporias), and repeats the lament exclamation "Alas" (oimoi) three times.
- **1:17** — The Greek reads "Heifers have leaped at their mangers," a striking divergence from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "The seeds shriveled under their clods."
- **1:18** — The Greek opens with the question "What will we store up for ourselves?" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "How the beasts groan!"
