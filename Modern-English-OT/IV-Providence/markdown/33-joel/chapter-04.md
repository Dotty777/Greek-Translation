## Text

**4:1** For look, I, in those days and at that time, when I restore the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,

**4:2** will gather all the nations and bring them down into the valley of Jehoshaphat, and I will enter into judgment with them there on behalf of My people and My inheritance Israel, who were scattered among the nations; and they divided up My land,

**4:3** and they cast lots for My people, and they gave the boys to prostitutes, and they sold the girls for wine and drank.

**4:4** And what are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon, and all Galilee of the foreigners? Are you paying back a repayment to Me? Or are you sharply holding a grudge against Me? And swiftly I will pay back your repayment onto your heads,

**4:5** because you took My silver and My gold, and you brought My choice and fine things into your temples;

**4:6** and you sold the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem to the sons of the Greeks, so that you might drive them out from their borders.

**4:7** Look, I am rousing them from the place where you sold them, and I will pay back your repayment onto your heads,

**4:8** and I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them into captivity to a nation far away, for the Lord has spoken.

**4:9** Proclaim these things among the nations: consecrate war, rouse the warriors, and draw near and come up, all you men of war;

**4:10** beat your plows into swords and your sickles into spears; let the weak one say, "I am strong."

**4:11** Assemble and come in, all you nations all around, and be gathered there; let the meek one be a warrior.

**4:12** Let them be roused, let all the nations come up into the valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit to judge all the nations all around.

**4:13** Send out sickles, for the grape harvest is at hand; come in, tread, because the winepress is full; make the vats overflow, for their evils have multiplied.

**4:14** Sounds resounded in the valley of judgment, for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of judgment.

**4:15** The sun and the moon will grow dark, and the stars will withdraw their brightness.

**4:16** And the Lord will cry out from Zion, and from Jerusalem He will utter His voice, and heaven and earth will be shaken; but the Lord will spare His people and will strengthen the sons of Israel.

**4:17** And you will know that I am the Lord your God, dwelling in Zion, on My holy mountain; and Jerusalem will be holy, and foreigners will no longer pass through her.

**4:18** And it will be in that day that the mountains will drip sweetness, and the hills will flow with milk, and all the channels of Judah will flow with waters, and a spring will come out from the house of the Lord and will water the wadi of the rushes.

**4:19** Egypt will become a desolation, and Idumea will become a plain of desolation because of the injustices against the sons of Judah, because they shed righteous blood in their land.

**4:20** But Judea will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem for generations of generations;

**4:21** and I will avenge their blood and will by no means acquit, and the Lord will dwell in Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek uses the proper name "Galilee of the foreigners" where the Hebrew reads "regions of Philistia" (the Septuagint regularly renders Philistines as "foreigners"), and punctuates "sharply" with the preceding question before "And swiftly."
- **4:8** — The Greek reads "into captivity" rather than the Hebrew "to the Sabeans," reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants.
- **4:11** — The Greek reads "let the meek one be a warrior," diverging from the Hebrew text ("bring down your mighty ones").
- **4:14** — The Greek reads "Sounds resounded in the valley of judgment," translating the Hebrew "Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision" through the verbal sense of clamor/roaring and a courtroom rendering of the valley's name.
- **4:19** — The Greek uses the Hellenistic territorial name "Idumea" for Edom (paralleling "Judea" in 4:20) and renders "desolate wilderness" as "plain of desolation."
