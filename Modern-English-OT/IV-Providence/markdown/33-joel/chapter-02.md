## Text

**2:1** Sound a trumpet in Zion, proclaim on My holy mountain, and let all who inhabit the land be thrown into confusion, because the day of the Lord is here, for it is near:

**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and mist. Like dawn a numerous and strong people will be poured out upon the mountains; one like it has not been from ages past, and after it none will be added, even to years for generations of generations.

**2:3** In front of it is a consuming fire, and behind it a blazing flame; like a garden of delight is the land before its face, and behind it a plain of desolation, and there will be no one who escapes it.

**2:4** Like the appearance of horses is their appearance, and like horsemen, so they will pursue;

**2:5** like the sound of chariots they will leap upon the tops of the mountains, and like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, and like a numerous and strong people arrayed for war.

**2:6** Before its face peoples will be crushed, every face like the scorch of a pot.

**2:7** Like warriors they will run, and like men of war they will climb upon the walls; and each one will march in his own way, and they will by no means turn aside from their paths,

**2:8** and each one will not keep away from his brother; weighed down in their armor they will march, and among their missiles they will fall, and they will by no means be brought to an end.

**2:9** They will seize the city, and upon the walls they will run, and upon the houses they will climb, and through windows they will enter like thieves.

**2:10** Before its face the earth will be thrown into confusion and heaven will be shaken; the sun and the moon will grow dark, and the stars will withdraw their brightness.

**2:11** And the Lord will utter His voice before His force, because His camp is exceedingly numerous, because strong are the works of His words; for great is the day of the Lord, great and exceedingly manifest, and who will be equal to it?

**2:12** And now the Lord your God says, "Turn back to Me with all your heart, and with fasting and with weeping and with wailing,

**2:13** and tear your hearts and not your garments, and turn back to the Lord your God, because He is merciful and compassionate, patient and rich in mercy, and relenting over the calamities."

**2:14** Who knows whether He will turn back and relent, and leave behind Him a blessing, a sacrifice and a drink offering for the Lord our God?

**2:15** Sound a trumpet in Zion, sanctify a fast, proclaim a service,

**2:16** gather the people, sanctify an assembly, choose elders, gather infants nursing at the breasts; let a bridegroom come out from his bedroom and a bride from her bridal chamber.

**2:17** In the midst of the platform of the altar the priests who minister to the Lord will weep and say, "Spare Your people, Lord, and do not give Your inheritance to reproach, for nations to rule over them, so that they may not say among the nations, 'Where is their God?'"

**2:18** And the Lord became zealous for His land, and spared His people.

**2:19** And the Lord answered and said to His people, "Look, I am sending you the grain and the wine and the oil, and you will be filled with them, and I will no longer give you to reproach among the nations;

**2:20** and the one from the north I will drive away from you, and I will drive him out into a waterless land, and I will make his front vanish into the first sea, and his rear into the last sea; and his rottenness will rise up, and his stench will rise up, because he has magnified his works."

**2:21** Take courage, land, rejoice and be glad, because the Lord has acted greatly.

**2:22** Take courage, beasts of the plain, because the plains of the wilderness have sprouted, because the tree has borne its fruit; fig tree and vine have given their strength.

**2:23** And children of Zion, rejoice and be glad in the Lord your God, because He has given you food for righteousness, and He will rain down for you early and late rain, as before,

**2:24** and the threshing floors will be filled with grain, and the vats will overflow with wine and oil.

**2:25** And He will repay you for the years that the locust and the wingless locust and the blight and the caterpillar devoured, My great force that I sent against you;

**2:26** and you will eat and eat and be filled, and you will praise the name of the Lord your God for the things He has done with you as marvels, and My people will by no means be put to shame forever;

**2:27** and you will recognize that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and there is no other besides Me, and My people will by no means be put to shame again, forever.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The Greek phrase "the scorch of a pot" (proskauma chytras) describes faces blackened with dread, interpreting a difficult Hebrew expression.
- **2:17** — The Greek reads "in the midst of the platform [krepis] of the altar" with no conjunction before "altar," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "between the vestibule and the altar."
- **2:20** — The "first sea" and "last sea" render the Hebrew expressions for the eastern (Dead) and western (Mediterranean) seas; βρόμος (normally "noise, roar") here stands as an orthographic variant of βρῶμος ("stench").
- **2:23** — The Greek renders the Hebrew phrase for the autumn rain (or teacher) as "food" (ta bromata), yielding "food for righteousness."
- **2:25** — The Greek shifts within the verse from third person ("He will repay," antapodosei) to first person ("My great force that I sent"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses first person throughout.
