## Text

**2:1** Look, upon the mountains are the feet of one proclaiming good news and announcing peace. Celebrate your festivals, Judah; pay your vows, because they will certainly never again pass through you to decay.

**2:2** It has been brought to an end; it has been removed. One breathing into your face has come up, rescuing from distress. Watch the road, take hold of your loins, be exceedingly courageous in strength;

**2:3** for the Lord has turned back the insolence of Jacob, just as the insolence of Israel, because, shaking out, they shook them and their vine branches out. They destroyed

**2:4** the weapons of their power from among men, mighty men mocking in fire; the reins of their chariots in the day of his preparation, and the horsemen will be thrown into uproar

**2:5** in the roads, and the chariots will be thrown into confusion and will become entangled in the broad squares; their appearance is like torches of fire and like lightning flashes darting about.

**2:6** And their nobles will be remembered, and they will flee by day and grow weak in their march, and they will hurry to her walls and prepare their outposts.

**2:7** The gates of the cities were opened wide, and the palace collapsed,

**2:8** and the foundation was uncovered, and she was going up, and her female servants were being led away like doves moaning in their hearts.

**2:9** And Nineveh, like a pool of water are her waters, and they, fleeing, did not stop, and there was no one looking back.

**2:10** They plundered the silver, they were plundering the gold, and there was no end to her adornment; they have been weighed down over all her desirable vessels.

**2:11** Shaking out and shaking up and upheaval and shattering of heart, and loosening of knees and labor pains upon all loins, and the face of all is like the scorching of a pot.

**2:12** Where is the dwelling place of the lions, and the pasture belonging to the cubs? Where did a lion go for a lion's cub to enter there, and there was no one causing terror?

**2:13** A lion seized enough for his cubs and strangled for his lions, and he filled his lair with prey and his dwelling place with plunder.

**2:14** "Look, I am against you," says the Lord Almighty, "and I will burn up your multitude in smoke, and a sword will devour your lions, and I will utterly destroy your prey from the earth, and your deeds will certainly never be heard again."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek "to decay" (εἰς παλαίωσιν) renders Hebrew beliyya'al ("worthlessness / the wicked one") by connecting it with the root meaning "to grow old, wear out."
- **2:2** — The Greek reads "One breathing into your face... rescuing from distress," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "A scatterer has come up against you; guard the rampart."
- **2:4** — The verb "They destroyed" at the end of 2:3 governs the accusative phrases opening 2:4 in the Greek syntax. The Greek "from among men" and "mocking" reflect different readings of the Hebrew terms translated "dyed red" and "in scarlet."
- **2:7** — The Greek reads "gates of the cities" (πόλεων) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "gates of the rivers," likely reflecting an early inner-Greek transcriptional variant for ποταμῶν.
- **2:14** — The Greek reads "your multitude" (πλῆθός σου) and "your deeds" (τὰ ἔργα σου), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "her chariots" and "the voice of your messengers."
