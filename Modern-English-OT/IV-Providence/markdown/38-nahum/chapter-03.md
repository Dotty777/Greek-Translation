## Text

**3:1** Ah, city of bloodshed, wholly false, full of injustice, prey will not be handled.

**3:2** The sound of whips and the sound of the shaking of wheels, and of a pursuing horse and of a jolting chariot,

**3:3** and of a horseman going up and of a gleaming sword and of flashing weapons, and of a multitude of wounded and of a heavy fall; and there was no end to her nations, and they will grow weak in their bodies from a multitude of prostitution.

**3:4** A beautiful and charming prostitute, mistress of sorceries, who sells nations by her prostitution and peoples by her sorceries.

**3:5** "Look, I am against you," says the Lord God Almighty, "and I will uncover your back parts over your face, and I will show nations your shame and kingdoms your dishonor;

**3:6** and I will throw abomination upon you according to your impurities, and I will make you an example;

**3:7** and it will happen that everyone who sees you will go down from you and will say, 'Wretched Nineveh! Who will groan over her? From where will I seek comfort for her?'"

**3:8** Prepare a portion, tune the string, prepare a portion, Ammon, who dwells among rivers; water is around her, whose dominion is the sea, and water is her walls.

**3:9** And Ethiopia is her strength, and Egypt, and there is no end to the flight, and Libyans became her helpers.

**3:10** And she herself will go into exile as a captive, and they will dash her infants to the ground at the heads of all her streets, and over all her glorious things they will cast lots, and all her nobles will be bound with manacles.

**3:11** You too will become drunk and will be overlooked, and you too will seek for yourself a standing place away from enemies.

**3:12** All your strongholds are fig trees having watchmen; if they are shaken, they will fall into the mouth of one who eats.

**3:13** Look, your people are like women in your midst; to your enemies the gates of your land will certainly be opened; fire will devour your bars.

**3:14** Draw siege water for yourself, and strengthen your strongholds; step into the clay and be trampled in straw; grow stronger than brick.

**3:15** There fire will devour you, a sword will utterly destroy you, it will devour you like a locust, and you will be weighed down like a young locust.

**3:16** You multiplied your commercial dealings more than the stars of heaven; a young locust rushed forward and flew away.

**3:17** Your mixed multitude leaped out like a grasshopper, like a locust settled on a fence on a day of frost; the sun rose, and it leaped away, and it did not know its place. Woe to them!

**3:18** Your shepherds slumbered, Assyrian king; he put your rulers to sleep; your people departed to the mountains, and there was no one receiving them.

**3:19** There is no healing for your fracture, your wound has become inflamed; all who hear the news about you will clap their hands over you; for upon whom has your wickedness not come continually?

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek "prey will not be handled" (οὐ ψηλαφηθήσεται θήρα) renders Hebrew yamish ("depart") by linking it with the root mashash ("to feel, grope").
- **3:3** — The Greek reads "to her nations" (τοῖς ἔθνεσιν αὐτῆς) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "to the corpses," and places "from a multitude of prostitution" at the close of 3:3 rather than at the start of 3:4.
- **3:8** — The Greek retains the proper name "Ammon" (Ἀμμών) and renders the opening Hebrew question ("Are you better than No-Amon [Thebes]?") as imperatives ("Prepare a portion, tune the string, prepare a portion, Ammon").
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "fig trees having watchmen" (συκαῖ σκοποὺς ἔχουσαι), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "fig trees with first-ripe figs."
- **3:17** — The Greek reads "Your mixed multitude," uses the active verb "it did not know its place," and ends with "Woe to them!" (οὐαὶ αὐτοῖς), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text asks "Where are they?"
