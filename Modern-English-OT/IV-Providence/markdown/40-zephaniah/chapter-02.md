## Text

**2:1** Gather together and be bound together, you undisciplined nation,

**2:2** before you become like a flower passing away, before the wrath of the Lord comes upon you, before the day of the fury of the Lord comes upon you.

**2:3** Seek the Lord, all you humble of the earth; practice justice and seek righteousness, and answer them, so that you may be sheltered on the day of the wrath of the Lord.

**2:4** For Gaza will be plundered, and Ashkelon will be for desolation, and Ashdod will be cast out at midday, and Ekron will be uprooted.

**2:5** Woe to you who dwell in the stretch of the sea, sojourners of the Cretans! The word of the Lord is against you, Canaan, land of foreigners, and I will destroy you from habitation;

**2:6** and Crete will become a pasture of flocks and a fold of sheep,

**2:7** and the stretch of the sea will belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; upon them they will graze in the houses of Ashkelon, in the evening they will lodge from the presence of the sons of Judah, because the Lord their God has visited them and has turned back their captivity.

**2:8** I have heard the reproaches of Moab and the buffetings of the sons of Ammon, with which they reproached My people and magnified themselves against My borders.

**2:9** Therefore, as I live, says the Lord of powers, the God of Israel, surely Moab will be like Sodom, and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah, and Damascus abandoned like a heap of a threshing floor and made desolate forever; and the remnant of My people will plunder them, and the remnant of My nation will inherit them.

**2:10** This is for them in return for their arrogance, because they reproached and magnified themselves against the Lord Almighty.

**2:11** The Lord will appear against them, and He will utterly destroy all the gods of the nations of the earth; and they will worship Him, each from his own place, all the islands of the nations.

**2:12** You also, Ethiopians, are the wounded of My sword;

**2:13** and He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy the Assyrian, and He will make Nineveh a waterless desolation like a wilderness;

**2:14** and flocks will graze in her midst, and all the wild beasts of the earth; and chameleons and hedgehogs will lie down in her paneled ceilings, and wild beasts will cry out in her breaches, ravens in her gateways, because cedar is her stature.

**2:15** This is the scornful city, who dwells in confidence, who says in her heart, "I am, and there is no one after me anymore." How she has become a desolation, a pasture of wild beasts! Everyone who passes through her will hiss and wave his hands.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — Greek reads "before you become like a flower passing away," differing from the Hebrew ("before the decree takes effect—the day passes like chaff").
- **2:3** — Greek renders "and answer them" (apokrinesthe auta), reading the Hebrew noun for "humility" from the homonymous root meaning "to answer."
- **2:5** — Greek names the coastal inhabitants "sojourners of the Cretans" (and "Crete" in 2:6) rather than the Hebrew "Cherethites," and renders Philistines with its standard term "foreigners" (allophyloi).
- **2:9** — Greek introduces the distinct proper name "Damascus" ("abandoned like a heap of a threshing floor"), where the Hebrew reads "a possession of nettles and a salt pit."
- **2:14** — Greek specifies "chameleons and hedgehogs" in the paneled ceilings, "ravens in her gateways," and "because cedar is her stature," diverging from the Hebrew fauna and architectural wording.
