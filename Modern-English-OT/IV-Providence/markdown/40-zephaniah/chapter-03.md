## Text

**3:1** Alas, the illustrious and redeemed city,

**3:2** the dove! She did not heed a voice; she did not accept discipline; she did not trust in the Lord, and she did not draw near to her God.

**3:3** Her rulers within her are like roaring lions; her judges are like wolves of Arabia; they left nothing behind until the morning.

**3:4** Her prophets are spirit-driven, contemptuous men; her priests profane the holy things and act impiously toward the law.

**3:5** But the Lord is righteous in her midst and will by no means do wrong; morning by morning He will give His judgment to the light, and He was not hidden, and He did not know injustice in exaction, nor injustice to victory.

**3:6** In destruction I tore down the proud; their corner towers were made desolate. I will lay waste their roads completely so that no one passes through; their cities vanished because no one is present and no one dwells there.

**3:7** I said, "Only fear Me and accept discipline, and you will by no means be utterly destroyed from her eyes, all that I avenged on her." Prepare yourself, rise early; all their gleaning has been ruined.

**3:8** Therefore wait for Me, says the Lord, for the day of My rising up for a testimony; because My judgment is for assemblies of nations, to take in kings, to pour out upon them all the wrath of My fury; because in the fire of My jealousy all the earth will be consumed.

**3:9** For then I will turn to peoples a tongue for its generation, so that all may call upon the name of the Lord, to serve Him under one yoke.

**3:10** From the ends of the rivers of Ethiopia I will accept among My dispersed ones; they will bring sacrifices to Me.

**3:11** On that day you will by no means be put to shame because of all your practices in which you acted impiously against Me; for then I will take away from you the scornful acts of your arrogance, and you will no longer continue to boast upon My holy mountain.

**3:12** And I will leave behind in you a gentle and humble people, and they will stand in awe of the name of the Lord,

**3:13** the remnant of Israel, and they will not do injustice and will not speak vain things, and a deceitful tongue will by no means be found in their mouth; because they themselves will graze and lie down, and there will be no one who terrifies them.

**3:14** Rejoice, daughter of Zion; proclaim, daughter of Jerusalem! Be glad and delight yourself with all your heart, daughter of Jerusalem.

**3:15** The Lord has taken away your wrongdoings; He has redeemed you from the hand of your enemies. The King of Israel, the Lord, is in your midst; you will no longer see evil things.

**3:16** At that time the Lord will say to Jerusalem, "Take courage, Zion; do not let your hands grow slack."

**3:17** The Lord your God is in you; the Mighty One will save you. He will bring gladness upon you and renew you in His love, and He will be glad over you with delight as on a day of festival.

**3:18** And I will gather the crushed. Woe! Who took up a reproach against her?

**3:19** Look, I am acting in you for your sake at that time, says the Lord, and I will save her who has been oppressed and her who has been driven away; I will take them in for a boast and as renowned ones in all the earth.

**3:20** And they will be put to shame at that time when I do good to you, and at the time when I take you in; because I will make you renowned and a boast among all the peoples of the earth, when I turn back your captivity before you, says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Across 3:1-2, Greek reads "illustrious and redeemed city, the dove," construing the unvocalized Hebrew consonants from roots meaning "appear," "redeem," and "dove" rather than "rebellious, defiled, oppressing."
- **3:3** — Greek uses the distinct proper name "Arabia" ("wolves of Arabia"), reading the unvocalized Hebrew consonants for "evening" as "Arabia."
- **3:5** — Greek keeps the Lord as subject in the closing clauses ("He did not know injustice in exaction, nor injustice to victory"), whereas the Hebrew contrasts "the unjust [who] knows no shame."
- **3:7** — Greek reads "from her eyes" (rather than "her dwelling") and ends with "Prepare yourself, rise early; all their gleaning has been ruined," reading the Hebrew word for "deeds" as "gleanings."
- **3:17** — Greek reads "renew you in His love" (rather than "be quiet in His love") and closes the verse with "as on a day of festival," which opens 3:18 in the Hebrew; similarly, 3:20 opens with "And they will be put to shame" from the end of Hebrew 3:19.
