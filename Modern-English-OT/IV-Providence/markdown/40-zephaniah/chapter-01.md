## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Zephaniah, the son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah.

**1:2** Let it utterly vanish from the face of the earth, says the Lord;

**1:3** let man and livestock vanish; let the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea vanish. And the impious will grow weak, and I will remove the lawless from the face of the earth, says the Lord.

**1:4** And I will stretch out My hand against Judah and against all who dwell in Jerusalem; and I will remove from this place the names of Baal and the names of the priests,

**1:5** and those who worship the host of heaven on the rooftops, and those who worship and those who swear by the Lord, and those who swear by their king,

**1:6** and those who turn aside from the Lord, and those who do not seek the Lord, and those who do not hold fast to the Lord.

**1:7** Stand in awe before the presence of the Lord God, because the day of the Lord is near; for the Lord has prepared His sacrifice, He has consecrated His invited guests.

**1:8** And it will be on the day of the Lord's sacrifice that I will take vengeance on the rulers and on the house of the king and on all who are clothed in foreign garments;

**1:9** and I will openly take vengeance on the gateways on that day, those who fill the house of the Lord their God with impiety and deceit.

**1:10** And there will be on that day, says the Lord, the sound of an outcry from the gate of those who stab, and a wailing from the second, and a great crashing from the hills.

**1:11** Lament, you who dwell in the place that has been cut down, because all the people of Canaan have been made alike; all who are lifted up with silver have been utterly destroyed.

**1:12** And it will be on that day that I will search Jerusalem with a lamp, and I will take vengeance on the men who despise their observances; and as for those who say in their hearts, "The Lord will by no means do good, nor will He do harm,"

**1:13** their wealth will be for plunder, and their houses for desolation; and they will build houses and will by no means dwell in them, and they will plant vineyards and will by no means drink their wine.

**1:14** For the great day of the Lord is near, near and exceedingly swift; the sound of the day of the Lord has been appointed bitter and harsh, mighty.

**1:15** A day of wrath is that day, a day of affliction and distress, a day of untimely ruin and desolation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and mist,

**1:16** a day of trumpet and outcry against the fortified cities and against the high corner towers.

**1:17** And I will afflict human beings, and they will walk like the blind, because they sinned against the Lord; and He will pour out their blood like dust, and their flesh like dung;

**1:18** and their silver and their gold will by no means be able to rescue them on the day of the wrath of the Lord. And in the fire of His jealousy all the earth will be consumed, because He will bring about a complete end and haste upon all who dwell on the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Greek uses the feminine article with Baal ("the names of the [fem.] Baal"), reflecting the Septuagint convention of reading the feminine noun aischyne ("shame") in its place, and reads "names" where the Hebrew has "remnant."
- **1:9** — Greek reads "openly on the gateways" and "the house of the Lord their God," diverging from the Hebrew ("everyone who leaps over the threshold... the house of their master").
- **1:10** — Greek reads "the gate of those who stab" (apokentounton) where the Hebrew has "the Fish Gate."
- **1:11** — Greek translates the district name Maktesh as a participle ("the place that has been cut down") and renders the Hebrew verb as "have been made alike" (from homoioo) rather than "have been silenced/destroyed."
- **1:12** — Greek reads "who despise their observances" (ta phylagmata auton) rather than the Hebrew idiom "stagnant on their dregs," and has singular "a lamp."
