## Text

**4:4** I, Nebuchadnezzar, was prospering in my house and flourishing.

**4:5** I saw a dream, and it frightened me, and I was troubled on my bed, and the visions of my head greatly troubled me.

**4:10** On my bed I was watching, and look, a tree in the middle of the earth, and its height was great.

**4:11** The tree grew great and became strong, and its height reached to heaven, and its expanse to the end of all the earth.

**4:12** Its leaves were lovely, and its fruit was abundant, and food for all was in it; underneath it the wild beasts lodged, and in its branches the birds of the sky dwelt, and from it all flesh was fed.

**4:13** I was watching in a vision of the night on my bed, and look, a watcher and a holy one came down from heaven,

**4:14** and called out with strength, and thus he said, "Cut down the tree, and tear off its branches, and shake off its leaves, and scatter its fruit; let the beasts be dislodged from underneath it and the birds from its branches.

**4:15** Yet leave the stump of its roots in the earth, and in a band of iron and bronze, and in the green grass outside, and in the dew of heaven he will lie down, and his portion will be with the beasts in the grass of the earth.

**4:16** His heart will be altered away from humans, and a beast's heart will be given to him, and seven times will change over him.

**4:17** By the decree of a watcher is the word, and a saying of holy ones is the demand, so that the living may know that the Most High is Lord of the kingdom of humans, and to whomever He sees fit He will give it, and He will set up over it the most despised of humans."

**4:18** This is the dream that I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw; and you, Baltasar, tell the interpretation, because all the wise men of my kingdom are not able to explain its interpretation to me; but you, Daniel, are able, because a holy spirit of God is in you.

**4:19** Then Daniel, whose name was Baltasar, was astounded for about one hour, and his thoughts greatly troubled him. And Baltasar answered and said, "Sir, may the dream be for those who hate you, and its interpretation for your enemies.

**4:20** The tree that you saw, that grew great and was strong, whose height reached to heaven and its expanse to all the earth,

**4:21** and whose leaves were flourishing, and its fruit was abundant, and food for all was in it; underneath it the wild beasts dwelt, and in its branches the birds of the sky lodged;

**4:22** it is you, O king; for you have grown great and become strong, and your greatness has grown great and reached to heaven, and your dominion to the ends of the earth.

**4:23** And in that the king saw a watcher and a holy one coming down from heaven, and he said, 'Tear up the tree and destroy it, yet leave the stump of its roots in the earth, and in a band of iron and in bronze, and in the green grass outside, and in the dew of heaven he will pass the night, and with wild beasts his portion will be until seven times are altered over him';

**4:24** this is its interpretation, O king, and it is a decree of the Most High that has come upon my lord the king:

**4:25** and they will drive you away from humans, and your dwelling will be with wild beasts, and they will feed you grass like an ox, and from the dew of heaven you will pass the night, and seven times will change over you, until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdom of humans, and to whomever He sees fit He will give it.

**4:26** And in that they said, 'Leave the stump of the roots of the tree,' your kingdom will remain yours from the time you know the heavenly authority.

**4:27** Therefore, O king, let my counsel please you, and redeem your sins by acts of mercy, and your injustices by compassion for the poor; perhaps God will be patient toward your transgressions."

**4:28** All these things came upon King Nebuchadnezzar.

**4:29** After twelve months, while walking on the temple of his kingdom in Babylon,

**4:30** the king answered and said, "Is this not Babylon the great that I myself built for a royal house, by the might of my strength, for the honor of my glory?"

**4:31** While the word was still in the king's mouth, a voice came from heaven: "To you they speak, King Nebuchadnezzar: the kingdom has passed away from you,

**4:32** and they are driving you away from humans, and your dwelling will be with wild beasts, and they will feed you grass like an ox, and seven times will change over you, until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdom of humans, and to whomever He sees fit He will give it."

**4:33** In that very hour the word was fulfilled upon Nebuchadnezzar, and he was driven away from humans, and he ate grass like an ox, and his body was drenched with the dew of heaven, until his hair grew long like lions' and his nails like birds'.

**4:34** And after the end of the days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes to heaven, and my understanding returned to me, and I blessed the Most High, and I praised and glorified Him who lives forever, because His authority is an everlasting authority and His kingdom is from generation to generation.

**4:36** At that very time my understanding returned to me, and I came into the honor of my kingdom; and my appearance returned to me, and my rulers and my nobles sought me out, and I was firmly established over my kingdom, and surpassing greatness was added to me.

**4:37** King Nebuchadnezzar to all the peoples, tribes, and languages who dwell in all the earth: May peace be multiplied to you. The signs and the wonders that the Most High God did with me, it has seemed good before me to declare to you. How great and mighty they are! His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom; His authority is from generation to generation. Now therefore, I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and exalt and glorify the King of heaven, because all His works are true and His paths are justice, and all those who walk in pride He is able to humble.

## Translator Notes

- **4:13** — Greek εἲρ (eir) is an untranslated transliteration of Aramaic 'ir ("watcher," an angelic being); in 4:17 the form lacks a plural ending (cf. MT plural "watchers").
- **4:18** — Greek uses Baltasar (transliterating Aramaic Belteshazzar) for Daniel's Babylonian name, identical to the king's name in chapter 5, and reads singular "a holy spirit of God" (cf. MT "spirit of the holy gods").
- **4:27** — Greek reads "perhaps God will be patient toward your transgressions," differing from the Aramaic MT ("perhaps there will be a lengthening of your prosperity").
- **4:29** — Greek ναῷ ("temple") translates Aramaic hekela', which can mean either "temple" or "palace."
- **4:33** — Greek reads "like lions'" (leonton) where the Aramaic MT has "like eagles'." Also note that 4:37 concludes the chapter with Nebuchadnezzar's greeting and doxology (paralleling MT 4:1-3), while MT 4:6-9 and 4:35 are absent in this Greek text.
