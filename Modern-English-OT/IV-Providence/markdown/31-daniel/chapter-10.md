## Text

**10:1** In the third year of Cyrus king of the Persians, a word was revealed to Daniel, whose name was called Baltasar; and the word is true, and great power and understanding were given to him in the vision.

**10:2** In those days I, Daniel, was mourning for three weeks of days.

**10:3** I did not eat desirable bread, and meat and wine did not enter into my mouth, and I did not anoint myself with ointment, until the completion of three weeks of days.

**10:4** On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was beside the great river, that is, Tigris Hiddekel.

**10:5** And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and look, one man clothed in linen, and his waist was girded with gold of Uphaz,

**10:6** and his body was like tharsis, and his face was like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes were like torches of fire, and his arms and legs were like the appearance of gleaming bronze, and the sound of his words was like the sound of a multitude.

**10:7** And I, Daniel, alone saw the vision, and the men who were with me did not see the vision; yet a great terror fell upon them, and they fled in fear.

**10:8** And I alone was left, and I saw this great vision, and no strength was left in me, and my glory was turned into ruin, and I retained no strength.

**10:9** And I heard the sound of his words, and when I heard him, I was stunned, with my face to the ground.

**10:10** And look, a hand was touching me, and it raised me onto my knees.

**10:11** And he said to me, "Daniel, man of delight, understand the words that I am speaking to you, and stand in your place, for now I have been sent to you." And when he spoke this word to me, I stood up trembling.

**10:12** And he said to me, "Do not fear, Daniel; for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to afflict yourself before your God, your words were heard, and I have come because of your words.

**10:13** And the prince of the kingdom of the Persians stood against me for twenty-one days; and look, Michael, one of the princes, came to help me, and I left him there with the prince of the kingdom of the Persians.

**10:14** And I have come to give you understanding of what will happen to your people in the last days, for the vision is yet for days."

**10:15** And when he spoke with me according to these words, I set my face upon the ground.

**10:16** And look, one like the likeness of a son of man touched my lips; and I opened my mouth and spoke, and I said to the one standing before me, "My lord, in your vision my inward parts turned within me, and I had no strength.

**10:17** And how will your servant, my lord, be able to speak with this lord of mine? And as for me, from now on no strength will stand in me, and no breath was left in me."

**10:18** And one like the appearance of a man touched me again, and strengthened me,

**10:19** and said to me, "Do not fear, man of delight; peace to you; be courageous and be strong." And when he spoke with me, I grew strong, and I said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

**10:20** And he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? And now I will return to fight against the prince of the Persians; and I was going in, and the prince of the Greeks was coming.

**10:21** Yet I will declare to you what is inscribed in the writing of truth, and there is not one holding fast with me concerning these things except Michael your prince."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek reads Baltasar (the Septuagint/Theodotion form used for both Belteshazzar and Belshazzar), and renders "great power and understanding were given to him" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a great conflict."
- **10:4** — Greek uniquely combines both river names, Tigris and Hiddekel (Eddekel), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions only Hiddekel.
- **10:5** — βαδδείν (also in 12:6-7) transliterates Hebrew baddim, "linen garments"; it is rendered by its meaning.
- **10:6** — "tharsis" is an untranslated Greek transliteration of Hebrew tarshish, referring to a golden gemstone such as chrysolite or beryl.
- **10:13** — Greek has active "I left him [Michael] there with the prince of the kingdom of the Persians" and omits "chief" before "princes" (contrasting with MT "I was left there beside the kings of Persia"); similarly in v. 20, Greek reads "I was going in" rather than "going out."
