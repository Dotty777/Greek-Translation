## Text

**11:1** "And I, in the first year of Cyrus, stood for might and strength.

**11:2** And now I will declare the truth to you: look, three more kings will arise in Persia, and the fourth will become rich with great wealth beyond all; and after he has gained mastery of his wealth, he will rise up against all the kingdoms of the Greeks.

**11:3** And a mighty king will arise, and he will rule over a great dominion, and he will act according to his will.

**11:4** And as soon as he stands, his kingdom will be shattered and divided toward the four winds of heaven, and not to his posterity, nor according to his dominion that he ruled; for his kingdom will be plucked up, and for others besides these.

**11:5** And the king of the south will grow strong; and one of their princes will grow strong over them and will rule a great dominion.

**11:6** And after his years they will join together, and the daughter of the king of the south will come to the king of the north to make a treaty with him; and she will not retain strength of arm, and his seed will not stand, and she will be handed over, and those who bring her, and the young woman, and the one who strengthens her in those times.

**11:7** And one from the blossom of her root, of his position, will stand, and he will come to the army, and he will enter into the strongholds of the king of the north, and he will act against them and prevail.

**11:8** And indeed their gods with their cast images, every desirable vessel of theirs, of silver and gold, he will carry with captivity into Egypt; and he will stand above the king of the north.

**11:9** And he will come into the kingdom of the king of the south, and he will return into his own land.

**11:10** And his sons will gather a multitude among many, and he will indeed come and overflow, and he will pass through and settle down and engage in conflict as far as his strength.

**11:11** And the king of the south will be enraged, and he will go out and fight against the king of the north, and he will raise up a great multitude, and a multitude will be handed over into his hand;

**11:12** and he will take the multitude, and his heart will be lifted up, and he will cast down tens of thousands, yet he will not prevail.

**11:13** And the king of the north will return and bring a great multitude beyond the former, and at the end of the times of years he will come in an invasion, with a great army and with many possessions.

**11:14** And in those times many will rise up against the king of the south, and the sons of the remnant of your people will lift themselves up to establish a vision, and they will grow weak.

**11:15** And the king of the north will enter, and he will pour out a siege mound and capture fortified cities; and the arms of the king of the south will stand, and his chosen ones also will rise up, and there will be no strength to stand.

**11:16** And the one who comes against him will do according to his will, and there is no one standing before him; and he will stand in the land of Sabeir, and it will be accomplished in his hand.

**11:17** And he will set his face to enter with the strength of all his kingdom, and he will make all things straight with him; and he will give him a daughter of women to ruin her, yet she will not remain, and she will not be for him.

**11:18** And he will turn his face to the islands and capture many, and he will put an end to the princes of their reproach; yet his reproach will return to him.

**11:19** And he will turn his face toward the strength of his own land, and he will grow weak and fall, and he will not be found.

**11:20** And there will arise from his root a plant of the kingdom in his position, causing to pass over, exacting the glory of the kingdom; and in those days he will be shattered, yet not in faces, nor in battle.

**11:21** One will stand in his position; he was despised, and they did not bestow the glory of the kingdom on him; yet he will come in prosperity, and he will gain control of the kingdom through slippery schemes.

**11:22** And the arms of the one who floods will be flooded away from before him and will be shattered, along with a leader of a covenant.

**11:23** And from the alliances with him he will practice deceit, and he will go up and overpower them with a small nation.

**11:24** And in prosperity and in rich regions he will come, and he will do what his fathers and the fathers of his fathers did not do; plunder and spoil and possessions he will scatter among them, and against Egypt he will devise plans, even until a time.

**11:25** And his strength and his heart will be stirred up against the king of the south with a great army, and the king of the south will join battle with a great and exceedingly strong army; and they will not stand, because they will devise plans against him.

**11:26** And they will eat his provisions and shatter him, and he will flood armies, and many wounded will fall.

**11:27** And as for both kings, their hearts are set on evil, and at one table they will speak lies, but it will not succeed; for the end is yet for a time.

**11:28** And he will return to his own land with many possessions, and his heart will be against the holy covenant; and he will act, and he will return to his own land.

**11:29** At the time he will return and come into the south, and it will not be like the first and the last.

**11:30** And the Kittim who go out will come against him, and he will be humbled; and he will return and become enraged against the holy covenant, and he will act and return, and he will pay attention to those who forsake the holy covenant.

**11:31** And seeds from him will rise up, and they will profane the sanctuary of power, and remove the continual offering, and set up an abomination made desolate.

**11:32** And those who act lawlessly against the covenant will lead on with slippery schemes; and a people knowing its God will prevail and act,

**11:33** and those of the people who have understanding will understand in many things, yet they will grow weak by sword and by flame, and by captivity and by plundering for days.

**11:34** And when they grow weak, they will be helped with a little help, and many will join themselves to them with slippery schemes.

**11:35** And some of those who have understanding will grow weak, in order to refine them and to choose and to be revealed until a time's end; for it is yet for a time.

**11:36** And he will do according to his will; and the king will exalt himself and magnify himself above every god, and he will speak arrogant things, and he will succeed until the wrath is completed; for it comes to completion.

**11:37** And to any god of his fathers he will pay no attention, nor to the desire of women, nor to any god will he pay attention; for he will magnify himself above all.

**11:38** And the god Maozein in his place he will glorify, and a god whom his fathers did not know he will glorify with gold and silver and precious stone and with desirable things.

**11:39** And he will deal with the strongholds of the refuges with a foreign god, and he will multiply glory, and he will subject many to them, and he will divide land for gifts.

**11:40** And at the end of a time he will lock horns with the king of the south, and the king of the north will be gathered against him with chariots and horsemen and with many ships; and they will enter into the land, and he will shatter and pass through.

**11:41** And he will enter into the land of Sabaein, and many will grow weak; yet these will be rescued out of his hand: Edom and Moab and the chief of the sons of Ammon.

**11:42** And he will stretch out his hand against the land, and the land of Egypt will not be for deliverance.

**11:43** And he will rule over the hidden treasures of gold and silver, and over all the desirable things of Egypt and of the Libyans and of the Ethiopians, in their strongholds.

**11:44** And reports and urgencies will trouble him from the east and from the north, and he will come with great fury to destroy many.

**11:45** And he will pitch his tent Ephadano between the seas, at the holy mountain of Sabaein; and he will come to his portion, and there is no one who delivers him.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Greek names Cyrus here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Darius the Mede.
- **11:15** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("will not stand"), Greek has no negative particle before "will stand" (v. 15); in v. 16, Greek turns "the glorious land" into a place name, Sabeir (spelled Sabaein in vv. 41, 45).
- **11:20** — Greek adds "a plant of the kingdom," translates the sending of an exactor with the participles "causing to pass over, exacting," and renders Hebrew appayim ("anger/faces") literally as "in faces."
- **11:31** — Greek reads "seeds" (from Hebrew zera'im rather than MT zero'im, "arms/forces") and uses a passive participle ("made desolate") for the abomination; in v. 30, Greek reads "the Kittim who go out" where MT has "ships of Kittim."
- **11:38** — Greek leaves Maozein (Heb. ma'uzzim, "fortresses") untranslated as the name of a god; similarly in v. 45, Ephadano (Heb. appadno, "his palatial tents") is treated as a place name, and "his portion" (Greek merous) stands where MT reads "his end."
