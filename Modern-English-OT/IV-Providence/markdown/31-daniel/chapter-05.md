## Text

**5:1** King Baltasar made a great feast for his thousand nobles, and before the thousand was the wine.

**5:2** And while drinking, Baltasar gave orders at the taste of his wine to bring the gold and silver vessels that Nebuchadnezzar his father had brought out from the temple that was in Jerusalem, and let the king and his nobles and his concubines and his wives drink from them.

**5:3** And the gold and silver vessels were brought that he had brought out from the temple of God that was in Jerusalem, and the king and his nobles and his concubines and his wives drank from them;

**5:4** they drank wine, and praised the gods of gold and silver and bronze and iron and wood and stone.

**5:5** In that very hour fingers of a human hand came out and were writing opposite the lamp on the plaster of the wall and house of the king, and the king was watching the knuckles of the hand that was writing.

**5:6** Then the king's appearance was altered, and his thoughts greatly troubled him, and the joints of his waist were loosened, and his knees knocked together.

**5:7** And the king cried out with strength to bring in magicians, Chaldeans, diviners, and he said to the wise men of Babylon, "Whoever reads this writing and makes known its interpretation to me will be clothed in purple, and the gold collar will be on his neck, and he will rule as third in my kingdom."

**5:8** And all the king's wise men came in, and they were not able to read the writing nor to make known the interpretation to the king.

**5:9** And King Baltasar was troubled, and his appearance was altered within him, and his nobles were greatly troubled.

**5:10** And the queen entered the drinking hall and said, "O king, live forever! Do not let your thoughts trouble you, and do not let your appearance be altered.

**5:11** There is a man in your kingdom in whom is a spirit of God, and in the days of your father watchfulness and understanding were found in him, and King Nebuchadnezzar your father appointed him chief of enchanters, magicians, Chaldeans, diviners,

**5:12** because an extraordinary spirit is in him, and wisdom and understanding—interpreting dreams and declaring hidden matters and loosening knots—Daniel, and the king gave him the name Baltasar; now therefore, let him be called, and he will declare its interpretation to you."

**5:13** Then Daniel was brought in before the king, and the king said to Daniel, "Are you Daniel, the one from the sons of the captivity of Judea, which the king my father brought?

**5:16** And I have heard about you, that you are able to interpret judgments; now therefore, if you are able to read the writing and make known its interpretation to me, you will be clothed in purple, and the gold collar will be on your neck, and you will rule as third in my kingdom."

**5:17** And Daniel said before the king, "Let your gifts be yours, and give the reward of your house to another; yet I will read the writing and make known its interpretation to you. And this is the writing that has been inscribed: 'Mane, Thekel, Phares.'

**5:23** And against the Lord God of heaven you exalted yourself, and you brought the vessels of His house before you, and you and your nobles and your concubines and your wives drank wine from them; and you praised the gods of gold and silver and bronze and iron and wood and stone, which do not see and which do not hear and do not know, but the God in whose hand is your breath and all your ways, Him you did not glorify.

**5:26** This is the interpretation of the word: 'Mane': God has measured your kingdom and brought it to an end;

**5:27** 'Thekel': it has been weighed on a balance and found lacking;

**5:28** 'Phares': your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians."

**5:29** And Baltasar gave the command, and they clothed Daniel in purple, and they put the gold collar around his neck, and he proclaimed concerning him that he was third ruler in the kingdom.

**5:30** In that very night Baltasar the king of the Chaldeans was killed.

**5:20** *(after `5:17`)* And when his heart was lifted up and his spirit grew hardened to act arrogantly, he was brought down from the throne of the kingdom, and the honor was taken away from him,

**5:21** *(after `5:17`)* and he was driven away from human beings, and his heart was given to be with the wild beasts, and his dwelling was with wild donkeys, and they fed him grass like an ox, and his body was drenched with the dew of heaven, until he knew that the Most High God rules over the kingdom of human beings, and to whomever He chooses, He will give it.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek uses Baltasar for King Belshazzar (the same spelling as Daniel's Babylonian name in 4:18-19 and 5:12) and concludes the verse with the verbless clause "before the thousand was the wine."
- **5:7** — Greek γαζαρηνούς (gazarenoi, also in 5:11) transliterates Aramaic gazerin ("diviners" or "astrologers").
- **5:11** — Greek γρηγόρησις ("watchfulness" or "alertness") reflects a reading related to wakefulness where the Aramaic MT has "illumination/insight."
- **5:17** — Greek reads three inscribed words ("Mane, Thekel, Phares") rather than the Aramaic MT's four ("Mene, Mene, Tekel, Upharsin"); verses 5:14-15, 5:18-22, and 5:24-25 of the MT are absent from this Greek text.
- **5:27** — Greek ὑστεροῦσα is feminine singular ("it [your kingdom] ... found lacking"), whereas the Aramaic MT addresses the king directly in the second person ("you have been weighed ... and found deficient").
- **5:22 *(additional)*** — The Greek reads the rhetorical question 'did you not know all these things?' (ou panta tauta egnos) where the Aramaic reads 'even though you knew all this.'
- **5:24 *(additional)*** — Greek astragalos cheiros literally means 'the knuckle [or wrist/palm joint] of a hand,' matching 'knuckles of the hand' in 5:5.
