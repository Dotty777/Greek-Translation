## Text

**7:1** In the third year of Baltasar king of the Chaldeans, Daniel saw a dream and the visions of his head upon his bed, and he wrote down his dream.

**7:2** I, Daniel, was watching, and look, the four winds of heaven were striking against the great sea.

**7:3** And four great beasts were coming up out of the sea, differing from one another.

**7:4** The first was like a lioness, and it had wings like an eagle's; I was watching until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from the earth and stood on a man's feet, and a man's heart was given to it.

**7:5** And look, a second beast, resembling a bear, and it stood on one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth; and thus they were saying to it, "Arise, eat much flesh."

**7:6** After this I was watching, and look, another beast like a leopard; and it had four wings of a bird above it, and the beast had four heads, and authority was given to it.

**7:7** After this I was watching, and look, a fourth beast, terrifying and dreadful and exceedingly strong, and its teeth were iron, eating and crushing, and it was trampling the remainder with its feet, and it was exceedingly different from all the beasts that were before it; and it had ten horns.

**7:8** I was considering its horns, and look, another small horn came up among them, and three of the horns before it were uprooted from its presence; and look, eyes like the eyes of a man were in this horn, and a mouth speaking great things.

**7:9** I was watching until thrones were placed, and the Ancient of Days was seated; His garment was white like snow, and the hair of His head like pure wool; His throne was a flame of fire, its wheels a blazing fire.

**7:10** A river of fire was flowing before Him; a thousand thousands were ministering to Him, and ten thousand myriads were standing before Him; a court sat, and books were opened.

**7:11** I was watching then because of the sound of the great words which that horn was speaking, until the beast was killed and perished, and its body was given to the burning of fire.

**7:12** And the dominion of the remaining beasts was removed, and a length of life was given to them for a time and a time.

**7:13** I was watching in a vision of the night, and look, with the clouds of heaven one like a son of man was coming, and reached the Ancient of Days, and was brought to Him.

**7:14** And to him was given dominion and honor and kingdom, and all the peoples, tribes, and languages serve him; his authority is an eternal authority that will not pass away, and his kingdom will not be destroyed.

**7:15** My spirit shuddered within my frame, I, Daniel, and the visions of my head were troubling me.

**7:16** And I approached one of those standing by, and was seeking the exact truth from him concerning all these things; and he told me the exact truth, and made known to me the interpretation of the words:

**7:17** "These four beasts are four kingdoms that will arise upon the earth, which will be taken away;

**7:18** and the holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and possess it forever and ever."

**7:19** And I inquired closely concerning the fourth beast, because it was different from every beast, exceedingly terrifying, its teeth iron and its claws bronze, eating and crushing, and it was trampling the remainder with its feet;

**7:20** and concerning its ten horns that were on its head, and the other one that came up and shook off some of the first ones, which had the eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and its appearance was greater than the rest.

**7:21** I was watching, and that horn was making war with the holy ones, and prevailed against them,

**7:22** until the Ancient of Days came and gave judgment to the holy ones of the Most High, and the time arrived and the holy ones took possession of the kingdom.

**7:23** And he said, "The fourth beast will be a fourth kingdom on the earth, which will surpass all the kingdoms, and will devour all the earth, and trample it down and cut it to pieces.

**7:24** And as for its ten horns, ten kings will arise; and after them one will arise who will surpass in evils all those before him, and he will humble three kings,

**7:25** and he will speak words against the Most High, and will wear out the holy ones of the Most High, and he will intend to alter times and law, and it will be given into his hand for a time and times and even half a time.

**7:26** And the court sat, and they will remove the dominion, to annihilate and to destroy it to the end,

**7:27** and the kingdom and the authority and the greatness of the kings under the whole heaven was given to the holy ones of the Most High; and His kingdom is an eternal kingdom, and all the dominions will serve and obey Him." Up to here is the end of the matter.

**7:28** As for me, Daniel, my thoughts were greatly troubling me, and my appearance was altered, and I kept the matter in my heart.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek text dates this vision to the "third" year of Baltasar "king of the Chaldeans," whereas the Aramaic reads the "first" year of Belshazzar "king of Babylon" and adds a reference to "the sum of the matters."
- **7:14** — Pronouns referring to "one like a son of man" (7:13) are kept lowercase here; Greek manuscripts make no uppercase/lowercase distinction, and Christian tradition often capitalizes them ("Him/His") as messianic/divine.
- **7:17** — The Greek identifies the four beasts as "four kingdoms" (rather than "four kings" as in the Aramaic) and includes the closing clause "which will be taken away" (αἳ ἀρθήσονται).
- **7:20** — Unlike the Aramaic ("before which three fell"), the Greek omits the number "three" and uses the active participle ἐκτινάξαντος ("shook off") with the partitive genitive τῶν πρώτων ("some of the first ones").
- **7:24** — Where the Aramaic reads "he will be different from the former ones," the Greek text has ὃς ὑπεροίσει κακοῖς πάντας τοὺς ἔμπροσθεν ("who will surpass in evils all those before him").
- **7:27** — The Greek reads "of the kings" (τῶν βασιλέων) rather than "of the kingdoms," and the angel's speech ends before the closing editorial sentence ("Up to here is the end of the matter"), which stands at the beginning of verse 28 in the Aramaic tradition.
