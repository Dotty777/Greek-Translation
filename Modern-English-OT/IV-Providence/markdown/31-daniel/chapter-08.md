## Text

**8:1** In the third year of the reign of Belshazzar the king, a vision appeared to me—I, Daniel—after the one that appeared to me at the beginning.

**8:2** And I was in Susa the fortress, which is in the region of Elam, and I was by the Ubal.

**8:3** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, one ram standing before the Ubal, and it had high horns; and the one was higher than the other, and the high one was coming up last.

**8:4** I saw the ram butting toward the sea and north and south; and none of the beasts will stand before it, and there was no one rescuing from its hand, and it did according to its will and became great.

**8:5** And I was considering, and look, a male goat was coming from the west over the face of all the earth, and it was not touching the earth; and the male goat had a horn between its eyes.

**8:6** And it came up to the ram that had the horns, which I had seen standing before the Ubal, and it ran toward it in the rush of its strength.

**8:7** And I saw it reaching up to the ram, and it became enraged against it; and it struck the ram and shattered both of its horns, and the ram had no strength to stand before it; and it threw it to the earth and trampled it, and there was no one rescuing the ram from its hand.

**8:8** And the male goat became exceedingly great; and when it had grown strong, its great horn was shattered, and four horns came up underneath it toward the four winds of heaven.

**8:9** And out of one of them came forth one strong horn, and it became extraordinarily great toward the south and toward the host;

**8:10** it became great, up to the host of heaven; and some of the host of heaven and some of the stars fell to the earth, and they trampled them,

**8:11** and until the chief commander delivers the captivity, and because of him sacrifice was dashed down, and it prospered for him; and the sanctuary will be made desolate.

**8:12** And sin was given over the sacrifice, and righteousness was thrown to the ground; and it acted and prospered.

**8:13** And I heard a holy one speaking; and one holy one said to Phelmuni who was speaking, "How long will the vision stand—the sacrifice that was taken away and the sin of desolation that was given—and the sanctuary and the host be trampled?"

**8:14** And he said to him, "Until evening and morning, two thousand three hundred days; and the sanctuary will be cleansed."

**8:15** And it happened, when I, Daniel, saw the vision, that I was seeking understanding; and look, there stood before me one like the appearance of a man.

**8:16** And I heard a man's voice in the midst of the Ubal, and he called out and said, "Gabriel, make that one understand the vision."

**8:17** And he came and stood next to where I stood; and when he came, I was terrified, and I fell on my face; and he said to me, "Son of man, for the vision is still for the end of time."

**8:18** And while he was speaking with me, I fell on my face to the earth, and he touched me and set me on my feet,

**8:19** and he said, "Look, I am making known to you the things that will be at the end of the wrath; for the vision is still for the end of time.

**8:20** The ram that you saw, the one having the horns, is the king of the Persians and Medes.

**8:21** And the male goat is the king of the Greeks; and the great horn that was between its eyes, he is the first king.

**8:22** And as for the one that was shattered, where four horns stood underneath, four kings from his nation will arise, and not in their strength.

**8:23** And at the end of their kingdom, when their sins are filled up, a king will arise, shameless in face and understanding riddles.

**8:24** And his strength will be mighty, and he will destroy astonishingly, and he will succeed and act, and he will destroy strong ones and a holy people.

**8:25** And the yoke of his collar will succeed; deceit is in his hand, and in his heart he will make himself great, and by deceit he will destroy many, and he will stand over the destruction of many, and like eggs he will shatter by hand.

**8:26** And the vision of the evening and of the morning that was spoken of is true; and you, seal up the vision, for it is for many days."

**8:27** And I, Daniel, slept and was sick; and I got up and was doing the king's work, and I was astonished at the vision, and there was no one who understood.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek reads "the Ubal" (τοῦ Οὐβάλ), treating the Hebrew common noun ubal ("canal/stream") as a proper name corresponding to "the Ulai" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and omits the Hebrew clause "and I saw in the vision."
- **8:9** — The Greek reads "one strong horn" (ἰσχυρόν) rather than "one little horn" as in Hebrew, and reads "toward the south and toward the host" (τὴν δύναμιν) without Hebrew's "toward the east" and "beautiful land."
- **8:11** — The Greek differs notably from the Hebrew here, reading "until the chief commander delivers the captivity" (vs. Hebrew "even up to the prince of the host it magnified itself") and "because of him sacrifice was dashed down (ἐράχθη), and it prospered for him."
- **8:13** — "Phelmuni" (φελμουνεί) is a Greek transliteration of Hebrew palmoni ("a certain one"), retained as a name or title for the speaking angelic figure; the Greek also reads "sin of desolation that was given" and omits "regular/daily" before "sacrifice."
- **8:25** — The Greek reads "the yoke of his collar" (vs. Hebrew "his cunning"), "over the destruction of many" (vs. Hebrew "against the Prince of princes"), and "like eggs he will shatter by hand" (vs. Hebrew "without hand he will be broken").
