## Text

**12:1** And at that time Michael, the great prince, the one who stands over the sons of your people, will arise; and there will be a time of affliction, affliction such as has not occurred since a nation has existed on the earth until that time. At that time your people will be saved, everyone who is written in the book.

**12:2** And many of those sleeping in the dust of the earth will be awakened, these to eternal life, and those to reproach and to eternal shame.

**12:3** And those who understand will shine like the brilliance of the expanse, and some from among the many righteous, like the stars forever and beyond.

**12:4** And you, Daniel, close up the rest and seal the book until the time of the end, until many are taught and knowledge is multiplied."

**12:5** And I, Daniel, saw, and look, two others were standing, one on this side of the bank of the river and one on that side of the bank of the river.

**12:6** And he said to the man clothed in linen, who was above the water of the river, "How long until the end of the wonders that you have spoken?"

**12:7** And I heard the man clothed in linen, who was above the water of the river, and he raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven, and he swore by the One who lives forever, "For a time of times and half a time; when scattering is completed, they will know all these things."

**12:8** And I heard and did not understand, and I said, "Lord, what is the final outcome of these things?"

**12:9** And he said, "Come, Daniel, for the words are closed up and sealed until the end of the time,

**12:10** until many are chosen and made white and refined, and lawless ones act lawlessly; and lawless ones will not understand, and the discerning will understand.

**12:11** And from the time of the alteration of the continual offering, an abomination of desolation will be given, one thousand two hundred ninety days.

**12:12** Blessed is the one who endures and arrives at one thousand three hundred thirty-five days.

**12:13** And you, come and rest; for there are still days until the fulfillment of the end, and you will arise to your allotted share at the end of days."

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Greek literally reads "from among the many righteous" (apo ton dikaion ton pollon) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "those who lead the many to righteousness," and renders the closing phrase as "forever and beyond" (eis tous aionas kai eti).
- **12:4** — The Greek text reads tous loipous ("the rest," a notable variant for tous logous, "the words"; cf. v. 9) and has "until many are taught" (didachthosin polloi) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "many will run to and fro."
- **12:7** — Greek literally reads "for a time of times" (kairon kairon, genitive plural) and omits the Hebrew phrase "the power of the holy people" after "scattering" (diaskorpismon). It also reads "they will know all these things" (gnosontai) where the Masoretic Text has "all these things will be finished," while "water" (vv. 6-7) is singular and "linen" translates the Hebrew loanword ta baddein.
- **12:10** — This Greek text leaves v. 9 open without final punctuation so that "until" (heos) in v. 9 governs the aorist subjunctives in v. 10; eklegosin literally means "be chosen" rather than "be purified."
- **12:13** — The clause "for there are still days until the fulfillment of the end" is present in the Greek text though absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and deuro (also in v. 9) literally means "come."
