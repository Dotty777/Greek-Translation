## Text

**9:1** In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, who became king over the kingdom of the Chaldeans,

**9:2** I, Daniel, understood in the books the number of the years, concerning which the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet for the completion of the desolation of Jerusalem, seventy years.

**9:3** And I set my face toward the Lord God to seek prayer and supplications in fastings and sackcloth.

**9:4** And I prayed to the Lord my God and made confession and said, "Lord God, the great and wondrous One, who keeps Your covenant and mercy for those who love You and for those who keep Your commandments,

**9:5** we have sinned, we have committed iniquity, we have acted lawlessly, and we have rebelled and turned aside from Your commandments and from Your judgments;

**9:6** and we have not listened to Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings and our rulers and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

**9:7** To You, Lord, belongs righteousness, and to us belongs shame of face, as on this day, to the man of Judah and to those dwelling in Jerusalem and to all Israel, those who are near and those who are far off in every land where You scattered them, because of their treachery with which they acted treacherously.

**9:8** In You, Lord, is our righteousness, and to us belongs shame of face, and to our kings and to our rulers and to our fathers, who have sinned against You.

**9:9** To the Lord our God belong compassions and pardons, because we have rebelled,

**9:10** and we have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God to walk in His laws, which He placed before our face by the hands of His servants the prophets.

**9:11** And all Israel have transgressed Your law and turned aside so as not to hear Your voice; and the curse and the oath written in the law of Moses, the servant of God, came upon us, because we sinned against Him.

**9:12** And He established His words which He spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, to bring upon us great evils, such as have not happened under all heaven like what happened in Jerusalem.

**9:13** Just as it is written in the law of Moses, all these evils came upon us; yet we did not entreat the face of the Lord our God to turn back from our iniquities and to understand in all Your truth.

**9:14** And the Lord kept watch and brought them upon us, for the Lord our God is righteous in all His work that He has done, and we have not listened to His voice.

**9:15** And now, Lord our God, who brought Your people out from the land of Egypt with a mighty hand and made for Yourself a name as on this day, we have sinned, we have acted lawlessly.

**9:16** Lord, in all Your mercy, please let Your anger and Your wrath turn away from Your city Jerusalem, Your holy mountain; because we have sinned, and in our iniquities and those of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people have become a reproach among all those around us.

**9:17** And now listen, Lord our God, to the prayer of Your servant and to his supplications, and make Your face shine upon Your desolate sanctuary for Your own sake, Lord.

**9:18** Incline, my God, Your ear and hear; open Your eyes and see our ruin and that of Your city upon which Your name has been called; for not upon our righteous deeds do we cast our compassion before You, but upon Your many compassions, Lord.

**9:19** Listen, Lord! Pardon, Lord! Pay attention, Lord! Do not delay for Your own sake, my God, because Your name has been called upon Your city and upon Your people."

**9:20** Now while I was still speaking and praying, and confessing my sins and the sins of my people Israel, and casting my mercy before the Lord my God concerning the holy mountain,

**9:21** even while I was still speaking in prayer, look, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, was flying, and he touched me about the hour of the evening sacrifice.

**9:22** And he gave me understanding, and spoke with me and said, "Daniel, now I have come out to instruct you in understanding.

**9:23** At the beginning of your supplication a word went out, and I have come to tell you, because you are a man greatly desired; so consider the message and understand the vision.

**9:24** Seventy weeks have been determined upon your people and upon the holy city, for sin to be ended, and to seal up sins and to blot out the iniquities, and to make atonement for iniquity, and to bring in eternal righteousness, and to seal up vision and prophet, and to anoint a holy of holies.

**9:25** And you will know and understand: from the going out of a word to answer and to build Jerusalem until an anointed one, a ruler, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; and it will return and be built, plaza and wall, and the times will be emptied out.

**9:26** And after the sixty-two weeks, anointing will be utterly destroyed, and there is no judgment in it; and he will destroy the city and the sanctuary with the ruler who is coming; they will be cut off in a flood, and until the end of a determined war, there will be ruins by decree.

**9:27** And one week will confirm a covenant for many; and in the half of the week My sacrifice and drink offering will be taken away, and upon the temple will be an abomination of desolations, and until the end of time, an end will be given upon the desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — The Greek preserves an opening confession ("In You, Lord, is our righteousness") that is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:24** — The Greek contains seven infinitive purpose clauses (adding "and to blot out the iniquities") rather than six as in Hebrew, and reads singular "prophet" (προφήτην) rather than "prophecy."
- **9:25** — Notable Greek readings here include "to answer" (τοῦ ἀποκριθῆναι, vs. Hebrew "to restore"), "an anointed one, a ruler" (χριστοῦ ἡγουμένου), and "the times will be emptied out" (ἐκκενωθήσονται οἱ καιροί, vs. Hebrew "in troubled times").
- **9:26** — The Greek reads neuter "anointing" (χρίσμα, corresponding to Hebrew "anointed one"), "there is no judgment in it" (vs. Hebrew "and have nothing"), "with (σύν) the ruler who is coming" (reading Hebrew as "with" rather than "people of"), and "ruins by decree" (τάξει ἀφανισμοί).
- **9:27** — In Greek, "one week" (ἑβδομὰς μία) stands in the nominative as the grammatical subject of "will confirm" (or as an adverbial time phrase), "My sacrifice" includes the first-person pronoun μου ("My"), and "upon the temple" (ἐπὶ τὸ ἱερόν) corresponds to Hebrew "upon the wing of abominations."
