## Text

**3:1** In the eighteenth year, Nebuchadnezzar the king made a golden image, its height was sixty cubits, its width six cubits; and he set it up in the plain of Deira, in the province of Babylon.

**3:2** And he sent to gather the chief magistrates and the commanders and the district governors, leaders and potentates and those in authority, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image that he had set up.

**3:3** And the district governors, chief magistrates, commanders, leaders, great potentates, those in authority, and all the rulers of the provinces were gathered to the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up. And they stood before the image.

**3:4** And the herald cried out with strength: "To you it is declared, peoples, tribes, languages,

**3:5** at whatever hour you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, trigon and psaltery, and every kind of music, fall down and worship the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king set up;

**3:6** and whoever does not worship, in that very hour will be thrown into the burning furnace of fire."

**3:7** And it happened when the peoples heard the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, trigon and psaltery, and every kind of music, all the peoples, tribes, languages fell down and worshiped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

**3:8** Then Chaldean men came forward and accused the Jews to the king,

**3:9** Nebuchadnezzar: "King, live forever!

**3:10** You, king, issued a decree that every person who hears the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, trigon and psaltery, and every kind of music,

**3:11** and does not fall down and worship the golden image, will be thrown into the burning furnace of fire.

**3:12** There are Jewish men whom you appointed over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, who have not obeyed, king, your decree; they do not serve your gods and they do not worship the golden image that you set up."

**3:13** Then Nebuchadnezzar in fury and anger gave orders to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; and they were brought before the king.

**3:14** And Nebuchadnezzar answered and said to them, "Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, that you do not serve my gods and do not worship the golden image that I set up?

**3:15** Now therefore, if you are ready, when you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe and lyre, trigon and psaltery, and bagpipe, and every kind of music, to fall down and worship the image that I made—but if you do not worship, in that very hour you will be thrown into the burning furnace of fire. And who is a god who will rescue you out of my hand?"

**3:16** And Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego answered, saying to King Nebuchadnezzar, "We have no need to answer you concerning this matter.

**3:17** For there is a God, whom we serve, able to rescue us from the burning furnace of fire; and out of your hands, king, He will deliver us.

**3:18** And if not, let it be known to you, king, that we do not serve your gods and we do not worship the image that you set up."

**3:19** Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with fury, and the appearance of his face changed toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, and he gave orders to heat the furnace sevenfold, until it was completely heated.

**3:20** And he gave orders to men mighty in strength, after binding Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, to throw them into the burning furnace of fire.

**3:21** Then those men were bound with their trousers and turbans and leggings, and were thrown into the middle of the burning furnace of fire,

**3:22** because the king's word was urgent, and the furnace was heated exceedingly.

**3:23** And these three, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the middle of the burning furnace, bound.

**3:24** And they were walking about in the middle of the flame, singing hymns to God and blessing the Lord.

**3:25** And Azariah stood up and prayed in this way, and opening his mouth in the middle of the fire, he said,

**3:26** "Blessed are You, Lord, the God of our fathers, and praiseworthy, and glorified is Your name forever.

**3:27** For You are righteous in all that You have done, and all Your works are true, and Your ways are straight, and all Your judgments are truth.

**3:28** And You have executed judgments of truth according to all that You have brought upon us and upon the holy city of our fathers, Jerusalem; for in truth and judgment You have brought all these things because of our sins.

**3:29** For we have sinned and acted lawlessly in turning away from You, and we have sinned greatly in all things; and we have not listened to Your commandments,

**3:30** nor kept them, nor done as You commanded us, so that it might go well with us.

**3:31** And everything that You have brought upon us and everything that You have done to us, You have done in true judgment.

**3:32** And You have handed us over into the hands of lawless enemies and most hateful rebels, and to an unjust and most wicked king above all the earth.

**3:33** And now it is not possible for us to open our mouth; shame and reproach have come upon Your servants and upon those who revere You.

**3:34** Please do not hand us over completely for the sake of Your name, and do not annul Your covenant,

**3:35** and do not turn away Your mercy from us, for the sake of Abraham who is loved by You, and for the sake of Isaac Your servant and Israel Your holy one,

**3:36** to whom You spoke of multiplying their seed like the stars of heaven and like the sand along the shore of the sea.

**3:37** For, Master, we have been made smaller than all the nations, and we are lowly in all the earth today because of our sins.

**3:38** And there is not at this time ruler, or prophet, or leader, nor burnt offering, nor sacrifice, nor offering, nor incense, no place to offer sacrifice before You and find mercy.

**3:39** Yet with a broken soul and a spirit of humility may we be accepted; as with a burnt offering of rams and bulls, and with tens of thousands of fat lambs,

**3:40** so let our sacrifice be before You today and carry through completely after You, for there will be no shame for those who trust in You.

**3:41** And now we follow with a whole heart, and we fear You and seek Your face. Do not put us to shame,

**3:42** but deal with us according to Your gentleness and according to the abundance of Your mercy.

**3:43** And rescue us according to Your wonders, and give glory to Your name, Lord.

**3:44** And may all who show evil to Your servants be ashamed, and may they be put to shame from all their power, and may their strength be broken;

**3:45** let them know that You are the Lord God alone and glorious over the whole inhabited world."

**3:46** And the king's attendants who threw them in did not stop stoking the furnace with naphtha and pitch and tow and brushwood.

**3:47** and the flame spread out above the furnace for forty-nine cubits,

**3:48** and passed through and burned up those of the Chaldeans whom it found around the furnace.

**3:49** But the angel of the Lord went down together with Azariah and those with him into the furnace, and drove the flame of the fire out of the furnace,

**3:50** and made the middle of the furnace like a whistling wind of dew; and the fire did not touch them at all, and neither distressed nor troubled them.

**3:51** Then the three, as from one mouth, were singing hymns and glorifying and blessing God in the furnace, saying:

**3:52** "Blessed are You, Lord, the God of our fathers, and praiseworthy and highly exalted forever. And blessed is the holy name of Your glory, and supremely praiseworthy and highly exalted for all ages.

**3:53** Blessed are You in the temple of Your holy glory, and supremely worthy of hymns and supremely glorious forever.

**3:54** Blessed are You on the throne of Your kingdom, and supremely worthy of hymns and highly exalted forever.

**3:55** Blessed are You who look upon the depths, seated upon cherubim, and praiseworthy and highly exalted forever.

**3:56** Blessed are You in the expanse of heaven, and worthy of hymns and glorified forever.

**3:57** Bless the Lord, all works of the Lord; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:58** Bless the Lord, angels of the Lord; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:59** Bless the Lord, heavens; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:60** Bless the Lord, waters and all things above heaven; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:61** Let all the host bless the Lord; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:62** Bless the Lord, sun and moon; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:63** Bless the Lord, stars of heaven; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:64** Let all rain and dew bless the Lord; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:65** Bless the Lord, all winds; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:66** Bless the Lord, fire and heat; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:71** Bless the Lord, nights and days; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:72** Bless the Lord, light and darkness; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever. Bless the Lord, cold and heat; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever. Bless the Lord, frost and snow; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:73** Bless the Lord, lightnings and clouds; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:74** Let the earth bless the Lord; let it sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:75** Bless the Lord, mountains and hills; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:76** Bless the Lord, all things that grow on the earth; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:77** Bless the Lord, springs; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:78** Bless the Lord, seas and rivers; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:79** Bless the Lord, sea monsters and all things that move in the waters; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:80** Bless the Lord, all birds of the sky; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:81** Bless the Lord, all wild animals and livestock; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:82** Bless the Lord, sons of men; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:83** Bless the Lord, Israel; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:84** Bless the Lord, priests; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:85** Bless the Lord, servants; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:86** Bless the Lord, spirits and souls of the righteous; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:87** Bless the Lord, godly and humble in heart; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever.

**3:88** Bless the Lord, Hananiah, Azariah, Mishael; sing hymns to Him and highly exalt Him forever; for He has rescued us from Hades, and from the hand of death He has saved us, and He has delivered us from the middle of a burning furnace of flame, and from the middle of the fire He has delivered us.

**3:89** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**3:90** Bless the God of gods, all who revere the Lord; sing hymns and give thanks, for His mercy is forever."

**3:91** And Nebuchadnezzar heard them singing hymns and was amazed, and he stood up in haste and said to his nobles, "Did we not throw three men into the middle of the fire, bound?" And they said to the king, "Truly, king."

**3:92** "Look, I see four men unbound and walking about in the middle of the fire, and there is no harm in them, and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of God."

**3:93** Then Nebuchadnezzar approached the door of the burning furnace of fire and said, "Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out and come here." And Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego came out from the middle of the fire.

**3:94** And the satraps and the commanders and the district governors and the king's officials gathered together and observed the men, that the fire had no power over their bodies, and the hair of their head was not singed, and their trousers were not altered, and there was no smell of fire on them.

**3:95** And Nebuchadnezzar answered and said, "Blessed be the God of Shadrach and Meshach and Abednego, who sent His angel and rescued His servants, because they trusted in Him; and they altered the king's word and handed over their bodies to fire, so that they might not serve nor worship any god except their God.

**3:96** And I issue the decree: 'Every people, tribe, language, if it speaks blasphemy against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, they will be for destruction and their houses for plunder, because there is no other god who will be able to deliver in this way.'"

**3:97** Then the king caused Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego to prosper in the province of Babylon, and deemed them worthy to lead all the Jews who were in his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text opens with the date "In the eighteenth year" (absent in the Aramaic text) and reads the place name "Deira" (corresponding to Aramaic "Dura").
- **3:15** — Greek "symphonia" (a musical instrument, probably a bagpipe or ensemble) appears in the Greek text here, whereas it is omitted in the parallel lists of vv. 5, 7, and 10.
- **3:17** — The Greek reads "For there is a God, whom we serve, able to rescue us" (or "For God, whom we serve, is able"), without the conditional clause and possessive "our" found in the Aramaic text.
- **3:24** — Verses 24–47 (continuing further in the chapter) form part of the Greek-only addition containing the Prayer of Azariah and the expanded narrative of the furnace.
- **3:40** — The clause "and carry through completely after You" (Greek: kai ektelesai opisthen sou) is grammatically difficult and likely expresses wholehearted persistence in following God.
- **3:61** — The Greek reads singular "all the host" (Greek: pasa he dynamis) without "of the Lord," followed by plural imperatives in the second half of the verse.
- **3:72** — In this Greek versification (with vv. 67–70 omitted from the numbering), v. 72 combines three refrains ("light and darkness," "cold and heat," and "frost and snow").
- **3:92** — The Greek begins Nebuchadnezzar's statement directly without an introductory "he answered and said," and reads "like a son of God" (Greek: hyio theou, singular without the article).
- **3:97** — The closing clause ("and deemed them worthy to lead all the Jews who were in his kingdom") is a Greek-only expansion absent from the Aramaic text.
