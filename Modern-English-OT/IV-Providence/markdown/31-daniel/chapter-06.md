## Text

**6:1** And Darius the Mede received the kingdom, being sixty-two years old.

**6:2** And it was pleasing before Darius, and he appointed over the kingdom one hundred twenty satraps to be throughout all his kingdom,

**6:3** and over them three administrators, of whom Daniel was one, for the satraps to give account to them, so that the king would not be troubled.

**6:4** And Daniel was above them, because an extraordinary spirit was in him, and the king appointed him over his whole kingdom.

**6:5** And the administrators and the satraps were seeking to find a pretext against Daniel, and they found no pretext or offense or fault against him, because he was faithful.

**6:6** And the administrators said, "We will not find a pretext against Daniel except in the ordinances of his God."

**6:7** Then the administrators and the satraps presented themselves to the king and said to him, "King Darius, live forever!

**6:8** All the commanders and satraps, high officials and district governors over your kingdom have consulted together to establish a royal statute and to enforce an injunction, that whoever asks a petition from any god or man for thirty days except from you, O king, will be thrown into the den of lions.

**6:9** Now therefore, O king, establish the injunction and issue a written document, so that the decree of the Persians and Medes may not be altered."

**6:10** Then King Darius ordered the decree to be written.

**6:11** And Daniel, when he knew that the decree had been enacted, entered his house; and the windows were open for him in his upper chambers facing Jerusalem, and three times a day he was kneeling on his knees, and praying and giving thanks before his God, just as he had been doing previously.

**6:12** Then those men watched closely, and found Daniel petitioning and imploring his God.

**6:13** And they approached and said to the king, "O king, did you not ordain an injunction that every person who asks a petition from any god or man for thirty days except from you, O king, will be thrown into the den of lions?" And the king said, "The word is true, and the decree of the Medes and Persians will not pass away."

**6:14** Then they answered and said before the king, "Daniel, from the sons of the captivity of Judea, has not submitted to your decree, and three times a day he asks his petitions from his God."

**6:15** Then the king, when he heard the matter, was deeply grieved over it, and strove concerning Daniel to rescue him.

**6:17** Then the king spoke, and they brought Daniel and threw him into the den of lions; and the king said to Daniel, "Your God, whom you serve continually, He Himself will rescue you."

**6:18** And they brought a stone and placed it over the mouth of the den, and the king sealed it with his signet ring and with the signet ring of his nobles, so that nothing might be altered concerning Daniel.

**6:19** And the king went away to his house and lay down without supper, and they did not bring delicacies in to him, and sleep departed from him. And God shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not trouble Daniel.

**6:20** Then the king arose in the morning at daylight, and went in haste to the den of lions.

**6:21** And as he approached the den, he cried out with a loud voice, "Daniel, servant of the living God, was your God, whom you serve continually, able to rescue you from the mouth of the lions?"

**6:22** And Daniel said to the king, "Live forever, O king!

**6:23** My God sent His angel and shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not harm me, because before Him uprightness was found in me; and before you also, O king, I have committed no offense."

**6:24** Then the king was very glad over him, and ordered Daniel to be brought up out of the den. And Daniel was brought up out of the den, and no harm at all was found on him, because he trusted in his God.

**6:25** And the king spoke, and they brought the men who had accused Daniel, and they were thrown into the den of lions, they and their sons and their wives; and they had not reached the floor of the den before the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones.

**6:26** Then Darius the king wrote to all the peoples, tribes, languages dwelling in all the earth: "May peace be multiplied to you.

**6:27** From my presence this decree is issued, that in every dominion of my kingdom people are to tremble and fear before the God of Daniel, for He is the living God and remains forever, and His kingdom will not be destroyed, and His sovereignty is to the end;

**6:28** He helps and delivers, and performs signs and wonders in heaven and on the earth, He who rescued Daniel from the hand of the lions."

**6:29** And Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Unlike the Aramaic tradition ("planned to appoint"), the Greek text uses the indicative κατέστησεν ("appointed"), stating that the king actually appointed Daniel over his whole kingdom.
- **6:15** — The Greek text moves directly from the king's distress and effort to rescue Daniel in 6:15 to his command in 6:17, omitting the phrase "until sunset" and verse 16 (the conspirators' second appeal in the Aramaic tradition).
- **6:19** — The final sentence ("And God shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not trouble Daniel") stands in the Greek text here but is absent from the Aramaic tradition.
- **6:21** — The Greek reads "from the mouth of the lions" (ἐκ στόματος τῶν λεόντων), whereas the Aramaic has simply "from the lions."
