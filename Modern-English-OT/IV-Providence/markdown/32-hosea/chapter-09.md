## Text

**9:1** Do not rejoice, Israel, nor be glad like the peoples; because you played the prostitute away from your God, you loved gifts on every threshing floor of grain.

**9:2** Threshing floor and winepress did not know them, and the wine deceived them.

**9:3** They did not dwell in the land of the Lord; Ephraim dwelt in Egypt, and among the Assyrians they will eat unclean things.

**9:4** They did not pour out wine to the Lord, and they were not pleasing to Him; their sacrifices are like bread of mourning to them; all who eat them will be defiled, because their loaves for their souls will not enter into the house of the Lord.

**9:5** What will you do in the days of a festal assembly and on the day of a feast of the Lord?

**9:6** Therefore, look, they are going away from the distress of Egypt, and Memphis will receive them, and Machmas will bury them; their silver—destruction will inherit it; thorns in the tents.

**9:7** The days of punishment have come, the days of your recompense have come, and Israel will be afflicted like the prophet who is out of his mind, the spirit-bearing man; because of the multitude of your iniquities, your madness has been multiplied.

**9:8** Ephraim is a watchman with God; a prophet is a crooked snare on all his ways; they planted madness in the house of God.

**9:9** They became corrupt as in the days of the hill; He will remember their iniquities; He will punish their sins.

**9:10** Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel, and like an early watchman in a fig tree I saw their fathers; they themselves went in to Baal-peor, and they were alienated into shame, and the detested ones became like the beloved ones.

**9:11** Ephraim flew away like a bird—their glories from births and birth pains and conceptions.

**9:12** For even if they bring up their children, they will be made childless from among mankind; for woe also is to them—My flesh is from them.

**9:13** Ephraim, just as they presented their children for prey, so also Ephraim is to bring out his children for stabbing.

**9:14** Give to them, Lord—what will You give them? A childless womb and dry breasts.

**9:15** All their wicked deeds are in Gilgal, for there I hated them because of the wickedness of their practices; out of My house I will drive them; I will not continue to love them; all their rulers are disobedient.

**9:16** Ephraim has suffered pain; he has dried up in his roots; he will no longer bear fruit; for even if they give birth, I will kill the cherished ones of their womb.

**9:17** God will reject them, because they did not listen to Him, and they will be wanderers among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — The Septuagint connects "Egypt" to "distress" and preserves the distinctive proper name Machmas (cf. Michmash), translating a Hebrew word that the Masoretic Text reads as "precious things" ("their precious things of silver").
- **9:9** — The Greek translates the place name Gibeah literally as a common noun, "the hill" (tou bounou).
- **9:10** — The Septuagint reads "an early watchman in a fig tree" (skopon) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the firstfruit on the fig tree," and renders the closing clause "the detested ones became like the beloved ones."
- **9:12** — The Greek concludes with "My flesh is from them," reading the Hebrew consonants as "My flesh" rather than the Masoretic Text's "when I depart from them."
- **9:13** — The Septuagint reads "for prey" (eis theran) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to "Tyre," and "for stabbing" (eis apokentesin) for MT's "to the slayer."
