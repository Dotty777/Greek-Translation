## Text

**12:1** Ephraim surrounded Me with falsehood, and the house of Israel and Judah with ungodliness. Now God has known them, and they will be called a holy people of God.

**12:2** But Ephraim is an evil spirit; he pursued scorching heat all day long. He multiplied empty and futile things, and he made a covenant with the Assyrians, and he traded oil to Egypt.

**12:3** And the Lord has a judgment against Judah, to punish Jacob according to his ways, and according to his practices He will repay him.

**12:4** In the womb he seized his brother by the heel, and in his toils he proved strong with God,

**12:5** and he proved strong with an angel and prevailed. They wept and pleaded with Me; in the house of On they found Me, and there it was spoken to them.

**12:6** Now the Lord God Almighty will be His memorial.

**12:7** And you will return in your God; guard mercy and judgment, and draw near to your God continually.

**12:8** Canaan: in his hand is a balance of injustice; he loved to oppress.

**12:9** And Ephraim said, "Yet I have become rich; I have found relief for myself." None of his labors will be found for him because of the injustices in which he sinned.

**12:10** But I, the Lord your God, brought you up out of the land of Egypt; I will again make you live in tents, as in the days of a festival.

**12:11** And I will speak to prophets, and I multiplied visions, and by the hands of prophets I was likened.

**12:12** If it is not Gilead, then the rulers sacrificing in Gilead were false, and their altars are like tortoises on the untilled ground of a field.

**12:13** And Jacob withdrew to the plain of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept guard.

**12:14** And by a prophet the Lord brought Israel up out of the land of Egypt, and by a prophet he was guarded.

**12:15** Ephraim aroused wrath and provoked anger; and his blood will be poured out upon him, and the Lord will pay back his reproach to him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Greek joins "and Judah" to the first clause and reads "Now God has known them, and they will be called a holy people of God," diverging from the Hebrew (numbered 11:12 in Hebrew and most English Bibles).
- **12:5** — The Greek shifts to plural verbs ("they wept," "pleaded with Me," "found Me"), uses a passive ("it was spoken to them"), and reads "the house of On" where the Hebrew has "Bethel."
- **12:6** — The Greek pronoun autou may refer either to God ("His memorial," designating His covenant title) or to Jacob ("his memorial").
- **12:11** — The Greek uses a future tense ("I will speak") and a passive verb ("I was likened," meaning represented through prophetic similitudes), where the Hebrew has past and active forms.
- **12:12** — The Greek names Gilead twice (instead of Gilead and Gilgal), reads "rulers" instead of Hebrew "bulls," and renders the altar simile as "tortoises" (chelonai) rather than Hebrew "stone heaps."
