## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

**1:2** The beginning of the word of the Lord in Hosea. And the Lord said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of prostitution and children of prostitution, because the land will surely commit prostitution away from following the Lord."

**1:3** And he went and took Gomer, daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

**1:4** And the Lord said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, because in a little while I will avenge the blood of Jezreel on the house of Judah, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel;

**1:5** and it will be in that day that I will shatter the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel."

**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him, "Call her name 'Not Shown Mercy,' because I will no longer continue to show mercy to the house of Israel, but rather I will surely oppose them.

**1:7** But I will show mercy to the sons and will save them by the Lord their God, and I will not save them by bow, nor by sword, nor by war, nor by horses, nor by horsemen."

**1:8** And she weaned Not Shown Mercy, and she conceived again and bore a son.

**1:9** And He said, "Call his name 'Not My People,' because you are not My people, and I am not yours."

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Greek text reads "on the house of Judah" (ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον Ἰούδα), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "on the house of Jehu."
- **1:6** — The Greek renders the closing clause as "but rather I will surely oppose them" (ἀλλ᾽ ἢ ἀντιτασσόμενος ἀντιτάξομαι αὐτοῖς), differing from the debated Hebrew clause often translated "that I should forgive them at all."
- **1:7** — The Greek text reads simply "the sons" (τοὺς δὲ υἱοὺς) without specifying "of Judah," whereas the Hebrew MT and some Greek witnesses specify the house or sons of Judah.
