## Text

**10:1** Israel is a vine rich in branches; its fruit is plentiful. According to the abundance of its fruits, he multiplied altars; according to the good things of his land, he built pillars.

**10:2** They divided their hearts; now they will be destroyed. He Himself will demolish their altars; their pillars will come to ruin.

**10:3** For now they will say, "We have no king, because we did not fear the Lord. And what will the king do for us,

**10:4** speaking words, false pretexts?" He will make a covenant; judgment will spring up like wild grass on the untilled ground of a field.

**10:5** Those who dwell in Samaria will sojourn beside the calf of the house of On, because its people mourned over it; and just as they provoked it, they will rejoice over its glory, because it was deported from it.

**10:6** And having bound it, they carried it off to the Assyrians as gifts for King Jarim; Ephraim will receive a gift, and Israel will be put to shame in his counsel.

**10:7** Samaria cast off her king like a twig on the surface of water.

**10:8** And the shrines of On, the sins of Israel, will be removed; thorns and thistles will come up over their altars; and they will say to the mountains, "Cover us," and to the hills, "Fall on us."

**10:9** From the time of the hills, Israel sinned; there they stood. War will by no means overtake them on the hill against the children of injustice

**10:10** to discipline them. And peoples will be gathered against them when they are disciplined for their two injustices.

**10:11** Ephraim is a heifer trained to love strife, but I will come upon the finest part of her neck; I will mount Ephraim, I will pass over Judah in silence, Jacob will grow strong for him.

**10:12** Sow for yourselves for righteousness, harvest for the fruit of life, light for yourselves a light of knowledge; seek out the Lord until crops of righteousness come to us.

**10:13** Why did you pass over ungodliness in silence, and harvest its injustices, and eat false fruit? Because you hoped in your sins, in the abundance of your strength,

**10:14** destruction will indeed rise up among your people, and all your fortified places will be gone. Just as the ruler Shalman from the house of Jeroboam, in days of war they dashed mother to the ground upon children,

**10:15** so I will do to you, house of Israel, because of the injustice of your evils.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — The Greek reads "the house of On" (corresponding to Hebrew "Beth-aven"), translates the verb as "sojourn beside" rather than "fear," and reads "just as they provoked it, they will rejoice" where the Hebrew refers to its idol-priests.
- **10:6** — The Greek names "King Jarim" (Hebrew "Jareb" or "the great king") and reads literally "in a gift Ephraim will receive," diverging from the Hebrew "Ephraim will receive shame."
- **10:9** — The Greek renders the place name "Gibeah" as a common noun ("the hills" / "the hill") and states the war clause as an emphatic negation continuing into the infinitive at the start of 10:10.
- **10:11** — The Greek reads "strife" (neikos, sometimes understood by itacism as nikos, "victory") instead of Hebrew "threshing," and translates "pass over in silence" here and in 10:13 by taking the Hebrew verb hrsh as "be silent" rather than "plow."
- **10:14** — The Greek reads "the ruler Shalman from the house of Jeroboam," where the Hebrew refers to Shalman's devastation of Beth-arbel.
