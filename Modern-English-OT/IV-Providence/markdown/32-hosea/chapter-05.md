## Text

**5:1** Hear these things, priests, and pay attention, house of Israel, and house of the king, listen closely, because the judgment is against you; for you have become a snare to the watchtower, and like a net spread out over Tabor,

**5:2** which those who hunt prey have fixed firmly. But I am your discipliner.

**5:3** I have known Ephraim, and Israel is not absent from Me. For now Ephraim has prostituted himself; Israel has been defiled.

**5:4** They have not given their plans to return to their God, for a spirit of prostitution is in them, and they have not known the Lord.

**5:5** And the arrogance of Israel will be humbled to his face, and Israel and Ephraim will grow weak in their injustices, and Judah also will grow weak with them.

**5:6** With sheep and calves they will go to seek out the Lord, and they will by no means find Him, because He has turned away from them.

**5:7** For they abandoned the Lord, because foreign children were born to them; now the blight will devour them and their allotments.

**5:8** Sound a trumpet on the hills, resound on the heights, proclaim in the house of On. Benjamin was astounded;

**5:9** Ephraim has come to ruin in the days of rebuke. Among the tribes of Israel I have shown trustworthy things.

**5:10** The rulers of Judah have become like those who move boundaries; upon them I will pour out My fury like water.

**5:11** Ephraim oppressed his adversary; he trampled judgment, because he began to go after worthless things.

**5:12** And I am like turmoil to Ephraim, and like a goad to the house of Judah.

**5:13** And Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah his pain; and Ephraim went to the Assyrians and sent envoys to King Jarim. And he was not able to heal you, and pain will by no means cease from you.

**5:14** Because I am like a panther to Ephraim, and like a lion to the house of Judah; and I will seize and go away, and I will carry off, and there will be no one who rescues.

**5:15** I will go and return to My place, until they are brought to ruin and seek My face. In their affliction they will rise early to Me, saying:

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Septuagint translates Hebrew "Mizpah" as the common noun "the watchtower" (skopia) and continues in verse 2 with a relative clause describing the hunting net fixed down by hunters of prey.
- **5:7** — The Septuagint reads "the blight" (erysibe, crop mildew or blight) where the Hebrew text reads "the new moon" (or "a month").
- **5:8** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew "Gibeah" and "Ramah" as common nouns ("the hills" and "the heights"), repeats "the house of On" (cf. 4:15), and reads "Benjamin was astounded" rather than "Behind you, Benjamin!"
- **5:12** — Instead of the Hebrew metaphors of a moth and rottenness, the Septuagint reads "turmoil" (tarache) and "a goad" (kentron).
- **5:13** — "King Jarim" preserves the Septuagint proper name Iareim (corresponding to Hebrew yareb, often rendered "Jareb" or "the great king"), and the Greek explicitly states that Ephraim sent "envoys."
