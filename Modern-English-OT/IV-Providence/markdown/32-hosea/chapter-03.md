## Text

**3:1** And the Lord said to me, "Go again and love a woman who loves evil things and is an adulteress, just as God loves the sons of Israel, yet they look to foreign gods and love cakes with raisins."

**3:2** And I hired her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver and a homer of barley and a jar of wine,

**3:3** and I said to her, "For many days you will remain for me, and you will certainly not commit prostitution nor belong to a man, and I will be for you."

**3:4** Because for many days the sons of Israel will remain with no king, nor ruler, nor sacrifice, nor altar, nor priesthood, nor manifestations.

**3:5** And after these things the sons of Israel will return and seek the Lord their God and David their king, and they will be amazed at the Lord and at His good things in the last days.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek reads "who loves evil things" (ἀγαπῶσαν πονηρὰ) where the Hebrew MT reads "loved by a companion/lover," and uses "God" (ὁ θεός) where the MT uses the divine name.
- **3:2** — The Greek uses the verb "hired" (ἐμισθωσάμην) and reads "a jar of wine" (νέβελ οἴνου, transliterating Hebrew nebel, a wine-jar or skin-bottle) where the Hebrew MT reads "a lethech [half-homer] of barley."
- **3:4** — Where the Hebrew MT lists sacred pillar, ephod, and teraphim, the Greek reads "altar" (θυσιαστηρίου), "priesthood" (ἱερατίας), and "manifestations" (δήλων, the standard Septuagint term for the priestly Urim).
