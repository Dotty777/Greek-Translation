## Text

**2:1** And the number of the sons of Israel was like the sand of the sea, which will not be measured out nor counted; and it will be in the place where it was said to them, "You are not My people," they also will be called sons of the living God.

**2:2** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel will be gathered together, and they will establish for themselves one rule, and they will go up out of the land, because great is the day of Jezreel.

**2:3** Say to your brother, "My People," and to your sister, "Shown Mercy."

**2:4** "Contend with your mother, contend, because she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. And I will remove her prostitution from My presence and her adultery from between her breasts,

**2:5** so that I may strip her naked and restore her just as on the day of her birth; and I will make her a wilderness and set her like a waterless land, and I will kill her with thirst;

**2:6** and I will certainly not show mercy to her children, because they are children of prostitution.

**2:7** For their mother committed prostitution, she who bore them acted shamefully, because she said, 'I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water and my garments and my linens, my oil and all that is due to me.'

**2:8** Therefore, look, I am hedging up her way with thorns, and I will wall up the ways, and she will certainly not find her path;

**2:9** and she will pursue her lovers and will certainly not overtake them, and she will seek them and will certainly not find them. And she will say, 'I will go and return to my former husband, because it was better for me then than now.'

**2:10** And she did not know that I gave her the grain and the wine and the oil, and I multiplied silver for her; yet she made things of silver and gold for Baal.

**2:11** Therefore, I will return and take back My grain in its season and My wine in its appointed time, and I will take away My garments and My linens so as not to cover her indecency;

**2:12** and now I will uncover her uncleanness in the sight of her lovers, and no one will by any means rescue her out of My hand;

**2:13** and I will turn away all her joys, her festivals and her new moons and her Sabbaths and all her festive assemblies;

**2:14** and I will lay waste her vine and her fig trees, of which she said, 'These are my wages that my lovers gave me,' and I will make them a testimony, and the wild beasts of the field and the birds of the sky and the creeping things of the earth will devour them;

**2:15** and I will punish her for the days of the Baals in which she offered sacrifices to them, and put on her earrings and her necklaces, and went after her lovers, but she forgot Me," says the Lord.

**2:16** "Therefore, look, I am leading her astray and will set her like a wilderness, and I will speak to her heart,

**2:17** and I will give her possessions to her from there, and the valley of Achor to open up her understanding; and she will be humbled there according to the days of her infancy and according to the days of her going up out of the land of Egypt.

**2:18** And it will be in that day," says the Lord, "that she will call Me 'My Husband,' and she will no longer call Me 'Baals';

**2:19** and I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and their names will certainly no longer be remembered.

**2:20** And I will make a covenant for them in that day with the wild beasts of the field and with the birds of the sky and the creeping things of the earth; and I will shatter bow and sword and war from the earth, and I will cause you to dwell in hope.

**2:21** And I will betroth you to Myself forever; and I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and in justice and in mercy and in compassions,

**2:22** and I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness, and you will come to know the Lord.

**2:23** And it will be in that day," says the Lord, "that I will listen to the sky, and it will listen to the earth,

**2:24** and the earth will listen to the grain and the wine and the oil, and they will listen to Jezreel.

**2:25** And I will sow her for Myself on the earth, and I will love Not Loved, and I will say to Not My People, 'You are My people,' and he will say, 'You are the Lord my God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek begins with the past-tense imperfect ἦν ("was") before shifting to future verbs, and adds "they also" (καὶ αὐτοὶ) in the closing promise. Verses 2:1-2:25 in the Septuagint correspond to 1:10-2:23 in the Hebrew MT.
- **2:4** — The Greek reads first-person "I will remove ... from My presence" (ἐξαρῶ ... ἐκ προσώπου μου) and continues in 2:5 with a positive purpose clause "so that I may strip her naked" (ὅπως ἂν ἐκδύσω), whereas the Hebrew MT urges her to remove prostitution from her own face "lest" God strip her.
- **2:14** — The Greek reads "a testimony" (εἰς μαρτύριον) where the Hebrew MT has "a forest," and expands the devourers to include "the birds of the sky and the creeping things of the earth," matching 2:20.
- **2:17** — The Greek contains several distinct readings compared to the Hebrew MT: "her possessions" (MT "her vineyards"), "to open up her understanding" (MT "for a door of hope"), "she will be humbled" (MT "she will answer/sing"), and "her infancy" (MT "her youth").
- **2:18** — The Greek reads plural Βααλείμ ('Baals,' transliterated 'Baalim') rather than Hebrew singular 'my Baal,' matching 'the Baals' in 2:15, 2:19, and 11:2.
- **2:25** — Here the Greek shifts from "mercy" (ἐλεέω in 1:6-2:6) to "love" (ἀγαπήσω τὴν Οὐκ ἠγαπημένην, "I will love Not Loved," echoed in Rom 9:25), and expands the closing reply to the full confession "You are the Lord my God."
