## Text

**6:1** "Let us go and return to the Lord our God, for He Himself has seized and will heal us; He will strike and will bandage us;

**6:2** He will make us well after two days; on the third day we will also rise up,

**6:3** and we will live before Him, and we will know. We will pursue knowing the Lord; like a ready dawn we will find Him, and He will come to us like early and late rain to the earth."

**6:4** What shall I do to you, Ephraim? What shall I do to you, Judah? For your mercy is like a morning cloud and like early dew that goes away.

**6:5** Therefore I cut down your prophets; I killed them by the word of My mouth, and My judgment will go forth as light.

**6:6** Because I desire mercy rather than sacrifice, and knowledge of God rather than whole burnt offerings.

**6:7** But they are like a man who transgresses a covenant; there Gilead despised Me, a city

**6:8** working worthless things, troubling water,

**6:9** and your strength is that of a bandit; priests hid the way, they murdered Shechem, because they committed lawlessness

**6:10** in the house of Israel. I saw horrifying things there, the prostitution of Ephraim; Israel and Judah were defiled. Begin to harvest for yourself

**6:11** when I bring back the captivity of My people,

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Septuagint reads "He will make us well" (hygiasei) and intransitive "we will also rise up" (anastesometha), and places "and we will live before Him" at the opening of verse 3 rather than the end of verse 2.
- **6:3** — The Septuagint reads "like a ready dawn we will find Him" (heuresomen auton), taking the Hebrew form from the verb "to find" rather than "His going forth."
- **6:5** — In the Septuagint, "your prophets" is the direct object of "I cut down" (apetherisa, literally "mowed down"), and the final clause reads "My judgment will go forth as light."
- **6:10** — The Septuagint verse divisions differ from the Hebrew in verses 7-11: verse 7 begins the clause about Gilead (continued in verse 8 with "troubling water"), and verse 10 joins "and Judah" to "Israel was defiled" before beginning the command "Begin to harvest for yourself," which continues into verse 11.
