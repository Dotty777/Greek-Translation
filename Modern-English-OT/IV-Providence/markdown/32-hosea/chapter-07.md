## Text

**7:1** When I heal Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim and the wickedness of Samaria will be revealed, because they practiced falsehoods; and a thief will come in to him, a robber plundering on his road,

**7:2** so that they may sing together like those singing in their heart. I have remembered all their wicked deeds; now their designs have surrounded them; they have come before My face.

**7:3** With their wicked deeds they gladdened kings, and with their lies, rulers.

**7:4** All of them are committing adultery, like an oven burning for baking with scorching heat from the flame, from the kneading of dough until it is leavened.

**7:5** The days of your kings—the rulers began to become enraged from wine; he stretched out his hand with pestilent men.

**7:6** Because their hearts were kindled like an oven, while they were crashing down, all night Ephraim was filled with sleep; morning came; it was kindled like a brightness of fire.

**7:7** All of them grew hot like an oven, and they devoured their judges; all their kings fell; there was no one among them calling upon Me.

**7:8** Ephraim was mingling himself among his peoples; Ephraim became a hearth cake not turned over.

**7:9** Foreigners devoured his strength, yet he did not know; and gray hairs sprouted on him, and he did not know.

**7:10** And the arrogance of Israel will be humbled to his face, yet they did not return to the Lord their God, nor did they seek Him out in all these things.

**7:11** And Ephraim was like a senseless dove, having no heart; he was calling upon Egypt, and they went to the Assyrians.

**7:12** As they go, I will cast My net over them; like the birds of the sky I will bring them down; I will discipline them at the report of their affliction.

**7:13** Woe to them, because they leaped away from Me; they are wretched, because they acted impiously against Me. I redeemed them, yet they spoke falsehoods against Me.

**7:14** And their hearts did not cry out to Me, rather they were wailing on their beds; for grain and wine they were cutting themselves.

**7:15** They were disciplined by Me, and I strengthened their arms, yet against Me they devised evil.

**7:16** They turned away to nothing; they became like a stretched bow. Their rulers will fall by the sword because of the lack of discipline of their tongue; this is their mockery in the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Greek reads "so that they may sing together like those singing in their heart," diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "they do not consider in their heart."
- **7:4** — The Septuagint translates the Hebrew "heated by a baker" as "burning for baking with scorching heat," omitting any explicit mention of a baker ceasing to stir the fire.
- **7:6** — The Greek verb rendered "crashing down" (katarasso) is difficult here, and the Septuagint reads "Ephraim was filled with sleep" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "their baker sleeps."
- **7:16** — The Greek reads "a stretched bow" (entetamenon) and "lack of discipline" (apaideusian), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a treacherous/slack bow" and "insolence/rage."
