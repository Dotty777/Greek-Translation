## Text

**4:1** Hear the word of the Lord, sons of Israel, for the Lord has a judgment against those who dwell in the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God upon the land.

**4:2** Cursing and falsehood and murder and theft and adultery have been poured out upon the land, and they mingle blood upon blood.

**4:3** Therefore the land will mourn, and it will be diminished together with all who dwell in it, with the wild beasts of the field, and with the creeping things of the land, and with the birds of the sky, and the fish of the sea will vanish,

**4:4** so that no one may plead a case, nor may anyone rebuke. But My people are like a priest spoken against,

**4:5** and he will grow weak by day, and a prophet will grow weak with you; I have likened your mother to night;

**4:6** My people have become like one who has no knowledge. Because you rejected knowledge, I also will reject you from serving as priest to Me; and you forgot the law of your God, and I also will forget your children.

**4:7** According to their multitude, so they sinned against Me; I will turn their glory into dishonor.

**4:8** They will eat the sins of My people, and in their injustices they will take their souls.

**4:9** And it will be, just as the people, so also the priest; and I will avenge his ways upon him, and I will repay his plans to him.

**4:10** And they will eat and will by no means be filled; they prostituted themselves and will by no means prosper, because they abandoned the Lord to keep

**4:11** prostitution. And the heart of My people received wine and strong drink.

**4:12** They inquired by omens, and by his staffs they reported to him; by a spirit of prostitution they were led astray, and they prostituted themselves away from their God.

**4:13** On the tops of the mountains they sacrificed, and on the hills they made offerings under an oak and a white poplar and a shading tree, because shelter is good. Therefore your daughters will prostitute themselves, and your daughters-in-law will commit adultery.

**4:14** And I will by no means call your daughters to account when they prostitute themselves, nor your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery; for they themselves mingled with the prostitutes, and they made offerings with the initiated ones, and the people who understand were intertwined with a prostitute.

**4:15** But you, Israel, do not be ignorant; and Judah, do not go into Gilgal, and do not go up to the house of On, and do not swear by the living Lord.

**4:16** Because like a frenzied heifer, Israel has become frenzied; now the Lord will pasture them like a lamb in a wide place.

**4:17** A partner of idols, Ephraim has set stumbling blocks for himself,

**4:18** he chose Canaanites. Prostituting themselves, they utterly prostituted themselves; they loved dishonor from her arrogance.

**4:19** You are a whirling wind in her wings, and they will be put to shame because of their altars.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Here and in verse 6, the Septuagint uses the verb homoioo ("liken" / "become like"), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew root as "resemble" rather than "destroy" or "cut off."
- **4:10** — In the Septuagint, "to keep" at the end of verse 10 governs "prostitution" at the start of verse 11, and the remainder of verse 11 makes "the heart of My people" the subject that received wine and strong drink.
- **4:14** — The Greek tetelesmenon ("the initiated ones") refers to cultic initiates (where the Hebrew has cult prostitutes), and the final clause reads positively "the people who understand" without the negative found in the Hebrew text.
- **4:15** — The Septuagint renders the place name as "the house of On" (oikon On, echoing the Egyptian city On) rather than Hebrew "Beth-aven" (so also in 5:8).
- **4:18** — The Septuagint reads "he chose Canaanites" (and in verse 17 "set stumbling blocks for himself"), diverging sharply from the Hebrew text ("leave him alone; their drink is gone").
