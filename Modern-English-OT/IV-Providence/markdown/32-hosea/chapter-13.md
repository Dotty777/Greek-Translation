## Text

**13:1** According to the word of Ephraim, he himself received ordinances in Israel, and he established them for Baal and died.

**13:2** And they continued to sin, and they made for themselves a cast image from their silver according to the likeness of idols, works of craftsmen completed for them; they themselves say, "Sacrifice human beings, for calves have come to an end."

**13:3** Therefore they will be like a morning cloud and like early dew passing away, like chaff blown away from a threshing floor, and like vapor from tears.

**13:4** Yet I am the Lord your God, establishing heaven and creating earth, whose hands created all the host of heaven, and I did not show them to you so that you would walk after them; and I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and you will know no god besides Me, and there is no one who saves apart from Me.

**13:5** I shepherded you in the wilderness, in an uninhabited land,

**13:6** according to their pastures; and they were filled to the full, and their hearts were lifted up; because of this they forgot Me.

**13:7** And I will be to them like a panther and like a leopard along the way of the Assyrians;

**13:8** I will meet them like a bear in distress, and I will tear open the enclosure of their heart, and lion cubs of the forest will devour them there; wild beasts of the field will tear them apart.

**13:9** In your destruction, Israel, who will help?

**13:10** Where is this king of yours? And let him deliver you in all your cities; let him judge you, the one about whom you said, "Give me a king and a ruler."

**13:11** And I gave you a king in My anger, and I held back in My wrath.

**13:12** A conspiracy of Ephraim's injustice; his sin is hidden.

**13:13** Labor pains like those of a woman giving birth will come upon him. This is your wise son, because he will by no means endure in the crushing of children.

**13:14** Out of the hand of Hades I will rescue and out of death I will redeem them. Where is your penalty, Death? Where is your sting, Hades? Comfort is hidden from My eyes,

**13:15** because this one will divide between brothers. The Lord will bring a scorching wind out of the wilderness upon him, and He will dry up his veins; He will lay waste his springs; He Himself will completely dry up his land and all his desirable vessels.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Greek reads "Sacrifice human beings, for calves have come to an end," diverging sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("those who sacrifice men kiss calves").
- **13:3** — The Greek literally reads "vapor from tears" (apo dakryon), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "smoke from a lattice" or "chimney."
- **13:4** — The Greek includes an extended creation clause concerning the heavens and their host and supplies the verb "I brought you up," neither of which appears in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:13** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("an unwise son" at the opening of the womb), the Greek reads "your wise son" (often understood ironically) and "in the crushing of children."
- **13:14** — The Greek asks "Where is your penalty, Death?" (dike), differing from both the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("your plagues") and Paul's quotation in 1 Corinthians 15:55 ("your victory").
