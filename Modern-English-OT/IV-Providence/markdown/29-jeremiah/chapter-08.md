## Text

**8:1** "At that time," says the Lord, "they will bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of its rulers, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of prophets, and the bones of those who dwell in Jerusalem out of their tombs,

**8:2** and they will dry them out before the sun and the moon, and before all the stars, and before all the host of heaven, which they loved, and which they served, and after which they walked, and to which they clung, and which they worshiped; they will not be mourned and they will not be buried, and they will be an example upon the face of the earth,

**8:3** because they chose death rather than life, and to all the remaining ones who are left from that generation, in every place wherever I drive them out."

**8:4** For thus says the Lord: "Does the one who falls not get up? Or does the one who turns away not turn back?

**8:5** Why has this people of Mine turned away with a shameless turning away, and been held fast in their choice, and refused to turn back?

**8:6** Give ear now and listen; they will not speak thus. There is no one repenting of his wickedness, saying, 'What have I done?' The runner has ceased from his course, like a horse drenched in sweat in its neighing.

**8:7** Even the stork in the sky knows its season; turtledove and swallow of the field, sparrows keep the seasons of their arrivals; but My people do not know the judgments of the Lord.

**8:8** How will you say, 'We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us'? A false reed pen has proved in vain for scribes.

**8:9** The wise have been put to shame, and dismayed, and captured, because they have rejected the law of the Lord; what wisdom is in them?

**8:10** Therefore I will give their wives to others, and their fields to heirs,

**8:13** and they will gather their produce," says the Lord. "There are no grapes on the vines, and there are no figs on the fig trees, and the leaves have fallen off."

**8:14** "Why are we sitting here? Gather together and let us enter the fortified cities and be cast away, for God has cast us away and given us water of gall to drink, because we have sinned before Him.

**8:15** We were gathered for peace, and there were no good things; for a time of healing, and look, alarm."

**8:16** From Dan we will hear the sound of the swiftness of his horses; at the sound of the neighing of the riding of his horses, the whole land shook; and he will come and devour the land and its fullness, a city and those who dwell in it.

**8:17** "For look, I am sending out among you deadly serpents, which cannot be charmed, and they will bite you

**8:18** incurably with pain, while your heart is perplexed."

**8:19** Look, the sound of the cry of the daughter of my people from a land far away: "Is the Lord not in Zion? Or is a king not there?" "Why have they provoked Me to anger with their carved images and with foreign vanities?"

**8:20** "Summer has passed, harvest has gone by, and we have not been saved."

**8:21** Over the shattering of the daughter of my people I have been darkened; in perplexity, birth pangs like those of a woman giving birth have overpowered me.

**8:22** Is there no resin in Gilead, or is there no physician there? Why has healing for the daughter of my people not come up?

**8:23** Who will give water to my head and a spring of tears to my eyes, so that I may weep for this people of mine day and night, the wounded of the daughter of my people?

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek reads "dry them out," "will not be mourned," and "an example" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "spread them out," "will not be gathered," and "like dung."
- **8:6** — The Greek opens with plural imperatives and closes with "the runner has ceased from his course, like a horse drenched in sweat in its neighing," differing markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **8:7** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew term for stork as asida, and lists "swallow of the field, sparrows" where the Hebrew lists swallow and crane.
- **8:10** — Verses 8:10b-12 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text (parallel to 6:12-15) are absent from the Greek text, which continues directly from 8:10 into 8:13.
- **8:18** — In the Greek text, 8:18 completes the Lord's sentence begun in 8:17 ("they will bite you incurably with pain, while your heart is perplexed") rather than beginning the prophet's lament as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
