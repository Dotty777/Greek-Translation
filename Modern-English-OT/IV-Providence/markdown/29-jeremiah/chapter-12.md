## Text

**12:1** You are righteous, Lord, that I will make a defense to You; yet I will speak judgments to You. Why is it that the way of the ungodly prospers? Have all those who deal treacherously with acts of treachery thrived?

**12:2** You planted them, and they took root; they bore children and produced fruit. You are near to their mouth, and far from their kidneys.

**12:3** And You, Lord, know me; You have tested my heart before You. Purify them for the day of their slaughter.

**12:4** How long will the land mourn, and all the grass of the field wither, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it? Livestock and birds have vanished, because they said, "God will not see our ways."

**12:5** Your feet run and exhaust you; how will you prepare yourself for horses? And in a land of peace you are not confident; how will you do in the roaring of the Jordan?

**12:6** For even your brothers and the house of your father, even they have dealt treacherously with you; and they themselves cried out, they gathered together from behind you. Do not trust in them, because they speak good things to you.

**12:7** I have forsaken My house; I have abandoned My inheritance; I have given My beloved soul into the hands of her enemies.

**12:8** My inheritance became to Me like a lion in a forest; it gave its voice against Me; therefore I hated it.

**12:9** Is My inheritance a hyena's cave to Me, or a cave around it? Go, gather all the wild beasts of the field, and let them come to eat it.

**12:10** Many shepherds have ruined My vineyard; they have defiled My portion; they have turned My desirable portion into an untrodden wilderness;

**12:11** it was made a desolation of destruction; because of Me the whole land has been desolated with desolation, because there is no man taking it to heart.

**12:12** Upon every pass in the wilderness destroyers have come, because a sword of the Lord will devour from the end of the land; there is no peace for any flesh.

**12:13** Sow wheat, and reap thorns; their lots will not profit them. Be ashamed because of your boasting, because of reproach before the Lord.

**12:14** For thus says the Lord concerning all the wicked neighbors who touch My inheritance which I apportioned to My people Israel: "Look, I am tearing them away from their land, and I will cast Judah out from their midst.

**12:15** And it will happen, after I have cast them out, I will turn back and have mercy on them, and I will settle them, each one into his inheritance and each one into his land.

**12:16** And it will happen, if they truly learn the way of My people to swear by My name, 'The Lord lives,' just as they taught My people to swear by Baal, then it will be built up in the midst of My people;

**12:17** but if they do not turn back, then I will remove that nation with removal and destruction."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Greek reads "they bore children" (ἐτεκνοποιήσαντο) rather than the Hebrew MT "they grow/go on"; "kidneys" (νεφρῶν) is a biblical idiom for the innermost mind and affections.
- **12:3** — Greek ἅγνισον ("purify" or "consecrate") omits the Hebrew MT's preceding clause "drag them out like sheep for slaughter" while rendering the verb for setting apart.
- **12:5** — Greek reads "Your feet run" (σοῦ οἱ πόδες τρέχουσιν) and "you are not confident" (οὐ πέποιθας), differing from Hebrew MT ("you have run with footmen" and "you feel safe"); "roaring" translates φρύαγμα (lit. snorting, insolent roaring).
- **12:9** — Greek reads "a hyena's cave ... or a cave around it" (σπήλαιον ὑαίνης ... ἢ σπήλαιον κύκλῳ αὐτῆς), diverging notably from the Hebrew MT ("a speckled bird of prey").
- **12:12** — Greek reads only "from the end of the land" (ἀπ᾽ ἄκρου τῆς γῆς), omitting the Hebrew MT's continuation "even to the other end of the land."
