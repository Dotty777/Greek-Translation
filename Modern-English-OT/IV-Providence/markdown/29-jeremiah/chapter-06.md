## Text

**6:1** Be strong, sons of Benjamin, out of the midst of Jerusalem, and in Tekoa give a signal with a trumpet, and over Beth-hakkerem raise a signal; for evils have peered out from the north, and great destruction is taking place,

**6:2** and your height will be taken away, daughter of Zion.

**6:3** To her shepherds and their flocks will come, and they will pitch tents against her all around, and they will pasture, each one with his hand.

**6:4** "Prepare yourselves against her for war; arise, and let us go up against her at midday! Woe to us, for the day has declined, for the shadows of the day are failing!

**6:5** Arise, and let us go up against her by night, and let us destroy her foundations."

**6:6** For thus says the Lord: "Cut down her trees; pour out a force against Jerusalem! O false city, all oppression is in her!

**6:7** As a cistern cools water, so her wickedness cools; ungodliness and misery will be heard in her, before her face continually.

**6:8** With pain and a scourge you will be disciplined, Jerusalem, so that My soul may not depart from you, so that I may not make you an untrodden land that will not be inhabited."

**6:9** For thus says the Lord: "Glean, glean like a vine the remnants of Israel; turn back like the grape-gatherer to his basket."

**6:10** To whom shall I speak and bear witness, that he will hear? Look, their ears are uncircumcised, and they will not be able to hear; look, the word of the Lord has become a reproach to them; they will certainly not desire it.

**6:11** "And I filled up My wrath and held back, and I did not consume them; I will pour it out upon infants outside, upon the assembly of young men together; for husband and wife will be captured, an elder along with one full of days;

**6:12** and their houses will be turned over to others, fields and their wives together, for I will stretch out My hand against those who inhabit this land," says the Lord.

**6:13** "For from the least of them even to the greatest, all have accomplished lawless deeds; from priest to false prophet, all have practiced false things.

**6:14** And they were healing the fracture of My people, treating it with contempt and saying, 'Peace, peace!' And where is peace?

**6:15** They were put to shame because they failed; yet not even as those being shamed were they ashamed, and they did not know their dishonor. Therefore they will fall in their falling, and at the time of visitation they will perish," said the Lord.

**6:16** Thus says the Lord: "Stand on the roads and look, and ask for the eternal paths of the Lord, and see which is the good way, and walk in it, and you will find purification for your souls. And they said, 'We will not go.'

**6:17** I have appointed watchmen over you; listen to the sound of the trumpet. And they said, 'We will not listen.'

**6:18** Therefore the nations heard, and those who shepherd their flocks.

**6:19** Hear, earth! Look, I am bringing evils upon this people, the fruit of their turning away, because they did not pay attention to My words, and they rejected My law.

**6:20** Why do you bring Me frankincense from Sheba and cinnamon from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices did not please Me."

**6:21** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Look, I am putting weakness upon this people, and fathers and sons together will grow weak; a neighbor and his companion will perish."

**6:22** Thus says the Lord: "Look, a people is coming from the north, and nations will be stirred up from the farthest part of the earth;

**6:23** they will grasp bow and spear; it is fierce and will not show mercy; its sound is like a surging sea; upon horses and chariots it will draw up in array like fire for war against you, daughter of Zion."

**6:24** "We have heard the report of them; our hands have grown limp; distress has seized us, birth pains like those of a woman giving birth."

**6:25** Do not go out into the field, and do not walk on the roads, for the sword of the enemies dwells all around.

**6:26** Daughter of my people, wrap yourself in sackcloth, sprinkle yourself in ashes; make for yourself mourning for a beloved one, a piteous lamentation, for suddenly misery will come upon you.

**6:27** "I have appointed you as a tester among tested peoples, and you will know Me when I test their way.

**6:28** All are disobedient, walking crookedly; bronze and iron, all of them are corrupted.

**6:29** The bellows has failed from the fire, the lead has failed; in vain the silversmith works silver; their wickedness has not melted away.

**6:30** Call them rejected silver, for the Lord has rejected them."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek renders the opening imperative as "Be strong" (ἐνισχύσατε) rather than "Flee for safety" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; "Beth-hakkerem" translates Greek Βαιθθαχαρμά.
- **6:11** — In the Greek, the Lord Himself speaks throughout 6:11 ("I filled up My wrath... I will pour it out"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the prophet speaking of being full of the Lord's wrath before a command to pour it out.
- **6:16** — The Greek promises "purification" (ἁγνισμόν) for their souls, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text promises "rest."
- **6:18** — The Greek renders this as a past statement ("Therefore the nations heard, and those who shepherd their flocks") rather than an imperative call to the nations and congregation as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **6:27** — The Greek reads "among tested peoples, and you will know Me when I test their way," repeating the root for testing (δοκιμ-) and making the Lord the one who tests their way, unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
