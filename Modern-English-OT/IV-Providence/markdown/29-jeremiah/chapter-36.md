## Text

**36:1** And these are the words of the scroll that Jeremiah sent from Jerusalem to the elders of the exile, and to the priests, and to the false prophets—a letter to Babylon for the exiles—and to all the people,

**36:2** after Jeconiah the king, and the queen, and the eunuchs, and every free man and prisoner and craftsman had gone out from Jerusalem,

**36:3** by the hand of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to the king of Babylon in Babylon, saying,

**36:4** "Thus says the Lord God of Israel concerning the exiles whom I sent into exile from Jerusalem:

**36:5** Build houses and live in them, and plant orchards and eat their fruits,

**36:6** and take wives and have sons and daughters, and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, and multiply, and do not be diminished,

**36:7** and seek the peace of the land where I sent you into exile, and pray for them to the Lord, for in its peace is your peace.

**36:8** For thus says the Lord: Do not let the false prophets who are among you persuade you, and do not let your diviners persuade you, and do not listen to your dreams that you yourselves dream;

**36:9** for they prophesy unjust things to you in My name, and I did not send them.

**36:10** For thus says the Lord: When seventy years are about to be fulfilled for Babylon, I will visit you and establish My words upon you, to bring your people back to this place,

**36:11** and I will plan for you a plan of peace and not evil things, to give you these things.

**36:12** And pray to Me, and I will listen to you;

**36:13** and seek Me out, and you will find Me; for you will seek Me with all your heart,

**36:14** and I will appear to you.

**36:15** Because you said, 'The Lord has appointed prophets for us in Babylon,'

**36:21** thus says the Lord concerning Ahab and concerning Zedekiah: Look, I am giving them into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will strike them down before your eyes.

**36:22** And they will take a curse from them among all the exiles of Judah in Babylon, saying, 'May the Lord do to you as He did to Zedekiah and as to Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in fire,'

**36:23** because of the lawlessness that they committed in Israel, and they were committing adultery with the wives of their fellow citizens, and uttered a word in My name that I did not command them; and I am a witness, declares the Lord."

**36:24** And to Shemaiah the Elamite you shall say,

**36:25** "I did not send you in My name." And to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, the priest, say,

**36:26** "The Lord has appointed you as priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to be an overseer in the house of the Lord over every man who prophesies and every man who is mad, and you shall put him into the prison and into the dungeon.

**36:27** And now, why did you join in reviling Jeremiah from Anathoth, who prophesied to you?

**36:28** Did he not for this reason send to you in Babylon, saying, 'It is far off; build houses and live in them, and plant gardens, and you will eat their fruit'?"

**36:29** And Zephaniah read the scroll in the ears of Jeremiah.

**36:30** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,

**36:31** "Send to the exiles, saying, 'Thus says the Lord concerning Shemaiah the Elamite: Because Shemaiah prophesied to you, and I did not send him, and he made you trust in unjust things,

**36:32** therefore thus says the Lord: Look, I will punish Shemaiah and his family, and there will not be a man of them in your midst to see the good things that I will do for you; they will not see them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — The Greek explicitly reads "the false prophets" (MT: "the prophets") and adds "a letter to Babylon for the exiles." Throughout this chapter, LXX ch. 36 corresponds to MT ch. 29, with several substantial differences.
- **36:11** — Instead of the MT's "For I know the plans I have for you ... to give you a future and a hope," the Greek reads "and I will plan for you a plan of peace and not evil things, to give you these things." The Greek also shortens v. 14 and omits vv. 16-20 entirely, connecting v. 15 directly into v. 21.
- **36:24** — The Greek reads "the Elamite" (ton Ailameiten), a distinct gentilic name where the Hebrew MT has "the Nehelamite" (likewise in 36:31).
- **36:25** — In vv. 25-28 the Greek diverges sharply from the MT: instead of reporting that Shemaiah sent letters in his own name, the Lord tells Jeremiah to say to Shemaiah "I did not send you in My name" and then directly commands Jeremiah to address Zephaniah (with v. 27 lacking "not" and v. 28 reading "to you" rather than "to us").
