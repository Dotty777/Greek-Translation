## Text

**37:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,

**37:2** "Thus says the Lord God of Israel, saying, 'Write all the words that I have uttered to you on a scroll.

**37:3** For look, days are coming, declares the Lord, and I will turn back the exile of My people Israel and Judah, says the Lord, and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they will possess it.'"

**37:4** And these are the words that the Lord spoke concerning Israel and Judah.

**37:5** Thus says the Lord: "You will hear a sound of fear; fear, and there is no peace.

**37:6** Ask and see whether a male has given birth, and concerning fear in which they will hold loins and deliverance; because I have seen every man, and his hands are on his loins; faces were turned; they became jaundice.

**37:7** For great is that day, and there is none like it; and it is a distressing time for Jacob,

**37:8** and from this he will be saved. In that day, says the Lord, I will shatter the yoke from their neck, and I will tear apart their bonds, and they themselves will not serve foreigners,

**37:9** and they will serve the Lord their God, and I will raise up David their king for them."

**37:12** Thus says the Lord: "I have raised up a fracture; painful is your wound,

**37:13** there is no one judging your case; you were treated for pain; there is no benefit for you.

**37:14** All your friends have forgotten you; they will by no means inquire; for I struck you with the blow of an enemy, a severe discipline; because of all your injustice, your sins have multiplied.

**37:16** Therefore all who eat you will be eaten, and all your enemies will eat all their flesh; because of the multitude of your injustices your sins were multiplied; they did these things to you; and those who plunder you will become plunder, and all who pillage you I will give over to pillage.

**37:17** For I will bring up your healing; from a painful wound I will heal you, declares the Lord; because you were called Scattered: 'She is your prey, because there is no one seeking her.'"

**37:18** Thus says the Lord: "Look, I will turn back the exile of Jacob, and I will have mercy on his captivity; and a city will be built upon its height, and the people will sit according to its judgment.

**37:19** And those who sing will go out from them, a sound of those celebrating; and I will increase them, and they will by no means be diminished.

**37:20** And their sons will enter as before, and their testimonies will be set upright before My face; and I will punish those who afflict them,

**37:21** and those stronger than he will be over them, and his ruler will go out from him; and I will gather them, and they will return to Me; for who is this who has given his heart to return to Me, declares the Lord?"

**37:23** For the furious wrath of the Lord has gone out; a whirling wrath has gone out; it will come upon the ungodly.

**37:24** The wrath of the Lord's fury will by no means turn back until He carries out, and until He establishes, the undertaking of His heart; at the end of the days you will know these things.

## Translator Notes

- **37:6** — The Greek includes the difficult clause "and concerning fear in which they will hold loins and deliverance" (absent from MT), reads "because" (dioti) rather than "why," and renders the pallor of the faces literally as "jaundice" (ikteron).
- **37:12** — The Greek reads "I have raised up a fracture" (Anestesa syntrimma), where the Hebrew MT has "Your hurt is incurable." Verses 10-11, 15, and 22 are absent from the Greek text of this chapter (MT ch. 30).
- **37:16** — Instead of the MT's "every one of them shall go into captivity," the Greek reads "all your enemies will eat all their flesh," and includes the clause on multiplied sins (with plural "they did these things to you") within v. 16 rather than v. 15.
- **37:17** — The Greek reads "She is your prey" (thereuma hymon estin), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants where the MT has "It is Zion."
- **37:18** — The Greek reads "his captivity" (MT: "his dwellings") and "the people" (ho laos; MT: "the palace"), and in v. 20 reads "their testimonies" (MT: "his congregation").
