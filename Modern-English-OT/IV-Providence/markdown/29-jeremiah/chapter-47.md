## Text

**47:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan the chief cook from Daman had sent him away, when he took him in shackles among the exiles of Judah who had been led to Babylon.

**47:2** And the chief cook took him and said to him, "The Lord your God spoke these evils against this place,

**47:3** and the Lord has done it, because you sinned against Him and did not listen to His voice.

**47:4** Look, I have released you from the shackles that are on your hands. If it is good in your sight to come with me to Babylon, then I will set my eyes upon you;

**47:5** but if not, go away, return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed in the land of Judah, and live with him among the people in the land of Judah; to all the places that are good in your eyes to go, go." And the chief cook gave him gifts and sent him away.

**47:6** And he came to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and remained among the people who were left in the land.

**47:7** And all the commanders of the army that was in the field, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah in the land, and they had entrusted to him men and their wives whom he had not exiled to Babylon.

**47:8** And there came to Gedaliah at Mizpah Ishmael son of Nethaniah, and Johanan son of Kareah, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Jophe the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, they and their men.

**47:9** And Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, saying, "Do not be afraid of the servants of the Chaldeans; dwell in the land and work for the king of Babylon, and it will be better for you.

**47:10** And look, I am staying before you at Mizpah to stand before the Chaldeans who may come to you; and as for you, gather wine and summer fruit and oil, and put them into your vessels, and live in the cities that you have occupied."

**47:11** And all the Judeans who were in Moab and among the sons of Ammon and those in Idumea and those in all the land heard that the king of Babylon had given a remnant to Judah, and that he had appointed over them Gedaliah son of Ahikam.

**47:12** And they came to Gedaliah in the land of Judah, to Mizpah, and they gathered wine and very much summer fruit and oil.

**47:13** And Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the army who were in the fields came to Gedaliah at Mizpah

**47:14** and said to him, "Do you indeed know that Belisa king of the sons of Ammon has sent Ishmael to you to strike your life?" And Gedaliah did not believe them.

**47:15** And Johanan said to Gedaliah secretly in Mizpah, "Let me go now and strike down Ishmael, and let no one know, so that he may not strike your life, and all Judah who have been gathered to you be scattered, and the rest of Judah perish."

**47:16** And Gedaliah said to Johanan, "Do not do this thing, for you are speaking lies concerning Ishmael."

## Translator Notes

- **47:1** — Greek archimageiros ("chief cook") is the Septuagint's literal rendering of the Hebrew title for the captain of the imperial guard. The Greek also reads "from Daman" where the Hebrew text has "from Ramah."
- **47:8** — The Greek names Jophe (corresponding to Hebrew Ephai) and lists only Johanan son of Kareah (omitting Jonathan).
- **47:9** — The Greek reads "the servants of the Chaldeans," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants that the Masoretic Text vocalizes as "serving the Chaldeans."
- **47:14** — Belisa is the Septuagint's form of the Ammonite king's name, corresponding to Hebrew Baalis.
