## Text

**27:1** The word of the Lord which He spoke against Babylon.

**27:2** Announce among the nations and make it heard, and do not conceal it; say, "Babylon has been captured; Bel, the fearless one, has been put to shame; the delicate one, Merodach, has been delivered up."

**27:3** For a nation from the north has come up against her; this one will make her land a desolation, and there will be no one dwelling in it, from man even to beast.

**27:4** In those days and at that time, the sons of Israel will come, they and the sons of Judah together; walking and weeping they will go, seeking the Lord their God.

**27:5** They will ask the way to Zion, for here they will set their face, and they will come and take refuge with the Lord God; for an everlasting covenant will not be forgotten.

**27:6** My people have become lost sheep; their shepherds drove them out, upon the mountains they led them astray; from mountain to hill they went; they forgot their resting place.

**27:7** All who found them consumed them; their enemies said, "Let us not spare them, because they sinned against the Lord; a pasture of righteousness belongs to the One who gathered their fathers."

**27:8** Estrange yourselves from the midst of Babylon and from the land of the Chaldeans and go out, and become like dragons before sheep.

**27:9** For look, I am raising up against Babylon assemblies of nations from the land of the north, and they will draw up in battle array against her; from there she will be captured; like the dart of a skilled warrior, it will not return empty.

**27:10** And Chaldea will become plunder; all who plunder her will be filled.

**27:11** Because you rejoiced and boasted while pillaging My inheritance, because you leaped like calves in the grass and butted like bulls.

**27:12** Your mother has been put to shame greatly, a mother for good things, the last of the nations, a wilderness,

**27:13** because of the wrath of the Lord she will not be inhabited; and all of her will become a desolation, and everyone passing through Babylon will look grim, and they will hiss over all her wound.

**27:14** Draw up in battle array against Babylon all around, all you who draw a bow; shoot at her, do not spare your arrows,

**27:15** and overpower her; her hands have been enfeebled, her battlements have fallen, and her wall has been demolished; for it is vengeance from God; take vengeance on her, just as she has done, do to her.

**27:16** Utterly destroy seed from Babylon, the one holding a sickle in the time of harvest; from the face of the Greek sword each one will turn back to his people, and each one will flee to his land.

**27:17** Israel is a straying sheep; lions drove him out; the first one devoured him, the king of Assyria, and afterward this one his bones, the king of Babylon.

**27:18** Therefore, thus says the Lord: "Look, I am taking vengeance on the one of Babylon and on his land, just as I took vengeance on the king of Assyria.

**27:19** And I will restore Israel to his pasture, and he will graze on Carmel and on Mount Ephraim and in Gilead, and his soul will be filled.

**27:20** In those days and at that time, they will seek the unrighteousness of Israel, and it will not exist, and the sins of Judah, and they will by no means be found, because I will be merciful to those left behind on the earth," says the Lord.

**27:21** Bitterly advance against her and against those who dwell upon her; take vengeance, sword, and make desolate, says the Lord, and do according to all that I command you.

**27:22** A sound of war and great shattering in the land of the Chaldeans.

**27:23** How has the hammer of all the earth been broken and shattered? How has Babylon become a desolation among the nations?

**27:24** They will advance against you, and you will not know, like Babylon, and you will be captured; you were found and you were taken, because you resisted the Lord.

**27:25** The Lord has opened His treasury and brought out the instruments of His wrath, for the Lord God has a work in the land of the Chaldeans,

**27:26** because her times have come. Open her storehouses, search her like a cave and utterly destroy her; let there be no remnant of her.

**27:27** Dry up all her fruits, and let them go down to the slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come and the time of their vengeance.

**27:28** A sound of those fleeing and escaping from the land of Babylon, to announce in Zion the vengeance from the Lord our God.

**27:29** Summon many against Babylon, everyone who draws a bow; encamp against her on every side; let there be no one escaping from her; repay her according to her works; according to all that she has done, do to her, because she resisted the Lord, the holy God of Israel.

**27:30** Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all her men of war will be cast down, said the Lord.

**27:31** Look, I am against you, you arrogant one, says the Lord, for your day has come and the time of your vengeance,

**27:32** and your arrogance will grow weak and fall, and there will be no one raising it up; and I will kindle a fire in her forest, and it will devour all that is around her.

**27:33** Thus says the Lord: "The sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been oppressed; together all those who took them captive oppressed them, because they were not willing to send them away.

**27:34** And the One who redeems them is mighty; the Lord Almighty is His name; He will render judgment against His adversaries, so that He may remove the earth, and He will provoke those who dwell in Babylon.

**27:35** A sword against the Chaldeans and against those who dwell in Babylon, and against her nobles and against her discerning ones;

**27:36** a sword against her warriors, and they will be enfeebled; a sword against their horses and against their chariots;

**27:37** a sword against their warriors and against the mixed crowd that is in her midst, and they will become like women; a sword against her treasuries, and they will be scattered

**27:38** upon her water, and they will be put to shame, for it is a land of carved images, and in the islands where they boasted.

**27:39** Therefore apparitions will dwell in the islands, and daughters of sirens will dwell in her; she will by no means be inhabited again forever.

**27:40** Just as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighboring cities," said the Lord, "no man will dwell there, and no son of man will sojourn there."

**27:41** Look, a people is coming from the north, and a great nation and many kings will be roused from the end of the earth,

**27:42** holding bow and dagger; it is ruthless and will by no means show mercy; their voice will roar like the sea; upon horses they will ride, prepared like fire for war, against you, daughter of Babylon.

**27:43** The king of Babylon heard the report about them, and his hands were enfeebled; distress overpowered him, birth pangs like those of a woman giving birth.

**27:44** Look, like a lion he will come up from the Jordan to Gaithan, for swiftly I will drive them away from her, and every young man I will set over her. For who is like Me? And who will resist Me? And who is this shepherd who will stand before Me?

**27:45** Therefore hear the counsel of the Lord which He has planned against Babylon, and His designs which He has devised against the Chaldean inhabitants: surely the lambs of their sheep will be destroyed, surely pasture will be made desolate from them.

**27:46** For at the sound of the capture of Babylon the earth will shake, and an outcry will be heard among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — Jeremiah 27 in the Septuagint corresponds to Jeremiah 50 in the Hebrew Bible.
- **27:2** — The Septuagint describes Bel as "the fearless one" (ἡ ἀπτόητος) and Merodach (Greek Μαιωδάκ) as "the delicate one" delivered up, differing markedly from the Hebrew text and echoing 26:28.
- **27:8** — Greek δράκοντες ("dragons") differs from the Hebrew "male goats" leading the flock.
- **27:18** — The adopted Greek text reads epi ton Babylonos ("on the one of Babylon"), omitting the noun basilea ("king") before Babylon.
- **27:24** — Greek ὡς Βαβυλών ("like Babylon") is translated literally; it likely arose within Greek transmission from vocative ὦ Βαβυλών ("O Babylon").
- **27:38** — The Septuagint links "upon her water" to the preceding verse ("scattered") and reads "put to shame" and "in the islands" (continued in v. 39 with "apparitions" and "daughters of sirens"), diverging sharply from the Hebrew.
- **27:44** — "Gaithan" (Γαιθάν) is a Septuagint-distinct place name where the Hebrew reads "an enduring pasture."
