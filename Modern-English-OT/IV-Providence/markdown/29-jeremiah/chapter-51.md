## Text

**51:1** The word that came to Jeremiah for all the Jews who were dwelling in the land of Egypt, and those residing in Migdol and in Tahpanhes and in the land of Pathros, saying:

**51:2** Thus said the Lord, the God of Israel: "You have seen all the evils that I brought upon Jerusalem and upon the cities of Judah; and look, they are desolate, without inhabitants,

**51:3** because of their wickedness that they committed to provoke Me, going to burn incense to other gods whom you did not know.

**51:4** And I sent to you My servants the prophets at dawn, and I sent them, saying, 'Do not do this thing of defilement that I have hated.'

**51:5** And they did not listen and did not incline their ear to turn away from their evils, so as not to burn incense to other gods.

**51:6** And My wrath and My fury poured down and blazed up in the gates of Judah and outside Jerusalem, and they became a desolation and an untrodden place, as it is this day."

**51:7** And now, thus said the Lord Almighty: "Why are you doing great evils against your own souls, to cut off from you man and woman, infant and nursing child from the midst of Judah, so that none of you is left,

**51:8** provoking Me by the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt, into which you came to dwell, so that you may be cut off and become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

**51:9** Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, and the evils of the kings of Judah, and the evils of your rulers, and the evils of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and outside Jerusalem?

**51:10** And they have not ceased to this day, and they did not hold fast to My ordinances that I set before their fathers."

**51:11** Therefore, thus said the Lord: "Look, I am setting My face

**51:12** to destroy all the remnant who are in Egypt, and they will fall by sword and by famine, and they will perish from the least to the greatest, and they will become a reproach and a destruction and a curse.

**51:13** And I will call to account those residing in the land of Egypt, just as I called Jerusalem to account, by sword and by famine,

**51:14** and there will be no one saved from the remainder of Judah who are sojourning in the land of Egypt to return to the land of Judah, for which they themselves hope in their souls, to return there; they will by no means return, except those who are rescued."

**51:15** And all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense answered Jeremiah, and all the women, a great assembly, and all the people residing in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, saying:

**51:16** "As for the word that you spoke to us in the name of the Lord, we will not listen to you;

**51:17** for we will surely do every word that will go out from our mouth, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her, just as we and our fathers and our kings and our rulers did in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem; and we were filled with bread, and we were well off, and we saw no evils.

**51:18** And since we stopped burning incense to the queen of heaven, we were all diminished, and by sword and by famine we perished.

**51:19** And since we burn incense to the queen of heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, did we make cakes for her and pour out drink offerings to her without our husbands?"

**51:20** And Jeremiah said to all the people, to the mighty men and to the women, and to all the people who had answered him with words, saying:

**51:21** "Did not the Lord remember the incense that we burned in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem—you and your fathers and your kings and your rulers and the people of the land—and did it not come up into His heart?

**51:22** And the Lord could no longer bear it because of the wickedness of your deeds and because of the abominations that you committed; and your land became a desolation and an untrodden place and a curse, as on this day,

**51:23** because of what you burned as incense and how you sinned against the Lord; and you did not listen to the voice of the Lord, and you did not walk in His ordinances and in the law and in His testimonies, and these evils overtook you."

**51:24** And Jeremiah said to the people and to the women, "Hear the word of the Lord."

**51:25** Thus said the Lord, the God of Israel: "You women spoke with your mouth and fulfilled it with your hands, saying, 'We will surely perform our vows that we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.' You surely stood by your vows, and you surely performed them."

**51:26** Therefore, hear the word of the Lord, all Judah who reside in the land of Egypt: "Look, I have sworn by My great name," said the Lord, "that My name will no longer be in the mouth of anyone of Judah to say, 'The Lord lives,' in all the land of Egypt.

**51:27** For I am keeping watch over them to harm them and not to do them good; and all Judah who dwell in the land of Egypt will perish by sword and by famine until they come to an end.

**51:28** And those saved from the sword will return to the land of Judah, few in number; and the remnant of Judah who settled in the land of Egypt to dwell there will know whose word will stand.

**51:29** And this is the sign to you that I Myself will call you to account for harm."

**51:30** Thus said the Lord: "Look, I am giving Hophra king of Egypt into the hands of his enemy and into the hands of those seeking his life, just as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy and the one seeking his life."

**51:31** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he was writing these words in the book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

**51:32** Thus said the Lord concerning you, Baruch,

**51:33** because you said, "Woe is me, woe is me! For the Lord has added trouble to my pain; I lay down in groanings; I found no rest";

**51:34** say to him, "Thus said the Lord: 'Look, those whom I built, I am tearing down; and those whom I planted, I am uprooting.

**51:35** And will you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek them; for look, I am bringing evils upon all flesh,' says the Lord, 'and I will give your life as a windfall in every place where you may walk.'"

## Translator Notes

- **51:6** — Greek reads "in the gates of Judah and outside Jerusalem" (similarly "outside Jerusalem" in 51:9, 17, 21), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem."
- **51:21** — The Greek text reads the first-person plural "that we burned" (ethumiasamen), shifting immediately to "you and your fathers"; the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads second-person plural throughout.
- **51:27** — Greek literally reads "if they come to an end" (ean ekliposin), widely understood as a shortened or corrupt form of heos an ekliposin ("until they come to an end").
- **51:33** — Greek reads "I lay down in groanings" (ekoimethen, "lay down/slept"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I am weary with my groaning."
