## Text

**26:2** For Egypt: against the army of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt, who was at the Euphrates River in Carchemish, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck in the fourth year of Jehoiakim king of Judah.

**26:3** Take up weapons and shields, and advance to battle,

**26:4** and saddle the horses; mount up, horsemen, and take your stand in your helmets; thrust forward the spears and put on your breastplates.

**26:5** Why is it that they are terrified and withdraw backward? Because their mighty ones will be cut down; in flight they fled and did not turn back, surrounded on every side, says the Lord.

**26:6** Let not the swift flee, and let not the mighty one escape to the north; those beside the Euphrates grew weak, and they have fallen.

**26:7** Who is this who will rise up like a river, and like rivers that surge with water?

**26:8** The waters of Egypt will rise up like a river, and it said, "I will rise up and cover the earth and destroy those who dwell in it."

**26:9** Mount the horses, prepare the chariots; go out, warriors of the Ethiopians, and Libyans armed with weapons, and Lydians, go up, draw the bow,

**26:10** and that day belongs to the Lord our God, a day of vengeance, to take vengeance on His enemies; and the sword of the Lord will devour, and it will be filled and become drunk from their blood; for the Lord has a sacrifice from the land of the north at the Euphrates River.

**26:11** Go up, Gilead, and take resin for the virgin daughter of Egypt; in vain you have multiplied your remedies; there is no benefit for you.

**26:12** Nations heard your voice, and the earth was filled with your outcry, because warrior grew weak against warrior; together both of them fell.

**26:13** The things that the Lord spoke by the hand of Jeremiah, that the king of Babylon would come to strike down the land of Egypt.

**26:14** Announce in Migdol, and proclaim in Memphis; say, "Take your stand and prepare, for a sword has devoured your bindweed."

**26:15** Why did Apis flee from you? Your choice calf did not remain, because the Lord enfeebled him.

**26:16** And your multitude grew weak and they fell, and each one spoke to his neighbor, "Let us rise and return to our people, to our homeland, from the face of the Greek sword."

**26:17** Call the name of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt "Saon Esbei Emoed."

**26:18** As I live, says the Lord God, surely like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel that is by the sea, he will come.

**26:19** Make for yourself baggage for exile, inhabitant, daughter of Egypt; for Memphis will become a desolation, and it will be called "Woe," because there are no inhabitants in it.

**26:20** Egypt is a beautified heifer; a detachment from the north has come upon her,

**26:21** and her mercenaries within her are like fattened calves nourished in her; for they also turned away and fled with one accord; they did not stand, because the day of destruction came upon them, and the time of their vengeance.

**26:22** A sound like a hissing serpent, for they march in sand; with axes they will come against her,

**26:23** like those cutting wood they will cut down her forest, says the Lord, for it cannot be estimated, because it is more numerous than locusts, and there is no number to them.

**26:24** The daughter of Egypt has been put to shame; she has been delivered into the hands of a people from the north.

**26:25** Look, I am taking vengeance on Amon her son, upon Pharaoh and upon those who trust in him.

**26:27** But you, do not fear, My servant Jacob, nor be terrified, Israel; for look, I am saving you from far away, and your seed from their captivity; and Jacob will return and be at rest and sleep, and there will be no one troubling him.

**26:28** Do not fear, My servant Jacob, says the Lord, for I am with you; the fearless and delicate one has been delivered up; for I will make a complete end among all to whom I drove you out there, but as for you, I will by no means cause you to fail; and I will discipline you with judgment, and I will not acquit you as innocent.

## Translator Notes

- **26:2** — Jeremiah 26:2-28 in the Septuagint corresponds to Jeremiah 46:2-28 in the Hebrew Bible (there is no verse 26:1 in the Greek text; Septuagint Jeremiah places the Oracles Against the Nations in chapters 25:14-31, corresponding to Hebrew chapters 46-51).
- **26:14** — Greek σμίλαξ ("bindweed" or "yew") is a Septuagint rendering distinct from the Hebrew "your surroundings."
- **26:15** — The Septuagint explicitly names Apis (ὁ Ἆπις), the sacred bull deity of Memphis, whereas the Hebrew reads "your mighty ones" (or "your mighty one").
- **26:16** — Greek μαχαίρας Ἑλληνικῆς ("the Greek sword") reflects the Septuagint reading of the Hebrew term for "oppressing" (hayyonah) as "Greece" (Yavan), a rendering repeated in 27:16.
- **26:17** — "Saon Esbei Emoed" (Σαὼν Ἑσβεὶ Ἑμωήδ) is the Septuagint transliteration as a proper name of the Hebrew phrase meaning "Noise, he has let the appointed time pass."
- **26:28** — The clause "the fearless and delicate one has been delivered up" (ἡ ἀπτόητος καὶ τρυφερὰ παρεδόθη) is a Septuagint-specific reading closely paralleling 27:2.
