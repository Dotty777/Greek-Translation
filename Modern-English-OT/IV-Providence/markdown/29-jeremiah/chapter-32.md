## Text

**32:15** Thus says the Lord God of Israel: "Take the cup of this unmixed wine from My hand, and you will make all the nations to whom I am sending you drink,

**32:16** and they will vomit and go mad from before the sword that I am sending among them."

**32:17** And I took the cup from the hand of the Lord, and I made the nations drink to whom the Lord sent me:

**32:18** Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, and the kings of Judah and its rulers, to make them a desolation and an untrodden waste and an object of hissing,

**32:19** and Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and his servants, and his nobles,

**32:20** and all his people, and all the mixed peoples, and all the kings of the foreigners, Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod,

**32:21** and Idumea, and the Moabite land, and the sons of Ammon,

**32:22** and the kings of Tyre, and the kings of Sidon, and the kings who are on the other side of the sea,

**32:23** and Dedan, and Teman, and Rosh, and everyone shaved around his face,

**32:24** and all the mixed peoples who lodge in the wilderness,

**32:25** and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Persians,

**32:26** and all the kings from the east, those far and those near, each one with his brother, and all the kingdoms that are on the face of the earth.

**32:27** And you will say to them, "Thus says the Lord Almighty: 'Drink, get drunk, and you will vomit and fall, and you will by no means rise from before the sword that I am sending among you.'"

**32:28** And it will be that when they are unwilling to accept the cup from your hand to drink, you will say, "Thus says the Lord: 'You will surely drink,

**32:29** for in the city upon which My name has been called I am beginning to bring harm, and you will by no means be cleared, for I am summoning a sword upon those who dwell on the earth.'"

**32:30** And you will prophesy against them these words, and you will say, "The Lord will utter an oracle from on high; from His holy place He will give His voice; He will utter a word over His place, and these will answer like grape harvesters. And upon those who inhabit the earth destruction has come,

**32:31** upon a part of the earth, for the Lord has a judgment among the nations; He Himself enters into judgment with all flesh, and the ungodly have been given over to the sword," says the Lord.

**32:32** Thus says the Lord: "Look, evils are coming from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind is going out from the end of the earth,

**32:33** and there will be those struck down by the Lord in the day of the Lord from one part of the earth even to another part of the earth; they will by no means be buried; they will become dung on the face of the earth.

**32:34** Wail, you shepherds, and cry out, and beat yourselves in mourning, you rams of the sheep, for your days for slaughter have been fulfilled, and you will fall like the choice rams;

**32:35** and flight will perish from the shepherds, and deliverance from the rams of the sheep.

**32:36** A sound of the cry of the shepherds, and wailing of the sheep and of the rams, for the Lord has destroyed their flocks,

**32:37** and the remnants of peace will cease from before the wrath of My fury.

**32:38** He has left His lair like a lion, for their land has become an untrodden waste from before the great sword."

## Translator Notes

- **32:15** — Jeremiah 32:15-38 in the Septuagint corresponds to Jeremiah 25:15-38 in the Hebrew Bible. The Greek reads "unmixed wine" (undiluted, full-strength wine) where the Hebrew text reads "wine of wrath."
- **32:20** — Greek allophyloi (lit., "foreigners" or "people of another tribe") is the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines.
- **32:23** — The Greek reads Teman and Rosh where the Hebrew text reads Tema and Buz.
- **32:25** — The Greek reads "the Persians" here (and "from the east" in v. 26) where the Hebrew text reads "the Medes" (and "from the north" in v. 26).
- **32:34** — The Greek reads "like the choice rams" where the Hebrew text reads "like a choice vessel."
