## Text

**34:2** Thus says the Lord: "Make bonds and collars, and put them around your neck,

**34:3** and you shall send them to the king of Idumea, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the sons of Ammon, and to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon, by the hands of their messengers who are coming to meet them in Jerusalem, to Zedekiah king of Judah.

**34:4** And you shall command them to say to their masters, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Thus you shall say to your masters:

**34:5** I made the earth by My great strength and by My uplifted arm, and I will give it to whoever seems right in My eyes.

**34:6** I have given the earth to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to serve him, and the wild beasts of the field to work for him.

**34:8** And the nation and the kingdom, all who will not put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will punish them with sword and with famine, says the Lord, until they come to an end by his hand.

**34:9** And as for you, do not listen to your false prophets, and those who practice divination for you, and those who dream for you, and your omens, and your sorcerers, who say, "You shall by no means work for the king of Babylon,"

**34:10** for they prophesy false things to you, in order to remove you far from your land.

**34:11** And the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and works for him, I will leave it on its own land; and it shall work for him and dwell in it.'"

**34:12** And to Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke according to all these words, saying, "Bring your neck in and work for the king of Babylon.

**34:14** For they prophesy unjust things to you,

**34:15** for I did not send them, declares the Lord, and they prophesy in My name unjustly in order to destroy you; and you shall perish, you and your prophets who prophesy false things to you unjustly."

**34:16** To you and to all this people and to the priests I spoke, saying, "Thus says the Lord: Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you, saying, 'Look, the vessels of the house of the Lord will return from Babylon,' for they prophesy unjust things to you; I did not send them.

**34:18** If they are prophets, and if the word of the Lord is in them, let them meet with Me;

**34:19** for thus says the Lord: And concerning the remaining vessels,

**34:20** which the king of Babylon did not take when he exiled Jeconiah from Jerusalem,

**34:22** they shall go into Babylon, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — Greek kloios denotes a wooden or iron neck collar (rendering the Hebrew term for yoke-bars). Verses 1, 7, 13, 17, and 21 are absent from the Septuagint text of this chapter (MT Jer 27).
- **34:3** — Greek reads "Idumea," the Hellenistic name for Edom, and includes the phrase "to meet them," which has no direct equivalent in MT 27:3.
- **34:5** — Greek epicheiron ("arm") is unique in the Septuagint, rendering the Hebrew "outstretched arm." In v. 6, the Greek omits the title "My servant" for Nebuchadnezzar (cf. MT 27:6).
- **34:11** — Greek reads masculine "for him" (auto), referring to the king of Babylon, whereas MT 27:11 has feminine "till it," referring to the land.
- **34:18** — Greek reads "let them meet with Me" (apantesatosan moi), continuing the Lord's first-person voice from v. 16 rather than the third-person "entreat the LORD of hosts" in MT 27:18. In v. 15, the Greek pairs "unjustly" (ep' adiko) with "false things" (pseude).
