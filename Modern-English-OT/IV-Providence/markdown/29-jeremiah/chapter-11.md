## Text

**11:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, saying:

**11:2** "Hear the words of this covenant, and you shall speak to the men of Judah and to those who dwell in Jerusalem,

**11:3** and you shall say to them, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Cursed is the man who will not listen to the words of this covenant,

**11:4** which I commanded your fathers on the day when I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, saying, "Listen to My voice, and do all that I command you, and you shall be a people to Me, and I will be God to you,"

**11:5** so that I may confirm My oath which I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day.'" And I answered and said, "May it be so, Lord."

**11:6** And the Lord said to me, "Read aloud these words in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem, saying, 'Hear the words of this covenant and do them.'"

**11:8** And they did not do so.

**11:9** And the Lord said to me, "A conspiracy has been found among the men of Judah and among those who dwell in Jerusalem;

**11:10** they have turned back to the iniquities of their earlier fathers, who were not willing to listen to My words; and look, they themselves are going after foreign gods to serve them, and the house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken My covenant which I made with their fathers.

**11:11** Therefore, thus says the Lord: 'Look, I am bringing calamities upon this people from which they will not be able to come out, and they will cry out to Me, and I will not listen to them.

**11:12** And the cities of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods to whom they burn incense; they will certainly not save them in the time of their calamities.

**11:13** For according to the number of your cities were your gods, Judah, and according to the number of the ways out of Jerusalem you set up altars to burn incense to Baal.'

**11:14** And as for you, do not pray for this people, and do not plead for them in supplication and prayer, for I will not listen in the time when they call upon Me, in the time of their affliction.

**11:15** Why has the beloved one committed an abomination in My house? Will vows and holy meats take away your wickednesses from you, or by these will you escape?

**11:16** A beautiful olive tree, well-shaded in appearance, the Lord called your name; at the sound of its circumcision fire was kindled against it, great is the affliction upon you, its branches have been made useless,

**11:17** and the Lord, who planted you, has spoken calamities against you in return for the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Judah, because they acted for themselves to provoke Me to anger by burning incense to Baal."

**11:18** Lord, make it known to me, and I will know; then I saw their practices,

**11:19** but I, like an innocent lamb being led to be sacrificed, did not know. Against me they devised an evil plot, saying, "Come, and let us cast wood into his bread, and let us wipe him out from the land of the living, and his name will never be remembered again."

**11:20** Lord, who judges righteously, who tests kidneys and hearts, may I see the vengeance from You upon them, for to You I have revealed my righteous cause.

**11:21** Therefore, thus says the Lord concerning the men of Anathoth who seek my life, who say, "You shall by no means prophesy in the name of the Lord, or else you will die by our hands":

**11:22** "Look, I will bring punishment upon them: their young men will die by the sword, and their sons and their daughters will perish by famine,

**11:23** and there will be no remnant of them, for I will bring calamities upon those who dwell in Anathoth in the year of their punishment."

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — Greek reads "Read aloud" and "outside Jerusalem" where MT has "Proclaim" and "in the streets of Jerusalem," omits MT 11:7, and retains only the brief clause in 11:8.
- **11:13** — Greek places the feminine article before Baal (also in 11:17), a Septuagint convention reflecting the oral substitution of the feminine noun "shame" (Hebrew bosheth) for the deity's name.
- **11:16** — Greek reads "at the sound of its circumcision," deriving the Hebrew word for "tumult" from the root meaning "to circumcise," and adds "great is the affliction upon you."
- **11:19** — Greek reads "let us cast wood into his bread" where the Hebrew MT reads "let us destroy the tree with its fruit" (literally "its bread").
- **11:21** — Greek reads first-person "my life" (in Jeremiah's voice) where MT has second-person "your life," and omits the renewed messenger formula at the opening of MT 11:22.
