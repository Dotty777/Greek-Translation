## Text

**21:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur son of Malchijah and Zephaniah son of Manassaeus, the priest, saying,

**21:2** "Inquire of the Lord on our behalf, because the king of Babylon has come against us, whether the Lord will act according to all His wonders, and he will depart from us."

**21:3** And Jeremiah said to them, "Thus you shall say to Zedekiah king of Judah,

**21:4** 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am turning back the weapons of war with which you are fighting against the Chaldeans who have shut you in outside the wall, into the midst of this city,

**21:5** and I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a mighty arm, with fury and great wrath;

**21:6** and I will strike all those who dwell in this city, the people and the livestock, with a great death, and they will die.

**21:7** And after these things, thus says the Lord: I will give Zedekiah king of Judah and his servants and the people who are left in this city from death and from famine and from the sword into the hands of their enemies who seek their lives, and they will cut them down with the edge of the sword; I will not spare them, and I will by no means have compassion on them.'"

**21:8** And to this people you shall say, "Thus says the Lord: Look, I have set before your face the way of life and the way of death:

**21:9** The one who remains in this city will die by the sword and by famine, and the one who goes out to surrender to the Chaldeans who have shut you in will live, and his life will be as plunder, and he will live.

**21:10** For I have firmly set My face against this city for evil and not for good; it will be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it up with fire."

**21:11** House of the king of Judah, hear the word of the Lord:

**21:12** House of David, thus says the Lord: "Render judgment in the morning and direct it rightly, and rescue the one who has been plundered from the hand of the one who wrongs him, so that My wrath may not be kindled like fire and burn, and there be no one who will extinguish it.

**21:13** Look, I am against you who dwell in the level valley of Sor, those who say, 'Who will terrify us? Or who will enter into the dwelling place?'

**21:14** And I will kindle a fire in its forest, and it will devour all things around it."

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — The Greek reads Manassaiou (Manassaeus), differing from the Hebrew Maaseiah.
- **21:6** — Greek thanatos ("death") is the Septuagint's regular rendering for deadly pestilence (so also in 21:7).
- **21:7** — Unlike the Hebrew, the Greek omits Nebuchadnezzar here, makes the enemies the plural subject of "they will cut them down," and attributes the refusal to spare directly to the Lord in the first person.
- **21:13** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew tsur ("rock") as the proper name Sor and reads "terrify" instead of "come down against"; in 21:14 the Greek also omits the opening clause found in the Hebrew.
