## Text

**14:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah concerning the drought.

**14:2** Judea mourned, and her gates were emptied, and they grew dark upon the ground, and the outcry of Jerusalem went up.

**14:3** And her nobles sent their young men for water; they came to the wells and did not find water, and they brought back their vessels empty.

**14:4** And the works of the land failed, because there was no rain; the farmers were ashamed; they covered their heads.

**14:5** Even does in the field bore young and forsook them, because there was no vegetation.

**14:6** Wild donkeys stood on glens; they drew in wind; their eyes failed, because there was no grass.

**14:7** Our sins have stood against us; Lord, act for us for Your sake, because our sins are many before You, because we have sinned against You.

**14:8** Hope of Israel, Lord, and You save in a time of evils: why have You become like a sojourner in the land, and like a native turning aside to a lodging place?

**14:9** Will You be like a sleeping person, or like a man unable to save? Yet You are among us, Lord, and Your name has been called upon us; do not forget us.

**14:10** Thus the Lord says to this people: "They have loved to move their feet and have not spared them, and God has not prospered in them; now He will remember their injustice."

**14:11** And the Lord said to me, "Do not pray for this people for good,

**14:12** because if they fast, I will not listen to their supplication, and if they offer burnt offerings and sacrifices, I will not be pleased with them; for by sword and by famine and by death I will bring them to an end."

**14:13** And I said, "O One who is, Lord, look, their prophets are prophesying and saying, 'You will not see a sword, nor will famine be among you, for I will give truth and peace upon the land and in this place.'"

**14:14** And the Lord said to me, "The prophets are prophesying false things in My name; I did not send them, and I did not command them, and I did not speak to them; for false visions and divinations and omens and the choices of their heart they themselves are prophesying to you.

**14:15** Therefore, this is what the Lord says concerning the prophets who prophesy false things in My name, and I did not send them, who say, 'Sword and famine will not be in this land': By a diseased death they will die, and by famine the prophets will be brought to an end,

**14:16** and the people to whom they are prophesying, and they will be thrown out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the sword and the famine, and there will be no one to bury them—and their wives and their sons and their daughters—and I will pour out upon them their evils.

**14:17** And you will say this word to them: 'Bring down tears upon your eyes day and night, and let them not cease, because the daughter of my people has been crushed with a crushing, and with an exceedingly painful blow.

**14:18** If I go out into the plain, look, those wounded by the sword! And if I enter the city, look, the pain of famine! For priest and prophet have gone into a land which they did not know.'"

**14:19** Have You completely rejected Judah, and has Your soul departed from Zion? Why have You struck us, and there is no healing for us? We waited for peace, and there were no good things; for a time of healing, and look, turmoil!

**14:20** We know, Lord, our sins, the injustices of our fathers, because we have sinned before You.

**14:21** Relent for the sake of Your name; do not destroy the throne of Your glory; remember, do not break Your covenant with us.

**14:22** Is there among the idols of the nations one who brings rain? And will the sky give its abundance? Are You not He? And we will wait for You, Lord, because You have made all these things.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — Greek uses "Judea" (ἡ Ἰουδαία) here—distinct from "Judah" (Ἰούδας, as in 14:19)—and reads "her gates were emptied" (ἐκενώθησαν) rather than the Hebrew MT "languish."
- **14:8** — Greek reads "like a native" (ὡς αὐτόχθων) rather than the Hebrew MT "like a traveler," contrasting sharply with "sojourner" (πάροικος); "Hope" renders ὑπομονή ("patient waiting, endurance, expectation").
- **14:10** — Greek reads "God has not prospered in them" (ὁ θεὸς οὐκ εὐόδωσεν ἐν αὐτοῖς, rather than εὐδοκέω "be pleased with" as in 14:12) and omits the Hebrew MT's closing clause "and He will punish their sins."
- **14:13** — Greek "O One who is, Lord" (Ὁ ὤν, Κύριε) echoes the divine title of Exodus 3:14 LXX, rendering the Hebrew exclamation "Alas, Lord GOD!"
- **14:15** — Greek reads "By a diseased death they will die" (Ἐν θανάτῳ νοσερῷ ἀποθανοῦνται), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "By sword and famine those prophets will meet their end."
