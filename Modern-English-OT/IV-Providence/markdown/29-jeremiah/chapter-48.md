## Text

**48:1** And it happened in the seventh month that Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elasa, from the lineage of the king, and ten men with him, came to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and they ate bread there together.

**48:2** And Ishmael and the ten men who were with him rose up and struck down Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land,

**48:3** and all the Judeans who were with him in Mizpah and all the Chaldeans who were found there.

**48:4** And it happened on the second day after he had struck down Gedaliah, while no one knew,

**48:5** that men came from Shechem and from Salem and from Samaria, eighty men, with their beards shaved and their garments torn and beating themselves, with manna and frankincense in their hands to bring into the house of the Lord.

**48:6** And Ishmael went out to meet them; they were going along and weeping, and he said to them, "Come in to Gedaliah."

**48:7** And it happened, when they had entered into the middle of the city, that he slaughtered them into the cistern.

**48:8** And ten men were found there and said to Ishmael, "Do not kill us, for we have stores in the field—wheat and barley, honey and oil." And he passed by and did not kill them in the midst of their brothers.

**48:9** And the cistern into which Ishmael threw all whom he had struck down—this is a great cistern that King Asa made because of Baasha king of Israel—this Ishmael filled with those struck down.

**48:10** And Ishmael turned back all the people who were left in Mizpah and the daughters of the king whom the chief cook had entrusted to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, and he departed to the other side of the sons of Ammon.

**48:11** And Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the force who were with him heard all the evil things that Ishmael had done,

**48:12** and they led their entire army and departed to fight against him, and they found him by much water in Gibeon.

**48:13** And it happened, when all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan and the commanders of the force that was with him,

**48:14** that they turned back to Johanan.

**48:15** And Ishmael escaped with eight men and departed to the sons of Ammon.

**48:16** And Johanan and all the commanders of the force who were with him took all the remnant of the people whom he had brought back from Ishmael—mighty men in war, and the women, and the rest, and the eunuchs whom he had brought back from Gibeon—

**48:17** and they departed and stayed in Gaberochamaa, which is near Bethlehem, to go into Egypt

**48:18** because of the Chaldeans, for they were afraid of them, because Ishmael had struck down Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had appointed in the land.

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — The Greek reads Elasa, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Elishama.
- **48:5** — The Greek names Salem (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Shiloh) and uses the Greek word manna to represent the Hebrew grain offering (minchah).
- **48:6** — In the Greek text the visiting men are the ones walking along and weeping, whereas the Hebrew text describes Ishmael as weeping as he went out.
- **48:9** — The Greek reads "this is a great cistern," reflecting a well-known textual variant compared with the Hebrew text's "by the side of Gedaliah."
- **48:10** — The Greek "turned back" differs from the Hebrew "took captive," and "chief cook" (Greek archimageiros) is the standard Septuagint rendering of the Babylonian official title "captain of the guard."
- **48:17** — Gaberochamaa is the Greek transliteration of the place name rendered Geruth Chimham in the Hebrew text; similarly, "the rest" in 48:16 and 50:6 translates Greek ta loipa where the Hebrew has "the little ones."
