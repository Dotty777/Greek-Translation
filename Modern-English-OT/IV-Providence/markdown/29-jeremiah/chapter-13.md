## Text

**13:1** This is what the Lord says: "Go and acquire for yourself a linen loincloth, and put it around your waist, and it shall not pass through water."

**13:2** And I acquired the loincloth according to the word of the Lord, and put it around my waist.

**13:3** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**13:4** "Take the loincloth that is around your waist, and arise and go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in the cleft of the rock."

**13:5** And I went and hid it at the Euphrates, just as the Lord commanded me.

**13:6** And it happened after many days that the Lord said to me, "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the loincloth which I commanded you to hide there."

**13:7** And I went to the Euphrates River and dug, and took the loincloth from the place where I had buried it; and look, it was ruined—it would by no means be used for anything.

**13:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**13:9** "This is what the Lord says: In this way I will ruin the arrogance of Judah and the arrogance of Jerusalem,

**13:10** this great arrogance—those who are unwilling to obey My words and who have gone after foreign gods to serve them and to bow down to them; and they will be like this loincloth, which will not be used for anything.

**13:11** For just as the loincloth clings around a man's waist, so I made the house of Israel and the entire house of Judah cling to Myself, to become for Me a renowned people and a boast and a glory; yet they did not listen to Me.

**13:12** And you will say to this people, 'Every wineskin will be filled with wine.' And it will happen, if they say to you, 'Do we not surely know that every wineskin will be filled with wine?'

**13:13** then you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord says: Look, I am filling those who dwell in this land, and their kings, the sons of David who sit on their throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and Judah, and all those who dwell in Jerusalem, with drunkenness,

**13:14** and I will scatter them, a man and his brother, and their fathers and their sons together. I will not yearn, says the Lord, and I will not spare and I will not have compassion from their ruin.'"

**13:15** Listen and give ear, and do not be lifted up, for the Lord has spoken.

**13:16** Give glory to the Lord your God before it grows dark, and before your feet stumble upon dark mountains; and you will wait for light, and there is the shadow of death, and they will be put into darkness.

**13:17** But if you do not listen, your soul will weep in secret because of arrogance, and your eyes will bring down tears, because the flock of the Lord has been crushed.

**13:18** Say to the king and to those who rule, "Humble yourselves and sit down, because the crown of your glory has been taken down from your head."

**13:19** The cities toward the south were shut up, and there was no one to open them; Judah was exiled; they completed a complete exile.

**13:20** Lift up your eyes, Jerusalem, and see those who are coming from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, the sheep of your glory?

**13:21** What will you say when they inspect you? And you yourself taught them lessons against yourself for rule. Will not birth pains seize you like a woman giving birth?

**13:22** And if you say in your heart, "Why have these things happened to me?"—because of the abundance of your injustice your back parts have been uncovered, so that your heels are exposed to disgrace.

**13:23** Will an Ethiopian change his skin, and a leopard its spots? Then you also will be able to do good, having learned evil things.

**13:24** And I scattered them like dry brushwood carried by wind into a wilderness.

**13:25** This is your lot and the portion of your disobeying Me, says the Lord; since you forgot Me and hoped in lies,

**13:26** I also will uncover your back parts over your face, and your dishonor will be seen,

**13:27** and your adultery, and your neighing, and the alienation of your prostitution. On the hills and in the fields I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, Jerusalem, because you have not been cleansed after Me; how much longer?

## Translator Notes

- **13:14** — Greek reads "I will scatter them" (διασκορπιῶ) and "I will not yearn" (οὐκ ἐπιποθήσω), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "I will dash them one against another" and "I will not pity."
- **13:17** — Greek attributes the weeping soul and eyes to the people ("your soul ... your eyes") and reads "has been crushed" (συνετρίβη), whereas the Hebrew MT has "my soul ... my eye" and "has been taken captive."
- **13:18** — Greek addresses "those who rule" (τοῖς δυναστεύουσιν) rather than the Hebrew MT's "queen mother."
- **13:22** — Greek τὰ ὀπίσθιά σου ("your back parts"; cf. τὰ ὀπίσω σου in 13:26) renders a Hebrew term for skirts or lower garments, conveying public exposure and disgrace.
