## Text

**25:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah,

**25:2** which he spoke to all the people of Judah and to those who dwell in Jerusalem, saying:

**25:3** "In the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amos, king of Judah, and until this day—twenty-three years—I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking.

**25:4** And I sent to you My servants the prophets, sending early in the morning, yet you did not listen and did not pay attention with your ears,

**25:5** saying, 'Turn back, each one from his evil way and from your evil practices, and you will dwell on the land that I gave to you and to your fathers from everlasting to everlasting.

**25:6** Do not go after foreign gods to serve them and to worship them, so that you do not provoke Me to anger by the works of your hands, to bring harm upon you.'

**25:7** Yet you did not listen to Me.

**25:8** Therefore, thus says the Lord: 'Because you did not believe My words,

**25:9** look, I am sending and will take a clan from the north, and I will bring them against this land and against those who dwell in it and against all the nations around it; and I will make them desolate and give them over to ruin and to hissing and to everlasting reproach.

**25:10** And I will abolish from them the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the scent of perfume and the light of a lamp.

**25:11** And all the land will become a ruin, and they will serve among the nations seventy years,

**25:12** and when seventy years are fulfilled, I will punish that nation and I will make them an everlasting ruin,

**25:13** and I will bring upon that land all My words that I have spoken against it, all the things written in this book.'"

**25:14** What Jeremiah prophesied against the nations of Elam. At the beginning of the reign of King Zedekiah, this word came concerning Elam.

**25:15** Thus says the Lord: "The bow of Elam is broken, the chief of their might.

**25:16** And I will bring upon Elam four winds from the four ends of heaven, and I will scatter them among all these winds; and there will be no nation to which those driven out of Elam will not come.

**25:17** And I will terrify them before their enemies who seek their life; and I will bring it upon them according to the wrath of My fury, and I will send My sword after them until I completely consume them.

**25:18** And I will set My throne in Elam, and I will send away from there king and nobles;

**25:19** and it will come about at the end of the days that I will bring back the captivity of Elam," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — Greek reads "Amos" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Amon" as the father of Josiah.
- **25:9** — Here and in verses 11-12, the Septuagint has a shorter text that refers to "a clan from the north" and "that nation" without naming Nebuchadnezzar or Babylon.
- **25:10** — Greek reads "the scent of perfume" (osmen myrou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the sound of the millstones."
- **25:14** — In the Septuagint arrangement of Jeremiah, the Oracles Against the Nations begin here with Elam (corresponding to 49:34-39 in the Hebrew text), and the Greek phrase "of Elam" (ta Ailam) is sometimes punctuated as a separate subheading ("The matters concerning Elam").
- **25:17** — Greek reads "according to" (kata) rather than "disaster" (kaka), omitting an explicit direct object before "the wrath of My fury."
