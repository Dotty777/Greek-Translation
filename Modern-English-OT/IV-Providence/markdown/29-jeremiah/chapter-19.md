## Text

**19:1** Then the Lord said to me, "Go and acquire a formed earthenware jar, and you will take some of the elders of the people and some of the priests,

**19:2** and you will go out to the burial ground of the sons of their children, which is at the entrance of the Tharsis Gate, and read aloud there all these words that I will speak to you,

**19:3** and you will say to them, 'Hear the word of the Lord, kings of Judah and men of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem and those who enter through these gates. Thus says the Lord God of Israel: Look, I am bringing evils upon this place, so that the ears of everyone who hears them will ring,

**19:4** because they have forsaken Me and alienated this place, and burned incense in it to foreign gods whom they and their fathers did not know; and the kings of Judah filled this place with innocent blood,

**19:5** and built high places to Baal to burn up their sons in fire; I did not command it, nor did I consider it in My heart.

**19:6** Therefore, look, days are coming, says the Lord, and this place will not be called Collapse and Burial Ground of the son of Hinnom, but rather Burial Ground of the Slaughter.

**19:7** And I will slaughter the counsel of Judah and the counsel of Jerusalem in this place, and I will cast them down by the sword before their enemies and by the hands of those who seek their lives, and I will give their dead bodies as food to the birds of the sky and to the wild beasts of the earth.

**19:8** And I will bring this city down to desolation and to hissing; everyone who passes by it will look gloomy and will hiss over its entire plague.

**19:9** And they will eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and each one will eat the flesh of his neighbor in the encirclement and siege with which their enemies will besiege them.'

**19:10** And you will shatter the jar before the eyes of the men who go out with you,

**19:11** and you will say, 'Thus says the Lord: In this way I will shatter this people and this city, just as an earthenware vessel is shattered, which will not be able to be healed.

**19:12** For in this way I will do, says the Lord, to this place and to those who dwell in it, so that this city is made like the collapsing one.

**19:13** And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be just like the collapsing place, because of their impurities, in all the houses in which they burned incense on their rooftops to all the host of heaven and poured out drink offerings to foreign gods.'"

**19:14** And Jeremiah came from the Collapse, where the Lord had sent him to prophesy, and stood in the court of the house of the Lord and said to all the people:

**19:15** "Thus says the Lord: Look, I am bringing upon this city and upon all its cities and upon its villages all the evils that I spoke against it, because they have stiffened their necks so as not to listen to My commands."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Greek "burial ground" (polyandrion, also in 19:6) replaces Hebrew "valley," and "sons of their children" appears where the Hebrew has "Ben-Hinnom." The Septuagint's "Tharsis Gate" (pylēs tēs Tharseis) is a distinct Greek name corresponding to the Hebrew "Potsherd (Harsith) Gate."
- **19:6** — Greek Diaptōsis ("Collapse," also in 19:14, with the related participle "collapsing" in 19:12-13) renders the Hebrew place name "Topheth" as a descriptive noun of ruin.
- **19:7** — The Septuagint reads "I will slaughter" (sphaxō), creating a wordplay with "Burial Ground of the Slaughter" in 19:6, where the Hebrew reads "I will make void."
- **19:15** — The Septuagint concludes with "My commands" (tōn entolōn mou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "my words."
