## Text

**40:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still bound in the court of the guard, saying,

**40:2** "Thus says the Lord, who makes the earth and forms it to establish it, the Lord is His name:

**40:3** Call out to Me and I will answer you, and I will declare to you great and mighty things that you did not know.

**40:4** For thus says the Lord concerning the houses of this city and concerning the houses of the king of Judah that have been torn down for ramparts and bulwarks,

**40:5** to fight against the Chaldeans and to fill it with the dead bodies of the people whom I struck down in My anger and in My wrath, and I turned My face away from them because of all their wicked acts:

**40:6** Look, I am bringing up for it recovery and healing, and I will reveal to them, and I will heal it, both peace and faithfulness;

**40:7** and I will restore the captivity of Judah and the captivity of Israel, and I will build them up just as before,

**40:8** and I will cleanse them from all their iniquities by which they sinned against Me, and I will by no means remember their sins by which they sinned against Me and departed from Me.

**40:9** And it will be for gladness and praise and for majesty to all the people of the earth, who will hear of all the good things that I will do; and they will fear and be embittered because of all the good things and because of all the peace that I will make for them.

**40:10** Thus says the Lord: Again there will be heard in this place of which you say, 'It is desolate, without people and livestock,' in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem, which have been made desolate because there is no person and no livestock,

**40:11** the voice of gladness and the voice of joy, the voice of a bridegroom and the voice of a bride, the voice of those saying, 'Give thanks to the Lord Almighty, for the Lord is good, for His mercy is forever,' and they will bring gifts into the house of the Lord; for I will restore all the captivity of that land as before, says the Lord.

**40:12** Thus says the Lord of hosts: In this place, which is desolate because there is no person and no livestock, in all its cities there will be resting places of shepherds making sheep lie down;

**40:13** in the cities of the hill country, and in the cities of the Shephelah, and in the cities of the Negev, and in the land of Benjamin, and in those around Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, sheep will again pass by the hand of one who counts, says the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **40:4** — "for ramparts and bulwarks" translates Greek eis charakas kai promachonas, whereas the Hebrew MT reads "against the siege mounds and the sword."
- **40:6** — The Greek interposes "and I will heal it" (kai iatreuso auten) between "and I will reveal to them" (kai phaneroso autois) and its object "both peace and faithfulness" (kai eirenen kai pistin), preserving the distinctive clause order of the Septuagint.
- **40:9** — "be embittered" translates Greek pikranthesontai (matching "embitter" in 39:32), where the Hebrew MT has "tremble."
- **40:13** — The Septuagint concludes chapter 40 here (corresponding to MT 33:13), lacking the passage found in MT 33:14-26.
