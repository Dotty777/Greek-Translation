## Text

**5:1** "Run about in the streets of Jerusalem, and see and know, and search in her squares, if you can find whether there is one who does justice and seeks faithfulness, and I will be merciful to them," says the Lord.

**5:2** "The Lord lives," they say; therefore, do they not swear in falsehoods?

**5:3** Lord, Your eyes are toward faithfulness; You scourged them, and they did not feel pain; You consumed them, and they were not willing to receive discipline; they made their faces harder than rock, and they were not willing to turn back.

**5:4** And I said, "Perhaps they are poor, because they were unable, for they did not know the way of the Lord and the judgment of God.

**5:5** I will go to the prominent men and speak to them, for they themselves have come to know the way of the Lord and the judgment of God." And look, with one accord they shattered a yoke; they tore apart bonds.

**5:6** Therefore a lion from the forest struck them, and a wolf as far as the houses destroyed them, and a leopard kept watch over their cities; all who go out from them will be hunted down, because they multiplied their ungodly deeds; they grew strong in their turnings away.

**5:7** "For which of these things shall I be merciful to you? Your sons forsook Me and swore by those who are not gods; and I fed them to the full, and they committed adultery and lodged in the houses of prostitutes.

**5:8** They became horses mad for females; each one neighed after the wife of his neighbor.

**5:9** Shall I not punish for these things?" says the Lord, "or on a nation such as this shall My soul not take vengeance?"

**5:10** "Go up upon her battlements and demolish them, yet do not make a complete end; leave behind her supports, for they belong to the Lord.

**5:11** For the house of Israel has utterly acted treacherously against Me," says the Lord, "and the house of Judah

**5:12** lied to their own Lord, and they said, 'These things are not so; evils will not come upon us, and we will not see sword or famine;

**5:13** our prophets became wind, and the word of the Lord was not in them; so it will happen to them.'"

**5:14** Therefore thus says the Lord Almighty: "Because you spoke this word, look, I have made My words in your mouth fire, and this people wood, and it will devour them.

**5:15** Look, I am bringing upon you a nation from afar, house of Israel," says the Lord, "a nation the sound of whose language you will not hear;

**5:16** all are mighty, and they will devour your harvest

**5:17** and your loaves of bread, and they will devour your sons and your daughters, and they will devour your sheep and your calves, and they will devour your vineyards and your fig groves and your olive groves; and they will thresh your fortified cities, in which you have trusted, with the sword.

**5:18** And it will be in those days," says the Lord your God, "I will certainly not make you into a complete end.

**5:19** And it will be when you say, 'For what reason has the Lord our God done all these things to us?' then you shall say to them, 'Because you served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve foreigners in a land not yours.'"

**5:20** "Declare these things to the house of Jacob, and let it be heard in Judah.

**5:21** Hear now these things, foolish and senseless people: they have eyes and do not see; they have ears and do not hear.

**5:22** Will you not fear Me?" says the Lord, "or will you not stand in awe before My face, the One who appointed sand as a boundary for the sea, an eternal decree, and it will not pass over it; and it will be agitated, yet it will not prevail, and its waves will roar, yet they will not pass over it?

**5:23** But this people came to have a disobedient and rebellious heart, and they turned aside and went away.

**5:24** And they did not say in their heart, 'Let us now fear the Lord our God, who gives us early and late rain at the season of the fulfillment of the decree of harvest, and He preserved it for us.'

**5:25** Your lawless deeds have turned these things aside, and your sins have kept good things away from you.

**5:26** For ungodly men were found among My people, and they set snares to destroy men, and they kept capturing them.

**5:27** Like a set snare full of birds, so their houses are full of deceit; therefore they became great and grew rich,

**5:28** and they transgressed justice; they did not judge the case of an orphan, and the case of a widow they did not judge.

**5:29** Shall I not punish for these things?" says the Lord, "or on such a nation as this shall My soul not take vengeance?

**5:30** Astonishment and horrible things have occurred upon the land.

**5:31** The prophets prophesy unjust things, and the priests clapped with their hands, and My people loved it so; and what will you do for the things after this?"

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Greek reads "a wolf as far as the houses" (ἕως τῶν οἰκιῶν), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a wolf of the deserts" (or "of the evenings").
- **5:10** — The Greek commands to "leave behind her supports, for they belong to the Lord," contrasting sharply with the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("strip away her branches, for they are not the Lord's").
- **5:11** — In the Greek syntax, "the house of Israel" is the subject of the verb in 5:11 while "the house of Judah" serves as the singular subject of "lied" (ἐψεύσατο) at the beginning of 5:12.
- **5:31** — The Greek reads "the priests clapped with their hands" (ἐπεκρότησαν ταῖς χερσὶν αὐτῶν), picturing approval of the prophets, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the priests rule at their direction."
