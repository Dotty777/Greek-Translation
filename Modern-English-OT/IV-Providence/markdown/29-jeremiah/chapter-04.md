## Text

**4:1** "If Israel returns," says the Lord, "to Me he will return; if he removes his abominations from his mouth, and stands in awe before the presence,

**4:2** and swears, 'The Lord lives,' with truth, in judgment and in righteousness, then nations will bless in him, and in him they will praise God in Jerusalem."

**4:3** For thus says the Lord to the men of Judah and to those who dwell in Jerusalem: "Break up fallow fields for yourselves, and do not sow upon thorns.

**4:4** Be circumcised to your God, and circumcise your hardness of heart, men of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem, so that His wrath will not go out like fire and burn, and there will be no one to quench it, because of the evil of your practices."

**4:5** Declare in Judah, and let it be heard in Jerusalem; speak, sound a signal with a trumpet in the land, cry out loudly; say, "Gather together, and let us enter the fortified cities."

**4:6** Pick up and flee to Zion; hurry, do not stand still, for I am bringing calamities from the north and great destruction.

**4:7** A lion has gone up from his lair; a destroyer of nations has departed and gone out from his place to make the land a desolation, and cities will be torn down so that they are not inhabited.

**4:8** Because of these things, wrap yourselves in sackcloth, and lament and wail, for the wrath of the Lord has not turned away from you.

**4:9** "And it will be in that day," says the Lord, "the heart of the king will perish, and the heart of the rulers, and the priests will be appalled, and the prophets will be amazed."

**4:10** And I said, "O Master, Lord, have You utterly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'There will be peace,' and look, the sword has reached even to their soul?"

**4:11** At that time they will say to this people and to Jerusalem, "A spirit of error in the wilderness: the way of the daughter of My people is not for what is pure nor for what is holy.

**4:12** A spirit of fullness will come to Me; and now I speak judgments against them."

**4:13** Look, like a cloud he will come up, and like a whirlwind are his chariots; swifter than eagles are his horses. Woe to us, for we are ruined!

**4:14** Wash your heart clean from wickedness, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long do the thoughts of your troubles remain in you?

**4:15** For a voice of one announcing will come from Dan, and trouble will be heard from Mount Ephraim.

**4:16** Remind the nations; look, they have come! Declare in Jerusalem, "Bands are coming from a distant land, and they have raised their voice against the cities of Judah."

**4:17** "Like those guarding a field they have come against her all around, because you have neglected Me," says the Lord.

**4:18** Your ways and your practices have done these things to you; this is your wickedness, for it is bitter, for it has reached even to your heart.

**4:19** I ache in my belly and in the senses of my heart; my soul is in turmoil, my heart is convulsed; I will not keep silent, for my soul has heard the sound of a trumpet, the shout of war.

**4:20** And it calls upon ruin, destruction, for all the land has been ruined; suddenly the tent has been ruined, my curtains have been torn apart.

**4:21** How long will I see those who flee, hearing the sound of trumpets?

**4:22** For the leaders of My people have not known Me; they are foolish sons and without understanding. They are wise to do evil, but to do good they have not known.

**4:23** I looked upon the earth, and look, nothing; and to heaven, and its lights were not there.

**4:24** I saw the mountains, and they were trembling, and all the hills being shaken.

**4:25** I looked, and look, there was no human being, and all the birds of heaven were terrified.

**4:26** I saw, and look, Carmel was desolate, and all the cities were burned with fire before the presence of the Lord, and before the anger of His wrath they were wiped out.

**4:27** Thus says the Lord: "All the earth will be desolate, yet I will by no means make a complete end.

**4:28** Because of these things let the earth mourn, and let the heaven above grow dark; because I have spoken and will not relent; I have set out and will not turn back from it."

**4:29** At the sound of a horseman and a drawn bow, every region withdrew; they slipped into the caves, and hid in the groves, and climbed up onto the rocks; every city was abandoned, no person was dwelling in them.

**4:30** And you, what will you do if you clothe yourself in scarlet and adorn yourself with golden ornament, if you paint your eyes with antimony? In vain is your beautifying; your lovers have rejected you; they are seeking your life.

**4:31** For I have heard a voice as of a woman in labor, of your groaning, as of one bearing her first child, the voice of the daughter of Zion! She will grow faint and will let her hands drop: "Alas for me, for my soul fails because of those who have been killed!"

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Greek reads 'from his mouth' (rather than Hebrew 'from before Me') and 'before the presence' without a possessive pronoun ('My'), followed by 'stands in awe' (eulabethe).
- **4:11** — Greek pneuma can mean either 'spirit' or 'wind.' In 4:11-12 the Septuagint renders the Hebrew winnowing-wind imagery as 'a spirit of error in the wilderness,' 'not for what is pure nor for what is holy,' and 'a spirit of fullness.'
- **4:20** — The Greek places two accusative nouns ('ruin, destruction') directly with the verb epikaleitai ('calls upon'), a compressed and unusual Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew.
- **4:23** — The Greek condenses the Hebrew phrase 'formless and void' (cf. Gen 1:2) to the single stark word 'nothing' (outhen).
- **4:26** — The Greek treats Hebrew hak-karmel ('the fruitful land/orchard') as the proper place name 'Carmel,' and reads 'burned with fire' rather than 'torn down.'
