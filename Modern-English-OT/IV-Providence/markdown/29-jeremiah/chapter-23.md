## Text

**23:1** Ah, shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of their pasture!

**23:2** Therefore, thus says the Lord against those who shepherd My people: "You have scattered My sheep and driven them out and have not attended to them; look, I am bringing punishment upon you according to your evil practices.

**23:3** And I Myself will gather in the remnant of My people from all over the earth where I drove them out, and I will restore them to their pasture, and they will increase and multiply.

**23:4** And I will raise up for them shepherds who will shepherd them, and they will no longer be afraid nor terrified," says the Lord.

**23:5** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will raise up for David a righteous shoot, and a king will reign and have understanding and practice justice and righteousness on the earth.

**23:6** In his days Judah will be saved, and Israel will dwell in confidence; and this is his name which the Lord will call him: Josedek." Concerning the prophets.

**23:9** My heart is broken within me; all my bones tremble. I have become like a broken man and like a man overcome by wine, because of the Lord and because of the splendor of His glory.

**23:10** For because of these things the land mourned, the pastures of the wilderness dried up, and their forest became evil, and their strength likewise.

**23:11** "For priest and prophet have been defiled, and in My house I saw their evil deeds.

**23:12** Therefore, let their way become for them a slippery place in thick darkness, and they will be tripped up and fall in it; because I will bring evil upon them in the year of their visitation.

**23:13** And among the prophets of Samaria I saw lawless deeds: they prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel astray.

**23:14** And among the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen horrifying things: committing adultery, and walking in lies, and taking hold of the hands of many so that no one turns back from his evil way; they have all become to Me like Sodom, and those who dwell in her like Gomorrah."

**23:15** Therefore, thus says the Lord: "Look, I will feed them pain and give them bitter water to drink, because from the prophets of Jerusalem defilement has gone out to all the land."

**23:16** Thus says the Lord Almighty: "Do not listen to the words of the prophets, because they make a vain vision for themselves; they speak from their own heart and not from the mouth of the Lord.

**23:17** They say to those who reject the word of the Lord, 'There will be peace for you,' and to all who walk in their own desires—to everyone who walks in the deception of his heart they have said, 'Evil will not come upon you.'

**23:18** For who has stood in the council of the Lord and seen His word? Who has given ear and heard?

**23:19** Look, a tempest from the Lord! And wrath goes out into a whirlwind; whirling around, it will come upon the ungodly,

**23:20** and the fury of the Lord will no longer turn back until He carries it out from the design of His heart; at the end of the days they will understand it.

**23:21** I did not send the prophets, yet they ran; nor did I speak to them, yet they prophesied.

**23:22** And if they had stood in My council and if they had listened to My words, then they would have turned My people back from their evil practices.

**23:23** I am a God who draws near, and not a God from afar.

**23:24** If someone hides in secret places, will I not see him? Do I not fill heaven and earth?" says the Lord.

**23:25** "I have heard what the prophets are saying; they prophesy falsehoods in My name, saying, 'I have dreamed a dream.'

**23:26** How long will this be in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehoods, as they prophesy the desires of their own heart,

**23:27** who plan to forget My law by their dreams which they recounted, each one to his neighbor, just as their fathers forgot My name because of Baal?

**23:28** Let the prophet in whom the dream is recount his dream, and let the one in whom My word is recount My word in truth. What is the chaff to the wheat?

**23:29** So are My words," says the Lord. "Look, are not My words like fire, and like an axe cutting rock?"

**23:30** "Look, therefore I am against the prophets," says the Lord God, "who steal My words, each one from his neighbor.

**23:31** Look, I am against the prophets who put forth prophecies of the tongue and slumber their slumber.

**23:32** Therefore, look, I am against the prophets who prophesy false dreams, and did not recount them, and led My people astray by their lies and by their deceptions; yet I did not send them and I did not command them, and they will not benefit this people at all."

**23:33** "And if this people, or a priest, or a prophet asks you, 'What is the burden of the Lord?' then you will say to them, 'You are the burden, and I will dash you down,'" says the Lord.

**23:34** "As for the prophet and the priests and the people who say, 'The burden of the Lord,' I will punish that man and his house.

**23:35** Thus you will say, each one to his neighbor and each one to his brother: 'What has the Lord answered, and what has the Lord spoken?'

**23:36** And do not mention 'The burden of the Lord' any longer, because the burden for a man will be his own word."

**23:37** And why has the Lord our God spoken?

**23:38** Therefore, thus says the Lord our God: "Because you spoke this word, 'The burden of the Lord,' though I sent to you, saying, 'You must not say, "The burden of the Lord,"'

**23:39** therefore, look, I am taking you up and dashing you down, and the city that I gave to you and to your fathers,

**23:40** and I will put upon you everlasting reproach and everlasting disgrace, which will not be forgotten. Therefore, look, days are coming, says the Lord, and they will no longer say, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel out of the land of Egypt,' but, 'The Lord lives, who gathered all the seed of Israel from the land of the north and from all the countries where He drove them out, and restored them to their land.'"

## Translator Notes

- **23:6** — Greek reads "which the Lord will call him: Josedek," treating the Hebrew title ("the Lord is our righteousness") as a proper name, and closes the verse with the superscription "Concerning the prophets" (literally "Among the prophets"), which introduces verse 9 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:10** — Greek reads "their forest" (drymos), likely an early scribal shift from dromos ("course"), and omits the negation in the final clause ("and their strength likewise," versus Hebrew "and their might is not right").
- **23:31** — Greek reads "and slumber their slumber," interpreting the Hebrew verb ("utter an oracle") from the root meaning "to slumber."
- **23:33** — Greek lemma means "burden" or "what is taken up" (used for a prophetic oracle), preparing for the wordplay on lambano ("taking you up") in verse 39; Greek also reads "You are the burden" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "What burden?"
- **23:40** — In this Greek text the words that the Hebrew Masoretic Text has at 23:7-8 follow the threat of everlasting reproach within verse 40 (there are no separate verses 7-8), and it reads 'and restored them to their land' where the Hebrew reads 'and they shall dwell on their own soil.'
