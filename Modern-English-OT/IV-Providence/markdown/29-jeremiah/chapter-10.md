## Text

**10:1** Hear the word of the Lord which He spoke concerning you, house of Israel.

**10:2** Thus says the Lord: "Do not learn according to the ways of the nations, and do not be afraid of the signs of heaven, for they fear them in their faces.

**10:3** For the customs of the nations are vain; it is wood cut out from the forest, a work of a craftsman and a cast image,

**10:4** adorned with silver and gold; with hammers and nails they fastened them.

**10:5** They will set them up, and they will not move; it is hammered silver; they will not walk.

**10:9** Plated silver will come from Tarshish, gold of Mophaz, and the hand of goldsmiths; all are works of artisans. They will clothe them in blue and purple. Surely they will be carried, for they will not take a step. Do not be afraid of them, for they will by no means do harm, and there is no good in them."

**10:11** Thus you shall say to them: "Gods who did not make heaven and earth, let them perish from the earth and from beneath this heaven."

**10:12** It is the Lord who made the earth by His strength, who established the inhabited world by His wisdom, and by His understanding He stretched out heaven

**10:13** and a multitude of water in heaven, and He brought up clouds from the end of the earth; He made lightning flashes for rain, and He brought out light from His storehouses.

**10:14** Every person has been made foolish, without knowledge; every goldsmith has been put to shame over his carved images, for he cast falsehoods; there is no breath in them.

**10:15** They are vain, works of mockery; in the time of their punishment they will perish.

**10:16** Such is not the portion of Jacob, for the One who formed all things, He is his inheritance; the Lord is His name.

**10:17** He has gathered your substance from outside, you who dwell in choice places.

**10:18** For thus says the Lord: "Look, I am overthrowing those who dwell in this land in affliction, so that your wound may be found."

**10:19** Woe over your shattering! Painful is your wound. And I said, "Truly this is your injury, and it has overtaken you."

**10:20** Your tent has been ruined, it has perished, and all your curtains have been torn apart; my sons and my sheep are no more; there is no longer a place for my tent, a place for my curtains.

**10:21** For the shepherds acted foolishly and did not seek the Lord; therefore all the pasture did not understand, and they were scattered.

**10:22** Look, a sound of a report is coming, and a great shaking from the land of the north, to make the cities of Judah a desolation and a resting place of ostriches.

**10:23** I know, Lord, that a human being's way is not his own, nor will a man walk and direct his own course.

**10:24** Discipline us, Lord, yet in judgment and not in wrath, so that You do not make us few.

**10:25** Pour out Your wrath upon the nations that do not know You and upon the generations that have not called upon Your name. For they have devoured Jacob and completely consumed him, and they have laid waste his pasture.

## Translator Notes

- **10:9** — This Greek text has no verses 10:6-8 or 10:10 (present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and preserves the Greek place-name 'Mophaz' where the Hebrew has 'Uphaz.'
- **10:13** — Greek lacks the opening clause of MT ("when He utters His voice") and reads "light" brought out from God's storehouses where MT reads "wind."
- **10:17** — Greek has a third-person past statement ("He has gathered your substance from outside") and "in choice places," whereas MT has a second-person imperative ("Gather up your bundle from the ground") and "under siege."
- **10:19** — Greek uses second-person pronouns throughout 10:19 and the opening of 10:20 ("your shattering... your wound... your injury"), shifting to first person in 10:20 where it also reads "my sheep" and "a place for my tent, a place for my curtains."
- **10:24** — Greek reads first-person plural "us" twice where MT has singular "me."
