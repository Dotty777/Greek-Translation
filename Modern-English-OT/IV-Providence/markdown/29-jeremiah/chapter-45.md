## Text

**45:1** And Saphanias son of Nathan, and Gedaliah son of Pashhur, and Jehucal son of Shelemiah heard the words that Jeremiah was speaking to the people, saying,

**45:2** "Thus says the Lord: 'The one who dwells in this city will die by sword and by famine, and the one who goes out to the Chaldeans will live, and his life will be as a find, and he will live.'

**45:3** For thus says the Lord: 'This city will certainly be handed over into the hands of the army of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.'"

**45:4** And they said to the king, "Please let that man be put to death, because he is weakening the hands of the fighting men who remain in the city and the hands of all the people by speaking to them words like these, for this man does not prophesy peace to this people, but only evil."

**45:5** And the king said, "Look, he is in your hands," for the king could not prevail against them.

**45:6** And they threw him into the cistern of Malchijah the king's son, which was in the court of the guard, and they lowered him into the cistern; and in the cistern there was no water, only mud, and he was in the mud.

**45:7** And Ebed-melech the Ethiopian heard, while he was in the house of the king, that they had put Jeremiah into the cistern; and the king was at the Gate of Benjamin.

**45:8** And he went out to him, and spoke to the king and said,

**45:9** "You have acted wickedly in what you have done, to kill this man because of the famine, for there are no longer loaves of bread in the city."

**45:10** And the king commanded Ebed-melech, saying, "Take thirty men from here into your hands, and bring him up out of the cistern so that he may not die."

**45:11** And Ebed-melech took the men and went into the underground house of the king, and took from there old rags and old ropes, and threw them to Jeremiah into the cistern,

**45:12** and said, "Put these underneath the ropes." And Jeremiah did so.

**45:13** And they pulled him with the ropes and brought him up out of the cistern; and Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard.

**45:14** And the king sent and called him to himself into the house of Haseleisel, which is in the house of the Lord. And the king said to him, "I will ask you a question, and please do not hide a word from me."

**45:15** And Jeremiah said to the king, "If I tell you, will you not certainly put me to death? And if I give you counsel, you will certainly not listen to me."

**45:16** And the king swore to him, saying, "As the Lord lives, who made this life for us, I will certainly not kill you, and I will not give you into the hands of these men."

**45:17** And Jeremiah said to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'If you indeed go out to the commanders of the king of Babylon, your soul will live, and this city will certainly not be burned down with fire, and you and your household will live.

**45:18** And if you do not go out, this city will be given into the hands of the Chaldeans, and they will burn it with fire, and you will certainly not be saved.'"

**45:19** And the king said to Jeremiah, "I am concerned about the Judeans who have fled to the Chaldeans, that they might give me into their hands, and they will mock me."

**45:20** And Jeremiah said, "They will certainly not hand you over. Listen to the word of the Lord that I am speaking to you, and it will be better for you, and your soul will live.

**45:21** And if you are not willing to go out, this is the word that the Lord has shown me:

**45:22** And look, all the women who were left in the house of the king of Judah were being led out to the officials of the king of Babylon, and they were saying, 'Your men of peace have deceived you and will prevail over you; and they will bring down your feet in slippery places; they have turned away from you.'

**45:23** And they will bring out your wives and your children to the Chaldeans, and you will certainly not be saved, for you will be captured by the hand of the king of Babylon, and this city will be burned down."

**45:24** And the king said to him, "Let no one know any of these words, and you will certainly not die.

**45:25** And if the officials hear that I have spoken to you, and they come to you and say to you, 'Tell us, what did the king speak to you? Do not hide it from us, and we will certainly not put you to death; and what did the king speak to you?'

**45:26** then you will say to them, 'I am casting my mercy before the eyes of the king, so that he will not send me back to the house of Jonathan to die there.'"

**45:27** And all the officials came to Jeremiah and questioned him; and he told them according to all these words that the king had commanded him; and they fell silent, because the word of the Lord was not heard.

**45:28** And Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard until the time when Jerusalem was captured.

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The Greek names Saphanias son of Nathan (corresponding to Hebrew Shephatiah son of Mattan) and lists three officials rather than four (omitting Pashhur son of Malchijah).
- **45:2** — Greek heurema literally means "a find" or "unexpected gain," rendering the Hebrew idiom for escaping with one's life as a prize of war (so also in 46:18).
- **45:4** — Greek chresimologei is a rare verb meaning "speaks beneficially" or a variant of chresmologei ("prophesies/delivers oracles"), where the Hebrew text reads "seeks."
- **45:9** — The Greek has Ebed-melech accuse the king directly ("You have acted wickedly in what you have done"), whereas the Hebrew text directs the charge against the officials ("these men").
- **45:14** — Haseleisel (Greek haseleisel) is an untranslated Septuagint transliteration corresponding to the Hebrew "the third [entrance]."
