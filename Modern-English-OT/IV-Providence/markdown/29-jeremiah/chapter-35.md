## Text

**35:1** And it happened in the fourth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fifth month, that Hananiah son of Azzur, the false prophet from Gibeon, spoke to me in the house of the Lord before the eyes of the priests and all the people, saying,

**35:2** "Thus says the Lord: I have shattered the yoke of the king of Babylon.

**35:3** Two more years of days, and I will bring back to this place the vessels of the house of the Lord,

**35:4** and Jeconiah and the exiles of Judah, for I will shatter the yoke of the king of Babylon."

**35:5** And Jeremiah spoke to Hananiah before the eyes of all the people and before the eyes of the priests who were standing in the house of the Lord,

**35:6** and Jeremiah said, "Truly, may the Lord do so; may He establish your word that you prophesy, to bring back the vessels of the house of the Lord and all the exiles from Babylon to this place.

**35:7** However, hear the word of the Lord that I speak into your ears and into the ears of all the people:

**35:8** The prophets who were before me and before you from ancient times prophesied against much land and against great kingdoms concerning war;

**35:9** as for the prophet who has prophesied concerning peace, when the word comes, they will know the prophet whom the Lord has sent to them in faithfulness."

**35:10** And Hananiah took the collars from the neck of Jeremiah before the eyes of all the people and shattered them.

**35:11** And Hananiah spoke before the eyes of all the people, saying, "Thus says the Lord: In this way I will shatter the yoke of the king of Babylon from the necks of all the nations." And Jeremiah went off on his way.

**35:12** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah after Hananiah had shattered the collars from his neck, saying,

**35:13** "Go and speak to Hananiah, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: You have shattered wooden collars, and I will make iron collars in their place.

**35:14** For thus says the Lord: I have placed an iron yoke on the neck of all the nations, to work for the king of Babylon.'"

**35:15** And Jeremiah said to Hananiah, "The Lord has not sent you, and you have caused this people to trust in what is unjust.

**35:16** Therefore, thus says the Lord: Look, I am sending you away from the face of the earth; this year you shall die."

**35:17** And he died in the seventh month.

## Translator Notes

- **35:1** — As in chs. 33-34, the Greek explicitly labels Hananiah "the false prophet" (ho pseudoprophetes), where MT 28:1 reads "the prophet."
- **35:9** — Greek uses the active verb "they will know" with "to them" and renders the Hebrew "in truth" with en pistei ("in faithfulness").
- **35:13** — Greek reads first-person "I will make" (poieso), attributing the iron collars directly to the Lord, whereas MT 28:13 has second-person "you have made."
