## Text

**20:1** And Pashhur son of Immer, the priest—and he was appointed leader of the house of the Lord—heard Jeremiah prophesying these words.

**20:2** And he struck him and threw him into the dungeon that was in the gate of the upper house set apart, which was in the house of the Lord.

**20:3** And Pashhur brought Jeremiah out of the dungeon, and Jeremiah said to him, "The Lord has not called your name Pashhur, but rather Exile.

**20:4** For thus says the Lord: 'Look, I am giving you over to exile along with all your friends; and they will fall by the sword of their enemies, and your eyes will see it; and I will give you and all Judah into the hands of the king of Babylon, and they will exile them and cut them down with swords.

**20:5** And I will give all the strength of this city and all its labors and all the treasures of the king of Judah into the hands of his enemies, and they will bring them to Babylon.

**20:6** And you and all who dwell in your house will go in captivity, and in Babylon you will die, and there you will be buried, you and all your friends to whom you prophesied falsehoods.'"

**20:7** You deceived me, Lord, and I was deceived; You were strong and prevailed. I became an object of laughter; all day long I continued being mocked;

**20:8** for with my bitter word I will laugh; I will call out treachery and misery, because the word of the Lord has become a reproach to me and a derision every day of mine.

**20:9** And I said, "I will certainly not name the name of the Lord, and I will no longer speak in His name." And it became like a burning fire blazing in my bones, and I am weakened on every side and cannot bear it,

**20:10** for I heard the censure of many gathering together all around, all around: "Band together, and let us band together against him, all you men who are his friends! Watch his intention, whether he will be deceived, and we will prevail against him and take our vengeance on him."

**20:11** But the Lord is with me like a strong warrior; therefore they pursued and were unable to understand. They were utterly put to shame, because they did not understand their disgraces, which will not be forgotten forever.

**20:12** Lord, testing righteous things, understanding kidneys and hearts, may I see the vengeance from You upon them, for to You I have uncovered my defenses.

**20:13** Sing to the Lord, praise Him, for He has delivered the soul of a poor person from the hand of evildoers.

**20:14** Cursed be the day on which I was born. Let the day on which my mother gave birth to me not be blessed;

**20:15** cursed be the man who brought good news to my father, saying, "A male child has been born to you," rejoicing.

**20:16** Let that man be like the cities that the Lord overthrew in wrath and did not relent; let him hear an outcry in the morning and a battle cry at noon,

**20:17** because he did not kill me in the womb, and my mother would have become my grave for me, and her womb of eternal conception.

**20:18** Why is it that I came out of the womb to see toils and pains, and my days have ended in shame?

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — Greek kataraktēn ("dungeon" or "trapdoor cell," also in 20:3) corresponds to Hebrew "stocks," and "the upper house set apart" appears where the Hebrew names the "Upper Gate of Benjamin."
- **20:3** — Greek Metoikon ("Exile" or "Migrant") connects with "exile" (metoikian) and "they will exile" (metoikiousin) in 20:4, interpreting the Hebrew name Magor-missaviv ("Terror on every side") from the root meaning "to sojourn" or "migrate."
- **20:8** — The Septuagint reads "with my bitter word I will laugh" (linked to "laughter" in 20:7) where the Hebrew reads "whenever I speak, I cry out."
- **20:10** — The Septuagint renders Hebrew "terror on every side" as "gathering together all around, all around" and makes the call to watch Jeremiah's "intention" a direct command addressed to his friends.
- **20:11** — The Septuagint reads "they pursued and were unable to understand" where the Hebrew reads "my persecutors will stumble and will not prevail."
