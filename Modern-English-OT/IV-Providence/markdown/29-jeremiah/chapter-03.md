## Text

**3:1** "If a man sends away his wife, and she goes away from him and belongs to another man, will she really return to him again? Will that woman not surely be defiled? And you prostituted yourself with many shepherds, and were you returning to Me?" says the Lord.

**3:2** "Lift your eyes straight ahead and see: where have you not been polluted? By the roads you sat for them like a deserted crow, and you defiled the land with your acts of prostitution and with your wicked deeds,

**3:3** and you had many shepherds as a stumbling block to yourself; you came to have the face of a prostitute; you were shameless toward everyone.

**3:4** Did you not call Me as a house, and father, and guide of your virginity?

**3:5** Will it remain forever, or will it be kept to the end? Look, you spoke, and you did these evil things, and you prevailed."

**3:6** And the Lord said to me in the days of Josiah the king, "Have you seen what the dwelling of Israel has done to Me? They went upon every high mountain and beneath every shady tree, and prostituted themselves there.

**3:7** And I said, after she committed all these acts of prostitution, 'Return to Me.' Yet she did not return, and faithless Judah saw her faithlessness.

**3:8** And I saw that on account of all the things that I also saw, on account of all the things in which the dwelling of Israel was caught in which she was committing adultery, I even sent her away and gave her a certificate of divorce into her hands; yet faithless Judah was not afraid, and she went and prostituted herself as well,

**3:9** and her prostitution came to nothing, and she committed adultery with the wood and the stone.

**3:10** And in all these things faithless Judah did not return to Me with all her heart, but in falsehood."

**3:11** And the Lord said to me, "Israel has justified his soul more than faithless Judah.

**3:12** Go and read these words toward the north, and you shall say, 'Return to Me, dwelling of Israel, says the Lord, and I will not set My face against you; for I am merciful, says the Lord, and I will not remain angry with you forever.

**3:13** Only acknowledge your unrighteousness, that you have acted impiously against the Lord your God, and have poured out your ways to strangers beneath every shady tree, and have not obeyed My voice, says the Lord.'"

**3:14** "Return, sons who have fallen away," says the Lord, "for I will rule over you, and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and I will bring you into Zion,

**3:15** and I will give you shepherds according to My heart, and they will shepherd you, shepherding with knowledge.

**3:16** And it will be, when you multiply and increase in the land in those days," says the Lord, "they will no longer say, 'Ark of the covenant of the Holy One of Israel.' It will not enter the heart, it will not be named, nor will it be visited, and it will not be made again.

**3:17** In those days and at that time they will call Jerusalem 'Throne of the Lord,' and all the nations will be gathered into it, and they will no longer walk after the thoughts of their evil heart.

**3:18** In those days the house of Judah will come together to the house of Israel, and they will come as one from the land of the north and from all the countries to the land that I gave as an inheritance to their fathers."

**3:19** And I said, "May it be so, Lord!" For I will set you among nations, and I will give you a choice land, an inheritance of God Almighty of nations. And I said, "You will call Me Father, and you will not turn away from Me."

**3:20** "Yet as a woman breaks faith with her companion, so the house of Israel has broken faith with Me," says the Lord.

**3:21** A voice from lips was heard, of weeping and supplication of the sons of Israel, because they acted unrighteously in their ways; they forgot God, their Holy One.

**3:22** "Return, returning sons, and I will heal your fractures." "Look, we will be servants to You, for You are the Lord our God.

**3:23** Truly the hills were for falsehood, and the power of the mountains; yet through the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.

**3:24** And shame has consumed the labors of our fathers from our youth, their sheep and their calves, and their sons and their daughters.

**3:25** We have lain down in our shame, and our dishonor has covered us, because before our God we have sinned, we and our fathers, from our youth until this day, and we have not obeyed the voice of the Lord our God."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text differs from the Hebrew in several notable details: the woman (rather than the husband) is the subject of 'return,' 'that woman' replaces 'that land,' 'shepherds' replaces 'lovers/companions' (as also in 3:3), and the final clause is an imperfect question ('were you returning to Me?') rather than an imperative.
- **3:3** — The Greek reads 'many shepherds as a stumbling block to yourself' (cf. 3:2 'deserted crow'), diverging markedly from the Hebrew text's imagery of withheld showers and an Arab in the wilderness.
- **3:6** — Greek 'dwelling' (katoikia) here and in 3:8, 12 reflects a Septuagint reading of the Hebrew term for 'backsliding' as derived from the root 'to dwell.'
- **3:8** — The Greek text preserves an ancient repetition ('on account of all the things that I also saw, on account of all the things in which...') found in major Septuagint witnesses.
- **3:19** — The Greek begins with 'May it be so, Lord!' before shifting abruptly to God speaking ('For I will set you among nations... an inheritance of God Almighty of nations'), diverging substantially from the Hebrew ('How I would set you among sons').
