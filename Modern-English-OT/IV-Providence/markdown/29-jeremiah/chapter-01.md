## Text

**1:1** The word of God that came upon Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests, who lived in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,

**1:2** to whom the word of God came in the days of Josiah son of Amos, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

**1:3** And it came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the captivity of Jerusalem in the fifth month.

**1:4** And the word of the Lord came to him:

**1:5** "Before I formed you in the womb, I know you, and before you came out, I have sanctified you; I have set you as a prophet to the nations."

**1:6** And I said, "O One Who Is, Master, Lord, look, I do not know how to speak, for I am young."

**1:7** And the Lord said to me, "Do not say, 'I am young,' for to all to whom I send you, you will go, and according to all that I command you, you will speak.

**1:8** Do not be afraid of their faces, for I am with you to rescue you," says the Lord.

**1:9** And the Lord stretched out His hand to me and touched my mouth, and the Lord said to me, "Look, I have put My words into your mouth.

**1:10** Look, I have appointed you today over nations and kingdoms, to uproot and to tear down and to destroy and to rebuild and to plant."

**1:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "A nut-tree staff."

**1:12** And the Lord said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watching over My words to perform them."

**1:13** And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "A cauldron kindled from underneath, and its face is from the face of the north."

**1:14** And the Lord said to me, "From the face of the north evils will blaze out upon all who inhabit the land.

**1:15** For look, I am summoning all the kingdoms from the north of the earth," says the Lord, "and they will come, and each one will set his throne at the entrances of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all the walls around her, and against all the cities of Judah.

**1:16** And I will speak to them with judgment concerning all their wickedness, how they forsook Me and sacrificed to foreign gods and worshiped the works of their hands.

**1:17** And you, gird your loins, and arise and speak everything that I command you; do not be afraid of their faces nor be terrified before them, for I am with you to rescue you," says the Lord.

**1:18** "Look, I have set you on this day as a fortified city and as a bronze wall, strong against all the kings of Judah and its rulers and the people of the land;

**1:19** and they will fight against you, and they will by no means prevail against you, because I am with you to rescue you," said the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Septuagint opens with "The word of God that came upon Jeremiah," whereas the Hebrew MT begins "The words of Jeremiah."
- **1:2** — The Greek reads "Amos" (Ἀμώς) rather than MT "Amon." The relative clause literally reads "which word of God came to him," reflecting a Semitic resumptive pronoun construction.
- **1:5** — The Greek uses the present tense "I know" (ἐπίσταμαι) alongside the perfects "I have sanctified" and "I have set," and omits "from the womb" after "before you came out."
- **1:6** — The Greek translates the Hebrew exclamation 'ahah ("Ah!") as the divine title "One Who Is" (Ὁ ὤν), echoing Exodus 3:14.
- **1:11** — Greek καρυίνην literally means "of a nut tree" (walnut or almond), rendering Hebrew shaqed ("almond"), whose wordplay with shoqed ("watching") in v. 12 is not preserved in Greek.
