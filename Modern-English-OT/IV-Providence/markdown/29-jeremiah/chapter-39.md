## Text

**39:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah in the tenth year of King Zedekiah; this was the eighteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

**39:2** And the army of the king of Babylon laid siege against Jerusalem, and Jeremiah was being kept under guard in the court of the guard, which is in the house of the king,

**39:3** in which King Zedekiah had shut him up, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am giving this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will take it,

**39:4** and Zedekiah will by no means escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, for he will surely be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon, and his mouth will speak to his mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes,

**39:5** and Zedekiah will enter Babylon, and there he will remain.'"

**39:6** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,

**39:7** "Look, Hanamel son of Shallum, your father's brother, is coming to you, saying, 'Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth, for to you belongs the right to receive it as a possession.'"

**39:8** And Hanamel son of Samol, my father's brother, came to me into the court of the guard and said, "Buy my field that is in the land of Benjamin, the one in Anathoth, for to you belongs the right to buy it, and you are the elder." And I knew that it was the word of the Lord.

**39:9** And I bought the field of Hanamel, the son of my father's brother, and I weighed out to him seven shekels and ten of silver.

**39:10** And I wrote in a deed and sealed it, and I called witnesses, and I weighed out the silver on a scale.

**39:11** And I took the deed of purchase, the sealed one,

**39:12** and I gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, before the eyes of Hanamel, the son of my father's brother, and before the eyes of the men who were standing by and writing in the deed of purchase, and before the eyes of the Jews who were in the court of the guard.

**39:13** And I commanded Baruch before their eyes, saying,

**39:14** "Thus says the Lord Almighty: Take this deed of purchase and the deed that has been read, and you shall place it in an earthenware vessel, so that it may remain for many days.

**39:15** For thus says the Lord: Fields and houses and vineyards will again be established in this land."

**39:16** And I prayed to the Lord after I had given the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, saying,

**39:17** "O Existing One, Lord, You made heaven and earth by Your great strength and by Your high and uplifted arm; nothing will be hidden from You,

**39:18** showing mercy to thousands and repaying the sins of fathers into the bosoms of their children after them, the great God, the mighty One,

**39:19** Lord of great counsel and powerful in works, the great God, the Almighty, and Lord of great name; Your eyes are upon the ways of the sons of men, to give to each according to his way;

**39:20** You who performed signs and wonders in the land of Egypt until this day, both in Israel and among the earthborn, and You made for Yourself a name as it is this day,

**39:21** and You brought Your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and with wonders, with a strong hand

**39:22** and with a high arm and with great visions, and You gave them this land which You swore to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**39:23** And they went in and took it, and they did not listen to Your voice, and they did not walk in Your ordinances; all that You commanded them they did not do, so that all these evils happened to them.

**39:24** Look, a crowd has come to the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword and the famine; just as You spoke, so it has happened.

**39:25** And You say to me, 'Buy for yourself the field for silver.' And I wrote a deed and sealed it, and called witnesses, and the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans."

**39:26** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**39:27** "I am the Lord, the God of all flesh; will anything be hidden from Me?

**39:28** Therefore thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: This city will surely be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will take it,

**39:29** and the Chaldeans will come fighting against this city, and they will burn this city with fire, and burn down the houses in which they burned incense on their rooftops to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods in order to provoke Me to bitterness.

**39:30** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah alone have been doing what is evil before My eyes from their youth;

**39:31** for this city has been for My anger and for My wrath, from the day they built it even until this day, to remove it from My presence,

**39:32** because of all the wicked acts of the sons of Israel and of Judah which they did to embitter Me, they and their kings and their rulers and their priests and their prophets, the men of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem.

**39:33** And they turned their back to Me and not their face, and I taught them early in the morning and taught them, yet they no longer listened to receive discipline.

**39:34** And they placed their defilements in the house upon which My name was called, in their impurities.

**39:35** And they built the altars to Baal that are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to offer up their sons and their daughters to Molech the king, things which I did not command them and which did not come up into My heart, that they should do this abomination, so that Judah would sin.

**39:36** And now thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, concerning the city of which you say, 'It will be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon by sword and by famine and by banishment':

**39:37** Look, I am gathering them from all the earth where I scattered them in My anger and My wrath and great indignation, and I will bring them back to this place, and I will settle them in confidence,

**39:38** and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them.

**39:39** And I will give them another way and another heart, to fear Me all the days, and for good to them and to their children after them.

**39:40** And I will make an eternal covenant with them, which I will by no means turn away from behind them; and I will put My fear into their heart, so that they will not depart from Me.

**39:41** And I will visit them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness and with all heart and with all soul.

**39:42** For thus says the Lord: Just as I brought upon this people all these great evils, so I will bring upon them all the good things that I have spoken concerning them.

**39:43** And fields will again be bought in the land of which you say, 'It will be impassable, without people and livestock, and they have been handed over into the hands of the Chaldeans.'

**39:44** And they will buy fields with silver, and you will write a deed and seal it, and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, and around Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the hill country of the Shephelah, and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their captivities."

## Translator Notes

- **39:8** — The Greek text reads "Samol" (a metathesis of "Shallum" in v. 7) and "you are the elder" (Greek presbyteros), where the Hebrew MT reads "Shallum" and "the right of redemption is yours."
- **39:15** — The Greek reads ktisthesontai ("will be established/founded," from ktizo), contrasting with ktethesontai ("will be bought," from ktaomai) in v. 43; this reflects either an ancient Greek variant or the dual sense of Hebrew qanah ("acquire/create").
- **39:17** — "O Existing One, Lord" renders Greek Ho on Kyrie, echoing the divine title in Exod 3:14 ("the One who is"), while "nothing will be hidden from You" (also v. 27) renders Greek apokrybe where the Hebrew MT has "too difficult."
- **39:35** — "Molech the king" preserves the Greek doublet to Moloch basilei, rendering Hebrew lammolekh both as a proper name and as "king."
- **39:39** — "another way and another heart" faithfully reflects Greek hodon heteran kai kardian heteran, differing in both order and wording ("another" vs. "one") from the Hebrew MT's "one heart and one way."
