## Text

**28:1** Thus says the Lord: "Look, I am raising up against Babylon and against the Chaldean inhabitants a scorching, destroying wind.

**28:2** And I will send out insolent men into Babylon, and they will treat her with insolence and ravage her land; woe upon Babylon all around in the day of her affliction.

**28:3** Let the one who draws his bow draw it, and let the one who has his armor put it on; and do not spare her young men, and wipe out all her army.

**28:4** And wounded men will fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and those pierced through outside her."

**28:5** For Israel and Judah have not been widowed from their God, from the Lord Almighty; for their land was filled with unrighteousness from the holy things of Israel.

**28:6** Flee from the midst of Babylon, and each of you save his own life, and do not be cast away in her unrighteousness, for it is the time of her vengeance from the Lord; He Himself is repaying a repayment to her.

**28:7** Babylon was a golden cup in the hand of the Lord, making all the earth drunk; nations drank from her wine; therefore they were shaken.

**28:8** And suddenly Babylon fell and was shattered. Lament for her; take resin for her destruction, if perhaps she will be healed.

**28:9** "We treated Babylon, and she was not healed; let us forsake her and let us go away, each to his own land, for her judgment has reached to heaven; it has risen up as far as the stars."

**28:10** "The Lord has brought forth His judgment; come, and let us declare in Zion the works of the Lord our God."

**28:11** Prepare the arrows, fill the quivers! The Lord has roused the spirit of the king of the Medes, for His wrath is against Babylon to destroy her utterly, for it is the vengeance of the Lord; it is the vengeance of His people.

**28:12** Upon the walls of Babylon raise a signal, set up quivers, raise up watches, prepare weapons, for He has undertaken it; and the Lord will do the things He spoke against those who dwell in Babylon,

**28:13** who encamp upon many waters and upon the abundance of her treasures; your end has come, truly into your inward parts.

**28:14** For the Lord has sworn by His arm: "I will fill you with men like locusts, and those who come down will cry out against you."

**28:15** Making the earth by His strength, preparing the inhabited world by His wisdom, by His understanding He stretched out heaven.

**28:16** At a voice He set a roar of water in heaven, and He brought up clouds from the end of the earth; He made flashes of lightning for rain, and He brought forth light out of His treasuries.

**28:17** Every man has been made futile, devoid of knowledge; every goldsmith has been put to shame because of his carved images, for they cast false things; there is no breath in them.

**28:18** They are futile things, mocked works; in the time of their visitation they will perish.

**28:19** Not such is Jacob's portion, for the One who formed all things, He Himself is his inheritance; the Lord is His name.

**28:20** "You scatter for Me weapons of war, and I will scatter nations in you, and I will remove kings from you.

**28:21** And I will scatter in you horse and its rider,

**28:22** and I will scatter in you chariots and their riders; and I will scatter in you young man and virgin, and I will scatter in you man and woman.

**28:23** And I will scatter in you shepherd and his flock, and I will scatter in you farmer and his farm, and I will scatter in you your governors and commanders.

**28:24** And I will repay Babylon and all the Chaldean inhabitants for all their evils that they did against Zion before your eyes," says the Lord.

**28:25** "Look, I am against you, destroyed mountain that destroys all the earth; and I will stretch out My hand against you and roll you down upon the rocks, and I will make you like a burned mountain,

**28:26** and they will never take from you a stone for a corner or a stone for a foundation, for you will be a desolation forever," says the Lord.

**28:27** "Raise a signal upon the earth, sound a trumpet among the nations, consecrate nations against her, give orders against her, raise kings from Me, and to the Ashkenazites; set up siege engines against her, bring up horses against her like a multitude of locusts.

**28:28** Bring up nations against her, the king of the Medes and of all the earth, his leaders and all his commanders."

**28:29** The earth shook and was in pain, because the purpose of the Lord rose up against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon a desolation and for it not to be inhabited.

**28:30** The warrior of Babylon has ceased from fighting; they will sit there in a stronghold; their might has been broken; they have become like women; her dwellings have been burned; her bars have been shattered.

**28:31** A runner will run to meet a runner, and a messenger to meet a messenger, to report to the king of Babylon that his city has been captured,

**28:32** from the end of his crossings they have been seized, and they have burned their pools with fire, and his men of war are going out.

**28:33** For thus says the Lord: "The houses of the king of Babylon will be threshed like a ripe threshing floor; yet a little while, and her harvest will come."

**28:34** "He devoured me, he divided me up, a thin darkness overtook me; Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon swallowed me up; like a dragon he filled his belly from my delicacies;

**28:35** my toils and my distresses drove me out into Babylon," she who dwells in Zion will say; "And my blood is upon the Chaldean inhabitants," Jerusalem will say.

**28:36** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Look, I will judge your adversary and execute your vengeance, and I will lay waste her sea and dry up her spring;

**28:37** and Babylon will become a desolation, and will not be inhabited.

**28:38** For together like lions they awoke, and like cubs of lions.

**28:39** In their heat I will give them a drink, and I will make them drunk so that they may fall into a stupor and sleep an eternal sleep, and they will never awake," says the Lord.

**28:40** And bring them down like lambs to slaughter, and like rams with young goats.

**28:41** How has the boast of all the earth been captured and hunted down? How has Babylon become a desolation among the nations?

**28:42** The sea has come up over Babylon with the roar of its waves, and she has been covered over.

**28:43** Her cities have become like a waterless and impassable land; not even one will dwell in it, nor will a son of man lodge in it.

**28:44** "And I will execute vengeance upon Babylon, and I will bring out from her mouth the things she swallowed, and the nations will never again be gathered to her,

**28:49** and in Babylon wounded men of all the earth will fall."

**28:50** You who are escaping from the land, go and do not stand still; you from afar, remember the Lord, and let Jerusalem come up upon your heart.

**28:51** "We have been put to shame, because we have heard our reproach; dishonor has covered our face; foreigners have entered into our holy places, into the house of the Lord."

**28:52** "Therefore, here are days," says the Lord, "and I will execute vengeance upon her carved images, and in all her land wounded men will fall.

**28:53** For though Babylon should ascend like heaven, and though she should fortify the walls with her strength, from Me those who utterly destroy her will come," says the Lord.

**28:54** A sound of an outcry in Babylon, and a great shattering in the land of the Chaldeans,

**28:55** for the Lord has utterly destroyed Babylon, and He has destroyed from her a great voice roaring like many waters; He has given her voice to destruction.

**28:56** For distress has come upon Babylon; her warriors have been captured; their bow is dismayed, for God is repaying them.

**28:57** "The Lord is repaying, and He will make drunk with drunkenness her governors and her wise men and her commanders," says the King; the Lord Almighty is His name.

**28:58** Thus says the Lord: "The wall of Babylon was made broad; it will surely be razed, and her high gates will be burned; and peoples will not labor in vain, and nations will fail in the beginning."

**28:59** The word that the Lord commanded Jeremiah the prophet to speak to Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when he went from Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon, in the fourth year of his reign; and Seraiah was ruler of gifts.

**28:60** And Jeremiah wrote all the evils that will come upon Babylon in a book, all these words that are written against Babylon.

**28:61** And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you come to Babylon, you will see and read all these words,

**28:62** and you will say, 'Lord, Lord, You have spoken against this place to destroy it utterly and for there to be no inhabitants in it, from man to beast, for it will be a desolation forever.'

**28:63** And it will be, when you finish reading this book, you will tie a stone onto it and throw it into the midst of the Euphrates,

**28:64** and you will say, 'Thus Babylon will sink and will never rise from before the Chaldeans whom I am bringing upon her.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:5** — The Greek plural ton hagion ("the holy things" or "the holy ones") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's singular "the Holy One of Israel" (Jer 51:5 MT).
- **28:16** — The Greek opening clause ("At a voice He set a roar of water") reflects a literal rendering of the Hebrew thunder idiom, and the closing clause reads phos ("light") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:16) has "wind."
- **28:20** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:20) reads "You are My war-club," the Greek uses the verb diaskorpizeis ("You scatter") and repeats en soi ("in you," which can also be read instrumentally as "by you"), aligning with "from you" (v. 20) and "your commanders" (v. 23).
- **28:27** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:27) lists the kingdoms of "Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz," the Septuagint reads the first two as the command and phrase "raise ... from Me" (arate par' emou) and renders the third as the plural gentilic tois Aschanazeois ("to the Ashkenazites").
- **28:34** — The Greek reads "a thin darkness overtook me" (katelaben me skotos lepton) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:34) reads "he has made me an empty vessel."
- **28:49** — Verses 45-48 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:45-48) are absent from the Septuagint, so verse 49 continues the sentence directly from verse 44.
- **28:58** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:58), the Septuagint includes the negative ou ("will not labor in vain") and reads en arche ("in the beginning" or "in rule") where the Hebrew has "for fire."
- **28:64** — The Greek reads ton Chaldaion ("the Chaldeans") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 51:64) reads "the disaster/evil," and omits the Masoretic closing line "Thus far are the words of Jeremiah."
