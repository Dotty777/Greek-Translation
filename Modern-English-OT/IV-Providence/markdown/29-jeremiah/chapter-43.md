## Text

**43:1** In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**43:2** "Take for yourself a scroll of a book, and write on it all the words that I have spoken to you against Jerusalem and against Judah and against all the nations, from the day I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah king of Judah and until this day.

**43:3** Perhaps the house of Judah will hear all the evils that I am planning to do to them, so that they may turn back from their evil way, and I will be merciful to their wrongdoings and to their sins."

**43:4** And Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and he wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the Lord, which He spoke to him, onto a scroll of a book.

**43:5** And Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, "I am kept under guard; I certainly cannot enter the house of the Lord.

**43:6** And you shall read from this scroll in the ears of the people in the house of the Lord on a day of fasting, and in the ears of all Judah who come from their city you shall read to them.

**43:7** Perhaps their mercy will fall before the Lord, and they will turn back from their evil way, for great is the wrath and the anger of the Lord, which He spoke against this people."

**43:8** And Baruch did according to all that Jeremiah commanded him, to read from the book the words of the Lord in the house of the Lord.

**43:9** And it happened in the eighth year of King Jehoiakim, in the ninth month, that all the people in Jerusalem and the house of Judah convened a fast before the Lord.

**43:10** And Baruch was reading from the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the Lord, in the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court, at the entrance of the new gate of the house of the Lord, and in the ears of all the people.

**43:11** And Micaiah son of Gemariah son of Shaphan heard all the words of the Lord from the book.

**43:12** And he went down to the king's house, to the chamber of the scribe, and look, all the officials were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah son of Shelemiah, and Jonathan son of Achbor, and Gemariah son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the officials.

**43:13** And Micaiah declared to them all the words that he heard while Baruch was reading in the ears of the people.

**43:14** And all the officials sent to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Nethaniah son of Shelemiah son of Cushi, saying, "The scroll from which you are reading in the ears of the people, take it in your hand and come." And Baruch took the scroll and went down to them.

**43:15** And they said to him, "Read again in our ears." And Baruch read.

**43:16** And it happened, when they heard all the words, that they took counsel, each one with his neighbor, and said, "Let us surely declare to the king all these words."

**43:17** And they asked Baruch, saying, "Where did you write all these words?"

**43:18** And Baruch said, "From his mouth Jeremiah declared to me all these words, and I was writing in a book."

**43:19** And they said to Baruch, "Go, hide yourself, you and Jeremiah; let no one know where you are."

**43:20** And they went in to the king into the court, and they gave the scroll to be kept in the chamber of Elisha, and they declared to the king all the words.

**43:21** And the king sent Jehudi to take the scroll. And he took it from the chamber of Elisha, and Jehudi read in the ears of the king and in the ears of all the officials who were standing around the king.

**43:22** And the king was sitting in the winter house, and a brazier of fire was before him.

**43:23** And it happened, as Jehudi was reading three and four columns, that he would cut them off with the scribe's razor and throw them into the fire that was on the brazier, until the entire scroll vanished into the fire that was on the brazier.

**43:24** And they did not inquire, and they did not tear their garments, the king and his servants who heard all these words.

**43:25** And Elnathan and Gedaliah advised the king to burn the scroll.

**43:26** And the king commanded Jerahmeel son of the king and Seraiah son of Azriel to seize Baruch and Jeremiah; and they hid themselves.

**43:27** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, after the king burned the scroll, all the words that Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah, saying,

**43:28** "Again, you take another scroll and write all the words that were on the scroll, which King Jehoiakim burned,

**43:29** and you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: You have burned this scroll, saying, "Why have you written on it, saying, 'The king of Babylon will surely come and destroy this land, and man and livestock will vanish from it'?"

**43:30** Therefore thus says the Lord against Jehoiakim king of Judah: He shall have no one sitting on the throne of David, and his corpse shall be thrown out in the heat of the day and in the frost of the night.

**43:31** And I will punish him and his offspring and his servants, and I will bring upon him and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem and upon the land of Judah all the evils that I spoke to them, and they did not listen.'"

**43:32** And Baruch took another scroll and wrote on it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book that Jehoiakim had burned; and still more words like these were added to it.

## Translator Notes

- **43:7** — The Greek literally reads "their mercy" (ἔλεος), an LXX rendering of the Hebrew word for "supplication" or "plea for mercy" (compare 44:20).
- **43:9** — The Septuagint dates the fast to "the eighth year" of Jehoiakim, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew text (Jer 36:9) reads "the fifth year."
- **43:12** — The Septuagint names "Jonathan" (Ἰωναθάν) son of Achbor here rather than Elnathan as in the Hebrew text, though Elnathan appears in v. 25.
- **43:20** — Here and in v. 21 the Septuagint reads the shorter name "Elisha" (Ἐλεισά) rather than "Elishama" (Ἐλεισαμά) as in v. 12.
- **43:25** — Unlike the Hebrew text, where Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urge the king not to burn the scroll, the Septuagint names only Elnathan and Gedaliah (Γοδολίας) and states that they advised the king to burn it.
