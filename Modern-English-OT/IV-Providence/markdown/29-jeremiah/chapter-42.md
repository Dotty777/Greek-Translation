## Text

**42:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the days of Jehoiakim king of Judah, saying,

**42:2** "Go to the house of Archabin, and you shall bring them into the house of the Lord, into one of the courts, and you shall give them wine to drink."

**42:3** And I brought out Jeconiah son of Jeremin, son of Chabasin, and his brothers and his sons, and all the household of Archabin,

**42:4** and I brought them into the house of the Lord, into the chamber of the sons of Jonan son of Hananiah, son of Gedaliah, the man of God, which is near the house of the rulers who are above the house of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the court.

**42:5** And I set before them a jar of wine and cups, and I said, "Drink wine."

**42:6** And they said, "We will by no means drink wine, for Jonadab son of Rechab, our father, commanded us, saying, 'You shall by no means drink wine, you and your sons, forever;

**42:7** and you shall by no means build a house, and you shall by no means sow seed, and you shall have no vineyard; for you shall dwell in tents all your days, so that you may live many days upon the land where you reside.'

**42:8** And we listened to the voice of Jonadab our father, not to drink wine all our days, we and our wives and our sons and our daughters,

**42:9** and not to build houses to dwell there; we have had neither vineyard nor field nor seed;

**42:10** and we dwelt in tents, and we listened and did according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.

**42:11** And it happened, when Nebuchadnezzar came up against the land, that we said to go in, and we went into Jerusalem from before the force of the Chaldeans and from before the force of the Assyrians; and we were dwelling there."

**42:12** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**42:13** "Thus says the Lord: Go and say to the man of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, 'Will you never receive discipline to listen to My words?

**42:14** The sons of Jonadab son of Rechab upheld the word that he commanded his children, not to drink wine, and they did not drink; and I spoke to you early in the morning, and I spoke, and you did not listen.

**42:15** And I sent to you My servants the prophets, saying, "Turn away, each one from his evil way, and make your practices better, and do not go after other gods to serve them, and you will dwell upon the land that I gave to you and to your fathers." And you did not incline your ears, and you did not listen.

**42:16** And the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab upheld the command of their father, but this people did not listen to Me.'"

**42:17** Therefore thus said the Lord: "Look, I am bringing upon Judah and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evils that I spoke against them."

**42:18** Therefore thus said the Lord: "Because the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab listened to the command of their father, to do just as their father commanded them,

**42:19** there will by no means be lacking a man from the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab standing before Me all the days of the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — Greek "Archabin" transliterates the Hebrew definite article and plural name for "the Rechabites" (compare "Rechab" in vv. 6 and 14-19); "courts" likewise follows the Greek text where the Hebrew has "chambers."
- **42:3** — The Greek names Jeconiah, Jeremin, and Chabasin correspond respectively to Jaazaniah, Jeremiah, and Habazziniah in the Hebrew text.
- **42:4** — The Greek expands the genealogy to three generations, "Jonan son of Hananiah, son of Gedaliah," where the Hebrew text has "Hanan son of Igdaliah."
- **42:11** — The Greek reads "the Assyrians," whereas the Hebrew text reads "the Arameans" (Syrians).
- **42:18** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which inserts a transition addressing the house of the Rechabites and repeats the messenger formula in v. 19, the Greek proceeds directly into a single oracle ending with "all the days of the earth" in v. 19.
