## Text

**31:1** Concerning Moab. Thus says the Lord: "Woe upon Nebo, for it has perished; Kiriathaim has been captured; Hamath and Hagath have been put to shame.

**31:2** There is no longer healing for Moab, no boasting in Heshbon; one devised evil against her: 'We have cut her off from being a nation, and she will surely cease.' Behind you a sword will march,

**31:3** for there is a sound of those crying out from Horonaim, destruction and great shattering;

**31:4** Moab has been shattered. Announce to Zoar

**31:5** that Haloth has been filled with weeping; he will go up weeping on the road of Horonaim; you have heard a cry of shattering.

**31:6** Flee and save your lives, and become like a wild donkey in a wilderness.

**31:7** Since you trusted in your strongholds, you also will be seized; and Chemosh will go out into exile, both his priests and his rulers together.

**31:8** And destruction will come upon every city; it will by no means be saved; and the valley will perish, and the plain will be utterly destroyed," just as the Lord has spoken.

**31:9** Give signs to Moab, for she will surely be set on fire, and all her cities will become an untrodden waste; from where is there an inhabitant for her?

**31:10** Cursed is the one who does the works of the Lord negligently, withdrawing his sword from blood.

**31:11** Moab has rested from boyhood, and he was trusting in his glory; he has not poured from vessel to vessel, and he has not gone into exile. Therefore his taste has remained in him, and his scent has not failed.

**31:12** "Therefore look, his days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will send to him those who tilt, and they will tilt him, and they will pulverize his vessels, and they will cut his horns to pieces,

**31:13** and Moab will be put to shame by Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was put to shame by Bethel, their hope, having trusted in them.

**31:14** How will you say, 'We are strong, and a man strong for warfare'?

**31:15** Moab has perished, his city, and his chosen young men have gone down to slaughter.

**31:16** The day of Moab is near to come, and his evil is very swift.

**31:17** Mourn for him, all you around him; all of you, proclaim his name; say, 'How has the glorious staff been shattered, the rod of greatness?'

**31:18** Come down from glory and sit in dampness, O inhabitant. Dibon is being wiped out, for Moab has perished; one ravaging your stronghold has come up against you.

**31:19** Stand by the road and watch, you who dwell in Aroer, and ask one fleeing and one escaping, and say, 'What has happened?'

**31:20** Moab has been put to shame, for he has been shattered; wail and cry out; announce in Arnon that Moab has perished,

**31:21** and judgment is coming into the land of Mishor, upon Holon, and Rephas, and Mophas,

**31:22** and upon Dibon, and upon Nebo, and upon the house of Diblathaim,

**31:23** and upon Kiriathaim, and upon the house of Gamul, and upon the house of Meon,

**31:24** and upon Kerioth, and upon Bozrah, and upon all the cities of Moab, those far and those near.

**31:25** The horn of Moab has been broken, and his arm has been shattered.

**31:26** Make him drunk, for he has magnified himself against the Lord; and Moab will clap with his hand, and he also will become an object of laughter.

**31:27** And was he not an object of mockery to you, Israel, and found in your thefts, that you were making war against him?

**31:28** Those who inhabit Moab left the cities and lived in rocks; they became like doves nesting in rocks at the mouth of a pit.

**31:29** And I have heard of the insolence of Moab—he has been exceedingly insolent—his insolence and his arrogance, and his heart has been lifted up;

**31:30** but I know his works. Is it not enough for him? Has he not acted thus?

**31:31** Therefore wail for Moab on every side; cry out for the men of Kir-hadas of drought.

**31:32** Like the weeping of Jazer I will weep for you, O vine of Oserema; your branches crossed the sea; the cities of Jazer reached to your summer fruit; upon your grape harvesters destruction has fallen;

**31:33** joy and gladness have been swept away from the Moabite land, and wine was in your winepresses; in the morning they did not tread, nor in the afternoon did these do so.

**31:34** From the cry of Heshbon to Etam their cities gave their voice, from Zoar to Horonaim, and a message of Salasia; for even the water of Nimrim will become a burnt waste.

**31:35** And I will destroy Moab," says the Lord, "as he goes up upon the altar and burns incense to his gods.

**31:36** Therefore the heart of Moab will hum like flutes; My heart will hum like a flute for shorn people; therefore what he acquired has perished from man.

**31:37** They will be shaved on every head in every place, and every beard will be shaved, and all hands will beat in mourning, and on every waist there is sackcloth,

**31:38** and on all the rooftops of Moab and in her squares—for I have shattered her," says the Lord, "like a vessel of which there is no need.

**31:39** How has she changed? How has Moab turned her back? She has been put to shame, and Moab has become an object of laughter and resentment to all those around her."

**31:40** For thus says the Lord:

**31:41** "Ekron has been captured, and the strongholds have been seized,

**31:42** and Moab will perish from being a multitude, for he has magnified himself against the Lord.

**31:43** A snare and terror and a pit are upon you, you who dwell in Moab.

**31:44** The one fleeing from before the terror will fall into the pit, and the one coming up out of the pit will be caught in the snare; for I will bring these things upon Moab in the year of their visitation."

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — Jeremiah 31 in the Septuagint corresponds to Jeremiah 48 in the Hebrew Bible. The Greek reads two place names, Hamath and Hagath, where the Hebrew text reads "the fortress [Misgab] and is dismayed."
- **31:5** — The Greek reads the place name Haloth here (and Zoar in v. 4) where the Hebrew text reads "the ascent of Luhith" (and "her little ones" in v. 4).
- **31:21** — The Greek treats Mishor (Hebrew "the tableland/plain") as a place name and reads Rephas and Mophas where the Hebrew text has Jahzah and Mephaath.
- **31:31** — The Greek reads "Kir-hadas of drought" here (and "shorn people" in v. 36) where the Hebrew text has the place name Kir-heres.
- **31:32** — The Greek reads the place name Oserema where the Hebrew text reads Sibmah.
- **31:33** — The Greek pronoun "these" (haide, mirrored by hoide in 32:30) reflects a Greek rendering of the Hebrew vintage shout hedad.
- **31:34** — The Greek reads Etam and "a message of Salasia" where the Hebrew text has Elealeh and Eglath-shelishiyah (or "a three-year-old heifer").
- **31:41** — The Greek reads Ekron here (and omits the eagle imagery of v. 40) where the Hebrew text reads Kerioth.
