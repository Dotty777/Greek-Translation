## Text

**16:1** "And you, do not take a wife," says the Lord God of Israel,

**16:2** "and no son nor daughter will be born to you in this place."

**16:3** For thus says the Lord concerning the sons and concerning the daughters who are born in this place, and concerning their mothers who have borne them, and concerning their fathers who have fathered them in this land:

**16:4** "By a diseased death they will die; they will not be lamented, and they will not be buried; they will be for an example on the face of the earth, and they will be for the wild beasts of the earth and for the birds of the sky; by the sword they will fall, and by famine they will be brought to an end."

**16:5** Thus says the Lord: "Do not enter their gathering, and do not go to lament, and do not mourn for them, because I have withdrawn My peace from this people.

**16:6** They will certainly not lament for them, nor will they make incisions, and they will not be shaved;

**16:7** and bread will certainly not be broken in their mourning for comfort over one who has died; they will not give him a cup to drink for comfort over his father and mother.

**16:8** Into a house of drinking you will not enter to sit down with them to eat and drink."

**16:9** For thus says the Lord God of Israel: "Look, I am abolishing from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride."

**16:10** And it will be, when you declare to this people all these words, and they say to you, "Why has the Lord spoken against us all these calamities? What is our injustice? And what is our sin that we have sinned before the Lord our God?"

**16:11** then you will say to them, "Because your fathers forsook Me, says the Lord, and went off after foreign gods and served them and worshiped them, and forsook Me and did not keep My law,

**16:12** and you acted more wickedly than your fathers; and look, each of you walks after the pleasing things of your evil heart so as not to obey Me.

**16:13** And I will cast you away from this land into the land that you and your fathers did not know, and you will serve other gods there, who will not give you mercy."

**16:14** "Therefore, look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and they will no longer say, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt,'

**16:15** but, 'The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel from the land of the north and from all the countries where they were driven out.' And I will restore them to their land that I gave to their fathers.

**16:16** Look, I am sending the many fishermen," says the Lord, "and they will fish for them; and after these things I will send the many hunters, and they will hunt them upon every mountain and upon every hill and out of the crevices of the rocks.

**16:17** For My eyes are upon all their ways, and their unjust deeds were not hidden before My eyes.

**16:18** And I will repay because of all their evils and their sins, by which they profaned My land with the carcasses of their abominations and with their lawless deeds, by which they trespassed against My inheritance."

**16:19** Lord, You are my strength and my help and my refuge in days of calamities; to You nations will come from the end of the earth and say, "How false are the idols our fathers acquired, and there is no benefit in them!

**16:20** Will a man make gods for himself, when these are not gods?"

**16:21** Therefore, look, I will reveal to them at this time My hand, and I will make known to them My power, and they will know that My name is the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Septuagint omits the introductory formula found in the Hebrew MT ("The word of the Lord came to me, saying") and opens directly with the Lord's command.
- **16:4** — Greek renders the Hebrew term for "dung" as paradeigma ("an example") and places the wild beasts and birds before the sword and famine.
- **16:12** — Greek ton areston ("the pleasing things") corresponds to "stubbornness" in the Hebrew MT.
- **16:13** — In the Septuagint the "other gods" are the subject of the final clause ("who will not give you mercy"), whereas the Hebrew MT has the Lord declaring that He will show no favor.
- **16:18** — The Septuagint lacks the Hebrew MT's "first" and "doubly," and reads "trespassed against" (eplemmelesan) where the MT has "filled."
