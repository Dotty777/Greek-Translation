## Text

**7:2** Hear the word of the Lord, all Judea.

**7:3** Thus says the Lord God of Israel: "Correct your ways and your practices, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.

**7:4** Do not trust in yourselves, in false words—for they will not benefit you at all—saying, 'The temple of the Lord, it is the temple of the Lord.'

**7:5** For if you truly correct your ways and your practices, and truly do justice between a man and his neighbor,

**7:6** and do not oppress the resident alien, the orphan, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not walk after foreign gods to your own harm,

**7:7** then I will cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers from eternity to eternity.

**7:8** But if you trust in false words from which you will not be benefited,

**7:9** and you murder and commit adultery and steal and swear unjustly, and were burning incense to Baal and walking after foreign gods whom you do not know, so that it goes badly for you,

**7:10** and you came and stood before Me in the house on which My name has been called, and said, 'We have refrained from doing all these abominations.'

**7:11** Is My house, on which My name has been called, a cave of robbers there before you? And I, look, I have seen," says the Lord.

**7:12** "For you went to My place that is in Shiloh, where I made My name dwell formerly, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel.

**7:13** And now, because you did all these deeds, and I spoke to you and you did not listen to Me, and I called you and you did not answer,

**7:14** I will do to the house on which My name has been called, in which you trust, and to the place that I gave to you and to your fathers, just as I did to Shiloh.

**7:15** And I will cast you away from My presence, just as I cast away your brothers, all the seed of Ephraim."

**7:16** "And you, do not pray for this people, and do not ask for them to be shown mercy, and do not plead, and do not approach Me for them, for I will not listen.

**7:17** Or do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**7:18** Their sons gather wood, and their fathers kindle a fire, and their women knead dough to make cakes for the host of heaven, and they poured out drink offerings to foreign gods in order to provoke Me to anger.

**7:19** Is it Me they are provoking to anger?" says the Lord. "Is it not themselves, so that their faces may be put to shame?"

**7:20** Therefore thus says the Lord: "Look, My anger and wrath are being poured out upon this place, and upon the people, and upon the livestock, and upon every tree of their field, and upon the produce of the ground; and it will burn and will not be quenched."

**7:21** Thus says the Lord: "Gather your burnt offerings with your sacrifices and eat meat.

**7:22** For I did not speak to your fathers and did not command them on the day I brought them up out of the land of Egypt concerning burnt offerings and sacrifice;

**7:23** rather, I commanded them this word, saying, 'Listen to My voice, and I will be God to you, and you will be a people to Me; and walk in all My ways that I command you, so that it may go well with you.'

**7:24** And they did not listen to Me and their ear did not pay attention, but they walked in the thoughts of their evil heart, and turned backward and not forward,

**7:25** from the day their fathers came out of the land of Egypt even until this day. And I sent out to you all My servants the prophets, by day and at dawn, and I sent them,

**7:26** and they did not listen to Me and their ear did not pay attention, and they stiffened their neck more than their fathers.

**7:27** And you shall say this word to them:

**7:28** 'This is the nation that did not listen to the voice of the Lord and did not accept discipline; faithfulness has vanished from their mouth.'

**7:29** Shear your head and cast it away, and take up a lament on your lips, for the Lord has rejected and thrust away the generation that does these things.

**7:30** For the sons of Judah have done what is evil before Me," says the Lord. "They have set their abominations in the house on which My name has been called, to defile it;

**7:31** and they have built the altar of Topheth, which is in the Valley of the son of Hinnom, to burn up their sons and their daughters in fire, which I did not command them and did not consider in My heart.

**7:32** Therefore, look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and they will no longer say, 'Altar of Topheth' and 'Valley of the son of Hinnom,' but 'The Valley of the Killed'; and they will bury in Topheth because there is no place,

**7:33** and the corpses of this people will be food for the birds of the sky and for the wild beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to drive them away.

**7:34** And from the city of Judah and from the thoroughfares of Jerusalem I will put an end to the voice of those celebrating and the voice of those rejoicing, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, for the whole land will become a desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Greek text begins this oracle directly at 7:2 without the temple-gate narrative of the Hebrew Masoretic Text (7:1-2a), and addresses Judea rather than Judah.
- **7:4** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek adds "in yourselves" and "for they will not benefit you at all," and repeats "the temple of the Lord" twice rather than three times.
- **7:10** — The Greek reads "We have refrained from doing all these abominations" (a hypocritical claim of innocence), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "We are delivered—to do all these abominations."
- **7:18** — The Greek identifies the recipient of the sacrificial cakes as "the host of heaven" rather than "the queen of heaven" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:29** — The Greek reads "on your lips" rather than "on the bare heights," and "the generation that does these things" rather than "the generation of His wrath."
