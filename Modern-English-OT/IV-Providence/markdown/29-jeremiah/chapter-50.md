## Text

**50:1** And it happened, when Jeremiah finished speaking to the people all the words of the Lord with which the Lord had sent him to them, all these words,

**50:2** that Azariah son of Maaseiah and Johanan son of Kareah and all the men who addressed Jeremiah spoke, saying, "You are lying! The Lord has not sent you to us, saying, 'Do not enter into Egypt to dwell there,'

**50:3** but rather Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, so that you may give us into the hands of the Chaldeans to put us to death and for us to be exiled to Babylon."

**50:4** And Johanan and all the commanders of the force and all the people did not listen to the voice of the Lord, to dwell in the land of Judah.

**50:5** And Johanan and all the commanders of the force took all the remnant of Judah who had returned to dwell in the land,

**50:6** the mighty men and the women and the rest and the daughters of the king, and the souls whom Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, and Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah,

**50:7** and they entered into Egypt, because they did not listen to the voice of the Lord, and they entered into Tahpanhes.

**50:8** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

**50:9** "Take for yourself large stones and hide them in the entranceway, at the gate of the house of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes, before the eyes of men of Judah,

**50:10** and you will say, 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am sending and will bring Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will set his throne over these stones that you hid, and he will lift up weapons against them,

**50:11** and he will enter and strike the land of Egypt: those for death, to death, and those for exile, to exile, and those for the sword, to the sword;

**50:12** and he will kindle a fire in the houses of their gods, and he will burn them up and take them into exile, and he will delouse the land of Egypt just as a shepherd delouses his garment. And he will go out in peace,

**50:13** and he will shatter the pillars of Heliopolis that are in On, and he will burn up their houses with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **50:2** — The Greek reads Maaseiah (where the Hebrew text has Hoshaiah) and "all the men who addressed Jeremiah" (where the Hebrew text has "all the arrogant men").
- **50:9** — Greek en prothyrois ("in the entranceway") differs from the Hebrew text's "in the mortar in the brick terrace."
- **50:10** — The Greek reads "that you hid" (second person, addressing Jeremiah) and "he will lift up weapons against them," whereas the Hebrew text reads "that I have hidden" and "he will spread his canopy over them."
- **50:12** — Greek phtheirizo literally means "delouse" (pick lice off), a well-known Septuagint rendering where the Hebrew text reads "wrap himself in."
- **50:13** — The Greek identifies the site as Heliopolis (the city of the Sun) in On, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt."
