## Text

**9:1** Who would give me an outermost lodging place in the wilderness, and I will leave my people and go away from them? For they all commit adultery, an assembly of treacherous ones,

**9:2** and they bent their tongue like a bow; falsehood and not faithfulness prevailed upon the land, for they went out from evils to evils, and they did not know Me.

**9:3** Guard yourselves, each one from his neighbor, and do not trust in their brothers; for every brother will trip up by the heel, and every friend will walk deceitfully.

**9:4** Each one will mock his friend; they will by no means speak truth. Their tongue has learned to speak falsehoods; they acted unjustly and did not cease so as to turn back.

**9:5** Usury upon usury, and deceit upon deceit; they were not willing to know Me.

**9:6** Therefore, thus says the Lord: "Look, I will refine them by fire and test them, for I will act because of the wickedness of the daughter of My people.

**9:7** A wounding arrow is their tongue; deceitful are the words of their mouth. To his neighbor he speaks peaceable things, and within himself he holds enmity.

**9:8** Shall I not bring punishment for these things," says the Lord, "or on a people such as this shall My soul not take vengeance?

**9:9** Over the mountains take up wailing, and over the paths of the wilderness a dirge, for they have failed because there are no people; they did not hear the sound of possessions. From the birds of the sky even to the livestock, they panicked, they were gone.

**9:10** And I will give Jerusalem over to deportation and into a dwelling place of dragons, and the cities of Judah I will make a desolation, without being inhabited."

**9:11** Who is the man of understanding? Then let him understand this; and the one to whom the word of the mouth of the Lord comes, let him declare to you for what reason the land has perished; it has been set on fire like a wilderness, without being passed through.

**9:12** And the Lord said to me, "Because they have forsaken My law which I set before them, and did not listen to My voice,

**9:13** but walked after the pleasing things of their evil heart and after the idols that their fathers taught them.

**9:14** Therefore, thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'Look, I will feed them distresses, and I will give them water of gall to drink,

**9:15** and I will scatter them among the nations whom they and their fathers did not know, and I will send the sword upon them until I completely consume them by it.'"

**9:16** Thus says the Lord: "Call the mourning women, and let them come, and send to the skilled women, and let them speak out,

**9:17** and let them take up a dirge over you, and let your eyes pour down tears, and let your eyelids flow with water,

**9:18** for a voice of lamentation was heard in Zion: 'How we have been ruined! We have been greatly put to shame, because we have forsaken the land and have cast away our dwellings.'"

**9:19** Hear now, women, the word of God, and let your ears receive the words of His mouth, and teach your daughters lamentation, and each woman her neighbor a dirge.

**9:20** For death has climbed up through your windows; it has entered into your land to wipe out little children from outside and young men from the squares.

**9:21** And the dead bodies of people will be as an example upon the face of the field of your land, like grass behind a harvester, and there will be no one gathering.

**9:22** Thus says the Lord: "Do not let the wise man boast in his wisdom, and do not let the strong man boast in his strength, and do not let the rich man boast in his wealth,

**9:23** but rather let the one who boasts boast in this: to understand and to know that I am the Lord, who practices mercy and judgment and righteousness upon the earth; for in these things is My will," says the Lord.

**9:24** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will bring punishment upon all who are circumcised in their foreskins,

**9:25** upon Egypt and upon Idumea and upon Edom, and upon the sons of Ammon and upon the sons of Moab, and upon everyone who shaves around his face, those who dwell in the wilderness; for all the nations are uncircumcised in flesh, and all the house of Israel are uncircumcised in their heart."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — Greek reads "did not cease so as to turn back" and opens 9:5 with "Usury upon usury," diverging from the Hebrew MT ("they weary themselves committing iniquity; your dwelling is in the midst of deceit").
- **9:9** — Greek reads "the sound of possessions" where MT has "the sound of livestock," and in 9:10 reads "deportation" and "dragons" where MT has "heaps of ruins" and "jackals."
- **9:21** — Greek reads "as an example" and "grass" where MT has "like dung" and "sheaves," and lacks the introductory speech formula found at the start of MT 9:22.
- **9:25** — Greek lists "Idumea" (Idoumaia) alongside "Edom" where MT has "Judah" alongside "Edom," and explicitly adds "in flesh" in contrast to 9:24 ("circumcised in their foreskins").
