## Text

**33:1** In the beginning of King Jehoiakim son of Josiah, this word came from the Lord.

**33:2** Thus says the Lord: "Stand in the court of the house of the Lord, and you shall proclaim to all the Judeans and to all who come to worship in the house of the Lord all the words that I have commanded you to proclaim to them; do not take away a word.

**33:3** Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each one from his wicked way, and I will cease from the evils that I am planning to do to them because of their wicked practices.

**33:4** And you shall say, 'Thus says the Lord: If you do not listen to Me, to walk in My statutes which I set before you,

**33:5** to heed the words of My servants the prophets, whom I send to you at dawn, and I sent them, and you did not listen to Me;

**33:6** then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make the city a curse for all the nations of all the earth.'"

**33:7** And the priests and the false prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the Lord.

**33:8** And it happened, when Jeremiah had finished speaking all that the Lord had commanded him to speak to all the people, that the priests and the false prophets and all the people seized him, saying, "You shall surely die,

**33:9** because you have prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, 'This house shall be like Shiloh, and this city shall be made desolate, without inhabitants.'" And all the people were assembled against Jeremiah in the house of the Lord.

**33:10** And the rulers of Judah heard this word, and they went up from the house of the king to the house of the Lord, and sat at the entrance of the New Gate.

**33:11** And the priests and the false prophets said to the rulers and to all the people, "A judgment of death for this man, because he has prophesied against this city, just as you have heard with your ears."

**33:12** And Jeremiah spoke to the rulers and to all the people, saying, "The Lord sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.

**33:13** And now, make your ways and your deeds better, and listen to the voice of the Lord, and the Lord will cease from the evils that He has spoken against you.

**33:14** And look, I am in your hands; do to me as is beneficial and as is better for you.

**33:15** Only, you shall surely know that if you put me to death, you bring innocent blood upon yourselves and upon this city and upon those who dwell in it; for in truth the Lord has sent me to you to speak all these words into your ears."

**33:16** And the rulers and all the people said to the priests and to the false prophets, "There is no judgment of death for this man, because he has spoken to us in the name of the Lord our God."

**33:17** And some men from the elders of the land rose up and said to all the assembly of the people,

**33:18** "Micah the Moreshethite was in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and he said to all the people of Judah, 'Thus says the Lord: Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall become an untrodden place, and the mountain of the house a forest grove.'

**33:19** Did Hezekiah and all Judah put him to death at all? Was it not that they feared the Lord, and that they pleaded before the face of the Lord, and the Lord ceased from the evils that He had spoken against them? And we have done great evils against our own souls."

**33:20** And there was a man prophesying in the name of the Lord, Uriah son of Shemaiah, from Kiriath-jearim, and he prophesied concerning this land according to all the words of Jeremiah.

**33:21** And King Jehoiakim and all the rulers heard all his words, and they sought to kill him; and Uriah heard and went into Egypt.

**33:22** And the king sent out men to Egypt,

**33:23** and they brought him out from there and brought him in to the king; and he struck him down with a sword, and threw him into the tomb of the sons of his people.

**33:24** However, the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he would not be handed over into the hands of the people, nor put to death.

## Translator Notes

- **33:1** — Greek reads "In the beginning of King Jehoiakim" rather than "the reign of Jehoiakim" (MT 26:1); in the Septuagint chapter arrangement, Jeremiah 33-35 correspond to MT Jeremiah 26-28.
- **33:2** — Greek reads "all the Judeans" where MT 26:2 has "all the cities of Judah." In v. 3 (and vv. 13, 19), Greek translates the Hebrew for God relenting with the verb "cease" (pauomai).
- **33:7** — Here and in vv. 8, 11, 16 (as well as 34:9 and 35:1), the Greek explicitly designates Jeremiah's opponents as "false prophets" (pseudoprophetai), where the Hebrew has "prophets."
- **33:9** — Greek opens with "because" (hoti) rather than the Hebrew question "Why" (MT 26:9), linking the accusation directly to the threat of death in v. 8.
