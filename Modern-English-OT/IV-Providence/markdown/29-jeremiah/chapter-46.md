## Text

**46:1** And it came about in the ninth month of Zedekiah king of Judah that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came against Jerusalem, and they were besieging it.

**46:2** And in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth of the month, the city was breached.

**46:3** And all the leaders of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the Middle Gate: Marganasar, and Samagoth, and Nabusachar, and Nabusaris, Nagargasnaser, Rabamath, and the rest of the commanders of the king of Babylon.

**46:14** And they sent and took Jeremiah out of the court of the guard, and gave him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, and they brought him out, and he remained among the people.

**46:15** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in the court of the guard, saying,

**46:16** "Go and say to Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Look, I am bringing My words upon this city for evil and not for good,

**46:17** and I will save you on that day, and I will certainly not give you into the hands of the men whose presence you fear.

**46:18** For I will certainly save you, and by the sword you will certainly not fall; and your life will be as a find, because you trusted in Me,'" says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — The Greek reads "in the ninth month of Zedekiah," whereas the Hebrew text reads "in the ninth year of Zedekiah... in the tenth month."
- **46:3** — Marganasar, Samagoth, Nabusachar, Nabusaris, Nagargasnaser, and Rabamath are the Septuagint's distinct transliterations of the Babylonian officials' names and titles, differing in form from the Hebrew text.
- **46:14** — In the Septuagint, the narrative passes directly from verse 3 to verse 14, omitting the account of Zedekiah's flight, capture, and blinding found in Hebrew Jeremiah 39:4-13.
