## Text

**38:1** "At that time," said the Lord, "I will be God to the family of Israel, and they will be a people to Me."

**38:2** Thus said the Lord: "I found warmth in the wilderness with those who perished by the sword; go, and do not destroy Israel."

**38:3** The Lord appeared to him from afar: "With an eternal love I have loved you; therefore I have drawn you into compassion.

**38:4** For I will build you, and you will be built, virgin Israel; you will take up your tambourine and go out with a gathering of those making merry.

**38:5** For you have planted vineyards on the mountains of Samaria; plant and praise,

**38:6** for there is a day of calling by those making a defense on the mountains of Ephraim: 'Arise and go up to Zion, to the Lord our God.'"

**38:7** For thus said the Lord to Jacob: "Be glad and neigh over the head of the nations; make it heard and praise; say, 'The Lord has saved His people, the remnant of Israel.'

**38:8** Look, I am bringing them from the north, and I will gather them from the end of the earth at the feast of Passover; and you will bear a great crowd, and they will return here.

**38:9** With weeping they went out, and with consolation I will bring them back, lodging them by channels of water on a straight path, and they will by no means go astray on it; for I have become a father to Israel, and Ephraim is My firstborn."

**38:10** Hear the words of the Lord, nations, and declare to the more distant islands; say, "He who winnowed Israel will gather him, and will guard him as one who tends his flock."

**38:11** For the Lord has ransomed Jacob; He has rescued him from the hand of those stronger than he.

**38:12** And they will come and be glad on Mount Zion, and they will come to the good things of the Lord, to a land of grain and wine and fruits and cattle and sheep; and their soul will be like a fruitful tree, and they will not hunger anymore.

**38:13** Then virgins will rejoice in a gathering of young men, and old men will rejoice; and I will turn their mourning into joy, and I will make them glad.

**38:14** I will magnify and saturate the soul of the priests, the sons of Levi, and My people will be filled with My good things.

**38:15** Thus said the Lord: "A voice was heard in Ramah, of lamentation and weeping and wailing; Rachel, weeping bitterly, was unwilling to cease over her sons, because they are no more."

**38:16** Thus said the Lord: "Let your voice cease from weeping, and your eyes from your tears, for there is a reward for your works, and they will return from the land of enemies;

**38:17** there is permanence for your children."

**38:18** "I have surely heard Ephraim wailing: 'You disciplined me, and I was disciplined; I, like a calf, was not taught. Turn me back, and I will return, for You are the Lord my God.

**38:19** For after my captivity I repented, and after I came to know, I groaned over days of shame, and I showed to You that I received reproach from my youth.'

**38:20** Ephraim is a beloved son to Me, a delightful child; for because My words are in him, I will surely remember him; therefore I have hastened over him; I will surely have mercy on him," says the Lord.

**38:21** Station yourself, Zion, make retribution, set your heart on the shoulders; return by the path on which you went, virgin Israel, return to your cities mourning.

**38:22** How long will you turn away, dishonored daughter? For the Lord has created salvation for a new planting; in salvation people will go about.

**38:23** For thus said the Lord: "Again they will speak this word in the land of Judah and in its cities, when I turn back its captivity: 'Blessed is the Lord upon His righteous, holy mountain';

**38:24** and those dwelling in the cities of Judah and in all its land together with a farmer, and it will be lifted up with a flock.

**38:25** For I have saturated every thirsty soul, and every hungry soul I have filled."

**38:26** Because of this I woke up and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me.

**38:27** "Therefore, look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when I will sow Israel and Judah with the seed of man and the seed of beast.

**38:28** And it will be that just as I watched over them to tear down and to afflict, so I will watch over them to build and to plant," says the Lord.

**38:29** In those days they will by no means say, "The fathers ate sour grapes, and the teeth of the children were set on edge";

**38:30** but each one will die in his own sin, and the teeth of the one who eats the sour grapes will be set on edge.

**38:31** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,

**38:32** not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took hold of their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, because they did not remain in My covenant, and I disregarded them," says the Lord.

**38:33** "For this is My covenant that I will make with the house of Israel: After those days," says the Lord, "I will surely put My laws into their mind, and upon their hearts I will write them; and I will be God to them, and they will be a people to Me.

**38:34** And they will not teach each one his fellow citizen and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' for all will know Me, from the least of them to the greatest of them, because I will be merciful toward their injustices, and their sins I will by no means remember anymore."

**38:35** "If heaven is lifted up on high," says the Lord, "and if the foundation of the earth is brought low below, I also will not reject the family of Israel," says the Lord, "for all that they have done."

**38:36** Thus said the Lord, who gave the sun for the light of the day, the moon and stars for the light of the night, and a cry in the sea, and its waves roared; the Lord Almighty is His name:

**38:37** "If these laws cease from before My face," says the Lord, "then the family of Israel will also cease from being a nation before My face all the days."

**38:38** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when a city will be built for the Lord from the Tower of Hanamel to the Corner Gate,

**38:39** and its measurement will go out opposite them as far as the hills of Gareb, and it will be encircled all around with chosen stones,

**38:40** and all Hasaremoth as far as Nachal Kidron, to the corner of the Horse Gate on the east, a sanctuary to the Lord; and never again will it fail, and it will by no means be torn down forever."

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — The Greek reads 'I found warmth in the wilderness with those who perished by the sword,' a distinctive Septuagint rendering differing from the Masoretic Text ('the people who survived the sword found grace').
- **38:8** — The Greek reads 'at the feast of Passover' (en heorte phasek), a notable Septuagint reading where the Masoretic Text has 'among them the blind and the lame.'
- **38:21** — The Greek reads 'Zion,' 'make retribution,' 'set your heart on the shoulders,' and 'mourning,' reflecting distinctive Septuagint renderings where the Masoretic Text speaks of setting up road markers and signposts toward the highway.
- **38:35** — Verses 35-37 follow a different order from the Masoretic Text (31:37, 35, 36), and verse 35 uniquely affirms 'I also will not reject the family of Israel' rather than the conditional rejection found in the Masoretic Text.
- **38:38** — The Greek names 'Hanamel' (Hanameel) where the Masoretic Text reads 'Hananel.'
- **38:40** — The Greek retains two untranslated Hebrew geographical terms as 'Hasaremoth' (hasaremoth, corresponding to the fields/terraces) and 'Nachal Kidron' (nachal Kedron, 'wadi/brook of Kidron').
