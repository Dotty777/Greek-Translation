## Text

**15:1** And the Lord said to me, "Even if Moses and Samuel stand before My face, My soul is not toward them; send this people away, and let them go out.

**15:2** And it will be, if they say to you, 'Where shall we go out?' then you will say to them, 'Thus says the Lord: As many as are for death, to death; and as many as are for the sword, to the sword; and as many as are for famine, to famine; and as many as are for captivity, to captivity.

**15:3** And I will punish them with four kinds, says the Lord: the sword for slaughter, and the dogs for tearing apart, and the wild beasts of the earth and the birds of the sky for devouring and destruction.

**15:4** And I will deliver them into distresses for all the kingdoms of the earth because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, concerning all that he did in Jerusalem.

**15:5** Who will have pity on you, Jerusalem? And who will be dismayed over you? Or who will turn back for your peace?

**15:6** You turned away from Me, says the Lord; you will go backward. And I will stretch out My hand and destroy you, and I will no longer spare them.

**15:7** And I will scatter them in dispersion; in the gates of My people they were made childless; they destroyed My people because of their evils.

**15:8** Their widows were multiplied beyond the sand of the sea; they brought upon a mother young men, misery at midday; they cast upon her suddenly trembling and haste.

**15:9** She who bears seven was emptied; her soul grew faint; the sun went down for her while the day was still at midday; she was put to shame and disgraced; the rest of them I will give to the sword before their enemies.'"

**15:10** Woe is me, mother! As what did you bear me? A man disputing and contending in all the land! I neither gave benefit, nor did anyone benefit me; my strength failed among those who curse me.

**15:11** May it be so, Master, while they prosper, if I did not stand before You in the time of their calamities and in the time of their affliction, for good against the enemy.

**15:12** Will iron be known? And a bronze covering is your strength.

**15:13** And your treasures I will give for plunder, as an exchange because of all your sins and in all your borders.

**15:14** And I will enslave you all around to your enemies in the land that you did not know; for a fire has been kindled from My wrath; upon you it will burn.

**15:15** Lord, remember me and visit me, and acquit me from those who pursue me, not in forbearance; know that I received reproach on Your account

**15:16** from those who reject Your words; bring an end to them, and Your word will be to me for gladness and joy of my heart, because Your name has been called upon me, Lord Almighty.

**15:17** I did not sit in their assembly as they made merry, but I stood in awe before the presence of Your hand; I sat alone, because I was filled with bitterness.

**15:18** Why do those who grieve me prevail over me? My wound is severe; from where will I be healed? Indeed it has become to me like deceitful water, having no reliability.

**15:19** Therefore, thus says the Lord: "If you turn back, then I will restore you, and you will stand before My face; and if you bring out what is precious from what is worthy, you will be like My mouth; and they will turn back to you, and you will not turn back to them.

**15:20** And I will make you to this people like a fortified bronze wall; and they will fight against you, and they will certainly not prevail against you, because I am with you to save you

**15:21** and to rescue you from the hand of evil ones, and I will redeem you from the hand of pestilent ones."

## Translator Notes

- **15:10** — Greek reads "I neither gave benefit, nor did anyone benefit me; my strength failed among those who curse me," differing from the Hebrew MT ("I have not lent, nor have they lent to me; all of them curse me").
- **15:11** — In the Septuagint Jeremiah speaks here, addressing God as "Master" (despota) and protesting his intercession while his enemies prosper, whereas the Hebrew MT attributes this verse to the Lord.
- **15:16** — Greek reads "from those who reject Your words; bring an end to them," continuing the sentence from verse 15, where the Hebrew MT has "Your words were found, and I ate them."
- **15:18** — The feminine verb phrase refers back to Jeremiah's "wound" rather than addressing God directly ("You have become to me") as in the Hebrew MT.
- **15:19** — This Greek text reads axiou ("worthy") rather than anaxiou ("unworthy") or the Hebrew MT ("worthless").
