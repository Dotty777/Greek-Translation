## Text

**22:1** Thus says the Lord: "Go and go down to the house of the king of Judah, and you shall speak this word there,

**22:2** and you shall say, 'Hear the word of the Lord, king of Judah who sits on the throne of David, you and your house and your people and those who enter by these gates:

**22:3** Thus says the Lord: Do justice and righteousness, and rescue the one who has been plundered from the hand of the one who wrongs him, and do not oppress the resident alien and the orphan and the widow, and do not act impiously, and do not shed innocent blood in this place.

**22:4** For if you indeed do this word, then there will enter through the gates of this house kings sitting on the throne of David and riding on chariots and horses, they and their servants and their people.

**22:5** But if you do not do these words, I have sworn by Myself, says the Lord, that this house will become a desolation.'"

**22:6** For thus says the Lord against the house of the king of Judah: "You are Gilead to Me, the summit of Lebanon; surely I will make you into a wilderness, cities that will not be inhabited;

**22:7** and I will bring upon you a destroying man and his axe, and they will cut down your choice cedars and throw them into the fire.

**22:8** And nations will pass through this city, and each one will say to his neighbor, 'Why has the Lord done thus to this great city?'

**22:9** And they will say, 'Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshiped foreign gods and served them.'"

**22:10** Do not weep for the one who has died, nor lament him; weep bitterly for the one who goes away, because he is not returning again, nor will he see the land of his native country.

**22:11** For thus says the Lord concerning Shallum son of Josiah, who reigned in place of Josiah his father, who went out from this place: "He will not return there again,

**22:12** but in the place where I led him into exile, there he will die, and this land he will not see again."

**22:13** The one who builds his house not with righteousness and his upper rooms not with justice will work with his neighbor for nothing, and will by no means pay him his wages.

**22:14** You have built for yourself a well-proportioned house, airy upper rooms pierced with windows and paneled in cedar and painted with vermilion.

**22:15** Will you reign, because you are provoked against Ahaz your father? They will not eat and they will not drink; it is better for you to do justice and righteousness.

**22:16** They did not know; they did not judge the cause of the humble nor the cause of the poor. Is this not your not knowing Me, says the Lord?

**22:17** Look, your eyes and your heart are not good, but are on your greed, and on innocent blood to shed it, and on wrongdoing and on murder to do them.

**22:18** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: "And for this man they will by no means mourn him, 'Ah, brother!' nor will they weep for him, 'Alas, lord!'

**22:19** With the burial of a donkey he will be buried; swept away, he will be thrown beyond the gate of Jerusalem."

**22:20** Go up to Lebanon and cry out, and in Bashan lift up your voice, and shout to the far side of the sea, because all your lovers have been crushed.

**22:21** I spoke to you in your transgression, and you said, "I will not listen." This has been your way from your youth; you have not listened to My voice.

**22:22** The wind will shepherd all your shepherds, and your lovers will go out in captivity, for then you will be ashamed and dishonored because of all those who love you.

**22:23** You who dwell in Lebanon, who nest in the cedars, you will groan deeply when pains come upon you like those of a woman giving birth.

**22:24** "As I live," says the Lord, "even if Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were indeed a signet ring on My right hand, from there I will tear you off,

**22:25** and I will hand you over into the hands of those who seek your life, whose face you dread, into the hands of the Chaldeans.

**22:26** And I will hurl you and your mother who bore you into a land where you were not born, and there you will die;

**22:27** but to the land for which they pray in their souls they will by no means return."

**22:28** Jeconiah has been dishonored like a vessel for which there is no use, because he has been cast out and thrown into a land that he did not know.

**22:29** Land, land, hear the word of the Lord:

**22:30** "Write this man down as an outcast man, because no one from his offspring will flourish sitting on the throne of David, ruling any longer in Judah."

## Translator Notes

- **22:6** — The Greek conditional ean me ("if I do not...") represents a Hebrew oath formula expressing emphatic certainty ("surely I will...").
- **22:13** — The Greek lacks "Woe to" at the opening and renders the Hebrew idiom for exacting unpaid labor from a neighbor literally as "will work with his neighbor for nothing."
- **22:15** — Where the Hebrew reads "competing in cedar; did not your father eat and drink?", the Greek reads "against Ahaz your father" and shifts to negative statements ("They will not eat and they will not drink"), continuing with negative verbs in 22:16 ("they did not judge... your not knowing Me").
- **22:20** — Where the Hebrew has the place name Abarim, the Greek translates the term as "the far side of the sea"; in 22:21-22 it also reads "in your transgression" (Hebrew: "in your prosperity") and "all those who love you" (Hebrew: "all your wickedness").
- **22:28** — Unlike the Hebrew's rhetorical questions about Coniah and his offspring, the Greek makes a statement about Jeconiah alone, and in 22:30 calls him ekkerykton ("an outcast/banished man") rather than "childless."
