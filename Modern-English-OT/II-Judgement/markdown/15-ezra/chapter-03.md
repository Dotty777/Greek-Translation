## Text

**3:1** And the seventh month arrived, and the Israelites were in their cities, and the people were gathered together as one man to Jerusalem.

**3:2** And Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brothers rose up, and they built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings on it, according to the things written in the law of Moses, the man of God.

**3:3** And they prepared the altar on its prepared base, and a burnt offering went up on it to the Lord in the morning and at evening.

**3:4** And they kept the feast of booths according to what is written, and offered burnt offerings day by day by number according to the ordinance, each day's requirement day by day;

**3:5** and after this, continual burnt offerings, and those for the new moons and for all the consecrated feasts, and for everyone who willingly offered a freewill offering to the Lord.

**3:6** On the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the Lord; and the foundation of the house of the Lord had not been laid.

**3:7** And they gave silver to the stonecutters and to the carpenters, and food and drink and oil to the Sedamin and to the Sorin, to bring cedar timbers from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, according to the permission of Cyrus king of the Persians for them.

**3:8** And in the second year of their coming to the house of God, to Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak and the rest of the brothers, the priests and the Levites, and all those coming from the captivity to Jerusalem made a beginning, and they appointed the Levites from twenty years old and upward over the works of the Lord.

**3:9** And Jeshua and his sons and his brothers, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, stood over those doing the works in the house of God; the sons of Henadad, their sons and their brothers, the Levites.

**3:10** And they laid the foundation to build the house of the Lord; and the priests stood robed with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, to praise the Lord according to the directions of David king of Israel.

**3:11** And they responded in praise and thanksgiving to the Lord: "For it is good, for His mercy is forever upon Israel." And all the people sounded a loud shout to praise the Lord at the laying of the foundation of the house of the Lord.

**3:12** And many of the priests and the Levites and leaders of the fathers' houses, the older men who had seen the first house on its foundation, and this house before their eyes, were weeping with a loud voice, and a crowd was shouting with gladness to lift up a song.

**3:13** And the people did not distinguish the sound of the shouting of gladness from the sound of the weeping, for the people cried out with a loud voice, and it was heard even from afar.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Greek text omits "the priests, and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and his brothers," which appear in the Hebrew text.
- **3:3** — The Greek text omits the clause about dread of the peoples of the lands found in the Hebrew text and uses an intransitive singular verb ("a burnt offering went up").
- **3:7** — Greek Sedamein and Sorein transliterate the Hebrew terms corresponding to the Sidonians and Tyrians.
- **3:10** — The Greek text reads "laid the foundation to build" (taking the Hebrew participle for "builders" as an infinitive), omits "with cymbals," and expresses the liturgical-order idiom literally as epi cheiras Dauid ("upon the hands of David," meaning "according to the directions/order of David").
- **3:11** — The Greek text reads neuter agathon ("it is good") rather than masculine ("He is good"); similarly in v. 12 it reads "a song" (oden) where the Hebrew has "the voice."
