## Text

**5:1** And Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo prophesied a prophecy to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel over them.

**5:2** Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak rose up and began to build the house of God that is in Jerusalem, and with them were the prophets of God, helping them.

**5:3** At that very time Tattenai, governor beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai and their fellow servants came to them and said such things to them: "Who issued you a decree to build the house and to finish this outfitting?"

**5:4** Then they said these things to them: "What are the names of the men who are building this city?"

**5:5** And the eyes of God were upon the captivity of Judah, and they did not stop them until a report was brought to Darius; and then it was sent to the tax collector concerning this.

**5:6** The copy of the letter that Tattenai, governor of the region beyond, and Shethar-bozenai and their fellow servants, the Apharsachites beyond the River, sent to King Darius;

**5:7** they sent a report to him, and these things were written in it:

**5:8** "To King Darius, all peace. Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judea, to the house of the great God, and it is being built with choice stones, and timbers are being placed in the walls, and that work is being skillfully done and is prospering in their hands.

**5:9** Then we asked those elders and spoke to them like this: 'Who issued you a decree to build this outfitting and to finish it?'

**5:10** And we asked them their names to make them known to you, so as to write down the names of the men who were their leaders.

**5:11** And they answered us with this word, saying, 'We are servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are building the house that had been built many years before this, and a great king of Israel built it and finished it for them.

**5:12** But after our fathers provoked the God of heaven to anger, He gave them into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he destroyed this house and deported the people to Babylon.

**5:13** However, in the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus issued a decree that this house of God be built.

**5:14** And the silver and gold vessels of God, which Nebuchadnezzar brought out from the house that is in Jerusalem and carried away to the temple of the king, he gave to Bagasar the treasurer, who was over the treasury,

**5:15** and he said to him, "Take all the vessels; go, put them in the house that is in Jerusalem, in their own place."

**5:16** Then that Sarbagar came and laid the foundations of the house of God that is in Jerusalem; and from that time until now it has been built, and it has not been finished.'

**5:17** And now, if it is good to the king, let a search be made in the treasure house of the king of Babylon, so that you may know that a decree came from King Cyrus to build that house of God that is in Jerusalem; and when the king knows concerning this, let him send to us."

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — Greek choregia ("outfitting" or "equipment," also in v. 9) translates a rare Aramaic architectural term for structure or wall; v. 9 also omits "this house."
- **5:4** — The Greek text reads third-person "they said" and "this city" where the Aramaic text has "we said" and "this building."
- **5:5** — Greek ten aichmalosin Iouda ("the captivity of Judah") reflects reading the Aramaic consonantal text for "elders of the Jews" as "captivity."
- **5:14** — Greek Bagasar (and Sarbagar in v. 16) are distinct Greek forms corresponding to Sheshbazzar in the Aramaic text, and the Greek describes him as "the treasurer, who was over the treasury" rather than "governor."
- **5:17** — The Greek text reads "so that you may know that" (rather than "whether") and "when the king knows" (gnous) where the Aramaic has "the king's pleasure."
