## Text

**7:1** And after the events, in the reign of Artaxerxes, king of the Persians, Ezra went up, the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,

**7:2** the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,

**7:3** the son of Samariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,

**7:4** the son of Zerahiah, the son of Saouia, the son of Bukki,

**7:5** the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, the ancestral priest.

**7:6** He went up from Babylon, and he was a swift scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord, the God of Israel, gave; and the king gave to him, because the hand of his God was upon him in everything that he sought.

**7:7** And some of the sons of Israel, and of the priests, and of the Levites, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

**7:8** And they came to Jerusalem in the fifth month; this was the seventh year of the king.

**7:9** For on the first day of the fifth month they came to Jerusalem, because the hand of his God was good upon him.

**7:10** For Ezra had set in his heart to seek the law, and to do and to teach statutes and judgment in Israel.

**7:11** And this is the copy of the edict that Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the book of the words of the commandments of the Lord and of His statutes over Israel:

**7:12** "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra, scribe of the law of the God of heaven. The word and the answer had been completed.

**7:13** A decree has been issued by me that everyone in my kingdom from the people of Israel and the priests and the Levites who is willing to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

**7:14** From the presence of the king and the seven counselors, he was sent to inspect Judea and Jerusalem by the law of their God that is in your hand,

**7:15** and for the house of the Lord, silver and gold that the king and the counselors have freely offered to the Lord of Israel, who dwells in Jerusalem,

**7:16** and all the silver and gold that you may find in all the province of Babylon, with the freewill offering of the people and the priests who freely offer for the house of God that is in Jerusalem;

**7:17** and everything that comes in, promptly enter this in this book: young bulls, rams, lambs, and their sacrifices and their drink offerings; and you will offer them upon the altar of the house of your God that is in Jerusalem.

**7:18** And further, did it seem good to you and your brothers to act with the rest of the silver and the gold? Do as is pleasing to your God.

**7:19** And the vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of God, deliver before God in Jerusalem.

**7:20** And the rest of the need of the house of God, whatever seems good to you to give, you will give from the treasury houses of the king.

**7:21** And from me, I myself, King Artaxerxes, have issued a decree to all the treasuries that are beyond the River, that everything that Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, asks of you, let it be done promptly,

**7:22** up to talents of silver, and up to one hundred kors of wheat, and up to one hundred storehouses of wine, and salt for which there is no written record.

**7:23** Everything that is in the decree of the God of heaven, let it be done. Take care not to attempt anything against the house of the God of heaven, so that wrath may not come upon the kingdom of the king and of his sons.

**7:24** And it has been made known to you concerning all the priests and the Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, and ministers of this house of God: let there be no tribute for you; you will not have authority to enslave them.

**7:25** And you, Ezra, who have the wisdom of God in your hand, appoint scribes and judges, so that they may be judging all the people who are beyond the River, all those who know the law of God; and to the one who does not know, you will make it known.

**7:26** And everyone who is not promptly doing the law of God and the law of the king, judgment will be executed upon him, whether for death, or for discipline, or for forfeiture of property, or for custody."

**7:27** Blessed be the Lord, the God of our fathers, who put it thus into the heart of the king, who glorified the house that is in Jerusalem,

**7:28** and upon me He extended mercy in the eyes of the king and of his counselors and of all the exalted leaders of the king. And I was strengthened, as the good hand of God was upon me, and I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek genealogy reads the distinct name "Samariah" (Samareia) here, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Amariah."
- **7:4** — The Greek genealogy reads the distinct name "Saouia" here, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Uzzi."
- **7:9** — The Greek omits the clause concerning Ezra's departure from Babylon on the first day of the first month (found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and uses plural "they came" here and in 7:8.
- **7:17** — The Greek reads "everything that comes in, promptly enter this in this book" (apparently reading Aramaic "book" for "silver"), contrasting with the Aramaic Masoretic Text's instruction to "diligently buy with this silver."
- **7:22** — The Greek lacks the numeral "one hundred" before "talents of silver," omits oil from the list, and renders "baths" of wine as "storehouses" (apothekon).
