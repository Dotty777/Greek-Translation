## Text

**6:1** Then King Darius issued a decree, and he searched in the archives where the treasury lies in Babylon.

**6:2** And there was found in the city, in the citadel, one scroll, and this memorandum was written in it:

**6:3** "In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus issued a decree concerning the sacred house of God that is in Jerusalem and the place where they offer the sacrifices. And he set an elevation sixty cubits in height,

**6:4** and three courses of stone and one course of timber; and the expense will be given from the house of the king.

**6:5** And the silver and gold vessels of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar brought out from the house that is in Jerusalem, were set in place in the house of God."

**6:6** "Now you will give, governors beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai and their fellow servants, the Apharsachites beyond the River, keeping far from there;

**6:7** leave alone the work of the house of God; and let the elders of the Jews build that house of God in its place.

**6:8** And a decree has been issued by me that you never do anything with the elders of the Jews for the building of that house of God; and from the possessions of the king, from the tributes beyond the River, let the expense be diligently given to those men, so that it may not be stopped;

**6:9** and whatever is lacking, both young of cattle and of rams, and lambs for burnt offerings to the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, oil, according to the word of the priests who are in Jerusalem, let it be given to them day by day, whatever they ask,

**6:10** so that they may be offering pleasing aromas to the God of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and of his sons.

**6:11** And a decree has been issued by me that any person who alters this word, a beam will be pulled down from his house, and being set upright, he will be struck upon it, and his house will be made what belongs to me.

**6:12** And the God whose name dwells there will overthrow every king and people who stretches out his hand to alter or to destroy the house of God that is in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have issued a decree; it will be done diligently."

**6:13** Then Tattenai, governor beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and his fellow servants, according to what King Darius sent, did so diligently.

**6:14** And the elders of the Jews and the Levites were building through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo; and they built and completed it by the decree of the God of Israel and by the decree of Cyrus and Darius and Artaxerxes, kings of the Persians.

**6:15** And they finished this house by the third day of the month of Adar, which is the sixth year of the reign of King Darius.

**6:16** And the sons of Israel, the priests and the Levites and the rest of the sons of the exile, celebrated the dedication of the house of God with joy.

**6:17** And they offered for the dedication of the house of God one hundred young bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs; twelve male goats for a sin offering on behalf of all Israel, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

**6:18** And they appointed the priests in their division and the Levites in their portions for the service of God who is in Jerusalem, according to the writing of the book of Moses.

**6:19** And the sons of the exile celebrated the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**6:20** For the priests and the Levites had purified themselves; to a single one, all were pure. And they slaughtered the Passover for all the sons of the exile, and for their brothers the priests, and for themselves.

**6:21** And the sons of Israel, from the exile, ate the Passover, and everyone separating himself to them with regard to the impurities of the nations of the land, to seek the Lord, the God of Israel.

**6:22** And they celebrated the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days with joy, because He made them joyful, and the heart of the king of Assyria turned toward them to strengthen their hands in the works of the house of the God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Greek reads simply "in the city, in the citadel," omitting the names of Ecbatana and the province of Media found in the Aramaic Masoretic Text.
- **6:6** — The Greek reads "you will give" (dosete), apparently interpreting the Aramaic name Tattenai (which appears as Tanthanai in 6:13) as a verb meaning "to give," and also uses plural "governors" and "their fellow servants."
- **6:8** — The Greek reads "that you never do anything" (me pote ti poiesete), taking the Aramaic clause ("concerning what you shall do") as a prohibition.
- **6:11** — The Greek phrase "what belongs to me" (to kat' eme) indicates confiscation to the crown, contrasting with the Aramaic Masoretic Text's "a refuse heap."
- **6:21** — The Greek omits "who had returned" before "from the exile" and reads "with regard to the impurities" (eis akatharsias, literally "into/regarding the impurities") rather than "from the impurity."
