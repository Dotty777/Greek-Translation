## Text

**10:1** And when Ezra prayed, and when he spoke out, weeping and praying before the house of God, a very large assembly gathered to him from Israel, men and women and youths; for the people wept and raised their weeping high.

**10:2** And Shecaniah son of Jehiel, from the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, "We acted unfaithfully toward our God and married foreign wives from the peoples of the land; yet now there is hope for Israel concerning this.

**10:3** And now let us make a covenant with our God to cast out all the wives and those born from them, as you wish; arise and frighten them with the commandments of our God, and let it be done according to the law.

**10:4** Arise, for the matter is upon you, and we are with you; be strong and act."

**10:5** And Ezra arose and made the leaders, the Levites, and all Israel swear to act according to this word; and they swore.

**10:6** And Ezra arose from before the house of God and went into the treasury of Johanan son of Eliashib, and he went there; he ate no bread and drank no water, for he was mourning over the unfaithfulness of the exile.

**10:7** And they sent out a proclamation to Judah and in Jerusalem:

**10:8** "Everyone who does not come within three days, according to the counsel of the leaders and the elders, will have all his property placed under the ban, and he himself will be separated from the assembly of the exile."

**10:9** And all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered to Jerusalem within the three days; this was the ninth month. On the twentieth day of the month, all the people sat in the open square of the house of God, because of their agitation concerning the matter and because of the storm.

**10:10** And Ezra the priest arose and said to them, "You have acted unfaithfully and married foreign wives, to add to the guilt of Israel.

**10:11** And now give praise to the God of our fathers, and do what is pleasing before Him, and separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign wives."

**10:12** And all the assembly answered and said, "Great is this word of yours for us to do.

**10:13** But the people are many, and the place is wintry, and there is no strength to stand outside; and the work is not for one day nor for two, for we have multiplied wrongdoing in this matter.

**10:14** Now let our leaders stand; and as for all those in our cities who married foreign wives, let them come at due times from the congregations, and with them the elders of each city and judges, to turn away the fierce wrath of our God from us concerning this matter."

**10:15** Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Lazeia son of Helkiah were with me concerning this, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite were helping them.

**10:16** And the sons of the exile did so; and Ezra the priest and men, leaders of ancestral houses, were set apart by house and all by name, for they returned on a day of the tenth month to investigate the matter.

**10:17** And they finished with all the men who had married foreign wives by the first day of the first month.

**10:18** And among the sons of the priests there were found those who had married foreign wives: from the sons of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers, Meessel and Eliezer and Jarim and Gedaliah;

**10:19** and they gave their hand to send out their wives, and a guilt-offering ram from the flock for their offense;

**10:20** and from the sons of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah;

**10:21** and from the sons of Harim: Masael and Elijah and Shemaiah and Jehiel and Uzziah;

**10:22** and from the sons of Pashhur: Eliona, Maaseiah and Samael and Nethanel and Jozabad and Elasah;

**10:23** and from the Levites: Jozabad and Samu and Kelaiah, that is, Kolieu, and Pedaiah and Jodom and Eliezer,

**10:24** and from the singers: Elisaph; and from the gatekeepers: Gellem and Telem and Oduth;

**10:25** and from Israel, from the sons of Parosh: Ramiah and Aziah and Malchijah and Amamin and Eleazar and Benaiah;

**10:26** and from the sons of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah and Jehiel and Abdiah and Jeremoth and Elijah;

**10:27** and from the sons of Zathuia: Eliona, Eliashib, Alathaniah and Amon and Zabadab and Uzziah;

**10:28** and from the sons of Bebai: Johanan, Niana and Zabu, Thali;

**10:29** and from the sons of Binnui: Melusam, Alum, Ada, Jasud and Saluia and Memon;

**10:30** and from the sons of Pahath-moab: Aidaine, Chael, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel and Thanui and Manasseh;

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Greek verb translated "married" (here and in vv. 10, 14, 17, 18) literally means "settled," rendering the Hebrew idiom for bringing women into one's household as wives.
- **10:3** — The Greek text reads "as you wish; arise and frighten them with the commandments of our God," diverging notably from the Hebrew ("according to the counsel of my lord and of those who tremble at the commandment of our God").
- **10:7** — Translated verbatim from the Greek text, which lacks the clause "to all the returned exiles to assemble at Jerusalem" found in the Hebrew, likely through scribal eye-skip between the two occurrences of "Jerusalem."
- **10:15** — The Greek reads "were with me concerning this" (contrast Hebrew "opposed this") and preserves the LXX-distinct names Lazeia son of Helkiah (Hebrew: Jahzeiah son of Tikvah).
- **10:37** — The Greek renders the final Hebrew name (Jaasu/Jaasai) as a verb, "and they acted." Throughout vv. 18-43, numerous LXX-distinct personal names (such as Meessel, Jarim, Masael, Eliona, Samael, Samu, Kolieu, Jodom, Elisaph, Gellem, Oduth, Amamin, Alathaniah, Amon, Zabadab, Niana, Melusam, Alum, Memon, Chael, Thanui, Baluch, Atha, Zabel, Eliphaneth, Ani, Modediah, Mari, Baraiah, Hilkiah, Mathanan, Machadnabu, Sariu, Mariah, Thamathiah, Sedem, and Dia) are retained faithfully from the Greek text.
