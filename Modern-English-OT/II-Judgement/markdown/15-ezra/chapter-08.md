## Text

**8:1** And these are the leaders of their ancestral families, the guides going up with me in the reign of Artaxerxes, the king of Babylon:

**8:2** From the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; from the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; from the sons of David, Tous;

**8:3** from the sons of Shecaniah, from the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him the company of one hundred and fifty;

**8:4** from the sons of Pahath-moab, Eliana the son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred males;

**8:5** and from the sons of Zattu, Shecaniah the son of Aziel, and with him three hundred males;

**8:6** and from the sons of Adin, Ebed the son of Jonathan, and with him fifty males;

**8:7** and from the sons of Elah, Jeshaiah the son of Athaliah, and with him seventy males;

**8:8** and from the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah the son of Michael, and with him eighty males;

**8:9** and from the sons of Joab, Adaiah the son of Jema, and with him two hundred and eighteen males;

**8:10** and from the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and with him one hundred and sixty males;

**8:11** and from the sons of Bebai, also Azariah the son of Bebai, and with him seventy-eight males;

**8:12** and from the sons of Azgad, Johanan the son of Hakkatan, and with him one hundred and ten males;

**8:13** and from the sons of Adonikam, the last ones, and these are their names: Eliphelet and Euia and Shemaiah, sixty males;

**8:14** and from the sons of Bigvai, Uthai, and with him eighty males.

**8:15** And I gathered them to the river that comes to Euim, and we camped there three days. And I observed among the people and among the priests, and from the sons of Levi I did not find any there.

**8:16** And I sent for Eleazar, Ariel, Shemaiah, Alonam, Areb, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, men; and for Areib and Elnathan, men of understanding.

**8:17** And I brought them out before a leader in the silver of the place, and I put words in their mouth to speak to their brothers of the Athanim in the silver of the place, to bring us singers for the house of our God.

**8:18** And there came to us, as the good hand of our God was upon us, a man, Sachoch, from the sons of Mahli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel; and at the beginning his sons and his brothers came, eighteen;

**8:19** and Hashabiah and Hoshaiah from the sons of Merari, his brothers and their sons, twenty;

**8:20** and from the Nethinim, whom David and the leaders gave for the service of the Levites, two hundred and twenty Nethinim; all of them were gathered by name.

**8:21** And I proclaimed a fast there at the river Thue, to humble ourselves before our God, to seek from Him a straight way for us and for our children and for all our possessions.

**8:22** For I was ashamed to ask from the king a force and horsemen to save us from an enemy on the way, because we had spoken to the king, saying, "The hand of our God is upon all those who seek Him for good, and His power and His wrath are upon all who forsake Him."

**8:23** And we fasted and sought from our God concerning this, and He listened to us.

**8:24** And I set apart twelve from the leaders of the priests, Seraiah, Hashabiah, and with them twelve from their brothers.

**8:25** And I weighed out to them the silver and the gold and the vessels of the firstfruits of the house of our God, which the king and his counselors and his leaders and all Israel who were present had offered up.

**8:26** And I weighed out into their hands six hundred and fifty talents of silver, and one hundred silver vessels, and one hundred talents of gold,

**8:27** and twenty gold kaphudeth, for the way one thousand chamanim, and choice vessels of good gleaming bronze, desirable in gold.

**8:28** And I said to them, "You are holy to the Lord, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are freewill offerings to the Lord God of our fathers.

**8:29** Keep watch and guard them until you stand before the leaders of the priests and the Levites and the leaders of the ancestral families in Jerusalem, in the tents of the house of the Lord."

**8:30** And the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver and the gold and the vessels, to bring them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.

**8:31** And we departed from the river Thue on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem; and the hand of our God was upon us, and He delivered us from the hand of an enemy and a foe on the way.

**8:32** And we came to Jerusalem, and we remained there three days.

**8:33** And it happened on the fourth day that we weighed out the silver and the gold and the vessels in the house of our God into the hand of Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest, and with him was Eleazar the son of Phinehas, and with them were Jozabad the son of Jeshua and Noadiah from Ebannaia, the Levites;

**8:34** everything by number and by weight, and all the weight was written down.

**8:35** At that time those who had come out of captivity, the sons of the exile, offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve young bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, twelve male goats for a sin offering, all of them as burnt offerings.

**8:36** And they delivered the decree of the king to the administrators of the king and to the governors beyond the River; and they honored the people and the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Greek reads 'the guides going up with me' and 'the king of Babylon,' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the genealogy of those who went up with me' and 'from Babylon.'
- **8:5** — The Greek preserves the ancestral family of Zattu (absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text here). Across the 8:2-14 roster, the Greek also preserves several distinct names and numbers compared with the Hebrew text: Tous (8:2, for Hattush), Eliana (8:4, for Eliehoenai), Aziel (8:5, for Jahaziel), Elah (8:7, for Elam), Adaiah and Jema (8:9, for Obadiah and Jehiel), Azariah and seventy-eight (8:11, for Zechariah and twenty-eight), Euia (8:13, for Jeiel), and eighty (8:14, for seventy).
- **8:15** — The Greek names the river destination Euim here and Thue in 8:21 and 8:31, where the Hebrew text reads Ahava. In 8:16 the Greek also reads Eleazar, Alonam, Areb, and Areib (for Hebrew Eliezer, Elnathan, Jarib, and Joiarib), and in 8:24 and 8:33 it reads Seraiah (with twelve brothers) and 'from Ebannaia' (for Sherebiah with ten, and 'son of Binnui').
- **8:17** — The Greek renders the place-name Casiphia as 'in the silver of the place' (from the Aramaic/Hebrew root for silver), reads Athanim (a variant of Nethinim, cf. 8:20), and has 'singers' rather than 'ministers.' In 8:18-19 'Sachoch' is an untranslated transliteration of Hebrew sekel ('discretion'), 'at the beginning' replaces Sherebiah, and Hoshaiah appears for Jeshaiah.
- **8:27** — Both 'kaphudeth' and 'chamanim' are untranslated Greek transliterations corresponding to Hebrew 'bowls' and 'darics,' with the first part of 'darics' interpreted as 'for the way.' The adjective 'choice' translates Greek diaphora ('distinct/choice') where the Hebrew text reads 'two.'
