## Text

**10:1** And after Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, son of his paternal uncle, a man of Issachar, rose up to save Israel; and he lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.

**10:2** And he judged Israel twenty-three years, and he died and was buried in Shamir.

**10:3** And after him, Jair of Gilead rose up, and he judged Israel twenty-two years.

**10:4** And he had thirty-two sons riding on thirty-two colts, and they had thirty-two cities; and they called them Settlements of Jair to this day, in the land of Gilead.

**10:5** And Jair died and was buried in Ramnon.

**10:6** And the sons of Israel again did what was evil before the Lord, and they served the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and the gods of Arad, and the gods of Sidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the sons of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines; and they abandoned the Lord and did not serve Him.

**10:7** And the Lord was angry with wrath against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of the Philistines and into the hand of the sons of Ammon.

**10:8** And they afflicted and crushed the sons of Israel at that time for eighteen years, all the sons of Israel who were beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorites who were in Gilead.

**10:9** And the sons of Ammon crossed the Jordan to do battle against Judah and Benjamin and against Ephraim; and Israel was severely afflicted.

**10:10** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord, saying, "We have sinned against You, because we have abandoned God and have served the Baals."

**10:11** And the Lord said to the sons of Israel, "Was it not from Egypt, and from the Amorites, and from the sons of Ammon, and from the Philistines,

**10:12** and the Sidonians, and Amalek, and Midian, who afflicted you; and you cried out to Me, and I saved you out of their hand?

**10:13** Yet you abandoned Me and served other gods; therefore I will not save you again.

**10:14** Go and cry out to the gods whom you have chosen for yourselves, and let them save you in the time of your affliction."

**10:15** And the sons of Israel said to the Lord, "We have sinned; do to us Yourself according to all that is good in Your eyes; only deliver us on this day."

**10:16** And they put away the foreign gods from their midst and served the Lord alone; and His soul was distressed over the misery of Israel.

**10:17** And the sons of Ammon went up and encamped in Gilead, and the sons of Israel gathered together and encamped at the watchtower.

**10:18** And the people, the leaders of Gilead, said, each man to his neighbor, "Who is the man who will begin to do battle against the sons of Ammon? He will be leader over all the inhabitants of Gilead."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek renders Hebrew dodo as "his paternal uncle" rather than as the proper name Dodo found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:4** — The Codex Vaticanus Greek text reads "thirty-two" three times here (versus "thirty" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and translates the place name Havvoth-jair as "Settlements of Jair."
- **10:5** — Greek reads "Ramnon" (Ῥαμνών), an LXX-distinct proper name where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Kamon."
- **10:6** — Greek reads "Arad" (Ἀράδ), an LXX-distinct proper name where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Aram" (Syria).
- **10:12** — Greek reads "Midian" (Μαδιάμ), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Maon."
- **10:17** — Greek translates the Hebrew place name Mizpah literally as "the watchtower" (σκοπιά) here and in 11:29, whereas 11:11 and 11:34 transliterate it as Mizpah (Μασσηφά).
