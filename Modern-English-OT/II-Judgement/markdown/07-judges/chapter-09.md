## Text

**9:1** And Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem to his mother's brothers, and he spoke to them and to all the clan of the house of his mother's father, saying,

**9:2** "Please speak in the ears of all the men of Shechem, 'What is better for you: for seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, to rule over you, or for one man to rule over you? And remember that I am your bone and your flesh.'"

**9:3** And his mother's brothers spoke all these words about him in the ears of all the men of Shechem; and their heart inclined after Abimelech, for they said, "He is our brother."

**9:4** And they gave him seventy pieces of silver from the house of Baal-berith, and Abimelech hired for himself worthless and cowardly men, and they went after him.

**9:5** And he went into his father's house at Ephrathah and killed his brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, on one stone; yet Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, was left, because he hid himself.

**9:6** And all the men of Shechem and all the house of Beth-maalon gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king by the found oak of the station that was in Shechem.

**9:7** And it was reported to Jotham; and he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice and wept, and said to them, "Listen to me, men of Shechem, and God will listen to you.

**9:8** Going, the trees went to anoint a king over themselves, and they said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us.'

**9:9** And the olive tree said to them, 'Should I leave my richness, by which men will glorify God, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:10** And the trees said to the fig tree, 'Come, reign over us.'

**9:11** And the fig tree said to them, 'Should I leave my sweetness and my good fruits and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:12** And the trees said to the vine, 'Come, you reign over us.'

**9:13** And the vine said to them, 'Should I leave my wine that gladdens God and people and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:14** And all the trees said to the bramble, 'Come, you reign over us.'

**9:15** And the bramble said to the trees, 'If in truth you are anointing me to reign over you, come, take shelter in my shadow; and if not, let fire come out from me and devour the cedars of Lebanon.'

**9:16** And now, if you have acted in truth and integrity and have made Abimelech king, and if you have done good to Jerubbaal and to his house, and if you have done to him according to the repayment of his hand—

**9:17** since my father fought for you, and cast his life out in front, and rescued you from the hand of Midian,

**9:18** yet you have risen up against my father's house today and killed his sons, seventy men, on one stone, and have made Abimelech, the son of his female servant, king over the men of Shechem, because he is your brother—

**9:19** and if you have acted in truth and integrity with Jerubbaal and with his house on this day, may you rejoice in Abimelech, and may he also rejoice over you.

**9:20** But if not, may fire come out from Abimelech and consume the men of Shechem and the house of Beth-maalon, and may fire come out from the men of Shechem and from the house of Beth-maalon and devour Abimelech."

**9:21** And Jotham fled and escaped, and went as far as Beer and lived there, away from the presence of his brother Abimelech.

**9:22** And Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.

**9:23** And God sent out an evil spirit between Abimelech and the men of Shechem, and the men of Shechem acted treacherously against the house of Abimelech,

**9:24** in order to bring the wrong done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal and their blood, to place them upon their brother Abimelech, who killed them, and upon the men of Shechem, because they strengthened his hands to kill the brothers.

**9:25** And the men of Shechem set ambushers against him on the tops of the mountains, and they were plundering everyone who passed by them on the road; and it was reported to King Abimelech.

**9:26** And Gilead son of Jobel and his brothers came and passed into Shechem, and the men of Shechem trusted in him.

**9:27** And they went out into the field and harvested their vineyards and trod them, and made helloulim, and brought them into the house of their god, and ate and spoke and cursed Abimelech.

**9:28** And Gaad son of Jobel said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is the son of Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, and is not Zebul, his overseer, his servant with the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem? And why should we serve him?

**9:29** And who would give this people into my hand? Then I will remove Abimelech, and I will say to him, 'Increase your army and come out!'"

**9:30** And Zebul, the ruler of the city, heard the words of Gilead son of Jobel, and he himself became furious with anger.

**9:31** And he sent messengers to Abimelech in secret, saying, "Look, Gilead son of Jobel and his brothers are coming to Shechem, and look, they are besieging the city against you.

**9:32** And now, rise up by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in ambush in the field;

**9:33** and it will be in the morning, as soon as the sun rises, you will rise early and advance against the city; and look, he and the people who are with him are coming out against you, and you will do to him whatever your hand finds."

**9:34** And Abimelech and all the people with him rose up by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four companies.

**9:35** And Gilead son of Jobel went out and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city, and Abimelech and the people who were with him rose up from the ambush.

**9:36** And Gilead son of Jobel saw the people, and he said to Zebul, "Look, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains." And Zebul said to him, "You are seeing the shadow of the mountains as men."

**9:37** And Gilead spoke yet again and said, "Look, people are coming down toward the sea from next to the navel of the land, and another company is coming by the way of Elon-maonemin."

**9:38** And Zebul said to him, "And where is your mouth, as you spoke, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Is this not the people whom you despised? Come out now and fight against him!"

**9:39** And Gilead went out before the men of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.

**9:40** And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled from before him; and many wounded fell as far as the entrance of the gate.

**9:41** And Abimelech entered Arumah; and Zebul drove out Gilead and his brothers from living in Shechem.

**9:42** And it happened on the next day that the people went out into the field, and someone reported it to Abimelech.

**9:43** And he took the people and divided them into three companies, and lay in ambush in the field; and he looked, and see, the people came out from the city, and he rose up against them and struck them down.

**9:44** And Abimelech and the leaders who were with him advanced and stood beside the entrance of the gate of the city, and the two companies advanced against all who were in the field and struck them down.

**9:45** And Abimelech fought against the city all that day, and he captured the city and killed the people who were in it; and he tore down the city and sowed it with salt.

**9:46** And all the men of the towers of Shechem heard, and they came into the assembly place of Beth-er-berith.

**9:47** And it was reported to Abimelech that all the men of the towers of Shechem had gathered together.

**9:48** And Abimelech went up to Mount Hermon, and all the people who were with him; and Abimelech took the axes in his hand, and cut off a branch of a tree and lifted it and placed it on his shoulders, and he said to the people who were with him, "What you saw me doing, quickly do as I did."

**9:49** And each one, every man, also cut off a branch, and they went after Abimelech and placed them upon the assembly place, and burned the assembly place over them with fire; and all the men of the tower of Shechem also died, about a thousand men and women.

**9:50** And Abimelech went from Beth-er-berith and encamped against Thebez, and he captured it.

**9:51** And there was a strong tower in the middle of the city, and all the men and the women of the city fled there, and shut it on the outside of them, and went up onto the roof of the tower.

**9:52** And Abimelech came as far as the tower, and they fought against him; and Abimelech drew near to the door of the tower to burn it with fire.

**9:53** And a certain woman threw a piece of an upper millstone onto Abimelech's head, and broke his skull.

**9:54** And he cried out quickly to the young man who carried his armor and said to him, "Draw my sword and put me to death, so that they do not say, 'A woman killed him.'" And his young man pierced him through, and he died.

**9:55** And the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, and they went, each man to his place.

**9:56** And God returned the wickedness of Abimelech, which he had done to his father in killing his seventy brothers.

**9:57** And all the wickedness of the men of Shechem God returned upon their head, and the curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came upon them.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — Codex Vaticanus reads Ephrathah where the Hebrew MT reads Ophrah.
- **9:6** — Codex Vaticanus reads "house of Beth-maalon" (also in 9:20; Hebrew MT "Beth-millo") and "the found oak of the station," a double rendering reflecting Hebrew "oak of the pillar."
- **9:26** — Codex Vaticanus names this figure Gilead son of Jobel throughout the chapter (spelled Gaad in 9:28), where the Hebrew MT reads Gaal son of Ebed.
- **9:27** — Codex Vaticanus transliterates Hebrew hillulim (a harvest thanksgiving festival or festival offerings) as helloulim, reads "brought them" for MT "entered," and reads "spoke" (Greek eipon) where MT has "drank" (likely from Greek epion).
- **9:37** — Codex Vaticanus adds "toward the sea" before "from next to the navel of the land" and leaves the place name Elon-maonemin (Hebrew "Diviners' Oak") untranslated.
- **9:46** — Codex Vaticanus renders Hebrew tsariach ("stronghold" or "crypt") as "assembly place" (Greek syneleusis, also in 9:49) and gives the name Beth-er-berith (MT "house of El-berith"), which it also inserts in 9:50.
- **9:48** — Codex Vaticanus reads Mount Hermon where the Hebrew MT reads Mount Zalmon.
