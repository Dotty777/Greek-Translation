## Text

**1:1** And it happened after the death of Joshua that the sons of Israel inquired through the Lord, saying, "Who will go up for us against the Canaanites, leading the way to fight against them?"

**1:2** And the Lord said, "Judah will go up; look, I have given the land into his hand."

**1:3** And Judah said to Simeon his brother, "Come up with me into my lot, and let us engage in battle against the Canaanites, and I also will go with you into your lot." And Simeon went with him.

**1:4** And Judah went up, and the Lord delivered the Canaanite and the Perizzite into their hands, and they cut them down in Bezek, up to ten thousand men.

**1:5** And they came upon Adoni-bezek in Bezek and engaged in battle against him, and they cut down the Canaanite and the Perizzite.

**1:6** And Adoni-bezek fled, and they chased after him and caught him, and they cut off the extremities of his hands and the extremities of his feet.

**1:7** And Adoni-bezek said, "Seventy kings with the extremities of their hands and the extremities of their feet cut off were gathering what was underneath my table; therefore, just as I did, so God has repaid me." And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**1:8** And the sons of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, and burned the city with fire.

**1:9** And after these things the sons of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanite who lived in the hill country and the south and the plain.

**1:10** And Judah went against the Canaanite who lived in Hebron, and Hebron came out in opposition; and the name of Hebron formerly was Kariarboxepher. And they struck down Sheshai and Ahinaan and Talmai, offspring of Anak.

**1:11** And they went up from there against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir previously was Kiriath-sepher, City of Letters.

**1:12** And Caleb said, "Whoever strikes the City of Letters and captures it, I will give him Aza my daughter as a wife."

**1:13** And Othniel the younger, the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, captured it; and Caleb gave him Aza his daughter as a wife.

**1:14** And it happened at her coming in that Othniel urged her to ask for a field from her father; and she murmured and cried out from the donkey, "You have given me away into a land of the south." And Caleb said to her, "What is the matter with you?"

**1:15** And Achsah said to him, "Please give me a blessing, because you have given me away into a land of the south, and you shall give me a redemption of water." And Caleb gave her, according to her heart, a redemption of high places and a redemption of low places.

**1:16** And the sons of Jethro the Kenite, the father-in-law of Moses, went up from the City of Palms with the sons of Judah into the wilderness that is in the south of Judah, which is at the descent of Arad. They lived with the people.

**1:17** And Judah went with Simeon his brother and cut down the Canaanite who lived in Sephek, and they utterly destroyed them; and he called the name of the city Anathema.

**1:18** And Judah did not inherit Gaza nor its borders, nor Ashkelon nor its borders, nor Ekron nor its borders, nor Ashdod nor its surrounding fields.

**1:19** And the Lord was with Judah, and he inherited the mountain, because they were not able to utterly destroy the inhabitants of the valley, because Rechab commanded them.

**1:20** And they gave Hebron to Caleb, just as Moses spoke; and he inherited from there the three cities of the sons of Anak.

**1:21** And the sons of Benjamin did not dispossess the Jebusite who lived in Jerusalem, and the Jebusite lived with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem until this day.

**1:22** And the sons of Joseph went up, they also, to Bethel, and the Lord was with them.

**1:23** And they encamped and spied out Bethel; now the name of their city previously was Luz.

**1:24** And the watchmen saw, and look, a man was coming out of the city; and they seized him and said to him, "Show us the entrance of the city, and we will show mercy to you."

**1:25** And he showed them the entrance of the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword, but they sent the man and his relatives away.

**1:26** And the man went into the land of the Hittites, and built a city there and called its name Luz. This is its name until this day.

**1:27** And Manasseh did not remove Beth-shean, which is the City of the Scythians, nor its daughters nor its surrounding settlements, nor Taanach nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Dor nor its daughters, nor the inhabitant of Balak nor its surrounding settlements nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Megiddo nor its surrounding settlements nor its daughters, nor the inhabitants of Ibleam nor its surrounding settlements nor its daughters; and the Canaanite began to live in this land.

**1:28** And it happened when Israel grew strong that he made the Canaanite subject to tribute, and he did not completely remove him.

**1:29** And Ephraim did not remove the Canaanite who lived in Gezer, and the Canaanite lived in his midst in Gezer and became subject to tribute.

**1:30** And Zebulun did not remove the inhabitants of Kedron nor the inhabitants of Domana, and the Canaanite lived in their midst and became subject to tribute to him.

**1:31** And Asher did not remove the inhabitants of Acco (and it became subject to tribute to him), and the inhabitants of Dor, and the inhabitants of Sidon, and the inhabitants of Dalaph, Aschazei, and Chebda, and Naei, and Ereo.

**1:32** And Asher lived in the midst of the Canaanite who lived in the land, because he was not able to remove him.

**1:33** And Naphtali did not remove the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and the inhabitants of Beth-anath; and Naphtali lived in the midst of the Canaanite who lived in the land, but the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath became subject to tribute to them.

**1:34** And the Amorite pressed the sons of Dan into the mountain, because they did not allow him to go down into the valley.

**1:35** And the Amorite began to live in the potsherd mountain, where the bears are and where the foxes are, in the Myrtle Grove and in Thalabin; and the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy upon the Amorite, and he became subject to tribute to them.

**1:36** And the border of the Amorite was from the ascent of Akrabbim, from the Rock and upward.

## Translator Notes

- **1:10** — Codex Vaticanus reads "Kariarboxepher" (a conflation of Kiriath-arba and Kiriath-sepher) and "Ahinaan" (for MT "Ahiman"), and adds "and Hebron came out in opposition" and "offspring of Anak."
- **1:15** — Codex Vaticanus spells Caleb's daughter's name "Aza" in vv. 12-13 and "Achsah" (Greek Ascha) here, and reads "redemption" (Greek lytrosin) of water, high places, and low places where MT has "springs."
- **1:18** — Unlike MT ("Judah captured..."), the Septuagint states that Judah did not inherit these cities and includes Ashdod (Greek Azotos) and its surrounding fields; in v. 17, "Sephek" and "Anathema" correspond to MT "Zephath" and "Hormah."
- **1:19** — Instead of MT's "because they had chariots of iron," Codex Vaticanus reads "because Rechab commanded them," interpreting the Hebrew word for chariots as the proper name Rechab.
- **1:31** — Codex Vaticanus preserves several distinct place-name forms in Manasseh, Zebulun, and Asher: "Balak" (v. 27), "Kedron" and "Domana" (v. 30, MT Kitron and Nahalol), and here "Dor," "Dalaph," "Aschazei," "Chebda," "Naei," and "Ereo" (corresponding to MT Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob).
- **1:35** — Codex Vaticanus renders the place names of MT ("Mount Heres, in Aijalon and in Shaalbim") as "the potsherd mountain, where the bears are and where the foxes are, in the Myrtle Grove [Greek Myrsinon] and in Thalabin."
