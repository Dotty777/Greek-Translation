## Text

**2:1** And the angel of the Lord went up from Gilgal to the Place of Weeping and to Bethel and to the house of Israel, and said to them, "Thus says the Lord: I brought you up out of Egypt and brought you into the land that I swore to your fathers, and I said, 'I will not break My covenant with you forever.

**2:2** And you shall not make a covenant with those who dwell in this land, nor shall you bow down to their gods, but you shall shatter their carved images, you shall tear down their altars.' Yet you did not listen to My voice, because you did these things.

**2:3** And I also said, 'I will certainly not remove them from before you, and they will become distresses to you, and their gods will become a snare to you.'"

**2:4** And it happened, when the angel of the Lord spoke these words to all the sons of Israel, that the people lifted up their voice and wept.

**2:5** And they called the name of that place Places of Weeping, and they sacrificed there to the Lord.

**2:6** And Joshua sent the people away, and each man went to his inheritance to inherit the land.

**2:7** And the people served the Lord all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who prolonged their days with Joshua, who knew all the great work of the Lord, all that He had done in Israel.

**2:8** And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died, one hundred ten years old.

**2:9** And they buried him within the border of his inheritance in Timnath-heres, in Mount Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

**2:10** And all that generation also was added to their fathers; and another generation arose after them who did not know the Lord, nor even the work that He had done in Israel.

**2:11** And the sons of Israel did what was evil before the Lord, and served the Baals.

**2:12** And they abandoned the Lord, the God of their fathers, who brought them out from the land of Egypt, and went after other gods from among the gods of the nations that were around them, and bowed down to them; and they provoked the Lord to anger.

**2:13** And they abandoned Him, and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.

**2:14** And the Lord became angry with wrath against Israel, and delivered them into the hands of plunderers, and they thoroughly plundered them; and He sold them into the hands of their enemies all around, and they were no longer able to stand before their enemies wherever they went out.

**2:15** And the hand of the Lord was against them for harm, just as the Lord had spoken, and just as the Lord had sworn to them, and He oppressed them greatly.

**2:16** And the Lord raised up judges, and the Lord saved them from the hand of those who plundered them. Yet they did not even obey the judges,

**2:17** because they prostituted themselves after other gods and bowed down to them, and turned aside quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked to listen to the words of the Lord; they did not do so.

**2:18** And when the Lord raised up judges for them, the Lord was with the judge and saved them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for the Lord was moved to pity by their groaning on account of those who besieged them and oppressed them.

**2:19** And it happened, whenever the judge died, that they turned back and again acted more corruptly than their fathers, to go after other gods, to serve them and to bow down to them; they did not cast off their practices and their stubborn ways.

**2:20** And the Lord became angry with wrath against Israel and said, "Because this nation has abandoned My covenant that I commanded their fathers, and did not listen to My voice,

**2:21** I also will no longer remove a man from before them from the nations that Joshua the son of Nun left in the land." And He left them

**2:22** to test Israel by them, whether they keep the way of the Lord to walk in it, just as their fathers kept it, or not.

**2:23** And the Lord will leave these nations, so as not to remove them quickly, and He did not deliver them into the hand of Joshua.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek translates Hebrew "Bochim" as "the Place of Weeping" (plural "Places of Weeping" in v. 5) and adds "and to Bethel and to the house of Israel."
- **2:3** — Greek "distresses" (synochas) differs from MT's "sides" (often rendered "thorns in your sides"); in v. 2, Greek also has a declarative clause ("because you did these things") instead of MT's question.
- **2:14** — In the Greek text, "wherever they went out" closes v. 14 rather than opening v. 15, and in v. 16 the clause "Yet they did not even obey the judges" closes v. 16 rather than opening v. 17.
- **2:21** — Where MT reads "when he died," Codex Vaticanus reads "in the land. And He left them," continuing directly into v. 22; in v. 23, Greek also uses the future tense "the Lord will leave."
