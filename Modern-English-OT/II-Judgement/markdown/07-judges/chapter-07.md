## Text

**7:1** And Jerubbaal, that is, Gideon, and all the people with him rose early, and they camped by the spring of Harod; and the camp of Midian was north of him, from Gabaathamora in the valley.

**7:2** And the Lord said to Gideon, "The people with you are too many for Me to deliver Midian into their hand, so that Israel will not boast against Me, saying, 'My hand saved me.'

**7:3** And now speak in the ears of the people, saying, 'Who is fearful and timid? Let him turn back and depart from Mount Gilead.'" And twenty-two thousand from the people turned back, and ten thousand were left.

**7:4** And the Lord said to Gideon, "The people are still too many; bring them down to the water, and I will sift them for you there; and it will be that the one of whom I say to you, 'This one will go with you,' he will go with you; and everyone of whom I say to you, 'This one will not go with you,' he will not go with you."

**7:5** And he brought the people down to the water; and the Lord said to Gideon, "Everyone who laps from the water with his tongue as a dog laps, you will set him by himself, and everyone who bows down on his knees to drink."

**7:6** And the number of those who lapped with their hand to their mouth was three hundred men, and all the rest of the people bowed down on their knees to drink water.

**7:7** And the Lord said to Gideon, "With the three hundred men who lapped I will save you and give Midian into your hand, and all the people will go, each man to his place."

**7:8** And they took the provisions of the people in their hand and their horns; and he sent away all the men of Israel, each man to his tent, and he retained the three hundred men. And the camp of Midian was below him in the valley.

**7:9** And it happened on that night that the Lord said to him, "Rise, go down against the camp, for I have delivered it into your hand.

**7:10** And if you are afraid to go down, go down, you and Purah your servant, to the camp,

**7:11** and you will hear what they will say; and after this your hands will be strong, and you will go down against the camp." And he and Purah his servant went down to the edge of the fifty who were in the camp.

**7:12** Now Midian and Amalek and all the sons of the east were lying in the valley like locusts in multitude, and their camels were without number; rather, they were like the sand on the shore of the sea in multitude.

**7:13** And Gideon came, and look, a man was relating a dream to his companion, and he said, "See, I dreamed a dream, and look, a cake of barley bread was tumbling into the camp of Midian, and it came up to the tent and struck it, and it fell, and it turned it upside down, and the tent fell."

**7:14** And his companion answered and said, "This is nothing other than the sword of Gideon son of Joash, a man of Israel; God has delivered Midian and all the camp into his hand."

**7:15** And it happened, when Gideon heard the account of the dream and its interpretation, that he worshiped the Lord; and he returned to the camp of Israel and said, "Rise, for the Lord has delivered the camp of Midian into our hand."

**7:16** And he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put horns into the hand of all of them, and empty water jars, and torches inside the jars.

**7:17** And he said to them, "Look at me, and so you will do; and see, I am going in at the edge of the camp, and it will be that just as I do, so you will do.

**7:18** And I myself will blow the horn, and all of you with me will blow the horns around the entire camp, and you will say, 'For the Lord and for Gideon.'"

**7:19** And Gideon and the one hundred men who were with him went in at the edge of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch; and they indeed roused the guards, and they blew the horns and shook out the water jars that were in their hands.

**7:20** And the three companies blew the horns and shattered the water jars, and they held the torches in their left hands and the horns in their right hands to blow, and they cried out, "A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!"

**7:21** And they stood, each man in his place, around the camp; and all the camp ran, and they sounded the alarm and fled.

**7:22** And they blew the three hundred horns, and the Lord set each man's sword against his companion throughout the entire camp; and the camp fled as far as Bethseedta Garagatha, as far as the edge of Abomeoula by Tabbath.

**7:23** And the men of Israel cried out from Naphtali and from Asher and from all Manasseh, and they pursued after Midian.

**7:24** And Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim, saying, "Come down to meet Midian, and seize for yourselves the water as far as Bethera and the Jordan." And every man of Ephraim cried out, and they seized the water beforehand as far as Bethera and the Jordan.

**7:25** And they captured the leaders of Midian, both Oreb and Zeeb, and they killed Oreb at Sur, and Zeeb they killed at Jakephzeph; and they pursued Midian, and they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon from beyond the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Codex Vaticanus transliterates the Hebrew phrase 'the hill of Moreh' as a single place name, Gabaathamora.
- **7:11** — Codex Vaticanus reads 'of the fifty' (ton pentekonta), interpreting the Hebrew term for 'armed men' (hamushim) as the similar-looking numeral 'fifty' (hamishim).
- **7:19** — The emphatic Greek phrase 'they indeed roused the guards' literally renders a Hebrew idiom meaning 'they had just stationed the guards,' and 'shook out' (exetinaxan) preserves the Greek verb where verse 20 uses 'shattered.'
- **7:22** — Codex Vaticanus preserves the distinct place names Bethseedta Garagatha and Abomeoula where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Beth-shittah toward Zererah and Abel-meholah.
- **7:25** — Codex Vaticanus retains the distinct place name Bethera in verse 24 (Hebrew Beth-barah) and transliterates Hebrew 'rock of Oreb' and 'winepress of Zeeb' as the place names Sur and Jakephzeph.
