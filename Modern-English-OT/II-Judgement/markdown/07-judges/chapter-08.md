## Text

**8:1** And the men of Ephraim said to Gideon, "What is this thing you have done to us, in not calling us when you went to do battle against Midian?" And they argued with him strongly.

**8:2** And he said to them, "What have I done now compared with you? Is not the gleaning of Ephraim better than the grape harvest of Abiezer?

**8:3** Into your hand the Lord has delivered the leaders of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb; and what was I able to do compared with you?" Then their spirit subsided toward him when he spoke this word.

**8:4** And Gideon came to the Jordan, and he crossed over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, hungry and pursuing.

**8:5** And he said to the men of Succoth, "Please give loaves of bread for food to these people who are at my feet, for they are fainting, and look, I am pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian."

**8:6** And the leaders of Succoth said, "Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand? We will not give loaves of bread to your force."

**8:7** And Gideon said, "For this reason, when the Lord gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, then I will thresh your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with the habarkenein."

**8:8** And he went up from there to Penuel and spoke to them in the same way; and the men of Penuel answered him just as the men of Succoth had answered.

**8:9** And Gideon said to the men of Penuel, "When I return in peace, I will demolish this tower."

**8:10** Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their camp was with them, about fifteen thousand, all who were left from the entire camp of the foreigners; and those who had fallen were one hundred twenty thousand men who drew the sword.

**8:11** And Gideon went up by the way of those who dwell in tents, east of Nabai and Jegebal; and he struck the camp, while the camp was feeling secure.

**8:12** And Zebah and Zalmunna fled; and he pursued after them and captured the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and threw the entire camp into panic.

**8:13** And Gideon son of Joash returned from the battle, from above the battle of Heres.

**8:14** And he captured a young man from the men of Succoth and questioned him; and he wrote down for him the names of the leaders of Succoth and of their elders, seventy-seven men.

**8:15** And Gideon came to the leaders of Succoth and said, "Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, 'Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give loaves of bread to the men who are fainting?'"

**8:16** And he took the elders of the city with the thorns of the wilderness and the barakeneim, and threshed the men of the city with them.

**8:17** And he tore down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of the city.

**8:18** And he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, "Where are the men whom you killed at Tabor?" And they said, "As you are, so were they, in the likeness of a king's son."

**8:19** And Gideon said, "They were my brothers and the sons of my mother; as the Lord lives, if you had kept them alive, I would not kill you."

**8:20** And he said to Jether his firstborn, "Rise, kill them." But the young man did not draw his sword, because he was afraid, for he was still very young.

**8:21** And Zebah and Zalmunna said, "Rise yourself and fall upon us, for your strength is like that of a man." So Gideon rose and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent ornaments that were on the necks of their camels.

**8:22** And the men of Israel said to Gideon, "Master, rule over us, both you and your son, for you have saved us from the hand of Midian."

**8:23** And Gideon said to them, "I will not rule, and my son will not rule over you; the Lord will rule over you."

**8:24** And Gideon said to them, "I will make a request of you: let each man give me an earring from his plunder." For they had gold earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.

**8:25** And they said, "We will surely give them." And he spread out his garment, and each man threw an earring from his plunder there.

**8:26** And the weight of the gold earrings that he requested was one thousand five hundred gold pieces, besides the crescent ornaments and the necklaces and the garments and purple robes that were on the kings of Midian, and apart from the collars that were on the necks of their camels.

**8:27** And Gideon made it into an ephod and set it up in his city, Ephrathah; and all Israel prostituted themselves after it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and to his house.

**8:28** And Midian was subdued before the sons of Israel, and they did not lift up their heads again. And the land was quiet for forty years in the days of Gideon.

**8:29** And Jerubbaal son of Joash went and lived in his house.

**8:30** And Gideon had seventy sons who came from his loins, for he had many wives.

**8:31** And his concubine was in Shechem; and she also bore him a son, and he gave him the name Abimelech.

**8:32** And Gideon son of Joash died in his city, and he was buried in the tomb of Joash his father in Ephrathah of the Abiezrites.

**8:33** And it happened, as soon as Gideon died, that the sons of Israel turned back and prostituted themselves after the Baals, and they made a covenant for themselves with Baal, so that he would be a god to them.

**8:34** And the sons of Israel did not remember the Lord God, who had rescued them from the hand of all who oppressed them all around;

**8:35** and they did not show mercy to the house of Jerubbaal, that is, Gideon, according to all the good things that he had done for Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — Codex Vaticanus leaves the rare Hebrew term barqanim (likely briers or threshing-sledges) untranslated as 'habarkenein' (retaining the Hebrew definite article) and in verse 16 as 'barakeneim.'
- **8:11** — Codex Vaticanus preserves the distinct place names Nabai and Jegebal (Hebrew Nobah and Jogbehah); in verse 10 it likewise reads 'foreigners' (allophylon) where the Hebrew reads 'sons of the east.'
- **8:13** — Codex Vaticanus repeats 'of the battle' (tes parataxeos), reading 'from above the battle of Heres' where other witnesses and the Hebrew read 'by the ascent of Heres.'
- **8:26** — Codex Vaticanus gives the weight as one thousand five hundred gold pieces, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads one thousand seven hundred.
- **8:27** — Codex Vaticanus reads the distinct city name Ephrathah (also in verse 32) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Ophrah.
- **8:33** — Codex Vaticanus translates the divine title Baal-berith as 'a covenant with Baal,' and in verse 32 reads that Gideon died 'in his city' rather than the Hebrew 'in a good old age.'
