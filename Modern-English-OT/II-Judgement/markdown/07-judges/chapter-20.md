## Text

**20:1** And all the sons of Israel went out, and the congregation was assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba and the land of Gilead, to the Lord at Mizpah.

**20:2** And all the tribes of Israel took their stand in the presence of the Lord in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand men on foot, drawing the sword.

**20:3** And the sons of Benjamin heard that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. And the sons of Israel came and said, "Speak, where did this evil happen?"

**20:4** And the man, the Levite, the husband of the woman who was murdered, answered and said, "I and my concubine came to Gibeah of Benjamin to spend the night.

**20:5** And the men of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house against me by night. They wanted to murder me, and they violated my concubine, and she died.

**20:6** And I took hold of my concubine and cut her into pieces and sent her throughout all the territory of the inheritance of the sons of Israel, because they committed lewdness and disgrace in Israel.

**20:7** Look, all of you, sons of Israel, give yourselves a word and counsel there."

**20:8** And all the people rose up as one man, saying, "We will not go away, each man to his tent, and we will not return, each man to his house.

**20:9** And now this is the thing that will be done to Gibeah: we will go up against it by lot.

**20:10** However, we will take ten men for every hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred for every thousand, and a thousand for every ten thousand, to take provisions to cause them to come to Gibeah of Benjamin, to do to it according to all the disgrace that it committed in Israel."

**20:11** And every man of Israel was gathered to the city as one man.

**20:12** And the tribes of Israel sent men throughout the whole tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has happened among you?

**20:13** And now hand over the men, the sons of lawless ones, who are in Gibeah, and we will put them to death and purge evil from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin were not willing to listen to the voice of their brothers, the sons of Israel.

**20:14** And the sons of Benjamin were gathered from their cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the sons of Israel.

**20:15** And the sons of Benjamin were mustered on that day from the cities: twenty-three thousand, each man drawing the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who were mustered as seven hundred men,

**20:16** chosen men from all the people, ambidextrous; all of these were slingers with stones at a hair and did not miss.

**20:17** And the men of Israel were mustered, besides Benjamin, four hundred thousand men drawing the sword; all of these were men of battle.

**20:18** And they rose up and went up to Bethel, and inquired of God, and the sons of Israel said, "Who will go up for us first to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" And the Lord said, "Judah will go up first as leader."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel rose up in the morning and encamped against Gibeah.

**20:20** And all the men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin and joined battle with them at Gibeah.

**20:21** And the sons of Benjamin went out from Gibeah and destroyed in Israel on that day twenty-two thousand men to the ground.

**20:22** And the men of Israel strengthened themselves and again joined battle in the place where they had joined battle on the first day.

**20:23** And the sons of Israel went up and wept before the Lord until evening, and inquired of the Lord, saying, "Should we again draw near to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brothers?" And the Lord said, "Go up against them."

**20:24** And the sons of Israel approached the sons of Benjamin on the second day.

**20:25** And the sons of Benjamin went out to meet them from Gibeah on the second day, and destroyed from the sons of Israel another eighteen thousand men to the ground.

**20:26** And all the sons of Israel and all the people went up and came to Bethel, and wept and sat there before the Lord, and fasted on that day until evening, and offered up burnt offerings and complete offerings before the Lord.

**20:27** For the ark of the covenant of the Lord God was there,

**20:28** and Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, was standing before it in those days. And the sons of Israel inquired of the Lord, saying, "Should we go out yet again to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brothers, or should we hold back?" And the Lord said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give them into your hands."

**20:29** And the sons of Israel set ambushes around Gibeah.

**20:30** And the sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day and joined battle against Gibeah as once and again.

**20:31** And the sons of Benjamin went out to meet the people and were drawn out from the city, and they began to strike down casualties from the people as once and again on the roads, of which one goes up to Bethel and one to Gibeah in the field, about thirty men in Israel.

**20:32** And the sons of Benjamin said, "They are falling before us as at the first." And the sons of Israel said, "Let us flee and draw them out from the city into the roads." And they did so.

**20:33** And every man rose up from his place, and they joined battle at Baal-tamar. And the ambush of Israel was advancing from its place, from Maraagabe.

**20:34** And ten thousand chosen men out of all Israel came from opposite Gibeah. And the battle was heavy, and they did not know that calamity was overtaking them.

**20:35** And the Lord struck Benjamin before the sons of Israel, and the sons of Israel destroyed from Benjamin on that day twenty-five thousand one hundred men; all of these drew the sword.

**20:36** And the sons of Benjamin saw that they had been struck. And the men of Israel gave ground to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

**20:37** And as they withdrew, the ambush moved, and they spread out against Gibeah, and the ambush poured out and struck the city with the edge of the sword.

**20:38** And the sons of Israel had a sign with the ambush of the battle, that they should send up a signal of smoke from the city.

**20:39** And the sons of Israel saw that the ambush had already seized Gibeah, and they took their stand in the battle. And Benjamin began to strike down casualties among the men of Israel, about thirty men, for they said, "Again they are surely falling before us, as in the first battle."

**20:40** And the signal went up more and more over the city like a pillar of smoke. And Benjamin looked behind him, and look, the complete destruction of the city went up to heaven.

**20:41** And the men of Israel turned back, and the men of Benjamin hurried, because they saw that evil had come upon them.

**20:42** And they turned before the sons of Israel toward the road of the wilderness and fled; and the battle overtook them, and those from the cities were destroying them in their midst.

**20:43** And they were cutting down Benjamin, and pursued him from Noua close at his heels as far as opposite Gibeah toward the rising of the sun.

**20:44** And eighteen thousand men fell from Benjamin; all of these were men of might.

**20:45** And the rest turned and were fleeing into the wilderness toward the rock of Rimmon, and the sons of Israel gleaned five thousand men from them. And the sons of Israel went down after them as far as Gedan, and struck down two thousand men of them.

**20:46** And all who fell from Benjamin were twenty-five thousand men drawing the sword on that day; all of these were men of might.

**20:47** And the rest turned and fled into the wilderness toward the rock of Rimmon, six hundred men, and they stayed at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

**20:48** And the sons of Israel turned back against the sons of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword, from the city of Methla even to the livestock and even to everything that was found in all the cities; and they burned with fire the cities that were found.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — Greek zema is a transliteration of Hebrew zimmah ("lewdness, depravity"), paired here with apoptoma ("moral fall, disgrace").
- **20:15** — Codex Vaticanus reads "twenty-three thousand" (against twenty-six thousand in the Hebrew text) and in v. 16 describes the seven hundred men of Gibeah as "ambidextrous" (Greek amphoterodexioi) without repeating the number seven hundred.
- **20:26** — Greek teleias ("complete offerings") renders Hebrew shelamim ("peace offerings") etymologically from the root for completeness; in vv. 27-28 Codex Vaticanus places Israel's inquiry after the notice concerning the ark and Phinehas.
- **20:33** — Maraagabe is the Greek transliteration as a proper name of the Hebrew expression ma'areh-geba ("clearing/meadow of Geba").
- **20:43** — Noua is a Septuagint-distinct place name corresponding to Hebrew menuchah ("resting place" or "with ease"), and the Greek reads "cutting down" rather than "surrounding."
- **20:45** — Gedan is the Septuagint form of the place name corresponding to Gidom in the Hebrew text, and Codex Vaticanus reads "went down after them" rather than "pursued closely."
- **20:48** — Methla is a Septuagint-distinct city name reflecting a Greek rendering of the difficult Hebrew phrase meaning "from an inhabited city" or "from city, people."
