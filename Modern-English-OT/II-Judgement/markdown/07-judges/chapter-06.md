## Text

**6:1** And the sons of Israel did evil before the Lord, and the Lord gave them into the hand of Midian for seven years.

**6:2** And the hand of Midian prevailed over Israel; and because of Midian the sons of Israel made for themselves the crevices that are in the mountains, and the caves, and the steep places.

**6:3** And it happened whenever the sons of Israel sowed, Midian and Amalek would come up, and the sons of the east would come up with them; and they encamped against them,

**6:4** and they destroyed their produce as far as coming to Gaza, and they did not leave sustenance of life in the land of Israel, nor among the flocks a bull and a donkey.

**6:5** For they and their livestock would come up, and their tents would arrive like locusts in multitude, and for them and their camels there was no number; and they would come into the land of Israel and destroy it,

**6:6** and Israel became exceedingly poor because of Midian.

**6:7** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord because of Midian.

**6:8** And the Lord sent a man, a prophet, to the sons of Israel, and he said to them, "Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: I am the One who brought you up from the land of Egypt, and I brought you out from the house of your slavery,

**6:9** and I rescued you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of all who oppressed you, and I drove them out from before you, and I gave you their land;

**6:10** and I said to you, 'I am the Lord your God; you shall not fear the gods of the Amorite, among whom you will dwell in their land.' And you did not listen to My voice."

**6:11** And the angel of the Lord came and sat under the terebinth that was in Ephrathah, in what belonged to Joash, father of Esdri; and Gideon his son was beating out wheat in a winepress in order to escape from before Midian.

**6:12** And the angel of the Lord appeared to him and said to him, "The Lord is with you, mighty one of powers."

**6:13** And Gideon said to him, "In me, my lord, and if the Lord is with us, why have these evils found us? And where are all His wonders that our fathers recounted to us, saying, 'Did not the Lord bring us up from Egypt?' And now He has cast us off and given us into the hand of Midian."

**6:14** And the angel of the Lord turned to him and said, "Go in this strength of yours, and you will save Israel from the hand of Midian; look, I have sent you."

**6:15** And Gideon said to him, "In me, my lord, with what shall I save Israel? Look, my thousand has grown weak in Manasseh, and I am the least in the house of my father."

**6:16** And the angel of the Lord said to him, "The Lord will be with you, and you will strike Midian as one man."

**6:17** And Gideon said to him, "Now if I have found mercy in your eyes, and you will do for me today everything that you have spoken with me,

**6:18** do not depart from here until I come to you; and I will bring out the offering and set it before you." And he said, "I myself will sit until you return."

**6:19** And Gideon went in and prepared a kid of goats and an ephah of flour, unleavened cakes, and he put the meat in the basket and put the broth in the pot, and he brought them out to him under the terebinth, and came near.

**6:20** And the angel of God said to him, "Take the meat and the unleavened cakes and place them by that rock, and pour out the broth close by." And he did so.

**6:21** And the angel of the Lord stretched out the tip of the staff that was in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened cakes, and fire came up out of the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened cakes; and the angel of the Lord went away from his eyes.

**6:22** And Gideon saw that he was the angel of the Lord; and Gideon said, "Ah, ah, my Lord, Lord, for I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face!"

**6:23** And the Lord said to him, "Peace to you; do not fear, you will certainly not die."

**6:24** And Gideon built an altar there to the Lord and called it "Peace of the Lord"; to this day it is still in Ephrathah of the father of Esdri.

**6:25** And it happened on that night that the Lord said to him, "Take the bull calf that belongs to your father, and a second calf seven years old, and you shall tear down the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and you shall destroy the grove that is by it;

**6:26** and you shall build an altar to the Lord your God on the top of this Mauek in the ordered arrangement, and you shall take the second calf and offer up a burnt offering with the wood of the grove that you will utterly destroy."

**6:27** And Gideon took ten men from his own servants and did just as the Lord had spoken to him; and it happened, because he was too afraid of the house of his father and the men of the city to do it by day, that he did it by night.

**6:28** And the men of the city rose early in the morning, and look, the altar of Baal had been torn down, and the grove that was by it had been destroyed; and they saw the second calf, which he had offered up on the altar that had been built.

**6:29** And each man said to his neighbor, "Who has done this thing?" And they inquired and investigated, and they learned that Gideon son of Joash had done this thing.

**6:30** And the men of the city said to Joash, "Bring out your son and let him die, because he tore down the altar of Baal, and because he destroyed the grove that was by it."

**6:31** And Gideon son of Joash said to all the men who rose up against him, "Are you now contending for Baal? Or will you save him? Whoever contends for him, let him be put to death by morning; if he is a god, let him contend for himself, because he tore down his altar."

**6:32** And he called it Arbaal on that day, saying, "Let Baal contend against him, because his altar was torn down."

**6:33** And all Midian and Amalek and the sons of the east were gathered together, and they encamped in the valley of Jezreel.

**6:34** And the Spirit of the Lord empowered Gideon, and he blew a horn, and Abiezer was afraid after him.

**6:35** And he sent messengers into all Manasseh, and into Asher, and into Zebulun and Naphtali, and he went up to meet them.

**6:36** And Gideon said to God, "If You are saving Israel by my hand, just as You have spoken,

**6:37** look, I am placing the fleece of wool on the threshing floor; if dew is on the fleece alone and dryness on all the ground, I will know that You will save Israel by my hand, just as You have spoken."

**6:38** And it was so; and he rose early the next day and squeezed out the fleece, and dew dripped from the fleece, a full bowl of water.

**6:39** And Gideon said to God, "Please do not let Your anger be provoked against me, and I will speak once more; let me test even once more with the fleece, and let the dryness be on the fleece alone, and on all the ground let there be dew."

**6:40** And God did so on that night; and there was dryness on the fleece alone, and on all the ground there was dew.

## Translator Notes

- **6:11** — Vaticanus reads 'Ephrathah' (Ἐφραθά) where the Hebrew has Ophrah, and divides 'the Abiezrite' into 'father of Esdri' (πατρὸς τοῦ Ἐσδρεί; cf. v. 24 and 'Abiezer' in v. 34).
- **6:13** — Ἐν ἐμοί ('In me', also in v. 15) is a hyper-literal Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew particle of entreaty bî ('please' or 'pardon me').
- **6:17** — Vaticanus reads σήμερον ('today') rather than 'a sign' (σημεῖον), linking the conditional clause directly to the request in v. 18.
- **6:26** — Μαουέκ ('Mauek') is an untranslated Vaticanus transliteration of the Hebrew word for 'stronghold'.
- **6:31** — Codex Vaticanus makes 'Gideon son of Joash' the speaker here rather than Joash, and in v. 32 reads 'he called it Arbaal' (Ἀρβάαλ, for Hebrew Jerubbaal) with passive 'was torn down'.
- **6:34** — Vaticanus reads ἐφοβήθη ('was afraid') where the Hebrew means 'was summoned/called out'.
