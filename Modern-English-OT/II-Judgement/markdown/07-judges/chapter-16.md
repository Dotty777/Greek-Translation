## Text

**16:1** And Samson went to Gaza, and saw there a woman who was a prostitute, and he went in to her.

**16:2** And it was reported to the Gazites, saying, "Samson has come here." And they surrounded him and lay in ambush for him all night at the gate of the city, and they kept silent all night, saying, "Until dawn breaks, and let us kill him."

**16:3** And Samson slept until midnight; and he arose in the middle of the night and took hold of the doors of the gate of the city along with the two posts, and lifted them up along with the bar, and placed them on his shoulders, and went up to the top of the mountain that faces Hebron, and placed them there.

**16:4** And it came about after this that he loved a woman in Halsorek, and her name was Delilah.

**16:5** And the rulers of the foreigners went up to her and said to her, "Deceive him, and see in what his great strength lies, and by what means we will prevail over him and bind him in order to humble him; and we will give you, each man, one thousand one hundred pieces of silver."

**16:6** And Delilah said to Samson, "Please tell me in what your great strength lies, and with what you may be bound in order to be humbled."

**16:7** And Samson said to her, "If they bind me with seven moist bowstrings that have not been spoiled, then I will become weak and be like any man."

**16:8** And the rulers of the foreigners brought up to her seven moist bowstrings that had not been spoiled, and she bound him with them.

**16:9** And the ambush was sitting for her in the inner room, and she said to him, "Foreigners are upon you, Samson!" And he snapped the bowstrings as if someone were to pull apart a strand of flax when it smells fire. And his strength was not known.

**16:10** And Delilah said to Samson, "Look, you have deceived me and spoken lies to me; now then, tell me with what you may be bound."

**16:11** And he said to her, "If they securely bind me with new ropes with which no work has been done, then I will become weak and be like any man."

**16:12** And Delilah took new ropes and bound him with them, and the ambush came out of the inner room, and she said, "Foreigners are upon you, Samson!" And he snapped them off his arms like a thread.

**16:13** And Delilah said to Samson, "Look, you have deceived me and spoken lies to me. Please tell me with what you may be bound." And he said to her, "If you weave the seven braids of my head with the warp and drive them with the peg into the wall, then I will be weak like any man."

**16:14** And it came about while he was sleeping that Delilah took the seven braids of his head and wove them into the warp and fastened them with the peg into the wall, and she said, "Foreigners are upon you, Samson!" And he awoke from his sleep and pulled out the peg of the woven cloth from the wall.

**16:15** And Delilah said to Samson, "How do you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? This third time you have deceived me, and you have not told me in what your great strength lies."

**16:16** And it came about, when she pressed him hard with her words every day and distressed him, that he grew fainthearted to the point of dying.

**16:17** And he told her all his heart and said to her, "No iron has come upon my head, for I am a holy one of God from my mother's womb. If therefore I am shaved, my strength will depart from me, and I will become weak and be like all men."

**16:18** And Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, and she sent and called the rulers of the foreigners, saying, "Come up just this once more, for he has told me all his heart." And the rulers of the foreigners went up to her and brought up the silver in their hands.

**16:19** And Delilah put Samson to sleep on her knees, and she called a man and shaved the seven braids of his head; and she began to humble him, and his strength departed from him.

**16:20** And Delilah said, "Foreigners are upon you, Samson!" And he awoke from his sleep and said, "I will go out as once and again, and shake myself free." But he himself did not know that the Lord had departed from him.

**16:21** And the foreigners seized him and gouged out his eyes, and they brought him down to Gaza and shackled him with bronze shackles; and he was grinding in the prison house.

**16:22** And the hair of his head began to sprout after he had been shaved.

**16:23** And the rulers of the foreigners gathered together to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god and to rejoice, and they said, "The god has given Samson our enemy into our hand."

**16:24** And the people saw him and sang praise to their god, "Our god has delivered our enemy into our hand, the one who was devastating our land and who multiplied our wounded."

**16:25** And when their heart was gladdened, they said, "Call Samson out of the house of custody, and let him perform before us." And they called Samson from the prison house, and he was performing before them; and they were slapping him, and they stood him between the pillars.

**16:26** And Samson said to the young man who was holding his hand, "Let me go, and let me feel the pillars on which the house stands, and I will lean against them."

**16:27** And the house was full of the men and the women, and all the rulers of the foreigners were there; and on the roof were about seven hundred men and women looking on at the performances of Samson.

**16:28** And Samson wept to the Lord and said, "Adonai Lord, please remember me now and strengthen me just this once more, God, and I will repay one repayment to the foreigners for my two eyes."

**16:29** And Samson clasped the two pillars of the house on which the house stood, and leaned against them, and took hold of one with his right hand and one with his left hand.

**16:30** And Samson said, "Let my soul die with foreigners!" And he heaved with strength, and the house fell upon the rulers and upon all the people who were in it. And the dead whom Samson put to death in his death were more than those whom he put to death in his life.

**16:31** And his brothers and the house of his father went down and took him and went up, and they buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father. And he judged Israel for twenty years.

## Translator Notes

- **16:4** — Codex B fuses the Hebrew phrase "the Valley of Sorek" (nahal Soreq) into a single proper name, Halsorek (Halsorech).
- **16:7** — Codex B reads "that have not been spoiled" (me diephtharmenais, also in v. 8), reflecting a different sense of the Hebrew root than "that have not been dried."
- **16:13** — Codex B preserves the fuller wording in vv. 13-14 about driving the woven braids into the wall with a peg and becoming weak, which is shorter in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **16:17** — Codex B reads "iron" (sideros) where the Hebrew has "razor," and renders Hebrew "Nazirite to God" as "a holy one of God" (hagios theou).
- **16:27** — Codex B states "about seven hundred" men and women on the roof, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "about three thousand."
