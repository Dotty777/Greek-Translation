## Text

**13:1** And the sons of Israel again did evil before the Lord, and the Lord delivered them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

**13:2** And there was a certain man from Saral, from the clan of the family of the Danites, and his name was Manoah; and his wife was barren and had not given birth.

**13:3** And the angel of the Lord appeared to the woman and said to her, "Look, you are barren and have not given birth, and you will conceive a son.

**13:4** And now be careful, and do not drink wine or intoxicating drink, and do not eat anything unclean;

**13:5** for look, you are with child and will bear a son, and no iron shall come up upon his head, for the boy will be a Nazirite of God from the womb; and he himself will begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines."

**13:6** And the woman went in and spoke to her husband, saying, "A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of an angel of God, exceedingly fearsome; and I did not ask him where he was from, and he did not tell me his name.

**13:7** And he said to me, 'Look, you are with child and will bear a son. And now do not drink wine or intoxicating drink, and do not eat anything unclean, for the boy will be a holy one of God from the womb until the day of his death.'"

**13:8** And Manoah prayed to the Lord and said, "Please, Lord Adonai, may the man of God whom You sent come to us once more, and instruct us what we should do for the child who is to be born."

**13:9** And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the angel of God came again to the woman; and she was sitting in the field, and Manoah her husband was not with her.

**13:10** And the woman hurried and ran and told her husband, and said to him, "Look, the man who came to me the other day has appeared to me."

**13:11** And Manoah arose and went after his wife, and came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" And the angel said, "I am."

**13:12** And Manoah said, "Now your word will come to pass. What will be the rule for the child and his deeds?"

**13:13** And the angel of the Lord said to Manoah, "She shall guard herself from all that I have spoken to the woman.

**13:14** She shall not eat of anything that comes from the vine of wine, and let her not drink wine or strong intoxicating drink, and let her not eat anything unclean; all that I commanded him she shall observe."

**13:15** And Manoah said to the angel of the Lord, "Let us detain you here, and let us prepare before you a kid of the goats."

**13:16** And the angel of the Lord said to Manoah, "If you detain me, I will not eat of your bread; and if you prepare a burnt offering, you shall offer it up to the Lord." For Manoah did not know that he was the angel of the Lord.

**13:17** And Manoah said to the angel of the Lord, "What is your name? For may your word come to pass, and we will honor you."

**13:18** And the angel of the Lord said to him, "Why is it that you ask my name? And it is wonderful."

**13:19** And Manoah took the kid of the goats and the sacrifice and offered them up on the rock to the Lord, and He did a distinct thing, and Manoah and his wife were watching.

**13:20** And it happened when the flame went up above the altar to heaven, that the angel of the Lord went up in the flame of the altar. And Manoah and his wife were watching, and they fell on their faces to the ground.

**13:21** And the angel of the Lord did not appear again to Manoah and to his wife; then Manoah knew that he was the angel of the Lord.

**13:22** And Manoah said to his wife, "We will surely die, because we have seen God."

**13:23** And his wife said to him, "If the Lord had desired to put us to death, He would not have received a burnt offering and a sacrifice from our hand, and He would not have shown us all these things, and at such a time He would not have made us hear these things."

**13:24** And the woman bore a son and called his name Samson. And the boy grew up, and the Lord blessed him.

**13:25** And the Spirit of the Lord began to go out with him in the camp of Dan, and between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Codex Vaticanus reads the distinct place name "Saral" here (contrast Zorah in 13:25).
- **13:8** — Greek "en emoi" (literally "in me") is a literal rendering of the Hebrew entreaty particle "bi" ("please"), and "Kyrie Adonaie" combines "Lord" with a transliteration of Hebrew Adonai.
- **13:14** — Codex Vaticanus renders Hebrew strong drink with a double phrase ("sikera methysma," "strong intoxicating drink") and reads the masculine/neuter pronoun "auto" ("him") rather than "her."
- **13:19** — Greek "diechorisen poiesai" (literally "separated/distinguished to do") reflects a literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for acting wondrously.
- **13:25** — Codex Vaticanus reads "to go out with him" (Greek synekporeuesthai), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to stir him."
