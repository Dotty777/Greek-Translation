## Text

**5:1** And Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam were saying on that day:

**5:2** "A revelation was revealed in Israel; when people were unwilling, bless the Lord.

**5:3** Hear, kings, and give ear, satraps: I am for the Lord; I myself will sing, I will sing praises to the Lord, the God of Israel.

**5:4** Lord, at Your going forth in Seir, when You departed from the field of Edom, the earth shook, and heaven dripped dews, and the clouds dripped water.

**5:5** Mountains were shaken from the presence of the Lord Eloi, this Sinai from the presence of the Lord, the God of Israel.

**5:6** In the days of Shamgar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, they abandoned roads and walked footpaths; they walked crooked roads.

**5:7** Mighty men failed in Israel, they failed, until Deborah arose, until a mother arose in Israel.

**5:8** They chose new gods when cities of rulers fought, if a shield should be seen and a spear among forty thousand in Israel.

**5:9** My heart is toward the things ordained for Israel; you who offer yourselves willingly among the people, bless the Lord.

**5:10** You who ride on a female donkey at midday, sitting on a judgment seat and walking on roads of councilors by the road.

**5:11** Recount from the sound of those playing music, in the midst of those drawing water; there they will give righteous deeds. Lord, increase righteous deeds in Israel; then the people of the Lord went down to the cities.

**5:12** Awake, awake, Deborah; awake, awake, speak a song. Arise, Barak, and take captive your captivity, son of Abinoam.

**5:13** Then a remnant went down for the strong; the people of the Lord went down for Him among the mighty from me.

**5:14** Ephraim uprooted them in Amalek; after you, Benjamin, among your peoples. In me, Machir went down searching out, and from Zebulun, those drawing with a staff of a scribe's account.

**5:15** And leaders in Issachar were with Deborah and Barak. So Barak sent forth in valleys at his feet. For the portions of Reuben, great ones reaching the heart.

**5:16** Why did they sit between the double load to hear the piping of messengers? For the divisions of Reuben, great searchings of heart.

**5:17** Gilead camped beyond the Jordan; and Dan, why does he sojourn by ships? Asher sat on the coast of the seas, and at his outlets he will camp.

**5:18** Zebulun is a people that scorned its life to death, and Naphtali on the heights of the field.

**5:19** Their kings came, they did battle; then the kings of Canaan fought in Taanach by the water of Megiddo; they did not take a gift of silver.

**5:20** From heaven the stars did battle; from their paths they did battle with Sisera.

**5:21** The torrent of Kishon swept them away, the torrent of ancient ones, the torrent of Kishon; my mighty soul will trample him down.

**5:22** Then the heels of a horse were entangled; in haste his strong ones hastened.

**5:23** 'Curse Meroz,' said the angel of the Lord, 'curse; cursed is everyone who inhabits it, because they did not come to the help of the Lord, to help among the mighty.'

**5:24** May Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, be blessed among women; above women in tents may she be blessed.

**5:25** Water he asked for, milk she gave; in a bowl of nobles she brought butter.

**5:26** Her left hand she stretched out to a peg, and her right hand to a hammer of laborers; and she hammered Sisera, pierced through his head and struck, pierced through his temple.

**5:27** Between her feet he rolled down, he fell and lay down between her feet; sinking down, he fell; just as he sank down, there he fell, undone.

**5:28** Through the window the mother of Sisera peered out beyond the arrow-slit: 'Why was his chariot put to shame? Why have the feet of his chariots delayed?'

**5:29** Her wise princesses answered her, and she returned her words to herself:

**5:30** 'Will they not find him dividing spoil? A compassionate one will show compassion for the head of a man; spoil of dyed fabrics for Sisera, spoil of dyed fabrics of embroidery, dyed fabrics of embroiderers are these, for his neck, spoil.'

**5:31** So may all Your enemies perish, Lord; and may those who love Him be like the going forth of the sun in its power." And the land was quiet forty years.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Vaticanus text reads the imperfect ἦσαν with λέγοντες ('were saying'); in uncial script without iota subscript this is identical to ᾖσαν ('sang'), which reflects the Hebrew.
- **5:2** — Codex Vaticanus uniquely reads ἀκουσιασθῆναι ('were unwilling'), contrasting with ἑκουσιαζόμενοι ('offer themselves willingly') in v. 9; ἀπεκαλύφθη ἀποκάλυμμα literally means 'a revelation was revealed' (or 'an uncovering was uncovered').
- **5:5** — The Vaticanus phrase Κυρίου Ἐλωεί ('the Lord Eloi') retains an untranslated transliteration of the Hebrew title Elohei ('God') alongside 'the Lord'.
- **5:16** — Vaticanus reads ἀγγέλων ('messengers' or 'angels'), likely an inner-Greek corruption of ἀγελῶν ('herds/flocks'); διγομίας literally denotes a double pack-load.
- **5:30** — Οἰκτείρμων οἰκτειρήσει ('a compassionate one will show compassion') reflects the Greek translator reading the Hebrew root r-ḥ-m as 'mercy/compassion' rather than 'womb/maiden'.
