## Text

**17:1** And there was a man from Mount Ephraim, and his name was Micah.

**17:2** And he said to his mother, "The one thousand one hundred pieces of silver that you took for yourself, and you cursed me and spoke in my ears, look, the silver is with me; I took it." And his mother said, "Blessed be my son by the Lord."

**17:3** And he returned the one thousand one hundred pieces of silver to his mother. And his mother said, "I have wholly consecrated the silver to the Lord from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image; and now I will return it to you."

**17:4** And he returned the silver to his mother; and his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave it to a silversmith, and he made it into a carved image and a cast image; and it was in the house of Micah.

**17:5** And the house of Micah was for him a house of God; and he made an ephod and teraphim, and he filled the hand of one of his sons, and he became a priest to him.

**17:6** Now in those days there was no king in Israel; each man was doing what was right in his eyes.

**17:7** And there was a young man from Bethlehem of the clan of Judah, and he was a Levite, and this man was residing there.

**17:8** And the man went from Bethlehem, the city of Judah, to reside in whatever place he might find; and he came as far as Mount Ephraim and as far as the house of Micah, to make his way.

**17:9** And Micah said to him, "Where are you coming from?" And he said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem of Judah, and I am going to reside in whatever place I may find."

**17:10** And Micah said to him, "Stay with me, and become a father and a priest to me, and I will give you ten pieces of silver per day and a set of garments and the things for your living."

**17:11** And the Levite went and began to reside with the man; and the young man was with him like one of his sons.

**17:12** And Micah filled the hand of the Levite, and he became a priest to him, and he was in the house of Micah.

**17:13** And Micah said, "Now I know that the Lord will do good to me, because the Levite has become a priest to me."

## Translator Notes

- **17:2** — Codex B reads active "that you took for yourself" and "you cursed me," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads passive "that were taken from you" and "you uttered a curse."
- **17:5** — Codex B reads "the house of Micah" where the Hebrew has "the man Micah"; "filled the hand" (also in v. 12) is a literal idiom for consecrating or installing someone as a priest.
- **17:10** — Codex B reads singular "per day" (eis hemeran), where the Hebrew has plural "for the days" (idiomatically "per year").
- **17:11** — Codex B renders the Hebrew verb as "began" (erxato) rather than "agreed" or "was content," reflecting a homonymous Hebrew root.
