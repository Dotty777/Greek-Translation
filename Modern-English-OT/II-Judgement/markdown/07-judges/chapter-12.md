## Text

**12:1** And the men of Ephraim cried out, and crossed over to the north, and said to Jephthah, "Why did you cross over to do battle against the sons of Ammon, and have not called us to go with you? We will burn your house over you with fire."

**12:2** And Jephthah said to them, "I was a fighting man, I and my people and the sons of Ammon, exceedingly; and I cried out to you, and you did not save me from their hand.

**12:3** And I saw that you are not a savior, and I placed my life in my hand and crossed over to the sons of Ammon, and the Lord gave them into my hand. And why have you come up against me on this day to do battle against me?"

**12:4** And Jephthah gathered together all the men of Gilead and did battle against Ephraim; and the men of Gilead struck down Ephraim, because the survivors of Ephraim said, "You are Gilead in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasseh."

**12:5** And Gilead seized beforehand the fords of the Jordan of Ephraim. And the survivors of Ephraim said to them, "Let us cross over." And the men of Gilead said to them, "Are you an Ephraimite?" And he said, "No."

**12:6** And they said to him, "Say now, 'Ear of grain.'" And he did not manage to speak that way, and they seized him and slaughtered him at the fords of the Jordan. And there fell at that time from Ephraim forty-two thousand.

**12:7** And Jephthah judged Israel sixty years. And Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in his city in Gilead.

**12:8** And after him Ibzan from Bethlehem judged Israel.

**12:9** And he had thirty sons and thirty daughters, whom he sent outside, and thirty daughters he brought in for his sons from outside. And he judged Israel seven years.

**12:10** And Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

**12:11** And after him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years.

**12:12** And Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Elon in the land of Zebulun.

**12:13** And after him Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel.

**12:14** And he had forty sons and thirty grandsons, riding on seventy colts. And he judged Israel eight years.

**12:15** And Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of Amalek.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Greek renders the Hebrew directional term as "to the north" rather than the town name Zaphon.
- **12:4** — The Greek makes "the survivors of Ephraim" the subject of "said," whereas the Hebrew is commonly read as "Fugitives of Ephraim are you, Gilead."
- **12:6** — Codex Vaticanus translates the Hebrew test word Shibboleth by its literal meaning, "Ear of grain" (Greek Stachys), rather than transliterating its sound.
- **12:7** — Codex Vaticanus reads "sixty years," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "six years."
- **12:12** — The Greek uses the same name form (Ailom) for both the judge and his burial place, corresponding to Elon and Aijalon in the Hebrew.
