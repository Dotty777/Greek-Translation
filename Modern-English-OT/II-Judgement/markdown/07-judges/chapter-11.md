## Text

**11:1** And Jephthah the Gileadite was exalted in strength; and he was the son of a prostitute, who bore Jephthah to Gilead.

**11:2** And the wife of Gilead bore sons to him; and the sons of the wife grew up, and they drove out Jephthah and said to him, "You will not inherit in the house of our father, because you are the son of a courtesan."

**11:3** And Jephthah fled from before his brothers and lived in the land of Tob; and worthless men gathered to Jephthah and went out with him.

**11:4** And it happened, when the sons of Ammon did battle with Israel,

**11:5** that the elders of Gilead went to get Jephthah from the land of Tob.

**11:6** And they said to Jephthah, "Come, and you will be commander over us, and let us do battle against the sons of Ammon."

**11:7** And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "Did you not hate me, and drive me out of my father's house, and send me away from you? And why have you come to me now when you are in need?"

**11:8** And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "For this reason we have now returned to you, and you will go with us and do battle against the sons of Ammon, and you will be leader over us, over all who live in Gilead."

**11:9** And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "If you bring me back to do battle against the sons of Ammon, and the Lord delivers them before me, then will I be leader over you?"

**11:10** And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "May the Lord be listening between us if we do not do so according to your word."

**11:11** And Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people set him over them as head and as commander; and Jephthah spoke all his words before the Lord in Mizpah.

**11:12** And Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon, saying, "What is there between me and you, that you have come to me to do battle in my land?"

**11:13** And the king of the sons of Ammon said to the messengers of Jephthah, "Because Israel took my land when he came up out of Egypt, from the Arnon even to the Jabbok and to the Jordan; and now return them in peace, and I will go."

**11:14** And Jephthah once again sent messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon.

**11:15** And he said to him, "This is what Jephthah says: Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the sons of Ammon.

**11:16** For when they came up out of Egypt, Israel went in the wilderness as far as the Sea of Siph, and came to Kadesh.

**11:17** And Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, 'Please let me pass through your land.' And the king of Edom did not listen; and he sent to the king of Moab, and he did not consent. And Israel stayed in Kadesh.

**11:18** And he went in the wilderness and went around the land of Edom and the land of Moab; and he came from the rising of the sun to the land of Moab, and they encamped beyond the Arnon, and he did not enter into the borders of Moab, for the Arnon is the border of Moab.

**11:19** And Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, king of Heshbon, and Israel said to him, 'Please let us pass through your land to our place.'

**11:20** And Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his territory; and Sihon gathered all his people together, and they encamped at Jahaz, and he did battle against Israel.

**11:21** And the Lord God of Israel delivered Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and he struck him; and Israel took possession of all the land of the Amorites who lived in that land,

**11:22** from the Arnon even to the Jabbok, and from the wilderness to the Jordan.

**11:23** And now the Lord God of Israel has removed the Amorites from before His people Israel, and will you possess them?

**11:24** Will you not possess whatever Hamos your god gives you to possess? And all those whom the Lord our God has removed from before us, them we will possess.

**11:25** And now, are you any better at all than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever contend with Israel, or did he ever wage war against him

**11:26** while they lived in Heshbon and in its territories, and in the land of Aroer and in its territories, and in all the cities that are beside the Jordan, for three hundred years? And why did you not rescue them at that time?

**11:27** And now I myself have not sinned against you, yet you are doing evil toward me by doing battle against me. May the Lord, the Judge, judge today between the sons of Israel and the sons of Ammon."

**11:28** And the king of the sons of Ammon did not listen to the words of Jephthah that he had sent to him.

**11:29** And the Spirit of the Lord came upon Jephthah, and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh, and he passed through the watchtower of Gilead to the region beyond the sons of Ammon.

**11:30** And Jephthah vowed a vow to the Lord and said, "If You indeed give the sons of Ammon into my hand,

**11:31** then the one coming out, whoever comes out from the door of my house to meet me when I return in peace from the sons of Ammon, will belong to the Lord; I will offer him up as a burnt offering."

**11:32** And Jephthah crossed over to the sons of Ammon to do battle against them, and the Lord delivered them into his hand.

**11:33** And he struck them from Aroer until one comes to Arnon, twenty cities in number, and as far as Abel-keramim, with a very great slaughter; and the sons of Ammon were subdued before the sons of Israel.

**11:34** And Jephthah came to Mizpah, to his house, and look, his daughter was coming out to meet him with tambourines and dances; and she was an only child; he had no other son or daughter.

**11:35** And it happened, when he saw her, he tore his clothes and said, "Ah, ah, my daughter! You have greatly troubled me, and you were in my trouble; and I myself have opened my mouth against you to the Lord, and I will not be able to turn back."

**11:36** And she said to him, "Father, you have opened your mouth to the Lord; do to me just as has gone out from your mouth, since the Lord has executed vengeance for you on your enemies, on the sons of Ammon."

**11:37** And she said to her father, "Please let my father do this thing: leave me alone for two months, and I will go and go down upon the mountains, and I will weep over my virginity, I myself and my companions."

**11:38** And he said, "Go." And he sent her away for two months. And she went, she and her companions, and wept over her virginity upon the mountains.

**11:39** And it happened at the end of the two months that she returned to her father, and he carried out on her his vow that he had vowed; and she did not know a man. And it became an ordinance in Israel:

**11:40** Year by year the daughters of Israel would go to lament the daughter of Jephthah of Gilead for four days in the year.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Greek describes Jephthah as "exalted in strength" (ἐπηρμένος δυνάμει) and makes his mother the subject of the final clause ("who bore Jephthah to Gilead").
- **11:2** — Greek reads "a courtesan" (ἑταίρας) rather than "another" (ἑτέρας, corresponding to the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "another woman").
- **11:16** — Greek transliterates Hebrew Suf as "Siph" (Σείφ) in "Sea of Siph" rather than translating it as "the Red Sea."
- **11:24** — Greek reads "Hamos" (Ἁμώς), a Codex Vaticanus variant form of the deity Chemosh (Hebrew Kemosh).
- **11:26** — Greek reads "its territories" (for Hebrew "its villages"), "the land of Aroer," and "beside the Jordan" (where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "beside the Arnon").
- **11:27** — Greek uses the emphatic kaige idiom ἐγώ εἰμι (literally "I am") before a finite verb here and in 11:35 (and with a noun in 11:37) to render emphatic Hebrew "I," translated here as "I myself."
- **11:33** — Greek reads "Arnon" (Ἀρνών) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Minnith," and adds "in number" before "twenty cities."
