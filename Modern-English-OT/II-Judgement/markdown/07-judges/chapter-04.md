## Text

**4:1** And the sons of Israel again did what was evil before the Lord; and Ehud had died.

**4:2** And the Lord sold the sons of Israel into the hand of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; and the commander of his army was Sisera, and he lived in Harosheth of the nations.

**4:3** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord, because he had nine hundred iron chariots, and he oppressed Israel forcefully for twenty years.

**4:4** And Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, she judged Israel at that time.

**4:5** And she sat under the palm tree of Deborah between Bama and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the sons of Israel went up to her for judgment.

**4:6** And Deborah sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam from Kedesh-naphtali, and she said to him, "Has not the Lord, the God of Israel, commanded you, and you shall go to Mount Tabor, and you shall take with yourself ten thousand men from the sons of Naphtali and from the sons of Zebulun,

**4:7** and I will march to you to the torrent Kishon against Sisera, the commander of the army of Jabin, and his chariots and his multitude, and I will deliver him into your hands?"

**4:8** And Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go, and if you do not go, I will not go; for I do not know the day on which the Lord prospers the angel with me."

**4:9** And she said, "I will surely go with you; only know that your honor will not be on the way that you are going, for the Lord will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman." And Deborah rose up and went with Barak from Kedesh.

**4:10** And Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali from Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up at his feet; and Deborah went up with him.

**4:11** And Heber the Kenite had separated himself from Kain, from the sons of Jobab, the father-in-law of Moses, and he pitched his tent as far as the oak of the greedy, which is next to Kedesh.

**4:12** And it was reported to Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.

**4:13** And Sisera called all his chariots, nine hundred iron chariots, and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth of the nations to the torrent Kishon.

**4:14** And Deborah said to Barak, "Rise up, for this is the day on which the Lord has delivered Sisera into your hand, for the Lord will go out before you." And Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand men were behind him.

**4:15** And the Lord threw Sisera and all his chariots and all his camp into panic with the edge of the sword before Barak; and Sisera got down from his chariot and fled on his feet.

**4:16** And Barak was pursuing after the chariots and after the camp as far as Harosheth of the nations; and all the camp of Sisera fell by the edge of the sword; not even one was left.

**4:17** And Sisera fled on his feet to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the companion, the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin, king of Hazor, and the house of Heber the Kenite.

**4:18** And Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not be afraid." And he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

**4:19** And Sisera said to her, "Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty." And she opened the skin of milk and gave him a drink, and she covered him.

**4:20** And Sisera said to her, "Please stand at the door of the tent, and it will be, if a man comes to you and asks you and says, 'Is there a man here?' then you shall say, 'There is not.'"

**4:21** And Jael, the wife of Heber, took the tent peg and put the hammer in her hand, and she went in to him secretly and drove the peg into his temple, and it went right through into the ground; and he, stunned, fainted and died.

**4:22** And look, Barak was pursuing Sisera, and Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, and I will show you the man whom you are seeking." And he went in to her, and look, Sisera lay cast down, dead, and the peg was in his temple.

**4:23** And on that day God routed Jabin, king of Canaan, before the sons of Israel.

**4:24** And the hand of the sons of Israel kept going on and growing harder against Jabin, king of Canaan, until they completely destroyed Jabin, king of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — "Bama" (Bama) is the distinctive Codex Vaticanus place name where other witnesses and the Hebrew read Ramah.
- **4:7** — Codex Vaticanus includes the preposition epi ("against") before Sisera, yielding an intransitive military sense for epaxo ("march" or "advance"), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew omit it ("draw Sisera to you").
- **4:8** — The closing clause "for I do not know the day on which the Lord prospers the angel with me" is a notable Septuagint addition absent from the Hebrew text.
- **4:9** — Codex Vaticanus reads "from Kedesh" (ek Kades), repeated in 4:10, where the Hebrew has "to Kedesh."
- **4:11** — "Jobab" (Iobab) is the Septuagint name form corresponding to Hebrew Hobab, and "the oak of the greedy" (dryos pleonektounton) translates the Hebrew place name Zaanannim as a Greek participle.
- **4:17** — "Heber the companion" (Chaber hetairou) reflects a Codex Vaticanus doublet that both transliterates the name Heber and translates its Hebrew root meaning ("companion" or "ally").
