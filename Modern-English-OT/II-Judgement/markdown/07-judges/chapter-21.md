## Text

**21:1** And the sons of Israel swore at Mizpah, saying, "No man from among us will give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife."

**21:2** And the people came to Bethel and sat there until evening before God; and they lifted up their voice and wept with a great weeping.

**21:3** And they said, "Why, Lord, God of Israel, has this happened, that today one tribe should be missing from Israel?"

**21:4** And it happened on the next day that the people rose early, and built an altar there, and offered up burnt offerings and complete offerings.

**21:5** And the sons of Israel said, "Who did not go up in the assembly from all the tribes of Israel to the Lord?" For the oath was great against those who had not gone up to the Lord, to Mizpah, saying, "He will certainly be put to death."

**21:6** And the sons of Israel were moved with compassion toward Benjamin their brother, and said, "One tribe has been cut off today from Israel.

**21:7** What shall we do for them, the remaining ones who are left, for wives? And we have sworn by the Lord not to give them any of our daughters as wives."

**21:8** And they said, "Which one from the tribes of Israel did not go up to the Lord, to Mizpah?" And look, no man had come to the camp from Jabesh-gilead, to the assembly.

**21:9** And the people were mustered, and there was not a man there from the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead.

**21:10** And the congregation sent twelve thousand men there from the sons of might, and commanded them, saying, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword.

**21:11** And this you shall do: every male and every woman who has known lying with a male you shall devote to destruction, but the virgins you shall preserve alive." And they did so.

**21:12** And they found from among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young virgin women, who had not known a man by lying with a male, and they brought them to the camp, to Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

**21:13** And the whole congregation sent and spoke to the sons of Benjamin at the rock of Rimmon, and called them to peace.

**21:14** And Benjamin returned to the sons of Israel at that time, and the sons of Israel gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the daughters of Jabesh-gilead; and it pleased them so.

**21:15** And the people were moved with compassion for Benjamin, because the Lord had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.

**21:16** And the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for the remaining ones for wives? For women have been wiped out from Benjamin."

**21:17** And they said, "An inheritance of survivors belongs to Benjamin, and a tribe shall not be blotted out from Israel.

**21:18** For we will not be able to give them wives from our daughters, because we swore among the sons of Israel, saying, 'Cursed is the one who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

**21:19** And they said, "Look now, there is a feast of the Lord in Shiloh year by year, which is north of Bethel, toward the rising of the sun, on the road that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah."

**21:20** And they commanded the sons of Benjamin, saying, "Go, lie in ambush in the vineyards.

**21:21** And you shall watch, and look, if the daughters of the inhabitants of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then you shall come out from the vineyards and seize for yourselves, each man a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**21:22** And it will happen, when their fathers or their brothers come to contend with you, that we will say to them, 'Show mercy to us regarding them, because we did not take, each man his wife, in the battle, for it was not you who gave them to them; as a lot, incur guilt.'"

**21:23** And the sons of Benjamin did so, and took wives according to their number from the dancing women whom they seized; and they went and returned to their inheritance, and built the cities and settled in them.

**21:24** And the sons of Israel departed from there at that time, each man to his tribe and to his clan, and they went out from there, each man to his inheritance.

**21:25** Now in those days there was no king in Israel; each man would do what was right in his own sight.

## Translator Notes

- **21:4** — The Codex Vaticanus Greek reads teleias ("complete [offerings]"), rendering Hebrew shelamim (traditionally "peace offerings") by its root sense of wholeness or completeness, rather than eirenikas ("peace offerings") as in Codex Alexandrinus.
- **21:11** — The explicit command to preserve the virgins alive and the notice "And they did so" are present in the Septuagint Greek but absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **21:14** — Codex Vaticanus reads "and it pleased them so" (kai eresen autois houtos), contrasting with the Hebrew ("yet there were not enough for them") and Codex Alexandrinus ("and it did not suffice for them so"), likely reflecting an early Greek scribal shift from ouk erkesen ("did not suffice") to eresen ("pleased").
- **21:22** — The Codex Vaticanus Greek reads "with you" (rather than the Hebrew "with us") and ends with the obscure "as a lot, incur guilt" (hos kleros plemmelesate), likely an early Greek corruption of "at this time [lit. as the season], you will incur guilt" (hos kairos plemmelesete; cf. Hebrew "now you would be guilty").
- **21:25** — Codex Vaticanus reads "in his own sight" (enopion autou, lit. "before himself") rather than "in his own eyes" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and Codex Alexandrinus.
