## Text

**18:1** In those days there was no king in Israel; and in those days the tribe of Dan was seeking an inheritance for itself to live in, because until that day no inheritance had fallen to it among the tribes of Israel.

**18:2** And the sons of Dan sent from their clans five men, sons of strength, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and explore it, and they said to them, "Go and explore the land." And they came as far as Mount Ephraim, as far as the house of Micah; and they themselves spent the night there,

**18:3** in the house of Micah. And they recognized the voice of the young man, the Levite, and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? And what are you doing in this place? And what do you have here?"

**18:4** And he said to them, "Micah did such and such for me, and hired me, and I became a priest for him."

**18:5** And they said to him, "Please inquire of God, and we will know whether our way on which we are going will prosper."

**18:6** And the priest said to them, "Go in peace; your way on which you are going is before the Lord."

**18:7** And the five men went and came to Laish; and they saw the people who were in the midst of it living in security, like the quiet custom of the Sidonians; and there is no one deterring or shaming any matter in the land, an heir squeezing from a treasury; and they are far from the Sidonians, and they have no dealings with anyone.

**18:8** And the five men came to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and they said to their brothers, "Why are you sitting?"

**18:9** And they said, "Rise up and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and look, it is very good; and are you keeping quiet? Do not hesitate to go and enter to inherit the land.

**18:10** And when you come, you will enter to a people in security, and the land is wide, because God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

**18:11** And they set out from there from the clans of Dan, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, six hundred men girded with weapons of battle.

**18:12** And they went up and encamped in Kiriath-jearim in Judah. Therefore in that place it was called Camp of Dan, until this day; look, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

**18:13** And they passed on from there to Mount Ephraim and came as far as the house of Micah.

**18:14** And the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish answered and said to their brothers, "You know that in this house there is an ephod and teraphim and a carved image and a cast image; and now know what you will do."

**18:15** And they turned aside there and entered into the house of the young man, the Levite, the house of Micah, and asked after his welfare.

**18:16** And the six hundred men who were girded with their weapons of battle were standing by the doors of the gate, those from the sons of Dan.

**18:17** And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up,

**18:18** and they entered there into the house of Micah, and the priest was standing; and they took the carved image and the ephod and the teraphim and the cast image. And the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

**18:19** And they said to him, "Keep silent, put your hand over your mouth, and come with us, and become a father and a priest to us; is it better for you to be a priest of the house of one man, or for you to become a priest of a tribe and a house for a clan of Israel?"

**18:20** And the heart of the priest was made glad, and he took the ephod and the teraphim and the carved image and the cast image, and went into the midst of the people.

**18:21** And they turned and departed, and they put the children and the possessions and the baggage in front of them.

**18:22** They had gone far from the house of Micah, and look, Micah and the men who were in the houses beside the house of Micah cried out and overtook the sons of Dan.

**18:23** And the sons of Dan turned their faces and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have cried out?"

**18:24** And Micah said, "Because you have taken my carved image that I made, and the priest, and have gone away; and what do I have left? And why do you say this to me, 'Why are you crying out?'"

**18:25** And the sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, or else men bitter in soul may confront us, and they will add a life and the life of your house."

**18:26** And the sons of Dan went on their way; and Micah saw that they were stronger than he was, and he returned to his house.

**18:27** And the sons of Dan took what Micah had made and the priest who belonged to him, and they came against Laish, against a people quiet and trusting in security; and they struck them with the edge of the sword, and they burned the city with fire.

**18:28** And there was no deliverer, because it is far from the Sidonians, and they have no dealings with anyone, and it is in the valley of the house of Rahab. And they built the city and settled in it,

**18:29** and they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; and Oulamais was the name of the city formerly.

**18:30** And the sons of Dan set up for themselves the carved image; and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the exile of the land.

**18:31** And they placed for themselves the carved image that Micah had made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh. And it happened in those days that there was no king in Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — The Greek idiom 'in security' literally reads 'in hope' (used in the Septuagint for living securely). The phrase 'deterring or shaming any matter' doubles the rendering of the underlying Hebrew, and 'an heir squeezing from a treasury' reflects the Codex Vaticanus rendering of an obscure Hebrew phrase concerning an oppressive ruler.
- **18:25** — The Codex Vaticanus Greek reads 'confront us' (first person) and 'add a life' (rendering Hebrew 'gather/lose' as 'add'), differing from the Hebrew text's 'fall upon you, and you lose your life.'
- **18:28** — The Greek reads 'the house of Rahab,' an LXX-distinct form corresponding to the place name Beth-rehob in the Hebrew text.
- **18:29** — The Greek proper name Oulamais fuses the Hebrew conjunction and city name ('however, Laish') into a single former name for the city.
- **18:30** — Codex Vaticanus reads 'Manasseh,' agreeing with the Masoretic Hebrew reading (with its suspended letter nun), whereas other Greek witnesses such as Codex Alexandrinus read 'Moses.'
