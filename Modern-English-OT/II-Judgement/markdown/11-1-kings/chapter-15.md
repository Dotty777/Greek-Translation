## Text

**15:1** And in the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abiu son of Jeroboam became king over Judah,

**15:2** and he reigned six years; and the name of his mother was Maacah, daughter of Absalom.

**15:3** And he walked in the sins of his father which he committed before him, and his heart was not complete with the Lord his God like the heart of his father.

**15:4** For on account of David the Lord gave him a remnant, so that He might establish his children after him and establish Jerusalem,

**15:5** since David did what was right before the Lord; he did not turn aside from all that He commanded him all the days of his life.

**15:7** And the rest of the matters of Abiu and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah? And there was war between Abiu and Jeroboam.

**15:8** And Abiu slept with his fathers in the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Asa his son became king in his place.

**15:9** In the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa became king over Judah,

**15:10** and he reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Ana, daughter of Absalom.

**15:11** And Asa did what was right before the Lord, like David his father.

**15:12** And he removed the initiation rites from the land, and he sent away all the practices which his fathers had done.

**15:13** And Ana his mother he removed from being leader, because she held an assembly in her grove; and Asa cut down her hiding places and burned them with fire in the brook of the cedars.

**15:14** But the high places he did not remove; yet the heart of Asa was complete with the Lord all his days.

**15:15** And he brought in the pillars of his father, and he brought his own pillars into the house of the Lord, of silver and gold, and vessels.

**15:16** And there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all the days.

**15:17** And Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah, and he built Ramah so that there would be no one going out or coming in to Asa king of Judah.

**15:18** And Asa took the silver and the gold that was found in the treasuries of the house of the king, and he gave them into the hands of his servants; and King Asa sent them to the son of Hader, son of Taberema, son of Hazin, king of Syria, who lived in Damascus, saying,

**15:19** "Make a covenant between me and my father and your father; look, I have sent you gifts, silver and gold; come, break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, and he will go up from me."

**15:20** And the son of Hader listened to King Asa, and he sent the commanders of his own army against the cities of Israel; and they struck Ain, Dan, and Adelmath, and all Chezrath, as far as all the land of Naphtali.

**15:21** And it happened, when Baasha heard, that he stopped building Ramah and returned to Tirzah.

**15:22** And King Asa summoned all Judah to Ainakim, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its timbers which Baasha had built, and with them King Asa built every hill of Benjamin and the watchtower.

**15:23** And the rest of the matters of Asa, and all his might which he performed, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah? Yet in the time of his old age he suffered pain in his feet.

**15:24** And Asa slept and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Jehoshaphat his son became king in his place.

**15:25** And Nebat son of Jeroboam became king over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned in Israel two years.

**15:26** And he did what was evil before the Lord, and he walked in the way of his father and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

**15:27** And Baasha son of Ahijah besieged him at the house of Belaan; the son of Ahijah also wounded him at Gibbethon, which belonged to the foreigners; and Nebat and all Israel were besieging Gibbethon.

**15:28** And Baasha put him to death in the third year of King Asa son of Abiu, and he became king.

**15:29** And it happened, when he became king, that he struck the house of Jeroboam, and he did not leave behind any breath of Jeroboam until he destroyed him, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of the servant Ahijah the Shilonite,

**15:30** because of the sins of Jeroboam, in that he caused Israel to sin, and in his provocation by which he provoked the Lord God of Israel to anger.

**15:31** And the rest of the matters of Nebat and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:33** And in the third year of the king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah reigned over Israel in Tirzah twenty-four years.

**15:34** And he did what was evil before the Lord, and he walked in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat and in his sins, in that he caused Israel to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — Greek reads "Abiu son of Jeroboam" (and "six years" in 15:2, omitting 15:6 and 15:32), where the Hebrew text has "Abijam" son of Rehoboam reigning three years.
- **15:13** — Here and in 15:10, Greek names Asa's mother "Ana" (Hebrew: Maacah), describes an "assembly in her grove" and "hiding places" (Hebrew: an abominable image for Asherah), and renders Hebrew "Kidron" literally as "of the cedars."
- **15:18** — Greek mentions only the royal treasuries (omitting the Lord's house) and renders the Syrian royal lineage as "the son of Hader, son of Taberema, son of Hazin" (Hebrew: Ben-hadad, Tabrimmon, Hezion); in 15:20 Greek similarly reads "Ain," "Adelmath," and "Chezrath" (Hebrew: Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Chinneroth).
- **15:22** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew phrase "none was exempt" as a place name ("to Ainakim") and translates the place names Geba and Mizpah as common nouns ("every hill of Benjamin and the watchtower").
- **15:25** — Here and in 15:27, 31, Greek names Jeroboam's son "Nebat" (Nabath/Nabat, identical to Jeroboam's father) where the Hebrew has "Nadab"; in 15:27 Greek also reads "house of Belaan" (Hebrew: house of Issachar) and an unusual verb (literally "gashed/cut") rendered "wounded."
