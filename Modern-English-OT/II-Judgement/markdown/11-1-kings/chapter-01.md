## Text

**1:1** Now King David was old, advanced in days, and they kept covering him with garments, yet he did not grow warm.

**1:2** And his servants said, "Let them search for a young virgin woman for the king, and she will stand before the king and keep him warm, and she will lie with him, and my lord the king will grow warm."

**1:3** And they searched for a beautiful young woman from all the territory of Israel, and they found Abishag the Shunammite and brought her to the king.

**1:4** And the young woman was exceedingly beautiful; and she was keeping the king warm and serving him, yet the king did not know her.

**1:5** Now Adonijah son of Haggith was exalting himself, saying, "I will be king." And he prepared for himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.

**1:6** And his father had never restrained him, saying, "Why have you done this?" And moreover, he himself was very handsome in appearance, and she bore him after Absalom.

**1:7** And his words were with Joab the son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, and they gave help, following Adonijah.

**1:8** But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei, and Resei, and the mighty sons of David were not following Adonijah.

**1:9** And Adonijah sacrificed sheep and calves and lambs by Aithe of Zoheleth, which was next to Rogel, and he invited his brothers and the prominent men of Judah, servants of the king.

**1:10** But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, or Benaiah, or the mighty men, or Solomon his brother.

**1:11** And Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon, saying, "Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, and our lord David does not know?

**1:12** And now, come, please let me give you counsel, and rescue your life and the life of your son Solomon.

**1:13** Come, go in to King David and speak to him, saying, 'Did you not, my lord the king, swear to your maidservant, saying, "Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he himself will sit on my throne"? And why has Adonijah become king?'

**1:14** And look, while you are still speaking there with the king, I also will come in afterward and complete your words."

**1:15** So Bathsheba went in to the king into the inner chamber. Now the king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was serving the king.

**1:16** And Bathsheba bowed and paid homage to the king. And the king said, "What is the matter with you?"

**1:17** And she said, "Lord, you swore by your God to your maidservant, saying, 'Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne.'

**1:18** And now, look, Adonijah has become king, and you, my lord the king, do not know.

**1:19** And he has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the sons of the king, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army, yet he has not invited Solomon your servant.

**1:20** And as for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are toward you, to tell them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.

**1:21** And it will be, when my lord the king sleeps with his fathers, that I and Solomon my son will be offenders."

**1:22** And look, while she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came.

**1:23** And it was reported to the king, "Here is Nathan the prophet." And he went in before the king and paid homage to the king with his face to the ground.

**1:24** And Nathan said, "My lord the king, did you say, 'Adonijah will be king after me, and he himself will sit on my throne'?

**1:25** For he has gone down today and has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the sons of the king, and the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest; and look, they are eating and drinking before him, and they have said, 'May King Adonijah live!'

**1:26** Yet he has not invited me myself, your servant, or Zadok the priest, or Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or Solomon your servant.

**1:27** Has this matter come about through my lord the king, and you have not made known to your servant who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?"

**1:28** And David answered and said, "Call Bathsheba to me." And she came in before the king and stood before him.

**1:29** And the king swore and said, "As the Lord lives, who has redeemed my life from all distress,

**1:30** just as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, 'Solomon your son will be king after me, and he himself will sit on my throne in my place,' so indeed I will do on this day."

**1:31** And Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground, and paid homage to the king, and said, "May my lord King David live forever!"

**1:32** And King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me." And they came in before the king.

**1:33** And the king said to them, "Take the servants of your lord with you, and mount my son Solomon on my own mule, and bring him down to Gihon.

**1:34** And anoint him there, Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet, as king over Israel, and blow the horn and say, 'May King Solomon live!'

**1:35** And he will sit on my throne and be king in my place, and I have commanded that he be ruler over Israel and Judah."

**1:36** And Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, "May it be so! May the God of my lord the king thus confirm it.

**1:37** Just as the Lord was with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and may He make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David."

**1:38** And Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites went down, and they seated Solomon on the mule of King David and led him to Gihon.

**1:39** And Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon. And he blew the horn, and all the people said, "May King Solomon live!"

**1:40** And all the people went up after him, and they were dancing in dances and rejoicing with great joy; and the earth split open at their sound.

**1:41** And Adonijah and all his invited guests heard it, and they had finished eating. And Joab heard the sound of the horn and said, "What is the sound of the city roaring?"

**1:42** While he was still speaking, look, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest came in. And Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a man of valor; and bring good news."

**1:43** And Jonathan answered and said, "Indeed, our lord King David has made Solomon king;

**1:44** and the king sent with him Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and they seated him on the mule of the king;

**1:45** and Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him at Gihon, and they went up from there rejoicing, and the city roared. This is the sound that you heard.

**1:46** And Solomon sat on the throne of the kingdom,

**1:47** and the servants of the king went in to bless our lord King David, saying, 'May God make the name of Solomon better than your name, and make his throne greater than your throne.' And the king bowed in worship on the bed,

**1:48** and moreover the king spoke thus: 'Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who has given today one from my seed sitting on my throne, and my eyes see it.'"

**1:49** And all the invited guests of Adonijah rose up, and went, each man his own way.

**1:50** And Adonijah was afraid of Solomon, and he arose and went away and took hold of the horns of the altar.

**1:51** And it was reported to Solomon, saying, "Look, Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon, and he is holding onto the horns of the altar, saying, 'Let King Solomon swear to me today that he will not put his servant to death with the sword.'"

**1:52** And Solomon said, "If he proves to be a son of valor, not one of his hairs will fall to the ground; but if wickedness is found in him, he will be put to death."

**1:53** And King Solomon sent, and brought him down from the altar. And he came in and paid homage to King Solomon, and Solomon said to him, "Go to your house."

## Translator Notes

- **1:8** — Greek reads Resei (corresponding to Hebrew Rei) and 'mighty sons of David' (where the Hebrew text and some Greek witnesses read 'the mighty men who belonged to David').
- **1:9** — Greek reads 'by Aithe of Zoheleth, which was next to Rogel' (where the Hebrew and some Greek witnesses read 'by the stone of Zoheleth, which is beside En-rogel'), and 'the prominent men of Judah' rather than 'all the men of Judah'.
- **1:40** — Greek reads 'dancing in dances' (reflecting the Hebrew root for dancing), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads 'playing on flutes'.
- **1:48** — Greek explicitly includes 'from my offspring' before 'sitting on my throne,' a phrase not present in the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **1:52** — Greek uses a Hebraistic oath conditional ('if') to express an emphatic negative ('not one of his hairs will fall'), and reads passive 'he will be put to death' where the Hebrew has 'he shall die'.
