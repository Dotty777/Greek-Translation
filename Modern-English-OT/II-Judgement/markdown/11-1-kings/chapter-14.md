## Text

**14:21** And Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned over Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the Lord had chosen from all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. And his mother's name was Maacham, an Ammonite.

**14:22** And Rehoboam did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and he provoked Him to jealousy in all that their fathers had done, in their sins that they had committed.

**14:23** And they built for themselves high places and pillars and groves on every high hill and beneath every shady tree.

**14:24** And there was a conspiracy in the land, and they did some of all the abominations of the nations whom the Lord had removed from before the sons of Israel.

**14:25** And it happened in the fifth year of Rehoboam's reign that Shishak king of Egypt went up against Jerusalem,

**14:26** and he took all the treasures of the house of the Lord and the treasures of the king's house, and the golden spears that David had taken from the hand of the servants of Hadarezer king of Zobah and had brought into Jerusalem, all that he took, the golden weapons.

**14:27** And King Rehoboam made bronze weapons in their place; and the leaders of the runners, who guarded the gateway of the king's house, set upon him.

**14:28** And it happened, whenever the king went into the house of the Lord, that the runners would carry them and would deposit them into the chamber of the runners.

**14:29** And the rest of the matters of Rehoboam and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**14:30** And there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days.

**14:31** And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Abiu his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **14:21** — Verses 14:1-20 are absent at this position in the Greek text (parallel material appears in the Greek supplement after 12:24). The Greek names Rehoboam's mother Maacham, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Naamah.
- **14:24** — The Greek reads 'a conspiracy' (syndesmos), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's reference to cult prostitutes.
- **14:26** — The Greek adds the reference to the golden spears that David took from the servants of Hadarezer king of Zobah (cf. 2 Samuel 8:7) and refers to 'golden weapons' (hopla, a term also used for shields), omitting the Hebrew reference to golden shields made by Solomon.
- **14:27** — The Greek reads 'the leaders of the runners ... set upon him,' differing sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('he entrusted them to the commanders of the guards').
- **14:31** — The Greek reads Abiou (corresponding to Hebrew Abiyahu/Abijah, as in 2 Chronicles 13:20), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text of 1 Kings reads Abijam; the Greek also omits the repeated mention of Rehoboam's mother here.
