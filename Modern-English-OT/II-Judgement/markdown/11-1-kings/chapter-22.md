## Text

**22:1** And he remained three years, and there was no war between Syria and Israel.

**22:2** And it happened in the third year that Jehoshaphat king of Judah went down to the king of Israel.

**22:3** And the king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth-gilead belongs to us, and we are keeping silent about taking it from the hand of the king of Syria?"

**22:4** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you go up with us to Ramoth-gilead to battle?"

**22:5** And Jehoshaphat said, "As I am, so also are you; as my people are, so are your people; as my horses are, so are your horses." And Jehoshaphat king of Judah said to the king of Israel, "Please inquire of the Lord today."

**22:6** And the king of Israel gathered together all the prophets, about four hundred men, and the king said to them, "Should I go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or hold back?" And they said, "Go up, and the Lord will surely give it into the hands of the king."

**22:7** And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Is there not a prophet of the Lord here, that we may inquire of the Lord through him?"

**22:8** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is one man through whom to inquire of the Lord, and I hate him, because he does not speak good concerning me, but only evil, Micaiah son of Jemias." And Jehoshaphat king of Judah said, "Let the king not say so."

**22:9** And the king of Israel called one eunuch and said, "Quickly, Micaiah son of Jemias!"

**22:10** And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting, each man on his throne, armed, in the gates of Samaria, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

**22:11** And Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns for himself and said, "Thus says the Lord, 'With these you will gore Syria until it is brought to an end.'"

**22:12** And all the prophets were prophesying in this way, saying, "Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and it will prosper; and the Lord will give into your hands even the king of Syria."

**22:13** And the messenger who went to call Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Look now, all the prophets are speaking with their mouth for good concerning the king; now you also be one in your words, according to the words of one of these, and speak good."

**22:14** And Micaiah said, "As the Lord lives, whatever the Lord says to me, these things I will speak."

**22:15** And he came to the king, and the king said to him, "Micaiah, should I go up to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or hold back?" And he said, "Go up, and the Lord will prosper it into the hand of the king."

**22:16** And the king said to him, "Five times I put you under oath that you speak truth to me in the name of the Lord."

**22:17** And he said, "Not so. I have seen all Israel scattered on the mountains like a flock that has no shepherd; and the Lord said, 'Is the Lord not God to these? Let each one return to his house in peace.'"

**22:18** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Did I not say to you, 'This man does not prophesy good for me, but only evil'?"

**22:19** And Micaiah said, "Not so. Not I; hear the word of the Lord; not so, I saw the God of Israel sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven was standing around Him on His right and on His left.

**22:20** And the Lord said, 'Who will deceive Ahab king of Israel, so that he will go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?' And one spoke in this way, and another in that way.

**22:21** And a spirit came out and stood before the Lord and said, 'I will deceive him.'

**22:22** And the Lord said to him, 'By what means?' And he said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' And He said, 'You will deceive him, and indeed you will prevail; go out and do so.'

**22:23** And now, look, the Lord has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the Lord has spoken evil against you."

**22:24** And Zedekiah son of Canaan approached and struck Micaiah on the cheek and said, "What kind of spirit of the Lord is it that spoke in you?"

**22:25** And Micaiah said, "Look, you will see on that day when you enter a chamber of the hidden chamber."

**22:26** And the king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Semer the king of the city; and to Joash the son of the king

**22:27** say to put this man in prison, and that he eat bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace."

**22:28** And Micaiah said, "If you indeed return in peace, the Lord has not spoken in me."

**22:29** And the king of Israel went up, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah with him, to Ramoth-gilead.

**22:30** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "I will disguise myself and enter into the battle, and you put on my apparel." And the king of Israel disguised himself and entered into the battle.

**22:31** And the king of Syria commanded the thirty-two commanders of his chariots, saying, "Do not fight small or great, except the king of Israel alone."

**22:32** And it happened, when the commanders of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat king of Judah, that they said, "It appears this is the king of Israel." And they surrounded him to fight, and Jehoshaphat cried out.

**22:33** And it happened, when the commanders of the chariots saw that this was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from him.

**22:34** And one man drew the bow with good aim and struck the king of Israel between the lung and the breastplate; and he said to his charioteer, "Turn your hands around and bring me out of the battle, because I have been wounded."

**22:35** And the battle turned on that day; and the king was standing on the chariot facing Syria from morning until evening, and blood was pouring out from the wound into the hollow of the chariot; and he died in the evening, and the blood of the defeat was flowing out as far as the hollow of the chariot.

**22:36** And the army herald stood as the sun was setting, saying, "Each one to his own city and to his own land,

**22:37** because the king is dead." And they came to Samaria, and they buried the king in Samaria.

**22:38** And they washed off the blood at the spring of Samaria, and the pigs and the dogs licked up the blood, and the prostitutes washed themselves in the blood, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke.

**22:39** And the rest of the affairs of Ahab, and all that he did, and an ivory house which he built, and all the cities which he made, look, these are written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel.

**22:40** And Ahab slept with his fathers, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

**22:41** And Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king over Judah; in the fourth year of Ahab he became king.

**22:42** Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Azubah daughter of Shimei.

**22:43** And he walked in all the way of Asa his father; he did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of the Lord.

**22:44** Yet he did not remove the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places.

**22:45** And Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.

**22:46** And the rest of the affairs of Jehoshaphat, and his mighty deeds, all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of Jehoshaphat?

**22:51** And he slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David his father; and Joram his son reigned in his place.

**22:52** And Ahaziah son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah; and he reigned in Israel two years.

**22:53** And he did what was evil before the Lord, and he walked in the way of his father Ahab and in the way of Jezebel his mother and in the sins of the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat who caused Israel to sin.

**22:54** And he served the Baals and worshiped them, and provoked the Lord God of Israel to anger according to all the things that had happened before him. And Moab rebelled against Israel after Ahab died.

## Translator Notes

- **22:8** — Jemias (Greek Iemias, genitive Iemia in v. 9) preserves the Septuagint name form where the Hebrew text reads Imlah.
- **22:16** — The Greek text reads "Five times" (pentakis) as a statement, whereas the Hebrew asks "How many times?" (and some Greek manuscripts read posakis).
- **22:17** — The Greek renders this clause as a question, "Is the Lord not God to these?", contrasting with the Hebrew "These have no master."
- **22:24** — The Greek literally reads Canaan (Chanaan, compared to Chaana for Hebrew Chenaanah in v. 11) and asks "What kind of spirit of the Lord is it that spoke in you?" rather than the Hebrew "Which way did the Spirit of the LORD go from me to speak to you?"
- **22:26** — The Greek names the official Semer and titles him "the king of the city," where the Hebrew text has Amon the governor of the city.
- **22:42** — The Greek names Jehoshaphat's maternal grandfather Shimei (Semeei) where the Hebrew has Shilhi; verses 47-50 do not appear here in the Septuagint order (parallel material stands earlier in 16:28a-h).
