## Text

**21:1** And Son of Hader gathered all his army together and went up and besieged Samaria, and thirty-two kings were with him, and every horse and chariot; and they went up and besieged Samaria, and fought against it.

**21:2** And he sent to Ahab, king of Israel, into the city and said to him, "Thus says Son of Hader:

**21:3** Your silver and your gold are mine, and your wives and your children are mine."

**21:4** And the king of Israel answered and said, "Just as you have spoken, lord king, I am yours, and all that is mine."

**21:5** And the messengers returned and said, "Thus says Son of Hader: I sent back, saying, 'Your silver and your gold and your wives you shall give to me,'

**21:6** for at this hour tomorrow I will send my servants to you, and they will search your house and the houses of your servants; and it will be that whatever desirable things of their eyes they lay their hands on, they will take."

**21:7** And the king of Israel called all the elders and said, "Know now and see that this man is seeking evil, for he has sent to me concerning my wives and concerning my sons and concerning my daughters; my silver and my gold I did not withhold from him."

**21:8** And the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen, and do not consent."

**21:9** And he said to the messengers of Son of Hader, "Say to your lord, 'All that you sent to your servant at first I will do, but this thing I will not be able to do.'" And the men departed and brought back word to him.

**21:10** And Son of Hader sent to him, saying, "So may God do to me, and so may He add, if the dust of Samaria will suffice for the foxes, for all the people, my foot soldiers."

**21:11** And the king of Israel answered and said, "Let it be enough; do not let the hunchbacked man boast like the upright man."

**21:12** And it came about, when he answered him this word, that he was drinking, he and all the kings with him, in tents; and he said to his servants, "Build a palisade." And they set up a palisade against the city.

**21:13** And look, one prophet approached the king of Israel and said, "Thus says the Lord: Have you seen this great multitude? Look, I am giving it today into your hands, and you will know that I am the Lord."

**21:14** And Ahab said, "By whom?" And he said, "Thus says the Lord: By the young men of the rulers of the districts." And Ahab said, "Who will begin the battle?" And he said, "You."

**21:15** And Ahab mustered the rulers, the young men of the districts, and they were two hundred and thirty; and after these things he mustered the people, every son of might, sixty.

**21:16** And he went out at noon; and Son of Hader was drinking, drunk, in Succoth, he and the kings, thirty-two, allied kings with him.

**21:17** And the rulers, the young men of the districts, went out first; and they sent and reported to the king of Syria, saying, "Men have come out from Samaria."

**21:18** "Tell them, for peace—for they are not coming out—to seize them alive; and for war, to seize them alive;

**21:19** and let not the ruling young men, ruling the districts, come out from the city, and the army behind them."

**21:20** Each struck the one beside him, and each struck a second time the one beside him, and Syria fled; and Israel pursued them, and Son of Hader, king of Syria, escaped on a horseman's horse.

**21:21** And the king of Israel went out and took all the horses and the chariots, and struck a great blow against Syria.

**21:22** And the prophet approached the king of Israel and said, "Strengthen yourself, and know and see what you will do, for at the return of the year Son of Hader, king of Syria, is coming up against you."

**21:23** And the servants of the king of Syria said, "A god of mountains is the God of Israel, and not a god of a valley; therefore He was stronger than us; but if we fight them on level ground, surely we will be stronger than them.

**21:24** And do this thing: remove the kings, each to their place, and put satraps in their place,

**21:25** and we will replace for you an army like the army that fell, and horse like the horse, and chariots like the chariots, and we will fight against them on level ground, and we will be stronger than them." And he listened to his voice, and did so.

**21:26** And it came about at the return of the year that Son of Hader mustered Syria, and went up to Aphek for war against Israel.

**21:27** And the sons of Israel were mustered, and came to meet them, and Israel encamped opposite them like two flocks of goats, and Syria filled the land.

**21:28** And the man of God approached and said to the king of Israel, "Thus says the Lord: Because Syria has said, 'A god of mountains is the Lord God of Israel, and not a god of valleys is He,' I will give this great army into your hand, and you will know that I am the Lord;

**21:29** and these will encamp opposite those for seven days." And it came about on the seventh day that the battle drew near, and Israel struck Syria, one hundred thousand foot soldiers in one day.

**21:30** And those who remained fled to Aphek, into the city, and the wall fell on twenty-seven thousand of the men who remained; and Son of Hader fled and went into the house of the bedroom, into the inner room.

**21:31** And he said to his servants, "I know that the kings of Israel are kings of mercy; let us now put sackcloth on our waists and ropes on our heads, and let us still go out to the king of Israel, if perhaps he will keep our souls alive."

**21:32** And they tied sackcloth around their waists and put ropes on their heads, and said to the king of Israel, "Your servant Son of Hader says, 'Please let our soul live.'" And he said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

**21:33** And the men took it as an omen and made a truce, and picked up the word from his mouth and said, "Your brother is Son of Hader." And he said, "Go in and get him." And Son of Hader came out to him, and they brought him up to him onto the chariot.

**21:34** And he said to him, "The cities that my father took from your father I will return to you, and you will set up streets for yourself in Damascus, just as my father set up in Samaria." "And I will send you away with a covenant." And he made a covenant with him, and sent him away.

**21:35** And one man from the sons of the prophets said to his neighbor by the word of the Lord, "Please strike me." And the man was not willing to strike him.

**21:36** And he said to him, "Because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord, look, you are running away from me, and a lion will strike you." And he departed from him, and a lion found him and struck him.

**21:37** And he found another man and said, "Please strike me." And the man struck him; striking him, he bruised him.

**21:38** And the prophet went and stood for the king of Israel on the road, and bound his eyes with a bandage.

**21:39** And it came about, as the king was passing by, that this man cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out to the campaign of war, and look, a man brought out a man to me and said to me, 'Guard this man; and if he indeed escapes, then your life shall be in place of his life, or you shall weigh out a talent of silver.'

**21:40** And it happened, your servant looked around here and there, and this man was not there." And the king of Israel said to him, "Look, you have also murdered the ambushes with me."

**21:41** And he hurried and removed the bandage from his eyes, and the king of Israel recognized him, that he was one of the prophets.

**21:42** And he said to him, "Thus says the Lord: Because you yourself have let a destructive man out of your hand, your life shall be in place of his life, and your people in place of his people."

**21:43** And the king of Israel departed, confounded and disheartened, and came to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — In the Septuagint, 3 Kingdoms 21 corresponds to 1 Kings 20 in the Hebrew Bible. The Greek renders the Aramean royal name Ben-hadad literally as "Son of Hader" (υἱὸς Ἁδέρ, reflecting an r/d interchange).
- **21:10** — The Greek reads "for the foxes" (ταῖς ἀλώπεξιν), interpreting the unvocalized Hebrew consonants for "handfuls" (lish'alim) as "foxes" (shu'alim), and renders "those at my feet" as "my foot soldiers."
- **21:11** — Where the Hebrew proverb contrasts strapping on armor with taking it off, the Greek renders the saying as a contrast between a "hunchbacked man" (ὁ κυρτός) and an "upright man" (ὁ ὀρθός).
- **21:18** — Verses 18-19 present a difficult Greek text with infinitives of command (εἰπεῖν, συλλαβεῖν), the clause "for they are not coming out" (οὐ γὰρ ἐκπορεύονται), and a prohibition in v. 19 ("let not ... come out," μὴ ἐξελθάτωσαν) where the Hebrew shifts to narrative.
- **21:40** — The Greek diverges sharply from the Hebrew verdict ("So shall your judgment be; you yourself have decided it"), reading "the ambushes with me" (τὰ ἔνεδρα παρ᾽ ἐμοί) and rendering the verb as "you have murdered" (ἐφόνευσας).
