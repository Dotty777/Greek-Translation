## Text

**18:1** And it happened after many days that the word of the Lord came to Elijah in the third year, saying, "Go and appear to Ahab, and I will give rain on the face of the land."

**18:2** And Elijah went to appear to Ahab; and the famine was severe in Samaria.

**18:3** And Ahab called Obadiah the steward; and Obadiah greatly feared the Lord.

**18:4** And it happened, when Jezebel was striking down the prophets of the Lord, that Obadiah took a hundred men who were prophets and hid them by fifties in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water.

**18:5** And Ahab said to Obadiah, "Come, and let us pass through the land to the springs of water and to the wadis; perhaps we may find grass and preserve horses and mules, and they will not be destroyed from the tents."

**18:6** And they divided between themselves the way to pass through it; Ahab went by one way, and Obadiah went by another way alone.

**18:7** And Obadiah was on the way alone, and Elijah came to meet him alone; and Obadiah hurried and fell on his face and said, "Is it you yourself, my lord Elijah?"

**18:8** And Elijah said to him, "It is I. Go, tell your lord, 'Here is Elijah.'"

**18:9** And Obadiah said, "How have I sinned, that you are giving your servant into the hand of Ahab to put me to death?

**18:10** As the Lord your God lives, there is surely no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to search for you; and if they said, 'He is not here,' he burned down the kingdom and its regions, because he had not found you.

**18:11** And now you say, 'Go, report to your lord.'

**18:12** And it will happen that if I depart from you, the Spirit of the Lord will carry you away to a land that I do not know; and I will go in to report to Ahab, and he will kill me; yet your servant has feared the Lord from his youth.

**18:13** And was it not reported to you, my lord, what things I have done when Jezebel was killing the prophets of the Lord, that I hid a hundred men of the prophets of the Lord by fifties in a cave and fed them with loaves of bread and water?

**18:14** And now you say to me, 'Go, tell your lord, "Here is Elijah,"' and he will kill me."

**18:15** And Elijah said, "As the Lord of powers lives, before whom I have stood, today I will surely appear to him."

**18:16** And Obadiah went to meet Ahab and reported to him; and Ahab ran out and went to meet Elijah.

**18:17** And it happened, when Ahab saw Elijah, that Ahab said to Elijah, "Is it you yourself, the one who perverts Israel?"

**18:18** And Elijah said, "I am not perverting Israel, but rather you and the house of your father are, by forsaking the Lord your God; and you went after the Baals.

**18:19** And now send, gather to me all Israel to Mount Carmel, and the prophets of shame, four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the groves, four hundred, who eat at Jezebel's table."

**18:20** And Ahab sent to all Israel, and gathered all the prophets together to Mount Carmel.

**18:21** And Elijah drew near to all of them; and Elijah said to them, "How long will you limp on both knee-joints? If the Lord is God, go after Him; but if Baal, go after him." And the people did not answer a word.

**18:22** And Elijah said to the people, "I alone have been left as a prophet of the Lord, while the prophets of Baal are four hundred and fifty men, and the prophets of the grove are four hundred.

**18:23** Let them give us two oxen, and let them choose one for themselves, and cut it in pieces and lay it on the wood, and put no fire to it; and I will prepare the other ox, and I will certainly not put fire to it.

**18:24** And call out in the name of your gods, and I will call in the name of the Lord my God; and it will be that the god who answers by fire, that one is God." And all the people answered and said, "The word that you have spoken is good."

**18:25** And Elijah said to the prophets of shame, "Choose for yourselves the one young bull and prepare it first, because you are many, and call in the name of your god, and put no fire to it."

**18:26** And they took the young bull and prepared it, and they kept calling in the name of Baal from morning until noon, and said, "Answer us, Baal, answer us." And there was no voice and there was no response; and they kept running about on the altar that they had made.

**18:27** And it became noon, and Elijah the Tishbite mocked them and said, "Call out with a loud voice, because he is a god; because he is occupied in conversation, and at the same time perhaps he is transacting business, or perhaps he is asleep and will wake up."

**18:28** And they kept calling out with a loud voice, and they kept cutting themselves with a sword and with lances until blood poured out upon them,

**18:29** and they prophesied until the afternoon passed. And it happened, around the time for the sacrifice to be offered up, that Elijah spoke to the prophets of the abominations, saying, "Step aside from now on, and I will prepare my burnt offering." And they stepped aside and departed.

**18:30** And Elijah said to the people, "Draw near to me." And all the people drew near to him.

**18:31** And Elijah took twelve stones according to the number of the tribes of Israel, as the Lord spoke to him, saying, "Israel will be your name."

**18:32** And he built the stones in the name of the Lord, and he healed the altar that had been torn down, and he made a sea holding two measures of seed around the altar.

**18:33** And he stacked the split wood on the altar that he had made, and he cut the burnt offering in pieces and placed the split wood on it, and stacked it on the altar.

**18:34** And he said, "Fetch for me four jars of water, and pour it on the burnt offering and on the split wood." And they did so. And he said, "Do it a second time." And they did it a second time. And he said, "Do it a third time." And they did it a third time.

**18:35** And the water flowed around the altar, and they filled the sea with water.

**18:36** And Elijah cried out to heaven and said, "Lord, God of Abraham and Isaac and Israel, answer me, Lord, answer me today by fire, and let all this people know that You are the Lord, the God of Israel, and I am Your servant, and because of You I have done these works.

**18:37** Answer me, Lord, answer me, and let this people know that You are the Lord God, and that You have turned the heart of this people back."

**18:38** And fire fell from the Lord out of heaven, and consumed the burnt offerings and the split wood and the water that was in the sea, and the fire licked up the stones and the dust.

**18:39** And all the people fell on their faces and said, "Truly the Lord is God; He is God."

**18:40** And Elijah said to the people, "Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape." And they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the wadi of Kishon and slaughtered them there.

**18:41** And Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up and eat and drink, for there is a sound of the feet of the rain."

**18:42** And Ahab went up to eat and drink; and Elijah went up onto Carmel, and bowed down to the ground and put his face between his knees,

**18:43** and said to his young attendant, "Go up and look toward the sea." And the young attendant looked and said, "There is nothing." And Elijah said, "And you, go back seven times, and return seven times."

**18:44** And the young attendant returned seven times; and it happened on the seventh time, and look, a small cloud like the footprint of a man, bringing up water. And he said, "Go up and say to Ahab, 'Hitch up your chariot and go down, so that the rain does not overtake you.'"

**18:45** And it happened in a little while that the sky grew dark with clouds and wind, and the rain became heavy; and Ahab wept and went to Israel.

**18:46** And the hand of the Lord was upon Elijah; and he bound up his waist, and ran ahead of Ahab to Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:10** — The Greek reads "he burned down the kingdom and its regions" where the Hebrew MT reads "he made the kingdom or nation swear"; similarly in v. 5 the Greek reads "from the tents" (vs. MT "of the animals").
- **18:19** — Here and in v. 25 the Greek replaces the name Baal with "shame" ("prophets of shame"), and renders Asherah as "the groves" (singular "the grove" in v. 22); in v. 29 the Greek adds Elijah's command to "the prophets of the abominations" to step aside.
- **18:21** — Literally "limp on both knee-joints," a vivid Greek idiom (vs. Hebrew MT "between two opinions/branches"); similarly in v. 27 the Greek renders Elijah's taunt as "occupied in conversation" and "transacting business."
- **18:32** — Literally "a sea" (also in vv. 35 and 38), the Greek term for the water trench or basin around the altar, echoing the bronze "sea" of Solomon's temple.
- **18:45** — The Greek reads "wept" (vs. Hebrew MT "rode") and "to Israel" here and in v. 46 (where the Hebrew MT has "to Jezreel"); in v. 44 the Greek likewise reads "the footprint of a man, bringing up water" (vs. MT "a man's palm, rising from the sea").
