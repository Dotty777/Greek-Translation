## Text

**7:1** And King Solomon sent and fetched Hiram from Tyre,

**7:2** the son of a widowed woman, and he was from the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a Tyrian man, a craftsman of bronze and filled with skill and understanding and knowledge to do every work in bronze. And he was brought in to King Solomon, and he did all the works.

**7:3** And he cast the portico of the house: eighteen cubits was the height of the pillar, and a circumference of fourteen cubits encircled it, the thickness of the pillar; the hollows were four fingers; and thus was the second pillar.

**7:4** And he made two cast capitals to put on the tops of the pillars; five cubits was the height of the one capital.

**7:5** And he made two lattices to cover over the capital of the pillars, both a lattice for the one capital and a lattice for the second capital.

**7:6** And hanging work, two rows of bronze pomegranates, latticed, hanging work, row upon row; and thus he did for the second capital.

**7:7** And he set up the pillars of the portico of the temple; and he set up the one pillar and called its name Jachum, and he set up the second pillar and called its name Balaz.

**7:8** And on the tops of the pillars was lily work by the portico, of four cubits.

**7:9** And a roof-beam was on both of the pillars, and above the sides the roof-beam was a capital, by the cubit.

**7:10** And he made the sea ten cubits from its wall to its brim, round all around, the same; five cubits was its height, and thirty-three cubits were gathered together.

**7:11** And supports underneath its brim all around encircled it, ten to a cubit all around.

**7:12** And its brim was like the work of the brim of a cup, a blossom of a lily, and its thickness was a handbreadth.

**7:13** And twelve oxen were underneath the sea, three looking north, and three looking west, and three looking south, and three looking east. And all the hindquarters were toward the house, and the sea was upon them above.

**7:14** And he made ten bronze stands; five cubits was the length of the one stand, and four cubits was its width, and six cubits was its height.

**7:15** And this was the work of the stands: they had an enclosure, and an enclosure between the projections.

**7:16** And on their enclosures between projections were lions and oxen and cherubim; and on the projections it was thus also above, and underneath the lions and the oxen were spaces, a work of descent.

**7:17** And there were four bronze wheels for the one stand, and the bronze attachments, and their four parts, shoulder-pieces underneath the washing basins.

**7:18** And there were axles in the wheels in the stand; and the height of the one wheel was a cubit and a half.

**7:19** And the work of the wheels was the work of chariot wheels; their axles and their rims and their workmanship were all cast.

**7:20** The four shoulder-pieces were on the four corners of the one stand; from the stand were its shoulders.

**7:21** And on the top of the stand, half a cubit was its size, round all around on the top of the stand; and there was a beginning of its handles and its enclosures; and it opened onto four beginnings of its handles.

**7:22** And its enclosures were cherubim and lions and palm trees standing, each one adjoining in front, inward, and the parts all around.

**7:23** According to it he made all the ten stands, one order and one measure for all.

**7:24** And he made ten bronze cauldrons, holding forty choes for the one cauldron by measure; the one cauldron was on the one stand, for the ten stands.

**7:25** And he set the five stands at the shoulder of the house on the left; and the sea was at the shoulder of the house on the right, toward the east, on the south side.

**7:26** And Hiram made the pots and the tongs and the bowls; and Hiram finished making all the works that he made for King Solomon in the house of the Lord:

**7:27** two pillars, and the two twisted bands of the pillars on the tops of the pillars, and the two lattices to cover both the twisted bands of the carvings that were on the pillars;

**7:28** the four hundred pomegranates for both the lattices, two rows of pomegranates for the one lattice to cover over both the twisted bands of the stand that were on both the pillars;

**7:29** and the ten stands, and the ten cauldrons on the stands;

**7:30** and the one sea, and the twelve oxen underneath the sea;

**7:31** and the pots and the tongs and the bowls and all the vessels that Hiram made for King Solomon for the house of the Lord. And the pillars of the house of the king and of the house of the Lord were forty-eight. Hiram made all the works of the king entirely of bronze.

**7:32** There was no weight of the bronze with which he made all these works because of the very great abundance; there was no limit to the weights of the bronze.

**7:33** In the region around the Jordan he cast them in the thickness of the earth, between Succoth and Seira.

**7:34** And King Solomon took the vessels that he made in the house: the golden altar, and the table on which were the loaves of the offering, of gold,

**7:35** and the lampstands, five on the left and five on the right in front of the inner sanctuary, of gold, enclosed, and the lamp-holders and the lamps and the oil-ladles, of gold;

**7:36** and the vestibules and the nails and the bowls and the dishes and the censers, of gold, enclosed; and the door-frames of the doors of the innermost house, the Holy of Holies, and the doors of the temple, of gold.

**7:37** And the work of the house of the Lord that Solomon did was completed; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father and all the holy things of Solomon; the silver and the gold he put into the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**7:38** And for thirteen years Solomon built the house for himself.

**7:39** And he built the house with the forest of Lebanon: one hundred cubits was its length, and fifty cubits was its width, and it was of three rows of cedar pillars, and there were cedar shoulder-pieces for the pillars.

**7:40** And he paneled the house above upon the sides of the pillars; and the number of the pillars was forty-five to a row,

**7:41** and three roof-beams, and space facing space threefold.

**7:42** And all the door-frames and the spaces were square, roofed with beams, and from the door-frame to the door threefold.

**7:43** And the portico of the pillars was fifty in length and fifty in width, joined together, a portico in front of them; and there were pillars and a thickness in front of it for the porticos.

**7:44** And there was the portico of the thrones, where he would judge there, the portico of judgment.

**7:45** And their house, in the house he would dwell there, was one court out of one winding around these according to this work; and a house for the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Solomon took, like this portico.

**7:46** All these were of costly stones, chiseled at intervals on the inside, and from the foundation to the cornices, and on the outside to the great court,

**7:47** which was founded with large costly stones, ten-cubit stones and the eight-cubit ones,

**7:48** and above with costly ones according to the measure of unhewn stones, and with cedars.

**7:49** The circuits of the great court were three rows of unhewn stones and a row of joined cedar.

**7:50** And Solomon finished his whole house.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek reads "the portico" (transliterating Hebrew ulam as ailam) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions casting the two bronze pillars, and gives the pillar's circumference as fourteen cubits rather than twelve.
- **7:7** — The Greek preserves the distinct pillar names Jachum (Iachoum) and Balaz (Balaz), corresponding to Jachin and Boaz in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:10** — The Greek uniquely reads "from its wall to its brim" (compared to "from brim to brim" in Hebrew) and gives the measurement as thirty-three cubits "gathered together" rather than a thirty-cubit measuring line.
- **7:33** — Seira is the Septuagint's distinct proper name for the site called Zarethan in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:38** — In the Greek order of 3 Kingdoms, Solomon's palace (7:38-50) follows Hiram's bronze furnishings (7:1-37), reversing the sequence of the Hebrew Masoretic Text (1 Kings 7:1-12 and 7:13-51); in 7:48-49, the Greek also specifies "unhewn stones" (apeleketon) where the Hebrew has hewn stone.
