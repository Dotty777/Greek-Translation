## Text

**9:1** And it happened, when Solomon finished building the house of the Lord and the house of the king, and all the undertaking of Solomon, all that he desired to do,

**9:2** that the Lord appeared to Solomon a second time, just as He had appeared in Gibeon,

**9:3** and the Lord said to him, "I have heard the voice of your prayer and your supplication which you have pleaded before Me. I have done for you according to all your prayer; I have consecrated this house which you have built, to place My name there forever, and My eyes will be there forever, and My heart all the days.

**9:4** And as for you, if you walk before Me just as David your father walked, in holiness of heart and in uprightness, and to do according to all that I commanded him, and keep My statutes and My commandments,

**9:5** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, just as I spoke to David your father, saying, 'There will not be removed from you a man ruling in Israel.'

**9:6** But if you and your children indeed turn away from Me, and do not keep My commandments and My statutes which Moses set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them,

**9:7** then I will remove Israel from the land which I gave them, and this house which I consecrated for My name I will cast away from My presence; and Israel will become a desolation and a byword among all the peoples.

**9:8** And this house will be the lofty one; everyone passing through it will be astonished and will hiss, and they will say, 'For what reason has the Lord done so to this land and to this house?'

**9:9** And they will say, 'Because they forsook the Lord their God, who brought their fathers out of Egypt, out of a house of slavery, and took hold of foreign gods and worshiped them and served them, therefore He brought this evil upon them.'" Then Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to his house which he had built for himself in those days.

**9:10** For twenty years, in which Solomon built the two houses, the house of the Lord and the house of the king,

**9:11** Hiram king of Tyre assisted Solomon with cedar timbers and with pine timbers and with gold and with all his desire. Then the king gave to Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

**9:12** And Hiram went out from Tyre and traveled to Galilee to see the cities which Solomon had given him, and it did not please him.

**9:13** And he said, "What are these cities which you have given me, brother?" And he called them Border until this day.

**9:14** And Hiram brought to Solomon one hundred and twenty talents of gold.

**9:26** And King Solomon made a ship for himself in Ezion-geber, which is beside Elath on the shore of the farthest sea in the land of Edom.

**9:27** And Hiram sent in the ship some of his servants, seafaring men to row, who knew the sea, with the servants of Solomon.

**9:28** And they came to Sophera, and took from there one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Greek text reads 'Gabaoth,' a variant form of Gibeon.
- **9:9** — The closing sentence about Pharaoh's daughter appears here in the Septuagint rather than at 9:24 as in the Hebrew text, and the Greek makes Solomon the subject bringing her up to his own house.
- **9:13** — The Greek names the region Horion ('Border'), whereas the Hebrew text reads 'Cabul.' Verses 9:15-25 of the Hebrew text are absent from chapter 9 in the Septuagint, with most of that material appearing in 10:22.
- **9:26** — The Greek phrase 'hyper hou' ('for himself' or 'on this account') is textually difficult; the Greek also reads the variant spelling 'Emaeseion-gaber' for Ezion-geber and 'the farthest sea' where the Hebrew text reads the Sea of Reeds (Red Sea).
- **9:28** — The Greek reads the distinct place name 'Sophera' (corresponding to Hebrew 'Ophir'; compare 'Suphir' in 10:11) and gives the weight as 120 talents of gold rather than 420 talents as in the Hebrew text.
