## Text

**4:1** And King Solomon was reigning over Israel.

**4:2** And these were the officials who were his: Azari son of Zadok,

**4:3** and Eliaph and Ahijah son of Saba, scribes, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud, recorder,

**4:4** and Zadok and Abiathar, priests,

**4:5** and Ornia son of Nathan, over the deputies, and Zabud son of Nathan, companion of the king,

**4:6** and Ahi was steward, and Eliak the steward, and Eliab son of Saph, over the family, and Adoniram son of Ephra, over the taxes.

**4:7** And Solomon had twelve deputies over all Israel to supply the king and his household; a month in the year fell to each one to supply.

**4:8** And these are their names: Baior in the hill country of Ephraim, one;

**4:9** son of Rechas in Machemas and Bethalami and Beth-shemesh and Elon as far as Bethlaman, one;

**4:10** son of Hesed, Bernemalousamencha and Respharachein,

**4:11** throughout Dan and throughout Phathei, husband of Tablethei—a daughter of Solomon was his wife—one;

**4:12** Baccha son of Ahimach, Polamach and Megiddo, and all the house of Dan that is beside Sesathan below Esrae, and from Baisaphut, Abel-meholah as far as Maeber Lukam, one;

**4:13** son of Geber, Ramoth-gilead—to him belonged the region of Eretabam in Bashan, sixty great walled cities and bronze bars—one;

**4:14** Ahinaab son of Achel, Mahanaim;

**4:15** Ahimaaz in Naphtali, and he also took Basemath the daughter of Solomon, one;

**4:16** Baana son of Hushai in Maala, one;

**4:17** son of Ela in Benjamin;

**4:18** son of Adai in the land of Gad, the land of Sihon king of Heshbon and of Og king of Bashan; and one naseph in the land of Judah;

**4:19** Jehoshaphat son of Phuasud in Issachar.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — In verses 2-6, the Greek cabinet list preserves several names and entries differing from the Hebrew: Azari (Heb. Azariah), Eliaph (Heb. Elihoreph), Saba (Heb. Shisha), Ornia (Heb. Azariah), Ahi (Heb. Ahishar), Ephra (Heb. Abda), plus Eliak and Eliab son of Saph (absent from the Hebrew), while omitting Benaiah in verse 4.
- **4:8** — In verses 8-9, the Greek preserves several names differing from the Hebrew: Baior (Heb. Ben-hur), Rechas (Heb. Deker), Machemas (Heb. Makaz), Bethalami (Heb. Shaalbim), and Bethlaman (Heb. Beth-hanan), and concludes districts with the tally "one."
- **4:10** — In verses 10-11, the Greek transliterates corrupted phrases as proper names: Bernemalousamencha and Respharachein (Heb. "in Arubboth; to him belonged Socoh and all the land of Hepher"), and "throughout Dan and throughout Phathei, husband of Tablethei" (Heb. "Ben-abinadab, all Naphath-dor; Taphath").
- **4:12** — In verses 12-16, the Greek preserves distinct forms including Baccha son of Ahimach, Polamach, "the house of Dan," Sesathan, Esrae, Baisaphut, and Maeber Lukam (v. 12); Eretabam (v. 13, Heb. Argob); Ahinaab son of Achel (v. 14, Heb. Ahinadab son of Iddo); and Maala (v. 16, Heb. Asher and Bealoth).
- **4:18** — Verses 17-19 follow the Septuagint order (placing Benjamin and Gad before Issachar) and preserve distinct readings: "son of Ela" without Shimei (v. 17), "son of Adai" in "the land of Gad" of Sihon king of "Heshbon" and "one naseph"—an untranslated transliteration of Hebrew netsib ("officer")—in Judah (v. 18), and Phuasud (v. 19, Heb. Paruah).
- **4:33 *(additional)*** — The Saints Greek Critical Text has this sentence as 4:33 at the end of chapter 4; it has no separate place in the Saints.Bible numbering, so it is shown after 4:19.
