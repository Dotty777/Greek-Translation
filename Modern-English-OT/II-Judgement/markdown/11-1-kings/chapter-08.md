## Text

**8:1** And it happened, when Solomon finished building the house of the Lord and his own house after twenty years, then King Solomon assembled all the elders of Israel in Zion to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the city of David, which is Zion,

**8:2** in the month of Athamein.

**8:3** And the priests lifted up the ark

**8:4** and the tent of testimony and the holy vessels that were in the tent of testimony.

**8:5** And the king and all Israel were before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen without number.

**8:6** And the priests brought the ark into its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Holy of Holies, under the wings of the cherubim.

**8:7** For the cherubim had their wings spread out over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered over the ark and over its holy things from above,

**8:8** and the consecrated things extended out, and the heads of the consecrated things were visible from the holy place in front of the inner sanctuary, and they were not seen outside.

**8:9** There was nothing in the ark except two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant which Moses placed at Horeb, which the Lord covenanted with the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**8:10** And it happened, when the priests came out of the holy place, that the cloud filled the house;

**8:11** and the priests were not able to stand to minister on account of the cloud, for the glory of the Lord filled the house.

**8:14** And the king turned his face around, and the king blessed all Israel; and all the assembly of Israel was standing.

**8:15** And he said, "Blessed is the Lord God of Israel today, who spoke with His mouth concerning David my father and with His hands fulfilled it, saying,

**8:16** 'From the day I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I did not choose a city in a single tribe of Israel to build a house for My name to be there; and I chose Jerusalem for My name to remain there, and I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**8:17** And it was in the heart of my father to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**8:18** And the Lord said to David my father, 'Because it came into your heart to build a house for My name, you did well that it was in your heart.

**8:19** You will not build the house; rather, your son who comes out from your sides, he will build the house for My name.'

**8:20** And the Lord established His word which He spoke; and I arose in place of David my father, and I sat on the throne of Israel just as He spoke, and I built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**8:21** And I set there a place for the ark, in which is the covenant of the Lord which the Lord made with our fathers when He brought them out of the land of Egypt."

**8:22** And Solomon stood up in front of the altar of the Lord, before all the assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven,

**8:23** and said, "Lord God of Israel, there is no god like You in heaven above or on the earth below, keeping covenant and mercy for Your servant who walks before You with all his heart,

**8:24** which You kept for Your servant David my father; for indeed You spoke with Your mouth, and with Your hands You fulfilled it, as it is this day.

**8:25** And now, Lord God of Israel, keep for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him, saying, 'A man of yours will not be removed from My presence, sitting on the throne of Israel, if only your children keep their ways to walk before Me, just as you walked before Me.'

**8:26** And now, Lord God of Israel, please let Your word be confirmed to David my father.

**8:27** For will God truly dwell with people on the earth? If heaven and the heaven of heaven will not suffice for You, how much less even this house which I built for Your name?

**8:28** Yet You will look upon my supplication, Lord God of Israel, to hear the delight which Your servant prays before You, to You today,

**8:29** that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place of which You said, 'My name will be there,' to hear the prayer which Your servant prays toward this place day and night.

**8:30** And You will hear the supplication of Your servant and of Your people Israel, whatever they pray toward this place; and You will hear in the place of Your dwelling in heaven, and You will act and be merciful.

**8:31** In whatever way each one sins against his neighbor, and if he takes up a curse against him to place him under a curse, and he comes and confesses in front of Your altar in this house,

**8:32** then You will hear from heaven and act, and You will judge Your people Israel, so that the lawless one is declared lawless, putting his way upon his head, and to justify the righteous one, giving to him according to his righteousness.

**8:33** When Your people Israel stumble before enemies because they sin against You, and they turn back and confess to Your name, and pray and make supplication in this house,

**8:34** then You will hear from heaven and be merciful to the sins of Your servant Israel, and You will bring them back to the land which You gave to their fathers.

**8:35** When heaven is shut up and there is no rain because they sin against You, and they pray toward this place and confess to Your name, and turn back from their sins when You humble them,

**8:36** then You will hear from heaven and be merciful to the sins of Your servant and of Your people Israel; for You will show them the good way to walk in it, and You will give rain upon the land which You gave to Your servant as an inheritance.

**8:37** If there is famine, if there is death, when there is scorching, if there is locust or mildew, and if his enemy afflicts him in one of his cities, every misfortune, every pain,

**8:38** every prayer, every supplication that is made by any person, when each one knows the affliction of his heart and spreads out his hands toward this house,

**8:39** then You will hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and be merciful, and You will act and give to a man according to his ways, just as You know his heart, for You alone know the heart of all the sons of men,

**8:40** so that they may fear You all the days that they live upon the land which You gave to our fathers.

**8:41** And concerning the foreigner who is not from Your people,

**8:42** when they come and pray toward this place,

**8:43** then You will hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and act according to everything for which the foreigner calls upon You, so that all the peoples may know Your name and fear You, just as Your people Israel do, and know that Your name has been called upon this house which I built.

**8:44** When Your people go out to war against their enemies by the way in which You turn them, and they pray in the name of the Lord toward the city which You chose and the house which I built for Your name,

**8:45** then You will hear from heaven their supplication and their prayer, and You will execute justice for them.

**8:46** When they sin against You (for there is no person who will not sin), and You bring them and hand them over before enemies, and the captors take them captive to a land far or near,

**8:47** and they turn their hearts in the land where they were carried away, and they turn back in the land of their exile and make supplication to You, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted lawlessly,'

**8:48** and they turn back to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their enemies where You carried them away, and they pray to You toward their land which You gave to their fathers, the city which You chose, and the house which I have built for Your name,

**8:49** then You will hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place,

**8:50** and be merciful to their wrongdoings by which they sinned against You and concerning all their transgressions by which they transgressed against You, and You will grant them compassion before those who hold them captive, and they will have compassion on them;

**8:51** for they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt, from the midst of an iron smelting furnace.

**8:52** And let Your eyes and Your ears be open to the supplication of Your servant and to the supplication of Your people Israel, to hear them in all things for which they call upon You.

**8:53** And You set them apart for Yourself as an inheritance from all the peoples of the earth, just as You spoke by the hand of Your servant Moses when You brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt, Lord, Lord." Then Solomon spoke concerning the house when he finished building it: "The Lord made known the sun in heaven; He said He would dwell in thick darkness. Build my house, a magnificent house for Yourself, to dwell in newness." Look, is this not written in the book of the song?

**8:54** And it happened, when Solomon finished praying to the Lord all this prayer and supplication, that he arose from in front of the altar of the Lord, having knelt on his knees with his hands spread out toward heaven.

**8:55** And he stood and blessed all the assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying,

**8:56** "Blessed is the Lord today, who gave rest to His people Israel according to all that He spoke; not one word has failed among all His good words which He spoke by the hand of His servant Moses.

**8:57** May the Lord our God be with us, just as He was with our fathers; may He not forsake us nor turn away from us,

**8:58** to incline our hearts toward Him, to walk in all His ways and to keep all His commandments and His statutes which He commanded our fathers.

**8:59** And let these words, as I have made supplication before the Lord our God, be near to the Lord our God day and night, to execute justice for Your servant Israel, the matter of a day in the day of the year;

**8:60** so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the Lord God, He is God, and there is no other.

**8:61** And let our hearts be complete toward the Lord our God, and to walk devoutly in His statutes and to keep His commandments, as it is this day."

**8:62** And the king and all the sons of Israel offered a sacrifice before the Lord.

**8:63** And King Solomon offered the sacrifices of peace offerings which he offered to the Lord, twenty-two thousand oxen; and the king and all the sons of Israel dedicated the house of the Lord.

**8:64** On that day the king consecrated the middle of the courtyard that was in front of the house of the Lord; for there he offered the burnt offering and the sacrifices and the fat portions of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar that was before the Lord was too small to be able to hold the burnt offering and the sacrifices of the peace offerings.

**8:65** And Solomon held the feast on that day, and all Israel with him, a great assembly from the entrance of Hamath to the river of Egypt, before the Lord our God in the house which he built, eating and drinking and rejoicing before the Lord our God for seven days.

**8:66** And on the eighth day he sent the people away, and he blessed them, and each one went away to his tents rejoicing, and their heart was good over the good things which the Lord had done for David His servant and for Israel His people.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Greek reads "Athamein" (a metathesized form of the Hebrew month name "Ethanim") and omits the longer assembly and festival notice found in the Masoretic Text of vv. 1-2, while v. 1 opens with the twenty-year building completion notice.
- **8:6** — The term rendered "inner sanctuary" here and in v. 8 transliterates the Hebrew architectural term directly into Greek as "dabir."
- **8:8** — Where the Masoretic Text refers to the ark's poles, the Greek text reads "its holy things" (v. 7) and "the consecrated things" (v. 8), and omits "and they are there to this day."
- **8:11** — Verses 12-13 are absent at this point in the Greek Septuagint; Solomon's poetic pronouncement on the temple appears instead after v. 53.
- **8:28** — Greek "delight" (terpsis) reflects the joyful sense of Hebrew rinnah ("ringing cry" or "shout of joy"), and Greek also includes "to You" alongside "before You."
- **8:46** — Greek reads "bring them" (epaxeis autous) where the Masoretic Text reads "be angry with them."
- **8:53** — The Septuagint places Solomon's poetic pronouncement here (corresponding to MT vv. 12-13), reading "made known the sun," "dwell in thick darkness" (lit. "out of thick darkness"), "Build my house... to dwell in newness," and citing "the book of the song" (a transposed reading of Hebrew "Book of Jashar").
- **8:59** — Greek shifts to second person ("Your servant Israel") and renders the closing idiom literally as "the matter of a day in the day of the year," expressing each day's need throughout the year.
- **8:63** — The Greek text lists only twenty-two thousand oxen, omitting the one hundred twenty thousand sheep found in the Masoretic Text.
- **8:65** — The Greek text adds "in the house which he built, eating and drinking and rejoicing before the Lord our God" and specifies seven days (omitting the additional seven days in MT); in v. 66 Greek also reads "he blessed them" rather than "they blessed the king."
- **8:66** — Greek agathe he kardia literally reads "the heart [was] good" (echoing "the good things," tois agathois), an idiom meaning "glad" or "cheerful" (compare 2 Chr 7:10).
