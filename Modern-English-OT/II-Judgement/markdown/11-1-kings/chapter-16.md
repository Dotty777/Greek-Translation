## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came by the hand of Jehu son of Hanani to Baasha:

**16:2** "Because I lifted you up from the earth and appointed you leader over My people Israel, and you walked in the way of Jeroboam and caused My people Israel to sin, to provoke Me to anger with their worthless things,

**16:3** look, I am stirring up behind Baasha and behind his house, and I will make his house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**16:4** The one of Baasha who dies in the city, the dogs will devour him, and the one of his who dies in the field, the birds of the sky will devour him."

**16:5** And the rest of the matters of Baasha and all that he did and his acts of might, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:6** And Baasha slept with his fathers, and he was buried in Tirzah; and Elah his son became king in his place in the twentieth year of King Asa.

**16:7** And by the hand of Jehu of Hanani the Lord spoke against Baasha and against his house regarding all the evil which he did before the Lord, to provoke Him to anger with the works of his hands, so as to be like the house of Jeroboam, and because he struck it.

**16:8** And Elah son of Baasha reigned over Israel two years in Tirzah.

**16:9** And Zimri, the commander of half of the cavalry, conspired against him, and he was in Tirzah drinking, drunk in the house of Osa, the steward in Tirzah.

**16:10** And Zimri went in and struck him and put him to death, and he became king in his place.

**16:11** And it happened, when he became king, when he sat on his throne, that he struck the whole house of Baasha,

**16:12** according to the word which the Lord spoke against the house of Baasha and to Jehu the prophet,

**16:13** because of all the sins of Baasha and of Elah his son, in that he caused Israel to sin, to provoke the Lord, the God of Israel, to anger with their worthless things.

**16:14** And the rest of the matters of Elah which he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:15** And Zimri reigned seven years in Tirzah; and the camp of Israel was at Gibeon, which belonged to the foreigners.

**16:16** And the people in the camp heard them saying, "Zimri has conspired and struck the king." And in Israel they made Zimri, the leader of the army, king over Israel on that day in the camp.

**16:17** And Zimri went up, and all Israel with him, at Gibbethon, and they besieged Tirzah.

**16:18** And it happened, when Zimri saw that his city had already been captured, that they went into a cave of the house of the king, and the king burned the house of the king over him, and he died,

**16:19** for his sins which he committed, in doing what was evil before the Lord, walking in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat and in his sins, in that he caused Israel to sin.

**16:20** And the rest of the matters of Zimri and his conspiracies which he formed, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:21** Then the people of Israel were divided: half of the people went after Tibni son of Ginath to make him king, and half of the people went after Zimri.

**16:22** And the people who were after Tibni son of Ginath were defeated; and Tibni died, and Joram his brother, at that time, and Zimri became king after Tibni.

**16:23** In the thirty-first year of King Asa, Zimri reigned over Israel twelve years; in Tirzah he reigned six years.

**16:24** And Zimri bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer, the owner of the hill, for two talents of silver, and he built the hill; and they called the name of the hill which they built, after the name of Shemer the owner of the hill, Samaria.

**16:25** And Zimri did what was evil before the Lord; he acted more wickedly than all who had been before him.

**16:26** And he walked in every way of Jeroboam son of Nebat and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin, to provoke to anger with their worthless things.

**16:27** And the rest of the matters of Zimri and all that he did and his might, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:28** And Zimri slept with his fathers, and he was buried in Samaria; and Ahab his son became king in his place. And in the eleventh year of Zimri, Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king; he became king at thirty-five years of age in his reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Gabuza, daughter of Shilhi. And he walked in the way of Asa his father, and he did not turn aside from it, doing what was right before the Lord; yet they did not remove any of the high places; they sacrificed on the high places and burned incense. And what Jehoshaphat agreed upon, and all the might which he performed, and those against whom he waged war, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah? And the rest of the entanglements which they had set up in the days of Asa his father he removed from the land. And there was no king in Syria; a nasib was the king. He made a ship for Tarshish to go to Sophir, to go for the gold; and it did not go, because the ship was shattered at Ezion-geber. Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will send out your servants and my young servants in the ship." And Jehoshaphat was not willing. And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers in the city of David; and Joram his son became king in his place.

**16:29** In the second year of Jehoshaphat, Ahab son of Zimri became king; he reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.

**16:30** And Ahab did what was evil before the Lord; he acted more wickedly than all who were before him.

**16:31** And it was not enough for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and he took as a wife Jezebel, daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians, and he went and served Baal and worshiped him.

**16:32** And he set up an altar to Baal in the house of his abominations which he had built in Samaria.

**16:33** And Ahab made a grove; and Ahab continued to commit provocations to provoke his own soul to anger, so as to be utterly destroyed; he did more evil than all the kings of Israel who had been before him.

**16:34** Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho; at the cost of Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of Segub his youngest he set up its doors, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of Joshua son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **16:6** — Greek dates Elah's accession here to Asa's twentieth year (Hebrew places it in Asa's twenty-sixth year in 16:8), and in 16:9 names the steward "Osa" (Hebrew: Arza).
- **16:15** — Greek states that Zimri reigned "seven years" in Tirzah while Israel's camp was at "Gibeon" (Hebrew: "seven days" and "Gibbethon").
- **16:16** — From 16:16 through 16:29, this Greek text consistently reads "Zimri" where the Hebrew distinguishes "Omri" from Zimri; in 16:18 it also reads "into a cave" and "the king burned," and in 16:22 adds "and Joram his brother."
- **16:28** — Greek places the entire summary of Jehoshaphat's reign here (corresponding to Hebrew 1 Kings 22:41-50), dating it to Zimri's eleventh year, naming his mother "Gabuza" (Hebrew: Azubah), reading "Syria" (Hebrew: Edom) and "Sophir" (Hebrew: Ophir), and transliterating the Hebrew word for "deputy" as "nasib."
- **16:33** — Greek reads "grove" (Hebrew: Asherah) and "to provoke his own soul to anger, so as to be utterly destroyed," where the Hebrew reads "to provoke the Lord, the God of Israel, to anger."
