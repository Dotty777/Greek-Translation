## Text

**5:1** And thus the appointed officials supplied King Solomon, and all orders for the king's table, each one in his month; they do not deviate from a word. And they brought the barley and the straw for the horses and for the chariots to the place wherever the king might be, each one according to his assignment.

**5:2** And these were the requirements for Solomon in one day: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal,

**5:3** and ten choice calves and twenty pasture-fed oxen and one hundred sheep, besides deer and gazelles and choice birds, fattened.

**5:4** For he was ruler beyond the river, and he had peace from all sides all around.

**5:9** And the Lord gave understanding to Solomon, and exceedingly great wisdom, and vastness of heart like the sand beside the sea.

**5:10** And Solomon abounded exceedingly beyond the understanding of all ancient men and beyond all the discerning men of Egypt.

**5:11** And he was wiser than all men, and he was wiser than Gaithan the Zareite, and Ainan, and Chalkad, and Darala, son of Mal.

**5:12** And Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs were five thousand.

**5:13** And he spoke about the trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon even to the hyssop that comes out through the wall; and he spoke about the beasts, and about the birds, and about the creeping things, and about the fish.

**5:14** And all the peoples came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth, as many as heard of his wisdom. And Solomon took the daughter of Pharaoh for himself as a wife, and brought her into the city of David until he completed the house of the Lord, and his own house, and the wall of Jerusalem. When Pharaoh king of Egypt went up, he captured Gezer beforehand and burned it with fire, and the Canaanite dwelling in Mergab; and Pharaoh gave them as parting gifts to his daughter, Solomon's wife.

**5:15** And Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to anoint Solomon in place of David his father, because Hiram continually loved David all the days.

**5:16** And Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,

**5:17** "You know David my father, that he was not able to build a house for the name of the Lord my God because of the wars that surrounded him, until he placed them under the soles of his feet.

**5:18** And now the Lord my God has given me rest all around; there is no adversary and there is no evil offense.

**5:19** And look, I say, 'I will build a house for the name of the Lord my God,' just as the Lord God spoke to David my father, saying, 'Your son, whom I will set in your place upon your throne, he will build the house for My name.'

**5:20** And now give command, and let them cut timber for me from Lebanon; and look, my servants will be with your servants; for your service I will give to you according to everything that you say, because you know that it is not possible for us on our own to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:21** And it came about, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced exceedingly and said, "Blessed be God today, who has given to David a discerning son over this numerous people."

**5:22** And he sent to Solomon, saying, "I have heard concerning everything that you have sent to me; I will do all your desire, cedar and pine timber.

**5:23** My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea; I will set them as rafts as far as the place of which you send word to me, and I will cast them loose there, and you will take them away; and you will do my desire in giving bread to my household."

**5:24** And Hiram kept giving to Solomon cedars and all his desire.

**5:25** And Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat and macheir for his household, and twenty thousand baths of beaten oil; in this manner Solomon gave to Hiram year by year.

**5:26** And the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon, just as He spoke to him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between themselves.

**5:27** And the king raised a levy also from all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.

**5:28** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month, alternating; a month they were in Lebanon and two months at their home; and Adoniram was over the levy.

**5:29** And Solomon had seventy thousand carrying burdens and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain,

**5:30** besides the officials appointed over the works of Solomon, three thousand six hundred overseers, who performed the works.

**5:32** And they prepared the stones and the timber for three years.

## Translator Notes

- **5:11** — The Greek preserves distinct forms of these names (Gaithan the Zareite, Ainan, Chalkad, and Darala) and reads singular "son of Mal," corresponding to Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, sons of Mahol, in the Hebrew text.
- **5:14** — The Greek places the account of Pharaoh's daughter and Gezer here rather than in chapters 3 and 9, names the Canaanite city Mergab, and has no separate verb for killing the Canaanite.
- **5:15** — The Greek reads "to anoint Solomon," whereas the Hebrew text reads "because he heard that they had anointed him king."
- **5:20** — The Greek lacks "wages" before "your service" and reads the adverb idios ("on our own"), which may reflect an ancient orthographic variant of eidos ("one who knows how").
- **5:25** — "Macheir" is an untranslated Greek transliteration corresponding to Hebrew "food" or "provisions," and the Greek specifies twenty thousand baths of oil rather than twenty kors.
