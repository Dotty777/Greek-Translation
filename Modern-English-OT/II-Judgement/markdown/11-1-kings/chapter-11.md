## Text

**11:1** And King Solomon was a lover of women, and he had seven hundred princesses and three hundred concubines; and he took foreign wives, and the daughter of Pharaoh: Moabites, Ammonites, Syrians and Idumeans, Hittites and Amorites.

**11:2** From the nations concerning which the Lord had forbidden the sons of Israel, "You shall not go in to them, and they shall not go in to you, so that they do not turn your hearts aside after their idols," to them Solomon clung in love.

**11:4** And it happened in the time of Solomon's old age that his heart was not whole with the Lord his God, as was the heart of David his father; and the foreign wives turned his heart aside after their gods.

**11:5** Then Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, the idol of Moab, and for their king, the idol of the sons of Ammon,

**11:6** and for Astarte, the abomination of the Sidonians.

**11:7** And so he did for all his foreign wives; they burned incense and sacrificed to their idols.

**11:8** And Solomon did what was evil in the sight of the Lord; he did not walk after the Lord as David his father did.

**11:9** And the Lord became angry with Solomon, because he turned his heart aside from the Lord, the God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice

**11:10** and had commanded him concerning this matter not at all to walk after other gods, and to take care to do what the Lord God had commanded him; nor was his heart whole with the Lord, according to the heart of David his father.

**11:11** And the Lord said to Solomon, "Because these things have happened with you and you have not kept My commandments and My statutes which I commanded you, I will surely tear your kingdom from your hand and give it to your servant.

**11:12** However, in your days I will not do these things, for the sake of David your father; from the hand of your son I will take it.

**11:13** However, I will certainly not take the whole kingdom; I will give one tribe to your son for the sake of David My servant and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen."

**11:14** And the Lord raised up an adversary against Solomon: Hader the Idumean, and Hesrom son of Eliada, the one in Raemmaer, Hadrazar king of Zobah, his lord. And men gathered to him, and he was commander of a band, and he seized Damascus; and they were an adversary to Israel all the days of Solomon. And Hader the Idumean was of the seed of the kingdom in Idumea.

**11:15** And it happened, when David utterly destroyed Edom, when Joab the commander of the army went to bury the fallen, that they struck down every male in Idumea;

**11:16** for six months Joab and all Israel remained there in Idumea, until he had utterly destroyed every male in Idumea.

**11:17** And Hader fled, he and all the Idumeans of his father's servants with him, and they went into Egypt; and Hader was a little boy.

**11:18** And men arose from the city of Midian and came to Paran, and they took men with them and came to Pharaoh king of Egypt; and Hader went in to Pharaoh, and he gave him a house and appointed bread for him.

**11:19** And Hader found great favor in the sight of Pharaoh, and he gave him as a wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Thekemina the elder. And the sister of Thekemina bore to him, to Hader, Genubath her son;

**11:20** and Thekemina brought him up in the midst of the sons of Pharaoh, and Genubath was in the midst of the sons of Pharaoh.

**11:21** And Hader heard in Egypt that David had slept with his fathers, and that Joab the commander of the army was dead; and Hader said to Pharaoh, "Send me away, and I will return to my land."

**11:22** And Pharaoh said to Hader, "What do you lack with me, that look, you seek to depart to your land?" And Hader said to him, "Yet you must surely send me away." And Hader returned to his land.

**11:25** This was the evil that Hader did: he abhorred Israel, and he reigned in Edom.

**11:26** And Jeroboam son of Nebat, the Ephrathite from Sareira, the son of a widow woman, was the servant of Solomon.

**11:27** And this is the matter of how he lifted up his hands against the king. Solomon built the citadel; he closed up the enclosure of the city of David his father.

**11:28** And the man Jeroboam was mighty in strength; and Solomon saw the young man, that he was a man of works, and he appointed him over the levies of the house of Joseph.

**11:29** And it happened at that time that Jeroboam went out from Jerusalem, and Ahijah the Shilonite, the prophet, found him on the road, and drew him aside from the road; and Ahijah was wrapped in a new garment, and both of them were in the field.

**11:30** And Ahijah took hold of his new garment that was on him and tore it into twelve torn pieces;

**11:31** and he said to Jeroboam, "Take for yourself ten torn pieces, for thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: 'Look, I am tearing the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and I will give you ten tribes;

**11:32** and two tribes will belong to him for the sake of David My servant and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel;

**11:33** because he has forsaken Me, and has sacrificed to Astarte, the abomination of the Sidonians, and to Chemosh and among the idols of Moab, and to their king, the detestable thing of the sons of Ammon, and has not walked in My ways to do what is right in My sight, as David his father did.

**11:34** And I will certainly not take the whole kingdom out of his hand, because I will surely oppose him all the days of his life, for the sake of David My servant whom I chose.

**11:35** And I will take the kingdom out of the hand of his son, and I will give you the ten tribes;

**11:36** but to his son I will give the two tribes, so that there may be a position for My servant David all the days before Me in Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen for Myself to place My name there.

**11:37** And I will take you, and you shall reign over all that your soul desires, and you shall be king over Israel.

**11:38** And it will be, if you keep everything that I command you, and walk in My ways, and do what is right in My sight by keeping My commandments and My statutes, just as David My servant did, then I will be with you and build for you a faithful house, just as I built for David.'"

**11:40** And Solomon sought to put Jeroboam to death; and he arose and withdrew and fled to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and he was in Egypt until Solomon died.

**11:41** And the rest of the acts of Solomon, and everything that he did, and all his understanding, look, are these not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?

**11:42** And the days that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem were forty years.

**11:43** And Solomon slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David his father. And it happened, when Jeroboam son of Nebat heard, while he was still in Egypt, since he had fled from the presence of Solomon and was living in Egypt, that he went straight on; and he came to his city, to the land of Sareira in the hill country of Ephraim. And King Solomon slept with his fathers, and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — Greek renders Hebrew Molech/Milcom as "their king" (also in 11:33), and rearranges the material of MT 11:1-8 (omitting v. 3, whose numbers appear in v. 1).
- **11:13** — "Tribe" translates Greek skeptron (lit., "scepter"), an LXX idiom for Hebrew shevet ("staff, scepter, tribe") used throughout chs. 11-12; contrast the LXX reading "two tribes" in 11:32 and 11:36 (MT "one tribe").
- **11:14** — Greek merges MT 11:14 and 11:23-25a here, transliterates "adversary" as satan, and preserves the distinct names Hader (MT Hadad), Hesrom (MT Rezon), and Hadrazar (MT Hadadezer). "The one in Raemmaer" reflects a Greek transliteration of Hebrew "who fled from," leaving "Hadrazar king of Zobah, his lord" syntactically loose in the Greek.
- **11:19** — Greek preserves the distinct name Thekemina (MT Tahpenes); likewise, in 11:26, 11:43, and 12:24 Greek reads Sareira (MT Zeredah).
- **11:34** — Greek reads "I will surely oppose him" (antitassomenos antitaxomai auto) where MT reads "I will make him prince"; similarly in 11:36 Greek reads thesis ("a position") where MT has "a lamp."
