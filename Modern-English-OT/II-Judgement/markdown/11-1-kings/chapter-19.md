## Text

**19:1** And Ahab reported to Jezebel his wife all that Elijah had done, and how he had killed the prophets with the sword.

**19:2** And Jezebel sent to Elijah and said, "If you are Elijah and I am Jezebel, may God do thus to me and add thus, that at this hour tomorrow I will make your life like the life of one of them."

**19:3** And Elijah was afraid, and he arose and departed for his own life, and he came to Beersheba, the land of Judah, and left his young servant there.

**19:4** And he himself went in the wilderness a day's journey, and came and sat down beneath a broom bush. And he asked for his life to die, and said, "Let it be enough now; please take my life from me, Lord, for I am no better than my fathers."

**19:5** And he lay down and fell asleep there under a plant; and look, someone touched him and said to him, "Arise and eat."

**19:6** And Elijah looked, and look, at his head was a spelt cake baked in ashes and a flask of water. And he arose and ate and drank, and turning back, he lay down.

**19:7** And the angel of the Lord returned a second time, and touched him and said to him, "Arise, eat, for the journey is too great for you."

**19:8** And he arose and ate and drank, and he went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights as far as Mount Horeb.

**19:9** And he entered there into the cave and lodged there; and look, the word of the Lord came to him and said, "Why are you here, Elijah?"

**19:10** And Elijah said, "With zeal I have been zealous for the Lord Almighty, because the sons of Israel have forsaken You; they have demolished Your altars and killed Your prophets with the sword, and I am left all alone, and they seek my life, to take it."

**19:11** And He said, "You shall go out tomorrow and stand before the Lord on the mountain; look, the Lord will pass by." And there was a great, mighty wind breaking up mountains and shattering rocks before the Lord, in the wind of the Lord; and after the wind, an earthquake; the Lord was not in the earthquake;

**19:12** and after the earthquake, a fire; the Lord was not in the fire; and after the fire, the sound of a light breeze.

**19:13** And it happened, when Elijah heard it, he covered his face with his sheepskin cloak, and he went out and stood beneath the cave; and look, a voice came to him and said, "Why are you here, Elijah?"

**19:14** And Elijah said, "With zeal I have been zealous for the Lord Almighty, because the sons of Israel have forsaken You; they have overthrown Your covenant and Your altars and killed Your prophets with the sword, and I am left all alone, and they seek my life, to take it."

**19:15** And the Lord said to him, "Go, return on your way, and you shall come to the way of the wilderness of Damascus; and you shall come and anoint Hazael as king of Syria;

**19:16** and you shall anoint the son of Jehu, son of Nimshi, as king over Israel; and you shall anoint Elisha son of Shaphat, from Abel-meholah, as prophet in your place.

**19:17** And it will be that the one who escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu will put to death, and the one who escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha will put to death.

**19:18** And you will leave in Israel seven thousand men, all the knees that have not bent the knee to Baal, and every mouth that has not worshiped him."

**19:19** And he departed from there and found Elisha son of Shaphat, and he was plowing with oxen; twelve yokes were before him, and he was among the twelve. He came up to him and threw his sheepskin cloak upon him.

**19:20** And Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah and said, "Let me kiss my father, and I will follow after you." And Elijah said, "Return, for I have done it to you."

**19:21** And he returned from behind him, and took the yokes of oxen and slaughtered and boiled them with the equipment of the oxen, and gave it to the people, and they ate. And he arose and went after Elijah, and served him.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Greek opens Jezebel's oath with the distinctive clause 'If you are Elijah and I am Jezebel' (absent in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and uses singular 'God' with a positive oath statement introduced by hoti ('that').
- **19:4** — 'broom bush' renders the Greek transliteration rathmen (from Hebrew rotem, a desert broom shrub), whereas v. 5 uses the general Greek noun phyton ('plant'); in v. 3 the Greek also reads 'was afraid' (ephobethe) rather than Hebrew 'saw.'
- **19:11** — This Greek text reads en to pneumati Kyriou ('in the wind of the Lord') without the negation 'not' that appears in the following clauses and in the Hebrew text ('the LORD was not in the wind').
- **19:16** — The Greek reads 'the son of Jehu, son of Nimshi' rather than Hebrew 'Jehu son of Nimshi,' and in v. 18 likewise has second-person 'you will leave' (kataleipseis) rather than first-person 'I will leave.'
- **19:20** — The Greek omits 'and my mother' and renders Elijah's reply as a statement, Anastrephe, hoti pepoieka soi ('Return, for I have done it to you'), without the interrogative 'what' of the Hebrew text.
