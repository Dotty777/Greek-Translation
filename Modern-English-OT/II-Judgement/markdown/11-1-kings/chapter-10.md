## Text

**10:1** And the queen of Sheba heard of the name of Solomon and the name of the Lord, and she came to test him with riddles.

**10:2** And she came to Jerusalem with a very great force, and camels carrying spices and very much gold and precious stones. And she came in to Solomon and spoke to him all that was in her heart.

**10:3** And Solomon declared to her all her matters; there was not a matter overlooked by the king which he did not declare to her.

**10:4** And the queen of Sheba saw all the understanding of Solomon, and the house that he had built,

**10:5** and the food of Solomon, and the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and his apparel, and his cupbearers, and his burnt offering which he offered in the house of the Lord; and she was beside herself.

**10:6** And she said to King Solomon, "True is the word which I heard in my land concerning your word and concerning your understanding;

**10:7** and I did not believe those speaking to me until I arrived and my eyes have seen; and look, they are not half as they declared to me; you have added good things to them beyond all the report which I heard in my land.

**10:8** Blessed are your wives! Blessed are these servants of yours who stand before you continually, who hear all your understanding!

**10:9** May the Lord your God be blessed, who delighted in you to set you on the throne of Israel, because the Lord loves Israel to establish it forever; and He appointed you king over them to execute judgment in righteousness and in their judgments."

**10:10** And she gave to Solomon one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and very many spices, and precious stones; there never again came any in abundance like those spices which the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**10:11** And the ship of Hiram was carrying the gold from Suphir; it brought very many hewn timbers and precious stones.

**10:12** And the king made the hewn timbers into supports of the house of the Lord and of the house of the king, and harps and lyres for the singers; no such hewn timbers had come upon the earth, nor have they been seen anywhere until this day.

**10:13** And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all that she desired, whatever she asked, besides all that he had given her by the hand of King Solomon. And she turned back and went to her land, she and all her servants.

**10:14** And the weight of the gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

**10:15** apart from the tributes of those subjected, of the merchants, and of all the kings of the region beyond, and of the satraps of the land.

**10:16** And Solomon made three hundred spears of beaten gold; three hundred gold pieces were upon each spear;

**10:17** and three hundred shields of beaten gold; and three minas of gold were in each shield. And he put them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.

**10:18** And the king made a great ivory throne, and overlaid it with tested gold.

**10:19** The throne had six steps, and busts of calves were on the throne at its back, on this side and on that side at the place of the seat, and two lions standing beside the arms,

**10:20** and twelve lions standing on the six steps, on this side and on that side. Nothing like this had been made for any kingdom.

**10:21** And all the vessels made by Solomon were gold, and golden basins; all the vessels of the forest of the house of Lebanon were encased in gold; there was no silver, because it was not taken into account in the days of Solomon.

**10:22** For the king had a ship of Tarshish on the sea with the ships of Hiram; once every three years a ship came to the king from Tarshish with gold and silver and carved and hewn stones. This was the matter of the requisition which King Solomon raised to build the house of the Lord and the house of the king and the wall of Jerusalem and the citadel, to fence around the enclosure of the city of David, and Asshur and Midian and Gezer and Upper Beth-horon and Jethermath, and all the cities of the chariots and all the cities of the horsemen, and the undertaking of Solomon which he undertook to build in Jerusalem and in all the land, so that all the people indicated by the Hittite and the Amorite and the Perizzite and the Canaanite and the Hivite and the Jebusite and the Girgashite, those who were not of the sons of Israel, might not rule over him. Their children who were left behind after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel were not able to utterly destroy, Solomon brought up to tribute until this day. And of the sons of Israel Solomon did not assign a task, because they were the warrior men, and his servants, and commanders of his chariots, and his horsemen.

**10:23** And Solomon was magnified above all the kings in wealth and in understanding.

**10:24** And all the kings of the earth were seeking the presence of Solomon to hear his understanding, which the Lord had given to his heart.

**10:25** And they were bringing, each one his gifts: vessels of gold and apparel, oil of myrrh and spices, and horses and mules, year by year.

**10:26** And Solomon had four thousand mares for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he placed them in the cities of the chariots and with the king in Jerusalem. And he was ruler of all the kings from the river even to the land of the foreigners and to the borders of Egypt.

**10:27** And the king made the gold and the silver in Jerusalem like stones, and the cedars he made like the sycamore trees that are in the lowland in abundance.

**10:28** And Solomon's export of horsemen was also from Egypt, and the merchants of the king were from Tekoa, and he received them from Tekoa in exchange;

**10:29** and the export came up from Egypt: a chariot for one hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for fifty pieces of silver. And in this way for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Syria they went out by sea.

## Translator Notes

- **10:11** — The Greek reads the distinct place name 'Suphir' (corresponding to Hebrew 'Ophir') and 'hewn timbers' where the Hebrew text specifies almug wood.
- **10:16** — The Greek records three hundred spears with three hundred gold pieces on each, differing from the Hebrew text's two hundred large shields of six hundred shekels each.
- **10:19** — The Greek reads 'busts of calves' (protomai moschon) at the back of the throne, whereas the Hebrew text describes a rounded top.
- **10:22** — The Greek lists a cargo of carved and hewn stones and incorporates the building and forced-labor account corresponding to Hebrew 9:15-22, with distinct place names ('Asshur,' 'Midian,' 'Upper Beth-horon,' and 'Jethermath' for Hebrew Hazor, Megiddo, Lower Beth-horon, and Baalath/Tamar). The clause 'so that all the people indicated by...' reflects a distinct Greek rendering of the Hebrew text and an early Greek textual variant for 'left over.'
- **10:28** — The Greek reads the distinct place name 'Tekoa' (Thekoue), corresponding to 'Kue' (or 'droves') in the Hebrew text, and speaks of 'horsemen' rather than horses; in 10:29 the Greek likewise gives lower prices (100 and 50 pieces of silver) and reads 'by sea' instead of 'by their hand.'
