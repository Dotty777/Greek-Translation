## Text

**3:2** Burning incense on the high places, because a house had not been built for the Lord until now.

**3:3** And Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the statutes of David his father; only at the high places he was sacrificing and burning incense.

**3:4** And he arose and went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, because it was highest and great; Solomon offered up a thousand burnt offerings on the altar in Gibeon.

**3:5** And the Lord appeared to Solomon in sleep at night, and the Lord said to Solomon, "Ask some request for yourself."

**3:6** And Solomon said, "You showed great mercy to Your servant David my father, just as he walked before You in truth and in righteousness and in uprightness of heart with You, and You kept for him this great mercy, to set his son on his throne as it is this day.

**3:7** And now, Lord my God, You have given Your servant in place of David my father; and I am a small child, and I do not know my going out and my coming in.

**3:8** And Your servant is in the midst of Your people whom You have chosen, a numerous people who cannot be numbered.

**3:9** And give to Your servant a heart to hear and to judge Your people in righteousness, and to understand between good and evil; for who will be able to judge this weighty people of Yours?"

**3:10** And it was pleasing before the Lord that Solomon had asked for this thing.

**3:11** And the Lord said to him, "Because you have asked this thing from Me, and have not asked for yourself many days, and have not asked for wealth, nor have you asked for the lives of your enemies, but have asked for yourself understanding to hear judgment,

**3:12** look, I have carried out your word; look, I have given you a discerning and wise heart; there has been no one like you before you, and after you no one like you will arise.

**3:13** And what you did not ask for I have given you, both wealth and glory, so that there has not been a man like you among kings;

**3:14** and if you walk in My way to keep My commandments and My statutes, as David your father walked, then I will multiply your days."

**3:15** And Solomon awoke, and look, it was a dream. And he arose and came to Jerusalem, and he stood before the altar that was before the ark of the covenant of the Lord in Zion, and offered up burnt offerings and made peace offerings, and made a great banquet for himself and for all his servants.

**3:16** Then two women who were prostitutes appeared to the king and stood before him.

**3:17** And the one woman said, "Pardon me, lord, I and this woman live in one house, and we gave birth in the house.

**3:18** And it happened on the third day after I gave birth that this woman also gave birth. And we were together, and there is no one with us except the two of us in the house.

**3:19** And this woman's son died at night, since she lay upon him;

**3:20** and she arose in the middle of the night and took my son from my arms and laid him in her bosom, and her dead son she laid in my bosom.

**3:21** And I arose in the morning to nurse my son, and he was dead; and now, I examined him in the morning, and look, he was not my son whom I had borne."

**3:22** And the other woman said, "No, rather my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one." And they spoke before the king.

**3:23** And the king said to them, "You say, 'This is my son, the living one, and this woman's son is the dead one,' and you say, 'No, rather my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.'"

**3:24** And the king said, "Fetch a sword." And they brought the sword before the king.

**3:25** And the king said, "Divide the living nursing child in two, and give half of him to this one and half of him to that one."

**3:26** And the woman whose son was the living one answered and spoke to the king, because her womb was stirred over her son, and she said, "Pardon me, lord, give her the child, and by no means put him to death." And the other said, "Let him be neither mine nor hers; divide him."

**3:27** And the king answered and said, "Give the child to the one who said, 'Give him to her, and by no means put him to death'; she is his mother."

**3:28** And all Israel heard this judgment that the king had judged, and they feared before the king, because they saw that the discernment of God was in him to do justice.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Greek text omits verse 1 and the opening clause of verse 2 (found in the Hebrew), beginning abruptly with the participle "burning incense."
- **3:15** — The Greek adds that Solomon stood "before the altar that was before the ark of the covenant of the Lord in Zion" and made "a great banquet for himself," expanding on the shorter Hebrew text.
- **3:17** — "Pardon me, lord" translates the Septuagint idiom "in me, lord" (en emoi, kyrie; also in v. 26), a literal rendering of the Hebrew entreaty particle bi; the plural "we gave birth" also differs from the Hebrew singular.
- **3:26** — "Her womb was stirred" renders the Greek literally (he metra autes), reflecting the Hebrew idiom for deep maternal compassion.
- **3:27** — The Greek clarifies the king's verdict by explicitly adding "to the one who said" before quoting the first woman's plea, whereas the Hebrew simply reads "Give her the living child."
