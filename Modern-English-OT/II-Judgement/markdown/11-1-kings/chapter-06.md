## Text

**6:1** And it came about in the four hundred and fortieth year of the departure of the sons of Israel from Egypt, in the fourth year, in the second month, while King Solomon was reigning over Israel, that they brought great, costly stones for the foundation of the house, and unhewn stones; and the sons of Solomon and the sons of Hiram hewed them and laid them. In the fourth year he laid the foundation of the house of the Lord, in the month of Niso and the second month. In the eleventh year, in the month of Baad (this is the eighth month), the house was completed in every detail of it and in every plan of it.

**6:2** And as for the house that the king built for the Lord, its length was forty, and its width was twenty by the cubit, and its height was twenty-five by the cubit.

**6:3** And the ailam in front of the temple was twenty by the cubit in its length, along the width of the house, in front of the house. And he built the house and completed it.

**6:4** And he made for the house slanted, hidden windows.

**6:5** And he placed chambers against the wall itself of the house, all around the temple and the dabir.

**6:6** The lowest side-story was five cubits by the cubit in its width, and the middle one six, and the third seven by the cubit in its width; for he put a setback on the house all around on the outside of the house, so that they would not take hold of the walls of the house.

**6:7** And the house, while it was being built, was built with quarried, unworked stones; and no hammer or axe or any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.

**6:8** And the entrance of the lowest side-story was under the right shoulder of the house, and there was a winding stairway to the middle story, and from the middle one to the third story.

**6:9** And he built the house and completed it; and he paneled the house with cedars.

**6:10** And he built the tie-frames throughout the whole house, five by the cubit in its height, and he held the joint together with cedar timbers.

**6:15** And he built the walls of the house with cedar timbers, from the floor of the house even to the beams and to the walls; he paneled it continuously with wood on the inside, and he overlaid the inside of the house with pine planks.

**6:16** And he built the twenty cubits from the end of the wall, the one side from the floor to the beams; and he made it from the dabir into the Holy of Holies.

**6:17** And the temple was forty cubits in front of the dabir, in the middle of the house on the inside, to place there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**6:19** To place there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**6:20** Twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits was its height.

**6:21** And he overlaid it with enclosed gold; and he made an altar in front of the dabir and overlaid it with gold.

**6:22** And he overlaid the entire house with gold, until the completion of the whole house.

**6:23** And he made in the dabir two cherubim, ten cubits in measured size.

**6:24** And its wing was five cubits; and the second was ten by the cubit from the end of its wing.

**6:25** Likewise for the second cherub, one finish in one measure; for both there was one finish.

**6:26** And the height of the cherub was by the cubit, and likewise the second cherub.

**6:27** And both cherubim were in the middle of the innermost house; and he spread out their wings, and one wing touched the wall, and a wing touched the second wall; and its wings that were in the middle of the house touched wing to wing.

**6:28** And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.

**6:29** He engraved all the walls of the house all around with a graving tool with carved cherubim, and palm trees in the inner and in the outer.

**6:30** And he overlaid the floor of the house with gold, of the innermost and of the outermost.

**6:31** And for the doorway of the dabir he made doors of juniper wood; porticoes, fourfold.

**6:34** In both doors there was pine wood: the one door was two leaves, and their pivots, and the second door was two leaves, pivoting.

**6:35** Carved cherubim and palm trees and outspread petals, and overlaid with gold brought down upon the carved relief.

**6:36** And he built the innermost court, three courses of unhewn stones and a course of worked cedar all around. And he built a curtain of the court of the ailam of the house that was in front of the temple.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek dates the work to the 440th year after the departure from Egypt (rather than the 480th), reads "sons" where the Hebrew has "builders," and gives the month names as Niso and Baad (corresponding to Ziv and Bul).
- **6:3** — "Ailam" is a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew architectural term ulam, denoting the porch or vestibule of the temple.
- **6:5** — "Dabir" is a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew architectural term debir, denoting the inner sanctuary of the temple.
- **6:21** — "Enclosed gold" literally translates Greek synkekleismeno, reflecting the Hebrew idiom for refined, choice gold.
- **6:26** — The Greek omits the numeral "ten" before "by the cubit" here, and similarly compresses the wing measurements in verse 24.
- **6:36** — The closing sentence ("And he built a curtain of the court of the ailam...") is a distinctive Septuagint reading with no counterpart in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
