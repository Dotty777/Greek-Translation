## Text

**12:1** And King Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel were coming to Shechem to make him king.

**12:3** And the people spoke to King Rehoboam, saying,

**12:4** "Your father made our yoke heavy; and now, lighten the harsh service of your father and his heavy yoke that he laid on us, and we will serve you."

**12:5** And he said to them, "Go away for three days, and return to me." And they went away.

**12:6** And the king summoned the elders, who had been standing before Solomon his father while he was still alive, saying, "How do you advise that I answer this people with a word?"

**12:7** And they spoke to him, saying, "If on this day you will be a servant to this people, and serve them, and speak good words to them, then they will be servants to you all the days."

**12:8** And he abandoned the counsel of the elders which they had counseled him, and took counsel with the young men who had been brought up with him, who stood in his presence,

**12:9** and said to them, "What do you advise? And what shall I answer this people who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke that your father put on us'?"

**12:10** And the young men who had been brought up with him, who stood in his presence, spoke to him, saying, "Thus you shall speak to this people who spoke to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, and now you lighten it from us'; thus you shall speak to them: 'My littleness is thicker than my father's waist.

**12:11** And now my father loaded you with a heavy yoke, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with scorpions.'"

**12:12** And all Israel came to King Rehoboam on the third day, just as the king had spoken to them, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

**12:13** And the king answered the people harshly, and Rehoboam abandoned the counsel of the elders which they had counseled him,

**12:14** and spoke to them according to the counsel of the young men, saying, "My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions."

**12:15** And the king did not listen to the people, for it was a turn of events from the Lord, so that He might establish His word that He spoke by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite concerning Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**12:16** And all Israel saw that the king did not listen to them; and the people answered the king, saying, "What portion do we have in David? And we have no inheritance in the son of Jesse! Run off, Israel, to your tents; now feed your house, David!" And Israel went away to their tents.

**12:18** And the king sent Aram, who was over the tribute, and they stoned him with stones, and he died; and King Rehoboam hurried to go up to flee to Jerusalem.

**12:19** And Israel has rebelled against the house of David to this day.

**12:20** And it happened, when all Israel heard that Rehoboam had returned, that they sent and called him to the assembly, and they made him king over Israel; and there was no one following David except the tribe of Judah and Benjamin alone.

**12:21** And Rehoboam entered into Jerusalem, and he assembled the assembly of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin, one hundred and twenty thousand young men making war, to fight against the house of Israel, to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam son of Solomon.

**12:22** And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah, a man of God, saying,

**12:23** "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people, saying,

**12:24** 'Thus says the Lord: You shall not go up nor fight with your brothers, the sons of Israel; let each one return to his own house, for this matter has come from Me.'" And they listened to the word of the Lord, and they ceased from going, according to the word of the Lord. And King Solomon slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place in Jerusalem, being sixteen years old when he became king; and he reigned twelve years in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Naanan, the daughter of Ana son of Nahash, king of Ammon; and he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord and did not walk in the way of David his father. And there was a man from the hill country of Ephraim, a servant to Solomon, and his name was Jeroboam, and the name of his mother was Sareisa, a prostitute; and Solomon set him as commander of the rod over the levies of the house of Joseph. And Solomon built Sareira, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, and he had three hundred chariots of horses; this man built the citadel with the levies of the house of Ephraim; this man enclosed the city of David, and he was lifting himself up against the kingdom. And Solomon was seeking to put him to death; and he was afraid, and he himself fled to Shishak king of Egypt, and he was with him until Solomon died. And Jeroboam heard in Egypt that Solomon was dead, and he spoke in the ears of Shishak king of Egypt, saying, "Send me away, and I will go away to my land." And Shishak said to him, "Ask any request, and I will give it to you." And Shishak gave to Jeroboam Ano, the elder sister of Thekemina his wife, to him as a wife; she was the great one among the daughters of the king, and she bore to Jeroboam Abijah his son. And Jeroboam said to Shishak, "Indeed, send me away, and I will go away." And Jeroboam went out from Egypt and came to the land of Sareira, which is in the hill country of Ephraim; and the whole tribe of Ephraim gathered there; and Jeroboam built a fortification there. And his child fell sick with a very severe sickness; and Jeroboam went to inquire concerning the child; and he said to Ano his wife, "Arise, go; inquire of God concerning the child, whether he will live from his sickness." Now there was a man in Shiloh and his name was Ahijah, and this man was sixty years old, and the word of the Lord was with him. And Jeroboam said to his wife, "Arise and take in your hand for the man of God loaves of bread, and cakes for his children, and grapes, and a jar of honey." And the woman arose and took in her hand loaves of bread and two cakes and grapes and a jar of honey for Ahijah; and the man was elderly, and his eyes were too dim to see. And she arose from Sareira and went; and it happened, when she came into the city to Ahijah the Shilonite, that Ahijah said to his young servant, "Go out now to meet Ano the wife of Jeroboam, and you shall say to her, 'Come in and do not stand still, for thus says the Lord: I am sending harsh things upon you.'" And Ano went in to the man of God, and Ahijah said to her, "Why have you brought me loaves of bread and grapes and cakes and a jar of honey? Thus says the Lord: Look, you will go away from me, and it will happen when you enter the gate into Sareira that your maidservants will come out to meet you and will say to you, 'The child is dead.' For thus says the Lord: Look, I will utterly destroy from Jeroboam anyone who urinates against a wall; and it will be that those of Jeroboam who die in the city, the dogs will devour, and the one who dies in the field, the birds of the sky will devour. And the child will be mourned, 'Woe, lord!' because a good thing was found in him concerning the Lord." And the woman went away when she heard; and it happened, as she entered into Sareira, that the child died, and the outcry went out to meet her. And Jeroboam went to Shechem, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, and gathered the tribes of Israel there, and Rehoboam son of Solomon went up there. And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah the Enlamite, saying, "Take for yourself a new garment that has not entered into water, and tear it into twelve torn pieces; and you shall give them to Jeroboam, and you shall say to him, 'Thus says the Lord: Take for yourself twelve torn pieces to wrap around yourself.'" And Jeroboam took them; and Shemaiah said, "Thus says the Lord over the ten tribes of Israel." And the people said to Rehoboam son of Solomon, "Your father made his yoke heavy upon us, and made heavy the food of his table; and now you shall lighten it upon us, and we will serve you." And Rehoboam said to the people, "Three more days, and I will answer you a word." And Rehoboam said, "Bring the elders in to me, and I will take counsel with them as to what word I should answer the people on the third day." And Rehoboam spoke in their ears just as the people had sent to him; and the elders of the people said, "Thus the people have spoken to you." And Rehoboam rejected their counsel, and it was not pleasing in his sight; and he sent and brought in his companions, and he spoke the same things to them: "These things also the people sent to me, saying." And his companions said, "Thus you shall speak to the people, saying, 'My littleness is thicker than my father's waist; my father flogged you with whips, but I will rule you with scorpions.'" And the word was pleasing in the sight of Rehoboam; and he answered the people just as his companions, the young men, had counseled him. And all the people spoke as one man, each to his neighbor, and they all cried out, saying, "We have no portion in David, nor an inheritance in the son of Jesse! To your tents, Israel, for this man is not to be a ruler nor a leader!" And all the people scattered from Shechem, and each went away to his tent. And Rehoboam exerted himself, and departed and mounted his chariot and entered into Jerusalem; and the whole tribe of Judah and the whole tribe of Benjamin went after him. And it happened at the turn of the year that Rehoboam gathered every man of Judah and Benjamin, and went up to fight against Jeroboam at Shechem. And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah, a man of God: "Speak to Rehoboam king of Judah, and to all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the remnant of the people, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: You shall not go up nor fight against your brothers, the sons of Israel; return, each one to his own house, for this matter has come from Me.'" And they listened to the word of the Lord, and refrained from going, according to the word of the Lord.

**12:25** And Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived in it; and he went out from there and built Penuel.

**12:26** And Jeroboam said in his heart, "Look, now the kingdom will return to the house of David:

**12:27** if this people goes up to offer sacrifice in the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, then the heart of the people will turn back to the Lord and to their lord, to Rehoboam king of Judah, and they will kill me."

**12:28** And the king took counsel, and went and made two golden heifers, and said to the people, "Let it be enough for you to go up to Jerusalem; here are your gods, Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt."

**12:29** And he set the one in Bethel, and the other he put in Dan.

**12:30** And this matter became a sin; and the people went before the one as far as Dan.

**12:31** And he made houses on high places, and he made priests from a part of the people who were not of the sons of Levi.

**12:32** And Jeroboam instituted a festival in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like the festival that was in the land of Judah, and he went up to the altar that he had made in Bethel to sacrifice to the heifers that he had made, and he stationed in Bethel the priests of the high places that he had made.

**12:33** And he went up to the altar that he had made, on the fifteenth day in the eighth month, in the festival that he had fabricated from his own heart, and he instituted a festival for the sons of Israel. And he went up to the altar to offer sacrifice.

## Translator Notes

- **12:10** — "My littleness" translates Greek he mikrotes mou literally (also in 12:24), a euphemistic idiom traditionally understood as "my little finger."
- **12:18** — Greek reads the distinct name Aram (MT Adoram) and omits "into his chariot"; verses 2 and 17 are absent in the LXX (the material of MT 12:2 appears in 11:43).
- **12:20** — The Greek text reads "Rehoboam" here (where MT has "Jeroboam") and adds "and Benjamin" to the tribe following David; in 12:21 Greek reads "one hundred and twenty thousand" (MT 180,000).
- **12:24** — The LXX includes a long alternate account of Rehoboam's accession and Jeroboam's rise (often numbered 12:24a-z), featuring distinct numbers (Rehoboam aged sixteen, reigning twelve years), distinct names (Naanan, Ana, Sareisa, Ano, Thekemina, Enlamite), and Shemaiah performing the twelve-piece garment sign.
- **12:27** — Greek reads "to the Lord and to their lord" (pros Kyrion kai kyrion auton, where MT has only "to their lord"); in 12:28 and 12:32 Greek uses feminine damalis ("heifers") where MT has "calves."
