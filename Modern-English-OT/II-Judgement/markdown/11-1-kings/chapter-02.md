## Text

**2:1** And the days of David drew near for him to die, and he answered Solomon his son, saying,

**2:2** "I am going in the way of all the earth; and you shall be strong and become a man,

**2:3** and you shall keep the charge of your God, to walk in His ways, to keep His commandments and the statutes and the judgments that are written in the law of Moses, so that you will understand what you will do according to all that I may command you,

**2:4** so that the Lord may establish His word which He spoke, saying, 'If your sons keep their way, to walk before Me in truth with all their heart,' saying, 'a man will not be cut off for you from upon the throne of Israel.'

**2:5** And indeed you know all that Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, all that he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner son of Ner and to Amasa son of Jether, and he killed them and put the blood of war on his belt that was on his waist and on his sandal that was on his foot.

**2:6** And you shall act according to your wisdom, and you shall bring down his gray hair in peace to Hades.

**2:7** And to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite you shall show mercy, and they will be among those who eat at your table; for in this way they drew near to me when I was fleeing from the presence of Absalom your brother.

**2:8** And look, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a son of the Jeminite from Bahurim; and he cursed me with a grievous curse on the day when I was going to the camps, and he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'

**2:9** And you shall by no means hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you will do to him; and you shall bring down his gray hair in blood to Hades."

**2:10** And David slept with his fathers, and he was buried in the city of David.

**2:11** And the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years; in Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem thirty-three years.

**2:12** And Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was greatly established.

**2:13** And Adonijah came in to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon and bowed down to her. And she said, "Is your coming in peace?" And he said, "Peace;

**2:14** I have a word for you." And she said to him, "Speak."

**2:15** And he said to her, "You know that the kingdom was mine, and upon me all Israel set its face to be king; and the kingdom turned around and became my brother's, for it became his from the Lord.

**2:16** And now I am asking one request from you; do not turn away your face." And Bathsheba said to him, "Speak."

**2:17** And he said to her, "Please speak to Solomon the king, for he will not turn away his face from you, and he will give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife."

**2:18** And Bathsheba said, "Very well, I will speak about you to the king."

**2:19** And Bathsheba went in to King Solomon to speak to him about Adonijah. And the king rose up to meet her and kissed her, and he sat on the throne; and a throne was set for the mother of the king, and she sat at his right hand.

**2:20** And she said to him, "I am asking one small request from you; do not turn away your face." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, and I will not turn you away."

**2:21** And she said, "Please let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as a wife."

**2:22** And Solomon the king answered and said to his mother, "And why have you asked for Abishag for Adonijah? Ask for him the kingdom also, for he is my brother who is older than me, and he has Abiathar the priest, and he has Joab the son of Zeruiah, the chief commander, as a companion."

**2:23** And King Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, "May God do so to me, and so also may He add, for Adonijah has spoken this word against his own life.

**2:24** And now, as the Lord lives, who established me and set me on the throne of David my father, and He made a house for me just as the Lord spoke, surely today Adonijah will be put to death."

**2:25** And Solomon the king sent by the hand of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he killed him, and Adonijah died on that day.

**2:26** And to Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go off to Anathoth, to your field, for you are a man of death on this day; yet I will not put you to death, because you carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord before my father, and because you were afflicted in all things in which my father was afflicted."

**2:27** And Solomon cast out Abiathar from being a priest of the Lord, so that the word of the Lord might be fulfilled, which He spoke against the house of Eli in Shiloh.

**2:28** And the report came to Joab the son of Zeruiah, for Joab had turned after Adonijah, and he had not turned after Absalom; and Joab fled into the tabernacle of the Lord and took hold of the horns of the altar.

**2:29** And it was reported to Solomon, saying, "Joab has fled into the tent of the Lord, and look, he is holding on to the horns of the altar." And Solomon sent to Joab, saying, "What has happened to you, that you have fled to the altar?" And Joab said, "Because I was afraid of your presence, and I fled to the Lord." And Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go and kill him, and bury him."

**2:30** And Benaiah son of Jehoiada came into the tent of the Lord and said to him, "Thus says the king, 'Come out.'" And Joab said, "I am not going out, for here I will die." And Benaiah son of Jehoiada returned and spoke to the king, saying, "This is what Joab has spoken, and this is what he has answered me."

**2:31** And the king said to him, "Go and do to him just as he has said, and kill him and you shall bury him; and you shall remove today the blood which he shed without cause from me and from the house of my father.

**2:32** And the Lord has returned the blood of his unrighteousness upon his own head; and he fell upon the two men more righteous and better than himself and killed them with the sword, and my father David did not know of their blood: Abner son of Ner, chief commander of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, chief commander of Judah.

**2:33** And his blood has returned upon his own head and upon the head of his offspring forever; and to David and to his offspring and to his house and to his throne may there be peace forever from the Lord."

**2:34** And Jehoiada fell upon Joab and put him to death; and he buried him in his house in the wilderness.

**2:35** And the king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada in his place over the army command; and the kingdom was being set in order in Jerusalem. And Zadok the priest, the king appointed him as first priest in place of Abiathar. And the Lord gave discernment to Solomon, and very great wisdom, and breadth of heart like the sand that is beside the sea. And the discernment of Solomon was multiplied beyond all ancient sons and beyond all the discerning men of Egypt. And he took the daughter of Pharaoh, and he brought her into the city of David until he finished the house of the Lord first and the wall of Jerusalem all around; in seven years he made and finished it. And Solomon had seventy thousand bearing a burden and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain. And Solomon made the sea and the supports and the great basins and the pillars and the fountain of the courtyard and the bronze sea. And he built the citadel, a battlement upon it. He cut through the city of David. Thus the daughter of Pharaoh went up from the city of David to her house which he had built for her. Then he built the citadel. And Solomon offered up three times in the year burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar which he had built to the Lord, and he burned incense before the Lord. And he finished the house. And these were the officials appointed over the works of Solomon: three thousand six hundred overseers of the people who were doing the works. And he built Hazor and Magao and Gezer and Upper Beth-horon and Baalath. However, after he built the house of the Lord and the wall of Jerusalem all around, after these things he built these cities. And while David was still living, he commanded Solomon, saying, "Look, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a son of the offspring of the Jeminite from Hebron. This man cursed me with a grievous curse on the day when I was going to the camps; and he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'He will not be put to death with the sword.' And now do not hold him guiltless, for you are a discerning man, and you will know what you will do to him; and you shall bring down his gray hair in blood to Hades."

**2:36** And the king called Shimei and said to him, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem and stay there, and you shall not go out from there anywhere.

**2:37** And it will be on the day of your going out, when you cross the brook Kidron, you will certainly know that you will certainly die; your blood will be on your head." And the king put him under oath on that day.

**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, "Good is the word that you have spoken, my lord the king; so your servant will do." And Shimei stayed in Jerusalem three years.

**2:39** And it happened after the three years that two servants of Shimei ran away to Achish son of Amesa, king of Gath. And it was reported to Shimei, saying, "Look, your servants are in Gath."

**2:40** And Shimei rose up and saddled his donkey and went to Gath to Achish, to seek out his servants; and Shimei went and brought his servants from Gath.

**2:41** And it was reported to Solomon, saying, "Shimei went from Jerusalem to Gath and brought back his servants."

**2:42** And the king sent and called Shimei and said to him, "Did I not put you under oath by the Lord and solemnly warn you, saying, 'On whatever day you go out from Jerusalem and go to the right or to the left, you will certainly know that you will certainly die'?

**2:43** And why is it that you did not keep the oath of the Lord and the command which I commanded against you?"

**2:44** And the king said to Shimei, "You know all your wickedness which your heart has known, the things that you did to David my father; and the Lord has repaid your wickedness upon your head.

**2:45** And King Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David will be established before the Lord forever."

**2:46** And King Solomon commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and killed him. And King Solomon was exceedingly discerning and wise; and Judah and Israel were very many, like the sand that is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking and rejoicing. And Solomon was ruling over all the kingdoms, and they were bringing gifts and serving Solomon all the days of his life. And Solomon began to open the dominions of Lebanon; and he himself built Thermai in the wilderness. And this was the meal for Solomon: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal, ten choice calves and twenty pasture-fed oxen and one hundred sheep, besides deer and gazelles and choice pasture-fed fowl. Was he not ruling over everything beyond the river from Raphei to Gaza, over all the kings beyond the river? And he had peace on all his sides all around; and Judah and Israel lived confidently, each under his vine and under his fig tree, eating and drinking, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon. And these were the officials of Solomon: Azariah son of Zadok the priest; and Orniou son of Nathan, chief of those in charge, and he ran to his house; and Suba, scribe; and Basa son of Achithalam, recorder; and Abi son of Joab, chief commander; and Achire son of Edrai, over the burdens; and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, over the court administration and over the brickworks; and Kachour son of Nathan, the counselor. And Solomon had forty thousand brood mares for chariots, and twelve thousand horses; and he was ruling over all the kings from the river even to the land of the foreigners and to the borders of Egypt. Solomon son of David reigned over Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The Greek text reads "and you shall bring down" (καὶ σὺ κατάξεις) without the negation found in the Hebrew ("do not let ... go down"), likely reflecting an early uncial confusion between ΟΥ ("not") and ΣΥ ("you").
- **2:34** — The Greek text names "Jehoiada" (Ἰωδᾶε) as Joab's executioner, omitting "Benaiah son of" before his father's name.
- **2:35** — The Septuagint inserts an extensive summary of Solomon's wisdom, building works, and David's charge concerning Shimei not placed here in the Hebrew text. It preserves distinct Greek forms and details, including "Magao" (corresponding to Megiddo), "Upper Beth-horon" (Hebrew "Lower"), and Shimei's origin "from Hebron" rather than Bahurim (cf. 2:8).
- **2:39** — The Greek text names Achish's father "Amesa" (Ἀμησά) rather than Maacah as in the Hebrew text.
- **2:46** — The Septuagint appends a second summary of Solomon's reign, provisions, and officials not placed here in the Hebrew text. Distinct Septuagint proper names are preserved—"Thermai" (Tamar/Tadmor), "Raphei" (Tiphsah), and the official names "Orniou," "Suba," "Basa son of Achithalam," "Abi son of Joab," "Achire son of Edrai," and "Kachour"—along with "the foreigners" (ἀλλοφύλων), the Septuagint's regular term for the Philistines.
