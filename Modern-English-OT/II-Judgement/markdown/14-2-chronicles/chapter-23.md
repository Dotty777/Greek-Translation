## Text

**23:1** And in the eighth year Jehoiada grew strong and took the commanders of hundreds, Azariah son of Joram and Ishmael son of Jehohanan and Azariah son of Obed and Maaseiah son of Aziah and Elishaphan son of Zechariah, with him into a house.

**23:2** And they went around Judah, and he gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah and the leaders of the families of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**23:3** And the entire assembly of Judah made a covenant in the house of God with the king; and he showed them the king's son and said to them, "Look, let the king's son reign, just as the Lord spoke concerning the house of David.

**23:4** Now this is the thing that you shall do: let a third of you, of the priests and of the Levites, come in on the Sabbath, and at the gates of the entrances,

**23:5** and a third in the house of the king, and a third at the middle gate, and all the people in the courts of the house of the Lord.

**23:6** And let no one enter into the house of the Lord except the priests and the Levites and those of the Levites who minister; they shall enter, because they are holy; and let all the people keep the watches for the Lord.

**23:7** And the Levites shall surround the king all around, each man with his every weapon in his hand, and the one who enters into the house shall die; and they shall be with the king as he comes in and goes out."

**23:8** And the Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded; and each one took his men from the beginning of the Sabbath until the close of the Sabbath, because Jehoiada did not dismiss the divisions.

**23:9** And he gave the swords and the shields and the weapons that belonged to King David in the house of God.

**23:10** And he stationed all the people, each one with his weapons, from the right side of the house to the left side of the altar and of the house, all around the king.

**23:11** And he brought out the king's son, and placed upon him the crown and the testimonies; and Jehoiada and his sons made him king and anointed him, and said, "May the king live!"

**23:12** And Athaliah heard the sound of the people running and giving thanks and praising the king, and she came to the king into the house of the Lord.

**23:13** And she looked, and see, the king was at his station, and at the entrance were the leaders and the trumpets, leaders around the king, and all the people rejoiced and blew the trumpets, and those singing with the instruments, singers and those chanting praise; and Athaliah tore her robe and cried out, "You are surely conspiring!"

**23:14** And Jehoiada the priest went out; and Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds and the chiefs of the army and said to them, "Drive her outside the house, and go in after her, and let her die by the sword." For the priest said, "Let her not die in the house of the Lord."

**23:15** And they gave her room, and she passed through the gate of the horsemen of the house of the king, and they put her to death there.

**23:16** And Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and the people and the king, to be a people for the Lord.

**23:17** And all the people of the land entered into the house of Baal, and tore it down, and its altars and its idols, and crushed them fine; and they put Mattan the priest of Baal to death before his altars.

**23:18** And Jehoiada the priest undertook the works of the house of the Lord by the hand of priests and Levites, and he set up the divisions of the priests and of the Levites, which David had assigned over the house of the Lord, and to offer up burnt offerings to the Lord, just as it is written in the law of Moses, with gladness and with songs by the hand of David.

**23:19** And the gatekeepers stood at the gates of the house of the Lord, and no one unclean in any matter shall enter.

**23:20** And he took the patriarchs and the mighty men and the leaders of the people and all the people of the land, and they brought the king up into the house of the Lord, and he entered through the inner gate into the house of the king, and they seated the king upon the throne of the kingdom.

**23:21** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city became quiet; and they put Athaliah to death.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — The Greek text reads "the eighth year" (MT "seventh"), lists "Aziah" and "Elishaphan son of Zechariah" (MT "Adaiah" and "Elishaphat son of Zichri"), and has "into a house" (likely reading Hebrew bbyt instead of bbryt, "into a covenant").
- **23:5** — The Greek text reads "the middle gate," whereas the Hebrew MT has "the Gate of the Foundation."
- **23:13** — Greek epitithemenoi epitithesthe (lit. "conspiring, you conspire") renders Athaliah's outcry as an emphatic accusation of conspiracy (MT "Treason! Treason!").
- **23:14** — The Greek text instructs the commanders to drive Athaliah outside the house, go in after her, and put her to death, whereas the Hebrew MT orders the death of anyone who follows her.
- **23:20** — The Greek text names "the patriarchs" (MT "commanders of hundreds"), says they "brought the king up into the house of the Lord" (MT "down from the house of the Lord"), and specifies "the inner gate" (MT "the upper gate").
