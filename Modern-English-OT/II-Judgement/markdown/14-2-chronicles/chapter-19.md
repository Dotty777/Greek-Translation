## Text

**19:1** And Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house in Jerusalem.

**19:2** And Jehu son of Hanani the prophet went out to meet him and said to him, "King Jehoshaphat, do you help a sinner, or make friends with one hated by the Lord? Because of this, wrath has come upon you from the Lord;

**19:3** yet good things have been found in you, because you removed the groves from the land of Judah and directed your heart to seek the Lord."

**19:4** And Jehoshaphat stayed, and again went out to the people from Beersheba to Mount Ephraim, and turned them back to the Lord, the God of their fathers.

**19:5** And he appointed judges in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.

**19:6** And he said to the judges, "See what you are doing, for you do not judge for man, but for the Lord, and with you are the matters of judgment.

**19:7** And now, let the fear of the Lord be upon you; be careful and act, for there is no injustice with the Lord our God, nor showing partiality, nor taking gifts."

**19:8** And indeed in Jerusalem Jehoshaphat appointed some of the priests and of the Levites and of the patriarchs of Israel for the judgment of the Lord and to judge those who dwell in Jerusalem.

**19:9** And he commanded them, saying, "In this way you are to act in the fear of the Lord, in truth and with a full heart:

**19:10** As for every man, the case that comes before you of our brothers who dwell in the midst of blood, and between ordinance and commandment, and statutes and judgments; you will warn them, and they will not sin against the Lord, and wrath will not be upon you and upon your brothers. In this way you are to act, and you will not sin.

**19:11** And look, Amariah the priest is leader over you for every matter of the Lord, and Zebadiah is the leader over the house of Judah for every matter of the king, and the scribes and the Levites are before you. Be strong and act, and the Lord will be with the good."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Greek reads passive "one hated by the Lord" where MT has active "those who hate the LORD," and places "King Jehoshaphat" as a direct vocative opening Jehu's speech.
- **19:6** — Greek has nominative plural "the matters of judgment" (logoi tes kriseos) as the subject ("with you are the matters of judgment"), whereas MT reads "He is with you in the matter of judgment."
- **19:8** — Greek reads "to judge those who dwell in Jerusalem," reading the Hebrew consonants as "inhabitants" rather than MT's "and they returned to Jerusalem."
- **19:10** — The Greek syntax is compressed and broken, reading "every man" alongside "the case," "our brothers" (MT "your brothers"), and a single "blood" without "in their cities."
