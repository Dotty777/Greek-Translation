## Text

**13:1** In the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam, Abijah became king over Israel.

**13:2** He reigned three years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Maacah, the daughter of Uriel from Gibeon; and there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

**13:3** And Abijah drew up for battle with a force, warriors of valor, four hundred thousand mighty men; and Jeroboam drew up battle against him with eight hundred thousand, mighty warriors of valor.

**13:4** And Abijah rose up from Mount Somoron, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, and said, "Listen, Jeroboam and all Israel!

**13:5** Is it not for you to know that the Lord, the God of Israel, gave a king over Israel forever to David and to his sons by a covenant of salt?

**13:6** And Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, rose up, and rose up away from his master.

**13:7** And pestilent men, lawless sons, gathered to him, and he rose up against Rehoboam the son of Solomon, and Rehoboam was rather young and timid in heart, and he did not withstand him to his face.

**13:8** And now you yourselves speak of withstanding the kingdom of the Lord through the hand of the sons of David; and you are a great multitude, and with you are golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods.

**13:9** Or did you not drive out the priests of the Lord, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and make for yourselves priests from the people of the whole land? Whoever came forward to fill his hands with calves from the cattle and with seven rams became a priest to that which is not God.

**13:10** And we have not abandoned the Lord our God, and His priests, the sons of Aaron, minister to the Lord, and the Levites in their divisions;

**13:11** they offer up in smoke to the Lord a burnt offering morning and evening, and compound incense, and arrangements of bread on the pure table, and the golden lampstand and the lamps for burning to light in the evening; for we ourselves keep the charges of the Lord, the God of our fathers, and you have abandoned Him.

**13:12** And look, with us at the head is the Lord, and His priests, and the signal trumpets to sound the signal over us. Sons of Israel, you will fight against the Lord, the God of our fathers, for it will not go well for you."

**13:13** And Jeroboam sent the ambush around to come behind them; and he was in front of Judah, and the ambush was behind.

**13:14** And Judah turned around, and look, the battle was against them from the front and from behind; and they cried out to the Lord, and the priests sounded the trumpets.

**13:15** And the men of Judah cried out; and it came about, as the men of Judah cried out, that the Lord struck Jeroboam and Israel before Abijah and Judah.

**13:16** And the sons of Israel fled from before Judah, and the Lord delivered them into their hands.

**13:17** And Abijah and his people struck a great blow among them, and five hundred thousand mighty men from Israel fell wounded.

**13:18** And the sons of Israel were humbled on that day, and the sons of Judah grew strong, because they hoped in the Lord, the God of their fathers.

**13:19** And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam and captured cities from him: Bethel and its villages, and Cana and its villages, and Ephron and its villages.

**13:20** And Jeroboam no longer had strength all the days of Abijah, and the Lord struck him, and he died.

**13:21** And Abijah grew strong, and he took for himself fourteen wives, and he fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

**13:22** And the rest of the acts of Abijah, and his deeds, and his words, are written in the book of the prophet Iddo.

**13:23** And Abijah died with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his place. In the days of Asa, the land of Judah was at rest for ten years.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Greek text reads "over Israel" where the Hebrew has "over Judah."
- **13:2** — The Greek reads "Maacah" (matching 11:20, against Heb. Micaiah) and "from Gibeon" (against Heb. Gibeah).
- **13:4** — The Greek names the mountain "Somoron" (a form elsewhere associated with Samaria) where the Hebrew has Zemaraim. In v. 5, the Greek likewise reads "a king" rather than the Hebrew "kingship."
- **13:12** — The Greek text omits the negative "do not" before "you will fight" (present in other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew), and reads "over us" and "our fathers" where the Hebrew has "against you" and "your fathers."
- **13:19** — The Greek reads "Cana" where the Hebrew has Jeshanah.
