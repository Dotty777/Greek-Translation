## Text

**10:1** And Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel was coming to Shechem to make him king.

**10:2** And it happened when Jeroboam the son of Nebat heard of it (and he was in Egypt, as he had fled from the presence of Solomon the king and Jeroboam lived in Egypt), that Jeroboam returned from Egypt.

**10:3** And he sent and called him; and Jeroboam came, and all the assembly came to Rehoboam, saying,

**10:4** "Your father made our yoke harsh; and now ease the harsh service of your father and his heavy yoke which he put upon us, and we will serve you."

**10:5** And he said to them, "Go for three days, and come to me." And the people departed.

**10:6** And King Rehoboam assembled the elders who had stood before Solomon his father while he lived, saying, "How do you advise to give an answer to this people?"

**10:7** And they spoke to him, saying, "If today you are good to this people, and are favorably disposed and speak good words to them, then they will be servants to you all the days."

**10:8** And he abandoned the counsel of the elders who had counseled him, and took counsel with the young men who had been brought up with him, who stood before him.

**10:9** And he said to them, "What do you advise so that I may give an answer to this people who spoke to me, saying, 'Ease the yoke which your father put upon us'?"

**10:10** And the young men who had been brought up with him spoke to him, "Thus you will speak to the people who spoke to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, and you, relieve us,' thus you will say, 'My little finger is thicker than the waist of my father.

**10:11** And now my father disciplined you with a heavy yoke, and I will add to our yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions.'"

**10:12** And Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

**10:13** And the king answered harshly, and King Rehoboam abandoned the counsel of the elders,

**10:14** and spoke to them according to the counsel of the younger men, saying, "My father made our yoke heavy, and I will add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions."

**10:15** And the king did not listen to the people, for there was a turn of events from God, saying, "The Lord established His word which He spoke by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat

**10:16** and all Israel, because the king did not listen to them." And the people answered the king, saying, "What portion is ours in David, and inheritance in the son of Jesse? To your tents, Israel! Now look to your house, David!" And all Israel went to its tents.

**10:17** And as for the men of Israel and those living in the cities of Judah, Jeroboam reigned over them.

**10:18** And Rehoboam sent Adoniram, who was over the tribute, to them, and the sons of Israel stoned him with stones, and he died. And King Jeroboam hurried to mount the chariot to flee to Jerusalem.

**10:19** And Israel broke faith with the house of David until this day.

## Translator Notes

- **10:11** — Greek reads "our yoke" here and in 10:14 (reflecting a Greek pronoun interchange with "your yoke") and uses the verb "disciplined" for the yoke as well as the whips.
- **10:15** — Greek reads "saying" (introducing direct speech) where the Hebrew has "in order that," and continues the clause into 10:16 with the genitive "and all Israel" governed by "concerning."
- **10:17** — Greek reads "Jeroboam" (rather than Hebrew "Rehoboam") as the king who reigned over the men of Israel and those living in the cities of Judah.
- **10:18** — Greek reads "Adoniram" (matching 1 Kings 4:6; Hebrew 2 Chronicles has "Hadoram") and again reads "King Jeroboam" (rather than Hebrew "King Rehoboam") as the king who fled to Jerusalem.
