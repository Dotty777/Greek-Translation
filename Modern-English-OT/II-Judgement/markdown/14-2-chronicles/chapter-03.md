## Text

**3:1** And Solomon began to build the house of the Lord in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the Lord had appeared to David his father, in the place that David had prepared, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**3:2** And building began in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3:3** And these things Solomon began, to build the house of God: the length in cubits, the first measurement, was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**3:4** And the vestibule in front of the house: the length across the front of the width of the house was twenty cubits, and the height one hundred twenty cubits; and he overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.

**3:5** And the great house he paneled with cedar timber, and he overlaid it with pure gold, and he carved on it palm trees and hanging chains.

**3:6** And he adorned the house with precious stones for glory, and with gold of the gold from Parvaim.

**3:7** And he overlaid the house and the walls and the gateways and the ceilings and the doorways with gold, and he carved cherubim on the walls.

**3:8** And he made the house of the Holy of Holies: its length across the front, the width was twenty cubits, and the length was twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with pure gold for cherubim, for six hundred talents.

**3:9** And the weight of the nails: the weight of one was fifty shekels of gold; and he overlaid the upper room with gold.

**3:10** And he made in the house, the Holy of Holies, two cherubim, a work of wood, and he overlaid them with gold.

**3:11** And the wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits in length, and the one wing was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing was five cubits, touching the wing of the other cherub.

**3:13** And the wings of the cherubim were spread out twenty cubits, and they were standing on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

**3:14** And he made the veil of blue and purple and crimson and fine linen, and he wove cherubim into it.

**3:15** And he made in front of the wall two pillars, thirty-five cubits in height, and their capitals were five cubits.

**3:16** And he made chains in the inner sanctuary and put them on the capitals of the pillars, and he made one hundred pomegranates and placed them on the hanging chains.

**3:17** And he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and the one on the left, and he called the name of the one on the right Establishment, and the name of the one on the left Strength.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — Greek reads "for cherubim, for six hundred talents," a doublet arising from reading Hebrew kikkarim ("talents") both as kerubim ("cherubim") and as "talents."
- **3:11** — Verse 3:12 of the Hebrew text, which describes the wings of the second cherub, is absent from the Greek text here.
- **3:15** — Greek reads "in front of the wall" (tou toichou) rather than "in front of the house" (tou oikou), likely through an inner-Greek scribal error.
- **3:16** — Greek retains the transliterated Hebrew architectural terms serseroth ("chains") and dabeir ("inner sanctuary"), just as it transliterates Hebrew ulam ("vestibule") as ailam in 3:4.
- **3:17** — Instead of transliterating the Hebrew pillar names Jachin and Boaz, the Greek translates their meanings as Katorthosis ("Establishment" or "Setting Upright") and Ischys ("Strength").
