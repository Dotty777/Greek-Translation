## Text

**14:1** And he did what was good and right before the Lord his God.

**14:2** And he removed the altars of the foreigners and the high places, and he shattered the pillars and cut down the groves.

**14:3** And he told Judah to seek out the Lord God of their fathers and to do the law and the commandments.

**14:4** And he removed from all the cities of Judah the altars and the idols, and he was at peace.

**14:5** Walled cities were in the land of Judah, because the land was at peace; and he had no war in those years, because the Lord gave him rest.

**14:6** And he said to Judah, "Let us build these cities, and let us make walls and towers and gates and bars; we will have dominion before the land, because just as we sought the Lord our God, He sought us out, and gave us rest all around and granted us success."

**14:7** And Asa had an army of armed men carrying large shields and spears in the land of Judah, three hundred thousand, and in the land of Benjamin, light-shield bearers and archers, two hundred and fifty thousand; all these were mighty warriors.

**14:8** And Zerah the Ethiopian came out against them with an army, with thousands and three hundred chariots, and he came as far as Marisel.

**14:9** And Asa went out to meet him, and drew up battle lines in the valley to the north of Mareshah.

**14:10** And Asa cried out to the Lord his God and said, "Lord, it is not impossible with You to save with many and with few; strengthen us, Lord our God, because we have trusted in You, and in Your name we have come against this great multitude. Lord our God, do not let man prevail against You."

**14:11** And the Lord struck the Ethiopians before Judah, and the Ethiopians fled.

**14:12** And Asa and his people pursued them as far as Gedor; and the Ethiopians fell so that there was no survival among them, because they were shattered before the Lord and before His army; and they plundered much spoil.

**14:13** And they destroyed their villages around Gedor, because terror of the Lord came upon them; and they plundered all its cities, because much spoil belonged to them.

**14:14** And they also destroyed the tents of possessions, the Amazoneis, and they took many sheep and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The Greek text lacks a verb before 'walled cities in the land of Judah' (where the Hebrew and some other witnesses read 'and he built fortified cities'), leaving a verbless clause following verse 4.
- **14:6** — The Greek reads 'we will have dominion before the land' and 'He sought us out,' differing from the Hebrew ('the land is still before us; we have sought him'), and keeps 'and granted us success' inside Asa's speech.
- **14:8** — The Greek gives Zerah's force simply as 'thousands' (rather than a million, as in the Hebrew; compare also 'two hundred and fifty thousand' in v. 7 vs. 280,000 in Hebrew) and spells the destination 'Marisel,' distinct from 'Mareshah' in v. 9 (where the Greek also reads 'to the north of Mareshah' rather than 'Zephathah at Mareshah').
- **14:12** — The Greek reads 'Gedor' (also in v. 13) where the Hebrew text has 'Gerar.'
- **14:14** — The Greek uniquely identifies the object of attack alongside 'the tents of possessions' as the masculine group 'the Amazoneis,' a Septuagint-distinct name not found in the Hebrew text.
