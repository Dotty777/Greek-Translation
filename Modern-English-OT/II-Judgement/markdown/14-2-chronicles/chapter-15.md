## Text

**15:1** And as for Azariah son of Oded, the Spirit of the Lord came upon him.

**15:2** And he went out to meet them in all Judah and Benjamin, and said, "The Lord is with you while we are with Him; and if you seek Him out, He will be found by you; and if you forsake Him, He will forsake you.

**15:3** And there are many days for Israel without a true God and without law.

**15:4** And He will turn them back to the Lord God of Israel, and He will be found by them.

**15:5** And in that time there is no peace for the one coming in and for the one going out, because terror of the Lord is upon all who inhabit the lands.

**15:6** And nation will fight against nation and city against city, because God terrified them in every distress.

**15:7** And you, be strong, and do not let your hands grow weak, because there is a reward for your work."

**15:8** And when he heard these words and the prophecy of Adad the prophet, he grew strong and cast out the abominations from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had seized in Mount Ephraim, and he renewed the altar of the Lord that was in front of the temple of the Lord.

**15:9** And he assembled Judah and Benjamin and the resident aliens who were residing with him from Ephraim and from Manasseh and from Simeon, because many of Israel joined themselves to him when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.

**15:10** And they were gathered to Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

**15:11** And he sacrificed to the Lord on that day from what he had brought: seven hundred calves and seven thousand sheep.

**15:12** And he entered into a covenant to seek the Lord God of their fathers with the whole heart and with the whole soul.

**15:13** And everyone who does not seek out the Lord God of Israel will die, from young to old, from man to woman.

**15:14** And they swore by the Lord with a loud voice and with trumpets.

**15:15** And all Judah rejoiced over the oath, because they swore with all their soul and sought Him with full desire, and He was found by them, and the Lord gave them rest all around.

**15:16** And he removed his mother Maacah from ministering to Astarte, and he cut the idol to pieces and burned it in the brook Kidron.

**15:17** Yet the high places were not removed; they still existed in Israel; but the heart of Asa was whole all his days.

**15:18** And he brought in the holy things of David his father and the holy things of the house of the Lord God: silver and gold and vessels.

**15:19** And there was no war with him until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Greek shifts to the first-person plural 'while we are with Him' and omits the direct address to Asa found in the Hebrew text.
- **15:3** — In the Greek, verses 3-6 omit 'without a teaching priest' and frame Azariah's words in present and future tenses ('He will turn them back... there is no peace... nation will fight') rather than as past history.
- **15:8** — The Greek gives the prophet's name here as 'Adad' (distinct from 'Oded' in v. 1) and reads 'the temple of the Lord' where the Hebrew has 'the vestibule of the Lord.'
- **15:16** — Rather than removing Maacah from being queen mother for making an Asherah image (as in the Hebrew), the Greek reads that he removed her 'from ministering to Astarte,' using the distinct Greek divine name Astarte.
- **15:18** — The Greek explicitly names 'David' as his father and reads 'the holy things of the house of the Lord God' (rather than 'his own dedicated things into the house of God' as in the Hebrew).
