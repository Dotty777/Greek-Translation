## Text

**26:1** And all the people of the land took Ahaziah, and he was sixteen years old, and they made him king in place of his father Amaziah.

**26:2** He built Elath; he restored it to Judah after the king slept with his fathers.

**26:3** Uzziah was sixteen years old, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Chaaia from Jerusalem.

**26:4** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.

**26:5** And he kept seeking the Lord in all the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the fear of the Lord; and in his days he sought the Lord, and the Lord prospered him.

**26:6** And he went out and waged war against the foreigners, and tore down the walls of Gath and the walls of Abenner and the walls of Ashdod, and built cities of Ashdod and among the foreigners.

**26:7** And the Lord strengthened him against the foreigners, and against the Arabs who dwell upon the rock, and against the Minaeans.

**26:8** And the Minaeans gave gifts to Uzziah, and his name reached as far as the entrance of Egypt, because he grew strong to the highest degree.

**26:9** And Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem, both at the Corner Gate and at the gate of the corner of the valley and at the corners, and he grew strong.

**26:10** And he built towers in the wilderness, and hewed out many cisterns, because he had much livestock in the Shephelah and in the plain, and vinedressers in the hill country and in Carmel, because he was a farmer.

**26:11** And Uzziah had forces that waged war and went out to battle array by number; and their number was by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Amasaiah the judge, by the hand of Hananiah, the deputy of the king.

**26:12** The whole number of the patriarchs of those mighty for war was two thousand six hundred,

**26:13** and with them a fighting force of three hundred seven thousand five hundred; these were the ones who waged war with mighty strength to help the king against the adversaries.

**26:14** And Uzziah prepared them for the whole army: shields and spears and helmets and breastplates and bows and slings for stones.

**26:15** And he made in Jerusalem machines devised by a designer, to be on the towers and on the corners, to shoot arrows and large stones; and their construction was heard of far away, because he was marvelously helped until he grew strong.

**26:16** And when he grew strong, his heart was lifted up to bring ruin; and he did wrong against the Lord his God, and entered the temple of the Lord to burn incense on the altar of incense.

**26:17** And Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him eighty priests of the Lord, mighty men.

**26:18** And they stood against Uzziah the king and said to him, "It is not for you, Uzziah, to offer sacrifice to the Lord, but rather for the priests, the sons of Aaron, who have been consecrated to offer sacrifice. Go out from the sanctuary, because you have turned away from the Lord; and this will not be for glory to you from the Lord God."

**26:19** And Uzziah became angry, and in his hand was the censer to burn incense in the temple; and while he was angry with the priests, leprosy sprang up on his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord and above the altar of incense.

**26:20** And the chief priest and the priests turned toward him, and look, he was leprous on his forehead; and they hurried him out from there, and indeed he himself hurried to go out, because the Lord had rebuked him.

**26:21** And King Uzziah was a leper until the day of his death, and he lived as a leper in a house of apphousion, because he was cut off from the house of the Lord; and Jotham his son was over his kingdom, judging the people of the land.

**26:22** And the rest of the acts of Uzziah, the first and the last, are written by Jesseiah the prophet.

**26:23** And Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial of the kings, because they said, "He is a leper." And Jotham his son became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek text reads Ochozeias ("Ahaziah") here before referring to the king as Ozeias ("Uzziah") in verse 3 and throughout the rest of the chapter.
- **26:3** — "Chaaia" renders Greek Chaaia, differing from Hebrew Jecoliah.
- **26:6** — "Foreigners" translates Greek allophyloi, the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines. "Abenner" renders Greek Abenner, where Hebrew has Jabneh.
- **26:11** — "Amasaiah" renders Greek Amasaiou (where Hebrew has Maaseiah), and "deputy" reflects Hellenistic court usage of Greek diadochos (literally "successor").
- **26:21** — "Apphousion" is an untranslated Greek transliteration of the Hebrew term for a separate or isolated house. Greek also reads "over his kingdom" where Hebrew has "over the king's house."
- **26:22** — "Jesseiah" renders Greek Iesseiou, a distinct form corresponding to Hebrew "Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz" (with "son of Amoz" absent in Greek).
