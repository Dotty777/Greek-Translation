## Text

**8:1** And it happened after twenty years, in which Solomon built the house of the Lord and his own house,

**8:2** that Solomon built the cities that Hiram had given to Solomon, and he settled the sons of Israel there.

**8:3** And Solomon went to Baisoba and overpowered it.

**8:4** And he built Tadmor in the wilderness, and he built all the fortified cities in Hamath.

**8:5** And he built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars,

**8:6** and Baalah, and all the fortified cities that belonged to Solomon, and all the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen, and everything that Solomon desired according to his desire to build in Jerusalem and in Lebanon and in all his kingdom.

**8:7** All the people who were left from the Hittites and the Amorites and the Perizzites and the Hivites and the Jebusites, who are not from Israel,

**8:8** were from their sons who were left with him in the land, whom the sons of Israel destroyed; and Solomon levied them for tribute until this day.

**8:9** And from the sons of Israel Solomon did not appoint any as servants for his kingdom, for look, they were fighting men and commanders, and the mighty men, and commanders of chariots and horsemen.

**8:10** And these were the commanders of the overseers of King Solomon, two hundred and fifty acting as taskmasters among the people.

**8:11** And Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to the house which he had built for her, for he said, "My wife will not live in the city of David, the king of Israel, because it is holy where the ark of the Lord entered."

**8:12** Then Solomon offered up burnt offerings to the Lord on the altar which he had built in front of the temple,

**8:13** and according to the requirement of each day, to offer up according to the commands of Moses on the Sabbaths and on the new moons and at the feasts, three times in the year: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, at the Feast of Tabernacles.

**8:14** And he established according to the ordinance of David the divisions of the priests according to their ministries; and the Levites were at their posts to minister and to praise before the priests according to the requirement of each day; and the gatekeepers according to their divisions at each gate, for such were the commands of David, the man of God.

**8:15** They did not disregard the commands of the king concerning the priests and the Levites regarding any matter and regarding the treasuries.

**8:16** And all the work was made ready from the day it was founded until Solomon completed the house of the Lord.

**8:17** Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Elam by the sea in the land of Idumea.

**8:18** And Hiram sent by the hand of his servants ships and servants who knew the sea, and he went with the servants of Solomon to Sophira, and took from there the four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and they came to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — Greek reads "Baisoba," a Septuagint place name corresponding to Hebrew "Hamath-zobah."
- **8:4** — Greek reads "fortified cities" here and in 8:6 where the Hebrew has "storage cities."
- **8:8** — Greek reads "with him" (rather than Hebrew "after them") and lacks a negative before "destroyed," reading "whom the sons of Israel destroyed" where the Hebrew reads "whom the sons of Israel had not destroyed."
- **8:17** — Greek reads "Elam" (Ailam), corresponding to Hebrew "Eloth" (Elath), and uses "Idumea," the Greek form for Edom.
- **8:18** — Greek reads "Sophira" (and "Suphir" in 9:10), the Septuagint forms corresponding to Hebrew "Ophir," and uses singular verbs ("went... took") before shifting to plural ("they came").
