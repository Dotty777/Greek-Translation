## Text

**34:1** Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.

**34:2** And he did what was right before the Lord, and walked in the ways of David his father, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**34:3** And in the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a youth, he began to seek the Lord, the God of David his father; and in the twelfth year of his reign he began to cleanse Judah and Jerusalem from the high places and the groves, and from the cast images.

**34:4** And he tore down the altars of the Baals that were before him and the high places that were above them, and he cut down the groves and the carved images, and he broke in pieces and ground fine the cast images and threw them upon the surface of the tombs of those who sacrificed to them.

**34:5** And he burned the bones of priests upon the altars, and cleansed Judah and Jerusalem,

**34:6** and in the cities of Ephraim and Manasseh and Simeon and Naphtali, and in their places all around.

**34:7** And he tore down the groves and the altars, and chopped the idols into fine pieces, and cut down all the high places from all the land of Israel, and returned to Jerusalem.

**34:8** And in the eighteenth year of his reign, to cleanse the land and the house, he sent Shaphan son of Selia, and Maasa the ruler of the city, and Juach son of Joah his recorder, to strengthen the house of the Lord his God.

**34:9** And they came to Hilkiah the high priest and gave the silver that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites guarding the gate had collected from the hand of Manasseh and Ephraim and the rulers, and from all the remnant in Israel, and from the sons of Judah and Benjamin, and from those living in Jerusalem.

**34:10** And they gave it into the hand of those doing the work who were appointed in the house of the Lord, and they gave it to those doing the work who were working in the house of the Lord, to repair and strengthen the house.

**34:11** And they gave it to the carpenters and to the builders, to buy squared stones and timber for beams, to roof the houses that the kings of Judah had destroyed.

**34:12** And the men were faithful over the work, and over them as overseers were Ie and Obadiah, the Levites from the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam from the sons of Kohath, to oversee, and every Levite, everyone skilled with instruments of song.

**34:13** And they were over the burden-bearers and over all those doing the work, task by task; and from the Levites there were scribes and judges and gatekeepers.

**34:14** And while they were bringing out the silver that had been brought into the house of the Lord, Hilkiah the priest found a book of the law of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**34:15** And Hilkiah answered and said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found a book of the law in the house of the Lord." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

**34:16** And Shaphan brought the book to the king, and further brought back word to the king: "All the silver that was given is in the hand of your servants who are doing the work.

**34:17** And they melted down the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and he gave it into the hand of the overseers who do the work."

**34:18** And Shaphan the scribe reported to the king, saying, "A book that Hilkiah the priest gave me." And Shaphan read it before the king.

**34:19** And it happened, when the king heard the law, that he tore his garments.

**34:20** And the king commanded Hilkiah, and Ahikam son of Shaphan, and Abdodom son of Micah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Isaiah the servant of the king, saying,

**34:21** "Go, inquire of the Lord concerning me and concerning all that is left in Israel and Judah, concerning the words of the book that has been found; for great is the wrath of the Lord that has been kindled among us, because our fathers did not listen to the words of the Lord to do according to all that is written in this book."

**34:22** And Hilkiah and those to whom the king had spoken went to Huldah the prophetess, wife of Shallum son of Kathual son of Chelles, who kept the commandments; and she lived in Jerusalem in Maasanai. And they spoke to her according to these things.

**34:23** And she said to them, "Thus said the Lord, the God of Israel: Say to the man who sent you to Me,

**34:24** 'Thus says the Lord: Look, I am bringing evils upon this place, all the words that are written in the book that has been read before the king of Judah,

**34:25** because they forsook Me and burned incense to foreign gods, so that they might provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands; and My wrath has been kindled in this place and will not be quenched.'

**34:26** And to the king of Judah who sent you to inquire of the Lord, thus you shall say to him: 'Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: As for the words that you heard,

**34:27** and your heart was contrite, and you humbled yourself before My face when you heard My words against this place and against those who live in it, and you humbled yourself before Me and tore your garments and wept in My presence, and I also have heard, says the Lord.

**34:28** Look, I am adding you to your fathers, and you will be added to your tombs in peace, and your eyes will not look upon all the evils that I am bringing upon this place and upon those who live in it.'" And they brought back word to the king.

**34:29** And the king sent and gathered the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**34:30** And the king went up to the house of the Lord, and all Judah and those living in Jerusalem, and the priests and the Levites and all the people, from great to small; and he read in their hearing all the words of the book of the covenant that were found in the house of the Lord.

**34:31** And the king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk before the Lord, to keep His commandments and testimonies and His statutes with a whole heart and with a whole soul, the words of the covenant that are written in this book.

**34:32** And he caused all who were found in Jerusalem and Benjamin to stand; and those living in Jerusalem made a covenant in the house of the Lord, the God of their father.

**34:33** And Josiah removed all the abominations from all the land that belonged to the sons of Israel, and made all who were found in Jerusalem and in Israel serve the Lord their God all his days; he did not turn aside from following the Lord, the God of his fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **34:8** — The Greek names Selia, Maasa, and Juach son of Joah differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's Azaliah, Maaseiah, and Joah son of Joahaz.
- **34:12** — The Greek reads the proper name Ie where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Jahath.
- **34:17** — The Greek verb literally means "melted down" (corresponding to a Hebrew verb meaning "poured out" or "emptied out") and shifts to the singular "he gave it" where the Hebrew has plural "they gave it".
- **34:20** — The Greek names Abdodom and Isaiah correspond to Abdon and Asaiah in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **34:22** — The Greek gives Shallum's ancestors as Kathual and Chelles (Hebrew: Tokhath and Hasrah), describes Huldah as "who kept the commandments" (Hebrew: Shallum, "keeper of the wardrobe"), and transliterates Hebrew "the Second Quarter" as a place name, Maasanai.
