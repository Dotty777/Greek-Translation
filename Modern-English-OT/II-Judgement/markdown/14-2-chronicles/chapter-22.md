## Text

**22:1** And those living in Jerusalem made Ahaziah, his youngest son, king in his place, for the raiding band that had come upon them, the Arabs, the Alimazonites, had killed all the older ones; and Ahaziah son of Joram king of Judah became king.

**22:2** Ahaziah was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Athaliah, daughter of Omri.

**22:3** He also walked in the way of the house of Ahab, for his mother was a counselor to sin.

**22:4** And he did what was evil before the Lord like the house of Ahab, for they were counselors to him after his father died, to utterly destroy him,

**22:5** and he walked in their counsels. And he went with Joram son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of Syria to Ramoth-gilead; and the archers struck Joram.

**22:6** And Joram returned to Israel to be healed from the wounds with which the Syrians had struck him in Ramah when he fought against Hazael king of Syria; and Ahaziah son of Joram, king of Judah, went down to Israel to see Joram son of Ahab, because he was sick.

**22:7** And the downfall of Ahaziah came from God in coming to Joram; and when he went out, Joram went out with him to the son of Nimshi, the anointed of the Lord, the house of Ahab.

**22:8** And it came about when Jehu took vengeance on the house of Ahab, that they found the officials of Judah and the brothers of Ahaziah serving Ahaziah, and he killed them.

**22:9** And he commanded to seek Ahaziah; and they captured him while he was being healed in Samaria and brought him to Jehu, and he killed him. And they buried him, for they said, "He is a son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the Lord with all his heart." And there was no one in the house of Ahaziah to hold power concerning the kingdom.

**22:10** And Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, and she arose and destroyed all the seed of the kingdom in the house of Judah.

**22:11** And Jehosheba took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him from the midst of the king's sons who were being put to death, and put him and his nurse into a chamber of beds; and Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah and wife of Jehoiada, hid him; and she hid him from before Athaliah, and she did not kill him.

**22:12** And he was hidden with him in the house of God for six years, and Athaliah reigned over the land.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — The Greek text adds "the Alimazonites" (hoi Aleimazoneis) after "the Arabs," which does not appear in the Hebrew text.
- **22:2** — The Greek text reads "twenty years old," whereas the Hebrew text reads "forty-two years old" (compare "twenty-two" in 2 Kings 8:26).
- **22:5** — The Greek text reads "the archers" (hoi toxotai), whereas the Hebrew text reads "the Syrians" (Arameans).
- **22:6** — The Greek text twice reads "Israel," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Jezreel."
- **22:7** — The Greek text omits the name "Jehu" before "the son of Nimshi" and omits a verb (such as "to cut off" in the Hebrew text) before the closing phrase "the house of Ahab."
- **22:9** — The Greek text reads "while he was being healed" (iatreuomenon), whereas the Hebrew text reads "while he was hiding himself."
