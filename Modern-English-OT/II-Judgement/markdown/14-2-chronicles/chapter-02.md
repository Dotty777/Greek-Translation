## Text

**2:1** And Solomon gathered seventy thousand men and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain, and the overseers over them were three thousand six hundred.

**2:2** And Solomon sent to Hiram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with my father David and sent him cedars to build a house for himself to live in,

**2:3** now look, I, his son, am building a house for the name of the Lord my God, to consecrate it to Him, to burn incense before Him, and for the continual presentation, and to offer burnt offerings continually in the morning and in the evening, and on the Sabbaths and on the new moons and on the festivals of the Lord our God; this is forever upon Israel.

**2:4** And the house that I am building is great, for our God is great above all the gods.

**2:5** And who will be able to build a house for Him? For heaven and the heaven of heaven do not bear His glory; and who am I, building a house for Him, except to burn incense before Him?

**2:6** And now send me a wise man who knows how to work in gold and in silver, in bronze and in iron, and in purple and in crimson, and who understands how to engrave an engraving, with the wise men who are with me in Judah and in Jerusalem, which David my father prepared.

**2:7** And send me cedar, juniper, and pine timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut timber from Lebanon; and look, your servants with my servants

**2:8** will go to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house that I am building is great and glorious.

**2:9** And look, to the workers, those who cut timber, for food I have given wheat as gifts to your servants, twenty thousand kors, and twenty thousand kors of barley, and twenty thousand measures of wine."

**2:10** And Hiram king of Tyre spoke in writing and sent to Solomon, "Because the Lord loved His people, He gave you as king over them."

**2:11** And Hiram said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who gave David a wise son, versed in understanding and knowledge, who will build a house for the Lord and a house for his kingdom.

**2:12** And now I have sent you a wise man who knows understanding, Hiram my father.

**2:13** His mother is from the daughters of Dan, and his father is a Tyrian man; he knows how to work in gold and in silver and in bronze and in iron, in stones and timber, and to weave in purple and in blue and in fine linen and in crimson, and to engrave engravings, and to devise every design, whatever you may give him, with your wise men and the wise men of David my lord, your father.

**2:14** And now, the wheat and the barley and the oil and the wine that my lord spoke of, let him send to his servants.

**2:15** And we will cut timber from Lebanon according to all your need, and we will bring it on rafts to the sea of Joppa, and you will bring it to Jerusalem."

**2:16** And Solomon gathered all the men who were resident aliens in the land of Israel after the count with which David his father had counted them, and there were found one hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred.

**2:17** And he made from them seventy thousand burden-bearers, and eighty thousand stonecutters, and three thousand six hundred over the people.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — "Continual presentation" (prothesin dia pantos) refers to the regular setting out of the bread of the Presence before the Lord.
- **2:5** — Greek reads "do not bear His glory," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "cannot contain Him."
- **2:9** — Greek renders Hebrew "crushed [wheat]" doubly as "for food" and "as gifts," and omits the twenty thousand measures of oil present in the Hebrew text (though oil is mentioned in 2:14).
- **2:12** — Greek renders the Hebrew title Huram-abi literally as "Hiram my father," where "father" functions as an honorific for a master craftsman.
