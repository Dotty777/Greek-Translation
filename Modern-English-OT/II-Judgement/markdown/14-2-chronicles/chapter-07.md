## Text

**7:1** And when Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the whole burnt offerings and the sacrifices, and the glory of the Lord filled the house.

**7:2** And the priests were not able to enter the house of the Lord at that time, because the glory of the Lord filled the house.

**7:3** And all the sons of Israel were watching the fire coming down, and the glory of the Lord was upon the house; and they fell facedown to the ground on the stone pavement, and worshiped and were praising the Lord, "For it is good, for His mercy is forever."

**7:4** And the king and all the people were offering sacrifices before the Lord.

**7:5** And Solomon offered the sacrifice, twenty-two thousand calves, and the king dedicated the house of God; and all the people

**7:6** and the priests were standing at their posts, and the Levites with instruments of the songs of the Lord belonging to King David, to give thanks before the Lord, for His mercy is forever, with the hymns of David by their hand; and the priests were blowing the trumpets before them, and all Israel was standing.

**7:7** And Solomon consecrated the middle of the courtyard that was in the house of the Lord; for he offered there the whole burnt offerings and the fat portions of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar that Solomon had made was not sufficient to receive the whole burnt offerings and the grain offerings and the fat portions.

**7:8** And Solomon held the festival at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly, from the entrance of Hamath to the brook of Egypt.

**7:9** And on the eighth day he held a closing assembly, for he held the dedication of the altar for seven days as a festival,

**7:10** and on the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people away to their tents, rejoicing and with a glad heart for the good things that the Lord had done for David and for Solomon and for Israel His people.

**7:11** And Solomon finished the house of the Lord and the house of the king, and everything that Solomon formed in his soul to do in the house of the Lord and in his own house succeeded.

**7:12** And God appeared to Solomon by night and said to him, "I have heard your prayer, and I have chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice.

**7:13** If I shut up heaven and there is no rain, and if I command the locust to devour the trees, and if I send death among My people,

**7:14** and if My people, upon whom My name has been called, are humbled and pray and seek My face, and turn away from their evil ways, then I will listen from heaven and pardon their sins and heal their land.

**7:15** Now My eyes will be open and My ears attentive to the prayer of this place.

**7:16** And now I have chosen and consecrated this house for My name to be there forever, and My eyes and My heart will be there all the days.

**7:17** And as for you, if you walk before Me like David your father, and act according to everything that I commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**7:18** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom as I covenanted with David your father, saying, 'A man leading in Israel will not be removed from you.'

**7:19** But if you turn away, and forsake My statutes and My commandments that I set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them,

**7:20** then I will remove you from the land that I gave to them, and this house that I consecrated for My name I will turn away from My presence, and I will make it a proverb and a tale among all the nations.

**7:21** And as for this lofty house, everyone who passes by it will be astonished and will say, 'For what reason has the Lord done this to this land and to this house?'

**7:22** And they will say, 'Because they forsook the Lord, the God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods and worshiped them and served them; for this reason He brought all this calamity upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek uses the neuter adjective agathon ("For it is good") rather than the masculine "He is good."
- **7:5** — The Greek mentions only twenty-two thousand calves (lacking the 120,000 sheep of the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and links "and all the people" at the end of 7:5 directly into the standing clause of 7:6.
- **7:7** — "Grain offerings" translates ta manaa, the Greek transliteration of the Hebrew cultic term minhah.
- **7:9** — The Greek uses singular "he held" and describes a single seven-day festival for the dedication of the altar, without the additional seven-day period found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:13** — The Greek reads to xylon (lit. "the tree/wood," i.e., "the trees") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the land," and uses thanatos ("death," as in 6:28) idiomatically for deadly pestilence.
