## Text

**29:1** And Hezekiah became king when he was twenty-five years old, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Abba, the daughter of Zechariah.

**29:2** And he did what was right before the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.

**29:3** And it happened, when he stood over his kingdom, in the first month he opened the doors of the house of the Lord and repaired them.

**29:4** And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and stationed them on the side toward the east,

**29:5** and said to them, "Listen, Levites! Now purify yourselves, and purify the house of the Lord, the God of your fathers, and cast out the uncleanness from the sanctuary.

**29:6** For our fathers turned away and did what was evil before the Lord and forsook Him, and they turned their face away from the tabernacle of the Lord and gave the back of the neck.

**29:7** They also shut the doors of the temple, and put out the lamps, and did not burn incense, and did not offer burnt offerings in the holy place to the God of Israel.

**29:8** And the Lord was angry with wrath against Judah and Jerusalem, and He gave them over to astonishment and to hissing, as you yourselves see with your eyes.

**29:9** And look, your fathers have been struck down by the sword, and your sons and your daughters and your wives are in captivity in a land not their own, which is so even now.

**29:10** Because of these things, it is now upon the heart to make my covenant, a covenant of the Lord, the God of Israel; and He has turned away the wrath of His fury from us.

**29:11** And now do not cease, for He has chosen you to stand before Him to minister, and to be ministering and burning incense to Him."

**29:12** And the Levites stood up: Mahath the son of Masi and Joel the son of Zechariah from the sons of Kohath; and from the sons of Merari, Kish the son of Abdi and Zechariah the son of Helle; and from the sons of Gershon, from Zimmah and Jodan, these were sons of Joah;

**29:13** and of the sons of Elizaphan, both Zimri and Jeiel; and of the sons of Asa, Azariah and Mattaniah;

**29:14** and of the sons of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and of the sons of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.

**29:15** And he gathered their brothers, and they purified themselves according to the command of the king by the decree of the Lord.

**29:16** And the priests went all the way into the house of the Lord to purify it, and they cast out all the uncleanness that was found in the house of the Lord, even into the courtyard of the house of the Lord; and the Levites received it to cast it out into the brook Kidron outside.

**29:17** And they began to purify on the third day, the new moon of the first month, and on the eighth day of the month they entered into the temple of the Lord, and they purified the house of the Lord in eight days, and on the thirteenth day of the first month they finished.

**29:18** And they went inside to Hezekiah the king and said, "We have purified all the things in the house of the Lord, the altar of the burnt offering and its vessels, and the table of the presentation and its vessels,

**29:19** and all the vessels that Ahaz the king defiled in his reign in his rebellion, we have prepared and purified; look, they are before the altar of the Lord."

**29:20** And Hezekiah the king rose early and gathered the leaders of the city, and went up to the house of the Lord.

**29:21** And he brought up seven young bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom and for the sanctuary and for Israel; and he told the sons of Aaron, the priests, to go up onto the altar of the Lord.

**29:22** And they slaughtered the young bulls, and the priests received the blood and poured it out upon the altar; and they slaughtered the rams, and poured the blood out upon the altar.

**29:23** And they brought forward the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and they laid their hands on them.

**29:24** And the priests slaughtered them, and made atonement with their blood at the altar, and made atonement for all Israel; for, "For all Israel," the king said, "are the burnt offering and the sin offerings."

**29:25** And he stationed the Levites in the house of the Lord with cymbals and with harps and with lyres, according to the command of David the king, and of Gad the prophet, and of Nathan the prophet; for the decree was by the command of the Lord through the hand of the prophets.

**29:26** And the Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.

**29:27** And Hezekiah gave word to offer the burnt offering upon the altar. And when they began to offer the burnt offering, they began to sing to the Lord, along with trumpets to the accompaniment of the instruments of David the king.

**29:28** And the whole assembly was worshiping, and the singers were singing, and the trumpets were sounding, until the burnt offering was finished.

**29:29** And when they finished offering, the king and all who were present bowed down and worshiped.

**29:30** And Hezekiah the king and the leaders told the Levites to sing praise to the Lord in the words of David and of Asaph the prophet. And they sang praise with gladness, and they fell down and worshiped.

**29:31** And Hezekiah answered and said, "Now you have filled your hands to the Lord; come near and bring sacrifices and offerings of praise to the house of the Lord." And the assembly brought sacrifices and offerings of praise to the house of the Lord, and everyone willing in heart, burnt offerings.

**29:32** And the number of the burnt offering that the assembly brought was seventy young bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs; all these were for a burnt offering to the Lord.

**29:33** And the consecrated young bulls were six hundred, and sheep, three thousand five hundred.

**29:34** However, the priests were few and were not able to skin the burnt offering; so their brothers the Levites helped them until the work was finished and until the priests had purified themselves, because the Levites purified themselves more eagerly than the priests.

**29:35** And the burnt offering was abundant, with the fat pieces of the completion of the peace offering and the drink offerings of the burnt offering. And the work in the house of the Lord was set in order.

**29:36** And Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced because God had made preparation for the people, for the matter came about suddenly.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — The Greek gives the king's mother's name as Abba (compared with Abijah in the Hebrew text and Abi in 2 Kings 18:2).
- **29:10** — The Greek attaches "my" to "covenant" rather than "heart" and uses a past indicative verb ("He has turned away") rather than a wish or purpose clause.
- **29:12** — The Greek Levite list here and in verses 13-14 differs in several names and relationships from the Hebrew text, including Masi (Amasai), Zechariah twice (Azariah), Helle (Jehallelel), Jodan (Eden), Zimri (Shimri), Asa (Asaph), and Azariah (Zechariah).
- **29:17** — The Greek dates the start of the purification to "the third day, the new moon of the first month" and its completion to "the thirteenth day" (compared with the first and sixteenth days in the Hebrew text).
- **29:35** — "The completion of the peace offering" reflects a Greek doublet (tes teleioseos tou soteriou) translating the Hebrew term for peace offerings (shelamim).
