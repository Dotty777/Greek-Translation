## Text

**17:1** And Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place, and Jehoshaphat grew strong against Israel.

**17:2** And he placed a force in all the fortified cities of Judah, and appointed leaders in all the cities of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had previously captured.

**17:3** And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the first ways of his father, and he did not seek the idols,

**17:4** but he sought the Lord, the God of his father, and walked in the commandments of his father, and not like the deeds of Israel.

**17:5** And the Lord established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat, and he had wealth and much glory.

**17:6** And his heart was lifted up in the way of the Lord, and he removed the high places and the groves from the land of Judah.

**17:7** And in the third year of his reign he sent his leaders and the sons of the mighty men, Abijah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah, to teach in the cities of Judah.

**17:8** And with them were the Levites: Samuas, Manthanias, Zebadiah, Jasiel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonijah, and Tobadobiah, Levites; those with them were Elishama and Joram, the priests.

**17:9** And they taught in Judah, and with them was the book of the law of the Lord; and they went through the cities of Judah and taught the people.

**17:10** And a dread of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the land around Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.

**17:11** And some of the foreigners brought gifts and silver and presents to Jehoshaphat, and the Arabs brought him seven thousand seven hundred rams of sheep.

**17:12** And Jehoshaphat kept growing greater, even to great height, and he built dwellings in Judea and fortified cities.

**17:13** And there were many works in Judea, and men of war, mighty and strong, were in Jerusalem.

**17:14** And this is their number according to their fathers' houses; and for Judah, commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand sons mighty in strength;

**17:15** and after him Johanan the leader, and with him two hundred eighty thousand;

**17:16** and after him Masaias son of Zari, who volunteered for the Lord, and with him two hundred thousand mighty in strength.

**17:17** And from Benjamin, a mighty man of strength, even Eliada, and with him two hundred thousand archers and shield-bearers;

**17:18** and after him Jehozabad, and with him one hundred eighty thousand mighty in war.

**17:19** These were those who served the king, besides those whom the king placed in the fortified cities in all Judea.

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — Greek reads simply "his father" (i.e., Asa), omitting "David," and renders Hebrew "the Baals" as "the idols"; in v. 4 Greek similarly reads "the commandments of his father" where MT has "His commandments."
- **17:7** — Greek translates the Hebrew proper name Ben-hail literally as "the sons of the mighty men" and reads "Abijah" (Abias) where MT has Obadiah.
- **17:8** — Several names differ from MT: Greek has Samuas (MT Shemaiah), Manthanias (MT Nethaniah), Jasiel (MT Asahel), and a single name Tobadobiah (MT Tobijah and Tob-adonijah).
- **17:11** — Greek "foreigners" (allophyloi) is the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines; Greek also lists only seven thousand seven hundred rams of sheep, omitting the seven thousand seven hundred male goats found in MT.
- **17:16** — Greek reads "Masaias son of Zari" where MT has Amasiah son of Zichri.
