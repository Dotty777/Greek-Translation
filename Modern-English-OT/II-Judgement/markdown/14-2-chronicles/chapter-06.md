## Text

**6:1** Then Solomon said, "The Lord said that He would dwell in thick darkness.

**6:2** And I have built a house for Your name, holy to You and prepared for You to dwell in forever."

**6:3** Then the king turned his face and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel was standing by.

**6:4** And he said, "Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to David my father and fulfilled it with His hands, saying,

**6:5** 'From the day that I brought My people up from the land of Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a house for My name to be there, and I did not choose a man to be leader over My people Israel;

**6:6** but I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**6:7** And it was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the Lord, the God of Israel.

**6:8** And the Lord said to David my father, 'Because it was in your heart to build a house for My name, it was well in your heart;

**6:9** nevertheless, you will not build the house, for your son who will come out from your loins, he will build the house for My name.'

**6:10** And the Lord established this word that He spoke, and I came to be in place of David my father and sat on the throne of Israel just as the Lord spoke, and I built the house for the name of the Lord, the God of Israel,

**6:11** and I placed the ark there, in which is the covenant of the Lord that He made with Israel."

**6:12** Then he stood in front of the altar of the Lord before the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands;

**6:13** for Solomon had made a bronze platform and placed it in the middle of the courtyard of the sanctuary, five cubits was its length, five cubits its width, and three cubits its height; and he stood on it, and fell on his knees before the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven

**6:14** and said, "Lord, the God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven and on the earth, keeping the covenant and the mercy for Your servants who walk before You with a whole heart.

**6:15** You kept for Your servant David my father what You spoke in telling him, and You spoke with Your mouth and with Your hands fulfilled it, as it is this day.

**6:16** And now, Lord, the God of Israel, keep for Your servant David what You spoke to him, saying, 'A man will not fail you from the presence, sitting on the throne of Israel, if only your sons keep their way to walk in My name as you walked before Me.'

**6:17** And now, Lord, the God of Israel, let Your word indeed be confirmed that You spoke to Your servant David.

**6:18** For will God truly dwell with people on the earth? If heaven and the heaven of heaven will not suffice for You, then what is this house that I have built?

**6:19** Yet You will look upon the prayer of Your servant and upon my supplication, Lord God, to heed the supplication and the prayer before You today,

**6:20** that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward this place of which You said that Your name would be called upon there, to hear the prayer that Your servant prays toward this place.

**6:21** And You will hear the supplication of Your servant and of the people of Israel, whatever they pray toward this place; and You will listen in the place of Your dwelling from heaven, and You will hear and pardon.

**6:22** If a man sins against his neighbor and lays a curse on him to put him under a curse, and he comes and invokes the curse in front of the altar in this house,

**6:23** then You will listen from heaven and act, and judge Your servants, to repay the lawless one and return his ways upon his head, to justify the righteous one, to repay him according to his righteousness.

**6:24** And if Your people Israel are defeated in front of the enemy if they sin against You, and they turn back and confess to Your name and pray and make supplication before You in this house,

**6:25** then You will listen from heaven and pardon the sins of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land that You gave to them and to their fathers.

**6:26** When heaven is shut up and there is no rain because they will sin against You, and they pray toward this place and praise Your name, and turn back from their sins because You will humble them;

**6:27** then You will listen from heaven and pardon the sins of Your servants and of Your people Israel, for You will show them the good way in which they will walk, and You will give rain upon Your land that You gave to Your people as an inheritance.

**6:28** If famine comes upon the land, if death comes upon the land, if blight and mildew, locust and caterpillar come, if the enemy oppresses them in front of their cities, concerning every plague and every distress,

**6:29** and every prayer and every supplication that may be made by any person and by all Your people Israel, if a person knows his affliction and his sickness, and spreads out his hands toward this house,

**6:30** then You will listen from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and heal, and You will give to a man according to his ways, as You know his heart, for You alone know the heart of the sons of men;

**6:31** so that they may fear all Your ways all the days that they live on the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**6:32** And as for every foreigner who is not from the people of Israel, and comes from a distant land because of Your great name and Your mighty hand and Your uplifted arm, when they come and pray toward this place,

**6:33** then You will listen from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, and act according to everything for which the foreigner calls upon You, so that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name, and fear You like Your people Israel, and know that Your name has been called upon this house that I have built.

**6:34** Now if Your people go out to war against their enemies on the way by which You will send them, and they pray to You in the direction of this city that You chose, and of the house that I have built for Your name,

**6:35** then You will hear from heaven their supplication and their prayer, and execute their just cause.

**6:36** For they will sin against You (for there will be no person who will not sin), and You will strike them and hand them over before enemies, and those who capture them will take them captive to a land of enemies, to a land far or near,

**6:37** and should they turn their heart back in their land where they were led away, and indeed turn back and make supplication to You in their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted lawlessly,'

**6:38** and should they turn back to You with a whole heart and with all their soul in the land of those who took them captive, and pray in the direction of their land that You gave to their fathers, and of the city that You chose, and of the house that I built for Your name,

**6:39** then You will hear from heaven, from Your prepared dwelling place, their prayer and their supplication, and execute judgments, and pardon the people who sinned against You.

**6:40** Now, Lord, let Your eyes indeed be open and Your ears attentive to the supplication of this place.

**6:41** And now arise, Lord God, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. May Your priests, Lord God, clothe themselves with salvation, and let Your sons rejoice in good things.

**6:42** Lord God, do not turn away Your face; remember the mercies of David Your servant."

## Translator Notes

- **6:6** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek text does not include a clause about choosing Jerusalem here, moving directly to the choice of David.
- **6:16** — The Greek reads "in My name" (en to onomati mou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "in my law," and has "from the presence" (apo prosopou) without a possessive pronoun.
- **6:19** — Throughout Solomon's prayer (6:19-39), the Greek uses future indicative verbs to render Hebrew petitionary weqatal forms; in 6:21, 25, 27, 39 (and 7:14), "pardon" translates the Septuagint idiom hileos eimi (lit. "be gracious/propitious").
- **6:30** — The Greek reads "and heal" (kai iase) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and forgive," and in 6:31 reads "fear all Your ways" rather than "fear you, to walk in your ways."
- **6:41** — The Greek reads "Your sons" (hoi huioi sou) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "your faithful ones," and in 6:42 reads "Your face" (to prosopon sou) rather than "the face of your anointed one."
