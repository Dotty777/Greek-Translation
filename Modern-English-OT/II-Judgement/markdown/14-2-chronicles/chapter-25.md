## Text

**25:1** Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Jonaa from Jerusalem.

**25:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, yet not with a full heart.

**25:3** And it came about, when the kingdom was established in his hand, that he put to death his servants who had murdered the king his father.

**25:4** Yet he did not kill their sons, according to the covenant of the law of the Lord, just as it is written, as the Lord commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die for children, and sons shall not die for fathers; rather, each shall die for his own sin."

**25:5** And Amaziah assembled the house of Judah, and stationed them according to their fathers' houses under commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds for all Judah and Jerusalem; and he numbered them from twenty years old and upward, and found them to be three hundred thousand, able to go out to war, holding spear and shield.

**25:6** And he hired from Israel one hundred thousand, mighty in strength, for one hundred talents of silver.

**25:7** And a man of God came to him, saying, "King, the army of Israel shall not go with you, because the Lord is not with Israel, all the sons of Ephraim.

**25:8** For if you think you will prevail with these, then the Lord will rout you before the enemies, because it is from the Lord both to be strong and to rout."

**25:9** And Amaziah said to the man of God, "And what shall I do about the one hundred talents that I gave to the army of Israel?" And the man of God said, "The Lord is able to give you far more than these."

**25:10** And Amaziah separated the army that had come to him from Ephraim, to depart to their own place; and they became very angry against Judah, and returned to their own place in furious anger.

**25:11** And Amaziah grew strong and took his people, and went to the Valley of Salt, and struck down ten thousand of the sons of Seir there.

**25:12** And the sons of Judah captured ten thousand alive, and brought them to the top of the cliff, and hurled them down from the top of the cliff, and all of them were broken to pieces.

**25:13** And the sons of the army whom Amaziah had sent back so as not to be found with him for war attacked the cities of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon, and struck down three thousand among them, and plundered much spoil.

**25:14** And it came about, after Amaziah arrived from striking down Idumea, that he brought to them the gods of the sons of Seir, and set them up for himself as gods, and stood before them to bow down, and he himself was sacrificing to them.

**25:15** And the wrath of the Lord came upon Amaziah, and He sent prophets to him, and they said to him, "Why did you seek the gods of the people who did not rescue their own people from your hand?"

**25:16** And it came about as he spoke to him, that he said to him, "Have I made you counselor of the king? Take care that you are not flogged." And the prophet became silent, and said, "I know that He purposed against you to destroy you, because you did this and did not listen to my counsel."

**25:17** And Amaziah took counsel.

**25:18** And Joash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The chozei in Lebanon sent to the cedar that is in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife,' and look, the wild beasts of the field that are in Lebanon will come; and the wild beasts came and trampled down the achouch.

**25:19** You said, 'Look, you struck down Idumea,' and your heavy heart lifts you up. Now stay in your house; and why do you engage in harm, and you will fall, you and Judah with you?"

**25:20** And Amaziah did not listen, because it came about from the Lord to deliver him into hands, because he sought out the gods of the Idumeans.

**25:21** And Joash king of Israel went up, and they faced one another, he and Amaziah king of Judah, at Beth-shemesh, which belongs to Judah.

**25:22** And Judah was routed before Israel, and each fled to his tent.

**25:23** And Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, the son of Joash, at Beth-shemesh, and brought them into Jerusalem, and tore down four hundred cubits of the wall of Jerusalem, from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate.

**25:24** And all the gold and the silver and all the vessels found in the house of the Lord and with Jabdedom, and the treasures of the king's house, and the sons of the mixtures; and he returned to Samaria.

**25:25** And Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after Joash the son of Joash, king of Israel, died.

**25:26** And the rest of the acts of Amaziah, the first and the last, look, are they not written in the book of Judah and Israel?

**25:27** And at the time when Amaziah turned away from the Lord, they laid a plot against him, and he fled from Jerusalem to Lachish; and someone sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

**25:28** And they carried him up on the horses, and buried him with his fathers in the city of David.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — "Jonaa" renders Greek Ionaa, differing from Hebrew Jehoaddan.
- **25:17** — The Greek text ends concisely after "took counsel," lacking the longer clause in Hebrew where Amaziah sends a challenge to Joash king of Israel.
- **25:18** — "Chozei" and "achouch" are untranslated Greek transliterations of the Hebrew term for "thistle" or "thornbush." The Greek also doubles the clause about the wild beasts, first in the future and then in the past tense.
- **25:24** — "Jabdedom" renders Greek Iabdedom (corresponding to Hebrew Obed-edom), and "sons of the mixtures" reflects Greek symmixeis ("mixtures"), translating the Hebrew term for "hostages." As in Hebrew, an explicit verb ("took") is omitted.
- **25:25** — The Greek text reads "Joash the son of Joash, king of Israel," whereas Hebrew has "Joash the son of Jehoahaz."
- **25:27** — The Greek shifts abruptly from plural epethento ("they laid a plot") to third-person singular verbs apesteilen ("he/someone sent") and ethanatosen ("he/someone put to death"), referring collectively to the conspirators or an unnamed agent rather than Amaziah.
