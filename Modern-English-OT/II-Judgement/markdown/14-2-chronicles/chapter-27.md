## Text

**27:1** Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Jerusha, the daughter of Sador.

**27:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that Uzziah his father had done, yet he did not enter the temple of the Lord; and the people were still corrupting themselves.

**27:3** He built the high gate of the house of the Lord, and on its wall Opla he built much,

**27:4** in the mountain of Judah and in the forests, both dwellings and towers.

**27:5** He fought against the king of the sons of Ammon and prevailed over him. And they gave him each year one hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand kors of wheat, and ten thousand of barley; these the king of Ammon brought to him each year, in the first year, and the second, and the third.

**27:6** And Jotham grew strong, because he prepared his ways before the Lord his God.

**27:7** And the rest of the acts of Jotham, and the war, and his deeds, look, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**27:9** And Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David; and Ahaz his son became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — "Sador" renders Greek Sador, differing from Hebrew Zadok.
- **27:3** — "Opla" renders Greek Opla (preceded by "its" or "his"), corresponding to Hebrew Ophel.
- **27:4** — The Greek text continues directly from verse 3 without the Hebrew clause "and he built cities," and reads "dwellings" (oikeseis) where Hebrew has "fortresses."
- **27:9** — Verse 27:8, which in Hebrew repeats Jotham's age and reign length from verse 1, is absent from this Greek text.
