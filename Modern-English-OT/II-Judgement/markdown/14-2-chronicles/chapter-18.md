## Text

**18:1** And Jehoshaphat still had wealth and much glory, and he allied himself by marriage with the house of Ahab.

**18:2** And at the end of some years he went down to Ahab in Samaria, and Ahab slaughtered many sheep and calves for him and for the people who were with him, and he showed him affection so that he would go up with him to Ramoth of Gilead.

**18:3** And Ahab king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Will you go with me to Ramoth of Gilead?" And he said to him, "As I am, so also are you; as my people, so also your people, with you in war."

**18:4** And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please inquire of the Lord today."

**18:5** And the king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, "Shall I go to Ramoth-gilead to war, or shall I refrain?" And they said, "Go up, and God will give it into the hands of the king."

**18:6** And Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not still a prophet of the Lord here, that we may inquire from him?"

**18:7** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man through whom to inquire of the Lord, and I have hated him, because he does not prophesy concerning me for good, for all his days are for evil; this is Micaiah son of Jemaa." And Jehoshaphat said, "Do not let the king speak like this."

**18:8** And the king called one eunuch and said, "Quickly, Micaiah son of Jemaa!"

**18:9** Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting each on his throne and clothed in robes, sitting in the open square at the entrance of the gate of Samaria, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

**18:10** And Zedekiah son of Canaan made for himself horns of iron and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'With these you will gore Syria until it is consumed.'"

**18:11** And all the prophets were prophesying like this, saying, "Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and you will prosper, and the Lord will give it into the hands of the king."

**18:12** And the messenger who went to call Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Look, the prophets have spoken good things concerning the king with one mouth; so please let your words be like those of one of them, and you will speak good things."

**18:13** And Micaiah said, "As the Lord lives, whatever God says to me, that I will speak."

**18:14** And he came to the king, and the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall I go to Ramoth-gilead to war, or shall I refrain?" And he said, "Go up, and you will prosper, and they will be given into your hands."

**18:15** And the king said to him, "How many times do I put you under oath not to speak to me anything except the truth in the name of the Lord?"

**18:16** And he said, "I saw Israel scattered in the mountains like sheep that have no shepherd; and the Lord said, 'They do not have a leader; let each one return to his house in peace.'"

**18:17** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not say to you that he does not prophesy good concerning me, but only evil?"

**18:18** And he said, "Not so! Hear the word of the Lord. I saw the Lord sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven was standing on His right and on His left.

**18:19** And the Lord said, 'Who will deceive Ahab king of Israel, so that he will go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?' And one said this, and another said that.

**18:20** And the spirit came forward and stood before the Lord and said, 'I will deceive him.' And the Lord said, 'By what means?'

**18:21** And it said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' And He said, 'You will deceive him, you will prevail; go out and do so.'

**18:22** And now, look, the Lord has put a lying spirit in the mouth of these prophets of yours, and the Lord has spoken evil against you."

**18:23** And Zedekiah son of Canaan came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek and said to him, "By what way—a spirit from me, a spirit to you, the Spirit of the Lord from me to speak to you?"

**18:24** And Micaiah said, "Look, you will see on that day; he will enter an inner room from an inner room to hide himself."

**18:25** And the king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Emer, ruler of the city, and to Joash the ruler, son of the king,

**18:26** and say, 'Thus said the king: Put this man in the prison house, and let him eat bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace.'"

**18:27** And Micaiah said, "If you indeed return in peace, the Lord has not spoken by me. Hear, all peoples!"

**18:28** And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.

**18:29** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Disguise me, and I will enter into the battle; and you, put on my clothing." And the king of Israel disguised himself and entered into the battle.

**18:30** And the king of Syria commanded the commanders of the chariots who were with him, saying, "Do not fight the small or the great, but only the king of Israel."

**18:31** And it happened, when the commanders of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, "He is the king of Israel," and they surrounded him to fight. And Jehoshaphat cried out, and the Lord saved him, and God turned them away from him.

**18:32** And it happened, when the commanders of the chariots saw that he was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from him.

**18:33** And a man drew a bow with sure aim and struck the king of Israel between the lung and the breastplate; and he said to the charioteer, "Turn your hand around and bring me out of the battle, for I am wounded."

**18:34** And the battle turned on that day, and the king of Israel was standing in the chariot until evening facing Syria, and he died as the sun was setting.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — Greek reads "Jemaa" (Iemaas here and Iemaa in v. 8) where MT has Imlah.
- **18:10** — Greek reads "Canaan" (Chanaan, also in v. 23) where MT has Chenaanah.
- **18:18** — Greek opens Micaiah's speech with "Not so!" (reading the Hebrew consonants as lo ken) where MT has "Therefore" (laken).
- **18:23** — The Greek text lacks a verb and repeats "spirit" (pneuma) three times in an apparent textual conflation (contrast MT "Which way did the Spirit of the Lord go from me to speak to you?").
- **18:25** — Greek reads "Emer" where MT has Amon, and adds "the ruler" before "son of the king"; in v. 24 Greek also reads third-person singular "he will enter" where MT has second-person "when you go."
- **18:29** — Greek reads the imperative "Disguise me" and "my clothing," whereas MT has "I will disguise myself" and "your robes."
- **18:33** — Greek reads "with sure aim" (eustochos) where MT has "at random," and "the lung" (tou pneumonos) where MT has "the joints/scale-armor."
