## Text

**35:1** And Josiah kept the Passover to the Lord his God; and they sacrificed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**35:2** And he appointed the priests to their duties and strengthened them for the work of the house of the Lord.

**35:3** And he told the Levites who were capable in all Israel to consecrate themselves to the Lord; and they placed the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David, the king of Israel, had built. And the king said, "You are not to carry anything on your shoulders; now therefore minister to the Lord your God and to His people Israel,

**35:4** and prepare yourselves according to your ancestral houses and according to your courses, according to the writing of David king of Israel and by the hand of King Solomon his son;

**35:5** and stand in the house according to the divisions of your ancestral houses for your brothers, the sons of the people, and a portion of an ancestral house for the Levites;

**35:6** and sacrifice the Passover, and prepare for your brothers, to do according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses."

**35:7** And Josiah contributed to the sons of the people sheep and lambs and kids from the young of the goats, all that were for the Passover, and all those found to the number of thirty thousand, and three thousand calves; these were from the possessions of the king.

**35:8** And his rulers contributed to the people and to the priests and Levites; Hilkiah and Zechariah and Jehiel, the rulers of the house of God, gave to the priests, and they gave for the Passover two thousand six hundred sheep and lambs and kids, and three hundred calves.

**35:9** And Conaniah and Benaiah and Shemaiah and Nethanel his brother, and Hashabiah and Joel and Jozabad, rulers of the Levites, contributed to the Levites for the Passover five thousand, and five hundred calves.

**35:10** And the ministry was set in order, and the priests stood at their station and the Levites in their divisions, according to the command of the king.

**35:11** And they sacrificed the Passover, and the priests poured out the blood from their hand, and the Levites skinned them.

**35:12** And they prepared the burnt offering to hand it over to them according to the division by ancestral houses for the sons of the people, to offer to the Lord, as it is written in the book of Moses; and so until the morning.

**35:13** And they roasted the Passover in fire according to the ordinance, and they boiled the holy offerings in the bronze pots and in the cauldrons; and it went well, and they ran to all the sons of the people.

**35:14** And after preparing for them and for the priests, because the priests were offering up the fat portions and the burnt offerings until night, the Levites prepared for themselves and for their brothers, the sons of Aaron.

**35:15** And the singers, the sons of Asaph, were at their station according to the commands of David, and Asaph and Heman and Jeduthun, the prophets of the king; and the rulers and the gatekeepers, gate by gate, did not have to move from the ministry of the holy things, for their brothers the Levites prepared for them.

**35:16** And all the ministry of the Lord was set in order and prepared on that day, to keep the Passover and to offer the burnt offerings upon the altar of the Lord, according to the command of King Josiah.

**35:17** And the sons of Israel who were found kept the Passover at that time, and the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days.

**35:18** And there had not been a Passover like it in Israel; from the days of Samuel the prophet and of every king of Israel they had not kept one like the Passover that Josiah and the priests and the Levites and all Judah and the Israel that was found and those living in Jerusalem kept to the Lord,

**35:19** in the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah. And the mediums and the spiritists and the teraphim and the idols and the karaseim that were in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, King Josiah burned with fire, so that he might establish the words of the law that were written in the book that Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the Lord. There was no one like him before him who turned to the Lord with all his heart and with all his soul and with all his strength according to all the law of Moses, and after him no one like him arose. Yet the Lord did not turn from the anger of His great wrath, with which the Lord was angered in wrath against Judah because of all the statutes with which Manasseh provoked Him to anger. And the Lord said, "I will also remove Judah from sight, just as I removed Israel, and I have rejected the city that I chose, Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, 'My name will be there.'"

**35:20** And Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt went up against the king of the Assyrians at the Euphrates River, and King Josiah went to meet him.

**35:21** And he sent messengers to him, saying, "What is there between me and you, king of Judah? And I have not come against you today to wage war; and God has told me to hurry. Watch out for God who is with me, so that He may not destroy you."

**35:22** And Josiah did not turn his face away from him, but rather strengthened himself to fight against him, and did not listen to the words of Neco from the mouth of God, and came to fight in the plain of Megiddo.

**35:23** And the archers shot at King Josiah; and the king said to his servants, "Take me out, for I am severely hurt."

**35:24** And his servants took him out of the chariot and put him up into the second chariot that he had, and they brought him to Jerusalem; and he died and was buried with his fathers. And all Judah and Jerusalem mourned over Josiah.

**35:25** And Jeremiah lamented over Josiah, and all the male and female rulers spoke a lament over Josiah until this day; and they made it a statute in Israel, and look, it is written in the Laments.

**35:26** And the acts of Josiah and his hope were written in the law of the Lord,

**35:27** and his acts, the first and the last, look, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **35:3** — The Greek differs markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, describing the Levites as "capable" (Hebrew: "who taught"), turning "who were holy" into an indirect command "to consecrate themselves", and rendering "Put the holy ark" as a completed action, "and they placed the holy ark".
- **35:9** — The Greek includes Benaiah (absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and reads Joel where the Hebrew has Jeiel.
- **35:12** — The Greek "until the morning" reflects reading the unvocalized Hebrew consonants for "cattle" as "morning"; similarly in v. 13, "and it went well" reflects reading the Hebrew word for "pans" as a verb meaning "prospered".
- **35:19** — Following the opening temporal phrase, the Greek preserves a lengthy addition parallel to 2 Kings 23:24-27 that is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text of 2 Chronicles. Within this addition, "karaseim" is an untranslated Greek transliteration (corresponding to "abominations" in 2 Kings 23:24), and "statutes" corresponds to "provocations" in 2 Kings 23:26.
- **35:25** — The Greek reads "male and female rulers" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "singing men and singing women" (from similar-looking Hebrew consonants); in v. 26, "his hope" likewise reflects an alternative reading of the Hebrew for "his loyal deeds".
