## Text

**31:1** And when all these things were completed, all Israel who were found in the cities of Judah went out and shattered the pillars and cut down the groves and tore down the high places and the altars from all the land of Judea and Benjamin and Ephraim and from Manasseh to completion; and all Israel returned, each one to his inheritance and to their cities.

**31:2** And Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites, and the divisions of each one according to his own service, for the priests and for the Levites, for the burnt offering and for the sacrifice of deliverance, and to praise and to give thanks and to serve in the gates, in the courts of the house of the Lord.

**31:3** And the king's portion from his possessions was for the burnt offerings, the morning and the evening, and burnt offerings for Sabbaths and for the new moons and for the feasts written in the law of the Lord.

**31:4** And they told the people dwelling in Jerusalem to give the portion of the priests and the Levites, so that they might be strong in the service of the house of the Lord.

**31:5** And when he commanded the matter, Israel abundantly gave firstfruits of grain and wine and oil and honey and all the produce of the field. And the sons of Israel and Judah brought all the tithes in abundance.

**31:6** And those dwelling in the cities of Judah, they also brought tithes of calves and sheep and tithes of goats, and consecrated them to the Lord their God, and brought them in and placed them in heaps.

**31:7** In the third month the foundation heaps began to be laid, and in the seventh month they were completed.

**31:8** And Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, and they blessed the Lord and His people Israel.

**31:9** And Hezekiah inquired of the priests and the Levites concerning the heaps.

**31:10** And Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, spoke and said, "Since the firstfruits began to be brought into the house of the Lord, we have eaten and drunk and left some over; for the Lord has blessed His people, and we have left over up to this abundance."

**31:11** And Hezekiah gave orders to prepare chambers in the house of the Lord. And they prepared them.

**31:12** And they brought the firstfruits and the tithes there in faithfulness, and over them as overseer was Chomenias the Levite, and Shimei his brother was second in command.

**31:13** And Eiel and Uzziah and Mahath and Asahel and Jerimoth and Ezabath and Jeiel and Samachiah and Thanai, Benaiah and his sons, were appointed through Conaniah and Shimei his brother, just as King Hezekiah and Azariah the ruler of the house of the Lord commanded.

**31:14** And Kore the son of Heman the Levite, the gatekeeper toward the east, was over the gifts, to give the firstfruits to the Lord and the most holy things,

**31:15** by the hand of Odom and Benjamin and Jeshua and Shimei and Marias and Shecaniah, by the hand of the priests in faithfulness, to give to their brothers according to the divisions, both the great and the small,

**31:16** each one of the offspring of the males from three years old and upward, to everyone entering the house of the Lord, for the daily reckoning day by day, for service in the divisions of their order.

**31:17** This was the enrollment of the priests by fathers' houses, and the Levites in their divisions from twenty years old and upward in order,

**31:18** to enroll them with all the offspring of their sons and their daughters, for the whole multitude, because in faithfulness they purified what is holy.

**31:19** For the sons of Aaron who served as priests, and those from their cities, in every single city there were men who were named by name to give a portion to every male among the priests and to everyone numbered among the Levites.

**31:20** And Hezekiah did so throughout all Judah, and he did what was good and what was upright before the Lord his God.

**31:21** And in every work that he began in labor in the house of the Lord, and in the law and in the commands, he sought his God with all his soul, and he acted and prospered.

## Translator Notes

- **31:6** — The Greek reads "tithes of goats" (ἐπιδέκατα αἰγῶν) where the Hebrew reads "tithes of dedicated things," and places "the sons of Israel and Judah" at the end of v. 5 rather than at the start of v. 6.
- **31:10** — The Greek reads "and drunk" (καὶ ἐπίομεν) where the Hebrew reads "and had plenty," and ends with a first-person plural active verb ("we have left over up to this abundance").
- **31:12** — The Greek spells this Levite's name Chomenias (Χωμενίας) here, whereas v. 13 uses the form Conaniah (Χωνενίου) for the same overseer.
- **31:13** — Several names in the Greek differ from the Hebrew list: Eiel (Jehiel), Uzziah (Azaziah), Mahath (Nahath), Ezabath (Jozabad), Jeiel (Eliel), Samachiah (Ismachiah), and Thanai (Mahath); the Greek also adds "and his sons" after Benaiah.
- **31:15** — The Greek reads Heman in v. 14 (Hebrew Imnah) and here lists Odom (Eden), Benjamin (Miniamin), Shimei (Shemaiah), and Marias (Amariah), as well as "by the hand of the priests" where the Hebrew reads "in the cities of the priests."
