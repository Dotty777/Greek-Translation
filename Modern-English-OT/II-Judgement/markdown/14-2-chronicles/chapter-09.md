## Text

**9:1** And the queen of Sheba heard of the name of Solomon, and came to Jerusalem to test Solomon with riddles, with a very great force and camels carrying spices and gold in abundance and precious stones; and she came to Solomon and spoke to him all that was in her soul.

**9:2** And Solomon declared to her all her matters, and no matter escaped Solomon, a matter which he did not report to her.

**9:3** And the queen of Sheba saw the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he had built,

**9:4** and the food of the tables, and the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing and that of his cupbearers, and their apparel, and the burnt offerings which he offered up in the house of the Lord; and she was beside herself.

**9:5** And she said to the king, "The word is true which I heard in my land concerning your words and concerning your wisdom.

**9:6** And I did not believe the words until I came and my eyes saw; and look, half of the abundance of your wisdom was not reported to me; you have surpassed the report that I heard.

**9:7** Blessed are the men, blessed are these servants of yours who stand before you continually and hear your wisdom.

**9:8** May the Lord your God be blessed, who took pleasure in you to set you on His throne as king for the Lord your God. Because the Lord your God loved Israel to establish it forever, He set you over them as king to execute justice and righteousness."

**9:9** And she gave to the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices in great abundance, and precious stones; and there were none like those spices which the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**9:10** And the servants of Solomon and the servants of Hiram brought gold to Solomon from Suphir, and pine wood and precious stones.

**9:11** And the king made the pine wood into steps for the house of the Lord and for the house of the king, and lyres and harps for the singers; and such things had not been seen before in the land of Judah.

**9:12** And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desires which she asked for, besides all that she had brought to King Solomon; and she returned to her land.

**9:13** And the weight of the gold that was brought to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

**9:14** besides what the subordinate men and the merchants brought; and all the kings of Arabia and the satraps of the land brought gold and silver to King Solomon.

**9:15** And King Solomon made two hundred large shields of beaten gold; six hundred pure gold pieces were for each large shield, six hundred gold pieces were upon each large shield;

**9:16** and three hundred shields of beaten gold; and the king placed them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.

**9:17** And the king made a great throne of ivory tusks, and overlaid it with refined gold.

**9:18** And there were six steps to the throne, bound with gold, and armrests on this side and on that side on the seating throne, and two lions standing beside the armrests.

**9:19** And twelve lions were standing there upon the six steps on this side and on that side; nothing like this was made in all the kingdom.

**9:20** And all the vessels of King Solomon were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were covered with gold; silver was not counted as anything in the days of Solomon.

**9:21** For a ship of the king went to Tarshish with the servants of Hiram; once every three years ships came from Tarshish to the king, full of gold and silver and ivory tusks and apes.

**9:22** And Solomon became greater than all the kings both in wealth and in wisdom.

**9:23** And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon to hear his wisdom, which God had put in his heart.

**9:24** And they brought, each one his gifts: silver vessels and gold vessels and clothing, myrrh and sweet spices, horses and mules, year by year.

**9:25** And Solomon had four thousand mares for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he placed them in the cities of the chariots and with the king in Jerusalem.

**9:26** And he was ruler over all the kings from the River even to the land of foreigners and to the borders of Egypt.

**9:27** And the king made the gold and the silver in Jerusalem like stones, and the cedars like the sycamore trees in the lowland in abundance.

**9:28** And the export of Solomon's horses was from Egypt and from all the earth.

**9:29** And the rest of the acts of Solomon, the first and the last, look, they are written in the words of Nathan the prophet and in the words of Ahijah the Shilonite and in the visions of Joel the seer concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

**9:30** And King Solomon reigned over all Israel forty years.

**9:31** And Solomon slept, and they buried him in the city of David his father; and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — Greek reads "the burnt offerings which he offered up in the house of the Lord" where the Hebrew reads "his ascent by which he went up to the house of the Lord."
- **9:15** — Greek preserves a textual doublet repeating the six hundred gold pieces for each large shield, whereas 9:16 omits the gold weight for the three hundred shields found in the Hebrew.
- **9:24** — Greek reads stakte ("myrrh") where the Hebrew has "weapons," and in 9:25 Greek reads "mares for chariots" rather than Hebrew "stalls for horses and chariots."
- **9:26** — Greek allophylon literally means "foreigners" and is the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines.
- **9:29** — Greek names the seer "Joel," differing from the Hebrew name "Iddo," and refers to "the words" of Ahijah rather than his "prophecy."
