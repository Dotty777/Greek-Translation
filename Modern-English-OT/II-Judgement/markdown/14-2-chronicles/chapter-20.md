## Text

**20:1** And after these things, the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and with them some of the Meunites, came against Jehoshaphat for war.

**20:2** And some came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, "A great multitude has come against you from beyond the sea, from Syria; and look, they are in Hasam Thamara, which is En-gedi."

**20:3** And he was afraid, and Jehoshaphat set his face to seek the Lord, and they proclaimed a fast.

**20:4** And Judah gathered together to seek the Lord, and from all the cities Judah came to seek the Lord.

**20:5** And Jehoshaphat stood up in the assembly of Judah in Jerusalem, in the house of the Lord, before the new court,

**20:6** and said, "Lord God of our fathers, are You not God in heaven above? And You rule over all the kingdoms of the nations, and in Your hand is strength of might, and there is no standing against You.

**20:7** Did You not, Lord, utterly destroy the inhabitants of this land from before Israel and give it to the seed of Abraham, Your beloved, forever?

**20:8** And they lived in it, and built in it a sanctuary for Your name, saying,

**20:9** 'If evils come upon us, sword, judgment, death, famine, we will stand before the house and before You, for Your name is upon this house, and we will cry out to You from the distress, and You will hear and save.'

**20:10** And now, look, the sons of Ammon and Mount Seir, through whom You did not allow Israel to pass when they came out of the land of Egypt, for they turned aside from them and did not utterly destroy them;

**20:11** and now, look, they are attacking us, coming out to drive us out from our inheritance that You gave us,

**20:12** Lord our God, will You not pass judgment on them? For we have no strength to stand against this great multitude that has come against us, and we do not know what we should do to them, but our eyes are upon You."

**20:13** And all Judah was standing before the Lord, along with their little children and wives.

**20:14** And as for Oziel, the son of Zechariah, of the sons of Eleael, the son of Manthanias, the Levite from the sons of Asaph, the Spirit of the Lord came upon him in the assembly,

**20:15** and he said, "Listen, all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem and King Jehoshaphat! Thus says the Lord to you yourselves: 'Do not be afraid nor be terrified before this great crowd, for the battle is not yours, but God's.

**20:16** Tomorrow go down against them; look, they are going up by the ascent of Hasae, and you will find them at the end of the river of the wilderness of Jeriel.

**20:17** It is not for you to fight; understand these things and see the salvation of the Lord with you! Look, also Jerusalem: do not be terrified nor be afraid of him to go out to meet them, and the Lord is with you.'"

**20:18** And Jehoshaphat bowed down upon his face, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell before the Lord to worship the Lord.

**20:19** And the Levites from the sons of Kohath and from the sons of Korah stood up to praise the Lord God of Israel with a loud voice on high.

**20:20** And they rose early in the morning and went out into the wilderness of Tekoa; and as they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and cried out, "Listen to me, Judah and those living in Jerusalem! Trust in the Lord your God, and you will be trusted; trust in His prophet, and you will prosper."

**20:21** And he took counsel with the people, and appointed psalm-singers and praisers to give thanks and to praise the holy things as they went out before the army; and he was saying, "Give thanks to the Lord, for His mercy is forever."

**20:22** And when he began his praise of thanksgiving, the Lord caused the sons of Ammon to fight against Moab and Mount Seir, who had come out against Judah, and they were routed.

**20:23** And the sons of Ammon and Moab revolted against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to utterly destroy and wipe them out; and when they had finished off the inhabitants of Seir, they rose up against one another to be utterly destroyed.

**20:24** And Judah came to the lookout of the wilderness, and looked out and saw the multitude; and look, all were dead, fallen upon the ground; there was no one who escaped.

**20:25** And Jehoshaphat and his people came to plunder their spoil, and they found much livestock and possessions and spoil and desirable vessels, and he plundered among them; and there were three days of their plundering the spoil, for it was plentiful.

**20:26** And it came about on the fourth day that they assembled together in the valley of blessing, for there they blessed the Lord; therefore they called the name of that place Valley of Blessing until this day.

**20:27** And every man of Judah returned to Jerusalem, with Jehoshaphat leading them, in great gladness, because the Lord made them glad over their enemies.

**20:28** And they entered into Jerusalem with harps and with lyres and with trumpets, into the house of the Lord.

**20:29** And dread of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the earth when they heard that the Lord had fought against the adversaries of Israel.

**20:30** And the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was at peace, and his God gave him rest on every side.

**20:31** And Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah; he was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Azubah, daughter of Shilhi.

**20:32** And he walked in the ways of his father Asa, and did not turn aside from doing what was right before the Lord.

**20:33** However, the high places still remained, and still the people had not directed their heart to the Lord God of their fathers.

**20:34** And the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, the first and the last, look, they are written in the words of Joshua the son of Hanani, who wrote the book of the king of Israel.

**20:35** And after these things Jehoshaphat partnered with Ahaziah king of Israel, and he committed lawlessness

**20:36** in acting and going to him to make ships to go to Tarshish; and he made ships in Ezion-geber.

**20:37** And Eliada the son of Odeia from Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you befriended Ahaziah, the Lord has shattered your work, and your ships have been wrecked." And he was not able to go to Tarshish.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Greek text reads "Meunites" (Meinaiōn), whereas the Hebrew text reads "Ammonites."
- **20:2** — The Greek text reads "Hasam Thamara," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Hazazon-tamar."
- **20:14** — The Greek genealogy reads "Oziel, the son of Zechariah, of the sons of Eleael, the son of Manthanias," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah."
- **20:16** — The Greek text reads "Hasae" and "Jeriel," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Ziz" and "Jeruel."
- **20:17** — The Greek text reads "understand these things," "Look, also Jerusalem," and "be afraid of him" (auton), whereas the Hebrew text reads "position yourselves, stand," "Judah and Jerusalem," and "tomorrow" (reflecting an inner-Greek shift from aurion to auton).
- **20:34** — The Greek text reads "Joshua" (Iēsou) and "who wrote the book of the king of Israel," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Jehu" and "which were recorded in the book of the kings of Israel."
- **20:37** — The Greek text reads "Eliada the son of Odeia," whereas the Hebrew text reads "Eliezer son of Dodavahu."
