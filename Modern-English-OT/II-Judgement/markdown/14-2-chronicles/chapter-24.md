## Text

**24:1** Joash was seven years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Abiah from Beersheba.

**24:2** And Joash did what was right in the sight of the Lord all the days of Jehoiada the priest.

**24:3** And Jehoiada took for himself two wives, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**24:4** And it came about after these things that it came upon the heart of Joash to repair the house of the Lord.

**24:5** And he gathered the priests and the Levites and said to them, "Go out to the cities of Judah and collect from all Israel money to strengthen the house of the Lord year by year, and hasten to speak." And the Levites did not believe.

**24:6** And King Joash called Jehoiada the leader and said to him, "Why have you not inquired concerning the Levites, to bring in from Judah and Jerusalem what was decreed by Moses the man of God, because he assembled Israel to the tent of testimony?"

**24:7** For Athaliah was the lawless one, and her sons tore down the house of God, and indeed they used the sacred things of the house of the Lord for the Baals.

**24:8** And the king said, "Let a chest be made and let it be placed at the gate of the house of the Lord outside.

**24:9** And let them proclaim in Judah and in Jerusalem to bring in to the Lord, just as Moses the servant of God spoke concerning Israel in the wilderness."

**24:10** And all the leaders and all the people gave, and they kept bringing it in and dropping it into the chest until it was filled.

**24:11** And it came about, when they brought the chest in to the officers of the king by the hand of the Levites, and when they saw that the money was abundant, that the scribe of the king and the officer of the high priest came and emptied the chest, and set it back in its place; thus they did day by day, and they collected much money.

**24:12** And the king and Jehoiada the priest gave it to those doing the works for the work of the house of the Lord; and they hired stonecutters and carpenters to repair the house of the Lord, and smiths of iron and bronze to repair the house of the Lord.

**24:13** And those doing the works kept working, and the extent of the works went up in their hands, and they restored the house of the Lord to its standing and strengthened it.

**24:14** And when they had finished, they brought to the king and to Jehoiada the remainder of the money, and they made utensils for the house of the Lord, service utensils for burnt offerings and censers of gold and silver; and they offered up burnt offerings in the house of the Lord continually all the days of Jehoiada.

**24:15** And Jehoiada grew old, full of days, and he died, being one hundred and thirty years old when he died.

**24:16** And they buried him in the city of David with the kings, because he had done good with Israel and with God and His house.

**24:17** And it came about after the death of Jehoiada that the leaders of Judah came in and bowed down to the king; then the king listened to them.

**24:18** And they forsook the Lord, the God of their fathers, and served the Astartes and the idols; and wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem on this day.

**24:19** And He sent prophets to them to turn them back to the Lord, and they did not listen; and they testified against them, and they did not obey.

**24:20** And the Spirit of God clothed Azariah the son of Jehoiada the priest, and he stood up above the people and said, "Thus says the Lord, 'Why do you transgress the commandments of the Lord? And you will not prosper; because you have forsaken the Lord, He also will forsake you.'"

**24:21** And they conspired against him, and stoned him by the command of King Joash in the court of the house of the Lord.

**24:22** And Joash did not remember the mercy that Jehoiada his father had shown him, and he put his son to death. And as he was dying, he said, "May the Lord see and judge!"

**24:23** And it came about after the completion of the year that the army of Syria went up against him, and came against Judah and against Jerusalem; and they destroyed all the leaders of the people among the people, and sent all their plunder to the king of Damascus.

**24:24** For the army of Syria arrived with few men, and God delivered into their hands a very great army, because they had forsaken the Lord, the God of their fathers; and He executed judgments on Joash.

**24:25** And after they departed from him, when they left him in severe illnesses, his servants conspired against him because of the bloodshed of the son of Jehoiada the priest, and they put him to death on his bed, and he died; and they buried him in the city of David, but they did not bury him in the tomb of the kings.

**24:26** And those who conspired against him were Zabel the son of Sama the Ammonite and Zozabed the son of Somaioth the Moabite,

**24:27** and all his sons, and the five came to him. And the rest, look, are written in the record of the kings; and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Greek text gives the queen mother's name as "Abiah," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "Zibiah."
- **24:5** — The Greek text reads "hasten to speak" (MT "hasten the matter") and "the Levites did not believe" (episteusan, likely an inner-Greek variant for espeusan, "did not hasten," matching MT).
- **24:18** — The Greek text reads "the Astartes" (MT "the Asherim") and "on this day" (MT "for this guilt of theirs").
- **24:20** — The Greek text names the priest's son "Azariah," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "Zechariah."
- **24:26** — The Greek text gives the conspirators' names and patronymics as "Zabel the son of Sama the Ammonite" and "Zozabed the son of Somaioth the Moabite," using masculine forms throughout (MT "Zabad the son of Shimeath the Ammonitess" and "Jehozabad the son of Shimrith the Moabitess").
- **24:27** — Continuing the sentence from verse 26, the Greek text reads "and all his sons, and the five came to him" (apparently reading Hebrew hammas'a, "the oracle/burden," as a word for "five") and omits the MT clause about rebuilding the house of God.
