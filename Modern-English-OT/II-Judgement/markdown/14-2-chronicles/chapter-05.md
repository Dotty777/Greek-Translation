## Text

**5:1** And Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father, the silver and the gold and the utensils; he put them into the treasury of the Lord.

**5:2** Then Solomon assembled the elders and all the rulers of the tribes, the leaders of the families of the sons of Israel, to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord from the city of David, that is, Zion.

**5:3** And all Israel assembled to the king at the feast, that is, the seventh month.

**5:4** And all the elders of Israel came, and all the Levites took up the ark

**5:5** and the tent of testimony and all the holy utensils that were in the tent, and the priests and the Levites brought it up.

**5:6** And King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel and those who feared and those of them who were gathered together were before the ark, sacrificing calves and sheep, which could not be counted and which could not be reckoned because of the abundance.

**5:7** And the priests brought in the ark of the covenant of the Lord to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Holy of Holies, underneath the wings of the cherubim.

**5:8** And the cherubim had spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and over its poles from above,

**5:9** and the poles projected out, and the ends of the poles were seen from the holy place in front of the inner sanctuary; they were not seen outside; and they were there until this day.

**5:10** There was nothing in the ark except two tablets that Moses placed at Horeb, the things that the Lord covenanted with the sons of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**5:11** And it happened, when the priests came out of the holy place, for all the priests who were found were consecrated; they were not arranged according to division.

**5:12** And all the Levites, the singers, belonging to the sons of Asaph, to Heman, to Jeduthun, and to his sons and his brothers, clothed in robes of fine linen, with cymbals and with harps and with lyres, were standing opposite the altar, and with them one hundred twenty priests blowing the trumpets.

**5:13** And there was one voice in blowing trumpets and in singing and in calling out with one voice to give thanks and to praise the Lord; and they lifted up their voice with trumpets and with cymbals and with instruments of songs, and they were saying, "Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good; for His mercy is forever." And the house was filled with a cloud of the glory of the Lord.

**5:14** And the priests were not able to stand to minister on account of the cloud, because the glory of the Lord filled the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Greek adds "and those who feared" alongside "and those of them who were gathered together" and reverses the order to "calves and sheep."
- **5:9** — The Greek reads "from the holy place" (matching the phrasing in 5:11), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from the ark."
- **5:12** — The Greek specifies "the sons of Asaph" (rather than "Asaph"), has singular "his sons and his brothers," and reads "opposite the altar" without "east."
- **5:13** — The Greek uses the neuter adjective "for it is good" (rather than masculine "for He is good") and ends with "a cloud of the glory of the Lord" (rather than "the house of the Lord").
