## Text

**33:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.

**33:2** And he did what was evil before the Lord, from among all the abominations of the nations whom the Lord utterly destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**33:3** And he turned back and built the high places that Hezekiah his father had torn down, and he set up pillars for the Baals and made groves, and he worshiped all the host of heaven and served them.

**33:4** And he built altars in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord said, "In Jerusalem My name shall be forever."

**33:5** And he built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.

**33:6** And he himself led his children through fire in Ge-bene-Hinnom; and he practiced soothsaying and practiced sorcery and read omens, and he appointed mediums and enchanters; he multiplied doing what was evil before the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**33:7** And he placed the carved, cast image, a likeness that he made, in the house of God, of which God said to David and Solomon his son, "In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will place My name forever.

**33:8** And I will not again move the foot of Israel from the land that I gave to their fathers, only if they are careful to do all that I commanded them, according to all the law, the statutes, and the ordinances by the hand of Moses."

**33:9** And Manasseh led Judah and those dwelling in Jerusalem astray, to do what was evil beyond all the nations that the Lord removed from before the sons of Israel.

**33:10** And the Lord spoke to Manasseh and to his people, and he did not listen.

**33:11** And the Lord brought against them the commanders of the army of the king of Assyria, and they captured Manasseh in bonds and bound him in fetters and brought him to Babylon.

**33:12** And when he was in distress, he sought the face of the Lord his God, and he humbled himself greatly before the God of his fathers;

**33:13** and he prayed to Him, and He heard him, and He heard his cry, and He brought him back to Jerusalem to his kingdom; and Manasseh knew that the Lord Himself is God.

**33:14** And after these things he built a wall outside the city of David on the west toward Gihon in the torrent valley, and as one goes out through the surrounding gate, and up to Ophel itself, and he raised it very high; and he stationed commanders of the army in all the walled cities in Judah.

**33:15** And he removed the foreign gods and the carved image from the house of the Lord, and all the altars that he had built on the mountain of the house of the Lord and in Jerusalem and outside the city.

**33:16** And he restored the altar of the Lord, and he sacrificed on it a sacrifice of deliverance and praise, and he told Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel.

**33:17** Yet the people were still on the high places; yet the Lord was their God.

**33:18** And the rest of the deeds of Manasseh, and his prayer to God, and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of the God of Israel,

**33:19** see, they are in the words of his prayer, and He heard him; and all his sins and his rebellions, and the places on which he built the high places and set up there groves and carved images before he turned back, see, they are written in the words of the seers.

**33:20** And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the garden of his house; and Amos his son reigned in his place.

**33:21** Amos was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.

**33:22** And he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasseh his father had done; and Amos sacrificed to all the idols that Manasseh his father had made, and he served them.

**33:23** And he did not humble himself before the Lord as Manasseh his father had humbled himself, for his son Amos multiplied guilt.

**33:24** And his servants plotted against him and struck him down in his house.

**33:25** And the people of the land struck down those who had plotted against King Amos; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — "Ge-bene-Hinnom" preserves the Greek transliteration (γὲ βανὲ Ἑννόμ) of the Hebrew phrase meaning "the valley of the sons of Hinnom."
- **33:14** — The Greek renders this passage distinctively as "as one goes out through the surrounding gate" (omitting "Fish" from the Fish Gate) and transcribes Ophel as "Opla" (Ὄπλα).
- **33:17** — The Greek omits "sacrificing" and places "the Lord their God" in the nominative case ("yet the Lord was their God") rather than the dative ("only to the Lord their God").
- **33:19** — The Greek links 33:18 directly to "the words of his prayer, and He heard him" (omitting the reference to the chronicles of the kings of Israel found in the Hebrew) and reads "the seers" where the Hebrew reads "Hozai."
- **33:20** — Here and throughout vv. 20-25, the Greek calls Manasseh's son "Amos" (Ἀμώς) rather than the Hebrew "Amon."
