## Text

**21:1** And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers and was buried in the city of David; and his son Joram reigned in his place.

**21:2** And he had six brothers, sons of Jehoshaphat: Jehiel, Zechariah, Mishael, and Shephatiah; all these were sons of Jehoshaphat king of Judah.

**21:3** And their father gave them many gifts, silver and gold and weapons, along with fortified cities in Judah; but the kingdom he gave to Joram, because he was the firstborn.

**21:4** And Joram rose up over his kingdom and grew strong, and killed all his brothers with the sword, and some of the officials of Israel.

**21:5** When he was thirty-two years old, Joram was established over his kingdom, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.

**21:6** And he walked in the way of the king of Israel, as the house of Ahab had done, for a daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did what was evil before the Lord.

**21:7** Yet the Lord was not willing to utterly destroy the house of David, because of the covenant that He had made with David, and as He had said to him, to give a lamp to him and to his sons all the days.

**21:8** In those days Edom revolted from Judah, and they installed a king over themselves.

**21:9** And Joram went out with the commanders, and all the cavalry with him; and it came about that he arose by night and struck Edom, who was surrounding him, and the commanders of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.

**21:10** And Edom revolted from Judah until this day; then Libnah revolted at that time from his hand, because he had forsaken the Lord God of his fathers.

**21:11** Moreover, he himself made high places in the cities of Judah, and they led those living in Jerusalem into prostitution, and he led Judah astray.

**21:12** And it came to him in writing from Elijah the prophet, saying, "Thus says the Lord, God of your father David: 'Because you have not walked in the way of Jehoshaphat your father and in the ways of Asa king of Judah,

**21:13** and have walked in the ways of the king of Israel, and have led Judah and those living in Jerusalem into prostitution, as the house of Ahab committed prostitution, and have killed your brothers, the sons of your father, who were better than you;

**21:14** look, the Lord will strike you with a great blow in your people and in your sons and in your wives and in all your possessions;

**21:15** and you yourself will be in a severe sickness, in a disease of the bowels, until your bowels come out with the sickness day after day.'"

**21:16** And the Lord stirred up against Joram the foreigners and the Arabs and those bordering the Ethiopians;

**21:17** and they went up against Judah and overpowered it, and carried off all the possessions that they found in the house of the king, and his sons and his daughters, and no son was left to him except Ahaziah, the youngest of his sons.

**21:18** And after all these things, the Lord struck him in the bowels with a sickness for which there is no cure.

**21:19** And it went on day after day; and when the time of the days came, two days, his bowels came out with the disease, and he died in a severe sickness; and his people did not make a funeral like the funeral of his fathers.

**21:20** He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem; and he departed without praise, and was buried in the city of David, and not in the tombs of the kings.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Although the Greek text states "six" brothers, it names only four (omitting the two named Azariah in the Hebrew text) and reads "Mishael" (Meisaēl) where the Hebrew text reads "Michael."
- **21:16** — Greek allophylous ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard rendering for "Philistines."
- **21:17** — The Greek text reads "his daughters," whereas the Hebrew text reads "his wives."
- **21:19** — The Greek text literally reads "two days" (hēmeras dyo, rendering the Hebrew idiom for "two years") and "funeral" (ekphoran, where the Hebrew text reads "burning").
