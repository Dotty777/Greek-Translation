## Text

**1:1** And Solomon son of David became strong over his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him and magnified him to great height.

**1:2** And Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and to the commanders of hundreds and to the judges and to all the rulers before Israel, the rulers of the ancestral houses.

**1:3** And Solomon and all the assembly went to the high place that was in Gibeon, where the tent of testimony of God was, which Moses, the servant of the Lord, made in the wilderness.

**1:4** However, David had brought up the ark of God from the city of Kiriath-jearim, because he had prepared a tent for it in Jerusalem.

**1:5** And the bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, had made was there before the tent of the Lord, and Solomon and the assembly sought it out.

**1:6** And Solomon offered there on the bronze altar before the Lord, which was in the tent, and he offered on it a burnt offering of a thousand.

**1:7** In that night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I should give you."

**1:8** And Solomon said to God, "What great mercy You showed with David my father, and You made me king in his place.

**1:9** And now, Lord God, let Your name be confirmed toward David my father, for You made me king over a numerous people, like the dust of the earth.

**1:10** Now give me wisdom and understanding, and I will go out before this people and come in; for who will judge this great people of Yours?"

**1:11** And God said to Solomon, "Because this was in your heart, and you did not ask for wealth of possessions nor glory nor the life of the adversaries, and you did not ask for many days, but asked for yourself wisdom and understanding so that you may judge My people over whom I made you king,

**1:12** wisdom and understanding I am giving you, and wealth and possessions and glory I will give you, such that there has not been anyone like you among the kings before you, and after you it will not be so."

**1:13** And Solomon came from Maba, which was in Gibeon, which was in Jerusalem, before the tent of testimony, and he reigned over Israel.

**1:14** And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen, and he had one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen; and he left them in chariot cities, and the people were with the king in Jerusalem.

**1:15** And the king made gold and silver in Jerusalem like stones, and the cedars in Judea like the sycamore trees in the lowland in abundance.

**1:16** And the export of horsemen for Solomon was from Egypt, and the price of the king's merchants; they were buying to go.

**1:17** And they would go in and bring out from Egypt one chariot for six hundred in silver, and a horse for one hundred fifty; and in this way they brought them through their hands to all the kings of the Hittites and to the kings of Syria.

**1:18** And Solomon gave word to build a house for the name of the Lord and a house for his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Greek reads "tent of testimony" (skene tou martyriou), the standard Septuagint rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "tent of meeting."
- **1:8** — Greek opens Solomon's speech with the relative pronoun "what" (ho) rather than "You" (sy), likely an early scribal variant or an exclamatory construction.
- **1:9** — Greek reads "Your name" (to onoma sou), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Your word."
- **1:13** — Greek transliterates Hebrew bamah ("high place") as a place name, "Maba," and reads "which was in Jerusalem" where the Hebrew has "to Jerusalem."
- **1:16** — Greek reads "horsemen" (hippeon) rather than "horses," and renders the obscure Hebrew geographic term Kue as "price" (time) and "to go" (poreuesthai).
