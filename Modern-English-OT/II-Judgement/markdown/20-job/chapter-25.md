## Text

**25:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**25:2** "For what prelude or fear is from Him, the One who makes the whole universe in the highest?

**25:3** For could anyone suppose that there is respite for raiders? And upon whom will an ambush from Him not come?

**25:4** For how will a mortal be righteous before the Lord? Or who, born of a woman, could purify himself?

**25:5** Indeed, He commands the moon, and it does not shine; and the stars are not pure before Him.

**25:6** Yet alas, man is decay, and a son of man is a worm."

## Translator Notes

- **25:2** — The Greek reads "prelude" (prooimion, also used in 27:1) and "the whole universe" (ten sympasan), reflecting a distinctive Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew terms for "dominion" and "peace."
- **25:3** — The Greek reads "respite for raiders" (parelkysis peiratais) and "ambush" (enedra), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("His troops" and "His light").
- **25:5** — Greek e is rendered as the asseverative particle "Indeed" rather than the conditional variant ei ("If").
