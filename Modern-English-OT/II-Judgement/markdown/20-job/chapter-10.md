## Text

**10:1** "I am weary in my soul. Groaning, I will let loose my words against Him; I will speak, constrained by the bitterness of my soul,

**10:2** and I will say to the Lord, 'Do not teach me to act ungodly; and why have You judged me this way?

**10:3** Or is it good to You if I act unjustly, that I should renounce the works of Your hands, while You have paid attention to the counsel of the ungodly?

**10:4** Or as a mortal sees, do You observe? Or just as a human being sees, will You look?

**10:5** Or is Your life human, or are Your years those of a man,

**10:6** that You have searched out my lawlessness and tracked down my sins?

**10:7** For You know that I have not acted ungodly; yet who is there who delivers from Your hands?

**10:8** Your hands molded me and made me; after these things, turning around, You struck me.

**10:9** Remember that You molded me as clay, yet to earth You are turning me back again.

**10:10** Or did You not milk me like milk, and curdle me like cheese?

**10:11** With skin and flesh You clothed me, and with bones and sinews You wove me together;

**10:12** and life and mercy You placed with me, and Your oversight guarded my spirit.

**10:13** You have these things within Yourself; I know that You can do all things, and nothing is impossible for You.

**10:14** For if I sin, You watch me, and from lawlessness You have not made me guiltless.

**10:15** For if I act ungodly, woe to me! And if I am righteous, I cannot look up, for I am full of dishonor.

**10:16** For I am hunted like a lion for slaughter; for turning around again, You terribly destroy me,

**10:17** renewing my examination against me; and with great wrath You dealt with me, and You brought raiding bands against me.

**10:18** Why then did You bring me out from the womb, and I did not die, and no eye saw me,

**10:19** and I became as one not existing? For why was I not removed from the womb to the tomb?

**10:20** Or is the life of my time not short? Let me rest a little

**10:21** before I depart, from where I will not return, to a dark and gloomy land,

**10:22** to a land of eternal darkness, where there is no light, nor seeing the life of mortals.'"

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Greek opens with a participial phrase ("being weary in my soul") punctuated as an independent clause, which in some Greek editions is linked either to 9:35 or to the following participle "groaning."
- **10:2** — The Septuagint reads "Do not teach me to act ungodly" rather than the Masoretic Hebrew "Do not condemn me."
- **10:3** — Both "if I act unjustly" and "that I should renounce" are first-person singular in the Greek, unlike the second-person verbs of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:17** — Greek peirateria refers primarily to "raiding bands" (marauders) and can also carry the sense of "trials" or "ordeals"; etasis denotes a judicial examination or scrutiny.
- **10:18** — The Greek uses simple aorist indicatives ("and I did not die, and no eye saw me, and I became" in 10:18-19) following "Why...?" to express the unrealized outcome Job wished had happened at birth.
