## Text

**6:1** Then Job answered and said,

**6:2** "If only someone would truly weigh my anger, and lift up my pains in a balance all together,

**6:3** then indeed it will be heavier than the sand of the seashore; but as it seems, my words are worthless.

**6:4** For the arrows of the Lord are in my body, whose fury drinks up my blood; whenever I begin to speak, they pierce me.

**6:5** What then? Will a wild donkey cry out for nothing, unless seeking food? Or will an ox also break forth with a cry at a manger while having food?

**6:6** Will bread be eaten without salt? Or is there also taste in empty words?

**6:7** For my anger cannot cease; for I see my food as a stench, like the smell of a lion.

**6:8** If only He would grant it, and my request would come, and the Lord would grant my hope.

**6:9** Having begun, let the Lord wound me, yet let Him not destroy me completely.

**6:10** And may my city be a grave, upon whose walls I used to leap. I will not spare; for I have not lied about the holy words of my God.

**6:11** For what is my strength, that I endure? Or what is my time, that my soul bears up?

**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones? Or is my flesh of bronze?

**6:13** Or did I not trust in Him? Yet help is absent from me.

**6:14** Mercy has rejected me, and the visitation of the Lord has overlooked me.

**6:15** Those closest to me did not look upon me; like a failing winter torrent, or like a wave, they passed me by;

**6:16** those who used to revere me have now fallen upon me like snow or frozen ice;

**6:17** just as, having melted when heat came, it was not recognized for what it was,

**6:18** so I also was forsaken by all, and I perished and became homeless.

**6:19** Look at the ways of the Temanites, the paths of the Sabeans, you who observe;

**6:20** and those who trust in cities and wealth will incur shame.

**6:21** Yet you also have set upon me without mercy; therefore, seeing my wound, be afraid.

**6:22** What then? Did I ask anything from you, or am I in need of strength from you,

**6:23** so as to save me from enemies, or to deliver me from the hand of mighty ones?

**6:24** Teach me, and I will be silent; if I have gone astray in anything, tell me.

**6:25** But as it seems, the words of one who is truthful are worthless, for I am not asking for strength from you;

**6:26** nor will your reproof stop me with words, for neither will I bear the sound of a word from you.

**6:27** However, you fall upon an orphan, and you leap upon your friend.

**6:28** But now, looking into your faces, I will not lie.

**6:29** Sit down now, and let there be no injustice, and come together again with the righteous one.

**6:30** For there is no injustice on my tongue; or does my throat not meditate on understanding?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:7** — Greek bromon (a Koine spelling of bromos, 'stench') pairs with osmen ('smell'), giving a distinctive Septuagint reading in which Job's anger cannot cease and his food appears as a stench like a lion's odor.
- **6:9** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, where Job asks God to cut him off, the Greek includes the negative particle me ('yet let Him not destroy me completely').
- **6:10** — The Greek presents distinctive imagery ('may my city be a grave, upon whose walls I used to leap') and renders the following clause as Job affirming he has not lied about God's holy words.
- **6:19** — Greek Thaimanon ('Temanites') and Sabon ('Sabeans') are plural ethnic names; 'Temanites' preserves a distinct Septuagint reading compared with the singular place name Tema in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
