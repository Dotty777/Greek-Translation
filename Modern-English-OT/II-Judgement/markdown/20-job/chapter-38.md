## Text

**38:1** Now after Elihu stopped speaking, the Lord said to Job through a whirlwind and clouds:

**38:2** "Who is this who hides counsel from Me, and holds back words in his heart, and thinks he hides them from Me?

**38:3** Gird your waist like a man; I will ask, and you answer Me.

**38:4** Where were you when I founded the earth? And declare to Me, if you know understanding.

**38:5** Who set its measurements, if you know? Or who is the one who drew a cord over it?

**38:6** Upon what are its rings fixed? And who is the one who laid a cornerstone upon it?

**38:7** When stars came into being, all My angels praised Me with a loud voice.

**38:8** And I enclosed the sea with gates, when it surged violently as it came forth from a mother's womb;

**38:9** and I set a cloud as its clothing, and with mist I swaddled it;

**38:10** and I set boundaries for it, placing bars and gates around it;

**38:11** and I said to it, 'Up to this point you will come, and you will not pass beyond, but within yourself your waves will be shattered.'

**38:12** Or in your time have I commanded the morning light, and did the morning star see its own station,

**38:13** to take hold of the wings of the earth, to shake the ungodly out of it?

**38:14** Or did you take clay of the earth and form a living creature, and set him upon the earth able to speak?

**38:15** And have you taken away the light from the ungodly, and shattered the arm of the arrogant?

**38:16** And have you come to the spring of the sea, and walked in the tracks of the abyss?

**38:17** And do the gates of death open to you in fear, and did the gatekeepers of Hades cower when they saw you?

**38:18** And have you been instructed about the breadth of what is under heaven? Report to Me now just how large it is.

**38:19** And in what land does the light lodge, and what is the place of darkness?

**38:20** Could you lead Me to their boundaries? And do you also know their paths?

**38:21** I know, then, that you were born at that time, and the number of your years is great.

**38:22** And have you come to the treasuries of snow, and have you seen the treasuries of hail?

**38:23** And is it stored up for you for an hour of enemies, for a day of wars and battle?

**38:24** And from where does frost come forth, or is the south wind scattered over what is under heaven?

**38:25** And who prepared a course for a violent rain, and a way of tumults,

**38:26** to send rain upon a land where there is no man, a wilderness in which no human being exists,

**38:27** to satisfy what is untrodden and uninhabited, and to make an outgrowth of tender grass sprout?

**38:28** Who is the father of rain? And who is the one who has given birth to clods of dew?

**38:29** And from whose womb does the ice come forth? And who has given birth to frost in heaven,

**38:30** which comes down like flowing water? And who has terrified the face of an ungodly person?

**38:31** And have you understood the bond of the Pleiades, and have you opened the enclosure of Orion?

**38:32** Or will you open up Mazuroth in its season, and Hesperus by its hair, will you lead them?

**38:33** And do you know the turnings of heaven, or the things under heaven that take place in unison?

**38:34** And will you call a cloud with a voice, and will it obey you with a violent trembling of water?

**38:35** And will you send out thunderbolts, and will they go? And will they say to you, 'What is it?'

**38:36** And who gave women wisdom of weaving, or knowledge of embroidery?

**38:37** And who is the one who counts clouds in wisdom, and bent heaven to the earth?

**38:38** And dust has been poured out like earth, and I have bonded it like a cube to a stone.

**38:39** And will you hunt prey for lions, and fill the souls of dragons?

**38:40** For they are afraid in their dens, and they sit in thickets lying in ambush.

**38:41** And who prepared prey for a raven? For its young cry out to the Lord, wandering about seeking food."

## Translator Notes

- **38:1** — The Greek text opens with an explicit transition from Elihu's speech and states that the Lord spoke "through a whirlwind and clouds," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "out of the whirlwind" without mentioning Elihu or clouds.
- **38:7** — The Greek reads "all My angels" (pantes angeloi mou) where the Hebrew has "all the sons of God," and links the praise to the moment when the stars came into being.
- **38:14** — Unlike the Hebrew image of clay changed under a seal, the Greek depicts forming a living creature from clay and placing him on the earth able to speak, shifting from neuter zoon to masculine auton.
- **38:32** — "Mazuroth" transliterates the Hebrew astronomical term Mazzaroth, while "Hesperus" (the evening star) renders Greek Hesperon (matching 9:9; where Hebrew has the Bear), and "by its hair" literally renders epi komes autou.
- **38:36** — The Greek refers to women's wisdom of weaving and knowledge of embroidery, diverging markedly from the obscure Hebrew terms often translated "inward parts" and "mind" (or "ibis" and "rooster").
