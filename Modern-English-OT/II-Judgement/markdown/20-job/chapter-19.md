## Text

**19:1** Then Job answered and said,

**19:2** "How long will you make my soul weary and tear me down with words? Only know that the Lord has made me like this.

**19:3** You speak against me; without being ashamed before me, you press upon me.

**19:4** Yes, indeed, in truth I have erred, and error lodges with me, to speak words that ought not to be spoken, and my words err and are not in season.

**19:5** Yet let that be, for you magnify yourselves against me; and you leap upon me with reproach.

**19:6** Know therefore that the Lord is the One who has troubled me, and He has raised up His stronghold against me.

**19:7** Look, I laugh at reproach, and I will not speak; I will cry out, and nowhere is there justice.

**19:8** I have been walled in all around, and I will certainly not pass through; He set darkness upon my face.

**19:9** He stripped the glory from me, and He removed a crown from my head.

**19:10** He tore me apart all around, and I was gone; and He cut down my hope like a tree.

**19:11** He dealt with me terribly in anger, and He regarded me like an enemy.

**19:12** Together His raiding bands came against me; men lying in ambush surrounded my ways.

**19:13** My brothers withdrew from me; they recognized strangers rather than me, and my friends have become merciless.

**19:14** Those closest to me did not acknowledge me, and those who knew my name forgot me.

**19:15** Neighbors of the house and my maidservants: I was a foreigner before them.

**19:16** I called my servant, and he did not answer; and my mouth was begging,

**19:17** and I was imploring my wife, and I was calling to the sons of my concubines, flattering them;

**19:18** but they rejected me forever; whenever I rise up, they speak against me.

**19:19** Those who saw me loathed me; the very ones whom I had loved rose up against me.

**19:20** In my skin my flesh rotted, and my bones are held in teeth.

**19:21** Have mercy on me, have mercy on me, friends, for the hand of the Lord is what has touched me.

**19:22** Why do you pursue me just as the Lord also does, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

**19:23** For who would grant that my words be written, and that they be placed in a book forever,

**19:24** with an iron stylus and lead?

**19:25** For I know that the One who is about to release me is eternal.

**19:26** May He raise up on earth my skin that endures these things. For from the Lord these things were accomplished for me,

**19:27** which I myself know within myself, which my eye has seen, and not another; and all things have been accomplished for me in my bosom.

**19:28** And if you also say, 'What will we say before him? And we will find a root of a matter in him,'

**19:29** then you yourselves also beware of a covering; for wrath will come upon the lawless, and then they will know where their substance is."

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — Greek ἔα δὲ ὅτι may be taken as the imperative of ἐάω ('yet let that be, for...') or as an exclamation ('ah, indeed...').
- **19:7** — The Septuagint reads γελῶ ὀνείδει ('I laugh at reproach'), diverging sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'I cry out, Violence!'
- **19:20** — The Septuagint's 'my bones are held in teeth' (ἐν ὀδοῦσιν ἔχεται) leaves the possessor of the teeth unspecified and differs substantially from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **19:26** — Accented as the aorist optative ἀναστήσαι, this expresses a wish ('May He raise up'); accented as the infinitive ἀναστῆσαι, it depends on μέλλων in 19:25 ('about to release me, to raise up on earth my skin').
- **19:29** — Greek ἐπικάλυμμα literally means 'covering,' 'cloak,' or 'pretext' (where the Hebrew has 'sword'), and ὕλη means 'wood,' 'material,' or 'substance.'
