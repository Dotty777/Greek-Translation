## Text

**8:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**8:2** "How long will you speak these things? The wind of your mouth is full of words.

**8:3** Will the Lord act unjustly when He judges? Or will He who made all things disturb justice?

**8:4** If your sons sinned before Him, He sent them away in the hand of their lawlessness.

**8:5** But you, turn early to the Lord Almighty, pleading.

**8:6** If you are pure and truthful, He will listen to your plea, and He will restore to you a dwelling of righteousness;

**8:7** therefore your former state will be small, but your latter state will be beyond telling.

**8:8** For inquire of a former generation, and search out according to the lineage of fathers;

**8:9** for we are from yesterday and do not know, for our life on the earth is a shadow.

**8:10** Or will these not teach you and tell you, and bring out words from the heart?

**8:11** Does papyrus flourish without water? Or will sedge grow tall without drink?

**8:12** While it is still on the root, it will certainly not be harvested; before drinking, does not every plant dry up?

**8:13** Thus, then, will be the latter state of all who forget the Lord; for the hope of an ungodly person will perish.

**8:14** For his house will be uninhabited, and his tent will turn out to be a spider's web.

**8:15** If he props up his house, it will certainly not stand; and when he takes hold of it, it will certainly not endure;

**8:16** for he is moist under the sun, and out of his decay his shoot will come out.

**8:17** On a heap of stones he sleeps, and in the midst of pebbles he will live.

**8:18** If it swallows him up, the place will deceive him; you have not seen such things.

**8:19** For such is the overthrow of an ungodly person, and out of the earth it will cause another to sprout.

**8:20** For the Lord will certainly not reject the innocent person, and He will not accept any gift of an ungodly person.

**8:21** But He will fill the mouth of the truthful with laughter, and their lips with thanksgiving;

**8:22** and their enemies will clothe themselves with shame, and the dwelling of an ungodly person will not exist."

## Translator Notes

- **8:12** — Greek reads "before drinking, does not every plant dry up?" (pro tou piein), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("before any other grass it withers").
- **8:14** — Greek reads "his house will be uninhabited" (aoiketos), diverging from the Hebrew ("his confidence is cut off"); the noun arachne ("spider") denotes a spider's web here.
- **8:16** — Greek reads "out of his decay" (ek saprias autou, also "rottenness"), diverging from the Hebrew ("over his garden").
- **8:18** — The Greek verb katapie ("swallows up") has no explicit subject or object, and the final clause reads "you have not seen such things" rather than direct speech from the place ("I have never seen you").
