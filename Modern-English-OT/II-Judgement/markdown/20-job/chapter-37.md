## Text

**37:1** "At this also my heart was troubled and slipped away from its place.

**37:2** Hear a sound in the anger of the wrath of the Lord, and meditation will go out from His mouth.

**37:3** Beneath the whole heaven is His dominion, and His light is upon the wings of the earth.

**37:4** After it He will cry out with a voice; He will thunder with the voice of His pride, and He will not exchange them, because you will hear His voice.

**37:5** The Mighty One will thunder wondrous things with His voice, for He has done great things that we did not know,

**37:6** commanding the snow, 'Be on the earth,' and a storm of rain, and a storm of the rains of His power.

**37:7** He sets a seal on the hand of every person, so that every person may know his own weakness.

**37:8** And wild beasts entered under shelter, and they grew still in a resting place.

**37:9** Out of chambers pains come, and from summits, cold.

**37:10** And from the breath of the Mighty One He will give frost, and He steers the water however He wishes;

**37:11** and a cloud covers over what is choice. A cloud will scatter His light.

**37:12** And He Himself will turn the circuits in theeboulathoth for their works; all that He commands them, these things have been ordered by Him upon the earth,

**37:13** whether for discipline, whether for His earth, whether for mercy, He will find him.

**37:14** Give ear to these things, Job; stand still, being admonished concerning the power of the Lord.

**37:15** We know that God appointed His works, having made light out of darkness.

**37:16** And He understands the separation of clouds, and the extraordinary downfalls of the wicked.

**37:17** And your robe is warm, and it is quiet upon the earth.

**37:18** You will make firm with Him for ancient things, strong as the appearance of an outpouring.

**37:19** Why? Teach me what we will say to Him, and let us stop saying many things.

**37:20** Does a book or a scribe stand beside me, so that, standing, I should silence a person?

**37:21** And the light is not visible to all; it is radiant in the ancient things, just like that which is from Him upon clouds.

**37:22** From the north, clouds gleaming with gold; upon these, great is the glory and honor of the Almighty.

**37:23** And we do not find another equal to His strength; the One who judges righteous things, do you not think that He listens?

**37:24** Therefore people will fear Him, and the wise in heart will also fear Him."

## Translator Notes

- **37:4** — Greek hybreos literally means "pride" or "arrogance," used here in the Septuagint to render the Hebrew term for "majesty" or "pride."
- **37:12** — Greek theeboulathoth is an untranslated transliteration of the Hebrew noun tahbulot, meaning "steering," "guidance," or "counsels."
- **37:18** — Greek horasis epichyseos literally means "appearance of an outpouring" (or "casting"), rendering the Hebrew imagery of a "molten mirror."
- **37:24** — The Greek reads positively that "the wise in heart will also fear Him," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text states that God does not regard any who are wise of heart.
