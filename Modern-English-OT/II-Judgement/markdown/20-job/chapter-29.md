## Text

**29:1** And continuing further, Job spoke in his prelude:

**29:2** "Who would place me as in a former month of the days in which God guarded me?

**29:3** As when His lamp shone above my head, when by His light I walked in darkness;

**29:4** when I was pressing heavily upon the ways, when God kept watch over my house;

**29:5** when I was exceedingly wooded, and my servants were around me;

**29:6** when my ways flowed with butter, and my mountains flowed with milk;

**29:7** when I went out at dawn in the city, and in the squares my seat was set.

**29:8** Seeing me, young men hid themselves, and all the older men stood up;

**29:9** and prominent men stopped speaking, placing a finger on their mouth.

**29:10** And those who heard called me blessed, and their tongue clung to their throat;

**29:11** for an ear heard and called me blessed, and an eye, seeing me, turned aside.

**29:12** For I rescued a poor person from the hand of a mighty one, and I helped an orphan who had no helper.

**29:13** May the blessing of one who is perishing come upon me, and the mouth of a widow blessed me.

**29:14** And I had put on righteousness, and I clothed myself in justice like a cloak.

**29:15** I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame.

**29:16** I was a father of the powerless, and a case that I did not know I searched out;

**29:17** and I shattered the molars of the unrighteous; from the middle of their teeth I snatched away prey.

**29:18** And I said, 'My lifespan will grow old; like the trunk of a palm tree, I will live a long time;

**29:19** the root is opened wide upon water, and dew will lodge in my harvest;

**29:20** my glory is new with me, and my bow goes forward in his hand.'

**29:21** Hearing me, they paid attention, and they kept silent at my counsel;

**29:22** to my word they did not add, and they became overjoyed whenever I spoke to them.

**29:23** Just as thirsty earth waits for the rain, so did these for my speech.

**29:24** If I laugh at them, they will by no means believe it; and the light of my face did not fall away.

**29:25** I chose their way and sat as chief, and I encamped like a king among light-armed troops, just as one comforting those who suffer."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek prooimion (literally "prelude" or "opening") is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew term for a wisdom discourse or proverb (mashal).
- **29:4** — Greek epibrithon hodous ("pressing heavily upon the ways") and hylodes lian ("exceedingly wooded," v. 5) are distinctive Septuagint metaphors of abundance and vigor, diverging from the Hebrew ("in the days of my prime" and "when the Almighty was still with me").
- **29:6** — The Septuagint reads "my mountains flowed with milk," diverging from the Hebrew ("the rock poured out for me streams of oil").
- **29:18** — The Septuagint reads "My lifespan will grow old; like the trunk of a palm tree," where the Hebrew reads "I shall die in my nest" and refers to sand or a phoenix; in v. 19 the Greek similarly reads "harvest" (therismos) rather than "branches."
- **29:20** — The Greek reads literally "in his hand" (en cheiri autou) where the Hebrew has "in my hand"; ancient Greek interpreters sometimes understood "his hand" as referring to God.
