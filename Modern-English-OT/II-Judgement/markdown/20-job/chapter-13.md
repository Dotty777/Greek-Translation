## Text

**13:1** Look, my eye has seen these things, and my ear has heard them.

**13:2** And I know as much as you also understand, and I am no less intelligent than you.

**13:3** Nevertheless, I will speak to the Lord, and I will argue before Him if He wills.

**13:4** But you are unjust physicians and healers of evils, all of you.

**13:5** Yet may it be yours to keep silent, and it will turn out to be wisdom for you.

**13:6** Hear the argument of my mouth, and pay attention to the judgment of my lips.

**13:7** Do you not speak before the Lord, and before Him utter deceit?

**13:8** Or will you shrink back? Rather, you yourselves become judges.

**13:9** For is it good if He searches you out? For if, doing all things, you attach yourselves to Him,

**13:10** nonetheless He will reprove you; and if even in secret you show partiality,

**13:11** will not His terrors whirl you about? And the dread from Him will fall upon you,

**13:12** and your boasting will turn out like ashes, and your body made of clay.

**13:13** Keep silent, so that I may speak and find rest from anger,

**13:14** taking my flesh in my teeth, and I will place my soul in my hand.

**13:15** If the Mighty One overpowers me, since He has indeed begun, surely I will speak and argue before Him.

**13:16** And this will turn out for my salvation, for deceit will not enter before Him.

**13:17** Hear, hear my words, for I will declare them in your hearing.

**13:18** Look, I am near to my judgment; I know that I will be shown righteous.

**13:19** For who is the one who will contend with me, that now I will keep silent and expire?

**13:20** Yet deal with me in two things; then I will not hide myself from Your face:

**13:21** Keep Your hand away from me, and do not let the dread of You terrify me.

**13:22** Then if You call, I will answer You; or if You speak, I will give You a reply.

**13:23** How many are my sins and my iniquities? Teach me what they are.

**13:24** Why do You hide Yourself from me, and consider me an adversary to You?

**13:25** Or will You be wary as of a leaf moved by wind, or do You stand against me as against grass carried by a gust?

**13:26** For You wrote down evils against me, and placed upon me sins of youth.

**13:27** And You put my foot in a restraint, and You watched all my deeds, and You came to the roots of my feet,

**13:28** which wears out like a wineskin, or like a moth-eaten garment.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Greek qualifies Job's resolve to argue before the Lord with the third-person condition "if He wills" (ἐὰν βούληται), whereas the Hebrew expresses Job's own desire to argue with God.
- **13:9** — The Greek speaks of going to all lengths to attach oneself to God (προστεθήσεσθε αὐτῷ), diverging from the Hebrew comparison to deceiving a human.
- **13:15** — In a notable divergence from the Hebrew ("Though he slay me, I will hope in him"), the Greek affirms that even if the Mighty One overpowers Job, since He has already begun, Job will surely speak and argue before Him.
- **13:22** — In the Greek, God is the subject of both "call" and "speak" and Job responds in both clauses, unlike the Hebrew where Job speaks in the second half.
- **13:28** — The neuter relative pronoun "which" (ὃ) refers back loosely to Job's bodily condition, and the Greek compares it to a "wineskin" (ἀσκῷ) rather than a rotten thing.
