## Text

**16:1** Then Job answered and said:

**16:2** "I have heard many such things; you are all comforters of evils.

**16:3** What then? Is there any order to words of wind? Or what will trouble you that you answer?

**16:4** I also will speak like you. If indeed your soul were put in place of mine, then I would pounce on you with words, and I would shake my head against you.

**16:5** And may there be strength in my mouth, and I will not spare the movement of my lips.

**16:6** For if I speak, I will not feel pain in the wound; and even if I keep silent, in what way will I be wounded less?

**16:7** But now He has made me exhausted, foolish, rotten, and You seized me.

**16:8** It became a testimony; and my falsehood rose up in me; it answered back to my face.

**16:9** Acting in wrath, He cast me down; He gnashed His teeth at me; the arrows of His raiders fell upon me.

**16:10** With the sharp points of His eyes He pounced; with a sharp edge He struck me on the knees, and with one accord they rushed down upon me.

**16:11** For the Lord handed me over into the hands of an unrighteous man, and threw me upon ungodly men.

**16:12** While I was at peace, He scattered me; seizing me by the hair, He plucked it out; He set me up like a target.

**16:13** They surrounded me with spears, striking into my kidneys; without sparing, they poured out my gall onto the ground;

**16:14** they cast me down, fall upon fall; they ran toward me in might.

**16:15** They sewed sackcloth upon my hide, and my strength was extinguished in the earth.

**16:16** My belly has been scorched from weeping, and on my eyelids is a shadow;

**16:17** yet nothing unrighteous was in my hands, and my prayer is pure.

**16:18** Earth, do not cover over the blood of my flesh, nor may there be a place for my cry.

**16:19** And now, look, my witness is in the heavens, and my confidant is in the highest places.

**16:20** May my supplication reach the Lord, and before Him may my eye drip.

**16:21** And may there be an argument for a man before the Lord, and for a son of man with his neighbor.

**16:22** And numbered years have come, and I will go by a way by which I will not return."

## Translator Notes

- **16:6** — Greek οὐκ ἀλγήσω τὸ τραῦμα literally states "I will not feel pain in the wound," contrasting with the Masoretic Text ("my pain is not assuaged").
- **16:8** — Greek τὸ ψεῦδός μου ("my falsehood") translates a Hebrew noun that can mean either "leanness" (as usually understood in the Masoretic Text) or "lying/deception."
- **16:10** — Greek ὀξεῖ ("with a sharp edge/point") and εἰς τὰ γόνατα ("on the knees") preserve a distinct Septuagint reading where the Masoretic Text has "with reproach they have struck my cheeks."
- **16:16** — The Greek reads ἡ γαστήρ μου ("My belly") rather than "My face" and simply σκιά ("a shadow") rather than "shadow of death" as in the Masoretic Text.
- **16:21** — Greek ἔλεγχος denotes a legal "argument," "cross-examination," or "reproof," and the second clause places both "a son of man" and "his neighbor" in the dative (υἱῷ ἀνθρώπου τῷ πλησίον αὐτοῦ), rendering a comparison to pleading between neighbors.
