## Text

**28:1** For there is a place for silver from which it comes, and a place for gold where it is refined.

**28:2** For iron comes from the earth, and copper is quarried like stone.

**28:3** He set an order for darkness, and He Himself closely examines every limit: a stone of gloom and shadow of death,

**28:4** a cleft of a torrent from dust; and those who forget a righteous way grew weak from among mortals.

**28:5** The earth: from it bread will come forth; beneath it, it was turned over like fire.

**28:6** Its stones are a place of sapphire, and it has gold dust.

**28:7** A path: no bird has known it, and the eye of a vulture has not caught sight of it;

**28:8** and sons of the arrogant have not trodden it; a lion has not passed over it.

**28:9** Upon sharp rock He stretched out His hand, and He overturned mountains from the roots;

**28:10** and He broke open whirlpools of rivers, and my eye saw everything precious;

**28:11** and He uncovered the depths of rivers, and He displayed His own power to the light.

**28:12** But from where was wisdom found? And what is the place of knowledge?

**28:13** A mortal does not know its way, nor indeed was it found among humans.

**28:14** The abyss said, "It is not in me," and the sea said, "It is not with me."

**28:15** One will not give an enclosure in place of it, and silver will not be weighed out for it as its exchange;

**28:16** and it will not be weighed against gold of Sophir, with precious onyx and sapphire;

**28:17** gold and glass will not be equaled to it, nor will its exchange be vessels of gold.

**28:18** Lofty things and gabis will not be mentioned, and draw wisdom above the innermost things;

**28:19** topaz of Ethiopia will not be equaled to it; it will not be weighed against pure gold.

**28:20** But from where was wisdom found? And what is the place of understanding?

**28:21** It has escaped the notice of every human, and it was hidden from the birds of heaven.

**28:22** Destruction and death said, "We, however, have heard its report."

**28:23** God well established its way, and He Himself knows its place.

**28:24** For He Himself looks upon all that is under heaven, knowing all the things on the earth

**28:25** He made, a weight of winds, measures of water,

**28:26** when He made them; thus, having seen, He numbered them, and a way in a shaking, sounds.

**28:27** Then He saw it and declared to it; having prepared it, He searched it out.

**28:28** And He said to humanity, "Look, reverence for God is wisdom, and to abstain from evil things is knowledge."

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — The Greek leaves the third-person subject of vv. 3 and 9-11 unexpressed. While the Hebrew imagery is commonly read of a human miner, the Septuagint wording ("set an order for darkness" in v. 3, and "displayed His own power to the light" in v. 11) presents these as actions of God.
- **28:15** — Greek synkleismos ("enclosure" or "something shut up") translates the rare Hebrew term for choice gold (segor) etymologically through the root "to shut/enclose."
- **28:16** — "Sophir" (Greek Sopheir) is the distinct Septuagint form of the place name Ophir.
- **28:18** — The Greek renders rare Hebrew gem terms literally or by transliteration: meteora ("lofty things") for coral, gabeis ("gabis," transliterated) for crystal, and ta esotata ("the innermost things") for pearls, while taking the Hebrew noun for "acquisition" as the imperative helkyson ("draw").
- **28:25** — The Greek text lacks a relative pronoun before "He made" (epoiesen, where some witnesses read ha epoiesen, "which He made"), and v. 26 retains the accusative plural phonas ("sounds") alongside "a way" (where some witnesses read the genitive "of sounds").
