## Text

**11:1** Then Zophar the Minaean answered and said:

**11:2** "The one who says many things will also hear in return; or does the eloquent one also think he is righteous?

**11:3** Blessed is the short-lived one born of a woman. Do not be abundant in words, for there is no one who answers you back.

**11:4** For do not say, 'I am pure in my works and blameless before Him.'

**11:5** But how would the Lord speak to you? And will He open His lips with you?

**11:6** Then He will declare to you the power of wisdom, for He will be double what concerns you; and then you will know that things worthy of what you have sinned have come upon you from the Lord.

**11:7** Or will you find a trace of the Lord? Or have you reached the utmost limits that the Almighty made?

**11:8** Heaven is high, and what will you do? And they are deeper than the things in Hades; what do you know?

**11:9** Or longer than the measure of the earth or the breadth of the sea.

**11:10** And if He overturns all things, who will say to Him, 'What have You done?'

**11:11** For He Himself knows the works of the lawless, and seeing wrongful things, He will not overlook them.

**11:12** But a man swims idly in words, and a mortal born of a woman is like a desert donkey.

**11:13** For if you have made your heart pure, and hold up your hands toward Him;

**11:14** if there is anything lawless in your hands, put it far away from you, and do not let injustice lodge in your dwelling.

**11:15** For in this way your face will shine like pure water, and you will put off filth and will certainly not be afraid;

**11:16** and you will forget your toil like a wave that has passed by, and you will not be terrified;

**11:17** and your prayer will be like the morning star, and from midday life will rise for you;

**11:18** and you will be confident that there is hope for you, and out of anxiety and care peace will appear for you.

**11:19** For you will be at rest, and there will be no one fighting against you; and many, turning around, will plead with you.

**11:20** But salvation will forsake them; for their hope is destruction, and the eyes of the ungodly will melt away."

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Septuagint identifies Zophar as "the Minaean" (Greek ho Meinaios, referring to the Minaeans of Arabia) rather than the Hebrew "Naamathite."
- **11:3** — The opening beatitude ("Blessed is the short-lived one born of a woman") is a distinctive Septuagint reading with no direct counterpart in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **11:6** — The Greek literally states "for He will be double of the things concerning you," and the following clause asserts that what has come upon Job from the Lord is worthy of his sins (unlike the Hebrew, which speaks of God exacting less than guilt deserves).
- **11:12** — Literally "a man swims idly in words" (allos nechetai logois), a vivid Septuagint rendering distinct from the Hebrew proverb about an empty man gaining understanding.
