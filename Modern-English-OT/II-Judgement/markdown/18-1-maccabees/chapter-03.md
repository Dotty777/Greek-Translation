## Text

**3:1** And his son Judas, who was called Maccabeus, rose up in his place.

**3:2** And all his brothers and all who had joined his father helped him, and they fought the battle of Israel with gladness.

**3:3** And he extended glory for his people, and put on a breastplate like a giant, and strapped on his weapons of war; he waged battles, protecting the camp with a sword.

**3:4** And he was like a lion in his deeds, and like a young lion roaring for prey.

**3:5** And he pursued lawless men, searching them out, and set ablaze those who troubled his people.

**3:6** And the lawless shrank back from fear of him, and all the workers of lawlessness were thrown into turmoil, and deliverance prospered in his hand.

**3:7** And he embittered many kings, and made Jacob glad by his deeds, and forever his memory is for a blessing.

**3:8** And he passed through the cities of Judah, and utterly destroyed ungodly men upon it, and turned away wrath from Israel.

**3:9** And he became renowned to the end of the earth, and he gathered together those who were perishing.

**3:10** And Apollonius gathered nations, and from Samaria a great force, to fight against Israel.

**3:11** And Judas learned of it, and went out to meet him, and struck him and killed him; and many fell wounded, and the rest fled.

**3:12** And they took their equipment, and Judas took the sword of Apollonius, and he fought with it all his days.

**3:13** And Seron, the commander of the force of Syria, heard that Judas had mustered a company and an assembly of faithful men who were with him and going out to battle.

**3:14** And he said, "I will make a name for myself, and I will be glorified in the kingdom, and I will fight against Judas and those with him, and those who despise the word of the king."

**3:15** And he proceeded, and a strong army of ungodly men went up with him to help him, to execute vengeance on the sons of Israel.

**3:16** And he drew near to the ascent of Methoron, and Judas went out to meet him with very few men.

**3:17** Now when they saw the army coming to meet him, they said to Judas, "How will we, being so few, be able to fight against so great a multitude? And we ourselves are faint, having gone without food today."

**3:18** And Judas said, "It is easy for many to be shut up in the hands of a few, and there is no difference in the sight of Heaven to save by many or by few.

**3:19** For victory in battle is not in the size of a force, but rather strength is from Heaven.

**3:20** They come against us in an abundance of arrogance and lawlessness, to destroy us and our wives and our children, to plunder us.

**3:21** But we fight for our lives and our laws.

**3:22** And He Himself will crush them before our face; as for you, do not be afraid of them."

**3:23** Now when he finished speaking, he leaped upon them suddenly, and Seron and his army were crushed before him.

**3:24** And they pursued them down the descent of Beth-horon as far as the plain, and from among them about eight hundred men fell; and the rest fled into the land of the Philistines.

**3:25** And the fear of Judas and his brothers began, and terror fell upon the nations around them.

**3:26** And his name reached as far as the king, and every nation told of the battles of Judas.

**3:27** Now when King Antiochus heard these reports, he became enraged with fury, and he sent and gathered all the forces of his kingdom, an exceedingly strong army.

**3:28** And he opened his treasury and gave pay to his forces for the year, and he commanded them to be ready for every need for a year.

**3:29** And he saw that the silver had run out from the treasuries, and the tributes of the country were small because of the dissension and calamity that he had brought about in the land, to remove the laws that had been from the earliest days.

**3:30** And he feared that he would not have enough, as once and twice before, for the expenses and the gifts that he had previously given with a lavish hand.

**3:31** And he was greatly perplexed in his soul, and he resolved to go to Persia and to collect the tributes of the regions and to gather much silver.

**3:32** And he left Lysias, a distinguished man and of royal descent, over the affairs of the king from the river Euphrates as far as the borders of Egypt,

**3:33** and to raise his son Antiochus until he returned.

**3:34** And he handed over to him half of the forces and the elephants, and commanded him concerning everything that he wished, and concerning all those who inhabited Judea and Jerusalem,

**3:35** to send a force against them to cast out and destroy the strength of Israel and the remnant of Jerusalem, and to remove its memory from the place,

**3:36** and to settle foreigners in all their borders, and to apportion their land by lot.

**3:37** And the king took the half of the forces that remained, and departed from Antioch, from the city of his kingdom, in the one hundred forty-seventh year; and he crossed the Euphrates River and traveled through the upper regions.

**3:38** And Lysias chose Ptolemy son of Dorymenes, Nicanor, and Gorgias, mighty men from among the friends of the king.

**3:39** And he sent with them forty thousand men and seven thousand cavalry to go into the land of Judah and destroy it according to the word of the king.

**3:40** And they departed with all their force and came and encamped near Emmaus in the plain country.

**3:41** And the merchants of the country heard of their fame, and took a very great amount of silver and gold, and servants, and came into the camp to take the sons of Israel as servants; and forces of Syria and of the land of the foreigners joined them.

**3:42** And Judas and his brothers saw that evils had multiplied and that the forces would encamp within their borders; and they learned of the words of the kingdom that he had commanded to do to the people for destruction and complete ruin.

**3:43** And each one said to his neighbor, "Let us raise up the ruin of our people and of the sanctuary."

**3:44** And the congregation was assembled to be ready for battle, and to pray and ask for mercy and compassion.

**3:45** And Jerusalem was uninhabited like a wilderness; there was not one of her offspring going in or going out; and the sanctuary was trampled down, and sons of foreigners were in the citadel, a lodging place among the nations; and delight was taken away from Jacob, and flute and lyre ceased.

**3:46** And they gathered together and came to Mizpah, opposite Jerusalem, because formerly there was a place of prayer in Mizpah for Israel.

**3:47** And they fasted on that day, and put on sackcloth and put ashes on their heads, and tore their garments.

**3:48** And they spread open the book of the law concerning the things about which the nations were consulting the likenesses of their idols.

**3:49** And they brought the garments of the priesthood and the firstfruits and the tithes, and they raised up the Nazirites who had completed the days.

**3:50** And they cried out aloud to Heaven, saying, "What should we do with these, and where should we take them?

**3:51** And Your sanctuary has been trampled down and profaned, and Your priests are in mourning and humiliation.

**3:52** And look, the nations have gathered together against us to destroy us; You know what they plot against us.

**3:53** How will we be able to stand before their face, unless You help us?"

**3:54** And they sounded the trumpets and cried out with a loud voice.

**3:55** And after this Judas appointed leaders of the people: commanders of thousands, and commanders of hundreds, and commanders of fifties, and commanders of tens.

**3:56** And he told those who were building a house, and those who were betrothed to wives, and those who were planting vineyards, and those who were fearful, to return, each one to his house, according to the law.

**3:57** And the army departed, and they encamped to the south of Emmaus.

**3:58** And Judas said, "Arm yourselves and be valiant men, and be ready for the morning to fight against these nations who have assembled against us to destroy us and our sanctuary.

**3:59** For it is better for us to die in battle than to look upon the evils of our nation and of the sanctuary.

**3:60** But as the will in Heaven may be, so He will do."

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — The Greek text reads 'upon it' (feminine singular, referring to the land of Judah); some manuscripts read 'out of it.'
- **3:15** — Greek 'and he proceeded, and ... went up' reflects a Hebraic idiom expressing continuation or addition (often understood as 'and moreover a strong army ... went up with him').
- **3:16** — The Greek text reads 'Methoron,' a textual variant of 'Beth-horon' (compare 3:24).
- **3:25** — The Greek text has two finite verbs ('began ... and terror fell'); some witnesses read an infinitive ('the fear ... and terror began to fall').
- **3:30** — Greek 'as once and twice' is a Hebraic idiom meaning 'as on former occasions.' The Greek sentence continues into verse 31.
- **3:35** — The Greek text reads 'to cast out' and singular 'its memory'; some manuscripts read 'to wipe out' and 'their memory.'
- **3:41** — The Greek text reads 'servants' (or 'slaves'), whereas some ancient versions read 'fetters.' 'Foreigners' translates Greek allophyloi, the standard Septuagint term used for the Philistines (contrast 'Philistines' for Phylistieim in 3:24).
- **3:43** — Some manuscripts add 'and let us fight for our people' before 'and of the sanctuary.'
- **3:48** — This clause is debated; the Greek literally indicates that they spread open the book of the law concerning the matters about which the nations consulted the likenesses of their idols.
