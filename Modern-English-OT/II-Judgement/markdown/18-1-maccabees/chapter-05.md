## Text

**5:1** And it happened, when the nations all around heard that the altar had been built and the sanctuary had been dedicated as before, that they became very angry.

**5:2** And they plotted to do away with the race of Jacob, those who were in their midst, and they began to put to death and to wipe out those among the people.

**5:3** And Judas waged war against the sons of Esau in Judea, at Akrabattene, because they were besieging Israel; and he struck them with a great blow, and subdued them, and took their plunder.

**5:4** And he remembered the wickedness of the sons of Baean, who were a snare and a stumbling block to the people by lying in ambush for them on the roads.

**5:5** And they were shut up by him in the towers, and he encamped against them, and devoted them to destruction, and burned its towers with fire along with all who were inside.

**5:6** And he crossed over against the sons of Ammon, and found a strong hand and a numerous people, and Timothy as their leader.

**5:7** And he engaged in many battles with them, and they were crushed before him, and he struck them down.

**5:8** And he seized Jaze and its daughters, and returned to Judea.

**5:9** And the nations that were in Gilead gathered together against Israel, those who were within their borders, to wipe them out; and they fled to Dathema, the fortress.

**5:10** And they sent letters to Judas and his brothers, saying, "The nations around us have gathered together against us to wipe us out,

**5:11** and they are preparing to come and seize the fortress into which we fled; and Timothy leads their army.

**5:12** Now therefore, come and deliver us from their hand, because a multitude of us has fallen.

**5:13** And all our brothers who were in the territory of Tubias have been put to death, and they have taken captive their wives and their children, along with their belongings, and they destroyed about one thousand men there."

**5:14** The letters were still being read, and look, other messengers arrived from Galilee, having torn their garments, reporting according to these words,

**5:15** saying, "Those from Ptolemais and Tyre and Sidon, and all Galilee of the foreigners, have gathered together against them to annihilate us."

**5:16** Now when Judas and the people heard these words, a great assembly gathered together to deliberate on what they should do for their brothers who were in distress and being attacked by him.

**5:17** And Judas said to Simon his brother, "Choose men for yourself, and go and rescue your brothers in Galilee; while Jonathan my brother and I will go into Gilead."

**5:18** And he left Joseph the son of Zechariah, and Azariah, a leader of the people, with the remainder of the force in Judea for protection;

**5:19** and he commanded them, saying, "Take charge of this people, and do not engage in battle with the nations until we return."

**5:20** And three thousand men were allotted to Simon to go into Galilee, and to Judas, eight thousand men into Gilead.

**5:21** And Simon went into Galilee, and engaged in many battles with the nations; and the nations were crushed before him.

**5:22** And he pursued them as far as the cities of Ptolemais; and about three thousand men from the nations fell, and he took their plunder.

**5:23** And he took them from Galilee and in Arbacta, with the wives and the children and all that belonged to them, and they brought them into Judea with great gladness.

**5:24** And Judas Maccabeus and Jonathan his brother crossed the Jordan, and went a journey of three days in the wilderness.

**5:25** And they encountered the Nabataeans, who met them peacefully and recounted to them all the things that had happened to their brothers in Gilead,

**5:26** and that many of them have been shut up in Bozrah and Bosor, in Alama, Casphor, Makeb, and Carnaim; all these cities are fortified and large.

**5:27** And in the remaining cities of Gilead they have been shut up; for tomorrow they are set to encamp against the fortresses, and to capture them and wipe out all of them in one day.

**5:28** And Judas and his army suddenly turned onto the road into the wilderness to Bosor; and he captured the city, and killed every male by the edge of the sword, and took all their plunder, and burned it with fire.

**5:29** And he departed from there by night, and marched all the way to the fortress.

**5:30** And morning came, and they lifted up their eyes, and look, a numerous people of whom there was no number, carrying ladders and siege engines to capture the fortress; and they were attacking them.

**5:31** And Judas saw that the battle had begun, and the cry of the city went up to heaven with trumpets and a loud cry;

**5:32** and he said to the men of the force, "Fight today for our brothers."

**5:33** And he went out in three divisions from behind them; and he blew the trumpets, and they cried out in prayer.

**5:34** And the army of Timothy recognized that it was Maccabeus, and they fled from before him, and he struck them with a great blow; and about eight thousand men from among them fell on that day.

**5:35** And he turned aside to Maapha and fought against it, and captured it, and killed every male in it, and took its plunder, and burned it with fire.

**5:36** From there he departed and seized Chasphoth, Maked, Bosor, and the remaining cities of Gilead.

**5:37** Now after these things Timothy gathered another army, and encamped opposite Raphon on the other side of the stream.

**5:38** And Judas sent to spy out the camp; and they reported to him, saying, "All the nations around you have gathered together to them, a very large force.

**5:39** And Arabs have been hired to help them, and they are encamped on the other side of the stream, ready to come against you for battle." And Judas went to meet them.

**5:40** And Timothy said to the commanders of his force, as Judas and his army drew near to the stream of water, "If he crosses over to us first, we will not be able to withstand him, because he will surely prevail against us.

**5:41** But if he is afraid and encamps on the other side of the river, we will cross over to him."

**5:42** Now when Judas drew near to the stream of water, he stationed the scribes of the people by the stream, and commanded them, saying, "Do not allow any man to encamp, but let all come into the battle."

**5:43** And he crossed over to them first, and all his people in front of him; and all the nations were crushed before him, and they threw down all their weapons, and fled into the shrine in Carnaim.

**5:44** And they seized the city, and burned the shrine with fire along with all who were in it; and Carnaim was routed, and could no longer stand before Judas.

**5:45** Then Judas gathered all Israel, those who were in Gilead, from the least to the greatest, and their wives and their children and their belongings, a very great camp, to go into the land of Judah.

**5:46** And they came as far as Ephron, and this city was large, the one at the entrance, very strong; there was no way to turn aside from it to the right or to the left, but only to go through the middle of it.

**5:47** And those from the city shut them out, and blocked up the gates with stones.

**5:48** And Judas sent to them with peaceful words, saying, "I will pass through your land to go to our own land; and no one will harm you; only on foot will we pass through." And they were not willing to open to him.

**5:49** And Judas gave orders to proclaim in the camp that each one should encamp in the place where he was.

**5:50** And the men of the city encamped, and they fought against the city all that day and all the night, and the city was delivered into his hands.

**5:51** And he destroyed every male by the edge of the sword, and uprooted it, and took the plunder of the city, and passed through the city over those who had been killed.

**5:52** And they crossed the Jordan into the great plain opposite Beth-shan.

**5:53** And Judas kept gathering together those who were lagging behind and encouraging the people along the whole way, until he came into the land of Judah.

**5:54** And they went up to Mount Zion with gladness and joy. And they offered burnt offerings, because not one of them had fallen until they returned in peace.

**5:55** And in the days when Judas and Jonathan were in Gilead, and Simon his brother was in Galilee opposite Ptolemais,

**5:56** Joseph the son of Zechariah and Zechariah, commanders of the forces, heard of the brave deeds and of the warfare, what they had done;

**5:57** and they said, "We also will make a name for ourselves, and let us go to fight against the nations around us."

**5:58** And they gave orders to those of the force that was with them, and went against Jamnia.

**5:59** And Gorgias came out of the city, and his men, to meet them for battle.

**5:60** And Joseph and Azariah were routed, and they were pursued as far as the borders of Judea; and on that day about two thousand men from the people of Israel fell;

**5:61** and there was a great rout among the people, because they did not listen to Judas and his brothers, supposing that he would act bravely.

**5:62** But they were not of the seed of those men to whom the deliverance of Israel was given through their hand.

**5:63** And the man Judas and his brothers were greatly honored before all Israel and all the nations, wherever their name was heard;

**5:64** and they kept gathering together to them, praising them.

**5:65** And Judas and his brothers went out, and fought against the sons of Esau in the land toward the south, and he struck Hebron and its daughters; and he tore down its fortress, and burned its towers all around.

**5:66** And he departed to go into the land of the foreigners, and was passing through Samaria.

**5:67** On that day priests fell in battle while he wished to act bravely, when he went out into battle rashly.

**5:68** And Judas turned aside to Ashdod, the land of the foreigners, and tore down their altars, and burned up the carved images of their gods, and plundered the spoils of the cities, and returned to Judea.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Greek text reads "in Judea" (en te Ioudaia), whereas other witnesses read "in Idumea" (Idoumaia).
- **5:8** — The Greek text reads "Jaze" (Iazen), where other witnesses read "Jazer" (Iazer); "its daughters" is a biblical idiom for dependent surrounding towns.
- **5:15** — The Greek shifts abruptly from third-person "against them" (ep' autous) to first-person "us" (hemas) within the messengers' report, where some witnesses smooth the first pronoun to "against us."
- **5:16** — The Greek text reads singular "by him" (hup' autou, apparently referring back to Timothy in v. 11 or to a collective enemy), while other witnesses read plural "by them" (hup' auton).
- **5:22** — The Greek text reads "the cities of Ptolemais" (ton poleon Ptolemaidos), whereas other witnesses read "the gate of Ptolemais" (tes pyles Ptolemaidos).
- **5:26** — The Greek forms "Alama" (Alamois) and "Makeb" (Makeb) correspond to "Alema" and "Maked" (Maked) in other witnesses; Bozrah (Bossora) and Bosor (Bossor) are two distinct Transjordanian towns.
- **5:35** — The Greek text reads "Maapha" (Maapha), whereas other witnesses read "Maspha" or "Malle."
- **5:36** — The Greek text reads "Chasphoth" (Chasphoth) and "Maked" (Maked), variant forms of "Casphor" and "Makeb" in v. 26.
- **5:43** — The Greek text reads "all his people in front of him" (emprosthen autou), whereas other witnesses read "behind him" (opisthen autou).
- **5:50** — The Greek text reads "the men of the city" (hoi andres tes poleos), whereas other witnesses read "the men of the force" (hoi andres tes dunameos).
- **5:56** — The Greek text reads "Zechariah" (Zacharias) for the second commander, whereas other witnesses (and vv. 18, 60) read "Azariah" (Azarias).
- **5:67** — The Greek text has singular pronouns ("while he wished ... when he went out"), whereas other witnesses read plural forms referring to the priests (compare also the singular "he" [auton] in v. 61, omitted in some witnesses).
