## Text

**7:1** In the fifty-first year, Demetrius the son of Seleucus went out from Rome and went up with a few men to a coastal city, and reigned there.

**7:2** And it happened that as he was entering the royal house of his fathers, the forces seized Antiochus and Lysias to bring them to him.

**7:3** And the matter became known to him, and he said, "Do not show me their faces."

**7:4** And the forces killed them, and Demetrius sat on the throne of his kingdom.

**7:5** And all the lawless and ungodly men from Israel came to him, and Alcimus was leading them, wanting to serve as priest.

**7:6** And they accused the people to the king, saying, "Judas and his brothers have destroyed all your friends, and he has scattered us from our land.

**7:7** Now therefore, send a man whom you trust, and let him go and see all the destruction that he has done to us and to the king's country, and he has punished them and all those who help them."

**7:8** And the king chose Bacchides, one of the king's friends, who was ruling in the region beyond the River, and was great in the kingdom and faithful to the king.

**7:9** And he sent him and Alcimus the ungodly, and they established the priesthood for him, and he commanded him to carry out the vengeance on the sons of Israel.

**7:10** And he departed and came with a large force into the land of Judah; and he sent messengers to Judas and to his brothers with peaceful words, deceitfully.

**7:11** And they did not pay attention to their words, for they saw that they had come with a large force.

**7:12** And an assembly of scribes gathered together to Alcimus and Bacchides to seek what was just.

**7:13** And the Hasideans were the first among the sons of Israel, and they sought peace from them;

**7:14** for they said, "A man who is a priest from the seed of Aaron has come with the forces, and he will not wrong us."

**7:15** And he spoke peaceful words with them and swore to them, saying, "We will not seek evil against you or your friends."

**7:16** And they trusted him, and he seized sixty men from among them and killed them in one day, according to the words that he wrote:

**7:17** "The flesh of Your godly ones and their blood they poured out around Jerusalem, and there was no one to bury them."

**7:18** And fear of them and trembling fell upon all the people, because they said, "There is no truth or justice in them, for they have violated the agreement and the oath that they swore."

**7:19** And Bacchides set out from Jerusalem and encamped at Bezeth, and he sent and seized many of the men with him who had deserted, and some of the people, and slaughtered them into the great cistern.

**7:20** And he committed the country to Alcimus and left a force with him to help him; and Bacchides departed to the king.

**7:21** And Alcimus contended for the high priesthood.

**7:22** And all those who were troubling their people gathered to him, and they gained control of the land of Judah and inflicted a great blow in Israel.

**7:23** And Judas saw all the evil that Alcimus and those with him had done among the sons of Israel, more than the nations;

**7:24** and he went out into all the territory of Judea and all around, and carried out vengeance on the men and on those who had deserted, and they were restrained from going into the country.

**7:25** Now when Alcimus saw that Judas and those with him had grown strong, and knew that he was able to withstand them, he returned to the king and accused them of wicked things.

**7:26** And the king sent Nicanor, one of his honored officials and one who hated and was hostile to Israel, and he commanded him to wipe out the people.

**7:27** And Nicanor came to Jerusalem with a large force, and sent to Judas and his brothers deceitfully with peaceful words, saying,

**7:28** "Let there be no fighting between me and you; I will come with a few men so that I may see your faces in peace."

**7:29** And he came to Judas, and they greeted one another peacefully; and the enemies were ready to snatch Judas away.

**7:30** And the matter became known to Judas, that he had come against him deceitfully; and he was alarmed because of him, and did not want to see his face again.

**7:31** And Nicanor knew that his plan had been revealed, and he went out to meet Judas near Charpharsarama.

**7:32** And about five thousand men of those with Nicanor fell, and they fled into the city of David.

**7:33** And after these things Nicanor went up to Mount Zion; and some of the priests came out from the sanctuary, and some of the elders of the people, to greet him peacefully and to show him the burnt offering that was being offered on behalf of the king.

**7:34** And he mocked them, and laughed at them, and defiled them, and spoke arrogantly.

**7:35** And he swore in fury, saying, "Unless Judas and his army are handed over into my hands right now, then it will be that if I return in peace, I will burn down this house." And he went out in fury.

**7:36** And the priests went in and stood before the altar and the temple, and wept and said,

**7:37** "You chose this house for Your name to be called upon it, to be a house of prayer and supplication for Your people.

**7:38** Carry out vengeance on this man and on his army, and let them fall by the sword; remember their blasphemies, and do not grant them survival."

**7:39** And Nicanor went out from Jerusalem, and they encamped at Beth-horon, and a force from Syria met him.

**7:40** And Judas encamped at Adasa with three thousand men; and Judas prayed and said,

**7:41** "When those from the king blasphemed, Your angel went out and struck down among them one hundred and eighty-five thousand.

**7:42** In the same way, crush this army before us today; and let the rest know that he spoke evil against Your sanctuary, and judge him according to his evil."

**7:43** And the armies joined in battle on the thirteenth of the month of Adar, and Nicanor's army was crushed; and he himself fell first in the battle.

**7:44** Now when his army saw that Nicanor had fallen, they threw down their weapons and fled.

**7:45** And they pursued them a day's journey, from Adasa until they came to Gazara, and they sounded the signal trumpets behind them.

**7:46** And people came out from all the villages of Judea all around and outflanked them, and these turned back toward those; and they all fell by the sword, and not even one of them was left.

**7:47** And they took the spoils and the plunder, and they cut off Nicanor's head and his right hand, which he had stretched out arrogantly, and brought them and stretched them out beside Jerusalem.

**7:48** And the people rejoiced greatly, and they observed that day as a great day of gladness.

**7:49** And they decreed to observe this day every year on the thirteenth of Adar.

**7:50** And the land of Judah had rest for a few days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek text reads "the fifty-first year," omitting "and hundredth" found in other Greek witnesses (which date the event to year 151 of the Seleucid era, 162/161 BC).
- **7:7** — The Greek text reads the aorist indicative "he has punished" (ekolasato) rather than the imperative "let him punish" (kolasato) found in other witnesses.
- **7:16** — The active verb "he wrote" introduces the citation of Psalm 79:2-3 (LXX 78:2-3) in verse 17, referring to the psalmist or Scripture.
- **7:25** — The Greek text reads "that he was able to withstand them," lacking the negation "not" (ou) present in other Greek witnesses.
- **7:31** — The Greek text reads the place-name "Charpharsarama," whereas other Greek witnesses read "Capharsalama."
