## Text

**9:1** And Demetrius heard that Nicanor and his forces had made war, and he proceeded to send Bacchides and Alcimus a second time into the land of Judah, and the right wing with them.

**9:2** And they went along the road to Gilgal, and they encamped against Messaloth, which is in Arbela, and captured it, and he destroyed many human lives.

**9:3** And in the first month of the one hundred and fifty-second year, they encamped at Jerusalem.

**9:4** And they set out and went to Berea with twenty thousand men and two thousand horses.

**9:5** And Judas was encamped at Alasa, and three thousand chosen men were with him.

**9:6** And they saw the multitude of the forces, that they were many, and they were greatly afraid; and many slipped away from the camp; none of them were left except eight hundred men.

**9:7** And Judas saw that his camp had melted away, and the battle was pressing him; and he was crushed in heart, because he had no time to gather them together.

**9:8** And he grew faint, and he said to those who were left, "Let us rise and go up against our adversaries, if perhaps we may be able to fight them."

**9:9** And they tried to turn him back, saying, "We will by no means be able; rather, let us save our own lives for now. Let us return with our brothers and fight against them; for we are few."

**9:10** And Judas said, "Far be it from me to do this thing, to flee from them; and our time has drawn near, and let us die with courage for the sake of our brothers, and let us not leave a reproach against our glory."

**9:11** And the force set out from the camp, and they took their stand to meet them, and the cavalry was divided into two divisions, and the slingers and the archers went ahead of the force, and the front-line fighters, all the mighty men.

**9:12** Now Bacchides was on the right wing, and the phalanx drew near from the two sides, and they were sounding the trumpets; and those on Judas's side themselves also blew the trumpets.

**9:13** And the earth was shaken by their camps, and the battle was joined from morning until evening.

**9:14** And Judas saw that Bacchides and the strength of the camp were on the right, and all those courageous in heart came together with him.

**9:15** And the right wing was crushed by them, and he pursued after them as far as the mountain of Ashdod.

**9:16** And those on the left wing saw that the right wing was crushed, and they turned close on the heels of Judas and those with him from the rear.

**9:17** And the battle grew heavy, and many fell wounded from one side and the other.

**9:18** And Judas fell, and the rest fled.

**9:19** And Jonathan and Simon took up Judas his brother and buried him in the tomb of his fathers in Modein.

**9:20** And they wept for him there, and all Israel lamented him with a great lamentation, and they mourned for many days and said,

**9:21** "How a mighty man has fallen, who saved Israel!"

**9:22** And the rest of the acts of Judas, and of the wars and the valiant deeds that he did, and of his greatness, were not written down, for they were very many.

**9:23** And it came about after the death of Judas that the lawless emerged in all the borders of Israel, and all those who practiced unrighteousness sprang up.

**9:24** In those days there was a very great famine, and the country defected with them.

**9:25** And Bacchides chose the ungodly men and appointed them lords of the country.

**9:26** And they sought and searched out the friends of Judas and brought them to Bacchides; and he took vengeance on them, and they mocked them.

**9:27** And there was great distress in Israel, such as had not occurred since the day no prophet appeared to them.

**9:28** And all the friends of Judas assembled and said to Jonathan,

**9:29** "Since your brother Judas has died, there is no man like him to go out against the enemies and Bacchides, and against those who are hostile to our nation.

**9:30** Now therefore, we have chosen you today to be in his place as ruler and leader for us, to fight our battle."

**9:31** And Jonathan accepted the leadership at that time, and he rose up in place of Judas his brother.

**9:32** And Bacchides learned of it, and he sought to kill him.

**9:33** And Jonathan and Simon his brother and all those with him learned of it, and they fled into the wilderness of Tekoa and encamped by the water of the cistern of Asphal.

**9:34** And Bacchides learned of it on the Sabbath day, and he himself and all his army came across the Jordan.

**9:35** And he sent his brother as leader of the crowd, and he appealed to the Nabataeans, his friends, that he might deposit their abundant baggage with them.

**9:36** And the sons of Jambrin came out from Medeba and seized John and all that he had, and they went away with them.

**9:37** Now after these things, they reported to Jonathan and to Simon his brother, "The sons of Jambrin are holding a great wedding and are bringing the bride from Nadabath, the daughter of one of the great nobles of Canaan, with a great escort."

**9:38** And they remembered John their brother, and they went up and hid under the cover of the mountain.

**9:39** And they lifted up their eyes and looked, and look, there was a tumult and much baggage; and the bridegroom came out, and his friends and his brothers, to meet them with tambourines and musical instruments and many weapons.

**9:40** And they rose up against them from the ambush and killed them; and many fell wounded, and the rest fled to the mountain, and they took all their goods.

**9:41** And the wedding was turned into mourning, and the sound of their musical instruments into a lament.

**9:42** And they took vengeance for the blood of their brother, and they returned to the mountain of the Jordan.

**9:43** And Bacchides heard of it, and he came on the Sabbath day as far as the banks of the Jordan with a great force.

**9:44** And Jonathan said to his brothers, "Let us rise now and fight for our lives, for today is not like yesterday and the day before.

**9:45** For look, the battle is in front of us and behind us, and the water of the Jordan is on this side and on that, and marsh and thicket, and there is no place to turn aside.

**9:46** Now therefore, cry out to Heaven, so that you may be delivered from the hand of our enemies."

**9:47** And the battle was joined, and Jonathan stretched out his hand to strike Bacchides, but he turned aside backward away from him.

**9:48** And Jonathan and those with him leaped into the Jordan and swam across to the other side, and they did not cross the Jordan against them.

**9:49** And about a thousand men fell on Bacchides's side on that day.

**9:50** And they returned to Jerusalem and built fortified cities in Judea: the fortress that is in Jericho, and Emmaus, and Beth-horon, and Bethel, and Thamnatha Pharathon, and Tephon, with high walls and gates and bars.

**9:51** And they placed a garrison in them to be hostile to Israel.

**9:52** And they fortified the city, and Beth-zur, and Gazara, and the citadel, and they placed in them forces and stores of food.

**9:53** And they took the sons of the leaders of the country as hostages, and they placed them in the citadel in Jerusalem in custody.

**9:54** And in the one hundred and fifty-third year, in the second month, Alcimus gave orders to tear down the wall of the inner court of the sanctuary, and he tore down the works of the prophets, and he began the tearing down.

**9:55** At that time Alcimus was struck, and his works were hindered, and his mouth was shut, and he was paralyzed, and he was no longer able to speak a word or to give instructions concerning his house.

**9:56** And Alcimus died at that time with great torment.

**9:57** And Bacchides saw that Alcimus had died, and he returned to the king, and the land of Judah was quiet for two years.

**9:58** And all the lawless took counsel, saying, "Look, Jonathan and those on his side are dwelling in quietness, confident; now therefore, we will bring Bacchides, and he will seize them all in one night."

**9:59** And they went and took counsel with him.

**9:60** And he set out to come with a great force, and he sent letters secretly to all his allies who were in Judea, so that they might seize Jonathan and those with him; and he was not able, because their plan became known to them.

**9:61** And they seized from the men of the country, the ringleaders of the wickedness, about fifty men, and he killed them.

**9:62** And Jonathan and Simon and those with him withdrew to Bethbasi, which is in the wilderness, and he rebuilt the parts of it that had been torn down and strengthened it.

**9:63** And Bacchides learned of it and gathered together all his multitude, and he sent orders to those from Judea.

**9:64** And he came and encamped against Bethbasi, and he fought against it for many days and made siege engines.

**9:65** And Jonathan left Simon his brother in the city and went out into the country, and he came with a small number.

**9:66** And he struck Odomera and his brothers and the sons of Phasiron in their encampment, and he began to strike and to go up with the forces.

**9:67** And Simon and those with him went out from the city and burned the siege engines.

**9:68** And they fought against Bacchides, and he was crushed by them, and they pressed him greatly, because his plan and his expedition were in vain.

**9:69** And they were enraged with fury against the lawless men who had counseled him to come into the country, and they killed many of them; and he planned to depart to his own land.

**9:70** And Jonathan learned of it, and he sent envoys to him to conclude peace with him and to restore the captives to them.

**9:71** And he accepted and did according to his words, and he swore to him not to seek harm against him all the days of his life.

**9:72** And he restored to him the captives whom he had previously taken captive from the land of Judah; and he turned back and departed to his own land, and he did not come again into their borders.

**9:73** And the sword ceased from Israel, and Jonathan settled in Michmash; and Jonathan began to judge the people, and he destroyed the ungodly from Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek text here reads "had made war" (epoiēsen ... polemon), whereas other Greek witnesses read "had fallen in battle" (epesen ... en polemō).
- **9:10** — The Greek text lacks the conditional "if" (ei), reading "and our time has drawn near" rather than "and if our time has drawn near" as in other witnesses.
- **9:13** — The Greek text reads "by their camps" (apo tōn parembolōn autōn), whereas other witnesses read "from the sound of the camps" (apo tēs phōnēs tōn parembolōn).
- **9:15** — Greek Azōtou orous ("the mountain of Ashdod/Azotus"); because Ashdod lies on the coastal plain, scholars often suggest an underlying Hebrew phrase such as "the slopes of the mountain" or "Mount Hazor."
- **9:33** — The Greek text reads Asphal (compare Alasa in 9:5 and Iambrein/Iambrin in 9:36-37), where other Greek witnesses read Asphar (and Elasa, Iambri).
- **9:42** — The Greek text reads "the mountain of the Jordan" (to oros tou Iordanou), whereas other Greek witnesses read "the marsh of the Jordan" (to helos tou Iordanou; compare 9:45).
- **9:50** — Greek Thamnatha Pharathon appears as a single combined name without "and," and Tephon is widely identified with Tappuah. In 9:50-53 the Greek text uses plural verbs ("they returned," "they built," etc.), where other witnesses use singular verbs referring to Bacchides.
- **9:52** — The Greek text reads "the city, and Beth-zur" (tēn polin kai Bethsouran), distinguishing "the city" (Jerusalem) from Beth-zur, whereas other witnesses omit "and" and read "the city of Beth-zur."
- **9:65** — Greek ēlthen arithmō (literally "came with a number") reflects a Hebrew idiom (be-mispar) meaning "with a small number" of men.
