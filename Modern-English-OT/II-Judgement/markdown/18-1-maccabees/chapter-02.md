## Text

**2:1** In those days Mattathias, son of John, son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarim, arose from Jerusalem and settled in Modein.

**2:2** And he had five sons: John, who was surnamed Gaddi;

**2:3** Simon, who was called Thassi;

**2:4** Judas, who was called Maccabeus;

**2:5** Eleazar, who was called Avaran; Jonathan, who was called Sapphus.

**2:6** And he saw the blasphemies that were taking place in Judah and in Jerusalem.

**2:7** And he said, "Woe is me! Why was I born for this, to see the devastations of my people and the devastation of the holy city, and to sit there when it was given into the hand of enemies, the sanctuary into the hand of foreigners?

**2:8** Her temple has become like an honored man.

**2:9** The vessels of glory have been carried away captive; her infants have been killed in her streets, her young men by the sword of an enemy.

**2:10** What nation has not inherited palaces and has not seized her spoils?

**2:11** All her adornment has been taken away; instead of a free woman, she has become a slave.

**2:12** And look, our sanctuary and our beauty and our glory have been laid waste, and nations have profaned them.

**2:13** Why should we live any longer?"

**2:14** And Mattathias and his sons tore their garments, put on sackcloth, and mourned greatly.

**2:15** And those from the king who were enforcing the apostasy came to the city of Modein, so that they might sacrifice.

**2:16** And many from Israel came forward to them, and Mattathias and his sons gathered together.

**2:17** And those from the king answered and spoke to Mattathias, saying, "You are a ruler, honored and great in this city, and supported by sons and brothers.

**2:18** Now come forward first and carry out the command of the king, as all the nations have done, and the rulers of Judah, and those who were left in Jerusalem; and you and your house will be among the friends of the king, and you and your sons will be honored with silver and gold and many gifts."

**2:19** And Mattathias answered and said with a loud voice, "Even if all the nations that are in the house of the king's kingdom obey him, so that each one departs from the worship of his fathers, and have chosen his commands,

**2:20** yet I and my sons and my brothers—let us walk in the covenant of our fathers.

**2:21** Far be it from us to forsake law and ordinances.

**2:22** We will not obey the law of the king, to turn aside from our worship to the right or to the left."

**2:23** And when he finished speaking these words, a Jewish man came forward before the eyes of all to burn incense on the altar in Modein, according to the command of the king.

**2:24** And Mattathias saw it and became zealous, and his inward parts trembled, and he gave vent to wrath according to the judgment; and he ran and slaughtered him on the altar.

**2:25** And at that time he killed the king's man who was compelling them to sacrifice, and he tore down the altar.

**2:26** And he was zealous for the law, just as Phinehas did to Zimri son of Salom.

**2:27** And Mattathias cried out in the city with a loud voice, saying, "Everyone who is zealous for the law and upholds the covenant, let him come out after me."

**2:28** And he and his sons fled to the mountains, and left behind all that they had in the city.

**2:29** Then many who were seeking righteousness and justice went down into the wilderness to settle there,

**2:30** they and their sons and their wives and their livestock, because the evils had become severe upon them.

**2:31** And it was reported to the king's men and to the forces that were in Jerusalem, the city of David, that men who had broken the command of the king had gone down into the hiding places in the wilderness.

**2:32** And many ran after them, and overtaking them, they encamped against them and drew up battle against them on the Sabbath day.

**2:33** And they said to them, "Enough now! Come out and do according to the word of the king, and you will live."

**2:34** And they said, "We will not come out, nor will we do the word of the king, to profane the Sabbath day."

**2:35** And they hastened battle against them.

**2:36** And they did not answer them, nor did they hurl a stone at them, nor did they block up the hiding places,

**2:37** saying, "Let us all die in our innocence; heaven and earth bear witness for us that you are destroying us unjustly."

**2:38** And they rose up against them in battle on the Sabbath, and they and their wives and their children and their livestock died, up to a thousand human souls.

**2:39** And Mattathias and his friends learned of it, and they mourned over them very greatly.

**2:40** And each man said to his neighbor, "If we all do as our brothers did and do not fight against the nations for our lives and our ordinances, they will now quickly destroy us from the earth."

**2:41** And they resolved on that day, saying, "Every man who comes against us to battle on the Sabbath day, let us fight against him, and we will certainly not all die, just as our brothers died in the hiding places."

**2:42** Then a company of Hasideans gathered to them with mighty force from Israel, everyone who offered himself willingly for the law.

**2:43** And all who were fleeing from the evils were added to them and became a support to them.

**2:44** And they organized a force and struck down sinners in their anger and lawless men in their wrath; and the rest fled to the nations to be saved.

**2:45** And Mattathias and his sons went around and tore down their altars,

**2:46** and they circumcised the uncircumcised boys, as many as he found within the borders of Israel, by force.

**2:47** And they pursued the sons of arrogance, and the work prospered in their hand.

**2:48** And they rescued the law from the hand of the nations and from the hand of the kings, and they did not give a horn to the sinner.

**2:49** And the days drew near for Mattathias to die, and he said to his sons, "Now arrogance and rebuke and a time of ruin and fierce wrath have grown strong.

**2:50** And now, children, be zealous for the law and give your lives for the covenant of our fathers.

**2:51** Remember the deeds of our fathers, which they did in their generations, and receive great glory and eternal glory.

**2:52** Was not Abraham found faithful in testing, and righteousness was credited to him?

**2:53** Joseph, in the time of his distress, kept the commandment and became lord of Egypt.

**2:54** Phinehas our father, by being deeply zealous, received a covenant of a holy priesthood.

**2:55** Joshua, by fulfilling the word, became a judge in Israel.

**2:56** Caleb, by testifying to the assembly, received land as an inheritance.

**2:57** David, in his mercy, inherited a throne of an eternal kingdom.

**2:58** Elijah, by being deeply zealous for the law, was taken up, as it were, into heaven.

**2:59** Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael, by believing, were saved from the flame.

**2:60** Daniel, in his innocence, was rescued from the mouth of lions.

**2:61** And thus consider, generation by generation, that all who hope in Him will not grow weak.

**2:62** And do not be afraid of the words of a sinful man, because his glory is for dung and for worms.

**2:63** Today he will be lifted up, and he will certainly not be found, because he has returned to his dust, and his thought has perished.

**2:64** And you, children, be strong and courageous in your law, because in it you will be honored.

**2:65** And look, Simeon your brother, I know that he is a man of counsel; listen to him all your days; he will be a father to you.

**2:66** And Judas Maccabeus himself, mighty in strength from his youth, he will be commander of the army for you, and you will fight the battle of the peoples.

**2:67** And you will bring to yourselves all the doers of the law, and execute the vengeance of your people.

**2:68** Repay recompense to the nations, and pay attention to the commands of the law."

**2:69** And he blessed them, and he was gathered to his fathers.

**2:70** And he died in the one hundred and forty-sixth year, and his sons buried him in the tombs of his fathers in Modein, and all Israel lamented for him with a great lamentation.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Joarim reflects the Greek spelling Ioareim, the Septuagint form of the priestly division Jehoiarib (1 Chronicles 24:7), where some Greek witnesses read Joarib.
- **2:5** — Sapphus translates the Greek reading Sapphous here (found in Codex Alexandrinus), whereas other Greek witnesses read Apphous (Apphus).
- **2:8** — The Greek text here reads endoxos ("an honored man"), as in Codex Alexandrinus, whereas other witnesses read adoxos ("a dishonored man" or "a man without honor").
- **2:22** — The Greek text here reads ton nomon ("the law") of the king, whereas other Greek manuscripts read ton logon ("the words") of the king.
- **2:23** — The Greek text here reads thymiasai ("to burn incense"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read thysiasai ("to sacrifice").
- **2:26** — Salom preserves the Septuagint form Salom (also in LXX Numbers 25:14), corresponding to Hebrew Salu.
- **2:51** — The Greek text here reads doxan aionion ("eternal glory"), as in Codex Alexandrinus, whereas other Greek manuscripts read onoma aionion ("an eternal name").
- **2:54** — The Greek text here reads hierosynes hagias ("a holy priesthood"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read hierosynes aionias ("an everlasting priesthood").
- **2:58** — The Greek text reads hos ("as it were, into heaven"), echoing Septuagint 2 Kings 2:11, whereas other witnesses read heos ("up into heaven").
- **2:63** — The Greek text here omits aurion ("tomorrow"), which appears in other witnesses ("and tomorrow he will not be found").
- **2:66** — The Greek text here has the second-person plural verb polemesete ("you will fight"), addressing Mattathias's sons together, whereas other manuscripts read the third-person singular polemesei ("he will fight"), referring to Judas.
