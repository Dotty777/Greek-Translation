## Text

**15:1** And Antiochus, son of King Demetrius, sent letters from the islands of the sea to Simon, priest and ethnarch of the Jews, and to the whole nation.

**15:2** And their contents were in this manner: "King Antiochus to Simon, high priest and ethnarch, and to the nation of the Jews, greetings.

**15:3** Since certain pestilent men have seized control of the kingdom of our fathers, I plan to lay claim to the kingdom so that I may restore it as it was before; and I have recruited a multitude of mercenary forces and prepared warships,

**15:4** and I intend to land in the country so that I may punish those who have destroyed our country and those who have laid waste many cities in the kingdom.

**15:5** Now therefore I confirm to you all the remissions that the kings before me granted you, and whatever other remissions they granted you;

**15:6** to strike your own coinage as currency for your country;

**15:7** and Jerusalem and the sanctuary are to be free; and all the weapons that you have prepared and the fortresses that you have built, which you hold, let them remain yours.

**15:8** And every royal debt and any future royal debts, from now and for all time, let them be remitted to you.

**15:9** And as soon as we establish our kingdom, we will glorify you and your nation and the temple with great glory, so that your glory will become manifest in all the earth."

**15:10** In the one hundred seventy-fourth year Antiochus went out into the land of his fathers, and all the forces came together to him, so that there were few with Trypho.

**15:11** And King Antiochus pursued him, and he came fleeing to Dor, which is by the sea.

**15:12** For he knew that troubles had gathered upon him, and the forces had abandoned him.

**15:13** And Antiochus encamped against Dor, and with him were one hundred twenty thousand fighting men and eight thousand cavalry.

**15:14** And he surrounded the city, and the ships closed in from the sea; and he pressed the city hard from the land and from the sea, and he did not allow anyone to go out or come in.

**15:15** And Numenius and those with him came from Rome with letters for the kings and the countries, in which these things were written:

**15:16** "Lucius, consul of the Romans, to King Ptolemy, greetings.

**15:17** The envoys of the Jews have come to us as our friends and allies, renewing the original friendship and alliance, having been sent by Simon the high priest and the people of the Jews.

**15:18** And they brought a gold shield of five thousand minas.

**15:19** Therefore it has pleased us to write to the kings and the countries that they should not seek harm for them, nor make war against them or their cities or their countries, and that they should not ally themselves with those who make war against them.

**15:20** And it seemed good to us to accept the shield from them.

**15:21** If therefore any pestilent men have escaped from their country to you, hand them over to Simon the high priest, so that he may punish them according to their law."

**15:22** And he wrote the same things to King Demetrius, and to Attalus, and to Arathes, and to Arsaces,

**15:23** and to all the countries, and to Sampsace, and to the Spartans, and to Delos, and to Myndus, and to Sicyon, and to Caris, and to Samos, and to Pamphylia and Lycia, and to Halicarnassus, and to Cos, and to Side, and to Aradus, and to Rhodes, and to Phaselis, and Gortyna, and Cnidus, and Cyprus, and Cyrene.

**15:24** And he wrote a copy of them to Simon the high priest.

**15:25** Now King Antiochus encamped against Dor a second time, continually bringing his troops against it and making siege engines; and he shut Trypho in from coming in or going out.

**15:26** And Simon sent him two thousand chosen men to fight as his allies, and silver and gold and abundant equipment.

**15:27** And he was unwilling to accept them, but annulled everything that he had agreed to with him before, and became estranged from him.

**15:28** And he sent to him Athenobius, one of his friends, to confer with him, saying, "You hold control of Joppa and Gazara and the citadel in Jerusalem, cities of my kingdom.

**15:29** You have laid waste their territories, and you have inflicted a great blow upon the land, and you have taken dominion over many places in my kingdom.

**15:30** Now therefore hand over the cities that you have captured, and the tributes of the places over which you have taken dominion, the territories outside Judea.

**15:31** And if not, give in place of them five hundred talents of silver, and for the destruction that you have caused and for the tributes of the cities, another five hundred talents; otherwise, they will come and wage war against you."

**15:32** And Athenobius, a friend of the king, came to Jerusalem, and he saw the glory of Simon, and a sideboard with gold vessels and silver vessels, and a large retinue; and he was amazed, and he reported to them the words of the king.

**15:33** And Simon answered and said to him, "We have neither taken foreign land nor held what belongs to others, but the inheritance of our fathers; yet it was wrongfully seized by our enemies for a certain time.

**15:34** But we, having the opportunity, hold fast to our inheritance and that of our fathers.

**15:35** And concerning Joppa and Gazara you make a demand; these were inflicting a great blow among the people and on our land; for these we will give one hundred talents." And he did not answer him a word.

**15:36** But he returned in fury to the king, and reported to him these words and the glory of Simon and all that he had seen; and the king became angry with great anger.

**15:37** Now Trypho boarded a ship and fled to Orthosia.

**15:38** And the king appointed Cendebeus chief commander of the coastal region, and gave him infantry and cavalry forces.

**15:39** And he commanded him to encamp facing Judea; and he commanded him to build Kedron and to fortify the cities, and to make war against the people; meanwhile the king was pursuing Trypho.

**15:40** And Cendebeus arrived at Jamnia, and he began to provoke the people, and to invade Judea, and to take the people captive and kill them.

**15:41** And he built Kedron; and he stationed horsemen and forces there so that going out they might make raids along the roads of Judea, just as the king had ordered him.

## Translator Notes

- **15:6** — Our Greek text omits the verb clause "and I permit you" found in some other witnesses, leaving the infinitive "to strike" continuing the royal confirmation from verse 5 (where "remissions" also appears twice rather than "remissions ... gifts").
- **15:18** — Our Greek text reads "five thousand" minas here, whereas some other Greek manuscripts read "one thousand" minas (compare 14:24).
- **15:23** — Our Greek text preserves the distinct names "Sampsace" (Sampsake, often associated with Sampsame or Lampsacus) and "Caris" (Karida, where other witnesses read "Caria"), as well as "Arathes" in verse 22 (for Ariarathes V of Cappadocia).
- **15:25** — Greek en te deutera (literally "in the second") reflects a Hebraism for "a second time" (or possibly "on the second day"), and "troops" renders tas cheiras (literally "the hands," used for military detachments).
- **15:31** — Our Greek text reads the third-person plural "they will ... wage war" (ekpolemesousin), where other witnesses read the first-person plural "we will ... wage war."
