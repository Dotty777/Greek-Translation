## Text

**14:1** And in the one hundred and seventy-second year, King Demetrius gathered his forces and went into Media to obtain help for himself, so that he might fight against Trypho.

**14:2** And Arsaces, the king of Persia and Media, heard that Demetrius had come into his borders, and he sent one of his officers to capture him alive.

**14:3** And he went and struck the camp of Demetrius and captured him, and he brought him to Arsaces, and he put him under guard.

**14:4** And the land had rest all the days of Simon, and he sought good things for his nation; and his authority and his glory pleased them all the days.

**14:5** And along with all his glory, he took Joppa for a harbor and made an entrance to the islands of the sea.

**14:6** And he enlarged the borders for his nation and gained control of the country.

**14:7** And he gathered many captives, and he became master of Gazara and Beth-zur and the citadel; and he removed the impurities from it, and there was no one who opposed him.

**14:8** And they were tilling their land in peace, and the land yielded its produce, and the trees of the plains their fruit.

**14:9** Elders sat in the squares; all conversed about good things, and the young men put on glory and robes of war.

**14:10** For the cities he supplied food, and he equipped them with implements of fortification, until the name of his glory was named to the end of the earth.

**14:11** He made peace in the land, and Israel rejoiced with great gladness.

**14:12** And each one sat under his vine and his fig tree, and there was no one making them afraid.

**14:13** And there ceased to be anyone fighting against them in the land, and their kings were crushed in those days.

**14:14** And he strengthened all the humble of his people; he sought out the law, and he removed every lawless and wicked person.

**14:15** He glorified the sanctuary, and he multiplied the vessels of the sanctuary.

**14:16** And it was heard in Rome that Jonathan had died, and as far as Sparta, and they were greatly grieved.

**14:17** But when they heard that Simon his brother had become high priest in his place and held control of the country and the cities in it,

**14:18** they wrote to him on bronze tablets to renew with him friendship and the alliance which they had established with Judas and Jonathan his brothers.

**14:19** And they were read before the assembly in Jerusalem.

**14:20** And this is the copy of the letters which the Spartans sent: "The rulers of the Spartans and the city, to Simon the great priest and to the elders and to the priests and to the rest of the people of the Jews, brothers, greetings.

**14:21** The envoys who were sent to our people reported to us about your glory and honor, and we rejoiced at their arrival.

**14:22** And we recorded the things spoken by them in the resolutions of the people as follows: 'Numenius son of Antiochus and Antipater son of Jason, envoys of the Jews, came to us, renewing the friendship with us.

**14:23** And it pleased the people to receive the men with honor and to place the copy of their words in the books designated for the people, so that the people of the Spartans might have a memorial; and they wrote a copy of these things to Simon the high priest.'"

**14:24** After these things Simon sent Numenius to Rome with a great golden shield weighing one thousand minas, in order to establish the alliance with them.

**14:25** Now when the people heard these words, they said, "What thanks shall we repay to Simon and to his sons?

**14:26** For he and his brothers and the house of his father stood firm, and they fought off the enemies of Israel from them." And they established freedom for it,

**14:27** and he inscribed it on bronze tablets, and they placed them on a pillar on Mount Zion. And this is the copy of the writing: "On the eighteenth of Elul, in the one hundred and seventy-second year, and this is the third year under Simon the high priest,

**14:28** in Saramel, in a great assembly of the priests and people and rulers of the nation and the elders of the country, it was made known to us:

**14:29** Since wars often occurred in the country, Simon the son of Mattathias, a son of the sons of Joarib, and his brothers gave themselves to danger and withstood the adversaries of their nation, so that their sanctuary and the law might stand, and they glorified their nation with great glory.

**14:30** And Jonathan assembled their nation and became high priest for them, and he was gathered to his people.

**14:31** And their enemies intended to invade their country in order to destroy their country and to stretch out their hands against their sanctuary.

**14:32** Then Simon arose and fought for his nation, and he spent much money of his own, and he armed the men of the army of his nation and gave them wages.

**14:33** And he fortified the cities of Judea, and Beth-zur, which is on the borders of Judea, where the weapons of the enemies were formerly, and he placed there a garrison of Jewish men.

**14:34** And he fortified Joppa, which is by the sea, and Gazara, which is on the borders of Ashdod, in which the enemies formerly lived, and he settled Jews there, and he placed in them whatever was necessary for their restoration.

**14:35** And the people saw the faithfulness of Simon and the glory which he purposed to bring about for his nation, and they appointed him their leader and high priest, because he had done all these things, and because of the righteousness and the faithfulness which he kept toward his nation; and he sought in every way to exalt his people.

**14:36** And in his days things prospered in his hands, so that the nations were removed from their country, and also those in the city of David, those in Jerusalem, who had made for themselves a citadel from which they went out and defiled all around the sanctuary and inflicted a great blow upon purity.

**14:37** And he settled Jewish men in it and fortified it for the security of the country and of the city, and he heightened the walls of Jerusalem.

**14:38** And King Demetrius confirmed the high priesthood to him in accordance with these things,

**14:39** and made him one of his friends, and glorified him with great glory.

**14:40** For it was heard that the Jews are called friends and allies and brothers by the Romans, and that they met the envoys of Simon with honor;

**14:41** and that the Jews and the priests were well pleased that Simon should be their leader and high priest forever, until a faithful prophet should arise,

**14:42** and that he should be commander over them, and that he should care for the sanctuary, that through him those over their works and over the country and over the weapons and over the strongholds should be appointed,

**14:43** and that he should care for the sanctuary, and that he should be obeyed by all, and that all contracts in the country should be written in his name, and that he should be clothed in purple and wear gold.

**14:44** And it is not lawful for anyone of the people or of the priests to nullify any of these things, or to contradict what will be spoken by him, or to convene a gathering in the country without him, or to be clothed in purple, or to wear a golden buckle.

**14:45** And whoever acts contrary to these things or nullifies any of these things will be liable."

**14:46** And all the people were well pleased to grant to Simon to act according to these words.

**14:47** And Simon accepted, and was well pleased to serve as high priest, and to be commander and ethnarch of the Jews and priests, and to preside over all.

**14:48** And they gave orders to place this writing on bronze tablets and to set them up in the precinct of the sanctuary in a secure place,

**14:49** and to place the copies of them in the treasury, so that Simon and his sons might have them.

## Translator Notes

- **14:7** — Gazara is the Hellenistic Greek name for the Old Testament city of Gezer.
- **14:13** — Some Greek manuscripts omit "their" and read simply "the kings," while the text translated here reads "their kings" (the kings of Israel's enemies).
- **14:28** — "Saramel" transliterates a debated Hebrew phrase variously explained as a place name, "court of the people of God" (the temple court), or a title of Simon, "prince of the people of God." The verb rendered "it was made known" is literally active ("he made known") in the Greek, used impersonally.
- **14:43** — The clause "and that he should care for the sanctuary" repeats the same clause from verse 42 in the Greek text, likely reflecting an early scribal duplication in the manuscript tradition.
- **14:48** — "In a secure place" is literally "in a faithful/reliable place," reflecting a Hebrew idiom for a firm or permanent place (cf. Isaiah 22:23).
