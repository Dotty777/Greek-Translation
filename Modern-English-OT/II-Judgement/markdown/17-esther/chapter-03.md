## Text

**3:1** And after these things King Artaxerxes honored Haman son of Hammedatha, a Bugaean, and exalted him, and he held the first seat among all his friends.

**3:2** And all those in the court were bowing down to him, for so the king had commanded to be done; but Mordecai did not bow down to him.

**3:3** And those in the court of the king spoke to Mordecai, "Mordecai, why do you disregard the things spoken by the king?"

**3:4** Every day they spoke to him, and he would not listen to them; and they informed Haman that Mordecai was opposing the king's words; and Mordecai had disclosed to them that he was a Jew.

**3:5** And when Haman realized that Mordecai did not bow down to him, he became exceedingly furious,

**3:6** and he resolved to annihilate all the Jews under the kingdom of Artaxerxes.

**3:7** And he made a decree in the twelfth year of the reign of Artaxerxes, and he cast lots day by day and month by month so as to destroy in one day the race of Mordecai; and the lot fell on the fourteenth of the month, which is Adar.

**3:8** And he spoke to King Artaxerxes, saying, "There is a nation scattered among the nations in all your kingdom, and their laws are different from all the nations, and they disregard the laws of the king; and it does not benefit the king to leave them alone.

**3:9** If it seems good to the king, let him decree to destroy them, and I will pay into the treasury of the king ten thousand talents of silver."

**3:10** And the king took off his signet ring and put it into the hand of Haman, to set a seal on the things written against the Jews.

**3:11** And the king said to Haman, "Keep the silver, and deal with the nation as you wish."

**3:12** And the scribes of the king were summoned in the first month, on the thirteenth day, and they wrote as Haman ordered to the generals and to the rulers in every province from India to Ethiopia, to the one hundred twenty-seven provinces, and to the rulers of the nations according to their own language, by authority of Artaxerxes the king.

**3:13** And it was sent through couriers into the kingdom of Artaxerxes, to annihilate the race of the Jews in one day of the twelfth month, which is Adar, and to plunder their possessions. Now this is the copy of the letter: "The great king Artaxerxes writes these things to the rulers and subordinate district governors of the one hundred twenty-seven provinces from India to Ethiopia: Having ruled over many nations and having gained mastery over the whole inhabited world, I purposed—not lifted up by the arrogance of power, but always governing more reasonably and with gentleness—to make the lives of my subjects continually tranquil, and, rendering the kingdom peaceful and passable to its borders, to renew the peace longed for by all people. And when I inquired of my counselors how this might be brought to completion, Haman—who excels among us in soundness of mind, and has been proven unvaryingly in goodwill and steadfast loyalty, and has attained the second honor of the kingdoms—pointed out to us that mixed among all the tribes throughout the inhabited world there is a certain hostile people, opposed in their laws to every nation, and continually disregarding the commands of kings, so that the unified administration blamelessly directed by us is not established. Having understood, therefore, that this nation alone stands continually in opposition to every person, deviating into a strange way of life with its laws, and being ill-disposed toward our affairs, committing the worst evils, even so that the kingdom cannot attain stability; we have commanded, therefore, to destroy all those indicated to you in the things written by Haman, who is appointed over affairs and is our second father, together with wives and children, root and branch, by the swords of their enemies, without any pity or sparing, on the fourteenth day of the twelfth month, Adar, of the present year, so that those who have been hostile both long ago and now, having gone down violently into Hades in one day, may provide us with stable and untroubled affairs continually for the time to come."

**3:14** And the copies of the letters were posted province by province; and all the nations were commanded to be ready for this day.

**3:15** And the matter was hastened even to Susa; and the king and Haman were carousing, but the city was in turmoil.

## Translator Notes

- **3:7** — The Greek notes that Haman made a decree and that the lot fell on the fourteenth of Adar (matching Addition B in 3:13), whereas the Hebrew sets the date on the thirteenth of Adar in 3:13.
- **3:13** — Includes the Septuagint text of the royal edict (Addition B), composed in formal Hellenistic chancery style, fixing the destruction for the fourteenth of Adar and depicting the victims descending violently into Hades.
