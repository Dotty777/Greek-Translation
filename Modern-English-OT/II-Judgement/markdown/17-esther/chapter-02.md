## Text

**2:1** And after these events the king ceased from his wrath, and he no longer remembered Astin, recalling what sorts of things she had spoken and how he had condemned her.

**2:2** And the attendants of the king said, "Let uncorrupted young women, beautiful in appearance, be sought for the king;

**2:3** and the king will appoint local officials in all the provinces of his kingdom, and let them select virgin young women, beautiful in appearance, into the city of Susa, into the women's quarters, and let them be handed over to the king's eunuch, the guardian of the women, and let ointment and the rest of the care be given;

**2:4** and the woman who pleases the king will reign as queen in place of Astin." And the command pleased the king, and he did so.

**2:5** And there was a Jewish man in the city of Susa, and his name was Mordecai, son of Jair, son of Shimei, son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin,

**2:6** who was a captive from Jerusalem, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had taken captive.

**2:7** And he had a foster child, the daughter of Amminadab, his father's brother, and her name was Esther; and when her parents died, he raised her for himself as a wife; and the young woman was beautiful in appearance.

**2:8** And when the king's command was heard, many young women were gathered into the city of Susa under the hand of Gai, and Esther was brought to Gai, the guardian of the women.

**2:9** And the young woman pleased him, and she found favor before him; and he hurried to give her the ointment and the portion and the seven young women appointed for her from the royal palace, and he treated her and her maidservants well in the women's quarters.

**2:10** And Esther did not disclose her race nor her homeland; for Mordecai had commanded her not to report it.

**2:11** And every day Mordecai walked around by the women's court, watching to see what would happen to Esther.

**2:12** Now this was the time for a young woman to go in to the king, when she had completed twelve months; for thus the days of treatment are completed: six months being anointed with oil of myrrh, and six months with the spices and with the ointments of the women,

**2:13** and then she goes in to the king; and to whomever he speaks, he will hand her over to go in with him from the women's quarters to the royal palace.

**2:14** In the evening she goes in, and toward daybreak she hurries back to the second women's quarters, where Gai, the king's eunuch, the guardian of the women, is; and she does not go in to the king anymore unless she is called by name.

**2:15** And when the time was completed for Esther, the daughter of Amminadab, the brother of Mordecai's father, to go in to the king, she did not disregard anything of what the eunuch, the guardian of the women, had commanded; for Esther was finding favor from all who saw her.

**2:16** And Esther went in to Artaxerxes the king in the twelfth month, which is Adar, in the seventh year of his reign.

**2:17** And the king fell in love with Esther, and she found favor beyond all the virgins, and he placed the women's diadem on her.

**2:18** And the king held a drinking feast for all his friends and the forces for seven days, and he exalted the wedding of Esther, and he granted a remission to those under his kingdom.

**2:19** Now Mordecai was serving in the court.

**2:20** And Esther did not disclose her homeland; for so Mordecai had commanded her, to fear God and to do His commands, just as when she was with him; and Esther did not change her way of life.

**2:21** And the two eunuchs of the king, the chief bodyguards, were grieved because Mordecai had been promoted, and they sought to kill Artaxerxes the king.

**2:22** And the matter was made known to Mordecai, and he reported it to Esther, and she revealed the matters of the plot to the king.

**2:23** And the king examined the two eunuchs and hanged them; and the king commanded to enter a record as a memorial in the royal library concerning the goodwill of Mordecai, in a commendation.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Greek states that the king "no longer remembered Astin," directly contrasting with the Hebrew text ("remembered Vashti").
- **2:7** — The Greek names Esther's father Amminadab (Hebrew Abihail in 2:15) and states that Mordecai raised Esther "for himself as a wife" (Greek eis gynaika), whereas the Hebrew reads "as his own daughter."
- **2:8** — Greek Gai corresponds to Hebrew Hegai; in 2:14 the Greek again names Gai as guardian of the second women's quarters, where the Hebrew names Shaashgaz.
- **2:13** — The Greek wording differs from the Hebrew, describing the guardian handing the young woman over to whichever designated escort is instructed to bring her to the royal palace.
- **2:16** — The Greek dates Esther's entry to the king to "the twelfth month, which is Adar" (Hebrew "the tenth month, Tebeth"); in 2:20 the Greek also adds Mordecai's command "to fear God and to do His commands."
