## Text

**5:1** And it happened on the third day, when she ceased praying, that she took off the garments of worship and clothed herself in her glory. And having become splendid, after calling upon God, the Overseer of all and Savior, she took along the two maids; and upon the one she leaned as if behaving delicately, while the other followed behind, lifting up her robe. And she herself was blushing at the height of her beauty, and her face was cheerful as if affectionate, yet her heart was constricted with fear. And having passed through all the doors, she stood before the king. And he was sitting on the throne of his kingdom, and he had put on every robe of his splendor, all in gold and precious stones; and he was exceedingly terrifying. And lifting his face, ablaze with glory, he looked in the height of anger; and the queen fell, and her color changed in faintness, and she leaned down upon the head of the maid who was going ahead. Then God changed the spirit of the king into gentleness; and in alarm he sprang up from his throne and took her up into his arms until she recovered, and he comforted her with peaceful words and said to her, "What is it, Esther? I am your brother. Take courage; you will certainly not die, for our decree is a general one. Come near."

**5:2** And lifting the golden scepter, he laid it upon her neck, and he embraced her and said, "Speak to me." And she said to him, "I saw you, my lord, like an angel of God, and my heart was troubled from fear of your glory. For you are wondrous, my lord, and your face is full of graces." And while she was speaking, she fell from faintness; and the king was troubled, and all his retinue comforted her.

**5:3** And the king said, "What do you want, Esther? And what is your petition? Up to half of my kingdom, and it will be yours."

**5:4** And Esther said, "Today is my special day; if therefore it pleases the king, let both him and Haman come to the banquet that I will prepare today."

**5:5** And the king said, "Hurry Haman, so that we may carry out Esther's word." And both of them came to the banquet that Esther had mentioned.

**5:6** And at the drinking feast, the king said to Esther, "What is it, Queen Esther? And whatever you petition will be done."

**5:7** And she said, "My request and petition:

**5:8** if I have found favor before the king, let the king and Haman come again tomorrow to the banquet that I will prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do the same."

**5:9** And Haman went out from the king overjoyed and rejoicing; but when Haman saw Mordecai the Jew in the courtyard, he became exceedingly angry.

**5:10** And going into his own home, he called his friends and Zosara his wife,

**5:11** and he showed them his wealth and the honor that the king had bestowed on him, and how he had made him hold first place and lead the kingdom.

**5:12** And Haman said, "The queen has invited no one with the king to the banquet except me; and for tomorrow too I have been invited.

**5:13** And these things do not please me whenever I see Mordecai the Jew in the courtyard."

**5:14** And Zosara his wife and his friends said to him, "Let a pole of fifty cubits be cut for you, and at dawn speak to the king, and let Mordecai be hanged on the pole; and you, go in to the banquet with the king and rejoice." And the word pleased Haman, and the pole was prepared.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Verses 5:1-2 preserve the expanded Septuagint account of Esther's audience before the king (traditionally called Addition D). "Our decree is a general one" (Greek koinon, literally "common" or "public") means that the law applies to ordinary subjects rather than to the queen.
- **5:4** — "Today is my special day" (Greek episemos, "notable" or "festive") is unique to the Septuagint and has no counterpart in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:10** — "Zosara" is the Septuagint name of Haman's wife, corresponding to Zeresh in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:14** — Greek xylon literally means "wood," "beam," or "pole" (used for hanging or impaling), traditionally rendered "gallows."
