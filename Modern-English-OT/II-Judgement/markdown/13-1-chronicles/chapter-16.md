## Text

**16:1** And they brought in the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and offerings of deliverance before God.

**16:2** And David finished offering burnt offerings and offerings of deliverance, and he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.

**16:3** And he distributed to every man of Israel, from man even to woman, to each person one baker's loaf of bread and a sweet cake.

**16:4** And he appointed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord some of the Levites to minister, sounding aloud, and to give thanks and to praise the Lord, the God of Israel:

**16:5** Asaph the leader, and second to him Zechariah, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jeiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel with instruments, harps, and lyres, and Asaph sounding aloud with cymbals,

**16:6** and Benaiah and Uzziel the priests with the trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God

**16:7** on that day. Then David first appointed the praising of the Lord by the hand of Asaph and his brothers. A song.

**16:8** Give thanks, call upon Him in His name, make known His deeds among the peoples.

**16:9** Sing to Him and sing hymns to Him; recount to all His marvels that the Lord has done.

**16:10** Praise in His holy name; the heart seeking His good pleasure will be glad.

**16:11** Seek and be strong; seek His face continually.

**16:12** Remember His marvels that He has done, wonders and judgments of His mouth,

**16:13** offspring of Israel, His servants, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**16:14** The Lord is our God; in all the earth are His judgments.

**16:15** We remember His covenant forever, His word that He commanded for a thousand generations,

**16:16** which He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac.

**16:17** He established it for Jacob as a statute, for Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**16:18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the measured portion of your inheritance."

**16:19** When they were very few in number, diminished as they were, they lived as strangers in it.

**16:20** And they went from nation to nation, and from a kingdom to another people.

**16:21** He did not permit any man to oppress them, and He rebuked kings concerning them:

**16:22** "Do not touch My anointed ones; against My prophets do no harm."

**16:23** Sing to the Lord, all the earth; proclaim from day to day His salvation.

**16:25** For the Lord is great and exceedingly praiseworthy; He is to be feared above all the gods.

**16:26** For all the gods of the nations are idols, and our God made heaven.

**16:27** Glory and praise are before Him; strength and exultation are in His place.

**16:28** Give to the Lord, Father of the nations, give to the Lord glory and strength;

**16:29** take gifts and bring them before Him, and worship the Lord in His holy courts.

**16:30** Let all the earth fear before His presence; let the earth be established and let it not be shaken.

**16:31** Let heaven be glad and let the earth rejoice, and let them say among the nations, "The Lord is reigning!"

**16:32** The sea will roar together with its fullness; the tree of the field and all that is in it.

**16:33** Then the trees of the forest will be glad before the presence of the Lord, for He has come to judge the earth.

**16:34** Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good, for His mercy is forever.

**16:35** And say, "The God of our salvation has saved us! And deliver us from the nations, to praise Your holy name and to exult in Your praises."

**16:36** Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, from eternity. And all the people will say, "Amen." And they praised the Lord.

**16:37** And they left there before the ark of the covenant of the Lord Asaph and his brothers, to minister before the ark continually, each day's duty day by day;

**16:38** and Obed-edom and his brothers, sixty-eight; and Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah as gatekeepers;

**16:39** and Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests before the tent of the Lord at Bama, which is in Gibeon,

**16:40** to offer burnt offerings to the Lord on the altar of burnt offerings continually, in the morning and in the evening, and according to all that is written in the law of the Lord, all that He commanded for the sons of Israel by the hand of Moses, the servant of God.

**16:41** And with him were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of those chosen by name to praise the Lord, "For His mercy is forever."

**16:42** And with them were trumpets and cymbals for sounding aloud, and instruments of the songs of God; the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.

**16:43** And all the people went, each to his house, and David returned to bless his household.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Greek "amoreiten" (a spelling of "amorites") denotes a sweet honey cake; the Greek lists two food items rather than the three found in the Hebrew.
- **16:23** — The Greek critical text omits verse 24, moving directly from verse 23 to verse 25, and likewise lacks the opening clause of verse 29 found in the Hebrew.
- **16:28** — The Greek text reads "Father of the nations" ("patri ton ethnon") rather than "families of the nations" ("patriai ton ethnon") found in the Hebrew and other Greek witnesses.
- **16:32** — Greek "bobbesei" (a spelling of "bombesei," "will roar") is future indicative, and "the tree of the field" lacks a separate verb where the Hebrew reads "let the field exult."
- **16:35** — The Greek reads past-tense "has saved us" rather than the imperative "Save us" of the Hebrew; similarly, verse 34 has neuter "for it is good," and verse 36 omits "and to everlasting" and uses future "will say."
- **16:39** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew "bamah" ("high place") as a proper place name, "Bama, which is in Gibeon"; in verse 40, the Greek adds "by the hand of Moses, the servant of God."
