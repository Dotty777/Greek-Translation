## Text

**3:1** And these were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn, Amnon, by Ahinoam the Israelite; the second, Damniel, by Abigail the Carmelite;

**3:2** the third, Absalom, the son of Maacah the daughter of Thoamai, king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah, the son of Haggith;

**3:3** the fifth, Shephatiah, of Sabital; the sixth, Ithream, by Ala his wife.

**3:4** Six were born to him in Hebron. And he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**3:5** And these were born to him in Jerusalem: Saman, Soban, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bathsheba the daughter of Ammiel;

**3:6** and Baar, and Elisha, and Eliphelet,

**3:7** and Nogah, and Naphath, and Janue,

**3:8** and Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphala, nine.

**3:9** All were sons of David, besides the sons of the concubines; and Tamar was their sister.

**3:10** The sons of Solomon: Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

**3:11** Joram his son, Ozia his son, Joash his son,

**3:12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

**3:13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

**3:14** Amnon his son, Josiah his son.

**3:15** And the sons of Josiah: the firstborn, Johanan; the second, Jehoiakim; the third, Zedekiah; the fourth, Shallum.

**3:16** And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.

**3:17** And the sons of Jeconiah: Assir, Shealtiel his son,

**3:18** Malchiram, and Phaldaias, and Shenazzar, and Jekenia, and Hosamoth, and Denethi.

**3:19** And the sons of Shealtiel: Zerubbabel; Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister,

**3:20** and Hashubah, and Osa, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, and Arobasok, five.

**3:21** And the sons of Hananiah: Phalleti, and Isaba his son, Raphal his son, Orna his son, Obadiah his son, Shecaniah his son.

**3:22** And the son of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. And the son of Shemaiah: Hattush, and Joel, and Marei, and Noadiah, and Shaphat, six.

**3:23** And the sons of Noadiah: Elithana, and Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three.

**3:24** And the sons of Elithenan: Hodolia, and Asib, and Phara, and Jakun, and Johanan, and Delaiah, and Manei, seven.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek reads "Israelite" (rather than Hebrew "Jezreelitess") and "Damniel" (rather than Hebrew "Daniel"); similarly, vv. 2-8 preserve several distinct Septuagint name forms (such as Thoamai, Sabital, Ala, Baar, Elisha, Naphath, and Janue, plus Bathsheba in v. 5 for Hebrew Bath-shua).
- **3:4** — The Greek omits the clause found in the Hebrew stating that David reigned seven years and six months in Hebron.
- **3:11** — The Greek reads "Ozia" where the Hebrew has "Ahaziah," and in v. 14 reads "Amnon" where the Hebrew has "Amon."
- **3:19** — The Greek makes Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel (rather than Hebrew Pedaiah) and omits Shimei and the heading "the sons of Zerubbabel."
- **3:21** — Unlike the Hebrew, which lists parallel clans ("sons of..."), the Greek repeats "his son," extending Hananiah's lineage linearly; vv. 18-24 also preserve several Septuagint-distinct personal names (including Phaldaias, Denethi, Osa, Arobasok, Joel, Marei, Noadiah, Elithana/Elithenan, and Asib).
