## Text

**26:1** For the divisions of the gates, the sons of the Korahites: Mosolael the son of Kore; from the sons of Abijah, Saphar.

**26:2** And as for Mosalea, son of Zechariah: the firstborn Iderel, Zechariah, Jenonel,

**26:3** Jolam, Jonah, Elioenai the seventh.

**26:4** And Obed-edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jozabad, Joath, Sacar, Naas, Jehiel,

**26:5** Ammiel, Issachar, Japhthoslaathi; for God blessed him.

**26:6** And to his son Shemaiah were born sons of the firstborn Rosai for his father's house, for they were mighty.

**26:7** The sons of Shemaiah: Goonei, Rephael, Obed, Elzabad, and Ahihud—mighty sons—Ennu, Sabchia, and Isbakom.

**26:8** All from the sons of Obed-edom, they and their brothers and their sons, acting mightily in the work, were sixty-two in all for Obed-edom.

**26:9** And Mosomameid had sons and brothers, eighteen mighty men.

**26:10** And Hosah, of the sons of Merari, had sons keeping the leadership, for he was not the firstborn; and his father made him leader of the second division.

**26:11** Tablai the third, Zechariah; all these sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.

**26:12** To these—to the leaders of the mighty men—belonged the divisions of the gates, with turns of duty just like their brothers, to serve in the house of the Lord.

**26:13** And they cast lots, small and great alike, according to the houses of their ancestral families, for gateway after gateway.

**26:14** And the lot for those toward the east fell to Shelemiah and Zechariah, sons of Soaz; for Malchiah they cast lots, and the lot came out for the north;

**26:15** for Obed-edom, the south, opposite the house of Esephin;

**26:16** secondly, for Hosah toward the west, past the gate of the chamber of the ascent; watch opposite watch.

**26:17** Toward the east, six a day; on the north, four a day; on the south, four a day; and for the Esephin, two

**26:18** as reliefs, and for Hosah toward the west, past the gate of the chamber, three. Watch opposite watch of the ascent: toward the east, six a day, and on the north, four, and on the south, four, and for Esephim, two as reliefs, and toward the west, four, and for the path, two relieving one another.

**26:19** These are the divisions of the gatekeepers for the sons of Kohath and for the Merarites.

**26:20** And the Levites, their brothers, were over the treasuries of the house of the Lord and over the treasuries of the consecrated things.

**26:21** These were the sons of Chadan, the Gershonite: for Ladan, and Jaieel son of Jehu; the sons of Jehiel, leaders of ancestral families for Ladan; for the Gershonite, Jehiel.

**26:22** The sons of Jehiel, Zetham and Joel, the brothers, were over the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**26:23** For Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**26:24** And Joel, son of Gershom, son of Moses, was over the treasuries.

**26:25** And his brother Eliezer had a son Rehabiah, and Hoshaiah, and Joram, and Zichri, and Shelomoth.

**26:26** Shelomoth himself and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the holy things that King David, and the leaders of the ancestral families, the captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, and the commanders of the army consecrated,

**26:27** which he took from cities and from the spoils, and consecrated from them so that the building of the house of God would not fall short;

**26:28** and over all the holy things of God, of Samuel the prophet, and of Saul son of Kish, and of Abner son of Ner, and of Joab son of Zeruiah—everything that he consecrated was by the hand of Shelomoth and his brothers.

**26:29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and sons of the outside work were over Israel, to act as scribes and to judge.

**26:30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers, the mighty men, one thousand seven hundred, were over the supervision of Israel beyond the Jordan toward the west, for all the service of the Lord and the work of the king.

**26:31** Of the Hebronites, Tudeias was the leader of the Hebronites, according to their generations, by ancestral families. In the fortieth year of his reign they were inspected, and a mighty man was found among them in Riazer of Gilead.

**26:32** And his brothers, the mighty sons, were two thousand seven hundred, the leaders of ancestral families; and King David appointed them over the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every ordinance of the Lord and matter of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek reads 'of the gates' (also in v. 12, vs. 'gatekeepers' in v. 19), renders Meshelemiah as Mosolael (and as Mosalea in v. 2, Mosomameid in v. 9), and divides Hebrew Asaph/Ebiasaph into 'from the sons of Abijah, Saphar.'
- **26:2** — The Greek syntax reads 'as for Mosalea, son of Zechariah' and omits the ordinals through the sixth, while vv. 2-7 preserve Septuagint-distinct names (Iderel, Zechariah, Jenonel, Jolam, Jonah; Joath, Naas, Jehiel, Japhthoslaathi; 'the firstborn Rosai'; Goonei, Ahihud, Ennu, Sabchia, and Isbakom).
- **26:10** — The Greek translates Hebrew 'Shimri the chief' as 'keeping the leadership' and 'Hilkiah the second' as 'of the second division,' followed by the distinct form Tablai in v. 11.
- **26:14** — Soaz and Malchiah preserve Septuagint-distinct names where the Hebrew describes Zechariah as a prudent counselor; in vv. 15-18, Esephin/Esephim transliterates the Hebrew term for storehouses, and v. 18 preserves the expanded Greek repetition (including both three and four guards on the west).
- **26:21** — Several Septuagint-distinct readings appear across vv. 19-31: Kohath (v. 19), 'their brothers' (v. 20), Chadan and 'Jaieel son of Jehu' (v. 21), Joel (v. 24), Hoshaiah (v. 25), 'from cities' (v. 27, Greek poleon vs. polemon 'wars'), and Tudeias with singular 'a mighty man' in Riazer (v. 31).
