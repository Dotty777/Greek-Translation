## Text

**18:1** And it came about after these things that David struck down the foreigners and routed them, and he took Gath and its villages from the hand of the foreigners.

**18:2** And he struck down Moab, and Moab became servants to David, bringing gifts.

**18:3** And David struck down Hadarezer king of Zobah of Hamath, as he was going to set his hand upon the Euphrates River.

**18:4** And David captured from them one thousand chariots and seven thousand horses and twenty thousand men; and David disabled all the chariots, and reserved from them one hundred chariots.

**18:5** And the Syrians came from Damascus to help Hadarezer king of Zobah, and he struck down among the Syrians twenty-two thousand men.

**18:6** And David placed a garrison in Syria near Damascus, and they became servants to David, bringing gifts; and the Lord saved David wherever he went.

**18:7** And David took the golden collars that were on the servants of Hadarezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

**18:8** And from Metabechas and from the chosen wars of Hadarezer, David took very much bronze; from it Solomon made the bronze sea and the pillars and the bronze vessels.

**18:9** And Thoa king of Hamath heard that David had struck down the entire force of Hadarezer king of Zobah,

**18:10** and he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask him concerning peace and to bless him because he had fought against Hadarezer and struck him down (for Thoa was a man at war with Hadarezer), and all kinds of silver and gold vessels.

**18:11** And these David consecrated to the Lord along with the silver and the gold that he had taken from all the nations: from Idumea and Moab, and from the sons of Ammon, and from the foreigners, and from Amalek.

**18:12** And Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down Idumea in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand,

**18:13** and he placed garrisons in the valley; and all the Idumeans became servants of David. And the Lord saved him wherever he went.

**18:14** And David reigned over all Israel, and he was doing justice and righteousness for all his people.

**18:15** And Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahijah was recorder,

**18:16** and Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Joshua was scribe,

**18:17** and Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the priests and Phalteia; and the sons of David were the first successors of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — "the foreigners" translates Greek allophyloi (literally "people of another tribe"), the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines (also in 18:11).
- **18:7** — "collars" renders Greek kloioi (neckbands or collars), where the Hebrew has "shields"; similarly in 18:4 the Greek reads "seven thousand horses" rather than "horsemen."
- **18:8** — "Metabechas" is a Septuagint-distinct name reflecting Hebrew "from Tibhath," and "the chosen wars" translates Greek ton eklekton polemon (where the Hebrew has "from Cun, cities of Hadarezer").
- **18:9** — "Thoa" is the Septuagint form corresponding to Hebrew Tou (also in 18:10); in 18:11-13 "Idumea" and "Idumeans" render Greek Idoumaia/Idoumaioi (Hebrew Edom), and 18:13 places the garrisons "in the valley" rather than "in Edom."
- **18:17** — The Greek reads "over the priests and Phalteia" (a Septuagint-distinct name) where the Hebrew has "over the Cherethites and the Pelethites"; likewise in 18:15-16 the Greek names "Ahijah" (for Hebrew Ahilud) and "Joshua" (for Hebrew Shavsha).
