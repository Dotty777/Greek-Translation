## Text

**17:1** And it came about, when David settled in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I dwell in a cedar house, and the ark of the covenant of the Lord is under curtains."

**17:2** And Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your soul, for God is with you."

**17:3** And it came about in that night that the word of the Lord came to Nathan:

**17:4** "Go and say to David My servant, 'Thus the Lord has said: You will not build a house for Me to dwell in.

**17:5** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought Israel up until this day,

**17:6** and I was in a tent and in a covering wherever I walked among all Israel. Did I ever speak to one tribe of Israel to shepherd My people, saying, "Why have you not built for Me a cedar house?"'

**17:7** And now thus you will say to My servant David, 'This is what the Lord Almighty says: I took you from the sheepfold, from behind the flocks, to be ruler over My people Israel,

**17:8** and I was with you wherever you went, and I utterly destroyed all your enemies from before you, and I made for you a name like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**17:9** And I will set a place for My people Israel and plant them, and they will dwell on their own and will be anxious no longer, and injustice will not again humble them as at the beginning,

**17:10** and from the days when I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I humbled all your enemies, and I will increase you, and the Lord will build you.

**17:11** And it will come about, when your days are fulfilled and you sleep with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who will be from your body, and I will prepare his kingdom.

**17:12** He will build a house for Me, and I will set his throne upright forever.

**17:13** I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me; and I will not withdraw My mercy from him, as I withdrew it from those who were before you.

**17:14** And I will confirm him in My house and in his kingdom forever, and his throne will be set upright forever.'"

**17:15** According to all these words and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**17:16** And King David came and sat before the Lord, and said, "Who am I, Lord God, and what is my house, that You have loved me forever?

**17:17** And these things were small before You, God, and You have spoken concerning the house of Your servant from afar, and You have looked upon me as a vision of a man, and You have exalted me, Lord God.

**17:18** What more will David add to You to give glory? And You know Your servant,

**17:19** and according to Your heart You have done all the greatness.

**17:20** Lord, there is no one like You, and there is none besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**17:21** And there is no other nation on the earth like Your people Israel, as God led them to redeem for Himself a people, to set for Himself a great and renowned name, to cast out nations from before Your people, whom You redeemed out of Egypt.

**17:22** And You gave Your people Israel to Yourself as a people forever, and You, the Lord, are God to them.

**17:23** And now, Lord, let Your word that You have spoken to Your servant and concerning his house be confirmed forever,

**17:24** as they say, 'Lord, Lord Almighty, God of Israel.' And the house of David Your servant is set upright before You.

**17:25** For You have opened the ear of Your servant to build a house for him; therefore Your servant has found occasion to pray before You.

**17:26** And now, Lord, You Yourself are God, and You have spoken these good things concerning Your servant.

**17:27** And now You have begun to bless the house of Your servant, that it may be forever before You; for You have blessed it, and bless it forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — The Greek reads "one tribe of Israel" (where the Hebrew has "any of the judges of Israel") and omits "whom I commanded" before "to shepherd My people."
- **17:10** — The Greek reads "I will increase you, and the Lord will build you," whereas the Hebrew reads "I declare to you that the LORD will build a house for you."
- **17:11** — "from your body" translates Greek ek tes koilias sou (literally "from your belly"), whereas the Hebrew has "from your sons."
- **17:16** — The Greek reads "that You have loved me forever," differing from the Hebrew "that You have brought me thus far."
- **17:17** — "as a vision of a man, and You have exalted me" renders the Greek reading of a difficult Hebrew clause; similarly in 17:23-24 the Greek omits two clauses present in the Hebrew and transitions directly into "as they say."
