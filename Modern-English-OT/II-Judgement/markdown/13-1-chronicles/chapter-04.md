## Text

**4:1** And the sons of Judah: Perez, Harson, and Carmi, and Hur, Shobal,

**4:2** and Rada his son; and Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These are the generations of the Arathite.

**4:3** And these were the sons of Etam: Azrael, and Ragma, and Jabas; and the name of their sister was Heselebbon.

**4:4** And Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hosan. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethladen.

**4:5** And Sara, the father of Tekoa, had two wives, Aoda and Thoada.

**4:6** And Aoda bore him Ochaia, and Hephal, and Teman, and Aseran. All these were the sons of Aoda.

**4:7** And the sons of Loada: Areth, and Saar, and Sennon.

**4:8** And Koe fathered Ennon and Sabatha, and the descendants of the brother of Rechab, the son of Jarim.

**4:9** And Igabes was more honored than his brothers; and his mother called his name Igabes, saying, "I gave birth as gabes."

**4:10** And Igabes called upon the God of Israel, saying, "If You indeed bless me, and increase my borders, and Your hand is with me, and You make knowledge so as not to humble me." And God brought about all that he asked.

**4:11** And Caleb, the father of Ascha, fathered Machir; he was the father of Assathon.

**4:12** He fathered Bathraia, and Bessee, and Teman, the father of the city of Nahash, the brother of Eselon the Kenizzite. These were the men of Rechab.

**4:13** And the sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. And the sons of Othniel: Hathath.

**4:14** And Manathi fathered Ophrah. And Seraiah fathered Jobab, the father of Ageaddair, for they were craftsmen.

**4:15** And the sons of Caleb, the son of Jephunneh: Er, Adai, and Noom. And the sons of Ada: Kenaz.

**4:16** And the sons of Aleel: Zib, and Ziphah, and Tiria, and Asarel.

**4:17** And the sons of Esri: Jetheri, Porad, and Epher, and Amon. And Jether fathered Maion, and Shemen, and Mareth, the father of Esthemon.

**4:18** And his wife—she was Hadeia—bore Jered, the father of Gedor, and Habisa, the father of Soco, and Chetiel, the father of Zamon. And these were the sons of Gelia, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Noroel took.

**4:19** And the sons of the woman Idouia, the sister of Nacheth, were both Dalila, the father of Keilah, and Semegon, the father of Joman; and of Manaem, the father of Keilah: Hatamei and Esthemone the Nochathite.

**4:20** And the sons of Shimon: Amnon, and Ana the son of Phana, and Inon. And the sons of Seei: Zoan. And the sons of Zoab,

**4:21** the sons of Shelom, the son of Judah: Er, the father of Lecah, and Madath, the father of Maicha; and the generations of the houses of ephrath habak for the house of Esoba;

**4:22** and Jokim, and the men of Sochetha, Joada and Saia, who lived in Moab; and he turned them back, habederein athoukiein.

**4:23** These were the potters who lived in Azaim and Gabaera with the king; in his kingdom they grew strong and lived there.

**4:24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, and Jamin, Jarin, Zares, Shaul;

**4:25** Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son,

**4:26** Shimei his son.

**4:27** Shimei had sixteen sons and three daughters; and their brothers did not have many sons; and all their clans did not increase like the sons of Judah.

**4:28** And they lived in Beersheba, and Sama, and Moladah, and Hesereulab;

**4:29** Abella, and Boosal, and Thulaem,

**4:30** and Bathun, and Hormah, and Okla,

**4:31** and Beth-mareimoth, and Hemisusesoram, and the house of Braumseorim. These were their cities until King David.

**4:32** And their villages were Etam, and Rimmon, and Thokka, and Aisar, five cities.

**4:33** And all their villages around these cities as far as Baalath; this was their possession and their enrollment.

**4:34** And Meshobab, and Jamlech, and Josiah the son of Amaziah,

**4:35** and Joel; and he was the son of Isabia, the son of Saraau, the son of Asiel;

**4:36** and Elioenai, and Jaakobah, and Jasuia, and Asaiah, the sons of Aosal, the son of Saphal,

**4:37** the son of Amon, the son of Idia, the son of Samar, the son of Simeon.

**4:38** These were those who divided by the names of leaders in their generations; and in their ancestral houses they multiplied into a multitude.

**4:39** And they went until they came to Gerar, as far as the east of Gai, to seek pastures for their livestock.

**4:40** And they found rich and good pastures; and the land was wide before them, and there was peace and quiet, for they were from the sons of Ham who lived there previously.

**4:41** And these who were written by name came in the days of Hezekiah, king of Judah, and struck their houses and the Minaeans whom they found there, and devoted them to destruction to this day; and they lived in their place, because there were pastures for their livestock there.

**4:42** And from them, from the sons of Simeon, five hundred men went to Mount Seir, and Pelatiah, and Noadiah, and Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Jesthen, were their leaders;

**4:43** and they struck those who were left behind, the remnant of Amalek, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Verses 1-20 contain numerous Septuagint-distinct personal and place names diverging from the Hebrew (including Harson in v. 1, Bethladen in v. 4, Sara, Aoda, Thoada, and Loada in vv. 5-7, "brother of Rechab" in v. 8, Gelia and Noroel in v. 18, and the expanded list in v. 19).
- **4:9** — The Greek renders Hebrew "Jabez" as "Igabes" and leaves the etymological wordplay untranslated as "as gabes" (where the Hebrew reads "in pain").
- **4:10** — The Greek reads "make knowledge so as not to humble me," diverging markedly from the Hebrew ("keep from harm so that it may not pain me"), and adds "all" before "that he asked."
- **4:21** — The Greek leaves the Hebrew expression for linen-working untranslated as "ephrath habak," and in v. 22 leaves the final clause untranslated as "habederein athoukiein" (Hebrew: "the records are ancient").
- **4:22** — Greek leaves the Hebrew phrase for "and the records are ancient" untranslated as the transliterated words habederein athoukiein.
- **4:26** — The Greek omits Hammuel and Zaccur before Shimei; in v. 27 it reads "three daughters" (Hebrew: "six") and "their brothers," and in v. 32 lists four towns while keeping the total "five cities."
- **4:39** — The Greek reads "Gerar" (Hebrew: "Gedor") and treats Hebrew "the valley" as the proper name "Gai"; in v. 41 it reads "their houses" (Hebrew: "their tents") and "Minaeans," and in v. 43 omits "and they lived there."
