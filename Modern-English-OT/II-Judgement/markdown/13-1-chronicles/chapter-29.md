## Text

**29:1** And King David said to all the assembly, "Solomon my son, whom the Lord has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great, for it is not for man, but for the Lord God.

**29:2** According to all my ability, I have prepared for the house of my God gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, soom stones and stones for setting, and costly and multicolored stones, and every precious stone, and much Parian marble.

**29:3** And moreover, because I take delight in the house of my God, I have gold and silver that I have acquired for myself, and look, I have given it for the house of my God above measure, apart from what I have prepared for the house of the holy things:

**29:4** three thousand talents of the gold from Suphir and seven thousand talents of refined silver, for the walls of the temple to be overlaid with them

**29:5** by the hand of craftsmen. And who is willing to fill his hands today for the Lord?"

**29:6** And leaders of ancestral houses, and the leaders of the sons of Israel, and the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, and the overseers of the works, and the builders of the king offered willingly.

**29:7** And they gave for the works of the house of the Lord five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand gold coins, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

**29:8** And those with whom a stone was found gave it to the storehouses of the house of the Lord by the hand of Besiel the Gersomnite.

**29:9** And the people rejoiced over offering willingly, because with a full heart they offered willingly to the Lord, and King David rejoiced greatly.

**29:10** And King David blessed the Lord in the presence of the assembly, saying,

**29:11** "Blessed are You, Lord, the God of Israel, our Father from eternity and to eternity. You, Lord, are the greatness and the power and the boast and the victory and the strength, for You hold dominion over all things in heaven and on the earth; before Your face every king and nation is shaken.

**29:12** From You come wealth and glory; You rule over all things, Lord, the Ruler of every rule; and in Your hand are strength and might, and it is in Your hand, Almighty, to make great and to strengthen all things.

**29:13** And now, Lord, we give thanks to You and praise the name of Your boasting.

**29:14** And who am I, and who are Your people, that we have had the strength to offer willingly to You in this way? For all things are Yours, and from what is Yours we have given to You.

**29:15** For we are sojourners before You and residents, like all our fathers. Like a shadow are our days on earth, and there is no endurance.

**29:16** Lord our God, as for all this abundance that I have prepared for a house to be built for Your holy name, it is from Your hand, and all things belong to You.

**29:17** And I know, Lord, that You are the One who examines hearts, and You love righteousness. In sincerity of heart I have willingly offered all these things, and now I have seen Your people who are found here offering willingly to You with gladness.

**29:18** Lord, the God of Abraham and Isaac and Israel our fathers, guard these things in the thought of the heart of Your people forever, and direct their hearts toward You.

**29:19** And to Solomon my son give a good heart to do Your commandments and Your testimonies and Your statutes, and to bring to completion the construction of Your house."

**29:20** And David said to all the assembly, "Bless the Lord our God." And all the assembly blessed the Lord, the God of their fathers, and bending their knees, they bowed down to the Lord and to the king.

**29:21** And David sacrificed sacrifices to the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings to God on the day after the first day: one thousand calves, one thousand rams, one thousand lambs, and their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**29:22** And they ate and drank before the Lord on that day with joy, and they made Solomon the son of David king, and anointed him to the Lord as king and Zadok to the priesthood.

**29:23** And Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and he was favored, and all Israel obeyed him.

**29:24** The leaders and the mighty men and the sons of King David his father submitted to him.

**29:25** And the Lord made Solomon great from above, before all Israel; and they gave him the glory of a king, such as had not been upon any king before him.

**29:26** And David the son of Jesse reigned over Israel

**29:27** forty years: in Hebron seven years, in Jerusalem thirty-three years.

**29:28** And he died in a good old age, full of days, in wealth and glory; and Solomon his son reigned in his place.

**29:29** Now the rest of the deeds of King David, the former and the latter, are written in the accounts of Samuel the seer and in the accounts of Nathan the prophet and in the accounts of Gad the seer,

**29:30** concerning all his kingdom and his might, which came upon him and upon Israel and upon all the kingdoms of the earth. Solomon the son of David grew strong over his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him and made him grow to the heights.

## Translator Notes

- **29:2** — Greek soom transliterates Hebrew shoham (commonly identified as onyx or carnelian), and parion denotes Parian marble from the island of Paros where the Hebrew text refers to alabaster or marble.
- **29:4** — Suphir (Greek Soupheir) is the Septuagint form of the place name corresponding to Ophir in the Hebrew text.
- **29:8** — Besiel the Gersomnite (Greek Besiel tou Gersomnei) is the Septuagint name corresponding to Jehiel the Gershonite in the Hebrew text.
- **29:11** — The Greek text reads nominative "You, Lord, are..." rather than "Yours is...", supplies the verb "hold dominion", and ends with "before Your face every king and nation is shaken" where the Hebrew text speaks of God's kingdom and headship over all.
- **29:30** — The Greek text lacks the noun "times" before the plural relative pronoun "which" and includes the closing sentence concerning Solomon, which corresponds to 2 Chronicles 1:1 in the Hebrew text.
