## Text

**24:1** And as for the sons of Aaron by division: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**24:2** And Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no sons; and Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron, served as priests.

**24:3** And David divided them, and Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their enrollment, according to their service, according to their paternal houses.

**24:4** And the sons of Eleazar were found more numerous in chiefs of the mighty men than the sons of Ithamar; and he divided them: for the sons of Eleazar, chiefs for paternal houses, sixteen; for the sons of Aaron, Ithamar, according to paternal houses.

**24:5** And he divided them by lots, these with those, for there were chiefs of the holy things and chiefs of the Lord among the sons of Eleazar and among the sons of Ithamar.

**24:6** And Shemaiah the son of Nethanel, the scribe, of Levi, wrote them down in the presence of the king and the chiefs, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the chiefs of the paternal families of the priests and of the Levites: of a paternal house, one by one for Eleazar and one by one for Ithamar.

**24:7** And the first lot came out for Jarim, for Anaidiah the second,

**24:8** for Hareb the third, for Seorim the fourth,

**24:9** for Malchijah the fifth, for Benjamin the sixth,

**24:10** for Koz the seventh, for Abijah the eighth,

**24:11** for Jeshua the ninth, for Ischaniah the tenth,

**24:12** for Eliabiei the eleventh, for Jakim the twelfth,

**24:13** for Hocchoppha the thirteenth, for Gelba the fourteenth,

**24:14** for Immer the fifteenth, for Hezin the sixteenth,

**24:15** for Aphese the seventeenth, for Pethahiah the eighteenth,

**24:16** for Ezekiel the nineteenth, for Achim the twentieth,

**24:17** for Gamul the twenty-first, for Adallai the twenty-second,

**24:18** for Maasai the twenty-third.

**24:19** This was their enrollment according to their service, to enter into the house of the Lord according to their ordinance by the hand of Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel commanded.

**24:20** And for the remaining sons of Levi: for the sons of Amram, Jobael, Jehdeiah;

**24:21** for Rehabiah, the chief.

**24:22** And for the Izharite, Shelomoth; for the sons of Shelomoth, Inath.

**24:23** The sons of Jedimu: Amadiah the second, Jase the third, Jokom the fourth.

**24:24** Of Uzziel, Micah; the sons of Micah, Shamir;

**24:25** the brother of Micah, Isshiah; the son of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Uzziah:

**24:27** of Merari, for Uzziah, his sons: Isoam, and Zaccur, and Abai.

**24:28** For Mahli: Eleazar and Ithamar; and Eleazar died, and he had no sons.

**24:29** For Kish: the sons of Kish, Jerahmeel.

**24:30** And the sons of Mushi: Mahli, and Elah, and Arimoth. These were the sons of the Levites according to their paternal houses.

**24:31** And they also received lots just as their brothers the sons of Aaron, before the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the paternal families of the priests and of the Levites, the patriarchs of Araab, just as his younger brothers.

## Translator Notes

- **24:4** — The Greek text inserts 'Aaron' before 'Ithamar' and omits the number 'eight' for Ithamar's paternal houses, while in v. 6 it reads 'one by one' (reflecting Hebrew 'one' rather than Masoretic 'taken').
- **24:14** — The Greek text omits the fifteenth course of the Hebrew list (Bilgah), shifting the remaining courses so that the list ends at twenty-three rather than twenty-four (v. 18); across vv. 7-18 it also preserves distinctive Greek name forms (Jarim, Anaidiah, Hareb, Benjamin, Ischaniah, Eliabiei, Hocchoppha, Gelba, Hezin, Aphese, Achim, Adallai, and Maasai).
- **24:21** — The Greek text omits the name of Rehabiah's son (Isshiah in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), reading simply 'for Rehabiah, the chief,' and similarly in v. 20 omits 'for the sons of Shubael' and reads Jobael.
- **24:28** — The Greek text adds 'and Ithamar' and 'and Eleazar died' (neither in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and across vv. 22-30 preserves distinctive Greek name forms: Inath (v. 22), Jedimu, Amadiah, Jase, and Jokom (v. 23, omitting Hebron), Uzziah (vv. 26-27), Isoam and Abai (v. 27), and Elah and Arimoth (v. 30).
- **24:31** — The Greek text omits 'David' after 'the king,' uses singular 'chief,' and renders the Hebrew 'ancestral houses of the head' as 'the patriarchs of Araab,' followed by 'just as his younger brothers.'
