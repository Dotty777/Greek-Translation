## Text

**22:1** And David said, "This is the house of the Lord God, and this is the altar for burnt offering for Israel."

**22:2** And David said to gather all the proselytes in the land of Israel; and he appointed stonecutters to cut dressed stones to build a house for God.

**22:3** And David prepared much iron for the nails of the doors and of the gates and for the hinges, and bronze in abundance; there was no weighing it.

**22:4** And for cedar timbers, there was no number; for the Sidonians and the Tyrians were bringing cedar timbers in abundance to David.

**22:5** And David said, "Solomon my son is a tender young boy, and the house to be built for the Lord is to be for greatness above, for a name and for glory throughout all the earth; I will prepare for it." And David prepared in abundance before his death.

**22:6** And he called Solomon his son and commanded him to build the house for the Lord God of Israel.

**22:7** And David said to Solomon, "Child, it was upon my soul to build a house for the name of the Lord God.

**22:8** And a word of the Lord came to me, saying, 'You have shed blood in abundance, and you have made great wars; you shall not build a house for My name, because you have shed much blood upon the earth before Me.

**22:9** Look, a son is being born to you; he will be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all the enemies around, for Solomon is his name, and I will give peace and quiet upon Israel in his days.

**22:10** He will build a house for My name, and he will be to Me as a son, and I to him as a father, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom in Israel forever.'

**22:11** And now, son, the Lord will be with you, and He will grant success, and you will build a house for the Lord your God, as He spoke concerning you.

**22:12** Only may the Lord give you wisdom and understanding, and strengthen you over Israel, both to keep and to do the law of the Lord your God.

**22:13** Then He will grant success if you take care to do the statutes and the judgments that the Lord commanded Moses concerning Israel. Be courageous and be strong; do not fear and do not be terrified.

**22:14** And look, according to my poverty I have prepared for the house of the Lord one hundred thousand talents of gold, and one million talents of silver, and bronze and iron of which there is no weighing, because it is in abundance; and timbers and stones I have prepared, and add to these.

**22:15** And with you, add an abundance of those who do work: craftsmen and builders of stone and carpenters of wood, and every skilled person in every work,

**22:16** in gold, in silver, in bronze, and in iron, there is no number. Arise and act, and may the Lord be with you."

**22:17** And David commanded the servants, the leaders of Israel, to help Solomon his son:

**22:18** "Is not the Lord with you? And He has given you rest all around, for He has given the inhabitants of the land into your hands, and the land has been subdued before the Lord and before His people.

**22:19** Now give your hearts and your souls to seek the Lord your God, and rise up and build a sanctuary for your God, to bring in the ark of the covenant of the Lord and the holy vessels of God into the house being built for the name of the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — Greek proselytoi ("proselytes") is the Septuagint's standard equivalent for Hebrew gerim ("resident aliens").
- **22:11** — Here and in verse 13, Greek uses the third-person future active euodosei ("He will grant success") with the Lord as subject, whereas the Hebrew has second-person forms ("you will succeed").
- **22:15** — Greek includes the imperative prosthes ("add"), echoing the end of verse 14 and not present in the Hebrew.
- **22:17** — Greek reads tois paisin ("the servants"), likely an early scribal variant for pasin ("all"), as in 20:3; in verse 18 Greek has plural en chersin ("into your hands," without an explicit possessive pronoun) where Hebrew reads "into my hand."
