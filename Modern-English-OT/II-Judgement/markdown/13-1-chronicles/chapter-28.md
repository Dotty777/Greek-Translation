## Text

**28:1** And David assembled all the leaders of Israel, leaders of the judges, and the leaders of the divisions around the king's person, and leaders of the thousands and of the hundreds, and the treasurers, and those over his property, and the mighty men, and the warriors, in Jerusalem.

**28:2** And David stood in the midst of the assembly and said, "Listen to me, brothers and my people. It came upon my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the Lord and a standing place for the feet of our Lord, and I prepared the things needed for the dwelling place.

**28:3** But God said, 'You shall not build a house for Me to call My name upon it, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.'

**28:4** And the Lord, the God of Israel, chose me from all the house of my father to be king over Israel forever; and in Judah He has chosen the kingdom, and from the house of Judah, the house of my father; in me He took pleasure, that I should become king over all Israel.

**28:5** And from all my sons, because the Lord gave me many sons, He chose Solomon my son to seat him on the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

**28:6** And God said to me, 'Solomon your son will inherit My house and My court, because I have chosen him to be My son, and I will be a father to him.

**28:7** And I will establish his kingdom forever, if he is strong to keep My commandments and My judgments as on this day.'

**28:8** And now, in the sight of all the assembly of the Lord, seek the commandments of the Lord our God, so that you may inherit the good land for our sons forever.

**28:9** And now, Solomon, know the God of your fathers and serve with a complete heart and a willing soul, because the Lord examines all hearts and knows every thought; if you seek Him, He will be found by you, and if you forsake Him, He will forsake you to the end.

**28:10** See, therefore: the Lord has chosen you to build a house for Him as a sanctuary; be strong and act."

**28:11** Then David gave Solomon his son the pattern of the temple, and of its houses, and of its zakcho, and of the upper rooms, and of the inner storerooms, and of the house of atonement,

**28:12** and the pattern that he had in his spirit of the courts of the house of the Lord, and of all the chambers all around, those for the storerooms of the Lord and of the storerooms of the holy things,

**28:13** and of the lodging rooms of the divisions of the priests and of the Levites for all the work of the service of the house of the Lord, and of the storerooms of the vessels of service.

**28:14** And he gave him the measure of their weight, both of the gold and of the silver, the weight of the lampstands

**28:15** and of the lamps.

**28:16** Likewise he gave him the weight of the tables of the presentation, of each gold table, and in the same way of the silver ones,

**28:17** and of the meat hooks and libation vessels and the gold bowls, and the weight of the gold and of the silver ones, for each weight.

**28:18** And he showed him the weight of the altar of incense of refined gold, and the pattern of the chariot of the cherubim with their wings spread out and overshadowing the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**28:19** David gave to Solomon all things in writing from the hand of the Lord, according to the understanding that had come to him of the workmanship of the pattern.

**28:20** And David said to Solomon his son, "Be strong and courageous and act; do not fear and do not be terrified, because the Lord my God is with you; He will not fail you and will by no means forsake you until you finish all the work of the service of the house of the Lord. The pattern of the temple and of its house and of its sakcho, and the upper rooms, and the inner storerooms, and the house of atonement, and the pattern of the house of the Lord.

**28:21** And here are the divisions of the priests and of the Levites for all the service of the house of the Lord, and with you in every undertaking, and every willing person with wisdom in every craft, and the leaders and all the people for all your commands."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — Greek reads "leaders of the judges" (reflecting Hebrew shoftim) rather than "leaders of the tribes" (shevatim), and renders the royal attendants with the Hellenistic phrase "around the king's person."
- **28:2** — Greek reads "of our Lord" (kyriou hemon) rather than Hebrew "of our God," and "the things needed for the dwelling place" rather than "preparations to build."
- **28:6** — Greek reads "will inherit My house and My court" (singular) rather than Hebrew "shall build My house and My courts." In v. 8 the Greek text is shorter than the Hebrew and reads "for our sons" (first-person plural) rather than "for your sons."
- **28:11** — "Zakcho" (and "sakcho" in v. 20) is an untranslated Greek transliteration of a rare Hebrew loanword (ganzak) meaning treasuries or storerooms. Also in v. 11 Greek reads "of the temple" rather than Hebrew "of the porch," and in vv. 14-15 the Greek text is substantially shorter than the Hebrew.
- **28:19** — Unlike the Hebrew, which shifts into first-person speech from David, the Greek keeps v. 19 in third-person narrative ("David gave to Solomon"). In v. 20 the Greek adds a distinctive recapitulation of the temple plan from vv. 11-12 at the end of David's charge.
