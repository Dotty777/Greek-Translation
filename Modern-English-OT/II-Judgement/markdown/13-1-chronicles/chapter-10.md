## Text

**10:1** And the foreigners fought against Israel, and they fled from before the foreigners and fell wounded on Mount Gilboa.

**10:2** And the foreigners pursued after Saul and after his sons; and the foreigners struck down Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.

**10:3** And the battle grew heavy against Saul; and the archers found them with bows and pains, and they suffered pain from the bows.

**10:4** And Saul said to the one who carried his armor, "Draw your sword and pierce me through with it, so that these uncircumcised men may not come and mock me." But the one who carried his armor was not willing, because he was greatly afraid; so Saul took the sword and fell upon it.

**10:5** And the one who carried his armor saw that Saul had died, and he also fell upon his sword.

**10:6** And Saul and three of his sons died on that day, and all his house died together.

**10:7** And every man of Israel who was in the valley saw that Israel had fled, and that Saul and his sons had died, and they abandoned their cities and fled; and the foreigners came and lived in them.

**10:8** And it happened on the next day that the foreigners came to plunder the wounded, and they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

**10:9** And they stripped him, and took his head and his armor, and sent word into the land of the foreigners all around to proclaim the good news to their idols and to the people.

**10:10** And they placed their armor in the house of their god, and they placed his head in the house of Dagon.

**10:11** And all those who lived in Gilead heard everything that the foreigners had done to Saul and to Israel.

**10:12** And every mighty man arose from Gilead, and they took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh; and they fasted seven days.

**10:13** And Saul died because of his lawless deeds that he committed against God concerning the word of the Lord, because he did not keep it; because Saul consulted the medium to inquire, and Samuel the prophet answered him,

**10:14** and he did not seek the Lord; so He put him to death and turned the kingdom over to David son of Jesse.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek 'allophyloi' (literally 'foreigners') is the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines from Judges onward and is translated consistently throughout this chapter.
- **10:3** — Unlike the singular Hebrew text, the Greek uses plural forms ('found them ... they suffered pain'), includes 'and pains,' and reads 'from the bows' rather than 'from the archers.'
- **10:10** — The Greek reads plural 'their armor' (rather than 'his armor'), singular 'their god,' and 'placed his head' rather than 'fastened his skull.'
- **10:13** — The clause 'and Samuel the prophet answered him' is a notable Septuagint addition not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (cf. 1 Samuel 28:15-19).
