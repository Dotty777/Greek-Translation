## Text

**5:1** And the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: for he was the firstborn, and when he went up onto his father's bed, he gave his blessing to his son Joseph, the son of Israel, and he was not enrolled in the genealogy for the birthright,

**5:2** for Judah was mighty in strength even among his brothers, and a ruler was to come from him, and the blessing belonged to Joseph.

**5:3** The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**5:4** The sons of Joel: Shimei, and Benaiah his son. And the sons of Gog, the son of Shimei:

**5:5** his son Echa, his son Recha, his son Joel,

**5:6** his son Beel, whom Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria exiled; he was chief of the Reubenites.

**5:7** And his brothers, by his clan, in their genealogical enrollments according to their descent: the chief, Joel, and Zechariah,

**5:8** and Balek the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel; he lived in Aroer and up to Nebo and Beelmasson,

**5:9** and toward the east he lived as far as the entrance of the wilderness from the river Euphrates, because he had much livestock in the land of Gilead.

**5:10** And in the days of Saul they made war against the resident aliens, and they fell by their hands, living in tents, even all of them to the east of Gilead.

**5:11** The sons of Gad lived opposite them in Bashan as far as Elcha:

**5:12** Joel the firstborn, and Sabat the second, and Janin the scribe in Bashan.

**5:13** And their brothers, according to their ancestral houses: Michael, Meshullam, and Sheba, and Jorai, and Chima, and Zia, and Obed, eight.

**5:14** These were the sons of Abihaia, the sons of Huri, the son of Idai, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Isai, the son of Juri, the son of Zabucham,

**5:15** the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni; chief of an ancestral house,

**5:16** of the inhabitants in Gilead, in Bashan and in their villages, and all the surrounding regions of Geriam as far as the exit.

**5:17** The genealogical enrollment of all of them was in the days of Jotham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

**5:18** The sons of Reuben and Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh, from the sons of might, men bearing shields and a sword, and drawing the bow, and trained in war, were forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty going out to battle formation.

**5:19** And they made war with the Hagrites, the Turaeans, the Naphisadaeans, and the Nodabites,

**5:20** and they prevailed over them; and the Hagrites and all their encampments were given into their hands, because they cried out to God in the war and He listened to them, because they hoped in Him.

**5:21** And they captured their possessions: five thousand camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand souls of men;

**5:22** for many wounded fell, because the war was from God. And they lived in their place until the exile.

**5:23** And the half-tribe of Manasseh lived from Bashan as far as Baileim and Senir and Mount Hermon; and in Lebanon they multiplied.

**5:24** And these were the heads of their ancestral house: Epher, and Sei, and Eliel, and Azriel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jeliel, men strong in might, men of renown, chiefs of their ancestral houses.

**5:25** And they acted unfaithfully against the God of their fathers, and they prostituted themselves after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had removed from before them.

**5:26** And the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Phaloch king of Assyria and the spirit of Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and he exiled Reuben and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Chaach and Habor and to the river Chozar, to this day.

**5:27** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**5:28** And the sons of Kohath: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**5:29** And the children of Amram: Aaron and Moses and Miriam. And the sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**5:30** Eleazar fathered Phinehas, Phinehas fathered Abishua,

**5:31** Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi,

**5:32** Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, Zerahiah fathered Mariel,

**5:33** and Mariel fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub,

**5:34** and Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz,

**5:35** and Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan,

**5:36** and Johanan fathered Azariah; he served as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**5:37** And Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub,

**5:38** and Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum,

**5:39** and Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah,

**5:40** and Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.

**5:41** And Jehozadak went in the exile with Judah and Jerusalem by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek reads 'his blessing' here and in v. 2 (MT 'birthright') and singular 'his son Joseph' (MT 'the sons of Joseph').
- **5:4** — The Greek Reubenite genealogy (vv. 4-8) diverges from MT, reading 'Shimei' and the proper name 'Benaiah' (for MT 'his son') here, 'Echa,' 'Recha,' 'Joel,' and 'Beel' in vv. 5-6, 'Joel' in v. 7, and 'Balek' and 'Beelmasson' in v. 8.
- **5:13** — Greek lists seven names, including 'Chima' (MT Jacan) and 'Obed' (MT Eber), yet totals them as 'eight' (MT 'seven'). Elsewhere in vv. 10-16, Greek reads 'resident aliens' (v. 10, MT Hagrites), 'Elcha,' 'Sabat,' 'Janin the scribe' (vv. 11-12), 'Abihaia,' 'Idai,' 'Isai,' 'Juri,' 'Zabucham' (v. 14), and 'Geriam' (v. 16).
- **5:21** — Greek reads 'five thousand camels' (MT fifty thousand), with other notable LXX readings in vv. 19-24 including 'Turaeans' and 'Naphisadaeans' (v. 19), 'prevailed over them' and 'encampments' (v. 20), and 'Baileim,' 'in Lebanon,' 'Sei,' and 'Jeliel' (vv. 23-24).
- **5:26** — Greek reads 'Phaloch' (MT Pul), 'Chaach' (MT Halah), and 'Chozar' (MT Gozan) while omitting MT's Hara; Tiglath-pileser appears in Greek as Thalgabanasar (v. 6) and Thagnaphamasar (v. 26).
- **5:32** — Greek reads 'Mariel' here and in v. 33 (MT Meraioth). Verses 27-41 follow the LXX and Hebrew chapter division (1 Chronicles 6:1-15 in most English Bibles), with v. 41 omitting MT's mention of the Lord.
