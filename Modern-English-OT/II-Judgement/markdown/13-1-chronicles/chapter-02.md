## Text

**2:1** These are the names of the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.

**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, three; they were born to him by the daughter of Aua the Canaanite woman. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was wicked before the Lord, and He put him to death.

**2:4** And Tamar his daughter-in-law bore him Perez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

**2:5** The sons of Perez: Harson and Jemuel.

**2:6** And the sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calca, and Dara, five in all.

**2:7** And the sons of Carmi: Achar, the obstructor of Israel, who acted unfaithfully concerning the devoted thing.

**2:8** And the sons of Ethan: Zaria.

**2:9** And the sons of Hezron who were born to him: Jerahmeel, Ram, Chabel, and Aram.

**2:10** And Arran fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, ruler of the house of Judah.

**2:11** And Nahshon fathered Salmon, and Salmon fathered Boaz.

**2:12** And Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse,

**2:13** and Jesse fathered his firstborn Eliab, Amminadab the second, Shimea the third,

**2:14** Nethanel the fourth, Zaddai the fifth,

**2:15** Ozem the sixth, David the seventh.

**2:16** And their sister was Zeruiah. And the sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three.

**2:17** And Abigail gave birth to Amasab, and the father of Amasab was Jether the Ishmaelite.

**2:18** And Caleb, the son of Hezron, fathered Azubah, a wife, and Elioth. And these are her sons: Joasar, Jasub, and Orna.

**2:19** And Azubah died, and Caleb took Ephrath for himself, and she bore him Hur.

**2:20** And Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.

**2:21** And after these things Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead, and he took her, and he was sixty years old; and she bore him Serug.

**2:22** And Serug fathered Jair. And he had twenty-three cities in Gilead.

**2:23** And Geshur and Arran took the villages of Sair from them, Kenath and its villages, sixty cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir, the father of Gilead.

**2:24** And after Hezron died, Caleb came to Ephrathah; and the wife of Hezron was Abijah, and she bore him Ascho, the father of Tekoa.

**2:25** And the sons of Jerahmeel, the firstborn of Hezron, were Ram the firstborn, Benaiah, Araiah, Amram, and Asan his brother.

**2:26** And Jerahmeel had another wife, and her name was Atarah; she is the mother of Ozom.

**2:27** And the sons of Aram, the firstborn of Jerahmeel, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.

**2:28** And the sons of Ozom were Shammai and Jada, and the sons of Shammai were Nadab and Abishur.

**2:29** And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abihaia, and she bore him Achabar and Moel.

**2:30** The sons of Nadab: Alsalad and Ephraim; and Salad died having no children.

**2:31** And the sons of Ephraim: Isemiel; and the sons of Isemiel: Sheshan; and the sons of Sheshan: Achai.

**2:32** And the sons of Iduda: Achisamas, Jether, Jonathan; and Jether died having no children.

**2:33** And the sons of Jonathan: Thaleth and Ozam. These were the sons of Jerahmeel.

**2:34** And Sheshan had no sons, but only daughters. And Sheshan had an Egyptian servant, and his name was Jochel.

**2:35** And Sheshan gave his daughter to Jochel his servant as a wife, and she bore him Attai.

**2:36** And Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad,

**2:37** and Zabad fathered Aphamel, and Aphamel fathered Obed,

**2:38** and Obed fathered Joshua, and Joshua fathered Azariah,

**2:39** and Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Emas,

**2:40** and Emas fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shallum,

**2:41** and Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

**2:42** And the sons of Caleb, the brother of Jerahmeel: Mareshah his firstborn (he was the father of Ziph), and the sons of Mareshah, the father of Hebron.

**2:43** And the sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.

**2:44** And Shema fathered Ramee, the father of Jaklan, and Jaklan fathered Shammai.

**2:45** And his son was Maon, and Maon was the father of Gedsur.

**2:46** And Gaiphael, the concubine of Caleb, gave birth to Haran, Josan, and Gezue. And Haran fathered Gezue.

**2:47** And the sons of Joshua: Regem, Jotham, Sogar, Peleg, Ephah, and Sagae.

**2:48** And the concubine of Caleb, Maacah, gave birth to Sheber and Tharam.

**2:49** And she gave birth to Sagae, the father of Marmena, and Sau, the father of Machbenah and the father of Gaibal. And the daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

**2:50** These were the sons of Caleb. The sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim;

**2:51** Solomon, the father of Betha; Lammon, the father of Bethlehem; Harim, the father of Beth-gaidon.

**2:52** And Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim, had sons: Haio, Hesira, Monaio,

**2:53** Emospheos, the cities of Jair: Haithalim, Miphithim, Hesamathim, and Hemasaraim. From these came out the Zorathites and the sons of Esthaam.

**2:54** The sons of Solomon: Bethlehem, Metophathi, Ataroth of the house of Joab, and half of Malathi, Hesari,

**2:55** the families of writings living in Games: Argathim, Samathim, Socathim. These are the Kenites who came from Mesema, the father of the house of Recha.

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — The Greek lists four sons of Hezron (Jerahmeel, Ram, Chabel, and Aram) and names Arran in v. 10, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists three sons (Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai) with Ram in v. 10. Throughout vv. 3-17, the Septuagint also preserves several distinct proper-name forms, including Aua (v. 3), Harson and Jemuel (v. 5), Calca (v. 6), Zaria (v. 8), Zaddai (v. 14), and Amasab (v. 17).
- **2:18** — The Greek takes Azubah and Elioth as direct objects of "fathered" ("Azubah, a wife, and Elioth") rather than "with Azubah his wife and with Jerioth" as in the Hebrew, and names her sons Joasar, Jasub, and Orna (Hebrew: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon).
- **2:24** — The Greek reads "Caleb came to Ephrathah" (with Ascho as Abijah's son), whereas the Hebrew reads "in Caleb-ephrathah" (with Ashhur). Similarly, vv. 21-23 preserve the distinct Greek names Serug, Arran, and Sair (Hebrew: Segub, Aram, and Jair).
- **2:32** — The Greek reads the Hebrew "Jada, brother of Shammai" as a father and an additional first son ("Iduda: Achisamas"), yielding three sons instead of two. Surrounding verses (vv. 25-49) likewise preserve numerous Septuagint-distinct names, such as Ozom, Alsalad, Ephraim, Isemiel, Jochel, Joshua (vv. 38, 47), Emas, Jaklan, Gedsur, Gaiphael, Peleg, and Sagae.
- **2:51** — The Greek expands the line into three pairs (Solomon of Betha, Lammon of Bethlehem, and Harim of Beth-gaidon; Hebrew: Salma of Bethlehem and Hareph of Beth-gader). Verses 52-55 also follow distinct Septuagint readings, including transliterated clan names, "the cities of Jair" (v. 53), Solomon again (v. 54), and "the families of writings" (v. 55, reflecting Hebrew sepharim "writings" rather than sopherim "scribes").
