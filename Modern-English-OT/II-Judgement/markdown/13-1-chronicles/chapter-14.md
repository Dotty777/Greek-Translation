## Text

**14:1** And Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David, and cedar logs and builders and woodworkers, to build a house for him.

**14:2** And David knew that the Lord had prepared him over Israel, because his kingdom was increased to a height on account of His people Israel.

**14:3** And David took more wives in Jerusalem; and more sons and daughters were born to David.

**14:4** And these are the names of those born who were his in Jerusalem: Shammua, Isoboam, Nathan, Solomon,

**14:5** and Baar, and Ektae, and Eliphelet,

**14:6** and Nageth, and Naphath, and Januu,

**14:7** and Elishama, and Balegdae, and Emphalet.

**14:8** And the foreigners heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, and all the foreigners went up to seek David; and David heard, and went out to meet them.

**14:9** And the foreigners came and rushed together in the Valley of the Giants.

**14:10** And David inquired through God, saying, "Should I go up against the foreigners, and will You give them into my hands?" And the Lord said to him, "Go up, and I will give them into your hands."

**14:11** And he went up to Phaalphathisim, and David struck them there; and David said, "He has broken through my enemies by my hand like a breakthrough of water." Because of this, he called the name of that place Breakthrough of Pharisin.

**14:12** And they abandoned their gods, and David said to burn them in fire.

**14:13** And once more the foreigners rushed together again in the Valley of the Giants.

**14:14** And David inquired again of God, and God said to him, "You shall not go after them; turn away from them, and you will come to them near the pear trees.

**14:15** And it will be, when you hear the sound of the shaking of the very tops of the pear trees, then you will go out to the battle, because God has gone out before you to strike the camp of the foreigners."

**14:16** And he did just as God commanded him, and he struck the camp of the foreigners from Gibeon as far as Gezer.

**14:17** And the name of David came to be in all the earth, and the Lord put the fear of him upon all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **14:4** — Several names of David's sons in 14:4-7 preserve distinct Septuagint forms (Isoboam, Baar, Ektae, Nageth, Naphath, Januu, Balegdae, and Emphalet) differing from the Hebrew list.
- **14:8** — Here and throughout 14:8-16 (as in 12:20), Greek allophyloi ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard rendering for the Philistines; in 14:9 and 14:13, "Valley of the Giants" translates Hebrew "Valley of Rephaim."
- **14:11** — Greek reads "Phaalphathisim" and "Breakthrough of Pharisin" for Hebrew "Baal-perazim," and omits an explicit subject "God" before "has broken through."
- **14:14** — Greek renders the trees as "pear trees" (Hebrew: balsam or mulberry trees) and in 14:15 reads "the sound of the shaking of the very tops" (Hebrew: "the sound of marching in the tops").
