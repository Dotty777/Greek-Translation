## Translator Notes

- **1:10** — Greek reads "giant hunter" (aligning with Gen 10:9 LXX) rather than MT "mighty one." In vv. 5-9 the Greek also adds Elishah among the sons of Japheth (v. 5), reads Riphath (v. 6) and "the Rhodians" (v. 7) where MT has Diphath and Rodanim, and reads Judadan (v. 9, absorbing the Hebrew conjunction) for MT Dedan.
- **1:17** — The Old Greek text omits vv. 11-16 and vv. 18-23, passing directly from Shem's sons Elam and Asshur to Arpachshad and the patriarchal line down to Abraham (vv. 24-27, where v. 27 reads simply "Abraham" without MT "Abram, that is").
- **1:30** — Verses 29-37 preserve several LXX-distinct readings and names: "of the firstborn of Ishmael" with Nabdael, Massa, Mama, Iduma, Manasseh, Chondan, and Teman (vv. 29-30); reversed pairs Midian/Medan and Dedan/Sheba alongside Sobak and Gapher (vv. 32-33); "Jacob and Esau" (v. 34, MT "Esau and Israel"); and Jeul, Zophar, "by Timna, Amalek" (clarifying Timna as mother), and Omoze (vv. 35-37).
- **1:40** — The Horite genealogy in vv. 38-42 differs extensively in the Greek: Onan appears in place of Ezer and Dishan (v. 38), Onam (v. 40), and Jaakan (v. 42); Heman, Elath, and Namna appear in v. 39 instead of Homam and Lotan's sister Timna; and vv. 40-42 read Solam, Machanam, Sob, Aith, Sonan (for Anah), Gethram, Balaam (for Bilhan), and Zukam (for Zaavan).
- **1:47** — Verses 43-54 preserve a major LXX textual dislocation: v. 43 omits the reference to Edom and Israel's kings, v. 47 breaks off before its subject, v. 48 repeats Hadad's death, and v. 49 contains the displaced king Balaennor son of Achbor (MT Baal-hanan). Distinct Greek names in this section also include Balak (vv. 43-44), Barad and Gittaim (v. 46), Samaa from Masekkah (v. 48), Hadad son of Barad and Peor without his wife's name (v. 50), and Teman, Gola, Jetheth, Elibamas, Mazar, Mediel, and Zaphoin (vv. 51-54, MT Timna, Alvah, Jetheth ... Iram).
