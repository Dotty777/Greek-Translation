## Text

**13:1** And David took counsel with the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, with every leader;

**13:2** and David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it is good to you, and it has been prospered from the Lord our God, let us send to your brothers who remain in all the land of Israel, and with them the priests, the Levites, in the cities of their possession, and they will be gathered to us,

**13:3** and let us bring over the ark of our God to us; because they have not sought it since the days of Saul."

**13:4** And all the assembly said to do so, because the word was right in the eyes of all the people.

**13:5** And David assembled all Israel from the borders of Egypt even to the entrance of Hamath, to bring in the ark of God from the city of Jearim.

**13:6** And David brought it up; and all Israel went up to the city of David, which belonged to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God the Lord, who sits upon the cherubim, where His name was called.

**13:7** And they put the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Amminadab; and Uzza and his brothers were driving the cart.

**13:8** And David and all Israel were playing before God with all might, and with psalm-singers and with lyres and with harps, with tambourines and with cymbals and with trumpets.

**13:9** And they came as far as the threshing floor; and Uzza stretched out his hand to take hold of the ark, and the calf turned it aside.

**13:10** And the Lord became angry against Uzza, and struck him there because he stretched out his hand upon the ark; and he died there before God.

**13:11** And David was disheartened because the Lord had broken through with a breakthrough against Uzza, and he called that place Breakthrough of Uzza until this day.

**13:12** And David feared God on that day, saying, "How will I bring in the ark of God to myself?"

**13:13** And David did not turn the ark to himself into the city of David, but turned it aside into the house of Abeddara the Gittite.

**13:14** And the ark of God stayed in the house of Abeddara three months; and God blessed Abeddaram and all that belonged to him.

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — Greek reads "David brought it up; and all Israel went up to the city of David," differing from the Hebrew ("David and all Israel went up to Baalah, to Kiriath-jearim").
- **13:7** — Greek reads "Amminadab" (Hebrew: "Abinadab") and plural "his brothers" (Hebrew: "Ahio" or "his brother").
- **13:9** — Greek omits the name of the threshing floor (Hebrew: "Chidon") and reads "and the calf turned it aside" (Greek moschos, "calf," also in 12:41).
- **13:13** — Greek reads "Abeddara" (and "Abeddaram" in 13:14), a Septuagint form corresponding to Hebrew "Obed-edom."
