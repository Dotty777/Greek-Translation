## Text

**9:1** And all Israel, their enrollment—and these are recorded in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah, with those who were resettled in Babylon because of their lawless deeds.

**9:2** And those who formerly lived in their possessions in the cities of Israel were Levite sons, the priests, and those who were given.

**9:3** And in Jerusalem there lived some of the sons of Judah, and some of the sons of Benjamin, and of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh.

**9:4** And Gothi, and the son of Sammiu, son of Omri, sons of the sons of Perez and of the son of Judah.

**9:5** And from the Shilonites, Asaiah his firstborn.

**9:6** From the sons of Zerah, Epiel and their brothers, six hundred ninety.

**9:7** And from the sons of Benjamin: Shallum son of Moollam, son of Hodaviah, son of Haana,

**9:8** and Banaam son of Jeroham; and these were the sons of Uzzi son of Machir; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Benaiah;

**9:9** and their brothers according to their generations were nine hundred fifty-six; all the men were leaders of families according to their family houses.

**9:10** And from the priests: Jedaiah, and Joiarim, and Jachin,

**9:11** and Azariah, sons of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Marmoth, son of Ahitub, ruler of the house of God,

**9:12** and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasaiah son of Adiel, son of Jediu, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer,

**9:13** and their brothers, leaders of family houses, one thousand seven hundred sixty, mighty in strength for the work of the service of the house of God.

**9:14** And from the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, from the sons of Merari,

**9:15** and Bakar, and Rarael, Gilead, and Mattaniah son of Micah, son of Zichri, son of Asaph,

**9:16** and Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Gilead, son of Jothon; and Berechiah son of Ossa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.

**9:17** The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akum, Tammam, and Aimam, and their brothers; Shallum was the leader;

**9:18** and until this time they are at the king's gate toward the east; these are the gates of the camps of the sons of Levi.

**9:19** And Solomon son of Koreb, son of Abiasaph, son of Korah, and his brothers of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the works of the service, guarding the watches of the tent; and their fathers were over the camp, guarding the entrance.

**9:20** And Phinehas son of Eleazar was ruler over them formerly, and these were with him.

**9:21** Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper of the door of the tent of testimony.

**9:22** All those chosen for the gates, at the gates, were two hundred twelve; these were in their courtyards—their enrollment; David and Samuel the seer established these in their faithfulness.

**9:23** And these and their sons were over the gates in the house of the Lord, in the house of the tent, to keep guard.

**9:24** Toward the four winds were the gates: toward the east, the sea, the north, and the south.

**9:25** And their brothers were in their courtyards, to enter every seven days from time to time with these,

**9:26** for four mighty men of the gates are in faithfulness. The Levites were over the chambers, and over the treasuries of the house of God they will encamp,

**9:27** for a watch is upon them; and these are over the keys morning by morning to open the doors of the temple.

**9:28** And some of them are over the vessels of the service, for they will bring them in by number.

**9:29** And some of them were appointed over the vessels and over all the holy vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.

**9:30** And some of the sons of the priests were mixers of the ointment and for the spices.

**9:31** And Mattathias from the Levites—he was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite—was in faithfulness over the works of the sacrifice of the griddle of the high priest.

**9:32** And Benaiah the Kohathite, from their brothers, was over the loaves of presentation, to prepare them Sabbath by Sabbath.

**9:33** And these were the singers, leaders of the families of the Levites, appointed divisions; for day and night were upon them in the works.

**9:34** These were the leaders of the families, leaders according to their generations; these lived in Jerusalem.

**9:35** And in Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, and the name of his wife was Maacah;

**9:36** and his firstborn son was Sabadon, and Isir, and Kish, and Baal, and Ner, and Nadab,

**9:37** and Jedur, and a brother, and Zechariah, and Mikloth.

**9:38** And Mikloth fathered Shamaa. And these lived in the midst of their brothers in Jerusalem, in the midst of their brothers.

**9:39** And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, and Malchishua, and Amminadab, and Eshbaal.

**9:40** And the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; and Merib-baal fathered Micah.

**9:41** And the sons of Micah: Pithon, and Melech, and Tahrea.

**9:42** And Ahaz fathered Jada, and Jada fathered Gameleth, and Gazaoth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Moza,

**9:43** and Moza fathered Baana; Rephaiah was his son, Azel his son.

**9:44** And Azel had six sons, and these were their names: Azrikam his firstborn, and Ishmael, and Seraiah, and Obadiah, and Hanan; these were the sons of Azel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Greek reads 'in the cities of Israel' followed by 'Levite sons, the priests, and those who were given' (Greek 'hoi dedomenoi,' literally rendering the Hebrew Nethinim, or temple servants). In 9:1 the Greek likewise joins 'Judah' to 'the kings of Israel and Judah' rather than starting a separate clause about Judah's exile.
- **9:8** — The Greek reads 'and these were the sons of Uzzi son of Machir' (interpreting Hebrew Elah as 'these' and Michri as Machir), alongside other distinct Septuagint names across 9:4-17 such as Gothi, Sammiu, Epiel, Shallum, Moollam, Haana, Banaam, Benaiah, Joiarim, Marmoth, Jediu, Bakar, Rarael, Gilead, Jothon, Ossa, Akum, Tammam, and Aimam.
- **9:20** — The Greek reads 'and these were with him' instead of 'and the Lord was with him.' In 9:19 it likewise preserves the distinct Septuagint readings 'Solomon son of Koreb' (for Shallum son of Kore) and 'guarding the watches' (for thresholds).
- **9:32** — The Greek treats Hebrew 'sons of' as the proper name 'Benaiah the Kohathite.' Similarly, 9:31 adds 'of the high priest' to the griddle sacrifice, and 9:33 reads 'appointed divisions' where the Hebrew describes chambers free from other service.
- **9:44** — Although six sons are stated, only five names appear because the Greek renders Hebrew Bocheru as 'his firstborn.' Elsewhere in 9:36-43 the Greek preserves distinct readings including Sabadon, Isir, Jedur, 'a brother' (translating Ahio as a common noun in 9:37), Jada, Gameleth, Gazaoth, Baana, and the omission of Eleasah before Azel (Greek 'Esael').
