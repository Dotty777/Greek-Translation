## Text

**21:1** And the devil stood up in Israel, and incited David to number Israel.

**21:2** And King David said to Joab and to the leaders of the force, "Go now, number Israel from Beersheba even to Dan, and bring it to me, and I will know their number."

**21:3** And Joab said, "May the Lord add to His people a hundred times as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king look on. All of them are servants to my lord. Why does my lord seek this? Let it not become a sin to Israel."

**21:4** And he came to Jerusalem.

**21:5** And Joab gave the number of the mustering of the people to David; and all Israel was one million one hundred thousand men who drew the sword.

**21:6** And he did not number Levi and Benjamin among them, because the word of the king prevailed over Joab.

**21:7** And it was evil before God concerning this matter, and He struck Israel.

**21:8** And David said to God, "I have sinned greatly because I did this thing; and now please take away the wickedness of Your servant, for I have been exceedingly foolish."

**21:9** And the Lord spoke to Gad the seer:

**21:10** "Go and speak to David, saying, 'Thus says the Lord: Three things I lift up over you; choose for yourself one of them, and I will do it to you.'"

**21:11** And Gad came to David and said to him, "Thus says the Lord: 'Choose for yourself

**21:12** either three years of famine, or three months for you to flee from before your enemies, and a sword from your enemies to destroy, or for three days the sword of the Lord and death in the land, and the angel of the Lord destroying in all the inheritance of Israel.' And now see what I should answer to Him who sent word."

**21:13** And David said to Gad, "Even all three are exceedingly distressing to me; I will fall now into the hands of the Lord, for His mercies are exceedingly many, and into the hands of men I will by no means fall."

**21:14** And the Lord gave death in Israel, and seventy thousand men fell from Israel.

**21:15** And God sent an angel to Jerusalem to destroy it. And as he was destroying, the Lord saw and relented concerning the calamity, and said to the angel who was destroying, "Let it be enough for you; hold back your hand." And the angel of the Lord was standing at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:16** And David lifted up his eyes and saw the angel of the Lord standing between the earth and heaven, and his sword was drawn in his hand, stretched out over Jerusalem; and David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell on their faces.

**21:17** And David said to God, "Was it not I who said to number the people? And I am the one who sinned; I have indeed done evil. But these sheep, what have they done? Lord God, let Your hand be upon me and upon the house of my father, and not upon Your people for destruction, Lord."

**21:18** And the angel of the Lord told Gad to say to David that he should go up to set up an altar to the Lord at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:19** And David went up according to the word of Gad, which he had spoken in the name of the Lord.

**21:20** And Ornan turned and saw the king and his four sons with him, methachabein; and Ornan was threshing wheat.

**21:21** And David came to Ornan, and Ornan came out from the threshing floor and bowed down to David with his face to the ground.

**21:22** And David said to Ornan, "Give me your place of the threshing floor, and I will build on it an altar to the Lord; for silver of full worth give it to me, and the plague will cease from the people."

**21:23** And Ornan said to David, "Take it for yourself, and let my lord the king do what is good before him; look, I have given the calves for a burnt offering, and the plow for wood, and the grain for a sacrifice; I have given everything."

**21:24** And King David said to Ornan, "No, for I will surely buy it for silver of full worth; for I will by no means take what belongs to you for the Lord, to offer up a burnt offering without cost to the Lord."

**21:25** And David gave Ornan for his place six hundred shekels of gold by weight.

**21:26** And David built there an altar to the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings and offerings of deliverance; and he cried out to the Lord, and He answered him with fire from heaven upon the altar of burnt offering, and consumed the burnt offering.

**21:27** And the Lord spoke to the angel, and he put the sword away into the sheath.

**21:28** At that time, when David saw that He had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he sacrificed there.

**21:29** Now the tent of the Lord, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offerings were at that time in Bamoth in Gibeon.

**21:30** And David was not able to go before it to seek God, because he did not hasten from before the sword of the angel of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — Greek diabolos lacks the definite article and can mean "an adversary/accuser" or "the devil" (rendering Hebrew satan); Greek also reads "in Israel" rather than "against Israel."
- **21:4** — The Greek text is much shorter than the Hebrew, omitting Joab's departure and tour through Israel; the Hebrew clause "the king's word prevailed over Joab" appears instead in verse 6 (replacing Hebrew "was abhorrent to Joab"), and verse 5 omits the separate count for Judah.
- **21:20** — Greek reads "the king" (Hebrew "the angel") and leaves the Hebrew participle mithabbeim ("hiding themselves") untranslated as methachabein.
- **21:26** — The final clause "and consumed the burnt offering" is a Septuagint addition not present in the Hebrew text.
- **21:30** — Greek ou katespeusen ("did not hasten") differs from Hebrew "was terrified"; in verse 29 Greek also transliterates Hebrew bamah ("high place") as the place name "Bamoth."
