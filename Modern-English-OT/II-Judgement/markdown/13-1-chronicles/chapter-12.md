## Text

**12:1** And these are those who came to David at Sokla, while he was still confined from the presence of Saul son of Kish; and these were among the mighty men, helping in war,

**12:2** and with a bow from the right and from the left, and slingers with stones and bows, among the brothers of Saul from Benjamin:

**12:3** the chief, Ahiezer, and Joa the son of Ama the Gibeathite, and Joel and Jophalet, sons of Azmaveth, and Berechiah, and Jehul the Anathothite,

**12:4** and Shemaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the thirty and over the thirty, Jeremiah, and Jezel, and Johanan, and Joazabab the Gederathite,

**12:6** Azai, and Arimoth, and Badaiah, and Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite,

**12:7** Elkanah, and Jesuni, and Azarel, and Jozara, and Sobokam, and the Korahites,

**12:8** and Elia and Zebadiah, sons of Raham, and those of Gedor.

**12:9** And from the Gadites, strong, mighty men of battle array for war separated themselves to David from the wilderness, carrying shields and spears; and their faces were the face of a lion, and they were nimble as gazelles on the mountains in swiftness:

**12:10** Aza the chief, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,

**12:11** Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,

**12:12** Attai the sixth, Eliab the seventh,

**12:13** Joan the eighth, Eliezer the ninth,

**12:14** Jeremiah the tenth, Melchabannai the eleventh.

**12:15** These from the sons of Gad were chiefs of the army; the least one was for a hundred, and the greatest for a thousand.

**12:16** These are those who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was overflowing over every bank of it; and they drove out all those inhabiting the valleys, from the east to the west.

**12:17** And some from the sons of Benjamin and Judah came to the help of David.

**12:18** And David went out to meet them and said to them, "If you have come to me for peace, may my heart be at one toward you; and if to hand me over to my enemies not in truth of hand, may the God of your fathers see and reprove."

**12:19** And a spirit clothed Amasai, chief of the thirty, and he said, "Go, and your people, David son of Jesse; peace, peace to you, and peace to your helpers, because your God has helped you." And David received them and appointed them chiefs of the forces.

**12:20** And some from Manasseh went over to David when the foreigners came against Saul to war; and they did not help them, because it came about in counsel from the commanders of the foreigners, saying, "With the heads of those men he will return to his lord Saul."

**12:21** When he went to Soglam, there went over to him from Manasseh Adnah, and Tozabath, and Rodiel, and Michael, and Josabaith, and Elimuth, and Semathi; they are captains of thousands of Manasseh.

**12:22** And they fought alongside David against the geddur, because they were all mighty men of strength; and they were leaders in the army, in the force,

**12:23** for day by day they were coming to David, into a great force, like a force of God.

**12:24** And these are the names of the chiefs of the army who came to David at Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the word of the Lord.

**12:25** The sons of Judah, shield-bearers and spear-bearers: six thousand eight hundred mighty men of battle array.

**12:26** Of the sons of Simeon, mighty men of strength for battle array: seven thousand one hundred.

**12:27** Of the sons of Levi: four thousand six hundred;

**12:28** and Toadas, the leader for Aaron, and with him three thousand seven hundred;

**12:29** and Zadok, a young man mighty in strength, and of his father's house twenty-two chiefs.

**12:30** And of the sons of Benjamin, the brothers of Saul: three thousand; and still the greater part of them was keeping watch over the guard of the house of Saul.

**12:31** And from the sons of Ephraim: twenty thousand eight hundred, mighty in strength, men of renown according to their ancestral houses.

**12:32** And from the half-tribe of Manasseh: eighteen thousand, who also were named by name to make David king.

**12:33** And from the sons of Issachar, knowing understanding for the times, knowing what Israel should do: two hundred; and all their brothers were with them.

**12:34** And from Zebulun, going out to battle array of war with all weapons of war: fifty thousand, to help David not empty-handed.

**12:35** And from Naphtali: one thousand chiefs, and with them, with shields and spears, thirty-seven thousand.

**12:36** And from the Danites, drawing up in battle array for war: twenty-eight thousand eight hundred.

**12:37** And from Asher, going out to help in war: forty thousand.

**12:38** And from beyond the Jordan, from Reuben and the Gadites and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, with all weapons of war: one hundred twenty thousand.

**12:39** All these were fighting men, drawing up in battle array with a peaceful soul, and they came to Hebron to make David king over all Israel; and the rest of Israel was of one soul to make David king.

**12:40** And they were there three days, eating and drinking, because their brothers had made preparation.

**12:41** And those bordering on them, as far as Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, were bringing to them on camels and donkeys and mules and on calves food, flour, fig cakes, raisins, wine and oil, calves and sheep in abundance, because there was gladness in Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Greek reads "Sokla" here and "Soglam" in 12:21, Septuagint forms corresponding to Hebrew "Ziklag." Several personal names in 12:3-14, 21, and 28 also preserve distinct Greek textual forms (such as Joel for Jeziel, Eliab for Eliel, Eliezer for Elzabad, and Toadas for Jehoiada).
- **12:18** — Greek "at one" renders the idiom kath' heauten ("by itself / in accord"), and "not in truth of hand" differs from the Hebrew ("with no violence in my hands").
- **12:19** — Greek reads "Go, and your people" (taking the Hebrew consonants as an imperative and "your people"), where the Hebrew has "Yours we are... and with you."
- **12:22** — Greek ton geddour ("the geddur") is an untranslated transliteration (with a d/r shift) of the Hebrew word for "the raiding band."
- **12:34** — Greek reads "not empty-handed" (Hebrew: "not with a double heart"); in 12:36, Greek reads "twenty-eight thousand eight hundred" (Hebrew: 28,600).
- **12:5 *(additional)*** — Parallels the closing list of names merged into 12:4 in the site numbering (with minor Greek spelling variants Jeziel and Joazabath).
