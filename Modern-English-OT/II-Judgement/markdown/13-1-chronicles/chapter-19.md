## Text

**19:1** And it came about after these things that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and Hanan his son reigned in his place.

**19:2** And David said, "I will show mercy to Hanan son of Hanas, as his father showed mercy to me." And David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And the servants of David came to the land of the sons of Ammon to comfort him.

**19:3** And the leaders of Ammon said to Hanan, "Is it to honor your father in your sight that he sent comforters to you? Have his servants not come to you so that they might search out the city to spy out the land?"

**19:4** And Hanan took the servants of David and shaved them, and cut off half of their cloaks up to the fold, and sent them away.

**19:5** And they came to report to David about the men, and he sent to meet them, for they were greatly dishonored. And the king said, "Stay in Jericho until your beards grow out, and return."

**19:6** And the sons of Ammon saw that the people of David had been put to shame; and Hanan and the sons of Ammon sent one thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves chariots and horsemen from Syria of Mesopotamia and from Syria of Maacah and from Sobal.

**19:7** And they hired for themselves chariots and horsemen, thirty-two thousand chariots, and the king of Maacah and his people, and they came and encamped opposite Medeba; and the sons of Ammon were gathered from their cities and came to fight.

**19:8** And David heard, and he sent Joab and the entire army of the mighty men.

**19:9** And the sons of Ammon came out and drew up for battle beside the gate of the city; and the kings who had come encamped by themselves in the plain.

**19:10** And Joab saw that they stood facing him to fight against him in front and behind; and he chose from every young man of Israel, and they drew up against the Syrians.

**19:11** And the rest of the people he gave into the hand of Abishai his brother, and they drew up opposite the sons of Ammon.

**19:12** And he said, "If the Syrians are too strong for me, then you will be a deliverance to me; and if the sons of Ammon are too strong for you, then I will save you.

**19:13** And the Lord will do what is good."

**19:14** And Joab and the people who were with him drew up opposite the Syrians for battle, and they fled from him.

**19:15** And the sons of Ammon saw that the Syrians had fled, and they also fled from before Joab and from before his brother, and went into the city. And Joab came to Jerusalem.

**19:16** And the Syrians saw that Israel had routed them, and they sent messengers and brought out the Syrians from beyond the river; and Sophar, commander of the force of Hadarezer, was before them.

**19:17** And it was reported to David, and he gathered all Israel, and crossed the Jordan and came upon them and drew up against them. And the Syrians drew up opposite David and fought against him.

**19:18** And the Syrians fled from before David, and David killed from the Syrians seven thousand chariots and forty thousand foot soldiers, and he killed Saphath, commander of the force.

**19:19** And the servants of Hadarezer saw that they had been defeated before Israel, and they made terms with David and served him; and the Syrians were no longer willing to help Ammon.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — "Hanan" (also in 19:1, 3, 4, 6) and "Hanas" are Septuagint-distinct forms corresponding to Hebrew Hanun and Nahash (whose name is absent in the Greek of 19:1).
- **19:4** — "up to the fold" translates Greek heos tes anaboles (the fold or drape of a garment), rendering a rare Hebrew term meaning "hips" or "buttocks"; in 19:3 the Greek likewise reads "search out the city" where the Hebrew has "search and overthrow."
- **19:6** — The Greek reads "that the people of David had been put to shame" (where the Hebrew has "that they had made themselves odious to David") and names "Sobal" (distinct from Zobah in chapter 18).
- **19:13** — The Greek text here is much shorter than the Hebrew, omitting Joab's exhortation to be strong and courageous for the people and cities of God.
- **19:16** — "Sophar" (and "Saphath" in 19:18) are Septuagint-distinct forms corresponding to Hebrew Shophach.
