## Text

**23:1** And David was an old man and full of days, and he made Solomon his son king in his place over Israel.

**23:2** And he gathered all the chiefs of Israel, and the priests and the Levites.

**23:3** And the Levites counted from thirty years old and upward, and their number, head by head, came to thirty-eight thousand men:

**23:4** of the overseers over the works of the house, twenty-four thousand, and scribes and judges, six thousand,

**23:5** and four thousand praising the Lord with the instruments which he made to praise the Lord.

**23:6** And David divided them into courses for the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, Merari.

**23:7** And for Parosom: for Edan and for Shimei.

**23:8** The sons of Edan: the chief Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel. And three

**23:9** sons of Shimei: Alothim, and Hieiel, and Haidan, three; these were chiefs of the paternal families of Edan.

**23:10** And for the sons of Shimei: Jahath, and Ziza, and Joash, and Beriah; these were the sons of Shimei, four.

**23:11** And Jahath was the chief, and Ziza the second; and Joash and Beriah did not multiply sons, and they became a paternal house, for one enrollment.

**23:12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, Uzziel, four.

**23:13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. And Aaron was set apart to be consecrated as most holy, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before the Lord, to serve and to invoke a blessing in His name forever.

**23:14** And as for Moses, the man of God, his sons were called into the tribe of Levi.

**23:15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**23:16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.

**23:17** And the sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the chief, and there were no other sons; and the sons of Rehabiah increased to a high degree.

**23:18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomoth the chief.

**23:19** Of the sons of Hebron: Idud the chief, Amadiah the second, Uzziel the third, Ikemias the fourth.

**23:20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief, and Isshiah the second.

**23:21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**23:22** And Eleazar died, and he had no sons, only daughters; and the sons of Kish, their brothers, took them.

**23:23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, and Aidath, and Arimoth, three.

**23:24** These were the sons of Levi according to their paternal houses, chiefs of their paternal families according to their enrollment, according to the number of their names, head by head, doing the works of the service of the house of the Lord, from twenty years old and upward.

**23:25** For David said, "The Lord God of Israel has given rest to His people, and dwells in Jerusalem forever."

**23:26** And the Levites were not carrying the tent and all its vessels for its service;

**23:27** for in the last words of David is the number of the sons of Levi from twenty years old and upward,

**23:28** for he stationed them at the hand of Aaron to serve in the house of the Lord, over the courts and over the chambers, and over the cleansing of all the holy things, and over the works of the service of God;

**23:29** for the loaves of the presentation, for the fine flour of the offering, and for the unleavened wafers, and for a griddle, and for that which is mixed, and for every measure;

**23:30** and to stand in the morning to praise, to give thanks to the Lord, and likewise in the evening;

**23:31** and over all the burnt offerings offered up to the Lord on the Sabbaths, and on the new moons, and at the feasts, according to number, according to the ordinance upon them, continually to the Lord.

**23:32** And they shall keep the charges of the tent of the testimony and the charges of the sons of Aaron their brothers, to serve in the house of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:3** — The Greek text reads the active verb 'counted' (rather than passive 'were counted') with the Levites as subject.
- **23:5** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek text omits the four thousand gatekeepers (leaving the itemized figures totaling thirty-four thousand rather than thirty-eight thousand) and uses the third-person verb 'he made' rather than first-person 'I made.'
- **23:7** — The Greek text reads the distinct names Parosom (for Hebrew 'the Gershonites') and Edan (for Hebrew Ladan, reading the initial consonant as a preposition).
- **23:9** — Across this chapter's genealogies the Greek text preserves distinctive names differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text: Alothim, Hieiel, and Haidan (v. 9), Joash (vv. 10-11), Idud, Amadiah, Uzziel, and Ikemias (v. 19), and Aidath and Arimoth (v. 23); in vv. 8-9 'and three' runs across the verse division into the sons of Shimei.
- **23:32** — The Greek text lists only two charges here, omitting 'the charge of the sanctuary' found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; earlier in v. 28 it similarly reads 'at the hand of Aaron' and 'the service of God' without 'sons of' and 'house of.'
