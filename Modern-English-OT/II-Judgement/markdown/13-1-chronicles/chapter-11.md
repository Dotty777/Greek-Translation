## Text

**11:1** And all Israel came to David in Hebron, saying, "Look, we are your bones and your flesh.

**11:2** Both yesterday and the day before, while Saul was king, you were the one leading out and bringing in Israel. And the Lord of Israel said to you, 'You will shepherd My people Israel, and you will be ruler over Israel.'"

**11:3** And all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the Lord; and they anointed David as king over Israel, according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Samuel.

**11:4** And the king and his men went to Jerusalem, that is, Jebus; and there the Jebusites, who inhabited Jebus,

**11:5** said to David, "You will not enter here." And he captured the stronghold of Zion, that is, the City of David.

**11:6** And David said, "Anyone who strikes a Jebusite first will become chief and commander." And Joab son of Zeruiah went up against it first, and he became chief.

**11:7** And David lived in the stronghold; therefore he called it the City of David.

**11:8** And he built the city all around; and he fought and took the city.

**11:9** And David went on advancing, and the Lord Almighty was with him.

**11:10** And these are the chiefs of the mighty men whom David had, who stood strong with him in his kingdom with all Israel to make him king, according to the word of the Lord concerning Israel.

**11:11** And this is the number of the mighty men of David: Jesebada son of Hachmoni, first of the thirty; he drew his sword once against three hundred wounded men at one time.

**11:12** And after him was Eleazar son of Dodai the Archonite; he was among the three mighty men.

**11:13** He was with David in Phasodome, and the foreigners were gathered there for battle; and there was a portion of the field full of barley, and the people fled from before the foreigners.

**11:14** And he stood in the middle of the portion and saved it, and struck down the foreigners; and the Lord accomplished a great salvation.

**11:15** And three of the thirty chiefs went down to the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam, and a camp of the foreigners was in the Valley of the Giants.

**11:16** And David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the foreigners was then in Bethlehem.

**11:17** And David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the cistern of Bethlehem that is at the gate?"

**11:18** And the three broke through the camp of the foreigners and drew water from the cistern in Bethlehem, which was at the gate, and took it to David; and David was not willing to drink it, and he poured it out as a drink offering to the Lord

**11:19** and said, "May God spare me from doing this thing! Shall I drink the blood of these men at the risk of their lives? For at the risk of their lives they brought it." And he would not drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**11:20** And Abishai the brother of Joab was chief of the three; he drew his sword against three hundred wounded men at one time, and he was renowned among the three,

**11:21** of the three, honored above the two, and he was chief over them; yet he did not attain to the three.

**11:22** And Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the son of a mighty man, whose deeds were many beyond Kabzeel: he struck down the two ariels of Moab, and he went down and struck down the lion in the cistern on a day of snow,

**11:23** and he struck down the Egyptian man, an impressive man five cubits tall; and in the hand of the Egyptian was a spear like a weavers' beam. And Benaiah went down against him with a staff and snatched the spear from the hand of the Egyptian, and killed him with his own spear.

**11:24** These things Benaiah son of Joad did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**11:25** He was honored above the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three; and David appointed him over his family.

**11:26** And the mighty men of the armies were: Asahel brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem,

**11:27** Shammoth the Hadite, Helez the Pelonite,

**11:28** Orai son of Ektes of Tekoa, Abiezer the Anathothite,

**11:29** Sibbecai the Hathite, Ilai the Achonite,

**11:30** Neere the Netophathite, Chthaod son of Nooza the Netophathite,

**11:31** Airi son of Ribai from the hill of Benjamin, Benaiah the Pirathonite,

**11:32** Hurai from Nachali Gaash, Abiel the Garabaithite,

**11:33** Azbon the Beermin, Samaba the Homite;

**11:34** Bennaias the Somologennounin, Jonathan son of Sola the Harachite,

**11:35** Achim son of Achar the Hararite, Elphat son of Sthur,

**11:36** Hepher Mochor, Ahijah the Phedonite,

**11:37** Hesere the Charmadaite, Naarai son of Ezbai,

**11:38** Joel son of Nathan, Mebaal son of Hagri,

**11:39** Sele the Ammonite, Nahor the Berothite, the armor-bearer for the son of Zeruiah,

**11:40** Ira the Ithrite, Gareobai, Jotheri;

**11:41** Uri the Hittite, Zabet son of Achaia,

**11:42** Adina son of Shiza, a chief of Reuben, and thirty with him,

**11:43** Hanan son of Maacah, and Jehoshaphat the Baithanite,

**11:44** Uzzia the Ashterathite, Samatha and Jeia, sons of Kothan the Ararite,

**11:45** Elthiel son of Shimri, and Joazae his brother the Jeasite,

**11:46** Liel the Miei, and Jeribai, and Josiah his son, Ellaam, and Ethema the Moabite,

**11:47** Daliel, and Jobeth, and Essiel the Minabia.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The Greek text reads "eipen Israel Kyrios soi" (lit. "Israel's Lord / the Lord of Israel said to you"), a distinctive Septuagint reading where other witnesses have "the Lord your God said to you."
- **11:8** — The Septuagint has the shorter, distinctive clause "and he fought and took the city" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text mentions the Millo and Joab restoring the rest of the city.
- **11:13** — Greek "allophyloi" (lit. "foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard term for the Philistines in Samuel and Chronicles, and "Phasodome" preserves the distinctive Greek form of the place name (cf. MT Pas-dammim).
- **11:22** — Greek translates the preposition comparatively as "beyond Kabzeel" (rather than "from Kabzeel") and leaves Hebrew "ariel" untranslated ("the two ariels of Moab"), a rare term often understood as "lion-like heroes" or "champions."
- **11:25** — The Greek text reads "over his family" ("patrian") where the Hebrew has "over his bodyguard," and in 11:24 uses the shortened patronymic "Joad" for Jehoiada.
- **11:34** — Throughout the warrior list (11:11-47), the Septuagint preserves numerous Greek-distinct personal, place, and gentilic names (such as Jesebada, the Archonite, Nachali Gaash, and Bennaias the Somologennounin where the Hebrew has "the sons of Hashem the Gizonite"), along with readings such as "son of Nathan" (11:38), "the son of Zeruiah" without "Joab" (11:39), and "Josiah his son, Ellaam" (11:46).
