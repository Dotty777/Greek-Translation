## Text

**7:1** And for the sons of Issachar: Tholaek, Phut, Jassur, and Shimron, four.

**7:2** And the sons of Thole: Uzzi, Raphara, Riel, Eikan, Basan, and Isamuel, chiefs of the houses of their families belonging to Tholai, mighty in strength according to their generations. Their number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

**7:3** And the sons of Zirri: Zaria and Michael; and the sons of Zaria: Mibdia, Rael, and Isshiah, five, all of them chiefs.

**7:4** And with them, according to their generations, according to their ancestral houses, those mighty to draw up in battle array for war were thirty-six thousand, because they multiplied wives and sons.

**7:5** And their brothers among all the families of Issachar, also mighty in strength, were eighty-seven thousand, the number of all of them.

**7:6** Benjamin, Abira, and Adiel, three.

**7:7** And the sons of Badee: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Arimoth, and Uri, five, chiefs of ancestral houses, mighty in strength; and their number was twenty-two thousand thirty-four.

**7:8** And the sons of Abachi: Amarias, Joash, Eliezer, Elithainan, Amaria, Auremoth, Abihud, Anathoth, and Gemeeth. All these were sons of Amachir,

**7:9** and their number according to their generations, chiefs of the houses of their families, mighty in strength, was twenty thousand two hundred.

**7:10** And the sons of Ariel: Balaan. And the sons of Balaan: Jeush, Benjamin, Aoth, Canaan, Zethan, Ramessai, and Achisadar.

**7:11** All these were sons of Ariel, chiefs of the families, mighty in strength, seventeen thousand two hundred, going out in force to wage war;

**7:12** and Sapphin and Happhin, and the sons of Raom, his son Aher.

**7:13** The sons of Naphtali: Jisiel, Guni, Issier, and Solomon, sons of Balam.

**7:14** The sons of Manasseh: Asriel, whom his Syrian concubine bore; she bore Machir, the father of Gilead.

**7:15** And Machir took a wife for Hamphin and Mamphin; and the name of his sister was Maacah, and the name of the second was Sapphaad.

**7:16** And Maacah the wife of Machir bore a son, and she called his name Suros; his son was Ulam,

**7:17** Badam. These were sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh.

**7:18** And his sister Malecheth bore Isadek, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

**7:19** And the sons of Semira were Jaaim, Shechem, Lakeim, and Alialim.

**7:20** And the sons of Ephraim: Sothalath. The sons of Laada: his son Noome,

**7:21** his son Zabad, his sons Ozar and Elead; and the men of Gath who were born in the land killed him, because they went down to take their livestock.

**7:22** And Ephraim their father mourned many days, and their brothers came to comfort him.

**7:23** And he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Bargaa, because, "In misfortunes he came to be in my house

**7:24** and among those who remained." And he built Beth-horon, the lower and the upper. And the sons of Ozan: Seera

**7:25** and Raphe, his sons; Saraph and Thalees, his sons; Tahan, his son.

**7:26** To Ladan his son: sons of Amioueid, sons of Eleimasai,

**7:27** sons of Num, sons of Joshua, his sons.

**7:28** And their possession and their dwelling place were Bethel and its villages, to the east Naarnan, to the west Gezer and its villages, and Shechem and its villages as far as Gaian and its villages;

**7:29** and as far as the borders of the sons of Manasseh: Beth-shean and its villages, Thalme and its villages, and Balad and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, Dor and its villages; in this the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, lived.

**7:30** The sons of Asher: Inina, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriga, and Sore, and their sister.

**7:31** The sons of Bericha: Gaber and Mellie; he was the father of Bezaith.

**7:32** And Heber fathered Iphamel, Samer, Hothan, and Sola, their sister.

**7:33** And the sons of Aphalech: Baisechi, Imabael, and Asith; these were sons of Japhalel.

**7:34** And the sons of Shemer: Achiuia, Hobab, Akaran,

**7:35** and Balaam; his brothers were Sochath, Imna, Zeme, and Amaa.

**7:36** The sons of Sophas: Chuchi, Hanarphar, Sula, Sabri, Imare,

**7:37** Basan, Oa, Shamma, Salisa, Jethra, and Beera.

**7:38** And the sons of Jether: Iphina, Phasphai, and Ara.

**7:39** And the sons of Ola: Orech, Hanniel, and Rasia.

**7:40** All these were sons of Asher, all chiefs of families, chosen, mighty in strength, leading chiefs; their number for battle array to wage war, their number was twenty-six thousand men.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Greek text lists Michael under Zirri rather than Zaria and preserves distinctive forms for several genealogical names in vv. 1-3 (including Tholaek, Thole, and Tholai for Tola, as well as Phut, Jassur, Raphara, Riel, Eikan, Basan, Isamuel, Zirri, Zaria, Mibdia, and Rael) where it diverges from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **7:6** — The Greek reads "Benjamin, Abira, and Adiel, three," omitting Bela here and preserving LXX-distinct names across the Benjaminite, Naphtalite, and Manassite lists in vv. 6-19 (such as Badee, Abachi/Amachir, Ariel, Ramessai, Sapphin, Happhin, Raom, Solomon, Balam, Hamphin, Mamphin, Sapphaad, Suros, Badam, and Semira).
- **7:21** — The Greek has singular "him" (αὐτόν) rather than plural "them," preserves a shorter Ephraimite lineage in vv. 20-21 (Sothalath, Laada, Noome, Ozar), and reads plural "their brothers" (ἀδελφοὶ αὐτῶν) in v. 22.
- **7:24** — The Greek attaches "and among those who remained" (καὶ ἐν ἐκείνοις τοῖς καταλοίποις) to Ephraim's naming of Bargaa from v. 23 instead of naming a daughter Sheerah, making "he" the subject of "built" and rendering the Hebrew place name Uzzen-sheerah as a personal lineage ("the sons of Ozan: Seera and Raphe").
- **7:26** — Following Codex Vaticanus/Swete, the Greek critical text reads plural 'sons of Amioueid, sons of Eleimasai' (υἱοὶ Ἀμιουείδ, υἱοὶ Ἐλειμασαί), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read singular 'Ammihud his son, Elishama his son.'
- **7:29** — The Greek includes an extra town (Balad) alongside LXX-distinct place names (Naarnan, Gaian, Thalme) in vv. 28-29 and ends with singular "in this" (ἐν ταύτῃ) rather than plural "in these"; vv. 25-27 and 30-39 likewise preserve numerous LXX-distinct personal names (including Gedson, Jeeth, Zemma, Num, Beriga/Bericha, Gaber, Iphamel/Aphalech/Japhalel, Hobab, Balaam, and Sophas).
