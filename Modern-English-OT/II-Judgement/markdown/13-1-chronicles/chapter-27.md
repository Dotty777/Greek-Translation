## Text

**27:1** Now the sons of Israel according to their number, leaders of the fathers' houses, commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and scribes who served the people and in every matter of the king according to divisions, every matter of the one coming in and going out month by month, for all the months of the year: one division was twenty-four thousand.

**27:2** And over the first division of the first month was Sobal the son of Zabdiel, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:3** He was from the sons of Perez, leader of all the leaders of the army of the first month.

**27:4** And over the division of the second month was Dodai the Ekchoch, and in his division were twenty-four thousand, leaders of the army.

**27:5** The third, for the third month, was Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the priest, the leader; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:6** This Benaiah was mightier than the thirty and over the thirty; and over his division was Laibazath his son.

**27:7** The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Obadiah his son, and the brothers; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:8** The fifth, for the fifth month, was the ruler Salaoth the Ezrahite; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:9** The sixth, for the sixth month, was Hodaviah the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:10** The seventh, for the seventh month, was Chesles from Phallous, of the sons of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:11** The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of Zerah; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:12** The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer from Anathoth, from the land of Benjamin; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:13** The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai from Netophah, of Zerah; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:14** The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah from Pirathon, of the sons of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:15** The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai from Netophah, of Othniel; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:16** And over the tribes of Israel: for the Reubenites, the ruler was Eliezer the son of Zichri; for Simeon, Shephatiah the son of Maacah;

**27:17** for Levi, Hashabiah the son of Samuel; for Aaron, Zadok;

**27:18** for Judah, Eliab, of the brothers of David; for Issachar, Omri the son of Mishael;

**27:19** for Zebulun, Shemaiah the son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth the son of Azriel;

**27:20** for Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Uzziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel the son of Phaladaia;

**27:21** for the half-tribe of Manasseh, those in Gilead, Jaddai the son of Zabdiah; for the sons of Benjamin, Asier the son of Abner;

**27:22** for Dan, Azarel the son of Joram. These were the patriarchs of the tribes of Israel.

**27:23** And David did not take their number from twenty years old and under, because the Lord had said He would multiply Israel like the stars of heaven.

**27:24** And Joab the son of Zeruiah began to number among the people, and did not finish; and because of these things wrath came upon Israel, and the number was not recorded in the book of the chronicles of King David.

**27:25** And over the storehouses of the king was Azmaveth the son of Adiel, and over the storehouses in the field, and in the villages, and in the hamlets, and in the towers was Jonathan the son of Uzziah.

**27:26** And over those farming the land, the workers, was Ezri the son of Chobud.

**27:27** And over the estates was Shimei from Rael, and over the storehouses of wine in the estates was Zichri the son of Sephni.

**27:28** And over the olive groves and over the sycamore trees in the lowland was Baal-hanan the Gedorite; and over the storehouses of oil was Joash.

**27:29** And over the grazing cattle in Asidon was Asartais the Sharonite, and over the cattle in the valleys was Sophan the son of Adai.

**27:30** And over the camels was Abijah the Ishmaelite. And over the donkeys was Jehdeiah from Merathon.

**27:31** And over the sheep was Jaziz the Garite. All these were overseers of the property of King David.

**27:32** And Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding; and Jehiel the son of Hachami was with the king's sons.

**27:33** Ahithophel was counselor to the king, and Hushai was the first friend of the king.

**27:34** And after this Ahithophel, coming next were Jehoiada the son of Benaiah and Abiathar; and Joab was the king's commander-in-chief.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — Greek reads "who served the people," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "who served the king."
- **27:2** — Throughout vv. 2-15 the Greek preserves several distinct names compared with the Hebrew: Sobal (v. 2; Heb. Jashobeam), Dodai the Ekchoch (v. 4; Heb. the Ahohite), Laibazath (v. 6; Heb. Ammizabad), Salaoth the Ezrahite (v. 8; Heb. Shamhuth the Izrahite), Hodaviah (v. 9, Greek Odouias; Heb. Ira), and Chesles from Phallous (v. 10; Heb. Helez the Pelonite). Also in v. 4 the Greek ends with "leaders of the army" and lacks the Hebrew reference to Mikloth.
- **27:7** — Greek reads "Obadiah" (Abdeias) and "and the brothers," whereas the Hebrew reads "Zebadiah" and "after him."
- **27:17** — In the tribal list (vv. 17-22) the Greek differs from the Hebrew on several names: Samuel (v. 17; Heb. Kemuel), Eliab and Mishael (v. 18; Heb. Elihu and Michael), Shemaiah (v. 19; Heb. Ishmaiah), Uzziah and Phaladaia (v. 20; Heb. Azaziah and Pedaiah), Jaddai, Zabdiah, and Asier (v. 21; Heb. Iddo, Zechariah, and Jaasiel), and Joram (v. 22; Heb. Jeroham).
- **27:27** — Greek reads "estates" (choria) rather than Hebrew "vineyards," and in vv. 26-32 preserves several distinct names: Chobud (v. 26; Heb. Chelub), Rael, Zichri, and the son of Sephni (v. 27; Heb. the Ramathite, Zabdi, and the Shiphmite), Asidon, Asartais, Sophan, and Adai (v. 29; Heb. Sharon, Shitrai, Shaphat, and Adlai), Abijah and Merathon (v. 30; Heb. Obil and the Meronothite), the Garite (v. 31; Heb. the Hagrite), and Hachami (v. 32; Heb. Hachmoni).
- **27:33** — Greek calls Hushai "the first friend of the king," reflecting a Hellenistic court title rather than the Hebrew gentilic "the Archite." In v. 34 the compressed Greek wording "after this Ahithophel, coming next" identifies those who succeeded Ahithophel as royal counselors.
