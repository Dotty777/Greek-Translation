## Text

**15:1** And he made houses for himself in the city of David, and he prepared the place for the ark of God and made a tent for it.

**15:2** Then David said, "No one may carry the ark of God except the Levites, for the Lord chose them also to minister to Him forever."

**15:3** And David assembled all Israel to Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the Lord to the place that he had prepared for it.

**15:4** And David gathered the sons of Aaron, the Levites.

**15:5** Of the sons of Kohath: Uriel the chief and his brothers, one hundred ten;

**15:6** of the sons of Merari: Asaiah the chief and his brothers, two hundred fifty;

**15:7** of the sons of Gershom: Joel the chief and his brothers, one hundred fifty;

**15:8** of the sons of Elisaphat: Shemaiah the chief and his brothers, two hundred;

**15:9** of the sons of Hebron: Ener the chief and their brothers, eighty;

**15:10** of the sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the chief and his brothers, one hundred twelve.

**15:11** And David called Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites: Ariel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Enel, and Amminadab.

**15:12** And he said to them, "You are the chiefs of the fathers' houses of the Levites; purify yourselves, you and your brothers, and you will bring up the ark of the God of Israel to where I prepared for it.

**15:13** Because you were not there at the first, our God broke out among us, because we did not seek according to the ordinance."

**15:14** And the priests and the Levites purified themselves to bring up the ark of the God of Israel.

**15:15** And the sons of the Levites took up the ark of God, as Moses had commanded by the word of God according to the writing, with poles upon themselves.

**15:16** And David said to the chiefs of the Levites, "Appoint their brothers, the singers, with instruments, harps, lyres, and cymbals, to raise the sound on high with a voice of gladness."

**15:17** And the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; from his brothers, Asaph son of Berechiah; and from the sons of Merari, his brothers, Ethan son of Kushaiah.

**15:18** And with them were their brothers of the second rank: Zechariah, Uzziel, Shemiramoth, Jeiel, Elioel, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphena, Makelleiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Uzziah, the gatekeepers.

**15:19** And the singers: Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, with bronze cymbals to make the sound heard;

**15:20** Zechariah, Uzziel, Shemiramoth, Eithel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with harps according to alamoth;

**15:21** and Mattithiah, Enphanaias, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Uzziah, with lyres of amasenith to be strong.

**15:22** And Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was chief of the songs, because he was discerning.

**15:23** And Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers of the ark.

**15:24** And Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, were blowing the trumpets before the ark of God. And Obed-edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers of the ark of God.

**15:25** And David and the elders of Israel and the commanders of thousands were going to bring up the ark of the covenant from the house of Obed-edom with gladness.

**15:26** And it came about, when God strengthened the Levites who were carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord, that they sacrificed seven calves each and seven rams each.

**15:27** And David was wrapped in a robe of fine linen, and all the Levites carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and the singers, Jeconiah the chief of the songs of those singing; and upon David was a robe of fine linen.

**15:28** And all Israel were bringing up the ark of the covenant of the Lord with shouting, and with the sound of the shofar, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, sounding aloud on harps and with lyres.

**15:29** And it came about that the ark of the covenant of the Lord came as far as the city of David; and Michal daughter of Saul looked out through the window, and saw King David dancing and celebrating, and she despised him in her soul.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Greek text lacks the phrase "to carry the ark of the Lord" found in the Hebrew, reading literally "for the Lord chose them also to minister to Him forever."
- **15:5** — In verses 5-7, the Greek gives distinct totals for three Levitical clans (one hundred ten, two hundred fifty, and one hundred fifty) compared with the Hebrew (one hundred twenty, two hundred twenty, and one hundred thirty), and in verses 8-11 preserves distinct name forms (Elisaphat, Ener, Ariel, and Enel).
- **15:18** — In verses 18-21, the Greek preserves several distinct name forms compared with the Hebrew (Uzziel, Elioel, Eliphena, Makelleiah, Uzziah, Eithel, and Enphanaias); in verse 21, "amasenith" transliterates the musical term corresponding to Hebrew "hasheminith," and "to be strong" reflects a distinct Greek rendering of the Hebrew musical direction.
- **15:26** — The Greek uses the distributive expression "seven ... each" for the sacrifices, and renders Hebrew "bulls" with "calves."
- **15:27** — The Greek reads "Jeconiah" rather than "Chenaniah" (as in verse 22 and the Hebrew) and repeats "a robe of fine linen" at the end of the verse rather than mentioning a linen ephod.
