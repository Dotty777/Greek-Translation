## Text

**23:1** And it was reported to David, saying, "Look, the foreigners are fighting against Keilah, and they are plundering; they are trampling the threshing floors."

**23:2** And David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Should I go and strike these foreigners?" And the Lord said, "Go, and you will strike among these foreigners, and you will strike Keilah."

**23:3** And the men of David said to him, "Look, we are afraid here in Judea; and how will it be if we go to Keilah? Will we go into the spoils of the foreigners?"

**23:4** And David proceeded to inquire again through the Lord; and the Lord answered him and said to him, "Arise and go down to Keilah, for I am delivering the foreigners into your hands."

**23:5** And David and the men who were with him went to Keilah, and he fought against the foreigners; and they fled from his presence, and he led away their livestock, and he struck a great blow among them; and David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.

**23:6** And it happened, when Abiathar son of Abimelech fled to David, that he went down with David to Keilah, having an ephod in his hand.

**23:7** And it was reported to Saul that David had come to Keilah, and Saul said, "God has sold him into my hands, for he has been shut in by entering a city of doors and bars."

**23:8** And Saul commanded all the people for war, to go down to Keilah to hem in David and his men.

**23:9** And David knew that Saul was not keeping silent about the evil concerning him, and David said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring near the ephod of the Lord."

**23:10** And David said, "Lord, God of Israel, Your servant has surely heard that Saul seeks to come against Keilah to destroy the city because of me.

**23:11** Will it be shut up? And now, will Saul come down, just as Your servant has heard? Lord, God of Israel, tell Your servant." And the Lord said, "It will be shut up."

**23:13** And David and the men who were with him, about four hundred, rose up and went out from Keilah, and they went wherever they went; and it was reported to Saul that David had escaped from Keilah, and he refrained from going out.

**23:14** And he stayed in the wilderness in Maserem, in the narrow passes, and he remained in the wilderness on the mountain of Ziph, in the parched land; and Saul was seeking him all the days, but the Lord did not deliver him into his hands.

**23:15** And David saw that Saul was coming out to seek David; and David was on the parched mountain, in Kaine of Ziph.

**23:16** And Jonathan son of Saul rose up and went to David at Kaine, and he strengthened his hands in the Lord.

**23:17** And he said to him, "Do not be afraid, for the hand of Saul my father will by no means find you; and you will reign over Israel, and I will be second to you; and Saul my father knows that it is so."

**23:18** And both of them made a covenant before the Lord; and David stayed in Kaine, and Jonathan departed to his house.

**23:19** And the Ziphites went up from the parched land to Saul on the hill, saying, "Look, is David not hidden with us in Nessara, in the narrow passes, in Kaine, on the hill of Hachilah, which is on the right of Jeshimon?

**23:20** And now, according to all that is in the king's soul for coming down, let him come down to us; they have shut him up into the hands of the king."

**23:21** And Saul said to them, "Blessed are you by the Lord, because you have felt sorrow for me.

**23:22** Go now and prepare further, and find out his place where his foot will be quickly, there where you said, so that he may not act craftily.

**23:23** And see and know, and we will go with you; and it will be, if he is in the land, that I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah."

**23:24** And the Ziphites rose up and went ahead of Saul; and David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, westward, on the right of Jeshimon.

**23:25** And Saul and his men went to seek him; and they reported to David, and he went down to the rock that is in the wilderness of Maon. And Saul heard, and he pursued after David into the wilderness of Maon.

**23:26** And Saul and his men went on this side of the mountain, and David and his men were on that side of the mountain; and David was taking cover to go away from the presence of Saul, and Saul and his men encamped against David and his men to seize them.

**23:27** And a messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and come, for the foreigners have attacked the land."

**23:28** And Saul turned back from pursuing after David, and he went to meet the foreigners; therefore that place was named the Divided Rock.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — Greek reads "you will strike Keilah," repeating the verb "strike," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you will save Keilah."
- **23:11** — Verse 12 is absent in the Greek text; the inquiry about surrender is condensed here into the singular passive "Will it be shut up?" and answered "It will be shut up."
- **23:13** — Greek reads "about four hundred" men (consistent with 22:2), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "about six hundred."
- **23:15** — Greek Kaine (literally "New") is used as a place name here and in vv. 16, 18, and 19, corresponding to "Horesh" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:19** — "Nessara" (like "Maserem" in v. 14) is a Greek transliteration corresponding to Hebrew "strongholds," placed here alongside the translation "in the narrow passes."
