## Text

**30:1** And it happened, when David and his men entered Keilah on the third day, that Amalek had made a raid against the south and against Ziklag, and had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire;

**30:2** and as for the women and all that was in it, from small to great, they did not put to death man or woman, but took them captive and went off on their way.

**30:3** And David and his men came to the city, and look, it was burned with fire, and their wives and their sons and their daughters had been taken captive.

**30:4** And David and his men lifted up their voice and wept until there was no longer strength in them to weep.

**30:5** And both of the wives of David had been taken captive: Ahinoam the Israelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**30:6** And David was greatly distressed, because the people spoke of stoning him, for the soul of all the people was deeply grieved, each one for his sons and for his daughters; but David was strengthened in the Lord his God.

**30:7** And David said to Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, "Bring the ephod near."

**30:8** And David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Should I pursue after this raiding band? Will I overtake them?" And He said to him, "Pursue, for you will surely overtake and you will surely rescue."

**30:9** And David went, he and the four hundred men with him, and they came as far as the brook Besor; and the rest stopped.

**30:10** And the rest pursued with four hundred men, while two hundred men stayed behind, who sat across the brook Besor.

**30:11** And they found an Egyptian man in a field, and they took him and brought him to David in the field; and they gave him bread and he ate, and they gave him water to drink;

**30:12** and they gave him a piece of a fig cake and he ate, and his spirit was restored in him, because he had not eaten bread and had not drunk water for three days and three nights.

**30:13** And David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" And the Egyptian young man said, "I am a slave of an Amalekite man, and my master left me behind because I fell ill three days ago today.

**30:14** And we made a raid against the south of Cholthei and against the regions of Judea and against Gilboa, and we burned Ziklag with fire."

**30:15** And David said to him, "Will you lead me down to this raiding band?" And he said, "Swear now to me by God not to put me to death and not to deliver me into the hands of my master, and I will lead you down to this raiding band."

**30:16** And he led him down there, and look, they were spread out over the face of all the land, eating and drinking and celebrating over all the great spoils that they had taken from the land of the foreigners and from the land of Judah.

**30:17** And David came upon them and struck them from dawn until evening and on the next day, and not a man of them escaped except four hundred young men who had mounted the camels and fled.

**30:18** And David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and he rescued both of his wives.

**30:19** And nothing was missing to them, from small to great, from the spoils even to sons and daughters and to all that they had taken of theirs; and David brought everything back.

**30:20** And he took all the flocks and the herds and drove them ahead of the spoils; and concerning those spoils it was said, "These are the spoils of David."

**30:21** And David came to the two hundred men who had grown too faint to go after David, and he had settled them at the brook Beana; and they came out to meet David and to meet the people who were with him, and David drew near to the people, and they asked him about his welfare.

**30:22** And every malicious and wicked man of the fighting men who had gone with David answered and said, "Because they did not pursue with us, we will not give them any of the spoils that we rescued, except to each man his wife and his children; let them lead them away and turn back."

**30:23** And David said, "You shall not do so, after the Lord has delivered to us and guarded us, and the Lord has delivered the raiding band that came against us into our hands.

**30:24** And who will listen to these words of yours? For they are no less than you; for as is the share of the one going down into battle, so shall be the share of the one sitting by the baggage; they shall share alike."

**30:25** And it happened from that day onward that it became a statute and an ordinance for Israel until today.

**30:26** And David came to Ziklag, and sent some of the spoils to the elders of Judah and to his neighbors, saying, "Look, some of the spoils of the enemies of the Lord,"

**30:27** to those in Beth-zur, and to those in Ramah of the south, and to those in Geththor,

**30:28** and to those in Aroer, and to those of Ammadei, and to those in Saphei, and to those in Estheie,

**30:29** and to those in Gath, and to those in Keimath, and to those in Saphek, and to those in Theimath, and to those in Carmel, and to those in the cities of Israel, and to those in the cities of Kenezei,

**30:30** and to those in Jerimuth, and to those in Beersheba, and to those in Noo,

**30:31** and to those in Hebron, and to all the places through which David had passed, he and his men.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — The Greek reads "Keilah" for the town David entered before naming "Ziklag" twice later in the verse, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "Ziklag" throughout.
- **30:8** — The Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for a raiding band as geddour (also in 30:15, 23).
- **30:9** — The Greek numbers David's company at "four hundred men" rather than the Masoretic Text's "six hundred," and in 30:10 reads "the rest pursued with four hundred men" and "who sat across the brook Besor" (MT: "too exhausted to cross").
- **30:14** — The Greek reads "Cholthei" (MT: "Cherethites"), "Judea" (MT: "Judah"), and "Gilboa" (MT: "the Negev of Caleb").
- **30:27** — Across 30:27-30 the Septuagint preserves a longer, partly distinct list of towns (including Beth-zur, Geththor, Ammadei, Saphei, Estheie, Gath, Keimath, Saphek, Theimath, Carmel, the cities of Israel and Kenezei, Jerimuth, Beersheba, and Noo), and in 30:21 names the brook "Beana" rather than "Besor."
