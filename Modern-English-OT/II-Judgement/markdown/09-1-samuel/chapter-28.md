## Text

**28:1** And it happened in those days that foreigners gathered together in their camps to go out to fight against Israel; and Achish said to David, "You will surely know that you will go out with me to battle, you and your men."

**28:2** And David said to Achish, "So now you will know what your servant will do." And Achish said to David, "Thus I will make you chief bodyguard all the days."

**28:3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Armathaim, in his city. And Saul had removed the mediums and the wizards from the land.

**28:4** And the foreigners gathered together and came and encamped at Shunem; and Saul gathered together every man of Israel, and they encamped at Gilboa.

**28:5** And Saul saw the camp of the foreigners, and he was afraid, and his heart was greatly terrified.

**28:6** And Saul inquired of the Lord, and the Lord did not answer him, either by dreams, or by the manifestations, or by the prophets.

**28:7** And Saul said to his servants, "Seek for me a woman who is a medium, and I will go to her and inquire through her." And his servants said to him, "Look, there is a woman who is a medium in Endor."

**28:8** And Saul covered himself up and put on other garments, and he went, he and two men with him, and they came to the woman by night, and he said to her, "Please divine for me by the familiar spirit, and bring up for me the one whom I tell you."

**28:9** And the woman said to him, "Look now, you know all that Saul has done, how he has utterly destroyed the mediums and the wizards from the land. And why are you ensnaring my life to put it to death?"

**28:10** And Saul swore to her, saying, "As the Lord lives, no guilt will come upon you in this matter."

**28:11** And the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" And he said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

**28:12** And the woman saw Samuel, and she cried out with a loud voice; and the woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? And you are Saul!"

**28:13** And the king said to her, "Do not be afraid; say whom you have seen." And she said to him, "I have seen gods coming up out of the earth."

**28:14** And he said to her, "What did you recognize?" And she said to him, "An upright man coming up out of the earth, and he is wrapped in a double cloak." And Saul knew that this was Samuel, and he bowed down upon his face to the ground and paid homage to him.

**28:15** And Samuel said, "Why have you disturbed me, that I should come up?" And Saul said, "I am greatly distressed, and the foreigners are fighting against me, and God has departed from me and has not listened to me any longer, either by the hand of the prophets or by dreams; and now I have called you to make known to me what I should do."

**28:16** And Samuel said, "Why do you inquire of me, when the Lord has departed from you and has come to be with your neighbor?

**28:17** And the Lord has done to you just as the Lord spoke by my hand, and the Lord will tear your kingdom out of your hand and will give it to your neighbor, to David.

**28:18** Because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord and did not carry out the fury of His wrath upon Amalek, therefore the Lord has done this thing to you on this day.

**28:19** And the Lord will deliver Israel with you into the hands of foreigners, and tomorrow you and your sons with you will fall, and the Lord will give the camp of Israel into the hands of foreigners."

**28:20** And Saul hastened and fell as he stood to the ground, and he was greatly afraid because of the words of Samuel; and there was no longer any strength in him, for he had not eaten bread all the day and all that night.

**28:21** And the woman came in to Saul and saw that he was greatly agitated; and she said to him, "Look now, your maidservant has listened to your voice, and I have put my life in my hand, and I have listened to the words that you spoke to me.

**28:22** And now, please listen to the voice of your maidservant, and I will set before you a morsel of bread, and eat; and there will be strength in you, because you will go on a journey."

**28:23** And he was not willing to eat; and his servants and the woman strongly urged him, and he listened to their voice, and he arose from the ground and sat on the seat.

**28:24** And the woman had a pasture-fed heifer in the house; and she hurried and slaughtered it, and she took flour and kneaded it and baked unleavened cakes,

**28:25** and she brought it before Saul and before his servants; and they ate, and arose and went away that night.

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — The Greek uses the form 'Armathaim' (reflecting dual Ramathaim; compare 1 Samuel 1:1) where the Hebrew has 'Ramah.' 'Mediums' translates a Greek term literally meaning 'belly-speakers,' used for both necromancers and the familiar spirits speaking through them (compare 28:8).
- **28:6** — 'The manifestations' translates the Greek rendering of the Hebrew 'Urim' (compare Exodus 28:30).
- **28:14** — The Greek reads 'What did you recognize?' and 'an upright man' where the Hebrew text has 'What is his appearance?' and 'an old man.'
- **28:16** — The Greek reads 'has come to be with your neighbor' where the Hebrew text has 'has become your adversary.'
- **28:19** — The Greek reads 'tomorrow you and your sons with you will fall' where the Hebrew text has 'tomorrow you and your sons will be with me.'
