## Text

**15:1** And Samuel said to Saul, "The Lord sent me to anoint you as king over Israel, and now listen to the voice of the Lord.

**15:2** This is what the Lord Sabaoth said: 'Now I will avenge what Amalek did to Israel, how he met him on the way as he was coming up out of Egypt.

**15:3** And now go, and you shall strike Amalek and Jerim and all that belongs to him, and you shall not preserve anything from him, and you shall utterly destroy him; and you shall devote him and all that belongs to him to destruction, and you shall not spare him; and you shall kill from man even to woman, and from young child to nursing infant, and from calf to sheep, and from camel to donkey.'"

**15:4** And Saul summoned the people and mustered them in Gilgal: four hundred thousand troops, and Judah thirty thousand troops.

**15:5** And Saul came as far as the cities of Amalek and set an ambush in the wadi.

**15:6** And Saul said to the Kenite, "Go away and turn aside from the midst of the Amalekite, so that I do not add you along with him, for you showed mercy to the sons of Israel when they were coming up out of Egypt." And the Kenite turned aside from the midst of Amalek.

**15:7** And Saul struck Amalek from Havilah as far as Asshur, facing Egypt.

**15:8** And he captured Agag king of Amalek alive, and all the people of Jerim he killed with the edge of the sword.

**15:9** And Saul and all the people preserved Agag alive, and what was good of the flocks and of the herds and of the provisions and of the vineyards and of all the good things, and he was not willing to utterly destroy them; and every work that was dishonored and despised they utterly destroyed.

**15:10** And the word of the Lord came to Samuel, saying,

**15:11** "I regret that I made Saul reign as king, because he turned back from following Me and did not keep My words." And Samuel was disheartened, and he cried out to the Lord all night.

**15:12** And Samuel rose early and went to meet Israel in the morning; and it was reported to Saul, saying, "Samuel has come to Carmel and has set up a monument for himself." And he turned the chariot around and went down to Gilgal to Saul, and look, he was offering up a whole burnt offering to the Lord, the first of the spoils that he had brought from Amalek.

**15:13** And Samuel came to Saul, and Saul said to him, "Blessed are you by the Lord; I have carried out all that the Lord spoke."

**15:14** And Samuel said, "And what is the sound of this flock in my ears, and the sound of the cattle that I hear?"

**15:15** And Saul said, "I brought them from Amalek, those which the people preserved, the finest of the flock and of the cattle, so that they might be sacrificed to the Lord your God; and the rest I utterly destroyed."

**15:16** And Samuel said to Saul, "Stop, and I will report to you what the Lord spoke to me in the night." And he said to him, "Speak."

**15:17** And Samuel said to Saul, "Are you not small before Him, leader of a tribe of the family of Israel? And the Lord anointed you as king over Israel.

**15:18** And the Lord sent you on a way and said to you, 'Go and utterly destroy; you shall put to death those who sin against Me, Amalek, and you shall make war against them until you make an end of them.'

**15:19** And why did you not listen to the voice of the Lord, but rushed to fall upon the spoils and did what is evil before the Lord?"

**15:20** And Saul said to Samuel, "Because I listened to the voice of the people; and I went on the way on which the Lord sent me, and I brought Agag king of Amalek, and I utterly destroyed Amalek.

**15:21** And the people took from the spoils flocks and herds, the first of what was to be utterly destroyed, before the Lord our God in Gilgal."

**15:22** And Samuel said, "Are whole burnt offerings and sacrifices as desired by the Lord as listening to the voice of the Lord? Look, listening is better than a good sacrifice, and paying heed is better than the fat of rams.

**15:23** For divination is a sin; pain and toil bring on healing. Because you rejected the word of the Lord, the Lord will also reject you from being king over Israel."

**15:24** And Saul said to Samuel, "I have sinned, because I transgressed the word of the Lord and your word, because I feared the people and listened to their voice.

**15:25** And now, please take away my sin and return with me, and I will worship the Lord your God."

**15:26** And Samuel said to Saul, "I am not returning with you, because you rejected the word of the Lord, and the Lord will reject you from being king over Israel."

**15:27** And Samuel turned his face to go away, and Saul seized the hem of his cloak and tore it.

**15:28** And Samuel said to him, "The Lord has torn your kingdom from Israel out of your hand today, and He will give it to your neighbor who is better than you;

**15:29** and Israel will be divided in two, and He will not turn back nor change His mind, for He Himself is not like a man to change His mind."

**15:30** And Saul said, "I have sinned, yet please honor me before the elders of Israel and before my people, and return with me, and I will worship the Lord your God."

**15:31** And Samuel returned after Saul, and he worshiped the Lord.

**15:32** And Samuel said, "Bring Agag king of Amalek to me." And Agag came to him trembling. And Agag said, "Is death so bitter?"

**15:33** And Samuel said to Agag, "Just as your sword has made women childless, so shall your mother be made childless among women." And Samuel slaughtered Agag before the Lord in Gilgal.

**15:34** And Samuel went away to Armathaim; and Saul went up to his house in Gibeah.

**15:35** And Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death, for Samuel was mourning over Saul; and the Lord regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Greek "Sabaoth" transliterates the Hebrew word for "hosts" or "armies."
- **15:3** — Greek "Jerim" (also in 15:8) treats the Hebrew root for "devote to destruction" (herem) as a proper name, alongside a doublet of the command to destroy Amalek.
- **15:7** — Greek reads "Asshur," where the Hebrew text reads "Shur."
- **15:12** — Literally "a hand," a Hebraism for a monument. The Greek also diverges from the Hebrew by making Samuel the subject who came to Carmel and turned the chariot back to Gilgal, and by adding Saul's offering of spoils.
- **15:23** — The Greek differs sharply from the Hebrew here; "healing" (Greek therapeian, also "service" or "care") likely reflects a sound-alike rendering of Hebrew teraphim ("household idols").
- **15:29** — Greek reads "Israel will be divided in two," where the Hebrew text reads "the Glory (or Eternity) of Israel will not lie."
- **15:34** — Greek "Armathaim" (also in 16:13) reflects the dual name Ramathaim, where the Hebrew text reads "Ramah."
