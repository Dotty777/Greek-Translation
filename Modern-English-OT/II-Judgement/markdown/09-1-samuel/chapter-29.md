## Text

**29:1** And the foreigners gathered all their camps to Aphek, and Israel encamped in Aeddon, which is in Israel.

**29:2** And the satraps of the foreigners were passing along by hundreds and thousands, and David and his men were passing along at the rear with Achish.

**29:3** And the satraps of the foreigners said, "Who are these passing through?" And Achish said to the commanders of the foreigners, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel? He has been with us for days, this second year, and I have found nothing in him from the day he defected to me even until this day."

**29:4** And the commanders of the foreigners were displeased with him and said to him, "Send the man back to his place where you stationed him, and let him not come with us into the battle, and let him not become a plotter against the camp. And with what will this man be reconciled to his lord? Will it not be with the heads of those men?

**29:5** Is this not David, for whom they led the song in dances, saying, 'Saul struck in his thousands, and David in his ten thousands'?"

**29:6** And Achish called David and said to him, "As the Lord lives, you are upright and good in my eyes, and so are your going out and your coming in with me in the camp, and I have found no evil against you from the day you came to me until this present day, and in the eyes of the satraps you are good.

**29:7** And now return and go in peace, and you will certainly not do evil in the eyes of the satraps of the foreigners."

**29:8** And David said to Achish, "What have I done to you, and what have you found in your servant from the day I was before you even until this day, that I should not come to fight the enemies of my lord the king?"

**29:9** And Achish answered David, "I know that you are good in my eyes, but the satraps of the foreigners say, 'He will not come with us into battle.'

**29:10** And now rise early in the morning, you and the servants of your lord who have come with you, and go to the place where I stationed you; and do not put a malicious word in your heart, for you are good before me. And rise early on the way, and let it grow light for you, and go."

**29:11** And David rose early, he and his men, to depart and guard the land of the foreigners; and the foreigners went up to fight against Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek allophyloi ("foreigners," literally "of another tribe") is the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines in 1 Samuel. The Greek also reads "Aeddon, which is in Israel," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the spring which is in Jezreel."
- **29:3** — The Greek reads "these passing through" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "these Hebrews" (reflecting a different reading of the same Hebrew consonants), and specifies "this second year" rather than "these years."
- **29:6** — The Greek text lacks a negative particle at the end of the verse ("you are good"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you are not good."
- **29:10** — The middle clauses ("and go to the place where I stationed you; and do not put a malicious word in your heart, for you are good before me") are a Septuagint plus absent from the Masoretic Text; in 29:11 the Greek similarly reads "guard the land of the foreigners" and "to fight against Israel" (MT: "return to the land of the Philistines" and "to Jezreel").
