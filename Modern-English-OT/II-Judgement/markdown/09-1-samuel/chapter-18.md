## Text

**18:6** And the dancing women came out to meet David from all the cities of Israel with tambourines and with gladness and with cymbals;

**18:7** and the women led the song and said, "Saul has struck down in his thousands, and David in his ten thousands."

**18:8** And the matter appeared evil in the eyes of Saul concerning this saying, and he said, "To David they have given the ten thousands, and to me they have given the thousands."

**18:9** And Saul was looking suspiciously at David from that day onward.

**18:12** And Saul was afraid of David's presence,

**18:13** and he removed him from himself and appointed him for himself as commander of a thousand; and he went out and went in before the people.

**18:14** And David was acting with understanding in all his ways, and the Lord was with him.

**18:15** And Saul saw that he was acting with great understanding, and he was wary of his presence.

**18:16** And all Israel and Judah loved David, because he went in and went out in the presence of the people.

**18:20** And Michal the daughter of Saul loved David, and it was reported to Saul, and it was right in his eyes.

**18:21** And Saul said, "I will give her to him, and she will become a stumbling block to him." And the hand of the foreigners was upon Saul.

**18:22** And Saul commanded his servants, saying, "Speak secretly to David, saying, 'Look, the king delights in you, and all his servants love you; now you, become son-in-law to the king.'"

**18:23** And the servants of Saul spoke these words into the ears of David; and David said, "Is it a light thing in your eyes to become son-in-law to a king? And I am a lowly man and not honored."

**18:24** And the servants of Saul reported to him according to these words that David spoke.

**18:25** And Saul said, "This is what you will say to David, 'The king has no desire for a gift, except a hundred foreskins of foreigners, to take vengeance on enemies of the king.'" Now Saul planned to cast him into the hands of the foreigners.

**18:26** And the servants of Saul reported these words to David, and the matter was right in the eyes of David to become son-in-law to the king;

**18:27** and David rose up and went, he and his men, and struck down among the foreigners a hundred men, and he brought up their foreskins to the king, and he became son-in-law to the king, and he gave him his daughter Michal as a wife.

**18:28** And Saul saw that the Lord was with David and that all Israel loved him,

**18:29** and he continued to be wary of David still more.

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — The shorter Septuagint text of chapter 18 omits 18:1-5, 10-11, 17-19, and 29b-30, and here has the dancing women come out to meet David rather than King Saul (MT).
- **18:21** — Unlike the Hebrew MT, which places a wish about the Philistines against David inside Saul's speech, the Greek ends Saul's speech after "stumbling block" and adds the narrative statement that the hand of the foreigners was upon Saul.
- **18:27** — The Septuagint reads "a hundred men," matching Saul's demand in 18:25, whereas the Hebrew MT reads "two hundred men."
- **18:28** — The Greek reads "and that all Israel loved him," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "and that Michal, Saul's daughter, loved him."
