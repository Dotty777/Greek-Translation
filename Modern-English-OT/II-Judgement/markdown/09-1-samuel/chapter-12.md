## Text

**12:1** And Samuel said to all Israel, "Look, I have listened to your voice in all that you said to me, and I have made a king reign over you.

**12:2** And now, look, the king walks before you, and I have grown old and will sit, and look, my sons are among you; and look, I have walked before you from youth and until this day.

**12:3** Here I am! Testify against me before the Lord and before His anointed: whose calf have I taken, or whose donkey have I taken, or whom among you have I oppressed, or whom have I exploited, or from whose hand have I taken a ransom and a sandal? Testify against me, and I will repay you."

**12:4** And they said to Samuel, "You have not wronged us, and you have neither oppressed nor crushed us, and you have taken nothing from the hand of anyone."

**12:5** And Samuel said to the people, "The Lord is witness among you, and His anointed is witness today on this day, that you have found nothing in my hand." And they said, "He is witness."

**12:6** And Samuel spoke to the people, saying, "The Lord is witness, who made Moses and Aaron, who brought up our fathers out of Egypt.

**12:7** And now, take your stand, and I will judge you before the Lord, and I will declare to you all the righteousness of the Lord, the things which He did among you and among your fathers:

**12:8** When Jacob and his sons entered into Egypt, and Egypt humbled them, then our fathers cried out to the Lord, and the Lord sent Moses and Aaron, and they brought our fathers out of Egypt, and He settled them in this place.

**12:9** And they forgot the Lord their God, and He sold them into the hands of Sisera, commander-in-chief of Jabis king of Hazor, and into the hands of foreigners, and into the hands of the king of Moab, and he fought against them.

**12:10** And they cried out to the Lord and said, 'We have sinned, because we have forsaken the Lord and have served the Baals and the groves; and now deliver us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve You.'

**12:11** And He sent Jeroboam and Barak and Jephthah and Samuel, and He delivered you from the hand of your enemies all around; and you lived in confidence.

**12:12** And you saw that Nahash king of the sons of Ammon came against you, and you said, 'No, rather a king will reign over us.'

**12:13** And now, here is the king whom you chose, and look, the Lord has given a king over you.

**12:14** If you fear the Lord and serve Him and listen to His voice and do not contend with the mouth of the Lord, and both you and the king who reigns over you are walking after the Lord—

**12:15** but if you do not listen to the voice of the Lord and contend with the mouth of the Lord, then the hand of the Lord will be against you and against your king.

**12:16** And now, take your stand and see this great thing which the Lord will do before your eyes.

**12:17** Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call upon the Lord, and He will give thunder and rain; and know and see that your wickedness is great, which you have done before the Lord by asking for a king for yourselves."

**12:18** And Samuel called upon the Lord, and the Lord gave thunder and rain on that day; and all the people feared the Lord greatly, and Samuel.

**12:19** And all the people said to Samuel, "Pray for your servants to the Lord your God, so that we will not die; for we have added wickedness to all our sins by asking for a king for ourselves."

**12:20** And Samuel said to the people, "Do not be afraid; you have done all this wickedness, yet do not turn aside from following the Lord, and serve the Lord with all your heart;

**12:21** and do not turn away after those that are nothing, who will accomplish nothing and who will not deliver, because they are nothing.

**12:22** For the Lord will not cast off His people because of His great name, because the Lord graciously took you to Himself as a people.

**12:23** And as for me, far be it from me to sin against the Lord by ceasing to pray for you; and I will serve the Lord, and I will show you the good and straight way.

**12:24** Only fear the Lord and serve Him in truth and with all your heart, for you have seen what great things He has done with you;

**12:25** and if in wickedness you do wickedly, both you and your king will be added."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Greek reads "and will sit" (kathēsomai), taking the Hebrew verb from the root "to sit" rather than "to grow gray-haired" (Masoretic Text).
- **12:3** — Greek reads "a ransom and a sandal" (exilasma kai hypodēma; cf. Sirach 46:19), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a bribe to blind my eyes with it."
- **12:9** — Greek names "Jabis king of Hazor" (cf. Jabin in Judges 4:2), a phrase absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text; "foreigners" (allophyloi) is the Septuagint's regular term for the Philistines in 1-2 Kingdoms.
- **12:11** — Greek reads the distinct names "Jeroboam and Barak," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Jerubbaal and Bedan."
- **12:25** — Greek reads "will be added" (prostethēsesthe), rendering the Hebrew verb for "be swept away" as if from the root "to add" (likely understood in the sense of being gathered to the dead).
