## Text

**9:1** Now there was a man from the sons of Benjamin, and his name was Kish, son of Abiel, son of Ared, son of Bachi, son of Aphek, son of a Jeminite man, a mighty man.

**9:2** And he had a son, and his name was Saul, of great stature, a good man, and there was no one among the sons of Israel better than he, from the shoulders and upward tall above all the land.

**9:3** Now the donkeys of Kish, Saul's father, were lost; and Kish said to Saul his son, "Take with you one of the young servants, and get up and go and search for the donkeys."

**9:4** And they passed through the hill country of Ephraim and passed through the land of Selcha, and they did not find them; and they passed through the land of Easakem, and they were not there; and they passed through the land of Jakim, and they did not find them.

**9:5** When they came to Siph, Saul said to his young servant who was with him, "Come, and let us turn back, or else my father will give up on the donkeys and worry about us."

**9:6** And the young servant said to him, "Look now, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is honored; everything that he speaks will surely come to pass. And now let us go, so that he may tell us our way on which we have gone."

**9:7** And Saul said to his young servant who was with him, "And look, we will go, and what shall we bring to the man of God? For the loaves of bread are gone from our containers, and there is nothing more with us to bring to the man of God that belongs to us."

**9:8** And the young servant answered Saul again and said, "Look, a quarter of a shekel of silver is found in my hand, and you shall give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

**9:9** And formerly in Israel each person used to say this when going to inquire of God: "Come, let us go to the seer"; for the people formerly called the prophet "The Seer."

**9:10** And Saul said to his young servant, "The word is good; come, and let us go." And they went to the city where the man of God was.

**9:11** As they were going up the ascent of the city, they found the young women who had come out to draw water, and they said to them, "Is the seer here?"

**9:12** And the young women answered them and said to them, "He is; look, right ahead of you. Now on account of the day he has come to the city, for the people have a sacrifice today in Bama.

**9:13** As soon as you enter the city, you will at once find him in the city before he goes up to Bama to eat; for the people will by no means eat until he enters, because he blesses the sacrifice, and after that the guests eat. And now go up, for on account of the day you will find him."

**9:14** And they went up to the city. As they were entering into the middle of the city, look, Samuel came out to meet them, to go up to Bama.

**9:15** Now the Lord had uncovered Samuel's ear one day before Saul came to him, saying,

**9:16** "About this time tomorrow I will send to you a man from Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as ruler over My people Israel, and he will save My people from the hand of foreigners; for I have looked upon the humiliation of My people, because their cry has come to Me."

**9:17** And Samuel saw Saul, and the Lord answered him, "Look, the man of whom I spoke to you! This one will rule over My people."

**9:18** And Saul approached Samuel in the middle of the city and said, "Please tell me which is the house of the seer."

**9:19** And Samuel answered Saul and said, "I am he. Go up ahead of me to Bama and eat with me today, and I will send you away in the morning and tell you all that is in your heart.

**9:20** And concerning your donkeys that were lost three days ago today, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. And for whom are the finest things of Israel? Are they not for you and for the house of your father?"

**9:21** And Saul answered and said, "Am I not the son of a Jeminite man, of the small tribe of the family of Israel, and of the least family out of the whole tribe of Benjamin? And why have you spoken to me according to this word?"

**9:22** And Samuel took Saul and his young servant and brought them into the guest hall, and set a place for them there among the foremost of those who were invited, about seventy men.

**9:23** And Samuel said to the cook, "Give me the portion that I gave you, which I told you to set aside with you."

**9:24** And the cook boiled the thigh and set it before Saul. And Samuel said to Saul, "Look, here is what is left over; set it before you and eat, for it has been set aside for you for a testimony above the others; tear it off." And Saul ate with Samuel on that day.

**9:25** And he went down from Bama into the city; and they spread a bed for Saul on the roof, and he slept.

**9:26** And it came about as the dawn was rising that Samuel called Saul on the roof, saying, "Get up, and I will send you away." And Saul got up, and he and Samuel went all the way outside.

**9:27** As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the young man, and let him pass on ahead of us; and you, stand still for now and hear a word of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Greek has the distinct ancestral names Ared, Bachi, and Aphek (for MT Zeror, Becorath, and Aphiah); in 9:2 Greek reads "all the land" rather than "all the people" (compare 10:23).
- **9:4** — Greek preserves the distinct place names Selcha, Easakem, and Jakim (for MT Shalishah, Shaalim, and Yemini), and Siph in 9:5 (for MT Zuph).
- **9:12** — Greek transliterates Hebrew bamah ("high place") as the place name Bama throughout chapters 9-11; "on account of the day" renders dia ten hemeran (also in 9:13).
- **9:16** — "Foreigners" renders Greek allophyloi (lit. "people of another tribe"), the Septuagint's regular designation for the Philistines in 1-2 Kingdoms.
- **9:24** — Following 9:22 ("about seventy men," vs. MT thirty), Greek here reads "boiled," "for a testimony," and "tear it off" (apoknize), and in 9:25 reads "they spread a bed for Saul on the roof, and he slept."
