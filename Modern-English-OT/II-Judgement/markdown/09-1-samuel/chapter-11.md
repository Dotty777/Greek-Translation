## Text

**11:1** And it happened about a month later that Nahash the Ammonite went up and encamped against Jabesh-gilead; and all the men of Jabesh said to Nahash the Ammonite, "Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you."

**11:2** And Nahash the Ammonite said to them, "On this condition I will make a covenant with you: by gouging out every right eye of yours, and I will lay a reproach upon Israel."

**11:3** And the men of Jabesh said to him, "Leave us alone for seven days, and we will send messengers into all the territory of Israel; if there is no one to save us, we will come out to you."

**11:4** And the messengers came to Gibeah, to Saul, and spoke the words in the ears of the people; and all the people lifted up their voice and wept.

**11:5** And look, Saul was coming after the morning from the field; and Saul said, "Why is it that the people are weeping?" And they recounted to him the words of the sons of Jabesh.

**11:6** And the Spirit of the Lord leaped upon Saul when he heard these words, and his anger burned greatly against them.

**11:7** And he took two oxen and cut them in pieces, and sent them into all the territory of Israel by the hand of messengers, saying, "Whoever does not go out after Saul and after Samuel, in this way they will do to his oxen." And the dread of the Lord came upon the people of Israel, and they cried out as one man.

**11:8** And he mustered them at Abiezek in Bama, every man of Israel, six hundred thousand, and the men of Judah, seventy thousand.

**11:9** And he said to the messengers who had come, "Thus you shall say to the men of Jabesh, 'Tomorrow you will have salvation when the sun grows hot.'" And the messengers came to the city and reported to the men of Jabesh, and they rejoiced.

**11:10** And the men of Jabesh said to Nahash the Ammonite, "Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you will do to us what is good in your sight."

**11:11** And it happened on the next day that Saul arranged the people into three divisions, and they entered the middle of the camp in the morning watch, and they struck the sons of Ammon until the day grew hot. And it came about that those who remained were scattered, and there were not left among them two together.

**11:12** And the people said to Samuel, "Who is the one who said that Saul will not reign over us? Hand over the men, and we will put them to death."

**11:13** And Samuel said, "No one shall die on this day, for today the Lord has worked salvation in Israel."

**11:14** And Samuel spoke to the people, saying, "Let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingdom there."

**11:15** And all the people went to Gilgal, and Samuel anointed Saul there as king before the Lord in Gilgal, and he sacrificed there sacrifices and peace offerings before the Lord; and Samuel and all Israel rejoiced exceedingly.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Greek opens with "about a month later" (hos meta mena, matching 4QSama), whereas the Masoretic Text ends 10:27 with "he kept silent."
- **11:5** — Greek reads "after the morning" (meta to proi), reflecting Hebrew habboqer ("the morning") rather than MT habbaqar ("the oxen").
- **11:8** — Greek preserves the distinct place name Abiezek (for MT Bezek) together with "in Bama," and gives the muster numbers as six hundred thousand for Israel and seventy thousand for Judah (vs. MT three hundred thousand and thirty thousand).
- **11:13** — Greek names Samuel rather than Saul as the speaker here, and in 11:15 likewise attributes the anointing, sacrifices, and rejoicing specifically to Samuel (vs. the people and Saul in MT).
