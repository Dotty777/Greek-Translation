## Text

**27:1** And David said in his heart, saying, "Now one day I will be given over into the hands of Saul, and there is nothing good for me unless I escape to the land of foreigners and Saul ceases from seeking me within all the territory of Israel, and I will escape from his hand."

**27:2** And David set out, and the four hundred men with him, to Achish son of Ammach, king of Gath.

**27:3** And David lived with Achish, he and his men, each one and his household, and David and both of his wives, Ahinoam the Israelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**27:4** And it was reported to Saul that David had fled to Gath; and he did not continue any longer to seek him.

**27:5** And David said to Achish, "If now your servant has found favor in your eyes, please let them give me a place in one of the cities in the country, and I will live there; and why does your servant live in a royal city with you?"

**27:6** And on that day he gave him Ziklag; therefore Ziklag belonged to the king of Judea until this day.

**27:7** And the number of the days that David lived in the way of the foreigners was four months.

**27:8** And David and his men would go up and attack all the Geshurites and the Amalekites; and look, the land was inhabited from the adjoining places, the one from the fortified places of Gelampsour even as far as Egypt.

**27:9** And he would strike the land and would not leave man or woman alive; and he would take flocks and herds and donkeys and camels and clothing, and they turned back and came to Achish.

**27:10** And Achish said to David, "Whom did you attack today?" And David said to Achish, "Against the south of Judea, and against the south of Jesmega, and against the south of the Kenizzites."

**27:11** And I did not leave man or woman alive to bring into Gath, saying, "May they not report to Gath against us, saying, 'This is what David does.'" And this was his rule all the days that David lived in the country of the foreigners.

**27:12** And David was greatly trusted by Achish, saying, "He has been utterly shamed among his people, in Israel; and he will be a servant to me forever."

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — Throughout 1 Kingdoms, the Greek uses a common term meaning 'foreigners' where the Hebrew has 'Philistines.' Here 'I will be given over' translates a Greek verb literally meaning 'be added' or 'handed over.'
- **27:2** — The Greek reads 'four hundred men' and 'Ammach' where the Hebrew text has 'six hundred men' and 'Maoch.'
- **27:3** — The Greek reads 'Ahinoam the Israelite' where the Hebrew text has 'Ahinoam the Jezreelitess,' and omits 'in Gath.'
- **27:7** — The Greek reads 'in the way of the foreigners' and 'four months' where the Hebrew text has 'in the country of the Philistines' and 'a year and four months.'
- **27:8** — This Greek clause is textually difficult and contains a doublet ('Gelampsour' and 'fortified places') corresponding to Hebrew 'from of old, as you go to Shur'; the Greek also omits the Girzites.
- **27:10** — The Greek reads 'Jesmega' and 'the Kenizzites' where the Hebrew text has 'the Jerahmeelites' and 'the Kenites.'
- **27:11** — The Greek text here shifts abruptly to the first-person singular ('I did not leave ... alive') where the Hebrew has third-person ('David would leave neither ... alive').
