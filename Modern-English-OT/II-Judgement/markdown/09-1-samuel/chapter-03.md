## Text

**3:1** Now the boy Samuel was ministering to the Lord before Eli the priest. And a word of the Lord was precious in those days; there was no distinct vision.

**3:2** And it happened on that day that Eli was sitting in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow heavy, and he could not see.

**3:3** And before the lamp of God was trimmed, Samuel was sleeping in the temple where the ark of God was.

**3:4** And the Lord called, "Samuel, Samuel!" And he said, "Here I am."

**3:5** And he ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." And he said, "I did not call you; go back, sleep." And he went back and slept.

**3:6** And the Lord called again, "Samuel, Samuel!" And he went to Eli a second time and said, "Here I am, for you called me." And he said, "I did not call you; go back, sleep."

**3:7** Now Samuel did not yet know God, and a word of the Lord had not yet been revealed to him.

**3:8** And the Lord called Samuel again a third time. And he got up and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." Then Eli discerned that the Lord had called the boy.

**3:9** And he said, "Go back, sleep, child; and it will be, if He calls you, that you will say, 'Speak, for Your servant is listening.'" And Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**3:10** And the Lord came and took His stand, and called him as once and again. And Samuel said, "Speak, for Your servant is listening."

**3:11** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Look, I am performing My words in Israel: for everyone who hears them, both of his ears will ring.

**3:12** On that day I will raise up against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house; I will begin and I will finish.

**3:13** And I have declared to him that I Myself am punishing his house forever for the iniquities of his sons, because his sons were speaking evil of God, and he did not admonish them,

**3:14** and not even so. I have sworn to the house of Eli, 'Surely the iniquity of the house of Eli will not be atoned for with incense and with sacrifices forever.'"

**3:15** And Samuel lay until morning, and rose early in the morning and opened the doors of the house of the Lord. And Samuel was afraid to report the vision to Eli.

**3:16** And Eli said to Samuel, "Samuel, child." And he said, "Here I am."

**3:17** And he said, "What is the word that was spoken to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do so to you if you hide from me a word from all the words that were spoken to you in your ears."

**3:18** And Samuel reported all the words and hid nothing from him. And Eli said, "He is the Lord; He will do what is good before Him."

**3:19** And Samuel grew up, and the Lord was with him, and none of all his words fell to the ground.

**3:20** And all Israel from Dan even to Beersheba knew that Samuel was faithful as a prophet to the Lord.

**3:21** And the Lord manifested Himself again in Shiloh, for the Lord was revealed to Samuel. And Samuel was trusted to become a prophet to the Lord for all Israel, from one end of the land to the other. Now Eli was very old, and his sons kept going on and on, and their way was evil before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — Greek "before the lamp of God was trimmed" (prin episkeuasthenai) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("before the lamp of God had gone out").
- **3:10** — The Septuagint reads "called him as once and again" without repeating the spoken name here, having doubled "Samuel, Samuel" in 3:4 and 3:6; both 3:9 and 3:10 also omit "Lord" from Samuel's reply.
- **3:13** — Greek "speaking evil of God" preserves an ancient reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "bringing a curse on themselves" (one of the traditional scribal corrections).
- **3:14** — Greek "and not even so" (kai oud' houtos) completes the thought of 3:13 (reflecting Hebrew "and not so" rather than "and therefore"); in the oath, Greek ei (literally "if") functions as a Semitic idiom for emphatic negation.
- **3:21** — The Septuagint includes an expanded conclusion describing Samuel's accreditation across all Israel and the ongoing wickedness of Eli's sons, not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
