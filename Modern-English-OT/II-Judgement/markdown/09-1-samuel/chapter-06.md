## Text

**6:1** And the ark was in the country of the foreigners seven months, and their land swarmed with mice.

**6:2** And the foreigners called the priests and the diviners and their enchanters, saying, "What shall we do with the ark of the Lord? Make known to us with what we shall send it to its place."

**6:3** And they said, "If you are sending away the ark of the covenant of God, the Lord of Israel, do not send it away empty, but surely repay it for the torment, and then you will be healed, and atonement will be made for you; will not His hand turn away from you?"

**6:4** And they said, "What shall we repay to it for the torment?" And they said, "According to the number of the satraps of the foreigners, five golden seats, for a calamity is among you and your rulers and the people;

**6:5** and golden mice, a likeness of your mice that are destroying the land. And you shall give glory to the Lord, so that He may lighten His hand from you and from your gods and from your land.

**6:6** And why do you make your hearts heavy, as Egypt and Pharaoh made their heart heavy? When He mocked them, did they not send them away, and they departed?

**6:7** And now take and make a new cart and two cows that have borne their first young, without their young; and yoke the cows to the cart, and lead the young home, away from behind them.

**6:8** And you shall take the ark and put it on the cart, and the golden articles you shall repay to it for the torment, and you shall not put them in a berechthan box at its side; and you shall send it away and drive it off, and you shall depart.

**6:9** And you shall watch: if it goes on the way to its territory toward Beth-shemesh, He has done this great evil to us; and if not, then we will know that it is not His hand that has touched us, but this has happened to us as a coincidence."

**6:10** And the foreigners did so, and they took two cows that had borne their first young and yoked them to the cart, and they kept their young back at home.

**6:11** And they put the ark on the cart, and the ergab box and the golden mice.

**6:12** And the cows went straight on the way toward the way of Beth-shemesh; on one path they went and grew weary, and they did not turn aside to the right or to the left; and the satraps of the foreigners went after it as far as the borders of Beth-shemesh.

**6:13** And those in Beth-shemesh were reaping a wheat harvest in a valley, and they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark of the Lord, and they rejoiced to meet it.

**6:14** And the cart entered the field of Hoshea, the one in Beth-shemesh, and they set up a great stone there beside it; and they split the wood of the cart, and offered up the cows as a whole burnt offering to the Lord.

**6:15** And the Levites brought up the ark of the Lord and the ergab box with it and the golden articles upon it, and they put them on the great stone; and the men of Beth-shemesh offered up whole burnt offerings and sacrifices on that day to the Lord.

**6:16** And the five satraps of the foreigners were watching, and they returned to Ashkelon on that day.

**6:17** And these are the golden seats which the foreigners repaid for the torment to the Lord: for Ashdod one, for Gaza one, for Ashkelon one, for Gath one, for Ekron one.

**6:18** And the golden mice were according to the number of all the cities of the foreigners of the five satraps, from a fortified city even to a village of the Perizzite, and as far as the great stone upon which they placed the ark of the covenant of the Lord, which is in the field of Hoshea the Beth-shemite.

**6:19** And the sons of Jeconiah were not pleased among the men of Beth-shemesh because they saw the ark of the Lord; and He struck among them seventy men and fifty thousand men. And the people mourned, because the Lord struck among the people a very great blow.

**6:20** And the men from Beth-shemesh said, "Who will be able to pass before this Holy One? And to whom shall the ark of the Lord go up from us?"

**6:21** And they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The foreigners have returned the ark of the Lord; come down and bring it up to yourselves."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Greek uses 'allophyloi' ('foreigners'), the Septuagint's regular designation throughout 1-2 Kingdoms for the Philistines. The closing clause 'and their land swarmed with mice' is a Septuagint plus not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **6:4** — Greek 'hedras' ('seats') is an anatomical euphemism for the buttocks or posterior, reflecting the affliction of chapter 5 (where the Hebrew has 'tumors'). Likewise, 'for the torment' (Greek 'tes basanou', also in vv. 3, 8, 17) corresponds to the Hebrew term for a guilt offering.
- **6:8** — The Greek text (following Codex Vaticanus) reads the negative 'shall not put' and pairs 'box' with the transliteration 'berechthan' (comparable to 'ergab' in vv. 11 and 15), a double rendering of the Hebrew word for a coffer or chest.
- **6:14** — Greek reads 'Hoshea' (also in v. 18) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Joshua,' and states transitively that they set up a great stone beside it rather than merely noting a stone was there.
- **6:16** — Greek reads 'Ashkelon' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Ekron.'
- **6:18** — Greek renders Hebrew 'unwalled village' as 'a village of the Perizzite' and reads 'the great stone' where the Masoretic Text has 'the great Abel' (or 'meadow').
- **6:19** — The opening clause concerning 'the sons of Jeconiah' is a distinctive Septuagint reading not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
