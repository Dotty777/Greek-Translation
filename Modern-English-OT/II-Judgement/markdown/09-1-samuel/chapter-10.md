## Text

**10:1** And Samuel took the flask of oil and poured it on his head, and kissed him and said to him, "Has not the Lord anointed you as ruler over His people, over Israel? And you will rule over the people of the Lord, and you will save them from the hand of their enemies all around. And this is the sign to you that the Lord has anointed you over His inheritance as ruler:

**10:2** When you depart from me today, you will find two men by the tombs of Rachel in the hill country of Benjamin, leaping greatly, and they will say to you, 'The donkeys that you went to search for have been found; and look, your father has shaken off the matter of the donkeys, and has become anxious because of you, saying, "What shall I do about my son?"'

**10:3** And you will depart from there and come farther, as far as the oak of Tabor, and you will find there three men going up to God at Bethel, one carrying three young goats, and one carrying three containers of bread, and one carrying a skin of wine;

**10:4** and they will ask after your welfare and give you two firstfruits of bread, and you shall take them from their hand.

**10:5** And after these things you will enter into the hill of God, where the outpost of the foreigners is; there is Nasib the foreigner. And it will come about when you enter there into the city that you will meet a band of prophets coming down from Bama, and before them a harp and a tambourine and a flute and a lyre, and they will be prophesying;

**10:6** and the Spirit of the Lord will leap upon you, and you will prophesy with them, and you will be turned into another man.

**10:7** And it will come about when these signs come upon you, do everything that your hand finds, for God is with you.

**10:8** And you shall go down ahead of Gilead, and look, I am going down to you to offer up a burnt offering and peace sacrifices. You shall wait seven days until I come to you, and I will make known to you what you shall do."

**10:9** And it came about when he turned his shoulder to depart from Samuel that God changed his heart to another; and all the signs came to pass on that day.

**10:10** And he came from there to the hill, and look, a band of prophets was opposite him; and the Spirit of God leaped upon him, and he prophesied in the midst of them.

**10:11** And it came about that all who knew him yesterday and the day before saw, and look, he was in the midst of the prophets. And the people said, each one to his neighbor, "What is this that has come about for the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:12** And one of them answered and said, "And who is his father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:13** And he finished prophesying, and came to the hill.

**10:14** And his relative said to him and to his young servant, "Where did you go?" And they said, "To search for the donkeys; and we saw that they were not there, and we went in to Samuel."

**10:15** And the relative said to Saul, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**10:16** And Saul said to his relative, "He certainly told me that the donkeys have been found." But he did not tell him the matter of the kingdom.

**10:17** And Samuel summoned all the people to the Lord at Mizpah,

**10:18** and he said to the sons of Israel, "Thus the Lord, the God of Israel, spoke, saying, 'I brought up the sons of Israel out of Egypt, and I delivered you from the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and from all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.'

**10:19** Yet you today have despised God, who Himself is your Savior from all your evils and your afflictions, and you said, 'No, rather you shall set a king over us.' And now present yourselves before the Lord according to your tribes and according to your families."

**10:20** And Samuel brought near all the tribes of Israel, and the tribe of Benjamin was chosen by lot.

**10:21** And he brought near the tribe of Benjamin by families, and the family of Matri was chosen by lot; and they brought near the family of Matri by men, and Saul son of Kish was chosen by lot. And he searched for him, and he was not found.

**10:22** And Samuel inquired further of the Lord, "Is the man coming here?" And the Lord said, "Look, he has hidden himself among the baggage."

**10:23** And he ran and took him from there, and set him in the midst of the people; and he stood tall above all the people from the shoulders and upward.

**10:24** And Samuel said to all the people, "Have you seen the one whom the Lord has chosen for Himself, that there is no one like him among all of you?" And all the people recognized him and said, "May the king live!"

**10:25** And Samuel spoke to the people the right of the king, and wrote it in a book and placed it before the Lord. And Samuel sent all the people away, and each one departed to his place.

**10:26** And Saul departed to his house at Gibeah; and men of might, whose hearts the Lord had touched, went with Saul.

**10:27** And pestilent men said, "Who will save us—this one?" And they dishonored him, and did not bring him gifts.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Greek preserves the longer reading ("over His people, over Israel? And you will rule over the people of the Lord, and you will save them from the hand of their enemies all around. And this is the sign to you..."), omitted in the Masoretic Text by homoioteleuton.
- **10:2** — Greek reads plural "tombs," "in the hill country of Benjamin" (orei, vs. MT "border"), and "leaping greatly" (hallomenous megala, where MT has the place name Zelzah).
- **10:5** — Greek contains a doublet for Hebrew netsiv ("garrison/prefect"), first translating it as "the outpost" (to anastema) and then transliterating it as a personal name, "Nasib the foreigner."
- **10:8** — Greek reads "ahead of Gilead" (Galaad) rather than "before me to Gilgal" (compare Gilgal in 11:14-15).
- **10:19** — Greek renders "tribes" with skeptra (lit. "scepters," also in 9:21; 10:20-21) and "families" with phylas; in 10:24 Greek reads "recognized him" (egnosan) rather than "shouted."
