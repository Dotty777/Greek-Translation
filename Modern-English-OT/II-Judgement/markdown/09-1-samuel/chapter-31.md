## Text

**31:1** And the foreigners were fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel fled from before the foreigners and fell wounded on Mount Gilboa.

**31:2** And the foreigners closed in on Saul and his sons, and the foreigners struck down Jonathan and Jonadab and Malchishua, son of Saul.

**31:3** And the battle grew heavy against Saul, and the marksmen, men who were archers, found him, and he was wounded in the abdomen.

**31:4** And Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me through with it, so that these uncircumcised ones do not come and pierce me through and mock me." And his armor-bearer was not willing, because he was greatly afraid; so Saul took the sword and fell upon it.

**31:5** And his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, and he also fell upon his sword and died with him.

**31:6** And Saul died, and his three sons, and his armor-bearer, on that day together.

**31:7** And the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley and those who were on the other side of the Jordan saw that the men of Israel had fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, and they abandoned their cities and fled; and the foreigners came and lived in them.

**31:8** And it happened on the next day that the foreigners came to strip the dead; and they found Saul and his three sons fallen on the mountains of Gilboa.

**31:9** And they turned him over and stripped off his armor, and sent it into the land of the foreigners all around, proclaiming good news to their idols and to the people;

**31:10** and they placed his armor in the temple of Astarte, and they fastened his body on the wall of Beth-em.

**31:11** And the inhabitants of Jabesh of Gilead heard what the foreigners had done to Saul.

**31:12** And every man of valor arose and marched all night, and took the body of Saul and the body of Jonathan his son from the wall of Beth-sam, and brought them to Jabesh and burned them there.

**31:13** And they took their bones and buried them under the field of Jabesh, and fasted seven days. And it happened after Saul died that David returned from striking Amalek, and David stayed in Ziklag two days.

## Translator Notes

- **31:2** — The Greek names Saul's second son "Jonadab" (MT: "Abinadab") and uses singular "son of Saul."
- **31:3** — The Greek specifically states that Saul was wounded "in the abdomen" (hypochondria), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "badly wounded by the archers."
- **31:9** — The Greek reads "turned him over" (apostrephousin auton) where the Masoretic Text reads "cut off his head."
- **31:10** — The Greek names the city "Beth-em" here and "Beth-sam" in 31:12 (MT: "Beth-shan" in both), and in 31:12 specifies only the bodies of Saul and Jonathan rather than Saul and his sons.
- **31:13** — The Greek reads "under the field of Jabesh" (MT: "under the tamarisk tree in Jabesh"), and appends the opening notice of 2 Samuel 1:1 ("And it happened after Saul died... two days") directly to the end of the verse.
