## Text

**17:1** And the foreigners gathered their camps for battle, and they gathered at Socoh of Idumea, and they encamped between Socoh and Azekah, Ephermem.

**17:2** And Saul and the men of Israel gathered and encamped in the valley; they drew up in array for battle opposite the foreigners.

**17:3** And the foreigners stood on the mountain on one side, and Israel stood on the mountain on the other side, in a circle between them.

**17:4** And a mighty man came out from the battle line of the foreigners, Goliath by name, from Gath; his height was four cubits and a span.

**17:5** And a helmet was on his head, and he was clothed in a chain-mail breastplate, and the weight of his breastplate was five thousand shekels of bronze and iron;

**17:6** and bronze greaves were over his legs, and a bronze shield was between his shoulders;

**17:7** and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam, and his spearhead was six hundred shekels of iron; and the one carrying his armor went before him.

**17:8** And he stood up and shouted to the battle line of Israel and said to them, "Why do you come out to draw up in array for battle opposite us? Am I not a foreigner, and you Hebrews and Saul? Choose for yourselves a man, and let him come down to me;

**17:9** and if he is able to fight against me and if he strikes me down, then we will be servants to you; but if I prevail and strike him down, you will be servants to us and will serve us."

**17:10** And the foreigner said, "Look, I have reproached the battle line of Israel today, on this day; give me a man, and the two of us will fight in single combat."

**17:11** And Saul and all Israel heard these words of the foreigner, and they were stunned and very afraid.

**17:32** And David said to Saul, "Please do not let the heart of my lord sink because of him; your servant will go and fight with this foreigner."

**17:33** And Saul said to David, "You will certainly not be able to go against the foreigner to fight with him, for you are a boy, and he is a fighting man from his youth."

**17:34** And David said to Saul, "Your servant was shepherding for his father with the flock; and whenever the lion and the bear came and took a sheep from the herd,

**17:35** I went out after it and struck it and pulled it out of its mouth; and if it rose up against me, I seized its throat and struck it and put it to death.

**17:36** Both the bear and the lion your servant struck, and the uncircumcised foreigner will be like one of them; will I not go and strike him down, and remove reproach from Israel today? For who is this uncircumcised one who has reproached the battle line of the living God?

**17:37** The Lord, who rescued me from the hand of the lion and from the hand of the bear, He Himself will rescue me from the hand of this uncircumcised foreigner." And Saul said to David, "Go, and the Lord will be with you."

**17:38** And Saul put a cloak on David and a bronze helmet around his head,

**17:39** and he belted his sword onto David over his cloak; and he grew weary walking once and twice. And David said to Saul, "I will certainly not be able to go in these, for I have no experience." And they removed them from him.

**17:40** And he took his staff in his hand, and chose for himself five perfect stones from the stream and put them in the shepherd's pouch that he had for gathering, and his sling was in his hand; and he approached the foreign man.

**17:42** And Goliath saw David and disdained him, for he was a boy, and he was ruddy with beauty of eyes.

**17:43** And the foreigner said to David, "Am I like a dog, that you come against me with a rod and stones?" And David said, "No, rather worse than a dog." And the foreigner cursed David by his own gods.

**17:44** And the foreigner said to David, "Come here to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the sky and to the beasts of the earth."

**17:45** And David said to the foreigner, "You come to me with a sword and with a spear and with a shield, and I go to you in the name of the Lord God Sabaoth of the battle line of Israel, which you have reproached today;

**17:46** and the Lord will deliver you up today into my hand, and I will kill you and remove your head from you, and I will give your limbs and the limbs of the camp of the foreigners on this day to the birds of the sky and to the wild beasts of the earth; and all the earth will know that there is a God in Israel.

**17:47** And all this assembly will know that the Lord does not save with sword and spear; for the battle belongs to the Lord, and the Lord will hand you over into our hands."

**17:48** And the foreigner rose up and went to meet David.

**17:49** And David stretched out his hand into the pouch and took from there one stone, and he slung it and struck the foreigner on his forehead, and the stone penetrated through the helmet into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground.

**17:51** And David ran and stood over him, and took his sword and put him to death and removed his head; and the foreigners saw that their mighty man was dead, and they fled.

**17:52** And the men of Israel and Judah rose up and raised a battle cry, and they pursued after them as far as the entrance of Gath and as far as the gate of Ashkelon; and the wounded of the foreigners fell on the road of the gates, and as far as Gath and as far as Ekron.

**17:53** And the men of Israel returned, turning away from behind the foreigners, and they trampled down their camps.

**17:54** And David took the head of the foreigner and brought it to Jerusalem, and he placed his equipment in his tent.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Septuagint regularly renders Philistines literally as allophyloi ("foreigners"), here locates Socoh in "Idumea" (MT: "Judah"), and gives the third place name as "Ephermem" (MT: "at Ephes-dammim").
- **17:4** — The Greek gives Goliath's height as "four cubits and a span" (agreeing with 4QSam^a and Josephus), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "six cubits and a span."
- **17:8** — The Greek reads "Hebrews and Saul" (Hebraioi kai Saoul), differing from the Hebrew MT's "servants of Saul."
- **17:32** — The shorter Septuagint narrative omits 17:12-31, 41, 50, and 55-58, and here reads "the heart of my lord" where the Hebrew MT reads "the heart of a man."
- **17:52** — The Septuagint preserves distinct geographic readings here: "entrance of Gath" (MT: "a valley"), "gate of Ashkelon" (MT: "Ekron"), and "road of the gates" (MT: "Shaaraim").
