## Text

**25:1** And Samuel died, and all Israel assembled together and mourned for him, and they buried him in his house in Armathaim. And David rose up and went down to the wilderness of Maon.

**25:2** And there was a man in Maon, and his flocks were in Carmel. And the man was very great, and he had three thousand sheep and one thousand goats; and it happened as he was shearing his flock in Carmel.

**25:3** And the name of the man was Nabal, and the name of his wife was Abigail. And his wife was good in understanding and very good in appearance, while the man was harsh and wicked in his practices, and the man was doglike.

**25:4** And David heard in the wilderness that Nabal the Carmelite was shearing his flock.

**25:5** And David sent ten young men, and said to the young men, "Go up to Carmel and go to Nabal, and ask after his peace in my name,

**25:6** and you will say this: 'For the seasons to come, may you be in good health, your house and all that is yours in good health.

**25:7** And now, look, I have heard that your shepherds who were with us in the wilderness are now shearing for you, and we did not hinder them, and we did not command them anything all the days while they were in Carmel.

**25:8** Ask your young men, and they will report to you. And let your young men find favor in your eyes, because we have come on a good day. Please give whatever your hand finds to your son David.'"

**25:9** And the young men came and spoke these words to Nabal and all these sayings in the name of David, and he sprang up,

**25:10** and Nabal answered the servants of David and said, "Who is David, and who is the son of Jesse? Today servants have multiplied who depart, each from before his master.

**25:11** And should I take my loaves of bread and my wine and my meat that I have slaughtered for those who shear my sheep, and give them to men of whom I do not know where they are from?"

**25:12** And David's young men turned back on their way, and returned and came and reported to David according to these words.

**25:13** And David said to his men, "Each of you, strap on his sword." And about four hundred men went up after David, and the two hundred stayed with the baggage.

**25:14** And one of the young men reported to Abigail, the wife of Nabal, saying, "Look, David sent messengers from the wilderness to bless our master, and he turned away from them.

**25:15** And the men were very good to us; they did not hinder us, nor did they command us all the days that we were with them;

**25:16** and while we were in the field, they were like a wall around us both night and day, all the days that we were with them shepherding the flock.

**25:17** And now know and see what you will do, because evil has been determined against our master and against his house; and he is a pestilent son, and it is not possible to speak to him."

**25:18** And Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves of bread, and two vessels of wine, and five prepared sheep, and five ephahs of barley meal, and one gomor of raisins, and two hundred fig cakes, and placed them on the donkeys.

**25:19** And she said to her young men, "Go on ahead before me, and look, I am coming behind you." And she did not report it to her husband.

**25:20** And it happened, as she was riding on the donkey and coming down in the shelter of the mountain, that look, David and his men were coming down to meet her; and she met them.

**25:21** And David said, "Perhaps for injustice I have guarded all that is his in the wilderness, and we did not command to take anything from all that is his, yet he has repaid me evil in return for good.

**25:22** May God do so to David and add so to it, if I leave of all that belongs to Nabal until morning one who urinates against a wall."

**25:23** And Abigail saw David, and hurried and leaped down from the donkey, and fell before David on her face, and bowed in homage to him to the ground

**25:24** at his feet, and said, "On me, my lord, be my wrongdoing; please let your maidservant speak into your ears, and listen to the word of your maidservant.

**25:25** Please let my lord not set his heart upon this pestilent man, because according to his name, so he is: Nabal is his name, and folly is with him. And I, your maidservant, did not see your young men whom you sent.

**25:26** And now, lord, as the Lord lives and as your soul lives, just as the Lord has hindered you from coming into innocent blood and from your hand bringing deliverance for you, now may your enemies and those who seek evil against my lord become like Nabal.

**25:27** And now receive the blessing that your maidservant has brought to my lord, and you will give it to the young men who stand beside my lord.

**25:28** Please take away the transgression of your maidservant, because the Lord will certainly make for my lord a faithful house, because the Lord fights the battle of my lord, and evil will never be found in you.

**25:29** And a man will rise up pursuing you and seeking your soul, and the soul of my lord will be bound up in the bundle of life with the Lord God, and you will sling the soul of your enemies in the middle of the sling.

**25:30** And it will be that the Lord will do for my lord all the good things that He has spoken concerning you, and He will appoint you as ruler over Israel.

**25:31** And this will not be to you an abomination and a stumbling block to my lord, to shed innocent blood without cause and for the hand of my lord to bring deliverance for himself. And may the Lord do good to my lord, and you will remember your maidservant, to do good to her."

**25:32** And David said to Abigail, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who sent you to meet me.

**25:33** And blessed be your conduct, and blessed be you, who have hindered me today on this day from coming into bloodshed and from my hand bringing deliverance for myself.

**25:34** Nevertheless, as the Lord God of Israel lives, who hindered me today from harming you, if you had not hurried and come to meet me, then I said, 'Surely there will not be left to Nabal until the light of morning one who urinates against a wall.'"

**25:35** And David received from her hand all that she had brought to him, and said to her, "Go up in peace to your house; see, I have listened to your voice and have favored your face."

**25:36** And Abigail came to Nabal, and look, he had a king's banquet, and Nabal's heart was glad within him, and he was exceedingly drunk; and she did not report to him a word, small or great, until the light of morning.

**25:37** And it happened in the morning, when Nabal sobered up from the wine, that his wife reported these words, and his heart died within him, and he became like a stone.

**25:38** And it happened after about ten days that the Lord struck Nabal, and he died.

**25:39** And David heard and said, "Blessed be the Lord, who has judged the case of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and has preserved His servant from the hand of evils; and the Lord has returned the evil of Nabal upon his own head." And David sent and spoke concerning Abigail, to take her for himself as a wife.

**25:40** And the servants of David came to Abigail at Carmel and spoke to her, saying, "David has sent us to you, to take you for himself as a wife."

**25:41** And she rose up and bowed in homage to the ground on her face and said, "Here is your maidservant, to be a servant girl to wash the feet of your servants."

**25:42** And Abigail rose up and mounted the donkey, and five young women followed her; and she went after the servants of David, and she became his wife.

**25:43** And David took Ahinoam from Israel, and both of them were his wives.

**25:44** And Saul gave Michal his daughter, the wife of David, to Palti, the son of Ameis, who was from Romma.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — Greek reads "Armathaim" and "Maon," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Ramah" and "Paran."
- **25:3** — Greek reads "doglike," rendering the Hebrew tribal name "Calebite" etymologically from the root for "dog."
- **25:22** — Greek reads "to David," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to the enemies of David."
- **25:28** — In the Greek syntax the Lord is the subject fighting David's battle ("the Lord fights the battle of my lord"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text says that David fights the battles of the Lord.
- **25:43** — Greek reads "from Israel," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "of Jezreel."
- **25:44** — Greek reads the proper names "Ameis" and "Romma," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Laish" and "Gallim."
