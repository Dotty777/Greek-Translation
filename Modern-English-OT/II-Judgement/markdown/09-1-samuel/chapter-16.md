## Text

**16:1** And the Lord said to Samuel, "How long will you mourn over Saul, since I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil, and come, I will send you to Jesse, to Bethlehem, for I have seen among his sons one to reign for Me."

**16:2** And Samuel said, "How can I go? Saul will hear and kill me." And the Lord said, "Take a heifer from the cattle in your hand and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to the Lord.'

**16:3** And you shall invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will make known to you what you shall do, and you shall anoint the one whom I tell you."

**16:4** And Samuel did everything that the Lord spoke to him, and came to Bethlehem. And the elders of the city were alarmed at meeting him and said, "Is your coming in peace, seer?"

**16:5** And he said, "Peace; I have come to sacrifice to the Lord. Consecrate yourselves and rejoice with me today." And he consecrated Jesse and his sons, and invited them to the sacrifice.

**16:6** And it happened as they were entering that he saw Eliab and said, "Surely before the Lord is His anointed."

**16:7** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Do not look at his appearance nor at the bearing of his height, because I have rejected him; for not as a man looks does God see, because a man looks at the face, but God looks at the heart."

**16:8** And Jesse called Amminadab, and he passed before Samuel, and he said, "Neither has God chosen this one."

**16:9** And Jesse brought Shammah forward; and he said, "This one also the Lord has not chosen."

**16:10** And Jesse brought his seven sons forward before Samuel; and Samuel said, "The Lord has not chosen among these."

**16:11** And Samuel said to Jesse, "Are there no more young men?" And he said, "There is still the youngest; look, he is shepherding the flock." And Samuel said to Jesse, "Send and bring him, for we will certainly not recline until he comes."

**16:12** And he sent and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, with beauty of eyes, and good in appearance to the Lord. And the Lord said to Samuel, "Rise and anoint David, for this one is good."

**16:13** And Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers; and the Spirit of the Lord leaped upon David from that day onward; and Samuel rose and went away to Armathaim.

**16:14** And the Spirit of the Lord departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the Lord was choking him.

**16:15** And Saul's attendants said to him, "Look now, an evil spirit of the Lord is choking you.

**16:16** Let your servants speak now before you, and let them seek for our lord a man who knows how to play on a lyre; and it will happen, when an evil spirit is upon you, that he will play on his lyre, and it will be good for you, and he will give you rest."

**16:17** And Saul said to his attendants, "Please look for a man for me who plays well, and bring him in to me."

**16:18** And one of his young men answered and said, "Look, I have seen a son of Jesse, a Bethlehemite, and he knows music, and the man is intelligent, and the man is a warrior, and wise in speech, and a man good in appearance, and the Lord is with him."

**16:19** And Saul sent messengers to Jesse, saying, "Send to me your son David, who is with your flock."

**16:20** And Jesse took a gomor of loaves of bread and a skin of wine and one kid from the goats, and sent them by the hand of his son David to Saul.

**16:21** And David came in to Saul and stood before him; and he loved him greatly, and he became his armor-bearer.

**16:22** And Saul sent to Jesse, saying, "Please let David stand before me, for he has found favor in my eyes."

**16:23** And it happened, whenever an evil spirit was upon Saul, that David would take the lyre and play with his hand; and Saul would be refreshed, and it would be good for him, and the evil spirit would depart from him.

## Translator Notes

- **16:8** — Greek reads "Amminadab" (Ameinadab), where the Hebrew text reads "Abinadab."
- **16:12** — Greek adds "to the Lord," names "David" explicitly, and reads "for this one is good" where the Hebrew text reads "for this is he."
- **16:14** — Greek "was choking" (also "is choking" in 16:15) renders the Hebrew "was terrorizing" with a physical image of suffocation, contrasted with Saul being "refreshed" (literally breathing freely again) in 16:23.
- **16:20** — Greek "gomor" (a dry measure corresponding to Hebrew omer or homer) reflects a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants for "donkey" (a donkey laden with bread).
