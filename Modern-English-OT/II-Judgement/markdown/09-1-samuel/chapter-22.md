## Text

**22:1** And David departed from there and escaped, and he came to the cave of Adullam; and his brothers and the house of his father heard, and they went down to him there.

**22:2** And everyone in distress and everyone in debt and everyone deeply grieved in soul gathered to him, and he was leader over them; and there were about four hundred men with him.

**22:3** And David departed from there to Mizpah of Moab, and he said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother be with you, until I know what God will do for me."

**22:4** And he appealed to the face of the king of Moab, and they lived with him all the days while David was in the stronghold.

**22:5** And Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold; go, and you will come into the land of Judah." And David went, and he came and stayed in the city of Sarik.

**22:6** And Saul heard that David and the men who were with him had become known. Now Saul was sitting on the hill below the field in Bama, and the spear was in his hand, and all his servants were standing beside him.

**22:7** And Saul said to his servants who were standing beside him, and he said to them, "Listen now, sons of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse truly give to all of you fields and vineyards, and will he appoint all of you commanders of hundreds and commanders of thousands?

**22:8** For all of you have conspired against me, and there is no one who uncovers my ear when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse, and there is no one among you who feels sorrow for me and uncovers my ear, that my son has stirred up my servant against me as an enemy, as it is this day."

**22:9** And Doeg the Syrian, who was set over the mules of Saul, answered and said, "I have seen the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Abimelech son of Ahitub the priest;

**22:10** and he inquired for him through God, and he gave him provisions, and he gave him the sword of Goliath the foreigner."

**22:11** And the king sent to summon Abimelech son of Ahitub and all the sons of his father, the priests who were in Nob; and they all came to the king.

**22:12** And Saul said, "Listen now, son of Ahitub." And he said, "Here I am; speak, lord."

**22:13** And Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, by giving him bread and a sword, and inquiring for him through God, to set him against me as an enemy, as it is this day?"

**22:14** And he answered the king and said, "And who among all your servants is as faithful as David, and son-in-law of the king, and commander of every order of yours, and honored in your house?

**22:15** Or have I begun today to inquire for him through God? By no means! Let not the king lay a charge against his servant or upon the whole house of his father, for your servant did not know a small or great matter in all these things."

**22:16** And King Saul said, "You will surely die, Abimelech, you and all the house of your father."

**22:17** And the king said to the runners who were standing by him, "Advance and put to death the priests of the Lord, because their hand is with David, and because they knew that he was fleeing and did not uncover my ear." But the servants of the king were not willing to put forth their hands to sin against the priests of the Lord.

**22:18** And the king said to Doeg, "You turn and fall upon the priests." And Doeg the Syrian turned and put to death the priests of the Lord on that day, three hundred and five men, all bearing an ephod.

**22:19** And Nob, the city of the priests, he struck by the edge of the sword, from man to woman, from infant to nursing child, and calf and donkey and sheep.

**22:20** And one son of Abimelech son of Ahitub escaped, and his name was Abiathar, and he fled after David.

**22:21** And Abiathar reported to David that Saul had put to death all the priests of the Lord.

**22:22** And David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day that Doeg the Syrian would surely report to Saul; I am responsible for the lives of the house of your father.

**22:23** Stay with me, do not be afraid; for wherever I seek a place for my life, I will also seek one for your life, for you are kept safe with me."

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — Greek reads "in the city of Sarik," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the forest of Hereth."
- **22:6** — Greek translates "Gibeah" as "the hill," renders "tamarisk tree" as "field," and reads "Bama" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Ramah."
- **22:8** — "Uncovers my ear" is a literal idiom for informing or disclosing something (also in v. 17); Greek also reads "as an enemy" here and in v. 13 (Hebrew MT: "to lie in ambush").
- **22:18** — Greek reads "three hundred and five men" (Hebrew MT: "eighty-five men") and omits "linen" before "ephod."
- **22:23** — Greek reads in the first person, "wherever I seek a place for my life, I will also seek one for your life," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "he who seeks my life seeks your life."
