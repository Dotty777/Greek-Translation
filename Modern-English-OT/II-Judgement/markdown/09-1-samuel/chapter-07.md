## Text

**7:1** And the men of Kiriath-jearim came and brought up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and they brought it into the house of Amminadab, the one on the hill; and they consecrated Eleazar his son to guard the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**7:2** And it came about from the day the ark was in Kiriath-jearim that the days multiplied, and they became twenty years; and all the house of Israel gazed after the Lord.

**7:3** And Samuel spoke to all the house of Israel, saying, "If with all your heart you are returning to the Lord, remove foreign gods from your midst, and the groves, and prepare your hearts toward the Lord and serve Him alone; and He will deliver you from the hand of the foreigners."

**7:4** And the sons of Israel removed the Baals and the groves of Ashtaroth, and they served the Lord alone.

**7:5** And Samuel said, "Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray for you to the Lord."

**7:6** And they assembled at Mizpah, and they drew water and poured it out before the Lord upon the ground; and they fasted on that day and said, "We have sinned before the Lord." And Samuel judged the sons of Israel at Mizpah.

**7:7** And the foreigners heard that all the sons of Israel had gathered together to Mizpah, and the satraps of the foreigners went up against Israel; and the sons of Israel heard, and they were afraid before the foreigners.

**7:8** And the sons of Israel said to Samuel, "Do not keep silent away from us so as not to cry out to the Lord your God, and He will save us from the hand of the foreigners."

**7:9** And Samuel took one nursing lamb and offered it up as a whole burnt offering with all the people to the Lord; and Samuel cried out to the Lord for Israel, and the Lord heard him.

**7:10** And Samuel was offering up the whole burnt offering, and the foreigners were drawing near for battle against Israel; and the Lord thundered with a great voice on that day against the foreigners, and they were thrown into confusion and were defeated before Israel.

**7:11** And the men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the foreigners, and they struck them as far as below Beth-car.

**7:12** And Samuel took one stone and set it up between Mizpah and the Old, and he called its name Ebenezer, Stone of the Helper, and said, "Up to here the Lord has helped us."

**7:13** And the Lord humbled the foreigners, and they did not come anymore into the territory of Israel; and the hand of the Lord was against the foreigners all the days of Samuel.

**7:14** And the cities which the foreigners had taken from the sons of Israel were restored, and they restored them to Israel from Ashkelon to Azob, and they took back the territory of Israel from the hand of the foreigners; and there was peace between Israel and the Amorite.

**7:15** And Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

**7:16** And he went year by year and made a circuit of Bethel and Gilgal and Mizpah, and he judged Israel in all these consecrated places.

**7:17** And his return was to Armathaim, for his house was there; and he judged Israel there, and he built an altar there to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Greek reads 'Amminadab' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Abinadab.'
- **7:9** — Greek reads 'with all the people' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'entirely' (a whole offering).
- **7:12** — Greek reads 'the Old' ('tes palaias', reflecting a Hebrew reading 'Jeshanah') where the Masoretic Text has 'Shen,' and gives both the transliteration 'Ebenezer' and its Greek gloss 'Stone of the Helper.'
- **7:14** — Greek reads 'from Ashkelon to Azob' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'from Ekron to Gath.'
- **7:16** — Greek reads 'in all these consecrated places' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads simply 'in all these places'; in v. 17 (and 8:4) Greek reads 'Ramathaim' where the Masoretic Text reads 'Ramah.'
