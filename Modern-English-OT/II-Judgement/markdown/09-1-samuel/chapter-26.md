## Text

**26:1** And the Ziphites came from the parched land to Saul at the hill, saying, "Look, David is hiding with us on the hill of Chelmath, which faces Jeshimon."

**26:2** And Saul arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, and with him three thousand chosen men from Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**26:3** And Saul encamped on the hill of Hachilah, facing Jeshimon by the road, and David stayed in the wilderness; and David saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness.

**26:4** And David sent spies, and he learned that Saul had come ready from Keilah.

**26:5** And David arose secretly and went into the place where Saul was sleeping, and Abner son of Ner, his commander-in-chief, was there; and Saul was sleeping in a covered wagon, and the people were encamped around him.

**26:6** And David answered and said to Abimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, saying, "Who will go in with me to Saul into the camp?" And Abishai said, "I will go in with you."

**26:7** And David and Abishai went in among the people by night, and look, Saul was fast asleep in a covered wagon, and his spear was driven into the ground beside his head, and Abner and his people were sleeping around him.

**26:8** And Abishai said to David, "Today the Lord has delivered up your enemy into your hands; and now let me strike him with the spear to the ground once, and I will not do it to him a second time."

**26:9** And David said to Abishai, "Do not humble him, for who will lay his hand on the Lord's anointed and be held guiltless?"

**26:10** And David said, "As the Lord lives, surely the Lord will discipline him, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go down into battle and be added;

**26:11** far be it from me before the Lord to lay my hand on the Lord's anointed. And now, please take the spear from beside his head and the flask of water, and let us go away by ourselves."

**26:12** And David took the spear and the flask of water from beside his head, and they went away by themselves; and there was no one who saw, and there was no one who knew, and there was no one who woke up; all were sleeping, because a stupor from the Lord had fallen upon them.

**26:13** And David crossed over to the other side and stood on the top of the mountain at a distance, and there was a long way between them.

**26:14** And David called out to the people and spoke to Abner, saying, "Will you not answer, Abner?" And he said, "Who are you who calls?"

**26:15** And David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? And why do you not guard your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy the king your lord.

**26:16** And this thing that you have done is not good. As the Lord lives, you are sons of death, you who guard the king your lord, the Lord's anointed. And now, see the king's spear and the flask of water: where are the things that were beside his head?"

**26:17** And Saul recognized the voice of David and said, "Is this your voice, child, David?" And David said, "Your servant, lord king."

**26:18** And he said, "Why is it that the lord pursues after his servant? For what have I sinned, and what wrongdoing has been found in me?

**26:19** And now let my lord the king hear the word of his servant: if God incites you against me, may He smell your sacrifice; and if it is sons of men, cursed are they before the Lord, for they have driven me out today from being established in the inheritance of the Lord, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

**26:20** And now may my blood not fall to the ground opposite the face of the Lord; for the king of Israel has come out to seek my life, just as the night raven pursues in the mountains."

**26:21** And Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, child David, for I will not harm you, because my life was precious in your eyes; and on this day I have acted foolishly and have erred very greatly."

**26:22** And David answered and said, "Here is the king's spear; let one of the young men cross over and take it.

**26:23** And the Lord will repay each one his righteous deeds and his faithfulness, for the Lord delivered you today into my hands, and I was not willing to lay my hand on the Lord's anointed.

**26:24** And look, just as your life was magnified on this very day in my eyes, so may my life be magnified before the Lord, and may He shelter me; and He will rescue me from all distress."

**26:25** And Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, child; you will both surely act and surely prevail." And David went away to his place, and Saul returned to his way.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Greek reads 'Chelmath' (compare 'Hachilah' in 26:3 and the Hebrew text) and adds 'from the parched land,' also rendering Hebrew 'Gibeah' literally as 'the hill.'
- **26:4** — The Greek reads 'ready from Keilah' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'for certain.'
- **26:6** — The Greek reads 'Abimelech' where the Hebrew text has 'Ahimelech.'
- **26:10** — The Greek uses a verb meaning 'discipline' where the Hebrew has 'strike,' and literally 'be added' (that is, gathered in death), reflecting a reading of Hebrew 'sapa' ('be swept away') as 'asap' ('gather/add').
- **26:16** — The Greek omits the negative particle, reading 'you who guard' where the Hebrew text has 'because you have not guarded.'
- **26:20** — The Greek reads 'to seek my life, just as the night raven pursues in the mountains' where the Hebrew has 'to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains.'
