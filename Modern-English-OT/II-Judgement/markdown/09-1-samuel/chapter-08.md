## Text

**8:1** And it came about when Samuel grew old that he appointed his sons as judges for Israel.

**8:2** And these were the names of his sons: the firstborn was Joel, and the name of the second was Abijah, judges in Beersheba.

**8:3** And his sons did not walk in his way, and they turned aside after profit, and they took gifts, and they turned aside judgments.

**8:4** And the men of Israel gathered together and came to Armathaim to Samuel,

**8:5** and they said to him, "Look, you have grown old, and your sons do not walk in your way; and now appoint over us a king to judge us, just like the rest of the nations."

**8:6** And the word was evil in the eyes of Samuel when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." And Samuel prayed to the Lord.

**8:7** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Listen to the voice of the people according to whatever they speak to you; for they have not despised you, but rather they have despised Me, so that I should not reign over them.

**8:8** According to all the deeds which they have done to Me from the day that I brought them up out of Egypt until this day, and they forsook Me and served other gods, so they are doing to you also.

**8:9** And now listen to their voice; only, you shall solemnly warn them, and you shall declare to them the right of the king who will reign over them."

**8:10** And Samuel spoke every word of the Lord to the people who were asking for a king from him,

**8:11** and he said, "This will be the right of the king who will reign over you: he will take your sons and place them with his chariots and his horsemen, and as runners ahead of his chariots,

**8:12** and to appoint them for himself as commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and to reap his harvest and to gather his vintage, and to make his implements of war and the implements of his chariots.

**8:13** And he will take your daughters as perfumers and as cooks and as bakers.

**8:14** And he will take your fields and your vineyards and your olive groves, the good ones, and he will give them to his servants.

**8:15** And he will tithe your seeds and your vineyards, and he will give to his eunuchs and to his servants.

**8:16** And he will take your male servants and your female servants and your herds of cattle and the good things and your donkeys, and he will tithe them for his works,

**8:17** and he will tithe your flocks; and you yourselves will be servants to him.

**8:18** And you will cry out in that day because of your king whom you chose for yourselves; and the Lord will not hear you in those days, because you yourselves chose a king for yourselves."

**8:19** And the people were not willing to listen to Samuel, and they said to him, "No, but rather a king shall be over us,

**8:20** and we too will be like all the nations; and our king will judge us, and he will go out before us, and he will fight our battle."

**8:21** And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and he spoke them into the ears of the Lord.

**8:22** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Listen to their voice and enthrone a king for them." And Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Let each one go off to his city."

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — Greek 'synteleia' can mean 'completion' or, in Hellenistic administrative usage, 'contribution/profit,' corresponding here to Hebrew 'dishonest gain.'
- **8:12** — Greek reads 'commanders of hundreds' (where the Masoretic Text has 'fifties') and 'reap his harvest and gather his vintage' (where the Masoretic Text has 'plow his ground and reap his harvest').
- **8:16** — Greek reads 'your herds of cattle and the good things' (where the Masoretic Text has 'your best young men') and 'he will tithe them for his works' (where the Masoretic Text has 'put them to his work').
- **8:18** — The closing clause 'because you yourselves chose a king for yourselves' and the plural 'in those days' are Septuagint readings not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
