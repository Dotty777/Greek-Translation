## Text

**2:1** "My heart has been made firm in the Lord, my horn has been exalted in my God; my mouth has opened wide against enemies, I have rejoiced in Your salvation.

**2:2** For there is no one holy like the Lord, and there is no one righteous like our God; there is no one holy besides You.

**2:3** Do not boast and do not speak lofty things; let no arrogant speech come out of your mouth, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and a God who prepares His deeds.

**2:4** The bow of the mighty has grown weak, and the weak have girded themselves with strength.

**2:5** Those full of bread have been diminished, and the weak have bypassed the land. For a barren woman has given birth to seven, and the one abundant in children has grown weak.

**2:6** The Lord puts to death and brings to life; He brings down to Hades and brings up.

**2:7** The Lord makes poor and makes rich; He humbles and lifts high.

**2:8** He raises the needy from the earth and lifts up the poor from the dung heap, to seat them with rulers of peoples, assigning to them a throne of glory as an inheritance,

**2:9** granting a vow to the one who vows; and He has blessed the years of the righteous. For a man is not mighty by strength:

**2:10** the Lord will make His adversary weak; the Lord is holy. Let not the prudent man boast in his prudence, and let not the mighty man boast in his might, and let not the rich man boast in his riches; but rather let him who boasts boast in this: to understand and to know the Lord, and to practice justice and righteousness in the midst of the earth. The Lord went up into the heavens and thundered; He Himself will judge the ends of the earth. And He gives strength to our kings, and He will exalt the horn of His anointed one."

**2:11** And she left him there before the Lord and went away to Armathaim; and the boy was ministering in the presence of the Lord before Eli the priest.

**2:12** Now the sons of Eli the priest were wicked sons, not knowing the Lord

**2:13** or the due of the priest from all the people who sacrificed. And the priest's young attendant would come when the meat was boiled, with a three-pronged meat-fork in his hand,

**2:14** and he would thrust it into the large cauldron, or into the bronze kettle, or into the pot; everything that came up on the meat-fork the priest would take for himself. In this manner they used to do to all Israel, to those who came to sacrifice to the Lord in Shiloh.

**2:15** And before the fat was offered up in smoke, the priest's young attendant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give meat to roast for the priest, and I will by no means take boiled meat from you out of the cauldron."

**2:16** And the man who was sacrificing would say, "Let the fat first be offered up in smoke as is proper, and take for yourself from all that your soul desires." And he said, "No, for you will give it now; and if not, I will take it by force."

**2:17** And the sin of the young men was exceedingly great before the Lord, because they treated the sacrifice of the Lord with contempt.

**2:18** And Samuel was ministering before the Lord, a boy girded with an ephod bar.

**2:19** And his mother made a little cloak for him, and she would bring it up to him from days to days when she went up with her husband to offer the sacrifice of the days.

**2:20** And Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, saying, "May the Lord repay you with seed from this woman in place of the loan that you lent to the Lord." And the man went away to his place.

**2:21** And the Lord visited Hannah, and she gave birth to three more sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel grew up before the Lord.

**2:22** Now Eli was a very old man, and he heard what his sons were doing to the sons of Israel.

**2:23** And he said to them, "Why are you acting according to this thing that I hear from the mouth of all the people of the Lord?

**2:24** No, children, for the report that I hear is not good; do not act this way, for the reports that I hear are not good, so that the people do not serve God.

**2:25** If a man indeed sins against a man, then they will pray for him to the Lord; but if he sins against the Lord, who will pray for him?" Yet they did not listen to the voice of their father, for the Lord surely desired to destroy them.

**2:26** And the boy Samuel went on, and was good both with the Lord and with people.

**2:27** And a man of God came to Eli and said, "Thus says the Lord: I indeed revealed Myself to the house of your father, when they were in the land of Egypt as servants to the house of Pharaoh;

**2:28** and I chose the house of your father out of all the tribes of Israel to serve as priests to Me, and to go up to My altar and to burn incense and to bear an ephod; and I gave to the house of your father all the fire offerings of the sons of Israel for food.

**2:29** And why have you looked upon My incense and at My sacrifice with a shameless eye? And have you honored your sons above Me, to be blessed from the firstfruits of every sacrifice of Israel before Me?

**2:30** Therefore thus has the Lord, the God of Israel, said: I said, 'Your house and the house of your father will walk before Me forever.' Yet now, says the Lord, far be it from Me! For on the contrary, those who honor Me I will honor, and the one who despises Me will be dishonored.

**2:31** Look, days are coming when I will cut off your seed and the seed of the house of your father,

**2:32** and there will not be an old man of yours in My house all the days.

**2:33** Yet I will not cut off a man from My altar, so that his eyes fail and his soul wastes away; and every survivor of your house will fall by the sword of men.

**2:34** And this will be the sign to you that will come upon these two sons of yours, Hophni and Phinehas: in one day both of them will die.

**2:35** And I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who will do everything that is in My heart and in My soul; and I will build for him a faithful house, and he will walk before My anointed one all the days.

**2:36** And it will come about that the survivor in the house will come to bow down to him for an obol of silver, saying, 'Cast me into one of your priestly offices to eat bread.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Greek reads 'the weak have bypassed the land' (vs. Hebrew 'the hungry have ceased'), continuing the distinctive Septuagint readings in Hannah's song (see also vv. 2-3 and 8-9).
- **2:10** — The Greek includes a substantial expansion paralleling Jeremiah 9:23-24 ('Let not the prudent man boast...') and concludes with plural 'our kings' (vs. Hebrew 'His king').
- **2:18** — The Greek text reads 'ephod bar,' an untranslated manuscript variant of 'bad' (transliterating the Hebrew word for 'linen'), referring to a linen ephod.
- **2:22** — Like the Dead Sea Scrolls manuscript 4QSam-a, the Greek omits the clause found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text about Eli's sons lying with the women serving at the entrance to the tent of meeting.
- **2:31** — The Greek reads 'your seed' (sperma) rather than the Masoretic Text's 'your arm,' and in v. 32 omits the first half of the Hebrew verse while reading 'in My house.'
