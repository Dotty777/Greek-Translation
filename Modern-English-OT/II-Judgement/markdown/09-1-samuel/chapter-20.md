## Text

**20:1** And David fled from Auath in Ramah, and came before Jonathan and said, "What have I done? And what is my wrongdoing, and what have I sinned before your father, that he is seeking my life?"

**20:2** And Jonathan said to him, "Far be it from you! You shall certainly not die. Look, my father will certainly not do a small thing and not uncover my ear; and why would my father hide this matter? This is not so."

**20:3** And David answered Jonathan and said, "Your father knows very well that I have found favor in your eyes, and he said that Jonathan should not know this, so that he might not be unwilling. But as the Lord lives and as your soul lives, just as he said, it has been filled up between me and death."

**20:4** And Jonathan said to David, "What does your soul desire, and what shall I do for you?"

**20:5** And David said to Jonathan, "Look now, tomorrow is the new moon, and I will certainly not sit down to eat; and you shall send me away, and I will hide myself in the plain until evening.

**20:6** If your father indeed misses me, then you shall say, 'David earnestly asked leave of me to run all the way to Bethlehem his city, because a sacrifice of the days is there for the whole tribe.'

**20:7** If he says thus, 'Well,' there is peace for your servant; and if he answers you harshly, know that the evil has been determined by him.

**20:8** And you shall show mercy to your servant, because you have brought your servant into a covenant of the Lord with yourself. And if there is wrongdoing in your servant, put me to death yourself; and why do you bring me to your father like this?"

**20:9** And Jonathan said, "Far be it from you! For if I certainly know that the evil has been determined by my father to come upon you, and if it is not to your cities, I will report to you."

**20:10** And David said to Jonathan, "Who will report to me if your father answers harshly?"

**20:11** And Jonathan said to David, "Go and stay in the field." And both of them went out into the field.

**20:12** And Jonathan said to David, "The Lord God of Israel knows that I will examine my father about the time on the third day, and look, should there be good concerning David, and I do not send to you into the field,

**20:13** may God do thus to Jonathan and add thus; for I will report the evils against you and uncover your ear, and I will send you away, and you shall go in peace, and the Lord will be with you just as He was with my father.

**20:14** And indeed, while I am still alive, you shall show mercy to me; and if I certainly die,

**20:15** you shall not remove your mercy from my house forever. And if not, when the Lord removes the enemies of David, each one from the face of the earth,

**20:16** let the name of Jonathan be found from the house of David; and may the Lord seek out the enemies of David."

**20:17** And Jonathan swore yet again to David, because he loved the soul of the one who loved him.

**20:18** And Jonathan said, "Tomorrow is the new moon, and you will be missed, because your seat will be noticed.

**20:19** And you shall stay until the third day and watch, and you shall come to your place where you may hide on the working day, and you shall sit beside that ergab.

**20:20** And I will shoot three times with the arrows, sending them out toward Armattarei.

**20:21** And look, I am sending the boy, saying, 'Come, find the gouza for me.' If I say to the boy, 'Here is the arrow, on this side of you; take it,' come; for there is peace for you and there is no word of the Lord, as the Lord lives.

**20:22** If I say thus to the young man, 'Here is the arrow, beyond you,' go, because the Lord has sent you away.

**20:23** And as for the word that I and you have spoken, look, the Lord is a witness between me and you forever."

**20:24** And David hid himself in the field, and the new moon came, and the king came to the table to eat.

**20:25** And he sat on his seat as once and again, on the seat by the wall, and he preceded Jonathan, and Abner sat at Saul's side, and David's place was noticed.

**20:26** And Saul did not speak on that day, because he said, "It is a mishap; he appears not to be clean, because he has not been cleansed."

**20:27** And it happened on the day after the new moon, on the second day, that the place of David was noticed. And Saul said to Jonathan his son, "Why is it that the son of Jesse has not come either yesterday or today to the table?"

**20:28** And Jonathan answered Saul and said to him, "David asked leave of me to go all the way to Bethlehem his city,

**20:29** and he said, 'Please send me away, because we have a sacrifice of the tribe in the city, and my brothers commanded me. And now, if I have found favor in your eyes, let me please slip away and see my brothers.' Therefore he has not come to the table of the king."

**20:30** And Saul was greatly furious with anger against Jonathan and said to him, "Son of deserting girls! For do I not know that you are a partner with the son of Jesse to your own shame and to the shame of your mother's uncovering?

**20:31** For all the days that the son of Jesse lives on the earth, your kingdom will not be established. Now therefore send and take the young man, for this one is a son of death."

**20:32** And Jonathan answered Saul, "Why is he to die? What has he done?"

**20:33** And Saul lifted up the spear against Jonathan to put him to death, and Jonathan knew that this evil had been determined by his father, to put David to death.

**20:34** And Jonathan leapt up from the table in furious anger, and he did not eat bread on the second day of the month, because his father had determined against him.

**20:35** And it happened in the morning that Jonathan went out into the field, just as he had arranged for the testimony of David, and a small boy was with him.

**20:36** And he said to the boy, "Run, find for me the arrows with which I am shooting." And the boy ran, and he shot the arrow and made it pass beyond.

**20:37** And the boy came as far as the place of the arrow where Jonathan had shot; and Jonathan called out after the young man and said, "There is the arrow, beyond you."

**20:38** And Jonathan called out after his boy, saying, "Hurry quickly, and do not stand still." And Jonathan's boy gathered up the arrows to his master.

**20:39** And the boy knew nothing, except Jonathan and David.

**20:40** And Jonathan gave his weapons to his boy, and said to his boy, "Go, enter into the city."

**20:41** And when the boy entered, David arose from the argab and fell on his face and bowed down to him three times; and each kissed his companion, and each wept with his companion to a great completion.

**20:42** And Jonathan said, "Go in peace, and as both of us have sworn in the name of the Lord, saying, 'The Lord will be a witness between me and you, and among your seed forever.'"

**20:43** *(after `20:42`)* And David arose and departed, and Jonathan went into the city.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — Greek reads 'just as he said, it has been filled up between me and death,' differing from the Masoretic Text ('there is but a step between me and death').
- **20:9** — Greek reads 'and if it is not to your cities, I will report to you,' diverging from the Masoretic Text's rhetorical question ('would I not tell you?').
- **20:16** — Greek reads 'let the name of Jonathan be found from the house of David,' where the Masoretic Text reads 'so Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David.'
- **20:19** — Greek reads 'on the working day' and transliterates 'ergab' (corresponding to 'argab' in v. 41, a term for a stone mound or cairn), where the Masoretic Text reads 'on the day of the deed' and 'the stone Ezel.'
- **20:20** — Armattarei transliterates the Hebrew phrase for 'the target,' and Greek schizais (literally 'wooden shafts' or 'splinters') is used throughout this chapter for arrows.
- **20:21** — Greek gouzan is an obscure textual form or loanword referring to the arrow (called schiza in the next clause), and 'word of the Lord' reflects the Greek's extra 'of the Lord' before 'as the Lord lives.'
