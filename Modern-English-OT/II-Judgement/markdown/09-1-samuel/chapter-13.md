## Text

**13:2** And Saul chose for himself three thousand men from the men of Israel; and two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel; one thousand were with Jonathan in Geba of Benjamin; and the remainder of the people he sent away, each one to his tent.

**13:3** And Jonathan struck Nasib the foreigner who was on the hill, and the foreigners heard; and Saul blew a trumpet throughout all the land, saying, "The slaves have rebelled."

**13:4** And all Israel heard people saying, "Saul has struck Nasib the foreigner," and Israel was put to shame among the foreigners; and the sons of Israel went up after Saul at Gilgal.

**13:5** And the foreigners gathered for war against Israel, and thirty thousand chariots and six thousand horsemen and people like the sand beside the sea in multitude came up against Israel; and they came up and encamped at Michmash, opposite Beth-horon to the rear.

**13:6** And the men of Israel saw that they were hard pressed so that they could not advance, and the people hid in the caves and in the sheepfolds and in the rocks and in the pits and in the cisterns.

**13:7** And those crossing over crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead; and Saul was still in Gilgal, and all the people were terrified behind him.

**13:8** And he waited seven days for the testimony, as Samuel had said; and Samuel did not arrive at Gilgal, and his people were scattered from him.

**13:9** And Saul said, "Draw near so that I may offer a burnt offering and peace offerings." And he offered up the burnt offering.

**13:10** And it happened as he finished offering up the burnt offering, that Samuel arrived; and Saul went out to meet him to bless him.

**13:11** And Samuel said, "What have you done?" And Saul said, "Because I saw that the people were scattered from me, and you did not arrive as you directed at the testimony of the days, and the foreigners were gathered at Michmash,

**13:12** and I said, 'Now the foreigners will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not implored the face of the Lord.' And I forced myself and offered up the burnt offering."

**13:13** And Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly, because you have not kept my command which the Lord commanded you; for now the Lord would have established your kingdom forever over Israel.

**13:14** And now your kingdom will not stand; and the Lord will seek for Himself a man according to His heart, and the Lord will command him to be ruler over His people, because you have not kept what the Lord commanded you."

**13:15** And Samuel rose and went away from Gilgal on his way; and the remainder of the people went up after Saul to the encounter, following the fighting people. When they arrived from Gilgal at Gibeah of Benjamin, Saul mustered the people who were found with him, about six hundred men.

**13:16** And Saul and Jonathan his son and the people and those who were found with them sat in Geba of Benjamin and wept, while the foreigners had encamped at Michmash.

**13:17** And a destroyer went out from the field of foreigners in three companies: one company facing the road of Ophrah toward Shual,

**13:18** and one company facing the road of Beth-horon, and one company facing the road of Geba that looks down toward Gai of Samin.

**13:19** And no worker of iron was found in all the land of Israel, because the foreigners said, "Let the Hebrews not make a sword and a spear."

**13:20** And all Israel went down to the land of foreigners to forge each one his reaping hook and the implement, and each one his axe and his sickle.

**13:21** And the vintage was ready for reaping; and the implements were three shekels per tooth, and for the axe and for the sickle the setting was the same.

**13:22** And it came about in the days of the battle of Michmash, that a sword and a spear were not found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and with Jonathan, yet they were found with Saul and with Jonathan his son.

**13:23** And he went out from the outpost of the foreigners, the one on the other side of Michmash; and he did not tell his father.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — Greek treats Hebrew netsiv ("garrison" or "prefect") as a proper name, "Nasib," translates Geba as "the hill," and reads "The slaves have rebelled" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Let the Hebrews hear." The Greek text omits verse 1, the difficult chronological formula in the Hebrew.
- **13:5** — Greek reads the distinct place name "Beth-horon to the rear," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "east of Beth-aven."
- **13:15** — The Septuagint preserves a longer text here ("on his way; and the remainder of the people... arrived from Gilgal") that is absent from the shorter Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:18** — Greek reads "Geba" where the Hebrew has "the border," transliterates Hebrew gei ("valley") as the proper name "Gai," and has the distinct name "Samin" where the Hebrew reads "Zeboim."
- **13:21** — This difficult verse diverges sharply from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (which refers to a pim weight): Greek speaks of "the vintage... ready for reaping," "three shekels per tooth," and the same "setting" (hypostasis).
- **13:23** — Greek reads "from the outpost" (ex hypostaseōs), apparently taking the initial letter of Hebrew matsav ("garrison") as the preposition "from," and includes "and he did not tell his father" here rather than in 14:1.
