## Text

**14:1** And a day came, and Jonathan son of Saul said to the young man carrying his armor, "Come, and let us cross over to Messab of the foreigners, the one on that other side." And he did not tell his father.

**14:2** And Saul was sitting on the edge of the hill under the pomegranate tree that was from Magon, and about six hundred men were with him.

**14:3** And Ahijah son of Ahitub, brother of Jochabel son of Phinehas son of Levi, priest of God in Shiloh, was carrying an ephod; and the people did not know that Jonathan had gone.

**14:4** And in the middle of the pass where Jonathan was seeking to cross over to the outpost of the foreigners, there was a crag of rock on one side and a crag of rock on the other side, and a tooth of rock on this side; the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other was Sennaar.

**14:5** The one way was from the north as one comes to Michmash, and the other way was from the south as one comes to Geba.

**14:6** And Jonathan said to the young man carrying his armor, "Come, let us cross over to Messaph of these uncircumcised ones, if perhaps the Lord might do something for us; for there is no hindrance for the Lord to save by many or by few."

**14:7** And the one carrying his armor said to him, "Do all that your heart inclines to; look, I am with you; as your heart is, so is my heart."

**14:8** And Jonathan said, "Look, we are crossing over to the men and will roll down to them.

**14:9** If they say this to us, 'Stand back there until we tell you,' then we will stand in our own place and will by no means go up against them.

**14:10** And if they say this to us, 'Come up to us,' then we will go up, because the Lord has delivered them into our hands; this will be the sign for us."

**14:11** And both of them entered into Messaph of the foreigners. And the foreigners said, "Look, the Hebrews are coming out of their holes where they hid themselves."

**14:12** And the men of Messaph answered Jonathan and the one carrying his armor and said, "Come up to us, and we will make a matter known to you." And Jonathan said to the one carrying his armor, "Go up behind me, for the Lord has delivered them into the hands of Israel."

**14:13** And Jonathan went up on his hands and on his feet, and the one carrying his armor was with him; and they looked toward the face of Jonathan, and he struck them, and the one carrying his armor was pressing on behind him.

**14:14** And the first blow that Jonathan and the one carrying his armor struck was about twenty men, with darts and pebbles of the field.

**14:15** And there came to be panic in the camp and in the field, and all the people, those in Messaph and the destroyers, were panic-stricken, and they themselves were unwilling to act; and the earth was terrified, and there came to be panic from the Lord.

**14:16** And the watchmen of Saul in Geba of Benjamin looked, and see, the camp was thrown into turmoil on this side and on that.

**14:17** And Saul said to the people who were with him, "Count now and see who has gone from among you." And they counted, and look, Jonathan and the one carrying his armor were not found.

**14:18** And Saul said to Ahijah, "Bring near the ephod," for he carried the ephod on that day before Israel.

**14:19** And it happened while Saul was speaking to the priest that the noise in the camp of the foreigners kept going on continually and increasing. And Saul said to the priest, "Withdraw your hands."

**14:20** And Saul and all the people who were with him went up and came as far as the battle; and look, each man's sword was against his neighbor, a very great confusion.

**14:21** And the slaves who had been with the foreigners yesterday and the day before, who had gone up into the camp, they also turned around to be with Israel, those who were with Saul and Jonathan.

**14:22** And all Israel who were hiding in Mount Ephraim also heard that foreigners had fled, and they also joined in behind them into battle.

**14:23** And the Lord saved Israel on that day; and the battle passed through Bamoth, and all the people with Saul were about ten thousand men, and the battle was spread out to every city in Mount Ephraim.

**14:24** And Saul acted in great ignorance on that day, and he laid a curse on the people, saying, "Cursed is the man who eats bread until evening, and I will take vengeance on my enemy." And none of the people tasted bread.

**14:25** And all the land was dining. And Jaal was a forest of an apiary across the face of the field.

**14:26** And the people entered into the apiary, and look, they went along speaking; and look, there was no one bringing his hand back to his mouth, because the people feared the oath of the Lord.

**14:27** And Jonathan had not heard when his father put the people under oath; and he stretched out the tip of his staff that was in his hand and dipped it into the comb of honey, and brought his hand back to his mouth, and his eyes saw again.

**14:28** And one of the people answered and said, "Your father strictly put the people under oath, saying, 'Cursed is the man who eats bread today.'" And the people became faint.

**14:29** And Jonathan knew and said, "My father has done away with the land. See how my eyes have seen, because I tasted a little of this honey.

**14:30** Rather, if the people had freely eaten today from the spoils of their enemies which they found, then now the blow among the foreigners would have been greater."

**14:31** And on that day he struck some of the foreigners in Michmash, and the people grew very weary.

**14:32** And the people turned aside to the spoils; and the people took flocks and herds and young of cattle, and slaughtered them on the ground; and the people were eating with the blood.

**14:33** And it was reported to Saul, saying, "The people have sinned against the Lord by eating with the blood." And Saul said, "In Gittaim, roll a large stone here to me."

**14:34** And Saul said, "Disperse among the people and tell them each to bring here his calf and each his own sheep, and let him slaughter it on this, and you will by no means sin against the Lord by eating with the blood." And all the people brought, each one what was in his hand, and they slaughtered them there.

**14:35** And Saul built an altar to the Lord there; this Saul began to build as an altar to the Lord.

**14:36** And Saul said, "Let us go down after the foreigners during the night and plunder among them until day dawns, and let us not leave a man among them." And they said, "Do all that is good before you." And the priest said, "Let us draw near here to God."

**14:37** And Saul inquired of God, "Shall I go down after the foreigners? Will You deliver them into the hands of Israel?" And He did not answer him on that day.

**14:38** And Saul said, "Bring near here all the corners of Israel, and know and see in whom this sin has occurred today.

**14:39** For as the Lord lives who saved Israel, even if He answers against Jonathan my son, he will surely die." And there was no one answering from all the people.

**14:40** And he said to all Israel, "You will be for bondage, and I and Jonathan my son will be for bondage." And the people said to Saul, "Do what is good before you."

**14:41** And Saul said, "Lord God of Israel, why is it that You have not answered Your servant today? Is the wrongdoing in me or in Jonathan my son? Lord God of Israel, give clear signs; and if it says this, give now to Your people Israel, give now holiness." And Jonathan and Saul were taken by lot, and the people went out.

**14:42** And Saul said, "Cast lots between me and Jonathan my son; the one whom the Lord takes by lot, let him die." And the people said to Saul, "This thing is not to be." And Saul prevailed over the people, and they cast lots between him and Jonathan his son, and Jonathan was taken by lot.

**14:43** And Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you have done." And Jonathan told him and said, "I indeed tasted a little honey with the tip of the staff that was in my hand; look, I am to die."

**14:44** And Saul said to him, "May God do this to me and add this as well, for you will surely die today."

**14:45** And the people said to Saul, "Shall the one who accomplished this great salvation in Israel be put to death today? As the Lord lives, not a hair of his head will fall to the ground, for the people of God acted this day." And the people prayed for Jonathan on that day, and he did not die.

**14:46** And Saul went up from behind the foreigners, and the foreigners departed to their own place.

**14:47** And Saul obtained by lot to reign; he was allotted the work over Israel. And he fought against all his enemies all around, against Moab and against the sons of Ammon and against the sons of Edom and against Betheor and against the king of Zobah and against the foreigners; wherever he turned, he was saved.

**14:48** And he displayed strength, and struck Amalek, and rescued Israel from the hand of those who trampled him.

**14:49** And the sons of Saul were Jonathan and Jessiul and Malchishua; and the names of his two daughters: the name of the firstborn was Merab, and the name of the second was Michal.

**14:50** And the name of his wife was Ahinoam, daughter of Ahimaaz; and the name of the commander of the army was Abner, son of Ner, son of a relative of Saul.

**14:51** And Kish was the father of Saul, and Ner the father of Abner was the son of Jamin, son of Abier.

**14:52** And the war against the foreigners was fierce all the days of Saul; and when Saul saw any mighty man and any man who was a son of might, he gathered them to himself.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Messab (spelled Messaph in vv. 6, 11, 12, and 15) transliterates Hebrew matsav ("garrison" or "outpost"). Here and throughout the chapter, "foreigners" translates Greek allophyloi, the Septuagint's standard designation for the Philistines in 1–2 Kingdoms.
- **14:2** — Greek reads "the hill" (where the Masoretic Text has the place name Gibeah) and "Magon" (where the Masoretic Text has Migron).
- **14:3** — Greek reads "Jochabel" and "Levi" where the Masoretic Text has "Ichabod" and "Eli".
- **14:4** — Greek reads "Sennaar" (where the Masoretic Text has Seneh) and preserves a textual doublet describing both "a crag of rock" and "a tooth of rock".
- **14:18** — Greek reads "the ephod" twice where the Masoretic Text reads "the ark of God".
- **14:23** — Greek reads "Bamoth" (where the Masoretic Text has Beth-aven) and includes an expansion describing ten thousand men with Saul and fighting throughout every city of Mount Ephraim.
- **14:25** — Jaal transliterates Hebrew ya'ar ("forest") alongside its Greek translation "forest". Greek also reads "all the land was dining" (and in v. 26 "went along speaking" where Hebrew refers to flowing honey).
- **14:33** — Greek reads the place name "In Gittaim" (En Geththaim) where the Masoretic Text reads "You have acted treacherously".
- **14:41** — Greek preserves a longer prayer of Saul in which "clear signs" (delous) and "holiness" (hosioteta) represent the Urim and Thummim.
- **14:47** — Greek includes the distinct name "Betheor" (Baitheor, absent from the Masoretic Text) and opens with a double clause regarding Saul's allotment to reign and his allotted work.
- **14:48** — The Greek text adds the untranslatable form aunanein after dynamin ("strength"), widely recognized as an ancient scribal duplication of dynamin.
- **14:49** — Greek reads "Jessiul" where the Masoretic Text has "Ishvi".
- **14:51** — Greek reads "son of Jamin, son of Abier" where the Masoretic Text has only "son of Abiel".
