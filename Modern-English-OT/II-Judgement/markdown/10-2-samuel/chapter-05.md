## Text

**5:1** And all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said to him, "Look, we are your bones and your flesh.

**5:2** Both yesterday and the day before, while Saul was king over us, you were the one leading out and bringing in Israel; and the Lord said to you, 'You will shepherd My people Israel, and you will be leader over Israel.'"

**5:3** And all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the Lord, and they anointed David as king over all Israel.

**5:4** David was thirty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for forty years.

**5:5** For seven years and six months he reigned in Hebron over Judah, and for thirty-three years he reigned over all Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

**5:6** And David and his men departed for Jerusalem, to the Jebusite who inhabited the land; and it was said to David, "You will not enter here," because the blind and the lame stood in opposition, saying, "David will not enter here."

**5:7** And David captured the stronghold of Zion; this is the city of David.

**5:8** And David said on that day, "Everyone who strikes a Jebusite, let him attack with a dagger both the lame and the blind and those who hate the soul of David." Therefore they will say, "The blind and the lame will not enter the house of the Lord."

**5:9** And David settled in the stronghold, and it was called the city of David; and he built it as a city all around from the citadel, and his own house.

**5:10** And David went on advancing and growing great, and the Lord Almighty was with him.

**5:11** And Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and cedar timbers, and craftsmen of wood and craftsmen of stone, and they built a house for David.

**5:12** And David knew that the Lord had prepared him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom had been exalted for the sake of His people Israel.

**5:13** And David took more wives and concubines from Jerusalem after he came to Hebron; and more sons and daughters were born to David.

**5:14** And these are the names of those born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon,

**5:15** and Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia,

**5:16** and Elishama, Epidae, and Eliphaath; Samae, Jessibath, Nathan, Galamaan, Jebaar, Theesus, Elphalat, Naged, Nepheg, Janatha, Leasamys, Baalimath, Eliphaath.

**5:17** And the foreigners heard that David had been anointed king over Israel, and all the foreigners went up to seek David; and David heard and went down to the stronghold.

**5:18** And the foreigners came and converged on the Valley of the Titans.

**5:19** And David inquired through the Lord, saying, "Should I go up against the foreigners, and will You deliver them into my hands?" And the Lord said to David, "Go up, for I will certainly deliver the foreigners into your hands."

**5:20** And David came from the Upper Breaches and struck the foreigners there, and David said, "The Lord has broken through the hostile foreigners before me as waters break through." Therefore the name of that place was called Upper Breaches.

**5:21** And they left their gods there, and David and the men who were with him took them.

**5:22** And the foreigners went up yet again and converged in the Valley of the Titans.

**5:23** And David inquired through the Lord, and the Lord said, "You must not go up to meet them; turn away from them, and you will come upon them near the place of weeping.

**5:24** And it will be, when you hear the sound of the closing in from the grove of weeping, then you will go down against them, for then the Lord will go out before you to strike in the battle of the foreigners."

**5:25** And David did just as the Lord commanded him, and he struck down the foreigners from Gibeon as far as the land of Gezer.

## Translator Notes

- **5:8** — The Greek reads "with a dagger" (en paraxiphidi) and explicitly adds "of the Lord" to "the house" (cf. also 5:6, where the blind and the lame "stood in opposition"), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "water shaft" and shorter saying.
- **5:13** — The Greek text reads "after he came to Hebron" (eis Chebron), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from Hebron."
- **5:16** — Alongside the distinct Septuagint spellings Epidae (MT: Eliada) and Eliphaath (MT: Eliphelet), the Greek appends a thirteen-name duplicate list (Samae through the second Eliphaath) reflecting a parallel tradition related to 1 Chronicles 3:5-8 and 14:4-7.
- **5:17** — Greek allophyloi ("foreigners") is the standard Septuagint designation for the Philistines in Judges and Kingdoms; in 5:18 and 5:22, "Valley of the Titans" preserves the distinct Septuagint proper name (MT: Valley of Rephaim).
- **5:20** — The Greek translates the place name as "Upper Breaches" and reads "from the Upper Breaches" (MT: "to Baal-perazim"). Similarly, in 5:23-24 the Greek reads "place/grove of weeping" and "the closing in" (MT: "balsam trees" and "marching"), and in 5:25 "Gibeon" (MT: "Geba").
