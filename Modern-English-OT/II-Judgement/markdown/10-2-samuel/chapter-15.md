## Text

**15:1** And it happened after these things that Absalom made for himself chariots and horses, and fifty men to run before him.

**15:2** And Absalom rose early in the morning and stood beside the way of the gate; and it happened that every man who had a case came to the king for judgment, and Absalom called out to him and would say to him, "From what city are you?" And he said, "Your servant is from one of the tribes of Israel."

**15:3** And Absalom said to him, "Look, your words are good and straightforward, and there is no one from the king listening to you."

**15:4** And Absalom said, "Who will appoint me judge in the land, so that every man who has a dispute and a case will come to me, and I will give him justice?"

**15:5** And it happened when a man came near to bow down to him, that he stretched out his hand and took hold of him and kissed him.

**15:6** And Absalom did according to this matter to all Israel who came to the king for judgment, and Absalom made the heart of the men of Israel his own.

**15:7** And it happened at the end of forty years that Absalom said to his father, "Please let me go and pay my vows which I vowed to the Lord in Hebron;

**15:8** for your servant vowed a vow while I was living in Geshur in Syria, saying, 'If the Lord indeed brings me back to Jerusalem, then I will serve the Lord.'"

**15:9** And the king said to him, "Go in peace." And he arose and went to Hebron.

**15:10** And Absalom sent spies among all the tribes of Israel, saying, "When you hear the sound of the horn, then you shall say, 'King Absalom is reigning in Hebron!'"

**15:11** And with Absalom went two hundred men from Jerusalem who were invited; and they went in their innocence and did not know anything.

**15:12** And Absalom sent to Ahithophel the Tekonite, the counselor of David, in his city, to Gola, while he was offering sacrifices. And there was a severe shattering, and the people going along were also numerous with Absalom.

**15:13** And the messenger arrived to David, saying, "The heart of the men of Israel has gone after Absalom."

**15:14** And David said to all his servants who were with him in Jerusalem, "Arise and let us flee, for there is no deliverance for us from the face of Absalom; hurry to go, so that he may not hurry and overtake us, and thrust evil upon us and strike the city with the edge of the sword."

**15:15** And the servants of the king said to the king, "According to everything that our lord the king chooses, look, here are your servants."

**15:16** And the king went out, and all his household on foot; and the king left ten women of his concubines to guard the house.

**15:17** And the king went out, and all his servants on foot, and they stood at the Far House.

**15:18** And all his servants were passing by beside him, and all the Chettites and all the Pelethites, and they stood at the olive tree in the wilderness; and all the people were passing by next to him, and all those around him and all the prominent men and all the warriors, six hundred men, and they were present beside him; and all the Cherethites and all the Pelethites and all the Gittites, the six hundred men who came on foot to Gath and were going before the face of the king.

**15:19** And the king said to Seththi the Gittite, "Why are you also going with us? Return and live with the king, for you are a foreigner and because you have migrated from your place.

**15:20** If you arrived yesterday, shall I today move you with us? And indeed you will leave your place; yesterday was your coming out, and today shall I make you move away to go with us? And I will go wherever I may go; return, and bring back your brothers with you, and the Lord will do mercy and truth with you."

**15:21** And Ittai answered the king and said, "As the Lord lives, and as my lord the king lives, surely in the place wherever my lord may be, whether for death or for life, surely there your servant will be."

**15:22** And the king said to Seththi, "Come and cross over with me." And Seththi the Gittite passed over, and all his servants and all the crowd that was with him.

**15:23** And all the land was weeping with a loud voice; and all the people were passing along in the brook of the cedars, and the king crossed the brook Kidron; and all the people and the king were passing along facing the road to the wilderness.

**15:24** And look, Zadok also, and all the Levites with him, were carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord from Baithar. And they set down the ark of God, and Abiathar went up when all the people finished passing out of the city.

**15:25** And the king said to Zadok, "Return the ark of God to the city. If I find favor in the eyes of the Lord, then He will bring me back and show me it and its beauty;

**15:26** and if He says thus, 'I have no delight in you,' look, here I am; let Him do to me according to what is good in His eyes."

**15:27** And the king said to Zadok the priest, "See, you are returning to the city in peace, and Ahimaaz your son and Jonathan the son of Abiathar, your two sons, with you.

**15:28** See, I am campaigning in Araboth of the wilderness until a word comes from you to report to me."

**15:29** And Zadok and Abiathar returned the ark to Jerusalem and remained there.

**15:30** And David was going up by the ascent of the olive trees and with his head covered, and he himself was walking barefoot; and all the people who were with him, each man covered his head, and they were going up, ascending and weeping.

**15:31** And it was reported to David, saying, "Ahithophel also is among those conspiring with Absalom." And David said, "Please frustrate the counsel of Ahithophel, Lord my God."

**15:32** And David was coming as far as Roos, where he worshiped God there; and look, coming to meet him was Hushai the Archite, the friend of David, with his tunic torn and earth on his head.

**15:33** And David said to him, "If you cross over with me, then you will be a burden upon me.

**15:34** But if you return to the city, and you say to Absalom, 'Your brothers have passed on, and the king, your father, has passed on behind me; and now I am your servant, king, let me live; I was your father's servant then and just now, and now I am your servant,' then you will frustrate for me the counsel of Ahithophel.

**15:35** And look, with you there are Zadok and Abiathar the priests. And it shall be that every word that you hear from the king's house, you shall report to Zadok and Abiathar the priests.

**15:36** Look, there with them are their two sons, Ahimaaz son of Zadok and Jonathan son of Abiathar; and you shall send by their hand to me every word that you hear."

**15:37** And Hushai the friend of David went into the city, and Absalom was entering Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **15:12** — The Greek identifies Ahithophel as "the Tekonite" (Thekonei) from "Gola" (corresponding to MT "the Gilonite" from "Giloh") and uses "shattering" (syntrimma) where the MT has "conspiracy."
- **15:18** — The Greek preserves an expanded doublet naming "the Chettites" (Chettei, alongside "the Cherethites" later in the verse) and adding the halt "at the olive tree in the wilderness," and it reads "to Gath" (MT "from Gath").
- **15:19** — The Greek spells this Gittite commander's name "Seththi" (Seththei) here and in v. 22 (apparently from dittography of the final sigma of the preceding Greek preposition pros) while using the standard "Ittai" (Eththei) in v. 21.
- **15:24** — The Greek adds the place name "from Baithar" (apo Baithar), likely arising from a textual doublet of the name Abiathar; in the preceding verse (15:23) the Greek renders Kidron first as "of the cedars" (ton kedron) and then as "Kidron" (Kedron).
- **15:28** — The Greek reads "campaigning" (strateuomai, likely an inner-Greek corruption of stranggeuomai, "lingering/waiting") and leaves the Hebrew geographical term araboth ("plains/steppes") untranslated as "Araboth."
- **15:32** — The Greek transliterates Hebrew harosh ("the summit") as a proper name, "Roos" (Rhoos); in v. 34 it also preserves a longer speech ("Your brothers have passed on... let me live") absent from the Hebrew MT.
