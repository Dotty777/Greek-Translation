## Text

**21:1** And there was a famine in the days of David for three years, year after year, and David sought the face of the Lord. And the Lord said, "Upon Saul and upon his house is injustice on account of him, by a death of bloodshed, because he put the Gibeonites to death."

**21:2** And King David called the Gibeonites and spoke to them. Now the Gibeonites are not sons of Israel, but rather from the blood of the Amorites, and the sons of Israel swore to them; and Saul sought to strike them down in his zeal for the sons of Israel and Judah.

**21:3** And David said to the Gibeonites, "What shall I do for you, and with what shall I make atonement, so that you will bless the inheritance of the Lord?"

**21:4** And the Gibeonites said to him, "There is no silver and gold for us with Saul and with his house, and there is no man for us to put to death in Israel." And he said, "What do you say, and I will do it for you?"

**21:5** And they said to the king, "The man brought destruction upon us and pursued us, who schemed to utterly destroy us; let us wipe him out so that he may not stand in any territory of Israel.

**21:6** Let him give us seven men from his sons, and let us expose them in the sun to the Lord in Gibeon of Saul, chosen ones of the Lord." And the king said, "I will give them."

**21:7** And the king spared Mephibosheth the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, because of the oath of the Lord that was between them, even between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.

**21:8** And the king took the two sons of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bore to Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth, and the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul, whom she bore to Serei the son of Barzillai the Meholathite,

**21:9** and he gave them into the hand of the Gibeonites. And they exposed them in the sun on the mountain before the Lord, and the seven of them fell together. And they were put to death in the days of harvest, in the first days, at the beginning of the barley harvest.

**21:10** And Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and pitched it for herself by the rock at the beginning of the barley harvest until water dripped upon them from the sky, and she did not allow the birds of the sky to rest upon them by day nor the wild beasts of the field by night.

**21:11** And it was reported to David all that Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done; and they grew faint, and Dan the son of Joa, from the descendants of the giants, overtook them.

**21:12** And David went and took the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son from the men, the sons of Jabesh-gilead, who stole them from the open square of Beth, because the foreigners had set them up there on the day when the foreigners struck down Saul in Gilboa.

**21:13** And he brought up from there the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and he gathered the bones of those exposed in the sun.

**21:14** And they buried the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and of those exposed in the sun who were in the land of Benjamin, in the side, in the tomb of Kish his father. And they did all that the king commanded, and God listened to the land after these things.

**21:15** And there was again war for the foreigners with Israel; and David went down, and his servants with him, and they fought with the foreigners, and David departed.

**21:16** And Ishbi, who was among the offspring of Rapha—and the weight of his spear was three hundred shekels of bronze by weight, and he was girded with a club—intended to strike down David.

**21:17** And Abishai the son of Zeruiah helped him, and struck down the foreigner and put him to death. Then the men of David swore, saying, "You shall not go out with us to war anymore, and you shall by no means extinguish the lamp of Israel."

**21:18** And after these things there was again war in Gath with the foreigners; then Oebocha the Hastatothite struck down Saph, who was among the offspring of Rapha.

**21:19** And the war took place in Rom with the foreigners; and Elhanan the son of Ariorgim the Bethlehemite struck down Godolias the Hittite, and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam.

**21:20** And there was again war in Gath; and there was a man of Madon, and the fingers of his hands and the toes of his feet were six and six, twenty-four in number; and he also was born to Rapha.

**21:21** And he taunted Israel, and Jonathan the son of Shimei, the brother of David, struck him down.

**21:22** These four were born descendants of the giants in Gath, a house for Rapha, and they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — The Greek syntax is elliptical ("injustice on account of him, by a death of bloodshed"), differing from the Hebrew reading of bloodguilt on Saul and his house.
- **21:6** — The Greek reads "Gibeon of Saul" (rather than Hebrew "Gibeah of Saul") and plural "chosen ones of the Lord" referring to the seven men; "expose in the sun" renders the specific Greek verb exeliazō (also in vv. 9, 13-14).
- **21:8** — The Greek has the distinct name "Serei" where the Hebrew reads "Adriel."
- **21:11** — The closing clause ("and they grew faint, and Dan the son of Joa...") is a Septuagint addition absent from the Hebrew text, introducing the distinct names Dan and Joa.
- **21:12** — The Greek reads "Beth" (lacking the second element of Hebrew "Beth-shan"), and renders Hebrew "Philistines" with allophyloi ("foreigners") here and in vv. 15 and 17-19, its standard term in 1-2 Kingdoms.
- **21:14** — The Greek translates the Hebrew place name "Zela" as a common noun, "in the side."
- **21:18** — The Greek reads "Gath" (Hebrew "Gob") and the distinct proper name "Oebocha the Hastatothite" (Hebrew "Sibbecai the Hushathite").
- **21:19** — The Greek reads the distinct proper names "Rom" (Hebrew "Gob"), "Ariorgim" (Hebrew "Jaare-oregim"), and "Godolias the Hittite" (Hebrew "Goliath the Gittite").
- **21:20** — The Greek treats the Hebrew expression for "a man of stature" as a proper name, "a man of Madon."
