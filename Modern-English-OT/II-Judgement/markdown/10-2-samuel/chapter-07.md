## Text

**7:1** And it happened when the king settled in his house, and the Lord gave him an inheritance all around from all his enemies who were all around,

**7:2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look now, I live in a cedar house, and the ark of God sits in the middle of the tent."

**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, "Everything that is in your heart, go and do, for the Lord is with you."

**7:4** And it happened during that night that a word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,

**7:5** "Go and say to My servant David, 'Thus says the Lord: You will not build a house for Me to live in.

**7:6** For I have not lived in a house from the day I brought the sons of Israel up out of Egypt until this day, and I was walking about in a lodging and in a tent.

**7:7** In all the places through which I passed among all Israel, did I speak at all to a single tribe of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built Me a cedar house?"'

**7:8** And now thus you will say to My servant David: 'Thus says the Lord Almighty: I took you from the sheepfold for you to be a leader over My people, over Israel,

**7:9** and I was with you wherever you went, and I utterly destroyed all your enemies from before you, and I made you renowned, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**7:10** And I will appoint a place for My people Israel and plant them, and they will dwell on their own and will no longer be anxious, and a son of unrighteousness will no longer continue to humble them as from the beginning,

**7:11** from the days when I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will give you rest from all your enemies, and the Lord will declare to you that you will build a house for Him.

**7:12** And it will be, when your days are fulfilled and you sleep with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who will come from your own body, and I will prepare his kingdom.

**7:13** He will build Me a house for My name, and I will establish his throne forever.

**7:14** I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me. And if his unrighteousness comes, then I will reprove him with a rod of men and with blows of the sons of men,

**7:15** yet I will not remove My mercy from him, as I removed it from those whom I removed from before Me.

**7:16** And his house and his kingdom will be confirmed forever before Me, and his throne will be established forever.'"

**7:17** According to all these words and according to this entire vision, thus Nathan spoke to David.

**7:18** Then King David went in and sat before the Lord, and said, "Who am I, my Lord, Lord, and what is my house, that You have loved me up to these things?

**7:19** And I was diminished a little before You, my Lord, yet You spoke concerning the house of Your servant for the distant future; and this is the law of man, my Lord, Lord.

**7:20** And what more will David add to speak to You? And now You know Your servant, Lord.

**7:21** Because of Your servant You have acted; and according to Your heart You did all this greatness, to make it known to Your servant, for the sake of magnifying You,

**7:22** Lord, my Lord. For there is no one like You, and there is no God besides You in all that we heard with our ears.

**7:23** And what other nation on the earth is like Your people Israel, as God guided them to redeem a people for Himself, for You to establish a name, to do greatness and manifestation, for You to drive out from before Your people, whom You redeemed for Yourself out of Egypt, nations and dwellings?

**7:24** And You prepared for Yourself Your people Israel as a people forever, and You, Lord, became God to them.

**7:25** And now, my Lord, confirm forever the word that You spoke concerning Your servant and his house, Lord Almighty, God of Israel; and now, as You spoke,

**7:26** may Your name be magnified forever.

**7:27** Lord Almighty, God of Israel, You uncovered the ear of Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you.' Therefore Your servant found his heart to pray this prayer to You.

**7:28** And now, my Lord, Lord, You are God, and Your words will be true, and You spoke these good things concerning Your servant.

**7:29** And now begin and bless the house of Your servant, to be before You forever; for it is You, my Lord, Lord, who spoke, and from the blessing the house of Your servant will be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek reads kateklēronomēsen ('gave him an inheritance'), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb than the Masoretic Text's 'gave him rest.'
- **7:11** — The Greek uniquely reads oikon oikodomēseis autō ('you will build a house for Him'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the Lord will make a house for you' (contrast v. 27 below).
- **7:15** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Greek omits the name of Saul and reads 'from those whom I removed from before Me,' and in v. 16 continues in the third person ('his house... his kingdom... before Me... his throne') rather than second-person 'your.'
- **7:18** — The Greek reads ēgapēkas me ('You have loved me'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'You have brought me'; similarly in v. 19 the Greek reads 'I was diminished a little' rather than 'this was a small thing.'
- **7:23** — The Greek reads 'another nation' (ethnos allo) and 'nations and dwellings' (ethnē kai skēnōmata), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'one nation' and 'nations and their gods.'
- **7:26** — The Greek text of v. 26 is considerably shorter than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, placing 'Lord Almighty, God of Israel' as a direct address in v. 25 and omitting the clause about David's house being established before God.
