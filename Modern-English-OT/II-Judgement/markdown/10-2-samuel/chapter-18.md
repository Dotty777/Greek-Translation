## Text

**18:1** And David inspected the people who were with him, and appointed over them commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds.

**18:2** And David sent out the people: a third under the hand of Joab, and a third under the hand of Abishai the son of Zeruiah, the brother of Joab, and a third under the hand of Ittai the Gittite. And David said to the people, "I myself will also surely go out with you."

**18:3** And they said, "You must not go out, for if we indeed flee, they will not pay attention to us, and if half of us die, they will not pay attention to us, for you are like ten thousand of us; and now it is good that you will be a help to us in the city to give help."

**18:4** And the king said to them, "Whatever is pleasing in your eyes I will do." And the king stood beside the gate, and all the people were going out by hundreds and by thousands.

**18:5** And the king commanded Joab and Abishai and Ittai, saying, "Spare for my sake the young man Absalom." And all the people heard the king commanding all the commanders concerning Absalom.

**18:6** And all the people went out into the forest against Israel, and the battle took place in the forest of Ephraim.

**18:7** And the people of Israel were defeated there before the servants of David, and the slaughter was great on that day, twenty thousand men.

**18:8** And the battle there became spread out over the face of all the land, and the forest devoured more from among the people than those whom the sword devoured among the people on that day.

**18:9** And Absalom came face to face with the servants of David; and Absalom was riding on his mule, and the mule went under the dense branches of the great oak, and his head was suspended in the oak, and he was suspended between heaven and earth, and the mule beneath him passed on.

**18:10** And one man saw it and reported to Joab, and said, "Look, I have seen Absalom hanging in the oak."

**18:11** And Joab said to the man reporting it, "And look, you have seen him! Why did you not strike him to the ground? And I would have given you ten pieces of silver and one belt."

**18:12** But the man said to Joab, "Though I am weighing out a thousand shekels of silver into my hands, I will by no means lay my hand on the king's son; for in our ears the king commanded you and Abishai and Ittai, saying, 'Guard for me the young man Absalom,

**18:13** so as not to do wrong against his life.' And no matter will be hidden from the king, and you yourself will stand in opposition."

**18:14** And Joab said, "This I will begin; I will not wait like this before you." And Joab took three darts in his hand, and they thrust them into the heart of Absalom while he was still alive in the heart of the oak.

**18:15** And ten young men carrying Joab's armor circled around, and they struck Absalom and put him to death.

**18:16** And Joab sounded a horn, and the people turned back from pursuing Israel, for Joab was sparing the people.

**18:17** And he took Absalom and threw him into a great chasm in the forest, into the great pit, and erected over him a very great heap of stones; and all Israel fled, each man to his tent.

**18:18** Now Absalom, while still alive, had set up for himself the pillar in which he was taken and erected it to take, the pillar that is in the Valley of the King; for he said that he had no son to keep his name in remembrance, and he called the pillar Hand of Absalom to this day.

**18:19** And Ahimaaz the son of Zadok said, "Please let me run and bring good news to the king that the Lord has rendered judgment from the hand of his enemies."

**18:20** And Joab said to him, "You are not a man of good news on this day, and you will bring good news on another day; but on this day you will not bring good news, because the king's son has died."

**18:21** And Joab said to Cushi, "Go, report to the king all that you have seen." And Cushi bowed down to Joab and went out.

**18:22** And Ahimaaz the son of Zadok continued further and said to Joab, "And let it be that I also run after Cushi." And Joab said, "Why is it that you are running, my son? Come, you have no good news for profit as you go."

**18:23** And he said, "For what if I run?" And Joab said to him, "Run." And Ahimaaz ran by the way of Kechar and passed Cushi.

**18:24** And David was sitting between the two gates; and the watchman went to the roof of the gate by the wall, and he lifted up his eyes and looked, and see, a man was running alone before him.

**18:25** And the watchman cried out and reported to the king. And the king said, "If he is alone, good news is in his mouth." And he kept coming on and drawing near.

**18:26** And the watchman saw another man running, and the watchman called out at the gate and said, "Look, another man running alone!" And the king said, "This one also is bringing good news."

**18:27** And the watchman said, "I see the running of the first as the running of Ahimaaz the son of Zadok." And the king said, "This is a good man, and he also will come with good news."

**18:28** And Ahimaaz called out and said to the king, "Peace!" And he bowed down to the king with his face to the ground and said, "Blessed is the Lord your God, who has delivered up the men who hate their hand against my lord the king."

**18:29** And the king said, "Is there peace for the young man Absalom?" And Ahimaaz said, "I saw the great crowd when Joab sent the king's servant and your servant, and I did not know what was there."

**18:30** And the king said, "Turn aside, take your stand here." And he turned aside and stood.

**18:31** And look, Cushi arrived and said to the king, "Let my lord the king receive good news, for the Lord has rendered judgment for you today from the hand of all those rising up against you."

**18:32** And the king said to Cushi, "Is there peace for the young man Absalom?" And Cushi said, "May the enemies of my lord the king, and all who rose up against him for evil, become like the young man."

## Translator Notes

- **18:14** — The Greek preserves a doublet in Joab's opening remark ("This I will begin; I will not wait like this before you") and uses a plural verb ("they thrust") following singular "Joab took," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a singular verb ("he thrust").
- **18:18** — The Greek contains an expanded doublet ("in which he was taken and erected it to take") absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reports Absalom's reason in indirect speech, and renders the monument's name literally as "Hand of Absalom."
- **18:21** — "Cushi" transliterates the Greek proper name Chousei, rendering the Hebrew gentilic "the Cushite" as a personal name; at the end of the verse the Greek also reads "went out" where the Hebrew has "ran."
- **18:23** — "Kechar" transliterates the Greek place name Kechar, which renders Hebrew ha-kikkar ("the plain" or "the Jordan valley") as a proper name.
- **18:28** — The Greek literally reads "who hate their hand" (tous misountas), reflecting a reading of the Hebrew verb as "hated" rather than the Masoretic Text's "lifted up."
