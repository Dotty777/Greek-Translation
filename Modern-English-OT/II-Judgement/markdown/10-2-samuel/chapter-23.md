## Text

**23:1** Faithful is David son of Jesse, and faithful is the man whom the Lord raised up over the anointed of the God of Jacob, and lovely are the psalms of Israel.

**23:2** The Spirit of the Lord spoke in me, and His word was upon my tongue.

**23:3** The God of Israel says; the Guardian from Israel spoke to me, "Speak a parable: 'In man, how may you strengthen the fear of the anointed?

**23:4** And in God, the light of morning, may the sun rise; in the morning the Lord did not pass by from brightness, and as from rain of fresh grass from the earth.'"

**23:5** For is not this my house with the Mighty One? For He established an eternal covenant with me, prepared at every time, guarded; for it is all my salvation and every desire; for the lawless one will by no means sprout.

**23:6** Like a thorn thrust away are all these, for they will not be taken by hand.

**23:7** And a man will not toil among them; and it is full of iron and the shaft of a spear, and in fire it will burn, and they will set their shame.

**23:8** These are the names of the mighty men of David: Jebosthe the Canaanite is commander of the third; Adinon the Asonite, he drew his sword against eight hundred soldiers at one time.

**23:9** And after him was Elhanan son of his uncle, son of Susi, who was among the three mighty men with David; and when he taunted among the foreigners, they were gathered there for battle, and the men of Israel cried out.

**23:10** He arose and struck among the foreigners until his hand grew weary, and his hand clung to the sword; and the Lord brought about a great salvation on that day, and the people sat behind him only to strip them.

**23:11** And after him was Samaia son of Asa, the Aruchite; and the foreigners were gathered at Theria, and there was a plot of the field there full of lentils, and the people fled from before the foreigners.

**23:12** And he stood firm in the middle of the plot, and rescued it and struck the foreigners, and the Lord brought about a great salvation.

**23:13** And three of the thirty went down and went down to Kadon to David, to the cave of Adullam; and there was a troop of the foreigners, and they encamped in the valley of Rephaim.

**23:14** And David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the foreigners was then in Bethlehem.

**23:15** And David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the cistern that is in Bethlehem, the one at the gate?" Now the company of the foreigners was then in Bethlehem.

**23:16** And the three mighty men broke through into the camp of the foreigners and drew water from the cistern that is in Bethlehem, the one at the gate, and took it and came to David, yet he was not willing to drink it; and he poured it out as a drink offering to the Lord.

**23:17** And he said, "Far be it from me, Lord, to do this! Should I drink the blood of the men who went at the peril of their lives?" And he was not willing to drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**23:18** And Abishai brother of Joab, son of Zeruiah, he was commander among the three, and he raised up his spear against three hundred wounded men; and he had a name among the three.

**23:19** Of those three he was renowned, and he became a commander for them, yet he did not reach the three.

**23:20** And Benaiah son of Jehoiada, he was a man of many deeds from Kabzeel, and he struck down the two sons of Ariel of Moab; and he went down and struck the lion in the middle of the pit on the day of snow.

**23:21** He struck down the Egyptian man, a conspicuous man; now in the Egyptian's hand was a spear like the beam of a gangplank; and he went down to him with the spear and snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.

**23:22** These things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**23:23** Of the three he was renowned, yet he did not reach the three; and David appointed him to his hearing. And these are the names of the mighty men of King David:

**23:24** Asahel brother of Joab, he was among the thirty; Elhanan son of Dudi his uncle, in Bethlehem;

**23:25** Saima the Rudite;

**23:26** Selles the Kelothi; Ira son of Iska, the Tekoite;

**23:27** Abiezer the Anothite, from the sons of the Anothite;

**23:28** Ellon the Ahohite; Noere the Entophathite.

**23:29** Esthai son of Riba from Gibeah, son of Benjamin the Ephrathite;

**23:31** Asboth the Bardiamite;

**23:32** Emasu the Salabonite; sons of Asan, Jonathan;

**23:33** Samnan the Harodite; Amnan son of Arai, a Saraurite;

**23:34** Eliphelet son of the Asbite, son of the Machachaachi; Eliab son of Ahithophel the Gilonite;

**23:35** Hasarai the Carmelite, of Uraioerchi;

**23:36** Gaal son of Nathan from the army, son of Galaaddi;

**23:37** Hadaoi from the brooks; Gadabiel son of the Arabothite. Elie the Ammonite; Gelore the Bethorite, carrying the armor of Joab, son of Zeruiah;

**23:38** Ira the Aithirite; Gerab the Etthenite;

**23:39** Uriah the Hittite; thirty-seven in all.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — In this Greek text the introductory clause 'And these are the last words of David' appears at the end of 22:51 rather than opening 23:1; the Septuagint also reads 'Faithful' (pistos) instead of 'oracle', reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants, and renders 'over the anointed' rather than 'raised on high'.
- **23:3** — The Greek diverges markedly from the Masoretic Text here and in 23:4, rendering 'Rock of Israel' as 'Guardian from Israel', reading 'Speak a parable' for 'one who rules', and reading 'fear of the anointed' (christou) rather than 'fear of God'.
- **23:9** — The Greek reads Elhanan (MT: Eleazar) and uses 'the foreigners' (hoi allophyloi), the Septuagint's regular term for the Philistines in 1-2 Kingdoms. At the end of the verse, 'cried out' (aneboesen) differs from MT 'withdrew'.
- **23:21** — The Greek uniquely describes the Egyptian's spear as 'like the beam of a gangplank' (xylon diabathras) and states that Benaiah went down to him 'with the spear' rather than 'with a club'.
- **23:25** — Throughout 23:8-38 the Greek preserves numerous distinctive personal and place names (such as Jebosthe, Adinon, Theria, Kadon, Saima the Rudite, Selles, Noere, Esthai, Emasu, and Gelore), omits 23:30 (with surviving elements appearing in 23:29 and 23:37), and in 23:36 translates 'from Zobah' as 'from the army' (apo dynameos).
