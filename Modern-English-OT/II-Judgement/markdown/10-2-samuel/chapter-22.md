## Text

**22:1** And David spoke to the Lord the words of this song on the day when the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

**22:2** And he said: A song. "Lord, my rock and my stronghold, and the One who delivers me for myself.

**22:3** My God will be my guardian; I will trust in Him, my defender and the horn of my salvation, my supporter and my refuge of my salvation; from injustice You will save me.

**22:4** I will call upon the praiseworthy Lord, and from my enemies I will be saved.

**22:5** For crushings of death encompassed me; torrents of lawlessness terrified me;

**22:6** pangs of death surrounded me; severities of death confronted me.

**22:7** In my distress I will call upon the Lord, and to my God I will cry out; and He will hear my voice from His temple, and my cry will be in His ears.

**22:8** And the earth was agitated and shaken, and the foundations of heaven were thrown into turmoil and were convulsed, because the Lord was angry with them.

**22:9** Smoke went up in His wrath, and fire from His mouth will devour; coals were kindled from Him.

**22:10** And He bent the heavens and came down, and thick darkness was beneath His feet.

**22:11** And He sat upon cherubim and flew, and He appeared upon the wings of the wind.

**22:12** And He set the darkness of His hiding place around Him; His tent was darkness of waters; He thickened it in clouds of air.

**22:13** From the brightness before Him, coals of fire were kindled.

**22:14** The Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave His voice;

**22:15** and He sent arrows and scattered them, lightning, and confounded them.

**22:16** And the channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the inhabited world were uncovered at the rebuke of the Lord, from the blast of the breath of His fury.

**22:17** He sent from on high and took me; He pulled me out of many waters.

**22:18** He rescued me from my strong enemies, from those who hate me, for they became stronger than me.

**22:19** The days of my distress confronted me, and the Lord became my support;

**22:20** and He brought me out into a broad place, and He delivered me, because He delighted in me.

**22:21** And the Lord repaid me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He repaid me.

**22:22** For I kept the ways of the Lord, and I did not act impiously away from my God;

**22:23** for all His judgments were before me, and as for His ordinances, I did not turn away from them.

**22:24** And I will be blameless before Him, and I will guard myself from my lawlessness;

**22:25** and the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness and according to the cleanness of my hands before His eyes.

**22:26** With a devout one You will show Yourself devout, and with a perfect man You will show Yourself perfect;

**22:27** and with a chosen one You will be chosen, and with a crooked one You will show Yourself crooked.

**22:28** And the poor people You will save, and eyes upon the lofty You will humble.

**22:29** For You are my lamp, Lord, and the Lord will illuminate my darkness for me.

**22:30** For by You I will run lightly armed, and by my God I will scale a wall.

**22:31** As for the Mighty One, His way is blameless; the word of the Lord is strong, refined by fire; He is a defender to all those who trust in Him.

**22:32** Who is mighty except the Lord? And who will be a creator except our God?

**22:33** The Mighty One is the One who strengthens me with power, and He shook out my way blameless,

**22:34** making my feet like those of deer, and setting me upon the heights,

**22:35** teaching my hands for war, and breaking a bronze bow with my arm.

**22:36** And You gave me the defense of my salvation, and Your listening multiplied me, into a broad place for my steps beneath me,

**22:37** and my legs were not shaken.

**22:38** I will pursue my enemies and wipe them out, and I will not turn back until I bring them to an end;

**22:39** and I will crush them, and they will not rise, and they will fall under my feet.

**22:40** And You will strengthen me with power for war; You will bend down those who rise up against me beneath me.

**22:41** And You gave me the back of my enemies, those who hate me, and You put them to death.

**22:42** They will cry out, and there is no helper; to the Lord, and He did not listen to them.

**22:43** And I ground them fine like dust of the earth; like mud of the streets I pulverized them.

**22:44** And You will rescue me from the strife of peoples; You will keep me as head of nations. A people whom I did not know served me;

**22:45** foreign sons lied to me; at the hearing of the ear they obeyed me;

**22:46** foreign sons will be cast away and will stumble out of their enclosures.

**22:47** The Lord lives, and blessed is my guardian, and my God, the guardian of my salvation, will be exalted.

**22:48** Mighty is the Lord, who gives acts of vengeance to me, disciplining peoples beneath me,

**22:49** and bringing me out from my enemies; and from those who rise up against me You will lift me up; from a man of wrongdoings You will rescue me.

**22:50** Therefore I will give thanks to You, Lord, among the nations, and in Your name I will sing praise.

**22:51** He magnifies the acts of salvation of His king and shows mercy to His anointed, to David and to his seed forever." And these are the last words of David.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Greek text includes the introductory rubric Ode ("A song") between "And he said" and the vocative "Lord," reflecting a Septuagint canticle heading not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **22:30** — Where the Hebrew reads "troop" or "raiding band," the Greek has the nominative adjective monozonos ("lightly armed," literally "single-girded"), describing an agile light-armed soldier.
- **22:32** — Where the Hebrew reads "rock," the Greek translates with ktistes ("creator") and adds the future verb "will be" (compare phylax, "guardian," in vv. 3 and 47).
- **22:36** — The Greek reads "Your listening" (hypakoe) rather than "Your gentleness," and includes the verbless phrase "into a broad place for my steps beneath me" at the end of v. 36 rather than at the start of v. 37.
- **22:51** — This Greek text concludes 22:51 with the introductory clause "And these are the last words of David," which is usually placed at the opening of 23:1.
