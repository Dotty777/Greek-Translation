## Text

**4:1** And Mephibosheth son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron, and his hands grew weak, and all the men of Israel grew faint.

**4:2** And Mephibosheth son of Saul had two men who were leaders of raiding bands: the name of the one was Baanah, and the name of the second was Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, from the sons of Benjamin, for Beeroth was reckoned to the sons of Benjamin.

**4:3** And the Beerothites fled to Gittaim, and they were living there as resident aliens until this day.

**4:4** Now Jonathan son of Saul had a son who was stricken in his feet; he was five years old when the report of Saul and Jonathan his son came from Israel, and his nurse picked him up and fled; and it happened, as he hurried and withdrew, that he fell and became lame; and his name was Mephibosheth.

**4:5** And the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, went and entered the house of Mephibosheth in the heat of the day, and he was sleeping in bed at midday.

**4:6** And look, the female doorkeeper of the house was cleaning wheat, and she grew drowsy and slept; and Rechab and Baanah the brothers slipped past unnoticed,

**4:7** and they went into the house, and Mephibosheth was sleeping on his bed in his bedroom; and they struck him and put him to death, and removed his head, and took his head and went away by the road toward the west all night.

**4:8** And they brought the head of Mephibosheth to David in Hebron, and said to the king, "Here is the head of Mephibosheth son of Saul your enemy, who was seeking your life; and the Lord has given to the lord king vengeance on his enemies as on this day, from Saul your enemy and from his offspring."

**4:9** And David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said to them, "As the Lord lives, who redeemed my life from all distress, the one who reported to me that Saul was dead,

**4:10** and he was like one bringing good news before me, yet I seized him and killed him in Ziklag, the one to whom I was supposed to give a reward for good news.

**4:11** And now wicked men have killed a righteous man in his house upon his bed; and now I will require his blood from your hand and wipe you out from the earth."

**4:12** And David commanded his young men, and they killed them; and they cut off their hands and their feet and hung them by the spring in Hebron, and they buried the head of Mephibosheth in the tomb of Abner son of Ner.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek reads 'from Israel' (MT: 'from Jezreel') and uses the masculine noun ho tithenos ('his nurse' or male caretaker) along with the masculine pronoun 'he'.
- **4:6** — The Greek preserves a narrative detail about the female doorkeeper cleaning wheat and falling asleep so Rechab and Baanah slipped past unnoticed, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text states that they entered as though fetching wheat and struck him in the belly.
- **4:7** — The Greek reads 'the road toward the west' (interpreting the Hebrew consonant string for 'the Arabah' as 'evening/west'), and in 4:10 reads 'before me' (MT: 'in his own eyes') and 'a reward for good news' (euangelia).
