## Text

**10:1** And it happened after these things that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and Hanun his son reigned in his place.

**10:2** And David said, "I will show mercy to Hanun son of Nahash, just as his father showed mercy to me." And David sent to comfort him by the hand of his servants concerning his father; and the servants of David came into the land of the sons of Ammon.

**10:3** And the rulers of the sons of Ammon said to Hanun their lord, "Is it really because David honors your father in your sight that he has sent comforters to you? Rather, has David not sent his servants to you so that they may search the city and spy it out and examine it?"

**10:4** And Hanun took the servants of David and shaved their beards and cut off their cloaks in half as far as their hips, and sent them away.

**10:5** And they reported to David concerning the men, and he sent to meet them, because the men were greatly dishonored; and the king said, "Remain in Jericho until your beards grow out, and you will return."

**10:6** And the sons of Ammon saw that the people of David had been put to shame; and the sons of Ammon sent and hired Syria and Rehob, twenty thousand foot soldiers, and the king of Amalek, one thousand men, and Istob, twelve thousand men.

**10:7** And David heard, and sent Joab and all the army, the mighty men.

**10:8** And the sons of Ammon came out and drew up for battle at the entrance of the gate of Syria of Zobah, and Rehob and Istob and Amalek were alone in the field.

**10:9** And Joab saw that the battlefront was against him, directly in front and from behind, and he chose from all the young men of Israel, and they drew up opposite Syria.

**10:10** And the remainder of the people he gave into the hand of Abishai his brother, and they drew up opposite the sons of Ammon.

**10:11** And he said, "If Syria proves stronger than I, then you will be a deliverance for me; and if the sons of Ammon prove stronger than you, then we will be ready to deliver you.

**10:12** Be courageous, and let us be strong for our people and for the cities of our God, and the Lord will do what is good in His eyes."

**10:13** And Joab and his people with him approached for battle against Syria, and they fled from before him.

**10:14** And the sons of Ammon saw that Syria had fled, and they fled from before Abishai and entered the city. And Joab turned back from the sons of Ammon, and they came to Jerusalem.

**10:15** And Syria saw that it had suffered defeat before Israel, and they gathered together.

**10:16** And Hadarezer sent and gathered Syria from beyond the river Chalamak, and they came to Helam; and Shobach, the commander of the army of Hadarezer, was before them.

**10:17** And it was reported to David, and he gathered all Israel, and crossed the Jordan, and they came to Helam; and David drew up opposite Syria, and they fought against him.

**10:18** And Syria fled from before Israel; and David destroyed from Syria seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen, and he struck down Shobach the commander of his army, and he died there.

**10:19** And all the kings, the servants of Hadarezer, saw that they had suffered defeat before Israel, and they defected to Israel and served them; and Syria was afraid to deliver the sons of Ammon any longer.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text concludes the spies' supposed aim with "to overthrow it," the Greek reads "to examine it" (kataskepsasthai), providing a third verb of reconnaissance.
- **10:4** — The Greek states simply that Hanun shaved "their beards," without the Masoretic Text's "half of."
- **10:6** — The Greek reads that "the people of David had been put to shame" (MT: that the Ammonites had become odious to David) and names the mercenaries as "Syria and Rehob," "the king of Amalek" (MT: Maacah), and "Istob" (rendering Hebrew "men of Tob" as a single proper name).
- **10:8** — The Greek attaches "of Syria of Zobah" in the genitive directly to "the gate," rather than listing Aram-zobah alongside the allies in the field as in the Masoretic Text.
- **10:16** — The Greek uniquely names the river "Chalamak" (likely a doublet of Helam) before stating that the Syrians came to Helam; similarly in 10:19 the Greek uses a military verb for "defected" (automoleo) rather than "made peace."
