## Text

**6:1** And David again gathered every young man from Israel, about seventy thousand.

**6:2** And David arose and went, and all the people who were with him, from the rulers of Judah in an ascent, to bring up from there the ark of God, over which was called the name of the Lord of powers, who sits upon the cherubim on it.

**6:3** And he placed the ark of the Lord on a new cart and carried it to the house of Amminadab, who was on the hill; and Uzzah and his brothers, the sons of Amminadab, drove the cart with the ark,

**6:4** and his brothers were walking before the ark.

**6:5** And David and the sons of Israel were celebrating before the Lord with tuned instruments, with strength, and with songs, and with lyres, and with harps, and with tambourines, and with cymbals, and with flutes.

**6:6** And they came as far as the threshing floor of Nodab; and Uzzah stretched out his hand to the ark of God to hold it, and he grasped it, because the calf pulled him aside, in order to hold it.

**6:7** And the Lord became angry with Uzzah, and God struck him there, and he died there beside the ark of the Lord before God.

**6:8** And David became disheartened because the Lord had broken through with a breach against Uzzah; and that place was called Breach of Uzzah until this day.

**6:9** And David feared the Lord on that day, saying, "How will the ark of the Lord come in to me?"

**6:10** And David was not willing to turn the ark of the covenant of the Lord aside to himself into the city of David; and David turned it aside into the house of Abeddara the Gittite.

**6:11** And the ark of the Lord remained in the house of Abeddara the Gittite for three months; and the Lord blessed the whole house of Abeddara and all that belonged to him.

**6:12** And it was reported to King David, saying, "The Lord has blessed the house of Abeddara and all that belongs to him on account of the ark of God." And David went and brought up the ark of the Lord from the house of Abeddara to the city of David with gladness.

**6:13** And with them, carrying the ark, were seven choirs, and as a sacrifice, a calf and a lamb.

**6:14** And David was playing music with tuned instruments before the Lord, and David was wearing an unusual robe.

**6:15** And David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the Lord with shouting and with the sound of a trumpet.

**6:16** And it happened, as the ark was coming as far as the city of David, that Michal the daughter of Saul was looking out through the window, and she saw King David dancing and playing music before the Lord, and she despised him in her heart.

**6:17** And they brought the ark of the Lord and set it in its place in the middle of the tent that David had pitched for it; and he offered for it whole burnt offerings before the Lord, and peace offerings.

**6:18** And David finished offering up the whole burnt offerings and the peace offerings, and he blessed the people in the name of the Lord of powers.

**6:19** And he distributed to all the people, to all the host of Israel from Dan to Beersheba, from man to woman, to each a loaf of bread, and a hearth-baked cake, and a flat cake from a frying pan; and all the people departed, each to his own house.

**6:20** And David returned to bless his own house, and Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David and blessed him and said, "How the king of Israel has been honored today, who uncovered himself today in the eyes of the maidservants of his own servants, just as one of the dancers completely uncovers himself!"

**6:21** And David said to Michal, "Before the Lord I will dance! Blessed be the Lord, who chose me above your father and above all his house, to appoint me as leader over His people, over Israel. And I will celebrate and dance before the Lord,

**6:22** and I will uncover myself still like this, and I will be worthless in your eyes, and with the maidservants about whom you said that I was honored."

**6:23** And Michal the daughter of Saul had no child until the day she died.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Greek reads "about seventy thousand" young men, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "thirty thousand."
- **6:2** — The Greek renders the Hebrew place name Baale-judah as "from the rulers of Judah in an ascent" and translates the divine title as "Lord of powers" (Kyrios ton dynameon, also in 6:18; MT: "Lord of hosts"). In 6:3, the Greek reads "to the house of Amminadab" (MT: "from the house of Abinadab"; cf. 1 Sam 7:1) and translates the Hebrew name Ahio as "his brothers" (also in 6:4).
- **6:6** — The Greek gives the distinct proper name Nodab (MT: Nachon) and reads "because the calf pulled him aside, in order to hold it" (repeating "to hold it"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "because the oxen stumbled."
- **6:10** — The Greek gives the distinct proper name Abeddara (vv. 10-12) for the Hebrew Masoretic Text's Obed-edom, and explicitly calls the ark "the ark of the covenant of the Lord."
- **6:13** — The Greek describes "seven choirs" carrying the ark with a sacrifice of a calf and a lamb (and in 6:14 "an unusual robe" rather than "a linen ephod"), differing substantially from the Masoretic Text's "six steps." In 6:22, the Greek reads "in your eyes" (MT: "in my own eyes") and a condensed indirect-speech clause echoing Michal's taunt in 6:20.
