## Text

**3:1** And the war continued for a long time between the house of Saul and the house of David; and the house of David kept going on and growing stronger, and the house of Saul kept going on and growing weaker.

**3:2** And sons were born to David in Hebron; and his firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Israelite,

**3:3** and his second, Daluia, by Abigail the Carmelite, and the third, Absalom son of Maacah, daughter of Talmai king of Geshur,

**3:4** and the fourth, Orneil son of Pheggith, and the fifth, Shephatiah, by Abital,

**3:5** and the sixth, Ithream, by Eglah, wife of David. These were born to David in Hebron.

**3:6** And it happened, while the war was going on between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abner was holding power over the house of Saul.

**3:7** And Saul had a concubine, Rizpah daughter of Ial; and Mephibosheth son of Saul said to Abner, "Why did you go in to my father's concubine?"

**3:8** And Abner became very angry about Mephibosheth's word, and Abner said to him, "Am I a dog's head? I have shown mercy today to the house of Saul your father, and toward brothers and acquaintances, and I have not defected to the house of David; and do you call me to account concerning a woman's wrongdoing today?

**3:9** May God do so to Abner and add still more to him, for just as the Lord swore to David, surely so I will do for him on this day:

**3:10** to remove the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to establish the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan to Beersheba."

**3:11** And Mephibosheth could no longer answer Abner a word, because he feared him.

**3:12** And Abner sent messengers to David at Thailam, where he was, immediately, saying, "Make your covenant with me, and look, my hand is with you to turn the whole house of Israel to you."

**3:13** And David said, "Very well, I will make a covenant with you; only one thing I ask from you, saying, 'You will not see my face unless you bring Michal daughter of Saul when you come to see my face.'"

**3:14** And David sent messengers to Mephibosheth son of Saul, saying, "Give back to me my wife Michal, whom I took for one hundred foreskins of foreigners."

**3:15** And Mephibosheth sent and took her from her husband, from Paltiel son of Selles.

**3:16** And her husband went with her, weeping behind her as far as Baraki; and Abner said to him, "Go, turn back." And he turned back.

**3:17** And Abner spoke to the elders of Israel, saying, "Yesterday and the day before you were seeking David to reign over you.

**3:18** And now do it, for the Lord spoke concerning David, saying, 'By the hand of My servant David I will save Israel from the hand of foreigners and from the hand of all their enemies.'"

**3:19** And Abner spoke in the ears of Benjamin; and Abner went to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that was pleasing in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of the whole house of Benjamin.

**3:20** And Abner came to David in Hebron, and with him twenty men; and David made a banquet for Abner and for the men who were with him.

**3:21** And Abner said to David, "I will now arise and go, and I will gather all Israel to my lord the king, and I will make a covenant with him, and you will reign over all that your soul desires." And David sent Abner away, and they went in peace.

**3:22** And look, the servants of David and Joab were arriving from the raid, and they brought much plunder with them; and Abner was not with David in Hebron, because he had sent him away and he had departed in peace.

**3:23** And Joab and all his army arrived, and it was reported to Joab, saying, "Abner son of Ner has come to David, and he has sent him away, and he went away in peace."

**3:24** And Joab went in to the king and said, "What is this you have done? Look, Abner came to you, and why have you sent him away, and he has departed in peace?

**3:25** Or do you not know the wickedness of Abner son of Ner, that he came to deceive you, and to know your going out and your coming in, and to know everything that you do?"

**3:26** And Joab turned back from David and sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the well of Sirah; and David did not know.

**3:27** And he brought Abner back to Hebron, and Joab turned him aside to the side of the gate to speak to him, lying in ambush; and he struck him there in the loin, and he died for the blood of Asahel, Joab's brother.

**3:28** And David heard after these things and said, "I and my kingdom are innocent before the Lord and forever of the bloodshed of Abner son of Ner.

**3:29** May it come down upon the head of Joab and upon all his father's house, and may there never cease from the house of Joab one who has a discharge, or a leper, or one who grasps a staff, or one who falls by the sword, or one who lacks bread."

**3:30** Now Joab and Abishai his brother were keeping close watch on Abner, because he had put to death Asahel their brother in Gibeon in the war.

**3:31** And David said to Joab and to all the people who were with him, "Tear your garments and wrap yourselves in sackcloth and mourn before Abner." And King David walked behind the bier.

**3:32** And they buried Abner in Hebron; and the king lifted up his voice and wept at his tomb, and all the people wept over Abner.

**3:33** And the king lamented over Abner and said, "Should Abner die like the death of Nabal?

**3:34** Your hands were not bound, your feet were not in fetters; he did not draw near like Nabal, before sons of injustice you fell." And all the people gathered together to weep for him.

**3:35** And all the people came to serve David a funeral meal of bread while it was still day; and David swore, saying, "May God do so to me and add still more, for unless the sun goes down, I will by no means taste bread or anything at all."

**3:36** And all the people took note, and everything that the king did before the people was pleasing before them.

**3:37** And all the people and all Israel knew on that day that it did not come from the king to put Abner son of Ner to death.

**3:38** And the king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a great leader has fallen on this day in Israel?

**3:39** And that I am today a relative and appointed by a king? But these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too harsh for me; may the Lord repay the one who does evil according to his wickedness."

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek reads Daluia (MT: Chileab), just as 3:2 reads 'the Israelite' (MT: 'the Jezreelitess') and 3:4 reads Orneil son of Pheggith (MT: Adonijah son of Haggith).
- **3:7** — Throughout chapters 3-4 the Greek calls Saul's son Mephibosheth (MT: Ish-bosheth), the same name as Jonathan's son in 4:4; the Greek also preserves distinct names Ial (MT: Aiah), Thailam in 3:12 (MT: 'where he was' or 'on the spot'), Selles in 3:15 (MT: Laish), and Baraki in 3:16 (MT: Bahurim).
- **3:30** — The Greek reads 'were keeping close watch on' (diepareterounto), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'killed'; similarly, in 3:14 and 3:18 'foreigners' (allophyloi) is the Septuagint's regular term for the Philistines.
- **3:34** — In 3:33-34 the Greek renders Hebrew nabal ('fool') as the proper name Nabal, reading 'he did not draw near like Nabal' (MT: 'were not put [into fetters]; as one falls'), and reads 'gathered together' where MT has 'wept again'.
- **3:39** — The Greek reads 'a relative and appointed by a king' (syngenes kai kathestamenos hypo basileos), differing notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'weak, though anointed king'.
