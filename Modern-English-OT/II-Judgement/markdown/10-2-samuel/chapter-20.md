## Text

**20:1** And there was one called a lawless man, and his name was Sheba son of Bichri, a Jeminite man; and he blew the horn and said, "We have no portion in David, nor do we have an inheritance in the son of Jesse! Each man to your tents, Israel!"

**20:2** And all Israel went up from behind David, after Sheba son of Bichri; but the men of Judah clung to their king from the Jordan even to Jerusalem.

**20:3** And David went into his house in Jerusalem. And the king took the ten women, his concubines, whom he had left to guard the house, and put them in a house under guard, and he provided for them, yet he did not go in to them. And they were confined until the day of their death, living widows.

**20:4** And the king said to Amasa, "Summon the men of Judah for me within three days, and you stand here."

**20:5** And Amasa went to summon Judah, but he delayed beyond the time that he had appointed for him.

**20:6** And David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom; and now you take with yourself the servants of your lord and pursue after him, so that he may not find fortified cities for himself and overshadow our eyes."

**20:7** And Abishai went out after him, and the men of Joab, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and all the mighty men; and they went out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:8** And they were beside the great stone that is in Gibeon, and Amasa came in front of them. And Joab was belted in a cloak, his garment, and over it he was belted with a sword fastened upon his waist in its sheath; and the sword came out, and it came out and fell.

**20:9** And Joab said to Amasa, "Are you well, brother?" And the right hand of Joab took hold of Amasa's beard to kiss him.

**20:10** And Amasa was not on guard against the sword that was in Joab's hand; and Joab struck him with it in the abdomen, and his entrails poured out onto the ground, and he did not strike him a second time, and he died. And Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:11** And a man from Joab's attendants stood over him and said, "Who favors Joab, and who is for David? After Joab!"

**20:12** And Amasa was drenched in blood in the middle of the road, and a man saw that all the people were standing still; and they removed Amasa from the road into a field, and he threw a garment over him, because he saw everyone who came up to him standing still.

**20:13** And when he was gone from the road, all the men of Israel passed on after Joab to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:14** And he passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and to Beth-maacah, and all in Charri, and they were assembled; and he came after him.

**20:15** And they arrived and besieged him in Abel of Beth-maacah, and they cast up a siege mound against the city, and it stood at the outer wall; and all the people who were with Joab were planning to throw down the wall.

**20:16** And a wise woman cried out from the wall and said, "Listen, listen! Please say to Joab, 'Come near to here,' and I will speak to him."

**20:17** And he came near to her, and the woman said, "Are you Joab?" And he said, "I am." And she said to him, "Listen to the words of your maidservant." And Joab said, "I myself am listening."

**20:18** And she spoke, saying, "They spoke a saying in former times, saying, 'Surely one was asked in Abel and in Dan whether the things that the faithful of Israel established had failed; they will surely inquire of one in Abel, and so, whether they failed.'

**20:19** I am peaceful among the supports of Israel, yet you seek to put to death a city and a mother city in Israel. Why are you engulfing the inheritance of the Lord?"

**20:20** And Joab answered and said, "Far be it from me, far be it from me, that I should engulf or that I should destroy!

**20:21** This is not the matter; for a man from the hill country of Ephraim, Sheba son of Bichri by name, has lifted up his hand against King David. Give him to me alone, and I will depart away from the city." And the woman said to Joab, "Look, his head will be thrown to you through the wall."

**20:22** And the woman went in to all the people, and she spoke to all the city in her wisdom; and she cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri, and took it away and threw it to Joab. And he blew the horn, and they dispersed from the city, away from him, each man to his tents. And Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.

**20:23** And Joab was over all the army of Israel, and Benaiah son of Ahilud was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites,

**20:24** and Adoniram was over the tribute, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder,

**20:25** and Joshua was the scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar were priests,

**20:26** and Ira the Jairite was also a priest of David.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — Greek reads "overshadow our eyes" (skiasei tous ophthalmous hemon), reflecting a Hebrew root meaning "to shade" rather than the Masoretic Text's "escape from our sight."
- **20:14** — The Greek reads the place name "Charri" (en Charrei) and a singular verb "he came," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to "the Berites" (or Bichrites) and has a plural verb.
- **20:18** — The Septuagint preserves a much longer form of the proverb than the Hebrew Masoretic Text, explicitly mentioning "and in Dan" and "whether the things that the faithful of Israel established had failed."
- **20:23** — The Greek text names Benaiah's father as "Ahilud" (Acheilouth, matching verse 24), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jehoiada."
- **20:25** — The Greek text names the scribe as "Joshua" (Greek Iesous), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Sheva" (Kethib "Sheya").
