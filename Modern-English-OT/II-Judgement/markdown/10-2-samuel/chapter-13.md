## Text

**13:1** And it happened after these things that Absalom son of David had a sister very beautiful in appearance, and her name was Tamar, and Amnon son of David loved her.

**13:2** And Amnon was so distressed that he became ill because of Tamar his sister, for she was a virgin, and it was too difficult in Amnon's eyes to do anything to her.

**13:3** And Amnon had a friend, and his name was Jonadab, son of Shammah, David's brother; and Jonadab was a very wise man.

**13:4** And he said to him, "What is the matter with you, that you are so weak, son of the king, morning by morning? Will you not tell me?" And Amnon said to him, "I love Tamar, the sister of Absalom my brother."

**13:5** And Jonadab said to him, "Lie down on your bed and act sick, and your father will come in to see you, and you will say to him, 'Please let Tamar my sister come and feed me, and prepare food before my eyes, so that I may see and eat from her hands.'"

**13:6** And Amnon lay down and became ill, and the king came in to see him. And Amnon said to the king, "Please let Tamar my sister come to me and make two cakes before my eyes, and I will eat from her hand."

**13:7** And David sent to Tamar at the house, saying, "Please go to your brother's house and prepare food for him."

**13:8** And Tamar went to the house of Amnon her brother, and he was lying down. And she took the dough and kneaded it and made cakes before his eyes, and cooked the cakes.

**13:9** And she took the pan and emptied it out before him, but he was not willing to eat. And Amnon said, "Send out every man from before me." And they sent out every man from before him.

**13:10** And Amnon said to Tamar, "Bring the food into the inner room, and I will eat from your hand." And Tamar took the cakes that she had made and brought them to Amnon her brother into the bedroom.

**13:11** And she brought them near to him to eat, and he seized her and said to her, "Come, lie with me, my sister."

**13:12** And she said to him, "No, my brother, do not humiliate me, for it is not to be done like this in Israel; do not do this folly.

**13:13** And as for me, where will I carry my disgrace? And you will be like one of the fools in Israel. And now, please speak to the king, for he will certainly not withhold me from you."

**13:14** But Amnon was not willing to listen to her voice, and he overpowered her and humiliated her, and lay with her.

**13:15** And Amnon hated her with a very great hatred, for great was the hatred with which he hated her, the last evil greater than the first, beyond the love with which he had loved her. And Amnon said to her, "Get up and go."

**13:16** And Tamar spoke to him concerning this great evil, "Greater than the other that you did with me is sending me away." But Amnon was not willing to listen to her voice.

**13:17** And he called his young man who was in charge of the house and said to him, "Please send this one out away from me, and bolt the door behind her."

**13:18** Now upon her was a long-sleeved tunic, for in this way the virgin daughters of the king used to wear their outer garments. And his attendant brought her outside and bolted the door behind her.

**13:19** And Tamar took ashes and put ashes on her head, and tore the long-sleeved tunic that was on her, and put her hands on her head, and went away, walking and crying out.

**13:20** And Absalom her brother said to her, "Has Amnon your brother been with you? Now then, my sister, keep silent, for he is your brother; do not set your heart to speak about this matter." And Tamar remained in widowhood in the house of Absalom her brother.

**13:21** And King David heard all these matters and grew exceedingly angry; yet he did not grieve the spirit of Amnon his son, because he loved him, because he was his firstborn.

**13:22** And Absalom did not speak with Amnon either bad or good, for Absalom hated Amnon on account of the matter that he had humiliated Tamar his sister.

**13:23** And it happened after two years of days that there were shearers for Absalom in Baal-hazor, which is near Ephraim, and Absalom invited all the king's sons.

**13:24** And Absalom came to the king and said, "See now, they are shearing for your servant; please let the king and his servants go with your servant."

**13:25** And the king said to Absalom, "No, please, my son, let not all of us go, and we will certainly not become a burden on you." And he pressed him, yet he was not willing to go, and he blessed him.

**13:26** And Absalom said, "And if not, please let Amnon my brother go with us." And the king said to him, "Why should he go with you?"

**13:27** But Absalom pressed him, and he sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him. And Absalom made a banquet like the banquet of the king.

**13:28** And Absalom commanded his young men, saying, "Watch: when Amnon's heart is gladdened with wine and I speak to you, strike down Amnon and put him to death. Do not be afraid, for am I not the one who commands you? Be courageous and be sons of strength."

**13:29** And Absalom's young men did to Amnon just as Absalom had commanded them. And all the king's sons arose, and each man mounted his mule, and they fled.

**13:30** And it happened while they were on the way that the report came to David, saying, "Absalom has struck down all the king's sons, and not even one of them has been left."

**13:31** And the king arose and tore his garments and lay down on the ground, and all his servants who were standing around him tore their garments.

**13:32** And Jonadab son of Shammah, David's brother, answered and said, "Let not my lord the king say that he has put to death all the young men, the king's sons, for Amnon alone has died; for by the mouth of Absalom he was appointed from the day he humiliated Tamar his sister.

**13:33** And now let not my lord the king set a word upon his heart, saying, 'All the king's sons have died,' for only Amnon alone has died."

**13:34** And Absalom fled away. And the young man, the watchman, lifted up his eyes and saw, and look, many people were coming along the road behind him from the side of the mountain in the descent. And the watchman arrived and reported to the king and said, "I have seen men from the road of Oronen, from the side of the mountain."

**13:35** And Jonadab said to the king, "Look, the king's sons are here; according to the word of your servant, so it has happened."

**13:36** And it happened when he finished speaking that, look, the king's sons came, and they lifted up their voice and wept; and indeed the king and all his servants wept with a great weeping.

**13:37** Now Absalom fled and went to Tholmailem son of Ammihud, king of Geshur, to Machad. And King David mourned for his son all the days.

**13:38** And Absalom fled away and went to Geshur, and he was there three years.

**13:39** And King David ceased from going out to Absalom, for he was comforted concerning Amnon, because he had died.

## Translator Notes

- **13:15** — Greek includes the clause "the last evil greater than the first" (absent from the Masoretic Text), anticipating Tamar's protest in 13:16, where Greek introduces her speech with "concerning this great evil."
- **13:21** — Greek adds "yet he did not grieve the spirit of Amnon his son, because he loved him, because he was his firstborn," a reading supported by 4QSam^a but absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **13:27** — Greek adds "And Absalom made a banquet like the banquet of the king," a plus attested in 4QSam^a but absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **13:34** — Greek includes the watchman's report naming "the road of Oronen" (probably Horonaim or Beth-horon), a plus supported by 4QSam^a and missing from the Masoretic Text.
- **13:37** — Greek spells the king's name "Tholmailem" (corresponding to Hebrew "Talmai") and adds the place name "to Machad" (absent from the Masoretic Text).
- **13:39** — Greek reads "ceased" (kopazo), giving a clear sense where the Masoretic Hebrew verb is famously debated between "ceased" and "longed."
