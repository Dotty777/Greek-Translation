## Text

**2:1** And it happened after these things that David inquired of the Lord, saying, "Shall I go up to one of the cities of Judah?" And the Lord said to him, "Go up." And David said, "Where shall I go up?" And He said, "To Hebron."

**2:2** And David went up there to Hebron, and both of his wives, Ahinoam the Israelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite,

**2:3** and the men who were with him, each one and his household; and they lived in the cities of Hebron.

**2:4** And men of Judea came and anointed David there to reign over the house of Judah. And they reported to David, saying that the men of Jabesh of Gilead had buried Saul.

**2:5** And David sent messengers to the leaders of Jabesh of Gilead, and said to them, "Blessed are you by the Lord, because you have shown the mercy of God toward your lord Saul, and buried him.

**2:6** And now may the Lord show mercy and truth with you, and I also will do this good thing with you, because you did this thing.

**2:7** And now let your hands be strengthened, and become mighty sons, for your lord Saul is dead, and moreover the house of Judah has anointed me as king over itself."

**2:8** And Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Saul, took Ish-bosheth son of Saul and brought him up from the camp to Mahanaim,

**2:9** and made him king over Gilead, and over Thaseiri, and over Israel, and over Ephraim, and over Benjamin, and over all Israel.

**2:10** Ish-bosheth son of Saul was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned two years, except for the house of Judah, who followed David.

**2:11** And the days that David reigned in Hebron over the house of Judah were seven years and six months.

**2:12** And Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon;

**2:13** and Joab son of Zeruiah and the servants of David went out from Hebron and met them at the spring of Gibeon together; and these sat down by the spring of Gibeon on one side, and those by the spring on the other side.

**2:14** And Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men arise now and play before us." And Joab said, "Let them arise."

**2:15** And they arose and came forward by number: twelve of the servants of Benjamin belonging to Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve from the servants of David.

**2:16** And each one seized with his hand the head of his fellow, with his sword into the side of his fellow, and they fell together; and the name of that place was called Portion of the Plotters, which is in Gibeon.

**2:17** And the battle became exceedingly fierce on that day; and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated before the servants of David.

**2:18** And three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab and Abishai and Asahel; and Asahel was swift on his feet like one gazelle in a field.

**2:19** And Asahel pursued after Abner, and he did not turn aside to go to the right or to the left from behind Abner.

**2:20** And Abner looked behind him and said, "Is that you yourself, Asahel?" And he said, "I am."

**2:21** And Abner said to him, "Turn aside to the right or to the left, and seize for yourself one of the young men, and take for yourself his full armor." And Asahel was not willing to turn aside from behind him.

**2:22** And Abner again spoke to Asahel, saying, "Turn away from me, so that I do not strike you to the ground; and how shall I lift up my face to Joab?

**2:23** And where are these things? Turn back to Joab your brother." And he was not willing to turn away. And Abner struck him with the back end of the spear in the loin, and the spear came out through his back, and he fell there and died underneath him; and it happened that everyone who came to the place where Asahel fell and died stood still.

**2:24** And Joab and Abishai pursued after Abner, and the sun was setting; and they came as far as the hill of Amman, which is facing Gai, by the wilderness road of Gibeon.

**2:25** And the sons of Benjamin who were behind Abner gathered together, and they became one gathering and stood on the top of one hill.

**2:26** And Abner called to Joab and said, "Will the sword devour to victory? Or do you not know that it will be bitter in the end? And how long will you not tell the people to turn back from behind our brothers?"

**2:27** And Joab said, "As the Lord lives, if you had not spoken, then surely from morning the people would have gone up, each one from behind his brother."

**2:28** And Joab blew the trumpet, and all the people turned away and did not pursue after Israel, and they did not continue any longer to fight.

**2:29** And Abner and his men went off toward the west all that night, and crossed the Jordan and marched through the entire stretch, and came to the camp.

**2:30** And Joab turned back from behind Abner and gathered all the people together, and nineteen men of the servants of David were missing.

**2:31** And Asahel and the servants of David struck down from the sons of Benjamin, from the men of Abner, three hundred and sixty men from him.

**2:32** And they took up Asahel and buried him in the tomb of his father in Bethlehem; and Joab and the men who were with him marched all night, and day dawned for them in Hebron.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — Greek identifies her as "Ahinoam the Israelite" (ἡ Ἰσραηλεῖτις), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Jezreelite."
- **2:5** — The Greek text (with Codex Vaticanus) reads "the mercy of God" (τὸ ἔλεος τοῦ θεοῦ) rather than "this kindness/mercy" found in the Hebrew text and other Greek witnesses.
- **2:9** — Greek reads the distinct proper name "Thaseiri" (Θασειρεί) and "Israel" (Ἰσραήλ), corresponding to "the Ashurites" and "Jezreel" in the Hebrew text (see also the doublet "from the camp to Mahanaim" in v. 8).
- **2:16** — Greek translates the Hebrew place name Helkath-hazzurim as "Portion of the Plotters" (Μερὶς τῶν ἐπιβούλων).
- **2:24** — Greek preserves the distinctive names "Amman" (Ἀμμάν) and "Gai" (Γαί) for Hebrew "Ammah" and "Giah"; similarly, v. 29 translates the Hebrew place names Arabah, Bithron, and Mahanaim as "the west," "the entire stretch," and "the camp."
- **2:31** — In this Greek text, "And Asahel" (καὶ Ἀσαήλ) opens v. 31 rather than concluding the tally of David's missing men at the end of v. 30, and the verse ends with "from him" (παρ᾽ αὐτοῦ) instead of "died" as in the Hebrew text.
