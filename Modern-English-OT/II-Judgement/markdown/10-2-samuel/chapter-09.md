## Text

**9:1** And David said, "Is there still anyone left of the house of Saul, that I may show mercy to him for the sake of Jonathan?"

**9:2** Now from the house of Saul there was a servant, and his name was Ziba, and they called him to David. And the king said to him, "Are you Ziba?" And he said, "I am your servant."

**9:3** And the king said, "Is there still a man left from the house of Saul, that I may show the mercy of God to him?" And Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, stricken in his feet."

**9:4** And the king said, "Where is he?" And Ziba said to the king, "Look, he is in the house of Machir son of Amaer, from Lo-debar."

**9:5** And King David sent and took him from the house of Machir son of Ammiel, from Lo-debar.

**9:6** And Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came to King David, and fell on his face and bowed down to him. And David said to him, "Mephibosheth." And he said, "Here is your servant."

**9:7** And David said to him, "Do not be afraid, for I will surely show mercy to you for the sake of Jonathan your father, and I will restore to you all the land of Saul, the father of your father, and you will eat bread at my table continually."

**9:8** And Mephibosheth bowed down and said, "Who am I, your servant, that you have looked upon a dead dog like me?"

**9:9** Then the king called Ziba, the attendant of Saul, and said to him, "Everything that belongs to Saul and to all his house I have given to the son of your master.

**9:10** And you will work the land for him, you and your sons and your servants, and you will bring in bread for the son of your master, and he will eat bread; yet Mephibosheth, the son of your master, will continually eat bread at my table." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

**9:11** And Ziba said to the king, "According to everything that my lord the king has commanded his servant, so your servant will do." And Mephibosheth ate at David's table like one of the king's own sons.

**9:12** And Mephibosheth had a young son, and his name was Micah. And the entire household of Ziba's own house were servants of Mephibosheth.

**9:13** And Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, for he ate continually at the king's table. And he was lame in both his feet.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek here spells the father's name Amaer (Amaēr), whereas v. 5 has Ammiel (Ameiēl).
- **9:11** — The Greek reads third-person narration 'at David's table' (with 'one of the king's own sons'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads first-person 'at my table.'
