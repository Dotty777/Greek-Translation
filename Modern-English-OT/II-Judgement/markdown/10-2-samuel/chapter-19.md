## Text

**19:1** And the king was troubled, and he went up to the upper chamber of the gate and wept. And thus he said as he went, "My son, my son, Absalom! Who would grant my death in place of you? I in place of you, Absalom, my son, my son!"

**19:2** And it was reported to Joab, saying, "Look, the king is weeping and mourning over Absalom."

**19:3** And the deliverance on that day turned into mourning for all the people, because the people heard on that day, saying, "The king is grieved over his son."

**19:4** And the people were creeping stealthily on that day to enter the city, just as people who are ashamed creep stealthily when they flee in battle.

**19:5** And the king hid his face, and the king cried out with a loud voice, saying, "My son Absalom, Absalom, my son!"

**19:6** And Joab went in to the king into the house and said, "You have put to shame today the face of all your servants who rescued you today, and the life of your sons and of your daughters, and the life of your wives and of your concubines,

**19:7** by loving those who hate you and hating those who love you; and you have declared today that your commanders and servants are nothing, for I know today that if Absalom were alive, all of us dead today, then that would have been right in your eyes.

**19:8** And now arise, go out, and speak to the heart of your servants, for I have sworn by the Lord that if you do not go out today, not a man will lodge with you this night; and know for yourself: this will be evil for you beyond all the evil that has come upon you from your youth until now."

**19:9** And the king arose and sat in the gate, and all the people reported, saying, "Look, the king is sitting in the gate." And all the people came in before the king. And Israel had fled, each man to his tents.

**19:10** And all the people were disputing throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, "King David delivered us from the hand, from all our enemies, and he himself rescued us from the hand of foreigners; and now he has fled from the land and from his kingdom and from Absalom.

**19:11** The one whom we anointed over us died in battle. And now why are you keeping silent about returning to the king?" And the word of all Israel came to the king.

**19:12** And King David sent to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, saying, "Speak to the elders of Judah, saying, 'Why are you becoming the last to return to the king to his house? And the word of all Israel has come to the king.

**19:13** You are brothers to me; you are my bones and my flesh. Why are you becoming the last to bring back the king to his house?'

**19:14** And to Amasa you shall say, 'Are you not my bone and my flesh? And now may God do so to me, and so also may He add, if you will not be commander of the army before me all the days in place of Joab.'"

**19:15** And he inclined the heart of all the men of Judah, even to one man; and they sent to the king, saying, "Return, you and all your servants."

**19:16** And the king returned and came as far as the Jordan; and the men of Judah came to Gilgal to go to meet the king, to bring the king across the Jordan.

**19:17** And Shimei son of Gera, son of the Jeminite, from Bahurim hurried and went down with the men of Judah to meet King David,

**19:18** and a thousand men of Benjamin were with him, and Ziba the attendant of the house of Saul, and his fifteen sons with him and his twenty servants with him; and they made straight for the Jordan before the king,

**19:19** and they performed the service of bringing the king across; and the ferry crossed over to rouse the house of the king and to do what was right in his eyes. And Shimei son of Gera fell on his face before the king as he was crossing the Jordan,

**19:20** and said to the king, "May my lord not reckon lawlessness, and do not remember all the wrong your servant did on the day when my lord was going out from Jerusalem, for the king to lay it to his heart;

**19:21** for your servant knows that I have sinned, and look, I have come today first of all the house of Joseph to come down to meet my lord the king."

**19:22** And Abishai son of Zeruiah answered and said, "Should Shimei not be put to death for this, because he cursed the anointed of the Lord?"

**19:23** And David said, "What do I have to do with you, sons of Zeruiah, that you are becoming an adversary to me today? Today no man from Israel shall be put to death; for do I not know whether today I reign over Israel?"

**19:24** And the king said to Shimei, "You shall certainly not die." And the king swore to him.

**19:25** And Mephibosheth, the son of the son of Saul, went down to meet the king; and he had not cared for his feet, nor trimmed his nails, nor groomed his mustache, and he had not washed his garments from the day the king departed until the day he himself arrived in peace.

**19:26** And it happened, when he entered Jerusalem to meet the king, that the king said to him, "Why is it that you did not go with me, Mephibosheth?"

**19:27** And Mephibosheth said to him, "My lord the king, my servant deceived me; for your servant said to him, 'Saddle the donkey for me so that I may mount her and go with the king,' because your servant is lame.

**19:28** And he schemed against your servant to my lord the king; and my lord the king did what is good openly, like an angel of God. And do what is good in your eyes;

**19:29** for all the house of my father was nothing except men of death to my lord the king, yet you set your servant among those who eat at your table. And what just claim do I still have, even to cry out any longer to the king?"

**19:30** And the king said to him, "Why do you still speak your words? I have said, 'You and Ziba shall divide the field.'"

**19:31** And Mephibosheth said to the king, "Let him even take everything, after my lord the king has arrived in peace at his house."

**19:32** And Barzillai the Gileadite came down from Rogelim and crossed the Jordan with the king to escort him across the Jordan.

**19:33** And Barzillai was a very old man, eighty years old; and he himself had provided for the king while he lived in Mahanaim, for he was a very great man.

**19:34** And the king said to Barzillai, "You shall cross over with me, and I will provide for your old age with me in Jerusalem."

**19:35** And Barzillai said to the king, "How many are the days of the years of my life, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem?

**19:36** I am eighty years old today. Can I indeed discern between good and bad, to evil? Will your servant still taste what I will eat or drink? Or will I still hear the voice of singing men and singing women? Why should your servant still be a burden upon my lord the king?

**19:37** Your servant will cross the Jordan just a short way with the king; and why is the king rewarding me with this reward?

**19:38** Please let your servant stay, and I will die in my city, beside the tomb of my father and of my mother. And look, your servant Chimham will cross over with my lord the king; and do for him what is good in your eyes."

**19:39** And the king said, "Let Chimham cross over with me, and I will do for him what is good in your eyes, and all that you choose of me I will do for you."

**19:40** And all the people crossed the Jordan, and the king crossed over; and the king kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and he returned to his place.

**19:41** And the king crossed over to Gilgal, and Chimham crossed over with him; and all the people of Judah were crossing over with the king, and also half of the people of Israel.

**19:42** And look, all the men of Israel came to the king and said to the king, "Why is it that our brothers, the men of Judah, have stolen you away, and have brought the king and his house across the Jordan, and all the men of David with him?"

**19:43** And all the men of Judah answered the men of Israel and said, "Because the king is close to me? And why have you become so angry about this matter? Have we eaten at all from the king, or has he given a gift, or lifted a portion for us?"

**19:44** And the men of Israel answered the men of Judah and said, "I have ten shares in the king, and I am firstborn rather than you, and indeed I am in David more than you. And why have you insulted me like this, and was my word not reckoned first for me ahead of Judah, to bring back the king for me?" And the word of the men of Judah was harsher than the word of the men of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **19:1** — The Septuagint begins chapter 19 here (corresponding to 18:33 in most English Bibles) and renders the Hebrew wish idiom literally as "Who would grant my death in place of you?" (Greek tis doe).
- **19:10** — The Greek text includes "David" and "and from his kingdom" (absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and regularly renders "Philistines" with allophyloi ("foreigners").
- **19:19** — The Greek includes the opening clause "and they performed the service of bringing the king across" (not in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and reads exegeirai ("to rouse") where the Masoretic Text has "to bring across."
- **19:36** — The Greek phrase "to evil" (eis poneron) is a Septuagint doublet alongside "and bad" (kai kakou), both rendering the same Hebrew expression.
- **19:44** — Greek literally reads "ten hands" (an idiom for shares or parts) and contains the notable Septuagint additions "and I am firstborn rather than you" and "ahead of Judah," which are absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
