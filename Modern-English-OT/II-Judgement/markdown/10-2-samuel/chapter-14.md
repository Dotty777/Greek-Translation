## Text

**14:1** And Joab son of Zeruiah knew that the king's heart was toward Absalom.

**14:2** And Joab sent to Tekoa, and took from there a wise woman and said to her, "Please mourn, and put on mourning garments, and do not anoint yourself with oil; and you shall be like a woman mourning over one who has died, now for many days,

**14:3** and you shall go to the king and speak to him according to this word." And Joab put the words in her mouth.

**14:4** And the Tekoite woman went in to the king, and fell on her face to the ground and bowed down to him and said, "Save, king, save!"

**14:5** And the king said to her, "What is the matter with you?" And she said, "Truly indeed I am a widow woman, and my husband died.

**14:6** And indeed your female servant had two sons, and both of them fought in the field, and there was no one rescuing between them, and the one struck the other, his brother, and put him to death.

**14:7** And look, the whole clan has risen up against your female servant and they said, 'Give over the one who struck his brother, and we will put him to death for the life of his brother whom he killed, and we will remove even your heir.' And they will extinguish my coal that is left, so as not to leave for my husband a remnant and a name upon the face of the earth."

**14:8** And the king said, "Go in good health to your house, and I myself will give orders concerning you."

**14:9** And the Tekoite woman said to the king, "Upon me, my lord the king, be the lawlessness, and upon the house of my father, and the king and his throne are innocent."

**14:10** And the king said, "Who is the one speaking to you? And you shall bring him to me, and he will not again touch him."

**14:11** And she said, "Please let the king remember the Lord his God, that the kinsman of blood is multiplied to destroy, and they will by no means remove my son." And he said, "As the Lord lives, not a hair of your son shall fall to the ground."

**14:12** And she said, "Please let your female servant speak a word to my lord the king." And he said, "Speak."

**14:13** And the woman said, "Why have you devised such a thing against the people of God? Or is this word from the mouth of the king like a trespass, in that the king does not bring back his banished one?

**14:14** For we will surely die, and are like the water that is wasted upon the ground, which will not be gathered up; and God will take a life, and is devising to banish from Himself a banished one.

**14:15** And now, that I have come to speak this word to the king my lord is because the people will see me, and your people will say, 'Please let her speak to the king; perhaps the king will do the word of his female servant,'

**14:16** for the king will listen; let him deliver his female servant from the hand of the man seeking to remove me and my son from the inheritance of God."

**14:17** And the woman said, "Please let the word of my lord the king be for sacrifices, for just as an angel of God, so is my lord the king to hear the good and the evil; and the Lord your God will be with you."

**14:18** And the king answered and said to the woman, "Please do not hide from me a word that I am asking you." And the woman said, "Please let my lord the king speak."

**14:19** And the king said, "Is the hand of Joab with you in all this?" And the woman said to the king, "As your soul lives, my lord the king, there is nothing to the right or to the left from all that my lord the king has spoken; for your servant Joab himself commanded me, and he himself put all these words in the mouth of your female servant,

**14:20** in order to turn around the face of this matter which your servant Joab has done, this deceit. And my lord is wise, like the wisdom of an angel of God, to know all things that are in the earth."

**14:21** And the king said to Joab, "Look now, I have done for you according to this word of yours; bring back the young man Absalom."

**14:22** And Joab fell on his face to the ground and bowed down and blessed the king; and Joab said, "Today your servant knows that I have found favor in your eyes, my lord the king, because my lord the king has done the word of his servant."

**14:23** And Joab arose and went to Geshur, and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.

**14:24** And the king said, "Let him turn back to his house, and let him not see my face." And Absalom turned back to his house, and he did not see the king's face.

**14:25** And like Absalom there was no man in all Israel so exceedingly praiseworthy; from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head there was no blemish in him.

**14:26** And when he sheared his head, and it happened from the beginning of days to days whenever he sheared it, because it grew heavy upon him, then shearing it, he weighed the hair of his head at two hundred shekels by the royal shekel.

**14:27** And to Absalom were born three sons and one daughter, and her name was Tamar; she was a very beautiful woman, and she became wife to Rehoboam son of Solomon and bore to him Abiathar.

**14:28** And Absalom lived in Jerusalem two years of days, and he did not see the king's face.

**14:29** And Absalom sent to Joab to send him to the king, and he was not willing to come to him; and he sent a second time to him, and he was not willing to come.

**14:30** And Absalom said to his servants, "See, Joab's portion in the field is next to mine, and he has barley there; go and burn it with fire." And Absalom's servants burned it, the portion. And Joab's servants came to him with their garments torn and said, "Absalom's servants have burned the portion with fire."

**14:31** And Joab arose and came to Absalom at the house, and said to him, "Why have your servants burned my portion with fire?"

**14:32** And Absalom said to Joab, "Look, I sent to you, saying, 'Come here, and I will send you to the king, saying, "Why did I come from Geshur? It was good for me to be there."' And now, look, I have not seen the king's face; and if there is unrighteousness in me, then put me to death."

**14:33** And Joab went in to the king and reported to him, and he called Absalom. And he went in to the king and bowed down to him, and fell on his face to the ground and before the face of the king. And the king kissed Absalom.

## Translator Notes

- **14:11** — The Greek omits any negation before "is multiplied" (plethynthenai), reading literally that the kinsman of blood is multiplied to destroy, whereas the Hebrew MT implies restraining the avenger of blood from multiplying destruction.
- **14:14** — Unlike the Hebrew MT, which negates both clauses ("God does not take away life" and "that a banished one not remain banished"), the Greek omits both negations and states that God will take a life and is devising to banish a banished one from Himself.
- **14:15** — The Greek reads "the people will see me" (reflecting a root meaning "see" rather than MT "fear") and "your people will say" (where the MT has "your maidservant said").
- **14:17** — The Greek reads "for sacrifices" (eis thysias), reflecting a reading of Hebrew minhah ("offering") rather than MT menuhah ("rest/comfort").
- **14:27** — The closing clause ("and she became wife to Rehoboam son of Solomon and bore to him Abiathar") is a Septuagint addition absent from the Hebrew MT and names Rehoboam's son Abiathar rather than Abijam or Abijah (compare 1 Kings 15:1; 2 Chronicles 11:20).
