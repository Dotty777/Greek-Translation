## Text

**24:1** And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shiloh, and he called together their elders and their scribes and their judges, and he stationed them before God.

**24:2** And Joshua said to all the people, "This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: 'Beyond the river your fathers lived from the beginning, Terah the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor, and they served other gods.

**24:3** And I took your father Abraham from beyond the river, and I led him through all the land; and I multiplied his seed and gave him Isaac,

**24:4** and to Isaac, Jacob and Esau; I gave to Esau Mount Seir for him to inherit. And Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt, and there they became a great and numerous and mighty nation; and the Egyptians afflicted them.'

**24:5** And they struck Egypt with what they did to them. And after these things He brought out

**24:6** our fathers from Egypt, and you entered the Red Sea; and the Egyptians pursued after our fathers with chariots and with horses into the Red Sea.

**24:7** And we cried out to the Lord; and He put a cloud and darkness between us and the Egyptians, and He brought the sea upon them and covered them. And your eyes saw all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt; and you were in the wilderness many days.

**24:8** And He brought us into the land of the Amorites who lived beyond the Jordan, and the Lord has handed them over into our hands; and you inherited their land, and you utterly destroyed them from before you.

**24:9** And Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and arrayed himself for battle against Israel, and he sent and called Balaam to curse us.

**24:10** And the Lord your God was not willing to destroy you; and He blessed us with a blessing, and He rescued us from their hands, and He handed them over.

**24:11** And you crossed the Jordan, and you came to Jericho; and those who lived in Jericho fought against us, the Amorite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hivite and the Jebusite and the Hittite and the Girgashite, and the Lord handed them over into our hands.

**24:12** And He sent out the hornet ahead of us, and He sent them away from before us, twelve kings of the Amorites, not with your sword nor with your bow.

**24:13** And He gave you a land on which you did not labor, and cities that you have not built, and you were settled in them; and from vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant, you yourselves will eat.

**24:14** And now fear the Lord and serve Him in uprightness and in righteousness, and remove the foreign gods whom our fathers served beyond the river and in Egypt, and serve the Lord.

**24:15** But if it does not please you to serve the Lord, choose for yourselves today whom you will serve, whether the gods of your fathers, those beyond the river, or the gods of the Amorites, among whom you live on their land; but I and my household will serve the Lord, because He is holy."

**24:16** And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to abandon the Lord, so as to serve other gods.

**24:17** The Lord our God, He is God; He brought us and our fathers up from Egypt, and preserved us along all the way in which we went, and among all the nations through whom we passed;

**24:18** and the Lord drove out the Amorite and all the nations living in the land from before us. Indeed, we also will serve the Lord, for He is our God."

**24:19** And Joshua said to the people, "You will by no means be able to serve the Lord, because He is a holy God, and being jealous, He will not forgive your sins and your lawless deeds.

**24:20** Whenever you abandon the Lord and serve other gods, He will come and afflict you and consume you, instead of how He did good to you."

**24:21** And the people said to Joshua, "No, but we will serve the Lord."

**24:22** And Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you yourselves have chosen the Lord, to serve Him.

**24:23** And now remove the foreign gods that are among you, and direct your heart to the Lord, the God of Israel."

**24:24** And the people said to Joshua, "We will serve the Lord, and we will listen to His voice."

**24:25** And Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day, and he gave them a law and a judgment in Shiloh before the tent of the God of Israel.

**24:26** And he wrote these words in a book, the law of God; and he took a great stone, and Joshua set it up under the terebinth before the Lord.

**24:27** And Joshua said to the people, "Look, this stone will be among you as a witness, because it has heard all the things spoken to it by the Lord, what He spoke to us today; and it will be among you as a witness in the last days, whenever you lie to the Lord my God."

**24:28** And Joshua sent the people away, and they went, each one to his own place.

**24:29** And Israel served the Lord all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who prolonged their time with Joshua and who saw all the works of the Lord, all that He did for Israel.

**24:30** And it happened after those things that Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died, one hundred and ten years old.

**24:31** And they buried him by the borders of his inheritance in Timnath-serah, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gilead; there they placed with him in the tomb in which they buried him the stone knives with which he circumcised the sons of Israel in Gilgal, when he brought them out from Egypt, just as the Lord commanded them; and they are there to this day.

**24:32** And the sons of Israel brought up the bones of Joseph from Egypt and buried them in Shechem, in the portion of the field that Jacob bought from the Amorites who lived in Shechem for one hundred ewe lambs.

**24:33** And it happened after these things that Eleazar son of Aaron, the high priest, died and was buried in Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which he gave to him in the hill country of Ephraim. On that day the sons of Israel took the ark of God and carried it around among themselves; and Phinehas served as priest in place of Eleazar his father until he died, and he was buried in their own Gibeah. And the sons of Israel went away, each one to their place and to their own city. And the sons of Israel worshiped Astarte and Ashtaroth and the gods of the nations around them; and the Lord handed them over into the hands of Eglon, the king of Moab, and he ruled over them eighteen years.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Greek text places the assembly at Shiloh (also in v. 25), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Shechem.
- **24:5** — Here the Greek shifts from first-person divine speech (vv. 3-4) to third-person narration about the Lord (continuing through v. 13 alongside first-person plural 'our' and 'we'), omits the mention of Moses and Aaron, and reads third-person plural verbs ('they struck... they did').
- **24:12** — The Greek reads 'twelve kings of the Amorites,' whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'two kings of the Amorites.'
- **24:31** — The Greek reads 'Mount Gilead' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Mount Gaash,' arranges vv. 29-31 in a different order, and preserves an additional notice that the stone circumcision knives were buried in Joshua's tomb.
- **24:33** — The Greek concludes the book with an expanded ending—absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text—recounting the ark, Phinehas's priesthood and burial, Israel's worship of Astarte and Ashtaroth, and eighteen years of rule by Eglon of Moab (cf. Judges 3:12-14).
