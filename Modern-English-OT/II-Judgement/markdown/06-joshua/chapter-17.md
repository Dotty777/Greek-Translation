## Text

**17:1** And the borders of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh were, because he was the firstborn to Joseph, for Machir, the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead—for he was a man of war—in the region of Gilead and in the region of Bashan.

**17:2** And they were for the remaining sons of Manasseh according to their clans: for the sons of Jezei, and for the sons of Kelez, and for the sons of Jezeiel, and for the sons of Shechem, and for the sons of Symareim, and for the sons of Hepher. These are the males according to their clans.

**17:3** And as for Zelophehad son of Hepher, he had no sons, only daughters; and these are the names of the daughters of Zelophehad: Mahlah and Noah and Hoglah and Milcah and Tirzah.

**17:4** And they stood before Eleazar the priest and before Joshua and before the rulers, saying, "God commanded through the hand of Moses to give us an inheritance in the midst of our brothers." And a lot was given to them by the command of the Lord among the brothers of their father.

**17:5** And their measured portion fell from Anassa, and the plain of Labek from Gilead, which is beyond the Jordan;

**17:6** because the daughters of the sons of Manasseh inherited a lot in the midst of their brothers. And the land of Gilead belonged to the sons of Manasseh who were left.

**17:7** And the borders of the sons of Manasseh were Delanath, which is facing the sons of Anath, and they proceed to the borders, to Hiamein and Jasseib, to the spring of Thaphthoth.

**17:8** It will belong to Manasseh, and Thapheth on the borders of Manasseh to the sons of Ephraim.

**17:9** And the borders will go down to the ravine of Karana to the south, by the ravine of Jariel, a terebinth for Ephraim in the midst of a city of Manasseh; and the borders of Manasseh are on the north to the brook. And its termination will be the sea,

**17:10** on the south for Ephraim, and on the north for Manasseh; and the sea will be borders for them; and they will meet Jaseb on the north, and Issachar on the east.

**17:11** And Manasseh will have in Issachar and in Asher Kaithoan and their villages, and the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages, and the third part of Mapheta and its villages.

**17:12** And the sons of Manasseh were not able to utterly destroy these cities; and the Canaanite began to live in this land.

**17:13** And it happened that when the sons of Israel grew strong, they made the Canaanites subjects, but they did not utterly destroy them at all.

**17:14** Now the sons of Joseph spoke back to Joshua, saying, "Why have you given us one lot and one measured portion as an inheritance? Yet I am a numerous people, and God has blessed me."

**17:15** And Joshua said to them, "If you are a numerous people, go up into the forest and clear it for yourself, if the mountain of Ephraim is too narrow for you."

**17:16** And they said, "The mountain of Ephraim does not please us; and choice cavalry and iron belong to the Canaanite who lives in it, in Beth-shean and in its villages, in the valley of Israel."

**17:17** And Joshua said to the sons of Joseph, "If you are a numerous people and have great strength, you will not have one lot;

**17:18** for the forest will be yours, because it is a forest, and you will clear it, and it will be yours even when you utterly destroy the Canaanite, because he has choice cavalry; for you are stronger than he is."

## Translator Notes

- **17:2** — The Greek preserves distinct forms for several Manassite clans: Jezei (Hebrew: "Abiezer"), Kelez (Hebrew: "Helek"), Jezeiel (Hebrew: "Asriel"), and Symareim (Hebrew: "Shemida," listed before Hepher).
- **17:5** — The Septuagint diverges markedly here, reading "their measured portion fell from Anassa, and the plain of Labek from Gilead" (taking Hebrew "Manasseh" and "besides" as the proper names Anassa and Labek) instead of the Hebrew "ten portions fell to Manasseh besides the land of Gilead and Bashan."
- **17:7** — The Greek preserves several distinct place names in 17:7-8: Delanath (for Hebrew "from Asher to Michmethath"), Anath (Hebrew: "Shechem"), Hiamein and Jasseib (transliterating Hebrew "southward" and "the inhabitants of" as proper names), and Thaphthoth and Thapheth (Hebrew: "Tappuah").
- **17:9** — The Greek reads "a terebinth for Ephraim in the midst of a city of Manasseh" (reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew for "these [cities]"), alongside the distinct names Karana (Hebrew: "Kanah"), Jariel, and Jaseb in 17:10 (Hebrew: "Asher").
- **17:11** — This shorter Septuagint list reads Kaithoan (Hebrew: "Beth-shean") and "the third part of Mapheta" (rendering Hebrew "the three heights [Naphath]" as "the third part" with a distinct place name).
- **17:16** — The Greek reads "choice cavalry and iron" (Hebrew: "chariots of iron") and "the valley of Israel" (Hebrew: "the valley of Jezreel"); in 17:18 it similarly reads "choice cavalry" and concludes "for you are stronger than he is" (Hebrew: "though he is strong").
