## Text

**3:1** And Joshua rose early in the morning and set out from Shittim; and they came as far as the Jordan, and they lodged there before crossing over.

**3:2** And it happened after three days that the scribes passed through the camp

**3:3** and commanded the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord our God, and our priests and the Levites carrying it, you shall set out from your places and go after it.

**3:4** However, let there be a distance between us and it; you shall stand about two thousand cubits away; do not approach it, so that you may know the way that you are going; for you have not gone the way from yesterday and the third day."

**3:5** And Joshua said to the people, "Purify yourselves for tomorrow, because tomorrow the Lord will do wondrous things among us."

**3:6** And Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and go ahead of the people." And the priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and went in front of the people.

**3:7** And the Lord spoke to Joshua, "On this day I begin to exalt you before all the sons of Israel, so that they may know: just as I was with Moses, so I will also be with you.

**3:8** And now command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant, saying, 'As soon as you enter into the middle of the water of the Jordan, then in the Jordan you shall stand.'"

**3:9** And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Draw near here and listen to the word of the Lord our God.

**3:10** By this you shall know that a living God is among you, and He will surely destroy from before us the Canaanite and the Hittite and the Perizzite and the Hivite and the Amorite and the Girgashite and the Jebusite.

**3:11** Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing the Jordan.

**3:12** Choose for yourselves twelve men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe.

**3:13** And it shall be, as soon as the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth come to rest in the water of the Jordan, the water of the Jordan will fail, and the water coming down will stand."

**3:14** And the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, and the priests carried the ark of the covenant ahead of the people.

**3:15** And as the priests carrying the ark of the covenant were entering into the Jordan, and the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord were dipped into a part of the water of the Jordan (now the Jordan was full along its entire bank, as in days of wheat harvest),

**3:16** then the waters coming down from above stood; they stood as one solid mass, standing off very, exceedingly far, as far as a part of Kathiairein, while that which was coming down went down into the sea of Arabah, the Sea of Salt, until it failed completely; and the people stood opposite Jericho.

**3:17** And the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan; and all the sons of Israel were crossing over through dry ground, until all the people finished crossing the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Greek distinguishes "our priests and the Levites" (and reads "the Lord our God") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the Levitical priests" and "the Lord your God."
- **3:4** — "From yesterday and the third day" literally renders the Septuagint idiom (ἀπ᾽ ἐχθὲς καὶ τρίτης ἡμέρας), reflecting the Hebrew expression meaning "previously" or "before."
- **3:8** — The Greek text here reads "into the middle of" (ἐπὶ μέσου) the water of the Jordan, differing from the variant "into a part of" (ἐπὶ μέρους) and the Hebrew Masoretic Text "to the edge of."
- **3:16** — "Kathiairein" preserves the distinct Greek place name (related to Kiriath-jearim) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "at Adam, the city beside Zarethan"; the Greek also closes the verse with the people "stood" (ἱστήκει) opposite Jericho rather than "crossed over."
