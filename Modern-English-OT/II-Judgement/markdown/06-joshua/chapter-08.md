## Text

**8:1** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear nor lose heart; take with you all the men of war, and rise up and go up to Ai. Look, I have given into your hands the king of Ai and his land.

**8:2** And you shall do to Ai just as you did to Jericho and its king, and the plunder of the livestock you shall take as plunder for yourself. Now set for yourself an ambush behind the city."

**8:3** And Joshua rose up, and all the people of war, to go up to Ai. And Joshua chose thirty thousand men, mighty in strength, and sent them out by night.

**8:4** And he commanded them, saying, "You, lie in ambush behind the city; do not go far from the city, and you shall all be ready.

**8:5** And I and all those with me will approach the city, and it will be that when the inhabitants of Ai come out to meet us just as previously, then we will flee from before them.

**8:6** And when they come out after us, we will draw them away from the city; and they will say, 'These men are fleeing from before us just as before.'

**8:7** But you shall rise up from the ambush and go into the city.

**8:8** According to this word you shall act; look, I have commanded you."

**8:9** And Joshua sent them out, and they went to the ambush; and they sat in wait between Bethel and Ai, on the west of Ai.

**8:10** And rising early in the morning, Joshua mustered the people; and they and the elders went up before the people against Ai.

**8:11** And all the people of war with him went up, and marching on, they came opposite the city on the east,

**8:12** and the ambush of the city was on the west.

**8:14** And it happened that when the king of Ai saw it, he hurried and went out to meet them straight ahead to the battle, he and all the people who were with him; and he himself did not know that there was an ambush against him behind the city.

**8:15** And Joshua and Israel saw it and withdrew from before them.

**8:16** And they pursued after the sons of Israel, and they drew away from the city.

**8:17** No one was left in Ai who did not pursue after Israel; and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.

**8:18** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Stretch out your hand with the javelin that is in your hand toward the city, for into your hands I have handed it over; and the ambushers will rise up quickly from their place." And Joshua stretched out his hand, the javelin, toward the city.

**8:19** And the ambushers rose up quickly from their place and went out when he stretched out his hand, and came upon the city and captured it; and hurrying, they burned the city with fire.

**8:20** And the inhabitants of Ai looked around behind them and saw smoke going up from the city into the sky; and they no longer had anywhere to flee, this way or that.

**8:21** And Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambushers had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city had gone up into the sky; and turning around, they struck down the men of Ai.

**8:22** And these came out from the city to meet them, and they were in the middle of the army, some on this side and some on that side; and they struck them down until not a survivor or an escapee of them was left.

**8:23** And they captured the king of Ai alive, and brought him to Joshua.

**8:24** And when the sons of Israel finished killing all those in Ai, those in the plains and on the mountain at the descent where they had pursued them from it to the end, then Joshua returned to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.

**8:25** And those who fell on that day, from man even to woman, were twelve thousand, all the inhabitants of Ai.

**8:27** Except for the spoils that were in the city, all of which the sons of Israel took as plunder for themselves according to the command of the Lord, just as the Lord had ordered Joshua.

**8:28** And Joshua burned the city with fire; he made it an uninhabited mound forever, until this day.

**8:29** And he hanged the king of Ai on a forked tree, and he was on the tree until evening; and as the sun was setting, Joshua gave orders, and they took down his body from the tree and threw him into the pit, and raised over him a heap of stones, until this day.

## Translator Notes

- **8:12** — The Greek text of Joshua 8 is shorter than the Hebrew MT, omitting the second ambush of five thousand men here and all of 8:13. Here and in 8:9, "on the west" translates the Greek idiom literally meaning "from the sea."
- **8:18** — The clause "and the ambushers will rise up quickly from their place" is present in the Greek text but absent in the Hebrew MT. Greek gaisos refers to a heavy iron javelin or throwing spear.
- **8:27** — Verse 8:26 of the Hebrew MT is absent in the Greek text. Verse 8:27 omits mention of livestock and opens with an exception clause ("Except for the spoils...") linked to the destruction of Ai in 8:24-25.
- **8:29** — "Forked tree" translates Greek xylou didymou (literally "double tree/wood"), denoting a forked timber or gallows. The Greek also reads "into the pit" where the Hebrew MT reads "at the entrance of the city gate."
