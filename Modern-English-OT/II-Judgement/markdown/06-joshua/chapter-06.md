## Text

**6:1** Now Jericho was shut up and fortified, and no one was going out from it nor going in.

**6:2** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Look, I am delivering Jericho into your hand, and its king who is in it, who are mighty in strength.

**6:3** Now you, station the fighting men around it in a circle.

**6:5** And it will be, whenever you sound the trumpet, let all the people shout together; and when they shout, the walls of the city will fall of themselves, and all the people will enter, each one rushing straight ahead into the city."

**6:6** And Joshua the son of Nun went in to the priests

**6:7** and spoke to them, saying, "Order the people to go around and encircle the city, and let the fighting men march armed before the Lord;

**6:8** and let seven priests holding seven sacred trumpets pass along likewise before the Lord and sound the signal vigorously; and let the ark of the covenant of the Lord follow behind.

**6:9** And let the fighting men march in front, and the priests who form the rear guard behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord, sounding the trumpets."

**6:10** Now Joshua commanded the people, saying, "Do not shout, nor let anyone hear your voice, until the day he himself announces to shout out; and then you will shout out."

**6:11** And having gone around, the ark of the covenant of God immediately departed to the camp and lodged there.

**6:12** And on the second day Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord,

**6:13** and the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets were marching ahead before the Lord. And after these the fighting men were marching in, and the rest of the crowd behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord;

**6:14** and the priests sounded the trumpets, and all the rest of the crowd encircled the city from close by and departed again to the camp. So they did for six days.

**6:15** And on the seventh day they rose at dawn, and they went around the city six times;

**6:16** and on the seventh circuit the priests sounded the trumpets, and Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Shout! For the Lord has delivered the city to you.

**6:17** And the city will be devoted to destruction, it and everything that is in it, to the Lord Sabaoth. Only keep Rahab the prostitute alive, her and everything that is in her house.

**6:18** But as for you, guard yourselves strictly from what is devoted to destruction, so that, desiring it, you yourselves do not take from what is devoted to destruction and make the camp of the sons of Israel devoted to destruction, and wipe us out.

**6:19** And all silver or gold or bronze or iron will be holy to the Lord; it will be brought into the treasury of the Lord."

**6:20** And the priests sounded the trumpets; and when the people heard the trumpets, all the people shouted together with a great and mighty shout. And the entire wall fell all around, and all the people went up into the city.

**6:21** And Joshua devoted it to destruction, and all that was in the city, from man even to woman, from young man even to old man, and even to calf and beast of burden, by the edge of the sword.

**6:22** And to the two young men who had spied, Joshua said, "Go into the house of the woman and bring her out from there and all that belongs to her."

**6:23** And the two young men who had spied out the city went into the house of the woman, and they brought out Rahab the prostitute and her father and her mother and her brothers and her relatives and everything that was hers, and they settled her outside the camp of Israel.

**6:24** And the city was burned with fire along with everything in it, except for silver and gold and bronze and iron; they gave them to be brought into the treasury of the Lord.

**6:25** And Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute and all her father's household alive, and she lived in Israel until this day, because she hid the spies whom Joshua had sent to spy out Jericho.

**6:26** And Joshua pronounced an oath on that day before the Lord, saying, "Cursed is the man who will build that city. At the cost of his firstborn he will lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he will set up its gates." And so did Ozan of Bethel: at the cost of Abiram the firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of the youngest who survived he set up its gates.

**6:27** And the Lord was with Joshua, and his name was throughout all the land.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Septuagint presents a shorter form of the Lord's instructions here, omitting verse 4 entirely; in 6:5 it uniquely specifies that the city walls will fall "of themselves" (automata).
- **6:10** — "he himself announces" (autos diangeile) shifts to the third person inside Joshua's direct speech, referring to Joshua himself (cf. 6:16).
- **6:15** — Greek states that the Israelites marched around the city "six times" (hexakis) prior to the seventh circuit in 6:16, whereas the Hebrew reads "seven times."
- **6:17** — "Sabaoth" (σαβαώθ, transliterating Hebrew "hosts") is an addition in the Greek; the Hebrew of this verse reads only "to the Lord."
- **6:26** — The Septuagint adds a fulfillment notice (paralleled in 1 Kings 16:34), naming the rebuilder "Ozan" (Ozan, distinct from Hebrew Hiel), using the Greek form Abeiron (Abiram), and rendering the youngest son's name (Segub) as "the youngest who survived" (to elachisto diasothenti); "at the cost of" renders instrumental Greek en ("in/with").
