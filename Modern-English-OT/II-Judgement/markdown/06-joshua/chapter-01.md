## Text

**1:1** And it happened after the death of Moses that the Lord spoke to Joshua son of Nun, the assistant of Moses, saying,

**1:2** "Moses My servant has died. Now then, rise up and cross the Jordan, you and all this people, into the land which I am giving to them.

**1:3** Every place on which you step with the sole of your feet, I will give it to you, just as I have spoken to Moses:

**1:4** the wilderness and the Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, and as far as the farthest sea; your borders shall be from the setting of the sun.

**1:5** No man shall stand opposed before you all the days of your life; and just as I was with Moses, so I will also be with you, and I will not abandon you nor overlook you.

**1:6** Be strong and courageous; for you shall apportion to this people the land which I swore to your fathers to give to them.

**1:7** Be strong therefore and courageous to be careful and to do according to what Moses My servant commanded you, and you shall not turn aside from them to the right or to the left, so that you may understand in all things that you do.

**1:8** And the book of this law shall not depart from your mouth, and you shall meditate in it day and night, so that you may know how to do all the things that have been written; then you will be prospered, and it will prosper your ways, and then you will understand.

**1:9** Look, I have commanded you: be strong and courageous; do not be timid nor be afraid, because the Lord your God is with you in all things, wherever you go."

**1:10** And Joshua commanded the scribes of the people, saying,

**1:11** "Enter into the middle of the camp of the people, and command the people, saying, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, because in three more days you are crossing this Jordan, entering to take possession of the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving to you.'"

**1:12** And to Reuben and to Gad and to the half of the tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said,

**1:13** "Remember the word which Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, saying, 'The Lord your God has given you rest and has given you this land.'

**1:14** Your wives and your little children and your livestock shall dwell in the land which He gave you; but you shall cross over armed ahead of your brothers, everyone who is strong, and you shall fight alongside them,

**1:15** until the Lord our God gives rest to your brothers just as also to you, and they too inherit the land which the Lord our God is giving to them; and you shall depart, each one to his inheritance, which Moses has given you across the Jordan toward the rising of the sun."

**1:16** And answering Joshua, they said, "All things that you command us we will do, and to every place where you send us we will go.

**1:17** According to all things in which we listened to Moses, we will listen to you; only may the Lord our God be with you just as He was with Moses.

**1:18** And the man who disobeys you, and whoever does not listen to your words according to whatever you command him, let him die. But be strong and courageous."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text spells Joshua's father's name "Naue" (Ναυή) for Hebrew "Nun" (and renders Joshua as Ἰησοῦς); standard modern English spellings are used here.
- **1:4** — The Greek reads "the Anti-Lebanon" rather than Hebrew "this Lebanon," omits "all the land of the Hittites," and places "from the setting of the sun" with "your borders" following "the farthest sea."
- **1:8** — The Greek reads "so that you may know how to do" (rather than Hebrew "be careful to do") and uses a third-person singular verb ("it will prosper your ways"), with "the book of this law" as its implied subject.
- **1:10** — The Greek renders Hebrew "officers" as "scribes" (γραμματεῖς), a term it also uses in 3:2.
