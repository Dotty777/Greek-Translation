## Text

**12:1** And these are the kings of the land whom the sons of Israel struck down and whose land they inherited beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the gorge of Arnon as far as Mount Hermon, and all the land of Arabah on the east:

**12:2** Sihon the king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, exercising dominion from Arnon, which is in the gorge along part of the gorge, and half of Gilead as far as Jabbok, the borders of the sons of Ammon,

**12:3** and Arabah as far as the Sea of Chinnereth toward the east, and as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Sea of Salt, on the east, the way toward Hasimoth, from Teman, that which is below Medoth-pisgah;

**12:4** and Og king of Bashan remained from the giants, the one who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

**12:5** ruling from Mount Hermon and from Sekchai, and all Bashan as far as the borders of Gergesi and Machei, and half of Gilead, the borders of Sihon king of Heshbon.

**12:6** Moses the servant of the Lord and the sons of Israel struck them; and Moses gave it as an inheritance to Reuben and Gad and to the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**12:7** And these are the kings of the Amorites whom Joshua and the sons of Israel struck down on the other side of the Jordan by the sea, Baal-gad in the plain of Lebanon and as far as Mount Chelcha as one goes up to Seir, and Joshua gave it to the tribes of Israel to inherit according to their allotment,

**12:8** in the mountain, and in the plain, and in Arabah, and in Asedoth, and in the wilderness and the Negev, the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite;

**12:9** and the king of Jericho, and the king of Ai, which is near Bethel,

**12:10** the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron,

**12:11** the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish,

**12:12** the king of Ailam, the king of Gezer,

**12:13** the king of Debir, the king of Asei,

**12:14** the king of Hermath, the king of Airath, the king of Arath,

**12:15** the king of Libnah, the king of Adullam,

**12:16** the king of Elad,

**12:17** the king of Ataphout, the king of Hepher,

**12:18** the king of Aphek of Arok,

**12:19** the king of Hasom,

**12:20** the king of Symoon, the king of Mamroth, the king of Aziph,

**12:21** the king of Kedesh, the king of Zakach,

**12:22** the king of Maredoth, and the king of Jekom of Carmel,

**12:23** the king of Eldom of Phenneddor, the king of Geei of Galilee,

**12:24** the king of Tirzah; all these were twenty-nine kings.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Greek reads Hasimoth (MT Beth-jeshimoth), takes MT "from the south" as the proper name Teman, and renders MT "slopes of Pisgah" as Medoth-pisgah (following Arnon for MT Aroer in 12:2).
- **12:5** — The Greek preserves the distinct forms Sekchai (MT Salecah), Gergesi (MT Geshurites), and Machei (MT Maacathites).
- **12:7** — The Greek reads "kings of the Amorites" (MT "kings of the land") and Mount Chelcha (MT Mount Halak; cf. Hachel in 11:17), and in 12:8 transliterates MT "the slopes" as the proper name Asedoth.
- **12:14** — The Greek text lists three kings here—Hermath (MT Hormah) plus the doublet Airath and Arath (MT Arad)—bringing the enumerated kings in 12:9-24 to thirty even though 12:24 states the LXX total as twenty-nine (MT thirty-one).
- **12:18** — In 12:12-19 the Greek preserves distinct city names and groupings compared to the MT: Ailam (Eglon), Asei (Geder), Elad (Makkedah, with Bethel omitted), Ataphout (Tappuah), Aphek of Arok (combining Aphek and Lasharon into one king), and Hasom (one entry where the MT lists Madon and Hazor).
- **12:23** — In 12:20-23 the Greek preserves Symoon and Mamroth (splitting MT Shimron-meron into two kings), Aziph (Achshaph), Zakach (Taanach), Maredoth (Megiddo), Jekom (Jokneam), Eldom of Phenneddor (Dor in Naphath-dor), and Geei of Galilee (MT Goiim in Gilgal).
