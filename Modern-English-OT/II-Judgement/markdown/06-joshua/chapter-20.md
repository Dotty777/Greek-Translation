## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying,

**20:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Give the cities of refuge about which I spoke to you through Moses,

**20:3** a refuge for the killer who struck a person unintentionally; and the cities will be a refuge for you, and the killer will not be put to death by the avenger of blood until he stands before the assembly for judgment.'"

**20:7** And he set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, and Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and the city of Arba, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**20:8** And on the other side of the Jordan he gave Bezer in the wilderness on the plain from the tribe of Reuben, and Ramoth in Gilead out of the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan out of the tribe of Manasseh.

**20:9** These are the designated cities for the sons of Israel and for the foreigner who resides among them, for anyone who strikes a person unintentionally to flee there, so that he may not die by the hand of the avenger of blood until he stands before the assembly for judgment.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — The Septuagint omits verses 4-6 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text, moving directly from verse 3 to verse 7 and placing the trial clause ("until he stands before the assembly for judgment") here in verse 3. "Avenger of blood" renders Greek ho anchisteuon to haima, literally "the one acting as next of kin for the blood."
- **20:7** — The Greek uses singular verbs here ("he set apart") and in 20:8 ("he gave"), making Joshua the subject rather than plural Israel as in the Hebrew, and renders Kiriath-arba as "the city of Arba" (Greek: Arbok).
- **20:9** — "Foreigner" translates Greek proselytos in its Septuagint sense of a resident alien or newcomer dwelling among Israel.
