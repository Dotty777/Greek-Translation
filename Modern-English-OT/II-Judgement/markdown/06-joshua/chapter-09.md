## Text

**9:1** Now when the kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan—those in the hill country and those in the lowland and those along the entire coast of the Great Sea and those near Anti-Lebanon—and the Hittites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites heard,

**9:2** they assembled in one place to make war against Joshua and Israel, all of them together. Then Joshua built an altar to the Lord, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded the sons of Israel, as it is written in the law of Moses—an altar of whole stones upon which no iron had been laid; and he offered up burnt offerings there to the Lord, a sacrifice of deliverance. And Joshua wrote on the stones the second law, the law of Moses, in the presence of the sons of Israel. And all Israel, and their elders, and the judges, and their scribes were marching along on this side and on that side opposite the ark—and the priests and the Levites lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord—both the resident alien and the native-born, half of whom were near Mount Gerizim and half of whom were near Mount Ebal, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded to bless the people first. And after these things Joshua accordingly read all the words of this law, the blessings and the curses, according to everything written in the law of Moses. There was not a word from all that Moses had commanded Joshua which Joshua did not read in the ears of the entire assembly of the sons of Israel—to the men, the women, and the children, and to the resident aliens who joined Israel.

**9:3** And the inhabitants of Gibeon heard everything that the Lord had done to Jericho and to Ai.

**9:4** And they also acted with craftiness; going out, they gathered provisions and prepared themselves. And they took old sacks on their shoulders, and old wineskins that were torn and tied up;

**9:5** and the hollows of their shoes and their sandals were old and resoled on their feet, and their garments were worn out upon them, and the bread of their provisions was dry and moldy and eaten away.

**9:6** And they came to Joshua to the camp of Israel at Gilgal, and they said to Joshua and Israel, "We have come from a distant land; and now, make a covenant with us."

**9:7** And the sons of Israel said to the Horite, "See, perhaps you are living among me; and how could I make a covenant with you?"

**9:8** And they said to Joshua, "We are your servants." And Joshua said to them, "Where are you from, and from where have you come?"

**9:9** And they said, "From a very distant land your servants have come in the name of the Lord your God; for we have heard His name, and all that He did in Egypt,

**9:10** and all that He did to the kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon king of the Amorites and to Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei.

**9:11** And when they heard, our elders and all the inhabitants of our land spoke to us, saying, 'Take for yourselves provisions for the journey and go to meet them, and you shall say to them, "We are your servants; and now, make a covenant with us."'

**9:12** These loaves of bread—we took them warm as provisions on the day we went out to come to you, but now they have dried out and have become eaten away;

**9:13** and these wineskins that we filled new, these have torn; and our garments and our shoes have worn out from the very long journey."

**9:14** And the leaders took some of their provisions, and did not inquire of the Lord.

**9:15** And Joshua made peace with them, and they made a covenant with them to preserve them, and the leaders of the congregation swore to them.

**9:16** And it happened after three days, after making a covenant with them, that they heard that they were near them and that they were living among them.

**9:17** And the sons of Israel set out and came to their cities. Now their cities were Gibeon, and Chephirah, and Beeron, and the cities of Jarin.

**9:18** And the sons of Israel did not fight against them, because all the leaders had sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel; and the entire congregation grumbled against the leaders.

**9:19** And the leaders said to the entire congregation, "We have sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel, and now we will not be able to touch them.

**9:20** This we will do: spare them alive, and we will preserve them, and there will be no wrath against us because of the oath that we swore to them.

**9:21** They will live, and they will be woodcutters and water carriers for the entire congregation"—just as the leaders said to them.

**9:22** And Joshua summoned them and said to them, "Why have you deceived me, saying, 'We are very far from you,' when you are locals, of those living among us?

**9:23** And now you are cursed; there will never be lacking from among you a servant or a woodcutter for me and for my God."

**9:24** And they answered Joshua, saying, "It was reported to us all that the Lord your God commanded Moses His servant, to give you this land and to utterly destroy us and all those living upon it from before you; and we were greatly afraid for our lives before you, and we did this thing.

**9:25** And now, look, we are under your hand; as it pleases you and as it seems right to you, do to us."

**9:26** And they did so to them; and Joshua rescued them on that day from the hands of the sons of Israel, and they did not kill them.

**9:27** And on that day Joshua appointed them woodcutters and water carriers for the entire congregation and for the altar of God. Therefore the inhabitants of Gibeon became woodcutters and water carriers of the altar of God to this day, and for the place that the Lord may choose.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Greek specifies "the kings of the Amorites" and names the mountain range "Anti-Lebanon" (Hebrew "Lebanon").
- **9:2** — The Greek places the Mount Ebal altar and law-reading account (Hebrew 8:30-35) here inside 9:2; "the second law" translates Greek deuteronomion (Hebrew "a copy of the law").
- **9:7** — The Greek reads "the Horite" (ton Chorraion; Hebrew "the Hivites"); earlier in 9:4, the Greek also reads "on their shoulders" rather than "on their donkeys."
- **9:17** — The Greek names the third and fourth towns "Beeron" (Hebrew "Beeroth") and "the cities of Jarin" (Hebrew "Kiriath-jearim").
- **9:21** — The Greek omits the Hebrew's introductory "and the leaders said to them" and renders both verbs in the future tense, continuing the leaders' declaration before the closing clause "just as the leaders said to them."
