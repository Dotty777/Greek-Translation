## Text

**23:1** And it happened after many days, after the Lord had given Israel rest from all their enemies all around, that Joshua was old, advanced in days.

**23:2** And Joshua called together all the sons of Israel, and their council of elders, and their leaders, and their scribes, and their judges, and said to them, "I have grown old and am advanced in days.

**23:3** And you have seen all that the Lord our God has done to all these nations before our face, because the Lord our God is the One who fought for us.

**23:4** See that, just as I told you, these nations that are left for you are in the lots for your tribes; from the Jordan I also utterly destroyed all the nations, and from the Great Sea it will border toward the setting of the sun.

**23:5** And the Lord our God, He will utterly destroy them from before our face until they perish; and He will send the wild beasts against them until He utterly destroys them and their kings from before your face; and inherit their land, just as the Lord our God spoke to you.

**23:6** Be very strong, therefore, to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, so that you do not turn aside to the right or to the left;

**23:7** so that you do not go in among these nations that are left; and the names of their gods shall not be named among you; you shall by no means bow down to them, nor shall you serve them,

**23:8** but to the Lord our God you shall cling, just as you have done until this day.

**23:9** And the Lord will utterly destroy them from before your face, great and strong nations, and no one has stood before us until this day;

**23:10** one of you chased a thousand, because the Lord our God was fighting for us, just as He said to us.

**23:11** And be very careful to love the Lord our God.

**23:12** For if you turn away and join yourselves to these remaining nations that are with you, and make intermarriages with them, and mingle together with them and they with you,

**23:13** know that the Lord will by no means continue to utterly destroy these nations from before your face; and they will be to you as snares and as stumbling blocks, and as nails in your heels and as darts in your eyes, until you perish from this good land which the Lord your God has given you.

**23:14** Now I am departing on the way, just as all those on the earth do; and you will know in your heart and in your soul that not one word has fallen from all the words that the Lord our God spoke concerning all the things pertaining to us; none of them has failed.

**23:15** And it will be that just as all the good words that the Lord spoke to us have come to us, so the Lord God will bring upon you all the evil words, until He utterly destroys you from this good land which the Lord has given you,

**23:16** when you transgress the covenant of the Lord our God which He commanded us, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them."

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — Greek reads "what I told you" (hoper eipa), likely an early Greek textual corruption from a verb meaning "I allotted" (matching the Hebrew Masoretic Text), and describes the western boundary from the Great Sea as bordering toward the setting of the sun.
- **23:5** — Greek uniquely adds that the Lord will send wild beasts against the remaining nations until He utterly destroys them and their kings, a clause absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:13** — Greek renders the paired metaphors as "nails in your heels" and "darts in your eyes," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a whip on your sides" and "thorns in your eyes."
- **23:16** — Greek concludes chapter 23 here without the final sentence found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text warning that the Lord's anger will burn against them and they will quickly perish from the good land.
