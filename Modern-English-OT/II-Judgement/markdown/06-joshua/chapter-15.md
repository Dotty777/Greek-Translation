## Text

**15:1** And the borders of the tribe of Judah according to their clans were from the borders of Judea, from the wilderness of Zin as far as Kadesh toward the south.

**15:2** And their borders on the south were as far as the edge of the Salt Sea, from the ridge that extends toward the south;

**15:3** and they pass across opposite the ascent of Akrabbim, and go out around Ennak, and go up on the south to Kadesh-barnea, and go out to Hezron, and ascend to Sarada, and go out along the west of Kadesh,

**15:4** and go out to Selmona, and will extend as far as the valley of Egypt, and the outlet of its borders will be at the sea; these are their borders on the south.

**15:5** And the borders on the east are all the Salt Sea as far as the Jordan. And their borders on the north, both from the ridge of the sea and from the edge of the Jordan,

**15:6** the borders go up to Beth-aglaam, and pass along on the north to Beth-arabah, and the borders ascend to the stone of Bohan the son of Reuben;

**15:7** and the borders ascend to the fourth part of the valley of Achor, and go down to Taagad, which is opposite the approach of Adummim, which is to the south of the valley, and will extend to the water of the spring of the sun, and its outlet will be the spring of Rogel;

**15:8** and the borders go up into the valley of Hinnom to the south of Jebus on the south (this is Jerusalem), and the borders run out to the top of a mountain which is facing the valley of Hinnom toward the sea, which is at the edge of the land of Rephaim on the north;

**15:9** and the border extends from the top of the mountain to the spring of the water of Maphtho, and extends to Mount Ephron, and the border will lead to Jebaal (this is the city of Jearim);

**15:10** and the border will turn around from Baal toward the sea, and will pass on to Mount Assar, at the back of the city of Jearim on the north (this is Chesalon), and will go down to the city of the sun, and will pass on toward the south;

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — The Greek reads Judea (likely an inner-Greek variant of Idumea/Edom) and as far as Kadesh, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Edom and at the extreme south. Throughout the boundary description in vv. 3-11, the Greek also preserves distinct place names (Ennak, Sarada, Selmona, Beth-aglaam, Taagad, Maphtho, Jebaal, Baal, Assar, Succoth, Lemna), reads the fourth part of the valley of Achor in v. 7 (where Hebrew has Debir from the valley of Achor), and translates the spring of the sun (v. 7) and the city of the sun (v. 10).
- **15:8** — The Greek reads to the south (notou), an orthographic variant of back/ridge (notou with omega, as in vv. 10-11, rendering Hebrew shoulder/slope), alongside on the south (libos, the standard Septuagint term for the south/southwest), and has land of Rephaim rather than valley of Rephaim.
- **15:18** — The Greek reads as she was going out, she consulted with him, and she cried out from the donkey, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has when she came, she urged him, and she alighted from the donkey.
- **15:19** — Instead of translating Hebrew springs of water and upper/lower springs, the Greek renders them as proper place names: Boththanis and upper/lower Gonaithlan.
- **15:21** — The Greek preserves the repeated cities in v. 21 and reflects a distinctive Septuagint list of Negev towns across vv. 21-32 (including Kaibaiseleel, Ara, Hazor, Ikam, Regma, Aruel, Hasorionain, Mainam, Balmainan, Sen, Salmaa, Seri, Baiphalad, Cholaseola, Bakok, Elboudad, Bethel, Macharim, Sethennak, Labos, Sale, and Eromoth) that differs markedly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **15:33** — Across the lowland and hill-country lists (vv. 33-58), the Greek preserves numerous Septuagint-distinct place names and totals that differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, including ten cities in vv. 42-44 (Hebrew has nine), Gemna in v. 46 (where Hebrew reads westward), and nine cities in vv. 55-57 (omitting Kain, where Hebrew has ten).
- **15:59** — The second half of this verse (Tekoa through Manocho, eleven cities including Ephrathah/Bethlehem) is a well-known Septuagint section absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, likely lost in the Hebrew tradition through scribal eye-skip.
- **15:61** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text introduces a six-city wilderness district (Beth-arabah through En-gedi), the Greek omits the wilderness heading and lists seven cities across vv. 61-62 (Baddargis, Tharabaam, Ainon, Aichioza, Naphlazon, the cities of Sadom, replacing the City of Salt, and Aknades).
- **15:63** — The Greek reads to destroy them (Hebrew: to dispossess them) and until that day (Hebrew: to this day), and omits with the sons of Judah in the final clause.
