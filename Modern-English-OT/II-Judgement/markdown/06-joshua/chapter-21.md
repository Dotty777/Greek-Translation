## Text

**21:1** And the heads of the fathers' houses of the sons of Levi approached Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun, and the tribal heads of the fathers' houses from the tribes of Israel.

**21:2** And they spoke to them in Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "The Lord commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to live in, and the pasturelands for our livestock."

**21:3** And the sons of Israel gave to the Levites, while receiving their inheritance, by the command of the Lord, the cities and their pasturelands.

**21:4** And the lot came out for the clan of Kohath; and to the sons of Aaron the priests, the Levites, there fell from the tribe of Judah and from the tribe of Simeon and from the tribe of Benjamin, by lot, thirteen cities.

**21:5** And to the remaining sons of Kohath, from the tribe of Ephraim and from the tribe of Dan and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, by lot, ten cities;

**21:6** and to the sons of Gershon, from the tribe of Issachar and from the tribe of Asher and from the tribe of Naphtali and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan, thirteen cities;

**21:7** and among the sons of Merari according to their clans, from the tribe of Reuben and from the tribe of Gad and from the tribe of Zebulun, by lot, twelve cities.

**21:8** And the sons of Israel gave to the Levites, by lot, the cities and their pasturelands, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**21:9** And the tribe of the sons of Judah and the tribe of the sons of Simeon, and from the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, gave these cities; and they were designated

**21:10** for the sons of Aaron from the clan of Kohath of the sons of Levi, because this was the lot.

**21:11** And he gave them Kiriath-arba, the mother city of the Anakim, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah; but the pasturelands around it

**21:12** and the fields of the city and its villages Joshua gave to the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh as a possession.

**21:13** And to the sons of Aaron he gave the city of refuge for the one who has killed, Hebron and the lands set apart with it, and Libnah and the lands set apart beside it,

**21:14** and Ailom and the lands set apart for it, and Tema and the lands set apart for it,

**21:15** and Gella and the lands set apart for it, and Debir and the lands set apart for it,

**21:16** and Asa and the lands set apart for it, and Tanu and the lands set apart for it, and Beth-shemesh and the lands set apart for it; nine cities from these two tribes.

**21:17** And from the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon and the lands set apart for it, and Gatheth and the lands set apart for it,

**21:18** and Anathoth and the lands set apart for it, and Gamala and the lands set apart for it; four cities.

**21:19** All the cities of the sons of Aaron the priests were thirteen.

**21:20** And for the clans of the sons of Kohath, the Levites who remained from the sons of Kohath, the city of their priests was from the tribe of Ephraim.

**21:21** And they gave them the city of refuge for the one who has killed, Shechem and the lands set apart for it, and Gezer and the areas beside it and the lands set apart for it,

**21:22** and Beth-horon and the lands set apart for it; four cities.

**21:23** And from the tribe of Dan: Elkothaim and the lands set apart for it, and Gethedan and the lands set apart for it,

**21:24** and Aijalon and the lands set apart for it, and Gath-rimmon and the lands set apart for it; four cities.

**21:25** And from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach and the lands set apart for it, and Jebatha and the lands set apart for it; two cities.

**21:26** All the cities were ten, and the lands set apart for each, those beside them, for the clans of the sons of Kohath who remained.

**21:27** And to the sons of Gershon, the Levites, from the half-tribe of Manasseh: the cities set apart for those who have killed, Golan in Bashan and the lands set apart for it, and Bosora and the lands set apart for it; two cities.

**21:28** And from the tribe of Issachar: Kishion and the lands set apart for it, and Debba and the lands set apart for it,

**21:29** and Remmath and the lands set apart for it, and Spring of Letters and the lands set apart for it; four cities.

**21:30** And from the tribe of Asher: Basellan and the lands set apart for it, and Dabbon and the lands set apart for it,

**21:31** and Helkath and the lands set apart for it, and Rehob and the lands set apart for it; four cities.

**21:32** And from the tribe of Naphtali: the city set apart for the one who has killed, Kedesh in Galilee and the lands set apart for it, and Nemmath and the lands set apart for it, and Themmon and the lands set apart for it; three cities.

**21:33** All the cities of Gershon according to their clans were thirteen cities.

**21:34** And to the clan of the sons of Merari, the remaining Levites, from the tribe of Zebulun: Maan and its pasturelands, and Kadesh and its pasturelands,

**21:35** and Sella and its pasturelands; three cities.

**21:36** And beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, from the tribe of Reuben: the city of refuge for the one who has killed, Bezer in the wilderness, Miso and its pasturelands, and Jazer and its pasturelands,

**21:37** and Dekmon and its pasturelands, and Mapha and its pasturelands; four cities.

**21:38** And from the tribe of Gad: the city of refuge for the one who has killed, even Ramoth in Gilead and its pasturelands, Kamein and its pasturelands,

**21:39** and Heshbon and its pasturelands, and Jazer and its pasturelands; all the cities were four.

**21:40** As for all the cities for the sons of Merari according to their clans, who remained from the tribe of Levi, the borders came to be twelve cities.

**21:41** All the cities of the Levites in the midst of the possession of the sons of Israel were forty-eight cities, and their pasturelands

**21:42** around these cities; each city and the pasturelands around the city for all these cities. And Joshua finished dividing the land within their borders. And the sons of Israel gave a portion to Joshua according to the command of the Lord; they gave him the city that he asked for; they gave him Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim. And Joshua built the city and lived in it; and Joshua took the stone knives with which he circumcised the sons of Israel who were born on the way in the wilderness, and he placed them in Timnath-serah.

**21:43** And the Lord gave to Israel all the land that He swore to give to their fathers, and they inherited it and lived in it.

**21:44** And the Lord gave them rest on every side, just as He swore to their fathers; no one stood before them from among all their enemies; the Lord delivered all their enemies into their hands.

**21:45** Not one failed from all the good words that the Lord spoke to the sons of Israel; all of them came about.

## Translator Notes

- **21:11** — Greek calls Kiriath-arba 'the mother city of the Anakim' (MT 'the father of Anak') and connects 'the pasturelands around it' syntactically to v. 12 as part of what Joshua gave to the sons of Caleb.
- **21:14** — In vv. 14-18 and 23-25, the Greek preserves several distinct city names compared to the Hebrew: Ailom (MT Jattir), Tema (MT Eshtemoa), Gella (MT Holon), Asa (MT Ain), Tanu (MT Juttah), Gatheth (MT Geba), Gamala (MT Almon), Elkothaim (MT Eltekeh), Gethedan (MT Gibbethon), and Jebatha (MT Gath-rimmon).
- **21:20** — Greek reads 'the city of their priests' (hiereon, likely an early Greek scribal shift from horion, 'borders'; cf. v. 40 and MT 'the cities of their lot'), and vv. 21-22 name only three Ephraimite cities (omitting MT Kibzaim) while stating the total as four.
- **21:29** — Greek literally reads 'Spring of Letters' (Pegen grammaton, vs. MT En-gannim, 'Spring of Gardens'); other distinct Greek city names in vv. 27-32 are Bosora (MT Beeshterah), Debba (MT Daberath), Remmath (MT Jarmuth), Basellan (MT Mishal), Dabbon (MT Abdon), Nemmath (MT Hammoth-dor), and Themmon (MT Kartan).
- **21:34** — Greek names only three Zebulunite cities, Maan, Kadesh, and Sella (MT has four: Jokneam, Kartah, Dimnah, Nahalal), leaving eleven named Merarite cities though v. 40 totals twelve; other distinct Greek names in vv. 36-38 are Miso (transliterating Hebrew mishor, 'tableland'), Jazer (MT Jahaz), Dekmon (MT Kedemoth), Mapha (MT Mephaath), and Kamein (MT Mahanaim).
- **21:42** — The second half of v. 42 is a major Septuagint addition absent from the Masoretic Text here (partly paralleling 19:49-50), recording Joshua's completion of the land division, his reception of Timnath-serah (spelled Thamnasacharath at the end of the verse), and his placement of the stone circumcision knives there.
