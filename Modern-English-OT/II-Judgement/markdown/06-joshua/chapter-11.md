## Text

**11:1** Now when Jabis king of Hazor heard, he sent to Jobab king of Marron, and to the king of Symoon, and to the king of Aziph,

**11:2** and to the kings who were near great Sidon, in the hill country, and in Raba opposite Chinneroth, and in the plain, and in Phenaeddor,

**11:3** and to the coastal Canaanites from the east, and to the coastal Amorites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, and the Perizzites who were in the mountain, and the Hittites who were below the wilderness toward Maseuman.

**11:4** And they went out, they and their kings with them, like the sand of the sea in multitude, and very many horses and chariots.

**11:5** And all the kings themselves assembled and came to the same place, and they encamped at the water of Marron to fight against Israel.

**11:6** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid before them, for tomorrow at this hour I am handing them over routed before Israel; you shall hamstring their horses, and you shall burn up their chariots with fire."

**11:7** And Joshua and all the fighting people came upon them suddenly at the water of Marron, and fell upon them in the hill country.

**11:8** And the Lord handed them over into the hands of Israel, and striking them, they pursued them as far as great Sidon, and as far as Maseron, and as far as the plains of Massoch toward the east; and they cut them down until no survivor of them was left.

**11:9** And Joshua did to them just as the Lord commanded him: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

**11:10** And Joshua turned back at that time and captured Hazor and its king; now Hazor was formerly ruler of all these kingdoms.

**11:11** And they killed everything that breathed in it with the sword and utterly destroyed all of them, and nothing that breathed was left in it; and they burned Hazor with fire.

**11:12** And Joshua took all the cities of the kings and their kings and struck them down with the edge of the sword, and they utterly destroyed them, just as Moses the servant of the Lord ordered.

**11:13** However, Israel did not burn any of the cities raised on mounds; only Hazor alone Israel burned.

**11:14** And the sons of Israel took all its spoil as plunder for themselves; but all of them they utterly destroyed with the edge of the sword until he destroyed them; they did not leave even one of them that breathed.

**11:15** Just as the Lord ordered Moses His servant, so likewise Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did; he did not set aside anything of all that Moses ordered him.

**11:16** And Joshua took all the mountainous land, and all Adeb, and all the land of Goshen, and the lowland, and the land toward the west, and Mount Israel, and the low places

**11:17** that are by the mountain, from Mount Hachel, and what goes up to Seir, and as far as Baal-gad, and the plains of Lebanon below Mount Hermon; and all their kings he took and struck down and killed.

**11:18** And for many days Joshua made war against these kings.

**11:19** There was not a city that Israel did not take; they took everything in war,

**11:20** for it came about through the Lord that their heart grew strong to meet Israel in war, so that they might be utterly destroyed, so that mercy might not be given to them, but that they might be utterly destroyed, just as the Lord said to Moses.

**11:21** And Joshua came at that time and utterly destroyed the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron and from Debir and from Anaboth, and from all the race of Israel and from all the mountain of Judah, with their cities; and Joshua utterly destroyed them.

**11:22** None of the Anakim was left from the sons of Israel, but only in Gaza and in Aseldo were any left.

**11:23** And Joshua took all the land, just as the Lord commanded Moses; and Joshua gave them as an inheritance to Israel by division according to their tribes. And the land ceased from being warred against.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Greek preserves distinct forms for several northern coalition names: Jabis (MT Jabin), Marron (MT Madon; also used in 11:5, 7 for MT Merom), Symoon (MT Shimron), and Aziph (MT Achshaph).
- **11:2** — The Greek reads "near great Sidon" where the Hebrew MT has "in the north," and renders MT Arabah and Naphoth-dor as Raba and Phenaeddor.
- **11:3** — The Greek places the Hittites "below the wilderness toward Maseuman" (MT "the Hivite under Hermon in the land of Mizpah") and describes both the Canaanites and Amorites as "coastal."
- **11:8** — The Greek preserves Maseron (MT Misrephoth-maim) and Massoch (MT Mizpeh); in 11:6 it likewise reads "routed" where the MT has "pierced/killed."
- **11:16** — The Greek renders MT Negev as Adeb, MT Arabah as "the land toward the west," and MT Mount Halak in 11:17 as Mount Hachel.
- **11:19** — Unlike the MT, the Greek omits the exception regarding the Hivites of Gibeon making peace with Israel.
- **11:21** — The Greek reads Anaboth (MT Anab) and "all the race of Israel" (MT "all the hill country of Israel"); in 11:22 it omits Gath and reads Aseldo (MT Ashdod).
