## Text

**2:1** And Joshua son of Nun sent out two young men from Shittim to spy, saying, "Go up and see the land and Jericho." And the two young men went and entered into Jericho, and they entered into the house of a woman who was a prostitute, whose name was Rahab, and they lodged there.

**2:2** And it was reported to the king of Jericho, saying, "Men of the sons of Israel have come in here to spy out the land."

**2:3** And the king of Jericho sent and spoke to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who have entered into your house tonight; for they have come to spy out the land."

**2:4** And the woman took the two men and hid them, and she spoke to them, saying, "The men have come in to me;

**2:5** but as the gate was being shut in the dark, the men went out. I do not know where they have gone; pursue after them, to see if you will overtake them."

**2:6** Now she had brought them up onto the roof and hidden them in the stalks of flax that had been stacked by her on the roof.

**2:7** And the men pursued after them along the road to the Jordan, to the fords, and the gate was shut.

**2:8** And it happened, when those pursuing after them had gone out, and before they themselves lay down to sleep, she went up onto the roof to them

**2:9** and said to them, "I know that the Lord has given you the land, for the fear of you has fallen upon us.

**2:10** For we have heard that the Lord God dried up the Red Sea from before you when you were coming out from the land of Egypt, and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites who were across the Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you utterly destroyed.

**2:11** And when we heard, we were dismayed in our heart, and no spirit stood any longer in any one of us before you; because the Lord your God is He who is in heaven above and on the earth below.

**2:12** And now swear to me by the Lord God; because I am showing mercy to you, you also show mercy in the house of my father.

**2:13** And keep alive the house of my father, my mother and my brothers and all my house and all that belongs to them, and you shall deliver my life from death."

**2:14** And the men said to her, "Our life in place of yours to death." And she said, "When the Lord hands over the city to you, you shall show mercy and truth to me."

**2:15** And she let them down through the window;

**2:16** and she said to them, "Go away into the hill country, so that the pursuers do not meet you, and hide yourselves there three days until those pursuing after you return, and after this you shall depart on your way."

**2:17** And the men said to her, "We are guiltless regarding this oath of yours:

**2:18** Look, we are entering into a part of the city, and you shall set the sign: you shall tie this scarlet cord onto the window through which you let us down, and you shall gather your father and your mother and your brothers and all the house of your father to yourself into your house.

**2:19** And it shall be that everyone who goes outside the door of your house will be liable for himself, while we are guiltless regarding this oath of yours; and for as many as are with you in your house, we will be liable.

**2:20** But if anyone wrongs us or even reveals these words of ours, we will be guiltless regarding this oath of yours."

**2:21** And she said to them, "According to your word let it be." And she sent them away.

**2:22** And they went and came into the hill country, and they remained there three days; and the pursuers searched out all the roads and did not find them.

**2:23** And the two young men returned and came down from the mountain, and crossed over to Joshua son of Nun, and recounted to him all the things that had happened to them.

**2:24** And they said to Joshua, "The Lord has handed over all the land into our hand, and everyone dwelling in that land has cowered before us."

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The Greek introduces the final clause with "if" (εἰ, rendered "to see if you will overtake them") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "for" ("for you will overtake them").
- **2:11** — The Greek text reads the relative pronoun "who" (ὅς, "is He who is in heaven above...") rather than the noun "God" ("He is God in heaven above..."), a textual variant likely arising from visual confusion between abbreviated Greek ΘΣ ("God") and ΟΣ ("who").
- **2:14** — In the Greek text, the men speak only the opening oath ("Our life in place of yours to death"), and Rahab speaks the second half of the verse concerning "the city," whereas in the Hebrew Masoretic Text the men speak the entire verse concerning "the land."
- **2:19** — The Greek condenses the Hebrew blood-guilt expressions ("his blood shall be on his own head... on our head") into concise legal liability terms ("liable for himself... we will be liable").
