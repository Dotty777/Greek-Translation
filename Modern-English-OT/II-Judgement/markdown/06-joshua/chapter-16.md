## Text

**16:1** And the borders of the sons of Joseph were from the Jordan opposite Jericho on the east, and they will go up from Jericho into the wilderness hill country to Bethel Luz,

**16:2** and they will go out to Bethel, and pass on to the borders of Chatarothi,

**16:3** and they will cross over toward the sea to the borders of Haptaleim as far as the borders of lower Beth-horon, and their termination will be at the sea.

**16:4** And the sons of Joseph, Ephraim and Manasseh, received an inheritance.

**16:5** And the borders of the sons of Ephraim were according to their clans; and the borders of their inheritance on the east were Astaroth and Erok as far as upper Beth-horon and Gezer.

**16:6** And the borders will go toward the sea to Hikasmon, on the north of Therma; they will go around toward the east to Thenasa and Sellesa, and pass on from the east to Janoah

**16:7** and to Macho and Astaroth, and their villages; and they will go to Jericho, and run out to the Jordan.

**16:8** And from Taphos the borders will proceed toward the sea to Chelkana, and their termination will be at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim according to their clans.

**16:9** And the cities that were set apart for the sons of Ephraim in the midst of the inheritance of the sons of Manasseh, all the cities and their villages.

**16:10** And Ephraim did not destroy the Canaanite who lived in Gezer; and the Canaanite lived in Ephraim until this day, until Pharaoh king of Egypt went up and took it and burned it with fire, and they pierced through the Canaanites and the Perizzites and those who lived in Gezer; and Pharaoh gave it as a dowry to his daughter.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — In 16:1-3 the Septuagint reads "Bethel Luz" in 16:1 and "to Bethel" in 16:2, and uses the distinct names Chatarothi (merging Hebrew "the Archite" and "Ataroth") and Haptaleim in 16:3 (Hebrew: "the Japhletite").
- **16:5** — The Greek reads "Astaroth and Erok" (Hebrew: "Ataroth-addar") and places Gezer (Greek: Gazara) at the end of this verse rather than in 16:3.
- **16:6** — Along Ephraim's border in 16:6-8 the Septuagint has several distinct place names: Hikasmon and Therma (Hebrew: "Michmethath"), Thenasa and Sellesa (Hebrew: "Taanath-shiloh"), Macho and Astaroth (Hebrew: "Naarah" and "Ataroth"), Taphos (Hebrew: "Tappuah"), and Chelkana (merging Hebrew "the brook Kanah").
- **16:10** — The Septuagint adds an account of Pharaoh capturing, burning, and giving Gezer as a dowry (paralleling 1 Kings 9:16), whereas the Hebrew instead states that the Canaanites became forced labor.
