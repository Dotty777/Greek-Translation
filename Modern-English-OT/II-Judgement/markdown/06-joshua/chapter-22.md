## Text

**22:1** Then Joshua called together the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**22:2** and said to them, "You have heeded all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, and you have obeyed my voice according to all that he commanded you.

**22:3** You have not abandoned your brothers these many days; until this very day you have kept the commandment of the Lord your God.

**22:4** And now the Lord our God has given rest to our brothers, just as He said to them; now therefore, turn back and go to your houses and to the land of your possession, which Moses gave you on the other side of the Jordan.

**22:5** But be very careful to do the commandments and the law that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded us to do: to love the Lord our God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments and to hold fast to Him, and to serve Him with all your mind and with all your soul."

**22:6** And Joshua blessed them and sent them away; and they went to their houses.

**22:7** Now to the half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had given a portion in Bashan, and to the other half Joshua gave a portion with their brothers on the other side of the Jordan by the sea. And when Joshua sent them away to their houses and blessed them,

**22:8** they went away to their houses with much wealth, and very many livestock, and silver and gold and iron and much clothing; and they divided the plunder of the enemies with their brothers.

**22:9** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of the sons of Manasseh departed from the sons of Israel in Shiloh in the land of Canaan to go to Gilead, to the land of their possession, which they had inherited through the command of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**22:10** And they came to Gilgal of the Jordan, which is in the land of Canaan; and the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh built there an altar by the Jordan, a great altar to look at.

**22:11** And the sons of Israel heard it said, "Look, the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar on the borders of the land of Canaan, at Gilead of the Jordan, on the other side of the sons of Israel."

**22:12** And all the sons of Israel gathered together at Shiloh to go up and fight against them.

**22:13** And the sons of Israel sent to the sons of Reuben and to the sons of Gad and to the sons of the half-tribe of Manasseh, to the land of Gilead, Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the high priest,

**22:14** and ten of the leaders with him, one leader from each father's house from all the tribes of Israel; they are leaders of fathers' houses, commanders of thousands of Israel.

**22:15** And they came to the sons of Gad and to the sons of Reuben and to the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, and spoke to them, saying,

**22:16** "This is what the whole congregation of the Lord says: 'What is this trespass that you have committed before the God of Israel, to turn away today from the Lord by building an altar for yourselves, so that you become rebels against the Lord?

**22:17** Is the sin of Peor too small for us? For we have not been cleansed from it until this day, and there was a plague in the congregation of the Lord.

**22:18** And you have turned away today from the Lord; and it will be that if you rebel today against the Lord, tomorrow the wrath will be upon all Israel.

**22:19** And now, if your land of your possession is small, cross over into the land of the Lord's possession, where the tabernacle of the Lord dwells, and you will inherit among us; and do not become rebels against God, and do not rebel against the Lord by your building an altar outside the altar of the Lord our God.

**22:20** Look, did not Achar the son of Zerah surely commit a trespass regarding the devoted thing, and wrath came upon the whole congregation of Israel? And this one man alone died in his own sin.'"

**22:21** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered and spoke to the commanders of thousands of Israel, saying,

**22:22** "God, God is the Lord, and God, God Himself knows, and Israel itself will know; if in rebellion we have trespassed before the Lord, may He not rescue us in this;

**22:23** and if we have built for ourselves an altar to rebel against the Lord our God, to offer up upon it a sacrifice of whole burnt offerings, to offer upon it a sacrifice of deliverance, the Lord will require it.

**22:24** Rather, out of concern over a matter we did this, saying, 'So that tomorrow your children may not say to our children, "What have you to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?

**22:25** And the Lord has set the Jordan as a border between us and you, and you have no portion in the Lord." And your sons will estrange our sons, so that they do not revere the Lord.'

**22:26** And we said we would do this, to build this altar, not for burnt offerings nor for sacrifices,

**22:27** but so that this may be a witness between us and you and between our generations after us, to perform service to the Lord before Him with our burnt offerings and with our sacrifices and with the sacrifices of deliverance; and your children will not say to our children tomorrow, 'You have no portion in the Lord.'

**22:28** And we said, 'If it ever happens that they speak to us and to our generations tomorrow, then they will say, "Look at the likeness of the altar of the Lord, which our fathers made not for burnt offerings nor for sacrifices, but it is a witness between you and between us and between our sons."'

**22:29** Far be it from us, therefore, to turn away from the Lord in these present days, to rebel against the Lord by building an altar for the burnt offerings and for the sacrifices of salamein and for the sacrifice of deliverance, apart from the altar of the Lord that is before His tabernacle."

**22:30** And when Phinehas the priest and all the leaders of the congregation of Israel who were with him heard the words that the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh spoke, it pleased them.

**22:31** And Phinehas the priest said to the sons of Reuben and to the sons of Gad and to the half-tribe of Manasseh, "Today we know that the Lord is with us, because you have not committed a trespass before the Lord, and that you have rescued the sons of Israel from the hand of the Lord."

**22:32** And Phinehas the priest and the leaders returned from the sons of Reuben and from the sons of Gad and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, from Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and gave them the words in reply.

**22:33** And it pleased the sons of Israel; and they spoke to the sons of Israel, and blessed the God of the sons of Israel, and said they would no longer go up against them to war to utterly destroy the land of the sons of Reuben and of the sons of Gad and of the half-tribe of Manasseh; and they lived on it.

**22:34** And Joshua named the altar of the Reubenites and of the Gadites and of the half-tribe of Manasseh, and said, "It is a witness between them that the Lord is their God."

## Translator Notes

- **22:10** — Greek reads "Gilgal" (Galgala) of the Jordan (and "Gilead" of the Jordan in 22:11), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "districts" (gelilot). Throughout chapter 22, the Greek also generally distinguishes the contested altar (bomos, vv. 10-11, 16, 19, 23, 26, 34) from the authorized altar of the Lord (thysiasterion, vv. 19, 28-29).
- **22:19** — Greek reads "small" (mikra), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "unclean" (teme'ah), and has "against the Lord" instead of "against us" later in the verse.
- **22:20** — Greek preserves the name "Achar" (matching 1 Chronicles 2:7) rather than "Achan" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Joshua. Greek also lacks the negative particle in the final clause, reading "this one man alone died in his own sin" rather than "did not perish alone."
- **22:29** — Greek retains "salamein," a transliteration of the Hebrew sacrificial term shelamim ("peace offerings"), alongside its Greek equivalent "the sacrifice of deliverance."
- **22:34** — Greek credits Joshua with naming the altar and casts the statement in the third person ("between them ... their God"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the Transjordanian tribes name it in the first person.
