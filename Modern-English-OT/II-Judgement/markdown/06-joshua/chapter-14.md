## Text

**14:1** And these are those of the sons of Israel who received an inheritance in the land of Canaan, to whom Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the rulers of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel apportioned an inheritance.

**14:2** By lots they inherited, just as the Lord commanded by the hand of Joshua for the nine tribes and for the half-tribe,

**14:3** from the other side of the Jordan. And to the Levites he did not give an allotment among them,

**14:4** for the sons of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim; and no portion in the land was given to the Levites, except cities to live in, and their set-apart lands for the livestock, and their livestock.

**14:5** Just as the Lord commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

**14:6** And the sons of Judah came to Joshua in Gilgal, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, "You know the word that the Lord spoke to Moses, the man of God, concerning me and you in Kadesh Barnea.

**14:7** For I was forty years old when Moses the servant of God sent me from Kadesh Barnea to spy out the land, and I answered him with a word according to his mind.

**14:8** Yet my brothers who went up with me turned away the heart of the people, but I devoted myself to follow the Lord my God.

**14:9** And Moses swore on that day, saying, 'The land on which you stepped will be for you as an allotment and for your children forever, because you devoted yourself to follow after the Lord our God.'

**14:10** And now the Lord has sustained me, just as He said, this forty-fifth year since the Lord spoke this word to Moses, and Israel walked in the wilderness. And now, here I am today, eighty-five years old;

**14:11** I am still as strong today as when Moses sent me; in the same way I am strong now to go in and to go out to war.

**14:12** And now I ask you for this mountain, as the Lord said on that day; for you have heard this word on that day. And now the Anakim are there, fortified and great cities; therefore, if the Lord is with me, I will utterly destroy them, just as the Lord spoke to me."

**14:13** And Joshua blessed him, and he gave Hebron to Caleb the son of Jephunneh, the son of Kenaz, as an allotment.

**14:14** Therefore Hebron came to belong to Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as an allotment until this day, because he followed the command of the Lord, God of Israel.

**14:15** Now the name of Hebron formerly was the city of Argob; this was the mother city of the Anakim. And the land rested from war.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Greek reads "by the hand of Joshua" where the Hebrew has "by the hand of Moses," and in verse 3 omits the Hebrew clause explaining Moses's earlier grant to the two and a half tribes beyond the Jordan.
- **14:7** — The Greek reads "the servant of God" and "according to his mind" (third person) where the Hebrew reads "the servant of the Lord" and "as it was in my heart" (first person).
- **14:14** — The Greek reads "followed the command of the Lord" where the Hebrew has "wholly followed the Lord," and in verse 13 includes the addition "the son of Kenaz."
- **14:15** — The Greek reads "the city of Argob; this was the mother city of the Anakim" (Greek metropolis) where the Hebrew reads "Kiriath-arba (Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim)."
