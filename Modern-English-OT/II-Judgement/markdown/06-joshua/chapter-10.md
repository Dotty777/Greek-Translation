## Text

**10:1** Now when Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had captured Ai and utterly destroyed it—just as they had done to Jericho and its king, so they had done to Ai and its king—and that the inhabitants of Gibeon had defected to Joshua and to Israel,

**10:2** they were greatly afraid of them; for he knew that Gibeon was a great city, like one of the mother cities, and all its men were strong.

**10:3** And Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem sent to Elam king of Hebron, and to Phidon king of Jarmuth, and to Jephtha king of Lachish, and to Dabin king of Adullam, saying,

**10:4** "Come, come up to me and help me, and let us make war against Gibeon; for they have defected to Joshua and to the sons of Israel."

**10:5** And the five kings of the Jebusites went up—the king of Jerusalem, and the king of Hebron, and the king of Jarmuth, and the king of Lachish, and the king of Adullam—they and all their people, and they encamped around Gibeon and were besieging it.

**10:6** And the inhabitants of Gibeon sent to Joshua to the camp of Israel at Gilgal, saying, "Do not relax your hands from your servants; come up to us quickly and rescue us and help us, for all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hill country have assembled against us."

**10:7** And Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and all the fighting people with him, everyone mighty in strength.

**10:8** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for I have delivered them into your hands; not one of them will be left before you."

**10:9** And when Joshua came upon them suddenly, he had marched in all night from Gilgal.

**10:10** And the Lord threw them into panic before the sons of Israel, and the Lord crushed them with a great crushing at Gibeon; and they pursued them along the road of the ascent of Horonin, and kept cutting them down as far as Azekah and as far as Makkedah.

**10:11** Now while they were fleeing from before the sons of Israel on the descent of Horonin, the Lord hurled hailstones upon them from heaven as far as Azekah; and those who died because of the hailstones were more than those whom the sons of Israel killed with the sword in the battle.

**10:12** Then Joshua spoke to the Lord on the day God delivered the Amorite under the hand of Israel, when He crushed them at Gibeon and they were crushed before the sons of Israel; and Joshua said, "Let the sun stand still opposite Gibeon, and the moon opposite the valley of Aijalon."

**10:13** And the sun and the moon stood at a standstill until God took vengeance on their enemies. And the sun stood in the middle of heaven; it did not advance toward setting until the end of one day.

**10:14** And there was no day like that, neither before nor afterward, that God listened to a man; for the Lord fought alongside Israel.

**10:16** And these five kings fled and hid themselves in the cave at Makkedah.

**10:17** And it was reported to Joshua, saying, "The five kings have been found hidden in the cave at Makkedah."

**10:18** And Joshua said, "Roll stones against the mouth of the cave, and appoint men to keep watch over them;

**10:19** but you, do not stand still; keep pursuing after your enemies, and overtake their rear guard, and do not let them enter their cities; for the Lord our God has delivered them into our hands."

**10:20** And it happened, when Joshua and every son of Israel had finished cutting them down with a very great slaughter to the very end, that those who were escaping escaped into the fortified cities.

**10:21** And all the people returned to Joshua at Makkedah unharmed, and not one of the sons of Israel muttered with his tongue.

**10:22** And Joshua said, "Open the cave, and bring these five kings out of the cave."

**10:23** And they brought the five kings out of the cave: the king of Jerusalem, and the king of Hebron, and the king of Jarmuth, and the king of Lachish, and the king of Adullam.

**10:24** And when they had brought them out to Joshua, Joshua summoned all Israel and the commanders of the war who went with him, saying to them, "Step forward and place your feet on their necks." And coming near, they placed their feet on their necks.

**10:25** And Joshua said to them, "Do not fear them nor be fainthearted; be courageous and be strong, for in this way the Lord will do to all your enemies against whom you fight."

**10:26** And Joshua killed them and hung them on five trees; and they were hanging on the trees until evening.

**10:27** And it happened toward sunset that Joshua gave a command, and they took them down from the trees and threw them into the cave into which they had fled for refuge, and they rolled stones against the cave, to this day.

**10:28** And they captured Makkedah on that day, and they slaughtered it by the edge of the sword and utterly destroyed every breathing thing in it, and not one was left in it who survived and escaped; and they did to the king of Makkedah just as they had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:29** And Joshua went, and all Israel with him, from Makkedah to Libnah, and he laid siege to Libnah.

**10:30** And the Lord delivered it into the hands of Israel, and they captured it and its king, and he slaughtered it by the edge of the sword and every breathing thing in it, and no one was left in it who survived and escaped; and they did to its king just as they had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:31** And Joshua went, and all Israel with him, from Libnah to Lachish, and he encamped around it and laid siege to it.

**10:32** And the Lord delivered Lachish into the hands of Israel, and he captured it on the second day, and they slaughtered it by the edge of the sword and utterly destroyed it, just as they had done to Libnah.

**10:33** Then Helam king of Gaza went up to help Lachish; and Joshua struck him and his people down by the edge of the sword, until not one of them was left who survived and escaped.

**10:34** And Joshua went, and all Israel with him, from Lachish to Adullam, and he encamped around it and besieged it.

**10:35** And the Lord delivered it into the hand of Israel, and he captured it on that day, and he slaughtered it by the edge of the sword; and they slaughtered every breathing thing in it, just as they had done to Lachish.

**10:36** And Joshua went, and all Israel with him, to Hebron, and he encamped around it.

**10:37** And he struck it down by the edge of the sword, and every breathing thing—all that were in it; there was no one who survived. Just as they had done to Adullam, they utterly destroyed it and all that was in it.

**10:38** And Joshua and all Israel turned back to Debir, and having encamped around it,

**10:39** they captured it and its king and its villages, and they struck it down by the edge of the sword, and they utterly destroyed it and every breathing thing in it, and they did not leave in it a single one who survived. Just as they had done to Hebron and to its king, so they did to Debir and to its king.

**10:40** And Joshua struck down all the land of the hill country, and Nabai, and the lowland, and Asedoth, and its kings; they did not leave one of them who survived, and he utterly destroyed everything breathing life, just as the Lord, the God of Israel, had commanded.

**10:41** From Kadesh-barnea as far as Gaza, all of Goshen as far as Gibeon.

**10:42** And Joshua struck down all their kings and their land at one time, because the Lord, the God of Israel, was fighting alongside Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Greek names the king of Jerusalem "Adoni-bezek" (Hebrew "Adoni-zedek") and states that the Gibeonites "defected" rather than "made peace."
- **10:3** — Several royal and place names differ from the Hebrew: "Elam" (Hoham), "Phidon" (Piram), "Jephtha" (Japhia), and "Dabin king of Adullam" (Debir king of Eglon); 10:5 likewise calls them "the five kings of the Jebusites" (Hebrew "Amorites"), and 10:10-11 reads "Horonin" (Hebrew "Beth-horon").
- **10:13** — The Greek credits vengeance to "God" (Hebrew "the nation"), omits the Hebrew reference to the Book of Jashar, and lacks verses 10:15 and 10:43.
- **10:21** — Literally "not one of the sons of Israel muttered [or grunted] with his tongue"; unlike the Hebrew ("none moved his tongue against any of the people of Israel"), the Greek makes "the sons of Israel" the subject.
- **10:33** — The Greek reads "Helam king of Gaza" (Hebrew "Horam king of Gezer"); in 10:40, "Nabai" and "Asedoth" reflect the Greek rendering of Hebrew "the Negev" and "the slopes."
