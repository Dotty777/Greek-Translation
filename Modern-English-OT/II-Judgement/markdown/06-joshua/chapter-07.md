## Text

**7:1** And the sons of Israel committed a great offense and secretly took for themselves from the devoted thing; and Achar son of Carmi, son of Zambri, son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah, took from the devoted thing; and the Lord was enraged with anger against the sons of Israel.

**7:2** And Joshua sent men to Ai, which is near Bethel, saying, "Scout out Ai."

**7:3** And the men went up and scouted out Ai, and returned to Joshua and said to him, "Do not let all the people go up, but let about two thousand or three thousand men go up and take the city by siege; do not bring all the people up there, for they are few."

**7:4** And about three thousand men went up, and they fled from before the men of Ai.

**7:5** And the men of Ai killed about thirty-six men from among them, and pursued them from the gate and crushed them from the slope; and the heart of the people was terrified and became like water.

**7:6** And Joshua tore his garments, and Joshua fell to the ground on his face before the Lord until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they threw dust on their heads.

**7:7** And Joshua said, "I plead, Lord, why did Your servant bring this people across the Jordan, to hand them over to the Amorite to destroy us? And if only we had remained and settled beside the Jordan!

**7:8** And what shall I say, since Israel has turned its neck before its enemy?

**7:9** And when the Canaanite and all who inhabit the land hear, they will surround us and wipe us out from the earth; and what will You do with Your great name?"

**7:10** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Rise up! Why have you fallen like this on your face?

**7:11** The people have sinned and transgressed the covenant which I made with them; having stolen from the devoted thing, they put it into their belongings.

**7:12** The sons of Israel will by no means be able to stand firm before their enemies; they will turn their necks before their enemies, because they have become a devoted thing; I will no longer continue to be with you unless you remove the devoted thing from among yourselves.

**7:13** Rise up, consecrate the people, and tell them to consecrate themselves for tomorrow. Thus says the Lord God of Israel: 'The devoted thing is among you; you will not be able to stand up before your enemies until you remove the devoted thing from among you.

**7:14** And you shall all be gathered in the morning by tribes, and it shall be that the tribe which the Lord points out, you shall bring forward by clans; and the clan which the Lord points out, you shall bring forward by household; and the household which the Lord points out, you shall bring forward man by man.

**7:15** And whoever is pointed out shall be burned up with fire, and all that belongs to him, because he transgressed the covenant of the Lord and committed a lawless deed in Israel.'"

**7:16** And Joshua rose early and brought the people forward by tribes; and the tribe of Judah was pointed out.

**7:17** And it was brought forward by clans, and the Zerahite clan was pointed out; and it was brought forward man by man,

**7:18** and Achar son of Zambri, son of Zerah, was pointed out.

**7:19** And Joshua said to Achar, "Give glory today to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession, and tell me what you have done, and do not hide it from me."

**7:20** And Achar answered Joshua and said, "Truly I have sinned before the Lord God of Israel; I have acted in this way and that.

**7:21** I saw among the plunder a smooth multicolored cloak, and two hundred didrachmas of silver, and one golden tongue of fifty didrachmas, and desiring them, I took them; and look, they are hidden in my tent, and the silver is hidden underneath them."

**7:22** And Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent in the camp; and these things were hidden in the tent, and the silver was underneath them.

**7:23** And they brought them out of the tent and brought them to Joshua and the elders of Israel, and placed them before the Lord.

**7:24** And Joshua took Achar son of Zerah and brought him up to the Valley of Achor, and his sons and his daughters, and his calves and his pack animals and all his sheep, and his tent and all his possessions, and all the people were with him; and he brought them up to Emek-achor.

**7:25** And Joshua said to Achar, "Why have you destroyed us? May the Lord utterly destroy you, just as today."

**7:26** And all Israel stoned him with stones, and raised over him a great heap of stones; and the Lord ceased from the fury of His anger. Therefore he named it Emek-achor, until this day.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek preserves the distinct proper names Achar (Hebrew MT: Achan) and Zambri (MT: Zabdi). "Devoted thing" (Greek anathema) denotes spoils set apart to the Lord for destruction.
- **7:5** — The Greek reads "crushed them from the slope" (rendering the Hebrew place name Shebarim as a verb meaning "crushed/shattered") and "was terrified" where the Hebrew MT reads "melted."
- **7:7** — The Greek uniquely reads "why did Your servant bring this people across the Jordan" (rather than MT "why have You brought") and "beside the Jordan" (rather than MT "beyond the Jordan").
- **7:21** — Greek psilen poikilen denotes a smooth, multicolored cloak or rug (omitting MT's "from Shinar"). "Didrachmas" renders Hebrew shekels with a Greek silver weight unit, and "golden tongue" refers to a tongue-shaped bar or ingot of gold.
- **7:24** — The Greek renders the place name as "Valley of Achor" here and also transliterates the Hebrew phrase as the proper name "Emek-achor" at the end of 7:24 and in 7:26; in 7:26, the Greek reports only the stoning of Achar himself (singular "him") without mention of burning or stoning his family.
