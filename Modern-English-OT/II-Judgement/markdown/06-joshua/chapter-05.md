## Text

**5:1** And it came about, when the kings of the Amorites who were across the Jordan and the kings of Phoenicia who were by the sea heard that the Lord God had dried up the Jordan River from before the sons of Israel while they were crossing, that their minds melted and they were dismayed, and there was no understanding at all in them before the presence of the sons of Israel.

**5:2** Now about this time the Lord said to Joshua, "Make for yourself stone knives from sharp-edged rock, and sitting down, circumcise the sons of Israel."

**5:3** And Joshua made sharp-edged stone knives, and he circumcised the sons of Israel at the place called Hill of the Foreskins.

**5:4** Now as for the manner in which Joshua thoroughly cleansed the sons of Israel: all who were ever born on the way, and all who were ever uncircumcised among those who had come out of Egypt,

**5:5** all these Joshua circumcised; for forty-two years Israel had moved about in the desert of Madbaritis.

**5:6** Therefore, most of them, the fighting men who had come out of the land of Egypt, were uncircumcised, those who had disobeyed the commands of God, to whom He also decreed that they should not see the land which the Lord swore to our fathers to give, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**5:7** And in place of these He raised up their sons, whom Joshua circumcised because they had been born uncircumcised along the way.

**5:8** And having been circumcised, they remained still, staying right there in the camp until they were healed.

**5:9** And the Lord said to Joshua son of Nun, "On this very day I have taken away the reproach of Egypt from you." And he called the name of that place Gilgal.

**5:10** And the sons of Israel kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, from evening, to the west of Jericho, in the region across the Jordan, on the plain;

**5:11** and they ate from the grain of the land, unleavened bread and fresh grain.

**5:12** On this day the manna ceased after they had eaten from the grain of the land, and there was no longer manna for the sons of Israel; rather, they harvested the crop of the Phoenicians in that year.

**5:13** And it came about, while Joshua was at Jericho, that lifting up his eyes he saw a man standing opposite him, and his sword was drawn in his hand. And Joshua approached and said to him, "Are you ours, or one of the adversaries?"

**5:14** And he said to him, "I, commander of the army of the Lord, have now come." And Joshua fell on his face to the ground and said to him, "Master, what do you command your servant?"

**5:15** And the commander of the Lord said to Joshua, "Untie the sandal from your feet, for the place on which you are now standing is holy."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Greek uses "Phoenicia" (Phoinikes) for Canaan here (and "Phoenicians" in 5:12), a regular Septuagint geographical rendering.
- **5:2** — Greek reads "sitting down" (kathisas), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb ("sit" rather than "return/again"), and omits "a second time."
- **5:5** — Greek uniquely specifies "forty-two years" (versus forty in the Hebrew) and transliterates Hebrew midbar ("wilderness") as the place name "Madbaritis" (Madbareitidi).
- **5:10** — Greek locates the Passover "to the west of Jericho, in the region across the Jordan, on the plain," differing from the Hebrew "in the plains of Jericho."
- **5:12** — "crop" translates Greek kouran (literally "shearing" or "cutting," hence harvest or yield; some manuscripts read choran, "country"), and "Phoenicians" renders Phoinikon (for Hebrew "Canaan").
