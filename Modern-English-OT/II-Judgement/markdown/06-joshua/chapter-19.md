## Text

**19:1** And the second lot of the sons of Simeon came out, and their inheritance was in the midst of the lots of the sons of Judah.

**19:2** And their lot was Beersheba, Samaa, and Koladam,

**19:3** and Harsola, Bola, and Iason,

**19:4** and Elthula, Bula, and Hormah,

**19:5** and Ziklag, Beth-machereb, and Sarsusin,

**19:6** and Batharoth, and their fields: thirteen cities and their villages;

**19:7** Eremmon, Thalcha, Jether, and Ashan: four cities and their villages

**19:8** around their cities as far as Barek, going to Bameth toward the south. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon according to their clans.

**19:9** From the lot of Judah was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, because the portion of the sons of Judah was greater than theirs; and the sons of Simeon inherited in the midst of their lot.

**19:10** And the third lot came out for Zebulun according to their clans. The borders of their inheritance will be Esedekgola; their borders

**19:11** will be the sea and Maragelda, and they will reach to Beth-arabah, to the ravine that is facing Jekman;

**19:12** and they turned back from Sedduk, opposite from the east of Beth-shemesh, to the borders of Chaselothaith, and they will pass on to Dabiroth, and will go up to Phangai;

**19:13** and from there they will go around opposite toward the east to Gebere, to the city of Katasem, and they will pass on to Remmona Hamathar Haoza;

**19:14** and the borders will go around to the north to Hamoth, and their end will be at Gaiphael,

**19:15** and Katanath, Nabaal, Symoon, Jericho, and Bethman.

**19:16** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun according to their clans, cities and their villages.

**19:17** And for Issachar the fourth lot came out.

**19:18** And their borders were Jazel, Chesulloth, and Shunem,

**19:19** and Hagin, Siona, Reeroth, and Anaharath,

**19:20** and Dabiron, Kishon, and Rebes,

**19:21** and Remmas, Ieon, Tomman, Aimarek, and Bersaphes.

**19:22** And the borders will reach to Gaithbor and to Salim toward the sea and Beth-shemesh; and the end of its borders will be the Jordan.

**19:23** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Issachar according to their clans, the cities and their villages.

**19:24** And the fifth lot came out for Asher.

**19:25** And their borders were from Heleketh, Haleph, Baithok, and Keaph,

**19:26** and Elimelech, Amiel, and Maasa; and they will reach Carmel toward the sea, and Seion and Labanath.

**19:27** And they will turn from the rising of the sun and Beth-egeneth, and they will reach Zebulun, and from Gai and Phthaiel on the north, and the borders will enter Saphthaibethme and Inael, and will pass on to Chobamasomel,

**19:28** and Elbon, Raab, Ememaon, and Kanthan as far as great Sidon;

**19:29** and the borders will turn back to Ramah and as far as the spring of Masphassat and of the Tyrians; and the borders will turn back to Iasiph, and its end will be the sea, and from Leb and Echozob,

**19:30** and Archob, Aphek, and Raau.

**19:31** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Asher according to their clans, cities and their villages.

**19:32** And for Naphtali the sixth lot came out.

**19:33** And their borders were Moolam, Mola, Besemiin, Arme, Nabok, and Jephthamai as far as Dodam; and its ends were the Jordan.

**19:34** And the borders turn toward the sea to Enath-tabor, and from there they will pass on to Hiakana, and they will reach Zebulun on the south, and they will reach Asher toward the sea, and the Jordan from the rising of the sun.

**19:35** And the fortified cities of the Tyrians: Tyre, Homatha, Daketh, and Chinnereth;

**19:36** and Armaith, Harael, and Hazor,

**19:37** and Kedesh, Assarei, and the spring of Hazor,

**19:38** and Keroe, Megala, Harim, Beth-thame, and Thessamys.

**19:39** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali.

**19:40** And for Dan the seventh lot came out.

**19:41** And their borders were Sarath and Asa, cities of Sammaus,

**19:42** and Salabin, Ammon, and Silatha,

**19:43** and Aijalon, Timnah, and Ekron,

**19:44** and Alkatha, Begethon, and Gebeelan,

**19:45** and Hazor, Banaibakat, and Gath-rimmon,

**19:46** and from the sea, Jerakon, a border near Joppa.

**19:47** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Dan according to their clans, their cities and their villages. And the sons of Dan did not drive out the Amorite who was pressing them in the hill country; and the Amorites would not allow them to go down into the valley, and they pressed back from them the border of their portion.

**19:48** And the sons of Judah went and fought against Lachish, and captured it and struck it with the edge of the sword, and settled in it and called its name Lasenndak. And the Amorite persisted in living in Elom and in Salamin; and the hand of Ephraim grew heavy upon them, and they came under tribute to them.

**19:49** And they went to take possession of the land according to their border. And the sons of Israel gave a lot to Joshua the son of Nun among them

**19:50** by the command of God; and they gave him the city that he asked for, Thamarchares, which is in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city and lived in it.

**19:51** These are the divisions which Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun and the leaders of the ancestral houses in the tribes of Israel apportioned as an inheritance by lots in Shiloh before the Lord beside the doors of the tent of testimony; and they went to take possession of the land.

## Translator Notes

- **19:6** — The Greek reads "and their fields" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists the town Sharuhen, and throughout 19:2-8 the Septuagint preserves several distinct place-name forms (including Samaa, Koladam, Harsola, Iason, Beth-machereb, Sarsusin, Batharoth, Thalcha, Barek, and Bameth).
- **19:15** — The Septuagint reads "Jericho" and "Bethman" here where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Idalah and Bethlehem. Across Zebulun's border (19:10-15), the Greek also preserves distinct geographical names such as Esedekgola, Maragelda, Beth-arabah, Sedduk, Beth-shemesh, Phangai, Gebere, Katasem, and Remmona Hamathar Haoza (treating Hebrew descriptive words as proper names).
- **19:26** — "Seion and Labanath" (Greek Seion kai Labanath) reflects the Septuagint's rendering of the coastal boundary name Shihor-libnath in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (distinct from Mount Zion); the Greek also reads Elimelech, Amiel, and Maasa for Hebrew Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal.
- **19:29** — The Greek reads "the spring of Masphassat and of the Tyrians" where the Hebrew has "the fortified city of Tyre," and in 19:35 similarly reads "the fortified cities of the Tyrians: Tyre" for Hebrew Ziddim and Zer. Across 19:18-38 the Septuagint preserves many distinct place-name forms (including Jazel, Hagin, Reeroth, Ieon, Tomman, Aimarek, Gaithbor, Salim, Saphthaibethme, Chobamasomel, Moolam, Besemiin, Hiakana, and Thessamys).
- **19:48** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text (19:47) describes the sons of Dan capturing Leshem and renaming it Dan, the Septuagint attributes the campaign to the sons of Judah against Lachish, renaming it Lasenndak. In 19:47-48 the Greek also includes material paralleling Judges 1:34-35 concerning the Amorites in Elom and Salamin and the tribute imposed by Ephraim.
- **19:50** — The Greek reads "of God" (MT "of the Lord") and names Joshua's city Thamarchares (MT Timnath-serah; cf. Timnath-heres in Judges 2:9). In 19:49 and 19:51, the Septuagint also has "went to take possession of the land" where the Hebrew has "finished dividing the land."
