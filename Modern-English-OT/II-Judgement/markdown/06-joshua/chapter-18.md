## Text

**18:1** And the whole congregation of the sons of Israel was assembled at Shiloh, and they pitched the tent of testimony there, and the land was subdued by them.

**18:2** And the sons of Israel who had not received an inheritance were left, seven tribes.

**18:3** And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "How long will you be slack in inheriting the land that the Lord our God has given?

**18:4** Give from among yourselves three men from each tribe, and let them rise up and go through the land, and map it out before me just as it will be necessary to go through it." And they came over to him,

**18:5** and he divided seven portions for them. Judah will stand as a border for them on the south, and the sons of Joseph will stand for them on the north.

**18:6** "Now you divide the land into seven portions and bring it to me here, and I will draw a lot for you before the Lord our God.

**18:7** For there is no portion for the sons of Levi among you, because the priesthood of the Lord is his portion; and Gad and Reuben and half the tribe of Manasseh received their inheritance beyond the Jordan toward the east, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave them."

**18:8** And the men rose up and went. And Joshua commanded the men who were going to survey the land, saying, "Go and survey the land, and come to me; and here I will draw a lot for you before the Lord in Shiloh."

**18:9** And they went and surveyed the land, and saw it, and wrote it down according to cities in seven portions in a book, and brought it to Joshua.

**18:10** And Joshua cast a lot for them in Shiloh before the Lord.

**18:11** And the lot of Benjamin came out first according to their clans; and the borders of their lot went out between Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**18:12** And their borders were on the north: from the Jordan the borders will go up along the back of Jericho on the north, and they will go up to the mountain toward the sea, and its termination will be Mabdareitis Baithon;

**18:13** and from there the borders will cross over to Luz, to the back of Luz on its south, that is, Bethel. And the borders will go down to Maatarothorech, to the hill country that is south of lower Beth-horon;

**18:14** and the borders will cross over and pass on to the mountain that looks along the sea on the south, from the mountain facing Beth-horon on the south, and its termination will be at Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, a city of the sons of Judah; this is the side toward the sea.

**18:15** And the side toward the south is from the edge of Kiriath-baal; and the borders will cross over to Gasein, to the spring of water of Nephtoah;

**18:16** and the borders will go down to the edge, that is, facing the glen of Sonnam, which is on the edge of Emek-raphaein on the north; and they will go down to Gehenna, to the south of the Jebusite on the south; they will go down to the spring of Rogel;

**18:17** and they will cross over to the spring of Beth-shemesh, and pass on to Galiaoth, which is opposite toward the ascent of Aithamein, and go down to the stone of Baion of the sons of Reuben;

**18:18** and they will cross over along the back of Beth-arabah on the north, and go down to the borders, to the back of the sea on the north;

**18:19** and the termination of the borders will be at the crest of the Sea of Salt on the north, to the edge of the Jordan on the south. These are the borders on the south.

**18:20** And the Jordan will mark the border on the east side. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, its borders all around according to clans.

**18:21** And the cities of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans were Jericho, and Bethegaio, and Amekaseis,

**18:22** and Bethabara, and Sara, and Besana,

**18:23** and Aiein, and Parah, and Jephratha,

**18:24** and Karapha, and Chephirah, and Monei, and Geba—twelve cities and their villages;

**18:25** Gibeon, and Ramah, and Beeroth,

**18:26** and Massema, and Meiron, and Hamoke,

**18:27** and Pheira, and Kaphan, and Nakan, and Selekan, and Taralah,

**18:28** and Jebus, that is, Jerusalem, and cities, and Gabaothiarim—thirteen cities and their villages. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans.

## Translator Notes

- **18:4** — At the end of 18:4 and through 18:5 the Greek shifts from Joshua's direct speech into third-person narrative ("And they came over to him, and he divided...") before resuming direct address in 18:6; in 18:4 the Greek also reads "to go through" (dielthein) rather than "to divide."
- **18:12** — Along Benjamin's border in 18:12-15 the Septuagint preserves the distinct names Mabdareitis Baithon (transliterating Hebrew "wilderness of Beth-aven"), Maatarothorech in 18:13 (Hebrew: "Ataroth-addar"), and Gasein in 18:15 (Hebrew: "westward").
- **18:16** — The Septuagint transliterates Hebrew "valley of Hinnom" as Gehenna, alongside the distinct names Sonnam (Hebrew: "Ben-hinnom") and Emek-raphaein (transliterating Hebrew "valley of Rephaim"). The phrase "to the south of the Jebusite" reflects the Greek word for "south," likely an inner-Greek shift from the similar word for "back/slope."
- **18:17** — The Greek uses the distinct names Galiaoth (Hebrew: "Geliloth"), Aithamein (Hebrew: "Adummim"), and Baion (Hebrew: "Bohan," with plural "sons of Reuben"), and in 18:18 reads "to the back of the sea" where the Hebrew refers to Beth-hoglah.
- **18:21** — In the first Benjamite city list (18:21-24), the Septuagint has the distinct names Bethegaio, Amekaseis, Bethabara, Sara, Besana, Aiein, Jephratha, Karapha, and Monei; it names thirteen cities (splitting Hebrew Chephar-ammoni into Karapha, Chephirah, and Monei) while retaining the summary "twelve cities."
- **18:26** — In the second city list (18:26-28), the Septuagint has the distinct names Massema, Meiron, Hamoke, Pheira, Kaphan, Nakan, Selekan, and Gabaothiarim, includes the unusual phrase "and cities" in 18:28, and totals thirteen cities (Hebrew: fourteen).
