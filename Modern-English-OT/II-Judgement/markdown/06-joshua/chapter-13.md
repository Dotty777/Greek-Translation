## Text

**13:1** Now Joshua was old, advanced in days. And the Lord said to Joshua, "You are advanced in days, and much of the land remains for an inheritance.

**13:2** And this is the land that remains: the territory of the Philistines, the Geshurite, and the Canaanite;

**13:3** from the uninhabited land facing Egypt as far as the borders of Ekron on the left of the Canaanites, it is reckoned to the five satrapies of the Philistines: the Gazite, and the Ashdodite, and the Ashkelonite, and the Gittite, and the Ekronite, and the Hivite;

**13:4** from Teman and to all the land of Canaan opposite Gaza, and the Sidonians as far as Taphek, as far as the borders of the Amorites;

**13:5** and all the land of Galiath of the Philistines, and all Lebanon toward the rising of the sun, from Galgaa under Mount Hermon as far as the entrance of Hamath.

**13:6** Everyone who inhabits the hill country from Lebanon as far as Maserethmemphonmaim, all the Sidonians, I will utterly destroy them from before Israel; but distribute it as an allotment to Israel, just as I commanded you.

**13:7** And now divide this land as an inheritance to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**13:8** from the Jordan as far as the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun you will give it; the Great Sea will form the border." To the tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh, to Reuben and to Gad, Moses gave on the other side of the Jordan; toward the rising of the sun Moses the servant of the Lord had given to him,

**13:9** from Aroer, which is on the edge of the brook of Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the ravine, and all the Mishor from Daidaban;

**13:10** all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the borders of the sons of Ammon;

**13:11** and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurite and of the Maacathite, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Acha;

**13:12** all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Edrei and in Ashtaroth; he was left from the giants, and Moses struck him down and utterly destroyed him.

**13:13** And the sons of Israel did not utterly destroy the Geshurite and the Maacathite and the Canaanite; and the king of the Geshurite and the Maacathite lived among the sons of Israel until this day.

**13:14** Only to the tribe of Levi was no inheritance given; the Lord, the God of Israel, He is their inheritance, as the Lord said to them. And this is the distribution that Moses distributed to the sons of Israel in Araboth of Moab, on the other side of the Jordan opposite Jericho.

**13:15** And Moses gave to the tribe of Reuben according to their clans.

**13:16** And their territory was from Aroer, which is facing the ravine of Arnon, and the city that is in the ravine of Arnon, and all the Mishor

**13:17** as far as Heshbon, and all the cities that are in the Mishor, and Dibon, and Baimon Baal, and the house of Meelboth,

**13:18** and Basan, and Bakedmoth, and Mephaath,

**13:19** and Kiriathaim, and Sibmah, and Serada, and Seion on Mount Enab,

**13:20** and Beth-peor, and Ashdoth-pisgah, and Beth-thaseinoth,

**13:21** and all the cities of the Mishor, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, whom Moses struck down, him and the leaders of Midian, and Evi, and Robok, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba the ruler, the spoils of Sihon, and those who inhabited Sihon.

**13:22** And they killed Balaam the son of Beor, the diviner, in the rout.

**13:23** And the borders of Reuben were the Jordan as a border. This is the inheritance of the sons of Reuben according to their clans, their cities and their settlements.

**13:24** Now Moses gave to the sons of Gad according to their clans.

**13:25** And their territory was Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half of the land of the sons of Ammon as far as Araba, which is facing Arad,

**13:26** and from Heshbon as far as Araboth opposite Mizpah, and Botanei, and Baan as far as the borders of Dibon,

**13:27** and Enadom, and Othargaei, and Bainthanabra, and Succoth, and Zaphon, and the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon; and the Jordan will form the border as far as a part of the Sea of Chinnereth beyond the Jordan on the east.

**13:28** This is the inheritance of the sons of Gad according to their clans; they will turn the neck before their enemies, because it came to be according to their clans: their cities and their settlements.

**13:29** And Moses gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh according to their clans.

**13:30** And their territory was from Maana, and all the kingdom of Bashan, and all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the villages of Jair that are in Bashan, sixty cities;

**13:31** and half of Gilead, and in Ashtaroth and in Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, to those of Machir, sons of Manasseh, and to half of the sons of Machir, sons of Manasseh, according to their clans.

**13:32** These are those to whom Moses apportioned an inheritance beyond the Jordan in Araboth of Moab, on the other side of the Jordan opposite Jericho on the east.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Greek reads "the uninhabited land" where the Hebrew names the Shihor, uses the literal Semitic idiom "on the left" for north, and reads "the Hivite" where the Hebrew has the Avvim.
- **13:5** — In verses 4-6 the Greek preserves several distinctive place names where the Hebrew differs: Teman (for "south"), Taphek (for Aphek), Galiath of the Philistines (for the land of the Gebalites), Galgaa (for Baal-gad), and Maserethmemphonmaim (for Misrephoth-maim).
- **13:8** — The opening clause ("from the Jordan as far as the Great Sea... will form the border") is a Septuagint addition extending the Lord's speech into verse 8. In verses 9-14 the Greek also reads Daidaban (for Medeba to Dibon), Acha (for Salecah), and transliterates Hebrew araboth ("plains") as Araboth (vv. 14, 26, 32).
- **13:21** — The Greek reads "the spoils of Sihon, and those who inhabited Sihon" where the Hebrew has "vassals of Sihon, who lived in the land," and in verse 22 reads "in the rout" (Greek rhope) where the Hebrew has "with the sword." Verses 17-21 also preserve Greek-distinct names: Baimon Baal, house of Meelboth, Basan, Bakedmoth, Serada, Seion, Mount Enab, Beth-thaseinoth, and Robok.
- **13:28** — The clause "they will turn the neck before their enemies, because it came to be according to their clans" is unique to the Septuagint. Verses 25-30 also preserve Greek-distinct place names: Araba, Arad, Botanei, Baan, Dibon (for Debir), Enadom, Othargaei, Bainthanabra, and Maana.
