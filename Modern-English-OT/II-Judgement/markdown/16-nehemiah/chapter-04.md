## Text

**4:1** And it happened, when Sanballat and Tobiah and the Arabs and the Ammonites heard that the growth went up on the walls of Jerusalem, that the breaches began to be closed up, it appeared very evil to them.

**4:2** And they all gathered together to come to do battle in Jerusalem.

**4:3** And we prayed to our God, and we stationed watchmen against them day and night because of them.

**4:4** And Judah said, "The strength of the enemies has been crushed, and there is a large crowd, and we will not be able to build on the wall."

**4:5** And those oppressing us said, "They will not know and will not see until we come into their midst and kill them and stop the work."

**4:6** And it happened, when the Jews who lived next to them came, they said to us, "They are coming up from all the places against us."

**4:7** And I stationed them in the lowest parts of the place behind the wall in the sheltered places, and I stationed the people by clans with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

**4:8** And I looked, and I rose up and said to the nobles and to the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them; remember our God, who is great and awesome, and do battle for your brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your houses."

**4:9** And it happened, when our enemies heard that it was known to us, God frustrated their counsel; and all of us returned to the wall, each man to his work.

**4:10** And it happened from that day that half of the shaken-out ones were doing the work, and half of them were holding fast, and there were spears and shields and the bows and the breastplates, and the rulers were behind the whole house of Judah,

**4:11** who were building on the wall. And those lifting with the carrying-straps were in arms; with one hand each was doing the work itself, and with one was holding the javelin.

**4:12** And as for the builders, each man had his sword strapped at his waist, and they were building; and the one sounding the horn was next to him.

**4:13** And I said to the nobles and to the rulers and to the rest of the people, "The work is broad and great, and we are scattered on the wall, each man far from his brother.

**4:14** In whatever place you hear the sound of the horn, there you will gather to us, and our God will fight for us."

**4:15** And we were doing the work, and half of them were holding the spears from the rising until the dawn, until the coming out of the stars.

**4:16** And at that time I said to the people, "Spend the night in the midst of Jerusalem, and let the night be a watch for you, and the day work."

**4:17** And I was there, and the men of the watch behind me, and not a man among us was taking off his clothes.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — Greek reads "the strength of the enemies" and "a large crowd" (or "much trouble"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("the strength of the burden-bearers" and "much rubble").
- **4:7** — Greek "in the sheltered places" (en tois skepeinois) contrasts with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "in the exposed/open places."
- **4:10** — Greek "the shaken-out ones" (ton ektetinagmenon) reflects the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew "young men/servants" (na'ar) via the homonymous root meaning "to shake out" (see also 5:15).
- **4:12** — Greek reads "next to him" (autou) rather than "next to me" as in the Hebrew text.
- **4:15** — Greek reads "from the rising until the dawn" with an extra "until" (heos), whereas the Hebrew text reads "from the rising of the dawn."
