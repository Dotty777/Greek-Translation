## Text

**1:1** The words of Nehemiah son of Helkiah. And it happened in the month of Secheelu, in the twentieth year, that I was in Susa Abira.

**1:2** And Hanani, one of my brothers, came, he and men of Judah, and I asked them about those who had been saved, who were left from the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

**1:3** And they said to me, "Those who remain, who were left from the captivity there in the province, in the city, are in great distress and in reproach; and the walls of Jerusalem are torn down, and its gates were burned with fire."

**1:4** And it happened, when I heard these words, that I sat down and wept and mourned for days, and I was fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

**1:5** And I said, "Please, not so, Lord God of heaven, the Mighty One, the great and awesome, keeping the covenant and Your mercy for those who love Him and for those who keep His commandments:

**1:6** let Your ear now be attentive and Your eyes be open to hear the prayer of Your servant, which I am praying before You today, day and night, concerning the sons of Israel Your servants, and confessing regarding the sins of the sons of Israel which we have sinned against You; both I and the house of my father have sinned.

**1:7** We have utterly dissolved, and we have not kept the commandments and the ordinances and the judgments which You commanded Moses Your servant.

**1:8** Remember now the word which You commanded Moses Your servant, saying, 'If you break faith, I will scatter you among the peoples;

**1:9** and if you return to Me, and keep My commandments and do them, if your dispersion is from the end of heaven, from there I will gather them, and I will bring them into the place which I have chosen to make My name dwell there.'

**1:10** And they are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great power and by Your mighty hand.

**1:11** Please, not so, but let Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants who desire to fear Your name; and grant success now to Your servant today, and grant him compassion before this man." And I was a eunuch to the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text reads Helkiah (Chelkia) where the Hebrew has Hacaliah, Secheelu where the Hebrew has Chislev, and transliterates Hebrew habbirah ('the citadel') as abira.
- **1:5** — Greek me de (lit. 'not so, please', also in 1:11) renders an urgent Hebrew entreaty; the Greek also shifts from second person ('Your mercy') to third person ('love Him... His commandments').
- **1:7** — Greek dialysei dielysamen (lit. 'with dissolution we have dissolved') renders an emphatic Hebrew idiom for acting corruptly or destructively, and omits 'against You'.
- **1:11** — The Greek text reads eunouchos ('eunuch', attested in major Septuagint witnesses) rather than oinochoos ('cupbearer', matching the Hebrew).
