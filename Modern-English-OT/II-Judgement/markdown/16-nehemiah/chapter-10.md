## Text

**10:1** And in all these things we make a pledge and write it down, and our leaders, our Levites, and our priests seal it.

**10:2** And over those who sealed: Nehemiah son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah

**10:3** son of Seraiah, and Azariah, and Jeremiah,

**10:4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**10:5** Hattush, Shebani, Malluch,

**10:6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**10:7** Daniel, Tnatoth, Baruch,

**10:8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**10:9** Nadeiah, Belseiah, Shemaiah; these were the priests.

**10:10** And the Levites: Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadab, Kadmiel

**10:11** and his brothers: Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kanta,

**10:12** Pelaiah, Hanan, Mica, Rehob,

**10:13** Hashabiah, Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**10:14** Hodum, the sons of Benjamin.

**10:15** The leaders of the people: Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, the sons of Bani,

**10:16** the sons of Bani, Azgad, Bebai,

**10:17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**10:18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**10:19** Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai,

**10:20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,

**10:21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**10:22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**10:23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Aiah,

**10:24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**10:25** Hallohesh, Phadaeis, Shobek,

**10:26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**10:27** and Ara, Hainan, Enam,

**10:28** Malluch, Harim, Baanah.

**10:29** And the rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the Nethinim, and everyone who came over from the peoples of the land to the law of God, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, everyone who had knowledge and understanding,

**10:30** prevailed over their brothers, cursed them, and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the law of God, which was given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, and to observe and do all our commandments and His judgments,

**10:31** and not to give our daughters to the peoples of the land, and we will not take their daughters for our sons.

**10:32** And as for the peoples of the land who bring wares and all merchandise on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and we will forego the seventh year and the exaction of every hand.

**10:33** And we will establish commandments upon ourselves, to lay upon ourselves a third of a didrachma each year for the service of the house of our God,

**10:34** for the bread of the Presence and the continual offering, and for the continual burnt offering, of the Sabbaths, of the new moons, for the feasts and for the holy things, and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for the works of the house of our God.

**10:35** And we cast lots concerning the lot of the wood offering, the priests and the Levites and the people, to bring it to the house of our God, for the house of our ancestral families, at times of seasons, year by year, to burn around the altar of our God, as it is written in the book;

**10:36** and to bring the first produce of our land and the first produce of the fruit of every tree, year by year, to the house,

**10:37** and the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the law, and the firstborn of our cattle and of our flocks, to bring to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God.

**10:38** And the firstfruits of our grain and the fruit of every tree, of wine and of oil, we will bring to the priests, to the treasury of the house of God, and a tithe of our land to the Levites; and the Levites themselves are the ones collecting tithes in all the cities of our labor.

**10:39** And the priest Aaron will be with the Levite at the tithe of the Levite, and the Levites will bring up the tithe of the tithe to the house of our God, to the treasuries, to the house of God.

**10:40** For the sons of Israel and the Levites will bring to the storehouses the firstfruits of the grain and of the wine and of the oil, and there are the holy vessels, and the priests and the ministers and the gatekeepers and the singers; and we will not abandon the house of our God.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — The Greek links Zedekiah to Seraiah with "son of Seraiah," while 10:2 reads literally "over those who sealed" and omits "the governor" after Nehemiah's name.
- **10:7** — The Greek text preserves several distinct name forms in the priestly and Levitical lists: "Shebani" (10:5, MT "Shebaniah"), "Tnatoth" (10:7, MT "Ginnethon"), "Nadeiah" and "Belseiah" (10:9, MT "Maaziah, Bilgai"), "Henadab" (10:10, MT "Henadad"), and "Kanta" (10:11, MT "Kelita").
- **10:14** — Instead of MT "Hodiah, Bani, Beninu," the Greek reads "Hodum, the sons of Benjamin" and repeats "the sons of Bani" in 10:15-16; later in the lay list it reads "Aiah" (10:23, MT "Anaiah"), "Phadaeis" (10:25, MT "Pilha"), and "Ara, Hainan, Enam" (10:27, MT "Ahijah, Hanan, Anan").
- **10:30** — The Greek renders the Hebrew word for "their nobles" as a verb, "cursed them," and reads "all our commandments" without "of the Lord our Lord."
- **10:39** — The Greek reads "the priest Aaron" (omitting "son of") and ends with "to the house of God" (MT "to the house of the storehouse"); similarly, 10:35 reads "concerning the lot of the wood offering" and "in the book" (MT "in the law"), omitting "of the Lord" there and in 10:36.
