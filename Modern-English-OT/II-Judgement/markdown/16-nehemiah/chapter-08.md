## Text

**8:1** And the seventh month arrived, and the sons of Israel were in their cities, and all the people gathered together as one man into the open square in front of the Water Gate. And they told Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the law of Moses, which he commanded Israel.

**8:2** And Ezra the priest brought the law before the assembly, both men and women and everyone who could listen with understanding, on the first day of the seventh month.

**8:3** And he read from it from the hour when the sun shone forth until the middle of the day, facing the men and the women, and they understood; and the ears of all the people were directed toward the book of the law.

**8:4** And Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform, and next to him stood Mattithiah, Shemaiah, Ananiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah on his right, and on the left Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, and Zechariah.

**8:5** And Ezra opened the book before all the people, for he was above the people; and it happened that when he opened it, all the people stood up.

**8:6** And he blessed the Lord, the great God, and all the people answered and said, "Amen," and they bowed low and worshiped the Lord, face to the ground.

**8:7** And Jeshua, Benaiah, and Sherebiah were giving the people understanding in the law, and the people were in their place.

**8:8** And they read from the book of the law of God, and Ezra was teaching and explaining clearly in the knowledge of the Lord, and the people understood in the reading.

**8:9** And Nehemiah spoke, and Ezra the priest and scribe and the Levites were giving the people understanding, and they said to all the people, "It is a holy day to the Lord our God; do not mourn or weep." For all the people were weeping when they heard the words of the law.

**8:10** And he said to them, "Go, eat rich foods and drink sweet drinks, and send a portion to those who have nothing, for the day is holy to our Lord; and do not be disheartened, for He is our strength."

**8:11** And the Levites were quieting the people, saying, "Be quiet, for the day is holy, and do not be cast down."

**8:12** And all the people went away to eat and to drink and to send portions and to celebrate with great gladness, because they understood the words that he had made known to them.

**8:13** And on the second day the leaders of the ancestral families gathered together with all the people, the priests, and the Levites to Ezra the scribe, to give attention to all the words of the law.

**8:14** And they found written in the law which the Lord had commanded Moses, that the sons of Israel should live in booths at the feast in the seventh month,

**8:15** and that they should signal with trumpets in all their cities and in Jerusalem. And Ezra said, "Go out to the mountain and bring olive leaves, leaves of cypress trees, myrtle leaves, palm leaves, and leaves of dense trees, to make booths according to what is written."

**8:16** And the people went out and brought them, and they made booths for themselves, each man on his roof, and in their courtyards, and in the courtyards of the house of God, and in the squares of the city, and as far as the Gate of Ephraim.

**8:17** And all the assembly, those who had returned from the captivity, made booths and sat in booths; for the sons of Israel had not done so from the days of Joshua son of Nun until that day, and there was great gladness.

**8:18** And he read from the book of the law of God day by day, from the first day until the last day; and they kept a feast for seven days, and on the eighth day a closing festival.

## Translator Notes

- **8:4** — The Greek names Shemaiah (Samaias) and Ananiah (Anania) differ slightly from Hebrew MT Shema and Anaiah, and the Greek text lists four men on the left rather than seven.
- **8:7** — The Greek names only three Levites here (with Benaiah, Greek Banaia, corresponding to Hebrew MT Bani), whereas the Hebrew MT lists thirteen.
- **8:8** — The Greek text explicitly names Ezra as teaching and explaining clearly in the knowledge of the Lord, differing from the Hebrew MT.
- **8:10** — The Greek reads simply "for He is our strength" (or possibly "for it is our strength"), lacking the phrase "the joy of the Lord" found in the Hebrew MT and some Greek manuscripts.
- **8:15** — The Greek reads "signal with trumpets" and introduces the command with "And Ezra said," whereas the Hebrew MT has "proclaim and publish a word... saying"; the Greek also specifies "cypress trees" where MT has "wild olive."
