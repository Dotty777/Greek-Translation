## Text

**13:1** On that day it was read in the book of Moses in the ears of the people; and it was found written in it that Ammonites and Moabites should not enter into the assembly of God forever,

**13:2** because they did not meet the sons of Israel with bread and with water, and they hired Balaam against him to curse, and our God turned the curse into a blessing.

**13:3** And it happened, when they heard the law, that all of mixed descent in Israel were separated.

**13:4** And before this, Eliashib the priest, living in a storeroom of the house of our God, was closely related to Tobiah.

**13:5** And he made for himself a large storeroom, and there previously they were putting the manaam, and the frankincense, and the vessels, and the tithe of the grain and of the wine and of the oil, the command of the Levites and of the singers and of the gatekeepers, and the firstfruits of the priests.

**13:6** And during all this I was not in Jerusalem, because in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon I went to the king, and after the end of days I asked the king,

**13:7** and I came to Jerusalem; and I understood the evil, to make a storeroom for him in the court of the house of God.

**13:8** And it seemed very evil to me, and I threw all the goods of the house of Tobiah from the storeroom;

**13:9** and I spoke, and I purified the storerooms, and I returned there the vessels of the house of God, the manna, and the frankincense.

**13:10** And I learned that the portions of the Levites had not been given, and the Levites and the singers who did the work had fled, each man to his field.

**13:11** And I contended and said, "Why has the house of God been forsaken?" And I gathered them together, and they set them at their station.

**13:12** And all Judah brought the tithe of the wheat and of the wine and of the oil into the treasuries,

**13:13** under the hand of Shelemiah the priest, and Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah from the Levites; and under their hand was Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Nathaniah, because they were considered faithful; it was upon them to distribute to their brothers.

**13:14** Remember me, God, in this, and do not let my mercy be blotted out that I have done in the house of the Lord God.

**13:15** In those days I saw in Judah people treading winepresses on the Sabbath, and bringing sheaves, and loading them onto the donkeys, as well as wine, and grapes, and figs, and every load, and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day; and I testified on the day of their selling.

**13:16** And they lived in it, bringing fish and all merchandise, selling on the Sabbath to the sons of Judah and in Jerusalem.

**13:17** And I contended with the freeborn sons of Judah and said to them, "What is this evil thing that you are doing, and profaning the Sabbath day?

**13:18** Did your fathers not do this, and our God brought upon them and upon us all these evils, and upon this city? And are you adding wrath upon Israel by profaning the Sabbath?"

**13:19** And it happened, when the gates of Jerusalem were positioned before the Sabbath, that I shut the gates, and I gave word that they should not be opened after the Sabbath; and I stationed some at the gates, so as not to carry loads on the Sabbath day.

**13:20** And they all spent the night and made sales outside Jerusalem once and twice.

**13:21** And I testified against them and said to them, "Why are you spending the night opposite the wall? If you do it a second time, I will stretch out my hand against you." From that time they did not come on the Sabbath.

**13:22** And I spoke to the Levites, who were purifying themselves and coming, guarding the gates, consecrating the Sabbath day. For these things remember me, God, and spare me according to the abundance of Your mercy.

**13:23** And in those days I saw the Jews who had married Ashdodite, Ammonite, Moabite women,

**13:24** and half of their sons were speaking the language of Ashdod, and they did not know how to speak the language of Judah.

**13:25** And I contended with them and cursed them; and I struck men among them and made them swear by God, "You must not give your daughters to their sons, and you must not take any of their daughters for your sons.

**13:26** Did not Solomon king of Israel sin in this way? And among many nations there was no king like him, and he was beloved by God, and God appointed him as king over all Israel, and even him the foreign women turned aside.

**13:27** And we will not listen to you to do all this evil, to break faith with our God by marrying foreign women."

**13:28** And there were some of the sons of Joiada son of Eliashib the high priest, son-in-law of Sanballat; and I expelled them from me.

**13:29** Remember them, God, on account of the kinship of the priesthood and of the covenant of the priesthood, and the Levites.

**13:30** And I purified them from everything foreign, and I established divisions for the priests and for the Levites, each man according to his work,

**13:31** and the gift of the wood-bearers at times from time to time and in the bacchuria. Remember me, our God, for goodness.

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — The term "manaam" (and "manna" in verse 9) is an untranslated Greek transliteration of the Hebrew word for "grain offering" (minhah). In verse 4, the Greek describes Eliashib as "living in" rather than "appointed over" a storeroom.
- **13:19** — "Were positioned" renders Greek katestesan (Hebrew has "grew dark"), and "after the Sabbath" reflects the Greek omission of "until." The Greek text also leaves the direct object of "I stationed" unexpressed.
- **13:23** — "Had married" (and "by marrying" in verse 27) renders Greek ekathisan, literally "settled" or "caused to dwell," reflecting the Hebrew idiom for taking wives.
- **13:25** — "You must not give... and you must not take" renders the literal Greek oath formula "If you give... and if you take," which expresses an emphatic prohibition.
- **13:29** — "Kinship" literally renders Greek anchisteia, reflecting a reading of the Hebrew root for "defilement" as "kinship" or "redemption." In verse 31, "bacchuria" is an untranslated Greek transliteration of the Hebrew word for "firstfruits" (bikkurim).
