## Text

**6:1** And it happened, when it was heard by Sanballat and Tobiah and Geshem the Arab and the rest of our enemies that the wall had been built and no breath was left in them—until that time I had not set up doors in the gates—

**6:2** that Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, and let us gather together in the villages in the plain of Ono." And they were planning to do evil to me.

**6:3** And I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work, and I will not be able to come down, so that the work may not stop; as soon as I finish it, I will come down to you."

**6:4** And they sent to me according to this word, and I sent to them according to these things.

**6:5** And Sanballat sent to me his servant, and an open letter in his hand.

**6:6** And it was written in it, "Among the nations it has been heard that you and the Jews are planning to rebel; for this reason you are building the wall, and you will be king for them.

**6:7** And in addition to these things, you have appointed prophets for yourself, so that you may sit in Jerusalem as king over Judah; and now these words will be reported to the king. And now come, let us take counsel together."

**6:8** And I sent to him, saying, "It has not happened according to these words that you are saying; because from your heart you are falsely inventing them."

**6:9** For all of them are frightening us, saying, "Their hands will grow weak from this work, and it will not be done." And now I strengthened my hands.

**6:10** And I entered the house of Shimei son of Delaiah, son of Meitael, and he was shut in; and he said, "Let us gather into the house of God, in the midst of it, and let us shut its doors, because they are coming by night to kill you."

**6:11** And I said, "Who is the man who will enter into the house and live?"

**6:12** And I recognized, and look, God had not sent him, because the prophecy was a word against me; and Tobiah and Sanballat had hired

**6:13** a crowd against me, so that I might be afraid and do so and sin, and become an evil name for them, so that they might reproach me.

**6:14** Remember, God, Tobiah and Sanballat according to these deeds of his, and Noadiah the prophet and the rest of the priests who were frightening me.

**6:15** And the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Edud, in fifty-two days.

**6:16** And it happened, when our enemies heard, that all the nations around us were afraid, and fear fell greatly in their eyes, and they knew that it came about from our God that this work was finished.

**6:17** And in those days letters were going from many nobles of Judah to Tobiah, and those of Tobiah were coming to them;

**6:18** for many in Judah were bound by oath to him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and Johanan his son had taken the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah as a wife.

**6:19** And they were speaking his words to me, and they were carrying my words out to him; and Tobiah sent letters to frighten me.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Greek reads "and no breath was left in them" (pnoe), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("no breach was left in it").
- **6:9** — Greek reads a first-person past indicative "I strengthened my hands" (ekrataiosa) rather than the imperative prayer "strengthen my hands" found in the Hebrew text.
- **6:10** — Greek Semeei ("Shimei") and "Meitael" are distinctive Septuagint forms corresponding to Hebrew "Shemaiah" and "Mehetabel."
- **6:13** — Continuing from 6:12, Greek reads "hired a crowd against me" (ochlon) instead of "hired him; for this reason he was hired" as in the Hebrew text.
- **6:14** — Greek spells Sanballat as "Anaballat," has singular "his deeds," makes Noadiah masculine ("the prophet"), and refers to "the rest of the priests" instead of "the rest of the prophets."
- **6:15** — Greek "Edud" is a distinctive Septuagint form of the month name corresponding to Hebrew "Elul."
