## Text

**9:1** And on the twenty-fourth day of this month the sons of Israel gathered together with fasting and in sackcloth.

**9:2** And the sons of Israel separated themselves from every son of a foreigner, and they stood and confessed their sins and the lawless deeds of their fathers.

**9:3** And they stood in their place and read from the book of the law of their God, and they were confessing to the Lord and worshiping their God.

**9:4** And on the steps of the Levites stood Jeshua and the sons of Kadmiel, Sherebiah son of Arabiah, and they cried out with a loud voice to the Lord their God.

**9:5** And the Levites Jeshua and Kadmiel said, "Stand up, bless the Lord our God from eternity to eternity; and they will bless the name of Your glory, and they will exalt it above all blessing and praise."

**9:6** And Ezra said, "You Yourself are the Lord alone; You made the heaven of heaven and all their array, the earth and all that is in it, the seas and all that is in them, and You give life to all things; and the hosts of the heavens worship You.

**9:7** You are God; You chose Abram and brought him out of the country of the Chaldeans, and gave him the name Abraham.

**9:8** And You found his heart faithful before You, and You made a covenant with him to give to him the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Girgashites, and to his seed; and You confirmed Your words, for You are righteous.

**9:9** And You saw the humiliation of our fathers in Egypt, and You heard their cry at the Red Sea.

**9:10** And You gave signs and wonders in Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his servants and against all the people of his land, for You knew that they acted arrogantly against them; and You made a name for Yourself, as it is this day.

**9:11** And You split the sea before him, and they passed through the middle of the sea on dry ground; and those who pursued them You threw into the depth like a stone in violent water.

**9:12** And with a pillar of cloud You led them by day, and with a pillar of fire by night, to light for them the way in which they were to go.

**9:13** And You came down on Mount Sinai and spoke to them from heaven, and You gave them right judgments and laws of truth, statutes and good commandments.

**9:14** And You made known to them Your holy Sabbath; You commanded them commandments, statutes, and law by the hand of Moses Your servant.

**9:15** And You gave them bread from heaven for their food supply, and You brought out water from a rock for them for their thirst. And You told them to enter and inherit the land over which You stretched out Your hand to give it to them.

**9:16** And they stiffened their neck, and they did not listen to Your commandments.

**9:17** And they refused to listen, and they did not remember Your wonders which You did with them; and they stiffened their neck and appointed a ruler to return to their slavery in Egypt. And You are a merciful and compassionate God, slow to anger and abounding in mercy, and You did not abandon them.

**9:18** Moreover, they even made for themselves a molten calf and said, 'These are the gods who brought us out of Egypt,' and committed great provocations.

**9:19** And You, in Your many compassions, did not abandon them in the wilderness; You did not turn away the pillar of cloud from them by day to lead them in the way, nor the pillar of fire by night to light for them the way in which they were to go.

**9:20** And You gave Your good Spirit to give them understanding, and You did not withhold Your manna from their mouth, and You gave them water for their thirst.

**9:21** And for forty years You sustained them in the wilderness; You did not fall short; their garments did not wear out, and their feet did not split open.

**9:22** And You gave them kings and peoples, and You distributed to them; and they inherited the land of Sihon and that of Og king of Bashan.

**9:23** And You multiplied their sons like the stars of heaven, and You brought them into the land which You spoke of to their fathers, and they inherited it.

**9:24** And You wiped out before them those who lived in the land of the Canaanites, and You gave them into their hands, both their kings and the peoples of the land, to do to them as was pleasing before them.

**9:25** And they captured high cities, and they inherited houses full of all good things, hewn cisterns, vineyards and olive groves, and every tree for food in abundance; and they ate and were filled and grew fat, and they delighted themselves in Your great goodness.

**9:26** And they shouted aloud and turned away from You, and they threw Your law behind their body, and they killed Your prophets who testified against them to turn them back to You, and they committed great provocations.

**9:27** And You gave them into the hand of those who afflicted them, and they afflicted them; and they cried out to You in the time of their affliction, and You heard from Your heaven, and in Your great compassions You gave them saviors, and You saved them from the hand of the one who afflicted them.

**9:28** And when they had rest, they returned to do evil before You; and You abandoned them into the hands of their enemies, and they ruled over them. And again they cried out to You, and You heard from heaven and rescued them in Your many compassions,

**9:29** and You testified against them to turn them back to Your law; and they did not listen, but sinned against Your commandments and against Your judgments, which, if a man does them, he will live by them; and they turned a disobedient back and stiffened their neck, and did not listen.

**9:30** And You bore with them for many years, and You testified against them by Your Spirit through the hand of Your prophets; and they did not give ear, and You gave them into the hand of the peoples of the land.

**9:31** And You, in Your many compassions, did not make a complete end of them and did not abandon them, for You are mighty and merciful and compassionate.

**9:32** And now, our God, the mighty, the great, the powerful, and the awesome, who keeps Your covenant and Your mercy, do not let all the hardship seem small before You which has found us, and our kings, our leaders, our priests, our prophets, and our fathers, and among all Your people, from the days of the kings of Assyria even until this day.

**9:33** And You are righteous in all that comes upon us, for You have acted in truth, and we have sinned,

**9:34** and our kings, our leaders, our priests, and our fathers did not do Your law, and did not pay attention to Your commandments and Your testimonies which You testified to them.

**9:35** And they, in Your kingdom and in the abundant goodness which You gave them and in the broad and rich land which You set before them, did not serve You, and they did not turn away from their evil practices.

**9:36** Look, we are slaves today, and the land which You gave to our fathers to eat its fruit,

**9:37** belongs to the kings whom You set over us because of our sins; and they exercise authority over our bodies and over our livestock as is pleasing to them, and we are in great affliction."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Greek reads "the sons of Kadmiel, Sherebiah son of Arabiah" (retaining the Greek-distinct name Arabiah), whereas the Hebrew MT lists a longer series of Levite names.
- **9:6** — The Greek explicitly introduces Ezra as the speaker of the prayer ("And Ezra said"), whereas in the Hebrew MT the Levites of 9:5 continue speaking.
- **9:21** — The Greek verb is second-person singular ("You did not fall short"), referring to God's unfailing provision, whereas the Hebrew MT and some Greek witnesses read third-person plural ("they lacked nothing").
- **9:26** — The Greek reads "they shouted aloud" (elalaxan, likely a transmission shift from ellaxan, "they changed"), whereas the Hebrew MT reads "they were disobedient."
- **9:36** — The Greek text omits the clauses "and its good things; look, we are slaves in it. And its abundant produce" found in the Hebrew MT and some Greek witnesses, so "the land" in 9:36 connects directly to "belongs to the kings" in 9:37.
