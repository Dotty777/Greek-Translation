## Text

**3:1** And Eliashib the high priest and his brothers the priests rose up and built the Sheep Gate; they consecrated it and set up its doors, and as far as the Tower of the Hundred they consecrated it, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**3:2** And at the side of the men, the sons of Jericho, and at the side of the sons of Zabaur son of Imri.

**3:3** And the sons of Hasan built the Fish Gate; they roofed it, and roofed its doors and its bolts and its bars.

**3:4** And at their side it held to Ramoth son of Uriah son of Hakkoz. And at their side Zadok son of Baanah held fast.

**3:5** And at their side the Tekoites held fast, and Adoreem did not bring their neck into their service.

**3:6** And Joeiaa son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Badiah repaired the Gate of Jeshanah; they roofed it, and set up its doors and its bolts and its bars.

**3:8** And at their side Hananiah son of Joiakim repaired, and they left Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**3:9** And at their side Rephaiah, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired.

**3:10** And at their side Jedaiah son of Heromath repaired, even opposite his house. And at his side Hatuth son of Hasbanam repaired.

**3:11** And second, Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired, and as far as the Tower of the Nathurim.

**3:12** And at his side Shallum son of Heliah, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired, he and his daughters.

**3:13** Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate; they built it, and set up its doors and its bolts and its bars, and a thousand cubits on the wall as far as the Dung Gate.

**3:14** And Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-acham, repaired the Dung Gate, he and his sons; and they covered it, and set up its doors and its bolts and its bars,

**3:15** and the wall of the Pool of the Fleeces for the king's shearing, and as far as the stairs going down from the city of David.

**3:16** After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of the district of Besor, repaired as far as the garden of the tomb of David, and as far as the pool that had been made, and as far as Beth-abarim.

**3:17** After him the Levites repaired: Basuth son of Bani. At his side Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, repaired for his district.

**3:18** After him their brothers repaired: Bedei son of Henadalat, ruler of half the district of Keilah.

**3:19** And at his side Azzur son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired a second section of the tower of the ascent joining the corner.

**3:20** After him Baruch son of Zabu repaired a second section from the corner as far as the door of Beth-eliashib the high priest.

**3:21** After him Meremoth son of Uriah son of Hakob repaired a second section from the door of Beth-eliashib as far as the end of Beth-eliashib.

**3:22** And after him the priests, the men of Hachechar, repaired.

**3:23** And after him Benjamin and Hasshub repaired opposite their house. After him Azariah son of Madasel, son of Ananiah, repaired beside his house.

**3:24** After him Bani son of Henadad repaired a second section from Beth-azariah as far as the corner and as far as the bend.

**3:25** Palal son of Evei opposite the corner, and the projecting tower from the house of the king, the upper one, which belongs to the court of the guard. And after him, Pedaiah son of Parosh.

**3:26** And the Katheinim were living in Ophel as far as the garden of the Water Gate toward the east, and the projecting tower.

**3:27** After him the Tekoites repaired a second section opposite the great projecting tower and as far as the wall of Ophel.

**3:28** Above the Horse Gate the priests repaired, each man opposite his house.

**3:29** After him, Zadok son of Immer opposite his house. And after him Shemaiah son of Echeniah, the keeper of the house of the east, repaired.

**3:30** After him Hananiah son of Telemiah and Hanum, the sixth son of Sele, repaired a second section. After him Meshullam son of Berechiah repaired opposite his treasury.

**3:31** After him Malchijah son of Saraphi repaired as far as Beth-anathim, and the peddlers opposite the Gate of Miphkad and as far as the middle of the bend.

**3:32** And in the middle of the ascent of the Sheep Gate, the smiths and the peddlers repaired.

**3:33** And it happened, when Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, that it seemed displeasing to him, and he became very angry, and he was mocking the Jews.

**3:34** And he said before his brothers, "Is this the army of Samaria, that these Jews are building their own city?"

**3:35** And Tobiah the Ammonite came beside him; and they said to one another, "Surely they will not sacrifice or eat in their place? Will not a fox climb up and tear down their wall of stones?"

**3:36** Hear, our God, for we have become an object of scorn, and turn back their reproach upon their head, and give them over to scorn in a land of captivity,

**3:37** and do not cover over lawlessness.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Greek epi cheiras literally reads "upon/at the hands of," an idiom meaning "at the side of / next to" (as in vv. 4-19). The Greek text also lacks the verb "built" (reading "sons of" instead) and has "Zabaur" for Hebrew "Zaccur"; verse 7 of the Hebrew text is absent from this Septuagint text.
- **3:4** — Greek katecho ('hold fast', vv. 4-5) and krateo ('take hold of/strengthen', translated 'repaired' in vv. 6-32) render the Hebrew verb for repairing the wall. Verse 4 reads 'to Ramoth' (omitting Meshullam's clause), and v. 5 transliterates Hebrew 'their nobles' as Adoreem.
- **3:15** — This Septuagint text omits the first half of the verse (Shallun and the Fountain Gate) and reads 'Pool of the Fleeces for the king's shearing' where the Hebrew has 'Pool of Shelah of the king's garden'.
- **3:26** — Greek Katheinim corresponds to the Hebrew Nethinim (temple servants); here and in v. 16 the Greek reads 'garden' where the Hebrew has 'opposite', and elsewhere transliterates several Hebrew titles and terms (such as Nathurim in v. 11, Hachechar in v. 22, and Beth-eliashib in vv. 20-21).
- **3:34** — In vv. 33-37 (4:1-5 in English Bibles), the Greek text is markedly shorter than the Hebrew, spelling Sanballat as Anaballat in v. 33, placing 'Is this the army of Samaria' inside his speech in v. 34, reading 'sacrifice or eat' in v. 35, and ending abruptly in v. 37.
