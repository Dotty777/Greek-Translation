## Text

**11:1** And the leaders of the people lived in Jerusalem; and the rest of the people drew lots to bring one out of ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, and nine parts in the cities.

**11:2** And the people blessed all the men who volunteered to live in Jerusalem.

**11:3** And these are the leaders of the province who lived in Jerusalem; and in the cities of Judah each man lived in his possession in their cities: Israel, the priests and the Levites and the sons of the servants of Solomon.

**11:4** And in Jerusalem some of the sons of Judah and some of the sons of Benjamin lived. Of the sons of Judah: Athaiah son of Azed, son of Zechariah, son of Samariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Malelem, and of the sons of Perez.

**11:5** And Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Chalea, son of Hazaiah, son of Dalea, son of Joiarib, son of Thezeiah, son of Delone.

**11:6** All the sons of Seres who lived in Jerusalem were four hundred sixty-eight men of strength.

**11:7** And these are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Amesula; Joed son of Pelaiah, son of Kodiah, son of Magael, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah;

**11:8** and after him, Gabbai, Sallai: nine hundred twenty-eight.

**11:9** And Joel son of Zichri was overseer over them, and Judah son of Hassenuah was second over the city.

**11:10** Of the priests: also Dadeiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,

**11:11** Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Apoboch, opposite the house of God.

**11:12** And their brothers who did the work of the house: Amzi son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah,

**11:13** leaders of ancestral families, two hundred forty-two. And Amasiah son of Azriel,

**11:14** and his brothers, mighty men of battle, one hundred twenty-eight, and the overseer was Badiel.

**11:15** And of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Ezri;

**11:17** and Mattaniah son of Mica, and Obeb son of Shammua,

**11:18** two hundred eighty-four.

**11:19** And the gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers, one hundred seventy-two.

**11:22** And the overseer of the Levites was the son of Bani, Uzzi son of Hashabiah, son of Mica, of the sons of Asaph, the singers, opposite the work of the house of God.

**11:23** For there was a command of the king concerning them.

**11:24** And Pethahiah son of Baseza was at the hand of the king for every matter concerning the people.

**11:25** And as for the villages in their field, some of the sons of Judah also lived in Kiriath-ar,

**11:26** and in Jeshua,

**11:27** and in Beersheba,

**11:30** and their villages, Lachish and its fields; and they encamped in Beersheba.

**11:31** And the sons of Benjamin were from Gala, Michmash.

**11:36** And of the Levites, divisions of Judah were for Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **11:4** — The Greek Judahite genealogy preserves several distinct names: "Azed," "Samariah," and "Malelem" (11:4, MT "Uzziah, Amariah, Mahalalel"), "Chalea," "Dalea," "Thezeiah," and "Delone" (11:5, MT "Col-hozeh, Adaiah, Zechariah, the Shilonite"), and "Seres" (11:6, MT "Perez," contrasting with "Perez" in 11:4).
- **11:7** — The Greek Benjaminite list reads "Amesula," "Pelaiah," "Kodiah," and "Magael" (MT "Meshullam, Pedaiah, Kolaiah, Maaseiah") and omits "son of" before Joed.
- **11:11** — The Greek reads "Apoboch" (MT "Ahitub") and "opposite the house of God" (taking Hebrew nagid, "ruler," as neged, "opposite"; cf. 11:22), and in 11:10 reads "also Dadeiah" (MT "Jedaiah").
- **11:14** — The Greek text of 11:12-19 is substantially shorter than the MT (omitting v. 16) and preserves distinct names: "Amasiah" and "Azriel" (11:13, MT "Amashsai, Azarel"), "Badiel" (11:14, MT "Zabdiel"), "Ezri" (11:15, MT "Azrikam"), and "Obeb" (11:17, MT "Abda").
- **11:25** — The Greek text of 11:20-36 is heavily abbreviated (omitting vv. 20-21, 28-29, and 32-35), places "the son of Bani" before "Uzzi" in 11:22, and reads "Baseza" (11:24, MT "Meshezabel"), "Kiriath-ar" (11:25, MT "Kiriath-arba"), and "Gala" (11:31, MT "Geba").
