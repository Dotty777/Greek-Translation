## Text

**3:1** And she stayed with her mother-in-law. And Naomi her mother-in-law said to her, "Daughter, should I not seek rest for you, so that it may go well with you?

**3:2** And now, is not Boaz our acquaintance, with whose young women you were? Look, he is winnowing the threshing floor of barley tonight.

**3:3** Now wash yourself and anoint yourself and put your clothing on yourself, and go up to the threshing floor. Do not make yourself known to the man until he finishes drinking and eating.

**3:4** And it will happen, when he lies down, that you will note the place where he lies; and you will go and uncover what is at his feet and lie down, and he himself will tell you what you are to do."

**3:5** And Ruth said to her, "All that you say I will do."

**3:6** And she went down to the threshing floor and did according to all that her mother-in-law had commanded her.

**3:7** And Boaz ate, and his heart was gladdened, and he came to lie down at the side of the heap; and she came secretly and uncovered what was at his feet.

**3:8** And it happened at midnight that the man was startled and troubled, and look, a woman was lying at his feet.

**3:9** And he said, "Who are you?" And she said, "I am Ruth, your servant; spread the edge of your garment over your servant, because you are a kinsman-redeemer."

**3:10** And Boaz said, "Blessed are you by the Lord God, daughter, because you have made your latter mercy better than the first, in not going after young men, whether poor or rich.

**3:11** And now, daughter, do not be afraid; all that you say I will do for you, for every tribe of my people knows that you are a woman of strength.

**3:12** And truly, I am a kinsman-redeemer; yet there is also a kinsman-redeemer closer than I.

**3:13** Lodge for the night, and it will happen in the morning that if he acts as kinsman-redeemer for you, good, let him act as kinsman-redeemer; but if he does not wish to act as kinsman-redeemer for you, then I myself will act as kinsman-redeemer for you, as the Lord lives; You are the Lord. Lie down until morning."

**3:14** And she lay at his feet until morning; and she rose up before a man could recognize his neighbor. And Boaz said, "Let it not be known that a woman came to the threshing floor."

**3:15** And he said to her, "Bring the wrap that is on you." And she held it, and he measured out six measures of barley and laid it on her; and he went into the city.

**3:16** And Ruth went in to her mother-in-law, and she said to her, "Daughter." And she told her all that the man had done for her.

**3:17** And she said to her, "These six measures of barley he gave to me, because he said to me, 'Do not go in empty to your mother-in-law.'"

**3:18** And she said, "Sit still, daughter, until you know how the matter will not fall; for the man will certainly not rest until he finishes the matter today."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The opening clause "And she stayed with her mother-in-law" stands at the beginning of 3:1 in the Greek versification rather than at the end of 2:23.
- **3:11** — Greek reads "every tribe of my people" (pasa phyle) rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "all the gate [city] of my people."
- **3:13** — Following "as the Lord lives," this Greek text uniquely reads "You are the Lord" (su ei Kyrios), a distinctive Septuagint reading absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **3:16** — In the Greek text, Naomi greets Ruth simply with "Daughter," omitting the Hebrew question "Who are you?"
- **3:18** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("how the matter turns out"), the Greek adds a negative, reading "how the matter will not fall" (that is, fail).
