## Text

**2:1** Now Naomi had a man who was an acquaintance of her husband, and the man was mighty in strength, from the clan of Abimelech, and his name was Boaz.

**2:2** And Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Let me go now into a field, and I will gather among the heads of grain behind the one in whose eyes I find favor." And she said to her, "Go, daughter."

**2:3** And she went and gleaned in the field behind the reapers; and she happened by chance upon the portion of the field of Boaz, who was from the clan of Abimelech.

**2:4** And look, Boaz came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, "The Lord be with you." And they said to him, "May the Lord bless you."

**2:5** And Boaz said to his young man who was set over the reapers, "Whose young woman is this?"

**2:6** And the young man who was set over the reapers answered and said, "She is the Moabite girl who returned with Naomi from the country of Moab;

**2:7** and she said, 'Let me glean now and gather among the sheaves behind the reapers.' And she came and stood from morning until evening; she did not rest in the field even a little."

**2:8** And Boaz said to Ruth, "Have you not heard, daughter? Do not go to glean in another field, and you must not go from here; stay close here with my young women.

**2:9** Let your eyes be on the field where they are reaping, and go behind them. Look, I have commanded the young men not to touch you; and whenever you become thirsty, go to the vessels and drink from where the young men draw water."

**2:10** And she fell on her face and bowed down to the ground, and said to him, "Why is it that I have found favor in your eyes, that you take notice of me, when I am a foreigner?"

**2:11** And Boaz answered and said to her, "It has been fully reported to me all that you have done for your mother-in-law after your husband died, and how you left your father and your mother and the land of your birth, and went to a people whom you did not know yesterday and the day before.

**2:12** May the Lord repay your work, and may your reward be full from the Lord, the God of Israel, to Whom you have come to trust under His wings."

**2:13** And she said, "May I find favor in your eyes, lord, because you have comforted me, and because you have spoken to the heart of your servant; and look, I will be like one of your female servants."

**2:14** And Boaz said to her, "It is already time to eat; come here and eat of the bread, and dip your morsel in the vinegar." And Ruth sat beside the reapers; and Boaz heaped up barley meal for her, and she ate and was satisfied and left some over.

**2:15** And she rose up to glean. And Boaz commanded his young men, saying, "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not put her to shame;

**2:16** and be sure to carry some for her, and even be sure to set some aside for her from the heaps; and she will eat and glean, and do not rebuke her."

**2:17** And she gleaned in the field until evening, and she beat out what she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.

**2:18** And she took it up and went into the city; and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned, and Ruth brought out and gave to her what she had left over from what had satisfied her.

**2:19** And her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today, and where did you work? May the one who took notice of you be blessed." And Ruth told her mother-in-law where she had worked, and said, "The name of the man with whom I worked today is Boaz."

**2:20** And Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "Blessed is he by the Lord, because He has not forsaken His mercy toward the living and toward the dead." And Naomi said to her, "The man is close to us; he is one of our kinsman-redeemers."

**2:21** And Ruth said to her mother-in-law, "Indeed, he also said to me, 'Stay close with my young men until they finish the entire harvest that belongs to me.'"

**2:22** And Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, "It is good, daughter, that you went with his young women, and they will not accost you in another field."

**2:23** And Ruth stayed close to the young women of Boaz to glean until she finished the harvest of barley and of wheat.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("from morning until now; she sat in the house a little"), the Greek reads "from morning until evening; she did not rest in the field even a little."
- **2:13** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("though I am not like one of your maidservants"), the Greek omits the negation and reads positively, "and look, I will be like one of your female servants."
- **2:16** — The Greek preserves an expanded double rendering ("be sure to carry some... and even be sure to set some aside") and reads "and she will eat," where the Hebrew has "and leave them."
- **2:20** — The Greek pronoun in "He has not forsaken His mercy" may refer either to the Lord or to Boaz. Greek anchisteuonton (and anchisteus in ch. 3) denotes the legal next of kin bearing the right and duty of redemption.
