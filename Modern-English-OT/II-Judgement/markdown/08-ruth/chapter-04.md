## Text

**4:1** Now Boaz went up to the gate and sat down there, and look, the next of kin of whom Boaz had spoken was passing by. And Boaz said to him, "Turn aside and sit down here, hidden one." And he turned aside and sat down.

**4:2** And Boaz took ten men from the elders of the city and said, "Sit down here." And they sat down.

**4:3** And Boaz said to the next of kin, "The portion of the field that belongs to our brother Abimelech, which has been given to Naomi, who is returning from the country of Moab—

**4:4** and I said, 'I will uncover your ear, saying, "Acquire it before those who are sitting and before the elders of my people."' If you are going to act as next of kin, act as next of kin; but if you are not going to act as next of kin, tell me, and I will know, for there is no one besides you to act as next of kin, and I am after you." And he said, "I am; I will act as next of kin."

**4:5** And Boaz said, "On the day you acquire the field from the hand of Naomi and from Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the deceased, you must also acquire her, so as to raise up the name of the deceased upon his inheritance."

**4:6** And the next of kin said, "I will not be able to act as next of kin for myself, or else I might ruin my inheritance. Exercise for yourself my next-of-kin right, because I will not be able to act as next of kin."

**4:7** Now this was the ordinance formerly in Israel concerning the next-of-kin right and concerning exchange, to establish every matter: a man would untie his sandal and give it to his neighbor who was exercising his next-of-kin right; and this was a testimony in Israel.

**4:8** And the next of kin said to Boaz, "Acquire for yourself my next-of-kin right." And he untied his sandal and gave it to him.

**4:9** And Boaz said to the elders and all the people, "You are witnesses today that I have acquired all that belongs to Abimelech and everything that belongs to Chilion and Mahlon from the hand of Naomi.

**4:10** Moreover, I have acquired for myself Ruth the Moabite, the wife of Mahlon, as a wife, to raise up the name of the deceased upon his inheritance, and the name of the deceased will not be wiped out from his brothers and from the tribe of his people. You are witnesses today."

**4:11** And all the people who were at the gate said, "Witnesses." And the elders said, "May the Lord make your wife who is coming into your house like Rachel and like Leah, both of whom built the house of Israel and achieved strength in Ephrathah; and there will be a name in Bethlehem.

**4:12** And may your house be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, from the offspring that the Lord will give you from this young woman."

**4:13** And Boaz took Ruth, and the Lord gave her conception, and she bore a son.

**4:14** And the women said to Naomi, "Blessed be the Lord, who has not removed the next of kin from you today, and may he call your name in Israel;

**4:15** and he will be to you a restorer of life and one to sustain your old age, because your daughter-in-law who loved you bore him, she who is better for you than seven sons."

**4:16** And Naomi took the child and placed him in her bosom, and she became a nurse to him.

**4:17** And the neighbor women gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi." And they called his name Obed. He is the father of Jesse, the father of David.

**4:18** Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,

**4:19** and Hezron fathered Arran, and Arran fathered Amminadab,

**4:20** and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon,

**4:21** and Salmon fathered Boaz, and Boaz fathered Obed,

**4:22** and Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Greek anchisteutes (along with anchisteus, anchisteia, and anchisteuo throughout the chapter) refers to the legal next of kin with family succession and redemption rights (rendering Hebrew go'el). Greek kryphie literally means "hidden one," translating the Hebrew idiom for an unnamed person.
- **4:3** — The Greek text uses the distinct proper name "Abimelech" (Abeimelech, also in 4:9) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Elimelech." Greek he dedotai Noemein ("which has been given to Naomi") leaves "the portion of the field" in an abrupt hanging accusative construction, unlike the Hebrew "Naomi is selling."
- **4:11** — The Greek splits the reply between the people at the gate and the elders, uses a third-person plural past indicative ("achieved strength") keeping Rachel and Leah as the subjects, and concludes with a future indicative ("and there will be a name in Bethlehem") rather than imperatives addressed to Boaz.
- **4:14** — The Greek reads active optative kalesai with second-person to onoma sou ("and may he call your name," addressed to Naomi), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads passively "may his name be proclaimed."
- **4:19** — The Greek text preserves the distinct proper name "Arran" (Arran), corresponding to "Ram" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and "Aram" in some Greek witnesses and Matthew 1:3).
