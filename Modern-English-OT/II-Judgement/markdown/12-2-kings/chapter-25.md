## Text

**25:1** And it happened in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came against Jerusalem, and encamped against it, and built a siege wall against it all around.

**25:2** And the city came under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah, on the ninth day of the month.

**25:3** And the famine grew severe in the city, and there were no loaves of bread for the people of the land.

**25:4** And the city was breached, and all the men of war went out by night by the way of the gate between the walls, the one that belongs to the king's garden, while the Chaldeans were against the city all around. And he went by the way of the Arabah.

**25:5** And the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king and overtook him in Araboth of Jericho, and all his army was scattered away from him.

**25:6** And they seized the king and brought him to the king of Babylon at Ierdeblatha, and he spoke judgment with him.

**25:7** And he slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and blinded the eyes of Zedekiah, and bound him in fetters, and brought him to Babylon.

**25:8** And in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan the chief cook, standing before the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.

**25:9** And he burned the house of the Lord, and the house of the king, and all the houses of Jerusalem, and the chief cook burned every house.

**25:11** And Nebuzaradan the chief cook carried away the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the defectors who had defected to the king of Babylon, and the remainder of the support.

**25:12** And the chief cook left behind some of the poor of the land as vinedressers and as tabein.

**25:13** And the Chaldeans broke in pieces the bronze pillars that were in the house of the Lord, and the mechonoth, and the bronze sea that was in the house of the Lord, and carried their bronze to Babylon.

**25:14** And he took the pots, and the iamein, and the bowls, and the censers, and all the bronze vessels with which they minister.

**25:15** And the chief cook took the firepans and the bowls, the gold ones and the silver ones.

**25:16** Two pillars, the one sea, and the mechonoth that Solomon made for the house of the Lord: there was no weighing of the bronze of all the vessels.

**25:17** Eighteen cubits was the height of the one pillar, and the bronze chothar was upon it, and the height of the chothar was three cubits; sakacharthai were upon the chothar all around, all of bronze; and in the same way for the second pillar upon the gabacha.

**25:18** And the chief cook took Seraiah the first priest, and Zephaniah, son of the second rank, and the three who guarded the threshold.

**25:19** And from the city he took one eunuch who was overseer of the fighting men, and five men of those who saw the face of the king who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the army who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.

**25:20** And Nebuzaradan the chief cook took them and led them away to the king of Babylon at Deblatha.

**25:21** And the king of Babylon struck them and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. And Judah was carried into exile away from its land.

**25:22** And as for the people who were left in the land of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon left behind, he appointed over them Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan.

**25:23** And all the commanders of the army, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah; and they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah, and Jonah son of Kareth, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Nepphathite, and Ozonias son of the Achathite, they and their men.

**25:24** And Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, and said to them, "Do not fear the passage of the Chaldeans; settle in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you."

**25:25** And it happened in the seventh month that Manael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, of the seed of the kings, came, and men with him; and he struck Gedaliah so that he died, as well as the Judeans and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah.

**25:26** And all the people arose, from small even to great, and the commanders of the armies, and entered into Egypt, because they were afraid of the presence of the Chaldeans.

**25:27** And it happened in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiakim king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, that Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the year of his reign, lifted up the head of Jehoiakim king of Judah and brought him out of the house of his imprisonment.

**25:28** And he spoke good things with him, and set his throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.

**25:29** And he changed the garments of his imprisonment, and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.

**25:30** And his food allowance was a continual food allowance; it was given to him from the house of the king, a day's portion in its day, all the days of his life.

## Translator Notes

- **25:6** — The Greek renders the place name as Ierdeblatha here (and Deblatha in v. 20), contrasting with Rheblatha (rendered Riblah) in v. 21; similarly, Araboth of Jericho in v. 5 transliterates the Hebrew word for plains.
- **25:8** — Greek archimageiros literally means chief cook (rendering the Hebrew title for the captain of the guard), and standing before the king of Babylon replaces the Hebrew servant of the king of Babylon.
- **25:17** — Several Hebrew architectural and occupational terms in vv. 12-17 are transliterated rather than translated in the Greek: tabein (v. 12, plowmen), mechonoth (vv. 13, 16, stands), iamein (v. 14, shovels), chothar (v. 17, capital), and sakacharthai and gabacha (v. 17, latticework). Similarly, v. 11 reads support (for Hebrew multitude), and v. 18 reads son of the second rank (for Hebrew second priest).
- **25:23** — Several names here and in v. 25 differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text: Jonah son of Kareth (for Johanan son of Kareah), the Nepphathite (for the Netophathite), Ozonias son of the Achathite (for Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite), and Manael in v. 25 (for Ishmael, also omitting the number ten). In v. 24, Greek parodon (passage) reflects a variant Hebrew reading rather than servants.
- **25:27** — The Greek reads Ioakim (Jehoiakim) twice here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Jehoiachin; the Greek also adds from the house of the king in v. 30.
