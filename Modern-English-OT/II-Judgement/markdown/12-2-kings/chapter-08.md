## Text

**8:1** Now Elisha spoke to the woman whose son he had restored to life, saying, "Rise and go, you and your household, and sojourn wherever you may sojourn, for the Lord has called a famine on the land, and indeed it has come on the land for seven years."

**8:2** And the woman rose and did according to the word of Elisha, and she went, she and her household, and sojourned in the land of the foreigners for seven years.

**8:3** And it happened after the end of the seven years that the woman returned from the land of the foreigners to the city, and she came to cry out to the king concerning her house and concerning her fields.

**8:4** And the king was speaking to Gehazi, the attendant of Elisha the man of God, saying, "Please recount to me all the great things that Elisha has done."

**8:5** And it happened while he was relating to the king how he had restored a dead son to life, that look, the woman whose son Elisha had restored to life was crying out to the king concerning her house and concerning her fields. And Gehazi said, "My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha restored to life."

**8:6** And the king questioned the woman, and she recounted it to him. And the king gave her one eunuch, saying, "Restore all her produce of the field, from the day she left the land until now."

**8:7** Now Elisha came to Damascus, and Son of Hader, king of Syria, became ill. And they reported to him, saying, "The man of God has come as far as here."

**8:8** And the king said to Hazael, "Take a present in your hand, and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the Lord through him, saying, 'Will I live through this illness of mine?'"

**8:9** And Hazael went to meet him, and he took a present in his hand and all the good things of Damascus, a load of forty camels, gifts. And he came and stood before him and said to Elisha, "Your son, Son of Hader, king of Syria, has sent me to you to inquire, saying, 'Will I live through this illness of mine?'"

**8:10** And Elisha said, "Go, say, 'You will surely live'; and the Lord has shown me that you will surely die."

**8:11** And he stood before his face and set it to the point of shame, and the man of God wept.

**8:12** And Hazael said, "Why is it that my lord is weeping?" And he said, "Because I know all the evil things you will do to the sons of Israel: you will send their strongholds up in fire, and you will kill their chosen men with the sword, and you will smash their infants, and you will rip open their pregnant women."

**8:13** And Hazael said, "Who is your servant, the dead dog, that he should do this thing?" And Elisha said, "The Lord has shown me you reigning over Israel."

**8:14** And he departed from Elisha and went in to his master. And he said to him, "What did Elisha say to you?" And he said, "He said to me, 'You will surely live.'"

**8:15** And it happened on the next day that he took the coverlet and dipped it in the water and wrapped it over his face, and he died. And Hazael became king in his place.

**8:16** In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, and of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, Joram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, became king.

**8:17** He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem.

**8:18** And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done, for a daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did what was evil before the Lord.

**8:19** Yet the Lord was not willing to destroy Judah for the sake of David His servant, just as He had spoken to give him a lamp all the days.

**8:20** In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and they set a king over themselves.

**8:21** And Joram went up to Seior, and all the chariots with him. And it happened when he rose that he struck Edom, who had surrounded him, and the commanders of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.

**8:22** And Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah until this day. Then Senna revolted at that time.

**8:23** And the rest of the deeds of Joram and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah?

**8:24** And Joram slept with his fathers, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father. And Ahaziah his son became king in his place.

**8:25** In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Joram became king.

**8:26** Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Israel. And his mother's name was Athaliah, daughter of Omri, king of Israel.

**8:27** And he walked in the way of the house of Ahab, and he did what was evil before the Lord, just as the house of Ahab did.

**8:28** And he went with Joram son of Ahab to war against Hazael, king of the foreigners, at Ramoth-gilead; and the Syrians struck Joram.

**8:29** And King Joram returned to be healed in Israel from the wounds with which they had struck him at Ramoth when he fought against Hazael, king of Syria. And Ahaziah son of Joram went down to see Joram son of Ahab in Israel, because he was ill.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The Greek text renders the Syrian king's name literally as "Son of Hader" (Hebrew Masoretic Text: "Ben-hadad"). In 8:8-9, "present" translates the Greek loanword maana (from Hebrew minchah, "gift/tribute").
- **8:10** — The Greek text reads second-person singular "you will surely die" (Hebrew Masoretic Text: "he will surely die"). In 8:11, "set it to the point of shame" literally renders the Greek expression for staring fixedly until embarrassed.
- **8:13** — The Greek text reads "the dead dog" (Hebrew: "the dog") and "reigning over Israel" (Hebrew Masoretic Text: "king over Aram/Syria"). In 8:15, "coverlet" renders the transliterated Greek loanword chabba (a woven bedcloth).
- **8:17** — The Greek text states that Joram reigned "forty years" in Jerusalem, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "eight years." In 8:21-22, the Greek place names "Seior" and "Senna" correspond to Hebrew "Zair" and "Libnah."
- **8:28** — "King of the foreigners" follows the Greek text (the same term used for the Philistines in 8:2-3), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "king of Aram/Syria." In 8:26 and 8:29, the Greek text reads "Israel" where the Hebrew reads "Jerusalem" (8:26) and "Jezreel" (8:29).
