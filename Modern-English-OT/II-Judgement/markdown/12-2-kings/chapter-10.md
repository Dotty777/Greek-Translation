## Text

**10:1** Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. And Jehu wrote a letter and sent it to Samaria, to the rulers of Samaria, and to the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab, saying,

**10:2** "And now, as soon as this letter comes to you, since with you are the sons of your master, and with you are the chariot and horses and fortified cities and the weapons,

**10:3** look for the good and the upright one among the sons of your master, and set him on the throne of his father, and fight for the house of your master."

**10:4** And they feared greatly and said, "Look, the two kings did not stand before him, and how will we stand?"

**10:5** And those who were over the house and those who were over the city and the elders and the guardians sent to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and whatever you say to us we will do; we will not make any man king; we will do what is good in your eyes."

**10:6** And he wrote to them a second letter, saying, "If you are for me and you listen to my voice, take the head of the men, the sons of your master, and bring it to me about this time tomorrow to Israel." Now the sons of the king were seventy men; these prominent men of the city were raising them.

**10:7** And it came about, when the letter came to them, that they took the sons of the king and slaughtered them, seventy men; and they placed their heads in baskets and sent them to him to Israel.

**10:8** And the messenger came and reported, saying, "I have brought the heads of the sons of the king." And he said, "Put them in two heaps beside the doorway of the gate of the city until morning."

**10:9** And it came about in the morning that he went out and stood, and he said to all the people, "You are righteous; look, I am the one who conspired against my master and killed him, and who struck down all these?

**10:10** See, appho, that nothing from the word of the Lord will fall to the ground which the Lord spoke concerning the house of Ahab; and the Lord has done all that He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah."

**10:11** And Jehu struck down all those who were left in the house of Ahab in Israel, and all his prominent men and his acquaintances and his priests, so as not to leave them a remnant.

**10:12** And he rose and went to Samaria. He was at Beth-eked of the shepherds on the way,

**10:13** and Jehu found the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah and said, "Who are you?" And they said, "We are the brothers of Ahaziah, and we have come down for the peace of the sons of the king and the sons of the ruling lady."

**10:14** And he said, "Seize them alive." And they slaughtered them at Beth-eked, forty-two men; he did not leave a man of them.

**10:15** And he went from there and took Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him, and he blessed him; and Jehu said to him, "Is your heart upright with my heart, just as my heart is with your heart?" And Jehonadab said, "It is." And Jehu said, "And if it is, give your hand." And he gave his hand, and he brought him up to himself onto the chariot,

**10:16** and he said to him, "Come with me and see my zeal for the Lord." And he seated him in his chariot.

**10:17** And he entered Samaria, and he struck down all those who were left of Ahab in Samaria until he wiped him out, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke to Elijah.

**10:18** And Jehu was zealous for all the people and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.

**10:19** And now, all you prophets of Baal, call all his servants and his priests to me; let no man be missing, for I have a great sacrifice to Baal; everyone who is missing will not live." Now Jehu acted with deceit, in order to destroy the servants of Baal.

**10:20** And Jehu said, "Sanctify a sacred service to Baal." And he proclaimed it.

**10:21** And Jehu sent throughout all Israel, saying, "And now, all his servants and all his priests and all his prophets, let no one stay away, for I am offering a great sacrifice; whoever stays away will not live." And all the servants of Baal came, and all his priests and all his prophets; not a man was left who did not arrive. And they entered the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled mouth to mouth.

**10:22** And he said to the one over the house of mesthaal, "Bring out clothing for all the servants of Baal." And the vestment-keeper brought it out to them.

**10:23** And Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab entered the house of Baal; and he said to the servants of Baal, "Search and see if there is with you any of the servants of the Lord, but only the servants of Baal alone."

**10:24** And he entered to offer the sacrifices and the burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed for himself eighty men outside, and he said, "Any man who escapes from the men whom I am bringing into your hands, his life shall be for his life."

**10:25** And it came about, when he finished offering the burnt offering, that Jehu said to the guards and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let no man come out from among them." And they struck them down with the edge of the sword, and the guards and the officers threw them out, and they went as far as the city of the house of Baal.

**10:26** And they brought out the robe of Baal and burned it.

**10:27** And they tore down the pillars of Baal, and he struck it down into latrines until this day.

**10:28** And Jehu wiped out Baal from Israel.

**10:29** However, from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, Jehu did not turn away from before them, the golden heifers in Bethel and in Dan.

**10:30** And the Lord said to Jehu, "Because you have done well to do what is upright in My eyes, having done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in My heart, sons of the fourth generation will sit for you on the throne of Israel."

**10:31** And Jehu was not careful to walk in the law of the Lord, the God of Israel, with all his heart; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, who caused Israel to sin.

**10:32** In those days the Lord began to cut off in Israel, and Hazael struck them down even in every border of Israel,

**10:33** from the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, all Gilead, of the Gadite and of Reuben and of Manasseh, from Aroer, which is on the bank of the torrent Arnon, both Gilead and Bashan.

**10:34** Now the rest of the matters of Jehu, and all that he did, and all his might, and the conspiracies that he formed, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**10:35** And Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**10:36** And the days that Jehu reigned over Israel were twenty-eight years in Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — Greek reads "Israel" here and in vv. 7 and 11 (and "Samaria" in v. 1) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jezreel." Greek also reads "these prominent men" rather than "with the great men."
- **10:10** — Greek leaves the Hebrew particle epho ("then, now") untranslated as the transliteration appho.
- **10:18** — Greek reads "was zealous for" (ezelosen) where the Hebrew text reads "gathered."
- **10:22** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "wardrobe" (meltahah) as mesthaal, and renders the closing subject as "the vestment-keeper" (ho stolistes).
- **10:26** — Greek reads "the robe of Baal" (ten stolen, likely an inner-Greek shift from "pillar") rather than "the pillars of the house of Baal," and in v. 27 reads singular "struck it down into latrines" (lytronas).
