## Text

**4:1** Now a certain woman from the sons of the prophets was crying out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant, my husband, has died, and you know that he was a servant who feared the Lord; and the creditor has come to take my two sons for himself as slaves."

**4:2** And Elisha said, "What shall I do for you? What do you have in the house?" And she said, "Your servant has nothing except oil with which I will anoint myself."

**4:3** And he said to her, "Go, ask for vessels for yourself from outside, from all the neighbors, empty vessels; do not ask for just a few.

**4:4** And you shall go in and shut the door behind you and behind your sons, and you shall pour out into these vessels; and what is filled you shall take away."

**4:5** And she went away from him and shut the door behind herself and behind her sons; they were bringing them near to her, and she was pouring until the vessels were filled.

**4:6** And she said to her sons, "Bring yet another vessel near to me." And they said to her, "There is no longer a vessel." And the oil stopped.

**4:7** And she came and reported to the man of God. And Elisha said, "Go and sell the oil and you shall pay your interest, and you and your sons shall live on the remaining oil."

**4:8** Now a day came when Elisha crossed over to Shunem, and a great woman was there, and she pressed him to eat bread. And it happened that as often as he went in, he turned aside to eat there.

**4:9** And the woman said to her husband, "Look now, I know that this is a holy man of God who passes by us continually.

**4:10** Let us now make for him an upper room, a small place, and let us place for him there a bed and a table and a stool and a lampstand; and it will be, when he comes in to us, that he will turn aside there."

**4:11** And a day came when he entered there, and he turned aside into the upper room and lay down there.

**4:12** And he said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite woman for me." And he called her, and she stood before him.

**4:13** And he said to him, "Say now to her, 'Look, you have put out for us all the possession; what must be done for you? Is there a word for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" And she said, "In the midst of the people I am dwelling."

**4:14** And he said, "What must be done for her?" And Gehazi his servant said, "Indeed, she has no son, and her husband is an old man."

**4:15** And he called her, and she stood by the door.

**4:16** And Elisha said to her, "At this time, as the season lives, you will be embracing a son." And she said, "No, my lord, do not deceive your servant."

**4:17** And the woman conceived and bore a son at this time, as the season lives, as Elisha had spoken to her.

**4:18** And the boy grew up. And it happened, when he went out to his father, to the reapers,

**4:19** that he said to his father, "My head, my head!" And he said to the servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**4:20** And he lay upon her knees until noon, and he died.

**4:21** And she carried him up and laid him down upon the bed of the man of God, and she shut him in and went out.

**4:22** And she called her husband and said, "Send now to me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, and I will run to the man of God and return."

**4:23** And he said, "Why is it that you are going to him today? It is neither new moon nor Sabbath." And she said, "Peace."

**4:24** And she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on, go; do not hold back my riding, for if I say to you,

**4:25** 'Go,' then you shall go, and you shall come to the man of God at Mount Carmel." And it happened, when Elisha saw her coming, that he said to Gehazi his servant, "Look now, there is that Shunammite woman.

**4:26** Now run to meet her and say, 'Is it peace with you? Is it peace with your husband? Is it peace with the boy?'" And she said, "Peace."

**4:27** And she came to Elisha at the mountain and took hold of his feet. And Gehazi came near to push her away. And Elisha said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is deeply grieved within her, and the Lord has hidden it from me and you and has not told me."

**4:28** And she said, "Did I ask for a son from my lord? For did I not say, 'You shall not deal deceitfully with me'?"

**4:29** And Elisha said to Gehazi, "Gird your waist and take my staff in your hand and go. For if you find a man, you shall not bless him; and if a man blesses you, you shall not answer him. And you shall place my staff upon the face of the boy."

**4:30** And the mother of the boy said, "As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will certainly not leave you." And Elisha rose up and went after her.

**4:31** And Gehazi passed on ahead of her and placed the staff upon the face of the boy, and there was no voice and there was no hearing. And he returned to meet him and reported to him, saying, "The boy has not awakened."

**4:32** And Elisha entered into the house, and look, the boy was dead, laid upon his bed.

**4:33** And Elisha entered into the house and shut the door behind the two of them, and he prayed to the Lord.

**4:34** And he went up and lay down upon the boy, and he put his mouth upon his mouth and his eyes upon his eyes and his hands upon his hands, and he bent down over him; and the flesh of the boy grew warm.

**4:35** And he turned back and walked in the house this way and that, and he went up and bent down over the boy up to seven times; and the boy opened his eyes.

**4:36** And Elisha called out to Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite woman." And he called, and she came in to him. And Elisha said, "Take your son."

**4:37** And the woman came in and fell at his feet and bowed down to the ground, and she took her son and went out.

**4:38** Now Elisha returned to Gilgal, and the famine was in the land, and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. And Elisha said to his servant, "Set on the pot, and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

**4:39** And he went out into the field to gather arioth, and he found a vine in the field, and he gathered from it wild gourds, his garment full, and threw them into the pot of stew, for they did not know. And he poured out for the men to eat.

**4:40** And it happened, as they were eating from the stew, that look, they cried out and said, "Death is in the pot, man of God!" And they were not able to eat.

**4:41** And he said, "Take flour and throw it into the pot." And Elisha said to Gehazi the servant, "Pour out for the people, and let them eat." And there was no longer any bad thing there in the pot.

**4:42** Now a man passed through from Beth-sarisa and brought to the man of God twenty barley loaves of firstfruits and fig cakes. And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat."

**4:43** And his attendant said, "What? Shall I set this before one hundred men?" And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat, for thus says the Lord, 'They shall eat and leave some over.'"

**4:44** And they ate and left some over, according to the word of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Greek reads "oil with which I will anoint myself" (ho aleipsomai elaion), rendering the Hebrew term for a flask or jar as a verb meaning to anoint.
- **4:13** — The Greek reads "all the possession" (ten pasan enktesin), widely regarded as an early scribal variant for ekstasin ("anxiety" or "trouble," matching the Hebrew).
- **4:35** — The Greek states that Elisha bent down over the boy up to seven times before the boy opened his eyes, lacking the Hebrew statement that the boy sneezed seven times.
- **4:39** — "Arioth" is an untranslated Greek transliteration of a Hebrew plural noun referring to wild herbs or greens.
- **4:42** — The Greek reads the distinct place name Beth-sarisa (Baithsareisa, corresponding to Baal-shalishah in Hebrew) and "fig cakes" (palathas, where the Hebrew has fresh grain in a sack).
