## Text

**9:1** Now Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird your waist, and take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-gilead.

**9:2** And you will go in there, and you will see there Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi; and you will go in and make him rise from the midst of his brothers, and bring him into the room within a room.

**9:3** And you will take the flask of oil and pour it on his head, and say, 'Thus says the Lord: I have anointed you as king over Israel.' And you will open the door, and flee, and not wait."

**9:4** And the prophet, the young man, went to Ramoth-gilead.

**9:5** And he went in, and look, the commanders of the army were sitting there. And he said, "I have a word for you, commander." And Jehu said, "For which one of all of us?" And he said, "For you, commander."

**9:6** And he rose and went into the house, and he poured the oil on his head and said to him, "Thus says the Lord God of Israel: 'I have anointed you as king over the people of the Lord, over Israel.

**9:7** And you will destroy the house of Ahab your master from before your face, and you will avenge the blood of My servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of the Lord from the hand of Jezebel

**9:8** and from the hand of all the house of Ahab; and you will destroy from the house of Ahab every one who urinates against a wall, both confined and abandoned in Israel.

**9:9** And I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.

**9:10** And the dogs will devour Jezebel in the portion of Israel, and there is no one to bury her.'" And he opened the door and fled.

**9:11** And Jehu went out to the servants of his master, and they said to him, "Is it peace? Why is it that this madman came in to you?" And he said to them, "You yourselves know the man and his chatter."

**9:12** And he said, "False! Please tell us." And Jehu said to them, "Thus and thus he spoke to me, saying," and he said, "Thus says the Lord: 'I have anointed you as king over Israel.'"

**9:13** And when they heard, they hurried, and each one took his garment and they placed them under him on the top of the steps, and they blew the horn and said, "Jehu has become king!"

**9:14** And Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Now Joram himself was keeping guard at Ramoth-gilead, he and all Israel, because of Hazael, king of Syria.

**9:15** And King Joram had returned to be healed in Israel from the wounds with which the Syrians had struck him when they fought against Hazael, king of Syria. And Jehu said, "If your soul is with me, let no fugitive go out from the city to go and report in Israel."

**9:16** And Jehu rode and went, and he went down to Israel, because Joram king of Israel was being treated in Israel from the arrow wounds with which the Arameans had shot him at Ramoth in the war against Hazael, king of Syria, for he was mighty and a man of strength. And Ahaziah king of Judah had gone down to see Joram.

**9:17** And the watchman went up onto the tower of Israel, and he saw the dust of Jehu as he was approaching, and he said, "I see dust." And Joram said, "Take a rider and send him before them, and let him say, 'Is it peace?'"

**9:18** And a rider on a horse went to meet them and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" And Jehu said, "What do you have to do with peace? Turn behind me." And the watchman reported, saying, "The messenger came up to them and has not returned."

**9:19** And he sent a second rider on a horse, and he came to him and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" And Jehu said, "What do you have to do with peace? Turn behind me."

**9:20** And the watchman reported, saying, "He came up to them and has not returned; and the driver was driving Jehu son of Nimshi, for he was in madness."

**9:21** And Joram said, "Hitch up!" And he hitched up a chariot. And Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each man in his chariot, and they went out to meet Jehu, and they found him in the portion of Naboth the Israelite.

**9:22** And it happened when Joram saw Jehu that he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" And Jehu said, "What peace? There are still the prostitutions of Jezebel your mother and her many sorceries."

**9:23** And Joram turned his hands and fled, and he said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**9:24** And Jehu filled his hand with the bow and struck Joram between his arms, and his arrow went out through his heart, and he sank down on his knees.

**9:25** And he said to Bidkar his officer, "Throw him into the portion of the field of Naboth the Israelite; for I remember, when you and I were riding in pairs behind Ahab his father, that the Lord took up this pronouncement against him:

**9:26** 'Surely I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday,' says the Lord, 'and I will repay him in this portion,' says the Lord. And now, please lift him up and throw him into the portion, according to the word of the Lord."

**9:27** And Ahaziah king of Judah saw it, and he fled by the way of Bethan. And Jehu pursued after him and said, "Him also!" And he struck him by the chariot at the ascent of Gai, which is Ekblaam. And he fled to Megiddo, and he died there.

**9:28** And his servants placed him on the chariot and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb in the city of David.

**9:29** And in the eleventh year of Joram, king of Israel, Ahaziah became king over Judah.

**9:30** And Jehu came to Israel, and Jezebel heard, and she painted her eyes and adorned her head, and looked out through the window.

**9:31** And Jehu was entering the city, and she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of his master?"

**9:32** And he lifted up his face to the window and saw her, and he said, "Who are you? Come down with me!" And two eunuchs looked down at him.

**9:33** And he said, "Roll her down!" And they rolled her down, and some of her blood splattered against the wall and against the horses, and they trampled her.

**9:34** And he went in and ate and drank, and he said, "Please attend to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

**9:35** And they went to bury her, and they found nothing of her except the skull and the feet and the palms of the hands.

**9:36** And they returned and reported to him, and he said, "This is the word of the Lord which He spoke by the hand of Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 'In the portion of Israel the dogs will devour the flesh of Jezebel,

**9:37** and the carcass of Jezebel will be like dung on the face of the field in the portion of Israel, so that they will not say, "Jezebel."'"

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — The Greek text adds "from before your face" and uses second-person verbs in 9:7-8 ("you will avenge" and "you will destroy"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has first-person verbs spoken by God ("I will avenge" and "I will cut off").
- **9:13** — "Top of the steps" renders the Greek phrase containing the transliterated Hebrew loanword garem (literally "the garem of the steps"), referring to the bare landing or upper platform of the stairs.
- **9:16** — The Greek text includes a major expansion detailing Joram's arrow wounds from "the Arameans" and adding "for he was mighty and a man of strength." Throughout chapter 9 (9:10, 15-17, 21, 25, 30, 36-37), the Greek reads "Israel" and "Israelite" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jezreel" and "Jezreelite."
- **9:20** — The Greek text literally reads "the driver was driving Jehu son of Nimshi, for he was in madness," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives furiously."
- **9:27** — The Greek place names "Bethan," "Gai," and "which is Ekblaam" correspond to Hebrew "Beth-haggan," "Gur," and "by Ibleam." In 9:32, the Greek text also adds "and saw her," reads "Who are you? Come down with me!" (Hebrew: "Who is on my side? Who?"), and mentions "two eunuchs" (Hebrew: "two or three eunuchs").
