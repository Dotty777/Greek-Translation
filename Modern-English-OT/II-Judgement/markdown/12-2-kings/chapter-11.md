## Text

**11:1** Now Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son had died, and she destroyed all the seed of the kingdom.

**11:2** And Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of her brother and stole him from the midst of the king's sons who were being put to death, him and his nurse, in the chamber of the beds, and she hid him from the presence of Athaliah, and he was not put to death.

**11:3** And he was with her in the house, hidden for six years, while Athaliah was reigning over the land.

**11:4** And in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and took the commanders of hundreds, the Chorri and the Rasin, and brought them to himself into the house of the Lord, and made with them a covenant of the Lord and put them under oath. And Jehoiada showed them the son of the king,

**11:5** and commanded them, saying, "This is the thing that you shall do: let a third of you enter on the Sabbath, and you shall keep the watch of the house of the king at the gateway,

**11:6** and a third at the gate of the ways, and a third at the gate behind the guards; and you shall keep the watch of the house.

**11:7** And two bands among you, everyone who goes out on the Sabbath, they shall keep the watch of the house of the Lord near the king.

**11:8** And surround the king all around, each man with his weapon in his hand, and whoever enters into aedoth shall die." And he was with the king when he went out and when he came in.

**11:9** And the commanders of hundreds did all that Jehoiada the discerning commanded; and each man took his men and those entering on the Sabbath, and came in to Jehoiada the priest.

**11:10** And the priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears and the threefold things of King David that were in the house of the Lord.

**11:11** And the guards stood, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house, along the altar and the house, all around the king.

**11:12** And he sent out the son of the king, and put upon him iezer and the testimony, and made him king and anointed him; and he took hold with his hand and said, "May the king live!"

**11:13** And Athaliah heard the sound of the runners of the people, and she went in to the people, into the house of the Lord.

**11:14** And she saw, and look, the king was standing by the pillar according to the ordinance, and the singers and the trumpets were beside the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing with trumpets. And Athaliah tore her garments and cried out, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**11:15** And Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds, the overseers of the army, and said to them, "Bring them out from inside the aseroth, and whoever enters after her shall surely be put to death by the sword." For the priest had said, "And let her not die in the house of the Lord."

**11:16** And they laid hands on her, and they entered by the way of the entrance of the horses of the house of the king, and she died there.

**11:17** And Jehoiada made a covenant between the Lord and the king and the people, that they should be a people for the Lord, and between the king and the people.

**11:18** And all the people of the land entered the house of Baal and tore it down, and his altars and his images they shattered thoroughly; and they killed Magthan the priest of Baal before the altars. And the priest set overseers in the house of the Lord.

**11:19** And he took the commanders of hundreds, and the Chorri, and the Rassim, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the Lord; and he entered by the way of the gate of the guards of the house of the king, and they seated him on the throne of the kings.

**11:20** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and they had put Athaliah to death with the sword in the house of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **11:4** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew military titles for "the Carites and the guards" with singular articles as "the Chorri and the Rasin" (spelled "Rassim" in v. 19).
- **11:8** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "ranks" (sederoth) as aedoth here and aseroth in v. 15 (where Greek also reads plural "them" and "from inside"), and closes v. 8 with past indicative "and he was" rather than an imperative.
- **11:10** — Greek "the threefold things" (tous trissous) translates a rare Hebrew term for shields or quivers, apparently connecting it with the Hebrew root for "three"; in v. 9 Greek likewise styles Jehoiada "the discerning" (ho synetos) rather than "the priest."
- **11:12** — Greek transliterates the Hebrew word for "crown" or "diadem" (hannezer) as iezer, and reads singular "took hold with his hand" (ekratesen, likely a variant of ekrotesen, "clapped") where Hebrew has plural "they clapped their hands."
- **11:18** — Greek reads the distinct name form Magthan (an uncial variant of Mattan) where Hebrew has Mattan; earlier in v. 14 Greek reads "the singers" (hoi oidoi) where Hebrew has "the captains."
