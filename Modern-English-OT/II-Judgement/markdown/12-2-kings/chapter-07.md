## Text

**7:1** And Elisha said, "Hear the word of the Lord: Thus says the Lord, 'About this hour tomorrow, a measure of fine flour for a shekel in the gates of Samaria.'"

**7:2** And the officer on whose hand the king was leaning answered Elisha and said, "Look, the Lord will make floodgates in heaven; could this thing happen?" And Elisha said, "Look, you will see it with your eyes, and from there you will not eat."

**7:3** And there were four leprous men beside the entrance of the city, and one man said to his neighbor, "Why are we sitting here until we die?

**7:4** If we say, 'Let us enter into the city,' then the famine is in the city, and we will die there; and if we sit here, then we will die. And now come, and let us throw ourselves into the camp of Syria; if they keep us alive, then we will live; and if they put us to death, then we will die."

**7:5** And they rose in the darkness to enter into the camp of Syria; and they came into the middle of the camp of Syria, and look, there was not a man there.

**7:6** And the Lord made the camp of Syria hear a sound of chariots and a sound of horses, a sound of a great force; and one man said to his brother, "Now the king of Israel has hired against us the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to come against us."

**7:7** And they rose and ran away in the darkness; and they abandoned their tents and their horses and their donkeys in the camp as it was, and fled for their lives.

**7:8** And these leprous men entered as far as a part of the camp, and they entered into one tent and ate and drank, and carried off from there silver and gold and clothing; and they went and returned from there and entered into another tent, and took from there and went and hid it.

**7:9** And one man said to his neighbor, "We are not doing thus; this day is a day of good news, and we are keeping silent; and we are waiting until the light of morning, and we will find lawlessness. And now come, and let us go in and report to the house of the king."

**7:10** And they went in and cried out toward the gate of the city, and reported to them, saying, "We entered into the camp of Syria, and look, there is not a man there nor the voice of a person, only tied horses and donkeys, and their tents as they are."

**7:11** And the gatekeepers cried out and reported to the house of the king inside.

**7:12** And the king rose by night and said to his servants, "I will tell you now what Syria has done to us: they know that we are hungry, and they have gone out from the camp and hidden themselves in the field, saying, 'They will come out of the city, and we will seize them alive and enter into the city.'"

**7:13** And one of his servants answered and said, "Let them take now five of the remaining horses that were left here; look, they are like all the multitude of Israel that is perishing; and we will send there and see."

**7:14** And they took two riders of horses, and the king of Israel sent them after the king of Syria, saying, "Go and see."

**7:15** And they went after them as far as the Jordan, and look, the whole road was full of garments and equipment that Syria had thrown away in their terror; and the messengers returned and reported to the king.

**7:16** And the people went out and plundered the camp of Syria; and a measure of fine flour was for a shekel, according to the word of the Lord, and two measures of barley for a shekel.

**7:17** And the king appointed the officer, on whom the king was leaning with his hand, over the gate, and the people trampled him in the gate, and he died, just as the man of God had spoken, who spoke when the messenger came down to him.

**7:18** And it happened just as Elisha had spoken to the king, saying, "Two measures of barley for a shekel and a measure of fine flour for a shekel, and it will be about this hour tomorrow in the gate of Samaria."

**7:19** And the officer answered Elisha and said, "Look, the Lord is making floodgates in heaven; this thing will certainly not happen." And Elisha said, "Look, you will see it with your eyes, and from there you will certainly not eat."

**7:20** And it happened so, and the people trampled him in the gate, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Greek here omits the parallel clause about two measures of barley for a shekel, which appears later in vv. 16 and 18.
- **7:9** — "We are not doing thus" literally renders the Greek ouch houtos (a calque of the Hebrew idiom for "not right"), and "we will find lawlessness" makes the speakers the subject rather than guilt finding them.
- **7:13** — The Greek presents a single comparison ("they are like all the multitude of Israel that is perishing," using eisin pros) where the Hebrew has a doubled clause.
- **7:14** — The Greek reads "two riders of horses" and "after the king of Syria," where the Hebrew reads "two chariots with horses" and "after the army of Aram."
- **7:17** — The Greek reads "the messenger" (matching 6:33), whereas the Hebrew reads "the king."
