## Text

**17:1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king in Samaria over Israel for nine years.

**17:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, yet not like the kings of Israel who were before him.

**17:3** Against him Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians went up; and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

**17:4** And the king of the Assyrians found wrongdoing in Hoshea, because he sent messengers to Segor king of Egypt and did not bring tribute to the king of the Assyrians in that year; and the king of the Assyrians besieged him and bound him in a prison house.

**17:5** And the king of the Assyrians went up in all the land, and went up to Samaria and laid siege against it for three years.

**17:6** In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of the Assyrians captured Samaria, and deported Israel to the Assyrians, and settled them in Halah and in Habor, the rivers of Gozar, and the mountains of the Medes.

**17:7** And it happened because the sons of Israel sinned against the Lord their God, who brought them up out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and feared other gods,

**17:8** and walked in the statutes of the nations that the Lord removed from before the sons of Israel, and the kings of Israel, as many as did so,

**17:9** and as many of the sons of Israel as clothed themselves with words that were not so against the Lord their God; and of which they built for themselves high places in all their cities, from a tower of watchmen to a fortified city.

**17:10** And they set up for themselves pillars and groves on every high hill and under every leafy tree.

**17:11** And they burned incense there at all the high places, just as the nations the Lord deported from before them, and they made partners and carved, to provoke the Lord to anger.

**17:12** And they served the idols concerning which the Lord said to them, "You shall not do this thing to the Lord."

**17:13** And the Lord testified against Israel and against Judah by the hand of all His prophets, of every seer of a word: "Turn back from your evil ways, and keep My commandments and My statutes and all the law that I commanded your fathers, all that I sent to them by the hand of My servants the prophets."

**17:14** And they did not listen, and they hardened their back more than the back of their fathers;

**17:15** and His testimonies, all that He testified to them; and they walked after worthless things and became worthless, and after the nations around them, concerning which He commanded them not to do like them.

**17:16** They forsook the commandments of the Lord their God, and made for themselves a cast image, two heifers, and made groves, and bowed down to all the host of heaven, and served Baal.

**17:17** And they led their sons and their daughters through fire, practiced divinations and read omens; and they were sold to do what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**17:18** And the Lord was very angry with Israel, and removed them from His presence; and none was left except the tribe of Judah all alone.

**17:19** And indeed Judah did not keep the commandments of God, and they walked in the statutes of Israel that they had made. And they rejected the

**17:20** Lord in all the seed of Israel. And He shook them and gave them into the hand of those who plundered them, until He cast them away from His presence.

**17:21** For Israel was apart from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king; and Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the Lord and caused them to sin a great sin.

**17:22** And the sons of Israel walked in every sin of Jeroboam that he committed; they did not turn away from it

**17:23** until the Lord removed Israel from His presence, just as the Lord spoke by the hand of all His servants the prophets; and Israel was deported from its land to the Assyrians to this day.

**17:24** And the king of the Assyrians brought from Babylon, the one from Cuthah, from Avva, and from Hamath and Sepharvaim; and they were settled in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel, and they inherited Samaria and lived in its cities.

**17:25** And it happened at the beginning of their dwelling, they did not fear the Lord, and the Lord sent the lions among them, and they were killing among them.

**17:26** And they spoke to the king of the Assyrians, saying, "The nations that you deported and resettled in the cities of Samaria did not know the ordinance of the God of the land, and He sent the lions among them; and look, they are putting them to death, because they do not know the ordinance of the God of the land."

**17:27** And the king of the Assyrians commanded, saying, "Lead away from there, and let them go and live there, and they will instruct them in the ordinance of the land."

**17:28** And they brought one of the priests whom they had deported from Samaria, and he settled in Bethel, and he was instructing them how they should fear the Lord.

**17:29** And nation by nation they were making their own gods, and placed them in the house of the high places that the Samaritans had made, the nations in their cities in which they lived.

**17:30** And the men of Babylon made Rochchoth-bainithei, and the men of Cuth made Ergel, and the men of Hamath made Asimath,

**17:31** and the Hivites made Eblazer and Tartak and Sepharun, when they burned their sons in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech of Sepharun.

**17:32** And they were fearing the Lord, and they settled their abominations in the houses of the high places that they made in Samaria, nation by nation, in the city in which they lived; and they were fearing the Lord, and they made priests of the high places, and they acted for themselves in the house of the high places.

**17:33** They feared the Lord, and they served their gods according to the ordinance of the nations from where he deported them.

**17:34** To this day they were acting according to their ordinance; they fear, and they act according to their statutes and according to their judgment, and according to the law and according to the commandment that the Lord commanded the sons of Jacob, whose name He set as Israel.

**17:35** And the Lord made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods or bow down to them, and you shall not serve them, and you shall not sacrifice to them;

**17:36** but rather to the Lord, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great strength and with a high arm; Him you shall fear, and to Him you shall bow down, to Him you shall sacrifice.

**17:37** And be careful all the days to do the statutes and the ordinances and the law and the commandments that He wrote for you, and you shall not fear other gods.

**17:38** And you shall not forget the covenant that He made with you; and you shall not fear other gods,

**17:39** but rather you shall fear the Lord your God, and He will deliver you from all your enemies;

**17:40** and you shall no longer listen to their ordinance that they practice."

**17:41** And these nations were fearing the Lord, and they were serving their carved images; and indeed the sons and the sons of their sons do just as their fathers did, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — "Segor" is the distinct Septuagint name corresponding to "So" in the Hebrew text; "tribute" here and in v. 3 translates the Greek Semitic loanwords manaa and manach, and "wrongdoing" renders Greek adikian (vs. Hebrew "conspiracy").
- **17:6** — The Greek reads plural "rivers of Gozar" (vs. Hebrew "river of Gozan") and "the mountains of the Medes" (Ore Medon), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "cities of the Medes."
- **17:11** — The Greek reads "made partners and carved" (koinonous kai echaraxan), whereas the Hebrew text has "did wicked things."
- **17:19** — The Greek splits "they rejected the Lord" across vv. 19-20, making the people the subject (followed in v. 20 by "He shook them"), whereas the Hebrew reads "the Lord rejected all the offspring of Israel and afflicted them."
- **17:30** — In vv. 30-31 the Greek preserves distinctive names—Rochchoth-bainithei, Ergel, Asimath, the Hivites (Huaioi), and Eblazer—and treats Sepharun as a third deity made by the Hivites rather than as the people who burned their children.
- **17:34** — Unlike the Hebrew text, the Greek omits the negations here ("they fear, and they act according to their statutes..."), and in v. 40 continues the Lord's command in the second person ("you shall no longer listen") rather than resuming third-person narrative.
