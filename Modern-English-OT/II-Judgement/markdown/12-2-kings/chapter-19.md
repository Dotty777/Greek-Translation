## Text

**19:1** And it happened, when King Hezekiah heard it, that he tore his garments and clothed himself in sackcloth, and went into the house of the Lord.

**19:2** And he sent Eliakim the steward and Shebna the scribe and the elders of the priests, clothed in sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz.

**19:3** And he said to him, "Thus says Hezekiah: This day is a day of distress and rebuke and provocation, for children have come to birth pangs, and there is no strength in the one giving birth.

**19:4** Perhaps the Lord your God will hear all the words of Rabshakeh, whom the king of the Assyrians, his master, sent to reproach the living God and to blaspheme with the words that the Lord your God has heard; so take up a prayer for the remnant that is found."

**19:5** And the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

**19:6** And Isaiah said to them, "This is what you will say to your master: 'Thus says the Lord: Do not be afraid of the words that you have heard, with which the lads of the king of the Assyrians have blasphemed.

**19:7** Look, I am putting a spirit in him, and he will hear a report and return to his own land; and I will strike him down by the sword in his own land.'"

**19:8** And Rabshakeh returned and found the king of the Assyrians fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that he had departed from Lachish.

**19:9** And he heard concerning Thara, king of the Ethiopians, saying, "Look, he has come out to fight with you." And he returned and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,

**19:10** "Do not let your God, in whom you trust, elate you, saying, 'Jerusalem will by no means be handed over into the hands of the king of the Assyrians.'

**19:11** Look, you have heard all that the kings of the Assyrians have done to all the lands, devoting them to destruction; and will you be rescued?

**19:12** Did the gods of the nations deliver them? Did not my fathers destroy both Gozan and Haran, and Raphis, and the sons of Eden who were in Thaesthen?

**19:13** Where is the king of Math and the king of Arpad? And where are Sepharvaim, Hanes, and Udu?"

**19:14** And Hezekiah took the letters from the hand of the messengers and read them; and he went up to the house of the Lord, and Hezekiah unfolded them before the Lord.

**19:15** And he said, "Lord, the God of Israel, who sits upon the cherubim, You alone are God in all the kingdoms of the earth; You made heaven and earth.

**19:16** Incline Your ear, Lord, and hear; open Your eyes, Lord, and see, and hear the words of Sennacherib, which he sent to reproach the living God.

**19:17** For in truth, Lord, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the nations.

**19:18** And they have put their gods into the fire, for they are not gods, but only works of human hands, wood and stone, and they destroyed them.

**19:19** And now, Lord our God, save us from his hand, and all the kingdoms of the earth will know that You alone are the Lord God."

**19:20** And Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, "This is what the Lord, the God of powers, the God of Israel, says: What you have prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib king of the Assyrians, I have heard.

**19:21** This is the word that the Lord has spoken against him: The virgin daughter of Zion has despised you and mocked you; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at you.

**19:22** Whom have you reproached and blasphemed? And against whom have you raised your voice and lifted up your eyes on high? Against the Holy One of Israel.

**19:23** By the hand of your messengers you have reproached your Lord, and you have said, 'With the multitude of my chariots I will ascend to the height of the mountains, the flanks of Lebanon; and I cut down the stature of its cedar, the choicest of its cypresses; and he came into the midst of forest and Carmel.

**19:24** I cooled off and drank foreign waters, and with the sole of my foot I laid waste all the rivers of siege.'

**19:25** I formed it, I gathered it; and it came to be for elevations from warlike dwellings, fortified cities.

**19:26** And those who live in them grew weak in hand; they stumbled and were put to shame; they became grass of the field or green vegetation, green shoots of the rooftops, and something trampled before one who stands.

**19:27** And I know your sitting down and your going out and your coming in, and your rage against Me.

**19:28** Because you have raged against Me, and your arrogance has come up into My ears, I will put My hooks in your nostrils and a bridle in your lips, and I will turn you back on the way by which you came.

**19:29** And this will be the sign for you: eat this year what grows of itself, and in the second year what springs up; and in the third year, sowing and reaping and planting of vineyards, and you will eat their fruit.

**19:30** And what is left will add the survivor of the house of Judah, a root below, and will produce fruit above.

**19:31** For out of Jerusalem a remnant will go forth, and a survivor from Mount Zion. The zeal of the Lord of powers will do this.

**19:32** Not so! Thus says the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians: He will not enter this city, and he will not shoot an arrow there, and a shield will not go before him, and he will by no means pour out a siege mound against it.

**19:33** By the way that he came, by it he will return, and he will not enter this city, says the Lord.

**19:34** And I will shield this city for My own sake and for the sake of David My servant."

**19:35** And it happened at night that an angel of the Lord went out and struck down in the camp of the Assyrians one hundred eighty-five thousand; and they rose early in the morning, and look, they were all dead bodies.

**19:36** And Sennacherib king of the Assyrians departed and went and returned, and lived in Nineveh.

**19:37** And it happened, as he was worshiping in the house of Esdrach his god, that Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons struck him down with the sword; and they escaped into the land of Ararat. And Esarhaddon his son became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — Greek reads 'and to blaspheme with the words' (parallel to 'to reproach') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'and will rebuke the words'; in 19:3 Greek also reads singular 'he said' rather than plural, and here spells 'remnant' as lemmatos (for leimmatos).
- **19:12** — Greek divides the verse into two questions rather than one relative clause and uses the LXX-distinct names Raphis (Hebrew Rezeph) and Thaesthen (Telassar), as well as Thara (Tirhakah) in 19:9 and Math (Hamath), Hanes (Hena), and Udu (Ivvah) in 19:13, where it also omits 'the king of the city of' before Sepharvaim.
- **19:23** — Greek reads 'your Lord' (kyrion sou, MT 'the Lord') and shifts to third-person 'he came into the midst of forest and Carmel'; in 19:24 it renders 'I dug' as 'I cooled off' (epsyxa) and 'Egypt' as 'siege' (perioches).
- **19:25** — Greek lacks the opening clause ('Have you not heard? Long ago I did it...') and reads 'for elevations from warlike dwellings'; in 19:30 Greek has the unusual accusative 'will add the survivor,' and in 19:32 reads 'Not so!' (ouch houtos, for Hebrew lo ken) instead of MT 'Therefore' (laken).
- **19:37** — Greek names Sennacherib's god Esdrach, corresponding to Nisroch in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
