## Text

**23:1** And the kings brought back the word; and the king sent and gathered to himself all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**23:2** And the king went up to the house of the Lord, and every man of Judah and those dwelling in Jerusalem with him, and the priests and the prophets and all the people, from small even to great; and he read before them all the words of the book of the covenant that was found in the house of the Lord.

**23:3** And the king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord, to keep His commandments and His testimonies and His statutes with all the heart and with all the soul, to establish the words of this covenant, the things written in this book; and all the people stood in the covenant.

**23:4** And the king commanded Hilkiah the high priest and the priests of the second order and those guarding the threshold to bring out of the temple of the Lord all the vessels that had been made for Baal and for the grove and for all the host of heaven; and he burned them outside Jerusalem in Salemoth of Kidron, and took their dust to Bethel.

**23:5** And he burned the chomarim whom they had given to the king of Judah, and they were burning incense on the high places and in the cities of Judah and in the places around Jerusalem, and those burning incense to Baal and to the sun and to the moon and to the mazuroth and to all the host of heaven.

**23:6** And he brought out the grove from the house of the Lord outside Jerusalem to the brook Kidron, and burned it in the brook Kidron and ground it fine into dust, and threw its dust into the grave of the sons of the people.

**23:7** And he demolished the house of the kadesim who were in the house of the Lord, where the women were weaving chettiin there for the grove.

**23:8** And he brought up all the priests from the cities of Judah, and defiled the high places where the priests had burned incense there, from Gaibal even to Beersheba; and he demolished the house of the gates that was beside the doorway of the gate of Joshua, ruler of the gate, those on a man's left in the gate of the city.

**23:9** However, the priests of the high places did not go up to the altar of the Lord in Jerusalem, but rather they ate unleavened bread in the midst of their brothers.

**23:10** And you will defile Topheth in the valley of the son of Hinnom, for a man to make his son pass through and a man his daughter to Molech in fire.

**23:11** And he burned the horses that the kings of Judah had given to the sun at the entrance of the house of the Lord, by the treasury of Nathan the king, the eunuch, in Pharurim; and he burned the chariot of the sun with fire.

**23:12** And the altars that were on the roof of the upper room of Ahaz, which the king of Judah had made, and the altars that Manasseh had made in the two courts of the house of the Lord, the king demolished and pulled down from there, and threw their dust into the brook Kidron.

**23:13** And the house facing Jerusalem, on the right of the mountain of Mosoath, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Astarte, the detestable thing of the Sidonians, and for Chemosh, the detestable thing of Moab, and for Milcom, the abomination of the sons of Ammon, the king defiled.

**23:14** And he shattered the pillars and utterly destroyed the groves, and filled their places with human bones.

**23:15** Furthermore, the altar that was in Bethel, the high place that Jeroboam son of Nebat made, who caused Israel to sin—even that altar, the high place, he pulled down, and shattered its stones and ground them fine into dust, and burned the grove.

**23:16** And Josiah turned aside and saw the graves there in the city, and sent and took the bones from the graves and burned them on the altar, and defiled it according to the word of the Lord that the man of God had spoken when Jeroboam stood at the feast by the altar. And turning, he lifted up his eyes to the grave of the man of God who had spoken these words.

**23:17** And he said, "What is that monument that I see?" And the men of the city said to him, "The man of God who came out of Judah and proclaimed these words that he proclaimed against the altar of Bethel."

**23:18** And he said, "Leave it alone; let no man move his bones." And his bones were rescued along with the bones of the prophet who came from Samaria.

**23:19** Moreover, all the houses of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel had made to provoke the Lord to anger, Josiah removed, and he did in them all the deeds that he had done in Bethel.

**23:20** And he sacrificed all the priests of the high places who were there upon the altars, and burned human bones upon them, and returned to Jerusalem.

**23:21** And the king commanded all the people, saying, "Keep the Passover to the Lord our God, as it is written in the book of this covenant."

**23:22** For this Passover had not been held since the days of the judges who judged Israel, nor in all the days of the kings of Israel and the kings of Judah.

**23:23** But only in the eighteenth year of King Josiah was the Passover held to the Lord in Jerusalem.

**23:24** Moreover, the wishers and the knowers and the teraphim and the idols and all the detestable things that were in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem Josiah cleared away, so that he might establish the words of the law that were written in the book that Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the Lord.

**23:25** Like him there was no king before him who turned to the Lord with all his heart and with all his strength and with all his soul, according to all the law of Moses, and after him no one like him arose.

**23:26** However, the Lord did not turn away from the great wrath of His anger with which He burned in His anger against Judah, on account of the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked Him to anger.

**23:27** And the Lord said, "I will also remove Judah from His presence, just as I removed Israel, and I will reject this city that I chose, Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, 'My name will be there.'"

**23:28** And the rest of the acts of Josiah and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**23:29** Now in his days Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt went up to the king of the Assyrians, to the Euphrates River, and Josiah went to meet him; and Neco put him to death at Megiddo when he saw him.

**23:30** And his servants carried him dead from Makedon and brought him to Jerusalem and buried him in his grave. And the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah and anointed him, and made him king in place of his father.

**23:31** Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Hamitai, daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

**23:32** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.

**23:33** And Pharaoh Neco deposed him at Ablaa in the land of Hamath so that he would not reign in Jerusalem, and he gave one hundred talents of silver and one hundred talents of gold.

**23:34** And Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, king over them in place of Josiah his father, and changed his name to Jehoiakim; and he took Jehoahaz and brought him into Egypt, and he died there.

**23:35** And Jehoiakim gave the silver and the gold to Pharaoh; however, they assessed the land to give the silver at the command of Pharaoh; each man according to his assessment, they gave the silver and the gold with the people of the land, to give to Pharaoh Neco.

**23:36** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Iella, daughter of Edil from Kruma.

**23:37** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — The Greek text begins this verse with plural "the kings brought back the word," corresponding to the close of 22:20 in the Hebrew ("and they brought back word to the king").
- **23:5** — Greek reads "burned" (Hebrew "deposed"), singular "to the king of Judah," and retains untranslated Hebrew terms as "chomarim" (idolatrous priests) and "mazuroth" (constellations), as also with "Salemoth" (v. 4), "kadesim" and "chettiin" (v. 7), and "Pharurim" (v. 11). Throughout the chapter, "grove" translates Greek alsos (Hebrew Asherah).
- **23:10** — Greek has second-person plural future "you will defile" and omits the negation before "for a man to make his son pass through" (Hebrew "and he defiled... so that no one might make... pass through"). Nearby, Greek reads "Gaibal" for Geba and "house of the gates" (v. 8), translates Nathan-melech literally as "Nathan the king" (v. 11), and turns Hebrew "of Destruction" into the proper name "Mosoath" (v. 13).
- **23:16** — Greek reads "in the city" (Hebrew "on the mountain") and contains a longer ending describing Jeroboam standing by the altar at the feast and Josiah lifting his eyes toward the grave of the man of God.
- **23:24** — "Wishers" and "knowers" are literal Greek etymological renderings of the Hebrew terms for mediums and spiritists. In v. 27, the Greek text reads third-person "from His presence" inside the Lord's first-person speech.
- **23:33** — Greek reads "deposed him at Ablaa" (Hebrew "imprisoned him at Riblah") and "one hundred talents of gold" (Hebrew "one talent of gold"). Nearby LXX-distinct proper names are "Makedon" (v. 30, for Megiddo), "Hamitai" (v. 31, for Hamutal), and "Iella", "Edil", and "Kruma" (v. 36, for Zebudah, Pedaiah, and Rumah).
