## Text

**3:1** And Joram son of Ahab became king in Israel in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

**3:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, yet not like his father and not like his mother, and he removed the pillars of Baal that his father had made;

**3:3** yet he clung to the sin of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin; he did not turn away from it.

**3:4** And Mesha king of Moab was a noketh, and he returned to the king of Israel in the rebellion one hundred thousand lambs and one hundred thousand fleecy rams.

**3:5** And it happened after Ahab died that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

**3:6** And King Joram went out on that day from Samaria and mustered Israel;

**3:7** and he went and sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me; will you go with me against Moab to war?" And he said, "I will go up; just as I am, so are you; as my people, so your people; as my horses, so your horses."

**3:8** And he said, "By what way shall I go up?" And he said, "The way of the wilderness of Edom."

**3:9** And the king of Israel and the king of Judah and the king of Edom went, and they made a circuit of a seven-day journey; and there was no water for the camp or for the livestock at their feet.

**3:10** And the king of Israel said, "Alas, for the Lord has called the three kings passing through, to give them into the hand of Moab!"

**3:11** And Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not a prophet of the Lord here, that we may inquire of the Lord from him?" And one of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, "Here is Elisha son of Jehoshaphat, who poured water over the hands of Elijah."

**3:12** And Jehoshaphat said, "He has a word." And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah and the king of Edom went down to him.

**3:13** And Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What is there between me and you? Go to the prophets of your father." And the king of Israel said to him, "No, for the Lord has called the three kings to hand them over into the hands of Moab."

**3:14** And Elisha said, "As the Lord of powers lives, before whom I have stood, were it not that I have regard for the person of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would neither look toward you nor see you;

**3:15** and now, get me a harpist." And it happened as the harpist was playing, that the hand of the Lord came upon him;

**3:16** and he said, "Thus says the Lord: 'Make this streambed pits upon pits.'

**3:17** For thus says the Lord: 'You will not see wind, and you will not see rain, yet this streambed will be filled with water, and you will drink, you and your herds and your livestock.

**3:18** And even this is a light thing in the eyes of the Lord; and I will hand Moab over into your hand.

**3:19** And you shall strike every fortified city, and you shall fell every good tree, and stop up all springs of water, and you shall make every good plot of land useless with stones.'"

**3:20** And it happened in the morning, as the offering was going up, that look, waters were coming from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.

**3:21** And all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight them; and they cried out, from everyone wearing a belt, and said, "Alas!" And they stood on the border.

**3:22** And they rose early in the morning, and the sun rose upon the waters; and Moab saw opposite them the waters red like blood,

**3:23** and they said, "This is blood of the sword! The kings have fought, and each man has struck his neighbor; and now, to the plunder, Moab!"

**3:24** And they entered into the camp of Israel; and Israel rose up and struck Moab, and they fled before them. And they went on entering and striking Moab,

**3:25** and they tore down the cities; and on every good plot of land, each man threw his stone and filled it, and they stopped up every spring, and they felled every good tree, until they left the stones of the wall torn down; and the slingers surrounded and struck it.

**3:26** And the king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, and he took with him seven hundred men with drawn swords to break through to the king of Edom; but they could not.

**3:27** And he took his firstborn son, who reigned in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering upon the wall; and great remorse came upon Israel. And they departed from him and returned to the land.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — Greek transliterates Hebrew noqed ("sheep breeder") as noketh and adds "in the rebellion" (en te epanastasei).
- **3:11** — Greek distinctly names Elisha's father as "Jehoshaphat" (Iosaphath), identical to the king of Judah in the same verse, where the Masoretic Text has "Shaphat."
- **3:21** — Greek renders the mobilization phrase actively and reads "and said, 'Alas!'" where the Masoretic Text has "and upward."
- **3:25** — Where the Masoretic Text names the city Kir-hareseth, Greek translates the elements as common words: "the stones of the wall torn down."
- **3:27** — Greek reads "great remorse" (metamelos megas) rather than "great wrath" (Masoretic Text qetsep gadol).
