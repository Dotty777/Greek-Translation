## Text

**2:1** And it happened when the Lord was bringing Elijah up in a whirlwind as into heaven, that Elijah and Elisha went from Jericho.

**2:2** And Elijah said to Elisha, "Now, please stay here, because God has sent me as far as Bethel." And Elisha said, "As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." And he came to Bethel.

**2:3** And the sons of the prophets who were in Bethel came to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the Lord is taking your master today from over your head?" And he said, "I also know; be silent."

**2:4** And Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, because the Lord has sent me to Jericho." And he said, "As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you." And they came to Jericho.

**2:5** And the sons of the prophets who were in Jericho approached Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the Lord is taking your master from over your head?" And he said, "Yes, I also know; be silent."

**2:6** And Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, because the Lord has sent me as far as the Jordan." And Elisha said, "As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you." And both of them went on.

**2:7** And fifty men, sons of the prophets, stood opposite at a distance; and both of them stood by the Jordan.

**2:8** And Elijah took his sheepskin and rolled it up and struck the water, and the water was divided this way and that; and both of them crossed over on barren ground.

**2:9** And it happened when they had crossed over that Elijah said to Elisha, "What shall I do for you before I am taken up from you?" And Elisha said, "Please let double in your spirit be upon me."

**2:10** And Elijah said, "You have asked something hard; if you see me being taken up from you, it will be so; and if not, it will certainly not happen."

**2:11** And it happened as they were going along, they walked on and talked; and look, a chariot of fire and a horse of fire, and it separated between both of them; and Elijah was taken up in a whirlwind as into heaven.

**2:12** And Elisha was watching, and he kept crying out, "Father, father, the chariot of Israel and its horseman!" And he saw him no longer; and he took hold of his garments and tore them into two torn pieces.

**2:13** And he lifted up the sheepskin of Elijah that had fallen from upon Elisha; and he stood on the bank of the Jordan.

**2:14** And he took the sheepskin of Elijah that had fallen from upon him, and struck the water and said, "Where is the God of Elijah, aphpho?" And he struck the waters, and they split apart this way and that; and Elisha crossed over.

**2:15** And the sons of the prophets and those in Jericho saw him from opposite and said, "The spirit of Elijah has rested upon Elisha." And they came to meet him and bowed down to him to the ground.

**2:16** And they said to him, "Look now, with your servants there are fifty men, sons of strength; please let them go and search for your master, in case the Spirit of the Lord has found him and cast him into the Jordan, or onto one of the mountains, or onto one of the hills." And Elisha said, "You shall not send."

**2:17** And they urged him strongly until he was ashamed, and he said, "Send." And they sent fifty men, and they searched three days, and did not find him.

**2:18** And he was staying in Jericho; and Elisha said, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

**2:19** And the men of the city said to Elisha, "Look, the habitation of the city is good, as the master sees, but the waters are bad and the land is becoming childless."

**2:20** And Elisha said, "Bring me a new water jar and put salt there." And they brought it to him.

**2:21** And Elisha went out to the outlet of the waters, and threw salt there and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'I have healed these waters; there will no longer be from there death or childlessness.'"

**2:22** And the waters were healed until this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

**2:23** And he went up from there to Bethel; and as he was going up on the road, small boys came out from the city and mocked him and said to him, "Go up, baldhead, go up!"

**2:24** And he turned behind them and saw them, and cursed them in the name of the Lord. And look, two bears came out from the forest and tore apart forty-two boys from among them.

**2:25** And he went from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Greek reads "from Jericho" (where the Masoretic Text has "from Gilgal") and qualifies Elijah's ascent with "as into heaven" (also in 2:11).
- **2:8** — Greek melote specifically denotes a sheepskin garment (also in 2:13-14), and en eremo (literally "in a desert/barren ground") contrasts with the Masoretic Text's "on dry ground."
- **2:13** — Greek explicitly reads "from upon Elisha," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "from upon him" (referring to Elijah).
- **2:14** — Greek leaves Hebrew aph-hu ("even he") untranslated as the transliteration aphpho, and omits "the Lord" before "the God of Elijah."
- **2:16** — Greek reads "has found him" (rather than "has lifted him up") and adds "into the Jordan" before the mountains and hills.
