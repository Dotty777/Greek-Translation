## Text

**12:1** Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.

**12:2** In the seventh year of Jehu, Joash became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Abia, from the land of Beersheba.

**12:3** And Joash did what was right before the Lord all the days in which Jehoiada the priest enlightened him.

**12:4** However, the high places were not removed, and there the people still sacrificed and were burning incense on the high places.

**12:5** And Joash said to the priests, "All the silver of the holy things that is brought into the house of the Lord, silver of assessment, each man taking silver of assessment, all silver that may take hold upon a man's heart to bring into the house of the Lord,

**12:6** let the priests take for themselves, each man from his sale, and they themselves shall secure the breach of the house, for everything wherever a breach is found there."

**12:7** And it happened in the twenty-third year of King Joash that the priests had not reinforced the breach of the house.

**12:8** And King Joash called Jehoiada the priest and the priests, and said to them, "Why is it that you were not reinforcing the breach of the house? And now, do not take silver from your sales, for you shall give it for the breach of the house."

**12:9** And the priests agreed not to take silver from the people and not to strengthen the abomination of the house.

**12:10** And Jehoiada the priest took one chest, and bored a hole in its hole, and placed it beside Iameibin in the house of a man, the house of the Lord; and the priests who guarded the threshold gave all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord.

**12:11** And it happened, when he saw that there was much silver in the chest, that the scribe of the king and the high priest went up, and they bound up and counted the silver that was found in the house of the Lord.

**12:12** And he gave the silver that had been prepared into the hands of those doing the work, the overseers of the house of the Lord; and they paid it out to the craftsmen of wood and to the builders who were working in the house of the Lord,

**12:13** and to the wall-builders and to the cutters of stone, to acquire wood and cut stones to secure the breach of the house of the Lord, for everything that was spent on the house to reinforce it.

**12:14** However, there shall not be made for the house of the Lord silver doors, nails, bowls, and trumpets, or any gold vessel or silver vessel, from the silver that was brought into the house of the Lord;

**12:15** for they shall give it to those doing the work. And they reinforced the house of the Lord with it.

**12:16** And they did not take an account from the men into whose hands they were giving the silver to give to those doing the work, because they act in their faithfulness.

**12:17** Silver for sin and silver for trespass, whatever was brought into the house of the Lord, belonged to the priests.

**12:18** Then Hazael king of Syria went up and fought against Gath, and captured it. And Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.

**12:19** And Joash king of Judah took all the holy things that Jehoshaphat and Joram and Ahaziah, his fathers and kings of Judah, had consecrated, and his own holy things, and all the gold that was found in the treasuries of the house of the Lord and of the house of the king, and sent them to Hazael king of Syria; and he went up from Jerusalem.

**12:20** And the rest of the matters of Joash, and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**12:21** And his servants rose up and bound every conspiracy, and they struck Joash in the house of Maalo, Gaalla.

**12:22** And Jezichar son of Jemuath and Jezebuth, his son Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died; and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David; and Amaziah his son became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Greek names Joash's mother Abia (Hebrew: Zibiah) and adds "the land of" before Beersheba.
- **12:6** — Greek reads "his sale" (and "your sales" in v. 8), interpreting Hebrew makkar as "sale" rather than "acquaintance." Here and in vv. 7-8, 13, "breach" renders the Hebrew loanword bedek ("breach/disrepair") transliterated directly in the Greek.
- **12:9** — Greek reads "the abomination (bdelygma) of the house," likely an inner-Greek scribal corruption of the transliterated bedek ("breach") appearing in vv. 6-8 and 13.
- **12:10** — Greek literally reads "in its hole" (Hebrew: "in its lid"), retains a garbled location name Iameibin (Hebrew: "the altar on the right side"), and reads "in the house of a man" (Hebrew: "as a man enters").
- **12:17** — Unlike Hebrew ("was not brought"), Greek omits the negation ("whatever was brought") and places sin silver before trespass silver.
- **12:21** — Greek literally reads "bound every bond/conspiracy" and has the distinct place names Maalo and Gaalla (Hebrew: Millo and Silla).
- **12:22** — Greek has the distinct names Jezichar, Jemuath, and Jezebuth (Hebrew: Jozacar, Shimeath, and Jehozabad), and literally reads "his son Shomer" (Hebrew: "son of Shomer").
