## Text

**13:1** In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king in Samaria for seventeen years.

**13:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, and walked after the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin; he did not turn away from it.

**13:3** And the Lord became angry in wrath against Israel, and gave them into the hand of Hazael king of Syria and into the hand of Son of Hader, son of Hazael, all the days.

**13:4** And Jehoahaz implored the face of the Lord, and the Lord listened to him, because He saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Syria oppressed them.

**13:5** And the Lord gave deliverance to Israel, and it came out from under the hand of Syria; and the sons of Israel dwelt in their tents just as yesterday and the day before.

**13:6** However, they did not turn away from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who caused Israel to sin; in it he walked, and even the grove stood in Samaria.

**13:7** For there was not left to Jehoahaz a people except fifty horsemen and ten chariots and ten thousand foot soldiers, because the king of Syria had destroyed them, and they made them like dust for trampling.

**13:8** And the rest of the matters of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:9** And Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Joash his son became king.

**13:10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Joash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria for sixteen years.

**13:11** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not turn away from all the sin of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin; in it he walked.

**13:12** And the rest of the matters of Joash, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds that he did with Amaziah king of Judah, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:13** And Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat with his fathers and in Samaria with the brothers of Israel.

**13:14** And Elisha fell sick with his illness from which he died; and Joash king of Israel went down to him and wept over his face and said, "Father, father, the chariot of Israel and its horseman!"

**13:15** And Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." And he took a bow and arrows to him.

**13:16** And he said to the king, "Set your hand upon the bow." And Joash set his hand on it, and Elisha laid his hands upon the hands of the king,

**13:17** and said, "Open the window toward the east." And he opened it. And he said, "Arrow of deliverance for the Lord, and arrow of deliverance in Syria; and it will strike Syria in Aphek to a complete end."

**13:18** And Elisha said to him, "Take bows." And he took them. And he said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground." And the king struck three times and stopped.

**13:19** And the man of God was grieved at him and said, "If you had struck five or six times, then you would have struck Syria to a complete end; and now you will strike Syria three times."

**13:20** And Elisha died, and they buried him. And raiders of Moab came into the land when the year came.

**13:21** And it happened, as they were burying the man, that look, they saw the raider, and they threw the man into the tomb of Elisha; and he went and touched the bones of Elisha, and he came to life and stood up on his feet.

**13:22** And Hazael oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

**13:23** And the Lord showed mercy to them and had compassion on them, and looked upon them because of His covenant with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, and the Lord was not willing to destroy them, and He did not cast them away from His presence.

**13:24** And Hazael king of Syria died, and Son of Hader his son became king in his place.

**13:25** And Joash son of Jehoahaz returned and took the cities from the hand of Son of Hader, son of Hazael, which he had taken from the hand of Jehoahaz his father in the war. Three times Joash struck him, and he restored the cities of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — Greek renders Hebrew Ben-hadad literally as "Son of Hader" (also in vv. 24-25), with final -r reflecting a Hebrew d/r interchange.
- **13:5** — Greek reads the abstract noun "deliverance" (soteria) where Hebrew has "deliverer/savior."
- **13:13** — Greek reads "Jeroboam sat with his fathers and in Samaria with the brothers of Israel," diverging sharply from Hebrew ("Jeroboam sat on his throne; and Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel").
- **13:17** — Greek omits the command "Shoot!" and its execution, and uses third-person singular "it will strike" (Hebrew: "you will strike").
- **13:18** — Greek uses plural toxa ("bows," sometimes meaning arrows) where Hebrew has "the arrows," and v. 19 reads "was grieved" where Hebrew has "was angry."
