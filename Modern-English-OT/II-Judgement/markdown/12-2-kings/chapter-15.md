## Text

**15:1** In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah king of Judah became king.

**15:2** He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Jecoliah, from Jerusalem.

**15:3** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that Amaziah his father did.

**15:4** However, he did not remove the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places.

**15:5** And the Lord touched the king, and he was leprous until the day of his death, and he reigned in the house of Aphphusoth; and Jonathan the king's son was over the house, judging the people of the land.

**15:6** And the rest of the matters of Azariah and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**15:7** And Azariah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David; and Jonathan his son became king in his place.

**15:8** In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam reigned over Israel in Samaria six months.

**15:9** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, just as his fathers did; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:10** And Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him, and they struck him down at Keblaam and put him to death; and he became king in his place.

**15:11** And the rest of the matters of Zechariah, look, they are written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel.

**15:12** The word of the Lord that He spoke to Jehu, saying, "Fourth-generation sons will sit for you on the throne of Israel"; and it happened so.

**15:13** And Shallum son of Jabesh became king; and in the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Shallum reigned for days in Samaria.

**15:14** And Menahem son of Gadi went up from Tirzah and came to Samaria, and he struck down Shallum son of Jabesh in Samaria and put him to death.

**15:15** And the rest of the matters of Shallum and his conspiracy that he formed, look, they are written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel.

**15:16** Then Menahem also struck down Tirzah and everything in it and its borders from Tirzah, because they did not open to him; and he struck it down, and he ripped open the pregnant women.

**15:17** In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem son of Gadi reigned over Israel ten years in Samaria.

**15:18** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:19** In his days Phua king of the Assyrians went up against the land, and Menahem gave Phua one thousand talents of silver, that his hand might be with him.

**15:20** And Menahem levied the silver upon Israel, upon everyone mighty in strength, to give to the king of the Assyrians, fifty shekels for each man; and the king of the Assyrians turned back and did not remain there in the land.

**15:21** And the rest of the matters of Menahem and all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:22** And Menahem slept with his fathers, and Pekahiah his son became king in his place.

**15:23** In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem reigned over Israel in Samaria two years.

**15:24** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:25** And Pekah son of Remaliah, his officer, conspired against him and struck him down in Samaria in front of the king's house, with Argob and with him Arieh; and with him were fifty men from the four hundred; and he put him to death and became king in his place.

**15:26** And the rest of the matters of Pekahiah and all that he did, look, they are written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel.

**15:27** In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty years.

**15:28** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**15:29** In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser king of the Assyrians came and took Ain and Abel and Thamaacha and Anioch and Kenez and Hazor and Gilead and Galilee, all the land of Naphtali, and he deported them to the Assyrians.

**15:30** And Hoshea son of Elah formed a conspiracy against Pekah son of Remaliah, and he struck him down and put him to death, and he became king in his place in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Ahaz.

**15:31** And the rest of the matters of Pekah and all that he did, look, they are written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel.

**15:32** In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jonathan son of Azariah king of Judah became king.

**15:33** He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Jerusha daughter of Zadok.

**15:34** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that Uzziah his father did.

**15:35** However, he did not remove the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places. He himself built the upper gate of the house of the Lord.

**15:36** And the rest of the matters of Jotham and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**15:37** In those days the Lord began to send out Rezin king of Syria and Pekah son of Remaliah.

**15:38** And Jotham slept with his fathers, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father; and Ahaz his son became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — The Greek reads 'reigned' for Hebrew 'lived', transliterates Hebrew 'separate house' as Aphphusoth, and names the king's son Jonathan here and in verses 7 and 32, but Jotham in verses 30, 36, and 38.
- **15:10** — The Greek treats the Hebrew phrase 'before the people' as the place name Keblaam and uses plural verbs for the assassins.
- **15:16** — The Greek reads Tirzah in both occurrences, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Tiphsah followed by Tirzah.
- **15:19** — The Greek names the Assyrian king Phua where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Pul, and omits the closing Hebrew clause about strengthening the kingdom in his hand.
- **15:25** — The Greek reads 'in front of the king's house' and 'from the four hundred', whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'in the citadel of the king's house' and 'of the Gileadites'.
- **15:29** — The Greek gives distinct place-name forms (Ain, Abel and Thamaacha as two towns, Anioch, and Kenez) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, and Kedesh; the Greek also spells Tiglath-pileser as Algathphellasar.
- **15:30** — The Greek reads 'Jotham son of Ahaz', whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Jotham son of Uzziah'.
