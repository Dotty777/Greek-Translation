## Text

**18:1** And it happened in the third year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, that Hezekiah son of Ahaz, king of Judah, became king.

**18:2** He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Abu, daughter of Zechariah.

**18:3** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.

**18:4** He himself removed the high places, and shattered the pillars, and destroyed the groves and the bronze serpent that Moses had made, because until those days the sons of Israel had been burning incense to it; and he called it Nesthalei.

**18:5** In the Lord, the God of Israel, he put his hope; and after him there was no one like him among the kings of Judah, nor among those who were before him.

**18:6** And he clung to the Lord; he did not turn away from following Him, and he kept His commandments, all that He had commanded Moses.

**18:7** And the Lord was with him, and in all that he did he acted wisely; and he rebelled against the king of the Assyrians and did not serve him.

**18:8** He himself struck down the foreigners as far as Gaza and as far as its border, from a tower of watchmen even to a fortified city.

**18:9** And it happened in the fourth year of King Hezekiah, which was the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, that Shalmaneser, king of the Assyrians, went up against Samaria and laid siege against it.

**18:10** And he seized it at the end of three years, in the sixth year of Hezekiah, which was the ninth year of Hoshea, king of Israel, and Samaria was captured.

**18:11** And the king of the Assyrians deported Samaria to the Assyrians, and placed them in Halah and in Habor, the river of Gozan, and in Ore of the Medes,

**18:12** because they did not listen to the voice of the Lord their God, and they transgressed His covenant, all that Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded; and they did not listen and did not do them.

**18:13** And in the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib, king of the Assyrians, went up against the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.

**18:14** And Hezekiah, king of Judah, sent messengers to the king of the Assyrians at Lachish, saying, "I have sinned; turn back from me. Whatever you impose on me, I will bear." And the king of the Assyrians imposed on Hezekiah, king of Judah, three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.

**18:15** And Hezekiah gave all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord and in the treasuries of the king's house.

**18:16** At that time Hezekiah cut off the doors of the temple and the supports that Hezekiah, king of Judah, had overlaid with gold, and gave them to the king of the Assyrians.

**18:17** And the king of the Assyrians sent Tartan, Raphis, and Rabshakeh from Lachish to King Hezekiah with a heavy force against Jerusalem; and they went up and came to Jerusalem, and they stood at the aqueduct of the upper pool, which is on the road of the fuller's field.

**18:18** And they called out to Hezekiah, and Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and Jehoshaphat the recorder came to him.

**18:19** And Rabshakeh said to them, "Say now to Hezekiah, 'Thus says the great king, the king of the Assyrians: What is this confidence in which you trust?

**18:20** You said—only words of the lips—"Counsel and strength for war." Now then, in whom do you trust, that you have rebelled against me?

**18:21** Now look, you have trusted for yourself on this crushed reed staff, on Egypt; if any man leans on it, it will go into his hand and pierce it. So is Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to all who trust in him.

**18:22** And since you said to me, "We trust in the Lord God," did not this very Hezekiah remove His high places and His altars, and say to Judah and to Jerusalem, "Before this altar you shall worship in Jerusalem"?'

**18:23** And now enter into dealings with my lord, the king of the Assyrians, and I will give you two thousand horses, if you are able to put riders on them for yourself.

**18:24** And how will you turn back the face of one governor among the least of my lord's servants? Yet you yourself have put your hope in Egypt for chariots and horsemen.

**18:25** And now, is it without the Lord that we have come up against this place to destroy it? The Lord said to me, 'Go up against this land and destroy it.'"

**18:26** And Eliakim son of Hilkiah, and Shebna, and Joash said to Rabshakeh, "Please speak to your servants in Syrian; we understand it, and do not speak with us in Judean. And why are you speaking in the ears of the people who are on the wall?"

**18:27** And Rabshakeh said to them, "Was it to your lord and to you that my lord sent me to speak these words? Was it not to the men sitting on the wall, who are to eat their own dung and drink their own urine together with you?"

**18:28** And Rabshakeh stood and cried out loudly in Judean, and spoke and said, "Hear the words of the great king of the Assyrians.

**18:29** Thus says the king: 'Do not let Hezekiah elate you with words, for he will by no means be able to deliver you from his hand,

**18:30** and do not let Hezekiah make you hope in the Lord, saying, "The Lord will surely deliver; this city will by no means be given into the hand of the king of the Assyrians."'

**18:31** Do not listen to Hezekiah, for thus says the king of the Assyrians: 'Make a blessing with me and come out to me, and each man will drink his own vine, and each man will eat his own fig tree, and drink water from his own cistern,

**18:32** until I come and take you to a land like your own land, of grain and wine and bread and vineyards, a land of oil-bearing olive trees and honey; and you will live and by no means die. And do not listen to Hezekiah, for he deceives you, saying, "The Lord will rescue you."

**18:33** Have the gods of the nations indeed rescued, each one his own country, from the hand of the king of the Assyrians?

**18:34** Where is the god of Hamath and Arpad? Where is the god of Sepharvaim? And have they delivered Samaria from my hand?

**18:35** Who among all the gods of the lands have delivered their lands from my hand, that the Lord should deliver Jerusalem from my hand?'"

**18:36** And they kept silent and did not answer him a word, for it was the king's command, saying, "You shall not answer him."

**18:37** And Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and Joash son of Shaphan, the recorder, came in to Hezekiah with their garments torn, and reported to him the words of Rabshakeh.

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — Greek reads "Abu" (Abou), whereas the Hebrew text has "Abi."
- **18:4** — Greek reads "Nesthalei" where Hebrew has "Nehushtan" (and other Greek witnesses read "Neesthan"); Greek also uses plural "the groves" for Hebrew "the Asherah."
- **18:11** — Greek reads "Samaria" where Hebrew has "Israel," and accents "Ore of the Medes" as a proper name transliterating Hebrew are ("cities of"), whereas other Greek editions accent it ore ("mountains of the Medes").
- **18:17** — Greek reads "Raphis" where Hebrew has "Rab-saris," and spells Tartan as "Thanthan."
- **18:18** — Greek names the recorder "Jehoshaphat" here and "Joash" or "Joash son of Shaphan" in verses 26 and 37, where Hebrew consistently has "Joah son of Asaph."
- **18:31** — "Make a blessing with me" translates Greek eulogian literally, reflecting a Hebrew idiom for coming to peaceful terms; Greek also reads "drink" of the vine where Hebrew has "eat."
