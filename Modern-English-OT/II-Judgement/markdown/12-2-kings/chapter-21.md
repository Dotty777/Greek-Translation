## Text

**21:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for fifty-five years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Hephzibah.

**21:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, according to the abominations of the nations whom the Lord removed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:3** And he returned and built the high places that Hezekiah his father had torn down, and he set up an altar to Baal, and made groves just as Ahab king of Israel had done, and bowed down to all the host of heaven and served them.

**21:4** And he built an altar in the house of the Lord, as He had said, "In Jerusalem I will place My name."

**21:5** And he built an altar for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.

**21:6** And he led his sons through fire, and practiced divination and augury, and appointed a medium and multiplied spiritists, so as to do what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, to provoke Him to anger.

**21:7** And he placed the carved image of the grove in the house of which the Lord had spoken to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house in Jerusalem, I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, and I will place My name forever,

**21:8** and I will not again move the foot of Israel from the land that I gave to their fathers—those who will keep all that I commanded, according to the entire commandment that My servant Moses commanded."

**21:9** And they did not listen, and Manasseh led them astray to do what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, more than the nations that the Lord destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:10** And the Lord spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets, saying,

**21:11** "Because Manasseh the king of Judah has done these abominations, more evil than all that the Amorite who was before did, and has even caused Judah to sin with their idols;

**21:12** not so; thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, 'Look, I am bringing calamities upon Jerusalem and upon Judah, so that both ears of everyone who hears will ring;

**21:13** and I will stretch out over Jerusalem the measure of Samaria and the plumb weight of the house of Ahab, and I will wipe out Jerusalem just as an alabaster jar is wiped out when it is wiped, and is turned over onto its face;

**21:14** and I will cast off the remnant of My inheritance, and I will hand them over into the hands of their enemies, and they will become plunder and spoil to all their enemies,

**21:15** because he did what was evil in My eyes, and they have been provoking Me to anger from the day I brought their fathers out of Egypt and until this day.'"

**21:16** And indeed, Manasseh shed very much innocent blood, until he filled Jerusalem from edge to edge, besides their sins by which he caused Judah to sin, to do what was evil in the eyes of the Lord.

**21:17** And the rest of the matters of Manasseh, and all that he did, and his sin that he sinned, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**21:18** And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza; and Amos his son reigned in his place.

**21:19** Amos was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for two years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Meshullam, daughter of Haruz from Jesebal.

**21:20** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the Lord, just as Manasseh his father had done.

**21:21** And he walked in every way in which his father had walked, and served the idols that his father had served, and bowed down to them.

**21:22** And he abandoned the Lord God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the Lord.

**21:23** And the servants of Amos conspired against him, and put him, the king, to death in his house.

**21:24** And the people of the land struck down all those who had conspired against King Amos; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

**21:25** And the rest of the matters of Amos, all that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**21:26** And they buried him in his tomb in the garden of Uzza; and Josiah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Greek text reads "ellen," a scribal corruption in Codex Vaticanus of "theleten" ("medium" or "necromancer," cf. 23:24) preserved in other Greek witnesses. The Greek also reads plural "his sons" (Hebrew: "his son").
- **21:12** — Greek "not so" (ouch houtos) is a characteristic kaige-Septuagint rendering of Hebrew "therefore" (lakhen) read as "lo khen" ("not so"); the same rendering recurs in 22:20.
- **21:13** — The Greek reads "an alabaster jar" (ho alabastros), where the Hebrew text reads "a dish."
- **21:16** — Literally "mouth to mouth" (stoma eis stoma), a Semitic idiom meaning from one edge or end to the other. The Greek also reads plural "their sins" (Hebrew: "his sin").
- **21:18** — The Greek text names Manasseh's son "Amos" throughout vv. 18-25 (Hebrew: "Amon"), and in v. 19 calls his mother "Meshullam" from "Jesebal" (Hebrew: "Meshullemeth" from "Jotbah").
