## Text

**14:1** In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, became king.

**14:2** He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Jehoaddin, from Jerusalem.

**14:3** And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, yet not like David his father; he did according to all that Joash his father did.

**14:4** However, he did not remove the high places; the people still sacrificed and were burning incense on the high places.

**14:5** And it happened, when the kingdom became strong in his hand, that he struck down his servants who had struck down his father.

**14:6** And he did not put to death the sons of those who struck him down, just as it is written in the book of the laws of Moses, as the Lord commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die for sons, and sons shall not die for fathers, but only in his own sins shall one die."

**14:7** He himself struck down ten thousand of Edom in Remele, and he captured the rock in the war and called its name Kathoel, until this day.

**14:8** Then Amaziah sent messengers to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us see one another face to face."

**14:9** And Joash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thistle that is in Lebanon sent to the cedar that is in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' And the wild beasts of the field that were in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle.

**14:10** You have indeed struck down Idumea, and your heart has lifted you up. Be glorified while staying in your house. And why do you contend in your evil? And you will fall, you and Judah with you."

**14:11** And Amaziah did not listen; and the king of Israel went up, and they saw one another face to face, he and Amaziah king of Judah, at Beth-shemesh, the land of Judah.

**14:12** And Judah was defeated before Israel, and each man fled to his tent.

**14:13** And Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh; and he came to Jerusalem and tore down four hundred cubits in the wall of Jerusalem, at the Gate of Ephraim up to the Corner Gate.

**14:14** And he took the gold and the silver and all the vessels found in the house of the Lord and in the treasuries of the king's house, and the sons of the comminglings, and he returned to Samaria.

**14:15** And the rest of the matters of Joash, all that he did in his might, what he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**14:16** And Joash slept with his fathers, and he was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; and Jeroboam his son became king in his place.

**14:17** And Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

**14:18** And the rest of the matters of Amaziah and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Judah?

**14:19** And they formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; and he sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

**14:20** And they carried him on horses, and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the city of David.

**14:21** And all the people of Judah took Azariah, and he was sixteen years old, and he became king in place of his father Amaziah.

**14:22** He himself built Elath and restored it to Judah after the king slept with his fathers.

**14:23** In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash reigned over Israel in Samaria forty-one years.

**14:24** And he did what was evil before the Lord; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.

**14:25** He himself removed the border of Israel from the entrance of Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, according to the word of the Lord, God of Israel, which He spoke by the hand of His servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet who was from Gath-hepher.

**14:26** For the Lord saw that the affliction of Israel was very bitter, and that those who were confined, depleted, and forsaken were few, and there was no one helping Israel.

**14:27** And the Lord did not speak to blot out the seed of Israel from under heaven, and He saved them from the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash.

**14:28** And the rest of the matters of Jeroboam and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, how he fought and how he restored Damascus and Hamath to Judah in Israel, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel?

**14:29** And Jeroboam slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel, and Azariah son of Amaziah became king in place of his father.

## Translator Notes

- **14:7** — The Greek renders Hebrew 'Valley of Salt' as the place name Remele, translates Hebrew Sela as 'the rock', and reads Kathoel where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Joktheel.
- **14:13** — The Greek names Amaziah's grandfather as Jehoahaz, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Ahaziah.
- **14:14** — The phrase 'the sons of the comminglings' reflects a literal Greek rendering of the Hebrew idiom for hostages, connecting the Hebrew term with a root meaning 'to mingle'.
- **14:25** — The Greek text reads 'removed' (apestesen), likely an early textual shortening of 'restored' (apekatestesen), corresponding to 'restored' in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **14:27** — The Greek reads 'the seed of Israel' and 'from the hand of Jeroboam', whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the name of Israel' and 'by the hand of Jeroboam'.
- **14:29** — The Greek text names Azariah son of Amaziah—king of Judah—as reigning in place of his father here, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text names Jeroboam's successor as Zechariah his son.
