## Text

**1:1** And Moab rebelled against Israel after Ahab died.

**1:2** And Ahaziah fell through the lattice in his upper chamber in Samaria and became ill; and he sent messengers and said to them, "Go and inquire of Baal the fly, god of Ekron, whether I will live from this illness of mine." And they went to inquire through him.

**1:3** And an angel of the Lord called Elijah the Tishbite, saying, "Rise, go to meet the messengers of Ahaziah king of Samaria, and you shall speak to them, 'Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to inquire of Baal the fly, god of Ekron? And it is not so;

**1:4** for thus says the Lord: "The bed onto which you went up there, you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die."'" And Elijah went and spoke to them.

**1:5** And the messengers returned to him, and he said to them, "Why is it that you have returned?"

**1:6** And they said to him, "A man came up to meet us and said to us, 'Go, return to the king who sent you, and speak to him, "Thus says the Lord: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to inquire of Baal the fly, god of Ekron? Not so! The bed onto which you went up, surely you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die."'"

**1:7** And he spoke to them, "What was the manner of the man who came up to meet you and spoke these words to you?"

**1:8** And they said to him, "A hairy man, wearing a leather belt around his waist." And he said, "This is Elijah the Tishbite."

**1:9** And he sent to him a commander of fifty and his fifty, and he went up to him; and look, Elijah was sitting on the top of the mountain. And the commander of fifty spoke to him and said, "Man of God, the king has called you; come down."

**1:10** And Elijah answered and said to the commander of fifty, "And if I am a man of God, fire will come down from heaven and devour you and your fifty." And fire came down from heaven and devoured him and his fifty.

**1:11** And the king again sent to him another commander of fifty and his fifty; and the commander of fifty spoke to him and said, "Man of God, thus says the king: 'Come down quickly.'"

**1:12** And Elijah answered and spoke to him and said, "If I am a man of God, fire will come down from heaven and devour you and your fifty." And fire came down from heaven and devoured him and his fifty.

**1:13** And the king proceeded yet again to send a leader and his fifty; and the third commander of fifty came and bowed on his knees before Elijah, and pleaded with him and spoke to him and said, "Man of God, let my life and the life of these servants of yours be precious in your eyes;

**1:14** look, fire came down from heaven and devoured the first two commanders of fifty; and now, please let my life be precious in your eyes."

**1:15** And an angel of the Lord spoke to Elijah and said, "Go down with him; do not be afraid of them." And Elijah rose and went down with him to the king.

**1:16** And Elijah spoke to him and said, "Thus says the Lord: 'Why is it that you sent to inquire of Baal the fly, god of Ekron? Not so! The bed onto which you went up there, you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die.'"

**1:17** And he died according to the word of the Lord that Elijah spoke.

**1:18** And the rest of the matters of Ahaziah that he did, look, are these not written in the book of the records of the days of the kings of Israel? And Joram son of Ahab reigned over Israel in Samaria twelve years, in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah. And he did what was evil before the Lord, yet not like his brothers nor like his mother; and he sent away the pillars of Baal that his father had made and shattered them; yet he clung to the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who caused Israel to sin; he did not turn away from them. And the Lord became angry with wrath against the house of Ahab.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Greek renders the deity's name literally as "Baal the fly" (translating Hebrew zebub, "fly," and shifting from the masculine article in 1:2-3 to the feminine article in 1:6, 16), and adds the final clause "And they went to inquire through him."
- **1:3** — Greek reads "And it is not so" (and "Not so!" in 1:6, 16), reflecting Hebrew lo-ken ("not so") where the Masoretic Text reads laken ("therefore").
- **1:7** — Greek krisis (literally "judgment") renders Hebrew mishpat in the sense of "manner, bearing, or appearance."
- **1:18** — The Septuagint places Joram's accession summary here (instead of the Masoretic synchronism in 1:17), reading "his brothers" rather than "his father" (compare 3:2) and adding that he shattered Baal's pillars and that the Lord became angry against the house of Ahab.
