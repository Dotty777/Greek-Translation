## Text

**51:1** A prayer of Jesus son of Sirach. I will give thanks to You, Lord, King, and I will praise You, God my Savior; I give thanks to Your name,

**51:2** for You became a protector and helper to me, and You redeemed my body from destruction and from the snare of a tongue's slander, from lips that work falsehood, and before those standing by, You became a helper,

**51:3** and You redeemed me, according to the abundance of Your mercy and name, from gnashing jaws ready for food, from the hand of those seeking my life, from the many afflictions that I had,

**51:4** from the suffocation of a blaze all around, and from the midst of a fire that I did not kindle,

**51:5** from the depth of the belly of Hades, and from an unclean tongue and a false word.

**51:6** To a king, the slander of an unrighteous tongue; my soul praised to the point of death, and my life was very near Hades below.

**51:7** They surrounded me on every side, and there was no one helping; I was looking for the assistance of men, and there was none.

**51:8** And I remembered Your mercy, Lord, and Your working from eternity, that You will rescue those who wait for You, and You save them from the hand of the nations.

**51:9** And I lifted up my supplication on the earth, and I pleaded for deliverance from death.

**51:10** I called upon the Lord, the Father of my lord, not to forsake me in days of affliction, in a time of arrogant acts, of helplessness.

**51:11** I will praise Your name continually, and I will sing hymns with thanksgiving; and my plea was heard.

**51:12** For You saved me from destruction, and You rescued me from an evil time; therefore I will give thanks and praise You, and I will bless the name of the Lord.

**51:13** While I was still young, before I wandered, I sought wisdom openly in my prayer.

**51:14** Before the temple I asked for her, and to the very end I will seek her out.

**51:15** From the blossom, as the grape ripens, my heart rejoiced in her; my foot stepped in uprightness; from my youth I tracked her.

**51:16** I inclined my ear a little and received her, and I found for myself much instruction.

**51:17** Progress came to me in her; to Him who gives me wisdom I will give glory.

**51:18** For I resolved to put her into practice, and I was zealous for the good, and I will never be ashamed.

**51:19** My soul has wrestled in her, and in the practice of hunger I was strict; I stretched out my hands toward the height, and I mourned the acts of ignorance concerning her.

**51:20** I directed my soul toward her; I acquired a heart with them from the beginning, and in purification I found her; therefore I will never be forsaken.

**51:21** And my innermost being was stirred to seek her out; therefore I acquired a good possession.

**51:22** The Lord gave me a tongue as my reward, and with it I will praise Him.

**51:23** Draw near to me, you uninstructed, and lodge in the house of instruction.

**51:24** And because you say that you are lacking in these things, and your souls thirst greatly,

**51:25** I opened my mouth and spoke: "Acquire for yourselves without silver;

**51:26** put your neck under a yoke, and let your soul receive instruction; she is near to be found.

**51:27** See with your eyes that I labored a little, and I found for myself much rest.

**51:28** Share in instruction for a great sum of silver, and acquire much gold through her.

**51:29** May your soul rejoice in His mercy, and may you not be ashamed in His praise.

**51:30** Do your work before the time, and He will give your reward in His time."

## Translator Notes

- **51:3** — The Greek literally has the nominative singular ἕτοιμος εἰς βρῶμα ("ready for food"), which may refer either to the speaker as ready to become prey or, by grammatical discord (where other witnesses read the genitive plural ἑτοίμων), to gnashing jaws ready to devour.
- **51:6** — The verbless opening phrase ("To a king, the slander of an unrighteous tongue") is sometimes joined to the end of verse 5. The verb "praised" translates the Greek reading ᾔνεσεν found in major uncial witnesses, whereas other manuscripts and the Hebrew parallel reflect ἤγγισεν ("drew near").
- **51:10** — The Greek phrase 'the Lord, the Father of my lord' is an unusual address whose wording is debated.
- **51:11** — Retained in its adopted Saints Greek Critical Text position between 51:10 and 51:12. In the Saints.Bible numbering, 51:11 spans the end of 51:10 ('I will praise...') and the opening clause of 51:11 ('and my plea was heard'), while 51:12 spans the remainder of 51:11 through 51:12.
- **51:19** — "In the practice of hunger" translates the Greek reading λιμοῦ ("hunger/famine") found in early uncial manuscripts, whereas other Greek witnesses read νόμου ("of the law").
- **51:20** — The Greek text places this clause before "and in purification I found her" and reads the plural pronoun μετ᾽ αὐτῶν ("with them"), whereas other witnesses invert the clauses and read the singular μετ᾽ αὐτῆς ("with her").
