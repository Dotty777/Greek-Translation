## Text

**19:1** A drunken laborer will not become rich; the one who despises small things will fall little by little.

**19:2** Wine and women will lead astray those with understanding, and the one who clings to prostitutes will be more reckless.

**19:3** Decay and worms will inherit him, and a reckless soul will be taken away.

**19:4** The one who trusts quickly is flighty in heart, and the one who sins will wrong his own soul.

**19:5** The one who delights in heart will be condemned,

**19:6** and the one who hates chatter has less evil.

**19:7** Never repeat a word, and nothing at all will be diminished for you.

**19:8** To friend and enemy do not tell it, and unless it is a sin for you, do not reveal it.

**19:9** For he has heard you and guarded himself against you, and in time he will hate you.

**19:10** Have you heard a word? Let it die with you. Take courage, it will certainly not burst you.

**19:11** Because of a word a fool will suffer labor pains, as a woman giving birth does because of a baby.

**19:12** An arrow lodged in a thigh of flesh; so is a word in the belly of a fool.

**19:13** Reprove a friend; perhaps he did not do it, and if he did anything, so that he will not do it again.

**19:14** Reprove your friend; perhaps he did not say it, and if he has said it, so that he will not repeat it.

**19:15** Reprove a friend, for slander often occurs, and do not believe every word.

**19:16** There is one who slips, and not from the soul; and who has not sinned with his tongue?

**19:17** Reprove your neighbor before threatening, and give place to the law of the Most High.

**19:20** All wisdom is the fear of the Lord, and in all wisdom is the doing of the law.

**19:22** And the knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, and nowhere is the counsel of sinners prudence.

**19:23** There is wickedness, and it is an abomination, and there is a foolish person lacking in wisdom.

**19:24** Better is a reverent person who falls short in understanding than one who abounds in prudence and transgresses the law.

**19:25** There is a precise cleverness, and it is unjust, and there is one who perverts favor to bring forth a judgment.

**19:26** There is one who acts wickedly, bowed down in gloom, and what is within him is full of deceit.

**19:27** Bowing down his face and pretending to be deaf, where he has not been recognized he will take you by surprise.

**19:28** And if by a lack of strength he is prevented from sinning, if he finds an opportunity, he will do evil.

**19:29** From appearance a man will be recognized, and from the meeting of the face a perceptive person will be recognized.

**19:30** The attire of a man and the laughter of the teeth and the steps of a person declare the things concerning him.

## Translator Notes

- **19:3** — The critical Greek text reads sepe ('decay, rot'), whereas later manuscripts read setes ('moths').
- **19:5** — The critical Greek text reads kardia ('in heart'), while some later manuscripts read kakia ('in wickedness').
- **19:13** — Greek elenxon can mean 'reprove,' 'confront,' or 'question,' alluding to Leviticus 19:17 (cf. v. 17).
- **19:24** — Greek emphobos literally means 'in fear' or 'filled with fear,' referring to the fear of the Lord (v. 20).
- **19:25** — Greek diastrephon charin tou ekphanai krima literally means 'perverting favor to bring forth a judgment,' describing the abuse of favors or kindnesses to obtain a desired legal verdict.
