## Text

**9:1** Do not be jealous of the wife of your bosom, and do not teach an evil lesson against yourself.

**9:2** Do not give your soul to a woman, so that she may tread upon your strength.

**9:3** Do not go to meet a woman who acts as a courtesan, so that you may not fall into her snares.

**9:4** Do not keep constant company with a female musician, so that you may not be caught in her schemes.

**9:5** Do not look intently at a virgin, so that you may not be caused to stumble by her penalties.

**9:6** Do not give your soul to prostitutes, so that you will not lose your inheritance.

**9:7** Do not look around in the streets of a city, and do not wander about in its deserted places.

**9:8** Turn your eye away from a shapely woman, and do not look intently at beauty belonging to another; by the beauty of a woman many have been led astray, and from this, love is kindled like fire.

**9:9** Do not sit at all with a married woman, and do not carouse with her in wine, so that your soul may not turn aside to her and you may not slip in your spirit into destruction.

**9:10** Do not forsake an old friend, for a recent one is not equal to him. A new friend is new wine; when it has grown old, you will drink it with gladness.

**9:11** Do not envy the glory of a sinner, for you do not know what his overthrow will be.

**9:12** Do not take pleasure in the pleasure of the ungodly; remember that all the way to Hades they will by no means be justified.

**9:13** Keep far away from a man who has authority to kill, and you will by no means dread the fear of death. And even if you approach, do not commit an offense, so that he will not take away your life. Recognize that you are passing through the midst of snares and walking upon the battlements of cities.

**9:14** According to your strength, assess your neighbors, and take counsel with the wise.

**9:15** And let your discussion be with people of understanding, and all your discourse in the law of the Most High.

**9:16** Let righteous men be your dinner companions, and let your boast be in the fear of the Lord.

**9:17** By the hand of artisans a work will be praised, and the leader of a people is wise in his speech.

**9:18** A loose-tongued man is dreaded in his city, and the one who is rash in his speech will be hated.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — "Her penalties" translates the Greek tois epitimiois autes, referring to the legal fines or penalties incurred for wronging a virgin (cf. Exod 22:16-17; Deut 22:28-29).
- **9:9** — The Greek reads "in your spirit" (to pneumati sou), whereas the surviving Hebrew manuscript tradition reads "in blood," reflecting a textual divergence between the Greek and Hebrew witnesses.
- **9:14** — The verb "assess" renders Greek stochasai (literally "aim at," "gauge," or "estimate"), advising careful discernment of those around one.
