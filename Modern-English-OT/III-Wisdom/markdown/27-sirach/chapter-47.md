## Text

**47:1** And after him Nathan arose to prophesy in the days of David.

**47:2** Just as the fat set apart from the peace offering, so was David from the sons of Israel.

**47:3** With lions he played as with young goats, and with bears as with lambs of the sheep.

**47:4** In his youth, did he not kill a giant, and remove reproach from the people by raising his hand with a slingstone and bringing down the arrogance of Goliath?

**47:5** For he called upon the Lord, the Most High, and He gave might in his right hand to remove a man in war; He will lift up the horn of His people.

**47:6** So He glorified him for ten thousands, and praised him with the blessings of the Lord, when a diadem of glory was brought to him.

**47:7** For he wiped out enemies all around, and he brought to nothing the Philistines, the adversaries; to this day he shattered their horn.

**47:8** In every work of his he gave thanksgiving to the Holy One, the Most High, with a word of glory; with all his heart he sang hymns and loved the One who made him.

**47:9** And he stationed psalm-singers in front of the altar, and by their sound to make melodies sweet.

**47:10** He gave beauty to the festivals, and he adorned the seasons to completion, while they praised His holy name and from morning the sanctuary resounded.

**47:11** The Lord took away his sins, and lifted up his horn forever, and gave him a covenant of kings and a throne of glory in Israel.

**47:12** After him a knowledgeable son arose, and because of him he dwelt in spaciousness.

**47:13** Solomon reigned in days of peace, to whom God gave rest all around, so that he might establish a house for His name and prepare a sanctuary forever.

**47:14** How wise you became in your youth, and you were filled like a river with understanding!

**47:15** Your soul covered the earth, and you filled it with parables of riddles.

**47:16** To distant islands your name reached, and you were loved in your peace.

**47:17** For songs and proverbs and parables and for interpretation, lands marveled at you.

**47:18** In the name of the Lord God, who is called the God of Israel, you gathered gold like tin, and like lead you multiplied silver.

**47:19** You laid your loins beside women, and you were brought under dominion in your body.

**47:20** You put a blemish on your glory, and you profaned your seed, so as to bring wrath upon your children, and I was grieved over your folly,

**47:21** that sovereignty became split in two, and a disobedient kingdom ruled out of Ephraim.

**47:22** But the Lord will never forsake His mercy, and nothing of His works will ever perish; nor will He ever blot out the descendants of His chosen one, and the seed of the one who loved Him He will never remove. And to Jacob He gave a remnant, and to David a root from himself.

**47:23** And Solomon rested with the fathers, and he left behind after him from his seed the folly of the people and one lacking in understanding, Rehoboam, who drove the people away by his counsel, and Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin and gave to Ephraim a way of sin.

**47:24** And their sins were greatly multiplied, so as to drive them away from their land;

**47:25** and they sought out every wickedness, until vengeance came upon them.

## Translator Notes

- **47:5** — The critical text omits δυνατόν ("mighty") after ἄνθρωπον ("a man") and reads the future indicative ἀνυψώσει ("He will lift up") where other witnesses read the infinitive ἀνυψῶσαι ("to lift up").
- **47:6** — The critical text has singular verbs ἐδόξασεν and ᾔνεσεν ("He glorified... praised"), whereas other witnesses read plural ("they glorified... praised," echoing 1 Sam 18:7).
- **47:20** — The critical text reads the first-person singular κατενύγην ("I was grieved"), where other manuscripts read the infinitive κατανυγῆναι ("so that grief came").
- **47:22** — Greek reads ἀπὸ τῶν ἔργων αὐτοῦ ("of His works"), whereas the surviving Hebrew manuscript reads "of His words."
