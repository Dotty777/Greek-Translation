## Text

**49:1** The memorial of Josiah is like a blending of incense, prepared by the work of a perfumer; in every mouth it will be sweet like honey, and like music at a banquet of wine.

**49:2** He himself was directed rightly in the turning back of the people, and he removed the abominations of lawlessness.

**49:3** He directed his heart toward the Lord; in the days of lawless men he strengthened godliness.

**49:4** Apart from David and Hezekiah and Josiah, all of them committed trespass; for they forsook the law of the Most High; the kings of Judah came to an end.

**49:5** For they gave their horn to others and their glory to a foreign nation;

**49:6** it burned the chosen city of the sanctuary, and they made its streets desolate by the hand of Jeremiah.

**49:7** For they afflicted him, yet he was consecrated as a prophet in the womb, to uproot and to afflict and to destroy, likewise to build and to plant.

**49:8** It was Ezekiel who saw a vision of glory, which He showed him upon a chariot of cherubim;

**49:9** for he also remembered the enemies in a rainstorm, and to do good to those who direct their ways straight.

**49:10** And may the bones of the twelve prophets flourish again from their place. Now He comforted Jacob and redeemed them in faithfulness of hope.

**49:11** How are we to magnify Zerubbabel? And he himself is like a signet ring on a right hand;

**49:12** so also is Joshua son of Jozadak, who in their days built a house and exalted a holy people to the Lord, prepared for eternal glory.

**49:13** And they maintain for a long time the memorial of the one who raised up for us fallen walls, and set up gates and bars, and rebuilt our ruins.

**49:14** Not even one was created on the earth such a one as Enoch, for he himself was taken up from the earth.

**49:15** And Joseph was born a man, a leader of brothers, a support of the people, and his bones were cared for.

**49:16** Shem and Seth were glorified among people, and above every living creature in creation was Adam.

## Translator Notes

- **49:9** — The Greek syntax is difficult, joining an infinitive ('to do good') to 'remembered,' and diverges sharply from the Hebrew Ben Sira manuscript, which states that Ezekiel made mention of Job.
- **49:10** — This Greek text reads singular verbs ('He comforted... redeemed'), likely referring to God (cf. 48:20), whereas other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read plural verbs referring to the twelve prophets.
- **49:12** — This Greek text reads 'a holy people' (λαὸν ἅγιον), whereas other witnesses and the Hebrew read 'a holy temple' (ναὸν ἅγιον).
- **49:13** — This Greek text reads the verb 'they maintain' (νέμουσιν) where other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew read the proper name 'Nehemiah' (Νεεμίου).
- **49:15** — This Greek text reads 'And Joseph' (ὁ δὲ Ἰωσήφ), whereas other Greek witnesses read 'Neither was a man born like Joseph' (οὐδὲ ὡς Ἰωσήφ).
