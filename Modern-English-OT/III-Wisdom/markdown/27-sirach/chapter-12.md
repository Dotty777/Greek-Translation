## Text

**12:1** If you do good, know to whom you are doing it, and there will be gratitude for your good deeds.

**12:2** Do good to a godly person, and you will find a repayment, and if not from him, yet from the Most High.

**12:3** There are no good things for the one who persists in evil and for the one who does not freely give charity.

**12:4** Give to the godly person, and do not help the sinner.

**12:5** Do good to the humble person and do not give to an ungodly person; hold back his bread and do not give it to him, so that he may not overpower you through it; for you will find double evil for all the good things you may do for him.

**12:6** For the Most High also hates sinners, and He will repay vengeance to the ungodly.

**12:7** Give to the good person, and do not help the sinner.

**12:8** A friend will not be vindicated in good times, and an enemy will not be hidden in bad times.

**12:9** In a man's good times his enemies are in sorrow, and in his bad times even his friend will separate himself.

**12:10** Never trust your enemy; for just as bronze corrodes, so is his wickedness.

**12:11** Even if he humbles himself and walks bowed down, keep your soul alert and guard yourself from him; and you will be to him like one who has wiped a mirror clean, and you will know that it has not corroded to the end.

**12:12** Do not set him beside yourself, so that he may not overthrow you and stand in your place; do not seat him at your right hand, so that he may not seek your seat, and in the end you will recognize my words, and at my sayings you will be stung with remorse.

**12:13** Who will pity a charmer bitten by a snake, and all who approach wild beasts?

**12:14** So it is with the one who associates with a sinful man and becomes entangled in his sins.

**12:15** For an hour he will remain with you, and if you give way, he will by no means stand firm.

**12:16** And with his lips the enemy will speak sweetly, yet in his heart he will plot to overthrow you into a pit; with his eyes the enemy will weep, yet if he finds an opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.

**12:17** If bad things meet you, you will find him there before you, and as though helping, he will trip up your heel.

**12:18** He will shake his head and clap his hands, and whisper many things and change his face.

## Translator Notes

- **12:8** — The Greek text reads ekdikethesetai ("will be vindicated"), the reading of the major uncial manuscripts; some modern editors conjecture ekgnosthesetai ("will be known") to align with the Hebrew.
- **12:11** — The Greek clause "it has not corroded to the end" (ouk eis telos katiosen) implies that wiping a bronze mirror never removes its tendency to corrode permanently; the enemy's malice is never finished for good.
