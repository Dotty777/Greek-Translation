## Text

**48:1** And Elijah arose, a prophet like fire, and his word burned like a torch.

**48:2** He brought a famine upon them, and by his zeal he made them few.

**48:3** By the word of the Lord he held back heaven; thus he brought down fire three times.

**48:4** How you were glorified, Elijah, in your wondrous deeds! And who is like you to boast?

**48:5** He who raised a dead person from death and from Hades by the word of the Most High;

**48:6** he who brought down kings to destruction and honored men from their bed;

**48:7** hearing rebuke at Sinai and judgments of vengeance at Horeb;

**48:8** he who anointed kings for retribution, and prophets as successors after him;

**48:9** he who was taken up in a whirlwind of fire, in a chariot of fiery horses;

**48:10** he who was recorded in rebukes for appointed times, to calm wrath before fury, and to turn the heart of a father to a son, and to restore the tribes of Jacob.

**48:11** Blessed are those who saw you and those who have been adorned in love, for we also will surely live.

**48:12** It was Elijah who was covered in a whirlwind, and Elisha was filled with his spirit; and in his days he was not shaken by a ruler, and no one overpowered him.

**48:13** No matter surpassed him, and in sleep his body prophesied;

**48:14** and in his life he performed wonders, and in death his works were wondrous.

**48:15** In all these things the people did not repent, and they did not turn away from their sins, until they were carried off as plunder from their land and were scattered in all the earth; and the people were left very few, and a ruler for the house of David.

**48:16** Some of them did what was pleasing, while others multiplied sins.

**48:17** Hezekiah fortified his city and brought Gog into their midst; he dug through hard rock with iron and built cisterns for water.

**48:18** In his days Sennacherib came up and sent Rabshakeh, and he departed; and his hand rose up against Zion, and he boasted greatly in his arrogance.

**48:19** Then their hearts and hands were shaken, and they suffered labor pains like women giving birth;

**48:20** and they called upon the merciful Lord, stretching out their hands to Him. And the Holy One from heaven quickly heard them and redeemed them by the hand of Isaiah.

**48:21** He struck the camp of the Assyrians, and His angel wiped them out.

**48:22** For Hezekiah did what was pleasing to the Lord and held strong in the ways of David his father, which Isaiah the prophet commanded, great and faithful in his vision.

**48:23** In his days the sun went backward, and he added life to the king.

**48:24** By a great spirit he saw the last things and comforted those who mourned in Zion.

**48:25** He showed what was to come until the end of the age, and the hidden things before they came about.

## Translator Notes

- **48:11** — This Greek text reads 'adorned' (κεκοσμημένοι), whereas some witnesses read 'fallen asleep' (κεκοιμημένοι). 'Will surely live' renders a Hebraic emphatic idiom literally meaning 'will live with life.'
- **48:17** — The Greek text reads the proper name 'Gog' (τὸν Γώγ), widely understood as an ancient scribal corruption for the spring 'Gihon' (cf. 2 Chr 32:30), whose waters Hezekiah channeled into Jerusalem.
- **48:18** — The Greek text places 'his hand' in the nominative case as subject ('his hand rose up'), whereas some manuscripts read the accusative ('he lifted up his hand').
