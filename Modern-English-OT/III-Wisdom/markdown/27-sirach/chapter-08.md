## Text

**8:1** Do not contend with a powerful man, or else you may fall into his hands.

**8:2** Do not quarrel with a rich man, or else he may overbalance your weight; for gold has destroyed many and has turned aside the hearts of kings.

**8:3** Do not contend with a talkative man, and do not pile wood upon his fire.

**8:4** Do not jest with an undisciplined person, so that your ancestors may not be dishonored.

**8:5** Do not reproach a man who is turning away from sin; remember that we are all under penalties.

**8:6** Do not dishonor a man in his old age, for some of us are also growing old.

**8:7** Do not gloat over a dead person; remember that we all die.

**8:8** Do not neglect the discourse of the wise, and be conversant with their proverbs, for from them you will learn discipline and how to serve great men.

**8:9** Do not miss the discourse of the aged, for they themselves also learned from their fathers; for from them you will learn understanding and how to give an answer in a time of need.

**8:10** Do not kindle the coals of a sinner, or else you may be burned in the fire of his flame.

**8:11** Do not rise up before an insolent man, so that he may not lie in wait as an ambush for your mouth.

**8:12** Do not lend to a man stronger than you, and if you do lend, be as one who has lost it.

**8:13** Do not give surety beyond your means, and if you do give surety, take thought as one who will pay.

**8:14** Do not go to court with a judge, for they will judge in his favor according to his honor.

**8:15** Do not travel on the road with a reckless man, so that he may not weigh heavily against you; for he himself will act according to his own will, and through his folly you will perish with him.

**8:16** Do not start a fight with a hot-tempered man, and do not travel through the wilderness with him; for blood is as nothing in his eyes, and where there is no help, he will strike you down.

**8:17** Do not take counsel with a fool, for he will not be able to keep a word secret.

**8:18** Before a stranger do not do anything secret, for you do not know what he will bring forth.

**8:19** Do not reveal your heart to every person, and do not let him return a favor to you.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — "Overbalance your weight" renders balance-scale imagery in Greek (antistese sou ten holken, literally "weigh against your weight"), pointing to how a wealthy opponent can tip the scales through money.
- **8:11** — "Rise up before" literally translates Greek exanastes, cautioning against being provoked into confronting an insolent person, who waits in ambush to trap one's words.
- **8:14** — Greek doxa here denotes the judge's honor or prestige, though the term can also carry the sense of "opinion" or "judgment."
- **8:19** — The second clause translates the Greek literally ("do not let him return a favor to you"), warning against putting oneself in an untrustworthy person's debt or ironically noting that no kindness will be repaid; the surviving Hebrew text differs here.
