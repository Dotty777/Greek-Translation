## Text

**44:1** Hymn of the fathers. Let us now praise glorious men and our fathers by descent.

**44:2** The Lord created much glory, His majesty from eternity.

**44:3** Those ruling in their kingdoms, and men renowned in power; they will take counsel in their understanding, having declared in prophecies;

**44:4** leaders of the people in counsels and in understanding of the learning of the people, wise words in their instruction;

**44:5** those seeking out melodies of music, recounting verses in writing;

**44:6** wealthy men furnished with strength, living in peace in their dwellings.

**44:7** All these were glorified among generations, and in their days they were a boast.

**44:8** There are some of them who left behind a name for declaring praises,

**44:9** and there are some of whom there is no memorial, and they perished as though they had not existed, and became as though they had never been, and their children after them.

**44:10** But rather, these were men of mercy, whose righteous deeds were not forgotten.

**44:11** With their offspring a good inheritance will endure, their descendants. In the covenants

**44:12** their offspring stood firm, and their children because of them.

**44:13** Forever their offspring will remain, and their glory will not be blotted out.

**44:14** Their bodies were buried in peace, and their name lives for generations.

**44:15** Peoples will recount their wisdom, and the assembly declares their praise.

**44:16** Enoch pleased the Lord and was taken up, an example of repentance to the generations.

**44:17** Noah was found perfect, righteous; in a time of wrath he became a replacement; therefore there was a remnant for the earth, therefore a flood came.

**44:18** Everlasting covenants were made with him, so that all flesh would not be blotted out by a flood.

**44:19** Abraham was a great father of a multitude of nations, and no one was found like him in glory,

**44:20** who kept the law of the Most High, and entered into a covenant with Him, and in his flesh he established a covenant, and in testing he was found faithful.

**44:21** Therefore by an oath He established for him that nations would be blessed in his offspring, to multiply him like the dust of the earth, and like the stars to exalt his offspring, and to cause them to inherit from sea to sea and from the river to the end of the earth.

**44:22** And in Isaac He likewise established, because of Abraham his father, the blessing of all people and the covenant,

**44:23** and caused them to rest upon the head of Jacob; He acknowledged him in His blessings, and gave it to him as an inheritance; and He divided his portions; among twelve tribes He distributed them.

## Translator Notes

- **44:3** — The Greek text uses the future indicative "they will take counsel" (bouleusontai), whereas other witnesses read a participle ("giving counsel").
- **44:4** — The Greek text reads the genitive noun "of learning" (grammateias), whereas some manuscripts read the plural noun "scribes" (grammateis).
- **44:11** — The phrase "In the covenants" stands at the end of verse 11 in the Greek text and completes its clause at the start of verse 12.
- **44:17** — The Greek text repeats "therefore" (dia touto) before "a flood came," whereas other witnesses read "when" (hote); "replacement" translates antallagma (literally "exchange" or "substitute").
