## Text

**3:1** Listen to me, your father, children, and act in this way, so that you may be saved.

**3:2** For the Lord glorified a father over children, and confirmed the judgment of a mother over sons.

**3:3** The one who honors a father will atone for sins;

**3:4** and like one who stores up treasure is the one who glorifies his mother.

**3:5** The one who honors a father will be gladdened by children, and like one who stores up treasure is the one who glorifies his mother; and in the day of his prayer he will be heard.

**3:6** The one who glorifies a father will live a long life, and the one who listens to the Lord will give rest to his mother,

**3:7** and as to masters, he will serve those who brought him forth.

**3:8** In deed and word honor your father, so that a blessing from him may come upon you;

**3:9** for the blessing of a father establishes the houses of children, but the curse of a mother uproots foundations.

**3:10** Do not glorify yourself in the dishonor of your father, for there is no glory for you with respect to dishonor;

**3:11** for the glory of a person is from the honor of his father, and a mother in disgrace is a reproach to children.

**3:12** Child, help your father in his old age, and do not grieve him in his life;

**3:13** and even if he fails in understanding, have forbearance, and do not dishonor him in all your strength.

**3:14** For mercy toward a father will not be forgotten, and in place of sins it will be built up for you;

**3:15** in the day of your affliction you will be remembered; as fair weather upon frost, so your sins will dissolve.

**3:16** Like a blasphemer is the one who forsakes a father, and cursed by the Lord is the one who provokes his mother to anger.

**3:17** Child, conduct your works in gentleness, and you will be loved by an acceptable person.

**3:18** The greater you are, the more humble yourself, and before the Lord you will find favor;

**3:20** because great is the might of the Lord, and He is glorified by the humble.

**3:21** Do not seek things too difficult for you, and do not investigate things too strong for you.

**3:22** Reflect on the things that have been commanded to you; for you have no need of the hidden things.

**3:23** Do not meddle in what is beyond your works; for more than human understanding has been shown to you.

**3:24** For their supposition has led many astray, and evil conjecture has caused their minds to slip.

**3:26** And the one who loves danger will fall into it; a hard heart will suffer harm in the end, a hard heart will be weighed down in the end.

**3:27** A hard heart will be weighed down with troubles, and the sinner will add sin upon sins.

**3:28** For the calamity of an arrogant person there is no healing, for a plant of wickedness has taken root in him.

**3:29** The heart of an understanding person will reflect on a proverb, and the ear of a listener is the desire of a wise person.

**3:30** Water will extinguish a blazing fire, and mercy will atone for sins.

**3:31** The one who repays favors is mindful of what comes afterward, and in a time of downfall he will find support.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — The Greek text here repeats the clause from v. 4 ("and like one who stores up treasure is the one who glorifies his mother") before concluding with the promise of answered prayer.
- **3:10** — The Greek text reads "with respect to dishonor" (πρὸς ἀτιμίαν); many other Greek witnesses read "a father's dishonor" (πατρὸς ἀτιμία), yielding "for a father's dishonor is not glory to you."
- **3:14** — Greek ἐλεημοσύνη ("mercy," also in v. 30) denotes compassionate aid or charitable kindness; the rare verb προσανοικοδομηθήσεται ("will be built up") carries the sense of being built up additionally or credited to one's account.
- **3:26** — The Greek text places the clause on loving danger first with "will fall into it" (ἐμπεσεῖται, whereas other witnesses read ἀπολεῖται, "will perish"), followed by two parallel clauses on a hard heart (the second echoing v. 27).
