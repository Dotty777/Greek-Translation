## Text

**32:1** Have they appointed you leader? Do not exalt yourself; be among them as one of them. Take care of them, and then sit down.

**32:2** And when you have done all your duty, recline, so that you may be glad because of them and receive a wreath on account of good order.

**32:3** Speak, older man, for it is fitting for you, with accurate knowledge, and do not hinder the music.

**32:4** Where there is a performance, do not pour out talk, and do not display wisdom at the wrong time.

**32:5** A signet of ruby on a gold ornament; such is a concert of music at a banquet of wine.

**32:6** In a gold setting, a signet of emerald; such is a melody of music with sweet wine.

**32:7** Speak, young man, if there is need of you, scarcely twice if you are asked.

**32:8** Sum up your speech, many things in few words; be as one who knows and at the same time keeps silent.

**32:9** In the midst of great men do not make yourself an equal, and when another is speaking do not chatter much.

**32:10** Before thunder lightning speeds ahead, and before a modest person favor will go ahead.

**32:11** Rise up at the right time and do not lag behind; hurry off home and do not loiter.

**32:12** There amuse yourself and do what you have in mind, and do not sin with an arrogant word.

**32:13** And for these things bless the One who made you and fills you to overflowing from His good things.

**32:14** The one who fears the Lord will accept discipline, and those who rise early will find favor.

**32:15** The one who seeks the law will be filled with it, and the one who acts hypocritically will stumble over it.

**32:16** Those who fear the Lord will find justice, and they will kindle righteous deeds like light.

**32:17** A sinful man will turn aside from reproof, and according to his will he will find a ruling.

**32:18** A man of counsel will by no means overlook a thought; a stranger and an arrogant man will not cower at fear, even after acting by himself without counsel.

**32:19** Without counsel do nothing, and when you have acted, do not feel regret.

**32:20** Do not walk on a road of obstacles, and do not stumble in stony places.

**32:21** Do not trust in an unobstructed road,

**32:22** and guard yourself from your children.

**32:23** In every work trust your soul, for indeed this is the keeping of the commandments.

**32:24** The one who trusts the law heeds the commandments, and the one who relies on the Lord will not suffer loss.

## Translator Notes

- **32:7** — The Greek word order literally reads 'scarcely twice if you are asked,' which can mean speaking at most twice when questioned or speaking reluctantly even if asked twice.
- **32:14** — Greek paidian here is a manuscript spelling variant of paideian ('discipline' or 'instruction'). 'Those who rise early' is a Septuagint idiom for seeking the Lord diligently.
- **32:17** — Greek synkrima means a comparison, interpretation, or ruling, here referring to a precedent or pretext suited to the sinner's own will.
- **32:18** — The Greek text reads allotrios ('stranger' or 'outsider') and ends with an obscure clause literally meaning 'even after acting with him/himself without counsel,' where autou is taken reflexively (compare 33:12).
