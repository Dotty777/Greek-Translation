## Text

**36:1** Have mercy on us, Master, the God of all, and look, and cast the fear of You upon all the nations.

**36:2** Lift up Your hand against foreign nations, and let them see Your might.

**36:3** Just as in their sight You were shown holy in us, so in our sight may You be magnified in them.

**36:4** And let them know You, just as we also have come to know that there is no God besides You, Lord.

**36:5** Renew signs and make other wonders;

**36:6** glorify Your hand and right arm; rouse fury and pour out wrath; remove the adversary and wipe out the enemy.

**36:7** Hasten the time and remember the oath, and let them declare Your mighty deeds.

**36:8** In the wrath of fire let the survivor be devoured, and may those who wrong Your people find destruction.

**36:9** Crush the heads of enemy rulers who say, "There is no one besides us."

**36:10** Gather all the tribes of Jacob. And I was the last to keep watch; and You will give them an inheritance, just as from the beginning.

**36:11** Have mercy on the people, Lord, called by Your name, and on Israel, whom You likened to a firstborn.

**36:12** Have compassion on the city of Your sanctuary, Jerusalem, the city of Your rest.

**36:13** Fill Zion with the proclamation of Your wonders, and Your people with Your glory.

**36:14** Give testimony to Your creatures from the beginning, and raise up the prophecies that are in Your name.

**36:15** Give a reward to those who wait for You, and let Your prophets be proved trustworthy.

**36:16** Hear, Lord, the plea of Your suppliants according to the blessing of Aaron concerning Your people, and all those upon the earth will know that You are the Lord, the God of the ages.

**36:18** The stomach will eat any food, yet one food is finer than another.

**36:19** The palate tastes foods of wild game; so an understanding heart tastes false words.

**36:20** A crooked heart will cause grief, and a man of much experience will repay him.

**36:21** A woman will accept any man, yet one daughter is better than another.

**36:22** The beauty of a woman cheers the face and surpasses every desire of a man.

**36:23** If mercy and gentleness are upon her tongue, her husband is not like the sons of men.

**36:24** The one who acquires a wife makes a beginning of property, a helper corresponding to him and a pillar of rest.

**36:25** Where there is no fence, property will be plundered; and where there is no wife, one will groan while wandering.

**36:26** For who will trust a nimble robber straying from city to city? So it is with a man who has no nest and lodges wherever evening overtakes him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — Verse 36:1 combines native Greek manuscript verses 33:1 and 33:2 in the restored Saints.Bible chapter order (see note on 30:25).
- **36:5** — Greek ἀλλοίωσον literally means "make other" or "make different," reflecting a Hebrew verb (shanah) that can mean either "change" or "repeat."
- **36:10** — This verse combines native Greek 33:11 and 36:16 in the restored Saints.Bible order. The clause "And I was the last to keep watch" (Κἀγὼ ἔσχατος ἠγρύπνησα) is a textual dislocation from Sirach 33:16 preserved at the seam of transposed leaves in Greek manuscripts. Also, κατακληρονομήσεις αὐτούς ("give them an inheritance") can alternatively mean "take them as Your inheritance."
- **36:13** — "Proclamation of Your wonders" renders the rare Greek term ἀρεταλογία (recounting of divine mighty deeds). The Greek text reads "Your people" (τὸν λαόν σου), whereas some witnesses read "Your temple" (τὸν ναόν σου).
- **36:18** — Verse 36:17 of the parallel numbering has no separate Greek GI line here; native Greek 36:23 follows 36:22 (v. 16 here) directly with nothing omitted.
- **36:23** — Literally "not according to the sons of men," an idiom meaning that her husband is uniquely fortunate beyond ordinary mortals.
- **36:26** — The Greek text reads σφαλλομένῳ (literally "stumbling" or "straying"), whereas some witnesses and emendations read ἀφαλλομένῳ ("leaping" or "darting").
