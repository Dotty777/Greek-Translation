## Text

**23:1** Lord, Father and Master of my life, do not abandon me to their counsel; do not let me fall by them.

**23:2** Who will set whips over my thought and the discipline of wisdom over my heart, so that they may not spare my errors of ignorance, and it will by no means pass over their sins,

**23:3** so that my errors of ignorance may not multiply and my sins increase, and I fall before the adversaries, and my enemy gloat over me?

**23:4** Lord, Father and God of my life, do not give me haughtiness of eyes,

**23:5** and turn desire away from me.

**23:6** Do not let appetite of the belly and lust overtake me, and do not give me over to a shameless soul.

**23:7** Discipline of the mouth. Listen to the discipline of the mouth, children, and the one who keeps it will by no means be caught; in his lips

**23:8** a sinner and a reviler and an arrogant person will be left behind; they will stumble by them.

**23:9** Do not accustom your mouth to an oath, and do not become accustomed to the naming of the Holy One.

**23:10** For just as a household servant who is examined constantly will not lack a bruise, so also the one who swears and utters the name continually will by no means be cleansed from sin.

**23:11** A man of many oaths will be filled with lawlessness, and a whip will not depart from his house. If he offends, his sin is upon him, and if he disregards it, he has sinned doubly; and if he has sworn in vain, he will not be justified, for his house will be filled with calamities.

**23:12** There is speech matched with death; may it not be found in the inheritance of Jacob. For all these things will depart from the godly, and they will not wallow in sins.

**23:13** Do not accustom your mouth to foul crudeness, for there is a word of sin in it.

**23:14** Remember your father and mother, for you sit in council among great men, so that you may never forget yourself before them and become foolish by your habit, and wish that you had not been born, nor curse the day of your birth.

**23:15** A man accustomed to words of reproach will by no means be disciplined in all his days.

**23:16** Two kinds multiply sins, and the third will bring on wrath: a hot soul, like a burning fire, will by no means be quenched until it is consumed; a sexually immoral man in the body of his flesh will by no means stop until he kindles a fire.

**23:17** To a sexually immoral man all bread is sweet; he will by no means rest until he dies.

**23:18** A man who strays from his bed, saying in his soul, "Who sees me? Darkness is all around me, and the walls cover me, and no one sees me; why should I be afraid? The Most High will by no means remember my sins."

**23:19** And the eyes of people are his fear, and he does not know that the eyes of the Lord are ten thousand times brighter than the sun, looking upon all the ways of people and looking closely into hidden places.

**23:20** Before they were created, all things were known to Him, and so also after their completion.

**23:21** This man will be punished in the streets of the city, and where he did not suspect, he will be seized.

**23:22** So also is a woman who leaves her husband and presents an heir by another man.

**23:23** For first, she disobeyed the law of the Most High, and second, she offended against her own husband, and third, she committed adultery in sexual immorality; she presented children by another man.

**23:24** This woman will be brought out into the assembly, and there will be a reckoning upon her children.

**23:25** Her children will not spread into a root, and her branches will not yield fruit.

**23:26** She will leave her memory for a curse, and her reproach will not be blotted out.

**23:27** And those who are left behind will recognize that nothing is better than the fear of the Lord, and nothing is sweeter than heeding the commandments of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Greek shifts from plural pheisontai ('they may not spare,' whips and discipline) to singular pare (from pariemi, 'it will not pass over,' likely the discipline of wisdom) and 'their sins' (auton, referring to his thought and heart, or the lips and tongue of 22:27-23:1).
- **23:8** — Greek kataleiphthesetai literally means 'will be left behind' (or 'abandoned'); many editors regard it as an early spelling variant for katalemphthesetai ('will be caught/overtaken'), paralleling 'be caught' in 23:7.
- **23:12** — Greek antiperibeblemene is a unique compound (lit. 'clothed opposite to'), meaning 'matched with' or 'comparable to' death, pointing to speech such as blasphemy that carries a death-worthy penalty.
- **23:14** — Greek synedreuei is rendered as second person singular ('you sit in council,' as a middle form or variant for synedreueis), and the Greek text retains a resumptive negative me before katarase ('nor curse').
- **23:16** — The phrase 'in the body of his flesh' (en somati sarkos autou) is literal to the Greek and reflects a Semitic idiom that can denote either misuse of one's own physical body or illicit relations with close kin.
