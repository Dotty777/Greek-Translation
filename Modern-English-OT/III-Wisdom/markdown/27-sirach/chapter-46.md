## Text

**46:1** Mighty in wars was Joshua son of Nun, and the successor of Moses in prophecies, who became according to his name great for the salvation of His chosen ones, to take vengeance on enemies rising up, so that he might give Israel an inheritance.

**46:2** How glorified he was in raising his hands and in turning a sword against cities!

**46:3** Who before him stood like this? For the Lord Himself brought on the enemies.

**46:4** Did not the sun turn back by his hand, and one day become as two?

**46:5** He called upon the Most High, the Mighty One, when enemies pressed him all around; and the great Lord answered them

**46:6** with hailstones of mighty power. He hurled down war upon a nation, and on the descent He destroyed those who resisted, so that the nations might know their full armor, that his war was before the Lord; for indeed he followed after the Mighty One.

**46:7** And in the days of Moses he showed mercy, he and Caleb son of Jephunneh, to stand against an enemy, to hold back the people from sin, and to quiet a grumbling of wickedness.

**46:8** And they, being two, were preserved out of six hundred thousand foot soldiers, to bring them into an inheritance, into a land flowing with milk and honey.

**46:9** And the Lord gave Caleb strength, and until old age it remained with him, so that he went up onto the height of the land, and his seed possessed an inheritance,

**46:10** so that all the sons of Israel might see that it is good to walk after the Lord.

**46:11** And the judges, each by his own name, all whose heart did not prostitute itself and all who did not turn away from the Lord, may their memorial be in blessings.

**46:12** May their bones flourish again from their place, and may their name be renewed upon the sons of their glorified ones.

**46:13** Beloved by his Lord, a prophet of the Lord appointed a king and anointed rulers over His people.

**46:14** In the law of the Lord he judged the assembly, and the Lord visited Jacob.

**46:15** By his faithfulness he was proved a prophet, and he was known by his faithfulness to be faithful in vision.

**46:16** And he called upon the Lord, the Mighty One, when his enemies pressed him all around, with the offering of a nursing lamb.

**46:17** And the Lord thundered from heaven, and with a great sound He made His voice heard,

**46:18** and He wiped out the leaders of the Tyrians and all the rulers of the Philistines.

**46:19** And before the time of eternal sleep he testified before the Lord and the anointed one, "Money, even down to sandals, I have not taken from any flesh." And no one accused him.

**46:20** And after he had fallen asleep he prophesied and showed the king his end, and he lifted up his voice from the earth in prophecy, to blot out the lawlessness of the people.

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — Greek Ἰησοῦς Ναυῆ ("Joshua son of Nun") creates a wordplay between Joshua's name ("the Lord saves") and "salvation" (σωτηρίᾳ).
- **46:3** — The critical text reads τοὺς γὰρ πολεμίους Κύριος αὐτὸς ἐπήγαγεν ("for the Lord Himself brought on the enemies"), whereas other witnesses read "for he himself waged the wars of the Lord."
- **46:7** — Greek reads ἐχθροῦ ("an enemy"), whereas the surviving Hebrew text reads "the assembly/congregation" (alluding to Num 14:6-10).
- **46:13** — The critical text omits the proper name "Samuel" (present in other witnesses), identifying him only by title, and reads βασιλέα ("a king") rather than βασιλείαν ("a kingdom").
- **46:18** — Greek reads Τυρίων ("of the Tyrians"), reflecting a rendering of the Hebrew word for "enemy/foe" as the city name Tyre.
