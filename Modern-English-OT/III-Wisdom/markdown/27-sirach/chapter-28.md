## Text

**28:1** The one who takes vengeance will find vengeance from the Lord, and He will surely hold his sins fast.

**28:2** Forgive your neighbor a wrong, and then, when you pray, your sins will be released.

**28:3** A man harbors anger against another man, and does he seek healing from the Lord?

**28:4** He has no mercy on a man like himself, and does he pray for his own sins?

**28:5** He himself, being flesh, holds onto a grudge; who will make atonement for his sins?

**28:6** Remember the end and stop being hostile; remember decay and death, and remain in the commandments.

**28:7** Remember the commandments and do not hold a grudge against your neighbor, and remember the covenant of the Most High, and overlook ignorance.

**28:8** Keep away from strife, and you will lessen sins; for a wrathful man will kindle strife,

**28:9** and a sinful man will trouble friends, and in the midst of those who are at peace he casts slander.

**28:10** According to the fuel of a fire, so it will blaze up; according to a man's strength his wrath will be, and according to his wealth he will raise up his anger, and according to the strengthening of the strife it will blaze up.

**28:11** A hurried dispute kindles fire, and hasty strife sheds blood.

**28:12** If you blow on a spark, it will blaze up, and if you spit on it, it will be extinguished; and both come out of your mouth.

**28:13** Curse the whisperer and the double-tongued, for they have destroyed many who were at peace.

**28:14** A third tongue has shaken many and driven them from nation to nation; it has torn down fortified cities and overturned the houses of great men.

**28:15** A third tongue has cast out valiant women and deprived them of their labors.

**28:16** The one who pays attention to it will never find rest, nor will he dwell in quietness.

**28:17** The blow of a whip makes welts, but the blow of a tongue will shatter bones.

**28:18** Many have fallen by the edge of the sword, but not as many as those who have fallen because of the tongue.

**28:19** Blessed is the one who has been sheltered from it, who has not passed through its wrath, who has not dragged its yoke, and has not been bound in its bonds.

**28:20** For its yoke is an iron yoke, and its bonds are bronze bonds.

**28:21** Its death is an evil death, and Hades is more profitable than it.

**28:22** It will never have power over the godly, and in its flame they will not be burned.

**28:23** Those who forsake the Lord will fall into it, and it will blaze up in them and will never be extinguished; it will be sent out against them like a lion, and like a leopard it will ravage them.

**28:24** Look, fence your property around with thorns; tie up your silver and gold.

**28:25** And make a balance and a weight for your words, and make a door and a bolt for your mouth.

**28:26** Take care that you do not somehow slip by it, that you do not fall before one who lies in wait.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Greek doubles the verb (διαστηριῶν διαστηρίσει, literally 'firmly setting fast, He will set fast'), a Hebraic intensification meaning that the Lord will strictly retain or hold his sins to account, contrasting with 'released' (literally 'loosed') in 28:2.
- **28:10** — The Greek text places the fourth clause ('and according to the strengthening of the strife it will blaze up') at the end of the verse, whereas some manuscripts and editions transpose it directly after the first clause.
- **28:14** — 'A third tongue' (γλῶσσα τρίτη) is a Semitic idiom for a slanderer or gossip whose words enter as a third party to divide two others.
- **28:18** — 'By the edge of the sword' renders the literal Greek idiom 'by the mouth of the sword' (ἐν στόματι μαχαίρας), forming a wordplay with 'the tongue' in the second clause.
