## Text

**16:1** Do not desire a multitude of useless children, nor rejoice over ungodly sons.

**16:2** If they multiply, do not rejoice over them; if the fear of the Lord is not with them,

**16:3** do not trust in their life, and do not rely on their place; for one is better than a thousand, and to die childless than to have ungodly children.

**16:4** For from one person of understanding a city will be populated, but a tribe of lawless people will be made desolate.

**16:5** Many such things I have seen with my eyes, and mightier things than these my ear has heard.

**16:6** In an assembly of sinners fire will be kindled, and in a disobedient nation wrath was kindled.

**16:7** He did not grant pardon for the ancient giants, who rebelled in their strength;

**16:8** He did not spare the community where Lot sojourned, whom He detested because of their arrogance;

**16:9** He did not have mercy on a nation of destruction, those who were removed in their sins;

**16:10** and likewise six hundred thousand foot soldiers, those who were gathered together in their hardness of heart.

**16:11** Even if there is one stiff-necked person, this is a marvel if he will go unpunished. For mercy and wrath are from Him; He is mighty in pardons and pours out wrath.

**16:12** According to His great mercy, so also great is His reproof; He will judge a man according to his works.

**16:13** A sinner will not escape with plunder, and He will by no means delay the endurance of a godly person.

**16:14** He will make a place for every act of mercy; each one will find according to his works.

**16:17** Do not say, "I will be hidden from the Lord; will anyone from on high remember me? Among a vast people I will by no means be remembered, for what is my soul in an immeasurable creation?"

**16:18** Look, heaven and God's heaven of heaven, the abyss and the earth will be shaken at His visitation;

**16:19** together the mountains and the foundations of the earth quake with trembling when He looks upon them.

**16:20** And on these things no heart will reflect; and who will ponder His ways?

**16:21** And there is a tempest that no one will see, and most of His works are in hidden places.

**16:22** Who will declare works of righteousness? Or who will endure? For the covenant is far away.

**16:23** One lacking in heart thinks these things, and a senseless and straying man thinks foolish things.

**16:24** Listen to me, child, and learn knowledge, and pay attention to my words with your heart.

**16:25** I set forth instruction by weight, and with exactness I declare knowledge.

**16:26** In the judgment of the Lord are His works from the beginning, and from the making of them He separated their portions.

**16:27** He set His works in order forever, and their dominions for their generations; they neither hungered nor grew weary, and they did not cease from their works;

**16:28** not one crowded its neighbor, and forever they will not disobey His word.

**16:29** And after these things the Lord looked upon the earth and filled it with His good things;

**16:30** with the life of every living creature He covered its face, and to it is their return.

## Translator Notes

- **16:18** — The Greek text here includes 'of God' (τοῦ θεοῦ) after 'the heaven of heaven' (as in Codex Vaticanus and Rahlfs), which is omitted in some other Greek witnesses.
- **16:21** — The elliptical Greek opening ('and a tempest that no one will see') differs from the Hebrew of Ben Sira ('if I sin, no eye will see me') and presents God's hidden ways as an unseen storm.
- **16:26** — The Greek text reads 'in the judgment' (ἐν κρίσει), whereas some Greek witnesses read 'in the creation' (ἐν κτίσει), matching the Hebrew ('when God created').
- **16:27** — Greek ἀρχάς can mean either 'dominions' (spheres of rule, as in Genesis 1:16 LXX) or 'beginnings.'
- **16:30** — Literally 'with the soul [or life] of every living creature He covered its face,' using a double accusative construction modeled on the Hebrew idiom.
