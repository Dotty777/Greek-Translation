## Text

**39:1** He will seek out the wisdom of all the ancients, and he will be occupied with prophecies.

**39:2** He will preserve the discourses of renowned men, and he will enter into the subtleties of parables.

**39:3** He will seek out the hidden things of proverbs, and he will be conversant with the riddles of parables.

**39:4** He will serve among great men, and he will appear before a ruler; he will travel through the land of foreign nations, for he has tested good and evil among people.

**39:5** He will devote his heart to rise early to the Lord who made him, and before the Most High he will make supplication; and he will open his mouth in prayer, and for his sins he will make supplication.

**39:6** If the great Lord wills, he will be filled with a spirit of understanding; he himself will pour forth words of wisdom, and in prayer he will give thanks to the Lord.

**39:7** He himself will direct his counsel and knowledge, and he will reflect on His hidden things.

**39:8** He himself displays the instruction of his teaching, and he will boast in the law of the covenant of the Lord.

**39:9** Many will praise his understanding; it will not be blotted out forever. His memorial will not depart, and his name will live for generations of generations.

**39:10** Nations will recount his wisdom, and the assembly will proclaim his praise.

**39:11** If he remains, he will leave a greater name than a thousand; and if he rests, it suffices for him.

**39:12** Yet again, having reflected, I will speak forth, and like a full moon I have been filled.

**39:13** Listen to me, devout sons, and sprout like a rose growing beside a stream of the field,

**39:14** and like frankincense emit a sweet fragrance, and put forth a blossom like a lily; spread fragrance abroad and sing a song of praise. Bless the Lord for all the works,

**39:15** give greatness to His name, and give thanks in His praise, with songs of the lips and with lyres; and in this way you will say in thanksgiving:

**39:16** "All the works of the Lord are indeed very good, and every command will come about in its time.

**39:17** One cannot say, 'What is this? What is this for?' for everything will be sought out in its time. At His word water stood like a heap, and at the utterance of His mouth, reservoirs of waters.

**39:18** At His command is all good pleasure, and there is no one who will diminish His salvation.

**39:19** The works of all flesh are before Him, and it is not possible to be hidden from His eyes.

**39:20** From eternity to eternity He looks on, and nothing is marvelous before Him.

**39:21** One cannot say, 'What is this? What is this for?' for all things have been created for their uses.

**39:22** His blessing covered over like a river, and like a flood it drenched the dry land.

**39:23** So nations will inherit His wrath, just as He turned waters into brine.

**39:24** His ways are straight for the devout; so for the lawless they are stumbling blocks.

**39:25** Good things have been created for the good from the beginning; so evil things for sinners.

**39:26** The chief of every need for human life is water, fire, iron, salt, fine flour, wheat, honey, milk, the blood of the grape, oil, and clothing.

**39:27** All these things are for good to the godly; so to sinners they will be turned to evil.

**39:28** There are spirits that have been created for vengeance, and in their fury they strengthened their scourges; and in the time of consummation they will pour out strength, and they will appease the fury of the One who made them.

**39:29** Fire and hail and famine and death—all these have been created for vengeance.

**39:30** The teeth of wild beasts, and scorpions and vipers, and a sword taking vengeance on the ungodly to destruction—

**39:31** at His commandment they will rejoice, and on the earth they will be prepared for uses, and in their times they will not transgress a word."

**39:32** Therefore from the beginning I was firmly established, and I reflected, and I left it in writing.

**39:33** All the works of the Lord are good, and He will supply every need in its hour.

**39:34** And one cannot say, "This is worse than that," for all things will be approved in due time.

**39:35** And now with all heart and mouth we have sung praise, and bless the name of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **39:7** — The pronouns are grammatically ambiguous; "His hidden things" refers to the mysteries of the Lord (v. 6), while "He himself" and "his counsel" refer to the scribe.
- **39:11** — The Greek comparative "than a thousand" omits "greater," and the verb rendered "suffices for him" means "is enough for him" or "works to his advantage."
- **39:26** — This Greek text reads nominative "wheat" as a separate item after "fine flour," rather than genitive "of wheat" as in some witnesses.
- **39:28** — Or "winds"; Greek pneumata can denote either destructive spirits or storm winds.
- **39:35** — This Greek text reads the first-person plural indicative "we have sung praise," whereas some manuscripts read the second-person plural imperative "sing praise."
