## Text

**35:1** The one who keeps the law multiplies offerings; the one who pays heed to the commandments sacrifices a deliverance offering.

**35:2** One who repays a kindness offers fine flour, and the one who gives alms sacrifices a praise offering.

**35:3** The good pleasure of the Lord is to turn away from wickedness, and atonement is to turn away from injustice.

**35:4** Do not appear before the face of the Lord empty-handed, for all these things are because of the commandment.

**35:5** The offering of a righteous person enriches the altar, and its fragrant aroma is before the Most High.

**35:6** The sacrifice of a righteous man is acceptable, and its memorial will not be forgotten.

**35:7** With a generous eye glorify the Lord, and do not diminish the firstfruits of your hands.

**35:8** In every gift make your face cheerful, and with gladness consecrate a tithe.

**35:9** Give to the Most High according to His gift, and with a generous eye according to what your hand finds.

**35:10** For the Lord is One who repays, and He will repay you sevenfold.

**35:12** Do not offer a bribe, for He will not accept it; and do not rely on an unjust sacrifice, because the Lord is a judge, and with Him there is no partiality.

**35:13** He will not show partiality against a poor person, and He will hear the plea of one who has been wronged.

**35:14** He will never disregard the supplication of an orphan, nor a widow when she pours out her words.

**35:15** Do not the tears of a widow run down her cheek, and is not the outcry against the one who brought them down?

**35:16** One who serves with good pleasure will be accepted, and his plea will reach to the clouds.

**35:17** The prayer of the humble has pierced the clouds, and until it draws near, it will never be consoled; and it will never depart until the Most High visits,

**35:21** and judges righteously and executes judgment. And the Lord will never delay, nor will He be patient with them, until He crushes the loins of the merciless and repays vengeance to the nations; until He removes the multitude of the insolent and crushes the scepters of the unjust;

**35:22** until He repays a man according to his deeds, and the works of men according to their intentions; until He judges the cause of His people and gladdens them with His mercy.

**35:24** Timely is mercy in a time of his distress, like clouds of rain in a time of drought.

## Translator Notes

- **35:1** — "Deliverance offering" translates Greek σωτηρίου, the standard Septuagint term for the Old Testament peace or fellowship offering.
- **35:4** — Verse 35:4 combines native Greek manuscript verses 32:4 and 32:5 in the restored Saints.Bible chapter order (see note on 30:25).
- **35:7** — Literally "with a good eye" (repeated in v. 9), a Hebrew idiom denoting generosity and a willing spirit.
- **35:12** — "Partiality" translates Greek δόξα προσώπου, literally "glory of a face," meaning favoritism based on outward status or appearance. Verse 35:11 of the Saints.Bible parallel numbering has no separate Greek GI line here (nothing is omitted from the Greek text).
- **35:21** — Verses 35:18-20 of the parallel numbering correspond to longer verse divisions in other traditions; this Greek GI verse (native 32:18) continues directly from 35:17 with nothing omitted.
- **35:24** — Greek αὐτοῦ ("his") is masculine singular, referring either to an afflicted person or back to the singular noun "people" (λαός) in v. 22. Verse 35:23 of the parallel numbering has no separate Greek GI line here (native 32:20 follows 32:19 directly).
