## Text

**50:1** It was Simon son of Onias, the high priest, who in his life repaired the house and in his days strengthened the temple;

**50:2** and by him was laid the foundation of the height of the double wall, the high retaining wall of the temple enclosure;

**50:3** in his days a reservoir of water was diminished, bronze like the sea in circumference;

**50:4** he who cared for his people against downfall, and strengthened the city in a siege.

**50:5** How he was glorified in the encircling of the people, on coming out of the house of the curtain:

**50:6** like the morning star in the midst of a cloud, like the full moon in its days;

**50:7** like the sun shining forth upon the temple of the Most High, and like a rainbow gleaming in clouds of glory;

**50:8** like a blossom of roses in the days of spring, like lilies by outlets of water, like a shoot of Lebanon in the days of summer;

**50:9** like fire and frankincense upon a censer, like a vessel of solid hammered gold adorned with every precious stone;

**50:10** like an olive tree putting forth fruit, and like a cypress lifted high in the clouds,

**50:11** when he took up the robe of glory and clothed himself in the perfection of splendor, at the ascent of the holy altar he glorified the enclosure of the sanctuary.

**50:12** And when he received the portions from the hands of the priests, while he himself stood beside the hearth of the altar, all around he himself was a crown of brothers, like a shoot of cedar in Lebanon, and they surrounded him like trunks of palm trees;

**50:13** and all the sons of Aaron were in their glory, and the offering of the Lord was in their hands before the whole assembly of Israel;

**50:14** and serving to completion at the altars, to arrange the offering of the Most High, the Almighty,

**50:15** he stretched out his hand to the libation cup and poured a drink offering from the blood of the grape; he poured out at the foundations of the altar a sweet-smelling aroma to the Most High, the King of all.

**50:16** Then the sons of Aaron shouted aloud; they sounded on hammered trumpets and made a loud sound to be heard for a memorial before the Most High.

**50:17** Then all the people hurried together and fell face down on the ground to worship their Lord, Almighty God, the Most High.

**50:18** And the singers praised with their voices; in the vast house melody was made sweet.

**50:19** And the people implored the Lord, the Most High, in prayer before the Merciful One, until the order of the Lord was completed and they finished His service.

**50:20** Then coming down, he lifted up his hands over the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, to give blessing to the Lord from his lips and to boast in His name.

**50:21** And a second time he bowed in worship to show forth the blessing from the Most High.

**50:22** And now bless God, all of you, who does great things everywhere, who exalts our days from the womb and deals with us according to His mercy.

**50:23** May He grant you gladness of heart, and may there be peace in our days in Israel as in the days of old;

**50:24** may He entrust His mercy to us, and in His days may He redeem us.

**50:25** Two nations my soul loathes, and the third is not a nation:

**50:26** those who dwell on Mount Samaria, the Philistines, and the foolish people who live in Shechem.

**50:27** Instruction of understanding and knowledge I have inscribed in this book, Jesus son of Sirach Eleazar, the Jerusalemite, who poured forth wisdom from his heart.

**50:28** Blessed is the one who will occupy himself with these things, and laying them upon his heart, he will become wise;

**50:29** for if he does them, he will be strong for all things, because the light of the Lord is his path.

## Translator Notes

- **50:3** — The Greek verb literally means 'was diminished' (ἠλαττώθη), where the Hebrew reads 'was dug,' and 'bronze' (χαλκός) alludes to Solomon's bronze sea (1 Kgs 7:23).
- **50:12** — This Greek text reads the nominative 'he himself' (αὐτός), whereas other witnesses read the genitive 'around him' (αὐτοῦ).
- **50:18** — This Greek text reads 'in the vast house' (ἐν πλείστῳ οἴκῳ, the reading of the major Greek uncials), which some editors emend to 'with the greatest sound' (ἐν πλείστῳ ἤχῳ).
- **50:20** — This Greek text reads the dative 'to the Lord' (Κυρίῳ), whereas other manuscripts read the genitive 'of the Lord' (Κυρίου), referring to the priestly blessing.
- **50:21** — This Greek text reads singular 'he bowed a second time' (ἐδευτέρωσεν) and 'to show forth' (ἐπιδείξασθαι), whereas other witnesses read plural 'they bowed a second time' (ἐδευτέρωσαν) and 'to receive' (ἐπιδέξασθαι).
- **50:26** — The Greek text reads 'Mount Samaria' (ὄρει Σαμαρείας), whereas the Hebrew Ben Sira manuscript reads 'Mount Seir' (Edom).
