## Text

**15:1** The one who fears the Lord will do this, and the one who holds fast to the law will obtain her.

**15:2** And she will meet him like a mother, and like a virgin wife she will welcome him.

**15:3** She will feed him bread of understanding, and she will give him water of wisdom to drink.

**15:4** He will lean on her and will by no means give way, and he will hold fast to her and will by no means be put to shame.

**15:5** And she will exalt him above his neighbors, and in the midst of the assembly she will open his mouth.

**15:6** He will inherit gladness and a crown of exultation and an eternal name.

**15:7** People without understanding will by no means obtain her, and sinful men will by no means see her.

**15:8** She is far from arrogance, and lying men will by no means remember her.

**15:9** Praise is not fitting in the mouth of a sinner, because it was not sent from the Lord.

**15:10** For praise will be spoken in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it.

**15:11** Do not say, "Because of the Lord I fell away," for what He has hated, you must not do.

**15:12** Do not say, "He Himself led me astray," for He has no need of a sinful man.

**15:13** The Lord has hated every abomination, and it is not loved by those who fear Him.

**15:14** He Himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his own deliberation.

**15:15** If you wish, you will keep commandments, and to practice faithfulness is a matter of choice.

**15:16** He has set fire and water before you; to whichever you wish, you will stretch out your hand.

**15:17** Before people are life and death, and whatever one chooses will be given to him.

**15:18** For great is the wisdom of the Lord; He is mighty in power and sees all things.

**15:19** And His eyes are upon those who fear Him, and He Himself will know every deed of a person.

**15:20** And He has not commanded anyone to be ungodly, and He has not given anyone permission to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Literally 'a wife of virginity' (gyne partheneias), rendering the Hebrew idiom for a wife married in youth.
- **15:11** — The Greek text reads second-person singular poieseis ('you must not do' / 'you will not do'), whereas some Greek witnesses and the Hebrew read third-person 'He will not do' (poiesei).
- **15:14** — Greek diaboulion ('deliberation,' 'counsel,' or 'choice') renders the Hebrew term yetser ('inclination' or 'purpose').
- **15:15** — The Greek clause kai pistin poiesai eudokias (lit. 'and to do faithfulness of good pleasure') is terse; the genitive eudokias functions as a predicate genitive indicating that practicing faithfulness is a matter of choice or goodwill.
