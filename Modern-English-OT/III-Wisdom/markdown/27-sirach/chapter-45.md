## Text

**45:1** And He brought forth from him a man of mercy, finding favor in the eyes of all flesh, beloved by God and people, Moses, whose memorial is in blessings.

**45:2** He made him like the glory of the holy ones, and He magnified him in the terrors of enemies.

**45:3** By his words He caused signs to cease; He glorified him in the presence of kings. He gave him commands for His people, and He showed him part of His glory.

**45:4** In his faithfulness and meekness He sanctified him; He chose him out of all flesh.

**45:5** He made him hear His voice, and He led him into the thick darkness, and He gave him commandments face to face, a law of life and knowledge, to teach Jacob a covenant, and His judgments to Israel.

**45:6** He exalted Aaron, a holy one like him, his brother, from the tribe of Levi.

**45:7** He established him as an everlasting covenant, and He gave him the priesthood of the people. He blessed him with fine adornment, and He wrapped him in a vestment of glory.

**45:8** He clothed him with perfection of splendor, and He strengthened him with articles of strength: undergarments, a full-length robe, and a shoulder-garment.

**45:9** And He encircled him with pomegranates, with very many golden bells all around, to ring out a sound at his steps, to make a ringing heard in the temple for a memorial to the sons of His people;

**45:10** with a holy garment, with gold and blue and purple, of the works of an embroiderer, with an oracle of judgment, with manifestations of truth,

**45:11** with twisted scarlet, the work of a craftsman, with precious stones of seal engraving, in a setting of gold, of the works of a stone-engraver, for a memorial in an engraved inscription according to the number of the tribes of Israel;

**45:12** a golden crown upon a turban, an engraving of a seal of holiness, a splendor of honor, a work of strength, desires of the eyes, adorned. Beautiful things,

**45:13** before him none like them had existed; never has an outsider put them on, except his sons alone, and his descendants continually.

**45:14** His sacrifice will be wholly burned twice every day continually.

**45:15** Moses filled his hands and anointed him with holy oil; it became for him an eternal covenant, and in his seed during the days of heaven, to minister to Him and at the same time to serve as priest, and to bless His people in the name.

**45:16** He chose him from every living being, to offer a burnt offering to the Lord, incense and a pleasing aroma for a memorial, to make atonement for Your people.

**45:17** He set him in His commandments, with authority in covenants of judgments, to teach Jacob the testimonies, and in His law to call Israel.

**45:18** Strangers rose up together against him and were jealous of him in the wilderness, the men around Dathan and Abiram and the assembly of Korah, in fury and wrath.

**45:19** The Lord saw and was not pleased, and they were brought to an end in the fury of wrath. He worked wonders against them, to consume them in the fire of His flame.

**45:20** And He added glory to Aaron, and He gave him an inheritance. He allotted to them the firstfruits of the first produce; He prepared bread in abundance for them first.

**45:21** For indeed they will eat the sacrifices of the Lord, which He gave both to him and to his seed.

**45:22** Yet in the land of the people he will not inherit, and there is no portion for him among the people; for He Himself is your portion, an inheritance.

**45:23** And Phinehas son of Eleazar is third in glory, by being zealous in the fear of the Lord and taking his stand in the turning away of the people in the goodness of the eagerness of his soul; and he made atonement for Israel.

**45:24** Therefore a covenant of peace was established for him, to be leader of the sanctuary and for his people, so that the greatness of the priesthood might belong to him and to his seed forever.

**45:25** And a covenant for David, a son from the tribe of Judah: the inheritance of a king is of a son from a son alone; the inheritance of Aaron is also for his seed.

**45:26** May He give you wisdom in your heart to judge His people in righteousness, so that their good things may not vanish, and give their glory to their generations.

## Translator Notes

- **45:3** — Greek κατέπαυσεν literally means "caused to cease" (recalling Moses stopping the plagues at his word), whereas the surviving Hebrew manuscript reads "hastened"; τῆς δόξης αὐτοῦ is a partitive genitive ("part of His glory").
- **45:10** — "Oracle of judgment" (λογίῳ κρίσεως) and "manifestations of truth" (δήλοις ἀληθείας) are the Septuagint terms corresponding to the high priest's breastpiece and the Urim (cf. Exod 28:30).
- **45:12** — The neuter plural adjective ὡραῖα ("Beautiful things") stands at the end of verse 12 in this versification and introduces the clause completed in verse 13.
- **45:17** — The critical text reads φωνῆσαι ("to call/summon"), whereas other manuscripts read φωτίσαι ("to enlighten").
- **45:25** — The critical text omits "Jesse" after υἱῷ ("a son") and contrasts Davidic royal succession (passing to one son from a son) with Aaron's priestly inheritance shared across his seed.
