## Text

**41:1** O death, how bitter is the remembrance of you to a person living at peace in his possessions, to a man free from distraction and prospering in all things, and still having strength to receive food!

**41:2** O death, your sentence is good to a person who is in need and diminishing in strength, in extreme old age and distracted about all things, and who is disobedient and has lost endurance.

**41:3** Do not fear the sentence of death; remember those before you and those after; this is the sentence from the Lord for all flesh,

**41:4** and why do you reject the good pleasure of the Most High? Whether ten, or a hundred, or a thousand years, there is no inquiry about life in Hades.

**41:5** The children of sinners become abominable children, and those who frequent the dwellings of the ungodly;

**41:6** the inheritance of the children of sinners will perish, and with their offspring reproach will remain continually.

**41:7** Children will blame an ungodly father, because on account of him they will be reproached.

**41:8** Woe to you, ungodly men, who have forsaken the law of God the Most High;

**41:9** and if you are born, you will be born to a curse, and if you die, you will be apportioned to a curse.

**41:10** All things that are from earth will depart to earth; so the ungodly go from a curse to destruction.

**41:11** The mourning of people is over their bodies, but the name of sinners, which is not good, will be blotted out.

**41:12** Have concern for a name, for it will endure for you longer than a thousand great treasures of gold;

**41:13** a good life has a number of days, and a good name will endure forever.

**41:14** Preserve discipline in peace, children; but hidden wisdom and unseen treasure, what benefit is there in both?

**41:15** Better is a person who hides his folly than a person who hides his wisdom.

**41:16** Therefore, feel shame in accordance with my word; for it is not good to keep every kind of shame, and not all things are approved by all in faithfulness.

**41:17** Be ashamed before father and mother of sexual immorality, and before a leader and a mighty one of a lie,

**41:19** before a judge and a ruler of an offense, before an assembly and a people of lawlessness, before a partner and a friend of injustice, and before the place where you dwell of theft,

**41:20** and before the truth of God and the covenant, and of leaning an elbow on the bread, of scorn in receiving and giving,

**41:21** and before those who greet you of silence, of looking at a prostitute,

**41:23** and of turning away the face of a relative, of taking away a portion and a gift, and of looking intently at a married woman,

**41:25** of meddling with his maidservant, and do not approach her bed; before friends of words of reproach, and after giving do not reproach;

**41:27** of repetition and of a word heard, and of revelations of hidden words; and you will be truly modest and finding favor before every person.

## Translator Notes

- **41:2** — Greek apeithounti literally means 'disobedient' or 'refusing to comply,' conveying a person stubbornly or despairingly at odds with life.
- **41:4** — Greek elegmos zoes literally means 'reproof' or 'inquiry of life,' expressing that in Hades there is no questioning or argument over the length of one's life.
- **41:20** — The Greek literally reads 'and before the truth of God and the covenant' without naming a separate offense, either continuing from the preceding clause or reflecting an ancient translation rendering of a Hebrew clause about breaking an oath and covenant.
- **41:27** — Greek deuteroseos kai logou akoes literally means 'of repetition and of a word of hearing,' a hendiadys for repeating something one has heard.
