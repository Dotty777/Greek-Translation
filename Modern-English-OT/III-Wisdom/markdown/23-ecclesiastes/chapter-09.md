## Text

**9:1** that the righteous and the wise and their deeds are in the hand of God; neither love nor hatred does man know; all things are before their face,

**9:2** futility in all things. There is one event for the righteous and for the ungodly, for the good and for the bad, and for the clean and for the unclean, and for the one who sacrifices and for the one who does not sacrifice. As is the good man, so is the sinner; so is the one who swears, just as the one who fears the oath.

**9:3** This is an evil in everything done under the sun, that there is one event for all. And also the heart of the sons of man is filled with evil, and madness is in their heart during their life, and after them, to the dead.

**9:4** For who is there who shares with all the living? There is hope, for the living dog itself is better than the dead lion.

**9:5** For the living will know that they will die, but the dead know nothing; and there is no longer a reward for them, for their memory has been forgotten.

**9:6** Indeed, their love, and their hatred, and their jealousy as well have already perished; and there is no longer a portion for them forever in everything that has been done under the sun.

**9:7** Come, eat your bread in gladness, and drink your wine with a good heart, for God has already been pleased with your works.

**9:8** At every time let your garments be white, and let oil not be lacking on your head.

**9:9** And see life with a wife whom you have loved all the days of the life of your futility that have been given to you under the sun. All are days of your vapor, for this is your portion in your life and in your toil at which you toil under the sun.

**9:10** All that your hand finds to do, do according to your strength, for there is no work or reasoning or knowledge or wisdom in Hades, where you are going.

**9:11** I turned and saw under the sun that the race is not to the swift, and war is not to the mighty, nor bread to the wise man, nor wealth to the discerning, nor favor to those who know; for time and chance will meet all of them.

**9:12** For indeed, man also does not know his time: like the fish that are caught in an evil net, and like birds that are caught in a trap, like them the sons of man are trapped at an evil time, when it falls upon them suddenly.

**9:13** This also I saw as wisdom under the sun, and it is great to me:

**9:14** a small city, and few men in it; and a great king may come against it and surround it and build great siegeworks against it,

**9:15** and may find in it a poor wise man, and he himself may deliver the city by his wisdom, yet no one remembered that poor man.

**9:16** And I said, "Wisdom is better than strength," yet the wisdom of the poor man is despised, and his words are not heeded.

**9:17** Words of the wise are heard in quietness more than the shout of rulers in follies.

**9:18** Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner will destroy much goodness.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — Greek opens with "futility in all things" (where Hebrew reads "all is the same to all") and includes "and for the bad" after "for the good."
- **9:9** — Greek shifts from "futility" (mataiotes) to "vapor" (atmos) in the nominative clause "All are days of your vapor," rendering two occurrences of the same Hebrew word (hevel) with distinct Greek terms.
- **9:14** — In verses 14-15, Greek uses aorist subjunctive verbs ("may come ... surround ... build ... find ... deliver") for the hypothetical scenario before shifting to the indicative "remembered" at the end of 9:15.
- **9:17** — Greek uses the plurals "rulers" and "in follies" rather than the Hebrew "a ruler among fools."
