## Text

**8:1** A man's wisdom will illuminate his face, and one shameless in his face will be hated.

**8:2** Keep the mouth of a king, and concerning the word of an oath of God,

**8:3** do not be hasty; you will go from his presence; do not stand in an evil matter, for everything that he wills he will do,

**8:4** just as a king exercising authority; and who will say to him, "What are you doing?"

**8:5** The one who keeps a commandment will not know an evil thing, and the heart of a wise man knows a time of judgment.

**8:6** For to every matter there is a time and a judgment, because the knowledge of man is great upon him;

**8:7** for there is no one who knows what will be, for who will report to him how it will be?

**8:8** There is no man who has authority over the spirit to restrain the spirit, and there is no authority in the day of death, and there is no discharge in the day of war, and ungodliness will not deliver the one who belongs to it.

**8:9** And all of this I saw, and I gave my heart to every work that has been done under the sun, all the things in which man has exercised authority over man to harm him.

**8:10** And then I saw the ungodly brought into tombs, and from the holy place; and they went and were praised in the city, because they did so. Indeed, this also is futility.

**8:11** Because no contradiction is made quickly from those who do evil, therefore the heart of the sons of man within them is fully convinced to do evil.

**8:12** He who sinned did evil from then on and from their long duration; yet I also know that it is good for those who fear God, so that they may fear before His face.

**8:13** And it will not be good for the ungodly man, and he will not prolong days in a shadow, who does not fear before the face of God.

**8:14** There is a futility that has been done on the earth: that there are righteous people upon whom it comes like the work of the ungodly, and there are ungodly people to whom it comes like the work of the righteous. I said that this also is futility.

**8:15** And I praised gladness, because there is no good for man under the sun except to eat and to drink and to be glad; and this will accompany him in his toil for the days of his life, as many as God has given him under the sun.

**8:16** When I gave my heart to know wisdom and to see the preoccupation that has been done on the earth, for both by day and by night one does not see sleep with his eyes,

**8:17** then I saw all the works of God, that man will not be able to find the work that has been done under the sun. However much man may toil to seek, he will not find; and even however much a wise man may say he knows, he will not be able to find. For all of this I put to my heart, and with my heart I saw all of this:

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Greek reads "one shameless in his face will be hated," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb than the Masoretic "the hardness of his face is changed."
- **8:6** — Greek reads "knowledge" (gnosis), reflecting a consonant difference from the Hebrew Masoretic reading "evil" or "trouble."
- **8:10** — Greek reads "were praised" rather than the Hebrew Masoretic "were forgotten."
- **8:12** — Greek reads "from then on and from their long duration" where the Hebrew reads "a hundred times and prolongs his life."
- **8:17** — Following Septuagint versification, the final sentence belongs to 8:17 rather than 9:1, and Greek reads "with my heart I saw all of this" instead of the Hebrew "to examine all of this."
