## Text

**39:1** For the end; a psalm for David.

**39:2** Waiting, I waited for the Lord, and He gave attention to me and heard my supplication;

**39:3** and He brought me up out of a pit of misery and from muddy clay, and He set my feet on a rock and directed my steps;

**39:4** and He put a new song into my mouth, a hymn to our God. Many will see and fear, and will hope in the Lord.

**39:5** Blessed is the man whose hope is the name of the Lord, and who has not looked to futilities and false frenzies.

**39:6** You have made Your wonders many, Lord my God, and there is no one who will be likened to Your thoughts; I declared and spoke; they have multiplied beyond number.

**39:7** Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but You fashioned a body for me; a whole burnt offering and a sin offering You did not require.

**39:8** Then I said, "Look, I have come; in the scroll of a book it is written about me;

**39:9** I have desired to do Your will, my God, and Your law is in the midst of my heart."

**39:10** I proclaimed good news of righteousness in a great assembly; look, I will certainly not restrain my lips. Lord, You have known

**39:11** my righteousness; I did not hide Your truth in my heart, and I spoke of Your salvation; I did not hide Your mercy and Your truth from a vast congregation.

**39:12** But You, Lord, do not keep Your compassions far from me; Your mercy and Your truth have continually upheld me.

**39:13** For evils of which there is no number have surrounded me; my lawless deeds have overtaken me, and I was not able to see; they have multiplied beyond the hairs of my head, and my heart has forsaken me.

**39:14** Be pleased, Lord, to deliver me; Lord, give attention to helping me.

**39:15** May those who seek my soul to take it away be put to shame and disgraced together; may those who desire evil for me be turned backward and disgraced.

**39:16** Let those who say to me, "Aha, aha!" immediately receive their shame.

**39:17** May all who seek You exult and be glad in You, Lord, and let those who continually love Your salvation say continually, "May the Lord be magnified!"

**39:18** But I am poor and needy; the Lord will care for me. You are my helper and my defender; my God, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **39:5** — The Greek specifically reads "the name of the Lord" as the object of hope, and "futilities and false frenzies" where the Hebrew refers to the proud and those who turn aside to lies.
- **39:7** — The Greek reads "You fashioned a body for me" (soma de katertiso moi), whereas the Hebrew has "ears you have dug [or opened] for me." This Septuagint wording is quoted in Hebrews 10:5.
- **39:11** — The Greek reads "my righteousness" as the direct object of "Lord, You have known" at the end of v. 10, rather than "Your righteousness" with "I have not hidden" as in the Hebrew.
- **39:16** — "Immediately" translates Greek parachrema (where the Hebrew has "because of their shame"), and "Aha, aha!" renders Greek euge, euge (literally "Well done, well done!"), used here as a mocking taunt.
- **39:17** — The Greek text includes "continually" (dia pantos) twice, modifying both the loving of God's salvation and the continual speaking.
