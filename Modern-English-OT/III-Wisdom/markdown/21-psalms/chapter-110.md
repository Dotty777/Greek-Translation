## Text

**110:1** Hallelujah. I will give thanks to You, Lord, with my whole heart, in the council of the upright and the assembly.

**110:2** Great are the works of the Lord, sought out for all His desires;

**110:3** thanksgiving and majesty are His work, and His righteousness remains forever and ever.

**110:4** He made a remembrance of His wonders; the Lord is merciful and compassionate.

**110:5** He gave food to those who fear Him; He will remember His covenant forever.

**110:6** He declared the strength of His works to His people, to give them the inheritance of the nations.

**110:7** The works of His hands are truth and justice; all His commandments are faithful,

**110:8** established forever and ever, made in truth and uprightness.

**110:9** He sent redemption to His people; He commanded His covenant forever; His name is holy and to be feared.

**110:10** The beginning of wisdom is the fear of the Lord; good understanding belongs to all who practice it; His praise remains forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **110:2** — Greek exezetemena eis panta ta thelemata autou ("sought out for all His desires") reflects the Septuagint rendering, differing from the Hebrew sense "studied by all who delight in them."
- **110:10** — Greek auten ("it") is feminine singular, referring back to "wisdom" or "understanding," whereas the Hebrew has a plural pronoun referring to the commandments.
