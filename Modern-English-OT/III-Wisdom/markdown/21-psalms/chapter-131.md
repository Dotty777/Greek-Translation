## Text

**131:1** A song of the steps. Remember, Lord, David and all his meekness,

**131:2** how he swore to the Lord, vowed to the God of Jacob:

**131:3** "I will certainly not enter into the tent of my house, nor go up onto the bed of my couch,

**131:4** nor give sleep to my eyes, or drowsiness to my eyelids, or rest to my temples,

**131:5** until I find a place for the Lord, a tent for the God of Jacob."

**131:6** Look, we heard of it in Ephrathah; we found it in the thickets of the forest.

**131:7** I will enter into His tents; we will worship at the place where His feet stood.

**131:8** Arise, Lord, into Your rest, You and the ark of Your holiness.

**131:9** Your priests clothed themselves with righteousness, and Your devout ones will exult.

**131:10** For the sake of David Your servant, do not turn away the face of Your anointed one.

**131:11** The Lord swore truth to David, and He will by no means nullify it: "I will place one from the fruit of your body upon your throne.

**131:12** If your sons keep My covenant and these testimonies of Mine that I will teach them, their sons also will sit upon your throne forever."

**131:13** For the Lord chose Zion; He selected her as a dwelling for Himself:

**131:14** "This is My resting place forever and ever; here I will dwell, because I selected her.

**131:15** Her widow I will certainly bless; her poor I will satisfy with bread.

**131:16** Her priests I will clothe with salvation, and her devout ones will exult with exultation.

**131:17** There I will make a horn sprout for David; I have prepared a lamp for your anointed one.

**131:18** His enemies I will clothe with shame, but upon him My holiness will flourish."

## Translator Notes

- **131:3** — Literally "If I will enter..." (and similarly in v. 4), a Hebraistic oath formula expressing an emphatic negative; verse 4 also includes the Greek phrase "or rest to my temples," absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **131:7** — The Greek text shifts from first-person singular "I will enter" (εἰσελεύσομαι) to plural "we will worship" (προσκυνήσομεν); likewise, verse 9 reads aorist "clothed themselves" (ἐνεδύσαντο) alongside future "will exult."
- **131:15** — Translating the Greek text "her widow" (τὴν χήραν αὐτῆς), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "her provision."
- **131:17** — The Greek text reads "for your anointed one" (τῷ χριστῷ σου), apparently addressing Zion or reflecting a variant influenced by verse 10, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and other witnesses read "for my anointed one."
- **131:18** — Greek ἁγίασμα ("holiness," also in v. 8) can also mean "sanctuary" or "consecration," corresponding here to the Hebrew term for a royal crown or sign of consecration.
