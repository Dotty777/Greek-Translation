## Text

**76:1** For the end, on behalf of Jeduthun; a psalm for Asaph.

**76:2** With my voice I cried out to the Lord, and my voice was to God, and He paid attention to me.

**76:3** In the day of my distress I sought out God, with my hands by night before Him, and I was not deceived; my soul refused to be consoled.

**76:4** I remembered God and was gladdened; I mused, and my spirit grew faint. Interlude.

**76:5** My enemies seized the watches beforehand; I was troubled and did not speak.

**76:6** I considered ancient days, and everlasting years

**76:7** I remembered, and I meditated; at night I was musing with my heart, and my spirit was searching.

**76:8** Will the Lord reject forever, and will He no longer continue to be well pleased?

**76:9** Or will He completely cut off mercy from generation to generation?

**76:10** Or will God forget to have compassion, or will He hold back His compassions in His wrath? Interlude.

**76:11** And I said, "Now I have begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High."

**76:12** I remembered the works of the Lord; for I will remember Your wonders from the beginning,

**76:13** and I will meditate on all Your works, and I will muse on Your deeds.

**76:14** God, Your way is in the holy place; what god is great like our God?

**76:15** You are our God who does wonders; You made known Your power among Your peoples;

**76:16** You redeemed Your people with Your arm, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Interlude.

**76:17** The waters saw You, God; the waters saw You and feared, and the depths were troubled;

**76:18** a multitude of the sound of water; the clouds gave a voice, for indeed Your arrows pass through;

**76:19** the voice of Your thunder was in the wheel; the earth was shaken and trembled.

**76:20** Your way is in the sea, and Your paths are in many waters, and Your footprints will not be known.

**76:21** You guided Your people like sheep by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

## Translator Notes

- **76:3** — LXX reads "with my hands by night before Him, and I was not deceived" (MT: "in the night my hand was stretched out without growing weary").
- **76:5** — The Greek critical text reads "my enemies" (hoi echthroi mou, supported by Vaticanus and Sinaiticus), whereas other Greek witnesses and MT read "my eyes."
- **76:9** — The Greek critical text follows early uncials (including Vaticanus and Sinaiticus) in omitting "has His promise ended?" and reads literally "to the end ... from generation and generation."
- **76:11** — LXX reads "Now I have begun; this is the change," interpreting the Hebrew differently from MT ("This is my grief/appeal: the years...").
- **76:19** — "In the wheel" translates Greek en tō trochō literally (MT: "in the whirlwind"), and the Greek critical text omits the clause "Your lightnings lit up the world" found in MT and other witnesses.
