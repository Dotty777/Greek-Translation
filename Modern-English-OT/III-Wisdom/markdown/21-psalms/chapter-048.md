## Text

**48:1** For the end: a psalm for the sons of Korah.

**48:2** Hear these things, all nations; give ear, all who inhabit the world,

**48:3** both the earthborn and the sons of men, rich and poor together.

**48:4** My mouth will speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart, understanding.

**48:5** I will incline my ear to a parable; I will open my riddle with a harp.

**48:6** Why do I fear in an evil day? The lawlessness of my heel will surround me,

**48:7** those who trust in their strength and boast in the abundance of their wealth.

**48:8** A brother does not redeem; will a man redeem? He will not give to God his atonement,

**48:9** nor the price of the redemption of his soul; and he labored forever,

**48:10** and he will live to the end, for he will not see destruction,

**48:11** when he sees the wise dying. Together the senseless and the foolish will perish, and they will leave their wealth to strangers.

**48:12** And their tombs are their houses forever, their dwellings to generation and generation; they called their names upon their lands.

**48:13** And a man who is in honor did not understand; he was compared to the senseless beasts and became like them.

**48:14** This way of theirs is a stumbling block to them, and after these things they will bless with their mouth. Interlude.

**48:15** Like sheep they were placed in Hades; death shepherds them, and the upright will rule over them in the morning, and their help will grow old in Hades, away from their glory.

**48:16** Yet God will redeem my soul from the hand of Hades, when He receives me. Interlude.

**48:17** Do not fear when a man becomes rich, and when the glory of his house is multiplied;

**48:18** for when he dies he will not take all things, nor will his glory go down with him.

**48:19** For his soul will be blessed in his life; he will give thanks to You when You do good to him.

**48:20** He will enter as far as the generation of his fathers; forever he will not see light.

**48:21** A man who is in honor did not understand; he was compared to the senseless beasts and became like them.

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — Greek 'For the end' (Eis to telos) is the standard Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew musical superscription usually translated 'For the choir director.'
- **48:6** — Literally 'the lawlessness of my heel,' where the Greek translates the Hebrew word literally as 'heel' rather than 'supplanters'; the plural participles in v. 7 follow in loose apposition.
- **48:8** — The Greek syntax of vv. 8-10 diverges notably from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reading 'A brother does not redeem; will a man redeem?' and taking 'the price of the redemption of his soul' as a direct object of 'He will not give,' followed by 'he labored forever' in v. 9.
- **48:15** — Greek 'Hades' renders Hebrew 'Sheol'; the Greek also reads 'their help' (boetheia) and 'away from their glory' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'their form' and 'from their dwelling.'
- **48:19** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('when you do well for yourself'), the Greek reads third-person 'to him' (auto) and uses psalmic verbs of thanksgiving and divine favor, naturally understood as addressing God (though some read the second person as addressing the hearer of v. 17).
