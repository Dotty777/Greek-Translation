# Psalm 74 — Saints Modern English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**74:1** For the end; do not destroy; a psalm of a song for Asaph.

**74:2** We will give thanks to You, God; we will give thanks and call upon Your name; I will recount all Your wonders,

**74:3** when I take an appointed time; I will judge with uprightness.

**74:4** The earth melted, and all who inhabit it; I made its pillars firm. Interlude.

**74:5** I said to the lawless not to act lawlessly, and to those who sin, "Do not lift up a horn;

**74:6** do not raise your horn on high; do not speak unrighteousness against God."

**74:7** For neither from the east nor from the west nor from deserts,

**74:8** for God is judge; He humbles this one and lifts up that one.

**74:9** For a cup is in the hand of the Lord, of unmixed wine, full of mixture, and He poured from this into that; yet its dregs were not emptied out, and all the sinners of the earth will drink.

**74:10** But I will rejoice forever; I will sing psalms to the God of Jacob;

**74:11** and I will break all the horns of the sinners, and the horns of the righteous one will be lifted up.

## Translator Notes

- **74:2** — LXX reads "and call upon Your name" and first-person singular "I will recount" (MT: "Your name is near; they recount").
- **74:3** — Greek punctuation links "when I take an appointed time" with 74:2; "with uprightness" translates the Greek adverbial accusative plural euthytētas (lit. "uprightnesses").
- **74:4** — "Interlude" translates Greek diapsalma, a musical pause or interlude corresponding to Hebrew Selah (similarly in 75:4, 75:10, 76:4, 76:10, and 76:16).
- **74:7** — The Greek sentence is elliptical without an explicit verb and omits "mountains"; "the east" translates exodōn (lit. "goings-out [of the sun]").
- **74:9** — The Greek literally has the paradox "of unmixed wine, full of mixture," uses eklinen (lit. "tilted/inclined") for pouring, and reads "its dregs were not emptied out" (differing from MT).
