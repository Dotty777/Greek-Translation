## Text

**42:1** A psalm for David. Judge me, God, and decide my case against an unholy nation; rescue me from an unjust and deceitful man.

**42:2** For You are God, my strength; why have You rejected me? And why do I walk about downcast while my enemy oppresses me?

**42:3** Send out Your light and Your truth; they led me and brought me to Your holy mountain and to Your dwellings.

**42:4** And I will go in to the altar of God, to God who gladdens my youth; I will give thanks to You, Lord my God, with a lyre.

**42:5** Why are you deeply grieved, soul, and why do you greatly trouble me? Hope in God, for I will give thanks to Him; the salvation of my face is my God.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — Septuagint Psalm 42 corresponds to Hebrew Psalm 43 and includes the superscription "A psalm for David" (ψαλμὸς τῷ Δαυίδ, a dative that can also mean "of/by David" or "pertaining to David"), which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **42:3** — The Greek uses aorist indicative verbs ("they led me and brought me") rather than the jussive request ("let them lead me and bring me") found in the Hebrew text.
