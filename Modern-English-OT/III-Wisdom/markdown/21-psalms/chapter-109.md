## Text

**109:1** A psalm for David. The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand until I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet."

**109:2** The Lord will send forth a rod of power from Zion; and rule in the midst of Your enemies.

**109:3** With You is dominion on the day of Your power, in the splendor of the holy ones; from the womb before the morning star I brought You forth.

**109:4** The Lord swore and will not relent, "You are a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek."

**109:5** The Lord at Your right hand shattered kings on the day of His wrath;

**109:6** He will judge among the nations; He will fill them with corpses; He will shatter heads over a wide earth.

**109:7** He will drink from a stream along the way; because of this He will lift up His head.

## Translator Notes

- **109:1** — Greek uses kyrios twice ("the Lord said to my lord/Lord"); capitalizing "my Lord" and the pronouns in vv. 1-7 follows traditional messianic reading, as Greek manuscripts make no case distinction between a human royal lord and a divine Lord.
- **109:3** — The Septuagint differs markedly from the Hebrew here, reading "with You is dominion [Greek arche, also 'beginning'] ... from the womb before the morning star I brought You forth."
- **109:5** — Greek synethlasen ("shattered") is aorist indicative, contrasting with the future tenses in v. 6 and depicting the Lord's victory as already accomplished.
- **109:6** — Greek plerosei ptoma literally reads "He will fill [with] corpse/downfall" using a singular noun, taken either collectively ("fill them with corpses") or as "bring downfall to full measure."
