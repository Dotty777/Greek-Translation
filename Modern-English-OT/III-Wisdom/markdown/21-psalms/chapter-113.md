## Text

**113:1** Hallelujah. When Israel departed from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a foreign-speaking people,

**113:2** Judea became His sanctuary, Israel His dominion.

**113:3** The sea saw and fled; the Jordan turned backward.

**113:4** The mountains leaped like rams, and the hills like lambs of sheep.

**113:5** What is it, sea, that you fled? And you, Jordan, that you withdrew backward?

**113:6** Mountains, did you leap like rams?

**113:7** At the presence of the Lord the earth was shaken, at the presence of the God of Jacob,

**113:8** who turned the rock into pools of water, and the flinty rock into springs of water.

**113:9** Not to us, Lord, not to us, but rather to Your name give glory, because of Your mercy and Your truth,

**113:10** so that the nations may never say, "Where is their God?"

**113:11** But our God is in heaven above; in the heavens and on the earth He has done all that He willed.

**113:12** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, works of human hands.

**113:13** They have a mouth and do not speak; they have eyes and will not see;

**113:14** they have ears and will not hear; they have noses and will not smell;

**113:15** they have hands and will not feel; they have feet and will not walk; they will not make a sound in their throat.

**113:16** May those who make them become like them, and all who trust in them.

**113:17** The house of Israel has hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:18** The house of Aaron has hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:19** Those who fear the Lord have hoped in Him; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:20** The Lord has remembered us and has blessed us; He has blessed the house of Israel; He has blessed the house of Aaron;

**113:21** He has blessed those who fear the Lord, the small with the great.

**113:22** May the Lord add to you, to you and to your sons.

**113:23** Blessed are you by the Lord, who made heaven and earth.

**113:24** The heaven of heaven belongs to the Lord, but He has given the earth to the sons of men.

**113:25** The dead will not praise You, Lord, nor will all who go down into Hades;

**113:26** but let us who live bless the Lord from now and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **113:2** — Greek explicitly names "Judea" rather than "Judah," as in the Hebrew MT.
- **113:6** — This Greek text contains only the first clause ("Mountains, did you leap like rams?"), without the parallel line about the hills found in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **113:7** — Greek uses a past indicative ("the earth was shaken"), whereas the Hebrew MT uses an imperative ("Tremble, earth").
- **113:9** — In the Septuagint, vv. 9-26 continue as part of Psalm 113 rather than starting a separate psalm (MT Ps 115); similarly, vv. 17-21 use past indicative verbs ("has hoped," "has blessed") where the Hebrew MT has imperatives and future verbs.
- **113:25** — Greek addresses the Lord in the second person ("praise You, Lord") and reads "into Hades" where the Hebrew MT reads "into silence"; v. 26 uses the hortatory subjunctive ("let us bless").
