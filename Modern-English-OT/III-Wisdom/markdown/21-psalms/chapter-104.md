## Text

**104:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord and call on His name; declare His works among the nations.

**104:2** Sing to Him and make music to Him; recount all His marvels.

**104:3** Boast in His holy name; let the heart of those seeking the Lord be glad.

**104:4** Seek the Lord and be strengthened; seek His face continually.

**104:5** Remember His marvels that He did, His wonders and the judgments of His mouth,

**104:6** offspring of Abraham, His servants, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**104:7** He Himself is the Lord our God; in all the earth are His judgments.

**104:8** He remembered His covenant forever, the word that He commanded for a thousand generations,

**104:9** which He covenanted with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac,

**104:10** and He established it for Jacob as a decree, and for Israel an everlasting covenant,

**104:11** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan, the measured portion of your inheritance,"

**104:12** when they were small in number, very few, and sojourners in it.

**104:13** And they passed from nation to nation, from a kingdom to another people.

**104:14** He did not allow anyone to wrong them, and He rebuked kings on their behalf:

**104:15** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do not do evil to My prophets."

**104:16** And He called a famine upon the land; He broke every support of bread.

**104:17** He sent a man ahead of them; Joseph was sold as a slave.

**104:18** They afflicted his feet with fetters; his soul passed through iron.

**104:19** Until his word came, the oracle of the Lord refined him.

**104:20** A king sent and released him, a ruler of peoples, and let him go;

**104:21** he appointed him lord of his house and ruler of all his property,

**104:22** to instruct his rulers as himself, and to make his elders wise.

**104:23** And Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

**104:24** And He increased His people greatly, and strengthened them more than their enemies.

**104:25** And He turned their heart to hate His people, to deal deceitfully with His servants.

**104:26** He sent Moses His servant, Aaron whom He chose.

**104:27** He placed in them the words of His signs and wonders in the land of Ham.

**104:28** He sent darkness and it grew dark, and they rebelled against His words.

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood, and killed their fish.

**104:30** Their land swarmed with frogs, in the chambers of their kingdoms.

**104:31** He spoke, and dog-flies came, and gnats in all their territories.

**104:32** He made their rains hail, blazing fire in their land.

**104:33** And He struck their vines and their fig trees, and broke every tree of their territory.

**104:34** He spoke, and locusts came, and young locusts, of which there was no number,

**104:35** and they devoured all the vegetation in their land, and devoured the fruit of their land.

**104:36** And He struck every firstborn from their land, the firstfruits of all their labor.

**104:37** And He brought them out with silver and gold, and there was no one weak among their tribes.

**104:38** Egypt was glad at their departure, for the fear of them fell upon them.

**104:39** He spread out a cloud as a covering for them, and fire to illuminate the night for them.

**104:40** They asked, and quail came, and He filled them with bread of heaven.

**104:41** He split open a rock, and waters flowed; rivers ran in waterless places.

**104:42** For He remembered His holy word to Abraham His servant.

**104:43** And He brought out His people with exultation, and His chosen ones with gladness.

**104:44** And He gave them the lands of nations, and they inherited the labors of peoples,

**104:45** so that they might keep His statutes, and seek out His law.

## Translator Notes

- **104:4** — The Greek reads the imperative verb κραταιώθητε ("be strengthened"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a noun ("and His strength").
- **104:22** — The Greek reads τοῦ παιδεῦσαι ... ὡς ἑαυτόν ("to instruct ... as himself"), reflecting a different reading from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("to bind ... at his pleasure").
- **104:27** — The Greek uses the singular verb ἔθετο ("He placed"), making God the subject, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses a plural verb ("they performed").
- **104:28** — The Greek omits any negative particle ("and they rebelled against His words"), unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("and they did not rebel against His words").
- **104:30** — The Greek text here reads τῶν βασιλειῶν αὐτῶν ("of their kingdoms"), whereas other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew read "of their kings."
