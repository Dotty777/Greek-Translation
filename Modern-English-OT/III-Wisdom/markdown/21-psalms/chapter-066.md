## Text

**66:1** For the end, with hymns; a psalm for David.

**66:2** May God have compassion on us and bless us; may He cause His face to shine upon us—Interlude—

**66:3** so that Your way may be known on the earth, and Your salvation among all nations.

**66:4** Let peoples give thanks, God; let all peoples give thanks.

**66:5** Let nations be glad and exult, for You will judge peoples in uprightness, and You will guide nations on the earth. Interlude.

**66:6** Let peoples give thanks to You, God; let all peoples give thanks to You.

**66:7** The earth has given its fruit; may God, our God, bless us;

**66:8** may God bless us, and let all the ends of the earth fear Him.

## Translator Notes

- **66:1** — The Septuagint renders the musical heading as "with hymns" (en hymnois) and attributes the psalm to David, an attribution absent from the Hebrew text.
- **66:4** — Unlike verse 6, the Greek text here omits the pronoun "to You" (soi) in both clauses, a reading preserved in major early Septuagint witnesses.
