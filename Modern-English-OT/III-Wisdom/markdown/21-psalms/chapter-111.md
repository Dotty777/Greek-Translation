## Text

**111:1** Hallelujah. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord; in His commandments he delights greatly.

**111:2** His offspring will be powerful on the earth; the generation of the upright will be blessed.

**111:3** Glory and wealth are in his house, and his righteousness remains forever and ever.

**111:4** Light has dawned in darkness for the upright; he is merciful and compassionate and righteous.

**111:5** Good is the man who is compassionate and lends; he will order his words in judgment.

**111:6** For he will never be shaken; the righteous man will be in eternal remembrance.

**111:7** He will not be afraid of an evil report; his heart is ready to hope in the Lord.

**111:8** His heart is established; he will not be afraid until he looks upon his enemies.

**111:9** He has scattered, he has given to the needy; his righteousness remains forever and ever; his horn will be exalted in glory.

**111:10** The sinner will see and be angry; he will gnash his teeth and melt away; the desire of sinners will perish.

## Translator Notes

- **111:3** — Greek reads "Glory and wealth," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "Wealth and riches."
- **111:4** — The masculine singular adjectives "merciful and compassionate and righteous" have no explicit subject in Greek and may describe either the righteous man (continuing vv. 1-3, 5) or the Lord (cf. Ps 110:4).
- **111:5** — Greek literally reads "his words," reflecting the broad range of the underlying Hebrew term for "words" or "affairs."
- **111:7** — Greek reads "ready to hope," whereas the Hebrew MT reads "steadfast, trusting."
