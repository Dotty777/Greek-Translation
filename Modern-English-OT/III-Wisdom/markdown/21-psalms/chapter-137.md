## Text

**137:1** For David. I will give thanks to You, Lord, with my whole heart, because You heard the words of my mouth; and before angels I will sing praise to You.

**137:2** I will bow down toward Your holy temple, and I will give thanks to Your name for Your mercy and Your truth, because You magnified Your holy name above everything.

**137:3** On whatever day I call out, hear me quickly; You will care greatly for me in my soul with much strength.

**137:4** Let all the kings of the earth give thanks to You, Lord, because they heard all the words of Your mouth;

**137:5** and let them sing in the ways of the Lord, for the glory of the Lord is great;

**137:6** for the Lord is high, and He looks upon what is lowly, and what is high He knows from afar.

**137:7** If I walk in the midst of affliction, You will keep me alive; against the wrath of enemies You stretched out my hands, and Your right hand saved me.

**137:8** Lord, You will repay on my behalf; Lord, Your mercy is forever; Lord, do not overlook the works of Your hands.

## Translator Notes

- **137:1** — The Greek text includes the clause "because You heard the words of my mouth" and renders the Hebrew term for heavenly beings or gods as "angels" (angelon).
- **137:2** — The Greek phrase epi pan onoma to hagion sou is syntactically ambiguous and may be construed either as "Your holy name above everything" (followed here) or as "Your holy one/sanctuary above every name."
- **137:7** — The Greek text literally reads "against the wrath of enemies You stretched out my hands" (echthron ... cheiras mou), whereas other manuscripts read "my enemies" and "Your hand."
