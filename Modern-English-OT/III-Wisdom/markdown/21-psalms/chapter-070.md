## Text

**70:1** For David, of the sons of Jonadab and of those first taken captive. God, in You I have hoped; may I never be put to shame.

**70:2** In Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline Your ear to me and save me.

**70:3** Become for me a defending God, a fortified place to save me, for You are my stronghold and my refuge.

**70:4** God, deliver me from the hand of a sinner, from the hand of a lawbreaker and wrongdoer;

**70:5** for You are my expectation, Lord; the Lord is my hope from my youth.

**70:6** Upon You I have leaned from the womb; from my mother's womb You are my protector; in You is my song of praise continually.

**70:7** I have become like a portent to many, and You are a strong helper.

**70:8** Let my mouth be filled with praise, so that I may sing of Your glory, all day long of Your majesty.

**70:9** Do not cast me away at the time of old age; when my strength fails, do not forsake me.

**70:10** For my enemies spoke concerning me, and those who watch for my soul took counsel together,

**70:11** saying, "God has forsaken him; pursue and overtake him, for there is no one who delivers."

**70:12** God, do not keep far from me.

**70:13** Let those who slander my soul be ashamed and fail; let those who seek evil for me be clothed with shame and disgrace.

**70:14** But I will hope continually, and I will add to all Your praise.

**70:15** My mouth will proclaim Your righteousness, all day long Your salvation, for I have not known business affairs.

**70:16** I will enter in the might of the Lord; Lord, I will remember Your righteousness, Yours alone.

**70:17** You have taught me, God, from my youth, and up to now I will declare Your wonders,

**70:18** and even to old age and advanced years; God, do not forsake me until I declare Your arm to every generation that is coming, Your might

**70:19** and Your righteousness, God, up to the highest places, the great things that You have done. God, who is like You?

**70:20** Because You showed me many afflictions and evils, and turning back You made me alive, and from the abysses of the earth You brought me up again,

**70:21** multiplying Your righteousness. And turning back You comforted me, and from the abysses You brought me up again.

**70:22** For I will also praise Your truth to You with a musical instrument; I will sing to You, God, with a lyre, Holy One of Israel.

**70:23** My lips will exult when I sing to You, and my soul, which You have redeemed.

**70:24** And moreover my tongue also will meditate on Your righteousness all day long, when those who seek evil for me are ashamed and disgraced.

## Translator Notes

- **70:1** — The superscription associating this psalm with David, the sons of Jonadab, and the first captives is distinctive to the Septuagint; the Hebrew psalm has no superscription.
- **70:12** — This shorter Greek text omits the second clause ("my God, attend to my help") found in other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew.
- **70:15** — The Greek text reads πραγματίας ("business affairs"), a major early Septuagint reading; other Greek manuscripts read γραμματείας ("learning" or "reckoning"), and the Hebrew reads "numbers."
- **70:21** — The Greek text reads τὴν δικαιοσύνην σου ("Your righteousness") where the Hebrew and other manuscripts read "Your greatness," and it retains the repeated closing clause ("and from the abysses You brought me up again") absent from the Hebrew.
