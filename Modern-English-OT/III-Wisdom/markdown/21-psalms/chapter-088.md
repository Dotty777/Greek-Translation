## Text

**88:1** Of understanding, for Ethan the Israelite.

**88:2** I will sing of Your mercies, Lord, forever; to generation and generation I will declare Your truth with my mouth.

**88:3** For You said, "Forever mercy will be built up"; in the heavens Your truth will be prepared.

**88:4** "I have made a covenant with My chosen ones; I have sworn to David My servant:

**88:5** 'Forever I will prepare your seed, and I will build your throne to generation and generation.'" Interlude.

**88:6** The heavens will praise Your wonders, Lord, and Your truth in the assembly of holy ones.

**88:7** For who in the clouds will be made equal to the Lord? And who will be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?

**88:8** God is glorified in the council of holy ones, great and fearsome over all those around Him.

**88:9** Lord, God of powers, who is like You? You are mighty, Lord, and Your truth is around You.

**88:10** You rule over the might of the sea, and You calm the surging of its waves.

**88:11** You humbled a proud one like a wounded man, and with the arm of Your strength You scattered Your enemies.

**88:12** Yours are the heavens, and Yours is the earth; the world and its fullness You founded.

**88:13** The north and the seas You created; Tabor and Hermoniim will exult in Your name.

**88:14** Yours is the arm with might; let Your hand be strengthened, let Your right hand be exalted.

**88:15** Righteousness and judgment are the preparation of Your throne; mercy and truth will go before Your face.

**88:16** Blessed is the people that knows the joyful shout; Lord, in the light of Your face they will walk,

**88:17** and in Your name they will exult all day long, and in Your righteousness they will be exalted.

**88:18** For You are the boast of their strength, and in Your favor our horn will be exalted.

**88:19** For protection belongs to the Lord, and to the Holy One of Israel, our King.

**88:20** Then You spoke in a vision to Your sons, and said, "I have placed help upon a mighty one; I have exalted a chosen one from My people.

**88:21** I have found David My servant; with holy mercy I have anointed him.

**88:22** For My hand will assist him, and My arm will strengthen him.

**88:23** An enemy will not gain an advantage over him, and a son of lawlessness will not proceed to harm him.

**88:24** And I will cut down his enemies from before his face, and I will put to flight those who hate him.

**88:25** And My truth and My mercy will be with him, and in My name his horn will be exalted.

**88:26** And I will set his hand on the sea, and his right hand on the rivers.

**88:27** He will call upon Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the protector of my salvation.'

**88:28** And I will make him firstborn, high among the kings of the earth.

**88:29** Forever I will keep My mercy for him, and My covenant will be faithful to him.

**88:30** And I will establish his seed forever and ever, and his throne like the days of heaven.

**88:31** If his sons forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments,

**88:32** if they profane My statutes and do not keep My commandments,

**88:33** I will visit their lawless deeds with a rod, and their sins with lashes.

**88:34** Yet I will certainly not disperse My mercy from him, nor will I act unjustly in My truth;

**88:35** nor will I profane My covenant, and the things that go out through My lips I will certainly not nullify.

**88:36** Once I have sworn by My holiness; I will certainly not lie to David:

**88:37** his seed will remain forever, and his throne like the sun before Me,

**88:38** and like the moon, established forever; and the witness in heaven is faithful." Interlude.

**88:39** But You rejected and despised; You put off Your anointed one.

**88:40** You overturned the covenant of Your servant; You profaned his sanctuary to the ground.

**88:41** You tore down all his walls; You made his strongholds cowardice.

**88:42** All who pass along the way plundered him; he became a reproach to his neighbors.

**88:43** You exalted the right hand of his enemies; You gladdened all his enemies.

**88:44** You turned back the help of his sword, and You did not support him in battle.

**88:45** You removed him from purification; You dashed his throne to the ground.

**88:46** You shortened the days of his throne; You poured shame over him. Interlude.

**88:47** How long, Lord, will You turn away to the end? Will Your wrath burn like fire?

**88:48** Remember what my existence is; for did You create all the sons of men in vain?

**88:49** Who is the man who will live and not see death? Will he deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Interlude.

**88:50** Where are Your ancient mercies, Lord, which You swore to David in Your truth?

**88:51** Remember, Lord, the reproach of Your servants, which You bore in my bosom, of many nations,

**88:52** with which Your enemies reproached, Lord, with which they reproached the recompense of Your anointed one.

**88:53** Blessed be the Lord forever. So be it, so be it.

## Translator Notes

- **88:3** — Greek reads second-person "For You said" (whereas the Hebrew has "For I said") and in v. 4 plural "My chosen ones."
- **88:13** — Greek reads "seas" (thalassas, where the Hebrew has "south") and uses the plural form "Hermoniim" for Mount Hermon; in v. 11, Greek renders Hebrew Rahab as the common term "a proud one."
- **88:21** — Greek reads "with holy mercy" (en eleei hagio) rather than "with My holy oil" (as in the Hebrew and some Greek manuscripts); in v. 20, Greek reads "to Your sons" where the Hebrew has "to your godly ones."
- **88:45** — Greek reads "from purification" (where the Hebrew has "his splendor") and in v. 46 "the days of his throne" (where the Hebrew has "the days of his youth").
- **88:51** — The Greek text reads second-person singular "which You bore" (hypeschou, whereas other Greek witnesses read first-person "which I bore," matching the Hebrew); in v. 52, Greek "recompense" (antallagma) reflects the alternative sense of the Hebrew root for "footsteps."
