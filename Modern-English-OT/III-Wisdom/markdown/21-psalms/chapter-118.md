## Text

**118:1** Hallelujah. Blessed are the blameless in the way, those who walk in the law of the Lord.

**118:2** Blessed are those who search out His testimonies; with a whole heart they seek Him out.

**118:3** For those who work lawlessness have not walked in His ways.

**118:4** You commanded Your commandments to be kept diligently.

**118:5** Oh, that my ways might be directed to keep Your statutes!

**118:6** Then I will certainly not be ashamed when I look upon all Your commandments.

**118:7** I will give thanks to You, Lord, with uprightness of heart, when I have learned the judgments of Your righteousness.

**118:8** I will keep Your statutes; do not forsake me utterly.

**118:9** By what will a young man set his way right? By keeping Your words.

**118:10** With a whole heart I have sought You out; do not thrust me away from Your commandments.

**118:11** In my heart I have hidden Your utterances, so that I may not sin against You.

**118:12** Blessed are You, Lord; teach me Your statutes.

**118:13** With my lips I have declared all the judgments of Your mouth.

**118:14** In the way of Your testimonies I delighted, as over all wealth.

**118:15** On Your commandments I will reflect, and I will seek out Your ways.

**118:16** On Your statutes I will meditate; I will not forget Your words.

**118:17** Recompense Your servant; I will live, and I will keep Your words.

**118:18** Uncover my eyes, and I will perceive Your wonders from Your law.

**118:19** I am a sojourner on the earth; do not turn Your commandments away from me.

**118:20** My soul has longed to desire Your statutes at all times.

**118:21** You have rebuked the arrogant; cursed are those who turn aside from Your commandments.

**118:22** Take away reproach and contempt from me, because I have sought out Your testimonies.

**118:23** For indeed rulers sat and were speaking against me, but Your servant was reflecting on Your statutes.

**118:24** For indeed Your testimonies are my meditation, and my counsels are Your statutes.

**118:25** My soul has clung to the ground; I will live according to Your utterance.

**118:26** I have declared Your ways, and You heard me; teach me Your statutes.

**118:27** And make me understand the way of Your statutes, and I will reflect on Your wonders.

**118:28** My soul has grown drowsy from despondency; confirm me in Your words.

**118:29** Remove the way of unrighteousness from me, and by Your law have mercy on me.

**118:30** I have chosen the way of truth; I have not forgotten Your judgments.

**118:31** I have clung to Your testimonies; Lord, do not put me to shame.

**118:32** I ran the way of Your commandments when You enlarged my heart.

**118:33** Lay down as law for me, Lord, the way of Your statutes, and I will seek it out continually.

**118:34** Give me understanding, and I will seek out Your law, and I will keep it with my whole heart.

**118:35** Guide me in the path of Your commandments, because I have desired it.

**118:36** Incline my heart to Your testimonies and not to greed.

**118:37** Turn my eyes away from seeing futility; in Your way give me life.

**118:38** Establish for Your servant regarding Your utterance, for the fear of You.

**118:39** Take away my reproach which I have dreaded, for Your judgments are good.

**118:40** Look, I have desired Your commandments; in Your righteousness give me life.

**118:41** And may Your mercy come upon me, Lord, Your salvation according to Your mercy.

**118:42** And I will answer those who reproach me with a word, because I have hoped in Your words.

**118:43** And do not utterly take away a word of truth from my mouth, because I have set my hope on Your judgments.

**118:44** And I will keep Your law continually, forever and to the age of the age.

**118:45** And I was walking in a wide place, because I have sought out Your commandments.

**118:46** And I was speaking of Your testimonies before kings, and I was not ashamed.

**118:47** And I was meditating on Your commandments, which You loved greatly.

**118:48** And I lifted up my hands toward Your commandments, which You loved greatly, and I was reflecting on Your statutes.

**118:49** Remember Your word concerning Your servants, in which You have caused me to hope.

**118:50** This comforted me in my humiliation, because I have not turned aside from Your utterance.

**118:51** The arrogant were acting lawlessly to the utmost, yet I did not turn aside from Your law.

**118:52** I remembered Your judgments from of old, Lord, and I was comforted.

**118:53** Dejection took hold of me because of sinners who forsake Your law.

**118:54** Your statutes were songs to me in the place of my sojourning.

**118:55** I remembered Your name in the night, Lord, and I kept Your law.

**118:56** This has become mine: I have sought out Your statutes.

**118:57** You are my portion, Lord; I said that I would keep Your commandments.

**118:58** I implored Your face with my whole heart; have mercy on me according to Your utterance.

**118:59** For I reasoned according to Your ways, and I turned my feet back to Your testimonies.

**118:60** I was prepared and was not troubled to keep Your commandments.

**118:61** Cords of sinners wrapped around me, and I did not forget Your law.

**118:62** At midnight I would rise up to give thanks to You for the judgments of Your righteousness.

**118:63** I am a companion of all those who fear You and keep Your commandments.

**118:64** All the earth is full of Your mercy, Lord; teach me Your statutes, Lord.

**118:65** You have shown goodness to Your servant, Lord, according to Your utterance.

**118:66** Teach me goodness and discipline and knowledge, because I have believed Your commandments.

**118:67** Before I was humbled, I erred; therefore I have kept Your utterance.

**118:68** You are good, Lord, and in Your goodness teach me Your statutes.

**118:69** The unrighteousness of the arrogant has been multiplied against me, but with my whole heart I will search out Your commandments.

**118:70** Their heart has curdled like milk, but I have meditated on Your law.

**118:71** It is good for me that You humbled me, so that I might learn Your statutes.

**118:72** The law of Your mouth is better for me than thousands of gold and silver.

**118:73** Your hands formed me and prepared me; give me understanding, and I will learn Your commandments.

**118:74** Those who fear You will see me and rejoice, because I have set my hope on Your words.

**118:75** I know, Lord, that in righteousness and truth Your judgments have humbled me.

**118:76** Now let Your mercy come to comfort me, and Your utterance to Your servant.

**118:77** Let Your compassions come to me, and I will live, because Your law is my meditation.

**118:78** Let the arrogant be put to shame, because they unjustly acted lawlessly against me; but I will reflect on Your commandments.

**118:79** Let those who fear You and those who know Your testimonies return.

**118:80** Let my heart become blameless in Your statutes, so that I may not be ashamed.

**118:81** My soul fails for Your salvation, and I have set my hope on Your word.

**118:86** All Your commandments are truth; they have persecuted me unjustly; help me.

**118:88** According to Your mercy I will live, and I will keep the testimonies of Your mouth.

**118:93** Forever I will certainly not forget Your statutes, because by them You have given me life, Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **118:38** — The Greek text includes the preposition "εἰς" before "Your utterance" ("στῆσον τῷ δούλῳ σου εἰς τὸ λόγιόν σου"), creating an elliptical construction compared to witnesses that make "Your utterance" the direct object of "establish."
- **118:47** — Here and in 118:48, the Greek text has the second-person verb "ἠγάπησας" ("You loved"), rather than the first-person "I loved" found in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **118:50** — The Greek text reads "οὐκ ἐξέκλινα" ("I have not turned aside from Your utterance"), rather than "Your utterance has given me life" as in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **118:75** — The Greek text pairs "δικαιοσύνῃ καὶ ἀληθείᾳ" ("in righteousness and truth") in the dative and makes "Your judgments" the subject of the third-person plural verb "ἐταπείνωσάν με" ("have humbled me"), rather than the second-person "You humbled me."
- **118:82** — The Greek text ends with the declarative third-person plural "Παρεκάλεσάν με" ("They have comforted me"), rather than the question "When will You comfort me?" found in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **118:95** — The Greek text contains only this clause ("τὰ δὲ μαρτύριά σου συνῆκα"), omitting the opening line about sinners waiting to destroy the psalmist found in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **118:102** — The Greek text reads "κλιμάτων" ("regions" or "directions") rather than "κριμάτων" ("judgments"), an ancient variant differing by a single letter.
- **118:114** — The Greek text reads "τὸν λαόν σου" ("Your people") rather than "τὸν λόγον σου" ("Your word") as in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
- **118:147** — The Greek text reads the third-person plural "προέφθασάν με" ("they came upon me") rather than the first-person "προέφθασα" ("I rose early"); in 118:148 it similarly reads "πρὸς σὲ ὀρθροῦν" ("rising early toward You") rather than "πρὸς ὄρθρον" ("before dawn").
- **118:176** — The Greek text reads "ζῆσον" ("give life to") rather than "ζήτησον" ("seek") found in other witnesses and the Hebrew MT.
