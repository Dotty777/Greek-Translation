# Psalm 150 — Saints Modern English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**150:1** Hallelujah. Praise God in His holy places; praise Him in the expanse of His power.

**150:2** Praise Him for His mighty deeds; praise Him according to the abundance of His greatness.

**150:3** Praise Him with the sound of a trumpet; praise Him with harp and lyre.

**150:4** Praise Him with tambourine and dancing; praise Him with strings and flute.

**150:5** Praise Him with clear-sounding cymbals; praise Him with cymbals of jubilation.

**150:6** Let every breath praise the Lord. Hallelujah.

## Translator Notes

- **150:1** — Greek en tois hagiois autou is plural and ambiguous in gender, meaning either 'in His holy places' or 'in His sanctuary' (neuter, matching the spatial parallel) or 'among His holy ones' (masculine).
- **150:4** — Greek organon literally means 'musical instrument' and is used in the Septuagint (here and in 151:2) for a wind instrument such as a flute or pipe.
- **150:6** — Greek pasa pnoe literally means 'every breath,' used as a poetic figure for every breathing creature.
