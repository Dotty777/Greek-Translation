# Psalm 107 — Saints Modern English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**107:1** A song of a psalm for David.

**107:2** My heart is ready, God, my heart is ready; I will sing and make melody in my glory.

**107:3** Awake, harp and lyre; I will awaken at dawn.

**107:4** I will give thanks to You among the peoples, Lord, and I will make melody to You among the nations;

**107:5** for great above the heavens is Your mercy, and up to the clouds is Your truth.

**107:6** Be exalted above the heavens, God, and let Your glory be over all the earth.

**107:7** So that Your beloved ones may be rescued, save with Your right hand and hear me.

**107:8** God spoke in His sanctuary: "I will be exalted and will divide Shechem, and I will measure out the Valley of Tents.

**107:9** Mine is Gilead, and Mine is Manasseh, and Ephraim is the protection of My head; Judah is My king;

**107:10** Moab is the basin of My hope; over Idumea I will stretch out My sandal; to Me the foreigners were subjected."

**107:11** Who will bring me into a fortified city? Who will lead me as far as Idumea?

**107:12** Is it not You, God, who rejected us? And will You not go out, God, with our armies?

**107:13** Give us help from affliction, and the salvation of man is futile.

**107:14** In God we will exercise power, and He Himself will bring our enemies to nothing.

## Translator Notes

- **107:3** — Greek ἐξεγερθήσομαι ὄρθρου means "I will awaken at dawn" (passive verb with genitive of time), in contrast to the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "I will awaken the dawn."
- **107:8** — The Septuagint reads "I will be exalted" (Ὑψωθήσομαι, vs. Hebrew "I will exult") and translates the place name Succoth literally as "the Valley of Tents" (τὴν κοιλάδα τῶν σκηνωμάτων).
- **107:10** — The Septuagint reads "basin of My hope" (λέβης τῆς ἐλπίδος μου, vs. Hebrew "washbasin"), uses the Greek geographical name Ἰδουμαία ("Idumea") for Hebrew Edom, and renders Hebrew "Philistines" with its standard term οἱ ἀλλόφυλοι ("the foreigners").
- **107:14** — Greek ἐξουδενώσει ("will bring to nothing" or "will treat with contempt") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "will tread down."
