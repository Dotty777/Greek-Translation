## Text

**79:1** For the end, concerning those who will be changed; a testimony for Asaph, a psalm concerning the Assyrian.

**79:2** You who shepherd Israel, pay attention, You who lead Joseph like sheep; You who sit upon the cherubim, appear.

**79:3** Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh, rouse Your might and come to save us.

**79:4** God, turn us back and make Your face shine, and we will be saved.

**79:5** Lord God of powers, how long are You angry at the prayer of Your servant?

**79:6** Will You feed us bread of tears and give us tears to drink by measure?

**79:7** You made us a contention to our neighbors, and our enemies mocked us.

**79:8** Lord God of powers, turn us back and make Your face shine, and we will be saved. Interlude.

**79:9** You transferred a vine out of Egypt; You drove out nations and planted it.

**79:10** You made a way before it, and planted its roots, and the land was filled.

**79:11** Its shadow covered mountains, and its climbing vines the cedars of God.

**79:12** It stretched out its branches as far as the sea, and its offshoots as far as the river.

**79:13** Why have You torn down its fence, so that all who pass along the road pluck it?

**79:14** A boar from the forest ravaged it, and a wild donkey grazed on it.

**79:15** God of powers, turn back now; look down from heaven and see, and visit this vine,

**79:16** and restore it, which Your right hand planted, and upon the son of man whom You strengthened for Yourself.

**79:17** It is burned with fire and dug up; at the rebuke of Your face they will perish.

**79:18** Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, and upon the son of man whom You strengthened for Yourself,

**79:19** and we will never depart from You; You will give us life, and we will call upon Your name.

**79:20** Lord God of powers, turn us back and make Your face shine, and we will be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **79:1** — The Greek superscription renders the Hebrew heading as "For the end," reads "concerning those who will be changed" where Hebrew has "lilies," and includes the Septuagint-distinct phrase "concerning the Assyrian."
- **79:5** — Greek ton dynameon ("of powers," also used for military forces or hosts) renders Hebrew tseva'ot ("of hosts"); here the Greek also reads "Your servant" where the Hebrew reads "Your people."
- **79:8** — "Interlude" translates Greek diapsalma, the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew selah indicating a musical pause; the Greek text also includes "Lord" here as in verses 5 and 20.
- **79:14** — Greek onos agrios means "a wild donkey," where the Hebrew text reads "whatever moves in the field."
- **79:16** — Greek katartisai ("restore") reads a verb where the Hebrew has a noun ("stock/root"), and the Septuagint reads "the son of man" here just as in verse 18, where the Hebrew has simply "son."
