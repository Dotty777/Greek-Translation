## Text

**141:1** Of understanding, for David, when he was in the cave; a prayer.

**141:2** With my voice I cried out to the Lord; with my voice I made supplication to the Lord.

**141:3** I will pour out my supplication before Him; my affliction I will declare before Him.

**141:4** When my spirit was failing from me, then You knew my paths; in this way in which I was walking, they hid a snare for me.

**141:5** I was looking closely to the right and watching, for there was no one who recognized me; escape perished from me, and there is no one who inquires after my soul.

**141:6** To You, Lord, I cried out, and I said, "You are my hope, my portion in the land of the living."

**141:7** Pay attention to my supplication, for I have been brought very low; rescue me from those who pursue me, for they have grown stronger than me.

**141:8** Bring my soul out of prison, so that I may give thanks to Your name, Lord; righteous ones will wait for me until You repay me.

## Translator Notes

- **141:1** — Greek "Syneseos" ("Of understanding") renders the Hebrew heading "maskil," and the dative "to Dauid" is literally "for David."
- **141:5** — Unlike the Hebrew MT imperatives ("Look... and see"), the LXX uses first-person imperfect verbs describing David's own searching, and this Greek witness reads "hoti" ("for/that") rather than "kai" ("and").
- **141:8** — The Greek text includes the vocative "Kyrie" ("Lord") and reads "hypomenousin" ("will wait for"), where the Hebrew MT reads "will surround."
