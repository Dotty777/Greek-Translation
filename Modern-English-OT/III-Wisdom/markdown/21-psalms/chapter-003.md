## Text

**3:1** A psalm for David, when he was fleeing from the presence of Absalom his son.

**3:2** Lord, why have those who afflict me multiplied? Many are rising up against me;

**3:3** many are saying to my soul, "There is no salvation in his God." Interlude.

**3:4** But You, Lord, are my sustainer, my glory, and the One who lifts up my head.

**3:5** With my voice I cried out to the Lord, and He heard me from His holy mountain. Interlude.

**3:6** I lay down and slept. I awoke, for the Lord will sustain me.

**3:7** I will not be afraid of tens of thousands of people who are attacking me all around.

**3:8** Arise, Lord; save me, my God; for You have struck all who are hostile to me without cause; You have shattered the teeth of sinners.

**3:9** Salvation belongs to the Lord, and Your blessing is upon Your people.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — In Septuagint versification, the psalm's superscription is counted as verse 1, advancing the remaining verse numbers by one relative to most English translations based on the Hebrew.
- **3:3** — This Greek text omits the pronoun auto ("for him") present in some Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew text, reading simply "There is no salvation in his God."
- **3:4** — Greek antilemptor ("sustainer" or "protector") replaces the Hebrew metaphor "a shield around me" and echoes the cognate verb antilempsetai ("will sustain") in 3:6.
- **3:8** — Greek mataios ("without cause" or "groundlessly") differs from the Hebrew text, which reads "on the cheek/jaw."
