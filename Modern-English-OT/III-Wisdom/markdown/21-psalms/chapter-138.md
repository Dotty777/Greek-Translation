## Text

**138:1** For the end; a psalm for David. Lord, You have examined me and known me;

**138:2** You have known my sitting down and my rising up; You have understood all my thoughts from afar;

**138:3** You have traced out my path and my measuring line, and You have foreseen all my ways,

**138:4** because there is no unjust word on my tongue; look, Lord, You have known all things,

**138:5** the last things and the ancient things; You formed me and placed Your hand upon me.

**138:6** Your knowledge has become wondrous beyond me; it has grown strong; I will by no means be equal to it.

**138:7** Where can I go from Your Spirit? And from Your presence where can I flee?

**138:8** If I ascend into heaven, You are there; if I descend into Hades, You are present;

**138:9** if I take up my wings straight ahead and dwell at the farthest parts of the sea,

**138:10** for even there Your hand will guide me, and Your right hand will hold me fast.

**138:11** And I said, "Surely darkness will trample me, and night is illumination in my delight."

**138:12** As its darkness is, so also is its light.

**138:13** For You acquired my inward parts, Lord; You took hold of me from my mother's womb.

**138:14** I will give thanks to You, because You have been shown fearfully wondrous; wondrous are Your works, and my soul knows it very well.

**138:15** My bone was not hidden from You, which You made in secret, nor my substance in the lowest parts of the earth.

**138:16** My eyes saw Your unfinished work, and in Your book all will be written; by day they will be formed, and no one is among them.

**138:17** But to me Your friends have been exceedingly honored, God; their dominions have grown exceedingly strong;

**138:18** I will count them, and they will be multiplied beyond sand; I awoke, and I am still with You.

**138:19** If You would kill sinners, God! Men of blood, turn away from me.

**138:20** Because You will speak concerning a thought; they will take Your cities in vain.

**138:21** Did I not hate those who hate You, Lord, and waste away over Your enemies?

**138:22** With complete hatred I hated them; they became enemies to me.

**138:23** Examine me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my paths;

**138:24** and see if You have seen lawless deeds in me, and guide me in an eternal way.

## Translator Notes

- **138:9** — The Greek text reads kat' orthon ("straight ahead" or "in a straight course"), whereas other manuscripts read kat' orthron ("at dawn").
- **138:12** — The Greek text preserves a shorter reading here, omitting the clauses found in other witnesses ("for darkness will not be darkened from You, and night will be illuminated as day"); the feminine pronoun "its" (autes) refers back to "night" in verse 11.
- **138:14** — The Greek verb ethaumastothes is second person singular, addressing God ("You have been shown fearfully wondrous"), rather than first person singular as in the Hebrew text.
- **138:16** — The Greek text reads to akatergaston sou eidosan hoi ophthalmoi mou ("My eyes saw Your unfinished work"), reversing the pronouns found in most other witnesses; hemeras may be understood as temporal genitive ("by day") or accusative plural ("for days").
- **138:20** — This difficult Septuagint verse reads ereis eis dialogismon ("You will speak concerning a thought") and tas poleis sou ("Your cities"), reflecting a different parsing of the Hebrew consonants.
- **138:24** — The Greek text reads ei ides anomias ("if You have seen lawless deeds"), where other manuscripts read ei hodos anomias ("if there is a way of lawlessness").
