## Text

**72:1** A psalm for Asaph. How good God is to Israel, to those who are upright in heart!

**72:2** But as for me, my feet were nearly shaken; my steps were almost poured out.

**72:3** For I was envious of the lawless, observing the peace of sinners.

**72:4** For there is no reluctance in their death, and firmness is in their scourge.

**72:5** They are not in the troubles of men, and with men they will not be scourged.

**72:6** Therefore arrogance has taken hold of them; they clothed themselves with their injustice and ungodliness.

**72:7** Their injustice will come out as from fat; they passed through into the disposition of the heart.

**72:8** They thought and spoke in wickedness; they spoke injustice toward the height.

**72:9** They set their mouth toward heaven, and their tongue passed across the earth.

**72:10** Therefore my people will turn back here, and full days will be found for them.

**72:11** And they said, "How has God known? And is there knowledge in the Most High?"

**72:12** Look, sinners—and they prosper; forever they have taken possession of wealth.

**72:13** And I said, "Surely in vain I have justified my heart, and washed my hands among the innocent,

**72:14** and I have been scourged all day long, and my reproof is for the mornings."

**72:15** If I said, "I will recount it this way," look, I have broken faith with the generation of Your sons.

**72:16** And I undertook to know; this was trouble before me,

**72:17** until I entered into the sanctuary of God and understood the end.

**72:18** Nevertheless, because of the deceits You appointed it for them.

**72:19** How have they come to desolation? Suddenly they failed; they perished because of their lawlessness.

**72:20** Like a dream of one waking up, Lord, in Your city You will despise their image.

**72:21** For my heart was gladdened, and my kidneys were changed;

**72:22** and I was despised and did not know; I became beastlike before You.

**72:23** Yet I am continually with You; You took hold of my right hand;

**72:24** in Your counsel You guided me, and with glory You received me.

**72:25** For what do I have in heaven? And from You, what have I desired upon the earth?

**72:26** My heart and my flesh have failed; God of my heart, and my portion is God forever.

**72:27** For look, those who distance themselves from You will perish; You have destroyed everyone who commits prostitution away from You.

**72:28** But for me, clinging to the Lord is good, to place my hope in the Lord, so as to declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **72:4** — The rare Greek noun ἀνάνευσις denotes throwing back the head in refusal or reluctance, while στερέωμα means 'firmness'; both differ notably from the Masoretic Text.
- **72:7** — Greek reads ἡ ἀδικία αὐτῶν ('their injustice'), whereas the Masoretic Text has 'their eye.'
- **72:18** — This critical text preserves only the first clause of the verse, with the elliptical ἔθου αὐτοῖς (literally 'You set/appointed for them') and διὰ τὰς δολιότητας ('because of the deceits,' corresponding to MT 'in slippery places').
- **72:21** — Greek reads ηὐφράνθη ('was gladdened') and ἠλλοιώθησαν ('were changed'), contrasting with the Masoretic Text's 'was embittered' and 'was pierced'; 'kidneys' (νεφροί) is a literal biblical idiom for the inner being.
- **72:28** — This Greek text reads 'the Lord' twice, 'Your praises' (MT 'Your works'), and includes the closing phrase 'in the gates of the daughter of Zion,' which is absent from the Masoretic Text.
