## Text

**71:1** For Solomon. God, give Your judgment to the king, and Your righteousness to the son of the king,

**71:2** to judge Your people in righteousness and Your poor in justice.

**71:3** Let the mountains take up peace for Your people, and the hills.

**71:4** In righteousness he will judge the poor of the people; and You will save the sons of the needy, and he will humble a false accuser.

**71:5** And he will remain alongside the sun, and before the moon through generations of generations.

**71:6** And he will come down like rain upon a fleece, and like drops dripping upon the earth.

**71:7** Righteousness will spring up in his days, and an abundance of peace until the moon is taken away.

**71:8** And he will have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the inhabited world.

**71:9** Before him Ethiopians will fall down, and his enemies will lick dust.

**71:10** Kings of Tarshish and the islands will offer gifts; kings of the Arabs and Saba will present gifts.

**71:11** And all the kings will bow down to him; all the nations will serve him.

**71:12** For he rescued a poor man from the hand of a mighty one, and a needy man for whom there was no helper.

**71:13** He will spare the poor and the needy, and he will save the souls of the needy.

**71:14** From usury and from injustice he will redeem their souls, and their name will be precious before him.

**71:15** And he will live, and there will be given to him from the gold of Arabia; and they will pray for him continually; all day long they will bless him.

**71:16** There will be a support in the earth upon the tops of the mountains; his fruit will be lifted high above Lebanon, and they will blossom from a city like grass of the earth.

**71:17** May his name be blessed forever; before the sun his name will endure; and in him all the tribes of the earth will be blessed; all the nations will call him blessed.

**71:18** Blessed is the Lord God of Israel, who alone does wondrous things,

**71:19** and blessed is the name of His glory forever, and forever and ever; and all the earth will be filled with His glory. May it be so, may it be so.

**71:20** The hymns of David, the son of Jesse, have come to an end.

## Translator Notes

- **71:4** — The Greek places 'in righteousness' at the opening of v. 4 rather than v. 3 and reads second-person singular σώσεις ('You will save'), momentarily addressing God directly; συκοφάντην literally denotes a false accuser or extortioner.
- **71:9** — LXX renders Hebrew 'desert-dwellers' as Αἰθίοπες ('Ethiopians'), as also in 73:14, and in v. 10 reads 'kings of the Arabs and Saba' where the Masoretic Text has 'Sheba and Seba.'
- **71:10** — Greek reads "Arabs and Saba," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Sheba and Seba."
- **71:14** — Greek reads τόκου ('usury') and τὸ ὄνομα αὐτῶν ('their name'), where the Masoretic Text has 'oppression' and 'their blood.'
- **71:16** — Greek reads στήριγμα ('a support'), reflecting a different understanding of the Masoretic Text's 'abundance of grain'; the pronoun αὐτοῦ ('his' or 'its') can refer either to the king or to the support.
