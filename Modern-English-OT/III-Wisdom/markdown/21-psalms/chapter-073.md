## Text

**73:1** Of understanding, for Asaph. Why have You rejected us, God, to the end? Has Your wrath grown angry against the sheep of Your pasture?

**73:2** Remember Your congregation, which You acquired from the beginning; You redeemed the rod of Your inheritance, this Mount Zion in which You dwelt.

**73:3** Lift up Your hands against their acts of arrogance to the end—all that the enemy has done wickedly in Your holy places.

**73:4** And those who hate You boasted in the midst of Your feast,

**73:5** as at the entrance above; as in a thicket of trees, with axes they cut down

**73:6** its doors; together, with hatchet and chisel they smashed it down.

**73:7** They burned Your sanctuary with fire; to the ground they profaned the dwelling place of Your name.

**73:8** They said in their heart, their kin together, "Come, and let us put an end to the feasts of the Lord from the earth."

**73:9** We have not seen our signs; there is no longer a prophet, and He will know us no longer.

**73:10** How long, God, will the enemy taunt? Will the adversary provoke Your name to the end?

**73:11** Why do You turn away Your hand, and Your right hand from the midst of Your bosom, to the end?

**73:12** Yet our King before the age worked salvation in the midst of the earth.

**73:13** You strengthened the sea in Your power; You crushed the heads of the dragons upon the water.

**73:14** You gave him as food to the Ethiopian peoples.

**73:15** You split open springs and torrents.

**73:16** Yours is the day, and Yours is the night; You prepared sun and moon.

**73:17** You made all the boundaries of the earth; summer and spring You made.

**73:18** Remember this creation of Yours. An enemy has taunted the Lord, and a foolish people has provoked His name.

**73:19** Do not hand over to the wild beasts a soul that gives thanks to You; do not forget the souls of Your needy to the end.

**73:20** Look upon Your covenant, for the darkened ones of the earth have been filled with houses of lawlessness.

**73:21** Do not let one who is humbled and put to shame be turned away; the poor and the needy will praise Your name.

**73:22** Rise up, God, judge Your case; remember the taunts against You, those from a fool all day long.

**73:23** Do not forget the voice of Your suppliants; may the arrogance of those who hate You ascend continually to You.

## Translator Notes

- **73:4** — This critical text omits the second clause of v. 4 found in some witnesses ('they set up their signs as signs and did not know'); v. 5 then renders an obscure Hebrew phrase literally as 'as at the entrance above.'
- **73:9** — Greek καὶ ἡμᾶς οὐ γνώσεται ἔτι ('and He will know us no longer') differs from the Masoretic Text ('none among us knows how long'); the unexpressed subject of γνώσεται could refer either to God ('He') or to a prophet ('he').
- **73:14** — This critical text omits the opening clause of v. 14 ('You shattered the heads of the dragon') and the closing clause of v. 15 ('You dried up the rivers of Etham'), leaving αὐτὸν ('him') in v. 14 without an explicit singular antecedent; 'Ethiopian' again renders Hebrew 'desert-dwellers.'
- **73:19** — Greek reads ψυχὴν ἐξομολογουμένην σοι ('a soul that gives thanks to You') where the Masoretic Text has 'the life of your dove.'
- **73:23** — Greek reads τῶν ἱκετῶν σου ('of Your suppliants') where the Masoretic Text has 'of your adversaries,' and uses the optative ἀναβαίη with πρὸς σέ ('may ... ascend to You').
