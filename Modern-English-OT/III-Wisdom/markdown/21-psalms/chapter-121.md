## Text

**121:1** A song of the steps. For David. I rejoiced over those who said to me, "We will go to the house of the Lord."

**121:2** Our feet were standing in the courts of Jerusalem.

**121:3** Jerusalem, being built as a city whose fellowship is together.

**121:4** For there the tribes of the Lord went up, a testimony of Israel, to give thanks to the name of the Lord,

**121:5** because there thrones sat for judgment, thrones over the house of David,

**121:6** and prosperity for those who love you. Ask now for the things pertaining to peace for Jerusalem.

**121:7** May your peace now be in your strength, and prosperity in your fortified towers,

**121:8** for the sake of my brothers and my neighbors. And I was speaking peace concerning you;

**121:9** for the sake of the house of the Lord our God, I sought on your behalf.

## Translator Notes

- **121:3** — Greek hēs hē metochē autēs epi to auto literally reads "whose fellowship (or participation) is together," contrasting with the Hebrew image of a city "bound firmly together."
- **121:6** — The Greek source text reverses the two clauses found in most Greek witnesses and the Hebrew text, placing "and prosperity for those who love you" first and using the dative tē Ierousalēm ("for Jerusalem").
- **121:7** — The Greek source text reads "your peace" (adding "your"), renders Hebrew "rampart/wall" as dynamei ("strength"), and uses the rare compound pyrgobaresin ("fortified towers" or "citadels").
- **121:8** — The punctuation and the conjunction de ("And," where other witnesses read dē, "indeed") attach "for the sake of my brothers and my neighbors" to the blessing of verse 7 rather than to "I was speaking peace concerning you."
- **121:9** — The Greek source text reads simply exezētēsa soi ("I sought on your behalf"), omitting the direct object agatha ("good things") found in other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew text.
