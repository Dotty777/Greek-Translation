## Text

**14:1** A psalm for David. Lord, who will sojourn in Your tent? And who will dwell on Your holy mountain?

**14:2** One who walks without blemish and works righteousness, speaking truth in his heart;

**14:3** who has not deceived with his tongue, nor done evil to his neighbor, and has not taken up a reproach against those nearest to him.

**14:4** One who acts wickedly is despised before him, but he honors those who fear the Lord. He swears to his neighbor and does not break faith.

**14:5** He has not given his money at interest, and he has not taken bribes against the innocent. The one who does these things will never be shaken.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — "has not deceived" follows the Greek verb ἐδόλωσεν (referring to deceitful speech), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Psalm 15:3 has a verb meaning "slander."
- **14:4** — "to his neighbor" translates the Septuagint reading τῷ πλησίον αὐτοῦ, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Psalm 15:4 reads "to his own hurt."
