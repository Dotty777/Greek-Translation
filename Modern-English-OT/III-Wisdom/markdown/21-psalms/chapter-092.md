## Text

**92:1** For the day before the Sabbath, when the earth was inhabited; a praise of a song for David. The Lord reigned; He clothed Himself with splendor. The Lord clothed Himself with power and girded Himself, for indeed He made the inhabited world firm, which will not be shaken.

**92:2** Your throne is prepared from then; from eternity You are.

**92:3** The rivers lifted up, Lord; the rivers lifted up their voices.

**92:4** From the voices of many waters, wondrous are the surges of the sea; wondrous in the heights is the Lord.

**92:5** Your testimonies have been proven exceedingly trustworthy; holiness is fitting for Your house, Lord, for length of days.

## Translator Notes

- **92:1** — The Septuagint superscription (absent from the Hebrew MT) assigns this psalm to the day before the Sabbath (Friday), linking it to the completion of creation when the earth was inhabited.
- **92:3** — The Greek text has two clauses with plural "voices," omitting the third clause found in the Hebrew MT.
- **92:4** — Greek ἀπό ("from") renders a Hebrew preposition that can be taken causally ("from") or comparatively ("more than"); μετεωρισμοί refers to the rising swells or surges of the sea.
