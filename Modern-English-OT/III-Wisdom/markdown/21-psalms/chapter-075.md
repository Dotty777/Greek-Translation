## Text

**75:1** For the end, with hymns; a psalm for Asaph, a song, concerning the Assyrian.

**75:2** God is known in Judea; in Israel His name is great.

**75:3** And His place was in peace, and His dwelling place in Zion;

**75:4** there He shattered the powers of the bows, shield and sword and war; there He will break the horns. Interlude.

**75:5** You shine wondrously from eternal mountains.

**75:6** All the senseless in heart were troubled; they slept their sleep, and all the men of wealth found nothing in their hands.

**75:7** At Your rebuke, God of Jacob, those who mounted the horses slumbered.

**75:8** You are fearsome, and who will withstand You because of Your wrath?

**75:9** From heaven You hurled judgment; the earth feared and grew still,

**75:10** when God arose for judgment, to save all the meek in heart. Interlude.

**75:11** For the thought of man will give thanks to You, and a remnant of thought will keep a festival to You.

**75:12** Make vows and pay them to the Lord our God; all those around Him will bring gifts

**75:13** to Him who is fearsome and takes away the spirits of rulers, fearsome among the kings of the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **75:1** — LXX adds "concerning the Assyrian" to the superscription (absent from MT), linking the psalm to deliverance from Assyria.
- **75:4** — LXX reads "the powers of the bows" (MT: "flashing arrows"), and the Greek critical text includes the additional clause "there He will break the horns" (absent from MT).
- **75:6** — LXX reads "senseless in heart" and "all the men of wealth found nothing in their hands" (MT: "stouthearted" and "none of the men of might found their hands").
- **75:9** — "You hurled" translates Greek ēkontisas (from akontizō, "hurl/shoot," attested in Sinaiticus), whereas other witnesses read ēkoutisas ("caused to be heard").
- **75:11** — LXX renders the underlying Hebrew as "thought" (enthymion) and "will keep a festival" (heortasei), whereas MT reads "wrath" and "gird Yourself."
