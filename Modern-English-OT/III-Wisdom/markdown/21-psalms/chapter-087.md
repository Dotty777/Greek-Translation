## Text

**87:1** A song of a psalm for the sons of Korah. For the end, concerning Mahalath, to answer; of understanding, for Heman the Israelite.

**87:2** Lord, God of my salvation, by day I cried out and in the night before You.

**87:3** Let my prayer enter before You; incline Your ear to my supplication, Lord.

**87:4** For my soul has been filled with evils, and my life has drawn near to Hades.

**87:5** I was counted with those going down into a pit; I became like a helpless man,

**87:6** free among the dead, like wounded men thrown down, sleeping in a tomb, whom You remembered no longer, and they were thrust away from Your hand.

**87:7** They placed me in the lowest pit, in dark places and in the shadow of death.

**87:8** Your fury pressed heavily upon me, and You brought all Your billows upon me. Interlude.

**87:9** You put my acquaintances far from me; they made me an abomination to themselves. I was handed over and did not go out.

**87:10** My eyes grew weak from poverty, and I cried out to You, Lord, all day long; I spread out my hands.

**87:11** Will You perform wonders for the dead? Or will physicians raise them up, and will they praise You?

**87:12** Will anyone recount Your mercy in a tomb, and Your truth in destruction?

**87:13** Will Your wonders be known in the darkness, and Your righteousness in a forgotten land?

**87:14** And I, Lord, cried out to You, and in the morning my prayer will come before You.

**87:15** Why, Lord, do You reject my prayer and turn Your face away from me?

**87:16** I am poor and in labors from my youth; yet having been exalted, I was humbled and utterly perplexed.

**87:17** Your wrath passed over me, and Your terrors troubled me.

**87:18** They surrounded me like water all day long; they hemmed me in together.

**87:19** You put a friend far from me, and my acquaintances because of misery.

## Translator Notes

- **87:1** — The Greek superscription renders the Hebrew heading literally as "for the end," "to answer," and "of understanding," transliterates Mahalath, and calls Heman "the Israelite" (where the Hebrew has "the Ezrahite," and likewise for Ethan in 88:1).
- **87:7** — Greek reads third-person plural "They placed me" here and "they made me" in v. 9 (where the Hebrew has second-person singular "You"). In v. 8, "Interlude" translates Greek diapsalma, the Septuagint musical direction corresponding to Hebrew Selah.
- **87:11** — Greek reads "physicians" (iatroi), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants for "shades/departed spirits" (rephaim), followed by the active verb "raise [them] up."
- **87:16** — Greek reads "in labors" and "having been exalted, I was humbled and utterly perplexed," diverging markedly from the Hebrew ("about to die; I suffer your terrors; I am helpless"). Similarly, in v. 15 Greek reads "my prayer" where the Hebrew has "my soul."
- **87:19** — Greek closes the psalm with "because of misery" (apo talaiporias), whereas the Hebrew ends with "darkness."
