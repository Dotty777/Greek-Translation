## Text

**112:1** Hallelujah. Praise the Lord, servants; praise His name.

**112:2** May the name of the Lord be blessed from now and forever.

**112:3** From the rising of the sun to its setting, the name of the Lord is praised.

**112:4** High above all the nations is the Lord; above the heavens is His glory.

**112:5** Who is like the Lord our God? The One who dwells on high,

**112:6** and looks upon the lowly things in heaven and on earth;

**112:7** the One who raises a poor man from the earth and lifts up a needy man from the dung heap,

**112:8** to seat him with rulers, with the rulers of His people;

**112:9** the One who makes a barren woman dwell in a house, a mother of rejoicing children.

## Translator Notes

- **112:1** — Greek addresses "servants" (paides, also "children") without "of the Lord" and ends with "His name" rather than "the name of the Lord."
- **112:3** — Greek uses the present passive indicative "is praised" rather than a verbal adjective ("is to be praised").
- **112:8** — The pronoun in "His people" could alternatively refer to the needy man ("his people").
- **112:9** — The Greek participle "rejoicing" is genitive plural modifying "children," whereas the Hebrew MT and some Greek witnesses modify "mother" ("a joyful mother of children").
