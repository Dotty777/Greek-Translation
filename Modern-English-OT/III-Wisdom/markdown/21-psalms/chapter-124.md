## Text

**124:1** A song of the steps. Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion; the one who dwells in Jerusalem will never be shaken.

**124:2** Mountains are around her, and the Lord is around His people from now on and forever.

**124:3** For He will not leave the rod of the sinners upon the allotment of the righteous, so that the righteous may not stretch out their hands in lawlessness.

**124:4** Do good, Lord, to those who are good and to those who are upright in heart.

**124:5** But those who turn aside into twisted ways the Lord will lead away with those who practice lawlessness. Peace upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **124:1** — Greek construes "Jerusalem" (Ierousalēm) as the object of the participle "the one who dwells" (ho katoikōn) at the end of v. 1, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text opens v. 2 with "Jerusalem." "Will never be shaken" translates the idiomatic ou saleuthēsetai eis ton aiōna.
- **124:3** — Greek uses the transitive future active aphēsei ("He will not leave," with the Lord as implied subject) and plural "of the sinners" (tōn hamartōlōn), rather than the Hebrew "the scepter of wickedness shall not rest."
- **124:5** — Greek strangalias literally denotes twisted knots, entanglements, or crooked ways, with no possessive pronoun ("their").
