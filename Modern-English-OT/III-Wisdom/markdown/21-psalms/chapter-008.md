## Text

**8:1** For the end, concerning the winepresses; a psalm for David.

**8:2** Lord, our Lord, how wondrous is Your name in all the earth; for Your magnificence has been lifted up above the heavens.

**8:3** Out of the mouth of infants and nursing babies You have prepared praise on account of Your enemies, to destroy an enemy and an avenger.

**8:4** For I will see the heavens, the works of Your fingers, the moon and stars that You founded.

**8:5** What is man, that You remember him, or a son of man, that You attend to him?

**8:6** You made him a little lower than angels; with glory and honor You crowned him.

**8:7** And You appointed him over the works of Your hands; You subjected all things beneath his feet,

**8:8** sheep and all cattle, and also the beasts of the field,

**8:9** the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, the things that pass through the paths of the seas.

**8:10** Lord, our Lord, how wondrous is Your name in all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — "For the end" (eis to telos) and "concerning the winepresses" (hyper ton lenon) reflect the Greek rendering of Hebrew musical rubrics usually understood as "for the choirmaster" and "according to the Gittith."
- **8:3** — The Greek reads "You have prepared praise" (quoted in Matthew 21:16), whereas the Hebrew text reads "You have established strength."
- **8:6** — The Greek reads "than angels" (quoted in Hebrews 2:7), where the Hebrew has Elohim ("God" or "heavenly beings"). The Greek phrase brachy ti can mean either "a little" in rank or "for a little while" in duration.
