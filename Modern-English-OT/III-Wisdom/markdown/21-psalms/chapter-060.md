## Text

**60:1** For the end, with hymns; of David.

**60:2** Hear my supplication; attend to my prayer.

**60:3** From the ends of the earth I cried out to You when my heart grew despondent. On a rock You lifted me up; You guided me,

**60:4** because You became my hope, a tower of strength from before an enemy.

**60:5** I will sojourn in Your tent forever. Interlude. I will be sheltered in the shelter of Your wings.

**60:6** For You, God, heard my prayers; You gave an inheritance to those who fear Your name.

**60:7** You will add days to the days of the king; his years will be until the day of generation and generation.

**60:8** He will abide forever before God; who among them will seek out His mercy and truth?

**60:9** Thus I will sing praise to Your name forever and ever, so that I may pay my vows day after day.

## Translator Notes

- **60:2** — The Greek critical text omits the vocative 'O God' found in some manuscripts and in the Hebrew text.
- **60:3** — Greek reads two past indicatives ('On a rock You lifted me up; You guided me') rather than the Hebrew petition ('Lead me to the rock that is higher than I').
- **60:5** — The Greek critical text places 'Interlude' (diapsalma) in the middle of the verse rather than at the end as in the Hebrew text.
- **60:8** — Greek reads the question 'who among them will seek out His mercy and truth?' (including the genitive plural pronoun auton) where the Hebrew text reads a petition to appoint steadfast love and faithfulness to preserve the king.
