## Text

**101:1** A prayer for the poor man, when he becomes despondent and pours out his petition before the Lord.

**101:2** Listen, Lord, to my prayer, and let my cry come to You.

**101:3** Do not turn Your face away from me; on whatever day I am afflicted, incline Your ear to me; on whatever day I call upon You, listen to me quickly.

**101:4** For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones have been parched like firewood.

**101:5** I have been struck like grass and my heart has withered, because I forgot to eat my bread.

**101:6** Because of the sound of my groaning, my bone has clung to my flesh.

**101:7** I came to resemble a desert pelican; I became like a night-raven in a ruined house.

**101:8** I lay awake and became like a solitary sparrow on a rooftop.

**101:9** All day long my enemies reproached me, and those who praised me swore against me.

**101:10** For I ate ashes like bread, and mixed my drink with weeping,

**101:11** because of Your anger and Your fury, for You lifted me up and dashed me down.

**101:12** My days have declined like a shadow, and I have withered like grass.

**101:13** But You, Lord, remain forever, and Your remembrance to generation and generation.

**101:14** You will arise and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to have compassion on her, for the time has come.

**101:15** For Your servants have taken pleasure in her stones, and they will have compassion on her dust.

**101:16** And the nations will fear Your name, Lord, and all the kings Your glory.

**101:17** For the Lord will build Zion, and He will appear in His glory.

**101:18** He looked upon the prayer of the humble, and He did not despise their petition.

**101:19** Let this be written for another generation, and a people being created will praise the Lord.

**101:20** For He looked out from His holy height; the Lord looked from heaven upon the earth,

**101:21** to hear the groaning of those who are shackled, to release the sons of those put to death,

**101:22** to declare in Zion the name of the Lord and His praise in Jerusalem,

**101:23** when peoples are gathered together, and kingdoms, to serve the Lord.

**101:24** He answered Him in the way of his strength, "Declare to me the fewness of my days.

**101:25** Do not take me up at the midpoint of my days; Your years are through generation of generations.

**101:26** At the beginning You, Lord, founded the earth, and the heavens are the works of Your hands.

**101:27** They will perish, but You endure; and all of them will grow old like a garment, and like a cloak You will roll them up, and they will be changed;

**101:28** but You are the same, and Your years will not fail.

**101:29** The sons of Your servants will settle, and their offspring will be guided straight forever."

## Translator Notes

- **101:4** — Greek φρύγιον ("firewood", or in later usage a roasting pan) is a rare term paired with συνεφρύγησαν ("were parched") in a wordplay; the Masoretic Text reads "like a hearth."
- **101:9** — Greek οἱ ἐπαινοῦντές με ("those who praised me") takes the underlying Hebrew root in the sense of "praise," whereas the Masoretic Text is usually understood as "those who deride/rage against me."
- **101:21** — Greek τοὺς υἱοὺς τῶν τεθανατωμένων ("the sons of those put to death") literally translates a Hebrew idiom meaning those doomed to die.
- **101:24** — Greek ἀπεκρίθη ("He answered") differs from the Masoretic Text ("He weakened/afflicted"), introducing vv. 24-29 as direct speech to the Lord; the pronoun αὐτοῦ ("his") can also be read as referring to the Lord's strength ("His").
- **101:26** — The Septuagint includes Κύριε ("Lord") here and reads ἑλίξεις ("You will roll them up") in v. 27, the wording quoted in Hebrews 1:10-12.
