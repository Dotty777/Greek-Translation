## Text

**52:1** For the end, concerning Mahalath: of understanding, for David.

**52:2** A fool said in his heart, "There is no God." They acted corruptly and became abominable in lawless deeds; there is no one who does good.

**52:3** God looked down from heaven upon the sons of men to see whether there is anyone who understands or seeks out God.

**52:4** All turned aside; together they became useless; there is no one doing good, there is not even one.

**52:5** Will not all who work lawlessness know, those who eat My people as a meal of bread? They did not call upon God.

**52:6** There they will fear a fear where there was no fear, because God scattered the bones of people-pleasers; they were put to shame, because God despised them.

**52:7** Who will give from Zion the salvation of Israel? When the Lord turns back the captivity of His people, Jacob will exult, and Israel will be glad.

## Translator Notes

- **52:1** — Greek "maeleth" transliterates the obscure Hebrew musical term "Mahalath," whose exact meaning is uncertain.
- **52:5** — Greek "brosei artou" ("as a meal of bread," lit. "with an eating of bread") renders the Hebrew idiom "as they eat bread"; "My people" reflects God's voice following verse 3.
- **52:6** — Greek "anthropareskon" ("people-pleasers"; cf. Eph 6:6; Col 3:22) differs sharply from the Hebrew MT ("the one who encamps against you"), and "kateischynthesan" ("they were put to shame") is third-person passive rather than second-person active.
- **52:7** — Greek "tis dosei" ("Who will give...?") literally renders the Hebrew wish idiom "mi yitten" ("Oh, that...!"), and "aichmalosian" specifically means "captivity."
