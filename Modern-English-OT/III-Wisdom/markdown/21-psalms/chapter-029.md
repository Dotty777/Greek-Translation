## Text

**29:1** For the end; a psalm of a song of the dedication of the house, of David.

**29:2** I will exalt You, Lord, because You upheld me and did not make my enemies glad over me.

**29:3** Lord my God, I cried out to You, and You healed me;

**29:4** Lord, You brought up my soul from Hades; You saved me from those going down into a pit.

**29:5** Sing praise to the Lord, His devout ones, and give thanks to the remembrance of His holiness.

**29:6** For wrath is in His anger, and life is in His will; in the evening weeping will lodge, and in the morning, exultation.

**29:7** As for me, I said in my prosperity, "I will by no means be shaken forever."

**29:8** Lord, in Your will grant power to my beauty; but You turned away Your face, and I became troubled.

**29:9** To You, Lord, I will cry out, and to my God I will make supplication.

**29:10** What profit is there in my blood, when I go down into corruption? Will dust give thanks to You, or will it declare Your truth?

**29:11** The Lord heard and had mercy on me; the Lord became my helper.

**29:12** You turned my mourning into joy for me; You tore apart my sackcloth and wrapped me in gladness,

**29:13** so that my glory may sing praise to You and I may by no means be pierced with grief. Lord my God, I will give thanks to You forever.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Greek eis to telos ("For the end") is the standard Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew musical heading often translated "for the choir director."
- **29:6** — The Greek reads "wrath is in His anger," whereas the Hebrew reads "His anger is for a moment."
- **29:8** — The Greek text uses the imperative paraschou ("grant") and reads "to my beauty," whereas the Hebrew reads "You made my mountain stand strong."
- **29:11** — The Greek uses past-tense indicative verbs ("heard," "had mercy," "became"), whereas the Hebrew uses imperatives ("Hear," "be gracious," "be").
- **29:13** — Greek katanugo literally means "be pierced" or "feel sharp grief/compunction," whereas the Hebrew reads "be silent."
