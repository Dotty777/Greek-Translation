## Text

**105:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, because He is good, because His mercy is forever.

**105:2** Who will speak of the mighty deeds of the Lord, make all His praises heard?

**105:3** Blessed are those who keep justice and do righteousness at all times.

**105:4** Remember us, Lord, in the favor toward Your people; visit us with Your salvation,

**105:5** to look upon the goodness of Your chosen ones, to rejoice in the joy of Your nation, to be praised with Your inheritance.

**105:6** We have sinned with our fathers; we have acted lawlessly, we have done wrong.

**105:7** Our fathers in Egypt did not understand Your wonders, and they did not remember the abundance of Your mercy, and they rebelled as they went up at the Red Sea.

**105:8** And He saved them for the sake of His name, to make known His mighty power.

**105:9** And He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; and He led them in the deep as in a wilderness.

**105:10** And He saved them from the hands of those who hated them, and He redeemed them from the hand of an enemy.

**105:11** And water covered those who oppressed them; not one of them was left.

**105:12** And they believed in His words, and they praised His praise.

**105:13** They hurried, they forgot His works; they did not wait for His counsel.

**105:14** And they craved a craving in the wilderness, and they tested God in a waterless place.

**105:15** And He gave them their request, and He sent fullness into their soul.

**105:16** And they provoked Moses to anger in the camp, and Aaron, the holy one of the Lord.

**105:17** The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered over the assembly of Abiram.

**105:18** And fire was kindled in their assembly, and a flame burned up sinners.

**105:19** And they made a calf at Horeb, and they worshiped the carved image.

**105:20** And they exchanged their glory for a likeness of a calf eating grass.

**105:21** They forgot God who saves them, who did great things in Egypt,

**105:22** wondrous things in the land of Ham and fearsome things by the Red Sea.

**105:23** And He said He would destroy them, if Moses, His chosen one, had not stood in the breach before Him, that He might turn away from the fury of His anger, so as not to destroy them.

**105:24** And they despised a desirable land, and they did not believe His word.

**105:25** And they grumbled in their tents; they did not listen to the voice of the Lord.

**105:26** And He lifted up His hand against them, to cast them down in the wilderness,

**105:27** and to cast down their offspring among the nations and to scatter them in the lands.

**105:28** And they were initiated to Baal-peor, and they ate sacrifices of the dead.

**105:29** And they provoked Him with their practices, and destruction was multiplied upon them.

**105:30** And Phinehas stood up and made atonement, and the plague ceased.

**105:31** And it was counted to him as righteousness, to generation and generation, forever.

**105:32** And they provoked anger at the water of Contention, and Moses was harmed on account of them,

**105:33** because they embittered his spirit, and he spoke distinctly with his lips.

**105:34** They did not destroy the nations of which the Lord spoke,

**105:35** and they mingled among the nations and learned their works.

**105:36** And they served their carved images, and it became a snare to them.

**105:37** And they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to the demons,

**105:38** and they shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters; they sacrificed to the carved images of Canaan, and the land was polluted with murder by the blood,

**105:39** and it was defiled by their works; and they prostituted themselves in their practices.

**105:40** And the Lord was angered with fury against His people, and He abhorred His inheritance.

**105:41** And He gave them over into the hands of nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.

**105:42** And their enemies oppressed them, and they were humbled under their hands.

**105:43** Many times He rescued them, but they rebelled in their counsel, and they were humbled in their lawless deeds.

**105:44** And He looked when they were oppressed, when He heard their plea.

**105:45** And He remembered His covenant, and He relented according to the abundance of His mercy,

**105:46** and He gave them over to compassion before all who had captured them.

**105:47** Save us, Lord our God, and gather us together from the nations, to give thanks to Your holy name, to boast in Your praise.

**105:48** Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, from eternity and to eternity; and all the people will say, "So be it."

## Translator Notes

- **105:7** — Greek reads ἀναβαίνοντες ("as they went up"), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants than the Masoretic Text's "by the sea."
- **105:15** — Greek πλησμονήν means "fullness" or "satiety," in contrast to the Masoretic Text's "leanness" or "wasting disease."
- **105:23** — The Greek text here reads ἀπὸ θυμοῦ ὀργῆς αὐτοῦ ("from the fury of His anger," echoing Exodus 32:12), whereas other Greek witnesses read τὴν ὀργὴν αὐτοῦ ("His wrath").
- **105:32** — Greek ὕδατος Ἀντιλογίας ("water of Contention," singular) translates the Hebrew place name Meribah ("waters of Meribah") rather than transliterating it.
- **105:33** — In context, τὸ πνεῦμα αὐτοῦ ("his spirit") most naturally refers to Moses, though some interpret it as God's Spirit ("His Spirit"). Greek διέστειλεν literally means "spoke distinctly" or "pronounced," differing from the Masoretic Text's "spoke rashly."
