## Text

**49:1** A psalm for Asaph. The God of gods, the Lord, spoke and called the earth from the rising of the sun and as far as sunset.

**49:2** Out of Zion is the splendor of His beauty.

**49:3** God will come visibly, our God, and He will not keep silent; fire will burn before Him, and around Him is a very fierce tempest.

**49:4** He will summon heaven above and the earth, to judge His people.

**49:5** Gather to Him His devout ones, those who make His covenant over sacrifices.

**49:6** And the heavens will proclaim His righteousness, for God is judge. Interlude.

**49:7** "Hear, My people, and I will speak to you; Israel, and I testify to you: I am God, your God.

**49:8** Not because of your sacrifices will I rebuke you, and your whole burnt offerings are continually before Me.

**49:9** I will not accept calves from your house, nor male goats from your flocks.

**49:10** For Mine are all the wild beasts of the forest, livestock on the mountains and oxen.

**49:11** I know all the birds of heaven, and the beauty of the field is with Me.

**49:12** If I become hungry, I will certainly not tell you; for Mine is the world and its fullness.

**49:13** Will I eat the flesh of bulls? Or will I drink the blood of goats?

**49:14** Sacrifice to God a sacrifice of praise, and pay your vows to the Most High;

**49:15** and call upon Me in a day of distress, and I will deliver you, and you will glorify Me." Interlude.

**49:16** But to the sinner God said, "Why do you recount My statutes, and take up My covenant through your mouth?

**49:17** Yet you hated discipline and kept casting My words behind you.

**49:18** If you saw a thief, you ran along with him, and with adulterers you set your portion.

**49:19** Your mouth multiplied evil, and your tongue wove deceit.

**49:20** Sitting down, you spoke against your brother, and against the son of your mother you placed a stumbling block.

**49:21** These things you did, and I kept silent; you supposed lawlessness, that I would be like you. I will rebuke you and set them before your face.

**49:22** Understand these things now, you who forget God, otherwise He will snatch you away, and there will be no one who rescues.

**49:23** A sacrifice of praise will glorify Me, and there is the way by which I will show him the salvation of God."

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — The Greek reads third-person pronouns ('to Him ... His ... His') rather than the first-person pronouns ('to Me ... My ... My') of the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **49:10** — Greek 'livestock on the mountains and oxen' reflects reading the Hebrew word behind 'a thousand' (eleph) as 'oxen' (alaphim).
- **49:11** — The Greek reads 'birds of heaven' (Hebrew: 'of the mountains') and 'the beauty of the field' (Hebrew: 'what moves in the field').
- **49:21** — Greek 'you supposed lawlessness' (anomian) reflects reading the Hebrew infinitive 'to be' as a noun meaning iniquity or ruin.
- **49:23** — Greek 'and there is the way' (kai ekei hodos) reflects vocalizing the Hebrew word as 'there' rather than 'sets' or 'orders.'
