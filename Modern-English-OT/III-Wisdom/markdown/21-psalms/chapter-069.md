# Psalm 69 — Saints Modern English (Greek Septuagint)

## Text

**69:1** For the end; for David, for a remembrance, that the Lord may save me.

**69:2** God, attend to my help.

**69:3** May those who seek my soul be ashamed and disgraced; may those who wish evil for me be turned back and put to shame;

**69:4** may those who say to me, "Aha, aha!" be turned back immediately, ashamed.

**69:5** Let all those who seek You exult and be glad in You, and let those who love Your salvation say continually, "Let God be magnified."

**69:6** But I am poor and needy; God, help me; You are my helper and my deliverer; Lord, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **69:1** — The closing clause of the superscription, "that the Lord may save me" (εἰς τὸ σῶσαί με Κύριον), is distinctive to the Septuagint and does not appear in the Hebrew.
- **69:2** — This shorter Greek text lacks the second clause ("Lord, hasten to help me") present in other Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew.
