## Text

**99:1** A psalm for thanksgiving. Shout aloud to the Lord, all the earth;

**99:2** serve the Lord with gladness; enter into His presence with exultation.

**99:3** Know that the Lord, He is God; He made us, and not we; we are His people and the sheep of His pasture.

**99:4** Enter His gates with thanksgiving, His courts with hymns; give thanks to Him, praise His name,

**99:5** for the Lord is good; His mercy is forever, and His truth is to generation and generation.

## Translator Notes

- **99:1** — Greek ἐξομολόγησις (like the verb ἐξομολογέω in 98:3 and 99:4) denotes public acknowledgment, confession, or thanksgiving.
- **99:3** — The Septuagint reads καὶ οὐχ ἡμεῖς ("and not we"), agreeing with the Hebrew Ketiv against the Qere ("and His we are").
- **99:4** — The Septuagint reads ἐν ὕμνοις ("with hymns") and αἰνεῖτε ("praise") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "with praise" and "bless."
