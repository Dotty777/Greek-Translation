## Text

**30:1** For the end; a psalm for David, of dismay.

**30:2** In You, Lord, I have hoped; may I not be put to shame forever; in Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me.

**30:3** Incline Your ear to me; hurry to rescue me. Be to me a protecting God and a house of refuge to save me.

**30:4** For You are my stronghold and my refuge, and for the sake of Your name You will guide me and nourish me.

**30:5** You will bring me out of this snare which they hid for me, for You are my protector.

**30:6** Into Your hands I will entrust my spirit; You redeemed me, Lord, the God of truth.

**30:7** You have hated those who observe futile things in vain, but I have hoped in the Lord.

**30:8** I will exult and be glad in Your mercy, because You looked upon my humiliation; You saved my soul out of distresses,

**30:9** and You did not shut me up into the hands of an enemy; You set my feet in a spacious place.

**30:10** Have mercy on me, Lord, for I am distressed; my eye was troubled in anger, my soul and my belly.

**30:11** For my life failed in pain, and my years in groanings; my strength grew weak in poverty, and my bones were troubled.

**30:12** Because of all my enemies I became a reproach, and exceedingly so to my neighbors, and a dread to my acquaintances; those who saw me outside fled from me.

**30:13** I was forgotten like a dead man from the heart; I became like a ruined vessel.

**30:14** For I heard the slander of many dwelling all around; when they were gathered together against me, they plotted to take my life.

**30:15** But I have hoped in You, Lord; I said, "You are my God."

**30:16** In Your hands are my lots; deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from those who pursue me.

**30:17** Make Your face shine upon Your servant; save me in Your mercy.

**30:18** Lord, may I not be put to shame, because I called upon You; may the ungodly be put to shame and be brought down to Hades.

**30:19** Let the deceitful lips become mute, which speak lawlessness against the righteous in pride and contempt.

**30:20** How great is the abundance of Your goodness, Lord, which You hid for those who fear You; You accomplished it for those who hope in You before the sons of men.

**30:21** You will hide them in the hiding place of Your presence from the turmoil of men; You will shelter them in a tent from the dispute of tongues.

**30:22** Blessed be the Lord, for He made His mercy wondrous in a fortified city.

**30:23** As for me, I said in my dismay, "Then I have been cast away from the sight of Your eyes." Therefore You heard, Lord, the voice of my supplication when I cried out to You.

**30:24** Love the Lord, all His devout ones, for the Lord seeks out truths, and repays those who excessively practice pride.

**30:25** Be courageous, and let your heart be strengthened, all who hope in the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — The Greek superscription adds ekstaseos ("of dismay" or "of astonishment"), echoing the psalmist's expression in 30:23.
- **30:7** — The Greek has the second-person verb "You have hated," whereas the Hebrew reads first-person "I hate."
- **30:11** — The Greek reads "in poverty" (contrasting with Hebrew "because of my iniquity"), just as 30:10 reads "in anger" rather than "from grief."
- **30:16** — Greek hoi kleroi mou literally means "my lots" or "my portions," whereas the Hebrew reads "my times."
- **30:23** — Greek dia touto reads "Therefore" (contrasting with Hebrew "Yet"); likewise in 30:24 the Greek reads "seeks out truths" rather than "preserves the faithful."
