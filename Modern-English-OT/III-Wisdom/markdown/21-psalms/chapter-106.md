## Text

**106:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**106:2** Let those who have been redeemed by the Lord say so, whom He redeemed from the hand of an enemy.

**106:3** From the lands He gathered them, from east and west and north and the sea.

**106:4** They wandered in the wilderness, in a waterless place; they did not find a way to a city of habitation.

**106:5** Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted within them.

**106:6** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He rescued them from their distresses;

**106:7** and He led them onto a straight way to go to a city of habitation.

**106:8** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies and His wonders toward the sons of men,

**106:9** for He satisfied an empty soul, and a hungry soul He filled with good things.

**106:10** Those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, shackled in poverty and in iron,

**106:11** because they embittered the oracles of God and provoked the counsel of the Most High;

**106:12** and their heart was humbled in hardships; they grew weak, and there was no one to help.

**106:13** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He saved them from their distresses;

**106:14** and He brought them out of darkness and out of the shadow of death, and tore their bonds apart.

**106:15** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies and His wonders toward the sons of men,

**106:16** for He shattered bronze gates, and broke iron bars in pieces.

**106:17** He helped them out of the way of their lawlessness, for because of their lawless deeds they were humbled.

**106:18** Their soul loathed all food, and they drew near to the gates of death.

**106:19** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He saved them from their distresses;

**106:20** He sent His word and healed them, and He rescued them from their destructions.

**106:21** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies and His wonders toward the sons of men,

**106:22** and let them sacrifice a sacrifice of praise, and declare His works with exultation.

**106:23** Those who go down to the sea in ships, doing business in many waters,

**106:24** they themselves saw the works of the Lord and His wonders in the deep.

**106:25** He spoke, and a storm wind arose, and its waves were lifted high.

**106:26** They go up to the heavens, and they go down to the depths; their soul was melting away in calamities;

**106:27** they were troubled, they staggered like a drunken man, and all their wisdom was swallowed up.

**106:28** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He brought them out of their distresses,

**106:29** and He stilled its storm, and its waves fell silent.

**106:30** And they were glad because they became calm, and He led them with the care of His will.

**106:31** Let them give thanks to the Lord for His mercies and His wonders toward the sons of men;

**106:32** let them exalt Him in assemblies of the people, and in seats of elders let them praise Him.

**106:33** He turned rivers into a wilderness, and outlets of water into thirst,

**106:34** a fruitful land into saltiness, because of the wickedness of those dwelling in it.

**106:35** He turned a wilderness into harbors of water,

**106:36** and He settled the hungry there, and they established a city of dwelling,

**106:37** and they sowed fields and planted vineyards, and produced fruit of harvest.

**106:38** And He blessed them, and they multiplied greatly, and He did not diminish their livestock.

**106:39** And they became few and were harmed because of the affliction of calamities and pain.

**106:40** Contempt was poured out upon rulers, and He made them wander in a pathless place and not a way.

**106:41** And He helped a poor man out of poverty, and made families like sheep.

**106:42** The upright will see and will be glad, and all lawlessness will shut its mouth.

**106:43** Who is wise and will keep these things, and they will understand the mercies of the Lord?

## Translator Notes

- **106:3** — Greek θαλάσσης ("the sea") literally renders the Hebrew word for "sea" (often meaning "west") in the fourth compass position, where "south" might be expected.
- **106:17** — The Greek reads ἀντελάβετο αὐτῶν ("He helped them" or "came to their aid") rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "Fools."
- **106:29** — This critical Greek text reads the shorter clause καὶ ἔστησεν καταιγίδα αὐτῆς ("and He stilled its storm"), without the additional phrase "into a breeze" found in some Greek witnesses and the Hebrew.
- **106:30** — This critical Greek text reads ἐπιμελίᾳ θελήματος αὐτοῦ ("with the care of His will," an itacistic spelling of ἐπιμελείᾳ, "care"), whereas other Greek manuscripts read ἐπὶ λιμένα ("to a harbor") and/or plural "their."
- **106:35** — This critical Greek text reads λιμένας ("harbors," where other manuscripts read λίμνας, "pools") and omits the second clause ("and a waterless land into outlets of water") found in other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew.
