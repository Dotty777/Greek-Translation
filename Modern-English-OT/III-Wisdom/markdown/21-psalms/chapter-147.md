## Text

**147:1** Hallelujah. Of Haggai and Zechariah. Extol the Lord, Jerusalem; praise your God, Zion,

**147:2** for He strengthened the bars of your gates; He blessed your sons within you.

**147:3** He is the One who makes your borders peace and fills you with the choicest of wheat.

**147:4** He is the One who sends out His utterance to the earth; very swiftly will run the word of Him

**147:5** who gives snow like wool, who sprinkles mist like ashes,

**147:6** who casts His ice like morsels; in the face of His cold, who will stand?

**147:7** He will send out His word and will melt them; His wind will blow, and waters will flow.

**147:8** He proclaims His word to Jacob, His ordinances and judgments to Israel.

**147:9** He did not do so for any nation, and He did not reveal His judgments to them.

## Translator Notes

- **147:3** — Literally 'the fat of wheat' (στέαρ πυροῦ), a biblical idiom referring to the richest, choicest portion of the wheat.
- **147:5** — Here and in verse 6 the Greek uses genitive participles dependent on 'of Him' (αὐτοῦ) at the end of verse 4. The Greek also reads 'mist' (ὁμίχλην) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'frost.'
- **147:7** — Unlike the Hebrew causative ('He causes His wind to blow'), the Greek uses the intransitive verb πνεύσει with 'His wind' (τὸ πνεῦμα αὐτοῦ) as its subject.
- **147:9** — The Greek has an active third-person singular verb with God as subject ('He did not reveal His judgments to them') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'they have not known them,' and lacks the closing 'Hallelujah' of MT 147:20.
