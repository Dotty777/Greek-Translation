## Text

**117:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**117:2** Let the house of Israel now say that He is good, that His mercy is forever.

**117:3** Let the house of Aaron now say that He is good, that His mercy is forever.

**117:4** Let all who fear the Lord now say that He is good, that His mercy is forever.

**117:5** In distress I called upon the Lord, and He answered me into a broad place.

**117:6** The Lord is a helper to me; I will not fear. What will man do to me?

**117:7** The Lord is a helper to me, and I will look upon my enemies.

**117:8** It is better to rely on the Lord than to rely on man.

**117:9** It is better to hope in the Lord than to hope in rulers.

**117:10** All the nations surrounded me, and by the name of the Lord I repelled them.

**117:11** They completely surrounded me, and by the name of the Lord I repelled them.

**117:12** They surrounded me like bees around a honeycomb, and they blazed up like fire among thorns, and by the name of the Lord I repelled them.

**117:13** Having been pushed, I was toppled so as to fall, and the Lord supported me.

**117:14** The Lord is my strength and my song of praise, and He became salvation to me.

**117:15** A sound of exultation and salvation is in the tents of the righteous. The right hand of the Lord performed a mighty deed.

**117:16** The right hand of the Lord exalted me.

**117:17** I will not die, but I will live, and I will recount the works of the Lord.

**117:18** The Lord severely disciplined me, yet He did not hand me over to death.

**117:19** Open for me the gates of righteousness; entering through them, I will give thanks to the Lord.

**117:20** This is the gate of the Lord; righteous ones will enter through it.

**117:21** I will give thanks to You, for You answered me and became salvation to me.

**117:22** The stone that the builders rejected, this one became the head of the corner.

**117:23** From the Lord this came about, and it is marvelous in our eyes.

**117:24** This is the day that the Lord made; let us exult and be glad in it.

**117:25** O Lord, save now; O Lord, grant success now.

**117:26** Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord; we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.

**117:27** The Lord is God, and He shone upon us; arrange a festival with dense branches up to the horns of the altar.

**117:28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to You; You are my God, and I will exalt You. I will give thanks to You, for You answered me and became salvation to me.

**117:29** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

## Translator Notes

- **117:2** — The Greek text reads "house of Israel" (rather than simply "Israel" as in the Hebrew) and includes the clause "that He is good" in verses 2-4, matching the refrain of verse 1.
- **117:5** — Literally "answered me into a broad place" (or "heard me into a wide space"), a compressed Greek idiom meaning the Lord responded by bringing the psalmist out of distress (literally "pressure") into open space and relief.
- **117:12** — The Greek includes "a honeycomb" (kerion) and reads "they blazed up" (exekauthesan), whereas the Hebrew text describes the nations as bees that were extinguished like a fire of thorns.
- **117:16** — This Greek text contains only this single clause in verse 16, without repeating "the right hand of the Lord performed a mighty deed" as found in the Hebrew text and some Greek manuscripts.
- **117:27** — The Greek verb epephanen ("shone upon") can also mean "appeared to." In the second half, the Greek renders the command as arranging a festival with dense or leafy branches (en tois pykazousin) rather than binding a festal sacrifice with cords as in the Hebrew.
