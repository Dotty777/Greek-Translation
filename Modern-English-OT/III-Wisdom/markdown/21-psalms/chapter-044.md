## Text

**44:1** For the end, concerning those who will be changed; for the sons of Korah, for understanding; a song concerning the beloved.

**44:2** My heart poured forth a good word; I speak my works to the king; my tongue is the reed pen of a swift-writing scribe.

**44:3** You are lovely in beauty beyond the sons of men; grace was poured out on your lips; therefore God blessed you forever.

**44:4** Fasten your sword on your thigh, Mighty One, with your loveliness and your beauty,

**44:5** and press on, and prosper, and reign for the sake of truth and meekness and righteousness, and your right hand will guide you marvelously.

**44:6** Your arrows are sharpened, Mighty One; peoples will fall beneath you in the heart of the king's enemies.

**44:7** Your throne, God, is forever and ever; a scepter of equity is the scepter of Your kingdom.

**44:8** You loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; therefore God, Your God, anointed You with oil of exultation beyond Your companions.

**44:9** Myrrh and stacte and cassia are from your garments, from ivory palaces, from which they gladdened you.

**44:10** Daughters of kings are in your honor; the queen stood at your right hand, arrayed in attire interwoven with gold, adorned in varied colors.

**44:11** Listen, daughter, and see, and incline your ear, and forget your people and the house of your father.

**44:12** For the king desired your beauty, because he is your lord.

**44:13** And daughters of Tyre will bow down to him with gifts; the rich of the people of the earth will implore your face.

**44:14** All the glory of her, the daughter of the king of Heshbon, is arrayed in golden fringes, adorned in varied colors.

**44:15** Virgins will be brought to the king after her; those near her will be brought to you.

**44:16** They will be brought with gladness and exultation; they will be led into the temple of the king.

**44:17** In place of your fathers, sons were born to you; you will appoint them rulers over all the earth.

**44:18** They will remember your name in every generation and generation; therefore peoples will give thanks to you forever and forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **44:1** — The Greek superscription reads 'for the end' (εἰς τὸ τέλος) and 'concerning those who will be changed' (ὑπὲρ τῶν ἀλλοιωθησομένων), corresponding to the Hebrew musical headings 'for the choirmaster' and 'according to Lilies'.
- **44:5** — The Greek imperative ἔντεινον literally means 'stretch tight' or 'press on' (often used of bending a bow), reflecting the Septuagint's reading of the Hebrew consonantal text rather than 'in your majesty'.
- **44:7** — The nominative ὁ θεός is read here as a vocative ('God') addressing the royal figure; pronouns in vv. 7-8 are therefore capitalized. Verses 7-8 are quoted in Hebrews 1:8-9.
- **44:9** — Greek στακτή ('stacte') refers to a fragrant oil or resin of myrrh, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'aloes'.
- **44:14** — The Greek text reads the proper name Ἑσεβών ('Heshbon', attested in Codex Vaticanus [B] and Codex Sinaiticus [א]), where Codex Alexandrinus (A) and other Greek witnesses read ἔσωθεν ('within', matching the Hebrew). In v. 16, Greek ναός literally means 'temple' rather than 'palace'.
