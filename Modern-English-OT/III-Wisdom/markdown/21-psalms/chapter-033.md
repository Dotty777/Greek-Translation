## Text

**33:1** For David, when he altered his face before Abimelech, and he sent him away, and he departed.

**33:2** I will bless the Lord at all times; continually His praise is in my mouth.

**33:3** In the Lord my soul will be praised; let the meek hear and be glad.

**33:4** Magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt His name together.

**33:5** I sought out the Lord, and He listened to me, and He delivered me from all my sojournings.

**33:6** Come near to Him and be enlightened, and your faces will by no means be put to shame.

**33:7** This poor man cried out, and the Lord heard him and saved him out of all his afflictions.

**33:8** The angel of the Lord will encamp around those who fear Him and will deliver them.

**33:9** Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the man who hopes in Him.

**33:10** Fear the Lord, all His holy ones, for there is no lack for those who fear Him.

**33:11** The rich became poor and went hungry, but those who seek out the Lord will not come short of any good thing. Interlude.

**33:12** Come, children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the Lord.

**33:13** Who is the person who desires life, loving to see good days?

**33:14** Restrain your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit.

**33:15** Turn aside from evil and do what is good; seek peace and pursue it.

**33:16** For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears are toward their supplication.

**33:17** But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil things, to wipe out their remembrance from the earth.

**33:18** The righteous cried out, and the Lord heard them and delivered them out of all their afflictions.

**33:19** The Lord is near to those who are broken in heart, and He will save the humble in spirit.

**33:20** Many are the afflictions of the righteous, and out of them all He will deliver them.

**33:21** The Lord guards all their bones; not one of them will be broken.

**33:22** The death of sinners is evil, and those who hate the righteous one will incur guilt.

**33:23** The Lord will redeem the souls of His servants, and all who hope in Him will by no means incur guilt.

## Translator Notes

- **33:1** — The Greek reads "altered his face" where the Masoretic Text has "changed his behavior/sanity"; Abimelech appears here as the name or title of the Philistine king called Achish in 1 Samuel 21.
- **33:5** — Greek paroikion means "sojournings" (from Hebrew gur, "sojourn"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "terrors" or "fears."
- **33:6** — The Greek has second-person plural imperatives ("Come near... be enlightened") and "your faces," whereas the Masoretic Text has third-person plural indicatives ("They looked... were radiant... their faces").
- **33:11** — Greek plousioi means "the rich," diverging from the Masoretic Text's "young lions"; the Greek also includes "Interlude" (diapsalma), absent from the Hebrew.
- **33:22** — The Greek reads "The death of sinners is evil" rather than the Masoretic Text's "Evil will put the wicked to death." Greek plemmelesousin (also in v. 23) literally means "will err/offend" and in the Septuagint carries the judicial sense "will incur guilt."
