## Text

**96:1** Of David, when his land is being established. The Lord has become king; the earth will exult; let many islands be glad.

**96:2** Cloud and thick darkness are around Him; righteousness and judgment are the firm foundation of His throne.

**96:3** Fire will go before Him and will set His enemies ablaze all around.

**96:4** His lightning flashes shone upon the inhabited world; the earth saw and was shaken.

**96:5** The mountains melted like wax before the face of the Lord, before the face of the Lord of all the earth.

**96:6** The heavens declared His righteousness, and all the peoples saw His glory.

**96:7** Let all who worship carved images be put to shame, those who boast in their idols; worship Him, all His angels.

**96:8** Zion heard and was glad, and the daughters of Judea exulted because of Your judgments, Lord.

**96:9** For You are the Lord, the Most High over all the earth; You have been greatly exalted far above all the gods.

**96:10** You who love the Lord, hate evil; the Lord guards the souls of His devout ones; He will rescue them from the hand of sinners.

**96:11** Light has dawned for the righteous one, and gladness for the upright in heart.

**96:12** Be glad in the Lord, you righteous ones, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness.

## Translator Notes

- **96:1** — The Greek superscription ("Of David, when his land is being established") has no counterpart in Hebrew Psalm 97. Here and in 95:10, the aorist ἐβασίλευσεν carries an ingressive sense ("has become king").
- **96:7** — Greek reads "all His angels" (πάντες οἱ ἄγγελοι αὐτοῦ), corresponding to Hebrew "all gods," a wording echoed in Hebrews 1:6.
- **96:8** — Greek uses the regional name "Judea" (Ἰουδαίας) rather than the tribal or kingdom name "Judah."
- **96:11** — Greek reads "has dawned" (ἀνέτειλεν), reflecting a different understanding of the Hebrew verb usually translated "is sown."
