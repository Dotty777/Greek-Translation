## Text

**77:1** Of understanding, for Asaph. Pay attention, my people, to my law; incline your ear to the words of my mouth.

**77:2** I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter riddles from the beginning,

**77:3** all the things that we heard and knew, and that our fathers recounted to us.

**77:4** They were not hidden from their children to another generation, declaring the praises of the Lord and His mighty acts, and His wonders that He did.

**77:5** And He raised up a testimony in Jacob, and set a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to make known to their sons,

**77:6** so that another generation might know, sons who will be born, and they will rise up and declare them to their sons;

**77:7** so that they might set their hope on God, and not forget the works of God, and will seek out His commandments;

**77:8** so that they might not become like their fathers, a crooked and provoking generation, a generation that did not keep straight in its heart, and whose spirit was not proven faithful with God.

**77:9** The sons of Ephraim, drawing and shooting a bow, turned back on the day of battle.

**77:10** They did not keep the covenant of God, and they were not willing to walk in His law.

**77:11** And they forgot His good deeds and His wonders that He showed them,

**77:12** wonders that He did before their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the plain of Tanis.

**77:13** He split open the sea and brought them through; He made the waters stand like a waterskin.

**77:14** And He guided them with a cloud by day, and all the night with the light of fire.

**77:15** He split open a rock in the wilderness, and gave them drink as in a great abyss.

**77:16** And He brought out water from a rock, and brought down waters like rivers.

**77:17** And they continued still more to sin against Him; they provoked the Most High in a waterless place,

**77:18** and they tested God in their hearts, by asking for foods for their souls.

**77:19** And they spoke against God and said, "Will God be able to prepare a table in the wilderness?

**77:20** Since He struck a rock, and waters flowed, and torrents overflowed, will He also be able to give bread? Or to prepare a table for His people?"

**77:21** Therefore the Lord heard and delayed, and fire was kindled in Jacob, and wrath rose up against Israel,

**77:22** because they did not believe in God, nor did they hope in His salvation.

**77:23** And He commanded the clouds above, and opened the doors of heaven.

**77:24** And He rained manna for them to eat, and He gave them bread of heaven.

**77:25** Man ate bread of angels; He sent them provisions to the full.

**77:26** And He raised up a south wind from heaven, and by His might He brought in a southwest wind,

**77:27** and He rained flesh upon them like dust, and winged birds like the sand of the seas.

**77:28** And they fell into the middle of their camp, all around their tents.

**77:29** And they ate and were exceedingly filled, and He brought them their desire.

**77:30** They were not deprived of their desire; while the food

**77:31** of theirs was still in their mouth, the wrath of God rose up against them and killed among the majority of them, and fettered the chosen ones of Israel.

**77:32** In all these things they sinned still more, and they did not believe in

**77:33** His wonders; and their days failed in futility, and their years with haste.

**77:34** Whenever He was killing them, they sought Him, and turned back and

**77:35** came early to God; and they remembered that God is their helper, and God the Most High is their redeemer.

**77:36** And they loved Him with their mouth, and with their tongue

**77:37** they lied to Him; and their heart was not upright with Him, nor were they proven faithful in His covenant.

**77:38** But He is compassionate and will pardon their sins, and will not destroy; and many times He will turn away His anger, and He will not kindle all His wrath.

**77:39** And He remembered that they are flesh, a wind going on and not returning.

**77:40** How many times did they provoke Him in the wilderness, anger

**77:41** Him in a waterless land? And they turned back and tested God, and exasperated the Holy One of Israel.

**77:42** They did not remember His hand, the day on which He redeemed them from the hand of an oppressor,

**77:43** how He set His signs in Egypt, and

**77:44** His portents in the plain of Tanis; and He turned their rivers into blood, and their rainwaters, so that they might not drink.

**77:45** He sent out against them the dog-fly, and it devoured them, and the frog, and it destroyed them.

**77:46** And He gave their fruit to the blight, and their labors to the locust.

**77:47** He killed their vine with hail, and their sycamore trees with

**77:48** the frost; and He gave up their livestock in hail, and their possessions to fire.

**77:49** He sent out against them the wrath of His anger, anger and wrath and affliction, a dispatch through

**77:50** evil angels. He made a path for His wrath; He did not spare their souls from death, and He confined their livestock to death.

**77:51** And He struck every firstborn in Egypt, the firstfruits of their labors in the tents of Ham.

**77:52** And He brought His people out like sheep, and led them like

**77:53** a flock in the wilderness; and He guided them in hope and they were not afraid, and the sea covered their enemies.

**77:54** And He brought them in to the mountain of His sanctuary, this mountain which

**77:55** His right hand acquired; and He drove out nations from before them, and He allotted them by a line of inheritance, and made a dwelling in their tents for the tribes of Israel.

**77:56** And they tested and provoked God the Most High, and they did not keep His testimonies.

**77:57** And they turned back and broke faith just as their fathers also did, and they were turned into a warped bow.

**77:58** And they angered Him on their hills, and with their carved images they provoked Him to jealousy.

**77:59** God heard, and disdained and despised Israel exceedingly.

**77:60** And He rejected the tent of Shiloh, His dwelling

**77:61** where He dwelt among men; and He gave over their strength into captivity, and His beauty into the hands of an enemy.

**77:62** And He confined His people to the sword, and He disdained His inheritance.

**77:63** Fire devoured their young men, and their virgins did not mourn.

**77:64** Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows will not be wept for.

**77:65** And the Lord awoke like one sleeping, like a mighty man dazed

**77:66** from wine. And He struck their enemies in the rear; He gave them an eternal reproach.

**77:67** And He rejected the dwelling of Joseph, and He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim;

**77:68** and He chose the tribe of Judah; Mount Zion He loved.

**77:69** And He built His sanctuary like that of unicorns; on the earth He founded it forever.

**77:70** And He chose David His servant, and took him up from the

**77:71** flocks of sheep; from behind those bearing young He took him, to shepherd Jacob His servant and Israel His inheritance.

**77:72** And he shepherded them in the innocence of his heart, and with the understanding of his hands he guided them.

## Translator Notes

- **77:12** — Tanis (Greek Taneos) is the Septuagint name for the Egyptian Delta city known as Zoan in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **77:13** — The Greek reads "waterskin" (askon, a leather liquid container), reflecting a different reading from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "heap."
- **77:21** — The Greek verb anebaleto literally means "delayed" or "put off," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "was full of wrath."
- **77:31** — The Greek phrase en tois pleiosin auton means "among the majority of them" (compared with Hebrew "among their stoutest ones"). Here and across verses 30-36, the Septuagint verse divisions fall mid-clause.
- **77:36** — The Greek reads "loved" (egapesan), contrasting outward speech with the deceit completed in verse 37, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "flattered" or "deceived."
- **77:50** — The Greek text reads "and He confined their livestock to death," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "and He gave over their life to the plague."
- **77:63** — The Greek reads "did not mourn" (ouk epenthesan), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "were not praised" (i.e., had no wedding songs).
- **77:64** — The Greek verb klausthesontai is future passive ("will not be wept for"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "did not weep."
- **77:65** — The Greek participle kekraipalekos specifically describes one suffering the dazed after-effects or hangover of heavy drinking.
- **77:69** — The Greek reads "like that of unicorns" (hos monokeroton, literally one-horned beasts), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic Text's "like the heights."
