## Text

**53:1** For the end, with hymns; of understanding, for David,

**53:2** when the Ziphites came; and he said to Saul, "Look, is David not hidden with us?"

**53:3** God, in Your name save me, and in Your power judge me.

**53:4** God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.

**53:5** For strangers rose up against me, and mighty ones sought my soul; they did not set God before them. Interlude.

**53:6** For look, God helps me, and the Lord is the upholder of my soul.

**53:7** He will turn back the evils upon my enemies; in Your truth utterly destroy them.

**53:8** Willingly I will sacrifice to You; I will give thanks to Your name, for it is good;

**53:9** for out of every affliction You delivered me, and my eye looked upon my enemies.

## Translator Notes

- **53:2** — The Greek verb eipen ("he said") is third-person singular following the plural "the Ziphites," a Septuagint reading that treats the delegation collectively or through a spokesman.
- **53:5** — Greek diapsalma ("Interlude") is the Septuagint musical direction corresponding to Hebrew Selah.
- **53:9** — The Septuagint reads second-person "You delivered" (where the Hebrew MT has third-person "he has delivered"); "looked upon" translates the idiomatic epeiden en, implying looking down in triumph.
