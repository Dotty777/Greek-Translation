## Text

**93:1** A psalm for David, on the fourth day of the week. The God of vengeance is the Lord; the God of vengeance acted boldly.

**93:2** Be exalted, You who judge the earth; repay retribution to the proud.

**93:3** How long will sinners, Lord, how long will sinners boast?

**93:4** Will they utter and speak unrighteousness? Will all those who work lawlessness speak?

**93:5** They humiliated Your people, Lord, and afflicted Your inheritance.

**93:6** They killed a widow and an orphan, and they murdered a resident foreigner.

**93:7** And they said, "The Lord will not see, nor will the God of Jacob understand."

**93:8** Understand now, you senseless ones among the people; and you fools, come to your senses at last.

**93:9** He who planted the ear, does He not hear? Or He who formed eyes, does He not observe?

**93:10** He who disciplines nations, will He not reprove, He who teaches man knowledge?

**93:11** The Lord knows the reasonings of men, that they are futile.

**93:12** Blessed is the man whom You discipline, Lord, and whom You teach from Your law,

**93:13** to give him relief from evil days, until a pit is dug for the sinner.

**93:14** For the Lord will not reject His people, and He will not abandon His inheritance,

**93:15** until righteousness returns to judgment, and all the upright in heart hold fast to it. Interlude.

**93:16** Who will rise up for me against those who do evil, or who will stand beside me against those who work lawlessness?

**93:17** If the Lord had not helped me, my soul would almost have dwelt in Hades.

**93:18** If I said, "My foot has been shaken," Your mercy, Lord, helps me.

**93:19** Lord, according to the multitude of my pains in my heart, Your consolations loved my soul.

**93:20** Will a throne of lawlessness be joined with You, one that forms trouble by decree?

**93:21** They will hunt for the soul of a righteous man, and they will condemn innocent blood.

**93:22** And the Lord became a refuge for me, and my God a helper of my hope.

**93:23** And He will repay to them their lawlessness and their wickedness; the Lord our God will destroy them.

## Translator Notes

- **93:1** — The Septuagint superscription assigns this psalm to Wednesday ("the fourth day of the week"), and ἐκδικήσεων is plural ("acts of vengeance"). Greek ἐπαρρησιάσατο ("acted boldly" or "spoke openly") is an aorist indicative rather than the Hebrew imperative "shine forth."
- **93:15** — The Greek reverses the Hebrew subject and object so that righteousness returns to judgment, and closes with διάψαλμα ("Interlude"), a liturgical/musical pause corresponding to Hebrew Selah that is absent here in the MT.
- **93:17** — Greek τῷ ᾅδῃ ("in Hades") differs from the Hebrew MT reading "in silence."
- **93:19** — Greek ἠγάπησαν ("loved") is the reading of major Septuagint witnesses, diverging from the Hebrew "delighted/cheered" and later Greek manuscripts that read "gladdened."
- **93:23** — In this Greek text, both "their lawlessness" and "their wickedness" are direct objects of "repay," followed by a single concluding verb "will destroy."
