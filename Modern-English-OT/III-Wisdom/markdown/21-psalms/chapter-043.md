## Text

**43:1** For the end; to the sons of Korah, for understanding, a psalm.

**43:2** God, with our ears we have heard; our fathers told us the work that You did in their days, in ancient days.

**43:3** Your hand utterly destroyed nations, and You planted them; You afflicted peoples and drove them out.

**43:4** For they did not inherit the land by their sword, and their arm did not save them, but Your right hand and Your arm and the illumination of Your face, because You were well pleased with them.

**43:5** You Yourself are my King and my God, the One who commands the deliverances of Jacob.

**43:6** In You we will gore our enemies, and in Your name we will treat as nothing those who rise up against us.

**43:7** For I will not hope in my bow, and my sword will not save me.

**43:8** For You saved us from those who oppress us, and You put to shame those who hate us.

**43:9** In God we will be praised all day long, and in Your name we will give thanks forever. Interlude.

**43:10** But now You have rejected us and put us to shame, and You will not go out with our armies.

**43:11** You turned us back before our enemies, and those who hate us were plundering for themselves.

**43:12** You gave us like sheep for food, and You scattered us among the nations.

**43:13** You sold Your people without a price, and there was no abundance in their shouts.

**43:14** You made us a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and a derision to those around us.

**43:15** You made us into a byword among the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.

**43:16** All day long my disgrace is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me,

**43:17** because of the voice of one who reproaches and reviles, because of the presence of an enemy and a pursuer.

**43:18** All these things have come upon us, yet we have not forgotten You, and we have not acted unjustly in Your covenant;

**43:19** and our heart has not turned back, yet You turned our paths aside from Your way.

**43:20** For You humbled us in a place of affliction, and the shadow of death covered us over.

**43:21** If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread out our hands to a foreign god,

**43:22** will not God search out these things? For He Himself knows the hidden things of the heart.

**43:23** For on Your account we are put to death all day long; we were counted like sheep for slaughter.

**43:24** Awake! Why do You sleep, Lord? Arise, and do not reject us to the end.

**43:25** Why do You turn Your face away? Do You forget our poverty and our oppression?

**43:26** For our soul has been humbled to the dust; our belly has clung to the ground.

**43:27** Arise, Lord, help us, and redeem us on account of Your name.

## Translator Notes

- **43:13** — The Greek reads "there was no abundance in their shouts" (alalagmasin, "shouts" or "war cries"), diverging notably from the Hebrew text's "you made no gain from their prices."
- **43:19** — The Septuagint reads a second-person verb ("yet You turned our paths aside from Your way"), whereas the Hebrew text continues the third-person denial ("nor have our steps turned aside from your way").
- **43:20** — The Greek reads "in a place of affliction" (topo kakoseos), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the place of jackals."
- **43:27** — The Septuagint includes the vocative "Lord" and ends with "on account of Your name," whereas the Hebrew text reads "for the sake of your steadfast love."
