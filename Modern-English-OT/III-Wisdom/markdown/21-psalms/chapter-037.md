## Text

**37:1** A psalm for David; for remembrance concerning the Sabbath.

**37:2** Lord, do not reprove me in Your wrath, nor discipline me in Your anger.

**37:3** For Your arrows have stuck fast in me, and You have pressed Your hand down upon me.

**37:4** And there is no healing in my flesh because of Your anger; there is no peace for my bones because of my sins.

**37:5** For my lawless deeds have risen above my head; like a heavy burden they have weighed heavily upon me.

**37:6** My wounds stank and rotted because of my foolishness.

**37:7** I suffered misery and was utterly bowed down; all day long I walked about with a gloomy face.

**37:8** For my soul has been filled with mockeries, and there is no healing in my flesh.

**37:9** I was afflicted and humbled exceedingly; I was roaring from the groaning of my heart.

**37:10** And before You is all my desire, and my groaning was not hidden from You.

**37:11** My heart has been troubled, my strength has abandoned me, and the light of my eyes is not with me.

**37:12** My friends and my neighbors drew near and stood opposite me, and those nearest to me stood far off.

**37:13** And those who seek my life used violence, and those who seek evil against me spoke futile things, and they meditated on deceits all day long.

**37:14** But I, like a deaf man, did not hear, and was like a mute man who does not open his mouth.

**37:15** And I became like a man who does not hear, and who has no reproofs in his mouth.

**37:16** For in You I have hoped, Lord; You will hear, Lord my God.

**37:17** For I said, "May my enemies never gloat over me," and when my feet were shaken, they spoke boastfully against me.

**37:18** For I am ready for scourges, and my pain is before me continually.

**37:19** For I will declare my lawlessness, and I will be anxious over my sin.

**37:20** But my enemies live and have grown stronger than I, and those who hate me unjustly have multiplied.

**37:21** Those who repay evil for good were slandering me, because I pursued righteousness.

**37:22** Do not abandon me, Lord; my God, do not depart from me.

**37:23** Attend to my help, Lord of my salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The Septuagint superscription adds "concerning the Sabbath" (περὶ σαββάτου), which is absent from the Masoretic Text; this chapter corresponds to Psalm 38 in Hebrew numbering.
- **37:8** — Greek reads "my soul has been filled with mockeries" (ἡ ψυχή μου ἐπλήσθη ἐνπαιγμῶν), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "my loins are filled with burning."
- **37:12** — Greek reads "drew near and stood opposite me" (ἐξ ἐναντίας μου ἤγγισαν καὶ ἔστησαν), reflecting a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants for "my plague" as the verb "draw near."
- **37:18** — Greek reads "for scourges" (εἰς μάστιγας) where the Masoretic Text has "for stumbling."
