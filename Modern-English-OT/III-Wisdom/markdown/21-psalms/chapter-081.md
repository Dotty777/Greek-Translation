## Text

**81:1** A psalm for Asaph. God stood in the assembly of gods, and in the midst He judges gods.

**81:2** How long will you judge with injustice and show partiality to sinners? Interlude.

**81:3** Do justice for the orphan and the poor; vindicate the lowly and the needy.

**81:4** Rescue the needy and the poor; deliver them from the hand of a sinner.

**81:5** They did not know, nor did they understand; they walk about in darkness. All the foundations of the earth will be shaken.

**81:6** I said, "You are gods, and sons of the Most High, all of you."

**81:7** Yet indeed, like humans you die, and like one of the rulers you fall.

**81:8** Rise up, God, judge the earth; for You will inherit among all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **81:1** — Greek places theous ("gods") in the accusative as the direct object of diakrinei ("judges"), with en meso ("in the midst") standing adverbially, whereas the Hebrew reads "in the midst of the gods He judges."
- **81:2** — Greek prosopa hamartolon lambanete literally means "accept the faces of sinners," a Semitic idiom for showing partiality. Diapsalma ("Interlude") is the Septuagint's rendering of Hebrew selah, indicating a musical pause or interlude.
- **81:8** — Greek katakleronomeseis en pasin tois ethnesin literally means "You will inherit among all the nations," preserving the prepositional phrase rather than making the nations a direct object.
