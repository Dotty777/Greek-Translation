## Text

**132:1** A song of the steps. For David. Look now, what is good or what is pleasant, except for brothers to dwell together?

**132:2** It is like fragrant oil upon the head, coming down upon the beard, the beard of Aaron, coming down upon the border of his garment;

**132:3** like the dew of Hermon coming down upon the mountains of Zion; for there the Lord commanded the blessing and life forever.

## Translator Notes

- **132:1** — Unlike the exclamation in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("How good and how pleasant..."), the Greek frames the verse as a rhetorical question ("what is good or what is pleasant, except...").
- **132:2** — Greek ᾤα denotes the border, edge, or hem of a garment (singular ἐνδύματος), corresponding to the Hebrew phrase often rendered "collar of his robes."
