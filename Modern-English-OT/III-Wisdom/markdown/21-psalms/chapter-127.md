## Text

**127:1** A song of the steps. Blessed are all those who fear the Lord, those who walk in His ways.

**127:2** You will eat the fruits of your labors; you are blessed, and it will be well with you.

**127:3** Your wife will be like a flourishing vine on the sides of your house; your sons will be like young olive plants around your table.

**127:4** Look, in this way the man who fears the Lord will be blessed.

**127:5** May the Lord bless you from Zion, and may you see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of your life,

**127:6** and may you see sons of your sons. Peace upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **127:2** — The Greek reads the plural "the fruits of your labors" (τοὺς καρποὺς τῶν πόνων σου), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the labor of your hands."
- **127:3** — The Greek participle εὐθηνοῦσα means "flourishing" or "thriving," and ἐν τοῖς κλίτεσι literally means "on the sides" of the house.
