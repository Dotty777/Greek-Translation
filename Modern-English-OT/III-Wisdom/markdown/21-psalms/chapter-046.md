## Text

**46:1** For the end; for the sons of Korah, a psalm.

**46:2** All nations, clap your hands; shout to God with a voice of exultation.

**46:3** For the Lord Most High is fearsome, a great king over all the earth.

**46:4** He subjected peoples to us, and nations under our feet.

**46:5** He chose for us His inheritance, the beauty of Jacob which He loved. Interlude.

**46:6** God ascended with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet.

**46:7** Sing praise to our God, sing praise; sing praise to our king, sing praise.

**46:8** For God is king of all the earth; sing praise with understanding.

**46:9** God reigned over the nations; God sits on His holy throne.

**46:10** Rulers of peoples were gathered together with the God of Abraham, for God's mighty ones of the earth were exceedingly lifted up.

## Translator Notes

- **46:5** — The Greek reads 'His inheritance' (αὐτοῦ) and 'the beauty of Jacob' (τὴν καλλονὴν Ἰακώβ) with feminine relative ἣν ('which He loved'), whereas the Hebrew has 'our inheritance' and 'the pride of Jacob'.
- **46:10** — The Greek reads 'with the God of Abraham' (μετὰ τοῦ θεοῦ Ἀβραάμ) rather than 'as the people of the God of Abraham', and employs a plural passive verb (ἐπήρθησαν, 'were lifted up') with 'God's mighty ones of the earth' as subject.
