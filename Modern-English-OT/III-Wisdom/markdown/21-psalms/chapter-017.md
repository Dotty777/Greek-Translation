## Text

**17:1** For the end; for the servant of the Lord, David, what he spoke to the Lord, the words of this song, on the day when the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul,

**17:2** and he said, "I will love You, Lord, my strength.

**17:3** The Lord is my stronghold and my refuge and my deliverer; my God is a helper, and I will hope in Him; my protector and a horn of salvation, my upholder.

**17:4** Praising, I will call upon the Lord, and I will be saved from my enemies.

**17:5** Pangs of death surrounded me, and torrents of lawlessness terrified me;

**17:6** pangs of Hades encircled me; snares of death confronted me.

**17:7** And in my distress I called upon the Lord, and to my God I cried out; He heard my voice from His holy temple, and my cry before Him will enter into His ears.

**17:8** And the earth was shaken and trembled, and the foundations of the mountains were troubled and shaken, because God was angry with them.

**17:9** Smoke went up in His wrath, and fire blazed fiercely before Him; coals were kindled from Him.

**17:10** And He bent heaven and came down, and thick darkness was under His feet.

**17:11** And He mounted upon cherubim and flew; He flew upon wings of winds.

**17:12** And He made darkness His hiding place; around Him was His tent, dark water in clouds of the skies.

**17:13** From the brightness before Him the clouds passed through, hail and coals of fire.

**17:14** And the Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave His voice.

**17:15** And He sent out arrows and scattered them, and He multiplied lightning flashes and threw them into confusion.

**17:16** And the springs of the waters appeared, and the foundations of the inhabited world were uncovered at Your rebuke, Lord, at the blast of the breath of Your wrath.

**17:17** He sent out from on high and took me; He took hold of me from many waters.

**17:18** He will deliver me from my strong enemies and from those who hate me, because they grew stronger than I.

**17:19** They confronted me on the day of my affliction, and the Lord became my support,

**17:20** and He brought me out into a broad place; He will deliver me, because He desired me.

**17:21** He will deliver me from my strong enemies and from those who hate me. And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness, and according to the cleanness of my hands He will repay me.

**17:22** For I kept the ways of the Lord, and I did not act ungodly away from my God;

**17:23** for all His judgments were before me, and His ordinances did not depart from me.

**17:24** And I will be blameless with Him, and I will guard myself from my lawlessness.

**17:25** And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness and according to the cleanness of my hands before His eyes.

**17:26** With a devout one You will show Yourself devout, and with an innocent man You will be innocent,

**17:27** and with a chosen one You will be chosen, and with a crooked one You will act crookedly.

**17:28** For You will save a humble people, and You will humble the eyes of the arrogant;

**17:29** for You will light my lamp, Lord; my God, You will light up my darkness.

**17:30** For by You I will be delivered from a raiding band, and by my God I will scale a wall.

**17:31** My God, His way is blameless; the oracles of the Lord are refined by fire; He is a protector of all who hope in Him.

**17:32** For who is God besides the Lord? And who is God besides our God?

**17:33** The God who girds me with strength, and made my way blameless;

**17:34** who makes my feet firm like a deer's, and sets me upon the high places;

**17:35** teaching my hands for war, and You made my arms a bronze bow.

**17:36** And You gave me the protection of my salvation, and Your right hand upheld me; and Your discipline set me upright to the end, and Your discipline itself will teach me.

**17:37** You broadened my steps beneath me, and my footsteps did not grow weak.

**17:38** I will pursue my enemies and overtake them, and I will not turn back until they fail;

**17:39** I will crush them, and they will by no means be able to stand; they will fall under my feet.

**17:40** And You girded me with strength for war; You bound the feet of all those who rise up against me beneath me.

**17:41** And You gave me the back of my enemies, and You utterly destroyed those who hate me.

**17:42** They cried out, and there was no one to save; to the Lord, and He did not hear them.

**17:43** And I will grind them fine like dust before the face of the wind; like mud of the streets I will flatten them.

**17:44** Deliver me from the disputes of a people; You will appoint me as head of nations; a people whom I did not know served me;

**17:45** at the hearing of the ear they obeyed me; foreign sons lied to me;

**17:46** foreign sons grew old and limped away from their paths.

**17:47** The Lord lives, and blessed be my God, and let the God of my salvation be exalted,

**17:48** the God who gives acts of vengeance to me and subdued peoples under me,

**17:49** my deliverer from wrathful enemies; from those who rise up against me You will exalt me; from an unrighteous man You will deliver me.

**17:50** Therefore I will give thanks to You among the nations, Lord, and I will sing praises to Your name,

**17:51** magnifying the acts of salvation of His king, and showing mercy to His anointed, to David and to his seed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — "For the end" (Εἰς τὸ τέλος) is the standard Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew superscription often translated "for the choirmaster."
- **17:21** — This Greek textual witness repeats the opening sentence of verse 18 ("He will deliver me from my strong enemies and from those who hate me") before the statement of repayment.
- **17:32** — The Greek repeats "God" (θεός) in both questions where the Hebrew reads "a rock" in the second clause.
- **17:36** — The spelling παιδία here represents παιδεία ("discipline" or "instruction," feminine singular), as confirmed by the feminine pronoun αὐτή; the Greek renders this clause with a doubled statement.
- **17:46** — The Septuagint reads "grew old and limped away from their paths" (ἐπαλαιώθησαν καὶ ἐχώλαναν ἀπὸ τῶν τρίβων αὐτῶν), diverging from the Hebrew "fade away and come trembling out of their fortresses."
