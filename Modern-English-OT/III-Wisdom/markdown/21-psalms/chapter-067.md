## Text

**67:1** For the end; of David, a psalm of a song.

**67:2** Let God rise up, and let His enemies be scattered, and let those who hate Him flee from His presence.

**67:3** As smoke vanishes, let them vanish; as wax melts from the presence of fire, so may the sinners perish from the presence of God.

**67:4** And let the righteous be glad. Interlude. Let them exult before God; let them delight in gladness.

**67:5** Sing to God, sing praise to His name; make a road for the One who rides upon the west—the Lord is His name—and exult before Him.

**67:6** They will be troubled from the presence of Him, the Father of the orphans and Judge of the widows; God is in His holy place.

**67:7** God causes the solitary to dwell in a house, bringing out those who are fettered with valor, likewise those who rebel, those who dwell in tombs.

**67:8** God, when You went out before Your people, when You passed through the wilderness—Interlude—

**67:9** the earth was shaken, for indeed the heavens dripped from the presence of the God of Sinai, from the presence of the God of Israel.

**67:10** You will set apart a voluntary rain, God, for Your inheritance; and it grew weak, but You restored it.

**67:11** Your living creatures dwell in it; You prepared in Your goodness for the poor.

**67:12** God the Lord will give a word to those who proclaim good news with great power;

**67:13** the king of the powers of the beloved, of the beloved, and for the beauty of the house to divide spoils.

**67:14** If you sleep in the midst of the allotments, wings of a dove overlaid with silver, and her back in the greenish-yellow of gold. Interlude.

**67:15** When the Heavenly One separates kings upon it, they will be covered with snow in Zalmon.

**67:16** A mountain of God is a fat mountain, a curdled mountain, a fat mountain.

**67:17** Why do you suppose, curdled mountains? The mountain in which God was well pleased to dwell—for indeed the Lord will take up His dwelling to the end.

**67:18** The chariot of God is ten-thousandfold, thousands of those who prosper; the Lord is among them in Sinai, in the holy place.

**67:19** Having gone up to the height, You captured captivity; You received gifts in man, for even those who disobey, to take up dwelling.

**67:20** The Lord God is blessed, blessed is the Lord day by day, and the God of our salvations will prosper our way. Interlude.

**67:21** Our God is the God of saving, and to the Lord belong the ways out of death.

**67:22** Yet God will crush the heads of His enemies, the crown of hair of those who walk on in his trespasses.

**67:23** The Lord said, "From Bashan I will turn back, I will turn back in the depths of the sea,

**67:24** so that your foot may be dipped in blood, the tongue of your dogs from enemies, from it."

**67:25** Your processions were seen, God, the processions of my God, the King, who is in the holy place.

**67:26** Rulers went ahead, next to those making melody, in the midst of young women playing tambourines.

**67:27** In assemblies bless God, the Lord from the springs of Israel.

**67:28** There is Benjamin, the younger, in ecstasy; rulers of Judah, their leaders; rulers of Zebulun, rulers of Naphtali.

**67:29** Command, God, Your power; strengthen, God, this which You prepared for us.

**67:30** From Your temple at Jerusalem, to You kings will bring gifts.

**67:31** Rebuke the wild beasts of the reed, the assembly of the bulls among the heifers of the peoples, so that those who have been tested with silver may not be shut out; scatter nations that desire wars.

**67:32** Envoys will come from Egypt; Ethiopia will hasten her hand to God.

**67:33** Kingdoms of the earth, sing to God, sing praise to the Lord. Interlude.

**67:34** Sing praise to God, who rides upon the heaven of heaven toward the east. Look, He will give with His voice a voice of power.

**67:35** Give glory to God; over Israel is His majesty, and His power is in the clouds.

**67:36** Wondrous is God in His devout ones; the God of Israel, He Himself will give power and strength to His people. And blessed is God.

## Translator Notes

- **67:7** — Differing from the Hebrew text ("the rebellious dwell in a parched land"), the Septuagint links "those who rebel" with the preceding deliverance and renders the final word as "in tombs" (en taphois).
- **67:13** — The Septuagint diverges markedly from the Hebrew text here, rendering the repeated verb as "of the beloved, of the beloved" (tou agapetou, tou agapetou) and linking "for the beauty of the house" with dividing spoils.
- **67:16** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew "mountain of Bashan" and "many-peaked mountain" as "a fat mountain" (oros pion) and "a curdled mountain" (oros tetyromenon), imagery echoed in verse 17.
- **67:19** — Unlike the Hebrew text, the Septuagint ends verse 19 at "to take up dwelling" and begins verse 20 with "The Lord God is blessed"; it also reads the singular "in man" (en anthropo).
- **67:31** — The Greek text includes the negative particle me ("may not be shut out"), a reading of major early Septuagint manuscripts differing from witnesses that omit it.
- **67:36** — The Greek text reads "in His devout ones" (en tois hosiois autou), a textual variant alongside Septuagint witnesses that read "in His holy ones/sanctuaries" (en tois hagiois autou).
