## Text

**55:1** For the end, concerning the people who have been removed far from the holy things; for David, for a pillar inscription, when the foreigners seized him in Gath.

**55:2** Have mercy on me, Lord, for man trampled on me; all day long, fighting, he afflicted me.

**55:3** My enemies trampled on me all day long from the height of the day; for many who fight against me

**55:4** will be afraid, but I will hope in You.

**55:5** In God I will praise my words all day long; in God I have hoped; I will not fear what flesh will do to me.

**55:6** All day long they detested my words; all their thoughts are against me for evil.

**55:7** They will sojourn and hide; they themselves will watch my heel, just as I waited in my soul;

**55:8** On account of nothing You will save them; in anger You will bring peoples down. God,

**55:9** I declared my life to You; You set my tears before You, as also in Your promise.

**55:10** My enemies will turn back on whatever day I call upon You; look, I know that You are my God.

**55:11** In God I will praise an utterance, in God I will praise a word.

**55:12** In God I have hoped; I will not fear what man will do to me.

**55:13** In me, God, are the vows of praise that I will repay;

**55:14** for You delivered my soul from death, and my feet from slipping, so as to be well-pleasing before God in the light of the living.

## Translator Notes

- **55:1** — The Septuagint superscription renders the heading as "concerning the people who have been removed far from the holy things" and translates Hebrew Miktam as "a pillar inscription"; Greek allophyloi ("foreigners") is the Septuagint's standard term for the Philistines.
- **55:3** — This Greek text reads "from the height of the day" (apo hypsous hemeras) in v. 3 and third-person plural "will be afraid" (phobethesontai) in v. 4, whereas other Septuagint witnesses read "by day I will not fear" and the Hebrew MT has "When I am afraid."
- **55:7** — This Greek text reads the first-person singular hypemeina ("I waited in my soul"), whereas other Septuagint witnesses read third-person plural "they waited."
- **55:8** — Greek hyper tou methenos soseis autous ("On account of nothing You will save them") is a distinctive Septuagint reading (differing from the Hebrew MT), often understood ironically or rhetorically.
- **55:11** — This Greek text repeats "in God" (epi to theo) in both clauses, where other witnesses and the Hebrew MT read "in the Lord" in the second clause.
