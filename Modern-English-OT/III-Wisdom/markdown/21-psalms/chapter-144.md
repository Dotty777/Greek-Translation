## Text

**144:1** A praise of David. I will exalt You, my God, my King, and I will bless Your name forever, and forever and ever.

**144:2** Every day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name.

**144:3** Great is the Lord and exceedingly praiseworthy, and there is no limit to His greatness.

**144:4** Generation and generation will praise Your works, and they will declare Your power;

**144:5** and they will speak of the majesty of the glory of Your holiness, and they will recount Your wonders;

**144:6** and they will tell of the power of Your awesome deeds, and Your greatness, I will recount it, and they will speak of Your might.

**144:7** They will pour forth the memory of the abundance of Your goodness, and they will exult in Your righteousness.

**144:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord, patient and abundant in mercy.

**144:9** Good is the Lord to those who endure, and His compassions are over all His works.

**144:10** Let all Your works give thanks to You, Lord, and let Your holy ones bless You.

**144:11** They will tell of the glory of Your kingdom, and they will speak of Your might,

**144:12** to make known to the sons of men Your might and the glory of the majesty of Your kingdom.

**144:13** Your kingdom is a kingdom of all the ages, and Your dominion is in every generation and generation. Faithful is the Lord in His words, and holy in all His works.

**144:14** The Lord supports all who are falling, and raises up all who are cast down.

**144:15** The eyes of all hope in You, and You give their food at the proper time.

**144:16** You open Your hands, and fill every living creature with goodwill.

**144:17** Righteous is the Lord in all His ways, and holy in all His works.

**144:18** Near is the Lord to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth.

**144:19** He will do the will of those who fear Him, and He will hear their supplication and save them.

**144:20** The Lord keeps all who love Him, and He will utterly destroy all the sinners.

**144:21** My mouth will speak the praise of the Lord, and let all flesh bless His holy name forever, and forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **144:6** — The Greek text shifts from third-person plural ("they will tell") to first-person singular ("I will recount it") and includes a third clause ("and they will speak of Your might") attested in major Septuagint witnesses.
- **144:9** — The Greek text reads τοῖς ὑπομένουσιν ("to those who endure" or "to those who wait patiently"), whereas the Masoretic Text and some Septuagint manuscripts read "to all".
- **144:13** — The second sentence ("Faithful is the Lord in His words, and holy in all His works") preserves the nun line of this acrostic psalm, present in the Septuagint, Dead Sea Scrolls, and Syriac though absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **144:16** — The Greek uses the plural "Your hands" and places εὐδοκίας ("goodwill" or "favor") in the genitive, expressing that God fills every living creature with His goodwill rather than satisfying their desire as in the Masoretic Text.
