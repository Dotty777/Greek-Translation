## Text

**68:1** For the end, concerning those who will be changed; for David.

**68:2** Save me, God, for waters have come in up to my soul.

**68:3** I sank fast into the mire of the deep, and there is no foothold; I came into the depths of the sea, and a storm submerged me.

**68:4** I grew weary crying out, my throat became hoarse, my eyes failed from drawing near to my God.

**68:5** Those who hate me without cause have multiplied beyond the hairs of my head; my enemies who pursue me unjustly have become strong; the things I did not seize, then I was paying back.

**68:6** God, You know my foolishness, and my offenses have not been hidden from You.

**68:7** May those who wait for You not be put to shame on my account, Lord of powers; may those who seek You not be disgraced on my account, God of Israel.

**68:8** Because for Your sake I have borne reproach, disgrace has covered my face;

**68:9** I have become estranged from my brothers, and a stranger to the sons of my mother;

**68:10** because zeal for Your house will consume me, and the reproaches of those who reproach You fell on me.

**68:11** And I bowed down my soul in fasting, and it became a reproach to me;

**68:12** and I made sackcloth my garment, and I became a byword to them.

**68:13** Those who sit in the gate were chattering against me, and those who drink wine were singing about me.

**68:14** But as for me, in my prayer to You, Lord, it is a time of favor, God; in the abundance of Your mercy listen, in the truth of Your salvation.

**68:15** Save me from the clay, so that I may not sink fast; may I be delivered from those who hate me and from the depth of the waters.

**68:16** Do not let a storm of water submerge me, nor let the deep swallow me up, nor let a well shut its mouth over me.

**68:17** Hear me, Lord, for Your mercy is good; according to the abundance of Your compassions, look upon me.

**68:18** Because I am afflicted, listen to me quickly.

**68:19** Attend to my soul and redeem it; because of my enemies, deliver me.

**68:20** For You know my reproach and my shame and my disgrace; before You are all those who afflict me.

**68:21** My soul expected reproach and misery; and I waited for one who would sympathize, and there was none, and for one who would comfort, and I found none.

**68:22** And for my food they gave gall, and for my thirst they gave me sour wine to drink.

**68:23** Let their table before them become a snare, and a retribution, and a stumbling block;

**68:24** let their eyes be darkened so that they do not see, and bow down their back continually;

**68:25** pour out Your wrath upon them, and may the fury of Your wrath overtake them.

**68:26** Let their homestead become desolate, and in their tents let there be no one who dwells;

**68:27** because they pursued the one whom You struck, and they added to the pain of my wounds.

**68:28** Add lawlessness upon their lawlessness, and let them not enter into Your righteousness;

**68:29** let them be blotted out from the book of the living, and let them not be written with the righteous.

**68:30** I am poor and in pain, and the salvation of Your face upheld me.

**68:31** I will praise the name of God with a song, I will magnify Him with praise,

**68:32** and it will please God more than a young calf bringing forth horns and hooves.

**68:33** Let the poor see and be glad; seek God, and you will live.

**68:34** For the Lord has heard the needy, and He has not despised His prisoners.

**68:35** Let the heavens and the earth praise Him, the sea and all things that move in them.

**68:36** For God will save Zion, and the cities of Judea will be built, and they will dwell there and inherit it;

**68:37** and the seed of His servants will possess it, and those who love His name will settle in it.

## Translator Notes

- **68:1** — The Septuagint superscription reads "concerning those who will be changed" (ὑπὲρ τῶν ἀλλοιωθησομένων), interpreting the Hebrew heading ("according to Lilies") from a related root meaning "to change."
- **68:4** — The Greek text reads ἐγγίζειν ("drawing near"), found in major early Septuagint manuscripts, whereas other Greek witnesses read ἐλπίζειν ("hoping") and the Hebrew reads "waiting."
- **68:10** — The Greek text has the future tense καταφάγεταί ("will consume"), matching the quotation in John 2:17, where other Greek witnesses and the Hebrew have a past tense.
- **68:18** — This shorter Greek text omits the opening clause ("and do not turn away Your face from Your servant") found in some Greek manuscripts and the Hebrew.
- **68:23** — The Septuagint renders the Hebrew as ἀνταπόδοσιν ("retribution", cf. Romans 11:9), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "for those at peace" (or "for peace offerings").
