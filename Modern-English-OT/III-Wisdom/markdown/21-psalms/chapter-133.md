## Text

**133:1** A song of the steps. Look now, bless the Lord, all servants of the Lord, who stand in the courts of the house of our God.

**133:2** In the nights, lift up your hands toward the holy place, and bless the Lord.

**133:3** The Lord will bless you from Zion, the One who made heaven and earth.

## Translator Notes

- **133:1** — The Greek text reads "who stand in the courts of the house of our God," without "in the house of the Lord" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and other Septuagint witnesses, and places "in the nights" at the beginning of verse 2.
- **133:2** — Literally "toward the holy things" (εἰς τὰ ἅγια), the standard Greek designation for the sanctuary or holy place.
- **133:3** — The Greek uses the future indicative εὐλογήσει ("will bless") rather than an optative wish ("may... bless").
