## Text

**9:1** For the end, concerning the hidden things of the son; a psalm for David.

**9:2** I will give thanks to You, Lord, with my whole heart; I will recount all Your wonders;

**9:3** I will be glad and exult in You; I will sing praise to Your name, Most High.

**9:4** When my enemy is turned back, they will grow weak and perish from Your presence.

**9:5** For You have executed my judgment and my cause; You sat on a throne, You who judge righteousness.

**9:6** You rebuked nations, and the ungodly one perished; You blotted out their name forever and ever.

**9:7** The swords of the enemy have failed completely, and You tore down cities; their memory perished with a sound;

**9:8** And the Lord remains forever; He has prepared His throne in judgment.

**9:9** And He Himself will judge the inhabited world in righteousness; He will judge peoples in uprightness.

**9:10** And the Lord became a refuge for the needy, a helper at opportune times, in distress.

**9:11** And let those who know Your name hope in You, because You have not forsaken those who seek You out, Lord.

**9:12** Sing praise to the Lord who dwells in Zion; proclaim His deeds among the nations;

**9:13** for He who seeks out their blood has remembered; He has not forgotten the plea of the needy.

**9:14** Have mercy on me, Lord; see my humiliation from my enemies, You who lift me up from the gates of death,

**9:15** so that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion; I will exult in Your salvation.

**9:16** Nations have been stuck fast in the destruction that they made; in this snare that they hid, their foot was caught.

**9:17** The Lord is known as executing judgments; in the works of his hands the sinner was caught. Song of an interlude.

**9:18** Let the sinners be turned back into Hades, all the nations that forget God.

**9:19** For the poor person will not be forgotten to the end; the endurance of the needy will not perish forever.

**9:20** Arise, Lord; let man not grow strong; let nations be judged before You.

**9:21** Appoint, Lord, a lawgiver over them; let nations know that they are men. Interlude.

**9:22** Why, Lord, have You stood far off? Do You take no notice at opportune times, in distress?

**9:23** While the ungodly one acts arrogantly, the poor person is set on fire; they are caught in the schemes that they devise.

**9:24** For the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul, and the one who does injustice is blessed.

**9:25** The sinner has provoked the Lord; according to the abundance of his wrath he will not inquire; God is not before him.

**9:26** His ways are profaned at every time; Your judgments are taken away from his presence; he will have dominion over all his enemies.

**9:27** For he said in his heart, "I will never be shaken, from generation to generation without evil."

**9:28** His mouth is full of cursing and bitterness and deceit; under his tongue are toil and trouble.

**9:29** He sits in ambush with the rich in hidden places to kill an innocent person; his eyes look intently at the needy.

**9:30** He lies in ambush in a hidden place, like a lion in his den; he lies in ambush to seize a poor person, to seize a poor person as he drags him; in his snare

**9:31** he will humble him; he will stoop and fall when he has dominion over the needy.

**9:32** For he said in his heart, "God has forgotten; He has turned away His face so as never to see."

**9:33** Arise, Lord God; let Your hand be lifted up; do not forget the needy.

**9:34** For what reason has the ungodly one provoked God? For he said in his heart, "He will not inquire."

**9:35** You see, for You will observe trouble and fury, to deliver them into Your hands. To You, therefore, the poor person has been left; to an orphan You were a helper.

**9:36** Crush the arm of the sinner and wicked one; his sin will be sought out, and he will by no means be found on account of it.

**9:37** The Lord will reign forever and ever; you will perish, nations, from His land.

**9:38** The Lord has heard the desire of the needy; Your ear has paid attention to the readiness of their heart,

**9:39** to render justice for the orphan and the humble, so that man may no longer boast arrogantly upon the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — "Concerning the hidden things of the son" reflects the Greek understanding of the obscure Hebrew heading al-mut labben.
- **9:7** — The Greek reads "swords" where the Hebrew text has "ruins," and "with a sound" (met' echous) where the Hebrew has an emphatic pronoun ("they themselves").
- **9:17** — Greek ode diapsalmatos ("Song of an interlude") is the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew musical rubric Higgaion Selah.
- **9:21** — The Greek reads "a lawgiver" (nomotheten), understanding the Hebrew term as "teacher/lawgiver" rather than "terror."
- **9:22** — In the Septuagint, Psalm 9 continues here as a single composition; verses 9:22-39 correspond to Psalm 10:1-18 in the Hebrew numbering.
- **9:36** — The Greek text includes "on account of it" (di' auten, feminine referring to "his sin"), indicating that the sinner himself will vanish because of his sin.
