## Text

**36:1** Of David. Do not be envious of those who do evil, nor be jealous of those who practice lawlessness.

**36:2** For like grass they will quickly dry up, and like herbs of green grass they will quickly fall away.

**36:3** Hope in the Lord and practice goodness, and dwell in the land, and you will be pastured on its wealth.

**36:4** Delight in the Lord, and He will give you the requests of your heart.

**36:5** Reveal your way to the Lord and hope in Him, and He Himself will act.

**36:6** And He will bring out your righteousness like light, and your judgment like midday.

**36:7** Submit to the Lord and implore Him; do not be envious of the one who prospers in his life, of a man who practices transgressions.

**36:8** Cease from anger and abandon wrath; do not be envious so as to do evil.

**36:9** For those who do evil will be utterly destroyed, but those who wait for the Lord, they themselves will inherit the land.

**36:10** And yet a little while, and a sinner will by no means exist; and you will look for his place, and you will by no means find it.

**36:11** But the meek will inherit the land, and will delight in an abundance of peace.

**36:12** The sinner will closely watch the righteous one, and will gnash his teeth at him.

**36:13** But the Lord will laugh at him, for He foresees that his day will come.

**36:14** The sinners drew a sword, they bent their bow, to bring down the poor and needy, to slaughter those upright in heart.

**36:15** May their sword enter into their heart, and may their bows be broken.

**36:16** Better is a little for the righteous one than the abundant wealth of sinners.

**36:17** For the arms of sinners will be broken, but the Lord upholds the righteous.

**36:18** The Lord knows the ways of the blameless, and their inheritance will be forever.

**36:19** They will not be put to shame in an evil time, and in days of famine they will be satisfied.

**36:20** For the sinners will perish, and the enemies of the Lord, at the very moment they were glorified and exalted, vanishing like smoke, vanished.

**36:21** The sinner borrows and will not repay, but the righteous one shows compassion and gives.

**36:22** For those who bless Him will inherit the land, but those who curse Him will be utterly destroyed.

**36:23** By the Lord the steps of a man are directed, and He will take pleasure in his way.

**36:24** When he falls, he will not be dashed down, for the Lord supports his hand.

**36:25** I was young, and indeed I have grown old, and I have not seen a righteous one abandoned, nor his offspring seeking bread.

**36:26** All day long he shows mercy and lends, and his offspring will be a blessing.

**36:27** Turn away from evil and do good, and dwell forever and ever.

**36:28** For the Lord loves justice, and He will not abandon His devout ones; they will be kept forever. The blameless will be vindicated, and the offspring of the ungodly will be utterly destroyed.

**36:29** But the righteous will inherit the land, and they will dwell upon it forever and ever.

**36:30** The mouth of a righteous one will meditate on wisdom, and his tongue will speak justice.

**36:31** The law of his God is in his heart, and his steps will not be tripped up.

**36:32** The sinner will observe the righteous one, and he seeks to put him to death.

**36:33** But the Lord will by no means abandon him into his hands, nor will He condemn him when he is on trial with him.

**36:34** Wait for the Lord and keep His way, and He will exalt you to inherit the land; when sinners are utterly destroyed, you will see it.

**36:35** I saw an ungodly one highly exalted and lifted up like the cedars of Lebanon.

**36:36** And I passed by, and look, he was no more; and I sought him, and his place was not found.

**36:37** Keep innocence and see uprightness, for there is a remnant for a peaceable man.

**36:38** But the transgressors will be utterly destroyed together; the remnants of the ungodly will be utterly destroyed.

**36:39** But the salvation of the righteous is with the Lord, and He is their protector in a time of affliction.

**36:40** And the Lord will help them and deliver them, and He will rescue them from sinners and save them, because they hoped in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:3** — Greek reads "you will be pastured on its wealth" (ποιμανθήσῃ ἐπὶ τῷ πλούτῳ αὐτῆς), reflecting a Septuagint rendering distinct from the Masoretic Text's "feed on faithfulness."
- **36:11** — Greek γῆ ("land") can also be rendered "earth," as in the quotation of this verse in Matthew 5:5.
- **36:20** — Greek renders the clause as "at the very moment they were glorified and exalted, vanishing like smoke, vanished," differing markedly from the Masoretic Text's imagery of "the glory of the pastures."
- **36:22** — Greek uses active participles ("those who bless... those who curse") rather than the Hebrew passives ("blessed by... cursed by"); "Him" is understood here as referring to the Lord, though grammatically it could also refer back to the righteous one in verse 21.
- **36:28** — Following the Greek critical text here, ἄμωμοι ἐκδικηθήσονται ("The blameless will be vindicated"); another prominent Septuagint manuscript tradition reads ἄνομοι ἐκδιωχθήσονται ("The lawless will be driven out"), corresponding to the missing ayin line of this acrostic psalm in the Masoretic Text.
