## Text

**116:1** Hallelujah. Praise the Lord, all the nations; let all the peoples praise Him,

**116:2** for His mercy has been made strong over us, and the truth of the Lord remains forever.

## Translator Notes

- **116:1** — Septuagint Psalm 116 corresponds to Hebrew Psalm 117. Unlike the Hebrew second-person imperative, the Greek shifts to a third-person imperative ('let all the peoples praise Him') and uses the simple verb ainesatosan rather than the compound form quoted in Romans 15:11.
- **116:2** — Greek aletheia ('truth') and the explicit verb menei ('remains') reflect the Septuagint wording, where the Hebrew MT has 'faithfulness' without an explicit verb; Greek ekrataiothe ('has been made strong') renders the passive voice literally.
