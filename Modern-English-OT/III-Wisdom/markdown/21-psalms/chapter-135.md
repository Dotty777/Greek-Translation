## Text

**135:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**135:2** Give thanks to the God of gods, for His mercy is forever.

**135:3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His mercy is forever;

**135:4** to Him who alone does great wonders, for His mercy is forever;

**135:5** to Him who made the heavens with understanding, for His mercy is forever;

**135:6** to Him who established the earth upon the water, for His mercy is forever;

**135:7** to Him who alone made great lights, for His mercy is forever,

**135:8** the sun for authority over the day, for His mercy is forever,

**135:9** the moon and the stars for authority over the night, for His mercy is forever;

**135:10** to Him who struck Egypt with their firstborn, for His mercy is forever;

**135:11** and brought Israel out from their midst, for His mercy is forever,

**135:12** with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm, for His mercy is forever;

**135:13** to Him who divided the Red Sea into divisions, for His mercy is forever;

**135:14** and led Israel through its midst, for His mercy is forever;

**135:15** and shook Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea, for His mercy is forever;

**135:16** to Him who led His people through the wilderness, for His mercy is forever;

**135:17** to Him who struck great kings, for His mercy is forever;

**135:18** and killed mighty kings, for His mercy is forever,

**135:19** Sihon king of the Amorites, for His mercy is forever,

**135:20** and Og king of Bashan, for His mercy is forever;

**135:21** and gave their land as an inheritance, for His mercy is forever,

**135:22** an inheritance to Israel His servant, for His mercy is forever.

**135:23** For in our humiliation the Lord remembered us, for His mercy is forever;

**135:24** and He redeemed us from the hand of our enemies, for His mercy is forever;

**135:25** He who gives food to all flesh, for His mercy is forever.

**135:26** Give thanks to the Lord of heaven, for His mercy is forever.

## Translator Notes

- **135:6** — The Greek verb "established" (stereosanti, lit. "made firm") and singular "the water" (to hydor) reflect the Septuagint text rather than the Hebrew "spread out ... the waters."
- **135:7** — The Greek adds "alone" (mono), echoing verse 4; the word is not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **135:12** — The Greek reads "with an uplifted arm" (en brachioni hypselo, lit. "a high arm") where the Hebrew has "an outstretched arm."
- **135:26** — Our Greek text reads "the Lord of heaven" (to kyrio tou ouranou), whereas other Septuagint witnesses and the Hebrew read "the God of heaven."
