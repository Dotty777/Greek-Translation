## Text

**1:1** Blessed is the man who has not walked in the counsel of the ungodly, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat on the seat of the pestilent;

**1:2** rather, his desire is in the law of the Lord, and in His law he will meditate day and night.

**1:3** And he will be like the tree planted beside the channels of the waters, which will give its fruit in its season, and its leaf will not fall off; and everything that he does will be prospered.

**1:4** Not so are the ungodly, not so; rather, they are like the dust that the wind hurls away from the face of the earth.

**1:5** Therefore the ungodly will not rise in judgment, nor sinners in the council of the righteous;

**1:6** for the Lord knows the way of the righteous, and the way of the ungodly will perish.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Greek loimon ("pestilent ones") literally refers to plagues and is used in the Septuagint for corrupt, destructive people, whereas the Hebrew text reads "scoffers."
- **1:4** — Unlike the Hebrew text, the Greek repeats "not so" for emphasis, uses chnous ("fine dust" or "chaff"), and adds "from the face of the earth."
- **1:5** — Greek boule ("council") is the same word rendered "counsel" in 1:1, carrying both the sense of advice and of a deliberative assembly; anastesontai literally means "will rise" rather than Hebrew "will stand."
