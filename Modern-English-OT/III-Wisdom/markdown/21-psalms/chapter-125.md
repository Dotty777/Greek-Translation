## Text

**125:1** A song of the steps. When the Lord brought back the captivity of Zion, we became like those who have been comforted.

**125:2** Then our mouth was filled with joy, and our tongue with exultation; then they will say among the nations, "The Lord did great things with them."

**125:3** The Lord did great things with us; we became glad.

**125:4** Bring back, Lord, our captivity, like the winter torrent in the south.

**125:5** Those who sow in tears will reap in exultation.

**125:6** Going along, they went on and wept, carrying their seeds; but coming, they will arrive in exultation, carrying their sheaves.

## Translator Notes

- **125:1** — The Greek superscription "of the steps" (τῶν ἀναβαθμῶν) corresponds to the Hebrew title commonly translated "of ascents." "Those who have been comforted" (παρακεκλημένοι) faithfully renders the Septuagint reading, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "those who dream."
- **125:2** — The future tense "they will say" (ἐροῦσιν) follows the Greek verbatim, and "did great things with them" renders the Greek idiom ἐμεγάλυνεν τοῦ ποιῆσαι μετ᾽ αὐτῶν (lit., "magnified to do with them").
- **125:4** — The Greek uses the singular "winter torrent" (ὁ χειμάρρους, a seasonal wadi) and "in the south" (ἐν τῷ νότῳ), corresponding to the Hebrew plural streams in the Negev.
