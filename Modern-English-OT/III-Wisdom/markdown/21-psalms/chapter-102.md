## Text

**102:1** Of David. Bless the Lord, my soul, and everything within me, His holy name.

**102:2** Bless the Lord, my soul, and do not forget all His praises;

**102:3** the One who forgives all your lawless deeds, the One who heals all your diseases,

**102:4** the One who redeems your life from corruption, the One who crowns you with mercy and compassion,

**102:5** the One who fills your desire with good things; your youth will be renewed like an eagle's.

**102:6** The Lord performs acts of mercy, and justice for all who are wronged.

**102:7** He made His ways known to Moses; to the sons of Israel, His desires.

**102:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord, patient and abounding in mercy.

**102:9** He will not be angry to the end, nor will He remain wrathful forever.

**102:10** He has not done to us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our lawless deeds.

**102:11** For according to the height of heaven above the earth, the Lord has strengthened His mercy upon those who fear Him.

**102:12** As far as the east is distant from the west, He has removed our lawless deeds far from us.

**102:13** And just as a father has compassion on sons, the Lord has had compassion on those who fear Him,

**102:14** for He Himself knows our form. Remember that we are dust.

**102:15** As for a human being, his days are like grass; like a flower of the field, so he will blossom.

**102:16** For a wind has passed through him, and he will not exist, and he will no longer recognize his place.

**102:17** But the mercy of the Lord is from eternity and to eternity upon those who fear Him, and His righteousness upon sons of sons,

**102:18** for those who keep His covenant and remember His commandments to do them.

**102:19** The Lord has prepared His throne in heaven, and His kingdom holds dominion over all.

**102:20** Bless the Lord, all His angels, mighty in strength, who carry out His word, to hear the voice of His words.

**102:21** Bless the Lord, all His powers, His ministers who carry out His desires.

**102:22** Bless the Lord, all His works, in every place of His dominion; bless the Lord, my soul.

## Translator Notes

- **102:2** — This Greek text reads αἰνέσεις ("praises"), whereas other Septuagint witnesses read ἀνταποδόσεις ("recompenses" or "benefits"), matching the Masoretic Text.
- **102:5** — Greek τὴν ἐπιθυμίαν σου ("your desire") renders a debated Hebrew word variously translated "your years," "your prime," or "your mouth."
- **102:7** — Greek τὰ θελήματα αὐτοῦ (plural, "His desires" or "His will") differs from the Masoretic Text, which reads "His deeds."
- **102:14** — Greek μνήσθητι is a second-person singular imperative ("Remember"), turning the clause into direct address to God, whereas the Masoretic Text reads "He remembers."
- **102:16** — In Greek, τὸν τόπον αὐτοῦ ("his place") is accusative, making the human being the subject ("he will no longer recognize his place"), whereas in the Masoretic Text "its place" is the subject ("its place knows it no more").
