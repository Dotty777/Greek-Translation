## Text

**54:1** For the end, with hymns; of understanding, for David.

**54:2** Give ear, God, to my prayer, and do not overlook my supplication,

**54:3** attend to me and hear me. I was grieved in my meditation, and I was troubled

**54:4** because of the voice of an enemy and because of the affliction of a sinner; for they turned lawlessness upon me, and in anger they held a grudge against me.

**54:5** My heart was troubled within me, and dread of death fell upon me.

**54:6** Fear and trembling came upon me, and darkness covered me.

**54:7** And I said, "Who will give me wings like those of a dove, and I will fly and rest?"

**54:8** Look, I went far away fleeing, and I lodged in the wilderness. Interlude.

**54:9** I was waiting for Him who saves me from faintheartedness and storm.

**54:10** Submerge, Lord, and divide their tongues, for I saw lawlessness and strife in the city.

**54:11** Day and night it will encircle her upon her walls; lawlessness and toil are in her midst,

**54:12** and injustice, and trouble and deceit did not depart from her squares.

**54:13** For if an enemy had reproached me, I would have endured it, and if the one who hates had spoken boastfully against me, I would have hidden from him.

**54:14** But you, a man of like soul, my guide and my acquaintance,

**54:15** who together sweetened meals; in the house of God we walked in harmony.

**54:16** Let death come upon them, and let them go down to Hades alive; for evils are in their dwellings, in their midst.

**54:17** I cried out to God, and the Lord heard me.

**54:18** Evening and morning and noon I will recount; I will announce, and He will hear my voice.

**54:19** He will redeem my soul in peace from those who draw near to me, for among many they were with me.

**54:20** God will hear and will humble them, He who exists before the ages. Interlude.

**54:21** For there is no exchange for them, and they did not fear God. He stretched out His hand in repaying; they profaned His covenant,

**54:22** they were scattered from the anger of His face, and his heart drew near; his words were made softer than oil, yet they are darts.

**54:23** Cast your anxiety upon the Lord, and He Himself will nourish you; He will never give shaking to the righteous one.

**54:24** But You, God, will bring them down into a pit of corruption; men of bloodshed and deceit will by no means reach half their days; but I will hope in You, Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:3** — Greek adoleschia ("meditation") can also convey anxious musing or complaining.
- **54:9** — The Septuagint reads oligopsychias ("faintheartedness"), where the Hebrew MT has "rushing wind."
- **54:15** — The Septuagint reads "sweetened meals" (eglykanas edesmata), differing from the Hebrew MT "sweetened counsel/fellowship."
- **54:21** — In the Septuagint, vv. 21-22 differ markedly from the Hebrew MT; "He stretched out His hand in repaying; they profaned His covenant" and "the anger of His face" (v. 22) naturally follow God as the singular antecedent before shifting to the deceitful adversary later in v. 22.
