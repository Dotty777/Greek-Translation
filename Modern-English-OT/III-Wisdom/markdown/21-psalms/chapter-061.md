## Text

**61:1** For the end, for Jeduthun; a psalm for David.

**61:2** Will not my soul be subject to God? For from Him is my salvation.

**61:3** For indeed He is my God and my Savior, my protector; I will certainly not be shaken any further.

**61:4** How long will you attack a man? All of you commit murder, as against a leaning wall and a pushed-over fence.

**61:5** Yet they planned to thrust away my honor; they ran in thirst; with their mouth they were blessing, and in their heart they were cursing. Interlude.

**61:6** Yet be subject to God, my soul, for from Him is my endurance.

**61:7** For He is my God and my Savior, my protector; I will certainly not move away.

**61:8** In God are my salvation and my glory; He is the God of my help, and my hope is in God.

**61:9** Hope in Him, all the congregation of the people; pour out your hearts before Him; God is our helper. Interlude.

**61:10** Yet futile are the sons of men; false are the sons of men in scales to do injustice; they themselves are of futility altogether.

**61:11** Do not hope in injustice, and do not long for plunder; if wealth flows, do not attach your heart to it.

**61:12** Once God spoke; these two things I heard, that power belongs to God,

**61:13** and to You, Lord, belongs mercy; for You will repay each one according to his works.

## Translator Notes

- **61:2** — Greek opens with a rhetorical question ('Will not my soul be subject to God?') where the Hebrew text states 'For God alone my soul waits in silence.'
- **61:5** — Greek reads 'they ran in thirst' where the Hebrew text reads 'they delight in falsehood.'
- **61:9** — Greek reads 'all the congregation of the people' (reflecting Hebrew edah, 'congregation') rather than Hebrew 'at all times, O people' (et, 'time').
- **61:10** — Greek repeats 'the sons of men' in both clauses (where Hebrew contrasts men of low and high degree), reads 'to do injustice' (instead of Hebrew 'to go up'), and renders Hebrew 'lighter than breath' as 'of futility.'
