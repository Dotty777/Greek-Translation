## Text

**32:1** For David. Exult, you righteous, in the Lord; praise is fitting for the upright.

**32:2** Give thanks to the Lord with a lyre; with a ten-stringed harp make music to Him.

**32:3** Sing to Him a new song; make music skillfully with a joyful shout.

**32:4** For the word of the Lord is upright, and all His works are in faithfulness.

**32:5** He loves compassion and justice; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord.

**32:6** By the word of the Lord the heavens were made firm, and by the breath of His mouth all their power.

**32:7** He gathers the waters of the sea as in a wineskin; He puts the deeps in storehouses.

**32:8** Let all the earth fear the Lord, and because of Him let all who dwell in the world be shaken.

**32:9** For He spoke, and they came into being; He commanded, and they were created.

**32:10** The Lord scatters the counsels of the nations; He nullifies the thoughts of the peoples, and He nullifies the counsels of rulers.

**32:11** But the counsel of the Lord remains forever, the thoughts of His heart from generations to generations.

**32:12** Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, a people whom He chose as an inheritance for Himself.

**32:13** From heaven the Lord looked down; He saw all the sons of men.

**32:14** From His prepared dwelling place He looked down upon all who dwell on the earth,

**32:15** the One who formed their hearts individually, the One who understands all their works.

**32:16** A king is not saved by great power, and a giant will not be saved by the abundance of his strength.

**32:17** A horse is false for salvation, and by the abundance of its power it will not be saved.

**32:18** Look, the eyes of the Lord are upon those who fear Him, those who hope in His mercy,

**32:19** to deliver their souls from death and to nourish them in famine.

**32:20** Our soul waits patiently for the Lord, for He is our helper and protector.

**32:21** For in Him our heart will be glad, and in His holy name we have hoped.

**32:22** May Your mercy, Lord, be upon us, just as we have hoped in You.

## Translator Notes

- **32:5** — Greek eleemosyne ("compassion" or "almsgiving") stands where the Masoretic Text reads "righteousness."
- **32:7** — Greek hos askon literally means "as a wineskin," reflecting a different Hebrew vocalization (nod, "wineskin") from the Masoretic Text (ned, "heap").
- **32:10** — The third clause, "and He nullifies the counsels of rulers," is a Septuagint reading not found in the Masoretic Text.
- **32:15** — Greek kata monas means "individually" or "one by one," whereas the Masoretic Text reads "all alike" or "together."
