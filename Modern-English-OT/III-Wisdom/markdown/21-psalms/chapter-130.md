## Text

**130:1** A song of the steps. For David. Lord, my heart was not lifted up, nor were my eyes raised high; nor did I walk in great things nor in wondrous things beyond me,

**130:2** but rather I was humble-minded. And I lifted up my soul, like the weaned child upon its mother, until You repay upon my soul.

**130:3** Let Israel hope in the Lord from now on and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **130:2** — This Greek text connects "ei me etapeinophronoun" ("but rather I was humble-minded") to verse 1, then reads "kai hypsosa" ("and I lifted up") and "heos antapodoseis" ("until You repay"). Other Greek manuscripts read "alla" ("but") and "hos" ("so") as an oath, while the Hebrew reads "I have calmed and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with its mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me."
