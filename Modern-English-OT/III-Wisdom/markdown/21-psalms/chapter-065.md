## Text

**65:1** For the end; a song of a psalm of resurrection. Raise a joyful shout to God, all the earth;

**65:2** sing praise now to His name; give glory to His praise.

**65:3** Say to God, "How fearsome are Your works! In the abundance of Your power Your enemies will lie to You.

**65:4** Let all the earth worship You and sing praise to You; let them sing praise to Your name." Interlude.

**65:5** Come, children, and see the works of God; He is fearsome in counsels beyond the sons of men,

**65:6** who turns the sea into dry land; through a river they will pass on foot. There we will be glad in Him,

**65:7** who rules in the might of His eternity. His eyes look upon the nations; let those who rebel not be exalted in themselves. Interlude.

**65:8** Bless our God, nations, and make the voice of His praise heard,

**65:9** who placed my soul into life, and did not give my feet to shaking.

**65:10** For You tested us, God, and You refined us with fire as silver is refined with fire.

**65:11** You brought us into the snare; You placed afflictions before us;

**65:12** You made men ride upon our heads; we passed through fire and water, and You brought us out into refreshment.

**65:13** I will enter into Your house with whole burnt offerings; I will pay You my vows,

**65:14** which my lips uttered and my mouth spoke in my affliction.

**65:15** Whole burnt offerings full of marrow I will offer up to You with incense and rams; I will offer to You oxen with male goats. Interlude.

**65:16** Come, listen, and I will recount, all you who fear the Lord, all that He has done for my soul.

**65:17** To Him I cried out with my mouth, and I exalted Him under my tongue.

**65:18** If I looked upon unrighteousness in the heart, let the Lord not hear.

**65:19** Therefore God heard me; He paid attention to the voice of my prayer.

**65:20** Blessed is God, who did not remove my prayer and His mercy from me.

## Translator Notes

- **65:1** — The Septuagint superscription adds "of resurrection" (anastaseos), which is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **65:5** — The Greek text includes the vocative "children" (tekna), a reading found in several Septuagint witnesses though absent from the Hebrew text.
- **65:11** — The Greek text reads "before us" (enopion hemon), a Septuagint variant differing from witnesses (and the Hebrew text) that read "upon our back."
- **65:16** — The Greek text here reads "the Lord" (ton kyrion), whereas the Hebrew text and some Septuagint witnesses read "God."
