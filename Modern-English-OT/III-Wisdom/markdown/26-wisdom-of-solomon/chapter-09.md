## Text

**9:1** "God of the fathers and Lord of Your mercy, who made all things by Your word,

**9:2** and by Your wisdom fashioned man, so that he might have dominion over the creatures made by You,

**9:3** and rule the world in holiness and righteousness, and render judgment in uprightness of soul:

**9:4** give me wisdom, who sits beside Your throne, and do not reject me from among Your children.

**9:5** For I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant, a weak and short-lived man, and deficient in comprehension of judgment and laws.

**9:6** For even if someone is perfect among the sons of men, if the wisdom that comes from You is absent, he will be counted as nothing.

**9:7** You chose me as king of Your people and judge of Your sons and daughters.

**9:8** You gave command to build a temple on Your holy mountain, and an altar in the city of Your dwelling, a copy of the holy tent that You prepared beforehand from the beginning.

**9:9** And with You is wisdom, who knows Your works and was present when You were making the world, and who understands what is pleasing in Your eyes and what is right in Your commandments.

**9:10** Send her forth from the holy heavens, and from the throne of Your glory send her, so that being present with me she may labor, and I may know what is well-pleasing before You.

**9:11** For she knows and comprehends all things, and she will guide me prudently in my actions and guard me in her glory.

**9:12** And my works will be acceptable, and I will judge Your people righteously, and I will be worthy of my father's throne.

**9:13** For what man will know the counsel of God? Or who will conceive what the Lord wills?

**9:14** For the reasonings of mortals are timid, and our designs are precarious;

**9:15** for a perishable body weighs down a soul, and the earthy tent presses down a mind full of many cares.

**9:16** And we scarcely surmise the things on earth, and the things at hand we find with labor; but who has traced out the things in the heavens?

**9:17** And who has known Your counsel, unless You gave wisdom and sent Your Holy Spirit from on high?

**9:18** And in this way the paths of those on earth were set straight, and people were taught the things pleasing to You, and by wisdom they were saved."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Following the critical Greek text reading tou eleous sou ("of Your mercy"); some Greek witnesses omit sou ("Lord of mercy").
- **9:4** — Greek thronōn (lit. "thrones," also in 9:12) is a poetic plural referring to a single royal throne (cf. singular thronou in 9:10); Greek paidōn can mean either "children" or "servants."
- **9:15** — The imagery of a perishable body and "earthy tent" (skēnos) weighing down the soul echoes Hellenistic philosophical language and parallels the tent metaphor of 2 Corinthians 5:1–4.
