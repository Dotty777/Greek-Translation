## Text

**16:1** Therefore they were deservedly punished by similar things, and were tormented by a multitude of vermin.

**16:2** Instead of this punishment, showing kindness to Your people, You prepared quail as food, an unusual taste for the desire of appetite,

**16:3** so that the former, desiring food, might turn away even from necessary appetite because of the sight shown of the things sent against them, while the latter, having been in want for a little while, might even share in an unusual taste.

**16:4** For it was necessary that relentless want should come upon those who were acting as tyrants, while to these it should only be shown how their enemies were being tormented.

**16:5** For even when the terrible fury of wild beasts came upon them, and they were perishing by the bites of twisting serpents, Your wrath did not remain to the end;

**16:6** but for admonition they were troubled for a little while, having a symbol of salvation as a reminder of the commandment of Your law.

**16:7** For the one who turned was not saved because of what was looked at, but because of You, the Savior of all.

**16:8** And in this also You convinced our enemies that You are the One who delivers from every evil.

**16:9** For the bites of locusts and flies killed them, and no healing was found for their life, because they deserved to be punished by such creatures;

**16:10** but not even the teeth of venomous serpents conquered Your sons, for Your mercy came to their aid and healed them.

**16:11** For they were stung as a reminder of Your oracles, and were swiftly rescued, so that, falling into deep forgetfulness, they might not become unresponsive to Your kindness.

**16:12** For indeed, neither herb nor poultice cured them, but Your word, Lord, which heals all things.

**16:13** For You have authority over life and death, and You bring down to the gates of Hades and bring up.

**16:14** But a man, though he kills by his wickedness, does not bring back a spirit that has departed, nor release a soul that has been taken.

**16:15** Yet it is impossible to escape Your hand.

**16:16** For the ungodly, refusing to know You, were scourged by the strength of Your arm, pursued by unusual rains and hailstorms and relentless downpours, and consumed by fire.

**16:17** For most astonishing of all, in the water that quenches all things, the fire was all the more active; for the world is a defender of the righteous.

**16:18** For at one time the flame was tamed, so that it might not burn up the animals sent against the ungodly, but that they themselves, seeing it, might know that they were being driven by God's judgment;

**16:19** and at another time, even in the midst of water, it burns beyond the power of fire, so that it may destroy the produce of an unrighteous land.

**16:20** Instead of these things, You fed Your people the food of angels, and sent them prepared bread from heaven without toil, capable of every delight and suited to every taste.

**16:21** For Your sustenance manifested Your sweetness toward children; and serving the desire of the one who ate, it transformed itself into whatever anyone wished.

**16:22** And snow and ice endured fire and did not melt, so that they might know that fire, burning in the hail and flashing in the rains, was destroying the enemies' crops;

**16:23** yet this fire again, so that the righteous might be nourished, has even forgotten its own power.

**16:24** For creation, serving You who made it, intensifies for punishment against the unrighteous, and relaxes into kindness on behalf of those who trust in You.

**16:25** Therefore at that time also, transformed into all things, it served Your all-nourishing gift, according to the wish of those in need,

**16:26** so that Your sons, whom You loved, Lord, might learn that it is not the production of crops that nourishes a person, but Your word preserves those who believe in You.

**16:27** For what was not destroyed by fire melted when merely warmed by a brief ray of the sun,

**16:28** so that it would be known that one must be ahead of the sun for thanksgiving to You, and at the rising of the light pray to You.

**16:29** For the hope of an ungrateful person will melt like winter frost, and will flow away like useless water.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The Greek text reads ten deichtheisan ('the [sight] shown'), a feminine participle without an expressed noun (perhaps implying 'sight' or 'appearance'). Many critics emend or adopt the reading eidechtheian ('loathsomeness' or 'hideousness').
- **16:10** — The verb antiparelthen literally means 'came forward to meet' or 'intervened to help,' used positively here of God's rescuing mercy (contrast its sense 'passed by on the other side' in Luke 10:31-32).
- **16:11** — The adjective aperispastoi ordinarily means 'undistracted,' but here with the genitive it carries the sense 'unmoved by,' 'unresponsive to,' or 'cut off from' God's kindness.
- **16:21** — Greek hypostasis can mean either 'sustenance/provision' (referring to the manna) or 'substance/nature' (referring to God's own essence revealed in the gift).
- **16:25** — The verb metalleuo ordinarily means 'to mine,' but the author of Wisdom uses it (here and in 4:12) to mean 'to alter' or 'to transform,' apparently playing on meta ('change') and allos ('other').
