## Text

**5:1** Then the righteous one will stand with great boldness before the face of those who afflicted him and those who disregarded his labors.

**5:2** At the sight they will be troubled with dreadful fear, and they will be astounded at the unexpectedness of salvation.

**5:3** They will say among themselves in remorse, and through distress of spirit they will groan, "This was the one whom we once held as an object of laughter and as a byword of reproach,

**5:4** we fools! We counted his life madness and his end without honor.

**5:5** How was he numbered among the sons of God, and his lot is among the holy ones?

**5:6** So then we strayed from the way of truth, and the light of righteousness did not shine on us, and the sun did not rise for us.

**5:7** We had our fill of the paths of lawlessness and destruction, and we traveled through trackless deserts, but we did not know the way of the Lord.

**5:8** What did arrogance profit us? And what has wealth with boasting contributed to us?

**5:9** All those things passed away like a shadow, and like a message hurrying by;

**5:10** like a ship passing through billowing water, of which, when it has gone by, no trace can be found, nor the track of its keel in the waves;

**5:11** or as when a bird flies through the air, no evidence of its course is found, but the light air, lashed by the stroke of pinions and split by the force of their rushing, is traversed as wings move, and after this no sign of passage is found in it;

**5:12** or as when an arrow is shot at a target, the parted air immediately closes back into itself, so that its path is unknown;

**5:13** so we also, once born, ceased to be, and we had no sign of virtue to show, but in our evil we were utterly consumed."

**5:14** For the hope of an ungodly person is like chaff carried by wind, and like thin frost driven by a storm, and like smoke it is dispersed by wind, and like the remembrance of a one-day guest it passes by.

**5:15** But the righteous live forever, and their reward is in the Lord, and care for them is with the Most High.

**5:16** Therefore they will receive the royal crown of splendor and the diadem of beauty from the hand of the Lord, because with His right hand He will shelter them, and with His arm He will shield them.

**5:17** He will take His zeal as full armor, and He will arm creation for vengeance against enemies.

**5:18** He will put on righteousness as a breastplate, and He will wear impartial judgment as a helmet.

**5:19** He will take holiness as an invincible shield,

**5:20** and He will sharpen relentless wrath into a sword, and the world will fight alongside Him against the senseless.

**5:21** Well-aimed bolts of lightning will go forth, and as from a well-curved bow of the clouds they will leap to the target,

**5:22** and from a catapult hailstones full of fury will be hurled; the water of the sea will rage against them, and rivers will overwhelm them relentlessly.

**5:23** A wind of power will stand against them, and like a storm it will winnow them away; and all lawlessness will lay waste the earth, and evildoing will overturn the thrones of rulers.

## Translator Notes

- **5:7** — Greek reads eneplēsthēmen ("we were filled / had our fill"); a conjectural emendation reads eneplakēmen ("we were entangled").
- **5:14** — Greek reads pachnē ("frost"), matching the major uncial witnesses; other manuscripts or conjectures read achnē ("spray" or "chaff") or arachnē ("spiderweb").
- **5:16** — Greek basileion can mean either "royal crown" (parallel here with "diadem") or "kingdom."
- **5:23** — The Greek critical text reads nominative pasa ... anomia ("all lawlessness") as the subject rather than accusative pasan tēn gēn ("the whole earth") found in some other witnesses.
