## Text

**6:1** Hear therefore, kings, and understand; learn, judges of the ends of the earth.

**6:2** Give ear, you who rule over a multitude and boast over throngs of nations.

**6:3** For dominion was given to you from the Lord, and sovereignty from the Most High, who will examine your works and search out your counsels,

**6:4** because, being servants of His kingdom, you did not judge rightly, nor keep the law, nor walk according to the counsel of God.

**6:5** Terribly and swiftly He will come upon you, because relentless judgment comes upon those in high places.

**6:6** For the lowliest may be pardoned in mercy, but the mighty will be mightily tested.

**6:7** For the Master of all will not shrink before any person, nor stand in awe of greatness, because He Himself made small and great, and He provides for all alike.

**6:8** But for the powerful, a strict inquiry awaits.

**6:9** To you therefore, O monarchs, are my words, so that you may learn wisdom and not fall away.

**6:10** For those who keep holy things in holiness will be made holy, and those who have been taught them will find a defense.

**6:11** Therefore desire my words; long for them, and you will be instructed.

**6:12** Wisdom is radiant and unfading, and she is easily seen by those who love her.

**6:13** She anticipates those who desire her, making herself known beforehand.

**6:14** The one who rises early for her will not grow weary, for he will find her sitting beside his gates.

**6:15** For to reflect upon her is the perfection of prudence, and the one who stays awake for her sake will quickly be free from anxiety.

**6:16** For she herself goes about seeking those worthy of her, and in the paths she appears graciously to them, and in every thought she meets them.

**6:17** For the truest beginning of her is desire for instruction, and care for instruction is love,

**6:18** and love is the keeping of her laws, and attention to laws is the assurance of incorruptibility,

**6:19** and incorruptibility causes one to be near God.

**6:20** Therefore desire for wisdom leads up to a kingdom.

**6:21** If therefore you delight in thrones and scepters, monarchs of peoples, honor wisdom, so that you may reign forever.

**6:22** Now I will declare what wisdom is and how she came into being, and I will not hide mysteries from you, but I will trace her out from the beginning of creation and bring the knowledge of her into the open, and I will by no means pass by the truth.

**6:23** Neither indeed will I travel with wasting envy, because it will have no fellowship with wisdom.

**6:24** But a multitude of the wise is the salvation of the world, and a prudent king is the stability of a people.

**6:25** Therefore be instructed by my words, and you will profit.

## Translator Notes

- **6:9** — Greek tyrannoi in this context is a neutral Hellenistic title meaning "monarchs" or "sovereigns" (also in 6:21), not the modern pejorative "tyrants."
- **6:10** — The Greek uses a threefold wordplay on the same root: hosiōs ta hosia hosiōthēsontai ("who keep holy things in holiness will be made holy").
- **6:12** — The Saints Greek Critical Text follows the shorter uncial reading and does not include the additional clause "and is found by those who seek her" present in some other witnesses.
