## Text

**3:1** But the souls of the righteous are in God's hand, and torment will by no means touch them.

**3:2** In the eyes of the foolish they appeared to have died,

**3:3** and their departure was considered affliction, and their journey away from us ruin; but they are in peace.

**3:4** For even if they are punished in the sight of people, their hope is full of immortality.

**3:5** And having been disciplined a little, they will receive great good, because God tested them and found them worthy of Himself.

**3:6** Like gold in a smelting furnace He proved them, and like a whole burnt offering of sacrifice He accepted them.

**3:7** And in the time of their visitation they will shine forth, and like sparks among stubble they will run about.

**3:8** They will judge nations and rule over peoples, and the Lord will reign over them forever.

**3:9** Those who have put their trust in Him will understand truth, and those who are faithful will remain with Him in love, for grace and mercy belong to His chosen ones.

**3:10** But the ungodly will receive punishment according to what they reasoned, those who disregarded the righteous one and turned away from the Lord.

**3:11** For whoever despises wisdom and discipline is wretched, and their hope is empty, and their labors are unprofitable, and their works are useless.

**3:12** Their wives are foolish, and their children are wicked;

**3:13** cursed is their offspring. For blessed is the barren woman who is undefiled, who has not known a bed in transgression; she will have fruit in the visitation of souls.

**3:14** And blessed is the eunuch who has done no lawless deed with his hand, nor thought evil things against the Lord; for a choice favor will be given to him for his faithfulness, and a more pleasing lot in the temple of the Lord.

**3:15** For the fruit of good labors is glorious, and the root of understanding is unfailing.

**3:16** But children of adulterers will not reach maturity, and the seed from an unlawful bed will be wiped out.

**3:17** For even if they become long-lived, they will be considered as nothing, and in the end their old age will be without honor.

**3:18** And if they die quickly, they have no hope, nor consolation in the day of decision;

**3:19** for the end of an unrighteous generation is harsh.

## Translator Notes

- **3:9** — Following Codices Vaticanus and Sinaiticus, the Greek text ends with 'His chosen ones,' omitting the additional clause 'and He watches over His holy ones' found in some witnesses.
- **3:10** — Greek tou dikaiou can be taken either as masculine ('the righteous one,' cf. 2:12) or as neuter ('what is right').
- **3:14** — The opening predicate 'blessed is' is implied from v. 13, and 'a more pleasing lot in the temple of the Lord' alludes to the promise to the eunuch in Isaiah 56:4-5.
- **3:18** — Greek diagnoseos ('decision') is a legal term referring to judicial examination and verdict.
