## Text

**1:1** Love righteousness, you who judge the earth; think about the Lord in goodness, and in simplicity of heart seek Him.

**1:2** For He is found by those who do not test Him, and He manifests Himself to those who do not distrust Him.

**1:3** For crooked reasonings separate one from God, and the power, when tested, convicts the foolish.

**1:4** For wisdom will not enter into a soul that devises evil, nor will it dwell in a body indebted to sin.

**1:5** For the holy spirit of discipline will flee from deceit, and will depart from senseless reasonings, and will be put to shame when unrighteousness approaches.

**1:6** For wisdom is a spirit that loves humanity, yet it will not hold a blasphemer guiltless for his lips; because God is a witness of his inward parts, and a true overseer of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.

**1:7** For the Spirit of the Lord has filled the inhabited world, and that which holds all things together has knowledge of speech.

**1:8** Because of this, no one who utters unrighteous things will escape notice, nor indeed will justice, when it convicts, pass him by.

**1:9** For there will be an examination into the counsels of an ungodly person, and the report of his words will come to the Lord for the conviction of his lawless deeds.

**1:10** Because an ear of jealousy listens to all things, and the sound of grumblings is not hidden.

**1:11** Guard yourselves, therefore, against useless grumbling, and spare your tongue from slander; for a secret utterance will not go empty, and a lying mouth destroys a soul.

**1:12** Do not eagerly desire death in the error of your life, nor draw destruction upon yourselves by the works of your hands;

**1:13** for God did not make death, nor does He delight in the ruin of the living.

**1:14** For He created all things so that they might exist, and the creatures of the world are wholesome, and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor is there a kingdom of Hades on earth.

**1:15** For righteousness is immortal.

**1:16** But the ungodly summoned him with their hands and with their words; considering him a friend, they pined away, and they made a covenant with him, because they are worthy to belong to his portion.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Greek elenchthesetai literally means 'will be put to shame' or 'will be rebuked,' here conveying the sense of being offended or repulsed at the approach of unrighteousness.
- **1:6** — Greek ton nephron literally means 'of his kidneys,' a biblical idiom for the seat of one's deepest thoughts, emotions, and conscience.
- **1:14** — Greek hai geneseis literally means 'the generations' or 'things brought into being,' often understood here as 'the creatures' or 'the generative forces.'
- **1:16** — The masculine pronouns ('him,' 'his') refer back to personified Death (v. 13; cf. Hades, v. 14). Greek etakesan ('pined away') literally means 'melted' or 'wasted away.'
