## Text

**1:1** And it happened, after Israel was taken captive and Jerusalem was laid waste, that Jeremiah sat weeping, and he lamented this lament over Jerusalem and said: Aleph. "How has she sat alone, the city that abounded with peoples? She has become like a widow, she who abounded among the nations; a ruler among the provinces, she has become subject to tribute.

**1:2** Beth. Weeping, she wept in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks, and there is no one who comforts her from among all those who love her; all those who cherish her have broken faith with her; they have become enemies to her.

**1:3** Gimel. Judea was deported because of her humiliation and because of the abundance of her servitude; she sat among the nations, she found no rest; all those pursuing her overtook her in the midst of those who afflict.

**1:4** Daleth. The roads of Zion mourn because there are none coming at the festival; all her gates are desolate, her priests groan, her virgins are being led away, and she herself is embittered within herself.

**1:5** He. Those who afflict her have become the head, and her enemies prospered, because the Lord humiliated her on account of the multitude of her impieties; her infants went into captivity before the face of one who afflicts.

**1:6** Waw. And all her splendor was taken away from the daughter of Zion; her rulers became like rams not finding pasture, and they went without strength before the face of one who pursues.

**1:7** Zayin. Jerusalem remembered the days of her humiliation and of her rejections, all her desirable things, as many as existed from ancient days, when her people fell into the hands of one who afflicts, and there was no one who helped her. Her enemies saw and laughed at her dwelling.

**1:8** Heth. Jerusalem sinned a sin; therefore she became an object of shaking; all those who honored her humiliated her, for they saw her indecency; and indeed she herself groans and has turned backward.

**1:9** Teth. Her uncleanness is at her feet; she did not remember her end, and she brought down extravagant things; there is no one who comforts her. See, Lord, my humiliation, for an enemy has magnified himself.

**1:10** Yod. One who afflicts stretched out his hand over all her desirable things; for she saw nations enter into her sanctuary, whom You commanded not to enter into Your assembly.

**1:11** Kaph. All her people are groaning, seeking bread; they gave her desirable things for food to restore life. See, Lord, and look, for she has become dishonored.

**1:12** Lamed. To you, all who pass along the way, turn and see if there is pain like my pain that has come about; having spoken against me, the Lord humiliated me in the day of the anger of His fury.

**1:13** Mem. From His height He sent fire; into my bones He brought it down; He spread out a net for my feet; He turned me backward; He made me desolate, in pain all the day.

**1:14** Nun. Watch was kept over my impieties; in my hands they were intertwined; they went up onto my neck; my strength grew weak, for the Lord put pains into my hands; I will not be able to stand.

**1:15** Samekh. The Lord removed all my strong ones from my midst; He summoned an appointed time against me to crush my chosen ones; the Lord trod a winepress for the virgin daughter of Judah; over these things I weep.

**1:16** Ayin. My eye has brought down water, for the one who comforts me is far removed from me, the one who restores my life; my sons have become desolate, for the enemy has prevailed.

**1:17** Pe. Zion spread out her hands; there is no one who comforts her. The Lord gave a command to Jacob: all around him are those who afflict him; Jerusalem has become a menstruating woman in their midst.

**1:18** Tsade. The Lord is righteous, for I embittered His mouth. Hear now, all you peoples, and see my pain: my virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.

**1:19** Qoph. I called to my lovers, but they deceived me; my priests and my elders expired in the city, for they sought food for themselves so that they might restore their lives, and they found none.

**1:20** Resh. See, Lord, that I am afflicted; my belly has been troubled, and my heart has been overturned within me; for embittering, I have been embittered; outside, a sword has bereaved, like death in the house.

**1:21** Shin. Hear now that I groan; there is no one who comforts me; all my enemies heard of my calamities and rejoiced, because You have done it; You brought on a day, You summoned an appointed time, they became like me.

**1:22** Taw. May all their wickedness come before Your face, and glean them in the way they made a gleaning on account of all my sins, for many are my groanings, and my heart is grieved."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Greek text opens with a narrative prologue attributing the lament to Jeremiah (not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text) and retains the transliterated Hebrew alphabet acrostic headings throughout chapters 1-2.
- **1:3** — The Greek uses the proper name Judea (rather than Judah, as in 1:15) and renders the final phrase as "those who afflict," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "narrow straits."
- **1:12** — The Greek opens with the difficult phrasing "To you, all who pass..." and adds the participle "having spoken against me," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("Is it nothing to you... which was dealt to me").
- **1:14** — Instead of the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "yoke of my transgressions... bound by His hand," the Greek reads "Watch was kept over my impieties; in my hands they were intertwined," and concludes "put pains into my hands."
- **1:22** — The Greek translates the Hebrew verb for dealing harshly as a vineyard-gleaning metaphor ("glean / gleaning," repeated in 2:20) and uses the third-person plural "they made" rather than the Masoretic Text's second-person singular.
