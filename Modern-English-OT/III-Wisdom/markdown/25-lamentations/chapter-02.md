## Text

**2:1** Aleph. How the Lord has darkened the daughter of Zion in His anger! He has cast down from heaven to earth the glory of Israel, and He has not remembered the footstool of His feet.

**2:2** Beth. In the day of His anger the Lord engulfed, not sparing; all the beautiful things of Jacob He tore down in His fury; the strongholds of the daughter of Judah He pressed to the ground; He profaned her kings and her rulers.

**2:3** Gimel. He called together in the anger of His fury every horn of Israel; He turned back His right hand from the face of an enemy, and He kindled in Jacob a flame like fire, and it devoured everything all around.

**2:4** Daleth. He bent His bow like a hostile enemy; He steadied His right hand like an adversary, and He killed all the desirable things of my eyes in the tent of the daughter of Zion; He poured out His fury like fire.

**2:5** He. The Lord has become like an enemy; He has engulfed Israel; He has engulfed her citadels; He has destroyed his strongholds, and He has multiplied for the daughter of Judah one being humiliated and one who has been humiliated.

**2:6** Waw. And He spread out His tabernacle like a vine; He destroyed His festival; the Lord forgot what He made in Zion of festival and Sabbath, and He provoked in the indignation of His anger king and priest and ruler.

**2:7** Zayin. The Lord has rejected His altar; He has shaken off His sanctuary; He has crushed in His hand the wall of her citadels; they gave voice in the house as on a day of festival.

**2:8** Heth. And He turned to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion; He stretched out a measure; He did not turn back His hand from trampling; and the outer wall mourned, and the wall grew weak together.

**2:9** Teth. Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has destroyed and crushed her bars, her king and her rulers among the nations; there is no law, and indeed her prophets saw no vision from the Lord.

**2:10** Yod. The elders of the daughter of Zion sat on the ground, they kept silent; they cast up dust onto their heads; they wrapped themselves with sackcloth; they brought down to the ground the leading virgins in Jerusalem.

**2:11** Kaph. My eyes have failed with tears; my heart has been troubled; my glory has been poured out to the ground over the crushing of the daughter of my people, as infant and nursing child expire in the squares of the city.

**2:12** Lamed. To their mothers they said, "Where are grain and wine?" as they fainted like the wounded in the squares of the city, as their lives were poured out into the bosom of their mothers.

**2:13** Mem. What shall I testify to you, or to what shall I compare you, daughter of Jerusalem? Who will save and comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion? For the cup of your crushing has been magnified; who will heal you?

**2:14** Nun. Your prophets saw for you vain things and folly, and they did not uncover your injustice so as to turn back your captivity, and they saw for you vain pronouncements and expulsions.

**2:15** Samekh. All who pass along the way clapped their hands at you; they hissed and shook their heads at the daughter of Jerusalem: "This is the city," they will say, "the crown of gladness of all the earth."

**2:16** Ayin. All your enemies opened wide their mouths against you; they hissed and gnashed their teeth, and they said, "We have swallowed her up! Surely this is the day that we were expecting; we have found it, we have seen it."

**2:17** Pe. The Lord has done what He planned; He has accomplished His words that He commanded from ancient days; He tore down and did not spare, and He made an enemy glad over you; He exalted the horn of the one who afflicts you.

**2:18** Tsade. Their heart cried out to the Lord. Walls of Zion, bring down tears like torrents day and night; give yourself no respite; let not your eye keep silent, daughter.

**2:19** Qoph. Arise, exult in the night at the beginnings of your watch; pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord; lift up your hands to Him for the life of the infants who are fainting with hunger at the head of all the streets.

**2:20** Resh. See, Lord, and look at whom You have gleaned like this! Will women eat the fruit of their womb? A butcher has made a gleaning; will infants nursing at the breasts be murdered? Will You kill priest and prophet in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**2:21** Shin. Young boy and old man lay down in the street; my virgins and my young men went into captivity; by sword and by famine You killed; in the day of Your anger You butchered, You did not spare.

**2:22** Taw. He summoned a day of festival of my sojourning all around, and in the day of the Lord's anger there was no one escaping and no one left behind, as I overpowered and multiplied all my enemies.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Greek text reads "He called together" (synekalesen), a Greek textual variant for "He broke in pieces" (syneklasen), corresponding to the Hebrew "He cut off."
- **2:10** — The Greek reads "the leading virgins" (archegous parthenous) as the direct object of "they brought down," rendering the Hebrew word for "head" in its sense of "leader/chief."
- **2:16** — In the Greek text the acrostic headings of 2:16 (Ayin) and 2:17 (Pe) follow standard alphabetical order, even though the underlying Hebrew verses of chapter 2 place Pe (2:16) before Ayin (2:17).
- **2:20** — Continuing the gleaning metaphor of 1:22, the Greek adds "A butcher has made a gleaning" (linked to "You butchered" in 2:21) and includes the question "will infants nursing at the breasts be murdered?"
- **2:22** — The Greek closes the chapter with "as I overpowered and multiplied all my enemies," reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew than the Masoretic Text's "those whom I bore and reared, my enemy has destroyed."
