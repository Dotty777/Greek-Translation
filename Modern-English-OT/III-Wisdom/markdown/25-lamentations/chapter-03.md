## Text

**3:1** Aleph. I am the man who sees poverty; with the rod of His wrath against me,

**3:2** He took me and led me away into darkness and not light.

**3:3** Only against me He turned His hand all day long.

**3:4** He made my flesh and my skin grow old; He shattered my bones.

**3:5** Beth. He built up against me, and surrounded my head and toiled.

**3:6** In dark places He seated me, like the dead of old.

**3:7** He built up against me, and I will not go out; He made my bronze heavy.

**3:8** Gimel. Even though I will cry out and shout, He has blocked off my prayer.

**3:9** Daleth. He walled up my ways; He blocked up my paths; He disrupted them.

**3:10** A bear lying in ambush, He is to me a lion in hidden places.

**3:11** He pursued one who had departed and stopped me; He made me desolate.

**3:12** Heh. He bent His bow and set me up like a target for an arrow.

**3:13** He brought the arrows of His quiver into my kidneys.

**3:14** I became a laughingstock to all my people, their song all day long.

**3:15** Waw. He filled me with bitterness; He made me drunk with gall.

**3:16** And He knocked out my teeth with gravel; He fed me ashes.

**3:17** And He thrust my soul away from peace; I forgot good things.

**3:18** And my victory and my hope from the Lord perished.

**3:19** Zayin. I remembered from my poverty and from persecution; my bitterness and gall will be remembered,

**3:20** and my soul will brood over me.

**3:21** This I will set in my heart; therefore I will wait.

**3:25** Heth. The Lord is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul that will seek Him as good;

**3:26** and it will wait and be quiet for the salvation of the Lord.

**3:27** Teth. It is good for a man when he lifts a yoke in his youth;

**3:28** he will sit alone and be silent, because he lifted it upon himself.

**3:30** Yod. He will give the cheek to the one who strikes him; he will be filled with reproaches,

**3:31** because the Lord will not forever reject.

**3:32** Kaph. For the One who humbled will have compassion, even according to the abundance of His mercy.

**3:33** He did not answer from His heart and humble the sons of man.

**3:34** Lamed. To humble all the prisoners of the earth under his feet,

**3:35** to turn aside the justice of a man before the face of the Most High,

**3:36** to condemn a person when he is being judged—the Lord did not speak.

**3:37** Who spoke in this way, and it came to be? The Lord did not command it.

**3:38** From the mouth of the Most High, will not evil things and good come out?

**3:39** Mem. Why should a living person grumble, a man concerning his sin?

**3:40** Nun. Our way has been searched out and examined, and let us return to the Lord;

**3:41** let us lift up our hearts upon our hands to the High One in heaven.

**3:42** We have sinned, we have acted impiously, and You have not pardoned.

**3:43** Samekh. You spread a covering in wrath and chased us away; You killed, You did not spare.

**3:44** You spread a cloud as a covering for Yourself because of prayer,

**3:45** so that I should close my eyes and be rejected. Ayin. You placed us in the midst of the peoples.

**3:46** All our enemies opened their mouth wide against us.

**3:47** Fear and wrath came upon us, lifting up and shattering.

**3:48** My eye will bring down streams of water over the shattering of the daughter of my people.

**3:49** Pe. My eye has been swallowed up, and I will not be silent, because there is no recovery,

**3:50** until the Lord peers down and sees from heaven.

**3:51** My eye will glean upon my soul more than all the daughters of the city.

**3:52** Tsade. Hunting, all my enemies hunted me like a sparrow without cause.

**3:53** They put my life to death in a pit and placed a stone upon me.

**3:54** Water poured over my head; I said, "I have been rejected."

**3:55** Qoph. I called upon Your name, Lord, from the lowest pit.

**3:56** You heard my voice; do not hide Your ears from my supplication.

**3:57** You drew near for my help; on the day I called upon You, You said to me, "Do not fear."

**3:58** Resh. You decided, Lord, the cases of my soul; You redeemed my life.

**3:59** You saw, Lord, my troubles; You judged my cause.

**3:60** You saw all their vengeance, into all their thoughts against me.

**3:61** Shin. You heard their reproach, all their thoughts against me,

**3:62** the lips of those who rise up against me and their meditations against me all day long,

**3:63** their sitting down and their rising up. Look upon their eyes;

**3:64** You will render to them a repayment, Lord, according to the works of their hands.

**3:65** Taw. You will render to them a shielding, hardship of heart.

**3:66** You will pursue them in anger and completely consume them beneath heaven, Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Greek text preserves transliterated Hebrew acrostic letter names (such as Βή, Οὐαύ, Ζάι, and Λάβδ) placed at irregular stanza divisions rather than the Hebrew three-verse intervals. The phrase 'against me' (ἐπ᾽ ἐμέ) stands at the end of v. 1 in the Greek tradition, leading directly into v. 2.
- **3:5** — The Greek reads 'my head' (κεφαλήν μου) and the verb 'toiled' (ἐμόχθησεν), reflecting a different interpretation of the Hebrew words that the Masoretic Text reads as 'gall and hardship.'
- **3:25** — Verses 22–24 (as well as v. 29) are absent from this Greek witness, matching the Codex Vaticanus tradition. In addition, 'good' (ἀγαθόν) stands at the end of v. 25 ('seek Him as good') rather than opening v. 26 as in the Hebrew text and some Greek editions.
- **3:33** — The Greek reads 'answer' (ἀπεκρίθη), rendering the Hebrew verb root as 'answer' rather than its homonym 'afflict.'
- **3:45** — The Greek places the opening infinitives ('so that I should close my eyes and be rejected') before the acrostic heading Ayin, connecting them to v. 44 rather than reading 'offscouring and refuse' with 'You placed us' as in the Hebrew.
- **3:51** — The Greek verb ἐπιφυλλιεῖ literally means 'will glean,' reflecting a Hebrew verb that can mean both 'glean' and 'deal severely with.' The preposition παρά with the accusative may be read either as 'more than' or 'because of.'
- **3:63** — The Greek ends the verse with 'Look upon their eyes' (ἐπίβλεψον ἐπ᾽ ὀφθαλμοὺς αὐτῶν), differing from the Hebrew 'Look! I am their mocking song.'
- **3:65** — The Greek translates the Hebrew word for 'covering/dullness (of heart)' literally as 'shielding' (ὑπερασπισμόν) and joins 'of heart' with 'hardship' (καρδίας μόχθον).
