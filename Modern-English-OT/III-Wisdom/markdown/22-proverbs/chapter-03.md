## Text

**3:1** Son, do not forget my statutes, and let your heart keep my sayings.

**3:2** For they will add to you length of life and years of life and peace.

**3:3** Let acts of mercy and faithfulness not fail you; fasten them upon your neck, and you will find favor.

**3:4** And take thought for good things before the Lord and people.

**3:5** Be trusting in God with your whole heart, and do not exalt yourself upon your wisdom.

**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge her, so that she may cut your ways straight.

**3:7** Do not be wise in your own estimation, but fear God and turn aside from every evil.

**3:8** Then there will be healing for your body, and care for your bones.

**3:9** Honor the Lord from your righteous labors, and offer firstfruits to Him from your fruits of righteousness,

**3:10** so that your storehouses may be filled to fullness with grain, and your vats may gush forth with wine.

**3:11** Son, do not make light of the discipline of the Lord, nor lose heart when you are reproved by Him.

**3:12** For the one whom the Lord loves He reproves, and He flogs every son whom He accepts.

**3:13** Blessed is the person who has found wisdom, and the mortal who has seen understanding.

**3:14** For it is better to trade for her than for treasures of gold and silver.

**3:15** And she is more precious than costly stones; nothing evil will oppose her. She is well known to all who draw near to her, and no precious thing is worthy of her.

**3:16** For length of life and years of life are in her right hand, and in her left hand are wealth and glory; righteousness goes forth from her mouth, and she bears law and mercy upon her tongue.

**3:17** Her ways are good ways, and all her paths are in peace.

**3:18** She is a tree of life to all who hold fast to her, and to those who lean upon her as upon the Lord.

**3:19** God founded the earth by wisdom, and prepared the heavens by understanding.

**3:20** By perception the depths were broken open, and the clouds poured forth dew.

**3:21** Son, do not drift away, but keep my counsel and discernment,

**3:22** so that your soul may live, and grace may be around your neck. And there will be healing for your flesh, and care for your bones,

**3:23** so that you may walk confidently in peace along all your ways, and your foot will by no means stumble.

**3:24** For if you sit, you will be without fear, and if you sleep, you will slumber sweetly.

**3:25** And you will not fear terror that comes on, nor onslaughts of the ungodly coming on.

**3:26** For the Lord will be over all your ways, and He will plant your foot firmly so that you may not be shaken.

**3:27** Do not hold back from doing good to a needy person, whenever your hand is able to help.

**3:28** Do not say, "Go back and return, tomorrow I will give," when you are able to do good; for you do not know what the coming day will bring forth.

**3:29** Do not devise evil things against your friend, who lives nearby and trusts in you.

**3:30** Do not quarrel with a person without cause, so that he may not do some evil to you.

**3:31** Do not acquire the reproaches of evil men, nor envy their ways.

**3:32** For every lawless person is unclean before the Lord, and he does not sit in council among the righteous.

**3:33** The curse of God is in the houses of the ungodly, but the dwellings of the righteous are blessed.

**3:34** The Lord opposes the arrogant, but to the humble He gives grace.

**3:35** The wise will inherit glory, but the ungodly have exalted dishonor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The Greek uses the feminine pronoun "her" (αὐτήν), referring to wisdom rather than masculine "Him" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, and the verb ὀρθοτομέω literally means "cut straight."
- **3:12** — The Greek reads "flogs" (μαστιγοῖ), a reading reflected in Hebrews 12:6, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "as a father [the son in whom he delights]."
- **3:15** — The Septuagint includes two additional clauses in verse 15 ("nothing evil will oppose her. She is well known to all who draw near to her") and an additional couplet in verse 16 ("righteousness goes forth from her mouth, and she bears law and mercy upon her tongue") not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **3:18** — The Greek renders the second clause "and to those who lean upon her as upon the Lord," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "and those who hold her fast are called blessed."
- **3:32** — Following the primary Septuagint witnesses, the Greek text includes the negation "not" (οὐ), making the lawless person the subject who does not sit in council among the righteous, rather than God having intimacy with the upright as in the Hebrew.
