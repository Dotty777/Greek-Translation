## Text

**31:1** My words have been spoken by God; an oracle of a king whom his mother instructed.

**31:2** What, child, will you keep? What? Sayings of God. Firstborn, I speak to you, son. What, child of my womb? What, child of my vows?

**31:3** Do not give your wealth to women, nor your mind and life to regret.

**31:4** Do all things with counsel; drink wine with counsel. Rulers are hot-tempered, and let them not drink wine,

**31:5** so that by drinking they may not forget wisdom and be completely unable to judge the weak rightly.

**31:6** Give strong drink to those in sorrow, and wine to drink to those in anguish,

**31:7** so that they may forget their poverty and no longer remember their troubles.

**31:8** Open your mouth with the word of God, and judge all soundly.

**31:9** Open your mouth and judge justly, and decide the case of the poor and the weak.

**31:10** Who will find a valiant woman? And such a one is more precious than costly stones.

**31:11** The heart of her husband has confidence in her; such a one will not lack fine spoils.

**31:12** For she works good things for her husband all her life.

**31:13** Spinning wool and flax, she made something useful with her hands.

**31:14** She became like a ship trading from afar; and she herself gathers her livelihood,

**31:15** and rises while it is still night. And she gave food to her household and tasks to her maidservants.

**31:16** Having inspected a field, she bought it, and from the fruits of her hands she planted a property.

**31:17** Having girded her waist strongly, she set her arms firmly to work.

**31:18** And she tasted that it is good to work, and her lamp is not extinguished the whole night.

**31:19** She stretches out her forearms to profitable things, and she sets her hands firmly to the spindle.

**31:20** And she opened her hands to the poor, and stretched out her wrist to the destitute.

**31:21** Her husband does not worry about those in the house whenever he lingers somewhere, for all those with her are clothed.

**31:22** She made double cloaks for her husband, and garments of fine linen and purple for herself.

**31:23** And her husband becomes distinguished at the gates whenever he sits in council with the elders, inhabitants of the land.

**31:24** She made and sold linen garments, and sashes to the Canaanites. She opened her mouth carefully and lawfully, and established order for her tongue.

**31:25** She clothed herself with strength and splendor, and rejoiced in the last days.

**31:26** The ways of her houses are closely kept, and she did not eat the food of idleness.

**31:27** And she opens her mouth to the wise in accordance with law, and her mercy

**31:28** raised up her children and they became rich, and her husband praised her:

**31:29** "Many daughters have acquired wealth, many have done mighty things; but you stand above and have surpassed them all."

**31:30** Charms are false, and a woman's beauty is vain; for a discerning woman is blessed, and let her praise the fear of the Lord.

**31:31** Give her from the fruits of her lips, and let her husband be praised at the gates.

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which names King Lemuel, the Greek text reads 'My words have been spoken by God,' rendering the Hebrew consonants as a reference to God.
- **31:20** — Greek karpon here translates the anatomical term for 'wrist' in parallel with 'hands' in the first clause, though the identical Greek form can also mean 'fruit.'
- **31:24** — Greek reads 'to the Canaanites' (τοῖς Χαναναίοις), a proper name where the Hebrew has the word for 'merchant'; in this Greek text the verse also includes her opening her mouth carefully and lawfully.
- **31:27** — The sentence runs across verses 27-28 in the Greek ('her mercy raised up her children'), and this Greek clause about opening her mouth to the wise has no counterpart in the Hebrew text.
- **31:31** — The Greek text reads 'fruits of her lips' (rather than 'hands') and closes with 'let her husband be praised at the gates' (rather than 'let her works praise her').
