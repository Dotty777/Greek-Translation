## Text

**4:1** Listen, children, to a father's discipline, and pay attention to know discernment.

**4:2** For I give you a good gift; do not forsake my law.

**4:3** For I too became a son obedient to a father, and loved in the presence of a mother,

**4:4** who spoke and taught me, "Let our word be planted firmly in your heart; guard commandments,

**4:5** do not forget, nor overlook the utterance of my mouth,

**4:6** nor forsake her, and she will hold fast to you; fall in love with her, and she will keep you.

**4:8** Build a rampart around her, and she will exalt you; honor her, so that she may embrace you,

**4:9** so that she may give to your head a crown of graces, and shield you with a crown of delight."

**4:10** Listen, son, and receive my words, and the years of your life will be multiplied, so that many ways of life may be yours.

**4:11** For I teach you ways of wisdom, and I lead you onto straight paths.

**4:12** For if you walk, your steps will not be hemmed in; and if you run, you will not grow weary.

**4:13** Take hold of my discipline, do not let go, but guard it for yourself for your life.

**4:14** Do not enter the ways of the ungodly, nor envy the ways of the lawless.

**4:15** In whatever place they make camp, do not go there, but turn aside from them and pass by.

**4:16** For they will by no means fall asleep unless they do evil; their sleep has been taken away, and they do not sleep.

**4:17** For these feed on food of ungodliness, and they get drunk on lawless wine.

**4:18** But the ways of the righteous shine like light; they go forward and give light until the day is established.

**4:19** But the ways of the ungodly are dark; they do not know how they stumble.

**4:20** Son, pay attention to my utterance, and incline your ear to my words,

**4:21** so that your springs may not fail you; guard them in your heart.

**4:22** For it is life to those who find it, and healing to all flesh.

**4:23** With all watchfulness keep your heart, for from these are the outlets of life.

**4:24** Remove from yourself a crooked mouth, and push unjust lips far away from you.

**4:25** Let your eyes look at what is straight, and let your eyelids incline to what is just.

**4:26** Make straight paths for your feet, and direct your ways straight.

**4:27** Do not turn aside to the right nor to the left, but turn your foot away from an evil way. For God knows the ways on the right, but those on the left are twisted; and He Himself will make your paths straight, and He will lead your journeys forward in peace.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Greek uses plural verbs ("who spoke and taught me") and "our word," presenting both the father and the mother as speakers of the instruction in verses 4-9.
- **4:6** — Because the Greek text lacks the opening clause of verse 5 and all of verse 7 found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the feminine pronouns in verses 6-9 grammatically look back to "utterance" (ῥῆσιν) in verse 5 while continuing the personification of wisdom.
- **4:21** — Reading the Hebrew term for "eye/spring" as "spring," the Greek has "your springs" (αἱ πηγαί σου) rather than "your eyes."
- **4:27** — The Septuagint concludes chapter 4 with an extended addition contrasting the ways on the right and on the left and promising that God will make paths straight, which has no counterpart in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
