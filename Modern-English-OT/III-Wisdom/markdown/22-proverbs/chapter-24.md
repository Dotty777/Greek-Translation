## Text

**24:1** Son, do not envy evil men, nor desire to be with them;

**24:2** for their heart meditates on falsehoods, and their lips speak troubles.

**24:3** With wisdom a house is built, and with understanding it is set upright;

**24:4** with perception storerooms are filled with all precious and fine wealth.

**24:5** A wise man is better than a strong man, and a man who has prudence is better than a large farm.

**24:6** With guidance war is waged, and help is with a heart given to counsel.

**24:7** Wisdom and good insight are in the gates of the wise; the wise do not turn aside from the mouth of the Lord,

**24:8** but they deliberate in councils. Death meets the undisciplined,

**24:9** and a fool dies in sins. And impurity will be stained into a pestilent man

**24:10** in an evil day and in a day of affliction, until he fails.

**24:11** Rescue those being led to death, and ransom those being killed; do not hold back.

**24:12** And if you say, "I do not know this man," know that the Lord knows the hearts of all; and the One who formed breath for all Himself knows all things, who repays each one according to his works.

**24:13** Eat honey, son, for honeycomb is good, so that your throat may be sweetened.

**24:14** In this way you will perceive wisdom in your soul; for if you find it, your end will be good, and hope will not forsake you.

**24:15** Do not bring an ungodly man to the pasture of the righteous, nor be deceived by the filling of the belly.

**24:16** For seven times a righteous man will fall and rise up, but the ungodly will grow weak in evils.

**24:17** If your enemy falls, do not rejoice over him, and at his stumbling do not exalt yourself;

**24:18** because the Lord will see and it will not please Him, and He will turn His wrath away from him.

**24:19** Do not rejoice over evildoers, nor envy sinners;

**24:20** for there will by no means be offspring for a wicked man, and the lamp of the ungodly will be extinguished.

**24:21** Fear God, son, and the king, and do not disobey either of them;

**24:22** for suddenly they will punish the ungodly, and who will know the punishments of both? A son who guards a word will be free from destruction, and receiving it, he has received it. Let no falsehood be spoken from a tongue to a king, and let no falsehood go forth from his tongue. A king's tongue is a sword, and not of flesh; and whoever is handed over will be crushed. For if his wrath is sharpened, it consumes people along with their sinews, and devours the bones of people, and burns them up like a flame, so that they are inedible for the young of eagles.

**24:23** And these things I say to you, the wise, to recognize: to show deference to a person in judgment is not good.

**24:24** The one who says of the ungodly man, "He is righteous," will be cursed by peoples and hated among nations;

**24:25** but those who rebuke will appear better, and upon them a blessing will come.

**24:26** And people will kiss lips that answer good words.

**24:27** Prepare your works for going out, and make yourself ready for the field, and walk behind me, and you will rebuild your house.

**24:28** Do not be a false witness against your fellow citizen, nor open wide with your lips.

**24:29** Do not say, "Just as he treated me, I will treat him, and I will pay him back for how he wronged me."

**24:30** Like a farm is a foolish man, and like a vineyard is a person lacking sense;

**24:31** if you leave him alone, he will become wasteland and run wholly wild with weeds, and he becomes abandoned, and his walls of stone are torn down.

**24:32** Afterward I repented; I looked closely to choose discipline.

**24:33** A little I doze, and a little I sleep soundly, and a little I clasp my chest with my hands;

**24:34** and if you do this, your poverty will come walking ahead, and your want like a good runner.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — The Greek reads "a large farm" (γεωργίου μεγάλου), differing from the Hebrew phrasing about increasing strength.
- **24:9** — The Greek syntax of 24:9-10 is difficult, with "impurity" (ἀκαθαρσία) in the nominative as the subject of the passive verb ἐμμολυνθήσεται ("will be stained into") alongside "a pestilent man" (ἀνδρὶ λοιμῷ).
- **24:22** — The Greek contains an extended section on royal speech and wrath not found in the Hebrew; in "A son who guards a word will be free from destruction," the genitive "destruction" (ἀπωλείας) is governed by "free from" (ἐκτός), not by "son."
- **24:23** — The Saints Greek Critical Text arranges Proverbs 24-31 in standard chapter sequence (24:23-34, 25-29, 30, 31) rather than the rearranged Septuagint order found in Codex Vaticanus and Rahlfs (which places 30:1-14 after 24:22 and 30:15-31:9 after 24:34 before chapters 25-29).
- **24:27** — The Greek reads "walk behind me" (πορεύου κατόπισθέν μου), reflecting a different reading from the Hebrew "and afterward."
- **24:31** — The masculine pronouns ("him," "he," "his") can refer either to the foolish person of 24:30 under the extended vineyard metaphor or to the vineyard itself; χορτομανήσει vividly means "run wild with weeds."
