## Text

**5:1** Son, pay attention to my wisdom, and incline your ear to my words,

**5:2** so that you may guard good understanding; and the perception of my lips commands you.

**5:3** Do not pay attention to a worthless woman; for honey drips from the lips of a woman who is a prostitute, who for a time makes your throat smooth.

**5:4** Afterward, however, you will find it more bitter than gall and sharper than a two-edged sword.

**5:5** For the feet of folly lead down with death into Hades those who associate with her, and her footsteps are not firmly planted;

**5:6** for she does not travel the ways of life, and her paths are slippery and not easily known.

**5:7** Now therefore, son, listen to me, and do not make my words void.

**5:8** Make your way far from her; do not come near the doors of her houses,

**5:9** so that you may not surrender your life to others, and your livelihood to the merciless,

**5:10** so that strangers may not be filled with your strength, and your labors may not go into the houses of strangers;

**5:11** and you will feel remorse at the end, when the flesh of your body is worn away,

**5:12** and you will say, "How I hated discipline, and my heart turned away from reproofs;

**5:13** I did not listen to the voice of the one disciplining me and teaching me, nor did I incline my ear;

**5:14** I was nearly in every evil in the midst of the assembly and congregation."

**5:15** Drink waters from your vessels and from the spring of your wells.

**5:16** Do not let waters overflow for you from your spring, but let your waters pass through into your streets.

**5:17** Let them belong to you alone, and let no stranger share with you.

**5:18** Let your spring of water be your own, and rejoice together with the wife from your youth.

**5:19** Let a deer of affection and a foal of your graces keep company with you, and let her who is your own lead you and be with you at all times; for, going about in her affection, you will be very great.

**5:20** Do not be much with a strange woman, nor join yourself to the arms of her who is not your own;

**5:21** for the ways of a man are before the eyes of God, and He observes all his paths.

**5:22** Lawless deeds ensnare a man, and each one is bound fast by the cords of his own sins.

**5:23** This one dies with the undisciplined, and from the abundance of his own life he was cast out and perished because of folly.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Greek reads 'and the perception of my lips commands you' (compare MT 'and that your lips may guard knowledge'), and 5:3 opens with the LXX-distinct warning 'Do not pay attention to a worthless woman.'
- **5:16** — Unlike MT's rhetorical question, the Greek draws a contrast between forbidding waters to overflow from one's spring and directing one's waters to pass through into one's own streets.
- **5:19** — Greek paraphrases the marital imagery compared to MT and uses the rare adjective pollostos ('very great' or 'greatly multiplied'), likely reflecting Hebrew sg' ('grow great') and playing on polys ('much') in 5:20.
- **5:23** — Greek reads 'dies with the undisciplined' (compare MT 'for lack of discipline') and adds that he 'was cast out from the abundance of his own life.'
