## Text

**2:1** Son, if you receive the utterance of my commandment and hide it with yourself,

**2:2** your ear will listen to wisdom, and you will apply your heart to understanding, and you will apply it for the admonition of your son.

**2:3** For if you call upon wisdom and give your voice to understanding,

**2:4** and if you seek it like silver and search it out like treasures,

**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the Lord, and you will find the knowledge of God.

**2:6** For the Lord gives wisdom, and from His presence come knowledge and understanding;

**2:7** and He stores up salvation for those who act uprightly; He will shield their journey

**2:8** to guard the ways of righteous ordinances, and He will closely guard the way of those who revere Him.

**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and judgment, and you will direct rightly all good courses.

**2:10** For if wisdom comes into your mind, and discernment seems good to your soul,

**2:11** good counsel will guard you, and holy thoughtfulness will keep you,

**2:12** so that it may rescue you from an evil way and from a man who speaks nothing trustworthy.

**2:13** Oh, those who forsake straight ways to walk in ways of darkness,

**2:14** who delight in evil things and rejoice in evil perversity,

**2:15** whose paths are crooked and their tracks are winding,

**2:16** so as to make you far from a straight way and a stranger to righteous judgment. Son, do not let evil counsel overtake you,

**2:17** which abandons the teaching of youth and has forgotten a divine covenant.

**2:18** For it has set its house beside death, and its courses beside Hades with the earthborn;

**2:19** all who walk in it will not return, nor will they by any means attain straight paths; for they are not overtaken by years of life.

**2:20** For if they had walked good paths, they would have found smooth paths of righteousness.

**2:21** For the upright will dwell in the land, and the holy will remain in it;

**2:22** the ways of the ungodly will perish from the land, and the lawless will be driven out from it.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Septuagint treats verse 2 as the future consequence of verse 1 and includes an additional third clause regarding the admonition of one's son.
- **2:16** — Here and through verse 19 the Septuagint warns against "evil counsel" (κακὴ βουλή) rather than the Hebrew's "strange" or "forbidden woman."
- **2:18** — Greek γηγενῶν ("earthborn") renders the Hebrew Rephaim (shades of the dead or primeval giants), and ἄξονας ("courses") literally means tracks or axles.
- **2:20** — The Septuagint casts this verse as a contrary-to-fact conditional ("For if they had walked... they would have found...") rather than a purpose clause.
