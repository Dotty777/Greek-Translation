## Text

**3:1** On my bed in the nights I sought him whom my soul has loved; I sought him, and I did not find him; I called him, and he did not answer me.

**3:2** I will rise up now and go around in the city, in the marketplaces and in the broad streets, and I will seek him whom my soul has loved. I sought him, and I did not find him.

**3:3** The watchmen found me, those who go around in the city: "Have you seen him whom my soul has loved?"

**3:4** It was just a little while after I passed from them before I found him whom my soul has loved; I took hold of him and did not let him go, until I brought him into the house of my mother and into the chamber of her who conceived me.

**3:5** I have put you under oath, daughters of Jerusalem, by the powers and by the strengths of the field, that you do not rouse and awaken love until it wishes.

**3:6** Who is this coming up from the wilderness like columns of smoke, scented with myrrh and frankincense, from all the powders of a perfumer?

**3:7** Look, the couch of Solomon: sixty mighty men are around it, from the mighty men of Israel,

**3:8** all holding a sword, trained in war; each man has his sword upon his thigh because of terror in the nights.

**3:9** King Solomon made for himself a palanquin from the timbers of Lebanon.

**3:10** He made its pillars of silver, and its backrest golden; its seat is purple, its interior is inlaid with stone, love from the daughters of Jerusalem.

**3:11** Go out and look upon King Solomon, in the crown with which his mother crowned him on the day of his wedding and on the day of the gladness of his heart.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Greek includes the final clause "I called him, and he did not answer me," which is absent from the Hebrew MT in 3:1 (compare 5:6).
- **3:10** — Greek renders Hebrew rasup ("inlaid/fitted") concretely as "inlaid with stone" (lithostroton), followed by the accusative "love" (agapen).
- **3:11** — This Greek text omits the vocative "daughters of Zion" found in the Hebrew MT and some other Greek witnesses.
