## Text

**8:1** Who would give you as my kinsman, nursing at the breasts of my mother? Finding you outside, I will kiss you, and indeed they will not despise me.

**8:2** I will take you, I will bring you into the house of my mother and into the chamber of her who conceived me; I will give you to drink from the spiced wine, from the juice of your pomegranates.

**8:3** His left hand is under my head, and his right hand will embrace me.

**8:4** I have put you under oath, daughters of Jerusalem, by the powers and by the strengths of the field, that you do not rouse or awaken love until it wishes.

**8:5** Who is this coming up made white, leaning on her beloved? Under an apple tree I awakened you; there your mother was in labor with you; there she who bore you was in labor with you.

**8:6** Place me like a seal upon your heart, like a seal upon your arm; for love is mighty like death, jealousy is harsh like Hades; its wings are wings of fire, its flames.

**8:7** Much water will not be able to quench love, and rivers will not flood over it. If a man gives all his livelihood for love, they will utterly despise him.

**8:8** We have a little sister, and she has no breasts. What shall we do for our sister on the day when she is spoken of?

**8:9** If she is a wall, let us build silver battlements upon her; and if she is a door, let us mark out a cedar board upon her.

**8:10** I am a wall, and my breasts are towers; I was in their eyes like one who finds peace.

**8:11** A vineyard belonged to Solomon in Beethlamon; he gave his vineyard to the keepers; each man will bring for its fruit a thousand pieces of silver.

**8:12** My vineyard, my own, is before me; the thousand are for Solomon, and the two hundred for those who keep its fruit.

**8:13** You who sit in the gardens, companions are paying attention to your voice; let me hear it.

**8:14** Flee, my kinsman, and be like a gazelle or a fawn of the deer upon the mountains of spices.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Greek reads "and into the chamber of her who conceived me" (matching 3:4, whereas the Masoretic Text has "she who would teach me") and "your pomegranates" (ῥοῶν σου) rather than "my pomegranates."
- **8:4** — Unlike the Masoretic Text in this verse, the Greek includes "by the powers and by the strengths of the field" (rendering Hebrew terms for gazelles and does as "powers" and "strengths"), and uses the Hebraic oath particle ἐάν (literally "if") to express a solemn prohibition.
- **8:5** — The Greek reads "made white" (λελευκαθισμένη, meaning completely white or clothed in white) where the Masoretic Text reads "from the wilderness."
- **8:6** — Greek περίπτερα literally means "wings" or "pinions" (rendering Hebrew "flashes"), and the verse ends simply with "its flames" (φλόγες αὐτῆς) without the Masoretic Text's divine element ("flame of Yah").
- **8:11** — The Greek text gives the place name "Beethlamon" (Βεεθλαμών), corresponding to "Baal-hamon" in the Masoretic Text.
