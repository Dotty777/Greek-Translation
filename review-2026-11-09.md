# Saints English Greek fidelity and reading review · 9 November 2026

This pass checked the structure of all seventy books and revised 67 verse units across 16 books. It focused on quotations used by Jesus, apostolic quotations and the passages they cite, plus the three unresolved Old Testament readings identified in the [7 November review](review-2026-11-07.md).

The [decision record](review-2026-11-09-changes.json) gives each prior and revised reading, Greek source links, and the reason for the change. The structured verse file and the HTML and plain-text readers carry the same revised wording. Earlier change logs, WordPress verification records, and backups document their dated editions.

## Translation and reading criteria

Greek sense, syntax, emphasis, and repeated images govern the English. NASB95 and NKJV are reference points for formal fidelity and literary cadence. The project’s working Greek editions supply the wording. The Old Testament remains based on Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 with the existing book-level source exceptions, and the New Testament remains based on NA28. Where the passages share Greek wording and sense, the English should allow listeners to hear the quotation. Where the Greek differs, each passage keeps its own wording.

The English uses an elevated, readable register. Words such as *behold*, *forth* and *hallow*, and parallel clauses give the passages a traditional cadence where the source supports it. Established *Thou*, *Thee* and *Thy* forms follow the Bespoke policy for divine address. The accepted Genesis 1–11 benchmark is preserved.

## Quotation differences carried through

- **Luke 4:4 / Matthew 4:4 / Deuteronomy 8:3.** The shared opening is consistent. Luke’s running text ends after bread alone, while Matthew and Deuteronomy continue. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/LUK.4) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MAT.4) · [Greek source 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/DEU.8)
- **Luke 4:18–19 / Isaiah 58:6 / Isaiah 61:1–2.** Luke includes the release of the crushed from Isaiah 58:6. The healing of the brokenhearted and the day of recompense remain in Isaiah 61. Luke’s displayed NA28 text has neither clause. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/LUK.4) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/ISA.58) · [Greek source 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/ISA.61)
- **Matthew 22:44 / Mark 12:36 / Luke 20:42–43 / Psalms 109:1.** Matthew and Mark say ‘beneath Thy feet’. Luke and the Psalm retain the footstool image. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MAT.22) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MRK.12) · [Greek source 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/LUK.20) · [Greek source 4](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/PSA.109)
- **Acts 15:17 / Amos 9:12.** Acts names the Lord as the object of seeking. The working Greek text of Amos leaves the object implicit. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/ACT.15) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/AMO.9)
- **Hebrews 10:5 / Psalms 39:7.** Hebrews reads ‘a body’. The Rahlfs–Hanhart running text of the Psalm reads ‘ears’. Each wording is preserved in its own passage. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/HEB.10) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/PSA.39)
- **Exodus 3:14 / John 8:58.** The possible echo remains visible in the inherited English. Exodus calls God ‘the One Who Is’, while the words of Jesus in John are ‘I am’. The Greek phrases differ, and John does not introduce them as a quotation. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/EXO.3) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/JHN.8)
- **Mark 10:7 / Matthew 19:5 / Genesis 2:24.** NA28 prints the clause about cleaving to one’s wife in square brackets in Mark. This pass retains the archive’s reading and records the bracketed status here. [Greek source 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MRK.10) · [Greek source 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MAT.19) · [Greek source 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/GEN.2)

## Verification and remaining scope

The baseline at `ef8f801dce8d` contains 70 books, 1,297 reading sections and 34,523 stored verse or grouped text units. Each structured file matched its Git blob. The check found populated text, references and anchors, unique references and section-level anchors, and agreement between paired plain and display text after whitespace normalization. Proverbs intentionally has split reading sections for chapters 24, 30 and 31. The Daniel total includes the chapter-five preface.

For the edited units, the exact original wording was verified in JSON, HTML and TXT before replacement. The corresponding verse references, IDs, sequence, poetic line breaks, and existing inline speech and note markup were preserved.

This is a focused editorial comparison against the displayed Greek running texts.
