## Exact review scope

The earlier [GT-003 pilot](../../reviews/2026-11-12-GT-003-john-1-1-18/README.md) supplies 18 verse records and 252 Greek token alignments. This batch adds verse-specific running-text comparisons for John 1:19–51, Romans 3:21–31, James 2:14–26, Philemon, 2 John, 3 John and Jude: 135 more units. These lighter records are not represented as exhaustive token alignments. Total newly evidenced candidate scope: **153 units**; the other **7,790 units remain inherited drafts without a fresh programme comparison**. Historical source records are preserved and linked, not silently counted as new checks.

NA28 remains the authorized provisional base. Source pages consulted for the new comparisons: [John 1](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/JHN.1), [Romans 3](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/ROM.3), [James 2](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/JAS.2), [Philemon](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/PHM.1), [2 John](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/2JN.1), [3 John](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/3JN.1), [Jude](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/JUD.1). Publisher running text was read; no full critical apparatus or UBS6/ECM collation is claimed. Greek edition: Nestle–Aland, Novum Testamentum Graece, 28th revised edition, © 2012 Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft, Stuttgart.

The James 2:24 study retains “is justified,” explains “is declared righteous,” and separates the contextual proposal “is shown righteous.” It does not insert “saved” or “before people.” Middle Liddell was consulted as preliminary general lexical evidence; specialist review remains pending. Romans 3:22 and 3:25 studies preserve open genitive and sacrificial-language questions. These drafts are not deployed website popups.
