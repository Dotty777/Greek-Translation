# Seventy-book review record · 7 November 2026

The edition contains 70 books, 1,297 reading sections, and 34,523 numbered or grouped verse units. The working sources remain Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 for the Old Testament, with book-level exceptions documented in the archives, and provisional NA28 for the New Testament. Revelation received a fresh running-text comparison of every verse unit and an English pass for precision and spoken cadence. The other 69 books retain their earlier provisional editorial records.

## Staging and repository agreement

The pre-revision GitHub and staging export comparison covered all 70 books. All 34,380 matching page and verse identities agreed after normalization of whitespace and attached numeric note markers. A separate check of 449 rendered reading pages across 37 books matched all 11,367 units examined. The service rate-limited the wider rendered-page check.

All 143 units absent from the export were verified on the actual staging reading pages at their expected anchors. The omissions affect 1 Kings (1 unit), Daniel (76), Esther (2), Jeremiah (3), Proverbs (53), and Sirach (8). They include the Proverbs source-block pages, Sirach’s prologue, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, and grouped material on chapter pages. The export also includes 11 legacy navigation labels from Genesis 35 and Exodus 37–38 among the verse entries.

After the Revelation revision, all 405 of its verse units matched the saved WordPress content, the rendered reading pages, and the regenerated staging JSON export. [Revelation verification](revelation/verification.json)

Rainbow highlights are tied to exact verse wording. The staging reader still applies them to the 75 unchanged Revelation units and suppresses them on the 330 revised units pending re-alignment. Some rich cross-reference quotations still reflect the earlier wording. The parallel reader shows the revised reading text.

## Outstanding Greek checks

The earlier records document gaps in fresh source retrieval for 1 Samuel 7, 2 Samuel 20, and 2 Chronicles 5. The publisher’s Greek is accessible for all three. Review the idiom behind “looked after the Lord” in 1 Samuel 7:2, the missing opening interjection in 2 Samuel 20:21, and the two separate “one voice” expressions in 2 Chronicles 5:13. [1 Samuel 7](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/1SA.7), [2 Samuel 20](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/2SA.20), [2 Chronicles 5](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/2CH.5)

The wider review still requires checking source alignment where the Greek tradition and the site’s reading order diverge, comparing textual variants against the critical apparatus and the relevant editions, and independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews. Review of the UBS6 and ECM changes is especially relevant to Mark, Acts and Revelation. [German Bible Society on UBS6](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/the-sixth-edition-of-the-greek-new-testament-ubs6)
